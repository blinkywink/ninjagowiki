Ninjago Legends: Monstrosity is a series of Ninjago: Legends shorts. The episodes take place before the events of the first season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising.[1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Sun Creature Studio returned to animate the shorts, having previously worked on "Golden Legend." As such, the animation and art styles bear many similarities.
  • These shorts are set immediately after the events of the Merge.[1]
  • There were events in the Roblox games Creatures of Sonaria and Blade Ball based on these shorts.
Errors

Errors[]

  • While the description of this series from LEGO states that the shorts occur when Kai traveled across the Merged Lands after meeting Lloyd, Chris "Doc" Wyatt stated that they actually occur before Kai reunited with him.[1]
