Series 1 of the LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game debuted in 2016. It contains 180 mainline cards, 10 extra cards and 10 Limited Edition cards.

Trivia[]

  • This series is branded around Season 6: Skybound.
  • Out of every series, this one has the lowest number of Limited Edition cards.
  • The cards for Series 1 do not feature an icon denoting the series installment in the bottom right corner.
