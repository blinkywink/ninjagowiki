Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This series is branded around Season 8: Sons of Garmadon.
  • This was the final series of the game to be sold in the UK and in the Dutch language.
  • This was the first series of the game to be sold in Hungary.
    • Hungary recieved the UK version of the game, despite its only official language being Hungarian.
      • However, to account for this, the rules included in the Starter Pack for the Hungarian release was written in Hungarian instead of in English.
  • This is the only series to feature characters from The LEGO Ninjago Movie.
    • Cards featuring things from The LEGO Ninjago Movie include extra copyright information on the front of the card (in addition to what's already on the back of the card). This is to account for Warner Bros.' rights over the property not applicable to cards based on the mainline Ninjago brand.
      • However, applicable Puzzle cards feature the extra copyright information on the side reverse to the side with the image.
  • In the English release of the Starter Pack for this series, the included card checklist in the inner binder cover capitalizes the first letter of "Of" in the names of the "Garmadon and the Sons of Garmadon" Puzzle cards.[4]
