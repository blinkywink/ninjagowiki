Series 9 of the LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game, subtitled Dragons Rising,[4] debuted in 2023. It contains 256 mainline cards, 30 Limited Edition cards, and 2 XXL cards.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This series is branded around Dragons Rising Season 1.
  • The Action and Trap cards in this series only feature screenshots from Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 part one episodes.
    • Screenshots of part two episodes were used for Action and Trap cards in Series 9 Next Level.
  • Unlike the preceding and immediate proceeding series, Series 9 does not feature a "Platinum" card rarity, instead including a "Gold" card rarity in its place.
  • This is the last series to have corresponding Tins.
