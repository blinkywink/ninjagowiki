Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This series is branded around Dragons Rising Season 3.
  • This is the first series to feature symbols printed on each card to denote the card's rarity.
  • For the first time, a Ruby card rarity takes the place of the Platinum and Gold card rarities used in previous series.
  • In this series, only one card with a corresponding star-rarity was obtainable per Booster pack.
  • Tox and Morro are both considered as villains in this series, despite not being villains in the Ninjago: Dragons Rising season this series was intended to coincide with.
  • This was the first series since Series 4 to be without a corresponding "Next Level" series.
