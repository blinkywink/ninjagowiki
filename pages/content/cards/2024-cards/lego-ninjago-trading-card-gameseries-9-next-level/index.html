Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This series is branded around Dragons Rising Season 2.
  • Card LE19 - Thunderrob Limited Edition was only present in the German release of this series.
    • The German card gallery and Limited Edition card list are both missing this card, possibly due to its limited release exclusively for Blue Ocean's online shop, which didn't correspond with the debut of the series.[4]
  • Card LE6 - Action Arin Limited Edition was not present in the German release of the game,[1] but is present in the Polish version.[3] It is unknown if this card was present in other languages'/regions' releases of the game.
  • This was the final series to have the "Next Level" subtitle.
