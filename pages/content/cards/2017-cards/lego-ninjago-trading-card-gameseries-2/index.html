Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This series is branded around Season 7: The Hands of Time.
  • This was the first series to include Puzzle cards and XXL cards.
  • Starting with this series, all cards are marked with an icon in the bottom right corner to identify which series they belong to; for Series 2, the icon is that of a snake head.
  • In the English release of the Starter Pack for this series, the included card checklist in the inner binder cover fully capitalizes Wu's name in the listing of Card 27 - Action Wu and Card 28 - Super Wu.
