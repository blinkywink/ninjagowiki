How to use

How to use[]

This is your Character Card. It shows who you are in the game, what your default golden weapon is, and how much Spinjitzu Power (SP) you have.

Requirements to Use

Requirements to Use[]

In order to play a Battle card, your Character card's SP must be greater than or equal to a Battle Card's SP.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This card holds the only promotional art of Lloyd ZX (including CGI) that LEGO has ever made. It's also the art used on the packaging for the second wave of 2012 sets.
  • This is one of the cards with more SP tied with Sensei Wu and Garmadon.
  • A miniature version of this card can be found on a 1x2 stickered tile in the Lego City set 60380 Downtown. This is the first non-Ninjago set to feature one of these miniature card recreations.
