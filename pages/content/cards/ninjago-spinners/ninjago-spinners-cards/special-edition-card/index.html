Overview

This article is about the Special Edition cards from the Ninjago Spinners game. For the Limited Edition cards found in the TCG, see Character card (TCG)#Limited Edition cards.


A Special Edition card is a type of card that is used in the Ninjago Spinners game.

Most Special Edition cards are either 3D lenticular or golden foil alterations of existing Character or Battle card designs, whereas others, such as Card ★7 - Sensei Wu, are exclusive designs of the same types. Of the Wave 2 Special Edition cards, all but Card ★22 - Rattla are unique designs not available elsewhere.
