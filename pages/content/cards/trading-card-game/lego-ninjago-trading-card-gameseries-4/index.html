Series 4 of the LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game, subtitled Dragon Hunters,[3] debuted in 2019. It contains 252 mainline cards, 25 Limited Edition cards, 6 XXL cards, and 2 XXXL cards.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This series is branded around Season 9: Hunted.
  • This was the first series of the game not to be sold in the UK or in the Dutch language.
    • Despite not being sold in the UK, English cards for this series were still sold in Hungary. English cards have continued to be sold in Hungary to this day, despite the game never returning to the UK.
