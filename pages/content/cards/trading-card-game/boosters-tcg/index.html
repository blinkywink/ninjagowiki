Overview

Boosters are a type of product released for the LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game. They typically include a few of the respective series' cards, as well as a miniature rules book every so often. They're sold on their own, but can also be included with Starter Packs, Blisters, Multipacks, Tins, issues of the Ninjago Magazine, and other products from Blue Ocean.
