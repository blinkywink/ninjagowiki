Overview

Card sleeves are an item used in the LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game. They can be used for cosmetic purposes, to differentiate between one's own cards and their opponent's, or to protect cards from damage. They're included in Extra-packs, and have been known to be included with issues of 5! magazine.
