Overview

Not to be confused with Extra-packs.

Multipacks are a type of product released for the LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game. They typically include a few Boosters and a Limited Edition or XXL card. There are usually multiple Multipacks per series, each differing by the Limited Edition or XXL card they include.
