Overview

Tins are a type of product released for the LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game. They typically include a few Boosters and a with some also including minifigure foil/paper packs (that were first seen bundled with past issues of the Ninjago Magazine). There are usually multiple Tins per series, each differing by their designs and inclusions.
