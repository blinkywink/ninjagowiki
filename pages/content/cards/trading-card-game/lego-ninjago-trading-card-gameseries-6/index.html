Series 6 of the LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game, subtitled The Island,[2] debuted in 2020. It contains 252 mainline cards, 27 Limited Edition cards, and 6 XXL cards.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This series is branded around The Island.
  • In this series, only one card with a corresponding star-rarity was obtainable per Booster pack.
  • In the Hungarian release of the Starter Pack for this series, the included binder cover is incorrectly labelled as "Serie 6" instead of "Series 6."
    • This is probably because the Hungarian release (intended to be translated to English), was based on the original German version of the product. "Serie" is German for "Series."
