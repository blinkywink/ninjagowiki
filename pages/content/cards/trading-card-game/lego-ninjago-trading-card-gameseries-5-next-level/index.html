Series 5 Next Level of the LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game, subtitled Prime Empire,[2] debuted in 2020. It contains 126 mainline cards, 18 Limited Edition cards, and 4 XXL cards.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This series shares a name with and is branded around Season 12: Prime Empire.
  • This was the first "Next Level" series in the LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game.
    • As such, this was also the first series to include Level Up cards.
  • This was the first series since Series 1 to not include Puzzle cards.
