Overview

Starter Packs are a type of product released for the LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game. They typically include a collectors binder, a game board/arena, a few Limited Edition cards, an XXL card, some Boosters, and a full game rulebook with a card gallery on the other side.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The contents of the Hungarian Starter Packs often include a mixture of Hungarian and English text, despite Hungary's only official language being Hungarian.
  • Starting from Series 10, Starter Pack packaging switched from plastic to paper.
