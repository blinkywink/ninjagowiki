Overview

Many clothing designs in the Ninjago franchise are later repurposed for other characters in different contexts. Below is a list of instances of these instances throughout the sets and TV series, excluding any designs that originate from other themes.

Introduced in 2011

Introduced in 2011[]

Original character Character Description
Jay's trainee belt design is used on the First Mate.
A recolored version of Jay's trainee hip printing appears on one of the Destiny's Bounty pirates.
A recolored version of Kai and Cole's trainee leg printing appears on one of the Destiny's Bounty pirates.
Nya's original legs are recolored for Maya's flashback/Season 7 outfit.
Nya's original leg design is used on the ninja's tea uniforms in "Winds of Change."
