Overview

The following is a list of non-musical audio that has been recycled throughout the Ninjago TV series and related media, listed by the season in which it was first heard.

Season 1: Rise of the Snakes

Season 1: Rise of the Snakes[]

  • In "Rise of the Snakes," Garmadon's laugh is heard when the ninja see Lloyd's shadow in Jamanakai Village.[1]
    • In "Tick Tock," part of this audio is used after Garmadon greets Wu upon the latter's arrival in the Realm of Madness.[2]
  • In "Rise of the Snakes," Kai growls during his match with Cole.[3]
    • In "The Green Ninja," this audio is used when Garmadon is keeping Kai and the other ninja awake.[4]
    • In "Enter the Digiverse," this audio is used when Kai and the other ninja are sinking in the Digiverse.
    • In "Ghost Story," this audio is used when Kai tries to get his Walloper to move.
    • In "Dead Man's Squall," this audio is used when Kai, Jay, and Cole are fighting the hacked Samurai X.
  • In "Home," when Kai says the ninja have to destroy Lloyd's treehouse fortress, Jay responds, "Whoa! Are you sure?" before commenting on the treehouse's appearance.[5]
    • In "Enter the Digiverse," the "Whoa" part is used when Jay assumes Cyrus Borg is about to say the ninja will have multiple lives in the Digiverse.
    • In great devouler Jay the whoa part was when him and Zane was sliding down the ice.
    • In "Operation Land Ho!," the "Whoa" part is used after Jay watches Skylor jump from a pirate jet to a rooftop.
    • In "The Mask of Deception," the "Whoa" part is used when the ninja are reuniting.[6]
  • In "Home," the Wilhelm scream is heard when Mezmo falls off the collapsing treehouse.[7]
    • In "The Rise of the Great Devourer," this audio is used when Cole launches Fang-Suei off his Tread Assault,[8] and again when Jay knocks several Serpentine off the tour bus using Spinjitzu.[9]
    • In "The Last Hope," this audio is used when Cole knocks a Stone Scout off the construction platform.[10]
    • In "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master," this audio is used when the ninja knock a Stone Warrior away using Spinjitzu.[11]
    • In "The Surge," this audio is used when Nya kicks one of the mechanical arms as she, Wu, and the students escape Borg Tower.
    • In "The Art of the Silent Fist," this audio is used when Lloyd kicks a Nindroid Warriors off a ledge,[12] and again when a Nindroid Warrior is knocked away by the water wheel.[13]
    • In "The Greatest Fear of All," this audio is used when the prisoners fall back into Kryptarium after Kai destroys Zane's ice bridge.
    • In "Public Enemy Number One," this audio is used in the background when Jay tries to use his electricity to get ninja chips from the vending machine at the Biker Tavern.
    • In "Attack of the Cursed Specters," this audio is used when Zugu drives into a tree branch.
    • In "A Line in the Sand," this audio is used when Nya knocks Slackjaw off a Ferris wheel at Mega Monster Amusement Park.
    • In "Operation Heavy Metal: Buffmillion," this audio is used when Buffmillion knocks over a car in Ninjago City.
    • In "Operation Heavy Metal: Blunck," this audio is used when Slackjaw is flung from a junk pile in Ed & Edna's Scrap N Junk.
    • In "Out of the Fire and Into the Boiling Sea," this audio is used when Cole kicks Tannin from the construction platform.
    • In The LEGO Ninjago Movie, this audio is used several times when Lloyd fires missiles at the Shark Army.
    • In The LEGO Ninjago Movie blooper reel, this audio is used when Garmadon fires several generals out of his volcano lair.[14]
    • In "The Weekend Drill," this audio is used when a police officer flies through the air behind Noble on Cole's screen.[15]
    • In "Blue Lightning," this audio is used when Jay knocks Lasha off a noodle truck using Spinjitzu.[16]
    • In "Samurai X-Treme," this audio is used when Nya knocks several Skulkin off a roof in her Samurai Mech.[17]
    • In "The Darkness Comes," this audio is used when a civilian is taken over by the darkness at the train station.
    • In "A Day in the Life of a Golden Ninja," this audio is used when the outhouse is shot into the air with the Golden Ninja inside.[18]
  • In "Snakebit," Jay says "Uh oh" after his Storm Glider vanishes.[19]
    • In "Grave Danger," this audio is used before Jay falls from an ice stalactite.
  • In "Can of Worms," the ninja argue with each other as the result of Lloyd's pranks.[20]
    • In "The Belly of the Beast," part of this audio is used when the ninja argue about who should enter Beohernie's stomach.[21]
  • In "Can of Worms", Jay screams "No! NO!!" when his sparring bot malfunctions as the result of Lloyd's sabotaging.
    • In "Day of the Great Devourer", this audio is reused when Jay and Zane fly off the latter's ice track as the Devourer opens wide.
    • In "The Last Hope", this audio is reused when the team falls off a cliff when the Celestial clock reaches the end of its countdown and Nya is captured.
    • In "Peak-a-Boo", this audio is reused when Jay's ElectroMech is sliding towards boulder before getting destroyed.
    • In "On a Wish and a Prayer", this audio is reused when Nadakhan knocks Jay down the collapsed bridge.
    • In "The Turn of the Tide", the second "NO!!" is used when Wojira seemingly vaporizes Nya.
  • In "The Snake King," Jay says, "Ha! I love it!" when the ninja agree that whoever learns the samurai's identity will become the Green Ninja.[22]
    • In "The Surge," this audio is used after Jay uses his Techno Blade to transform a security tank into the Lightning off-roader.
    • In "My Dinner With Nadakhan," this audio is used after Nadakhan taunts Jay while making him clean the side of the Misfortune's Keep.
  • In "The Royal Blacksmiths" Cole says, "He-Hey, Dad. How long has it been?" when meeting his father after years.
    • In "The Stone Army," the "He-Hey" is used when Cole is sliding down the dinosaur bones after crushing the Giant Stone Warrior.
  • In "The Green Ninja," when Kai asks Nya if she's the Green Ninja, she says, "What? Of course not."[23]
    • In "On a Wish and a Prayer," the "What?" is used by Nya after Jay says he sees Zane.
  • In "Day of the Great Devourer," when Kai says he noticed a weak spot on the Great Devourer's forehead, Jay begins his response with a sarcastic, "Oh, great!"[24]
    • In "Ghost Story," this audio is used after Jay watches Morro throw Cole.
  • In "Day of the Great Devourer," Nya screams when she and Lloyd are stuck in the Samurai Mech in the Great Devourer's mouth.[25]
    • In "The Art of the Silent Fist," this audio is used when P.I.X.A.L. is tied to a tree by Zane.
    • In "Curseworld, Part I." this audio is used when Morro gloats about The Preeminent grabbing Lloyd into the Cursed Realm.
    • In "Wasted True Potential," this audio is used when Nya wakes atop the Monastery of Spinjitzu.[26]
    • In "Riddle of the Sphinx," this audio is used when Nya screams into the water in frustration.[27]
Season 3: Rebooted

Season 3: Rebooted[]

  • In "The Art of the Silent Fist," Lloyd says "Hehehe, Yeah, this is sweet." after putting on his Techno robes for the first time.
    • In "Only One Can Remain," the audio is reused and altered to instead say "Yeah, this is sweet. Hehe." after Lloyd puts on his Tournament robes fpr the first time.
Season 4: Tournament of Elements

Season 4: Tournament of Elements[]

  • In "The Invitation," Skylor says, "I'm sorry if I was cold to you earlier," before she helps Kai up and absorbs his power.
    • In "Only One Can Remain," this audio is used when Skylor and Kai talk outside their rooms. In this scene, the audio is part of a longer line, indicating that it was originally recorded from this episode.
  • In "Spy for a Spy," Zane says "Not that way. That will only take us back to where we started."
    • In "Grave Danger," The "Not that way" part is used by Zane when P.I.X.A.L informs him he's going the wrong way.
Season 5: Possession

Season 5: Possession[]

  • In "Curseworld, Part I," Lloyd growls while attempting to destroy the Realm Crystal.
    • In "Enkrypted," this audio is used when Lloyd tries to break out of his handcuffs.
Season 6: Skybound

Season 6: Skybound[]

  • In "Public Enemy Number One," when the Police Commissioner says he's not blind, Simon says, "But your eye is twitching."
    • In the same episode, this audio is used after Simon tells the Commissioner that the ninja sent them on a wild goose chase.
    • In "The Last Resort," this audio is used when Simon, Tommy, and the Commissioner are being circled by sharks in the Endless Sea.
  • In "Enkrypted," before stopping the Mechanic and another Kryptarium inmate from disassembling Zane, P.I.X.A.L. says, "Brace yourself, Zane. I'm rerouting all existing power to your chest in three, two, one."
    • In "Scavengers," this audio is used before Zane shocks away the Vermillion closing in around him and Nya.
Season 8: Sons of Garmadon

Season 8: Sons of Garmadon[]

  • In "The Jade Princess," Ultra Violet goes, "Oh-ho-ho-ho yeah" while watching Starfarer at a theater.
    • In the same episode, this audio is used when Ultra Violet gets the Mask of Deception.
  • In "Dead Man's Squall." Jay goes "He did not just-" and Kai responds "He just did." after Lloyd jumps off the Destiny's Bounty.
    • In "Game of Masks." both audios are used in the same order after Lloyd lands on the Destiny's Bounty's anchor.
Season 11: Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu

Season 11: Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

  • In "The Kaiju Protocol," Gayle Gossip's helicopter is blown away by The Preeminent while she shouts, "We'll be back after a short message!.
    • In "The Calm Before the Storm," this audio is used when Gayle's helicopter is blown away by the tide caused by Wojira.
  • In "Awakenings," Cole tells Nya, "I knew you could do it!" after she seemingly defeats Boreal.[28]
    • In "The Turn of the Tide," this audio is used when the ninja congratulate Nya after she defeats Wojira.[29]
Season 12: Prime Empire

Season 12: Prime Empire[]

  • In "The Temple of Madness," after Nya gets cubed, Jay says, "Don't leave me! I love you."[30]
    • In "The Turn of the Tide," both parts of this audio are used separately when Jay tries to stop Nya from leaving to be one with the Endless Sea.[31][32]
  • In "One Step Forward, Two Steps Back," after Jay suggests the name Fast Chickens, Nya sighs and says, "Jay."
    • In "Unsinkable," this audio was used when Jay accidentally faired and dropped his harpoon gun.
