Media whose events can be seen as canonical

Media whose events can be seen as canonical[]

Some Ninjago installments were written in a way that they could be considered canon by fans, while not being explicitly confirmed.

  • Decoded[23] (set between "Golden Hour" and Way of the Departed)
  • Way of the Departed (takes place between Decoded and Episode 75)
  • The Splinter in the Blind Man's Eye (takes place between Season 10 and Season 11)
Books whose events can be seen as canonical

Books whose events can be seen as canonical[]

Tommy Andreasen has stated that stories that do not contradict the show can be considered canonical.[24] Below is a list of installments that do not contradict the main narrative and thus can be seen as canonical. Doc Wyatt has also stated that any Ninjago material that doesn't contradict the show are meant to be canon.[25]

  • Pilot episode character books
    • Kai: Ninja of Fire (Intro while training, Episode 1. Main story on the way to the Ice Temple, between Episodes 2 and 3.)[26]
    • Zane: Ninja of Ice (Intro on the way to the Caves of Despair, Episode 2. Main story on the way to the Floating Ruins, Episode 3.)[27]
    • Cole: Ninja of Earth (Intro in the Floating Ruins, Episode 3. Main story on the way to the Ice Temple, between Episodes 2 and 3.)[28]
    • Jay: Ninja of Lightning (Intro after making plan to ride dragons, Episode 4. Main story on the way to the Ice Temple, between Episodes 2 and 3.)[29]
  • Mask of the Sensei (takes place between Pilot episodes and Mini-movies)
  • The Bravest Ninja of All (#1 takes place between "Rise of the Snakes" and "Home", #2 takes place between "Home" and "Snakebit")
  • Snakes in the Grass (both stories take place between "Snakebit" and "Never Trust a Snake")
  • Farm of Fear (#1 takes place after "The Snake King", #2 takes place between "The Snake King" and "Tick Tock")
  • Nothing in the Dark (#1 takes place between "Can of Worms" and "The Snake King", #2 takes place during "The Snake King")
  • "It's Hard to be a Snake" ( takes place after the opening scene of "The Snake King," as all the Snake Clans have already been liberated)
  • "Snake's Honour" (takes place sometime between "The Snake King" and "All of Nothing")
  • "Danger in the Dark" (take place between "Blackout" and "The Curse of the Golden Master"
  • Who is the Phantom Ninja?[30] (takes place between "Enter the Digiverse" and "Codename: Arcturus")
  • A Team Divided (takes place between Season 3 and Season 4)
  • The Search for Zane (takes place within the time span of "Only One Can Remain")
  • "A Cup of Victory" (one of the fake stories published by a charlatan called Lar)
  • "Forgive Me" (takes place between "The Corridor of Elders" and "Winds of Change")
  • The New Ninja (takes place between "Curseworld, Part II" and The Realm of Shadows)
  • "The Clean Bucket" (takes place between episodes 48 and 49)
  • Ghost Stories (#1 take place during "Ghost Story", #2 is the book version of the episode "Stiix and Stones", #3 take place between Episodes 48 and 49)
  • "Be Careful What You Wish For" (takes place between Episodes 55 and 56)
  • Sky Pirates (#1 takes place 200 years before Ninjago, #2 takes place between final scenes of "Enkrypted," #3 takes place between Episodes 55 and 56)
  • The Last Wish (#1 takes place between "The Last Resort" and "The Way Back", #2 takes place during "The Way Back")
  • Legend of the Brown Ninja (takes place between Season 6 and the Dark Island Trilogy)
  • Brother/Sister Squad (#1 takes place during Episode 67 and #2 during Episode 68. #3 takes place right after Episode 74.)
  • School for Crooks (#1 and #2 take place between Way of the Departed and Episode 75. #3 takes place within the time span of episode 76)
  • The Return of Garmadon (#2 take place during "The Oni and the Dragon")
  • Fired Up! (takes place between Season 9 and Tales from the Monastery of Spinjitzu)
  • The Mysterious Pyramid (#1 briefly summarizes episodes 99-102 and also shows a short story right after "The Belly of the Beast," #2 and #3 both takes place during "Boobytraps and How to Survive Them")
  • The Legend of Jay (takes place during and after "The Meaning of Victory", "The Stowaway", "Manhunt")
  • Sneak Attack! (takes place between "Unsinkable" and "The Storm Amulet")
  • Tests and Trials (#1 takes place between "The Battle of the Second Monastery" and "The Temple of the Dragon Cores", #2 takes place before Season 1, #3 takes place during "The Blood Moon")
  • Skills and Powers (#1 takes place before "The Administration," #2 takes place between "Shattered Dreams" and "Beyond the Phantasm Cave," #3 takes place between "Rising Ninja" and "The Shape of Motion")
  • In the Land of Monsters (#2 takes place years before Shatterspin, #1 and #3 are not canon)
  • The Land of Monsters (takes place between "To Become a Monster" and "Fallen Master")

Website

Website[]

  • Tommy Andreasen has said that, while he may not personally like some of the website's additions to the lore of the show, the information on LEGO.com is most likely canonical,[34] though it "Depends on how false the info on the website is."[35] However, he has indicated that some facts originating from the Rebooted character descriptions are not true.[36]
    • However, he previously stated he was the one to have written Kai's Rebooted description in 2012[37][38] (and presumably the others' as well) and that Kai's being called "Ash" for the first two months of his life can be considered canonical.[39]
    • According to Tommy Kalmar, all information from the Museum is canonical.[40]
Encyclopedias and guidebooks

Encyclopedias and guidebooks[]

  • Tommy Andreasen has stated that every guidebook and encyclopedia made back when he was working on Ninjago is intended to be canon.[41]
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt has claimed that any Ninjago media that works with the show's canon can be considered canonical. This should expand to guidebooks and encyclopedias as well.[42]
