Overview

In many episodes of Ninjago, notably earlier ones, there are quite a few notable instances of characters' voices being swapped. This can be due to animating the wrong model or the wrong line being chosen. The characters in which this error applies generally consist of the four main ninja before Lloyd and Nya joined the team, both of whom are rarely affected. Zane is also rarely switched.
