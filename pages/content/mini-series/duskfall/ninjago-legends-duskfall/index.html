Ninjago Legends: Duskfall, stylized as DuskFall, is an upcoming series of Ninjago: Legends shorts[1] that will be released in September 2026.[2] A set promotion featuring some clips from the episodes was posted on Bilibili, Rednote, and Weibo on June 1, 2026.[3] The series takes place in the future.[4]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • According to Glen Lakin, Duskfall will be canon to the main timeline until they decide to contradict it with future media.[5]
  • The first three teasers released on LEGO's YouTube channel all use a fan orchestral remix of the original Overture.[6]
