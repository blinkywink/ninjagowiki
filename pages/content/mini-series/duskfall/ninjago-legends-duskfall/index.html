Overview

“We're not fighting demons or monsters anymore. It's people. Nations. They call us warriors. Protectors. Ninja. Drops of rain on a wildfire that only spreads. What if we're really just embers, sparking new conflict wherever we go? If the world is ever going to know peace, peace that lasts longer than the change of seasons, someone has to stop fanning the flames.”
— Nya, "Epilogue"

Ninjago Legends: Duskfall, occasionally stylized as DuskFall, is a series of Ninjago Legends shorts that was released on September 18, 2026 in South Korea and on September 19, 2026 on YouTube. A set promotion featuring some clips from the episodes was posted on Bilibili, Rednote, and Weibo on June 1, 2026.[1] The series takes place in the future.[2]

Official description

Official description[]

Trailer: Encounter new monsters, giant serpents and an army of mechs! What else awaits Kai and the rest of the ninja crew in LEGO NINJAGO Legends: DuskFall?[3]

Three EPIC Battles!: Duskfall has arrived, and the enemies are stronger than ever but so are the Ninja.

A Storm is Brewing in the all new LEGO NINJAGO Legends: Duskfall storyline. Watch exhilarating elemental clashes and masterful weaponry attacks from Cole. Witness the team protect Ninjago City with the raw power of an elemental dragon against a colossal serpent. And finally, watch Kai and Nya stand their ground to protect the elementals in a high action mecha showdown!

Across eras, the Ninja legacy endures as heroes face ancient Oni, city-sized monsters, and future wars to protect the spirit of Ninjago. How will the Ninja win these complex battles and solve these deep mysteries? Find out right now in the new LEGO NINJAGO Legends: Duskfall episodes, available now! 🎥🎬[4]

ITVX: The Masters of Spinjitzu tell the stories of legends who faced ancient Oni, city-sized monsters, and the future wars that will be fought to protect the spirit of Ninjago.

Extended: The spirit of Ninjago travels through time, from the raw magic of the Oni age to the neon-lit tragedy of the Mech Wars. As the Ninja grow old and their battles fade into legend, their elemental artefacts and machines remain. These stories show that while heroes eventually walk away, their courage is never lost - it is simply rediscovered by new generations in a merged world.[5]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The shorts were animated by La Chouette Compagnie.
  • According to Glen Lakin, Duskfall is canon to the main timeline until they decide to contradict it with future media and it "does not railroad the main series into any events."[6]
  • According to Glen Lakin, the short were made to fill in some lore between Embers + Ashes and the mainline series and showcase the potential for a Ninjago anime.[7]
  • The first three teasers released on LEGO's YouTube Channel all use a fan orchestral remix of the original Overture.[8]
  • Fritz is the only character introduced in Ninjago: Dragons Rising to appear.
