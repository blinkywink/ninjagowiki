Overview

Celebration! is the seventh and final episode of Crossroads' Crosstalk.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

The Crossroads Crosstalk podcast is back for a Ninja Appreciation Day special! Hear just how knowledgeable (or not!) our hosts are and listen out for an extra special guest – the Green Ninja himself – Lloyd![1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This episode aired on the same day pilot episodes aired in 2011.
  • During the episode, Kreel incorrectly refers to the ninja as Crud, the Master of Dirt (Cole; Earth), Zoot, the Master of (Zane; Ice), Nada, the Master of Melted Ice (Nya; Water), and two or three red ninja, the Masters of Annoying (Kai and Wyldfyre; Fire and Heat). She additionally misnames Arin as "Art" and Sora as "Sorghum."
  • This episode marks the fifth time Garmadon has been mentioned in Ninjago: Dragons Rising, he was previously mentioned in:
    • The Season 1 episode "The Temple of the Dragon Cores".
    • The Season 2 episode "The Spell at the Waterfall".
    • The Peak of 1000 Suns story.
    • The Season 3 episode "Casket of Bones".
  • This episode marks the first time the term "Sensei" has been used in Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the first time in an episode since "My Dinner With Nadakhan" nearly 10 years before this episodes released.
