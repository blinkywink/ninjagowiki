Overview

Conspiracy! is the second episode of Crossroads' Crosstalk.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

In this episode, Kreel and Lobbo’s special guest reveals all the latest conspiracy theories to hit the Crossroads.[1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The opening scene features the first in-universe mention of LEGO, with the LEGO Play app advertised, although it is unknown if the scene is fully canon.
  • Streets supposedly named after Skales and Ulysses Trustable are revealed.
  • It is implied that the wastelands and the Sea of Sand are the same location, as in "The Missing," Frak stated that Dorama's puppet had been terrorizing the wastelands, while Intelligent George, the source of Frak's information, stated that the puppet was in the Sea of Sand.
  • The jingle for Merlopian Rotfish Mouthwash was written, played, and sung by YouTuber MiniSuperHeroesToday.[2]
  • During the podcast, Kreel calls Arin and Sora "Yellow-Pants" and "Soreria," respectively.
