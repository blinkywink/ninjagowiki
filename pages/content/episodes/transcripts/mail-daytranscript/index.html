Mail Day/Transcript

Overview

(Kai enters to find Lloyd in the courtyard.)

Kai: Hey! The Postman was just here.

Lloyd: Oh! I haven't seen that guy in ages. How's he doing?

Kai: Eh, he's pretty mad at us.

Lloyd: What? Why?

Kai: Because of what he had to drag up the mountain! (Grunts)

Lloyd: Woah. That's a big bag of mail... But he's delivered bigger bags up here before.

Kai: Yeah, this is only bag number one.

Lloyd: What.

(Arin, Sora, and Wyldfyre enter, dragging huge bags of mail, all grunting.)

Arin: I just pulled my spleen.

Sora: Don't we have E-Mail here?!

Wyldfyre: BURN IT ALLLLLLLL!!!!!

Arin: What even is all this? Has Mr. Frohicky not been paying the bills? (Sighs.) Again?

Kai: Okay, no, it's, uh... not bills. It's fan mail! Must've been undeliverable when we were scattered across the lands.

Lloyd: Okay, well, let me get the other Ninja to read them with us.

Wyldfyre: Well, except Jay, right?

Arin: W-why not Jay?

Wyldfyre: Are you saying Jay has fans?!

Kai: We ALL do! My fan mail often comes in red envelopes like this one! "Dear Red Ninja, you are soooooooo incredibly awesome!" (Chuckles) We have the smartest fans.

Wyldfyre: Ugh

Kai: "But I like your hair better with a bone thing in it. Is it true you were raised by dragons..."

Wyldfyre: What?! Let me see that! This is for me! You're right, our fans are brilliant. But this one is addressed to you, Kai! "Dearest Kai, your last poem was the best yet! I love you sooo much, and I'm sorry my mission is taking me so far from-

Kai: NO NO NO NO

Wyldfyre: -for so long

Kai: Stop! Stop it! Give me that! Give me that!

Wyldfyre: You wrote- You write love poems?! Who is this Skylor person?!

Kai: HAHAHAHAHA. Hey, Lloyd! This one's for you. Take it. Read it. NOW.

Lloyd: Okay, okay! Okay, um... "Dear Lloyd, since the Merge, I've invited you over for... tea dozens of times, but you never make it. I know you're busy but I bet all your friends are wondering if I disappeared in the Merge or something. Call me. Love, mom." Huh.

Arin: Lloyd, you... really should stay in better touch with your mom.

Sora: Hey, he might have a good reason not to talk to her.

Lloyd: Uh, no. Arin's right. I should call her more often. My mom's great! Heheh.

Kai: Lloyd's totally over that whole thing where she put him in that evil school while she went off on her expeditions.

Arin: She did what now?

Sora: Told 'ya.

Lloyd: I'm- I'm- Moving on! Arin, here.

Arin: There's really mail for me? ...Okay! "Dear Arin, you're the one and only, very best Ninja in the world!" Aww, who is this? "Which is why I'm offering you 10% off a limited selection of items in Kreel's Junkyard. Sincerely, your best friend, Kreel."

Lloyd: Ah.

Arin: Ohhhh... Wait, look, she sent one to each of us! I mean, I think. They're addressed to... Boyd, Zang, Nip, Jojo, and Coleslaw.

Sora: Hey, a non-Kreel one for me! "Dearest Sora, I hope you are having a good day." Aww, that's so nice. "Kidding. No, I don't. You're seriously the worst. "Technology?" Is that even a real element? Plus, you don't have a color. Some white, some blue, some pink. Pick a lane! I can't believe they let you on the team when there are so many other better-trained candidates. Wishing you the worst, signed"... Does anyone know a "Dareth?"

Lloyd: (Sighs) ...Yeeeah. And trust me, he gets stuff wrong sometimes. Like that.

Kai: Look, it's easy to write mean messages when you're not out there every day putting in the work. You can't take mail like that seriously.

Wyldfyre: I take all my mail very seriously. See how many Os are in the "sooooooooo incredibly awesome" here?

Lloyd: Look, the younger Ninja aren't used to this, but you have to take all fan mail with a grain of salt. Don't let the mean ones get under your skin and don't let the nice ones go to your head.

Wyldfyre: Uh, this fan letter is 100% in my head.

Lloyd: Okay.

Sora: But I've gotta admit, kinda stings... This guy thinks I'm the worst Ninja on the team!

Wyldfyre: (Snorts) He clearly hasn't heard that Jay's back.

Arin: At least you two got letters and not just a coupon.

Kai: No, 10% off at Kreel's is pretty sweet, though. I mean- Yeah, I feel bad, bud.

Lloyd: Look, can I read you all one more letter? I got it long ago, but... Sometimes I carry it as a reminder.

Sora: Sure.

Lloyd: (Wu's voice) Dear nephew, I remember the day you were born. Your parents loved you the moment they set eyes on you. As did I. Your father said to me: "I feel he has the power to be a better man than I have ever been." Not long after, the evil planted inside him grew. And, tragically, he lost his battle with it. I know my brother has said some terrible things to you. Things that come from an evil place. But his words were only lies and hate. It can be hard to hear the harsh things from strangers, let alone from those who are closest to us, that should be closest to us. But do your best to shut out the hate. Positivity, love, and light. These are the things a Ninja seeks out. I love you nephew. Uncle Wu.

Lloyd: I... try to keep his words in mind.

Sora: Wow. Thanks for sharing that. Guess it's normal to have self-doubts.

Lloyd: Well, we all have them, Sora. Believe me.

Wyldfyre: Yeah, you all do. I wonder what that would be like!

Kai: Listen, I got a idea. From now on, whenever we get a mean letter, Wyldfyre uses them as target practice.

Wyldfyre: YES!

Lloyd: YEAH!

(All cheer in agreement)
