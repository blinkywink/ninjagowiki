Master Class is the first short film from the Tales from the Monastery of Spinjitzu mini-series based around the ninja and Master Wu, taking place sometime before the tenth season of Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu.

Wu is the focus character of this episode.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

Ninja sharpens ninja! Master Wu makes it clear to the ninja that just because they’re Golden now, there’s more to Spinjitzu than spinning, kicking and whipping. Like trying not tripping over their own feet, for one thing! And knowing your worst enemy – how to turn their strength into yours and make them your friend. Can the ninja remember the keys to unlocking the full power of Spinjitzu?[1]

Plot

Plot[]

Master Wu gives the ninja a review in Spinjitzu, teaching them balance conquering fear, focus, and teamwork. In the process of learning the lesson in teamwork, the ninja spill the painter's bowl of golden paint, which in turn covers Master Wu and Kai. Wu then says they are golden and concludes the lessons.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the only episode of Tales from the Monastery of Spinjitzu to not feature any antagonists and to take place in the present day for its entirety.
  • This episode is modeled after 70670 Monastery of Spinjitzu, though Wyplash, who is present in the set, does not appear in the episode.

Continuity[]

  • Master Wu holds an in-universe version of The Book of Spinjitzu.
  • This is the first time multiple characters that know Spinjitzu attack each other while in the tornado form.

Errors[]

  • In the scene of Wu throwing the fruits in slow motion, some more fruits appear out of nowhere.
  • Cole is somehow able to cut some fruit with his hammer even though hammers are unable to cut objects, unlike the other ninja's weapons.
    • Jay is also able to cut fruit with his nunchucks even though there are no sharp ends.
  • Despite paint splattering everywhere, only Wu and Kai are shown to be covered in paint, while the others weren't even stained.
  • Cole, and Lloyd are shown to laughing at Wu and Kai after they're covered in paint, but for whatever reason there's no sound.
