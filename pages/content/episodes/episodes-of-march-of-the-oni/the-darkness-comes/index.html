Overview

— Garmadon to the ninja

The Darkness Comes is the first episode of the tenth season of Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu and the 95th episode overall. It aired in New Zealand on January 19, 2019, April 6, 2019 in Canada, and on April 19, 2019, in the United States, with the rest of Season 10 as a film.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

As NINJAGO City rebuilds, Faith arrives with a dire warning that the Oni have returned and are destroying the realms, one by one. NINJAGO must prepare, and Lloyd faces the possibility that to defeat this foe, he may need the help of another enemy.[1]

Extended: Lord Garmadon tells Lloyd that the "Bringers of Doom" are coming and Lloyd is going to need his help if the world is to survive. Meanwhile, the Commissioner of NINJAGO City presents the ninja with a new Destiny's Bounty, rebuilt from the original blueprints. A battered Faith suddenly appears riding Firstboune. She warns that a darkness has spread over the realm of Oni and Dragon and Master Wu deduces that the Oni are responsible. Back in NINJAGO City, the Oni use the Realm Crystal to enter the NINJAGO world. A black cloud spreads over the city, frightening the populace.[2]

Plot

Plot[]

Noble shows Lloyd to Garmadon's wing, stating it was built just for him. After unlocking the entrance, he runs off, leaving Lloyd to confront Garmadon by himself. When he reminds Garmadon that he had requested to see Lloyd, Garmadon claimed that it was unusual. When Lloyd threatened to leave, Garmadon remembered and told him that the Bringers of Doom were coming and that although he didn't care, he would rather not be destroyed along with Lloyd. He said that he had decided to help the ninja defeat them and that he was the only one powerful enough to do so. Lloyd, knowing his father could no longer be trusted, angrily rejects the offer and storms off, but Garmadon assured him that he will come back.

Meanwhile, the citizens rebuild Ninjago City while Cole, Zane, and P.I.X.A.L. wait in traffic, complaining that they're thirty minutes late. They wanted to use the Earth Driller to dig under the road, but it was new, so the trio decided to get out of the Earth Driller and run to their destination. Once they arrived, the Police Commissioner gave a speech and presented them with a rebuilt Destiny's Bounty as a reward for saving Ninjago.

At the Monastery, Jay is practicing how to ask Nya to be his Yang with Kai. Kai tells him to be confident, but Jay says it doesn't sound right. Suddenly, Dareth bursts in and asks them what's happening. Kai explains it to him and tells him to keep it a secret. Nya comes in and tells them that Wu is in the courtyard looking at the new Mural. They went outside to join him, witnessing Firstbourne crashing near the base of the mountains. They went down to check on her, but she opened her claws and let Faith fall out. Faith informed them that "Darkness was coming" and they took her inside of the Monastery to heal. The rest of the ninjas arrive. Wu said that both Faith and Firstbourne would heal and continued explaining what Faith had told him.

A cloud of darkness had overtaken her home realm, and whoever touched it had been petrified. Lloyd told the others what Garmadon had said. Wu showed them his history book containing a picture of an Oni. He said that his father had called them the "Bringers of Doom." The ninja head out to warn the city.

Meanwhile, in Borg Tower, a watchman explains to his partner that artifacts from Hiroshi's Labyrinth had been relocated to the tower because it has better security. Unbeknownst to them, darkness escaped from the Realm Crystal and started to engulf the tower. The two escaped and wheeled Borg out while the other citizens evacuated.

The ninja arrived at the Bounty and admired it. When they tried to fly, they realized that the thrust lever was wired backward. After Zane fixed the label, they took off.

The Commissioner was working on a model boat, but an officer came in and told him that there was trouble in Ninjago. In the city, the dark clouds started petrifying everyone in its path. Zane claimed that they were too late and the ninja sped off to rescue the people. The Commissioner and cops tried to stop the darkness, but without proper weapons, they had to back away. Fortunately, the ninja arrived in time to save them and helped them up to the Bounty. The ninja's Elemental Powers had no effect on the darkness, even when they used them together, and they also had to evacuate. Cole was grabbed by one of the tentacles, but Lloyd saved him. They saved more citizens, and Cole explained what the tentacle felt like. He said that he saw something inside the cloud that looked like an Oni, so the ninja went back to Kryptarium to get Garmadon. Without another choice, they release him from prison.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the third episode with a name including the word "Darkness", the others being "Darkness Shall Rise" and "Island of Darkness" in Season 2.
  • This is the third season premiere to recount the previous episode.
    • The others are Season 2 and Season 9, the episodes being "Darkness Shall Rise" and "Firstbourne" respectively.
  • The words on Garmadon's magazine reads "Villain of the Year 2ime", the "2ime" possibly meaning "two-time". It also features Garmadon's face, presumably meaning that he won "Villain of the Year" twice.
  • This is the first season premiere to have an intro sequence since "The Surge".
  • In the book that Wu pulls out, the Omega is shown having a staff that bears a notably similar design to Borg Tower.
  • When March of the Oni aired in the US as a film, Faith's explanation about the Darkness from "Endings" is included here, as the dark cloud consumes Ninjago City.
  • This marks the thirteenth time the Wilhelm scream sound effect is used after "Home," "The Rise of the Great Devourer," "The Last Hope," "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master," "The Art of the Silent Fist," "The Greatest Fear of All," "Operation Heavy Metal: Buffmillion," "Operation Heavy Metal: Blunck," "A Line in the Sand," and "Out of the Fire and Into the Boiling Sea," "The Weekend Drill," and "Blue Lightning" by a civilian falling into the darkness.

Continuity[]

  • Kryptarium Prison built a special cell for Garmadon, naming it Cell One. It is the most heavily protected cell in the prison.
  • The magazine Garmadon is reading in his cell translates to "Time," which is likely a reference to the real-life magazine of the same name.
  • At the beginning of the episode, a scene appears in which Mystake's Tea Shop is being rebuilt with an inscription from Steeper Wisdom and a large teapot logo from Steep Wisdom.
  • The Destiny's Bounty was rebuilt from the blueprints that Rufus McCallister mysteriously obtained.
  • Jay's underwear can be seen on the tree in the room when Jay talks to Kai about his future Yin-Yang promise to Nya. This calls back to "The Oni and the Dragon" when Harumi found Jay's underwear left on the Destiny's Bounty.
  • It is unknown how there is a mural of the Sky Pirates and Nadakhan when the events of Skybound were erased from existence.
    • When the ninja are observing the Skybound mural, Kai and Dareth look at each other, confused.
    • Tommy Andreasen gave a few rhetorical questions on X to imply some possible explanation for the existence of the Skybound mural:[3]
      • Who wrote the 'Book of Spinjitzu'?
      • When was it written?
      • What information is in it?
      • How could he know?
  • This episode marks the first time Faith enters Ninjago.
  • It is revealed that those touched by the darkness become frozen in place like statues.
  • The Realm Crystal, along with other artifacts, were transferred from Hiroshi's Labyrinth Stronghold to the basement of Borg Industries because the tower had better security.
  • Cyrus Borg, Faith, Vinny, and the Omega appear in this episode but don't speak.
    • This is the first physical appearance of Cyrus Borg since "Lost in Time."
  • When the Commissioner tells the Ninjago Police to draw their weapons, an officer says "We only have tasers, remember?" This is a reference to a similar scene in "Dread on Arrival."
  • This episode marks the final time Cole creates Earth out of nothing.

Errors[]

  • When Cole got into the car, he opened both rear doors. When he leaves, both rear doors seem to be closed.
  • At the beginning of the episode, the monks carry the monastery door up the stairs, even though it was already there in Tales from the Monastery of Spinjitzu.
  • At the Monastery, the picture showing the ninja, Nya and Wu from the second half of the Season 2 intro in the Relaxation Room is mirrored.
  • When Wu admires the Monastery Mural, his staff shortens.[4] This error was previously seen in the first episode of the series.[5]
  • Despite the enormous height of the mountains, when Firstbourne and Faith came to Ninjago and fell under the mountains, Wu and the ninja run down the stairs surprisingly fast.
  • In the last shot of the ninja on the Bounty, Zane's hair is misaligned with his head.[6]
  • In the credits, Garmadon is listed as Emperor Garmadon though he is no longer an emperor, and Alan Marriott is misspelled "Alan Marriot." Tourist Dad is also misspelled "Turist Dad."
