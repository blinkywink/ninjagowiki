The Invitation is the first episode of the fourth season of Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu and the 35th episode overall. It first aired on February 23, 2015, in the United States on Cartoon Network.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

When the Ninja receive an invitation to partake in the Tournament of Elements and a clue that Zane might be alive, they accept and travel to a Lost Island owned by notorious crime lord Master Chen in order to save their friend.[1]

Plot

Plot[]

A group of Nindroids patrol a jungle, unaware that Lloyd is watching them from behind some yellow flowers. Suddenly, the Green Ninja takes action, defeating the robots one by one. He then enters a cave and dodges some lasers, before using his Spinjitzu to destroy some more Nindroids. Lloyd hangs a rope by his ribs, and travels down to Borg Industries, attempting to obtain armor. It is revealed to be a test, to which Wu states that he requested for all the ninja, not just Lloyd. Lloyd attempts to explain the situation, though Borg understands that Zane's death affected all of them, revealing that he hasn't heard from P.I.X.A.L. since the memorial. Lloyd then decides to get the team back together.

Jay now hosts his own T.V. show to find a new hero for Ninjago. He introduces the player as Cathy; a returning contestant and asks why she came back after losing the last time she competed. She states, "Because ninja never quit!" and starts jumping through obstacles. However, she fails but is proud to have gotten farther than the previous time. The game is over; Jay goes to his dressing room. Unbeknownst to the Master of Lightning, Lloyd appears after sneaking in. He tells Jay to visit Wu and consider rejoining the team. Jay declines, due to his hatred against Cole for Nya's feelings towards him and Lloyd disappears, while telling him to visit him if he changes his mind.

Cole is shown to have become a lumberjack. Out of nowhere, the Green Ninja appears again. Lloyd tries to convince Cole to get on the team, but Cole refuses, being sick of fighting Serpentine, Nindroids, and Jay. Meanwhile, a huge chunk of wood rolls towards the other lumberjacks, so Cole makes rocks to stop it, revealing to the other lumberjacks that he is indeed the Ninja of Earth. Lloyd leaves, also telling Cole to visit him if he changes his mind.

Kai is now wrestling in underground Slither Pits as the Red Shogun and defeats Kruncha before taking a break. As he sips some juice, Lloyd arrives and asks Kai to return. Like his friends, Kai refuses, saying Lloyd doesn't need a team and wishing it was him who gave his life instead of Zane. Lloyd reminds him he always wants to be the one, using his obsession with becoming the Green Ninja as an example before telling him to think about someone other than himself. Lloyd goes away, telling Kai the same thing he did to his other former teammates: visit him if he changes his mind.

Kai goes to the park to see the statue of the Titanium Ninja (now covered in moss and bird poop) as well as the Falcon perched on its shoulder before deciding to take up Lloyd's offer. However, Kai isn't the only one to meet the Green Ninja at Chen's Noodle House, as Jay and Cole had also been convinced to meet Lloyd there too. The Master of Fire reaches for sushi but Lloyd stops him, telling him to explain his plan first. Lloyd informs the ninja of his new plan: hiring a new ninja. The others completely dislike the idea, saying Zane is irreplaceable and Kai states there may not be a team without Zane. They quickly get interrupted by a thug and his accomplices robbing the register. Fighting the thugs and easily defeating them, Lloyd asks how they could walk away from this kind of action. The gang immediately runs out the back, where the ninja follow only to see the three have disappeared. The foursome soon sees a drawn picture of Zane attached to a shrine. They also see a plate of fortune cookies, each with an invitation to a Tournament of Elements hosted by Master Chen, the owner of the Noodle House. They're surprised to see the invites saying that Zane is still alive and will be freed if they participate and win. They decide to accept and avoid telling anybody due to the invite's recommendations.

At Garmadon's monastery, The former lord sees blueprints for the Destiny's Bounty and questions Nya if she plans to rebuild the ship. She says yes and asks him to see if Lloyd wants to join in. In his room, the Green Ninja finishes packing and comes close to taking his weapons when his father comes in and asks him where he's going. Lloyd lies and tells him that he and the others are going fishing to strengthen their team bonding and questions if there are other Elemental Masters, which Garmadon becomes suspicious of. Lloyd tells him to ignore the question and leaves; Garmadon soon sees that Lloyd left his fishing poles behind and upon seeing a takeout box of Chen's Noodle House, he realizes that Lloyd had lied to him.

At the docks, the ninja see the other Elemental Masters while realizing their suits give them away. Lloyd soon approaches and explains what happened with his father. A ferry soon arrives, and everyone begins boarding when a woman in orange looks back at Kai, causing him to ignore everything Lloyd says. A man soon stops the ninja, telling them that as masters of Spinjitzu, they'll have a favorable advantage before throwing Jay's nunchucks into the sea. They soon begin boarding the ship when Garmadon comes and tries to stop them, warning them that Master Chen is a dangerous man. Lloyd retorts by saying that if there's any chance of the ninja being a whole team again they have to get on the ship and find Zane. Against the better judgment of the man, Clouse, Chen's second-in-command, Garmadon accompanies the ninja on board, where they meet the other Elemental Masters.

On deck, Lloyd questions Garmadon about his knowing Clouse while Kai questions him about the other elements. Garmadon explains that there were things they didn't intend for them to know as they were led to believe they were special, yet never question where or how they got their powers. He then states everyone on board is a descendant of an original Elemental Master, guardians to the First Spinjitzu Master. Kai then realizes the same thing might go for his sister Nya, though he states she can't do what he can. Garmadon states that might not be the case, as he says power can only be awoken when needed most. He goes on, saying the fighters serve no master, yet unlocked their True Potentials. He uses a pale stranger turning himself invisible with the power of Light as one example, then uses Griffin Turner, Master of Speed as another. Kai questions him about the hooded girl, though Garmadon says he doesn't recognize everyone; he then warns the ninja they will gun for them as they fight with honor, which means nothing where they're going. The girl is soon being pulled by a brute wearing gauntlets, annoying Kai to take action. The brute, introducing himself as Karlof, states he only wants her cloak, though Kai begins to insult him and his gauntlets. Despite the girl and Garmadon telling him to ease up, Kai continues insulting Karlof until he punches Kai across the deck. Jay, Cole, and Lloyd attempt to intervene, but Garmadon tells them Kai needs to be the one to end what he started. Kai soon gets back on his feet and starts shooting fireballs at Karlof, however, he blocks them all with his gauntlets while also revealing his element: Metal. The two brawl all over the ship until Karlof pins Kai down; just before he finishes it, Clouse breaks them up. The girl apologizes to Kai and helps him up; unbeknownst to him, strange energy flows from his arm and into hers.

Clouse announces that they've reached Chen's Island, then calls Chen, telling him that Garmadon has returned. Garmadon states he swore to never return and while the ninja wonder where they will find Zane, Chen checks up on a prisoner being kept under lock and key. As Chen says he holds all the cards, Zane, in his new Titanium Ninja form, is shown awakening in his cell.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the first episode to air in 2015.
  • The ninja wearing different outfits in this episode symbolizes the division in the team after the loss of Zane, and that they're unable to function properly/unite without him.[2] This was director Trylle Vilstrup's idea.[3]
  • Jay Vincent and Michael Kramer have released twelve known pieces of music used in season 4 of Ninjago. Of the twelve tracks, seven of them are from this episode, one is from "Only One Can Remain," another from "Ninja Roll," and three are from "The Corridor of Elders."
  • The Anacondrai Cultists are the first villainous faction in the series to be humans. Previously, the ninja have fought the Skulkin (who were undead humans), the Serpentine, Stone Army, and Nindroids. Before this season, the only human antagonists in the series were Lord Garmadon, Lloyd Garmadon (who was briefly an antagonist), and the original crew of the Destiny's Bounty.
  • The episode's air date was a constant debate[citation needed] until it finally aired on February 23, 2015.
  • The Weekend Whip is heard when Lloyd is packing.
  • In one scene in the place where Kai fights as "The Red Shogun," Dan and Kevin Hageman can be seen.
  • The song that plays when Eyezor enters the Noodle House is a parody of Bad to the Bone by George Thorogood and the Destroyers.
  • Jay's alternate hairstyle from "Once Bitten, Twice Shy" (originally Gilderoy Lockhart's hair from LEGO Harry Potter) is seen again when he is the host for Most Ultimate Extreme Ninja Challenge Ever.
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, Cole's fortune cookie said "BURP!!!"[4]
    • As such, the full message from Chen would be "Master Chen has personally invited you to participate in his Tournament of Elements. Secrecy is of the utmost importance. Tell no one, or suffer the consequences. If you ever want to see your friend again, meet on the pier at midnight and leave your weapons behind. BURP!!!"
  • The ninja's designs in this episode draw from various minifigures from other LEGO themes:
    • Jay's TV host torso is based on Mr. Gold's from Minifigures Series 10.
    • Cole's lumberjack torso is based on the Motorcycle Mechanic's from Minifigures Series 10, and his belt design is taken from the 2010 variant of Argus Filch from Harry Potter.
    • Kai's wrestler torso and mask are based on Super Wrestler's from Minifigures Series 1.
  • This is the first episode of the entire series that doesn't have an intro, excluding the pilot episodes.
    • This episode also started a cycle of 10-episode seasons up until Season 10.
    • It also started a trend of season premieres not having an intro sequence that was broken by "The Darkness Comes," continued by "Wasted True Potential," and then broken again by "The Blood Moon".

Continuity[]

  • This episode marks Chen, Clouse, multiple elemental masters, and the Anacondrai Cultists' first appearances.
  • This is the first episode where the Serpentine are seen living peacefully in Ninjago alongside its human inhabitants.
  • In this episode, Lloyd wears his Techno robes despite previously upgrading to his Stone Armor design.
  • Lloyd is shown to be pretty good at disappearing and reappearing unnoticed when convincing Jay, Cole, and Kai to get back together.
  • Lloyd remarks Kai thinking that he should have been the Green Ninja and told him to stop being selfish.
  • Kai unknowingly foreshadows Nya's future as the Water Ninja when he tells Garmadon "She can't do what I can" when discussing the Elemental Masters and Garmadon replies with "Can't she?".
  • The picture of Zane is similar to his picture from the Season 2 intro.
  • Jay's boss from "Darkness Shall Rise" is seen as the bartender at the Yang Tavern.
  • Garmadon tells the other ninja not to get involved in Kai and Karlof's battle, saying, "Kai started this. He can finish it." This is similar to when Kai told the other ninja not to help Wu battle Samukai in "Weapons of Destiny," saying, "This is Sensei's fight."
  • Zane's fate revealed in this episode mirrors Dr. Julien's original fate revealed in "The Last Voyage." In both cases, the character returned from his apparent death and subsequently imprisoned on on a remote island (in Dr. Julien's case, he was brought back by Resurrectea and was taken by Samukai to the lighthouse prison).
  • Garmadon is the first Sensei who has a new outfit in the series. Sensei Wu has had on the same outfit for the entire series up until "Winds of Change".
  • This is the second Ninjago episode where Zane does not have a speaking role, with first being "Way of the Ninja," although he does have a brief cameo at the end in both episodes.
  • Garmadon reveals there are other Elemental Masters besides the ninja, all of which inherited their powers from the protectors of the First Spinjitzu Master, with newly revealed elements being: Smoke, Light, Gravity, Nature, Mind, Metal, Form, Sound, Speed, Amber, and Poison, with Shadow becoming an official element.
    • He also states and implies that Nya has an Elemental Power that can be unlocked.
  • Skylor is introduced, as is Zane's Titanium form.
  • This is the first episode since "Wrong Place, Wrong Time" not to feature the Overlord.
  • The ninja physically appear with previous suits from past seasons:
    • Cole wears his DX gi which was last seen in "Rise of the Snakes." For some reason, the earth dragon and boulders on his suit are now golden and reflective.
    • Jay wears his ZX gi which was last seen in "Island of Darkness."
    • Kai wears his trainee gi which was last seen in present-time in "The Snake King," and overall since "Wrong Place, Wrong Time."
    • Lloyd wears his Techno gi which was last seen in "Codename: Arcturus."
  • The photo from the Yellow II poster seen at Chan's Theater in "Codename: Arcturus" is seen in Jay's dressing room.

Errors[]

  • When Jay says "Ninja never quit, huh Zane?" his lips are not moving in his reflection of the picture.
  • In a shot of Yang Tavern, No-Eyed Pete appears twice.[5]
    • A civilian corrupted by Dark Matter can be seen in the same scene despite Lloyd defeating the Overlord in "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master" which resulted in the cure of everyone affected by it.[6]
  • An animation overlook resulted in Kruncha and Nuckal's models being switched.
    • It's possible this error was referenced because 10 years after this episode aired, in the Ninjago: Dragons Rising episode "Casket of Bones" Arin can be seen placing Nuckal's head onto Kruncha's body before they swapped heads. That could be a subtle callback to this error.
  • When Kruncha says "You said to go down in the first" his helmet clips through his eyes.
  • When the ninja talk at the noodle house, two women in the background appear to be frozen almost every shot.
  • The noodle Eyezor lands in after Cole flips him are yellow when earlier they were purple and white.
    • Also when he gets back up the noodle disappear and he somehow has sushi and shrimp on his face.
  • When Jay says "Hey!" Eyezor and the thugs leave the noodle house, he says it in Cole's voice.
  • When the fortune cookie blows up in Cole's stomach, Cole yells in Jay's voice.
  • When Kai says "I told you we shouldn't have wore them." he spoke with Jay's voice.
  • When the ninja are about to go to the ferry, the ninja's positions from front to back are Kai, Lloyd, Jay, Cole; but when Sensei Garmadon is coming onboard their positions suddenly changed to: Jay, Kai, Cole, Lloyd.
  • In some scenes, Jay and Griffin Turner's hair are orange instead of brown.
  • As the ninja read their fortune cookies, Cole couldn't help but be surprised when he learned that Chen was called Master Chen. However, when Chen's Noodle House was introduced, it was called "Master Chen's" instead of "Mr. Chen's," as said by the recording of the Chen mascot. Furthermore, Chen was only referred to as Mr. Chen by Cole when he compared it to Master Chen.
