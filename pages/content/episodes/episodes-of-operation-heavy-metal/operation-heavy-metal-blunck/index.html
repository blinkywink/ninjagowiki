Overview

— Blunck

Operation Heavy Metal: Blunck is the third short film from the Operation Heavy Metal mini-series. It aired on LEGO's YouTube channel on May 23, 2017.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

他是一個兇惡戰士，但當他懷疑別人在自己背後議論紛紛，或是不讓他為野外行動任務取名時，就會覺得缺乏安全感。雖然他非常害怕馬至將軍General Machia，但他仍會忍不住在當值中偷偷溜走，然後吃個不停

English translation: He is a fierce fighter, but when he suspects that others are talking about him behind his back, or he is not allowed to name his field mission, he feels insecure. Although he is very afraid of General Machia, he still can't help but sneak away while on duty, and then eat nonstop.

Plot

Plot[]

Blunck leads a platoon of Vermillion soldiers to Ed & Edna's Scrap N Junk to steal lots of metal. Determined to be Machia's favorite after completing his mission, Blunck enters the scrapyard first, being recorded by a surveillance camera unknowingly, and sets of several traps to prevent him from stealing anything. His Vermillion platoon then charge through and steal metal, and Machia reports to Blunck on the urgency of requiring more metal. Blunck then accidentally sets off yet another trap, and is squashed by two large tires.
