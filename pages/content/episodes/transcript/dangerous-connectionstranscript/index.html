(At the Blacksmith shop, Kai and Nya are playing while Jay is hiding behind a pillar. Ray, Wu, and Edna discuss about the peculiar medallion.)

Past Ray: Well, I thought if anyone knew what this thing was, it'd be you.

Past Kai: Yes!

Past Edna: It was buried with the pile of junk.

(Wu examines the medallion.)

Past Wu: This must be an old depiction of the Realms. Interesting.

Past Ray: What do those lines between the Realms mean?

Past Wu: I believe they show that all the Realms are linked?

Past Ray: Are the Realms linked?

Past Wu: According to legend, they all share a connection. Some stories even suggest this connection between the Realms could be exploited. Something could pull or force the realms to collide together.

Past Edna: And I imagine that would pretty much junk up all the Realms?

Past Wu: Pretty much, but legend also has it, that if a location in each land was somehow the same-- identical-- the Realms could collide yet be balanced in unity.

Past Ray: Sounds like horse pucky to me.

Past Nya: (to Jay) Hey, want to join? (grabs Jay's shoulder and shakes it.)

(back in the present, the Ninja are fighting the Elemental Monsters at the Abyss of Eternal Night.)

Nya: Jay, come on! Come on! Wake up, Jay! Wake up!

(Jay wakes up.)

Lloyd: The Weapon is corrupting you, Zilvar! Destroy it!

Zilvar: This Weapon can help bring back my tribe, everything I've lost, and you will not stop me, even if that means I have to obliterate every last one of you! (He activates the Weapon of Desolation to summon more Elemental Monsters.)

(The Loyalists escort Sora in Cryptor's lab.)

Loyalist 1: Don't get any ideas, ninja.

Sora: So, uh, what's with your boss? Weird time to take a nap, don'tcha think?

Loyalist 2: General Cryptor doesn't owe nap explanations to anyone.

Sora: Hey, quiet down. Great. Now you woke him up.

(The Loyalists look behind to see Cryptor, still deactivated. Sora performs Spinjitzu to knock them down.)

Sora: I lowkey can't believe that worked.

(Sora walks up to the machine and shuts it down, freeing Zeatrix.)

Zeatrix: Where am I?

Sora: I'm rescuing you. Come on.

(They walk to the elevator to escape. The Loyalists get up.)

Loyalist 1: Cryptor said to keep you alive, but you just proved that's a bad idea. (The Loyalists aim their weapons.) I'm sure he'll understand.

(At the Cloud factory, the Nindroid MechDragon powers up.)

Wyldfyre: Oh, no, it's powering up!

Euphrasia: Oh, this is bad.

Nindroid MechDragon: Yes, for you. (It activates its weapons and fires at Wyldfyre, Euphrasia, and Riyu.) You may have forced our hand, but you will not stop us! (Riyu fights against it.)

(P.I.X.A.L. projects a message.)

Euphrasia: That thing attacked you when you tracked it down? Have you been hunting Cryptor this whole time? Now we're here, so maybe we have a chance, right, Wyldfyre? Uh... Wyldfyre?

(Wyldfyre reveals herself and charges towards the Nindroid MechDragon with her mech.)

Wyldfyre: Your mech dragon is prime mediocrity. Try this, Cryp-snore! "Cryp-snore". I'm amazing.

(Sora and Zeatrix are surronded by Loyalists.)

Sora: Hey, any chance we could sort this out by arm wrestling or something?

(Arin jumps down from the elevator and performs Spinjitzu to knock the Loyalists down. Two Loyalists aim their weapons at them, but before they can fire, Frak appears behind them and knocks them down.)

Frak: Still got it! (high-fives Arin)

Sora: Arin? How'd you find us?

Arin: Your... your parents.

Sora: They accessed the comms device? But that means they are...

Arin: Yeah, I'm so sorry.

Sora: Don't have time to think about that right now. Percival's about to hold the vote on the unity agreement. We don't know where Cryptor got off to.

Frak: But looks like you stopped his plans

Sora: His plan A, but evil people always have a plan B. We got to get to the summit now.

(They go to the elevator and the doors close.)

(The ninja continue to fight against the Elemental Monsters.)

Kai: If you won't throw that thing in the Abyss-- H'yah! --we might have to throw him in with it!

Lloyd: Not an option, Kai. We've got to get it out of his hands.

(Cole and Zane fly towards Zilvar, but he uses his laser eyes to bring them down.)

(Arin, Sora, Frak, and Zeatrix arrive at the Summit of the Merged Lands.)

Percival: I realize some might still hold doubts, but before we vote.

Arin: No signs of Loyalists.

Sora: Yeah, they could be hiding anywhere. Spread out. Eyes peeled.

(Zeatrix grabs a pad, breaks it, and hides under a tray cart.)

Percival: If we work together, we can protect the rights of all people, all lands respected. Zeatrix! Perfect. Before the final vote I'd like to present the finest example of the kind of peace a united merged lands can achieve: Zeatrix Vespasian-Orus, from the royal family of Imperium, has returned not to rule, but to help.

(Riyu and Wyldfyre fight the Nindroid MechDragon)

Wyldfyre: Can you do this! (The dragon mech transforms into mech form and uses its sword.) Didn't think so!

(The Nindroid MechDragon fends off Wyldfyre and Riyu. It then shifts its attention to Euphrasia and P.I.X.A.L., eating them and roaring.)

Nindroid MechDragon: It is time. (It flies off, leaving Wyldfyre and Riyu behind)

(Zeatrix is having a speech at the Summit of the Merged Lands.)

Zeatrix: Representatives of the Merged Lands. You believe you're here-- to speak for the needs of your people, but in reality--

Sora: (She checks the pad under the tray cart and notices something.) Oh, no..

Zeatrix: You are here for the needs of one person.

Beatrix (via Zeatrix's body): Me! All hail the Good Empress!

Percival: (whispers) Beatrix...

Sora: The mind transfer worked.

(The waiters throw their trays away and reveal themselves as undercover Loyalists with Imperium swords. They begin to capture all of the delegates.)

Beatrix: Your eyes do not deceive you! I have returned and I declare myself empress of all the merged lands!

Percival: We will not stand for this! (The Loyalists grab him.)

Beatrix: You do not have a choice. (The Nindroid MechDragon arrives at the Old Town Courthouse and break open the roof, while Beatrix laughs maniacally and the Loyalists attack.) You will bow before me, or become my prisoners! Your choice!

Bone Regent: We will never bend before you!

Beatrix: The choice is made then!

(The Cocoons arrive through the roof. Arin and Sora perform Spinjitzu to attack the Loyalists.)

Arin: Are we technically to blame for this? Cuz we brought her back here. and didn't ask for her ID?

Sora: Not the time for blame, but look, a whole plan given to Beatrix, to read when she woke up. Endgame of Operation Mentax.

Arin: Who did all this? Cryptor?

Sora: Can't be. Someone rebuilt him!

Arin: Then, who's behind--? Ah!

(The Acona delegates attack the Nindroid MechDragon, but it eats them. Akita transforms into her wolf form and attacks the Loyalists.)

Frak: H'yah! (fights against the Loyalists)

Nindroid MechDragon: I should have done away with you when I had the chance!

(Wyldfyre arrives with her mech dragon.)

Wyldfyre: Round 2, Cryp-snore! Ha! Still funny!

(At the Abyss of Eternal Night, Zilvar attacks the Ninja.)

Lloyd: Wait for my signal!

Jay: What signal?

(Lloyd fights past the Elemental Monsters, then attacks Zilvar and manages to knock the weapon out of his hands.)

Lloyd: Jay! Now! (Jay uses lightning to knock the Weapon of Desolation into the Abyss of Eternal Night.)

Jay: Yes! Eat that, Weapon!

(Suddenly, The Weapon of Desolation begins to levitate and possesses Zilvar.)

Weapon of Desolation (through Zilvar): Fools... The mortal I possess has its limitations. But did you really believe you could defeat a Primordial Artifact of Dawn by throwing me into a pit? Bah! (summons more Elemental Monsters.)

(The Lees get restrained by Cocoons. Akita tries to get them off, but is also restrained. Arin and Sora try to fight the Loyalists.)

Sora: Arin... H'ah! If this is it, I'm so sorry. I never should have turned my back on my real family.

Arin: Together forever, right? (The Loyalists grab him.)

Sora: Arin! get off him! (The Loyalists grabs her too, and the Cocoons approach.)

Weapon of Desolation (through Zilvar): You cannot destroy me, for I draw power from everything, from the very Sources themselves.

Kai: H'ah! Hope someone's got ideas, cause me? H'yah! All outta them. Zilch!

Nya: And this thing is only getting more powerful. Not expected!

Lloyd: It draws power from everything! (He remembers the things he learned about his power during the Ritual of Unrooting.) I can stabilize Elemental Powers.

Kai: Sure, Lloyd. I can set the record roasting marshmallows! But let's focus on the monsters!

Nya: Focus on the Weapon!

Kai: Hello! Touching that thing will make the monsters worse!

Lloyd: Maybe not for the elemental master who stabilizes powers. Cover me!

Kai: Lloyd, You can't touch it!

Lloyd: Trust me! I can handle it! (whispers) I hope. (He flies in and manages to grab onto the Weapon) It's working... (The Weapon of Desolation powers down and all the Elemental Monsters are pulled into it.)

(Loyalists hold Arin and Sora as the cocoons approach. Sora suddenly gets her powers back and uses them to destroy all the cocoons and hit the Loyalists with the debris.)

Arin: (breaks free from the Loyalists) How?

Sora: No idea. Is Lloyd back?

(Frak gets his powers back and attacks the Loyalists. The Nindroid MechDragon blasts at Wyldfyre as she is holding one of its wings and gets her powers back.)

Wyldfyre: Oh, yes! This just stopped being a fair fight, clown! (blasts the Nindroid MechDragon's wing apart.)

Euphrasia: (uses her powers from inside the Nindroid MechDragon's chest to free herself, along with P.I.X.A.L. and the Acona delegates.) Ninja... go!

(Loyalists attack them as Euphrasia lands. The Acona shoot a Cocoon. Wyldfyre continues to fight the Nindroid MechDragon.)

Wyldfyre: (gasps as Riyu strike down the Nindroid MechDragon's second wing) Whoo-hoo!

(The Nindroid MechDragon falls down in defeat. The Cocoons shut down.)

Beatrix: This isn't over, ninja! Huah! Oof!

Sora: All hail the Good Empress.

(The fight ends and the Loyalists fall back.)

(Lloyd finally shuts down the Weapon of Desolation.)

Kai: Never doubt the Green Ninja, right?

Nya: How did you know that would work?

Lloyd: I didn't. But I learned that the power of the green ninja is to form connection between. Wait... (He starts glowing.) Oh no.

Jay: What? What!

Lloyd: I feel-- something's happening-- (screams)

(Lloyd suddenly bursts into a cloud of dust.)

Kai: Lloyd! No!

(The Weapon of Desolation falls into the Abyss of the Eternal Night. The ninja lose their Draconus Forms.)

Jay: Lloyd! No... This can't be happening.

Kai: He was never afraid to risk everything. Finally caught up to him.

Nya: After he saved us all.

Zilvar: W-What happened? Where are my people? They were just here.

Kai: You... don't you dare--

Jay: He was possessed, Kai! He didn't know what he was doing.

Zilvar: Possessed...? The Weapon... What have I done?

Cole: We never should have made you carry it, but it's destroyed now.

Zilvar: The Green Ninja... is gone?

Kai: Yes.

Jay: No! He can't be gone. I just got my memories of you all back. (He realizes his powers have stabilized.) Look! Powers! It must be Lloyd.

Nya: Or this was his final gift to us.

Zane: Ever since the Temple of the Green Ninja, Lloyd had a new energy, a different energy. (He looks at Lloyd's golden sword.) I cannot feel that energy anymore.

(At the Summit, the delegates asses the damage.)

Percival: The biggest disaster in the history of summits and the history of disasters. The dream of a unity agreement is nothing but dust.

Bone Regent: I came here with no intention of agreeing to surface dweller nonsense. But this attack, seeing the ninja risk their mortal lives to save others, I now see the value of unity. The Underworld is ready to unite for the common defense! Who else will join?

(Everyone raises their hands and agrees.)

Pontifex Magicus: Well, it looks like we all will. Yes! Hear hear!

(Everyone cheers and Percival smiles.)

(Arin and Frak hold Beatrix while Sora activates the machine in Cryptor's lab.)

Beatrix (via Zeatrix's body): (grunts) You will not defeat me! No! (The machine activates and swaps her mind back into her original body.) No!

Zeatrix: Oh-- (gasps) I hate my sister so much!

Arin: Zeatrix? Did it work?

Zeatrix: (groans) What do you think? Her mind is back in that body.

Sora: I sure hope so.

Zeatrix: Borg has to heal her... so she's aware of every moment spent rotting in Imperium Prison!

Arin: Yeah... you're definitely Zeatrix.

(The ninja arrive at the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms.)

Zilvar: Ninja, you are too quick to forgive. What I have taken from you...

Nya: It wasn't your fault.

Jay: And now that we know Ras came to you...? He's a master at manipulation. Trust me.

Zilvar: No, the shame is mine alone. I will make up for my actions, if such a thing is possible. Farewell, ninja.

(The ninja leave as Jay turns around to see Zilvar leaving.)

(Arin, Frak, and Sora return to the Monastery of Spinjitzu.)

Arin: When it mattered, they did activate the comms so we could find you.

Sora: Don't defend them. They used me. They-- (She holds a letter from her parents.) "Sora, by the time you find this, you must know that, yes, we did come to the Monastery to hide from authorities... (Sora's parents enter a bus in the Crossroads.) But, spending time with you, the ninja, Arin's parents, showed us there is another way. We made mistakes, we're confused... ...but one thing we're not confused about is what an impressive young woman you are. We should have realized that when you were little. Goodbye, Sora. We do love you, even if we're not worth loving back." (She sheds tears from the letter.)

(Arin and Sora confront Dr. LaRow in Imperium Prison.)

Dr. LaRow: Surprised you figured it out.

Arin: We also found the Loyalist guard here who was sneaking out your instructions.

(In the prison corridors, two guards are leading Johnny Courageous towards a cell.)

Sora: Directing the Loyalists, Cryptor, Operation Mentax? Takes a lot of intelligence, but how are you not smart enough to know Beatrix is dangerous and uncontrollable?

Dr. LaRow: I can manipulate, orchestrate, but not inspire, not like Beatrix.

Sora: She didn't inspire. She demanded.

Dr. LaRow: True power can only be held by a few. Your "unity" gives power to everyone. Spreading it too thin, weakening it. The United Lands will fall under the pressure. I promise.

Sora: You're wrong. We're stronger together, not weaker.

(Arin and Sora leave the cell.)

(The door opens, revealing the original Ninja, who have returned.)

Frak: They're back! (laughs) They're back!

Sora: Hey! We have so much to tell you!

(The original Ninja look saddened.)

Wyldfyre: What is it? What's wrong?

Arin: Where's Lloyd?

Percival: Lloyd Garmadon was a hero, a leader who inspired all of us to achieve the peace we have today. And that is why not only is the first order of business of the United Lands to honor the Legendary Green Ninja, but to honor those that carry on his ideals. Never has our world been safer and never has the future been brighter than today. Thank you, Ninja.

(Everyone applauds as the Ninja bow.)

(In the courtyard, Jay remains at the Monastery of Spinjitzu beneath Master Wu's portrait. A portrait of Lloyd now sits beside it.)

Jay: It's not that I don't think you're worth a statue, Lloyd. It's that I'm not going to a memorial for someone I'm certain is still alive. I'm sure you understand (Lloyd in his "ghost" form appears) What? (He follows Lloyd, who leads him to Master Wu's study, revealing a secret compartment. He opens the compartment, revealing slots where the Icon of the Source Dragon of Motion and the Realm medallion used to be stored.) The thing from the blacksmith shop...

(At the Monastery of the Gates, Wu remains trapped in the Protective container with the Realm medallion now sitting atop it. Ras emerges from one of the portal gates, carrying the Icon of the Source Dragon of Energy.)

Ras: (to Wu) You may think your underlings scored a victory, old man, but it was a mere skirmish. True victory is closer than ever. My master will be triumphant!

(Ras walks out of the Monastery of the Gates and into the Land of Eternal Dawn. He raises the Icon of the Source Dragon of Energy and roars while looking toward the Shadow Dojo.)

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
