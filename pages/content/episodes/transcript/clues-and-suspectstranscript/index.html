(In a flashback, the crowd is cheering for Lloyd, who is standing on a large platform wielding a hammer. Zeatrix suddenly jumps into the air behind him wielding a sword while yelling.)

Past Sora: Lloyd, look out!

(Lloyd turns around too late to avoid Zeatrix's attack and is hit by the sword, which shatters into pieces. He falls off the platform.)

(In the present, Lloyd is laying unconscious on a bed in the ninja's quarters surrounded by the other ninja and Geo.)

Zane: The medical bots assured us Lloyd's condition is stable, but he shows no signs he will wake up from this coma.

Sora: He has to wake up! We can't do this without him. Whatever fears he had about being our Master in the games, I-

(Roby knocks on the wall with the Elemental Cup. He walks in along with Bleckt and Zeatrix, the latter of whom is in chains and surrounded by four Security Bots.)

Roby: Oh, I thought Lloyd would be, uh... Sorry. We came to do the official transfer of elemental powers.

Bleckt: Lloyd is technically the victor of that competition.

Roby: But I guess we can wait until he's... better. Good news though, we expelled that pain-in-the-butt Zeatrix!

Zeatrix: I can hear you.

Roby: And not just from this tournament, but from all future tournaments.

Sora: That's it? After what she did? Come on, Roby, you can do better than that.

Wyldfyre: Or we can do worse.

Zeatrix: Listen to me. I- I was so caught up on winning, in proving my worth, that I blanked out. I just acted out of rage.

Arin: Rage?

(Roby looks at Zeatrix, and then lifts his hand to his face to think. Arin looks at the cufflink on Roby's sleeve.)

Wyldfyre: Was that supposed to be an apology? I don't know what your Caregiver bot taught you, but mine always said apologies should have the words "I'm sorry" in them. (She approaches Zeatrix.) You know what should be done to you, Creep-atrix? You should be... tied up in front of like, a really delicious melon that you'll never be able to reach. No, no, you should be poked with a million sticks while playing song you hate on a loop, forever!

Zeatrix: (She uses the power of Shockwave to create a burst of energy around herself, knocking Wyldfyre back.) You don't know who you're messing with!

(The ninja and Geo get ready to fight. Wyldfyre growls.)

Roby: (He gets between them and Zeatrix) Stop! There's no fighting outside the arena! I keep telling you this!

(Zeatrix fires a shockwave at the ninja from behind Roby.)

Arin: Ah! Tell that to her!

Roby: (He quickly uses the Element Cup to drain Zeatrix's powers.) Sorry, Zeatrix. Lose your fight, lose your powers. Them's the rules.

(The Security Bots move in to take Zeatrix away.)

Zeatrix: Ah!

Roby: I'm sorry about that. If Lloyd wakes up-

Sora: When Lloyd wakes up.

Roby: When Lloyd wakes up, I'll transfer all the powers to him. He earned them. (He and Bleckt leave.)

Arin: (He grabs Wyldfyre.) Wyldfyre, you saw Roby was still wearing those cufflinks I told you about?

Wyldfyre: Yeah. They look so fire.

Arin: No! I- I mean, sure, they are stylish, but that's not the point. Those cufflinks have the same design that was on the wall from where someone sent those attacker bots.

Wyldfyre: Roby can't be a suspect. Did you how he just saved me? Us.

Arin: Just because someone acts nice doesn't mean they aren't capable of turning on you. (He looks at Sora.)

Wyldfyre: But this is Roby. (She shakes Arin.) What about Zeatrix? Or Ras? They're way badder!

Arin: They're still suspects, sure, but the more I think about it, the more Roby could easily be behind the attack on the Matriarch, and the attacks on the other players.

Wyldfyre: I think you're the wrongest kind of wrong, but if this means I get to prove the stang-zoot Roby's innocence, then sign me up.

Sora: (She approaches them.) I can help too, Arin.

Arin: Thanks, but no, thanks. I need ninja I can trust. (He exits the building with Wyldfyre, leaving Sora behind.)

(In a flashback, a young Arin is playing a detective game with his parents.)

Past Barry: Remember, Dr. Arin, when trying to solve a mystery, trust no one. Everyone is a suspect until proven innocent. Including... your mother!

Past Jenny: (Laughs and drops her cards.) You got me.

(All three of them start laughing.)

(In the present, Arin and Wyldfyre come out from behind a pile of crates and barrels on Foundation Street.)

Wyldfyre: You sure you don't want to go to the Games Plaza to see Sora fight?

Arin: (He crawls on the ground.) Very sure. (He does a somersault and stands up.) Now's the only time we know Roby and Bleckt won't be around their office. (He suddenly gasps and pulls Wyldfyre aside.)

(Two Security Bots fly over them while communicating with strange noises. Arin and Wyldfyre manage to hide from them.)

Arin: We don't have much time 'til the game is over, so we gotta hurry.

(The two of the arrive at the door to the office.)

Wyldfyre: (She tries to open the door.) Well, we tried. Guess you'll have to trust me that Roby's innocent. (She starts walking away, but Arin grabs her.)

Arin: Would an innocent man lock his office door?

Wyldfyre: Yeah. Normal people lock doors. I was raised by dragons and even I know that.

Arin: Hmm...

(In another part of the City of Temples, Sora is training relentlessly.)

Sora: (She jumps into the air, does a kick, lands back down and punches while grunting.) Ugh! Lloyd was trying to teach me Spinjitzu, but I just don't know what's holding me back. There's no chance I'm making it past the next level of the tournament without him. Yah! (Sighs.)

Roby (from the announcement system): And for our next match, she'll wreck you with tech...ew, and now a bunch of other cool powers- Sora! (The crowd cheers.) Versus the Quake Snake ready to break- the competition! Frak! (The crowd cheers again.)

(Both make their way to the arena. Sora waves at Jordana, who looks away.)

Cole: Something's up with... What's her name?

Sora: Jordana. Yeah. One minute she's asking me for help, the next, she's pretending she doesn't know me. Given how I ignored her when we were in school, I wouldn't want to know me either. Maybe her and Arin can become friends. Friends that hate me. (She continues walking to the arena.)

(The floor of the arena sinks down, and a large floating platform appears in its place, followed by two smaller platforms which Sora and Frak use to get to the large platform.)

Roby: First game today is a spectacular, multi-platform fight! (Sora jumps onto the large platform. She breathes in an lifts up her mask.) You know the rules, first to fall, first to lose! (Frak also hops onto the platform. He bows at Sora, who bows back.) Is everybody ready? (The crowd cheers with excitement.)

(A bell is rung, marking the start of the fight.)

(Frak and Sora run at eachother. Frak blasts Sora with Ice, she dodges it by jumping up and fires back with Heat. Frak also dodges her attack by jumping to the side. Sora fires two more blasts, which Frak runs away from before blasting Sora with Ice again. Sora ducks under the first blast and jumps while avoiding another and retaliating with a blast of Heat that barely misses Frak. She then fires a beam of Heat that Frak jumps over. While in the air Frak uses Ice to blast Sora twice, but Sora dodges these attacks with a series of flips.)

Sora: Uh! (She jumps onto the smaller platform behind her, which detaches from the larger platform. Frak responds by doing the same thing.)

(The two continue their battle in the air, using the elements of Ice and Heat to fire blasts at eachother while dodging by rapidly moving their platforms around. Frak finally gets a few hits onto Sora's platform, which slows down and starts to shake.)

Sora (to herself): Use your balance. (She starts using the element of Balance to stay on the platform.) Whoa! (She regains her balance, and starts to blast at Frak again with Heat.)

(The two continue exchanging blasts until Frak gets a clear hit at Sora's platform with Ice, which causes it to start emitting smoke and falling. Frak then backs away and hits the platform from below with Earth, knocking Sora off. He then dives after her. Sora falls but is able to use Balance again to land smoothly onto the large platform, followed by Frak who she jumps away from.)

Sora: (She jumps back towards Frak and the two of them start to fistfight.) What's someone (She hits Frak away.) like you doing (She fires a blast of Heat towards Frak.) fighting for Ras?

Frak: You ninja (He dodges the blast.) got him all wrong! Sure, he's strict, but he taught me lots. (He fires a beam of Ice at Sora.) Hah!

(Sora respond by firing a beam of Heat, and the two attacks collide, knocking both combatants back and creating a huge cloud of steam over the platform. Frak looks at the steam in the arena, trying to spot Sora. Sora emerges from the cloud using the elemental power of Smoke, and kicks Frak off the platform before using Balance to barely keep herself on it. Frak grabs onto a panel on the edge of the platform and thinks about his next move. He looks over to Ras, who nods while looking back. Frak nods back at Ras and leaps back into the arena while kicking away Sora, who retreats with a backflip. Frak pulls out a Wolf Mask, and Sora gasps whe she sees it. Frak pauses to think while looking at the Wolf Mask.)

(In a flashback, Arin talks to Frak about the Wolf Masks.)

Past Arin: Those masks will make you more powerful, but they corrupt you, poison you. Ras only cares about power, no matter what the cost.

(In the present, Frak is holding the Wolf Mask while thinking. He looks away from it, and then looks back at Ras.)

Ras (angrily): Do it! Do not disappoint me!

(Frak looks back at Sora, then decides to trow the Wolf Mask away and gets ready to continue fighting. Sora is surprised by this. Ras lets out a disappointed growl. Frak charges at Sora, and the two of them resume their fight. Frak kicks Sora, and Sora responds by punching Frak. Frak tries to kick Sora again, but Sora ducks underneath the kick.)

Sora: (She kicks back at Frak.) Hi-yah!

Frak: Ohh! (He falls to the ground before getting back up and firing an Ice blast at Sora.)

(Sora dodges the blast with a backflip, and gets ready to use heat. Frak slams down and uses Quake, causing the platform to shake and launching Sora into the air, but Sora fires two blasts of Heat while airborne, which is enough to knock off Frak. Sora lands on the opposite edge of the platform.)

Frak: (He falls down.) Aah!

Sora (exhausted): Ugh.

Roby: Sora is the winner!

(The crowd cheers, Frak uses the power of Earth to get out of the pit below the arena and bows at Roby, who approaches him while holding the Elemental Cup. Roby then uses the cup to drain Frak's powers. He them approaches Sora, who bows at him, and gives Frak's powers to her. Sora then uses Earth to create two large rocks, which she uses to hop down from the platform.)

Frak: You outplayed me. Congratulations. (He bows at her.)

Sora: You were a worthy opponent. (She bows back.)

Zane: Congratulations, Sora!

Cole: You have more powers, Sora!

(Sora look toward Ras' team, and sees Frak approaching Ras while looking down while the rest of his team look at him disappointed. Cole puts his hand of Sora's shoulder.)

Ras: You have failed me!

Frak: It was a good fight. A fair fight.

Ras: I will not suffer disobedience! I offered you certain victory, (He picks Frak up.) and you refused! (He trows Frak behind him.)

Frak: Ugh.

Ras: I never want you in my sight again. (He and the rest of the Wolf Clan walk away.)

(Jordana sneaks towards Sora.)

Sora: What? Jordana? Oh, hey.

(Jordana hands Sora a folded note before walking off.)

Sora: (She opens the note.) Huh? An Imperium math equation? What does this mean? (She, Cole and Zane look at where Jordana left, confused.)

(Back at the door to Roby and Bleckt's office, Arin tries picking the lock with a fork while Wyldfyre looks out to the street. She then notices Roby and Bleckt approaching.)

Wyldfyre: Uh- (She runs to the street in a panic.)

(Arin hides the fork in a flower pot and follows Wyldfyre.)

Wyldfyre (to Roby): Hey, heh, I was just looking for you! By your office! But of course you were at the games. That makes way more sense. Anyway, I just wanted to tell you that... (She looks at Arin, who shakes his head.) you're funny and cute and I like that! (Roby Smiles happily at her while Bleckt scoffs.) And you're a snazzy dresser! Those cufflinks are fantastic! Can I see them? (She grabs Roby's arm and pulls him towards her.)

Roby: Sure! I think they're kinda basic, TBH, but dad used to wear them so Uncle Bleckt thought I should wear them for my first Tournament.

Wyldfyre: Aww.

Roby: Right? (Bleckt walks to the door and unlocks it.) Who'd have thought Bleckt'd be such a softie? He gave them to me right before the first game, like for good luck. As if I needed it. (He winks at Wyldfyre before entering the office with Bleckt, who loudly closes the door on the two ninja.)

Wyldfyre: So, Roby got those cufflinks right before the first game- the morning after the bot attack. Told ya he was too cool and good-looking to have left the mark!

(In another flashback, young Arin is playing the same detective game with his parents again.)

Past Jenny: You have to trust the evidence, Arin. Even if it goes against what you believe. (Barry plays a card.) Even if your dad is being a real sneak. (She reveals one of the cards on her side of the table, and Barry throws his cards into the air while Arin laughs.)

(In the present, Arin reconsiders the situation.)

Arin: Yeah, you're-

Wyldfyre: Right! I'm right! Feels good! Yeah!

(The door of the office opens again as Roby and Bleckt exit.)

Roby: (He chuckles.) We interrupting something?

Wyldfyre: Uh, just... feeling good. Not because of you or anything. (She nervously chuckles.) But not not because of you! You're great! We're fine! No big deal! Bye! (She starts walking towards the door of the office.)

Roby: (He chuckles.) That's the wrong way. (Wyldfyre awkwardly stops and turns around.) Um... (He clears his throat.) ...it was good to see you too, Wyfy. (He clicks his tongue.)

(Wyldfyre looks exitedly at Roby and makes a squeaking noise.)

Arin: And the elemental master of Heat has zero chill.

Bleckt: Why aren't you at the games? (He points at the pair with his cane.) Even if you don't support your teammates, (Arin notices the symbol in Roby's cufflinks is also present at the end of Bleckt's cane.) have some respect for the tournament.

(Arin directs Wyldfyre's attention to the cane.)

Wyldfyre: (She starts to gasp, but masks it with a fake cough.) Yeah, good point, okay! (Bleckt begins to leave. Arin jams the office door by quickly throwing the book he's carrying.) Bye, now! (She and Arin wave at Bleckt while he leaves.) Way to go, Sneaky Boots!

(The two ninja sneakily enter the office and close the door behind them.)

Arin: Detective Arin and Dr. Wyldfyre are on the case! (He walks to a desk with an exercise ball next to it.) Hmm. (He jumps onto the exercise ball, only for it to slip away from underneath him and bounce against a wall before hitting the floor again. He swiftly gets up from the floor before Wyldfyre, who is examining another desk, sees him.) You finding anything?

Wyldfyre: Nothing. What were you expecting? A notebook that says, "I'm guilty"? The entrance to a secret lair?

Arin: No? Yes.

Wyldfyre: And what's this? (She picks up an old rotary phone. The desk suddenly slides to the right, revealing a hidden staircase. She gasps in panic and drops the phone on the floor.) I didn't break it! You broke it!

Arin: Well, well, well! The entrance to a secret lair! (He laughs.)

Wyldfyre: For the record, I'm the one that found it.

Arin: (Picks the phone back up.) It was smart of Bleckt to hide the room's access in this old thing. No way Roby was gonna touch a rotary phone. (He hands the phone to Wyldfyre.)

Wyldfyre: I still don't know what this is. (She places the phone back on the table.)

(Arin walks into the secret lair, followed by Wyldfyre. There the two find many vandalized pictures and other objects that express Bleckt's hatred towards Roby.)

Wyldfyre: (She gasps upon seeing the pictures.) Who would do this to his beautiful, beautiful face?

Arin: So, I'm getting the feeling that Bleckt doesn't like Roby. That sounds like a motive.

(Wyldfyre finds a doll resembling Roby. It's head falls off after she picks it up.)

Wyldfyre: Ah! Can we go now? This room is gonna give me nightmares forever.

Arin: How is this all tied to the Matriarch horns? We need evidence.

Wyldfyre: (She notices a calendar on the wall while picking up the doll's head.) Is that evidence?

Arin: (He looks at the markings on the calendar.) "Meet with Ras."

Wyldfyre: Sounds like evidence.

Arin: But why would Bleckt meet with Ras the day before the Tournament? And if it was a meeting to commit a crime, which it obviously was, why write about it?

Wyldfyre: He is pretty fussy with his boring stuff, like ledgers and rules.

Arin: We need more answers.

(In the ninja's quarters, Lloyd is still laying on his bed while barely moving. Cole and Geo are looking after him while Arin and Wyldfyre enter the room. Sora is looking at the math equation Jordana gave her.)

Sora: (She notices the two enter.) Arin! I've been waiting for you. (Arin walks past while ignoring her.) (She walks in front of him.) I need your help deciphering this note Jordana gave me.

Arin: Who's Jordana? Oh, that girl from Imperium? Doesn't she hate you?

Sora: Something weird is definitely going on with her. And this is an Imperium math problem. Maybe it's a clue or something? You're the best at solving mysteries.

Arin: I guess...

Sora: I figured out the math... for all the good it did me. It spells out the words "Crown, Mono, Stereo." What could that mean?

Arin: Crown... mono... stereo... Crown... uh, hat... head? Horns? Horns! Maybe the dragon horns? Then, mono... one? Stereo... around... hmm. Mono... stereo... monostereo... Monastery? Horns in the Monastery?

Sora: Is Jordana trying to tell me the dragon horns are in the Monastery?

Arin: Maybe? But why would they be back in the Monastery?

Cole: Not in our Monastery. I think I know which one they're in. But are you sure we trust what's-her-name?

Sora: Not sure we have a choice.

(Meanwhile, Lloyd is having another one of his visions. He is walking through an empty void while the Blood Moon can be seen in the background. Suddenly six Wolf Mask Claw Warriors emerge from the ground, surrounding him. Arin also appears, looking very angry. Zeatrix comes in from behind Lloyd and hits him with the sword, causing him to fall to the floor looking hurt. Suddenly, a large tail appears and knocks away Two of the Wolf Mask Claw Warriors. The Source Dragon of Motion appears from the mist behind Zeatrix, causing her, Arin, and the remaining warriors to flee. The Source Dragon of Motion then flies toward the Blood Moon and fires a beam of energy at it, causing it to explode. The Source Dragon of Motion lands before Lloyd

Lloyd: Motion Source Dragon... thank you.

Source Dragon of Motion: Your visions are from exposure to Source Dragon Energy. I can ward them off, but they will be back. Being a conduit, as you are, is a grave responsibility.

Lloyd: And a responsibility I have failed. I've lost in the Tournament.

Source Dragon of Motion: A Source Dragon like myself would not have come to you if I did not believe you could do this.

Lloyd: But we still haven't discovered who's behind the attack on the Arc Dragons, or who's threatening you.

Source Dragon of Motion: Which is exactly why you must get back in the games, no matter what. Lloyd, you must win!

(The vision end and Lloyd wakes up in his bed, gasping for air. He finds himself alone in the ninja's quarters.)

(At the games Plaza, the arena for the next round rises up from the ground. It consists of a medium sized platform, along with several smaller ones, which include three regular platforms, three platforms with flamethrowers in them, and four pairs of platforms connected to each other using large poles. there is also a crane to the edge of the arena with a handle attached to it.)

Roby: (He flies in with a small floating platform holding his phone on a selfie stick.) Good afternoon, everybody! (The crowd cheers at him.) "Wooh" is right! The next game is gonna be a spicy one. First up, he's mysterious and his handshakes really hurt... Nokt! Versus... he's lean, green, and definitely not mean, Lloyd! (Someone gasps, and the crowd starts muttering.)

Sora(angrily): What is Roby trying to pull? He knows Lloyd is still in a coma.

Roby: (He looks at Wyldfyre, who turns to look away. He then sighs.) The tournament must go on. (The crowd mutters again, and Roby lets out another sigh.) With Lloyd not attending, I'm afraid I must call the fight. And the victory goes to-

Sora: (She looks around and spots Lloyd.) Lloyd!

(Lloyd appears, and the crowd starts cheering again. Ras and his team are angry about this development.)

Sora: Lloyd! Come on, Lloyd! You can do it!

Wyldfyre: Woo-woo-woo!

Sora: Yes!

(Elsewhere, Cole and Arin are walking on a mountain.)

Arin: Where are we go- (He sees the Monastery of Gates with four glowing orbs around it.) Whoa...

Cole: Looks like the orb that led me here has made some friends. If you're right about the Imperium girl's clue, this has gotta be the place.

Arin: But how are we gonna get over there? Our dragons were sent home. And you don't have your elemental powers anymore to make a bridge.

Cole: Thanks for the reminder.

Arin: And Sora's too busy fighting to help us out by building us a ship or something.

Cole: Hey, I could come up with a million ideas that won't work, too, like... Okay, I'm bad at improv but let's focus on trying to come up with a plan that might work. Like- Ooh! I've got an idea! Wait right here.

Arin: Okay...

(Meanwhile, Nokt and Lloyd get into position for their fight.)

Roby: What an unexpected comeback! Lloyd is back in the Tournament! (The crowd cheers.) This is so much cooler than anything I could've planned. (to Bleckt.) I'm not even mad you wouldn't let me use killer jellyfish anymore.

Sora: Lloyd! I'm so glad you're here, but you sure you should be here?

Lloyd: I can still fight. I have to.

Roby: (He floats to Lloyd with the Element Cup.) And here are the powers that are rightfully yours! (He uses the Cup to give Lloyd the powers he drained from Zeatrix earlier.)

(Bleckt dings the bell with his cane, marking the start of the match. Nokt puts his mask on and rushes towards Lloyd.)

Zane: Lloyd, look out!

(Nokt jumps onto a small platform, then leaps over one of the poles to the main platform and fires two blasts of water at Lloyd.)

Lloyd: (He puts on his mask and jumps to the side to avoid the blasts.) Yah! (He lands one one of the small platforms and struggles to balance for a moment.) Ah... (He grunts while grabbing his right leg.) (Nokt fires a lightning blast at Lloyd, which he dodges.) Ah!

(Lloyd then jumps away and uses wind to float upward and reach the crane on the side of the arena. He uses it to swing upward and performs a Spinjitzu tornado while jumping up. While in the air he then fires two blasts of life at Nokt (He dodges them and responds with another blast of lightning.)

Lloyd: (He dodges the blast by jumping to the main platform from the pole he landed on.) Uhn! (He makes a shockwave upon landing to attack back!)

Nokt: (He uses lightning to block the shockwave.) Ah! (The crowd cheers. He stomps his foot on the platform twice to activate Shatterspin.)

Source Dragon of Motion (In Lloyd's mind.): Lloyd, you must win!

(Nokt sends a wave of Shatterspin energy at Lloyd. Lloyd jumps over it and uses Rising Dragon to attack Nokt, but is unable to break throug his Shatterspin and is launched back.)

Lloyd: Ugh! (he gets up to continue fighting.)

Bleckt: (Rings the bell with his cane again.) Stop the fight!

(The crowd gasps.)

Lloyd: (He pulls down his mask.) What?! Why?

Bleckt: You are clearly (He pulls out his ledger.) an injured combatant! According to ancient rulings, the Source Dragons, in their desire for fairness, do not permit injured fighters.

Roby: Oh. Wow. Well, if it's in the rule book, I guess...

Lloyd: But you saw me. I could do it! I'm just a little sore, no big deal!

Roby: I... I don't have a choice, Lloyd. Even though that match was awesome! Your health is the most important thing. You did not look well this morning, and you still don't, to be honest. Sorry.

Bleckt: And the victor by default is... Nokt!

(Nokt laughs silently. The ninja gasp. Ras smirks at Bleckt, who slightly nods.)

(Elsewhere, Cole sneaks past a Security bot watering a plant.)

Cole: (He climbs into the mech he saw earlier.) Well, hello there.

(Back at the Games Plaza, Roby drains Lloyds powers into the Element Cup and gives them to Nokt. Nokt uses the element of life to make his hand glow green for a moment before turning away.)

Roby: I'm glad you're awake, Lloyd. I hope... I hope you'll be okay.

(Lloyd walks past the other ninja present.)

Zane: The odds were not in our favor. You did your best.

Lloyd: It wasn't good enough. I've failed us. I've failed all of us!

Sora: No, you haven't. I'm still in the games, I just need you to train me. (Lloyd smiles.)

(Meanwhile, Cole arrives at the cliff opposite the Monastery of Gates in the mech he took earlier.)

Arin: Cole, is that you? (The mech opens up to reveal Cole's head.) But that's an elemental earth mech, you don't have your powers. How can you get it to work?

Cole: I'm pretty sure this mech was my mother's. I can feel something in it, some connection. So, maybe if I focus...

(A brief flashback of Lilly appears. Cole closes the mech back up and powers it on. The six blades on the mech's back spread out and start glowing. Cole jumps foward with the mech and uses it to pull up. Rocks start floating up from the bottom of the chasm, and Cole uses the mech's powers to form them into a bridge to the Monastery of Gates.)

Arin: All right! Let's go! (He grabs onto the mech's leg, and Cole begins to walk on the bridge.)

(When the two arrive at the Monastery, the floating orbs circle around them before combining into one, which takes on the shape of Master Wu.)

Cole: Master Wu?

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
