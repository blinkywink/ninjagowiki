Clues and Suspects

(In the Nether-Space, Kai and Bonzle hide and watch the Forbidden Five.)

Bonzle: Any clue what they're saying?

Kai: How would I know? I can't read lips.

Bonzle: They look upset. Why?

Kai: Again, how would I--

Bonzle: Where are they going?

Kai: They're not going a... Oh wait! They are.

(The Forbidden Five walk away.)

Kai: Nice! Let's give this another shot.

(Kai and Bonzle jump down from their hiding spot.)

(In the City of Temples, Lloyd opens the door to the ninja's quarters.)

Lloyd: Let's go everyone.

(The ninja rise, and Cole yawns.)

Lloyd: You too, Wyldfyre. Time for warm-ups.

Wyldfyre: (She groans.) I lost my Heat power. I've literally got nothing to warm up with!

Lloyd: There's more to being a ninja than Elemental Powers.

Wyldfyre: Yeah, like sleeping in. Don't ninja sleep in?

Lloyd: You need to focus on learning Spinjitzu.

(Wyldfyre grumbles and walks out, and Riyu yawns. Lloyd walks towards Nya, who is packing.)

Lloyd: I know you'll find Jay, Nya.

Nya: I have to. Even if he doesn't remember anything about us, Jay belongs with us. His family. But I'm scared, Lloyd. He shattered his goodness. What if he can't heal it?

Lloyd: There has to be a way. And we'll all join you as soon as the tournament ends and we find out whoever's behind the attacks on the dragons.

Nya: Be careful. (They stand up and hug)

Lloyd: You too. We'll miss you.

(Lloyd leaves, and Nya sighs.)

(Nya walks up to portraits of Kai and Jay.)

Nya: I could really use my older brother right now. I'd do anything to hear your voice again, Kai.

Kai: Hello? Are you there?

Nya: Kai? (She turns around. Riyu squeals.) Riyu, did you hear him too?

(Riyu roars, and picks up an egg-shaped piece of Dragon Ivory.)

Kai: (Through the stone.) Riyu, is someone with you?

Nya: Kai! Kai, can you hear me?

(Riyu rolls the ivory towards her.)

Kai: I said, Riyu, if there's someone else there, let me know!

Nya: He can't hear me? There must be some way to--

(Riyu gives Nya a spark.)

Kai: We might not have much time.

Nya: Kai?

Kai: Nya! Nya, is that you?

Nya: You have a sister I don't know about? How are you talking through this stone?

Kai: "Stone"? I don't know. I've got some of Riyu's scales stuck to me, so Bonzle thinks the physical connection lets us communicate. Dunno why it works with you, though.

Nya: You're my brother. We share a lot more than a few scales.

Kai: The Forbidden Five, four, whatever, they're also talking to someone on the outside. Probably planning something terrible.

Nya: Great, more bad news.

Kai: Let me guess, wherever you are, the world's in danger again?

Nya: Kai, I found Jay. Only he's not our Jay. He lost his memory. It's like he's a completely different person.

Kai: In Jay's case, that might be an improvement. Ha!

Nya: Save the jokes 'till after we free you. (She picks up the rock.) Maybe this stone could do more than allow us to speak. It could potentially open a doorway out.

(The door bangs, and the connection is lost.)

Kai: Nya, Nya, are you there? What happened? (Bonzle shrugs.) Nya!

(Zane, Cole, Sora, Lloyd, and Wyldfyre walk through the Foundation Street.)

Cole: (He yawns.) It took me forever to get back to sleep. Every time I closed my eyes I saw that monastery again. Why now? Why me? Why is it here?

Zane: Perhaps rather than asking why you were guided to a mysterious monastery in the middle of the night, a better line of inquiry might be how you could reach it.

(The ninja return to their quarters.)

Sora: I need a hot bath.

Arin: You're back.

Lloyd: Arin, where've you been? You were gone again when we woke up.

Arin: I had stuff to think over.

Lloyd: Right, the detective work. Did you find something in Ras's dorm?

Arin: No. Ras caught Riyu and me.

(The other ninja gasp.)

Wyldfyre: How'd you escape? Smoke bomb? (they look at her.) I really thought being a ninja would involve way more smoke bombs.

Arin: Ras let us go.

Cole: Ras? Ras Ras? Are we talking about the same Ras here? Big scary tiger-man, hates dragons? He let you go?

Arin: Maybe we don't understand his real motivation yet.

Lloyd: (Looks at Sora, then turns to Arin) So you don't think he's behind what happened to the Matriarch Dragons?

Arin: Ras is the obvious suspect. He led the Claws of the Imperium against a Matriarch before. But nothing connects him to the stolen horns yet. And don't forget, Ras was also attacked by a bot that first night.

Zane: Our past with Ras may have created a confirmation bias that blinds us to ther threats.

Wyldfyre: Yeah, (She grabs a drawing.) threats like this ugly guy, Bleckt! Have you seen the way he talks to Roby? Total villain energy. Even his name sounds like the sound you make when you barf. Bleeckt!

Zane: Bleckt defeating three Matriarch-level dragons seems improbable.

Arin: (He snatches the drawing from Wyldfyre.) Agree. And that's a drawing of Zur.

Wyldfyre: Uh, if you say so.

Cole: The Devonian who beat Zane? Why would he be a suspect?

Sora: Well, I did hear rumors back in the Crossroads about Devonians grinding dragon horns into a powder they mix with food for strength in battle. Dragon horns can make you stronger, but also fill you with rage.

Lloyd: Rumors and gossip aren't evidence.

Cole: (He grabs another drawing.) Zeatrix has been oddly confident about winning the games. It's like she knows something we don't.

Arin: And she wasn't attacked by bots, even though she drew a lot of attention at the banquet earlier that night.

Sora: (She takes the Zeatrix drawing from Cole.) She definitely has a grudge against us for dethroning Beatrix. Even though we totally did her a favor.

Wyldfyre: (She takes the Zeatrix drawing from Sora.) Psh! She's super powerful but can't control her anger. Huh! Pathetic.

(Lloyd takes the drawing and looks at it.)

Arin: Uh, Lloyd? What do you think?

Lloyd: Well, Zeatrix was once next in line for the throne of a kingdom that ran on Dragon Power. If anyone knows what to do with those horns, it's her.

Cole: Great, we have a bunch of suspects but no proof. Now what?

Arin: I still have one lead. I found this symbol in the area where the bot attack started from. But I have no idea what it means.

Cole: Hey, you should check the Temple City Museum. Geo and I walked through there and saw all kinds of ancient art and writing.

Arin: Really? I'll go right away.

Sora: I could come with you.

(Arin pushes past her as he leaves.)

Cole: Did that feel weird to you?

Zane: My Social Encounter Algorithm registered high levels of cringe.

Sora: Lloyd, can we talk about Arin?

(Lloyd stares at the drawing for a moment, then has a vision of Zeatrix, Ras, and the sword. Sora and Zane rush to his side.)

Sora: Another vision? I thought Rontu taught you how to control them!

Lloyd: This one, so strong. I think today's the day I fight Zeatrix.

(Cole gasps.)

Lloyd: And I'm gonna lose.

(Arin arrives at the Temple City Museum and sees the Titan Mech. Inside, he walks past a scimitar, a painting of the Source Dragons, and a portrait of a past competetitor.)

Roby: (To Geo.) No, no, no. Oops, I mean, yes, and make sure you capture my broad shoulders. (He sees Arin.) Arin! Whatdaya think? I'm gonna replace these boring old artifacts with some bold modern art. New and daring, like me. The past is for dead people, right? Can I get a stang-zoot?

Arin: I don't know. History can be cool. (Roby rolls his eyes.) Hey, Geo.

(Geo waves at Arin.)

Roby: Ugh! You sound like my uncle. He spends hours here. And not asleep, like you'd think. If it were up to him, there'd be an exhibit to watch paint dry. Good thing ya boi's the Game Master and not him! I see a modern, interactive experience with screens everywhere. Over there, a holo-display of the seven Source Dragons locked in an epic battle. Stang-zoot, right?

Arin: That does sound cool. I mean, stoot-zang, or whatever.

Roby: Right? I don't believe any of those old dragon myths, but you can't beat the branding! And this'll house the Element Cup. Imagine lights hitting the glorious sheen of its Dragon Ivory.

Arin: Dragon Ivory?

Roby: Sure. the cup is made from the polished horn of an Arc dragon.

Arin: Whoa, whoa, whoa. Arc as in Matriarch?

Roby: Matriarchs, Patriarchs. Centuries ago, people used Arc dragon horns to amplify Elemental Powers. The cup's made of it, it's as old as the games themselves. Let me show you where I'll build the VR Experience-slash-gift shop.

(They walk past a painting.)

Arin: Who's that?

Roby: My great, great, great, great, you get the idea. The tournament's first games master. A visionary, like me. I'm actually wearing his vestments. Tailored for max swagger, drip-drip. Thought I'd throw my uncle a bone with my outfit, at least. (Music plays in the distance.) Oops! Gotta go! The games won't master themselves! (He picks up his selfie stick.)

(Arin holds up his book to the painting. He sees the same symbol as the one he found after the bot attacks. He gasps.)

Arin: Roby, wait!

Roby: Sorry, duty calls! (He raises his arm and Arin sees the symbol on his cufflink.)

Arin: Could the villain I've been looking for this whole time be, Roby?

(Sora walks through the city, meeting up with the rest of the ninja.)

Sora: Has anyone seen Riyu?

Cole: He probably helped Nya leave the city and got distracted by all the Temple City concessions.

(Roby flies in on his hoverboard.)

Roby: Elemental Masters, are you ready to battle? To entertain? To win? (The crowd cheers.) First up, the hard-fighting snake who's bound to make you shake. I's Frak, the Elemental Master of Quake! (Frak walks towards the arena. Lloyd sighs.) Versus a decendant of the Devonian King, who'll throw you right out of the ring, it's Zur, the Elemental Master of Reflex!

(Blue cubes rise from the arena, making a platform for the competitiors. Frak runs up to Cole.)

Frak: Excuse me, Master Cole, sir. Can I ask a question about your Earth power?

Cole: Uh, sure, I guess.

Frak: I've been practicing with it since our match, but I feel resistance from the stone I don't experience when using my Quake energy.

Cole: You're probably trying to lift. Earth's power comes from below. Instead of pulling up, reach down and push out.

Frak: Wow, I never considered how two similar powers could be so different. (He bows.) Thank you, sir! (He runs back to start his match. Ras growls angrily.)

Sora: Are you sure it's a good idea to help someone on Ras's team?

Cole: You heard what Zane said. We can't let our past with Ras color our judgment. (Frak jumps onto a cube.) I've got a good feeling about Frak. Besides, isn't he Arin's friend?

(Bleckt rings the bell to commence the match. Frak runs to the center of the arena and hits the ground with his Quake. Zur uses his Reflex to dodge.)

Roby: Frak opens strong with a Quake, but the Master of Relfex dodges with ease!

(The cube Zur is standing on turns red and starts to lower. He climbs back up.)

Roby: Uh-oh, Zur! Better look before you leap and after. (Zur summons Ice.) We don't call this game Grid Drop for nothing!

(Zur shoots a bit of ice at Frak, who dodges it and lands in a corner. A cube behind Frak turns red and lowers. Zur shoots more ice at Frak. Frak jumps away as three more cubes turn red.)

Zeatrix: If that were us up there, the battle would already be over. When I was in exile, all I had was the dream of exacting revenge on my sister. (Lloyd experiences another vision of Zeatrix and the sword.) You ninja stole that chance from me. (He see the sword shattering on the ground.) Seems only right to exact that same revenge on you. (Lloyd grunts and sees Ras laughing. He groans and Zeatrix laughs.) This is the legendary Green Ninja who dethroned my sister? Sad. (She walks away.)

(Zur uses Reflex to swerve through red cubes, and tries to punch Frak, who dodges. Zur creates an ice shield that Frak tries to punch through, eventually breaking it and causing Zur and him to stumble backwards. More red cubes appear, and Zur produces more ice and traps Frak. Unable to move, his cube starts to fall. The crowd gasps.)

Cole: Frak, reach down and push out! (Frak successfully uses Earth, propelling his cube up.) Yeah!

(Frak breaks the ice around his feet and jumps off of the cube and dodges another Ice attack. Frak uses Earth again, sending Zur flying into the stands. The crowd cheers.)

Roby: We have a winner. (Roby transfers Zur's powers to Frak, who demonstrates his new abilities. He returns to Ras's group.)

Ras: How dare you seek counsel from the enemy!

Frak: What? Master Cole? Well, it's his power, I just thought--

Ras: Master Cole? I am your Master, no other. Do you understand?

Frak: Yes, Master.

(Wyldfyre gulps down some tea, then coughs.)

Wyldfyre: Ugh! The tea's cold and I've got no powers to heat it with! (She throws the cup on the ground.) Not having Elemental Powers is just the worst! (Arin sits down beside her.) I mean, it's fine for some people. Not everyone is suited for an exciting life. Oh, hi, Arin!

Arin: Wyldfyre, what do you think of Roby?

Wyldfyre: He's alright, I guess, for a super cool genius that everyone adores. Why? did he ask about me?

Arin: Just trying to follow the facts.

(More battles commence. Lloyd fights Arccrack, who uses his element to attack his opponent, but misses and lands on a roof. In a different match, Algu uses Figment to duplicate himself. The new Master of Sound uses his powers to get rid of the clones, but Algu sneaks up on him and knocks him down. Geo and the Master of Size face off next. The Master of Size enlarges himself, to which Geo uses Fusion to turn the cubes beneath him red. Mord, the Master of Surface Tension, fights Zeatrix next. She summons bubbles to jump across, but Zeatrix uses Shockwave and elimates her. In a new match, Neuro uses Mind to figure out how to solve a puzzle, but Nokt electrocutes him. Geo and Lloyd fight next, the former using Size to try and grab the crystals faster. Lloyd uses Propusion to speed over and grab them first. Zeatrix and Algu battle, with Zeatrix using Shockwave to get rid of Algu's clones, and Wind to get rid of Algu. Chamille and Sora face off, and the latter breaks the rope beneath the Master of Form. Lloyd, Nokt, Zeatrix, and Sora gain their new powers.)

(Sora sits down next to Lloyd.)

Sora: That was--

Lloyd: A lot.

Sora: (She sighs.) At least it's over. Guess your vision about fighting Zeatrix today was wrong.

Roby: Well, it's all over for today. Or is it? Wait, what? Surprise! Ya boi planned a secret bonus game. (The crowd cheers.) Wow! That's what all your minds sound like right now! He's lean, he's green, but he's never mean. It's Lloyd, Elemental Master of Life! His opponent is a former twin, who will do anything to win. The very angry Elemental Master of Shockwave, Zeatrix!

Roby: Welcome to The Gauntlet. What-what? This game's goal is simple. Retrieve these epic hand-to-hand weapons and yeet your opponent off the platform.

Lloyd: The sword from my vision. (In a vision, the sword shatters again.) Gotta get to it before Zeatrix does.

Roby: The challengers are free to use any and all Elemental Powers, but the final blow must be executed with a weapon from The Gauntlet. Copy? Cool. Ready? Set? Go, go, go!

(The bell rings. Lloyd uses Propusion.)

Roby: Lloyd's coming out hot! Ba-bam! (Lloyd ricochets off a fan blade and nearly falls off.) Oops, maybe too strong! Looks like Lloyd hasn't mastered that Elemental Power yet! Can't all be winners! (Zeatrix uses Wind to break the fan blades. She grabs a trident.) Easy breezy. Zeatrix uses the Elemental Power of Wind. Be sure to thank Euphrasia later, Z! (Zeatrix lunges at Lloyd, who manages to dodge. He uses Spinjitzu to break the fan blades blocking a scimitar, which he grabs and fuses with Zeatrix's trident.) Elemental Fusion fused those weapons! Clever move! (The fused weapons are thrown into the crowd, which a Cragling catches.) Look out below! (Zeatrix tries to kick Lloyd, then uses Shockwave. Lloyd uses Size and shrinks himself.) Zing! Lloyd uses the Elemental Power of Size to duck Zeatrix's blast. Remember, there are no small powers, only small Masters.

(Zane, Cole, Sora, and Wyldfyre watch from below.)

Zane: Lloyd is deploying an unusual fight strategy.

Sora: Yeah, it's not fighting. It's running away.

(Above, Lloyd jumps out of Zeatrix's way and grabs a shield to block a Shockwave blast. He jumps back on the main platform, and blocks another attack, but Zeatrix goes right through him. Five more Zeatrixes appear.)

Roby: And Zeatrix pulls out the Elemental Power of Figment! She's her own party, but which one is real?

Lloyd: (He takes down all six Zeatrix clones.) Your sister Beatrix didn't need these tricks to fight me.

Zeatrix: No, she needed an army. My army!

Lloyd: Gotcha. (He uses Spinjitzu and gets rid of the Zeatrix clones, then knocks Zeatrix away with his shield. She gets knocked onto the platform with the sword.)

Roby: Ouch! Lloyd Zea-tricked her into giving up her real location! (The crowd cheers.)

Lloyd: (He sees the sword.) No! (He shoots Energy at Zeatrix, causing her to move to another platform.)

(Zeatrix grabs a spear and tries to hit Lloyd with it, but he blocks it with the shield. Zeatrix hits him and he nearly rolls off the platform, but grabs the edge and jumps back up. Zeatrix charges at him, but Lloyd disappears.)

Roby: Blink and you miss it! Lloyd pulls a Mr. Pale and goes invisible. (Zeatrix uses Shockwave, and Lloyd appears and kicks her down.) This could be it!

(Lloyd turns invisible again, and appears next to another scimitar.)

Cole: Why doesn't Lloyd knock her off?

Lloyd: I've gotta find that sword from my visions. (He turns invisible.)

Zeatrix: (She gets up.) Where did you go coward? (She uses sound to make Lloyd appear briefly.) There you are.

Roby: Wow! Zeatrix using Elemental Sound to echolocate. Folks, I've never seen and not seen anything like this! (Zeatrix summons Surface Tension and Shockwave.) Seems like Zeatrix is using the Elemental Power of Surface Tension to fill a bubble with Shockwave energy. She's making an Elemental bomb! (Zeatrix sends the bomb straight towards Lloyd, who appears and shoots it with Energy, causing it to explode and break the arena in half.) They've broken the game! In thousands of years, this has never happened before! Is my tournament wild or what? Stang-zooot! We need a short intermission while bots repair The Gauntlet from this unprecedented intensity. I encourage you to visit concessions, I hear there's some amazing pies!

(Lloyd sits with the ninja.)

Cole: You're doing great buddy.

Zane: You have already beaten the odds by getting this far.

Sora: You're losing, Lloyd, but not to her, to your own fear.

Lloyd: You're right. But how do I fight a battle I've seen myself lose?

Sora: When I faces Cinder, you told me not to make my fear a self-fufilling prophecy. And I won. Ninja don't fight because we know we'll win, we do it because some things are worth fighting for, no matter what the outcome is.

Lloyd: Maybe I should take my own advice.

(The bots finish fixing the main platform.)

Roby: Welcome back, competitors and spectators. These games would be nothing without you. Well, they'd be just me, which is still pretty amazing. But I dare say, enough about me! (Bleckt rings the bell to re-start the match. Zeatrix summons a bubble around her, using wind to propel her forward.) Zeatrix brings back the bubble! But adding some Elemental Wind to give herself the edge! (Lloyd uses Energy to pop the bubble. Zeatrix continues using Wind to get to the platform with the sword.) Whoa! I hate to burst her bubble, but Lloyd just burst her bubble!

(Zeatrix grabs the sword.)

Lloyd: No more running. (He jumps at Zeatrix, who deflects him with the sword. He grabs a mallet and begins swinging at her, and she blocks him with the sword. Lloyd enlarges himself, and knocks her off the platform with his mallet.)

Sora: Yes! (The crowd cheers wildly. Ras, Cinder, and Jordana look angry.)

Roby: Lloyd is the winner! (Zeatrix flies up behind Lloyd.) Hey, hey, hey, stop! The game's already over.

Sora: Lloyd, look out!

(Lloyd turns around, and is too late to dodge as Zeatrix drives the sword into his arm, shattering the sword and sending him flying off the platform. Ras laughs at him as he falls. Lloyd lands on the ground, and the pieces of the sword land on and around him. The ninja run over, and Zeatrix is grabbed by Security Bots. Lloyd coughs, groans, and falls unconsious.)

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
