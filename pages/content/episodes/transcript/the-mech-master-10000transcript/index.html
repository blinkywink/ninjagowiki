(In the Land of Madness, the original ninja drive the Land Bounty through rocky terrain.)

Nya: Oof! Ooh… Couldn't our journey to destroy the Weapon of Desolation have been… a little more scenic?

Lloyd: Not here for sight-seeing. Time is of the essence. Zane assures me this is the fastest way to the Never Kingdoms, Though clearly not the smoothest.

Nya: Anyone want one of Frohicky's swamp tarts? They're pretty good… if taste and flavor aren't your thing. (eats one of the Swamp Tarts and covers her mouth in disgust.)

Kai: (uses Fire to blast some of the Swamp Tarts) Woah! (a Swamp Tart lands on the windshield.) Sorry!

Lloyd: Hey! Frohicky worked hard making those!

Nya: (burp) Oh… (gulp)

Cole: Not true. I saw him pull these out of a dusty box in the bottom of his pantry. That's why they're so stale and make great targets.

Jay: Boo-ya! (uses lightning to shoot some of the swamp tarts)

(Cole and Kai laugh)

Lloyd: Huh. I really missed this. After everything we've been through-- --we still find time to laugh in the middle of saving the world. I hope the other Ninja are having as good a time back in Ninjago.

(In Ninjago City, The young ninja chase two hechmen in hover cars, and Arin, Frak, and Wyldfyre scream as they hold onto one of the cars.)

Wyldfyre: This was your brilliant plan?

Arin: It was part of my plan! Just don't let them get away with that experimental nitro fuel!

Wyldfyre: Well, this part of your plan is really stupid, Arin! (screams)

(The hover car avoids the Neon signs and obstacles.)

Wyldfyre: (screams)

Sora: Hit them from above, Riyu! We can't stop these guys in the sky! We gotta drive them to the ground!

(Riyu growls)

Euphrasia: I don't think Riyu agrees with your plan, Sora!

Wyldfyre: I have an idea!

Arin: Your powers don't really work right now, Wyldfyre.

Wyldfyre: Dang it! Out of ideas. Ah!

Frak: Weren't there three getaway cars?

Wyldfyre: Way to count, Frak.

Frak: He's using the nitro fuel in the blaster? That stuff is highly unstable and really dangerous, right?

Sora: Faster, Riyu!

Frak: Oh no!

(Frak lets go of the hover car when the blaster starts shooting at them and Arin, Frak, Wyldfyre scream as they fall.)

Euphrasia: Arin, no! Powers, please work! (She uses her powers to save Arin, Frak, and Wyldfyre.)

Frak: I'm not sure this is better.

(Euphrasia's powers stop working and she falls off of Riyu.)

Wyldfyre: (lands on one of the hover cars) Hey! want to hear my new plan?

Frak: (lands on Riyu with Sora) Ugh… I should not have eaten three breakfast burritos today.

(Euphrasia fails to use her power, but Arin catches her with his Grapple Gun.)

Arin: Going my way, madam?

Euphrasia: Yeah, I missed my bus, so this will keep me on schedule. (Arin laughs.) Oh, I'm terrible at banter.

Wyldfyre: You guys are lousy drivers.

Henchman 2: Woah!

Wyldfyre: Thanks for the lift. H'yah! H'ah! H'yah!

(Everyone screams.)

Sora: What did I tell you all? We couldn't stop them in the air. We needed to get them to the ground.

Frak: Looks like you might get your wish.

Sora: We all got to stop that crash.

Arin: No! we got to split up and get the third car. It has the nitro fuel. That stuff is super dangerous.

(Riyu growls.)

Frak: So… what are we doing?

Sora: Crash first!

Arin: Split up!

Euphrasia: Ugh, I'm getting those people out! Move it! Go!

Arin: Fine. I'll go after the fuel myself!

Wyldfyre: Aah!

Frak: I think Wyldfyre's already on it.

Sora: Arin, give me your Grapple Gun.

Arin: For what?

Sora: Just gimme it!

Frak: Did we win?

Wyldfyre: (screams and crashes down on top of a building) I'm okay!

(Riyu crashes down with two hover cars.)

(The young ninja watch a video of the events in the hangar bay.)

Citizen: Glad they're helping, but… when are the old ninja returning?

Sora: (sighs) At least no one mentioned that we let one of the thieves get away with that dangerous fuel.

Arin: But who even were they, and what did they want that fuel for? The two we caught aren't telling the police anything.

Euphrasia: Well, the hits keep coming-- "After winning over my early skepticism, the shine has worn off. These young Ninja have me wondering if they aren't just sad imitations of the originals."

Frak: Who wrote that? My dad?

Euphrasia: Intelligent George. Even he's over us.

Wyldfyre: Rrr… Beep! Rrr…! What's with the sad-sackery? All I saw was us being awesome and doing awesome stuff! I mean, none of the regular people helped out. They just stood around watching.

Frak: That's because they're regular people. We're Ninja. We're supposed to help everyone out.

Wyldfyre: Okay, guess you know everything about being a Ninja even though I've been a Ninja longer.

Arin: Look, the problem's obvious. We're not acting like a team. I had a plan, and no one followed it.

Sora: I had a plan, and you all did your own thing.

Euphrasia: Sorry, Sora. I just thought Arin's plan was better.

Frak: No, Sora is the best at strategy.

Wyldfyre: Look, a tribe is only as strong as its leader, and with Lloyd gone, we don't have one, so I volunteer.

Frak: You can't just declare yourself leader.

Wyldfyre: (puts her sunglasses on) Just did. Who wants to challenge me? Warning, I fight dirty.

Sora: You're right, Wyldfyre.

Wyldfyre: Oh, come on. I don't fight that dirty.

Sora: No, No, we need a leader… And there's only one way to choose one we all agree on. We vote.

(The young ninja ride the elevator to the Relaxation room.)

Sora: I'll grab paper to make ballots.

Kreel: About time. Almost fell asleep.

Arin: Kreel? Lobbo?

Lobbo: (sadly) Lobbo-Lobbo.

Kreel: Frohicky let us in. We saw you bungle that chase on the news, so we're here to offer you a chance to win back the public's respect.

Lobbo: That is not exactly the truth. The Crossroads needs your help, Ninja. Lobbo-Lobbo.

Kreel: We need you to enter the Mech-Master 10,000. It's not just for mechs anymore. Any vehicle can join. And the course spans into Ninjago City.

Sora: You need us to enter this? Why?

Kreel: Look, the Crossroads is having some problems. An Old Town Crime Boss has been trying to buy up property to bulldoze the whole place down--

Lobbo: Like my Arcade, which is the only thing that gives my life meaning. Plus Mikloshe and Mrs. R's stores could be crushed! Lobbo-lobbo.

Frak: Why don't you just not sell the Crossroads to this Crime Boss?

Kreel: Well, that's the real rub. I may have lost a few games of pool to this no-account villain, and he bet all the property I own he'd win this race…

Euphrasia: Don't you have the fastest racers?

Kreel: Of course, but this Crime Boss now has some illegal experimental fuel for his vehicles. Now no one can beat him.

Arin: Those were his henchmen we fought today?

Kreel: His henchmen who escaped with the only canister of fuel out there.

Sora: Who is this Crime Boss?

Kreel: William Yards.

(The young ninja burst out in laughter.)

Arin: That smiley guy on billboards who sells pool tables? How is he evil?

Lobbo: Behind his facetiously grinning exterior is a heart of molten lava… Like Lobbo's mother. Lobbo-Lobbo.

Frak: Really? That guy is a Crime Boss?

Kreel: He's good, gotta admit. Been running organized crime for years, but covers his tracks. No one ever suspects him. None of his henchmen rat him out. He's untouchable. The only people that can stop him… are my best friends. (The young ninja confused) Y-you. I'm talking about you. (The young ninja seriously)

(In William Yards Pool and Billiards.)

William Yards: That's right. Pool is fun for the whole family. And if you order from me right now, I'll donate two more tables to the orphanage of your choosing. Oh, golly gilickers. Isn't that amazing? Oh, yes. I'd be pleased as punch to do business with you. My floor manager will call you first thing tomorrow. Uh-huh. Okay. Tata for now. (Ends the telephone call) What took you so long?

Henchmen 1: Ran into the ninja. Well, some ninja. None I really seen before. Kind of goofy you want my opinion?

William Yards: I don't. (notices the fuel) Is that the fuel?

Henchmen 1: Yep. We got that race in the bag.

William Yards: Then after tomorrow, the Crossroads will be mowed right to the ground, and turned into the parking lot for the William Yards Billiards Center!

(The Mech-Master 10,000 is about to start.)

Arin: I uploaded the route and marked off possible choke points… also some straightaways we can use to break out.

Sora: Arin, we can't win by solely focusing on the race. It's more important to counter what Yards throws at us.

Arin: Speed is going to get us through.

Sora: Yards is using illegal speed. We need to be on the defense.

(Riyu groans and lifts up the back of the X-1.)

Wyldfyre: Hey, don't scratch the paint! This is Kai's, and he'll kill both of us! I'm sorry dragons aren't allowed in the race. It's ridiculous, but we need you as our eyes in the sky.

Euphrasia: You have driven before, right?

Frak: Of course. I mean, not on a road, and only in vehicles that attach to other vehicles driven by other people, but we'll be fine

(One of Yard's henchmen pulls up in a small car and injects the nitro fuel into it. The lights count down, then the race begins. The audience cheers.)

Sora: Wow I've missed this.

Arin: Me too.

Lobbo: Lobbo-Lobbo! (Lobbo's mech gets knocked away, but Riyu catches it and puts it back on the race track.) Lobbo-Lobbo…

(Frak and Euphrasia's vehicle drifts, and crashes into another racer.)

Euphrasia: Oh, sorry. Oh, didn't mean to.

(The vehicle rams into another mech knocking it away. The track leads a bridge, then into Ninjago City roads.)

Arin: Tight turn up ahead!

Sora: Doesn't matter. Yards's men are gaining. Deploy the bumpers!

(The bumpers are deploy as the mechs and cars slow down.)

Arin: They're slowing! Sora, the turn! (Sora gasps and tries to turn, but the mech crashes into the walls.) What did I tell you?

Sora: We can still catch up!

(The mech gets up. After the turn, mechs and cars pick up the pace again.)

Wyldfyre: Hey, nice ride! Remember me? (The X-1 drives beside the henchman's vehicle, but the henchman speeds up, overtaking the X-1) Rude. One Turbo Boost will show him what I think about that. (She presses a button, and the X-1 releases a motor bike.) What? Whoa-ho! Kai, you have the coolest stuff! Whoa!

(The motorbike overtakes vehicles, including Frak's and Euphrasia vehicle.)

Euphrasia: Can this thing go any faster?

Frak: I hope not.

Wyldfyre: Wha-ho!

(The motor bike overtakes a mech, which falls onto the ground. Euphrasia presses a button that boosts the vehicle up over the fallen mech.)

Euphrasia: So sorry!

(The SA Mark 1 is attacked on both sides by two mechs.)

Sora: Ugh, Arin, the bumpers!

(Arin activates the bumpers and they are able to push away the two mechs. The Henchman's vehicle approaches behind them.)

Arin: Sora!

Sora: I see him.

(Sora knocks down the mech beside her as the Henchman's vehicle speeds up.)

Arin: We're clear. Just win the race!

Sora: No, we got to knock him back. He's gonna fight dirty!

Arin: But we're ahead. Go!

(The henchman activates the fuel making his car faster than the SA Mark 1. He overtakes Sora and Arin, knocking them out of the track, and winning the race.)

Kreel: They… lost?

(Wyldfyre, Frak. and Euphrasia make it to the finish line.)

Wyldfyre: (shouts) Wyldfyre! Whoo-hoo! Oh yeah!

Euphrasia: Did we win?

(They see the henchman getting the trophy, and don't see Arin or Sora.)

Wyldfyre: No, and I don't see Arin or Sora anywhere.

(Arin and Sora wake up tied to a chair with two triangle racks.)

Sora: (groans) What? Where are we?

Arin: A storehouse? A storehouse with one single lightbulb. The royal treatment.

(They try to untie themselves.)

Sora: We lost the race, and now the Crossroads is doomed… all because I didn't listen to you, Arin. I should have focused on that straightaway at the end.

Arin: No. They fought dirty and would have found a way to beat us. We needed to be more on the defense. You were right.

Sora: No, I'm pretty sure you were.

Arin: Well, one thing's for certain… not working together made us lose.

Sora: You'd think we'd know better by now. Let's promise never to stop listening to each other again.

Arin: Done and done.

(A door opens blinding Sora and Arin.)

William Yards: I don't believe we've met before. William Yards, but you call me Bill Yards.

Arin: Bill… Yards? Are you serious? A Pool Store owner named "Billiards"?

William Yards: Yeah, my dad thought it was hilarious too. I'm sure he'll find it even funnier when his new Crossroads apartment is turned into a parking lot.

Sora: We're going to overturn your win! You cheated with illegal fuel.

William Yards: You have no proof of that.

Arin: We're the proof! We were there when your henchmen stole it, and we were there when they used it.

William Yards: Like I said, you have no proof… because you aren't going to exist long enough to tell anyone. (Snaps his fingers to the henchmen, who escort Arin and Sora) I've been running my operations at a pretty low level for a while, but i knew eventually I'd have to take care of you, Ninja. I really thought it'd be one of the good ones, though, like Kai or-or Zane.

Arin: (winces) Hurtful.

William Yards: But maybe they'll come running to save you and then they'll meet the same fate I have in store for you. (He knocks a ball and presses a button. The floor opens up, revealing a deep swimming pool with Ripper Sharks.)

Arin: Pool Sharks? Really?

William Yards: I commit to the bit… the bit of your imminent doom. (aims his staff at them)

(Outside William Yards Pool and Billiards, two henchmen are talking.)

Henchmen 1: You think the crossroads has a good dry cleaner? This sweat suit is starting to stink. (sniffs) Oof!

Henchmen 2: (scoffs) Starting to?

Wyldfyre: You sure this is the place you followed them to, Riyu?

Frak: The henchmen at the front door isn't exactly subtle.

Wyldfyre: Ah, only two. I can take 'em.

Frak: No, we should keep a low profile. Sneak in. You know, like Ninja?

Euphrasia: Oh, uh, me? What if we just talk to this Mr. Yards guy, uh, nicely?

Wyldfyre: (scoffs) All your thoughts are terrible, and you should definitely listen to me. Egalt himself said I was leader material. You can't just see that because you're both blinded by your crushes on Arin and Sora.

Euphrasia: (laughs awkwardly) What? I don't know what you're talking about?

Wyldfyre: Ha, cloud girl, when you look at Arin, hearts literally float outta your eyes, and you're even worse, Frak. When you're around Sora, you make getting flustered and saying dumb things an art form.

Frak: (laughs) What? The heat's gone to your head, Wyldfyre. A crush on Sora? Ha! ha!

Euphrasia: And I act the same around Arin as I do anyone else.

Wyldfyre: Really? Well, then, who were you going to vote for leader?

Euphrasia: Hey, look! (She points to an open window.) there's an open window we can sneak into.

Frak: Good plan! Ninja go!

(Riyu grumbles.)

Wyldfyre: Pathetic.

(Inside William Yards Pool and Billiards.)

Sora: So, you have a plan now?

Arin: I'm thinking this is really more-of-a-make-it-upon-the-go situation.

(On the second floor of the storehouse, a henchman walks with a cue stick. The open window opens fully and the henchman gets grabbed by Riyu and thrown out the window. Euphrasia, Frak, Wyldfyre enter, and Riyu flies to the roof window, opening it.)

Sora: (sees Riyu and whispers) Okay, I got an idea. On the count of three, we jump.

Arin: (whispers) Your idea is to get eaten by sharks?

Sora: (whispers) Trust me. (loudly) Oh, well, just going to leap. I really wish someone could catch us.

(Riyu perks up. Sora and Arin jump and Riyu flies to Sora and Arin, catching them and avoiding the ripper sharks.)

William Yards: What? Who is that, now? (Riyu lands, safely dropping off Arin and Sora.) Oh, You fools! Stop them!

(Riyu breaks Arin and Sora free.)

Arin: Ninja… go! (performs Spinjitzu to knock down the henchmen.)

Frak: That must be our cue. Huh? (spots a paper)

(Wyldfyre and Euphrasia jump off and slam down two Henchmen.)

Arin: (spots an henchmen heading towards Sora) Look out!

Sora: (spots an henchmen heading towards Arin) Behind you!

Frak: H'ah! H'yah! (knocks one down, then another behind him)

(Riyu chases after a henchman. Another henchman attacks him, but he shakes him off. William Yards runs towards the exit, but Wyldfyre appears and blocks his way.)

Wyldfyre: H'yah! Time for a leader-on-leader showdown. No henchmen… except mine.

(William Yards runs away and walks on the plank.)

William Yards: (chuckles wickedly) You think I'm just a salesman? (breaks his own stick and attacks Wyldfyre with the pieces)

Wyldfyre: H'yah! H'ah! (fights William Yards)

William Yards: Y'ah!

Wyldfyre: R'ah! (She kicks Yards near the end of the plank. She then jumps on the plank, flinging him up.)

Arin: H'yah! (He uses Object Spinjitzu on a ball to hit a button and close the floor as Yards crashes. Sora grabs a triangle rack and uses it to restrain Yards.)

William Yards: You got nothing on me, ninja. I'm just a salesman. Your word against mine. You can't prove I cheated.

Frak: (hols a paper) Actually, we can prove a lot of things, William Yards. When people see this secret history of the crimes you've been hiding for years, your pool business is going to scratch.

William Yards: I gotta admit… solid wordplay.

(In the Monastery of Spinjitzu, the young ninja finish their vote.)

Sora: Okay, everybody gets one vote. (She reads all the papers.) One vote for Arin, one for me, one for Wyldfyre, two for Wyldfyre, three for Wyldfyre… …four votes for Wyldfyre. (Wyldfyre high-fives Riyu) I guess Wyldfyre's… our new leader. Maybe voting wasn't the best idea.

Wyldfyre: A wise choice, I must say. Wyldfyre!

(Percival opens the door with Frohicky.)

Arin: (chuckles nervously) A vote's a vote.

Percival: Am I interrupting?

Arin: Percival! Oh, don't tell us Imperium is in trouble again.

Percival: Quite the opposite. I'm here to ask for the ninja's assistance with the Summit of the Merged Lands.

Euphrasia: You're in charge of that?

Percival: It was my idea! After Beatrix's attempt to retake Imperium, I thought more lands could benefit from a better sense of connection, and maybe quell any tensions that might already exist, but I'm having trouble tracking down the remaining evil Loyalists. I mean, they can just take off their masks and hide anywhere. If any Loyalists are still out there, they could jeopardize our peace efforts. I won't rest until I bring every last Loyalist to justice.

(Sora's parents opens the door and eavesdrop on the conversation. Suddenly they're interrupted by Barry and Jenny. They close the door pretending they never saw anything.)

Sora's mother: Hi, Barry! Jenny!

(Sora's father waves at them.)

Barry: Hey there! Still up for having that first meeting of the Ninja Parents Support Group?

Sora's mother: Are we ever! I made a cheese plate!

Barry: Ooh, yum. That sounds great. See you there!

(Barry and Jenny walk away, and so do Sora's parents. In the living room Barry and Jenny are on the couch with Arin.)

Arin: But we proved that Yards cheated, and now he's on trial for all of his crimes. The Crossroads are safe!

(Sora's parents walk up to Sora with a cheese plate.)

Sora's Mother: What you did was wonderful, saving that whole community like that.

Sora: Uh… thanks, Mom.

Sora's Father: Ninjago is lucky to have you.

Sora's Mother: We're very proud of you… Sora.

Sora: You… you used my name.

Sora's Mother: Sure. We--

(Sora runs away. Arin tries to go after Sora but is stopped by Jenny. Sora goes to a wall and cries.)

(In the Loyalist Hideout, the Loyalists power up Cryptor, and he wakes up.)

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
