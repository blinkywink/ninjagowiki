(Thunderfang is in his prison when he realizes something.)

Thunderfang: Ahh... the final two Prismatic Blades are in the hands of my servants. Source Dragons! You seven cosmic frauds! You've kept me from what I deserve for eons, but soon... I will rise!

(Arin has a sad face.)

Arin: It's true. My parents are dead. I saw the headstones myself. I didn't even get to say goodbye to them.

(The Ninja are also sad when they hear Arin say that his parents died.)

Frak: (to Arin) Aw, man.

Sora: Arin, I'm so sorry.

Lloyd: How did...? I mean, what happened?

Arin: The Merge happened! My parents died because of what Master Wu did!

Lloyd: If my uncle really caused the Merge, I have to believe he had a good reason.

Arin: No reason makes it okay that my family died!

Lloyd: No, I didn't mean... I just...

Arin: But more people will die if the Forbidden Five use those blades, and I need Ras back.

Nya: So you want to work with us?

Arin: If... if that's okay.

Lloyd: Of course it is.

Kai: You're one of us. (He and Arin smile.)

Zane: I concur.

Frak: Was hoping we'd get to fight together.

Sora: I'd rather risk my life with my best friend then be safe without you.

Nya: Then let's get going.

Zane: Not to precipitate on this procession, but given the rate Drix was traveling with the final two Prismatic Blades, catching up to him before he reaches Thunderfang is an impossibility.

Frak: Impossible as in... it can't happen?

Zane: Yes, I am indeed using the word "impossible" to mean "impossible."

Kai: I hate Drix. Second worst time I've had fighting insects. First time was that picnic with Jay.

Nya: That was my picnic with Jay. I didn't invite you! And I told you not to rub honey in your hair!

Kai: Well, Jay double-dog dared me! I didn't want to back out and look uncool.

Sora: (To Arin) Did you miss this?

Nya: "Look uncool?" (She laughs.) Did running though the Monastery with swarm of clamp-beetles on your tail look cool?

Kai: Yes, I happen to think that--

Lloyd: Hold it. "Through the Monastery." That's it. I know how to beat Drix.

(In the City of Temples, Wyldfyre is speaking to a Medical Bot about Roby's condition.)

Wyldfyre: What do you mean, "He won't survive much longer"? Is that med-bot talk for "He's definitely getting better"?

Medical Bot: It is not. It is only a matter of time before Roby's vital signs will cease. The same for the dragon.

(Wyldfyre turns and sees Hermes, who is also in poor health.)

Wyldfyre: Wrong answer. There has to be something we can do. Check all those boring books again.

Medical Bot: We have searched every volume in the library several times for any--

Wyldfyre: (She grabs the bot and turns it around.) Check them again!

(The Medical Bot opens the book.)

Medical Bot: Read this. Read that. Already read it. Waste of my time.

(A Spirit Dragon enters through the window.)

Wyldfyre: What--?

(The Spirit Dragon lands next to Hermes.)

Wyldfyre: (To the Spirit Dragon.) You're worried about your friend too, huh?

(The Spirit Dragon breaths a breath of rainbow fire at Hermes.)

Wyldfyre: Hey! Don't do that!

(Suddenly, Hermes shines again and recovers from the effects of Decay. The two fly out the window. Wyldfyre turns to Roby, apparently having a idea, and makes a determined face.)

(At the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Mr. Frohicky is cleaning the Hangar Bay with his automatic mop machine.)

Mr. Frohicky: And... done. Personal reward time! (He takes a box of sweets out of his pocket) Mm, candied Zip Flies!

(When he was trying to open the box, the ninja arrive and mess up the Hangar Bay even more. Kai and Frak run, but they start to slip.)

Mr. Frohicky: Careful! I just waxed the--

(Frak hits a box and Kai falls on top of him.)

Mr. Frohicky: Oh, my mirror finish! This is a shoes-off temple!

(Arin slips and falls to the ground, but Nya helps him up.)

Sora: Aah!

Mr. Frohicky: Tragically, my polishing skills are unparalleled. (He helps Kai to get up.) My apolish-gies. Master Zane, did you get that?

(Zane lifts the automatic mop machine and Sora grabs it just before she falls.)

Sora: Wha-ha-ha! Zane, how long will Lloyd's plan take?

Zane: (He extends his arm to her.) Set-up should only take a few minutes, if all goes as intended. We have only done this once before, and it's was not intentionally planned that time.

Sora: A few minutes is all I needed.

(Everyone keeps slipping.)

Nya: (To Sora) For what?

Sora: To build you all something to even the odds against Drix and the Dragonians. (She slips a little) Whoa.

Lloyd: (to the ninja) You all heard Zane. We only have a few minutes to grab supplies, weapons, anything you need. Meet back in the Dragon Stable!

(All slip.)

Mr. Frohicky: (sighs) Well, I wasn't really interested in personal rewards anyway. (He puts the box back in his pocket.) Aah! Ow. (He slips.)

(Later, Arin rides the elevator to the living room and looks at his parents' old swing set, starting to feel sad. In a flashback, Arin is posing on top of the house's gate, then comes down doing a front flip and approaches his parents. Jenny pets Arin on the head, and Barry gives him a piece of pie, which Arin bites into. In the present, Arin is sitting on the swing, sad, and holding his Lloyd plush.)

Sora: (To Arin) Sorry, just came to grab some parts.

Arin: You know, I missed this swing.

Sora: When we didn't know what happened to you, I'd sometimes imagine you sitting there. It's good to see you back.

Arin: I'm not back. I'm not sure I could ever stay in Master Wu's monastery again, knowing what he did.

Sora: Arin, I know I lied to you. I let you think you hit Ras with the--

Arin: Sora, please.

Sora: I really need to apologize.

Arin:^No. You don't. I'm the one who needs to apologize. I was so upset and... embarrassed. I overreacted, when you were just trying to make me feel good about myself... like you always do. Ever since I was a kid, I had this dream of being a Ninja, and then, somehow, I got my chance, and... Sora, do you have any idea how heartbreaking it is to find out you're not very good at the thing you love the most?

Sora: But you are a good Ninja.

Arin: I'm getting better now. And Ras, of all people, is helping me do it. He showed me that I was holding myself back. Holding my emotions back.

Sora: You've never held back how you feel, Arin. You're the sweetest person I've ever met.

Arin: Not those kind of emotions. My anger. The hate I feel deep inside. The stuff I never show. It gives you a burst of force. (He throws his Lloyd plush on the swing.) A fuel I've never had before.

Sora: Look, you know me. I'm not the best at training.

Arin: That's not true.

Sora: Last week, I failed a Spinjitzu exercise so hard, I got dizzy and barfed all over Zane.

Arin: Okay. Gross.

Sora: It's fine, he froze it so it was easier to clean up. The point is I don't know training, but I know you. There's no way hate and anger are your greatest strengths. The Arin I know, my best friend, his strength comes from his kindness. His generosity. His hope.

Arin: But all that didn't help me.

Sora: It helped me. And it helped everyone who met you.

(Arin's depressed face turns into a smile. Nya appears.)

Nya: (To Arin and Sora) Come on. They've almost got it. (She runs and is followed by Sora and Arin.)

(At the Dragon Stables, Lloyd, Zane and P.I.X.A.L are working on a machine.)

Zane: This was truly an inspired idea, Lloyd.

Lloyd: Only if it works. You think that thing will generate enough power?

Zane: Like the time you carried Source Dragon energy.

Lloyd: Not something I plan on doing ever again, thank you.

Zane: I believe I can increase the power of our Portal Gate enough to get all of us and our gear though to the First Land Monastery.

Lloyd: Let's just hope it's in enough time to cut off Drix.

(Zane presses a button and P.I.X.A.L. hits the machine with a wrench, causing it to activate and the antenna fires a beam at the Portal Gate, powering up it.)

(At the First Land Monastery, the Ninja appears in their vehicles and new gliders.)

Kai: Whoo-hoo-hoo! These new gliders are awesome, Sora!

Sora: All thanks to Arin for getting us a prototype!

Arin: Any time!

Lloyd: Alright, let's get back those blades, stop the Forbidden Five--

Kai: And get busy saving the world!

Ninja: Ninja, go!

(In the City of Temples, the Medical Bot is talking to Wyldfyre)

Medical Bot: ...zero research, no proper testing, absolutely no prior experience, your lack of a medical degree from an accredited university.

(Wyldfyre rubs her hands together and activates her elemental power of heat.)

Medical Bot: Shall I continue with my list of why this course of action is ill-advised?

Wyldfyre: Ill-advised actions are my favorite kind of actions.

Medical Bot: That fact will now go on the top of my list.

Wyldfyre: You got any better ideas, Dr. Robot? 'Cause if I don't at least to try this, Roby is definitely gonna die.

Medical Bot: There are no courses of action to my knowledge that would save Roby.

Wyldfyre: That's what I thought. Please work. (She places her heat hands on Roby and he groans in pain)

(A sandstorm rages in the First Land as the Ninja approach and remove their masks.)

Kai: Blech! I hate sand on my tongue.

(A tornado of purple, pink, red, and black colors, which has a type of electricity coming out of it, is seen.)

Lloyd: Looks like that's the center of the winds.

Nya: And I'm guessing where Thunderfang is imprisoned.

Sora: If he's still imprisoned.

Lloyd: We'd know if he was free.

Zane: Then your idea worked, Lloyd. Drix has not yet arrived.

(As everyone watches, Sora jumps on top of Riyu and climbs onto Zane to get a better look.)

Frak: (To Arin) How cool is this?

Arin: Getting dust blown in our faces? I can think of cooler things.

Frak: No, being on an adventure with the Ninja. Talk about a childhood dream come true.

Arin: (He sighs.) I didn't know dreams could get so complicated.

Frak: Blame me, buddy.

Arin: For what?

Frak: For making you think Ras's teaching weren't trash.

Arin: I'm a stronger Ninja now than I ever was before.

Frak: If you think Ras can teach you to be a better Ninja than Lloyd, maybe you don't understand what a Ninja really is.

Zane: Everyone... They're here.

(Drix arrives flying with the last two Prismatic Blades and the Dragonian Trackers right behind him, with two of them holding Ras.)

(Roby starts to wake up.)

Wyldfyre: (She shakes the Medical Bot.) He's waking up!

Roby: (He is shown to have recovered from the effects of Decay.) What happened? I felt like I had a fever.

Wyldfyre: What happened is you're beautiful again!

Roby: (He takes a picture with Wyldfyre and the Medical Bot.) I thought that Kur lady unstanged my zoot for good.

Wyldfyre: Yeah, well, I used my vast knowledge of science and medicine to cure your decay.

Medical Bot: That is not what happ--

Wyldfyre: Vast knowledge. So, how we feeling? Healed, totally fine?

Roby: Uh, I think so, but why, you got somewhere to be?

Wyldfyre: The Ninja ran off to fight some giant dragon that shoots chaos storms, and without me--

Roby: There's no way they can survive without their best Ninja. Go! I'll be fine.

(Outside, Wyldfyre climbs on her Mech Storm Rider and flies towards the Monastery of Gates.)

(Ras wakes up and struggles to free himself, then sees Drix in front of him. Drix turns his head and sees Ras awakes, then turns his head again and smiles, but shortly after becomes angry.)

Drix: Impossible! (Lloyd appears flying in font of him with his glider.)

(Lloyd appears flying in font of him with his glider.)

Drix: I left you behind in the World Forest! You couldn't have beaten me here!

Lloyd: Ninja are full of surprises!

(Drix activate Shatterspin, while Lloyd uses Dragon Spinjitzu to activate Rising Dragon, which pushes Drix away.)

Drix: (To the Dragonian Trackers.) Attack!

(They attack as Lloyd retreats.)

Sora: (To Arin.) I'll go low, you go--

Arin: High! Just like in the Crossroads!

(They split up and are followed by the Dragonian Trackers. Sora shoots at two but they dodge and start ramming her jet. Arin shoots two more, but a third grabs the back of his drone. He then dives in and manages to make the Dragonians drop the drone. Meanwhile, Sora still has the two Dragonian Trackers crashing into her jet. Lloyd fires Energy at the Dragonians, knocking them off the jet. Kai and Nya join the fight, and Kai fires fire at a Dragonian.)

Kai: (To Arin.) Hey! Don't hog all the fun!

(He performs Dragon Spinjitzu.)

Kai: (To Lloyd) Did you see that? My Spinjitzu looked more awesome than usual!

Lloyd: It's a First Realm thing. You get used to it

(Arin notices the two Dragonians holding Ras.)

Arin: (To the Dragonians.) Whoa, whoa, I need him! (He goes after them and dodges other dragonians.) Whew!

(Drix tries to escape, but is stopped by Lloyd.)

Lloyd: Sorry, I'm canceling the appointment you and those blades have with Thunderfang.

(He fires Energy at Drix, the letter laughs and sees another path.)

Drix: The conqueror's path is rarely a straight line. (He heads the other way but is stopped by Riyu, Zane and P.I.X.A.L.)

(Riyu screeches.)

Zane: (To Drix) If you did not understand Riyu, he said we hypothesized you would try to escape via this route.

(Realizing he is surrounded, Drix spits a fly out of his mouth, which flew towards the tornado.)

(Near the tornado, the rest of the Forbidden Five are waiting for Drix.)

Kur: (To Nokt.) Told you he'd be late.

Nokt: Late? I'm betting he failed.

(The fly comes to them and lands on Rox's hand, then they prepare themselves.)

(Arin continues trying to save Ras in his drone.)

Ras: Arin! Free your master!

(Arin is attacked by other Dragonians. Lloyd and Drix continue their fight, Lloyd destroys Drix's left arm, causing the blue Prismatic Blade to fall to the ground.)

Lloyd: Thought you might do that! (He picks the blade from the ground.) One down, one to go!

(Drix regenerates his arm. P.I.X.A.L. hits two Dragonians with her car's laser, and they fall. Kai and Nya punch a Dragonian each and Zane shoots ice at another.)

Lloyd: Ninja, we got this!

(Drix looks to the side, notices something, and starts laughing wickedly. Lloyd looks confused by this, then turns back and sees the rest of the Five and more Dragonians approaching. Lloyd gasps.)

Rox: (To Lloyd.) That belongs to us!

Lloyd: Those things aren't yours just 'cause you want them really bad, Rox.

(He kicks Rox and a Dragonian, Zarkt grabs Kai and Kur kicks Zane. Other Dragonians lift P.I.X.A.L.'s car. Nya kicks Zarkt and releases Kai.)

Kai: I'm not great at math, but we are way outnumbered here.

(Sora's fires lasers from her jet at a Dragonian, but misses. Nokt land on top of the jet.)

Nokt: (To Sora.) Time for a rematch, girl.

(He uses Brute Force to punch the jet, damaging it. Arin is surrounded by several Dragonians, but Frak arrives and saves him.)

Frak: (To Arin.) Remember you of that time I saved you from those bullies?

Arin: Uh... why you gotta bring that up?

(Nokt continues chasing Sora's jet, but he soon notices Drix along with the Dragonians who are holding Ras.)

Nokt: Now, who do we have here?

Drix: I brought you a present, Nokt.

Nokt: Priorities, priorities. (He pushes Drix aside and takes Ras's hammer.) I can take him from here.

(He flies Ras away. Lloyd and Rox fight and Rox kicks Lloyd down. Nokt and Ras pass by her.)

Rox: (To Nokt.) No! Focus on What matters!

(They keep moving away.)

Rox: Oh, so impulsive, and so foolish.

(Lloyd comes back and tries to attack Rox from behind, but she blocks. Nokt throws Ras to the ground and then he lands.)

Nokt: (To Ras.) I've been waiting a long time for this. (He frees Ras from the vine and gives him back his hammer.) I have no interest in fighting someone weakened... like you did to me with that control collar.

Ras: I should never have freed you.

Nokt: Aw, that hurts. Because if you didn't free me, we'd never have moments like this!

(They start fighting. Zane hits the two Dragonians with ice fires it at Drix. He dodges it, but is knocked by Riyu. This sends the Returning Blade flying and Rox tries to catch it, nut Arin uses his Grapple Gun to catch it first.)

Arin: (To Rox.) Sorry, lady!

Rox: You little runt! Don't you know who you are messing w--

(She gets hit by Lloyd, then uses Shatterspin to push him away. Lloyd uses Dragon Spinjitzu to use Rising Dragon. Rox and Lloyd fly towards each other, and Rox hits Lloyd with Shatterspin and makes him drop the blue Prismatic Blade, but then is caught by Kur, who is hit by Nya's water, who tries to catch the blade, but a Dragonian gets it first, but is punched by Zane and Frak catches it.)

Frak: I've got it!

(He is attacked by three Dragonians, who make him drop the blade, but Riyu manages to catch it. Drix transforms into a swarm and attacks Riyu.)

Arin: Riyu!

(Riyu throws the blade to Arin and he catches it. Rox starts to follow him, but Lloyd catches her.)

Lloyd: Arin, we'll hold them off! Get back to the Monastery with those! (He throws Rox away.)

Arin: On it!

(Nokt takes down Ras using Shatterspin.)

Arin: Ras... no...

(He dives with the done towards them. Nokt approaches Ras, ready to kill him.)

Nokt: Now, Lord Ras, let me show you what real power can--

(Arin arrives and knocks Nokt with the drone. Nokt takes the drone off him with Shatterspin and faces Arin. Rox and Lloyd continues their fight, with the latter kicking Rox, but then he sees Arin facing Nokt.)

Lloyd: Arin, no! (He is kicked in the back by Rox.) Gaah! (He falls.)

(Arin and Nokt face each other.)

Nokt: (to Arin.) Now the little underling wants to save his master?

Arin: Let him go!

Nokt: I don't take orders from you. I don't take orders from anyone.

Arin: Then I'll force you to stop. (He then wields both Prismatic Blades.)

Nokt: (He laughs.) I see you're a comedian.

Arin: It's not fun--

(Rox arrives and kicks Arin in the back, and Nokt kicks him using Shatterspin. Rox then takes the two blades. Nokt jumps, does Shatterspin and kicks Arin into the tornado,)

Rox: (She laughs.) Come, my brethren! It is time!

(Zarkt throws Kai away, Drix leaves Riyu and reforms himself. The Forbidden Five reunites and go into the tornado.)

Lloyd: (To the other Ninja) Come on! We've gotta stop them!

(Zane pulls the Dragonians off the P.I.X.A.L. car, and Nya kicks the Dragonian holding Kai. They all enter the tornado, and begin to struggle due to the strong winds. Lloyd's glider is destroyed, and Riyu manages to catch Arin. Zane, P.I.X.A.L., and Sora use their thrusters to go against the wind. Nya's glider is accidentally destroyed by Kai's, and his and Frak's gliders destroy each other. The Five enter the cave, and Nokt uses his elemental power to destroy some rocks, which are then sucked into the tornado, and one of them hits Zane.)

Sora: P.I.X.A.L.! Zane! Hold tight!

(She uses her elemental powers to combine her jet, P.I.X.A.L.'s car, and Zane into a single mech, which destroys the rocks and manages to land.)

Ninja: Aah!

(The Forbidden Five arrive at Thunderfang's prison. The last two Prismatic Blades begin to glow, as Zarkt uses the blue Prismatic Blade to cut the chain around Thunderfang's left leg, and Drix throws the Returning Blade to cut the chain around Thunderfang's neck. The collar holding Thunderfang to the chain disappears and he roars, driving the Forbidden Five away.)

Rox: (She extends her arm to Thunderfang.) The powers you promised.

Thunderfang: My minions. You are such fools. If you really trusted me, you are even more pathetic than I realized.

(Thunderfang take the souls of the Forbidden Five. Then they fall to the ground with Rox's mask coming off her. Thunderfang roars, causing rockfalls that end the episode.)
