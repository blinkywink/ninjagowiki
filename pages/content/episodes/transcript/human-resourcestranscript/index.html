Human Resources
Between You and Lee
≫

(In Abracalabria, the Hollow Ones walk through the village as Arin, Sora, Gandalaria, the Wizard Guard and Ras look up to see Kur floating and playing her magical flute.)

Ras: What is that?

Sora: Or... who is that?

(Arin squints and notices Kur playing her magical flute.)

Arin: Kur? I thought Thunderfang ate all the Forbidden Five, or something.

Sora: Are we sure he didn't? Kur looks... (She notices that Kur has light blue skin and her eyes are glowing red.) not amazing.

Wizard Guard: (He realizes something.) They're back!

Sora: "They"?

(Everyone looks around, then the Wizard Guard starts to back away, floats out of nowhere, and screams as he transforms into a hollow. This scares a villager, who runs away, also floats out of nowhere, and transforms into a hollow.)

Ras: It's an attack!

Sora: By what? I don't see anything!

Gandalaria: I told you, they're invisible! We must push them back somehow!

(Gandalaria shoots lightning from her crystal ball while Arin and Sora try to hit the invisible enemies with their blows.)

Arin: Kind of hard to smack someone in the face when you can't see their face!

(They, along with Ras, continue trying to hit the invisible enemies, but another Wizard Guard is hollowed out. A villager, who was with this Wizard Guard, approaches them.)

Villager: This is just like last night!

Sora: I've never seen anything like this before.

(Arin and the villager are confused.)

Sora: Pun not intended! Now is not the time for jokes!

Arin: Wait! I have seen something like this before! (He picks up the Returning Blade, looks through it, and sees Spectral Dragonians.) They're Dragonians?

Sora: Invisible Dragonians?

Arin: They look like Soul Suckers from the Spectral Lands!

(The Spectral Dragonians extract the villagers' souls and they fly to Kur's magical flute and enter it.)

Arin: They're draining their lives into that weird instrument!

(Screams of panic are heard and a Geckle has her soul extracted by a Spectral Dragonian.)

Arin: When I say "duck," duck! My blade's made of Prismatic Steel! It should stop them!

Sora: Arin? When for the ducking?

(The Spectral Dragonian who extracted Geckle's soul flies towards Arin.)

Arin: Now! (He jumps, spins around in the air and strikes the Spectral Dragonian with his sword, but it breaks and he is pushed back and falls to the ground.) Oh, that's bad! Not only did my blade not destroy them, these things can break Prismatic Steel!

(In the skies, the Temple Bounty is flying and Jay is talking to Nya and Lloyd on the deck of the ship)

Jay: When we get there, let me do all the talking. I don't need so-called Ninja ruining my flow. (The ship suddenly shakes, causing Nya to fall on top of him, but they get up and he pulls her off of him.) Did we hit something?

Lloyd: (He groans.) No... (Wyldfyre laughs and makes the ship shake again.) ...just Wyldfyre's driving. (To Wyldfyre.) Weren't you supposed to practice flying this thing before we left?

Wyldfyre: (She stands on the rudder.) Aw, me, me, me, me! (She runs on top of the rudder and makes the ship turn.) Weren't you supposed to not be so judgy about my driving? (She does a somersault and heads back to the bridge.) I'm the captain now! My ship, my rules! (She makes the ship spin again and causes Nya, Lloyd and Jay to be thrown across the deck.)

Nya: Can you just keep your eyes on the... sky?

Wyldfyre: Okay! How about your face to the sky?

(She turns the ship upside and back again, sending Jay, Lloyd, Nya and some crates flying and falling back onto the deck. Wyldfyre then whistles nonchalantly)

Lloyd: I've been to the Administration before. Fine by me if you want to do all the talking, Jay. They'll probably fine me for not having a talking permit.

Nya: How did you ending up working there, anyway?

Jay: It's a long story... (Lloyd and Nya wait for him to continue the sentence.) Or a short story. Who knows? I have zero memory of anything before the Merge!

Nya: We've told you what your life was like before the Merge.

Jay: Yeah. So did the Administration. So did Ras. I now know not to believe any of you. But I do know this-- my fist memories, I found myself alone in the Land of Madness. Didn't even know my name. Then I spotted some nice young people in business suits, and I just had a feeling I could trust them.

Lloyd: Because they were wearing suits?

Jay: Yeah! So I went up to them, and it turns out they were looking for me. They told me my name was Jay Walker, and that I was an Agent of the Administration, just like them.

Nya: How'd they know your name?

Jay: How does the Administration know anything? They said I'd worked there my whole life, and had to get back to my job managing the Realm Reassignment Office.

Lloyd: And you just... believed them?

Jay: Suits, Greenie. Suits.

(A flashback of Jay in the Administration, wearing his agent suit, appears.)

Jay: (Narrating.) Turns out I was a natural, so it made sense that's who I really was.

Nya: So these suits claimed to know you?

(The flashback ends and the Temple Bounty spins.)

Jay: Their boss did. Apparently the Administrator sent them a message to go and get me.

(The Temple Bounty flies back and forth.)

Nya: Who's the Administrator?

Jay: No clue. No one I talked to had ever actually seen him, or her, or them, or it?

Nya: Sounds creepy.

Lloyd: So, what did you actually do there?

(Other flashback shows Jay typing on his computer keyboard in the Administration.)

Jay: (Narrating exasperatedly.) Ugh! Realm Reassignment! You know this! People they'd find in empty space bubbles after the Merge. My job was to send them back to wherever I thought they came from.

Lloyd: Where you thought they came from? You, who has no memory of anything or anywhere from before the Merge?

(The flashback ends with Jay falling out of his chair. In the present, Jay falls backward onto the deck.)

Jay: I was working with the best information I could get, and sure, there might have been a few mix-ups, but there's no proof of that, and if there was proof, it would have been shredded and/or burned. (He slides down the side of the deck.) Aah!

Lloyd: "Just a few mix-ups"? You're famously terrible at guessing games.

Jay: Hey!

Nya: No lie. You tank every Charades team you've ever been on.

Jay: You expect me to believe I ever played Charades? I don't even know what Charades is!

Nya: Yet you remember Arin's parents?

Jay: Of course. Jenny and Barry. I had to enter reassignments logs every night in triplicate, then in the weekly reports, then the monthly reports, plus corporate memos.

Lloyd: So that's how you know where they are now?

Jay: Yeah, where I sent them. The Land of Lee.

Wyldfyre: Then why aren't we flying straight there instead of wasting our time at the Administration?

(Wyldfyre turns the ship from left to right, making Nya, Lloyd and Jay go left and right on the deck.)

Jay: You can get to the location by air or boat, but the only way to get into the real Land of Lee is through the Realm Reassignment Area. It won't make any sense until you experience it for yourselves.

Wyldfyre: (She shudders.) Oh... sounds dangerous. (She jumps to the lower part of the bridge.) Now I'm excited.

Lloyd: (To Jay.) And we can trust you? You did shatter your goodness.

Jay: You have no reason to trust me, but you don't have a choice, do you?

Wyldfyre: H'yah! (She jumps back to the highest part of the bridge.) Sold! Let's go!

(She turns the rudder and turns it upside down, causing the boat to shake again and a box with a cake on top to slide to the side.)

Lloyd: But... what's with that cake?

Jay: Let's just hope we don't have to use it.

(The ship accelerates and descends.)

(Arin, Sora, Ras, Gandalaria and some villagers run as Arin looks through the Returning Blade to keep an eye on the Spectral Dragonians.)

Ras: (To Arin.) This is not our battle!

Arin: These people need our help!

Ras: The Pontifex Magicus has been hollowed, and is of no use to me anymore.

Sora: We're not leaving!

(One of the villagers is caught by a Spectral Dragonian and becomes hollowed. Arin sees the villager's soul float to Kur, then sees another Spectral Dragonian flying toward them.)

Arin: Duck!

(They crouch and Arin tries to hit the Spectral Dragonian, but misses, then a Devonian is hollowed out.)

Arin: Can't say I'm into fighting with a stubby blade.

Sora: Where's the other half?

Arin: What, you have some glue?

(Arin looks through the Returning Blade and Sora searches and finds the other half of it. She takes a lamp and uses her Technology to fuse it with the other half of the blade, creating a Prismatic Lamp.)

Sora: (To Gandalaria.) Can you light this up?

Gandalaria: A brighter color? I think that might be a bit gaudy for the--

Sora: No, illuminate it!

Gandalaria: Oh, light it up. Why didn't you say so? (She tries to light the Prismatic Lamp with her crystal ball, but it ends up dropping a coconut from inside.) Could you use a coconut?

Sora: No! I need light!

Gandalaria: Just asking. Some people like coconuts. I guess not you.

(Gandalaria zaps and lights the Prismatic Lamp, making the Spectral Dragonians visible.)

Sora: Wow. There are way more of these things than I realized.

Arin: Come on! This way!

(They run.)

Ras: We must leave! The only reason we came here was to find the Underworld.

Gandalaria: Oh, the Underworld. I know exactly where that is. Well, not exactly, but I know exactly where my map is, and that would tell me exactly.

Ras: Where?

(A Spectral Dragonian attacks Gandalaria.)

Ras: Ninja! Protect this woman! She can help us on our quest.

Gandalaria: (She gets stuck upside down inside a barrel and talks.) I can take care of myself!

(In the Land of Madness, the Temple Bounty descends from the sky and Wyldfyre lowers the anchor and attaches it to a rock, sending Lloyd, Nya, and Jay sliding across the deck.)

Lloyd: Everyone alive?

Nya: Not sure.

(Jay, Wyldfyre, Lloyd and Nya disembark from the ship and approach the Administration building.)

Lloyd: I've never seen the Administration from the outside. It's so... boring.

Nya: I'm looking directly at it and already forgot everything about it.

Lloyd: So, how are we sneaking in? Air vent? Basement?

Wyldfyre: Blast a giant hole? Please?

Jay: My instinct is a full attack, swords slicing, taking out any and every Agent that dares stand up to us. Show them what true power is... (He chuckles.) ...but there's too many of them, and that plan could fail.

(Wyldfyre liked Jay's plan.)

Lloyd: And that's not how Ninja handle things.

Nya: You really did shatter your goodness, didn't you?

Jay: It's so cute you think I had goodness to shatter. (Nya gets sad.) Nope, straight-on attack won't work, so I got something better. (He runs to the side and comes back wearing his Agent suit.) Suits, am I right? Now, watch this.

(Jay uses his access card to open the door to Administration. He enters, followed by Lloyd, Wyldfyre, and Nya, who closes the door. They accidentally encounter Agents Underwood and Allen, who notice them.)

Underwood: (She points to them.) Halt!

Jay: Allen! Underwood! What's shakin'?

Underwood: A little informal for a morning greeting, but it is casual Friday, so I'll allow it.

Allen: Returning from sabbatical, Agent Jay Walker?

Jay: Yep, and since today is "Bring Your Ninja Warrior Friends To Work Day," (He points to the Ninja.) I have companions. Heading to Guest Services to fill out their paperwork and get temporary badges.

Underwood: Hmm... "Bring Your Ninja Warrior Friends To Work Day" was last week.

(Underwood and Allen point their guns at Jay and his friends.)

Nya: That escalated quickly.

Lloyd: Uh, we must've just got the B-Y-N... W-F... T-W day wrong?

Underwood: That's not your most egregious offense, Agent Walker, you have long been on our most wanted list for abandoning your post!

(Jay scowls at the Agents.)

(A Spectral Dragonian tries to attack Gandalaria, but she fends it off by throwing a banana peel from her crystal ball. She throws more banana peels at it, but it attacks again and one of the banana peels she threw falls to the ground in front of Arin.)

Arin: (He slips on the banana peel.) Whoa!

(The Spectral Dragonian passes under Arin before he falls. As he falls, the banana peel falls on top of him and the Spectral Dragonian passes next to Sora.)

Arin: I'm okay! (He picks up the banana peel from his back.) Yuck! (He throws the banana peel away.)

Ras: We must leave! It's the only way to keep you truly safe!

Gandalaria: Forget it, kitten! I'm going to protect this village with my last puff of magic, if I must!

(A Spectral Dragonian grabs Gandalaria and extracts her soul, which Kur quickly sucks into her magical flute. Gandalaria hollows out and drops her crystal ball, which Sora catches. Just as more Spectral Dragonians are about to attack Sora, Arin, and Ras, the sun rises, forcing them and Kur to retreat.)

Arin: What's happening?

Sora: It's morning. They must not be able to stand the sun!

Arin: I don't understand. It's like the Spectral Lands spread into this town, but only have power at night.

Sora: Can these people be cured? Do we need to hunt down Kur and return their life-forces?

Ras: Forget them. Our goal is to forge Source Dragon Icons, and because of your choises, we failed.

Arin: Hey, that's not our fault.

Ras: It is your fault. Yours and the Sorceress' selfishness. Without her help, we are forced to find another way to the Underworld! If that's even possible.

(They see Gandalaria pass by them.)

Sora: Cole and Zane said she lives in the Monastery that appeared here. Maybe if we take her back home, we can help her get less... hollowed out, and find her map to the Underworld.

Arin: Sora, that's a great idea!

Ras: (He growls.) Let us find this Monastery.

(Jay, Lloyd, Nya and Wyldfyre are trapped in a cell.)

Lloyd: (To Jay.) I can't believe you got us stuck in here. What kind of plan was that?

Nya: This is just like you. You always had the weirdest, worst ideas, but usually they were charming somehow. This one is just plain stupid.

Jay: Trust me. Everything is going exactly as planned.

Lloyd: Don't see how that's possible.

Jay: I knew the moment we entered the building, alarms would go off, 'cause I left the company, which no one ever does. Then we'd get sent here, deep into the inner workings, exactly where we need to be.

Nya: In a cell with no escape?

Jay: Yeah! It's perfect, 'cause now the Administration thinks they have us under their control. Now all we have to do is escape. (To a Merlopian.) Hey! Agent Etha! Yes, I'm talking to you! I know you don't get much company in here... (He chuckles.) So, Agent Underwood told me you were really into Merlopian Dance Rituals and Celebrations! Any interest in telling us more about that?

Etha: I've been waiting for someone to ask me that my whole life! Dance ethnography is a really fascinating subject... (Time passes.) ...and they showered them with exotic flowers... (Time passes.) ...the town would elect a sacrifice, and... (Time passes.) ...the lights would fade up, and the lead dancer would descend with a fan and a lantern... (She whispers as she dances.) ...and the magical eel... (She notices that Jay is sleeping standing up, but Lloyd wakes him up.) Y-- You sure I'm not boring you? Anyway, the audience threw gummy candy on the stage! Shall I demonstrate? Ha! (She spins and giggles.) Ta-da! (She throws the gummy candies aside.) Eat the candy... (She hits Jay on the head and wakes him up.)

Jay: That was informative, Etha, and, uh, doesn't their sacred winter dance involve a blindfold?

Etha: I can't believe you know that! (She takes a blindfold out of her pocket.) And I just so happen to have my dance blindfold with me for exactly this potential scenario! (She dances with the blindfold on her face and then Jay takes the access card from her pocket and opens the door. He leaves the cell, followed by Nya, Wyldfyre, who is interested in her dancing, and Lloyd.) I trust you're still there and enjoying this silently!

(They escape to the offices.)

Wyldfyre: (To Lloyd.) She's a fantastic dancer!

Jay: Be right back. I need something from the locker room.

Lloyd: What could you need from a locker room?

Jay: It's where Agent Leopold keeps his gym shorts.

Nya: I'm going to need more information.

Jay: He never washes them.

(Jay enters the locker room and comes out holding his nose and holding a smelly pair of gym shorts, which disgusts Nya, Lloyd, and Wyldfyre.)

Wyldfyre: Oh, man!

(Jay puts the gym shorts in a tube capsule, causing the stench from the shorts to escape through other tube capsules and spread throughout the building, disgusting the agents.)

Merlopian agent: Ugh! What is that?

Munce agent: That smells like Agent Leopold!

Merlopian agent: Call the Detox Crew!

(As the agents faint from the stench, Jay, Lloyd, Wyldfyre, who is humming Agent Etha's song, and Nya run past them, holding their noses.)

Wyldfyre: Ooh,I should learn Merlopian dances! (She imitates the dance and bumps into a control panel, activating the alarm and talks to Jay and Lloyd.) It wasn't me.

Jay: Why did you come along? All you do is mess things up! You can't drive right, you dance into alarms, you--

(Underwood and Allen see them.)

Underwood: (She gasps.) More Ninja? When it's not "Bring Your Ninja Warrior Friends To Work Day"?

Allen: Those are the same Ninja! And Jay Walker! They flew the coop!

(Underwood and Allen point their guns at them again.)

Underwood: Curse you, Agent Etha!

(Lloyd runs up to the agents, does a somersault, and makes them uncover their noses.)

Allen: (He gags.) Oh, the stench!

Underwood: It's... (She gags.) ...In my lungs!

(They faint from the stench and Jay and the Ninja run away again.)

(Arin, who is with Gandalaria on his motorcycle, Sora and Ras approach the Monastery of Mysterium Monastery on their motorcycles.)

Arin: I think the Sorceress is coming too. She sees her home!

(Gandalaria mumbles.)

Sora: Maybe it'll get better inside?

(They arrive at the Monastery, get off their motorcycles and then enter, activating an alarm.)

Ras: (He grumbles.) Some kind of security. It shouldn't be a probl-- (He steps on a rake, which appears on the ground, and it hits him in the face.)

Sora: Ha-ha-ha! (She laughs at Ras and steps on another rake, which also appears on the ground, and it hits her in the face.) Oof!

Arin: Ha-ha! (He laughs Sora and steps on another rake, which once again appears on the ground, and it hits him in the face.) Oof!

(A holographic Gandalaria appears floating.)

Holographic Gandalaria: I hope you enjoy my traps! That's what you get for trying to break in, whoever you are. (She floats down to Sora.) Predictable as always, Janet! (She maskes a "I'm watching you" gesture to Sora as she walks away.)

Sora: Who's Janet?

(The ground shakes and then Sora, Ras, Arin and Gandalaria begin to magically sink into the ground.)

(Jay and the Ninja continue running.)

Jay: We're almost to Realm Reassignment! We'll be safe as soon as--

(They accidentally encounter other agents and are cornered by Allen and Underwood.)

Nya: (To Jay.) Was this part of the plan?

Jay: (He sighs.) I didn't want to have to do this. (To Lloyd.) Green guy... (He takes a pen in his right hand and a birthday card in his left hand.) Cake me.

(Lloyd takes off his backpack, takes out the cake and hands it to Jay.)

Jay: Everybody! We've got an office birthday today, and you've all got to sign this card!

Green Merlopian agent: Ooh, a birthday!

Prentis: Leave some space for me!

Female agent: Who didn't sign? Did you sign?

Nya: Hey, we need plates! And napkins! Who's got plates and napkins?

(Jay and the Ninja take advantage of the distraction to escape.)

Male agent 1: Forks? Are there forks?

Female agent: Whose birthday is it? Carl's?

Male agent 2: No, he just had his birthday!

(Jay and the Ninja manage to lose the agents.)

Jay: Come on, we need new clothes from Lost and Found! (He runs, followed by the others.)

(Sora, Ras, Arin and Gandalaria continue to magically sink into the ground.)

Ras: (To Sora.) Do something! Use your Elemental Powers!

Sora: (She tries to use her Elemental Powers, but fails.) There's no tech in this monastery!

(They hear Gandalaria muttering and then Arin realizes something.)

Arin: Wait a minute... (He tries to use Gandalaria's crystal ball, but she protest.)

Ras: (To Arin.) What are you doing?

Arin: Casting a spell!

Sora: You don't know any spells!

Arin: Nope, but maybe we'll hit on something.

(Arin accidentally makes the crystal ball release green smoke, making him, Ras, and Sora cough.)

Arin: Not that. (He tries to use the crystal ball again, but accidentally turns Gandalaria's hat into bubbles.) Feels like we're getting closer?

Sora: I'm not sure that's true.

(Arin keeps trying to use the crystal ball, but ends up making Gandalaria's hat return to normal and floating fish appear.)

Ras: This is getting us nowhere!

Sora: No, but maybe these could get us somewhere. (She climbs onto one of the floating fish.) We can hold onto these fish and pull ourselves out!

(Sora manages to escape sinking into the ground and Arin and Ras do the same as her.)

Arin: This now tops my "Weirdest Things I've Ever Done" list.

(Arin grabs and pulls Gandalaria as he climbs onto the floating fish and Ras catches the last one with his hammer. Arin reaches Sora and Ras throws Gandalaria at him.)

Arin: Aah!

Ras: (He jumps and lands where they are.) Oof!

(They go to the monastery library and begin searching for the map to the Underworld.)

Sora: A-ha! (She shows Ras and Arin that she found the map.)

Ras: The Underworld.

(Jay and the Ninja are shown wearing Lost and Found clothing, with Wyldfyre scratching herself.)

Nya: (To Jay.) Why?

Jay: Told you, the Land of Lee is weird, and you definitely can't parade in there dressed like a Ninja.

(They arrive at the Department of Reassignment and Jay starts typing on the keyboard.)

Jay: Ha-ha! Still got it. Oh, one more thing. For some reason, everyone who got lost in the space bubbles were mostly a pair, like, siblings, couples, whatever. We need fake stories like that.

Lloyd: If we're going undercover, Wyldfyre, you and I are brother and sister...

Wyldfyre: Barf.

Lloyd: ...and Jay and Nya are married.

Jay: Gross, but okay.

(Nya feels sad when she hears Jay say that.)

Jay: (He finishes typing.) Everyone ready?

(The portal starts to turn on and they go in front of it. They hear a noise, look back and see Underwood and Allen come through the door, pointing their guns and holding pieces of cake.)

Underwood: There they are!

(They shoot Jay and the Ninja while eating cake.)

Allen: Let's shut down the system!

(Underwood continues shooting at them as Allen climbs the stairs to the monitors.)

Wyldfyre: No! (She jumps up to the monitors.) H'yah! (She fires Heat at the larger monitor, causing it to turn red and release electricity. The portal opens and she dodges Allen's shots.) Aah! (Underwood manages to knock her down by shooting her in the back.)

Lloyd: Wyldfyre!

Wyldfyre: (She falls to the ground.) Yaah!

(The portal begins to close.)

Jay: Leave her! The portal's closing!

Nya: You might have shattered your goodness, but we didn't! (She performs Spinjitzu and jumps up to the monitors.) Yaah! (She kicks Allen and helps Wyldfyre.)

(As Jay has already gone through the portal, Nya and Wyldfyre also jump into it. Underwood tries to shoot Lloyd, but he also jumps into the portal before it closes.)

Underwood: They've escaped!

Allen: (He finishes eating his piece of cake.) I forgot how much I love custard.

(The lights suddenly go out and the mist from the Spectral Lands rolls in under the door.)

Underwood: What is this, now?

Allen: Appears... (He shivers.) ... and feels unauthorized.

(Sora sits Gandalaria on one of the library benches.)

Sora: Hope she'll be okay.

Arin: We're gonna have to come back and try to save her and that town.

(Arin and Sora leave the library and then Sprite appears and approaches Gandalaria. In the monastery courtyard Ras floats and Arin and Sora see it.)

Sora: (To Arin.) What is he doing?

Arin: I have no idea.

(Ras is summoned by his master in a red void.)

Ras: Yes, master, it will be done. (He comes back and stops floating.)

Arin: Oh, h-- hey, Ras. The, uh, Sorceress is settled in. As safe as can be, I guess.

Ras: I do not care about her well-being. She has served her purpose. Now... to the Underworld.

(They begin to leave the monastery.)
