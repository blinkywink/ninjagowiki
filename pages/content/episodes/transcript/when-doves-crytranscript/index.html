(In Djinjago, Arrakore talks to the Ninja and Zanth.)

Arrakore: (He picks up a banner montage from the floor.) Recognize this banner mount, Zanth? I wielded one just like it when we rode into the Battle of Two Skies. (Zanth grumbles roar.) (He chuckles.) So, I'm glad to have guests. Tea?

Sora: Actually, Arrakore, we have to get to a place called the Cave of Tones and wake a dragon, or, like, half a dragon? We don't know if it's the front half or the back half.

Arrakore: Aw, Sora, please. You'd only need to wake the Arc Dragon if you were going up against the Shatter Dragon of Chaos, which, I mean... (He laughs.) ...good luck if that ever happens, be-- because-- (He understands the situation.) Oh, for real? You're fighting Thunderfang? Well, that's tough. Oh, but at least he didn't die and go to the Spectral Lands, where his power could... (He chuckles and then understands the situation again.) Oh, he did? (Frak confirms.) Well, you know... good luck, anyway, I guess?

Sora: Please tell me you've restored enough of Djinjago that the Cave of Tones is back.

Arrakore: I sure have. Right there. (He points to the side and reveals the cave.) But there's no half-dragon in it.

Wyldfyre: (She groans.) Why not?

Arrakore: My wish magic isn't yet strong enough to bring back living things, like the Arc Dragon, or my people, so I've had to settle for reviving our cultural artifacts. The upside? I've brought back some really excellent pottery. The workmanship! (Sora picks up a vase and Arrakore takes it back.) Delilah doesn't like to be moved! I told you, it gets pretty lonely.

Sora: Maybe all your magic needs is the right motivation, like saving the world. Arrakore, I wish for the Arc Dragon of Focus to be restored.

Arrakore: (He breaths deeply.) Your wish is yours to keep... (He tries to use his wish magic, but fails.) maybe. (Zanth approaches them.) Actually, with Zanth's new boost of Djinjagan magic, we might have enough power... if we work together. (Zanth agrees.) A wish of this magnitude will take time and complete concentration. That means no distractions. Even loud noises could destroy our focus and ruin the wish.

Frak: The world hangs in the balance, so you won't hear a peep from us. We're Ninja. Stealth is our thing.

(Arrakore nods and enters, along with Zanth, the Cave of Tones. Wyldfyre appears wearing a pair of metal shoes.)

Wyldfyre: These metal shoes are not comfortable, but I like the noise they make! Clang! Clang! (Sora and Frak look at her.) What? Did I miss something?

(At the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Geo is thrown against Master Wu's portrait and is helped by Mr. Frohicky.)

Ras: Leaving you alone, defenseless, gone on more important business. Does it hurt to realize the Ninja care so little for you?

(Geo and Mr. Frohicky get angry.)

(Sora and Frak continue trying to memorize the spell song.)

Sora and Frak: ♪ Da-da-da-da-da... ♪

Frak: No, it-- It's... ♪ Da-da ♪ (Riyu grumbles.) It-- It's... ♪ Da-da ♪ You go-- You go up after.

Wyldfyre: ♪ Bum-ba-ba-bum-bum Bum-bum! ♪

Sora and Frak: Shush!

Wyldfyre: (Whispering.) Oh, right. How are we supposed to remember this boring song if we can't make any noise?

(A novelty car horn blaring "La Cucuracha" is heard.)

Sora: (To Wyldfyre.) No, you're way off. (She and Frak stare at her.)

Wyldfyre: That wasn't me.

(The horn is heard again and is revealed to be Rapton and the Freebooters, who land on Djinjago.)

Rapton: (He gets down from his hover chariot.) Rapton lives!

Sora, Wyldfyre and Frak: Shh!

Rapton: Shushing Rapton? How rude!

Wyldfyre: What are you goons doing here?

Rapton: Thievin'. All this Djinjago loot is Djinja-going home with us! Heh. So, why are we whispering?

Sora: Because... these relics belong to a powerful Djinn asleep in that cave. He'll be real angry if he wakes up.

Rapton: Huh, okay, an angry Djinn would cut into Rapton's profit margin. (He sighs.) Freebooters, spread out and grab all the treasures you can, quiet-like, but remember, if you find that jeweled box, it belongs to me.

Sora: We can't let you steal this stuff.

Rapton: There's time to fight.

(The Freebooters wield their Imperium Swords.)

Frak: What about noise?

Rapton: (He scoffs.) Everyone knows Rapton is a Mighty Combat Panther of Stealth! (He knocks Sora down.)

Freebooter: (Whispering.) Attack!

(In Mysterium, Nya, holding the coconut copy, dodges a rock.)

Powerlid: No gem-stealing, nin-joker! (He picks up another rock and prepares to throw it at Nya.)

Nya: Hold up, big guy. We-- (Coconut copy clears its throat.) I mean, I... I don't want your gems, because it's just me. I just need into the cave behind there to ring bells, or else the world will be destroyed.

Powerlid: World destroyed? But Powerlid needs world! That's where Powerlid keeps his gems! Oh, no! Powerlid just said out loud where he keeps gems. Anyone could've heard. (He jumps and covers his gem crate with his body.) Security risk!

Nya: Uh... okay, so I'll just go in.

Powerlid: Yes, little bi-clops, you go save world. Only cost you four gems.

Nya: You're charging me? To save the world? I demand you let me pass!

Powerlid: "Demand"? Great! Now cost eight gems! Powerlid practice surge pricing at times of peak demand.

Nya: But I don't have any gems!

Powerlid: Then kiss rock! (He throws the rock at Nya, but a fireball destroys it.)

Kai: Leave my sister alone! (He comes running, does a somersault and lands in front of Nya.)

Nya: Kai? What are you doing here?

Kai: I came to help! Sora and Wyldfyre told me Jerk-face Jay bailed, and you're doing a mission alone. (He looks down and notices the coconut copy.) Nice coconut.

Coconut copy: Not so bad yourself, (She winks at him.) handsome.

(Kai is confused when he sees that the coconut can talk.)

Powerlid: Gandy? That you?

Coconut copy: Nope. Uh, uh... No, no, Pow-Pow, just a normal, non-magical coconut.

Powerlid: Stop lie, Gandy. It been a while, but Powerlid recognize voice of former BFF anywhere, and it good to see you again, because now Powerlid will get revenge! (He tries to crush them with a punch, but they dodge.)

(Inside the Cave of Tones, Arrakore concentrates to grant the wish, but a loud noise nearly breaks his concentration. Outside, the Ninja and the Freebooters continue to fight in silence, with Sora facing off against Rapton, Riyu stopping two Freebooters from stealing a treasure chest, and Frak facing off against another.)

Frak: Shh! Keep it down!

(During the fight, they nearly drop a teapot on the ground, but Frak catches it in time, preventing Arrakore from losing focus. Frak knocks the Freebooter to the ground, Wyldfyre fights another, and Sora continues his fight with Rapton.)

Rapton: Hey, seen a jeweled box about yea big?

Sora: No, what's in it?

Rapton: A little something called "mind your own business." (He kicks Sora away.)

Sora: I'll show you my business! (She grabs Rapton, spins him around, and puts him in a chair that spins and he stares at a teapot.)

Rapton: (To the teapot.) What are you looking at, buddy? (He looks at the table, sees a small pentagonal box and gasps in delight.) My treasure! (He takes the small box.)

Sora, Wyldfyre and Freebooters: Shh!

(Powerlid tries to crush Nya, but she dodges.)

Powerlid: Sorceress hurt Powerlid's feelings, (He tries to crush Kai, but he dodges.) so Powerlid will hurt everyone's everything! (He tries to crush Kai again but then turns to Nya.)

Kai: (He stops Powerlid with a fireball.) Wait, Powerlid! (He runs to Nya's side.) I used to be just like you!

Powerlid: (Confused.) Then you got a second eye?

Kai: What? No. No, I mean, it used to be easier for me to confront my enemies than my feelings, but I learned this-- lashing out dulls emotions, but not for long. Those bad feelings come back worse than ever.

Powerlid: Then what solution? Bash rocks?

Kai: (He chuckles.) I wish it were that easy, but no, it's communication.

Powerlid: (He sighs heavily.) Gandy? You were Powerlid's best friend, then one day you supposed to come on gem hunt, never show up. Powerlid try to find you. You just gone. You abandon Powerlid. Not good friend.

Coconut copy: (She sighs heavily.) It was supposed to be an acappella vocal practice, but you scheduled a gem hunt without asking. It became "gem this," "gem that." Gems crowed out everything else in your brain. You changed!

Powerlid: You changed too! (He takes her.) You coconut now!

Coconut copy: This isn't me, you big dummy! This is my backup personality, but... you're right. I should have talked to you, not just disappeared. Sorry, Pow-Pow.

Powerlid: Powerlid sorry too, Gandy. (He puts her back in Nya's hands.)

Nya: Great. Now, sorry to cut this reunion short, but we need to ring those bells.

Powerlid: No problem. You go. (Kai and Nya start walking away.) Only cost six gems. Friends discount.

Nya: But-- But she apologized. You made up!

Powerlid: Yes, nice, but unrelated issue.

Kai: Powerlid, you can't focus on us. You've got to gather up your gems!

Powerlid: What Red talk about? My gems all safe in nice crate right over-- (Kai throws Fire at the crate and makes it tip over, knocking over the gems.) Aah! My gems! Powerlid coming for you! Make you safe!

Kai: Run, now. Run! Run, run, run!

(As they run, Powerlid collects his gems.)

(Rapton runs while holding the hexagonal box.)

Rapton: (He crackles.) Rapton stays winning!

(Rapton and the Freebooters run to their hover chariots while carrying treasure and are chased by the Ninja.)

Sora: (Quietly.) We can't let them get away. Arrakore trusted us to guard his treasure while he creates the Arc Dragon.

Wyldfyre: He did? I don't remember him saying that.

Sora: It was implied.

(Rapton throws two bombs, which look like spiked balls, and Sora and Frak stop.)

Rapton: Be sure to thank the angry Djinn for Rapton when he wakes up! Ha-ha!

Frak: (Quietly.) We can't let the bombs explode and distract Arrakore!

Sora: (Whispering.) Obviously. (She uses Tech on one of the bombs while Frak throws the other to Riyu and he kicks it into the air and it explodes. Sora finishes deactivating the other bomb and dismantles it.) Phew!

(The Freebooters fly away and Wyldfyre tries to hit them with Heat, but misses.)

Wyldfyre: Should we go after them?

Sora: (She takes off her mask and Frak takes off his hood.) No, we should wait for Arrakore.

Arrakore: Your wait is over. (He and Zanth apperar.)

Frak: Did you do it?

Arrakore: Go see for yourself. (He and Zanth look exhausted.) Meanwhile, I'm going to sleep for a week.

(Zanth lies down on the ground and sleeps with Arrakore.)

Sora: Well, what are we waiting for?

(They walk to the Cave of Tones.)

(In the Spectral Lands, Arin, inside his Spinjitzu Battle Mech, stands on top of Lloyd's Jet Mech, in jet form, as they advance.)

Lloyd: We're getting close. We should head down before Thunderfang spots us.

(They land in the village as Lloyd's mech changes into mech form.)

Arin: The well should be right up there--

(They see Thunderfang continue his evolution into a Source Dragon.)

Arin: Thunderfang's all over that well, like a lattice crust on a snowberry pie. How are we supposed to get the Unbidden Storm down in there?

(Thunderfang continues to gain more power.)

Lloyd: Even if we find a way to distract him long enough, he must have this place heavily-guarded.

Arin: Like, by them?

(Arin points upwards to see Drix, Rox, Nokt, and eight Spectral Dragonians floating overhead. Rox sends the Spectral Dragonians to attack him and Lloyd, but the mechs' Prismatic Ore coating prevents damage and they manage to fend them off.)

Lloyd: (He laughs.) All right! Prismatic armor works! Remind me to thank Sora.

Arin: If we get the chance.

(Rox, Nokt and Drix advance on them.)

(Ras walks towards Geo and Mr. Frohicky.)

Mr. Frohicky: Be brave like the ninja, brave like the ninja... I, Alfonzo Elias Frohicky, Assistant Keeper of the Monastery, will not permit you to--

Ras: Permit me? My strength permits me.

(Mr. Frohicky uses his tongue to grab Ras's hammer and tries to take it from him, but Ras jumps, pulls him with him, spins in the air and throws him to the ground. Geo stares at Ras.)

Geo: My family is in this Monastery, and I'll defend them to my last breath.

Ras: So not for long, then.

(Ras prepares to attack Geo, but Mr. Frohicky presses a button on the floor and activates the training course, causing Ras to drop his hammer, which Geo catches, hits Ras with it, sends him flying, runs to him and uses his Fusion power to pin his armor to an obstacle. As Mr. Frohicky tries to get up, Barry and Jenny open the door to see what's going on.)

Mr. Frohicky: (To Barry and Jenny.) Go! Hide!

(Barry and Jenny agree, close the door, and hide. Ras destroys his own armor and frees himself.)

Ras: I don't need a weapon to destroy you. I can defeat you with my bare hands.

(Geo tries to hit Ras with the hammer, but he dodges and kicks the hammer out of Geo's hand, then he tries to hit Ras with his hands, but he blocks, kicks him away and then grabs him.)

Ras: You are just as lost as those children you fail to protect!

(Ras throws Geo up, then jumps, lands, and pushes him down, making him pass out. Then he picks up his Source Dragon Icons from the ground, walks towards Mr. Frohicky, presses the button, and deactivates the training course, trapping Geo inside.)

Ras: (He grabs Mr. Frohicky.) You will take me to Wu's-- (Mr Frohicky smiles.) What is so amusing?

Mr. Frohicky: Something's coming from the east.

(Ras looks back and is knocked off his feet by Rontu's tail, releasing Mr. Frohicky, who steps back, then Ras gets up.)

Ras: (To Rontu.) I once trapped a Source Dragon. Am I to tremble in front of a decrepit old lizard on her last legs?

Rontu: A Tiger Tribe warrior without honor? Zarkar himself would be ashamed to find his last descendant sunk to-- (Ras shows her his Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength.) (She gasps.) A Source Dragon Icon? (Ras wields his other Source Dragon Icon in his other hand.) Two icons? Impossible.

Ras: (He strucks the icons to power.) Send my greetings to Egalt.

(Ras and Rontu engage in a fierce fight, which the former ultimately wins. Afterwards, he grabs Mr. Frohicky, who was trying to escape, by the foot and drags him.)

Ras: Now, show me to Wu's study.

(Rapton and the Freebooters continue flying through the skies.)

Rapton: (He crackles.) Rapton would risk a thousand henchmen's lives (He opens the hexagonal box.) for you, (It is revealed that inside the box is a unicorn figurine.) the greatest treasure of all, a rare Djinjagan variant. My collection is finally complete.

Freebooter: Incoming!

(Rapton puts the box away as he sees Kur, holding a spectral sword, flying toward them. He and the Freebooters clear a path for Kur and she passes through, apparently unaware of them.)

Rapton: Whew! I'd sure hate to be whoever she's after.

(Kur continues moving forward.)

(Rox, Drix, holding a spectral sword, and Nokt continue flying towards Arin and Lloyd, with Nokt firing Brute Force balls at the latter two, but they dodge, then Rox lands, performs Shatterspin and releases an energy wave at Lloyd, but he resists. Arin blocks Nokt's shots and fends off some Spectral Dragonians.)

Arin: Spectral armor or not, I don't know how much longer these mechs can hold up.

(Drix tries to attack Lloyd from behind using his spectral sword, but he notices him, then grabs him, spins him around and throws him at Rox, and they crash into a house.)

Arin: Whoo-hoo!

(Nokt approaches Arin and tries to take the Unbidden Storm from his hand, but Lloyd grabs him and crushes him to the ground. Nokt struggles to free himself.)

Lloyd: I'll hold them off! Get down that well!

(Arin tries to go to the Well of the Lost, but is cornered by the Spectral Dragonians.)

Arin: How? (He blocks their blows.)

Lloyd: (He notices something.) Wait... Bleckt said there's a whole cavern beneath the well, right?

(Nokt uses his power to make an explosion and breaks free. A Spectral Dragonian attacks Lloyd, but he blocks it. He slams into the cracks that appeared in the ground when he trapped Nokt.)

Lloyd: (To Arin.) Help me! (He hits the ground as Arin approaches him.)

Arin: You sure this is the best ide--?

(Nokt, Rox, Drix, and the Spectral Dragonians approach, then Lloyd and Arin jump and crash into the ground, causing it to collapse and them to fall into the cave.)

(Sora, Wyldfyre, Frak, and Riyu enter the Cave of Tones (Djinjago) and see the tan half of the Arc Dragon of Focus sleeping.)

Sora: Arrakore and Zanth, they did it!

Frak: (He gasps.) Look! (He points up and shows the bells.) Okay. (He runs to a rock and picks up a hammer.) Song-spell time.

Sora: (She takes the hammer from him.) Slow your roll, Mr. Eager. Let's rehearse and get this right. ♪ Hmm-hmm-hmm... ♪

Frak: ♪ Da-da-da... ♪ ♪ La-da-da La-da-da-da... ♪

Frak and Sora: (To each other.) You're messing it up!/That's not right!

Wyldfyre: You're both wrong! It was... (She sings "La Cucaracha.")

Frak and Sora: That was Rapton's horn!

Wyldfyre: Oh, right.

Sora: Oh, no. In all the chaos, we've forgotten the song!

(Nya, holding the coconut copy, and Kai enter the Cave of Tones (Mysterium) and see the purple half of the Arc Dragon of Focus sleeping. Nya hums the spell song.)

Coconut copy: That's a Spell Song of Waking, all right. Very powerful magic.

Kai: Uh, one small problem. (He points up and shows doves instead of bells.)

Nya: Where are the bells?

Coconut copy: Uh, bummer. The bells have been in Mysterium so long, the natural magic of the land must have transfigured them. Meh, it happens. Bells into doves, purses into piglets, shopping bags into wandering theater troupes.

Nya: How are we supposed to ring a dove?

(Sora, Wyldfyre, and Riyu try to remember the melody of the spell song.)

Frak: We can't keep going back and forth. We're running out of time!

Sora: You don't know the melody. You weren't even there, Frak.

Frak: I listened the first time you taught me. I have perfect pitch.

(Frak climbs a ladder and hits the first bell with the hammer and then the second. A wrong-toned buzz is heard and the whole cave glows red.)

Sora: Looks like we only have three chances... (She points up to show three crystals on the ceiling, with one glowing red.) and we've already wasted one.

(Kai squawks to try to communicate with one of the doves, but it pecks him and makes him fall, though he manages to hold on to the ladder.)

Kai: I never thought I'd say this, but I wish Fugi-Dove were here.

Coconut copy: Doves are the worst. No self-respecting spellcaster would have one as a familiar. Now, Janet had a dove. Trained it to befoul my cauldron whenever I--

Nya: Wait, you say this Janet trained her dove? How?

Coconut copy: Snacks. All simple-minded creatures are motivated by food.

(Kai comes down the ladder.)

Kai: (He grumbles.) Oh, don't mention food. I'm so hungry! I could actually eat these gross wafers Geo made. (He takes one of Geo's wafers from his pocket and bites into it, much to his disgust.) Ugh, never mind. No, I'm not.

Nya: You have snacks? Gimme those! (She hands the coconut copy to Kai, takes the wafer from his hand, climbs the ladder and offers it to the dove.) Come on... Polly want a... this thing? Come on, birdy, we need this! (The dove eats the wafer and chirps a note, making the Focus crystals glow.) It worked!

(Sora rings one of the bells with the hammer.)

Wyldfyre: You got this! Dominate those bells! Raar!

(Sora rings two more bells with the hammer and, before ringing another, hums the melody.)

Frak: By all means, take your time. Not like the world is going to end!

(Sora, having finished humming the melody, rings the bell with the hammer, causing the cave and the second Focus crystal to glow red.)

Frak: (He sighs.) One chance left. (He goes to Sora.) I'll take it from here.

Sora: I can do it! I only made one mistake.

Frak: Which I watched and learned from.

Wyldfyre: (She takes the hammer from Sora's hand.) Nope. You've each gone already. My turn! (She jumps to the next bell and prepares to ting it.)

Frak and Sora: Stop!

(Wyldfyre and Nya, each in one of the two Caves of Tones, play the notes of the spell song, making it play itself shortly after and awaken the two halves of the Arc Dragon of Focus, which roar and teleport.)

Frak: Did it works?

Sora: Maybe? Let's hope everyone else is doing their part now.

(Lloyd frees himself from the rocks of the collapse.)

Lloyd: Arin!

Arin: I'm okay!

(Lloyd picks up Arin's stones and helps him to his feet.)

Lloyd: Arin, I'm sorry. I was focused on getting us down here, I didn't think about the consequences.

Arin: I'm fine, and look! (He points to the side and shows the beam of energy from the healing elixir emanating from the ground.)

Lloyd: Is that...?

Arin: Let's find out. (He and Lloyd approach and realize where they are.) We're below the Well of the Lost! You did it! Now we just need... The Unbidden Storm! I must have dropped it in the fall!

(They return, look for the Unbidden Storm, and Arin finds it, cracked, under a rock and gives it to Lloyd.)

Lloyd: It broke during the cave-in. I ruined our last chance to stop Thunderfang.

(Outside, Thunderfang continues to gain more power and transform, laughing in the end.)

(End of episode. For more information, click here.)
