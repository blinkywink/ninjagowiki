The Spell at the Waterfall/Transcript

(In the dragon stables beneath the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Zane walks up to Mr. Frohicky while holding a frog man doll.)

Zane: Mr. Frohicky, I have questions.

Mr. Frohicky: I'm just decorating this portal gate.

(Mr. Frohicky turns around and looks at the doll in Zane's hand.)

Mr. Frohicky: Oh, you mean the doll I left you? It's to work out conflict! If you have an issue with me, talk to the doll like it's the real me.

Zane: But it is not the real you. I would just speak to the real you if we had a conflict.

Mr. Frohicky: But your heightened emotions could prevent a productive conversation!

Zane: I do not experience heightened emotions.

(Mr. Frohicky adjusts the lanterns he put on the portal gate.)

Zane: And please, do not block the gate with those lights.

Mr. Frohicky: Well, you told me this thing doesn't work because the gate it's attached to in Imperium is not assembled.

Zane: The portal gate appeared from out of nowhere, caused by mystical energies we do not yet understand. It could activate at any time.

Mr. Frohicky: Well, then, whoever arrives will be impressed with my fancy lighting!

(The orb on the portal gate glows. Mr. Frohicky gasps as the doors open. Cole and the Finders knock over the ladder Mr. Frohicky was standing on and then land on top of him. They land in front of Zane.)

Cole: Zane, we— why do you have a frog man doll?

(Zane looks at the doll.)

Zane: It is a long and barely comprehensible story.

Cole: Doesn't matter.

(The Finders get up from Mr. Frohicky.)

Cole: We need your help.

Zane: What seems to be the problem?

(Two of the Wolf Mask Claw Warriors suddenly emerge from the portal gate.)

Cole: Them.

(The two Wolf Mask Claw Warriors lunge at Cole and Zane, who knock them out with their Spinjitzu. Mr. Frohicky and the Finders hide and watch the fight behind some barrels and crates. Zane and Cole continue pushing the Wolf Mask Claw Warriors back toward the portal gate. A gold orb suddenly appears and flies over Mr. Frohicky and the Finders. Cole and Zane knock the Wolf Mask Claw Warriors back into the portal gate. The gold orb touches the orb on the portal gate and closing it. Cole sighs in relief.)

Cole: Way to shut the portal down!

Zane: I did not do that. Perplexing. Almost as perplexing as Lord Ras' Wolf Mask fighters chasing you.

Cole: You know who they are?

(Cole, Zane, Mr. Frohicky, and the Finders relax in the relaxation room.)

Cole: Wait, so this Lord Ras guy took over Cloud Kingdom? Well I don't think he's gonna stop chasing us until he gets Bonzle here. We need to find a way to protect her.

Zane: I agree. But it would help us to know how to protect her if we knew why Ras wanted her so badly.

Bonzle: Yeah, about that. I guess Ras could be after me because of my secret.

Geo: What secret?

Bonzle: That I'm not really a person. I'm actually an extremely powerful ancient spell capable of banishing ultimate evil, but I've taken on the form of a physical being.

(Cole, Zane, and Geo are surprised.)

(At the training plateau, Kai and Sora fight each other in their elemental mechs while Lloyd and Wyldfyre fight Nya, Arin, and Riyu. Arin dodges Lloyd's Spinjitzu and a blast of heat from Wyldfyre, and Nya sends a blast of water at Wyldfyre's heat, creating a large puff of steam, knocking both of them back. Sora initiates a blade on her mech's arm, and cuts the arm of Kai's mech off, attaching it to her own mech after disconnecting her own arm. She uses it to launch fire attacks at Kai, knocking over his mech. Rontu whistles. Everyone stops fighting.)

Kai: Nice move there, Sora.

(Sora gives the arm of Kai's mech back.)

Sora: Eh, it helps when you actually built the mechs you're fighting in.

(Sora and Kai disassemble their mechs.)

Rontu: Thanks for the combat demonstration. Our evaluation of your basic battle skills is complete.

Egalt: (Grunts and snarls.) "Basic" is an understatement.

Rontu: Rising Dragon Technique is not just a set of skills that rivals Shatterspin. It is a guiding principle and cannot be achieved until one is a Third Fang Ninja.

Nya: Um, we've never heard of a Third Fang Ninja.

Kai: Or even a First or Second Fang.

Egalt: That's because we are the only ones who award Fang rankings.

Rontu: We're going to break you up into groups based on your Spinjitzu skills, because the motion of Spinjitzu is the foundation of Rising Dragon Technique. I'm the First Fang teacher. Sora, Riyu, you're with me. Once I teach you how to do Spinjitzu, you'll go to Egalt, who is the Second Fang teacher.

Egalt: You others get over here. I'm supposed to try and teach you Rising Dragon Technique, which is probably a waste of my time.

Sora: Go get 'em, Arin. Show them how it's done.

Arin: I know you'll be in our group in no time, Sora.

Wyldfyre: You didn't put me in any group, Rontu, so I guess that means there's like a Hundredth Fang level and I'm already there, right?

Rontu: You're clearly very talented, Wyldfyre, but your injuries are too fresh. You need to be patient and heal, or you won't be able to ever properly train again.

(Rontu gives Wyldfyre a twig.)

Rontu: Here, give that leg a rest.

Wyldfyre: You're just leaving me out because you already have a Fire Ninja!

Rontu: You think you're a second Fire Ninja? You realize there's only one Master of an element at a time.

Wyldfyre: That's what Lloyd said, but, hello?

(Wyldfyre presents her power to Rontu.)

Wyldfyre: Explain that.

Rontu: Kai is the Elemental Master of Fire. You are the Elemental Master of Heat. Fire and Heat go together, but are separate things.

Wyldfyre: Ohh. That explains a lot actually. Like, a lot a lot.

Rontu: You didn't even know your own element. You have much to learn even after your injury heals.

(Back at the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Cole, Zane, Mr. Frohicky, and the Finders question Bonzle.)

Cole: So, you're not a person, you're a magical spell?

Bonzle: Yep, who took physical form.

Geo: Wait, is that how you knew the magic holding us in the Land of the Lost Things was gone?

Bonzle: Yep. Spells, even ones that look like flesh and bone, or just bone, can sense other spells. Right before Ras came, it just disappeared.

Cole: Why didn't you tell us any of this before?

Bonzle: I— I was afraid of what you'd think if you knew about my past.

Zane: It seems the lives of everyone in the Merged Realms are now at risk. You need to tell us the truth, Bonzle.

(Bonzle goes away and quickly comes back with a shadow play.)

Cole: What's... all this?

Bonzle: I was afraid I'd have to tell my story one day, so I prepared the best way to do it. I've rehearsed the heck out of this. (Clearing throat.) My story starts with the great Dragon Masters. The legendary warriors came to the most skillful Sorceress in the magical realm of Mysterium because they needed a spell of intense power. They were about to confront the most dangerous beings to ever live, the Forbidden Five, Elemental Masters who turned to evil and were planning to conquer all the realms. The Dragons were confident they could defeat the Five in battle, but they feared no prison would hold the villains. So, the Sorceress worked day and night to weave a spell that would banish the defeated Five to a place even more permanent that the Realm of the Departed. That spell was me.

(In the shadow play, the Forbidden Five charge at the Dragon Masters, who used Rising Dragon Technique and defeat the Five.)

Bonzle: After the battle was over, the Forbidden Five were banished, and all the realms rejoiced. But no one wondered about me. Most spells disappear after they're used, the magic all spent. But I was a very strong spell, a very complicated spell. I didn't disappear. I even learned how to feel, and what I felt wasn't good. I traveled the realms for centuries, not sure what I was looking for, never losing this terrible, unnamed feeling.

(The shadow play turns into a flashback. In the flashback, Bonzle, still as a spell, ends up at a waterfall, where an old man was meditating while balancing his left leg on a stick.)

Bonzle: Until one day, I met someone who pretty much changed my life forever.

(The old man that was meditating is revealed to be Wu. He turns around and looks at the spell.)

Cole: Master Wu?

(At one of the training plateau's training courses, Rontu is training Sora.)

Rontu: Remember, spin-kick and through!

(Sora runs and goes through one of the hoops.)

Rontu: Good! Next one!

(Sora then continues to jump up and go through the next hoop and lands on her feet.)

Rontu: Yes! Go three for three!

(Sora attempts to go through the final hoop.)

Sora: No!

(Sora fails and jumps up as normal. She ends up falling short and landing roughly on the hoop before falling down onto the ground. Rontu and Riyu go over to her, and Riyu grabs her arm.)

Rontu: Strong spin-kick game, but you need more elevation. Lower bend and faster spring. Got it?

Sora: I'll get it. Am I going again?

(Rontu shakes her head.)

Rontu: Nope. Riyu's turn.

Sora: Uh. You sure? The— The hoop course doesn't seem, uh, perfectly suited to Riyu's body type.

Rontu: I'm sure. (To Riyu) Starting line, Riyu. Time to elevate and dominate!

(Sora pets Riyu.)

Rontu: Remember, spin-kick and through!

(Riyu bumps into the first hoop.)

Rontu: Reset. Going again!

Sora: Oh, uh... did no one tell you that Riyu can't fly yet?

Rontu: This exercise has nothing to do with flying. It's to teach your body the rotational movements used to launch a Spinjitzu tornado.

Sora: But he can't jump through those hoops. He's just a dragon.

Rontu: Just a dragon?! The dragons were the first species of creation!

Sora: No, I didn't— Sorry, I just mean, look at the hoops. Those are way too high for a dragon like him.

Rontu: I've trained many dragons on this course. I even trained on a course like it when I was his size.

Sora: Why? Dragons can't do Spinjitzu.

Rontu: Oh, really?

(Rontu performs Spinjitzu, spinning the hoops around.)

Sora: Woah.

(Sora and Riyu cheer.)

Rontu: I learned Spinjitzu from Ekesian Coldfang the Wise, who learned from the great King Urd Leatherwing, who had learned from the First Spinjitzu Master himself. And now, you will learn it from me.

(Riyu growls.)

Rontu: Reset! Going again!

(Wyldfyre watches from behind a tree.)

Wyldfyre: Dragon Spinjitzu, so cool. If anyone oughta be learning from dragons, it should be me! I'm basically half-dragon. (Wyldfyre grumbles. She hits a tree and a leaf falls on her face.)

(Elsewhere in the training plateau, Egalt is training Kai, Nya, Lloyd, and Arin.)

Egalt: Shatterspin is an obsession with strength. Strength is important, but only fools believe strength is all-important. Many things can be more powerful than strength. Intelligence. Love. Sometimes even kindness. Another example... (Egalt demonstrates the Rising Dragon Technique.) motion! Motion, used correctly, can be more powerful than sheer strength. Now, eliminate all motion. Be as still as the mountains. (Egalt walks around the ninja.) How fast are you moving?

Kai: I am perfectly still, Master.

Egalt: Wrong. You feel like you're still, but in reality, you speed through space. The land on which we stand revolves. The entire world is in motion. The universe is motion. But that is all outside of you. Except for the movement of the universe itself, are you motionless right now?

Lloyd: Yes, I am, Master.

Egalt: Wrong again. The body is always moving, inside. The lungs inflate. The brain fires. The muscles buzz with tension. Our bodies are motion. Motion outside. Motion inside. There is no life without motion. Even if the motion is unseen, you must feel it inside you.

Kai: OK. But why?

Egalt: Because, little fire man, only once you connect the motion of the universe to the motion inside yourselves can you achieve the Rising Dragon Technique. (Kai yelps as Egalt demonstrates the Rising Dragon Technique again.) And that is your only hope of saving the world from the Forbidden Five's Shatterspin.

(Back at the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Cole and Zane are surprised to hear that Bonzle met Master Wu.)

Zane: Master Wu never mentioned meeting a spell like you.

Bonzle: Our meeting probably wasn't a big deal to him. To me, it was everything.

(In the flashback, Wu is meditating while balancing on his staff. He hears something and gets off of his staff.)

Past Wu: Who's there?

(Wu spots Bonzle.)

Past Bonzle: I'm sorry. I'll go.

Past Wu: Oh. (Wu jumps toward Bonzle.) Excuse my harshness, little one. You moved like a whisper. (Wu examines Bonzle.) An unchained spell? Hmm. I've heard it was possible, but never expected to see one.

Past Bonzle: I never expected to be one, but here I am. I've been wandering the realms for centuries, but you're the first person who could see me.

Past Wu: Amazing. I'd imagine you have many tales to tell.

Past Bonzle: Less than you might think.

Bonzle: We spent hours talking. It was the first time in my long life that I'd connected with another living thing. He could even give me a name to the bad feeling I'd had.

Past Wu: It's called loneliness.

Past Bonzle: And you've felt this loneliness before?

Past Wu: Many times. Once, very terribly.

Past Bonzle: What happened?

Past Wu: I have a brother, and we were very close, going on adventures together, making our way through the Maze of the Sphinx and facing the Warriors of Felis. We were united and bonded.

Past Bonzle: Sounds amazing.

Past Wu: It was. But then my brother made decisions that drove us apart. Now we are no longer on the same side.

Past Bonzle: I'm sorry to hear that.

Past Wu: For a long time afterward, I felt so alone. But now I have students that I teach, friends that have become like a family to me.

Past Bonzle: Friends can become like family?

Past Wu: Definitely. Finding people, building friendships, that cured my loneliness. And, I bet that would cure yours.

Bonzle: Wu suggested I change myself into a physical form so I could interact with others. But I had a concern.

Past Bonzle: I'm one of the strongest spells ever woven, but to manifest a physical form would use up a lot of my strength.

Past Wu: There are more important things than strength.

Past Bonzle: I took Master Wu's advice. (Bonzle transforms into a skeleton.) I chose a form I felt would suit me, someone who originally felt empty on the inside.

Past Wu: Well, there you are, little one. How do you feel?

Past Bonzle: I feel like, for the first time ever, I've become who I was destined to be.

(Back at the training plateau, Sora successfully goes through all three hoops.)

Sora: I did it? I did it!

Rontu: Good job, Sora. Riyu, you're up!

(Riyu snarls in confirmation and runs toward the hoops. He fails.)

Rontu: Good effort. Go again, Riyu!

(Wyldfyre appears in a disguise behind Rontu.)

Wyldfyre: (exaggerated English accent) Oh, excuse me. Pardon the interruption, good peoples. It is I, a completely uninjured Mucoid Warrior, coming to partake in the dragon training I be seeing.

Sora: Uh, Wyldfyre, what are you doing?

Wyldfyre: (exaggerated English accent) Wyldfyre? Never heard that name— oh, but she's a great fighter, I bet!

Sora: Wyldfyre, we know it's you.

Wyldfyre: (exaggerated English accent) Nope, no. I'm a Mucoid. See my shell?

(Wyldfyre shows her "shell" made of a giant leaf.)

Rontu: You've got the heart of a winner, little champion. But training before your body is ready is worse than no training at all.

(Wyldfyre throws her glasses and walks away angrily.)

Wyldfyre: Stupid training to save the stupid world. Don't care about the world anyway. It's filled with jerks.

(While Wyldfyre walks up the stairs, Riyu successfully goes through the first hoop off-screen.)

Sora: Riyu, you did it! (Laughs) Yes!

(Wyldfyre sighs as she walks away in frustration.)

(Elsewhere in the training plateau, Kai and Nya are using Spinjitzu around the ramps. Kai and Nya end up at the ramp, prompting for Lloyd and Arin to Spinjitzu.)

Egalt: Go!

(Lloyd and Arin Spinjitzu around the ramps.)

Egalt: Rising Dragon Technique isn't just about being in motion, it's about being aware of the motion around you, moving around the flow of others.

(All four Spinjitzu around the ramps. Kai accidentally hits Nya's Spinjitzu with his Spinjitzu.)

Nya: Watch it.

Kai: Me watch it? Uh, you almost messed up the landing on my stunt, sis!

(Egalt roars and lands between Kai and Nya.)

Egalt: This is not about childish stunts! It's about pushing the awareness of yourselves and your motion out into the Spinjitzu tornado itself.

Arin: Uh, pushing the awareness? I—I'm not sure what that means.

Egalt: (grunts) I knew this was a waste of time. Back to start.

(The four Spinjitzu around the ramps until they land back on the top.)

Egalt: Adequate. Continue.

(The four continue to Spinjitzu around the ramps. Arin accidentally bumps into Nya, who bumps into Kai.)

Arin: Sorry, my bad. (Arin loses control of his Spinjitzu.) Whoa!

(Arin accidentally bumps into Lloyd and knocks him down.)

Arin: E—Everyone! I—I—I'm sorry. I didn't—

Lloyd: It's okay, Arin. Everyone makes mistakes.

Egalt: Don't lie to him. That was not okay. Because you could achieve a primitive form of Spinjitzu, I thought you were ready to train in Rising Dragon. But your fundamentals are too raw. Maybe you need to go back—

Arin: No, wait, Master! I can pull this together. I know I can!

Egalt: Do you not realize the fate of the world is at stake here?

Arin: I... I do. I won't mess up again.

Egalt: You had better not. (Egalt walks away from the ninja.) The session is over. Rest now, then do better.

(Egalt flies away.)

Lloyd: Maybe you and I could do some—

(Lloyd goes to get Arin's attention, but Arin stops him.)

Arin: I'm sorry, Lloyd. I... I need to be alone for a minute.

(Arin walks away from Lloyd, Kai, and Nya.)

(Back at the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Bonzle finishes telling her story.)

Bonzle: So, taking Master Wu's advice, I went searching. And it was in the Land of Lost Things where I finally found what I was looking for. My family. Cole, Geo, you and the Finders filled that lonely feeling inside me with a warmth I'd never known.

(Cole and Geo hold hands.)

Bonzle: I was afraid if you found out I wasn't a real person, you wouldn't want me to be in our family anymore.

(Cole and Geo reassure Bonzle by hugging her.)

Geo: Bonzle, we don't care where you came from. You're part of our family. Nothing's going to change that.

Zane: (clears throat) I am sorry to interrupt this moment, but I believe I know now why Ras wants you. You are the spell that locked away the Forbidden Five. Logic would therefore dictate...

Cole: That you're the spell that could also free them.

Bonzle: Maybe it would have been better if I'd just dissolved after all.

Cole: No, you are loved and needed. We'll find a way to protect you.

Zane: You said you were created in a place called Mysterium. I have never heard of it.

Bonzle: It's a magical, wonderful place. It was one of the Sixteen Realms, so it must be in the Merged Lands now. The Sorceress loved it there.

Cole: Then that's it. If anyone knows how to keep you safe, it'll be your creator. We have to find her.

Zane: A journey to a land we have never heard of, to find a person we do not know, in the hopes she has a way of stopping a villainous plan we only partially understand?

Cole: Yeah, I know it's not the best plan, but—

Zane: This reminds me of the old days. Count me in.

Cole: That's what I'm talking about. Time for a quest!

(In the Cloud Kingdom, Cinder continues to smash clay pots with Shatterspin.)

Ras: More force! Strength is all that matters in Shatterspin, Cinder.

(Cinder smashes another vase. Ras walks over to Jordana, who is looking at her crystal ball.

Ras: Jordana, tell me you found the escaped spell.

Jordana: Tracking her is not easy. Once they went through the portal, I— Whoa.

(Ras suddenly floats up into the air with his eyes glowing. He is brought to a yellow-tinted void.)

Ras: I await your instructions, master.

(Ras' master crackles, giving Ras instructions. Ras is suddenly brought back down in front of Cinder and Jordana.)

Jordana: Lord Ras?

Ras: I have a new command. Select five of the Cloud Monks. They shall be a sacrifice to the Blood Moon.

(Ras walks away from Cinder and Jordana as they look at each other.)

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
