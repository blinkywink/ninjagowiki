(Lloyd, Cole, Nya and Sora are attempting the Cavern Run with the Combo Vehicle. They speed past and dodge bursts of acid shooting at them.)

Sora: Oh, man, let's hope this works!

Cole: Hope what works?

(Sora presses a button on her control panel, causing her glider and Cole and Nya's bikes to separate from the vehicle.)

Cole: Awesome!

Nya: Whoo-hoo!

(The ninja use their split vehicles take different paths across the cavern, with Sora flying overhead.)

Sora: Not bad for something I didn't have any time to test out, right?

Nya: Sure, but a head's up would've been nice! (She dodges a few bursts of acid.)

Sora: Oh, did I not mention this thing splits apart?

Cole: Negatory.

Sora: Sorry. I mean, why'd you think we each had our own little cockpits?

Cole: Just thought it looked cool.

Sora: Come on, we gotta catch up to that Pace Dragon!

(The crowd cheers for the ninja as they watch the action through a large screen.)

Roby: Yeah, baby! Do I put on the sweetest games or what? What-what?

(Bleckt shakes his head disapprovingly.)

(Meanwhile in the Wolf Clan's quarters, Ras continues speaking to Arin and Riyu.)

Ras: The Merge was caused by Master Wu.

Arin: That can't be true!

Ras: But it is. The ninja's precious Master Wu was the very person who caused the Merge. He is solely responsible for your parents going missing, and every other catastrophic effect of the Merge.

Arin: No way. Master Wu was a wise man. He wouldn't have caused all that.

Ras: (He chuckles.) Arin, you still see the world as a tiny child does.

Arin: Yeah, right.

Ras: To you, the ninja are always good, and I am always the villain.

Arin: Okay, I agree with that.

Ras: (He sighs.) Real life doesn't work like that. There is light in the darkness and darkness in the light.

Arin: So I'm supposed to believe you're some kind of hero?

Ras: Well, I've done harsh things. But always in service of reversing the Merge.

Arin: That's really been your goal this whole time? Undoing the Merge?

Ras: Only by re-separating the Merged Lands will we be able to save them from each other. It must be done. And the process could bring back all of those who were lost.

Arin: I... I don't trust you.

Ras: I don't need you to believe me now, Arin. Because I know the day will come when you see I am right. Let's hope you do before it's too late. (He walks away from the pair.)

Arin: You mean, we're free to go?

Ras: You always were.

(Arin and Riyu leave, and Ras laughs to himself.)

(The Pace Dragon flies through the cavern, still far away from the ninja who are trying to follow it with their vehicles.)

Cole: I don't see the Pace Dragon anywhere.

Sora: (She maneuvers her glider through the gap between two stalactites.) Don't lose hope. I need a better vantage point. (She flies higher.) Found it!

Nya: Is it close?

Sora: I'd say it's the opposite of close. Pour on the speed! (She accelerates.)

(Lloyd drives the middle section of the Combo Vehicle over a large pile of rocks and approaches the Dragon.)

Cole: Ha! Pace this, Dragon! (He jumps to the central section of the track with his bike, and Nya follows his example.)

(The four vehicles follow the Pace Dragon into a narrow tunnel.)

Sora: Come on, we can pass it inside this tunnel... (She flies ahead.) Uh oh!

Cole: What "Uh oh?" Don't just say "Uh oh!"

Sora: There's been a cave-in!

(The Pace Dragon swoops through a narrow gap in the rock wall blocking the path.)

Lloyd: There's no other way through!

Nya: So what are you suggesting? Driving right into this wall?

Sora: Great idea, Nya!

Nya: What? That was not an idea!

(Sora presses the button again, causing the vehicle to recombine.)

Lloyd: I hope this works...

Cole: If it doesn't, we'll find out real quick.

(The Combo Vehicle smashes through the wall, and the ninja cheer.)

Sora: I was guessing this thing would be strong enough when combined.

Nya: Master Wu did say, "Only unity can save us."

Cole: Do you think he was talking about driving through a rock wall?

Nya: Probably not.

Lloyd: Come on! We gotta beat that Dragon!

Sora: Hold on to your masks! (She presses another button, causing the vehicle to accelerate rapidly as two massive jets of flame shoot out from it.)

(Everyone in the audience gasps when the Combo Vehicle comes into view. The vehicle quickly overtakes the Pace Dragon and crosses the finish line.)

Roby: What the what? They did it! That was ultra stang-zoot! (Zane, Wyldfyre and Geo run to congratulate the others for winning as they exit the vehicle.) I hereby gotta say, the ninja are officially still in the games!

(Lloyd lets out an exhausted sigh as Zane pats him on the back.)

(Arin is outside, looking thoughtfully at the sunrise as Riyu comes next to him.)

Arin: Thanks, Riyu. I know I should talk to Lloyd, but he'll never believe his Uncle Wu could've done something that bad. (Riyu growls.) Yeah, Ras is probably lying. But what if it's even partly true? Frak trusts him, and he was always a great judge of people. And Ras is right about one thing. I'm not the ninja I should be. You really think Sora faked my Object Spinjitzu and didn't tell me? (Riyu shakes his head.) Yeah. Yeah, Ras is probably lying about that too. I hope.

(Riyu starts to comfort Arin, but suddenly hears Kai.)

Kai: Riyu? Can you hear me? We need help. (Riyu looks away and starts roaring.)

Arin: Hey, buddy. Thought we were having a moment here, but okay. You do you. (Riyu runs off.)

(In the Games Plaza, a match between Euphrasia and Zeatrix is underway, where the two are standing on small platforms floating through the air. Zeatrix blasts Euphrasia with her element, but she dodges.)

Lloyd: Come on, Euphrasia...

(Zeatrix dodges a burst of Wind, and charges at Euphrasia while blasting Shockwave. Euphrasia dodges the attack by moving to the side, and then briefly manages to throw Zeatrix off balance with Wind. Zeatrix manages to regain her balance and blasts Euphrasia away, but she catches onto her platform at the last moment.)

Roby: Whoa! Close one! (Euphrasia climbs back up to blast Wind at Zeatrix again, only for her to dodge.) Remember, whoever knocks their opponent off their platform first equals, winner!

Zeatrix: (She jumps up to avoid another burst of Wind.) Cloud Monk! What do your precious scrolls say about this match?

(Zeatrix fires a short burst of Shockwave at Euphrasia, who uses her power to flip over it and levitate in the air.)

Euphrasia: The Writers of Destiny no longer believe our words dictate all outcomes. We also use our action (She tries to hit Zeatrix with wind, but Zeatrix is able to dodge.) to help decide our fate!

(Zeatrix flies up and dodges another burst of Wind from Euphrasia.)

Zeatrix: Well, my actions will decide yours! (She jumps down from her platform and blasts at Euphrasia while falling down to her, managing to knock her off the platform and onto the ground.)

Roby: We have a winner! (He uses the Elemental Cup to transfer Euphrasia's power to Zeatrix, who laughs and uses her new power to slightly float into the air.)

Zeatrix: The Elemental Power of Wind! A perfect compliment to my powers! (She lands back down and walks away from Euphrasia and approaches Ras.) See how I handled that monk? That's nothing compared to the destiny I'm gonna write for you, Ras. (She leaves, and Ras growls at her angrily.)

(In the next match, Tox is facing off against Nokt. There is a ball on the center of the arena, and two rings behind the contestants. Tox rushes in to grab the ball as Nokt stands still. She prepares to use her power as she approaches him.)

Roby: Uh-oh! A cloud of knockout gas so thick you could chew on it!

(Nokt stopms on the ground twice, activating Shatterspin. Before Tox reaches him, he launches a wave of energy that blasts her back. He runs forward and leaps next to her, causing another wave of energy to knock her away before she can get up. Nokt continues to attack her several times while she's down.)

Roby: Nokt wins! (Nokt continues attacking.) Whoa, whoa, whoa! Security! Stop, or you're disqualified! (Two Security Bots fly in two break up the fight, followed by two Medical Bots carrying a stretcher. Nokt walks away, and Lloyd, Cole, Zane and Nya show up to help Tox.)

Nokt: (He chuckles as he approaches "Jordana".) Told you it would be no problem, sister.

Sora: (She also runs in to help Tox.) Sister?

(Elsewhere in the City of Temples, Frak is training.)

Frak: One, two, strike. One, two, strike.

Arin: (He comes in) Hey, Frak.

Frak: Hey, man! Sorry I haven't been around much, my master put me on an intense training regiment. I haven't even seen many games.

Arin: Frak, why didn't you tell me Ras was your master?

Frak: Why? Do you know him?

Arin: Yeah. He's an enemy to the ninja. Rasd hunted Dragons for Imperium, he captured the Cloud Kingdom, he-

Frak: Uh, maybe we're talking about a different guy named Ras? Because he's been nothing but good to me.

Arin: Just tell me, have you used one of those Wolf Masks with the gong?

Frak: Oh, I wish! Shatterspin is amazing! But my master says first I need to get my skills up. That's why I've been training so hard.

Arin: Don't do it ever! The gong actually shatters the goodness inside you.

Frak: You're joking, right?

Arin: Those masks will make you more powerful, but they corrupt you. Poison you. Ras only cares about power, no matter what the cost.

Frak: That doesn't sound right. Everything Ras has told me to do has built me up. Look, I'll show you. Follow my movements. (The two begin exercising.) Ras says everyone spends too much time trying to find harmony. Hiyah! But you can't passively wait for the world to harmonize... Hiyah! So you must take from life what you need. Hiyah!

Arin: Well, I do feel like sometimes life is just dragging me along. Hiyah! I've been able to do a wonky form of Spinjitzu for a while. Hiyah! (He performs rudimentary Spinjitzu.) But I haven't progressed at all.

Frak: My master says we must force the progress we need. Hiyah! Force the world to work the way you want it to. Power through! Hiyah!

Arin: Hiyah! (He performs Spinjitzu, and is able to achieve a proper tornado. He spins around Frak several times before stooping in front of him.) I did it. I did it! A real Spinjitzu tornado! (He high-fives Frak.)

Frak: Nice!

Arin: You have no idea how long I've been trying to do that! That felt amazing! That felt... right!

Frak: And all it took was a little bit of my Master Ras' teachings. (Frak's phone buzzes, and he pulls it out.) Speaking of which, my master wants me to come to the plaza. Maybe I'm competing today! Let's go!

(Arin sighs before going Frak.)

(In the plaza, another match is starting, with Lloyd facing against Mr. Pale. Lloyd blasts Mr. Pale with his power, but he dodges the blast while turning invisible with his powers. Lloyd looks around as Mr. Pale appears near him and approaches.)

Roby: Ha! Lloyd looks absolutely green with envy at Mr. Pale's light-bending inviso-power!

(Mr. Pale jumps up and slams into Lloyd as he lands, but Lloyd recovers from the attack. He then proceeds to run at Mr. Pale, only for the latter to unleash a powerful burst of light, temporarily blinding Lloyd.)

Lloyd: Ow! When did you learn to do that?

Mr. Pale: (He appears behind Lloyd to punch him, but Lloyd dodges and tries to punch him back, only for him to disappear again.) You're not the only one who's been training! (He appears behind Lloyd to attack again, but Lloyd anticipates this and turns around to block, leading the two to engage in hand-to-hand combat.) We gotta lot of catching up to do, buddy, (He disappears again as Lloyd is about to attack him.) after I bring home that trophy!

Lloyd: Now this is just getting annoying. (He uses Spinjitzu to kick some dust up into the air, revealing Mr. Pale's location. He then proceeds to kick him off the arena they were fighting in.)

Roby: It was impossible not to see that victory! Lloyd wins!

Mr. Pale: Well-played, Greenie. Well played.

(Roby drains Mr. Pale's element, causing his face to become visible.)

Lloyd: Wow, since you've always been invisible, I've never actually seen your face before. That's an interesting beard.

Mr. Pale: Yeah, you try shaving with invisible skin. (He chuckles.)

Roby: (He grants the power of Light to Lloyd.) Now Lloyd has the powers of Mr. Pale. Care to prove it?

(Lloyd vanishes, and the crowd cheers.)

Crowd member: Where is he? (He laughs and looks at Ellie next to him, then looks elsewhere.)

Roby: Zero time to chillax, (He tosses the Element Cup to Bleckt.) 'cause the next two competitors are up! First, he's the ninja who flirts with dirt, Cole, Elemental Master of Earth.

Nya: Huh.

Cole: Really.

Roby: And his competitor, the snake who shakes and breaks! He's Frak, Elemental Master of Quake!

Nya: Why is Arin with him?

Sora: Hey, Arin... (Arin doesn't speak to her.)

(Frak and Cole approach each other.)

Frak: I'm here! It's an honor to compete with you, Cole! Sorry I was late, Roby!

Roby: No problem. No one loves a dramatic entrance more than me! (The arena starts changing.) This event puts the hurt in hurdle! To win, you gotta be the first to ring the bell! You ring, you cling onto victory. Stang-zoot!

(Bleckt rings the bell with his cane, starting the match. Both competitors leap onto the first hanging wall, and start climbing. Cole quickly clears it and jumps onto the next, but Frak uses Quake, causing all of the walls to starting shaking. After clinging on for a short while, Cole falls off the wall.)

Roby: Oh, no! Is it over for Cole?

(Cole uses Earth to rise back up on top of a large rock formation. He then trows smaller rocks at Frak with his power, but Frak dodges them by jumping across the walls. Frak then uses Quake, and everything starts shaking. Cole jumps onto a wall and attempts to use his power on it.)

Cole: Can't control metal! Whoa! (He starts losing his grip.)

(As the fight is going on, Sora approaches Arin.)

Sora: So now you believe me that Frak is on Ras' team? I told you I wouldn't lie to you.

Arin: Are you sure about that?

Sora: Huh?

Arin: Someone told me that during the Blood Moon Ritual, I didn't knock Ras out with my Object Spinjitzu. That you used your power to make me think I did.

(A loud crash is heard from the fight and the audience gasps.)

Sora: I... I did. I'm sorry. Nya was about to be sent into the Nether-Space. You were our only hope and I couldn't let you fail. And you were so frustrated when you got demoted in training, I- I just wanted you to have a win.

Arin: So not even my best friend thought I could do it on my own.

Sora: No, it's not like that, I... (She puts her hand on Arin's shoulder, but he shoves her away and walks off.)

(Meanwhile, Cole and Frak are racing to climb the hurdles. They both get within jumping range of the bell, with Cole being a little bit closer. Frak uses his powers, and everything shakes.)

Cole: It's like this whole thing is built for Frak's powers.

(Both of them jump for the bell, and Frak manages to grab on and ring it.)

Roby: Photo finish! But this photo has a clear winner. Frak is victorious!

Frak: (he laughs.) You have no idea how exiting it is to have met you, Cole. You've always been an inspiration to me.

(Roby drains Cole's powers.)

Cole: Uh, thanks?

Frak: (He receives the power of Earth.) I can't believe it! (He lifts up a large rock formation, and jumps on top of it.) It's like when I used to pretend to be a ninja, but it's a real power! Woo-hoo!

Lloyd: You fought hard, Cole. You should be proud.

Cole: Not to be that guy, but did this seem weighted against us?

Lloyd: Yes. And remember how we didn't get invitations in the first place? I'm having some suspicions.

(Later, Lloyd is training with Nya and Sora.)

Lloyd: We have some time before the next game, but we need to keep our skills sharp. Every time we lose, Ras gets closer to whatever his evil endgame is.

(As the trio does some hops, Sora leaps into the air and lands on her toes using Balance.)

Lloyd: How did you pull out that landing?

Sora: I've got the elemental power of Balance now, remember?

Lloyd: Sora, I can tell you're distracted. And if you're distracted, you'll never achieve Spinjitzu.

Sora: I know, I know. I just- I have a lot on my mind right now. (She looks over to Arin.)

(In another part of the City of Temples, Ras is talking with "Jordana".)

"Jordana": Are you sure you should've told the ninja brat all of that, Lord Ras? Information can be power.

Ras: And information can be poison. I omitted some of my intentions, but everything I told him was true. And that truth will poison his mind.

"Jordana": Ah, and that poison will spread to the other ninja. And drive them all apart.

Ras: Exactly. Because they are the only threat to my main goal in this tournament: gaining control of all the Elemental Powers. That is the only way I can create the Dragon Icons I need.

"Jordana": Is a Dragon Icon how you lured and trapped that Source Dragon under Imperium?

Ras: Yes. Without the proper Icons, summoning Source Dragons is impossible. So we cannot suffer any more losses at the hands of the ninja, like Jay Walker did. We must win by any means necessary.

"Jordana": Of course, Master.

(Nokt is watching as the two speak.)

(At the Games Plaza, the next round is about to begin.)

Roby: And we're back! Every game, the competition gets competitiver, the intensity gets intenser, and the fun gets funner! And that's not gonna stop now with our next battle. You know her, you love her. Nya! (The crowd cheers.) Versus the mysterious man of few words and fewer fears. Nokt!

(Nokt puts his mask on and walks away from where the rest of the Wolf Clan are. He and Nya approach each other, and begin walking side by side.)

Nokt: Where's your brother? Not here to cheer? Or what of that other useless ninja? The one you supposedly love?

Lloyd: He's just trying to get into your head, Nya.

Wyldfyre: Well I'm gonna get into his head with my fists!

Cole: Wyldfyre, your anger's not helping.

Wyldfyre: It's helping me stay angry!

(The arena for the match rises up. It includes many small platforms on a floor filled with spikes.)

Roby: This battle is ramping up to be legendary! During this fight, three... Count 'em, one, two, three holographic gems will appear randomly. The goal: whoever gets the most, wins!

(Nya puts up her mask and takes a stance. She and Nokt charge into the arena, with her using Spinjitzu to shove him away and jump up a ramp.)

Cole: Yeah! Go, Nya!

(The tiles under Nokt activate and start spewing flames, making him swiftly move away from them and jump into the air. Nya jumps away from where he lands and swings to the other side of the arena using a pole. She then shoots bursts of Water which Nokt easily evades while moving to the side and jumping at her. She backs up to dodge him, and evades a kick by jumping away. The two of them look around and see the first gem appear. Nya jumps towards the gem as fire shoots from under her, but Nokt leaps towards her and kick her away in midair, allowing him to grab the first gem.)

Roby: One gem for Nokt, and a big, fat zip for Nya! But the game's just started! Anything could happen!

Nokt: I was expecting the other member of your family.

Nya: You think Kai was though? You've never seen me when I'm mad.

(Another gem appears, and before Nokt can do anything, Nya hits him with a blast of Water. She then jumps up and shoots him with Lightning, and he is knocked backwards, letting Nya grab the second gem.)

Roby: And just like that, it's a tie game! Whoot-whoot-stang-zoot!

(Nokt stomps the ground twice, furious.)

Lloyd: Get your Rising Dragon ready, Nya, he's gonna Shatterspin!

(Nokt activates his Shatterspin just as the final gem appears between the two contestants. He shoots a wave of energy at Nya, but she jumps over it and uses Rising Dragon, blasting a beam of energy at Nokt's Shatterspin barrier before crashing into him and pushing him away.)

Wyldfyre: Yes! Destroy him so hard, Nya! (She looks at Cole and hesitates.) But not angrily. A friendly destroy.

(Nya jumps across the arena trying to get to the gem, but Nokt jumps at her and kicks her back. He is then about to jump down on her, but she recovers and evades him, tossing a few blasts of Water that he evades before getting close and grabbing her by the hand and tossing her aside. Nokt is about to reach the gem, but Nya knocks him away with Spinjitzu and blasts him with Lightning, knocking him to the side of the arena. Nya goes to grab the gem, but it disappears and reappears next to Nokt, allowing him to grab it.)

Roby: And Nokt is victorious! What an amazing, incredible battle!

Nya: What? But the gem disappeared right before I was about to get it!

Roby: Sorry! Rules of the game!

(Roby uses the Elemental Cup to transfer Nya's powers to Nokt, who then shoots both Water and Lightning into the air.)

Lloyd: Okay, I know this doesn't look good.

Wyldfyre: Yeah, it looks super terrible.

Lloyd: Thanks, Wyldfyre.

Wyldfyre: You're welcome.

Lloyd: But ninja never quit, right? (Sora nods before looking back at Arin, who walks away.)

Roby: Up next: Sora, Elemental Master of Tech and Balance! Versus Cinder, Elemental Master of Smoke and now Heat! (Wyldfyre growls at Cinder as he approaches them. Lloyd and Sora nod at each other, and Sora runs next to Cinder, with both jumping onto a platform.)

Cinder: I've been waiting for a rematch since the Wyldness, girl. (Sora puts on her mask.)

Roby: This isn't just a test of elemental skills. It's a challenge of the mind! The competitors must solve these puzzles, including a swing over a fiery pit!

(As the match is starting, Arin walks over to Ras.)

Arin: You were right. Sora admitted she used her powers and let me think I did it myself.

Ras: You'll see I'm right about a great many things, Arin.

Arin: Jay Walker worked at the Administration. I think he knows where my parents disappeared to.

Ras: Any information Jay Walker has, I also have.

(The bell rings, and the match starts. Cinder blasts at Sora with Heat, but she dodges by jumping back. Cinder then uses Smoke to approach Sora as she uses Technology to transform a nearby device into a staff, which she uses to hold Cinder in place and toss him down, allowing her to move to the first puzzle.)

Sora: Only a few ways these could work. (She flips the first two switches, then runs across to do the same to the other two, causing the pillar in the center to extend and fall down, creating a bridge.)

(Cinder gets up and runs at Sora, who splits the staff into two parts, throwing the top part in the air and the bottom part at Cinder. He manages to dodge the one headed straight for him, but the other one extends out and creates a small energy barrier, which falls and traps him inside.)

Sora: (She runs across the bridge.) You always fall for that trick!

Cinder: You think I'm not prepared? (He pulls out a Wolf Mask and puts it on, as Ras rings the Gong of Shattering with the mallet.)

(Sora takes a ball-shaped object from the top of the pillar, and places it in the center of the next platform. A new pillar with a combination lock rises up.)

Sora: There are like a gazillion patterns to this one! Ugh. Let's try something simple. One, one, one, one, one.

(The code works, and the pillar sinks back down. A floating rope appears at the end of the platform. As this is happening, Cinder bursts out of the barrier with Shatterspin, and uses Smoke to get near Sora just as she jumps to the rope and swings across. The gap is too big for Cinder, who falls into the flames as Sora makes it to the end.)

Roby: Sora wins!

(The spectators, especially the other ninja, cheer as Roby uses the Elemental Cup to transfer Cinder's powers to Sora. She fires off a small burst of Smoke before running to the rest of the ninja. Arin looks at this, mildly annoyed.)

(During the following night, a sprite flies into the ninja's quarters, where it seeks out Cole and wakes him up.)

Cole: Huh? (He gets out of bed and follows the sprite outside.)

(Meanwhile, Arin is practicing his Spinjitzu as Ras watches, smiling.)

(The sprite leads Cole away from the City of Temples.)

Cole: What are you? Where are you taking me? There's nothing here. If I had my powers, I'd make an earth bridge and follow you to whatever is out there...

(The sprite parts the clouds, revealing a monastery on a mountain nearby.)

Cole: Another monastery?

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
