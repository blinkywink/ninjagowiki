(In the Administration, the agents are shown being hollow, except for Allen, Underwood, and a Munce, who are hiding in an office.)

Underwood: (To Allen.) Any sign of the illicit invaders?

Allen: No seeing a peep with my peepers. (He grumbles.) I supposed that doesn't make a difference, considering we couldn't even see the attackers when they were attacking us last night.

Underwood: (She scoffs.) Invisibility requires reams of permits and background checks. Who could our assailants have been?

Allen: Who knows, but if they return, they won't be invisible to how hard I'm gonna bludgeon them with fines.

Munce agent: (He speaks in panic.) They can't come back. I can't handle it if they come back! What they did to Carl B, and Debra, and the entire HR Department!

Underwood: Shh! Don't panic!

Munce agent: Look at them, their minds, their souls, are gone! It can't happen to us, I have so much to live for! Well, a few things to live for. I love my ergonomic chair.

(The sound of a magical flute is heard as the lights flash.)

Underwood: The unauthorized visitors are back!

(Underwood and Allen grab their blasters as Drix floats in and plays a magical flute.)

Allen: Let's hit that scofflaw harder than I hit the fourth floor vending machine when it doesn't release my snacks!

(Underwood, a female agent, and a Merlopian have their souls sucked out and are hollowed out, followed by the Munce agent. The souls float into Drix's magical flute.)

(Drix returns to the Spectral Lands with the Spectral Dragonians, where he reunites with Thunderfang and the other Forbidden Five and is shown that they are all now ghosts and each of the Five possesses a magical flute. Drix plays his magical flute and deposits the stolen souls into the Well of the Lost, mixing them with the healing elixir and causing tablets with the symbols of the Source Dragons to charge.)

Thunderfang: The Source Dragons will rue the day they rejected me. (He laughs evilly.)

(In Mysterium, Ras, Arin and Sora are on their race bikes searching for the Underworld and then stop.)

Sora: The sun's setting already? Feels like the days are getting shorter.

Arin: Maybe it's a magical Mysterium thing? (He takes off his hood.) Ras, why are we stopping?

Ras: (He takes the map of Mysterium out of his pocket and looks at it.) The dwellers of the Underworld do not appreciate the presence of the living, so entrances to their land don't remain in the same place for long.

(The Underworld location disappears from the map.)

Arin: I thought the Spectral Lands were where people passed through to whatever's after this life. So then, what's the Underworld?

(Sora begins using her Elemental Power.)

Ras: Similar, but only disgraced warriors end up there, which is irrelevant to our goals. (Sora secretly uses her Tech Power on his bike and stops.) All we need is access to the Forge of the Forgotten.

Arin: And this Forge will bring my parents back?

Ras: The Forge will enable us to create a dragon icon to summon a Source Dragon, the first step to--

Arin: Reversing the Merge, I know. I just want to get to the part where you bring my parents back.

(The Underworld location reappears elsewhere on the map.)

Ras: (He looks at the map.) I have our heading. (He puts the map in his pocket and tries to use his bike, but ends up losing control, much to Sora's joy.) Enough, Sora!

Sora: (She sighs.) You're no fun.

(Sora starts using her powers and Arin looks at her with disappointment. She fixes Ras's bike and he goes ahead.)

Arin: (To Sora.) You shouldn't make him mad. I need him.

Sora: He needs us, Arin. He wouldn't be dragging a couple of kid Ninja around with him if he wasn't desperate for help.

Arin: Just don't mess this up for me.

(Arin puts on his hood and follows Ras. Sora is sad for a moment, but soon puts on his mask and follows them. They stop when they get near a village inhabited by Skulkin and Skulkin robots.)

Ras: We're here.

Arin: (He takes off his hood and Sora takes off her mask.) Uh, you sure about that?

Ras: Yes, somewhere here is the passage to the Underworld.

(They see a Skulkin entering a trapdoor.)

Sora: It's not even being guarded?

(Ras gets off his bike, approaches the trapdoor and opens it, but the passage to the Underworld has disappeared. Arin and Sora approach him.)

Arin: Okay, did we just see a mirage? I don't exactly know how mirages work.

Sora: There has to be some way in.

(They turn around when they hear a stomp and a Monster Skull approaches them.)

Sora: This thing doesn't look happy to see us.

Arin: Maybe we're falling into a stereotype about scary skeleton monsters. It could be friendly. (To the Monster Skull.) Excuse us! We're just looking for a way into the Under--

(The Monster Skull starts attacking them.)

(In the Community of Lee, Jay and Nya are walking and carrying paintings.)

Jay: Ah, what a class! Who knew I could paint? I didn't know I could paint.

Nya: I told you you could. You have a lot of skills, actually, because you used to be a Ninja.

Jay: Ugh, not this again. Just when I thought you were kind of fun.

Nya: "Kind of"? Oh, I am a lot of fun. You've become the fuddy-duddy.

Jay: "Fuddy-duddy"? Did you learn that word from our dance lessons with the retirees?

Nya: Maybe. Those lessons were the bee's knees.

(They approach the home.)

Nya: (She opens the door.) Lloyd! Wyldfyre?

Jay: You mean Doyll and Fyldwyre!

Nya: Right. We're home! Hmm. (The house is empty.) They should have been back by now. Hopefully they found a lead on Arin's parents.

Jay: Hopefully, but the Land of Lee does have a lot of distractions.

Nya: Let's look for them, just to make sure Wyldfyre isn't eating the ice cream hotel.

(They leave their paintings inside the house and go out, while being followed by two Lees.)

(Lloyd and Wyldfyre are trapped in a cage by Lee.)

Lee: It's no use trying to escape that cage. It's designed to optimize your journey to friendship. Just like Elway here, steadily having all the negative thought-pollution about me cleaned from his mind. The things I'll do for my friends, really.

Lloyd: I knew you had to have brainwashed everyone down here, Lee!

Lee: What? Of course I didn't do that! Everyone in the Land of Lee has chosen to be here! (Lloyd doesn't believe him.) Well, almost everyone. Once in a long while, some no-goodnik figures out the "truths" I sing about the Merge are not based in actual fact, and so those few have to be... corrected.

(The Dream-Guyver finishes brainwashing Elway and releases him. Lee presses the button on a hanging remote that opens Elway's cell, and he begins to leave.)

Lee: Hello, Elway! Hope you enjoyed your meditation! Just wondering, what do you think about your old pal, Lee?

Elway: Lee is a loyal, kind, and completely non-deceptive friend. (He approaches Lee.)

Lee: (To Lloyd and Wyldfyre.) Don't worry, when my little tentacled buddy gets rested up, you two will be next to learn the magic of friendship.

(Lee and Elway walk away as Lloyd and Wyldfyre watch in anger. He hits the cage and tries to break it using Energy, but it doesn't work.)

Wyldfyre: Heat is better than green. (She shoots Heat at the cage, but it has no effect.) That doesn't seem right.

Lloyd: What can this cage be made out of? Oh, no, not now...

(Lloyd has a vision and appears in the Source Dragons' void.)

Strength Symbol: We cannot get involved with mortal problems. It only poisons us and our powers. Weakens us!

Motion Symbol: Like it or not, Thunderfang is a threat to the Source Dragons.

(Lloyd silently approaches them.)

Life Symbol: Only because we got involved with mortals in the first place. We should never have made that promise to the First Spinjitzu Master.

Strength Symbol: I voted against granting boons to the First, if you remember.

Motion Symbol: We all recall it was a split until the Source Dragon of Balance favored the mortals and became the deciding vote, but Balance is no longer with us, and we must reach a consensus on our own. The only way to truly defeat Thunderfang is through the Arc Dragon of Focus.

Focus Symbol: No. I will not aid this time.

Energy Symbol: If it is so voted, you shall, like the last time.

Focus Symbol: I also regret getting involved with mortals. It has brought us nothing but pain and danger. We should remove our presence entirely.

Motion Symbol: It's too late for that. Thunderfang has more power than you realize. He will force his way onto this council and destroy everything.

Focus Symbol: My answer is still no.

Motion Symbol: You do realize that a mortal could activate the Arc Dragon of Focus.

(Lloyd is surprised to hear this.)

Strength Symbol: Impossible!

Motion Symbol: If both halves of the dragon were released from their sleep and focused upon soul energy...

Strength Symbol: (He sniffs and notices Lloyd.) Is there a mortal here now?

(Lloyd falls to the floor of the void and wakes up from his vision.)

Wyldfyre: How could you take a nap at a time like this? (She crosses her arms and sighs ironically.) Some leader.

Lloyd: The Source Dragons... Thunderfang is back.

(Wyldfyre gasps.)

(Ras, Arin and Sora dodge the Monster Skull's attack.)

Arin: Something tells me they don't want us here, Ras!

(Arin puts on his hood, Sora puts on her mask and Ras jumps and tries to attack the Skull Monster with his hammer, but is hit by it.)

(In the Land of Lost Things, Kai and Cole are training Frak.)

Kai: All right, Frak, you got this. Remember, half the battle is up here.

Frak: You want me to head-butt the bamboo?

Kai: What? No! Just hit them normal, but with mental focus.

Cole: Begin!

(Frak destroys all the bamboo.)

Frak: How was that?

Cole: You're really improving.

Geo: Refreshments!

(Geo and Bonzle arrive with a barrel, a tray of snacks in a bag and teas on top and the first one hands a drink to Cole.)

Cole: Thanks, Geo. Can you fuse that bamboo so we can do another run?

Geo: You bet. (He walks away to fix the bamboo.)

Bonzle: (To Kai.) Tea, two sugars, two more sugars, and honey, just the way you like it. (She hand the tea to Kai.)

Kai: (He gasps.) How'd you know? Hey, who made snacks? (He takes a snack and eats it.)

Geo: I did!

(Kai chokes on his snack as Geo fuses the bamboo back together.)

Geo: Sponge wafers with a hint of mint. Personal recipe.

Kai: This just sucked all the moisture out of my body. (He drinks the tea.)

Fritz: Kai! Kai! (He and Spitz come running with a cart full of things.) Check out what we scavenged from the geyser last week!

Kai: (He says hoarsely.) Can't wait.

(As Cole and Frak walk away, Fritz messes with the cart, causing a Prismatic Blade to fly out and Spitz catches it. Fritz emerges from the cart holding another Prismatic Blade.)

Fritz: Cool weapons, right?

(They play with the Prismatic Blades while Kai looks inside the cart, sees two more Prismatic Blades and takes one of them.)

Kai: (He coughs.) The Prismatic Blades? They went missing when we fought Thunderfang. No one knew where they went, so how'd they wind up in the Land of Lost-- (He realizes what he just said.) Oh, yeah, I hear it now.

(Geo finishes fusing the bamboo and leaves, then Cole and Frak approach it.)

Cole: Frak, this time, I want you to use your Elemental Quake Power to shatter the targets from below.

(Frak activates his powers, touches the ground and makes it tremble.)

Frak: (He yelps.) Uh, it's like the Earth is screaming!

Cole: You can feel it too? I sense it everywhere in the Merged Lands, but it's especially loud here.

Frak: What's causing it?

Cole: Not sure. All I know is a while back, the ghostly voice of Master Wu called me on a mission.

(A flashback shows Cole's last scenes during Absolute Power.)

Past Wu: Cole...

Past Cole: Huh?

(He follows Master Wu's spirit to a desert and climbs onto a sand mound.)

Cole: (Narrating.) After walking for what seemed like forever, I found the place that must be the source of the screaming Earth. (He steps down from the sand mound onto a stone floor.) I could sense something deep under ground, something big. (He uses his Earth Elemental Power and makes the ground rumble.) I tried and tried, but couldn't get the object up. Why would Wu send me out there if I couldn't do anything about it? Unless he wanted me to do anymore, and I just failed him or something?

(Flashback ends.)

Frak: Whoa. What could be down there? Let's check it out right now!

Kai: That sounds like a whole... ugh, what's the word for it? "Journey." I, for one, am not in the mood to go walking forever.

Geo: I have something that might help.

(Geo returns driving a red three-seater vehicle, the Ninja Combat Vehicle.)

Cole: (To Geo.) You made this?

Geo: Just fused together a bunch of parts that wound up at the Land of Lost Things. No big deal.

Cole: You could give Sora a run for her money.

Kai: Okay, (He does a somersault and sits in one of the seats.) a journey it is.

Frak: Yes!

(Arin is knocked to the ground by the Monster Skull, who prepares to crush him.)

Sora: Arin! No! (She performs rudimentary Spinjitzu, jumps, knocks the Skull Monster to the ground, and lands next to Arin.)

Arin: (He gets up from the ground and takes off his hood.) Sora! You just did Spinjitzu!

Sora: Did I? I did, right? Right?

(The Monster Skull gets up, jumps, and tries to crush Arin and Sora, but they dodge it. Ras runs toward the monster Skull and dismantles it, but its skull falls onto its ribs.)

Monster Skull: Mortals! You gained victory through the only thing the dishonorable warriors of the Underworld have ever respected-- strength! Your passage has been granted.

(The Monster Skull opens its ribs and reveals the passage to the Underworld.)

Sora: Bone stairs into darkness... Cool. Love hanging out with you guys.

Ras: (He pushes Sora aside.) Move. (He descends the stairs of the passage, followed by Arin and Sora.)

(Nya and Jay continue to be followed by the Lees, but they notice them and manage to lose them.)

Jay: (To Nya.) Well, definitely following us. Who's the paranoid egotist now?

Nya: Both things can be true. Come on.

(They go to the secret door and Jay tries to gain access, but fails.)

Nya: So this "backstage" area is supposed to be a Lee-only zone?

Jay: Mm-hmm. That's what Ellen Q said at needlepoint class. She also told me she sneaks extra cherries at the ice cream social, but that sounded like her problem. (He uses his Elemental Lightning on the access panel and the door opens.) Got it.

Nya: Pretty good for someone who claims to have never been a Ninja.

Jay: Maybe I was just a good thief.

(They enter the backstage area and the door closes. There, they run through the hallways and pass a Mech Dragon, which catches Jay's attention.)

Jay: Wish I had one of these. (He kicks the Mech Dragon.) In fact, (He takes a control implant from his pocket.) I deserve a little treat.

(He places the control implant inside the Dragon Mech and Nya returns.)

Nya: What did you just do?

Jay: You'll find out.

(They run through the halls again until they enter a room, where they sneak in and see the Lees creating gravestones.)

Nya: Carving gravestones?

Jay: Read the names.

Nya: Strong McCoolGuy. Maya McCoolGuy. Doyll Donmagar. Fyldwyre Donmagar! They're making gravestones for us?

(In the Underworld, Kruncha and Nuckal, with new appearances, are playing a game similar to bowling.)

Kruncha: (He chuckles.) Time to roll the bones! (He rolls the ball and knocks out all the bones except two.)

Nuckal: You got a spare-- rib! (He laughs.)

Ras: You two! (Kruncha and Nuckal see him approaching with Arin and Sora.) Direct me to the Forge of the Forgotten!

Nuckal: Sure. Second left past the--

Kruncha: (He punches Nuckal.) You dummy, why you tellin' some random mortal where the Forge is?

Nuckal: Ah! He asked all forceful. You know I respond to strong leadership.

Kruncha: You bonehead! (To Ras.) Look, mortal, we'll only tell you if you can beat us at a bone rolling challenge. Winner gets--

(Ras slams his hammer on Kruncha and Nuckal's Skulls, causing them to come out of their bodies and fall to the ground.)

Kruncha: Okay, you win.

Nuckal: Ah! Second left past the demon tree. Third passage. You can't miss it!

(Ras leaves and Arin takes Nuckal's skull.)

Arin: Sorry about him. He can get a little intense. (Kruncha's body approaches him and he places Nuckal's skull on it.) Here you go.

(Sora hands Kruncha's skull to Nuckal's body and then they place their skulls on the correct bodies.)

Nuckal: Yeah, we know the deal. We've worked with people like that.

(Arin and Sora catch up with Ras and arrive at the Forge of the Forgotten.)

Arin: Guess that's it. (To Sora.) How do we work it?

(Sora shrugs and approaches the Forge. She tries to use her powers to examine the Forge, but fails.)

Sora: (She gasps.) It's not responding to my powers. Must be magic, 'cause it's not tech.

Ras: It is far beyond technology. (He pulls a bone lever and activates the Forge, causing its molten iron to open a casket.)

Sora: It's big enough to crawl into.

Ras: The Forge is powered when someone enters the Casket of Bones.

Sora: Creepy. How do they get back out?

Ras: They don't.

Sora: Huh? (She steps back as Ras toward her.)

Ras: Why do you think I tolerated your presence for so long?

Sora: I thought you needed my help.

Ras: That's true, in a way.

(Arin stands in front of Ras.)

Arin: No. I won't let you do this.

Ras: Rather it was you, Arin?

Arin: No one dies here today, Ras!

Ras: Using this Forge is the only way to save your parents. Someone is going into that casket. You... or her? (Arin and Sora look at each other.) Have I ever steered you wrong? You were a weakling when I found you, and I turned you into a warrior. Warriors must make difficult choices, especially if they truly love their parents.

(Arin looks thoughtful.)

(Four Lees climb stairs holding tombstones while Nya and Jay watch from outside.)

Jay: (He speaks quietly to Nya.) Be stealthy.

(They walk discreetly, but Jay accidentally knocks over some gravestones like dominoes.)

Jay: Uh... (He speaks quietly.) Stealthy starting now!

(They go upstairs and reach the surface of the Land of Lee, where they see the Lees placing the gravestones with their fake names in the ground. The Lees leave and then Jay and Nya check the gravestones.)

Jay: Pretty surreal to be looking at your own grave marker.

Nya: Does this mean Lee's planning to kill us? It doesn't make any sense.

Jay: (He gasps as he realizes something.) Look!

(They run towards two gravestones and see that they are the ones with Arin's parents' names.)

Nya: Arin's parents? They really are dead. You were wrong, Jay.

Jay: (He looks to the side and notices something else.) Look here. (He and Nya run to a nearby gravestone.) Melba. We met her yesterday at dance class. She's old, but definitely not in that grave. (He stops for a moment.) I've got a hunch. (He uses his Lightning to blast a hole in Arin's parents' grave.) It's empty! I bet all these graves are empty!

Nya: But why?

Jay: What if Lee puts these here to stop anyone who comes looking for lost loved ones? They'd think the person they're looking for is dead and give up. That way, no one would ever find his hidden underground community.

Lee: Hey!

(Jay and Nya see three Lees running towards them.)

Lee: Who's lightninging my cemetery? Count and Countess McCoolGuy? Stop!

(Jay and Nya run away from them.)

(Arin and Ras continue their discussion.)

Arin: This wasn't part of the deal, Ras.

Ras: I never told you our mission would be easy. You have never been closer to bringing your parents back. Show me you have the courage to do what needs to be done.

Arin: It's not fair.

Ras: Have the strength to save your family.

Arin: Sora is my family too.

(Sora smiles upon hearing Arin say that.)

Ras: You are defying me?

Arin: I should have never trusted you in the first place. (He and Sora start to walk away.)

Ras: So be it.

(Ras starts attacking them, and then Arin performs Spinjitzu to counter, but Ras throws him upward with his hammer. Arin tries to kick Ras as he falls, but Ras stops him by grabbing his foot and then spins around. Arin uses Spinjitzu to free himself, sending Ras tumbling to the ground while he manages to land on his feet next to Sora. They brace themselves, and Ras shifts into his rage form. He runs on all fours, grabs Arin and Sora with his hammer, and throws them down, almost making them fall on top of Kruncha and Nuckal, who were going to play the bone-rolling challenge again. Kruncha and Nuckal run away, then Ras falls and tries to hit Arin and Sora with his hammer, but they dodge. They perform Spinjitzu and try to attack him, but he knocks them down.)

Ras: You will enter that casket.

Sora: You can't win, Ras, 'cause we have one thing you don't.

(A bone flies from behind towards Ras, but he blocks it with his hammer.)

Arin: Each other! (He performs Spinjitzu and throws another bone at Ras, but he dodges it.)

Ras: Your loyalty to each other (Arin jumps, performs Spinjitzu again, and throws another bone, which receives Object Spinjitzu, at him.) will be your downfall!

(Ras hits the bone with his hammer and makes it rise, but due to Arin's Spinjitzu Object the bone destroys part of the bottom of one of the floating islands, causing several rocks to fall. Arin and Sora run in one direction while Ras runs in the opposite direction and is crushed. Arin helps Sora up)

Arin: (To Sora.) Let's go before he digs himself out.

(They leave the Underworld through the Monster Skull's passage and return to Skeleton Village on the surface. The Monster Skull's ribs close.)

Sora: (She exhales deeply.) Nothing beats the fresh air of the living world.

Arin: They're gone. (He takes off his hood.) They're gone forever.

(Arin falls to his knees and begins to sob in sadness as he feels he has lost his parents forever and Sora hugs him.)

Arin: (He talks to Sora while crying.) You once told me change can be scary. I'm scared, Sora.

(They continue hugging.)

(Jay and Nya continue to flee from the Lees in the Comunity of Lee. Nya runs through backyards and Jay across rooftops before meeting again on a street.)

Nya: (To Jay.) Now that Lee's onto us, we need to find Lloyd and Wyldfyre, and-- (She gasps as she sees a Lee running toward them.)

Jay: (He takes his Lightning Daggers from his pocket.) I'm guessing Lee can still live even with a few less of his bodies. Let's find out. (He runs toward Lee.)

Nya: Jay, we can't kill him. It's not the Ninja way.

Jay: I'm not a Ninja!

(Jay jumps and Nya hits Lee with water, preventing Jay from killing him. She performs Spinjitzu on the other Lees and Jay jumps and kicks one of them aside. Barry and Jenny arrive and watch the fight.)

Jenny: (She gasps.) That was Spinjitzu. You're the Ninja! Our son, Arin, was your biggest fan.

(Jay and Nya look at each other and gasp as they realize they've found Arin's parents.)

(End of episode. For more information, click here.)
