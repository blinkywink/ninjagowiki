(The ninja swing through the forest with their Dragon Hooks. At the training plateau, Rontu is training Sora and Riyu.)

Rontu: Faster! Quicker! Mental Strength! You can do this!

(The injured Wyldfyre watches Sora and Riyu. She sits on a rock, grunting from the pain. The ninja continue swinging and Arin and Kai bump into each other.)

Egalt: (While flying.) No! You aren't in the moment. Lose your sense of self and surroundings and feel the motion. Be the motion! Never forget the universe is always in motion. Once you align with it, (He spins around.) that's when you'll unlock the skills you never knew you had.

(In the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Bonzle talks to Cole, Zane and Geo while holding the shadow puppet version of her spell form.)

Bonzle: I haven't been to Mysterium in forever, since way before the Merge. I'm not even sure the Sorceress who created me is still alive.

Zane: If she is, she would know the best way to protect you from Ras.

Cole: Cause if Ras gets ahold of you, I'm sure he plans to turn you back into a spell to free the Forbidden Five.

(Fritz and Spitz play video games on the couch.)

Bonzle: So... it's really our only choice.

Cole: What's bothering you, Bonzle?

(Spitz celebrates and tosses the gaming controller behind the couch while Fritz frowns.)

Bonzle: The sorceress. She only knows me as a spell. What if she doesn't believe in me as a real person?

(Cole walks toward Bonzle and puts his hand on her shoulder.)

Cole: The sorceress made everything about you. If anyone would believe in your true self, it's gotta be her.

Bonzle: I... I guess.

(Cole and Bonzle put their heads together. Geo puts one hand on each of their shoulders,)

Geo: Look, I know you all love adventure and danger and risking your lives, but me and the other Finders aren't exactly skilled warriors like you.

Cole: No problem, Geo. You, Spitz and Fritz can stay here.

(The couch is empty.)

Cole: Where'd they go anyway? (He sees the elevator go down.) Uh-oh.

(Fritz and Spitz talk in Hangar Bay.)

Fritz: You sure we should do this?

Spitz: Well, it's do this or be bored.

Fritz: Just say no to boredom. Hit it!

(Spitz, who is sitting inside the SA Mark 01 with Fritz, turns it on.)

Spitz: Amazing! Let's see if it- (The mech starts wobbling slightly.) Whoa, whoa!

(The SA Mark 01 starts wobbling around in circles. Cole, Zane, Geo and Bonzle arrive at the Hangar Bay and see the chaos. The mech knocks over some wooden boxes and the four climb up the stairs. Spitz screams.)

Geo: Fritz, Spitz, get outta there!

Fritz: We'd love to, but we can't stop it!

(The mech starts to spin around. Cole runs down the stairs while Zane uses his Ice power to freeze the mech in place. The ice shatters and sends Zane, Geo and Bonzle flying, causing them to almost fall off the edge. The SA Mark 01 continues to wreak havoc, and Zane stretches out his hand to protect Geo and Bonzle. Geo covers his eyes, bracing for impact.)

Cole: (In the Elemental Earth Mech.) Gotcha! (He wrestles the SA Mark 01 with his mech.) Zane!

(Zane slides behind the SA Mark 01 and presses the button behind its left leg, turning it off. The mech beeps and powers down.)

Cole: Good thing Sora stores my Elemental Mech in the cave. (He jumps out of the mech, and it turns back into a disc. The dis floats in midair and Cole catches it when it falls.) This thing rules. (Cole puts the disc on his back.)

Spitz: (Sighs.) I'm thinking boredom would've been less painful.

(Fritz groans. Later, Zane, Cole and Bonzle step out of the elevator and into the Dragon stables.)

Bonzle: So, (Jiro wakes up.) Jiro here is gonna fly us all the way to Mysterium?

Cole: That's the plan. Zane, can you give Jiro the directions?

Zane: I cannot. (Jiro brushes his snout against Zane.) There are no trustworthy maps of the post-Merge world in my databanks. But I'm sure Bonzle knows how to get there.

Bonzle: I haven't been to Mysterium since it was its own realm. I have no idea where it is.

Cole: So, no one knows where Mysterium is.

(At the Training plateau, Arin throws rocks at a tree while similar rocks lie on the ground. Lloyd walks down a flight of stairs to see Arin.)

Lloyd: Still practicing your technique of giving objects Spinjitzu?

Arin: Practicing and failing, just like my regular (He kicks a rock.) Spinjitzu.

Lloyd: (While walking towards Arin.) Arin, you have more natural Spinjitzu talent than anyone.

Arin: You heard what Egalt said. My Spinjitzu is unstable. It's still as wonky as it was when you met me. I'm gonna get demoted to First Fang, I just know it.

Lloyd: As Master Wu would tell me, "Patience, young ninja."

Arin: But there isn't time for patience, The Blood Moon is coming and if I can't learn Rising Dragon Technique, (Sighs.) I shouldn't even be a ninja.

Lloyd: That's nonsense. (He picks up a rock.) Maybe your path isn't what you expected, (He gives the rock to Arin.) but like I've told you, you have the potential to become the greatest of all the ninja.

Arin: I know, and that's a lot to live up to. (He sighs and drops the rock.) I wish I had your confidence. (Arin rolls the rock around with his foot.)

Lloyd: I'm not feeling very confident these days.

Arin: More visions?

Lloyd: I've never been so anxious. I have no idea when the visions will come, and they just shut me down. If I don't find a way to stop them, I won't be present enough to lead us to confront Ras. (Sighs.) I wish master Wu was here. I bet he never felt like this.

(Egalt trains Kai and Nya. A pile of rocks is stacked in the middle of the plateau with hoops on the left and right.)

Egalt: You can't dwell on where you came from or where you're going. Live in the moment. In this drill, you'll compete against each other. Highest score wins.

Kai: (Points to himself.) Highest score right here.

Nya: Not happening. You're going down.

(Nya performs Spinjitzu and Kai does the same soon after. Nya picks up a rock from the pile. Kai tries to block Nya, but she manages to throw the rock through the hoop behind him.)

Nya: Me one, you zip.

(Kai and Nya perform Spinjitzu. Kai stops to pick up a rock from the pile. Nya uses her Spinjitzu tornado to block Kai, but he does a backflip and throws the rock through the hoop.)

Kai: You flinched! One, one.

(Kai and Nya bump into each other's Spinjitzu tornados. Nya picks up Kai and slams him on the ground in order to reach the rocks. Nya picks one up and throws it but Kai lunges in front of the hoops and kicks the rock away.)

Kai: Denied!

(Kai does a flip over Nya's head and grabs a rock but Nya shoves him out of the way. He drops the rock.)

Kai: Hey! We're playing it like that? Fine!

Egalt: (While Kai and Nya crash into each other's Spinjitzu tornado.) No. You're not harmonizing with the world! You're fighting against it, frustrating each other. Break!

(Egalt flies away. Kai and Nya wrestle before performing Spinjitzu, stopping and walking away.)

(Wyldfyre places a rock with a drawn-on face on the ground.)

Wyldfyre: Isn't this annoying, just doing nothing? (As the rock.) I love doing nothing! (In normal voice.) You're a rock, nothing is what you do. But me, I do action. I'm the most actionist ever. (Sighs.) Being injured is the worst.

(Egalt flies over Wylfyre and the rock. He lands on a platform atop some stairs. Wyldfyre picks up her walking stick and starts walking towards the steps.)

(At the Crossroads, Bonzle and Cole have hood over their heads while Zane is in his detective outfit.)

Zane: It's too bad Arin and Sora are not here. They are far more knowledgeable about the Crossroads than we are.

(Cole bumps into a Lava-Tide wearing a top hat.)

Cole: Whoa, (Chuckles.) hey there.

(The Lave-Tide jumps, waves and breathes fire at Cole before walking away.)

Bonzle: Is it me, or is trying to not draw attention to ourselves just drawing attention to ourselves?

Cole: It'd be bad if we were recognized. Ras could have eyes everywhere, but someone's gotta have a map of the Merged Lands in this place.

(Cole rolls forward on the ground, get ups and sees the All-Realm Antiques Emporium.)

Cole: That place looks promising.

(Bonzle starts walking but bumps into someone. She starts running and Zane does the same. Zane stands in front of Cole and Bonzle.)

Zane: We should not stop here. The owner is not trustworthy. (Fedulian walks up to Zane from behind.) And if he recognizes me, we might be-

Fedulian: Can I help you (Zane uses his cloaking ability to give himself a mustache.) fine customers? (zane turns around.) Wait, do I know you?

Zane: The odds of that are statistically low, given that so many people call the Crossroads home.

(Fedulian looks at them closely.)

Fedulian: Well. I suppose so. Then welcome, stranger. We carry only the rarest treasures here. (He digs through a box.) Can I interest you in an authentic (He pulls out a toothbrush.) toothbrush of the Ancients? Only used once. Uh, twice.

(Zane sniffs the toothbrush while Cole covers his nose.)

Zane: Its smell is... unexpected.

(Fedulian chuckles, tastes and gags at the toothbrush while a fly encircles it before flying away.)

Fedulian: That's just the scent of authenticity, my friend. But I can tell you're a discerning gentleman. Would you like to buy a disco toaster?

(He hands the toaster to Cole and turns it on. It lights up, starts playing music and moves around. Bonzle dances to the music but stops when Zane looks at her.)

Cole: Uh, do you have any trustworthy maps of the Merged Lands?

Fedulian: You've come to the right place.

(Fedulian gags and a map comes out of his mouth. Cole, Zane and Bonzle express their shock, causing Zane's hat and the party toaster to fly in the air.)

Fedulian: Here you go, (Cole puts the disco toaster on the ground.) on the house. (He gives Bonzle the map.)

Bonzle: For free?

Fedulian: I have a drawer full of them. Use it in good health.

(Zane, Cole and Bonzle walk away and Fedulian picks up the party toaster.)

Fedulian: (Chuckles.) Such fools. They should have taken the toaster.

(He leaves the toaster on a box and walks into an ally.)

Fedulian: You are going to have some new guests. In fact, I think you might know them already.

(Zane, Bonzle and Cole ride on Jiro.)

Bonzle: According to the map, we should follow this line over a huge lake.

(They look down.)

Cole: Not seeing a lake down there.

Bonzle: And this cloud wasn't on the map.

(They enter a green cloud.)

Zane: Not a normal cloud. (They start coughing.) Poison detected!

(Jiro rushes to land and Bonzle, Cole and Zane fly off after he does so. Zane does Spinjitzu to land safely, catching Bonzle afterward. Cole lands behind them.)

Cole: Where are were? (He takes off his mask.) Jiro, can you fly us outta here, keeping it low?

(Jiro growls weakly.)

Zane: He has been injured by the poison.

Bonzle: Well, you were right. There is definitely no lake here.

Cole: And no life, either.

(Back at the training plateau, Riyu and Sora are balancing on upright logs with Rontu circling around them.)

Rontu: Master the basics. Fundamentals. You got this! (To Riyu.) I know you didn't expect to train, little one, but as you now know, we Dragon Masters are Ninja ourselves. You have much potential.

(Riyu then balances on one of his front paws before jumping to balance on his back one. However, he loses his balance and fall off, knocking the upright log he had been on over. Rontu lowers her head to Riyu. Sora starts having trouble balancing, and Rontu turns her attention to Sora.)

Rontu: Sora, I sense you're overthinking.

Sora: I know, it's just I feel like I'm doing everything backwards. (Sighs.) I already have an Elemental Power, but I can't do Spinjitzu, and it's a little intimidating when your best friend is some Spinjitzu prodigy.

Rontu: All living creatures walk their own path, even Ninja.

(Sora then promptly loses her balance on the log and falls off, landing on Riyu.)

Sora: (sliding off of Riyu's wing) I am the worst Ninja.

Rontu: (walking over and sitting down, her tail in a circle around Sora and Riyu) Trust me, I've met worse.

Sora: That does not help.

Rontu: You put too much pressure on yourself. Find some calm.

Sora: (Sighs.) If we don't learn Rising Dragon technique, we can't stop Ras. (Sighs.) And I'm the weak link on the team here.

(Wyldfyre approaches Egalt, who is resting.)

Egalt: Disturbing my privacy? You humans are all alike.

Wyldfyre: (flipping on to the top of ramp, sitting on it) Hey, I'm from a family of dragons, and you're like the only one I've ever met who hates people.

Egalt: I have my reasons.

Wyldyfre: Well, I'm going to change your mind about how great people can be just by existing and being my awesome self.

Egalt: Hmm...very few humans have ever achieved Rising Dragon. You realize you are probably all wasting your time.

Wyldfyre: If you really thought that, you wouldn't bother (Taps Egalt's snout with her crutch.) teaching us.

(Egalt grunts, spreads his wings, and then takes off. Wyldfyre watches before noticing a scale of Egalt's had fallen off, and watches it fall to the ground. She jumps off the edge of the ramp, and picks the scale up, looking at where Egalt had taken off to.)

(Back in the poison cloud...)

Cole: (While stroking Jiro's snout.) Stuck in the middle of the desert with no way out. I'm sorry, Bonzle.

Bonzle: About what?

Cole: I'm supposed to be protecting you. The Finders are like my family---(He looks at Zane.) my other family---and right now I'm putting you into more danger than even Ras.

Bonzle: You're not responsible for me, Cole. If I didn't want to come, I wouldn't have.

Cole: But this journey was my idea---

Zane: And we can continue on it. (He pulls out the map from Fedulian.) We still have the map-- (He notices that the line on the map has disappeared.) the line is gone? (He holds the map up for Bonzle and Cole to see.)

Cole: Did that map change? Were we given a trick map?

Zane: That no-account Fedulian! I should have trusted my memory banks.

Bonzle: Well, we were headed north before we hit that cloud. So, if we still go that way, we should reach Mysterium, right?

(Cole and Zane exchange looks, then look back at Bonzle.)

(Back at the Training plateau, Egalt trains Arin and Lloyd using the same exercise Kai and Nya did.)

Egalt: Begin!

(Lloyd performs Spinjitzu, grabs a rock and throws it through the hoop. He stands in front of the rock pile and looks at Arin.)

Arin: (Sighs then says to himself.) Don't mess this up.

(Arin performs a rudimentary version of Spinjitzu and Lloyd uses true Spinjitzu to get in front of the former. Lloyd tries to block Arin, but the latter uses his version of Spinjitzu to knock Lloyd away. Lloyd lands on his feet while Arin takes a rock from the stack. Lloyd and Arin bump into each other's Spinjtzu tornado. Nya and Kai watch from the sidelines. Arin stops and throws the rock, but it hits the side of the ring. Arin pants.)

Egalt: Don't force it, Arin! (He stands in front of Arin.) Force and strength work counter to the motion of the world. The universe has its own speeds you need to align with. Again.

(Arin and Lloyd perform Spinjitzu. Lloyd blocks Arin from reaching the pile of rocks before the latter picks uses his Spinjitzu to grab a rock. The two continue battling using Spinjitzu.)

Arin: Whoa!

(Arin runs his Spinjitzu tornado through the pile of rocks in the center. The rocks break off a tree branch, changes a floating hoop's rotation and almost hits Kai and Nya but they evade them. Egalt avoids a rock, but it bounces off a few trees and hits him in his left eye. He falls to the ground, grunting. Arin continues spinning out of control.)

Lloyd: Hold on, Arin!

(Lloyd performs Spinjitzu and stops to jump on top of Arin, stopping the latter's Spinjitzu. They both sit up and see the scattered rocks and the hurt Egalt.)

Kai and Nya: (While rushing over to Egalt.) Egalt!

Arin: (Gasps.) Oh no.

(Back in the cloud, Jiro, Zane, Bonzle and Cole are walking under the hot sun, looking down at the ground. Cole grunts and Jiro looks around. Jiro provides the three with shade with his wings.)

Cole: Thanks, buddy.

(Bonzle looks ahead and sees a well.)

Bonzle: (Gasps.) Whoa, are you seeing this?

Zane: Indeed. If we are all seeing it, then it cannot be a mirage.

Cole: Come on. Maybe we'll find water or even just some shade.

(The three run towards the well, but the floor beneath it explodes and sends it flying. Dorama's puppet emerges from the sand.)

Cole: Move!

(The three jump out of the way before the puppet attempts to crush them by hitting his hand on the ground. Bonzle lands and the puppet throws some large rocks at her, but Jiro blocks them using his wing and growls.)

Bonzle: What is that thing?

(Zane and Cole land next to each other in the same pose.)

Zane: And what does it want?

Cole: No idea, but I know what it's gonna get.

(Cole puts on their hoods and Zane follows suit. Cole runs toward the puppet and Zane activates his power. The puppet takes the sword on his back and shoves it into the ground, sending Cole flying. Zane blasts ice at the puppet but it evaporates before it can reach it.)

Bonzle: In this weather, your attacks are probably just cool and refreshing.

Zane: Cool and refreshing was not my intention.

(Jiro flies toward the puppet but it smacks him with its arm, and he lands on a rock wall. He falls to the ground and roars. Cole reaches behind his back, but the puppet stabs the ground with his sword again, making a crack that leads to Cole.)

(Cole wakes up and sees an Imperium force field and the ceiling of a stage.)

Cole: (Grunts.) Ow. (Cole sits up.) Well, at least we're alive.

Bonzle: (Sighs.) As alive as a spell can be.

(Bonzle, Cole and Zane stand up and see other Crossroad citizens and algae farmers sleeping and slowly walking. They walk towards the force field with Jiro and others stare at them.)

Cole: Huh?

(Cole squints at the force field.)

Zane: Are we on a stage?

Dorama: (Through speakers.) Oh, splendid, you're awake! Because the show is about (Spotlight turns on and points at Zane, Cole and Bonzle.) to begin!

(Back at the Training Plateau, Egalt's left eye is hurt and Kai, Nya and Lloyd stand around him, with Arin at a distance.)

Egalt: I can't see out of the eye. (He looks at Arin.) Not even sure of this type of injury will heal.

Arin: I'm so sorry. I messed this all up for everybody.

Egalt: I knew you weren't ready, child. You're demoted down to First Fang.

(Lloyd walks towards Arin.)

Lloyd: You'll be back in our group (He puts his hand on Arin's shoulder.) before you know it.

(Arin walks towards Rontu, Riyu and Sora. Sora put her arm on his shoulder.)

(Back at the stage, Dorama, who is staring at the ground, is getting lowered down to the ground by a wire attached to the ceiling.)

Dorama: Ninja, never thought I'd see the likes of (He lifts up his head to reveal his face and points his scepter at Cole, Zane and Bonzle.) you again.

Cole: (To Zane,) Are we supposed to know him?

Zane: I have never personally met him, but I believe his name is Dorama.

(Dorama jumps down and lands in front of the three.)

Dorama: Oh, you are not the ninja I was hoping for. (He walks away with a spotlight following him.) No matter, my revenge will still be sweet. Since it is (Points staff at Bonzle, Cole and Zane again.) your kind that ruined me! Ever since Imperium fell, I've been lost, without purpose. But then, I discovered this place. The perfect location for my next chapter. Dorama's Academy for Acting!

(Fireworks behind and in front of the stage go off.)

Cole: Huh?

(The people watch the fireworks go off and Dorama continues to monologue.)

Dorama: It's not easy finding students. Thankfully, my old friend Fedulian and I made a deal. His fake maps lead people to me school, and after I recruit them, (He lifts his arm.) we (He clenches his hand.) rehearse.

Cole: You mean "capture them"?

Dorama: (Shouting.) I said "recruit!" (Coughs and goes back to normal voice.) All day and all night, we perfect the art and craft of stage work.

Cole: (To Zane while shielding his mouth from others.) Has he always been this preposterous?

Zane: Well, he is a professional actor.

Dorama: After my intensive hands-on performance academy, you'll be ready to entertain the world in my new ultimate stunt show. If you survive, that is. (The other trapped "students" take a step back.) Time to start... (He stamps his scepter onto the wooden floor.) acting!

(Fire comes out from beneath the metal grails on the floor. The people cover their heads and face the wall. Jiro flies over to the people while Zane, Bonzle and Cole run towards them. Jiro stands in front of the people and covers them from the fire using his wing. Zane, Cole and Bonzle avoid the fire.)

Dorama: You're doing it all wrong! I need more fear in your eyes. We have a lot of rehearsal ahead of us, (Turns to face the other side.) but don't be discouraged. We all start somewhere. (He walks away.) Look at me.

(Cole, Zane and Bonzle turn around and huddle together while Dorama keeps monologuing.)

Cole: We have to stop this. I was never the performer in my family and also, I don't wanna be fried to a crisp.

Zane: Imperium electro cage. (Cole and Zane look behind.) It will not let people through. So, if we wanna get out, we have to find a way (They see a control panel outside the stage.) to shut it down from the inside.

(Bonzle walks towards the electro wall.)

Cole: Bonzle, stay back.

(Bonzle walks out of the wall, unharmed. Dorama stops talking.)

Bonzle: Mm. (She turns around.) I'm a spell.

Zane: Perhaps we are not protecting her. She is protecting us.

Dorama: I didn't tell you to explore the space. Stage manager!

(Bonzle turns back to Dorama and looks up to see Dorama's puppet on the roof of the stage. It jumps down and Bonzle yells.)

Cole: Go, Bonzle!

(The puppet attempts to grab Bonzle but she runs under it. The puppet smashes into the electro wall.)

Dorama: (To his puppet.) Get her!

(The puppet turns around. Bonzle runs up to the control panel and pulls a lever, deactivating the electro wall. Zane and Cole put on their hoods.)

Zane: (To the people.) Run! It is too dangerous here!

(The people start running.)

Cole: Jiro, get them somewhere safe.

(Jiro flies.)

Dorama: No self-respecting school allows the students to escape. (To his puppet.) Stop them!

(A person bumps into Dorama from behind. Purple Imperium Drones come out from behind the stage's speakers. Cole and Zane nod to each other. Cole jumps on top of one of the drones and they both perform Spinjitzu and destroy two of the drones in the air. They land and the two drones fall behind them. The people, who are now standing behind a truck, watch two drones come up to them. Jiro blasts the two drones, stands in front of the people and roars. The people get on Jiro's back, and he flies away. Dorama's puppet tries to grab Bonzle but she realizes in time and leaps out of the way. She climbs down the steps. Cole and Zane jump down from the stage, and they all run towards one another. The puppet tries to hit the three with its hand, but they jump out of the way. Cole lands and throws the disc on his back into the air. The disc transforms into the Elemental Earth Mech and Cole climbs into it. He smashes the mech's two fists together. The puppet grabs the sword on its back and swings it around. The puppet attacks but Cole uses the mech to grab the sword. The mech stomps and uses earth to push the sword from below, causing it to fly in the air and drop to the ground. The mech blasts the puppet but it is unaffected.)

Dorama: You think (Cole turns the mech to Dorama.) you could destroy my stage manager with dust a second time?! I have made upgrades! (Laughs.)

(The puppet bangs on its metal chest plate while running towards Cole. The mech stomps, takes parts from the ground and throws it at the puppet but it easily combats or avoids them. The puppet hits the mech, knocking it down to the ground. Dorama laughs while Zane and Bonzle run towards Cole.)

Dorama: Yes! Now I'm buying your performance. (The mech gets up.) You look absolutely terrified. Use that, go with it.

(Cole looks at the beams on the stage's ceiling,)

Cole: I have an idea. Follow me.

(Bonzle and Zane jump onto the mech's back while the puppet and drones run towards them. They avoid the puppet's attack and the drones' shots.)

Dorama: (While the puppet and drones continue to chase the earth mech which is heading towards the stage.) Oh, this stent show has so many twists and turns. All it's missing is a happy- What are you doing?!

(The Elemental Earth Mech jumps onto the stage and the puppet follows suit while Bonzle and Zane jump off the mech. Zane freezes a small part of the floor, causing the puppet to slip and fall. The mech punches the wall of the stage, making a crack that leads to the beams on the stage's ceiling. The mech runs toward Zane and Bonzle and they jump onto it while it gets off the stage. A beam falls onto the puppet and a light that was on it pushes Dorama to the ground. He gets up.)

Dorama: Truly unpredictable finale. No notes. (He falls to the ground, his hat flinging off.)

(A tied up Dorama gets thrown into a truck and the doors shut on him.)

Cole: You don't mind driving Dorama back to the authorities?

Crossroad citizen: Can't wait to lock him up. Need a lift?

Cole: Thanks, but we still have work to do. Stay safe.

(The citizen drives the van away.)

Cole: (To Zane.) Guess we're off to Mysterium. If we knew how to get there.

Zane: I may know have something. I have been examining Dorama's (He pulls out a map.) Imperium tech. Dorama had scanned the local terrain, creating a wealth of regional maps.

Cole: Nice (He bumps Zane with his shoulder.) work, Zane. Jiro, how you feeling?

(Jiro roars loudly.)

(They take flight, thunder crashes around them.)

Cole: Ok, this is weird right?

Bonzle: Is this the right place?

Zane: This is Mysterium.

(They fly through a portal and see a monastery that looks extremely similar to the Monastery of Spinjitzu on the other side.)

Cole: How'd the Monastery of Spinjitzu get out here?

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
