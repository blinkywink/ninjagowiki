(Zane, Cole, Bonzle stand before a Monastery in Mysterium, with Jiro waiting beside it. They start walking to its gates.)

Cole: It makes no sense that our Monastery is way out here. How is this even possible?

(Cole opens the gates and the three walk in.)

Zane: Because this isn't our Monastery.

(They look around.)

Cole: Whoa. From the outside, it looks exactly the same, but in here, pretty different.

Bonzle: For real. As if someone liked your crumbly Monastery so much they were like, "I'll have my own, please." (Unconvincingly after receiving looks from Zane and Cole.) I mean, your nice Monastery.

Cole: If Mysterium is a land of magic, could this be an illusion or something?

Zane: Scans confirm its solid existence. Bonzle, the Sorceress created you here?

Bonzle: I mean, in roughly this spot, but there was no building here then. The Sorceress is probably long gone, if she'd even remember or recognize me.

Zane: After the Merge, (He starts walking to the dragon fountain in the middle of the Monastery's courtyard.) I awoke inside a strange second Monastery under Imperium, something I have not yet been able to explain. And now, we have a third Monastery. Hmm, puzzling.

Cole: Both of you, come! Look!

(He points to a crack in the Monastery's walls that looks like a bowl of noodles. Zane and Bonzle approach Cole's finding.)

Bonzle: A crack in the wall? So?

Cole: Long time ago, back in our Monastery (narrating as Past-Jay and Past-Cole tumble out of the Monastery of Spinjitzu, fighting over a video game controller) my teammate, Jay and I got into a fight because he was totally annoying and I was completely blameless.

(Past-Jay's hands surge with Lightning as he and Past-Cole try to pull the video game controller away from one another. They both lose grip on the controller and it flies into the wall, creating a crack in the shape of noodles.)

Cole: Anyway, during the fight, we broke the wall, making a crack in the shape of a bowl of noodles.

(Past-Jay and Past-Cole approach the then newly-made crack.)

Zane (narrating): Cole and Jay thought it looked so delicious, they got hungry, stopped the fight, and went to Chen's Noodle House together.

(Past-Jay and Past-Cole pull out chopsticks, clink them together and run off.)

Bonzle: That's the weirdest way to end a fight I've ever heard.

Cole: The point is that's the exact crack we made.

Zane: But how could this Monastery have the same cracks in the walls as our Monastery?

Cole: I don't know, but it's making me hungry. I wish that bonehead Jay was around. Who knows where he ended up after the Merge?

(A ball of light approaches Zane, Bonzle, and Cole before zooming off to a set of doors, going through them. Cole and Zane watch, confused.)

Cole: You saw that too, right?

Zane: We have witnessed similar unexplained phenomena at our Monastery. (They start walking to the set of doors.) The post-Merge world has so many mysteries. If only someone would arrive and tell us everything we need to know.

(The set of doors suddenly burst open with a bunch of tiny, yellow lights, and a woman and the ball of light appear.)

Woman: Hi! Oh, you have no idea how glad I am to set eyes on you. (She gestures at the ball of light.) Sprite told me you finally got here. I am Gandalaria, the High Sorceress of Mysterium, and I have awaited your coming for quite some time. This will change everything!

(Back at the training plateau, Arin stands before three pillars before spinning into a partial Spinjitzu tornado. He then spins into two of the pillars, breaking them. However, on the third, he is unable to break it and is knocked back.)

Arin: Ah!

(He falls on to his back but the force of the impact flips him over on to his stomach. He gets back to his feet, grunting and groaning.)

Sora: Alright! Yeah! (She pops up from behind one of the now-destroyed pillars while Riyu jumps on to it.) Sorry, didn't mean to sneak up on you, Arin.

Arin: I'm just trying to practice Spinjitzu on my own.

Sora: And you're doing great!

Arin: (Turning away from Sora.) Don't make fun of me, Sora. You know I got demoted to First Fang.

Sora: I'm not making fun of you. You plowed right through those first two pillars.

Arin: I was trying to go through all three with a proper Spinjitzu tornado, for once.

Sora: Your Spinjitzu's been great since I met you. I mean, you can give Spinjitzu to inanimate objects. No one can do that.

Arin: I can barely do that.

Sora: Stop being so hard on yourself.

Arin: The Blood Moon is coming! The whole world is at stake! We're the only ones who can stop it, and I need to be getting better at being a Ninja, but I'm not.

(He walks away from Sora and Riyu, going up some steps. Sora makes a move to go after him but stops herself, exchanging looks with Riyu.)

(Meanwhile, Egalt sits with his head down. He looks up as Wyldfyre approaches, pushing a bowl on the ground with her uninjured foot.)

Egalt: I came here alone because I do not wish to be disturbed.

Wyldfyre: Yeah, me too. Sometimes all those other Ninja can get on my nerves. I mean, Kai's friends are all good people, but I'm not used to being around humans all the time. (She sits down.) I was raised by dragons. Did you know that?

Egalt: You may have mentioned it once or 12 times.

Wyldfyre: Yeah, so I fit in better with you and Rontu than the Ninja, really.

Egalt: Mm-hmm.

Wyldfyre: My family used to tell me stories. I'm sure they were made up, but they were very good. Some were about the two greatest dragon warriors that ever lived.

Egalt: Is that so?

Wyldfyre: Yeah, there warriors were epic. Legendary. They went to all the realms, protecting everyone who couldn't protect themselves whether they were dragons or not. And the older dragon was the bravest warrior anyone had ever seen.

Egalt: Hmm, was he, now?

Wyldfyre: Yep. We even named the largest, strongest mountain in our land after him: Mount Egalt. But those stories were probably all just fairy tales.

Egalt: There was a time when those stories could have been true, but that is long past. Time runs out for everyone, even once-great warriors.

Wyldfyre: I made you a stew from Janus Melons and Xoa Thorns. It helps ease the pain of the Wasting Sickness.

Egalt: (He grunts.) What are you talking about?

Wyldfyre: I lost some of my own dragon family to it. I know it's a disease older dragons can get.

Egalt: And what makes you believe I suffer from this ailment?

Wyldfyre: (She turns to Egalt's tail.) The black edges of your lost scales. That only happens when the sickness is getting really bad. (She pushes the bowl closer to Egalt.) This isn't a cure but it makes the pain less painful.

Egalt: I don't need any stew. I just need to be left alone.

Wyldfyre: Irritability is one of the symptoms of Wasting Sickness too, but that could also just be your personality.

Egalt: Ha! (He grabs the bowl of stew, and slurps it down.) So they named a mountain after me, huh?

(Wyldfyre sits down, nodding excitedly.)

(Back at Mysterium, Cole, Zane, and Bonzle stand before Gandalaria.)

Gandalaria: Now, pay attention. This is very important. I'll need 20 striped ones, five red betties, and two extra-slimy.

(The three exchange confused looks.)

Zane: I am sorry. We do not understand.

Gandalaria: What? What's not to understand? Oh, and do you have any with wings? I could really use some with wings.

Cole: Some whats with wings?

Gandalaria: Slugs, of course! Aren't you the traveling slug merchants I've been waiting for?

Cole: Slug merchants? But you said our arrival would change everything.

Gandalaria: Uh, yeah! I've been low on rare magic ingredients for, like, weeks now, and a really good set of slugs would change all that.

Bonzle: Slugs are pretty cool, actually.

Zane: Perhaps, yet we have none to sell.

Gandalaria: Ah, bummer. Well, what am I going to do now? (She looks at the three, realizing something, and pulls a jar out, jumping forward.) Would you all mind if I turn you into slugs? (She clinks the jar against Zane.)

Cole: (He pulls Zane away from Gandalaria a step.) We would mind that very, very much.

Gandalaria: Are you sure? Because you'd make really groovy slugs, I can tell just by looking at... (She gasps as she passes up close to Bonzle.) It's you! My dearest! You've come home!

Bonzle: You...you recognize me? Even in my boney physical form?

Gandalaria: Oh, I would know your true essence anywhere.

Bonzle: I was so afraid you wouldn't accept me for who I am now.

Gandalaria: Are you kidding? I put my heart, my soul into every spell I weave. And even though spells usually fade away after use, I always hoped you would be different--the most complex spell I've ever woven, (She hugs Bonzle.) and the first of my creations to ever come back to me! Oh!

(Bonzle hugs Gandalaria back.)

(Meanwhile, Lloyd and Rontu are drinking tea.)

Rontu: So you're afraid these visions are holding you back from progressing with your Rising Dragon training?

Lloyd: I'm just a bundle of nerves, stumbling around in a daze. I'm even afraid to rest because more visions might come.

Rontu: Why would that be a bad thing?

Lloyd: Because they're terrifying! I see evil things, my friends in danger. It's awful.

Rontu: I'm sure it is, but these visions are also important warnings, right?

Lloyd: (Sighs.) They are, but they're also tearing me apart. Master Wu taught that when there is no calm outside of you, you must find the calm inside, but I have no calm inside of me anymore.

Rontu: You held the power of a Source Dragon, something no mortal was ever meant to do. These possible futures are after-effects.

Lloyd: (He looks up, surprised.) Possible futures?

Rontu: Source Dragons experience time differently than the rest of us. They can see what came before and the things that might come to be. These visions you're having are like a fragment of that.

Lloyd: But how can I stop them?

Rontu: You can't. (Looks up to watch petals drifting in the wind.) You can never force the motion of the universe to bend to your will, you can only find your place within its current. But with careful meditation, it's possible you can find harmony with these visions so you no longer have to live in fear of them.

Lloyd: No. I need to stop them. Because when they come on, I can't -- (Shouts.)

(He suddenly gets a vision of Bonzle, Cole, and Zane in the courtyard of the Monastery of Mysterium. It switches to Wolf Mask Claw Warriors approaching. Then, to the Shadow Dojo where Lord Ras approaches Bonzle on an altar. She is looking scared and helpless as Ras lifts the Mallet of Shattering up. The vision then switches to Cole as a gong is rung.)

Cole: But that will kill him!

(The vision switches again to a Wolf Mask Claw Warrior unsheathing the claw features in their armor. The Wolf Mask Claw Warrior brings it down as Sora blocks it with her Dragon Hook. She knocks the Wolf Mask Claw Warrior away and turns to fight another. Kai is fighting Wolf Mask Claw Warriors, too. He dodges from a slash and kicks a Wolf Mask Claw Warrior away. He blocks another Wolf Mask Claw Warrior with his katana just as a red-orange portal appears in the air before the battle. The silhouettes of the Forbidden Five are shown. The vision pulls up to the Blood Moon above all of this, and Lloyd is then thrust out of the vision.)

Lloyd: (Yells.) The Blood Moon! It's so close! It's almost here! (While he is yelling this, Arin, Sora, Nya, and Kai are running down some stairs towards him. Egalt is shown to be flying towards them.) Unless we stop him, Lord Ras is going to sacrifice someone, and Cole and Zane are doomed!

Rontu: Then time is of the essence. Return to training immediately. If you cannot learn Rising Dragon Technique, all will be lost.

(Meanwhile, Bonzle and Gandalaria are still hugging.)

Cole: (Clears his throat.) Ahem, uh, Sorceress?

Gandalaria: (Pulling away from the hug.) Please, call me Gandalaria.

Cole: Of course. Gandull-- Gandollar, uh...Sorceress, we came because we gotta protect Bonzle. Scary Wolf Mask people are after her so their Lord Ras can use her to unleash evil on the Merged Lands.

Gandalaria: Okay, now I might've been getting a little slow on the uptake for the last couple of hundred years, but what's a "Bonzle" or a "Lord Ras"?

Bonzle: I'm Bonzle. That's the name I chose when I became a person.

Gandalaria: Well, that's a splendid name.

(Bonzle smiles.)

Zane: Lord Ras is a villain we Ninja have faced before. He is determined to free the Forbidden Five.

Gandalaria: The foes I created Bonzle to banish?

Cole: Exactly.

Gandalaria: Ah, pfft, then no problem. They're gone, exiled, never able to return.

Cole: You're certain?

Gandalaria: Of course. First off, it'd take a whole complicated ritual that no one remembers how to do, and second, it can only be done under a Blood Moon, and those happen like once a millennium.

Zane: It appears we have bad news. The details of the ritual were uncovered by Lord Ras from the Cloud Kingdom archives.

Cole: And the Blood Moon is coming soon.

Gandalaria: Oh. Oh! Yes, that does change things. If this Ras times it right, reversing the power of the spell--uh, Bonzle here-- I suppose he could very well free the Five.

Cole: And from what we can understand, if they get loose, they could conquer everything.

Gandalaria: Conquer? More like obliterate! You mustn't allow them to be freed!

Zane: We hoped you would know a way to keep Bonzle safely out of Ras's hands until the Blood Moon passes.

Gandalaria: Before I moved into this new place, I used to wander the lands. I keep most of my materials in storage.

Cole: So you have nothing to help us with?

Gandalaria: Well, not here, but I certainly do in my storehouse. It's in a land called the Wyldness. It'll be quite a journey on foot.

Cole: Good news: we've got our fastest dragon parked right outside.

Gandalaria: Ooh, how delightful! I haven't ridden on dragonback for a century!

(Someone knocks on the Monastery gate. They all turn to investigate.)

Cole: Did the Wolf Masks follow us here?

Zane: I do not believe they would knock. However, it would be wise to discover who is outside before we open that gate.

Gandalaria: (In a hushed tone.) If it's a snail merchant, ask about the ones with wings!

Zane: (Glancing over the edge of the Monastery walls.) Unfortunately, it is not.

(Bonzle and Cole start climbing the ladder Zane had used. Gandalaria grabs on to Sprite, who floats her up to the top of the wall. Beyond the gate stands Administration Agents.)

Agent Allen: Attention! My name is Agent Rodrick Allen, and this is my partner, Agent SsssSsss Underwood. By the all-encompassing authority of the Administration, we hereby demand you open this door!

Agent SsssSsss Underwood: We're after the stolen equipment known as Zane, who illegally fled from us!

Zane: Cole, have I mentioned that I was briefly kidnapped by a society of bureaucrats from the Land of Madness, who forced me to work for their mail room, where I introduced efficiencies that raised delivery accuracy by nearly 9%?

Cole: Are you serious?

Zane: (Excitedly.) Yes, nearly 9%. I feel I could have gotten it to ten, but Lloyd and Arin rescued me before I could finish.

Gandalaria: Bureaucrats? Pfft! Probably wouldn't even make good slugs then.

(Meanwhile, Kai is attempting Rising Dragon. He spins into a Spinjitzu tornado and jumps over a pile of rocks to another rock at the base of a tree. He then jumps off it to kick a lantern hanging off of the tree. He lands and spins back into his Spinjitzu tornado, ramming into a pile of rocks and continuing his Spinjitzu tornado. However, he trips on a rock, and falls on to the ground.)

Kai: (Groans.) Come on! This is impossible! It's too much!

(He punches the log hanging over him. The punch knocks the log off the rocks it is sitting on.)

Egalt: Did I not tell you it would be impossible? Rising Dragon Technique is not for those (Kai gets up and lifts the log back onto the rocks.) lacking in spirit.

Kai: Who are you saying lacks spirit? Me? You better not be talking about me.

Egalt: So quick to anger. It is no surprise you cannot find harmony with the universe's motion.

Nya: What if we did this drill together?

Kai: What?

Nya: You say Rising Dragon Technique is about finding harmony with everything, which means harmony with each other, right?

Egalt: It does.

Nya: Then, let (Nya puts her arm behind Kai and pushes him forward slightly.) my brother and I try this together.

Egalt: That is unprecedented. But I don't have a problem with it. Though it will most likely end in failure.

(Kai and Nya turn around and walk towards the center.)

Kai: (Quietly.) What are you doing? I can do this myself, Nya.

Nya: So far, (She grabs Kai, and he turns around.) you're doing better than any of us, but I know what's holding you back.

Kai: What?

Nya: You. Stop thinking about you. Start thinking of Counter Shot.

Kai: The game we made up in dad's blacksmith shop? (Chuckles.) Wow, miss those days. Just fun, not having to worry about saving the world.

Nya: And who was your best (She nudges Kai.) Counter Shot partner?

Egalt: Begin!

(Nya and Kai launch into a Spinjitzu tornado. While it flashes back to their past selves as kids at the blacksmith shop, they avoid a rock, and Kai jumps over Nya multiple times. Nya jumps to hit the lantern and Kai does the same. They both ram into the same rock they passed earlier and Rontu and Egalt smile at each other. Kai and Nya slide under the log and high five. Kai puts on his mask and manages to perform Rising Dragon. The other ninja, Rontu and Egalt watch him fly around and they celebrate. Kai lands with glowing red eyes and causes an explosion. Nya runs toward him.)

Kai: (Gasps.) I did it! I did it! (Nya hugs Kai and he spins her around.) Nya, thank you so much! Oh, you were so right!

(The rest of the ninja and Riyu run toward them, cheering. Riyu roars.)

Wyldfyre: Good job, buddy!

(Later at night, the Dragon Masters and the ninja stand on the steps.)

Rontu: Kai is now officially a Third Fang Ninja.

Egalt: When you first arrived, I was skeptical. But I had become old and tired, without inspiration, and it was clear I misjudged you. You are worthy students and have given this old dragon the spark of hope (The sky starts to turn red.) that Rising Dragon Technique will be passed on.

Arin: What's wrong with (He points at the Blood Moon.) the sky?

Lloyd: Oh, no.

(At the Cloud Kingdom, Ras sits on the roof of a building when he sees the Blood Moon, turns red and looks at his hands.)

(At the Monastery of Spinjitzu. Geo and Mr. Frohicky are talking when Fritz and Spitz run up to them. They all look up and see the Blood Moon.)

(At the Monastery of Mysterium, Bonzle, Cole, Gandalaria, and Zane stand on top of the wall, looking down on the Administration agents. Bonzle turns around and sees the Blood Moon.)

Bonzle: (To Cole.) The Blood Moon is here.

(Cole turns around to face the Blood Moon.)

(Back at the Training Plateau, the dragon masters and the ninja look at the Blood Moon.)

Lloyd: (He looks at Egalt and Rontu.) Come on! We have no time to waste. Help the rest of us learn how to do Rising Dragon Technique.

Rontu: We cannot. I'm sorry. We hoped we had more time, but the Blood Moon is also a bane for us.

Egalt: May the Source Dragons be on your side.

(Egalt and Rontu turn into stone.)

(Back at the Monastery of Mysterium, Zane tries to negotiate with the Administration agents.)

Zane: Excuse me. We have some rather pressing matters to attend to. Would it be easiest for my allies here if I just surrendered?

Agent Allen: Technically, you can't surrender, only people can. You're a robotic Nindroid, so you're classified as equipment, not personnel.

Agent SsssSsss Underwood: Which means you'll be decommissioned in our incinerator.

Cole: But that will kill him!

Agent Allen: Correction, it would kill him, if he were a person. But he's just equipment, so no big deal.

Zane: But if I turn myself over, my friends can go free?

Agent Allen: Nope. Don't you know Addendum 47-A? Anyone assisting a known fugitive shall be punished under the strictest guidelines. Not much room for misinterpretation there.

Cole: Whatever. Let's just get on our dragon and go, everyone.

Agent SsssSsss Underwood: Nice try, but your dragon's currently impounded.

(Jiro roars with clamps on his feet and jaw and falls to the floor.)

Cole: Hey! Let Jiro go!

Agent Allen: We can discuss it on our way to the incinerator. (To the other agents.) Breach this wall!

(The agents and the Master Mechs start moving towards the monastery.)

Zane: Halt! (They stop moving.) Where is your 35-C91 form, designating approved intent to breach a wall or barricade on foreign soil?

(Agent Allen and SsssSsss Underwood look at each other.)

Cole: (Whispering to Zane.) Did you just make that up?

Zane: No. My work in the mail room afforded me much access to handbooks. My knowledge of their regulations is vast.

Agent SsssSsss Underwood: (She groans and pulls out a book, reading from its pages.) Intent to breach water. Intent to breach walrus. Ah, here it is! Intent to breach walls. Ugh, he's right. (She closes the book.) We need forms!

Agent Allen: (Groans.) Okay, give us a minute. We'll get right on the paperwork.

(Zane, Bonzle, and Cole slide down the ladder while Gandalaria grabs onto Sprite and floats down.)

Gandalaria: Man, (She pulls out two potion bottles and walks toward the fountain.) none of this stuff is Administration-approved, (She dumps the potions into the fountain, takes off her hat, and dumps the things inside it into the fountain too.) and you brought them right to my door! (A cat comes out of her hat, yowls, and runs away.) Not cool! (She puts her hat back on and walks back to them.)

Zane: We passed through the Land of Madness to get here. I should have realized they would be scanning for me. This is all my fault.

Cole: No. We're in this together.

(A form slides under the door.)

Agent SsssSsss Underwood: There's your 35-C91! Hmm!

(Zane picks up the form and looks at it.)

Zane: This doesn't have the mandatory 5-21z appendix. Unacceptable.

(He slides the paper back under the door.)

Agent Allen: Ugh, he's right again! (Gandalaria walks away.) Who's got a copy of the appendix?

Gandalaria: (She turns around.) I know what to do! I'll weave an intricate escape spell.

Bonzle: How long will that take?

Gandalaria: Only two to three days.

Zane: That might be too long.

(The form flies under the door again.)

Agent Allen: Appendix attached, you scofflaw.

Zane: This is done correctly...

Agent Allen: Told you.

Zane: ...but not in triplicate.

(Zane slides the form back under the door. Agent Allen screams and, presumably, kicks the door.)

Gandalaria; Bonzle, hide in the passages under this place. Sprite will guide you. (Sprite flies over to Bonzle.)

Bonzle: Thanks, Sprite. But what are you gonna do?

Gandalaria: I'm going to fire up my crystal ball!

(Cole and Bonzle look at each other.)

Cole: I'm not sure offering to read their fortunes is gonna help.

Gandalaria: It's not that kind of (She takes out the crystal ball.) crystal ball.

(The Administration Agents break the door down and start marching toward the Monastery.)

Agent Allen: (In a Master Mech that is holding the three forms.) There's your triplicate right there. (He throws the forms onto the ground.)

(Bonzle gets up as the forms land in front of her. She looks at Sprite when the forms get blasted. She starts to run. Gandalaria blasts an Administration agent with her crystal ball. Cole throws the disc up in the air and it turns into the Elemental Earth Mech. Cole hops into the mech and starts to wrestle with the Master Mechs. Zane gets out of the Monastery's courtyard and uses his Ice power to blast some agents. Gandalaria continues zapping agents. Bonzle slides down a ladder into a passage.)

Bonzle: (Sighs.) Everything's so dark down here, Sprite. Are you sure there'll be a safe place to hide?

(Sprite guides her through the passage when a blast lands near Bonzle's feet, stopping her in her tracks.)

Agent Walker: Attention!

(Bonzle raises up her arms.)

Agent Walker: My name is Agent Jay Walker! By the all-encompassing authority of the Administration, I hereby demand your surrender!

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
