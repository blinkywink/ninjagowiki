Crashing Together/Transcript

(In a flashback, the Crossroads is being built. A pink Hypnobrai walks over to a board with missing posters and attaches a poster of her own to it.)

Past Hypnobrai: Oh, Delphee... hope someone's seen you.

(The Hypnobrai walks away as a young Sora approaches the posters and stops for a moment to look at them, thinking about her life in Imperium.)

Past Sora's father: We are quite proud of you, Ana.

Past Sora's mother: I know we don't say that much...

Past Sora: Or ever.

Past Sora's mother: ...but we see now that your... passion for your studies was all in service of Imperium.

(Back in the Crossroads, Sora walks away from the posters, still lost in her thoughts)

Past Sora: A dragon! A real dragon! (She reaches out to touch Zanth's head and laughs.) Aww. You sweet thing. What's her name?

Past Dr. LaRow: Subject 152-9a, but some of the lab techs have taken to calling her "Sora".

Past Sora: Sora? The ancient Imperium word for orphan? Did something happen to your family, Sora?

(Sora is still thinking about this as she continues walking.)

Past Sora: What are you doing?

Past Dr. LaRow: Demonstrating the possibilities. (She increases the amount of power being drained from Zanth, who roars in pain.)

Past Sora: What?! No! No, stop!

(In the Crossroads, Sora bumps into a man carrying a heavy crate, causing the crate to fall down and spill its contents on the ground. The man begins loading the crate back up, and Sora notices a screwdriver on the ground and goes to pick it up, only to suddenly see a car headed towards her, which she is barely able to dodge. She looks at the screwdriver and continues to think.)

Past Sora: (gasps) My workbench!

Past Sora's mother: If you won't use your scientific studies to support Imperium...

Past Sora's father: And all that we stand for...

Past Sora's mother: Then you'll just have to find a new interest.

(After thinking, Sora puts the screwdriver in her pocket and continues to walk.)

Past Sora: (to a poster of Dr. LaRow) I believed in you! I thought you were making Imperium a better place! For every living thing! (She takes the poster off her wall and puts it into a bin.) I know you wish I was a dutiful Imperian, Mom and Dad, but I'll never be the daughter you want me to be... (She grabs her backpack.) that Empress Beatrix wants me to be. (She grabs her headphones and leaves out of the window.)

(In the Crossroads, Sora sees a Hypnobrai playing a flute next to a dumpster. As she approaches her, the dumpster opens up to reveal a person inside of it. Sora runs away in panic as the person hisses at her. After running, Sora sees a person eating a fruit, and her stomach starts to rumble.)

Past Merlopian: Urchin Fruit, fresh from Merlopia. (Sora walks over to him and tries to grab a fruit, only to be stopped.) Uh-uh. Don't run a charity here, girl. No free food. And no foreign coin. The Crossroads is still part of Ninjago.

(An old woman walks up to the Merlopian and picks up a fruit.)

Past Old Woman: Hey, this price is triple what you charged yesterday. How can you think about money when people are hungry?

Past Merlopian: (snatches the fruit from the woman) A crisis for some is an opportunity for others. (He starts to eat the fruit, then looks at Sora before throwing it away and looking elsewhere.)

(Sora quickly grabs one of the fruits, but this causes the other fruits in the crate to start rolling away.)

Past Merlopian: Thief! (Sora starts running away.) Well? Go after that little pest! (The people around him aren't interested in chasing Sora.) Bah... Uh, fine, free fruit to whoever catches her. (Everyone starts to run after Sora upon hearing this.)

(Sora is running away when she looks back and sees the people coming after her. She tries to run faster, but trips and drops her headphones and the fruit. One of the people chasing her stops to examine the headphones. As Sora is running away, she bumps into Arin carrying several pies.)

Past Arin: Whoa! (He manages to prevent the pies from falling to the ground.) Sorry about that. Hard to see over all these pies.

Past Man: I think she went thataway!

(Arin gives Sora the pies so she can hide.)

Past Arin: Homemade pies. Plenty in stock. (The people run past him and Sora, but one man stops to get a pie.)

Past Man: Ooh, all this chasin's makin' me hungry. I'll take this one. (He goes to grab one of the pies, but Arin stops him.)

Past Arin: Uh, actually, all out of pies. We're closed. Come back tomorrow. (He and Sora run away.)

Past Man: I guess I could make myself a mustard sandwich when I get home.

Past Arin: I think we're safe. (He takes the pies from Sora, whose stomach starts rumbling again.) You can have one if you want. I baked them myself. (He hands Sora a slice of pie, which she hurriedly grabs and eats.)

Past Sora: Thanks for helping me.

Past Arin: Of course. The Merge brought everyone together, right? So I believe together is the only way to survive it. My name's Arin.

Past Sora: I'm An- Uh, you can call me Sora.

(In the present day World Forest, the ninja confront Arin and Ras.)

Arin: Sora? What are you all doing here? (Sora jumps down from her jet.) Frak? (Frak waves at Arin as Ras growls.) Zane, you're... big. I guess a lot's changed since we last saw each other.

Sora: Arin, I...

Lloyd: Ras, let him go!

Ras: He is with me by choice.

Kai: He's lying. Typical villain.

Arin: No. It's true... Ras is my new teacher.

(Riyu and the Ninja remain confused at his unexpected revelation)

(In the Crossroads' junkyard, a young Sora is searching through the trash when she finds her headphones again.)

Past Sora: Oh, hello. Let's see if you work. (She hops down, puts the headphones on, and activates them, causing music to play.) Nice! (She starts dancing to the music until she notices two dogs guarding the junkyard and begins running away from them. She manages to climb over the fence surrounding the junkyard before the dogs get to her and leaves while dancing.)

(Sora walks through the Crossroads, stopping to greet Mikloshe before continuing on until she sees Arin crash into the ground before her.)

Past Arin: This Grapple Gun thing you made me is amazing. (He accidentally fires it, and it attaches to a nearby pole, causing the Merlopian from earlier to trip over and drop the Urchin Fruit he was carrying.) ...or it will be once I get the hang of it.

Past Sora: You'll be a real samurai before you know it. Just like your heroes.

Past Arin: Not samurai, ninja. Masters of Spinjitzu. (He puts on Lloyd's old hood and performs a rudimentary Spinjitzu swirl.)

Past Sora: Right, ninja. Floyd, Kal, Lane, Mister Wu. (She walks over to investigate a box Arin accidentally broke.)

Past Arin: (stops his Spinjitzu and takes off the hood) No. Lloyd, Kai, Zane... (Sora laughs.) You're teasing me.

Past Sora: Only 'cause it's really fun. I mean, you carry that green mask everywhere you go. We didn't have anything like the ninja where I'm from.

Past Arin: Then how did you tease your friends?

Past Sora: (picks up a welding mask from the crate) I didn't have those either.

(In the evening, Arin and Sora are in their penthouse. Sora has just finished building an oven.)

Past Sora: About time you had a proper oven. That campfire you've been baking with is a fire hazard.

Past Arin: (spinning around) But it gave my pies their rustic, crispy flavor. (He falls down.)

Past Sora: You mean burnt? Let's fire this baby up. (She opens the oven and hits it, which causes it to heat up. She hits it again to increase the heat and closes it up.)

(The oven starts shaking and suddenly explodes.)

Past Arin: Aah! (He notices his book is burning.) Ah! No, my recipes! (He starts putting the fire out with Lloyd's hood.) I knew this was all a mistake! (Sora is shocked and runs away.) Of course, the ninja's masks would be flame-resistant. They'd have to be with Kai around. Sora, you okay? Sorry, I snapped. Sora?

(Back in the present, Arin and Ras are still talking with the ninja.)

Ras: You heard the boy. My student will not abandon his teacher.

Frak: Ras must be brainwashing him.

Ras: I'm training him. Like I was training you.

Frak: Until I learned who you really are.

Kai: I don't need a reminder of how evil Ras is. Nice people don't trap you for eternity in the Nether-Space.

Lloyd: Enough! Arin, come back with us. We've been searching everywhere for you.

Zane: Technically, the search that brought us here has been focused on finding the remaining Prismatic Weapon (He holds out the Prismatic Blade in his hand) that is currently in the possession of our former ally, Arin.

Sora: He's not our former ally.

Lloyd: Do you have a blade that looks like this? (He takes the blade from Zane and shows it to Arin.) It's really important we find it.

Arin: (briefly looks over to Ras before pulling out the Returning Blade) Is this what you came for?

Ras: (grabs the blade from Arin) Yes, this Returning Blade is made of Prismatic Steel. I've had it for years... an old battle trophy. (He puts the blade away.)

Sora: They're what the Forbidden Five have been after. With the five Prismatic Weapons, they can release a Chaos Dragon that would throw the world into, well, chaos.

(Suddenly, a branch can be heard breaking.)

Kai: Look out! (He pushes Sora back.)

(The ninja manage to dodge the giant branch that falls in front of them.)

Sora: These two blades are the last ones the Five need to free the dragon.

Ras: Then I'll be sure to keep it out of their hands. Arin, let us go. (They start leaving.)

Sora: Arin, wait!

Frak: (pushes past the other ninja) You won't trick Arin like you did me, Ras!

(Frak leaps towards Arin and Ras and prepares to use his element, but Arin stops him with his Spinjitzu.)

Arin: We don't want to fight you.

Kai: Obviously. You're outnumbered.

Arin: (sighs) Just let me go. I need Ras.

(As this is happening, Rogue suddenly swoops down and catches Ras.)

Lloyd: Uh, Arin...

Arin: (looks over to see Rogue flying away with Ras.) Oh, come on!

(In the Crossroads, Sora is walking away alone, not noticing the truck following her.)

Past Re-Awakened 1: (laughs) Looks like we got another stray. (The other Re-Awakened chuckles as well.)

Past Arin: (runs after Sora) Sora, come back! (He bumps into a Lava-Tide, but keeps running.) There's hardly any burning! Have you seen Sora?

Past Old Woman: She was just here. I think she went that way. (Some ooze from the berries she was holding gets onto Arin's face.)

Past Arin: (wipes the ooze off his face) Uh, thanks.

Past Sora: Can't go back to Imperium, but what else is out there? Gotta be somewhere I haven't made a fool of myself and alienated everyone I care about. (She notices a cat emerging from behind some barrels. The cat walks over to her.) Aw, poor thing. (She pets the cat.) You must be lost. (The cat starts running away, and Sora follows.) Here, kitty. I won't hurt you.

Past Arin: (sees Sora) Sora, wait up! I'm sorry!

(The cat climbs up to the back of a truck.)

Past Sora: Don't be scared... (She approaches the cat when it suddenly attacks her. The two Re-Awakened then close the door of the truck behind her.)

Past Re-Awakened 1: Another lonely Other-realmer. You were right. The Crossroads is a great source for workers for the mines. (The cat exits the truck and jumps into her arms.) So many lost souls.

(The two Re-Awakened start the truck, and Sora tries to punch her way out to no avail.)

Past Arin: Hey! Where are you going? That's my best friend!

(In the present, Ras is trying to get free from the rope Rogue is using to haul him, but fails.)

Kai: Who was that guy?

Arin: My new least favorite person. (He puts on his mask, activates his glider and flies away.)

Sora: (puts on her hood and leaps into her jet) I'm not losing Arin again. (She takes off, followed by Zane and Riyu.)

Nya: Come on! (The rest of the ninja rush to the car nearby, but quickly stop when the sky flashes red.)

(Drix arrives onto the scene, accompanied by six Dragonian Trackers.)

Kai: Since when is the World Forest Ninjago's newest hot spot? (He prepares his power.)

Lloyd: They're here for the Prismatic Blades.

Frak: They'll have to go through us first.

Drix: Get that blade. (The Dragonians fly off after Rogue, Ras, and everyone pursuing them as Drix lands before the rest of the ninja and hisses.) Oh, this should be easy.

Kai: You're gonna regret sending your little army away.

Drix: Am I?

(Ras is still getting dragged away by Rogue.)

Ras: Release me at once, or you'll face an eternity of retribution!

(Arin is quickly closing the distance to them. As Rogue sees him, he pulls out his daggers.)

(Sora dodges a tree while flying as fast as she can to reach Arin. Zane follows closely behind, as he lands on a branch before taking off again and using his hands to avoid another branch. Riyu navigates through the branches behind Zane.)

Sora: I see him! (As the three of them get closer, Dragonians appear behind them.) Behind you!

(Zane knocks off a branch, which falls on three Dragonians. Riyu slams the other three away by bending another branch with his tail. As Sora approaches Arin, he picks up speed and flies on top of Rogue, clinging to his back.)

Arin: Let him go!

(Rogue tilts himself to the right, causing Arin to slide to the tip of his wing. He then starts jabbing at Arin with his daggers.)

(Meanwhile, the ninja engage in combat with Drix. Kai charges at Drix with his fists lit up and tries to hit Drix with Fire, but Drix dodges. He tries again, only for Drix to dodge a second time and retaliate with a kick, which Kai blocks with his fist before kicking back. Drix dodges the kick, then grabs Kai and tosses him away. Frak tries to attack, but Drix sends him back with a Shatterspin-powered kick. P.I.X.A.L. flies in with her car to prevent Frak from falling down and blasts at Drix with the cannons on the vehicle. The blast hits Drix's Shatterspin barrier, and he jumps back without seeming to have received any major damage from it.)

Nya: Lloyd, get out of here with the blade. Let us handle him. (She and Lloyd exchange a high-five as Lloyd leaves.) Any thoughts on fighting against Rising Dragon Technique? (She charges up her attack and flies at Drix, who takes out his sickles and snarls. She hits the spot where Drix was standing, but when she looks around to find her, Drix is nowhere to be seen.) Huh? Where did he go?

(Drix suddenly emerges from the swarm of bugs behind Nya and kicks her into Kai.)

Kai: (gets up) Time to blast this guy back to the Nether-Space. (He shoots fire at Drix. The attack hits and creates a hole through Drix's torso, causing bugs to appear around him. However, Drix seems mostly unbothered by this.) Uh... is this guy bugs? (The hole in Drix's body closes up.) Is this guy bugs?!

Frak: Yaah! Aw, that's worse than puppets.

(Drix laughs and uses his power to attack Kai and Nya with a swarm of bugs.)

Nya: There's too many of them.

(Drix fires another swarm at P.I.X.A.L. and Frak, causing the car they're in to start malfunctioning.)

P.I.X.A.L.: Prepare for a premature landing.

Frak: Do you mean "crash"? (The car crashes onto a lower branch.)

Lloyd: (looks at the blade in his hand) What good is a legendary weapon if I'm not going to use it? (Drix charges at him.)

(Lloyd jumps out from his hiding spot and tries to hit Drix with the blade, but Drix turns into a swarm of bugs and moves behind him. He tries to strike at Lloyd with one of his sickles, but Lloyd dodges the attack. Lloyd tries to strike again, but Drix does the same thing as before to dodge it. Lloyd manages to jump out of the way of another attack from Drix's sickles, but gets kicked very close to the edge of the branch. He takes a brief moment to look down, at which point Drix has already disappeared.)

Drix: (appears behind Lloyd and grabs the blade) Thank you. (He kicks Lloyd away.)

(As Drix moves away, the swarm of bugs around Kai and Nya withdraws as well, and the two of them rush to Lloyd, along with Frak and P.I.X.A.L. Drix then uses a wave of Shatterspin to send them all flying away. The ninja manages to grab onto the lower branches of the tree.)

Lloyd: That didn't work out like I hoped.

(Drix flies away with the blade.)

(In the Crossroads, young Sora is still trapped in the truck.)

Past Sora: Let me outta here, you numbskulls! (The Re-Awakened don't answer.)

Past Arin: (tries to catch up to the truck) Hold on, Sora! (pants) Think ninja, Arin. There's more than one way to catch a villain. They have speed, but I know shortcuts. Coming through! (He jumps over a counter where the old woman from earlier is selling her berries, getting some ooze from them on his hands in the process.) Sorry, sorry. (He continues running, and tastes the ooze on his hand.) Hmm. These Ooze Berries would be great in a pie. (He enters the backyard of the building, and starts climbing to the roof with great effort. When he makes it up, he notices the truck going past the building.) Here goes nothing. (He puts on Lloyd's old ninja mask as the truck stops due to almost crashing into another car.) Ninja... (He tries jumping to the truck, but falls short and lands on the other car.) no!

(As the car he is on starts driving the opposite way the truck is going, Arin pulls out his Grapple Gun and successfully uses it to latch onto the truck. The pull from the truck lifts him into the air, and he pulls himself toward it with the grapple. Just as he's about to reach it, the truck suddenly stops, and Arin is flung forward.)

Past Re-Awakened 1: (reads a map) These Crossroads roads are so confusing. (Arin hits the windshield in front of them, but they don't pay attention to this.)

(The Re-Awakened start driving the truck again, and their cat jumps into the back where Sora is.)

Past Sora: It's okay, I forgive you. I know what it's like to be used and to hurt people without knowing better. (She extends her hand to the cat, but the cat scratches it.) Ow! Ugh! So much for being nice.

Past Arin: (kicks through the roof of the truck's back) Sora! I'm here to save you. (The cat hisses at him.)

Past Sora: Really? You said this was all a mistake. I thought you didn't want to be friends anymore.

Past Arin: What?! No way! Friends make mistakes all the time. That's normal. You really haven't had any friends before, have you? Come on. I told you, the only way through this is together. (He starts lifting Sora out of the truck.)

Past Re-Awakened 1: I said, Take a left! A left! (The truck makes a sharp turn.)

(As Arin struggles to lift Sora out, the cat tries to hold onto Sora's leg, but falls down. Arin successfully gets Sora to the top of the truck.)

Past Sora: I've never been so glad to see that green mask.

Past Arin: So, you think I look like a ninja?

(One of the Skulkin climbs up behind Arin)

Past Sora: Look out!

Past Re-Awakened 1: Hey! This isn't a bus. Get back in your cage. (She points at them with a sword.)

Past Arin: Hold on tight. (He grabs onto Sora and pulls out the Grapple Gun.)

Past Sora: You sure you can work that thing?

Past Arin: I hope so. It was built by a genius.

(Arin fires the grapple, and it latches onto a nearby building. Just as the Re-Awakened is about to hit them with her sword, the two are pulled away into safety, and she loses her balance and falls into the cage, where the cat attacks her. The other Re-Awakened stops the car and shouts angrily at Arin and Sora.)

(Back in the World Forest during the present, Arin is still fighting with Rogue. The two of them approach each other and start shooting from the blasters in their gliders. Arin dodges Rogue as he swoops past and flies back in front of him for another attack. Rogue does a quick spin, causing Ras to hit him away and Arin to fall to the ground. Rogue throws one of his daggers at Arin, which Arin blocks with the wings of his glider.)

Arin: (picks up the dagger) Thanks, just what I needed. (He notices Rogue flying past him, takes off his glider, and jumps down, falling onto the rope Rogue is using to carry Ras.)

Ras: What took you so long?

Arin: I guess I shouldn't expect to learn gratitude from you, huh?

Ras: Hurry! (Arin cuts the rope below him, and they start falling down. Arin then uses the dagger to latch onto a nearby tree to slow their fall. The two land on a branch, and Arin puts the dagger away while he breaks free from the rope.) This fool has become more of an obstacle than your former ninja allies.

Arin: They're not my former... Get down!

(Rogue swoops in, grabs Arin, and starts dragging him away. He drops him at a different branch, and the two of them begin fighting using the lightning daggers. Rogue jumps at Arin, who blocks his attack. As Rogue pulls away, Arin tries to jab at him, but gets grabbed and kicked away. Rogue then attempts to attack Arin by leaping at him, but Arin gets up and kicks him away. As they are fighting, Ras notices a Dragonian approaching him and stops them with his hammer before hitting them away. Another Dragonian lands behind him and strikes him with their blades, but he blocks the attack with his hammer. Elsewhere, Riyu hits a Dragonian down with his tail as Zane jumps to grab another one and slam them into a tree. As Zane lands, another Dragonian tries to approach him, but the one he defeated earlier falls down on top of them. As Sora is flying after Arin, two Dragonians grab onto her jet and start hitting it. She pulls some quick maneuvers to shake them off and continues flying to Arin. Meanwhile, Arin uses Spinjitzu to approach Rogue, only for Rogue to jump away to the other side of the branch and cut it off, causing Arin to fall.)

Sora: Arin! (Arin manages to grab onto a lower branch, only for Rogue to fly down to him and prepare to strike him away.) No!

(Sora uses her power to send Rogue's suit flying away. As Arin looses his grab and falls off the branch, a flashback to Arin making a pie using a new oven Sora made can be seen.)

Past Sora: (sniffs) Ah, those Ooze Berries smell amazing!

(In the present, Sora activates her jet's autopilot and jumps off towards Arin, and the flashback continues with Sora and Arin eating the pie.)

Past Sora: This could be your best pie yet.

Past Arin: You sure you don't miss the special burnt crust my campfire made? (They both laugh.)

(Present-day Sora is still falling towards Arin.)

Past Sora: Stronger together, right?

(While falling, Sora pulls out the Grapple Gun and shoots it towards Arin.)

Past Arin: Together forever. (The two grab each other's hands.)

(In the present, Arin grabs onto the hook of the grapple.)

Sora: Hold on! (The line between them catches onto a branch, stopping their fall, but causing them to swing around each other.) Take my hand!

Arin: I got you! Thanks for saving me.

Sora (simultaneously): I'm sorry.

Sora: Come back with us. It won't be like before. We can find your parents.

(Arin starts crying.)

Arin: I already found my parents. They're dead.

(Sora is shocked by this, and the two of them hug. Ras watches this happen before Drix ambushes him with Shatterspin, causing him to drop his hammer. He rushes back towards Drix, but Drix uses the hammer to slam Ras to the ground, causing the Returning Blade to also fall away from Ras. Drix then uses his power to grab the blade and holds both blades in the air triumphantly. Two Dragonians pin Riyu's wings to the ground. Zane kicks one of them away, but the other one slashes at his foot, causing it to malfunction. Two more Dragonians got to grab Ras away. Riyu and Zane look on as Drix flies away with four Dragonians, two of them carrying Ras and another carrying his hammer.)

Zane: They have all the blades. Now there is nothing to stop them from unleashing the Chaos Dragon.

(Riyu cries out as the remaining two Dragonians Drix brought with him emerge from the forest to join him in leaving.)

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
