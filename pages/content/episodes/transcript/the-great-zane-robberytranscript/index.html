(Sora, Frak, Riyu, and Lloyd are travelling together in the First Land.)

Sora: Excuse me. White, blue, and pink is where it's at. End of conversation.

Frak: It's a good look. I am not saying that it's not a good look. Mine's just cooler.

Sora: That's subjective.

Frak: Exactly. My gi color is subjectively cooler than yours.

Sora: Do you even know what subjective--

Lloyd: Will you two knock it off?

Sora: Of course, Lloyd.

Frak: As soon as you confirm my gi is the cooler color scheme.

Lloyd: I am not getting involved in this...Though everyone knows green is the best.

Sora: What was that?

Lloyd: Nothing! Stay focused. It's getting harder and harder to find the trail that Arin...and whoever he's with have left.

Frak: What if he's stuck alone with Ras? Ras is a master manipulator. He could be brainwashing Arin and Arin would have no clue.

Lloyd: It's getting dark. Hard to see. We better stop and make camp.

(Riyu chases fireflies)

Sora: Did you know Arin's the one who taught me how to make a campfire? Yeah. In Imperium, all our heating was turned on by a switch. (Sigh) I didn't know that I could miss a person this much. How does Zane deal with P.I.X.A.L. being missing for so long?

Frak: Zane's a Nindroid. Can he even have emotions?

Lloyd: (laughs) You have a lot to learn about Zane, Frak. He is much much more than a machine.

Sora: I hope he's okay. We haven't heard from him since we have split up.

Lloyd: Maybe he's having more success at finding Arin than we are.

(Zane and Zanth are exploring the First Land together.)

Zane: No significant signs of life--organic or otherwise. Let us move on to the next valley.

(Zanth sees a bird and begins to dance with it)

Zane: Do not dance with birds, Zanth. I once did such things but have since learned that behavior is considered weird.

(Zanth groans sadly. The bird sings as he flies away with Zane on his back.)

Zane: This area looks as empty as the others.

(As Zanth flies over a group of rocks, Zane is now able to see a new Monastery up on the horizon.)

Zane: Could that be what I think it is?

(Zanth lands down in front of the First Land Monastery.)

Zane: This is like the other Monasteries we have discovered. Similar to ours, yet strangely different. (Zane hops off of Zanth and approaches the Monastery gates.)

(Zanth sniffs)

Zane: If my theory that each realm gained its own Monastery during the Merge is true, then this must be this land's Monastery. (Zane enters the Monastery.) Hello? Is anyone here? (Zane observes the First Land's Monastery portal gate.) A portal gate! Exactly like the other Monasteries!

(The portal gate starts to activate, Zane quickly hides when he hears voices approaching, Rapton and the Freebooters come out from inside the monastery.)

Freebooter: Come on, Rapton! This would be a whole lot easier if you'd just let us use a cart!

Rapton: No way, carts are Rapton's nemesis! They know what they did. Ah, besides, a little hard work never hurt anybody.

Freebooter: Clearly, you've forgotten about Richard, and statistically, hard work is the leading cause of--

Rapton: It's about to become the leading cause of my boot in--

(Zane interrupts Rapton, introducing his presence to them)

Zane: Um... Excuse me, Rapton. I do not believe those items belong to you or the Freebooters.

Rapton: Oh, great, the ice cream ninja.

Freebooter: Uh, we have paperwork, Mr. ninja, from a... dude. The chain of ownership is kinda murky, but--

Rapton: Will you let Rapton do the talking? Just blast him!

(The Freebooters start blasting at Zane. Zane uses his Elemental Power of Ice to create a path for him to slide on.)

Rapton: Don't drop any of that stuff!

Freebooter: Please don't question our professionalism. We're very good looters.

Rapton: Oh, so then you don't need a cart!

Freebooter: Ugh! Foiled by my own logic!

(As the Freebooters and Rapton make their retreat, Zane examines the item the Freebooters have been carrying since they exited the portal gate. A Techno-cocoon, similar to that of what Zane was entrapped in after the Merge.)

Zane: It cannot be!

(Zane tries to open the Monastery doors, but when they won't open, he punches them off in a frantic rush. By then, Rapton and the Freebooters have already deposited all of their loot onto a hijacked Imperian train, and are starting their leave. Zane tries his best to catch the train, but he isn't fast enough.)

Zane: No!

(Zanth picks up Zane.)

Zane: Excellent timing, Zanth! Try to get me closer! (Zanth is rapidly approaching the train, Zane starts to prepare for his jump.) Almost... (Zane jumps and lands on the train, Zanth remains back. Zane starts scanning the train from the top.) No life forms inside... there! Just what I was looking for! (Zane jumps through a vent to get to the inside of the train shortly before he would've been hit by a rock. Zane observes the piles of loot in the train cart upon entering.) Kreel would be jealous. It is a literal junkyard in here. The Freebooters have been more successful at looting than I would have imagined. But where... A-ha! (Zane finds the Techno-cocoon.) If Kai and Nya could open the Cocoon I was found in, surely I can find a way to open this one. (Zane finds a cable to the cocoon that is unplugged, he plugs it in, and the cocoon starts powering up. It shortly opens.)

P.I.X.A.L.: Primary Interactive X-ternal Assistant Life-Form now online.

Zane: P.I.X.A.L.!

P.I.X.A.L.: Zane?

(Fedulian is pushing a cart outside of his shop in the Crossroads. Fedulian notices a ninja star thrown at his door.)

Fedulian: (sighing) Can't he ever come at a normal hour? Guess when you're him, you show up whenever you want. Just wish he'd stop poking holes in my door.

(Fedulian walks to the alleyway beside his shop.)

Fedulian: A successful mission? (Fedulain throws the ninja star back at Rogue. Rogue catches it.)

Rogue: I never fail.

Fedulian: May I see it?

Rogue: Money first.

Fedulian: Oh, of course. (Fedulian hands a bag of money to Rogue.)

Rogue: Congratulations. You just bought yourself a rare Oni chalice from the First Realm.

Fedulain: (hisses in delight) I never dreamed I'd get my hands on such a thing. Glorious!

Rogue: Then our transaction is complete.

Fedulain: Wait. I was asked to pass a message on to you. Someone else wants to contract your services.

Rogue: Who is this from?

Fedulian: I don't know. They were very secretive. Asked you to meet them at that location in two days' time.

(P.I.X.A.L. jumps down from the cocoon and hugs Zane.)

Zane: I was beginning to fear I would never see you again!

P.I.X.A.L.: I do not understand, Zane. Why are you acting like this? We just saw each other this morning. Though I am not sure I got in this weird place.

Zane: What is the last thing you remember?

P.I.X.A.L.: Some kind of strange storm. Then everything went dark. And now, you are letting me out of this...thing.

Zane: P.I.X.A.L., it has been years since we have seen each other. The storm you remember is something we now call "The Merge," a mysterious event that combined all the realms into one, apparently orchestrated by Master Wu.

P.I.X.A.L.: Master Wu? Is he here as well?

(Zane grabs both of P.I.X.A.L.'s hands.)

Zane: It is very likely he has departed. Many strange things have happened since The Merge. I woke up in a similar techno-cocoon inside another monastery we found. I will have to investigate why that would happen to us both.

P.I.X.A.L.: There are other monasteries?

Zane: There are also new ninja!

Zane: (Changes voice to mimic Arin): Like Arin! He's self-taught in the art of Spinjitzu...(he performs Spinjitzu) and the art of making pies!

Zane (mimicking Sora's voice): And Sora! A master at elemental tech abilities. Not sure about her pie-making abilities, however.

Zane: Then, there's Riyu! He's a small dragon and doesn't talk like this. He mostly makes sounds like--

(Zane growls and snorts in an imitation of Riyu's voice. P.I.X.A.L. chuckles.)

Zane (mimicking Wyldfyre's voice): Oh,and I can't forget Wyldfyre. She would be upset she wasn't mentioned first. She's fiery. But not Kai fiery. It was a thing.

P.I.X.A.L.: I am sad to have missed all that.

Zane: I am sad you have as well. During my most dire times since the Merge, when I was losing hope, I would hear a voice in my head. A voice that would center me, giving me drive to continue, even when the odds were increasingly against us.

(Zane reaches for both of P.I.X.A.L.'s hands. She takes them.)

Zane: That voice, P.I.X.A.L., was yours.

(They smile at each other.)

Rapton: Awww.

(Rapton and the Freebooters enter.)

Rapton: Hate to break up this tender moment...Ha! Nah! Rapton loves ruining tender moments!

Freebooter: That's an odd thing to be in love with.

Rapton: I was being rhetorically dramatic! You really should watch more theater!

(Rapton and the Freebooters start approaching closer.)

Rapton: Hey, Ninja, that robot is our loot, and we hate losing our loot! So either you voluntarily cage yourselves. or we're gonna have to turn you both into scrap.

(Zane and P.I.X.A.L. stand defiantly at them, then look at each other.)

P.I.X.A.L.: Shall we do what we do best?

Zane: I have missed you.

(Zane and P.I.X.A.L. plunge into Rapton and the Freebooters, starting the fight. Zane heads towards Rapton and jumps while on top of him.)

Zane: Ah... (Zane lands.) h'yah!

(Zane uses Spinjitzu to hide behind a stack of crates. He pushes the crates back, causing them to topple over some of the Freebooters.

Freebooter: Look out!

(P.I.X.A.L. grabs a plunger and uses it to fight.)

P.I.X.A.L.: Ah! R'yah!

(Zane and P.I.X.A.L. bump into each other and gasp. They then fight next to each other.)

Freebooter: H'yah!

(P.I.X.A.L. uses her plunger to stick to the lunging Freebooters face. P.I.X.A.L. then informs Zane.)

P.I.X.A.L.: Incoming!

Zane: Understood.

(The two of them knock out the Freebooter. The Freebooters then try to all attack them at once.)

Zane: H'yah! (Zane freezes two Freebooters, in which Zane and P.I.X.A.L. each smash one into the ground. Zane then speaks to P.I.X.A.L.) I love the other Ninja, but you are the only one who thinks the way I do, P.I.X.A.L.

P.I.X.A.L.: I am a bit surprised to hear you so emotional.

Zane: It has been a long time for me. (He uses his power to try and blast Rapton, who leaps behind a large pile of crates.)

Rapton: We have 'em!

Freebooter: How exactly do we have them?

(Behind them, several Freebooters are getting knocked back.)

Rapton: Ah, well, we will have them very shortly.

Freebooter: Appreciate the clarification (She picks up a metallic object and throws it over the crates at Zane and P.I.X.A.L.)

(The other Freebooters also start trowing objects. Zane and P.I.X.A.L. run behind a crate for cover.)

P.I.X.A.L.: We appear to be pinned down.

Zane: Appearances can be deceiving. We simply need to leave (He pulls out a smoke bomb.) in classic ninja style.

(Zane hops onto a pile of crates and trows the smoke bomb at his feet, causing a large cloud of smoke to cover both him and P.I.X.A.L.)

Rapton: (coughs) Wh-what? Is that a campfire? (coughs) (When the smoke clears, he sees that one of the doors on the side of the train had been opened, and is now closing back up.) There!

Freebooter: (rushes to the now closed door and tries to open it.) It's locked!

(Zane and P.I.X.A.L. are standing on the side of the train. They remove their masks.)

Rapton (from inside the train): Get it open!

Zane: We need a safe spot to leap!

(P.I.X.A.L. pushes Zane against the train, so he doesn't hit the boulders they pass.)

P.I.X.A.L.: Somewhere we won't get crushed against a rock.

Zane: That also. Up ahead! (He points to an enormous stone arch in the distance.) Get ready to jump! One, two...

(Zane gets interrupted by a sudden flash of red energy.)

P.I.X.A.L.: Is that lightning?

(The pair climb up and look to the sky to see Nokt approaching the train along with five Dragonian Trackers.)

Zane: This is very bad.

P.I.X.A.L.: You know them?

Zane: I know the one in front. (Nokt and the Dragonians land on the train.) And he'll probably tell us something like- (mimicking Nokt's voice) I will crush you to your doom.

Nokt: Come, Dragonians! Let us crush them to their doom!

Zane: Sometimes, I hate being so accurate. Nokt is most likely coming for me. We must get off this train and get as far away as possible. On the count of three, we jump. One, two-

Nokt: We don't leave here without the blade! (He jumps into the same hatch Zane used to enter the train, and the Dragonians follow his lead.) No one onboard remains alive!

Zane: So Nokt is here for some other reason. I hate when I am inaccurate as well.

P.I.X.A.L.: He wants some sort of blade? So we continue doing what we do best.

(Inside the train, the Freebooters and Dragonians are fighting. One Dragonian flies up high and swoops down to strike away a Freebooter with their swords, then proceeds to kick Rapton in the back and pursue him. Another Freebooter gets kicked away by a different Dragonian, who then flies off to pursue them. As this is going on, Nokt notices a Freebooter looking at him and turns to face them, only for a Dragonian to fly in and push them away. they briefly manage to use their sword to hold off the Dragonian, only to get swiftly tackled, grabbed and tossed away, nearly flying out of the train's back entrance.)

Rapton: (tries using his sword to hold back the Dragonians) Who are these people now? (He holds up his sword to block an attacking Dragonian.) Can't anyone just loot in peace anymore?

(The Dragonian that attacked Rapton flies away. A Freebooter next to him tries to shoot at the Dragonians, but get sent flying into a wall when a Dragonian rolls over to them and kicks them away. Rapton manages to evade this attack and hide behind a pile of boxes, only for another Dragonian to fly on top of the pile. Elsewhere, a Dragonian is swooping down on a Freebooter, who prepares to block. Another Dragonian is fighting a freebooter, who dodges one of their attacks by rolling away. In this chaos, Nokt is shifting trough the Freebooters' loot, looking for the Prismatic Blade.)

Nokt: (growls) It has to be here somewhere! (He looks over to his right, and gasps when he notices the blade lying on the ground. He starts walking over to pick it up.) Finally.

Zane: (picks up the blade before Nokt) H'yah! (He performs Spinjitzu to get away.)

Nokt: What? (Zane makes his way on top of a crate.) You! I'd been hoping for a rematch with the ninja.

Zane: I have recently seen another blade like this. Its kind of power should not be in your hands!

Nokt: I don't care what you think. (He jumps up to attack Zane with his sword.)

(Zane jumps away to avoid the attack, grabbing onto a nearby wall. Nokt swings at him again three more times with short hops, but Zane blocks it with the Prismatic Blade and leaps away to a crate, then quikly jumps up to avoid another leaping attack by Nokt, who pursues him. The two land facing each other, and Nokt performs a quick thrust with his sword that Zane avoids, he follows it up with a wide slash which Zane jumps over, quikly retaliating with a slash of his own that Nokt jumps over. Nokt then slashes again, and Zane leans back to avoid the attack, then steps forward to strike Nokt's sword with the Prismatic Blade. Nokt performs another thrust that Zane dodges, then follows up with a leaping overhead slash, locking blades with Zane. A Dragonian flies over them, and lands to scare away Freebooter and two other Freebooters, who run past Zane and Nokt fighting. The Dragonian corners them and prepares to strike, when P.I.X.A.L. suddenly tackles them from the side. They fall to the ground.)

Freebooter: Um, are we on the same side?

P.I.X.A.L.: For now.

Freebooter: Oh, thank goodness. I couldn't handle being kicked by more people.

(The Dragonian gets back up and charges towards P.I.X.A.L. with a quick jab. She dodges the attack and jumps up to kick down the Dragonian. Another Dragonian swoops down at her, but she performs a flip and lands on top of them, pinning them to the ground. A third Dragonian flies past her to get to the cowering Freebooters. They lock blades with one of them, but get disarmed by another one shooting at them. The two Freebooters then pin the Dragonian to the ground. P.I.X.A.L. looks over to see the Freebooters piled up on the Dragonian. She then turns around an sees another Dragonian attacking her, who she dodges and slams against a crate before kicking them in the back of the head. Meanwhile, Zane is dodging Nokt's attacks.)

Nokt: Give me that blade! (He charges at Zane to perform a quick slash, but Zane dodges it and kicks him back. Zane then retreats near the now empty Techno-cocoon, where P.I.X.A.L., Rapton and a few Freebooters are held up.)

(A Dragonian swoops in and tackles Zane, causing him to drop the Prismatic Blade, which is grabbed by another Dragonian. P.I.X.A.L. jumps after the Dragonian with the blade, but they fly off before she can reach them. The Dragonian then flies towards Nokt to give the blade to him, but P.I.X.A.L. jumps up behind them and kicks them away, causing the blade to go flying. She manages to grab the blade, and prepares to fight Nokt with it. Nokt slashes at her, but she jumps over him and starts to run away. She climbs onto a pile of crates, and exits the train via the hatch on the ceiling.)

Nokt: Bring me that sword! (The Dragonians start following P.I.X.A.L., allowing Zane and Rapton to breathe for a moment.)

(P.I.X.A.L arrives on top of the train, the Dragonians following closely behind her. Nokt leaps out of the hatch and takes a fighting stance. The Dragonians land behind him. Zane arrives on top of the train using Spinjitzu, and gets between P.I.X.A.L. and Nokt. Nokt starts to get ready to use his power, but gets interrupted by a shot from Rapton, who is standing next to him alongside three Freebooters. The Freebooters engage three of the Dragonians, and lock blades with them. Nokt starts approaching Zane and P.I.X.A.L., the latter of who starts to run away.)

Nokt: After her!

(The Dragonian next to Nokt strikes down the Freebooter they were fighting, and flies off after P.I.X.A.L. They eventually get ahead of her, and start delivering rapid stirek with their two swords. P.I.X.A.L. manages to attack them with the Prismatic Blade, and knocks them back. Meanwhile Nokt powers up his Shatterspin and charges towards Zane. Zane tries hitting him with blasts of Ice, but he swiftly dodges then and knocks Zane aside. The Dragonian continues delivering rapid strikes against P.I.X.A.L., until finally flying up and swooping down for a more powerful attack. P.I.X.A.L. is able to block it, but the Dragonian follows up with a kick that knocks P.I.X.A.L. back, and she nearly falls off the train. She looks behind her, only to see Noktswiftly charge at her with a Shatterspin attack. The force of this blow sends her rolling away, and she drops the blade.)

Nokt: (grabs the blade) Yes! (He sees Zane behind him, struggling to get up.)

Zane: No! (He blasts at Nokt with Ice, but Nokt deflects the blow with his sword. He then charges forward and attempts to blast Nokt again, but Nokt does a backflip to evade the attack)

(Zane notices two Dragonians swooping down at him from above. He jumps over them and freezes their wings with his power, and they fall behind the train.)

Nokt: Forget the ninja, the weak point is the train.

(The Dragonian standing next to Nokt puts away their swords and flies to the front of the train. There, they pull off a metal plate covering some wiring and proceed to stab both of their blades into the wires. P.I.X.A.L. tries to reach the front of the train as well, but before she can, the train starts to move uncontrollably. Everyone on top of the train struggle to not fall off, except for Nokt.)

Nokt: It was nice to see you again. (He flies off, accompanied by the Dragonian who damaged the train. The two Dragonians who had been stuck fighting the Freebooters also leave with them.)

P.I.X.A.L.: Zane! The train is going faster! We're going to crash!

Zane: Nokt is escaping, and there is no way to get the fallen Freebooters safely off the train.

P.I.X.A.L.: And we are about to crash. Don't forget the crash!

Zane: I must find a way to override the train controls!

P.I.X.A.L.: Manually? But the chances of success are-

Zane: I am well aware of my slim chances.

P.I.X.A.L.: Of course you are.

(Zane uses Spinjitzu to leap over to the exposed wiring. P.I.X.A.L. tries to follow him, but gets knocked back by a piece of debris suddenly breaking off the train.)

Zane: (removes his hood) This should work. (He pulls out two cables and tries to connect them, receiving a powerful electric shock that knocks him back in the process.) I was presumptuous. (The train is approaching a mountain.) And we are out of time. (Zane pulls out a cable from his back and prepares to plug it into the train.)

P.I.X.A.L.: Zane! No!

(Zane plugs the cable into a panel. This gives him a heavy shock again, but this time he holds firm. Everyone else on the train prepares for the inevitable crash, but before it can happen, the train screeches to a halt. P.I.X.A.L. opens her eyes to see Zane has suffered heavy damage.)

Rapton: (gets up) Rapton saves us yet again!

Zane: I was... not sure... (He falls off the train.)

P.I.X.A.L.: Zane! (She leaps down to Zane.) Zane, no! Listen to me! It's P.I.X.A.L., hear my voice! Zane, come back to me!

Zane: P.I.X.A.L... I thought I'd... lost you...

P.I.X.A.L.: Never again. (Zane struggles to get back up.) You're seriously damaged. We need to get you help, quickly.

Zane: We have... other problems. The Forbidden Five... must be stopped.

(At the Algae farmers' village, a storm of red energy suddenly appears. Rox, along with eight Dragonian Trackers, descend upon the village. The villagers begin to run away from them in panic, as Rox commands the Dragonians to start destroying the place. While they begin doing so, she senses the presence of another Prismatic Blade by a nearby building. She hovers over to the building, then stops and dives into the water surrounding it. There she finds the tooth Lloyd and Arin knocked out of the mouth of the Crab monster. She lifts it out of the water, and smashes it against the floor, revealing a small blade. She picks up the blade)

Rox: Hmm... Exactly what we came for. (laughs)

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
