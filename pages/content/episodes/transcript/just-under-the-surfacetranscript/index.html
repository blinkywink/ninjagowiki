(Mr. Frohicky and Geo are carrying bowls of soup at the Monastery of Spinjitzu.)

Mr. Frohicky: ...Well, they try in their own way, but not one of them I would consider a fine chef. Kai can defrost pizza. Poorly.

Geo: Had lots of time to learn how to cook in the Land of Lost Things. Ton of ingredients show up there. I once found a tray of diced garlic. Oh, I reeked for a month, but, mm... delicious!

Mr. Frohicky: Well, having you here has made the part of my job where I'm expected to be a Master Chef easier. (chuckles) Now, if you could learn to polish a floor...

Fritz: Come on, play with us!

Rontu: Oh, I'm not that healed yet. I'll just watch you two. Ah, refreshments. Mm. (grabs a bowl soup and drinks it) Ah, I've been parched.

Geo: Glad to you see you doing so well, Rontu. We, uh... had some doubts you would pull through.

Rontu: You are not the only one. I must confess, I had lost hope myself.

Mr. Frohicky: Well, fetid salamander broth is such a strong, natural medicine, it could grow skin on a Greenbone Warrior!

Rontu: (chuckles politely) Yes... though the broth did taste... (clears throat) ...fine, my return was more spiritual. I spoke to an old love, and discovered a new purpose. My work on this plane is not done.

Mr. Frohicky: Nope, pretty sure it was the broth.

Cole: (opens door and smells the food) That smells good. Geo, are you cooking here now?

Geo: Yup! but I have to go out and buy ingredients, not wait for a geyser to burp them up.

Mr. Frohicky: Well, apparently, that's more reliable than sending Arin's parents off with a grocery list. They've been gone for--

(Jenny and Barry barge in, exhausted)

Jenny: There was an attack in the Crossroads! Some kind of Energy Monster!

Cole: Energy Monster?

Jenny: Came out of nowhere. We got people to safety, but then it just disappeared as weirdly as it appeared. Smashed the whole place up.

Mr. Frohicky: And this monster kept you from getting the fermented beetles why?

Cole: No, they did the right thing, Mr. Frohicky. But an Energy monster? Ras stole scrolls about containing energy. Maybe the puzzle pieces are all in a row...

Geo: Um, "falling into place." Ducks are in a row, pieces fall into place.

Cole: I... I knew that.

(Cole arrives at the Crossroads. He finds the place damaged and sees people cleaning up.)

Cole: Did you see the Monster that attacked, and where it went?

Woman: Saw it? Dang near blasted my hair off!

Merlopian: Then the thing just evaporated into thin air.

(Cole sees a Loyalist scan the damage with a device. They are surprised to see Cole and run off.)

Cole: Not suspicious behavior at all.

(Cole chases after the Loyalist. The Loyalist pushes down some boxes, which fall on Cole. Cole tries to use his power, but fails.)

(The Loyalist runs away and people come to help get the boxes off of Cole, who gets out from under them.)

Cole: Anyone-- ow-- see where that person in the mask ran off to? Well, looks like I stumbled into something painfully nefarious...

(In the Department of Vacation Simulation, Kai is annoyed at how long Jay and Nya have been kissing each other.)

Kai: Uh... you two done yet?

Nya: You remember everything now?

Jay: Not everything. As soon as that machine processed, I got a flood of memories, but more keep popping up, and I don't know what I don't know, so who knows how many more memories I'm gonna recall.

Nya: But you're back?

Jay: I'm back.

Kai: So, uh, does that mean you remember the time you fought Bolobo in just your underpants?

Jay: That one... that one's not ringing a bell.

Kai: What about when you convinced a bunch of video game fan boys to dress up like you, and talk about how awesome you were?

Jay: Nope. Not that one either.

Kai: Are you sure? Maybe you just don't want to remember those stories? 'Cause if I were you, I'd try to forget them, too.

Jay: No, I'm serious. It feels like my memories are "healing", just repopulating my mind.

Nya: And who erased your memories in the first place?

Jay: That... I still don't remember, yet, but I'm sure it'll-- (realizes something and gasps) Oh my! (cries)

Kai: Uh... you okay, buddy?

Jay: Mr. Slowthers! (sobs) Poor Mr. Slowthers.

Kai: Who is Mr. Slowthers?

Jay: My turtle! (sniffles) My pet turtle I had when I was a little kid! He ran away, and I couldn't catch up to him!

Kai: You couldn't catch up to a... turtle?

Jay: Apparently, I wasn't very good at running as a kid, okay? Leave me alone! (sobs)

Nya: I know getting your memories back in weird bursts like this has got to be a shock, and I'm sure Mr. Slowthers found a new safe place somewhere, but we gotta do the same. The Administration is not going to stop looking for us. Come on.

Jay: (whispers) I bet these robots are guarding all the exits. Curses on whoever came up with these things.

Lead Agent: Attention, Delta League, another unauthorized energy being spotted in Region 412. Assemble a convoy!

Agent: Copy that.

(Administration Agents gather up and get in their vehicles. Jay, Kai, and Nya sneak under one of the vehicles.)

Lead Agent: Agents, roll out!

(Lloyd is still screaming as he falls under the Trees of Life during the Ritual of Unrooting. Lloyd sees all the roots touch the water at the bottom and glow with power. One of the roots can't reached the water and starts to wither. The other roots grab some of the water and bring it to the withered root, allowing it to grow and reach the water. Lloyd goes back up to the field.)

Lloyd: Hello? Where'd you go? What was all that? You're not gonna explain the meaning of that? It looked like a forest, sharing energy? Helping other trees out? Hello? What was I supposed to learn from that?

Tween Lloyd: Oh, you're gonna find out...

Lloyd: Another version of me? But... I don't recognize you.

Tween Lloyd: Of course you don't. I never existed. I am the you you skipped over, but on this journey, you have to know everything about your life... even paths not walked. My path.

Lloyd: You're the age I skipped over because of the Tomorrow's Tea. The age when I was supposed to hear the calling of the Whispering Wood. Pleased to finally... meet you.

(Lloyd shakes hands with himself.)

(Meanwhile, in front of the Temple of the Green Ninja. Zane is drawing and Euphrasia tries to practice her Spinjitzu. Arin is distracted by Lloyd's long absence in the temple.)

Euphrasia: Uh, is this right? I don't feel the balance you-- Arin?

Arin: Sorry, I just... He's been in there all night.

Euphrasia: I'm sure he's fine. Okay, not sure, but isn't that why we're training? To try not to focus on what's out of our control?

Arin: Yeah. Okay. So your stance is slightly off. The twist shouldn't put pressure on your knee, you'll get injured that way. It should come from your core, like this... (performs Spinjitzu.)

Zane: (scans Arin's Spinjitzu) Arin, I notice you have red streaks in your tornado. Why is that?

Arin: (nervously) Uh...

Euphrasia: No one else has them? Is that weird?

Zane: I've never encountered it before.

Arin: Is my Spinjitzu corrupted or something? Did training with Ras do something bad to me?

Zane: I'm sure there's no reason to be alarmed. I'd just like to study it more-- (They hear noises.) The Acona? (The Acona appear, panicking.) I'm getting the sense they want us to go with them.

Euphrasia: Are we going to just leave Lloyd?

Zane: We'll come back, but if these beings need help.

(Arin, Euphrasia adn Zane leave with the Acona.)

(The Administration Agents arrive at the location of the monster sighting.)

Lead Agent: We're here looking into reports of a monster on an unlicensed rampage. Did you report a monster? Because there's clearly no ongoing rampage. Filing a false report is a severe policy violation, punishable by being made to fill out many reports about the false report. Do you have anything to say in your defense?

Lead Loyalist: Here's the situation. I'm taking control of your convoy. You've got one chance to give your vehicles to me. If not, I take them by force, which will go badly for you.

Lead Agent: Ha! Look, not only are you unarmed, and badly outnumbered, but you've clearly got no idea who you're dealing with. We're from The Administration!

Lead Loyalist: I warned you. (She snaps her fingers, and several of the Administration Agents present reveal themselves as undercover Loyalists.) You're the one with no idea how many of our secret society have infiltrated every organization across the Merged Lands.

Merlopian Agent: Bonnie, no! You love stamping C-01 permits! You always smile--

Bonnie: I was faking it, every single time.

Lead Loyalist: Attack!

(the Administration Agents fight the Loyalists.)

Nya: (She puts on her mask and tries to head out, but Jay grabs her.) Ugh! Ah! We've gotta get out there!

Jay: Which side would we even join? We need to see how this plays out.

Kai: Let's find out what's going on.

Lead Agent: Agents, retreat!

(The Administration Agents fall back.)

Lead Loyalist: We've got what we need. (whistles) Mech Drivers, you know what to do! (The Loyalists use the Master mechs to smashed the nearby rock wall, revealing a hidden cave.) Success! All hail the Good Empress!

Nya: "Good Empress"?

Kai: As in Beatrix, the whacked-out, power-hungry maniac?

Jay: She sounds kinda fun.

Kai: No.

Nya: She's not.

Lead Loyalist: Come! We must find the weapon piece!

Nya: Weapon? Great... Last time Beatrix got her hands on a super weapon, she triggered a world-ending series of MergeQuakes!

Jay: That was her? That was messed up!

Kai: Whatever this new "weapon" is, we have to keep it out of her hands.

(Jay, Kai, and Nya put their masks on and enter the cave.)

(Arin, Euphrasia, Zane and The Acona hurry back to the tree house hideout, where the Arc Dragon of Life is being attacked by Elemental Monsters. The Acona try to fend them off. The Arc Dragon of Life roars and starts withering due to the decline of the Whispering Wood.)

Zane: Ninja... (puts his mask on) Go! (performs Spinjitzu)

(Euphrasia uses her powers and Arin performs Spinjitzu. The Arc Dragon of Life attacks an Acid Monster that spews out acid. Zane uses his Spinjitzu to block it, and throws his shurikens at the Acid Monster. Euphrasia blasts an Earth Monster, but an Ice Monster jumps behind her.)

Euphrasia: (tries to use her powers) Aw...

(Arin kicks an Earth Monster and Zane kicks the Acid Monster. The Arc Dragon of Life keeps withering and roars.)

(Jay, Kai, and Nya sneak around to find out what the Loyalists are doing in the hidden cave. Jay stares at the Tree of Madness and removes his mask. Nya grabs him and pulls him away.)

Nya: How can a tree be growing underground with no sunlight? -It's--

Jay: It's madness. The Land of Madness!

Lead Loyalist: (She uses a device to scan the tree.) This is it. (She puts her hand into a hole in the Tree of Madness and pulls out a yellow corkscrew-shaped object.) Behold! A portion of the Weapon of Desolation! The Good Empress is one step closer to making her triumphant return! (The Loyalists cheer.) Pack it up! We move.

(Jay suddenly laughs out loudly. Kai tries to shush him down as the Loyalists hear it.)

Kai: (whispers) What are you doing?

Jay: Hee-hee-he hit every branch on the way down! (cackles)

Nya: What?!

(A Loyalist spots the ninja.)

Loyalist: Ninja are here!

Jay: I'm sorry, I can't help it! A memory just suddenly hit me! This time, I hid Cole's cake at the top of a tree! He slipped and fell, and hit every single branch on the way down! -H'ah! -H'yah!

Kai: (chuckles) That was pretty funny.

Lead Loyalist: Cover my back!

Kai: H'yah! (performs Spinjitzu) I'll take that, thank you! (He touches the yellow object which causes his elemental powers to go haywire, creating an explosion.)

Jay: Nya!

Nya: I'm fine! They're getting away!

(A Loyalist throws down a device, which creates a force field to trap the Ninja. Kai tries to punch it.)

Jay: (takes his mask off) What happened to you back there?

Kai: I touched that thing, and my body was flooded with uncontrollable Elemental Energy. Real weird.

Nya: Whatever a "Weapon of Desolation" is, we can't let it get to Beatrix!

(Zane throws shurikens at an Acid Monster and an Earth Monster, Arin performs Spinjitzu to throw away another Acid Monster, and Euphrasia blasts an Ice Monster, which the Acona attack. The Whispering Wood keeps withering, Euphrasia and Arin dodge as a Mutation Monster attacks them from behind. it then goes to pursue Euphrasia. Arin wathces as the Ice Monster blasts back the Acona.)

Arin: Huah! (performs Object Spinjitzu with a rock, throwing it at the Ice Monster. The monsters arm breaks.)

Euphrasia: Ugh! That kinda worked. (She blasts the Mutation Monster.)

(The Ice Monster's arm quickly grows back.)

Arin: If I could do it faster... Hold it, I got an idea! When I say when, toss me rocks! (Euphrasia agrees.) Now! (He performs Spinjitzu, and Euphrasia throws rocks into his Spinjitzu. The rocks build up momentum from the Spinjitzu, and fly off to hit the Ice Monster and Mutation Monster, defeating them.) It's working. Keep' em coming! (Euphrasia keeps throwing rocks at Arin's Spinjitzu, which he charges with Spinjitzu power, defeating all the remaining Elemental Monsters. Arin gets exhausted and falls down from the bridge, but Arc Dragon of Life catches him.)

Zane: Arin! That was beyond all predictive models. (takes his mask off.) No one has ever used spinjitzu that way. It shouldn't even be possible.

Arin: (takes his mask off) I need a nap.

Zane: The monsters have returned to an energy state. They will come back, and there's no telling if Arin will be able to stop them again.

(The Arc Dragon of Life roars and faints.)

Zane: Lloyd is our only hope. If he doesn't succeed in his trial, the Whispering Wood will be lost.

(In Umbrasilvis)

Wyldfyre: I'm not saying you're the coolest dragon I've ever seen now, Riyu, but you're not not the coolest dragon I've ever seen! (Riyu grabs Wyldfyre and shows off his techniques.) Whoa-ho!

Frak: But all that energy he absorbed. Are you sure he's okay?

Wyldfyre: Let me answer your question with a question. How many times do I need to tell you I'm not a dragon doctor?

Sora: All that Elemental Energy must be what triggered Riyu's new form. Still doesn't explain who released the monster from its cage or why?

Frak: It has to be that masked guy I chased, right?

Sora: But who's under the mask?

Frak: Someone who knew the airship's layout. One of the Explorers?

Sora: No, that makes no sense. The Explorers were desperate to haul that monster back to Ninjago, and put it on display. Why would one of them release it?

Wyldfyre: (blows raspberry) Nothing those weirdos do makes sense. (The Explorer's Club members arrive.) Speaking of... look who's here.

Johnny Courageous: I say, if you still want a ride back to Ninjago, we will be leaving forthwith.

Reginald: Yes. Ever since someone destroyed our specimen, there's no point in staying in this benighted hayseed village. Best return to the Explorer's Clubhouse and await another chance to secure a glorious legacy.

Johnny Courageous: Can't get a decent cuppa round here anyway. Peasants.

Frak: We do still need to get back to the other Ninja as quickly as we can.

Johnny Courageous: We just need to resupply, then we'll be on our way.

Grand Inquisitor: No, you will not!

Wyldfyre: Who are you supposed to be? Some kind of headwear salesman?

Umbrasilvan #3: (blows a bugle) Presenting... the Grand Inquisitor of Umbrasilvis!

Grand Inquisitor: Outsiders! Nothing good ever comes from outsiders!

Reginald: Well, that's just nonsense. If we Explorers had never come, you'd never have been discovered by anyone. Where would you be then?

Grand Inquisitor: Someone released a monster on purpose to wreak havoc in our fair town! Umbrasilvis shall be on total lockdown until we determine who it was! The brigand must pay!

Sora: You don't understand. We need to get back to--

Grand Inquisitor: No one leaves here until someone is punished. So proclaims the Grand Inquisitor!

(The Umbrasilvans cheer.)

(Mr. Frohicky is scrubbing Rontu as he hears knocking on the gates. He opens them to find Percival.)

Mr. Frohicky: Senator Tartigrade! Always nice to see you, but this is a strange hour for a visit.

Percival: These are strange times, Mr. Frohicky. I need to talk to the Ninja.

Mr. Frohicky: My apologies, they are currently in parts unknown. Cole's the only one in town, but he's out investigating reports of an energy monster.

Percival: An energy monster? Where?

Mr.Frohicky: The Crossroads.

Percival: Wow. That's exactly why I'm here. Imperium was invaded by a strange energy creature. It caused a lot of damage, then disappeared. I was hoping the Ninja could help us figure out what it was.

Mr.Frohicky: I'll tell Cole as soon as he returns.

Percival: Thank you, we need the help. Imperium's infrastructure can't handle more attacks like that.

(Cole sneaks near the Loyalist hideout. The Loyalist he was chasing runs to the hideout's door and knocks on it in code. Another Loyalist opens the door, and the two of them perform a secret handshake before entering.)

Cole: Bigger than I thought. I should've brought back-up.

Dr.Larow/Chief Loyalist: Yes, you should have... but you didn't. Stun him but keep him alive. (The Loyalists start blasting at Cole and he dodges. Cole kicks one of the Loyalists and kicks a small rock to disarm another. A third Loyalist electrocutes Cole from behind.) A Ninja on our trail. How predictable.

(The Loyalists grab Cole and drag him away.)
