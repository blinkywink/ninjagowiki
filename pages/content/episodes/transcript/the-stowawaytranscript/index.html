(Jay is seen being chased by a pair of Red Visors through the streets of Terra Technica. He glances back at them and slides into another side street, only to find another pair approaching him. Jay runs away again, and they chase after him. Jay runs into the middle of a crowd of players walking and chatting. He sees the quartet of Red Visors walk into the area and search for him, while four others do the same.)

Red Visor: (Spots Jay.) Stop right there!

(Jay runs away. Three Red Visors shove past players and pursue Jay. He runs into another alley and sees the Ninja Tuner Car parked.)

Jay: Ooh! (He jumps inside the trunk of the car and closes the lid. He slightly lifts the lid and sees the Red Visors walk past him. He sighs and lifts up the lid completely.)

Red Visor: You! Step away from the car with your hands up! (Jay raises his hands, irritated. Suddenly, a man starts to speak.)

Man: Look, guys, this is just a big misunderstanding! (Jay turns around and peers over the lid to see him surrounded by three Visors.)

Red Visor: You heard us! (He points his gun at the man.) Move away from the—

(The man grabs the Visor's gun and wraps his arm around the Visor's arm, tearing the gun away from him. He throws him against another Visor, knocking them both to the ground. The man throws the gun against the third Visor's helmet, knocking him down as well. The man backflips into the car, puts on his seatbelt, and starts speeding away.)

Jay: Woah! (He shuts the lid of the trunk. The Red Visors get back up. While the man is speeding away, the three Visors each speed after him in their own vehicles. The first one catches up to him and rams his vehicle against the man's, who does the same to the Visor.) Ow! Ooh, ooh! Ow! Now I know how popcorn feels. (The second Visor approaches on the other side and they sandwich the Tuner Car between them.) Oh! What did I get myself into? Who is this guy? (The third Visor accelerates and rams against the back of the car. Jay lifts the lid and turns around, shouting in surprise.) Ahhh!! (A spike pops out from the front of the Visor's car.) Yikes! Whoa! (The visor accelerates and rams into the back of the car once again.)

Man: Heyyy! I just had those tail lights fixed!

Jay: Whoa! (Pushes the spike away.) Gotta get out of here. (The Visor in front of the man brakes causing the man to brake as well. Jay immediately flies out of the trunk and lands on the front hood.)

Man: Who are you?

Jay: Uh, hi. Uh, I'm—Ahhh!! (Jay grabs onto the edge of the front hood at the last moment and pulls himself up slowly, struggling. The Visor in front brakes and Jay scrambles up to avoid being squashed.) Ahhh!!

Man: Oh, you're like a ninja!

Jay: (Smiles.) Heh.

Man: Better hang on tight! (He accelerates and knocks the Visor ahead of him out. He turns into an alley and loses another Visor. The remaining try to sandwich the Tuner Car between them, but he shoves them away and approach a tight corner.)

Jay: We're not going to make it! (He twirls his weapon and uses it as a brake. They make the turn and Jay loses his weapon. The two Visors crash.)

Man: Ha! Try that one, fellas!

Red Visor: (Groans.) We need another car.

Man: Thanks, pal, you saved my— (They see a Visor approaching.) Oh, darn.

Jay: Ahhh!!! (They crash. Later, he wakes up to see the man.)

Man: You okay, buddy?

Jay: (Groans.) What just happened? I thought we were goners.

Man: You must be new here. (Lowers his bandana.) Heh. Name's Scott.

Jay: Ugh. I'm... Jay.

Scott: (He extends his hand out to Jay.) Good to meet you, Jay. I've been waiting for you a long time. (Jay takes his hand and shakes it.)

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
