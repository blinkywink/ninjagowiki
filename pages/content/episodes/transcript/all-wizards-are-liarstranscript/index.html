Zeatrix: You owe me, Fedulian. You set me up with that bounty hunter who took my money but never brought me Ras. So, it's time to pay up. Or I can always just Shockwave it out of you.

Fedulian: I thought Elemental Powers weren't working these days.

Zeatrix: Sometimes yes, sometimes no. How about we find out?

Fedulian: Fine! It's called "Operation Mentax". I don't know what it is, but they've been gathering pieces and now they have everything they need.

Zeatrix: Everything they need to do what?

Fedulian: No idea. But the chatter is that it's something no one will see coming and it could happen at any time. That's all I've got. I swear.

Zeatrix: Fine. Get out of here.

Fedulian: Always a pleasure.

Sora: Zeatrix. We got a breakthrough.

Zeatrix: Me first. The Loyalist plan is called Operation Mentax. That mean anything to you?

Sora: No, not to me. But maybe we have a way to find out.

Zeatrix: That's a dead end. It's old Imperium tech my dad funded. It has a biometric lock.

Arin: A bio- what now?

Zeatrix: It'll only activate when held by someone with permission to use it. In this case, a Loyalist.

Sora: There's got to be some way in. No system is unhackable.

Zeatrix: You two are delusional, but I like your enthusiasm. I'm going to keep digging. See what I uncover.

Wyldfyre: Wow. Imagine the size of the rabbit he could pull out of that hat.

Roby: (laughs) See, told you. Yeah.

Sora's mother: (slams the tray on the counter) Is that what people in this monastery think of us? That we're masked criminals?! I am appalled.
