(Kai and Wyldfyre eat noodles in the Monastery of Spinjitzu's library.)

Wyldfyre: So (Slurps noodles.) you rushed off to save your dragon friend, even though it was obviously a trap.

Kai: Of course. She saved me, so nothing was going to stop me from saving her. And it wasn't obviously a trap.

Wyldfyre: Well, sure, if you're stupid. For non-stupids, trap.

(In a flashback, lightning flashes in the Land of Monsters. Kai follows Bolsen up a mountain, with Fuzz on the former's back.)

Past Kai: Not that I'm not grateful, but why are you helping? This is a lot of danger for someone you just met.

Past Bolsen: The Land of Monsters changes a person, twists ya. I help others to remind myself of who I truly am.

(Kai yells in pain and holds his arm covered in green markings.)

Past Kai: Oh no. (They jump onto a platform and enter a cave. Kai sees Daidan laying on a large stone slab.) Daidan!

Past Daidan: (Weakly.) Kai. Stay back! Don't!

(The Queen of Monsters emerges from behind Daidan.)

Wyldfyre: What'd I say? Obvious trap.

(The Queen of Monsters attaches her antennas to Daidan's head. Daidan groans in pain.)

Past Kai: Daidan.

Past Fuzz: Mama, Fuzz found a tasty one.

Past Kai: Fuzz? After everything I've sacrificed for you. (He grabs Fuzz from his back and throws him, Fuzz slamming into a wall. Kai takes out his sword to attack when Bolsen stops him with his staff, the weapons clashing.) You too Bolsen? Really?

(Bolsen shoves Kai, pushing him away. Bolsen's eyes glow green, his green markings reappear, and his teeth sharpen.)

Past Bolsen: I told ya, the Land of Monsters changes a person.

Past Kai: (Looking at the green markings on his arm.) So that's what's happening to me?

Past Queen of Monsters: Finish him Bolsen, he will make a nice dessert after this dragon.

(Bolsen spins his staff and jumps at Kai. Kai avoids him and Bolsen's staff hits the ground.)

Past Fuzz: No Mama! (Fuzz jumps on Kai's back. Kai tries to shake him off.) Fuzz found this one, Fuzz will have him, not you.

(Fuzz attaches his antennas to Kai's head. Kai's eyes start to glow green and his hands and teeth get sharper. Kai and Bolsen run at each other, their weapons clashing mid-air.)

(In the present, Kai stands on a table holding a piece of paper in a dramatic pose while Wyldfyre watches.)

Wyldfyre: Woah, woah, woah, woah, hold on. So Fuzz betrayed you, then Bolsen betrayed you, then Fuzz betrayed the Queen?

Kai: Can I just finish the story?

Wyldfyre: Oh sure. Yeah. (To herself.) Peak betrayal.

(In the flashback, lightning flashes and Bolsen knocks Kai to the ground. Bolsen holds his staff at Kai's neck, but the latter tries to resist. Kai kicks Bolsen, slamming him against the wall. Kai successfully defeats Bolsen.)

Past Fuzz: See Mama, Fuzz's puppet can beat Mama's puppet. Fuzz is worthy.

Past Kai: Huh? (Kai looks at the green markings on his arm and throws Fuzz, slamming him into the wall again.) I'm no one's puppet!

(Kai's eyes return to normal. He charges towards the Queen of Monsters, but she easily swats him away with her antenna. Kai lands on the ground.)

Past Queen of Monsters: Yes, it appears Bolsen has outlived his purpose. So I shall drain him next. And you will be my new champion. (Laughs.)

(The Queen of Monsters propels her antennas towards Kai.)

Past Kai: Yeah thanks, but no thanks.

(Bolsen runs in front of Kai and protects him from the Queen of Monsters' antennas using his staff. Bolsen charges at the Queen of Monsters but she knocks him back. He lands next to Kai.)

Past Bolsen: I'll go high. You go low. Her underbelly's her weak spot.

(Bolsen and Kai charge at the Queen of Monsters. Bolsen attacks her head repeatedly.)

Past Kai: There it is.

(Kai cuts the Queen of Monsters' underbelly. Green goo sprays out of the cut and all over the cave, burying Fuzz in it. Kai helps Bolsen up.)

Past Kai: (Looking at his arm that is no longer covered in green markings.) Hey, we're free.

Past Bolsen: Yeah, but I seem to have some lasting effects. (Looks at his right hand with green markings.)

Past Fuzz: (Jumping.) Fuzz is free too! (He inches away.) I'm outta here!

(Fuzz runs away and jumps off the cave entrance, leaving his green footprints behind.)

(Daidan, Kai, and Bolsen stand the cave entrance and look at the moon.)

Past Kai: We're looking for a way out of the Land of Monsters before it really does change us forever. You in?

Past Bolsen: I can't. I have regrets. Too many. It's time to help others to really remember who I am.

(Kai and Bolsen shake hands.)

Wyldfyre: Aww, so you too wound up besties in the end.

(Bolsen jumps off the cave entrance and Kai waves.)

Kai: Oh, that wasn't the end. I had way more adventures in the Land of Monsters and had a lot left to learn.

(In the present day, Kai looks at a drawing of him and Daidan while Wyldfyre eats noodles.)

Kai: But that's all you're getting out of me today.

Wyldfyre: Glad you're not all gross and monster-y anymore.

Kai: Yeah, although sometimes I do worry that I...

(Kai starts moving weirdly and falls onto the ground. Wyldfyre puts her noodles down and stands up.)

Wyldfyre: Kai. Kai! What's happening?

Kai: Uh, I'm changing back. Run! Save yourself!

Wyldfyre: (While running.) I'm outta here!

Kai: Hah! Too easy. (Kai sits up and picks up Wyldfyre's noodle bowl, taking a slurp.) Mmm, that's good noodles.

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
