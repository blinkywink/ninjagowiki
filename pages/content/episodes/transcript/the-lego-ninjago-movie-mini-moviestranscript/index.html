SDCC Greeting

SDCC Greeting[]

Garmadon: Luh-Lloyd, what are you doing in my super-secret volcano office? Your father's working.

Lloyd: Dad, we're gonna be in San Diego. And I thought-

Garmadon: I could conquer it? Excellent warlord instinct, son. General #1, bring me my conquering flags to plant on this S-an Di-e-go Com-icon.

Lloyd: No, no more flags. We could do something father-son sight-seeing.

Garmadon: Oh! Oh, that'd be fun to conquer.

Lloyd: No, don't-

Garmadon: Oh, and that too!

Lloyd: That's not-

Garmadon: My gosh, Luh-Lloyd, can you imagine how beautiful this city would look getting smashed by my giant mech?

Lloyd: Please, just stop.

Garmadon: Oh, I'm totally gonna conquer this place. It's at the top of my bucket list.

Lloyd: Don't worry, he is not going to destroy San Diego.

Garmadon: Yet.

Lloyd: Today.

Garmadon: But someday!

Next, you need a ninja mask to protect your secret identity. Nobody knows mine except for Kai, Cole, Jay, Zane Lloyd, and you. 'Cause we're buds. I've also got sushi for lunch, my lucky chopsticks.
