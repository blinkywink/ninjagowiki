(The young ninja enter Borg Tower.)

Wyldfyre: Attention! We're here to see Cyrus Borg. It's important.

Woman Security Guard: And you are?

Wyldfyre: Also important.

Woman Security Guard: Front desk, we got a weirdo alert.

Sora: We're not weirdos, we’re the ninja.

Woman Security Guard: No, you're not, I've met the ninja.

Arin: We're the newer ninja. Lloyd and the others are working on something else, so we're in charge now.

Woman Security Guard: Got any proof you're ninja?

Wyldfyre: Of course. Try… this! (Wyldfyre tries to use her power to heat the Security Guard's coffee, but fails.)

Woman Security Guard: So, that's a no? Either way, request denied. Cyrus Borg is out of office. (She drinks her coffee, while Wally walks in.)

Wally: We need a battery replacement on the fourth floor. Can someone send the cost reports to accounting? And this tea clearly has almond milk in it, which gives me the tummy rumblies, and why haven't you called the Operations Department?

Euphrasia: Because… we don't work here?

Wally: Not you. (He talks into a communication device on his ear.) I'll call back in 10. But for the last time, I need oat milk. So, you're some kind of ninja?

Frak: Not some kind, just ninja. And we need to talk to Dr. Borg urgently.

Wally: Well, I'm Cyrus Borg's assistant, Wally. Anything you need to talk to Dr. Borg about, you can tell me. (The ninja talk over each other, and Wally points to Sora.) You. Go.

Sora: General Cryptor has returned and is working with Imperium Loyalists!

Wally: Oh, no! Send a plumber immediately! (He drinks his coffee, while the ninja stare at each other, confused.) Not you. Toilet's overflowing on the second floor. So, you were saying?

Sora: We have reason to believe General Cryptor is back.

Wyldfyre: And that reason is that he's back.

Frak: And he stole a powerful Mind Crystal, so--

Wally: And let me stop you right there. “Mind Crystal” sounds like magic malarkey, and magic isn't what we do here at Borg.

Arin: Cryptor was originally built from Borg's designs. He was supposedly destroyed, so either he survived, or someone with access to the designs rebuilt him.

Sora: Right, so we need Dr. Borg to help figure out who that could have been.

Wally: Cryptor couldn't have been rebuilt without someone stealing the plans we keep here. And I assure you that no one; ninja, evil genius, dove-costumed villain, no one could get through Borg Tower’s security.

Sora: Yeah, ‘cause nothing's ever gotten through Borg security before. You need to understand how dangerous this situation is.

Arin: Especially since Empress Beatrix is being held and treated here.

Wally: I can assure you Beatrix is being kept under the most secure conditions. (The ninja stare at him, unconvinced.) Fine, I will send a message to Dr. Borg, and to put your minds at ease, let me show you our medical suite.

(Wally leads the young ninja to Beatrix's room.)

Wally: See? Everything is secure. Nothing to worry about here.

(Arin stares at the comatose Beatrix.)

(In Old Town, Roby speaks to some Imperians carrying a billboard.)

Roby: Mhm. Mhm. Yeah. You're going to need at least three dozen more billboards. Marketing!

Percival: Thanks for coming out, Roby. I couldn't manage planning the Summit of the Merged Lands without your help and experience--

Zeatrix: (shouts) Percival Tartigrade! I have returned!

(Many people panic and run away from Zeatrix.)

Zeatrix: Wait! Please! I am not my abhorrent criminal sister, I am the true heir to the Imperian throne, Zeatrix Vespasian-Orus!

Roby: (He hides his face behind his clipboard.) Can you talk to her? We have a bad history.

Percival: Where have you been all this time?

Zeatrix: What you should be asking is why I'm here now. I've come to warn you. My sources have informed me that the filth still loyal to my murderous sister plan to sabotage the Summit of the Merged Lands.

Percival: We have long suspected Loyalists will try to target the summit, so we thank you for--

Zeatrix: It's already begun! (She whispers to Percival.) I believe they've infiltrated your own Imperium Guards.

Percival: Impossible. We have an entirely new contingent of guards. We've been tracking escaped Loyalists for weeks--

(The scaffolding nearby creaks before starting to fall down.)

Zeatrix: Watch out! (Zeatrix pushes Percival and Roby out of the way, while the two workers on top of the scaffolding panic.) Come on… powers! (Zeatrix successfully uses her powers to stop the scaffolding from falling, while Imperium Guards hold it up and put a box underneath it to keep it steady.)

Roby: Phew. Almost zooted my last stang.

Percival: The scaffolding's been cut! Somebody meant for this to happen!

Zeatrix: I did warn you.

Roby: Yeah, like 30 seconds ago! But how did they do it? This whole place has been under guard since we started this project.

Zeatrix: Exactly. As I said, someone, or someones, on the inside cannot be trusted, and I'm here to protect you and every innocent from falling prey to my sister's followers.

Percival: You do realize we elect our leaders now. There's no Imperian throne for you to reclaim.

Zeatrix: I'm here to help, not rule. I… while I was away, I've had much time to ponder. For all it has cost me, Imperium, the city founded by my ancestors, is my true home. I will do anything to defend it.

(In the Monastery of Spinjitzu, the young ninja are talking in the courtyard, while Sora’s parents play by throwing a green disc to one another.)

Frak: This is the worst. We got nothing.

Wyldfyre: Let's knock on every door in Ninjago until we find Cryptor's secret hideout.

Sora: That would take like 25 years.

Wyldfyre: Not if we find it on the first block, smarty-pants!

Sora's mother: (The green disc lands near Sora’s feet, and Sora’s mother waves to her.) Hey, Sora! Kids! Join us!

Sora: (She picks up the disc.) Sorry, we're kind of busy, trying to save the world.

Arin: (He takes the disc from Sora.) Maybe we should take a break. Might help us get a fresh perspective.

Sora: You just want to show off how good you are at this game.

(Arin gives the disc to Sora, and she throws it to her mother, who throws it back again. Frak catches it and throws it at Wyldfyre. Suddenly Mr. Frohicky comes out to the courtyard, and accidentally eats the disc.)

Arin: You just… inhaled it?

Alfonzo Frohicky: Just for that, I'm not going to tell you what I came to tell you. (He crosses his arms in defiance, but quickly gives up.) Percival's calling! It’s important! Oh… I always cave immediately.

(The young ninja run to the Hangar bay, where Percival and Roby are calling them through the screen.)

Percival: That's just it. I'm not sure we can trust Zeatrix.

Euphrasia: It is strange she showed up to warn you of sabotage just before saving you from sabotage.

Sora: Designed to make her look good--

(Sora is interrupted by Wyldfyre and Roby making loving faces and gestures at each other through the screen.)

Arin: Zeatrix does hate her twin. Might be serious about stopping her.

Frak: How was the scaffolding cut?

Roby: I don't know? Laser?

Frak: Like the one Cryptor used on my bike. So he and the Loyalists could have been behind it.

Euphrasia: Or Cryptor and Zeatrix could be in this together.

Sora: What we do know is you were sabotaged while guards were right there, so Zeatrix was correct-- we might not be able to trust them.

Percival: Ugh... the delegates from all the Merged Lands will start arriving soon. If one of them gets hurt--

Roby: My beautifully planned summit will be a disaster!

Percival: Or it will threaten the peace of all the Merged Lands. That's why I'm calling you, I'm keeping the Imperium Guards at the perimeter of Old Town, but I'm hoping you'll help protect all the delegates and dignitaries--

Wyldfyre: We're in! Protecting Roby-- I mean the Summit-- is our utmost priority!

(Outside the Old Town Hotel, there’s a crowd gathered around a red carpet, while the young ninja are in various places nearby, keeping watch. Gayle Gossip talks to a camera held up by Vinny Folson.)

Gayle Gossip: Gayle Gossip here, covering the arrival of the first delegates to the Summit of the Merged Lands!

Wyldfyre: (imitates static) Kssht! Wyldfyre here. This is boring. Wyldfyre out. Kssht!

Frak: Wyldfire, you don't have to make that sound when you talk on comms.

Wyldfyre: Kssht! Says you. Kssht!

Frak: There's a woman nearby acting suspiciously. She's my old math teacher, and she gave me a "B-" on my algebra final. I'm going to go down and question her.

Sora: Frak, do not.

(Lee arrives on a black van, and steps onto the red carpet, waving.)

Lee: Greetings, friends!

Gayle Gossip: It's our first delegate! From the Land of Lee... Lee! (Lee makes second copy of himself.) and our second delegate... also Lee.

(Lee makes a third copy.)

Lee(s): Hello, Ninjago! Meet my committee! Some of them might look a little famil-Lee-ar.

Percival: Welcome, sir...s. Honored to have you here. You'll be staying with all the other delegates in the Old Town Hotel right up here.

Lee: Fantastic! And I'll need--

Percival: The full luxury suites with extra beds? Already set up.

Lee: Love-Lee!

(Akita arrives in another black van, in her wolf form.)

Frak: Is this delegate housebroken?

(Akita transforms into her human form.)

Gayle Gossip: Aw... the Never Kingdoms' delegate is a puppy lady!

Sora: So far so good, Percival. No sign of Loyalists or sabotage.

Percival: I am still so nervous. I'm not sure I'm cut out for the pressure of charming people from every land.

Wyldfyre: I wonder what kind of delegate will come from that weirdo land with the pie bushes and cute cats? Did they even have people there?

Percival: Um, what land is that?

Wyldfyre: The creepy one. Kssht! Tell him, Sora. Kssht!

Sora: It was weird. It was dawn in every direction the whole time we were there, which I don't even know how long that was.

Percival: Hm... not ringing a bell, and I know every land there is.

Sora: Uh, if it's not a known land, then where were we?

(More black vans arrive, and the crowd around the red carpet rushes closer to them.)

Percival: Please, stay behind the ropes! I-- Ah! (He is pushed aside by Gayle Gossip, and falls to the ground.) Oof! --shouldn’t be in charge. I--(He gets dragged away by Roby.)

Roby: Percy, Big Perce. I know you're worried about this event. It's the biggest thing to happen in years, and it's success all depends on you. Everyone is watching your every move and judging you.

Percival: Are you trying to make it worse?

Roby: Right, where was I going with this? Oh, yeah! Pep talk. Remember, when things get tough, it doesn't really matter what happens, 'cause your family's rich. You can always do something else.

Percival: My family is not rich.

Roby: Really? Well, then... don’t screw it up!

Percival: But... I do have a lot of people in my life that care about me.

Roby: Stang-zoot! The Pep Talk is back on! (He pulls out his phone and turns the camera to him and Percival.) Look at yourself. Not as handsome as me, but not bad. Now say, "You've got the touch! You've got the power!"

Percival: Okay. You've got the-- What is that? Ah! (A mechanical arm appears from behind and grabs him, dragging him away.)

Roby: Oh no!

(Percival is taken away by a large insect-like robot with red eyes while Roby, Wyldfyre and Sora watch.)

Arin: How did a robot sneak up and take Percival without us noticing?

Frak: Are we terrible ninja?

Sora: We got to move fast and track that bot.

Wyldfyre: Kssht! Great idea. I already thought of it, like, 30 seconds ago. Kssht!

Frak: But we got to be real secret-like. We don't want a major panic here.

Wyldfyre: Kssht! Don't worry, Roby will be dreamily covering for Percival. Kssht!

(Suetonius arrives.)

Roby: Hello. Great to see you... guest!

Suetonius: Master Suetonius of the Cloud Kingdom. I was expecting Percival of Imperium would be present to greet me.

Roby: He got… carried away-- (Percival screams, still being carried by the robot.) --by the excitement, and had to go lie down. You know how it is.

Suetonius: Hm, I do. Destiny favors those who know when it's time for a good lie-down.

(A smaller robot, a Cocoon, comes out of the larger one carrying Percival, and jumps onto his face. He tries to get it off while screaming, but doesn’t manage to do it. He stops moving as if unconscious.)

Wyldfyre: Gross! But cool, but… no! (She jumps off a nearby roof and blasts the big robot with her power, while Arin and Frak jump down to the ground near Percival. Euphrasia uses her powers to attack the bot.)

Euphrasia: Oh! It's still moving!

Wyldfyre: H'yah!

(Wyldfyre dodges and kicks the bot’s robotic arms, while Riyu performs Spinjitzu to knock it down. Sora gets on the bot’s stomach and pulls out some wires, which turns off the robot.)

Wyldfyre: Oh, yeah! The off button!

Euphrasia: Percival, can you hear us?

Frak: (He gets closer to check on Percival.) At least he's still breathing.

Sora: (She groans and tries to get the Cocoon off of Percival’s head.) I can't figure out how to get him out of this thing.

Zeatrix: It's Imperium technology! An older design. You wouldn't recognize it.

(Zeatrix jumps down from the roof she was on, landing next to the ninja.)

Arin: You're here. That's... convenient.

Sora: Zeatrix is right. It's old school for sure, but I can see the Imperium influence.

(Zeatrix tries to walk closer to Percival, but Arin and Wyldfyre block her.)

Zeatrix: Please, I just want to help.

Arin: Why should we believe you?

Frak: Yeah, if you really wanted to help, why did you wait until after we kicked this thing's butt? H'yah! (He kicks the larger bot, reactivating it and releasing more of the Cocoons, which run out to the street.) Uh-oh. That was not intentional!

Wyldfyre: Great! Now we all gotta stop those bots Frak intentionally let out before they get to Roby! I mean, the delegates!

Sora: We can't just leave Percival here.

Zeatrix: I'll stay and take care of him. You know you don't have a choice, so just go.

(The ninja leave Percival and Zeatrix to go stop the Cocoons. Sora, Wyldfyre, and Riyu destroy three of them before they can hurt anyone.)

(Tyr and the Old Lady Dragonians arrive.)

Gayle Gossip: Ooh, it's Tyr from the First Land!

Roby: (He sees a couple of Cocoons on top of the van Tyr arrived in, and does his best to lead the First Land delegates away from them before anyone notices.) Welcome to the Summit of the Merged Lands, Tyr! Must be exhausted from your flight. Okay. Hey, this way.

(Frak grabs the two Cocoons on the van and destroys them.)

(A Cocoon comes near an Imperium Guard, and he notices it before Euphrasia stomps on it and destroys it.)

Imperium Guard Munce: What just happened?

Euphrasia: You have to keep it on the down-low. These bots are on the loose, which is bad enough, but if they get out of Old Town, they'll infest all of Ninjago City, and it'll be absolute chaos.

Imperium Guard Munce: I can help. We can help. (The Imperium Guard speaks through a comm in his ear.) We need all available Imperium guards at the edge of Old Town.

(Zeatrix repairs a green wire from the giant insect-like bot while Percival is still on the ground, cocooned.)

(Wyldfyre destroys a Cocoon and scatters its pieces while the Bone Regent and two Re-Awakened arrive to the red carpet. Roby winks at Wyldfyre while she destroys another Cocoon, and she roars.)

Roby: Welcome, Bone Regent! We are delighted—

Bone Regent: Do not waste my time any more than it has already been wasted!

Roby: Oh. That’s fine. I'm not for everyone.

(Sora is chasing some of the Cocoons, when she gets an incoming call from Cyrus Borg.)

Sora: Hello! Dr. Borg?

Cyrus Borg: Uh, yes. Sora, right? Uh, did I call it a bad time?

Sora: Nope. All good here. Taking it easy. Uh, What's up? (She destroys several Cocoons while she talks.)

Cyrus Borg: Uh, yes, my security told me you all stopped by, but she didn't say why.

Sora: Wait, the security guard told you? Not Wally?

Cyrus Borg: You talked to Wally? Oh, he never mentioned it, but that's not much of a surprise. He's a terrible assistant, but a good egg. Anyway, what was it you wanted to discuss?

Sora: We think someone stole the plans for Cryptor and rebuilt him.

Cyrus Borg: That would be impossible. Our security system is unparalleled. I'll show you. Share screen. (Dr. Borg shares his screen, which shows the text “folder empty” in Ninjargon.) What? “Folder empty”? Who could have taken the plans?

(In Old Town Hotel, Imperium guards march in front of the red carpet, while the Lees walk back outside,)

Lee: What a Magnificent place! First time I've stayed in a building I didn't build myselves!

(Cocoons walk nearby and Arin stomps on one, but then another manages to jump on his head.)

Arin: Whoa!

(The crowd gasps, some of them look at Arin.)

Gayle Gossip: Wow, Vinny, are you getting this?

(Vinny points the camera at Arin as Euphrasia lands. More cocoon bots run towards Euphrasia but get destroyed by Wyldfyre.)

Roby: Check it! The live streaming performance of my original story, "One Hundred Eyes To See My Soul," happening in the streets of Old Town right now! Hashtag: "dope"!

Citizen 1: Wow... a performance that escapes the confines of the screen?

Merlopian 1: This is no performance! That robot just face-cocooned that guy!

Citizen 2: Art imitating life? Or life imitating art?

(The crowd murmurs and Roby walks up to Lee, then whispers.)

Roby: (quietly) Lee... I need a hand here. Get down there and find your light.

Lee: O, ye cruel bots! You have stolen my beloved and cocooned her face as she cocooned my heart! Alas, I had never avowed true love before this fortnight...

(The crowd watches Lee.)

Gayle Gossip: Ooh, Vinny, get this Lee guy. (Vinny points his camera at Lee.) What charisma!

Lee: ...entanglement, as one would expect, due to my exalted social position

(Euphrasia drags Arin away.)

(Lee continues to sing, A Cocoon approaches Sora while she destroys more Cocoons.)

Lee: As a famous...

Cyrus Borg: This is terrible! Unprecedented. I need to investigate this breach. I will call you again soon.

(Cyrus Borg hangs up the call and Sora puts away the Datapad, then grabs two cocoon bots and smashes them.)

(Zeatrix uses Shockwave and prepares to destroy the Cocoon restraining Percival.)

(Lee continues to sing.)

Lee: Your robotic form may be cold, but I believe you have warmth in your heart-like processors--

(Sora grabs a cocoon bot and smashes it. She then stops to examine its remains.)

Sora: A beacon? Like a homing beacon? Sweet! Maybe we don't need to chase them after all.

(Sora activates the beacon and covers her ears because of the loud sound it makes. Euphrasia also covers her ears. Cocoon bots swarm Wyldfyre and Frak before suddenly heading towards to the location of the homing beacon, and Wyldfyre and Frak cover their ears.)

Wyldfyre: Hey! Come back! I wasn't done punching you!

(At the Old Town Hotel, Lee continues to sing while the Cocoon bots head towards the homing beacon.)

Lee: ...and so, as the robots fled from the streets, and from my soul, I realized the true face-cocoon was the one I had placed... on myself! (bows down)

(The crowd cheers, and Roby gets his phone and takes a selfie with the Lees.)

(Sora notices all the Cocoons heading towards the beacon.)

Sora: I didn't fully think this through.

(Zeatrix destroys the Cocoon restraining Percival with Shockwave,)

Percival: That... was awful!

(Sora prepares to destroy the Cocoons but as Frak and Wyldfyre land and Frak kicks a Cocoon.)

Wyldfyre: This is giving me inner peace! (smashes some Cocoons)

(Sora kicks another Cocoon. Euphrasia lands and punches another one.)

Euphrasia: Much easier when they're all in one place. Your doing?

Sora: Maybe. (slams down a cocoon)

Percival: Well done!

Frak: Percival! You're okay!

Zeatrix: Did those things get anyone else?

(The cocoon restraining Arin gets destroyed by Zeatrix using Shockwave.)

Arin: Whoa... Zeatrix?

Percival: Her shockwave powers are the only way to safely remove these bugs without hurting the person inside.

Arin: So they're gone? And we didn't create a major panic?

Roby: Nope. Thanks to Lee here.

Lee: All in a Lee's work.

Merlopian: Roby's original story was amazing but the true standout was that Lee guy. I'd watch him do anything.

Sora: Thank you for your helping us. I... I'm glad we trusted you.

Zeatrix: Have to say, using that homing beacon was very impressive.

Sora: Ah, it was too easy. (She stops to think.) Wait, was it too easy?

Arin: Learn to take a compliment.

Sora: No, Arin, I'm serious. Why would Cryptor build a homing device into each bot that would bring them all together where they could easily be destroyed?

Zeatrix: Hmm, she's right. Plus, those cocoons fell apart once I could hit them with my shockwave. So, what was the purpose of all this?

Arin: Hold it. Did the Imperian Guards on Beatrix get called away to...

Euphrasia: Oh, no.

(Sora gets her Data pad and calls Cyrus Borg.)

Cyrus Borg: Sora, my apologies. I don't have any answers about those plans yet. Wally would know who had access, but I can't find Wally anywhere.

Sora: Actually, I'm calling about something more urgent. Is Beatrix still guarded?

(In Borg Tower, Wally activates the hover engines on Beatrix's bed, and the wall in front of him gets destroyed, revealing Cryptor.)

Wally: General Crypt--

Cryptor: We don't have time for obsequiousness.

Wally: Of course. Wise of you to say so.

(Loyalists push the bed away as the door to the room opens.)

Cyrus Borg: Wally! What's going on?

Wally: Exactly what it looks like, boss. (takes off his glasses and puts on a Loyalist Mask.) Consider this my resignation. (throws his glasses at Cyrus Borg)

(Wally and the Loyalists jump as the bed hovers and takes off. Cyrus Borg pulls out his Data pad.)

Cyrus Borg: Uh, Sora? Big problem!

(At the Old Town Hotel.)

Frak: So, the bots were just a distraction. We got played.

Arin: Beatrix is in a coma. What could they want with her?

Zeatrix: It's not worth trying to make sense of it. The Loyalists have remained devoted to her, despite all logic. We just need to stop whatever their endgame is.

Percival: You really are nothing like her. I'm sorry I didn't see that earlier.

Zeatrix: Even Beatrix wasn't always like this. My father was an isolationist who thought Imperium was better than any other realm, but she took that view and went even further. Became extreme, hateful... evil.

Sora: But you didn't?

Zeatrix: I almost did, but I've seen how things are now in the Merged Lands. People from all over, coming together. I've chosen peace.

Sora: And we'll work together to make sure the Summit of the Merged Lands goes forward.

Percival: This isn't going to be easy. Loyalists could be anywhere.

(In the relaxation room, Arin’s parents open the door as Sora's parents are putting their Loyalist masks in a bag.)

Barry: Knock knock! Ready for game night?

Sora's mother: Of course! Born ready! Let's go right now and get some snacks on the way. We’re starving!

(Jenny notices there's a Loyalist mask in one of the bags as Sora’s father smiles awkwardly. Jenny lets Sora's parents pass, looks towards the bag for a moment, then walks away.)

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
