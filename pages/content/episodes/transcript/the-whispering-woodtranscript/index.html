The Whispering Wood/Transcript

(In the Whispering Wood, two Source Dragon acolytes are cleaning and placing candles in the Shrine of the Seven Sources, an altar through which the energy of the Source Dragons flows.)

Acolyte: For the glory of the Sources.

(The acolytes bow, but stop when they hear the bushes rustling and see the Chief Loyalist.)

Acolyte: Greetings, traveler.

Chief Loyalist: This is it? The Shrine of the Seven Sources?

Acolyte: Yes. Have you come to pay homage to our humble shrine?

Chief Loyalist: "Humble" is right. I expected something a little more impressive.

Acolyte: Praise of the Source Dragons has little to do with outward displays... only inner devotion.

Chief Loyalist: How very "enlightened" of you. So, each column channels the presence of the power of one of the Source Dragons?

Acolyte: You've done your research. As long as a Dragon's energy flows through the lands, its column will glow forth as a tribute. Sadly, one of the Source Dragons has passed (the stone sphere representing the Source Dragon of Balance is shown fallen), but one day, when the cycle renews, all--

Chief Loyalist: Yes, yes, yes, "Praise the Sources" or whatever, but have you observed any fluctuations or anomalies in the power distribution recently?

Acolyte: Uh... I'm... I'm sorry, I... I don't understand the question.

Chief Loyalist: (scoffs) I'm looking for any indication of--

(The sphere representing the Source Dragon of Life falls.)

Chief Loyalist: --of that!

Acolyte: (Gasping in shock)

Chief Loyalist: Ha! Just as I hypothesized. Source withdrawal.

Acolyte: I'm... I'm sure this outage is only temporary. Besides, we still have five mighty Sources--

(The sphere representing the Source Dragon of Focus falls.)

Acolyte: (gasping) No cause for alarm. There are still a majority--

(The sphere representing the Source Dragon of Strength falls.)

Acolyte: (gasping in horror)

(The spheres, including the ones that fell, start sparking with unstable energy.)

Acolytes: (Gasping)

Acolyte: A majority of Sources have removed their power from the lands? Great Dragons, why have you forsaken us mortals?

(The energy beams converge at the center, above the spheres, before striking the pool of water in the sanctuary's center, transforming it into an acidic liquid. The acolytes gasp. The beams also strike the ground, allowing a bubbling tar pit to emerge from the earth. From the pool, an Acid Monster begins to emerge, and from the tar pit, an Earth Monster begins to emerge. Both Elemental Monsters finish emerging, and the acolytes are startled. The Acid Monster spits acid at them, but they manage to dodge, and the acid hits and melts a branch. The Chief Loyalist begins to move away. The Earth Monster fires blasts of tar and goes after one of the acolytes while the Acid Monster spits acid and goes after the other. The Chief Loyalist continues to move away while speaking into a communication device.)

Chief Loyalist: As predicted, the manifestations have already begun. The foolish Ninja have played right into our hands. The quest for Desolation begins now.

(The young acolyte tries to escape, but falls and screams as she is pulled by the Earth Monster.)

(Flashbacks of Thunderfang are shown.)

Lloyd: He was called "Thunderfang, the Shatter Dragon of Chaos."

Arin: Scary name, scary dragon. Also long name, long dragon. Only way to beat him? Summon Thunderfang's old nemesis, the Arc Dragon of Focus.

Lloyd: Classic match-up, Chaos versus Focus, but Thunderfang was so powered up, the Focus Dragon couldn't do it alone.

Arin: So we helped guide them. I know it sounds unbelievable, but our minds were, like, sharing a dragon's brain!

Lloyd: With the Focus Dragon's help, we beat Thunderfang for good. World saved!

Arin: But some of the Source Dragons might not be happy with us for summoning that Arc Dragon without their permission.

Lloyd: So, we were heading back to Ninjago when I felt something weird going on with my powers. Then we went though the portal gate--

Arin: And it spat us out here. Wherever "here" is, it's obviously not home, so we tried to go back through the portal--

Lloyd: And it wouldn't work. Totally inactive, but how are you here?

Euphrasia: Basically same? Minus the dragon mind-brain thing. I was in the Cloud Kingdom, felt my Powers going weird. I wanted to ask you ninja about it, but... when I went through the gate, it sent me here.

Arin: Sorry you got stranded too, but it's nice to see you again.

Euphrasia: So you have no idea what happened? To our powers or the portal gates?

Lloyd: No idea. Let's check out the neighborhood. It might have some answers.

(Lloyd opens the monastery doors, revealing the Whispering Wood. The trio then exits.)

Arin: Wow... It's beautiful.

Lloyd: You Cloud Monks record stuff about all the lands, right? Any idea where we are?

Euphrasia: My focus is the Wyldness late medieval period. I've never seen anything quite like this place.

Lloyd: Me neither. Hopefully the other Ninja aren't having the same problems we are.

(Nya, Kai, and Jay are in another monastery.)

Nya: This is not Ninjago. We needed to get back to Ninjago. Why is the portal gate malfunctioning?

Kai: What's also weird? When we left Mysterium, it was day. Now it's the middle of the night.

Jay: A cloudy night. No moons, no stars.

(They open the monastery doors and see that it is dark outside.)

Kai: A fireball should light things up. (tries to use his powers, but fails) Oh, yeah, guess what. Elemental Powers are also on the fritz. Hold on, let me try this. H'yah!

(Kai is flung backward by an uncotrolled blast of Fire as Jay laughs at him.)

Jay: I was feeling bummed about our powers not working right, but that made me feel a little better.

Kai: I'll remember this when you end up zapping yourself with a giant lightning storm. (Jay helps him to his feet) Nya, can't you get the portal gate going again?

Nya: Get it going how? It's powered down. We don't even know how or why it spat us out here. Um... Take us to Ninjago! See? Nothing.

Jay: Looks like we have to find another way back to Ninjago. I say we wait till sun-up so we can see where we are, then take it from there. (breaks and takes some pieces of wood from the training circuit) Hey, Kai, (throws the wood at him) we're gonna need a campfire. Think you could, um...

Kai: (scoffing) Of course I can. Hah! (uses his fire to light the campfire, but ends up being thrown back) H'yah!

Nya: You set him up for that on purpose, didn't you?

Jay: (Shrugging) Hmm.

(Riyu, Wyldfyre, Sora, and Frak are at the ruins of a third monastery.)

Sora: portal gates are usually in monasteries, and I don't see a monastery around here, unless--

Wyldfyre: (grunting and trying to use her powers) Come on, come on! If I can't blast fireballs, who even am I?

Frak: Wait... (trying to use his powers) Feel that? Like a little tremor? You felt that, right?

(Riyu grumbling negatively)

Frak: (groaning) You're right. Who am I kidding? There was no tremor! If I can't quake, what'll I do? What am I even good for?

Sora: Your powers aren't what give you your worth.

Wyldfyre: Of course they are!

Frak: Yeah, what are you talking about, Sora? Please freak out like us.

Sora: I was solving problems long before I had an Elemental Power. And this is a problem. I mean, the portal gate spit us out in some random, unknown spot? Why?

Wyldfyre: And our powers. Don't forget our powers problem. Kai will know what's going on. We need to get back to Ninjago.

Sora: Yeah, but without a working portal gate, looks like we're walking. And in which direction? And... Wait. It's dawn everywhere.

Wyldfyre: So, this kinda makes knowing what direction to go a little hard, right?

Sora: A little.

(Kai, Nya and Jay hear a low grumble.)

Nya: I can hear your stomachs growling. I've got these wafers left.

Kai: It wasn't my stomach, for once.

Jay: Not me either. Plus, I'd rather starve than eat those.

Nya: What's with the miserable faces? It's not like this is the first time we've been stuck in some unknown place. Remember the Never-Realm? Or in Prime Empire, when... Oh... right. You don't remember any of that.

Jay: No, I don't. Back in the City of Temples, do you know why I left?

Kai: We hadn't paid you yet?

Jay: No!

(A flashback to when Jay left is shown.)

Jay: I was watching you all, the way you worked together, more like a family than a team... a family I had no memory of, and that made me feel more lonely than I'd ever felt. I had to get away. But as soon as I was gone, I regretted it, especially after what you said to me, Nya.

Nya: I don't know if you'll ever get those memories back, Jay, but we're together now. We can build new memories.

Jay: But that's just it. The more time I spend with you, I realize... I need more than new memories. I need to remember the first time we met. The first time we danced. The first time I made you smile.

Kai: Those memories can't be gone forever. They have to be somewhere in your mind. We just have to find a way to bring them back.

(Tentacled creatures lurk covertly above the monastery's walls.)

(Bird tweeting)

(In the Forest of Spirits, Zane and P.I.X.A.L. perform romantic dances before Zane suddenly collapses.)

P.I.X.A.L.: Zane, are you okay?

Zane: I'm not sure, P.I.X.A.L. I feel... off. (trying to use his powers) My powers... are gone? (Spluttering, yelping) Ah!

(Birds retreating, chirping in alarm.)

Zane: Something's very wrong. I've gotta find the other Ninja.

Arin: There are plenty of worse places to get stranded, I guess, and after everything we've been through, I'm cool with a little peace.

Lloyd: Yeah, this is the exact opposite of fighting undead dragons.

Arin: Or crawling through the Underworld.

Lloyd: You went to the Underworld?

Arin: I feel like Ras dragged me everywhere.

Euphrasia: Ras? That tiger guy that attacked the Cloud Kingdom? Who talks all-- "No one will stop me, I'm so mean, roar-roar" and stuff?

Arin: (chuckles) Yeah. A lot's happened since we last hung out, Euphrasia. I even stupidly left the Ninja team.

Lloyd: You did what you thought you needed to do, Arin. I was so caught up in other problems, I should have seen what you were going through.

Arin: You have so many responsibilities. I don't blame you for that, Lloyd.

Lloyd: I should have been the one to teach you proper Spinjitzu.

Arin: Ras showed me how to do that, yes, but I'm not sure it was the best way. He... exploited my anger.

Euphrasia: I can't believe you have an angry bone in your body.

Arin: I was mad at Master Wu for causing the Merge. I blamed him for the death of my parents, but my parents were still alive. I should never have trusted Ras.

Euphrasia: Master Wu caused the Merge? Why?

Lloyd: You have no idea how badly I wish I knew the answer to that question.

(The wind blows, and the three hear faint whispers.)

Arin: You hear that?

Lloyd: Is someone there?

(More disembodied whispers are heard, and the trio moves toward them and, upon stopping, hears more.)

Euphrasia: Odd.

(They follow some leaves that pass by them and then stop.)

Arin: Okay, a little bit creepy. Maybe a lot a bit creepy.

(They resume walking while hearing birds and disembodied whispers.)

Euphrasia: Is someone following us?

Lloyd: We don't even know if anyone lives here. We haven't seen any trails, not even footprints.

(More disembodied whispering is heard.)

Arin: Could the trees be whispering?

(The whispers swirl and grow.)

Lloyd: Follow the whispers!

Frak: ...and that's why we should obviously head that way, toward those large, pleasant trees.

Sora: But I hear running water over there. I'm sure it's clean and refreshing, and unless you brought a hose or a camel, we're going to need some.

Wyldfyre: No, we need to get to the Monastery of Ninjago, not to trees or water.

Frak: As the most accomplished Ninja here, I think we--

Sora: Most accomplished Ninja? Most accomplished at what, being annoying? I'll be leading this little group, thank you very much.

Wyldfyre: (chuckles) Listen, before he died, Egalt, one of the greatest dragons to ever live, told me... I'd be a leader someday. I'm thinking that day is today.

Sora: No offense to Egalt, but even great masters get things wrong sometimes.

Frak: And that doesn't change the fact that I was studying the Ninja before either of you even heard of Spinjitzu.

Sora: Really? Was that when you were working for Ras?

Frak: You worked for him after I did.

Sora: I was hanging with Arin. Ras just happened to be there.

Frak: You still did what Ras said.

(Sora groans.)

(The Finders are looking around the Monastery of Spinjitzu.)

Cole: Hello? Anyone? The place is empty.

Bonzle: Last I remember, something invisible attacked me, then I woke up here.

Cole: You were in Spectral Sleep, and I was so focused on trying to save you, I made some stupid mistakes and got my soul drained, too. I just hope Kai and the others pulled through. (He opens the door, and gasps upon seeing the injured Rontu being tended to by Jenny and Barry.)

Mr. Frohicky: (laying on a bed nearby) Make sure you keep her healing leaves warm with your hands. That's how my grandmother did it.

Cole: Oh, no, what happened here?

Geo: Ras happened. (He appears with a crutch.)

Cole: Are you okay?

Geo: I'll be fine, but I don't know about Rontu there.

(Rontu groans.)

Cole: What did Ras come here for?

Mr. Frohicky: No clue. He forced me to take him to Master Wu's old study, and I think he took something, but I don't know what, or why.

Geo: Then he left through the portal gate. Whatever Ras is up to, it can't be good. It never is.

(The disembodied whispers swirl.)

Arin: Where is this taking us?

(The three hear distant shrieking and roaring.)

Euphrasia: That sounds bad.

(Small woooden creatures are fending off an Acid Monster and an Earth Monster.)

Euphrasia: (gasps) What are those things?

Arin: No idea, but you thinking what I'm thinking?

Lloyd: Ninja... go!

(The Acid Monster spews out acid, but Arin performs Spinjitzu to block it.)

Lloyd: (Strains as he tries to use his powers.) Ugh, this is the worst!

Euphrasia: (attempts to use her powers, but loses control) Oh! Uh, sorry! Accident! (chuckles awkwardly) I'm usually better at that!

Arin: (grabs stick) These are the weirdest Elemental Masters I've ever seen.

Lloyd: I don't think they're masters. I think they're actually made of Elemental Powers.

(The Earth Monster snaps its claws.)

Arin: What? How is that even possible?

Lloyd: Don't ask me. Wu didn't prepare me for anything like this!

(Arin transfers Object Spinjitzu to the stick as he throws it, but it doesn't damage the Acid Monster. The Acid Monster shoots acid out of its mouth. One of the small creatures gives its crossbow to Euphrasia.)

Euphrasia: Oh! Uh... Never used one of this before, but, uh...

(Euphrasia and some of the creatures aim their slingshots and crossbows at the Acid Monster and stagger it for a moment. the Acid Monster hisses and roars.)

Euphrasia: Uh-oh

Lloyd: (performs Spinjitzu and tries to free himself from tar) H'ah!

Arin: (Grabs three rocks) Get to safety! (performs Spinjitzu and kicks the rocks at the monsters) H'yah!

(The Acid Monster shoots out acid. Both monsters screech.)

Lloyd: (struggling) No!

(The trees around him start to glow.)

Lloyd: What the...?

(Sparks burst from the trees, and a beam of energy hits Lloyd.)

Lloyd: (effortlessly uses his elemental powers) H'yah! H'ah! (performs Spinjitzu to free himself) H'yah! (blasts the Acid Monster)

Arin: Lloyd got his powers working?

Euphrasia: (regains her elemental powers and gasps) I'm back too!

(Lloyd and Euphrasia blast the Elemental Monsters and defeat them)

Euphrasia: Uh... did we... destroy them?

Lloyd: Not sure. If they're pure Elemental power, they may have just... changed to another form.

(The wooden creatures appear again. Lloyd's powers fade.)

Lloyd: Aw, no! (groans) Come on.

Euphrasia: (sighs) Too good to last.

Arin: Can I just say, this place might be beautiful, but it is weird!

(The creatures chitter.)

Euphrasia: (laughs) Guess they're happy we saved them?

(The creatures gesture for them to follow.)

Arin: Follow them?

Lloyd: Don't have any better ideas. (grabs his sword and straps it to his back)

(The creatures chitter.)

Arin: Weird, but cute.

(Riyu keeps sniffing while Frak, Sora, and Wyldfyre follow him.)

Frak: Another reason I should be the leader? If it wasn't for me, we'd still be fighting that... (nervously) ...puppet in the desert.

Sora: You stop one puppet, and you think that makes you our leader?

Frak: It was a big puppet! Man, all this walking has made me thirsty.

Sora: Then you should've listened when I suggested we follow the sound of running water!

Frak: (notices a river) Looks like we don't need to.

Sora: How did we not see that before?

Frak: Who cares?

Sora: (sighs) You're right about one thing, all this walking... (Frak slurps the water.) ...I could use a rest. (rocks appears beside her.) Hmm, perfect! What are the chances?

Wyldfyre: (stomach growls) Oh, wish we brought some food. I could use a Wyldmelon right about now. (looks around and sees Wyldmelons on a nearby rock, making her happy, nervous, and suspicious as she looks at them closely.)

(Arin, Euphrasia, and Lloyd keep following the creatures.)

Arin: Okay, so, thinking this through, both of your Elemental Powers are acting strange, the portal gates aren't working, and monsters that look like pure Elemental Energy show up.

Euphrasia: You think it's all related?

Lloyd: It's a good question, and how did these trees give me my powers back, at least for a little while?

Euphrasia: I didn't get a tree blast like you did, but mine still came back.

Lloyd: I hope these little guys are leading us to some answers.

(The arrive at the Tree house hideout, where the Arc Dragon of Life appears. They look up at the enormous dragon.)

Euphrasia: Wow...

(They approach the dragon from a treehouse.)

Arin: Whoa... Is that a Source Dragon?

Lloyd: I... don't think so?

(Arc Dragon of Life rumbles and stretches while looking at them.)

Arin: (quietly) Are we supposed to say hi?

Lloyd: Greetings, mighty dragon. My name is--

Arc Dragon of Life: I know who you are, Lloyd, Son of Garmadon. My trees sensed the Elemental Master of Life the moment you arrived in our wood, and sent their whispers to me. We had long given up on the Legendary Green Ninja ever returning to us.

Lloyd: Returned?

Arc Dragon of Life: (annoyed) For generations, the Protector of the Whispering Wood has performed their duties, but you are the first who ignored those duties, and we have long felt betrayed.

Lloyd: What...?

(The dragon roars mightily)

(In a dark hideout, the Chief Loyalist shows holograms of the Earth and Acid Monsters to other Loyalists, who also appear as holograms.)

Chief Loyalist: …and so, all I've hypothesized has been proven. Without realizing it, our enemies have inadvertently set in motion their own downfall, and our restoration. The energy manifestations have already begun.

Loyalists: (Applauding)

Loyalist #1: Ha ha! A triumph!

Loyalist #2: But these creatures, what do they want?

Chief Loyalist: Want? Why, nothing. They are beings of rage and destruction born from the imbalance of Source Energy, as manifested by the Weapon (shows a hologram of a staff-like weapon). If we back trace a manifestation, we'll find the origin point, the location of the Weapon of Desolation.

Loyalists: (applaud and laugh) Well done! Yes!

Chief Loyalist: Now, go. Search your assigned regions! Find the Weapon! Let nothing stand in your way!

(The Loyalists turn off their holograms.)

Chief Loyalist: Her time will finally come again.

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
