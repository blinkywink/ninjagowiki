Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon/Transcript

(In the Underworld, Ras, after freeing himself from the stones, managed to find another person to place in the Casket of Bones and activate the Forge of the Forgotten. He activates the Forge and this person's sacrifice allows the creation of a Source Dragon Icon, which he soon takes from the Forge. He is summoned by his master into the red void.)

Ras: Master, I've done it. (His master buzzes.) Endless interruptions by the Ninja, but at last, we have another Source Dragon Icon. We're one step closer to our goals. (His master buzzes.) I know exactly what I must do next, and no one, not even the Ninja, will get in our way. (His master buzzes.) Hmm... Yes, Master. The time of your glory will soon be at hand.

(Ras comes back, stops floating and Skulkin and Greenbone Warriors arrive.)

Greenbone Leader: A mortal using the Forge of the Forgotten is a grave insult! No one still living is allowed to touch it.

Ras: And yet, I have already done so.

Greenbone Leader: There's only one solution. We must make it so the one who used the Forge is no longer living.

(The Skulkin and Greenbone Warriors wield their weapons while Ras wields his hammer.)

Ras: You're welcome to try. (He walks toward them.)

(In the Community of Lee, Sora continues using her Prismatic Lamp to see the Spectral Dragonians.)

Sora: (She gasps.) As if we didn't have enough problems already. (She sees Lee groaning vacantly.) Sorry, Lee, gotta run. (She gets down from the roof of the house and runs.)

(Arin tries to protect his parents from the Lees...)

Lee: (He speaks moaning.) You're my best friend...

(...but the three realize that the Lees are hollowed.)

Jenny: What happened to Lee?

(Arin looks through the Returning Blade.)

Arin: Spectral Dragonians. You can't see them, but trust me. (He jumps, spins in the air and hits a Spectral Dragonian.) Get to cover! (He looks through the blade again.)

(Nya, Lloyd, and Wyldfyre see the hollow Lees.)

Wyldfyre: Did all the Lees just decide sleepwalk at the same time? Is this another one of his dance numbers?

Lloyd: No, this is something weird.

Nya: This is something weird?

(Sora approaches them.)

Sora: The same thing happened when we were in Mysterium.

Nya: Sora! When did you get here?

Wyldfyre: You bring Ras too?

Sora: Long story, but no.

Wyldfyre: Ugh! I was really looking forward to kicking him in the gut.

Lloyd: What "thing" happened in Mysterium?

(Sora raises her Prismatic Lamp and the others see the Spectral Dragonians flying by.)

Lloyd: Those look like Dragonians.

Sora: Yeah, but the kind that, if they touch you, they drain your souls or something.

(A Spectral Dragonian dives to attack them, but they dodge. A Devonian emerges from his home.)

Andrew: What's with all the--? (He is attacked by the Spectral Dragonian and becomes hollow.)

Sora: Whatever you do, you cannot touch them!

Wyldfyre: Who would want to? That's what throwing fire is for! (She fires Heat at a Spectral Dragonian, who dodges and disappears.) Ah! I'm better at hitting things I can see!

Sora: Yeah! that's why I made this thing out of a Prismatic Blade.

(More neighbors come out of their houses.)

Monk: Is it safe to come out yet? (He is caught by a Spectral Dragonian and becomes hollow.)

Lloyd: (To Sora.) We need to light this whole place up with your lamp!

Nya: Great idea. Problem. How's that possible?

Sora: I've got an idea. Nya, get me to the top of that house!

Nya: Get ready for a big boost. (She does Spinjitzu, grabs Sora, and throws het to the top of the house.)

Sora: Thanks!

(Nya, Wyldfyre, and Lloyd take the neighbors back to their homes.)

Lloyd: It's not safe out here! Go inside!

Geckle: But what happened to Lee? (Becomes hollow.)

(Sora runs across the rooftops until he reaches the remains of the mechanical moon.)

Sora: Okay, Sora, you got this. This better work. (She uses her Technology to rebuild the mechanical moon and combine it with her Prismatic Lamp. The brightness hurts Jay's eyes.)

Jay: Great, more of Lee. (He realizes that the Lees are hollow.) What was that all about?

(Sora causes the mechanical moon to rise back to the ceiling and, by activating the Prismatic Lamp inside it, illuminates the entire place and makes the Spectral Dragonians visible, leaving the neighbors frightened. Arin defends his parents from the Spectral Dragonians using Spinjitzu.)

Barry: It was less horrifying when we couldn't see those things!

(Agent Allen sees a Spectral Dragonian, which hollows out a Geckle, and approaches it holding a sheet of paperwork.)

Allen: Now that I can see you, I can arrest you! (He takes handcuffs out of his pocket.) You think you can just drain souls without a stamped Certificate of Soul Handling?

Arin: (To Allen.) No!

(Allen tries to trap the Spectral Dragonian, but has his soul extracted and is left hollow. Wyldfyre fires Heat at two Spectral Dragonians, but it does not affect them.)

Wyldfyre: (She takes the button remote from her pocket and presses it.) Huh? Why won't this thing work?

(Lloyd performs Spinjitzu, then takes out a sword and blocks the Spectral Dragonians' attacks with it. Arin performs Spinjitzu and saves Lloyd from a Spectral Dragonian, who was going to attack him from behind, but accidentally knocks him to the ground in the end.)

Arin: Lloyd! (He holds out his hand to him.)

Lloyd: Arin! (He takes his hand and stands up.)

Arin: (He takes off his hood and Lloyd takes off his mask.) Um... Lloyd? I, uh...

Lloyd: Arin... I...

Arin and Lloyd: I'm sorry.

(A Spectral Dragonian attacks them, but they dodge it, put on their hood and mask again, and perform Spinjitzu. Nya and Wyldfyre fire Water and Heat at the Spectral Dragonians, but to no effect.)

Wyldfyre: (She growls.) Powers don't work on these things, which is really just stupid!

Nya: Luckily, Ninja are more than just our powers. (She breaks a mailbox, performs Spinjitzu and uses it as a weapon.)

(Jay fends off two Spectral Dragonians, who were flying towards Nya, using the Mech Dragon.)

Nya: I was starting to think you left, McCoolGuy!

Jay: Not yet, but now's a good time to do exactly that. Come on. (He extends the mech's arm to Nya.)

Nya: What? We're saving people here!

Jay: The mission is over. We came for the parents. Let's grab them and go!

Nya: For Ninja, saving people is always the most important mission.

(Jay makes an irritated face and stops extending the mech's hand.)

Jay: Then you, Ninja, are a bunch of clowns. (He pulls away and flies away using the Mech Dragon.)

Wyldfyre: (To Jay.) You mom is a bunch of clowns!

(Zarkt floats out of the Administration portal and plays his magical flute.)

Lloyd: Zarkt? (He takes off his mask.) Are the Forbidden Five the ones doing all this?

Arin: (He takes off his hood.) Maybe? But they're really out of it, like they're asleep. Playing those weird musical instruments that the souls go into.

Lloyd: What's really going on here?

(Zarkt continues playing his magical flute and absorbing souls.)

(In the Spectral Lands, Rox approaches the Well of the Lost, plays her magical flute, and deposits the souls within it into the well, mixing them with the healing elixir and causing the tablets with the symbols of the Source Dragons to charge even more. Thunderfang approaches.)

Thunderfang: My time has never been closer.

(The Ninja gather, but are cornered by the Spectral Dragonians.)

Nya: Lloyd! I think we're the only ones left!

Lloyd: These things aren't going to stop until they get every one of us!

Arin: Don't Tell Me, I finally found my parents just to lose it all...

(Suddenly, an anchor falls from the sky and drives away the Spectral Dragonians.)

Wyldfyre: Finally!

Lloyd: Huh?

Wyldfyre: (She climbs onto the anchor.) What? Hop on!

(The Ninja, Barry, and Jenny climb onto the anchor, which begins to rise. The Spectral Dragonians follow them, but Arin and Nya fend some off, while Jay watches.)

Jay: Maybe the Ninja aren't as stupid as I thought.

(Arin throws the Returning Blade at the Spectral Dragonians while Lloyd defends himself from one of them with a sword and the Returning Blade returns to Arin. The anchor finishes coming out of the hole and is revealed to be the Temple Bounty's anchor.)

Sora: The Bounty? How did--? Where did--?

Wyldfyre: Auto-pilot remote Roby installed for me, but it took forever to get here!

(They enter the ship and Wyldfyre pilots. Sora uses her technology to activate the Race Jet and connect it to the bottom of the ship. Arin approaches his parents.)

Jenny: Oh, you saved us.

Arin: Let's not assume we're safe yet. Wyldfyre is driving.

Jenny: Wait, just how bad is she?

(Wyldfyre starts the ship and flies very fast. Later, Wyldfyre pilots the ship at normal speed while humming. Arin talks to his parents.)

Arin: ...then we were trapped in Imperium, which is from another realm, like I said, and then things got really complicated, but Sora, remember she thought her powers were from Riyu, the little dragon? Well she--

Jenny: Uh, Arin?

Arin: Yeah?

Barry: You haven't stopped talking since we got onboard.

Arin: I just have so much to tell you!

Jenny: You don't have to tell us everything right now, honey. We have time.

Arin: I think... I think I need a hug.

Barry: Well, we don't have Ninja skills, but we do have hug skills.

(They hug and Arin starts crying.)

Arin: I tried not to lose hope you were still out there somewhere. I'm sorry I didn't find you sooner.

Jenny: Oh, we should have found you! We got tricked by Lee's lies, like all those people in there.

Arin: So everyone in the Land of Lee are someone's lost loved ones?

Barry: Pretty much. It was a very convincing lie Lee pulled off. We should have seen through it, though. What kind of amateur detectives are we?

Arin: Oh, that's another story! I solved a mystery at a tournament! I even have my old notebook from when we used to play detective games!

(Lloyd comes down the stairs with his head down.)

Sora: (To Lloyd.) I had the same feeling when we escaped Mysterium.

Lloyd: You know what I'm feeling?

(Wyldfyre makes the ship shake.)

Wyldfyre: Sorry! Not sorry!

Sora: If it's guilt about not being able to help all those people that were attacked? Yeah.

Lloyd: (He approaches Sora and Nya.) Huh. We're more alike than I realized.

Nya: We need to figure out where the Five are taking their souls, and find a way to get them back.

Lloyd: I thought for sure Thunderfang ate the Forbidden Five or something.

Sora: Maybe they were released when Arin hit Thunderfang with that elixir that released all his trapped souls.

Arin: Someone say my name?

Lloyd: We're trying to figure out how the Forbidden Five are still alive.

Arin: They don't look too alive. It's like they're hollow.

Sora: You think someone drained their life-forces?

Arin: Maybe. I don't know. My instincts haven't been the best lately.

Lloyd: That's not true.

Arin: (He sighs.) Lloyd, come on.

Lloyd: Don't be so hard on yourself, Arin. (He goes down to him.) I'm the one who failed you. I should have made finding your parents a higher priority.

Arin: But you did find them! I fell for Ras' lies.

Jay: (To Arin.) Hey, kid! (Arin looks back and sees him on top of one of the ship's roofs.) I'm the one that really found your parents!

Nya: Can't you just let them have this moment?

Jay: Whatever. I just happen to know the truth.

(Wyldfyre moves the ship a little and makes Jay fall off the roof.)

Arin: (To Lloyd.) I see he hasn't un-shattered his goodness.

Lloyd: Or recovered his memories.

(Arin and Lloyd go to the edge of the ship.)

Arin: There are some memories I wish I didn't have. Going with Ras was a disaster. All of you were right.

Lloyd: The journey was bumpy, sure, but your parents are alive, and you have them back. That's all that matters.

(Barry and Jenny are seen talking.)

Arin: Yeah. I feel like I finally have a place I belong again.

Lloyd: You also belong with the Ninja.

Arin: Really? I don't have an Elemental Power. The only way I could learn real Spinjitzu is when an evil tiger-toothed jerk taught me. You always told me I was special, but I'm not special, Lloyd. I don't think I'm a real Ninja.

Lloyd: Arin, don't say that.

Arin: But it's true!

Lloyd: I know you're special, maybe the most special Ninja there ever was. Egalt said the same thing. He made me promise to teach you properly... the teachings my Master Wu would have given you.

Arin: The same Master Wu that caused the Merge and all that chaos that came with it?

Lloyd: When you told me my uncle caused the Merge, I think I was in denial. I couldn't accept it. I wish I could ask him why he did it, but I've come accept that Wu passed on, sacrificed himself in the process of creating the Merge.

Arin: Actually, no. He isn't dead.

Lloyd: What?

Arin: When I was with Ras, I had to go to the Spectral Lands to find that elixir. I met a departed Ninja who insisted Master Wu didn't pass through there. Wu must be alive, somewhere, somehow.

Lloyd: "The Spectral Lands"?

Arin: Yeah, they used to be the Realm of the Departed and the Cursed Realm, now all mixed together. The Spectral Dragonians who attacked us looked kinda like the creatures who attack the dead there.

(The ship shakes.)

Lloyd: Dead like Thunderfang and the Forbidden Five?

Arin: If they really died, they'd've had to pass through there.

Lloyd: I had another vision and overheard the Source Dragons. They claimed Thunderfang was still a threat. Could he be in the Spectral Lands?

Arin: I... suppose it's possible.

Lloyd: (To Wyldfyre.) Change of course. We need to go to the Spectral Lands!

(Wyldfyre agrees and turns the ship to head for the Spectral Lands.)

(Cole, Kai, and Frak return to the Land of Lost Things, with Cole and Kai on the bikes and Frak in the jet.)

Kai: (He takes one of Geo's wafers out of his pocket and takes a bite, much to his disgust.) Still terrible!

Cole: How can you eat when we just saw proof a Source Dragon was killed?

Kai: I didn't eat! 'Cause it was gross!

(They speed off, go over a ramp, and arrive at the Rookery, where they see Spitz, Bonzle, and Fritz coming out, and Fritz and Spitz use Prismatic Blades to fight the Spectral Dragonians, who are invisible. Cole gets off his bike and runs to help them, but Fritz has his soul extracted, followed by Spitz and Bonzle.)

Cole: Oh, no!

Frak: What is happening here?

(Geo exits the Rookery and gasps when he sees that his family is hollow.)

Geo: I don't know. That fog rolled in out of nowhere with that weird music!

(Cole looks to the sides, then up and sees, along with the others, Nokt floating, playing his magical flute and absorbing souls into it.)

Cole: Nokt?

(Nokt flies away.)

Kai: That guy's back? Aren't the Forbidden Five dead?

Geo: (He looks through a Prismatic Blade.) Cole! You've gotta see this!

(Geo hands the Prismatic Blade to Cole, who looks through it and sees the Spectral Dragonians flying alongside Nokt.)

Cole: Geo, take the Finders to the Monastery for safety. (Geo nods.) We've got a villain to catch.

(They continue to see Nokt fly away.)

(The Temple Bounty arrives in the Spectral Lands and Wyldfyre drops anchor, causing the deck to shake.)

Wyldfyre: You're welcome!

(Jay descends from the ship to the ground using the Mech Dragon while the others descend using the anchor.)

Barry: We're not going in there, are we?

Sora: Well, you're not. (She uses her Technology to activate the Race Jet, disconnect it from the ship and make it descend to the ground.) I'm programming the jet to take you back to the Monastery in Ninjago. (She gets into the jet and starts programming it.)

Jay: Aw, why do only they get to leave?

Lloyd: (To Jay.) I'll find a way to get you more treasure if you come with us. We're gonna need all the help we can get.

Nya: Especially Ninja help.

Jay: Not a Ninja, but not gonna pass up treasure.

(Arin and his parents talk.)

Jenny: Are you sure you should go in there, Arin?

Arin: The Land of Lee is filled with other people's missing loved ones. I have to do whatever I can to release their souls, and I'm the only one who's been in the Spectral Lands before.

Barry: When you're done here, we should go to the Livoti Springs, like we used to every summer.

Arin: Yeah, it's not there anymore, but we definitely need a family vacation. (He and his parents hug.)

(Barry and Jenny get into the jet and fly to the Monastery.)

Lloyd: Arin?

(They enter the Spectral Lands.)

Lloyd: (To Arin.) Should we have seen something by now? Anything?

Arin: Yeah, it's weird the Soul Suckers don't know we're here, or that departed Ninja I met. (He runs to the end of the path , followed by the others.) Found something.

(Everyone gasps as they see Thunderfang, Kur, Rox, Drix, and the Spectral Dragonians near the Well of the Lost.)

Lloyd: None of this looks good.

(Zarkt arrives, approaches the well, plays his magical flute and deposits the stolen souls inside.)

Arin: That's the Well of the Lost. It's where I got the elixir that saved Ras and destroyed Thunderfang.

Sora: What's down that well?

Jay: And where did those flying ghost things that were with that guy go?

(Three Spectral Dragonians appear and attack them, but they manage to dodge. Arin slashes one upward with the Return Blade and Jay uses the Dragon Mech to kick it. Lloyd faces another Spectral Dragonian, which Nya fends off.)

Lloyd: We gotta get out of here!

(They try to escape, but are cornered by more Spectral Dragonians and decide to keep fighting.)

(In the dragon stables, Geo and Mr. Frohicky look after Fritz, Spitz and Bonzle.)

Geo: It's like they're sleepwalking.

Mr. Frohicky: Well, sleeping or not, I'm sure they're thirsty. Hmm, fetid tea it is!

(A jet sound is heard and wakes Heatwave.)

Geo: Visitors?

(In the hangar bay, Barry and Jenny exit the Race Jet and go down the stairs.)

Jenny: Is this the Monastery?

Barry: Hello? Anyone home?

(Mr. Frohicky arrives and approaches them.)

Mr. Frohicky: Um, who are you? (He stammers.) Are you supposed to be here?

Barry: I think so. Sora put the jet on autopilot. We're Arin's parents.

Mr. Frohicky: What a pleasure! I'm Mr. Frohicky, Assistant Keeper of the Monastery, and I'm thrilled to see my belief in your demise was incorrect.

Jenny: Uh... thanks. We don't want to intrude, but we heard our old house is gone, and something called the Crossroads is there now?

Mr. Frohicky: All true, all true, but we have a place here you can stay.

(They take the elevator to Arin's room (the relaxation room).)

Mr. Frohicky: Arin's old room! Make yourself at home. Unfortunately, I'm needed to help our other guests.

(Mr. Frohicky returns to the elevator and the secret door closes. Jenny picks up a picture and she and Barry look into the room.)

Jenny: (She gasps.) Is that...?

Barry: Our old porch swing! (He sits on the swing.) Arin kept it!

(Jenny sits next to Barry on the swing and they look at the picture of Arin with the other Ninja, proud that their son was able to achieve his dream.)

(The Ninja and Jay advance on the Spectral Dragonians and fight them. Jay notices Sprite.)

Jay: What is that thing, now?

(Arin and Sora notice Sprite too.)

Sora: It's trying to lead us that way.

Arin: Should we follow it?

(Lloyd blocks a Spectral Dragonian's attack with his sword and sees Sprite right next to him.)

Lloyd: I don't have any better ideas.

(The Ninja and Jay follow Sprite, but are followed by the Spectral Dragonians. Lloyd performs Spinjitzu to follow Sprite faster and stops when he sees him pass through the gate doors similar to the ones in the Monastery of Spinjitzu.)

Lloyd: In here!

(Lloyd performs Spinjitzu to open the gate doors and enters, followed by the others. Wyldfyre and Arin close the doors to prevent the Spectral Dragonians from entering and Lloyd runs to other doors, takes off his mask and opens them.)

Arin: is this another...

(They go further into the place.)

Nya: Monastery.

(This monastery is shown as having a sinister and spectral appearance.)

Sora: Which is... (She runs toward Lloyd.) A Portal Gate! A way back to the Monastery of Spinjitzu!

Wyldfyre: No! We need to go to the City of Temples!

Arin: Not exactly the time to visit your boyfriend.

Wyldfyre: Not for Roby. Well, kinda for Roby, but more 'cause Thunderfang got beaten really hard back in oldy-times, right?

Nya: At the Battle of the City of Temples! It was in Roby's book!

Wyldfyre: Yeah, so if there's a way to defeat him, Roby will know!

Sora: That is... strangely logical.

Lloyd: To the City of Temples!

(The light on top of the gate starts to glow, and the doors open, and the Ninja and Jay start running through.)

(The Ninja and Jay arrive at the City of Temples, which has the mist of the Spectral Lands spread.)

Lloyd: Was there an invasion here, too?

Jay: None of those Dragonian things.

Wyldfyre: (She look to the side and then grumbles.) And no Roby.

(Upon hearing and seeing a door slam, the Ninja and Jay prepare to fight, with Sora taking her stance, Nya wielding her spear, Wyldfyre using Heat, Arin wielding and aiming his Grappling Gun, Lloyd wielding his sword, and Jay wielding his Lightning Daggers. When the door opens, Roby appears moaning and hollow.)

(End of episode. For more information, click here.)
