(In the Village of the Tiger Tribe.)

Past Mei: ...But the elders cast the stones. They say the tremors will pass.

Past Zilvar: Ah, reading stones? Superstition! The evidence is clear. The tremors are getting worse. We must find a new home. Look at the cubs of our tribe. Do we leave their fate to old men peering at stones? Or do we find a place we know they'll be safe? Especially if one day we have a cub of our own. (Suddenly, the ground quakes violently.) I will not... cannot fail. (Zilvar leaves and waves goodbye to his people. He travels across the Lava Wastes, and watches as a Fire Dragon fights Heatwave, avoiding the two dragons. He climbs a cliff in the Forest of Spirits.)

Zilvar: After a long journey, I found a place that was peaceful. The perfect place to rebuild our home. (Past Zilvar holds a leaf.) I returned to Mei with hope in my heart. (Past Zilvar returns to the Village of the Tiger Tribe and finds it in ruins.)

Past Zilvar: No... (He runs to see the fallen memorial stone.)

Zilvar: The angry earth swallowed my village whole. It was gone... because I was gone, not there to save or protect them. I searched for days, but there were no survivors, no sign of escape. I added the names of the lost to our tribe's memorial stone, and then I left, but I had nowhere to go, so I wandered.

Nya: That's awful... I'm so sorry,

Lloyd: Zilvar. I know this is hard for you, but we've... crossed paths with someone who could be from your tribe. His name is Ras.

Zilvar: That is not a name from the stone. Perhaps this Ras is from another tribe. The Wyldness is vast.

Jay: Does anyone else smell burning?

Kai: Don't look at me.

(The Weapon of Desolation is trying to escape the Containment Unit.)

Zane: The integrity of the Containment Unit is weakening. The energy of the weapon may be leaking out.

Cole: Like the weapon's trying to escape. That's not NOT creeping me out.

Kai: It can't be ''trying'' anything, right? I mean, it's not like it's alive or something, right? Right?

Lloyd: One thing's for certain. We've got to get the weapon to the Abyss of Eternal Night and destroy it before it has another chance to destroy us.

(Zilvar rides on Grimtak while Kai and Cole drive their vehicles.)

Kai: What are the chances we'd run into two tiger dudes with zero connection to each other?

Cole: I dunno. I once met another Cole. Nothing like me. The guy didn't even like cake!

Zilvar: That's it.

(The Ninja and Zilvar arrive at the Never Kingdoms' invisible barrier.)

Lloyd: Given the warning, I expected the border to be more... intimidating.

Jay: Yeah, what if we try and cross, and "Ka-blooey". Maybe we should send Kai across alone to test it.

Nya: (gasps) Look!

Zazkzaz: Oh, hello! I say Pebbleton, what a friendly welcome. I told you opening a trade route to the Never Kingdoms would be a brilliant idea.

Nya: Stop! Turn back! If you've never been there before, the barrier won't let you pass!

Zane: Maybe they think the dangerous barrier is just a rumor.

Cole: Maybe it is just a rumor. (Pebbleton and Zazkzaz walk through the barrier, but are suddenly flung back.) Not a rumor.

Lloyd: If Reggie's right about the barrier, he's probably also right about the exception. We've been to the Never-Realm before the Merge, so we should be able to get in now.

Zilvar: Enough waiting. Time to find out! (He enters through the barrier.)

Jay: I was about to do that.

(The Ninja enter the barrier.)

Cole: Huh... Let's scout what's up ahead.

(The Ninja and Zilvar see a Monastery and Lloyd's old Titan Mech.)

Lloyd: Look! My old mech and another Monastery? That wasn't here before.

Zane: Certainly fits with the theory that new Monasteries manifested in each land due to the merge. Now, if only the Monastery Portal Gates still worked, our journey home would be much shorter.

(The Ninja and Zilvar approach the Monastery.)

Lloyd: It's like a shrine.

Nya: Guess they remember us.

Children: Gotcha! Gotcha!

(Zilvar waves at the children.)

Jay: We get a shrine, but he gets the welcome.

Sorla: Nya. (waves at Nya)

Nya: (happily hugs Sorla) Sorla! What are you doing here?

Sorla: When this monastery appeared, both Fishers and Formlings sent pilgrims to pay their respects to the ninja who ended the Ice Emperor's reign.

(Zane walks up to the shrine of the old titan mech.)

Lloyd: Zane, I know what you're thinking, but it wasn't you.

Zane: I may not have been the Ice Emperor, but he still used (uses his power to create an ice flower for the pile of gifts and looks at the pile sadly.) my power to do terrible things. If the Weapon of Desolation can truly affect us, as the Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu once did to me, then our mission is even more urgent than we thought.

(A wolf barks and bumps into Lloyd.)

Lloyd: (Groans and laughs) Oh!

Akita: (She transforms back into her human form.) Lloyd! I knew I would see you again one day.

Lloyd: Akita! it's been so long.

Akita: Longer for me. Time passed faster in our realm, but that was before the Merge. Now we are the same. We are together.

Lloyd: Yes, uh, we are.

Beniro: Akita! You caught a scent and rushed ahead so fast. I thought I'd find you with prey in your jaws.

Akita: Lloyd, this is Beniro. We are to be Form-Bonded at the next changing of leaves.

Lloyd: Form-Bonded? I'm not sure I--

Akita: We are to be together.

Lloyd: Oh. Congratulations.

(Lloyd explains to Akita about to how the ninja need to get to the Abyss of Eternal Night.)

Lloyd: ...So that's why we need to reach the Abyss as soon as possible.

Akita: Hmm... The Abyss is a place of ill omen for my people. It is surrounded by a dense fog. You'll need a guide who can navigate by scent.

Lloyd: Then I know just the person!

Akita: Oh, I am sorry. I wish I could take you, but I only stopped here for supplies on my journey to Ninjago.

Lloyd: Ninjago? Why are you going there?

Akita: I am the Never Kingdoms' delegate to the Summit of the Merged Lands. I actually volunteered in the hopes of seeing you again.

Lloyd: Oh. I can't say I'm not disappointed, but I understand.

Akita: I cannot be the one to guide you, but I know one who can.

(Akita waves goodbye to Lloyd and leaves.)

Beniro: I'm excited for our journey to the Abyss, Lloyd of Ninjago. We leave at dawn.

(The ninja and Zilvar are sleeping when Kai suddenly starts sleepwalking and steps on Nya while muttering.)

Nya: Hmm... Oof! (Quietly, she wakes up Lloyd and Zane.) Hey! Wake up! Look!

Zane: Is Kai sleepwalking?

Wu (via Kai's body): Beware... the Beast Warrior.

Lloyd: Kai, what are you--?

Wu: Beast Warrior! Beware...

Lloyd: Master Wu?

Zilvar: What is this?

Nya: Sorry, we didn't mean to wake you. Kai was--

(Kai kneels down and Master Wu's "ghost" form comes out of his mouth and goes through the portal gate.)

(The next day, Jay buys some fish while Lloyd buys apples to put in the bucket Kai is holding.)

Kai: I'm fine. (coughs) Guess my throat's a little scratchy.

Jay: You're saying that ball of light was Master Wu? I'm sorry, I guess my question is-- What are you even talking about?

Kai: I followed Master Wu's voice before. He even spoke to Cole and me in a kind of spirit form after we unearthed the giant dragon fossil.

Lloyd: But not a spirit. Arin told me Master Wu never passed through the Spectral Lands. He's not departed.

Kai: Are we sure of that? It all seems a little "beyond the grave"-ish.

Lloyd: Wherever or whatever my uncle is now. We should probably take his warning seriously

Kai: (spots Zilvar with Grimtak, giving some coins to the shopmen.) Hey, Jay, I know you have history with him, but it can't be a coincidence our new Beast Warrior friend just happened to be going to the same secluded land where we're taking a powerful weapon of evil.

Lloyd: Zilvar's done nothing to lose our trust, but this mission is too important to leave anything to chance.

Jay: Zilvar saved my life, so I trust him more than a ball of light who thinks hanging out in Kai's mouth is a good idea, and now that he's out of the Gladiator pit, Zilvar has a right to start his new life. We don't need to drag him further into our mess.

(Lloyd and Kai nod. Zilvar feeds Grimtak, while Lloyd goes beside him.)

Zilvar: So you don't want my aid completing your journey?

Lloyd: You got us this far, and we have a guide to take us the rest of the way. You deserve to start your new life as a free man, Zilvar.

Zilvar: Hmm. Safe travels, Ninja. (shakes hands with Lloyd) You have my gratitude and my respect.

(Sorla watches Lloyd and Zilvar, while Nya fills a bucket with water.)

Sorla: Hmm... Zilvar could very well be this Beast Warrior. I remember when he arrived to us broken, and rebuilt himself piece by piece. I won't deny there is darkness in him, but pain can look like evil if it never sees the light.

Nya: I've also seen pain make good people do bad things more times than I'd like.

(Jay takes the Land Bounty's driver seat, Lloyd and Nya walk beside him, and Beniro is outside.)

Lloyd: We better get moving.

Beniro: I hope you can keep up, Ninja! (transforms into a fox.)

(The Land Bounty follows Beniro into the forest.)

Zane: Interference in the area is making our route impossible to track.

Lloyd: Which means we better not lose sight of Beniro.

(The Containment Unit sparks; the Weapon of Desolation shakes and charges up.)

Lloyd: Watch it!

Nya: Duck!

(The Weapon of Desolation makes a chaotic wave.)

Nya: What was that?

Zane: A buildup of energy. Like a boiling pot releasing steam. It's only a matter of time before the lid blows. Anything could set it off. Drive carefully, Jay.

Jay: Uh, guys, I lost Beniro

Lloyd: Maybe we should sto-- (spots boulders falling) Look out!

(The Land Bounty dodges the boulders.)

Jay: Ah! Aah! Oh, come on!

(The Land Bounty dodges a boulder as more boulders fall, blocking the path. The Land Bounty's engine powers down.)

Lloyd: Phew!

Cole: Huh? (looks at the boulders) Seems like claw marks.

(The ninja look up and see Beniro untransformed.)

Beniro: I thought I lost you in the fog. Lucky that this one's scent (taps Lloyd) was easy enough to follow.

Kai: Do random boulders just fall from the sky out here?

Beniro: The fog hides steep cliffs. Your land ship's vibrations probably caused an avalanche. We should make camp anyway. The path will become difficult to navigate at night.

(At night, the ninja and Beniro set up a campfire and sit down on the dirt and grass.)

Lloyd: Beniro, what can you tell us about the Abyss of Eternal Night?

Beniro: My people's legends claim the first Elemental Master of Form once roamed these lands as a Legendary Beast. (puts a stick into a campfire, causing it to show the image of a wolf) Able to assume the traits of any animal, (the image shifts into a bird) she was revered by the Nameless Tribe... (the image shifts to a wolf howling.) until the day a Cursed Thorn became embedded in her paw. (the image shifts into a Dragon-Ape roaring) She rampaged for generations, (the image shifts into the First Spinjitzu Master) only to be subdued with the help of the First Spinjitzu Master. As thanks, the restored Master of Form gifted her animal forms to the Nameless Tribe. Since that day, they became known as Formlings. The First Spinjitzu Master destroyed the Thorn, but in doing so created the Abyss of Eternal Night. He feared its power and forbade us from nearing it.

Kai: So Formlings' shape-changing ability was granted by Elemental Powers? That makes Formlings kinda like honorary Elemental Masters.

Lloyd: Thank you for going against your people's beliefs to aid us.

Beniro: I would do anything for Akita.

Lloyd: Yeah. Me too.

(Zilvar is walking around in the Monastery of Never Kingdoms, when a leaf lands on his shoulder.)

Zilvar: Mei, you would have loved it here.

(The monastery's portal gate flashes red and the door opens.)

Ras: Zilvar of the Wyldness. (Zilvar drops the leaf.)

(The ninja arrive at a frozen river, and Beniro un-transforms.)

Zane: This is no ordinary frozen river.

Beniro: The Boreal River is all that remains of the Ice Emperor's reign. Like a frozen scar on our land. Spring comes, summer comes... it does not thaw. This is not just ice, it's a curse.

Zane: We should avoid crossing it.

Lloyd: From the state of the Containment Unit, I'd say going around it would take time we don't have.

Zane: Then we fly across. (Zane goes to the top of the Land Bounty.) Prepare for separation. (The Land Bounty's engines power up but an alarm blares and the Land Bounty lands.) Separation failure. We're jammed!

Nya: (Sees the ice on the Land Bounty.) The system's frosted over. How'd that happen so quickly?

Kai: What about "cursed ice" are you not understanding?

Lloyd: I'm sorry, Zane. Looks like we'll have to cross the river after all.

(Most of the ninja go inside the Land Bounty, while Kai and Cole go to their vehicles.)

Beniro: May I join you inside? Formlings will not touch the cursed ice.

(The Land Bounty, Cole's combiner bike, and Kai's race buggy cross slowly cross the river.)

Beniro: Akita told me many things about the Green Ninja who saved our people.

Lloyd: Well, I didn't do it alone. Akita was a big part of everything.

Beniro: She said you would return one day. I thought you'd be taller.

Lloyd: We should probably focus on crossing the river.

Beniro: Oh, have I done something to offend you? If so. I apologize.

Lloyd: (sighs) Listen, Beniro--

(The Weapon of Desolation activates and corrupts Beniro.)

Nya: What's wrong with him?

Zane: He's reacting to the Weapon of Desolation.

(Beniro is controlled, transforming into a feral version of his fox form. He snarls and barks at the Ninja. Nya backs away.)

Lloyd: Don't hurt him. He's innocent!

(Beniro snarls at Nya, Lloyd and Jay, then grabs the Weapon of Desolation with his mouth and runs away.)

Jay: Don't let him get away!

Kai: I'm telling you, I heard something. (Beniro lands and snarls, then runs.) Oh! (Cole and Kai dodge him.)

Cole: Was that... Beniro?

Kai: Whoever it was, it has the Weapon. Let's go!

(Cole and Kai get on their vehicles and chase Beniro, the Weapon of Desolation activates and summons ice spikes. The vehicles dodge and avoid the ice spikes.)

Cole: Woah!

(Nya, Jay, Zane and Lloyd get out of the Land Bounty, then the ice below the Land Bounty cracks.)

Lloyd: Zane!

Zane: (tries to use his power but fails) I... I can't. This river was created by my Elemental Power. It too is reacting to the Weapon's energy.

Nya: Go! Jay and I will take care of the Bounty.

(Zane and Lloyd nod then run to Beniro. In front of them an ice spike forms and Beniro throws the Weapon of Desolation, which lands in the ice and activates, summoning Ice Monsters.)

Lloyd: Not more of these guys.

(Lloyd activates his Draconus Form, with Zane doing the same afterwards. Lloyd dodges an Ice Monster's attack, then slashes it away. Zane sharpens his claws and then charges at another Ice Monster. The ice beneath the Land Bounty cracks.)

Nya: No, no! We have to abandon ship!

Jay: Wait, I have an idea!

(Jay grabs Nya, then climbs to the top, going towards the steering wheel and buttons.)

Nya: I told you, it won't separate!

Jay: We don't need it to! (pulls the lever down to activate the thrusters)

Nya: We're too heavy. It'll never fly.

Jay: Who said anything about flying? I just want to not sink.

(The Land Bounty's thrusters activate. The ice beneath the Land Bounty is destroyed , but the Land Bounty floats with its thrusters. In the fight between Lloyd and the Ice Monsters, Lloyd smacks his tail and kicks an Ice Monster, dodges its attack, then spins and rams into it, while Zane blocks an attack and flies towards another Ice Monster, grabs it, spins it up, throws it, and slams it to the ground. Another Ice Monster gets summoned and attacks Zane, but Lloyd slashes it, then tries to ram it towards the weapon but fails as the Ice Monster jumps and slams the ground. Kai and Cole circle around the Weapon of Desolation, the Weapon activates and makes a shockwave that freezes Kai and Cole's vehicles.)

Kai: Ugh! (shoots fire at an Ice Monster, then tries to melt the ice on his vehicle wheels.)

Lloyd: Zane, get this ice under control!

Zane: But the Ice Emperor's corruption lingers here. What if I try to control it, and it controls me?

Lloyd: Raah! (slashes and rams into an Ice Monster) I won't let that happen. You were alone then. We're here for you now.

(Zane nods then flies away, Lloyd dodges an Ice Monster's attack, then slashes the Ice Monster. Kai blows an Ice Monster away with Fire. Zane lands near the Weapon, then tries to get the ice under control, but the Weapon reacts.)

Zane: The weapon is defending itself!

(Beniro rushes to Zane to attack but gets knocked down by Grimtak, Beniro runs away and Lloyd lands.)

Lloyd: Zilvar! Where did you--?

Zilvar: No time! I have something vital to-- (Beniro attacks Lloyd.) Leave the Ninja be!

Lloyd: I've got this. Zilvar, I need you to get ahold of that Weapon! You're the only one who can touch it!

(Zilvar and Grimtak run towards the weapon, while Lloyd dodges Beniro's attacks. In the Land Bounty, the engine blows smoke, then shakes. Zilvar and Grimtak dodge ice spikes summoned by the Weaoon, then grab it.)

Lloyd: Now, Zane!

(Zane jumps and slams the ground, making a shockwave. The ice beneath the Land Bounty returns, and it lands on the ice. Kai and Cole's vehicles are thawed and they drive to the Land Bounty.)

Nya: Whew! That was close!

Lloyd: I know you're in there, Beniro. Think of Akita!

(Beniro closes his eyes and calms down, then untransforms. The Land Bounty, Combiner bike and Race buggy cross the river with Lloyd and Zane untransformed.)

Jay: Zilvar with the last-minute save. Thanks, buddy! I knew you were a good egg the moment I met you.

Zilvar: I was not hatched from an egg.

Jay: Yeah, okay. Noted.

Cole: What made you come after us?

Zilvar: I had a feeling you might need me.

Beniro: Again, I apologize for my actions. Unfortunately, you must realize I cannot guide you to the Abyss. That Weapon is too... too...

Lloyd: We understand. Zilvar will help us from here.

Beniro: I made this map to guide our path. It is yours now. (gives the map to Lloyd) I'll return to the Monastery to wait for Akita. Akita is the love of my life... but I'm not sure that I am hers. Her people will always come first. I think you and her are alike in that way. (He walks away and transforms before running.)

(In the Land Bounty, Lloyd, Cole, Jay, Nya, Kai, and Zane check the Containment Unit.)

Nya: So the "Beast Warrior" warning was about Beniro, not Zilvar. Huh, Master Wu always was kinda cryptic.

Kai: But can we really trust Zilvar to carry it all the way to the Abyss?

Lloyd: Zilvar saved all of us today. I trust him, but I hear your concerns. The thing is, with the Containment Unit destroyed, do we have any other choice?

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
