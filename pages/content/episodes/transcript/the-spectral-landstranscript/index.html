(Arin begins his training, but took off his bag and looked at the Lloyd plush Aru gave him.)

Nya: So?

Kai: So get this, I've been practicing GX-P on an emulator. I think I've got the skills to finally beat his high score, and if that happens, he'll come from anywhere in the Merged Lands to take back his top spot.

Nya: He won't even remember it exists.

Kai: No matter what's happened to Jay, no matter if his goodness is shattered, or his memory gone, wherever he is, whatever he's doing, if his high score is broken, he'll feel it, and he'll come.

Wyldfyre: Why would Jay be so crazy about a video game? They're super boring. All that grammar and spelling. I played every game in my Caregiver Bot's memory banks, and they were all the worst. "Carrying the one." "Using proper manners." Blech!

Kai: Oh, Wyldfyre. I predict this is going to be a very big day for you.

(Arin is still carrying Ras on his back, while Ras is closing his eyes. They both almost get to the Spectral Lands.)

Ras: (Heavy breathing) Yes. Hurry.

Ras: No. I-I must- (Coughing)

Arin: Well of the Lost. Got it. Any clue where it is in there?

Ras: Village center... hilltop.

Arin: Okay, you stay here. Save your energy. I'm going in-

Ras: Arin! Returning Blade. Prismatic steel. You... will need it.

Arin: On it! Don't die on me, Ras.

Arin: (Shivering) This place is freezing. Okay, not seeing any dead people... yet.

Kai: You have a better idea we should try instead?

Wyldfyre: I wonder if they got that "Organize your bedroom" game. I was pretty good at that one.

Arin: Returning Blade? Nice. Why was Ras keeping this thing a secret? "Village Center, Hilltop." It's gotta be this way- Woah!

Wydlefyre plays more games, she plays multiple arcades in time. The clock spinning on how much time they took. Kai struggles while Wyldfyre is enjoying the arcade, as more time went by.)

Lobbo: Tuna Gelatin, with mints!

Arin: Yeah, I'd like to stay alive.

Arin: "Move on?" To where?

Arin: Why haven't you moved on?

Morro: Sometimes I feel the pull to do just that, but I told you, I was a Ninja, and I've decided it's my duty to protect everyone who passes through here. Even if they happen to still be alive. We're here.

Morro: Aah!
