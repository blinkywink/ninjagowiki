Lloyd: I hope there’s an explanation for all of this.

(Sora, Cole, Arin, Kai and Riyu are all covered in noodles)

Sora: There is.

Kai: But warning, it's weird. (Eats a noodle from his hair) Mmm.

Lloyd: You left the monastery for lunch and came back hours later, covered in food. It's already weird.

Arin: All we wanted to do was take Kai and Cole to the (Riyu jumps on his shoulder and slurps a noodle off it) noodle cook-off in the Crossroads. Which started out okay.

(A flashback starts on the Crossroads. The ninja run to Grab-Barg's stand)

Past Sora: This is not okay. We're late for the cook-off! Come on!

Past Cole: You say that the Crossroads has the best noodles? But I swear, Chen's Noodle House, cannot be beaten.

Past Sora: You brought outside noodle to the Crossroads' noodle cook-off?

Past Cole: What if I don't like the noodles here? I'm not gonna go hungry. (He starts eating his noodles. Meanwhile, a Lava-Tide is pushing a crate.)

Past Lava-Tony: Hurry up and help me here! I scored some secret ingredients that are sure to help us win the cook-off.

Past Lava-Tide 1: Yes, Chef! (Lava-Tides put the crate on top of their heads and run.)

Past Sora: (holding a bowl of strange noodles) Try this. Your mind will be blown!

Past Cole: (he takes string of noodle and slurps it) Ugh! Too many competing tastes.

Past Sora: Not my fault you like noodles with no flavor.

(Something roars.)

Past Citizen 1: What are those things?

(Two Ice Dragons come to the Crossroads.)

Past Citizen 2: Those Ice Dragons are in attack formation! Run!

(One breaths ice on the ground.)

Past Lava-Tony: Those Ice Dragons intend to harm me, for no reason. Defend my honor!

Past Kai: Hey, we're gonna protect everyone! But I'm not sure your "honor" has anything to with-

Past Lava-Tony: Not you! My sous-chefs!

(Six Lava-Tides come and blast the first Ice Dragons)

Past Arin: This has gotten way dangerous.

Past Sora: Yeah, it has. And we're the only ones who can stop it!

(Arin, Sora, and Cole run closer to the battle while Kai finishes his noodles.)

Past Cole: Kai!

Past Kai: (mouthful) Coming! (throws the bowl aside and follows the others.)

(Cole, Sora, and Kai open their Elemental Mechs and get into them).

Past Arin: Don't hurt them! If Ice Dragons are acting like this, they must have a good reason!

Past Cole: You're worried about us hurting them?

Past Lava-Tony: Don't let them near my crate! Uh. . . uh, uh. . . I mean me! Protect me!

Past Arin: That seem odd to you, Riyu?

(One of the dragons lands on a roof, spots Lava-Tony, and seemingly sets its sights on him.)

Past Lava-Tony: Ahh! (The dragon prepares to fly at him before three Lava-Tides move in front and start shooting fireballs at the dragon)

Past Cole: I got to put a plug in these Lava-Tides. (He causes a shockwave, knocking the Lava-Tides off their feet and having rocks clogging their heads.)

Past Lava-Tide 1: Hey, not fair! No fairness! The ninja are as bad as the dragons! End them!

(As the Lava-Tides approach Cole's mech, Sora launches a rope, binding the Lava-Tides up.)

Past Kai: Help! (While Lava-Tides attack his mech, he launches a burst of fire, dealing no damage to them.)

Past Kai: In this case, fire does not seem to fight fire!

Past Cole: Try this, Kai! Swap! (He swaps of his mech's arms with Kai's mech.)

(Kai uses the arm from Cole's mech to cause a shockwave, knocking the Lava-Tides out)

Past Kai: Nice! Cole, behind you!

(An Ice Dragons dives at Cole's mech and shoots a beam of ice at him. Cole uses the arm from Kai's mech to shoot a beam of fire at the ice, blocking it.)

Past Sora: Here! Take my legs! (Cole and Sora's mechs swap their legs.) You'll need this, too! (She also swaps one of her mech's arms with Cole's mech.)

(Cole flies at the Ice Dragon, dodging the dragon's attacks before binding it with a rope from Sora's mech.)

(Grunting, Sora uses one of the legs from Cole's mech to cause another shockwave, knocking the Lava-Tides out).

Past Sora: Woo-hoo! (She notices the other ice dragon, ready to attack her.)

Past Kai: Sora, heads up! Or arms up! (His mech swaps both of its arms with Sora's mech.)

(Sora uses one of the arms from Kai's mech to block the ice dragon's attack, while Kai uses one of the arms from Sora's mech to capture the dragon using a rope.)

Past Kai: See? There! And Lloyd says we don't focus enough on teamwork. (chuckles before his mech high-fives and fist bumps Cole's mech.)

(Arin swings to Lava-Tony and the crate.)

Past Arin: What's in the box, chef?

Past Lava-Tony: What!? Nothing! (Riyu approaches him from behind.) Sentimental items from when I was just a tiny ball red hot sulf-

Past Arin: Look out! Dragon!

Past Lava-Tony: You expect me to fall for-Ahhh!! (Riyu attacks him, pushing him out of the box's way.)

Past Arin: I tried to warn you.

(Arin opens the box.)

Past Arin: Dragon eggs? That's why the dragons are attacking. Did you steal these?

Past Lava-Tony: Ughh. Okay, got to run!

(Lava-Tony is pushed again by Riyu, preventing him from escaping. Screaming, he slams into his oven, causing the oven to heat up and make a whistling noise.)

Past Arin: Oh no! I think his oven's gonna-

(The oven explodes, spreading noodles all over the ninja, their mechs, and Riyu. The flashback ends.)

Sora: We gave the stolen eggs back to the Ice Dragons, but the Crossroads will be cleaning up noodles for a week. At least.

Cole: But get this. The Crossroads noodles mixed in with my noodles, it's the perfect blend.

Sora: He's not lying. (She and Cole begin eating the bowl of noodles that Cole is holding in his hand.) Ah! So savory.

Lloyd: But aren't you forgetting something? (He walks over to Kai.) You were supposed to bring some lunch back for me. I'm starving!

(Kai get some of the noodles off his hair and holds it out to Lloyd, who stares in dismay.)
