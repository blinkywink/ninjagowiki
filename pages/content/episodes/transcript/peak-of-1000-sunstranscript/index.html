Peak of 1000 Suns/Transcript

Overview

(At the Mountains of Impossible Height.)

Wyldfyre: (Out of breath. Huff-Huff.) Okay, finally here. (Huff) Hey! Where are the peppers?

Kai: (Huffing out of breath) They're not gonna just be lying around, Wyldfyre, you have to hunt for them.

Wyldfyre: What?! I get all the way up one of the “Hills of Crazy Tall”–

Kai: “Mountains of Impossible Height.”

Wyldfyre: That’s what I said! –because you told me that the hottest peppers in the Merged Lands were up here! But I see ZERO PEPPERS! (Gasps.) Was this just a trick to get me to hike? YOU KNOW I HATE HIKING! You evil genius!

Kai: No trick! The scroll said this was the “Peak of 1,000 Suns” cause of the tiny orange peppers that grow here.

Wyldfyre: TINY? How can they be all that hot if they’re tiny?

Kai: Small things can pack big punches, trust me. I know.

Wyldfyre: Well, they’re not here. You sure that scroll was right? Could be fake. That Fedulian guy? He’s been selling scrolls about how to turn into a dragon! And guess what? FAKE CITY, POPULATION: FAKE-HUNDRED AND ONE– a total rip-off. Even if you try to follow the directions over and over again… (Beat, covering.) …which is what I heard from someone, not me.

Kai: Fedulian’s the least trustworthy person I know, and I met Lloyd’s dad. What kind of idiot would buy anything from him and expect it to be real?

Wyldfyre: (Scoffs.) Yeah. Nobody would. Nope. Not me.

Kai: This scroll was in our library, which was founded by the monks who built the Monastery. Master Wu used to say he’d bet his life on the truths in any of these scrolls.

Wyldfyre: Why does the Monastery have scrolls about peppers? Aren’t they all about– I don’t know– training, ways to meditate, ancient legends of tombs with magical powers and junk?

Kai: That’s there too, but Wu said that all knowledge is important. I found the pepper scroll on a rack of botanical scrolls, like how to grow tea leaves. Maybe Master Wu used to make hot pepper tea or something?

Wyldfyre: Most tea is warm, weak garbage water, but that sounds like tea I could get behind. Ok, let’s look for these tiny peppers. But if we don’t find ‘em, I say we burn the scroll library down in revenge!

Kai: Wyldfyre! Revenge isn’t the way of the Ninja!

Wyldfyre: Then we’ll only burn half of it. For fun, not revenge.

Kai: Yeah, Lloyd would love that.

Wyldfyre: Nothing makes Mr. Green-Stuffy-Pants happy. He’s the Elemental Master of Angst. And he’s ready to kick us out anyway cuz of our “Fire vs Hot Challenge”.

Kai: Yeah, what’s his deal? All we did was challenge each other to taste the hottest peppers to see which of us can take the most FI-YAAAHHH.

Wyldfyre: You mean, which one of us can handle the HEAT. I was sure those “Devonian Skin-Blister Peppers” were gonna take you out.

Kai: For the Elemental Master of Fire, they’re basically a half-step up from the Land of Lee’s Candy Mushrooms. But I thought those “Shintaro Shishitos” were gonna knock you down for sure.

Wyldfyre: Those weak sauce peppers couldn’t get the Master of Heat to break a sweat. You know, I think Lloyd’s just jealous cuz he couldn’t even take a single bite of a “Hypnobrai Jalapeño.” He’s all: (With a Lloyd impression) “Enough! Stop this stupid challenge right now!” HA!

Kai: Nice Lloyd. Check it– (With a Lloyd impression) “Kai, how come you’re so cool all the time? It makes me super jealous. I’m GREEN with envy.”– HEY LOOK!!! The “Thousand Sun Peppers”!!!

Wyldfyre: WHERE?!

Kai: Under there. See? Beneath the shrubs. Look down... Lower.

Wyldfyre: Oh. Yeah. Ok. Jeez. They really are tiny. You sure these are them?

Kai: “Small, round, bright orange peppers on purple vines.” Just like the scroll said.

Wyldfyre: But, I mean, they’re tinier than tiny. Should we eat a whole handful, maybe?

Kai: The scroll made it sound like a pretty big deal. Let’s start with one each.

Wyldfyre: Ok. (Coughs.) Weak.

Kai: Let me just grab– (plucking effort.) THERE. One for you, one for me. On the count of three. One… two… three!

(Kai and Wyldfyre chomp.)

Wyldfyre: (Chewing with a mouthful.) See? I told you, no big– (Pained squealing.)

Kai: Are you feeling it? I’m not getting– (Pained sound.)

Wyldfyre: (Feeling the heat.) Yeah. Not– (Pained sound.) Not getting anything over here. (Groans.)

Kai: (Very pained.) Ugggggggg– YEOW. Me either. Totally feeling nothing.

Wyldfyre: (Still in pain.) You– sure? (Pain emote.) You’re suddenly sweating, like sooo much.

Kai: (Clearly in pain.) Hm. Am I? It’s a warm day. (Low growl of pain.) But I bet you’re feeling it. Your face is turning red and your hands are shaking.

Wyldfyre: (Pain gets worse.) No, just– uh– practicing hand dancing. Hand Dancing is a new hobby I’m inventing. It’s very popular. (Low pained groan.)

Kai: (Pain at its worst.) Okay then, so the Thousand Sun Peppers clearly don’t work on either of us, as expected.

Wyldfyre: Clearly. (In pain.) WHOOP-WHOOP.

Kai: On a completely unrelated note, I’m thinking of asking Zane to freeze my esophagus for 30 minutes. Wanna come?

Wyldfyre: Sure. I don’t need a frozen esophagus or anything, but maybe I’ll just try it for fun.

Kai: OK, LET’S GO!!! RIGHT NOW! RUN!!!

(Wyldfyre and Kai make painful howls as they run away.)
