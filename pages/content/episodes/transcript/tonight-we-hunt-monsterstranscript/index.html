(At the border between Ninjago City and the Old Town, The Chief Loyalist enters the Loyalist hideout, where she remotely contacts six other Loyalists.)

Chief Loyalist: Report.

Loyalist: I believe I'm close. I've been following a series of exotic readings that look promising.

Chief Loyalist: I will be the judge of what is promising or not. Keep me informed of any data you uncover. (turns towards another Loyalist) Report. (The Loyalist cowers in shame.) Nothing? How about the rest of you? (The other Loyalists also cower in shame.) Are you even trying? Her return to glory is in our hands! Do you understand that? We cannot fail! The Weapon of Desolation (shows the hologram of Weapon of Desolation) should be releasing massive amounts of energy during each creation event... pulses so significant they have to be traceable. So trace them! (The Loyalists all nod and leave the meeting. The Chief Loyalist walks up to the hologram of Weapon of Desolation.) You're out there making your little monsters, and we will find you so you can make them for us.

(A flashback to when Jay woke up in the Land of Madness after losing his memories.)

(Jay groans.)

Administration Agent 1: What's this? Your Mud Consumption Supplemental Permit has a smudge on the corner? (The agents point their weapons at a Cragling, who raises their hands in the air.) Hands on your head!

Jay: What's going on? Where am I? (groans) Uh... actually, who am I? Hey! You people in suits, you must be official or something, right?

Administration Agent 1: Sir, we are from the Administration. I can honestly tell you we are literally as official as it is possible to be. Indeed, "official" is our one defining trait.

(The Cragling sneaks off and runs away from the agents.)

Jay: Uh, great. Can you officially tell me who I am?

Administration Agent 1: Unlikely. Our field database only has files on important people, and, uh, well, don't get your hopes up. (He uses a device to scan Jay's face.) Agent Jay Walker.

Jay: Jay Walker, Jay Walker. Jay Walker.

Administration Agent 1: Hey, this guy's... management!

(The agent gives a water bottle to Jay.)

Jay: Oh, uh... thanks? (He drinks from it.)

Administration Agent 1: Sorry we didn't recognize you, Agent Walker. Can we get you anything else, sir? If this is a test, I hope we passed, sir. I'm up for a promotion in a few decades, so if you could put in a good word for me--

Blake Donovan: Jay, it's you!

Administration Agent 1: Agent Donovan!

Blake Donovan: Bro, I was so worried when you didn't show up for the inter-departmental pickleball.

Jay: You... know me?

Blake Donovan: Know you? Stop kidding around, Jay. You're only my best friend ever! What are you doing out here, buddy? What's with these clothes? Let's get you back to your department

(The flashback ends. In the present, Jay, Kai and Nya arrive at the clearing it happened in.)

Jay: This is where I woke up. First place I ever remember being. If coming here doesn't unlock my memories, I don't know what will.

Nya: All of us who flew into the Merge woke up in weird places, but we all kept our memories. Why are you the only one with amnesia?

Kai: Maybe his brain was less stable begin with?

Jay: Come on, Jay. (Nya scolds Kai and stomps on his foot, causing him to yelp.) Something here's gotta trigger your brain. Something! Anything.

(Frak and Reginald meet each other in Umbrasilvis.)

Sora: Frak, that's your dad?

Reginald: Indeed! Frak does have the honor of being my offspring! Sir Reginald Slitherbottom. The pleasure is all yours.

(Sora awkwardly smiles. Riyu and Wyldfyre are serious.)

Reginald: And these are my esteemed colleagues in the Explorer's Club of Ninjago! Johnny Courageous! Rowan Rockspike! Seafoam Green! Last, and also least, Gweeg. Ha! Just pulling your chain, Gweeg!

(Gweeg grumbles.)

Wyldfyre: Huh, your names are all surprisingly stupid.

(The Explorer's Club members laugh.)

Reginald: I like this one. She's filled with vim! (he chuckles) Good show!

Johnny: So, you're young Frak! Your father has spoken of you!

Reginald: Have I?

Johnny: (laughs) Remember? That one time we hired that incompetent slug creature, you jokingly referred to him as "Frak."

(Frak sighs.)

Reginald: Ah, yes, that I did, good sir. Yeah, you do have a rather impressive memory. At times. Introduce me to your friends, son.

Frak: Oh, uh, Dad, this is Sora, Wyldfyre, and Riyu. They're Ninja, just like me! I'm a Ninja now!

Reginald: A Ninja? You don't say! How exciting, but are there any real Ninja here with you?

Frak: Dad? we're all real Ninja (Sora, Wyldfyre, and Riyu growl) and Elemental Masters. I--

Reginald: Oh, yes, yes, yes, of course you are, but you do know what I mean. The "classic" Ninja? Who people have actually heard of... and like.

Frak: The original Ninja aren't with us right now, but we do know all the Ninja, and by the way, Master Lloyd, the Legendary Green Ninja, told me I'm an excellent student.

Reginald: I'm sure you're passable as a student, son, because you had a brilliant teacher for a dad!

(Wyldfyre and Riyu growl)

Sora: (To Reginald) Any chance you're heading back to Ninjago? We're in a bit of a hurry.

Reginald: Well, if you need a lift, there's plenty of room onboard the Expanding Frontier... that's the name of our airship, and if we run out of room, we'll just toss out Gweeg... preferably from 2,000 feet up! (The Explorer's Club members laugh and Gweeg grumbles.) Ah, just pulling your chain, Gweeg! Seriously, though, we'd be happy to take you all back to Ninjago.

Sora: Great!

Reginald: As soon as our expedition comes to a successful conclusion!

Wyldfyre: What? How long will that take?

Reginald: The Explorer's Club has come here to Umbrasilvis following rumors of a deadly monster attacking the area. The Club sent its strongest members, but they also sent Gweeg.

(The Explorer's Club members laugh again and Gweeg grumbles again.)

Wyldfyre: Why do you keep picking on that Gweeg guy?

Reginald: Worry not! Gweeg knows it's all in good jest! He likes it! This is bonding! -

(Gweeg walks away.)

Sora: So, you're here to protect the town from a monster?

Reginald: Well, that's one way of putting it.

Rowan: Our expedition is here to capture the monster so that we can bring it back to Ninjago!

Wyldfyre: Why would you bring a deadly monster back to where you live?

Rowan: To put it on display and charge admission as a Wonder of the Merged Lands, of course!

Sora: That sounds dangerous.

Johnny: No reason to be afraid, little lady, or my name isn't Johnny Courageous! Which it is! I had it legally changed... pending!

Frak: Dad, something's seriously going wrong with Elemental Powers. We need to get back to the Monastery as soon as we can.

Reginald: Undoubtedly! And I'm sure the real Ninja are eager to see you again, Frak. There are probably swords that need polishing, floors to sweep, and so forth. Important Ninja work! But in order for the Explorer's Club to return in glory, we'll first need to capture our prey. Tonight, we hunt monsters!

Explorer's Club: Huzzah!

Reginald: (To Frak) Why don't you come along, son? Even a mediocre student such as yourself might, uh. learn something.

Frak: Uh...

(Sora sighs.)

(In another flashback, Jay heads to the Department of Reassignment while holding a game controller when he sees Prentis working at his station.)

Jay: Sub-Agent Prentis, shift's over. What are you doing still working?

Prentis: New Merge Refugees. Figuring out where to send them.

Jay: You know my policy, "When in doubt you might be, send 'em to the Land..." (He hides the game controller behind his back.)

Prentis: "Of Lee," yes. I do love obeying a good rhyme, and there is a pair of them, pre protocol, but are you sure that's where they're from?

Jay: I'm sure it's all fine. Probably. Just keep records.

(Prentis agrees and sends the two people through the portal. He then leaves, and Jay starts up a video game. Donovan shows up to hang out with him.)

Blake Donovan: Ugh, thought he'd never leave.

Jay: I know, right? Making us miss out on valuable gaming time.

Blake Donovan: You gonna ask out that cute Merlopian girl in HR? She's totally head over gills for you.

Jay: Uh, she's nice, but I don't wanna date anybody. I can't explain it. It's like the perfect person is already out there somewhere.

Blake Donovan: Out there?

Jay: Yeah, look, even though HR has shown me my employee records, and I've worked here my whole life, since I still don't remember anything, sometimes I feel like...

Blake Donovan: (pulls his sunglasses down a bit) Feel like what? You know, I just found this picture (shows the picture of Jay and himself together.) from our first permit inspection. The applicant tried to claim a P-3 exemption on a CE-5! Ha, ha, ha! Sorry you don't remember it, buddy, but trust me, we've had the best of times.

Jay: Yeah. I guess we really did. And still undefeated Galamorph champ, Jay Walker!

(The flashback ends, Jay, Kai, and Nya are search for clues in the Land of Madness. Nya steps on a part of the ground that makes an unusual sound.)

Nya: That doesn't sound natural to me.

(Nya steps again to hear metal clanking.)

Kai: Looks like someone dug something up.

Jay: Or buried something.

(Kai digs up the ground and finds a metal briefcase.)

Jay: That's the kind of suitcase Administration Agents use.

(Kai opens the briefcase and finds Jay's old ninja suit.)

Kai: Your old gi. This makes zero sense. How could this be here?

Nya: "Request to bury incriminating evidence. Stamped and approved." What does this mean?

Jay: It means the Administration knew I was a Ninja. They hid this here, where I woke up with no memory. We need to get there. There's someone I need to threaten with intense violence! I mean, have a discussion with.

(Meanwhile, in Umbrasilvis.)

Reginald: Right! Who's ready for a night of monster hunting?

Explorer's Club members: Huzzah!

Reginald: Tonight we'll be using these brand-new prototype capture devices. Behold!

(Reginald tests the device by shooting it at Gweeg, restraining him.)

Gweeg: Ah! (grumbles)

Reginald: Explorers! Split up and find the monster!

Explorer's Club: Huzzah!

Frak: Uh, is that a good idea? There's safety in numbers. Maybe you should go out in groups of two or three? Especially since you don't know the area and have no idea what kind of monster you're dealing with.

Reginald: (chuckles) My apologies, Explorers. My naive son means well. You forget, Frak, the one who catches the monster gets the all-important naming rights, huh? If we went in pairs, who would get to name the monster? It'd be madness! (The Explorer's Club begins their hunting expedition.) Oh, don't fret, son. Yes, your idea was awful, but I do like that you tried to participate... almost like a little mini grown-up. (runs off)

Wyldfyre: There's probably no monster, right?

Sora: Monster or not, these explorers will probably get lost or end up in a ditch somewhere. We have to help.

Wyldfyre: Why? I don't help buffoons. Personal policy.

(Riyu growls.)

Sora: Why? 'Cause helping is the Ninja way

Frak: And one of them is my dad.

Wyldfyre: Yeah, still not convinced.

Sora: Okay, but if there's a chance there really is a monster, someone's gotta protect this town.

Wyldfyre: (sighs) I guess it'd be cool to fight a monster, if there is one, which I doubt.

(The Explorer's Club keeps searching as distant cats screech and dogs barking.)

Sora: Why do you let your dad talk to you that way?

Frak: He's my dad.

Sora: Look, my parents were a mess too.

Frak: Then you get it.

Sora: I haven't spoken to them in years, probably never will again. Just 'cause someone's related to you doesn't mean you have to take their abuse.

Frak: It's called family loyalty, Sora. Some of us obviously have a harder time just walking away from family than you do.

(Sora looks disappointed.)

(Jay, Kai, and Nya arrive at the outside of the Administration.)

Nya: Can't imagine the same trick we used the last time will work again. Got a new plan for getting in there?

Jay: Yeah, I got a guy. Called him as soon as I came up with the idea.

Kai: What Guy?

Jay: Trust me, you'll love him.

(Two vehicles playing novelty horns arrive. Jay, Kai, and Nya cough and Rapton appears.)

Rapton: Jay! My guy! (laughing)

Jay: Rapton!

Kai and Nya: (confused) Rapton?

Rapton: Aw, Rapton's thrilled to see you, you old outlaw!

Jay: It's been too long! Jay hasn't seen you since we pulled that job in Shintaro!

Rapton: (scoffs) Pfft! Can you believe some clown paid so much for an ugly cursed skull? (cackles)

Kai: (laughs) Don't make me laugh.

Nya: Jay, you know this jerk?

Rapton: Jay, you know these jerks?

Jay: Oh, you've met?

Rapton: We came when you called, Jay. Whatcha need, brother?

Jay: Gotta get in there. Jay's got a plan, but Jay needs you to help Jay pull it off. How much will it cost?

Rapton: For Jay? Free of charge!

(The Freebooters grumble at this, but reluctantly agree.)

Jay and Rapton: Pow!

(Rapton and the Freebooters drive around in circles near the buildng, making lots of noise and blasting fireworks from their weapons. Ramirez appears with six other Administration Agents.)

Ramirez: Cease! You're breaking literally 15 different local ordinances!

Rapton: (cackles) We think ordinances are dumb! It's cool to break 'em! Yeah!

Ramirez: These hooligans and scofflaws won't comply.

(The Administration Agents start blasting them. The Freebooters get off their vehicles.)

Ramirez: Move in!

(The Freebooters attack the Administration Agents. Rapton whistles a signal and the Freebooters pretend to be hurt and defeated.)

Freebooters: Oh! Ow! Oh, no! (The Administration Agents are confused.)

Rapton: Oh, why did we dare tangle with the unstoppably dapper Administration? We must flee for our lives!

(The Freebooters retreat, getting in one of the vehicles and driving off.)

Ramirez: Good work, Agents! And the scofflaws left a vehicle behind. Have it confiscated!

(Two Master Mechs arrive to grab the vehicle left there and put it away in one of the Administration's storage rooms. Jay, Kai, and Nya come out from under the vehicle.)

Nya: I can't believe something Rapton did actually worked!

Kai: So where do we go now?

Jay: He'll probably be in his office soon.

Nya: Who's "he"?

Kai: I stopped understanding anything back when we were in a stomach.

(Sora, Frak, Wyldfyre, and Riyu keep walking and run into a Mutation Monster.)

Wyldfyre: A real monster!

(Frak and Sora put their masks on. Gweeg gets scared by the monster and drops his Lariat Laucher.)

Frak: (attempts to use his powers, but loses control) Come on, work! (the ground rumbles)

Sora: Don't let it get away it!

(Frak, Riyu, Sora, and Wyldfyre chase the Mutation Monster. Johnny Courageous is scared when he looks up and sees it.)

Wyldfyre: Courageous might wanna change his name.

Rowan: Ha ha!

(Seafoam Green and Rowan Rockspike blast the Lariat Launchers at the Mutation Monster, but they accidentally tie up each other.)

Wyldfyre: Work, powers, work! Aah, fine, whatever! H'yah!

(Wyldfyre kicks, Mutation Monsters grabs her leg and throws her at a building before running off. Reginald looks up to see the Mutation Monster about to attack.)

Reginald: Hup! (He restrains the Mutation Monster, but it breaks free.) Aah!

Frak: Dad! No!

(The Mutation Monster turns around and attacks Frak.)

Frak: (attempts to use his powers but fails) Oh, boy. (The Mutation Monster grabs his leg and throws him at a pole.) Oof!

(Riyu attacks the monster, but get badly hurt from its energy blasts.)

Sora: (attempts to use her powers.) Come on, powers, come on! (She creates a containment unit.)

(Wyldfyre dodges the monster's attacks. The monster jumps off of Riyu to attack, Sora manages to trap it before it hits anyone else. Riyu collapses, clearly in a weakened state.)

Sora and Wyldfyre: Riyu!

Reginald: Son, you tried to rescue me!

Frak: It's what I do, Dad--

Reginald: But you only got in my way. I would've trapped it myself if you hadn't shown up. Next time, think.

(Frak turns around to see Riyu, fainted.)

(Jay, Kai, and Nya sneak into the Administration, avoiding detection. They sneak into a cubicle, where Donovan soon enters, trying to turn on the lights.)

Blake Donovan: Lousy lights. Now I have to requisition repairs. (The door closes and Jay grabs him and turns on the lights. Donovan sees the ninja.) Jay! Where have you been? And what's with the hair and uniform? You went out on that equipment reclamation operation in Mysterium and never came back.

Jay: Shut it, Blake! I know it's an act. My so-called best friend with fake photos of us as young agents together.

Blake Donovan: Fake? What are you talking about?

Jay: I got receipts. I wanted to believe you weren't in on it... maybe you had your memories altered too, but no! A request to bury incriminating evidence form, and whose name is on that signature line? Tell me.

Blake Donovan: Agent Blake Donovan. So you finally figured it out? Took you long enough.

Jay: Who erased my memories? Was it you? Why? Tell me why!

Blake Donovan: You don't get it. There are things you'll never understand, Jay.

Jay: Is that so? Well, one thing I do understand is (pulls out one of his daggers.) what this does.

Blake Donovan: You wouldn't.

Jay: You wanna bet?

Blake Donovan: Uh, what if, um... What if I told you how you could unblock your memories.

Jay: How?

Blake Donovan: 33rd floor, Section A9. Where your memories were blocked. There could be a way to reverse it.

Jay: Why should I trust you?

Blake Donovan: Cause I'm the only hope you have of ever remembering your real life.

Jay: Take me there.

Blake Donovan: No problem, I'll just get you the access code, and-- Chair! (kicks the chair to hit Jay before using the tubes to escape.) So long, suckers!

Kai: Ugh! That jerk-head!

Nya: (gets on the computer) According to this, the 33rd floor, Section A9 is the Department of Vacation Simulation.

Kai: It's gotta be another lie. Why would a Vacation department have anything to do with Jay's memory loss?

Nya: Cause according to what I'm seeing here, they've got some brain-accessing tech up there.

Jay: We can't stay here. I'm sure Blake's reported us already. Agents will be on us in seconds.

(They hear agents at the door and hide.)

Roderick Allen: Freeze, enemies of bureaucracy! Uh, I swear, (The ninja sneak away.) if this is another one of Agent Donovan's pranks...

(Arin and Lloyd walk through the Whispering Wood. Euphrasia floats above them with her elemental power, until it stops working and she falls.)

Euphrasia: Aah! (Arin catches her.)

Arin: I was afraid that'd happen.

Euphrasia: I do not like elemental powers being all messed up like this. Thanks for the catch.

Arin: Any time.

Lloyd: We've been wandering for hours. The dragon said I'd find the Temple of the Green Ninja if I was meant to. (sighs) Maybe I'm not meant to. Maybe it's true, I'm too old, and it's too late to do the Ritual.

Arin: Maybe you should try meditating.

Lloyd: Who are you? Me?

Arin: It's what you'd tell me to do, isn't it?

Lloyd: Should follow my own advice. (He starts to meditate.)

Arin: Get comfortable. This might take a while.

Lloyd: (hears energy sparking) I... feel something already. (He opens the eyes to see sparks of uncontrolled energy.)

Euphrasia: Well, good thing I didn't get comfortable.

Lloyd: Wait... this isn't from my meditations. It's...

(A Mutation Monsters appears, Arin puts on his mask, and he and Euphrasia prepare to fight. Two Earth Monsters appear as well as Lloyd stands up and puts on his mask and draws his sword. An Acid Monster also appears. The Acid Monster shoots out Acid, which Arin and Euphrasia dodge. Lloyd fights Mutation Monster, while dodging tar from the Earth Monsters. The monsters surround the trio.)

(The Chief Loyalist stares at the hologram of the Weapon of Desolation in the hideout as another Loyalist reports to her.)

Loyalist #1: We have captured one of the Elemental Energy creatures formed by the Weapon of Desolation.

Chief Loyalist: You know what you need to do, then... and let no one stand in your way.

(They both leave the meeting.)
