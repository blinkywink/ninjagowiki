(At the Training plateau, the ninja stare at the Dragon Masters that have been turned into stone from the Blood Moon. Nya walks toward them.)

Wyldfyre: Ha! This isn't real, right? This is a prank!

(Nya touches the stoned Rontu.)

Nya: I'm sorry, but I really don't think-

Wyldfyre: (She shoves Lloyd and Kai away to stand in front of them.) The Dragon Masters are too powerful to have been turned to stone. I pulled this once. Remember, Kai?

Kai: Wyldfyre.

(Wyldfyre walks toward the Dragon Masters.)

Wyldfyre: Time to wake them from their slumber.

(The other ninja start walking down the stairs.)

Wyldfyre: Ooh! I'm gonna... (She tickles Egalt.) Goochee-goochee-goo! (Laughs.) Egalt totally hates this, don'tcha?

(She looks up, waiting for a response.)

Wyldfyre: (She tickles Egalt. again) Goochee-goochee-goo? Okay, plan B. (She steps on Egalt's foot with her injured leg.) Ow! (She drops her crutch and hops onto her other foot. Nya helps her balance.) All right. Stone. Bad idea. (Nya picks up Wyldfyre's crutch and hands it to her. Wyldfyre takes it and sighs.) Okay, so it's not a prank, but it'll wear off, right? Right?

Kai: I'm not sure.

Wyldfyre: Lloyd?

Lloyd: I don't know. And we don't have time to find out. The Blood Moon's already rising. Which means Ras can start the ritual to free the Forbidden Five, which will unleash great evil on our world like we've never known.

Arin: But- but that'll never happen. Kai, you've mastered Rising Dragon technique. All we gotta do is-

Kai: I haven't mastered Rising Dragon. Look, I did it once.

Nya: (While walking down steps with Wyldfyre.) We could practice it more. Then you could teach us.

Kai: But that's just it. I don't know how I did it. Not exactly.

Sora: It's the only fighting style that can defeat Shatterspin. (Lloyd starts to struggle.) Is there any other way to stop Ras' ritual?

Arin: There's gotta be something.

Lloyd: No, not now! I can't handle more visions! (Yells.)

(In his vision, he sees the Shadow Dojo.)

Cole: (In Lloyd's vision,) The Wyldness!

(He sees Bonzle in a box and Ras using her for the ritual.)

Bonzle: (In Lloyd's vision.) Help! Help!

(He sees the Forbidden Five inside an open portal, Ras ringing the Gong of Shattering, and Wolf warriors running towards him. Lloyd gasps with his hands and knees on the ground.)

Arin: Lloyd! Are you okay?

Lloyd: More visions. I still can't control when they come. But I think I know where we have to go. (He gets up.) Ras is going to perform his ritual in the Wyldness. We may not be ready, but we don't have a choice. (Wyldfyre looks at the stoned Dragon Masters.) It's time to face Ras, or everything will be lost.

(At the Monastery of Mysterium, Gandalaria, Zane and Cole continue fighting Administration Agents. Zane kicks an agent and throws another.)

Agent Allen: (In a Master Mech.) Get him!

(The Master Mechs start walking towards Zane when Cole stands in front of them with his Elemental Earth Mech.)

Cole: File this (The Elemental Earth Mech kicks Allen's Mech.) permit in triplicate!

(Cole's mech punches Allen's mech multiple times, causing the latter's mech to fall over. Zane pushes an agent on top of another, causing them both to fall.)

Zane: Really?

Cole: What? There aren't that many places I can use that joke.

(Allen's Master Mech gets up and points at Zane.)

Agent Allen: Focus on deactivating the equipment known as Zane.

(Two agents start blasting at Zane.)

Zane: That is really quite rude. Could equipment do this?

(Zane throws his shurikens, knocking out both of the agents.)

Agent Allen: I mean, depending on its programming, but point taken.

(Administration agents continue to blast at Gandalaria, who is using her crystal ball to fight back. She zaps two agents that are blasting her from the roof.)

Gandalaria: (As she zaps another agent.) Ya! Ha, and that old biddy Janet said my (She tosses the ball to her other hand.) crystal ball didn't work right.

(She zaps another agent, causing him to hit a pillar and fall to the ground afterwards. One of the agents she zapped earlier jumps down from the roof and tries to sneak up on her.)

Gandalaria: What does she know? Works great.

(The ball zaps the agent behind her. Gandalaria prepares to zap more agents when her crystal ball begins to beep and cycle through multiple colors. It stops at green and throws an onion onto the ground, causing the agents to stop running.)

Gandalaria: Well, mostly.

(The onion starts to emit a green gas that envelops the agents.)

Administration Agent: Oh, ah! The onion! Oh, my eyes are watering. Oh, the stench burns! Oh, it burns!

(Blasts fly over Gandalaria's head and she sends a zap from her crystal ball in the direction they came from. Cole uses his Elemental Earth Mech to blast and kick agents on the ground when three Master Mechs line up in front of him.)

Cole: Come at me!

(Two of the mechs charge toward him.)

Cole: I did ask for that.

(The mechs attack Cole's mech but he dodges both of them and hits one to the side, causing the other to chase after him.)

Agent Allen: Surrender the equipment now, or we will charge you with an additional violation. Statute 27a, (Cole continues fighting the Master Mechs.) subparagraph 9.

Zane: (While fighting and avoiding blasts.) That statute is just a fine. You've already ordered all of us terminated.

Agent Allen: Right. Fair point. Guess fines aren't really an issue if you're no longer alive. My bad,

(The Elemental Earth Mech shoves Allen, who is in a mech, to the ground while more agents rush into the Monastery. Gandalaria's crystal ball flashes green then red when she uses it to shoot one of the agents. The ball throws a coconut at the agent's head, knocking him out while Zane fights another agent.)

Gandalaria: Wow, coconuts. That's a first. (Agents start blasting at Gandalaria from the roof again.) Come on! We've got to keep them away from Bonzle!

(She zaps coconuts at the agents on the roof and one of them gets knocked out by them.)

(Underneath the Monastery, Jay continues to hold Bonzle at gunpoint, with Sprite by her side.)

Agent Walker: (Unenthusiastically.) I said, by the all-encompassing authority of the Administration, I hereby demand your surrender.

Bonzle: Um... no. Thank you.

Agent Walker: (Sighs.) You're an accomplice of the equipment called Zane, right? Rules say I have to put you under arrest.

Bonzle: You could put me under arrest, sure, but I get the sense your heart's not really in it.

Agent Walker: (He lowers his gun.) Yeah, you're probably right. Seems a little silly to me. Ah, this job is really rulesy, you know? I don't remember what I used to do before the Administration, but this is not the coolest career or anything.

(He chuckles and swaps the blaster in his hand for the handcuffs in his pocket.)

Agent Walker: Oh, well, I'm supposed to capture you, so can you just let me do that? Don't make it weird, hmm?

(Sprite flies over to him and makes circles around him.)

Agent Walker: Hey! (He tries to swat Sprite away.) Get outta here, nightlight!

(Bonzle looks behind and then looks back at Jay, who is running around trying to get away from Sprite.)

Agent Walker: Ah! Knock it off! (Sprite shows Jay a reflection of himself in his dojo suit.) What the-?

(He walks towards Sprite and touches it, activating his power of lightning.)

Agent Walker: Whoa! (He looks at the hand with lightning and drops his handcuffs. He waves his hand to try to get rid of his power.) How did you know I can do that? (His lightning goes out.) No one knows I can control lightning, and no one can find out.

(Sprite stops reflecting Jay and flies behind him. Jay tries to catch it.)

Agent Walker: Ah! (Bonzle leaves while Jay continues trying to catch Sprite.) Get away! You're making this so weird! (He jumps on top of Sprite.)

(Outside the Monastery, Jiro, still in clamps, roars and stamps his tail on the ground, causing an Administration agent to fall down. Cole blasts another agent near Jiro.)

Cole: (To Jiro.) We'll get you in a minute, buddy. (Cole uses his mech to grab a Master Mech's spear and shove it away.) This is so easy. It almost seems unfair. (The Master Mech gets up and stands next to the other mechs. They all turn to face Cole and the agents inside them press a button, causing the mechs to separate into two. Cole steps back.) I quipped too soon.

(The mechs, Cole and Zane run towards each other, with Cole's mech meeting another mech in the middle and wrestling with it. Zane freezes the leg of the smaller mech.)

Zane: That's one down!

(Allen's mechs charges toward Zane and attempts to use its spear on him on but Cole uses his Earth power to block its attack, knocking it to the ground.)

Cole: That's two!

(Gandalaria raises her now color-flashing crystal ball and uses it to zap three of the mechs.)

Gandalaria: (As each of the mechs fall down.) Three, four, five! I'm crushing it!

(Zane avoids a mech's attacks and climbs up to the pilot's seat while the agent inside tries to grab him. Zane grabs the pilot by the neck.)

Nindroid Administration Agent: Are you properly licensed to (Zane throws him out of the pilot seat.) drive?

(The agent crashes to the ground. The mech Zane froze earlier breaks free from the ice and the Nindroid agent attempts to shoot Zane with its blaster. Zane takes off his hood.)

Zane: Keep them away while I hack into their system.

Gandalaria: Wouldn't kill you to say please, but (She raises her crystal ball.) sure.

(Gandalaria zaps the agents with coconuts, causing one of them to drop their blaster and fall down.)

Zane: I just need a bit more time so I can set them all to self-destruct.

(Cole runs toward the gate of the monastery and stamps the ground while using his Earth power, causing a rock to form underneath him and push him into the air.)

Cole: You got it!

(Cole uses his Earth Punch and punches the ground with it, causing all the Master Mechs to fall over except the one that Zane is in. Allen looks outside his mech then crawls back in and tries to get it upright with the help of its spear. Zane pulls a wire out from behind his head and plugs it into the mech.)

Zane: Connecting to the system.

(Two agents start blasting the mech Zane is in and Allen gets his mech up before throwing its spear into the back of Zane's mech. Electricity surges while the agents continue blasting.)

Zane: Data breach is failing! Can't connect! Error! (The mech falls to the ground.) Powering down. (Zane powers down.)

(Cole kicks a mech to the ground and sees Allen approaches the powered down Zane.)

Cole: Zane? (Cole's Elemental Earth Mech runs toward Allen's Master Mech and the former punches the later before he can take out the spear in the mech Zane is in. A smaller mech rushes toward Cole.) Zane! (The smaller mech tries to grab hold of the Earth Mech's arm but Cole shoves it off and pulls out the spear.) Talk to me, buddy.

(Allen's Master Mech runs toward Cole and the latter uses the spear to attack him. The Master Mech tries to punch the Elemental Earth Mech, but Cole uses the spear to make the mech fall to the ground. Cole shouts as he kicks the Master Mech. He drops to spear and tries to tend to Zane.)

Cole: Are you okay in there? I think his circuits got fried. (He starts to wrestle with another mech.)

Gandalaria: (As her crystal ball's colors change.) He just needs a little reboot. What's the magic word?

Cole: Uh... please?

Gandalaria: Finally!

(Gandalaria zaps the mech Zane is in, knocking two mechs off it. The mech and Zane start up again.)

Zane: Huh?

Cole: Zane! Are you okay?

Zane: (He gets the mech up while agents blast at him.) Confirmed. All systems are stabilized. (He kicks an agent on the ground.) And decoding is reactivating. (Zane types into the mech's computer.) Data breach completed. Hack attack!

(Cole's Elemental Earth Mech punches a mech and another one kicks his fist and backflips to the ground. The agent inside looks at the alert going off in his mech and so do the other agents. Zane jumps out of the mech.)

Agent Allen: Oh, (He lowers his sunglasses.) this is gonna be a lotta paperwork. (The agents scream as they get ejected from the mechs.) That can't be great. (Allen screams as he gets ejected, flying over Jiro's head, causing the dragon to drop an agent it was holding in its mouth. The agent gets up and Jiro growls at him.)

Cole: (As Zane runs toward him.) Hurry, no matter how difficult it is, we can't give up until we find Bonzle, (Bonzle appears from behind the Monastery's gate.) and-

Bonzle: I found you!

Gandalaria: I found her

Zane: that went more smoothly than (Jiro lands behind Zane.) anticipated.

(They take off on Jiro.)

Gandalaria: The magical storehouse is near.

Bonzle: So what now? You're just gonna stuff me in a storage box till the Blood Moon passes?

Gandalaria: Exactly.

Bonzle: Oh. I was kidding.

Gandalaria: Magical Containment units are incredibly safe. Small, but safe. You're not claustrophobic, are you? If so, this would be a really bad time to find out.

(Bonzle shakes her head.)

Zane: Oh, no. Look up ahead. (He points to the Cloud Kingdom.)

Cole: The Cloud Kingdom! Are they following us? Stay low!

(Jiro lands and Cole, Zane, and Bonzle hop off him. Gandalaria looks around.)

Gandalaria: The magical storehouse is just beyond these trees.

(They hear footsteps. Gandalaria hops off Jiro and they all hide in the bushes. Two wolf warriors walk by.)

Zane: Wolf Mask Warriors. Could they know where we're headed?

(One of the warriors starts to walk the bushes they are hiding in but they cover themselves with leaves. The warriors walk away.)

Gandalaria: This is not how I expected my day to go, which is weird. I drink fortunetelling tea every morning. You'd think it'd give me a heads-up.

Cole: Jiro, stay hidden here. We'll be right back.

(Jiro growls and nods while Zane, Cole, Bonzle, and Gandalaria run out of the bushes.)

(At the Cloud Kingdom, Jordana is using her crystal ball.)

Jordana: Lord Ras, I- I've managed to find the ninja. Not sure I can hold the image for long.

Ras: The Blood Moon is here, and the ninja are too close. You have to stop them.

Jordana: Uh, from here? That's impossible. Can we send warriors?

Ras: Use the forbidden magic you've been studying, Jordana.

Jordana: But I'm a scientist. I've only just started learning the magic on the scrolls you've shown me, and-

Ras: I have no time for failure!

Jordana: (Sighs.) The ninja are far, but...

(She uses her magic while groaning.)

(Lloyd is driving the Destiny's Bounty.)

Lloyd: As far as I know, the Wyldness should be the land up ahead. (Thunder rumbles and lightning strikes twice, with one of them hitting the Bounty. The Bounty starts to fall) Look out! Hold on! (Lloyd tries to control the ship but lightning strikes the Bounty again.)

Arin: Sora, watch out!

(Arin pushes Sora away from a falling crate.)

Lloyd: This wind is unnatural. (Lightning strikes again and breaks off the Bounty's sails. Lloyd lets go of the steering wheel.) Hold on! (Arin gets blown away but grabs onto the side of the Destiny's Bounty. Lloyd tries to save him but Arin falls off.) No!

(Riyu dives after Arin and Lloyd falls backward and crashes into Nya, causing them both to fall off. The Bounty enters a canyon.)

Kai: This looks bad, this looks bad, this looks bad! (The Destiny's Bounty crashes into the sides of the canyon multiple times while Kai, Sora, and Wyldfyre scream.) Worse than I thought! Worse than I thought! Worse than I thought!

(The Bounty crash-lands on a rock in the middle of the canyon before the rock breaks. The Bounty falls and the three ninja float in the air and scream before crashing back down onto the precariously balancing ship on a rock.)

Kai: Are you kidding me?

(The Bounty creaks while balancing on the rock.)

(Sora and Kai gasp.)

Wyldfyre: Whee!

Sora: Shh! No sudden movements.

(Smaller rocks break off and fall into the chasm beneath them.)

(Nya and Lloyd scream while falling. They land on top of a large tree.)

Lloyd: I can't believe it. We're safe. (They fall from the top of the tree to one of its branches.) Okay, now we're sa- (They fall onto a lower branch.) Now we're- (They fall yet again.) Now we're- (They continue falling.)

(Arin regains consciousness and notices he is falling. He starts to scream. Riyu dives down and catches him.)

Arin: You're flying! (Riyu growls excitedly, but starts to panic as they approach the ground.) Or sort of flying. (He screams again as Riyu tries to break their fall.)

(They manage to land without major injuries.)

Arin: We're alive. You saved me! (He hugs Riyu, and Riyu licks him.)

(Back at the Bounty, Sora, Kai and Wyldfyre are still recovering from the crash.)

Kai: Is everyone okay? (Sora nods. Wyldfyre walks to the edge of the ship.) What's down there?

Wyldfyre: Oh, just nothingness. Instant doom.

(Sora and Kai start to carefully walk on the bounty. Sora looks up the cliff.)

Sora: We need to get up to the top. (She and Kai look towards the folded up mech on the deck of the Bounty.)

Kai: My Climber Mech! Day saved! (He jumps slightly to strike a pose.) Boom!

(The ship tilts slightly before becoming balancing again.)

Sora: (worriedly) Please don't say boom.

Kai: (Chuckles.) Oops!

Sora: The Climber Mech is a great idea, but we can't get to it without tipping the ship. We'd for sure fall into all that…(Groans.)

Wyldfyre: Nothingness and instant doom.

Sora: Yeah, that. (She pauses to think.) If we jump together in exactly the right place, I think we can tilt the ship enough in the right direction for Kai to get to the mech.

Kai: I'm ready to try it.

Sora: Okay, on the count of three, we all jump, land hard, the ship tilts up. Then, Kai, you get to the mech as fast as you can. Got it?

Kai and Wyldfyre: Got it!

Sora: (She backs up to get into position.) One. Two. Three!

(Sora and Wyldfyre jump, and the Bounty starts to tilt. Kai makes his way to the Climber Mech by jumping on falling storage crates.)

Kai: Piece of cake!

(The Bounty tilts back the other way, and starts to fall.)

Sora: Faster!

Kai: Almost- (He is hit by a crate and falls towards the mech.)

Sora: We're going over! (She grabs onto Wyldfyre.)

(The Bounty falls down, and Sora and Wyldfyre scream.)

Kai: Come on! Come on! Come on! (He finally gets into the mech's cockpit and activates it.) Yes!

(The Climber Mech unfolds and Kai uses one of its hooks to cling onto the cliff. He is then able to grab Sora, who holds onto Wyldfyre.)

(The Bounty is seen falling into the chasm. It makes a faint thud, and then a louder crash as it finally hits the bottom. The ninja look down with sad expressions.)

Sora: Sorry, Bounty. You were a real good ship. But onward-

Kai: And upward. (He starts to scale the cliff using the mech.)

(Cole, Zane, Bonzle and Gandalaria arrive at a small mushroom-shaped building.)

Zane: This is the magical storage house?

Cole: It's cute, but it doesn't look big enough for all of us, or even one of us.

Gandalaria: You misunderstand magic. (She walks to the door and tries to open it, but is unable to.) Gah! Oh. It's stuck. (She tries again, to no avail.) (She takes out an onion from her robe and hands it to Zane.) Ah! Hold this. Let me try an opening spell. (She takes out her crystal ball.) And… (The crystal ball flashes blue and fires a bolt of energy at the lock, only for it to bounce back and hit the ball away.) Ouch! Oh, for crying out loud in the morning!

(The group hears footsteps approaching.)

Cole: Shh! Listen! They're almost here!

(Ganadalaria picks up her crystal ball and tries again.)

Gandalaria: (whispering) This should do it. (The crystal ball flashes blue again and fires another energy beam at the lock. The door finally opens.) Ha! Take that, Janet! Hurry! Let's go!

(The group enters the storehouse. Gandalaria's hat falls off upon hitting the doorframe, but she picks it up before closing the door behind her.)

(A group of four Wolf Mask Warriors is seen passing the storehouse.)

(Inside the magical storehouse, the ninja notice it to be bigger than it appears on the outside.)

Cole: Okay. I do misunderstand magic.

Zane: We did not anticipate the Wolf Mask Warriors would be here. We must hide Bonzle quickly, before we are discovered.

(Gandalaria approaches a shelf filled with books and other objects.)

Cole: What is this place?

Gandalaria: Hmm, this storage house is where we mystical folks store most of our valuable magical items. (She starts examining the shelf.)

Zane: (Looking at another shelf.) But nothing is alphabetized. This is highly inefficient.

Gandalaria: We have a system! Or we used to, probably. (She looks around.) Aha! There it is! (She walks to a large wooden box on one of the shelves.) Home sweet protective container.

Bonzle: Is this bigger on the inside, too?

Gandalaria: Yes. Yeah. No. Sorry! Help me get this out.

(Cole walks beside her, and the two of them lift the box out of the shelf. A vase falls down and breaks beside them, and faint screaming can be heard.)

Cole: That looked expensive.

Gandalaria: And cursed. But we have more pressing concerns here.

(The two carry the box to the center of the room and place it down on a circular carpet.)

Gandalaria: This is my second most powerful creation. (She points at Bonzle.) After you, of course, my dear. You will be perfectly safe in here. Once I have sealed it, no one will be able to open it. Not even this Lord Ras you keep telling me about.

Bonzle: Do I really have to?

Cole: Just until the Blood Moon passes, which hopefully won't be much longer.

Zane: Ras' army is hunting for us. I regret we have no other options, Bonzle.

Gandalaria: You'll be fine, dear. (She opens the storage container.)

Bonzle: Okay. (She hops into the box.) Oh, this is actually kinda cozy.

Gandalaria: I'm going to seal you in now and start the spell. (She pushes Bonzle's head down.) You ready?

Bonzle: (sighs) As ready as I'll ever be.

Zane: I do not believe in wishing people good luck, but I hope random probability outcomes align to your benefit. (He and Gandalaria close the container.)

Gandalaria: (She clears her throat and begins chanting an incantation before abruptly stopping.) Nope. (She starts chanting again, only to stop almost immediately.) Can you not look at me for a sec? I get nervous under pressure.

(Zane and Cole turn away, and Zane starts whistling a small tune.)

(Gandalaria starts the incantation properly this time, and the storage container starts glowing blue. Cole and Zane turn around to look.)

Cole: Wow. She really knows what she's doing.

(Gandalaria continues the incantation, and the container starts floating before slightly shaking and glowing red.)

Cole: Whoa!

Gandalaria: Wait, no.

Cole: What, no?

Gandalaria: I'm not doing that.

Zane: Then who is?

Gandalaria: I don't know!

Bonzle: What's happening? Let me out, let me out!

(Gandalaria pulls out her crystal ball and zaps the container, but nothing happens.)

Cole: Get her out of there!

Gandalaria: I'm trying! (She zaps the container again, but it does nothing.)

(Red symbols appear on the edges of the container.)

Zane: Look! Runes are appearing. It is Theroxian, the forbidden language.

Cole: Come on! (He tries to attack the container with Spinjitzu, but is stopped by the sudden appearance of a force field.) What the-

(Zane punches the force field, but it has no effect.)

(Bonzle yells)

(The container starts floating away, and Jordana reveals herself.)

Jordana: Thank you, ninja.

Gandalaria: Who are you? What have you done?

Cole: Never seen her before in my life.

Jordana: (angrily) My name is Jordana! Ugh! You ninja, especially the one you call Sora, are my sworn enemies.

Cole: Still not ringing a bell.

Jordana: (She yells.) I knew I could trap you with my old Imperium tech, but I wasn't sure how I'd contain the spell here. (She moves the storage container with Bonzle in it beside her with her magic.) I never thought you'd put her in a cage yourselves. I might just be learning Theroxian magic, but corrupting your paltry magic, Sorceress, has proven to be quite simple. Lord Ras will be very pleased with me. And now (She starts walking out with the storage container hovering beside her.) we have everything we need to begin the Ritual of the Blood Moon.

(Bonzle yells and tries to get out of the storage container to no avail.)

Gandalaria: Bonzle!

Jordana: You'll have plenty of time to think about your failures trapped in this stinky old mushroom building.

Gandalaria: It's a mystical storehouse!

Jordana: (She kicks the door open.) Don't care. (She leaves.)

Cole: We gotta get outta here and save her! (He tries to escape with Spinjitzu, but fails.)

(Elsewhere, Lloyd and Nya finally land on the ground.)

Nya: Whoa. That storm was so disorienting. I have no idea where we are, or where the others could've landed.

Lloyd: Well, I'm thinking we head towards that. (He points to the Blood Moon.)

(Meanwhile, Arin and Riyu are walking through the forest. Riyu growls at Arin.)

Arin: No, I don't know if any of the other ninja survived the fall, Riyu. But even if we're the only two ninja left, it's up to us to save the world.

(They continue walking in the direction of the Blood Moon.)
