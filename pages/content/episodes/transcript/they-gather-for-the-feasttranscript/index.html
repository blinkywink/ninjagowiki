(In a flashback, Arin practices outside his home with a mop dressed up as Wu.)

Past Arin: (impersonating Wu) Focus your Spinjitzu, Arin. (normal voice) Yes, Master Wu.

(Arin spins, attempting Spinjitzu, but is grabbed by the hood and pulled to the ground by a Bully, who stands over Arin with a friend.)

Past Bully 1: Aw, did that hurt, "ninja nerd"? I thought ninja are supposed to be tough. Oh, wait, maybe it's because you're not a ninja, loser.

(Arin backs up against a wall.)

Past Arin: Careful! I-I'm fully trained... well, partially fully trained, but my mom says I'm getting way good.

Past Bully 1: (laughs) Who trained you? Mr. Mop Master here?

(He pushes the mop dressed as Wu over. The bully's friend lays behind Arin, and the bully pushes Arin over him.)

Past Bully 1: Who cares about ninja anyway? Just a bunch of babies in pajamas.

(Frak appears from above the wall.)

Past Frak: You better not be making fun of the ninja. (He jumps down.)

Past Bully 1: This has nothing to do with you, Snake Boy.

Past Frak: The ninja don't sit back when someone's getting bullied, and neither do I.

(The bully runs away. Frak hisses at his friend, causing him to flee as well.)

Past Arin: I probably could've handled them, but thanks for the help. (He stands up.) I'm Arin, by the way.

Past Frak: Name's Frak. (He picks up the Master Wu mop.) You, uh, make that yourself?

Past Arin: Yeah. Kind of dorky, right?

Past Frak: Better than mine. I made it outta my uncle's shed skin, which is kind of gross, I guess, but I love Master Wu and the ninja.

Past Arin: Oh, no way!

Past Frak: Ninja, go! (He spins.)

Past Arin: Lloyd is my favorite, though I did get a Zane haircut one time.

Past Frak: I've always been a Cole guy. (He imitates a blast-off.) My dream is to get an Elemental Power, just like the ninja.

Past Arin: (imitates smashing blow.) Oh, wouldn't that be the best? I'm trying to learn Spinjitzu on my own, and it's going great, but also it's going awful. My parents keep telling me it's only a matter of time before I'll do a perfect Spinjitzu tornado.

Past Frak: (happy gasp) I'll practice with you.

(The two start spinning, attempting Spinjitzu together.)

(In the City of Temples, Arin and Riyu stand with their hands up, surrounded by Security Bots. Frak stands outside the circle.)

Frak: Arin, what are you doing here?

(One of the bots puts Arin in handcuffs.)

Arin: Right now, being hauled off by Security Bots.

Frak: (to a Security Bot) I'm here with my master and these are my guests.

(The Security Bots talk amongst themselves, before they remove the handcuffs from Arin and walk away. Arin walks to Frak.)

Arin: You're competing in the Tournament of the Sources?

Frak: That's right. You're looking at the Elemental Master of Quake!

(Frak uses his powers, causing the ground to shake and Arin to fall over. Frak stops using his powers.)

Arin: You have Elemental Powers now? How? Your mom or dad weren't masters, were they?

Frak: Nope, I was just a regular kid. But then the Merge happened, and boom, I could cause things to shake. The first time was so freaky. I burst a sewer pipe, which led to a kind of involved cleanup process that's probably best we don't talk about.

(Riyu makes amused noises.)

Frak: But then I found a master who trained me to use my powers properly. Why are you here?

Arin: You're not gonna believe this, but--

(Sora appears, accompanied by Lloyd, Nya, Wyldfyre, Cole, Zane, Roby, and Bleckt.)

Sora: Arin, Riyu, there you are. We were so worried.

Frak: Uh, you're here with the ninja?

Arin: I told you you wouldn't believe it. Hey, everyone. This is my old friend, Frak.

Sora: You mean Frak Frak? Like the guy that taught you how to make pies?

Cole: If that's true, we have to thank you. I'm normally a cake guy, but even I have to admit, Arin's pies are the best.

Frak: The Cole likes my pie recipes? (laughs)

Arin: It's okay, Frak. You wanna come hang out with us?

Frak: Oh, are you serious? I... (Frak's phone beeps, he takes it out and reads an alert.) Oh, wait, my master just sent a training alert. He does not like anyone to be late. I gotta run. I'll find you later, Arin. It's so amazing to meet you all.

Nya: Pleasure to meet you too, Frak.

Frak: Nya knows my name. (He gives a stunned shiver, then waves and runs away.)

Lloyd: Arin, we're now officially invited to the games, so we're getting a tour. This is Roby, Master of Ceremonies, and his uncle Bleckt, the Master of Ledgering. Did I get that right?

Bleckt: Welcome to the--

Roby: Try Minister of Boring. Ha, I kid, we're family. He loves it, now come on.

(The ninja are following Bleckt and Roby throughout the City of Temples.)

Bleckt: Behold, Foundation Street, the traditional center of the city.

(The ninja observe others on the street.)

Lloyd: (to Arin) This tour is a chance to keep our eye out for anyone suspicious. Remember the real reason we're here, to find out who killed the Matriarch.

Arin: On it, using my keen detective skills.

Bleckt: Observe the arrangement of architectural feature as the--

Roby: Arrangement of blah, blah, blah. Sorry, Uncle, but is your plan to put the ninja to sleep?

Bleckt: The City of Temples must be treated with reverent respect.

Roby: Forget the old rules, Uncs. It's a new era. The realms are merged. These are the first games where I'm in charge and tradition is out. It's time for disruption. Ninja, saddle up. I'm about to show you the tightest thing in the city.

(Wyldfyre gives a look of excitement. The group stops in front of a wall with a seven-headed dragon etched onto it.)

Roby: Check it, seven-headed dragon! (making animated noises) Sweet, right?

Wyldfyre: Yes, all the yeses!

(Roby winks at Wyldfyre, making her giggle.)

Roby: See, she gets it. She's clearly the smartest one.

Zane: I have no record of a seven-headed dragon in the history of any realm with which I am acquainted.

Roby: That's 'cause it's not real. It's interpretive art representing the seven Source Dragons of myth, if you believe in that. But it's also awesome, and it's been here like, a million years, so it's almost as old as Uncle Bleckt.

(Roby cackles, making Wyldfyre laugh as well.)

Roby: Anyhow, like the fairy tale goes, all Elemental Powers come from the Source Dragons.

Bleckt: It is not a fairy tale, Roby.

Roby: Whatever. The games were created forever ago to celebrate the gifts of the "Source Dragons." So Elemental Masters are called to compete. It's pretty much a celebration of your awesomeness.

Cole: What does the winner get?

Roby: Woah, we gotta talk rules first. When someone loses a match, they give their power to this Elemental Cup, and with it I give their powers to the winner, who then has both powers. Am I right? This goes on as each competitor that wins gains more and more powers to compete with. The winner ends up with everyone's Elemental Powers.

Bleckt: The champion then returns the Elements to their rightful Masters in a victory ceremony that commemorates the Source Dragons allowing mortals to be given Elemental Powers.

Roby: Victory ceremonies are boring. Ours will be an extreme victory extravaganza.

Nya: Uh, doesn't that seem super risky?

Arin: Yeah, what if the winner is evil? They'd have everyone's powers.

Sora: They could go rogue and take over the world or something.

Roby: Stop stressing. Nothing like that's ever happened, and it couldn't. The Elemental Cup's like a safety switch. Someone tries something stupid, I just pull the powers back into the cup, and only the Game Master, me, knows the secrets of how to use this cup.

(Zant-Tanz appears.)

Zant-Tanz: Lloyd, Arin, what a pleasure it is to see you old chums.

Lloyd: Zant-Tanz? No way! (to the others) This is the guy Arin and I told you about, leader of the Mother Garden. (to Zant-Taz) You're an Elemental Master? Why didn't you tell us?

Zant-Tanz: I didn't even know until I got invited to this delightful convivial convention.

Arin: What's your power?

Zant-Tanz: Plants. Witness a whimsical demonstration.

(Zant-Tanz begins to use his power. The air whooshes, and two massive vines emerge from the stone ground, connecting in an arch. The vines quickly grow flowers which begin to drop petals.)

Zant-Tanz: (laughs) Flower shower.

Roby: Cool.

Lloyd: Huh, cool.

Zant-Tanz: Oh, I am such a sucker for whimsey.

Sora: Wow.

Nya: And you didn't know you were an Elemental Master?

Zant-Tanz: I just thought I was very good at gardening. And I have splendid news for your tummies. I'll be bringing my extra sweet Mucoid Berries to tonight's banquet.

Sora: Banquet?

Roby: Yep. Pre-tournament festivities including dining and delights, and you're invited of course. You'll meet some of your competitors.

(At night, the ninja gather outside the banquet hall. They open the door to find their fellow Elemental Masters and a full buffet.)

Cole: (eyeing the pizza) Ooh. Oh.

(Cole rushes in to get food, the others follow.)

Roby: Ah, here's my favorite team. Of course, don't tell anyone else I said that. There's a full buffet, but also take a menu. You can order anything your heart desires. Come on, let me show you around this epic banquet hall.

(Nya notices Tox and Mr. Pale.)

Nya: Tox, is that you? Like the hair. Mr. Pale, so good to see you, uh, not see you. (laughs)

(Sora is pulled to the side by Jordana.)

Sora: (gasps) You! You disappeared after the Blood Moon. Where'd you--

Jordana: (She grunts and grabs Sora.) Help me, Ana.

Sora: Huh?

(Jordana unhands Sora. Her eyes quickly flash red, and she cracks her neck.)

Sora: Uh, Jordana?

(Nokt and Cinder appear.)

Nokt: I was wondering where you got off to, Jordana.

"Jordana": I was never far, Nokt.

Cinder: (to Sora) Look who we have here.

(Wyldfyre steps in front of Sora and snarls.)

Cinder: You brought the whole gang? Hey, didn't you have two Red Ninjas in your group? I can't remember.

Wyldfyre: (She charges her power) This is for Kai. Eat fire!

(Wyldfyre attempts to blast Cinder but is held back by a Security Bot. Roby runs in between Wyldfyre and Cinder.)

Roby: Hold up, hold up, hold up. No fighting outside the arena. Big-time rule.

Wyldfyre: But I gotta mess this guy up.

Roby: Love that energy. Love it. But save it for the games, or else, sad face, you'll be expelled. Don't blame me. Just how it is.

Nya: Roby's right. Don't let your anger get the best of you. We'll have a chance to beat them.

Wyldfyre: (sighs) Okay. I won't fight. Want to, but won't.

(Roby, Nokt, Cinder, and "Jordana" walk away.)

Arin: What's wrong, Sora?

Sora: Not sure. Something strange just happened.

(Lloyd attempts to grab a pie, but it gets taken by another master.)

Lloyd: Wha-- Hey, you're a devonian, right? I've never actually...

(The devonian master walks away.)

Lloyd: Nice to meet you, too.

(Lloyd accidentally knocks a bottle of water to the floor. He leans down to pick it up, only to discover it has rolled over to Ras.)

Lloyd: Ah!

Ras: Did I startle you, Lloyd?

Lloyd: Ras. If you've come to fight...

Ras: We've all come to fight. My team's here to win the tournament and honor the Source Dragons.

(A bell rings.)

Roby: Ting-ting, ding-ding, ding-ding, ting,-ting. Hi, hi. Attention, masters. One night before the New Tournament of the Sources, I am reminded of what the Cragling poet, Rockworth, once said. (Roby makes low, growling rock noises.)

(Suddenly, the door to the banquet is thrown open by Zeatrix.)

Zeatrix: I have been betrayed for the last time.

(The guests gasp.)

Lloyd: Empress Beatrix?

(Zeatrix approaches the ninja.)

Lloyd: We've beaten you once, Empress, and we'll do it again.

Zeatrix: Empress? (chuckles) So you must be the ninja who dethroned my sister. You had no right. I spent years plotting my revenge on my twin, and you stole that from me.

Lloyd: Twin? You're--

Roby: (He pushes past Lloyd.) Zeatrix! Yeah, don't love you storming in. All your sloppy drama's messing up my big speech.

Zeatrix: A banquet to which the Elemental Master of Shockwave was not invited is a grave act of betrayal I will not forgive.

Roby: Ugh. Everything with you is grave act of betrayal this, a pox upon your house that. I'm sure not inviting you was just an oversight, right, Uncle Bleckt?

Zeatrix: Oversight? I still haven't been assigned quarters like every other competitor, and now I'm excluded from the welcome banquet? I know what you're doing. You're sidelining me because you know I'm destined to destroy every single one of these so-called masters in the games.

Elemental Master 1: (laughs) All right.

Elemental Master 2: (laughs) Yeah, right.

Ras: Your sister didn't find you so difficult to defeat.

Wyldfyre: Ras! (snarls)

Zeatrix: What are you doing here? You're no Elemental Master.

(Nokt, Cinder, and "Jordana" gather around Ras.)

Ras: I command Elemental Masters.

Zeatrix: I know you conspired with my sister to take my father's life. I look forward to you meeting the same fate.

(Ras growls.)

Zeatrix: Roby, any more oversights, and you will pay, personally.

(Zeatrix walks away.)

Roby: Zeatrix, Elemental Master of Buzzkill, am I right? Dude, my speech was gonna be epic. But, the moment's gone. Everyone eat, eat. Game's tomorrow, whatever.

(Ras walks away.)

(The ninja enter their quarters.)

Nya: I've been to some awkward dinners, like when Jay ate the host's fern thinking it was a side salad. Or when Jay asked for sushi from a fish waiter in the undersea kingdom of Merlopia. Actually, being with Jay led me to so many awkward dinners. But tonight was in the top ten.

Lloyd: I thought I was seeing Beatrix in my visions this whole time. But was it Zeatrix? What could it mean?

Sora: Maybe it means she's the one who killed the Matriarch and took her horns.

Arin: What makes you think that?

Sora: We know the horns are a source of power. What if she plans to use them somehow to win the games? She seemed pretty confident she'd win, like she knows something that the rest of us don't.

Arin: It's gotta be Ras. He's attacked the Matriarch before, and he captured a Source Dragon.

Cole: And Ras did the whole Blood Moon thing because he wanted to bring back the Forbidden Five. What if it was for these games? And when he didn't get all five, maybe he needed the horns as a backup plan.

Nya: The winner does get everyone's Elemental Powers. That kind of thing smells like a Ras plan and ties it all to the Source Dragons.

(Wyldfyre enters.)

Wyldfyre: Crazy dinner. But it's obvious who the dragon killer is.

Arin: Right, Ras.

Sora: Or Zeatrix.

Wyldfyre: What? No, it's definitely Bleckt.

Nya: The stuffy ledger guy?

Wyldfyre: You've seen how he treats the amazing Roby. Jealous much?

Arin: "The amazing Roby"?

Zane: All of these theories are worth considering, but we do not have enough data to reach a conclusion.

Lloyd: Zane's right, we need to know more. Rest up. The game's start tomorrow.

(Zane turns off the lights and the ninja get into their beds. Lloyd sighs to himself.)

(Late in the night, a cloaked intruder enters the ninja's quarters through a window. He walks to Lloyd's bed, pulling out a weapon and preparing to stab Lloyd with it. Riyu's eyes twitch open, and he roars out upon seeing the intruder. The others wake up, and Lloyd moves out of the way of the weapon just in time.)

Nya: What's going on?

(Zane inspects the intruder.)

Zane: It is an armed intruder. Detecting high levels of hostility.

Wyldfyre: (charges her power) You tried to ninja into a room full of ninja? Not smart, buddy.

(Riyu roars. The ninja charge their powers as corner the intruder. They fire at the same time, and the intruder jumps, causing their powers to collide and knocking all of them down. The intruder leaps out of a window, with the ninja following behind. As they land on a building, Arin latches onto the intruder's left arm with his Grapple Gun.)

Arin: No one sneaks into-- (The intruder flings Arin using his grappling hook.) Hey!

(Cole and Zane continue chasing the intruder, charging with Spinjitzu only for the intruder to disappear into smoke. They reappear on the ledge of a building, and Riyu charges at them. They dodge Riyu, causing the dragon to fall off the building. Lloyd uses Spinjitzu to get up to the ledge.)

Lloyd: I was actually getting a good night's sleep for a change. Thanks for ruining that.

(Lloyd kicks the intruder, who jumps off the building and onto another. Lloyd uses Spinjitzu to follow and cut them off.)

Lloyd: Who are you? Did Ras send you?

(The intruder leaps to another building once again, with Lloyd following. The intruder lands on a building and throws two shurikens at Lloyd, both of which he dodges. The intruder attacks Lloyd with the same weapon they attacked him in his sleep with, but Lloyd grabs and throws them against a wall.)

Lloyd: Oh, we're not done talking.

(Lloyd launches himself at the intruder, grabbing on and forcing them both to the ground, where their fall is broken by a box. The two have a fist-fight on the ground. Lloyd pulls the intruder into the air then blasts them to the ground with his powers, knocking them down. The sound wakes up the other residents of the city, and Security Droids surround Lloyd and the intruder.)

Security Droid: Stop. You are disturbing the peace. Conflict is only allowed during the games.

(A crowd forms around them.)

Zant-Tanz: Lloyd, is that you? What's all this hubbub and commotion.

Lloyd: Whoever this is just tried to give me an early wake-up call with this.

(The crowd gasps. The other ninja arrive.)

Zane: It is true. We are all witnesses.

Cole: Who is it, Lloyd?

Lloyd: Let's find out.

(Lloyd removes their mask, revealing a bot.)

Nya: A bot?

(The bot pulses and crackles with electricity before falling to the ground, destroyed.)

Roby: That's definitely not one of our security bots, I can tell you that.

Lloyd: Whatever it is, I have a pretty good guess about who sent it.

(A second, identical bot is thrown on top of first one. Ras, "Jordana", and Cinder enter.)

Ras: Do you? Maybe it's the same person who sent one after me.

(A third bot is thrown onto the other two from a building. Lloyd looks up to see the Devonian master he had attempted to speak with before.)

Zane: And a third attacker, it appears. (He analyzes the bots.) The damage is too extensive to access its mechanical memory.

Arin: Then how can we know who sent them?

(Nya examines the weapon the bot attempted to use on Lloyd.)

Nya: Tox, you're the Elemental Master of Poison. What do you make of this?

Tox: (She examines the weapon.) Hmm, no question. That's a rare Gilba thorn from the Sevateam plant.

Lloyd: Never heard of it. Where are they from?

Tox: Only one place in the Merged Lands, the Mother Garden.

Roby: But the Mother Garden's protected. You can't take plants outta there without the permission of its leader.

(They all look at Zant-Tanz.)

Zant-Tanz: Well, yes, I am the general of the Mucoid Warriors and supreme leader of the Mother Garden, but I assure you, I've got nothing to do with this. Although, Tox is correct about the origins of the Gelba thorns. They can only come from my home.

(The crowd gasps and begins to back away from Zant-Tanz. Two Security Bots stay at his side.))

Lloyd: Zant-Tanz is our friend. I'm sure he didn't do this.

Ras: He is not my friend.

Roby: The thorns prove it could only be him. And attacking fellow competitors is penalized with immediate exile.

Lloyd: I'm telling you, there's no way Zant-Tanz--

Ras: I want him gone.

(The crowd mutters in agreement with Ras.)

Roby: Looks like you're not the only one.

Lloyd: But it's not fair.

Zant-Tanz: Lloyd, it's okay. I'm innocent. But if my leaving would comfort people, I will exile myself voluntarily. I do have some rock-stacking to do at home. However, be warned, I did not do this. Whoever sent these attacker bots is still among you.

Roby: Sure, sure, whatever. (snaps his fingers)

(Zant-Tanz turns and walks away, two Security Bots following him.)

Roby: Enough excitement for one night. Get some rest, everyone. First competitions are tomorrow!

(The crowd disperses. In the movement, Nya catches sight of Jay.)

Nya: Huh?

(He turns and walks away.)

Nya: Jay? (She runs after him.) Jay? Jay!

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
