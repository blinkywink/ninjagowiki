(At the Spectral Lands, Thunderfang continues to empower himself.)

Thunderfang: Source Dragons! You refuse me my rightful place among you, but Thunderfang will not be denied! (Several spines grow on his back.) I will force myself into your ranks, and you will all pay for your rejection of me! (He roars while Drix, Rox and Nokt watch.)

(In the chamber beneath the Well of the Lost, Arin and Lloyd converse as the tablets bearing the symbols of the Source Dragons begin to unload.)

Lloyd: (He groans.) We gotta get the Unbidden Storm working again.

Arin: It's impossible, that thing's been spinning for centuries. I told you I'd find a way to mess this up.

Lloyd: I'm the one who caused the cave-in, Arin, not you, and remember, Ninja never quit.

Arin: I'm not a Ninja anymore.

Lloyd: (He takes the Unbidden Storm.) There's gotta be a way to activate this thing again.

(In the Cave of Bells (Mysterium), Kai, Nya, and the coconut copy look at the ceiling.)

Nya: This plan only works with two halves of the dragon combining where the Unbidden Storm is. Let's hope everyone else did their part.

Kai: So we just sit here and wait to see if Thunderfang destroys everything? (He sighs.) Don't like this one bit.

(The mist from the Spectral Lands appears in the cave, surprising them.)

(In Djinjago, Kur enters the Cave of Bells, where Riyu, who was flying around the Focus crystals on the ceiling and soon lands, Wyldfyre, Sora, and Frak are.)

Sora: I guess if these crystals are glowing, our half of the Arc Dragon is still activated? Which is good, right?

Frak: This is like flying blind. We have no way of knowing if the other teams succeeded.

(The mist from the Spectral Lands appears in the cave, and everyone sees Kur floating.)

Sora: Kur? (Frak uses his Reveal Blade to make the Spectral Dragonians visible.) Spectral Dragonians? Here?

Frak: This doesn't bode well for the overall plan.

Wyldfyre: (She summons Heat.) Well, fighting is more fun than planning. (She shoots fireballs on Kur and the Spectral Dragonians.)

(Zarkt appears floating in the Cave of Bells (Mysterium) and faces Kai and Nya.)

Kai: I thought this was too easy.

Nya: We had to feed birds who were secretly bells. (Kai uses his Reveal Blade to make the Spectral Dragonians visible.) How is any of this easy?

Kai: (He puts on his mask.) I mean, I need someone to fight.

(Zarkt orders the Spectral Dragonians to attack them, but Kai uses his Reveal Blade to destroy them while Nya throws Water at them.)

Coconut copy: The problem with being a backup copy of the Sorceress, can't do magic. I'm kind of useless in a fight. Ooh, wait! (Kai destroys another Spectral Dragonian.) I've got it! (A Spectral Dragonian flies past her.) (To Nya.) Throw me!

Nya: You sure?

(A Spectral Dragonian flies past them.)

Coconut copy: It takes a lot to hurt a coconut. Do it!

(Nya uses her Water to launch the coconut copy at high speed.)

Coconut copy: Yee-haw! Whoo-hoo-hoo! (She hits three Spectral Dragonians...) Yes! (...then another...) Look, Ma, no hands! (..then more and lands on Nya's hand.) Whoo-hoo! This is the most exciting thing that's ever happened to this version of me!

Nya: Y'ah! H'yah! H'ah! (She throws water at the Spectral Dragonians.)

Kai: H'yah! (He destroys another with his Reveal Blade.) Thunderfang really did a number on you all. Hope this frees your souls! H'yah! (He destroys another.)

(Zarkt stops floating, then looks up, sees the Focus crystals on the ceiling, stomps his foot on the ground, and performs Shatterspin.)

Nya: H'yah! Zarkt's going to destroy those crystals.

Kai: Then those crystals must affect the Arc Dragon! (He destroys another.)

(Zarkt tries to jump towards the crystals, but is stopped by Nya, who uses her Rising Dragon against him and knocks him to the ground.)

(Riyu, Wyldfyre, Frak, and Sora continue fighting against Kur and the Spectral Dragonians.)

Frak: Let's hope this still works. Hup! (He leaps and uses his Reveal Blade to destroy a Spectral Dragonian...) H'yah! (...then another.)

Sora: How? (She strikes a Spectral Dragonian with the hammer.)

Frak: Imperium tech. (He jumps and destroys two more Spectral Dragonians...) H'yah! (...then another.)

Sora: I know Imperium's changed... (She strikes another Spectral Dragonian with the hammer.) ...but just hearing those words still gives me a chill.

(Kur stops floating, then looks up, sees the Focus crystals on the ceiling, stomps her foot on the ground, and performs Shatterspin.)

Wyldfyre: (She shoots Heat at a Spectral Dragonian and lands on a crystal.) Ugly over there is going to Shatterspin!

(Riyu hits Kur and throws her against the wall. They jump towards each other and Kur knocks him down with a Shatterspin energy wave.)

(The tablets bearing the symbols of the Source Dragons remain low on energy.)

Arin: Thunderfang's gotten almost all the power out of those things.

Lloyd: Come on, work! (He fires Energy at the Unbidden Storm in an attempt to restore it, but fails.) (He groans.) It's no use!

Arin: I wonder if... Hmm. (He gets out his mech.)

Lloyd: Not sure leaving your mech's a good idea. Dragonians could be here any second.

Arin: Just let me try something. (He exhales deeply and performs rudimentary Spinjitzu.) Give me the Unbidden Storm!

Lloyd: Huh? How?

Arin: Just toss it!

(Lloyd drops the Unbidden Storm to Arin, who catches it, performs Spinjitzu, and transfer the Object Spinjitzu to it, restoring it.)

Lloyd: Whoa... (Arin takes the restored Unbidden Storm.) I had no idea you could transfer your Object Spinjitzu so... intensely.

Arin: That makes two of us, but I've been training, and it seemed like our only option. (He throws the Unbidden Storm back to Lloyd and returns to his mech.) Now, let's finish the job.

Lloyd: Let's hope the other teams pulled off their part.

(Lloyd and Arin place their mechs' hands on the tablet bearing the Flow symbol, which suddenly recharges, then the Unbidden Storm begins to float and explodes in a flash of light.)

(Thunderfang, at the end of his evolution, looks at his left arm, but his attention is drawn away by Focus energy, which reveals itself to be the Arc Dragon of Focus.)

Arc Dragon of Focus: Thunderfang, we meet again.

Thunderfang: If you have been sent here to challenge me, the Source Dragons must truly be afraid.

(The tablet with the Flow symbol discharges, and Arin and Lloyd remove their hands from it in their mechs.)

Arin: (With tan eyes.) Why does everything look weird?

Lloyd: (With purple eyes.) Are you also seeing, like, a vision or something?

(Lloyd and Arin see Thunderfang through two circles: one purple (Lloyd's) and one tan (Arin's).)

Lloyd: We're each seeing through the eyes of one of the Arc Dragon's heads!

(Thunderfang walks towards the Arc Dragon of Focus.)

Thunderfang: You may have bested me last time, but now I am more powerful than I have ever been! (He roars and attacks the Arc Dragon.)

(Kai destroys another Spectral Dragonian, grabs the coconut copy, and throws her.)

Coconut copy: (She cheers.) Wa-hoo-hoo-hoo! (She hits one Spectral Dragonian...) Right (...then another...) in the (...then another.) poppa!

(Nya continues fighting Zarkt while Kai destroys more Spectral Dragonians. Nya and Zarkt fall to the ground, but he gets up and prepares to attack her, until he is distracted by the roar of a dragon.)

Nya: Hey ugly! (Zarkt tries to attack her, but she pushes him away by throwing a ball of water.) Made you look. (She looks at the cave wall ahead and sees Thunderfang.) Thunderfang!

(Kai and the coconut copy defeat more Spectral Dragonians.)

Kai: How are we seeing that?

Nya: We were right. These crystals are connected to the Focus Dragon. I think we're seeing through the dragon's eyes!

(Zarkt performs Shatterspin and attempts to attack the crystals again, but Nya stops him using Rising Dragon.)

(Sora takes down two Spectral Dragonians, Frak destroys another, and Wyldfyre throws two fireballs at Kur, but she dodges one and deflects the other.)

Wyldfyre: This is called a set-up! Yaah! (She activates two more fireballs at Kur, but Kur dodges and lands, only to be knocked away by a tail swipe from Riyu and crash into the cave wall that shows through the eyes of the purple half of the Arc Dragon.) (She and Riyu give each other a high fave (Riyu uses his tail.) ...and that's called the pay-off!

(They are attacked by the Spectral Dragonians and retreat. Frak destroys more Spectral Dragonians, and Sora joins him and prepares to strike one. He removes his mask and watches her, with a loving gaze, strike the Spectral Dragonian.)

Frak: Sora, in case we don't get out of this, there's something I need to tell you.

Sora: (She takes off her mask.) What?

Frak: I... um... I... ate all the yogurt in the monastery fridge. (He dodges an attack from a Spectral Dragonian, which Sora strikes.)

Sora: You... what? (She strikes another Spectral Dragonian.)

Frak: I confess. All of it, it's gone. (He destroys a Spectral Dragonian.)

Sora: Great. You're forgiven. Why did I need to know this now?

Frak: Um, in case you make it out and I don't, and later somebody wonders where all the yogurt went, you'll have a definitive answer.

Sora: (She pants.) Okay... weirdo. (She puts on her mask and watches Kur perform Shatterspin.) Kur's trying Shatterspin again!

Frak: (He jumps.) Wyldfyre, heads up! (He throws his Reveal Blade to her.)

Wyldfyre: (She catches the Reveal Blade in mid-ai and uses it to destroy a Spectral Dragonian, then lands beside Sora.) I am not giving this back. Keepsies!

(Frak uses his Quake power to stop Kur's Shatterspin, but his attention is diverted by two Spectral Dragonians who attack him. He dodges by rolling on the ground, but ends up getting close to Kur, who stomps her foot and uses the Shatterspin's energy to launch him against one of the cave walls. She uses Shatterspin again.)

Sora: No! (She performs rudimentary Spinjitzu and attempts to attack Kur, who knocks her away.) Ah! (She flies to the cave wall and lands next to Frak.) Oof!

(Kur leaps and attempts to destroy the Focus crystals, but Wyldfyre stops her with a kick. She crashes into a cave wall and then faces Wyldfyre.)

(Thunderfang uses his hand claws on the Arc Dragon of Focus, but the latter retaliates using its tail and then bites him in the neck with the head of its purple half, until he pushes it away using his arm claw.)

Thunderfang: You're nothing but a lapdog of the Sources! (He and the Arc Dragon grab each other's shoulders.) Puppet of corrupt masters! (They let go of each other's shoulders and moved away.) I will soon be the Eighth Source, and I will rain chaos across the Merged Lands!

(Thunderfang covers himself with his wings and fires lightning bolts at the Arc Dragon, pushing it away. Arin and Lloyd sense this, and the former kneels.)

Arin: I'm not just seeing what the Focus Dragon sees, I'm feeling what it feels, and it hurts!

Lloyd: Yeah, didn't need any more pain, but does it feel like the dragon wants our help?

Arin: Yeah, I sense it too.

Lloyd: Follow my lead.

(They position themselves and command the Arc Dragon towards Thunderfang, who fires another lightning bolt with his wings, but Thunderfang dodges and tries to attack from above, only to be punched and sent flying. Arin and Lloyd are thrown against the wall as the Arc Dragon falls on its back. Arin and Lloyd get up.)

Arin: You okay?

Lloyd: (He grunts.) I got this. Come on.

(They position themselves again.)

(Nya, with Rising Dragon, and Zarkt, with Shatterspin, continue fighting while Kai destroys the Spectral Dragonians.)

(Wyldfyre attempts to attack Kur with the Reveal Blade, but Kur uses Shatterspin to knock her down.)

(The Arc Dragon rises and, under Arin and Lloyd's command, attacks Thunderfang, but the latter dodges, then grabs the former, spins it around, and throws it against a pillar of rocks, causing pain to Lloyd and Arin.)

Lloyd: H'ah! H'ah! H'ah! H'ah! (He makes punching motions.)

Arin: Lloyd, I'm trying to stay in sync with you, but--

(They make coordinated punching motions and command the Arc Dragon to attack Thunderfang, who dodges and punches the tan head, knocking it down once more and causing Arin and Lloyd to fall to a seated position. Thunderfang rips off part of a rock pillar and throws it at the Arc Dragon.)

Lloyd: (He groans.) This isn't working. The pain... I can't focus.

Arin: This is when you would tell me Ninja never quit.

Lloyd: (He groans.) We don't, but we know when it's time to change the plan. Arin, you take the lead. I'll follow you.

Arin: No. No way! The Merged Lands are at stake here. I mess everything up!

Lloyd: You can do this. Just focus. Trust me, Arin.

(Arin remembers Ras, his parents, and Sora embracing him. Back in the present, he cracks his neck and gives a determined smile. The bronze half of the Arc Dragon does the same and grunts, along with the purple half, at Thunderfang, who roars back. He walks to the side and then the Arc Dragon positions itself in the same way as Arin and Lloyd.)

Thunderfang: (He runs towards the Arc Dragon and jumps to attack it while falling.) Tell your masters (The Arc Dragon dodges.) Thunderfang will reap veng--

(The Arc Dragon strikes him with its tail, rushes towards him, and shoves him with its heads, throwing him against stone pillars.)

Arc Dragon of Focus: The battle is not over yet.

(Nya fires Water at Zarkt, but he dodges and fires Misfortune at her. She dodges the shot and leaps to attack him with her spear, but he fires Misfortune again and hits her, throwing her against one of the cave walls. Kai destroys two more Spectral Dragonians and the coconut copy fends off another. Nya lands and faces Zarkt again.)

Nya: (She paints.) That all you got? (She performs Spinjitzu and tries to attack Zarkt, but he fires Misfortune at her.) Aah! (She crashes against the cave wall again and slides to the ground.)

(Kai gasps as he watched Zarkt fire Misfortune at his sister.)

Kai: Nya!

(Kai leaps and attempts to attack Zarkt, but Zarkt notices him and knocks him down with a Misfortune blow. He gets up and fires Fire at Zarkt, but retreats because of the Spectral Dragonians. Zarkt prepares to kill Nya.)

Kai: No!

(Zarkt advances on Nya with Misfortune in his hands, but Jay, in the Mech Dragon, arrives in time and pushes him away using the Mech Dragon's head.)

Jay: Boo-yah! Take that!

Nya: (She gasps.) Jay? (She sees Jay flying in the Mech Dragon.)

Jay: You're one of my least favorite people, Zarkt.

(Zarkt stands up and Jay descends from the Mech Dragon in his mech. Zarkt uses Misfortune on his hands and Jay uses his mech's spear. They charge towards each other and Jay tries to crush Zarkt with his spear, but Zarkt dodges and fires Misfortune at Jay, but he dodges and throws his mech's spear at Zarkt, but he dodges. Jay jumps out of his mech and charges his hands with Lightning.)

Jay: Aah... (He uses a Lightning blow on Zarkt and throws him to the ground.) haah! (He lands, takes off his hat and mask, and runs to help Nya.)

Nya: I knew you'd come back, Jay.

Jay: I-- I just happened to be in the area.

Nya: (She chuckles.) You just happened to be deep in a cave hidden behind a magical waterfall?

Jay: Maybe. You don't know. I could be really into caves, or... maybe you said some things that stuck with me. I don't remember you, but being with you makes me want to be... not a terrible person.

(Jay and Nya stand up and she hugs him, which he apparently accepts and reciprocates. Kai finishes destroying the Spectral Dragonians and approaches them.)

Kai: Jay! That was amazing! Forget everything I used to say about you being the worst Ninja!

Jay: You used to say what?

Kai: Huh? What? No, no, that was Cole. We should go.

(Kur, floating, looks down and sees Wyldfyre, Frak, and Sora on the ground, defeated. She looks back and flies towards the Focus crystals. Sora turns and sees Kur heading towards the crystals.)

Sora: No...

(Kur prepares to destroy the Focus crystals, but she is stopped by a dark blue Spinjitzu that sucks her in and throws her to the ground. The Ninja recover and watch as the Spinjitzu lands, dissipates, and reveals itself to be Riyu, who has mastered Spinjitzu.)

Frak and Sora: Riyu!

(Riyu groans with delight and strikes a pose.)

(Thunderfang roars upward and glows with electricity. He attacks the Arc Dragon, which blocks and, thanks to Arin and Lloyd's focus and sync, knocks him away with a punch.)

Lloyd: It's working!

(Thunderfang attacks again but Arin and Lloyd continue to control the Arc Dragon in sync, striking Thunderfang again with an punch. The Arc Dragon of Focus dodges Thunderfang's blow. With Arin and Lloyd's command, the Arc Dragon of Focus hit him again with claw strike and manage to push Thunderfang away with a blow from the Arc Dragon's tail..)

Thunderfang: How dare you seven keep all the power for yourselves! I belong amongst the Source Dragons! (He charges the Arc Dragon.)

(Lloyd and Arin jump, spin in the air, and take their positions.)

Arin and Lloyd: Ninja... (Thunderfang continues charging.) Go!

(They make the Arc Dragon fire focused beams from its heads and, due to Thunderfang blocking them with his wings, it used the medallion on its chest, launching Thunderfang away.)

(In the Caves of Bells, the Ninja see through the eyes of the Arc Dragon and celebrate Thunderfang's defeat.)

(Thunderfang is hurled back to the top of the hill where the Well of the Lost is located, and his weight destroys it. He begins to glow and then disappears in an explosion, releasing a surge of soul energy from all the Merged Realms that dissipates the spectral Hollow Ones.)

(At the Monastery of Mysterium, Gandalaria is walking against the wall.)

Gandalaria: (Muttering.) Janet... Janet... (The surge of soul energy passes through there and returns her soul.) Aah! Wha--? How did I get here? Janet!

(The surge of soul energy passes through Abracalabria and returns the souls of the inhabitants.)

(The surge of soul energy passes through the Administration and returns the agents' souls.)

Munce agent: Well, these forms aren't gonna stamp themselves. (He sits in the chair and begins to stamp the forms.)

Green Merlopian agent: (He types on his computer keyboard.) It's good we're not hollow anymore.

Munce agent: Yeah, seriously. Can you imagine just mindlessly stumbling through life like that?

(The Administration Agents continue their work.)

(The surge of soul energy passes through the City of Temples and returns Roby's soul.)

Roby: Whoa! (He sighs.) Yeah, I feel like I missed something.

(The surge of soul energy passes through the Community of Lee and returns the souls of the inhabitants.)

Lees: Friends! I've been a bad neighbor. I've lied to you, and it's time I tell you all the truth...

(The surge of soul energy passes through the Monastery of Spinjitzu and returns the souls of Cole, Fritz, Bonzle, and Spitz.)

Cole: Fritz! Spitz! Bonzle! (He runs over to them and hugs them.) You're okay!

(Arin and Lloyd use their mechs to exit the cave, then they get out of them and run towards where the hill with the Well of the Lost was located.)

Arin: Thunderfang's gone? We did it?

Lloyd: You did it, Arin.

Arc Dragon of Focus: You did well, mortals... (It flies upwards towards them.) in waking me from my slumber and guiding me with your focus.

Lloyd: (To Arin.) What'd I say?

Arc Dragon of Focus: However, my masters may not feel the same way. I return now to my slumber, and pray the consequences for your actions are not too dire. (It shines with Focus energy, splits into its two halves, and teleports.)

Arin: Well, that sounded ominous.

Lloyd: Arin, during the fight, I was trying to force things to go the way I wanted, to control it all, but now I realize I need to let go sometimes, which also means... letting go of you. I can't force you to become a Ninja, Arin.

Arin: (He sighs heavily.) You don't have to, Lloyd, because, after all this, I realize I already am. (Lloyd smiles when he hears him say that.) If that's still okay with you.

Lloyd: (He and Arin hug.) There's nothing I want more.

(Wyldfyre, Sora, Frak, and Riyu exit the Cave of Bells and see that Arrakore and Zanth are still sleeping.)

Wyldfyre: (She sighs.) Still exhausted form creating that Arc Dragon, I see. I feel them. I could sleep for a week.

Frak: But Zanth is kinda... our ride home.

Sora: We have other options. (She looks towards the Monastery of Djinjago.)

Frak: The Monastery Gates, of course!

(They start walking towards the monastery, but Frak begins to feel dizzy.)

Frak: Whoa. I'm suddenly light-headed or something.

(Wyldfyre and Sora get dizzy too.)

Wyldfyre: I feel weird too. Is Djinjago tilting?

Sora: No, but something's off.

(At the Monastery of Mysterium, Jay, Kai, and Nya return the coconut copy to Gandalaria, and while walking towards the Mech Dragon, the first three become dizzy.)

(At the Monastery of the Spectral Lands, Lloyd and Arin walk towards the portal gate.)

Arin: ...but before I get back to training, I need a vacation with my parents. It's been so long.

Lloyd: Absolutely-- (He gets dizzy and falls to the ground.)

Arin: Are you okay?

Lloyd: (He gasps.) I feel weird. It's like my connection... (He tries to use his Elemental Power...) My Elemental Power. (...but it stops working.) Something's wrong with it.

Arin: (He helps Lloyd to his feet.) What would cause that?

Lloyd: I don't know, but we need to get back to the Monastery... now.

(The three teams run towards the portal gates.)

Arin, Sora and Nya: Ninjago!

(Lloyd and Arin pass through the portal gate of the Spectral Lands Monastery.)

(Sora, Wyldfyre, Frak, and Riyu pass through the portal gate of the Monastery of Djinjago.)

(Jay, Kai, and Nya board the Mech Dragon and fly through the portal gate of the Monastery of Mysterium.)

(Lloyd and Arin end up in the Monastery of the Whispering Wood.)

Lloyd: (He gasps.) This isn't right.

(Kai, Nya and Jay end up in the Monastery of Madness.)

Kai: Whoa... We're not in Ninjago.

(They see the circular part of the portal gate glow red.)

(Frak, Sora, Wyldfyre and Riyu end up in Monastery of Eternal Dawn.)

Frak: Uh, the Gate took us to the wrong place.

(The gate closes and Wyldfyre tries to reopen it, but fails.)

Wyldfyre: Why isn't it opening?

(Lloyd tries to open the gate, but fails.)

Lloyd: We're stranded here.

(Nya fails to open the gate.)

Nya: How could this happen?

(At the Monastery of Gates, Ras imprisoned Wu in Gandalaria's protective container and took control the gates. Wu is trying to escape.)

Ras: (To Wu.) Your former students are lost now, old man, scattered across the farthest reaches of the Merged Lands. Now, nothing will stand in my way.

(In the Source Dragons' void, the symbols of only four of the seven Source Dragons are present.)

Energy Symbol: I would never have believed our siblings would do this. Withdraw from the Council? It's unthinkable.

Flow Symbol: The mortals dared command Focus' Arc Dragon, her personal avatar. Even you must admit it was highly... improper.

Motion Symbol: Improper, perhaps, yet it saved this council from Thunderfang's wrath. The Conduit made the right choice.

Energy Symbol: And yet, it is the mortals that will suffer.

(The symbol of the Source Dragon of Balance is seen to have faded to dark.)

Energy Symbol: With Focus, Life, and Strength withdrawn, the balance of Elemental Powers is askew. It will cause chaos for the powers of every Elemental Master in the Merged Lands.

Flow Symbol: Our promise to the First Spinjitzu Master is broken. Source Dragons violating their sacred word.... it may mean the end of this cycle.

Motion Symbol: Not just the end of this cycle, the end of all cycles. The universe has never been in more danger.

(Back at Whispering Wood monastery, Lloyd and Arin look at their surroundings.)

Arin: We're trapped who knows where, the gates stopped working, and your powers are all messed up. What are we gonna do, Lloyd?

Lloyd: What Ninja always do...

(The other two groups of ninja look at their surroundings in the realms they got trapped in. Back at the Whispering Wood monastery, Lloyd catches a falling leaf.)

Lloyd: We'll find a way.
