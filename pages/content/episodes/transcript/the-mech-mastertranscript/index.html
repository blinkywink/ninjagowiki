The Mech Master/Transcript

The Mech Master
A Pain in the Mech!
≫

(At the Mech Master 5000, Kai, Cole, Arin, Sora, Nya, and Riyu are walking on the road.)

Kai: (talking while eating a spoonful of Crème de Imperium) This is so good!

Cole: What Kai is saying is that you got to try this Crème de Imperium. I mean, I'm usually a cake guy, but wow! Who knew pie could be this good!

Arin: Yeah, Grab-Barg’s a great baker. I'm reminded of it every year I lose the pie baking contest to him.

Sora: At least you remembered to enter the pie baking contest. (sees a racer fixing up her mech.)

Arin: Sora, I know you're mad you forgot to register for the Mech Master 5000, but we've been kind of busy. You know, saving the world as ninja?

Nya: (coming over) There's always next year.

(The Mechanic looks at the ninja from behind a corner and laughs. The ninja come onto the stands to watch the Mech Master 5000.)

Announcer: The annual Mech Master 5000 race is about to begin! With homemade mechs competing from all around the Crossroads! Who will win the title of greatest racer?

Sora: (looks on, scowls.) Me. That should be me.

Arin: Sora, stop.

Announcer: All mechs on your marks, get set- (a bomb falls into the ground, sending an electrical shockwave and disabling the mechs.) There seems to be a problem. All the mechs have been shut down.

(The Mechanic jumps onto one of the mechs.)

Kai: The Mechanic!

Sora: Who's that? Is he here to repair stuff?

Nya: No, that's not what he does.

(The Mechanic opens the driver's mech.)

Mrs. R: Hey, this is not a sanctioned part of the... (The Mechanic throws them out of the mech) RACE!

Mechanic: (Gets in, planting a device on the mech, taking control of the other mechs.) Couldn't be easier.

(The mechs start moving towards the bridge.)

Announcer: Hey, he's stealing those mechs! I am shocked and chagrined!

Nya: That's what he does.

Kai: Ninja-go! (All the ninja jump onto a nearby roof.)

(The ninja start chasing the Mechanic. The remaining mech drivers eject out of their seats. Nya lands on one of the mechs and shoots a beam of water. The Mechanic's mech slips, and the mech Nya is on goes around to the other side of his mech.)

Nya: Caution, slippery road conditions! (The Mechanic knocks the mech Nya is on to the side.)

(Arin uses his grappling hook to land on the front of the Mechanic's mech and taps on the visor to get his attention.)

Arin: Hey, we've never met before, and I gotta say, you're making a very bad first impression. (notices that he would be knocked down by a sign if he continued to stay on top of the mech and uses his Grapple Gun to escape, landing in a room above full of pies. He soon eats a spoonful of Crème de Imperium) Wow. These really are delicious. (Chef Grab-Barg appears, unhappy.)

Mechanic: (Laughs)

(The Mechanic and the other mechs stop in front of Sora, Kai, and Cole's mechs)

Kai: You seem to be in a hurry. You didn't steal those mechs, did you? Not you, Mechanic.

(The Mechanic sends one of the empty mechs to crash into Sora, Kai, and Cole's mechs, breaking them into pieces.)

Mechanic: (laughs and passes through them) See you, jerks!

Sora: No! (One of the Mechanic's mechs hits the cockpit of Sora's mech.)

Cole: Sora, you're the fastest racer. Go after the Mechanic. (Sora starts putting her mech back together, using parts from Kai and Cole's mechs. She starts to run after the Mechanic.) Kai and I can stop these things. (Cole and Kai put their mechs back together, also using different parts from each others' mechs.)

(Both mechs charge at Cole and Kai's mechs. Cole, using an arm originally from Kai's mech, shoots fire at one of the mechs, knocking it down.)

Cole: What the-

Kai: Did we switch parts?

(Kai jumps at the other approaching mech and uses the arm that was originally from Cole's mech to hit the ground using the Earth power, sending a shockwave and knocking down the mech.)

Kai: That is so cool!

Cole: Yeah, ha ha!

(Both mechs charge at Cole and Kai, and Cole shoots fire at them from his mech while Kai uses the Earth punch to send a mini earthquake at them, combining their powers and destroying the mechs.)

Kai and Cole: Whoa. Now I know what it feels like to be you. Stop copying me!

(Sora is chasing the Mechanic's mech over the bridge Sora and Arin previously fought Ras in.)

Mechanic: The new ninja with tech powers, huh? Ha! (Zooms forward.) I don't need Elemental Power to be a Master of Technology!

(Sora's mech lunges at the Mechanic's, but is kicked off the bridge, resulting in Sora screaming.)

Mechanic: (laughs) Home free! (Zooms forward.)

(Sora, falling, activates the jet boosters on Kai's mech's legs' feet, allowing her to stay airborne, and flies back up to the bridge. Landing on the bridge, Sora quickly catches up with the Mechanic's mech, hitting it and causing it to uncontrollably tumble forward.)

Mechanic: My wheels! (The wheels from the Mechanic's mech lands next to the mech's cockpit, detached.) I wonder if this thing has spares in the trunk.

Sora: (Walks over to the Mechanic's mech.) They call you the Mechanic, huh? I am not impressed.

Kai: (Kai and Cole walk over to the Mechanic in their mechs.) We never were, either.

Mechanic: (Groans in disappointment and raises his hands.)

Sora: Guess we mixed up our mech parts?

Kai: Yeah, which is way cool.

Cole: It looks like you got your wish, Sora. You did want to race today.

(The ninja are walking along the start of the Mech Master 5000 racetrack, with Kai and Nya eating a spoonful of Creme de Imperium. Arin shows Sora one of Grab-Barg's pies.)

Arin: Riyu and I stocked up on these. You gotta try one. Grab-Barg's pies are legit good.

Sora: (Tries a spoonful of Creme de Imperium.) Mmmm. Wow! Best pie I have ever tasted. (Sora sees Arin looking distraught at her comment.) Uhhhh... second best.

Arin: (Laughs) Yeah. Yeah, thanks for that. (whispers) Yeah.

(The ninja continue walking down the racetrack.)
