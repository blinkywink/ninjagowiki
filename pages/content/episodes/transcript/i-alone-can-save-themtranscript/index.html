(In Thunderfang's prison, Nokt is standing on the chain holding Thunderfang's right arm. He pulls out the Prismatic Blade he obtained from behind his back. The blade and chain react to one another, shifting in color from light blue to red. Nokt jumps up, and slashes through the chain when he falls back down, causing it to disintegrate. Rox is holding the Prismatic Blade she found in her hand, which is now green in color. She jumps up to hit the chain on Thunderfang's left arm, disintegrating it as well. Thunderfang roars, pushing the two back slightly.)

Thunderfang: This is the closest to freedom I have been in a millenia. Yet three more chains keep me imprisoned.

Rox: Don't fear, (She stabs her Prismatic Blade into the ground.) the Forbidden Five hunt the remaining blades as we speak.

(Thunderfang roars again.)

(In the City of Temples, Roby is organizing the museum.)

Roby: Yeah, put that by the window, the backlight will be Stang-Zoot-Cubed! (He gestures to a pair of Security Bots holding a statue of him. He then walks over to inspect a sculpture on display.) That's the back of the piece. Turn it around, please. (He leaves, Security Bot arrives and turns the sculpture. He then comes back to look at it again.) Huh. No, you were right, it looked better before. (He walks to the Elemental Cup, and blows dust away from it.) When I'm done with this remodel, this place ain't gonna be a museum, it's gonna be a you-see-um, with the huge crowds that are gonna come through here! (The Security Bots look at him with neutral expressions and start walking away.) What, no reaction? Even Uncle Bleckt used to at least give me a scowl for my genius wordplay. Hermes? (He walks over to a resting Spirit Dragon.) Hermes? (The dragon wakes up and growls.) Hermes... (Hermes flies over to Roby.) I need you to take this note to Geo in the Land of Lost Things. Another sculpture order. (Hermes takes the note from Roby.) Tell him we need more blue in here, but not azul. I'd say we're pretty full up on azul. As they say, too much azul, is not a-cool. Hold on a sec (He runs over to the Security Bots still carrying his sculpute.) Hey! Wait, wait, wait, hey. That's gotta go right by the front entrance to this floor. That statue of me is going to be the most photographed art in here, so we have to place it in prime selfie light. (He points to a pedestal with his name written on it in Ninjargon. The bots start moving the statue.)

(Suddenly, the door to the museum is smashed open by two Dragonian Trackers, with four more flying outside behind them. One of the flying Dragonians throws one of their swords at the sculpture, causing it to break apart. Two more Security Bots with blasters enter the room to fight them.)

Roby: Hey, you can't just storm in- (The Dragonians enter, led by Kur.) Wait... you're one of the Forbidden Five! (A Dragonian pushes Roby down.)

(The Security Bots and Dragonians start to fight. One Bot is knocked down by a Dragonian slashing it from above. Another Bots flies up, but is knocked down by a flying Dragonian. Another Dragonian knocks down a Bot by charging at it. The fourth Bot tries to fire at a Dragonian, but misses, allowing the Dragonian to tackle it. Kur walks past this chaos, as another Bot lands on its back next to her. Hermes drops the note he was holding and flies towards Kur, but Kur grabs him and uses her Elemental Power to decay him. She then tosses him away and pulls out a bag as the walks towards the broken blade sitting on display. She picks up the hilt of the blade.)

Kur: What have they done to you? (She puts the pieces of the blade in her bag.)

(Roby gets up and runs to grab the Elemental Cup from its pedestal. He approaches Kur with it.)

Kur: You think you can sneak up on me, child? (Her eyes glow red, and she steps back and hits Roby away with her elbow. The Elemental Cup flies away, and hits the picture of Roake, leaving a large hole around where the face used to be.)

Roby: (gets up) Whatever you need that blade for, Wyldfyre and the other ninja will kick your butt all over the Merged Lands before you can use it.

Kur: (cackles) A funny one, you are. Let us see how hilarious you find my Elemental Power of... (She gets ready to used her power, and two Dragonians run to grab Roby from behind.) Decay. (She uses her power on Roby, causing him to weaken. Bolts of red lightning flash outside.)

(Arin and Ras are walking across a desert.)

Arin: You said you were taking me to my parents. There's nothing up ahead. I mean, literally nothing.

Ras: (snarls) Once again you are misjudging the situation.

Arin: Oh, am I? You see anything but miles and miles of sand?

Ras: Was I not right about Master Wu causing the Merge?

Arin: Just take me to my parents. Like you promised.

Ras: That's what I'm doing.

Arin: No, you're leading me across an empty desert. I risked my life saving you. I believed-

Ras: Stop.

(Ras walks over to a rock. He lifts it up to reveal it was a cover for two levers, one black and one red. Arin gasps and Ras pulls the red lever, causing the flat area beside them to open. A platform rises out of the opening, carrying a large yellow jet.)

Arin: How... how did you know this was here?

Ras: I have many resources hidden across the Merged Lands, as a contingency. Now perhaps you will cease doubting me.

Arin: I... I'm sorry.

Ras: Do you know how to pilot such a vehicle?

Arin: Yeah, pretty much. When I was a mech racer, my best friend Sor- Uh, a long time ago, someone taught me to drive and pilot all kinds of vehicles.

Ras: Hmm... Show me what you've learned.

(Arin and Ras enter the jet's cockpit.)

Arin: Huh... Okay, power should be... (He presses a button, causing an alarm to blare up. He quickly presses another button, which stops the alarm but causes a window wiper to come out of the front of the windscreen. Another button stops the wiper, but starts to play music. Then he presses a button that stops the music and retracts the wiper.) Yeah, that's there, so this is... (He presses another button, which lauches the drone on top of the jet. The Drone starts to circle the are around the jet.) It has a drone? I-I mean, I knew that. Just confirming. There it is. Now to make it land... (He presses more buttons, and the drone suddenly stops and crashes in front of them. He the presses the button to make the drone start up again, and it flies into the air.) Got to make it land on the jet... (He presses a button, and the drone starts speeding toward the jet.)

Ras: Fool! (He crawls forward to push another button, and the drone slows its movement and circles to the back of the jet to land. He then presses another button to start the engines.)

Arin: I was going to hit that next. Buckle up! (He puts on his seatbelt and moves the control stick forward, but the jet doesn't move.) Oh, right, uh... (He presses another button.) disengage landing gear. (The landing gear folds away.) Uh, okay, now hold on to- (The jet blasts off.) Whoa!

(Lloyd, Sora, Frak and Riyu are still walking trough the First Land in search of Arin.)

Sora: Hey, look! (She points to the sled Arin used to pull Ras.) Maybe Ras traveled on this, and Arin dragged him?

Lloyd: Oh, makes sense. Ras looked pretty hurt last time we saw him, and it would explain their strange tracks. What do you think, Frak?

Frak: Love it. Super cool.

Sora: You love Arin pulling Ras on a sled?

Frak: I'm sorry, I was kind of preoccupied wondering what that terrifying looking place is? (He points to the Spectral Lands.)

Lloyd: I have no idea. Looks like the edge of another realm that merged.

Frak: We don't have to go there, do we? It's probably full of puppets. I just know it.

(Riyu sniffs the sled and growls.)

Sora: Yep, this sled was definitely Ras'. (Riyu follows the scent to some footprints.) Two sets of prints. If Ras was injured, it looks like he got better.

Lloyd: Only two sets. Which makes it more likely they are alone. No Forbidden Five.

Sora: Where could the Five have gone then?

Frak: Who knows, who cares? We're back on track to find Arin, and that's all that matters, right? Let's go.

(Lloyd, Sora and Frak start following the footprints, when Riyu suddenly grumbles.)

Sora: What is it, Riyu?

Lloyd: He wants us to follow him.

Frak: Uh, that's the opposite direction that Arin was headed!

Lloyd: Maybe someone needs help. Riyu's never led us wrong.

Sora: Well, he has led us to more that one noodle house in the past.

Lloyd: And was that wrong?

Frak: Really? We're just...? Um, okay, but for the record, I think this is a mistake.

Sora: Noted.

(In a commercial, Rapton is walking through the Crossroads.)

Past Rapton: Hey there, Rapton here. (He snatches an ice cream from a young girl.) Are you sick of the 9 to 5 (He starts eating the ice cream, but quickly throws it away in disgust.) (In another scene, he is riding through the First Realm with two Freebooters in Hover chariots.) Rapton is here to invite you on the ride of a lifetime. (The trio ride towards a Mountain Dragon on the ground. The dragon knocks over one of the chariots with it's head, and one of the Freebooters is seen flying past the camera, while the other one is being held in the dragon's mouth. He walks in front of the battle as the dragon spits the Freebooter from its mouth) We have a great healthcare plan, (Another chariot flies towards the dragon, but gets headbutted away. The dragon looks at Rapton and roars.) (In another scene, Rapton is celebrating with three Freebooters as coins fall in front of the camera.) and you'll be rich! (In a different scene, Rapton comes from behind his chariot.) And famous! (A crowd of angry people approaches him.) No autographs, please. (He walks over to his chariot and tosses a smoke bomb at the crowd before flying off.) (He and three other Freebooter appear from an cloud with their chariots.) Join the Freebooters today!

(In the present, Rapton pauses the commercial he was watching.)

Rapton: Hello, handsome. (He walks over to one of the other Freebooters digging trough the wreckage of the hover train, but stumbles over a piece of debris.) What?

(Next to the train, there are piles of loot in the sand. One of the Freebooters is looking over the bodies of two others laying on the ground, and P.I.X.A.L. is lifting up the damaged Zane.)

Zane: Primary... electronics disrupted... secondary elements-

P.I.X.A.L.: Shh... Zane. Save your energy. (She rushes over to a box, and starts rummaging trough it.)

Rapton: Whoa, whoa, whoa, woah! What makes you think you can just use our stuff? We stole all that gear fair and square, lady!

P.I.X.A.L.: If it wasn't for that Nindroid there, you and your poorly named "Freebooters" would all be smashed against that cliff.

Freebooter: (to one of the Freebooters laying on the ground) Told you it was an awful name.

P.I.X.A.L.: The only reason you and your hair-piece are still alive is because of Zane, who needs a lot of help to survive this. So you are not only gonig to let me use your gear, you are going to help me find the pieces I need to save him. Have I made myself clear?

Rapton: (clears throat) Uh, Freebooters. I, Rapton, have decided it's up to us to save Zane. So when P.I.X.A.L. here, who's assisting me, asks for something, let her have it. Unless it's like super valuable, and then still ask- ask me first. Also this is definitely my real hair.

P.I.X.A.L.: Get me more parts like these, please. (She goes over to Zane, and plugs a small wire into his head.) We are not going to lose each other again, Zane. Whatever it takes.

(At the arcade, Nya pulls open a panel on the arcade cabinet and examines it with a flashlight as Kai and Wyldfyre watch him.)

Nya: I don't get it. How did Jay play this old game remotely? Nothing on the motherboard allows remote access.

Wyldfyre: Hmm... Did you check the fatherboard?

Kai: I'm thinking he cheated somehow. Only way he could have beaten my high score. Check for cheat-ware.

Nya: If we could figure out how he transmitted the signal, we could trace it back to Jay's location, and...

Kai: ...and force him to admit he cheated!

(Nya groans. A Spirit Dragon flies towards them carrying a scroll.)

Wyldfyre: Yes!

Kai: Ooh... Somebody's getting another "love-scroll".

Wyldfyre: (catches the scroll) Am not. (giggles) Shut up. Okay, I totally am. (She opens the scroll, only to suddenly gasp in horror.) No!

Kai: What? Is Roby breaking up with you? Let's go kick his little butt!

Wyldfyre: No, Roby's hurt! The City of Temples was attacked! (She shows the scroll to Kai and Nya, who both gasp.)

(Arin and Ras are flying over an ocean.)

Arin: Welcome to Arin Airlines, We've reached cruising altitude, so-

Ras: Your commentary is not necessary.

Arin: (looks over) Hey, what's that? (He sees the Algae farmers' village, where smoke is rising from several of the buildings.) I know that place. Lloyd and I were there. (He starts flying towards the village.)

Ras: What are you doing?

Arin: It looks like they need help.

Ras: Stay focused on the task at hand.

Arin: We can't just ignore this.

Ras: If you're distracted by everything in your path, you'll never find your parents. That's why Lloyd is so ineffectual, and he has clearly passed his bad habits onto you. Time to prioritize your needs.

(Arin turns the jet around and flies over the village.)

(Riyu is leading Lloyd, Sora and Frak across the First Land.)

Frak: Oh, wow! Look what we've found. It's nothing. And over there, more nothing!

Sora: When you get to know Riyu better, you'll learn to trust him.

Frak: If we'd stayed focused on finding Arin, we might already have-

(Riyu suddenly growls, then grabs the three and leaps behind some vegetation.)

Lloyd: Riyu, what gives?

(The sky suddenly darkens, and a faint wind begins blowing the sand.)

Frak: I'm... not hearing anything.

(Tyr and Zarkt walk into the clearing where the ninja just were.)

Zarkt: Fan out! The blade must be nearby! We should have found it by now!

(A group of six Dragonian Trackers appears behind them, and they start searching the area.)

Sora: (whispers) Now we know where the Forbidden Five are. Well, one of them.

Lloyd: That's Tyr. The leader of the Dragonians we met.

Frak: The ones that helped us out of the river?

Sora: What have the Forbidden Five done to them? Is this what you wanted to show us? (Riyu nods and growls.)

Lloyd: They look corrupted, maybe ancient magic. The Five must've come through the village after we did. Those poor Dragonians... (Riyu looks sad, and Sora comforts him.)

Zarkt: (growls) You fools. It's a small blade, about this big, of a shimmering metal. Thunderfang said you'd be able to sense it. (He grabs Tyr, and pushes him away.) What's wrong with you?

Frak: He mean this? (He takes out the Prismatic Blade from his back.)

(Tyr suddenly senses the presence of the blade, and starts approaching the ninja. Frak hands the blade to Lloyd, who closely examines it. Tyr suddenly appears in front of them, and cuts through the vegetation they were hidden behind, knocking them back.)

Zarkt: Ninja? Give that blade to me!

Lloyd: Yeah, I'd say this is what he's looking for! Ninja... go! (He puts on his hood and leaps up, slashing towards Zarkt with the blade.)

(At the Dragon stables in the Monastery, Mr. Frohicky is instructing Kai, Nya and Wyldfyre on how to use the Portal Gate.)

Frohicky: Oh, dear. I do hope I'm following these operating instructions from Master Zane correctly. "Just walk up to the portal and state your desired destination."

Wyldfyre: Take me to Roby! (The gate doesn't respond.)

Frohicky: No, state your destination.

Wyldfyre: Take me to Roby town! (The gate is silent yet again.)

Kai and Nya: The City of Temples!

(The light on top of the gate starts to glow, and the doors open. The three ninja start running through.)

Wyldfyre: Let's go!

Nya: Thanks, Mr. Frohicky.

(They arrive in the Monastery of Gates. While there, Nya notices a new gate has activated.)

Nya: Hey! When did that happen? That portal wasn't active before.

Wyldfyre: Who cares? My boyfriend's way more important than stupid gates! (They continue running.)

(In the hospital of the City of Temples, two Medical Bots are treating Roby. Wyldfre enters by breaking the door.)

Wyldfyre: Roby, what happened to you?

Roby: (weakly) Wyldfyre... I knew you'd come. Rawr.

Wyldfyre: Rawr. Whoever did this to you, I'll heat blast them! (She activates her power.) Then resurrect them again, then heat blast them again!

Roby: It was one of the Forbidden Five. She got Hermes, too. (Hermes roars weakly.) The zootless jerk and her goons came for a weapon.

Nya: The Five don't have enough weapons already?

Roby: I should know the importance of the Prismatic Blade she took, but I don't. I used to think old lore was just a waste of time, like the Source Dragons being a myth, but I was wrong. (He tries to pull up the book sitting on his lap.)

Kai: Let me help with that.

Roby: Ah thanks. (They open the book.) My eyes are getting worse. I can't read this, but the answers could be in here, "The Sagas of the Eternal Sources" by King Urd Leatherwing. It's a book of history my family kept. You have to find out what they're doing. You have to stop the Forbidden Five. (coughs)

Wyldfyre: Shh... Roby. The Forbidden Five are going to be the Forgotten Zero when I'm done with them.

(In the First Land, the ninja are fighting Zarkt. He block Lloyd's jumping attack with the chain of his flail, but is knocked back. Frak dodges a slash from one of the Dragonians.)

Frak: Come on! You were so nice before. (He grabs the Dragonian's sword as they try to jab him, and retaliates with a kick.) Can't we all just be cool? (he uses his power to shake the ground, but the two Dragonians before him fly into the air.) Quake works better against people who stay on the grou- (The Dragonians grab him and lift him away, and one of them punches him in the chest.)

Sora: Tyr, buddy, you got any tech on you? (She powers up.) Anything at all? (Tyr puts his swords away and runs to punch her, but she blocks it with the armor on her arm.) Guess not. (Tyr spins around to strike her with his wings, but she leans back to dodge it and pushes Tyr back by punching and kicking.) Then let's give Spinjitzu another try. (She tries spinning around, but nothing happens. Tyr takes advantage of this, and charges at her, slamming her into the air.) Did I do it? I don't think I did it! (She plummets onto the ground.)

(As Sora gets back up, Tyr draws his swords and jumps at her, but Riyu flies up and grabs him, taking him into the air and throwing him at the two dragonians holding up Frak, causing all four of them to fall.)

Frak: Woa-ah! Unh! (He lands on his feet as the Dragonians crash behind him.)

(As Frak runs back to fight, Zarkt is standing besides a Dragonian. He stomps the ground five times.)

Lloyd: Uh-oh. I hate that sound. (He uses Spinjitzu and jumps up high, activating Rising Dragon Technique. He blasts a beam of green energy towards, who has just activated Shatterspin. The Dragonian next to Zarkt get away from the attack, as he swoops down and knocks Zarkt against a nearby boulder.)

(Zarkt roars as he closes the distance on Lloyd by flying low to the ground. He strikes at Lloyds feet with his flail, but Lloyd jumps up and strikes at him againg with the Prismatic Blade, which he again blocks. The two of them keep their weapons locked.)

Zarkt: That belongs to me.

Lloyd: I don't know exactly who it belongs to, possibly a giant, creepy desert puppet, but i 100% know it doesn't belong to the Forbidden Five!

Zarkt: Everything we desire belongs to us by right of conquest! (He reaches for the blade with one of his hands as Lloyd throws it up into the air. He the swipes at Lloyd with his flail, but Lloyd ducks under it and grabs the blade again.)

(As Zarkt tries to grab the blade again, Lloyd drops it onto his foot. Zarkt reaches for it again, but Lloyd flings it up and kicks Zarkt away while he is distracted, then grabs the blade again. Zarkt activates his power, and fires a blast of it at Lloyd. Lloyd dodges, causing the blast to hit a pile of rocks. Zarkt fires three more blasts at Lloyd, who dodges them all, and retaliates with his own power. The two blasts collide in the air, creating a cloud of red and green energy. As the two prepare to continue their fight, Riyu and Tyr fly above them, Tyr charges at Riyu, who knocks him away with his tail. The rest of the Dragonians surround Sora and Frak.)

Frak: See, told you!

Sora: Told me what?

Frak: That we shouldn't follow Riyu. You noted it.

(Two Dragonians jump at them.)

Sora: Riyu can hear other dragons. (She holds up the sword of the Dragonian that attacked her to block another Dragonian and push them away, and then kicks away the other one as well. Frak evades and punches away the Dragonian that attacked him, grabbing their swords in his right hand and mouth. He tries to get the sword in his mouth to his hand, but it get knocked away by a Dragonian spinning trough the air) I bet he could sense the Dragonians were in trouble! (Frak turns around and parries a Dragonian jumping at him with the sword in his hand, sending them rolling away.)

Frak: Still shouldn't have followed!

(The two of them hear a dragon roaring.)

Sora: It's Zanth! Zane found us!

(P.I.X.A.L. jumps down from Zanth's back.)

Frak: Zane looks... different (He tosses the sword he was holding at one of the Dragonians.)

Lloyd: P.I.X.A.L.! (He blasts Zarkt away with his power.) Were where you? (He hugs her.) How'd you find us? Zane's been looking for you!

P.I.X.A.L.: Yes, we found each other.

(As Riyu is figting Tyr in the air, Zane flies in and grabs Tyr. He then blasts ice at the Dragonians on the ground and trows Tyr down, flying over to blast away two dragonians near the ninja.)

Sora: Somebody had a glow up.

(Zane lands on the ground next to the ninja. Zarkt gets back up and attacks P.I.X.A.L. with his flail. P.I.X.A.L. dodges the attack.)

P.I.X.A.L.: We haven't met. (She punches Zarkt.) I am P.I.X.A.L.

Zarkt: I don't care! (He swings again, only for P.I.X.A.L. to dodge.)

P.I.X.A.L.: You should. (Zarkt attempts to punch her, but she gabs Zarkt's hand, kicks him and shoves him away.) You are attacking my friend, (Zarkt strikes again, but she dodges his attack and grabs his flail, spinning him around.) And that is something I will not tolerate. (She puts her leg on top of Zarkt's arm and spins him around again, then pulls him close by his flail and punches him away.)

(The ninja prepare to fend off Zarkt. Zane, knocks down a Dragonian flying at him.)

Zarkt: Dragonians, pull back! (He flies into the air.) We can always find you, ninja, and when we return, (Tyr and four other Dragonians fly up next to him.) you will give us the blade, or die defending it!

Sora: The what?

Zarkt: The blade! You will give us the blade!

Sora: Parade? Why would we give you a parade?

Zarkt: That's not what I said!

Sora: We'll never throw you a parade. 'Cause of how you're evil.

Zarkt: Bah! (He and the Dragonians leave.)

Frak: He clearly said "blade", not "parade".

Sora: (takes off her mask and puts on her headphones) I know.

Lloyd: P.I.X.A.L.! Oh, you're back! (He hugs her again.) And Zane, you're... huge? What happened?

P.I.X.A.L.: We will explain everything.

Zane: But first, you must know the Five are gathering special blades for an unidentified nefarious purpose.

Lloyd: Yeah, blades like this. It looks different, but it sounds the same as the sword from my fight with Zeatrix.

Zane: (analyses the blade) It is made of the same kind of rare metal that sword was.

Sora: But why would the Five want them?

(At the Storm Village, Zarkt is reporting back to Rox.)

Rox: You let the ninja get the blade?

Zarkt: They already had it. I couldn't-

(Rox grabs Zarkt's hat and slams him to the ground, then she steps on top of him.)

Rox: Fail me again, and the Forbidden Five will be the Forbidden Four! (Kur approaches the pair, along with two Dragonians.) Kur, tell me you didn't also fail! (She hops down from on top of Zarkt.)

Kur: I got the blade, but I found it like this. (She pulls out the bag with the pieces of the blade.)

Rox: (grabs the hilt from the bag) Hmm... We can fix it. All it will take is a little wish magic.

Kur: But all the Djinn are gone.

Rox: No, not all of them.

(Arin and Ras land on an island covered in large stone pillars.)

Arin: You said they'd be here. (Ras points across the landscape.) No tricks? No lies? This is real, my parents are there?

Ras: No lies.

(Arin jumps out of the jet and runs towards where Ras was pointing.)

Arin: Mom? Dad? (He climbs over a hill, only to see a graveyard.) No... (He looks trough the gravestones in panic, until he finds two that bare his parents' names.) Mom! Dad! (He starts to cry.) No! (Ras comes over to comfort him.) Why didn't you tell me?

Ras: Would you have believed me without tseeing it for yourself?

Arin: No. How did they die?

Ras: That is unclear. Whatever happened, it must've been because of the Merge. But I can fix it all.

Arin: "Fix it"? They're dead! There's nothing to fix anymore.

Ras: Not true. Reverse the Merge, and you can create anything in its wake, and I will reverse the Merge. That's why I, alone, can save them.

Arin: What exactly are you saying?

Ras: Your parents. My people. Help me undo the Merge, and they will return, restored to life like nothing ever happened.
