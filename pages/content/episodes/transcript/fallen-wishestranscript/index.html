(At the Crossroads, Rogue arrives in a alley looking for his new employer.)

Rogue: I got a message to meet someone here.

(Rogue's new employer is revealed to be Zeatrix.)

Zeatrix: Were you followed?

Rogue: (chuckles) If you thought I could be followed, you wouldn't have sent a message to me in the first place.

Zeatrix: Huh. True.

Rogue: So, what do you need me for?

Zeatrix: Revenge. I want you to bring someone to me.

Rogue: Interesting, but let's be clear. Delivering people is a lot more expensive then delivering things. You got that kind of cash?

Zeatrix: Don't let my appearance fool you. I am not without resources. (She pulls out a bag full of coins.)

Rogue: Who's the target?

(She puts the bag back and shows him a photo, revealing that her target is Ras. Rogue starts to think.)

Zeatrix: I'll pay you five times your regular fee.

Rogue: Hmm. This target must be important.

Zeatrix: Long ago, he and my sister betrayed my family, But that is not your business. Do we have a deal?

(He nods positively.)

(At the reconstruction of Djinjago, people are lining up for wishes from Arrakore.)

Geckle: Sir, Arrakore, if you would please sir. Could you heal my flower? I’ve tried everything.

Arrakore: Is that a question or a wish?

Geckle: A wish, right. (clears throat) I wish my flower was blooming!

Arrakore: Your wish is yours to keep.

(Arrakore made his flower blooming again, causing a new part of Djinjago to appear.)

Geckle: (gasps) You’re back to looking like your usual beautiful self, Veronica.

(The Geckle walks back as a Munce wishes for a plunger.)

Arrakore: Your wish is yours to keep!

(The Munce leaves the line, and is followed by Dareth.)

Dareth: I wish I become a real Spinjitzu ninja, like for permanent.

Arrakore: (sighs) Uh-uh.

Dareth: Seriously? What gives?

Arrakore: I am never obligated to grant a wish.

Dareth: Yeah, yeah, yeah, but... Pretty please?

Arrakore: Mm… no. I can refuse for any reason. Sometimes, it’s beyond the limit of my power, and sometimes I just don’t think it’s a good idea.

Dareth: Which one is it this time?

Arrakore: Both. Definitely both.

(Suddenly, loud red lightning strikes, and the clouds get darker.)

Dareth: Huh? What the crackle?

(Rox and six Dragonian Trackers fly towards them.)

Arrakore: This cannot be good.

(As they get closer, Dareth tries to run away, but slips and falls.)

Dareth: I wish to be safe in my bed, right now! Still no!? Come on! Can you hear me?

(The Dragonians land in front of them, and Dareth runs away in fear.)

Arrakore: And who might you be?

Rox: I am Rox. Of the Forbidden Five. (Her eyes glow red.)

Arrakore: Never heard of you.

Rox: Do not worry. Soon the entire world will cower at the sound of my name, but that's not why I'm here. (She pulls out the handle of the Yellow Prismatic Blade out of its bag.) You will repair this blade.

Arrakore: (scoffs) You wish. I know exactly what this blade is, and what evil it can be used for.

(In the First Land, the ninja are examining the Blue Prismatic Blade.)

Lloyd: Does anyone have any clue what this thing can be used for?

(Sora shakes her head, and P.I.X.A.L. shrugs. Lloyd tries swinging the blade around, but nothing happens.)

(In the City of Temples, Kai is reading through The Sagas of the Eternal Sources as Nya walks in.)

Nya: Found anything about Prismatic Blades in there yet?

Kai: No. There should be an index or something, a table of contents. (He closes the book and slams it onto the table.) We should be out there looking for the Forbidden Five! Who knows what kind of damage they're doing now?

Nya: Look for them where?

Kai: I don't know, but I do know they aren't hiding in this book!

Nya: Whatever the Forbidden Five wanted with the sword is of grave importance.

Kai: "Grave" is probably right.

Nya: So we need to learn more about it. (Kai looks at her dumbfounded, and she starts to mimic his voice.) "You're so right, Nya! as per usual."

Kai: I would never say that.

(Nya takes the book from him and starts reading.)

Nya: Here! Prismatic Steel! Everything's in alphabetical order.

Kai: (stammers) Well, why didn't Roby tell me that?

Nya: According to this, if you look through a Prismatic Blade, it can reveal hidden souls around us. The blade was not forged in fire, but the resonance of soul energy.

Kai: Hidden souls? (shudders) Creep-tastic.

Nya: Get this- eons ago, there was this dragon named Thunderfang, who was desperate to become a Source Dragon. When all his attempts failed, he became enraged, bitter... and tried to destroy everything in his path. The only thing that defeated him was-

Kai: A giant comet! Quicksand! Heartburn.

Nya: Stop talking. The only thing that defeated Thunderfang was the Arc Dragon of Focus. Thunderfang, who they called the Shatter Dragon of Chaos, was then imprisoned, with chains that could only be cut by five Prismatic Blades. (gasps) This must be what the Forbidden Five wanted that blade for!

Kai: To free an ancient dragon of Chaos that tried to destroy the world? Don't like the sound of that. We gotta warn the other ninja.

(Back in Djinjago, two Dragonians are holding down Arrakore.)

Rox: You will repair this. (She holds up the hilt.)

Arrakore: I choose to grant wishes only to those whose need is pure.

Rox: If you won't help me by choice... (She pulls out a pyramid-shaped artifact, and Arrakore gasps.) Ah... you do know what this is. Once I trap you inside here, you will be my servant, and have no choice but to grant my wishes.

Arrakore: Never. (He uses his power to teleport away from the Dragonians' grasp, then tries to escape.)

Rox: Get him!

(Two of the other Dragonians run after Arrakore. They try to jump at him, but he teleports away before that can happen, appearing behind a nearby pillar.)

Arrakore: I'm still so weak...

(Rox gestures to the two remaining Dragonians, and they fly up to search from the air. Arrakore appears from behind the pillar.)

Arrakore: You're lucky the ninja aren't here. They would defeat you with ease. You're no match for my friends!

Rox: I think you mean the ninja are no match for us! Get him! (Two of the four Dragonians on the ground start running towards Arrakore.)

Arrakore: Really? The ninja have only saved the world... (He teleports away just as two of the Dragonians are about to land on him.) many times! You're nothing compared to them.

Row: How dare you! (The two Dragonians who flew into the air earlier land back down next to Arrakore.) I wish the ninja were here. Then you would see How easily I would crush them!

Arrakore: Your wish is yours to keep.

(While the ninja are still examining the Blue Prismatic Blade in the First Land, Sora and Riyu suddenly vanish.)

Lloyd: That was weird, right?

(In the City of Temples, Nya is looking through The Sagas of the Eternal Sources as Kai starts walking away.)

Kai: But how do we even find the others?

Nya: Maybe we can- (She vanishes.)

Kai: Maybe we can what, Nya? Don't leave me hanging- Nya? Nya?

(Sora and Riyu appear in Djinjago, quickly followed by Nya.)

Sora: Nya? What's going on here?

Nya: No clue. One minute I was-

Arrakore: Ninja! (A Dragonian tries to grab him by swooping down from the air, but he teleports away, leaving them crashing into the ground. The Dragonian next to them leaps at Arrakore, but he teleports again.) A little help here, please! (The Dragonians close in on him from all sides.)

Nya and Sora: Arrakore! (They put on their hoods.)

Nya: (takes out her spear) Ninja... go!

(On the island with rocky spires, Ras is training Arin. Arin tries doing Spinjitzu, but begins spinning out of control and falls to the ground.)

Ras: Sloppy. Embrace your anger. Find your strength!

Arin: (gets up) I'm trying.

Ras: You're failing. You're letting your emotions get the best of you.

Arin: I just found out my parents are dead.

Ras: And who is responsible for their deaths?

Arin: Master Wu. (He concentrates and performs a proper Spinjitzu tornado, smashing trough a pile of rocks.)

Ras: There. Your anger gives you strength. Power.

Arin: Are you certain you can bring my parents back?

Ras: Yes, but it will not be easy. The Merge can only be undone with power from all seven Source Dragons.

Arin: And how do we get that?

Ras: Each of the Source Dragons must heed the call of their of Icons, which can only be used once. That is how I summoned the Source Dragon of Energy, which Imperium helped me contain under their city, never knowing my ultimate goals. But Imperium was not ready to contain two dragons... hence I've been carrying the Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength with me all this time, but we need Icons for the other six, and ways to contain them. (He jumps up and smashes another pile of rocks with the Icon.) I had planned to create new Icons with the Elemental Powers I gathered at the Tournament, until the Forbidden Five betrayed me. Now we must find other ways to obtain the Icons we need.

Arin: If that will help bring my parents back, I'll help you. Whatever it takes.

(The ninja are fighting Rox's forces at Djinjago. Riyu does a loop in the air as he headbutts a Dragonian away. Sora jumps up to kick another flying Dragonian, which Riyu then pushes away with his head. Two Dragonians charge at Sora from both sides, slashing at her with their swords. She jumps into the air to dodge them and lands on their swords, which she uses to jump behind one of them and kick both down at the same time.)

Nya: (prepares her power) Happy to help, (She blasts Water at the Dragonians.) but... (Suddenly, a Dragonian jumps at her from the side, but she blocks their attack with her spear.) ...what's the deal here? (She kicks the Dragonian away.)

Arrakore: They wish for me to reassemble a shattered Prismatic Blade!

Nya: What? (Another Dragonian attacks her, but she blocks again.) I hope you refused! (She shoves the Dragonian away.)

Arrakore: No, we're fighting (Nya shoots a blast of Water.) because I agreed to do everything they wanted.

(Nya knocks away two Dragonians with Spinjitzu as Arrakore teleports away. A third Dragonian approaches her from behind, but Riyu drops Sora on them from above.)

Sora: A Prisma-what-now blade?

Nya: Prismatic Steel is a metal that looks like crystal. (The two of them knock down two Dragonians running at them, and Nya evades a sword that was thrown at her.) It's made of soul energy. (Sora backflips as she kicks away a Dragonian, and Nya fires another blast of Water.) Deeply wierd.

Sora: No way! We found one of those blades when we were looking for Arin! (A Dragonian slashes at her from behind with a sword, but she ducks under it and kicks them.) The Forbidden Five tried to get ours!

Nya: (She spins around as she kicks a Dragonian away.) You gather five of those blades, and you can unlock this big, bad news, world-killing Shatter Dragon of Chaos named Thunderfang! (She shoots two blasts of Water.)

Sora: Did I somehow not know you were a Prismatic Steel super-fan?

(A Dragonian flies in from behind Sora and kicks her to the ground. She rolls away as the Dragonian tries to hit her with their sword, then gets up and kicks them away. Sora then barely dodges the attack of another Dragonian and retreats towards Nya.)

Nya: I'm not. I just learned all that, like, three minutes ago! (She blasts away the Dragonian running at Sora.)

(As Nya and Sora are fighting, they see Rox chasing after Arrakore, who manages to hide from her behind a large pot for a moment before being discovered again. They manage to fight off the two Dragonians attacking them, and Nya blasts Rox away with Water.)

Nya: Looks like Rox is hoping wish magic can fix her blade.

Sora: Wish magic... (She jumps away from two Dragonians, and Riyu flies in and grabs her before tossing her towards Arrakore. As she is falling down, she grabs a flying Dragonian and slams them to the ground as she lands next to the Djinn.) Despite the circumstances, I gotta say, I love what you've done with the place.

Arrakore: I've been working very hard to try and restore Djinjago wish by wish.

Sora: Well, maybe I can help with that. I wish Rox and the Dragonians would disappear!

Nya: Brilliant!

Arrakore: Your wish is... too big for me to grant.

Sora: What? But-but-but I did the thing. I said "I wish".

Arrakore: I'm so sorry. Wish magic comes from Dinjago itself. (Nya and Riyu are fighting Rox's forces.) Though our realm is bigger than it once was, it's nowhere near its former glory. Bringing you here took almost all the strength I had. I can only do small wishes, maybe.

(Rox starts flying towards the two of them.)

Sora: I wish Rox's feet were stuck to the ground! (Arrakore grants the wish, and Rox gets stuck in front of them.) Ha! I wish you could see your face! (Arrakore starts granting the wish.) It's just an expression, never mind! (She sees Nya and Riyu fighting the Dragonians.) Nya! Riyu! Wishes work, just keep 'em small, and wierd, mostly cause thats fun for me.

Nya: (blasts away a Dragonian and lifts her spear behind her head to block the attack of another Dragonian that she then kicks away. She points to the dragonian she blasted at.) I wish this guy would be jolted by lightning!

(Lightning strikes the Dragonian, leaving them somewhat charred. They drop their swords and fall to the ground.)

Sora: (laughs) Nice!

(Another new part of Djinjago emerges from the ground as Nya dodges a Dragonian swooping at her. Sora gestures towards them.)

Sora: I wish he was as light as a feather!

(Before the Dragonian can attack Nya again, they start floating into the air, where Riyu tosses a Dragonian upwards by their sword before growling to Arrakore.)

Arrakore: One rubber ball coming up!

(The Dragonian launched into the air gets encased in a large clear rubber ball, which Riyu then starts playing with.)

Nya: Nice one, Riyu! (She runs to Arrakore.) How you holding up?

Arrakore: I'm a Djinn. This is my true purpose! Keep wishing!

Nya: (walks to a Dragonian running towards her) I wish this Dragonian had bones made of jelly! (She blocks and attack from the Dragonian, before her wish is granted and the Dragonian slumps forward.)

Sora: I wish this Dragonian's helmet was on backwards! (The helmet of the Dragonian chasing her suddenly flips around, and the Dragonian tries to grab onto it with their hands before she kicks them back.)

(Yet more new chunks of Djinjago emerge. Arrakore groans.)

Nya: We gotta scale back the wishing and do more ninjaing!

(The Dragonian in the rubber ball bounces at Nya, but Riyu pushes it away. Nya sweeps at the feet of the charred Dragonian with her spear and kicks it away as Riyu start to with the Dragonian with the reversed helmet and Sora tosses away one of the Dragonians unaffected by the wishes. Nya uses her spear to lauch Sora up towards Riyu and blasts away the wobbly Dragonian before attacking the charred Dragonian again. In the air, Sora has been grabbed by the Dragonian with a reversed helmet, and is trying to find anything that reacts to her power, unsuccessfully. She and the Dragonian plummet to the ground, and another Dragonian swoops onto her.)

Sora: There's no technology I can use to channel my Elemental Powers here! (She looks at Riyu, who kicks away the Dragonian in the rubber ball.) But I do have feet. (She kicks away the Dragonian on top of her and gets back up.)

(Meanwhile, Rox punches the ground twice to use Shatterspin, and breaks free from the ground. She flies towards Arrakore, as Nya knocks down two Dragonians, one of which had their helmet reversed and Riyu struggles against the wobbly Dragonian. Rox creeps behind the tired Arrakore.)

Rox: Niver-maste fo le ana-ton go so-lox pezotan menoo-gala poxel...

Arrakore: No! (He starts getting sucked into the device Rox is holding.)

Rox: ...guthero-vix da sempaste to!

Arrakore: No! (He dissapears into the object as it closes.)

(The ninja try to run toward Rox, but the Dragonian in the rubber ball, charred Dragonian and the Dragonian with a reversed helmet jump on top of them, while the wobbly Dragonian and another Dragonian help pin Riyu down.)

Arrakore: Let me out! I will never grant your wishes, no matter what you may do to me!

Rox: You are my prisoner. My servant. You must grant my wishes now. I wish you would repair my blade! (She pulls out the shards of the Yellow Prismatic Blade.)

Arrakore: Your wish is yours to keep.

(The shards of the blade start hovering, and reform into the shape of a Prismatic Blade.)

Sora: You're not getting out of here with Arrakore!

Nya: Or that Prismatic Blade! We know you plan to release Thunderfang!

Rox: And you three are gonig to stop me? (She grabs the blade out of the air.) Ha!

Nya: This isn't our first fight.

(The ninja get out from under the Dragonians and start running towards Rox.)

Rox: It's the first fight where I have the power of a Djinn on my side. I wish you would freeze!

Nya: Really? Is that the only trick- (The ninja suddenly stop.)

Rox: Ha ha! (She floats up.) I should just accept my victory and leave, or wish now for your doom, but this is too good. I wish for the ninja to admit that the Forbidden Five are their true superiors in every way.

Nya and Sora: The Forbidden Five are our true superiors in every way. (Riyu growls the same thing.)

Rox: (cackles) Too much fun. (She floats elsewhere.) I wish for the ninja to unfreeze, so that they may bow down before me. (The ninja walk before Rox and bow down.)

Sora: This is humiliating.

(Another part of Djinjago rises.)

Nya: I don't love it, but at least even these wishes help restore Arrakore's realm.

(More chunks of land emerge.)

Sora: A realm Arrakore's never going to be able to return to. (She noticed the top of the object Rox is holding whir around.) I don't think that's just a container, it's a timepiece!

Nya: So? You have an appointment you need to keep or something?

Sora: No, Nya, it's tech!

Nya: Oh... this is gonna be good.

Rox: Why didn't I think of imprisoning you sooner, Arrakore? This is pure bliss. Now... I wish for the ninja to get up... (The ninja get up, and Sora activates her powers.) ...and walk off the edge of the- (Sora starts to break the timepiece.) What? Impossible!

Arrakore: I'm free!

Rox: Uh... I wish you would-

Arrakore: You have no power over me, Rox!

Rox: Get him!

Nya: Get out of here, Arrakore! (The ninja start fighting the Dragonians.) Don't make me wish it.

(Arrakore teleports away from Rox attacking him and hides behind a pillar. Nya blasts Rox out of the air, and She falls to the ground and uses Shatterspin to send a wave of energy at Nya, but she dodges the attack and charges at Rox with Rising Dragon Technique, sending the nearby Dragonians flying away.)

Sora: (kicks away the Dragonian with a reversed helmet) Ha! Young ninja team for the win! (Riyu roars.)

(Nya approaches Rox, who is lying on the ground with the Prismatic Blade in frot of her. Rox quickly grabs the blade and gets up, as Nya gets ready to use her powers. Rox looks at the tired Dragonians.)

Rox: We got what we came for! Three of the blades are ours! Let us go!

(The Dragonians fly up into the air as Nya starts blasting Water at Rox. She deflects the blasts woth the Prismatic Blade before using Shatterspin to knock Sora and Riyu down. Nya dodges the attack, but is unable to do anything as Rox flies away after the five Dragonians. Arrakore appears on top of Riyu.)

Arrakore: They're gone? Oh, thank goodness. Boy, you have grown, little dragon.

Nya: Are you okay?

Arrakore: I will be. You saved me. Just like I knew you would.

Nya: Well, that battledefinitely helped you grow Djinjago a bit more.

Arrakore: I would have preferred if we'd grown the realm by granting wishes for kittens or something.

Sora: Ooh, we still could.

Nya: No.

Arrakore: (chuckles weakly) I still have enough energy to grant one more wish today. Please. Anything you want, you may have.

Sora: (whispers) Kittens, let's wish for kittens, come on!

Nya: Sora, really?

Sora: I'm kidding! Sort of.

Nya: Okay, what I'd really wish to know is where our missing friends are.

Arrakore: Ah, I wish I could tell you, but that is beyond my spent powers. I'm sorry.

Nya: You have nothing to be sorry for. You've done incredible things for us and your realm, and one day, you will restore Djinjago to what it once was.

Sora: No. You'll make it even better.

Nya: But is there a chance you could send us back to where you got us?

Arrakore: Hmm, now, that... I can do.

Sora: Can I say the thing?

Nya: Do the honors.

Sora: I wish we would all go back to join our friends.

Arrakore: Your wish is yours to keep.

(The ninja float up before disappearing. Another piece of Djinjago emerges.)

(In the First Land, Sora falls on top of Lloyd.)

Lloyd: Sora! Where have you been- (Riyu also falls on top of him.) Ow.

(Nya falls on top of Kai in the City of Temples.)

Kai: Ah! What the heck, Nya? You've been hiding this whole time just to prank attak me? Good job. I wish I'd thought of that. (Nya smiles.)

(Sora is explaining to the others what happened.)

Sora: ...and then Arrakore sent us back to where we came from.

Lloyd: So you had another run-in with Rox?

(Nya talks about the same the same thing with Kai.)

Nya: Yeah, and our problen with the Forbidden Five just got worse.

Sora: Nya knows why the Forbidden Five are collecting the Prismatic Blades.

Nya: They are definitely going to unlock Thunderfang, the Shatter Dragon of Chaos.

Sora: And they have three of the five blades to do it now. We have one.

Lloyd: So, where is the fifth blade?

Sora: No idea.

Kai: Sorry, Nya, but finding Jay will have to wait. We need to hunt down that fifth blade before the Forbidden Five does.

(Arin and Ras are preparing to leave the rocky island.)

Arin: Whew! Where are we going now?

Ras: You tell me.

Arin: Huh?

Ras: How did you and the ninja learn of the Source Dragon energy in the Dragon Cores?

Arin: There was this place in the World Forest. The Temple of the Dragon Cores.

Ras: Can you show me how to get there?

Arin: Yes.

(They get into their jet, and Ras pilots it away, unaware that Rouge is watching them from behind a rock.)
