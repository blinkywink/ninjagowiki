(At some point prior to the Merge, Lloyd, Zane, and Nya land in the courtyard of the Monastery of Spinjitzu using Spinjitzu.)

Past Lloyd: I take it you got the same message?

Past Nya: From Master Wu? Saying there'd be special training today?

(Cole and Kai land in the courtyard of the monastery using Spinjitzu.)

Past Cole: Yeah, we heard the same thing.

(Jay enters the courtyard yawning.)

Past Zane: I see you received Master Wu's message as well, Jay.

Past Jay: Uh, totally. I wasn't playing video games for so long, my legs went numb and I needed a walk. That message, which I absolutely got, is definitely why I'm here.

(Master Wu enters the courtyard.)

Past Wu: Excellent. It is time for an important lesson. Today, we are going to dance.

(Wu moves a gramophone next to him. The ninja groan.)

Past Lloyd: (groans) Not more "Spin Harmony."

Past Cole: Come on, Master Wu, you know I ran away from that dancing school my dad put me in when I was a kid.

Past Wu: Focus. Just like a warrior, a dancer is completely in tune with their body. Dance will only improve your fighting skills. Plus, you might have fun in the process.

(Wu turns on the gramophone and waltz music starts.)

Past Wu: Now, follow me. One, two, three, one, two, three.

(The ninja awkwardly try to waltz. Jay steps over to Nya.)

Past Jay: May I have this dance?

Past Nya: (chuckles) Sure.

(Jay and Nya begin to dance, waltzing around the courtyard. Jay twirls and then dips Nya.)

Past Jay: I will love you forever, Nya.

(Back at the City of Temples, Nya is staring after who she thinks is Jay.)

Nya: Jay! Jay!

(Nya started to run after him, brushing past people.)

Nya: Whoop! (The figure she is chasing shoves through a group and disappears.) Jay!

(Nya sees him again and runs up to him, taps his shoulder, but finds that it is a Munce with Jay's hair. She gasps and giggles nervously.)

Nya: Jay!

(Wyldfyre, Riyu, Arin, Sora, and Lloyd walking in the streets. Nya runs up to them.)

Nya: Lloyd! I saw Jay!

Lloyd: What? Where?

Nya: He went south, but I lost him in the crowd. Come on!

(Nya turns and runs in the direction she came from, and Lloyd follows. Lloyd and Nya stop running, and Nya continues to walk forward a few paces.)

Lloyd: Did he see you?

Nya: No, and I didn't see his face, but I'm sure it was him. I think. I don't know. Maybe I'm just seeing things. Jay's been missing since the Merge. I miss him so much.

Lloyd: (He places his hand on her shoulder.) We all do.

(They turn and walk in the direction they came from. The next morning at the tournament quarters, Arin is talking to Sora.)

Arin: I know Zant-Tanz. He doesn't have a cruel bone in his body! (He punches a wooden training dummy.) Actually, he doesn't have any bones in his body, but that's not the point.

Sora: I don't believe he was behind those attacks either, but who was?

Arin: My guess, the same person who's responsible for attacking the Matriarch, and his name rhymes with "paws." Ras, I'm talking about Ras.

Sora: Yeah, I gathered that, but Ras was also attacked last night.

Arin: Right. Man, this whole mystery-solving stuff is hard. My parents were so good at it! They'd probably solve this whole thing in a day.

Sora: Have you asked the other ninja for help?

Arin: No, Lloyd made this my mission. If I can't compete in the tournament, I'm determined to prove myself by solving this mystery. The safety of the Source Dragons is at stake here. (To Riyu) Come on Riyu, time to hunt for some clues.

(Riyu grunts and gets up. Arin leaves the sleeping quarters with Riyu following.)

Sora: You're not the only ones with a mystery to solve today.

(Before the Merge, a young Arin and his mother sitting on their swing at their old house reading Detective James.)

Arin's mother: In all these mystery books your dad and I are always reading, the first thing a detective does is retrace the steps of everyone involved. Why do you think they do that?

Past Arin: 'Cause it might lead to clues, to help the detective solve the case?

Past Arin's mother: That's right! (She closes Detective James and gives it to Arin.) Look at you, already a great little detective, Arin.

(Back in the present at the City of Temples, Arin closes his notebook. Riyu sniffs the ground as they walk)

Arin: The attacker must have come through here last night, Riyu. We need to try and retrace that bot's steps.

(Riyu squawks.)

Arin: (gasps) You find something?

(Riyu continues sniffing and squawks again.)

Arin: You found the same scent as last night? If we can figure out where the attacker came from, maybe we can figure out who sent them.

(Riyu continues sniffing and walking with Arin walking behind him. They turn into an alley and meet a wall.)

Arin: Dead end. (Arin notices a symbol of a dragon on the wall.) Wait. Some kind of symbol scratched into the wall.

Arin: I wonder if this is a clue to whoever sent the bot? Let me copy it down. (He sketches the symbol in his notebook. Frak enters the alley.)

Frak: Arin! What are you up to?

Arin: Oh, Frak, hey! Riyu and I were just, uh, well, (chuckles) It's complicated ninja business, really.

Frak: (laughs) Do you know how cool it is you get to say stuff like that? My master's been training me hard, but I'm taking a break to grab a pie. Interested?

(Riyu's stomach growls and Arin and Riyu look at it.)

Arin: I might know a dragon who'd be up for a pie, or ten. Let's go!

(The three of them exit the alley. Arin opens his notebook again and glances at the symbol he copied. At the pie stand, Arin takes a slice of Snowberry Pie from a Merlopian vendor, while Riyu slurps in the background.)

Frak: I can't believe what that Zant-Tanz guy tried to pull last night. (Arin and Frak start to walk away from the pie stand.) Attacker bots? Scary, right? (Riyu growls, shakes his head and sends his pie tin flying, growls, and then follows Arin and Frak.)

Arin: I don't think it was Zant-Tanz behind it. He's not the aggressive type. The guy's frightened by sharp corners on tables.

(Riyu tries to eat the slice of pie Frak has in his hand.)

Frak: But no one else could have done it.

(Riyu sniffs the air behind Frak and growls in disgust.)

Frak: So, you gotta tell me, how did you hook up with the ninja? And do you freak out about it every day?

Arin: Mm, pretty much. Sora and I met Lloyd while we were trying to save Riyu here from dragon hunters. It's been a dream come true, but it's also a lot of work. Training can be hard.

Frak: Tell me about it. You have to meet my master. I was so clueless and scared by my powers, and he really helped me out. (He finishes his pie.)

Arin: He sounds awesome.

(They arrive at a square, and see Wyldfyre approaching from another direction.)

Arin: Wyldfyre?

(Wyldfyre turns and waves at Arin.)

Wyldfyre: Oh, hey, Arin, (laughs) didn't see you there. I'm just doing some mental exercises on my own... uh, definitely not looking for someone or anything.

(Bleckt and Roby approach Wyldfyre, Frak, Riyu, and Arin.)

Roby: This whole tournament is tight, Uncle. You're a control freak. (He takes a selfie with an unhappy Bleckt.)

Bleckt: Your idea of control is chaos, Roby. I don't like it.

(Wyldfyre gets excited.)

Wyldfyre: Oh! (She giggles and runs over to Roby and nearly falls over.) Hi, Roby. I was just wandering here randomly, doing my own thing and not looking for you at all, (She nearly falls again but Roby catches her.) but since I'm seeing you now, I just wanted to say I can't wait to help make your tournament a success.

Roby: Oh, uh, thanks. Uh, Wyldfyre, isn't it?

Wyldfyre: (gasps)That's my name! It's a dragon name. I was raised by dragons, but I'm not a dragon, as you can see. Why am I still talking? Stop talking!

Roby: My uncle and I were, uh, just about to check out the Games Plaza before the first event. Wanna come with? (He nudges her.)

Wyldfyre: Yes, definitely! (changes her tone) I mean, I have a lot of things I could do, but they can wait, let's go!

(Wyldfyre runs after Bleckt and Roby, while Arin, Frak, and Riyu stare after her.)

Arin: Am I reading into things a bit too much here?

Frak: That that girl has a huge crush on Roby? She might as well be wearing a T-shirt that says "I heart Roby." (He makes a heart with his hands.)

Arin: Having a crush is the most normal thing I've ever seen her do.

(Riyu nods in agreement.)

Frak: Hey, I better head off to meet my team. Check ya later? (He nudges Arin.)

Arin: Oh, definitely! Go compete!

(Frak waves at Arin and walks away, and Arin waves after him.)

Arin: (sighs) Must be nice having a power to compete with.

(Arin walks away. Riyu glares and huffs in the direction Frak left, then follows Arin.)

(Cole is walking through the city streets. He sees a fruit stand and walks over.)

Cole: Hmm.

(He reaches out to pick up a piece of fruit, and his hand brushes another hand. He looks up.)

Cole: Huh?

Geo: Huh?

Cole: Geo? What? You're here? (The two embrace.)

Geo: My tournament invite showed up after you left. I figured you'd wind up here, too, so I came.

Cole: This is amazing! Come on, I'm checking out the city before the competition starts.

(Cole and Geo run off together. They stop near a tall white mech in front of the Temple City Museum.)

Cole: Whoa!

(Geo grabs his wrist and pulls him closer to read the plague below the mech.)

Geo: Check it out, apparently this mech belonged to a past Elemental Master of Earth. Someone you knew?

Cole: Huh, Elemental Powers are usually passed down from parents. I got mine from my mom. Maybe this was hers. Wow.

(Geo smiles at Cole and looks back at the mech, and puts a hand on Cole's shoulder.)

(Sora is running and hiding behind various objects on Foundation Street. She peers out from behind a crate at "Jordana" and follows her. "Jordana" turns around and looks behind her and sees nothing. She turns around and sees Sora facing her.)

Sora: Last night at the banquet, why did you ask me for my help?

"Jordana": What are you talking about? I did no such thing.

Sora: But you did. How could you not remember it?

"Jordana": If you think playing mind games is going to help you ninja win the tournament, you're about to be proven brutally wrong. (She pushes past Sora, who stares after her. "Jordana" nearly runs into a citizen, and walks past Nokt, who speaks to her.)

Nokt: I saw that. Does she suspect something?

("Jordana" and Nokt glare in Sora's direction. Sora hides and tries to listen.)

"Jordana": I don't know. We must be careful.

Sora: (She hides and peers around the column.) What are you up to, Jordana?

(Sora sees Frak run up to "Jordana" and Nokt and wave to them. She gasps.)

Sora: Frak? He's with them?

(The three of them start to walk in Sora's direction, and she runs away.)

(At the tournament quarters, the ninja are traning. Arin pulls out his notebook and opens it. He and Riyu look at the symbol he wrote down earlier. Sora runs in.)

Sora: (panting) Arin! You're not going to believe this.

Arin: That they have Merlopian cream pies here? I know! I had three of them.

Sora: What? No! Your old friend Frak, he's on Ras's team.

Arin: No way, that can't be true.

(Sora walks over and sits down next to Arin.)

Sora: I saw it with my own eyes.

(Riyu growls.)

Arin: You think so too, Riyu? But Frak is such a great guy! (He jumps down and backs up, facing Riyu and Sora.) You must've seen something else and got confused.

Sora: I didn't. And I wouldn't lie to you, Arin.

(Lloyd enters and addresses everyone in the room.)

Lloyd: We better head over to the Games Plaza. First event's coming up, and Roby, trying to make everything "as exciting as possible," won't tell anyone who's competing until the event itself.

(Arin, Riyu, Zane, and Cole exit with Lloyd. Sora turns back and looks at Nya, who is kneeling before a shrine with Kai's and Jay's portraits. As she watches, Nya gets up and walks away.)

(Kai and Bonzle are walking upside-down throughout the Nether-Space. They stop and hear the Forbidden Five speaking.)

Rōningasa hat member: Why the delay? What's taking so long?

(Kai and Bonzle hide in a doorway where they can see the remaining Forbidden Five in front of a red web of light and gathered around one of their own in a chair, who is glowing white.)

Rōningasa hat member: My patience is wearing thin.

(Kai notices the glowing member.)

Kai: (whispers) You know how one of the Five made it out of here, but we've only ever seen three in here? Guess that sleeping one is the last of them.

Bonzle: Sounds like they're talking to someone. But who? And how?

Rōningasa hat member: Make haste. The sooner we are free, the sooner our brutal dominance can continue. (The two other members of the Forbidden Five are watching as the rōningasa hat member speaks. The web suddenly vanishes, and the three conscious members walk away.)

Kai: They found a way to talk to someone on the outside who could free them?

Bonzle: We gotta check it out.

(Back in the City of Temples, the Elemental Masters are training. Roby and Bleckt are walking past them.)

Roby: Hey, hey, looking good, looking good! All engagement is good engagement. (He sees Mord practicing making bubbles.) All right, bubbles! Remember, play to the crowds!

Bleckt: (He briefly watches Teelk make soundwaves.) The tournament is and always has been about the honor of Elemental battle. It is not "hot."

Roby: You're not hot.

Bleckt: This is not how your father ran it.

Roby: That's. The. Point.

(Wyldfyre walks past them.)

Wyldfyre: Hi again, Roby! Hi!

(Wyldfyre keeps walking, and Cinder bumps into her intentionally.)

Wyldfyre: Oh! (She glares at Cinder.)

Cinder: (sarcastically) Oops. Sorry.

Wyldfyre: That was on purpose, Cinder!

Cinder: (Crosses his arms) So? What are you gonna do about it? You and your little ninja friends gonna cry?

(The other ninja hear Wyldfyre and turn around.)

Wyldfyre: Keep talking and you'll be the one crying, tears of fire!

(Roby runs up and gets between them.)

Roby: Hey, hey, hey! Calm it down!

(Wyldfyre growls and backs up to where Lloyd and Nya are standing. Nya puts a hand on Wyldfyre's arm.)

Roby: Tournament rules, remember? Any battle outside the games will lead to expulsion!

(Wyldfyre growls again as Nya pushes her away from Cinder, who waves after her. Wyldfyre, Nya, and Lloyd approach the rest of the team, who gather around Lloyd as he addresses them.)

Lloyd: Remember, we aren't here just to win. We're competing 'cause we believe whoever's threatening the Source Dragons is here to win and gain control of all Elemental Powers. The fate of the entire Merged Lands is in our hands.

Wyldfyre: Yeah, you should all just use Rising Dragon Technique from the start. Plow through everyone. (She pounds a fist into her palm.)

Lloyd: No, Wyldfyre, this is a Tournament of Elements. Rising Dragon comes from Spinjitzu and should only be used to defend oneself.

Cole: Like if Ras' team uses Shatterspin?

Lloyd: Exactly. The Motion Source Dragon wanted us to compete honorably, and that's what we're gonna do.

(Lloyd puts his hand in the middle of the circle, and the team adds their hands.)

All: Ninja, go! (They raise their hands over their heads.)

(The tournament starts.)

Roby: Welcome, all you peeps, to the starting event of the first Roby-certified Tournament of the Sources. (Roby is shown to be on a floating machine with a selfie stick and phone.) And this first game, we're kicking it old-school. (He flies over the crowd. The crowd cheers.) One-on-one, arena combat, no holds barred!

(Bleckt stands next to a large bell.)

Bleckt: In the grand tradition of the Tournament of the Sources, honor and sportsmanship are our—

(Roby interrupts Bleckt with a groan.)

Roby: Ugh, you're making this sound like boring old-school, Uncle Bleckt, listen. (He is shown on several large screens as he continues addressing the crowd.) Our first competitor is cold as ice but computes twice as nice, Zane, the Titanium Ninja!

(Zane is shown on the screens, and his teammates cheer him on.)

Sora and Arin: Come on, Zane. You got this.

Nya: You can do it!

(Zane smiles confidently and walks to the arena.)

Roby: Facing off against Zane, he's dark, mysterious, and oh-so-dangerous, the Master of Reflex himself, Zur!

(Zur is shown on the screens. Zane hops back and forth as Zur walks up and faces him.)

Zane: I am limber. I am loose. (Zur zips back and forth with a red energy glowing around him and Zane's face falls.) I am in trouble.

(Zane looks over at Lloyd.)

Lloyd: Don't worry, Zane! He's got fast reflexes, but ice can slow down anything!

(Zane looks back and puts his mask up. Zur and Zane face each other.)

Roby: (Still addressing crowd) Honored guests and Masters! Who's ready for the epicness to begin? Yeah!

(Bleckt rings the bell and the crowd cheers. Zane runs forward and tries to punch Zur, but Zur dodges his punches. He tries multiple times to land a blow but fails each time. He summons ice and his hands glow white. He shoots ice at Zur, who dodges it every time. Zur tries to land a blow on Zane, but Zane creates a wall of ice. Zur punches through it and breaks it. The crowd cheers as Zane finally manages to hit Zur with ice, knocking him over. Zur lands on his back, but zips away before Zane can land another blow. All the ninja groan as they watch Zane. Zur runs around Zane quickly, but Zane stops him by blasting ice at his foot. Right before Zane is about to hit Zur, the ice suddenly melts and Zur intercepts his blow, knocking him out of the arena. Zane makes a noise of pain and the crowd cheers and applauds. Roby flies down.)

Roby: We have a victor! Zur wins!

(Zane gets to his feet and walks back into the arena.)

Roby: What an incredible battle! Intense!

(Roby flies up to where Bleckt is standing and takes the Elemental Cup from him and flies back down.)

Roby: Time for the first Elemental transition! Behold, the Element Cup! Now, Zane, if you would. (He holds the cup and takes Zane's power into the cup. Zane watches sadly and stares at his hands.)

Roby: Like every game that has gone before, the winner gets the Elemental Power of the defeated to carry into their next competition.

(Roby gives Zane's power to Zur, who shoots ice into the air. The crowd cheers. Zane walks back to his teammates. Lloyd and Nya immediately step up to him.)

Sora: Did Zane's ice melt unnaturally quickly? Looked quick to me.

Wyldfyre: Told you ya shoulda used Rising Dragon.

Lloyd: Maybe Wyldfyre's right.

(Zane puts a hand on Lloyd's shoulder.)

Zane: She is not. Your leadership is sound, Lloyd. It is not the Ninja way to use excessive force to fight unfairly. That was a noble battle that I unfortunately lost, putting our mission in jeopardy. Now I can only spectate until my powers return after the final game.

Nya: You did the best you could, Zane. No one said this was gonna be easy. I wonder who's up next?

(Roby starts announcing again.)

Roby: Please welcome to the arena, she's slick, she's cool, and she's well-hydrated! Nya, Elemental Master of Water! (Nya is shown on the screens.)

Nya: I had to ask. (She starts walking forward and stretching.)

Nya: Get psyched, Nya. Ready to go, ready to win.

(She looks at Ras's team, the members of which are clustered around someone behind them.)

Lloyd: (Steps up to stand beside Nya.) Looks like Ras might be coaching your opponent.

Nya: Whoever it is, I can take 'em.

(Nya walks down the steps. Ras's team steps aside, and a figure straightens up and turns around, revealing himself to be Jay.)

Nya: Jay?

(Jay walks forward and glares at her. Roby hovers between Nya and Jay.)

Roby: The most shocking competitor in the tournament, Jay Walker, Elemental Master of Lightning! (Jay is shown on the screens.)

(Jay summons lightning while the crowd cheers. Geo is in the crowd, looking surprised.)

Nya: Jay! I knew I saw you. (She runs forward.)

Jay: What are you talking about? I've never seen you before in my life.

(Cole steps forward and speaks to the rest of the team.)

Cole: Jay could never forget Nya, or any of us! Is that some kind of fake Jay? Some loser in a Jay wig?

Lloyd: We don't know what happened to Jay after he disappeared in the Merge.

(They look at Ras, who steps forward and smiles.)

Ras: I knew the ninja would never see this coming.

("Jordana" looks at Sora, who looks at her questioningly. "Jordana" looks away. Jay pulls on his hood, while Nya still looks shocked. Roby starts announcing again.)

Roby: It's time now for– (A tower rises out of the floor of the arena and a floating dagger appears at the top.) The Tower Battle! The first to reach the top of the tower and get that glorious blinged-out dagger will be hey-o, winner!

(Geo still looks surprised. Jay and Nya walk into the arena and face each other.)

Nya: Lloyd, I can't fight him!

Lloyd: I don't think we have a choice.

(Bleckt rings the bell. Jay prepares to fight.)

Nya: Jay! It's me, Nya!

Jay: Is that supposed to mean something to me, lady? (He runs forward and kicks her down.) Hiya!

(The ninja gasp. Nya gets up again.)

Nya: Jay!

(Jay attacks again and she jumps aside. He keeps attacking and she keeps dodging.)

Lloyd: (Calling to Nya) Nya, focus on the dagger!

(Nya looks where Lloyd is pointing up. Jay attacks again, and Nya dodges. She tries to strike him with her fist glowing, but fails.)

Jay: You had a clear shot and didn't take it! Are you a terrible ninja?

Nya: I don't wanna hurt you. We care too much for each other!

Jay: Speak for yourself, lady. (He summons lightning and attacks again.) Ooh-ah!

(Nya blocks his attacks again and again.)

Jay: My master, Ras, told me you'd lie to me, try to use mind games on me. You won't shake my confidence! I don't believe a word you say!

(Nya pushes him away from her.)

Nya: Ras is the one lying to you! You're a ninja who disappeared after the Merge!

(Jay looks surprised and indignant.)

Nya: Jay, you're the love of my life.

Jay: Ugh, as if! Ras told me the truth. I was a great warrior, fighting alongside my master in the Wyldness. (At some point after the Merge, Jay is in an office sleeping in a chair with a video game controller. A tube drops a scroll onto Jay, waking him up. Jay falls over.) When the Merge happened, I lost my memory and ended up in some place called the Administration.

(Back in the present, the ninja are listening to Jay's story from the sidelines.)

Arin: The Administration? Jay is Agent Walker?

(Jay summons lightning again and attacks Nya, who dodges.)

Jay: I escaped from there, then Lord Ras found me and rescued me. (He kicks Nya and sends her to the ground. She cries out in pain.) I owe it to Ras to win this tournament, whatever it takes. (He pulls out a Wolf Mask.)

Nya: Jay, no! Don't do that!

(Jay flips back his hood and raises the mask.)

Arin: He's gonna shatter his goodness!

Lloyd: I'm afraid he already has.

(Jay puts on the mask and Ras pulls out the Gong and Mallet of Shattering. He strikes the gong, turning the mask red and disappearing, causing Jay's eyes to go red. Geo looks worried, and Bleckt looks surprised. Nya gets back up.)

Jay: Ooh-ah! (He rushes forward and strikes Nya with lightning, sending her into the air. He then jumps up and smashes her into the ground. Jay then kicks Nya with a jumping 360 degree roundhouse kick and sends her crashing into the side of the tower. Jay does Shatterspin, and the ninja look shocked and worried, while Ras is smiling, pleased at the results. Nya gets back up.)

Nya: You don't leave me any choice, Jay.

(She puts her bandana on and uses Rising Dragon Technique. Geo is watching in wonder. Nya hits Jay and knocks him backwards, knocking him to the very edge of the arena. Geo cheers, as do the ninja.)

Wyldfyre: Wow, didn't a super-smart and good-looking ninja here say Rising Dragon Technique would be epic?

(Jay starts to get up, Nya glares at him. She then looks up at the tower and starts to climb. Jay chases after her shooting lighting. She dodges and gets to the top first, and takes the dagger. She holds it high, while the audience cheers. Wyldfyre, Arin, and Sora cheer, but the other ninja look concerned. Ras growls.)

Roby: We have a winner! Nya, Master of Water! Woot-woot! Spa-lash!

(The tower folds back into the arena floor and Nya jumps down. Roby extracts Jay's powers into the cup and gives them to Nya.)

Roby: The thrills keep coming! Jay is now without his Elemental Powers, and Nya now controls water and lightning! Show us, Nya!

(Nya glances at Jay and dejectedly shoots lightning into the sky. The crowd cheers. Roby flies off. Jay gives Nya an angry look, and she steps towards him. Jay turns and walks away and approaches his team.)

Ras: I didn't rescue you so you could disappoint me, Jay. I do not tolerate failure.

Jay: I'm sorry, Lord Ras.

Ras: Sorry is not good enough. (He, Cinder, and "Jordana" turn away from Jay and leave. Jay looks angry, and Nya steps up a little bit behind Jay.)

Sora: She won. We're doing what the Source Dragon asked of us. So why does this just feel empty?

Lloyd: Because we might have won the match, but we lost Jay.

(Nya approaches Jay.)

Nya: Jay, I know you lost your memory, but you need to know the truth. I love you. I will always love you.

(She tries to touch him but he shoves her away, his eyes glowing red due to the corruption of Shatterspin.)

Jay: And I will always hate you. (He walks away leaving Nya hurt. Lloyd, Wyldfyre and Cole are shocked. Jay rejoins Ras, and Ras smiles cruelly at Nya. He then leaves and Nya bows her head.)

(In the Nether-Space. Kai and Bonzle jump down from their hiding place and approach the Forbidden Five member sleeping in the chair.)

Bonzle: Is she asleep, or...

(Kai walks in front of the Forbidden Five member and waves his hand in her face. She doesn't respond.)

Kai: She's breathing, but she's totally out of it or something. Hello? Boo! (He waves at her and tries to get her to wake up.) Nope, she's out.

(Bonzle looks at Kai's arm.)

Bonzle: What is that? Lint?

(Kai sees glowing silver dots on his arm.)

Kai: Huh, must be scales from Riyu, (Behind them, the red web of light forms in the Nether-Space. Kai flashes back to getting sucked into the portal.) from when he tried to keep me from getting sucked into the portal.

(Kai and Bonzle look behind them and see the red web of light.)

Bonzle: Those scales, you have a physical connection to someone outside of here. Maybe we can communicate with Riyu.

(Fireworks are being fired off in front of the Temple of Tournaments. Nya is sitting down and looking sad, while the ninja are either watching her or the fireworks.)

Kai: Riyu?

(Riyu hears Kai's voice and looks around.)

Kai: Riyu, can you hear us? It's Kai and Bonzle. Hello?

(Riyu squawks.)
