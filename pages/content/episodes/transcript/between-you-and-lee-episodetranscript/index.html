(A boat approaches the island with rocky towers and a man on the island places a box with a mushroom design on the ground. A female captain and a Merlopian appear on the boat.)

Captain: (To the man.) Hey, Lee! Got another shipment?

Lee: You bet! The best candy mushrooms I know how to nurture.

(A Merlopian takes the box of candy mushrooms to the boat and another delivers a cart with stacked boxes in return.)

Captain: Your supplies. Thanks again for the trade, Lee. (She looks around and sees how deserted the island is.) You sure you don't want to come with us? Meet some people, maybe? Seems pretty lonely out here.

Lee: I don't mind being alone. I'm my own best friend.

(Lee takes the supplies and tries to climb the hill, but he can't, so he makes a copy of himself to help him. When they reache the top of the hill, another Lee appears from behind a rock and presses a secret button, and two Lees come out and take the supplies on their way to enter. A portal appears.)

Lee: Awesome! Sounds like new neighbors. (The other Lees go with him.) It's been a while.

(Nya, Wyldfyre, Lloyd and Jay appear from the portal.)

Lee: (To them.) Welcome, friends!

Lees: Welcome to the Land of Lee!

(Jay and the Ninja are confused when they see the Lees.)

(The middle Lee waves to them and approaches with the other Lees.)

Wyldfyre: That portal must have messed me up. I'm seeing double. (She looks at the Lees.) Triple. (She look at the Lees again.) Five-le?

Jay: Follow my lead, no matter how weird it gets.

Lee: Hi, everybody! I'm Lee.

Lloyd: And who are those guys?

Lee: That's also me!

Lee: I'm here.

Lee: And here.

Lee: And here!

Lees: I'm all over the place!

Nya: You're all clones?

Lee: Nope!

Lee: Lee is just Lee!

Lee: I'm one person who happens to have more than one body.

Lee: I'm constant-Lee multitasking!

(Jay and the Ninja are confused by the pun.)

Lee: But enough about Lee. Who are you?

Lee: Probably four separate individuals with your own ideas and dreams, am I right?

Wyldfyre: yeah, we're Nin--

Lloyd: (He interrupts her.) Nincompoops, for not introducing ourselves sooner! I'm Doyll Donmagar, and this is my little sister, um, Fyldwyre.

Wyldfyre: Huh? Oh! Yes! That is exactly my name, Fyldwyre. It is a normal name, and I was given it at birth, so I have had it my whole life.

Lee: (He shakes her hand.) Nice to meet you, Fyldwyre.

(Another Lee shakes Lloyd's hand and then they look at Jay and Nya.)

Jay: Name's Strong McCoolGuy. This is my wife, whose name, uh, I've forgotten.

Nya: (She fake laughs and punches Jay in his left arm.) Sorry, he's kidding. My husband has an awful sense of humor. Just awful. My name is Maya. Maya... McCoolGuy.

Lee: I love new people!

Lee: Nice to meet you, Mrs. McCoolGuy. (He shakes Nya's hand.)

Lee: Mr. McCoolGuy. (He extends his hand to Jay.)

Jay: I-- It's Count McCoolGuy, actually.

Lee: Oh! Pardon me, your countship! (He and the other Lees bow to Jay, much to Nya's annoyance.)

Jay: Pardon granted, uh, but what happened to us? The last thing I remember, there was a big rip in the sky, then everything went dark, and we woke up here.

Lee: Don't freak out, okay? But the bad news is a while back, every single realm was suddenly destroyed in an event called "The Merge."

Wyldfyre: That's not what the Mer--

Jay: (He interrupts her.) Wow! Total bummer to hear, Lee.

Lee: I know, right? But don't you fret, there's good news. You all must've been pushed into what's called an "interstitial space bubble." It kept you safe!

Lee: Lots of Non-Lees have returned from space bubbles, so you'll have many people to hang out with, and will never, ever be lonely!

Wyldfyre: Uh... what people? (She looks around and sees how deserted the Land of Lee is and notices the gravestones) You don't mean...?

Lee: Let me show you!

(Two Lees place the supplies in the elevator inside the rock and enter it, along with the other Lees, Jay and the Ninja. The rock closes and the elevator descends.)

Nya: (To Lee.) So, everyone lives in, what, an underground bunker of some kind?

Lee: Yeah. The fun kind.

(They look out of the elevator and see an artificial neighborhood inside a large cavern.)

Jay: All this is down here? Who knew?

Lee: I built it myselves.

Lee: The hardest part was the mechanical sun.

(Everyone looks at the mechanical sun.)

Lee: On the surface, it can be pretty dreary, but it's always sunny down here!

(The elevator arrives at the floor and then Lloyd and Nya exit it. Two Lees approach and take the supplies from the elevator and another Lee, riding a Mech Dragon, takes the supplies and waves. Everyone else exits the elevator and sees that the artificial neighborhood is inhabited by Lees and other people of various species.)

Lees: Friends, an announcement! The Community of Lee has some new neighbors, and you know what that means...

Lees and others: New neighbor orientation!

Jay: (To the Ninja.) Buckle up.

(The lights go out and a spotlight appears on Lee.)

Lee: Can you handle this?

(The Lees continue dancing.)

Lee(s): ♪ Your friends and family Might re-emerge ♪ ♪ From super spacey bubbles Powered by cosmic surge ♪ ♪ But even if they never do ♪ ♪ A jet pack race Is waiting just for you! ♪

(Two Lees fly by jet pack.)

Lee: ♪ Our fake sky Gleams so bright ♪

Lee: Lovely day!

Lee: ♪ Endless sunshine Day and night! ♪

Lee: Get your shades! (He and another Lee put on sunglasses.)

Lee: ♪ Mech Dragon rides are free ♪

Lee: How prompt!

Lee: Oh, lovely!

Woman 2: Sublime!

Lloyd: Um... ♪ Rhyme ♪

Lees: ♪ Rhyme! ♪

Lee: Hoedown!

Lee: Yee-haw!

Lee: ♪ In the Land of Lee ♪ ♪ All you want is free ♪ ♪ Stay and play In our ice cream hotels ♪

Lee: Hoo, doggy!

Lee(s): ♪ In the Land of Lee ♪ ♪ Playgrounds under the sea ♪ ♪ Hear this Lee Totally rock out On the bells! ♪

(Lee rings the bells with sticks.)

Lee(s): ♪ The air outside Will make you sick ♪ ♪ Breaths are short I report ♪ ♪ And the haze Is crazy thick ♪ ♪ Why wander out When your friends are near? ♪ 'Cause inside ♪ ♪ Bona fide stand-up comics Bring the cheer! ♪

(People go to see Lee perform stand-up.)

Nya: (She speaks quietly to her friends.) Why is he doing this?

Lee: (To the people.) What's the deal with the Merge? Am I right?

(People laugh.)

Wyldfyre: (To Nya.) Uh, 'cause his vibe is fierce!

Nya: No, why is Lee lying and saying the Merge destroyed the realms and made the air toxic?

Jay: Guess he's just trying to keep these people here for some reason.

Lee: ♪ Time for the big finish! ♪

Lee: This is my favorite part!

Lee: Here we go!

Lee(s): ♪ In the Land of Lee ♪ ♪ Look around you see ♪ ♪ All your happiness Can be found ♪ ♪ Happiness can be found! ♪ ♪ When you go Way, way underground ♪ ♪ In the Land of Lee... ♪ ♪ Take a good look around ♪ ♪ In the Land of Lee... ♪ ♪ At my glorious town! ♪ ♪ 'Cause your heart Will resound ♪ ♪ With the happiest sound ♪ ♪ Every joy will abound ♪ ♪ When you go underground ♪ ♪ In the Land of Lee! ♪ ♪ With me! ♪ ♪ And me! ♪ ♪ And me! ♪ ♪ And me! ♪ ♪ 'Cause me Me! (The song ends and all the Lees breath heavily.)

Lloyd: Um... Thanks for having us?

Lee: They loved it! The new neighbors loved it! I knew they would.

(The Lees surround Jay and the Ninja and celebrate.)

Nya: (She speaks quietly to Jay.) You did tell us this place was weird.

(At the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Rontu is training Riyu, and Frak is trying to pass a part of the obstacle course, but fails at the final part and falls in front of Kai and Cole.)

Cole: Ouch. Ooh. I think someone could use a teacher.

Kai: What about two teachers?

Rontu: Good, your skills are improving, Riyu. Now try Spinjitzu!

Cole: Remember, focus on your target. You've got this, Frak.

(Frak and Riyu attempt to perform Spinjitzu, but end up destroying part of the training course. Mr. Frohicky comes out of the Monastery.)

Mr. Frohicky: What-what-what? All this damage!

Rontu: Apologies, Mr. Frohicky.

Cole: We were just trying to train--

Mr. Frohicky: As the official Assistant Keeper of the Monastery, I insist you get out so I can fix all this.

Cole: But to train Frak, we need to--

Mr. Frohicky: Find someplace else to go!

Cole: Come on, guys. (He helps Frak to get up.) Let's go to the Land of Lost Things. Plenty of room there. Plus, Geo's learning to bake, so there might be some practice cakes laying around.

(Cole, Kai and Frak start to leave.)

Rontu: Hmm... (To Riyu.) To not upset the bossy frog-man, we must continue elsewhere.

(Riyu says something to Rontu.)

Rontu: Hmm, yes, a junkyard might work. Can you lead us there?

(Riyu agrees and he and Rontu fly out of the monastery, leaving Mr. Frohicky to clean up the damage in the courtyard.)

(Wyldfyre drinks a cup of tea in a house.)

Wyldfyre: Never thought I'd like a lava-less cave, but this one's not bad.

Lloyd: The whole vibe weirds me out. I can't believe Lee just gave us this "starter house."

Jay: We can't all live in ancient monasteries! I mean, what's up with that, anyway? If it's a monastery, where are all the monks? Are you the monks?

(Jay's question irritates Nya and then two neighbors wave to them from afar.)

Man 1: Welcome, new neighbors!

(They wave back.)

Nya: (She sighs.) Ah! My arm's getting tired from waving at everyone who wave to us. Why are they so happy? Does Lee have them mind-controlled?

Jay: Nope! (He drinks a cup of tea.)

Wyldfyre: How do you know?

Jay: Back at the Administration, we thought all this happiness was strange, too, so we secretly scanned for unlicensed mind-control tech. Nothing. No magical manipulation. No spirit possession. Nothing strange being chemically added to the air, water, or food.

Lloyd: So why's everyone like this?

Jay: As far as we could tell, everyone here is happy because they actually like it here, so the Administration left Lee and the people down here alone.

Nya: But the neighbors think they can't leave. Lee's "happy little community" is built on lies.

Jay: The Administration isn't a police force. They're a bureaucracy. Lying doesn't require a license or a permit, so they don't care. In fact, they lie too. Tons.

(Everyone is silent for a few seconds as they hear Jay say this until Lloyd breaks the silence.)

Lloyd: Uh, let's focus on the mission. Arin's mom and dad. If they've been told there's no world outside, that would explain why they never came looking for Arin.

Wyldfyre: Why don't we just ask Lee where they are? He seems friendly enough.

Jay: That idea wins the stupid award for stupidest idea ever to stupid.

Wyldfyre: Okay, Count McCoolGuy. (She drinks more tea.)

Jay: If Lee figures out we're not who we say we are, he'll boot us right out. If we're gonna find Arin's parents, Lee can't know about it.

Lloyd: So we act like good neighbors, split up, and do some community events. (He picks up a tablet, stands up and fiddles with it.) Ask questions on the sly.

Wyldfyre: Just call me SlyFyre!

(At the Kreel's Junkyard, Riyu destroys the trash while Rontu watches.)

Rontu: Better! Spinjitzu is half mental. Feel it in your body and your mind!

(Riyu jumps, spins in the air, and kicks a container, causing a rusty bathtub to fall off of it. He then runs towards the bathtub and kicks it with his tail, causing it to hit the debris and get crushed.)

Rontu: Riyu... who did you say owned this junkyard? Because they might not be too happy with us.

(Lloyd approaches a house.)

Lloyd: Hi, uh, neighbors. I heard that the local book club meets here.

Woman 3: Yepperoni! Doyll, right? Join what promises to be a lively discussion.

Lloyd: Oh, thank you! My friends Jenny and Barry love to read. I wonder if they're members of the club?

Woman 3: Please hold all other topics until later, thank you so much. We're really jazzed to discuss this week's book now. (She and a Merlopian show a book with Lee on the cover.)

Lloyd: "Between You and Lee"?

Woman 3: The official autobiography of Lee! Spoiler, it's really Lee-rific!

Merlopian: Counterpoint, it's Double-Lee-rific! (He chuckles.)

Woman 3: Hmm... you've convinced me! (Lloyd finds this strange and starts to leave.) Let's discuss! So, my thoughts is that, you know, this book could use a little bit more Lee in this book...

(Wyldfyre enters a backyard party and places a casserole dish on a table. She then approaches a neighbor.)

Man 1: (To Wyldfyre.) Oh, hello, neighbor! Welcome to the par-par-par-taaay! What's your name?

Wyldfyre: I can't remember. Something dumb. Is it your birthday or something?

Man 1: Oh, I wish! Lee gives everyone free shoulder rubs on their birthday!

Wyldfyre: So, why the party?

Man 1: It's for how great life is! Isn't life great? It's great!

Wyldfyre: Sure, sure, but you know who loves parties? That couple, Jenny and Barry! Seen them around?

(Wyldfyre catches the eye of another man.)

Man 1: Never heard of them, but they sound awesome. Why you looking for them?

Wyldfyre: What? Who's looking for them? I'm just being sly in a way that doesn't draw attention to myself.

Man 1: Oh, okay, well, yeah, hmm. Well, in that case, I've got a question for you. How low can you go?

(Wyldfyre looks at a limbo bar.)

Wyldfyre: (She scoffs.) Not even a challenge. Lower. (A woman and a Merlopian lower the bar.) I said lower.

(They lower the bar even more.)

Man 1: No one could go that low

(Wyldfyre stretches and stands with her back to the ground, then passes under the bar. Everyone cheers and she notices the man she caught her eye and approaches him.)

Wyldfyre: Hey! You! Not impresses with my moves?

Man 2: What? Oh, no, it's not you. I can tell based on how you're acting, you're not like everyone else here.

Wyldfyre: What? I totally am. Wyldfyre is my real name. Dangit! I mean Fyldwyre. Fyld... wyre.

Man 2: Well, Fyldwyre, something tells me you also think it's weird everyone's fine to just party on and accept what Lee's telling us, but-- but--

Wyldfyre: It's okay, you're right. I've been thinking the same thing.

Man 2: I might have proof that Lee's version of what happened with the Merge is a big lie. Can you meet me later?

(Wyldfyre agrees.)

(Lloyd is at home reading "Between You and Lee" when Wyldfyre arrives.)

Wyldfyre: Lloyd, being the slyest clue-finder ever, I got something.

Lloyd: About Arin's parents?

Wyldfyre: Not really, but maybe. I found a neighbor who has some ideas about what's really going on down here, and he's ready to expose Lee's secrets.

(They approach a house.)

Wyldfyre: This is where he said to meet.

(Lloyd knocks on the door and it opens.)

Lloyd: Lee?

Lee: That's my name! Isn't it a delight to say? Just rolls off the tongue!

Wyldfyre: Uh, didn't know this was your house.

Lee: Isn't not. Just getting it ready for its next tenant.

Lloyd: What happened to the old tenant?

Lee: Moved to Cavern Five. Huge waterslide fan. Everyone's free to move around wherever they want!

Lloyd: I see. Well, we won't keep you.

(Lee closes the door and Wyldfyre and Lloyd walk away.)

Wyldfyre: The guy that lived here was gonna blow the whistle on Lee.

Lloyd: And Lee just made him disappear.

(The clockwork sun sets and the clockwork moon rises. The Lees finish preparing the house, and Wyldfyre and Lloyd watch them from hiding.)

Lloyd: Lee needs the neighbors to not ask certain questions. But why?

Wyldfyre: Look!

(A woman approaches the house and Wyldfyre is suspicious. She and Lloyd stare at her.)

Wyldfyre: Why are you snooping around here?

Woman 4: Keep your voice down! Lee has ears everywhere. You two are new, so you probably aren't working for him.

Lloyd: Who are you?

Woman 4: Who I am isn't important. What I am is a former journalist. I woke up in the Land of Lee about a year ago, so I figured I'd set up my own community newspaper. At first, Lee was supportive, but once I started digging into his story, I found inconsistencies.

(Wyldfyre and Lloyd look at each other.)

Lloyd: (To the journalist.) Go on.

Journalist: What if the world wasn't really destroyed? How do we know it's not still out there? I was supposed to get more info from the neighbor who lived here, but now he's gone missing.

Lloyd: The world is still there. We're Ninja from the outside. We're undercover here to find two people.

Journalist: (She sighs.) I'll help you find your people if you'll help me tell everyone the truth about the outside. Deal? (They agree.) Then follow me. There's something you need to see.

(Kreel arrives at her junkyard looking at her cell phone and then stops.)

Kreel: Hey! My junkyard! Who did this? (Rontu and Riyu aprroach her, with the latter being embarrassed.) Riyu, who's this big jerk?

Rontu: Junkyard-owner Kreel, I am Riyu's teacher, Master Rontu. My student and I are responsible for the condition of your yard.

Kreel: Huh, well, I was hired to demolish all that junk. I just hadn't gotten around to it yet 'cause it's hard work, and hard work's not my whole thing, but this is perfect! Two reckless dragons doing all the wrecking.

Rontu: I see. In that case, we can demolish anything you need, as long as we can continue to train here.

Kreel: Sure, works for me. (She leaves.)

Rontu: (To Riyu.) Sometimes things are not as we expect them to be. Let us resume.

(Riyu agrees and continues training by attacking a pile of scrap metal.)

(In a house, Nya and Jay, who has a rose in his mouth, are wearing name tags with their fake names and dancing the tango with the other neighbors.)

Jay: Neighborhood tango class? So dumb!

Nya: Just keep your eye out for name tags saying "Jenny" or "Barry."

(Jay and Nya dance a great tango and end up looking at each other.)

Man 3: Wow! You two don't need lessons. You're in perfect sync!

(Jay and Nya stop getting along.)

Nya and Jay: No, we're not!

(The journalist, Lloyd and Wyldfyre approach a secret door and the journalist uses her card to open the door. They enter, pass through some access doors and see the man from before trapped in a cage with tentacles on his head.)

Man 2: I love Lee. I love Lee.

Wyldfyre: That's the guy I told you about!

(Wyldfyre and Lloyd run towards the cage and the journalist stays behind them. They look up and see that the tentacles are from a Dream-Guyver.)

Lloyd: He's brainwashing him or something. We gotta get him out of here.

Journalist: No... (They turn to her.) you won't be doing that.

(The journalist reveals himself to be Lee in disguise.)

Lee: Gotcha, fakers! Boy howdy, you are some bad, bad neighbors.

Lloyd: What is this, Lee?

Lee: Look, almost everyone in my land is a true, honest friend who is just happy to be here, but every once in a while, some no-good beatnik gets a hair-brained idea that your old pal Lee isn't on the up and up! (They prepare to fight him.) Now, listen, everything will make sense when you look at this flower. (He takes a flower out of his pocket and they approach the flower, which releases green smoke and makes them cough.) Soon you'll forget all your uncharitable ideas about Lee, and we'll be best friends once again.
