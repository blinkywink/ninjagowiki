The Lean of a Tree/Transcript

(The scene opens with Jay meditating in the Monastery of Madness. Nya rests her hand on Jay's left shoulder.)

Nya: I can't believe we're finally together, Jay.

(Kai rests his arm on Jay's right shoulder.)

Kai: The team wasn't the same without you, buddy.

(Cole, Lloyd, and Zane walk towards the group. All six ninja huddle together. The other ninja make friendly facial gestures towards Jay. The ninja stop huddling. Jay's bouty hunter outfit has transformed to his Core gi to match the other ninja.)

Nya: Too bad you're a failure.

Jay: What did you say?!

(Nya transforms into Ras.)

Ras (illusion): I said, you are a failure. I placed my faith in you for nothing.

(Kai transforms into Zeatrix.)

Zeatrix (illusion): You said you could capture Ras. The only thing you've caught is feelings.

(Jay backs up fearfully)

Arin (illusion) : Face it, old man. Even if you were once a Ninja, you've fallen too far for them to ever take you back.

Jay: (Gasping) Uh... uh... stay back!

Administration Agent (illusion): You're one of us, Jay, not them.

(Jay changes into his Administration clothes)

Jay: (Straining, powers spluttering) Aah!

(Jay blasts at the Agents and a door appears. Jay opens the door to escape.) (Gasping)

Nya: Meditation didn't go well?

Jay: (Sighing) Didn't find any answers, if that's what you're asking. Just confusion. You sure I did this all the time? Seems boring.

Nya: (Sighing) You did. You didn't always like it, but meditation's part of being a Ninja. It doesn't just hand you clear answers, but it can process bad thoughts to make room for better ones.

Jay: Those weren't better thoughts, trust me. (Sighing) Life without memories is like feeling around in the dark.

Kai: Ugh, I can't meditate either. It's like I can't hold a thought in this place. I don't like it here. We should go.

Nya: We planned to stay til dawn, but the sun should be up by now, and I don't want to wait any longer. We need to figure out how to get home.

Kai: I'll light the way. (Straining, powers spluttering) Dang it. Keep forgetting our powers are on the fritz. This situation just keeps getting better.

(Jay activates the light on his mech's spear.)

Nya: Ooh! (gasping) What the--?

Jay: Who needs powers?

(Kai uses the Reveal Blade to analyse his surroundings, but sees nothing.)

Kai: I feel better already (laughing awkwardly) Sarcasm

Nya: Why is the ground all squishy?

(squelching)

Jay: What is that?

(Creatures fly towards them.)

Nya: Is the sky... moving?

Jay: Not the sky, those things!

(The creatures snarl.)

(In the Tree house hideout)

Lloyd: I'm sorry, there must be some kind of misunderstanding. I'm not a Protector of the Whispering Wood.

Arc Dragon of Life: You are the Green Ninja of legend, are you not, Lloyd, son of Garmadon?

Lloyd: I am, but I've never even heard of this place.

Arc Dragon of Life: (Roaring) Do not lie to us. You must have felt the call... but you ignored it. The first Green Ninja to ever abandon their duties, to neglect the wood from which mortal life itself originally sprang forth!

Lloyd: There have been other Green Ninja? The prophecy never said anything about--

Arc Dragon of Life: Of course there have been others, and each one has performed the Ritual of Unrooting to maintain the power of this wood, until you!

Lloyd: I didn't know any of this. I thought I'd reached my true potential!

Arc Dragon of Life: You believe you only have one true potential? That any of us do?

Arin: Hey, go easy! Lloyd protected this place as soon as he got here. He saved these little guys from some weird monster dudes!

Arc Dragon of Life: Too little, too late. Without the Master of Life, the Whispering Wood has weakened, its light dimmed.

Lloyd: I swear to you, I-I had no idea.

Arc Dragon of Life: All the other Life Masters found their way here as soon as they came of age.

Lloyd: "Came of age"?

Arc Dragon of Life: A stage of maturation for mortals. The transition out of childhood.

Lloyd: Oh... that must be the reason. When I was a kid, there was an incident with Tomorrow's Tea, and poof, I was instantly a teenager. I guess I... never really came of age.

Euphrasia: (Quietly) Did you know that? He skipped part of his childhood?

Arin: (Quietly) No idea.

Euphrasia: Gotta say, that's messed up.

Arc Dragon of Life: I see. You say your aging was... unnatural. We have been angry with you for so long, Life Master. Truly, you could not have known what was needed of you. We... apologize.

Lloyd: I never imagined my rapid aging would have consequences for anyone except me.

Arc Dragon of Life: The day you were exposed to that tea was the day our fate, and the fate of this wood, was sealed.

(In the Land of Eternal Dawn, Frak and Sora eat some fruits as Wyldfyre sits on Riyu.)

Wyldfyre: Are you two done yet? We gotta get back to the Monastery and figure out why my power's all wonky.

Sora: Our powers are acting weird too. You don't see us worrying about it.

Wyldfyre: Yeah, but my powers are cool. If shaking the dumb ground or building robot vacuums was the only thing I could do... (snorting) I wouldn't be bothered either.

Frak: Relax. We've earned a vacation. We just stopped an undead dragon.

Sora: Besides, how would we leave here even if we wanted to, Wyldfyre? We haven't seen anyone else, we have no way to contact the other Ninja... The other Ninja. Oh, wow. I really hope everyone's okay. We should probably head back. We just need to...

(The bushes rustle. Riyu and Wyldfyre prepare to fight, only for cats to appear from the bushes.)

Sora: Cats? Yes!

(cats meowing)

Frak: No, no, no, I'm allergic! I'll swell up like a pufferfish! (The cats approach him, but nothing happens.) Or... maybe my allergies are cured?

Sora: This place is purr...fect!

(The cats purr and Wyldfyre groans.)

(In the Monastery of Madness, flying creatures surround the ninja.)

Kai: I got this! Not our first time facing flying creeps!

Jay: Why you gotta keep rubbing it in? I don't remember anything!

(Nya Yelping)

Jay: Nya! (Struggling) Whoa!

Nya: (Screaming) Why is this cave so... wet? (Gagging)

Jay: I don't think it's a cave!

Kai: What is that? An earthquake?

Jay: Oh no. Grab onto something!

Kai: Grab something? Do you know how slimy everything is?

(A powerful rumble fills the air.)

Kai and Nya: Whoa!

Kai: It smells horrible.

Kai and Nya: Aah!

Jay: I can't believe I'm about to say these words, but I'm pretty sure we're in a Quealiche's stomach!

Nya: What's a Quealiche?

Jay: Enormous slug-like creatures that burrow through the Land of Madness. I was briefly transferred to the Decontamination Chamber in the Administration, and had to hose off agents unlucky enough to run into one. I'd know this stench anywhere!

Kai: You mean that gust of wind was a... belch? Oh, gross.

Nya: How did the Monastery end up inside the belly of a giant slug in the Land of Madness?

Kai: Bigger question, how do we get out? Preferably before we get digested.

Jay: Only two options, so I vote we find its mouth! Grab on!

(Arin and Euphrasia are sitting in the tree village as an Acona approaches and gives Euphrasia a flower.)

Euphrasia: Oh! (chuckling) Thank you. (She turns to Arin.) You're worried about Lloyd.

Arin: All this new info about who he's supposed to be, it's gotta be overwhelming. Like a nightmare where you have a test you didn't study for. I wish there was something I could do.

Euphrasia: Arin, you're the only one without Elemental Powers.

Arin: Thanks, Euphrasia. Rub it in.

Euphrasia: I mean, it's an advantage. You're the most dependable at the moment. You've never had powers, and you don't need them. (Arin smiles) Lloyd will rely on you now more than ever.

Lloyd: This thing you needed me to do...?

Arc Dragon of Life: The Rite of Unrooting. It maintains the Wood.

Lloyd: Maintains?

Arc Dragon of Life: All Realms are connected, even before the Merge. Like water through roots, or bees spreading pollen, Source Energy flows through unseen paths across every land and all people. To maintain the Whispering Wood is to cleanse those pathways, to sustain the connection between all life.

Lloyd: Okay. I'm late, but I'm here now. How do I perform this rite?

Arc Dragon of Life: I wish it were possible. While the Rite teaches you the truth about the Green Ninja's Elemental Powers, the time has passed. One as old as you would never survive it. Your roots have spread too deep and too wide to change your current path.

Lloyd: It would teach me the truth about my powers? I've never fully understood them. I've been called the Master of Power, the Master of Energy. Nothing ever really fit. Sometimes I didn't really fit. It is energy, but... what kind? It doesn't burn like fire, or shock like lightning.

Arc Dragon of Life: Would you name a flower from the shape of its seed? Know the lean of a tree before it is planted? The mystical nature of your sudden aging stunted your powers. The potential has not fully blossomed.

Lloyd: Well, now it may never. Something is wrong with all our Elemental Powers.

Arc Dragon of Life: I am aware. Some of the Source Dragons have withdrawn their connection to the Merged Lands. That's why the powers of Elemental Masters are unreliable.

Lloyd: Wait... seriously?

Arc Dragon of Life: Yes. Sadly, our own Source, Life, is one of those who no longer nourishes the land, and so, the decline of the Whispering Wood will soon quicken. Our end is at hand.

Lloyd: But why would-- Oh, no. This is my fault! I had my team summon the Arc Dragon of Focus. I was warned it might anger them, but I had no choice.

Arc Dragon of Life: Withdrawn Source Energy, unbalanced elemental powers-- if only the Rite had been performed, the power of the Master of Life might have overcome all of this.

Lloyd: All my life, I've been pushed in so many directions... prophecies, destiny, visions. I can't always tell what I'm choosing to do or what fate is forcing me to do, but if there's even a chance to stabilize Source Energy (Straining, powers spluttering) while saving this wood, then I choose to take that risk

(Jay, Kai, and Nya are trying to find the Quealiche's mouth to escape.)

Kai: It's not smelling any better.

Nya: (grunts)

Kai: Pretty sure this is the way to the mouth.

(The tentacles grab the Mech Dragon.)

Kai: We're not gonna make it!

Jay: Oh, yes, we are. Nya, flood the stomach. Kai, turn up the heat. A little heartburn is just what the doctor ordered.

(Nya uses her power and blasts the tentacles.)

Kai: (Grunting) Oh, man, not now.

Jay: (Groaning) I was hoping it wouldn't come to this. (He presses a button.) Goodbye, my friend.

Kai: Which one of us is he talking to?

(Jay grabs Kai and Nya and jumps away, leaving the Mech Dragon behind. The Mech Dragon explodes.)

Nya: Sorry. I know you loved that thing.

Jay: (sighs) Guess I love living more.

(deep rumbling and gurgling belch)

Jay: It worked! Get ready for it.

Kai: Get ready for what? Get ready for what?!

(A giant wave of stomach contents washes through the area. Jay, Kai, and Nya scream as they are swept up in it. The Earth Dragons sleeping in the Land of Madness wake up when they hear the ground rumbling. The Quealiche emerges from the ground and vomits out all the ninja, along with many other things.)

Nya: (gasps) Ew! I can't believe that worked!

Kai: (groans) I forgot how messy hanging out with you could be, Jay.

Jay: Don't anyone celebrate too soon.

(The Earth Dragons are angry)

Kai: These aren't the same dragons we ran into last time, are they?

Nya: They are, and they're not happy we woke them up.

(Wyldfyre uses her hands as binoculars to look around in the Land of Eternal Dawn.)

Wyldfyre: How am I supposed to guide us out when it's dawn in every direction?

Sora: Maybe we're not supposed to leave.

Wyldfyre: Where'd those cats even come from?

Sora: Why does that matter? (cats purring) Can't you just enjoy something for once?

Frak: (Gasps when he sees a plant filled with pies) Pie! (He runs to the plant and eats from it.) Hmm. They're a little cold. (A campfire appears.) Huh... That wasn't there a second ago. But I'm glad it's here now!

Wyldfyre: You guys don't think it's strange your favorite thing is pies, and then there's pies? And your favorite thing is cats, and then there's cats?

(Riyu grumbles as he gets some of the cats off of himself.)

Sora and Frak: No.

Wyldfyre: Ah! Pies are not supposed to grow on bushes! Wyldmelons can't grow outside the Wyldness! This place is full of trickery! (panting angrily)

(Riyu sniffs as he senses something.)

Wyldfyre: You got a scent?

(Riyu grumbles.)

Wyldfyre: An unusual scent? -Maybe it's a way out of here.

Frak: Whoa!

(Riyu grabs Frak)

Wyldfyre: Let's go

(Wyldfyre drags Sora away from the cats.)

Sora: Oh... (sad groans)

(The cats suddenly vanish.)

(The Earth Dragons attack the ninja.)

Jay: Even in the middle of nowhere, you two have enemies? (grunting) Why am I hanging out with you people?

(The Earth Dragons damage the mech, and countdown timer starts, Jay ejects himself out of the mech.)

Kai: Buddy, you're running out of things to blow up.

(Jay, Kai, and Nya run away while The Earth Dragons chase them. The mech explodes. The three of them keep running, but Jay stops upon seeing a ravine. Kai and Nya perform Spinjitzu to jump over it.)

Kai: Come on!

Nya: Wait!

(The Earth Dragons keep chasing them. Jay tries to perform Spinjitzu but it doesn't work.)

Jay: I can't do Spinjitzu! I can't do Spinjitzu! (panicked voice echoing and gasping)

(Kai and Nya perform Spinjitzu to go back Jay and attack the Earth Dragons.)

Jay: I'm fine

Kai: Over-under!

Jay: What-what?

Kai: (grabs Jay) It's what it sounds like.

Jay: (yelps as he slides under one of the Earth Dragons.)

Kai: H'yah! H'up!

(The Earth Dragons roar.)

Kai: H'yah!

Nya: What now?

Kai: You could try apologizing.

Nya: Me? They literally chased you!

Kai: Let's use their anger against them.

Nya: (agrees) Hey, dirt-eater! I've seen bigger horns on a tricycle!

Kai: Earth Dragon? More like Barf Dragon!

(The Earth Dragons to rush to attack. Kai and Nya perform Spinjitzu to dodge, and Nya grabs onto Jay. The Earth Dragons hit each other and retreat.)

Kai: (laughing) Man, that felt good! Working like that jog any memories, Jay?

Jay: No! You two share a look and know what the other's thinking. I can't even remember how to do Spinjitzu, which seems like the bare minimum to call yourself a Ninja. Even that kid with no powers can do it.

Kai: That's what training's for. Even we have to train. There's always room to grow.

Jay: (sighing) You don't get it. I hated working for the Administration. I lost in the Tournament of Sources. I was only a pretty good bounty hunter.

Nya: So?

Jay: So? No more failures. If I'm gonna be a Ninja, I need to be a great one. It's another reason I need to remember my forgotten memories. I don't know how, I don't know if it's even possible, but I have to.

Nya: We'll help you.

(Jay smiles)

(Riyu and Wyldfyre approach the Pit.)

Wyldfyre: Whoa...What is this thing?

Sora: Actually... I have no idea. (She grabs a rock and drops it in.) It's definitely not normal.

Wyldfyre: Well, in we go.

Frak: Whoa! I'm not jumping into some mystery black hole just cause you said so.

Sora: I agree with Frak. There's no rush to leave this place. It has everything we need.

Wyldfyre: Yeah, which isn't normal. It has to be a trap! It wants us to stay here forever!

Sora: No, that's a trap. (She points at the Pit.)

Frak: Totally. Now, where was that pie bush?

Wyldfyre: The pie bush is a trap, you clown-faces! Don't you see what this place is doing to you?

Sora and Frak: No.

Wyldfyre: (sighing) This is for your own good. Yaah!

(Wyldfyre pushes Sora and Frak down into Pit. They almost fall down, but Frak manages to grab onto the edge and Sora. Wyldfyre tries to stomp on Frak's hand.)

Sora: (gasps as she sees the Grapple Gun) Perfect! (pulls out the Grapple Gun and uses it to get out of the Pit with Frak.)

Sora: You're the one who's not in her right mind, Wyldfyre.

Frak: You should really listen to us.

(Straining, powers spluttering)

Sora: Hold on. Take a deep breath and calm down.

Wyldfyre: You don't get to tell me to calm down! (roaring)

(Wyldfyre fights Sora and Frak. Eventually the two manage to tie her up with the Grapple Gun, but she smiles.)

Frak: Why are you smiling? We have you beat.

Wyldfyre: Aw... you two teamed up. Cute, but I always team up with dragons.

(Frak, Sora, and Wyldfyre turn around to look at Riyu as he pushes them down into the Pit.)

(In the Tree house Hideout)

Arc Dragon of Life: Deep within the Whispering Wood is the Temple of the Green Ninja. Inside, you will be guided to perform the Rite of Unrooting. The temple is hidden, even from the eyes and ears of the forest.

Lloyd: Then... how will we find it?

Arc Dragon of Life: If you are meant to, it will call to you, but I must warn you again, the journey on which you are about to embark will be treacherous.

Lloyd: That's not enough to stop me.

(In Umbrasilvis, a woman is filling her bucket at the central fountain. Suddenly Frak, Riyu, Sora, and Wyldfyre arrive through the fountain and fall down to the ground.)

Umbrasilvan #1: Welcome to Umbrasilvis. (She leaves.)

Frak: Wait a second... pies don't grow on bushes!

Sora: And where did those cats come from? (sighing) I... I don't know what came over us.

Frak: (sighing) There was something seriously off about that place. Thanks for guiding us out. I guess you do make a pretty good leader.

Wyldfyre: My dragon heritage probably made me immune to whatever got into your heads. Riyu wasn't affected either. I may have heroically gotten us out of there, which some might say is the most heroic thing ever done. But where are we now?

(Two Umbrasilvans slam their doors.)

Frak: Friendly place.

Sora: Look, over there. (Sora points to an airship with the Explorer's Club's symbol. Frak gulps as he sees it.) Maybe we can hitch a ride back to Ninjago on that airship.

Frak: That's an Explorer's Club ship. They're probably in the middle of an important expedition. Too busy to help. Let's find another way home. I'm sure this place has tons of public transportation options

(Two more Umbrasilvans slam their doors. Frak, Riyu, Sora, and Wyldfyre explore the place when they come across members of the Explorer's Club.)

Frak: I, uh, think I dropped something back by the well.

Sora: is everything okay?

Reginald: Frak, is that you?

Wyldfyre: (muttering) Doesn't he look like--

Frak: (Sighing awkwardly) Hi, Dad.

(Sora and Wyldfyre shocked by this revelation.)

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
