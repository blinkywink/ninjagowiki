(At the Old Town Courthouse.)

Gayle Gossip: Gayle Gossip, reporting from Ninjago City's Old Town where the Summit of the Merged Lands is set to begin. This will be the very first time representatives from all of the Merged Lands have ever been called together and people here can't stop talking about it.

Woman: Ninjago City is about to go down in history.

Man: We really like all the other lands? Some are weird.

(The Acona delegates dance.)

Intelligent George: Accidents in the city. Masked weirdos escaping the sewers. Not to mention that weird performance with robots attacking people. Was it truly fiction or something much more sinister? Could all of this be part of a darker plan the inept authorities... (coughs) the young ninja... (coughs again) are hiding from the unsuspecting citizens of Ninjago?

NinjaFanInfinity: Like what's up with these ninja? No hate but hate it.

SpinjitzuForDays99: Disagree. The young ninja are still doing the thang!

Gayle Gossip: Now, let's tune in to Percival Tartigrade, elected leader of Imperium, the land that just suffered a major attack from its former ruler, Empress Beatrix, as he prepares to begin the summit.

Percival: It is no longer time for us to fear our differences, but to celebrate them. The Summit of the Merged Lands will be an expression of unity, sharing cultures, resources, and agreeing to stand up to evil and protect one another.

Frak: I really hope the summit goes the way Percy wants it to.

Arin: How? Cryptor and the Loyalists are out there now with Beatrix who's still dangerous even if she's unconscious.

Euphrasia: Yeah, doing this Operation Mentax. I don't know what a Mentax is, and I don't think I want to know.

Wyldfyre: Why are you all so worried? I'm worried nothing happens in the summit. It will be super boring. Talk talk. I'm pinching myself to stay awake already.

Frak: Where's Sora? It's not like her to not be here.

Arin: I know why she's not here. She's... kinda of furious with me.

Wyldfyre: Please tell me you didn't bake her one of those pies she hates.

Arin: No, a little worse. I sorta of accused her parents of being evil Loyalists of Beatrix. (Euphrasia and Wyldfyre gasp. Frak facepalms.) Yeah, I got Sora to confront her parents. It didn't go well.

Frak: Look, I can see how her parents history could make you suspicious, but sometimes parents are just bad parents, not secret evil outlaws.

Arin: I realize it was stupid. Now Sora's not answering my calls. She's gotta show up.

(Zeatrix arrives.)

Zeatrix: Happy to see you, too, ninja. I've checked the perimeter. Nothing suspicious, which makes me nervous. If our enemies attack, this supposed moment of peace could become the single most tragic event of all time. And it hangs on us.

(The Loyalists are setting up of Operation Mentax while Cryptor watches the broadcast.)

Percival: (broadcast) But we did not see, could not see.

Cryptor: Today, the merged lands will finally crumble under our rule. (He zooms screen onto Zeatrix.) All hail the good empress

(Zeatrix watches in the Security Room while using an electronic device to view Rogue, Lobbo, Kreel, and Beatrix.)

Percival: (broadcast) Which we learned when we realized our freedoms had been slowly stolen by the creeping threat of a leader.

Zeatrix: Our father never would have stood for what you've become, Beatrix. (She keeps swiping through Fedulian, a Greenbone Warrior, and Ras.) ugh. Ras, did my sister just use you like a pawn and throw you away like she does everyone? Guess it doesn't matter. You're probably long gone. Just more fodder in Beatrix's need for power.

(The ninja arrive.)

Frak: Whoa, very intense setup.

Zeatrix: Of course it is. We can't let our guard down for a second.

Euphrasia: We were just told the last delegates are about to get here.

Zeatrix: Then the opening ceremonies will begin right after that. Since I have eyes in the sky, I'll keep you aware of anything suspicious.

Arin: The rest of us will be on the streets and in the summit.

Zeatrix: Loyalists could be hiding anywhere, ready to strike at a moment's notice. They could even be people we know.

Frak: Riyu's already keeping an eye on things outside. The rest of us know our lookout spots and assignments.

Zeatrix: We're missing someone. Where's Sora?

Sora: Here.

Arin: Sora, I-

Sora: I looked over the plan, Zeatrix. Euphrasia, you're with Arin. I'll stay inside with Frak and Wyldfyre, but I think one of us should stay up here to protect Zeatrix.

Zeatrix: Me?

Sora: You're Empress Beatrix's sister. You're probably a target.

Zeatrix: Ha! You don't think I can protect myself? You're needed out there, ninja.

Sora: I don't like the risks.

Zeatrix: I don't need any help.

Wyldfyre: All right, I'm going to say the thing. You ready? (Wyldfyre puts his hand in the middle of the circle, the ninja team and Zeatrix add their hands.) Ninja, make it happen.

Arin: Go!

Wyldfyre: Go!

(The ninja leave while Zeatrix stays in the Security Room.)

Arin: Sora, wait!

Sora: I don't want to be around you, Arin. but we have a job to do, and that's the only reason I'm even speaking to you right now. So, get to work, and so will I.

(Roby and Percival are still waiting for last minute arrivals.)

Roby: Where's this last delegate coming from, the farthest edge of the merged lands? On a turtle. Cuz this is unfashionably late.

Percival: They RSVP'd, yes. Though they did it in an unorthodox way.

Wyldfyre: Personal question. Did you punch Arin in the stomach and call him a dweeb? Cuz that's what I would have done if I were you. So, that place of non-stop dawn no one seems to remember. Maybe the last delegate's from there.

(Morro arrives.)

Sora: No, that delegate's clearly from the Spectral Lands.

Wyldfyre: Must be that guy Arin met. I mean that guy someone met, someone dweebish.

(Roby shakes Morro's hand and escorts him inside.)

Sora: So, that's it? That weird land we ended up in sent no representative.

Percival: Like I told you, no one knows what land that could be. It must be part of somewhere else.

Wyldfyre: I don't think so. You sure everyone's here?

(Everyone applauds as Crag-Nor, Tyr, and the Pontifex Magicus are present.)

Percival: (He realizes something.) Hold on a second. There is a delegate missing. Where's Lloyd?

Sora: Lloyd's off on a mission. Wait, you expected Lloyd to be the delegate for Ninjago?

Percival: Of course. He's the most well-known and trusted citizen. Uh, this is going to make Ninjago look bad. If Lloyd's not coming, then there has to be a second in command, right?

Sora: Ugh...

Wyldfyre: Funny you should ask that.

(Everyone applauds as all the delegates arrive at the Summit of the Merged Lands while Euphrasia, Sora, and Frak check the surroundings.)

Euphrasia: All clear here so far.

Frak: Ditto.

Sora: Same.

(Arin and Riyu check the roof of the Old Town Courthouse.)

Arin: Nothing suspicious outside.

Zeatrix: Don't get lulled into lowering your guards.

Roby: Remember, you've got the

Percival: touch.

Roby: You've got the

Percival: power. (He walks to the stage) Greetings to all. I am Percival Tartigrade of Imperium, and I'm honored to welcome you to the Summit of the Merged Lands. In no particular order, because we are here as equals, I would like to introduce Tyr from the First Land. Then Lee from, you guessed it, the Land of Lee, Arrakore from Djinjago, Master Suetonius from the Cloud Kingdom. Baron Frye, Pontifex Magicus.

Frak: Hold it right there, weirdo. (He takes off their hood and reveals a Devonian) Oh. Uh, my bad. I mistook your face for a loyalist mask. That That sounded worse than I meant it to sound. Ugh! I'm a Serpentine and I totally prejudged a Devonian.

Zeatrix: We're supposed to be checking out anything suspicious. Don't beat yourselves up. It happens. (A person is hiding behind the poster near her.)

Percival: Akita from the Never Kingdoms. And lastly, but certainly not leastly, Wyldfyre from Ninjago.

(Everyone applauds.)

Wyldfyre: I deserve this! (She looks Roby, who winks at to her.)

Percival: For countless generations, we all lived separately. Most of us never even knowing each other existed. But a supernatural force brought us together. No one knows the reason for it. But here, starting today, we can make the merge mean something tremendous. Thrust together by circumstance.

Frak: Maybe Operation Mentax isn't gonna happen. Thoughts, Zeatrix? Zeatrix? Come in, Zeatrix?

Percival: ...but destiny is better shaped through cooperation...

(Frak runs to the security camera room, and sees Zeatrix missing.)

Sora: Frak, what's the word.

Frak: No sign of her, but no sign any struggle, either. It's.... wait... (spots an tunnel on the wall covered by a poster) Hold on. (enters the tunnel, which leads to the janitor room) Ah, never mind. Those masked goons couldn't have taken Zeatrix out this way. Everyone would've spotted them.

(Percival continues his speech.)

Percival: This is an unprecedented time of fear-- fear of each other, fear of change. As you all know, Imperium was recently the center of an intense battle. Some of your lands...

Wyldfyre: (makes a static sound) As much as I love and deserve this recognition, I can't just stand there doing nothing. We need a replacement delegate so I can get into the action!

Arin: Working on it.

Sora: Zeatrix isn't answering her comms, but.... maybe she still has them.

Euphrasia: What do you mean, Sora?

Sora: Maybe I can reverse the signal and turn it into a homing beacon. (sets a signal) Found it!

(In the Security Room.)

Sora: Okay, they're moving her through the west passageway towards the exit. (Frak runs and follows her directions.) We've gotta cut them off!

Arin: Roger that. We're on the way (He jumps onto Riyu and descends.)

(Inside the Old Town Courthouse, Percival continues his speech.)

Percival: Imagine a Unity Agreement between all of us, each land sharing cultures, ideas, The Merge has given us a unique opportunity to achieve unprecedented community by uniting together!

Bone Regent: What about natural disasters, like MergeQuakes? Uniting won't help everyone. For kingdoms like ours, having to help lesser kingdoms will just bring us down. (The crowd murmurs.) How is your agreement good for everyone? What do we get out of it?

(Outside, Riyu smells and runs towards the people pushing trollies filled with food.)

Arin: Riyu, the exit we want is right here! (runs towards Riyu) Where you going? (spots Riyu) Riyu! Now's not the time to listen to your stomach!

(In the middle of Old Town Courthouse, Percival answers and continues his speech.)

Percival: I see how this agreement might not seem like a one-to-one benefit for every land, but it is. There will be no more threats to any of us. If there is an issue, the Council of the Merged Lands will solve it without conflict. The greater good will help all.

Wyldfyre: (The crowd murmurs in disagreement as she slams her hands on the table, silencing the crowd.) Hey! Show Percival some respect, you blockheads! (spots Frohicky) Frohicky! Perfection! You'll be great. Best Ever! Don't vote for any bad laws!

(Outside, Euphrasia and Arin prepare to fight Loyalists.)

Euphrasia: Arin and I are at the west exit.

Sora: The Loyalists who have Zeatrix should be coming out those doors any second now!

(An Imperian drone comes out with Zeatrix's comms device.)

Arin: What? Where's Zeatrix?

Frak: (kicks the door) The comms! (grabs the device) It was a decoy!

Euphrasia: Then, where did Zeatrix go?

Arin: (He realizes something.) Riyu! (Riyu walks around sniffing and hits a trolley with his tail. A cabinet door opens revealing Zeatrix , who has been tied up.) Riyu had her scent!

(The waiters of the Summit reveal themselves as undercover Loyalists, and pull out their Saw blades.)

Sora: Well, the good news, we found Zeatrix.

(The Loyalists charge at them. Inside, Percival continues his speech.)

Percival: For most of Imperium's history, we were an advanced realm with the most cutting-edge technology, and when the Merge happened, we assumed we were superior to all others...

(Outside, Riyu kicks a Loyalists then flies up, and Arin uses Object Spinjitzu on a cooking pot to hit another Loyalist. Euphrasia charges and kicks a Loyalist, and Arin grabs another Loyalist and throws them away. Frak pushes a server away from the Loyalists' attacks. Arin blocks a Loyalist's attack, and Sora performs Spinjitzu and kicks another Loyalist away. Sora then rips off the tape on Zeatrix's mouth.)

Zeatrix: They snuck up on me. No idea what they want me for. (Puts on comms device.)

Sora: Told you, you could be a target.

(Zeatrix and Sora prepare to fight and Arin leaps backwards to them. Loyalists charge the group, and Arin punches and kicks an Loyalist while Sora blocks and dodges another Loyalist's attack. A Loyalist charges at Zeatrix with their Saw blade, but Zeatrix shoves the Loyalist and kicks their weapon away. A Loyalist goes in front of Arin and prepares to fight.)

Wyldfyre: Look out below! (stomps on a Loyalist) Can't say I didn't warn ya. (She kicks another Loyalist.) Order up!

Arin: (He punches a loyalist and kicks them.) I think we have the upper hand.

Sora: (She punches a Loyalist and kicks them away, but spots Cryptor and more Loyalists arriving with their Hover chariots.) I think we have a problem.

Cryptor: Loyalists, attack! (The Loyalists jump out and fire their weapons.)

(Percival continues his speech.)

Percival: Never did I dream of needing help from the outside, but if Imperium kept believing we could do it alone, we would be conquered by now.

(Euphrasia shoves a Loyalist and then slams them on the ground. Frak evacuates the non-Loyalist restaurant service people.)

Frak: Go! go! go!

(Arin slams a Loyalist onto a trolley then gets a plate to block the laser attacks and throws it at a Loyalist. he then pushes the trolley away. Zeatrix tries to use her power, but fails.)

Zeatrix: What? Ugh!

Sora: Zeatrix, you know powers aren't working! The Loyalists are after you! Get to safety! We got this.

Zeatrix: But I can't just...

Sora: You have to. You know I'm right!

Zeatrix: I... (sighs)

(Zeatrix runs away. The Loyalists chase her, but Riyu blocks the way and kicks away the Loyalists trying to chase Zeatrix. Cryptor shoots a laser to Riyu.)

Cryptor: Leave no one standing!

(Cryptor pulls out his dual swords and attacks Riyu. Wyldfyre spins around and slams a Loyalist, Arin blocks a Loyalist's attack and kicks them. Sora and Frak gather the trolleys to form cover. Riyu tries to slash Cryptor and slams the ground, pushing Cryptor away. Riyu charges at Cryptor but gets hit by Cryptor's laser, knocking him into a garage door.)

Wyldfyre: Riyu!

(Zeatrix stops.)

Zeatrix: Oh...

(Arin uses Object Spinjtizu and throws three plates at Cryptor. Cryptor charges his laser, but Zeatrix shoves him. Sora pushes a trolley to slam a Loyalist, and Arin slams another Loyalist to the ground. Zeatrix pushes a Loyalist but gets electrocuted from behind by another one.)

Arin: (punches an Loyalist) Zeatrix! (He gets restrained by another Loyalist.)

Frak: Oh, come on, can't we get a break here?

Cryptor: We have what we need! (The Loyalists drag Zeatrix away.) Regroup! (The Loyalists start leaving.)

(Percival continues his speech.)

Percival: Alone, there is a limit to what you can achieve, but together? Together, we have no limits! (The crowd cheers.)

(Outside, Riyu lays on the ground. As Frak walks to him, Riyu stands up.)

Arin: I can't believe we blew it again!

Frak: Wait, where's Sora?

Arin: (spots Sora) You gotta be kidding me.

(Sora is clinging onto the underside of a Hover chariot.)

Euphrasia: Sora? Sora, can you hear us? Sora, please! Sora, please res-- (Sora turns off her comms. The Hover chariots fly away.)

(In the forest near the Abyss of Eternal Night, the Land Bounty continues driving, Jay looks troubled.)

Jay: I'm worried about him. Something's troubling Zilvar. I can sense it.

Lloyd: This whole journey's been tough for all of us. The sooner we get to the Abyss and destroy that Weapon, the better. That thing breeds danger wherever it goes.

(Zilvar holds the Weapon.)

Zilvar: You must destroy it. It's too powerful and too dangerous.

Weapon of Desolation (in Zilvar's Mind): But destroying it could prevent your people from coming back. Do you even trust that's possible? He was one of your tribe. How could you not trust him?

(The portal gate of the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms is seen opening.)

Past Zilvar: A scion of the tiger tribe? How is that possible? I don't recognize you.

Past Ras: You knew me once, and I certainly know you, Zilvar Slashclaw. I was a mere cub when you left our tribe. My stripes were another color so many years ago, and I changed my name when my master saved me from certain doom. Back then, I was known to you as Zeras.

Past Zilvar: Young Zeras? Is it really you?

Past Ras: I go by Ras now, and have since that horrific day. I thought you had died on your trek. We all did. Either that, or you had abandoned us.

Past Zilvar: I would never abandon our tribe. if I'd known you'd survived, I would've done anything to find you. Knowing I failed you fills me with shame.

Past Ras: You can still make it up to me and our tribe. I just need you to do a task for me. The Weapon of Desolation was created from the very essence of my master. If that weapon is destroyed. it will greatly hurt the one I serve.

Past Zilvar: Who is this master that would create such a horrible Weapon?

Past Ras: That does not concern you. What does is that the ninja cannot succeed in destroying it. I need your help. I'm setting into motion a series of important events, and I cannot take the time to focus on this unexpected wrinkle in my master's plans.

Past Zilvar: Of course I want to help you, the last son of my tribe, but in the wrong hands, that weapon could lead to the obliteration of all the Merged Lands.

Past Ras: Such fears are short-sighted! My master and I are in the process of undoing the Merge, and in that process, we could bring back our tribe.

Past Zilvar: Bring them back? How?

Past Ras: By capturing all the Source Dragons, and through controlling their power, we will undo the Merge and build a new reality... one that will revive our people.

Past Zilvar: No, this plan is evil. Capturing Source Dragons? Blasphemy!

Past Ras: Think of our tribe! Think of Mei!

Past Zilvar: Mei died with a pure heart, and if I were to help you to commit unspeakable acts for a master who clearly does not respect the Sources? That would be an insult to Mei and to all our people. You should be ashamed of yourself, Zeras.

Past Ras: You have no right to insult me! I've done everything for our tribe... the tribe you failed to save! (pulls out his hammer and slams it on the ground)

Past Zilvar: Don't challenge me.

Past Ras: If you won't do it, then I'll be forced to take down the ninja myself, after I destroy you for betraying me and our people!

Past Zilvar: You betray our tribe, not I. (Ras walks near to Zilvar, Zilvar walks back and whistles to call Grimtak, who rams into Ras. Zilvar gets on Grimtak.) I will not let you harm the ninja!

(Zilvar leaves to reunite with the ninja.)

(Another flashback in the Boreal River)

Past Jay: Zilvar with the last-minute save. Thanks, buddy. I'd knew you were a good egg the moment I met you.

Past Zilvar: I was not hatched from an egg.

Past Jay: Yeah, okay, noted. What made you come after us?

Past Zilvar: I... (looks at the weapon)

Past Weapon of Desolation (in Zilvar's Mind): You must not destroy the weapon. You heard Zeras. He can bring your people back. You cannot tell the ninja.

Past Zilvar: I had a feeling you might need me.

(Back in the present, the Weapon continues pressuring Zilvar.)

Weapon of Desolation (in Zilvar's Mind): This could be all you've ever wanted. Your tribe, your family... Yes, but at what cost?

(Grimtak and The Land Bounty stop upon arriving at the Abyss of Eternal Night.)

Lloyd: We're here.
