(In Old Town, Lobbo goes super-fast, accidentally hitting crates, barrels, fences and construction scaffolding.)

Lobbo: (screams) Ack! Wha...! Mistakes have been made! Lobbo-Lobbo! Whoa!

Percival: What? No! Can anyone stop him?

(Lobbo turns, Wyldfyre's Prototype X-1 arrives.)

Wyldfyre: Ninja… get going!

(Frak arrives on a Battle Bike.)

Frak: That is not the phrase!

(Riyu flys above with Euphrasia riding him.)

Wyldfyre: I say my own thing!

Euphrasia: Why is Lobbo acting like this?

(Arin and Sora arrive with a Transforming Car.)

Arin: We have the answer to that question, but, uh, warning, it's gross.

(Previously, at Lobbo's Totally Rad Arcade, Lobbo-Lobbo, Lobbo eats leftover casserole as Arin plays arcade game and Sora watches.)

Past Arin: What?

Past Lobbo: No one wanted these perfectly good casseroles for some reason... ...and Lobbo doesn't waste food, Lobbo-lobbo. (keeps eating casserole)

Past Arin: Uh, that stuff's been sitting out for two weeks. Are you sure it won't mess you up?

Past Lobbo: (laughs) Lobbo, will be fine, (accidentally slams into the table) Lobbo-Lobb--oh!

(In the present, the ninja are still trying to restrain Lobbo.)

Euphrasia: Casserole did all of this?

Sora: Never underestimate the evil potential of bad casserole.

(Lobbo screams and hits a taxi as the young ninja try to stop him. Sora attempts to use her powers, but fails.)

Sora: Ugh! Let me be the first to say I really miss having Lloyd around to make our powers (Arin pulls out the Grapple Gun.) work right.

Wyldfyre: You're not the first! I say the same thing, like, eight times a day, but I got a plan! You all back off, for teamwork reasons, and I'll handle this myself!

Frak: How is that teamwork?

Wyldfyre: Who was elected team leader? Not you! (She speeds ahead and blocks the road.) Lobbo, as leader of the Ninja, I demand-- (Lobbo crashes into the X-1, but doesn't slow down.) -Wha...!

Lobbo: Ah! The casseroles are like a hurricane in my intestinal rotors! (shrieks) Lobbo-Lobbo

Wyldfyre: Whoa! (is caught by Riyu and Euphrasia) Why was I alone down there? You should always help your leader!

Euphrasia: Of course. Our bad.

(Riyu restrains Lobbo. Arin, Sora, and Frak stop their vehicles.)

Lobbo: (grunts) Thank you! Lobbo-lobbo!

Sora: (checks Lobbo's systems) Lobbo, your hover engine's gummed up with rotten casserole. It's stuck in overdrive. I need to vent you right now.

Lobbo: Let's all agree not to tell Lobbo's mother about this, Lobbo-Lobbo... Wha... wha... Ah! (jolts away from Riyu hand and hits Arin) Lobbo-Lobbo! Oh! Oh! Wha...! (charges uncontrollably at three people standing nearby)

Lobbo: (screams)

(Frak drives the Battle Bike forward and grabs Arin's Grapple Gun to hold Lobbo in place)

Frak: It's working! Steady... steady...

Lobbo: Flee, nice family! Flee the horrible nightmare Lobbo has become! Lobbo-Lobbo!

(Frak tries to hold Lobbo. Suddenly, the Battle Bike stalls and Frak is pulled along by Lobbo.)

Frak: Whoa-oh-oh!

Lobbo: (sputters) Lobbo-Lobbo!

Frak: Aah!

Sora: Flushing your hover engine!

Lobbo: Lobbo has casserole regret! Aah! (quietly) Look away. (He vomits up all the rotten casserole.) Ah... Much better. Lobbo-Lobbo.

Frak: (coughs) Better for some of us.

Wyldfyre: Oh, come on. Oh... (shudders) Sorry you got chunked on, but my sterling leadership got us a win.

Percival: Does this look like a win? The Summit of the Merged Lands is only days away! (groans) We just lost a week's worth of set-up.

(The young ninja return to the Monastery of Spinjitzu)

Euphrasia: I don't get it. We thought the reason we were having a hard time was that we had no leader.

Wyldfyre: And now we have the best leadership ever and still mess up. Weird.

(The young ninja enter the Relaxation Room.)

Barry, Jenny, and Sora's parents: Surprise!

Wyldfyre: I would never ever complain about unexpected snacks, but what are they for?

Jenny: We saw you stop that unfortunate purple robot guy.

Frak: Sure, but not before we let him destroy half of Old Town.

Barry: It might not have been perfect, but things would have been so much worse if you didn't act so bravely. We're proud of you, fine young ninja.

Frak: You're proud of us?

Arin: Thanks, Mom and Dad.

Jenny: Oh, don't thank just us. The Ninja Parent Support Group did it together.

Sora's mother: Sora, honey, what's wrong?

Sora: Nothing.

Sora's mother: Well, something must be wrong. Your dad and I have been trying to do nice things for you, but we feel like you're avoiding us.

Sora: I'm just busy. I'm a ninja.

Sora's mother: Honey, there must be a reason-

Sora: Fine! Yes, I've been avoiding you! I never wanted to see you again!

Sora's father: Never?

Sora: I ran away from Imperium because you chose your Good Empress over your own daughter. How do you think that made me feel? And now you show up at my home pretending what you did never happened.

Sora's mother: Sora, the way we were raised, Imperium was our lives. Being good citizens was all that mattered. But it shouldn't have mattered more than being good parents.

Sora's father: We came here hoping maybe for a fresh start to put that behind us.

Sora: Well, well, I don't know if I'll ever be ready for that.

(Frak is practicing in the courtyard.)

Arin: Were you just trying a double twist flying kick?

Frak: Yeah. And failing at it.

Arin: It's impossible. Remember, we made it up when we were ten.

Frak: Yeah, but I was still hoping I could somehow pull it off. I need a win today.

Arin: It's like my parents said. Sure, it wasn't perfect, but we saved Lobbo and Old Town. That's not bad.

Frak: But it isn't just today. (Frak is seen training when he gets hit. Then he's seen chasing a Re-Awakened before bumping into a Mucoid.) Sorry! (Frak is seen opening the door for Sora.) Milady. (The door hits him in the face.) Ow. (The flashback ends.) Maybe my dad's right. Maybe I'm not a real ninja.

Arin: (sighs) You have to stop worrying about what your dad thinks.

Frak: It's hard not to. All I ever wanted was his approval, and he'd just ignore me or belittle me so much I'd wish he'd go back to ignoring me. Now, you wouldn't understand. Your parents are awesome.

Arin: They are, but I do know a thing or two about not feeling worthy. No one gets it right all the time, but, the Frak I know always keeps trying, even when it feels like it's hopeless. You never quit.

Frak: I don't, do I? Huh? Maybe I should focus less on trying impossible moves and more on figuring out what went wrong today. Bike practice it is.

(In the hangar bay, Frak checks the Battle Bike and notices an odd burn mark.)

Intelligent George: (broadcast) These young ninjas stopped Lobo eventually, but they didn't stop the cavalcade of damage to the oldtown section of Ninjago City.

Frak: Burn marks?

Intelligent George: People are beginning to wonder if Ninjago really is the best place to hold the merged land summit. Isn't there somewhere safer, like inside of a volcano?

Frak: Wait, what was that? (pauses the video and rewinds it.)

(The video shows a laser hitting the back of the bike.))

Frak: A laser? I didn't stall the bike with the throttle. A laser blast stalled it. (rewinds the video again) But who…?

(The video moves to a Nindroid in the crowd.)

Frak: General Cryptor?!

Sora: I guess it could be a face?

Frak: Not a face! Cryptor's face! (the video switches to the image of Cryptor.)

Arin: But Cryptor's dead, Frak.

Sora: What's a cryptor? Not all of us grew up around here, Ninja Nerds.

Frak: He was an evil Nindroid general who commanded his robotic army to overrun Ninjago City!

Arin: We were just kids, but I'll never forget it. It was a terrifying time, until the Ninja saved us, but he can't be back, because he's dead.

Frak: What's death to a Nindroid? They just need robot repairs.

Arin: Cryptor was a living Nindroid! Like Zane. When he died, his spirit went to the Realm of the Departed, and the spirits there have moved on. You should really show this to P.I.X.A.L. She'll know if it was Cryptor for sure.

Frak: I tried to, but Frohicky told me she left a couple days ago. Told him she had to get some things done before Zane got back.

(The image disappears and the video plays again, then pauses.)

Sora: That red flash could just be a spark from your failing engine. Look, we're all having a rough time. I know it feels better to think someone sabotaged you.

Arin: Yeah, but this random red streak isn't exactly proving it.

(Back in Old Town, Frak searches for evidence at the scene of the incident.)

Frak: There's gotta be some kind of evidence here. (searches and looks for evidence)

(A mysterious man walks behind Frak.)

Frak: Hiyah! (kicks) I knew you'd come back to the scene of your crime, Cryp--! (Reginald groans.) Dad?!

Reginald Slitherbottom: Wow! (chuckles) You moved so fast I didn't see you hit me.

Frak: (helps Reginald stand) What were you doing sneaking up like that?

Reginald Slitherbottom: Uh… well, I was simply trying to say hello to my son. but I didn't take into account your lightning-fast reflexes. You wouldn't happen to have an ice pack, would you? Oh, dear.

Frak: Dad, it's the middle of the night. Why are you out here?

Reginald Slitherbottom: (sighs) Well, i-it's a bit complicated. (sits on an bench)

Frak: Yeah? I know complicated. Try me.

Reginald Slitherbottom: Well, you see, my reputation in the Explorer's Club has taken a bit of a hit recently. I took charge of our Rare Maps Collection. I was convinced I would be able to curate better than anyone had before me, but last night, someone stole a rare map from right under my nose.

Frak: Someone outsmarted you? You? You always said that was impossible.

Reginald Slitherbottom: (sighs) Uh, son, I have not been, how shall I say, in the greatest of headspaces recently, and therefore less than stellar at my duties as an Explorer, and now, because of this brazen theft, my name is mud. Well, not really "Mud", of course. My colleague Mud Johanson still has that name legally.

Frak: So, let me get this straight-- someone stole a rare map, and you're taking the blame yourself? You're not blaming it on anyone else? Like Gweeg?

Reginald Slitherbottom: I am not. As a matter of fact, Dr. Gweeg has been promoted to my superior. It seems his heroic actions in Umbrasilvis were looked upon with great favor, whereas my inability to notice an evil Loyalist within our ranks was received with, uh… less than royal fanfare. However! I am determined to get the map back and repair my sullied name, and the trail led me here, where I find my son, of all people. I'm sure this isn't the case, but I must ask, you didn't steal the map, did you Frak? As some sort of revenge on your neglectful father?

Frak: No. I'm here because someone sabotaged me today using a laser.

Reginald Slitherbottom: A laser? A finely honed laser was the tool used to cut through the glass containing the missing map.

Frak, Reginald: Are we after the same person?

(In Reginald's office, Reginald opens the fridge to get an ice cube, while Frak is walking around.)

Reginald Slitherbottom: I'm sure it's been a while since you've seen my office. (sits in his office chair)

Frak: A while…. or never.

Reginald Slitherbottom: Oh, well, uh… perhaps this is another one of the… blind spots in my parenting you've made me aware of.

Frak: I made you aware of?

Reginald Slitherbottom: You, uh, you've forgotten what you said in Umbrasilvis? How you thought I was capable of evil?

Frak: When I'm stressed out, sometimes I say things I don't mean.

Reginald Slitherbottom: Ah, but you're not wrong. I have been doing a lot of soul-searching since then, but I wasn't all that bad of a father. I mean, look-- I eat all my lunches and snacks on this plate you made for me.

Frak: Dad, that's a chestplate. Ninja armor? I made it so we could pretend to be Ninja together and fight bad guys. We never did.

Reginald Slitherbottom: Well, that would finally explain its odd shape. Perhaps you're not as bad as making plates as I long believed. So, this mystery laser user?

Frak: Yeah. Was the saboteur focused on me? All the Ninja? Or they want to damage the upcoming Summit of the Merged Lands? Your stolen map, what was it for?

Reginald Slitherbottom: Son, it was a map of passages under Old Town, where there's long been rumored a buried Mind Crystal.

Frak: A Mind Crystal?

Reginald Slitherbottom: Yes, yes, yes. Legend has it that it can be used to control minds or switch minds, or other mind-related business. It would give the one who possesses it a great deal of power, if it's even real.

Frak: I suspect Cryptor is behind my sabotage, and it would make sense if he stole your map. He's always been searching for great power.

Reginald Slitherbottom: Cryptor? The ninja's old foe? Then why are you here alone? Not working with your fellow Ninja?

Frak: They don't believe that Cryptor's back from the dead, so I came here to find evidence to prove it.

Reginald Slitherbottom: You know, I've never met anyone with knowledge of the Ninja like you. If you say it's this Cryptor chap, by Jove, it must be true.

Frak: But how do we stop Cryptor from getting this Mind Crystal? You wouldn't happen to have a copy of the map? To try to beat him there?

Reginald Slitherbottom: No… but I have something better than a copy! Ha ha! My own brain!

Frak: Not to doubt you, but how is your brain possibly better than a map.

Reginald Slitherbottom: Haven't you ever wondered how I remember the exact names and dates for every excavator In the Maltmer Expedition, hmm?

Frak: I have never once wondered that.

Reginald Slitherbottom: Or noticed that I can sing all 56 verses of the Serpentine Odyssey, unabridged, by memory?

Frak: Never noticed.

Reginald Slitherbottom: Son, I have a photographic memory!

Frak: Then why'd you forget my sixth bir--

Reginald Slitherbottom: Anyhow, I have perfect replica of the map… in my mind! We can try to beat Cryptor to the Mind Crystal! Let me pack my explorer's gear! Oh, this will be the Ninja-pretend time we never had!

(In Old Town, Frak and Reginald walk through paths to a sewer entrance.)

Reginald Slitherbottom: Now, if memory serves, there should be an old sewer entrance, uh…

Frak: Is that it right there?

Reginald Slitherbottom: Huh. Well, uh, perhaps my photographic memory has north and south mixed up. (Frak opens the manhole and jumps, Reginald jumps.)

(Frak and Reginald land inside the sewers.)

Frak: Haven't been down in the sewers in forever. I'm an embarrassment to Serpentine everywhere.

Reginald Slitherbottom: (laughing) Don't be so hard on yourself. I've never once been in these sewers beneath Old Town, and I'm a Serpentine and an explorer. So, do you still create pies? Oh, I sure do miss your Merlopian Crumble Custard pie. Mm, magnifique. M'wah!

Frak: Really? You like my pies?

Reginald Slitherbottom: Cooking was your mother's skill, I cannot even boil water. Now, uh, we go around this corner, and-- (spots a locked sewer gate) Oh!

Frak: Maybe there have been changes down here since that map was made?

Reginald Slitherbottom: Thank you, son, but even I am beginning to doubt the photographic nature of my memory.

(Some Loyalists approach Frak and Reginald from behind.)

Loyalist 1: Don't move! You're coming with us!

(Frak and Reginald raise their hands, More Loyalists appear.)

Frak: Loyalists?

Reginald Slitherbottom: So is this the end for us?

Frak: Ha! We're just getting started. (Frak charges, and the Loyalists fire at him. Frak kicks some of the Loyalists, then slam them to the ground. More Loyalists shoot at Frak.) Dad, run for it!

(Reginald runs but is halted by another Loyalist, he pulls out his shovel but it's grabbed by a Loyalist and slammed into Reginald.)

Frak: Dad!

(Frak gets slammed to the ground by another Loyalist and goes unconsicious. He later opens his eyes and finds himself in the mining site on the Sewers.)

Reginald Slitherbottom: Well, you were right, son.

Frak: Huh?

Reginald Slitherbottom: Look.

(They look at Cryptor.)

Frak: That's Cryptor! I knew it, he's still alive!

(Cryptor walks to the Loyalists' miners.)

Cryptor: Careful! We cannot damage the item!

Loyalist 1: Sir?

Cryptor: (looks at Frak and Reginald) Unexpected interlopers? You have witnessed too much. I'll have to determine what to do with you.

Frak: You don't know who you're messing with, Cryptor!

Cryptor: Someone pretending to be a Ninja?

Frak: I am a real Ninja, I'll have you know, and it's only a matter of time before the other Ninja get here! I activated a homing beacon, and they know exactly where we are!

Reginald Slitherbottom: Did you do that for real?

Frak: No, it's a total bluff. Just roll with me on this.

Cryptor: If other Ninja are on their way, I've made my determination: destroy you and your homing beacon, and dump your remains in the river.

Reginald Slitherbottom: Do you really think it's wise to eliminate the Merged Lands' foremost expert on the Mind Crystal?

Cryptor: Continue.

Reginald Slitherbottom: I have scrutinized hundreds of tomes regarding the power of this enchanted yet dangerous Crystal. Without my help, you'll likely turn yourself and your henchmen into drooling infants by misusing its power!

Frak: Is all that true?

Reginald Slitherbottom: Of course not, but roll with me on this. I'm buying us time!

Frak: For what?

Reginald Slitherbottom: (unties rope) For this! Ha! You naive fools fell for my ruse!

(The Loyalists' miners charge at Frak and Reginald, Frak kicks one of them into a wall and kicks another to the floor.)

Reginald Slitherbottom: I am no expert on the Mind Crystal, but I did win second place in this year's "Escaping from Enemies' Bindings" competition. Huzzah!

(Frak dodges a Loyalist's attack then kicks them.)

Cryptor: You will be crushed! (powers up his laser eye)

(The laser fires at Frak, who dodges and hits Cryptor with a jumping attack.)

Frak: (laughs) I did it! A double-twist flying kick! I can't believe--

(Frak gets slammed into the floor by a Loyalist carrying a shovel. Frak dodges.)

Cryptor: Halt! Focus on digging out the Mind Crystal! Leave them to me! (He fires a laser blast at Reginald, but Frak jumps to him and pushes Reginald to the ground.)

(Frak dodges lasers, then jumps onto a wall, grabbing a chain to swing with.)

Reginald Slitherbottom: Wow, he truly is a real Ninja.

(Cryptor targets a nearby wall, causing a boulder to fall on Frak, slamming him to the ground.)

Frak: Dad, we gotta get outta here and find help! What does your photographic mind map tell you?

Reginald Slitherbottom: Uh, uh, uh… there! (points at the exit) That should lead us just outside the Old Town Square!

(Reginald walks, then dodges Cryptor's laser fire.)

Cryptor: You will not get out of here alive! (fires laser)

Frak: (slams Cryptor down as Reginald dodges more laser blasts) Can we make that a bet? 'cause I could really use a win today.

(Cryptor fires his laser, but Frak dodges, Frak then climbs up as Reginald removes the lid of the exit, Cryptor fires another blast, but Reginald blocks it by putting the lid back.)

Reginald Slitherbottom: Did you just see that, son? I swung my legs up and kicked that manhole cover off. I was an action hero!

Frak: Yeah, great, Dad, but we gotta go before--

(Cryptor flies out of the sewer exit, causing citizens to panic.)

Cryptor: Run! Scream! Fear me! (fires a laser at Frak and Reginald)

(Frak spins and kicks Cryptor.)

Frak: Leave them out of this! Your fight's with us!

Cryptor: You?! My fight is with everyone in the Merged Lands! (fires another laser at Frak, then fires laser everywhere knocking down an billboard.) There will never be peace here!

(Frak kicks Cryptor, who fires another laser at Frak before dodging, then fires a laser at two people.)

Reginald Slitherbottom: (jumps and gets hit by a laser) Huzzah!

Frak: Dad! No! (jumps and punches Cryptor multiple times.)

Loyalist 1: General Cryptor, we've obtained it!

Cryptor: Our time here is done! (presses a button on his chest)

(Imperium Hover Chariots fly to the Loyalists, Cryptor gets in and flies away alongside the others.)

Frak: Dad, no! No… (gasps) I… I know you've been trying. I should have told you how I felt years ago. I… I didn't think you'd care, and I was wrong. and now…. and now it's all too late. We should had more time! (Reginald moves) Dad? How? I… I saw you get hit with a laser!

Reginald Slitherbottom: Yes, about that… You were right, this is better as a chestplate than a food plate. (He shows he was wearing the chestplate from earlier, which now has a burn mark from the laser blast. Frak and Reginald chuckle and laugh.)

(Back in the Hangar bay, the young ninja gather for a strategy meeting.)

Frak: So Cryptor and the Loyalists are working together, looks like to disrupt Ninjago City, but I have no idea what they want the Mind Crystal for.

Sora: Whatever the reason, we have to be on guard and track them down.

Arin: Frak, I gotta apologize. We should have believed you when you said Cryptor was back.

Frak: It's okay. No one is perfect all of the time, right? But you should have seen that double-twist flying kick. It was as awesome as we always imagined! (Frak and Arin laugh.)

(Frak used double twist flying kick to knock down a crate box, Reginald tries and fails.)

Reginald Slitherbottom: Oh! Well, uh, guess I'm not the Ninja in the family, or the baker, for that matter.

Frak: (laughs) Time for a break?

Reginald Slitherbottom: Ah, I was really hoping you'd say that. (Frak and Reginald grab pies and eat.) My boy. (chuckles) Jolly good…

Frak: If I do say so myself.

Reginald Slitherbottom: Mm!

(In the Relaxation Room, Sora's parents eat cake, Sora opens and walks in.)

Sora: Maybe I'll have a little slice.

Sora's Mother: Really?

Sora: Nothing big. Nothing major. Just a little bite… for now.

(Sora's mother gives a slice of cake to Sora.)

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
