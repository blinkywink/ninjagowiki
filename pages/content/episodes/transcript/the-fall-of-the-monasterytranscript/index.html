(In an abandoned subway car, the ninja search for a way to escape.)

Jay: What happened?

Kai: I think we're in trouble.

Zane: It appears the explosion collapsed the subway tunnel.

Kai: How deep down are we?

Cole: I've been deeper.

Jay: This old girl saved our lives! I told you they don't make them like they used to anymore. (He knocks on the car but nearly causes it to collapse.)

Cole: Do. Not. Do that again!

Jay: Sorry! Sorry.

Kai: Ugh. Why can't you use your powers to clear the earth away?

Cole: This ground doesn't look too stable. But I'll try. (He uses his powers, but the car becomes more unstable.)

Kai: Stop. Stop. Stop!

Jay: Cole! Bad idea!

Zane: The tunnel is too unstable.

Jay: What do we do?

Kai: Time to call for help. (He puts on his hood.) Nya? Come in. Nya, you there? I'm not getting a signal.

Zane: Hmm. The debris seems to be blocking our communications.

Jay: Oh, great. Just great! I'm buried alive inside my favorite toy train!

Zane: Kai, hand me your communicator, and I'll attempt to amplify the signal.

(In the hangar bay, P.I.X.A.L. and the others attempt to contact the ninja.)

P.I.X.A.L.: Do you read me? Zane. Jay. Cole. Kai.

Wu: I fear something terrible has happened to them.

Skylor: We don't know that.

Nya: We lost all contact. And seismic sensors indicate a powerful blast. Master Wu may be right.

(A light appears on the computer screen.)

P.I.X.A.L.: That is strange. The emergency escape elevator has been activated.

Wu: By whom?

(The elevator doors open to reveal Aspheera, The Mechanic, Pythor, and Vangelis. Wu and the others rush to confront them.)

Wu: Aspheera!

Aspheera: Hello, Wu-ser!

Wu: What is the meaning of this?

Vangelis: I would think it's obvious. We're here for —

Aspheera: Revenge!

(Pythor rolls his eyes in annoyance.)

Vangelis: No, not revenge! Not this time! Not everything is for revenge! We are here for the Weapons of Spinjitzu.

Wu: Come and get them. If you can.

Aspheera: I was hoping you would say that.

(A fight breaks out as the heroes and villains take on each other. Pythor uses his tail to strike Nya into a corner and laughs.)

Pythor: A little birdie told me you don't have any powers! You should've stayed in the ocean where you belonged.

Skylor: Nya! (She tackles Pythor, but he defeats both her and Nya.)

Mechanic: (to P.I.X.A.L.) I'm gonna fry your circuits! (He manages to send her flying into the stairs with his arm.)

Wu: P.I.X.A.L.! (He turns to see Vangelis, Aspheera, and the Mechanic surrounding him.)

Mechanic: Worry about yourself, gramps! Even you can't take on all three of us!

Wu: Let's find out. (He holds his own against the three villains.)

P.I.X.A.L.: (She sees Pythor materialize behind Nya.) Nya!

(Wu leaps up to tackle Vangelis, but Aspheera opens the compartment containing the Golden Weapons and knocks him off balance. Vangelis then sends him flying.)

P.I.X.A.L.: This fight seems a little unfair. I think it's time we even the odds.

(P.I.X.A.L. presses a button. Dozens of MiniPix emerge from below and surround the area.)

Wu: P.I.X.A.L., what are these?

P.I.X.A.L.: I call them, MiniPix.

Mechanic: Are they supposed to scare me? 'Cause they look adorable!

P.I.X.A.L.: MiniPix, attack! (The MiniPix begin to blast lasers at the villains.) You didn't think I built everything on my own, did you?

Nya: Actually, until now, yes.

Mechanic: (He strikes down a MiniPix.) Ha! Gotcha!

P.I.X.A.L.: The MiniPix are not designed for combat. They will not last long.

Wu: All we can do is try to prevent them from getting the Weapons of Spinjitzu! Quickly!

(Wu rejoins the fight and quickly surrounds Aspheera with the MiniPix. Just then, the elevator doors open and reveal dozens of Crystal Spiders.)

Aspheera: I win!

(The spiders quickly take down the MiniPix.)

Wu: Enough! (He fights, but is blasted into the computer.)

Nya: Master Wu!

Aspheera: Before the end, I promised to deliver a message from an old friend. Harumi sends her farewell.

Nya: Harumi?!

(Aspheera knocks her back with Spinjitzu.)

Wu: Nya!

(Aspheera sends the computer crashing down on Wu and the others. The villains then approach the Weapons of Spinjitzu.)

Aspheera: The Sword of Fire.

Vangelis: I'll take the Scythe of Quakes!

Mechanic: The Shurikens of Ice.

Pythor: And I'll take the — oh, what? These are broken!

Mechanic: Ha, Ha. They don't work without electricity!

Vangelis: Come on! Arm the spiders and let's get out of here!

Mechanic: With pleasure. Spider bombs activated! I love these things.

Pythor: (muttering) Bloody useless nunchucks!

(The villains depart, leaving behind spider bombs with a two-minute timer. On the ground, the last MiniPix repairs itself and rescues Wu, Skylor, and P.I.X.A.L.. Unable to find Nya, it leaves without her. In the monastery's courtyard, Wu and the others wake up.)

Wu: Where's Nya?

P.I.X.A.L.: She's still in the hangar.

(In the hangar bay, Nya emerges from the debris.)

Nya: P.I.X.A.L.? Master? (She sees the spider bombs.) No! (She attempts to exit through the doors, but they are sealed. She then runs in the opposite direction.)

Wu: Nya! (The bombs explode.) No! (He tries to run into the fire, but is stopped by Skylor.)

Skylor: You can't!

Wu: Nya is trapped! She needs our help! I will not give up on her again!

P.I.X.A.L.: Skylor is right. It is too late, Master Wu.

(Wu falls to his knees in despair. Suddenly, Nya emerges from the wreckage in her mech.)

Wu: Nya?

Skylor: Nya!

Wu: You made it!

Nya: Are you alright?

(The others nod. Just then, Zane's voice appears from the communicator.)

Zane: P.I.X.A.L.! Can you hear me? P.I.X.A.L.?

P.I.X.A.L.: Zane! Thank goodness!

(In the subway car, the ninja celebrate.)

Kai: You did it, Zane! Way to go!

Jay: Woo hoo!

Zane: P.I.X.A.L., our situation is dire. We are in need of immediate rescue. We're trapped inside a subway car in a collapsed tunnel. Do you copy?

P.I.X.A.L.: I copy. We'll figure something out. Stand by.

Cole: Tell her to hurry! This thing isn't gonna hold up much longer. (The car collapses even more.) Get down!

Jay: What do we do?

Kai: We gotta get out of here, now!

Zane: Look, there's a hatch! (He removes the rock on the hatch and opens it.) This way! Quick! Look out!

Cole: It's coming down!

Wu: What are you waiting for? Nya, go! Go after them, quickly!

Nya: What about you?

Wu: We'll be fine. Just go! There isn't a moment to lose!

(Nya blasts off in her mech.)

Wu: Good luck, Nya.

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
