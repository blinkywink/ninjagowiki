(Rox is approaching the imprisoned Thunderfang with the yellow Prismatic Blade. Rox triumphantly holds it In front of Thunderfang, causing it and one of the chains to shift in color.)

Rox: H'yah! (Rox cuts the yellow Prismatic Chain.)

Thunderfang: And I can expect the remaining two chains to be eliminated... when?

Rox: The final Prismatic Blades are being hunted down as we speak.

Thunderfang: They'd better be.

(Zane and P.I.X.A.L. are talking with each other in the First Land Monastery.)

P.I.X.A.L.: So the Merge created new monasteries in each realm?

Zane: That is my theory. But there is so much about the Merge we still do not understand.

P.I.X.A.L.: The idea of travelling to realms once nearly impossible to reach is... invigorating. I am surprised you have not gone exploring,

Zane: As much as I enjoy learning new things, I enjoy them more when I share them with you, and you were not here.

P.I.X.A.L.: I am here now.

(Zane and P.I.X.A.L. hold hands. However, it is revealed that Lloyd, Sora, Frak, Riyu, and Zanth are standing a few feet away.)

Lloyd: We're, uh, not trying to interrupt--

Zane: Oh, yes. Uh, of course. (Zane speaks to the Portal Gate in the Monastery.) Ninjago.

(The group all start heading to the portal gate.)

(Kai and Nya are waiting at thePortal Gate in the Monastery of Spinjitzu . The portal gate opens.)

Lloyd: Nya! Kai! So glad you're here. Where's Wyldfyre?

Nya: Roby's not getting any better, so she's staying back in-- (gasps) P.I.X.A.L.?! I can't believe it! (Nya hugs P.I.X.A.L.)

P.I.X.A.L.: A pleasure to see you again, Nya. I am told it has been some time.

Kai: Zane... you're... huge.

Zane: I got swole for buffness.

Kai: (laughs) What?

Zane: A joke. Nindroids do not build muscles in that way. Hence the humor. Amusing!

Lloyd: Sora told us about the Forbidden Five trying to unleash some... big ancient dragon?

Nya: The Shatter Dragon of Chaos. They need five specific Prismatic Blades to cut the dragon's enchanted chains, one blade for each chain. They already have three of them.

Lloyd: Then good thing we've got this one.

Kai: If they need all the blades, just destroy it. Problem solved. Just call me "the Solver."

Lloyd: One of them did shatter in the tournament. So maybe... (Lloyd tries to break the blade by slamming it into the ground. When it doesn't work, Lloyd sighs.)

Kai: Let the solver solve this. (Kai uses his Elemental Power of Fire and blasts fire through the blade. It doesn't break)

Nya: So maybe the blade breaking at the tournament was some special situation? Maybe magical?

Zane: The Dragonians can sense where the blades are. The Monastery is in danger.

Frak: Then let's take it with us to find the last missing blade.

Kai: How do we find it? We don't have Dragonian trackers.

Nya: Huh, the Solver has no solve?

Kai: I... (groaning) deserve that.

P.I.X.A.L.: I believe Zane and I can find a way to track down the final blade.

(Wyldfyre is looking after a sick Roby in the City of Temples.)

Wyldfyre: Roby! Looking so much better! (Talking to the Medical Bots.) He's looking so much worse! Where are we on a cure?

Medical Bot: No solution has been identified in any of our Books of Lore.

Wyldfyre: (groaning) Books of Lore are the worst! Got it! Janus Melon Stew!

Medical Bot: Roby is not suffering from hunger.

Wyldfyre: The stew helps dragons with the Wasting Sickness. If it'll help big dragons, imagine what it'll do for tiny people... maybe cure anything!

Medical Bot: That logic is--

Wyldfyre: Perfect, I know. Where can I get Janus Melons around here?

Medical Bot: They are rare, but have been seen at the end of the Jagged Tunnels, an underground path of almost always fatal dangers.

Wyldfyre: Cool. I'm gonna pop over there.

Medical Bot: Your chances of total destruction there are near certain.

Wyldfyre: Nah. I'm good. Can I borrow a ride, though?

Medical Bot: You may use any vehicle in the city's fleet.

Wyldfyre: Noice! Something with racing stripes? Kai taught me all about racing stripes. They make things go faster.

Medical Bot: It is as if you cannot hear my warning about your impending destruction if you pursue this course of action.

Wyldfyre: My guy, for Roby, I'd face, like, a million impending destructions. Now, point me at something that goes fast!

(Arin and Ras are flying towards the World Forest in their jet.)

Ras: Do you remember the exact location of the Temple of the Dragon Cores?

Arin: Of course.

Ras: I sense you are distracted.

Arin: No... Well, my parents. That cemetery. How'd you know it was there?

Ras: I told you. Everything Jay Walker learned from his time in the Administration is now known by me.

Arin: And you'll really be able to do what you said? Bring them back?

Ras: Undoing the Merge will release powers more potent than anything seen in these realms since their creation... the ability to reshape reality itself. Do you doubt me?

Arin: No, but I have so many questions about my parents. During all those Mergequakes over the Crossroads, I swear I saw- (Suddenly, something swoops past the jet at an incredible speed.) What was that?

Ras: (looks around) There.

(The two of them see a mechanical suit of armor with wings flying ahead of them.)

Arin: Friend of yours?

Ras: An unknown enemy.

Arin: Just because they're unknown doesn't make them an enemy.

Ras: The unknown is always the enemy.

Arin: Whoa, Mr. Cynical. The unknown is usually a potential friend. Where'd he go?

(An explosive device of some kind hits the jet from above, the two look up to see that it fell from the suit of armor. Another explosive is dropped, but they evade it.)

Ras: Our potential friend is bombing us.

(The suit appears in front of them and drops another explosive, which Ras avoids.)

Arin: Yeah, okay, not a friend!

(Ras flies the jet above another explosive, then evades a second one. However, he isn't fast enough to see a third explosive dropped at him, and it hits, causing the jet's systems to start beeping. The suit of armor then approaches them from the front, firing shots with its two blasters mounted on the wings. Ras blasts forward with the jet, and the suit breaks off from its attack before crashing into Ras.)

Arin: So you seriously have no idea who this is.

Ras: No.

Arin: And it doesn't bother you that random people just want to take you down?

Ras: Strength attracts challengers.

(Ras moves the jet behind the suit of armor and starts blasting at it. The suit drops another explosive before moving away from the front of the jet, which is now looking very damaged. Ras launches the drone on top of the jet, and it starts pursuing the suit of armor, only for the suit to retract its wings and grab onto it.)

Ras: The drone will finish him off. (He heads towards the World Forest at full speed.)

(Upon seeing this, the suit lets go of the drone and activates its wings again. It starts to blast at the drone, only for the drone to move behind it. The two switch position two more times, before the drone finally gets a hit on one of the suits wings, causing it to start falling. However, the suit is able to fly up again, and it opens up, revealing Rogue. Rogue jumps onto the drone before hopping towards to the jet as his armor continues to distract the drone. He lands on the jet's lower left wing, and latches onto it with two daggers as Ras starts spinning the jet around in an attempt to get him off. Rogue manages to hold on until Ras stops.)

Ras: Get rid of him!

(The cockpit opens up, and Arin takes off his seatbelt and puts on his hood before going outside and approaching Rogue, who has just pulled out his daggers from the wing.)

Arin: Stop there! No closer!

(Arin takes out the Returning Blade, and uses Object Spinjitzu to throw it. Rogue blocks the attack with his daggers before putting them away and jumping past Arin into the cockpit. He puches Ras, but Ras dodges the punch. Three more of his punches get blocked. Outside, Arin grabs onto the Returning Blade as it comes back to him, before he is knocked off his feet by the jet suddenly moving. Meanwhile, Rogue kicks Ras into the control panel, and the jet starts plummitting to the ground. Ras manages to hit a button that ejects his seat, and starts floating down with a parachute. Rogue recalls his armor, which he jumps on to get out of the jet. He then blasts at Ras again, but Ras is able to block the blasts by spinning his hammer, which he then uses to knock Rogue away, causing him to fall. Arin is still clinging on to the wing of the jet as it approaches the ground.)

Arin: This is going to hurt! (Suddenly, two Mountain Dragons lift up the jet and place it on top of a large tree branch. The dragons land in front of him.) Thank you! You're from Riyu's tribe, the Mountain Dragons, right? (The dragons look away, and one of them gestures at him.) You want me to go with you? Sure! (He gets onto the dragon, and the fly into one of the large trees, where more Mountain Dragons are.) Riyu's family! Hi, Everyone! (Eridany approaches him from behind the legs of a brown dragon. All of the dragons bow to her. Eridany jumps up, and he catches her.) So good to see you again, Matriarch!

Brown Dragon: The Matriarch of the Mountain Tribe welcomes your return, Arin of the Crossroads. I am Leaf Blade, regent for the baby Matriarch. I speak to outsiders for Her Majesty until she comes of age.

Arin: I owe you all big-time. I was about to be a goner when those two dragons saved me. (Eridany squeaks.)

Leaf Blade: The Matriarch says it is the least we could do for a hero like you.

Arin: "Hero"? Me?

Leaf Blade: Of course. You rescued and befriended Riyu. You battled the Claws of Imperium when they came for our tribe, and you protected the Baby Matriarch. Your actions speak of the person you are.

Arin: Well, I mean, there was a whole team- (Eridany licks him.)

Leaf Blade: The Matriarch is indebted to you. Any help you need, we will provide it, hero.

Arin: Oh. I did get separated from my teacher.

Leaf Blade: Oh no, Master Lloyd is lost?

Arin: No, it's a... different teacher. You wouldn't know him. Actually, on second thought, I think I can find him on my own. (Eridany squeaks.)

Leaf Blade: If your teacher needs rescue, then I myself will accompany you on your quest, by our Draconic Majesty's request.

Arin: Sure. Yeah. Good. Good.

(Arin and Leaf Blade fly through the World Forest searching for Ras.)

Arin: Down there! I think I see him!

(Arin directs Leaf Blade to where Ras is hanging, then throws the Returning Blade to cut him loose and swoops past to catch him.)

Ras: Fool.

Arin: Weird way to say "Thanks for the rescue".

Ras: Why did you bring this dragon to me? I once hunted these creatures for Imperium.

Arin: You don't think I know that? But they insisted they help.

Ras: If they recognize me-

(Arin notices an object on top of a large branch.)

Arin: Leaf Blade, can we check that out?

Leaf Blade: Of course.

(Leaf Blade flies to the branch and lands on it. Arin hops down from his back and approaches the object, noticing it to be the suit of armor used by Rogue. He picks up the glider from the back of the armor and retracts it.)

Arin: Looks like our potential friend left this behind. (He puts the glider on his back.)

Ras: (approaches Arin) Get rid of the dragon.

Arin: Thanks for everything, Leaf Blade, but my teacher and I have to get to the Temple of the Dragon Cores, and-

Leaf Blade: The Matriarch would want me to help you. I will take you there.

(In the Monastery of Spinjitzu, the ninja are preparing to go find the final Prismatic Blade. Zane and P.I.X.A.L are constructing a device around the hilt of the Blue Prismatic Blade.)

Frak: It's like watching a graceful dance. Are you seeing this?

Sora: Little busy. If we're gonna fight Dragonians, we gotta be able to fly like them. (She uses her power to combine a bunch of metal pieces.)

(P.I.X.A.L. activates the device on the blade, and Zane lifts it up.)

Zane: I can never operate at peak efficiency without you.

P.I.X.A.L.: That is the sweetest thing you have ever said.

Zane: Perhaps, once the threat from the Forbidden Five is neutralized-

P.I.X.A.L.: Yes.

Zane: But... I did not ask a question yet.

P.I.X.A.L.: Were you going to ask if we could explore the Merged Lands together?

Zane: Affirmative.

P.I.X.A.L.: My answer is yes.

(The device starts bleeping.)

Zane: We have located the final Prismatic Blade.

(Arin and Ras are flying on Leaf Blade when two other Mountain Dragons pass by them. They exchange a quick glance with Ras before flying away.)

Ras: They recognized me.

Arin: Don't be paranoid.

(The three of them arrive at the Temple of the Dragon Cores, and Arin and Ras get off Leaf Blade's back.)

Leaf Blade: I will wait for you.

Arin: You don't have to do that.

Leaf Blade: I insist.

Arin: Okay, then. (He runs to Ras, who is already at the gate of the temple.)

(In the Jagged Tunnels, Wyldfyre is starting up a vehicle with a mech on top of it.)

Wyldfyre: All right, Janus Melons... (She revs the engine of the vehicle.) ...get ready for the getting. (The vehicle starts and flies off.) Whoo-hoo!

(In the tunnels, Wyldfyre evades acid blasting out from large pools. Rocks falls at her from above, but she dodges them as well. One rock falls into a pool of acid, sending drops of acid and rock fragments towards Wyldfyre, and she lifts up the arm of the mech to prevent a rock from hitting her face. She flies into a small tunnel, where a tentacle suddenly appears. She dodges it, only for sseveral more tentacles to grab her vehicle. Wyldfyre jumps up with the mech to clear away the tehtacles by kicking them and blasting them with her power. She is then able to fly through the cave and arrives at a pillar with Janus Melons growing on top of it. She flies to the top of the pillar, and jumps on top of it with the mech.)

Wyldfyre: Ha! Impend this destruction. (She uses the mech to grab a melon.)

(Meanwhile, Ras and Arin enter the altar in the Temple of the Dragon Cores.)

Ras: Hmm. I suppose your old teacher told you who that is?

Arin: Lloyd's grandpa, the First Spinjitzu Master. People say he created Ninjago, and some of the other realms.

Ras: He did, but he's long gone now.

(Ras takes out his hammer, jumps up and smashed the hat of the statue. The head falls off and breaks into pieces.)

Arin: What-what did you do that for?

Ras: Spirit of the Temple! Face me!

(The spirit emerges from the altar.)

Spirit of the Temple: Welcome, travelers. I... The statue!

Ras: Spirit, is there a way to create Source Dragon Icons without gathering Elemental Powers?

Spirit of the Temple: There is, but that knowledge is reserved for descendants of the First Spinjitzu Master.

Ras: I have this. (He holds out his hammer.)

Spirit of the Temple: A Source Dragon Icon? Impressive. Almost as powerful as a Dragon Core.

Ras: Exactly. (He hits the spirit with the hammer, and it starts to damage the spirit.) Tell me what I need to know!

Spirit of the Temple: (groans in pain) Oh, please, no!

Arin: Ras, stop! You're hurting him! (Ras shoves him away.)

Ras: Tell me!

Spirit of the Temple: (groans) In the depths of the Underworld, the Casket of Bones... Forge of the Forgotten! (Ras shoves him away.)

Ras: Most helpful. You may go.

Spirit of the Temple: Fie! A pox upon you villains!

Arin: I'm not a- (The spirit disappears back into the altar.)

Ras: (grabs Arin) Why do you care what name that spirit calls you? (shoves Arin away)

Arin: Villains hurt people. I don't want to hurt anyone.

Ras: Will you do what it takes to get your parents back?

Arin: Of course.

(The pair leaves the Temple, but five dragons stop them outside.)

Leaf Blade: There he is! Is it truly him? You were foolish to come among us, Ras of Imperium. Did you think your crimes would be forgotten? (He notices Arin.) Beware, Arin! The person masquerading as your teacher was the leader of the Claws of Imperium!

Arin: I know. I... I can explain.

Leaf Blade: You knew? But his soldiers hunted us! They caged Riyu!

Arin: Yeah, but it's... complicated.

Leaf Blade: No, it is simple. We will take him before the Matriarch, where he will be judged for his crimes against Dragon-kind.

(Ras takes out his hammer and prepares to fight, but Arin gets between him and the dragons.)

Arin: This doesn't have to be a fight.

Leaf Blade: You defend this wretch? What happened to you, Arin of the Crossroads?

Arin: You have every right to be angry at Ras, to have a need for justice, but I have a need too. I lost my family in the Merge. I've sacrificed everything else in my life to find them. I need Ras if I have any hope of getting them back. You say you're indebted to me? Then let Ras go. I'm begging you.

(The dragons briefly gather to discuss the matter amongst themselves.)

Leaf Blade: No. It is too much to ask. We are taking Ras with us. (The dragons prepare to fight as Ras jumps in front of Arin to meet them.) I'm sorry, Arin of the Crossroads.

Arin: So am I. (He puts on his hood and activates the glider on his back.)

(As Ras is about to strike the dragons, Arin grabs him from behind and starts flying away with him.)

Ras: Running is needless. I can destroy them!

Arin: That's what I'm afraid of. (He quickly looks behind him.) I think we lost them.

(Suddenly, the five dragons appear behind them in pursuit. Upon seeing this, Arin tries to speed up.)

Ras: They have a tactical advantage in the skies. We must stand and fight.

Arin: Not happening. There's something I learned from being a kid on the streets that a big guy like you could never teach me.

Ras: What could that possibly be?

(The two approach a cluster of tightly packed branches.)

Arin: How being small... (He deactivates his glider and slips through the branches, causing two of the pursuing dragons to get stuck in them.) ...can be awesome.

(Arin makes a sharp turn upwards near a big tree, and two other dragons bump into the tree and fall down, leaving just Leaf Blade trailing behind them. Arin fires the blasters on the wings of the glider to break off a small branch that he then avoids, causing it to fall onto Leaf Blade.)

Ras: Ridiculous tactics.

Arin: (sighs) You're welcome.

(Wyldfyre brings the stew she made from the Janus Melons to Roby.)

Wyldfyre: I've got it! This stew is going to do wonders! (She feeds some of the stew to Roby.) Old Roby back in no time! In no time! (She feeds him some extra stew.) A little extra time, maybe?

Medical Bot: No change in his vitals.

Wyldfyre: How could it not work? You said my logic was perfect!

Medical Bot: That is not what I-

Wyldfyre: If this isn't the cure, w-what is?

Medical Bot: For many illnesses, there simply is no cure.

(Wyldfyre frustratedly trows the stew away, then uses her power to express her anger before finally laying onto the bed, defeated.)

(Ras and Arin land on a branch.)

Arin: This would all be a lot easier if everyone wasn't always trying to capture you.

Ras: I told you. Strength attracts-

Arin: Challenge, I know. Not sure we need extra challenges right now. (He deactivates the glider.) Everyone thinks I'm like you, a villain. The ninja probably think the same thing.

Ras: Who cares what the ninja think? Your need to be liked is a weakness.

Arin: Guess it's not like I'll see any of them anytime soon.

(Suddenly, Sora lands nearby in a jet she constructed, followed by Kai, Nya and Lloyd riding on Riyu's back, Zane, and a flying car with the rest of the ninja.)

Sora: Arin?

Arin: Sora?

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
