(At the City of Imperium, the electro-barrier is flickering and Imperians are waiting in long queues.)

Imperium Announcer: Please have your ID ready for your new household battery. We are low on inventory, so not everyone in line will be fortunate enough to get a replacement at this time.

(The Imperians groan.)

Imperium Citizen 1: Well, Percival did ask us to conserve energy. I bet I could make it another day or two if I had to.

Imperium Citizen 2: Wasn't like this when Empress Beatrix was in charge, that's all I'm sayin'.

Imperium Citizen 1: Beatrix was torturing dragons for power!

Imperium Citizen 2: Which was terrible, of course. Just didn't have an energy crisis before, is all I'm sayin'.

Imperium Citizen 1: Starting a new government and infrastructure is not easy.

(The lights in the city keep flickering, and the Imperians groan and grumble.)

Imperium Citizen 1: Seriously?

(A blackout occurs.)

Imperium Citizen 2: Ugh, another blackout?

(Percival and a scientist are trying to fix the power issue from the Imperium Broadcast Tower)

Percival: Third blackout this week! Our temporary solves clearly aren't doing enough.

Imperium Assistant 1: (sighs) Because this old grid is designed for dragon energy. We need the time for a complete conversion, all while running streetlights, public transportation, the energy barrier--

Percival: That's it! The energy barrier! That barrier is a leftover from Empress Beatrix. Let's reroute that power for the people's energy.

Imperium Assistant 1: But with the Claws gone, using that barrier is Imperium's only line of defense.

Percival: Who are we defending ourselves from? This is an era of peace! Shut it down!

(She presses an orange button to shut down the energy barrier. Percival looks as the city's lights turn back on and everyone cheers.)

Percival: Ah, looks like that did the trick.

(The Imperians panic and Percival is shocked when a Mutation Monster appears and attacks the city.)

Imperium Citizen 2: What is that thing?

(The monster attacks the Imperians and blasts them with Elemental Energy.)

(In Umbrasilvis, Freya brings a bucket of water to her windmill, where Sora, Wyldfyre, and Frak are nursing Riyu after he was blasted by the Mutation Monster.)

Sora: Come on, Riyu, pull through.

(Frak uses a cup to take water from the bucket and gives it to Riyu.)

Wyldfyre: He took a direct hit of whatever that monster was blasting. I've never seen a dragon survive such a thing, and I've seen a lot of dragons in battle.

Sora: He's always been a special dragon.

Freya: Do you think he could eat? We make delicious Umbrasilvan wheatcakes.

Sora: It's worth a shot, I guess. Thank--Thank you for all your help.

Freya: Thank you. That dragon is a hero. He saved our village--

Johnny Courageous: (enters the windmill) You still tending to that beast?

Sora: Riyu is not a beast, he's one of us, a Ninja, and you owe him your life, you uptight wad.

Johnny: By jove, I didn't realize this subject would lead to such crass name-calling. Anyway, I just wanted to let everyone know that monster is now safely contained in our airship for future study and display. In case you were wondering. (Some Umbrasilvans drag Johnny out of the windmill.) I-I'll just... see myself out then.

Wyldfyre: "The Numbness."

Frak: The what?

Wyldfyre: When dragons are exposed to a dangerous substance-- poisonous leffberries, the spray of a saber-toothed eel, stuff like that-- they can get something we call "The Numbness." It's... usually fatal.

Sora: Usually?

Wyldfyre: I heard stories of Dragon Elders curing the Numbness by converting a small amount of whatever caused the problem into an antidote.

Frak: Convert how?

Wyldfyre: I don't know! I'm just telling you what I heard. Do I look like a dragon doctor?

Sora: So converting venom could help his body's natural defenses?

Frak: But he wasn't poisoned by the venom of a buck-toothed eel--

Wyldfyre: Saber-toothed.

Frak: He was blasted by a monster!

Sora: Yeah, but that monster looked like it was made out of Elemental Energy.

Frak: So?

Sora: Look, before you joined the team, I didn't think I had my own powers. I thought Riyu gave them to me.

Frak: Weird, but okay.

Sora: Every time I got a spark of power, I could feel the energy rolling through me, like a wave.

(Riyu groans weakly.)

Sora: Like a substance that can affect the body.

Frak: Oh, so we reverse that monster's energy somehow, and give it back to Riyu?

Wyldfyre: Oh, so when she says it, it sounds smart? (blows a raspberry)

Sora: I just need to create something that can convert that kind of energy.

(Riyu groans weakly. Freya gives a box of equipment to Sora.)

Freya: We don't have much advanced technology in Umbrasilvis, but what little we have gathered is basically all here. I hope it helps you save the hero dragon.

Sora: Oh, wow, thank you. (Freya leaves. Sora tries to use her elemental power to build a device to cure Riyu, but her powers are too uncontrollable.) How am I going to get to do this with our powers on the fritz? (She tries again to use her power, but fails.)

(Arin, Euphrasia, and Lloyd try to fight against the Elemental Monsters in the Whispering Wood.)

Euphrasia: Y'ah! (Strains as she tries to use her powers.)

(A chopper lands nearby, and Zane comes out of it, attacking the Mutation Monster.)

Zane: Surprise-surprise! (Zane throws shurikens at the Acid Monster and Earth Monsters.) H'ah! (Zane performs Spinjitzu.)

Lloyd: Zane? How did you find us?

Zane: When my powers got all janky...(Zane throws shurikens at the Mutation Monster.) ...Pixal and I split up to search for you all.

Lloyd: Janky? (kicks the Acid Monster)

Zane: I say "janky" now, Lloyd. H'yah! (Zane kicks the Mutation Monster and an Earth Monster.) I was flying over these woods and heard sounds of battle, and guess who was right at the center of it? And I see you're dealing with Elemental Monsters in this land as well. H'yah! Oof!

Lloyd: They're in other places, too?

Zane: They're all-- Ugh! --over the place, and nearly unbeatable! H'yah!

(Lloyd strains as he tries to use his powers, hears the wood whispering, and channels the trees of life. Sparks burst from the trees. Lloyd grabs his sword and straps it to his back. A beam of energy hits Lloyd and restores his elemental powers.)

Lloyd: Ugh! Yes!

Euphrasia: Powers are back again!

Zane: Again?

(Euphrasia blasts an Earth Monster with her powers. The Mutation Monster blasts at Zane and Arin. Zane dodges and Arin collects nearby rocks. Zane jumps on the tentacles and uses them to lift up Arin, who performs Spinjitzu and transfers it to the rocks, dropping them on the Mutation Monster. Zane blasts the Mutation Monster with his powers and defeats it, Euphrasia defeats an Earth Monster, and Lloyd defeats the Acid Monster and the other Earth Monster.)

Euphrasia: Did we win?

Zane: I can sense trace readings of their energies, yet I think they've moved on, but why are our powers stable here?

Lloyd: It won't last (His powers fade and he takes off his mask.)

Zane: I see. What a bummer.

Arin: Zane, did you... reprogram yourself or something? "Bummer" didn't use to be part of your vocab.

Zane: My time with Pixal did wonders for my disposition. Some might say I'm more "chill," and when did you rejoin the team, Arin?

Arin: (chuckles) Oh, man, you've missed a lot!

Lloyd: Including the reason our powers are messed up. We, uh, did something that angered the Source Dragons, so half of them pulled their powers from the Merged Lands.

Zane: I skip one adventure, and the entire world turns to chaos.

Lloyd: Hey, we're searching for a way to fix it all. Any interest in helping us find something called the Temple of the Green Ninja?

(Zane agrees to help.)

(Jay, Kai, and Nya sneak around the Administration.)

Kai: So, what even is a "Department of Vacation Simulation"?

Jay: Administration employees earn vacation days, but aren't allowed to actually go anywhere... ...so we-- I mean, they-- go up and sit in Vacation Simulator chairs. It tricks your brain into thinking you're at the beach, the mountains, an amusement park, whatever.

Nya: So the Agents like that? A Fake-Cation?

Jay: (laughs) A "Fake-Cation!" I should've thought of that one! The Agents here don't even know it's weird. Land of Madness, remember?

Nya: So how do we get there?

Kai: Uh, the elevator, duh!

Jay: That's how Agents go up and down floors! Don't be ridiculous.

Kai: Don't have to bite my head off. Like you never had any bad ideas.

Jay: As far as I remember, no, never.

Kai: (scoffs and chuckles) When you get your memories back, you're in for a rude awakening.

Administration Announcer: Attention all Agents, there are three Ninja within the confines of the building. Please get to the nearest safety station--

Jay: "Safety Station"?

Administration Announcer: because the highly efficient Robo Eliminators are now online.

Freya: Our family doesn't have much, but we made this with what we have.

Frak: Thank you. (sees Sora finishing the device) Gave up on your powers?

Sora: I built inventions by hand long before I even knew what an elemental power was.

Wyldfyre: He's sinking deeper into the Numbness. Whatever you're doing, Sora, do it faster.

Sora: Got it!

Wyldfyre: I should be a motivational speaker.

Sora: Time to collect some Elemental Monster energy. Let's go!

(Sora and Frak go to the airship and find the mutation monster still trapped in the containment unit.)

Sora: What the--?

(Sora and Frak see a banner and balloons, and heard drumming as the members of the Explorer's Club salute. Johnny hits Gweeg and Rowan gives cake to Reginald.)

Reginald: A speech is in order! (clears throat) I'll give it. (Johny plays a drum roll.) Members of the Umbrasilvian Expedition, congratulations on my-- our-- great victory! Defeating an unknown monster in open combat and saving an innocent town!

Explorer's Club: Huzzah!

Sora: What? Riyu did that!

Reginald: Yes, yes, yes, the dragon was helpful, and certainly deserves honorable mention, but thankfully, we were the ones who contained the creature, rendering it safe.

Sora: I built that containment unit.

Reginald: And you did an excellent job... of obeying me, since I led you do to it.

Sora: What!

(Frak sighs)

Reginald: And now, thanks to my-- our skill, we'll transport this unparalleled scientific discovery to Ninjago, where it can be put on display for the benefit of all... and the profit of me-- uh-- us!

Explorer's Club: Huzzah! (Johnny hits Gweeg again.)

(Reginald eats a cake.)

Sora: Ugh! We don't have time for this! (Sora puts the device on the containment unit to collect the monster's elemental energy.)

Reginald: Excuse me. I don't know what you're doing, but that's property of the Explorer's Club, and I forbid you to interfere with it in any way.

Frak: Dad? We need this right now. It could save Riyu's life.

Reginald: Well, why didn't you say so? Well, in that case, we should enter negotiations on how much to charge you for access to--

(The device has fully charged and Sora puts it away off and switches it off.)

Sora: Done.

Reginald: Okay, yes, uh, you were only able to do that because I gave you permission. (Sora and Frak leave.) Permission granted!

Zane: ...so I said to Pixal, how was I to know the Ampfedrox people incubate their offspring in containers that look like teacups? She laughed for 45 seconds straight, and I laughed for 52 seconds. It was quite an experience.

(Arin and Euphrasia laugh.)

Lloyd: I'm... lost. Let's take a break here. Try to get our bearings.

Arin: Sounds good to me.

Euphrasia: Remember when I was too scared to admit I had powers?

Arin: How could I forget? Almost got eaten by a giant octopus thing.

Euphrasia: But now that my powers don't work, I'm scared that I can't protect the Cloud Kingdom anymore.

Arin: There's more to self-defense than powers. Trust me, it's something I'm very familiar with.

Euphrasia: Yeah, but you're like a Spinjitzu genius. I could never do that.

Arin: I taught myself basic Spinjitzu alone. Maybe I could teach you?

Euphrasia: Really?

Arin: If you'd like me to. (Euphrasia smiles and agrees.) Okay, it starts with balance. Try this first position. H'ah!

(Lloyd keeps concentrating to find the Temple of the Green Ninja.)

Zane: Why are you squinting?

Lloyd: Trying to sense where this temple could be.

Zane: By squinting?

Lloyd: By concentrating. Ugh! I don't even know what I'm even doing!

Zane: Maybe your doubts are clouding your senses. Don't focus on them.

Lloyd: How do I just not focus on doubts?

Zane: Sketch. The forest, the rocks, anything that comes to mind.

Lloyd: When did you get into art? Art is like... the opposite of you.

Zane: It's a way of processing problems without the constraints of logic-based thinking.

Lloyd: Trying to get away from logic? You?

Zane: Logic is an important and powerful tool, but I've learned being too focused on it can get in the way of creative insights.

Lloyd: Wow, Zane. This voyage with Pixal really did have an impact on you.

Zane: It did. Love is also an important and powerful tool. (The trees whisper.) If it helps, think of drawing as a form of meditation. Pencil to the paper, breathing

Lloyd: (starts breathing and drawing while the wood keeps whispering. Lloyd soon finishes the drawing.) Huh. I think it worked. (The drawing shows a map of the Temple of the Green Ninja.)

Sora: Please work. (She injects elemental energy into Riyu via the device.) Why didn't you say anything?

Frak: What?

Sora: Your dad, he took credit for everything, and you just let him do it.

Frak: Look, no one knows how annoying he is more than I do, but growing up, in my house, you didn't ever disagree with him. Speaking your mind? Not acceptable.

Sora: But you're not a kid anymore.

Frak: No. No, I'm not.

Sora: (finishes the injection) Well?

Wyldfyre: (She checks Riyu's body.) No change.

(Sora, Frak, and Wyldfyre sadly groan.)

(The Robo Eliminators keep searching for the Ninja.)

Eliminator 1: Ninja located?

Eliminator 2: Negative.

Eliminator 1: Ninja must be located with efficiency for purposes of... elimination.

Eliminator 1 and 2: Eliminate! Eliminate! Eliminate! Eliminate!

Kai: Uh, those things are supposed to be highly efficient?

Jay: For this place, absolutely. Maybe they brought in an outside Efficiency Consultant? We should be clear if we just go around-- Ooh (He, Kai, and Nya get caught by the Robo Eliminators.) Funny thing! Sometimes people think we're Ninja, but we're just Ninja cosplayers. (chuckles awkwardly)

Eliminator 3: Sneaky Ninja lie... detected. (they start blasting the Ninja.) Eliminate! Eliminate!

Sora: He just... he needs to be okay. This little guy changed my life.

Frak: Little?

Sora: He'll always be a little guy to me

(Riyu's breath wheezes.)

Wyldfyre: (checks Riyu's body again) No... (Riyu wheezes.) Come on.

Sora: Wh-what?

Wyldfyre: He's not getting better. Nothing's changing.

Frak: You said it would work.

Wyldfyre: I also said I'm not a dragon doctor, remember?

Sora: I-I just took a guess at how much energy he needed. Maybe he needs more?

Wyldfyre: We're losing him. We need to keep trying until it works

Sora: We'll be right back.

(Sora and Frak go back to the airship to collect more elemental energy from the Mutation Monster. Once they arrive, The find that Mutation Monster escaped from the containment unit.)

Frak: It's gone.

Sora: Someone opened the Containment Unit and let it out.

Frak: Who would--?

(Sora and Frak hear screaming.)

(The Robo Eliminators go to the Department of Vacation Simulation to check if the ninja are there.)

Eliminator 1: Locate Ninja. Eliminate!

Jay: Now!

Robo Eliminators: Eliminate! Eliminate!

(Kai dodges the attack. Jay kicks a Robo Eliminator near a simulator, and Kai and Nya plug the Robo Eliminator into it. Jay kicks another Robo Eliminator and Kai plugs the Robo Eliminator's head into another simulator.)

Jay: Nya! I'm thinking these bots are overworked!

Nya: (uses the computer to activate the vacation simulators connected to the Robo Eliminators.) Then it sounds like they need a vacation!

(The Robo Eliminators stop blasting them and power down.)

Robo Eliminator: Whee!

Kai: So, what Fake-Cation did we send them on?

Nya: Uh... the Land of Lee, Cavern Five.

Jay: Uh, Robots on waterslides? How does that work?

Nya: As much as I'd love to ruminate on the physics of robots and water tubes, we came here for a reason. Oh... Agent Donovan wasn't lying. This equipment that beams vacations into people's brains could be used to block memories, in theory.

Jay: Not in theory. I'm proof, but... can you reverse it?

Kai: Of course she can. You can, can't you, sis?

Nya: Maybe?

Jay: What are we waiting for?

Nya: Hold it. This is risky. I can't guarantee it's gonna work.

Jay: So? Then I don't get my memories back? Big deal. I already don't have them.

Nya: No. If this fails, we could damage your brain, making it impossible for you to form new memories ever again.

Jay: Like a permanent state of amnesia?

Nya: Yes.

Jay: Doesn't matter. Let's do it.

Kai: Jay, think this through. You're here, with us, back on the team. You can relearn Spinjitzu. We can make new memories, better than the old ones.

Nya: He's right. You're still the Jay I love even without your memories.

Jay: You know why I have to do this. I had a whole life, apparently a whole wonderful life, with all of you, and I can't go on another moment without remembering it.

Nya: This might hurt.

(Jay screams.)

Kai: Turn it off. Turn it off! It's hurting him!

Nya: I think this is how it works!

(Jay's memories start coming back and the machine powers down.)

Nya: Oh no... Oh no!

Kai: Jay? You with us, buddy?

(Nya takes off the machine from Jay's head and hold his hand. Jay looks surprised, and then smiles. Jay and Nya kiss each other.)

Kai: I'll take that as a yes. (Jay and Nya continue to kiss.) All right, we get it. That's enough. (Jay and Nya are still kissing.) Come on, dude, that's my sister!

(Jay and Nya stop kissing each other.)

Euphrasia: So he heard whispers, then drew a map? I mean, don't get me wrong, I'm a Writer of Destiny, but... I'm not sure--

Lloyd: We're here.

Arin: We are?

Euphrasia: Whoa!

Lloyd: We are.

(Arin, Euphrasia, Lloyd, and Zane see the Temple of the Green Ninja in front of them.)

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
