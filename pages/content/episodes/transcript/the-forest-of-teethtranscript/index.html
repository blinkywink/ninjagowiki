(Lightning starts to strike while Kai and Daidan are flying. Then Kai sees winged monsters coming towards them. Daidan tries to dodge them but eventually one of the monsters hits her.)

Wyldfyre: Hideous winged monster attack? Oh yeah! This is going to get good!

Kai: Wyldfyre! Stop interrupting. (Kai moves the picture away, revealing his face.) I never wanted anyone to find these journals from my time in the Land of the Monsters.

Wyldfyre: I know, but luckily, finding hidden stuff is one of my many, many talents. Continue!

Kai: Fine! Only because I know you'll never let it go until I do.

Wyldfyre: Not letting go is also one of my talents. (Takes out popcorn.)

(Kai and Daidan start falling down while flying bug-like monsters continues to attack Daidan. Kai tries to push them back by using Fire. But lightning strikes and both of them continue to fall.)

(Eventually, Kai wakes up in the Forest of Teeth and starts looking around.)

Past Kai: Daidan, Daidan, are you okay?

Past Daidan: (Grunts.) Do I look okay? Ninjas are supposed to be observant.

Past Kai: Can you walk? We need cover, in case those things come back.

Past Daidan: (Tries to stand up.) I'm trying. I just need a minute. (Falls to ground.)

Past Fuzz: HELP! Fuzz need help!

(Fuzz tries to escape from a plant-like monster, but monster grabs him and escapes. Fuzz screams.)

Past Kai: Huh?

Past Daidan: Go, ninja. I will manage.

(Kai starts running while looking around for Fuzz.)

Past Fuzz: Fuzz need help!

(A tree starts shooting spikes that gets stuck on the ground. Kai jumps over them. Then the tree shoots spikes again, but Kai sees them and blasts fire at the tree while stepping back. But nothing happens to the tree.)

Past Kai: Fireproof?

(A plant-like monster tries to attack Kai, but Kai escapes.)

Past Kai: Great. Just great.

(Multiple trees attack to Kai, but Kai jumps and all of the spikes get stuck on the ground. Kai starts running again while trees still attack him.)

Past Fuzz: HELP! PLEASE!

(Some monsters drop boulders on top of Kai from their mouths. Kai escapes but one of the boulders starts rolling towards Kai.)

Past Fuzz: HELP!

(Kai escapes from the boulder and sees the monster who grabs Fuzz.)

Past Kai: Let's find out of this thing is sword-proof, too.

(Kai attacks the monster and cuts it in a half.)

Past Fuzz: Thank you, hero. Now Fuzz can be of service for you, too.

Past Kai: Look, I know you mean well, but I'm a ninja, and I'm barely...

(Some tentacles start approaching Kai and Fuzz.)

Past Kai: Uh-oh! Fire doesn't work on these things. Looks like the old slice and dice is the only way out of here. So, hold on tight! (Kai holds Fuzz.)

(Kai attacks to one of them but gets knocked back.)

Past Fuzz: I can help.

Past Kai: I love your energy, but trust me. (Fuzz hops Kai's back.) Hey, what do you...

(Fuzz connects his antenna to Kai's head. Kai's eyes turn into green and a little green shockwave happens.)

Past Kai: Woah!

(Kai jumps one of the trees and then jumps again, destroying all of the spikes in the process. Then three tentacles try to attack him, but Kai cuts all of them one by one. Then Kai uses one of the severed tentacles and attack to another. He cuts two trees and attacks one of the boulder dropping monsters which causes the monster to drop its boulder. Kai dodges the boulder by jumping on one of the trees and kicks the boulder.)

Past Kai: You weren't kidding. You are a lot of help, Bug.

Past Fuzz: Fuzz is the name.

(Daidan, Kai and Fuzz walk in the Forest of Teeth. Fuzz stops connecting his antennas to Kai's head.)

Past Kai: (Grunts.) I'm not feeling so good.

(Kai's eyes turns to normal.)

Past Fuzz: Sorry, Fuzz's fault. Fuzz's help takes some getting used to.

Past Kai: (Looks at his hand.) I must have got hurt. But what is this? Why is it so weird?

Past Fuzz: Ahh, must've come from the fight. You'll back to normal soon.

Past Kai: I hope so. Come on. Let's go find shelter for the night.

(They start walking again while some mysterious figure watching them. The mysterious figure laughs and leaves.)

Wyldfyre: Woah. A sky attack, a monster jungle, insect power up and some creepy weirdo following you. That story is wild! But why did you want to keep all that secret?

Kai: Because that was only the beginning.

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
