(At the dragon stables, Sora and Wyldfyre arrive through the portal gate and run down the stairs.)

Sora: Come on, we gotta-- (She almost falls down the stairs...) Whoa! (...but Wyldfyre catches her.)

(They see Fritz, Spitz and Bonzle hollow.)

Sora: Fritz and Spitz? Oh, no! They're hollow!

Wyldfyre: Those spectral jerk-faces attacked the Monastery!

(Geo and Mr. Frohicky approach them.)

Mr. Frohicky: No one attacked the Monastery, Wyldfyre.

Geo: But can't say the same for the Land of Lost Things. Do you know what happened to them?

Sora: Yeah, the same thing is happening all over the place. (Riyu arrives and hugs her, making them fall of the ground.) Aw, I missed you, too, Riyu.

(Riyu growls something to Sora.)

Sora: I know, I'm sorry I left, but I had to make sure Arin was okay. (Riyu turns around.) And he'll be back to see you, too.

(Riyu groans in joy and carries Sora on his back to the others.)

Geo: (To Sora.) So people are getting drained like this outside of the Land of Lost Things?

Sora: It's Thunderfang... (She gets off Riyu.) using the Forbidden Five to collect souls, trying to become a Source Dragon.

Geo: Tell me there's a plan. The Ninja always have a plan.

Sora: We do, but it's wild.

(Fritz walks around groaning like a zombie.)

(In the Spectral Lands, Thunderfang continues to draw power from the healing elixir to become a Source Dragon, while the Forbidden Five watch.)

(Arin and Lloyd are in their mechs, with Arin holding the Unbidden Storm.)

Lloyd: So we take that thing to the well where Thunderfang's energy's coming from, and once the other teams wake up their halves of the Arc Dragon of Focus, it'll be summoned right to our spot. Piece of cake! Day saved. Uh, night saved.

Arin: "Piece of Cake"? Really? That's even optimistic for me.

Lloyd: Trying to get psyched up. Yes, this will be hard, but if we start thinking it's impossible, we don't stand a chance.

(They exit the Spectral Lands Monastery and see Thunderfang receiving energy.)

Arin: Uh, you take this thing. I'm gonna mess it up. (He offers the Unbidden Storm to Lloyd.)

Lloyd: Why would you mess it up?

Arin: (He sighs.) Come on, messing things up is what I do best. It's basically my Elemental Power.

Lloyd: You think I started out with skills?

Arin: Uh, yeah. You're the legendary Green Ninja.

Lloyd: You didn't know me as a kid. There were plenty of reasons to think I'd never be a Ninja, but confidence is just another skill to build. Someday, you're gonna be one of the greatest Ninja to ever live.

Arin: No, I'm not. Lloyd, I finally have my parents, and the Land of Lee is filled with other kids' parents, other peoples' loved ones. I came here with you to release their souls from Thunderfang, to help reunite families like mine, but when this is all over... I'm quitting the Ninja.

(Lloyd's mouth drops open when he hears Arin say this.)

(At the Monastery of Mysterium, a portal gate appears out of nowhere and Nya falls out of it, onto the dragon statue.)

Nya: What the--? (The portal gate disappears.) Weird. Well, that's Mysterium for ya. (She stands up.) Hmm. (She takes a paper out of her pocket.) All right, as long as Bleckt's directions to the cave are accurate, I got this. Yup, I got his. (She steps down from the statue.) Don't need anyone else. Definitely not Jay. (She chuckles.) I mean, forget about me and him, what about being a Ninja? How is being a mercenary who cares only about money more exciting? Hello? Ninja! You're talking to yourself again, Nya. (She sighs.) Anyway... Cave of Tones! (She walks towards the gate and someone or something outside pounds against the door.) I'm sure that's just Mysterium being weird and Mysterium-y

(Nya continues walking towards the gate, opens one of the doors and Gandalaria, still hollow, enters through it.)

Gandalaria: (Moaning.) Janet...

Nya: Sorceress?

Gandalaria: (Groaning.) Janet... Janet...

Nya: Oh, no. Not you too. (She is kicked by someone and falls to the ground.) What? (She gasps when she sees that whoever kicked her is wearing a large suit of armor.)

(Sora and Wyldfyre speak to Riyu.)

Sora: Here's the deal, Riyu. We gotta get to Djinjago, fast, but we don't know where it is. The only times we've been there, we were summoned, or Zanth flew us... (Riyu has an idea.) Looks like you're thinking what we're thinking. (She and Wyldfyre climb the stairs, while Riyu flies, to Zanth's stall.) Zanth! (Rontu appears.) And Rontu? (She runs to Rontu and hugs her.)

Rontu: I've been helping this one with his Ninja training. He has shown much potential. (Riyu grumbles modestly.) I anticipate his first Spinjitzu Tornado will be soon, if he keeps up his hard work.

Sora: I just achieved my first tornado too, like, yesterday! You can do it, buddy! We can do it. (She and Riyu give a high five (or high four in Riyu's case).) Zanth, you took us to Djinjago before. Can you do it again?

(Zanth roars something.)

Rontu: She says she feels her homeland re-growing, and would love to see what it's become.

Wyldfyre: Whoo! Let's go! Dibs on the front seat! (She comes down the stairs.)

Rontu: Riyu, you should stay and focus on your training. (Riyu grumbles to her.) True, little one. Not much point in training if you don't save the world now. You have always been a wise little dragon. Go, but please be careful.

(Riyu grumbles in agreement.)

(Nya and the warrior face each other, with Gandalaria between them.)

Nya: Sorceress, watch out!

(The warrior almost hits Gandalaria with their sword, but Nya stops them using her spear. She holds the blow with her spear for some time before jumping and shooting Water at them, but they takes it and she lands. The warrior tries Nya again, but she dodges and the blow almost hits Gandalaria, but ends up making her hat fly off and fall back on her head. The warrior tries to hit Nya again, but she dodges with a somersault and kicks the legs of the warrior's armor out, knocking them down and seemingly defeating them. Nya starts to walk away until she notices the warrior floating without them legs.)

Nya: What is this thing? (The armor tries to attack her, but she dodges it and cuts off its right arm, knocking it down gain.) Had enough? (The armor throws a punch that knocks her back, but she takes it, does a somersault, cuts off its other arm and knocks it down, but it gets up again .) Knock it off! (The armor advances with a headbutt.) Or I will! (She performs Spinjitzu, jumps and kicks the helmet of the armor, causing it to stop moving and a coconut to fall out of the helmet.) What the--? (She picks up the coconut from the ground.)

Coconut: (In Gandalaria's voice.) Put me down, you oaf!

(Nya gets scared and drops the talking coconut.)

Nya: Sorceress?

Coconut: Do I know you?

Nya: I'm Nya. We met after the Battle of the Blood Moon. You helped us save the Cloud Kingdom, remember?

Coconut: Blood Moon? Cloud Kingdom? You're just doing words, trying to confuse a kindly old coconut.

Nya: Are you the Sorceress or a coconut?

Coconut: Eh, a little bit of a column A, a little bit of column B, you know how it is. (Nya remains confused.) I'm a magical back-up copy of the Sorceress' personality. The non-coconut Sorceress made me a bunch of years ago, in case Janet's whole mind-wipe scheme ever worked.

Gandalaria: Janet... Janet...

Coconut copy: I just got activated, probably because that one's pfft! right now. (To Gandalaria.) No offense!

Gandalaria: Janet... Janet... (She walks to the pond underneath the dragon statue and trips over it.)

Nya: Okay... but why a coconut?

Coconut copy: Good melons were out of season. So, what's your deal? Did we, uh... did you and the non-coconut Sorceress have plans? Ladies' weekend? Staycation?

Nya: (She sighs.) Long story short, (She takes the paper out of her pocket.) I need to go to a place called Cave of Tones. Fast. Ever heard of it?

Coconut copy: Yeah, I'm not tone deaf. Pfft! Even as a coconut, I've still got it! Uh... (She reads the paper.) "West past Mt. Mollin"? (She jumps onto Nya's head.) Yowza! Those directions are outdated.

Nya: (She groans.) Bleckt!

Coconut copy: Yeah, that mountain isn't there anymore. Earthquake. Now it's just a vast chasm of doom. Where are you getting your travel info, an ancient vault?

Nya: Actually, yes. (She puts the paper in her pocket.)

Coconut copy: Huh! Then it's a good thing I'm going with you! I will show you a current route, one that won't get you killed. Maimed, maybe, but not killed... probably. Let's go!

(They leave and Gandalaria manages to get out of the pond.)

Gandalaria: Janet... Janet... Janet... Janet...

(Frak and Kai arrive through the portal gate with Cole hollow.)

Geo: (He gasps.) Oh no! Not you too, Cole!

Kai: He fought hard to free the Finders' souls, but...

Geo: Come on. I've got you. (He helps Cole down the stairs.)

Kai: Wyldfyre!

(Kai and Wyldfyre run towards each other.)

Kai and Wyldfyre: Flame fist! (They beat their flaming fists against each other.)

Wyldfyre: And look who I brought. (She turns to reveal Sora.)

Kai: Sora?

Sora: Hey. I'm back.

(Kai goes to Sora and hugs her, who hugs him back. Frak approaches them.)

Frak: Sora, I couldn't sleep, I was so worried about you.

Sora: You were?

Frak: Well, I mean, we all were, not just me. I'm sure the others couldn't sleep-- Where's Arin?

Sora: Uh, with Lloyd. Look, as you probably know already, the Merged Realms have a big problem, and we need to help.

Kai: What do we have to do?

(Nya and the coconut copy run towards the Cave of Tones as a thunderous sound from Thunderfang's storm is heard)

Nya: (She paints.) ...and after the Merge, all us Ninja ended up in different places.

Coconut copy: Did you find each other?

Nya: Yeah, but... I'm not sure that was a good thing.

Coconut copy: Ooh, this sounds juicy, and I would know. I'm a coconut. Go on.

(Nya stops running and the coconut copy falls from her head, but she catches her before falling into a cave with glowing, floating mushrooms.)

Coconut copy: Oh. Well, this shouldn't be here. (She jumps back onto Nya's head.)

Nya: Then why is it here? You led me to a dead end!

Coconut copy: Magical lands sometimes change for no reason. How am I supposed to know? I'm a backup coconut!

(Nya notices the glowing, floating mushrooms and gets an idea.)

Nya: Can I cross on those?

Coconut copy: Yes... and no. The mercurial mushrooms aren't always the most stable.

Nya: It's the only option we have. (She performs Spinjitzu and jumps on a mushroom.)

Coconut copy: Uh... so you were saying getting the Ninja (Nya jumps to another mushroom.) back together wasn't a good thing?

Nya: Yeah. (She jumps to another mushroom.) Jay... (Then to another.) I... We were together, before, but when I found him, he lost his memory... (She jumps to another mushroom.) And he was a totally different person. I thought if we could just spend time together, he'd remember, but... Hup! (She jumps to another mushroom.) ...it didn't work. How can people change like that? H'yah! (She jumps to another mushroom, which begins to destabilize...) Whoa! (...then jumps to another.)

Coconut copy: Oh, people change, sister. I had someone close, a BFF. We did everything together (Nya jumps from the mushroom to one of the cave walls and then to another mushroom.), studied, traveled, sang in the same acappella group. (Nya jumps to another mushroom.) I was the beatboxer! ♪ Boots, cats, cats and boots ♪ ♪ Boots, cats, cats and boots ♪ But... over time, our priorities changed. I was still focused on the magic, the people, friendships, (Nya jumps to another mushroom.) but all he cared about was more and more wealth.

Nya: I imagine he was another magic-wielder like you, right? Hup! (She jumps from the mushroom to one of the cave walls and then to another mushroom.) Someone you could share everything with? (She jumps to another mushroom.)

Coconut copy: Yeah... something like that.

(The mushroom Nya lands on begins to destabilize and fall, so she grabs the mycelium of another and catches the coconut copy, preventing her from falling.)

Coconut copy: I don't see how we're both making it out of this. Let me go! I'm just a coconut copy.

Nya: No way! Ninja have this whole thing about not quitting. (She puts the coconut copy on her head and uses the mushroom mycelia as vines, escaping the cave.) Let's hope there are no more surprises like that.

Coconut copy: This is Mysterium. Nothing but surprises.

(They continue moving forward and a figure appears on the other side of the cave.)

(In the skies, Zanth and Riyu are flying, with the former carrying Wyldfyre, Sora, and Frak on her back.)

Wyldfyre: Whee!

Frak: Are we there yet? How far away is this place?

Wyldfyre: Frak, Zanth is doing her best. We'll get to Djinjago when we get to Djinjago!

Frak: But time is, like, of the essence.

Wyldfyre: Then let's sing to pass the time! ♪ Oh... what do you do With a sleepy Ninja? ♪ ♪ What do you do with A sleepy Ninja? ♪ ♪ What do you-- ♪

Sora: Why don't we practice the melody Bleckt taught us? We're gonna need to play it on the bells to awaken our half of the Arc Dragon.(She sings the melody and Frak harmonizes, much to her annoyance.) Please don't harmonize. We've got to remember the primary notes, and that just makes it harder.

Frak: Sorry, I just habitually harmonize in thirds. Years of vocal training. (Rolling lips.) Brrr... ah! Okay, I'm warm. From the one, please?

(They sing the melody in unison and Wyldfyre joins in, singing loudly and out of tune, much to the annoyance of Riyu, who grumbles in protest and covers his ears.)

Wyldfyre: Let's add words for funzies! I've been super into sea shanties lately. ♪ Yo-ho, the ocean calls! ♪ ♪ The Ninja kicks and the bad guys fall! ♪

Frak: (He speaks quietly to Sora.) We cannot let her ring the bells.

Wyldfyre: I heard that! Not my fault if you can't appreciate a good shanty.

Sora: Let's play the Quiet Game. One, two, three, go!

(They are silent for a few seconds.)

Wyldfyre: (She groans.) Who wants snacks? I mean, I didn't bring any snacks. Why would I say that? (She goes to the back of Zanth and throws a bunch of stuff away until she gets a box of Zip Flies and eats one.) Okay, but seriously, are we there yet? Time is of the essence.

Sora: Zanth, my friend, can you sense Djinjago? Are we getting closer?

(Zanth growls.)

Frak: Was that a yes?

(Zanth starts flying extremely fast.)

All: Whoa!

(Zanth begins to glow as she accelerates.)

Sora: (She gasps.) Are you okay, girl?

Wyldfyre: Wha-hoo! I don't even know what's happening, but this is awesome!

(Zanth spins around and makes everyone on top of her yelp.)

Sora: Zanth, what is going on here?

(Zanth stops as she transforms into a new form, with larger horns, thicker wings, and pink details.)

Frak: Whoa! Does she normally do stuff like this?

Sora: She got really big once before, but it wasn't like this. Zanth, quite a glow-up, but what brought this on right-- aah!

(Zanth speeds up once more, but soon stops, followed by an exhausted Riyu.)

Sora: Djinjago! We made it

(They fly over to Djinjago, which is much bigger than the last time it appeared.)

(As Thunderfang engaged in the ritual of his evolution, his body gradually transformed, with spikes and a huge claw emerging from his left arm, then he sensed something.)

Thunderfang: (To the Forbidden FIve.) Someone is nearing the Arc Dragon of Focus. The Ninja... They dare attempt to awaken my eternal enemy?

(Zanth and Riyu land on Djinjago, with the latter so exhausted that he lays on his back on the ground.)

Sora: Whoa... (Frak falls off Zanth's back.) I had no idea Djinjago would've grown this much so fast, and it looks like Arrakore moved it over the water.

(Frak grunts, kisses the ground, and stands up. Sora climbs off Zanth's back.)

Sora: Looks at all this! New towers, and fountains?

Frak: (He looks around and notices something.) Is that a Monastery portal gate?

(The Monastery of Djinjago appears inside a mountain.)

Sora: So much has changed, and it's all so majestic.

(Arrakore appears out of nowhere, consequently scaring Wyldfyre.)

Arrakore: Glad you like it! (He looks to the side and sees Wyldfyre lying on the ground.)

Sora: Arrakore! (She jumps on Arrakore and hugs him.)

Arrakore: Sora! (He hugs her back.) Riyu! (Riyu runs towards him.) Oh, I do love when you visit! (He caresses Riyu.) So good to see you again!

(Riyu grumbles happily and Arrakore notices Wyldfyre and Frak.)

Arrakore: And you brought friends! (Frak waves at him.) Zanth, is that you? (Zanth growls.) Look at you! I knew my work expanding Djinjago would have amazing results, but I never expected it would infuse a Djinjagan Dragon with such energy! (He and Zanth hug.)

(Rapton and the Freebooters fly through the sky in their hover chariots.)

Rapton: There it is, Freebooters. Djinjago. Rapton never doesn't find what he wants.

Freebooter: Shouldn't it be, "Rapton always finds what he wants"?

Rapton: What's that?! Is someone question-alating Rapton's grammaticalism masteritude?

Freebooter: No, of course not.

(They advance and descend to Djinjago.)

(Nya and the coconut copy continue moving forward and the first one stops to rest.)

Coconut copy: We're here!

(It is shown that they arrived in a type of reverse waterfall.)

Nya: Wow... but we need a Cave of Tones.

Coconut copy: It's behind the water! Let's go! (Nya walks again.) We just need to play the tones in the order this Blecky--

Nya: Bleckt.

Coconut copy: Bleckt? Really? Sounds like someone barfing. His parents didn't do him any favors with that name. Anyhoosy, we do what he told you, then badda-bing, we awaken the half Arc Dragon of Focus and save the world! Na-na!

Nya: Only if the other Ninja pull off their parts too.

Coconut copy: Oh, you don't think they will?

Nya: They've never let me down before.

Coconut copy: Ah, sounds like you've got a pretty good group of friends behind you. Now, come on, let's do this! (As they approach the waterfall, the silhouette of a cyclops appears on the other side.) Oh, no...

(The silhouette of the cyclops gets closer and closer.)

Nya: Great. More Mysterium surprises?

(Cyclops reveals himself as Powerlid carrying a jewelry box.)

Powerlid: Who disturbs Powerlid, the ultimate cyclops, also know as the Ultimate Object of Admiration?

(Nya starts to back away.)

Coconut copy: Ugh... pretend I'm not here. (She rolls onto Nya's shoulder.) Hide me in a tree, I'm just your regular run-of-the-mill coconut.

Nya: Do you know that cyclops?

Coconut copy: No! Yes! I do. I did. Yeah, but I never thought I'd run into him again.

(Powerlid picks up the jewelry from the floor and puts it in the box.)

Nya: Wait, is this...?

Coconut copy: The former BFF I was talking about? Yes! And it looks like becoming a greedy guts didn't amount to much if he lives in a cave. Serves him right.

Powerlid: (He notices Nya.) Ugh, another one of you Ninjerks here to steal Powerlid's stuff! Time to grind your bones into powder like Powerlid does best! (He and Nya stare at each other.)

(While tending to the hollow Finders and Cole with Geo, Mr. Frohicky offers them a bowl of broth.)

Mr. Frohicky: Here you go. Some aged spore-rot broth. Auntie Croakwart's old recipe, yum, yum, yum! (He tries to give the broth to Spitz, who cannot drink it.) I'll be honest, the flavor's, uh, a little bit on the sharp side.

Geo: (He smells the broth and coughs.) Are you sure that's even safe to eat? It smells rank... and I live in a wasteland of junk.

Mr. Frohicky: It's very nourishing and healing... if you can keep it down.

Geo: Well, if nothing else, maybe the smell will revive them.

Mr. Frohicky: Don't worry, I've got plenty left. (He tries to give the broth to Bonzle, but it ends up going through her, due to her being a skeleton.) Oh. Does she not eat?

Geo: No, she eats all the time.

Mr. Frohicky: Well, how does...? Um, I mean, where does the food...? (He stammers.)

Geo: Of all the questions I need answers to right now, that's very far down the list. Cole? Can you hear me? It's Geo. (Cole moans.) What can I do? (He puts his hands on Cole's shoulders.) Can you... blink twice if you can understand me?

(Cole moans again and the portal gate suddenly activates.)

Mr. Frohicky: Who's coming now?

Geo: If the Ninja are back this fast, it probably means something went wrong.

(The portal gate opens and reveals Ras.)

Mr. Frohicky: No, no! Well, he can't be here! How can he be here?

(Ras finishes exiting the portal gate, goes down the stairs and approaches Geo and Mr. Frohicky.)

Geo: Don't get any closer!

Ras: The Ninja are scattered across the Merged Lands. Save yourself the pain and suffering. Submit.

Geo: You can't intimidate us. Ninja never quit.

Ras: You are not Ninja.

Mr. Frohicky: We're... Ninja-adjacent, and Ninja-adjacent never quit, either.

Ras: So be it. (He walks towards them.)

Mr. Frohicky: Eat this! (He throws the bowl at Ras.)

(The bowl of broth hits Ras in the face, enraging him. Geo and Mr. Frohicky flee before Ras wields his Source Dragon Icons from behind his back. Geo and Mr. Frohicky lead Cole and the Finders to the elevator and close the door just before Ras runs toward them.)

(End of episode. For more information, click here.)
