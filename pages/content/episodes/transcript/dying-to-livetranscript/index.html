Beatrix: (narrating) In my moment of triumph, all was taken away from me. (Flashbacks of the Liberation of Imperium are shown.) Though I was stronger ever than before, I was betrayed by those who should have worshipped me, my own people, the citizens of Imperium, their minds twisted by the lies of the ninja. (Beatrix gets knocked into a MergeQuake by Arin and screams. Her left eye turns mechanical and she falls down to a barren land.) I thought I was dead. I'm sure you all thought the same. But I survived, I found myself alone in a forsaken place, the Land of Monsters. (Beatrix fights against a few monsters.) For months, I battled the hideous creatures that roam that savage land, with one goal fueling me... (Part of her mech is destroyed by a tentacled beast, and a large tentacle arm grows to replace it.) revenge. The citizens of Imperium, the ninja, they all had to pay for taking what was rightfully mine! I escaped from that wretched land, but I was gravely injured. The betrayal of my people and the ninja nearly led to my destruction, yet my fury would not die, and fate had more in store for me. (Beatrix wakes up to see Dr. LaRow next to her.)

Past Dr. LaRow: You're awake. My experimental medical treatments must be working. (takes off her hood)

Past Beatrix: LaRow?

Past Dr. LaRow: All hail the Good Empress.

Beatrix: (stands up) I have been to the abyss and back, and have the scars to prove it, (The scene cuts back to the Loyalist hideout) but you, and only you, my Loyalists, never wavered in your faith in my almighty power. You will stand by my side when I take the kingdom that is rightfully mine!

Dr. LaRow: As most of you know, this is the fabled Weapon of Desolation, (shows the hologram of the Weapon) the item responsible for the deluge of out-of-control Elemental Monsters plaguing the Merged Lands. Control of this weapon means control of these deadly beings. It gives one the ability to create and lead an army of untold power. Our hidden Loyalists have obtained both halves of the armament and (shows a hologram of the two fragments combining) will be delivering them to us today. Then we will finally fully assemble the Weapon of Desolation.

Beatrix: With my army of Elemental Monsters, all who betrayed me, all who questioned my divine right will fall before me, and I will show them no mercy!

Loyalists: All hail the Good Empress!

Cole: (wakes up and gasps) What the...? Ah! Ow. (winces) Oh, right. Dr. LaRow blasted me. (sighs) That was an annoying voice I never wanted to hear again. (Cole stands and sees a box of sushi nearby. Cole opens the box and finds out the sushi inside is in poor quality.) Blech. Did they find the (closes the box) oldest prison in Ninjago to put me in? (sees a smiling face on one of the walls) Okay, whoever was here last must've gotten bored and made a friend. Trust me Mr. Smiley, I've been in worse situations. I should be out of here in no time. (Cole notices a door and tries to kick it.) H'yah!

(A forcefield around the door stops Cole.)

Cole: (groans) Ow! At least no one saw that. Except for you. Please don't tell anyone.

(Cole overhears the conversation between Beatrix and Dr. LaRow.)

Beatrix: ...worn out relic.

(Cole walks nearer to hear more and steps on a wooden stool.)

Dr. LaRow: While I don't believe it's "supernatural", the proof of the Weapon's power has been made obvious all over the Merged Lands, even if I haven't uncovered its scientific foundation, and I determined these are the "incantations", as they say, that separated the weapon into two pieces, apparently written by the First Spinjitzu Master himself.

Beatrix: I don't care who wrote them! Will they work?

Dr. LaRow: Based on my research, if they are reversed, like reversing a calculation, the result should combine the halves, empowering the Weapon and its wielder beyond all comprehension.

Beatrix: And you're certain this tablet is not some kind of trick? An outlander fraud?

Dr. LaRow: I have traveled to a previously unknown realm for this. It's likely I'm the only human who's been there since the Weapon of Desolation- was disposed of millennia ago.

(The Lead Loyalist approaches them, followed by her team.)

Lead Loyalist: Dr. LaRow, we extracted this from the Tree of Madness, as expected.

Dr. LaRow: You were able to retrieve this without being detected?

Lead Loyalist: We had a run-in with some Ninja, but we trapped them in a cave. Won't be seeing them for a while, if ever again.

Dr. LaRow: You had better hope that's true. (She grabs the fragment.) You're dismissed. (The other Loyalists leave.) As promised, one half of the Weapon. (She gives the fragment to Beatrix). I am told that the second sliver is on its way from Umbrasilvis as we speak.

(Beatrix runs one of the tentacles across the fragment.)

Dr. LaRow: I will prove you have no reason to doubt me. All Hail the Good Empress.

(The stool Cole is on starts wobbling.)

Cole: Whoa! (He falls to the ground.) "Weapon of Desolation"? That can't be good.

(At The Tree house hideout, The Whispering Wood is dying, and the Arc Dragon of Life exhales weakly.)

Zane: How long does a typical Trial of the Green Ninja take?

Arc Dragon of Life: No two trials are the same, and I would not hold out such hope for your friend, Lloyd.

Arin: I'm never going to lose hope. Lloyd faces danger all the time.

Arc Dragon of Life: That is the problem. It's nearly impossible to stay idealistic when you know so much about this world.

(An Earth Monster and a Mutation Monster appear behind them, roaring. Arin and Zane put on their masks as they and Euphrasia prepare for battle,)

(Lloyd is still meditating next to the archway.)

Lloyd: I'm here to face my greatest enemy... Who is that? Ras? Garmadon? The Overlord? Maybe an angry Source Dragon, or someone else that secretly hates me, and I don't know? Arin's not still mad at me, is he? (sighs) Worrying won't do me any good. I just need to focus. This is the only way I can achieve my True Potential as the Green Ninja. (He stands up and walks through the archway, entering what looks like the Monastery of Spinjitzu.) Home? How? This can't be real. Just a vision. But... why? (Someone appears behind him and he turns around to see another version of himself.)

Enemy Lloyd: Hello, Lloyd.

Lloyd: My greatest enemy is... me?

(Cole tries a find way out of the prison cell.)

Cole: (punches the wall) H'ah! H'yah!

(The forcefield around the wall stops him.)

Cole: Okay, Plan C... I got nothing. Don't judge me, I don't see you coming up with any ideas. If that Empress Beatrix and Dr. LaRow have some powerful weapon, it's up to me to stop them, but I'm stuck in this dusty old jail. Ah! Seriously? It's just dirt? (laughs) Dr. LaRow really should've sprung for the full renovation. In case you missed it, Mr. Smiley, I am an Elemental Master of Earth. You know, like this entire floor is made out of. I know my powers has been wonky, you don't have to give me that look. Stand back. Huah!

(He tries using his powers, but nothing happens.)

Cole: Yeah, yeah keep laughing. (He tries again, and suddenly the ground breaks from under him.) Whoa!

(The hideout's alarm system turns on, and two Loyalists start rushing to the prison cells.)

Lloyd: You, uh... I-I mean me... I'm not my own worst enemy.

Enemy Lloyd: You would want to take the easy way out. Why don't you blame someone else for your problems. like you usually do? Your dad. Ras. The Merge. Never realizing the real problem all along.

Lloyd: Me? I'm the real problem?

Enemy Lloyd: You're not a leader, that's for sure.

Lloyd: What?

Enemy Lloyd: How many times have you let your team down? You've nearly gotten them all killed. It's a wonder they haven't abandoned you. (punches Lloyd.)

Lloyd: No. We always have each other's backs. The ninja are a team.

Enemy Lloyd: A team you'll never be able to lead. Not like Master Wu did. Not even like Cole did. (kicks Lloyd down)

Lloyd: Ugh!

Enemy Lloyd: Where do I even begin with how many mistakes you've made in your life, Lloyd? Let's start with recently. Arin went with Ras because you were such a bad mentor. (He pins Lloyd to the ground.) You lost Jay for so long, his goodness was shattered. You ever wonder why Master Wu-- who's still alive-- hasn't even tried to come back? He's too ashamed of what a failure you've become.

Lloyd: (pushes Enemy Lloyd off of him and gets up.) Ah! (tries to attack Enemy Lloyd) H'ah! H'yah!

(Enemy Lloyd dodges the attacks using the Art of the Silent Fist. Lloyd uses Spinjitzu, but Enemy Lloyd restrains him to prevent the Spinjitzu attack.)

Lloyd: You're just trying to manipulate me. Get under my skin.

Enemy Lloyd: With the truth? You know it's all true, Lloyd, because I know it's true.

(Enemy Lloyd punches Lloyd, but Lloyd blocks it. Lloyd performs Rising Dragon technique, and Enemy Lloyd counters this with his own Rising Dragon technique.)

Enemy Lloyd: "Ninja never quit"? You ever think maybe you should quit? That you have no idea how to lead the ninja, and you only bring them harm? They'd be better off without you.

Lloyd: That... That's not true! H'yah!

(Lloyd punches Enemy Lloyd. Enemy Lloyd shoots out energy aimed at Lloyd, and Lloyd dodges the attack. It hits a pillar, leaving it cracked like a mirror and reflecting Enemy Lloyd behind him. Lloyd turns, but nothing is there—only another cracked, mirror-like surface. Lloyd slowly backs up and softly bumps into the mirror.)

Enemy Lloyd: Your failures know no limits. You even managed to anger the Source Dragons.

(Lloyd looks back and the mirror disappears from behind him.)

Enemy Lloyd: The most powerful beings in the universe are furious with you.

Lloyd: I know.

(Enemy Lloyd tries to tackle Lloyd with a sword. Lloyd looks back and blocks the attack with his sword.)

Enemy Lloyd: You caused them to destabilize the entire Merged Lands! Everything could end and it would all be your fault!

Lloyd: I know. Whoa!

Enemy Lloyd: You've done more damage to the Merged Lands than Ras. More than any villain has. You could not have brought more destruction if you tried!

Lloyd: I know! (grunts from the impact)

(Enemy Lloyd attacks Lloyd with his sword. Lloyd blocks the attacks. Enemy Lloyd’s golden sword knocks Lloyd’s golden sword away, and it lands on the monastery floor.)

Enemy Lloyd: H'yah!

(Enemy Lloyd tries to punch Lloyd using Life. Lloyd dodges but gets kicked by Enemy Lloyd.)

Lloyd: But I had to anger the Source Dragons to save the world!

Enemy Lloyd: Huh, Never expected "The Chosen One" to have so many excuses. You'll never reach your True Potential as the Green Ninja, because your only potential is failure. For yourself, and for those you love.

Lloyd: (gasps) It's not too late. It's never too late. I can still prove I'm the Chosen One!

(Lloyd uppercuts Enemy Lloyd using Life.)

Lloyd: H'yah!

(Lloyd uses Spinjitzu to grab his sword from the monastery floor.)

Lloyd: H'ah!

(The Loyalist hideout's alarm blares. Loyalists turn off the alarm and open the door to Cole's cell, seeing the hole that he made, and check for places he could be hiding in.)

Loyalist #1: This wasn't like this when we put him in here, right? There's no way he's still alive.

Cole: I'd say that's incorrect. (leaves and closes the cell door.)

Loyalist #1: Get back here! (They pound on the cell door.)

(Cole approaches the weapon fragment.)

Cole: Is this wooden corkscrew as powerful as they think?

(Cole accidentally steps on a laser, triggering another alarm.)

Cole: Seriously? Come on!

(Loyalists enter the room and begin attacking Cole.)

Cole: Hey there! Nice masks. This probably looks bad, right? Well... (Cole performs Spinjitzu to attack the loyalists.) ...its gonna feel even worse!

(Cole attacks the Loyalists.)

Cole: H'yah! H'yah!

(Cole is throws a Loyalist at the forcefield chamber containing the fragment, causing it to drop to the ground. He grabs onto the fragment.)

Cole: Gotcha!

(Cole’s powers begin to go out of control as he holds the fragment. He starts to float as rocks orbit around him and transforms into an enhanced version of his golem form.)

Cole: What the...? What? Wow! Unexpected bonus. (roars)

Lloyd: You can't beat me because there's nothing you can say that I haven't already said to myself.

Enemy Lloyd: Remember where Finn Tully pantsed you in gym class?

(Lloyd gasps and groans.)

(The mirror that had been cracked reappears, and Enemy Lloyd laughs maniacally.)

Lloyd: I... actually forgot about that.

(The mirror suddenly breaks into pieces and floats around Lloyd. Lloyd sees Enemy Lloyd and tries to attack him with Spinjitzu, but he disappears, appearing instead on the mirror.)

Enemy Lloyd: Look at you. A boy pretending to be a man. You never grew up and that's why you're a mere shadow of what Master Wu was!

(The mirror pieces disappear.)

Lloyd: That may be true, but I--

(Enemy Lloyd tackles Lloyd.)

Enemy Lloyd: You still don't know what being the Green Ninja really means. How deluded and full of yourself do you have to be to think you could master a power you can't even understand?

Lloyd: I still--

Enemy Lloyd: It's only a matter of time before the entire world falls because of your failures as a leader and the worst thing? You're too clouded by your own expectations to realize you can't handle it. That's what will destroy your friends.

(Enemy Lloyd drags Lloyd into a cliff of the mountain. Lloyd grunts and gasps.)

Enemy Lloyd: This is the limit of your potential.

(Meanwhile, in the Whispering Wood, the ninja continue fighting the Elemental Monsters. An Earth Monster fires a blast of tar, which Arin dodges. He then kicks the Earth Monster away. Zane uses Spinjitzu to fight a Mutation Monster.)

Arc Dragon of Life: (It senses Lloyd's trial isn't going well.) It's as I foresaw...

Euphrasia: What is it?

Arc Dragon of Life: Lloyd... is failing.

Arin: (pants) It can't be true. Lloyd can't be failing.

Euphrasia: I don't wanna lose hope either, but we have to be real about this, or we're gonna fail next! Ah! What's that thing you always tell me?

Arin: Ninja never qui-- Ah! (He gets hit from behind by an Ice Monster.)

(Zane grunts and kicks a Mutation Monster. Suddenly an Acid Monster appears behind him, and he gasps. Euphrasia lifts him with a gust of wind, and Zane yelps as Euphrasia tries her best to control her power.)

Zane: H'yah (He lands safely.)

(The monsters hiss and roar.)

(Arin, Euphrasia, and Zane run as the Elemental Monster pursues them, approaching the Arc Dragon of Life.)

Zane: I feel there is no hope.

(The monsters roar.)

(Meanwhile, Cole is still fighting against the Loyalists, and the Loyalists are starting to escape.)

Cole: (grabs one of the Loyalists) Not so brave anymore? (He throws the Loyalist at the other Loyalists, knocking them out of the storage room.)

(Beatrix enters and sees Cole.)

Beatrix: You dare, ninja?

Cole: Empress Beatrix? Learnt a lot about you, don't believe we've met. Cole. Now let me guess: you got some evil plans. Well, I'm here to crush them. (Beatrix starts to approach him, and he steps back.) Ugh. You look different than my friends described. New haircut?

(Beatrix strikes Cole and to knocks him down. Cole tries to strike back, but Beatrix dodges and strikes him again.)

Beatrix: I've been dreaming of this day, of crushing all of the ninja!

(Beatrix keeps striking Cole, but he dodges. Cole sees a turret on one of the Hover chariots in the room.)

Cole: (pulls off the turret) Was this part of your dream? (He shoots at Beatrix, but Beatrix throws boxes and barrels at him to block his shots until he runs out of ammo and strikes her down.)

(Cole tries to knock Beatrix out, but she grabs him by the leg, knocking him down. She then punches him in the head, knocking him out. Beatrix takes the fragment back, and Cole's golem form breaks apart.)

Beatrix: This is mine! And soon all of Imperium will be mine again!

(Loyalists come into the room, Cole opens his eyes, only to get knocked out by Beatrix. He wakes up in the Loyalists' captivity.)

Cole: (gasps) This is getting to be a habit. (He hopeless trying to break his shackles. The Grand Inquisitor enters the room with the other fragment in hand, and Cole silently laughs.) He wears that hat for real? (laughs before seeing the fragment.) Oh, crud.

Grand Inquisitor: Behold, I have acquired the mighty...

Dr. LaRow: (She takes the fragment from the Grand Inquisitor's hands and interrupts him.) Dispense with the formalities, please. (turns to Beatrix) I told you there was no reason to doubt me.

Beatrix: We shall see. Bring the other sliver!

(A Loyalist enters holding the second fragment. Dr. LaRow hands the fragment she was holding to another Loyalist and grabs the Tablet of Desolation from a third Loyalist.)

Dr. LaRow: Gebalow, Smegalow, Carniff Fie... Helagove, Tetrabo, Lesto Sie... (The two fragments float up and combine, and Beatrix grabs the reformed Weapon of Desolation.) With one final word, control will be yours, Empress. Bah-vaugnat.

Beatrix: Bah-vaugnat! (She holds up the Weapon, and it sparks with energy.)

Cole: No, no, no, no, no...

Beatrix: With this weapon, a weapon only one as almighty as I can wield, I will lead us to rightful glory, and eliminate all who dare defy me!

(Meanwhile, in Lloyd's illusion, Enemy Lloyd is about to strike him.)

Enemy Lloyd: (chuckles) I knew you weren't strong enough to face your own fears. I never had a doubt about that. Had you come when you were younger, you might've stood a chance, but you accrued a lifetime of failures to use against you. You feel the weight of them with every blow I strike, and believe me, I know how heavy your weakness weighs on your soul, so consider this a gift, an escape from all the mistakes you've made. (Enemy Lloyd attempts to strike Lloyd, but Lloyd blocks the attack.) It's too late, Lloyd!

Lloyd: I can feel how much hate is in your heart.

Enemy Lloyd: Yes! For you!

Lloyd: For us. That rage, it's hurt, it's shame, it's fear with nowhere to go. (gets back up) I've made a lot of mistakes. But without them, I'd never have learned. Mistakes are how we grow. You are just my weaknesses come to life. And I won't give you any more power.

Enemy Lloyd: You'll never be able to shake your own fears and failures! (kicks Lloyd down)

Lloyd: Ah! (He falls away from the illusion, and into the Source Dragons' void.)

Flow Symbol: We should have his fate coming.

Motion Symbol: It is a great loss among mortals.

Energy Symbol: This cannot be his end. The Legendary Green Ninja's power is to enhance, to connect. He may have failed the traditional trials, but his true gift, his true potential, is anything but traditional.

(Lloyd sinks into the ground, and wakes up in the Whispering Wood. He uses his power to feel the ground.)

Lloyd: Each tree is connected. Trees are life. I am the Master of Life.

(Cole is still held captive at the Loyalist hideout. His eyes turn green for a moment.)

Cole: Lloyd...

(Sora, Wyldfyre and Frak are flying through the sky on Riyu's back. Their eyes briefly flash green as well.)

(Jay is driving the Transforming Car through the Land of Madness, with Nya and Kai as passengers. All of their eyes momentarily turn green, And Jay stops the car.)

Nya: Did you just feel that?

(Arin, Zane and Euphrasia are fighting the Elemental Monster in the Whispering Wood. Their eyes flash green for a moment.)

Euphrasia: What just happened?

Zane: I experienced it, too!

Arin: (punches an earth monster) It was Lloyd. It was like I could feel his presence.

Euphrasia: But why? Did he... not survive?

Arin: If he didn't, we're doomed.

(The Earth Monster gets back up and shoots tar. Arin dodges, and uses Spinjitzu to get back in the fight. Zane and Euphrasia continue fighting as well.)

(Meanwhile, Lloyd lays on the ground. He opens his eyes, and they begin to glow brightly.)

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
