(More and more of the Whispering Wood decays as Zane, Arin, and Euphrasia defend the Arc Dragon of Life from the Elemental Monsters.)

Arin: Sorry hanging out with us got you into this.

Euphrasia: I'm ready to write this destiny with you, however it turns out.

(Zane defends the Arc Dragon of life from a blast from an Ice Monster. An Earth Monster attacks the dragon. Zane attempts to use his power, to no avail, before Arin kicks the Earth Monster to the side.)

Arc Dragon of Life: The time... has come. Thank you, Titanium Ninja. May our souls meet in the next cycle. (She falls to the ground.)

Zane: No, not yet! Trust in Lloyd. (A Mutation Monster attacks.) H'yah! There's fight left in us! Ah!

(The trio is repeatedly blasted by the Elemental Monsters. All of the sudden, whispering is heard all around them, and a green wave of power passes through them.)

Euphrasia: What was that?

(The whispers grow louder. There's a green blast from high in the air, revealing Lloyd as he flies and lands on the ground in his Draconus Form, destroying several of the monsters.)

Arin, Euphrasia, and Zane: Lloyd?

(Lloyd roars.)

(In Imperium, streetlights flicker as Percival walks with the lab assistants.)

Imperium Assistant 1: Several grid problems in sectors 7, 9 and 14-Alpha.

(A heavy blast sound is heart. Birds fly overhead.)

Percival: Another monster? We still haven't finished the repairs from the last attack.

(Percival, the lab assistants, and a crowd of Imperians exit the front gate of the city, looking out at the vast desert from their platform.)

Imperium Assistant 1: It's... not a monster.

Percival: Wrong. It's the worst monster Imperium has ever known.

(Some yards away, Beatrix stands on a hovering platform with the Weapon of Desolation and Dr. LaRow by her side. On the ground is a small army of Loyalists. Cole is handcuffed on his own small platform. Beatrix uses a device to project her image close to the city.)

Beatrix: People of Imperium! My people! Who represents you in my absence?

Percival: I am a duly elected official.

Beatrix: Aren't you that boy who once brought me a baby dragon? You dreamt of nothing more than serving me as one of my Claws!

Percival: That was a long time ago, when I was brainwashed by your propaganda, but I woke up. All of the people of Imperium woke up. We saw who you really are-- a tyrant who lied about safe energy while running secret dragon-draining chambers!

Beatrix: I drained those disgusting dragons to keep my kingdom prosperous. I hear your "free society" can barely keep the lights on. The lives of Imperium Citizens were perfect when the rightful ruler was on her throne!

Percival: You lied, and hurt, and oppressed the people. Our new Imperium has struggles, but we work hard to make our city function independently, without harming other beings!

Beatrix: (sighs) My subjects! You all once hailed me as your Good Empress. Despite your betrayal, I will give you one chance to surrender and receive mercy, or else I will take Imperium by force, and there will be no mercy!

Percival: I know where I stand. Being ruled by tyrants was the old way, but it's not about what I want. We decide by the voice of the people. All who wish to open the doors to Beatrix, speak! (There is silence from the crowd.) All who wish to stand firm for the freedom of Imperium and refuse her, no matter what she may throw at us? (The crowd cheers.) Former Empress, the people have spoken. Imperium rejects you. Again.

Beatrix: Then you choose "no mercy"? Good. That's a much more satisfying option... for me.

Percival: If you step one foot within these walls, you will be arrested and prosecuted in a fair trial for your crimes, including the death of your father, Emperor Levo Vespasian-Orus!

Beatrix: (laughs) Remember, despite my better judgement, I gave you a chance for peace. What happens next is on your hands! (The projection ends.) Time to rise and shine!

(Beatrix used the Weapon of Desolation, creating an army of Elemental Monsters. The Imperians react with fear.)

Imperium Assistant 1: Our walls can't hold against all that. Have we made a mistake?

Beatrix: (to Cole) Ninja! This is why I kept you alive in such a humiliating state. So you can helplessly witness by triumph. (Cole groans and struggles against his handcuffs, Beatrix turns away from him.) Now to take back what's mine. March on Imperium!

(The monsters charge, followed closely by Beatrix and her Loyalists.)

(Lloyd begins to fight the Elemental Monsters by himself, using his new Draconus form to attack. He begins to obliterate the monsters.)

Euphrasia: I didn't know he could get all dragony like that.

Arin: He can't, or he... couldn't. (Wind intensifies, and Euphrasia's hands glow as her power returns.) Look!

Euphrasia: Huh? Looks like the end of our destiny hasn't been written after all! Ninja... go!

(Euphrasia and Zane blast monsters with their restored powers, and Arin joins the fight with his Spinjitzu. Lloyd blasts straight through several of the monsters, causing the others to disappear into energy.)

Euphrasia: Huh?

(With the fight over, the others come up to Lloyd.)

Zane: How did you stabilize our powers--

Arin: You're a dragon now? How? Why--

Euphrasia: Nice tail! I've always wanted one.

Lloyd: Slow down. One at a time. I already hear so much, feel so much. The lean of a tree, the growth of the grass.

Arc Dragon of Life: It is overwhelming at first. Be patient. Be present.

(Lloyd sighs, and exits his Draconus form.)

Arc Dragon of Life: Your friend's hope was not false. The Legend of the Green Ninja continues in you, yet the form you've taken... I've never seen the likes of it before in a Green Ninja.

Lloyd: Why would I be the first to take a new form?

Arc Dragon of Life: Your life experiences, your connections... I feared they would bring you down, but perhaps they led you to unlock this part of your being.

Lloyd: Ah, my head... these feelings... Can I turn them down?

Arc Dragon of Life: You are connected to life itself now. It cannot be turned down, but you can rise to meet it.

Lloyd: It's... so strange.

Arc Dragon of Life: Life is strange. Life is also ordinary. Life is so many things. I will miss it.

(The Acona groan and wither.)

Euphrasia: Why aren't they better? I thought the ritual would fix everything.

Lloyd: It... it should have, but I sense... something out there manipulating unbalanced Source Energy, draining the Wood, even though it's distant.

Arin: What is it? A Source Dragon? Ras?

Lloyd: No. An artifact, maybe, or a weapon? It's more than just a weapon. It's evil. To save the Whispering Wood, I have to stop it.

Arin: No. We have to stop it.

Euphrasia: Sign me up, but how can we find this thing?

Zane: Lloyd, can you lead us there?

Lloyd: I just... don't know.

(Cole attempts to use his malfunctioning power, grunting and panting as he concentrates enough to bring up a rock directly underneath him, breaking the platform and freeing himself. Two Loyalists power up their blasters.)

Cole: Sorry, had to. Wrists were getting a little sore.

(Cole charges through them with Spinjitzu, catching up to Beatrix and landing on her platform.)

Beatrix: How?

(LaRow attempts to charge him, but Cole sends her flying off the platform, landing with a thud. Cole eyes the Weapon of Desolation.)

Beatrix: That's not for you, whelp!

Cole: (He dodges a blow from Beatrix.) H'yah! I just need to touch it once...

(Cole jumps and grabs ahold of the Weapon, immediately transforming into his Earth Golem. Beatrix gasps as Cole smashes the platform. She falls back as they crash on the ground, the Weapon flying out of her hand. The Elemental Monsters stop their ascent, becoming wilder.)

Cole: You've caused Imperium enough trouble. Let's wrap this up!

Beatrix: I will not be insulted by an outlander! (She picks the Weapon back up and charges it. The monsters regain their focus.) Attack!

(Several of the monsters now charge at Cole. He conjures an Earth sword to fight them off. The Acid and Earth Monsters blast at him.)

Cole: Ugh, this could not be more gross!

Beatrix: A minor distraction. Scale the wall!

(The monsters begin climbing the wall to the entrance of Imperium.)

Percival: We've gotta get the Electro-Barrier back up!

Imperium Assistant 1: You ordered us to shut it down to conserve energy. We re-routed every single power cell. How could we--?

Percival: We find a way! The Elemental Master of Earth is risking his life because he hasn't given up on us, so we can't give up either. Let's go!

(As Cole continues to fend of monsters, a horn sounds. Jay, Nya, and Kai come speeding up a hill in Nya's newly made transforming car.)

Kai: Ha-ha! Knew if we followed these masked jerks, we'd find something fun!

Cole: Jay? Nya? Kai?

Jay: Get off Cole, you freaks! Uh, that is Cole, right? I haven't gotten back any memories of him looking like that.

Nya: I'm like, 95% to 98% sure that's him. (She spots Beatrix.) There she is, and that must be the weapon her goons were talking about. We got here too late.

Kai: Told ya you should've let me drive.

Jay: (scoffs) My driving is great!

(They narrowly avoid colliding with some monsters.)

Kai: Yeah, real great!

Jay: Better than the time you melted the Land Bounty's steering wheel!

Kai: Can we lose this guy's memories again?

Jay: Hold tight!

(A Mutation Monster lands on the hood of the car.)

Nya: Jay, hit the thing! (Jay presses a button, causing the hood of the car to flip over and sending the monster flying.) Now we're talking!

Jay: Whoo-hoo! Racer Mode was my best idea yet!

Nya: It was my idea!

Jay: As the love of your life, I'm sure I inspired it.

Nya: You told me repeatedly not to do it 'cause we'd never use it... (She pauses to hit on Earth Monster off the car.) ...which just made me want to do it more.

Jay: Exactly! I'm a master of reverse inspiration.

(On top of the Imperium Broadcast Tower, Percival and the assistants attempt to reinstate the Electro-Barrier.)

Imperium Assistant 1: Lifting 88A, Side 2, on your mark.

Percival: Mark! (Imperium Assistant 1 flips a switch. Percival turns to another assistant.) Feed it in. (The second assistant hands the first a power cell. She inserts it into the hardware, and the Electro-Barrier begins to power up.) Yes!

(Just as the Elemental Monsters reach the entrance to the city, the barrier shocks them.)

Beatrix: No! They will not keep me out!

(More monsters begin attacking the barrier, and it begins to fade.)

Dr. LaRow: Empress, we have several covert Loyalists still inside. Looks like they sabotaged the barrier.

Beatrix: When we take back the kingdom, remind me to spare them from my wrath.

(Percival attempts to fix the barrier.)

Percival: The barrier is turning off!

(Jay and Nya observe the barrier going down from their vehicle.)

Nya: Their barrier failed!

Jay: They probably could use the help of the greatest engineer and inventor that's basically every existed.

Nya: Are you talking about me?

Jay: Yeah! Was that not charming enough?

Nya: You were a selfish mercenary like three days ago. Sometimes I'm still note sure how to take you.

Jay: That's fair. Now, go!

(Nya uses Spinjitzu to head towards the city, and Kai joins the fight with Cole.)

Cole: Kai! Didn't expect you. Last I heard, you got trapped in a cave.

Kai: No cave can keep me from saving your bacon. (to the Monsters.) What's crackin', fellow fire people! Up top! (The Fire Monsters snarl.) Leave me hanging? Uncool, fire bros!

(He charges them with Spinjitzu.)

(Percival continues searching for the problem with the Electro-Barrier.)

Percival: The fault's not in the parallel circuits.

(Nya arrives in the tower's elevator.)

Nya: Percival! Here to help.

Percival: Nya! Oh, man. We could use it. How did you get inside the walls?

Nya: Waste tunnels. Still gross.

Imperium Assistant 1: Beatrix could send her monsters in that way!

Percival: Doubt it. People like her never even wonder how people like us take out their trash.

Nya: What's wrong with the barrier?

Percival: We don't know. We can't find any faults!

Nya: I'll check the relays.

(Nya takes the elevator down.)

(On the lower floors, Nya comes across two Loyalists removing power cells, causing the problems with the barrier.)

Nya: What? Sabotage!

Loyalist: All hail the Good Empress!

(The Loyalists shoot their blasters at Nya. She dodges the blasts, falling onto her back. The two Loyalists race down the corridor to escape, Nya getting up and following closely behind.)

(The Elemental Monsters reach the front gate to the City of Imperium. Imperians scream and run as the monsters begin to attack. Sora, Frak, and Wyldfyre arrive on Riyu's back, knocking several of the monsters away from the citizens.)

Frak: Thought we'd never find where these weirdos were headed.

Sora: Soon as I heard the name "Beatrix," it was pretty obvious.

(They hop off of Riyu and head to the edge, looking out on the many monsters still climbing the wall.)

Frak: No offense, but so far, I'm not loving your hometown.

Sora: I've never been a big fan either, but to be fair, it had fewer monsters scaling the walls when I was a kid.

Wyldfyre: Eh, sounds like it was boring back then. This rules!

Sora: (She eyes Cole, Jay, and Kai fighting in the desert.) Look!

Frak: (simultaneously) Cole!

Wyldfyre: (simultaneously) Kai! (to Frak) Quit sitting around! (She begins hopping on the heads of the ascending monsters to join Kai and the others.) Monster fight!

Frak: We're gonna have to do that to, aren't we?

Sora: You are. I'm gonna see if I can get the city's defenses back up. (She runs in the other direction.)

Frak: Oh, cool. Good luck. (He gulps, puts one hand over his eye and begins to clumsily hop on monsters to make his way down, eventually landing flat on his stomach.) Maybe nobody saw that.

(Jay pulls up, laughing hysterically.)

Jay: Oh, man, kid! That was hilarious! (He drives off.)

Frak: Ha-ha. Yeah. Meant to do it.

(Beatrix observes the battle.)

Beatrix: The ninja brought a repulsive beast against me! Please tell me my dragon-draining facilities are still intact.

Dr. LaRow: The new senate dismantled them, but I should be able to make them functional again post haste.

(Beatrix smiles.)

(On the streets of Imperium, two Loyalists are running with a large device. Nya chases after them. She tries using her powers, but is unsuccessful. One of the Loyalists shoots at her, but she dodges and takes cover behind a cart. The Loyalist shoots the weapon into the air to disperse the crowd ahead of them. The other Loyalist takes out one of the batteries they stole and places it into the device. The two Loyalists then trow the device onto the wall, and it sticks to it and powers up.)

Nya: What's that supposed to--? (One of the Loyalists shoots at her again, and she gets to cover.)

(The device begins cutting a hole into the wall.)

Nya: No! Beatrix will be able to breach the city walls!

(Nya performs Spinjitzu, knocking down one of the Loyalists. She the does it again to get up to the device and destroy it. Sora notices the device exploding as she enters into the city.)

Sora: That doesn't sound good. (She starts running to the explosion.)

(Nya gets up after destroying the device, and continues pursuing the two Loyalists.)

Nya: Sora? Where'd you come from? (The two continue running together.)

Sora: Long story, but me, Frak, Wyldfyre, and Riyu ended up in a place with unexpected kittens, then we jumped through a fountain to where Frak's dad was, but a man in a big hat made us wear some shame boards.

Nya: Maybe I have a concussion, 'cause that sounded like a bunch of nonsense.

Sora: It was. Why are we running? (One of the Loyalists shoots at them, but they dodge.)

Nya: To stop them!

(Behind the Loyalist shooting at Nya and Sora, two more Loyalists throw another device onto the wall.)

Nya: Are your powers working? 'Cause we could use them right now!

Sora: Wait, are your powers wonky too?

Nya: I think everyone's are!

Sora: Well, worth a shot. Please work, please work... (She tries using her powers. It takes some time, but she is eventually able to manipulate the device.) Yes! I think I-- (The device explodes, and the Loyalists run off.) That's not what I was trying--

Nya: Take the win! Come on! (They run after the Loyalists.)

(Outside the city walls, the fighting continues. Riyu fights the Mutation Monsters attempting to scale the wall. Some of them grab onto him and drag him out of the air. And Ice Monster leaps to attack Kai, but he dodges. The Monster then blasts ice at Kai, and surrounds him with the help of another Ice Monster.)

Wyldfyre: Great news, everybody! Me! Yee-haw! (She arrives, riding a Fire Monster. The monster leaps at Kai, and she dismounts it. Kai dodges the Fire Monster.) I know you missed me.

Kai: Well, on a scale from zero to fire! (They do a fist bump.) Jay and my sister are below zero on the fun scale!

(Cole defends himself from the Elemental Monsters. Three Acid Monsters approach him and prepare to blast acid, but Frak kicks one of them away. The other two attempt to blast Frak, but end up hitting each other.)

Cole: Frak! Hey, man, probably not the best time, but I'm sorry how I acted when those specters--

Frak: Forgiven! There are a lot more kinds of monster here than in Umbrasilvis.

Cole: Yeah, what-- what's an Umbrasilvis?

(Jay drives through the Elemental Monsters and transforms his car to attack them. An Ice Monster freezes the ground below him, causing his tires to slip. An Earth Monster then covers his windshield in tar.)

Jay: Wipers! Where are the wipers? (He starts pressing buttons in panic.)

(Two Fire Monsters attack Jay, leading to his car flying into the air and getting stuck into the ground upon landing. The Elemental Monsters surround him.)

(The two Loyalists throw a third device onto the walls of Imperium, and it start cutting another hole. Nya and Sora arrive to stop it, and the Loyalists shoot at them. Another Loyalist approaches from behind with their sword out, but Sora kicks them down. Nya uses Spinjitzu to knock away one of the two Loyalists near the device, but the other one shoots at her, forcing her to retreat.)

Nya: Can you try to shut it down again?

Sora: Come on, one more time... (She tries using her powers again, but can't concentrate with the Loyalist shooting at her.) No!

(The device completes a rotation, and a hole opens up in the city wall.)

Nya: Oh, no!

(Outside, Riyu is being restrained by Mutation Monsters.)

Beatrix: Forget that beast! You must climb the walls! We cannot be kept out! (She notices a hole opening up in the wall.)

Dr. LaRow: Empress, your Loyalists inside the walls have not disappointed.

Beatrix: They will be thrilled at my return. Advance! (She points the Weapon of Desolation forward, and several Elemental Monsters start moving towards the opening.)

(Cole tries running after the monsters, but a few Mutation Monsters trip him up and restrain him.)

Cole: Head for that wall--

(Kai, Wyldfyre and Frak run after the monsters, but a Fire Monster and an Acid Monster block their path. The Loyalists and Elemental Monsters enter the city.)

Beatrix: Imperium! I have returned!

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
