The Screaming Earth/Transcript

The Screaming Earth
Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon
≫

(At the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Rontu trains Riyu as he tries to pass the training course, but he fails halfway through.)

Rontu: Do not be afraid to fail, Riyu, for every time we fail, we learn.

(Riyu says something to Rontu and Mr. Frohicky comes out of the Monastery.)

Mr. Frohicky: Has anyone seen Cole, Kai, or Frak? When I sent them elsewhere to train, I didn't mean for forever.

(Frak, Cole, and Kai arrive at the Imperium desert where Cole has been using the Ninja Combat Vehicle split into two bikes and a jet, with Frak and Kai on the bikes and Cole on the jet.)

Frak: How is it night already? It felt like we barely had any sun today. (To Cole.) Are we there yet?

Kai: Yeah, I thought Master Wu's ghost or whatever showed you where this thing was.

Cole: I was hearing his call. It's not like the ghost voice drew me a map! The terrain out here seems different. Could the realms still be shifting after the Merge?

Kai: Who knows? Maybe that explains why it's dark out already.

(Cole looks away.)

Cole: There! That's it!

(Cole presses a button on the control panel and reconnects Kai and Frak's motorcycles to the jet, reassembling the Ninja Combat Vehicle and then speeds off. When they arrive, they get out of the vehicle and Kai sees lines on the ground.)

Kai: Looks like there was an epic party here a long time ago, or maybe a battle. One of the two.

(Cole tries to use his Earth power on the ground, but feels something when he touches it.)

Cole: The feeling's so strong here. The Earth itself is crying out.

(Frak places his hands, while using his Quake power, on the ground and hears screams.)

Frak: This has to be where it's coming from.

Cole: Maybe what I couldn't do by myself, we can do together. (He puts his arm on Frak's shoulder, who does the same.)

Kai: Hope this works... unless whatever you're pulling up is evil, in which case, I hope you fail.

Frak: No way Master Wu's voice would try to get Cole to dig up something evil. I mean, right?

Kai: Master Wu apparently caused the Merge for some reason, so who knows? (He takes a bag out of his pocket and eats one of Geo's wafers, much to his disgust.)

Cole: If you hate Geo's wafers so much, why are you still eating them?

Kai: (He sighs.) I keep hoping they'll eventually taste good. (He eats another wafer and chockes.)

(Cole activates his Earth Punch, touches the ground and hears the earth scream.)

Cole: There, Frak, can you feel it? (Frak activates his Quake power, touches the ground and also hears the earth scream.) There's something huge down there.

Frak: (He gasps.) It's hard to sense anything over all the screaming-- Wait, is this...?

Cole: You got it. I feel your Quake.

Frak: What could it be? Oh, it's massive.

Cole: Only one way to find out. Pull!

(Cole raises his arms and his and Frak's powers combined open cracks in the ground and something underneath several rocks begins to rise.)

Frak: I think it's working.

(They look to the side and see the rocks rising.)

Frak: Is it a jet? A-- A building?

Kai: (He sees part of the thing under the rocks.) Whoa... (He eats another wafer and feels disgusted again.)

(As Cole and Frak continue to make the object rise, a dragon skull appears from beneath the rocks.)

(In Mysterium, Arin and Sora are on a rock eating moss-milk ice cream near an ice cream truck.)

Sora: Ah... Moss-milk ice cream never tasted so good. A frozen delight.

Arin: Sora, this stuff's only okay at best.

Sora: Ah, I guess barely escaping with your life from a crazed tiger dude who wants to shut you into a Casket of Bones makes everything taste better. (She finishes eating her ice cream.) Okay, I'm getting another one, but then we gotta find a way to help those poor hollow people in Mysterium. Should we head back to the Monastery and tell the others?

Arin: Yeah... Not sure they're gonna be happy to see me, though.

Sora: Trust me, everyone misses you.

Arin: Listen, there's somewhere I need to go first. Only problem is, we can't get there on bikes.

Sora: Let me see what I can do.

(She uses her Tech Power on her and Arin's bikes and combines them.)

(In the Community of Lee, four Lees march holding swords in their hands while Nya, Jenny, Barry, and Jay hide from them in Jenny and Barry's house. Jay sees the Lees through the gaps in the curtain.)

Jay: I didn't even know this place had weapons. I'm impressed.

Nya: Thanks for giving us a place to hide.

Jenny: Of course. It's an honor. We're from the suburbs of Ninjago City. You've probably saved our lives more times than we can count. Our son, he loved you all so much.

Barry: How did your Ninja survive the obliteration of the Merge?

Nya: There was no obliteration. Lee's been lying to you.

Jenny: Hmm. We'd started to suspect that, to be honest. So the Merge didn't really destroy the outside world?

Nya: No. In fact, it brought all the realms together. Ninjago survived, and so did someone else... your son.

Jenny: (She gasps.) Arin's alive?

(Nya nods and then Jenny and Barry hug and start crying with joy when they discover that Arin is alive.)

(Cole and Frak continue using their powers to make the dragon skeleton rise.)

Kai: There must be a whole skeleton down there!

Frak: You've got to be kidding me. There's more?

Kai: Take a break, you two. You're gonna burn out.

(Cole and Frak pause for a moment and the dragon skeleton begins to sink.)

Cole: No, no! We're losing ground!

(They use their powers again to stop the skeleton from sinking any further.)

Frak: I can't keep this up!

(The weight of the rocks prevents them from finishing removing the skeleton from underground.)

Kai: You've hit hard pack... (Some rocks fall from above the skeleton.) but don't worry, I'm here. (He uses his Fire power to destroy the rocks on and around the skeleton.)

Cole: Maybe we can do this, after all.

(Lloyd and Wyldfyre remain trapped in the cage backstage.)

Lloyd: In my vision, the Source Dragons were talking like Thunderfang was still alive, but Arin released the souls that empowered him. We saw Thunderfang turn to dust.

Wyldfyre: Yeah, yeah, sounds like a big problem. I'm thinking about our small problem. Mr. Ball-of-Handsome there is looking more rested by the second, and then he's gonna start tentacling our brains. How do we get out? (She thinks of an idea.) Oh, wait, (She takes a button remote from her pocket.) I got it! Ah, should've thought of this before!

Lloyd: What is that?

Wyldfyre: Our escape! (She presses the button on the remote.) Wait for it... (She continues pressing the button as the Dream-Guyver begins to wake up and float towards them.) What's taking so long? Just work! (She groans in frustration.) You stupid thing! H'yah! (She throws the button remote, which hits the button on the hanging controller and opens the cage.) Yes! Plan B worked!

Lloyd: "Plan B"? You just hurled that thing in anger.

Wyldfyre: Hurling stuff in anger is always my Plan B, Even when it's also my Plan A. Come on! (She dodges Dream-Guyver's tentacle, leaves the cage with Lloyd, and picks up the button controller from the floor.)

(Jay continues to watch from the window while Nya talks to Barry and Jenny on the couch.)

Nya: Arin's been searching for you ever since the Merge happened. We came here to find you.

Jenny: So Arin's here too?

Nya: He, uh... His investigation took him in another direction.

(Jay scoffs and Barry gets up from the couch.)

Barry: Well, we've actually been doing a little investigation of our own. We call it the (He takes a picture off the wall and turns it around to reveal drawings of an Imperian, a Geckle, Elway, and a Hypnobrai.) "Mystery of the Mood-Changing Neighbors."

Jenny: Barry and I have been suspicious of Lee's story for a while, (She gets up from the couch.) but anyone who openly questions Lee disappears, and comes back days later with all their doubts... gone.

Barry: Mm-hmm.

Nya: You think Lee is doing something to them? Altering their minds? Jay, you said the Administration checked for all kinds of mind control.

Jay: They did. On a wide scale, but if Lee's only using it selectively on one or two people, the Administration wouldn't have detected it.

Barry: What's the Administration?

Jay: Let's worry about one thing at a time.

Jenny: There's an off-limits backstage area in this cavern. We think Lee's bringing the neighbors there.

Jay: We've been back there. It was definitely weird, but we didn't see any kidnappees.

Nya: Might be worth looking with Barry and Jenny's help. Maybe that's where Lloyd and Wyldfyre are.

Barry: Okay, so this backstage entrance sees the most activity, but we haven't been able to break in.

Jay: Leave that to me.

(Arin and Sora arrive in the Land of Lee in their new jet, which is a combination of their bikes.)

Arin: This won't take long. I just-- I want to say goodbye.

Sora: Take all the time you need, Arin.

(Arin and Sora walk towards Arin's parents' grave and are unknowingly spied on by some Lees. Arin gasps when he sees the hole in his parents' grave)

Sora: What? Are their graves empty?

Arin: If my parents aren't here, where are they?

(Jenny, Barry, Nya, and Jay approach the backstage entrance, which is being guarded by two Lees holding swords.)

Barry: Bad neighbors are brought here. Sometimes it's days before they come out again.

(They see two more Lees holding swords approaching the backstage entrance.)

Jay: I can easily take out two guards, but alerting one Lee alerts all Lees.

Jenny: Yup, many bodies, one mind.

Nya: (To Jay.) Jenny and I will lure him away while you and Barry sneak in. Be careful.

Jay: You too. (He puts on his mask.)

(Barry approaches the Lees at the backstage entrance.)

Barry: Excuse me, aren't those the Bad Neighbors you're looking for?

(Jenny, disguised as Strong McCoolGuy, and Nya run away and a Lee runs after them.)

Lee: Thanks! It's neighbors like you that give neighbors a good name.

(Lee leaves and Jay arrives.)

Barry: I gotta admit, you're different than I thought you'd be.

Jay: We knew each other?

Barry: Well, not personally, but the Ninja are famous. My son had a poster of you up on his wall, and my wife was a big fan of your old game show.

Jay: Why would a Ninja host a game show? That sounds fake.

(Jay uses his Lightning on the access panel to open the door and he and Barry enter backstage.)

Barry: And Arin said one time you got trapped in a video game... (He laughs.) and became a rocking superstar! Oh, and when--

Jay: Now you're just making things up.

(They arrive in a hallway where a Lee is sweeping the floor.)

Lee: Sorry, but this area is for cast members only. It's off-limits to Neighbors. Not for any secret, nefarious reason--

(Wyldfyre uses her Heat to knock the door down on Lee and roars.)

Wyldfyre: (To Jay.) What are you doing here?

Jay: Uh, rescuing you.

Wyldfyre: (She snorts.) I rescued me. (Lloyd leaves the room too.) And him.

Barry: (He gasps.) You knocked out a Lee. Lee will not like that. More of him will already be on the way.

Lloyd: (He gasps.) You're Arin's dad! You have no idea how good it is to meet you.

Barry: Hey, the legendary Green Ninja!

Jay: Intros later! Things are about to get way Lee up in here!

(As the mechanical sun begins to set, Nya and Jenny hide from the Lees.)

Jenny: So glad you and Jay had each other through the Merge. Don't know what Barry and I would have done without each other.

Nya: Funny you say that. I only just found Jay. He lost his memories, and doesn't even remember who I am.

Jenny: Really? That's not what his body language says, and as an amateur detective, I study body language.

Nya: What does his body language tell you?

Jenny: That maybe his mind doesn't remember you, but his heart sure does.

(Nya smiles as she hears Jenny say this, but an alarm goes off and all the Lees gather.)

Lees: Neighbors! Please return to your homes for an emergency, um, street cleaning.

(As the neighbors return to their homes, Jay, Wyldfyre, Lloyd, and Barry exit through the backstage entrance.)

Jay: We need to meet up with Nya and get to the elevator.

Lloyd: What about the other residents?

Jay: Not paying me to rescue everyone.

(They run and accidentally encounter the Lees.)

Lee(s): Impostors! Here to ruin everything! This is all just so un-neighborly!

(Lloyd, Jay, and Wyldfyre prepare to fight them.)

Jay: Guess the only way out is through.

Wyldfyre: Love that! Eat fireball, Lee! H'yah! (She fires Heat at the Lees, but ends up hitting a pizza cart instead, which flies off and lands on top of a Lee.)

Jay: (To Wyldfyre.) I'm starting to like you.

(Lloyd runs in, performs Spinjitzu and knocks down some Lees, Wyldfyre jumps on their heads and Jay knocks down two Lees, but is knocked down by another Lee. Nya arrives and knocks down this Lee using her Water.)

Nya: Guess I'll never get to finish that needlepoint class I started.

(Nya helps Jay up. Jenny reunites with Barry as the Lees approach.)

(Sora and Arin continue looking at the hole.)

Arin: I-- I don't understand.

(They notice two Lees running to the elevator.)

Sora: Where'd those guys come from?

Arin: Beats me! Maybe they have some answers! (He and Sora run towards the Lees.) Hey, you guys! What's going on? (The stone elevator door closes.) Oh, no, you don't! (He performs Spinjitzu, destroys the stone door, and he and Sora see that the elevator is gone.)

Sora: An elevator shaft?

(Arin uses his Grapple Gun to send him and Sora down the elevator shaft and see the Community of Lee.)

Sora: What? This place is amazing, but probably evil, given our luck.

(They see the Lees on the street)

Arin: Look, some kind of street fight.

(They notice the Ninja fighting the Lees.)

Sora: Are those the Ninja?

(The Lees begin to gain the upper hand on the Ninja and knock them to the ground with a human chain.)

Lloyd: The Lees can out-teamwork us. We have to divide their attention! For once, don't work together!

Jay: I do my best work solo.

(Jay kicks the Lees' human chain down, but another Lee arrives with the Mech Dragon and attacks Jay, who dodges. Jay takes a button controller from his pocket and presses the button, activating the control implant and knocking the Mech Dragon down.)

Jay: I'll be taking this, thank you.

(Jay takes control of the Mech Dragon and manages to defeat more Lees, with the Ninja joining him. Arin and Sora run and jump across the rooftops towards the fight.)

Arin: If the Ninja are here, they're helping people... (He jumps to another roof.) what I should have been doing instead of following Ras.

Sora: Don't be so hard on yourself. You only did that for your parents.

(They stop and Arin realizes something.)

Arin: My parents!

Sora: Yeah, that's what I said.

Arin: No, look! My parents! (He sees Lloyd defending Jenny and Barry from the Lees.) Lloyd found them.

(Arin puts on his hood and uses his Grapple Gun to charge into the fight. Sora runs to join him, but a Lee drops in front of her with a cart.)

Sora: Whoa! You okay, Mister? (She helps Lee up and smiles.)

Lee: How'd you get here? Not really the best time to start a New Neighbor Orientation, but okay. ♪ Perhaps you're feeling down in the dumps... (Sora is confused.) Yeah, let's wait until I can get my other bodies to harmonize. It'll sound much better.

Sora: "Other bodies"?

(Frak, Cole and Kai continue removing the dragon skeleton from underground.)

Cole: I think... this is... it!

(They finish removing the dragon skeleton and are exhausted.)

Kai: You did it!

Cole: (He speaks while painting.) We... did it!

(They look at the dragon skeleton.)

Kai: This dragon makes the Firstbourne look like a pup.

Cole: It can only be one thing, right? (Kai uses his Fire to destroy the remaining stones on the top of the skeleton.) The remains of a dead Source Dragon.

Kai: Whoa!

(They see the skeleton of the Source Dragon of Balance begin to glow and disappear. Master Wu's spirit appears behind them.)

Wu: What was once lost... (They turn and notice him.) can now be found...

Cole: Master Wu?

(Master Wu's spirit disappears.)

(Nya kicks Lee to the door of a house, which is opened by the tenant.)

Lee: Greetings, Neighbor! Just going door to door assuring Neighbors that everything is fine. Oh, uh, one moment. (He makes two copies of himself.) Nothing to worry about.

(Arin knocks down several Lees and sees more of them approaching his parents. He tries to help them but is crushed by the Lees. Wyldfyre knocks down some Lees with kicks while Lloyd knocks down others using Spinjitzu.)

Lloyd: This isn't getting us anywhere.

Jay: Speak for yourself! (He runs through the Lees on top of the Mech Dragon, but one of them, on top of a house's roof, makes several copies of himself up to another roof. A bowl of noodles hits Jay in the face and, when he takes it, he bumps into Lees' chain and falls off the robot dragon, which flies up, leaving only the mech.) I should have seen that coming.

Lee: I'm gonna punch you so hard!

Jay: Like a single punch could take down a stone-cold warrior like me! (Sixteen Lees join together and form a giant fist.) That's cheating.

(Lees' giant fist hits Jay and he falls in front of a house.)

Wyldfyre: (She continues pressing the button control.) Why... isn't... this... thing... working?

(The Mech Dragon flies to the ceiling and destroys the mechanical moon into three pieces, which almost falls on Lee until Sora saves him. The other pieces of the mechanical moon fall onto the street.)

Lee: (To Sora.) Why'd you save me? You're from the outside world. You don't need me.

Sora: I need you not to be crushed.

Lee: Why?

Sora: 'Cause a nice, normal person wants people to be okay and not be crushed by falling debris. You don't spend a lot of time with other people or something?

Lee: I never met another person until the Merge.

Sora: Did you build this place? (Lee nods.) Why?

Lee: (He picks up a rock.) Because I need people.

(A flashback shows the Realm of Lee sometime before the Merge.)

Lee: (Narrating.) For so, so long, in the Realm of Lee, there was only Lee. (He makes copies of himself.) And Lee, and Lee, and Lee... (The Lees play hide-and-seek, the cup challenge, using bowls, and stamping, but are sad at the end of all of them.) I tried to keep myself busy, but no matter how many bodies I created, there was only just me. Just Lee. (The Lees sit down in sadness.) Then one day, something remarkable happened. (The Lees watch the Merge.) I didn't know it at first, but the Realm of Lee merged with other realms. Lee was no longer alone.

(After the Merge, a barrel approaches the Land of Lee and a man named Castaway appears inside it and Lee helps him out.)

Past Castaway: Where... where am I?

Past Lee(s): You're in Lee, of course!

Past Lee: Wait, are there other places to be?

Lee: (Narrating.) He was the first Non-Lee I had ever met, a whole other person who had thoughts that weren't my thoughts. (The Lees plays with Castaway.) Games are so much more fun when you don't know what the other player is going to do! Life was all new. I realized other people must be the key to being happy. He told me of the outside world, its wonders, and what he missed... (Castaway shows a drawing of a carnival.) until one day, he missed them so much, he left Lee. I stayed, because there is no Lee without... Lee. (He opens the Community of Lee's blueprints.) Right then, I vowed to make the Land of Lee so perfect, no one would ever want to leave again.

(Flashback ends.)

Lee: And I succeeded! Why else would the Administration dump its unwanteds here? I painted a wonderful rainbow of lies so my new Neighbors wouldn't ever be curious about what's outside, but these new outsiders want to wash it all away! I won't let those Ninja take my friends from me!

Sora: I was alone once, too. A single lie pushed away the first real friend I ever made. Friends shouldn't lie to friends, ever, but especially on, like, a city-wide level.

Lee: Well, if you have a way of making friends that doesn't involve mass deception and brainwashing, I'd like to hear it.

(Lloyd and Nya defeat some more Lees.)

Lloyd: Nya, one of us has to grab Arin's parents and go-- Oh, no, where are Barry and Jenny? (He sees Barry and Jenny and tries to go to them, but is stopped by two Lees.)

Lee: So you came here for those two? Then I'll be sure to keep them here forever!

(Some Lees run towards Barry and Jenny.)

Lloyd: No!

(Arin arrives and performs Spinjitzu on the Lees to stop them from hurting his parents.)

Barry: Another Ninja?

Jenny: I don't know this color. He must be new.

(Arin is crushed by the Lees.)

Lee: I am a good friend. I am a good friend!

Sora: (She watches what is happening and speaks to Lee.) No, you're not being a good friend right now, but you can be. Tell everyone the truth. Let them go if they want, and maybe some will stay, and those will be real friends. I've only been here a few minutes, and I think the Land of Lee is pretty amazing.

Lee: You-- You do? For real?

(Lee is happy and this makes all the other Lees stop attacking. Arin starts walking towards his parents, who still don't recognize him and are a little scared of him because of the hood.)

Arin: Mom? Dad?

(Jenny and Barry realize something when they hear this and then Arin takes off his hood and reveals his face to them.)

Jenny and Barry: Arin!

(They run towards their son and hug him, happy to finally be reunited after so many years. An Administration portal opens.)

Lee: A new neighbor? The Administration always drops them off above ground.

(Agent Allen steps out of the portal and falls to the ground.)

Allen: Whatever you are, get away from me!

(The hollow Administration Agents exit the portal as the sound of a magical flute is heard.)

Nya: Is that... music?

Arin: Oh, no.

Sora: Not here. Not now.

(Sora turns on her Prismatic Lamp and makes the Spectral Dragonians visible. One of them grabs and knocks down a Lee and extracts his soul, leaving him and all the other Lees hollow.)

Lees: Look around and see... in the Land of Lee...

(End of episode. For more information, click here.)
