(In the Land of Monsters, Kai takes out his sword during a storm.)

Kai: I had no idea what alone truly meant. (He slashes a monster with his sword and holds it out to defend against the monsters surrounding him.) Until I was stranded in a Land of Monsters. (Shouts.)

(Kai follows a compass through numerous places.)

Kai: A forsaken land so far from home, from family. (He hears a chime and hides. A monster steps out of the portal. A twig snaps in the distance and the monster follows the sound.) But I have to endure. (A creature roars and Kai turns around. He runs away and crosses a bridge.) I have to continue. (A monster's appendage comes up from the ground and Kai starts to run. Kai walks and runs past monsters. What looks to be a statue moves its head to face Kai's back. Kai takes a Sandogasa shaped hat from a skeleton and looks back when he hears a wolf-like monster howl. He stares at the compass as he sits between stone statues of humans. A creature turns to face him.) So I can see them again and make everything right.

(The compass powers down and Kai hits it. A dragon scorches the land and the ground morphs and explodes. Kai shouts as a chunk of land is lifted up by a monster. Kai grunts as he tries to grab hold of the tilted land. He manages to grab hold of a tree and the dragon and monster battle.)

Kai: Give me a break. (The dragon flies toward the Monster's egg. He sighs and takes out a red scarf.) Why can't I just mind my own business?

(He jumps off the tree and onto the dragon. The dragon roars and Kai throws a fireball between them and the egg. The dragon flies away from it.)

Daidan: You defend this creature?

Kai: That creature was defending itself from a big, green, flying monster.

Daidan: I am not the monster here.

Kai: Ha! Could've fooled me.

(The monster smacks them with its hand and they fall to the ground. Kai tries to get up, but Daidan screeches at him. Kai holds out his sword.)

Kai: Wait, wait, wait! Tell me what's going on. What is that thing?

Daidan: The beast that ravaged my homelands and devoured my future so I'm here to bring it to the same fate, to destroy its future.

Kai: So you know it's wrong. it's just an egg, not a monster.

Daidan: Yet!

(Daidan powers up and flies toward the egg.)

Kai: Stop! Look at what you're becoming. (Daiden gets ready to blast the egg.) Look at yourself! (Daidan stops in front of the egg and looks at her reflection in it. She flies away.) Yes! Uh-oh.

(The monster tries to grab Kai, but Daidan saves him.)

(On a ledge, Kai and Daidan sit face to face between a fire.)

Kai: Thank you.

Daidan: (Scoffs.) I should have left you to die. You're weak. You made me weak.

Kai: If you think sparing children is weak, then you have no idea what real strength is.

Daidan: Good people do not last very long here. It's impossible to survive in a Land of Monsters without becoming a monster yourself.

(She flies away. Kai looks at his red scarf.)

Kai: Let's hope you're wrong.

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
