Arin: Uh, does this really count as training?

Kai: Of course Kai's Super Awesome Slide Course counts as training! We're on a mediation carpet. Which means it's meditation.

Sora: But when Lloyd gets back, he's not going to be-

Kai: And we're off! (uses Fire to slide the carpet down, he, Arin, and Sora whoop as they slide down)

Cole: (Arriving on an Earth Dragon before jumping off) Ninja!

Kai, Arin, and Sora: (in unison as they were at the end of the course) COLE!

Cole: (turns to see them, only to be knocked down.) Ouch! Kai's Super Awesome Slide Course? Let me guess, Lloyd's away?

Kai: When you see him, say nothing.

Cole: Fine. But I need help. I learned a shipment of dangerous items was sent to Ninjago. Even Chronosteel.

Arin: Uh, Chrono-what now?

Kai: A very rare metal that can hold Elemental Powers.

Sora: Wow. Wonder what I could make with that?

Kai: Once, someone made blades out of Chronosteel that almost ruined all space and time.

Sora: I would try not to do that.

Cole: The real problem is I have no idea where in Ninjago the items were sent.

Arin: (He sighs) I think I know where they are.

(The screen changes to a homemade commercial of Kreel trying to sell the items.)

Kreel: That's right folks! Kreel's Junkyard's got a massive shipment of super rare, super danger- uhh... Exotic items from all over the Merged Realms! Get them before they sell out! Like this weird thing. (She picks up one of the items.) What's it do? No idea! But… (She presses the device's button, which gives a discharge to a nearby Whack Rat, giving him the power of flight.)

Whack Rat: Ouch. (flies again.)

Kreel: Uh, I'm, uh... I'm sure he'll be fine. So, come pay me huge amounts of money for any of this wild junk from rare places! (The Whack Rat recording stop the record.) And cut.

Sora: Kreel! You can't sell that stuff.

Kreel: What is this word “can't sell?” I literally don't know what it means.

Arin: You have been running ads about wild junk from weird places for days.

Cole: And it’s all too dangerous to have out like this.

Lobbo: Hm. (Lobbo picks up the device.) This would look swell on Lobbo’s new end table. Lobbo Lobbo!

Arin: Uh, Lobbo. Buddy, careful.

Kreel: Nothing here can harm anyone!

(Lobbo drops the device, which soon gives a discharge towards him and he immediately started to grow until he was giant with a red eye and spikes all over)

Sora: You were saying, Kreel?

Kreel: Meh. Do we really know if he's harmed?

Lobbo: Owww! Lobbo in pain! Lobbo Lobbo! (grabs a wrecked car and throws it towards the affect Whack Rat, who flies out of the way before grabbing a stand and throws it at the table, knocking it over)

Cole: We gotta stop him before someone gets hurt! Ninja go! (does Spinjitzu, along with Kai and Arin)

Lobbo: (swats them away) Lobbo angry! Lobbo Lobbo! (throws a tire)

Kai: (melts the tire with Fire) He's not thinking straight! (the boys do Spinjitzu again and charge toward Lobbo)

Sora: (looks at the device) Something in this device interacted with Lobbo’s mechanical body. (picks it up, notices a block of Chronosteel and picks it up)

Cole: (lands behind Sora and sees her with the Chronosteel) Don't touch that. It's Chronosteel.

Sora: I was hoping you'd say that. (uses Technology on the Chronosteel and some pieces of metal, and three frisbee-like devices were instantly created)

(Kai and Arin try to charge at Lobbo with Spinjitzu, only for him to grab Kai, slaps Arin back, and throws Kai forward. They both land before Arin charges while Kai was getting up)

Sora: Kai, heads up! (throws the red device to Kai, who catches it)

Kai: A frisbee? Fun, but not the time.

Sora: Toss it up!

Kai: (throws it up and activates it, turning into the Elemental Fire Mech) Wha. . . ha! Ho, ho! (gets in) A mech from Chronosteel? I can channel my Elemental Powers!

Lobbo: (spins as he tries to attack Arin, who backflips to dodge him before kicking him. Kai then charged and punches Lobbo, sending him to the ground) Owww!

Sora: (hands Cole the black device) One for you. And one for me. (holds up the white and pink device. They both throw up their devices and activate them, turning into the Elemental Earth Mech and Elemental Tech Mech respectively. Lobbo avoids a kick from Kai's mech before Cole grabs his arm) Hold him! (Kai grabs Lobbo's other hand) I might be able to drain that energy out of him. (comes in front of him)

Lobbo: You just want to hurt Lobbo! Lobbo Lobbo!

Arin: (uses his Grapple Gun to get on the back of Sora's mech to face Lobbo) Lobbo, I know whatever hit you is making you angry, but it's us! Arin and Sora from the Crossroads! We're trying to help.

Lobbo: You help Lobbo? Lobbo Lobbo.

Sora: You bet! (uses Technology to attract the device and turn the left arm into a blaster) Let's hope this thing drains as much energy as it projects! (aims at Lobbo before firing, draining the energy out of him, until he was back to normal, and Kai placed him down before a dust cloud occurs. Upon fading, Lobbo was back to normal size)

Lobbo: (moans) What happened here? Clearly nothing to do with Lobbo. Lobbo Lobbo. (leaves)

(Kai, Cole, and Sora get out of their mechs, which close up into their frisbee-like state, and they catch them)

Arin: Good work. Now we just- (squeaking gains their attention and they see Kreel with the items in a wheelbarrow as she and a Whack Rat try to sneak away) Hey! Where do you think you're going?!

Kreel: (stops and faces them) Me? (stammers) Oh, I just, I. . . Wow, the ninja are right. This stuff's way too dangerous to sell. (she looks away) On my property, where all my stuff can get smashed. Better to sell it somewhere else.

Cole: (steps in front of her) I don't think so. We'll store these items safely. (pushes the wheelbarrow as Arin and Sora follow him while Kai was thinking about something as Kreel glares at them)

Kai: Hey, I wonder if any of it could be used to build Kai's Even Super Awesomer Slide Course. (Cole, Arin, and Sora stop to glare at him) Kidding, just kidding! (follows them while muttering under breath) Sort of.
