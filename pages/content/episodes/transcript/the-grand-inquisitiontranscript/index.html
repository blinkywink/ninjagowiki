(The Loyalists escape the cave, with Kai, Nya, and Jay trapped inside.)

Jay: We can't let them get away with that weapon!

(Jay takes out his Lightning Daggers and hurls himself at the force field, causing zero damage and leaving him lying on the ground.)

Nya: Slick move. Now, stand back.

(She attempts to water blast the shield, causing water to spray about.)

Kai: Ugh! Are you wrecking my hair on purpose?

Jay: Ugh! I got this!

Kai: Wait, wait, wait, Jay! I'm standing in-- (Jay shoots Lightning at the force field, which travels through the water and electrocutes Kai.) N-N-N-No! What kind of Master of Lightning forgets water conducts electricity?

Jay: Maybe the kind who suffer from amnesia.

Kai: Hate to say it, but that actually gave me an idea. Even though all of our powers are on the fritz, maybe if we... combine all three together? H'yah! (Kai cartwheels to the left of Nya. (The three combine their powers and blast the force field, breaking through it.) Ha! I'm a genius.

(They run outside to find the Loyalists just leaving. The Lead Loyalist throws down an electric bomb which effects their machines.)

Kai: What was that? What'd they do?

Jay: An electro-magnetic pulse. Every vehicle here is fried.

Nya: They really don't want us following.

Kai: Well, they got their wish. Crossing the Land of Madness on foot takes forever. I speak from experience.

Jay: The Samurai X Mech. The D.B. Express. The Earth Driller! Nya, you're like the greatest engineer and inventor basically ever.

Nya: Those memories are coming back?

Jay: I can't remember everything, but everything I remember about you is amazing.

Kai: But all you remember about me is my bad ideas?

Jay: What else is there? Fried circuits or not, I bet you can take all these parts and make us one working... something?

Nya: Let's give it a shot!

(In Umbrasilvis, the Grand Inquisitor begins the Inquisition.)

Grand Inquisitor: Umbrasilvis is on lockdown until the devious perpetrator who released the monster on this humble burg can be identified, judged, and, most importantly, punished by a life sentence in the Restraints of Excruciation!

(The townsfolk jeer and wag wooden sticks.)

Frak: Uh... why is everyone shaking sticks?

Grand Inquisitor: "Shaking"? They wag Ancient Wagging Sticks, the traditional way of expressing oneself in Umbrasilvan court. Everyone knows that! Now, there's a criminal among us, and their evil must be purged! (A bugle is blown and the townsfolk shout more.) As Grand Inquisitor, I hereby declare the Inquisition begins!

(The Inquisitor questions Sora.)

Grand Inquisitor: True or false. You are an outsider to Umbrasilvis, and thus of suspicious character and motive.

Sora: Uh, false.

Grand Inquisitor: So you aren't an outsider?

Sora: No... I mean, yes. We're not from here, but--

Grand Inquisitor: Were you lying then or lying now?

Sora: Neither!

(The crowd jeers. The scene fades to Frak being questioned.)

Grand Inquisitor: Don't you think a fountain is a suspicious way to enter a city? Unless you're water. Are you water?

Frak: What? No! We didn't come through your fountain by choice.

Grand Inquisitor: So you were sent here under orders!

Frak: That's not what I said!

Grand Inquisitor: It seems there's a lot you're not saying.

(The crowd jeers again. Wyldfyre is now being questioned.)

Grand Inquisitor: Would you say you're known for how much you like to fight, Wyldfyre?

Wyldfyre: Of course! You wanna go right now?

Grand Inquisitor: And would your associates describe you as "thriving on chaos"?

Wyldfyre: Duh.

Grand Inquisitor: The kind of chaos that might result from, oh, I don't know, releasing a dangerous monster?

Wyldfyre: Can I wear your tall hat?

Grand Inquisitor: "Hat"? Hat?! Let the record show that the witness disrespected the Mantle of Righteousness! I think it's obvious here who the culprit is.

Freya: She was with the dragon, sir. (The scene shifts to Freya testifying.) The outsider known as Wyldfyre was in my windmill with the dragon, Riyu, all night. Wouldn't leave his side. She couldn't possibly have let that monster out.

Grand Inquisitor: Interesting, and were her companions also with the dragon?

Freya: Almost all night.

Grand Inquisitor: "Almost"? But you can't account for their whereabouts at every moment? Very interesting.

(The scene shifts to Reginald's testimony.)

Reginald: The Explorer's Club adheres to strict regulations. Members are required to log their comings and goings on the ship in this book. Now, as you can see, each member of the expedition is accounted for last night.

Sora: Those entires could've been written whenever. That's easily faked.

Grand Inquisitor: Nonsense! These records are immaculate. No one with margins that clean would falsify records! And do the names of the other outsiders, these "nin-jah," appear anywhere in the logbook?

Reginald: No.

Grand Inquisitor: Can you vouch for any of the "nin-jah" at all?

Reginald: No. I cannot.

(The scene shifts to Frak and Sora being questioned together.)

Grand Inquisitor: Isn't it true that you were on the airship the night in question?

Sora: I already told you, we went there to get a cure for Riyu.

Grand Inquisitor: And yet your names do not appear on the aforementioned logbook.

Frak: We didn't even know about that stupid book.

(The townsfolk gasp and shout.)

Grand Inquisitor: So you snuck onto a ship, violated it's regulations, and released the monster on an innocent population!

Sora: That's not what happened!

Grand Inquisitor: We shall see. The Court must gather additional facts based on today's testimony. In the meantime, bring forth the shame boards!

(Two people from the crowd put signs on Sora and Frak.)

Sora: "Warning: Dangerous Suspect." Are these really necessary?

Grand Inquisitor: Very much so. A Thread of Truth is woven through each cord. I will know if the signs are removed. Such an act would result in an immediate guilty verdict! This Inquisition is in recess!

(Everyone else disperses.)

Wyldfyre: I think I'd make a pretty good Inquisitor since I'm always right, and I look good in hats.

Frak: I can't believe my dad wouldn't vouch for us.

Sora: You saw someone with a mask on that airship last night. Someone who probably didn't sign that book either.

Frak: Yeah... we gotta find that weirdo, or we'll be declared guilty for sure.

Sora: Well, the Inquisitor isn't the only one who knows how to inquire. Any clues have to be on the airship. Let's go.

(In the Whispering Wood, Arin and Euphrasia observe a dying Acona.)

Arin: I don't know how to help them.

(The Acona's eyes close, and it dissolves into light, leaving behind an Acona seedling. Euphrasia gasps.)

Arin: It's a seedling! (An Acona chitters.) Huh?

Euphrasia: I think they want us to plant it.

(Zane talks to the Arc Dragon of Life.)

Arc Dragon of Life: I've never seen the wood reach this point. I don't know how much longer it-- or I-- can hold on, and if the Whispering Wood falls, the Merged Lands will eventually fall too. (It collapses onto the ground. Zane comes to its head to support it.) You are... comforting me? Though you are only a simulation of life, your emotions seem almost real.

Zane: I may sometimes struggle to communicate my feelings, but I feel them as deeply as anyone else.

(Nearby, Arin and Euphrasia plant the Acona seedling.)

Arc Dragon of Life: Thank you for being with me at the very end.

Zane: Maybe this isn't the end. Believe in Lloyd. There have been many times when he's faced dire situations and come out stronger.

Arc Dragon of Life: That is false hope. At his age, Lloyd is too deeply rooted in his path to cut himself free and live.

(The Acona seedling sprouts, causing the other Acona to chitter.)

Zane: The Whispering Wood and it's guardian, the Green Ninja, may be more resilient than even you realize.

(Lloyd stands face to face with age he skipped over.)

Lloyd: So, exactly how long would I have kept that haircut?

Tween Lloyd: Whatever, dork. You wanna talk hair? How old was I when I forgot what a comb was? No wonder I still don't have a girlfriend.

Lloyd: Hey! I'm busy taking care of a whole monastery, and--

Tween Lloyd: Whatever. Look, I'm here to prepare you for the rest of the ritual.

Lloyd: You?

(Tween Lloyd raises his eyebrows, then knocks Lloyd off his feet. He offers out a hand.)

Tween Lloyd: You ready, punk?

(Sora and Frak search through boxes on the airship.)

Frak: Sometimes I think "exploring" is just code for stealing. You really think we're gonna find a clue in one of these crates?

Sora: It'd help identify our suspect if we had a motive. They've gotta be hiding something somewhere. (Frak knocks a bottle off a box, Sora catches it with her foot.) Careful!

Frak: Show-off. Oh! (He falls off the box and lands on his back. While lying on the ground, he notices an orange scale.) A-ha! A scale! We have our villain!

Sora: Who?

Frak: There's only one Explorer who could have left this! My father!

Sora: You think your dad did it? You defended him when I suggested he maybe wasn't such a great dad.

Frak: Our relationship is... complicated, but you heard him at the inquisition. He couldn't put his neck on the line for his own son. Why? He's guilty.

Sora: But the expedition has been using this ship for a while. Your dad could've shed this scale at any point during their travels.

Frak: Yeah. The Inquisitor is gonna need more to prove his guilt. Let's keep searching.

(They walk down a hallway in the airship.)

Frak: After we heard that scream, I came here, and found my dad wearing a mask back there in the dark.

Sora: We don't know it was your dad.

Frak: I do. Come on. (The hallway gets tight and they bump into each other.) Oh--

(They face each other, pressing their signs together to squeeze by.)

Sora: Ooh, sorry.

Frak: (laughs) Yeah.

(They turn a corner to find a dead end.)

Frak: No door. No air vent. No way out. Why would he free a monster only to back himself into a corner?

Sora: Huh, this material looks newer than the rest of the corridor.

(Sora presses on a secret button, gasping as a latch opens, revealing a room with an electronic device inside.)

Sora: Woah! Blast from the past. It's Imperium tech, the model that was used in the Advanced Systems Lab.

Frak: Where would my dad get that?

Sora: Maybe it's stolen?

Frak: (scoffs) He's good at that. "Exploring."

Sora: It's analyzing the monster's energy signature and searching for resonance somewhere in Umbrasilvis.

Frak: But you don't have to break a monster loose to study it.

Sora: Maybe they needed readings as it moved around, to triangulate synchronous energy sources.

Frak: Oh, yes, the synchronous... S-synchronous.

Sora: It's still processing the readings, trying to find the location of the resonant item, but I might be able to give it a boost if I-- There! Found the location! We don't know what he was searching for, but we do know where it is.

Frak: You're putting it back?

Sora: The only way to prove their identity is to give them exactly what they want...

Frak: And be there to ambush them when they try to get it. Ha! My dad won't see us coming! (They attempt to climb out of the hatch, tripping.) Woah!

(Lloyd tries to take Tween Lloyd's hand, but he pulls it back.)

Tween Lloyd: Too slow! (laughs)

Lloyd: (He gets up.) I forgot what I brat I could be sometimes.

(They walk up to a massive tree with many large mushrooms growing out of it. Tween Lloyd jumps up a couple of the mushrooms.)

Tween Lloyd: You coming or not?

(Lloyd looks up at the tree, then jumps onto the same mushroom Tween Lloyd first did, where it crumbles beneath his feet.)

Lloyd: Woah! (He falls to the ground, grunting.)

Tween Lloyd: What did I say? I'm a path you didn't take, so take a different path.

(The two race up the tree, climbing a different set of mushrooms. Lloyd stops on one to look down.)

Tween Lloyd: What did you think of your vision?

Lloyd: I saw a network of roots. The trees were all... connected somehow? And when one needed help, the others gave it nutrients.

Tween Lloyd: And... what does that tell you about the power of the Green Ninja? (Lloyd thinks for a moment.) Or do you need to get stuck under the dirt again?

(Tween Lloyd hops onto Lloyd mushroom then onto another, causing Lloyd's to crumble and him to fall.)

Lloyd: Aah!

(Lloyd screams, falling before Tween Lloyd catches him by the hand, hanging him off a mushroom.)

Tween Lloyd: Think. How did we get our powers?

Lloyd: The Golden Weapons... and the other Ninja. They surrounded me. I could feel their energy, but what does that have to do with--? (Tween Lloyd lets go and Lloyd falls again.) Aah! (He screams before landing on a giant scroll with four trees surrounding one bigger one on it.) Power comes from within, but... not all of it. Some of our power comes from... those around us? Our friends, our family, our community?

Tween Lloyd: Bingo. (He drops from the sky onto the scroll, changing it to the drawing of the Ninja from the Green Ninja Prophecy.) Each tree is connected. Trees are life. You are the Master of Life.

Lloyd: The Elemental Master of Life is the master of the connection between and through all things.

(Tween Lloyd jumps again, adding drawings of Arin, Sora, Frak, and Nya to the scroll.)

Tween Lloyd: Just as you gained your powers through others' connection to you, your true potential can only manifest when you connect to those around you.

(In Umbrasilvis, Sora and Frak enter a gate to find the Tree of Burdens.)

Frak: It's beautiful, in a sad way.

Sora: The Tree of Burdens. I overheard a local talking about it. Each ribbon represents a worry. You tie a ribbon to the tree hoping its branches will help bear the weight of your problem.

Frak: Guess that's why it's so twisted and bent. Wish I had a ribbon for my Dad. Being perfect was the only way I could get his attention, but it was never enough. I was never enough.

Sora: (She puts her hand on Frak's shoulder.) You're more than enough. Sometimes too much.

(Footsteps approach and the gate reopens.)

Frak: That could be Dad. Quick, hide!

(The two hide behind a bush as a Loyalist enters and approaches the tree. He pulls out the same Imperian device from the airship.)

Frak: Come on, let's grab him.

Sora: (She pulls Frak back.) Let's see what he wants with the tree first. It may be a clue to fighting the Elemental Monsters.

(The Loyalist reaches into the tree and pulls out the fragment of the Weapon of Desolation.)

Sora: What could that thing be?

Frak: That's enough.

Sora: Frak, wait!

Frak: (He reveals himself.) Stop! I know it's you, Dad!

(The Loyalist attempts to run away, with Frak and Sora chasing behind them. The Loyalist fires a blaster at them, and Sora and Frak trip. Reginald appears and jumps on the Loyalist.)

Reginald: Leave my son alone!

Frak: (gasps) Dad? How?

(Reginald removes the Loyalist's mask to reveal Johnny Courageous.)

Reginald: Ha!

Sora: Johnny Courageous?

Reginald: You betrayed the Explorer's Club? Jolly bad form, old boy!

(Sora wipes his face to reveal an Imperian marking.)

Sora: (gasps) You are Imperian! Civil servant class? What did you take from the Tree?

Johnny: Tell her I don't speak to traitors.

Sora: Traitor? I helped liberate Imperium!

Johnny: You helped it fall! All hail the Good Empress!

Sora: "The Good Empress"? What?

Frak: At least this Empress won't get whatever this is--

(Frak picks up the fragmented Weapon of Desolation, causing his powers to surge uncontrollably. Johnny Courageous attempts to escape, but Sora entraps him with his own blaster.)

Frak: Something's happening! I can't control this!

Reginald: It's going to be okay, son. (He grabs onto the fragment of the Weapon.) You have to let it go. (Frak lets go and Reginald wraps the fragment of the Weapon in a handkerchief.) Yes, this handkerchief was enchanted by the Pontifex Magicus of Mysterium. Not my first time handling a cursed relic.

Sora: We're, uh, gonna need that. Hopefully it will help clear our name with the Inquisitor.

Reginald: Oh, right, right. (He reluctantly hands it over to Sora.) Uh, sorry. I'm not all that familiar with handing over rare artifacts.

Frak: Thanks, Dad.

Reginald: I overheard you. You thought the villain... was me?

Frak: I did. 'Cause it was easy to believe.

Reginald: Egads! I take offense.

Frak: You didn't even try to defend me in that court. But why would you have? You were never there for me when I needed you. Gone for every important moment in my life, chasing some discovery that would be your legacy? Guess you never thought I could be your legacy.

Reginald: (sighs) Frak, I just wanted you to have a father you could be proud of. You don't know what it used to be like for Serpentine in Ninjago. "Never trust a snake," they used to say. I had to work twice as hard for twice as long to get any recognition.

Frak: Yeah, well, you didn't even work half as hard at being a good dad.

(Reginald reaches out his hand, but Frak shoves past him, walking away with Sora. Reginald stares on before sighing to himself.)

(Lloyd and Tween Lloyd are back on the forest floor.)

Lloyd: Why'd the Temple show you to me? I'd just accepted the incident with Tomorrow's Tea as another part of my destiny.

Tween Lloyd: The Ritual of Unrooting requires you understand your connections to others, even to other yous. Who you were. Who you could have been. Who you might become. (He puts his hand on Lloyd's shoulder.) Remember that for this next part.

Lloyd: Uh, next part? This wasn't the test?

Tween Lloyd: (laughs) Are you serious? All this was just to give you a fighting chance in there.

(Tween Lloyd extends his arm, and an archway made of roots and lined with flowers appears in front of them.)

Tween Lloyd: Meditate on your past and prepare for the future. Through that archway is your greatest foe.

Lloyd: Who?

Tween Lloyd: An enemy you've faced many times. One you've temporarily bested, but never truly defeated.

Lloyd: Huh... That doesn't really narrow it down. (He sits.)

Tween Lloyd: Only by defeating this enemy will you be able to fully connect to the pathways of the Whispering Wood and embrace your power as the Legendary Green Ninja.

(Tween Lloyd turns to leave through the archway.)

Lloyd: Hey, wait. I know you're just a manifestation, but I need to know... are you happy?

(Tween Lloyd bows to Lloyd, walks up just before the archway, and turns back.)

Tween Lloyd: You tell me.

(Tween Lloyd walks through the archway, disappearing into nothing. Lloyd sighs.)

(The Grand Inquisitor and townsfolk have gathered for the end of the Inquisition.)

Grand Inquisitor: The Inquisition is back in session! (He scans the audience and clears his throat.) Well, if the accused can't even bother to show up, that seems like proof enough. I hereby pronounce them guil--

Sora: Not so fast! (Sora, Frak, Wyldfyre, and Riyu come through the crowd with a tied up Johnny Courageous.) Here's your culprit. He pulled this out of the Tree of Burdens. (She throws the fragment of the Weapon on the ground.)

(The Grand Inquisitor and the crowd gasp.)

Grand Inquisitor: Only a true villain would defile the sacred tree. I declare this miscreant guilty! (He bangs his staff on the ground.) These two are cleared of all suspicion! Lockdown lifted! Inquisition complete!

(Johnny joins the Grand Inquisitor onstage, taking his staff. A bugle sounds, and the crowd cheers. Sora breathes a sigh of relief. The Grand Inquisitor picks up the fragment of the Weapon.)

Grand Inquisitor: The only thing left to do now is deliver this to the Good Empress...

(The cheers from the crowd turn to confused murmuring.)

Sora: What?!

Grand Inquisitor: (He puts on a Loyalist mask and drops his Umbrasilvan accent.) Endless months of deep cover in this miserable backwater is finally over! You ignorant yokels, I'm part of the future, not the past! (The young Ninja run up to him.) All hail the Good Empress.

(Johnny flips open the end of the staff, pressing a red button that dispenses smoke everywhere. Sora coughs and performs Spinjitzu to clear out the smoke to find the two Loyalists gone.)

Frak: (gasps) Where did he--?

(An engine whirrs overhead. They look up to see the airship taking off.)

Wyldfyre: He's taking the airship!

Sora: If Empress Beatrix is really back, we're all in more danger than we ever imagined!

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
