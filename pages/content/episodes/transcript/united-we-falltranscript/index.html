(In the midst of The Merge, the Destiny's Bounty flies with Nya, Wu, Lloyd, and Zane on board. The Titan Mech is beside them.)

Past Nya: All those scrolls you had Lloyd running around with. Is this what you were researching?

Past Wu: (grunts) Not sure I can pull it off. It's arriving too soon. Too soon!

Past Nya: What are you saying? Raccoon? Baboon?

(A piece of debris hits the bounty and it begins to shake, Nya catches Wu.)

Past Wu: Only unity can save us. Separate, we have no chance.

(In the present day, Arin knocks on the door to the Wolf Clan's quarters in the City of Temples. Cinder answers.)

Cinder: Ugh, do you know what time it is? Trying to get my beauty sleep here. Boss, a bunch of jerks that apparently don't care about my beauty are here to see you!

(Cinder walks away as Ras takes his place to see Sora, Cole, Nya, Arin, Lloyd, Zane, and Riyu in the doorway.)

Nya: We've come for Jay!

Cole: Whatever you've done to him-

Ras: Me? The Merge left Jay Walker without his memory. Lost, alone, friendless. I found him, helped him when no one else did. Where were you?

Lloyd: He's one of us, Ras. Let him out!

Ras: Too late, he's gone. Jay was here for one purpose: to win matches.

(Jay is seen boarding an elevator that goes down.)

Ras: He failed that purpose, so I sent him away. If you truly value Jay, leave the games and go after him.

(Ras shuts the door and the ninja begin to leave.)

Nya: Come on! We need to...

(Riyu growls a remark.)

Nya: What? Maybe we can catch up to Jay before daylight!

Cole: Nya, Ras is playing us. He sent Jay away because he wants us to go after him.

Nya: Which we will, right? Jay needs us! Not only has he lost his memories, he used the Gong of Shattering! We have to find a way to heal his inner goodness! We have to save him!

Lloyd: We do, but this tournament is a threat to the Source Dragons, to the Merged Lands themselves.

Zane: If our world is destroyed, there will be no Jay to save.

Nya: Before everything changed. Master Wu told me, "Only unity can save us." Without Jay, we aren't united.

Lloyd: When the games are over, when the world is saved, we'll find Jay. I promise.

(The team begins to walk away. Arin seems down and Sora notices.)

Sora: What's wrong?

Arin: During their fight, Jay told Nya he worked at the Administration.

Sora: Wait, Jay is Agent Walker?

Arin: Yeah, the only person who might know what happened to my parents.

Sora: Great! So when this is all over, we'll go find him, then we can find your parents!

Arin: But when will this all be over?

(Arin and Sora stop for a moment while the rest of the team continues to walk.)

Arin: Lloyd promised we'd find my parents before, now he promised Nya we'd find Jay, but something always gets in the way.

(Nya is found training while Lloyd watches from a balcony. He is approached by Zane.)

Zane: Nya's training technique appears more aggressive than usual.

(Nya kicks a piece of wood and it goes flying, Lloyd catches it.)

Lloyd: You mean angrier? I can't blame her, she's working through stuff. She asked to be left alone.

Zane: But the games will resume shortly.

Lloyd: She'll be there when we need her. She always is.

(Nya's angry training continues.)

(Sora is using a tablet, to view her competitors in the tournament.)

Sora: Poison, Shade, and what does this guy have again? Elemental power of looking mean? Ugh! This is impossible! There's too many!

(Elsewhere in the room, Wyldfyre is also viewing a tablet and Cole is lifting weights next to a pie as Lloyd and Zane enter.)

Lloyd: Sora, studying your opponents is good prep, but you gotta find calm. Master Wu once said, "The garden of confidence grows in the soil of calm, not the rocks of panic."

Sora: My confidence isn't a garden, it isn't even a shrub! I'm pretty much all rocks of panic! What if I'm up against Cinder or Nokt? They can do Shatterspin, I can't even do Spinjitzu!

Zane: These games are often not about strength, but about out-maneuvering your opponent.

Lloyd: Zane's right, and Spinjitzu is within your reach, you've got the fundamentals down. Look, I know the games are getting more intense and it's affecting all of us. But remember, we're here for more than just this competition. The mystery of the Matriarch's death is somehow connected to the games, and Ras wants to win badly, so our job is to make sure he doesn't. Even if it means winning ourselves, we can do this.

Cole: And he doesn't think he's a good leader.

(Riyu wanders the city, searching for something.)

Kai: Riyu? Can you hear me?

(Riyu continues searching and squawks.)

Kai: Riyu, don't give up, we have to tell you something before the other three come back.

(While searching, Riyu bumps into a wall and sits sadly.)

(In the arena, fireworks are lit off as the Wolf Clan faces the Ninja team.)

Roby: Are you ready? I think you are! Yeah! When they talk about the Tournament of the Sources, they often say "legendary" and "mythic". But now they, they'll also say, "mega-tight" and "stang-zoot", a new word I just made up! Let the games continue!

(The crowds begin cheering.)

Roby: Get your umbrella hats on, cause it's about to rain elemental powers!

(A man in the stands puts on an umbrella hat).

Roby: First up today, it's the ninja with cat ears and and no fears. Sora, Elemental Master of Technology! (Sora is shown on the screens.)

(The crowd cheers and applauds.)

Ninja: All right, Sora./You can do this.

Roby: Versus the liquid warrior who will never bore ya'. From the Azure Reef, it's Kizzy, Elemental Master of Balance! (Kizzy is shown on the screens.)

Ninja: You can do this./You got this, Sora.

(Kizzy approaches, as the crowd cheers and screams.)

Sora: Okay, maybe I can do this.

Roby: These two masters will face the high-wire obstacle extravaganza.

(The obstacle course appears.)

Roby: The first to beat their opponent to the finish line and kick the kettle drum wins. Stang-zoot, am I right?

(Kizzy and Sora get ready , and Sora puts on her mask. Bleckt rings the bell.)

Roby: Go, go, go, go!

(Sora has trouble balancing on the tightrope, while Kizzy uses her Balance power to keep from falling and dodge all the spears and land on the next platform upside down.)

Sora: Whoa.

Arin: Sora's competing against the Elemental Master of Balance on a tightrope. How is this even fair?

Lloyd: Have some faith in her.

Nya: Let's hope she has faith in herself.

(Sora walks slowly along the tightrope to the next platform.)

Sora: If Kizzy can use her elemental power, so can I! (She uses her tech power to transform the obstacle into a balancing pole.)

Roby: What's this? Our Elemental Master of Tech is turning her obstacle into a tool! "notice!"

(Sora manages to get past the spears to the next platform, but almost falls off. Then she passes through the fire obstacles and almost reaches Kizzy, but the latter uses her balance power and aquatic abilities to get in her way.)

Roby: Whoa, Kizzy's showing the fighting spirit that makes the Reefling Water-Tides the Lords of Coral Corral!

(Sora uses her balance pole's battle mode to fend off Kizzy, who is nearly hit by the fire.)

Sora: (To Kizzy.) Sorry, can't hang. Got a drum to kick

(She sees that there are several fire obstacles between her and the drum, and Kizzy starts to approach.)

Sora: Just like Rontu taught me. Jump, spin-kick, and through!

(She manages to get past all the obstacles by doing the sequence of movements with Kizzy right behind, but she manages to kick the drum first.)

Roby: We have a winner.

(The crowd and the other Ninja celebrate, while the Wolf Clan are frustrated by Sora's victory. She and Kizzy descend onto a platform, and Sora takes off her mask.)

Sora: No hard feelings, Kiz?

(Kizzy bows to Sora to show that she accepts her defeat.)

Roby: Master Kizzy, transfer time.

(Roby extracts Kizzy's powers into the cup and gives them to Sora.)

Roby: Sora, you are now the Elemental Master of Technology and Balance.

(Sora uses her newfound power of balance to do a one-handed handstand and not fall.)

Roby: Up next, she's smoking hot and likes to fight a lot. It's Wyldfyre, Elemental Master of Heat!

Wyldfyre: Oh yeah. (She uses her elemental power in her right hand.)

Roby: Versus the cloudy man with the smoky hands! Cinder, Elemental Master of, poof, Smoke.

Wyldfyre: Yes, finally! Payback time!

Roby: Whoo, a classic. Capture the flag! Fist to get it out of circle wins!

(Cinder and Wyldfyre look at each other.)

Lloyd: Wyldfyre, I know you're itching to take revenge for Kai, but we got bigger things to focus on. Just go for the flag and win the game.

Wyldfyre: Don't worry, coach. I'll cream him.

(She and Cinder brace themselves and the bell rings. They start running and Wyldfyre traps Cinder with her Elemental Heat.)

Lloyd: Wyldfyre, just grab the flag before it's too late and they use the...

(Ras uses the gong and Mallet of Shattering.)

Wyldfyre: Oh, no.

Cinder: Oh, yeah.

(He puts on the mask and it transforms into armor, and he then uses Shatterspin to put out the heat and uses a wave of Shatterpin energy to throw Wyldfyre away and she falls. Cinder uses his elemental power of Smoke combined with his Shatterspin to throw Wyldfyre several times. Then he gives an evil smile and turns into smoke again. The crowd gasps.)

Sora: C'mon on, Wyldfyre. Get up!

(Wyldfyre gets up, but Cinder hits her with Shatterspin once more, and the other Ninja go to help her. Cinder then picks up the flag and throws it on the ground.)

Roby: We have a winner. Whoop!

(Ras makes a satisfied smile.)

Wyldfyre: (She gasps the other Ninja helps her up.) What happened?

Roby: (To Wyldfyre.) Are you okay?

Wyldfyre: I'm totally stang-zoot.

Roby: That curtain of fire was amazing, BT-dub. Sorry, but I have to transfer your powers now.

(Roby extracts Wyldfyre's powers into the cup, then makes a sad face. Wyldfyre does the same. Then Roby transfers Wyldfyre's powers to Cinder. The screen shows Cinder now having the powers of Smoke and Heat. The Ninja stand down and Cinder uses his new Heat power.)

(Later that night, the Ninja are walking down Foundation Street.)

Arin: (He scoffs.) They shouldn't even be allowed to use the Gong of Shattering. It's not fair.

Zane: I thought the same thing after Jay used it, but in combing through the rules, I found there is technically no prohibition on the use of such items, which Ras must have known.

Wyldfyre: (She sniffs.) Do you guys smell that?

(Arin also sniffs.)

Arin: (To Zane.) It's not me, right? I never known when it's me.

(Smoke appears at their feet and then reveals itself as Cinder.)

Cinder: The loser with her loser friends.

Nya: What do you want, Cinder?

Cinder: (To Wyldfyre.) Hey, if it helps, you never stood a chance in there, kid. Your mentor was Kai. Get taught by a loser, all you learn is how is lose, right?

(Wyldfyre screams and tries to use her powers, but she can't.)

Cinder: (He laughs.) You seriously just tried to blast your power? You forgot already? (He turns on the Heat in his left hand.) Bet I already know how to use this power better than you.

(He fires a fireball upwards and it explodes, drawing the attention of the Security Bots, who soon spot them.)

Wyldfyre: Dragons don't need fire to fight.

(She growls and tries to punch Cinder, who pretends to be hit and falls down.)

Wyldfyre: What the...?

(Cinder yelps and groanings.)

(Roby meets with Bleckt, then they look at the screen and see Cinder and the Ninja.)

Cinder: (On screen.) Please don't hurt me. I just wanted to say congratulations.

(Roby and Bleckt climb onto a floating platform and fly away. Roby and Bleckt climb onto a floating platform and fly away. Two Security Robots approach Cinder.)

Cinder: Please! Please! Mercy!

Zane: Such behavior defies logic.

(Roby and Bleckt arrive.)

Bleckt: Break up those rabble-rousers. (More Security Robots arrive.) Ninja, ganging up on the victor that defeated your teammate. We haven't seen such unsportsmanlike behavior in generations.

Lloyd: No, you've got it all wrong.

(Jordana and Ras approach and watch, as Ras grunts with glee.)

Nya: Lloyd, it's a set-up!

Bleck: Don't foist your excuses off on us. We saw the whole thing on-screen!

Roby: Bullying isn't tolerated. It's disqualification and immediate exile.

Wyldfyre: But we didn't, that's not--

Bleckt: Security Bots, escort the Ninja beyond the city gates!

(The Security Robots approach the Ninja, but Zane gets in front of them and stops them.)

Zane: Halt ! I invoke article 764!

(The other Ninja are confused.)

Bleckt: The Cavern Run? No one's done that in eons.

Roby: I totally know the rule book inside and out but for those who might not have devoted the hours of study, I definitely have, please fill us in?

Zane: It is a trial of worthiness. Compete in a dangerous, high-speed vehicle race through deadly caverns, beat the clock, and get immunity from exile.

Roby: A desperate race against the odds? Pure drama! Stang-zoot, let's do it!

Bleckt: But it's. But, uh...

Roby: Come on, Uncle Bleckt, you love old-school traditional rules. This is your jam!

Bleckt: (He sighs.) I cannot deny the legitimacy of article 764.

Roby: Yes! We race tonight. I knew my games would be the sweetest ever.

(Cinder leaves along with Ras and Jordana. Roby, Bleckt, and the Security Robots also leave.)

Nya: Sounds like we need a vehicle. One problem, we don't have a vehicle.

Arin: I'd let you use mine, but it got trashed on our way up here. Bleckt told me they dumped it in their junkyard. (He sighs.) Thanks, Bleckt.

Sora: There's a junkyard up here? That might be exactly what's needed for someone with Elemental Technology powers. (Her hands and eyes glow as she activates her Elemental Technology.)

(Riyu is still looking for something.)

Kai: Riyu, we're putting all our hope in you. Please!

(Riyu approaches the Wolf Clan quarters and sees Ras's shadow behind the door. Riyu then hides behind a fence, and sees Ras leaving with Cinder, Jordana, and Nokt.)

(In the caverns of the City of Temples. the Wolf Clan joins the already waiting Ninja and spectators.)

Cinder: Still don't have any wheels? You gonna beat the cavern run on foot?

(In the distance, an engine revs. The others look on as Lloyd, Nya, Cole, and Sora pull up in a newly assembled vehicle. The crowd cheers. Lloyd opens the hood of the vehicle.)

Lloyd: Woah! (Spectators rush up to greet them.)

Wyldfyre: What were you saying, Cinder?

(Arin hops up on the vehicle.)

Lloyd: Arin, all eyes will be on the race, including theirs. (They look towards the Wolf Clan.)

Arin: So, it's a good time for a certain detective to do some detectiving. I know that's not a word, but it sounded right in the moment.

Lloyd: Detective-away.

(Arin nods and runs off. Roby hovers above them.)

Roby: Racers, to your vehicle!

(The four ninja buckle up and drive to the starting line.)

Roby: The ninja will be racing the clock, right? Well, meet the clock! (He gestures upwards, and a small dragon flies into his hand.) The Pace Dragon! That racing stripe? My addition. The Pace Dragon will fly the course at the exact rate needed to win the race. If the ninja beat the Pace Dragon, victory! Zero-percent exile! (He points his phone in Lloyd's face.) High stakes, Lloyd! Win this, the ninja are still in the games. Lose, you're out. Do you have the leadership skills to get your team across the finish line in time?

Lloyd: Guess we'll find out.

Roby: Yes, yes, we will! Pure drama! Stang-zoot! (He flies upwards, holding the starting gun in the air.) Three, two, one. (He fires.) Go, go, go!

(The four ninja take off, as the crowd cheers. Arin looks to the Wolf Clan to make sure they're watching, before pulling up his mask and leaving. Within the cavern, the Ninja pass the Pace Dragon.)

Cole: Bye-bye, buddy! See you at the finish line.

(Arin approaches the Wolf Clan quarters. He pulls on the front door to no avail, before sneaking around the side. There he finds Riyu, trying to get in through a window.)

Arin: Riyu? (Riyu grumbles.) I was wondering where you were. So you're investigating too?

(Riyu chitters. Arin open the window and climbs in, closing it behind him, before he lets Riyu in through the front door.)

Arin: Okay, I'm searching for anything that connects Ras to what happened with the Matriarch or the attacker bots.

Kai: Can you hear me?

(Riyu gives a startled grumble, while Arin hears nothing.)

Arin: What is it? You hear something?

Kai: It's Riyu!

(Riyu grumbles again.)

Arin: I don't hear anything, buddy.

Kai: Are you there, boy?

(Riyu follows Kai's voice.)

Arin: Uh, okay, I guess you're working on your own lead.

(As Arin begins to search, he notices a photo of Frak and his parents.)

Arin: Sora was right. Frak is working for Ras. (gasps) Doesn't he know the guy is pure evil? Is Frak evil now too?

(Riyu sniffs around. He's led to a pillow, which he moves to find a strange stone. The stone suddenly glows when...)

Kai: Riyu. I hear you, buddy! Ah, it's like you're right next to me all of the sudden.

(Riyu whines at the stone, getting Arin's attention.)

Arin: Riyu, what's going on? Are you talking to a stone? 'Cause, no judgement, but that's kind of weird.

(Riyu chitters at the stone.)

Kai: What? Is someone with you? I can only hear you.

Arin: (Grabbing the stone) I don't get it. It's just a stone. I've never seen one glow like that before though. What's it made of?

(Suddenly, footsteps are heard and a shadow appears outside the front door. The door slides open to reveal Ras.)

(The ninja are racing through the cavern at a steady pace.)

Sora: You know, they had me worried, but this race is a breeze. We are so far ahead of that Pace Dragon. (She looks at the radar on her control panel.) And according to this, we're heading into the Green Zone. Huh, sounds pleasant.

(The ninja drive into a cloud of green mist, and enter into a very open section of the cavern with large stone spires with large vents coming out of them. one of the vents lets out a burst of green, gooey liquid that hits the ground ahead of the ninja, causing a small drop of it to hit their vehicle.)

Sora: Huh? (The drop is revealed to have seared a hole in the outer plating of the vehicle.) Wait, toxic goo? Not pleasant. Way not pleasant.

(The ninja's vehicle dodges another burst of goo.)

(Ras enters the Wolf Clan's quarters and closes the door behind him. He begins to approach Arin.)

Ras: Arin and his pet dragon. Just like I suspected. Did your master, Lloyd, set you up for this? Sending in one of the youngest ninja, and the only one without an Elemental Power, into enemy territory seems like Lloyd's brand of leadership.

Arin: I didn't need powers to take you out at the Ritual of the Blood Moon.

Ras: (He scoffs.) She really never told you. You didn't hit me. Your little friend used her tech abilities to power the object that struck me.

Arin: No, I did it! Sora would've told me if I didn't.

Kai: Riyu, what's going on? Is... Is something happening? I can't hear you. (Riyu grabs the stone from Arin's hand and hides it in his mouth.)

Ras: Sora is hiding the truth from you. Yet another lie from your so-called teammates. Covering up for their inability to train you, perhaps?

Arin: I get all the training I need.

Ras: Arin, you know that's not true. The cycle of mastery eludes you. You obviously have potential. If Lloyd Garmadon is incapable of unleashing that potential... (He sighs.) ...perhaps you need another master. One who sees you more clearly.

Arin: Who? You? I'd never join you.

Ras: Your friend Frak doesn't have an issue with being on my side.

Arin: He must not know how evil you are!

Ras: Is it evil to want to reverse the Merge?

Arin: Wait, you wanna what? (Riyu growls in shock.)

(The ninja are speeding through the cavern, dodging the bursts of acidic goo.)

Sora: Hold tight.

(They jump across a gap in the track with their vehicle. Nya uses the power of Water to intercept and dissipate an incoming burst of goo, and Cole uses Earth to create a wall, blocking more goo. They continue further, and more goo shoots up into the air.)

Lloyd: Sora, up front!

(Sora briefly glances behind her, and sees the small Pace Dragon passing the ninja.)

Sora: Oh, no!

(Ras continues speaking to Arin.)

Ras: You speak of evil. The Merge, that was truly an act of evil. And since the Merge was what took your parents away from you, I'm surprised you'd ever side with the students of the very person that caused it.

Arin: What?

Ras: You don't know? Your ninja's beloved mentor, Master Wu. If it wasn't for him, your parents never would have been lost.

Arin: What are you talking about?

Ras: The Merge was caused by Master Wu.

(Arin is shocked at this revelation.)
