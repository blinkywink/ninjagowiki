(In the First Land, the storm with the Winds of Chaos tornado worsens and the Ninja remain trapped inside the tornado and Zane in the Ultra Combiner Mech tries unsuccessfully to catch Kai and Nya.)

Zane: These unnatural winds defy all logic!

(Lloyd, Arin, and Frak, who is holding a boulder, hold hands until they are hit by another boulder. Riyu flies against the Winds of Chaos to rescue them, but is nearly hit by a boulder until it is destroyed by a fireball blast from Wyldfyre's mech.)

Wyldfyre: (To the Ninja.) I can't leave you all alone for a minute!

(Riyu is happy her and she catches Lloyd using the mech's right hand, and Frak with the mech's left thruster. Arin is still being blown away by wind.)

Arin: Whoa!

(Wyldfyre rescues him with the mech's chest.)

Arin: Wyldfyre! I have never been more excited to see you.

Wyldfyre: Arin? Why are you here? I thought you peaced out.

Arin: I'm trying not to let the world end.

Wyldfyre: Well, I'm here now, so it won't.

(Zane manages to catch Nya and Kai.)

Nya and Kai: Oof!

(Zane tries to escape the tornado with his friends, until Wyldfyre and the other Ninjas arrive and use the Wyldfyre mech's thruster to escape, followed by Riyu. When everyone manages to get out, Riyu ends up being hit by a rock that came out of the tornado, falling towards sharp rocks.)

Sora: Riyu!

(She uses her Tech Power to transform the mech's right arm into a blaster, which fires at the sharp rocks and destroys them. Riyu falls to the ground and passes out, so Arin and Sora go to check on him.)

Arin: (To Riyu.) You okay, boy?

(Riyu groans in pain, as Kai, Nya, Lloyd, and Frak disembark from the mechs, and Kai approaches Wyldfyre and her mech.)

Kai: (To Wyldfyre.) That thing is way cool! Can I try?

Wyldfyre: It won't look as cool on you.

Kai: Hurtful.

Lloyd: We have more pressing problems, you two. Anyone see where the Forbidden Five went?

(Arin, Riyu, and Sora approach them.)

Arin: I... I had the last two blades. I know I should have just flown off with them, but I tried to save Ras, then Rox attacked me.

Frak: Hold on. If the Five have all the blades, doesn't that mean they can free--

(The ground begins to shake, a Chaos energy wave appears, and Thunderfang's right arm comes out from under the ground.)

Ninja: Thunderfang!

(Thunderfang comes out from under the ground roaring upwards, his wings charged with Chaos.)

(Thunderfang continues to let out a deafening roar as the Ninja run away from him while rocks and the Returning Blade fall down.)

Sora: I knew he was big.

Kai: Yeah, but that is like big-big.

(Dragonian Warriors appear flying behind Thunderfang.)

(At the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Mr. Frohicky is sweeping the courtyard floor until he notices the storm and goes upstairs to get a better look.)

Mr. Frohicky: Be safe, Ninja.

(In the Land of Lost Things, Cole is looking at a rock that has some kind of writing carved into it. Geo is with him.)

Geo: Cole, I think the MergeQuake are back.

(Cole looks up at the sky and notices the storm.)

Cole: No, this is something different. We better gather up the Finders.

(Geo nods.)

(In the Land of Madness, outside the Administration, Agents Allen and Underwood are walking into the building until a wind from the storm sends the documents inside Allen's box out.)

Agent Allen: Uh! My documents! This weather is unauthorized!

(Thunderfang looks up and shouts to the sky.)

Thunderfang: Source Dragons! You will rue the day you rejected me!

Nya: Where'd the Forbidden Five go?

Kai: Maybe Thunderfang ate 'em. Would explain why he's such a foul mood. You know, 'cause they're probably hard to digest? Make you sick, and--

Nya: I got it.

Kai: You didn't laugh.

Nya: I reacted correctly.

(Thunderfang roars deafeningly again.)

Lloyd: (To the Ninja.) I have no idea how we beat this thing, except there's one of him and ten of us, so let's come at him from all sides!

Wyldfyre: Works for me! (She drives her mech.)

Lloyd: Ninja... go!

(Lloyd, Kai, and Nya use Dragon Spinjitzu while Arin uses his Spinjitzu. Sora climbs into the Ultra Combiner Mech. Wyldfyre fires a fireball at Thunderfang's right leg, which apparently has no effect. While the first four also use their Spinjitzu at Thunderfang's right leg, causing him pain, he then uses his to knock them down.)

Thunderfang: (To the Ninja.) Last time, it took an Arc Dragon of Focus to defeat me! You puny mortals have no chance!

(He tries to crush Arin, Nya, Lloyd, and Kai with his right foot, but they escape. A rock almost hits Zane, but he is saved by Riyu.)

Zane: (To Riyu.) Much appreciated.

(Frak is running, but is hit by a rock. He then uses his Quake to make a rock crumble and shield him from the wind. Wyldfyre is in her mech, being chased by Dragonians.)

Wyldfyre: You ugly jerks are a insult to dragons everywhere!

(Kai is trapped under a rock and Nya tries to help him out, but Thunderfang prepares to crush them with his left foot but Sora hits him in the face with the mech.)

Sora: H'ah! (To Thunderfang.) Like that? Have some more!

(She shoots Thunderfang with the mech's blaster and he roars in pain. Frak is being defeated by the Dragonians and almost ends up being crushed by Thunderfang, but he is saved by Arin using Spinjitzu at the last moment.)

Frak: (To Arin.) Thanks, man.

Arin: Couldn't let you get hurt on our first Ninja adventure together!

(They prepare to fight the Dragonians, until Arin notices Ras trapped under rocks.)

Arin: There you are. (He runs towards Ras, but sees Rogue coming up to his left in his armor.) Ras! Look out!

(He uses Spinjitzu to jump to Rogue and tries to knock him down.)

Arin: (To Rogue.) Leave him alone!

(Thunderfang's tail hits them and knocks them back. Arin is met by Dragonkin Warriors and retreats until he touches the Blade of Return. The Dragonkin advance, and Arin grabs the Blade and begins to fight them. Sora continues firing at Thunderfang, who blocks her shots with his left wing. He then roars, grabs the mech in his mouth, and tries to crush it, and Riyu begins to fall. Sora's cockpit begins to spark with tech-elemental energy.)

Wyldfyre: Can't you do anything without my help? Hi'yah! (She jumps off the motorcycle and punches Thunderfang using her mech's fire, releasing the Ultra combiner mech.) Whoo! That was satisfying.

(The Ultra Combiner Mech continues to fall, so Sora uses her Tech Power to separate the mech back into her jet, P.I.X.A.L.'s car, and Zane. Thunderfang prepares to destroy them, but Kai, Lloyd, and Nya use Dragon Spinjitzu to use their combined Rising Dragon and hit Thunderfang in the belly, weakening him and causing him to roar and release more Chaos energy.)

Thunderfang: I smell the power of a Source Dragon Conduit! (He searches and then stares at Lloyd.) A direct line to the Source Dragons themselves? (To Lloyd.) You! Draining a Conduit's power will be transcendent!

(Kai and Lloyd use Dragon Spinjitzu towards Thunderfang, who tries to hit Lloyd with lightning and then crush him with his right arm, but misses. Lloyd then shoots Energy at Thunderfang, but he simply eats it.)

Thunderfang: Who's energy do you channel? Motion? That incessant busybody? Or the self-obsessed Strength? (He drags his left hand along the ground in an attempt to hit Lloyd, but he jumps and dodges.) Or is it Life? (He tries to crush Lloyd with his right arm, but he manages to dodge again.) How ironic would that be?

(Thunderfang manages to hit Lloyd with a lightning bolt, who ends up flying in a spin and crashing into one of the mountain walls. Thunderfang approaches him, his eyes turn green, and begins to absorb his soul, but P.I.X.A.L., in her car, shoots Thunderfang in the face, causing him pain and preventing him from devouring Lloyd's soul. Then Sora also shoots with her jet, and Zane jumps out of the jet using Spinjitzu and delivers an ice punch to him. An unconscious Lloyd begins to fall, but is saved by Riyu.)

(Roby is watching the storm on a ladder, while a Security Bot approaches him.)

Security Bot: (To Roby.) You need to shelter from this natural disaster, sir.

Roby: There's nothing natural about this.

(Fritz, in his animal form, is trying to fly against the wind, but ends up being defeated. Cole takes his wing and he returns to normal.)

Cole: I've got you, Fritz!

(He uses his Earth Power to make the rock they were on descend and reunite with Geo and Spitz. They all start running.)

(Rogue, who is out of his armor, is almost attacked by a Dragonian, but the latter is hit by Nya's Water Power. He looks to the side and sees Nya fighting Dragonians.)

Nya: H'yah! (She takes one of their swords and uses it against them, then uses Dragon Spinjitzu on them.) (To Rogue.) On your feet if you want to survive!

(Rogue joins the fight and saves Nya from a Dragonian with a trip. He then kicks another Dragonian and takes his sword, using it against other Dragonians alongside Nya.)

Nya: (To Rogue.) Not bad for some rando mercenary.

(They continue to fight them while Wyldfyre dodges Thuderfang's attacks using the mech.)

Wyldfyre: (To Thunderfang.) Aren't you supposed to be unstoppable?

(Thunderfang gets angry and fires a beam at her mech's right thruster, causing her to fall.)

Wyldfyre: Aah! I knew I should never fly not on a dragon!

(Kai, who is fighting a Dragonian, notices the Wyldfyre's mech falling.)

Wyldfyre: Aah!

Kai: Wyldfyre!

(The mech finally falls and the impact makes it fall of the motorcycle. Kai runs to check on Wyldfyre, who exits the mech.)

Kai: You hurt?

Wyldfyre: No way, I don't get hurt. (She grunts in pain.) Hey, I gave it some thought. You would look really cool in that mech.

Kai: Seriously? You're thinking about that now?

Wyldfyre: (She pushes him into the mech.) Really cool!

(Kai activates the mech and gets excited.)

Kai: You're right, this is pretty cool.

(He puts on his mask, and, gets on the motorcycle with the mech.)

Kai: H'yah!

(He flies away. Meanwhile, Arin continues fighting the Dragonians, and manages to defeat two but is knocked down by a third. Two of them try to attack him, but the ground begins to shake and they are crushed by a rock. It is then revealed that Frak was the one who used his powers to knock down the rock and then helps Arin up.)

Frak: (To Arin.) Couldn't let you get hurt on our first Ninja adventure together.

(They bump fists and prepare to fight.)

Zane: (To P.I.X.A.L.) High velocity contact at the anterior may yield results.

P.I.X.A.L.: Acknowledged!

(They approach Thunderfang, and Zane shoots ice at his face, who tries to attack him, but he dodges, and then shoots ice at his face again.)

Thunderfang: Pests!

(He hits Zane and knocks him to the ground. P.I.X.A.L. drives her car into Thunderfang, who throws her and the car away. Sora also tries to attack with her jet, but Thunderfang quickly shoots a lightning bolt at her and makes the jet crash.)

Thunderfang: Conduit!

(He starts walking towards Lloyd, but is distracted by Kai's fire on the Wyldfyre mech, which manages to hit him and cause some pain. Thunderfang grabs the mech by the motorcycle's left thruster with his mouth.)

Kai: Oh, Come on! This is a loaner! And you just scratched it all up!

(He throws a fiery punch at Thundefang's right eyes, which roars in pain and throws Kai away. He ends up falling to the ground and exiting the mech. Nya, who continues fighting, uses Dragon Spinjitzu on a Dragonian, while another, who is fighting Rogue, manages to push him away. As two other Dragonian appear, Rogue punches the ground and his hands begin to glow, while his suit gains lightning details and his hands begin to shoot Lightning. Nya, who is facing another Dragonian, looks to the side and then realizes who Rogue really is: Jay)

Nya: No way...

(Jay uses his Lightning on the Dragonians, knocking all three of them down.)

Rogue: Thought I'd... lightning up the mood! (He makes a familiar pose.)

Nya: And terrible puns?

(Lloyd, who is standing on Riyu, fires Energy at two Dragonians, while Thunderfang watches them. Other Dragonians grab Riyu and Thunderfang appears behind them.)

Thunderfang: (To Lloyd.) Conduit!

(He begins absorbing Lloyd's soul again, as Sora watches in horror.)

Kai: Lloyd! Lloyd!

(Zane gasps. Nya and Jay take down more Dragonians, then they notice Thunderfang absorbing Lloyd's soul. Arin and Frak take down more Dragonians and they also notice Thunderfang absorbing Lloyd's soul.)

Arin: No!

(He uses Spinjitzu to try to stop Thunderfang, but the latter laughs evilly and pushes Arin away. Thunderfang finishes absorbing Lloyd's soul and then roars upwards, releasing more Chaos with his now green eyes and mouth as Lloyd's body begins to fall.)

Arin: No! (He does Spinjitzu, jumps forward, lands on the ground and manages to catch Lloyd's body.) Lloyd! Lloyd! Come on! No!

(He looks up and sees the storm getting worse. Thunderfang fires a bolt at Kai, who was inside the mech.)

Thunderfang: Source Dragons! I have reaped the soul of one of your mortal Conduits! You can deny me no longer! I know you hear me, and I will not stop until I devour all of your souls as well!

Arin: Souls?

(Arin looks through the Returning Blade and sees several souls around Thunderfang, including Lloyd's, then realizes something. He remembers meeting Morro in the Spectral Lands and the conversation they had beside the Well of the Lost.)

Past Arin: (To Morro.) What is this stuff?

Past Morro: Don't ask me. Drinking it is what helps souls move on.

(Back in the present, Arin sees that his bottle is still half full with the elixir.)

Arin: "Helps souls move on..."

(He puts the bottle in his pocket, puts on his mask, and moves using Spinjitzu. Sora notices him and also takes off her mask. Frak is fighting the Dragonians, and notices Arin, but is knocked down by one. Arin jumps using Spinjitzu while Frak is facing the Dragonian who knocked him down. Arin arrives on top of a mountain and stops his Spinjitzu, then takes off his mask and takes the bottle from his pocket.)

Ras: (To Arin.) Don't be a fool. A moving target through the Winds of Chaos? You don't have enough anger for that kind of strength.

(Arin looks thoughtful, then remembers the conversation he had with Sora at the monastery.)

Past Sora: The Arin I know, my best friend, his strength comes from his kindness, his generosity, his hope.

(Back to the present.)

Arin: (To Ras.) You're right. I don't have enough anger to pull this off...

(He uses memories of playing board games with his parents in his old house, Sora, young Frak at the Crossroads Junkyard, Riyu, Lloyd, himself with the other Ninja with Mr. Frohicky at the Monastery are shown, then back to the present.)

Arin: (He gives a smile.) I have something better. (He makes a determined face and starts running forward.) Ninja... (He does Spinjitzu, jumps and throws the bottle.) Go!

(The bottle receives Object Spinjitzu and flies towards Thunderfang, hitting him square in the face. The elixir begins to make him calcify.)

Thundefang: What?!

(Arin looks through the Returning Blade and sees the souls of several Oni leaving Thundefang. Nya and the Dragonians stop the fight to watch, while Riyu and other Dragonians do the same. Zane helps P.I.X.A.L. out of her car.)

Kai: That's weird, right?

Sora: Arin threw something at him.

(Thunderfang's body finishes calcifying and he falls to the ground.)

Thunderfang: Impossible...

(Arin continues looking through his blade and sees the souls escaping from Thunderfang's body. He pulls out the blade and can see the souls even without looking through it. Ras joins him and they watch the souls continue to leave, but five of them are devoured by Thunderfang, who soon explodes, allowing the souls of the Oni to move on and reverting the Dragonians, including Tyr, back to their normal state and Lloyd's soul returns to his body. He wakes up breathing heavily.)

Sora: Lloyd!

(All the Ninja go to help Lloyd as the storm dissipates.)

Lloyd: I've had some really weird days, but this might be the weirdest one yet.

(They all laugh and hug, and Arin runs to join them, while Ras watches and grunts angrily.)

(It is shown that the storm had also ended in the Merged Lands.)

Roby: Thank you, Wyldfyre, and I'm sure some of the other Ninja helped.

(The Dragonians approach the Ninja.)

Tyr: (To the Ninja.) We are so sorry for our actions. (He and two other Dragonians bow down to them, asking for forgiveness.)

Lloyd: We know you weren't yourselves, Tyr.

Tyr: We owe you Ninja our freedom.

Lloyd: It was really Arin who freed you.

(Frak and Sora step aside to reveal Arin.)

Tyr: Then you are a great hero, Arin. (He and the two Dragonians bow to him.)

Arin: Oh! Well, not really. I was the reason the Five freed Thunderfang to begin with, and fighting him was a group effort, and he--

Lloyd: Is a great hero.

Ras: Arin... Time to go.

(The Ninja glare at Ras and Riyu grunts at him angrily.)

Arin: (To the Ninja.) I'm sorry, everyone. My parents... (He starts approaching Ras, followed by Frak.)

Kai: What? No...

Frak: Arin, you know better.

(Arin ignores him.)

Sora: Lloyd? I'm sorry too. (She runs towards Arin and Ras.)

Nya: What the heck is going on here?

Sora: Arin, wait!

Arin: You're coming with us?

Sora: Together forever, right?

(Arin is happy that Sora wants to go with him.)

Ras: And why should I allow you to travel in my company?

Arin: You don't think having the Elemental Master of Technology will help us on our mission?

(Ras reluctantly agrees.)

Arin: (To Sora.) You don't have to do this.

Sora: Of course I do. Who else is gonna watch out for you?

(They leave with Ras.)

Kai: (To Lloyd.) Are you seriously going to let Ras leave with them?

Lloyd: They're free to make their own decisions. That's the difference between us and Ras.

Frak: And you're not worried about them?

Lloyd: I didn't say that.

(They watch as Arin, Sora, and Ras continue to walk away, and Riyu begins to groan at his two best friends leaving. Nya then looks back.)

Nya: There!

(She runs to help Jay, who during the storm ended up with rocks on top of him. She takes the rocks off him and helps him up. He ends up falling, because his leg is seriously injured.)

Rogue: Ah!

Nya: You need a splint on that leg. Let's get you back to the Monastery.

Rogue: Why are you gonna help me?

Nya: I know who you are, you big dummy.

Rogue: Uh, yeah, a secret man of mystery whose identity will never--

Nya: Elemental Lightning? Really?

Rogue: So? Lots of people probably have Elemental Lightning.

Nya: Nope.

(Jay grunts and takes off his hat, glasses, and mask, revealing a ponytail on top of his head.)

Ninja: Jay?

Jay: (He raises his hand.) Don't pretend you know me! (He gets up from the floor.) And I'm not going to your stupid Monastery! Normal people don't just have Monasteries! It's weird! And another thing-- (He stumbles on his injured leg and whimpers.) Ow! Owie-ow-ow-ow-ow! (He sobs.) Get me to your Monastery! Can't you see I need help?

(At the dragon stables, the Ninja and Jay arrive from the portal gate and Mr. Frohicky runs to greet them.)

Mr Frohicky: Congratulations, Ninja! You did it!

(Lloyd and Frak are depressed, while the others go downstairs and Nya helps Jay down the stairs.)

Lloyd: I guess, but this win doesn't exactly feel like a win

Mr. Frohicky: Well, our guests would disagree.

(The Ninja look confused.)

Lloyd: Guests?

(At the Monastery of Spinjitzu, the Ninja's friends are throwing a party to celebrate their victory.)

Lobbo: Lobbo-lobbo crushes pie-eating record!

(He eats a lot of pies and Kreel brings him more to eat.)

Crowd: Lobbo! Lobbo! Lobbo!

(The Ninja arrive and join the party.)

Roby: Wyldfyre! (He runs towards her.) I knew you'd save us! Rawr...

Wyldfyre: Rawr!

(Cole arrives holding a plate with a slice of pizza and hugs Kai.)

Cole: (To Kai.) Mr. Frohicky makes a great pizza! Without flies, even! (He notices Jay approaching.) Whoa. Jay?

Jay: (He winces in pain.) Ah!

Cole: I can't believe it! Bring it in! (He starts to approach Jay and tries to hug him, but he pulls away.)

Jay: Get away, weirdo.

Cole: (He gets sad.) Still no memory?

Jay: I got plenty of memories, bub. Just none of them are of you.

Cole: Wow. Okay... And still no Arin?

Lloyd: He and Sora went with Ras.

(Jay opens a soda.)

Lloyd: Arin think Ras can bring his parents back from the dead.

Jay: Dead? (He scoffs.) I know for a fact that Arin kid's parents are alive.

Lloyd: What?

Jay: (He drinks his soda.) Hello? I worked for the Administration. Head of the Department of Realm Reassigment? What do you think I did there? I know exactly where's Arin's parents were reassigned, and it's really, really weird.

(The Ninja are surprised to hear this.)

(Wind is shaking the bucket in the Well of the Lost. At the Spectral Lands, Morro stops the bucket and a soul appears.)

Morro: Dearly Departed, this may come as a shock, but you have transitioned out of the living world. I vow to protect you as you moved into...

(The departed soul expands as Thunderfang appears as a soul form of himself.)

Morro: Okay, wow. You're a big one.

(Suddenly, Soul Suckers start to screech. Four of them appear, ready to attack.)

Morro: Stay Back. I'll ward them off.

(Morro gets ready to defend, while the four Soul Suckers run towards them.)

Thunderfang: I can handle myself.

(Thunderfang transforms the Soul Suckers into departed souls and eats them.)

Morro: What? That's impossible.

Thunderfang: In the afterlife, anything is possible.

(Thunderfang then eats Morro as he is screaming, transforming him too. He laughs during the credits.)

(End of episode. For more information, click here.)
