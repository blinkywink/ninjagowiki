(As the original Ninja and Zilvar arrive at the Abyss of Eternal Night. Jay looks at Zilvar and hears metal clanging, which triggers a memory from his past. In the memory, Ray is forging a sword when Edna arrives holding young Jay, who is clutching Mister Cuddlywomp.)

Past Edna: Morning, blacksmith. I'm hoping you can help me identify an object.

Past Ray: I'm happy to try. Call me Ray.

Past Edna: Edna, from the scrap and junk out by the Sea of Sand. Well, my husband, Ed found this in the scrap. Or maybe in the junk. It's hard to tell. But it's so rusted. We weren't sure what it was. But we hoped a former elemental master might know. Hmm. Oh, careful. I have my suspicions about this thing, and none of them are good.

(Ray unravels the cloth Edna brought, revealing a peculiar medallion. Jay tries to examine the weapons in the shop, but accidentally causes them to fall. He sets them back up and picks up Mister Cuddlywomp.)

Past Ray: Huh? It's heavier than it looks. (Young Kai and Nya are playing outside the shop.) Yeah, it's a primordial artifact. all right. Hmm. I need to show it to a friend of mine. Uh he'll know what it is. Can you wait a bit?

(Back in the present Jay gets excited about recovering the memory.)

Jay: Nya, I just got another memory back. But this is when I totally forgot even before The Merge. I think we met as little kids.

Nya: I'm pretty sure I'd remember that. Also, not exactly the time to walk down memory lane, Jay.

Kai: I can see why this place isn't in the top ten tourist spots.

Cole: Let's just get this over with.

Nya: It's like a heartbeat.

(Zilvar walks forward, ready to drop the Weapon of Desolation as it starts glowing red.)

(Behind the Old Town Courthouse, Arin checks on Riyu.)

Arin: you okay, buddy? Nothing broken?

(Riyu groans.)

Euphrasia: Sora, can you hear me? Sora!

Frak: Maybe she just shut off her comms so they can't hear her stowed away.

Arin: This is all my fault.

Wyldfyre: (gasps) Are you secretly Cryptor?!

Arin: What? No! Sora and I were fighting over my stupid accusation about her parents. If we were working together like usual, she wouldn't have gone into danger alone.

Wyldfyre: You say danger like it's a bad thing.

(Riyu notices something.)

Euphrasia: (gasps) Sora's data pad! She must have dropped it in the fight.

Arin: So now we can't even track her with it. This keeps getting worse.

Frak: (pauses to think) Wait, we don't know where Cryptor is, but we know where he isn't. He invaded Ninjago City before. People all know who he is. He'd be recognized on the street. If he's on the move, it's where he can't be spotted. Either high in the sky or deep underground.

Wyldfyre: Frak. That idea is so good! It could have been mine. Let's split up. High and low.

Frak: I'll take the sewers. My dad and I found Loyalists down there.

Wyldfyre: Take Arin. He likes sewers.

Arin: I... I do?

Wyldfyre: It's how you got into Imperium, remember?

Arin: Doesn't mean I like them.

Wyldfyre: I'll search the sky with Riyu and Euphrasia. (Arin and Frak head out.) Thanks. But I got my own ride. (Euphrasia is riding with Riyu as Wyldfyre calls in her Transforming Dragon Mech.) Roby really gets me. (Sora's pad is beeping.) Uh, Riyu, you're beeping.

Euphrasia: (opens the incoming call from Cyrus Borg) Sora's datapad, how can I help you?

Cyrus Borg: Is Sora there? It's urgent.

Euphrasia: She's kind of... stuck with Cryptor. Can I take a message?

Cyrus Borg: No. This makes it even more urgent. See me immediately.

(Euphrasia, Riyu, and Wyldfyre fly off.)

(Arin and Frak traverse through Ninjago Sewers)

Arin: Sora was finally starting to get along with her parents and I had to go and ruin it.

Frak: You were only trying to do what you thought was right. Sora will see that once we get her back. If we get her back.

Arin: It's not like you to be such a downer, Frak.

Frak: It's just... Well, there's something I've been trying to tell her for, well, forever. I could never find the right words, and now I might never get the chance.

Arin: Words? What words? What are you talking about?

Frak: Well, I- Gah!

(The Loyalists surround Arin and Frak.)

(Euphrasia, Riyu, and Wyldfyre arrive at Borg Tower. The elevator opens to Cyrus Borg.)

Cyrus Borg: Since Cryptor's return, I've worked out a way to track him. His energy signature is unique, taking into account the heat flux-

Wyldfyre: (fakes a snore) Oh, you done science talking yet?

Cyrus Borg: Oh, well, it's kind of my thing.

Wyldfyre: Well, saving our friends is kind of our thing. So, can you speed it up?

Cyrus Borg: This will help you find Cryptor.

Wyldfyre: Now, that's the kind of science talk I like.

Cyrus Borg: But the range is limited. You have to be close.

(The original ninja are still waiting Zilvar to throw the Weapon of Desolation into the Abyss.)

Lloyd: Zilvar, it's time. Get as close as you can and throw the weapon in.

Jay: We've got your back.

Weapon of Desolation (in Zilvar's Mind): (Zilvar's eyes begin to glow red.) You cannot throw away the chance to save the ones you love.

Zilvar: I have no choice. This weapon is pure destruction.

Weapon of Desolation (in Zilvar's Mind): But Zeras said your tribe could be saved if you protect the weapon.

Zilvar: He is Ras now, not the boy I knew. He could easily be lying.

Weapon of Desolation (in Zilvar's Mind): But what if he's telling the truth? Your people depended on you and you failed them. This is your second chance. Your only chance.

(Zilvar remembers the ground shaking and his people running as the ground cracks and collapses a swallows them, including Mei.)

Zane: Did we not stress the urgency of this enough?

Weapon of Desolation (in Zilvar's Mind): If your hand lets go, you throw away their lives forever.

Lloyd: It's okay! You got this! Throw it in!

(Zilvar tries not to listen the Weapon of Desolation, until he sees an illusion of Mei.)

Mei (illusion): My love, why won't you save me?

Zilvar: I will! I will save you all! (He activates the Weapons and summons Elemental Monsters.)

(Arin and Frak are surrounded by the Loyalists.)

Arin: Hey there. Maybe we can talk this out. (The Loyalists blasts them, but Arin and Frak dodge their attacks.)

Frak: Lasers in a sewer. Great idea. (Arin fights the Loyalists.) Oh man. Just like when we were kids. Bullies beware.

Arin: Back then, our bullies weren't psychopaths with lasers.

Frak: I've been meaning to show you the impossible double twist flying kick. Check it out. Yah! (knocks one Loyalist down with the kick.)

Arin: Yeah! (sees a Loyalist about to blast Frak)

Frak: All right.

Arin: Frak! (performs Spinjitzu, creating a red energy wave to knock down the Loyalist.)

Frak: Whoa-ho-ho-ho! You shot energy out of your Spinjitzu? Whaaat?

Arin: Yeah. I don't know why it's doing that. I - (hears a sound nearby) Hey, what's all that? (Arin and Frak check around.) Is this an armory with more lasers? Do they not get how tunnels work?

Frak: But there's nothing here that can lead to Sora. Just a dead end.

Arin: Wait, Zeatrix told us about an access key for the Loyalist's comms.

Frak: That can override biometric locks. You think that'd really work?

Arin: Only one way to find out.

Frak: We triggered it.

(The Access Key beeps with some of the lights on it turning off.)

Arin: That look like a countdown timer to you?

Frak: We got to get to the monastery!

(In Cryptor's hideout, Sora sneaks around while the Loyalists drag Zeatrix to a small flat bed. Sora watches as the Loyalists connect a device powered by the Mind Crystal onto Zeatrix's head, with Beatrix's unconcious body on the other side.)

Cryptor: Activate the exchange.

(The machine powers up.)

Sora: Operation Mentax. It has to be! (The machine transfers Beatrix and Zeatrix's minds into the crystal.) A mind swap. They're giving Beatrix her sister's healthy body! (She sneaks under the small bed and tries using Technology to shut down the machine, but fails.) Come on, powers... (She tries it again.)

(In the skies, Euphrasia rides on Riyu while Wyldfyre rides her mech.)

Wyldfyre: Are we sure this thing even works?

Euphrasia: We just have to wait and s--

Wyldfyre: I hate waiting!

Euphrasia: The Writers of Destiny have a trick when waiting for inspiration... don't think about... alligators.

Wyldfyre: What!? Now all I want to do is think about alligators! In what world is this helpful? (The device gets a signal.) It worked! How did you do that?

Euphrasia: (laughs) I didn't I just distracted you long enough for the tech to work.

Wyldfyre: That was tricky! I like it! (They descend, but the device stops beeping.) As we were coming down, we lost the signal.

Euphrasia: (They ascend and the signal comes back.) Uh, could Cryptor be above us?

Wyldfyre: All I see are clouds. (They ascend higher.)

(At the Abyss of Eternal Night, The Elemental Monsters charge at the original Ninja.)

Lloyd: Zilvar! Why are you doing this? (The ninja pull out their weapons and charge at the Elemental Monsters. Lloyd slashes an Earth Monster and Kai kicks a Mutation Monster. Zane throws shurikens at another Earth Monster, which Nya shoves away. Kai slashes an Acid Monster while Lloyd jumps on a Mutation Monster's legs and slams it down.) Drop the weapon, Zilvar! (Zilvar summons more Elemental Monsters using the weapon. An Earth Monster appears in front of Lloyd and shoots Asphalt at him, sticking him to a rock.) Not this again.

(An Acid Monster shoots Acid at Cole, who dodges it. Zilvar whistles to call in Grimtak, who rams Nya, Kai, Zane and Jay, Zilvar jumps and rides on him. An Ice Monster slams down, creating ice, Kai dodges it, but Jay, Zane and Nya get hit. Kai tries to slash Grimtak, but the Dragon-Ape pins him to the ground. Jay rushes to them.)

Jay: Easy, boy. Easy... We're friends, remember? (Kai runs away.)

(Grimtak roars, and Zane rushes to him.)

Zane: He doesn't seem to agree. (pulls Jay back, preventing him from being crushed.)

(Lloyd tries to get out of the asphalt but fails, Zilvar rams the ninja with Grimtak. Lloyd transforms into his Draconus Form, allowing the other ninja to do the same. He then flies up and uppercuts an Ice Monster.)

Lloyd: Zilvar! You need to drop the weapon! H'yah!

(Grimtak tries to punch and slam Lloyd out of the way, but Lloyd slashes him back. Nya slams down a Mutation Monster, and Kai spins horizontally, grabbing an Fire Monster and slamming it down.)

Kai: Can't believe Zilvar betrayed us. We went on a road trip together, man! Does that bond mean nothing?

Zane: It's clear he's been corrupted by the Weapon, like Beniro was.

Cole: Or he's just pure evil.

Jay: No! Zilvar would never do this. He must not have control of himself.

(Grimtak slams the ground forcing Lloyd to back away. Lloyd slashes Zilvar down to the ground and dodges an Ice Monster's attack. He then jumps up and slashes Grimtak to slam down the Ice Monsters. Grimtak runs away.)

Lloyd: Good boy. Get to safety!

(Zilvar uses the Weapon of Desolation, and the Weapon absorbs an Ice Monster.)

Nya: I'm pretty hard to surprise by now, but that one got me.

(Zilvar begins to use Ice through the Weapon, blasting it at the ninja, who jump away. Zane makes an ice barrier, which stops Zilvar's attack. The Weapon then absorbs a Fire Monster, granting Zilvar access to Fire, and he uses a fireball to destroy the barrier, sending the ninja flying backwards.)

Cole: Since when can it do that?!

Zane: The weapon must somehow be telling Zilvar how to use its secrets.

(Lloyd charges at Zilvar, dodging the Fire. Zilvar uses the Weapon to absorb a Mutation Monster and an Acid Monster, then blasts Acid at Lloyd, Lloyd folds his wings to dodge it but gets hit by Fire, Jay rushes to Zilvar.)

Jay: Zilvar! (He dodges an Acid Beam.) I know it isn't you doing this! You have to fight it!

(Zilvar uses the Weapon again, absorbing an Earth Monster. He uses its powers at Jay, but Jay manages dodge it by sliding to the side, causing the asphalt to restrain an approaching Cole by the legs.)

Cole: (He reaches to Jay, who approaches Zilvar.) Jay, wait! That Zilvar isn't the one you knew. He won't hold back!

Jay: (He turns to Cole.) I have to get through to him. Zilvar is honorable. He saved me once. I owe him the same. (Zilvar summons more Elemental Monsters by using the Weapon.)

(Sora tries turn off the machine, but keeps failing. Cryptor and the Loyalists watch as the mind swap procedure continues.)

Sora: No time left. This has to work. (She gets near the device and tries to use her powers again, but keeps failing. She is then grabbed by a Loyalist.) No! (She gets slammed down.)

Cryptor: Welcome to my lab. And goodbye.

(Arin and Frak runs to the Monastery while the access key's contdown is nearly finished. Sora's parents are having tea together.)

Frak: Down to two lights!

Arin: We have to save Sora! (Sora's parents notice them.) Go, go go! (Sora's parents follow Arin as they rush to the Relaxation Room.) It's our only chance to find out where the Loyalists took Sora!

(Sora's parents hide behind the door to watch as Frak tries to use the key. Arin examines the device, then punches it to open up a slot. Frak puts the Access key in the slot, but the coundown ends just before that.)

Frak: No! How could we run out of time?

Arin: It's over. We failed to save Ninjago, and Sora.

(Sora's parents look concerned.)

(At the Abyss of Eternal Night, the fight continues, Nya smacks an Earth Monster and an Ice Monster, but gets slammed down by a Fire Monster. Zane punches an Acid Monster and gets hit by a Mutation Monster's laser eye along with Cole. An Acid Monster fires acid at Lloyd and Lloyd blocks it. Kai dodges a Mutation Monster's attack and an Acid Monster's attack but gets slammed down to the ground again by a Mutation Monster.)

Kai: There's a never-ending supply of these jerks! (Uses his power to repel the Mutation Monster.)

Lloyd: We won't be able to stop them until we get the Weapon out of Zilvar's hands.

(Lloyd sharpens his claws and slashes down an Acid Monster, Earth Monster, Fire Monster and an Ice Monster. Zilvar combines the elemental monsters' powers into a single attack and pushes the ninja back.)

Zane: We can't get close enough.

Jay: I can! (flies to Zilvar)

Nya: No, Jay! It's too dangerous!

Jay: (He dodges multiple Acid, Fire, and Ice Attacks, then spins around Zilvar.) Zilvar, listen, please! I understand how you feel. I was torn from my people once, too. (His mech is shown getting caught up in the Merge, and Donovan is shown erasing his memories.) I was lied to, and captured, and lost who I once was. (Ras is shown using the Gong of Shattering on him.) I was corrupted by a magical artifact, the same as you! I lost everyone important to me, and I still worry I'll never overcome that. The good in me is damaged forever, but I've found a path forward, (A picture of the original ninja together is shown.) with people who care about me, who see what I once was, and what I can be again! If I was able to survive, to throw off corruption and anger and still find myself, I know you can, too. (He lands.) Let me help you, Zilvar. (Zilvar's eyes stop glowing.)

Cole: Is Jay really getting through to him?

Nya: He's amazing. (Zilvar puts the weapon down but his eyes start glowing again as he grips the weapon tightly. Jay gasps and gets hit upwards, and Zilvar charges the weapon, blasting Jay down with an attack combining the Weapon's corrupted elements.) No! (She rushes to Jay, who is unconscious.) Jay!

(Zilvar summons more Elemental Monsters by using the Weapon again. Nya throws Water at a Mutation Monster, and Kai slashes an Acid Monster.)

Kai: You didn't like what Jay said? Try my pep talk! (He charges at Zilvar and spins horizontally, but gets hit by the combined elements.)

(In the skies, Euphrasia and Wyldfyre are still trying to find Cryptor's hideout.)

Wyldfyre: There's no secret Loyalist base up here, just clouds.

(The device beeps. Euphrasia looks at one of the clouds.)

Euphrasia: Hey, I know that cloud!

Wyldfyre: Huh? All clouds are basically the same. It's not like they have names.

Euphrasia: Well, maybe if you're a ground-dweller, but a Cloud Kingdom Monk knows a cloud from home when she sees one. We call that one Gary.

(They land on a piece of the Cloud Kingdom.)

Wyldfyre: You really know your clouds, but how'd part of your home get here?

Euphrasia: It must have broken away when Ras attacked our Kingdom.

Wyldfyre: (checks the device) We're definitely in the right place!

(They walk into the fog and find P.I.X.A.L., damaged)

Euphrasia: P.I.X.A.L? Has she been here the whole time?

Wyldfyre: What happened to you?

(P.I.X.A.L points across the fog, and Euphrasia lifts her on top of Riyu. They spots the Nindroid MechDragon.)

Euphrasia: That definitely isn't from Cloud Kingdom.

(They walk forward when a Security Camera detects them, alerting Cryptor.)

(In Cryptor's hideout, Loyalists prepare to fire their weapons at Sora.)

Cryptor: Stop. (The Loyalists pause.) If the transfer fails, we need a back-up body for Beatrix's mind.

Sora: Don't look at me. I don't want that creep in my brain.

Cryptor: (An alarm beeps in his eye.) Intruder detected at the Cloud Factory! Continue the procedure without me.

(The Loyalists nod, Cryptor shuts himself down and transfers himself into the Nindroid MechDragon. At the Cloud Factory, Wyldfyre and Euphrasia notice the dragon powering up.)

Euphrasia: Those three lights. It's like--

Wyldfyre: Cryptor!

(In the Relaxation Room, Arin sits down while Frak is still trying to activate the Loyalist Communicator)

Frak: There's got to be some other way to activate this thing.

Arin: Face it, Frak! We'll never get Sora back from Cryptor, and it's all my fault!

(The door opens. Sora's parents walk in to help.)

Sora's father: We can help.

Arin: You don't understand. Without an access key, only a loyalist can get this hunk of junk to work.

(Sora's mother activates the Loyalist Communicator.)

Frak: But... that means you two are--

Arin: B-but, you said...

Sora's father: Go save our daughter.

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
