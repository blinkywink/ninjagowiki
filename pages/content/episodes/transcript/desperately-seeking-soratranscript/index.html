(The original Ninja and Zilvar arrive at the Abyss of Eternal Night. Jay looks at Zilvar and hears the sound of forging, triggering a memory from his past. In the flashback, Ray is forging a sword when Edna arrives holding young Jay, who is clutching Mister Cuddlywomp)

Edna: Morning, blacksmith. I'm hoping you can help me identify an object.

Ray: I'm happy to try. Call me Ray.

Edna: Edna, from the scrap and junk out by the Sea of Sand. Well, my husband, Ed found this in the scrap. Or maybe in the junk. It's hard to tell. But it's so rusted. We weren't sure what it was. But we hoped a former elemental master might know. Hmm. Oh, careful. I have my suspicions about this thing, and none of them are good.

(Ray unrolls a cloth wrapping of Realm medallion. Young Jay accidentally get the weapon and drop it all. He sets them back up and picks Mister Cuddlywomp back up)

Ray: Huh? It's heavier than it looks.

(Young Kai and Nya were playing)

Ray: Yeah, it's a primordial artifact. all right. Hmm. I need to show it to a friend of mine. Uh he'll know what it is. Can you wait a bit?

(The flashback ends)

Jay: Nya, I just got another memory back. But this is when I totally forgot even before The Merge. I think we met as little kids.

Nya: I'm pretty sure I'd remember that. Also, not exactly the time to walk down memory lane, Jay.

Kai: I can see why this place isn't in the top ten tourist spots.

Cole: Let's just get this over with.

Nya: It's like a heartbeat.

(Zilvar walks forward, ready to drop the Weapon of Desolation as his eyes glows red, matching his eyes)

(In the back of the Old Town Courthouse, Arin checks on Riyu)

Arin: you okay, buddy? Nothing broken?

(Riyu groaning)

Euphrasia: Sora, can you hear me? Sora!

Frak: Maybe she just shut off her comms so they can't hear her stowed away.

Arin: This is all my fault.

Wyldfyre: (gasps) Are you secretly Cryptor?!

Arin: What? No! Sora and I were fighting over my stupid accusation about her parents. If we were working together like usual, she wouldn't have gone into danger alone.

Wyldfyre: You say danger like it's a bad thing.

(Riyu finds Sora's datapad)

Euphrasia: (gasps) Sora's data pad! She must have dropped it in the fight.

Arin: So now we can't even track her with it. This keeps getting worse.

Frak: (thinks, remembering) Wait, we don't know where Cryptor is, but we know where he isn't. He invaded Ninjago City before. People all know who he is. He'd be recognized on the street. If he's on the move, it's where he can't be spotted. Either high in the sky or deep underground.

Wyldfyre: Frak. That idea is so good! It could have been mine. Let's split up. High and low.

Frak: I'll take the sewers. My dad and I found loyalists down there.

Wyldfyre: Take Arin. He likes sewers.

Arin: I... I do.

Wyldfyre: It's how you got into Imperium, remember?

Arin: Doesn't mean I like them.

Wyldfyre: I'll search the sky with Riyu and Euphrasia. (Arin and Frak head out) Thanks. But I got my own ride. (Euphrasia is riding with Riyu and Wyldfyre used her Transforming Dragon Mech changes its stance from Mech to dragon) Roby really gets me. (Sora's pad is beeping) Uh, Riyu, you're beeping.

Euphrasia: (opens the incoming call from Cyrus Borg) Sora's datapad, how can I help you?

Cyrus Borg: Is Sora there? It's urgent.

Euphrasia: She's kind of... stuck with Cryptor. Can I take a message?

Cyrus Borg: No. This makes it even more urgent. See me immediately.

(Euphrasia, Riyu, and Wyldfyre flew off)

(Arin and Frak in the Ninjago Sewers)

Arin: Sora was finally starting to get along with her parents and I had to go and ruin it.

Frak: You were only trying to do what you thought was right. Sora will see that once we get her back. If we get her back.

Arin: It's not like you to be such a downer, Frak.

Frak: It's just... Well, there's something I've been trying to tell her for, well, forever. I could never find the right words, and now I might never get the chance.

Arin: Words? What words? What are you talking about?

Frak: Well, I- Gah!

(The Loyalists surround Arin and Frak)

(Euphrasia, Riyu, and Wyldfyre arrived at the Borg Tower. The elevator opens with Cyrus Borg)

Cyrus Borg: Since Cryptor's return, I've worked out a way to track him. His energy signature is unique, taking into account the heat flux-

Wyldfyre: (fakes a snore.) Oh, you done science talking yet?

Cyrus Borg: Oh, well, it's kind of my thing.

Wyldfyre: Well, saving our friends is kind of our thing. So, can you speed it up?

Cyrus Borg: This will help you find Cryptor.

Wyldfyre: Now, that's the kind of science talk I like.

Cyrus Borg: But the range is limited. You have to be close.

(The original ninja still waiting Zilvar to throw the Weapon of Desolation)

Lloyd: Zilvar, it's time. Get as close as you can and throw the weapon in.

Jay: We've got your back.

Weapon of Desolation (in Zilvar's Mind): (his eyes begin to glow red) You cannot throw away the chance to save the ones you love.

Zilvar: I have no choice. This weapon is pure destruction.

Weapon of Desolation (in Zilvar's Mind): But Zeras said your tribe could be saved if you protect the weapon.

Zilvar: He is Ras now, not the boy I knew. He could easily be lying.

Weapon of Desolation (in Zilvar's Mind): But what if he's telling the truth? Your people depended on you and you failed them. This is your second chance. Your only chance.

(Zilvar remembers the ground shaking with his people running as the ground cracks and collapses beneath them, including Mei)

Zane: Did we not stress the urgency of this enough?

Weapon of Desolation (in Zilvar's Mind): If your hand lets go, you throw away their lives forever.

Lloyd: It's okay! You got this! Throw it in!

(Zilvar tries not to listen the mind of Weapon of Desolation and see an illusion of Mei)

Mei (illusion): My love, why won't you save me?

Zilvar: I will... I will save you all! (The Weapon of Desolation activates and summoned all the Elemental Monsters)

Arin: Hey there. Maybe we can talk this out. (The Loyalists blasts them as Arin and Frak dodges their attacks)

Frak: Lasers in a sewer. Great idea. (Arin fights the Loyalists) Oh man. Just like when we were kids. Bullies beware.

Arin: Back then, our bullies weren't psychopaths with lasers.

Frak: I've been meaning to show you the impossible double twist flying kick. Check it out. Yah! (used impossible double twist flying kick to knock the Loyalist)

Arin: Yeah! (sees Loyalist is about to blast Frak)

Frak: All right.

Arin: Frak! (performed Spinjitzu and shoot out the chaos energy wave to knock the Loyalist)

Frak: Woah! You shot energy out of your Spinjitzu. What!

Arin: Yeah. I don't know why it's doing that. I - (hears a sound coming from the armory) Hey, what's all that? (Arin and Frak check in the armory) Is this an armory with more lasers? Do they not get how tunnels work?

Frak: But there's nothing here that can lead to Sora. Just a dead end.

Arin: Wait, Zeatrix told us about an access key for the Loyalist's comms.

Frak: That can override biometric locks. You think that'd really work?

Arin: Only one way to find out.

Frak: We triggered it.

(The Access Key is beeping with five chances)

Arin: That look like a countdown timer to you?

Frak: We got to get to the monastery!
