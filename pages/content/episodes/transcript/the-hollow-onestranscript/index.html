(Gandalaria approaches and enters a village, inhabited by wizards and other creatures.)

Villager seller: Hexes! Get your vile hexes! Only three coins per pox!

Gandalaria: Only three coins? Oh, that's a deal and a half! (She takes three coins from her pocket and hands them to the villager seller, who hands over a small box in return.) Ah, wonderful! I'm so glad to be back in Abracalabria. You know, I haven't been here in years. Or has it been centuries? What time is it? Ah, one of two.

Villager seller: (He gives a confused look and starts walking away.) Hexes! get your vile hexes!

(Gandalaria takes a mushroom, which she is using as a shopping list, from her pocket.)

Gandalaria: "New hexes" check. (She crosses it off the mushroom list.) Still need to visit the Slug Merchant, the Charm Boutique, and the Slime Spa, but first... (She gasps when she sees a stand with crystal balls.) ...the Crystal Ball Repair Store! (She takes her crystal ball out of her pocket and approaches Stuart.) Hello. (She clears her throat.) How much to get this old beauty patched up?

(Stuart takes off his magic top hat, twirls his hand around the rim of it, and a Magic-Tide, named Pilly-Wig, appears from within.)

Pilly-Wig: (To Gandalaria.) Need some repairs, huh, lady?

Gandalaria: Oh, just some minor adjustments, you know. The poor dear isn't as young as she used to be.

(She spins her crystal ball, which release green smoke.)

Pilly-Wig: (He coughs.) Adjustments? Come on! This thing's a total loss! Now, I could set you up with a brand-new, top-of-the-line solid ruby model for half of what it would cost to repair this old junker!

Gandalaria: Oh! This crystal ball saved me when everything went down with the Carnus Conclave. I am not abandoning it now!

Pilly-Wig: You were around way back then? Whoa. Then you should definitely invest in some updated equipment. Check out this marbled emerald number.

(The sound of a flute is heard and a mist begins to appear.)

Gandalaria: Wait, wait, what is that noise? Is it a sick troll?

(The sound increases and attracts the attention of all the inhabitants.)

Gandalaria: I don't remember this town having such strange customs.

(She turns around and sees Kur floating and playing a strange flute, making a cyclops and other people float.)

Gandalaria: (She groans in frustration.) Do I have to save the day yet again? (She takes her crystal ball back and makes it generate electricity, but a coconut ends up falling out of it.)

Pilly-Wig: Whoa! Told you it was a junker.

Gandalaria: How dare you insult her! She's one of the finest crystals from the--

(A roar is heard and something pushes the crystal ball into Gandalaria's face, causing her to fall and the crystal ball to drop another coconut.)

(A flashback shows scenes from the end of the Tournament of the Sources.)

Arin: (Narrating.) I was there, and it's still hard to explain. I saw Ras getting dragged into the portal gate, and since he was the only person who knew where my parents were, I freaked out. I couldn't lose him to the Forbidden Five. Then Nokt made everything explode, (Nokt uses Brute Force balls on him, but he dodges and the balls hit the portal gate, which explodes, and throws them into the Ethereal Divide.) and suddenly I felt like my whole body was turning inside out-- (He lands in the First Land.) Then I landed somewhere I didn't recognize. The Forbidden Five were gone... (He notices that Ras is with him.) ...but I had freed Ras. (He carries Ras across the land.) His injures were really bad, and getting worse. He didn't have long to live. He would only tell me where my parents were if I took him to a place that could heal him. It wasn't close. While I dragged him along, he trained me, and my skills got better like never before. (He performs Spinjitzu and destroys a bunch of rocks.) We finally made it to this creepy place called the Spectral Lands, where people who are departed pass through. There was some kind of healing elixir in a well, the only place you can get that stuff. Ras got better and kept his promise. I kinda wish he didn't. Ras made me another promise. He said if I helped him undo the Merge, to separate all the combined realms, he would be able to bring my parents back to life. So that's what we were trying to do when we ran into you and the Ninja. I used my Object Spinjitzu to destroy that big Chaos Dragon with some of that same elixir.

(The flashback ends showing Thunderfang's death. In the First Land, Arin and Sora are training Spinjitzu.)

Arin: I mean, I think it was my Object Spinjitzu that did it. Unless--

Sora: No, I didn't help with that. It was all you, Arin. So that's what you threw at the dragon, healing juice from some strange well? We should probably stock up on that stuff.

Arin: Yeah, it was not easy to get to, and there were these ugly things called Soul Suckers that attacked.

Sora: And do you really trust that Ras isn't lying? I mean, what if reversing the Merge can't bring your parents back like he says?

Arin: I have to try, because there's no other hope. (He performs Spinjitzu.) If this doesn't work, my family is really gone.

Sora: Hey, I get it, and that's why I'm here, because if we're doing something dangerous, we're doing it together. (She extends her hand.) We're a team, right?

(Arin squeezes her hand.)

Arin: You bet we are.

Sora: And remember, there's no "Ras" in "team."

(Arin laughs.)

Ras: Pack up. (He approaches them.) I know where we're headed.

Sora: How? Some desert animal tell you? There's no one around for miles.

Ras: You are with us by my permission, Sora. Do not test me.

Arin; But she has a point, Ras. Even though it merged like all the other realms, I thought no one knew how to get to the Underworld? It's all mystical and secret and stuff.

Ras: It is, and I now know who holds those secrets.

(Ras approaches three motorcycles, while Arin and Sora look on in confusion.)

Sora: (To Arin) I wonder what the Ninja are up to.

Arin: (He sighs.) Probably just another day at the Monastery.

(Arin and Sora grab their things and even Ras.)

(In the skies, Riyu, Heatwave, Zanth, and Jiro are flying alongside the Temple Bounty, which is flying wildly. The Ninja are on top of the ship.)

Zane: While this speed is optimal for rapid travel, I feel we would all be safer with better piloting!

Kai: You try talking to her!

(Wyldfyre is shown piloting the ship.)

Wyldfyre: (She laughs.) My airship, my rules! And the rule is... I drive!

(Everyone screams.)

Wyldfyre: Don't worry, I'll get us there in time!

Cole: That wasn't my main worry! Who gave her an airship again?

Kai: Roby. She saved his life.

Cole: No one ever got me anything for saving their life. Oh... my cake's coming back up! (He barfs.)

Nya: Ugh, I told him not to eat cake for breakfast!

(The Ninja and the dragons arrive at the training plateau of the Dragon Masters, where Rontu is at the entrance.)

Lloyd: (To Rontu.) Is it true?

Rontu: Yes. The great warrior will soon transition out of this life.

(They all approach Egalt, who is lying on the ground with his illness in its final stages, terrifying Wyldfyre.)

Egalt: (He grumbles softly.) Huh? Ninja... Thank you for coming.

Wyldfyre: Egalt. What can we do? There has to be something.

Egalt: Little one, you know the signs of late-stage Wasting Sickness as well as I. There is nothing to be done.

(Wyldfyre begins to cry, while a Messenger Dragon passes a message to Egalt.)

Egalt: Members of my tribe, the Dragons of the Northern Spiral, are on the way to honor my passing, but they will not arrive in time. I am glad to see, one last time with my mortal eyes, my final and greatest students.

Lloyd: We wouldn't be the Ninja we are today without the gift of your teachings, Master Egalt. (He bows to Egalt.)

Egalt: It is you who gave me a gift. You restored my faith in people. It is a joy to have lived long enough to receive this gift. (He realizes something.) Hmm. Where is my student Arin?

Lloyd: Uh... Master, he and Sora have chosen to follow a different path.

Egalt: That hurts my heart. As a teacher, I was hardest on Arin, not because of his failings, but because he had the most potential of any Ninja. The world needs that potential fulfilled. You cannot let him go astray, Master Lloyd.

(Lloyd bows to him again.)

Wyldfyre: The stories my family told about you, (She hugs Egalt.) I don't want them to end.

Egalt: All stories must end, but new ones begin. I will move along to the next stage of existence, whatever that may be. A new adventure for an old dragon. You are a credit to the Fire Tribe, little one. A true dragon.

Wyldfyre: You inspire me to be a great warrior.

Egalt: It is good to be a warrior, but one day, you will make a great leader. (He turns to Rontu.) Rontu, my companion of many centuries. The dangers we have faced. The life we have shared. As the Seven Source Dragons are my witness, (Rontu hugs him.) I would not change a moment of my time with you.

Rontu: Goodbye, my love.

(Egalt inhales sharply, exhales slowly, and finally dies. A pink leaf landing on his hat. The Ninja bow to the deceased dragon as the pink petal flies from the hat. Wyldfyre cries again, and she and the other dragons roar to the sky. Later, the Ninja begin to return to the Temple Bounty.)

Nya: (To Lloyd.) You're thinking about Arin and Sora.

Lloyd: Egalt was right. We have to find them.

(Riyu says something to Rontu.)

Rontu: That is a very kind offer, Riyu, and I'm sure living with you and the other dragons in the monastery would be delightful, but this has been my home for centuries. Egalt and I loved these lands. I have to stay here.

(Riyu nods as the other dragons fly after the Temple Bounty. He says something else to Rontu before flying after the others.)

Rontu: Farewell, young Riyu. (She enters the plateau.)

(Ras, Sora and Arin ride through the desert on their motorcycles.)

Sora: (To Ras.) Okay, let me just try to get this straight. The place we're going to is called Abraca-dooberdoo...

(They enter Mysterium.)

Ras: Abracalabria.

Sora: Right, which apparently can lead us to the Underworld, where your Source Dragon Icon Forge is, but until about three hours ago, you never said a word about it, then you walk off for a couple minutes, and come back with all kinds of answers. Are you just making this up? You can be honest with me.

Ras: I do not need to be interrogated by the likes of you, Imperian.

Sora: Wow, pretty ungrateful for a guy riding on a cycle I totally built for him.

Ras: I did not realize the price for these vehicles would be uninterrupted annoyance! Don't over-inflate your value to me... Ana.

(Ras speeds up his motorcycle as Arin and Sora stand side by side.)

(At the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Zane and P.I.X.A.L. are near a balloon while talking with the other Ninja.)

Kai: You see anything cool out there, you tell me right away, 'cause it's gonna be boring without you.

P.I.X.A.L.: We promise to stay in contact.

Zane: Roby is allowing us to use his City of Temples Messenger Dragons if the need to communicate arises.

Cole: Hope those little dragons fly better than Wyldfyre in the Bounty.

Wyldfyre: I head that, Mr. Barfy!

P.I.X.A.L.: I can think of no one more exciting (Zane kisses her on the cheek.) to explore these new Merged Lands with, Zane.

Lloyd: If you come across any other strange monasteries, let us know.

(Zane and P.I.X.A.L. enter the balloon.)

Zane: We will, but the only parameters of this trip are for P.I.X.A.L. and I to share new and exciting experiences together.

(Zane presses a button and the balloon begins to inflate.)

Lloyd: You two enjoy yourselves!

(The Ninja wave as the balloon moves away and Zane waves back. Lloyd stops waving and looks depressed.)

(Inside the monastery, Lloyd looks at the picture of the Ninja he saw before, while Nya passes by him, comes back and approaches.)

Nya: You said you were happy for Zane, but something tells me that wasn't the whole truth.

Lloyd: Zane and P.I.X.A.L. deserve some space to themselves, it's just... I feel like we're falling apart. Master Wu always managed to keep us together, to unite us, and I... I don't have that ability.

Nya: It's not your fault. The Merge changed so many things so dramatically.

Lloyd: Yeah. Even when we find missing Ninja, it's not the same.

Nya: We've never had to help someone who shattered their goodness before. He's not only rude to me, but to Cole, and Kai, and everyone, but I can't lose faith in him.

Lloyd: And right now he's our best hope at bringing back Arin and Sora.

(In the relaxation room, Jay is playing Lava Zombies while an irritated Mr. Frohicky hands him a teacup, which he drinks and spits out.)

Jay: Blech! these all taste like sewage, (He throws the teacup away.) and not the good kind!

Mr. Frohicky: I'll prepare another recipe. Hmm. (He slides the door aside, then Lloyd and Nya enter and he talks to them.) I'm pretty certain his leg is healed and he's faking it now. I really hope he's leaving soon.

(Mr. Frohicky leaves and slides the door back, then Lloyd and Nya approach Jay.)

Jay: (To Nya and Lloyd) Was it you two clowns who tried to beat my Galamorph record?

Nya: It was Kai, actually. He had this whole plan to lure you with that game because we knew it was your favorite game when you were a Ninja.

Jay: Ugh, quit acting like you know me, liars! You could've heard about Galamorph being my favorite game from anyone who's ever met me.

Lloyd: We're not liars, Jay. We have pictures of us together when you were a Ninja! You have a room here!

Jay: The Administration had pictures too, and Ras told me all kinds of stories about me working for him. That stuff can be faked. You know what can't be faked? Me not trusting anyone but myself ever again.

Lloyd: Well, I trust you. That's why I believe you when you say Arin's parents still are alive.

Jay: That's 'cause they are.

Lloyd: We need to find them and bring them back.

Jay: Good luck with that. I told you, they're in a really weird place, the Realm of Lee. Doubt you'd ever get close.

Lloyd: But you could get us in?

Jay: (He scoffs.) Pfft! Yeah, right! I'm only here until my leg, which is definitely still massively injured, heals, then I have to get back to my real job. I have someone paying me a small fortune to find that Ras guy your little buddy hangs out with.

Nya: So money is all that matters to you now, Jay?

Jay: Of course not. Video games matter too, (To Mr. Frohicky.) and drinking tea that's not terrible!

(In the hallway Mr. Frohicky hears this and walks away.)

Lloyd: Then we'll double what your other client's paying you... (Nya looks at him with a strange face, then he explains quietly.) I'll get it. I can sell every scroll in the library if I have to. (He returns to speaking normally to Jay.) ...and once we find Arin's parents, we need to find Ras too, to get Arin and Sora away from him, so you'll get money and our help.

Jay: Hmm... and I can bring Ras back to my client?

Nya: If we get back Arin and Sora back, you can do whatever you want with Ras.

(Jay presses a button on the video game contoller and turns off the TV.)

Jay: Fine, but I can only get three of you in the realm, max, and one last thing, I'm in charge, okay? None of this Master Ninja junk, cool?

(Nya and Lloyd agree.)

Lloyd: Cool.

(Jay stands up, apparently having his leg healed.)

Nya: (Confused.) What about your hurt leg?

Jay: Miraculously healed. Okay, then, time to prep. I'll need that frog guy to sharpen all my weapons, and do you know where there's a good dry-cleaner?

(As he approaches Abracalabria with Ras and Sora, Arin notices a lantern on the ground. They stop when they reach Abracalabria and see a wizard, four villagers, and two cyclopses. They get off their cycles and approach, but the cyclopses stop them.)

Wizard Guard: Hello! Welcome, strangers, to Abracalabria. Also, strangers are no longer welcome at Abracalabria.

Ras: I, Lord Ras of the Wyldness, seek an audience with the Pontifex Magicus, who holds information that is most important to me.

Wizard Guard: Maybe your homeland is too wild for my sensibilities, Lord, because where I come from, which is this humble city right behind me, "No longer welcome" means that you are... no longer. (The cyclopses roar at Ras.) Oh, and now you've irritated Torex and Xerot... Go away. (He walks toward them.) Last time the twins got angry, us wizards ended up in a hundred-year war. I found it most inconvenient.

Arin: Yeah, Ras, I'm thinking maybe we better leave. (He and Sora turn around.)

Ras: No one denies me!

(Arin and Sora turn back to see Ras jump, grab his hammer, and slam it down on the ground, knocking away the Wizard Guard and the cyclopes. The Wizard Guard and the villagers take their crystal balls from their pockets and prepare to fight.)

Sora: Great. Just greats.

(Ras jumps and starts attacking.)

(In the dragon stables, Riyu tempts Zanth to play with him, but fails. A cry is heard from above, and Rontu enters the stables through the opening above.)

Rontu: The plateau was much lonelier than I imagined, so I am reconsidering your offer to come live here. (Riyu is happy and the two caress each other.) But my loneliness is not the only reason. I once began your training to become a Ninja, Riyu... (Riyu grumbles.) ... and I am certain it would be Egalt's wish for me to continue.

(Riyu, Rontu, and Zanth hug each other.)

(Ras hits Torex with his hammer and lands on the ground. The Wizard Guard tries to zap Ras with his crystal ball and Xerot tries to grab Ras, but Ras dodges and the zap hits Xerot, who Ras lands on top of. Torex tries to attack, but Ras jumps in his face and then kicks him in the back, making him fall on top of his twin brother. The Wizard Guard and the villagers try to zap Ras, but he dodges, knocks one of them down, blocks another's zap with his hammer, and dodges another, which nearly hits Arin and Sora.)

Arin and Sora: Ah!

Arin: Come on! We were trying to leave! Don't blame us for Ras's anger managements issues! (He and Sora dodges another zap.) Hup!

Male villager 1: (To the Wizard Guard.) They must have been the who attacked us last night!

Sora: We were nowhere near here last night!

(Ras defeats most of the villagers, uses his hammer to open the gates, and enters Abracalabria. He jumps from a mushroom tent to one on top of a carriage while being chased.)

Ras: I demand an audience with the-- (He sees that the villagers have light blue skin and are walking like zombies.) What curse is this?

Arin: What happened here?

(Arin and Sora also notice people acting strange.)

Sora: Is this why they didn't want to let us in?

(Ras descends from the roof of the carriage.)

Ras: (To a villager.) You! Where is the Pontifex Magicus? (He grabs the villager with his left arm.) He alone can direct me to the Underworld!

Male villager 2: You want the Pontifex? There he is!

(The villager points to his left arm and the Pontifex is shown as also acting strange.)

Ras: What has befallen this village?

Wizard Guard: If you just listened to me, this whole situation could've been spared, but no one listens to me anymore, not since that head-shrinking incident in the year 45 A.L.

Ras: What are you talking about?

Wizard Guard: Well, I thought I had a spell for normal-sized heads, but boy, was I wrong...

Ras: No! What happened to the villagers?

Wizard Guard: Oh, that! We were attacked.

(The other villager approach them.)

Male villager 2: How do we know they aren't the ones who attacked us?

Villagers: (Simultaneously.) Yeah! Yeah, explain yourselves!

(Gandalaria appears among the villagers.)

Gandalaria: Arin? Sora? (She starts approaching them.)

Sora: Sorceress! (She and Gandalaria hug each other.) It's been so long!

(The villagers murmur.)

Gandalaria: (To the villagers.) They are Ninja, and the Ninja are allies! (She notices Ras.) Although, I've never met that one, and you do look like a little angry-cat man. you should try to smile more.

Arin: Sorceress, I'm assuming this place isn't always like this?

Gandalaria: Oh, goodness, no. Last night, at the precise moment the sun set, we heard these super-creepy noises, and then we were attacked by invisible enemies.

Ras: How did you defeat them?

Gandalaria: Uh... we didn't. When the sun came up, they were gone, and the victims now walk around like in living sleep. One attacker almost got me! My ball broke. For a second, it felt like something was trying to suck out my life force, but I managed to get away.

Arin: Invisible creatures sucking life?

Sora: Maybe those Soul Suckers you told me about?

Arin: But they can't leave the Spectral Lands.

Sora: What did these creepy noises you heard sound like?

(The sound that was heard before plays again as the village darkens.)

Gandalaria: Like that.

(The mist from before also appears and everyone looks up to see Kur floating and playing her magical flute again.)

Gandalaria: They've come back to finish the job.

(End of episode. For more information, click here.)
