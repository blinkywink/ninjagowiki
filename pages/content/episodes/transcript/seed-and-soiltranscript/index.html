(Arin, Euphrasia, Lloyd, and Zane arrive at the Temple of the Green Ninja.)

Lloyd: I've been so focused on finding it, I'm only realizing now, I have no idea what's waiting for me in that temple. The Arc Dragon was very... nonspecific.

Arin: Well, The Temple of the Dragons Cores was run by a helpful spirit guardian. Maybe it'll be like that?

Lloyd: All I know is, there'll be some "ritual" to help me reach my true potential as the Green Ninja.

Zane: The Dragon also said the ritual was something you would not survive.

Lloyd: Yeah, trying not to think about that part. How do we open it?

(The Wood around them whispers, as Lloyd uses his power to open the curtain of vines at the entrance to the temple and walks through it. Arin tries following him, but an invisible barrier stops him.)

Arin: Unh! Lloyd, you're sealed off! It's a trap!

(Lloyd runs back to the others, passing trough the barrier.)

Arin: Oh! Uh, or... not?

(Lloyd feels around the entrance to the temple for the invisible barrier, but doesn't feel it. Zane tries the same, and gets blocked by the barrier.)

Lloyd: (sighs) Guess I'm doing this one alone.

Arin: Or-- hear me out-- you could just not do it? This doesn't have to be your responsibility, Lloyd.

Lloyd: But it does, Arin. I'm the Green Ninja, but I've never discovered that true potential. Plus, this is the only chance we have to fix the messed up state of Elemental Energy in our world, so... I have to take the risk.

Arin: I knew you were gonna say something like that.

Zane: Good luck, old friend. See you on the other side.

(Lloyd smiles as he enters the Temple of the Green Ninja, leaving his friends behind. The Vine Curtains close, the room flashes white, and Lloyd finds himself on a grassy field.)

Lloyd: Hello? Anyone? (looks at the sky) I'm outside now? (The roots grabs him.) Hey! (He tries to use his power to escape, but is too weak.) Ah!

(Mr. Frohicky checks up on Rontu in the Monastery of Spinjitzu)

Rontu: Thank you for your care, but like Egalt before me, my time is near.

Mr. Frohicky: No, Master Rontu, don't say that. Together we'll fight for your recovery. I've sent Jenny and Barry for supplies. I'll cook you the most restorative bowl of--

Rontu: There is no point. Riyu, my final student, also lays dying, somewhere in a distant land.

Mr. Frohicky: What? No... I'm sure that's not true.

Rontu: I can feel it. He's fading, passing from this life, as I am

Mr. Frohicky: Your suffering must be causing bad dreams.

Rontu: It's no dream, but it is my fault. If I had been a better teacher, I should not have let him go. I didn't protect him, and now he... will... die.

(Mr. Frohicky sadly groans.)

(In Freya's Windmill, Wyldfyre is worried about Riyu's condition as she scrubs him.)

Wyldfyre: Don't worry, Riyu. Sora and Frak will get you what you need. You'll be fine. (She looks up to the airship.) Where are they? Why aren't they back yet?

(Frak and Sora search for the Mutation Monster inside the airship.)

Frak: Come on! We gotta catch that monster before it destroys everything onboard!

Sora: I'm more worried about getting its energy to save Riyu.

Frak: What direction do you think that scream come from?

Sora: Not sure. We'd better split up. We don't have much time.

(Frak and Sora split up. Sora checks to see if someone is there.)

Sora: H'up! H'ah!

(Sora finds Rowan Rockspike is one who screamed and shushes them.)

Sora: (whispers) I thought you were the monster.

Rowan: I saw it. I've been trying to hide.

Sora: You're the one who screamed? Which direction did it go?

(Rowan points to the left, and distant high squealing is heard coming from the vent. Sora stops the vent from making noise, only to be approached from behind by the Mutation Monster. Elsewhere Frak find a mysterious hooded figure, who is a Loyalist.)

Frak: (whispers) Someone there? (Frak presses a button and corners the Loyalist.) Who are you? What are you doing on this ship? (The Loyalist doesn't answer.) Did you free that elemental monster? (The Loyalist pushes down Frak and runs away.) Ugh! Get back here!

(Frak chases the Loyalist when he hears Sora screaming.)

Frak: Sora! (He runs to check on Sora.)

Sora: (lays down while being attacked by the Mutation Monster) Ah! No! (Sora kicks the Mutation Monster) H'ah! Ah! Nope. I need that! (performs Spinjitzu)

(The Mutation Monster blast at Sora with its tentacles and she dodges.)

Frak: Sora!

(Sora and Frak take off their masks.)

Sora: We can't let it escape! It's Riyu's only hope!

(The Mutation Monster breaks the vent and blasts them, but Sora and Frak dodge.)

Sora: No!

(The Mutation Monster escapes.)

Reginald: What? My creature escaped? What kind of Ninja are you? That monster is my valuable property!

(Frak and Sora put their masks on and leave. Reginald follows them.)

Sora and Frak: H'yah! (They get down from the ship to chase the Mutation Monster.)

Reginald: That's right, go get it back, or you'll have the Explorer's Club of Ninjago to answer to!

(The roots grab Lloyd and pull him into the ground, and he hears a little boy laughing deviously behind him.)

Lloyd: Hello? A-Are you... the Spirit of this Temple? (The boy points to the Source Dragons' symbols, surrounded by 18 wooden disks. One of them glows red and flies away.) I've seen some weird Source Dragon visions, but seriously, what was that?

Young Lloyd: Why ask me, old man?

Lloyd: No way...

Young Lloyd: Yes way. I'm the seed that grew into you, and you're the seed that will grow into your future self. Well, that is, unless your life ends here. (giggles deviously)

(Cole and Geo clean the mess left in Master Wu's study.)

Cole: Ras came through the Monastery, fighting everyone in his path, for the library?

Geo: This isn't the Cyrus Borg Children's Reading Room, it's Master Wu's study. It's gotta have secrets Ras would want, right? (uses his power to fix a torn scroll.)

Cole: I guess. To be honest, I'm not sure what Wu kept in here. Mr. Frohicky is not going to like this.

Geo: He's got bigger problems right now. Rontu isn't looking so good.

Cole: Can't believe I got so hotheaded I ran right into that fight with Nokt. I could've been here, I could have protected everyone from Ras.

Geo: Or... you'd be trapped wherever all the other Ninja are.

Cole: It can't be a coincidence Ras used the Gates, then they stopped working right after that. (He checks the scrolls.) These scrolls... They're all about finding, and containing, energy, and they're numbered, but one is missing, and one guess who took it.

Geo: Didn't Ras capture a Source Dragon? Sounds like he's pretty good at containing energy already.

Cole: Unless it's something specific he needs to contain. Something specific to Master Wu's knowledge.

(Frak and Sora run from the airship to Umbrasilvis.)

Frak: You'd think an Elemental Monster would be easier to track down.

(Frak and Sora hear the Umbrasilvans screaming.)

Sora: I'm guessing that way.

(Frak and Sora keep running. Two Umbrasilvans run and turn around to see the Mutation Monster. It blasts at them, but they dodge. The Mutation Monster is about to attack them again when Sora performs Spinjitzu to attack it.)

Sora: Tourists, am I right?

(The Umbrasilvans runs back while Sora and Frak cover them. The Mutation Monster fires a blast, which Sora and Frak dodge before runnig away. Wyldfyre is still waiting.)

Wyldfyre: Can't they keep it down out there? We got a hurt dragon in here!

Sora: Frak, keep it still!

Frak: How am I supposed to do that when it's trying to blast me to the Spectral Lands?

(Riyu groans.)

Wyldfyre: This can't be good.

(Wyldfyre checks outside to see Sora and Frak being attacked by the Mutation Monster while they save Umbrasilvans.)

Wyldfyre: Ugh! They had one job! (uses her power to hit the Mutation Monster) Are you two completely incompetent without me? It's like watching Lava-Tides trying to square dance!

Frak: (takes off his hoodie mask) Now's not the time for jokes!

Wyldfyre: Who's joking? Get that energy to Riyu, I can hold this thing back.

Sora: Yeah, we, uh, didn't get the energy yet.

Wyldfyre: What? Are you two trying to break the record for Most Failed Mission?

(Mutation Monster blasts at them, but Frak and Wyldfyre dodges. Mutation Monster roars and blasts them again.)

Wyldfyre: Ugh! Okay, I'll turn up the heat, you get to work. H'ah! (tries to use her power, but is unable to) Ah! Ugh! I keep forgetting about our messed up powers! Annoying!

Sora: We've gotta do this the old-fashioned way.

Wyldfyre: What does that mean? Take black and white pictures?

Sora: No. Classic Ninja moves, without Elemental Powers. (performs Spinjitzu) H'yah! Ah!

Frak: H'yah!

Wyldfyre: Hi'yah! (She grabs a flag pole and takes off the flag, noticing two Umbrasilvans watching her do it.) Uh, hope this isn't sacred or anything.

Umbrasilvan #2: That flag has stood in that spot for five centuries.

Wyldfyre: Wow! Someone's living in the past. (runs off) Hi'yah! (She hits the Mutation Monster with the flag pole several times.) H'yah! H'ah! Y'ah! Hi'yah! Yah! Yah!

Frak: I'm... afraid of her.

(Sora and Frak grab the Mutation Monster's tentacles while Wyldfyre hits it again. The three thow the monster back, and it grabs Sora before getting hit with the pole.)

Frak: Sora! Now!

(Sora uses her device to collect Elemental Energy from the monster.)

Sora: (The Mutation Monster lets go of her.) Riyu! (She drops the device.) No! (Freya shows up and catches the device.) Nice catch, Freya!

(Frak and Wyldfyre attack the Mutation Monster.)

Frak: Don't worry about us! Save Riyu!

Sora: (grabs the device from Freya.) Please be enough... (Sora injects Riyu with the device.)

(Riyu gets up, opens his glowing eyes and roars for a brief moment before laying back down.)

Frak: We need to use the environment to our strategic advantage.

Wyldfyre: Ugh! Even in a fight, you talk all boring!

Frak: What I mean is-- force that thing into the dead end! It can't escape.

Wyldfyre: Now, that's an idea.

(Wyldfyre hits the Mutation Monster and Frak slides down to kick it. Wyldfyre missed it and kick it. The two of them back it into a corner. The Mutation Monster starts surging with energy.)

Wydlfyre: Frak? It's acting all freaky!

(The Mutation Monster suddenly transforms into a larger form.)

Frak: We're going to need a bigger dead end.

Wyldfyre: Since when can it do that?

(The Loyalist from the airship stands on a roof nearby.)

Lloyd: Are you... really me? I mean, young me?

Young Lloyd: (laughs) Why are you even asking that? You know it's true.

Lloyd: Are you here to guide me?

Young Lloyd: (laughs) Does a tree guide what happens to its seeds?

Lloyd: What's that supposed to mean?

(Young Lloyd uses a seed to grow a tree.)

Young Lloyd: This is the Tree of Life.

(More seeds fall from the tree growing more trees.)

Lloyd: I... I don't understand.

Young Lloyd: Then get to the root of the mystery.

Lloyd: What am I supposed to be looking-- for!

(Lloyd falls and shrinks down while Young Lloyd laughs.)

(Jenny and Barry arrive at the Crossroads)

Barry: (sniffs) Ooh! Is that Merlopian seaweed cake I smell?

Jenny: Mm! I haven't had that since our honeymoon, and it's right here in our old neighborhood?

Barry: Guess everything's right here now.

Jenny: We've missed so much, Barry.

Barry: When Arin gets back, he has to give us a tour of this place.

Jenny: If he gets back. I'm worried. Being a Ninja mom is not easy.

Barry: We should start a Ninja Parent support group.

Jenny: Great idea, but first we gotta get everything Mr. Frohicky needs--

Woman: What is that?

Man: (nervously) That's normal, right?

(A Fire Monster appears.)

Jenny: Maybe this is just a regular part of the Crossroads?

(The Fire Monsters blasts at them. Jenny and Barry dodges as the blast destroys a merchant stall.)

Barry: I'm thinking no.

(Jenny and Barry save two kids from the Fire Monster.)

Jenny: Come on, get to safety!

(Frak and Wyldfyre run away from the Mutation Monster. It catches up and grabs them.)

Gweeg: Hey, you Gormless fopdoodle! Prepare to face the fury of the Lariat Launcher! (Gweeg hits the Mutation Monster with a bola from the laucher, and it lets go of Frak and Wyldfyre.)

Frak: Ugh!

(Gweeg groans)

Frak: Thanks for the distraction, Gweeg! My dad is so wrong about you!

Sora: Come on, Riyu. This can't be it.

Egalt: Your journey is not what you think, young Riyu.

(Riyu remembers playing fetch with Arin using Nunchucks, running around with Arin and Sora on his back, training under Rontu and performing Spinjitzu for the first time in Djinjago.)

(In the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Mr.Frohicky checks on Rontu again.)

Mr.Frohicky: Oh, her heartbeat is getting weaker.

(In the Spectral Lands)

Egalt: Rest easy, Morro. I will handle this one.

Rontu: Egalt, my love! I finally rejoin you, but my passing is not in the glory of battle nor the joy of peace. It is in failure. Source Dragons have abandoned the Merged Lands. All is falling to pieces.

Egalt: I miss you, dear one, but this is not your time.

Rontu: I... I don't understand.

Egalt: For good or ill, your student is important. Only you can show him the way. The fate of this cycle depends on it.

Rontu: But Riyu is dying. I can feel it. He is not going to make it.

Egalt: Never lose hope, Rontu.

(Rontu's soul leaves the Spectral Lands.)

(Back at the Monastery of Spinjitzu)

Rontu: (She wakes up with heavy breathing and coughing.) Riyu...

(Sora sheds her tears and hears the heavy thudding outside. She is shocked as Riyu gets up. Frak and Wyldfyre try to fight the Mutation Monster.)

Frak: No...

(Riyu starts to glow and transforms into a new form as Frak, Wyldfyre, and The Mutation Monster watch. Riyu flies up, breaks two blades from Freya's Windmill, picks them up and starts fighting the Mutation Monster with them.)

Wyldfyre: That is the coolest thing I've ever seen.

Frak: That... was a pretty powerful antidote.

Sora: I just wanted him to live. I had no clue this could happen.

(Mutation Monster blasts at Riyu, but he blocks it and defeats the monster.)

Sora: Riyu! Look at you, boy!

(Riyu grumble-talks.)

Sora: Oof!

(Riyu grumbles fondly.)

Frak: That was amazing! We owe you our lives!

Wyldfyre: I mean, I totally had that thing, but thanks for the assist.

(The Umbrasilvans celebrate the Mutation Monster' defeat)

Gweeg: Good show, old boy! Good show!

(The Loyalist watches from the rooftop and leaves.)

(At the Loyalist hideout, another Loyalist remotely briefs the Chief Loyalist on what happened.)

Loyalist: The monster was unleashed from the airship, and it behaved exactly as you predicted.

Chief Loyalist: Of course it did, but did you get the information we need?

Loyalist: Yes. Recorded at the precise moment of expansion. Sending now. (The data is shown on the holographic display.)

Chief Loyalist: Most interesting. (The Chief Loyalist turns around, bows down, and takes off her mask, revealing herself to be Dr. LaRow.) Positive news. We now know the exact location of the Weapon. However, it is splintered, in two different locations. but I promise, we will find and reassemble the Weapon of Desolation for you.

Beatrix: Then Imperium, the Ninja, and the entire Merged Lands will bow before me as I regain my rightful power!

Dr.LaRow: All hail the Good Empress.

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
