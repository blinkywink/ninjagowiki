(At the police impound lot, two guards are patrolling the area.)

Joe: Whoa. What was that?

Old Guard: Nothing! Will you relax? We're not working Borg Tower anymore, you know!

Joe: I know, I know.

Old Guard: Someone was always breaking in there. Compared to that, this job will be easy as cake! (He chuckles.)

Joe: Yeah. (He laughs nervously.)

(On the roof across from the lot, the ninja are getting ready to break in.)

Cole: Heh. Cake.

Nya: Focus, Cole.

Cole: Right. What's the plan?

Nya: Get to your vehicles. Don't start your engines until I give the signal. Then race to the gate as fast as you can.

Jay: What about the Bounty?

Nya: I'll get the Bounty.

Jay: Good plan! On three. One— (Nya leaves with her grappling hook.) Okay, guess we're not counting. Let's go!

(The ninja race to their vehicles, and Zane is spotted by the guards.)

Old Guard: Hey!

Joe: It's one of the ninja! What do we do?

Old Guard: Stay calm! Call it in, quick!

Joe: (Into his walkie talkie) Uh, this is Joe at the impound. We got a — (His walkie talkie is smacked away.)

Wu: Gentlemen, I apologize.

Zane: Master Wu!

Wu: Please note that I hold officers of the law in the highest regard. But I'm afraid I have no choice. (He backs the guards into their station and seals them in.)

Kai: Well, guess there's no point in being discreet anymore. (He deactivates his vehicle's camouflage.)

Zane: I thought you were at the Sam X Cave with P.I.X.A.L. and Skylor.

Wu: I stayed behind when you broke into Borg Tower to rescue Nya. I chose the letter of the law over my conscience. I was wrong, and now the threat has grown.

Zane: I do not question the urgency. I merely wanted to say, it's good to have you with us.

(The ninja leave in their vehicles, while Wu boards the Bounty.)

Nya: Master Wu?

Wu: Nya, kindly start the engine and set your course for Primeval's Eye.

Nya: With pleasure.

(At Primeval's Eye, Lloyd slashes his way through the jungle with Harumi.)

Harumi: That looks like hard work. Why don't you let me take a turn?

Lloyd: Ha! Fat chance.

Harumi: Suit yourself. You know, this reminds me of the last time we were here. Do you remember? Just you and me, fighting our way through the jungle together.

Lloyd: Stop it.

Harumi: Stop what?

Lloyd: You know what. Stop trying to get into my head.

Harumi: Oh, am I in your head? Are you thinking about me?

Lloyd: No. Now walk! (He shoves Harumi forward.)

Harumi: Well, I'm thinking about you. About how things could've been different between us.

Lloyd: You made your choices, Harumi.

Harumi: We can still be together. If you come back with me and surrender to the Crystal King, I can get him to spare your life. I know it.

Lloyd: I said no, and I meant — (Harumi knocks him back with the leaf behind her and takes her sword back.)

Harumi: Ha! Thanks. (She runs away.)

Lloyd: Get back here!

(Lloyd chases her until she yells behind some bushes.)

Lloyd: Harumi? Harumi!

(Lloyd goes into the bushes then slides down a long slope before he ends up falling into quicksand where Harumi is stuck as well.)

Lloyd: Well, that worked out well for you.

Harumi: Don't. Move.

Lloyd: What are you talking about? Of course I'm gonna move. I'm up to my waist in mud!

Harumi: I said don't move! It's not mud. It's quicksand.

Lloyd: Oh, great.

Harumi: Can you reach those vines?

Lloyd: (He reaches up, but the vines are out of reach.) Gimme the sword. (Harumi moves the sword away from him.) Do you wanna get out of here or not?

(Harumi hands over the sword reluctantly, and Lloyd manages to free himself before he saves Harumi from the quicksand, and when Lloyd does she lands on top of him.)

Harumi: You can get off me now.

Lloyd: I'm not on you. You're on me. (He shoves Harumi off him, then reaches for a cut vine.) Gimme your hands.

Harumi: Is that really necessary?

Lloyd: You literally attacked me like thirty seconds ago!

Harumi: I won't do it again! I think we're past that. You just saved my life. Truce? (She winks.)

Lloyd: No. Now gimme your hands!

Harumi: Fine. (She pretends to comply, but defeats him and takes the sword.) See? That's why you lose, Lloyd. Because you're gullible and — (Lloyd kicks her away, and she leaps back to attack.)

Lloyd: Okay, that's it! (He defeats her with Spinjitzu.)

Harumi: Cheater.

Lloyd: I'd rather be gullible than deceitful. I'd rather choose to see the good in people than the bad. All you have inside you is hate and anger and fear.

(Above them, the council search for them on Dragonides.)

Aspheera: There! I see them!

Harumi: Sorry, you were saying? (Lloyd pulls her into the shades.)

Vangelis: Where? Are you sure?

Aspheera: I saw something green! I swear it!

Vangelis: Green? Well that's helpful! He could be anywhere!

Harumi: (She kicks Lloyd away.) I'm over here!

(Vangelis hears her and the council surround them. Lloyd evades them and runs away.)

Aspheera: Stop him!

(Lloyd stops at a cliff.)

Harumi: You see? All your noble ideals and your foolish hopes. What did they get you? Where are your friends now, when you need them most?

(Just then, the Destiny's Bounty rises behind them. Lloyd sees it and smiles.)

Harumi: No. No! That's impossible!

Wu: Lloyd! Come!

(Lloyd leaps and nearly falls, but Nya catches him.)

Nya: I got ya!

Lloyd: Nya! Master Wu!

Wu: Hang on! We're not in the clear yet!

Nya: Get down!

Aspheera: Wu-ser! You're still alive? (She laughs.)

Wu: Is that the Sword of Fire?

Aspheera: What a wonderful surprise!

Wu: If you like surprises, you're going to love this! (Into his communicator) Zane, are you standing by?

Zane: Affirmative.

Wu: Fire at will!

(Zane flies in on his jet and shoots at the villains.)

Vangelis: Look out!

(Nya fires cannons at the villains. The Mechanic falls from his Dragonide.)

Mechanic: Get me out of here!

Aspheera: Out of my way!

(The heroes manage to drive the council away. Wu turns and spots the Crystal Island in the sky.)

Nya: What is that?

Lloyd: It's the Overlord. He's returned.

Wu: (Into his communicator) Cole. Kai. Do you copy?

Cole: We copy.

Kai: We're holding up the jungle perimeter. Standing by if you need us.

Wu: Negative. Pull back. All of you! We are not ready for this.

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
