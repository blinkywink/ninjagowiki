(In a flashback, a young Ras is hanging off tree branch on the side of a chasm. The branch breaks, and Ras falls to the bottom of the pit. He makes an attempt at climbing out before giving up and sitting down. From above him, a mysterious light eminantes.)

(In the present, Ras looks up towards the Blood Moon while standing in the Cloud Kingdom.)

Ras: My master, it begins. (He turns to Cinder.) Get down to the Shadow Dojo. Set the Gong of Shattering in place.

(He walks over to the Writers of Destiny.)

Ras: (to one of them) You! Have the last of the Theroxian texts been translated?

Writer of Destiny: Yes.

Ras: Good. (to Cinder) Destroy this place and everything in it.

Cinder: (motioning to the Writers of Destiny) What about them?

Ras: I said everything.

(The Writers of Destiny all gasp.)

Ras: We no longer need the Writers of Destiny. We are the masters of our destiny now.

(Cinder chuckles as the Writers of Destiny panic amongst themselves.)

(In the Wyldness, Kai, Sora, and Wyldfyre are in the process of climbing out of a chasm.

Kai: You two still with me?

Sora: (grunting) A smoother ride couldn't hurt.

Wyldfyre: (groans) I am not afraid of anyone or anything, especially heights! I'm just closing my eyes to rest them.

(The rocks Kai's Climber Mech grabbed onto break, causing Wyldfyre to fall off of the mech. She screams. Kai shoots down an expendable hook and catches her.)

Kai: Whoops!

Wyldfyre: Whoops? You're not allowed to say whoops!

Kai: I totally got this.

(Kai retracts the hook.)

Wyldfyre: Not cool, not cool, not cool!

(Wyldfyre lands back on the mech.)

Sora: You good, Wyldfyre?

Wyldfyre: Yeah. No thanks to Mr. "I totally got this."

(Kai shoots the hook upwards and latches it around a rock,)

Kai: I totally do got this! (The rock breaks.) Uh-oh.

(Wyldfyre blasts the rock with her powers.)

Sora: Huh? (sighs) Thanks.

(They climb the rest of the way and Sora and Wyldfyre jump to the ground.)

Wyldfyre: (laying on the ground) I love you. I love you, ground.

Kai: Come on, it wasn't that bad!

Sora: No, it was. But at least we're out of danger now.

(A branch snaps nearby. Four Wolf Mask Warriors jump from some bushes.)

Sora: Forget I said that.

(Lloyd and Nya run through the forest.)

Nya: We got so turned around in that storm, I have no idea how far out we are.

Lloyd: I know, but we gotta get to the Ritual of the Blood Moon before-- (Lloyd has a vision of the Shadow Dojo.) Not now! (groans) No!

Nya: More visions?

Lloyd: (groans) I can't let them come. Have to force them to go away. (groans)

(Lloyd has a vision showing the Wolf Mask Claw Warriors, a tower in the Cloud Kingdom collapsing, and Ras hitting Euphrasia into a portal on one end, and on the other, a member of the Forbidden Five emerging. Lloyd groans on the ground.)

Nya: Lloyd. Lloyd!

(Jordana uses her magic to levitate the protective container holding Bonzle.)

Jordana: Settle down, spell. (She sets the container down on a vehicle.) I don't know what you think you've been doing, playing person with your fake family, but I know your true purpose. You're a spell that can bridge our world with Nether-Space.

Bonzle: Please, no. I don't wanna be just a spell again.

Jordana: (scoffs) Like you have a choice. You should thank us. We're helping you to do what you were created to do.

(Jordana hops in the vehicle and takes off.)

(In the magical storagehouse, Gandalaria, Cole, and Zane are attempting to disable the forcefield surrounding them, having stacked books that Zane stands on.)

Gandalaria: Ooh, careful, Zane. Don't wanna fall and get a bump on your noggin.

(The force field zaps Zane.)

Zane: (gasps) I should be able to overload this Imperium electro-wall now!

(He sticks a wire from his head into the force field machine, and the field falls. Zane hops off the books, and Cole runs to open try to open the locked front door, and yells against it.)

Cole: (groans) We've gotta get out! We gotta get Bonzle back from that creep.

(Cole hits the door with his powers, and the entire building shakes.)

Zane: I understand your emotions, Cole, but we must remain calm to find a solution.

Gandalaria: He's right! Any spell storehouse worth its magic has a few other trap doors and secret passages.

Cole: Great! Let's find them!

Gandalaria: (Searching with her crystal ball.) Eh, no. (grunts) Ah! (The orb buzzes.) Eureka!

(A trapdoor magically appears in front of them. Gandalaria opens to it find that the trapdoor is Fang Mouth. They close the trapdoor.)

Gandalaria: Okay. Something turned that escape hatch into a raging Fang Mouth. Janet. No big deal. We'll find another way.

(They travel deeper into the storagehouse. On a wall of storage compartments, Gandalaria places and pulls a plunger, causing the compartments to move up and out of the way.)

Gandalaria: See? A secret passage. Come on! (She runs into the darkness, only to be stopped by the solid darkness.) What? Hmph. It seems the darkness has been made solid here. Fine! We'll have to go through the deadly and smelly hidden passageway. It's just behind this door. It smells like rotten manure covered in moldy toe jam. (Opens the door and sniffs, only to find another, smaller door.) I-I don't smell anything. (She opens the next door, only to find another, smaller door.) No! Don't tell me it's been hit with an "infinity door" curse".

(Gandalaria continues opening the smaller and smaller doors until one opens to a room of Lava-Tides having a party. The music stops and they look at her.)

Gandalaria: Uh... groovy!

(The party restarts and Gandalaria closes the door.)

Cole: Sorceress, there's tons of magic stored in here. Couldn't something "abracadabra" us out?

Gandalaria: Oh, yes. Good idea. Now, if only I could remember what all this stuff is.

(Arin and Riyu walk through the Forest of Spirits. They come across a road.)

Arin: Looks like a road. Any idea which way we should go, Riyu?

(Riyu growls. The two walk onward.)

Arin: Hmm. No footprints in this direction. In the Crossroads, they had street signs everywhere. Wish someone made a sign here that said, "Evil Blood Moon Ritual Up Ahead." Anything to show us how to get there and stop Ras.

(An engine roars from behind them. They hide on the side of the road as Jordana drives by with Bonzle trapped.)

Arin: Was that the girl from Imperium? The one who hates Sora? She's got someone captured!

(Riyu barks and growls.)

Arin: You're right, she's gotta be going to the Blood Moon ritual! But we'll never-- (Riyu puts Arin on his back and takes off running.) Oop, woah! Riyu you're a fast little dragon, but there's no what you can catch up to a moving vehicle--

(Riyu speeds up and Arin yells. Riyu takes flight.)

Arin: Wow, buddy! You're really flying! This is actually happening! Yes! Let's go!

(Riyu roars.)

(We are back with Sora, Kai, and Wyldfyre as they face four Wolf Mask Warriors.)

Sora: Okay. I sense the tension here, but we don't have to fight if we just take the temperature down a notch.

Wyldfyre: Taking the temperature down is never my thing!

(She blasts a fireball at the Wolf Mask Warriors. The group begins attacking with swords, a couple climbing onto Kai's mech.)

Kai: (grunts) Like an itch I can't scratch.

(Wyldfyre and Sora fend off more of the warriors.)

Sora: Wow! Rontu's training really worked! She'd be so proud of me right now--

(A Wolf Mask Warrior hits Sora with a sword from behind. Sora yells and falls to the ground. Wyldfyre blasts the Warrior.)

Wyldfyre: Come on, Wolf wannabes! I just hung off the edge of a cliff. You don't scare me at all! The cliff didn't scare me either, by the way. (She notices a Wolf Mask Warrior on the back of Kai's mech.) Hey, Kai! I've got your back!

(She blasts the Wolf Mask Warrior.)

Kai: Ha, "You've got my back." (laughs) Nice pun!

Wyldfyre: What's a pun?

(Another Wolf Mask Warrior charges at Wyldfyre, but is kicked off the cliff by Sora.)

Wyldfyre: Dang, you got moves now, Sora! Someday you might be as good as me.

Sora: (panting) I'm getting there. I need more practice. Please don't tell Lloyd I said that.

Kai: Okay. Now we're out of danger.

(Arin and Riyu fly after Jordana, who's speeding away with Bonzle.)

Arin: Whoever she's got in there, we have to free them.

(They fly down next to the vehicle. Jordana notices them.)

Jordana: Huh?

Arin: I thought it was you! Sorry I can't remember your name!

Jordana: Ugh, I hate you ninjas so much!

(Arin jumps on the vehicle.)

Jordana: Get off! (She swerves the vehicle.)

Arin: I'd rather get in,.

(Arin lifts up the vehicle hatch. Jordana slams on the breaks and Arin flies forward, screaming. He uses his Grapple Gun to attach to the front of the car. Jordana speeds forward and Arin is flung into the passenger's seat.)

Arin: Woah!

(Jordana gasps, and the two of them wrestle for control of the vehicle.)

Jordana: What do you think you're doing? Let go of that!

(She charges up her magic, but Riyu picks her up and flings her against a rock wall before she can strike. Arin and Riyu ride off.)

Jordana: Ugh! Why can't they ever remember my name?

(Arin is driving Jordana's vehicle, with Bonzle still trapped in the front.)

Arin: Don't worry! I'm with the ninja.

Bonzle: Yeah, your outfit kinda gave that away.

Arin: Why are you in that box?

Bonzle: I just like it! Very comfortable.

Arin: Really? It doesn't look it.

Bonzle: What's your elemental power? Imperviousness to sarcasm?

(Arin gasps, seeing that they are approaching a chasm. He speeds up.)

Arin: Hold on tight!

(The vehicle launches into the air, but begins to fall mid-air. Bonzle screams, and Riyu squawks. Arin launches a hook that latches to a rock, which pulls them up along the cliff wall. Arin breaks just before crashing into stone. Arin and Bonzle pant, catching their breath.)

Bonzle: First time driving?

Arin: Believe it or not, no. Come on, lemme get you out of that box.

Bonzle: You can't. It's magically sealed.

Arin: Maybe my Spinjitzu--

Bonzle: No time! Ras needs me for his Blood Moon ritual. No me, no ritual. So we gotta get as far away from Ras and his people as we can.

Arin: On it.

(Arin takes off.)

Bonzle: (grunting) You swear you've driven before?

Arin: I used to be a top racer! But not in cars. In mechs. And I mostly sat backwards while my friend drove.

Bonzle: Oh, well that explains everything. (grunts)

Arin: But don't worry. We'll be fine.

(Kai, Wyldfyre, and Sora stand over the fallen Wolf Mask Warriors.)

Wyldfyre: What now? I wanna blast something again.

(They hear noises and see a group of Wolf Mask Warriors marching.)

Kai: More of those clowns headed to the Blood Moon. That's gotta be where the ritual's happening.

Sora: (Looks down at the Wolf Mask.) Huh. That gives me an idea.

(Later, they've fully changed into the Wolf Mask Warrior armor.)

Kai: Nice. I think we can pull this off.

(Sora observes Wyldfyre using her crutch in the armor.)

Wyldfyre: What? Maybe one of them uses a crutch.

(Sora sighs.)

Wyldfyre: Okay, whatever. I'm sure my legs gonna hurt like it always did, and I shouldn't have had to rest while you all trained. (She sets her left foot down.) Huh? It-it doesn't hurt anymore! (She walks around and jumps.) What? Are you kidding me? Rontu was right! Resting my leg actually healed it! Huh! Who knew?

Kai: Rontu? I was the first one to tell you that you should rest.

Wyldfyre: Yeah, but when a dragon said it, it turned out to be true.

(Kai gives a look of mild irritation, and Wyldfyre grunts gleefully.)

(Cole, Zane, and Gandalaria search for magic to get them out of the storagehouse. Gandalaria pulls down a drawer.)

Gandalaria: An alphabetization spell probably wouldn't do here. And morphing into a parrot... (to Cole and Zane) takers? (Both give looks of disapproval.) Eh, okay. Ah invisible shoes! No. (She throws the shoe, and it collides directly with Zane's head.) Hmm, Anti-Gravity! Oh no.

(She throws the Anti-Gravity potion behind her. It shatters, spreading the magic inside it and causing Zane and Cole to float.)

Gandalaria: Anti-Anti Gravity potion? Pass.

(She throws the Anti-Anti Gravity potion behind her. Cole and Zane yell as they fall from the air. Gandalaria throws various items behind her.)

Gandalaria: (She finds a slime substance.) Ew.

(Gandalaria throws the slime behind her, and it falls firmly onto Cole's mouth. He grunts while attempting to pull it off. Zane helps him and stretches it off, causing the two of them to fall apart at release. Cole mouths words, but no sound comes out. Zane holds the slime up to his ear.)

Cole: (Through the slime) Can anyone hear me? What happened to my voice? (Zane puts the slime back on Cole's mouth.) Get this slimy blob off--(Zane rips off the slime and Cole speaks through his own mouth again.)--my mouth! Hey, it worked! My voice is back!

(Zane throws the slime behind him. Gandalaria opens a bottle and sniffs it. Purple, sparkly mist emerges from it.)

Gandalaria: Definitely not that one.

(Zane inhales the mist that emerges from the bottle and coughs before glowing purple and growing significantly larger. Cole gasps.)

Zane: This is not a desirable outcome.

Gandalaria: Don't worry, you'll be fine.

Zane: (Glowing purple again.) Huh? (Zane shrinks into a miniscule form.) I find this destabilizing.

(Cole stammers at Gandalaria.)

Gandalaria: It'll wear off soon... I think.

Cole: But--

Gandalaria: (Holding up a potion.) Here, smell this. Does it smell like newt tail juice?

Cole: What does newt tail juice smell like? (He opens the bottle and sniffs.) Ugh, gross!

(Cole sneezes out a pink mist and transforms into a dog. He whines.)

Gandalaria: Eh, should've labeled that.

Cole: (groans) Why do you have this? How is this helpful?

Zane: But at least you are adorable.

Cole: I'm always adorable.

(Cole sneezes again, letting out more pink mist and transforming back into a human.)

Gandalaria: (Holding up a bottle.) Aha! Found it. My "Instant Opener" spel! I use it on stubborn jars of slugs with lids that won't budge even after running them under hot water-- (She trips and screams, the bottle hurtles through the room and breaks on a chest. The chest opens, revealing Grouce.)

Grouce: Hey! I'm sleeping in here. (He closes the chest.)

Cole: The good news is you have lots more of that stuff?

(Gandalaria shakes her head.)

Zane: (Shrinking) I will admit that I am out of ideas, and yet urgency is pressing.

Cole: That's it.

(Cole picks up tiny Zane and walks to the front door.)

Zane: I cannot see. Where are we going?

Cole: (He aligns Zane with the keyhole.) The perfect size! Go through the keyhole and let us out!

Zane: Ah! Now I see your logic. (Zane goes through the keyhole.) I'm through! But the vertical distance between the keyhole and the ground is impossible for me to--

(Zane yells, growing extra large once again. The front door opens.)

Cole: Yes!

(Cole and Ganalaria run outside.)

Zane: I appear to still have a problem.

Gandalaria: Really? Should have worn off by now. Well, I guess you'll just have to buy larger-sized clothes.

(At the Shadow Dojo, the Wolf Mask Warriors have arrived for the Ritual of the Blood Moon, with Sora, Kai, and Wyldfyre disguised and among them.)

Sora: It worked. We're in! But there's a lot more of them than I expected.

Wyldfyre: And they have their fancy clanging thing.

Kai: The Gong of Shattering. Looks like saving the world's gonna be even harder than we thought.

(Cole, Gandalaria, and a giant Zane run through the Forest of Spirits. They come across the anchor to the Cloud Kingdom.)

Cole: Look!

(Zane trips over a rock.)

Zane: (grunts) Flee my impact zone!

(Before he crashes, the effects of the spell wear off, and Zane is normal sized again.)

Gandalaria: Told you it would wear off!

(They hear the Cloud Kingdom rumbling as it slowly descends from the sky.)

Zane: The Cloud Kingdom! It appears to be losing altitude.

Cole: That's probably where they took Bonzle. Let's go!

(Cole and Zane use Spinjitzu to ascend the anchor.)

Gandalaria: Oh, now how am I supposed to follow them? I can't-- Hold it.

(Gandalaria takes off her hat and takes out a bottle. She throws the bottle in her hat and a yellow mist emerges from it.)

Gandalaria: It's been a while, Hatty. Let's see if you can still do your old tricks. (She hops on and catches up to Cole and Zane.) See you at the top! Whee!

(Ras overlooks the Shadow Dojo.)

Ras: Position the captives.

Cinder: (He forces the Writers of Destiny to their knees.) Move it! (chuckles) I am so gonna enjoy watching you all be sacrificed to release the Forbidden Five.

Sora: Sacrifices?

Kai: Don't love the sound of that. They need them to release the Forbidden Five, or else the ritual fails?

Wyldfyre: Sounds like you just said, "Let's blast their chains and free them."

Kai: Wyldfyre, you're always pickin' up what I'm layin' down.

Ras: (to a Wolf Mask Warrior) Initiate.

(A Wolf Mask Warrior begins drumming. In the Nether-Space, the Forbidden Five wait.)

Nokt: Finally, after long centuries, we are being called home.

(At the Shadow Dojo, Cinder approaches Ras.)

Ras: Where is Jordana? She should be here by now.

Cinder: You ask me, she's super unreliable, Lord Ras. You should have sent me after that spell instead of her.

(Ras growls. Kai, Sora, and Wyldfyre walk forward with a group of Wolf Mask Warriors.)

Sora: (to herself) Nothing to be afraid of. We got this.

Wyldfyre: Who's afraid? I'm not afraid.

Sora: I was talking to myself.

(Jordana appears, running.)

Jordana: Hey! Lord Ras!

Ras: You come here empty-handed? Where is the spell?

Jordana: It was stolen by one of those pesky ninja.

Ras: You fool!

(Ras lifts Jordana up and slams her to the ground. He proceeds to jump off into the forest.)

Cinder: (clicks tongue) Not the teacher's pet anymore, I guess.

(Jordana gives an angry groan.)

Sora: What do you think all that was?

Kai: A good distraction, let's go!

(They continue forward with the rest of the Wolf Mask Warriors.)

(Arin continues speeding away with Bonzle and Riyu, but Ras is not hot on their trail. He jumps in front of Arin's vehicle and hits the car with his hammer, toppling it over. Riyu roars and charges at Ras, but is hit against the stone wall along the road, and falls down. Arin gets out of the vehicle.)

Arin: (Charging at Ras with his Spinjitzu.) Ninja-go!

(Ras grabs Arin by the leg and whirs him to the ground. The two fight, with Ras overpowering Arin each time. Arin is thrown to the ground again.)

Arin: (Attempting to get up.) Ninja never quit-- (yells)

Ras: (He lifts Arin up.) You could be something special. Too bad the ninja hold you back.

Arin: (groans in pain) No, they don't! They're making me better!

Ras: (scoffs) Then how come you aren't any better? I stopped you as easily now as the first time we met. I was weak like you, once. (We see a glimpse of the flashback at the beginning of the episode.) My master found me and taught me the most important thing in this world. Strength.

(Ras leaps into the air, holding on to Arin. He hurtles Arin towards the ground. Arin lays still.)

Ras: (growls) Maybe one day you'll find a true master who can teach you the same.

(Ras hops into the vehicle and takes off.)

Bonzle: Let me go, weirdo!

(Arin groans in pain on the ground. From his perspective, we see two figures approaching.)

Arin: Mom? Dad?

(At the Shadow Dojo, Bonzle is being brought to the altar.)

Bonzle: (grunts) Let me out! Let me out of here!

Ras: (He looks to the Blood Moon.) The pieces are all in place, Master! Your patience and planning are finally paying off! And the true power of all the realms will be ours.

(End of episode. For more information, click here.)
