(Eagle Spire - outside)

Eris: (sits on the edge of the spire looking at a page from her diary.) Dear diary. I don't know how to say this, so... I'll just draw it! (chuckles happily after she finishes drawing a whole page with both her and Rogon's heads inside a heart. then going into the library)

(Raven Town - race track)

Razar: (happily finishes filling in a page of a book with so much stuff having to do with money and gold while chuckling happily. after he does, he day dreams away)

(Another race goes on in the track, Winzar and a gorilla sending some obstacles Eglor's way.)

Eglor: Ugh! Yuck! Woah! (dodges something and eventually stops up ahead to clear something off of Eris's speedor)

Razar: Hello my friend! You'll be happy to know we are running a sale on those souvenirs you've collected from our raven race track, yes?

Eglor: You mean.. this, trash?!

Razar: You see trash! I see commemorative keep sake

Eglor: Sorry! I'm just cleaning them off my speedor.

Razar: (flies and walks over to the speedor.) Hm.. this ride. It belongs to Eris, no?

Eglor: Well, she uses it the most but technically nothing belongs to anyone on the eagle spires.

Razar: (leans on the speedor and laughs.) You are funny my friend!

(Eglor wags his head.)

Razar: Oh! You're serious? No owners? Then how do you know who is the richest and most important, yes?

Egor: Oh. We don't think that way! our tribe is a utopian collective where everyone is equal, and private ownership is strictly forbidden

Razar: (wags his head and smashes his hands on the speedor.) HUH?!

Eglor: Everyone in our tribe is extremely fair! And we share every-thing! in our village, what's yours is mine and vice versa!

Razar: Really? Will that apply to other tribes that visit your village too?

Eglor: Uh.. I don't know. That's never been an issue before since no one ever flies up to our spires. (returns to the track)

Razar: Until now...

(Skies)

(Multiple ravens fly their jets to the Eagle Spires)

Razar: To the treasure spires! I mean... the eagle spires!

(Crocodile Swamp - inside)

Laval: It's been a long time since I've been inside your home, Cragger.

Cragger: I know! Isn't it cozy?

Laval: Uh... yeah... well, the important thing is, we're friends again.

Cragger: I know what you mean Laval. We should celebrate, do something fun!

Laval: How about a race?

(Crocodile Swamp - outside)

(Laval and Cragger race each other on boats in the swamp)

Laval: (chuckles)

Crooler: (peeks from a bush and goes back to her garden of tiny persuader plants.) Huh?! (sighs.) I can't believe I used every single one of those persuader plants on Cragger. (pulls one out.) and none of them are blooming again. (blows out gas.) They're not completely dead though. Ugh! If only there was someone in Chima who was really good with flowers.

(Gorilla Fores - gorilla homes)

Gorzan: (sniffs a beautiful flower while lying on the ground.) I remember when you was just tiny little seeds flower dude. Nobody said you'd grow, but I cared for you and I believed in you, and now you make the whole world smile!

(Somehow, the flower kisses him)

Gorzan: Aw! I love you too flowers!

(A sudden darkness comes on the flower, making it a bit dead)

Gorzan: Huh? (looks up)

Crooler: How sweet. (shows her persuader plant.) If only I can get my precious plants to grow too.

Gorzan: (gasps and stands up.) You! BUMMER ALERT! (beats his chest while hooting, retreating back to the houses)

Gorillas: (hooting while they look outside)

Crooler: Oh come on! I'm not that bad.

G'Loona: (goes to look at Crooler.) The last time I helped you. I got kidnapped and locked outside with a bunch of psycho wolves! (is held back by Gorzan)

Crooler: (hides her plant.) Oh, that. Well, I turned over a new leaf. Actually, a new stock! (shows the plant.) And I need a truly gifted gardener to help me nurture this poor stock, back to health... So, what do you say?

Gorzan: (growls but gets surprised of something. he retreats into a house)

Gorillas: (whispering amongst each other)

Crooler: Y'know. I can hear you.

(Eagle Spires - Library)

(The ravens into the library, Ewald and Elkar reading peacefully.)

Ewald: Ravens? May I help you?

Razar: Yes! We'd like to have a word with this building's owner my friend.

Ewald: (closes his book.) Huh? I-I don't understand. No one really, "owns" our library.

Elkar: It belongs to everyone.

Razar: Hear that ravens? We're "everyone" too! And we're here to claim OUR property!

(The ravens make a mess of everything)

Razar: Grab as much as you can ravens!

Ewald: (gasps but Razar steals his book from behind so he reaches for it.) Hey! That's my book!

Razar: No it's not. It belongs to everyone, yes? (pushes Ewald off of the floating shelf.) And this "everyone", is taking his stuff back.

(Crocodile Swamp - outside)

Laval and Cragger: (they reach shore.) I WIN! (laughter)

Laval: Let's call it a tie ya mud lover!

Cragger: Sure thing fur boy! rematch?

Laval: How about a speedor ride instead?

(Forest)

(The two friends race each other through the forest.)

(Beaver Lodge)

(While the beavers work, Laval and Cragger wave at them riding past. Belkar giving a quick wave before he joins the ride.)

(Bear Village)

Bladvic: (snoring but he gets woken up by the riding crew and he waves at them)

(Bladvic, Rogon and Skinnit join the ride.)

(Eagle Spire - library)

Eris: (walks into a really messy library.) HEY! Leave our stuff alone!

Razar: Your stuff? (walks to her.) I heard it belonged to everyone, yes?

Eris: (walks down the rest of the stairs.) Everyone that respects our rules!

Razar: Okay! But can I see a copy of these rules? Just so we don't break any more of them.

Eris: (struggles for words.) T-the rules! They're not exactly written down!.. everyone here just knows them.

Razar: Hey! I'm here and I'm everyone too, yes? So that means, I know the rules! And as far as I can tell my friend. We ravens are not breaking any of them. (walks away.) See ya!

Eris: (struggles for words.) I!.. YOU!.. AND!.. (looks at Elkar and Ewald, shrugging)

(The two eagles shrug back)

(Gorilla Forest - gorilla homes)

Crooler: Last chance to help me fix this poor plant! (looks to a fire.) Or else, I BURN IT!

Gorzan: *almost goes down but is held back.* NO! That poor plant! Not happy!

Grumlo: Embrace the mallow brother G! The bummer croc only means to harm us.

Crooler: Fine. Let the barbeque begin! (throws the plant into the fire)

Gorzan: (gasps)

(The plant begins to burn, but a strange purple mist rises from the ashes and spreads, catching Crooler.)

Crooler: (yawns.) Tired... (lies down.) I think I will lie down for a while... (strangely, she's turned into a more realistic warrior with a red colour, it looking a bit like the animation someone lets out when plugging a chi.) What?.. What's happening here? I, feel so, ALIVE! (runs forward and cuts down multiple trees.) SO, POWERFUL! (she laughs, but it's shown to be all a dream when that laughter is heard coming from her sleep)

Gorillas: (hooting in curiosity)

Gorzan: Dudes. She's sleep laughing.

Gorilla: (laughing to the point they even shake their houses)

Crooler: I now know my true destiny: to rule Chima! and destroy you all! (laughter; sleep talking) Destroy you all...

Gorillas: (laughter)

(The crew rides past Crooler.)

Cragger: (stops and looks at Crooler.) Hey! Crooler! Are you okay?

Crooler: (sleep talking; laughter)

Gorzan: (whispering) *comes down hanging from a vine.* Don't wake her dudes! She's having too much fun!

Crooler: (sleep talking; laughter)

(Laval and Cragger shrug in confusion. going back to their ride, Gorzan even joining in.)

Crooler: (wakes up and looks at herself.) The power... W-what happened?...

Gorillas: (laughing)

Crooler: (growls, picking up her dead persuader plant and thinking in confusion)

(Eagle Spire - parking area)

(The ravens finish packing up nets with the eagle's stuff. Razar taking off with a jet carrying the first load.)

Eris: (stops him with a jet.) Last chance raven! drop our stuff! Or else!

Razar: Okay! We'll drop it!.. (backs up too far and drops two nets)

Eris: (gasps)

Ewald and Elkar: (gasps)

(Eagle Spire - underneath)

Ravens: (Ripnik and some others crow while they use shopping carts to catch all the items falling)

(Eagle Spire - parking area)

(Eris starts shooting at the enemy jets.)

Razar: Watch the merchandise!

Eris: (is grabbed by the claws of two raven jets and is dragged away.) Woah! (screaming while she falls from being thrown away)

(Multiple eagles run to some jets so they can help Eris.)

Eagle 1: COME ON!

Eagle 2: Eris is in trouble!

Eagle 3: Protect the spires!

Eagle 4: (screech)

(Four eagles in jets fly right through two raven jets.)

Raven 1: (is grabbed from above by an eagle jet and is thrown right into the spire's wall.) OUCH!

Eglor: (screeches while he glides down to attack a raven jet, but the raven turns upside down so their two jet's claws clash against each other)

Raven 2: (growls at Eglor)

Eglor: (growls at the raven and luckily pushes him into another jet, laughing at his victory but sees Ewald getting chased.) Huh? (goes to help and thankfully flys into the two enemy jets and sends them flying)

Eagles: (cheering)

Ewald: Hooray! We have vanquished our foe! (sees the fleet of ravens go back to the spire)

Ravens: (crowing)

Eglor: Hey! What are you doing?!

Razar: (goes back for the library with the ravens.) Retreating! To our new home.

Elkar: In our library!

Eris: But... my diaries in there!

Ewald: Alas. I spoke too soon...

(The ravens run inside, Razcal shutting the door)

(Forest)

(Laval and everyone else continue their ride through Chima)

Dom De La Woosh: *approaches in front.* Dom De La Woosh! He has arrived!

Laval: Chima's greatest speedor rider ever?!

Cragger: Cruising with us?!

Dom De La Woosh: Yes. The woosh, he loves the party line. There are speedorz! (stands on his speedor.) There are racers! And now there is... The Woosh! (speeds up and does multiple tricks on his speedor while laughing)

Laval, Cragger, Bezar, Bladvic, Skinnit and Rogon: (chuckles of being impressed)

(Lion Temple - outside)

*Lagravis and Longtooth watch as the riders drive past*

Laval, Cragger, Bezar, Bladvic, Skinnit and Rogon: (laughing while they follow Dom De La Woosh and do some tricks of their own)

Lagravis: Huh?.. What are those kids doing?

(Eagle Spire - library)

*a raven walks past Razar with a stack of books*

Razar: *holds a chain and sighs.* Just like home.

(Eagle Spire - outside)

Ewald: We have to get them out of there!

Eris: I know! But our tribe's are too evenly matched to beat in battle.

Elgor: Usually, we have the advantage because we can fly! not this time.

Ewald: Perhaps our allies could assist?

Eris: It's too dangerous to bring them up here! We'll spend most of our time trying to keep them from falling off our spires!

Ewald: HOPELESS! ALL MY PRECIOUS BOOKS!

Eris: And my diary... but.. I think we can defeat them, by exploiting the one thing they can't control!

Elgor and Ewald: Huh?!

(Eagle Spire - library)

Razar: *sighs while he uses Eris's diary as something to lean on.* Pretty fancy things, no?

Razcal: Views okay I suppose! But there's not a lot to do here, is there?..

Razar: Of course there is my friend! We just have to do what we always do! Real and deal with the locals, yes?

Eris: *knocking.* Hello? Anyone home?

Razar: *flies to the door.* And there's our first sucker! I mean... valued customer, now! *opens the door.* Welcome my friend! You are just in time for our grand opening clearance sale!

Eris: Huh?! *sees her diary*

Razar: How much will you pay for these one of a kind items?

Eris: Actually! Since everything belongs to everyone up here, we eagles never engage in economic activity on our spires.

Razar: WHAT?!

Eris: Sure. We'll buy and sell stuff at the chi day market in the lion's city, but up here! No shopping is needed! Or allowed! it's in our rules, which everyone knows!

Razar: Oh, yes... of course! I'm everyone too so I should know that!... just one moment please. (shuts the door.) I... think... I... how can they live like that?!

Razcal: How can WE live like that?!

Razar: We'll just find some other suckers.

Razcal: Who?! No one ever comes up here! our only customers are eagles, WHO DON'T BELIEVE IN SHOPPING!

(knocking.)

Razar: *opens the door.* What is it now?

Eris: I really just came by to ask if we eagles could use your old race track? that place is so much fun!

Razar: Heh. Yes!.. Of course!.. But it will cost you my friend.

Eris: Don't worry! We'll just leave some treasure for you, down there! Or will we?

Razar: WHAT?! YOU CAN'T USE OUR TRACK FOR FREE!

Eris: HEY! How will you know what happens down there, when you're all up here? *goes away.* See ya!

Razar: N-no! *hops out the door.* No! STOP!

(Forest)

*to make a guest appearance, ShadoWind appears before the riders*

Laval: ShadoWind?!

Dom De La Woosh: ah! yes! the woosh is not only the greatest, handsomest and super fantastic rider in Chima! he is also the most humble. the woosh yields the lead to.. The ShadoWind.

*ShadoWind takes the front and goes left*

Laval: woah! switch gears!

(The riders follow the mysterious rider.)

(Spiral Mountain)

(ShadoWind leads Cragger and Laval up Spiral Mountain but when the two reach the top, he's not there.)

Cragger: hey! where'd he go?!

Laval: I don't know! I thought that road was the only way up or down!

Cragger: *goes to look down from the edge.* did he?...

Laval: no one could make a jump like that!

Cragger: *his mouth drops when he sees ShadoWind standing on a rock in the distance.* ShadoWind did! and so can we, come on!

Laval: hold on! what about the others?

*everyone else picks up speed and almost makes it up to the top*

Laval: *points.* and what about that?

*the view from there is shown to be amazing*

Cragger: woah

*everyone joins the two to look at the amazing view*

(Eagle Spire - parking area)

Razcal: *goes to put an item in a net filled with others but turns around to Razar.* maybe taking over the eagle spires wasn't such a good idea after all!

Razar: *walks up to him.* WHAT?! but you can't just leave!

Razcal: we miss our race track! and our customers! *looks to the ravens.* and our junk!

Ravens: *crowing in agreement*

Razar: but what about all the eagles stuff?! we must be able to sell some of it!

(Roads of Chima)

Ripnik: *shows a not so interested audience some of the items.* big sale! 50% off everything! just look at all this! weird. I mean! wonderful merchandise! *picks up an item.* doesn't everyone need one of these? *looks to see what it is.* whatever it is!

*no one is interested, or even around at that matter*

Ripnik: did I say 50% off? *writes.* I meant 90% off! how about.. buy one, get two free? or maybe, ten free? how about, it's just free? period! *sighs and looks back at the item, bowing his head*

Crooler: *approaches.* I had the strangest day! there was this plant! and then a fog! and for a moment, I ruled it all.

Ripnik: sorry Crooler!.. I wasn't listening. but, do you wanna buy something?..

Crooler: huh? *looks at the items.* the only ones interested in this junk are those boring eagles.

Ripnik: I know! *throws into a pile multiple items.* I can't sell anything! I give up! just what you want! *pushes a giant cart away*

Crooler: *approaches a book.* but what's this? *opens the book.* a guide to exotic plants of Chima? *looks at her plant.* oh. SO THESE ROOTS HAVE EVEN MORE POWER THEN THE FLOWER?! *closes the book.* oh, whether he likes it or not, Cragger is going to love this plant. *laughter*

(Raven Town - race track)

eagles: *Ewald, Eglor and a random laugh while they continue through the track*

*Rawzom and Razcal approach the race track with their speedorz. only them two stayed on the side lines while one raven went to go race*

Razcal: ah! I really missed this place! so pretty, and profitable! hope Razar gets here soon! *enters the race with Rawzom*

(Eagle Spire - library - dawn)

Eris: *walks into a totally messy library, Razar landing on a floating book shelf.* huh?! Razar? didn't you hear? the take overs over! everyone's back at your track having a great time!

Razar: I have never gone an entire without making a profit my friend! and I am not starting now, yes?

Eris: but I already brought back all our stuff! Ripnik couldn't even give it away. except for some old plant book that no one ever read.

Razar: there must be one thing I can sell here! *shows Eris's diary.* perhaps this weird diary.

Eris: *gasps in fear.* HEY! THAT'S MINE! GIVE IT BACK!

Razar: yours? really? but I thought nothing belonged to anyone here

Eris: *struggles for words in fear*

Razar: I guess the eagle rules don't apply to this book! so in that case, how much will you pay me for it?

Eris: UGH! *throws her axe at Razar*

Razar: WOAH! that much?

Eris: *flies over to him and tries to grab him, but Razar dodges.* hand it over! that book's PRIVATE!

Razar: *lands on another shelf.* Then why'd you put it in a library?

Eris: Because that's where our best books go! and no eagle will ever read something so personal!

Razar: But I am not an eagle. *opens the diary.* all though! *shows the page Eris really didn't want him to see.* a certain RHINO maybe very interested in this latest entry!

Eris: *laughs awkwardly.* oh! that, doesn't mean anything! it's just some doodle!

Razar: looks a bit like you and Rogon?

Eris: oh! yeah? sure!.. Rogon.. y'know him? he's just, so.. big. *chuckles in a lovey dovey way.* and strong! and nice! ooh! and so fimple.. and... I-I mean! it's nothing! *flies to Razar and tries to grab him again.* now give me back my diary! *chases Razar*

Razar: *chuckles while he flies away*

Eris: *is hit by the diary all the way into another shelf.* OW!

Razar: *lands on another shelf and chuckles*

Eris: *screech*

Razar: *turns his head.* huh?! *sees Eris with some books and teases her by jumping around while laughing*

Eris: HA! *throws a book*

Razar: *dodges the book and is fired at with a barrage of more books, making him flee to the giant hole in the wall.* if you won't buy this book from me.. perhaps Rogon will! *flies away to find Rogon*

Eris: *flies after him.* OH NO YOU DON'T! *sees Razar get into his jet, so she flies over with a screech and hangs onto the claws of the jet as it takes off*

Razar: *chuckles*

Eris: GIVE ME BACK MY DIARY!

Razar: huh? *flies faster*

Eris: *screams as she tries to hold on*

(Spiral Mountain - dawn)

*the crew continues to stare at the sunset*

Laval: *sighs.* I think this has been one of the best days we've ever had

*Plovar begins pecking Cragger's teeth*

Cragger: no argument here! great jo on my teeth by the way!

Plovar: *stops.* what's that Craggy Wag? a compliment? you never say nice things to me. I feel so, happy

Laval: don't well all? y'know! now that we're all getting along so well, we should make this official! with some kind of, best friends forever treaty of peace! sort of thing.

Cragger: I like the sound of that! let's sigh something next week! we'll vow to never fight again, alright?

Dom De La Woosh, Laval, Cragger, Bezar, Bladvic, Skinnit and Rogon: *cheering*

Laval: wow! the only thing that'd make this more perfect, was if ALL our friends were here. including Eris

Cragger: yeah! but I even miss that thrifty shifty Razar a little bit!

(sky - dawn)

Razar: *looks behind and laughs to see Eris not there anymore, but when he looks back Eris appears right beside him.* huh?!

Eris: *tries her best to pull her diary out of Razar's hand but ends up pulling Razar out of the driver's seat*

Razar and Eris: *screaming while they fall*

(Spiral Mountain - dawn)

*the two make a giant bang on the top of Spiral Mountain giving everyone a fright. Eris pinning Razar down*

Laval: ey! where'd you guys come from? we were just talking about you!

Cragger: yeah! it's so great you can drop in like that!

*the two look at each other awkwardly*

Razar: (whispering) Uh... awkward!

Eris: (whispering) Yeah! I know!

Razar: (whispering) How about this my friend? I'll give back your diary if you save nothing of my failed business on the spires! Making no profits is very embarrassing for a raven!

Eris: (whispering) *stands up.* Sure! As long as you NEVER EVER come to our eagle homes again!

Razar: (whispering) *stands up.* Deal! *gives her back her diary*

Rogon: Hey! What's in the book?

Eris: *keeps it close.* Oh! Uh!.. nothing!

Razar: Uhhhh...

(Eris looks at him with a scared face.)

Razar: Just some doodles! nothing you'd be interested in.

(They join the group in looking at the sunset.)
