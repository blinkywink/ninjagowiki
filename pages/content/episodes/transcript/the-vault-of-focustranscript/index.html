(A flashback shows Cole with the Finders in the Land of Lost Things.)

Past Fritz: (He laughs.) Yes! Looks like a geyser just gone off! Fresh salvage! Wait till you see all this, Cole! (He and Spitz separated.)

Past Cole: So there's junk everywhere in the Land of Lost Things, but you're excited about this particular junk?

(Spitz returns holding a cart wheel.)

Past Spitz: We're in Sector Four! Lots of quality gear spews out in Sector Four. You've got a lot to learn, newbie.

(Spitz walks away, Cole laughs, and then he and Geo look at each other. Fritz continues rummaging through the junk and finds something.)

Past Fritz: (He gasps.) A new book! Boobytraps and How to Survive Them. Huh, people sure seem to lose a lot of copies of these. (He throws the book away.)

Past Spitz: Cool! Look what I found!

(Spitz appears wearing a Yeti costume and roaring, scaring Fritz, who turns into a crow and flies to Geo and Cole.)

Past Fritz: (He returns to normal.) No! No, no, no, no! It's back, it's back! (He gasps and sobs.)

Past Spitz: Fritz! (He takes off his costume.) I'm sorry, I forgot! (He runs to them.)

Past Geo: Fritz's village was attacked by a monster. (Bonzle runs to them.) It's how he ended up here.

Past Cole: Fritz, it's okay. (Fritz cries.) Spitz didn't mean it. You're safe.

Past Fritz: (He sighs and stops crying.) I am?

Past Cole: Sure. You'll always be safe with me. All of you.

(The Finders smile and Cole smiles back.)

Kai's voice: Cole?

(The flashback ends and Cole appears, in the Ninja Combat Vehicle jet, looking sad.)

Kai: Cole? Cole! Are you still with us?

Cole: The trail's leading straight to Imperium. Come on!

(Frak, Cole, and Kai approach Imperium, combine their vehicles back into the Ninja Combat Vehicle, and enter Imperium, which has its citizens panicking and going hollow. Kai and Cole look through their Prismatic Blades and see the Spectral Dragonians extracting the souls of the Imperians.)

Cole: Where's Nokt? He's the one behind this, and we gotta get the Finders back to normal.

Frak: I don't see him anywhere!

Percival: Everyone! Get to cover! There's too many of them! Run!

(Frak looks through his Prismatic Blade and sees the Spectral Dragonians going after Percival and others. The Ninja Combat Vehicle moves forward and splits into three vehicles again, while Percival sees the Spectral Dragonians advancing on him through his goggles.)

(In the City of Temples, the Ninja and Jay see that Roby is hollow, horrifying Wyldfyre.)

Wyldfyre: No! (She runs to Roby.) Roby, talk to me! (She roars affectionately.)

Roby: Womp, womp...

Lloyd: (He puts his sword on his back.) Just like all the people in the Land of Lee.

(Nya puts her spear on her back.)

Jay: Yeah, but with this guy, being hollow really improves his personality, am I right?

(Jay's comment angers Wyldfyre and she fires Heat at him and nearly hits him.)

Wyldfyre: Don't insult my boyfriend!

Jay: (He puts his Lightning Daggers in his pocket.) Hold up, you actually, unironically like that guy? For real? With all his "stangs" and his "zoots" and his "womp womp", he’s like if the sound of an airhorn became a person.

Wyldfyre: (She grabs Jay's right shoulder.) That beautiful genius is worth more than ten of you, you goodness shattered freak with bad breath!

(Jay feels his own breath and sees that Wyldfyre is right, then stares at her until Lloyd separates them.)

Lloyd: Stop this! Roby being hollow also means we can't learn how to stop Thunderfang and his Spectral Invasions.

(Arin and Sora look to the side and see two Security Bots approaching.)

Arin: Maybe they could help?

Sora: The Security Bots! Of course! No souls to drains.

Lloyd: (To a Security Bot.) Excuse us, we need informations on the ancient battle between--

Security Bot: The lore of the City of Temples cannot be granted to the public without an appropriate order.

Lloyd: Okay. I order you to tell us about--

Security Bot: Order rejected. Unauthorized user. Please apply to Games Master Roby for authorization.

Roby: Womp, womp...

Nya: That's not happening any time soon.

Sora: So this is just a dead end?

Wyldfyre: There's still one person who can tell us what we need to know, if he hasn't been hollowed out, too.

Arin: Who?

Wyldfyre: You won't like it. Come on. (Arin and Sora look at each other.) We're going to the dungeons.

(A Spectral Dragonian nearly attacks Percival until Kai saves him by hitting the Spectral Dragonian with his Prismatic Blade while doing a backflip with his bike.)

Percival: The Red Ninja? (He lifts the lenses of his glasses.) Thank the Sources!

(Frak gets off his bike and tries to hit the Spectral Dragonians with his Prismatic Blade.)

Frak: I'm gonna mess you goons up. (He keeps trying to land a hit on them.) Hey'ah!

Percival: And you brought... some guy? (He lowers the lenses of his glasses back down and sees two Spectral Dragonians standing next to Frak.) A little to the left! No, uh, your left. Okay, more... more... Almost.

(Cole exits his jet and jumps to attack the Spectral Dragonians.)

Frak: I definitely hit some too.

Cole: I'm sure.

(Percival looks to the side and notices the Prismatic Blade in Kai's hand.)

Percival: Prismatic Steel. Like the lenses in my googles. (He runs to Kai.) Can we use those?

Kai: I'm kinda in the middle of using this, Percival.

Percival: No, we need--

(Everyone looks up and sees Nokt sucking the souls into his magical flute.)

Cole: The souls of the people they're draining are going in that thing! That must be the key to saving the Finders!

(Nokt continues sucking souls.)

(The Ninja and Jay go down to the dungeons.)

Jay: (He groans.) Ugh... This walking tour gets negative five stars. Do not recommend.

Nya: Could you just tell us why we're here, Wyldfyre?

Wyldfyre: To talk to the traitor.

Arin: Roby's Uncle Bleckt?

Wyldfyre: The old jerk was Mr. Ledger-meister or whatever. If anyone knows what we need to know, it's him.

Jay: Oh, wait, so the plan is trust the guy who made a deal with Ras to backstab his own nephew? (He laughs derisively.) This'll go great.

(Several Security Bots appear in front of them.)

Security Bot: Halt. The prisoner is not allowed unauthorized visitors.

Nya: This is an emergency. The fate of the Merged Lands hangs in the balance.

Security Bot: Access denied by order of the Games Master.

Arin: Guess they only listen to Roby.

(Jay takes a small step forward and ignites his Lightning in his hand.)

Jay: (To Security Bot.) You heard the lady. Back away!

Security Bot: Negative. Unauthorized user.

Jay: Fine. Then eat Lightning! (He fires Lightning at the Security Bots, but it has no effect.)

Security Bot: Negative. We are not hungry.

Sora: (To Jay.) These bots were designed as security for the Tournament of the Sources. Elemental Powers aren't gonna work on them.

Jay: Let's see if feet work. (He jumps forward.)

(Wyldfyre smiles at Jay's attitude.)

LLoyd: No, wait!

(The security robot in front blocks Jay's kick.)

Security Bot: Alert! We are under attack! Defend and apprehend!

(The Ninja and Jay face the Security Bots. Two of them grab Wyldfyre.)

Wyldfyre: Hey! Back off! (The bots stop fighting.) Hey, bots... do jumping jacks. (The bots gather together and do jumping jacks.) Now dance a jig. (The bots dance a jig.) What's the deal here?

Security Bot: Games Master Roby instructed us that should he ever become incapacitated that User Wyldfyre is to be afforded all-access authority.

Wyldfyre: I'm his backup contact? Oh, Roby, you cutie! (She spins with joy.) Hmm, so I basically command my own city now? Interesting. Interesting...

(Jay shakes his head no.)

Jay: Romantic relationships make no sense to me. Especially this romantic relationship.

(Jay's comment irritates Sora, Arin, Nya, and Lloyd.)

Lloyd: Wyldfyre, we're still on a mission. Get the bots to take us to Bleckt.

Wyldfyre: Of course.

(Bleckt is reading The Saga of the Eternal Sources in his cell when Wyldfyre, sitting in a chair carried by the robots while holding grapes in her hands, enters.)

Wyldfyre: These are seedless, right? Roby would want me to have seedless. (She eats a grape.)

(Bleckt stops reading when he notices the Ninja and Jay entering.)

Nya: I knew it'd go to her head, just not this fast.

Bleckt: To what do I owe this... "honor," hmm? Come to gloat about my fall from grace?

Lloyd: We're here about the invasion.

Bleckt: Invasion?

Jay: You don't know?

Bleckt: The isolation of this cell has put a knowledge of history even more firmly at my command, but current events somewhat elude me.

(A bot cools Wyldfyre by fanning a fan.)

Wyldfyre: Faster. There's too much hot air in here.

(The bot waves the fan harder.)

Nya: The Merged Lands are being attacked. Spectral beings are hollowing people out. They got Roby and countless other so far.

Bleckt: I've read of such Spectral Invasions, but never imagined it could actually happen.

Arin: There's this dragon, Thunderfang. We stopped him once, and he died, but that didn't slow him down as much as you'd expect.

Bleckt: There are some things that only grow stronger in the rot of death. I'm not surprised that blasphemous old lizard is one of them. He tried to become a Source Dragon. Such unthinkable heresy is beyond evil.

Lloyd: He was once defeated here in the City of Temples, right? We need you to teach us how it was done.

Bleckt: I can not only tell you, I can show you, but not from this cell.

Jay: Whoa, whoa, whoa, what are you trying to pull? Professor Pompous here knows all the secrets passages in this place. As soon as our backs are turned, he's gonna bolt and turn on the booby traps or something.

Nya: Bleckt might be a villain, but he loves the Source Dragons. He understands the stakes here better than anyone.

Wyldfyre: Stakes, schmakes! We can't free this clown after he tried to get rid of Roby.

Jay: Her logic is terrible, but her conclusion is right. You can't let him out. He looks frail, but his knowledge makes him dangerous!

Sora: More dangerous than what's out there already?

Arin: I agree with Sora. I vote we take the risk.

Lloyd: It's not really a voting situation. There's only one person here with the authority to free a prisoner. Your call, Wyldfyre. What's it going to be?

(Wyldfyre begins to think.)

(Nokt continues playing his magical flute and sucking souls.)

Cole: I'm taking him down! (He tries to go to Nokt, but is stopped by Kai.)

Kai: You can't just run off. Nokt's one of the most dangerous of the Forbidden Five. Remember what he did to me?

Cole: (He takes off his mask.) I need to get the Finders' souls out of that... thing he's playing!

Frak: How? (He takes off his hood.) Even our Prismatic Blades barely hurt these creatures.

Percival: That's what I was trying to say! When we learned what was happening in other lands, these weird things attacking, the Advanced Science Lab designed new Prismatic Blades to eleminate them permanently.

Kai: Then why aren't we using them?

Percival: They're designed, not built. We need Prismatic Steel forged with soul energy, and we only had barely enough regular Prismatic Ore for my lenses here. But your blades! We can upgrade them and stop these things!

Kai: Then what are we waiting for?

Cole: We don't have time to run off to some lab. Nokt is right there! (He starts walking.)

Kai: Cole, stop. The hot-headed vibe is usually my thing. Think this through.

Cole: You don't understand. I should've protected the Finders.

Kai: You're letting your emotions takeover, and--

Cole: You're not my teacher, Kai! Protect Percival and get your blades upgraded. (He enters into the jet and flies towards Nokt.)

Kai: Let's get this done. Fast.

(Kai and Frak get on their bikes, Percival gets on Kai's bike behind him and they go to the laboratory.)

(The Ninja and Jay continue to wait for Wyldfyre's decision.)

Wyldfyre: I've decided... that I need a shoulder massage to help me decide.

(A bot flies up to Wyldfyre and massages her shoulders, annoying the other Ninja and Jay.)

Lloyd: Wyldfyre...

Wyldfyre: Okay, fine! As Acting President, Prime Minister, Boss of the City of Temples, I've come to a decision. Release Bleckt.

(A bot turns a crank and opens Bleckt's cell.)

Jay: Don't even think about running.

Bleckt: I never run. I walk in a stately manner. Follow me. (He walks and the scene changes to the Temple City Museum.) The secrets you need are kept hidden in the Vault of Focus. To enter the vault, I must speak the pass-phrase. Turn around and cover your ears.

Jay: Yeah, right. We turn, you run.

Wyldfyre: We're not taking our eyes off you. Make with the pass-phrase, Bleckty.

Bleckt: (He grumbles.) I hereby acknowledge that Roby is so dope that I can't even... Zoot-zoot.

(Sora, Wyldfyre, and Arin find this funny, especially Wyldfyre, who laughs. The statue rises and a pole appears below it. Bleckt grabs the pole and climbs down.)

Jay: Hmm, probably a trap. (Lloyd approaches the pole.) Next one down that pole is a goner. Your funeral, Greenie.

(Lloyd grabs the pole and climbs down. The others approach the pole.)

Jay: Oh, well, it was nice knowing him. I mean, not really. (He chuckles.) So neurotic.

Lloyd: Ninja! It's okay, I'm safe! But, well, it's a lot. Come see.

(They climb down the pole to the Vault of Focus.)

Sora: Is this all built with...

Bleckt: Prismatic Steel. The City of Temples sits on a deposit of raw ore. It was mixed with soul energy to create the chains for Thunderfang, the Shatter Dragon of Chaos. Chaos is an almost unstoppable force, (An image of Thundefang on the wall is shown.) but it has a natural enemy... (An image of the Arc Dragon of Focus on the wall is shown.) Focus. (He hits a pillar nearby and makes the lines on the wall glow.)

Nya: That's the Source Dragon of Focus?

Bleckt: No. The Arc Dragon of Focus, a mortal avatar of the Focus Source Dragon. Only the Council of Sources can vote to release it.

Lloyd: We can't count on the vote going our way this time. I have reason to believe the Source Dragons are deadlocked about helping mortals.

Bleck: If that's true, then chaos will reign.

Lloyd: But I've heard there's a way for mortals to wake the Arc Dragon of Focus.

Bleckt: There might be, but the consequences could be disastrous.

Wyldfyre: We never back down from disastrous consequences.

Arin: Even when we really should.

Bleckt: Summoning the Source Dragons' champion without their permission could mark you as an enemy of the Sources.

Lloyd: But if we don't, Thunderfang could destroy the Source Dragons and the Merged Lands.

Bleckt: If you're going to risk such an action, you will need this... (He hits the pillar again, causing the wall to split in two and slide aside, revealing a glowing stone.) The Unbidden Storm.

(Everyone except Jay is surprised.)

(Nokt continues playing his magical flute, sucking people's souls and hollowing them out, until he turns to the side and dodges shots from Cole, who is in the jet flying furiously towards him. He fires more, but Nokt dodges and flies towards Cole, passes over the jet and fires Brute Force balls, damaging the jet and causing it to crash, but Cole manages to turn it back on in time and continues his fight with Nokt.)

(In the Advanced Systems Lab, a scientist places Kai and Frak's Prismatic Blades inside a machine and another scientist works on the holographic control panel.)

Kai: (To the scientist.) Hurry, Cole's out there alone!

(As the scientist works at the panel, an energy bar is nearly full and the machine is activated.)

Bleckt: It has never been done, but when the Arc Dragon of Focus is inactive, it rests the two halves of itself in two caves. Each must be awakened separately by a spell-song, this song-- (he hits the pillar again and makes the bells on top of the walls ring.) Exactly note by note. (Sora moves her head to the rhythm of the music.) Then, once they're awake, the Unbidden Storm must be brought to the place where the Arc Dragon is to be summoned... in this case, (He points forward.) as close to Thunderfang's source of power as possible... (Everyone turns to see where he's pointing and another image of Thunderfang is shown on another wall.) but such actions have never been attempted by mortals before.

Lloyd: It's the only shot we have. Where are the two halves of the dragon?

Bleckt: (He points to the side.) One half is in Mysterium's Cave of Bells, (They look to the side he pointed and see an image of one of the dragon halves in Mysterium.) but the other half, I'm sorry to tell you, (He points to the other side.) was in Djinjago, (They look to the side he pointed and see an image of the other half of the dragon in Djinjago.) which has been destroyed.

Sora: Not anymore. It's back.

Bleckt: Thunderfang and Djinjago are back? No one tells me anything!

Nya: But Djinjago is only a sliver of its former self.

Sora: So maybe the cave where this thing sleeps isn't even there yet?

Jay: Even if you do wake up both halves, then what? March right into the Spectral Lands to summon it? You'd be eviscerated at the border!

Wyldfyre: (She sighs.) Meany-breath has a point. The last time we entered the Spectral Lands, you barely escaped alive. I mean, I was fine, but you all? (She scoffs.)

Sora: I might have an idea about that. Anyone want to visit the junkyard?

(The machine finishes upgrading the Prismatic Blades into Reveal Blades and the scientist takes them out. Kai takes the Reveal Blades and hands one of them to Frak.)

Kai: Time for a field test!

(Cole and Nokt continue shooting at each other until Nokt uses Shatterspin to block Cole's shots and unleash an energy wave at him, but he jumps out of the jet before being hit and performs Spinjitzu in the air while Nokt stops his Shatterspin and smiles.)

Cole: (He stops the Spinjitzu.) This is for the Finders! (He wields his Prismatic Blade and attacks Nokt as he falls.)

(Kai and Frak arrive on their bikes and use their Reveal Blades to make the Spectral Dragonians visible.)

Kai: There they are!

(Two Spectral Dragonians grab Cole before he can attack Nokt.)

Kai: COLE!!!

(Cole is hollowed out and Nokt extracts his soul into his magical flute. The Spectral Dragonians release Cole and let him fall. Frak puts his Revealing Blade on his back, revs his motorcycle, jumps off, and manages to catch Cole before he hits the ground. Kai approaches them.)

Kai: Is he...?

Frak: Alive, but... he's hollow.

Kai: (He sighs.) Cole, why didn't you listen?

(The Spectral Dragonians attack them, then Kai jumps in, attacks one of them with his Revealing Blade and destroys it.)

Kai: It works!

(Kai and Frak use their Reveal Blades to destroy more Spectral Dragonians, drawing Nokt's attention and causing him to stop playing his magical flute. The Spectral Dragonians retreat, then Nokt dives towards Kai and Frak and fires a Brute Force ball at Kai, who dodges.)

Kai: Nokt! Your music's terrible!

(Kai hurls his Reveal Blade at Nokt's magical flute, releasing most of the souls he stole, and the Reveal Blade returns to Kai's hand. Nokt and the Spectral Dragonians leave, and Kai checks on Cole, who remains hollow. The Imperians stop hiding and approach them.)

Percival: Citizens of Imperium! Thanks to the Ninja, once again, our city is free!

(The Imperians celebrate until they see that some others remain hollow.)

Percival: (To Kai.) You did it!

Kai: But look at the cost.

(Everyone watches Cole walk away, soulless.)

(Nya, Lloyd, Bleckt, Jay, and Wyldfyre are waiting for Arin and Sora.)

Jay: Whatever this Sora kid's up to better be worth it.

(Bleckt looks at the horizon.)

Lloyd: What are you looking at, Bleckt?

Bleckt: If Thunderfang has gathered enough souls to attempt to become a Source Dragon, the immense power will be visible from all the Merged Lands.

Nya: So it's good we don't see anything.

Bleckt: Yet.

(Arin and Sora arrive with two new mechs, the Spinjitzu Battle Mech and the Jet Mech.)

Sora: What do you think? Specially designed for the Spectral Lands. (She gets off the Jet Mech.) I went to the Junkyard to build these mechs, and Wyldfyre ordered bots to bring me some Prismatic Ore to cover them with.

Jay: So they're impervious to spectral attack? That's not entirely stupid.

(Arin appears outside the mech.)

Arin: More like brilliant! This is the chance we need to get all the way to Thunderfang at the Well!

Lloyd: Great work, Sora. Okay, team, we've got a plan, sort of, but we have to move fast. Let's go.

(Two Security Bots approach.)

Wyldfyre: (To Bleckt.) The bots will take you back to your cell, and if you lied about any of this, I'll come back and authorize your life to be miserable forever.

Bleckt: I am not betting on your chances of success. (He and the bots walk away.)

Wyldfyre: Always such a downer, Bleckt.

Bleckt: May the Source Dragons forgive us.

(At the Spectral Lands, Nokt approaches the Well of the Lost, plays his magical flute, and deposits the few remaining souls, including Cole's, into the well.)

Thunderfang: (To Nokt.) So few souls? Is not Imperium a vastly populated land?

(Nokt looks downcast, then Rox arrives, plays her magical flute, and deposits many more souls than Nokt into the well.)

Thunderfang: Now, that's more like it.

(The souls Rox deposited mix with the healing elixir and finish charging the tablets with the symbols of the Source Dragons, making the lines and the eyes of the dragon head statues of the chamber glow and the elixir shoots a beam upwards, destroying the well's covering and giving Thunderfang more power.)

Thunderfang: Finally! I have collected all the power I will need!

(Arin and Lloyd, in their mechs, along with the other Ninja and Jay see, from the stone bridge, the storm that Thunderfang created.)

Lloyd: Bleckt warned us this was coming. We've gotta go now!

(The scene shifts to inside the Monastery of Gates and Jay climbs into his mech.)

Lloyd: Okay, last time. Arin?

Arin: You and I will take this Unbidden Storm thing to the Well of the Lost, and can summon the Arc Dragon once its two halves are awake.

Lloyd: Good. Sora and Wyldfyre?

Sora: We'll get Zanth to take us to Djinjago. If this Cave of Tones exists, which I really hope it does, we ring the Spell Song.

Lloyd: Okay. Nya and Jay?

Nya: We'll go to Mysterium, ring the spell song, and wake the dragon's other half.

Jay: Yeah, except I'm not doing any of that. (Nya is shocked.) I've already done more than I bargained for on this little trip.

Nya: You can't bail on saving the world. That's not what Ninja do.

Jay: I'm not a Ninja! Why can't you all get that through your heads? You hired me for one job! I did it, and you still haven't paid me or helped me capture Ras! I thought Ninja kept their word.

Lloyd: We've been a little busy.

Jay: Yeah. Busy nearly getting me killed, and I'm done.

Nya: (She walks towards Jay.) I really believed, after all we'd been through, that you'd care about something other than yourself. I thought I saw glimpses of your old self, moments where you knew the truth, that you were a hero, but I was wrong. (She moves away from him.)

(Jay hesitates for a moment.)

Jay: Try not to die before you pay me. (He puts on his mask, climbs into the Mech Dragon and starts to leave.)

Lloyd: Nya, if you--

Nya: Lloyd, we've got a world to save.

(Nya hugs Sora and then Wyldfyre. Jay flies away in the Mech Dragon.)

Lloyd: Good luck, everybody!

(The teams and Nya run towards three different portal gates.)

Sora: We're gonna need it.

(They pass through the portal gates and Ras comes out from behind a pillar, wearing bone armor, slams his Source Dragon Icons together and strucks them to power.)

(End of episode. For more information, click here.)
