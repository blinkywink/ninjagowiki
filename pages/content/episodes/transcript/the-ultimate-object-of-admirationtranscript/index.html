(The episode begins with a boy named Aru running with his dog, while laughing and his dog barking, as he holds up his Lloyd plush.)

Aru: Up, up! Up, up! (He throws the plush.) Go get it! (His dog jumps on him, making him fall down, but Arin made a noise, interrupts their laughter, making the dog aggravated.) What is it boy?

Arin: (He comes out of the bushes) Hey! I think you dropped your doll.

(The boy's mother and father who are named Koji and Ume come out of the front door.)

Koji: Well, hello there!

Arin: Hi, I'm Arin! Ah, I wasn't supposed to tell anyone my name.

Koji: Hmm, why not?

Arin: My, uh, teacher told me not to tell strangers who I am, ever.

Koji: Well, Arin, I'm Koji, and that's Aru and Ume.

Aru: Hi!

Koji: So now we're not strangers. (Chuckles) How can we help you?

Arin: Well, I could use some food and maybe some water? Of course I'm willing to work for it! Anything around here you need help with? I know how to water plants, fold laundry, make pies, whatever!

Koji: (chuckles) We can figure that out later. First things first, join us for dinner.

Aru: Yeah!

Arin: Thanks?

(They all went inside for dinner, as Arin shuts the door.)

(A while later, screams could be heard coming from inside the house, but they soon turn into laughter. Arin exists the house with Koji, Ume, and Aru behind him.)

Koji: (Laughing) Oh, wow, that Lobbo guy sounds like a laugh riot.

Arin: "Lobbo Lobbo!" (Everyone laughs again.) Thank you so much for everything.

Aru: Thanks. (He gives Arin his Lloyd plush.)

Arin: Uh, I can't take this.

Aru: Please. You need someone to protect you out there.

Arin: (He smiles.) Maybe I do. (He takes the plush.) Thanks again.

Koji: Come back anytime. Lobbo Lobbo.

(Koji and Arin share a chuckle before Arin leaves, but not before taking on last look at the family. He sighs sadly to himself then continues on his way.)

(Later, Arin arrives near a campfire. He takes out the Lloyd plush from his bag and looks at it sadly before putting it back and continuing to the campfire, where Ras is lying on a sled with his hammer right next to him. He opens his eyes and turns to look at the approaching Arin.)

Arin: I'm back.

Ras: (growls) You were gone too long. (Arin starts to unpack his stuff, pulling out a pail of water that causes the Lloyd plush to fall out of the bag.) Did you bring food? (Arin quickly puts the plush back in the bag before Ras notices it.)

Arin: I did. (He walks to Ras with a wooden box, opening it to reveal it is filled with sushi.)

Ras: (grumbles) Did you steal this?

Arin: (He starts feeding Ras with chopsticks.) Here it comes. (He imitates the sound of an airship.) Here comes the bounty.

(Ras grunts disapprovingly at first, but ultimately accepts the food.)

Ras: Or have to attack the villagers for it?

Arin: I just said the magic word.

Ras: You know incantations? (He chews on another piece of food.)

Arin: I said "please". Some people just want to help. (He gives more food to Ras.)

Ras: You can't depend on help. If you want true power, you need to take things by force. People always act in their own self interest. (Arin starts to eat.) Put that away! It's time to focus on your training. Get up.

Arin: (gulps) I- (Ras growls angrily at him.) Of course.

(Arin puts the box away and gets into position. Ras picks up a chopstick from the box and throws it at him.)

Ras: Your form is sloppy. Shoulders back. Lower your center.

(Arin tries to follow Ras' advice and performs a rudimentary version of Spinjitzu. He moves around for a bit before crashing into a tree, then tries again, but stumbles into a small rock.)

Arin: Whoa! (He falls to the ground, but gets up.)

Ras: Weeks of training, and you're still not improving.

Arin: I thought I was.

Ras: I can see the doubt on your face. Push trough your weakness. (His voices starts to weaken.) Focus on your strength, on your anger. Turn your hurt into power.

Arin: But that's not how the ninja-

Ras: The ninja are no longer your teachers! (He gasps for air, while Arin tries to focus on his anger.) Ah... yes, there it is.

(In a flashback, Arin confronts the spirit of Master Wu at the Monastery of Gates.)

Past Arin: I need to know, did you cause the Merge?

Past Wu: Yes...

(In another flashback, Arin is being sucked into the Merge while his parents watch in shock.)

Past Jenny: Arin!

Past Arin: No!

(In the present, Arin screams angrily before performing a proper Spinjitzu tornado. He is able to easily knock down the tree this time.)

Ras: Mm... Never doubt my teachings.

(Elsewhere, Lloyd, Sora, Frak and Riyu are walking by some cliffs. Zane is there with them riding on Zanth. Riyu is trying to track Arin, but suddenly stops.)

Sora: (sighs) Riyu's lost Arin's scent.

Lloyd: Well, Arin had to have come this way, so lets keep on this path. (The path leads them to a dark cave and a rocky mountainside.) Okay, didn't see this coming.

Frak: Why didn't Arin leave like a trail of pie crumbs or something so we could follow him?

Sora: Because I'm not sure he wants to be followed.

(Riyu continues trying to find Arin's scent. He seems unsure of where to go.)

Frak: You ask me, the cave seems way more likely. That other way is super rocky and dangerous.

Zane: My thoughts as well, but the cave is too small for Zanth to enter. I can take her down the mountain and search that direction while you explore the cave.

Lloyd: I don't love the idea of sending you down that way alone.

(Zanth snorts in frustration.)

Zane: I will not be alone.

Lloyd: It is a good idea to search both directions. Just be careful.

Zane: "Careful" would be my middle name… if Nindroid naming conventions allowed for such things, which they do not.

Sora: Good luck, Zane. We'll meet you on the other side. Uh, that came out wierd. I mean, of the mountain, not meet you in the Departed Realm.

Frak: Does the Departed Realm still exist after the Merge?

Lloyd: That's actually a very good question, but I'm hoping we don't have to find out anytime soon.

(Lloyd, Sora, Frak and Riyu head into the cave, while Zanth flies off with Zane.)

Frak: I can see the path ahead. Follow me! I got this!

Sora (whispering to Lloyd): Who made this guy boss?

Lloyd: He's just trying to be helpful. Ease up on him a little. (He activates his power to create some light.)

Sora: What? I haven't said anything sarcastic all day.

Frak: I've got a really great cave sense.

Sora (mockingly): "I've got a really great cave sense." Okay, so one sarcastic thing now.

Frak: Us Serpentine are brilliant at underground spaces. This is just like the Ninjago sewers. Well, except the smell's a little, uh, different.

(They come across an opening with carvings and furniture. Frak accidentally knocks a candle off a table, which freaks out Sora.)

Sora: Not creepy at all.

Frak: Ah... check this out! It's just like the stuff I've been studying.

Sora: You study cave writing in your spare time?

Frak: Sure. I'm interested in all thing speleological.

Sora: No one has ever said that.

Frak: Okay, well, I can't translate these without my books, but from what I can tell, this cave has been used before.

Sora: Wow. You learned all that from your spel... obian books?

Frak: (scoffs) "Speleology", please.

Lloyd: can we not bicker? Let's go. (They start to leave.)

Frak: Look! There's another tunnel!

Sora: (She interrupts Frak.) Another tunnel! Let's go this way. Uh, this looks tight.

Frak: If you're worried, let me lead the way.

Lloyd: Hope no one is claustrophobic. Huh, probably should've asked that before we started.

(Sora tries to get through a tight gap, but starts stressing out.)

Frak: (grabs onto her) Hey, you okay?

Sora: I'm fine.

Frak: There's a cool Serpentine trick I use when I start to get claustrofobic. I just breathe in through my nose (He inhales.) ...then hiss it out. (hisses)

(Sora calms down by breathing, then continues past the gap. The others follow behind her.)

(They come across another, smaller gap.)

Sora: What now, cave man?

Frak: Let me just move some of these rocks. (He starts using his power of Quake to slightly shift them.)

Sora: You sure those aren't, like, holding up the cave roof or something?

Frak: Speleologist here. (He finishes shifting the rocks, but the cave starts to rumble.)

Sora: But what's that sound?

Frak: I don't hear anything. (The roof starts to break.) It's a cave in. I shouldn't have moved those rocks! Run!

(They start running out of the cave.)

Sora: Uh oh, but yay, I was right!

Lloyd: There's a light up ahead! We're saved! (They run out to a small, crumbling cliff in top a huge gorge.) We're not saved.

Sora: Any ideas?

Frak: I can't stop the cave-in with my Quake powers. It's too big, too fast!

Sora: Riyu, can you-

(The cliff collapses, and all four of them fall down. Riyu tries to grab Sora, but a rock knocks him out.)

(Kai, Nya, and Wyldfyre are sitting around a campfire. Kai is trowing a small rock into the air.)

Wyldfyre: I'm bored. This is boring! B-O-R-ing. I bet Lloyd and Sora and them are having none of the borings.

Kai: (groans) You wanna blow up some rocks?

Nya: (groans) Ugh.

Wyldfyre: (gasps) You'd never have to ask such things!

(Kai throws a rock while Wyldfyre uses Heat which makes the rock explode, they both cheer as the do another, as a Spirit Dragon flies through it to Wyldfyre giving her a scroll.)

Wyldfyre: Ooh! It's for me! It's for me! Ooh! I wonder who it's from? Aw look at that, Roby! (Kai tries to look at the drawing.) Go away! Aw, cute. He wants me to send a selfie! (Nya sighs. Wyldfyre draws herself onto the scroll, then Kai draws himself too.)

Kai: Photobomb!

Wyldfyre: (She rolls up the scroll and give it to the Spirit Dragon) Take this to Roby. 'Kay, thanks! Wait. Should I have waited a few minutes before sending that? Am I gonna seem too needy? Unsend! Unsend!

Nya: Will you two please focus? Am I the only one who cares about finding Jay?

Kai: (sighs) You're right, Nya. I haven't been much help.

Wyldfyre: (scoffs) You really haven't.

Kai: It's just... I don't know what to do, and when I don't know what to do, I hit rocks with Fire. (He lights up his hand with his power before putting it out again.)

Wyldfyre: Hard relate.

Nya: (sighs) I get it. This whole thing is overwhelming, but we need to focus and come up with a plan. If we don't, Jay could be lost forever.

(Kai and Wyldfyre nod.)

(Lloyd wakes up on a bed inside a wooden building. He looks outside to see a village filled with dragon-people. One of them walks up to him.)

Dragonian: Oh, you're awake! Here, (He takes out a glass bottle.) this will help your arm. (He opens he bottle, and Lloyd is shocked by the smell.) ...but not your nose. My apologies. (He pours out some of the bottle's contents onto his hand, and then proceeds to smear it on Lloyd's arm.)

Lloyd: (sighs) Thanks. So... who are you? Where am I? What happened?

Dragonian: My name is Tyr, head of the Dragonian tribe, and you're in our village. Some of us found you in the river, and I got to admit, they're a little disappointed that you weren't a tasty fish.

Lloyd: Sorry for not being a fish. But what about my friends?

(Tyr points to another building, where Sora, Frak and Riyu are sleeping.)

Tyr: They'll be fine as soon as they wake. (Another Dragonian comes over and hands him a bowl.) But here, you must be hungry. (He hands the bowl to Lloyd and walks away.)

(Lloyd looks at the bowl for a moment before drinking from it.)

(After they've rested, Lloyd, Sora, Frak and Riyu walk through the village, where they see a bunch of Dragonians. They come across a vendor selling rocks with carvings similar to the ones they saw in the cave.)

Sora: Wow, these designs are beautiful.

Lloyd: It's an old Oni design.

Sora: What's an Oni? (The Dragonians around them gasp.) Did I say something wrong?

Frak: (He pulls Sora away and whispers to her.) The Oni were this race of super-evil demons at war with the Dragons a long time ago, and-

Sora: Why?

Frak: Why what?

Sora: Why were they at war with the Dragons?

Frak: Because of... evil reasons.

Sora: So you don't know?

Lloyd: The Oni and Dragons were part of the First Realm, and were at war even before the First Spinjitzu Master was born.

Sora: And he was like a relative of yours, right?

Lloyd: Right. He originally came from the Realm of the Oni and the Dragons, too.

Frak: That's exactly waht I was gonna say.

Sora: The First Realm? But where was that?

Lloyd: (He picks up one of the rocks and looks at it.) Looks like... we're in it now. This must be where the First Realm appeared during the Merge, but only Dragons and these Dragonians are left here now.

Frak: And they're on our side. Man... if I ever saw one of those evil Oni... (shudders)

Sora: I wonder if anyone has seen Arin.

Frak: Let's ask Tyr.

Sora: Actually, you know who would know? (She points at two grey haired Dragonians sitting at a table.) Them. Old ladies know everything that happens in a village.

(They approach the table.)

Old Lady Dragonian 1: Ooh, hi there sweeet pea, what's good?

Sora: We're looking for my friend. Have you seen him?

Old Lady Dragonian 2: Maybe, but we're going to need more information than "my friend". We don't know your life.

Sora: Oh, yeah. His name is Arin. (The Dragonians shake their heads.) He's this high. (Shegestures with her hand, The Dragonians shake their heads again.) Is probably wearing a hood? (The Dragonians shake their heads very fast.)

Frak: With horns on it!

Old Lady Dragonian 1: Ooh, that guy!

Old Lady Dragonian 2: Why didn't you say "horns" first?

Sora: So you know where he is?

Old Lady Dragonian 1: Yes! Well, no. We did.

Old Lady Dragonian 2: But he looked kida Oni-like, what with the horns and all, so we chased him away.

Old Lady Dragonian 1: Right towards the South Woods.

Sora: He was alone?

Old Lady Dragonian 2: Well, he wasn't with anyone else when we saw him, but we don't know his relationship status, if that's what you're asking.

Lloyd: Well, the good news is he's alive.

Old Lady Dragonian 1: Yes! Well, no. He was.

Old Lady Dragonian 2: But he's probably dead now. Cyclops.

Old Lady Dragonian 1: Cyclops...

Sora: Cyclops?

Both Dragonians: Cyclops.

Old Lady Dragonian 1: There've been Cyclops sightings from some other realm in the South Woods.

Old Lady Dragonian 2: Yup. We sent a Dragonian Hunting Party out to look into it, but...

Old Lady Dragonian 1: But...

Sora: But?

Both Dragonians: They never came back.

(The ninja are shocked by this.)

Sora: Okay... Arin is a ninja. He could survive.

Lloyd: Yeah. Even if there were a...

Both Dragonians: Cyclops.

Lloyd: ...that doesn't mean Arin isn't still alive. So, into the South Woods we go.

(The ninja walk through South Woods. Someone is following them with a glider. Riyu smells the ground again.)

Sora: Got his scent, Riyu?

(Riyu growls affirmingly.)

Sora: Yes!

Frak: Yeah!

(Riyu sniffs the ground again, then starts growling aggressively.)

Lloyd: Who else you smelling? Ras? The Forbidden Five?

(Riyu starts sprinting forward and the others follow. the masked figure trailing the ninja takes off from a nearby tree.)

(The ninja arrive at a clearing a bunch of rocks, a crate filled with treasure and a crudely constructed shelter.)

Everyone: Whoa...

Frak: What is this place?

Lloyd: It looks like someone's home. Kinda cozy, actually.

(Everyone suddenly hears muffled cries.)

Frak: You hear that?

Sora: It's coming from... the ground?

Frak: I think it's coming from under that rock. Give me a hand. (Everyone starts pushing the large rock.) Push with your knees, not your back!

Sora: I know! This isn't my first giant rock, Frak! (The rock slightly moves, and the ninja are able to see under it.) It's the hunting party! They're still alive!

Dragonian Hunter: Kinda! Get us out of here!

(Frak runs forward and kicks the rock, Lloyd jumps up and attacks it with two blasts of his power and Riyu flies up and hits it from above, but the rock only slightly moves from all of their attacks.)

Lloyd: Take my hand! (The Dragonians start climbing on top of one anoother to reach Lloyd, but are interrupted when the ground suddenly shakes.) I don't wanna turn around...

(The ninja turn around to see a large blue cyclops behind them. The Cyclops roars.)

Powerlid: I am Powerlid! The ultimate Cyclops! (He bangs his chest.) Also known as the Ultimate Object of Admiration!

Dragonian Hunter: (whispers) I don't think that last part is true.

Lloyd: Wait here while we deal with this guy.

Dragonian Hunter: Ugh, would you? He's super annoying.

(Powerlid roars and picks up a rock which he throws at the ninja. Lloyd jumps backwards to dodge it.)

Lloyd: He's annoying, but also strong.

(Lloyd puts on his hood and does Spinjitzu. He jumps up and kicks Powerlid.)

Sora: Lloyd, your Spinjitzu!

Lloyd: Yeah, feels wierd.

(Powerlid roars again. Sora puts on her mask, and the ninja jump out of the way of another rock. Lloyd and Frak land on the right of Powerlid, and Lloyd charges at him with Spinjitzu again, jumping on his head. The mysterious masked figure looks at the fight from the top a stone arch.)

Powerlid: Eat rocks, Ninjerks! (He trows another rock that Sora jumps over. The ninja surround him, and he kicks a rock toward Frak, who jumps over it while running towards him. Lloyd uses his power to blast him on the back of the head.) Ha! (He turns towards Lloyd.) Hit me all you want. Powerlid can't feel a thing! Stub my toe, no pain! Bonk my head? (He hits his head with his fist.) Nothing! I can't even feel love. (He almost begins to cry.) But Powerlid is not ready to expose himself emotionally. (sniffs) Eat more rocks! (He picks up two rocks.)

(The ninja jump out of the way of the rocks.)

Frak: Wait, don't move! Cyclopses don't perceive depth with just one eye. He can't separate us from our surroundings as long as we don't move!

(The ninja stand still.)

Powerlid: (looks around and smells the air) Ninja? Where you go? Just kidding, Powerlid can see you! (He stomps the ground, causing the ninja to fall down.) That's a stupid and harmful Cyclops stereotype! (He jumps and slams the ground, but the ninja run away before he hits them.)

Sora: You little know-it-all! You almost got us killed! (She and Frak dodge another rock.)

(Lloyd tries to hit Powerlid with Spinjitzu, but gets punched away. Sora jumps onto Powerlids back, only for Powerlid to grab her by her hood and toss her away. Riyu swoops down at Powerlid, but Powerlid grabs his tail and trows him away. Frak punches Powerlid's leg, and Powerlid sends him flying with a powerful kick.)

Frak: Nothing is working!

(Riyu struggles to get up. Lloyd comes over to hold onto him.)

Powerlid: Run from Powerlid, The Ultimate Object of Admiration! (He picks up another rock and throws it at Sora.)

Sora: (She dodges the rock by jumping to the side, the looks at the large rock that has trapped the Dragonian Hunting Party.) Is that all you got?

Powerlid: That rock was pretty heavy. Denser than it looked, maybe. Probably peridotite, or gabbro.

Sora: Just saying, it looked tiny... for someone who calls himself the Ultimate Object of Admiration.

Powerlid: I can pick up bigger! Watch (He picks up one of the rocks on top of the large rock and tosses it so hard it breaks, but Sora backflips over it.)

Sora: Ha! Nice aim, Tiny Rocks. (She jumps out of the way of another rock.)

(Frak gets up and tries to go help Sora, but Lloyd stops him.)

Sora: Nope! (She laughs an jumps across several rocks on the ground, evading Powerlids attacks.)

Powerlid: Stop moving, little ninja!

Sora: Tell you what, I'll stop moving if you can pick up that rock (She points to the large rock.) and trow it at me.

Powerlid: Promise? That rock not that heavy, even! Watch Powerlid. Powerlid is swole! (He walks to the large rock and starts lifting it with great effort.) Powerlid getting tired. Maybe not enough protein today.

Sora: Doesn't count unless you lift it over your head.

Powerlid: It count!

Sora: Nope!

(Powerlid struggles greatly, but manages to lift the rock on top of his head. However, it quikly slips and falls on his head, knocking him out.)

Lloyd: Yeah!

Frak: Quick thinking, Sora. Nice.

Lloyd: Yeah. Now let's get... going? (The ninja notice the masked figure.) Who are you? (The masked figure activates his glider and swoops down. He snatches a chalice from the crate nearby and flies off.) What was that all about?

Powerlid: (murmurs in his sleep) Object of Admiration...

(The ninja pull the Dragonian Hunting Party out of the pit.)

Sora: Any chance you've seen my friend, Arin? He was wearing a hood with horns, and-

Dragonian Hunter: Dragging someone on a sled?

Sora: Maybe? When did you see him? Was he okay?

Dragonian Hunter: Mm, no idea. We were kind busybeing stuck in a cyclops hole, but I think he went that way. (He points South, then leaves with the others.)

Sora: Great! He's headed South. Come on. (Frak grabs her arm.) What now? Wanna serpent-splain "South" to me?

Frak: No! I... I want to apologise.

Sora: Oh.

Frak: I... I know I could have gotten us all killed. I was trying to show you I'm not just Ras' stooge.

Sora: We know that. Kinda.

Frak: Maybe I could've stopped Arin from going with Ras if I had just talked to him.

Sora: It's not your fault.

Frak: You really believe that?

Sora: Yeah, because... I think it's my fault. Arin and I weren't talking when he left. He was my first friend. Did you know that?

Frak: I... didn't.

Sora: Yeah, well, I'm not very good at making friends.

Lloyd: So, you two ready or what?

Sora: Yes.

Frak: Of course.

(They run off.)

(Elsewhere, Arin is dragging Ras on the sled. Ras is groaing in pain.)

Arin: We should stop soon, eat a bit, maybe put on some sunscreen.

Ras: No! Keep moving!

Arin: You can't run from the sun. It'll only take a minute.

Ras: I demand we go faster!

Arin: You think I don't want to find my parents as fast as we can? But we can't be stupid out here.

Ras: I promised we'd find your parents, and I always keep my word! (wheezes) But my injuries are grave. I must heal.

Arin: Of course.

Ras: To do that, there's only one place we must go... The Spectral Lands.
