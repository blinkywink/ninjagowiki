Codename: Arcturus is the sixth episode of the third season of Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu and the 32nd episode overall. It first premiered on July 13, 2014, in the United States on Cartoon Network.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

Lloyd is free but Pythor and the Overlord are nowhere to be found. The Ninja discover Codename: Arcturus. But what is it? They go somewhere they never knew they would.

Extended: Lloyd is free but Pythor and the Overlord are nowhere to be found. However the Ninja have a cryptic clue to their next plan - something called Codename: Arcturus. But what is it? Finding out won't be easy but it will ultimately take the Ninja to a place where they never ever thought they would go. Since evildoers will always seek his Golden Power, Lloyd selflessly shares his power with the others, endowing them each with elemental power and returning himself back to the Green Ninja. Meanwhile, time has passed and the Ninja use state-of-the-art technology to track down the remaining threat. When they uncover a secret mission only known to them as Codename: Arcturus, in a nod to the Bond series, they send Kai to track down a mysterious convoy in a superspy car… only to discover the Overlord has survived and has built a rocket in order to retrieve the Golden Weapons, which have been previously blasted off into space.[1]

Plot

Plot[]

In the ocean's depths, Pythor leads the Nindroids in attaching balloons to the Overlord's preserved body, allowing them to raise the Nindroid MechDragon above water. With their leader secure, Pythor, General Cryptor, and the Nindroids take off towards the Lost City of Ouroboros.

At the Temple of Light, Lloyd gives up his Golden Power to restore the ninja's Elemental Powers. Garmadon expresses his concerns about Lloyd dividing his power, but Wu assures him that he must trust his son. Wu also reassures that they will find Cyrus Borg and stop Pythor, hoping that they will be able to rest once this is over. However, Garmadon responds by saying that since they are the protectors of Ninjago, rest doesn't come easily.

Later, at Borg Tower, Sensei Wu spots General Cryptor aboard a train heading for Ouroboros. He tries to send Jay and Cole to investigate, but they are fighting over Nya once more at Chan's Theater. Wu sends Kai instead, but he has become overconfident with the heroes' previous victory over the Overlord. As a result, Kai is attacked by Nindroids, and Pythor himself grabs him, tapes his mouth, and takes him prisoner.

Cole, Jay, Zane, and Lloyd discover the secret of Arcturus and head for Ouroboros. When they arrive, the floor opens, revealing the OverBorg's ultimate creation, Codename: Arcturus—a rocket designed to reach Delta V, the comet that contains the lost Golden Weapons.

The ninja release Kai and secretly board the rocket, preparing to go somewhere they never expected: space!

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the second episode in the series to feature the name of an individual character in its title after "Return of the Overlord."
  • The Hageman Brothers had a cameo appearance in the theater. This marks the first time they appear in the series. They appear a few more times within the next few seasons.

Cultural references[]

  • While pursuing the Nindroid convoy, Kai says "I love it when a plan comes together," a line associated with the character John "Hannibal" Smith from the 1980s TV series The A-Team.
  • The way the ninja's allies shine Cole and Jay's elemental symbols in the sky to summon them is a reference to the Bat-Signal used to summon Batman.

Continuity[]

  • As of this episode, the four original ninja finally have their elemental powers fully restored thanks to Lloyd.
  • In this episode, the character of Arcturus is mentioned for the first time. Arcturus would play a greater role in the following season.
  • In this episode, Lloyd is first seen with his Stone Armor gi.
  • The Golden Weapons were last seen in "Wrong Place, Wrong Time", where they were believed to have been destroyed after being used against the Mega Weapon. This episode reveals that the weapons survived the event and went into outer space.

Errors[]

  • This episode has a very great error, as after Lloyd uses the last of his Golden Power to give the ninja their raw elemental power, he now has his Stone Armor gi, which he then wears for the rest of this episode, “The Void”, and a majority of “The Titanium Ninja”. This is an error and not a suit choice as when in “The Titanium Ninja” when the rest of the ninja get their Stone Armor, Lloyd is shown putting the armor over his Techno Gi and it transforms into the Stone Armor Gi as if he didn’t have it before, despite having it for all the other episodes since “Codename: Arcturus”.
  • When Zane says they can't let anything separate them again, his mouth doesn't appear to be in proper sync with what he's saying.
  • At the Temple of Light, the ninja are standing on the wrong Elemental symbols.
    • Kai is on the Earth symbol.
    • Zane is on the Fire symbol.
    • Jay is on the Ice symbol.
    • Cole is on the Lightning symbol.
      • This had also occurred in "Island of Darkness", and "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master".
  • When Dan Hageman is throwing popcorn at Jay, Nya, and Cole, Cole's eyebrows are nearly invisible.
  • When Kai looks at the fire catch up to the oil a moment before it explodes the truck, his appearance is a mirror image of his usual self.
  • When P.I.X.A.L. says that their facial recognition software picked up Cryptor, when the camera shows Cryptor driving no one is next to him, but later and throughout the episode Pythor can be seen sitting next to Cryptor.
  • Just before sneaking onto the ship, Jay, and Cole are missing their eyebrows in the ships reflections.
