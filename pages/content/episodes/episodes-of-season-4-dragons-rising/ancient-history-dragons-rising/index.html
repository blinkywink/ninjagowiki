Ancient History is the fourteenth episode of the fourth season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the 74th episode overall.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

LEGO's YouTube Channel[]

After a messy victory threatens peace talks, Frak suspects sabotage by a past villain. Ignored by the team, he partners with an unexpected ally to investigate Old Town’s dangerous underbelly. 🕊️🕵️‍♂️🌃[1]

Streaming services[]

Tubi[]

While the young Ninja struggle with their public perception, Frak investigates the possible return of a villain.

Roku[]

After a messy victory threatens the peace talks, the young Ninja investigate Frak’s claim of sabotage. Their search leads them into Old Town, where they uncover more trouble than expected.

Extended[]

After another messy victory threatens the success of upcoming peace talks, the young Ninja return to the Monastery to reflect on their failures. Frak believes he was sabotaged by a villain from the Ninja's past, but the others don't believe him. His investigation finds help from an unexpected source. The search takes them to the underbelly of Old Town where they find more than they bargained for.[2]

Plot

Plot[]

In Old Town, Lobbo zooms around super-fast, crashing into crates, barrels, fences, and shaking construction scaffolding while screaming in panic. Percival watches in distress, wondering if anyone can stop him. Wyldfyre arrives on her Prototype X-1, yelling "Ninja... get going!"—a catchphrase Frak points out isn't quite right. Wyldfyre retorts that she says her own thing as Euphrasia flies overhead on Riyu, asking why Lobbo is acting so wild. Arin and Sora arrive in the Transforming Car with a gross explanation. A flashback shows Lobbo eating leftover casseroles at Lobbo's Totally Rad Arcade that had been sitting out for two weeks. Sora quips, "Never underestimate the evil potential of bad casserole." As Lobbo collides with a taxi, Sora tries to use her powers to assist, but it fails. Frustrated, she admits how much she misses having Lloyd around to keep their powers working properly. Wyldfyre declares she will handle it solo "for teamwork reasons," prompting Frak to question her logic. Claiming authority as the self-appointed team leader, Wyldfyre uses her vehicle to block the road. Lobbo crashes right into her vehicle, shrieking about a hurricane in his "intestinal rotors" before Riyu swoops in to catch Wyldfyre with Euphrasia. Riyu successfully catches Lobbo, allowing Arin, Sora, and Frak to pull over. Lobbo thanks them as Sora inspects his back, discovering his hover engine is stuck in overdrive from being gummed up with rotten casserole. As she prepares to vent it, Lobbo nervously begs them not to tell his mother—just before slipping from Riyu's grip and careening straight toward Arin, Sora, and Frak.

Driving his Battle Bike, Frak fires a Grapple Gun at Lobbo to steady him. As Lobbo screams for families to flee the "horrible nightmare" he has become, Frak struggles to hold him in place. An unexpected blast hits Frak's Battle Bike, causing both Frak and Lobbo to shoot forward at super-speed alongside each other, causing the damage in the Old Town. Sora intervenes and flushes Lobbo's hover engine. Expressing "casserole regret," Lobbo tells everyone to look away as he vomits up the entire rotten casserole, feeling much better afterward—though a coughing Frak notes it's only better for some of them. Shuddering at Frak getting "chunked on," Wyldfyre nevertheless credits the resolution to her own "sterling leadership". Percival angrily shuts down her claim, asking if the mess looks like a win. He groans in frustration, pointing out that with the Summit of the Merged Lands only days away, Lobbo's out-of-control rampage just wiped out a week's worth of event.

The young ninja return to the Monastery of Spinjitzu, with Euphrasia expressing confusion over their struggling teamwork. Wyldfyre sarcastically notes how strange it is that they still messed up despite having what she considers the "best leadership ever". Entering the Relaxation Room, the group is greeted with a surprise setup by Barry, Jenny, and Sora's parents. Jenny explains that they prepared snacks to celebrate the ninja stopping the "unfortunate purple robot guy". Though Frak points out they let half of Old Town get destroyed first, Barry reassures them that things would have been much worse without their bravery, noting how proud they are of the group. Jenny adds that the surprise was a joint effort by the newly formed "Ninja Parent Support Group". Noticing Sora's subdued mood, her mother asks what is wrong and gently confronts her about feeling avoided. When Sora tries to brush it off as being busy, her mother presses further, prompting Sora to snap and admit that she has been intentionally avoiding them because she never wanted to see them again. Sora confronts her parents about running away from Imperium after they chose loyalty to the Beatrix administration over their own daughter. Her mother acknowledges their past mistake, explaining that they were raised to believe being good citizens was all that mattered, but admits it should never have taken priority over being good parents. Her father shares that they came to the monastery hoping for a fresh start to leave the past behind, but Sora responds that she doesn't know if she will ever be ready for that.

Frak attempts to execute a "double twist flying kick" but fails. Arin notices and reminds him that the move is impossible because they made it up when they were ten years old. Frak admits he was hoping to pull it off because he desperately needs a win. Arin tries to encourage him by recalling his parents' words that saving Lobbo and Old Town was still a success. However, Frak reflects on a series of recent blunders, shown in a quick flashback where he gets hit during training, accidentally catches Mucoids while chasing the Re-Awakened, and gets hit by a door while opening it for Sora. Crestfallen, Frak wonders if his dad is right about him not being a real ninja. Arin urges Frak to stop worrying about his father's opinion. Frak opens up about how hard it is, sharing that all he ever wanted was his father's approval, but his father constantly ignores or belittles him. He notes that Arin wouldn't understand because his parents are awesome. Arin agrees his parents are great but relates to Frak's feelings of unworthiness. He reminds Frak that no one gets everything right, but the Frak he knows never quits even when things feel hopeless. Realizing Arin is right, Frak resolves to stop attempting impossible moves and focus on analyzing what went wrong during the mission, deciding to hit the track for bike practice instead.

In the Hangar bay, Frak examines his Battle Bike and discovers suspicious scratches accompanied by burn marks. Meanwhile, a broadcast from Intelligent George reports on the destruction in Old Town, openly questioning whether Ninjago City is safe enough to host the Summit of the Merged Lands. Frak pauses and rewinds footage of the incident, catching a frame that shows a laser blast striking his bike's engine. Realizing he didn't stall the bike himself, he re-examines the clip and identifies a shadowy figure that resembles General Cryptor. Frak points out Cryptor's face to Sora and Arin. When Sora asks who Cryptor is, Frak explains that he was an evil Nindroid general who once commanded a robotic army to overrun Ninjago City. Arin insists Cryptor is dead and that his spirit moved on from the Departed Realm. Frak counters that Nindroids can simply be repaired. Arin suggests showing the footage to P.I.X.A.L., but Frak reveals that Mr. Frohicky told him she left a couple of days prior to handle business before Zane's return. Replaying the clip, Sora dismisses the visual evidence, suggesting the red flash was just an engine spark and that Frak is looking for proof of sabotage to make himself feel better about the mistake. Arin agrees that a random red streak isn't enough proof.

Frak returns to the debris area in Old Town during the middle of the night to look for physical evidence of sabotage. Sensing someone creeping up behind him, Frak turns around and kicks the mysterious figure, only to realize he just struck his own father, Reginald Slitherbottom. Helping his groaning father to a bench, Frak asks why he is out so late. Reginald admits that his standing in the Explorer's Club took a major blow when a rare map from the Rare Maps Collection was stolen right under his nose. He notes that Gweeg has since been promoted above him due to heroic actions in Umbrasilvis, while Reginald's reputation lies in ruins. Reginald asks Frak outright if he stole the map as a act of revenge against him for being a neglectful father. Frak denies it, explaining that he is actually investigating a laser sabotage against his bike. Reginald reveals that a finely tuned laser was used to slice through the glass display containing the missing map. Realizing the connection, both Frak and Reginald simultaneously ask: "Are we after the same person?"

Inside Reginald's office, Reginald grabs an ice cube for his face while Frak looks around. Reginald awkwardly admits that he hasn't been the best father, pointing out a "plate" Frak made him that he uses for lunch. Frak corrects him, pointing out it was actually ninja chest-plate armor he crafted so they could pretend to be ninja together—a play session that never happened. Frak asks about the purpose of the stolen map. Reginald reveals it detailed underground passages beneath Old Town leading to a rumored, buried Mind Crystal—a legendary artifact capable of controlling or swapping minds. Frak connects the dots, suspecting General Cryptor stole it to seek out power and disrupt the upcoming Summit of the Merged Lands. Frak admits the other ninja didn't believe Cryptor was back, forcing him to investigate alone. Surprisingly, Reginald validates Frak, stating that if Frak believes it's Cryptor, then it must be true. Planning to beat Cryptor to the crystal, Frak asks for a copy of the stolen map. Reginald boasts that he possesses a photographic memory and has a perfect replica of the map stored in his mind. Excitement builds as Reginald views this mission as the "ninja-pretend time" they never had as father and son. Frak and Reginald head back to Old Town to locate the sewer entrance leading to the passages. Despite Reginald getting north and south slightly mixed up, Frak spots the manhole, opens it, and both jump down into the tunnels.

After landing in the sewers beneath Old Town, Frak expresses self-doubt, calling himself an embarrassment to Serpentine everywhere. Reginald reassures him, pointing out that despite being both a Serpentine and an explorer, he has never been in these specific sewers either. The two share a brief moment discussing Frak’s baking skills before hitting a dead end at a locked sewer gate, leading Reginald to doubt the accuracy of his own memory. Loyalists sneak up behind Frak and Reginald, ordering them not to move as more reinforcements arrive and surround them. Recognising the threat, Reginald panics, but Frak jumps into action, declaring they are just getting started. Frak charges into combat, dodging enemy fire, kicking, and slamming the Loyalists while urging his father to run for safety. As Reginald attempts to flee, another Loyalist cuts him off, steals his shovel, and strikes him down with it. Distracted by his father getting hurt, Frak is slammed to the ground by a Loyalist and knocked unconscious.

Frak opens his eyes to find himself at a mining site located deeper inside the sewers. Reginald admits Frak was right as they come face-to-face with General Cryptor. Cryptor warns his Loyalist miners to be careful not to damage the Mind Crystal, then notices the captive father and son, threatening to destroy them and dump their remains in the river. Frak claims he is a real ninja who activated a homing beacon to summon the others—a claim he whispers to his father is a total bluff. Playing along to buy time, Reginald bluffs back, claiming to be the Merged Lands' top expert on the Mind Crystal and warning Cryptor that misusing it could turn him and his men into "drooling infants". While Cryptor is distracted, Reginald unties their ropes, revealing he took second place in an "Escaping from Enemies' Bindings" competition. As Loyalist miners charge, Frak jumps into action and successfully executes the "double-twist flying kick" on Cryptor, triumphantly celebrating that he finally pulled off the move. Ordering his miners to keep digging for the Mind Crystal, Cryptor powers up his laser to handle Frak and Reginald himself. Frak pushes his father out of harm's way, wall-jumps on a chain, and earns Reginald's admiration, who calls him a "real Ninja". Cryptor shoots a boulder down that slams Frak into the floor. Frak urges his father to use his photographic mind map to find an exit. Reginald points out a tunnel leading just outside Old Town Square. Frak tackles Cryptor to cover their escape, then climbs up the shaft right behind Reginald, who slams the manhole lid closed to block Cryptor's final laser blast.

Reginald excitedly how to boasts of kicking open the manhole cover like an action hero, but Frak tries to hurry him along before they are caught. General Cryptor flies out of the manhole opening, frightening nearby citizens as he fires lasers at Frak and Reginald, declaring that his fight is with everyone in the Merged Lands. Frak engages Cryptor directly to protect the bystanders, spinning and kicking the Nindroid General. Cryptor counters by blasting lasers across the area, bringing down a billboard and targeting innocent civilians. Seeing two people in danger, Reginald leaps in front of Cryptor's laser blast, taking the hit with a triumphant shout. Infuriated by his father taking the hit, Frak delivers a fury of powerful punches to Cryptor. Before the fight can continue, a Loyalist reports that they have successfully obtained the Mind Crystal. Declaring their mission complete, Cryptor boards a Hover chariot, and the Loyalists retreat into the sky. Frak rushes to his father's side, tearfully confessing his regret over not telling Reginald how he felt sooner. However, Reginald suddenly moves and reveals he survived unharmed. Showing off the scorched ninja chest plate Frak had made for him years ago, Reginald chuckles and admits it makes a much better protective chest plate than a food plate, leaving both father and son laughing in relief.

Back in the Hangar bay, Arin, Sora, Euphrasia, Riyu, and Wyldfyre meet up with Frak. Frak highlights that General Cryptor and the Loyalists are working together to disrupt Ninjago City, though he remains unsure why they need the Mind Crystal. Sora emphasizes that they must stay vigilant and track them down. Arin apologizes to Frak for doubting him when he warned them that Cryptor had returned. Frak forgives him, lightheartedly reminding Arin of the "double-twist flying kick" they always talked about. After Frak demonstrates the move by knocking down a crate box, Reginald tries it himself and fails, humorously admitting he isn't the ninja or the baker of the family. Frak calls for a break, and the two enjoy some pies together. In the Relaxation Room, Sora walks in while her parents are eating cake. Deciding to take a small step toward rebuilding her relationship with them, Sora asks for a small slice, which her mother happily hands to her.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This marks the first time that a Ninjago episode title has been reused. The title of this episode was previously used in the tenth episode of the eleventh season from the original series.
  • This is the first full 22-minute segment in the franchise where none of the original Ninja are present.
    • While they have all been absent in several 11-minute episodes in the past, at least one of the Ninja appeared in a neighboring episode.
  • Lobbo's line "Let's all agree not to tell Lobbo's mother about this!" finally confirms in the main series that Lobbo's mother is currently alive and keeps contact with her son. This was first revealed in Crossroads' Crosstalk episode, "Convention!" and implied in the episode "The Spectral Lands" from Season 3.
  • This is Kevin Burke's favorite episode of the series.[3]
  • This episode confirms that Arin and Frak were at least 10 years old when Frak moved away, and at least 10 years old when the Merge happened.

Continuity[]

  • It is confirmed that Arin and Frak were alive during Season 3: Rebooted and witnessed the Battle of New Ninjago City.
    • However, it is currently unknown if Frak was there when the Nindroids attacked the Tomb of the Stone Army.
  • Frak said P.I.X.A.L. left to do a couple of things before Zane and the other ninja returned. Her fate would be revealed in "Desperately Seeking Sora".

Errors[]

  • Frak and Reginald pronounce "Serpentine" ser-pen-TYNE, which is the actual pronunciation of the real word. Though this could just be Reginald’s personal preference.
