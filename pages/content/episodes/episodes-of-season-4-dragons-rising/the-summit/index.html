Overview

This article is about the episode. For the conference, see Summit of the Merged Lands.
“It is no longer time for us to fear our differences, but to celebrate them! The Summit of the Merged Lands will be an expression of unity, sharing cultures, resources, and agreeing to stand up to evil and protect one another!”
— Percival Tartigrade

The Summit is the eighteenth episode of the fourth season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the 78th episode overall.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

LEGO's YouTube Channel[]

As the Summit of the Merged Lands officially begins, tensions skyrocket when a dangerous plot threatens to derail the peace talks entirely. While the young ninja race to foil the scheme without ruining negotiations, the classic Ninja edge closer to the Abyss of Eternal Night, but mounting doubts leave them wondering if their new ally will actually keep his promise.[1]

Streaming services[]

Tubi[]

Tensions mount at the peace talks, and the new Ninja must foil a kidnapping plot without derailing negotiations.

Roku[]

The young Ninja race to stop the Loyalists from sabotaging the peace summit. Meanwhile, the classic Ninja near the Abyss of Eternal Night must decide if they can trust their ally to destroy the Weapon of Desolation.

Extended[]

It is opening ceremonies for the Summit of the Merged Lands, and the young Ninja are determined to stop the Loyalists' plans to derail the peace talks. Meanwhile, the classic Ninja get closer to the Abyss of Eternal Night - but can they trust their new ally to fulfill his promise to destroy the Weapon of Desolation?[2]

Plot

Plot[]

Gayle Gossip reports live from the Old Town Courthouse, where the Summit of the Merged Lands is about to begin. Citizens are excited about the historic event, though some criticize the inhabitants of other lands, such as the Acona. Meanwhile, Intelligent George questions the young ninja's competence, pointing to recent accidents, Loyalists escaping the sewers, and the so-called performance involving the Cocoons. His broadcast sparks online debate between NinjaFanInfinity and SpinjitzuForDays99 about whether the young ninja are doing a good job or not. Gayle introduces Percival Tartigrade, who announces the summit as a celebration of unity, cultural exchange, and mutual protection following Imperium's attack by Beatrix. He urges everyone to overcome their differences and stand together against evil. Among the crowd, Frak hopes the summit succeeds, while Arin and Euphrasia worry about Cryptor and Operation Mentax. Wyldfyre, meanwhile, fears only that the summit will be boring. When Frak asks about Sora's absence, Arin admits she is furious because he wrongly accused her parents of being Loyalists and encouraged her to confront them, much to Euphrasia and Wyldfyre's shock and Frak's dismay. Feeling guilty, he worries that she won't answer his calls. Zeatrix joins the ninja after checking the perimeter, warning that while nothing suspicious has turned up, the silence actually makes her nervous, for if the Loyalists decide to strike, the summit could easily turn into the single most tragic event of all time—and preventing that tragedy hangs entirely on them. Meanwhile, elsewhere, Cryptor and the Loyalists set up Operation Mentax while monitoring Percival's broadcast, with Cryptor targeting Zeatrix on screen and declaring the Merged Lands will finally crumble under their boots before declaring loyalty to Beatrix.

Inside a security room, Zeatrix monitors multiple individuals on an Imperium device, including Rogue, Lobbo, Kreel, and Beatrix. Listening to Percival's broadcast about stolen freedoms, she laments that Levo never would have tolerated what Beatrix had become. She continues sliding through profiles of Fedulian, a Bone Guard, and Ras, questioning out loud if Ras was just another pawn discarded by her power-hungry sister, noting that it doesn't really matter if it's true since Ras seems to escape the consequences of his actions. The young ninja arrives, with Frak commenting on the intense security setup. Zeatrix emphasizes the need for constant vigilance, especially since Euphrasia reveals that the final delegates are arriving and the opening ceremonies are about to begin. Zeatrix promises to act as their eyes in the sky to spot hidden Loyalists, while Arin notes that the rest of the team will watch the streets and the summit, aided by Riyu monitoring things outside. Sora arrives late and proposes a plan to leave one team member upstairs to protect Zeatrix, noting that as Beatrix's sister, she is a high-value target. Zeatrix scoffs at the idea, confidently insisting she can protect herself and needs no help. Wyldfyre gathers the team as they head out to their assignments while Zeatrix remains in the room. On their way out, Arin tries to talk to Sora, but she cold-shoulders him, stating she is only speaking to him for the sake of the job before they split up.

Meanwhile, outside the Old Town Courthouse, Roby and Percival wait for the final delegate. Roby complains about the delegate being unfashionably late, though Percival notes they RSVP'd in an unorthodox way. Wyldfyre asks Sora a personal question about whether she punched Arin for the accusation, then wonders if the missing representative is from the Land of Eternal Dawn. Just then, Morro arrives, prompting Sora to correct her and identify the newcomer as being from the Spectral Lands, while Wyldfyre recognizes Morro to be the departed ninja that Arin talked about. Sora expresses disappointment that the strange land they previously visited sent no representative, a point Percival once again dismisses by stating it must be part of another realm. Wyldfyre remains skeptical just as the crowd begins to applaud the presence of the delegates. Suddenly, Percival realizes a delegate was in fact missing: Lloyd. Sora explains that Lloyd is away on a mission, questioning why Percival expected him to be the delegate for Ninjago. Worried that the absence will reflect poorly on Ninjago, Percival notes that Lloyd is Ninjago's most trusted citizen and asks if a second-in-command exists, prompting a frustrated groan from Sora and an amused response from Wyldfyre, who takes on the role of Ninjago's delegate.

The Summit of the Merged Lands begins with the delegates coming out of the doors to prepare for the peace talks.
