Dangerous Connections is the twentieth and final episode of the fourth season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the 80th episode overall.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

LEGO's YouTube Channel[]

The Weapon of Desolation won’t go down without a fight! Good thing Lloyd has one last idea… But will the legendary Green Ninja have the strength to succeed? 🟢🥷[1]

Streaming services[]

Tubi[]

Loyalists disrupt the Merged Lands Summit, as the Weapon of Desolation resists its own destruction.

Roku[]

The classic Ninja make a final stand at the Abyss of Eternal Night as Lloyd fights to destroy the Weapon of Desolation. Meanwhile, the Loyalists attack the peace summit, forcing the young Ninja to face their most dangerous weapon yet.

Extended[]

The classic Ninja make a last stand at the Abyss of Eternal Night to destroy the Weapon of Desolation. Lloyd draws upon his training as the Legendary Green Ninja to try and quell this threat once and for all. At the Merged Lands Summit, the Loyalists make their move and jeopardise efforts for peace. The young Ninja face the Loyalists' most dangerous weapon yet.[2]

Plot

Plot[]

The episode begins with a flashback at Four Weapons Blacksmith, where Kai and Nya were playing while Jay shyly hides from them. Ray, Wu, and Edna examined the Realm medallion. Ray explains that he hoped someone would know what the mysterious object was, while Wu recognizes it as an old depiction of the realms. Wu explains that the lines on the medallion suggest that all the realms are connected. According to legend, this connection could potentially be exploited to pull or force the realms together, causing them to collide. However, Wu reveals that if the same location in each realm could somehow be aligned, the realms could collide while remaining balanced through unity. Ray remains skeptical of the idea, while Nya interrupts the conversation to invite Jay to join her and Kai.

As Jay regains consciousness, Lloyd confronts Zilvar, warning him that the Weapon of Desolation is corrupting him and demands him to destroy it. Driven by grief and desperation, Zilvar refuses and declares that he will use the Weapon's power to bring back his lost tribe and realm, even if it means destroying him and the other ninja. He activates the Weapon once more and summons another wave of Elemental Monsters.

Sora is being escorted as a prisoner by the Loyalists. She mockingly questions why their leader, General Cryptor, is "taking a nap" when the guard defends Cryptor, Sora tricks them into believing they woke him up. As the distracted Loyalists turn around to check on Cryptor, who remained deactivated, Sora immediately suprises them with Spinjitzu, knocking them out. Surprised that her simple trick actually worked, Sora approaches the device restraining Zeatrix and shuts it down, freeing her. A disoriented Zeatrix asks where she is, and Sora urges her to move quickly so they can escape. As Sora and Zeatrix reach the elevator to make their exit, the knocked out Loyalists regain consciousness. Cornering them with weapons aimed, the lead Loyalist declares that while Cryptor gave orders to keep Sora alive, her trickery proved that was a mistake and decides to take lethal aim instead.

At the Cloud factory, the Nindroid MechDragon powers up, prompting alarms from Wyldfyre, Euphrasia, and Riyu. The MechDragon agrees that the situation is bad for them, powering its weapons and firing at Wyldfyre, Euphrasia, and Riyu while declaring that they cannot stop them. A projected message from P.I.X.A.L. prompts Euphrasia to realize that the machine previously attacked her, asking Wyldfyre if she has been hunting Cryptor this entire time. Euphrasia turns to ask Wyldfyre if they stand a chance together, only to see Wyldfyre charging toward the Nindroid MechDragon inside her own mech dragon. Insulting Cryptor's machine as "prime mediocrity," Wyldfyre taunts him as she launches into battle.

Sora and Zeatrix find themselves surrounded by Loyalists, with Sora jokingly asking if they can settle things with arm wrestling instead. Arin drops down onto the elevator and uses Spinjitzu to knock down the surrounding Loyalists. When two remaining Loyalists aim their weapons, Frak strikes from behind to take them out, celebrating with a high-five from Arin. When Sora asks how Arin located them, he reveals it was thanks to her parents. She realize that her parents having successfully accessed the Loyalist Communicator, which means they are undercover Loyalists as Arin had correctly suspected. Pushing her personal shock aside but distressed over the truth, Sora points out that Percival is about to hold the vote on the unity agreement and General Cryptor's whereabouts remain unknown. Warning that evil people always have a "Plan B," Sora, Arin, Zeatrix, and Frak head into the elevator to rush to the Summit of the Merged Lands.

As the ninja continue to battle endless hordes of Elemental Monsters, Kai, frustrated by the escalating threat, suggests a drastic move: if they can't throw the Weapon into the Abyss of Eternal Night, they might have to knock Zilvar in with it. Lloyd firmly shuts down Kai's idea, refusing to abandon Zilvar or leave the Weapon sealed away with him. He insists that their only option is to disarm Zilvar directly and get the Weapon out of his hands. Taking action, Cole and Zane fly straight toward Zilvar in order to disarm him. However, Zilvar retaliates using his laser vision, striking Cole and Zane mid-air and bringing them down.

Arin, Sora, Frak, and Zeatrix arrive at the Summit while Percival prepares to address the crowd before the vote. Arin scans the area and notes there are no visible signs of Loyalists, but Sora warns that they could be hiding anywhere and instructs the group to spread out and keep their eyes peeled. Taking advantage of the team's distraction, Zeatrix covertly grabs a pad, breaks it, and hides beneath a tray cart. Unaware of the underlying tension, Percival continues his speech on the importance of working together to protect everyone's rights. Spotting Zeatrix, he proudly introduces her as a shining example of unity announcing that Zeatrix of Imperium's royal family has returned to not regain power, but to serve and help build peace.

Back at the Cloud factory, Wyldfyre and Riyu are trying to fight back the Nindroid MechDragon with his sword. However, the MechDragon overpowers Wyldfyre and Riyu before turning its attention to Euphrasia and P.I.X.A.L. It swallows them both and lets out a roar. Declaring that it is time to leave, the MechDragon flies away, leaving Wyldfyre and Riyu behind as Euphrasia and P.I.X.A.L. remain trapped inside it.

Zeatrix addresses the gathered representatives, claiming they are not actually there to serve their own people, but rather the needs of a single individual. Sora inspects a pad located beneath a tray cart and notices something is wrong just as the shift occurs. Beatrix, who had actually succeeded in taking over Zeatrix's body, suddenly reveals her identity, horrifying Percival and the Council of the Merged Lands. Seeing Beatrix having taken control of her sister's body, Sora realized the success of the mind transfer procedure. The waiters at the Summit reveal themselves as undercover Loyalists and begin apprehending the delegates while Beatrix proclaims herself as "Empress of the Merged Lands". Percival angrily tells her that neither he nor the Council will yield, only for Beatrix to reveal the Nindroid MechDragon, which breaks through the ceiling and attacks the Summit as she warns the Summit that those who refuse to submit will be held prisoner. The Bone Regent firmly refuses to yield to Beatrix, and she and her escorts readied themselves to fight back. In response to the Bone Regent's defiance, Beatrix summons her cocoon bots to forcefully brainwash the council to her will. While continuing to fight off the Loyalists, Arin asks Sora if they are to blame for the chaos; she throws to him the tablet containing all the details of Operation Mentax to take over the Summit. Arin questions if General Cryptor is responsible, but Sora states that it couldn't be him, given that he was rebuilt by someone. The Acona delegates for the Whispering Wood and its escort fired arrows at the MechDragon, only for them to bounce off its metal plating. In retaliation, the MechDragon swallows the Acona delegates before landing in front of Arin and Sora. Cryptor states that he should've ended Sora when he had the chance and was just about to attack her and Arin. Wyldfyre arrives in her mech and forcefully takes the MechDragon into the air to resume their battle.

Zilvar attacks the Ninja while Lloyd waits for the right moment to act. He fights his way past the elemental monsters before confronting Zilvar and knocking the Weapon of Desolation out of his hands. On Lloyd's signal, Jay uses his Lightning powers to send the weapon plunging into the Abyss. However, this victory proved to be short-lived as the Weapon of Desolation begins to levitate back out of the Abyss and possess Zilvar. Speaking through him, the weapon mocks the Ninja, declaring that Zilvar's mortal body has its limitations and that a Primordial Artifact of Dawn cannot be defeated simply by throwing it into a pit. It then summons more elemental monsters, escalating the battle once again.

The delegates are being overrun by the Loyalists and the Cocoons. Meanwhile, Arin and Sora fight against the Loyalists. Sora apologizes to Arin, fearing that they may not survive. She regrets turning her back on her real family. Arin reassures her by reminding her of their promise to stay together forever, but the Loyalists seize him. Sora desperately calls for them to release Arin, only to be captured herself as the cocoons close in on them.

The ninja continue fighting the Elemental Monsters as the Weapon of Desolation taunts them by boasting how it cannot be destroyed because it draws power directly from the Source Dragons themselves. As the monsters grow rapidly stronger, Kai admits that he's out of ideas, while Nya points out that touching or attacking the Weapon directly could make the Elemental Monsters even more dangerous. Remembering how he previously failed at the Ritual of Unrooting and connected with the ground back in the Whispering Wood, Lloyd recalled how his Life powers allow him to stabilize Elemental Powers. Hearing Kai's doubts and protests, Lloyd makes his way towards the Weapon of Desolation, banking on luck to neutralize it with his powers as the ninja cover him. Lloyd manages to reach and pull the Weapon from Zilvar's reach, freeing him from its possession. Lloyd then stabilized the Weapon, successfully shutting it down and drawing all of the Elemental Monsters back into it, while effectively stabilizing Elemental Powers.

Whilst held by Loyalists as Cocoons approach, the young ninja realize that their Elemental Powers have been permanently stabilized, using them to destroy the Cocoons, freeing the delegates from their control and attacked the Loyalists. The Nindroid MechDragon blasts Wyldfyre, but grabs a piece of his mech's wing right as her Elemental Power stabilizes. Declaring the fight unfair for her enemies, she blasts the MechDragon’s wing. Meanwhile, Euphrasia uses her Wind powers to break out from inside the MechDragon's chest, freeing herself, P.I.X.A.L., and the Acona delegates, covering their back by shooting the Cocoons. When the Nindroid MechDragon attempts to strike Wyldfyre with his laser sword, Riyu catches his second wind and strikes the MechDragon, knocking it down and defeating it and the Cocoons shut down. Beatrix insists that the fight is not over, but Sora sarcastically declares, "All hail the Good Empress." The battle ends as the Loyalists fall back.

With the Weapon of Desolation inactive, Kai and Nya were both relieved and impressed, questioning how Lloyd had known his plan would succeed. Lloyd confessed he had no certainty but reflected on his ability as the Green Ninja to form connections between forces. However, before he can finish his thought, an overload of energy occurs. Lloyd then explodes into pure Life energy, destroying his physical body, and scattering energy particles everywhere. As the Weapon of Desolation falls into the Abyss of Eternal Night, ridding the Merged Lands of the Weapon forever, Kai, Nya, Jay, Cole, and Zane permanently lose their Draconus Forms due to Lloyd's apparent demise. The five remaining ninja then mourn the supposed death of their leader and close friend, acknowledging that he risked everything to save them. Having snapped out of his state, Zilvar was confused about what happened to his summoned people. Kai furiously turns on him, but Jay intervenes, reminding Kai that Zilvar was possessed and therefore unaware of his actions. Horrified by what he has done under the Weapon's influence, Zilvar feels immense guilt, while Cole remorsefully admits that the team shouldn't have made Zilvar carry the Weapon of Desolation in the first place, to which Zilvar sadly realizes that the Green Ninja is gone. Devastated with losing Lloyd, Jay refuses to accept that he is gone especially now that he has finally recovered all of his memories of the team. As the ninja notice that their Elemental Powers have stabilized, Jay hopes it means that Lloyd was still out there helping them, though Nya gently suggests it might be his final gift to them. Zane recalls that ever since the time at the Temple of the Green Ninja, back at the Whispering Wood, Lloyd had possessed a new and different energy, one that he can no longer sense as he solemnly looks at Lloyd's golden sword.

Amidst the destruction at the damaged Summit, Percival despairs, believing the meeting to be disastrous and that any hope for a unity agreement between the realms has been reduced to dust. However, the Bone Regent steps forward with a surprising shift in perspective; She admits that she initially arrived with no intention of cooperating with "surface dwellers," but after having witnessed the selflessness of the Ninja who risked their lives to protect others convinces her of the necessity of unity. She declares that the Underworld is ready to unite for their common defense and challenges the other leaders to do the same. Moved by the Bone Regent’s speech and the young Ninja's bravery, all present factions agree to band together. Pontifex Magicus confirms their unanimous decision as everyone cheers, bringing a rare smile of hope to Percival’s face.

While Arin and Frak restrain Beatrix, Sora works quickly to activated the machine. Beatrix protests in defiance as the device is activated to swap their minds back into her original body. Upon regaining control of her own body, Zeatrix immediately voices her deep hatred for her sister. When Arin checks to see if the machine successfully worked, Zeatrix confirms that Beatrix’s mind has been sent back to her own body. She insists that Cyrus Borg must heal Beatrix so she is fully conscious and aware while enduring her punishment: rotting in Imperium Prison. Hearing her ruthless declaration, Arin dryly remarks that they can definitively tell the real Zeatrix is back.

At the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms, Zilvar expresses guilt, warning the Ninja that they are far too quick to forgive him after everything he has taken from them. Nya quickly reassures him that he isn't to blame for what happened. Jay backs Nya up, pointing out that Ras was the true mastermind behind his actions. Drawing from his own experiences, Jay warns that Ras is a master at manipulation who took advantage of Zilvar. Refusing to make excuses, Zilvar insists on taking full responsibility for his choices, declaring that he must find a way to make amends for his actions. He bids the Ninja farewell. As the Ninja depart, Jay turns back one last time to watch Zilvar go off on his own path toward redemption.

Meanwhile, Arin, Frak, and Sora return to the Monastery of Spinjitzu. Arin tries to defend Sora's parents by pointing out that they activated their comms when it mattered so the team could locate them. Sora rejects Arin's defense, insisting that her parents had used her. However, she soon finds a letter along with a plate of brownies that her parents had left behind. Reading their letter, Sora finds that they had left to start a new life. In the letter's contents, her parents admit that they originally came to the Monastery to hide from the authorities, but explained that spending time with Sora, the ninja, Arin's parents showed them a better path. Expressing regret for not recognizing what an impressive young woman she had become, they confess their love for their daughter and bids her goodbye. Touched and heartfelt by their words, Sora sheds tears as she finishes reading the letter, showing that she has forgiven them despite their past relationship.

Arin and Sora visit Dr. LaRow in Imperium Prison to confront her. Arin reveals that they caught Johnny Courageous, who was the Loyalist that infiltrated the Imperium Guards, smuggling out LaRow's orders. Sora points out the irony: while LaRow had the intelligence to direct the Loyalists, Cryptor, and Operation Mentax, she is still blind to how dangerous and unpredictable Beatrix truly was. LaRow justifies her reliance on Beatrix, explaining that while she herself can organize and manipulate, she lacks the ability to inspire followers like Beatrix could. Sora sharply counters, noting Beatrix never inspired anyone and that she merely ruled through demands. LaRow warns them that sharing power among everyone dilutes it and that the new United Lands will inevitably collapse under its own weight. Standing firm, Sora rejects her worldview, asserting that unity makes them stronger, not weaker. With that final exchange, Arin and Sora walk away, leaving LaRow behind in her cell.

Back at the Monastery, the original Ninja return and are excitedly greeted by Frak and Sora. However, the joyful reunion quickly turns somber when Wyldfyre and Arin notice the ninjas' sorrowful expressions and ask what happened and where Lloyd is. Though the ninja were too grief-stricken to speak, the look on their faces made the young ninja realize Lloyd is gone, leaving the entire team heartbroken. In a formal gathering, Percival delivers an inspiring speech, honoring Lloyd as a hero and leader whose vision made peace between the Merged Lands possible. He announces that the first official business of the United Lands is to honor the legendary Green Ninja and all those who carry on his legacy. Percival concludes by thanking the team, declaring that the world has never been safer or its future brighter. As the crowd applauds, the Ninja, who received medals of honor, bows in quiet reverence, carrying forward the memory and ideals of their fallen friend.

Jay still remains at the Monastery and stands by Master Wu's portrait, next to a newly-made portrait of Lloyd. Speaking aloud to Lloyd's memory, Jay reveals why he skipped the formal ceremony: he firmly refuses to attending a memorial for someone he is convinced isn't actually dead. After he finished and walked away, Lloyd's "ghost" form suddenly appears before him. Jay follows the ethereal manifestation, which leads him toward Master Wu's study and to a secret compartment. He presses a secret button concealed on the pillar, unlocking a hidden compartment. Looking inside, Jay finds that two key artifacts are missing: the Icon of the Source Dragon of Motion and the Realm medallion, the very same medallion he remembered seeing years ago at Four Weapons Blacksmith back in his childhood.

At the Monastery of the Gates, Master Wu remains trapped inside a Protective container with the Realm medallion glowing and floating above him. Ras emerges from one of the portal gates, carrying the re-forged Icon of the Source Dragon of Energy in his possession. Addressing the captive Wu, Ras taunts him, declaring that the small win Wu’s pupils think they secured was merely a minor skirmish. He asserts that true victory is closer than ever and that his master will ultimately triumph. Leaving Wu behind, Ras steps out of the Monastery of the Gates and into the Land of Eternal Dawn. Raising his Dragon Icons high into the air, he lets out a powerful roar while glaring toward the Shadow Dojo, setting up the next phase of his master's scheme.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Morro can be seen at the memorial of Lloyd. This is the first time the original ninja, aside from Lloyd, have seen Morro post-Merge. However, no interactions between them have occurred yet on-screen.
  • This episode marks the fourth time a ninja's physical body was destroyed/disappeared. The other times include:
    • In the Season 3: Rebooted episode "The Titanium Ninja", Zane sacrificed himself to defeat the Overlord.
    • In the Season 5: Possession episode "The Temple on Haunted Hill", Cole turned into a ghost after going back for the Scroll of Airjitzu when he dropped.
    • In the Season 14: Seabound episode "Nyad", Nya merged with the Endless Sea to remove the sea water from Jay's lungs and defeat Wojira.
  • The ending of this episode is very similar to that of "Birthright", as both feature Ras emerging from the Monastery of Gates with a new Source Dragon Icon as he goes to the Shadow Dojo.
  • The pose of Lloyd's memorial statue is similar to that in the Part 1 intro and the stance he's in before transforming into his Draconus Form.
  • This is the final appearance of Draconus Form, having been lost due to Lloyd's physical body being destroyed/disappearing.
  • Arin's line, "Together forever, right?", is the same line as in "The Shatter Dragon".
  • It is currently unknown who will serve as the formal leader for all the remaining ninja. Given that Rontu is the only other master living in the Monastery of Spinjitzu, this would make her suitable for the role. However, this is unlikely, as she had already retired from being a warrior and had earlier refused to be one of the ninja's masters (alongside Egalt) in "Enter the City of Temples". However, given the ninjas' recent loss and as she retains her martial arts skills, Rontu may possibly change her mind later on.
  • This is the final episode in which 'The Merged Whip' appears in the intro.
  • Excluding Jay since he didn't attend, P.I.X.A.L. is the only ninja not among her fellow ninja on the steps in the memorial scene.
  • Right when the young ninja find out about what happened to Lloyd, Kai can be seen briefly crying in his hands right before the scene transitions to Lloyd's memorial. Kai did something similar when Nya died in "The Way Back".

Cultural references[]

  • The episode title either refers to the 1782 French epistolary novel by Pierre Choderlos de Laclos or a modern Cold War-themed video game in Steam.

Continuity[]

  • The flashback in the cold open shows Wu already in his old appearance before Ray and Maya's capture. Due to the Battle Between Brothers and formers' capture happening close to each other, it is not fully clear if this is an animation error, or Wu had rapidly aged after Garmadon's banishment.
    • Alternatively, it's possible Jay's memories were slightly altered due to the time passage between the event and Jay's recollection. This occurred similarly with Lloyd during his recollection of his training session with Wu.[3]
    • It is also possible that Ray and Maya's capture by Krux happened after Garmadon's banishment to the Underworld as it has been implied that Wu's rapid aging happened after Garmadon's banishment.
  • In said flashback, Wu, Edna and Ray are looking upon an item that depicts the realms. Wu mentions that a building that's similar across all of them can help connect the realms and stabilize them. He's referring to each of the Monasteries across each of the realms.
  • Lloyd's sacrifice is similar to Zane's in "The Titanium Ninja" where after shutting down the Weapon of Desolation, Lloyd's mortal body was inadvertently obliterated just like how Zane's original body was after absorbing the power of the Golden Armor.
    • Another similarity to the aforementioned episode above, is that a statue is built in Lloyd's honor just like Zane once had and there is a picture of Lloyd but it's at the Monastery rather than at the memorial like Zane's picture was.
    • There is a similarity as well to "Endings".
      • In that episode, the ninja return to the Monastery just after losing Cole and Wu asks them what's wrong, the same thing happens in this episode where after losing Lloyd, the young ninja see something is wrong and Wyldfyre is the one to ask what happened, both times the ninja are struggling to keep it together.
      • Similarly again, both episodes also seem to show that both ninja are not fully dead as Cole survived his fall into the Oni cloud and Lloyd was able to appear to Jay in a similar ghost form to Wu.
  • Lloyd's "ghost" form leads Jay to Master Wu's study.
    • It hinted at why Ras came to the Monastery and forced Alfonzo Frohicky to take him to Master Wu's study in "When Doves Cry".
    • In the secret compartment that Lloyd leads Jay to there are two cutout spots.
