Full episode[]

Plot

Plot[]

Lloyd, Arin, Euphrasia, and Zane arrive at the Temple of the Green Ninja, where Lloyd is anxious about the unknown challenges that lie ahead, having been fixated on the quest itself. Arin suggests that the temple might be overseen by a spirit guardian, just like the Temple of the Dragon Cores. However, Lloyd is acutely aware that the ritual he must undergo to unlock his True Potential as the Green Ninja is fraught with danger, as the Arc Dragon of Life warned that it could be fatal. As they approach the entrance, Lloyd uses his powers to part the curtain of vines, but when Arin attempts to follow, he is halted by an invisible barrier. After a moment of confusion, Lloyd realizes that only he can pass since he's the Green Ninja. Arin urges Lloyd to reconsider the risks involved. Despite his friends' concerns, Lloyd felt responsible for embracing his destiny and rectifying everything. He steps into the temple, leaving his friends behind as he is transported to a grassy field, where he is suddenly ensnared by roots.

Lloyd is greeted by a mysterious figure in black clothing who shows him the symbols of the seven Source Dragons and discs that look to represent each of the Realms around them, before one turns red and flies off. Lloyd then tries to ask the figure what that was, only to be greeted by the question, "Why ask me, old man?" by the figure, revealing itself to be a physical manifestation of his young self before he joined the Ninja.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Lloyd's original theme from Season 1: Rise of the Snakes plays when he first meets his younger counterpart.
  • This marks the first instance where Riyu transforms into his new form as a Dragon Ninja to battle against the Mutation Monster.
    • Scenes of Riyu remembering himself, Arin, and Sora in the past include him training to become a ninja and performing Spinjitzu for the first time from "The Merge: Part 2", "The Blood Moon", "To Mysterium", and "Chaos Reigns".

Continuity[]

  • This episode marks Beatrix’s first appearance since "The Power Within", as she was pushed into a MergeQuake by Arin and wasn't seen since. Among its many changes, her Empress Mech still bears the hole punched through its chest by Arin's Object Spinjitzu.
  • The wooden disks seen in Lloyd's vision of the Source Dragons bear resemblance to those from the Spirit of the Temple's story in "The Temple of the Dragon Cores," which were used to represent the realms.
    • One of the disks turns red before flying off. The total count of the disks in this episode is 18, possibly hinting at another realm have once existed.
  • This episode marks the first time Jillian Michaels has voiced Lloyd in Ninjago: Dragons Rising, as she voiced Young Lloyd, a variant of Lloyd that he meets in this episode.
    • This also marks the first time she has voiced Lloyd in a season since Season 7: The Hands of Time, as she was replaced by Sam Vincent the following season.
  • Despite appearing in Season 3 of Ninjago: Dragons Rising, this is the first episode of this show where Morro is called by his name, as his name was never said on-screen, and he was credited as "Departed Soul". Additionally, after the events in Chaos Reigns, it is revealed that Morro’s soul has returned and continued guarding the Spectral Lands.
  • Barry and Jenny mention that they have not had Merlopian seaweed cake since their honeymoon, which implies that at some point after the events of Seabound and before the Merge, Merlopian culture was already semi-mainstream among Ninjago citizens.
  • Sora is now the third Ninja of the younger generation to perform a full Spinjitzu tornado. The first Ninja to do so was Arin in "Truth and Lies" and second Ninja to do so was Riyu in "Chaos Reigns".
    • Unlike Arin and Riyu, no one acts surprised or joyful when she achieved her full Spinjitzu tornado, with no one surprised at her development. Even the original ninja have no reaction to it.
