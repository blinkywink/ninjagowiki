Overview

Seed and Soil is the fifth episode of the fourth season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the 65th episode overall.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

Both Riyu and Lloyd undergo trials that might change them forever, while the Loyalist plot draws close to home in Ninjago.[1]

Netflix: Sora and Frak hunt the Elemental Monster aboard the Explorer's Club ship, while Lloyd faces a crucial ritual at the Temple of the Green Ninja.

Extended: Lloyd enters the Temple of the Green Ninja, but what he finds inside shocks him - maybe he won't survive the trial. Meanwhile, Sora, Frak and Wyldfyre are under attack in Umbrasylvis with their friend's life hanging in the balance. Back in Ninjago, the Loyalists draw closer to their goal and their leader is revealed.

Trivia

Trivia[]

Trivia[]

  • Lloyd's original theme from Season 1: Rise of the Snakes plays when he first meets his Young Lloyd variant.

Continuity[]

  • This episode marks Beatrix’s first appearance since "The Power Within" as she was pushed into a MergeQuake by Arin and hasn't been seen since.
  • This episode marks the first time Jillian Michaels has voiced Lloyd in Ninjago: Dragons Rising, as she is Young Lloyd the variant of Lloyd that he meets in this episode.
    • This also marks the first time she has voiced Lloyd in a season since Season 7: The Hands of Time as she was replaced by Sam Vincent the following season.
  • Despite appearing in Season 3 of Ninjago: Dragons Rising, this is the first episode of this show where Morro is called by his name, as previously his name was never said on-screen and he was credited as a "Departed Soul".
