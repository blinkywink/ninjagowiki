A Frozen Scar is the fifteenth episode of the fourth season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the 75th episode overall.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

LEGO's YouTube Channel[]

❄️ Lloyd and his hand-picked team are stepping past the borders of the known realms and entering the treacherous Never Kingdoms! As the temperature drops and the shadows lengthen, the line between ally and enemy begins to blur. Can the Green Ninja lead his squad through the frozen unknowns, or will this ultimate weapon rewrite the fate of the realms forever?[1]

Streaming services[]

Tubi[]

Crossing into the Never Kingdoms to destroy the Weapon of Desolation, Lloyd's team meets old friends and encounters new dangers.

Roku[]

After sharing his tragic past, the Ninja question their new ally’s loyalty. In the Never Kingdoms, a cryptic warning shakes their trust as they seek help from a Formling guide before the Weapon of Desolation escapes.

Extended[]

After their new ally shares his tragic past with the Ninja, Kai suspects the stoic warrior might be hiding his true allegiance. They cross into the Never Kingdoms and find a familiar sight - and familiar faces. A cryptic warning in the night shakes the Ninja's trust, but they find help with a Formling guide before the Weapon of Desolation can escape containment.[2]

Plot

Plot[]

The flashback begins in the Village of the Tiger Tribe, Mei tries to reassure Zilvar that the tremors affecting their home will pass, citing the predictions of the elders who read stones. Disillusioned by superstition and fearing for the safety of his tribe and future family, Zilvar sets out alone to find a secure home. His dangerous journey takes him through the perilous Lava Wastes where he witnesses two Fire Dragons fighting and up the cliffs of the Forest of Spirits, where he finally finds a peaceful sanctuary. Zilvar returns to his village full of hope, only to discover it completely swallowed by catastrophic tremors. Blaming himself for not being there to save his people or his beloved Mei, Zilvar carves the names of the lost into a fallen memorial stone and wanders the Wyldness aimlessly. In the present, Nya expresses deep sympathy for Zilvar's loss. Lloyd mentions Ras, suggesting he might be a surviving member of Zilvar's tribe. Zilvar dismisses this, explaining that "Ras" was never written on the memorial stone and that Ras likely hails from another tribe, citing how vast the Wyldness is. Jay smells something burning. Kai quickly defends himself, but attention shifts immediately to the containment unit housing the Weapon of Desolation. Zane analyzes the containment unit and warns that its structural integrity is failing as energy from the Weapon leaks out. Cole remarked that it seemed like the weapon was trying to escape, admitting that the notion was creeping him out. Kai brushed off the idea that the weapon could be trying to do anything, but quickly grew nervous as at the thought that the weapon could somehow be alive. Meanwhile, Lloyd stressed the need to throw the Weapon of Desolation to the Abyss of Eternal Night before it destroys them instead.

The original ninja, Zilvar and Grimtak arrive at the border of the Never Kingdoms, which features an invisible barrier. Kai, Cole, and Zilvar exchange quick banter before Lloyd notes that the border looks far less intimidating than warned, while Jay jokingly suggests sending Kai across first to test for explosive traps. Nya spots two travelers, Zazkzaz and Pebbleton, attempting to establish a new trade route. Nya warns them to turn back because first-time visitors cannot cross, but they try anyway and are blocked by the barrier, confirming the danger is real. Recalling Reggie's advice, Lloyd realizes that because they visited the Never-Realm prior to the Merge, they should qualify for the barrier's exception. Impatient, Zilvar and Grimtak step through the barrier safely first, followed immediately by the original ninja, who successfully cross and prepare to scout ahead.

The original ninja, Zilvar and Grimtak discover Lloyd's old Titan Mech and a new monastery in the Never Kingdoms. Zane notes that this supports the theory of new Monasteries manifesting across realms post-Merge, adding that functional portal gates would have sped up their return home. At the Monastery, which functions like a shrine built by the Fishers and Formlings to honor the ninja who ended the Ice Emperor's reign. Jay lightheartedly complains when Zilvar gets a warmer welcome from local children than they do. Nya warmly greets Sorla. Refecting on his past as the Ice Emperor, Zane uses his power to create an ice flower and reflects on how his abilities were manipulated. He notes that if the Weapon of Desolation can corrupt minds like the Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu did, their mission is all the more urgent. A wolf playfully bumps into Lloyd and transforms into Akita. She expresses joy at seeing him again, noting that while time used to move faster in her realm, the Merge has brought their timeline into sync. Akita's companion, Beniro, catches up to them. Akita introduces Beniro to Lloyd and reveals that they are to be "form-bonded" at the next changing of leaves. After a moment of initial confusion, Lloyd offers his warm congratulations.

Lloyd explains their mission to reach the Abyss of the Eternal Night. Akita notes that the abyss is considered a place of ill omen by her people, surrounded by dense fog that requires a guide capable of navigating strictly by scent. When Lloyd assumes Akita will guide them, she reveals she cannot as she is en route to Ninjago. She explains that she volunteered as the Never Kingdoms' delegate to the Summit of the Merged Lands in the hope of meeting him. Although disappointed, Lloyd accepts her political obligations. Before waving goodbye to Lloyd, Akita promises she knows someone else who can navigate the fog. Beniro then steps up to take on the role, expressing excitement for the journey to the abyss and announcing that they will depart at dawn.

While the original ninja, Zilvar and Grimtak are sleeping, Kai begins sleepwalking and muttering, waking up Nya, Lloyd, and Zane. Speaking through Kai's body, Master Wu's voice issues a warning: "Beware... the Beast Warrior". As Lloyd recognizes Master Wu and Zilvar asks what is happening, Kai kneels down. Master Wu's "ghost" form emerges directly from Kai's mouth and passes straight through the portal gate.

In the morning, Jay buys fish while Lloyd places apples into a bucket while Kai is holding. He coughs from a scratchy throat, prompting Jay to question if the ball of light was truly Master Wu. Kai recalls hearing Wu's spirit form before after unearthing the remains of the Source Dragon of Balance, though Lloyd points out that Arin confirmed Wu never passed through the Spectral Lands. Kai remains skeptical, noting the event felt "beyond the grave," but Lloyd insists on taking his uncle's warning seriously. Kai spots Zilvar buying supplies with Grimtak and points out to Jay that Zilvar fitting the description of a "Beast Warrior" traveling to the same secluded land is a strange coincidence. While Lloyd agrees they can't take chances, Jay defends Zilvar, stating of saved his life, deserves to start fresh after the Gladiator pit, and is more trustworthy than a ball of light coming out of Kai's mouth. Lloyd approaches Zilvar while he feeds Grimtak and tells him he deserves to start his new life as a free man, thanking him for bringing them this far. Zilvar handshakes Lloyd, offering his gratitude and respect before wishing the ninja safe travels. Watching the exchange while filling a water bucket, Sorla observes to Nya that Zilvar could be the Beast Warrior due to the darkness born from his pain, to which Nya agrees that pain often drives good people to do bad things. Jay takes the driver's seat of the Land Bounty while Beniro transforms into a fox, telling the walking ninja to keep up as they set off.

As the Land Bounty follows Beniro to the forest, Zane reports that local interference makes tracking their route impossible. Lloyd warns that they cannot afford to lose sight of Beniro. The Containment Unit sparks as the Weapon of Desolation charges up and releases a chaotic wave of energy. Zane compares the buildup to a boiling pot releasing steam, warning Jay to drive carefully since any disturbance could trigger a full detonation. Jay loses sight of Beniro just as falling boulders crash down around them. After maneuvering to dodge the debris, a final rockslide blocks their path entirely and causes the Land Bounty's engine to stall. Inspecting the rocks, Cole notices distinct claw marks on the fallen boulders. Beniro reverts to his human form and rejoins the team, explaining that he tracked Lloyd's scent in the fog and suggesting that the Land Bounty's vibrations likely triggered an avalanche off the steep, hidden cliffs. He advises making camp for the night since navigation will become far more treacherous in the dark.

As the group settles around the campfire, Beniro shares the lore behind the Abyss of Eternal Night. He explains that the original Elemental Master of Form roamed as a Legendary Beast, revered by the nameless tribe since she was capable to taken the form of any animal, until a cursed thorn lodged in her paw and drove her into a generations-long rampage. With the assistance of the First Spinjitzu Master, the Master of Form was finally freed from the thorn's influence. Out of gratitude, she gifted her shapeshifting abilities to the nameless tribe, establishing the Formlings. When the First Spinjitzu Master destroyed the cursed thorn, its destruction carved out the Abyss of Eternal Night—a place so dangerous he strictly forbade anyone from approaching it. Kai realizes that Formling shapeshifting stems directly from Elemental Powers, practically making them honorary Elemental Masters. Lloyd thanks Beniro for defying his tribe's sacred taboos to help their mission. Beniro simply replies that he would do anything for Akita, a sentiment Lloyd shares.

Zilvar walks around the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms, softly remarking to himself as a leaf lands on his shoulder that Mei would have loved it there. The monastery's portal gate suddenly flashes red and opens, interrupting his solitude. Ras emerges through the portal and addresses him directly as "Zilvar of the Wyldness," causing Zilvar to startle and drop the leaf in shock.

As the original ninja arrive at the site, Beniro reverts to his human form. Zane points out that it is no ordinary frozen river, and Beniro explains that the Boreal River is a lingering remnant of the Ice Emperor's reign, a cursed "frozen scar" on the land that refuses to thaw in spring or summer. Zane initially suggests avoiding crossing it, but Lloyd notes that the failing state of their Containment Unit leaves them no time for a detour. Zane climbs atop the Land Bounty to initiate a aerial separation maneuver, but an alarm blares as the separation mechanism jams. Nya observes that the Land Bounty's systems have rapidly frosted over, prompting Kai to sarcastically remind her of the ice's cursed nature. Accepting that flying over is no longer an option, Lloyd tells Zane they have to cross the river directly. As the ninja head inside and Kai and Cole move toward their vehicles, Beniro asks Lloyd to ride inside with them, explaining that Formlings refused to touch the cursed ice.

As the Land Bounty, Cole's combiner bike, and Kai's race buggy slowly make their way across the frozen river, Beniro mentions how Akita praised the Green Ninja for saving their people. Lloyd humbly credits Akita for her role, but Beniro quips that he thought Lloyd would be taller. When Lloyd deflects to focus on the trek, Beniro worries he caused offense and apologizes. Mid-conversation, the Weapon of Desolation suddenly activates, corrupting Beniro. As Nya asks what is happening, Zane identifies that Beniro is reacting directly to the weapon's dark power. Under its influence, Beniro transforms into a feral fox form, snarling and barking at the original ninja team. Lloyd urges the others not to hurt the innocent Formling, but the feral Beniro snarls at Nya, Lloyd, and Jay, snatches the Weapon of Desolation with his jaw, and flees. Jay shouts for the team to stop him as Beniro makes his escape downward. Kai hears movement right before the feral Beniro lands, snarls, and darts past, forcing Cole and Kai to dodge. Kai urges Cole into pursuit. As they give chase on their vehicles, the Weapon of Desolation activates, summoning massive ice spikes from the surface that the duo must weave through. Nya, Jay, Zane, and Lloyd exit the Land Bounty just as the ice underneath begins to crack. Zane attempts to use his ice elemental powers to stabilize the area, but the river originally born from his own power, reacts dangerously to the Weapon's dark energy and causes his power to fail. Nya and Jay stay behind to salvage the Land Bounty while Zane and Lloyd sprint after Beniro. The Weapon of Desolation activates on the ice floor, summoning a horde of Ice Monsters. Lloyd transforms into his Draconus Form, linking with Zane to grant him as well. Together, they slash, kick, and slam through the monsters with their enhanced claws and combat skills. Meanwhile, Jay fires up the Land Bounty's thrusters, not to fly, but to float above the collapsing ice sheet.

As Kai and Cole circle the weapon, a shockwave freezes their vehicles' wheels. Kai uses his fire to melt the ice while Lloyd calls on Zane to bring the ice under control. Zane hesitates, fearful that tapping into the ice will allow the Ice Emperor's lingering corruption to reclaim him. Lloyd reassures him that he isn't alone anymore, keeping the monsters at bay so Zane can focus. When Zane attempts to reach the Weapon of Desolation, it unleashes a defensive pulse that wards him off. Beniro lunges at Zane, but Grimtak knocks the feral Formling aside. Zilvar suddenly reappears, declaring he has something vital to take care of. As Beniro pivots to attack Lloyd, he urges Zilvar to bypass the fight and claim the Weapon, noting that Zilvar is the only one who can safely touch it. Riding Grimtak, Zilvar dodges Beniro's feral leaps and navigating past ice spikes to grab the Weapon of Desolation. With the Weapon in safe hands, Lloyd signals Zane to strike. Zane leaps down, slamming the ground to release a localized shockwave that instantly thaws Kai and Cole's vehicles and stabilizes the ice beneath the Land Bounty. As the team catches their breath, Lloyd approaches the cornered Beniro, pleading with him to fight the corruption by remembering Akita. Hearing Lloyd's words, Beniro closes his eyes, calms down, and untransforms back into his human form.

As the Land Bounty, Cole's combiner bike, and Kai's race buggy finish crossing the river, Lloyd and Zane revert from their Draconus Forms. Jay thanks Zilvar for the last-minute save, calling him a "good egg," which Zilvar takes literally, replying that he was not hatched from an egg. When Cole asks what brought him back, Zilvar simply states he had a feeling they might need him. A restored Beniro apologizes once more for his actions, explaining that he can no longer guide them to the Abyss because the Weapon's influence is too dangerous for him. Lloyd assures him they understand and that Zilvar will assist them instead. Beniro hands Lloyd a map he created to guide their path and shares that he will return to the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms to wait for Akita. He wistfully reflects on his love for Akita and notes that she and Lloyd are alike in prioritizing their people above all else, before transforming into a fox and running off. Inside the Land Bounty, Lloyd, Cole, Jay, Nya, Kai, and Zane inspect the ruined Containment Unit. Nya realizes that Master Wu's cryptic warning about the "Beast Warrior" was referring to Beniro's feral corruption rather than Zilvar. Kai questions whether they can truly trust Zilvar to carry the dark weapon all the way to the Abyss. Lloyd acknowledges Kai's concerns but points out that Zilvar saved them all today and, with the Containment Unit completely destroyed, they have no other choice.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Glen Lakin stated this is his favorite episode he has written for the series.[4]
  • This is the second episode where Cole uses his Storm Suit hood with his Draconus Suit.
  • This episode marks the debut of the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms.
  • Formlings are revealed to have gotten their shapeshifting abilities from the first Elemental Master of Form.
  • Sorla being still alive while she was already an eldery human in Season 11, could mean that time in the pre-Merge Never Realm, was passing only a little faster than in Ninjago.
  • The Never Kingdoms' barrier pushes out those who have never been to the Never-Realm pre-Merge.
    • It's unknown if the barrier's rules could be bypassed by using the portal gate if all the portal gates were still working for others than only Ras.
  • Akita appears in this episode, but her brother Kataru doesn't.
  • In his "ghost" form, Wu was able to possess Kai to warn the ninja before leaving and going towards the portal gate. It's indicates that not all of Wu's "ghost" orbs were captured by Ras.
    • This scene has Wu's only speaking lines in Dragons Rising Season 4 in the present day.
  • In the beginning flashback, there's a monument to fallen tigers in the Wyldness. Two of the names are Tormak and Tazar, possibly the very same ones from Legends of Chima.
  • Akita's transformation between forms now has a visual effect instead of the smooth transition between forms.
  • Lloyd reunites with Akita and Nya reunites with Sorla, leaving Cole as the only ninja to not reunite with the one they formed the closest connection with in the Never-Realm before the Merge, that being Krag. Krag's fate is unknown.
  • This is the first episode where none of the young ninja appear in any capacity.

Continuity[]

  • This episode marks the first appearance of Akita and Sorla since "Awakenings", and is the only episode in this season in which they speak.
    • This makes them the sixteenth and seventeenth named characters from the original series aside from the ninja to appear in Ninjago: Dragons Rising.
  • This episode marks the return of Lloyd's Titan Mech, now rebuilt and sporting the Legacy redesign.
    • The redesigned mech first appeared in the 15th Anniversary Whip.
  • Zane recalls his actions as the Ice Emperor in the Ice Chapter of Season 11. However, this is the first time he's acknowledging them on-screen. The first time he does this is his story in Quest for the Lost Powers. He even mentions the Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu that was once his frozen scepter.
    • This continues humanizing his character, as Season 4 Part 1 did before, addressing fans' concerns of Zane's more robotic personality.
    • Lloyd reminds Zane that the Ice Emperor was a separate person. He most likely was referring to the fact that whilst Zane's body did terrible things, it wasn't his mind nor soul.
  • The title refers to the Boreal River, being "a frozen scar on our land", according to Beniro.
  • Jay suggesting that Kai should go first to test the Never Kingdom's border could be a reference to Kai suggesting that Jay go get the part needed for the original Land Bounty from Beohernie: "It should be someone expendable. Like Jay."
  • Kai tells Jay that he heard Master Wu's voice before, referencing when Kai was led to the Monastery of Spinjitzu's portal gate, and when he, Cole and Frak unearthed the remains of the Source Dragon of Balance in "The Screaming Earth."
  • Lloyd references when Arin told him that Master Wu never passed through to the Spectral Lands in "Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon"
  • This episode marks the first time Jay has said Master Wu's name in Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the first time since "Kryptarium Prison Blues", 97 episodes before this one came out.
  • Cole indirectly mentions Bizarro Cole when he says he met another Cole once and claimed that Cole didn't like cake.[5]
    • This makes it the first time the Bizarro ninja have been acknowledged since their appearance in "Double Trouble".

Errors[]

  • When Zane says, "Separation failure. We're jammed.", his voice filter is not applied.
    • This is the first time, albeit erroneously, that Zane has spoken without his titanium voice filter in Dragons Rising.
  • When Zane creates an ice flower, there's no frosty air effects. In the next scene, there are.
  • Cole says that Bizarro Cole was nothing like him and that he didn't like cake, however, when Garmadon created the Bizarro ninja, he said "I have made you to be equal to the ninja" which should mean Bizarro Cole has the same likes and dislikes as Cole and even looks exactly like Cole just with pale skin and red eyes. It is also unknown how he knew Bizarro Cole didn't like cake since they didn't come face to face until their fight in the hallway of Darkley's Boarding School.
