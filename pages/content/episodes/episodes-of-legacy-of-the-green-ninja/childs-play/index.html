Overview

— Sensei Wu

Child's Play is the fifth episode of the second season of Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu and the 18th episode overall. It aired on Cartoon Network on August 15, 2012 in the United States.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

The Ninjas are turned into children by the Mega-Weapon.[1]

When the Ninja interrupt Garmadon from using his Mega-Weapon to 'turn back the clock' and bring to life the fossils of a monster, the Ninja are hit and turned into kids and are now forced to enlist Lloyd's help to battle the awakened monster.[2]

Plot

Plot[]

The episode begins with the Destiny's Bounty flying over the Jamanakai Village, with Lloyd lamenting that he must train and not play with others or enjoy his youth. Nya then tells the ninja that Lord Garmadon has been spotted at the Museum. As the ninja leave, Lloyd complains to Nya about being too old for comics, but too young to fight, before she offers him to fight her.

Once there, Lord Garmadon tells the Serpentine he is going to revive the extinct Grundle in another attempt to destroy the ninja. As he is using the Mega Weapon, the ninja watch, then burst in and interrupt the process.

Garmadon leaves in a hurry, while the Serpentine Generals steal a golden sarcophagus for compensation. The ninja chase after them but fall behind. The Generals can't fit the sarcophagus through a manhole with them, so they left it behind and escape. The ninja then realize they have been turned into kids. When the police arrive on the scene, they accuse the ninja of the theft and place them under arrest. They take them back to the museum the following morning to apologize for their actions. The museum director accepts their apologies, but questions where the Grundle fossil has disappeared to. The ninja realize the Grundle must have been revived after all, and with the cops not believing them, the ninja escape and disguise themselves and run off with some school kids that are on a field trip. The Grundle appears on top of the museum, scaring a crowd of people in the surrounding area.

Sensei Wu and Nya, worried that the ninja haven't returned yet, leave Lloyd behind to go search for them. As Lloyd is playing a video game, he receives a call from Jay telling him to meet them at a pizza parlor and to bring their weapons, since they can't ride the bus without an adult. Lloyd is confused but quickly arrives. When they meet up, Kai informs Lloyd of the situation and the group wonders what they should do about the Grundle. Lloyd, knowing exactly who would be the most knowledgeable on the creature, brings them to a comic book store and introduces them to the sales clerk, Rufus McCallister. Rufus informs him that Illuma-Swords would work well, as the Grundle is nocturnal, but the shop only gives them out as a prize for winning a comic book trivia competition. Lloyd leaves a message for Master Wu and Nya back at the Destiny's Bounty, quickly telling them of what happened and their current location. Master Wu and Nya then go to Mystake's Tea Shop for Tomorrow's Tea to turn the ninja back into their older selves and the Grundle back into bones.

Meanwhile, Lloyd loses the competition (due to the final question being from a last-issue comic that had just sold out) and the Grundle shows up. The ninja grab the Illuma-Swords to defend against the Grundle, but because they were meant as props they break easily. Lloyd attempts to take on the monster with his powers but is quickly knocked aside. Just as the Grundle is about to consume the ninja, Master Wu and Nya arrive with Tomorrow's Tea, tossing it to them and telling them to use it to age the creature back into bones, as well as themselves back into their normal states. The ninja are hesitant once they realize it would mean Lloyd growing older as well and missing out on his childhood. Lloyd, accepting the consequences, uses the tea to defeat the Grundle. The ninja are returned to normal, but now Lloyd has become their age as well. The episode ends with Rufus handing Lloyd his last-issue comic, which he says he has earned. Lloyd declines, saying he already knows how it ends.

The final scene is a comic cover where Lloyd is defensively fighting against his father under a classic comic book font-stylized title of "Green Ninja."

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Zane is turned into a child, even though he is a robot and was built with the appearance of a teenager, not of a child. This is due to the magic of the Mega Weapon.[3][4]
    • Another possible explanation is that Dr. Julien built an earlier body for Zane.[5][6]
  • This episode uses the "age regression" trope, where the main characters are regressed into kids.
  • The inaccurate Serpentine painting seen in the museum is early concept art for the Constrictai,[7] by Craig Sellars based on a sketch by Tommy Andreasen.[8]
    • "The Forgotten Element" later revealed the correct version of the painting, actually being a painting of the Anacondrai called The Charge of the Dark Brigade.
    • Next to the painting of the Constrictai, there were two other paintings. These were early concept art for the Fangpyre & the Venomari.
  • This episode is referenced in the set 70657 Ninjago City Docks, where one of the vendors is selling "Grundle Meat."
  • This is the first time the ninja are arrested.
    • They were all subsequently arrested in "Public Enemy Number One." However, these events were erased as a result of Jay's final wish in "The Way Back."
    • Later, in "A Painful Promise", all of remaining Ninja, at the time, were arrested.
    • This also means that all of the Ninja have a criminal record, except for Arin, Sora, Riyu, Wyldfyre and Frak.
  • Part of the reason Lloyd became older was so he would be able to have a spinner set.[9] However, it's unknown why this could not be done with shorter legs, as Chokun had a spinner despite having short legs.
  • The game Lloyd plays whilst Jay phones him is reused footage from "Flight of the Dragon Ninja" but hue edited.[10]
    • Lloyd first plays this game in "Darkness Shall Rise" before Skales attempts to kidnap him, using the same hued footage.
  • Jillian Michaels was originally going to be recast when Lloyd got older in this episode, but she convinced them to let her audition to voice the older Lloyd as well.[11]
    • Andrew Francis may have been set to replace her, as he is erroneously credited as "Lloyd Older" in the next episode despite not voicing any character in the series at the time.
      • Funny enough, Andrew Francis would eventually voice Lloyd in Season 5: Possession, though technically when he was under the possession of Morro.
    • Jillian Michaels would eventually be replaced by Sam Vincent as Lloyd from Season 8: Sons of Garmadon onwards.
  • On International Ninja Day in 2018, Vincent Tong shared a video of some of the voice actors recording lines for this episode.[12]

Cultural references[]

  • The title of the episode is likely a reference to the horror movie franchise of the same name.

Continuity[]

  • When Lloyd is walking into Buddy's Pizza, there is a TV in the upper left-hand corner that is showing Cole, Jay, and Zane doing what they did before they joined Sensei Wu's team (Cole mountain climbing, Jay testing out his homemade wings, and Zane meditating underwater in the frozen pond), as shown in a flashback in "The Golden Weapon."
  • When the ninja are kids, their Spinjitzu seems to be weak, because of their shorter legs.
    • However, this is an exception for young Lloyd, presumably because either he is the strongest of the ninja or because he had learned Spinjitzu with short legs in the first place.
  • Zane is asked if they were dreaming and he said Nindroids don't dream, but in "Never Trust a Snake," he seemed to dream about the Green Ninja, later through most of Season 4 about confronting a dragon, and of Aspheera and the Never-Realm in Season 11. However, these were visions (related to his sixth sense ability) rather than dreams.[13]
  • The book dropped by Principal Noble is Captain Soto's journal from "Pirates vs. Ninja."
  • This marks the last time Lloyd physically appears in his child form.
  • This episode introduces the Fritz Donnegan theme, a variation of the Green Ninja theme.
  • This episode marks the first appearance of Rufus McCallister.
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, the golden sarcophagus the Serpentine try to steal would appear in the series again, presumably after "Day of the Departed."[14]
    • This came true in "A Time of Traitors," in which Krux trapped Cyrus Borg within it.
  • In one scene, Wu says "Turn back the hands of time," similar to the title of Season 7. However, this is just a coincidence.
  • In "A Time of Traitors" and "Pause and Effect," young Kai is shown to have freckles, but he lacks them in this episode.
    • This could be due to his age being reversed rather than naturally attained, as Wu also experienced differences in appearance compared to his younger self when his age was reversed in "Golden Hour."
  • When Lloyd becomes older, he gains the same hairstyle as his father's younger self as seen in "Battle Between Brothers." Both are the LEGO hair piece originally used on Anakin from the LEGO Star Wars theme.
  • Excluding in-universe pictures, this episode marks the last time Lloyd is seen as a child up until "The Calm Inside".
  • This episode is the explanation as to why Lloyd missed the call of the Whispering Wood for the Ritual of Unrooting.

Errors[]

  • As the ninja prepare to attack Garmadon, and the Serpentine, Zane's shoulder pads are gone.
  • Kai's says "It didn't work." in Jay's voice.
    • He also speaks in Zane's voice when he says "But we didn't steal it." and in Cole's voice when he says, "This is the end, isn't it?".
  • Zane says that nindroids do not dream. However, in "Never Trust a Snake", he does have a dream.
    • This also occurs in "Wasted True Potential" and Season 4.
  • The boy, whose hat Jay stole, appears with his hat again after the ninja leave the museum.
  • When the ninja are in the comic book store and preparing to fight the Grundle, Jay picks up Kai's suit instead of his own, but later, he is wearing his suit.
    • He may have given it to Kai.
  • When Rufus asks Lloyd if he wants his comic, he is wearing shoulder pads, but in the previous shot, and the shot after the aforementioned shot, the shoulder pads are gone.
  • When Jay says that Nindroids don't turn into kids, he uses Kai's voice.
  • When the ninja were returned to normal thanks to Tomorrow's Tea, Cole asked "What happened?" in Kai's voice.
  • When the ninja turned into kids, Jay said that he told Nya to meet them at the Bounty; after their mission when it was Kai who told her that.
