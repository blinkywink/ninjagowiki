Curseworld, Part II is the tenth and final episode of the fifth season of Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu and the 54th episode overall. It premiered in the United States on July 10, 2015, on Cartoon Network.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

As the ghosts converge around and mobilize the Preeminent into a hulking giant behemoth, the Ninja use Airjitzu to fight the mobile beast in an attempt to slow it down — while, at the same time, Lloyd faces off with Morro, battling over the Realm Crystal; a fight that takes them in and out of different realms.

Plot

Plot[]

In the city of Stiix, Jay, Zane, Cole, Kai, and Nya gather around the remains of Ronin's pawn shop, as the Preeminent's tentacles emerge. Civilians desperately flee to the docks, where Sensei Wu, Misako, and Ronin agree that they need to evacuate the city. The Preeminent fully emerges, with the ninja unsuccessfully attempting to subdue it. In the meantime, Morro confronts Wu with the Realm Crystal, proclaiming that now that Lloyd is gone, he is the Green Ninja. Wu angrily retorts that he trained Morro to be a ninja that protects, not one that destroys. One of the Preeminent's tentacles seizes Misako, and Wu pursues, with Ronin ordering the civilians to ready the ferry, to be ready to leave when they return. The ninja hopelessly try to battle the Preeminent, with the Ghost Taker GT being destroyed, and the Ghost Cycle being lost trying to weaken the beast, while Wu saves Misako.

As the ninja battle the Preeminent, Lloyd awakens in the Cursed Realm and flees almost immediately when he sees a group of ghosts. This attracts the attention of the imprisoned Chen, who believes he saw Lloyd run by, but Clouse remarks that he's seeing things again. Lloyd runs through the Cursed Realm, and suddenly, he finds his father bound in chains on either arm. Lloyd embraces him and tries to break his chains, but Garmadon informs him that they're unbreakable. He then explains that the Cursed Realm is the Preeminent and the other way around. Lloyd states that now Morro has the Realm Crystal and now seems to be the Green Ninja. Garmadon tells him that he must save Ninjago and destroy the Preeminent, but Lloyd is uncertain, realizing that if he destroys the Preeminent, he'll also destroy the Cursed Realm and kill everyone inside it, including his father. Garmadon tells him that whatever happens to him, he'll always be with Lloyd and that at some point in life every boy must become a man, but it's their decision as to what kind of man they choose to be.

In Stiix, the ninja continue their desperate battle against the Preeminent and Morro's army of ghosts. Wu and Misako arrive, telling them that their only hope now is to flee the city with its citizens. The ninja flee the scene, heading to the docks where Ronin slashes apart the Preeminent's tentacles to allow citizens to get on the ship. As the ninja board, Morro arrives, with Ronin battling him using the Sword of Sanctuary. After a lengthy battle, Ronin loses the Sword, and Morro moves to retrieve it. Suddenly, Lloyd arrives, wearing his father's robes, seizing the Sword, and subduing Morro. He then orders Ronin and the ninja to sever the dock stilts and drop the Preeminent into the sea. As Lloyd battles Morro, the ninja sever many of the stilts, which causes the Preeminent to begin falling towards the sea. Morro orders his army to protect the Preeminent, as he and Lloyd are thrust into a battle that jumps from realm to realm. Back in Stiix, in order to prevent the Preeminent from being killed, Morro's army of ghosts attach houses, temples and buildings to its body to create a massive "suit" for it that would give the beast legs and prevent it from making contact with the water.

With help from Morro's army of ghosts, the Preeminent moves slowly through Stiix, heading towards the ship as it flees from the city. Kai, Cole, Jay, Zane, and Nya return to Stiix using Airjitzu, battling the ghosts and attempting to deal with the Preeminent. When the beast makes it to the docks, it is initially stopped by the water, but with help from the ghosts, it manages to wade into the sea. Sensei Wu orders everyone to throw anything they don't need off the ship so they can move faster. In the meantime, Morro and Lloyd go from realm to realm, including the Underworld and the Realm of Madness. Back in Stiix, the ninja continue battling Morro's army of ghosts, defeating such adversaries as Soul Archer only for them to re-emerge from the mouth of the Preeminent. Nya tells her fellow ninja that they aren't able to stop the Preeminent, and the best thing they can do for the time being is to protect the ship. The ninja agree and they all regroup with the civilians.

On the ship, Ronin tells Wu that they only have enough fuel to get back to Stiix, and not further away from it towards Hallows Trench. Kai then has an idea to drown the Preeminent using the trench, as it wouldn't expect the sheer drop. The ship continues on, but just as it passes the trench, the Preeminent knocks off its rudder, stopping it completely as Morro returns to Ninjago, having trapped Lloyd in a separate realm. The civilians on the ship run around in a panic, but Nya stands still. Encouraged by Wu telling her not to be afraid to fail, Nya unlocks her True Potential. In doing so, she creates a massive tidal wave that crashes into the Preeminent, sending it toppling forwards and falling into Hallows Trench. The Preeminent then sinks into the depths of the Endless Sea, destroying the Cursed Realm and killing all its inhabitants, with the Cursed Realm pulling all of the ghosts into the water except for Morro, who stays above the surface.

As the civilians and the ninja rejoice on the ship, Wu heads out over the sea on his dragon, as the ghosts are being pulled into the water and destroyed because of the Cursed Realm's destruction. As this happens, Morro attempts to fly off with the Realm Crystal, but he is being pulled down towards the water and is suddenly seized by one of the Preeminent's tentacles. Using his element of Wind, he manages to keep himself from being pulled into the water, which allows Wu to intervene and try to save him. Morro refuses Wu's help, but Wu pleads for Morro to take his hand and stand by their side. Morro thinks for a moment and then grabs Wu's hand, but Wu is unable to free him from the tentacle. Seeing that his fate is sealed, Morro tells Wu that he can only save those who want to be saved, and bids him farewell as he lets go of his hand and gives him the Realm Crystal. Morro is then pulled into the sea, where he dies as he is permanently dissolved by the water, much to Wu's despair.

Later that afternoon, the ninja regroup with Wu and Misako on the deck of the ship. Using the Realm Crystal, Wu returns Lloyd to Ninjago. Lloyd reminisces on what happened, with Wu noting him that his father's robes appear to have made him wiser. Lloyd states that the Cursed Realm is gone, as is his father, but now they're all in a better place. The ninja agree that their team remains strong, and will move forward together no matter what changes overcome them. Jay asks Lloyd what the other realms look like, and Lloyd assures him that everything looks bright, for them and for all of Ninjago.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the first episode, along with "Curseworld, Part I," to have two parts.
    • Originally, in Season 1, the episodes "The Rise of the Great Devourer" and "Day of the Great Devourer" were to be titled The Final Hour - Part 1 and The Final Hour - Part 2, respectively.[citation needed]
    • The second is "The Merge."
  • There was going to be an inappropriate joke about the Preeminent, but it was ultimately left out of the episode.[1]
    • The cut line was later discovered through a donated script: Jay - "I hope that's her mouth, cause if it isn't, I'm about to lose my lunch."[2]
      • The line was replaced with: "Ew, gross! I'm about to lose my lunch."

Continuity[]

  • Similar to the final battle with the Great Devourer in "Day of the Great Devourer," the last episode of the first season where the ninja had to protect the people of Ninjago City from the colossal serpent—in this episode, the ninja have to protect the people of Stiix from another gigantic beast, the Preeminent.
    • This would later be followed by the battle against Wojira in Seabound.
  • When Nya said that she just flip a switch but she doesn't have one. This is most likely a reference to Zane's True Potential in Tick Tock, how he turned on his memory switch to learn about his past.
  • When the Preeminent seized Misako, Morro tells Sensei Wu that the more souls the Preeminent takes, the bigger it gets. While this is never actually shown in the episode, this concept is extremely similar to that of the Great Devourer, whereas the more it eats, the bigger it gets.
  • This episode marks the first appearance of Clouse since "The Day of the Dragon." In that episode, he was inadvertently banished to the Cursed Realm by both Garmadon and himself. Clouse would return in the next episode, "Infamous," in which he would become further entangled in the series' plot.
  • The reason why Garmadon, Chen, and Clouse were not ghosts while in the Cursed Realm realm is because one's body remains intact upon entering the Cursed Realm; it's only when individuals leave the realm that they becomes ghosts, as seen with Clouse.[3] Lloyd is an exception because he is the First Spinjitzu Master's grandson[4] and because he was dragged into the Cursed Realm, not cursed.[5]
    • Garmadon and Chen were also human despite being Anacondrai when they were banished in "The Corridor of Elders." This is because the Spell of Transformation wore off when they were banished.[6]
  • Garmadon's last words to Lloyd, "Whatever happens to me, wherever I am—I will always be with you", are later echoed in "Day of the Departed," when Lloyd says, "You are still with me, dad!" when holding up the head of the statue at the Corridor of Elders while fighting Pythor.
  • Garmadon tells Lloyd, "There comes a time when every boy must become a man. What sort of man, is up to him." This may be a reference to two things that Garmadon said to Lloyd in "All of Nothing": "Every boy has a choice to grow up to be whoever they want to be," and "You're a good boy. Soon, you'll be a great man."
  • In order for Lloyd to have taken his father's robes, Garmadon would have had to have been free from the chains on his wrists, indicating that he must have escaped when the Preeminent died or he died before managing to escape. Yet, Lloyd remarks at the end of the episode that his father is gone, which leaves his fate very ambiguous. Tommy Andreasen said that Lloyd got his father's robes because of magic or the robes having lots of concealed zippers.[7][8]
  • The Skulkin Ferris wheel from the third pilot episode, "King of Shadows," appears during this episode when Lloyd and Morro are jumping between realms and accidentally land in the Underworld.
  • This is the first time Legends of Chima is seen in Ninjago.
    • The footage of Chima is edited footage taken from Legends of Chima's fifteenth episode "Ravens vs. Eagles," with Morro and Lloyd taking Razar and Eris' place. In the Ninjago version, Laval and Cragger stop moving after Laval says "where'd you guys come from?", while in the original episode, Laval and Cragger have more lines directly after that.
      • In the original script, this scene takes place in the jungle, where the characters step out and Laval says, "Don't be afraid. What tribe are you from?", to which Morro and Lloyd scream in terror.[9]
    • It was the Hageman Brothers' idea to include Chima as a realm in Ninjago.[10]
    • In Season 2 of Ninjago: Dragons Rising, it is confirmed that Chima is actually a floating island within the realm of the Wyldness.[11]
    • While it is unknown where do the events of Legends of Chima take place in the timeline,[12] the scene from "Ravens vs. Eagles" couldn't take place before the events of the series nor during the first fourteen episodes, which means that at least these fourteen episodes took place before "Curseworld, Part II", although it's also possible that this scene takes place between Legends of Chima Season 2 and Season 3 or after Season 3.
  • The water flying around when Nya unlocked her True Potential was not enough water to harm Cole as a ghost.[13]
  • Morro's line, "You can only save those who want to be saved," reflects what Wu said when explaining Morro's origins in "Ghost Story," which was "I could not teach those who would not listen."
  • Morro is, by technicality, the first Elemental Master whose death is shown in the series.
  • The Cursed Realm is the first realm in the series to be destroyed. Consequently, Djinjago began to fall apart shortly afterward and would become the second realm to be destroyed.
    • Djinjago's destruction also led to the death of most of its inhabitants, including the Caves of Bells' sole resident: the tan half of the Arc Dragon of Focus.
  • Both the antagonists of this season and the previous one are killed in this episode, all as a result of the destruction of the Cursed Realm.
    • In addition, the main and secondary antagonist of the pilot and Season 2 respectively, albeit redeemed, is also killed.
  • This is the first episode where Nya shouts "Ninja, go!".
  • Jay is able to hurt a ghost in this episode using his element - Lightning.
    • As such, every ninja's element except for Earth -- Lightning, along with Water, Fire ("Grave Danger"), Ice, and Life ("Curseworld, Part I") -- have been shown to be able to harm ghosts in some way, even though Water is the only element that can destroy them.

Errors[]

  • Cole can be seen wearing armor pads in multiple shots in this episode:
    • In the shot with Kai, Jay, Zane, and Cole when they first witness the Preeminent.
    • Just before he drives his Ghost Cycle into the Preeminent's mouth.
    • When running to the boat.
    • When he tries to catch Kai from falling into the water.
    • When he says "Not Morro, Nya!."
  • When Wu said "It's Morro," Ronin's mouth moves instead.
  • When the last of the civilians board the boat, Cole can be seen without his green ghostly glow, and is also not transparent.
  • When the ninja are running to the boat, Jay and Kai have their 2011 torso and legs.[14]
    • A similar error is later seen in "Wishmasters," where Jay uses his 2011 legs for a brief moment.
  • Garmadon's clothes were torn in "The Corridor of Elders" when he became an Anacondrai, but his clothes were not torn in this episode when he was human.
  • Kai should have not been afraid when the Soul Archer was attempted to shoot him with a Skreemer arrow, as in "Peak-a-Boo," five episodes prior, Kai was hit with a Skreemer arrow, but was immune to turning into a ghost because of the Deepstone armor, which he also wore here, so even if he was hit with the arrow a second time, nothing should have happened, besides being temporarily knocked out.
  • When Jay says, "ladies first" to Nya before they head from the Preeminent back to the boat, he says it in Cole's voice.
  • When the Preeminent starts to drown and falls into the trench her entire body is already in water, but in the next shot her body and even legs can be seen out of water before falling again.
  • Morro was standing on top of the Preeminent when Nya summoned the enormous wave, which would have destroyed him too.
  • In the credits, Wrayth's name is misspelled as "Raythe."
