Plot

Plot[]

On the roof of the train, Lloyd asks Arin and Sora about the 'ninja' on the bridge, with the two explaining that he saw Arin. While bewildered at how Arin knew Spinjitzu, a Claw Hunter takes Lloyd by surprise and knocks him into their compartment. While fighting the hunter, Lloyd has him slice the two teens' cuffs with his sword before knocking him out.

Getting on the rooftop, Lloyd tells Arin and Sora to jump, but they didn't want to leave the baby dragon behind. As they were about to search for him, Rapton and some hunters corner them and attack. Sora tries to use her newly acquired Technology powers, but is unable to summon them. While Lloyd and Sora hold off the guards, Arin takes off to rescue the dragon, who transfers power to Sora. Not having full control over her powers yet, Sora accidentally turns the Claw Hunters' swords into bombs. The trio jump off the train as they explode. Lloyd is still bewildered by Arin and Sora's abilities. Their conversation is interrupted when the dragon uses some nearby debris to form a sculpture of three mountains, indicating where his home is.

Lloyd brings Arin, Sora and the dragon to the Monastery of Spinjitzu, with Arin in particular being excited to be there. Lloyd looks through papers that were sent by Kai, who was exploring the newly merged realm. He finds a drawing similar to the dragon's sculpture and is able to map out where it is. Since the old Bounty is being used, the group must use the new Bounty, but it isn't fully finished. Sora volunteers to fix the ship up and prompts the dragon to give her a boost. Not sensing any immediate danger, the dragon doesn't respond.

Sora takes to fixing it normally while Lloyd and Arin wait, the latter saying Sora is a better at humming than at singing and how they met before expressing his belief about the ninja and his parents being lost in the Merge. Lloyd immediately started to explain what happened to him the day of the Merge.

Master Wu asked Lloyd to find scrolls about "the Coalescence", seeming distressed about something. Lloyd is about to leave, when Wu tells him that he hopes he'll do a good job when he becomes the new master of the monastary. That's when the Merge began to happen. After doing damage control and saving the people, including Arin himself, from being swept into the storm, the Ninja headed for the storm itself before it closed in on it itself and exploded. Lloyd woke up back at the Monastery alone, the building itself being moved far from Ninjago and placed on top of a large mountain peak. Lloyd tried looking through Wu's scrolls, but most were destroyed aside from one talking about entities known as Source Dragons, which were said to have some involvement in the Merge. Lloyd spent weeks alone, only for Kai to return on the Bounty.

Just as they were reuniting, another crack in reality opened nearby. These cracks were unstable spaces where multiple realities were trying to occupy the same area. Lloyd and Kai used their elemental powers to close the hole. Knowing they needed answers with the scrolls destroyed, Kai decided to go out and explore the realms, potentially finding the other ninja in the process. Lloyd chose to stay behind and keep what was left of Ninjago safe, leading to him going to the Crossroads and seeing Arin on the bridge.

Back in the present, Sora finishes the final touches on the new Bounty and the group sets off. Eventually, they arrive at their destination, only to be ambushed by the Claws of Imperium, who had been following them in secret. The hunters do heavy damage to the ship causing it to fall. Just as it’s about to crash into a nearby mountain, a dragon picks it up and places it on solid land. The dragon, speaking in human language, tries to take the dragon back, believing Lloyd, Arin and Sora to be enemies.

The dragon's name is revealed to be Riyu. Lloyd tries to resolve the issue diplomatically, wanting to prove to the dragon that they are allies. The Claws of Imperium swoop in, and capture the dragon, who is stated to be the Matriarch. The trio go into action, fending off the hunters. Ultimately, the Matriarch has to put trust in Lloyd, lending him some of her power. Lloyd uses his power boost to scare the hunters into retreating.

The Matriarch expresses how she felt a connection between herself and Lloyd, indicating a further connection between Source Dragons and Elemental Masters. The two parties agree on having to adapt to their newly merged world and change it for the better. Lloyd also decides to fully take Arin and Sora under his wing.

Arin and Sora say goodbye to Riyu, but the dragon isn't ready to part ways yet. The Matriarch consents to letting Riyu stay with them, also announcing that the herd will need to relocate, and that Riyu can find them if he needs help. The herd and the ninja go their separate ways.

In Imperium, Ras reports to Empress Beatrix, with the villainous party now witnessing Lloyd's connection with Riyu's Matriarch, meaning they can now hunt down more Source Dragons' existence. Beatrix goes to meet a Source Dragon, locked away in a giant sphere prison, declaring her goal of capturing all the dragons and having the power to rule over the Merged Realms.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In the shot of Lloyd and Wu arriving to the courtyard of the Monastery of Spinjitzu in the former's flashback of the Merge, from left to right, the ninja are placed in the order of which he reunited with them, with P.I.X.A.L. being the only ninja absent.
    • Kai was first, followed by Nya and Zane, Cole's first time reuniting with the other ninja happened off-screen and sometime before the Elemental Mech shorts, and Jay was the last.

Continuity[]

  • This episode, along with "The Merge: Part 1," marks two episodes as a two-part episode for the first time since "Curseworld, Part I" and "Curseworld, Part II."
  • The hangar bay is still wrecked from when the Crystal Council destroyed it in The Fall of the Monastery.
  • When Lloyd is about to fight Rapton and the Claws of Imperium to get the Dragon Matriarch to power him up, part of his theme "Revealing the Green Ninja" is heard. This is similar to when The Green Ninja was about to fight Garmadon in Zane's dream in "Never Trust a Snake".
  • In Lloyd's flashback where he, Master Wu and the other ninja saw the the beginning of the Merge, the ninja were arranged from left to right in the order in which they returned: Kai weeks after the Merge, Nya in "Beyond Madness," Zane in "The Calm Inside," Cole in "They Call It Doom" and Jay in "The Administration".
  • Lloyd mentioning the strange kingdoms Kai had to get through to get home after the Merge would later be explored in the Ninjago Legends: Monstrosity and Kai's Monstrous Journey series of shorts following Kai's adventures through the Land of Monsters.

Errors[]

  • When Lloyd talks to Arin for the first time after fighting the Claw Hunter and he puts his hands on his hips, his shoulder armor can be seen clipping through his arm.
  • The lower half of Sora's hood is seen as she climbs out of the compartment.
  • When Lloyd, Arin and Sora jump off the train, Lloyd uses his Core suit.
  • In the flashback scene, Lloyd's hands clip through each other when he picks up one of the scrolls.
  • When talking to Wu in the flashback scene, Lloyd says that Wu is almost a thousand years old. However, this is not true, since as Wu himself says in "A Painful Promise," he is thousands of years old.
    • He could be referring to how long Wu has been the master of the Monastery of Spinjitzu.
  • In Lloyd's flashback, he states that he was alone in the monastery for weeks before Kai returned. However, following the release of Monstrosity, Chris "Doc" Wyatt stated that Kai was in the Land of Monsters for months.[6]
  • In the Finnish dub of this episode, a narrator reads the titlecard, but not in any of the other episodes.
