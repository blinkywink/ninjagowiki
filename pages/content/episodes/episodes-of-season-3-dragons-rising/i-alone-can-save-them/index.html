I Alone Can Save Them is the fifth episode of the third season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the 45th episode overall.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

LEGO's YouTube Channel[]

The Ninja uncover the Forbidden Five’s dangerous quest to collect the powerful Prismatic Weapons, raising the stakes for their mission. ⚔️🔥 Meanwhile, Ras leads Arin to a long-awaited reunion with his parents. 💛 But with danger lurking, will their journey bring hope or new challenges?[1]

Streaming Services[]

Netflix[]

The reunited Ninja learn of the Forbidden Five's dangerous quest to find the Prismatic Blades. Meanwhile, Ras leads Arin across a desert.

Pluto TV[]

The Ninja learn of the Forbidden Five's quest to collect the Prismatic Blades, meanwhile Ras finally leads Arin to his parents.

Extended[]

The Forbidden Five's forces attack the city of Temples and draw Nya, Kai and Wyldfyre into the quest for the Prismatic Blades. Meanwhile, Riyu guides Lloyd, Sora and Frak away from Arin and into an encounter with Zarkt and the Demon Dragonians. A surprise arrival turns the tide of battle. Elsewhere, Ras finally leads Arin to his parents.[2]

Plot

Plot[]

Nokt cuts a chain of Thunderfang's using the Red Prismatic Blade. Right after, Rox uses the Green Prismatic Blade to cut Thunderfang's second chain. Three chains remain. Thunderfang roars in triumph, as it is the closest to freedom he has been in a millennia. Rox tells Thunderfang not to worry, as the remaining members of the Forbidden Five search for the rest of the blades.

Roby is busy with the Temple City Bots rearranging and organizing the Temple City Museum. He calls for his messenger dragon, Hermes, and he tells him to send a message to Geo for another sculpture order, wanting more blue. Suddenly, as Roby calls for the bots to fix something, the doors blasts open, and a sword hits the statue created by Geo, shattering it. Kur and numerous Dragonian Trackers storm in. Roby and the bots are attacked by the dragonians, while Kur searches for a Prismatic Blade. She uses her power of decay on Hermes, then finds the blade she is looking for, but it was shattered during the Tournament of the Sources. She remarks about what they could have done to the blade to break it, as she packs it into a sack. Meanwhile, Roby grabs the Elemental Cup, and tries to sneak up on Kur, who notices. She calls him a child, then knocks him against his ancestors portrait. Roby tells Kur about the other ninja, saying that Wyldfyre will follow them wherever they go to stop them. Kur just laughs, and then remarks about showing him her power. Kur decay's Roby, leaving him in the museum, and leaves with the Dragonians.

Arin and Ras and venturing across the First Land. Arin gets impatient about how long they have been walking in the same desert, and starts criticizing Ras, while he looks around for something. He tells Arin to stop, as he finds a lever, and pulls it. A hatch opens beneath the sand and reveals the Super Storm Jet. Ras tells Arin about the many resources he has hidden around the Merged Lands. Ras asks Arin to fly the jet, resulting in his failed attempts to start the jet. He eventually gets it to work, with the help of Ras.

In the First Land, Lloyd, Frak, Sora, and Riyu come across the sled that Arin used to carry Ras through the desert. The speculate the latter, as Frak is distracted by the Spectral Lands in the background, terrified of possible puppet encounters. Riyu senses danger nearby, and wants to follow, but Frak is more focused on Arin and wants to stay on the path that he took. He eventually gives in, but notes that he thinks it is a mistake

Rapton is watching an ad for the Freebooters on a tablet, after being stranded in the desert when their train stopped. P.I.X.A.L. is trying to find a way to fix Zane after he saved the train crash. She goes through Rapton's things, to which he complains about. PIXAL defiantly tells Rapton to help her find the parts needed to fix Zane, to which he agrees.

In the arcade, Nya inspects the motherboard of the game Jay played remotely. Wyldfyre and Kai mention about Jay possibly cheating to win the game, as a Messenger Dragon flies in. Wyldfyre assumes it is another letter from Roby, and likewise gets very excited. The letter tells of the attack on the City of Temples, and Roby's decay.

Arin and Ras are flying over the ocean in the jet, while Arin gives commentary, much to Ras' annoyance. Arin sees the attacked algae farmers' village, and wants to help them repair, as he did before. Ras reminds him of the mission at hand, and wanting to find his parents. Arin gives in, and passes by the village.

In the First Land, Riyu, continues to lead Sora, Frak and Lloyd down an unknown path, as Frak sarcastically remarks about finding nothing. Riyu senses someone coming, and pushes the Ninja behind some brambles. They peer out and see Zarkt and some Dragonian Trackers walk in. The Dragonians sense the blade on Frak, as Lloyd points out Tyr, whom they met previously. Zarkt complains about them not finding the blade yet, and Frak pulls it out, realizing that's what they were looking for. Tyr senses the blade, and finds the Ninja hiding. They engage in battle, with P.I.X.AL. later arriving on Zanth, the former of which Lloyd joyfully hugs right as Zane pulls up in a new, bigger body. The female Nindroid then single-handedly handled Zarkt, making him call for a retreat, with Sora and Frak mocking his threat of returning.

With Zarkt and his forces defeated, Lloyd properly greeted P.I.X.A.L. and took note in confusion about Zane's new big form. The nindroids informed Lloyd that they would explain everything to him later, but for now, they had to inform him that the Forbidden Five were after a set of five blades identical to the one in Lloyd's possession. Zane analyzed the blade in Lloyd's hands and confirmed it was made of the same material as the crystal sword from his fight with Zeatrix, leaving the ninja to question what the Five could want with them.

Meanwhile at the Storm Village, Zarkt reported his defeat to a very displeased Rox who angrily slammed him onto the ground for his failure and warned him that if he failed again, he would be cast out of the Forbidden Five. Kur returned shortly thereafter and produced the blade from the City of Temples but explained it was shattered prior to her arrival. Rox inspected the broken blade and remarked that they could fix the blade with a little wish magic, confusing Kur who remarked that all the djinn were gone. Rox then informed her and Zarkt that not all of them were truly gone.

Elsewhere, Arin and Ras arrived on the rocky shores of a strange island, one where Ras promised Arin's parents were. Frantically, Arin ran up a hill to see them, only to be greeted by a cemetery. Breaking down before his parents' graves, Ras informed Arin that he had a way to bring them back from the dead: by reversing the Merge, one could create anything in its wake. If Arin were to help him in attaining his goals, Ras promised the boy that he would resurrect his parents from the dead.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The mariachi music that plays when Arin is hitting buttons in the Super Storm Jet is the same as the music from the band playing in the Take Back Ninjago rally from "Day of the Great Devourer".

Continuity[]

  • According the the names on the tombstones, Arin's parents were named Barry and Jenny Nived.
    • This makes Arin one of the few Ninjago characters with a confirmed last name.
  • This episode marks the first time Lloyd and P.I.X.A.L. have seen each other since the Merge and they last fought together in "Roots".
  • P.I.X.A.L. becoming annoyed at Rapton for trying to stop her from using the train's parts to repair Zane is similar in tone to her annoyance at Milton Dyer for not recognizing Artificial Intelligence as sentient beings with emotion in "The Prodigal Father."
  • Sora trying to use Spinjitzu to fight the Dragonians but just twirling one way is similar to when Lloyd tried using Spinjitzu in the original Destiny's Bounty training room and also failed at it, which was seen in "The Rise of the Great Devourer".
