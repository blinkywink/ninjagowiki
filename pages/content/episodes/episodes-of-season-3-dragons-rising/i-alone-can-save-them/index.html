I Alone Can Save Them is the fifth episode of the third season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the 45th episode overall.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

LEGO's YouTube Channel[]

The Ninja uncover the Forbidden Five’s dangerous quest to collect the powerful Prismatic Weapons, raising the stakes for their mission. ⚔️🔥 Meanwhile, Ras leads Arin to a long-awaited reunion with his parents. 💛 But with danger lurking, will their journey bring hope or new challenges?[1]

Streaming services[]

Netflix[]

The reunited Ninja learn of the Forbidden Five's dangerous quest to find the Prismatic Blades. Meanwhile, Ras leads Arin across a desert.

Pluto TV[]

The Ninja learn of the Forbidden Five's quest to collect the Prismatic Blades, meanwhile Ras finally leads Arin to his parents.

Extended[]

The Forbidden Five's forces attack the city of Temples and draw Nya, Kai and Wyldfyre into the quest for the Prismatic Blades. Meanwhile, Riyu guides Lloyd, Sora and Frak away from Arin and into an encounter with Zarkt and the Demon Dragonians. A surprise arrival turns the tide of battle. Elsewhere, Ras finally leads Arin to his parents.[2]

Plot

Plot[]

Nokt cuts a chain of Thunderfang's using the Red Prismatic Blade. Right after, Rox uses the Green Prismatic Blade to cut Thunderfang's second chain. Three chains remain. Thunderfang roars in triumph, as it is the closest to freedom he has been in a millennia. Rox tells Thunderfang not to worry, as the remaining members of the Forbidden Five search for the rest of the blades.

Inside the City of Temples museum, Roby micro-manages Security Bots to display statues and sculptures of himself, boasting that the remodel will transform the building into a "you-see-um". He dispatches Hermes, a resting Spirit Dragon, to deliver a note to Geo in the Land of Lost Things for a new sculpture order. The museum doors are suddenly smashed open by Dragonian Trackers. One of the flying Dragonians throws a sword that shatters Roby's self-portrait sculpture. As Security Bots engage the intruders, Kur—one of the Forbidden Five—enters and leads the raid. Security Bots are quickly overwhelmed and dismantled by the Dragonians. When Hermes attempts to fly at Kur, she uses her Elemental Power to decay the Spirit Dragon and tosses him aside. Kur approaches a broken blade on display, gathers the hilt and shattered pieces into a bag, and laments what has been done to it. Roby grabs the Elemental Cup off its pedestal to attack Kur, but she easily strikes him back with her elbow. The thrown cup smashes through a portrait of Roake. Roby warns her that Wyldfyre and the rest of the ninja will stop her, but Kur cackles, has two Dragonians pin him down, and prepares to use her Elemental Power of Decay on him as red lightning flashes outside.

Arin and Lord Ras trek through a barren desert. Frustrated by seeing nothing but endless sand, Arin accuses Ras of misleading him about leading him to his parents. Ras counters by reminding Arin of his past accuracy regarding Master Wu causing the Merge. Ras lifts a cover hidden under a rock to reveal a set of levers. Pulling the red lever opens the ground, revealing a Super Storm Jet. Ras explains he has hidden resources like this across the Merged Lands as contingencies, causing a humbled Arin to apologize. Ras asks if Arin can pilot the jet. Arin almost mentions Sora before quickly cutting himself off and claiming someone taught him long ago. Inside the cockpit, Arin tries to operate the vehicle, accidentally setting off an alarm, activating windshield wipers, turning on music, and launching an attached drone. Calling Arin a fool, Ras steps in to land the drone properly and start the engines. Arin disengages the landing gear, tells Ras to hold on, and the jet blasts off into the sky.

While searching through the First Land, Sora spots the sled Arin used to drag the injured Lord Ras, explaining the unusual tracks they were following. Frak is distracted by a Spectral Lands. Lloyd notes it appears to be the edge of another merged realm, while Frak nervously hopes they won't have to go there, expressing a fear that it's "full of puppets". Riyu sniffs the sled and growls, leading them to two sets of prints. Sora observes that Ras seems to have recovered enough to walk, while Lloyd deduces that having only two sets of tracks means Arin and Ras are alone, without the Forbidden Five. Just as the group prepares to follow the tracks to find Arin, Riyu grumbles and tries to lead them in the opposite direction. Despite Frak's protest that it leads away from Arin and his warning that it's a mistake, Lloyd insists on trusting Riyu's instincts, noting the dragon has never led them wrong before

Rapton pauses a commercial starring his past self walking through the Crossroads and riding Hover Chariots with Freebooters in the First Realm. In the ad, he promises rich pay, fame, and a "great healthcare plan", despite showing his crew getting knocked around by a Mountain Dragon—before encouraging viewers to join the Freebooters. In the present, Rapton walks over to his crew digging through the wreckage of a crashed hover train surrounded by piles of loot in the sand. P.I.X.A.L. is present, lifting up a severely damaged Zane whose primary electronics and secondary elements are disrupted. P.I.X.A.L. tells Zane to save his energy and begins rummaging through a box for supplies. Rapton protests P.I.X.A.L. going through their stolen gear, but she firmly shuts him down. She points out that the only reason Rapton, his crew, and his "hair-piece" are alive instead of smashed against a cliff is because of Zane's actions. She demands their full assistance in gathering the necessary parts to repair him. Intimidated, Rapton clears his throat and announces to the Freebooters that he has decided they are going to save Zane, instructing them to give P.I.X.A.L. whatever she asks for while defensively insisting his hair is real. P.I.X.A.L. hands him sample parts to search for, returns to Zane, plugs a wire into his head, and vows that they will not lose each other again.

At the arcade, Nya inspects the motherboard of an old arcade cabinet with a flashlight while Kai and Wyldfyre watch. Nya expresses confusion over how Jay managed to play the game remotely since the hardware lacks remote access capabilities, while Wyldfyre humorously asks if she checked the "fatherboard". Kai insists Jay must have used "cheat-ware" to beat his high score. Nya notes that if they can determine how the signal was transmitted, they could trace it directly back to Jay's location. Kai interrupts, adding that it will let them force Jay to admit he cheated, drawing an annoyed groan from Nya. A Spirit Dragon flies into the arcade carrying a scroll. Kai teases Wyldfyre about receiving another "love-scroll". She giggles and admits it, but her excitement turns to horror as she opens the message. When Kai assumes Roby is breaking up with her, Wyldfyre reveals the alarming truth: Roby is injured and the City of Temples has been attacked. She shows the scroll to Kai and Nya, leaving them both gasping in shock.

Arin jokes around while piloting the jet, welcoming Ras to "Arin Airlines" as they reach cruising altitude, but Ras immediately shuts down his commentary. Spotting smoke rising from several buildings in the Algae Farmers' village, Arin recognizes the location from a past visit with Lloyd and veers the jet toward it to offer help. Ras orders Arin to stay focused, warning him that getting distracted by everything in his path will prevent him from finding his parents. He criticizes Lloyd as "ineffectual" for passing on these bad habits and pressures Arin to prioritize his own needs instead, prompting Arin to turn the jet around and fly past the burning village.

As Riyu leads Lloyd, Sora, and Frak through the First Land, Frak complains that they are wasting time finding "nothing" instead of searching for Arin. Sora encourages him to trust Riyu, who suddenly growls, grabs the group, and pulls them into hiding behind some vegetation. The sky darkens as Tyr and Zarkt step into the clearing, joined by six Dragonian Trackers. Zarkt aggressively demands his crew fan out to find a small, shimmering blade that Thunderfang mentioned, pushing Tyr aside in frustration. Sora and Lloyd observe the group from cover, recognizing Tyr and noticing the Dragonians look corrupted by ancient magic, realizing this was what Riyu wanted to show them. Sora comforts a visibly sad Riyu. Hearing Zarkt's description of the missing item, Frak pulls out the Prismatic Blade from his back, prompting Tyr to sense its presence and move toward them. Frak hands it to Lloyd for inspection. Tyr slices through their vegetation cover, knocking the hidden group backward. As Zarkt demands the blade, Lloyd puts on his hood, shouts "Ninja... go!", and leaps forward to slash at Zarkt.

At the Dragon stables, Mr. Frohicky attempts to guide Kai, Nya, and Wyldfyre on operating the Portal Gate using Zane's instructions. Wyldfyre tries commanding the portal to take her to "Roby" and then "Roby town," but it fails to respond until Kai and Nya correctly speak the actual destination: "The City of Temples". As the portal powers up, Nya quickly thanks Mr. Frohicky before they race through. Arriving at the Monastery of the Gates, Nya points out that a completely new portal has activated, one that was previously inactive. Focused entirely on saving Roby, Wyldfyre brushes off the anomaly, and the group continues rushing forward. Wyldfyre bursts through the door of the City of Temples hospital, where two Medical Bots are attending to a weakened Roby. Furious over his injuries, Wyldfyre vows to heat-blast whoever hurt him, resurrect them, and heat-blast them again. Roby reveals that Kur attacked him, targeting a weapon and taking down Hermes as well. Roby admits he didn't previously understand the significance of the Prismatic Blade stolen by the Forbidden Five, comparing his past skepticism of ancient lore to believing Source Dragons were just a myth. Kai helps him open a family heirloom book titled "The Sagas of the Eternal Sources" by King Urd Leatherwing. With his vision failing, Roby urges the ninja to use the historical text to discover what the Forbidden Five are planning and put a stop to them. Wyldfyre comforts him, swearing that the Forbidden Five will soon become the "Forgotten Zero".

Lloyd battles Zarkt while Frak and Sora attempt to fend off the corrupted Dragonians. Frak uses his quake power to shatter the ground, but Dragonians lift him into the air. Sora powers up and tries using Spinjitzu, but fails to trigger it, allowing Tyr to slam her to the ground. Riyu swoops in, grabbing Tyr and hurling him into the Dragonians holding Frak, freeing him. Zarkt using Shatterspin, prompting Lloyd to counter using the Rising Dragon Technique to knock Zarkt against a boulder. Zarkt demands the Prismatic Blade, claiming it by right of conquest, but Lloyd mocks the idea of it belonging to the Forbidden Five or a "creepy desert puppet". The two exchange weapon strikes and energy blasts, causing a massive cloud of red and green power. Meanwhile, Tyr knocks Riyu out of the sky as the remaining Dragonians encircle Sora and Frak. As Frak complains about following Riyu into this trap, Sora defends the dragon, realizing Riyu brought them here because he sensed the Dragonians were in trouble. A dragon roar echoes overhead as Zanth arrives carrying P.I.X.A.L., who jumps down to join the battle. Lloyd blasts Zarkt away and happily hugs P.I.X.A.L., asking where she has been. Joined by a modified Zane, who flies in to battle Tyr and freezes the ground Dragonians with ice, P.I.X.A.L. simply replies that she and Zane found each other. As Sora remarks on Zane's upgraded appearance, Zarkt attacks P.I.X.A.L. with his flail. Effortlessly dodging his strikes, P.I.X.A.L. introduces herself, grabs his hand, counterattacks, and uses his own flail to spin and punch him away, warning him that attacking her friends will not be tolerated. Overwhelmed by the team's combined strength, Zarkt orders the Dragonians to pull back. As he takes to the sky alongside Tyr and four other Dragonians, he threatens that they will return for the blade. Sora deliberately mishears his threat, mocking him by asking why they would ever throw him a "parade," prompting an annoyed Zarkt to fly off. Taking off her hood, Sora admits to Frak that she knew Zarkt said "blade" all along. Lloyd gives P.I.X.A.L. another hug and asks about Zane's massive new size. P.I.X.A.L. promises to explain everything soon, while Zane warns the team that the Forbidden Five are hunting for these specific blades for an unknown plan. Lloyd notes the Prismatic Blade resembles the sword from his fight with Zeatrix, which Zane confirms after analyzing its rare metallic composition, leaving Sora to question why the Five are gathering them.

At Storm Village, Zarkt reports back to Rox, attempting to explain that the ninja already had the Prismatic Blade. Furious, Rox grabs Zarkt's hat, slams him to the ground, and steps on top of him, threatening that if he fails again, the Forbidden Five will become the "Forbidden Four". Hopping off Zarkt, Rox turns to Kur and demands to know if she failed as well. Accompanied by two Dragonians, Kur presents the bag containing the shattered pieces of the blade she retrieved from the City of Temples museum. Rox inspects the hilt from the bag and assures Kur that the blade can be repaired using wish magic. When Kur points out that all the Djinn are gone, Rox ominously reveals that not all of them have disappeared.

Arin and Ras land the jet on a strange island covered in massive stone pillars. Seeking confirmation, Arin asks if his parents are truly there without any tricks or lies, which Ras promises is real. Arin leaps out of the cockpit and runs over a hill, expecting to find his parents. Instead, he comes across a mysterious graveyard and frantically searches the gravestones until he finds two bearing his parents' names. Overwhelmed with grief, Arin breaks down crying. As Ras moves in to comfort him, Arin asks why he wasn't told beforehand. Ras counters by asking if Arin would have believed it without seeing it with his own eyes. When Arin asks how they died, Ras blames the event on the Merge, though admits the exact details remain unclear. When Arin despairs that there's nothing left to fix, Ras reveals his core motivation. He explains that reversing the Merge allows one to reshape reality in its aftermath, asserting that he alone can save them. Ras offers Arin a bargain: help him undo the Merge, and his parents along with Ras' people will be restored to life as if nothing ever happened.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The mariachi music that plays when Arin is hitting buttons in the Super Storm Jet is the same as the music from the band playing in the Take Back Ninjago rally from "Day of the Great Devourer".

Continuity[]

  • According the the names on the tombstones, Arin's parents were named Barry and Jenny Nived.
    • This makes Arin one of the few Ninjago characters with a confirmed last name.
  • This episode marks the first time Lloyd and P.I.X.A.L. have seen each other since the Merge and they last fought together in "Roots".
  • P.I.X.A.L. becoming annoyed at Rapton for trying to stop her from using the train's parts to repair Zane is similar in tone to her annoyance at Milton Dyer for not recognizing Artificial Intelligence as sentient beings with emotion in "The Prodigal Father."
  • Sora trying to use Spinjitzu to fight the Dragonians but just twirling one way is similar to when Lloyd tried using Spinjitzu in the original Destiny's Bounty training room and also failed at it, which was seen in "The Rise of the Great Devourer".
