For Whom the Bell Tolls is the eighteenth episode of the third season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the 58th episode overall.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

The Ninja embark on their separate journeys to awaken the Arc Dragon of Focus but deal with unexpected - and annoying - interruptions.[1]

The groups split, with Frak joining Sora and Wyldfyre on Zanth to Djinjago. More than one surprise awaits the Ninja in Djinjago, and an old foe threatens the trial to awaken the Arc Dragon of Focus.

Netflix: To prepare for the battle against Thunderfang, the Ninja split up to awaken the Arc Dragon of Focus — but Arin questions his role in the plan.

Extended: Sora and Wyldfyre arrive back in Ninjago where they meet up with Frak, Kai and Cole. The groups split, with Frak joining Sora and Wyldfyre on Zanth to Djinjago. More than one surprise awaits the Ninja in Djinjago, and an old foe threatens the trial to awaken the Arc Dragon of Focus. Nya finds unusual help in Mysterium, while Arin shares doubts with Lloyd as they reach the Spectral Lands.[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This episode's title is referenced in LEGO Fortnite Odyssey, in the description of the bell that the Lost Master drops.

Cultural references[]

  • The name of the episode is most likely a reference to the book of the same name by Ernest Hemingway. The name could also be a referenced to the song of the same name by Metallica, of which was inspired by Hemingway.
  • The song Wyldfyre sang in the episode was an altered version of "Drunken Sailor".
    • Additionally her line of "What do you do with a sleepy ninja?" sounds very similar to the "What shall we do with the pirates, ninja?" line from The Pirate Whip which that in of itself is that song's take on "Drunken Sailor".

Continuity[]

  • This episode marks the first time Lloyd has been open about his childhood when asking Arin if he thought Lloyd started out with skills.
    • Additionally, this is also the first time Lloyd has directly talked about his childhood to any of his students, similar to how Wu would tell him and the other Ninja about his childhood.

Errors[]
