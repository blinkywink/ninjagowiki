Casket of Bones is the fourteenth episode of the third season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the 54th episode overall.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

The Underworld tests Arin and Sora's commitment to Ras, while Nya and Jay work together to expose Lee's secret.[1]

LEGO's YouTube Channel: 🌀 🔥 WILL SECRETS BE REVEALED?! In this full episode, the Underworld tests Arin and Sora’s loyalty to Ras, while Nya and Jay work to uncover Lee’s secret. Will they succeed? Meanwhile, Lloyd finds a vital clue from the Council of Source Dragons. Hey you — watch closely for every clue![2]

Ras, Arin and Sora face one last trial before entering the Underworld. In the Realm of Lee, Jay and Nya discover that Lloyd and Wyldfyre are missing and must work together to find the Ninja and expose Lee's secret.

Netflix: As they near the entrance to the Underworld, Ras and Arin face a powerful skeletal guardian. Lloyd learns a dark truth about Lee's utopian world.

Extended: Ras, Arin and Sora face one last trial before entering the Underworld, only to uncover a terrible truth at the Forge of the Forgotten that forces Arin to make a difficult choice. In the Realm of Lee, Jay and Nya discover that Lloyd and Wyldfyre are missing and must work together to find the Ninja and expose Lee's secret. Meanwhile, Lloyd learns a vital clue from the Council of the Source Dragons.[3]

Underwood says that invisibility requires lots of permits, But Allen fears that if they return, then he will bludgeon them with fines.

Suddenly, the Munce agent is panicking, hoping that they would not come back because of what they did to Carl, B, Debra, and the rest of the HQ, But Underwood tells him to be quiet, but then the Munce agent shows her how many souls the Spectral Dragonians they have taken from them. The Munce agent, Allen, and Underwood hear the magic flute, and they bring out their weapons to blast them, only for Underwood, a female agent, and a Merlopian being stripped of their souls. Continuity[]

  • This episode marks Kruncha's and Nuckal's first physical appearance since "The Greatest Fear of All." This is also their first appearance in the WildBrain animation.
  • Kruncha and Nuckal are now the eleventh and twelfth named characters from the original series to appear in Ninjago: Dragons Rising. The other characters are:
    • Gulch from Master of the Mountain who appeared in "Wyldly Inappropriate."
    • The Mechanic from Skybound who appeared in "The Mech Master."
    • Fugi-Dove from Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu who appeared in "The Blood Moon."
    • Tox and Mr. Pale from Tournament of Elements who appeared in "Enter the City of Temples."
    • Neuro and Chamille from Tournament of Elements who appeared in "The Sword Shatters."
    • Morro from Tournament of Elements and mainly Possession who appeared in "The Spectral Lands."
    • P.I.X.A.L. from Rebooted who appeared in "The Great Zane Robbery."
    • Dareth from Legacy of the Green Ninja who appeared in "Fallen Wishes."
    • Kruncha and Nuckal first debuted in the pilot episodes.
    • The two Skulkins' collective appearances marks the third instance of villains from the original series returning in Dragons Rising, with the first and second instances being the Mechanic and Morro, respectively.
  • While the comic "Special Delivery From Stiix!" suggested that Kruncha and Nuckal were freed from Kryptarium Prison, this was finally confirmed in this episode 9 years later.
  • This episode marks the first time the Underworld has appeared in Dragons Rising, as well as the first time it appears in its WildBrain animation. It was last seen in general in "The Kaiju Protocol," but this episode is the first time the Underworld has had new animation for it since "Curseworld, Part II."
  • This episode marks the fourth time Garmadon has been mentioned in Ninjago: Dragons Rising. He was first mentioned in "The Temple of the Dragon Cores" and then again in "The Spell at the Waterfall" and then a third time in "Peak of 1000 Suns".
    • Additionally, this episode marks the first time Samukai has been mentioned in Ninjago: Dragons Rising when Nuckal tells Arin and Sora that he and Kruncha worked with people similar to Ras.
    • Since Chris "Doc" Wyatt once stated that Clouse will be indirectly mentioned in Season 3,[4] it seems that he is also indirectly mentioned by that line, which confirms that Clouse indeed became the leader of Skulkin after the events of the Dark Island Trilogy, but was likely dethroned prior to the events of Dragons Rising, Season 3.
  • Arin placing Nuckal's head on Kruncha's body before the two swap heads could be seen as a subtle callback to the animation mishap in "The Invitation" where Kruncha's voice came out of Nuckal and vice versa.
  • The way Ras grabs Arin's leg is the same as during their fights near the end of "The Merge: Part 1" and "The Forest of Spirits," although this time Arin uses Spinjitzu and blows the attack back, signifying his growth in strength and strategy during their time together.
  • Ras mentions that Arin was a weakling when he found and that he turned him into a warrior, which is similar to what he believes his master did to him, as previously compared in "The Forest of Spirits."
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt confirmed it was the Source Dragon of Energy who allowed Lloyd to eavesdrop on the Council meeting to learn how to awaken the Arc Dragon of Focus.[5]

Errors[]

  • When Fritz and Spitz showed the Prismatic Blades to Kai, Fritz was holding the Returning Blade. However, at that moment, the Returning Blade was actually with Arin.
  • Despite Sora saying that she did Spinjitzu for the first time, this is actually the second time as she performed it in "The Final Game."[6]
  • When Kruncha pushes Nuckal after Nuckal says that he responds to strong leadership, Arin can be briefly seen with bright yellow eyes.[7]
    • That same character model would later be used in "Chaos Reigns," when Arin and Lloyd were linked to the Arc Dragon of Focus.
  • Although the Source Dragon of Flow is credited, she did not speak in this episode.
