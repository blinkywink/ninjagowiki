Fallen Wishes is the sixth episode of the third season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the 46th episode overall.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

Sora, Nya and Riyu are summoned to New Djinjago to help a friend, and the mystery bounty hunter receives a new mission.[1]

LEGO's YouTube Channel: Sora, Nya, and Riyu are summoned to New Djinjago to help a friend, but their journey may bring unexpected challenges. ⚔️🌊 Meanwhile, the mysterious bounty hunter is assigned a new mission—one that could change everything. 🌀 Will the Ninja be ready for what’s coming?[2]

Netflix: Arrakore the djinn transports Sora, Nya and Riyu to New Djinjago. Ras enlists Arin in his plan to reverse the Merge.

Extended: While the Ninja continue their search for Arin and Jay, Rox attacks new Djinjago to force Arrakore to repair the shattered Prismatic Blade with wish magic. Sora, Riyu and Nya get pulled into the battle to protect the Djinn and stop Rox. Meanwhile, the mystery bounty hunter accepts a job to capture Ras, who continues his mission with Arin to undo the Merge.[3]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This episode marks the point where WildBrain has animated more of the show than WILFilm ApS.
  • The formula for the timepiece that Rox uses is "Niver-maste fo le ana-ton go so-lox pezotan menoo-gala poxel guthero-vix da sempaste to".

Continuity[]

  • This episode marks Dareth's first appearance since "Roots" and his first appearance in Ninjago: Dragons Rising. This makes him the tenth named character besides the ninja to return in Dragons Rising. The other characters are:
    • Gulch from Master of the Mountain who appeared in "Wyldly Inappropriate."
    • The Mechanic from Skybound who appeared in "The Mech Master."
    • Fugi-Dove from Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu who appeared in "The Blood Moon."
    • Tox and Mr. Pale from Tournament of Elements who appeared in "Enter the City of Temples."
    • Neuro and Chamille from Tournament of Elements who appeared in "The Sword Shatters."
    • Morro from Tournament of Elements and mainly Possession who appeared in "The Spectral Lands."
    • P.I.X.A.L. from Rebooted who appeared in "The Great Zane Robbery."
    • Dareth first appeared in Legacy of the Green Ninja.
  • Ras' hammer is revealed to be a Source Dragon Icon for the Source Dragon of Strength.

Errors[]

  • When Nya wishes to know the location of her missing friends, she speaks in the voice of Sora.
  • In the title of the YouTube clip of this episode, Djinjago is misspelled "Djinjango".
