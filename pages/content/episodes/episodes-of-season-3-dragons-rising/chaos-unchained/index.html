Chaos Unchained is the ninth episode of the third season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the 49th episode overall.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

Pluto TV: The Ninja hurry to the First Land to stop the Forbidden Five from freeing ThunderFang, and Wyldfyre discovers a drastic cure to Roby's decay.

LEGO's YouTube Channel: The Ninja race to the First Land to stop the Forbidden Five from unleashing the powerful ThunderFang. ⚔️🐉 Meanwhile, Wyldfyre discovers a risky cure for Roby’s decay, one that could change everything. Can they succeed before it’s too late?[1]

Netflix: Heavily outnumbered, the Ninja race to stop the Forbidden Five from freeing Thunderfang. Wyldfyre desperately tries to cure one of the crew.

Extended: The Ninja attempt to beat Drix to the First Land to prevent the Forbidden Five from using all five Prismatic Blades to release ThunderFang. Arin temporarily joins the Ninja to save Ras, while in the City of Temples, Wyldfyre might have found a drastic cure to Roby's decay. A fight in the Dragonian village will decide the fate of the the Merged Lands.[2]

Plot

Plot[]

In the First Land, Thunderfang sensed that the final two Prismatic Blades were now in Drix's hands. He insulted the Source Dragons, accusing them of being cosmic frauds who had thwarted his ascension to Source Dragon for countless ages. However, he was resolute in his belief that his time to rise was imminent.

Meanwhile, in the World Forest, Arin delivered the news of his parents' deaths to the ninja, much to their distress. When Lloyd asked about the circumstances of their demise, Arin pointed out that it was Wu's actions that had triggered the Merge. Although Lloyd attempted to defend Wu, his words only served to deepen Arin's anguish. Arin warned that the release of Thunderfang by the Forbidden Five posed a grave threat to the entire world, and was in desperate need of Ras in hopes of getting his parents back. He proposed a collaboration with the ninja, who quickly united around him, eager to lend their support. However, as they prepared to confront Drix, Zane pointed out the impracticality of pursuing him, given his remarkable speed. This led to a nostalgic exchange between Kai and Nya, recalling a past incident where Kai had been chased by clamp-beetles due to a dare from Jay. The mention of Kai's frantic escape through the Monastery gave Lloyd an idea, suggesting a potential strategy to gain an advantage over Drix.

In the City of Temples, Roby's time was running out, causing great concern for Wyldfyre. Despite her hopes for a different outcome, the Medical Bot had already confirmed that every resource in the library had been thoroughly examined, even re-reading the texts. Just then, another Messenger Dragon arrived to check on Hermes. Wyldfyre was initially horrified to see the dragon breathing fire on Hermes, but soon realized that the flames were actually healing him, reversing the effects of Kur's decay. This gave Wyldfyre an idea that a similar method could save Roby.

Continuity[]

  • Back in "Fallen Wishes" Sora jokingly says she wishes she could see the look on Rox's face due to her wish, yet in this episode that does actually happen after the Forbidden Five free Thunderfang.

Errors[]

  • Nokt calls the Control implant Ras used on him in Season 2 a "control collar" even though it was just a device attached to the back of his head.
  • When Thunderfang called out the Source Dragons as frauds, the subtitles say "false dragons" instead of "Source Dragons."
  • When Rox calls Nokt foolish, Nya can be seen without her mask on.
  • When Thunderfang absorbed the Forbidden Five's souls, Kur's axe can be seen clipping through her leg.
