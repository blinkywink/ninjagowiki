The Shape of Motion is the eleventh episode of the second season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the 31st episode overall.

It was originally going to be released in Australia on June 29, 2024 but the airing was cancelled.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

While searching for his parents, Arin and the Ninja rescue a baby dragon, uncovering a new mystery and mission for the team.[1]

Netflix: Lloyd attempts to decipher his visions, while Arin uncovers a mysterious theft that threatens the Sixteen Realms while searching for his parents.

Roku: Arins quest to find his parents takes an unexpected turn when he and the Ninja stumble upon a baby dragon in need.

LEGO's Official Youtube Channel: Arin and Sora were looking for Arin's parents - along the way they find something completely unexpected...

Extended: A mission in the Land of Madness to find his parents leads Arin to a gravely wounded Matriarch of the Mountain Dragons. When Riyu’s tribe mother falls to her injuries, the Ninja race her offspring back to the nest and encounter an old foe. The Ninja must solve the Matriarch’s murder and accept a new mission from the Source Dragon of Motion to determine who stole the Matriarch’s horns – and why.[2]

Plot

Plot[]

At the Administration, Agents Allen and Underwood complain about the lack of action, unaware that Arin and Sora had infiltrated the Administration to look for info about Arin's parents in the Department of Reassignment, where they had been sending people recovered in the Merge to the wrong places. As they were searching however, they discover the information was all undocumented as the Manager of the Reassignment Department had abruptly quit without reason. Pretty soon, they both get caught and are forced to escape into the Land of Madness, where they meet up with Riyu, who was waiting by the Explorer.

At the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Lloyd attempts to give into his visions to try and locate the Shadow Dojo, but gets nowhere before confiding in Nya whether or not to believe them while Wyldfyre, sporting a new hairstyle, is practicing her Heat powers, including using a method called Boulder Golf. As they watch her, Lloyd relates to how he is trying to live up to a legacy himself before they both express how they miss Kai, who was trapped in the Nether-Space, and Lloyd then changed the subject to the well-being of Arin and Sora.

As they move through the Kingdom of Madness, Arin and Sora discover a dragon flying over before collapsing and discover it was the Matriarch of the Mountain Dragons, fatally wounded and her Crown of Horns stolen. They both call for the rest of the team as well as have the Craglings to try and keep her stable. The Matriarch manages to telepathically warn Lloyd that "The Source Dragons are in grave danger" and show the ninja a scroll before succumbing to her wounds. Wyldfyre then finds a baby dragon in the Matriarch's pouch, who is her daughter and the next in line. Riyu leads the ninja to where the Mountain Dragons fled to.

At the dojo, Ras over looks Cinder and Nokt's practice battle before Ras halts it by activating the control implant on the back of the latter's head to remind him it is practice before they make plans to head to the Tournament of the Sources.

Elsewhere, the ninja and dragons make their way to Riyu's herd when they are intercepted by a group of Imperium mercenaries, who had overheard Riyu, Zanth, and Heatwave's roar of grief. Lloyd discovers the mercenaries, called the Freebooters, led by Rapton, were now bounty hunting dragons before accusing the latter of killing the Matriarch, which he denies. Just as the tide was about to turn, Riyu's herd arrives to scare the Freebooters away before they lead them to their new home in the World Forest where they deliver Eridany to them. Later at the monastery, while the ninja are discussing the events that had just occurred, the Source Dragon of Motion shows up and explains that there's a mysterious enemy who keeps killing dragons and that she thinks they are coming for the Source Dragons next. Lloyd shows her the scroll he found in the Matriarch's hand, which contains a map. The Source Dragon of Motion explains that the map is displaying the City of Temples where the tournament is being held. Because of this, the ninja and the Source Dragon of Motion decide that the ninja should compete in the tournament since they think the mysterious enemy will also compete, giving the ninja an opportunity to catch them.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This episode has had it's release dates in several countries delayed multiple times.
    • Australian TV Channel 9Go! was going to air the episode on June 29, 2024, but it was replaced with a Season 11: Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu episode,[3] but it was also later moved to November 2, 2024.[4]
    • The Finnish TV Channel Nelonen had it's release date set for July 1[5], but it was also moved to October 4. 2024.[6]
  • It's revealed in this episode that one of the Source Dragons, later revealed to be the Source Dragon of Balance in "Casket of Bones," was killed before the Source Dragon of Energy was captured.
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, Nokt and Rox specifically targeted the tribe leaders of Riyu, Egalt and Rontu's tribes as a form of revenge.[7]

Continuity[]

  • This is the second episode to start with "The Shape of," the first being "The Shape of Nya."
    • Additionally, both episodes were written by Chris "Doc" Wyatt and Kevin Burke.
  • Mountain Dragons are directly called by their name for the first time in this episode, after the species' name being revealed in Ninja's Dragon Battle!.
  • Lloyd stating how he relates to Wyldfyre trying to be the new Red Ninja refers to how Lloyd was not only trying to live up to Wu but also trying to be like his father as seen in the early episodes of "Rise of the Snakes", he also questions in "The Hands of Time" if he will ever live up to the legacy of Garmadon and Wu.
Errors

Errors[]

  • When the Source Dragon of Motion transforms in her mortal form, Wyldfyre is in her original appearance.
  • The Source Dragon of Motion says the other Source Dragons are blind to the danger of the mortal world, but the Source Dragon of Energy experienced the danger first hand when he was captured and drained.
  • During Cinder's fight against Nokt, the right shoulder pad of his Shatterspin armor (the blue claws) has an animation error, disappearing or piercing his body.
