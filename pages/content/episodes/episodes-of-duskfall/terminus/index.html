Terminus is the second short of Ninjago Legends: Duskfall.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

LEGO's YouTube Channel[]

The Great Devourer is tearing through the Noodle District! There’s only one way to escape the chaos—turn it into an epic video game. New mission unlocked: reach the NGTV rooftop. GO![1]

LEGO.com[]

NINJAGO® City’s last citizen runs for freedom between battling monsters. Will he make it out?

ITVX[]

As a Devourer attacks, a gamer named Scott navigates the chaos like a video game while the Ninja summon the legendary Ultra Dragon.

Extended[]

Ninjago City faces total ruin from a Devourer. Scott, a not-so-young gamer, survives the falling skyscrapers by treating the chaos like a high-stakes video game. From the deck of the Bounty, he witnesses the Ultra Dragon’s divine light vaporise the serpent. Scott saves a glowing artefact, a relic that will link this era of heroes to a distant, mysterious future.[2]

Plot

Plot[]

In Ninjago City, Scott dozes off in his apartment, having fallen asleep during a video game session, and unaware that a new Devourer was rampaging in the city this very moment. Scott is suddenly jolted awake as the battle reaches his apartment, shattering the window. Dazed and confused, Scott first listened to the Reporter on his television announcing that for anyone brave (or foolish) enough to still be remaining in the evacuated city, the last flight out would soon be departing from the top of the NGTV news center. Alarmed and now realizing the danger he was in, Scott rushed toward his window and briefly gasped at the Devourer's ongoing rampage. With a sudden quake, Scott's apartment building began to tilt, sending the man out the window as he now scrambled to survive and escape.

Imaging his scenario as a platform game, Scott miraculously leapt across various pieces of falling debris until he landed safely on the next rooftop, albeit while faceplanting himself in the process. After recovering, Scott caught a glimpse of the Ultra Dragon soaring past to do battle with the Devourer. There was no time to gawk however, as Scott had plenty more ground to cover to escape the ongoing battle. Making use of a torn car door as a makeshift snowboard, Scott navigated his way down the sloping roof, dodging more falling debris before attempting to propel himself into the NGTV helicopter, and just barely missing the Reporter's outstretched hand. As he now plummeted, seemingly to his death, Scott's anguished screams of terror were cut off when he landed face first onto the deck of Destiny's Bounty, upon which the four original Ninja were performing a ritual with a set of Elemental Lanterns to control the Ultra Dragon.

Meanwhile in the city's skyline, the Ultra Dragon bit down on the Devourer with all four of its heads, pinning the giant snake against a skyscraper as the creature writhed in pain. This was now the moment the ninja had waited for. With the Devourer pinned, the Ultra Dragon used its Ice breath to freeze it in place. It then called down a bolt of lightning, striking the Devourer head on and greatly wounding it. With the serpent now weakened, injured, and unable to move, the Ultra Dragon wrenched the snake's jaw open and breathed all four elements of Creation into its throat, destroying it. With the threat ended, the Ultra Dragon faded away and the exhausted ninja collapsed as they ended their ritual.

Many years later, Master Ivo rediscovered the elemental lantern of fire in a volcanic location and gazed solemnly upon it as the lantern activated in his hand.
