Continue the Chase

Information

Start Date

January 23, 2018

End Date

March 15, 2018

Overview

Continue the Chase was a Ninjago-themed brickfilming contest hosted on LEGO's Rebrick platform taking place from January 23, 2018[1] to March 15, 2018. There were five categories, each holding one of the five winners. The "Viewer's Choice" winner was narrowed down to fifteen entries by the judges, then decided through a poll held on LEGO Life from March 28, 2018 to April 4, 2018.[2]

Official description

Official description[]

Create your own brickfilm version of the thrilling conclusion of the Chasing the Mask of Vengeance! How would you end the video? What crazy, unexpected turns will you have the story take? What sort of funny thing will happen to the ninja? What kind of stunts will they pull? What sort of fight scene will they have? You decide! Create a brickfilm using Ninjago characters to end the video. If you don’t have the exact characters or pieces, just re-create them the best you can to tell your story, and share your thrilling conclusion in this contest![3]

Prizes

Prizes[]

All five winners would each receive the following:

  • 70633 Kai Spinjitzu Master
  • 70634 Nya Spinjitzu Master
  • 70635 Jay Spinjitzu Master
  • 70636 Zane Spinjitzu Master
  • 70637 Cole Spinjitzu Master
  • 70638 Katana V11
  • 70639 Street Race of Snake Jaguar
  • 70640 S.O.G. Headquarters
  • 70641 Ninja Nightcrawler
  • 70642 Killow vs. Samurai X
  • 70643 Temple of Resurrection
  • A winner's certificate
  • A customized winner’s trophy from a LEGO Ninjago designer
  • A LEGO Ninjago swag package
  • A then-upcoming summer 2018 Lego Ninjago set (that was shipped to the winners upon its release)
Winners

Winners[]

  • "Jay wins A Mr.E Prize" by Aaron Paben (for "Best Brickfilm")
  • "The Surprise Biker" by GDS Studios (for "Funniest Moment")
  • "Insane Zane!" by Adrián Piqueras (for "Best Stunt")
  • "Vengeful Duel" by Electroknight Productions (for "Best Fight Scene")
  • "A Myster"E"Ous End!" by mickeyD0715 (for "Viewers' Choice")
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Residents of Quebec, Canada were unable to participate in the contest due to the province's provincial laws.[2]
