The Greatest Villain of all Time in NINJAGO

Information

Start Date

May 16, 2016

End Date

August 11, 2016

Overview

The Greatest Villain of all Time in NINJAGO was a Ninjago-themed brickfilming contest hosted on LEGO's Rebrick platform taking place from May 16, 2016[1] to August 11, 2016.[2]

Official description

Official description[]

Have you ever wanted to immerse yourself in a fun, action-packed ninja universe? Now is your chance to dive into the adventurous NINJAGO world and explore the greatest villains. We invite you to make a max. 1 minute stop-motion video with the theme “the greatest villain of all time in NINJAGO.” Create a story around existing NINJAGO bad guys or build your own super villain(s). We encourage you to be creative and original and show your twist on the NINJAGO universe.[2]

Prizes

Prizes[]

Grand prize winner[]

  • A trip for two to LEGOLAND, including:
    • Transportation (economy class)
    • Two nights stay at Hotel LEGOLAND in Billund in a NINJAGO room
    • Exclusive tour of the new NINJAGO world in LEGOLAND Billund opening summer 2016
    • Meeting one of the NINJAGO designers and the chance to enjoy the new NINJAGO Dark Ride and miniland
  • A copy of 70751 Temple of Airjitzu signed by the designer

Runner up winners[]

  • A copy of 70751 Temple of Airjitzu signed by the designer
