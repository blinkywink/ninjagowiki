Ninja For a Day

Information

Start Date

April 5, 2017

End Date

May 10, 2017

Overview

Ninja For a Day was a Ninjago-themed brickfilming contest hosted on LEGO's Rebrick platform taking place from April 5, 2017[1] to May 10, 2017.[2]

Official description

Official description[]

What does a day in the life of a Ninjagan (a resident of Ninjago city) look like? How does a ninja spend his/her day? Show us in this stop-motion contest for a chance to have your brickfilm featured in the LEGO NINJAGO Movie![2]

Prizes

Prizes[]

Grand prize winners[]

  • The possibility to have their entries featured in Act One of the Ninjago Movie with credits
  • The full LEGO Ninjago Movie assortment
  • One exclusive LEGO Ninjago mystery set valued at $300 USD
  • The Art of the Movie book signed by the filmmakers
  • Signed LEGO Minifigures from the movie

Bonus winners[]

  • The possibility to have their entries featured in Act One of the Ninjago Movie with credits

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Kale Hitsman's "Ninsure" was originally one of the grand prize winners, but had to be demoted to a bonus winner due to "email complications."[3]
  • Christof Muñoz's "Off To Work" has since been removed from YouTube.[4]
