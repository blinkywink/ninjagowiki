The Imperium science fair was an event held at Sora's school in the Kingdom of Imperium.

History

History[]

The fair was hosted by Mr. Koenig, the teacher for Sora's class. Her classmate, Jordana, made a typical volcano model for her project, but Sora outshined Jordana and the rest of their classmates by inventing a hard light technology known as a Photac. Sora's invention impressed the scientists and judges at the fair, and she was crowned the winner, which caused Jordana to harbor a deep-seated hatred towards Sora.
