The Shintaro Dungeon Whip is a remix of The Weekend Whip, the theme song for Ninjago. It was used in the intro for the thirteenth season.

A Christmas version of the song titled "The Christmas Whip" was later released, incorporating the Christmas carol, "Carol of the Bells."[1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Shintaro Dungeon Whip has the longest Weekend Whip title, not counting the first two remixes.
  • The Shintaro Dungeon Whip was also the first Weekend Whip to mention Elemental Powers, previously done in 21st Century Ninja.
  • The Shintaro Dungeon Whip took 21 days to be released after first being heard in "Into the Dark."
  • In the thirteenth episode of The Fold's "Weekly Whip" series, Dan revealed that this song's original name was just "The Shintaro Whip" until Tommy Andreasen suggested adding the word 'Dungeon' to the name.[2]
    • Dan also revealed that the group had the most fun making this Weekend Whip than any of the other remixes for the series.
  • The original idea for the instrumental music was composed in a different key and was later adjusted to the key Dan Castady would be singing in.[3]
  • This is the first remix to be remixed.
  • The demo version of this song, "The Dungeon Whip", is simply the opening song from season 13 with the opening of the Shintaro Dungeon whip added on, with this opening being pitched slightly higher.
  • This is the first remix to use a different time signature, using 6/8 instead of 4/4.
