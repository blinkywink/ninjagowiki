The Christmas Whip is a remix of The Shintaro Dungeon Whip by The Fold, incorporating the classic Christmas carol, "Carol of the Bells."[1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The song coins new terms, such as "Chriswhip" and "Whipmas".
  • The official video shows Christmas-related moments such as Kai hallucinating that Samurai X is Santa Claus in "Can of Worms" and Zane saying "Merry Christmas" to a swarm of Treehorns in "Tick Tock" (though in the latter case, the line is not from the original episode, but rather, a reuse of Brent Miller saying "Merry Christmas" from one of his videos[citation needed]).
  • Unlike the other Weekend Whip remixes, this song specifically focuses on a subject not canon to the Ninjago world, as Christmas does not exist in Ninjago[2] (though it's possible a similar holiday exists).[3]
