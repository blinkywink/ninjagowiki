Overview

Rise of the Vermillion is a song sung by The Fold. It was designed as an official theme for the seventh season of the cartoon, describing the various acts and crimes committed by the Vermillion in their plans to assist the Time Twins.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This song is played instrumentally in the third vehicle montage in the Decoded episode, "Vehicles and Mechs."
  • This is the first and currently only Ninjago song to take place from the villain's perspective.
  • This is currently the only non-Weekend Whip Ninjago song (apart from possibly "We Are Ninja") in which the title of the song is not said in the song itself.
  • The cover depicts Vermin.
