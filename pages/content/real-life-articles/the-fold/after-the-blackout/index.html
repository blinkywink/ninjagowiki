Overview

After the Blackout is an official song for Ninjago, composed, and sung by The Fold. This song's vocals are noticeably more digital than most of The Fold's songs, in reference to the technology motif of the series' third season, or Blackout.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The song was heard in the show for the first time in "Ninja Roll" during the final stretch between Lloyd and Chamille in the Thunderblade race, starting when Lloyd is driving his Jungle Raider.
  • Dan Castady described "The Arcadian Whip" as "What if After the Blackout was a remix of The Weekend Whip?"[1]
  • Filming outside his house following a blizzard in Chicago, Dan Castady sang a brief parody of the song, "After the Whiteout."[2]
  • The "ninja ninja ninja ninja" part of this song was used in a scrapped Weekend Whip remix for Season 11.[3]
