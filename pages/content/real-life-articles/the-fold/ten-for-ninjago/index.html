Overview

Ten for Ninjago is a song composed by The Fold for the 10th year anniversary of Ninjago. It was published on June 26, 2021 on Spotify.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In the song, Serpentine is mispronounced "serpen-TYNE," the pronunciation of the real word, in addition to the way Kalmaar pronounces it.
  • This is the first non-Weekend Whip Ninjago song to be released since "Digital Love" in 2020.
  • A number of additional references to the ten years of the series include:
    • "Jump it up, kick it back, whip around and spin again" - The Weekend Whip
    • "We got hunted" - Season 9: Hunted (This is confirmed by the shot in the music video featuring the opening scene of Hunted)
    • "Ninja Roll" - The episode or the song
    • "a wicked return" - Pilot (Garmadon), Season 1 (the Serpentine/the Great Devourer), Season 2 (The Overlord), Season 3 (The Overlord/Pythor), Season 4 (Chen), Season 5 (Morro), The Realm of Shadows (Clouse) Season 6 (Nadakhan), Dark Island Trilogy (Clouse again), Day of the Departed (almost all villains revealed up to that point), Season 7 (Time Twins), Season 8 (Garmadon again), Season 10 (Oni), Season 11 (Aspheera), Season 13 (Grief-Bringer), The Island (Ronin), Seabound (Wojira).
    • "New history lurking" - Though it is hard to objectively state which history is "new", any information not included between the Pilot and Season 2's finale could be considered new. Therefore, Season 5 (Wu's first student, Morro), Season 6 (Nadakhan and his crew), Day of the Departed (Yang's curse), Season 7 (Aftermath of the Serpentine War), Season 8/9 (Oni/Dragon history/First Spinjitzu Master's origins), Season 11 (Aspheera and the Ice Emperor's respective pasts), Season 12 (videogame development and history), Season 13 (Lilly's past), The Island (Keepers' history), Seabound (the first named original elemental master, and her story)
  • The segment which starts "Jay, Cole, Kai, Lloyd, Wu, Garmadon, Nya, and Zane") changes, at one point removing Garmadon and Nya's names whilst inserting P.I.X.A.L. and Misako's, then finalizes by having all except Misako's. This reflects the change in focus characters during years 1-3, 4-6, and 7-9; the same years as mentioned in the recap.
  • The music video was filmed and edited by Ninjago fan and video editor Hayley Wolfe.[1]
  • This song, along with "We Are Ninjago" and "The Weekend Whip" is one of the only songs to also be officially released in another language, that being German.
