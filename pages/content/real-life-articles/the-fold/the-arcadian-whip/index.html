The Arcadian Whip is a remix of The Weekend Whip, the theme song for Ninjago. It was used in the intro for the twelfth season.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Arcadian Whip was originally going to be called "The 64-Bit Whip" or "The Chip Whip".[1]
    • The word "arcadian" does not traditionally refer to arcades or video games; rather, it describes something "rural, rustic, or pastoral, especially suggesting simple, innocent contentment".[2]
  • Dan Castady described this song as, "What if After the Blackout was a remix of The Weekend Whip?"[3]
