Overview

“I knew a lot about the show before hand. And I also personally know a lot of the actors and writers. So coming onto ninjago was both amazing and also like coming back to a familiar place for me. It was really exciting and fun.”
— Panton on X[1]

External links
Giles Panton (born September 13, 1982) is a Canadian voice actor. He provides the voice of Kalmaar and Jimmy in "Papergirl" in Ninjago.[2][3] He also provides the voice of Rapton in Ninjago: Dragons Rising.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He also plays Iron Man/Tony Stark in LEGO Avengers: Climate Conundrum and Clay Moorington, Beast Master and Burnzie in Nexo Knights.
    • He is the last main cast member of Nexo Knights to receive a role in Ninjago.
  • In DREAMZzz, he voices the Sandman, the Masked Hero and Claudius.
  • He stated in 2021 that Kalmaar was the most fun villain he has played.[4]
  • He records short clips of himself acting as Kalmaar and sends them on X, often twisting fan requests.[5][6][7][8]
  • He answers many Kalmaar-related questions in character on X.
  • He, Ian Hanlin, Bill Newton, Michael Daingerfield, Brian Drummond, Cole Howard, Britt McKillip, Nicole Oliver and Adrian Petriw voiced characters in LEGO Avengers: Climate Conundrum.
  • Coincidentally, in Season 14: Seabound, he voices Kalmaar who is a commander of a character voiced by Brian Drummond, Gripe. In Season 1 of Dragons Rising, he voices Rapton who is under the command of a character voiced by Brian Drummond, Ras.
