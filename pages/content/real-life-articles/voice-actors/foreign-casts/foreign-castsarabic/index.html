Trivia[]

  • For years, it was believed that the movie wasn't dubbed in Arabic language. However, according to the voice actress, Sally Elnems, who said she worked on it, the Arabic dub of the movie was produced but never released at all. Additionally, she said she voiced Koko.
  • The Arabic dub currently lacks consistency of many voices, where some voices are replaced between seasons or even episodes, and sometimes, roles are switched, especially for Kai, Jay and Cole.
    • The unconsistency of Jay's voice would start after his original and second voice actor, Ahmed Abdel Hamid and Mohamed Abdel Khalek, had left.
  • The voice actor for Jay from pilots until season 10, Ahmed Abdel Hamid, was the director of the Arabic version during his presence as Jay. He also directed the first eight episodes of season 11. However, Ahmed Albanhawi recorded Jay's lines in these episodes instead of him.
    • Interestingly, Jay's voice actor in Latin America also directed the series from Season 3 of Dragons Rising to the present.
    • After leaving the dub, his brother, Sherif Abdel Hamid, has directed the dubbing since season 11 until now.
  • No games of the show were dubbed in Arabic. But some shorts were, like Wu's Teas, Wu-Cru, Happy Birthday to You!, Ninjago: Reimagined's Golden Hour and Ninjago 15th Anniversary Shorts.
