Overview

This page contains a listing of voice actors for the Croatian dubs of instalments in the Ninjago franchise.

Lego commissioned two dubs of Ninjago. The first, by AVID Production, was produced from the Pilot episodes to Season 2. The second, by Livada Produkcija and VSI-NET Croatia, was produced from Season 1 to Season 15.
