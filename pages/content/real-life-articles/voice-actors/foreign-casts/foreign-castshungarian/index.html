Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Károly Moser, the actor who voices Jay in this dub, shares a birthday with Brent Miller (the English voice of Zane), but was born exactly three years later than him.
  • The Hungarian dub of The LEGO Ninjago Movie is one of many foreign dubs of the film to have the same voice actors for most of the main cast as in the show at the time.
