Foreign casts/Ukrainian

Overview

This page contains a listing of voice actors for the Ukrainian dubs of instalments in the Ninjago franchise.

Tak Treba Production was commissioned to do a second dub for Season 1 of Dragons Rising. This is marked with “TTP” in the table. Other voice actors have been participated in a dub by studios "Le Doyen" and "1+1" and are unmarked because of that is main dub.
