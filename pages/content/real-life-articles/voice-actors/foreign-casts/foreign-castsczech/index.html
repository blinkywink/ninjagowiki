Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Czech version of Season 11: Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu is the only dub where Clutch Powers is voiced by the same voice actor (Ludvík Král) as in the movie LEGO: The Adventures of Clutch Powers.
  • In the Czech version of "Wrong Place, Wrong Time, Garmadon quite unusually uses a swear word after he meets the ninja in Ouroboros. When Lloyd says "I've grown a little since the last time you saw me, Dad.", Garmadon yells "Stop him!" in the original but calls him a "bastard" in the Czech dub.
