Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The German dub of The LEGO Ninjago Movie is one of many foreign dubs of the film to have the same voice actors for most of the main cast as in the show at the time.
  • Some of the voice actors in the franchise are related:
    • Jaro Haggège is the son of Julien Haggège.
    • Sebastian Kluckert is the son of Jürgen Kluckert.
    • Jimmy Le Vu Wiesner is the son of Michael Wiesner.
    • Rubina Nath is the wife of Tobias Nath.
  • Many voice actors in the cast were replaced for the Prime Empire Original Shorts and Season 12, as the show switched from a dubbing studio in Berlin to one in Hamburg in 2020.
    • With the launch of Ninjago: Dragons Rising, the Berlin studio was commissioned again.
