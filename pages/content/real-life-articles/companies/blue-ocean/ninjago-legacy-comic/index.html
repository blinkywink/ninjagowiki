Overview

This article is about one of the lines of Ninjago comics produced by Blue Ocean. For other uses, see Comics (disambiguation).
"I know it's a scroll, but what does it say? It's written in chicken scratch."
The subject of this article has never been officially released in English.
The name for this page was determined by using the title of the original German release.


The Ninjago: Legacy Comic is a Ninjago comic book series published by Blue Ocean in various European countries.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This line has been known to have released in the following countries:
    • Austria
    • Belgium
    • Czechia
    • France
    • Germany
    • Hungary
    • Italy
    • Poland
    • Slovakia
    • Spain
    • Switzerland
  • Issues of this series re-use comics from other publications, the first few of which were also previously reused in the Ninjago Comic.
  • Nr. 9: The Night of the Nindroids! re-published the version of Night of the Nindroids where the Ninja wear their Kimono gi, rather than the version where they wear their Techno gi.
