Overview


The Ninjago: Legacy Magazine is a magazine line which takes influence from various aspects across Ninjago's run. It has been running in several European countries since 2019, and in Canada for an unknown amount of time.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Lost Tomb! was never released in English.
  • The comic from the issue 10 features a real Comic-con logo.
  • Despite the comics from the magazines taking place in different periods of time, the ninja are only wearing their Legacy and Dojo suits.
    • Issues 11, 15, 20 and 28 feature ghost versions of these Cole's suits.
    • While some issues taking place before Season 5 make an exception for Nya and feature her in a casual outfit, the majority of these comics still feature her in Water Ninja outfit despite it not making sense timeline wise.
  • These magazines were the only media to provide some lore for the snake warriors and the Greenbone Warriors until at least some information on the Greenbone Warriors was given by Ninjago: Dragons Rising.
  • Internally this and the Lego Batman Magazine are considered the same magazine by retailers, known as: Lego Special Edition.
  • Issue 30 and 32 are the most expensive of any form of Ninjago magazine to date, being £8.99.
  • Not every issue releases in every country like Sweden and some countries get them later in some cases 6 months late.
