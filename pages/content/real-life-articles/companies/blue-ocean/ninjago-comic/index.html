Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This line has been known to have released in the following countries:
    • Austria
    • Belgium
    • Czechia
    • France
    • Germany
    • Poland
    • Russia (only issues 1-7 and 33)
    • Spain
    • Switzerland
  • Issues of this series re-use comics from other publications. For example:
    • Issues 1-11 consist of each instalment of the Papercutz Ninjago graphic novel series.
    • Issues 15-17 consist of each instalment of the Dark Island Trilogy.
      • Starting from issue 23, episode summaries used in instalments of Dark Island Trilogy were featured at the end of each issue.
    • Other issues consist of comics previously used in issues of the Ninjago Magazine.
  • A few years after the release of this series, Blue Ocean created the Ninjago: Legacy Comic.
  • The cover of issue 19 appeared as a poster in Rufus McCallister's room in Rock Bottom!.
  • None of the comics from this series ever used Season 2: Legacy of the Green Ninja or Day of the Departed branding.
