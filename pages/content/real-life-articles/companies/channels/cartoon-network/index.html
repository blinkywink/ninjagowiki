Cartoon Network

Cartoon Network

Information

Year Established

1992

Founder

Betty Cohen

Overview

Cartoon Network is the channel that aired Ninjago in several different countries, such as the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, Poland (since Season 4), Czech Republic and Latin America (only Season 10, Season 11, The Island and Season 14).

Cartoon Network was also the main home of Legends of Chima in the United States from 2013 to 2015.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Cartoon Network US website considers Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu and Ninjago to be two separate series. See here for more information.
  • Ninjago is Cartoon Network's longest currently running show, having run on Cartoon Network since the pilot episodes in 2011.
  • In 2021, Season 14 aired on Netflix before Cartoon Network in the United States. It has not aired on the network since October 2020.
  • In August 2024, Cartoon Network shut down their website which formerly hosted many web games and other Ninjago and Legends of Chima media.
