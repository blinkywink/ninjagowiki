Okoo is a French children's program broadcast on the France 4 channel. It has been broadcasting Ninjago since 2019 (year of creation). In France, it is also a free app on which Ninjago is available.

Since Ninjago: Dragons Rising, Okoo, France TV (France 4) and LEGO France have been organizing world premieres of Ninjago in several cities, where the first two episodes of the season are broadcast.
