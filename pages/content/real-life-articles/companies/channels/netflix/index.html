Overview

Netflix is a streaming service available worldwide. It was founded in 1997, starting as a DVD rental service, moving to streaming film and television in 2007. Ninjago is streamed globally on the service, with availability varying between countries. An exclusive "Happy Birthday" special mini-episode was also formerly available to watch.

Ninjago: Dragons Rising was a Netflix original series and premiered on Netflix on June 1, 2023 in the United States. It was released on Netflix Australia on June 15, 2023. At some point in January 2024, Netflix removed the "Original Series" branding from Ninjago: Dragons Rising, and stopped being the sole distributor of the show in the United States.

Availability

Availability[]

The selections of seasons available across different countries are as follows:

  • Season 1 to Season 7, Season 11 to Season 13 and Dragons Rising Season 1 to Season 3 are available to watch in the United States, UK, Canada, and New Zealand.
  • Season 1 to Season 7, Season 11 and Season 12 and Dragons Rising Season 1, Season 2 and Season 3 are available to watch in Australia.
    • At some point, Season 8 to Season 10, and Season 14 to Season 15 were removed from Netflix.
  • Taiwan has every single installment except for the pilot episodes or Day of the Departed.
    • Curiously, the WilFilm installments are only accessible when the language is set to English.
  • Mexico/Latin America has seasons 1-7, seasons 11-13, as well as Season 1, Season 2 and Season 3 of Dragons Rising. Previously, it also had the Pilot episodes, Happy Birthday to You! and The LEGO Ninjago Movie, similar to various other regions.
    • On April 16, 2025, the Oni Trilogy and Season 12 to Season 15 were removed in Mexico/Latin America.
  • The Philippines and Hong Kong had seasons one and eight to Seabound. Previously, it had all of the seasons up until Season 10 and Day of the Departed.
    • Then on April 15, 2022, all the seasons aside from the pilot episodes and Day of the Departed were returned.
    • On April 16, 2025, the Oni Trilogy and Season 14 to Season 15 were removed in Hong Kong.
  • Sweden has all seasons except Day of the Departed, The Island, and Season 14. However seasons 1 to 5 are only available in the Swedish dub. The Pilot episodes recently got removed.
  • Hungary has seasons 1-11 with the exception of seasons 6-8.
  • The Netherlands has seasons 1-7, 11-13 and Dragons Rising seasons 1, 2 and 3 Part 1. Previously, it also had the Pilot episodes and The LEGO Ninjago Movie.
  • Poland has seasons 1 to 7, 11 to 13 and all seasons of Dragons Rising. Previously, it had all seasons besides the pilot episodes and Day of the Departed. The LEGO Ninjago Movie used to be available, but was removed.
  • Italy has seasons 1-7, 11-13 and Dragons Rising seasons 1 and 2. It formerly had The Lego Ninjago Movie.
  • Switzerland has seasons 1-15, Dragons Rising season 1 and Part 1 of season 2 and The LEGO Ninjago Movie.
  • Finland and Sri Lanka have all seasons besides the pilot episodes and Day of the Departed. The LEGO Ninjago Movie is not available.
  • Norway has all seasons besides the pilot episodes and Day of the Departed. The LEGO Ninjago Movie is not available. Seasons 8-10 and 13-15 used to be available, but were removed.
  • France has seasons 1-7, the Fire Chapter of the season 11, the first ten episodes of season 12 and Dragons Rising seasons 1-2 and the part 1 of the season 3. Seasons 8-10, 13-15, other episodes of the seasons 11 and 12, Happy Birthday to You! and The LEGO Ninjago Movie used to be available, but were removed.
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Netflix numbers seasons of Ninjago differently than some other sources. See here for more information.
  • There is a slightly inaccurate description for the series: "Five golden weapons were forged in the heavens above. With them, a ninja master has the power to create -- or destroy!" This could be counting the Shurikens of Ice as two separate weapons.
  • In Italy, seasons 1 and 2's episodes are divided into two 11-minute episodes on Netflix. These episodes all feature a recap for the previous 11 minute episode and the episode count is also changed (for example: part 2 of episode 11 is referred as "episode 22"), the first part always ends with a "to be continued" text. This altered version is on Netflix because that is how the first 2 seasons were aired in Italy.
  • As of April 17th 2025, the same day Ninjago Dragons Rising: Season 3 premiered, the Oni Trilogy (Sons of Garmadon, Hunted and March of the Oni) alongside, The Island, Seabound and Crystalized were removed from Netflix in various countries for unknown reasons.
