Year Established

Overview

The Australian Broadcasting Corporation is Australia's principal public service broadcaster that airs and streams Ninjago and Ninjago: Dragons Rising on their channels ABC Family and ABC Entertains and streaming service ABC iview, alongside 9Go! and Cartoon Network.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Season 1 of Ninjago: Dragons Rising was added to ABC iview on September 12, 2025.
  • Season 2 of Ninjago: Dragons Rising was added to ABC iview on October 17, 2025.
  • Season 3 of Ninjago: Dragons Rising was added to ABC iview on November 21, 2025.
  • Season 11: Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu was added to ABC iview on December 19, 2025 being put under Ninjago: Season 1.
  • Season 12: Prime Empire and Season 13: Master of the Mountain were added to ABC iview on January 16, 2026 being put under Ninjago: Season 2.
  • The Island and Season 14: Seabound were added to ABC iview on February 13, 2026 being put under Ninjago: Season 3.
  • Season 15: Crystalized was added to ABC iview on March 6, 2026 being put under Ninjago: Season 4.
  • There is a multitude of episode naming errors, some being spelling and other grammatical across both Ninjago and Ninjago: Dragons Rising being:
    • "The Screaming Report" rather than "The Screaming Earth" for Ninjago: Dragons Rising, Season 3, Episode 15.
    • "The News Never Sleeps" rather than "The News Never Sleeps!" for Ninjago, Season 11, Episode 6.
    • "Ninja vs. Lava" rather than "Ninja vs Lava" for Ninjago, Season 11, Episode 7.
    • "Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire" rather than "Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?" for Ninjago, Season 12, Episode 1.
    • "Superstar Rockin" rather than "Superstar Rockin' Jay" for Ninjago, Season 12, Episode 4.
    • "One Step Forward Two Steps Back" rather than "One Step Forward, Two Steps Back" for Ninjago, Season 12, Episode 9.
    • "Race Seven" rather than "Racer Seven" for Ninjago, Season 12, Episode 10.
    • "Stop Drop and Side Scroll" rather than "Stop, Drop and Side Scroll" for Ninjago, Season 12, Episode 12.
    • "The Upply Strike Back" rather than "The Upply Strike Back!" for Ninjago, Season 13, Episode 15.
    • "Public Enemy 1, 2, 3, 4, 5" rather than "Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5!" for Ninjago, Season 15, Episode 5.
