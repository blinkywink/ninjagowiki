An unnamed realm was one of the realms before the Merge and is now part of the Merged Lands. It is a large, plain-like land in constant sunrise, and usually gives off meta-physical illusions.

History

History[]

Prior to the series[]

This section is a stub. You can help expand this section by adding some information.

At an unknown point in time, the realm came into existence as one of the realms. At some point the First Spinjitzu Master would visit the realm.[1]

After the Liberation of Imperium[]

Dr. LaRow learned about and how to use the Weapon of Desolation in this realm.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Rising Ninja[]

Ras's Master teleported the Shadow Dojo for this realm for convenance and safety of the Wolf Clan following the Ritual of the Blood Moon.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Chaos Reigns[]

Wyldfyre, Frak, Sora and Riyu. Were sent to this realm due to Ras's tampering on the orders by his Master and the monastery gates were disabled.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

The Whispering Wood[]

Wyldfyre, Frak, Sora and Riyu realized they were trapped and explored the land. They found a stream to drink, but were confused that it was endless prairie for as far as they could see.

The Lean of a Tree[]

Sora encountered cats whom she played with and Frak saw pies growing on plants which he tried to eat. Wyldfyre figured out these were what they wanted to see as the realm created illusions of her friend's favorite things. She realized they were falling for a trap and the cats disappeared proving they were actually illusions. She realized the team was falling for a trap and took them to a bottomless pit and forced them to enter it. Thankfully, upon traversing this passageway, they entered a land in the same plane as the Merged Lands and gravity reversed.

Birthright[]

The ninja discovered that Dr. LaRow learned about the Weapon of Desolation from this realm. Ras went through a portal gate into his Shadow Dojo in this realm upon gaining another Source Dragon Icon. He communicated with his master using the Shadow Temple and began the next phase of his plan.
