The Phantasm Cave is a cave in the Wyldness. It is inhabited by a Dream-Guyver, serving as the initial trial set by Egalt and Rontu. The cave is used as a passage to Egalt and Rontu's home.

History

History[]

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

The ninja's journey to find the Dragon Masters took them through the Phantasm Cave, where darkness engulfs everything around them. The ninja found themselves in complete darkness, unable to see anything. Unbeknownst to them, a Dream-Guyver appeared before them, its electricity reaching out to touch the heads of the ninja. The ninja were overcome by a deep slumber, each experiencing their own personal fears.

Arin came face to face with his parents. Overwhelmed with a deep desire to be reunited with them, he desperately attempted to sprint towards them, but his attempts proved futile. His mother's voice echoed through the air, filled with confusion and sadness, as she questioned why he had forsaken them and wondered if the ninja had become his new family. Arin's heart ached as he cried out, pouring out his yearning for his parents, only to watch them disappear into nothingness.

Meanwhile, Wyldfyre witnessed Heatwave succumbing to the Wasting Sickness. On the other hand, Sora saw a digital display featuring Beatrix and Ras. As she approached, a sudden surge of electricity shot out from the screen, striking her chest. Sora screamed out in agony as Beatrix's hand grabbed hold of her. Lloyd caught sight of Wu, who informed him that he did not succeed in leading the ninja team, and that Wu made a mistake in entrusting Lloyd with his responsibilities. Suddenly, Ras materializes behind Wu, and as they both speak to Lloyd, their voices become distorted, emphasizing their disappointment in him. They make it clear to Lloyd that he will never reach their expectations. Wu and Ras then vanish and reappear twice, each time growing in size.

Jay came into Nya's view and she felt a surge of happiness, but to her dismay, Jay doesn't recognize her. This made Nya angry, but she quickly understood that something wasn't right because the real Jay would never forget about her. All of a sudden, she opened her eyes and saw that the rest of her group was fast asleep because of the creature. Without hesitation, Nya frightened the monster away, successfully waking up her companions.
