Overview

“This storagehouse is where we mystical folks store most of our magical items.”
— Gandalaria to Cole, "Secrets of the Wyldness"

The Magical storagehouse (called the Magic storehouse for short, or the Sorcerers' Storehouse[1]) is a mystical storagehouse in the Forest of Spirits used by Gandalaria and other sorcerers. It is a huge mushroom that is bigger on the inside.

History

History[]

Background[]

Long ago, the many sorcerers and sorceresses of Mysterium used the storage house to store their magical items.
