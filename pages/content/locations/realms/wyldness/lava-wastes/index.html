The Lava Wastes are a volcanic desert in the Wyldness.

History

History[]

Background[]

In the Lava Wastes, a ship carrying Wyldfyre and the Caregiver bot crash-landed. Heatwave, a dragon, was asked by the Caregiver bot to take care of Wyldfyre, and he agreed. For years, Wyldfyre grew up under the care of Lava Dragons, including Heatwave and the Caregiver bot in the Lava Wastes. But most of the dragons that raised Wyldfyre died due to the Wasting Sickness, leaving Heatwave as her sole guardian.[1] During the Merge, Wyldfyre gained the Elemental Power of Heat. Eventually, Wyldfyre started playing pranks in the Lava-Tides at their village. The Claws of Imperium arrived in the Lava Wastes one day, aiming to capture Heatwave and exploit his dragon power for the Kingdom of Imperium. To save Heatwave, Wyldfyre made the decision to leave the Lava Wastes.[2][3]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

Kai and Wyldfyre journeyed to the Lava Wastes in order to request the purple Dragon Core from the Lava-Tides. Unfortunately, due to Wyldfyre's pranks, the Lava-Tides refused to grant their request unless she cleaned up the mess caused by her pranks. Despite their efforts in cleaning up, the Lava-Tides broke their promise and threw the core into the Pit of No Return. Determined, Kai and Wyldfyre ventured into the pit to retrieve the Dragon Core, facing Death Wasps along the way. Once they escaped, they sealed the pit, only to be confronted by Dorama and his stagehands.

They Call It Doom[]

Kai and Wyldfyre engaged in a fierce battle with Dorama, his stagehands, and the Lava-Tides over the purple Dragon Core. In the midst of the chaos, Dorama replaced the real core with a fake one. The Lava-Tide Leader unintentionally gained an advantage by consuming Heat, causing her to grow in size. Heatwave tried to intervene but ended up getting seriously injured by the Lava-Tide Leader. Eventually, Kai and Wyldfyre managed to escape with the fake Dragon Core, riding on Heatwave as they retreated to the outskirts of the Lava Wastes.

Land of Lost Things[]

Kai and Wyldfyre roamed the Lava Wastes to collect certain crystals that could be used to treat Heatwave's injuries.

The Administration[]

As Wyldfyre continued to worry over Heatwave's condition, Kai encouraged her to clear her mind through meditative forms. He then taught her some and the two began practicing until Heatwave recovered and they departed from the Lava Wastes.
