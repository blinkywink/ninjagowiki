Overview

The Frog Tribe is a tribe of humanoid frogs that reside in the Wyldness.[2]

History

History[]

Background[]

Millenia ago, the tribe was attacked by the Wolf Clan during the Forbidden Five's rampage of the realm. After the Five's banishment to the Nether-Space, the tribe eventually recovered.[2]

At some point before the Merge, the legends of the ninja from Ninjago came to the Wyldness, and Alfonzo Frohicky heard them when he was a tadpole.[4]
