Overview

“Sure it took some getting used to, and it was A LOT heavier than his old scythe, but it seemed very fitting for a Master of Earth wield a hammer.”
— Narration, Way of the Departed, Chapter 2

The hammer is a tool composed of a stick with a metal head on top of it, that is a common weapon in Ninjago and beyond.
