“Once, the Wyldness was lush and green and full of life! Birds flew in our skies and fish swam in our waters! It was really beautiful!”
— Iriel, The Lycan King

The Wyldness, formerly known as the Realm of the Wyldness,[9] was one of the realms before the Merge and is now part of the Merged Lands. It's a multi-terrain realm full of many different biomes inhabited by many different species of sentient life. It is composed among other things, a volcanic region called the Lava Wastes inhabited by Lava-Tides and Lava Dragons, and a dense and dark forest region called the Forest of Spirits. There is also a floating Island called Chima covered in jungles, mountains, and swamps, which are inhabited by various species of anthropomorphic animals. The realm was the native land of the Forbidden Five and their army, which greatly affected the inhabitants and landscapes of the world millennia ago. After their banishment, their successor Lord Argus accidentally released a curse on the Wyldness while manipulated by Zaiden Kane, a sorcerer who sought a sixth member of the Forbidden Five. However, the Children of the Wolf managed to lift the realm's curse with the help of Garmadon and rebuild the Wolf Clan though a new faction of the Clan was assembled by Ras and his Master.

History

History[]

Background[]

At an unknown point in time, the Wyldness came into existence as one of the realms. At some point the First Spinjitzu Master would visit the realm[10] and while there visited the Pinnacle of Crystals where he hid the Yellow Dragon Core, the Lava-Tide Village where he entrusted the Lava-Tides with the Purple Dragon Core[11], and created the Serpentine[12][13] which he eventually brought to Ninjago.[5] Some point after the realm came into existence, the Mother Sun created avian spirits known as the Phoenix who were bestowed the Elemental Power of fire and became the guardians of Chima. At an unknown point in time the Djinn had some presence in the Wyldness and built a tomb in the Pinnacle of Crystals.

During the Wyldness medieval period,[14] a group of Elemental Masters known as the Forbidden Five established the Wolf Clan in the Wyldness, particularly within the Forest of Spirits where they built the Shadow Dojo. Under the Five's leadership, the Wolf Clan became revered as a powerful and noble group[15] who built Lupindor as their capital city.[16] However, the Chaos of Thunderfang which initially empowered the Wolf Clan ultimately corrupted it, turning the clan evil.[17] They became relentless agents of Chaos, and by using Shatterspin, the Forbidden Five unleashed devastation across the Wyldness, leaving destruction in their wake and forcing the inhabitants to either submit to their tyranny or face death. They made alliances with some tribes although their alliances broke. To the Forbidden Five, their actions were a demonstration of dominance over the Wyldness, but in reality, they were driving the realm toward annihilation, a fate they intended to replicate across all realms. The Dragon Masters and the Phoenix,[18] recognized the threat. The Dragon Masters eventually intervened and banished the Forbidden Five to the Nether-Space.[19][20]

In the aftermath, Argus assumed leadership of the Wolf Clan, attempting to steer the wolf warriors in the same path as the Forbidden Five. Under the influence of Zaiden Kane, Argus struck the Gong of Shattering to perform Shatterspin, which unleashed a devastating curse that trapped the Wyldness in a state of half-life. Consequently, the Wyldness was considered a cursed land, leaving the realm in a state of desolation that persisted for countless ages.[8]

At some point a portal was created in a lake in a desert in Ninjago linking it to a lake in the Forest of Spirits.[21]

Following Garmadon's team-up with the Children of the Wolf, and the death of the Lycan King, the curse was lifted and many petrified wolf warriors were revived and freed from Thunderfang's corruption.[22]

The Village of the Tiger Tribe was endangered by frequent tremors. Although the Ancients of the Wyldness foretold that the disturbances would stop, Zilvar doubted this and decided to seek a new home for the tribe. Unfortunately, by the time he returned, the tremors had devastated the village, resulting in the deaths of everyone present. Overwhelmed by grief, chose to leave the Wyldness.[23] Unbeknownst to him, Zeras survived and was later rescued by a primordial being, choosing to adopt the name Ras.[24]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

A ship crash-landed in the Lava Wastes. Investigating the wreckage, Heatwave discovered a caregiver bot asking for help to raise the baby inside. Heatwave grew protective of Wyldfyre as the Lava Dragons and caregiver bot raised her. As she grew, Wyldfyre bonded deeply with her unique family. One night, the Merge occurred while they slept. The Merge resulted in Wyldfyre receiving the Elemental Power of Heat, which she discovered while reaching for a melon. Years later, while relaxing in a hot spring, Wyldfyre heard Heatwave's frantic roaring and watched the Claws of Imperium capture him. Gathering her supplies, she ignored the robot's warnings and left to rescue him.

Different teams of the ninja arrive in the Wyldness to secure ancient Dragon Cores. Kai and Wyldfyre went to look for the purple Dragon Core located inside the Lava-Tides' Village. However, the Lava-Tides gave them a frosty reception due to the chaotic pranks Wyldfyre had pulled on them in the past. The Lava-Tides refused to hand over the core unless Wyldfyre took responsibility and repaired all the damage from her past antics. After she and Kai successfully cleaned the village, they approached the leader to claim the core. To their dismay, the vindictive Lava-Tides tossed the Dragon Core into the Pit of No Return, revealing the entire deal was a prank designed to trick Wyldfyre into free labor. Undeterred, Kai and Wyldfyre braved the pit, which was heavily infested with Death Wasps. Though they successfully evaded the swarm and sealed the pit, Dorama and a squad of Imperium soldiers suddenly ambushed them and seized the artifact.

Simultaneously, Nya and Sora arrived alongside the dragons Jiro and Zanth to recover the yellow Dragon Core in the Pinnacle of Crystals, only to discover the case where it was supposed to be was empty. Seeking answers, they journeyed to an ancient tomb to locate the artifact using the Seeker Stones, but they ended up misusing them as they were cursed to be hunted by the Howlers. Unable to defeat the curse, the group was rescued by Jiro and Zanth, who hastily flew them away. Sensing a familiar presence, Zanth brought the ninja to Arrakore. By helping Arrakore regain his lost hope, the ninja empowered him to use his potent wish magic to defeat the pursuing Howlers. Once fully recovered, Arrakore granted Nya's earnest wish to find the true location of the yellow Dragon Core.

A battle erupted in the Lava-Tides' Village between Kai and Wyldfyre, Dorama's Imperium forces, and the Lava-Tides. Amidst the conflict, Wyldfyre inadvertently used her Heat powers on the Lava-Tide Leader, accidentally growing them to a gigantic size. This mutation gave the Lava-Tide an advantage, nearly destroying both Kai and Wyldfyre. Heatwave flew into the fray to intercept the giant, but he sustained a severe injury from the colossal leader. Kai and Wyldfyre managed to escape with the purple Dragon Core and retreated to the outskirts of the Lava Wastes. While trying to figure out how to heal Heatwave, Kai suddenly ran off to smash nearby boulders. A confused Wyldfyre initially thought he was wasting time until Kai explained he was hunting for medicinal geodes. With her help, they uncovered the rare crystals, rushed back to Heatwave, and melted the minerals down to apply as a cooling salve over his wound. As they waited for the treatment to work, an impatient Wyldfyre chewed Kai out for his calm demeanor. Her outburst amused Kai, who admitted he was just as hot-headed when he was younger, recounting how Master Wu had to teach him patience and forms after Nya merged with the Endless Sea. Reluctantly, Wyldfyre joined Kai in practicing his rhythmic martial arts forms to pass the time. Hours later, the crystal medicine successfully healed Heatwave's wound to Wyldfyre's immense relief. However, as they prepared to fly out of the Wyldness, Kai noticed that the Dragon Core was just a replica.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Ras brings Jordana to the Wyldness and she expresses astonishment as she was taught that the Wyldness solely consisted of the Lava Wastes. Ras explains otherwise and names the many landmarks before mocking the Imperians for belittling the other realms aside from Imperium. Then, they arrive at the Shadow Dojo where Ras demands that Jordana gives him the Source Dragon energy she extracted from the Source Dragon of Energy. She does so and Ras uses it to open the doors to the Shadow Dojo. He declares his intentions to access its dark secrets and release the Forbidden Five from the Nether-Space. Inside, he tasks Jordana with studying ancient Theroxian scrolls, introduces her to Cinder, and assembles his newly formed Wolf Clan.

Relying on a vision from the Source Dragons, the ninja travel to the Wyldness in pursuit the Dragon Masters. Their journey begins with a trek through the Phantasm Cave before they finally emerge onto the training plateau of the Dragon Masters. To prove their worth, the ninja must complete a final test, working together to successfully evade Egalt's attacks while managing to open the plateau's gates. Upon their success and learning that Ras planned to free the Forbidden Five, both Dragon Masters agree to teach the heroes the Rising Dragon technique. As the ninja begin their training in earnest on the plateau, Zane, Cole, Bonzle, and Jiro arrive in the foggy fesert, quickly realizing that Fedulian had lied to them about their destination to Mysterium. On the plateau, Egalt rigorously trains the classic ninja and Arin, whose training accidentally resulted in a rock hitting Egalt directly in the eye, ultimately demoting Arin down to the lowly First Fang ranking. Meanwhile, Cole, Zane, Bonzle, and Jiro were ambushed and defeated by one of Dorama's puppets. The group was captured and brought to Dorama's Academy for Acting, where Dorama himself forces them and other captives into a twisted theatrical performance. Fortunately, Bonzle helped Cole and Zane escape their electro-cage, allowing them to defeat Dorama and his puppet. Before the algae farmers and Crossroaders cart Dorama away to the authorities, Zane discoverd that the villain had scanned the local train lines to create highly detailed regional maps, providing the coordinates needed for their group to leave the Wyldness entirely and head toward Mysterium.

Back on the training plateau, the remaining ninja continue their regime until Kai became the first to successfully perform the Rising Dragon technique. However, their triumph was cut short when the Blood Moon rose in the sky, turning the Dragon Masters into petrified stone. Stranded without their teachers, the ninja left the plateau and venture to Forest of Spirits to stop Ras. Their journey took a chaotic turn when Jordana interfered, causing the Destiny's Bounty to crash-land. Concurrently, Gandalaria led Cole, Zane, and Bonzle into the exact same forest to locate the magical storagehouse, only for Jordana to intercept them. As the ninja traverse the woods in search of the Shadow Dojo, Jordana successfully captured Bonzle and attempted to deliver her to Ras, briefly fighting off a desperate rescue attempt by Arin and Riyu along the forest path.

During the Ritual of the Blood Moon, the ninja fight off the Wolf Clan. During this, Ras sacrifices Kai by knocking him into the portal, releasing Nokt. Just as the ninja manage to defeat the rest of the Wolf Clan, Ras calls upon his master; the master used their power to teleport Ras, the Wolf Clan, and the entire Shadow Dojo out of the forest, relocating them to the Land of Eternal Dawn. Months later, the Dragon Masters take over training Zane while Lloyd arrived bearing Janus Melon Stew. Lloyd informed the group about mysterious attacks targeting Arc Dragons and implored the Dragon Masters to sponsor them in the upcoming Tournament of the Sources. When Egalt and Rontu decline and insist Lloyd must lead the team, a conflicted Lloyd admitted he did not feel like a true master. In response, the two Dragon Masters suddenly ambush him; after Lloyd skillfully countered and defeated them both, Egalt and Rontu proudly declare that he was finally ready to lead the ninja into the trials ahead.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Explorer's Club reported mysterious lightning flashes deep within the Forest of Spirits. Trusting the lead, Kai, Nya, and Wyldfyre ventured into the woods in search of Jay, believing they had found their friend. Instead, they accidentally stumbled into and disrupted a sacred ritual held by the Lightning-Tides. Furious at the intrusion, the Lightning-Tides captured Kai and Nya, locked them in a prison cell, and put them on trial. Wyldfyre attempted a rescue by disguising herself as a Lava-Tide, but her cover failed, and she was thrown into the cell alongside them. Nya managed to explain their true mission, convincing the Lightning-Tides to grant her release, though Kai and Wyldfyre remained trapped. Nya quickly staged a breakout, allowing the entire group to escape together just as the Lightning-Tides issued a wanted poster for their arrest. Meanwhile, a truck driving through the Forest of Spirits was suddenly ambushed when the Forbidden Five landed directly in its path, raiding the truck for supplies for their journey to the First Land, where they plan to unchain Thunderfang. Back in the Lava Wastes, the ninja set up a campfire to rest and continue their search for Jay, just as Wyldfyre received an unexpected message from Roby.

Djinjago expanded thanks to Arrakore's efforts, eventually floating high above the Pinnacle of Crystals. As he was busy fulfilling wishes, Rox arrived with the Dragonian Trackers and demanded that Arrakore repair the broken Yellow Prismatic Blade. Arrakore defiantly refused her command, instead tricking Rox into making an accidental wish that teleported the ninja directly to them. This sudden arrival led to an intense battle between the ninja and the Dragonian Trackers. During the chaos, Rox managed to entrap Arrakore inside a timepiece, forcing him to comply and restore the Prismatic Blade. Fortunately, Sora used her elemental powers to rescue Arrakore, forcing Rox and her forces into a full retreat. Grateful for the rescue.

Following the release of Thunderfang, the ninja received news of Egalt's passing. They rushed to the training plateau alongside Zanth, Heatwave, and Jiro. After sharing a brief, final conversation with the ninja, Egalt peacefully passed away, leaving Wyldfyre and the gathered dragons to wail in deep mourning. As the ninja prepared to leave, Riyu invited Rontu to travel with them, but Rontu gently declined, choosing to stay behind where she felt truly at home.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

During their exploration of the Merged Lands, Zane and P.I.X.A.L. visited the Forest of Spirits where they danced together. During their dance, Zane Elemental Power started to malfunction, much to their surprise.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

One of the 16 parallel realms connected to the NINJAGO world, Chima is a world filled with intelligent, talking animals capable of walking on two legs. The various tribes have access to a surprisingly high level of technology and rely on a power source called CHI. It was the site of an epic battle between two alliances over the control of CHI, until other threats forced the warring sides to make peace and work together.[35]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Its name is derived from the term "Wildness," which is used to describe something in a natural and wild state or that lacks discipline or control, which corresponds with the nature of the realm that is composed of vaste biomes of wild nature spared from human societies. The trait is also often alluded to by the characters in some of the episodes.[36][37]
  • Tommy Andreasen has stated that this (specifically the island of Chima) is his favorite realm.[38]
  • It's unknown whether the Cloud Kingdom affects the Wyldness,[39] or whether disgraced warriors from the Wyldness go to the Underworld.[40]
  • The Mother Sun is similar to the Preeminent in that both are female deities that are the living incarnation of the realm. They both have an apparent control over their respective realms and are masters of a large group of beings with unique, distinct powers and neither the Preeminent nor Mother Sun were seen talking. Their existence following The Merge is unknown.
    • When asked if the Source Dragon of Motion and Mother Sun are the same entity just in different cultures, Chris "Doc" Wyatt replied, "No comment."[41]
      • He later said he liked the idea.[42]
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, the First Spinjitzu Master may have brought the Serpentine from Chima. One hint at this is the blue orb on Pythor's chest.[43]
    • The creators of Legends of Chima specifically did not include snakes in the TV series because there were already snake people in Ninjago.[44]
    • According to Tommy Andreasen, in addition to the original inhabitants of Chima, Serpentine can also use CHI.[citation needed]
    • It was later confirmed by Chris "Doc" Wyatt that the Serpentine originated from the Wyldness.[5] It is possible that the Serpentine migrated to Ninjago because of the Forbidden Five's devastation of the Wyldness.[45]
  • The Wyldness exists as a fictional world within Nexo Knights.[46]
  • The realm passageway under the Forever Rock is only known by the Phoenixes and possibly Eris.[47] However, members of the Wolves or Ravens such as Worriz and Razar would likely be the ones to use the passageway if they found it.[48]
  • When Chris "Doc" Wyatt was asked if the whole life energy of the Wyldness came from Mount Cavora, he replied that this is difficult to answer.[49]
  • The Wyldness is similar to the Matoran Universe from Bionicle, as both are realms in an apocalyptic state, with an island paradise above them.
  • The Wyldness is the realm that holds the record for the longest time without a confirmed name. It first appeared in Curseworld, Part II, (and chronologically in the first episode of Legends of Chima) and finally had its name revealed in "Mindless Beasts," almost 9 years after the end of Legends of Chima.
    • Before this, it was widely assumed the realm was Chima.
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt personally believes Fabuland is part of the Wyldness.[50]
  • The writing system used in Legends of Chima can be seen on the Tiger Tribe's memorial stone, suggesting that this script is the language used in the Wyldness beyond the island of Chima.
