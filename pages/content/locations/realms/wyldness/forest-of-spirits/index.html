The Forest of Spirits (also known as the Shadow Forest[1]) is a forest in the Wyldness. Like with the rest of the realm, it was cursed by Argus after the Exile of the Forbidden Five.

History

History[]

The Forest of Spirits used to be a vibrant and flourishing woodland located in the Wyldness. It was there that the Forbidden Five formed the Wolf Clan and established the Shadow Dojo. However, as the Forbidden Five grew more powerful, they fell into corruption and devastated the Wyldness using Shatterspin. Although the Dragon Masters managed to exile the Forbidden Five to the Nether-Space, Argus, determined to perform Shatterspin, struck the Gong of Shattering, unleashing a curse upon the Wyldness. Consequently, the Forest of Spirits was left in a state of cursed limbo as the inhabitants have perished year after year.[2][3]

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Blood Moon[]

Ras and Jordana walk through the Forest of Spirits to their destination the Shadow Dojo, where Ras used the Source Dragon Energy, Jordana had from Imperium and the dragon energy opens the doors, which Ras shows her the dojo's Gong of Shattering and the Wolf Masks. Jordana tries to read a scroll but dosen't recognize the language, which Ras tells her it is Theroxian, which has been banned for centuries when Cinder, the new Master of Smoke and one of Ras' students shows up.

After retrieving the Mallet of Shattering from All-Realm Antiques Emporium, Cinder gives the mallet to Ras, who uses it to activate the Gong of Shattering and empowers their Wolf warriors.

Secrets of the Wyldness[]

Cole, Zane, Bonzle, and Gandalaria flew Jiro to the Wyldness in the Forest of Spirits to get to one of a Magical storagehouse to find a way to protect Bonzle, who can create a portal to the Nether-Space and release the Forbidden Five. Jiro had to stay behind in the forest and the group narrolyw avoided the wolf warriors patrolling the area. Although after finding the protective container, Bonzle was captured by Jordana, who imprisoned Cole, Zane, and Gandalaria in an Imperium electro cage.

The Forest of Spirits[]

After breaking out of the Imperium electro cage, Cole, Zane, and Gandalaria chased after Jorndana to the Cloud Kingdom, which was set to crash.

Meanwhile Lloyd and Nya were traveling by foot after Jordana conjured a storm to knock them off course.

At the Shadow Dojo, the Wolf warriors were in their positions for the Ritual of the Blood Moon to happen. Although Jordana had lost Bonzle to Arin and Riyu, Ras manage to catch up to the two and drive the Explorer back to the Shadow Dojo. Unknown to Ras or the other Wolf warriors, Kai, Sora, and Wyldfyre were disguised as wolf warriors.
