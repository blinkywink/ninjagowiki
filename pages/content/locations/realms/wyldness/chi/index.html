Overview

This article is primarily about the appearance of Chi within Ninjago. For more information on it, visit the article on Legends of Chima Wiki.

External links

Chi is a powerful natural resource that flows through the Sacred Waters of the floating island of Chima in the Wyldness, coming from Mount Cavora's waterfalls itself in a liquid form, and when it arrives in the Lion Temple's Sacred Pool, the minerals in said pool convert it into orbs giving life and energy to much of the kingdom. Many beings across the Realms benefit from using Chi, although they do not call it by the same name.[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The inclusion of the Chi crystal in 71819 Dragon Stone Shrine was a personal choice of Chi Wing Lee, the set's designer, and it neither confirms nor denies the correlation between Lord Ras and Chima.[3]
    • The connection between Ras and Chima was confirmed in "The Blood Moon," where it is explained that Chima is a land in the realm of the Wyldness, Ras' homeland.
  • Tommy Andreasen once wrote a crossover short based on Ninjago and Legends of Chima, but it was never produced.[4] When asked about the short's plot, he added that there was Spinjitzu vs. Chi[5] and also Chi-powered Spinjitzu.[6]
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, the Serpentine could also use Chi to power up themselves.[7]
    • In addition, Pythor has a blue orb attached to his chest which looks like Chi.[8]
  • 71789 Kai and Ras's Car and Bike Battle and 892403 Jay also include a LEGO Chi piece but they don't represent Chi itself.
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt:
    • Chi is used in various ways and under different names across the Merged Realms. He also teased that the source of Chi may be explored in the future.[2]
    • There is a special connection between Source Dragon energy and Golden Chi.[9]
    • When asked about theories like whether Chima was lifted to the sky for protection from the Forbidden Five during their attack on the Wyldness or if the Forbidden Five overused Chi and became corrupted, he indicated that the truth is in one of those two. However, he chose not to clarify which theory he meant.[10]
    • When asked about Ras' affiliation with Chi, he stated that Ras knows about the Chi from Chima, but when asked if he knows about the power it can give him, he stated "It's complicated. Hold on to this question for two years, then ask again".[11]
  • Chi is a "cousin" to Cragling mud,[12] as both maintain a connection to dragon energy.[13]
  • There are variants of Chi in existence, including Fire Chi, and Golden Chi.
