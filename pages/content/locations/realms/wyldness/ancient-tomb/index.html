The ancient tomb was a tomb in the Pinnacle of Crystals that was built by the djinn.

History

History[]

The Last Djinn[]

After failing to recover the Yellow Dragon Core, Nya, Sora, and Riyu entered the ancient tomb to make use of a set of Seeker Stones, hoping that the stones could point the way to the missing core. Upon entering the tomb, the three were immediately fired upon by a wall of spears, giving Sora the impression that visitors were not welcomed.

As they made their way through the tomb, Sora expressed her continuous doubts over being a ninja while Nya did her best to encourage the young girl. Shortly thereafter, Nya accidentally trigged a trap door which sent the three plummeting into a pit of acid. Fortunately, Nya caught sight of an escape ledge and propelled herself towards it with torrents of Water before catching Sora and Riyu and hoisting the pair upward to safety as well.

Upon reaching the alter room, Nya answered Sora's questions about the Djinn while attempting to assemble the seeker stones. Unfortunately, neither Nya or Sora had any idea how to properly use the stones and unwittingly cast a curse upon themselves. Believing that the stones had failed to activate, the trio departed the Ancient Tomb to regroup with Zanth and Jiro. Outside, Zanth sensed the curse just as a pair of Howlers manifested and emerged from the tomb to attack those who had been marked for misuse of Djinn artifacts. The Ninja and their dragons then fought against the Howlers outside of the tomb's entrance but were forced to flee when it became clear that the spirits were too powerful to defeat.
