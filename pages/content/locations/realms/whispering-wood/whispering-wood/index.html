Overview

This article is about the realm. For the episode, see The Whispering Wood.
“You must have felt the call... but you ignored it. The first Green Ninja to ever abandon their duties, to neglect the wood from which mortal life itself originally sprang forth!”
— The Arc Dragon of Life to Lloyd, "The Lean of a Tree"

The Whispering Wood, also known as the Whispering Woods,[1] was one of the realms[2] before the Merge and is now part of the Merged Lands. It is a large forest guarded by the Arc Dragon of Life and is said to be the place where mortal life originated, and serves as a sacred reservoir for Life energy, of which the Green Ninja was named the protector.[3][4]

History

History[]

Background[]

At an unknown point in time, the Whispering Wood came into existence alongside the rest of the other Sixteen Realms, functioning as a reservoir for Life energy guarded by the Arc Dragon of Life and would be where mortal life originated from. At some point the First Spinjitzu Master would visit the realm.[5] When the First Elemental Master of Life received their Elemental Power, they became the first Green Ninja and realm's protector, starting a tradition that would be carried out by subsequent Green Ninja until Lloyd who never received the call of the Whispering Wood due to being aged up to a teenager by Tomorrow's Tea. As such, the realm began to slowly fade and wither with the inhabitants feeling betrayed.

During a clashing of realms known as the Merge, the Whispering Wood was merged with the other realms, its location currently unknown.

Chaos Reigns[]

After Lord Ras hijacked the portal gates to strand the ninja in various locations, Lloyd and Arin ended up in a monastery in the Whispering Wood.
