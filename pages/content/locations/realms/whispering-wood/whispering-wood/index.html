Overview

This article is about the realm. For the episode, see The Whispering Wood.
“You must have felt the call... but you ignored it. The first Green Ninja to ever abandon their duties, to neglect the wood from which mortal life itself originally sprang forth!”
— The Arc Dragon of Life to Lloyd, "The Lean of a Tree"

The Whispering Wood, also known as the Whispering Woods,[1] was one of the realms before the Merge and is now part of the Merged Lands. It is a large forest realm guarded by the Arc Dragon of Life and is said to be the place where mortal life originated, and serves as a sacred reservoir for Life energy, of which the Green Ninja was named the protector.[2]

History

History[]

Background[]

At an unknown point in time, the Whispering Wood came into existence alongside the rest of the other Sixteen Realms, functioning as a reservoir for Life energy guarded by the Arc Dragon of Life and being a place where mortal life originated from. At some point, the First Spinjitzu Master would visit the realm.[3] When the First Elemental Master of Life received their Elemental Power, they became the first Green Ninja and realm's protector, starting a tradition that would be carried out by future Green Ninja until Lloyd, who never received the call of the Whispering Wood due to being aged up to a teenager by Tomorrow's Tea. As such, the realm began to slowly fade and wither, causing its inhabitants to feel betrayed.

During a clashing of realms known as the Merge, the Whispering Wood was merged with the other realms, its location currently unknown.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Chaos Reigns[]

After Ras hijacked the portal gates to strand the ninja in various locations, Lloyd and Arin ended up in a monastery in the Whispering Wood.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

The Whispering Wood[]

In a mysterious forest Lloyd, Arin, and Euphrasia during the searching way to Ninjago face being attacked by Elemental Monsters made of imbalanced elemental energy. The ninja confront them but their disrupted Elemental Powers made the fight complicated. During the battle, the nearby trees restore the disrupted powers, allowing Lloyd and Euphrasia to successfully fend off the monsters. However, the restoration did not last long. After the battle, local inhabitants as known as the Acona lead the trio to the Arc Dragon of Life, who recognizes Lloyd as the son of Garmadon and the Elemental Master of Life and also reveals that the Green Ninja has a sacred duty to protect the Whispering Wood, accusing him of abandoning those duties.

The Lean of a Tree[]

Lloyd was confused about his role as the protector of the Whispering Wood, claiming he had never heard of the wood. The Arc Dragon accused him of dishonesty, highlighting that he was the first Green Ninja to neglect their duties. It revealed that previous Green Ninja had discovered the Wood during their transition from childhood. This realization struck Lloyd as he recalled using Tomorrow's Tea, which had hastened his aging. The dragon, although apologetic, warned that the Wood's fate was already sealed. The Arc Dragon of Life further explained how the Ritual of Unrooting purifies the pathways of Source Energy that connect all living things. Despite Lloyd's determination to perform the ritual, the dragon was skeptical about his ability to do so due to his age and the effects of Tomorrow's Tea on his powers. It also noted that the departure of three Source Dragons from the Council of Sources had disrupted the Merged Lands and Elemental Powers, particularly since one of them was the Source Dragon of Life, which would accelerate the decline of the Whispering Wood. Ultimately, the Arc Dragon of Life concluded that performing the Ritual of Unrooting can restore equilibrium to the Elemental Powers and Source Energy. Determined to undertake the ritual, Lloyd resolved to journey to the Temple of the Green Ninja, with the dragon assuring him he would locate the hidden temple while cautioning him about the dangers in the trial. Accompanied by Arin and Euphrasia, Lloyd set off on his quest to find the temple.

Tonight We Hunt Monsters[]

As they are making their way to the Temple of the Green Ninja, Euphrasia who is using her powers to levitate to get a better view, falls after her powers give out only to be caught by Arin who she thanks for the catch, Lloyd then says that they have been searching for hours for the temple and starts to wonder if he was even meant to find it and says maybe he is too old, but Arin recommends that he should meditate as it may help, Lloyd asks lightheartedly if Arin is him and Arin says it's what he would tell him and so Lloyd takes this advice and goes to meditate while Arin and Euphrasia wait, but just as Lloyd begins his meditation, more Elemental Monsters appear and starts attacking the three of them.

A Wonder of the Merged Lands[]

The trio continue their fight with the Elemental Monsters and are overwhelmed greatly by them, just before the Monsters can finish them off, Zane comes in to save them from the Monsters and joins the fight. Lloyd inquires of Zane at how he found them and he explains that he and P.I.X.A.L. split up to search for them and Zane caught signs of a battle which led him to the three of them, just then, the Wood gives Lloyd another elemental boost, restoring his powers along with Euphrasia and Zane for the first time, and the four of them take on the Monsters together, after defeating them, their powers vanish again to which Zane is bummed about, leading Arin to ask him if he reprogrammed himself as he didn't talk like that before, he says being P.I.X.A.L. helped with that and asks Arin in return when he rejoined the team to which Arin exclaims that he missed a lot. Lloyd explains the issue with their powers and asks Zane if he wants to join them in their journey to the Temple of the Green Ninja, Zane accepts and they all head to the temple together. Sometime later, Zane is telling the others of his time with P.I.X.A.L. and Lloyd stops saying he is lost and needs time to scout things out, Arin and Euphrasia sit together alone and Euphrasia confines in Arin that with her powers all wacky, she feels she can't protect the Cloud Kingdom, Arin comforts her by saying he is very familiar with fighting without powers and even offers to teach her Spinjitzu which she accepts. Lloyd at the same time is trying to focus so he can get a sense of where the temple is but gets frustrated after Zane distracts him and exclaims he doesn't even know what he is doing, Zane helps ease Lloyd's nerves by giving him a notebook and tells him to sketch out the path ahead and think of it as another form of meditation, after sometime, Lloyd is able to draw out a map to the temple and is pleased with the results. And so the four of them follow the map until they stop right in front of the temple and Arin asks if they have arrived and Lloyd says they have.

Seed and Soil[]

The four ninja walk up to the Temple of the Green Ninja and are in awe that they are there, Lloyd is nervous and Arin tells him he doesn't have to do this and that it doesn't have to be his responsibility, but Lloyd responds saying that it does as he is the Green Ninja and that he never reached this True Potential and states that the Ritual of Unrooting may be the only way to fix the current state of Elemental Powers and save the Whispering Wood, he then opens the doorway and walks through the barrier, Arin tries to follow but is stopped, Lloyd realizes that he has to do this alone while the other three wait and so Zane wishes his old friend good luck and says he will see him on the other side as Lloyd enters and begins his trial.

Just Under the Surface[]

Just outside of the Temple of the Green Ninja, Arin is continuing to train Euphrasia but is distracted at he is worried about Lloyd, she encourages him by saying to focus on what he can do at the moment instead of worrying about Lloyd and so he continues to show her how to do Spinjitzu, when he does it to show her exactly how he did it, Zane examines his tornado and notices red streaks in them, he points this out to Arin and asks him why he thinks those are there, Euphrasia asks if that is normal to which Zane says he never encountered it before, Arin thinks that training with Ras likely caused this but Zane calms him down by saying he simply just wanted to study it. Just then, the Acona arrive and urges them to come back but Euphrasia doesn't want to leave Lloyd, Zane assures her that they will come back as Lloyd would want them to help, so they head to the hideout where it's being attacked by Elemental Monsters and so they defend the hideout. The fight goes on for a long while and the Arc Dragon of Life begins to decay and the Whispering Wood deteriorates overtime, they continue fighting on and on and Arin uses his Object Spinjitzu to launch rocks at the Monsters defeating several of them, but this significantly drains him and falls off the drawn bridge but the Arc Dragon of Life catches him and lays him down on the ground to rest, as the conditions of the dragon and forest worsen, Zane exclaims that Lloyd is their only hope in saving the Whispering Wood as if he doesn't pass his trial, the Wood will be lost and so will the rest of the Merged Lands.

The Grand Inquisition[]

Still in the treehouse hideout, Arin and Euphrasia witness an Acona die as the energy in it dissipates and it turns into a seedling, in which another Acona wants Arin to plant it. Nearby, Zane is with the Arc Dragon of Life who tells him that the Wood as never been a worse state than it has been in that moment and says that neither the dragon or the Wood would last much longer and warns that if this happens, the Merged Lands collapse as well. Zane comforts the dragon who is taken aback by the gesture and says that it is ironic that despite being a Nindroid, Zane is willing to be at the dragon's side during its final moments of life, Zane says that while he has struggled to communicate his feelings, he feels them just as strongly as anyone else and urges the dragon to have faith in Lloyd as he has faced many dire situations before and came out stronger. The dragons dismisses this as false hope saying Lloyd is too rooted in his current path to free himself. In that moment however, the seedling sprouts and pops out a new Acona being. Seeing this, Zane points out that the Whispering Wood and the Green Ninja may be more resilient than even the dragon realized.

Dying to Live[]

Lloyd, having just finished his battle with Enemy Lloyd, concluded the Ritual of Unrooting and connected to his fellow ninja across the Merged Lands and their eyes turn green to show Lloyd connecting to them, Arin, Euphrasia and Zane experience this as they continue to fight off the Elemental Monsters, when asked what had happened, Arin realizes it must've been Lloyd as he felt his presence just then, Euphrasia wondered if that meant Lloyd passed his trial or not, Arin says if he didn't then they were doomed.

The Heartbeat of all the Realms[]

Zane, Arin, and Euphrasia fought hard against the Elemental Monsters as the Whispering Wood and its inhabitants continued to deteriorate. Just as they were cornered, Lloyd arrived in his Draconus Form and swiftly defeated the monsters. Seeing that Lloyd acheived his True Potential, Euphrasia was confused that the wood was still dying, especially since the Ritual of Unrooting was supposed to restore everything. Lloyd soon realized that Beatrix's use of the Weapon of Desolation was siphoning the wood's essence, and the only way to save it was to deactivate the weapon.

Birthright[]

During the Loyalist assault, Lloyd knocked the Weapon of Desolation from Beatrix's hands, allowing Arin to retrieve and deactivate it. This restored the energy of the Whispering Wood, reviving the inhabitants. Upon Lloyd's return to the Whispering Wood, the Arc Dragon of Life acknowledged that the Green Ninja's role extended to protecting all of the Merged Lands, which Lloyd vowed to live up to.

Other appearances and mentions

Other appearances and mentions[]

Celebration![]

During their podcast episode celebrating Ninja Appreciation Day, Kreel and Lobbo thanks to Mr. Frohicky, were able to contact Lloyd, who was currently exploring the Whispering Wood with Arin and Euphrasia, he answers their call but his signal is weak due to the interference of the forest and tries to tell the podcast hosts to get into contact with Zane to see if he can help but just then Elemental Monsters show up and the call ends as Lloyd, Arin and Euphrasia begin their fight with the Monsters.

All Wizards Are Liars[]

As guests are arriving from all over the Merged Lands, Percival greets two Acona who had travelled from the Whispering Wood to Ninjago to represent their land as delegates in the Summit of the Merged Lands.
