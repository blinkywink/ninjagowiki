Overview

“This is what teleported you ninja back from Imperium? How does it work?”
“I do not know. It is mystical, not technological, so it is difficult to scan. But if I can reactivate it, it may help explain the secrets of the Merge, including the whereabouts of those still missing.”
— Alfonzo Frohicky and Zane, "Wyldly Inappropriate"

The portal gates are mystical portals that provide entry to the Merged Realms. They are renowned for their existence in a singular structure within each realm. Master Wu has a strong connection to them.

History

History[]

Background[]

After the Merge, which was caused by Wu in some manner, the realms were brought together, resulting in the formation of portal gates. The gates were built immediately following the completion of the Merge and were established in specific locations within each realm.[1]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Before I Will Be the Danger[]

When Nya was in the Cloud Kingdom archives, Wu, in the form of a luminous yellow orb, led her to a portal gate, which sent her to the Monastery of Imperium.

I Will Be the Danger[]

Wu's orb led Kai to the Ninjago portal gate under the Monastery of Spinjitzu. This led to Kai meeting up with Nya inside the Monastery of Imperium.

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

Nya suggested a plan to evacuate the dragons by utilizing the portal gate to return to Ninjago. Zane, however, highlighted that the portal gate was designed for smaller transports, meaning that they could not transport the dragons unless they acquire additional energy to power the gate. Following Lloyd's empowerment by the Source Dragon of Energy, he harnessed his augmented Energy to energize the portal gate, enabling them and the dragons to return to the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

Zane started investigating the gate that's under the monastery being extremely puzzled that he could not understand on how to open the gate.

The Last Djinn[]

Zane continued to think of attempts to activate the portal gate.

Land of Lost Things[]

While out searching for parts with Cole, Nya discovers a part of the portal gate from the Monastery of Imperium.

The Administration (flashback)[]

Zane's attempts to activate the portal gate underneath the monastery gains the attention of Administration Agents Roderick Allen and SsssSsss Underwood, who detain him and take him with them back to The Administration.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Force From the East[]

Geo and the Finders search for parts and inadvertently rebuilt the Imperium portal gate, just as the Wolf Clan showed up for Bonzle, and, along with Cole, who returned, complete it and use the gate to reach the Monastery of Spinjitzu, where they literally bump into Alfonzo Frohicky before discovering two Wolf warriors managed to follow them.

The Spell at the Waterfall[]

Cole and Zane defeat the two Wolf warriors and send them back through the portal gate just as Wu's orb touched the plate and shuts the gate.

The Final Game[]

Arin and Cole continue their exhibition into the monastery. Cole theorize each 16 gates is to the original Sixteenth Realms. Wu confirmed this before helping them find the missing Crowns of Horns hung above, here they also find out from Wu that he caused the Merge before scattering across many gates.

Elements of Betrayal[]

After Nokt was drained of all the elemental powers, he and the Forbidden Five retreated to the portal gate that leads to the Cloud Kingdom. Ras tries to intervene, but Nokt knocks him out and drags him to the portal, wanting to take his revenge. Arin, still desperate to find his parents, goes after them before Nokt destroyed the gate to ensure they could not be followed. This resulted in Arin, Ras, and the Forbidden Five to end up being scattered across the Merged Lands. Cole went to the Cloud Kingdom and the monks told him that neither Arin or the Five came out of their portal.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Great Zane Robbery[]

While Zane and Zanth exploring a part of the First Land, Zane notices another monastery. They pass the gate, and see the Freebooters emerge from a portal gate, who were arguing among themselves of caring a Techno-cocoon to their prison train.

I Alone Can Save Them[]

After getting a message that the Forbidden Five attacked the City of Temples, Kai, Nya, and Wyldfyre used the portal gate in the dragon stables to get to the Monastery of Gates with Alfonzo Frohicky giving them Zane's instructions. While running Nya noticed a new portal gate glowed, which leads to the First Land.

Their Draconic Majesty's Request[]

After meeting with Lloyd, Frak, Sora, and Riyu, Zanth, Zane, and P.I.X.A.L. used the First Land Monastery portal gate to get back to the Monastery of Spinjitzu where Kai and Nya meet with the group to discuss the Prismatic Blades, which all five blades can release Thunderfang from his prison.

Chaos Unchained[]

After Drix took the Blue Prismatic Blade and the Returning Blade, the Ninja recover and wondered how they could beat Drix back to the First Land with Lloyd getting an idea of using the Monastery portal gate to also transport all of their equipment to the First Land Monastery, which Zane and P.I.X.A.L. created a device that generate enough power to the portal gate so it can get all of them and their gear though to the First Land Monastery. The group succeeded and beating Drix to the First Land where he was heading to Thunderfang's prison.

The Shatter Dragon[]

After defeating Thunderfang, the Ninja (except Sora and Arin but the Ninja brought Jay with them) used the portal gate to get back to the dragon stables where Alfonzo Frohicky had a party for their victory.

Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon[]

After Arin told them about the Soul Suckers, the Ninja flew the Temple Bounty to the Spectral Lands where they ran into the Spectral Dragonians. Thanks to Wu's spirit, the group were able to escape to the Spectral Monastery and use it's portal gate to get to the City of Temples.

The Vault of Focus[]

The group gathered in the Monastery of Gates, where each split up; Arin and Lloyd take the Unbidden Storm to the Spectral Lands, Sora and Wyldfyre go back to the Monastery of Spinjitzu to get Zanth to head to the Cave of Bells in Djinjago, and Nya heads to the Cave of Bells in Mysterium. The group were unaware that Ras was hiding behind several pillars.

For Whom the Bell Tolls[]

Sora and Wyldfyre came out of the Monastery of Spinjitzu portal gate where Kai, Frak, and Riyu meet with them. Frak and Riyu joined Sora and Wyldfyre to head to Djinjago while Kai went to help Nya. Ras exited the Monastery of Spinjitzu portal gate, meeting Geo and Alfonzo Frohicky.

Chaos Reigns[]

Ras managed to seize control of the portal gates, repurposing them to serve his own interests. He scattered the ninja across three different places - with Kai, Jay, and Nya ended up inside a Quealiche's stomach from using the Monastery of Mysterium, Lloyd and Arin ended up in the Whispering Wood realm from using the Spectral Monastery, and Frak, Sora, Wyldfyre, and Riyu used the Monastery of Djinjago and ended up in a realm of plains. Afterward, Ras shut down the portal gates unless he needed them for his own purposes.
