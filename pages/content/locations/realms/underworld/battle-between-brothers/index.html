The Battle Between Brothers was the fight between the Sons of the First Spinjitzu Master for the Golden Weapons that resulted in Garmadon's banishment to the Underworld.

History

History[]

Battle Between Brothers[]

Some time after Lloyd was born, Garmadon succumbed to the darkness from the Great Devourer and his Oni heritage and planned to steal the Golden Weapons. But when he tried, Wu confronted him. It was up to him to quench his brother's thirst to destroy. The brothers fought, and Wu won the battle, but at a cost. The kimono he was wearing had a protection spell to keep him safe from evil. This in turn worked on Garmadon, and cast Garmadon into the Underworld, banishing him for what could have been eternity.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • There are several differences between the drawings in "Way of the Ninja" and the flashback in "Battle Between Brothers."
    • In the drawings the battle took place on a rocky thin mountain, although in the flashback it was in the Monastery of Spinjitzu.
    • Wu had a mustache in the drawings and was wearing white clothes, although he had no mustache in the flashback and was wearing his father's black kimono.
    • Garmadon is already black in the drawings.
    • In the drawings, the ground does not open after Garmadon's banishment.
    • In the drawings the brothers are not fighting with the Golden Weapons.
  • It is unknown where Misako and Lloyd were during the battle.
