Overview

“The Forge will enable us to create a Dragon Icon to summon a Source Dragon.”
— Ras, "Casket of Bones"

The Forge of the Forgotten is a forge located in the Underworld. Within the forge is the Casket of Bones, which requires the sacrifice of a living being to power the forge, enabling the creation of Source Dragon Icons without the need for Elemental Powers. However, it is forbidden for someone that is living to be using it.

History

History[]

Casket of Bones[]

When Ras, Arin, and Sora arrived in the Underworld, the run into Kruncha and Nuckal who directs them to the Forge. When they got to the forge, they realized they need a sacrifice, which resulted in a fight where Arin and Sora manage to escape Ras, leaving him under rocks.

Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon[]

Ras activates the Forge after getting a sacrifice. This results in the creation of a Source Dragon Icon in the form of a bone sword. After his master contacts him of his progress, he is confronted by the Skulkin and Greenbone Warriors, The Greenbone Leader accuse him of committing a serious offense by using the Forge of the Forgotten, an act only reserved for the undead. He declares that Ras must face death as punishment for his actions. Undeterred, Ras readies himself for battle against the undead skeletons.
