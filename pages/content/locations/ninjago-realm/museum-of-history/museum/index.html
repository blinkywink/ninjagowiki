Overview

For the in-universe museum, see Ninjago Museum of History.

In 2016, a virtual Museum was released on LEGO.com to promote the upcoming "Day of the Departed" special, featuring characters, vehicles, locations, and weapons from Ninjago up to the sixth season. It was later updated in 2017 for the seventh season and the movie. According to Tommy Kalmar, all information from the Museum is canon.[1]

Pilot Season: Masters of Spinjitzu

Pilot Season: Masters of Spinjitzu[]

Long before time had a name, the NINJAGO world was created by the First Spinjitzu Master by using the Four Golden Weapons of Spinjitzu; weapons so powerful, no one could handle all of their power at once. When he passed away, his two sons swore to protect them, but the oldest, Lord Garmadon, was consumed by darkness and wanted to possess them all. A battle between brothers broke out. Lord Garmadon was defeated and banished to the Underworld. Peace returned to the island as the younger brother, Wu, hid the Golden Weapons in the far corners of NINJAGO Island. Centuries later, Wu seeks out four brave boys – Cole, Jay, Kai and Zane – whom he trains in the art of Spinjitzu to become protectors of the Golden Weapons. Kai is the last one to be recruited by Wu. When Kai meets Wu for the first time, Samukai and his evil Skeleton Army show up to kidnap Kai’s sister, Nya, and steal the map showing the location of the Golden Weapons. Behind the kidnapping is Lord Garmadon, who is controlling the Underworld. The fire ignites within Kai. He is determined to save Nya, so he follows Wu to join the rest of the ninja. Cole, Jay, Kai and Zane use their ninja training in defeating the Skeleton Army, naturally learning the art of Spinjitzu. Although the ninja successfully survive a harrowing quest to retrieve the Golden Weapons, they fall into Lord Garmadon’s master plan, allowing him to escape the Underworld through a portal to the Dark Realm, with hope that he one day would return with the ability to possess all four weapons. Peace returns to NINJAGO Island, while the four ninja swear to protect the Golden Weapons… Awaiting Lord Garmadon’s arrival.[2]

Characters[]

Image Name Description
Master Wu Master Wu, son of the First Spinjitzu Master and little brother to Garmadon, has vowed to uphold his father’s legacy and protect NINJAGO from evil. An expert in the art of Spinjitzu and the ways of the ninja, Wu shares his knowledge with his students and tries hard to impart his wisdom! He is a patient and peaceful man that enjoys a good cup of tea, but don’t let his old age fool you! This Master is still lightning-fast and more powerful than most ninja.

Wu seeks out the son of an old friend and tries to train him as a ninja. Kai, his new recruit, must learn patience…and so does Wu. While he has confidence in all four of his students, it seems they need time to master Spinjitzu. In the end, Wu learns that to protect NINJAGO there are sacrifices that only he must bear.[3]
Kai Kai’s impulsive personality, fierce temper and immense drive mirror the fire within him. His strong sense of justice makes him stubborn and he’ll stop at nothing if he has put his mind to it… but this is also the source of his bravery. As older brother to Nya, he is overprotective of her. While Kai’s self-confidence can be overwhelming to some people, this makes him one of the leaders and big personalities in the group.

Kai is the last young ninja to join Master Wu’s ninja training. His impatience with training is significant, as Lord Garmadon has kidnapped his sister. However, while he quickly becomes a skilful warrior and is the first to tame his dragon, he discovers that impatience is the enemy of a great ninja…[4]
Jay Jay is an inventive, fast-talking and fun ninja. He loves to build and fly hang-gliders, so the chance to learn to fly himself using Airjitzu is an exciting one. Jay is always ready with a joke when things get too tense. He wields the power of lightning.

Jay is captivated by Kai’s sister, Nya, and struggles with his feelings, especially when her overprotective big brother is around. He learns that he can apply his ninja training directly into combat. Inevitably, it is the Ninja of Lightning who is the first to master the art of Spinjitzu."Let's chop-socky this lemonade stand!"[5]
Zane Once the White Ninja, Zane is an intelligent and powerful nindroid (ninja robot) that fills the role of the computer intelligence in the group. He was created by the Tinkerer, Dr. Julien, to protect those who cannot protect themselves. Dedicated to the qualities of peace, freedom and courage, this rational ninja is brave, caring and has a big heart…even though he doesn’t have one!

Before Wu brought the ninja together, Zane lived the life of a loner, thinking himself a flesh and blood orphan. He finds being part of a big group of different personalities tricky, but overcomes it with humour and a little help from his friends.[6]
Cole Wielding the elemental power of earth, Cole possesses immense physical strength that comes in handy in difficult situations. Cole may have quit the family trade of singing and dancing because he never felt that he could make his father proud, but he still has the moves. He may ooze confidence, but the Ninja of Earth is suitably grounded. He loves the simple life – the outdoors, rock-climbing, old-school tech and most importantly: cake.Cole is the calm center of the team. However, there is one thing that he’s afraid of – dragons! He has to overcome his fears to ride his Earth Dragon, learning that they’re not that scary and a great way to enter the Underworld.[7]
Nya After a long time of wishing she could be a true part of the ninja team, Nya created the identity of Samurai X. She is skilled at inventing and building things and always yearns to be a bigger part of the action. When she gets the opportunity to be more than she is, she will have to decide how much of her past she will give up to have a better future.

Nya has been living a quiet life with her brother Kai in the family’s Blacksmith Shop. When Samukai and his Skeleton Army attack, she develops a taste for adventure. Her independence and fearlessness grow by the minute. Nya is ready for action![8]
Garmadon Bitten by the Great Devourer as a child, Wu’s brother slowly filled with evil to become Lord Garmadon, the arch nemesis of good ninja everywhere. When his son Lloyd defeated the Overlord, he was finally freed of the poison that flowed through his veins! Master Garmadon has now taken the oath of peace. He helps mentor the ninja, though his training methods are very different from Wu’s. Garmadon is a man of tradition and doesn’t like modern technology at all.

Evil mastermind Lord Garmadon is trapped in the Underworld, so he orders Samukai to find all four Golden Weapons. But when the four-armed skeleton king recovers them, he tries to take their power for himself, only to be instantly vaporised! This opens a portal to another realm, giving Lord Garmadon a possibility to escape. He knew that their power would corrupt even the most loyal henchman. This was his plan all along![9]
Samukai The four-armed leader of the Skeleton Army, Samukai was the ruler of the Underworld prior to Garmadon’s arrival there. He formed an uneasy alliance with Garmadon to conquer NINJAGO and obtain the four Golden Weapons of Spinjitzu. Samukai led the Skeleton Army to the surface, but he was often frustrated by just how dense many of his troops were. When he attempted to use all four Golden Weapons at once, he was destroyed by the energy discharge.[10]
Kruncha Kruncha is one of Samukai’s generals. He is in a constant rivalry with Nuckal for Samukai’s favor, but since neither one is talented or intelligent, they cannot top each other. Armed with two golden hatchets, Kruncha is famous for his great strength. He and Nuckal were frequently sent on missions together, despite the fact that they argued all the time. The one thing they did have in common was an affection for the surface world, particularly NINJAGO City. Sometimes they went up just to hang out and have fun (though this usually led to trouble).[11]
Nuckal Another of Samukai’s generals, Nuckal specializes in getting into trouble and annoying everyone, particularly Kruncha. He really wanted to be Samukai’s number two skeleton, and might have been if he had been able to count as high as two. Nuckal carries a golden double-bladed axe. He and Kruncha were often dispatched together on missions, despite the fact that they argued constantly about just about everything. Nuckal was enthusiastic about invading NINJAGO because it gave him a chance to spend time on the surface, which is a much more fun place than the Underworld.[12]
Bonezai Bonezai has one of the most important jobs in the Skeleton Army. He is the chief designer in charge of making sure the Skeleton Army vehicles are faster and more powerful than anything else. Unfortunately, since Bonezai doesn’t talk, he is never able to explain his design changes to anyone and sometimes that results in really bad mistakes in construction (weapons that fire backwards, brakes that make the vehicle go faster, etc.)[13]
Frakjaw Frakjaw works closely with Bonezai, although he often wishes he did not. Frakjaw is the official “test dummy” of the Skeleton Army, trying out Bonezai’s new vehicles. The idea is that if Frakjaw only gets blown up a little, the vehicle is safe to use.[14]
Chopov Chopov is one of the key members of the Skeleton Army. It’s his job to keep all the Skeleton Army vehicles in top shape. He is respected for his skills as a mechanic, but like Bonezai, he does not talk. As a result, he can never tell anyone what he has fixed or what other problems he may have spotted. Drivers usually find out somewhere down the road when things go boom.[15]
Krazi Wild and insane, no one ever knows what is going to happen when Krazi appears on the scene. Carrying his golden bone into battle, he is completely unpredictable and even the Ninja were cautious when they had to face him. Krazi has one big advantage over his comrades: he’s the only Skeleton soldier who can talk. So no matter how much Kruncha and Nuckal might want to ignore him, they just can’t.[16]

Locations[]

Image Name Description
Blacksmith Shop This small business, also known as The Four Weapons, originally belonged to Kai and Nya’s parents. It was renowned for the quality of its product, particularly the swords, which were incredibly durable. After Lord Garmadon was banished to the Underworld, Wu hid the Four Golden Weapons of Spinjitzu and created a map to their locations. He entrusted the map to Kai’s father, who hid it in the shop (thus the name The Four Weapons). After his death, Kai and Nya took over running the shop.[21]
Caves of Despair Hiding place of one of the Four Golden Weapons of Spinjitzu, the Scythe of Quakes. This was the first location to which the ninja traveled in search of the Golden Weapons. The caves are part of one of the largest mountains on NINJAGO Island and stretch for kilometers. Samukai ordered a Skeleton Army mining operation here in an effort to find the weapon, which resulted in the laying of railroad tracks, the carving of new tunnels and the construction of Skeleton Army watchtowers. The ninja succeeded in finding the Scythe first, but it was guarded by an Earth Dragon. The creature eventually became Cole’s mount.

[22]

Fire Temple This ancient temple is constructed inside an active volcano near the Forest of Tranquility. It was the hiding place for the Sword of Fire and a Fangblade was buried inside the volcano itself. There was an access point to the Underworld behind a lava fall, but Kai destroyed it. The Fire Temple was home to the mighty Fire Dragon, who guarded the Sword of Fire. Later, when the Serpentine traveled to its inner core to retrieve the Fangblade, the volcano was very unstable. The ninja's elemental powers initiated the explosive eruption.[23]
Floating Ruins Hiding place of one of the Four Golden Weapons of Spinjitzu, the Nunchucks of Lightning. The Floating Ruins are all that is left of an ancient and abandoned city, located above the clouds. A constant lightning storm hovers overhead. The nunchucks themselves were atop a landmark shaped like a huge nunchuck and accessible only via flying device or by climbing a long chain. Just how the city manages to float is unknown, though Zane has speculated that there may be some connection between its electrical charge and its defiance of gravity. The nunchucks were guarded by a dangerous Lightning Dragon, which eventually befriended Jay.[24]
Forest of Tranquility One of the largest and most beautiful woodlands in the NINJAGO world, and site of the Fangpyre Tomb. The Fire Temple is located at the outskirts of this forest. Although wildlife abounds in the forest, including tigers and bears, it tends to be harmless. The forest does indeed exude a peaceful feeling and it can be difficult for a visitor to say goodbye to his surroundings when it is time to go. It was here that the Skeleton Army ambushed the ninja and stole three of the Four Golden Weapons of Spinjitzu.[25]
Frozen Wasteland Hiding place of one of the Four Golden Weapons of Spinjitzu, the Shurikens of Ice. Bordered by an icy sea, the Frozen Wasteland is an inhospitable place inhabited only by the hardiest of creatures. It is partially forested, but much of it is ice-covered mountains, treacherous cliffs, and dangerous crevasses. A fierce Ice Dragon guarded the shurikens within the Ice Temple. The ninja finally succeeded in retrieving the weapon and Zane tamed the Ice Dragon so that he could ride it.[26]
Garmadon's Dark Fortress A castle in the Underworld, which served as Garmadon’s base of operation during the time he was exiled there. This fortress previously belonged to Samukai, but he was deposed from it when Garmadon took over the realm. This gloomy edifice still stands in the Underworld, now home to the Skeleton Army generals.[27]
Monastery of Spinjitzu A small temple in the Mountains of Impossible Height range, which formerly served as the home for the First Spinjitzu Master and his sons, Wu and Garmadon. The battle between the two youths took place here, leading to Garmadon’s banishment to the Underworld. The monastery could be reached only via a winding staircase that scaled the mountain. Carved into the rock below the monastery were pens where the four ninja kept their dragons. The monastery was eventually burnt down by the Hypnobrai.[28]
The Underworld The Underworld is the realm of the dead, populated by fallen warriors who have never fully realized or accepted their passing. It is home to the Skeleton Army, also known as the Skulkin. The Underworld is a dark place, where land masses float in mid-air and are connected by bridges constructed by the Skeletons. Dragons and Skeleton vehicles are able to travel between the NINJAGO world and the Underworld. Once there was a passageway to the Underworld inside the Fire Temple, but it was destroyed by Kai. Lord Garmadon’s Dark Fortress is still located here, symbolizing the fall of the late Samukai who once ruled the Underworld.[29]

Season One: Rise of the Serpentine

Season One: Rise of the Serpentine[]

Since Lord Garmadon’s disappearance from the Underworld, NINJAGO Island has been at peace but the young ninja have grown lazy. While Garmadon lurks in the shadows, waiting for the right moment to strike, the ninja meet his young son, Lloyd, who carelessly unleashes a fearsome new enemy upon NINJAGO Island – the ancient tribes of the Serpentine. The leader of the Serpentine, Pythor, hatches an evil plan to bring back the legendary, all-consuming snake, the Great Devourer. The ninja have to look within their hearts to unlock their full potential. The very existence of NINJAGO Island is at stake! Not only do the ninja have to deal with the Serpentine’s uprising, but they also have to take on babysitting duties when it is discovered that Lloyd would be the prophesized Green Ninja: the powerful ninja destined to defeat the Dark Lord in the final battle between good and evil. Over the course of the season, each ninja makes an important discovery about themselves, allowing them to unlock their elemental powers. To defeat the Great Devourer, the ninja have to rely on help from Lord Garmadon and his unique ability to wield all Four Golden Weapons. In the end, Lord Garmadon disappears with the Four Golden Weapons; the very weapons he needs in order to turn NINJAGO Island into his evil image…[34]

Ninjago City Police Station The NINJAGO City Police Station is the headquarters for the Commissioner of Police. From here, he oversees the safety and security of the city and its people. This is also the site of the NINJAGO Police archive (the password to get into it is PASSWORD).[242]
Repo Yard The repo yard is a site filled with old cars, metal junk and vehicles that have been confiscated by the NINJAGO City Police. It is located on the outskirts of NINJAGO City in the Northern Sea of Sand and protected by an electrified fence and a mean dog. Destiny’s Bounty wound up here after being seized by the police. The repo yard was the first site stolen by Nadakhan to be part of his recreated Djinjago, so nothing exists there now but a big hole.[243]
Teapot of Tyrahn The Teapot of Tyrahn is an ancient relic capable of trapping mortals inside for eternity. Captain Soto imprisoned Nadakhan inside the Teapot, where he remained until freed by Clouse. Nadakhan showed his “gratitude” by trapping Clouse inside, and later captured Master Wu and Misako as well. Opening the Teapot requires aligning the symbols on three rotating rings. Like a combination lock, the three rotating rings make up a pirate symbol. After Nadakhan destroyed the Teapot, Clouse, Wu and Misako went into the Djinn Blade.[244]
Tiger Widow Island Tiger Widow Island is a small tropical landmass in the Endless Sea. It is named for its most infamous inhabitant, the massive Tiger Widow spider. The Tiger Widow makes its nest here, but it is not the only resident. The island is also home to sand worms, which burrow beneath the surface and react aggressively to any movement above. The location of Tiger Widow Island is not listed on any published map or included in any map database. The only map to reveal its whereabouts was a chart prepared by Captain Soto and hidden in a lantern on board Misfortune’s Keep.[245]

Trivia[]

  • Chokun is the only Serpentine from Season 1 not to have a page. The reason for this is unknown.
  • Snike's image is oddly missing his torso printing, even though he has it the full version of the render used.
  • The character gallery for Season 2 originally only featured characters who appeared in the sets from the season,[271] but it was later updated to include Misako, Captain Soto, and Dareth.[272]
  • The vehicle gallery for Season 3 features the NinjaCopter twice.[273] The URL for the first one appears to link to a page for Cole's Earth Mech, but both pages are for the NinjaCopter.[126][274] As such, the Earth Mech does not have a page.
    • The same issue is present in Season 3's location gallery, with the Temple of Fortitude[135] appearing twice[275] at the expense of New Ninjago City.[276]
  • Jay's Lightning off-roader is referred to as the Thunder Raider, even though "Thunder Raider" is only the name of the vehicle when it's combined with Cole's Earth Mech.
  • The descriptions for the four Techno Blades are the same.
    • Originally, the weapon gallery for Season 5 mistakenly labeled all the weapons as Techno Blades.[277] This was later fixed,[278] but the page for Deepstone was left as if it was one of the Techno Blades.[279]
  • Morro's dragon is stated to be the spirit of the Elemental Dragon of Wind, but according to Tommy Andreasen[280] and the Visual Dictionary New Edition, it's a corrupted version of Lloyd's Energy Dragon.
