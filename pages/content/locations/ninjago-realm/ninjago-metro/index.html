Overview

“Uh, we don't have any trains entering on track nine.”
— A worker, "Day of the Great Devourer"

The Ninjago Metro is a set of underground stations and subway tracks in Ninjago. The S.O.G. Headquarters is in one of the underground stations.

History

History[]

Can of Worms[]

Pythor gathered the Serpentine together attempting to unite them.

Day of the Great Devourer[]

A group of people were waiting for their train when the Great Devourer suddenly appeared from the tunnel.

Darkness Shall Rise[]

After tying up Jay, Skalidor, Acidicus, and Fangtom used the metro to rob the bank and fight the ninja as a decoy. During the fight, Cole noticed the train is heading straight for Jay, who was jumping in order to find aid. They find that the train is automatically controlled and pull the break before it can hit Jay. Although the train comes to a complete stop, the Serpentine Generals escaped.

Snake Jaguar[]

Zane, under the disguise of Snake Jaguar, is taken on a train by the Sons of Garmadon to their hideout. The rest of the ninja try to find Zane including Lloyd who looks at a complicated subway map.

Zane along with other members of the S.O.G. would participate in a race which required racing through the metro. When Killow realized Snake Jaguar was one of the ninja, the S.O.G. fight Zane on their motorbikes even after they are out of the metro.

The Darkness Comes[]

A group of people were waiting for their train, but they spotted a dark, murky cloud inside the train. When the doors opened, the tentacles started grabbing people as others ran for their lives.

The Shape of Nya[]

In an effort to mysteries behind the rising occurrences of massive Vengestone shipments across Ninjago, the ninja ventured down into the abandoned N line to search for clues. There, they discovered yet another vengestone shipment and had a confrontation with Miss Demeanor and her henchmen - one that the ninja nearly lost had it not been for the timely intervention of the New Ninja.

The Council of the Crystal King[]

Disguised as the Mechanic, Lloyd followed a Crystal Spider into the Ninjago Metro, where he was then led back to the abandoned N Line to join the Council of the Crystal King. Later, the ninja pursued and briefly fought against the real Mechanic in the Ninjago Metro, but he was able to escape by grappling a passing subway car.

The Spider's Design[]

The ninja made their way towards the N Line in search of Lloyd, for that was the latest location they were able to track him to. As they searched, the ninja came across a derelict subway car which Jay identified as a BRTM-12. As they neared the N-Line, the ninja were attacked by a horde of Crystal Spiders, forcing them to retreat back into the BRTM-12.

The Fall of the Monastery[]

After the joy of surviving died down, the ninja realized that they were still trapped within the BRTM-12, which was now buried under a mountain of rubble. When the ninja realized that any misstep could potentially cause a total collapse, Cole attempted to move the debris with his powers, only to discover that this too posed an immense risk to their safety. With time running out, Zane borrowed Kai's communicator to dispatch a desperate call for help to the Monastery, reaching Wu and the others just as the entire train car began to cave in from the weight of the debris above. After a quick scan, Zane located an escape hatch and the four ninja frantically escaped from the collapsing car into the subway tunnels outside.
