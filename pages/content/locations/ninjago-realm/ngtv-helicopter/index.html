NGTV helicopter is an aerial vehicle owned by NGTV and used by Gayle Gossip.

History

History[]

Skybound[]

Infamous[]

After the Ninja escaped to the roof of Ninjago City Hospital, Dareth rode int he NGTV helicopter to record more footage of the Ninja. After Lloyd expressed urgency for them to make their way to Stiix to stop Clouse, Dareth offered them a lift in the helicopter.

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

My Dinner With Nadakhan[]

Gayle Gossip and her camera crew used the helicopter to report on a mysterious civilization that was developing in Ninjago's skyline. Although the reporter expressed an interest in interviewing the residents of the island, their helicopter was downed shortly after by the hostile residents and one of their aerial vehicles.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

The Way Back[]

Jay's final wish turned time back to the moment where he and the other Ninja attempted to hide from the NGTV helicopter atop the roof of Ninjago City Hospital. This time, however, Nya used Airjitzu to take Jay's hand, embracing him as she revealed she remembered all of the events that had been erased by Jay's wish. Instead of hiding alongside the billboard, she and Jay embraced one another on the rooftop, allowing for Dareth to broadcast their tender moment from the helicopter.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

The Kaiju Protocol[]

Gayle Gossip and Vinny Folson used the helicopter to cover the latest attack on Ninjago City, this time by a "slimy, tentacled monster." As they recklessly approached the monster, it grabbed the helicopter and roared with enough force to send the helicopter spinning out of control. Fortunately, they were rescued by Samurai X who then set the damaged helicopter safely down on an adjacent rooftop.

Seabound[]

The Calm Before the Storm[]

The helicopter was was on standby at the docks at Ninjago Harbor while Gayle Gossip and Vinny reported on an impending stormfront and the evacuation that had been issued as a result of the impending storm. After Kalmaar arrived atop of Wojira, triggering a tsunami, the reporters fled into the helicopter and flew off in an attempt to escape the approaching wave.
