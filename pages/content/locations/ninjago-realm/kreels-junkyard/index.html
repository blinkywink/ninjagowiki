Overview

“It's got everything you need: junk, other kinds of junk, stuff you're not sure is junk, stuff you definitely know is junk, legal junk, illegal junk, friendly junk, mean junk, junky junk. Literally everything! So come to Kreel's!”
— Kreel advertising her junkyard, "Convention!"

Kreel's Junkyard is a repository for discarded items at the Crossroads. It became under the ownership of Kreel after she won it in a lice game.

History

History[]

First Try![]

Skalidor allowed Arin and Sora to enter the junkyard to retrieve parts to build a mech.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

Arin and Sora broke into the junkyard to salvage parts for upgrading the SA Mark 01. While Sora searched for the mechanical part, Arin distracted the dogs by telling them the story of the Merge and his dream of becoming a ninja. Upon obtaining the mechanical component, Sora and Arin were detected by the Security Bots. Recognizing that the story of the Merge wouldn't be effective with the robots as it was with the dogs, Arin diverted the Security Bots' attention by vaulting over them while Sora hacked one of the robots, causing all three to engage in a synchronized dance. As they prepared to leave, Arin gave away one of his Snowberry Pies for the junkyard's owners in exchange for the part.

Crossroads Carnival[]

Lobbo was abducted by the Whack Rats and taken to the junkyard on the orders of Kreel, who sought to use Lobbo's prize money as ransom. Arin, Sora, and Riyu intervened to rescue Lobbo and engaged in a battle with the Whack Rats. Upon discovering Kreel's motives, they proceeded to Dorama's theater to rescue Targle, while Kreel, Lobbo, and the Whack Rats remained at the junkyard.

Return to Imperium[]

Jiro was attempting to evade the Claws of Imperium at the junkyard when confronted by the ninja. Despite their initial success, Rapton intervened by utilizing a Photac to engage the ninja in battle, thereby creating a distraction that allowed the dragon hunters to capture Jiro and escape.

Prior to Wyldly Inappropriate[]

After winning a lice game against the previous owner, Kreel took over the junkyard and rebranded it as "Kreel's Junkyard," establishing it as a salvage company under her name.

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

A disguised Zane and Alfonzo Frohicky head to the junkyard to get a wyconium coil, only to be thrown out by Kreel and her Security Bots when they didn't have money.

The Elemental Mechs[]

Prior to What the Mech?[]

Kreel comes across and attempts to sell many dangerous items, including Chronosteel, by advertising them.

What the Mech?[]

After filming another advertisement, Kreel is approached by Kai, Cole, Arin, and Sora, who try to stop her, which she denies knowing the dangers, until Lobbo mutated from touching one. After Sora created three mechs for herself, Kai, and Cole, they manage to calm Lobbo down for Sora to use the same type of artifact to drain Lobbo. Kreel then attempts to sell them someplace else, only to be stopped by Cole, who confiscates them.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Prior to The Blood Moon[]

While looking for the Mallet of Shattering, Cinder breaks into the junkyard and ransacks it, only to find it was not there and left.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Between You and Lee[]

After the Monastery's training course was wrecked by him and Frak, Riyu told Rontu that they could train at the junkyard. They headed there and trained, wrecking junk in the process. When Kreel came back and saw the damage they had done, she realized that the two dragons would help in the demolishing process and allowed them to continue.
