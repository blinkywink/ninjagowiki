The mountain pass is a road located in one of Ninjago's mountain ranges.

History

History[]

Codename: Arcturus[]

A Nindroid convey led by Pythor and Cryptor moved through the mountain pass to deliver parts for Project Arcturus. Kai attempted to disrupt the convey in his Prototype X-1 and succeeded in destroying one of the trucks but was soon captured by the Nindroid Army.
