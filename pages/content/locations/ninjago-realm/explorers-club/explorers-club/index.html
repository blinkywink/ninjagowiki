Overview

This article is about the club. For the episode, see The Explorers Club. For their headquarters, see Explorer's Clubhouse.
“I shall immediately begin the years-long reactivation process. In the meantime, however, I'm afraid I must deny entry. This club is for members only.”
— Cecil Putnam to Misako, "Riddle of the Sphinx"

The Explorer's Club is a strict members-only club consisting of renowned explorers and archaeologists managed by Cecil Putnam.

History

History[]

Prior to Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Decades ago, a man named Lord Belgrave attempted to join the club by participating in a Trial By Sphinx, but failed.[2] Later on, Misako and Clutch Powers became members of the club.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Cecil Putnam contacted Clutch Powers to notify him that his club membership was to be voided due to his failure in exploring anything for a decade. Determined not to lose his membership, Powers accompanied the ninja to a pyramid in the Desert of Doom. There, they accidentally released Aspheera who immediately sought out revenge against one she called the "Treacherous Deceiver." During the ordeal, Powers was blasted off the pyramid all the way to Ninjago City and was subsequently hospitalized.

Putnam later encountered three of the ninja: Lloyd, Zane, and Nya, when they came to the clubhouse to ask for the second Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu. Seeing as they were not members, Putnam denied them entry and kicked them out. Later that night, Smythe, Underhill, and Percy Shippelton were in the clubhouse when the ninja fell out of an air-conditioning vent, prompting the three to call for Putnam and club security. Although Putnam and the guards gave chase, they wound up causing extensive damage to the club in the process, leaving the other explorers to blame Clutch Powers for the mess.

The Island[]

Shortly after the return of the ninja and Wu from Shintaro, Misako organized a mission to confirm her theory of an uncharted island and navigate the storm belt, and went with her friend Wu, fellow archaeologist Clutch Powers, and his interns to explore the island. However, their expedition was mysteriously lost and the Explorer's Club was forced to cancel the membership of all members of the expedition. Cecil Putnam would later visit the Monastery of Spinjitzu to hand over items emptied from Mistako's club locker to her family, because according to the club's rules, the membership of a member who is lost somewhere must be canceled.[3]

Seabound[]

Following the events on the Island of the Keepers, Clutch Powers brought the real Storm Amulet to the Explorer's Clubhouse and began sharing a grossly exaggerated story about how he singlehandedly retrieved it and saved the ninja's lives. Cecil Putnam was working the front desk that day when to his surprise, Misako returned to the club alongside Wu, Cole, Kai, and Ray. In spite of her survival, Putnam smugly reminded her that restarting the application process would take a significant amount of time. With time running out, Misako invoked the Trial By Sphinx.

Following the ninja's passage of the trial, they confronted Clutch Powers about the Storm Amulet just as Kalmaar and his forces attacked the clubhouse. Clutch ran off screaming for his life, disgracing him in the eyes of the other explorers who actually participated in defending the clubhouse from the Merlopians.

At the end of the Great Flood, several members of the Explorer's Club visited the Monastery of Spinjitzu to pay their respects to Nya for her sacrifice.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Explorer's Club issued reports about sightings of lighting flashing from within the Forest of Spirits. Relying on these reports, Kai, Nya, and Wyldfyre followed them in search for Jay, only to discover instead the Lightning-Tides.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

The Explorer's Club hunted Elemental Monsters in Umbrasilvis. The Loyalists managed to steal an artifact from called the Mind Stone which they used to body swap Zeatrix and Beatrix.

Official description

Official description[]

This members-only club is for Ninjago world’s most renowned explorers and archaeologists. It is managed by Cecil Putnam, who seems to care more about the club itself than about its members.[4]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • They store artifacts in their main building in Ninjago City.
  • They mostly go on their own explorations 'for all the wrong reasons.'[5]
  • The club has multiple rules and protocols, including:[6]
    • Acceptance into the club requires 10,000 hours of exploring. This is equivalent to about 416.67 full days or 1.14 years.
    • Explorers with suspended or revoked memberships are denied entry.
    • When a member ends up missing, their membership is either suspended or revoked pending survival, and their personal belongings are sent to family members.
      • They can be reinstated, which takes several years.
    • Family members can gain access inside the club if they have a family pass.[7]
  • The logo of the club resembles a compass.
  • Each club member has their research stuff stored in the building.
  • Clutch Powers and Misako are the only members to have physical minifigures.
  • In "Riddle of the Sphinx" it is revealed that one way to get into the Explorer's Club without being a member is to correctly answer the riddle in a Trial By Sphinx.
    • Those who do not answer correctly are punished dangerously.
  • The club laughs in the face of danger. This was a question on a test, most likely for a membership.
    • "Ho, ho, ho" is also accepted.
  • The Explorer's Club is very similar to the Geographers Guild from Paddington, both of which are groups of discoverers with very strict rules and unpleasant strict leadership, with security and many artifacts from the paths of their adventurers. In both, the main characters also caused the trigger and they are inspired by British culture.
  • The Explorer's Club is seemingly based on the real life Explorers Club.
  • Misako and Reginald are the only members of the Explorer's Club to be parents to a member of the ninja team; Lloyd and Frak respectively.
