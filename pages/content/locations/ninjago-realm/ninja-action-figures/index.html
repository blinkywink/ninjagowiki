Ninja action figures[4] are toys based on the ninja.

History

History[]

Game of Masks (flashback)[]

Harumi was playing with action figures of the Red and Green Ninja, when the building in which they lived was attacked by the Great Devourer. Harumi was startled and threw the figures on the ground, which then remained there until the collapse of the building.

The New Ninja[]

Following the Ghost invasion, the ninja were catapulted to celebrity status and sales of ninja action figures rose immensely. As part of a "Ninja for a Day" promotion set up by Dareth, fifty crates of action figures based on the ninja were produced and set to be distributed to children at the Ninjago City Hospital. When the ninja arrived to distribute the action figures, Kai discovered to his dismay that none of the kids wanted his figures but instead, almost all wanted a figure of Lloyd.

Later, after Nya learned of several surviving Ghost Warriors that were terrorizing Stiix, she asked a nearby elderly couple to inform the rest of the ninja to hurry on to Stiix and that she would meet them there. As she ran off, the elderly man, Ernest, produced a Kai action figure and expressed an interest in having the ninja sign it.

After defeating the remnant Ghost Warriors, the ninja returned to the Ninjago City Hospital on the following weekend for the opening of the Ninja for a Day event. As the ninja watched the kids participate in the event's activities, Nya lined up a series of six action figures - one representing each of the ninja, including herself - with a hint of a pride.

Infamous[]

After hearing that there was trouble, Kai asked Wu if Kai action figures had run out.

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Later, as the ninja arrived at Stiix, Kai found a Kai Action Figure on the ground. As he admired the toy, Jay noticed that there were dozens of them littered on the ground.

After Nadakhan framed the ninja for various crimes, Rufus McCallister was interviewed on TV and spoke about his disappointment with the ninja, while various citizens rioted outside Ninjago Doomsday Comix and kicked around some Kai action figures.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

Radio Free Ninjago[]

During Lloyd's speech, a little girl and her family listened to him on television while she held a Green Ninja figure.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • These action figures are the closest thing to real LEGO minifigures in the show.
  • It is unknown whether there are other figures besides the Red and Green Ninja.
    • According to the Character Encyclopedia Updated and Expanded, models of each of the ninja are being sold at stores across Ninjago.
      • This is explicitly affirmed in The New Ninja.
  • There are several types of these figures, some of which are based only on ninjas by color, while others are based on a specific ninja, especially Kai.
  • Harumi was seen playing with the figures, one of them being green representing Lloyd, however, by the time of the Great Devourer incident, Lloyd was not yet publicly known as the Green Ninja.
    • However, the color could be just a coincidence, not meant to represent any character.
  • There are also many other items based on the ninja, especially Lloyd, such as clocks or corn flakes, both seen in Nelson's house in "The News Never Sleeps!"
