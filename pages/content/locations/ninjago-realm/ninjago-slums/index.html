Overview

“Just got a tip about an illegal bootlegging operation down at the wharfs.”
— Jimmy, "Questing for Quests"

The slums,[1] also referred to as the wharfs,[2] are a region in Ninjago City, located near the Old Town and Ninjago Harbor.

History

History[]

True Potential[]

Dareth's apartment was around the corner from the slums.

Questing for Quests[]

During the crime drought in Ninjago City, the ninja went to the Police Commissioner to see if there were any crimes. After many small crimes, they finally found one worthy of stopping: an illegal bootlegging operation at the slums. They hurried there only to come to a dead end, so they asked a ramen noodle seller for directions. They got to the slums, but they didn't find the bootlegging operation, only a man selling prune juice.

The News Never Sleeps![]

Antonia and Nelson delivered newspapers throughout the slums during Aspheera's attack on Ninjago City.

One Step Forward, Two Steps Back[]

Zane, P.I.X.A.L., and Wu went to the slums in search of Mrs. Dyer's apartment to ask about the whereabouts of her son.

Game Over[]

The slums were amongst the parts of Ninjago City that became besieged by Unagami's army when Unagami crossed over into the real world.

Papergirl[]

Antonia and Nelson biked through the slums in an effort to prevent Kalmaar from obtaining the Storm Amulet. Unfortunately, Kalmaar managed to capture the amulet and escape.

Assault on Ninjago City[]

The Ninjago slums were flooded by Kalmaar thanks to the powers of Wojira.

The Council of the Crystal King[]

The ninja drove across the slums as they followed Lloyd's positioning on their tracker.
