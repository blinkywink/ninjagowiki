Overview

The rice paddy[1][2] is a farming plantation located near the village in the valley.

History

History[]

The Art of the Silent Fist[]

While trying to get as far as possible from the Nindroid Warriors, Lloyd and Garmadon decided to rest in the rice paddy, only for Nindroid MechDragon spot them, causing a chase between them. While the MechDragon was trying to destroy Proto Sam-X, it got deactivated by the power shortage caused by the ninja in the Storm Farms, saving Lloyd and his father. However, the duo decided to move forward instead of going back, thinking that it's dangerous.

The Mask of Deception[]

At the rice paddy, the Cruel Lord and a few samurai were enslaving some rice farmers when Nya arrived. She freed the rice farmers from captivity by defeating the Lord and samurai.

Other appearances

Other appearances[]

Kingdom Come[]

In an illusion, the rice paddy is shown whilst The Preeminent arrives in Ninjago and darkens the whole area alongside the sky.
