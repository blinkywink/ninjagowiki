Overview

“We're live from Ninjago Harbor, where several of the ninja have just arrived to witness the unveiling of a new ship being presented to them as a gift from the mayor.”
— Gayle Gossip, "The Darkness Comes"

Ninjago Harbor, also referred as and Ninjago City Docks[1] or simply as the docks[2] is a harbor located in Ninjago City.

History

History[]

The Invitation[]

The Harbor was where the Elemental Masters met Clouse. When he didn't want his rival, Garmadon, to get on the ferry, the latter simply kicked off one of the guards just to "get a spot" on the ferry.

On a Wish and a Prayer[]

The ninja acquired Cliff Gordon's yacht from the Ship Captain and began their journey to Tiger Widow Island.

School for Crooks[]

Kai and Skylor go to the docks to track down the leader of the rumored "School for Crooks." After a lengthy chase, they corner the cloaked figure only to learn he is Ronin and the School for Crooks is a criminal reform course.

The Mask of Deception[]

When Lloyd summons the team, Zane and Kai fight the Mechanic in some part of the harbor. Zane freezes him and Kai after a short fight, so they are late for Lloyd's meeting.

The Jade Princess[]

Lloyd and Harumi rushed to the harbor to pick up the Destiny's Bounty and escape from Ultra Violet.

The Darkness Comes[]

Zane, P.I.X.A.L., and Cole rushed to the harbor to see their gift from the mayor. The Police Commissioner then presented their new Destiny's Bounty.

Unsinkable[]

When the ninja wanted to find the energy signal, they went to the harbor to see the vehicle P.I.X.A.L. built, the Hydro Bounty. At night, they went to the harbor again and used the sub.

Master of the Sea[]

When Kalmaar was escaping with the Storm Amulet from Wu, Ray, Misako, Kai and Cole, he went to the harbor where was his chariot. However, Wu attacked him there but after a short fight, they were defeated by the Maaray Guards.

The Calm Before the Storm[]

When the crew of the Mary Louise saw Wojira's storm at the sea, they called the Coast Guard in the harbor who told them there was no storm.

Later, Glutinous appeared in the harbor and told the Coast Guard that he needed to talk to the ninja.

When Gayle Gossip was recording in the harbor, Wojira appeared there and used the Fury of the Storm to damage the harbor.
