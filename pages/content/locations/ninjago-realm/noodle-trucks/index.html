Overview

Not to be confused with the Mechanic's truck.
“Jay, multiple reports of Serpentine attacks. Pythor has been reported at the Teeth raiding a convoy of noodle trucks.”
— Wu to Jay, "Blue Lightning"

The noodle trucks are a fleet of trucks owned by Skylor as part of her restaurant franchise, Chen's Noodle House. The trucks were originally used by her father, Chen, in his criminal enterprises involving the Anacondrai Cultists. The Mechanic drives one of the noodle trucks called the Noodle Truck of Crime.

History

History[]

Tales from the Monastery of Spinjitzu[]

Blue Lightning (flashback)[]

In a daydream, Jay uses his jet to prevent Pythor and the other Serpentine from stealing the vehicles.

Tournament of Elements[]

Ninja Roll[]

Several noodle trucks were boarding Chen's ferry when Nya discovered them and used the disguise feature of the D.B. Express to copy their appearance. She was then forced to take on Dareth as a passenger to avoid detection

Spy for a Spy[]

When Dareth tries to use the cloak feature on the D.B. Express, one of the options is a noodle truck.

The Greatest Fear of All[]

After making their way back to Ninjago, the Anacondrai Cultists took possession of Chen's fleet of noodle trucks to further their plans. The trucks soon appeared in a convoy that appeared to be heading straight for Ninjago City, only to scatter in various directions. The Elemental Masters scattered in pursuit, striving to prevent them from attacking various smaller settlements around Ninjago. However, upon stopping the trucks, the Elemental Masters discovered the horrifying truth: the trucks were empty except for the Anacondrai driving them. As such, the heroes had been drawn away from the people of Ninjago, leaving the cultists free to invade.

Possession[]

Curseworld, Part I[]

Wu and Misako attempted to use a noodle truck to get into Stiix, but they are caught by the Ghost Warriors.

Skybound[]

Enkrypted[]

The Mechanic tells Lloyd and other ninja that he was repairing noodle trucks for Master Chen and his Anacondrai warriors.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

The Absolute Worst (story)[]

After Ultra Violet speaks about Zane, The Mechanic argues Nya is the worst ninja. The Mechanic used his Noodle Truck of Crime in order to rob a jewelry store. When he is finished stealing, he spots Nya on the horizon and uses the weapon on his truck, but Nya avoids them all and stands on top of the hood. She then grabs him from his seat before he fights back and runs away with the cash. However, he wasn't fast enough when Nya used her Elemental Powers to defeat him and steals his noodle truck.

Prime Empire Original Shorts[]

Upgrade[]

The Mechanic robs money from a bank with his newly upgraded noodle truck, but Lloyd, Kai, Cole and Zane get in pursuit. He gets away though, which frustrates Lloyd. The Mechanic arrives at his headquarters, telling a voice on a computer that the noodle truck's upgrades worked and he finished the robbery. After showing his henchmen his truck, the Mechanic gets frustrated that he cannot get his noodle machine to work with just his flamethrower arm. 

Prime Empire[]

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

The Mechanic drives his noodle truck to an old electronics warehouse. The Mechanic's affairs are interrupted by the ninja, who jump towards them from above. They fight, but The Mechanic is able to flee with the motherboard by his truck. The ninja pursue The Mechanic on the streets. Cole remains on The Mechanic's tail, sticking a cable to his truck and tying it to a pole, which ultimately causes the truck to malfunction. As soon as The Mechanic gets out of his noodle truck, the ninja surround him.

Game Over[]

Two of The Mechanic's henchmen try to escape from Ninjago City on a noodle truck during Unagami's attack. The Empire Dragon tried to stop and destroy them, but Jay stopped him.

Crystalized[]

The Fifth Villain[]

After defeating and tying up the Mechanic, the ninja commandeered his Noodle Truck of Crime with the intention of using it to locate the Council of the Crystal King.

The Council of the Crystal King[]

Using the Mechanic's Noodle Truck of Crime, the ninja began to follow Lloyd's tracker, hoping to follow him to the Council of the Crystal King. While Kai drove the truck, Zane provided directions, and Jay and Cole were assigned to guard the Mechanic in the back. Unfortunately, the latter two quickly got bored with their assignment and focused instead on preparing a nice snack from the many boxes of cup noodles. This distraction allowed for the Mechanic to free himself and briefly regain control over his truck to escape from the ninja.

Distress Calls[]

Racer Seven passed by an inoperative noodle truck sitting amongst the infectious crystals while speeding through the city to get to the Ninjago News Center.

An Issue of Trust[]

As the Crystal Warriors were marching toward the newspaper warehouse, an inoperative noodle truck was sitting amongst the infectious crystals.
