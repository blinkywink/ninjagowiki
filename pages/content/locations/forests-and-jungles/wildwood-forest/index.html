Wildwood Forest is one of Ninjago's forests near the Mountains of Impossible Height.

History

History[]

Home[]

When Zane is taking out the trash in the Monastery, he notices the Falcon perched on a tree branch. Upon realizing that the Falcon mimics his movements, Zane decides to follow it into the forest. There, the Falcon leads him to Lloyd's treehouse fortress, and Zane decides to alert the others.

The Ninja devise a plan to destroy the treehouse and rush in, cutting each rope supporting the building except for one. While Cole waits for the Ninja to escape the treehouse before finishing the job, Skales hypnotizes him, which sends Cole to attack his friends. As Cole is about to cut the last rope while the other ninja are still on the falling building, Master Wu uses the Sacred Flute while riding atop Flame with Nya. Cole is snapped out of his hypnotism, and the Ninja escape on Flame before the treehouse collapses.

Snakebit[]

After being exiled from the Hypnobrai Tomb by Hypnobrai General Skales, Lloyd Garmadon decided to release another tribe of Serpentine to combat his former allies. To this end, Lloyd followed the Map of Dens to the depths of Wildwood Forest, where he discovered the Fangpyre Tomb and unleashed the snakes within.

The Snake King[]

Kai engages in a confrontation with a group of Constrictai who were attempting to harm a few girls. However, Samurai X, swiftly arrives and efficiently handles the Constrictai.

Day of the Great Devourer[]

After Pythor released the Great Devourer, the Serpentine fled to the Fangpyre Tomb when it became clear that they could not control the monstrous beast. The last of the snakes had barely finished jumping in and sealing the tomb when the Devourer slithered through the forest, knocking over many trees but ignoring the Fangpyre Tomb.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This forest appears very similar to the Forest of Tranquility. However, the forest from "The Snake King" was confirmed to be the Wildwood Forest in Snake Attack!, the novelization of the episode, where it's stated, "Kai came to the rescue of three young maidens in the Wildwood Forest."
  • In Scaredy Kai, it is mentioned that the ninja have already been in that forest. However, it marks the first chronological appearance of the forest, as the comic takes place before Season 1.
