Overview

“But this is the Mountain of a Million Steps. Aren't we pressed for time?”
— Zane, Can of Worms

The Mountain of a Million Steps is a large mountain with a long staircase winding around it, with a cavern near the top where the Constrictai were sealed away. The Serpentine's underground fortress is also located inside the mountain, beneath the cavern.

History

History[]

Strike of the Serpentine (flashback)[]

Levi used the Sacred Flute to escort the Fangpyre tribe to their tomb, which was in the Mountain of a Million Steps.

Rise of the Snakes[]

Can of Worms[]

Cole and Zane climbed up the Mountain of a Million Steps to find the Constrictai Tomb. The Postman is seen carrying mail up the steps.

Tick Tock[]

Pythor and Skales gathered the Serpentine in the Constrictai Tomb during the former's search for the map that would reveal the location of the Fangblades.

All of Nothing[]

When Nya discovered that the Serpentine had been moving underground to avoid detection by the ninja, she pinpointed their main base inside the Constrictai Tomb. With Pythor already on the trail of the fourth and final Fangblade, the ninja, Nya, and Sensei Wu go to the Mountain of a Million Steps to retrieve the three Fangblades already collected by the Serpentine.

After the ninja were captured by Pythor, Lloyd donned the Green Ninja suit for the first time and climbed the Mountain of a Million Steps to aid his friends. Shortly after his son's entrance into the fortress, Lord Garmadon sent the Skulkin Army up the mountain to reinforce the ninja.

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Skybound[]

Misfortune Rising[]

When Misfortune's Keep first flies, Flintlocke nearly crashes into Mountain of a Million Steps, temporarily causing the ship to become stuck.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.
