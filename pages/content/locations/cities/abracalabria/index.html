Overview

“Hello! Welcome, strangers, to Abracalabria. Also, strangers are no longer welcome at Abracalabria.”
— The Wizard Guard to Arin, Sora, and Ras, "The Hollow Ones"

Abracalabria is a village in Mysterium. It features a large marketplace in the center.

History

History[]

The Hollow Ones[]

Gandalaria wandered across Abracalabria to run multiple errands, remarking all the while that she had not been to the village for decades, if not centuries. Unfortunately, her visit would be marked by the start of the Spectral invasions, with the small village becoming the first of many targets.

The next day, Ras, Arin, and Sora arrived at the village gates in search of the Pontifex Magicus for knowledge on passage to the Underworld. When their request was denied, Ras viciously fought the guards to enter the village by force, only to discover to his shock the state of its citizens. When the trio questioned the survivors for answers, a dense fog rolled into the village accompanied by ominous music, signaling the return of the invaders.

Human Resources[]

As the Spectral invasion started, the inhabitants of Abracalabria started hiding. Arin realized that the flute player was Kur. Then, invisible creatures started to hollow to inhabitants of the village, much to Arin, Sora and Ras' shock. Using the Returning Blade, Arin noticed that the invisible creatures were actually Spectral Dragonians and souls of the hollowed goes to Kur's magical flute. Arin tried to fight towards them, only for one of the Spectral Dragonians to break his Returning Blade.

As the invasion continued, more people was hollowed, much to Arin and Sora's efforts. Then, Sora decided to use the broken piece of the Returning Blade and lamp to make a Prismatic Lamp with Gandalaria's help, which helped them to see the Spectral Dragonians without the blade. After finding out Gandalaria knows about where the Underworld is, Ras demanded Arin and Sora to protect her. Unfortunately, despite their efforts, one of the Spectral Dragonians hollowed Gandalaria. Then, one of the Spectral Dragonians tried to attack Arin, Ras and Sora, only for sun to rise and cause them to go back the Spectral Lands. Thus, ending the invasion. Sora and Arin decided to return Gandalaria to the Monastery of Mysterium, in hopes that it will help her to return to normal, which gave hope to Ras to find the Underworld.

Chaos Reigns[]

Following Thunderfang's defeat at the hands of the Arc Dragon of Focus, the effects of the spectral invasions on Abracalabria are undone, unhollowing its environment and inhabitants.

Etymology

Etymology[]

  • The name is a portmanteau of "Abracadabra", a word typically used by magicians, and "Calabria", the southernmost region of mainland Italy.
