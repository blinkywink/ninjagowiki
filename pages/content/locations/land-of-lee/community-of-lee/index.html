The Community of Lee is an large underground cave underneath the Land of Lee, containing an artificial neighborhood built and led by Lee where all who enter are brainwashed to believe Lee and his society are perfect.

History

History[]

At an unknown point in time after the Merge, Lee created this artificial neighborhood due to his lack of friends. After the Administration started to mistakingly relocate people to the Land of Lee, its sole inhabitant, Lee, would welcome them and let them live in an artificial neighborhood created by him. To ensure they would never leave, Lee also started to lie to them, telling them the Merge destroyed all the other realms except his, while also saying the air was now toxic, forcing them to live underground. To ensure no one would search for the newcomers, Lee would put on fake tombstones with their names to mislead their loved ones into believe they were dead. Those that would try to uncover the truth, Lee would brainwash them into believing there was nothing wrong. The Administration checked for large scale brainwashing technology and magic, but Lee only used it at the small scale, so they didn't detect any. Barry and Jenny were among the displaced people to be relocated to the Land of Lee by the Administration.
