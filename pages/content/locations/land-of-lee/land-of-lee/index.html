Overview

“You can get to the location by air or boat, but the only way to get into the real Land of Lee is through the realm reassignment area.”
— Jay, "Human Resources"

The Land of Lee, formerly known as the Realm of Lee, was one of the realms before the Merge and is now part of the Merged Lands. Initially barren and empty, it was home only to Lee, the sole sentient being. After the Merge, Lee constructed an artificial neighborhood in an effort to create a utopia.

History

History[]

Background[]

At an unknown point in time, the Realm of Lee came into existence as one of many realms parallel to Ninjago. At some point the First Spinjitzu Master would visit the realm.[2]

Between Seasons[]

During the Merge, the Realm of Lee was integrated with the other realms. As a consequence of this integration, the Realm of Lee ceased to be recognized as a separate realm and was subsequently renamed the Land of Lee.

After the Administration started to mistakingly relocate people to the Land of Lee, its sole inhabitant, Lee, would welcome them and let them live in an artificial neighborhood created by him. To ensure they would never leave, Lee started to lie to them, telling them the Merge destroyed all the other realms except his, while also saying the air was now toxic, forcing them to live underground. To ensure no one would search for the newcomers, Lee would put on fake tombstones with their names to mislead their loved ones into believe they were dead. Those that would try to uncover the truth, Lee would use brainwash them into believing there was nothing wrong. Not even the Administration managed to find anything wrong.

At some point, Barry and Jenny would be relocated to the Land of Lee.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

I Alone Can Save Them[]

Ras took Arin to the Land of Lee, where he found his parents' graves, which Lee created.

Fallen Wishes[]

Arin continued to train along the shores of the Land of Lee until he told Ras to head to the World Forest.
