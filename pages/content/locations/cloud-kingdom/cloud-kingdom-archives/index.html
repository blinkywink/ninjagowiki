Overview

“You write down everything that's happened everywhere, right?”
“Everything that has happened and everything that will happen.”
— Nya and Suetonius, "Writers of Destiny"

The Cloud Kingdom archives are a library in the Cloud Kingdom, containing the information of everyone and everything. The archives contains the Cloud Kingdom's portal gate.[1][2]

History[]

Background[]

At some point before Riyu's capture, Ras secretly broke into the archives to collect information and introduced a curse to the Scroll-Worms.[3][4]
