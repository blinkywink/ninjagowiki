Overview

“Dyer is a recluse. No one has seen him for almost 30 years. However, it is a private island and there is only one house. His.”
— Zane, "Dyer Island"

Dyer Island is an island that is inhabited by Bob the Intern and formerly Milton Dyer. It is full of boobytraps and has statue protectors that shoot lasers when they detect activity near and around the Dyer Mansion.

History

History[]

The Prodigal Father (flashback)[]

Milton Dyer tells Unagami of how the game was going into beta testing, and introduced Scott. Dyer then asks Unagami to give Scott an intense experience before leaving, however, Unagami misunderstands this and Scott is absorbed into the game. After Dyer discovers this, he grows afraid of what he had created, and the company he worked for decided to shut the game down.

Dyer Island[]

The ninja head to Dyer Island to find Milton Dyer. However, they encounter many traps before Bob the Intern turns them off. He answers all of the ninja's questions before they assume that Milton Dyer is Unagami.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the fifth island besides Ninjago to be revealed in the TV series, preceded by the Dark Island, Lighthouse Island, Chen's Island, Tiger Widow Island, and followed by the Island of the Keepers.
  • The name of the island is a play on "dire", meaning either serious or poor quality.
  • IN real life there is a Dyer Island it’s located in South Africa although it’s unlikely it’s like the one here in the show
