Overview

“They be the rarest of creatures, indigenous to only one island that's found on no map, but me own.”
— Soto to the ninja, "Enkrypted"

Tiger Widow Island is a largely unknown island somewhere in the Endless Sea, its location hidden except for a map located in a lantern on Nadakhan's ship, Misfortune's Keep. The island is home to the enormous, highly dangerous Tiger Widows, a species of spider with a venom so powerful that one drop of it can kill a man but can tire a Djinn long enough to trap it in the Teapot of Tyrahn.

The island seems to be abandoned, though at one point it might've been inhabited. This is indicated by the temple atop the entrance to the lair of the Tiger Widow the ninja encountered and the presence of several human skeletons stuck in the webs.

History

History[]

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

On a Wish and a Prayer[]

The ninja explore this island to get Tiger Widow's venom. While Jay explores the Tiger Widow's cave, he's attacked by the titular beast of the island and manages to retrieve its venom. After an attack by the Sky Pirates, the ninja send the Tiger Widow plummeting into a chasm by destroying a bridge.

My Dinner With Nadakhan[]

While building a raft to leave the island, Cole, Nya, and Lloyd are attacked by two sand eels before ultimately escaping the island.

The Last Resort[]

After falling into a portal made from the Traveler's Tea, Dogshank fell into the cave of the Tiger Widow and was confronted by the spider, only to easily scare it off.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

Trivia[]

  • This is the fourth island besides Ninjago to be revealed in the TV series, preceded by the Dark Island, Lighthouse Island, and Chen's Island and succeeded by Dyer Island and the Island of the Keepers.
  • The temple on the island is less prominent in the show than it is in the set, as it has no role in the show.
  • The instructions for 5004391 Sky Pirates Battle are printed on the back of a map of Tiger Widow Island.
