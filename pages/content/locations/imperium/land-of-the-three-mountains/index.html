The Land of the Three Mountains (also known as the Land of Three Peaks[2]) is a mountain range in Imperium.[1] It is the homeland of the Mountain Dragon tribe.

History

History[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

Like all the locations in the realms, the Land of the Three Mountains was in one of the realms that were involved with the Merge. It was merged far away from the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

The Merge: Part 2[]

Because the Claws of Imperium planned to capture all the Mountain Dragons, the ninja had to find out where Riyu came from before the Claws do. As they discovered that Riyu lived in the Land of the Three Mountains, the ninja used the Destiny's Bounty to head there. But the Claws of Imperium followed the ninja as they began to hunt down the herd until they were defeated by Lloyd when the Dragon Matriarch empowered him. Because they were discovered, the Matriarch and the rest of the herd had to relocate, while Riyu decided to join the ninja in Ninjago.
