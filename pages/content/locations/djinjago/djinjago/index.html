Overview

“I thought Djinjago was destroyed.”
“It was. However, I was on a long quest in another realm when it happened. Upon my return, there was nothing left of my homeland, except this grain of sand. It is all that remains of the once noble Djinjago.”
— Nya and Arrakore, "The Last Djinn"

Djinjago, also currently known as New Djinjago[1] and formerly as the Realm of Djinjago,[2] was one of the realms before the Merge and is now a part of the new Merged Lands. It was the realm of the djinns and the Djinjagan Dragons, and the sister realm of the Cursed Realm.

When the ninja destroyed The Preeminent to save Ninjago, Djinjago became destabilized in turn, causing it to slowly collapse. Khanjikhan passed down the Sword of Souls to his son, encouraging him to restore the realm. While Nadakhan did so, after being struck with the venom of a Tiger Widow, he lost control of the new realm and it fell apart. When Jay made his final wish, Nadakhan was sent back into the Teapot of Tyrahn, while Djinjago would collapse entirely with no chance of being reborn.

Before the Merge, Arrakore went back to his homeworld only to find nothing left except small grain of sand, the only thing left of the realm of Djinjago. He kept it safe in his necklace.

After an encounter with Nya, Sora and Riyu, Arrakore realized that due to the profound link between Djinjago and wish magic, he possessed the ability to restore Djinjago by tapping into his wish magic once more. Thanks to the relentless thrive from Arrakore in granting wishes, Djinjago had expanded to the point of becoming a small island floating on the Pinnacle of Crystals at the time of Thunderfang's release, until it increased so massively that it left its previous location to rest above the ocean. Djinjago's portal gate appeared there as well as Zanth was affected when she approached her native land, making her bigger, stronger and more majestic.

History

History[]

Prior to the series[]

Djinjago came into an existence at an unknown point in time along with the other realms, with the Cursed Realm as its sister realm. At some point the First Spinjitzu Master would visit the realm.[3] Millennia ago, one half of the Arc Dragon of Focus was put into slumber in one of the two Caves of Bells following Thunderfang's defeat.[4] At some point, Nadakhan left the realm to rule Ninjago as a pirate. After the defeat of the Preeminent and the destruction of its sister realm, Djinjago started collapsing.[5]

Prior to Skybound[]

Arrakore had left Djinjago to go on a long quest in another realm. Zanth had also travelled to the realm of Imperium, but was unfortunately captured by the Claws of Imperium and imprisoned in the Advanced Systems Lab.[2]

Skybound[]

Enkrypted[]

Following his release from imprisonment in the Teapot of Tyrahn, Nadakhan reassembled his old crew (save for the deceased Delara) and took them to Djinjago with the power of the Realm Crystal.

When the pirates arrived, the once-beautiful realm was collapsing before their very eyes. Khanjikhan, Nadakhan's father and the king of the realm, told him that the ninja had destroyed Djinjago's sister realm, the Cursed Realm, disturbing The Balance between realities and causing Djinjago to collapse. Passing the Djinn Blade down to his son, Khanjikhan implored him to avenge Djinjago's fall before ushering the pirates out of his realm before it collapsed completely.

After Enkrypted[]

Arrakore returned to Djinjago from his long quest, only to find the realm completely destroyed, except for one grain of sand. He took the sand and moved to the Pinnacle of Crystals.[2]

Misfortune Rising through The Way Back[]

Main article: Djinjago (reconstruction)

Following his narrow escape from Djinjago, Nadakhan swore vengeance on the ninja for their unwitting role in destroying his home realm. Using the Djinn Blade to absorb powerful souls (including several of the ninja and other Elemental Masters[6]), he tore out pieces of Ninjago's landmass and raised them into the sky, building a new incarnation of Djinjago above the city.

After Djinjago was reconstructed, Nadakhan married Nya and became king, gaining infinite wishes. Later, the djinn's power was drastically weakened when he was shot with Tiger Widow venom by Flintlocke, causing Djinjago to start falling on top of Ninjago. After Jay made his final wish, the recent events of were erased thus, Djinjago would remain destroyed.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Last Djinn[]

Some time after the Merge, Nya, Sora and their dragons found Arrakore in his cave. After some hesitation, he granted them their wish to abolish Howlers. Through the magic, the grain of sand grew and with it the hope and magic of Arrakore, which gives him the motivation to rebuild his realm.

They Call It Doom[]

Before Nya, Sora and the others set out on their journey, Arrakore granted Nya a wish which created a magical compass that would show them the way to any object. Through this act of magic, the grain of sand grew more.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Fallen Wishes[]

Arrakore continued to restore Djinjago, making it bigger than it was before. Although he was interrupted when Rox and the Dragonian Warriors arrived to force him to fix the yellow Prismatic Blade. Arrakore refused to comply due to knowing the dangers of the Prismatic Blade. Rox threatened to use a timepiece to make him her servant but he tricked Rox into wishing the Ninja were here. However, he only summoned Nya, Sora, and Riyu. Sora tried to wish for Rox and her allies to disappear, but Arrakore was too weak to grant a big wish and was forced to grant small wishes because despite Djinjago was near near its former glory. Sora, Nya, and Riyu wishes also harm the Dragonian warriors, make them funny, and Arrakore granting more wishes to use them in edge in battle. Rox managed to imprison him in the timepiece, forcing him to repair the Prismatic Blade and freeze the ninja. Due to the amount of wishes granted, Djinjago was able to regenerate further. Sora realized Arrakore's prison is tech so she used her Technology to break the timepiece, allowing him to escape. Unfortunately, Rox and the others still retreated with the blade.

For Whom the Bell Tolls[]

As Thunderfang strengthens his power in the Spectral Lands and continues his Spectral invasions, Frak, Sora, Wyldfyre, Zanth and Riyu came to Djinjago to awaken one half of the Arc Dragon of Focus within its Cave of Bells to defeat the Dragon of Chaos. While coming closer to Djinjago, Zanth gets evolved into another form, due to Arrakore's regrowth of Djinjago.

Later, Rapton and the Freebooters flew to Djinjago to find the thing Rapton wants.

When Doves Cry[]

Frak, Sora, Wyldfyre, Zanth, and Riyu told Arrakore they needed to awake the tan half of the Arc Dragon of Focus to fight Thunderfang. Although Arrakore told them the dragon isn't in the Cave of Bells, due to it's previous destruction and his wish magic isn't strong enough to bring back living beings like other Djinns or the tan half of the Arc Dragon. Seeing Zanth's new evolved form, Arrakore thinks he can bring back the tan half if they both work together.

After leaving Frak, Sora, Wyldfyre, and Riyu were then confronted by Rapton and the Freebooters who attmepted to steal Arrakore's treasure or in Ratpon's case a rare Djinjagan Unicorn figurine to complete his collection.

After the Freebooters left, Arrakore and Zanth came out where they fell asleep, as Frak, Sora, Wyldfyre, and Riyu went into the Cave of Bells to wake the tan half but Kur flew with the Spectral Dragonians.

Chaos Reigns[]

Zanth and Arrakore continue to sleep outside the Cave of Bells, while Frak, Sora, Wyldfyre, and Riyu fought Kur and the Spectral Dragonians.

After the Arc Dragon of Focus defeated Thunderfang, Frak, Sora, Wyldfyre, and Riyu decided to use the Monastery of Djinjago's portal gate to get back to Ninjago but they ended up in the Land of Eternal Dawn.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Due to its destruction, Nadakhan wished to create a "new" Djinjago by tearing apart Ninjago and causing pieces of land to be raised into the air.
  • If a Djinn Prince marries a person on Djinn land, he will gain the power to grant himself unlimited wishes.
  • Djinjago's destruction is the first event from the second half of "Infamous" through to the end of "The Way Back" that still occurred after Jay's final wish due to its destruction not being directly connected to the events of Skybound. The second was Cliff Gordon's death and the third is the Temple of Airjitzu still floating over the sky (though this was a result of Skybound).
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, there were no Elemental Masters originating from Djinjago.[7]
  • The First Spinjitzu Master did not create Djinjago.[8]
  • Djinjago had laws governing wish magic that prevented it from being abused. If a Djinn breaks a law, their wish magic will be restricted.[9]
    • When Djinjago was destroyed, all its laws to restrict and prevent wish magic from being abused were abolished, creating a loophole that allows Arrakore to use his wish without restrictions imposed by djinn laws, even though he cannot grant himself any wishes (this restriction can only be overcome by a djinn prince when he becomes king by marrying in Djinjago) and can't fulfill wishes that exceed power of his wish magic, whose power depends on Djinjago's condition.[9]
