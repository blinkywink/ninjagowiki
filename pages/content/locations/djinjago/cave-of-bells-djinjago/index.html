Overview

This article is about the cave in Djinjago. For the cave in Mysterium, see Cave of Bells (Mysterium).

The Cave of Bells[1][2] (otherwise known as the Cave of Tones) is a cave located in Djinjago. The tan half of the Arc Dragon of Focus rests in this cave while the purple half rests in the other Cave of Bells of Mysterium.

History

History[]

Background[]

At some point, the Cave of Bells in Djinjago became the resting place for the tan half of the Arc Dragon of Focus, where it would lay dormant until activated alongside its purple half in the Cave of Bells in Mysterium, either by the Focus Source Dragon or a mortal capable of passing the Trials of Focus. Unfortunately, the cave was destroyed when Djinjago collapsed, along with the tan half of the Arc Dragon, which perished along with it.

Chaos Reigns[]

Kur attacked the cave just as Frak, Sora, and Riyu managed to activate the tan half of the dragon to confront Thunderfang. At the end, Kur and the Spectral Dragonians were defeated by Riyu's first Spinjitzu tornado, after the jackal ghost attempted to destroy the Focus crystals to sabotage the mission.

Following their victory, the ninja team left the cave, just minutes before the Elemental Powers began to unravel.
