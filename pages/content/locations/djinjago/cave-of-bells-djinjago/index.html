Overview

This article is about the cave in Djinjago. For the cave in Mysterium, see Cave of Bells (Mysterium).

The Cave of Bells[1][2] (otherwise known as the Cave of Tones) is a cave located in Djinjago. The half of the Arc Dragon of Focus rests in this cave while the other half rests in the other Cave of Bells of Mysterium.

History

History[]

Background[]

At some point, the Cave of Bells in Djinjago was where the tan half of the Arc Dragon of Focus lay dormant, awaiting the moment it would be needed, whether through the Trials of Focus or by the direct activation of its Source.

Chaos Reigns[]

Kur attacked the cave just as Frak, Sora, and Riyu managed to activate that half of the dragon to confront Thunderfang. At the end, Kur and the Spectral Dragonians were defeated by Riyu's first Spinjitzu tornado, after Kur attempted to destroy the Focus crystals to sabotage the mission.

Following their victory, the ninja team left the cave, just minutes before the Elemental Powers began to unravel.
