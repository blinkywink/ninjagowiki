The Land of Eternal Dawn[1] (formerly known as the Realm of Eternal Dawn) is a mysterious region[note 1] within the Merged Lands. As the name suggests, it is a large plain-like land in constant sunrise.

History

History[]

Prior to the series[]

At an unknown point in time, the Land of Eternal Dawn came into existence alongside the rest of the other Sixteen Realms. At some point, the First Spinjitzu Master would visit the realm and hide the Tablet of Desolation in it to prevent the Weapon of Desolation from falling into the wrong hands.[2] Later on, it would get connected to a fountain in the realm of Umbrasilvis via the pit.

After the Liberation of Imperium[]

Dr. LaRow journeyed to the Land of Eternal Dawn, where she discovered the existence of the Weapon of Desolation after finding the Tablet of Desolation.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Rising Ninja[]

Ras' master teleported the Shadow Dojo to the Land of Eternal Dawn for the convenience and safety of the Wolf Clan following the Ritual of the Blood Moon.

The Shape of Motion[]

The Wolf Clan was training in the Shadow Dojo, which was now in the Land of Eternal Dawn. They watched Cinder and Nokt train, only for the Elemental Master of Brute Force to win. Ras told Nokt to save his strength for the upcoming Tournament of the Sources.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Chaos Reigns[]

Wyldfyre, Frak, Sora, and Riyu were sent to this realm due to Ras's tampering on the orders by his master, and the portal gates were compromised, leaving them as the only ones who could use them.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

The Whispering Wood[]

Wyldfyre, Frak, Sora, and Riyu realized they were trapped and explored the land. They found a stream to drink, but were confused that it was an endless prairie for as far as they could see.

The Lean of a Tree[]

Sora encountered cats whom she played with and Frak saw pies growing on plants, which he tried to eat. Wyldfyre also started to succumb to the Land’s illusory powers but eventually figured out that these were what she and the others wanted to see as the realm created manifestations of whatever any of the group wanted. She realized the team was falling for a trap and, with the help of Riyu, dragged the other two away, the cat and pie illusions disappearing after the group left. Eventually, they found the pit, and after some fighting, they all went through. Thankfully, upon traversing this passageway, they entered the village of Umbrasilvis in the realm of the same name.

Prior to Birthright[]

Ras' master transported the Monastery of the Gates to the Land of Eternal Dawn right in front of the Shadow Dojo.

Birthright[]

Ras went through a portal gate into his Shadow Dojo in this realm upon obtaining another Source Dragon Icon. He communicated with his master using the Shadow Dojo and began the next phase of his plan.

Dangerous Connections[]

Ras used a portal gate and reemerged into the Land of Eternal Dawn after recreating the Icon of the Source Dragon of Energy.

Other appearances and mentions

Other appearances and mentions[]

Dying to Live[]

Dr. LaRow spoke of the Land of Eternal Dawn to Beatrix, revealing that she had traveled there while discovering the tablet, and suggested that she might be the only human to set foot there since the Weapon of Desolation was disposed of.

The Heartbeat of all the Realms[]

When Nya asked Sora where she came from, Sora mentioned that they ended up in a place with unexpected kittens while also mentioning their time in Umbrasilvis.

Birthright[]

Dr. LaRow mentioned to the ninja that she learned about the Weapon of Desolation in the Land of Eternal Dawn, merely describing it as a place that she could never find and how it apparently lacked a name.

100 Eyes to See Your Soul[]

As Wyldfyre watched the delegates from the realms arrive for the Summit of the Merged Lands, she was curious about who would represent the Land of Eternal Dawn. However, she began to doubt whether there were any natives from that land present. Percival was confused about the Land of Eternal Dawn. Sora noted that it was perpetually dawn there, and she wasn't even sure how long she and the others had been in that place. Percival, who prided himself on knowing every land, admitted that such a realm didn't sound familiar to him at all. This left Sora pondering the land's alleged existence as a realm.

The Summit[]

As all delegates except Lloyd gathered, Sora and Wyldfyre were surprised to see no representative from the Land of Eternal Dawn, to which Percival suggested this might be because the land belongs to another realm.

Effect

Effect[]

  • Hallucinokinesis: The Land of Eternal Dawn generates metaphysical illusions in those who visit it, corresponding to their deepest desires. These illusions appear as real as reality itself and seem to mold themselves according to what each person wishes to see.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Once leaving, it seems that any manifestations affecting someone also leave as well, as seen with Frak and Sora.
  • In "Chaos Reigns" and Season 4, the sky appears to be bluer than it did in "Rising Ninja." It is unknown if this is a design change or something deeper.
  • The Land of Eternal Dawn shares similarities with the Never Kingdoms as being one of the remote realms. While the Never Kingdoms were called the Never-Realm for being nearly impossible to escape, the Land of Eternal Dawn, as noted by Dr. LaRow, is characterized by its difficulty to find, being uncharted for millennia, and was believed to lack a name. The Land of Eternal Dawn is also regarded as the most distant compared to the Never Kingdoms.[3] The Land of Eternal Dawn is also said to be in the farthest reaches of the Merged Lands.[4]
    • The Never Kingdoms also has a location known as the Abyss of Eternal Night, putting into question if there is a possible connection between the two. Additionally, they share some similarities in their names being opposites and the fact that one realm seems to fuel Ras' master's power and the other can destroy it.
    • Coincidentally, both realms play a significant role in Dragons Rising Season 4.
  • The Weapon of Desolation claims in "Dangerous Connections" that it is an artifact of Dawn, indicating that Ras' master may be tied to the realm.
