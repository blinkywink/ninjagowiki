Overview

Snowberries are a type of berry from the Never Kingdoms.

History

History[]

Season 11: Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

The Last of the Formlings (flashback)[]

Snowberries are seen being harvested by Formlings in Akita's story as she relates her origins to Lloyd. On the day of The Choosing, the Formling Leader held a bowl of crushed snowberries and used the juices to paint the markings present on the faces of Akita and her brother, Kataru. When Vex interrupted the ceremony, the Formling Leader held onto the bowl throughout his tense conversation with Vex, but gave the bowl to Kataru after Vex threatened the Formlings so that he could wield a staff in defiance against Vex.

Some time after finding their animal forms, Akita and Kataru consumed snowberries they collected from one of the shrubs the berries grow upon.

Season 1 (Dragons Rising)[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

Arin used snowberries to make the filling for his pie. He later used the pie as payment for Sora's entry in the Mech Master 5000, something that Alfonzo Frohicky, the registration official, remarked that Arin had done before.
