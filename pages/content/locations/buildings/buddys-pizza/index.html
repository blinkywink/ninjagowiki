Overview

“It's where Milton played his first videogame. That's where he found his life's calling as a game programmer. He'd called it "Videogame City." Always went back there as an adult. For inspiration.”
— Tony to Zane and P.I.X.A.L., "Ninjago Confidential"

Buddy's Pizza (also known as Pizza Station[1] and called Videogame City by Milton Dyer) is a pizza parlor in Ninjago City. It consists of a small singular room containing several booths, a kitchen, and three arcade games for kids to play on.

History

History[]

Child's Play[]

The ninja meet up with Lloyd at Buddy's Pizza to tell him about the Grundle and explain how they have been turned into kids.

Ninjago Confidential[]

Upon hearing a lead on Milton Dyer's location from Tony, Zane and P.I.X.A.L. head to Buddy's Pizza, which the Ninjago Police had closed down due to the Videogame Prohibition. They then get attacked and Zane gets taken by the Mechanic. P.I.X.A.L. then finds Milton Dyer on the roof of the building.

After Prime Empire[]

Buddy's Pizza was reopened after Unagami made peace with Milton and released his NPCs to live in Ninjago City. During his time at Vinny's apartment, Garmadon would frequently order pizzas from them.

The Shape of Nya[]

While the ninja were discussing who could be behind the Vengestone smuggling, Jay, who was still depressed from Nya's sacrifice, spotted a Buddy's Pizza delivery box. This made him realize that the Vengestone could be transported in the abandoned N line.

Christofern[]

While discussing his reason for being in the apartment, Garmadon finally received his overdue pizza, giving the delivery man one of Vinny's cameras as payment.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The three arcade games are called Iron, Rerender, and Speed.
    • Rerender can be seen being played on the Ultra Sonic Raider in "Double Trouble."
  • Jay claims he likes their pizza.
  • On the TV, the scenes from "The Golden Weapon" where Cole, Jay, and Zane meet Wu can be seen on screen.
  • It is mentioned the Milton Dyer got his inspiration for Prime Empire after seeing arcade games at Buddy's Pizza, meaning that the pizza parlor is at least 30 years old.
  • Buddy's Pizza is supposed to have a 20-minute delivery guarantee.
    • This is presumably a reference to Domino's Pizza's 20-minute delivery scheme.
