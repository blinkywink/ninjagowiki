Overview

“When I'm done with this remodel, this place ain't gonna be a museum, it's gonna be a you-see-um, with the huge crowds that are gonna come through here!”
— Roby, "I Alone Can Save Them"

The Temple City Museum is a museum located in the City of Temples.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Inside the Maze[]

When Cole and Geo were exploring the City of Temples, they saw the Temple City Museum and the Titan Mech outside.

The Sword Shatters[]

Taking Cole's suggesting of exploring the Temple City Museum, Arin went to the museum where he found Geo making a statue of Roby. As Roby talks about his plans to change the museum with replacing the old artifacts with bold modern art, putting up modern interactive experience with screens everywhere, a holo-display of the seven Source Dragons locked in an epic battle, and a pedestal that hold the Elemental Cup when the lights shine on its Dragon Ivory. Arin ask Roby about Dragon Ivory where Roby mentions they were use to harness elemental powers and offhandedly mentioned how the Elemental Cup is made from the police horns of an Arc Dragon. Noticing a portrait, Arin asks about it where Roby reveals it is his ancestor, Roake Fengxian, looking at the portrait cufflinks, Arin realizes Roby’s cufflinks has the same symbol he found.

Clues and Suspects[]

Cole snuck to the City Museum to steal the Elemental Titan Mech.

Temple Thieves![]

Because of the City of Temples staying in the same place after the Merge, some thieves took the opportunity to steal various artifacts from the museum. As Kai and Wyldfyre, the thieves stole the Elemental Cup from the museum alongside with the Book of Ceremonies. Thankfully, the ninja were able stop them and gave artifacts to Roby, who eventually took it back to the museum.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

I Alone Can Save Them[]

Roby and several Security Bots were making renovations to the museum when they were attacked by Kur and her Dragonians. Kur used her power of Decay on Roby when he attempted to drain her powers with the Elemental Cup, then stole the shattered remains of the Yellow Prismatic Blade.

The Vault of Focus[]

After Bleckt was freed from his cell, he took the ninja and Jay to the museum to show them how to defeat Thunderfang. Bleckt wanted them to turn around and cover their eyes as he said the password to the statue. However, Jay and Wyldfyre refused it, thinking that he is going to run away, which led to saying Bleckt saying the password in front of them, which Sora, Arin and Wyldfyre found funny. After the secret passage to Vault of Focus was revealed, Bleckt climbed down the passage. Jay thought it was a trap until Lloyd also climbed down and told the others to come in, taking everyone to the Vault of Focus.
