Overview

“Every time I closed my eyes, I saw that monastery again.”
— Cole to Zane, "The Sword Shatters"

The Monastery of Gates[1][2] is a monastery that was located outside of the City of Temples in Ninjago.[3] It has a portal gate for each of the realms, including the Spectral Lands, hence its name. Shortly after the Battle of the Spectral Lands, Ras' master moved the monastery to Land of Eternal Dawn, in front of the Shadow Dojo.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Truth and Lies[]

In the evening, after Cole loses his match against Frak, a portion of Wu leads him to the Monastery of Gates.

Clues and Suspects[]

Arin and Cole travel to the Monastery of Gates after Sora receives a coded message from Jordana, who is trying to resist Rox's Possession, stating that the missing dragon horns are located in the monastery. With no way across, Cole steals the Elemental Earth Mech from the museum to create a bridge with the excess elemental energy left in the mech's system and comes across Wu.

The Final Game[]

Arin and Cole continue their exhibition into the monastery and find the missing dragon horns hung above on the ceiling, here they also find out from Wu that he caused the Merge before the latter scatters parts of his form across many gates.

Elements of Betrayal[]

After Nokt and Rox take over the games, the ninja flee to the monastery where the rest of the Forbidden Five are freed. With her connection to the Dragon Ivory found in the horns and Riyu, Nya uses it to bring Kai and Bonzle back from the Nether-Space as well before Kai fights Nokt. However, just as the latter had the advantage, Roby used the Elemental Cup to strip him of his seized powers and return them to their rightful owners. As the Forbidden Five retreat to the Cloud Kingdom gate, Ras tries to intervene, but Nokt knocks him out and drags him to the portal. Arin, still desperate to find his mother and father, picks up Ras' hammer and goes after them before the portal explodes and scatters them all in different realms.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

I Alone Can Save Them[]

Kai, Nya, and Wyldfyre traveled to the Monastery of Gates from the Monastery of Spinjitzu after receiving word of Roby's condition. As they existed the portal gate, Nya noticed that a new portal gate to the First Land Monastery was now active, but Wyldfyre pressed for them to see Roby, stressing that he was more important than a "stupid gate."

Chaos Unchained[]

Wyldfyre flew the Mech Storm Rider to the Monastery of Gates to meet up with the Ninja in the First Land to help save the world to battle Thunderfang.

The Vault of Focus[]

The Ninja arrived at the Monastery of Gates to go to their respective destinations, with Arin and Lloyd going to the Spectral Lands with the Unbidden Storm while the others; Wyldfyre and Sora head to Djinjago to wake the tan half and Nya and Jay go to Mysterium to wake the purple half of the Arc Dragon of Focus. Although Nya ended up going by herself after a fight with Jay since they haven't held their end of the bargin; of getting him to Ras, paying him, and Nya driving him crazy.

Chaos Reigns[]

Ras traveled to the Monastery of Gates and disrupted the portal gates, scattering the ninja across three different realms.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

At some point after the Battle of the Spectral Lands, the Monastery of Gates was moved or transported by Ras' master right across from the Shadow Dojo.

Birthright[]

Ras emerges from one of the portal gates in the Monastery of Gates with a third Source Dragon Icon and goes outside of the Monastery, declaring to his master that he has collected another Dragon Icon.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • It is the fourth portal gate monastery to appear in the series, following the Monastery of Spinjitzu, the Monastery of Imperium and the Monastery of Mysterium.
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt justified the presence of only 16 gates in the Monastery of Gates by stating "If there are 17 monasteries, and you're in one of the monasteries, you only need 16 doors to get to all the other ones."[1] Since the Merge, there are only 16 regions left because the Cursed Realm and the Departed Realm merged into the Spectral Lands, so there are only 16 doors needed to access the 16 monasteries of the other realms, the Monastery of Gates working as an exceptional 17th monastery serving the other 16.
    • When asked, he stated that the monastery being in Ninjago was an intentional detail, and that it would make more sense once more of the Merged Realms are explored.[4]
