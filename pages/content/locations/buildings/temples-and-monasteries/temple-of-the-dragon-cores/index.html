The Temple of the Dragon Cores is an ancient temple located in a World Tree. It reveals the respective locations of the three Dragon Cores.

History

History[]

Background[]

Following the creation of the Dragon Cores, the First Spinjitzu Master proceeded to establish the Temple of the Dragon Cores,[1] a sacred place with the primary purpose of elucidating the tale surrounding both the temple itself and the Dragon Cores. It was the temple that the First Spinjitzu Master halted the Coalescence, averting the merging of the realms into a singular world. To safeguard the Dragon Cores from falling into the wrong hands, the First Spinjitzu Master concealed them, opting to depict their hidden locations through murals. Sacred knowledge would only be unveiled by the Spirit of the Temple, but solely upon the earnest request of a direct descendant of the First Spinjitzu Master.[2]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Temple of the Dragon Cores[]

The Ninja sought out the Temple of the Dragon Cores for information pertaining to the Coalescence. Arriving outside the temple, Kai immediately pulled one of the three levers on its gates, activating two runes. With no further idea how to proceed, Kai declared that he could use a break. Zane then analyzed the levers and the runes for several hours before ultimately declaring he could not see any known pattern or connection between them. Ultimately, it was the three members of the newest generation of ninja, Arin, Sora, and Wyldfyre, who realized that the three levers were meant to be pulled all at the same time.

Entering the temple, the ninja encountered the initially hostile Spirit of the Temple and fought in vain to defeat the incorporeal being. Lloyd was ultimately able to calm the spirit by rebuilding his alter and the Spirit regaled to the ninja the story of the Dragon Cores and how they had once stopped the Coalescence from occurring. When Lloyd then revealed he was the grandson of the First Spinjitzu Master, the Spirit provided the ninja with the locations of the three Dragon Cores, though unbeknownst to anyone, Rapton had infiltrated the temple and overheard the entire conversation!

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Their Draconic Majesty's Request[]

With the help of Leaf Blade, Arin and Ras made their way to the Temple of the Dragon Cores to learn whether or not there was a way to forge Source Dragon Icons without Elemental Powers. Upon entering the temple, Ras beheaded the statue of the First Spinjitzu Master before summoning the Spirit of the Temple. He then inquired about his query, to which the Spirit answered in the affirmative but refused to explain, citing that only a descendent of the First Spinjitzu Master could receive such information. In response, Ras presented the Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength to the Spirit, who identified it as an object of great power nearly equal to that of the Dragon Cores. Satisfied that the Spirit understood the powers of the icon, Ras immediately struck him with it, inflicting great pain upon the Spirit as he demanded the answers to his question. Left without a choice, the Spirit informed Ras that he would have to venture into the depths of the Underworld and find the Casket of Bones within Forge of the Forgotten. Satisfied with this answer, Ras released the Spirit who cursed Arin and Ras before vanishing.

Other appearances and mentions

Other appearances and mentions[]

Mindless Beasts[]

During her investigation on how to stop the MergeQuakes, Nya stumbled upon manuscripts mentioning the Coalescence and the Temple of the Dragon Cores. She affixed a translucent blue paper to a map, learning the temple's location.

The Power Within[]

Following the end of the MergeQuake Storm, Sora raised the question to Lloyd about returning the Dragon Cores to the temple. Nevertheless, Lloyd firmly believed that sacrificing the Dragon Cores was necessary in order to release the captive Source Dragon of Energy.
