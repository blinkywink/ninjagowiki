Overview

“Deep within the Whispering Wood is the Temple of the Green Ninja. Inside, you will be guided to perform the Rite of Unrooting. The temple is hidden from even the eyes and ears of the forest.”
— The Arc Dragon of Life to Lloyd, "The Lean of a Tree"

The Temple of the Green Ninja is a mysterious temple deep in the Whispering Wood, serving as the sacred site where the Green Ninja carries out the Ritual of Unrooting.
