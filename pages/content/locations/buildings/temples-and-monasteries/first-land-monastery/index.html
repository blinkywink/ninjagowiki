The First Land Monastery[1] is one of the parallel counterparts of the Monastery of Spinjitzu located in the First Land and contains the portal gate of the realm.

History

History[]

Background[]

Similar to many other monasteries, the First Land Monastery emerged from the energy of the Merge and shared features with the Monastery of Spinjitzu. Within its walls, P.I.X.A.L. was inexplicably confined in a techno-cocoon.

The Great Zane Robbery[]

While searching the First Land, Zane and Zanth discovered this monastery. Upon entering its gates, Zane watched the Freebooters emerged from the monastery carrying a purple techno-cocoon. Although Zane briefly engaged in battle with them, the Freebooters managed to escape from the monastery to their train.

Their Draconic Majesty's Request[]

Lloyd, Zane, Sora, Frak, Zanth, and P.I.X.A.L. used the monastery's portal gate to return to the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

Chaos Unchained[]

After using the machine P.I.X.A.L. and Zane made, the ninja arrived at the First Land through the First Land Monastery with their new vehicles and gliders.
