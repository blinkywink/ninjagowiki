The Monastery of Madness was one of the parallel counterparts of the Monastery of Spinjitzu located inside of a Quealiche and contained the portal gate of the Land of Madness.

History

History[]

Before the series[]

At some point, the Merge gave rise to the monastery along with its portal gate. Some time later, a Quealiche swallowed it, leaving the monastery trapped within its entrails.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Chaos Reigns[]

After using the portal of the Monastery of Mysterium to try to reach Ninjago, Kai, Nya, and Jay ended up in the dark monastery, somewhat confused at having arrived in a new realm. Immediately after arriving, the portal door suddenly closed and could not be reopened, leaving them stranded there.
