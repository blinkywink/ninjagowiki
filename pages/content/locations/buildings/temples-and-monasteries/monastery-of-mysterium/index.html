The Monastery of Mysterium is one of the parallel counterparts of the Monastery of Spinjitzu located in Mysterium and contains the portal gate of the realm. It serves as the home of the high sorceress Gandalaria.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

To Mysterium[]

Cole, Zane, Bonzle, and Jiro arrived at the Monastery of Mysterium. Cole at first mistook it for the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

Fugitives From Madness[]

Jiro waited outside the monastery while the other three entered and met Gandalaria, the monastery's resident and Bonzle's creator. Administration Agents tracking Zane later arrived outside the gates of the Monastery and despite Zane's efforts to stall them, smashed through the gates with the intention of recapturing him.

Secrets of the Wyldness[]

Gandalaria and the ninja fought against the Administration Agents on the grounds of the monastery. Once Zane deactivated the agents' Master Mechs, the group fled on Jiro to the Wyldness.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Human Resources[]

Thinking that it could help her return to normal, Sora, Arin and Ras took hollowed Gandalaria to the Monastery of Mysterium, only to accidentally active its security system, causing them to sink into the ground. Thankfully they were able to get out of it and reach the monastery thanks to Arin using Gandalaria's crystal ball. Then they searched for the Map of Mysterium in the library. After founding the map, Arin and Sora leaved the Sorceress in the library, hoping that she will be okay while also promising to help her and the other hollowed people in Mysterium. As they went to the monastery's courtyard, they saw Ras floating in the air, not knowing that he is contacting his master. After Ras stopped floating, they left the monastery to search for the Underworld.

For Whom the Bell Tolls[]

Nya arrived in the Monastery of Mysterium through its portal gate while she noticed how the portal gate appeared out of nowhere, much to her confusion. She then encountered Gandalaria's hollowed body as she was attacked by a someone who is wearing a giant armor. After she defeated the person, she realized that it's a coconut, much to her surprise. After coconut copy explained her the situation, Nya took a look at the map to find the Cave of Bells is, only for coconut copy to point out the fact that those directions are outdated. However, coconut copy promised her to show the current route. Then they left the monastery, leaving Gandalaria alone.

Later, Kai also arrived in the Monastery of Mysterium through its portal gate to find Nya.

Chaos Reigns[]

Following the Battle of the Spectral Lands, Gandalaria got her soul back and found herself in the Monastery of Mysterium, which she blamed Janet for it.

Later, Kai, Jay and Nya arrived in the Monastery of Mysterium to use its portal gate to go back Ninjago while also giving Gandalaria back her coconut copy.

Appearance

Appearance[]

The Monastery looks exactly like the Monastery of Spinjitzu, but with the aesthetic of Mysterium. There are invisible magic traps in the courtyard that Gandalaria set up for trespassers, especially her neighbor Janet. An invisible portal gate floats above the courtyard of the Monastery, appearing only when it is used. In the interior, there is the same library as the one in the Monastery of Spinjitzu, but darker and with mushrooms everywhere and tree trunks as pillars.
