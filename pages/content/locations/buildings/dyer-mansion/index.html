The Dyer Mansion is a mansion located on Dyer Island, where Bob the Intern currently resides. It was Milton Dyer's home when he was into creating games.

History

History[]

The Prodigal Father (flashback)[]

Thirty years ago, Milton Dyer and his team were ready to test out their newest game: Prime Empire. After briefly conversing with the game's artificial intelligence, Unagami, Dyer brought in their first beta tester: a young man named Scott. Dyer then left the room to allow Scott to play the new game, chuckling as he asked for Unagami to provide their tester with an experience that was "intense as possible." Sometime later, when Dyer returned to the room, he discovered to his horror that Scott was gone, a fact that was affirmed by Unagami.

Dyer Island[]

Learning about Milton Dyer and his history with Prime Empire, the Ninja set off to his private island in the hopes of asking him more about the strange game. Upon arriving at the island's shores, they discovered that the island had long been left in a state of disrepair, with Dyer's own mansion looking almost as if no one had lived there in ages.

As the ninja wandered through the hedge maze in front of the mansion to the front door, they were confronted by the island's various traps and defenses, including closing walls, robot dogs, and laser shooting stone angels! After some struggle, the Ninja were able to overcome the island's defenses and were greeted by Bob the Intern, who apologized for not turning them off sooner, explaining that he had been showering. Bob then welcomed the Ninja into the mansion where he spoke at length about his history with Dyer as well as all he knew about Dyer and Prime Empire, revealing that Dyer had not lived on the island for the last thirty years and that he seemingly swore revenge after Prime Empire's failed launch and his subsequent firing.
