The old electronics warehouse[1] is an abandoned electronics warehouse in Ninjago City.

History

History[]

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

The Mechanic drives his Noodle Truck of Crime to an old electronics warehouse. He and his henchmen investigate the hardware in the warehouse, but as soon as he sees it, he claims the Prime Empire motherboard. The Mechanic's affairs are interrupted by the ninja, who jump towards them from above. They fight, but The Mechanic is able to flee with the motherboard. The ninja follow behind and leave the warehouse.
