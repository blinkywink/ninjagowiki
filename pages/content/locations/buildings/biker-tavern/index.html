The Biker Tavern is a biker club located in Ninjago. It incorporates a bar and several pool tables.

History

History[]

Ghost Story[]

The Tavern was visited by Morro—at the time in possession of Lloyd's body—after he stole the Staff of the First Spinjitzu Master from Master Wu. When the patrons threatened to rough him up, Morro used the Allied Armor of Azure he was wearing to summon Wrayth, who helped disperse the bikers.

Morro then departed on his dragon to seek out the Scroll of Airjitzu while Wrayth turned one of the parked motorcycles into his Chain Cycle in order to hunt down the ninja.

Public Enemy Number One[]

Having been framed, Jay visits the tavern, and the only person there is busy watching the TV. Jay tries to obtain ninja chips from a vending machine using his Lightning but fails and leaves.
