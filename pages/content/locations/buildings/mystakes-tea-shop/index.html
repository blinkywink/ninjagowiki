Overview

“Mystake's Tea Shop. I wonder what magic tea she can brew up for us.”
— Cole, "The Oni and the Dragon"

Mystake's Tea Shop is a tea shop in Ninjago City owned by Mystake until her death. It was originally located in Jamanakai Village.

History

History[]

Rise of the Snakes[]

Tick Tock[]

Wu visited Mystake's Tea Shop to obtain some Traveler's Tea.

Day of the Great Devourer[]

Garmadon entered Mystake's Tea Shop so he could purchase Traveler's Tea and head back to the Underworld. However, he heard of the Great Devourer attacking Ninjago City and rushed to help.

Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

Child's Play[]

Nya and Wu entered Mystake's Tea Shop to find a tea that would make the ninja old again.

Prior to Sons of Garmadon[]

Harumi enters Mystake's Tea Shop and gets intrigued by a painting of an Oni and a Dragon. She then proceeds to learn about the history of them.[1]

Sons of Garmadon[]

The Oni and the Dragon[]

The ninja entered Mystake's Tea Shop in order to get the Tea of Truth. While at the shop, Lloyd spotted a painting in the background and asked Mystake about it. She gives the history of the Oni and the Dragon to Lloyd and Jay while Cole and Zane went out with the Tea of Truth.

Big Trouble, Little Ninjago[]

The ninja head to Mystake's Tea Shop in order to find something to heal an unconscious Lloyd. However, in order for the tea to work, Mystake required all the ninja to potentially give up their Elemental Powers. The ninja agree to help and wait for Lloyd who is still unconscious.

Suddenly, the ninja hear a sound and everyone except Nya headed out to fight.

Some time later, Lloyd wakes up from a weird vision and realized that his father wants young Wu, so he and Nya head out.

March of the Oni[]

The Darkness Comes[]

Mystake's Tea Shop is seen being repaired.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Resurrectea used to resurrect Dr. Julien may have been stolen from the tea shop.[4]
  • The shop's interior design was reused for the desk and back area of Steeper Wisdom in "Wu's Teas."
  • The tea shop in Season 8 is different from the one in previous seasons because it is located in Ninjago City and not in a small village. At the same time, it has a completely different look.
  • Oddly, the tea shop was rebuilt after the events of Season 9 in Season 10, even though Mystake is deceased, although its rebuilt appearance shares visual similarities with and uses an inscription from Steeper Wisdom.
