Overview

“There's only one rule: no eating the merchandise.”
— Clouse to Cole, "Ninja Roll"

The noodle factory is a large factory underneath Chen's Island, where goods for the Noodle House are produced.

History

History[]

Ninja Roll[]

After being eliminated, Cole is put to work in the factory where Karlof tells him not to bring trouble.

Spy for a Spy[]

Cole puts a secret message in a cookie for the ninja and creates a ruckus to get locked up, only to steal the head guard's keys and break Zane out.

Spellbound[]

The Elemental Masters tried to think of ways to escape but had to think of another plan when Dareth was captured.

The Forgotten Element[]

After Chen stole the Elemental Masters' powers, he had them taken to the factory, where they helped build the Roto Jet.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Located below the surface of Chen’s Island, this secret facility is where enslaved workers made the noodles served at Master Chen’s Noodle House restaurants. Laborers were under guard throughout their shift and were under orders not to eat the merchandise.[1]
