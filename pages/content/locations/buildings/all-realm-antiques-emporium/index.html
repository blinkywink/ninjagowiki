Overview

All-Realm Antiques Emporium is an antiques shop owned by Fedulian located in the Crossroads.

History

History[]

The Blood Moon[]

While Arin, Sora, and Riyu were looking where the “Night Ghost” was going next, they checked all the shops that sell antiques, and found one that was closed and had/has a grand opening. The three found that the shop is closed for the night so, the three had a stakeout. An old lady came to throw away trash and found Riyu. While Sora and Arin were talking about Sora’s powers, the “Night Ghost” came using Smoke to the shop. Cinder was trying to get in and used Smoke. Cinder then returned out of the shop using Smoke again with the Mallet of Shattering. Arin and Sora came to stop Cinder. Arin, Cinder, and Sora started a fight with each other while revealing that his boss is Lord Ras. A Wolf Mask Warrior tried getting away with the mallet.

Shattered Dreams[]

Arin, Sora, Lloyd, and Zane came to get answers about the mallet that was stolen from the shop. Fedulian, the owner of the shop, was getting ready for the grand opening. Fedulian and the Ninja came to talk about the relic. Fedulian lied telling that he has nothing to do with it. Fedulian says that people bring things and he sells it. He also says he says that he can’t research everything. The ninja went to the rooftop to see what Fedulian was doing because the ninja say he looked suspicious. Fedulian talked with Hantro about the ninja and he gave her a warning that the ninja might come.

To Mysterium[]

Zane, Cole, and Bonzle went to the shop to find maps. Fedulian just gave the three ninja ideas of what to buy. Fedulian gave the ninja a map for free. Fedulian says that they should have taken the disco toaster. Fedulian talked to someone saying the ninja will come.

The Great Zane Robbery[]

Fedulian was heading back to his shop when he notices a golden shuriken on the wall. This lead him to meet back with the mysterious mercenary known as Rogue who he pays him for the rare Oni chalice from the First Land. Fedulian also gave the bounty hunter a letter from someone.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • It seems that this antique shop is the newest shop in the Crossroads.
    • In all the episodes this shop has been in, the shop is shown to have a grand opening.
    • This suggests that not a lot of time has passed since the shop opened, or the banner was never taken down.
  • This shop is the second shop known to hold ancient artifacts after the Merge. The first is Kreel's Junkyard.
