Overview

“Kiddie Arcade. Definitely not as good as Buddy's Pizza.”
— Jay, "Legendary Places"

The Kiddie Arcade is an arcade in Ninjago City. As the name implies, it was designed purposefully to appeal to children, with the exterior itself being adorned in bright colors and poppy visuals. There are at least two Kiddie Arcades—one near the Ninjago City Bank, while the other is located on the same road as Grand Sensei Dareth's Mojo Dojo.

History

History[]

The Snake King[]

Kai took Lloyd to the arcade to occupy him while he continued the hunt for Samurai X. Lloyd complained about the trip claiming that it wasn't even a decent arcade, but soon discovered a group of Serpentine behind the arcade, talking about the Lost City of Ouroboros. Seeing an opportunity, Lloyd got a Rattla costume from the Kiddie Arcade and passed himself off as a Serpentine in order to board the Charter Bus.

The ninja later went to the arcade seeking Lloyd. Kai poked his head into the place, but not seeing the boy, they decided to check the video surveillance footage instead.

Day of the Great Devourer[]

When the Great Devourer was destroyed by Lord Garmadon, a group of children ran out from the Kiddie Arcade to celebrate the beast's destruction.

Pirates vs. Ninja[]

Kiddie Arcade first reflected from the mirror of Grand Sensei Dareth's Mojo Dojo. Later, after the ninja and Nya defeated the pirates, Ninjago City police arrested them outside the arcade.

The Day Ninjago Stood Still[]

As the ninja were evacuating the city, they drove by the arcade in the Ultra Sonic Raider.

Misfortune Rising[]

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Across the street from the arcade, Sir Chomps-A-Lot and his owner chased after Kai and Zane in the repo yard.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

Lloyd's Late (non-canon)[]

Two buses honk and crash into each other before Lloyd races past the Kiddie Arcade to get to Steeper Wisdom, determined not to be late.

Ancient History[]

Lloyd assigned the ninja their tasks across the street from the arcade.

Papergirl[]

Nelson and Antonia bike past the arcade while delivering newspapers.

Brave But Foolish[]

Jay passes by the arcade in his Golden Dragon Motorbike after destroying some Crystal Warriors with his bike's new powers.

Distress Calls[]

Racer Seven passes by the arcade while speeding through the city to get to the Ninjago News Center.
