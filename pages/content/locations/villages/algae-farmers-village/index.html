Overview

“You happen to pass by a big floating structure overrun by nasty jerks with arrows and a giant crab guarding it? That's where.”
— Dale to Lloyd and Arin, "Gangs of the Sea"

The village is a settlement owned by a community of algae farmers. It is located on a red-water ocean that is from a realm parallel to Ninjago.

History

History[]

Background[]

Sometime after the Merge, a group of Merlopians arrived, attacking the village with a giant crab. The algae farmers were forced to flee.

Gangs of the Sea[]

Lloyd and Arin came across the village when they were looking for a Dragon Core, and quickly retreated after being attacked by its new inhabitants. With the Bounty barely treading water, they happened to stumble upon the lost villagers. They came to an agreement to help the villagers reclaim their village in return for help repairing the Bounty. After Lloyd and Arin provided them with some training, they attacked the Merlopians. Lloyd went after the Ocean gang leader to get the control crown and stop the crab. After finally knocking the crown off his head, Lloyd realized that the monster was controlling the gang, instead of the other way around. The gang and villagers teamed up against the crab monster. Arin distracted it, while Lloyd knocked the control tooth out of its mouth. With the crab defeated, the villagers and gang now lived peacefully together.

The Great Zane Robbery[]

Rox and the Dragonian Warriors visited the village, and Rox ordered them to lay waste to it while she retrieved the Green Prismatic Blade from the crab tooth.

I Alone Can Save Them[]

As Ras was leading Arin to his parents, they flew over the village in their jet. Arin saw that it was in flames and immediately turned the jet around to help, but Ras told him to stay focused on the task at hand. Arin insisted that they couldn't just ignore the obvious signs of his old friends being in peril, prompting Ras to insist that if he allows himself to get distracted by everything in his path, he'll never be able to find his parents - which is exactly why Lloyd failed to help him do so. Encouraged to prioritize his own needs instead of that of others', Arin turns the jet around and flies past the village.
