Generative AI[]

This section is in need of citations. You can help Ninjago Wiki by contributing.

In March 2024, LEGO published a Ninjago character quiz that featured AI generated imagery. These renderings were intended to represent Kai as well as several other unknown characters. This quiz was met with strong backlash from the Ninjago fanbase as well as Tommy Andreasen[1] and Chris "Doc" Wyatt. LEGO swiftly removed the quiz and published an official statement.
