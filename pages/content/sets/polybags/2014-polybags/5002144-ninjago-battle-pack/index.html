Overview

5002144 Ninjago Battle Pack, also known as Dareth vs. Nindroid[1], is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu polybag set. It was released around September to December 2014, depending on the location, and includes Dareth and a Nindroid Warrior, as well as a "fake" trophy and a silver sai.

The set was a Toys "R" Us exclusive.

Grand Sensei Dareth takes on an evil Nindroid in this cool Ninjago Masters of Spinjitsu playset featuring 2 minifigures and a cup for the champion! Recreate favorite moments from the Ninjago Masters of Spinjitsu TV series with this awesome LEGO minifigure set![2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This was the first set to include Dareth, whose variant is exclusive to this set.
    • The face print is by far the most accurate depiction of Dareth to the TV series. The two other sets that feature him, 70751 Temple of Airjitzu and 70657 Ninjago City Docks, reuse a pre-existing face print from LEGO Fusion.
      • This is because this set is merchandise rather than a regular LEGO set, and merchandise has more freedom due to how many prints need to be made.[3]
