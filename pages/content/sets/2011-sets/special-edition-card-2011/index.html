Overview

Special Edition Card (2011) is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu set that was released on January 2, 2011. In the United States, it was part of a Toys 'R' Us offer in January 2011 that ran from the 2nd of January to the 29th.[1] Whoever bought a Ninjago set for $20.00 to $30.00 would receive Card ★6 - Flame Pit. Although the offer was supposed to end on January 30, Toys 'R' Us still sold them afterwards for $3.49.

In Australia, Card ★5 - Off Balance was distributed instead of Card ★6 - Flame Pit. In Europe, Card ★2 - Kai, Card ★3 - Krazi, Card ★5 - Off Balance, Card ★8 - Garmadon and Card ★9 - Sonic Roar were distributed.
