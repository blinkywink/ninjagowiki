Overview

4002021 The Temple of Celebrations is a LEGO employee gift. It includes Lloyd, Cole, Nya, Jay, Zane, Kai, and Wu in their Golden Legacy robes, casual Misako, Santa Garmadon, Mezmo, Mezmo Junior, the Temple of Celebrations, a Christmas tree, a dragon, and Garmadon's sleigh.

Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

"Designed together with Dimitrios Stamatis – a proper write-up will follow! :)

For all you worried ninjas out there: Knowing that a lot of you might want to build your own copy of this otherwise extremely rare set, we made sure that we didn't use any exclusive elements, minifigures or stickers. As such we hope some of you will be able to source the bricks to enjoy this little holiday treat from the Ninjago team :)

Graphic design by Michael Patton, Angel Grau Bullón & Kjeld Sørensen (not that they had any idea!)."[1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Although the set does not contain any newly printed pieces on minifigures, Golden Legacy Wu, casual Misako, Santa Garmadon, and Mezmo Junior are exclusive.
  • The dragon included in the set is strikingly similar to Chompy.
  • The set is similar to the 70751 Temple of Airjitzu, as both are temples of a similar color scheme and design, encompassing all the ninja, Wu and Misako, depicting a normal part of the ninja's life outside of the battles.
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, "the existence of this set DOES NOT establish Christmas as canon in Ninjago!"[2]
  • Tommy Andreasen wrote 6 Christmas poems for this set's instructions, though not all of them were included.[3]
  • This is the only set to contain all the Golden Legacy ninja.
  • The set contains a mini model of 71741 Ninjago City Gardens.
