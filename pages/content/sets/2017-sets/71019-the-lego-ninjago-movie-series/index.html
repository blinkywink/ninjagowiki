Overview

71019 The LEGO Ninjago Series is a LEGO Minifigures Series that is released in August 2017. It is based on the 2017 film, The LEGO Ninjago Movie. It contains 20 minifigures.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Bring exciting new play possibilities to LEGO® NINJAGO® sets with LEGO Minifigures—THE LEGO NINJAGO MOVIE™ series, featuring Kai Kendo, Spinjitzu Training Nya, Lloyd, Jay Walker, Cole, Zane, Lloyd Garmadon, Master Wu, Sushi Chef, N-POP Girl, GPL Tech, Shark Army Octopus, Shark Army Great White, Shark Army General #1, Shark Army Angler, Garmadon, Volcano Garmadon, Flashback Garmadon, Misako and Gong & Guitar Rocker. Each minifigure comes in a sealed ‘mystery’ bag, together with one or more accessories, plus a display plate and a collector’s leaflet.

  • Each minifigure comes with one or more accessories, plus a display plate and a collector’s leaflet.
  • Includes 2 versions of Lloyd, 3 versions of Garmadon and new-for-August-2017 versions of Nya and Kai.
  • Collect this series of LEGO® Minifigures characters and create your very own action-packed THE LEGO NINJAGO® MOVIE™ scenes!
  • New-for-August-2017 elements include a black hat and weapon for Garmadon, scarf for Jay, wigs for Lloyd, Jay, Nya, Kai and Misako, octopus helmet, angler fish helmet, bowls, spoons and a 2x3 blue tile with printed blueprint for Lloyd’s dragon mech.
  • Combine with LEGO® building sets for endless play possibilities.
  • Have fun trading minifigures with your friends.
  • PLEASE NOTE: Each ‘mystery’ bag contains one of the 20 characters available in this collection. Unfortunately, we cannot accommodate requests for specific minifigures. If you purchase more than one ‘mystery' bag, you may receive 2 or more identical minifigures.[1]

Character bios

Character bios[]

Character Bio
Kendo Kai Spinjitzu training with Kendo gear is all part of being a ninja. After all, if you want to be a master of fire you have be prepared to take some hits.[2]
Spinjitzu Training Nya Nya unlocks her true potential as a water ninja with more than a crushing spin kick. It requires patience, training, balance… and really solid foot work.[3]
Lloyd The Green Ninja. The chosen one, honor bound and sworn to protect NINJAGO - even when it's from his own father Garmadon.[4]
Master Wu Son of the first Spinjitzu master, he is more powerful and wise than we even know. Master Wu believes everything is about balance. Known to say "You can win the battle on the outside but lose the battle on the inside."[5]
Garmadon Four arms, red eyes, rides a giant robot shark. Garmadon wants to build his empire on the ashes of NINJAGO City as the greatest warlord ever. When he hunts down the ninja he will use all his warlord skills including a keen sense of Jungle attire.[6]
Jay Walker Growing up in a scrapyard with his parents on the outskirts of NINJAGO, Jay never dreamed he would one day become the blue ninja. A true master of lightning and questionable fashion choices.[7]
Lloyd Garmadon It wasn't hard enough secretly being the Green Ninja, Lloyd must deal with life in NINJAGO as the infamous son of Garmadon, the worst warlord ever.[8]
Cole The master of earth and a keen sense of music, even his subwoofers have woofers. Cole might seem too cool for school but it's not true, he is actually the most grounded and stable of all the ninja.[9]
Misako She will protect her son Lloyd by any means necessary. Working three jobs, cooking a mean waffle or battling evil warlords. There is more to Misako than meets the eye. Nicknamed Koko by Garmadon.[10]
Zane When he's not fighting evil as the ice ninja Zane needs to attend school like the rest of us. He's been running the numbers and believes "We have a 87.6666 (repeating of course) chance of losing this fight against Garmadon."[11]
Shark Army General #1 Her rise to top general was paved by many generals before her, and constantly under threat of being most likely to get fired out of a volcano when things go wrong. Can she find the ultimate weapon to take down NINJAGO before it's too late?[12]
Shark Army Octopus Nicknamed Four-Eyes by Garmadon. This warrior has an eye on everything in NINJAGO. He knows the best places to attack and has even been experimenting with propaganda by adding Garmadon to priceless artwork in NINJAGO.[13]
Shark Army Angler This army Angler is a master of distraction and misdirection. Whatever you do don't underestimate this Angler's ability totally take control.[14]
Shark Army Great White Formal General #1 has been scorched by flaming hot magma when he was fired from Garmadon's volcano. He was the apex predator of Garmadons Shark Army - nothing stands in his way, they mostly kneel. Who knows where he will surface next.[15]
Flashback Garmadon Being a Dad can be hard work. Garmadon has tried but it seems the life of warlord always gets the better of him. He never fails at looking super cool though, look at those shades and that tie, it to die for…[16]
Volcano Garmadon What do you get the guy who has almost everything? His army hasn't been able to deliver NINJAGO but they have made him more comfortable in his Volcano sleepware complements of Garmadon Propulsion Laboratories. Sweet dreams. Muuuhhahahahahaha[17]
Gong & Guitar Rocker The ninja need some music while saving the city and this rocker knows how to play. Known to say "We built this city, we built this city on rock and GONG."[18]
GPL Tech Garmadon Propulsion Laboratories (GPL) is an exciting place to work, where else are you likely to get shot out of a volcano. Garmadon's team of tech experts are creating something really wicked for Garmadon and this tech expert is the brains of the operation.[19]
Sushi Chef Master chef of NINJAGO and the best in the business. He used to serve Garmadon 5 LEGO star sushi omakase back in the college days. chop, Chop, CHOP!!!![20]
N-POP Girl It's a hard knock life in NINJAGO and it shouldn't be all about fighting and re-building the city. The NINJAGO Pop music circuit has just started and this girl is leading the dance revolution. She might also be the biggest fan girl of the secret ninja.[21]
