Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

LEGO.com[]

This beauty is given to the Croc King's heir when he or she comes of age. It is a symbol of the secrets, wit, and strength you need when you prepare for the dauting task of becoming ruler one day. Cragger's golden leochium blades are shaped like crocodile teeth in honor of the first mother and father of the Croc tribe. Cragger really likes that. He finds it quite poetic that he can use it to avenge the loss of his parents.[1]

Lego® Legends of Chima: Official Guide[]

One of Cragger's favorite weapons, Vengdualize, looks like a primitive collection of Crocodile teeth on a stick, but it's boosted by technology, A small interior motor spins the blades like a chain saw.
