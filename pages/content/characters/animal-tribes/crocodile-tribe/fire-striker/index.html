The Fire Striker is a tank used by Cragger.

History

History[]

Big Boys With Big Toys[]

After getting the keys of their new tanks from their fathers, Laval and Cragger went to outside, where they saw the Fire Lion and Fire Striker. The duo decided to race through the Sanctuary Forest with their tanks, despite their dads' warnings about using them wisely. However, after arriving in the Fangs and almost running to the Gorge of Eternal Depth, they decided to leave the place carefully and race to the Crocodile Swamp instead.

Fire Dreaming[]

While talking to Eris, Laval pointed how their dads gave him and Cragger new tanks, showing the Fire Lion and Fire Striker

Later, Laval and Cragger took Wonald and G'Loona on a ride with their tanks in the Sanctuary Forest, telling them about their quest on the Outlands. However, Eris stopped them and pointed out how most of the stolen Chi is still out there, angrily leaving the forest. Seeing this, Laval and Cragger followed Eris with their tanks to apologize her.

Trial by Fire[]

After getting Fire Chi from the Phoenix, Laval, Eris and Cragger arrived in the Beaver Dam to fought against the Hunters, with Cragger using his Fire Striker to fight against them, powering it up with Fire Chi.

The Crescent[]

The Heroes of Chima pushed the Hunters back from the Crocodile Swamp and melt the ice in it, with Cragger using his Fire Striker, which was powered by the Fire Chi. After the Hunters left, Cragger used his tank to search for the Crocodile Tribe.

The Phoenix Has Landed[]

Cragger used the Fire Striker to make his way to the Phoenix Outpost.

Other appearances

Other appearances[]

Attack of the Ice Clan (vision)[]

In Eris' vision, Eris saw Laval and Cragger driving their tanks, only for them to get powered by the Fire Chi.
