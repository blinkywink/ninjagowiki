Official Description

Official Description[]

(From LEGO.com Characters section)

Trakkar is one of the most skilled and elite warriors in the Tiger forces. In addition to being a talented Tiger Pursuit Bike rider, Trakkar is also responsible for the high-tech Tiger communication base. This base, which splits off from the Tiger Mobile Command vehicle, keeps all Tigers in touch with each other and gives the tribe an edge in battle. Trakkar not only can operate the sensitive equipment, but can repair almost any of it in minutes.

(From LEGO.com Explore section, Eye of the Tiger)

Trakkar is a specialized fighter in the Tiger forces. Riding his Tiger Pursuit Bike, he gathers intelligence and information from different parts of a battle. He is also responsible for the high-tech communication base that separates from the Tiger Mobile Command. From this base, Trakkar coordinates the different Tiger units across the battlefield, giving the Tigers a strong tactical edge. Trakkar is also the Tiger that can repair sensitive equipment in a matter of minutes.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • His name could be a pun of the word tracker.
  • With the story in Eye of the Tiger being a continuation of "Strike of the Tiger," it can be assumed that the two Tiger Warriors that appear in the series are indeed Tazar and Trakkar.
  • Although Trakkar is the one driving the Tiger Pursuit Bike according to LEGO.com descriptions, Tazar drives it on the set pictures.
