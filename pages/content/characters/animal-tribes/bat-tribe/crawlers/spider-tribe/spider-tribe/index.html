The Spider Tribe is a tribe of humanoid spiders from Chima. They formed an alliance between the Bats and the Scorpions for all the Chi. They have the ability to make webs.

History

History[]

Legends of Chima, Season 1[]

The Spiders were normal animals that used live at the bottom of the Gorge of Eternal Depth. However, after Laval dropped Chi into the gorge, they were transformed into more humanoid animals with the rest of the Crawlers, making them bigger, stronger and bigger. Then, the Spiders and the rest of the Crawlers chased Crunket and Crominus, who were at the bottom of the gorge at the time, and forced them to help them find more Chi. The Crocodiles lead them to the Outlands, where they showed them the Mount Cavora. Seeing Mount Cavora, the Crawlers were impressed by it and plotted to steal all the Chi. Hearing this, Crunket and Crominus tried to escape, only for the Crawlers to capture Crominus.[1]

After forming an alliance with the Scorpions and Bats, Spiders helped them to capture the Legend Beasts to stop them from ruining their plans,[2][1] with Spiders imprisoning the Gorilla and Rhino Legend Beast at their base, Spider Canyon, which ShadoWind eventually found out about.[1] Then, with the help of the Bats, Spiders arrived at Mount Cavora and used their webs to stop its waterfalls from flowing and stole the Chi. Even after the Tribes of Chima fend them off, Mount Cavora's waterfalls dried up because of the webs.[3][4] They even tried to capture the Lion Legend Beast because of Scorm's command, only for the ShadoWind to arrive and defeated them with the help of the Legend Beast.[5]

Later, Scorm got sick of Spinlyn always ravishing herself, saying that the only thing they need is the might of his Scorpions, causing an argument about who is superior. Eventually, this led to a fight where both the Spiders and Scorpions used their vehicles to fight each other. However, after observing the fight and realizing that they complement each other well, Spinlyn and Scorm decided to call this a draw and attack Chima together, only to decide that they could attack Chima another day after seeing their tribes tired.[6]

Legends of Chima, Season 2[]

The Spiders continued to steal Chi from Mount Cavora as the Tribes of Chima tried to stop them. Meanwhile at the Outlands, the Spiders were guarding the Spider Canyon to stop the Legend Beasts from escaping, with some spiders constantly spinning new webs to stop the Gorilla Legend Beast while the others guarded the Rhino Legend Beast's prison. Seeing this, the Heroes of Chima disguised the Rock Flinger as a fly and used it to enter the Spider Canyon, where Spinlyn commanded the Spiders to eat the fly. However, seeing that the Gorilla Legend Beast was almost going to escape, the Spiders returned, and some of them tried to keep him trapped while the others fought off the heroes, who had revealed themselves. Then, Gorzan used Chi to distract the Spiders, allowing the Legend Beast to free himself and join the fight. Seeing this, Rogon used a stoning mechanism, freeing the Rhino Legend Beast. Then, Eris and Rogon went to the Spider Canyon, with Rogon jumping onto his Legend Beast while Eris ran into a Spider web. Then, the heroes returned to the Rock Flinger and left with the Legend Beasts, only to realize that Eris is still captured by the Spiders. Thankfully, Rogon fended off the Spiders with his Legend Beast and saved Eris. Seeing this, Spinlyn tried to catch them, only for Rogon to catapult the rock autopilot at her and break one of her teeth.[4]

Later, at the Crocodile Swamp, Crunket wanted Crooler to interrogate some of the Spiders they captured. However, Crooler tried to make a deal with the Spiders, saying that they could collaborate and control Chima's Chi, only for the Spiders to unlock their locks with their Spider arm and chain her as well as capture Plovar, who was trying to clean Plovar's teeth. Then, they took Plovar to Spinlyn, who wanted Plovar to clean and fix her broken tooth.[7] Upon hearing the demands of Spinlyn, Plovar said that he needed top-quality supplies, which Spinlyn said that his subjects are at his disposal to gather anything he needs. Realizing that he can use the Spiders, Plovar traveled to different parts of the Outlands with the Spiders, collecting supplies with them, much to their struggle. Then, Plovar and the Spiders returned to the Spider Canyon with the supplies as the Spiders were tired.[8]

After Scorm told them that Razar's Speedor's wheel is made out of Tribe Stone, the Spiders and Bats went to Mount Cavora and went to steal Tribe Stone.[7] Then, by using it, they made their own Speedorz. Meanwhile, some of the Crawlers captured the Bear Legend Beast and put him on a large rock on a pillar to distract the Heroes of Chima as they finished their Speedorz. After the heroes saved the Bear Legend Beast, Laval encountered the Crawlers and their new Speedorz. The Crawlers tried to catch Laval, eventually cornering him, while the other Crawlers captured Bladvic and his Bear Legend Beast. Thankfully, Laval, Bladvic, and the Legend Beast were able to get out of there thanks to the Legend Beast snoozing and making a huge landslide.[9]

Because of Scorm thinking that they could crush their enemies if they gather their forces and make the heroes come to the Scorpion Cave,[9] Spiders went to the caves with the rest of the Crawlers while taking Crominus and the Wolf Legend Beast with them, with Spider occasionally webbing the mouth of the Mother Wolf to stop her from howling until a Scorpion stung her again.[10] On their way to the cave, they spotted Cragger and Worriz and captured them, taking heroes to the Scorpion Cave, where some of the Spiders and the Crawlers guarded the inside while the rest of them guarded the outside. While they were at the cave, Spinlyn and Scorm started to argue, with Scorm thinking it was the Great Scorpion in the Sky who dropped Chi to the gorge while Spinlyn insisted that it was the Great Spider in the Sky who dropped Chi to the gorge. Hearing this, Cragger got an idea to make the Crawlers destroy each other.[10]

Eventually, the Eight Heroes of Chima arrived and distracted the Spiders and the Crawlers to take back their Chi and use it to overpower them, allowing them to enter the cave, where they encountered the rest of the Spiders and Crawlers. Thankfully, the heroes were able to get past them and save their friends. However, the Crawlers overpowered them with their vehicles, causing the heroes to retreat. Seeing this, Cragger told them Laval was the one who dropped the Chi to the gorge, saying that they should build the statue of the Great Lion. However, the Crawlers did not believe him and continued to attack, even trying to shoot Laval while he tried to go and save the Lion Legend Beast.[10] As the fight in the Scorpion Caves continued, Spiders and the Crawlers continued to attack the heroes, with Spinlyn commanding the Spiders. During this, Plovar arrived and told Spinlyn that she needed beauty supplies to reach the "full bloom", leading her to a mud pit and leaving her trapped, causing Spinlyn to call some of her forces to save her, only for them to sink into the mud as well. Then, Scorm blasts the ceiling of the cave with the Korrodor to stop the heroes from leaving, causing the cave to start collapsing, eventually causing all of the Spiders and Crawlers to get trapped in the Scorpion Cave.[11]

Legends of Chima, Season 3[]

After the Scorpions accidentally released the Hunters, the Spiders tried to fight back, only to get frozen immediately.[12] Later, when the Hunters returned to the Hunter Glacier, the Spider and the rest of the Crawlers were put into Fangar's trophy room as a part of his collection.[13]

When Flinx tried to use his heat to release the imprisoned inhabitants of Chima, he accidentally released the Crawlers, including the Spiders, too. Still angry at the heroes for imprisoning them at the Scorpion Cave, the Crawlers attacked them. Thankfully, they were overpowered by Crominus, Crunket, and Lagravis. As the heroes attempted to leave the trophy room, the Crawlers tried to follow them, only for Flinx to use his heat again to melt the floor and send them down a hole. Thankfully, by using her webs, Spinlyn was able to save the Crawlers.[14]

Following the Second Great Illumination, the Spiders and the rest of the Crawlers reformed themselves and started living in peace with the inhabitants of Chima, even participating in numerous activities during the holiday celebrations.[15]
