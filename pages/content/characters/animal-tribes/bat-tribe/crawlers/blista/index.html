Blista is a bat warrior.

History

History[]

Originally, Blista was just an ordinary bat in the Gorge of Eternal Depth until Chi dropped by Laval caused him to evolve along with other bats and the Scorpions and Spiders, and they gained an intense hunger for more CHI. They captured Crominus and Crunket and took them to the surface where they were shown the source of Chi, Mount Cavora. Seeing in the mountain, Blista and the other Crawlers were amused by it and plotted to block the waterfalls and steal all the Chi for themselves. Taking the opportunity, Crominus and Crunket tried to escape, only for the Bat Tribe to unite into form a massive cloud and capture Crominus.

Legends of Chima, Season 2[]

Into the Outlands[]

The Bats attacked the Heroes of Chima after they passed the Predator Plants and chased them through the Outlands. Despite the heroes' attempts to get rid of them, the Bat Tribe chased them to a dead end. Thankfully, Rogon appeared and hit them with rocks thanks to his Rhino Bike, defeating the Bats.

A Tangled Web[]

The Bats returned to the Mount Cavora with the Spiders to steal more Chi. However, as they arrived in the mountain, they encountered the attack of the Tribes of Chima, who were trying to fend them off. Couple of Bats tried to get away with the stolen Chi, only to get blasted by the tribes.

Later, after the Tribes of Chima got more Chi thanks to the Crocodile Tribe, they used them to attack the Bats, fending them off from the Mount Cavora.

Horn to be Wild[]

While carrying some Chi, the Bat Tribe were hit by Rogon's Rhino Bike. Furious, the Bat Tribe chased him to a mountain. However, as they were bout to jump on him, Rogon started sharpening his "pillow" by using his bike, which knocked out the Bats, putting them to sleep. Seeing this, Rogon joined them as well.

The Legend Thief[]

After Scorm told them that the Kleptor S2's wheel is made out of Tribe Stone, the Bat and Spider Tribe returned to the Mount Cavora to chip off the mountain's Tribe Stone.

The Eagle and the Bear[]

In the Scorpion Cave, the Bat Tribe made their own Speedorz alongside with the other Crawlers. Meanwhile, some of the Bats captured the Bear Legend Beast and Eagle Legend Beast's eggs and put them on a seasaw trap.

After the Heroes of Chima saved the Bear Legend Beasts and the eggs, Lavertus was about to pick up Laval with his WindShadow, only to get ambushed by some Bats and their Bat Flyers. As Lavertus fend them off, Laval was ambushed by some of the Crawlers, including Bats, who tried to catch him with their new Speedorz. Thankfully, Laval was able to get rid of them during the chase. Fortunately, some Bats got ahead of Laval and tried to ambush him, only for one of them to crash into Braptor while others crashed into rocks. However, some of the Crawlers, with a bat even helping to capture Bladvic and the Bear Legend Beast. Thankfully, the Bat and some Crawlers run away after Bladvic used Chi.

Tooth or Consequences[]

Worriz and Cragger saw some Crawlers passing by with the Wolf Legend Beast and Crominus, including the Bats, who were going ahead of them.

At the Scorpion Cave, Bats and the Crawlers guarded the area to stop anyone from escaping, while some of them guarded the entrance to stop anyone from entering the caves. However, the Heroes of Chima distracted the guards and got their Chi back from them. Then, they fought off against the Crawlers, with the Bear Legend Beast falling on top of some Bats, and eventually entered the cave, where they encountered the rest of the Bats and the Crawlers. Thankfully, the heroes were able to overpower them and find the Wolf Legend Beast, Worriz, Cragger and Crominus. However, the Crawlers used their vehicles against them, overpowering the heroes and causing them to get to cover. Even after Cragger tried to make them fight each other, they continued to attack them. Then, Laval tried to save the Lion Legend Beast as the Crawlers tried to shoot him.

This May Sting a Bit[]

As the battle at the Scorpion Cave continued, the Bats attacked the Heroes of Chima. However, because of Scorm hitting the ceiling of the cave with his Korrodor, the cave collapsed, trapping the Bats with the other Crawlers.

Between seasons[]

Prior to Unfinished Business[]

Blista and some other Bats were able to get out of the cave and went to the Outland Base, using the place as their new base.

Unfinished Business[]

After Laval saw the bat weapons in the Outland Base, the bats arrived and saw Laval. Still angry at the hero, the Bat Tribe attacked him. Thankfully, Laval was able defeat him by using the Sir Kicks-a-Lot, making them escape.

Legends of Chima, Season 3[]

Fire Dreaming[]

When Scorm accidentally revived all of the Hunters, they froze all of the Crawlers, including Blista.

Prior to Cool and Collected[]

Blista and the rest of the Crawlers were brought to Sir Fangar's trophy room by the Hunters, where they were displayed.

Cool and Collected[]

Some Bats were shown frozen in the trophy room.

A Very Slippery Slope[]

The Bats were thawed out by Flinx and started to fight with Laval and the others with the other Crawlers. However, Lagravis, Crominus, and Crucket fought back. They were later sent down a slope by Flinx.

Prior to Merry Chima Everyone![]

Following the Second Great Illumination, Blista and the other Crawlers reformed themselves and started living in peace with the other inhabitants of Chima.

Merry Chima Everyone![]

During the holiday celebrations, Blista and the other Bats decorated the tree with Chi.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Blista is a sand-blue bat with a pink nose. He has the typical massive ears and black wings as well. For attire, he wears an enormous sliver chestplate with exposes some of his muscular stomach. There are two decorative chains hanging from this armor for his CHI-harness. On the legs, Blista wears nothing but a torn black loincloth made from fur, much like Braptor's, only without extra armor concealing it. His loincloth is held on by a matching black belt with a bat-shaped buckle.

Abilities[]

    • Flight - Blista can fly using his wings.
