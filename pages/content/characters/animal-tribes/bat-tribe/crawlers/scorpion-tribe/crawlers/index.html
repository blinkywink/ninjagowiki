Overview

“There's evil out there, Cragger… Bad tribes and black clouds… They want to steal all our Chi.”
— Crunket to Cragger, "For CHIMA!"

The Crawlers, also known as the Dark Tribes[citation needed] or the Outland Tribes,[1] are a united force of three newly evolved tribes reside in the Outlands.

History

History[]

Legends of Chima, Season 1[]

The Crawlers originated as normal cave-dwelling animals inhabiting the Gorge of Eternal Depth. When Crominus and Crunket were trapped at the bottom of the gorge, they became uneasy around the creatures but did not initially fear them. That changed when Laval dropped the Crocodile Tribe's Chi into the gorge to keep it out of their hands. Crominus and Crunket failed to recover the Chi because the Crawlers reached it first. Exposed to the energy source, the Crawlers evolved just like the other tribes of Chima, developing an insatiable craving for more Chi. They took Crominus and Crunket prisoner, forcing them to help locate the source of Chi. The royal couple complied, viewing it as their only way back to the surface. This journey led the Crawlers into the Outlands, where Crominus and Crunket pointed out Mount Cavora. Taking advantage of the Crawlers' distraction at the sight of the mountain, the crocodiles attempted an escape. However, they were separated when the Bat Tribe formed a massive cloud to attack them. Following this, the Crawlers captured the Legend Beasts across the Outlands, despite facing resistance from Lavertus, who used the alter ego "ShadoWind" to fight back.

Plotting to hoard all the Chi for themselves, the Spider and Bat Tribes were tasked with stealing Chima's entire supply. This included targeting Mount Cavora by bypassing its protective energy field. Although the Tribes of Chima initially mistook the Bat Tribe for a living cloud, they recognized the "Black Cloud" as a direct threat to their homeland. To ward off the threat, Laval rallied the alliance at the Lion City to shoot down the swarm. Overwhelmed by the defense, the Crawlers were forced to retreat. The Spider Tribe blocked the waterfalls of Mount Cavora with their webs to secure the Chi for later, leaving Chima to wither within two full moons unless the Chi flow was restored.

Official description

Official description[]

Deep in the dark and dangerous Outlands, amongst the poisonous plants, live the Bat, Spider and Scorpion Tribes. These creepy crawlies were “created ” when Laval accidently dropped CHI into the Gorge of Eternal Depth. Having made their way to the uncharted territory of the Outlands, these three tribes remain desperate for the power of CHI. The Spiders are master engineers and builders, and lead the assault on Chima. The vicious Scorpions add a sinister edge to the army, while the low-ranking Bats bulk up the vile and dangerous mass![2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The concept art shows the Crawlers with more monstrous designs, and with three more tribes; horn beetles, mosquitoes and mantises.
  • In another piece of concept art, the scorpions and the bats are shown working for a red cloaked figure. Currently, it's still unknown who this figure was originally supposed to be.
  • It is unclear if the Crawlers were affected by the Second Great Illumination in any way, though them being in peace with the Tribes of Chima in "Merry Chima Everyone!" conflicts with their behaviour in "A Very Slippery Slope," when they attacked the heroes who were rescuing them from the Hunters.
