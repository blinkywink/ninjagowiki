This article is about a subject that is relevant to both the Ninjago and Legends of Chima franchises. Information may be more directed toward one theme than the other.

The Skunk Tribe is a tribe of humanoid skunks from Chima. There is only one known member, Skinnet, he is usually referred to as a Nomad instead of a Tribe Member.  

Origins

Origins[]

The origins and whereabouts of the Skunk Tribe is unknown. It is said that at some point in the past, they left Chima.[1]
