Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Rhinos become extremely smart when they are near to the Rhino Legend Beast.
  • During Season 1, Rhinos as a tribe weren't clearly either on the side of the warring tribes, even though their Chi color was blue, until the episodes 19 and 20, where they joined the villain tribes.
    • Despite this, in the game Laval's Journey they were shown on the villain tribes side.
