The Claw Drillers were mechs used by the Ice Bear Tribe.

History

History[]

Background[]

Following the Great Illumination, the Claw Drillers were trapped in the ice alongside the Hunters.

Wings of Fire[]

The Claw Drillers were trapped inside the ice alongside with the Ice Bears and were released with them.

The Heart of Cavora[]

The Ice Bears arrived in the Lion City in their Claw Drillers, overwhelming the Tribes of Chima, destroying the Tiger Strikers as well as pushing the Hunter Glacier to the Lion Temple with them, allowing the Hunters to steal all the Chi from the Sacred Pool of Chi. As the fight continued, the Ice Bears continued to attack the Tribes of Chima and destroyed the Tiger Strikers, allowing the Hunters to raise a big ice to Mount Cavora with the Chi they stole. Then, the Bear Tribe arrived in the Lion City with their Rumble Bears, attacking the Ice Bears and destroying their Claw Drillers, eventually overwhelming them.

Other appearances

Other appearances[]

Naptime is Over[]

The awakening of the Ice Bears from their frozen confinement disrupted the dreams of the Bear Tribe, as the Ice Bears launched an assault using their Claw Drillers. In response, Bladvic used a Rumble Bear to defend his tribe. However, during the battle, one of the Ice Bears used their Claw Driller, which ultimately roused Bladvic from his slumber.

LEGO.com description[]

The Ice Bears are attacking Bulkar in Icenhowler's gigantic Claw Driller to try and steal the CHI! Fight ice with fire on Bulkar's Burner Bike, steering clear of the mighty mech's spinning drill, dual flick missiles and powerful swinging arms. Outwit the Ice Bears from the air with flying Razar and his Fire Harness. And watch out – Icepaw could launch into battle at any time on the Claw Driller's detachable Blaster Bike! Includes 4 minifigures with assorted weapons and accessories: Icebite, Icepaw, Razar and Bulkar.[1]
