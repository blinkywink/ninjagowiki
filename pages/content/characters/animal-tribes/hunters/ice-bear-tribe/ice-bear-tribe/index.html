The Ice Bear Tribe is a tribe of humanoid bears from Chima.

History

History[]

The Ice Bears used to be one of the primitive tribes on Chima, descending from the same forefathers as the Bear Tribe, viewing each other with nothing but enmity.[1] Although, unlike the Bears, the Ice Bears were overly energetic. As natural hunters, they heard call of the wild, felt the roar of their nature from the snowy mountains and icy tundras that called to them, commanding them to behave like the animals they were.[1] When Phoenix gave a new way of life to all the tribes of Chima and invited them to their institutions of learning and improvement, the Ice Bears were amongst them, despite being against their nature. The Phoenix wanted them to stop being natural hunters to become peaceful, civilized creatures. However, every fiber in their body fought the teachings of Phoenix, which caused Phoenix to stop and scold them for misbehaving. Eventually, the Ice Bears grew tired of this.[1] So, when Sir Fangar rebelled, they followed him and fought by his side, participating in the Hunter Wars. However, whenever they marched into the battle, the Ice Bears went berserk, and they could not always tell who friend or foe was and were more of a liability. Eventually, Fangar was not able to control the Ice Bears, who refused Fangar's orders or directly disobeyed him. [1] However, after the eight Phoenix Elders performed the Great Illimunation, the Ice Bears tried to fight back the Illimunation with the other Hunters. However, the Illumination fought back and pulled down the Gorge of Eternal Depth as well as pulling nearly all of the heat from the Fangs, causing the Ice Bears and the Hunters to froze and get trapped in the Gorge of Eternal Depth.[2] As years passed by, their skin began to decay and the bones were revealed.

Years later, when the tribes were unfrozen by Sir Fangar thanks to the Chi the Scorpion Tribe dropped, Fangar decided to leave the Ice Bears behind, thinking that his army and power of freeze are enough.[1]

Wings of Fire[]

To defeat the Tiger Strikers, the Vulture Tribe went back to the Gorge of Eternal Depth to release the Ice Bears under Fangar's command. However, as they released the Ice Bears, the Ice Bears attacked the Bears in their dream,[3] causing Bears to also wake up.

The Heart of Cavora[]

The Ice Bears arrived in the Lion City, overwhelming the Tribes of Chima, destroying the Tiger Strikers as well as pushing the Hunter Glacier to the Lion Temple, allowing the Hunters to steal all the Chi from the Sacred Pool of Chi. As the fight continued, the Ice Bears continued to attack the Tribes of Chima and destroying the Tiger Strikers, allowing the Hunters to rise a big ice to the Mount Cavora with the Chi they stole. Seeing this, Laval wanted to help only for Cragger to say that more troops are on the way. Then, the Bears arrived in the Lion City with their Rumble Bears, attacking the Ice Bears and destroying their Claw Drillers, eventually overwhelming them.

Following the Second Great Illumination, the Ice Bears were purified alongside with the rest of the Hunters.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This Tribe is a very strong tribe, even strong enough to beat the Tribes of Chima with ease, with only the Brown Bear Tribe being able to defeat them.
  • The Ice and Brown Bear Tribes are said to have originated from the same forefathers several millenia ago, thus being of the same species and the same blood as well as sharing a strong connection with each other. Despite this however, they view each other with nothing but enmity, similar to brothers, who fight constantly.
  • There seems to be a trend of each of the four named Ice Bears differing in some way from one of their tribe's main characteristics:
    • Icebite prefers to fight in the Claw Driller instead of "paw-to-paw" on the ground
    • Icerlot prefers planned combat over chaotic assaults
    • Icepaw prefers speed over strength
    • Iceklaw prefers combat "by pen" over direct combat
  • In the Concept art, there are two variations of bears shown, a white polar bear and a zombie bear. It's possible that these two bears were combined into the Ice Bears.
  • Because of their "Berserker" status and runic symbols on their bodies and armor, it would seem that the Ice Bears are possibly drawing inspiration from the Scandinavian Vikings.
  • According to The Battle for Chima book, the Ice Bears can't swim.
