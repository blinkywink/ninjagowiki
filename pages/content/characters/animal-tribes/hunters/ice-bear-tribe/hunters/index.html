Overview

“They can freeze us, bury us, pretend we never existed… But the Hunters always rise.”
— Fangar, "Attack of the Ice Clan"

The Hunters, also known as the Ice Clan[1] and the Ice Tribes,[2] were an army of ancient tribes that gained ice-based powers due to a combination of Illumination energy and being frozen in solid ice. They were some of the Tribes of ancient Chima, being educated by the Phoenix until Sir Fangar grew power-obsessed and led the Hunters against the Phoenix, who performed the Great Illumination to stop them by freezing them in ice. until the Scorpion Tribe dumped Chi down the cavern, releasing them from a frozen limbo between life and death and empowered with Ice powers.

History

History[]

Background[]

The Phoenix sought to educate the Tribes of Chima through learning institutions, sharing their wisdom to foster a better world. However, their efforts backfired when a young Saber-tooth Tiger named Fangar became consumed by the desire for total domination over Chima]. Proclaiming himself as Sir Fangar, he rallied followers, forming the Hunters. The Hunters launched devastating attacks that left opposing tribes powerless as Chima was turned into a desolate wasteland. As the Hunters wreaked havoc, the Phoenix worked to shield as many tribal members as possible.[2] As the threat from the Hunters grew, the Phoenix decided to perform the Great Illumination to restore balance and heal the Hunters. However, Fluminox hesitated, worried about leaving his infant son, Flinx, without a father.[3] This hesitation led to an imperfect Illumination, resulting in the Hunters being frozen and trapped in the Gorge of Eternal Depth for thousand years, with the ice granting them ice powers infused with Illumination energy.[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Hunters could arguably be considered the most menacing and most powerful villain tribe faction in the Chima series thus far, because of their frozen zombie appearance and their power to freeze anything they can touch.
  • They are the only villain tribe faction who is built up of four tribes, instead of three.
  • They had to have the CHI orb in their chest plate at all times, otherwise they would revert to a dormant state. This condition was reversed after the Second Great Illumination.
