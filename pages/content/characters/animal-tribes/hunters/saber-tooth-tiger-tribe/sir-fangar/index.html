Sir Fangar is the leader of the Saber-tooth Tiger Tribe and the Hunters.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Sir Fangar is a white Saber-Tooth Tiger with a golden fang. His eyes, left arm and right leg glow blue. Patches of flesh show on his left cheek and has a scar on his right eye. He wears a worn-out detailed silver and gold armor with a white cape.

After the second Great Illumination, Sir Fangar is healed. He wears a dark blue cape, his eyes are yellow and clean white fur covers his body, the scar and flesh patches gone.

Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

LEGO.com (2014)[]

Sir Fangar is the leader of the Ice Hunters who are menacing the land of Chima. Smart, unpredictable and dangerous, he intends to conquer Chima and then remake it into what he wants it to be. Sir Fangar is very aggressive and keeps his army on the move, but his unwillingness to retreat has cost him in battle. Along with taking over Chima, Sir Fangar hopes to “collect” and freeze one of every tribe, including the Phoenix, as trophies.[1]

LEGO.com (2015)[]

Sir Fangar is the leader of the Icehunters who are menacing the land of Chima. Smart, unpredictable and dangerous, he intends to conquer Chima and then remake it into what he wants it to be. He loves the sound of his own voice and never uses one word when eight could do. Sir Fangar is very aggressive and keeps his army on the move. Recent setbacks have led to him awakening the dangerous Ice Bears to help his cause.[2]

Vengeful Dictator[]

Deranged leader of the Sabre-tooth Tigers, “Sir” Fangar sees himself as a cultured and philosophical soul. Intent on taking over the world, he likes to think of himself as an artist and benefactor whose reign will enlighten the inhabitants of Chima.[3]

Trivia[]

  • The 'Sir' in his name can indicate him being a knight, but in reality, it is just a self-given title that he used to make himself sound important when he attempted to overthrow the Phoenix. Additionally, this also made his name correspond to the traditional naming pattern, where the first letter is the same as the first letter of the Tribe.
    • The title 'sir' is, even if self-given, the first title given to anyone in Chima.
  • In Rise of the Phoenix story, his sword is revealed to have come from a giant Saber-Tooth Tiger tooth, in which he traded the tooth for one of his own teeth.
  • Sir Fangar continues the common theme of each main antagonist having a hunger for something; Crooler hungered for power, Scorm hungered for CHI, and Sir Fangar hungered for knowledge.
  • He speaks with a British accent.
