Stealthor is a general of the Saber-tooth Tiger Tribe.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Stealthor is a grey saber-tooth tiger. His left arm is glowing blue, and so does his eyes. Patches of flesh show through his fur. He wears a blue worn-out skirt and shirt with light blue criss-crossing strands of cloth holding his CHI plate, and light blue belt tied on his waist.

Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

LEGO.com[]

Stealthor is a Saber Tooth general, as well as an experienced scout. Once, it was almost impossible to spot him if he didn’t want to be seen. But these days, his glowing bones and icy powers make it hard for him to conceal himself. It’s difficult to sneak around when you keep freezing everything. Although he is a formidable warrior, Stealthor misses the days when he was the tribe’s master of disguise and expert at spotting the enemy before they spotted him. He has tried wearing layers of really warm clothes to try to keep from spreading ice everywhere, but that has yet to work.[1]

General-In-Chief[]

Trusted with the Hunters' armies Stealthor is a ruthless and commanding leader. He leads from the front, going ahead to scout for the unsuspecting tribes of Chima. If you spot Stealthor, you can be sure that hordes of fierce soldiers are not far behind.

Glow Bones[]

In the good old days, Stealthor was a master of camouflage. He blended into his surroundings and was super hard to spot. But now, after thousands of years spent decaying in the ice, his exposed bones glow brightly whenever uses CHI. Not great for sneaking up on people in the dark!

Swift And Silent[]

Stealthor's stealthy three-wheeled trike is both fast and silent. This makes it the perfect vehicle for scouting for the enemy in icy Chima- where any sound would travel for miles.
