70224 Tiger's Mobile Command is a Legends of Chima - Season 3 set that was released on January 1, 2015. It features Eris with a Fire Wing Harness, Tazar, Trakkar, Breezor, Sir Fangar, Sibress, and a Tiger Striker.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Sir Fangar’s Saber-tooth army seems unstoppable as it freezes the kingdom of Chima and steals all the CHI. Team up with the elite Tiger warriors to launch a roaring counter-attack with their mighty Mobile Command vehicle. Detach the rear of the vehicle and convert it into a hi-tech Tiger communication base, complete with radar dish and computer panels, to plan your attack on. Engage fire CHI power mode to unveil the flame wings and fire the hidden disc shooters. Then unleash the Tiger Pursuit Bikes and track down Sir Fangar’s ice hunters! Includes 6 minifigures with assorted weapons and accessories: Tazar, Trakkar, Eris, Breezor, Sir Fangar and Sibress.

  • Includes 6 minifigures with assorted weapons and accessories: Tazar, Trakkar, Eris, Breezor, Sir Fangar and Sibress
  • Tiger’s Mobile Command features tiger’s head detail, huge rotating tracks, fire CHI crystal, fire CHI mode, flame details, 2 hidden disc shooters, adjustable ramps, 2 Tiger Pursuit Bikes and detachable Tiger communication base
  • Tiger communication base features radar dish, adjustable translucent flame search lights, computer dashboard, flip-out ladder, 2 storage compartmentsfor CHI and 2 metallic tiger claw weapons
  • Also includes the Abominator ice catapult with 3 ice bombs
  • Weapons include Sir Fangar’s Twin Saber-Klaw, Sibress’ Kold Blasta, Tazar’s Fire Pulsorz, Trakkar’s Slashenatorz and Eris’ Flaming Axterminator
  • Also includes Breezor’s Fix-it Stick
  • Engage fire CHI power mode and target Sir Fangar with the disc shooters
  • Detach the communication base and plan battle tactics
  • Dodge the ice catapult’s bombs
  • Repair the Mobile Command with Breezor’s Fix-it Stick
  • Lower the ramps and unleash the Tiger Pursuit Bikes
  • Tiger’s Mobile Command measures over 4" (12cm) high, 13" (34cm) long and 13" (34cm) wide
  • Tiger communication base measures over 5" (15cm) high, 4" (12cm) wide and 6" (16cm) deep
  • Tiger Pursuit Bikes each measure over 1" (4cm) high, 3" (8cm) long and 1" (4cm) wide
  • Saber catapult measures over 1" (3cm) high, 1" (3cm) wide and 1" (5cm) deep[1]
