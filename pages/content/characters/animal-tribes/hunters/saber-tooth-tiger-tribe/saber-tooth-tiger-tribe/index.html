The Saber-tooth Tiger Tribe is a tribe of humanoid saber-tooth tigers from Chima. They are distant relatives of the Tiger Tribe.[1]

History

History[]

Background[]

In ancient Chima, the Saber-tooth Tiger Tribe thrived during a time when the Phoenix sought to enlighten the various tribes. Among the students was Fangar, a young Saber-tooth Tiger who excelled under the Phoenix's guidance. However, as his knowledge and ambition grew, Fangar became corrupted, believing that conquering Chima was what he needed to accomplish next. He led the Saber-tooth Tigers, along with the Mammoth, Vulture, and Ice Bear Tribes, in a fierce war against the other tribes, becoming known as the Hunters. The Phoenix tried to shield the attacked tribes and ultimately resorted to the Great Illumination.[2] The Phoenix Elders employed the Fire Wing Harnesses to purify Chima and rid it of the Hunters' evil. Unfortunately, Fluminox, who couldn't leave his son behind, hesitated at the last second, causing the Illumination to be imperfect.[3] As the Illumination unfolded, the Hunters fought back but were ultimately overpowered by the Illumination, which created the Gorge of Eternal Depth. The Hunters were imprisoned in ice within the Gorge, and fell into a dreamless sleep. The Illumination energy also granted the Hunters their ice powers in the future.[2]

Legends of Chima, Season 3[]

When Scorm cast the Crawlers' Chi into an abyss, one of the Chi orbs revived Sir Fangar. The extra Chi that followed allowed Sir Fangar to awaken many of his fellow Hunters, freeing the Saber-Tooth Tigers, Mammoths, and Vultures. After escaping the chasm, Sir Fangar and his Hunters battled through the Crawlers to reclaim their vehicles and reached the surface.[4] They launched their campaign in the Crocodile Swamp, freezing the tribe except for Cragger, Crooler, and Crug. The Tribes of Chima tried to thwart them with their weapons and vehicles, but the Hunters' ice powers rendered their efforts useless, forcing a retreat.[5] The Hunters continued their conquest while Eris pursued mysterious visions that led her, Laval, and Cragger to Mount Cavora, where Fluminox offered them Fire Chi.[2] Armed with the new Chi, the Tribes of Chima managed to stop the Hunters, pushing them back into the Gorge of Eternal Depth. However, Sir Fangar overheard Gorzan mention the vast Chi held by the Gorilla Tribe and sought to exploit this knowledge. Joining the Vultures, the Saber-Tooth Tigers navigated the ancient underground tunnels of Chima to gather Chi from the forest. There, Sir Fangar encountered Flinx and planned to capture him as the most prized addition to his collection of frozen trophies.[6]

In the midst of the Hunters' campaign, Sir Fangar launched a surprise attack on the Lion Tribe while Laval was preoccupied with Li'Ella, allowing the Hunters to seize the Lion City. Sir Fangar's forces managed to freeze the defending Lions. Although Tormak arrived and fought off many Hunters, Sir Fangar escaped with Lagravis, adding him to his collection of frozen trophies.[7] Sir Fangar then initiated another major assault on the Lion City, where the Hunters aimed to capture Flinx and froze the city. The Phoenix intervened to prevent Sir Fangar from taking Flinx, but the Hunters still managed to damage the Lion defenses and capture Li'Ella, whom Sir Fangar intended to make his queen.[8] As the Hunter Glacier advanced toward the Lion City, the Heroes of Chima devised a plan to halt its movement and rescue the captives. Laval and his allies lured the mountain into a trap set by the Beaver Tribe at the Grand Archway, causing a collapse that sent the Glacier tumbling into the canyon. Amid the chaos, Flinx's heat melted the Hunter Glacier, leading to its destruction, while Sir Fangar and the remaining Hunters narrowly escaped.[9] After the original Hunter Glacier was destroyed, the Hunters set out to build a new one designed to assault Mount Cavora. Fangar, however, was concerned that the Phoenix might unleash another Great Illumination against them. Knowing that only the Artifact held the map to the Fire Wing Harnesses, Fangar planned to use Li'Ella as a bargaining chip, pretending to offer her freedom in exchange for Tormak stealing the Artifact. Tormak agreed to this deal, but when the Artifact was taken from the Phoenix Temple, it reacted explosively, embedding its energy into Tormak and transforming him into the Panther.[10]

Fangar pressed on with his wedding on Spiral Mountain, but the Heroes of Chima intervened, disrupting the ceremony and freeing Li'Ella, which forced Fangar and the Hunters to retreat. After the wedding disaster, Flinx tried to face Fangar alone but was tricked into the Fangs, where the heat from his wings melted the ice, causing him to plunge into the Gorge of Eternal Depth. Fangar and the Hunters then sealed the opening with ice, trapping Flinx below. Meanwhile, the Phoenix and the heroes tried to save him, but the Hunters thwarted their efforts and forced the Blazing Bastion to the gorge's bottom.[11] With the airship trapped, the Hunters gained the upper hand, freezing much of Chima. Eventually, the heroes escaped the gorge with Lavertus and his Golden Chi, only to find Chima nearly entirely frozen and the Hunter Glacier was on the move again.[12] As the Hunter Glaicer reached the Lion City, the arrival of the Tiger Strikers overwhelmed the Hunters. Fangar ordered the Vultures awaken the Ice Bears, ignoring the objections of his allies.[13] With the Ice Bears' help, Sir Fangar's forces were able to defeat the defending tribes and steal their Chi, which they used to construct an ice tower that reached Mount Cavora, where Fangar confronted Fluminox at the Phoenix Temple. Fangar then attacked Fluminox, and the other Hunters joined in to overpower the Phoenix King. Just as Fangar was about to finish Flinx, the eight heroes arrived with the Fire Wings. Flinx entered the Heart of Cavora and became the Ninth Phoenix, completing the Great Illumination. This unleashed a powerful force that melted the ice in Chima and restored Sir Fangar and the Hunters to their original forms, freed form their malevolence.[3]
