Overview

The Vulture Tribe is a tribe of humanoid vultures from Chima. They are allied with the Mammoth Tribe, the Saber-tooth Tiger Tribe and the Ice Bear Tribe to conquer and freeze Chima. They were eventually purified by the Second Great Illumination.

History

History[]

Background[]

Under Vardy's leadership, the Vulture Tribe was one of the known tribes in ancient Chima, tutored by the Phoenix. However, a student from one of the other tribes was Fangar, who became convinced that the only true measure of success lay in conquest. Adopting the title of Sir Fangar, he allied the Vultures and other tribes, including the Saber-Tooth Tigers, Mammoths, and Ice Bears, forming an army known as the Hunters. The Hunters swept across Chima, leaving a trail of devastation in their path. In a desperate attempt to counter their relentless advance, the Phoenix resorted to the Great Illumination, planning to cleanse the Hunters of their destructive tendencies. However, Fluminox, one of the nine essential participants, chose to remain with his infant son, Flinx, leading to a critical flaw in the process. This unleashed a surge of energy that not only drove the Hunters and their temple within the Gorge of Eternal Depth. The Vulture Tribe found themselves ensnared in the disaster, as the overwhelming release of power siphoned heat from the Fangs, encasing the Vultures and their allies in ice and putting them into a dreamless sleep.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Oddly, while the other two Ice Hunter tribes are extinct animals in real life, the Vultures aren't; currently, this has not been explained.
    • However, the tribe is more likely the extinct bird, Teratornis.
  • They have been shown to have great patience, as they only froze the Bear Tribe when the Bear Legend Beast woke up and breathed fire.
  • Despite having gray wings in the sets, they're instead depicted with black wings in the TV series.
