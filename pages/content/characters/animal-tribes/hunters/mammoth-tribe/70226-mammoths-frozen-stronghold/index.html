Overview

70226 Mammoth's Frozen Stronghold is a Legends of Chima set released in 2015.

LEGO.com Description

LEGO.com Description[]

Save Rinona from the Mammoth’s awesome fortress!

Rinona has been captured by the Mammoth tribe and imprisoned in the awesome Frozen Stronghold. Rush to rescue her with Rogon in his powerful Rhino Roller, but beware—the Mammoths are well-equipped to defend their icy fortress. Evade blasts from the trunk’s stud shooters and the rotating disc shooter. When the Mammoth Tusk Flipper is launched, swerve clear before Maula can activate the flipping function to tip the Rhino Roller over. Once inside the stronghold, grab the precious CHI crystal, release Rinona from the frozen prison turret and then make your escape! Includes 5 minifigures with assorted weapons and an accessory: Rinona, Maula, Mottrot, Vardy and Rogon.

  • Includes 5 minifigures with assorted weapons and an accessory: Rinona, Maula, Mottrot, Vardy and Rogon
  • Mammoth’s Frozen Stronghold features a mammoth-head-shaped control centre with computer screen, movable tusks and adjustable trunk with 2 stud shooters, Mammoth Tusk Flipper vehicle launch function, turret with rotating disc shooter, frozen prison turret, blacksmith area with hammer and anvil, ammunition depot with extra discs for the disc shooter, and blue and orange CHI crystal elements
  • Mammoth Tusk Flipper vehicle features a ramming function, 2 flick missiles and mammoth tusk elements
  • Rogon’s Rhino Roller features a cockpit, big wheels, flick missiles and a rhino horn element
  • Weapons include Rogon’s Blaze Boomer, Rinona’s Fyrecrakker, Maula’s Trunkor, Mottrot’s 2 Pummelor Gauntlets and Vardy’s Refreezor
  • Accessories include Rogon’s Fire Wings
  • Drive to the rescue in Rogon’s Rhino Roller
  • Beware of getting rammed and rolled over by Maula’s Tusk Flipper!
  • Evadeblasts from the rotating disc shooter and flick missiles
  • Release Rinona from the frozen prison turret
  • Mammoth’s Frozen Stronghold measures over 7" (18cm) high, 13" (35cm) wide and 7" (20cm) deep
  • Mammoth Tusk Flipper vehicle measures over 2" (6cm) high, 3” (9cm) long and 2" (7cm) wide
  • Rogon’s Rhino Roller measures over 2" (7cm) high, 4” (11cm) long and 3" (8cm) wide
