Overview

The Mammoth Tribe is a tribe of humanoid mammoths from Chima.

History

History[]

Background[]

The Mammoth Tribe used to be one of the primitive tribes of Chima, living under Maula's rule with the other tribes. Eventually, the Phoenix invited some of the tribes to their institutions of learning and improvement, including the Mammoths. However, after Fangar rebelled, the Mammoths joined him and together, they formed the Hunters, starting the Hunter Wars and eventually turning Chima into a lifeless wasteland. To stop the Hunters, the Phoenix Elders wore the Fire Wing Harnesses and fled to the heart of Mount Cavora, performing the Great Illumination.[2] However, since Fluminox did not join the Illumination, it was imperfect.[3] Because of this, when the Hunters tried to fight against the Illumination, the Illumination fought back and opened up a chasm that would be eventually known as the Gorge of Eternal Depth, causing the Hunters to fall into it and get trapped in ice, putting them into a dreamless sleep, including the Mammoths. The Illumination energy also granted the Hunters their ice powers in the future.[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Oddly, the tribe is titled the Mammut Tribe on LEGO.com. Whether that is the official name or a typo is unknown. However, this name may be referring to Mammuthus, the genus that mammoths are classified as.[4]
  • Oddly, on all three Mammoths, there is exposed bone in the trunks; this is biologically inaccurate, as the trunks are muscle and have no bone.
