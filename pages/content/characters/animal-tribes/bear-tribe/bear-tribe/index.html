Overview

The Brown Bear Tribe[1] is a tribe of humanoid brown bears from Chima. They are being able to communicate and interact with one another in their dreams.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Mind-control immunity: Their deep slumber protects them from mind control manipulation, causing hazards like Scorpion venom or Persuader Plant pollen to wear off completely.
  • Collective dreaming: The Bear Tribe share a dream space called the Bear Dream Zone, where they interact with each other.
