The Eagle Spires is the tribal village of the Eagle Tribe in Chima, located on Eagle Mountain.

History

History[]

This section is a stub. You can help expand this section by adding some information.

The Eagle Spires was presumably built sometime soon after the Eagles drank the CHI water.

Few visitors ever came to the Spires, according to Eris, and the Eagles created an advanced government, society, and a large amount of odd contraptions in their peaceful homes.

They remained safe from attack for thousands of years, until Cragger and Crooler, with Ripnik's help, tricked the Wolf Tribe into attacking. Using Bellow Plants, they were almost able to succeed until the Lion Tribe and Raven Tribe stepped in.

Soon later, the Ravens also staged an attack of sorts, trying to get their hands (and hooks) on their many possessions. They inhabited the Spires after driving the Eagles out for only a day or so, until getting bored and going back to the Raven Town.
