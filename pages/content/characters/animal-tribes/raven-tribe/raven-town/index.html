The Raven Town is the tribal village of the Raven Tribe in Chima.

History

History[]

Background[]

At some point after the Great Illumination, the Ravens built the Raven Town from the junk they found, not knowing that they built it over a Fire Wings Harness

The Power of the Chi[]

The Great Story[]

In the Raven Town, Razar explained the Ravens how they can get trinkets and treasures from the Crocodiles in exchange of attacking the Lion Temple.

Attack on Eagle Spire[]

To convince Ravens to fly the Lions to the Eagle Spire, Laval and Eris went to the Raven Town. Even though the Ravens were hesitant to help them at first, they decided to help them after Laval offered to pay them. Then, the Ravens got their Chi Raiders ready as the other Lions arrived.

The Hundred Year Moon[]

To warn the Ravens about the Hundred Year Moon, Wonald went to the Raven Town, where Razar tried to sell him locks that he claimed to be "%100 Wolfproof Hundred Year Moon locks", only for lock to get broken after Wonald touches it. Seeing this, Razar tried to explain by saying Wolproof is not valid when the lock is touched by a real Wolf.

The Chi Jackers[]

The Lions and Razar went to the Raven Town to bring the Raven Tribe's Chi to them.

Balancing Act[]

Laval and the Eagles passed over the Raven Town to deliver the Raven Tribe's Chi by dropping it, with the Ravens to catch it.

Prior to Crocodile Tears[]

After Lagravis cancelled the monthly Speedor Races, the Ravens started to host their own bootleg contests in their town.

Crocodile Tears[]

To find the ShadoWind, Laval and Eris went to the Raven Town, where they encountered Razar, who convinced them to race in their fake Grand Arena, saying that he knows the ShadoWind and they need to win the race first. As they started to race, Razcal started to sell merchandise, saying that it's to show their support to their favorite rider. Eventually, Laval arrived the nest and won the race, despite Razar saying the Ravens actually won because they sold a plenty of merchandise. After the race, Laval demands Razar to bring the ShadoWind, thinking that he is bluffing, only for the "ShadoWind" (who is actually Furtivo) to arrive. Then, they head to the Wolf Village to free the Crocodiles together.

After freeing the Crocodiles, Furtivo returns to the town to get his shares from the ShadoWind merch and helping Laval. Then, he leaves as the real ShadoWind watches him from the distance.

Fake CHI, Real Trouble[]

Crooler went to the Raven Town to want Razar to find a Persuader Plant for her, only for him to say he can't find them anymore since they picked them clean years ago. However, Razar directed her to Reegull, a half Raven half Eagle alchemist, while also warning her. After Crooler met with Reegull, he showed her how he can give her the Chi.

Later,in the Raven Town, Crooler showed Cragger how strong the Chi Reegull makes by making Crug use it. Seeing this, Cragger decided to gift this Chi to the other tribes to make up for his actions. However, as Cragger was giving the Chi to the Ravens in Raven Town, Razar warned Cragger about how dangerous Reegull's "Chigull" is. Then, Crug started acting like a bird because of the "Chigull".

Upon realizing this, Cragger went back to Reegull's laboratory and started questioning him. Reegull explained him that he did this because he wanted to take revenge of the tribes for not accepting him. However, as Cragger questioned Crooler about the "Chigull", Reegull tried to teach Crug the correct way he should act. Then, they leaved the laboratory to warn the tribes before it's too late.

Later, Cragger returned to the Raven Town with Laval to give Reegull a lesson, tying him to Bellow Plants and then causing him to fall because of the explosion of the plants. Then, Laval and Cragger leaved the Raven Town to bring real Chi to everyone.

Ravens vs Eagles[]

In the Raven Town, Razar was drawing treasures and trinkets while watching the bootleg Speedor Race, seeing how junk got thrown to Eglor. Razar pointed out how that Speedor belongs to Eris, which in response Eglor said that in the Eagle Spires, nothing technically belongs to anyone, everyone is equal and private ownership is strictly forbidden, shocking Razar. After Eglor left, Razar realized how the Ravens can take advantage of this fact.

After the Ravens took over the Eagle Spires, the Eagles decided to use their racetrack for fun. Eventually, the Ravens joined them too, since they missed the place.

Reunion Gone Wrong[]

Skinnet accidentally covered the Raven Town in his smell while celebrating his "true destiny", causing the Ravens to panic.

Laval in Exile[]

After getting exiled, Laval passed from the Raven Town.

The Black Cloud[]

In the Raven Town, Razar planned to capture some of the black cloud with Razcal, despite Razcal hesitation, flying with their Chi Raiders to catch it.

Chima Falls[]

To help the Crocodiles attack the Lion Temple again, the Ravens took their Chi Raiders from their town and left.

Later, Laval and Eris visited the Raven Town to get Ravens to help them, only to find no one. Seeing this, Laval hang up a paper that wanted them to stop by the Lion City to discuss a valuable opportunity. Then, he left the town with Eris.

Legend of the Fire Chi[]

Cool and Collected[]

Laval and Flinx brought the Raven Legend Beast to the Raven Town to protect the Ravens from the Hunters.

The Snowball Effect[]

In the Raven Town, the Raven Legend Beast sent a fire trail signal to let the other Legend Beasts know that it is doing fine.

The King Thing[]

In the Raven Town, Ravens were discussing their plans to turn the market area into a two-story shopping plaza.

The Heart of Cavora (flashback)[]

Tormak went to the Raven Town, where he found the fifth Fire Wings.

Appearance

Appearance[]

The Raven Town is a group of dwellings that are home to the Raven Tribe. It consists of many ramshackle buildings, for the most part resembling houses. The town is filled with junk and debris, which the Ravens hoard and plan to sell. It also has many treasures hidden in the rubbish, which are the profits that the Ravens have accumulated from making deals and stealing valuables.

The village also has a racetrack, where they hosted Speedorz races after the Lions stopped hosting them.

The town seems to be in a somewhat dry region with few plants, since there are many dead or leafless trees in and around the town. This means they might live near the Rhino Tribe or Wolf Tribe.
