Overview

The Raven Tribe is a tribe of humanoid ravens from Chima. The Raven Tribe is known primarily as a tribe of thieves and unscrupulous inventors, only caring about making maximum profit at the expense of others.

History

History[]

Legends of Chima, Season 1[]

The Raven Tribe is one of the eight primary Tribes of Chima. Following the Battle at the Fangs, Cragger, under the control of Crooler, enlisted Razar to have the Raven Tribe join the Crocodiles and Wolves in their assault on the Lion City during Laval's Age of Becoming ceremony. Although the Raven Tribe hesitated initially, they were swayed by their desire for money. As the Rebel Tribes attacked the Lion Temple, they gained the upper hand until the Lion Legend Beast arrived, leading to Laval's victory over Cragger and the subsequent retreat of the Raven Tribe with the other Rebels.[1]

In the following months, Cragger hired the Ravens to disrupt the Chi Day race. They first stole Laval's Speedor and repainted it, creating a distraction that allowed them to seize the Golden Chi when Leonidas and Longtooth went to look for Laval. However, their scheme unraveled when Laval blackmailed them to return the Golden Chi by threatening their merchandise.[2]

Hired by the Crocodiles, Ripnik stole the Mother Tooth from the Wolves and left behind one of Eris' feathers, making the Wolves believe the Eagles stole their relic. This provoked Battle at the Eagle Spires. Not knowing the Ravens were a part of this, Laval and Eris paid the Ravens to take the Lion Tribe to the Eagle Spires so they could help defend the Eagles from the Wolves. However, the Ravens lured the Lions to an ambush by the Wolves. Meanwhile, Ripnik also pilfered the Mother Tooth from the Crocodiles, which the Ravens planned to sell back to the Wolves for profit. However, the Ravens soon discovered from Ripnik that the Crocodiles intended to demolish the Eagle Spires, which would kill everyone present. Realizing the potential loss of their business, the Ravens cut the chains pulling down the structure, ultimately saving the Lions, Eagles, and Wolves.[3]

Razar learned from Eglor that in the Eagle Spires, no one truly owned anything, as the Eagles believe in sharing all their possessions. Taking this idea to heart, Razar saw a chance for the Ravens to seize whatever they desire, leading them to invade the Eagle Spires and start taking the Eagles' belongings. However, as they settle into the spires, the Ravens quickly realize that living there was not as beneficial as they had hoped, and their business ventures suffer. Eventually, the Ravens decided return to Raven Town, with the exception of Razar, who stayed behind until his fight with Eris led to an awkward situation at Spiral Mountain.[4]

Official description

Official description[]

Master thieves, the Ravens are out for one thing only, and that's a healthy profit. They are not prejudiced in who they steal from, targeting friends and foes alike in their quest for financial gain. Most of the other tribes agree that it's best to avoid the Ravens altogether - and if you don't, best check your pockets before leaving their company![5]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • A high majority of the Raven Tribe have prosthetics on their body, ranging from from small piercings or beak implants, to full scale peg-legs and eye patches like Rizzo.
