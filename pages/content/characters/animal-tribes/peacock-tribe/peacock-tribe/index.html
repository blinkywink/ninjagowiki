Overview

The Peacock Tribe is a tribe of humanoid peafowls from Chima.

History

History[]

Prior to the Great Illumination, the peacocks were normal animals that lived in Chima. However, following the Great Illumination, the peacocks transformed into more humanoid animals thanks to the Chi and started living with the other Tribes of Chima, forming the Peacock Tribe.

Legends of Chima, Season 1[]

Dom de la Woosh was a legendary peacock Speedor racer. Among his biggest fans were Laval and Cragger, who often watched his races together. When a Chi Day race with the biggest orb of Golden Chi as the prize was being held, Cragger secretly persuaded Dom de la Woosh to enter, hoping to improve his own chances of winning. Woosh agreed, mainly because he missed the excitement and attention that came with racing before large crowds.[1]

When Dom de la Woosh arrived at the competition, fans immediately surrounded him. Eris was particularly impressed and even asked him to sign her wing. Once the race began, Woosh performed daring maneuvers and quickly established himself as one of the leading racers. Laval, however, used a Chi orb to power his malfunctioning Speedor, allowing him to catch up with Dom de la Woosh. Surprised that Laval had managed to pass him, Dom de la Woosh continued the race. A mysterious racer entered and quickly overtook both Cragger and Dom de la Woosh. The newcomer ultimately won the race and was given the name ShadoWind.[1]

Afterward, Dom de la Woosh regretted his deal with Cragger. He revealed the truth to Laval and Eris, explaining his reasons for entering the race. Before returning to retirement, Dom de la Woosh praised Laval, declaring that he was one of the best racers he had ever seen. Eris, however, was furious that Dom de la Woosh had made such a deal with Cragger just for recognition.[1]

Days later, Dom de la Woosh joined the race of Laval, Cragger and their friends, getting ahead of everyone. Then, ShadoWind arrived, who lead them to the Spiral Mountain after the Woosh gave him the lead. Amused by the view of the Mount Cavora from the Spiral Mountain, the team decided to relax the rest of the day there and agreed to have a peace treaty for the Tribes of Chima, including Woosh.[2]

Legends of Chima, Season 3[]

On a flower hilltop, Dom de la Woosh giving flowers for his Speedorz, which he dubbed as his "true love". However, as he was kissing his Speedor, he got frozen by the Hunters, who were passing by the caverns. Seeing this, Stealthor gave report to Fangar, who said that they shall claim this trophy later,[3] eventually putting him into his trophy room in the Hunter Glacier.[4] Days later, the Heroes of Chima, Tormak and Flinx entered the Hunter Glacier, releasing the frozen inhabitants of Chima, including Dom de la Woosh, thanks to Flinx. Then, the Woosh left the ice fortress with the others before it melted completely. Later, Laval and the others returned the saved inhabitants back to their villages and Legend Beasts, including the Woosh.[5]

During the Second Great Illumination, Flinx released Dom de la Woosh's Speedor from ice, which the Woosh celebrated by giving it flowers and kissing it.[6]

Later, Dom de la Woosh joined the holiday celebrations, ice-skating in a frozen pond with other inhabitants of Chima.[7]

Trivia[]
