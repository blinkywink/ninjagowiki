Description

Description[]

The Lion Temple is a large temple serving as a base of operations for the Lion Tribe. It includes the Sacred Pool of CHI in the throne room which most of the formal meetings with other tribes and leaders occur. The "Age of Becoming" Ceremonies also occur in the temple near the pool.
