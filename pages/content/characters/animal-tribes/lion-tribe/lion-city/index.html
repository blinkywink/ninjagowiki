Lion City is a city, it is inhabited by the Lion Tribe. Its temple is the site of the Sacred Pool of CHI which produces the orbs of Chi which the Lions distribute equally every month to the Tribes of Chima.

Description

Description[]

The city is enclosed in a giant wall with watchtowers implanted in the front. In the middle of these watch towers is a gate shaped like a lions head which closes when a lever is pulled. There is an enormous set of stairs leading up to a bridge that reclines when the gate closes as a defense system. Buildings and houses are located on the left and right sides of the city. The right side houses the Chi Day Market for buying, selling and trading. The Lion Temple is located at the center of the city with a giant bridge connecting it to the gate. The Grand Arena is located outside of the city. The entire city is located at the foot of Mount Cavora.
