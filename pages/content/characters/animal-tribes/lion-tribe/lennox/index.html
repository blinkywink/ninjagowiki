Lennox is the vehicles expert of the Lion Tribe.

History

History[]

The Legend of Chima[]

Lennox was among the dozens of Lion warriors present at Laval's age of becoming ceremony, only to be called into battle when the "Rebel" tribes (Ravens, Crocs and Wolves) attacked the Lion Temple. Lennox, along with with other Lion soldiers, attempted to stop the army from stealing CHI, but failed as a large number of them, including Cragger (wearing the Crocodile Crown Helmet used by his father) broke into the Sacred Pool of Chi. Despite this, he could be seen driving around in a Royal Fighter tank as Laval chased after Cragger. In the flashback that takes up most of the episode, Lennox could seen during the battle at the Fangs driving his Lion Attack vehicle, having been summoned by LaGravis thanks to Cragger shooting a Croc Flare into the sky. Lennox could later be seen witnessing the loss of both Queen Crunket and King Crominous into the Gorge of Eternal Depth and steps down from his tank to see Laval comforting Cragger.

The Great Story[]

Not long after the supposed loss of Cragger's parents, a Speedor race was held for the Golden CHI, with the track chosen being the Joust of Jungle Judgement. During this, Lennox could be seen cheering for Laval when he defeated both Gorzan and Rogon, and was surprised when Worriz fell into a prickly bush. Much later, Lennox could be seen standing near the Sacred Pool when Laval noticed he lacked his harness and later was seen shooting at some crocodiles. When the Lion Legend Beast arrived at the temple with Laval, having saved him from drowning at Forever Rock, Lennox and the hundreds of others bowed in respect for the Legend Beast. After Cragger and his army retreated from the temple, Lennox, along with Bears, Eagles and Gorillas cheer for Laval becoming a real Lion warrior.

Attack on Eagle Spire[]

Lennox and the rest of the Lion Tribe drove out of the Lion Temple and off to the Raven Junkyard after Laval summoned them with a distress roar from atop the Eagle Spire. After arriving at the junkyard, Lennox hopped aboard one of the Raven Chi Raiders and flew towards the Eagle Spire, only to be ambushed by the Wolves and forced to fight. After the Spire started shaking thanks to the Crocodiles attempting to pull it down, Lennox and everyone else held on while wobbling and shaking around, before Razcal and some of the Ravens, who feared they would lose the Wolves if the Spire collapsed, shot and severed the chains. As the sun set, Lennox and the other Lions and Eagles prepared for another battle against the Wolves, only for the Mother Tooth to fall out of the sky and in-between Worriz and LaGravis. Lennox and his allies could only watch in confusion as the Wolves slid down the chains and back to their camp. Not long after that, Laval climbed down and Eris flew down from their hiding spot, and Laval proceeded to tell his dad, the Eagles, and the other Lions (including Lennox) the entire story in a motor-mouthed fashion.

Physical appearance

Physical appearance[]

Lennox has beige arms, torso, and legs, and white hands. His feet have markings of lion-like toes, and he wears a blue kilt with golden and black markings, and bands on his knees. Over his chest, he wears a blue medallion with an extra strap, in the center of which he can plug CHI. His chest markings are white and muscular-looking. Lennox has a spiky brown/red mane of hair with a dark blue or black border, his face is beige, and he has a white muzzle. His nose is black, and he has a small brown beard under his black-printed mouth. Lennox's eyes are orange.

Official description

Official description[]

LEGO.com[]

Always riding either a Speedor or his Lion Attack vehicle, Lennox is generally regarded as a freewheeling guy out for a good time. True, he drives the Lion Attack vehicle like there's no tomorrow, and anyone getting in the way of his rushing Speedor had better look out. But he has hidden depths, and actually knows just about everything there is to know about two and four wheeled vehicles. He might look like an easy mark to his enemies, but Lennox has a few surprises for them.[1]

Legends of Chima: Character Encyclopedia[]

Laidback Lennox likes nothing better than riding fast and free on his Speedor or Lion Attack vehicle. In fact, anything on wheels is comfortable ground for this mechanical whiz - he knows just about all there is to know about vehicles of all shapes and sizes. Lennox wears clothing similar to his fellow lion guards, but subtle differences make his gear perfectly suited for travelling super-fast: an extra strap secures his CHI medallion in place, particularly handy for racing through the forest at top speed. Enemies often underestimate Lennox, but the truth is this young Lion is both strong and skilled. He is a master of weapons and all types of vehicles - and is even unphased by driving in the Lions' speedboats on water! The adventurous and easy-going Lennox is the youngest foot soldier of the Lion Tribe.

Ultimate Sticker Collection: LEGO Legends of Chima[]

Lennox looks stern, but he is friendly - unless you are a Croc, a Raven or a Wolf!
