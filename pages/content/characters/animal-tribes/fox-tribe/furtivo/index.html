Furtivo (Furty for short) is a young member of the Fox Tribe.

History

History[]

Background[]

At a very young age, Furtivo became orphaned, which forced him to become a Nomad and learn taking care of himself.[1]

Season 1[]

Foxtrot[]

After Crooler wanted the Wolf Tribe to disrupt the monthly Speedor race, Worriz hired Furtivo. Shortly before the race started, Furtivo stole the racers' Speedor gear and stuff, putting them to different places and Speedorz, which causes tension between the tribes. Then he lies to Bladvic, saying that the Gorilla Tribe will get back on him by putting banana skins all over the track. Then he lies to Gorzan, saying the Bear Tribe will coat the track with honey to make it super sticky. Because of this, all of the tribes start arguing with each other. Then, Foxtroy goes to the Rhino Tribe, saying that everyone is cheating. However, the Rhinos find this cool, which surprises Furtivo. Then he tells Rogon that Eris has a crush on him, which excites Rogon. As he is about to tell Eris about his crush, Furtivo stops him and explains that this is a secret crush, saying that it's to keep her safe during the race. However, Rogon reassures him, saying that he will crush anyone who will try to crush his crush, which pleases Furtivo.

During the race, as the tribes started cheating because of Furtivo's lies, Furtivo arrived and asked Worriz how did he do, which in response Worriz said his work is supreme, expressing pleasement for hiring Furtivo. Hearing this, Furtivo tells him that this is his speciality and the best is yet to come. Then, he leaves the place.

Crocodile Tears[]

Since Laval won the Ravens' bootleg Speedor tournament, he and Eris demanded to see the ShadoWind, which forced Razar to hire Furtivo, who dressed up as the ShadoWind. Upon seeing him, Laval believed that he is the real ShadoWind and wanted him to help them save the Crocodile Tribe from the Wolf Camp, which in response Furtivo accepted. However, because of him speaking, Razar reminded him that the ShadoWind never speaks. Then Furtivo tried to start his Speedor to go the Wolf Camp, much to his struggle. Because of this, Eris got suspicious of him. While riding through the Sanctuary Forest, Eris told Laval about his suspicion about the "ShadoWind" and pointed out how different he is, but Laval continued to believe that he is the real ShadoWind.

After spying on the Wolf Tribe a little bit, Laval wanted the "ShadoWind" to go down the Wolf Camp and distract the Wolves. However, as he was driving his Speedor, he crashed and found himself in the Wolf Camp, which made the Wolf Tribe surround him. Seeing this, Eris saw his as a total fraud, while Laval insisted that this is brilliant.

The next day, Furtivo returned back to the Raven Town and complained to the Ravens that what makes them think that he would be a good ShadoWind. Razar explained him the situation by saying that no one can resist the ShadoWind merch. Then he gave him his part of the share and a bonus one for helping Laval. Furtivo said how the ShadoWind rules, which made the Ravens smile. Then he left the Raven Town as the real ShadoWind watched him.

Season 3[]

Prior to Cool and Collected[]

Furtivo was frozen by the Hunters and was taken to Sir Fangar's trophy room in the Hunter Glacier.

Cool and Collected[]

Furtivo is seen frozen in Sir Fangar's collection.

The Snowball Effect[]

Furtivo is seen frozen in Sir Fangar's trophy room.

A Very Slippery Slope[]

After finding the trophy room with the Heroes of Chima and Tormak, Flinx uses his heat to release the imprisoned inhabitants of Chima, including Furtivo. Then, Furty left the trophy room with the others and ran outside before the whole mountain melted. Later, Laval and the others returned the saved inhabitants back to their villages and Legend Beasts, including Furty.

Merry Chima Everyone![]

During Chima, Furtivo celebrated the holiday alongside Skinnet.

Official Description

Official Description[]

LEGO.com[]

A young orphan... VERY clever and street smart. He is his own boss, thinks of him first but always ends up doing the right thing because of a big heart. A clever little and charismatic thief.[2]

Lego® Legends of Chima: Official Guide[]

Furtivo is one of Chima's most cunning animals. He is said to be related to the Wolves, but he couldn't be more different from them. Furty's a loner. Orphaned at an early age, he quickly learned to take care of himself as his first priority.

Despite his self-centered nature, Furty seems so innocent that he's easily able to convince others to do what he wishes. He just gives them a little nudge in the right direction... and lets them do the rest. If he's found out, he's almost always forgiven - there's something so harmless about him that the other animals just can't stay mad.

Lone Ranger[]

Furty the Fox's quick fingers have a talent for stealing. It's rumored that he is an uncanny impressionist but his good heart usually keeps him from being too mean. Still, he can be convinced to play sneaky tricks- if the price is right.

Patched Up[]

Young orphan Furty has no one to look out for him. He gets by on his own. His patched-together outfit is travel-worn, but perfect for him. Layers of different fabric and loose neck tie ensure he;s equipped for any weather, terrain, or mission!

Fast Fox[]

Furty likes to keep to himself, but always turns up at the Speedor races, where he's easy to spot in his bright orange Speedor! Alternatively, he might be found causing trouble off the track.

Did you Know?[]

Furty the Fox is a distant cousin of those other canine troublemakers- the Wolves.
