The Fox Tribe is a tribe of humanoid foxes from Chima. They have close relations with the Wolf Tribe, as they are described as "cousins".[2]

Origins

Origins[]

Millennia ago, the foxes were normal animals that lived in Chima, being distant cousins of the wolves.[2] Following the Great Illumination the foxes transformed into more humanoid animals thanks to the Chi and started living with the other Tribes of Chima, forming the Fox Tribe.[3] However, at some point in time, Furtivo, a young member of the Fox Tribe became orphaned,[4] forcing him to become the only remaining member of his tribe.[2]

Legends of Chima, Season 1[]

After Crooler wanted the Wolf Tribe to help him steal all the Chi from the Sacred Pool of Chi in exchange of throwing the Pledge of the Pack into the Gorge of Eternal Depth, Worriz hired Furtivo, who stole the racers' stuff and lied to them, tricking them into cheating. During the race, as the tribes cheated, Furtivo came and told Worriz that the best is yet to come. Eventually, the tribes started to fight each other because of all the cheating, allowing the Wolf Tribe and Crooler to sneak into the Lion Temple and steal the Chi.[5] Because of this whole event, Lagravis cancelled the Chi Day races.[6]

After Laval arrived in the Raven Town to find the ShadoWind and save the Crocodile Tribe from the Wolf Tribe with his help, Razar promised to make him meet with the ShadoWind if he beats him in their bootleg Speedor race. After Laval defeated him, he and Eris wanted to meet with the ShadoWind, which forced Razar to hire Furtivo, who dressed up as the ShadoWind. Seeing him, Laval believed that he is the ShadoWind and wanted him to help them free the Crocodile Tribe, despite Eris' suspicions. After spying on the Wolf Tribe a little bit, Laval wanted Furtivo to go down there and distract the Wolves, which forced Furtivo to start driving his Speedor, only to crash and find himself in the Wolf Camp, distracting the Wolves.[7]

Legends of Chima, Season 3[]

After the Hunters were released from the Gorge of Eternal Depth, Furtivo was frozen by them and was taken to the Hunter Glacier, where he was displayed in Sir Fangar's trophy room.[8] Days later, the Heroes of Chima, Tormak and Flinx entered the Hunter Glacier and found the trophy room, releasing the frozen inhabitants of Chima, including Furtivo, thanks to Flinx. Then, Furty left the ice fortress with the others before it melted completely. Later, Laval and the others returned the saved inhabitants back to their villages and Legend Beasts, including Furty.[9]

Later, during Chima, Furtivo celebrated the holiday with Skinnet.[10]
