The Beaver Tribe is a tribes of humanoid beavers living in Chima.

History

History[]

Background[]

Before the Great Illumination, the beavers were big animals that lived in Chima.[1] However, following the Great Illumination, the beavers transformed into more humanoid animals thanks to the Chi and started living with the other Tribes of Chima, forming the Beaver Tribe.

Legends of Chima, Season 1[]

The Beaver Tribe had been hired by Cragger to construct a massive dam that would block the flow of Chi water and redirect it toward the Crocodile Swamp, depriving the Sacred Pool of its supply. While part of the tribe worked on the dam, Bezar and the remaining Beavers stayed behind at the Beaver Lodge.[2]

Meanwhile, after secretly taking Lagravis' Lion Tank on a road trip across Chima, Laval accidentally damaged the vehicle. Hoping to have it repaired before Lagravis returned, Eris brought Laval to the Beaver Lodge. As the two entered the village, they were surrounded menacingly by members of the Beaver Tribe emerging from the water. However, the Beavers' attitude changed instantly when they learned that Laval had brought them a damaged Lion Tank. The entire tribe became wildly excited at the prospect of repairing such a vehicle, and they eagerly set to work.[2]

Later, Laval and Eris discovered Cragger's plan to exploit the Beavers and steal Chima's Chi water. Before they could stop him, Crawley and Crug captured them and took their weapons. Fortunately, when the rest of the Beaver Tribe learned about the Lion Tank, they became even more excited. Eris used the Beavers' enthusiasm as a distraction, allowing her and Laval to retrieve their weapons from the Crocodiles and defeat them. She then encouraged the Beavers to return to the Beaver Lodge immediately so they could begin working on the damaged Lion Tank.[2]

Returning to the village, Beaver Tribe completed the repairs to Lagravis' Lion Tank. However, their enthusiasm for fixing the vehicle led them to make a few modifications, including the addition of a pair of wings. After the dam collapsed and the Chi water began flowing freely through Chima once again, the Beavers drove the Lion Tank back to the Lion Temple. However, Lagravis was stunned to discover that it now had wings.[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The only beaver minifigure reuse a yeti head piece instead of the headpiece initially intended for them, which corresponds to their design in the show, but that was never created, making the figure inaccurate.
  • While only the Beaver Tribe is seen, in "Child's Play," a gopher mascot with a beaver mask was seen in Buddy's Pizza, implying that regular beavers exist in Ninjago.
  • They are the most frequently appearing characters from Legends of Chima in Ninjago. They are also the only ones to appear in scenes that never appeared in Legends of Chima and were re-animated by WildBrain, as the M2Film animation shot was used in the scene in "Curseworld, Part II".
  • It is unknown how the beaver in "Ninjago Confidential" came to Ninjago. It's possible one remained in Ninjago after the beginning of "The Kaiju Protocol."
    • This beaver seemed a little wild and dangerous and stayed in the dark alleys, unlike other beavers.
      • It is unknown if this beaver is the same beaver as the one who appeared in "The Turn of the Tide".
  • They are mostly brown, with the exception of their leader, Bezar, who is white.
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, no beavers have appeared in Ninjago: Dragons Rising because the beaver asset was created for Unreal Engine 4 and cannot be utilized for Unreal Engine 5, the 3D graphics program currently used for the show, without being completely revamped which would be the same as creating a completely new asset.[3]
