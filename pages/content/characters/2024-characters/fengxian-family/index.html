Overview

“The Tournament has been run by my family for generations untold.”
— Bleckt to the ninja, "Enter the City of Temples"

The Fengxian family[2] is the family that manage the City of Temples. They are known for hosting the Tournament of the Sources to pay tribute to the Source Dragons.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Fengxian family is the second family to organize a tournament for Elemental Masters, following Skylor's family. However, chronologically, they are actually the first to host such a tournament.
  • It is suggested that the members serving as Games Masters in the tournament wear similar outfits, which sometimes include hand-me-downs. This is highlighted by Roby's comment about wearing identical clothing to Roake's.[3] Additionally, he sports the same cufflinks that belonged to his father when he held the position of Games Master.[4]
  • The family's surname shares a name with a district in Shanghai.
