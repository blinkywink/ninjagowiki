Overview

Scott McNeil (born September 15, 1962) is an Australian-Canadian voice actor who provides the voice of the Giant Stone Warrior, the Stone Warrior, the Overlord, Fangtom (Episode 30), Clouse, Karlof, Arcturus, Nadakhan, Tommy, the Ship Captain (Episode 59), and Chew Toy in Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He, along with Andrew Francis, Kathleen Barr, Trevor Devall, Lee Tockar, and Michael Dobson, appeared in LEGO's Bionicle: Mask of Light.
  • Scott McNeil has done voice work for many other series besides Ninjago. Some of his other most famous voice acting roles include Piccolo (Dragon Ball Z), Van Hohenheim (Full Metal Alchemist), Ali Al-Saachez (Mobile Suit Gundam 00), Orgon (Shakugan No Shana), Dinobot, Rattrap, Waspinator, and Silverbolt (Beast Wars), Grimian (Battle Force 5), Dr. Wily (Mega Man), Wolverine (X-Men Evolution), Stork (Storm Hawks), Mitchell "Bumper" Randalls, Mr. White, Zizrar (Johnny Test) and Shiki (One Piece).
    • He is one of eight voice actors to work on Johnny Test before working on Ninjago: the other seven were Trevor Devall, Richard Newman, Marÿke Hendrikse, Lee Tockar, Kathleen Barr, Ian James Corlett, and Andrew Francis.
  • He is one of five other actors who have worked on Tugs' spinoff Salty's Lighthouse to work on Ninjago, the other four being Ian James Corlett, Kirby Morrow, Michael Dobson, and Paul Dobson.
  • All of the characters he voices have fought against the ninja or the Elemental Masters before.
  • He has stated Karlof was originally a villain.[1]
  • He worked with Kirby Morrow on Ninja Turtles: The Next Mutation as a villain while Kirby played a hero.
  • He, along with Kathleen Barr, Pauline Newstone, and Maggie Blue O'Hara, voiced as characters in Grandma Got Run Over by a Reindeer.
  • McNeil's voice for the Overlord in Seasons 2 and 3 was almost identical to the voice he used for Dinobot in Beast Wars, another series for which he was a major cast member. This voice was deepened slightly in Season 10, and by Season 15, the Overlord's voice was at it's deepest.
