Petros Nicolaou is a British LEGO Ninjago set designer.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He is based in Denmark, previously in London.[6][7]
  • He worked alongside the design team for the production of Dragons Rising, and as of January 2024, is the newest set designer on the team.[8][2]
  • In 2017, he founded Brick by Brick Design, an independent design studio focused on making limited edition art piece out of LEGO.[6]
