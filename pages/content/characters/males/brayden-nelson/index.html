Overview

For the character of the same name, see Brayden Nelson (character).

Brayden Nelson, also known as NinjaWhip, is an American YouTuber[1][2] and LEGO Ambassador Network member.[2] He has appeared as a guest star in various Ninjago promotional videos, and partnered with LEGO to have a character based on him within the Ninjago universe.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Brayden is one of the four creators of the fan-made documentary, NINJAGO - Ten Years of Spinjitzu: A Documentary, created to celebrate Ninjago's 10 year anniversary.
  • Brayden, along with various other LEGO and Ninjago themed influencers, was given the first image in the #MergeTheFragments scavenger hunt (created to promote the initial release of Ninjago: Dragons Rising).
  • He attended the Crystalized Premiere in 2022.
