Overview

Christopher Lee, also known as DuckBricks, is an American YouTuber, entrepreneur and former LEGO Ambassador Network member.[1] He was the host of DuckBricks' Guide to Ninjago,[2] and has appeared as a guest star in various Ninjago promotional videos.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Christopher, along with various other LEGO and Ninjago themed influencers, was given the first image in the #MergeTheFragments scavenger hunt (created to promote the initial release of Ninjago: Dragons Rising).
  • He is not the only content creator to have hosted a Ninjago themed series on LEGO.com, as ParachutingKitten hosted her own series that released at the same time.
  • He shares a first name with Ninjago: Dragons Rising writer Chris "Doc" Wyatt.
    • As of May 27, 2025, this was Christopher's favorite trivia point on this page. He described it as "Absolutely LEGENDARY."[3]
  • Christopher, along with his friend and business partner Robert, were winners of LEGO Masters (USA) Season 4 in 2023.[4]
  • He attended the SDCC 2025 Ninjago panel.[5]
