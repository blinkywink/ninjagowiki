Security Mechs[1] (also known as Nindroid Mechs)[2] were surveillance mechs created by Borg Industries in order to protect New Ninjago City.

History

History[]

Prior to Rebooted[]

The Security Mechs were created by Borg Industries to aid the citizens of New Ninjago City, such as correcting traffic accidents.

The Surge[]

When a traffic accident occurred, Cole was trying to help out by lifting up the Postman's van, but one of the Security Mechs stopped him. Later, as with Borg's other creations, the mechs fell under the control of the Digital Overlord after he was reawakened within the Digiverse. They thus surrounded the ninja as they attempted to escape the city with the Techno Blades, only to be temporarily disabled by Lloyd's Golden Power. However, the energy ended up reactivating them, and the ninja were forced to attempt to escape the city yet again. Using his Techno Blade, Cole was able to hack one of the mechs, converting it into his Earth Mech.

The Art of the Silent Fist[]

The Overlord had the mechs who had absorbed Lloyd's energies brought to Cyrus Borg's lab in order to drain the Golden Power from them in hopes of creating a new body for himself; however, the trace amounts of energy proved insufficient for his purposes.

Blackout[]

Several Security Mechs could be seen at Ed & Edna's Scrap N Junk, Tech Wu struck one with his staff turning it on and grabbing Nya.

Enter the Digiverse[]

Due to having no power, a Security Mech was laying face-down on an isolated street in the city.

When the ninja purged the Digital Overlord from the Digiverse, a Security Mech that was about to strike a citizen became good once more and instead struck a Nindroid Warrior into the distance.

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Misfortune Rising[]

Security Mechs can be seen in Ed & Edna's Scrap N Junk

Operation Land Ho![]

When Jay fell out of the sky using the Traveler's Tea, he landed near one of the Security Mechs.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

Day of the Departed[]

Security Mechs were stored in Ed & Edna's Scrap N Junk.

Operation Heavy Metal: Blunck[]

Several inactive Security Mechs were at Ed & Edna's Scrap N Junk.
