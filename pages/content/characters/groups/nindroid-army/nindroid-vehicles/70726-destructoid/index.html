Overview

70726 Destructoid is a LEGO Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - Rebooted set released on January 1, 2014. It contains Techno Zane, the Blue Techno Blade, the Destructoid, General Cryptor, and Min-Droid.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Help Zane to escape the mighty mech arms and weapons of the Destructoid!

Zane faces an almighty battle to stop the Nindroids taking the precious Techno-Blade. From the 360-degree rotating command center of their menacing Destructoid mech, General Cryptor and Mindroid – the miniature Nindroid – activate the front chopping blades and prepare to attack with the powerful disc shooter and mighty mech arms with massive sword, shield and extendable spinning saw blades. Help the brave Ninja dodge the advancing Destructoid and escape to safety! Includes 3 minifigures with weapons and accessories: Zane, General Cryptor and Mindroid.

  • Includes 3 minifigures with weapons and accessories: Zane, General Cryptor and Mindroid
  • Features 360-degree rotating, armored command center, adjustable mech arms with giant sword, shield and extendable rotating saw blades, weapons storage, all-terrain treads, chopping front blades, front lasers and firing disc shooter
  • Weapons include translucent blue Techno-Blade for Zane, Nindroid swords and Nindroid Techno-Dagger sword
  • Accessories include General Cryptor’s armor
  • Place General Cryptor and the Mindroid in the command bay
  • Drive to activate the front chopping blades
  • Fire the disc shooter
  • Extend the spinning saw blade for extra danger
  • Measures over 3” (10cm) high, 7” (18cm) long and 9” (23cm) wide[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Min-Droid is exclusive to this set. However, no parts used on him exclusive because his mask and head are reused from regular Nindroid Warriors, his torso is reused from Nindroid Drones and he uses regular short black legs.
  • The front of the Destructoid's body features a silver Spinner Crown.
  • This set includes an undamaged version of Techno Zane.
    • Excluding books and promotional products, this set was the only way to get the undamaged version of Techno Zane.
  • This was a limited edition set.
  • All the minifigures in this set are Nindroids, making this the second set to do so, preceded by 70724 NinjaCopter and followed by 70639 Street Race of Snake Jaguar.
