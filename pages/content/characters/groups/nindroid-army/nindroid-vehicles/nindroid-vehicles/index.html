Overview

“Your Nindroids have failed, General Cryptor, but my next creation won't.”
— The Overlord, "The Art of the Silent Fist"

Nindroid Vehicles are the vehicles corrupted or designed by the Nindroids in Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu.

Some were designed by Cyrus Borg to protect New Ninjago City before the Digital Overlord corrupted them, whereas others were created to hunt down the ninja.

Security Mechs

Security Mechs[]

The Security Mechs were created by Borg Industries to aid the citizens of New Ninjago City, such as correcting traffic accidents.

After the Digital Overlord took over New Ninjago City, the Security Mechs aided him in taking over the city. It is the vehicle that Cole hacked in "The Surge" creating the Earth Mech.

Jet fighters

Jet fighters[]

Jet fighters were small surveillance jets created by Borg Industries to protect the citizens of New Ninjago City from threats.

The Jet Fighters were hacked by the Digital Overlord and scouted the skies of New Nnjago City for the ninja. Kai hacked a Jet Fighter creating the Kai Fighter, and was tailed by other Jet Fighters soon thereafter.

At Ed & Edna's Scrap N Junk, Tech Wu struck one with his staff turning it on before taking off and flying away.

Security tanks

Security tanks[]

Security tanks were surveillance tanks created by Borg Industries in order to protect New Ninjago City.

The Digital Overlord sent tanks after the ninja, with Jay hacking one and turning it into the Lightning off-roader.

Deactivated tanks later resurfaced as scrap at Ed & Edna's Scrap N Junk.

Hover-Copter

Hover-Copter[]

Hover-Copters were aerial surveillance drones created by Borg Industries to protect New Ninjago City.

The Hover Copters were used by the forces of the Digital Overlord to scan for the ninja and were also employed for transportation purposes. When discovering how the Techno Blades work, Zane transformed a Hover Copter into the Ninja Copter.

Hover Hunters

Hover Hunters[]

The Hover Hunters are Nindroid vehicles.

They are used for transportation and clearing paths with a sawblade on their underside. Tech Wu used one while hunting for Garmadon and Lloyd.

Set appearances[]

  • 70720 Hover Hunter
Attack gliders

Attack gliders[]

The attack gliders were Nindroid vehicles.

Attack Gliders can mount on the Nindroids' backs and fold outward with their wingspan. They were used to ambush the ninja when they attacked Garmadon's monastery.

Set appearances[]

  • 70724 NinjaCopter
  • 71737 X-1 Ninja Charger (Legacy)
Nindroid MechDragon

Nindroid MechDragon[]

The Nindroid MechDragon is a large Dragon Mech created by the Digital Overlord.

The MechDragon's objective is to capture Lloyd and steal his Golden Power. It was deactivated when the ninja shut off the power, but once power was restored, Pythor and the MechDragon captured him in Hiroshi's Labyrinth.

Set appearances[]

  • 70725 Nindroid MechDragon
Destructoid

Destructoid[]

The Destructoid is a large Nindroid machine used by General Cryptor.

The Destructoid destroyed the ninja's vehicles in Art of the Silent Fist and was also used to break into Borg Tower to uncover and cut communication ties belonging to the ninja.

Nya later took control of the Destructoid and used it to protect the Temple of Fortitude.

Set appearances[]

  • 70726 Destructoid
Disc flyers

Disc flyers[]

The disc flyers are Nindroid vehicles that resemble a smaller Hover Hunter.

Disc flyers were deployed from Nindroid Driver's Troop Transport to attack the Prototype X-1.

Set appearances[]

  • 70727 X-1 Ninja Charger
Nindroid Troop Transport

Nindroid Troop Transport[]

The Nindroid Troop transport was created for Transportation of Nindroid Warriors

The Nindroid Troop transport was piloted by the Nindroid Driver and was on its way to Ouroboros before being destroyed.

Arcturus

Arcturus[]

Arcturus was a rocket created by Cyrus Borg for the Nindroid Army. It is named after the late Anacondrai general, Arcturus.

Arcturus was commanded by General Cryptor and piloted by the Nindroid Pilot, to claim the Golden Weapons on Delta V.

Lunar rovers

Lunar rovers[]

The lunar rovers were vehicles used by the ninja and the Nindroids in outer space.

In The Void the Ninja and Nindroids used lunar rovers were used to get the remnants of the Golden Weapons and also to return to the Arcturus.

Overlord Mech

Overlord Mech[]

The Overlord Mech was a mech that was created from the rema of the Golden Weapons that were recovered by the Nindroids.

The Overlord Mech was used to take over New Ninjago City and produce a huge web system outside Borg Tower prior to the Overlord's defeat.

Set appearances[]

  • 70728 Battle for Ninjago City
Nindroid jet fighters

Nindroid jet fighters[]

The Nindroid jet fighters are Nindroid vehicles that are currently inactive.

Nindroid jet fighters never saw usage. They can be seen as scrap at Ed & Edna's Scrap N Junk.

Set appearances[]

  • 70724 NinjaCopter
Nindroids' jetpacks

Nindroids' jetpacks[]

The Nindroids' jetpacks were vehicles used to attack Zane during the Day of the Departed.

On the Day of the Departed, General Cryptor sent Nindroids to attack Zane, who destroyed them using shurikens.

Set appearances[]

  • 70588 Titanium Ninja Tumbler
  • 40374 Golden Zane Minifigure Accessory Set
Dark Mechs

Dark Mechs[]

The Dark Mechs are a set of mech suits that were created for the Nindroid Army.

At an abandoned factory in the Sea of Sand, a small group of Nindroids worked to construct a series of powerful mech suits powered by Electro-Cobrai. It's unknown if the Dark Mechs are canon or not.
