Overview

“Get to the police station. Something has happened.”
— Wu to Jay and Nya, "The Calm Before the Storm"

The Ninjago City Police Station is the headquarters of the Ninjago Police, located in Ninjago City.

History

History[]

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Skybound[]

Public Enemy Number One[]

The Police Commissioner holds a press conference in front of the station on the sightings of the ninja after they're proclaimed public enemy number one as well as confiscated all of their vehicles. He soon went inside where he hires Ronin to capture the ninja. After Ronin succeeds, he holds another conference while the officers escort the ninja on a prison bus.

Misfortune Rising[]

In order to locate Misfortune's Keep, Jay and Nya snuck into the Police Station to use their computer. At the same time, the Police Commissioner was present organizing a search for the ninja.

Operation Land Ho![]

Jay and the Ninja Replacements gathered together in the Police Station to prepare and develop a plan for infiltrating Djinjago's reconstruction.

The Way Back[]

As time began to rewind as a result of Jay's final wish, he and saw a glimpse of the moment where Nya told him outside the police station that they would only ever be friends.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

Sons of Garmadon[]

The Oni and the Dragon[]

Cole and Zane enter the police station in order to interrogate Luke Cunningham.

Dread on Arrival[]

The ninja and P.I.X.A.L. arrive at the police station for assistance against the Sons of Garmadon from the Commissioner, who after some confusion decided to help.

True Potential[]

When Harumi was arrested, she was brought to the interrogation room, where she remains silent before a stolen garbage truck rammed the front of the station. The police were all shocked to see it was Garmadon before he injured and maybe killed many officers so he could break Harumi out. The Commissioner told the ninja about the attack as the officers were cleaning up.

March of the Oni[]

The Darkness Comes[]

In his office, Commissioner paints a model boat before Jimmy barges in, telling him about their phones ringing off the hook about a black cloud, and see watch the officers taking them.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Questing for Quests[]

In need of a quest, the ninja decide to help the Ninjago Police and spend the day on nothing but false leads, rescuing cats out of trees, repairing faults, cleaning, and sorting out files, until they eventually give up.

Prime Empire[]

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

The Mechanic is taken into custody and is brought into the Ninjago City Police Station.

Dyer Island[]

The ninja went there to question the Mechanic about Unagami and the sudden disappearance of Jay.

Seabound[]

The Calm Before the Storm[]

Master Wu, Jay, Zane, Nya, and Benthomaar visit Glutinous, who turned himself in to warn them about Kalmaar awakening Wojira.

Nyad[]

Kalmaar and his forces stormed the police station in search of information about the ninja's whereabouts.

The Turn of the Tide[]

After Nya become one with the Endless Sea, the ninja grappled up to the roof of the Police Station to observe her battle with Kalmaar. There, they would also fight off Gripe and his platoon of Maaray Guards, remaining on top of the roof until Nya commanded the waters to recede from the flooded city.

Crystalized[]

Hounddog McBrag[]

The Police Commissioner was working on his model ship when he was interrupted by a phone call from Mayor Ulysses Trustable. When the Mayor ordered the Commissioner to chase after the ninja, who had recently escaped Kryptarium Prison, the Commissioner stated that he was unable to do so, for Kryptarium was outside of his jurisdiction.

Official description

Official description[]

The NINJAGO City Police Station is the headquarters for the Commissioner of Police. From here, he oversees the safety and security of the city and its people. This is also the site of the NINJAGO Police archive (the password to get into it is PASSWORD).[1]
