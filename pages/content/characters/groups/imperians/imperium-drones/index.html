Overview

Not to be confused with Imperium Droids.
“Drones attack!”
— Dr. LaRow, "Land of Lost Things"

The Imperium Drones are weapon drones created by Dr. LaRow. They were used by her and the Claws of Imperium on missions as well as inside Imperium by the Imperium Guards and later by Dorama in his academy.

History

History[]

Absolute Power (flashback)[]

Drones pushed over Levo's statue after Beatrix became Empress, and put up the latter's statue in place of the former's.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

A Claw Hunter deployed two Imperium Drones to chase after Arin and Sora. The drones began shooting at Arin, who intended to use Spinjitzu. Although he did, he instead ended up knocking one Imperium Drone against the other, and then a hover chariot, resulting in an explosion.

The Merge: Part 2[]

Several Imperium Drones were used to tie up the Matriarch of the Mountain Dragons as the Claws of Imperium attempt to capture her. Fortunately, Lloyd used dragon-powered Spinjitzu to free the Matriarch, knocking away the Imperium Drones.

Writers of Destiny[]

The Imperium Drones were used to carry the captive Ice Dragons to Imperium.

Return to Imperium[]

When the ninja arrived to save Jiro, Rapton called for a fleet of drones. The drones lifted Jiro while the ninja were fighting a Photac. Later, some Imperium Drones were seen attacking Arin, Lloyd, and Sora in the Imperium Sewers.

Mindless Beasts[]

The Imperium Guards used three drones to chase Arin and Riyu around Imperium. Later, Rapton brought two drones with him to trap Sora and her dragon.

I Will Be the Danger[]

Rapton summoned an entire fleet of drones to create a barrier against the escaped dragons. Unfortunately, the dragons easily crashed through them. As the dragons journeyed through the city, Wyldfyre and Lloyd used their powers to destroy any drones in their way.

The Calm Inside[]

When Beatrix and Ras exited the palace to oversee the battle against the attacking dragons, they brought a drone with them.

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

Imperium Drones were utilized during the Battle of the Second Monastery.

Land of Lost Things[]

As Nya and Cole searched for parts, Imperium drones attacked Jiro. Nya saved him while Cole defended them from the Hoarder. Dr. LaRow had upgraded the drones with force field projectors and captured Zanth. However, Jiro freed her, allowing her to fight against the Hoarder. Dr. LaRow later escaped with the Yellow Dragon Core using the field projector of the Imperium Drones as a floor to stand on.

Absolute Power[]

Three drones gave the three dragon cores to Dr. LaRow to put into the MergeQuake weapon to start the test. After an explosion caused by a fake core, some drones were seen helping to clean up the wreckage.

We Are All Dragons[]

Inert drones were mounted against several hover chariots deployed to combat the ninja with.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

To Mysterium[]

Dorama used some Imperium Drones, that he modified with his color scheme, to chase after the escaped prisoners but were easily destroyed by Jiro, Cole, and Zane.
