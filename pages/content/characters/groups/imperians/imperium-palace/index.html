The Imperium Palace is where the Royal Family of Imperium resided.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Beyond Madness[]

At the Imperium Palace, Ras, accompanied by Imperium Guards, made his way into the Advanced Systems Lab, seeking clarity regarding the Photac. Dr. LaRow conceded that although Dorama could repair the original Photac, Sora was the only one who could activate its copies. Overcome with frustration at the unsatisfactory response, Ras abruptly exited the laboratory.

Return to Imperium[]

Sora infiltrated the Imperium Palace with the intention of reaching the Advanced Systems Lab, where she planned to permanently shut down the Photac. Nevertheless, her mission was interrupted when she ran into Dr. LaRow.

Mindless Beasts[]

At the Advanced Systems Lab, Dr. LaRow tried to convince Sora into making improvements to the Photacs, but Sora instead used the device to incapacitate her and Dorama. She then tried to escape with Zanth but after running into many dead-ends, the pair were recaptured by Rapton and returned to the lab.

I Will Be the Danger[]

Percival successfully captured Riyu and began transporting him on his hoverboard towards the Imperium Palace, where he intended to hand over Riyu to Beatrix. Within the palace, the Claws of Imperium were equipped with Photacs and rushed out to deal with a group of dragons who were trying to escape the city. The Claws confronted the dragons and their ninja allies outside the palace, initiating a full out battle to begin. Sora and Zanth also managed to escape from the palace and joined their allies in the battle unfolding outside.

The Calm Inside[]

Arin and Sora surreptitiously infiltrated the palace premises, where they found Riyu, on the brink of having his dragon energy drained by Dr. LaRow. After a long struggle fighting with the Photacs, Lloyd proposed a strategic assault on the Imperium Palace, a proposition that garnered agreement from Wyldfyre. Without delay, Lloyd, Wyldfyre, and the dragons initiated a relentless attack against the Imperium Palace, giving Arin and Sora time to save Riyu, only for them to be caught by Jordana.

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

Sora and Arin were held at gunpoint by Jordana and Dr. LawRow. The two managed, however, to disarm both scientists, prompting LaRow to flee and to find Ras for assistance. Ras then confronted the teenagers within the palace, effortlessly tossing them around like ragdolls with little focus or attention towards his surroundings. This came to bite Ras when Sora was thrown against the control panel for Riyu's containment cage, allowing her to deactivate it and to receive a spark. Sora then dismantled Riyu's cage and buried Ras beneath the pieces before she and Arin fled from the palace.

Following the Battle of the Second Monastery, Ras, Rapton, and two Claw Hunters kneeled before Empress Beatrix and reported to her on the aftermath of the battle. Displeased with Ras' performance, the Empress had him stripped of his title and imprisoned before declaring that she would now take the fight directly to the ninja for their humiliation.

The Temple of the Dragon Cores[]

Days following the Battle of the Second Monastery, Empress Beatrix broadcasted a message to her citizens from her throne room, insisting that any and all rumors of escaped dragons and power shortages were false. In fact, she also denounced anyone who would entertain such rumors, branding them as traitors. When the broadcast finished, Beatrix asked Dr. LaRow about the current status of the kingdom, to which the latter replied that they have faced tremendous power shortages following the battle. Frustrated by this news, Beatrix placed blame on the ninja and swore vengeance.

Eventually, Rapton reported to Beatrix about the existence of the Dragon Cores and how they had the power to control the MergeQuakes. Pleased by this discovery, Beatrix vowed to use their power to crush the ninja once and for all.

Gangs of the Sea[]

In the palace hanger, Rapton snickered to himself while watching security footage of Ras inside his cell. At that moment, Empress Beatrix entered the hanger and ordered for Rapton and the Claw Hunters to begin searching for the Dragon Cores.

Land of Lost Things[]

Empress Beatrix was seated on her throne when she received a report from Dr. LawRow about the latter's acquisition of the Yellow Dragon Core.

Absolute Power[]

Empress Beatrix summoned Dorama, Dr. LaRow, and Jordana to her throne room and ordered them to begin an immediate test of the newly developed MergeQuake weapon. Unfortunately, one of the dragon cores turned out to be a counterfeit, resulting in a massive explosion that burned a hole through the palace roof.

We Are All Dragons[]

Lloyd, Nya, and Zane made their way to the palace to retrieve the two Dragon Cores held by Imperium. Although they were able to reach the palace gates, they were cut off from entry by Empress Beatrix who had retrieved the now functioning MergeQuake Weapon to use against them.

The Power Within[]

Empress Beatrix briefly fought the ninja in front of the palace before rampaging across the City of Imperium.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Vault of Focus[]

Kai, Frak and Percival arrived in the Advanced System Labs, where they turned two of the Prismatic Blades into Reveal Blades with the help of the Imperian scientists.
