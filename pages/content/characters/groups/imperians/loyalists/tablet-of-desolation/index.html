The Tablet of Desolation is a stone tablet containing incantations written by the First Spinjitzu Master that can split and combine the Weapon of Desolation. It was found by Dr. LaRow in the Land of Eternal Dawn and used to recreate the Weapon for Empress Beatrix.

History

History[]

Background[]

The tablet was created by the First Spinjitzu Master to use in splitting the Weapon of Desolation into two fragments. He would later hide the tablet in the Realm of Eternal Dawn for no one to find.[1]

Prior to Dragons Rising Season 4[]

Sometime after the Liberation of Imperium, Dr. LaRow traveled to the Land of Eternal Dawn, where she learned about the existence of the Weapon of Desolation when she discovered the tablet.[2]

Dying to Live[]

Dr. LaRow showed the tablet to Beatrix, explaining that while she doubted the Weapon of Desolation was supernatural, its power is real and can be restored by reciting the incantations on the tablet backwards. Later, when both pieces of the weapon were brought to them, LaRow read the incantations to fix the whole Weapon of Desolation, granting Beatrix command of the Elemental Monsters.

Birthright[]

During the Loyalist assault, the ninja retrieved the Weapon of Desolation from Beatrix. Cole remembered the last word of the incantations from the tablet and said it to Arin, who used it to seal all the Elemental Monsters back into the weapon.
