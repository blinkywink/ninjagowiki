Overview

“Search your assigned regions! Find the Weapon! Let nothing stand in your way!”
— Chief Loyalist, "The Whispering Wood"

The Quest for Desolation[1] was an event in which the Loyalists searched the Merged Lands to obtain the halves of the Weapon of Desolation.
