Overview

“Fools... The mortal I possess has his limitations, but did you really believe you could defeat a Primordial Artifact of Dawn by throwing me into a pit?”
— The Weapon of Desolation to the ninja through Zilvar, "Dangerous Connections"

The Weapon of Desolation was a primordial, sentient, and dangerous scepter of cosmic power created from the essence of Ras' master. It was capable of affecting, manipulating, and corrupting Elemental Powers by controlling imbalanced and unstable Source Energy.

As a result of the Battle of the Spectral Lands, the Weapon’s energy spread across the Merged Lands, creating Elemental Monsters that rampaged throughout the lands. The Loyalists then planned to find and recombine the two pieces of the weapon in order to control the Elemental Monsters. The weapon was then used by Beatrix Vespasian-Orus to lead an army of Elemental Monsters in an assault to reclaim control of the City of Imperium, which ultimately failed when Arin retrieved the weapon and disabled it.

When the ninja had an encounter with a Mutation Monster, they realized the Weapon must be destroyed. After consulting with Reginald, they traveled to the Abyss of Eternal Night and during their journey the ninja received help from Zilvar, who was capable of carrying the weapon safely. However, under Ras' influence and motivated by his desire to restore his deceased tribe, Zilvar became corrupted by the weapon and ended up battling the ninja.

Finally, Lloyd attempted to stabilize the Weapon of Desolation, successfully freeing Zilvar from its trance. However, this caused Lloyd's mortal body to explode while the Weapon of Desolation fell into the abyss, never to be reclaimed or fall into the wrong hands ever again.

History

History[]

Background[]

The Weapon of Desolation was created long ago from the essence of Ras' master,[2] tied to the very creation of the realms.[3] It was later divided into two parts by the First Spinjitzu Master, who used an incantation he inscribed on the Tablet of Desolation. He then concealed both halves in two distinct locations: the yellow fragment in the Tree of Madness at the Realm of Madness, and the red fragment in the Tree of Burdens at Umbrasilvis.[4][5]

Millennia later, Dr. LaRow would eventually learn about the Weapon of Desolation's existence when she discovered the tablet in the Land of Eternal Dawn following Beatrix's defeat. The withdrawal of half the Source Dragons from the Council of Sources caused both fragments of the Weapon of Desolation to conjure Elemental Monsters throughout the Merged Lands. Realizing this, LaRow concluded that the Weapon could be employed to control the Monsters and transform them into an army to assist the Loyalists with their effort to reinstate Empress Beatrix to her throne.[4][5]

The Whispering Wood[]

The fragments of the Weapon of Desolation conjured an Acid Monster and Earth Monster at the Shrine of the Seven Sources when the disrupted Source Energy of the shrine transmuted the water in the shrine to acid and the surrounding earth to asphalt. Both monsters then attacked the Source Dragon acolytes as the Chief Loyalist watched.

Later during the meeting with the Loyalists, the Chief Loyalist presented a hologram of the Weapon of Desolation, elucidating its connection to the Elemental Monsters. She explained that these creatures emerged from the imbalance of Source Energy associated with the weapon. Following the explanation, she urged them to trace back the Elemental Monsters to find the weapon's location.

Prior to The Lean of a Tree[]

The fragments of the Weapon of Desolation conjured a Mutation Monster in Umbrasilvis which the Explorer's Club dispatched a team led by Reginald Slitherbottom to capture, a team undercover Loyalist Johnny Courageous joined to help locate the Weapon of Desolation.

Tonight We Hunt Monsters[]

The Loyalists were having difficulty locating the Weapon of Desolation. The Chief Loyalist reprimanded them, projecting a hologram of the weapon and emphasizing that it should emit significant energy whenever it summoned an Elemental Monster, energy that should be detectable. The Chief Loyalist then approached the hologram of the Weapon of Desolation, determined to find it and use its power to create Elemental Monsters for their own use.

Later, the fragments of the Weapon of Desolation conjured a Mutation Monster alongside an Acid Monster and two Earth Monsters in the Whispering Wood, which attacked Lloyd, Arin, and Euphrasia.

A Wonder of the Merged Lands[]

The fragments of the Weapon of Desolation conjured a Mutation Monster in the City of Imperium which attacked the Imperians.

Seed and Soil[]

The fragments of the Weapon of Desolation conjured a Fire Monster in the Crossroads which shot fireballs at various targets before teleporting away in a bolt of Source Energy.

Following the Mutation Monster's disappearance, the Grand Inquisitor contacted the Chief Loyalist to inform her of the whereabouts of the Weapon of Desolation. He subsequently transmitted the relevant data. Seeing this, the Chief Loyalist, unmasking herself as Dr. LaRow, informed Beatrix that the weapon had been divided into two parts. However, she assured Beatrix that they would successfully reunite the pieces, a promise that instilled determination in the cyborg.

Just Under the Surface[]

The fragments of the Weapon of Desolation conjured an Elemental Monster in the Land of Madness which the Loyalists used to determine the approximate location of one of them.

After finding the Tree of Madness with the other Loyalists, the Lead Loyalist scanned the tree to see if one of the fragments is there. After putting her hand in the tree's core, she got the fragment, which the other Loyalists cheered about. When the ninja accidentally revealed themselves, the Lead Loyalist tried to escape with the weapon piece, only for Kai to touch it and release a big explosion, which caused her to drop the fragment. Fortunately, she still escaped with the yellow fragment alongside the other Loyalists.

The Grand Inquisition[]

As the ninja escaped from the cave, they saw Loyalists escaping with the yellow fragment.

While Sora and Frak were hiding near the Tree of Burdens, a Loyalist arrived, scanning the tree and retrieved the red fragment from the Weapon of Desolation. However, Frak's sudden interference startled the Loyalist, prompting him to flee in haste. Reginald Slitherbottom leaped onto the Loyalist, unmasking him as Johnny Courageous and causing the fragment to fall from his grasp. Seizing the opportunity, Frak caught the fragment, which surged with Source Energy, resulting in a powerful Quake that reverberated through the surroundings. Reginald carefully took the fragment from Frak, wrapping it in a handkerchief he had received from the Pontifex Magicus. He then gave it to Sora, despite hesitating a little bit.

Later, the two made their way to the Inquisition, presenting the red fragment to the Grand Inquisitor as evidence of Johnny's wrongdoing. However, as the Grand Inquisitor grabbed the fragment, he declared that the next course of action was to deliver it to Beatrix, a revelation that left everyone in shock as he unveiled his allegiance to the Loyalists. As the ninja charged towards him, Johnny activated a concealed button on the Inquisitor's cane, releasing a cloud of smoke that allowed both of them to escape.

Dying to Live[]

While LaRow and Beatrix discussed the Tablet of Desolation, the Lead Loyalist and two others approached, presenting them with the yellow fragment of the Weapon of Desolation. After dismissing her, LaRow handed the fragment to Beatrix, who examined it.

Subsequently, the Loyalists placed the fragment into a force field chamber. However, Cole managed to escape and battled the Loyalists. During the fight, he hurled one of the Loyalists into the force field chamber, resulting in a malfunction that caused the fragment to fall out. Cole seized the fragment, which enhanced his golem form, and continued to battle the Loyalists. However, Beatrix soon intervened, defeating Cole and reclaiming the fragment, which subsequently destroyed his golem exoskeleton. Later, the Grand Inquisitor and two other Loyalists arrived with the red fragment. The Loyalists held both fragments while Dr. LaRow recited the incantations from the tablet, causing the fragments to float and ultimately combine into the Weapon of Desolation. With the weapon now reassembled, Beatrix declared her intent to lead them to glory.

The Heartbeat of all the Realms[]

Beatrix used the Weapon of Desolation to create an army of Elemental Monsters, which marched toward the City of Imperium. Despite Lloyd’s completion of the Ritual of Unrooting, the weapon's manipulation of imbalanced Source Energy caused the Whispering Wood and its creatures to continue withering as the weapon drained its Life Energy. Determined to restore life to the Wood, Lloyd planned to deactivate the weapon. Meanwhile, Cole managed to break free from his restraints and reached out to the Weapon of Desolation, channeling its power to restore his enhanced Golem form. He attempted to confront Beatrix, but she commanded the Elemental Monsters to attack him and the ninja as they advanced on the city.

Birthright[]

Lloyd and Beatrix fought over the Weapon of Desolation. Lloyd managed to knock the weapon from Beatrix's grasp, but she reclaimed it. Despite Lloyd's warnings about the weapon's potential, Beatrix was confident in her understanding of its power. Using his Draconus Form, Lloyd effortlessly defeated countless Elemental Monsters. Recognizing the strength of Lloyd's transformation, Beatrix used the Weapon of Desolation to empower two Earth Monsters and a Mutation Monster, enhancing their size and power. However, as she continued to use the weapon, it began to exert its influence over her, leaving Dr. LaRow and the Loyalists astonished by its overwhelming capabilities. LaRow questioned whether Beatrix was still in control.

Beatrix further empowered the Elemental Monsters, which soon began to overpower the ninja. Lloyd activated Nya, Kai, Jay, and Zane's own Draconus Forms to turn the tide of battle, enabling them to battle the monsters. Lloyd knocked the Weapon of Desolation from Beatrix's hands, which caused the Elemental Monsters to turn against her. Arin retrieved the weapon with Euphrasia's help and recited the incantation to deactivate it. As a result, the Elemental Monsters were sealed away within the scepter, ultimately saving the Whispering Wood. Following the arrest of Beatrix and several Loyalists, the ninja and Percival Tartigrade interrogated Dr. LaRow about the Weapon, the former scientist believing the scepter was controlling Beatrix.

Troublesome Transport[]

As the City of Imperium was working to recover from the Loyalist assault, Percival asked the ninja what they were planning to do with the Weapon, with Lloyd responding that they will take the artifact to the Monastery of Spinjitzu, where it would be safe. As they were speaking, some undercover Loyalists revealed themselves and attacked the ninja, only to get overpowered by them. Sora reminded everyone that they need to transport the Weapon of Desolation safely. As if on cue, an Imperian citizen approached the ninja to offer his services, with the latter group unaware of the man's true loyalties.

The man showed the ninja their Luxury Cruiser, which he offered to them as a sign of gratitude for stopping Beatrix's assault. Accepting the man's offer, the ninja set off for the Monastery of Spinjitzu, while Arin and Frak left the Weapon of Desolation in the vehicle's cargo hold, despite Frak being a little hesitant at first. However, as they left, the Loyalists who were hiding in the cargo hold got out of their spots and obtained the Weapon of Desolation, radioing their success to their leader, the man who gave the ninja the Luxury Cruiser. The Loyalist commanded them to activate their position transmitters, saying that they will get on their way to the Mobile Lab immediately. However, shortly after the Loyalists leaved the vehicle and went to their Mobile Lab, the ninja realized they have been tricked. Then, Lloyd transformed himself and the older ninja into their Draconus Forms and set out to hunt down the Loyalists.

As Lloyd spotted the Mobile Lab, the Loyalists activated the Weapon of Desolation and channeled its Source Energy through the Photacs, making indestructible Elemental Monsters. After realizing that the Loyalists have given the creatures a Photac upgrade, Lloyd ordered Riyu to go and bring Sora. After Riyu brought the Master of Technology, Lloyd explained to her that the creatures they’re facing are Elemental Monster/Photac hybrids, meaning she has to disable their Photac component. As Sora turned the monsters into normal Elemental Monsters, the ninja destroyed them. Seeing this, the Loyalists panicked and ran away, leaving the Weapon of Desolation behind. However, Cole then asked how they will take the Weapon with them without risking their Elemental Powers. Realizing this, Lloyd asked Riyu if he could fly back and get Arin to carry the Weapon, much to Riyu's annoyance.

Prior to Monster in the Monastery[]

The ninja brought the Weapon of Desolation to the Monastery of Spinjitzu, where they put the weapon in a containment unit in the hangar bay.

Monster in the Monastery[]

While playing a video game, Kai, Cole and Jay accidentally knocked the Weapon of Desolation out of its containment unit, causing the artifact to activate by itself and create a Mutation Monster.

After the Mutation Monster crashed the ninja's dinner, Arin went to the hangar bay and got the weapon. Then, he returned back to the courtyard and used it to suck the monster back into the weapon. Arin told Lloyd that how the weapon's containment unit was busted open. Hearing this and seeing Jay, Kai, and Cole in Draconus Form, Lloyd realized that it was their fault.

After learning about the Abyss of Eternal Night, the ninja took the Weapon of Desolation with them to the Never Kingdoms to destroy it, with them keeping the scepter in a containment unit in the Land Bounty.

The Mech-Master 10,000[]

As the ninja passed by the Land of Madness, the Land Bounty shook because of the rocky road, causing the Weapon of Desolation to rattle inside the containment unit, which slightly worried Lloyd and Zane.

Prior to In the Pits[]

Word got out that the ninja had the Weapon of Desolation in their possession aboard the Land Bounty.[6]

In the Pits[]

When metal thieves attacked the Land Bounty to steal the Weapon of Desolation, one broke in and tried to grab it but was stopped by Jay.

Later, when Kai and Jay wanted to free the captured gladiators from the gladiator pit, Cole countered by saying that they have to deal with the Weapon of Desolation. Seeing this, the weapon influenced the conversation,[7] which led to an argument between them. Thankfully, Lloyd calmed everyone down before the argument escalated, causing the weapon to get deactivated again.

A Frozen Scar[]

As the ninja and Zilvar progressed on their quest toward the Never Kingdoms, they noticed the Weapon of Desolation was beginning to react as its containment unit was deteriorating. The imbalanced Source Energy within the weapon was leaking, trying to break free, which led the ninja to ponder the idea that the weapon might be sentient. Kai tried to dismiss this notion, but Lloyd was resolute in his belief that they needed to dispose of the weapon before it could retaliate.

Upon reaching the Never Kingdoms, Beniro helped the ninja on their journey to the abyss while Zilvar opted to remain at the Monastery. However, their journey was interrupted when the Weapon of Desolation accumulated a surge of unstable Source Energy, which it unleashed in a chaotic amount.

When they arrived at the Boreal River, the weapon activated once more, corrupting Beniro and transforming him into a wild, feral version of his fox form. Beniro seized the weapon and rushed outside, where he hurled it into the river, summoning a swarm of Ice Monsters that began to attack the ninja. Zane struggled to retrieve the weapon just as Beniro charged toward him. Fortunately, Zilvar and Grimtak intervened, managing to push Beniro back. Zilvar then secured the weapon, allowing Zane to restore the Boreal River while Beniro was freed from the corruption. Zilvar was about to explain to the ninja why he came back, but the Weapon of Desolation immediately began to worm its way into Zilvar's head, disguising itself as his thoughts & compelling him to convince himself that destroying the Weapon was not an option. This manipulation was rooted in the belief that Ras could revive the Tiger Tribe if the Weapon remained intact, leading Zilvar to deceive the ninja. With the containment unit now destroyed and Beniro unable to continue with the team, the ninja reluctantly assigned Zilvar to hold onto the Weapon of Desolation as they continued their journey.

All Wizards Are Liars[]

After Zilvar woke up, the Weapon of Desolation in his hands started manipulating him again, saying that he finally has a chance to save his people. However, Jay distracted Zilvar, asking if he is okay. Then, he said the sooner they get that thing gone, the better, which Zilvar agreed, as the Weapon of Desolation's eyes glowed.

The next day, when the scorpions attacked them, Zilvar used the Weapon of Desolation against them while riding Grimtak. However, during the fight, one of the scorpions knocked both of them, separating them in the process. After that, the scorpions captured Grimtak and tried to take him with them. Scared for his friends' life, Zilvar got manipulated by the Weapon of Desolation, who told him to use the power that can save Grimtak. Because of this, Zilvar used the weapon, summoning an Earth Monster and Acid Monster, who fend off the scorpions, rescuing Grimtak. When Lloyd shouted at him, Zilvar sucked the Elemental Monsters back into the weapon so the ninja wouldn't realize he used it. Then, they continued their journey to the abyss.

The Summit[]

As Zilvar and the ninja continued their journey to the Abyss of Eternal Night, Jay expressed his concerns about Zilvar, saying that he can sense something is bothering him. In response, Lloyd said how this journey is tough for all of them and the sooner they destroy the Weapon of Desolation, the better, saying that breeds danger whenever it goes. Meanwhile, the weapon continued to tempt Zilvar, saying that destroying the weapon could prevent his people from coming back. When Zilvar questioned if that's even possible, the weapon reminded him that Ras was one of his tribe, making him remember his encounter with Ras and the weapon talking to him for the first time. As the weapon continued to tempt him, Zilvar and the ninja arrived in the Abyss of Eternal Night.

Desperately Seeking Sora[]

As Zilvar stood on the brink of dropping the Weapon of Desolation into the Abyss of Eternal Night, he found himself gripped by uncertainty. The weapon's influence led to an internal dialogue that made him question the wisdom of his decision. He remembered how possessing the weapon could endanger everything, but his corrupted side argued that destroying it would mean forsaking the Tiger Tribe that depended on him and who he failed. Haunted by the memories of his fallen tribe and Mei, Zilvar succumbed to the weapon's power, summoning Elemental Monsters to attack the ninja. As the ninja transformed into their Draconus Forms to fight back, the Weapon of Desolation revealed its hidden abilities. Compelled, Zilvar absorbed the Elemental Monsters to unleash a fierce assault on the ninja with Elemental Powers. Jay attempted to reach Zilvar, hoping to help him break free from the weapon's grip, and for a moment, it seemed to work. However, the Weapon of Desolation soon reasserted its control, leading Zilvar to strike Jay with an uppercut and unleash a torrent of Elemental Powers before fending off Kai's attack.

Dangerous Connections[]

As the Weapon of Desolation continued to corrupt Zilvar, he utilized its power to summon an army of Elemental Monsters, all aimed at annihilating the ninja. Lloyd managed to disarm Zilvar, causing the weapon to dangle precariously at the edge of the abyss, which also freed Zilvar from the weapon's grasp. Seizing the moment, Jay used Lightning to send the Weapon of Desolation plummeting into the abyss, leading the ninja to believe they had successfully destroyed it. However, to their astonishment, the weapon began to levitate and took over Zilvar once more, taunting the ninja through the tiger warrior's voice. It ridiculed their attempts to eliminate it, reminding them that while Zilvar had limitations, they were foolish to think they could dispose of such an ancient artifact by merely casting it into a pit. The Weapon of Desolation revealed its true strength, claiming it drew power from everything around it, even from the Sources. Hearing this, Lloyd planned to neutralize the weapon by stabilizing it with his own powers. He reached out and grasped the Weapon of Desolation, pulling it away from Zilvar, which halted the relentless assault of the Elemental Monsters. Channeling his Life Energy, Lloyd succeeded in neutralizing the weapon, sealing away the Elemental Monsters and stabilizing Elemental Powers. However, the Weapon filled the Green Ninja with an uncontrollable surge of Life, causing his physical form to implode as the artifact fell into the Abyss of Eternal Night.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Cosmokinesis: The Weapon of Desolation is described to be an artifact with cosmic powers.
  • Draco-Ergokinesis: The Weapon of Desolation can manipulate Source Energy.
    • Draco-Ergogenesis: The Weapon of Desolation can generate Source Energy.
    • Draco-Ergokinetic Wave Emission: The Weapon of Desolation can project waves of Source Energy.
    • Elemento-Potentikinesis: The Weapon of Desolation can manipulate Elemental Powers via the unstable Source Energy it can exploit.
      • Elemento-Monstrokinesis: The Weapon of Desolation can control Elemental Monsters and grants its wielders that same ability.
        • Elemento-Monstrogenesis: The Weapon of Desolation can create an army of Elemental Monsters with just a single wave of unstable Source Energy.
          • Elemental Transmutation - The Weapon can sometimes transmute elements to create Elemental Monsters, such as turning water into acid and earth into asphalt.
        • Command Inducement: The wielder of the Weapon of Desolation can give the Elemental Monsters commands which they're forced to obey as long as the commander holds it.
        • Elemental Monster Empowerment: The Weapon of Desolation can empower the Elemental Monsters by granting them more Source Energy.
          • Shapeshifting Inducement: Earth and Mutation Monsters empowered by the Weapon of Desolation take on a more monstrous form.
            • Size Augmentation: The Earth and Mutation Monsters become larger in their more monstrous forms.
          • Combat Augmentation: The Weapon of Desolation can increase the combat capabilities of Elemental Monsters.
            • Strength Augmentation: Elemental Monsters empowered by the Weapon of Desolation become significantly stronger.
            • Defense Augmentation: Elemental Monsters empowered by the Weapon of Desolation become significantly more durable.
        • Elemental Monster Erasure: The Weapon of Desolation can erase Elemental Monsters by reverting them back into the Source Energy their bodies are composed of and absorbing them back into itself.
        • Elemental Monster Absorption: With the Weapon of Desolation, the user can absorb Elemental Monsters to power themselves up.
          • Elementokinesis Bestowal: As seen with Zilvar, the Weapon can grant its user the elemental powers of the Elemental Monsters they absorb.
            • Acidokinesis Bestowal: The Weapon of Desolation can grant its user the power to use a corrupted form of Water, which manifests as acid from Acid Monsters.
            • Asphakinesis Bestowal: The Weapon of Desolation can grant its user the power to use a corrupted form of Earth and Heat, which manifest as asphalt from Earth Monsters.
            • Mutakinesis Bestowal: The Weapon of Desolation can grant its user the power to use a corrupted form of Life and Size, which manifest as mutations from Mutation Monsters.
            • Pyrokinesis Bestowal: The Weapon of Desolation can grant its user the power to use a corrupted form of Fire from Fire Monsters.
            • Cryokinesis Bestowal: The Weapon of Desolation can grant its user the power to use a corrupted form of Ice from Ice Monsters.
            • Elemental Mixture: With the Weapon of Desolation, the user can combine the Elemental Monsters' powers.
              • Elementokinetic Attacks: By absorbing Elemental Monsters through the Weapon of Desolation, the user can attack with their combined powers.
                • Elementokinetic Beam Emission: The user can shoot concentrated beams comprised of the Elemental Monsters' powers.
                • Elementokinetic Ball Projection: The user can shoot balls comprised of the Elemental Monsters' powers.
                • Elementokinetic Vision: The user can shoot beams comprised of the Elemental Monsters' powers from their eyes.
      • Elemental Power Augmentation: In the hands of an Elemental Master, the Weapon of Desolation can amplify and enhance their powers to the point where they become uncontrollable within seconds while coming into contact with it. For example, when Kai came into contact with the yellow fragment, he erupted into an uncontrollable fire explosion that set him ablaze and sent him flying into Nya.
      • Elemental Power Disruption: The weapon has a catastrophic impact on Elemental Powers, rendering it unsuitable for an Elemental Master to wield.
    • Explosion/Implosion Inducement: The Weapon can cause anyone who wields it to explode, such as when it filled Lloyd with an uncontrollable amount of Life energy that his body imploded into a shower of Life particles.
    • Conceptukinesis: The weapon can manipulate the concepts represented by Source Energy.
      • Concept Empowerment: The weapon allows the user to empower themselves with the concepts represented by Source Energy.
        • Levitation Bestowal: The Weapon of Desolation can bestow the power of levitation to its user via Source Energy.
    • Life Absorption: The weapon can absorb Life energy from entire environments, as Beatrix's use of the artifact led to the continued decay of the Whispering Wood by draining the wood's Life essence, even after the Ritual of Unrooting had been completed.
      • Color Absorption: By absorbing Life from environments, the weapon can cause affected environments to lose color.
      • Decay Inducement: Also by absorbing Life from environments, the Weapon can cause affected environments to decay.
      • Death Inducement: By absorbing Life from environments, the Weapon of Desolation can cause the inhabitants of said environments to die.
  • Megethoskinesis: The weapon can change its size depending on the size of its user.
  • Mentokinesis: The Weapon of Desolation can manipulate the minds of its wielders, as seen with Beatrix and to a greater extent Zilvar.
    • Mind Control: As stated by LaRow, the weapon seems to exert its influence over its wielder, as seen with Beatrix and Zilvar.
      • Corruption Inducement: As seen with Beniro and Zilvar, the Weapon of Desolation can corrupt its wielder.
      • Hallucination Creation: As seen with Zilvar, the Weapon of Desolation can create hallucinations in its wielder's mind based on their memories for psychological torment.
      • Possession: As seen with Zilvar, the Weapon of Desolation can possess its wielder and take full control of their body.
    • Personality Splitting: As seen with Zilvar, the Weapon of Desolation can split its wielder's personality.
    • Telepathy: As seen with Zilvar, the Weapon of Desolation can communicate with its wielder and others via telepathy.
      • Mind Reading: The Weapon seems to read its wielder's minds, as it somehow knew of Zilvar’s desire to bring back the Tiger Tribe.
        • Memory Reading: The Weapon of Desolation can read its wielder's memories, as it knew of Zilvar’s tragic past.
  • Nigh-Indestructibility: According to Lloyd, the Weapon is nigh-indestructible, meaning it cannot be destroyed through almost any conventional means.
  • Levitation: The Weapon of Desolation can levitate in the air.

Trivia[]

  • In its set form, the Weapon of Desolation resembles Iron Baron's staff, as they both feature a long staff design topped with the image of a horned draconic entity. Additionally, both weapons share the same handle piece as an Aeroblade.
    • A more show-accurate version is included in 71868 Wyldfyre's Transforming Dragon Mech.
  • Cole is the only Elemental Master so far who can handle the Weapon’s power, using it to transform into an upgraded version of his Elemental Golem Form.
    • When asked why this happened, Chris "Doc" Wyatt replied, "He's tough".[8]
  • It will be revealed who used the Weapon of Desolation to even have the First Spinjitzu Master intervene.[9] It is unknown if this person is Ras' master or a unknown individual.
  • It is unclear what the sentience of the Weapon of Desolation is, as in "Desperately Seeking Sora", the weapon refers to itself in third person when it spoke to Zilvar, potentially suggesting it may be Ras' master speaking through the weapon. This is contradicted in "Dangerous Connections", where the weapon speaks of itself in first person.
  • The face symbol on the weapon resembles the insignia on Ras and the Evil ninja's outfits in the Dragons' Fury sets.
