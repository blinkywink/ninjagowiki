Overview

71855 Ninja Dragon Riyu's Battle is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 4 set that was released on January 1, 2026. It includes Draconus Arin, Draconus Form Nya, a Loyalist, a Mutation Monster and Riyu.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Boys and girls ages 7 and up will love building this LEGO® NINJAGO® Ninja Dragon Riyu’s Battle (71855) ninja toy and recreating scenes from season 4 of the NINJAGO Dragons Rising TV show. This impressive playset features a dragon toy with posable wings, legs, feet, arms, wrists, claws, head, tail and shoulders, and it’s armed with a large sword and shuriken weapon accessories. The LEGO NINJAGO range of make-believe toys for kids includes mechs, dragons and temples for fans to role-play ninja action. Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Draconus Form Nya is exclusive to this set.
  • This is the cheapest way to get a Loyalist and Draconus Arin.
  • This set is reminiscent of 71809 Egalt the Master Dragon, as they are both sets composed of a ninja dragon wearing a red fabric belt and using ninja weapons, especially a dragon-sized sword.
  • The weapons included in 71854 Cole's Mission Mech are compatible with Riyu and vice versa.
  • This set is the only way to get a complete version of a Mutation Monster.
