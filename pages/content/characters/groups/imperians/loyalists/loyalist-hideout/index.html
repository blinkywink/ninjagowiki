Overview

The Loyalist hideout[1] is the secret base of the Loyalists located in the Old Town.

History

History[]

The Whispering Wood[]

After visiting the Altar of the Seven Sources, the Chief Loyalist had a meeting with the other Loyalists through holograms in the hideout to tell them about the Elemental Monsters and to find the Weapon of Desolation.

Tonight We Hunt Monsters[]

In the hideout, the Chief Loyalist had another meeting with the other Loyalists to get report from them, only to get nothing from it.

Later, Grand Inquisitor informed the Chief Loyalist that they caught the Mutation Monster. Which the Chief Loyalist said he knows what to do.

Seed and Soil[]

After witnessing the Mutation Monster's power in Umbrasilvis, Grand Inquisitor had a meeting with the Chief Loyalist at the hideout and informed her about the location of the Weapon of Desolation. Which the Chief Loyalist (who is revealed to be Dr. LaRow in disguise) informed Beatrix about.
