Overview

This article is about the set. For the actual vehicle, see Transforming Car.

71856 Jay's Transforming Car is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 4 that was released on January 1, 2026. It includes Jay and Nya in their Draconus suits, Draconus Form Kai, the Loyalist Leader, an Earth Monster, the Weapon of Desolation, and the Transforming Car.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Boys and girls ages 8 and up can enjoy hours of fast action with the LEGO® NINJAGO® Jay’s Transforming Car (71856) toy building set. This 2-in-1 ninja car racing toy can be played with and displayed in 2 versions: regular mode or flip the front to put it into dragon mode, revealing golden claws, a dragon head and rotating shurikens. This great-value kids’ adventure toy features a spring-loaded shooter with 2 darts, which can be detached and placed on a plant-covered side build for kids to play out battles. The set also includes 5 NINJAGO minifigures: Jay, Nya and dragon form Kai, plus the villainous Earth Monster and Loyalist Leader to stage good vs. evil battles. The LEGO NINJAGO range of building sets and role-play toys for kids includes detailed mechs, dragons and temples for fans to role-play ninja action. Kids can build each playset with confidence using the LEGO Builder app – zoom, rotate in 3D and track progress with easy-to-follow digital instructions. Set contains 387 pieces.

Trivia[]

  • This is the cheapest way to get the Chief Loyalist.
