Overview

For other uses, see Destiny's Bounty (disambiguation).

71797 Destiny's Bounty - race against time is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 1 set that was released on June 1, 2023. It comes with Arin, Sora, Lloyd and Kai in their Merge outfits, Riyu, Wu in his Dojo robe, Rapton, Zanth and Destiny's Bounty.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Ninja fans aged 9+ will be excited to play out action with this updated Destiny’s Bounty – race against time (71797) playset. The iconic vehicle is now an airship, allowing the ninja to sail above the newly merged world. It features a removable dragon nesting at the front, posable sails, stud shooters at the back and 360-degree rotating engines. The roof can also be removed to reveal a navigation room and a playroom with an arcade machine.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Master Wu in this set reuses his outfit from Core.
  • Initial sketch models were produced by Dimitris Stamatis and Heiko König; set designer Niek van Slagmaat was tasked with building the test structure, while senior designer Florian Müller finalized it.[2][1]
    • van Slagmaat focused on maximizing play potential in the back, as the front would be taken up by Zanth's build.[2]
  • A sticker representing the TV displays a fighting game, with Kai in his Epic Battle suit and Wyplash as the playable characters.
    • Another stickered element depicts Kai’s drawing of the Land of the Three Mountains seen in "The Merge: Part 2."
  • Curiously, on the back of the box, the golden bar on the sides of the boat disappeared behind Sora and Zanth.
  • This set includes Lloyd's Core Mask, a reference to Arin wearing Lloyd's mask from "The Merge: Part 1".
