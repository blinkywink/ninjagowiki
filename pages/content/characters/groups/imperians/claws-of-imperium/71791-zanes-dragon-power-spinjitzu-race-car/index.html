71791 Zane's Dragon Power Spinjitzu Race Car is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 1 set that was released on June 1, 2023.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Ninja fans aged 7+ will love playing out scenes from the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV series using the never-before-seen spinning function on Zane’s Dragon Power Spinjitzu Race Car (71791). When they push down the spoiler at the back, the car’s rear wheels lift and it moves on its dragon power elements so that the ninja can take on the evil dragon hunters.

4 Minifigures to role-play gripping battles
This ninja car playset comes with 4 Minifigures: ninja warriors Zane and Cole armed with 2 katanas each, an Imperium Guard Commander and Imperium Claw Hunter, and an Imperium Droid. The Imperium Guard Commander has an Imperium sword, the Imperium Claw Hunter has an electric staff, and both travel on cool hover flyers. There are 2 blue dragon power elements and kids can collect 4 more in different colors in other NINJAGO sets.
