Overview

The Dragon Catcher (Alternative Title: Dragon Catcher & Dragon Friend (US))[1] is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 1 book released on August 9, 2024 in Poland.[2]

Official description

Official description[]

AMEET[]

This bestselling series of activity books feature word and picture puzzles, activities, and challenges PLUS LEGO® minifigures and accessories! Each hands-on, interactive book inspires building and roleplay through storytelling and adventures featuring the cover-mounted LEGO minifigure.[3]

Scholastic[]

The world after the Merge is...divided: there are some who want to catch the dragons, and others who want to protect them. Meet Rapton?professional dragon hunter, former leader of the Claws of Imperium, and the ninja's biggest enemy. Includes activities, stories, and comics and Rapton Minifigure![4]
