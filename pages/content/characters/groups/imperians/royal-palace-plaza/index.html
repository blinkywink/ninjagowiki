Overview

The Royal Palace Plaza[1] is the public square of the City of Imperium.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

I Will Be the Danger[]

Seeking to escape from Imperium, a group of Dragons accompanied by Lloyd and Wyldfyre made their way to the plaza where the city's main gates were located. Here, they were intercepted by the Claws of Imperium and their newly developed Photacs, initiating a massive battle at the plaza.

The Calm Inside[]

The battle continued to unfold in the plaza, only being momentarily interrupted when the Monastery of Imperium rose out from underneath the plaza grounds. Kai, Nya, and Zane emerged from the monastery and joined the ongoing conflict.

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

The battle continued in the plaza, ending when the ninja and the dragons escaped through the Monastery of Imperium's Portal gate.

The Temple of the Dragon Cores[]

Countless citizens of Imperium gathered in the plaza to watch a broadcast of Empress Beatrix's message regarding rumors about escaped dragons and power outages. The Imperium Teen Resistance Force were amongst those present but grew suspicious about the Empress when they realized she had blatantly lied about the situation going on in Imperium.

Absolute Power[]

With the help of Dorama, Empress Beatrix broadcasted a new announcement to her citizens from the plaza.

We Are All Dragons[]

Following the exposure of Empress Beatrix's lies, a large group of Imperian citizens marched into the plaza to protest against the Empress.

The Power Within[]

Empress Beatrix briefly fought against the ninja in the plaza before rampaging across the entire City of Imperium, initiating the MergeQuake Storm.
