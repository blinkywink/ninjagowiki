The Broadcast Tower is the radio tower of the City of Imperium. It is where information gets sent to the Imperians.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

We Are All Dragons[]

After encountering the Imperium Teen Resistance Force, Percival told Arin and Sora that they had an insider that could help broadcast Beatrix's confession throughout the kingdom. They arrive at the broadcast tower, only to find that the insider is Rapton. After a brief truce agreement, Sora starts to hack into the information in Imperium. Dorama then enters the broadcast tower and starts attacking them, only to be stopped by Riyu.

The Power Within[]

With Beatrix managed to get her MergeQuake weapon working, Rapton states his concerns with Dorama stating that Beatrix has an incredible flair for the dramatic with Rapton reminding him that he will be destroyed as well. With Arin saying that ninja never quit, he, Sora, and Percival left the broadcast tower to fight Beatrix.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

A Wonder of the Merged Lands[]

Percival tries to fix the energy power all over the kingdom. But not only the dragon energy to convert the power of the old grid. She need conversion the power of street light, public transportation, and the Energy Barrier. Percival gave him the idea that the Energy Barrier was left over by Empress Beatrix. It needs to reroute that power for the Imperians energy. Without the Claws, it converts the Imperium's line of defense. He orders her to shut down the energy barrier, and the city's power is restored.
