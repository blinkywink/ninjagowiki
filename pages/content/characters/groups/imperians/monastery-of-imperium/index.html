Overview

This article is about the Monastery of Imperium. For other monasteries, see Monastery (disambiguation).
“When I interfaced with it, it self-identified as the Monastery of Imperium. It shares some similarities with our own Monastery of Spinjitzu.”
— Zane, "The Battle of the Second Monastery"

The Monastery of Imperium was a monastery that was previously located underneath the Kingdom of Imperium. Like all the selective monasteries, it had a portal gate that provided access to the Merged Realms through other monasteries, until Beatrix had it taken apart and parts were either sent to the Land of Lost Things or taken underground. Even if the building itself was scrapped, its portal gate still remains, having been found by the Finders and put together by Geo, allowing a gateway to Imperium to continue to exist.
