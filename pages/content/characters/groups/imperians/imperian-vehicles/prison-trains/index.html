The prison trains[1] are technological trains from Imperium.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

When Arin and Sora were captured and taken to the capture bay, Ras ordered Rapton and the Claw Hunters to take the two inside the prison train along with Riyu, where they would be transferred to the Kingdom of Imperium.

The Merge: Part 2[]

Lloyd rescued Sora and Arin, with the latter rescuing Riyu. Together, they fought against Rapton and the Claw Hunters. During the battle, Riyu transferred dragon power to Sora, which caused her to accidentally turn all of the Imperium Swords into bombs. Lloyd, Arin, Sora, and Riyu quickly jumped off of the train.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Great Zane Robbery[]

At some point in time, Rapton and the Claw Hunters acquired one of Imperium's prison trains and used it to serve as both a mode of transportation as well as a storage for stolen goods. After one such successful pillaging, the Freebooters brought a purple techno-cocoon containing P.I.X.A.L. onboard. Unbeknownst to them however, Zane followed the Freebooters onboard and released P.I.X.A.L. from the cocoon. When the Freebooters confronted the pair, a brief fight broke out, during which the contents of the cargo car were knocked over and spilled.

Not long afterwards, Nokt and his Dragonian Warriors stormed the train in search of the Red Prismatic Blade, engaging in battle with the Freebooters as well as Zane and P.I.X.A.L. All hostilities would be stopped, however, after Nokt ordered one of his warriors to damage the train's controls while he recovered the Prismatic Blade and retreated. With its controls damaged, the train now began to accelerate at a dangerous rate as it raced towards a large rockface. Left with no options, Zane plugged himself into the damaged controls and barely managed to prevent the train from completely slamming into the rockface, although he was heavily damaged in the process.

I Alone Can Save Them[]

With the Freebooters' train derailed and damaged, P.I.X.A.L. began work on constructing a new body for Zane outside of the damaged train, ordering the pirates to bring her various materials and supplies that she required to complete her work.
