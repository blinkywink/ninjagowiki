Overview

“Wait, why did they park all these hover chariots in the cafeteria?”
— Sora, "Mindless Beasts"

The hover chariots are vehicles used by the Imperians.

History

History[]

Absolute Power (flashback)[]

Levo, along with some Imperium Guards, used hover chariots to fly over Imperium for a brief while until Levo's chariot suddenly exploded and crashed to the ground.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

Rapton used a hover chariot to chase after Riyu, drawing the attention of Arin and Sora. The two battled against the Claws of Imperium and their hover chariots until Ras arrived.

The Merge: Part 2[]

When the Destiny's Bounty arrived at the Land of the Three Mountains, the Claw Hunters used their hover chariots to shoot it down. They used their hover chariots to shoot down the Mountain Dragons and their Matriarch. However, after Lloyd received dragon power from the Matriarch, he sent several hover chariots crashing off the mountain.

Writers of Destiny[]

Rapton and two Claw Hunters waited in the sky near the Cloud Kingdom, spying on the ninja.

While the ninja were battling against the tentacled beasts, they used the distraction to capture the two Ice Dragons that were dwelling in the Cloud Kingdom.

Return to Imperium[]

The Claw Hunters used their hover chariots to pursue a Lightning Dragon and bring it down. However, they were ambushed by the ninja.

Mindless Beasts[]

In Rapton's recruitment video, hover chariots were used to showcase the technology and power of the Claws of Imperium.

The Calm Inside[]

Ras was seen riding a hover chariot before getting called to the palace by Beatrix. Later, Beatrix and Ras approached the ninja with their hover chariots.

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

Beatrix was riding her hover chariot.

The Temple of the Dragon Cores[]

Rapton was riding on his hover chariot as he followed the ninja to the Temple of the Dragon Cores.

Gangs of the Sea[]

Rapton rode his hover chariot past Lloyd and Arin when the two were stranded aboard the downed Destiny's Bounty. Although the two signalled to Rapton for help, not knowing he was piloting the hover chariot, the dragon hunter proudly ignored their signals for help.

The Shape of Motion[]
