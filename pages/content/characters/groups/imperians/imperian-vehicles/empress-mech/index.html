Overview

“I think, that mech, whatever she did to herself, it's not technology anymore. It's part of her.”
— Sora, "Birthright"

The Empress Mech was a mech used by Beatrix. It previously served as her throne until the mech became organic and fused with Beatrix during her time in the Land of Monsters.

History

History[]

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

As Ras was incarcerated for his failure to stop the ninja, Beatrix pressed a button on her throne, revealing the Empress Mech.

Absolute Power[]

As the panels in the floor of the throne room opened, Beatrix emerged from below, standing on the Empress Mech.

We Are All Dragons[]

The ninja and the Imperium Teen Resistance Force were sneaking through City of Imperium when Beatrix and some Claw Hunters ambushed them. However, Nya and Sora, arriving on Zanth and Jiro, blasted through Imperium's shield, attacking Beatrix. The dragons continued to distract Beatrix while the ninja left to formulate a plan. In the meantime, Beatrix defeated Zanth and Jiro with her mech. She later ambushed Lloyd, Nya, and Zane, defeating them until Sora, Percival, Arin, Rapton, and Riyu released the video exposing her lies to the citizens of Imperium. She quickly retreated, while being jeered at by the citizens she passed. However, with the MergeQuake weapon completed, she retrieved it and headed to the Advanced Systems Lab, where she ambushed Lloyd, Nya, and Zane once more. She fired the weapon into the sky, opening a MergeQuake.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Elemental Immunity - The Empress Mech is immune to most elemental powers.
  • Ergokinetic Wave Emission - The mech's Imperium Sword can create a large wave of energy that can repel opponents.
    • Sword Beam Emission - Said waves can take the form of beams as well.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Object Spinjitzu - The mech can be damaged or knocked back with the power of Object Spinjitzu. As seen in "The Power Within," Arin was able to damage the mech with his Object Spinjitzu.
