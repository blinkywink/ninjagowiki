Overview

“I think, that her mech, whatever she did to herself, it's not technology anymore. It's part of her, organic.”
— Sora, "Birthright"

The Empress Mech was a mech used by Beatrix. It previously served as the empress’ throne until the mech became organic, fused with her body and developed several monstorous features during her time in the Land of Monsters.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

As Ras was incarcerated for his failure to stop the ninja, Beatrix pressed a button on her throne, revealing the Empress Mech.

Absolute Power[]

As the panels in the floor of the throne room opened, Beatrix emerged from below, standing on the Empress Mech.

We Are All Dragons[]

The ninja and the Imperium Teen Resistance Force were sneaking through City of Imperium when Beatrix and some Claw Hunters ambushed them. However, Nya and Sora, arriving on Zanth and Jiro, blasted through Imperium's shield, attacking Beatrix. The dragons continued to distract Beatrix while the ninja left to formulate a plan. In the meantime, Beatrix defeated Zanth and Jiro with her mech. She later ambushed Lloyd, Nya, and Zane, defeating them until Sora, Percival, Arin, Rapton, and Riyu released the video exposing her lies to the citizens of Imperium. She quickly retreated, while being jeered at by the citizens she passed. However, with the MergeQuake weapon completed, she retrieved it and headed to the Advanced Systems Lab, where she ambushed Lloyd, Nya, and Zane once more. She fired the weapon into the sky, opening a MergeQuake.

The Power Within[]

Beatrix continued to use the mech to rampage across the City of Imperium, recklessly firing her MergeQuake Weapon and opening rifts throughout the city, only pausing to combat the ninja who still opposed her. After banishing Riyu through a rift, Arin retaliated against Beatrix by charging a piece of debris with Object Spinjitzu and smashed a hole through the Empress Mech. After Sora unlocked her True Potential and dismantled the MergeQuake Weapon, Arin struck the Empress Mech again, knocking it into a nearby rift where Beatrix was banished to the Land of Monsters.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Elemental Immunity - The Empress Mech is immune to most elemental powers. In particular, after fusing with Beatrix and becoming partially organic, it has become immune to being manipulated by the Technology element.
  • Ergokinetic Wave Emission (formerly) - The mech's Imperium Sword can create a large wave of energy that can repel opponents.
    • Sword Beam Emission - Said waves can take the form of beams as well.
  • Body Part Substitution - After fusing with Beatrix, the Mech can replace the severed or destroyed pieces of its body with parts from monsters to repair itself.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Object Spinjitzu - The mech can be damaged or knocked back with the power of Object Spinjitzu. As seen in "The Power Within," Arin was able to damage the mech with his Object Spinjitzu.
