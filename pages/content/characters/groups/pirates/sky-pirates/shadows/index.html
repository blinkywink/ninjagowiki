Overview

This article is about the beings. For other uses, see Shadow (disambiguation).
“Even shadows have their uses.”
— Garmadon to Kai, "King of Shadows"

Shadows are a type of ethereal being that can be formed from somebody's shadow, becoming a featureless and transparent black copy of the template with glowing red eyes. Armed with shadow copies of whatever weapons their template was holding at the time, shadows are invulnerable to physical blows and are capable of duplicating themselves, although are susceptible to blasts of Elemental energy, other shadows, and Golden Power.

History

History[]

The Lair of Tanabrax (flashback)[]

Many years before Wu and Garmadon's journey, a puppeteer named Tanabrax came into possession of a Djinn medallion following the destruction of his puppet theater. Claiming the medallion as just compensation for his losses, Tanabrax would soon find himself obsessed over the artifact, which over the years slowly twisted and corrupted his body until he had been transformed from flesh into a living shadow of his former being. Tanabrax would eventually be destroyed years later after one of his puppets, Moody, turned against him and exposed Tanabrax to direct sunlight.

King of Shadows[]

Near the end of the hunt for the Golden Weapons, Kai chased Lord Garmadon (disguised as Nya), into the Fire Temple. Lord Garmadon persuaded Kai to remove the Sword of Fire to save the real Nya from falling into a pit of lava. With no other choice, Kai removed the sword and used Spinjitzu and the Sword to cut the chain holding Nya, setting her free.

With the sword retrieved, Lord Garmadon attempted to claim it for his own by using Oni Sorcery to bring Kai's shadow to life. Kai attempted to fight back, but the shadow proved immune to his blows and struck back. Lord Garmadon proceeded to duplicate the shadow into a small army, and they assaulted Kai until he dropped the Sword of Fire.

Before the Shadows could claim the Golden Weapon however, Master Wu joined the fight, using his own shadow to fight the shadow warriors. After fending off several attacks, he created an eagle shadow puppet, scaring the shadows away.

The Green Ninja[]

Kai claimed that Lord Garmadon cheated in their previous fight by summoning shadows to do his bidding.

Wrong Place, Wrong Time[]

The ninja of the present and the Master Wu of the past worked together to preserve the past from Lord Garmadon's tampering. When Past Kai was lured to the Fire Temple, Present Kai explained to Past Master Wu that Lord Garmadon was going to summon shadows to fight Past Kai after he saved Past Nya. Instead, the shadowy villain called in his own future self to battle Past Kai, which forced the ninja of the present to intervene.

Shadow of Ronin (non-canon)[]

When the ninja went to the Fire Temple to get the Obsidian Sword, Kai remembered his encounter with Garmadon's shadow.

The Realm of Shadows[]

Following his loss of the Teapot of Tyrahn, Clouse eventually sought to conquer Ninjago by plunging it into Darkness, and thus summoned an army of Shadows modeled after Bat-like creatures to assist him. After taking notice of the young YinYang Dragon named Bandit, Clouse sent the Shadows after him. Kai and Nya, confronted the shadows, and succeeded in escaping aboard the Destiny's Bounty, and with the help from the other ninja managed to fend off the creatures. However, despite their best attempts, the shadows took advantage of their distraction to whisk Bandit away to Clouse's lair.

Later, when Kai and Nya arrived to rescue Bandit, the shadows succeeded in delaying them, allowing Clouse to continue the spell. With his spell reaching its final stages, Clouse had his shadows converge onto Bandit, slowly corrupting him into a sinister Shadow Dragon. However, when Kai, Nya, and a group of friends sent by Master Wu encouraged and supported Bandit to conquer the evil inside him, the young dragon succeeded, dispersing the Shadows from his heart and instead growing into a Light Dragon.

Dark Island Trilogy, Part 3[]

Having succeeded in corrupting the Temple of Light with Dark Matter, Clouse summoned an army of Shadows modeled after villagers and Samurai to help the Sky Pirates defend his plans from the incoming ninja. However, when Master Wu arrived within the Golden Mech, Clouse had the shadows converge onto him in order to form a duplicate shadow mech to combat his nemesis. Despite the strength of the shadow mech, however, its blade was quickly shattered by the Golden Mech before being destroyed by its raw, Golden Power, dispersing the shadows yet again.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • While trapped in the Underworld, Lord Garmadon used Oni Sorcery to manifest his will in the form of a massive shadow of himself for inter-realm communication. It is unclear if this shadowy visage had similar abilities to Kai's shadow after Garmadon corrupted it to battle the Red Ninja.
  • Lord Garmadon's shadow underwent a visual makeover between the pilot episodes and "Wrong Place, Wrong Time" — notably, the whole form became somewhat darker, and his helmet using the new samurai helmet LEGO piece.
  • A shadow's level of intelligence is unclear, as it does not speak. Kai's shadows displayed fighting abilities on par with their template, but it is unknown if they are sentient or simply puppets of Lord Garmadon. Interestingly, the shadows used by Clouse in The Realm of Shadows, modeled after Bats, acted like animals, indicating their intelligence is based on the template they were modeled after.
  • It is unknown if the ability to summon shadows is also found in Elemental Masters of Shadow, or just in Wu, Garmadon, Clouse, and Tanabrax.
  • When asked how Garmadon was able to use this ability, Tommy Andreasen stated it was "Just random but cool stuff. That's the pilot. There was no master plan at the time."[1]
