Flintlock pistols[citation needed] are guns used by the Sky Pirates, mainly used by Flintlocke.

History

History[]

The Tall Tale of Clancee (non-canon)[]

Clancee was practicing with a toy flintlock pistol.

The Tall Tale of Doubloon (non-canon)[]

Monkey Wretch had one of these flintlock pistols when trying to attack Doubloon.

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Enkrypted[]

After greeting each other, Nadakhan asks Flintlock if his gunpowder is "still dry," to which he responds shooting at a bunch of birds. Nadakhan believes that Flintlocke failed just before realizing that he threw the top of a palm tree into the sea. After greeting Clancee, Flintlocke asks him to clean his pistols.

Misfortune Rising[]

When the Sky Pirates start to attack Ninjago City, Flintlocke used the pistols to shoot the buildings and neutralize the Elemental ice shurikens thrown by Zane and almost shot him.

Later, Cole searched in the ship the lantern with the map to Tiger Widow Island, but Flintlocke discovered him and try to shoot at him. Due to Cole being a ghost, the bullet bounces in the room until it ends up destroying the Realm Crystal.

My Dinner With Nadakhan[]

While Jay is mopping the Misfortune's Keep at night, Flintlocke used his pistols to threaten Jay and lead him to a dining room where Nadakhan was already waiting for him.

Wishmasters[]

After fighting some Sky Pirates, Flintlocke prepares to shoot Lloyd, but he wishes that Flintlocke had a bad aim. Impressed and infuriated, Flintlocke starts to shoot at the ninja, but failed, bringing down a Raid Zeppelin and finally, being scolded by his captain before he causes a mess. After this, Flintlocke accidentally shot his foot.

The Last Resort[]

Suspicious of his captain's true motives, Flintlocke tries to make a mutiny with the pirate crew. He points him with one of his pistols and tries to shoot him, unsuccessfully because of the effect of Lloyd's wish.

The Way Back[]

In the ceremony of the wedding, Nadakhan used a pair of pistols, one to aim at Nya's head and the other to press Clancee to hurry up saying the vows. After making himself all-powerful and creating clones to chase the ninja with Dogshank and Flintlocke until they reach the Misfortune's Keep, Jay gave Flintlocke the confidence to shoot Nadakhan the Tiger Widow Venom dart, and this one shooting a coin in the distance just to prove that still have skill with the pistol.
