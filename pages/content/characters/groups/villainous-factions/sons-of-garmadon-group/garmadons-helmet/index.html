Garmadon's helmet is a large black helmet used by Garmadon after he was resurrected with the three Oni Masks.

After the Oni's invasion, Wu threw the helmet from the Mountains of Impossible Height after Garmadon allied with the ninja. Eventually it was found and used by Lord Mogra who successfully turned the Sons of Garmadon into the Red Crows.

History

History[]

Between Endings and Two Moon Village[]

After defeating the Oni and unsure of himself, Garmadon took off his helmet while sulking before Wu arrived to check on him. Without hesitating, Wu swiped the helmet and threw it into the distance.[1] At some point, the helmet was recovered by Mogra, who kept it for himself in an effort to emulate Garmadon.

Beginnings[]

After Mogra's defeat, the villagers of Two Moon Village recovered the helmet and Min offered to return it to Garmadon, only to find out he left, with a note asking for them to hold onto his helmet if they find it. After reading the note, Min placed the helmet over her head and happily declared that she would hold onto it for Garmadon for when he would return.

Prior to Christofern[]

At some point, Garmadon obtained another helmet, one similar to the original, and has worn it since.
