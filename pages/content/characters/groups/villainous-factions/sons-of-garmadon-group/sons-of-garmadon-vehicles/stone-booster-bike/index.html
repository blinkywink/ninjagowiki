Overview

“I heard she dragged a guy on her bike for two miles, just 'cause he was looking at it the wrong way.”
— A S.O.G. member to Mohawk, "The Jade Princess"

The Stone Booster Bike[1] is a purple dirt bike that is used by Ultra Violet. Killow later gave it to Snake Jaguar to use in his inauguration race. However, the Stone Booster Bike got destroyed when it got hit by a subway train. Although, Ultra Violet uses it again, which means that she got a new one after the original one got destroyed.

History

History[]

The Jade Princess[]

Ultra Violet used her Stone Booster Bike when the Sons of Garmadon invaded the palace. She later uses it to chase down Lloyd and Harumi when they had the Mask of Deception. After claiming the mask, the S.O.G. chase the two to the docks, but are able to escape.

The Oni and the Dragon[]

Ultra Violet puts a captured Cole onto her bike before speeding off when the rest of the ninja arrive.

Snake Jaguar[]

Killow allows Snake Jaguar to use Ultra Violet's bike much to her disliking. However, Zane is forced to abandon the bike after one of the Sons of Garmadon tried to attack him.

The Quiet One[]

Ultra Violet rides the bike to the location where the ninja crashed. As she and the Sons of Garmadon charge towards the Destiny's Bounty, they are met by P.I.X.A.L. in her mech.

Big Trouble, Little Ninjago[]

Ultra Violet rides the bike along with some other members of the Sons of Garmadon on their bikes.

Saving Faith[]

Ultra Violet rode the bike to the Ninja's headquarters in an effort to quash what remained of the Resistance. The bike would be abandoned however, once Skylor took control over the Colossus and used it to fling Ultra Violet across the city.
