The S.O.G. Headquarters is the former HQ of the Sons of Garmadon in Ninjago Metro.

History

History[]

Snake Jaguar[]

Zane, as Snake Jaguar, went undercover, and the Sons of Garmadon ended up at their HQ. When Zane asked if Nails was the "Big Man," he eventually found out it was Killow.

Cole was imprisoned in one of the cells for a short time, and Baby Wu was also kept in a room.

Cole was able to escape his cell and discovered the "key" to the third mask was a baby. Ultra Violet caught him, but Cole was able to escape.
