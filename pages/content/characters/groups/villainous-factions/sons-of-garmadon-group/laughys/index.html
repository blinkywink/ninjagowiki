“Well, you're in luck! Because guess what just reopened after extensive renovations? That's right! Laughy's Karaoke Club! So come... so come on down and have a nice refreshing boba drink and sing some songs! Tuesdays are half off! The savings will make your head spin!”
— Dareth advertising the bar, "Snaketastrophy"

Laughy's Karaoke Club, often shortened to Laughy's, is a bar owned by Dareth, who purchased it after Wu was lost in time and his days of "fighting side by side with the ninja." Like any other bar, it has a pool table, a dartboard, a karaoke stand, and a jukebox. According to Zane, it is on the south side of Ninjago City.

History

History[]

Prior to Sons of Garmadon[]

Around a year after Wu got lost in time, Dareth bought an old karaoke bar hoping to hold onto the glory days when he was fighting side-by-side with the ninja. However, the Sons of Garmadon then moved in and made the bar their dive.[1]

The Oni and the Dragon[]

After Luke Cunningham told them about their hangout, Cole (Rocky Dangerbuff) and Zane (Snake Jaguar) went to Laughy's to infiltrate the Sons of Garmadon. After Cole badly sang Glow Worm and his disguise came off, they had to move off.

True Potential[]

With the Sons of Garmadon in jail, Dareth was able to open his bar to the public again and the ninja decide to celebrate there. During the celebration, the ninja learn of Garmadon's resurrection and Harumi's escape. The ninja then go off to Police Headquarters while Dareth takes Misako and Wu to his apartment.

Hunted[]

The Sons of Garmadon managed to take over Laughy's again a week after Garmadon was proclaimed Emperor.

Radio Free Ninjago[]

Killow, Luke, and another guard head to Laughy's for karaoke after being Mind tricked by Neuro. They were later interrupted by Lloyd's speech, to which they angrily left and rushed back to help stop Lloyd.

After Hunted[]

Once the ninja returned and liberated the city, the Sons of Garmadon were sent back to Kryptarium Prison. Dareth kept ownership of the bar, where he could enjoy it without the evil gang around.

March of the Oni[]

The Darkness Comes[]

When the cloud of darkness swept over Ninjago City, the people in Laughy's ran out to escape.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Snaketastrophy[]

During the Elemental Cobras attack, Dareth does a commercial saying that Laughy's has reopened after extensive renovations.

Prime Empire[]

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

Dareth can be seen greeting customers as they walked in and out of Laughy's before he left to follow Gayle Gossip.

Ninjago Confidential[]

When Zane and P.I.X.A.L. enter Laughy's to search for clues about Milton Dyer, the brother of the outlaw Fugi-Dove confronts them before they easily defeat him.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Ninjargon on the side of the building reads "Laughy's Karaoke Bar."
  • The bar has the same general interior design as Yang Tavern.
  • According to Dareth, Tuesdays are half price.[2]
  • The current bartender is Tony, who happens to be an old friend of Milton Dyer.
  • The music notes on the logo of Laughy's are AFCE in Treble Clef and CAEG in Bass Clef. The Laughy's logo has a microphone in place of a clef symbol.
  • According to Dareth, Laughy's is a second-rate karaoke bar but he is a first-rate owner.[3]
