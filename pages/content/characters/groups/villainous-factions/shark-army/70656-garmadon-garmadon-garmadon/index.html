Overview

70656 garmadon, Garmadon, GARMADON! is a set from The LEGO Ninjago Movie that was released on December 2, 2017. It includes Lord Garmadon, Ninja Force Lloyd, Hammer Head, Frank, Nomis, Nancy, a hot dog-noodle cart, and the Shark Mech.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Help Lloyd to save Nomis and Nancy from Garmadon’s Shark Mech with this action-packed THE LEGO® NINJAGO® MOVIE™ garmadon, Garmadon, GARMADON! set. The mech features 2 opening cockpits, LEGO studs to stand the Garmadon minifigure on top of the shark's head, trigger-operated snapping jaws, posable legs, and 2 shooting cannons. This cool toy for kids also includes a hot dog stand and 6 minifigures with assorted play-starting weapons and accessory elements.

  • Includes 6 minifigures: Garmadon, Lloyd, Hammer Head, Frank, Nomis, and Nancy.
  • Garmadon Shark Mech features 2 opening minifigure cockpits (rear cockpit features transparent bubble windshield and opening weapon compartment with 2 katanas), LEGO® studs to stand the Garmadon minifigure on top of the shark's head, trigger-operated snapping jaws, posable legs, 2 shooting cannons, shark fins, 3 rotating propellers, and translucent-blue eye elements.
  • Hot dog stand features a big buildable sausage on the roof, ‘HOT Noodle DOG’ sign, ‘HOT’ flag and assorted elements including a hot dog and 2 condiment dispensers.
  • Weapons include Garmadon’s 2 katana swords, Lloyd’s sword, Hammer Head’s fish hammer, and Frank’s fish spear.
  • It also includes Nancy's hot dog element.
  • Nomis and Nancy minifigures are new for December 2017.
  • Recreate and role-play exciting THE LEGO® NINJAGO® MOVIE™ scenes.
  • This cool toy for kids is suitable for ages 9–14.
  • Garmadon Shark Mech measures over 9” (25 cm) high, 14” (38 cm) long and 10” (26 cm) wide.
  • Hot dog stand measures over 3” (9 cm) high, 3” (8 cm) wide and 1” (4 cm) deep.[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Nomis and Nancy are exclusive to this set.
  • This is the first and only Ninjago set to have a name not starting with a capital letter.
