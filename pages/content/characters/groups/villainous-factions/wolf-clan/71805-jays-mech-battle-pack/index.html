Overview

71805 Jay's Mech Battle Pack is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 2 set that was released in January 1, 2024. It includes Mech Pilot Jay, Master Lloyd, a Wolf Mask Claw Warrior, a Wolf Mask Guard, and the Elemental Lightning Mech.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Boys and girls aged 6+ can play out all-action battle scenes from season 2 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show with this Jay’s Mech Battle Pack (71805) adventure toy. This ninja construction toy for kids features a cool mech figure with posablearms, legs and torso that wields a large golden sword and can fire studs from its 2 shooters.

This ninja toy is packed with 4 NINJAGO minifigures and lots of accessories. Jay is armed with a golden sword and the Master Lloyd minifigure has 2 golden kat ana swords. The Wolf Mask Claw Warrior has a long set of claws on each hand and the Wolf Mask Guard comes with a crossbow.

The rest of the LEGO® NINJAGO range of buildable ninja toys for kids includes dragons, vehicles and temples so fans can role-play adventures with their heroes. Each adventure toy set can be created with the LEGO Builder app, which guides you and your child on an easy and intuitive building adventure. Zoom in and rotate models in 3D, save sets and track your progress.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The mech does not appear in the TV series.
  • This is the cheapest way to obtain Lloyd in his Master Robe.
  • Lloyd's headband uses the brighter green that was also used in his Merge suit.
  • Mech Pilot Jay was exclusive to this set before the release of 112503 Jay vs. Cinder
