Overview

Not to be confused with the actual location.

71814 Tournament Temple City is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 2 set that was released in June 2024. It includes Lloyd, Cole, Zane, Nya, and Arin in their Tournament of Sources suits, Red Ninja Wyldfyre, Lord Ras, Jordana, Mr. Pale, a Temple Guard, Bleckt, the Blacksmith, Roby, and the Leafy Dragon.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Boys and girls aged 14+ can build, play with and proudly display the Tournament Temple City (71814) adventure toy set from season 2 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show. This spectacular and detailed fantasy toy battle set for kids makes an impressive display model and contains lots of interactive features and functions for hours of action-packed playtime.

The NINJAGO temple built into a rock face features a 4-tiered pagoda, a bridge, a water mill kids can turn to move 2 battle platforms anda blacksmith’s hammer, plus falling rocks that are activated by pressing a lantern, hidden storage and a secret door. Kids can turn the temple around, where its open back provides extra play options and the chance to create fun little stories.

This buildable ninja toy for kids comes with 13 NINJAGO minifigures: Lloyd, Cole, Nya, Zane, Arin, Wyldfyre, Lord Ras, Jordana, Mr. Pale, Blacksmith, a Temple Guard, Roby and Bleckt. There is also an airship and a posable dragon toy with blade-style wings.

  • Playable ninja display model – Includes a water mill that can be turned to move 2 battle platforms, a blacksmith’s hammer and falling rocks that are activated by pressing a lantern
  • Posable dragon toy and airship – This action toy for kids also includes a posable dragon toy and an airship for theNINJAGO® minifigure characters to travel in
  • 13 NINJAGO® minifigures – This ninja toy for kids comes with Lloyd, Cole, Nya, Zane, Arin, Wyldfyre, Lord Ras, Jordana, Mr. Pale, Blacksmith, a Temple Guard, Roby and Bleckt for role play
  • LEGO® NINJAGO® gift for play and display – This ninja LEGO set for kids provides a fun build-and-play experience and is a birthday gift idea for boys and girls aged 14 and up
  • A helping hand – Discover intuitive instructions in the LEGO® Builder app, where builderscan zoom in and rotate models in 3D, track their progress and save sets as they develop new skills
  • Bigger ninja adventures – Look out for more LEGO® NINJAGO® toys (sets sold separately) featuring dragons, mechs and vehicles
  • Measurements – This big LEGO® set has 3,489 pieces and includes Temple Island measuring over 19 in. (48 cm) high, 25 in. (63 cm) wide and 12.5 in. (32 cm) deep[1]
Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

Aaron Newman: "The main designer on this set was my talented friend Markus. I just did the little dragon! We wanted to make the dragon in an unusual color, and tried everything from white to blue to purple before settling on the autumnal colors in the finished product.

Designer: Markus Rollbühler

Graphic Design: Michael Patton, Angel Grau Bullón & Renato Esper

Creative Lead: Sven Robin Kahl"[2]

Markus Rollbühler: "Dragon designed by Aaron Newman, blimp based on a sketch model by Andrew Dillon. Graphic design by Michael Patton, Angel Grau Bullón & Renato Esper."[3]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This marks the second appearance of Mr. Pale, following his initial release in the 2015 Ultra Agents set 70167 Invizable Gold Getaway. Furthermore, it marks his first appearance in a Ninjago set.
  • The set is similar to 70751 Temple of Airjitzu. Both are large temple builds with other side buildings with a lot of minifigures including civilians.
  • In the 71819 Dragon Stone Shrine there is a sticker representing a painting or drawing of the Tournament Temple City.
  • This is the only 2024 set to feature Wyldfyre without Kai.
    • However, this set also includes a microfigure of Kai.
  • Mr. Pale, the Security Bot, the Blacksmith, Roby and Bleckt are exclusive to this set.
    • Roby’s only exclusive part is his torso.
  • This is the only way to obtain Zane with his armor.
  • This set features the Jay ZX character card from the 2012 Ninjago Spinner game.
  • This set can be combined with 71818 Tournament Battle Arena.
  • Out of all the characters in the set, two of them, Ras and Temple Guard, all share same the voice actor in the TV show, Brian Drummond.
  • According to Markus Rollbühler who designed the set the portrait included in the inside of the temple is meant to depict Roby's father.[4]
  • At $249.99 USD, this is the most expensive Ninjago retail set to appear in the TV show.
