Fang claws[1][2] (also known as Shadow Claws[3]) are triple-bladed handheld weapons used by Master Chen, Morro, and the wolf warriors when powered by the Gong of Shattering.

History

History[]

Shattered Dreams[]

After Ras used the Gong of Shattering, Wolf Mask Warriors gained fang claws, which they used to fight against the ninja and threaten the Writers of Destiny.

Force From the East[]

After Cole made a shell around the rebuilt portal gate to protect himself and the Finders, Wolf Clan used the Gong of Shattering to Wolf Masks Warriors to gain fang claws. The warriors used fang claws to destroy the shell, only for the Finders and Cole to use the portal gate. However, two Wolf Mask Warriors who gained fang claws also used the portal gate, arriving at the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

The Spell at the Waterfall[]

The Wolf Mask Warriors who came from the portal gate used their fang claws to fight against Cole and Zane, only for them to knock them back to the portal gate.

Rising Ninja[]

After a Wolf Mask Warrior hit the Gong of Shattering, other Wolf Masks Warriors gained fang claws. Later, they used them to fight against the ninja. After the Wolf Masks Warriors teleported to the Land of Eternal Dawn with the rest of the Wolf Clan, fang claws disappeared.

The Final Game[]

After Nokt and Rox's betrayal, Ras summoned some Wolf Masks Warriors who used the Gong of Shattering to gain fang claws alongside with Cinder. However, after Rox used Theroxian magic on them, fang claws disappeared, giving them energy claws instead (excluding Cinder).

Other appearances

Other appearances[]

Fugitives From Madness[]

Several Wolf Masks Warriors who were using fang claws appeared in Lloyd's vision, where they used them to fight against the ninja.

Secrets of the Wyldness[]

Several Wolf Masks Warriors who were using fang claws appeared in Lloyd's vision again.

The Forest of Spirits[]

Several Wolf Masks Warriors who were using fang claws appeared in Lloyd's vision again.

The Shape of Motion[]

Several Wolf Masks Warriors who were using their fang claws for training appeared in Lloyd's vision.

Clues and Suspects[]

Several Wolf Masks Warriors with fang claws who surrendered Lloyd appeared in his vision alongside with Arin and Zeatrix. However, they were scared of by the Source Dragon of Motion.
