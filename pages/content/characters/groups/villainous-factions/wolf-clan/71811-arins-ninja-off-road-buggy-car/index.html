Overview

This article is about the set. For the actual vehicle, see Ninja Off-Road Buggy Car.

71811 Arin's Ninja Off-Road Buggy Car is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 2 set that was released in March 2024. It includes Merge Arin, Climber Cole, Riyu, a Wolf Mask Claw Warrior, Cinder, and the Off-Road Buggy Car.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Boys and girls aged 7+ can recreate exciting battles from season 2 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show with LEGO® NINJAGO Arin’s Ninja Off-Road Buggy Car (71811). This adventure set for kids includes a vehicle toy in Arin’s yellow color featuring moving wheels, suspension, an opening cockpit with space for 2 minifigures, 2 stud shooters with 6 studs to fire and a large hook on the back to help catch villains.

The ninja toy comes with a Baby Riyu model and 4 NINJAGO minifigures: Climber Cole with climbing hook and katana sword accessories, Arin with climbing hook and katana sword accessories, a Wolf Mask Claw Warrior with claws, and Cinder with a sword accessory.

The rest of the LEGO NINJAGO range of buildable ninja toys for kids includes mechs, dragons and temples so fans can role-play action with their heroes (sets sold separately). Each ninja playset can be created with the LEGO Builder app, which guides you and your child on an easy and intuitive building adventure.

  • Off-Road Buggy Car ninja toy for kids – Boys and girls aged 7+ can play out battles from season 2 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show with Arin’s ninja off-road vehicle toy
  • Vehicle building kit packed with features – The Off-Road Buggy toy has moving wheels, suspension, an opening cockpit with space for 2 minifigures, 2 stud shooters with 6 studs to fire and a large hook
  • 4 NINJAGO® characters – This adventure car set comes with ninja minifigures Arin and Climber Cole to battle Cinder anda Wolf Mask Claw Warrior, plus a Baby Riyu dragon model
  • Weapon accessories – Climber Cole and Arin are each armed with a climbing hook and a katana sword accessory and Cinder has a sword accessory
  • Ninja gift – This adventure toy provides a fun build-and-play experience and is a birthday gift idea for boys and girls aged 7+ who love playing out ninja action stories
  • Bigger ninja adventures – Look out for more NINJAGO® toys (sets sold separately) featuring dragons, mechs and vehicles
  • A universe of ninja toys – LEGO® NINJAGO® sets include dragons, vehicles and temples and let kids escape into a fantasy world of adventures with their ninja heroes
  • Measurements – This 267-piece adventure car set includes a vehicle model measuring over 4.5 in. (11 cm) high, 9 in. (23 cm) long and 5.5 in. (14 cm) wide[2]
