Overview

This article is about the actual temple. For the episode, see The Temple of Madness. For the set, see 71712 Empire Temple of Madness.
“Can we focus for a second on the reason we're here? To get one of the three keys we need to unlock the final arcade cabinet and get into Unagami's temple?”
— Nya, "I am Okino"

The Temple of Madness is a floating temple located in Terra Domina. It used to belong to Unagami and his army.

History

History[]

Prior to Prime Empire[]

At some point in time, the Temple of Madness was featured in an old commercial of Prime Empire to advertise the game.[1]

I am Okino[]

To stop the other ninja from arguing about the League of Jay, Nya mentioned the Temple of Madness by saying that they need the purple Key-Tana to go there.

Stop, Drop and Side Scroll[]

When Lloyd, Jay, and Nya entered Terra Domina, they saw the Temple of Madness.

The Temple of Madness[]

Nya and Jay are able to get through the pathway to the Temple of Madness, which is a conveyor belt with rotating shurikens moving in a certain pattern.

They then head inside Sushimi's sushi restaurant where they are attacked by a Hostess, monster sushi, Sushimi's sushi chefs, and Sushimi himself.

After completing the restaurant, Jay and Nya come across the fire wall and inserts the three Key-Tanas. The two hug after going through so much, but in the background, an injured Sushimi is seen. He throws his malice at Jay, but Nya pushes him to the side causing Nya to take the hit. Jay laments over the loss, but is forced to move on.

Jay heads inside the throne room and meets Unagami. Jay claims Unagami is Milton Dyer, but Unagami corrects him by revealing that Milton Dyer is his creator. Unagami then uses Nya's energy cube to complete the Manifestation Gate. As Unagami is about to enter Ninjago, Jay pulls Unagami down believing there was a reason Dyer shut down the game. However, Unagami transform into the Empire Dragon and blazes through the portal.

Meanwhile, the Red Visors begin crossing worlds. However, some of them attack Jay. Realizing he's overwhelmed, he summons the Blue Dragon and heads into Ninjago.

Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

LEGO.com description[]

Join forces with Digi Jay, Lloyd and Cole as they take on Unagami at his dangerous temple in the end-game battle of Prime Empire. Watch out as you climb the treacherous tower and try to avoid the booby-traps and get past Sushimi. At the top use all three Key-Tanas to unlock the final portal for Unagami's secret chamber. The ninjas must defeat him to complete their quest![2]

Prime Empire Moments[]

Ticking time bombs, giant, swinging sledgehammers, revolving ninja stars, and fierce, fanged sushi rolls? Anyone who tries to go near Unagami’s Temple of Madness must be… well, mad! But the ninja have no choice – they have to beat the game and defeat Unagami! How will they make it past all the threats and traps and escape the maki-roll-throwing, mad sushi chef, Sushimi?[3]

Trivia[]

  • The top of the temple's LEGO model seems to resemble a leaderboard, as it shows Unagami in first, Will in second, Jay in third, and Manovi in fourth. Manovi is the former design lead for Ninjago, but it's unknown who Will is.
  • Although there is the Manifestation Gate in the show, it does not appear in the set.
