Overview

71710 Ninja Tuner Car is a Ninjago - Prime Empire set that was released on January 2, 2020. It includes Digi Kai, Scott, two Red Visors, the Purple Key-Tana within a loot box, and the Ninja Tuner Car.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This set was first seen in the "The Stowaway," but in the episodes, it was first seen in "Superstar Rockin' Jay" and its flight form was first seen in "The Speedway Five-Billion."
  • Despite this being the vehicle Nya used in the Speedway Five-Billion race, she is not included in this set, and instead Scott pilots it.
  • A couple of the stickers on the hood of the car reference two past characters Garmadon and Akita.
  • It is the only Prime Empire that does not contain hair.
    • Although part 53451 was never originally used as a hair.
