Overview

70608 Master Falls is a set based on the Bridge of Fallen Mentors from the 2017 film The LEGO Ninjago Movie. Released in August 2017, it includes Ninja Force Kai without a hood, Master Wu, Jungle Garmadon, and a bridge structure with a stone entrance, a cave, and a bamboo cage in and around.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Team up with Wu and Kai to take on Jungle Garmadon in this awesome THE LEGO® NINJAGO® MOVIE™ Master Falls set. The jungle bridge features an opening cage and a hidden cave with a skeleton inside holding a secret map. Also includes 3 minifigures with assorted weapons—including Wu’s staff and 4-armed Jungle Garmadon’s 4 swords—to bolster the role-play action.

  • Includes 3 minifigures: Wu, Kai and Jungle Garmadon (with 4 arms and new-for-August-2017 jungle outfit decoration).
  • Jungle bridge features an entrance arch, opening cage, hidden cave with a skeleton holding a secret map, tropical leaf elements and translucent flame-style elements.
  • Weapons include Kai's 2 katanas, Wu's staff and Garmadon's 4 swords.
  • Recreate and role-play exciting THE LEGO® NINJAGO® MOVIE™ scenes.
  • Jungle bridge measures over 6” (16cm) high, 8” (22cm) wide and 5” (14cm) deep.[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This set is the first set to introduce Kai's new hairpiece.
  • The map piece in this set seems to have pictures of the Destiny's Bounty, Temple of the Ultimate Ultimate Weapon, and Master Falls itself.
  • The set's name seems to be a pun, as Master Wu falls from the bridge in the film The LEGO Ninjago Movie.
  • There is a cave under the ground under the archway.
  • The bridge itself is actually called the Bridge of Fallen Mentors.
  • This is the only The LEGO Ninjago Movie set to include Garmadon without Lloyd.
