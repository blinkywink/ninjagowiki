Overview

This article is about the set. For the actual mech, see Third Samurai X Mech.

71775 Nya's Samurai X MECH is a Ninjago - Crystalized set that was released in most countries on June 1, 2022 and on August 1, 2022 in the United States and Canada. It includes Dragon Armor Wu, Samurai X Nya, Golden Jay, Oni Garmadon, Nelson, The Mechanic, a Vengestone Warrior, General Pythor, and a Dragonide and the Samurai X Mech.

Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

"This mech was a real challenge. Not only was it huge, but it was also a lot more rounded and sculpted than I am used to. Under the watchful eye of Leonardo Lopez, I tried to perfect the design to the last little frill and detail. My favorite parts of this model are the way the X on the chest is built and angled and the color changed cockpits for the kneepads. From the first moment I saw those cockpits, I knew I wanted to try this and I think it works really well. One of the biggest challenges was what to do with the ankles, their articulation ended up being quite limited because of the weight, making the knees not nearly as exciting, but I have some ideas for how I can iterate on that in the future.

This set has a crazy amount of exclusive minifigures too."[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Pythor's tail piece features armor plating, as well as purple markings that keep continuity with his minifigure from 2015's 70750 Ninja DB X - an appearance that returns in Crystalized.
    • Of the Council of the Crystal King, Pythor is the only one with an exclusive leg/tail piece. The other generals reuse the same torso and leg parts - except Aspheera, whose purple tail appears in the Legacy Overlord figures.
  • This is the first appearance of Pythor's bleached form since 70596 Samurai X Cave Chaos in 2016.
  • This is Nelson's first appearance since The LEGO Ninjago Movie set 70657 Ninjago City Docks in 2018, and prior to that he appeared in 70589 Rock Roader in 2016.
  • Every minifigure, with the exception of Jay and the Vengestone Warrior, are exclusive to this set. That includes Wu, Samurai X Nya, Nelson, The Mechanic, and General Pythor.
    • Oni Garmadon is exclusive to this set in regions that don't sell the Ninjago Magazine, like the US.
    • However, the only exclusive part of The Mechanic is the face print.
  • Wu has on the Dragon Chestplate piece in this set.
  • This is the second largest Ninjago mech set, the largest being 71765 Ninja Ultra Combo Mech.
  • Several characters from the TV series and movie can be seen in the background of the box art.
    • Ed, Clutch Powers, and a Sushi Chef are pictured.
    • The Crystal Spiders also appear here, using the same design as in the show.
  • This is the last set of the original series.
