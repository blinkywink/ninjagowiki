Overview

70749 Enter the Serpent is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - Tournament of Elements set released in March 2015. It includes Jungle Lloyd, Jungle Jay, Master Chen, Kapau'rai, Zugu, the Anacondrai Temple, and the Staff of Elements.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Enter the Anacondrai Temple and rescue Jay!

Deep in the jungle, Jay is being held captive by the Anacondrai. Find your way to their temple with Lloyd’s map. Once you get there, beware – Kapau’rai is on guard with his Anacondrai Blade, and Zugu is armed with a crossbow. Do you dare to Enter the Serpent? There are traps everywhere, including hidden spring-loaded shooters in the stairs, poison balls dropping from the snake’s mouth and a trapdoor! Search the caves for a second map with directions through the Anacondrai Temple. Find your way across the suspension bridge to the prison tower and free Jay! Includes 5 minifigures with assorted weapons and accessories: Lloyd, Jay, Chen, Kapau’rai, and Zugu.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Lloyd's Jungle suit and Kapau's Anacondrai form are exclusive in this set.
    • This is technically the only Ninjago set to include Jay's Jungle suit, as it can also be found in 71215 Jay Fun Pack.
    • This is also the only set to include a caped Master Chen, which makes his minifigure more accurate to the show than his other variations.
  • The Staff of Elements in the set is black, with a straight staff and red crystal, unlike in the show where it had a curved, blue handle and a clear crystal.
  • Strangely, while Kapau is in Anacondrai form, Chen and Zugu are still in human form, whereas in the show, they were turned into Anacondrai simultaneously.
  • The set's name is likely based on the 1973 movie Enter the Dragon, which is what the Tournament of Elements season was inspired by.[2]
    • The phrase "Enter the Serpent" was previously used in a poster for Tomb of the Fangpyre that was inspired by the Enter the Dragon poster.[3]
