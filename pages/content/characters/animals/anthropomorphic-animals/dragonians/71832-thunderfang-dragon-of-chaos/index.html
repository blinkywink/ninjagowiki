Overview

This article is about the set. For the actual dragon, see Thunderfang.

71832 Thunderfang Dragon of Chaos is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 3 set that was released on March 1, 2025. It includes Nya and Lloyd in their Storm suits, Dark Merge Arin, Merge Sora, Tyr, Nokt, two Prismatic Blades, and Thunderfang.

LEGO.com description

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Tyr is exclusive to this set. However, his design closely resembles that of the Dragonian Warriors, but features longer horns.
  • In the show the chains are transparent, just like the Prismatic Blades, but in set they are gray.
  • Out of all the characters in the set, three of them, Thunderfang, Nokt, and Arin, all share the same voice actor in the TV show, Deven Mack.
  • In the show, Thunderfang's collar is rotated so that the golden ring is on top.
  • This is the second set to include two Prismatic Blades, the first set is 71830 Kai's Mech Storm Rider.
  • This is the cheapest way to get Storm Lloyd with his armor.
