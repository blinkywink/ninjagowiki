Overview

This article is about the set. For the actual vehicle, see Rattlercopter.

9443 Rattlecopter is a LEGO Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - Rise of the Snakes set that was released in 2012. It includes Kai ZX, Lloyd Garmadon, Fang-Suei, the Constrictai Staff, the Golden Thunder Bolt, two Golden Points, two Sly Vipers, and the Rattlercopter.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Charge into a sky-high villain battle and snatch the golden snake staff!

Lord Garmadon’s son Lloyd is getting away with the golden Constrictai staff – until he gets cornered by Fang-Suei, a fearsome soldier of the Fangpyre snake tribe. Will Lloyd taste the bite of Fang-Suei’s Rattlecopter and his mini-snake minions? Help Kai to swoop in on his fire-powered jetpack to snatch the staff and rescue Lloyd! Beware of the Fangpyre’s bite and its power to turn people into snakes! Grab the staff and the anti-venom hidden inside as you blast out of there! Includes Kai ZX, Lloyd Garmadon and Fang-Suei minifigures, Rattlecopter, golden Constrictai staff, 3 weapons, 2 mini-snakes and a jetpack.

  • Includes 3 minifigures: Kai ZX, Lloyd Garmadon, and Fang-Suei
  • Features mini-snake bomb function, 4 flick missiles, rotating blades, opening cockpit, and 2 snake spinner crowns
  • Accessories include golden Constrictai staff, 2 mini-snakes, and jet-pack
  • Weapons include 2 golden ninja daggers and thunderbolt dagger
  • Launch the mini-snake bombs from the Rattlecopter!
  • Blast into battle with Kai’s fire-powered jetpack!
  • Fire 4 flick missiles for an awesome air assault!
  • Rattlecopter measures over 10” (27 cm) long and 9” (23 cm) wide![1]
Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

Myself and my colleague Maarten Simons worked together on earlier versions of the rattlecopter and we both influenced the future look of all snake vehicles, such as the head, color combination and overall styling.

Some are confused as to why the front teeth wobble... it's so that the face doesn't get pushed up and broken when you place the helicopter on a flat surface.[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This set bears resemblance to 9442 Jay's Storm Fighter, as they are both air vehicles about the same size and are both used in Rise of the Snakes.
  • Numerically, this set marks the first appearance of Lloyd in a set.
  • The Golden Constrictai Staff is exclusive to this set, yet there are no Constrictai in this set.
