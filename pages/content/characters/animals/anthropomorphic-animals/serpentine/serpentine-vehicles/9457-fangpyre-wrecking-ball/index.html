Overview

9457 Fangpyre Wrecking Ball is a LEGO Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - Rise of the Snakes set that was released December 28, 2011. It includes Kendo Cole, Fangdam, Lloyd Garmadon, the Fangpyre Staff, Mezmo's Golden Hypno Fang, a Double-Bladed Axe, two Red Vipers, and the Fangpyre Wrecking Ball.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Rescue Lloyd and defeat the fearsome Fangpyre Wrecking Ball!

Lloyd Garmadon will stop at nothing to retrieve the golden Fangpyre staff from its slithery servants. Unfortunately for him, Fangdam activated the snakes Fangpyre Wrecking Ball crane to teach him a lesson. Can Lloyd still swipe the staff with Cole’s help? You decide! Watch out for the swinging ball, biting snakehead and venomous tail! Includes Kendo Cole, Lloyd Garmadon and Fangdam minifigures, Fangpyre Wrecking Ball crane, golden Fangpyre staff and 2 weapons.

  • Includes 3 minifigures: Kendo Cole, Lloyd Garmadon and Fangdam
  • Features golden Fangpyre snake staff, free-swinging wrecking ball, functioning winch, flexible snake tail, biting snake head, working treads and opening cockpit
  • Weapons include: golden double-bladed axe, lightning-bolt axe and 2 mini-snakes
  • Swing the Fangpyre Wrecking Ball!
  • Raise and lower the Fangpyre Wrecking Ball with the winch!
  • Open the cockpit for play inside!
  • Measures over 11” (30cm) long and 7” (18cm) wide[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In the show, the Fangpyre Wrecking Ball has a glowing face on its wrecking ball. Additionally, its piloting area is on the back of the machine, directly behind the crane, with the canopy shaped like a snake mouth and ropes attached from the tail to the wrecking ball.
  • This is one example of a set based on a vehicle from the show, not the other way around.[2]
