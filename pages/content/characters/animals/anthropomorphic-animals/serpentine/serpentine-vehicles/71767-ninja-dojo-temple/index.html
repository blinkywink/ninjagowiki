Overview

Not to be confused with the actual temple.

71767 Ninja Dojo Temple is a Ninjago: Core set that was released on January 1, 2022.[1] This set includes Lloyd, Cole, Kai, Nya and Wu in their Core suits, P.I.X.A.L., a Cobra Mechanic, a Boa Destructor, the Wisdom banner, a vehicle for the Snake warriors, a mech for Kai and a Dojo Temple.

Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

"I had a lot of fun designing this one as it was the first opportunity for me to do some more realistic architecture (Empire temple i'm looking at you). The ask was to create a new "hub" for the ninja from where they could venture out to the different adventures depicted on the other sets of the line. I tried to get a traditional look, still Ninjago style but a bit different from what we have done in the past few years, mainly the Temple of Spinjitzu. A lot was inspired by ideas, concepts and colour explorations done by my friend and colleague Markus Rollbühler."[3]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Until the release of 71786 Zane's Ice Dragon Creature, and 71787 Creative Ninja Brick Box the included variant of P.I.X.A.L. and Wu was also exclusive to this set.
  • This is the first set to include P.I.X.A.L. since the 2018 set 70651 Throne Room Showdown.
  • This is the first set to include P.I.X.A.L.'s hair in its design from Season 8 onwards.
  • The design of the temple is somewhat similar to the Temple of Fortitude, when the difference is the lack of dragon head in the front.
  • One of the stickered elements features a chart translating Ninjargon letters to English.
    • Akita in her wolf form is also shown on one sticker featuring a video game titled Street Ninja.
      • This video game series first appeared on the arcade cabinet in set 71741 Ninjago City Gardens.
    • On another sticker featuring a monitor a snake warrior is shown, although it is just a silhouette, which means it can be also used as Char, a Pyro Whipper or a Maaray Guard.
  • This is the first set to include Wu's face from The LEGO Ninjago Movie and is not a Legacy set.
  • Nya uses mini shooters in this set, symbolizing her Water powers, just as she did in the sets for Season 14.
