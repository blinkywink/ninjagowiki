Bite Cycles are small bike-like vehicles used and created by the Fangpyre from old motorcycles that were made by Ed.

History

History[]

Snakebit[]

They were originally built as small motorcycles that were built by Ed and were left in Ed & Edna's Scrap N Junk. They were bitten by some Fangpyre, transforming them into large bikes.

Never Trust a Snake[]

Some Bite Cycles are seen when the Fangpyre went to war with the Hypnobrai. Lloyd tries to escape by jumping on one, but the Bite Cycle throws him off.

The Rise of the Great Devourer[]

Six Bite Cycles, in three groups of two, were deployed against the Ninja as they pursued the Serpentine Express. Of the first group, the one piloted by a Venomari soldier was destroyed by Kai after he struck its hood with a katana, causing it to flip over and crash. The second one, piloted by a Hypnobrai soldier, was abandoned by its driver after the soldier leapt from the cycle onto Kai's Blade Cycle, but was thrown off.

The second group was deployed from the sides of the Serpentine Express and were piloted by a Fangpyre soldier and another Venomari soldier. These cycles were knocked off the road by a projectile hurled from the Samurai Mech.

The third group, piloted by a Fangpyre soldier and a Constrictai soldier, were abandoned when their drivers leapt onto Cole's Tread Assault and were subsequently hurled off.
