71776 Jay and Nya's Race Car EVO is a Ninjago: Core set that was released on January 1, 2022. The set includes Jay and Nya in their Dojo suits, a Boa Destructor, a Cobra Mechanic, the Teamwork banner, Jay and Nya's Race Car and a helicopter for the snake warriors.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Jay and Nya’s Race Car EVO (71776) building kit lets kids aged 7+ play out gripping adventures in NINJAGO® City. The ninja playset features a cool armored race car with two shooters and a detachable flyer and the snakes’ helicopter with rotating blades.

Ninja heroes vs. snake warriors
The impressive ninja playset includes 4 minifigures: Jay armed with a sword, Nya with a spear, Cobra Mechanic with a sword, and Boa Destructor. New for January 2022, when kids defeat the ninja enemies and complete their mission, they are rewarded with a collectible banner.

A digital experience for passionate LEGO® builders
The playset comes with interactive digital building instructions to make the building experience even more fun. Available in the free LEGO Building Instructions app for use on smartphones and tablets, the zoom and rotate tools help you visualize the model as you build. Builders can also save their progress at any point on the app.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the first car in Ninjago to have two seats next to each other.
  • The car in this set is similar to 71763 Lloyd's Race Car EVO, as both have very similar shapes and designs.
  • This set contains the largest vehicle for the snake warriors.
  • That is the third time Jay had a car. The first being Jay Walker One, and the second being Ninja Sub Speeder
  • This is Jay's first car to not be an open-wheeled car.
  • Strangely, on the 125th page of the instruction booklet for this set, Nya's left arm is detached from her torso.[2]
