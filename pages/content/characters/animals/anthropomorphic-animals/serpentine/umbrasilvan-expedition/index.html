Overview

“Our expedition is here to capture the monster so we can bring it back to Ninjago!”
— Rowan Rockspike stating the expedition, "Tonight We Hunt Monsters"

The Umbrasilvan Expedition was an expedition organized by the Explorer's Club and led by Reginald Slitherbottom to travel to Umbrasilvis and capture a Mutation Monster, planning to exhibit it as a Wonder of the Merged Lands for paying visitors.

History

History[]

The Weapon of Desolation created a Mutation Monster in Umbrasilvis. The creature's presence attracted attention, and rumors of the monster eventually reached the Explorer's Club. The club subsequently dispatched a team of explorers to Umbrasilvis aboard the Expanding Frontier, led by Reginald Slitherbottom.[1] Unbeknownst to the other members, Johnny Courageous had joined the expedition as an undercover Loyalist, hoping to locate the Weapon of Desolation by tracking the Mutation Monster.[2]

The expedition's arrival coincided with that of Sora, Wyldfyre, Frak, and Riyu, who had fallen through a pit in the Land of Eternal Dawn and emerged from the fountain in Umbrasilvis. Sora immediately recognized the Expanding Frontier as a potential means of returning to Ninjago, while Frak was less enthusiastic upon discovering that his father was among the explorers. When the ninja encountered the Explorer's Club, Reginald agreed to take them back to Ninjago, but only after the expedition had successfully completed its hunt for the Mutation Monster. Reginald subsequently organized a nighttime hunt for the creature. Before beginning the search, he introduced the Explorer's Club's Lariat Launchers, designed to restrain the Mutation Monster. He ordered the explorers to split up and search the surrounding area. Frak objected, arguing that they should remain together because they were unfamiliar with the terrain and did not yet understand the monster's capabilities. Reginald dismissed his concerns, mainly because whoever captured the creature first would receive the right to name it.[1]

During the hunt, the ninja and explorers encountered the Mutation Monster, which immediately attacked them. Reginald attempted to capture the creature with his Lariat Launcher but was unable to restrain it. Frak attempted to assist his father, but the instability affecting the Elemental Powers prevented him from using his Quake power effectively. Riyu consequently intervened, distracting the monster and giving the others an opportunity to escape. The creature nevertheless struck Riyu with a blast of its corrupted Elemental Energy, poisoning him. Sora subsequently constructed a containment unit and successfully captured the Mutation Monster. However, the corrupted energy caused Riyu to develop the Numbness.[1] The Umbrasilvans helped care for Riyu following the battle and came to regard him as a hero for protecting their village from the monster. This contrasted with the Explorer's Club's attitude, as the explorers remained primarily concerned with the Mutation Monster.[3]

After learning about the Numbness, Sora theorized that the Mutation Monster's Elemental Energy could potentially be converted into an antidote for Riyu. Freya provided Sora with the limited technological equipment available in the village, allowing her to construct a device capable of extracting Elemental Energy from the captured monster. While Sora and Frak went to obtain the energy, Reginald claimed credit for containing the Mutation Monster, much to Sora's frustration. He also considered the creature to be the property of the Explorer's Club and demanded payment from Sora for the energy she intended to extract. Sora ignored his demands and departed with Frak after obtaining the energy. The extracted energy was insufficient to cure Riyu, prompting Sora and Frak to return for more. Upon arriving at the containment site, however, they discovered that someone had released the Mutation Monster.[3] Sora and Frak began searching for the escaped creature while simultaneously attempting to determine who had freed it. During their investigation, Frak encountered a masked Johnny Courageous.[4]

The Mutation Monster eventually returned to the village and attacked the Umbrasilvans. Sora, Frak, and Wyldfyre confronted the creature while attempting to protect the village. During the battle, Sora managed to collect additional Elemental Energy from the monster. The creature subsequently transformed, becoming considerably larger and more dangerous. During the attack, the Mutation Monster restrained Frak and Wyldfyre, who were then saved by Gweeg when he used a Lariat Launcher against the monster. Sora returned to Freya's windmill and used the newly acquired energy on Riyu in another attempt to save him. The treatment inadvertently transformed Riyu. Empowered by his transformation, Riyu returned to the battle and defeated the Mutation Monster. Gweeg and the Umbrasilvans subsequently hailed the ninja as heroes for saving the village. Meanwhile, the Grand Inquisitor reported what he had witnessed to Dr. LaRow. By analyzing the information, LaRow concluded that the Weapon of Desolation had been divided into two fragments, with the other fragment hidden in the Land of Madness.[4]

Aftermath

Aftermath[]

Following the disappearance of the Mutation Monster, the Explorer's Club prepared to leave Umbrasilvis, since they saw little reason for the expedition to remain.[5] However, the subsequent Inquisition revealed Johnny Courageous's true allegiance and exposed his role as an undercover Loyalist.[2] Reginald's failure to recognize Johnny's betrayal severely damaged his reputation within the Explorer's Club while Gweeg was praised for his actions during the expedition and was promoted to a position above Reginald.[6]
