Overview

“Lucky for us, the last Serpentine were sealed within a tomb right under the city, so it's about time we pay them a little visit.”
— Cole to the ninja, "The Curse of the Golden Master"

The Serpentine village[1] is a village inhabited by the Serpentine, located in the tomb of the Stone Army in the Ninjago City sewers, exactly below Borg Tower.

History

History[]

After The Day Ninjago Stood Still[]

Over time when they were locked in the Stone Army Vault, the Serpentine adapted to it and made it their new home. Eventually, they managed to open the door, and some of them moved to Ninjago.

Rebooted[]

The Curse of the Golden Master[]

When the Nindroid crisis began, the ninja journeyed underground to find the Serpentine, seeking to know more about the Golden Master. Eventually, the ninja came across the village, and were confronted by Skales, who introduced them to their new life under the surface. Acidicus told the ninja of the curse of the Golden Master, before Cryptor arrived with the nindroids and attacked the Serpentine. The ninja protected their former adversaries, shutting down most of the nindroids. When they asked the Serpentine to help them in their quest, Skales told them they wished to stay out of the conflict. However, when the Overlord became the Golden Master, several of the Serpentine left the tomb and helped the people of Ninjago escape the ensuing war in the city.

After The Titanium Ninja[]

After the Golden Master's defeat, the Serpentine were accepted into Ninjago's society, and some abandoned the village in favor of living on the surface.[2][3]

Pythor's Revenge[]

After a short search for the Serpentine, Pythor found the Serpentine village, where he met his long-lost friend Skales. The Snake King didn't want to let him into the village, but Pythor finally convinced him. He tried to show the Serpentine that he was still worthy of being one of them, because he was the first to show them the Thermodynamic Temperature Gauge from the BorgWatch. When Skales Jr. began to believe Pythor, the ninja invaded the village and forced Pythor to escape.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Ancient History[]

When the ninja needed Skales' help with Aspheera, they found him in the sewers in the Serpentine village. Skales then took them to the Serpentine's library, where Acidicus explained to them everything they needed to know.

After The Calm Before the Storm[]

Shortly before Wojira flooded Ninjago City, the Serpentine left their village and came to the surface at the behest of the Police Commissioner. Later, the village was flooded with water, but after the defeat of Wojira, Nya returned the water to the sea.[4]

Crystalized[]

During Brave But Foolish[]

When Ninjago City was attacked by the Overlord and his crystal army, some of the Serpentine, including Skales, fled the village, although a few remained and were crystalized.[5]

Safe Haven[]

When Lloyd and Garmadon went to search for the Serpentine in the sewers, they visited the Serpentine's library, but found no one.

Compatible[]

While searching the library, Lloyd received communications from Wu, who inquired about everyone's status and subtly provided them with a rendezvous point.

Distress Calls[]

Lloyd and Garmadon continued to roam about the library in search of any Serpentine, but to no avail. Lloyd left a message on one of the chalkboards however before leaving.
