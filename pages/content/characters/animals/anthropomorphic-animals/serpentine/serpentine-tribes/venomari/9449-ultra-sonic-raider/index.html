Overview

This article is about the orginal set. For the vehicle, see Ultra Sonic Raider. For the Legacy remake, see 71739 Ultra Sonic Raider. For the set based off the upgraded version, see 70595 Ultra Stealth Raider.

9449 Ultra Sonic Raider is a LEGO Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - Rise of the Snakes set that was released On June 2, 2012. It includes Kai ZX, Jay ZX, Cole ZX, Zane ZX, Spitta, Pythor, the Shurikens of Ice, the Sword of Fire, the Nunchucks of Lightning, the Scythe of Quakes, the Fangpyre Fangblade, a silver katana, a black katana, a Silver Battle Mace, and the Ultra Sonic Raider, which includes fourteen golden sais.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Track down Pythor and the fang blade with the terrain-defying Ultra Sonic Raider!

In the amazing Ultra Sonic Raider, the Spinjitzu masters spot supreme leader Pythor and his protector Spitta, a soldier of the Venomari tribe, taking off with the Fangpyre fang blade. Chase them across everything with the all-terrain tracks or detach the flying vehicle, extend the golden blade wings and take the battle to the skies! If all else fails, crank up the huge speaker cannons and tame the snakes with some ancient snake music. Don't let them get away! Includes 6 minifigures: Cole ZX, Jay ZX, Kai ZX, Zane ZX, Pythor and Spitta. Set includes Ultra Sonic Raider, Fangpyre fang blade with red anti-venom capsule, 4 golden weapons, and 5 regular weapons.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This set has some inconsistencies with TV show:
    • In the set, the Ultra Sonic Raider can only hold two ninja, despite its show equivalent being able to hold all four.
    • Although the set contains a prison in the back, it is never shown in the TV series.
    • Pythor appears in this set, despite never interacting with the vehicle; by the time of the Raider's creation, he had already been eaten by the Great Devourer.
  • The set appears to represent all four of the LEGO versions of the Spinjitzu vehicles combined, just like the TV show equivalent.
  • According to the official set description, the music of the Sacred Flutes is being "some ancient snake music." However, it is most likely non-canon.
    • There is another oddity in the description, as it claims that the set comes with "5 regular weapons," which is either too low (if the sais are counted) or too high (if the sais are not counted).
  • Pythor's original minifigure is exclusive to this set.
  • This is the first set to include just the four original ninja; the other three are 70595 Ultra Stealth Raider, 70655 The Dragon Pit, and 71739 Ultra Sonic Raider.
  • This is the first threads with treads as threads in the forepart. The other three are 70595 Ultra Stealth Raider, 70654 Dieselnaut, and 71739 Ultra Sonic Raider.
    • A major flaw of this set was the inability of its treads to move on a flat surface, such as a tile floor. The new sets fixes this flaw by having rubber studs all over its treads, allowing for increased traction on all terrains.
  • In the designer video of this set, Zane has Sensei Wu's leg piece from the Epic Dragon Battle set.
