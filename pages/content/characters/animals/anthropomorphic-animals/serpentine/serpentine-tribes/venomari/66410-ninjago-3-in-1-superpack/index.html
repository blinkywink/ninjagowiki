Overview

66410 Ninjago 3-in-1 Superpack is a set that was released in 2012. It includes 9440 Venomari Shrine, 9441 Kai's Blade Cycle, and 9444 Cole's Tread Assault, with the minifigures being Cole ZX, Skales, Zane ZX, Kai ZX, and Rattla.
