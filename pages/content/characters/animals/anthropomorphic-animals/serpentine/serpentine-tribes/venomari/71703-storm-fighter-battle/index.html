Overview

This article is about the legacy 4+ set. For the original set, see 9442 Jay's Storm Fighter. For the Legacy remake, see 70668 Jay's Storm Fighter. For the actual vehicle, see Storm Glider.

71703 Storm Fighter Battle is a Ninjago: Legacy Wave 3 4+ set that was released on June 1, 2020 in most countries, on August 15, 2020 in most US stores, and on August 24, 2020 in Canada and LEGO stores in the US. It includes Legacy (dragon) Jay, Legacy (dragon) Kai, Legacy Lasha, the Sword of Fire, a 4+ model of the Storm Glider, a Serpentine Quad Bike and a small temple.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Lasha's truck resembles a miniature remake of the Fangpyre Truck from 9445 Fangpyre Truck Ambush, though Lasha is a member of the Venomari.
    • This makes it the first villain vehicle to be remade in the Legacy line aside from Eyezor's car in 71740 Jay's Electro-Mech.
  • It is one the 4+ sets in the Legacy wave the others being 71701 Kai's Fire Dragon, 71734 Kai's Blade Cycle, 71740 Jay's Electro-Mech, and 71749 Final Flight of Destiny's Bounty.
  • This is the second Legacy remake of the Storm Glider. The first was 70668 Jay's Storm Fighter.
    • The Blade Cycle and Flame also got 2 Legacy sets, with one of each being a 4+ set.
    • And it looks similar to the velocity jet with a blue jet and a green wheeled vehicle from 71709 Jay and Lloyd's Velocity Racers, which is a 2020 exclusive set
