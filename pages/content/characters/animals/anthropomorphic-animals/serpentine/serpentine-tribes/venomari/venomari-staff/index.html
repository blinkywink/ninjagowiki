Overview

“I fought hard for this staff and will not give it up easily.”
— Acidicus to Pythor, "The Snake King"

The Venomari Staff is one of the Serpentine Staffs. It belongs to the Venomari general, Acidicus, and holds a vial of anti-venom that can instantly cure the Venomari venom.

When mixed with the anti-venom of the other four Serpentine Staffs, a white liquid is formed that, when it drips on a piece of paper, creates a map to the four Fangblades.

At some point after the Final Battle, Acidicus obtained a replica of the staff, which was green in color.

History

History[]

Rise of the Snakes[]

Can of Worms[]

Acidicus carried the staff with him when the Venomari and Constrictai ambushed the Ninja within the Toxic Bogs. He later carried the staff again during the meeting to unite the tribes.

The Snake King[]

At the city of Ouroboros, Pythor challenged the four Serpentine generals for their staffs and allegiance. An insulted Acidicus declared that he had fought hard for the staff, and would not give it up easily. During the brief fight, Acidicus was seen striking Pythor's lower abdomen after the generals dogpiled Pythor. However, by using the Sacred Flute secretly given by Skales, Pythor defeated the four generals and briefly claimed their staves, demanding their allegiance before returning them as the generals bowed to Pythor.

Tick Tock[]

When Pythor struggled to find the map to locate the four Fangblades, the Serpentine generals took their staves back. When Skalidor claimed his, he accidentally knocked over Acidicus, who responded by repeatedly striking the former with the Venomari Staff and instigating a fight. As Acidicus and Skalidor briefly fight, their staves were knocked to the ground and spilled their antivenom, which combined together and led Pythor to realize the clue towards discovering the Map of Fangs. Pythor then gathered all five staves, including the Venomari Staff, and placed them facing downwards atop a pedestal, allowing the antivenom within all five to drip and produce a liquid that revealed the Map of Fangs.

The Green Ninja[]

Acidicus carried the staff as he accompanied Pythor and his group to the Fire Temple in search of the Fangpyre Fangblade.

All of Nothing[]

Acidicus is seen carrying the staff within the Serpentine's underground fortress, guarding the fangblades alongside the other generals. He would continue to carry it while observing the Slither Pit duel.

Day of the Great Devourer[]

Although not present on screen, it is implied that Acidicus brought the staff with him as he and the other Serpentine took refuge within the Fangpyre Tomb to hide from the Great Devourer.

Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

Darkness Shall Rise[]

Acidicus is seen holding the staff when Skales tries to convince the Serpentine to follow him, only for them to follow Garmadon after using the Golden Weapons to create the Black Bounty. He continues to carry it when the Serpentine Generals convey in the Ninjago Metro to brainstorm ways of winning back their followers. He later uses it in combat against the Ninja aboard one of the Metro trains, using it to defend himself while struggling against Zane, but was relieved of the staff seconds later. He would recover the staff later on in the battle, but would be defeated by Zane. Following the battle, Acidicus, Fangtom, and Skalidor managed to escape, taking their staves with them as they later joined Garmadon.

Pirates vs. Ninja[]

Acidicus is seen carrying the staff when he, Fangtom, and Skalidor question Garmadon about the functions of the Mega Weapon. He later is seen carrying it again while standing alongside Fangtom, Skalidor, and Garmadon after they reclaim the Black Bounty from Soto and his crew.

Child's Play[]

Acidicus is seen carrying the staff with him as the Serpentine rendezvous with Garmadon inside the Ninjago Museum of History.

The Stone Army[]

Acidicus and the other generals are seen carrying their staves as they observe a duel in Ouroborus and shortly thereafter, Garmadon's arrival aboard a Rattlercopter. After Garmadon reveals his intentions of seeking the Dark Island and that he needs a "few brave snakes" to join him, Acidicus raises the staff in the air alongside Fangtom and Skalidor, with all three, and a reluctant Skales, following Garmadon back onboard the Rattlercopter in search of the Dark Island, bringing the staves with them.

The Day Ninjago Stood Still[]

Acidicus is seen carrying the staff with him as the Serpentine tunnel below Ninjago City, eventually reaching the Tomb of the Stone Army. When the Stone Army left their chamber and imprisoned the Serpentine within their tomb, the staff was presumably imprisoned alongside them.

Rebooted[]

The Curse of the Golden Master[]

At some point after the Final Battle, Acidicus lost possession of the original Venomari Staff, but obtained a shiny, dark green staff, which he held while he explained the Curse of the Golden Master to the heroes.

LEGO.com descriptions

LEGO.com descriptions[]

Rise of the Snakes[]

The Golden Venomari Staff belongs to the Venomari General in command, Acidicus. It holds a vial of anti-venom that allows the user to stop hallucinating.[citation needed]

Rise of the Snakes (Museum)[]

Snake staff of the Venomari tribe and a symbol of the power of the Venomari general. It is designed to look like a snake wound around a pole, with the green symbol of the Venomari on the snake’s chest. It is one of five staffs in existence, one for each Serpentine tribe. Concealed within the staff is a vial of anti-venom which can be used to cure the hallucinatory effects of the Venomari attack. When the anti-venom is combined with that of the other four staffs, it creates a white liquid which provides a map to the location of the Fangblades.[1]
