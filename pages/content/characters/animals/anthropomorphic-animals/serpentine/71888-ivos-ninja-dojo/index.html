71888 Ivo's Ninja Dojo is a Ninjago Legends - Embers + Ashes Season 1 set that will be released on January 1, 2027. It comes with Jin, Mira, Nate, Tre, Master Ivo, Master Zane, Gigi, Duncan, Madge, Roland, the Oni Imperator, an Oni Warrior, Mira's hoverbike, and the Ninja Dojo.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Rise to the challenge of constructing this stunningly detailed LEGO® NINJAGO® Ivo’s Ninja Dojo collectible building toy from the NINJAGO Legends Embers + Ashes TV series, then explore the expansive interior’s many surprises. Remove levels to find hidden references, elemental lanterns, and more. Take advantage of falling parts and fighting mechanisms to defeat opponents, then ride off in victory on the hoverbike. Create an epic battle scene with 12 NINJAGO character minifigures. Equip Jin, Mira, Nate, Tre, Master Ivo, Master Zane, Gigi, Duncan, Oni Imperator, Oni Warrior, and Madge and Roland with swords, nunchucks, sai, a claw, and spears for the ultimate fantasy decor. Dive into an immersive 3D building experience with the LEGO Builder app, rotate models, track progress, and collect your builds. Mark an important milestone with an unforgettable gift for NINJAGO superfans ages 14 and up who love completing and proudly displaying intricate building model kits. Set contains 3,495 pieces.

  • TAKE ON A BIGGER BUILD – Construct an intricate model of Ivo’s Ninja Dojo and restage battle scenes from the LEGO® NINJAGO® Legends Embers + Ashes TV series with this building toy for teens
  • BRING THE BATTLE INSIDE – Create immersive action by moving ninja minifigures through sliding doors, dodging falling parts, and using fighting mechanisms across several rooms and detachable levels
  • STAGE AN EPIC DISPLAY – Equip Jin, Mira, Nate, Tre, Master Ivo, Master Zane, Gigi, Duncan, Madge, Roland, Oni Imperator, and Oni Warrior with a variety of weapons, then pose them in the dojo
  • DISCOVER UNIQUE FEATURES AND SECRETS – Park the hoverbike against the dojo with transparent bricks so it appears to float, and find surprise elemental lanterns, weapons, and more
  • GIFT IDEA FOR TEENS – Celebrate a major achievement with the ultimate gift for NINJAGO superfans and teens who enjoy immersive architecture and construction sets
  • EXPAND THE NINJAVERSE – Check out other LEGO® NINJAGO® sets (sold separately) to grow an exciting collection and recreate memorable moments from the animated series
  • YOUR CREATIVITY, YOUR WAY – Immerse yourself in new creative experiences with the LEGO® Builder app where you can access 3D instructions and keep track of your collection
  • DIMENSIONS – This 3,495-piece set measures over 14.5 in. (37 cm) high, 15.5 in. (40 cm) wide, and 10.5 in. (26 cm) deep[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The model was initially sketched by Chi Wing Lee and was later built by Marin Stipkovic.[2]
  • Tre, Master Zane, Gigi, Duncan, Madge and Roland is exclusive to this set.
  • This set was revealed at the China International Import Expo.[3]
