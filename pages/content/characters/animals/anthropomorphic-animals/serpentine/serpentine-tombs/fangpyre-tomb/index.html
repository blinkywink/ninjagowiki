The Fangpyre Tomb is a cave in the Cemetery of Souls where the Fangpyre tribe was sealed away by the Elemental Alliance, in an attempt to prevent the five Serpentine tribes from uniting again, causing another Serpentine War.

It is located in a graveyard in the middle of a forest, with the entrance beneath a mutated tree that resembles a two-headed snake.

History

History[]

Prior to the series[]

After the Serpentine War, the Elemental Alliance chose five different tombs in locations that copied the symbol of the Serpentine on the map of Ninjago. Libber then used the Sacred Flute and escorted the Fangpyre tribe into their tomb beneath the Cemetery of Souls where they were sealed never to be freed.[1]

Snakebit[]

When the Hypnobrai betrayed Lloyd, he sought revenge by releasing another tribe to go to war with them. Using the Map of Dens, he decided to seek out the Fangpyre tribe and journeyed to the graveyard where their tomb was supposed to be hidden. Although frightened by the eerie surroundings, Lloyd found the entrance and opened the tomb, releasing the Fangpyre. Their general, Fangtom, was more than happy to help bring down the Hypnobrai but admitted that his tribe would need reinforcements before it could stand a chance against the other Serpentine.

Day of the Great Devourer[]

The Serpentine used the Fangpyre Tomb to hide from the Great Devourer after it proved impossible to control. As they gathered with several food supplies in hand, Fangtom and Skales briefly had an argument, only for the Great Devourer to pass by, forcing the Serpentine to flee inside. Later, following the Devourer's death, the cheers of Ninjago's citizens reached the Serpentine inside the tomb.

Shadow of Ronin (non-canon)[]

Lloyd and Wu descended into the Fangpyre Tomb in search of Misako, who had gone there in search of information about the whereabouts of the Primal Fulcrum. The pair eventually found Misako outside of the tomb, examining the surrounding Cemetery of Souls.
