Overview

71764 Ninja Training Center is a Ninjago: Core set that was released on March 1, 2022. It includes Core Jay, Core Zane, a Boa Destructor and a spinner.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

This Ninja Training Center (71764) playset is packed with everything NINJAGO® fans aged 7+ need to help their heroes master Spinjitzu. The impressive buildable kit offers a set of challenges for the ninja, including going down a slide, jumping over a snake pit and fighting a dummy mech.

Enthralling ninja battles
This Buildable kit includes 3 minifigures: Jay armed with a sword, Zane with a spinner and sword, and Boa Destructor with a sword, to play out death-defying battles. New for January2022, when kids defeat the ninja enemies and complete their training and mission, they are rewarded with a collectible banner.

Extra creative fun for LEGO® builders
The playset comes with interactive digital building instructions to make the building experience even more fun. Available in the free LEGO Building Instructions app for use on smartphones and tablets, the zoom and rotate tools help you visualize the model as you build. Builders can also save their progress at any point in the app.

  • Interactive playset – Kids can train their ninja heroes with a spinner at the action-packed LEGO® NINJAGO® Ninja Training Center (71764) through a series of challenges
  • 3 minifigures – Ninja toy includes Zane and Jay, both with swords, plus Boa Destructor with his own large sword, for kids to stage death-defying battles
  • Spinning toy adventures – Kids can place a ninja in the spinner, pull the rip cord and watch as the ninja competes in a series of challenges including jumping a snake pitand fighting a dummy mech
  • Collectible banner – New for January 2022, when kids complete their missions they are rewarded with a Balance banner to display on the Ninja Training Center
  • More rewards – Look out for banners in 7 other NINJAGO® sets including Kai’s Fire Dragon EVO (71762), Ninja Dojo Temple (71767) and Lloyd’s Legendary Dragon (71766)
  • Build and play – This 524-piece construction set allows kids aged 7 and up to build as they play and can be combined with the Ninja Dojo Temple (71767), which is sold separately
  • Portable play – The largest building in the playset measures over 4.5 in. (11 cm) high, 2 in. (5 cm) wide and 1 in. (3 cm) deep, so passionate ninja fans can take it with them wherever they go
  • Interactive digital building – Using the free LEGO® Building Instructions app, builders can zoom, rotate and visualize a digital version of their model on their smartphones and tablets
  • Premium quality – For more than 6 decades, LEGO® bricks have been made to ensure they pull apart consistently every time. Ninja skills not needed
  • Always in safe hands – LEGO® building bricks meet stringent global safety standards[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This set can be combined with 71767 Ninja Dojo Temple.
    • This set includes Zane and Jay, while the other four ninjas are included in 71767 Ninja Dojo Temple.
  • The snake statue in this set is very similar to the statue from 71732 Epic Battle Set - Jay vs. Serpentine.
  • Although Zane is equipped with spinner in this set, the Zane's minifigure uses a normal Core suit instead of Spinjitzu Training suit.
