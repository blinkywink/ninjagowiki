The library is an underground hall that is located in the Serpentine village under Ninjago City. Acidicus is a main librarian.

History

History[]

After creating their village, Serpentine also built their library and Acidicus became a librarian there.

Ancient History[]

Kai, Cole, and Jay travel to the library to ask Skales more about Aspheera and the Treacherous Deceiver. Acidicus reads them a story and the three ninja "realized" who the deceiver was after they learned how the deceiver betrayed Aspheera.

Safe Haven[]

When Garmadon and Lloyd got to the library, they found out that it was abandoned and crystalized, and found a note that said the library was closed due to the Overlord's attack.

Compatible[]

After walking a while in the sewers, Garmadon and Lloyd accidentally found themselves at the Serpentine's library. Garmadon questioned why would someone build a library in a sewer with Lloyd answering the Serpentine.

Distress Calls[]

Lloyd and Garmadon rummaged through the library for any search of the Serpentine, only confirming that they had long abandoned it in the face of the invasion by the Crystal Army. Although Lloyd left a message on a chalkboard in case any snakes returned, he and Garmadon ventured back into the sewers in search of allies.

On their way back, Lloyd and Garmadon were in the library when they received a message from Wu, requesting status updates on their missions. Standing in the library, Lloyd informed Wu that his mission to recruit the Serpentine had been a failure and that they were all long gone.
