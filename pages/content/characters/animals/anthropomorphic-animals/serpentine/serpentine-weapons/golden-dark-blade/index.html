The Golden Dark Blade (also referred to as a big golden blade[1]) is a Ninjago weapon used by Chopov and Rattla. It is a golden cutlass with a heavily chipped blade, completely identical to the Silver Dark Blade apart from the golden hue. It is described as a highly desired weapon among the Skulkin due to its incredible power, and its potential is increased only if it is wielded by a master swordsman.
