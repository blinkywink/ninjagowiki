Anacondrai spears are weapons in Ninjago used by the Anacondrai Cultists. They are similar to Anacondrai Blades. One was hidden in the Borg Tower vaults.

History

History[]

Spellbound (flashback)[]

The Hypnobrai and Constrictai generals are seen wielding Anacondrai spears during the attack on Jamanakai Village.

Ninja Roll[]

Anacondrai Cultists were seen carrying the tan-edged spears as they patrolled the Noodle factory beneath Chen's Palace.

Spy for a Spy[]

Multiple cultists are seen carrying tan-edged spears in the Noodle Factory, guarding and watching the prisoners. Cole later fought against several of the spear wielding cultists as he created a diversion in an effort to steal Zugu's keys.

Kapau and Chope are later seen playing with their spears while listening to music that Dareth had inadvertently broadcasted through the D.B. Express's loudspeakers. After being yelled at by Eyezor, they, alongside multiple other spear wielding cultists, would investigate the waterfall for the source of the music.

Spellbound[]

Multiple cultists are seen carrying tan-edged spears in the Noodle Factory, though they departed alongside Zugu in search of Cole and Zane. One spear wielding cultist returned briefly to bring Dareth to the factory.

Later, when Clouse and a small group of cultists captured Shade in the forest, one of the cultists accompanying Clouse was carrying a spear.

The Forgotten Element[]

Cultists carrying spears escorted the captured Elemental Masters to the ceremony room, where others were present, standing guard with spears in hand. Kapau and Chope also carried spears while they served guard duty in front of a gate, watching as Eyezor approached and berated another spear wielding guard who had fallen asleep on duty.

As the recently drained Elemental Masters were brought to the factory, spear wielding cultists are seen patrolling the factory grounds.

After nightfall, Garmadon, Nya, and Lloyd fought against multiple spear wielding cultists as they broke into Chen's Palace to find and destroy the Staff of Elements. After Lloyd's defeat to Chen, Kapau and Chope carried spears as they escorted Lloyd to the ceremony room before discarding the spears to join the ritual chanting. Eyezor also carried a spear as he stood close to Chen and watched the ceremony proceedings. He would, however, be relieved of the weapon after being kicked in the chest by Kai and would not retrieve it again throughout the rest of the ceremony room battle.

Following the battle in the ceremony room, Kapau and Chope carried a spear each as they fled alongside Chen, Clouse, Zugu, and Eyezor while Griffin Turner, Gravis, and Mr. Pale carried spears as the Elemental Masters escorted the captured and defeated cultists outside the palace walls.

The Day of the Dragon[]

Chamille and several Noodle Factory Workers wielded spears as they guarded the captured cultists, watching as the Elemental Masters destroyed Chen's blimp and Chen's ferry to prevent Chen and the remaining cultists from escaping the island. Kapau and Chope also retained their spears as they were assigned to guard Skylor, whom the group managed to capture during the battle in the ceremony room. The two briefly pointed their spears at Skylor when she started them by rattling her chains and stated that she needed to scratch her face. Kapau obliged by moving his spear closer to Skylor's face so that she could scratch. The two would discard their spears after Chen ordered them to pursue Skylor, throwing away the weapons to drive their Condrai Crushers.

Later, Griffin Turner carried a spear as he and Neuro stood guard over the captured cultists, but would discard the spear as he ran off to inform the Ninja of Skylor's whereabouts.

The Greatest Fear of All[]

When the ninja went to Kryptarium Prison to see Pythor, Kapau'rai and Chop'rai attacked to kidnap him, with Kapau'rai wielding an Anacondrai spear. As the pair fought the ninja, sans Lloyd, Jay would relieve Kapau'rai of his spear by shocking him with bolts of lightning. The spear was thrown out of Kapau'rai's hands and landed at the cell of No-Eyed Pete, who uses it to flip a switch and free the prisoners. No-Eyed Pete would continue to wield the spear as he and the other inmates pursued the ninja through Kryptarium, though he seemingly discarded it at some point during the pursuit, as he no longer carried the spear by the time the inmates reached the prison courtyard.

Meanwhile, as Lloyd, Garmadon, and Wu escaped to the courtyard with Pythor, they were met with a larger force of transformed cultists who had just arrived at Kryptarium, some carrying Anacondrai spears. In the ensuing battle, one spear wielding cultist placed Lloyd in a chokehold as another wielding an Anacondrai blade approached him.

Anacondrai spears would be wielded again when the cultists attacked Jamanakai Village, marking the beginning of the Second Serpentine War.

The Corridor of Elders[]

The Anacondrai Cultists wielded the spears throughout their conquest of Ninjago. They would continue using them to battle against the ninja, Elemental Masters, and their allies within the Corridor of Elders. When the spirits of the Anacondrai generals were released from the Cursed Realm and began cursing the cultists, the cursed warriors would drop their weapons, including Anacondrai spears, as they were banished to the Cursed Realm. It is currently unknown where majority of the spears are following the battle at the Corridor of Elders, whether they have been discarded or are simply lost. However, at least one spear was recovered following the battle and was placed inside the Borg Tower vaults.

The Fall[]

Lloyd and Garmadon fought the Oni near an Anacondrai spear in the Borg Tower vaults.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • There appear to be two variants of the spear, one with a crystalline purple material forming part of the blade, and one without.
    • Just like with the Anacondrai Blades, the cultists wielded tan edged versions of these spears before the transformation spell was performed, after the spell was complete, they wielded the crystalline purple-edged versions of the spears.
  • Unlike the Anacondrai Blades, the spears do not appear in the sets, unless Chope's weapon in 70754 Jay's ElectroMech is meant to represent an Anacondrai spear.
