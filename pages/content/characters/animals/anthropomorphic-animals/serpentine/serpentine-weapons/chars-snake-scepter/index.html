Overview

Char's snake scepter[1][2] is a weapon used by Char.

History

History[]

Background[]

Sometime during his lifetime, Char gained his scepter and was buried in a sarcophagus after his death.

Powerless[]

When Char was brought back to life, he was resurrected with the scepter in his hand.

Ancient History[]

Char holds the weapon while talking to Aspheera.

Under Siege[]

Char uses the weapon while invading the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

Vengeance is Mine![]

Char escapes the fight with his scepter.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The snake scepter resembles a caduceus, Hermes' staff in Greek mythology, as both are staffs topped with two snakes.
  • In the sets, the snakes on the scepter are gray and not golden, as it is in the show.
  • The upper part of the scepter is depicted on the Serpentine book. It is unknown if there is an in-universe reason for this or if it is just the reuse of the same design.
