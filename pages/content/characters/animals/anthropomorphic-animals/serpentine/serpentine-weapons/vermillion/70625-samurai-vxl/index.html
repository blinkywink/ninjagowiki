Overview

70625 Samurai VXL is a LEGO Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - The Hands of Time set that was released on January 2, 2017. It includes Fusion Nya, General Machia, Rivett, Samurai X, the Slow-Mo Time Blade, and the Samurai VXL.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Steer the Samurai VXL towards the Slow-Mo Time Blade!

Steer the armored Samurai VXL vehicle to help Nya and new Samurai X defeat the Vermillion warriors and capture the Slow-Mo Time Blade. This detailed vehicle features an opening minifigure cockpit, special tilting four-wheel-drive steering and six-stud rapid shooters. This great set also includes four minifigures equipped with assorted weapons to intensify the role-play battle action.

  • Includes four minifigures: Nya, General Machia, Rivett and new-for-spring-2017 Samurai X.
  • The armored Samurai VXL vehicle features an opening minifigure cockpit with translucent-blue windshield, big rubber wheels, dual six-stud rapid shooters, a special tilting four-wheel-drive steering system and golden ninja blade elements.
  • Tilt the cockpit to steer the Samurai VXL around any obstacle.
  • The Time Blade stand has the detachable translucent-blue Slow-Mo Time Blade and swamp reed elements.
  • Weapons include Nya’s two katanas, Samurai X’s two golden katanas, General Machia’s Vermillion axe and Rivett’s Vermillion sword.
  • Accessory elements include General Machia’s Medusa hair with snake elements and Rivett’s Vermillion helmet.
  • Recreate and role-play epic scenes from the NINJAGO®: Masters of Spinjitzu TV show.
  • This set offers an age-appropriate build and play experience for ages 8+.
  • Samurai VXL measures over 3” (10cm) high, 7” (20cm) long and 5” (13cm) wide.
  • Vermillion egg measures over 1” (3cm) high, 1” (3cm) wide and 1” (3cm) deep.[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The new Samurai X is exclusive to this set, as is the Vermillion general Machia.
  • General Machia has a tail-like addition in this set, which she lacks in the TV Show. A leg piece is also included in the set to substitute for the tail.
  • This is the first to include more female characters than males and the only set that does not contain any male characters.
  • This set made its first appearance in the episode "Secrets Discovered" in different colors. It made its first appearance in "Out of the Fire and Into the Boiling Sea" in its set-accurate colors.
  • This is the last Ninjago set to feature Nya's face print that was used since the Possession wave, as in 70627 Dragon's Forge she received a new double-sided face print.
