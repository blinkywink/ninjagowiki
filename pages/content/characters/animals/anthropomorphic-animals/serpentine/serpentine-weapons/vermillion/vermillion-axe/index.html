Overview

The Vermillion axe is a type of weapon used by the Vermillion.

History

History[]

Season 7: The Hands of Time[]

A Time of Traitors[]

Multiple Vermillion Warriors are seen wielding axes when Jay and Cole confront a group of warriors in downtown. One uses their axe to cut open the manhole cover when Machia orders a retreat.

Scavengers[]

An orange Vermillion Warrior with a wastebasket over its head is seen wielding an axe in the powerplant.

A Line in the Sand[]

Multiple Vermillion Warriors wield axes during the skirmish for the Slow-Mo Time Blade in the Eastern Sea of Sand.

The Attack[]

Several Vermillion Warriors use axes during the attack on the Temple of Airjitzu. They also fall out of some of the Vermillion eggs that are slashed apart by Lloyd and are used by two warriors driving a Vermillion Invader.

Following Lloyd's attack, Machia calls for the sky team to begin their assault, with all warriors in the initial wave dual wielding axes.

Another axe wielding warrior is seen attempting to attack Wu, but is defeated by Zane.

Secrets Discovered[]

As the Time Twins and their forces move through the sewers, one of the warriors carrying Wu is seen carrying an axe in its other hand.

Pause and Effect[]

A few Vermillion Warriors carrying axes are seen patrolling the swamps as Cole and Jay make their way towards the building housing the kidnapped workers.

Multiple axe wielding warriors would later battle against the Ninja, with one in particular being struck by Cole's Earth Punch and knocked off a cliff, sending the axe down with it.

Out of the Fire and Into the Boiling Sea[]

An axe is present lying on the ground as Cole, Zane, Lloyd, and Jay make their way to the sewer swamps. Another is carried by a Vermillion Warrior standing behind Raggmunk and Blunck as they proclaim their victory against the fleeing Ninja.

Axes would be used again as the Vermillion Warriors attempt to prevent Borg from entering the Iron Doom's self destruction sequence and are carried onboard the Iron Doom as the army prepares to travel back in time.

Lost in Time[]

Returning forty years to the past, the Vermillion hordes wielded axes against the Elemental Alliance. Following the Time Twins' orders for all snakes to merge with the Iron Doom however, all weaponry and armor, including the axes, were discarded as the snakes broke down their humanoid forms to return into the machine.
