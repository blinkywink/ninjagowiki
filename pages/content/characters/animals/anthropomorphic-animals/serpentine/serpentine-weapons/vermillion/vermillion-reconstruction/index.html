Vermillion reconstruction is a special ability of the Vermillion. If the user is harmed, destroyed, wounded, or damaged, they can regenerate, heal, rebuild, reform, resurrect, or reconstruct themselves.

Effects

Effects[]

  • Reforming/Regeneration - If a Vermillion is damaged or dispersed, the user can rebuild or regenerate themselves by pulling the snakes that make up their bodies together to form their being.
    • Merging/Larger Body Formation - This ability can also allow multiple Vermillion to merge into a Buffmillion or a Vermillion samurai.
    • Self-Resurrection - Vermillion can also use this ability to revive themselves whenever they are destroyed.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Lack of Body/Metal Dependence - If the user is separated from metal, the power is useless, and it cannot reform. They also appear to require metal or objects in order to reform.
  • Forceful Separation - The user can be forcefully separated in various ways, mainly through Elemental Powers, such as Lightning, Water, Fire, or Sound, or by stripping an area of all metal components that can be used by the snakes.
