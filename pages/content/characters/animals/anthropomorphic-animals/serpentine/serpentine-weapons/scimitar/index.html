The scimitar,[1] also called the sickle sword,[citation needed] is a weapon used by the Ninjago soldiers, Pyro Vipers, and various skeletons.

History

History[]

Tournament of Elements[]

Spellbound (flashback)[]

The Ninjago Soldiers used their scimitars to fight in the first Serpentine War.

Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

The Day Ninjago Stood Still[]

A scimitar was present on the wall of weapons in Grand Sensei Dareth's Mojo Dojo when it was attacked by a Stone Warrior.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Ninja vs Lava[]

A couple of Pyro Vipers were released from their sarcophagus and hold scimitars when attacking the ninja.

Powerless[]

When Aspheera released some Pyro Vipers, they held a scimitar in their hand.

Ancient History[]

The Pyro Vipers attacked Lloyd and Nya with their scimitars.

Under Siege[]

The Pyro Vipers had their scimitars when they invaded the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

The Explorers Club[]

The Pyro Vipers are seen with their scimitars.

Vengeance is Mine![]

The Pyro Vipers used their scimitars to attack the ninja.

A Cold Goodbye (dream)[]

In P.I.X.A.L.'s dream, the Pyro Vipers had scimitars.

Awakenings (flashback)[]

In Zane's flashback, a Pyro Viper with a scimitar is included.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Sword Shatters[]

Two scimitars were on display in the Temple City Museum. During Lloyd's match with Zeatrix, he grabs a scimitar to fight off Zeatrix's trident where he used Geo's powers to fuse the weapons together. Arccrack caught it.

Elements of Betrayal[]

When Geo got his powers back, he unfused the trident and scimitar and refused the weapons into a double-bladed weapon when fighting Cinder and the Wolf Mask Warriors.
