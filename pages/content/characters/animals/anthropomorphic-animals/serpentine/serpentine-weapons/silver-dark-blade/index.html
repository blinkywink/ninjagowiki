Overview

"Hey, where'd you guys come from?"
This article is about a subject that is relevant to both the Ninjago and Legends of Chima franchises. Information may be more directed toward one theme than the other.

The Silver Dark Blade,[4][5] also known as Machete and Saber,[6] is a Skulkin, Serpentine, Munce and Dragonian weapon. It appears to be a silver sword with a short hilt, only large enough for a single hand, a wide handguard, and a large, pockmarked blade that curves backward near the tip.

History

History[]

Mother Earth (flashback)[]

Venomari used the sword in the battle with Lilly.

The Golden Weapon[]

Skulkin wielded the blades when fighting the ninja.

Weapons of Destiny[]

Skeletons wielded the blades when fighting the Wu.

Dark Island Trilogy, Part 3[]

A group of Skulkin wielded this weapon when Clouse was banished to the Underworld.

Grief-Bringer[]

Murt wielded a blade when the Munce and Geckles faced each other.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

A collector’s edition of the Dark Blade, the Silver Dark Blade was created in limited supply. It’s truly brutal, so only the most dangerous skeletons have one... they won it a deadly contest.[4][5]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Despite the fact that in sets, Condrai Crushers use Stone blade as a part of the vehicle, it is represented by regular Silver Dark Blade in the show, making the only appearance of non-modified version of the blade.
  • The Silver Dark Blade is sometimes referred to as a machete (2173 Nuckal, 2256 Lord Garmadon, 2508 Blacksmith Shop)
  • In Dragons Rising Season 3, two ninja briefly used a blade: Nya and Frak. Both of these uses are symbolic, as Nya worked in the blacksmith and even had this weapon in "Secrets of the Blacksmith", and Frak uses the blade just like the other Serpentine in Season 1.
