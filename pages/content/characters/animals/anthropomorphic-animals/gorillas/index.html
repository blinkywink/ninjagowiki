Overview

“Great. Either the gorillas or the sand are going to eat us. Which is worse?”
— Lloyd to Wu, Dark Island Trilogy, Part 1

Gorillas are a species of hominids that live in the Dark Island and Chima.

History

History[]

The Four Trials (non-canon)[]

Along with some other animals, a gorilla attacked Zane and Lloyd during one of Wu's trials.

Curseworld, Part II[]

Gorzan and his friends were enjoying a relaxing day before being interrupted by Morro and Lloyd, who fell into the area from a portal. He then glanced the others before Morro opened another portal, with both him and Lloyd falling in.

Dark Island Trilogy - Part 1[]

After arriving at the Dark Island, Lloyd took a banana from a tree, which really "upset" them, so they attacked. Getting trapped alongside Wu in a pool of quicksand during the chase, the two manage to escape using Airjitzu.
