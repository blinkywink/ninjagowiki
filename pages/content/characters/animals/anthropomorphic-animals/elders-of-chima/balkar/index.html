Overview

Balkar is an elder from the Bear Tribe.

History

History[]

Legends of Chima, Season 1[]

Foxtrot[]

Balkar was present for the race in the Grand Arena when Bladvic slipped on banana skins, causing him to lose the race and Balkar was arguing with Grumlo about how one another cheated and the fighting goes on long enough for Worriz and Crooler to break into the Lion Temple and steal the Chi from the Sacred Pool.

The Chi Jackers[]

As Laval and Lagravis arrived to deliver Chi to Balkar and Bladvic, the Wolves attacked and attempted to steal the Chi. Balkar and Bladvic, however, go to sleep until a miracle happens. When they do eventually wake up again, they are surrounded by orbs of Chi that was dropped by Eglor when the Wolves fired at his jet.

Crocodile Tears[]

Balkar is present in a meeting to Lagravis, Grumlo, Ewald, Rawzom and Bezar, when Skinnet comes along and tells them that the Wolves are treating the Crocs fairly well by giving them sandwiches and fruit shakes and when he wished he could be there too, Balkar spoke up saying how wrong this all was. He eventually agreed to have a conference later in the week to discuss whether or not to free the Crocs, but Laval and Eris already took care of that.

Fake CHI, Real Trouble[]

Balkar is riding on his Speedor sleeping, when Bladvic wakes him up to try and tell him about the Wolves stealing Chi from all over Chima but Balkar goes back to sleep. Just then, the Wolves attack them once more and are successfully in stealing their Chi. After Balkar and the Bears returned to the Bear Caves, Cragger came rolling by and supplied the tribe with a strange artifical form of Chi he got from Reegull, dubbed, Chigull. However, once Cragger learns of the side effects, he rushes back to each tribe he gave the Chi to, including the Bears and the Bears have already used the Chigull, temporarily turning Balkar and the others into birds.

Legends of Chima, Season 3[]

The Snowball Effect[]

Balkar is back at the Bear Caves when Laval has the Lion Legend Beast send out a distress roar to check in on the Bear Legend Beast to make sure the Bears are safe. However, the Vultures finally decide that they waited long enough to capture the Bears and so they freeze them to take back to Sir Fangar.

A Very Slippery Slope[]

Balkar is freed from the Ice Fortress by Laval, Cragger and the Phoenix child, Flinx and they all manage to flee before the Grand Archway canyon collapsed and Balkar and the Bears went back to their Bear Caves.

A Spark of Hope[]

Balkar is asleep back at the Bear Caves when the Bear Legend Beast uses its fire breath to melt the ice and went back to sleep just as the Hunters showed up and were discussing what to do with the Bears.

Wings of Fire[]

Once Sir Fangar sees that the battle is not going his way, he orders Vornon and VoomVoom to go awaken the Ice Bears. Once they power up the Ice Bears in the Gorge of Eternal Depth, Balkar and the Bears wake up with a new found desire to defeat the Ice Bears and so they leave the Bear Caves to go help the Tribes of Chima.

The Heart of Cavora[]

Balkar and the Bears arrive to the Lion City to help the Tribes of Chima defeat the newly released Ice Bears and the Hunters. Once the Heroes of Chima and Flinx fly into the heart of Mount Cavora and activate the Second Great Illumination, everything in Chima is healed, including the Hunters, causing the Bears to halt in their assault on the Ice Bears and they all go back to sleep after the battle.
