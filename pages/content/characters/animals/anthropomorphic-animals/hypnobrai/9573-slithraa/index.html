Overview

This article is about the spinner set. For the character, see Slithraa. For the character card, see Card 24 - Slithraa (Spinners).

9573 Slithraa is a Ninjago Spinners - Rise of the Snakes set that was released on August 28, 2012. It includes Slithraa, his spinner, a blue Serpentine-style crown, the Hypnobrai Staff, a Translucent Blue Viper, a Chained Sai, a Character card, four Battle cards, and three bricks.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the only spinner to include a Serpentine Staff, most likely as a reference to the fact that Slithraa was the general of the Hypnobrai before Skales took his place.
  • This is the cheapest way to get Slithraa.
