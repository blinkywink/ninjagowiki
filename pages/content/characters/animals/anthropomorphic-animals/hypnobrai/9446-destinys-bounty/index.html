Overview

For other uses, see Destiny's Bounty (disambiguation).

9446 Destiny's Bounty is a LEGO Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - Rise of the Snakes set that was released on December 27, 2011. It includes Kendo Jay, Kendo Zane, Sensei Wu, Lord Garmadon, Skales, Slithraa, the Hypnobrai Staff, the Destiny's Bounty, and nineteen weapons (a Nin-Jô, a Dark Talon, two Sais, a Gold Scimitar, a Major Spear, two golden Bowie Knives, four Staffs of the Dragons, four Silver katanas, and four black Crossbows).

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Sail into an epic battle on board Destiny’s Bounty!

On a dangerous journey aboard Destiny’s Bounty, an ancient shipwreck, Sensei Wu and the ninjas have located the powerful golden Hypnobrai staff deep inside of the secret snake shrine. As they land the flying ship in the water to seize the staff, Sensei Wu, Zane and Jay get a scaly surprise from the Hypnobrai General, Skales, and his accomplice warrior, Slithraa. Sensei Wu fires the ship’s cannon into the battle until his ancient enemy, Lord Garmadon, appears. Can Sensei Wu protect the Ninjago treasures on board without the help of the ninjas? Choose your battle in an epic showdown where the fate of the Ninjago world hangs in the balance! Includes 6 minifigures: Sensei Wu, Kendo Zane, Kendo Jay, Lord Garmadon, Skales and Slithraa; Destiny’s Bounty ninja ship, snake staff shrine, golden Hypnobrai staff and 16 weapons.

  • Includes 6 minifigures: Sensei Wu, Kendo Zane, Kendo Jay, Lord Garmadon, Skales and Slithraa.
  • Features Hypnobrai snake shrine and golden Hypnobrai staff.
  • Weapons include: 2 golden daggers, big spear, bo staff, 2 sai, golden serrated blade, 4 ninja swords, 4 crossbows and ninja claws.
  • Transform Destiny’s Bounty into a flying ship!
  • Fire the cannon shooter with 4 cannonballs!
  • Protect the secret treasure on board at all costs!
  • Stow the ninja weapons in the ship’s weapon racks!
  • Launch the working anchor when you reach the shrine!
  • Measures over 16” (43 cm) long, 10” (26 cm) tall and 11” (30 cm) wide.[1]
Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

"The Bounty was developed by me from it's earliest days when it was still a thought in my head. I was always fascinated by fantasy worlds, movies and books so I insisted on the flying ship idea to be written into the show. The show version of the Bounty has tons more features and size, this is something I couldn't do anything about, since the pricepoint of the set itself was set in stone."[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This set included Kendo variants of ninja even though Kendo armor was presumably destroyed with the Monastery of Spinjitzu before finding the Bounty in the show.
    • Although Jay used his Kendo Armor when testing his new training bot in "Can of Worms", hinting that they may have been recovered or new ones were made.
  • The map of the Golden Weapons is included in this set even though the ninja have found the Golden Weapons at this point.
  • Excluding 71749 Final Flight of Destiny's Bounty, this is the cheapest and only Destiny's Bounty model to not be $100 USD and over.
