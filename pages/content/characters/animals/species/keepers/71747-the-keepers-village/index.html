Overview

Not to be confused with the actual village.

71747 The Keepers' Village is a Ninjago - The Island set that was released on March 1, 2021. It includes Island Kai, Island Jay, Island Cole, Chief Mammatus, a Thunder Keeper, the Stone Guardians, the Keepers' Village, the Storm Amulet, a prison cell, and two traps.

Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

"I designed this set for the Seabound [note: The Island] season of Ninjago. A lot of inspiration for the jungle traps on the way from the beach to the Keeper's Village comes from classic movies and cartoons. The idea of incorporating the dragon head rock formation serves to create a unique and memorable silhouette for something that would otherwise would have been a simple looking cave. I am very proud of using the leaf element as a lever to open the secret escape route as well as the stone golems!"[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This set is the cheapest way to get Island Kai, Island Jay, and Chief Mammatus.
  • Island Cole is exclusive in this set.
  • The village in the set is very different from the counterpart in the show, because instead of a dragon head located in the middle of a large village with a throne not in the highest place and the Amulet mechanism, there is a dragon's head on the side, a throne on the highest place and the Storm Amulet on the Stone Guardian's head.
  • This set is very similar to 71717 Journey to the Skull Dungeons, as they both have a similar layout consisting of one cave with space and the collectible item of the season and a smaller attachable part consisting of a path to the main segment of the set.
