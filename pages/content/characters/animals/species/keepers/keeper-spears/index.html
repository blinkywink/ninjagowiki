Keeper spears are electrical spears that are mainly used by the Keepers.

History

History[]

Master of the Sea (flashback)[]

The spears were used in the fight against Wojira in the Battle of Nine Days.

The Island[]

The Keepers of the Amulet[]

The Keepers used the spears to ambush the ninja and made sure that they didn't attempt to escape when they were being brought to Mammatus.

The Gift of Jay[]

After taking most of the ninja to a cage, they are used to try their spears go zap Zippy, but the dragon dodges them and Lloyd uses a spear to free the ninja and the others.

The Keepers later zapped the ninja when they find them about to steal the Storm Amulet.

The Tooth of Wojira[]

The Keepers used their spears to battle the njnja. Later, after realizing that Clutch Powers stole the Storm Amulet, Nya used a spear to zap Clutch.

Seabound[]

The Storm Amulet[]

When Jay comes out of the Hydro Bounty, spears are pointed at him by the Keepers. Though they realized it was him and took the spears back.

Crystalize[]

Dragon Form[]

The Keepers used the spears to battle the Crystal Warriors.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Electric Blast: The spears are able to generate electricity/lightning of various intensities over a specific target area causing great damage and/or delivering great shock waves of pure force. They are most noticeably used to electrocute opponents.
    • Electric Ball Projection: The spear are able to generate balls of electricity/lightning as seen in "The Gift of Jay", as the Keepers were shooting balls of electricity to try to stun Zippy.
    • Electric Beam Emission: The spears are able to generate a concentrated beam of electricity/lightning to stun opponents.
    • Electric Bolt Projection: The spears are able to generate strong bolts of electricity/lightning, Mammatus was seen shooting a bolt into the sky making the door shine.
