Overview

“Akita is the love of my life…but I'm not sure that I am hers. Her people will always come first. I think you and her are alike in that way.”
— Beniro to Lloyd, "A Frozen Scar"

Beniro is a Formling who lives in the Never Kingdoms and Akita's fiancé. He has the ability to morph between the form of a fox and a human.

History

History[]

Background[]

After the Liberation of the Never-Realm, Beniro met Akita, and they fell in love. Akita shared stories with Beniro about Lloyd and how he saved the Formlings. As time passed, particularly after the Merge, their relationship progressed to the point of engagement.

A Frozen Scar[]

When the ninja arrived at the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms, Akita hurried to reunite with Lloyd, while Beniro followed closely behind. Upon seeing them together, Lloyd felt somewhat devastated upon realizing that Beniro and Akita were now engaged. The ninja needed to venture into the Abyss of Eternal Night to destroy the Weapon of Desolation, but required a guide capable of navigating the terrain by scent. Since Akita had to attend the Summit of the Merged Lands, she proposed that Beniro could help the ninja on their quest. Beniro eagerly accepted the offer, which only served to irritate Lloyd.

The following morning, Beniro transformed into his fox form to lead the group, but as they journeyed, the ninja lost sight of him just before a sudden rockslide obstructed their path. Using Lloyd's scent, Beniro managed to reunite with the team just as it was getting dark. While setting up for the night, Lloyd asked Beniro about the Abyss of Eternal Night. Beniro detailed how it was unintentionally created by the First Spinjitzu Master when he destroyed a cursed thorn embedded in the paw of the First Master of Form. He also shared how the Master of Form had bestowed her shapeshifting abilities upon the Formlings, who the First Spinjitzu Master subsequently forbade from approaching the Abyss. Grateful for Beniro's willingness to defy his tribe's beliefs to help the ninja, Lloyd thanked him. Beniro affirmed that he would do anything for Akita, but when Lloyd echoed that sentiment, Beniro turned away, unsettled.

The following day, Beniro and the ninja made their way to the Boreal River. Due to the deteriorating condition of the containment unit for the Weapon of Desolation, they needed to traverse the river swiftly to reach their destination. However, Beniro was apprehensive about the corrupted ice, so he joined the ninja in the Land Bounty, where he engaged in conversation with Lloyd. Despite Beniro's attempts to connect, Lloyd attempted to cut the discussion short, concentrating instead on the task of crossing the river. Sensing the tension, Beniro quickly apologized, worried that he had offended Lloyd. Just as Lloyd was about to consult with Beniro, the Weapon of Desolation suddenly activated, corrupting the latter by causing him to transform into a feral version of his fox form.

He seized the weapon and rushed outside, where he hurled it into the ice of the river. This unleashed a horde of Ice Monsters that attacked the ninja. Zane struggled to grab the weapon just as Beniro charged at him. Just then, Zilvar and Grimtak intervened, pushing Beniro back, but he quickly shifted his focus to Lloyd. Lloyd managed to pin Beniro down, allowing Zilvar to secure the Weapon of Desolation. Lloyd urged Beniro to remember Akita, which helped him break free from the corruption and revert to his human form. Beniro apologized to the ninja, stating he could no longer join their quest. He handed them a map to the Abyss and prepared to return to the monastery, waiting for Akita. Beniro explained that while Akita was the love of his life, he doubted his feelings were reciprocated, as their tribe would always take precedence. He noted a similarity between Lloyd and Akita in that regard before leaving.

Appearance

Appearance[]

In his fox form, he is an orange fox with black sclera and yellow pupils. The top of his ears are grey and fade into orange. He has a white marking going from his muzzle to his chest. Like his ears, his paws fade from orange to gray. The tip of his tail is white.

Personality

Personality[]

Beniro is a dedicated and deeply supportive Formling. He is highly motivated by his affection for Akita, openly stating that he would do anything for her. He possesses a humble and somewhat modest nature, which is evident when he meets Lloyd. He is also polite and quick to avoid misunderstanding, immediately apologizing when he worries he might have inadvertently offended Lloyd. Beniro is exceptionally selfless and demonstrates a strong sense of duty, willingly going against his tribe's traditional beliefs to guide the ninja toward the Abyss of Eternal Night. After he was freed from the Weapon of Desolation’s control, he apologizes for his actions and resourcefully provides the ninja with a map so they can safely continue their journey without him. He also carries a sense of romantic realism and emotional vulnerability, as he acknowledges that Akita's devotion to their tribe will always take precedence over their relationship.

Relationships

Relationships[]

Akita[]

Akita is Beniro's fiancé, and the two are to enter a Form-Bonded marriage when the season changes. He shows a great deal of affection towards her, from stating he would do anything for her to the thought of her freeing him from the corruption of the Weapon of Desolation. Later, Beniro said that while Akita was the love of his life, he doubted he was hers, as their tribe would always take precedence in her mind.

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, Beniro was made to spark relationship drama with Lloyd and Akita.[1]
  • Beniro shares various design elements with other characters from Ninjago and other LEGO themes:
    • He reuses the same animal pendent and cape as Akita, though it is unknown why he has a multi-tailed cape when his fox form only has one tail.
    • His fox form reuses a retextured model of Akita's wolf form from Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu with the tail from her current wolf model.
    • His animal hat is a modified version of Akita’s wolf one.
    • His robes are a modified version of Kataru's.
    • He reuses a modified generic male face.
