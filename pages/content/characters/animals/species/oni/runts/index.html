Overview

Runts[1][2] are Oni.

History

History[]

The End Begins[]

After kidnapping Fritz, the Diviner initiates an Oni ritual on a Runt, forcing an agonizing scream from him as Dark Magic coursed through his body. Using his Elemental Powers, Cole restrained the Diviner, determined to save Fritz. Cole hurled a barrage of massive rocks at the Runt, but the onslaught left the Oni completely unharmed. The Runt lunged forward. Cole blocked the attack with his hammer, coated his fists in thick Earth gauntlets, and charged right back. Fueled by the Diviner's magic, the Runt's muscles swelled as his physical size and power increased. He took Cole's hammer and countered, shattering Cole's stone gauntlets. Before the Runt could deliver a finishing blow, Cole retaliated with a kick to the face. Cole summoned massive stone pillars directly beneath the Runt, launching him high into the sky.

Mid-air, the dark magic mutated the Runt yet again, transforming him into a giant. As the Runt tried to grab Cole, the latter dodged the attack. Spotting the runes emblazoned on the Runt's stomach, Cole struck it with his katana—only for the blade to shatter against it. Cole spotted the ritual runes nearby. As the giant attempted to stomp him into the dirt, Cole sprinted toward the runes, utilizing his Earth element to reforge his shattered blade. Realizing Cole's strategy, the Runt slammed his fists into the earth, fracturing the entire battlefield. Cole leaped onto a floating fragment of debris, conjured a heavy chain from the stone, and whipped it over the Runt's horns. Locking the chain tight, Cole used Spinjitzu, dragging the giant behind him as he rocketed toward the center of the runes. He drove his reforged sword deep into the ritual circle. A colossal blade erupted from the ground, impaling the Runt and causing him to explode.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In the sets and promotional material for Duskfall, Runts are simply referred to as "Oni Warrior" and "Oni General."[3] It is unknown whether these titles are canonical roles or merely descriptions used to designate their different forms.
  • The Runts' appearance varies drastically between the sets and the show. In their original form, the minifigures feature shoulder armor that is absent in the show. During the second phase of their empowered form, the set version uses a different pants design. In their final form, the sets depict them with the same base appearance as their original form, but with golden shoulder armor instead of silver; conversely, the show portrays them as giants with a darker skin tone.
  • The design of the Runts' giant form shares a resemblance to the giant Oni carvings within the Oni Temple in "Game of Masks."
