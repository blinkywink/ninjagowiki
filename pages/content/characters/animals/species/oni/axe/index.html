Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Two axe blades are used on the handle of the Djinn Blade.
  • In The Challenge of Samukai, Samukai forces Cole to choose between two doors, one leading to a monster wolf and one to swinging axes, in order to save Kai and Jay who are tied up at the end of one of the halls. Ultimately Cole chooses the swinging axes, as he sees flies buzzing around them and realizes they are fake axes made of sugar rather than metal, and he bursts through the door and shatters the axes.
