Frost Wolves[1] are a type of wolf that lives in the Never-Realm.

History

History[]

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Corruption (flashback)[]

When Vex tried to look for food, he accidentally awakened a Frost Wolf and got chased by it.

The Never-Realm[]

After hiking through frigid temperatures, the ninja finally made it to a forest where it was still cold. In addition, the ninja faced danger when they were approached by Frost Wolves. The ninja fled, but Lloyd ran into a tree, giving a chance for the wolves to attack, but in spite of that, the wolves headed off somewhere else. What the ninja did not realize is that one of the wolves was frozen on a tree.

Later, the ninja ran into the wolves again, but this time they fought. They were able to hold their ground for some time until three Ice Fishers shot arrows at the wolves, causing them to run away.

An Unlikely Ally[]

When Lloyd went off to find Zane, he was spotted by Frost Wolves; however, a white wolf scared them off.

A Fragile Hope[]

When the ninja and Krag went off to find the Land Bounty, the Frost Wolves chased them. Eventually, their chase led them to the Land Bounty. There, they used a fire extinguisher to scare them off. But they returned once more to attack. Krag, Cole, and Jay distracted them while Kai and Nya worked on the Land Bounty. They were able to start it up and used it to outrun the wolves.

The Virtues of Spinjitzu[]

Honesty[]

In Wu's illsuion, the Blizzard Samurai used Frost Wolves to joust against Kai and Jay.

Appearance

Appearance[]

All the Frost Wolves have a light black coating to their fur combined with light blue eyes, which can be seen during the night. Furthermore, they have small but sharp canines that will frighten any predator or prey. Finally, they have skinny legs but a lot of compact muscles, which allows the Frost Wolves to sprint at insane speeds, making them one of the fastest land creatures in the Never-Realm.

Trivia[]

  • Their renders in the show are identical to Akita's wolf form, albeit with gray fur, blue eyes, and a single tail.
  • In "Honesty," a different, larger, and armored type of the Frost Wolves is shown in the Castle of Ice. However, it is unknown if they really exist, as it was just Wu's illusion.
