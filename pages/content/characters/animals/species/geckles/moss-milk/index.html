Overview

“The moss is particularly plumping juicy today. More moss-milk for everyone!”
— Ginkle, "Trial By Mino"

Moss-milk is a type of moss located in the Dungeons of Shintaro that the Geckles love to eat. It can also be made into ice cream.

History

History[]

Master of the Mountain[]

Trial By Mino[]

The Geckles were collecting moss-milk while singing a song about Gilly. Kai and Zane realize this and hide behind a rock. However, Groko fell from his ladder, and the moss-milk landed on Kai.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Crossroads Carnival[]

At the Crossroads Carnival, the Geckles serve ice cream made of moss-milk while Lobbo used his prize money to buy the citizens some. Kreel, Lobbo, and the Whack Rats later have moss-milk ice cream as they waited for the ninja to return with Targle.

The Power Within[]

Kreel buys Targle a moss-milk cone, but he doesn't get the chance to eat it before the MergeQuake storm occurs.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Blood Moon[]

Riyu spots a Geckle with a moss-milk ice cone and tries to lick it, but the Geckle moves, and he ends up licking Arin instead.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Between You and Lee[]

During the Community of Lee's new member orientation, the clones of Lee sing about moss-milk ice cream while holding some.

The Screaming Earth[]

Following their escape from Ras in the Underworld, Sora and Arin bought moss-milk ice cream from an vendor truck within the boundaries of Mysterium. Though Arin claimed the ice cream was mediocre at best, Sora retorted that after she avoided being locked in the Casket of Bones, her appreciation for the sweet led to her calling it a "frozen delight". After finishing her ice cream, Sora said she will buy another one before the two set out to assist the residents of Abracalabria, who had been hollowed after a recent attack.
