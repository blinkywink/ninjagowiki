The Geckle courthouse is a courtroom where the Geckles discuss propositions and trials.

History

History[]

Trial by Mino[]

Kai and Zane were caught by and brought to the courthouse, where Gulch sentenced them to a Trial-By-Mino. After Zane frees the Mino from her armor, Kai becomes the new chancellor.

Grief-Bringer[]

After a disagreement that the Munce were not to be trusted, the Geckles host a slug race to determine the answer.
