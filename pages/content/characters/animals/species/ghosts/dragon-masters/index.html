The Dragon Masters were two great martial art teachers specialized in training students to become ninja warriors. They invented the Rising Dragon technique, able to counter the Shatterspin technique, and they defeated and banished the Forbidden Five millennia ago.

Thousands of years later, they trained the ninja to learn Rising Dragon at the dawn of the Five's return. After Egalt succumbed to Wasting Sickness, Rontu remained as the last of the Dragon Masters, choosing to stay with the ninja and continue Riyu's training in accordance with Egalt's wishes.

History

History[]

Background[]

Originally from their respective tribes, Egalt and Rontu met at some point in their long life and became lovers. When they were still young, they both participated in the War of Oni and Dragon.[1] They were trained by Ekesian Coldfang the Wise[2][3] to become ninja and learned martial arts techniques. Eventually, they became instructors themselves, forming the Dragon Masters. Together, they established a training plateau in the Wyldness, where they taught both dragons and, on occasion, humans the art of Spinjitzu, as well as Rising Dragon, a technique they developed.[4]

One day, the Forbidden Five embarked on a mission to conquer other realms, leaving destruction in their wake and eliminating anyone who dared to oppose them. Fully aware of the threat posed by the Forbidden Five, the Dragon Masters devised a plan to put an end to their reign of terror. They knew that the key to stopping them was Rising Dragon, which could overpower Shatterspin. However, the Dragon Masters also understood that it was crucial to remove the presence of the Forbidden Five from the realms entirely. To achieve this, they sought the assistance of Gandalaria, the high sorceress of Mysterium. Gandalaria dedicated day and night to crafting a spell that would transport the Forbidden Five to the Nether-Space.[5]

Once the spell was completed, the Dragon Masters unleashed Rising Dragon, successfully subduing the Forbidden Five and banishing them to the Nether-Space. With the Forbidden Five no longer a threat, the Dragon Masters took further action to ensure that their dark legacy would be forgotten. They had the Forbidden Five be erased from memory, sealing their history within the confines of the Shadow Dojo.[6]

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Following their first encounter with the Wolf Clan, in which they were met with Shatterspin for the first time, Lloyd began receiving visions from the Source Dragons that instructs him to seek out Egalt, though the visions didn't reveal to him that there are two Dragon Masters. With a mission bestowed on him by the Source Dragons, the ninja and Riyu (alongside Wyldfyre, who stowed away) began seeking out the 'teacher' that would help them in countering Shatterspin. During their jounrey, they went through a trio of trials/tests that were played out by the Dragon Masters.

After the ninja successfully went through the first and second trial, they arrived at the entrance to the Dragon Masters' training plateau, where they were met by Egalt, who served as an obstacle for the final test, which is the Test of Unity. Working together as a team, the ninja successfully prevailed over Egalt and passed the test before they were suddenly met by Rontu. As the ninja readied themselves for an incoming attack, the Dragon Masters instead bowed to them, leaving the ninja confused. Rontu proceeded to congratulate the ninja for passing their tests, and explained each of them to the group. When Lloyd asks the two dragons about the ancient teacher they seek, Egalt refused to give a clear answer but Rontu reveals that she and the latter are the teachers they seek before introducing herself and Egalt and that they are the Dragon Masters.

When questioned on why he and the ninja came to seek them, Lloyd tells the Dragon Masters of the vision he had received and requested that they teach the ninja to counter Shatterspin. While Egalt was skeptical of it, as he knew that no one practiced the technique for centuries, Rontu asks Lloyd if he has seen anyone perform Shatterspin, which the ninja confirms, though Egalt still refused to believed it, as he has a strong distrust of humans, due to having bad experiences while teaching human students. Upset by the old dragon's skepticism, Lloyd informs him of Ras training an elemental master in mastering Shatterspin, the Wolf Masks, and the Gong of Shattering. The Dragon Masters were left shocked upon learning of Ras' plot to free the Forbidden Five.

They assumed that Ras could not have opened the Shadow Dojo without the energy of a Source Dragon, causing Sora to theorize that the Source Dragon energy Ras has must have come from the Source Dragon that was imprisoned in Imperium. Though Rontu tells Lloyd that she and Egalt can no longer fight the Forbidden Five, due to their old age, she, however, claims that the Dragon Masters can help train them in mastering Rising Dragon. This offer leads to an argument between Rontu and Egalt. A reluctant Egalt eventually agrees to help train the ninja.

After the ninja performed a combat demonstration before the Dragon Masters, each of them are then split into two separate groups; As Sora, Riyu, and Wyldfyre are promoted as First Fang Ninja, they went to train under Rontu to master Spinjitzu while Lloyd, Kai, Nya, and Arin went to train under Egalt, as since they have learned Spinjitzu, they are promoted to Second Fang. As Rontu trained Sora and Riyu in a separate part of the training plateau of the Dragon Masters, Wyldfyre was the only one of the group who didn't start training, due to having a broken leg, and she was told to let her leg heal, much to her chagrin. Meanwhile, Arin accidentally blinds one of Egalt's eyes, causing the latter to harshly demote him to First Fang, since Arin had lost control of his Spinjitzu.

As the Blood Moon nears, Kai, with help from Nya, was able to perform the Rising Dragon technique, which impressed the Dragon Masters. Because of Kai's success, he is made a Third Fang Ninja. When the Blood Moon suddenly appeared in the sky, Lloyd desperately pleads the Dragon Masters to help the rest of the ninja in mastering Rising Dragon. Unfortunately, however, Rontu tells him that they can't and confessed that since the Blood Moon had fully risen, it is also a bane to her and Egalt. After Egalt expressed his hope that the Source Dragons will be on the ninja's side, he and Rontu were turned to stone. Later, after the ninja defeated Ras and the Wolf Clan, though they were successful in freeing Nokt and unintentionally Rox's soul, the Blood Moon passes, allowing the curse on the Dragon Masters to be undone, reverting them back to normal.

Sometime later, Egalt and Rontu began training Zane to master Rising Dragon, which the Ice ninja succeeds in with a little help from Lloyd. When the Green Ninja asked the two dragons to be their masters in the coming Tournament of the Sources, Egalt and Rontu refused, however, as there is nothing else they would teach, and advised Lloyd that he should be the ninja's master. When Lloyd protested that he is no master, the Dragon Masters makes him go through a test where they would attack him and Lloyd has to dodge their every move, while at the same time avoiding to spill a single drop of a stew he had brought for them. Lloyd successfully passed the test, in which he managed to defeat both Egalt and Rontu at once, causing the duo to see that he is a truly a master.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Prior to their introduction, Wu was known as the Dragon Master in Hunted, a name he acquired after befriending Firstbourne and wearing the Dragon Armor. Additionally, the name was used for the ninja in the Dragon Master sets.
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, Egalt is stronger than Rontu, though she is faster than him. They are equals with different advantages.[7]
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, the Dragon Masters were in their prime at the same time Thunderfang was in his, implying that the Battle of the City of Temples and the Exile of the Forbidden Five didn't take place too far apart from each other.[8]
    • Moreover he stated the Dragon Masters would potentially win a direct confrontation with Thunderfang; however that would depend on the conditions of the fight.[8]
  • When asked on why the Blood Moon petrified the Dragon Masters, Chris "Doc" Wyatt replies that it is due to their involvement in the Forbidden Five's banishment.[9]
  • Their former student mentioned in "Force From the East" will not appear in the show.[10]
