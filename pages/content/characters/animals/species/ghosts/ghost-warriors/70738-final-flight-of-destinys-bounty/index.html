70738 Final Flight of Destiny's Bounty is a LEGO Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - Possession set released in August 2015. It includes Deepstone Lloyd, Deepstone Cole, Deepstone Nya, Battle Wu, Morro, Soul Archer, Bansha, Ghoultar, Spyder, two Skreemers, Wu's Aeroblade, the Realm Crystal, the Destiny's Bounty, a Ghost Dragon and Nya's airbike.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Battle Morro for the Aeroblade in Destiny’s Bounty!

Attention Ninja heroes! All aboard Destiny's Bounty for an aerial duel against Morro, the ultimate Ghost and Master of Wind! Check out the iconic LEGO® NINJAGO™ ship’s cool details including a detachable roof element containing Nya’s foldout workshop and an airbike. Pick up the ship with the handle, adjust the lever-operated sails and soar into battle. Avoid the snapping jaws and swiping tail of the Ghost Dragon and attack with the triple-stud-shooting cannons. Pull out the dual flick harpoon shooter to activate the engine transformation function. Unleash the airbike to fend off Morro’s Ghost Ninja and the menacing Skreemers. You must protect the Aeroblade from these evil Ghosts. Includes 9 minifigures with assorted weapon and accessory elements: Lloyd, Nya, Cole, Battle Wu, Morro, Blade Master Bansha, Bow Master Soul Archer, Scythe Master Ghoultar, and Ghost Ninja Spyder.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Unlike the original set released in 2012, the new version of the ship features a more well-designed bridge, with its sides being heavily armored and detailed. In the original set, its sides were almost completely exposed, with no walls on the sides.
  • Sensei Wu appeared in the original Destiny's Bounty set and is the only minifigure who returns in the second version of it.
  • Wrayth is the only Ghost Master to not appear in this set.
  • Despite the set's name, the Destiny's Bounty is not destroyed at any point during the season. The next time it would be destroyed would be three seasons later in Sons of Garmadon.
  • Unlike the original Destiny's Bounty set, the dragon head at the front of the ship is brick-built, while in the original set, it was a standard dragon head colored red. Also, in the TV Show, the newer version of the ship featured a larger head than the one in the set.
  • Spyder is exclusive to this set (although technically, his appearance is identical to some of the other Ghost Warriors).
  • Morro is also, technically, exclusive to this set. He appears in 70732 City of Stiix and 70736 Attack of the Morro Dragon in his possessed Lloyd form, but here he appears in his original ghost form. Additional details to his minifigure that are present here but not in the show include his mask and a black cape. Also, he doesn't have the green streak in his hair that was present in the show.
    • Morro does, however, appear in 70743 Airjitzu Morro Flyer in his original ghost form. The only difference with that set is he doesn't have a cape.
  • Soul Archer's legged minifigure is exclusive to this set.
  • From 2015 to early 2017, this was the second-largest Ninjago set to date, the first being 70751 Temple of Airjitzu. Since February 2021, both those sets have been bumped down the list by the 71741 Ninjago City Gardens and in summer 2018, the 70620 Ninjago City took the number 2 spot.
  • It is the second model of Destiny's Bounty.
  • Although Ghost Dragons lack limbs, the one in this set has limbs.
  • The box shows the Earth Aeroblade, but the actual set has the Creation Aeroblade.
  • In the set, the Staff of the First Spinjitzu Master is much more ornate and golden, but in the show, it resembles an ordinary wooden staff.
  • This is the cheapest way to get Deepstone Lloyd as well as Deepstone Nya with armor.
  • This is the first LEGO Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu set to include both the hoods and hair pieces of the figures included, in this case of the ninja. This would be followed by 853687 LEGO NINJAGO Accessory Set, 70626 Dawn of Iron Doom, 70627 Dragon's Forge, and 71738 Zane's Titan Mech Battle.
  • This is the only Destiny's Bounty set that does not contain a ship wheel.
  • The golden teapot in this set refers to the Teapot of Tyrahn, which played a significant role the following season a year later in 2016.
