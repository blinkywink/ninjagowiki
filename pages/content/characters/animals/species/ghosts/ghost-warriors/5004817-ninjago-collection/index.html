Overview

Not to be confused with the book set.

5004817 Ninjago Collection is a set that was released in 2015. It includes 70738 Final Flight of Destiny's Bounty, and 70736 Attack of the Morro Dragon, with the minifigures being Deepstone Lloyd, Battle Wu, Deepstone Cole, Deepstone Nya, Morro, Bansha, Ghoultar, Soul Archer (legs), Spyder, two Skreemers, Possessed Lloyd, Deepstone Kai, Deepstone Jay, Wrayth, Cowler, and Yokai.

Official description

Official description[]

Join the Ninja heroes in elemental battle against the ultimate Ghost, Morro!

Defend the Ninja temple from Morro Dragon attack and battle Morro for the Aeroblade with this exciting LEGO NINJAGO Collection. In 70736 Attack of the Morro Dragon, team up with Kai and Jay on their Ninja jet boards to intercept the possessed Green Ninja as he swoops to attack the Tomb of the First Spinjitzu Master on his mighty dragon. Use the Aeroblades and all your Ninja skills to stop these ghoulish invaders from stealing the Realm Crystal! Then it's all aboard Destiny's Bounty for an aerial duel against Morro, the ultimate Ghost and Master of Wind, in 70738 Final Flight of Destiny's Bounty. Avoid the snapping jaws and swiping tail of the Ghost Dragon. Attack with the ship's triple-stud-shooting cannons and dual flick harpoon shooter and unleash the airbike to fend off Morro's Ghost Ninja and the menacing Skreemers.

  • Includes 15 minifigures with assorted weapon and accessory elements: Kai, Jay, Lloyd, Nya, Cole, Battle Wu, Evil Green Ninja, Chain Master Wrayth, Ghost Warrior Cowler and Ghost Warrior Yokai, Morro, Blade Master Bansha, Bow Master Soul Archer, Scythe Master Ghoultar and Ghost Ninja Spyder.
  • Morro Dragon features a saddle with steering Ghost chain, posable head with opening jaws, huge posable fabric wings, posable legs and tail, and sharp talons
  • Tomb of the First Spinjitzu Master features a grand entrance, the Realm Crystal element, 2 Aeroblades, 2 Katanas, weapon storage with assorted elements: 2 golden spears, 2 golden flags and 2 torches with translucent flame details
  • Includes 2 Ninja jet boards with adjustable wings and Ninja sword details
  • Destiny's Bounty features a retractable handle for easy flying, 4 lever-operated adjustable sails, 2 triple-stud-shooting cannons with ammunition, storage compartments with a crowbar and Katana, rotating dual flick harpoon shooter with engine transformation function, opening roof, detailed interior, Aeroblade, detachable roof element including Nya's foldout workshop with hidden airbike and assorted tools, bucket, pirate hat with feather, cup, treasure chest with golden teapot, rat, cheese element, and golden Ninja blades and decorative elements
  • Morro's Ghost Dragon features a saddle, posable head with opening jaws, posable wings, legs and tail, and sharp talons
  • Also includes 4 Skreemers (attachable to minifigures) with a sai, axe and a dagger, plus 2 banana, 2 dynamite and sausage elements
  • Weapons include 3 Golden Katanas and 3 Deepstone Katanas (for Kai, Jay and Lloyd), Evil Green Ninja's Ninja Staff, Wrayth's Ghost Master Blade on a chain, Yokai's double-bladed spear, Cowler's ghost spear, Nya's Deepstone Katana and sai, Cole's Deepstone Scythe, Battle Wu's staff, Morro's Ghost Katana, Bansha's double-bladed Ghost Master Blade, Soul Archer's crossbow, Ghoultar's double-bladed scythe and Spyder's saber
  • Accessory elements include Deepstone armor for Kai and Jay, plus wigs for Lloyd, Nya and Cole[citation needed]
