Overview

For other uses, see Destiny's Bounty (disambiguation).

71749 Final Flight of Destiny Bounty is a Ninjago: Legacy Wave 5 4+ set that was released in most countries on June 1, 2021 and on August 1, 2021 in the United States and Canada. It comes with Kai, Zane and Jay in their Legacy (dragon) outfits, Karenn, a small outpost, a Ghost Dragon, a speedboat for Zane and the Destiny's Bounty.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This set is a 4+ Legacy remake of the 2015 set 70738 Final Flight of Destiny's Bounty from Possession.
    • Ironically, the ninja who come in this set are the ninja who didn't appear in the original set.
      • Overall, this is the second set containing Destiny's Bounty in Ninjago: Legacy. The first is 71705 Destiny's Bounty, which is, however, inspired by the original set.
  • It is the smallest Destiny's Bounty set, having only 147 pieces.
    • Nevertheless, this is the only non-movie Destiny's Bounty set that uses the classic LEGO ship's wheel piece, and not the scaled-down version used in other sets, if the wheel was even shown at all.
  • It has the most minfigures for a 4+ Ninjago set.
  • Despite the fact that it is a Possesion set, the ninja come in their Legacy (dragon) robes.
  • The ship wheel is located at the front of the ship, although no Bounty does it in the show, nor do real ships.
  • This is the only Destiny's Bounty set that does not include Master Wu.
  • Despite Karenn only being in this set, his minifigure is the same as the Legacy Soul Archer and Ghost Warrior minifigures.
  • This version of Zane is, technically, exclusive to this set. This is the most accurate set of Legacy Zane from March of the Oni, with his titanium head (wearing the hood) and no shoulder pad, though this could be created by swapping the yellow heads of previous Legacy Zanes with the titanium head and/or removing the golden shoulder pad off other variants also released in 2021.
