Overview

Ghost Blades[1] were serrated weapons used by the Ghost Warriors. Wielded in a variety of forms ranging from small daggers to swords, even to chain-weapons like flails and long scythes.

History

History[]

Ghost Story[]

After confronting his old master and clashing with his pupils, Morro pursued his adversaries as they made their escape on the Destiny's Bounty. He continued his attack on the ship, brandishing the two blades as the battle picked up again.

When Wrayth was fighting the ninja, his weapon featured a single ghost blade at the end of his chain whip/flail.

Stiix and Stones[]

When Morro summoned more Ghost Warriors several of them wielded a Ghost blade.

Curseworld, Part I[]

Bansha wielded a ghost blade when she spotted Lloyd running through the occupied City of Stiix. Before she could attack him however, Zane intervened by freezing her, angering the Blade Master into attacking Zane and his Titan Mech with her blade.
