Rowan Rockspike is a member of the Explorer's Club.

History

History[]

The Lean of a Tree[]

When Sora, Frak, Wyldfyre, and Riyu arrived in Umbrasilvis, Rowan was marking down or checking things off on a notepad.

Tonight We Hunt Monsters[]

As Sora, Riyu, Frak and Wyldfyre arrive in Umbrasilvis, they encounter Frak's father, Reginald Slitherbottom who indicates he's a member of the Explorer's Club alongside his fellow colleagues, Johnny Courageous, Rowan, Seafoam Green, and Gweeg.
