Overview

The lair is a mystical prison beneath the Sea of Sand, in which the Bone King and his Greenbone Warriors were imprisoned by Samukai.[1]

History

History[]

Desert of the Bone[]

The ninja were travelling through the dark mazes of the Sea of Sand, when Cole got a hallucination that he has spotted a ghost, which led to him hitting the ground with his hammer, causing it to collapse and everyone to fall down through the cave. After the ninja fall, they noticed the skeleton warriors and the imprisoned Bone King. The Bone King ordered his warriors to find the five Bone Weapons to free him from his prison. The Greenbone Warriors dig tunnels, but Zane creates an ice slide through which the ninja escape from the cave. The search for the bone weapons has started.

After some battles, the Bone King got all the Bone Weapons and freed himself from the prison.

Rise of the Bone King[]

After the Bone King lost control over the bone weapons, the Greenbone Warriors were imprisoned again, leading to the Greenbone Leader to plan his revenge on the ninja.

The Bone Tournament[]

After tricking the Ninja into drinking a special brew of tea, they find themselves transported to the Bone King's lair where the titular Bone King announced the beginning of his first Bone Tournament. As the tournament progressed, the Ninja were able to fight off the Bone King's warriors and escape from the lair on the backs of commandeered Bone Scorpions. After escaping, Cole then sealed off the exit, imprisoning the Greenbone Warriors until the Merge.

After the Merge[]

The lair was presumably destroyed by the Merge, since the Greenbone Warriors now returned to the surface of the merged world.
