The Walls of Punishment is a pit with spikes located in the Greenbone Warriors' forest.

History

History[]

Gangs of the Sea[]

After trespassing in their forest, the Greenbone Warriors surround the pit, where Kai and Wyldfyre were in, both insisting it was the other's fault.

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

As the Greenbone Warriors activated the walls to crush the pair of Ninja, Wyldfyre remarked that the walls were not as fun as she expected. The Bone King briefly halted the walls' advance to offer the ninja mercy in exchange for the return of their sacred gems. He would angrily reactivate them however when Wyldfyre insulted the gems as being "mega ugly." Nevertheless, the pair of ninja managed to escape the walls by leaping atop and across the spikes jutting from the sides.
