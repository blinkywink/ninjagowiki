The bone swords are a set of Bone Weapons. One of them was able to free the Bone King from prison and was later destroyed by the ninja.[1]

History

History[]

Desert of the Bone[]

The Bone Sword was retrieved by the Greenbone Warriors from a warehouse near the Sea of Sand.

Into the Forest[]

A replica bone sword served as part of the weapon carried by a mech used by the Greenbone Warriors.

Volcano Adventure[]

With all five Bone Weapons brought before him, the Bone King was able to use the energy of the Bone Sword to free himself from his prison.

Rise of the Bone King[]

Released from his prison, the Bone King and his forces began their assault on Ninjago City. The Bone Sword was wielded by the King in combat against the ninja, but he would later be relieved of the weapon by Zane after the ninja discovered that the weapons were the source of the Bone King's powers.

Once relieved of all five Bone Weapons, the Bone King and his army were transported back to their prison, leaving the Bone Sword and the other weapons behind. Shortly afterwards, the Bone Sword, alongside the other Bone Weapons, were destroyed by Cole.
