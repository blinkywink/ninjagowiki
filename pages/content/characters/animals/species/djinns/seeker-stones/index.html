Overview

“If I arrange the stones correctly, it'll show us anything we see. Well, anything that isn't alive.”
— Nya to Sora, "The Last Djinn"

The Seeker Stones are enchanted gemstones that possess the ability to guide users towards their desired object. Nya and Sora tried to use them but due to using them without proper guidance, they ended up getting cursed by them, resulting in being hunted by Howlers.

History

History[]

The Last Djinn[]

While looking for the Yellow Dragon Core, Nya brought a sack of Seeker Stones from the Cloud Kingdom, which she intended to use to find the Dragon Core once they reach the burial vault. When they reach the altar, Nya places the stones in the correct order will reveal their desired location. As they experiment with different combinations, they succeed in activating the stones. However, because they used the Seeker Stones without proper guidance, the Seeker Stone instead cursed Nya and Sora, causing a mysterious eye to appear as a small shockwave occurs.
