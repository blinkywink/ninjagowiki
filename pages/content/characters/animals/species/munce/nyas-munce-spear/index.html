The Munce spear is a weapon owned by Nya. Unlike the previous two models, this spear resembles a lance instead of a trident

History

History[]

Master of the Mountain[]

Queen of the Munce[]

When Nya went to fight Murtessa for Jay, she grabbed a spear from a Munce Sentry to use as her weapon, which she then used to fight in the Munce arena. During the fight, however, she dropped the spear and it remained lying on the ground.

The Darkest Hour[]

During Grief-Bringer's attack, Nya had a spear attached to her back and later used it to break the chain. When the Skull Sorcerer told the ninja to give up, Nya was forced to throw a spear at the ground.

Seabound[]

The Call of the Deep[]

When Maya was cleaning Nya's room in the Monastery of Spinjitzu, she put the Munce spear on the shelf toward the golden spear.

Crystalized[]

Kryptarium Prison Blues[]

The spear was standing in between the door to Nya's room and the weapons rack when she was packing all of her equipment.
