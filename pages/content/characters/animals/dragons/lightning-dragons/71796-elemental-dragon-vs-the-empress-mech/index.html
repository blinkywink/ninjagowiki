Overview

71796 Elemental Dragon vs. The Empress Mech is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 1 set that was released on June 1, 2023. It includes Jay, Zane & Sora in their Merge suits, Riyu, Empress Beatrix, Lord Ras, Melvin, the yellow Dragon Core, weaponized armor, the Empress Mech, and Jiro.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Ninja fans aged 9+ can recreate an epic showdown from the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV series with Elemental Dragon vs. The Empress Mech (71796). The building set features fearsome dragon Jiro, who can be upgraded by adding golden blade wings and stud shooters from a flyer vehicle to take on the Empress’s posable golden mech, who is wielding a large sword.

Premium toy for kids
The impressive playset includes 6 minifigures. Jay, Sora and Zane each come with 2 golden katanas to defeat the trio of Lord Ras with his hammer, Empress Beatrix with her Imperium sword and an Imperium Guard with a staff. There’s also a yellow dragon power element for the characters to battle over, and kids can collect 4 more in different colors and 4 golden dragon energy core elements in other NINJAGO sets.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Melvin is exclusvive to this set, but he is commonly known as the "Imperium Guard." He was only identified to be the Imperium Guard in this set by the LEGO Ninjago Dragons Rising Character Guide.
    • Despite the Imperium Guards being prevalent in the show, the Imperium Guard Commander appears in three sets yet never appears in the show. Given its sole appearance, the Imperium Guard is one of Ninjago's rarest foot soldier minifigures.
      • Granted, the figure shares most of its parts with the Claw Hunters, making the pearl gold "pan head" the only exclusive part.
      • This situation is similar to Crystalized's Vengestone Guards barely appearing in the sets, while the non-canon warriors and brutes appear much more frequently in the sets..
  • Out of all the characters in the set, two of them, Ras and Riyu, all share same the voice actor in the TV show, Brian Drummond.
