Overview

This article is about the set. For the dragon, see Arc Dragon of Life.

71859 The Dragon of Life is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 4 set that was released on January 1, 2026. It includes Cole and Lloyd in their Draconus Forms, Arin and Zane in their Draconus suits, Ghost Hunter Sora, the Chief Loyalist, an Acid Monster, and an powered up Earth Monster, the Weapon of Desolation, and the Arc Dragon of Life.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Boys and girls ages 9 plus will be thrilled to build this LEGO® NINJAGO® The Dragon of Life (71859) ninja toy before recreating scenes from season 4 of the NINJAGO Dragons Rising TV show. This dragon toy can spread its wings out wide or tuck them in, and it has a posable jaw, head, neck, legs, ankles, claws, tail and a saddle for a minifigure. The playset includes a side build with a tree and a plant kids can spin around that hides a golden sword accessory, plus a posable mini mech toy with a cockpit for a minifigure. To role-play epic battles, there are 8 minifigures: ninja Zane, Sora, Arin, dragon form Lloyd and dragon form Cole and the villainous trio of Acid Monster, Earth Monster and Loyalist Leader. Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Draconus Form Cole is exclusive to this set.
    • Cole and Lloyd also wield their personal weapons rather than the golden katanas that the other Draconus Form ninja use in the other sets.
    • This is the only set where two ninja are in Draconus Form.
  • This is the first appearance of the Sword of Fire in the Ninjago: Dragons Rising set, though it has been mentioned that it is included only for more playability, likely intended for the younger audience.[2]
