Overview

This article is about the set. For the actual dragon, see Daidan.

71847 The Guardian Dragon is a Ninjago: Legends - Monstrosity set that was released on June 1, 2025. It includes Wu in his Ghost Hunter suit, and Daidan.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Older NINJAGO® fans aged 14 and up can expand their collection of LEGO® NINJAGO models with this majestic The Guardian Dragon (71847) building kit. The dragon display model has a posable head, arms and scales, plus large wings, spikes and a halo above its head, and it makes a fun gift for fans of the NINJAGO Legends Monstrosity series.

With a highly detailed display stand featuring a pond, a floating magical sword, lantern elements and a gate, plus a Master Wu minifigure, this model will look great displayed in a bedroom on a shelf, desk or bedside table.

The rest of the LEGO NINJAGO range of sets (sold separately) includes dragons, vehicles and temples for fans to role-play adventures with their heroes. Each ninja playset can be created with the LEGO Builder app, which guides you and your child on an easy and intuitive building adventure. Zoom in and rotate models in 3D, save sets and track your progress. Set contains 1,650 pieces.

  • NINJAGO® dragon model – Older NINJAGO fans can add to their collection of LEGO® NINJAGO models with The Guardian Dragon display building kit
  • Packed with details – The Guardian Dragon’s display stand features a pond, a floating magical sword, lantern elements and a gate for ninja storytelling
  • Posable dragon figure – Ninja fans can move the dragon toy’s head, arms and scales into different positions when displayed on a shelf or bedside table
  • 1 NINJAGO® minifigure – The LEGO® set includes a Master Wu minifigure with a staff accessory
  • Gift for NINJAGO® fans – This playset provides a rewarding building experience and is a premium gift idea for fans of the NINJAGO Legends Monstrosity series who want to showcase their fandom
  • A fun way to build – Let the LEGO® Builder app guide kids on an intuitive building adventure, where they can save sets, track progress and zoom in and rotate models in 3D while they build
  • A universe of ninja toys – LEGO® NINJAGO® sets let kids escape into a fantasy world of adventures with their ninja heroes
  • Measurements – This 1,650-piece Guardian Dragon display model stands over 19 in. (48 cm) tall[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Wu figure included is exclusive to this set. The design is that of the Ghost Hunter Gi that the ninja wear in the second part of Dragons Rising Season 3.
