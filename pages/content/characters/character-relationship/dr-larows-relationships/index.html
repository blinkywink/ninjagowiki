This is all the relationships of Dr. LaRow, the former head scientist of the Advanced Systems Lab and the leader of the Loyalists.

Allies

Allies[]

Beatrix[]

“I'm well aware of how unstable Beatrix can be, but when she fell, when Imperium fell, I had nothing. I was certain if I helped her return, I could keep her worst impulses under control.”
— Dr. LaRow explaining the ninja why she still served Beatrix, "Birthright"

Dr. LaRow used to work for Empress Beatrix during her reign. She was in charge of draining Dragons of their energy for the City of Imperium and provided many inventions for the kingdom. After the Ninja liberate all the captured dragons, minus the Source Dragon of Energy, LaRow informed Beatrix the city had an energy crisis and tired offering some alternatives, but the empress refused to listen. Later on, Beatrix ordered LaRow to create a weapon capable of controlling the MergeQuakes. While testing the weapon, LaRow tried to inform Beatrix one of the Dragon Cores' energy signature was different from the others, because the one Rapton brought Beatrix was secretly a fake, but the empress disregarded her warning, causing a huge explosion in the lab. When Beatrix ordered LaRow to create a new core using the imprisoned Source Dragon, LaRow told her that doing so could destroy Imperium and refused.

Despite this, LaRow ultimately came to serve Beatrix again after finding her and forming the Loyalists to locate the Weapon of Desolation to aid Beatrix in reclaiming her throne. When Beatrix fell under the control of the weapon and caused the monsters to grow larger, LaRow deduced she was no longer in control and wished that she did not push it too far. After their failed attempt to take Imperium, LaRow displayed annoyance about Beatrix taking the credit for finding the weapon while she did all the work. She also admitted she felt like she had nothing when Beatrix was overthrown and believed she could control Beatrix's impulses. When Lloyd stated no one can control Beatrix, she retorted by saying that while she couldn't control Beatrix, she believed the weapon was controlling the former empress.

LaRow seems to harbor a certain admiration for Beatrix. She appears visibly moved when Beatrix seizes the Weapon of Desolation. When Cole breaks free and leaps onto the platform where Beatrix and LaRow are standing, LaRow tries in vain to stop him before he can attack Beatrix.

Dorama (formerly)[]

“This guy you found is a definite weirdo. He keeps inviting me to his autobiographical one-man musical. But he does know more than anyone else about extracting and exploiting exotic energy.”
— Dr. LaRow talking about Dorama to Ras, "Beyond Madness"

Dorama is a former ally of Dr. LaRow. Dr. LaRow first impression of Dorama is bad stating that he's a complete weirdo and flirted with her but Dr. LaRow did acknowledge Dorama's ability to activate the Photacs.

Jordana (formerly)[]

Dr. LaRow and Jordana are former allies. Originally, Jordana worked with Dr. LaRow serving her with loyalty, though the older woman made no attempt to spare her feelings when she expressed the belief that Sora was the brightest student she had even known, upsetting her assistant. However, the relationship slowly began to deteriorate after Jordana decided to take Dr. LaRow's job of draining the Source Dragon of Energy and with Dr. LaRow trying to stop Jordana by discouraging her. It backfired as Jordana told Imperium Guards to take away Dr. LaRow.

Ras (formerly)[]

Ras is a former ally of Dr. LaRow. As the lead scientist at the Advanced Systems Lab, she played a crucial role in the extraction of dragon power, which was a primary reason for Ras's reliance on her expertise. When Sora was arrested by the Claws of Imperium, Ras sought LaRow's assistance in completing the Photacs. During this, LaRow intentionally divulged too much information to Sora, prompting a glare of disapproval from Ras. The situation escalated when Dr. LaRow reported the escape of Arin and Sora; in a fit of rage, Ras seized her by the neck, lifted her off the ground, and forcefully threw her down.

Rapton (formerly)[]

As a member of the Claws of Imperium, Rapton issued reports on his conflicts with the ninja that reached Dr. LaRow, which allowed her to determine the whereabouts of Sora and learn that her former pupil had become a student of Lloyd. However, even though the pair worked besides one another in service to Empress Beatrix, LaRow showed little regard for Rapton's competency and had no issue stating this, expressing wonder at the idea that he had been able to complete a mission, much to his indignation.

Loyalists[]

Sora's parents (formerly)[]

Dr. LaRow encounter Sora's parents when her daughter, Sora, had win a concouras. She called them about Sora, nothing is between them and they don't seem to have any further relationship beyond the minor interaction they had, though the couple were nervous in her presence and gave a visible sigh of relief when the scientist expressed how impressed she was with Sora's Photacs. After the fall of Imperium, however, all three went on to become Loyalists who sought to restore the previous reign, though the conclusion of their campaign saw LaRow and Beatrix arrested, while the other two evaded capture.
