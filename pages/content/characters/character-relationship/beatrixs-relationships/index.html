Overview

These are the relationships of Beatrix, the former Empress of Imperium.

Family

Family[]

Levo[]

Beatrix longed for two things: Levo's favoritism and true power. However, as the second-born, she was overlooked by her father, who favored Zeatrix instead. This resulted in Beatrix harboring resentment towards Levo. One day, Beatrix conspired with Ras, who promised her true power through the capture of a Source Dragon, in order to overthrow Levo. Ras killed Levo by sabotaging his hover chariot, causing it to explode mid-flight. Upon witnessing Levo's demise, Beatrix briefly displayed a sad look before breaking into a smile, knowing that she was now one step closer to becoming Empress. Since then, Beatrix vowed that anyone who opposed her will suffer a similar fate as Levo.

Zeatrix[]

Beatrix had a huge amount of hatred and jealousy towards her twin sister, due to her having Elemental Powers and being the next in line to the throne. Beatrix and Ras plotted to kill the former's father and arresting Zeatrix, having the latter exiled from Imperium.

During Operation Mentax, the Loyalists swapped Zeatrix's mind in Beatrix body with the Mind Crystal thereby plunging her into a coma. Beatrix didn't seem to sympathize with her sister, who was in a coma in her place. But the young ninja swapped the minds, and Beatrix fell back into a coma.

Allies

Allies[]

Ras (formerly)[]

“I came here to offer you a chance to redeem yourself. But all you have done is proven exactly why I put you in here. Why I put anyone who defies me in here. You are a fraud! A failure! If it wasn’t for me, you would have been on the streets. Powerless! Never forget that, Lord Ras!”
— Beatrix to Ras, “Absolute Power”

Beatrix and Ras share a long history with one another.

Beatrix met Ras, during his meeting with her father, Levo. During the discussion, Beatrix hears about Ras trying to offer her father the unlimited power of the Source Dragons, but he refuses and has his daughter Zeatrix throw him out. Beatrix then finds Ras in the alley and allies herself with him to conspire to take Levo and take the throne for herself. After Beatrix became the Empress, Ras became the leader of Claws of Imperium with a task of hunting down dragons. However, when Ras fails to stop the ninja from releasing the enslaved dragons, his relationship with Beatrix declined to the point when Beatrix arrested Ras and stripped him off of his position.

Beatrix sought out Ras' assistance with the MergeQuake weapon, but he attempted to persuade her to release him by reminding her of his crucial role in her rise to Empress. Ras proposed that if they joined forces to eliminate the ninja, Beatrix would gain complete control over the realms. However, to Ras' anger, Beatrix declined his offer and departed.

Dr. LaRow[]

“Impossible? Those Energy Cores are created from Source Dragons. We have a Source Dragon imprisoned under my city! Are you telling me we don't have the power to finish this weapon?”
“You know we have not been successful at draining energy from that Source Dragon. And even if we could drain his power, replacing the third Core with pure dragon energy could be even worse than the explosion we just had. It could destroy all of Imperium. I — I won't do it.”
“You dare defy my order?”
— Beatrix and Dr. LaRow, "Absolute Power"

Dr. LaRow serves Beatrix, originally as a scientist and then as a society leader to help her reclaim Imperium.

Before learning about the ninja's arrival in Imperium for Dragon Cores, Dr. LaRow obediently followed Beatrix's instructions. However, upon hearing the news, Beatrix ordered LaRow to drain the Source Dragon's energy to make a new core. But LaRow refused, stating it could destroy the kingdom. Frustrated, Beatrix would later give the task to Jordana, who offered to make a replacement dragon core. Despite this decision, after the democratic reforms to Imperium left her believing that she had been left with nothing, Dr. LaRow decided that she could shackle the worst impulses of her former monarch if the Empress was restored to her throne.

As a result, she renewed her allegiance to Beatrix as seen when Dr. LaRow provided medical care to save Beatrix's life after she escaped the Land of Monsters, and alongside other Loyalists helped Beatrix obtain the Weapon of Desolation to control the Elemental Monsters as part of Beatrix's goal to reclaim the Kingdom of Imperium.

Loyalists[]

“I have been to the abyss and back, and have the scars to prove it, but you, and only you, my Loyalists, never wavered in your faith in my almighty power. You will stand by my side when I take the kingdom that is rightfully mine!”
— Beatrix to Loyalists, "Dying to Live"

The Loyalists are Imperians who continued to serve Beatrix despite her true nature being exposed, finding both fragments of the Weapon of Desolation to help her get back Imperium. Among them included Dr. LaRow, Johnny Courageous, and Sora's parents, who felt like they had nothing when she was overthrown. Despite their loyalty, Beatrix likely only saw the Loyalists as her pawns in her quest for revenge. However, she also expressed that she was proud of them, saying LaRow to remind her to spare the Loyalists who were sabotaging the energy barrier, and acknowledged their persistent allegiance to her. Unfortunately, the Loyalists were dissolved and captured after the Loyalist assault, while Sora's parents escaped without getting noticed.

Elemental Monsters (formerly)[]

After the Loyalists successfully completed the Weapon of Desolation, Beatrix used it to control the Elemental Monsters as her minions to reclaim the Kingdom of Imperium. However, after Lloyd knocked the Weapon of Desolation out of Beatrix's hands, the Elemental Monsters turned against her, with two Earth Monsters blasting her and rendering her unconscious.

Enemies

Enemies[]

Dragons[]

Beatrix hates the Dragons. She thinks are "lesser beings" and treat them like object. With Imperium technology, Beatrix drained the Dragons and uses their energy to power Imperium. With her Mech, she fighted Jiro and Zanth.

Riyu[]

Beatrix wanted to drain Riyu completely. For her, he is nothing more than an object.

Years later, during the Loyalist assault, Beatrix saw Riyu and he stops her.

Ninja[]

“I don’t care about dragons! Those ninja humiliated me! They need to be destroyed!”
— Beatrix to Ras about the ninja, "The Battle of the Second Monastery"

Beatrix harbored an immense level of animosity towards the ninja due to their actions of liberating the captive dragons and repeatedly defeating her, which ultimately led to her feeling humiliated. This drove her to pursue the acquisition of the Dragon Cores, as she believed that utilizing the destructive power of the MergeQuake weapon would result in the annihilation of the ninja. During the final fight with the ninja, Beatrix's fury intensified upon realizing that they had exposed her true nature to her kingdom. Pushed to her breaking point, Beatrix unleashed the MergeQuake weapon, summoning a multitude of MergeQuake rifts to engulf the ninja and Imperians, seemingly ending their lives. However, when one of the rifts ensnared Riyu, Arin instinctively used Object Spinjitzu to damage the Empress Mech, protecting Sora in the process. With Sora subsequently destroying the MergeQuake weapon to shield Arin and Lloyd from Beatrix's wrath, the vengeful Empress prepared to engage the ninja in yet another battle. However, Arin, seeking vengeance for Riyu, forcefully pushed Beatrix into one of rifts. Following the conclusion of the MergeQuake Storm, all those who were engulfed by the rifts reemerge unharmed. After Beatrix returned with the aid of the Loyalists and Elemental Monsters to reclaim the Kingdom of Imperium she tried to get revenge on the ninja again, which ultimately failed.

Rapton (former ally)[]

“Impressive. I will admit, I feared you had failed me once again, and my patience for your failures has grown thin.”
— Beatrix to Rapton, "Absolute Power"

Rapton was one of Beatrix's Claw of Imperium and served her willingly. When she imprisoned Ras for his failure, Rapton was made the new leader of the hunters. He later followed the Ninja to the Temple of the Dragon Cores and told her of the Dragon Cores. After failing to obtain one of the cores, Rapton began to question Beatrix and her plan to use the cores to create a weapon to control the Mergequakes, believing it would destroy the world and ultimate joined Percival Tartigrade's resistance against Beatrix and tried to give her a fake Dragon Core believing she would give up, only for his plan to cause an explosion in the lab. He later aided the Ninja in broadcasting Beatrix's true nature and her abuse of dragons to the entire city.

Citizens of Imperium (former allies)[]

“And all you traitors to your Empress. You are no better than the ninja!”
— Beatrix to citizens of Imperium, "The Power Within"

After taking the throne, Beatrix manipulated the Citizens of Imperium, making them believe that she is the savior of the city, causing them to apply her belief in the inherent evil of dragons. Because of this, Beatrix established the Claws of Imperium, which the citizens saw as the heroes. However, the citizens eventually turned against her after Percival and the ninja exposed her. This led to Beatrix to use the MergeQuake weapon on them, vowing to destroy anyone who dared to oppose her. Thankfully, the ninja were able to stop her and save the Imperians.

While some citizens quickly get used to their new lives and abandoned Beatrix's beliefs, some Imperians believed that Beatrix was right. Eventually, this led to them forming the Loyalists led by Dr. LaRow, aiding Beatrix and finding the fragments of the Weapon of Desolation to help her get back Imperium.

At the start of the Loyalist assault, Beatrix wanted the Citizens of Imperium and Percival to surrender, saying that they will receive mercy. However, the citizens refused to open the doors to her, causing Beatrix to summon the Elemental Monsters and march on the City of Imperium with them and the Loyalists. After they got into the city, some Imperians tried to fight back against Beatrix and her forces with the ninja. Eventually, Beatrix and forces were defeated, freeing Imperium from her once again. After the Loyalist assault, Beatrix was taken to the Imperium Prison, where she was treated for her injuries by some Imperians.

Percival Tartigrade[]

“Aren't you that boy who once brought me a baby dragon? You dreamt of nothing more than serving me as one of my Claws!”
“That was a long time ago, when I was brainwashed by your propaganda, but I woke up. All of the people of Imperium woke up. We saw who you really are-- a tyrant who lied about safe energy while running secret dragon-draining chamber!”
“I drained those disgusting dragons to keep my kingdom prosperous. I hear your "free society" can barely keep the lights on. The lives of Imperium Citizens were perfect when the rightful ruler was on her throne!”
“You lied, and hurt, and oppressed the people. Our new Imperium has struggles, but we work hard to make our city function independently, without harming other beings!”
— Beatrix and Percival, "The Heartbeat of all the Realms"

Once, Percival was a loyal Imperian to Beatrix, wanting to join to the Claws of Imperium to serve the City of Imperium, even bringing her Riyu. However, he then started to get suspicious of the Good Empress and discovered her true nature with the rest of the Imperium Teen Protection Force, eventually exposing her with the help of the ninja and Rapton. This made Beatrix to develop a hatred and grudge against Percival

At the start of the Loyalist assault, Beatrix wanted the Citizens of Imperium and Percival to surrender, saying that they will receive mercy. However, Percival refused this, reminding Beatrix how she killed her father and lied to her people, while also saying that she will be judged for her crimes. This led Beatrix to summon the Elemental Monsters and march to Imperium with them and the Loyalists. Percival tried to stop them with the electro-barrier, but to no avail. After entering the city, Beatrix grabbed Percival, questioning how he seized her power. However, Percival saying that he was elected and they we will be free or perished. Hearing this, Beatrix tried to kill Percival by squeezing him, only for Lloyd to arrive with his Draconus Form, which distracted Beatrix.
