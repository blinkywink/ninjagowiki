Overview

These are relationships of Drix, the master of Swarm, a member of the Forbidden Five and a former leader of the Wolf Clan.

Allies

Allies[]

Forbidden Five[]

The other members of the Forbidden Five, aside from Nokt and Rox are equals to Drix. Though they did not show any care for each other, Kur, Zarkt and Drix all worked together in the Nether-Space to escape and continue their brutal dominance over the realms. Following their escape from Nether-Space and the City of Temples, Drix and the Forbidden Five set in motion their plan to obtain the five Prismatic Blades to free Thunderfang. When Drix was late to return with the Returning Blade, Kur informed them of his absence, saying he took longer than expected, however, Nokt insisted he failed. Drix and Nokt also have a tense relationship[1]. Although Drix had additionally reclaim the Blue Prismatic Blade and captured Ras, when he brought the Returning Blade to Nokt, he was taken aback when Nokt abruptly shoved him aside, prioritizing his desire for revenge against Ras. Drix showed a confusing look but choose not to stop Nokt.

Wolf warriors (formerly)[]

Before his imprisonment, Drix was one of the leaders of the Wolf Clan and led the wolf warriors to launch attacks wherever the Forbidden Five wanted in the Wyldness.

Thunderfang (unwillingly)[]

One day, while they were normal Wyldness dwellers, Drix and the rest of the Five were empowered by Thunderfang with Elemental Powers, derived from his dragon energy. This allowed them to create a dark and powerful fighting technique similar to Spinjitzu that they named Shatterspin, and they were also able to use Theroxian magic. Following their release from the Nether-Space, Drix and the Five immediately went on their quest to free Thunderfang, with the intention of the latter granting them more power in exchange. Drix managed to retrieve the two remaining Prismatic Blades from the Ninja and the Five successfully freed Thunderfang. However as soon as they did, the Shatter Dragon of Chaos showed his disbelief at their gullibility to think he was willing to grant them more power. Like the others, Drix was shocked and visibly in fear upon realizing this act of betrayal. Thunderfang wasted no time and absorbed Drix and his comrades' souls, regaining his full power, and leaving their lifeless bodies behind. Following his demise at the hands of Arin Nived, Thunderfang moved on to the Spectral Lands. There, he had no intention of letting go of his former minions and started using them to collect souls across the Merged Lands in order to become a Source Dragon. He showed satisfaction when Drix returned with a considerable amount of souls from the Administration. When he sensed the Ninja were trying to awaken his eternal enemy, the Arc Dragon of Focus, he sent Drix and the Five to go after them and stop them, unaware of Lloyd and Arin's presence in the Spectral Lands.

Dragonian Trackers (formerly)[]

Drix, like the rest of the Five, did not care about the Dragonian Trackers and only used them to track down the Prismatic Blades to free Thunderfang.

Spectral Dragonians[]

After Drix fell under Thunderfang's control, he led the Spectral Dragonians to capture souls of the people in The Administration. In "When Doves Cry", he along with Nokt and Rox led the Spectral Dragonians to attack Lloyd and Arin in the Spectral Lands.

Enemies

Enemies[]

Dragon Masters[]

Because Egalt and Rontu were the ones who trapped Drix and his allies in the Nether-Space for millennia, Drix likely has a huge grudge against them.

Ninja[]

Drix first met the Ninja in "Crashing Together". Even though he does not know them as well as Nokt and Rox do, Drix considers them as his enemies, and fought them in the World Forest over the Blue Prismatic Blade and the Returning Blade, which he successfully obtained. He later encountered them again in "Chaos Unchained" where he fought them again to obtain all the Prismatic Blades to free Thunderfang.

Dragonians[]

Drix and his colleagues were merciless with the the peaceful Dragonian people, by ransacking their village to find Thunderfang's prison, not hesitating to be violent with them, and then corrupting them through Thunderfang's dark energy to make them their personal army.

Ras[]

Back during the Ritual of the Blood Moon, Ras intended to free Drix and his comrades from the Nether-Space. Even though they had not personally met, Ras had immense respect for Drix and the rest of the Forbidden Five. However they were on opposites sides when they officially met, with Drix's first interaction with Ras being delivering the latter a powerful blow empowered by Shatterspin following another one with the latter's hammer. He then took a unconscious Ras as an hostage, being confused when Nokt was interested in getting revenge on Ras instead of accepting the Returning Blade from Drix.
