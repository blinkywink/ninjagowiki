Overview

These are the relationships of Jordana, a former scientist of the Advanced Systems Lab and a former general of the Wolf Clan.

Allies

Allies[]

Beatrix (formerly)[]

“Ready to serve the Empress any way I can.”
— Jordana, “Absolute Power”

Jordana, similar to numerous Imperians, held Empress Beatrix in high regard. However, when Dr. LaRow declined to harness energy from the Source Dragon of Energy for a fourth Dragon Core, Jordana promptly stepped in and took on the task, which Beatrix ultimately approved, resulting in the creation of a new core. Following the MergeQuake Storm, when Ras indicated that his master had further intentions of conquering the Merged Realms, Jordana mistakenly assumed that Beatrix was the master and disregarded these plans.

Dr. LaRow (formerly)[]

“I warned you. Your pathetic desire to compete with Ana has caused you to bite off more than you can chew.”
“Ugh. Don't ever say that name to me, Dr. LaRow.”
“Ana doesn't even know who you are. You can't do this. Admit it.”
“Guards! Get her out of my sight!”
— Dr. LaRow and Jordana, "We Are All Dragons"

Originally, Jordana served Dr. LaRow with loyalty helping her in the lab dreaming that one day her actions will impress the doctor. However, her actions was quickly mocked after LaRow saying the desire to trying to compete with Sora is pathetic. In rage, Jordana ordered Imperium Guards to take her away. Before that, Jordana had lacked loyalty to her mentor by taking her place to carry out the project of Beatrix when LaRow refused. She revealed with arrogance that she found flaws in Dr. LaRow's methods. She therefore did not hesitate to break away from her and to take her place to serve the Empress when the moment presented itself.

Ras (formerly)[]

“Ugh, you're nothing but a failure, Jordana. You'll never be as good as that cursed Ana.”
“No. You'll be better.”
— Jordana and Ras, "The Power Within"

Although it is not known what her relationship with Ras was before he was stripped of his title, he wasted no time in recruiting Jordana for her efforts in draining the Source Dragon on behalf of his master, which she accepted. During her time with Ras, Jordana begun to learn magic from him until they came across the Shadow Dojo, where the saved Source Dragon essence opened it. Despite being nervous of his plans, she still showed support for him, believing it would mean proving herself better than Sora. Jordana often disappointed and angered Ras because of her failures, for example when she failed to capture Bonzle and then to release all the Forbidden Five. However, she remained a trustworthy person in Ras eyes, to whom he continued to confide about his plans, notably during the Tournament of the Sources, being unaware Jordana was possessed by Rox at this time.

Cinder (rival; formerly)[]

“There can only be one second-in-command. Sorry.”
— Jordana to Cinder, "Beyond the Phantasm Cave"

When Jordana initially encountered Cinder while attempting to read the Theroxian scrolls, she was suspicious and believed it to be a trap. However, Ras assured her that Cinder was just another recruit of the Wolf Clan. After their meeting, Jordana was jealous of Cinder's skills when he brought the Mallet of Shattering to Ras and defeated the ninja in the Cloud Kingdom. When Cinder boasted about becoming Ras' second-in-command, Jordana became angry and their rivalry escalated. Once Jordana discovered the whereabouts of Bonzle, she took the opportunity to taunt Cinder by echoing his previous remarks about there only being one second-in-command.

Despite their ongoing rivalry, both Jordana and Cinder appeared confused after Ras had a conversation with his master. After failing to capture Bonzle, Jordana became furious when Cinder once again taunted her for her failures. Their rivalry ended after Jordana parted ways leaving Cinder in a vengestone cage in the City of Temples.

Enemies

Enemies[]

Ninja (brief allies)[]

“Try all you want, ninja, but you'll never be as powerful as us.”
— Jordana to the ninja, "Shattered Dreams"

Jordana harbors intense animosity towards the ninja because of their association with Sora, and this animosity stems from Beatrix's determination to eliminate the ninja following the Battle of the Second Monastery.

After being freed from Rox's possession and realizing what she did wrong, she briefly aided them so they could stop Nokt and Rox.

Arin[]

“I thought it was you! Sorry, I can't remember your name.”
“I hate you ninja, so much!”
— Arin and Jordana, "The Forest of Spirits"

Jordana has a negative relationship with Arin as shown by her not hesitating to attack him when he was handcuffed and more prominently by her getting increasingly angry with him when he tried (and succeeded) to steal her car while she was delivering a trapped Bonzle to the Ritual of the Blood Moon.

Sora (rival; formerly)[]

“Look, I don't understand what's going on with you, Jordana, but I wanna tell you, I'm sorry. I didn't remember you from when we were in school together. I know that upset you and made you feel unimportant. Me and Arin have been… I just… I wished I hadn't been so wrapped up in own stuff. I wished I'd remembered you. School was really a lonely time in my life, and I could have used a friend. Maybe we both could have.”
— Sora to "Jordana", "The Final Game"

During her formative years, Jordana held a deep-seated resentment and envy towards Sora, as the latter's project, the Photac, was recognized as the most exceptional among all the science fair entries. Upon learning that Sora had been granted a position in the Advanced Systems Lab due to her project, Jordana made a personal commitment to surpass Sora in all aspects, beginning with securing her own place in the Advanced Systems Lab.

Years later, Jordana met Sora again and boasted that they could finally settle the score by defeating her. However, Jordana discovered that Sora had no recollection of her, further fueling her anger.

A year later, she met up again and Sora only remembered her as the girl from her class. Throughout her time, Jordana remained hostile towards her and the Ninja until she failed to release most of the Forbidden Five.

After her possession by Rox, much of her rivalry with Sora seems to have faded away entirely. When she can resist Rox's control for only a few seconds, Jordana seeks out help from Sora. She also informs her of the location of the Dragon Ivory, belonging to the fallen Dragon Patriarch and Matriarchs, through a note with an Imperium math equation.

Once freed from Rox's control over her body, it is unknown how Jordana feels about Sora, after fleeing from the City of Temples. It's possible she might have put their differences aside.

Bonzle[]

“Settle down, little spell. I don't think what you've been doing playing person with your fake family, but I know your true purpose. You're a spell that can bridge our world with Nether-Space.”
“Please, no. I don't want to be just a spell again.”
“Like you have a choice. You should thank us. We're helping you to do what you were created to.”
— Bonzle and Jordana, "The Forest of Spirits"

Upon learning about Bonzle, Jordana immediately identified her as the powerful spell responsible for banishing the Forbidden Five. Determined to capture her, Jordana dispatched numerous wolf warriors to bring Bonzle to justice. She also searched for her on her own.

After a successful capture, Jordana mocked Bonzle for her decision of "playing person" with her fake family and asserts Bonzle's true purpose of being a spell, acting as a gateway between the Merged Realms and Nether-Space. It appears she sees her as a mere tool, believing Bonzle's desires to be a person is laughable.

As the ritual was about to commence, Jordana was reprimanded by Ras for her inability to capture Bonzle, after her encounter with Arin and Riyu. After finally apprehending Bonzle, she coerced Bonzle to utilize her magic in order to liberate the Forbidden Five from their confinement in Nether-Space. Unfortunately, Jordana's endeavors were only partially fruitful, as she managed to release only Nokt before Bonzle intervened and sealed the portal, thereby preventing the majority of the Forbidden Five from escaping to the Merged Realms.

Gandalaria[]

“I might just be learning Theroxian Magic, but corrupting your poultry magic, Sorceress, has proven to be quite simple.”
— Jordana to Gandalaria, "Secrets of the Wyldness"

Jordana seems to not hold Gandalaria in much respect. She corrupts Gandalaria's magic to take Bonzle, even calling her magic "poultry'' and simple to corrupt even for someone just learning like her.

Rox[]

“Ninja, while Rox puppeted in my body I was trapped inside her mind, I've seen her thoughts... her evil plans... You've got to stop her.”
— Jordana to Nya, Zane and Cole after breaking free from Rox's possession, "Elements of Betrayal"

While she was a general of the Wolf Clan alongside Cinder, Jordana was well aware of the Forbidden Five's existence, despite not knowing them or Rox personally. Her goal was to keep the portal open during the Ritual of the Blood Moon, waiting for all of them to escape through the portal she had opened. At the cost of Kai's sacrifice, Nokt was able to escape the Nether-Space before Bonzle managed to shut down the portal. But unbeknownst to both the ninja and the Wolf Clan at that time, Rox’s soul had managed to escape before the gate closes, taking possession of Jordana's body. Rox therefore started to control her remotely from the Nether-Space.

For the following days, Jordana, was constantly fighting Rox inside her mind to regain control of her body (which she managed to do a few times before being controlled again).

She managed to break free from her control when Rox regained her body and was freed by Nokt, she was still shook from what she had experienced inside her mind, and deeply hates Rox for it. She was also frightened by her evil plans, realizing what she had been doing all this time is wrong, and accepted to briefly aid the ninja so they could go stop her and Nokt.
