Overview

These are relationships of Nokt, master of Brute Force co-leader of the Forbidden Five, and former leader of the Wolf Clan.

Family

Family[]

Rox[]

“Rox, good to see you sister. The real you.”
— Nokt to Rox, "Elements of Betrayal"

Rox is the sister of Nokt. During the Ritual of the Blood Moon, both Nokt and Rox's soul were liberated from the Nether-Space. Following this pivotal event, the two siblings joined forces to kill three Arc Dragons.

However, after the conclusion of the final game in the Tournament of the Sources, both Nokt and Rox turned against Ras and made their way to the Monastery of Gates with the intent of rescuing the remaining members of the Forbidden Five. Upon successfully liberating the remaining members of the Forbidden Five and Rox's physical form, Nokt, Rox and the other freed members attempted to escape through a portal gate to the Cloud Kingdom and destroyed it, ending up in a different realm to evade capture.

After Drix captured Ras, Nokt was happy to get his revenge on Ras, despite Rox telling him to focus on their task. When Nokt ignored her, she labeled him as foolish and impulsive.

Allies

Allies[]

Forbidden Five[]

“Come to me my brethren. You are finally free!”
— Nokt, "Elements of Betrayal”

With his sister, Nokt is the leader of the Forbidden Five. Nokt seemed to care about the Five as he was furious that Ras wasn't able to free them as promised and after freeing them, he immediately checked on them after they fell from the portal connected to the Nether-Space.

Despite this, Nokt particularly has a dislike for Drix, making fun of him along with Kur that he failed. Even when Drix presented the Returning Blade, Nokt dismissed Drix with a brusque shove, showing little regard and instead fixating on his desire for vengeance against Ras.

Wolf warriors (formerly)[]

Before his imprisonment, Nokt led the wolf warriors to launch attacks wherever the Forbidden Five pleased in the Wyldness. However, after Nokt's release, the wolf warriors formed by Ras showed greater loyalty to him than to Nokt. When him and Rox betrayed Ras in the Tournament of the Sources, the Wolf Warriors faced them and were ready to fight them before Rox used her magic to take control of them and send them against Ras and the ninja. When the Five fled from the City of Temples, the Wolf Warriors were freed from Rox's controle and lost once again their allegiance to the Five.

Ras (formerly)[]

“There's many things I've come hate about you. One of them is how clever you think you are. You put a technological control collar on me, then you sent me to fight the Elemental Master of Technology?”
— Nokt declaring his betrayal toward Ras, "The Final Game

After being freed from the Nether-Space by Ras during the Ritual of the Blood Moon, Nokt supported Ras' plan to free the others and started fighting the ninja.

After Ras' master teleported the Shadow Dojo to safety, Nokt was anger that Ras, fled without freeing the remaining four members. Angered, Ras told Nokt not to dare treat him like that and Nokt tried to attack him but Ras placed a device on the back of his head that electrocuted him whenever he disobeyed orders. In doing so, Ras earned the involuntary allegiance of Nokt.

After the Tournament of the Sources, Nokt was able to free himself from Ras and immediately started disobeying him. Nokt then freed the rest of the Forbidden Five although that wasn't Ras' plan. After losing all of the Elemental Powers and while retreating, Nokt wanted to punish Ras for what he did although he wasn't able to do it due to Arin's interference, robbing Nokt of his revenge.

After Drix captured Ras, Nokt was happy to get his revenge on Ras, despite Rox telling him to focus on their task. Nokt engaged in combat with Ras that he was about to win before Arin's intervention. Despite Nokt's claiming he has no interest fighting in someone weaken, Nokt used Shatterspin and his power to take down Ras.

Cinder (formerly)[]

When Nokt was freed he was forced to join Ras and his allies including Cinder. But Nokt didn't seem to like Cinder, indeed during a training session with the later he was very violent towards him and he probably would have seriously injured him if he had not been stopped by Ras. After this, Nokt even growled at Cinder, which brought fear to the Master of Smoke.

However the two corrupted Elemental Masters accepted to join forces to fight the ninja.

Thunderfang (unwillingly)[]

As with the rest of the Forbidden Five, Nokt believed that Thunderfang would grant them greater powers in exchange for his release. Acting on this belief, Nokt devoted himself towards releasing the Chaos Dragon, though expressed surprise and shock when the dragon betrayed the Five and absorbed their souls.

Following Thunderfang's banishment to the Spectral Lands, Nokt and the other members of the Five remained enslaved to his will, even in death. Now rendered an obedient and loyal servant, Nokt served his new master without question to obtain souls for the latter's ritual in an effort to become a Source Dragon. As Thunderfang's servant, Nokt was shown to express some degree of fear and reservation before his new master, cowering from his wrath on one occasion when questioned why he had only obtained a few souls from Imperium, a supposedly densely populated area.

Dragonian Trackers (formerly)[]

Nokt used Thunderfang's gem to corrupt the peaceful Dragonians into warriors. Under his command, Nokt was demanding to them to find the Prismatic Blade.

Spectral Dragonians[]

After Nokt fell under Thunderfang's control, he led the Spectral Dragonians to capture souls of the Finders, Imperians, and Cole. In "When Doves Cry", he along with Rox and Drix led the Spectral Dragonians to attack Lloyd and Arin in the Spectral Lands.

Enemies

Enemies[]

Dragon Masters[]

Because Egalt and Rontu were the ones who trapped Nokt and his allies in the Nether-Space, Nokt likely has a huge grudge against them.

Ninja[]

“I don’t need Shatterspin to stop you ninja… But now, I have more Elemental Powers than anyone in history!”
— Nokt to Kai, "Elements of Betrayal"

Nokt had encountered the Ninja during the Blood Moon ritual. He attempted to sacrifice them to free the rest of the Five but was unable to. Nokt later fought them again during the Tournament of Sources where he went up against Nya, Lloyd, and Sora. Thanks to Bleckt's rigging, Nokt managed to defeat the Ninja and gain their powers. After freeing the rest of the Five, Nokt fought them again, overpowering them with a combination of Shatterspin and the stolen powers. When Kai was released from Nether-Space, the two dueled but the villain was drained of the usurped powers, forcing him to retreat alongside the rest of the Five, even though he refused to admit defeat. He seems to be a bit familiar with them, as he taunted Nya with her loss of Kai and Jay and he knew the master-student relationship between Kai and Wyldfyre.

Arc Dragons[]

“Why did you kill those three dragons? They did nothing to you.”
“For the crowns, of course. With enough Dragon Ivory, and a multitude of elemental powers... we don't need a Blood Moon ritual to bring back the rest of the Forbidden Five!”
— Sora and Nokt, "The Final Game"

Nokt had attacked three Arc Dragons to obtain their Crowns of Horns. He was unconcerned of their death.

Roby[]

“You've really crushed my vibe, Nokt. Games are supposed to be fun, dude. Look it up.”
— Roby to Nokt, "Elements of Betrayal"

Nokt had a dislike to Roby, where Roby had threaten to disqualified him after he continued to attack Tox. After Roby canceled Nokt's victory ceremony due to discovering Bleckt rigged the games to the Wolf Clan's favor, Nokt "uncancelled" the ceremony and manage to obtain all Elemental Powers in the cup. Roby teamed up with Ras to stop Nokt but Nokt managed to blast Roby off the Monastery of Gate's cliff. Roby got the last laugh at Nokt by draining the latter of the stolen powers which drained him and forced him to retreat.

Dragonians[]

Nokt and his colleagues were merciless with the the peaceful Dragonian people, by ransacking their village to find Thunderfang's prison, not hesitating to be violent with them, and then by corrupting them through Thunderfang's dark energy to make them their personal army.
