Overview

These are the relationships of Ninja rookie, and Elemental Master of Heat, Wyldfyre.

Family

Family[]

Heatwave (guardian)[]

“You're better! Of course you are. I can tell. Oh, you're all healed!”
— Wyldfyre to Heatwave, "The Administration"

Heatwave has been a father figure to Wyldfyre ever since she was a baby. When he was captured by the Claws of Imperium, she made it her mission to rescue him as he was her family and they had been together ever since. When she lost most of her dragon family to the Wasting Sickness, Wyldfyre feared that Heatwave might get it.

Caregiver bot (guardian)[]

Her caregiver bot has always been by Wyldfyre's side during her time growing up in the Wyldness to help Heatwave raise her, educate her, and help her control her newly discovered Elemental Power. When Wyldfyre went to rescue Heatwave, the caregiver bot tried to stop her, but found it futile.

Love interests

Love interests[]

Roby[]

“Time to go, cutie.”
— Wyldfyre to Roby, "Elements of Betrayal"

Roby is Wyldfyre's boyfriend. They first met during the Tournament of the Sources, where Wyldfyre was immediately smitten by him, often following him around. He calls Wyldfyre dragon idiom cute and winks at her. In "They Gather for the Feast", as Bleckt gives the ninja a tour of the City of Temples, Roby mocks the way he talks which Wyldfyre giggles at. Roby shows them a seven-headed dragon wall carving. Wyldfyre agrees with Roby that the display looks really cool. Roby winks at Wyldfyre and she giggles. He makes a joke about the wall the being nearly as old as Bleckt and Wyldfyre laughs loudly. During the banquet, Cinder teases Wyldfyre asking where the other Red Ninja is. Wyldfyre wants to fight him, however Roby stops the fight from happening. He loves the energy Wyldfyre is showing, but he tells her to save her powers for when the games begins the next day. After the banquet, the ninja would gather in their room. Their main goal there was to investigate who was the one that killed the Matriach dragon. They have several suspects in mind, Wyldfyre thinks that Bleckt is the one behind the death because of the way he treats whom she calls “the amazing Roby”.

In "Inside the Maze", Wyldfyre wanders around the city to search for Roby, but pretends she isn't doing so when she encounters him. She excitedly tells him that she can't wait to make the tournament a success and Roby thanks her. He invites her to check out the game plaza together with his Uncle Bleckt, which Wyldfyre immediately accepts.

In "United We Fall", Wyldfyre's opponent in the Tournament is the Elemental Master of Smoke, Cinder. Unfortunately, she lost to him in the round. Roby asked if she was okay and Wyldfyre says she's totally "stang-zoot", using the word Roby came up himself earlier before the games began. He says Wyldfyre's curtain of fire strategy amazing and he apologizes that he has to transfer her powers to Cinder.

In "Clues and Suspects", Arin tells Wyldfyre that he suspects Roby is the one behind the attacker bots attack before the tournament began that night, due to the symbol on Roby’s cufflinks looking like a clue Arin found. Wyldfyre doesn’t believe him because earlier Roby just saved the ninja from being kicked by Zeatrix by stopping them from fighting. However, she decides to join Arin in his investigation and prove Roby’s innocence. As Arin and Wyldfyre attempt to get inside Roby’s office, Roby and Bleckt show up. Wyldfyre was able set a diversion by telling Roby she likes his personality and cufflinks. She asks for a closer look and Roby explains that the cufflinks were given to him by Bleckt before the first game. This is the proof Wyldfyre needed to prove to Arin that Roby is innocent and she feels good about it. When Roby comes out from his office, Wyldfyre awkwardly says bye to him and realizes she walked in the wrong direction after Roby points it out to her. Roby tells her that it was good to see her too and calls her “Wyfy”. Inside Roby’s office, Arin and Wyldfyre discover that Bleckt has a secret underground room filled with photos of him wanting to get rid off Roby. Wyldfyre was shock to find it and felt terrified to be in there.

In "Elements of Betrayal", Wyldfyre sees Roby with Ras and thinks that Ras captured him. They head towards the monastery nearby, where the battle between the ninja, Ras, and Nokt is happening. Outside the monastery, Roby is holding on a side of a cliff, barely holding himself up from falling down a deep trench. Wyldfyre shows up and grabs his hand to get him get back up. Roby declares his love for Wyldfyre and they both lean in to kiss. Wyldfyre suddenly roars, which caught Roby off guard. Wyldfyre explains it's a romance roar, which in her culture of fire dragons, is how they declare their love. Roby roars back and they both keep roaring back at each other. After the tournament is over, Wyldfyre has to go back with the Ninja. Roby and Wyldfyre agree to stay in touch with each other. She says to him that she’ll never forget their first roar and then they both roar together. Wyldfyre winks at Roby before she leaves, calling him cutie and Roby blows a kiss at her.

Instructor

Instructor[]

Kai[]

“No one ends the Red Ninja.”
— Wyldfyre to Nokt, "Elements of Betrayal"

Kai is Wyldfyre's master. They meet in "The Battle of the Second Monastery" she unintentionally insulted him about his Fire powers being the same as hers, Kai was confused and frustrated between them but they still shared a high five over their agreement of battle. Over time Kai and Wyldfyre kept being at odds with each other but with Kai's lessons on patience, Wyldfyre slowly learned to accept and care for Kai.

In "The Temple of the Dragon Cores", the grudge goes to a new height as Wyldfyre's cocky attitude angers Kai to the point they fight each other only to be broken up by Zane. Later Kai and Wyldfyre work together to fix the sanctuary but Kai says that her percision need work

In "Gangs of the Sea", Wyldfyre makes a joke about Kai's age.

In "Wyldly Inappropriate", Kai and Wyldfyre are trapped by the Greenbone Warriors; sentenced to death for stealing their gems, while they both deny they stole them, they both figured out a way to get out of the pit and Wyldfyre admits to stealing them as a prank. Kai made an agreement to the Greenbone Warriors that they won't come back but Wyldfyre makes a joke with her Heat power allowing them to escape. Kai lectures to Wyldfyre that she needs to take her power seriously but she ignores Kai and claims he's boring. He later reminds Wyldfyre of the plan to get the Dragon Core but she still takes it lightly. When they reach the Lava-Tides: Kai is surprised and irritated with Wyldfyre's antics and ignorance towards the Lava-Tides, but tries to win them over by explaining the dire situation, convinces them that Wyldfyre has changed, and begs for the Dragon Core. With Wyldfyre slowly understanding Kai's lesson on bullying, Wyldfyre accepts her deeds and made "peace" with the Lava-Tides only to be betrayed as a prank but they work together to get the Dragon Core and escape the Pit of No Return.

In "They Call It Doom", Wyldyre's ignorance and stubborness get in the way of getting the Dragon Core and lead to Heatwave getting injured.

In "Land of Lost Things", Kai's lesson on patience and his cure for Heatwave lead to Wyldfyre being more calm and a little more patient to the current situation.

In "The Administration", Wyldfyre's impatience makes her upset and stressed with Heatwave's injury and getting the Dragon Core back to the other ninja. But Kai makes Wyldfyre follow his forms to calm her down and make her more patient. Despite Wyldfyre's doubts, Kai encourages this because she reminds Kai of himself. The forms helped Wyldfyre calm down and be more patient, letting Heatwave heal.

In "Absolute Power", Kai tells Wyldfyre to let loose and ignore his lessons about patience long enough for the ninja to get into Imperium.

After some complications, such as her grudge with the Lava-Tides, Heatwave being injured, and being taught patience, she eventually warmed up to him. When she got injured by Cinder, Wyldfyre, despite Kai's demands, came with him after smuggling aboard the Bounty. When Kai was sent to the Nether-Space, she was completely sad and dedicated herself to ensuring that she lived up to what Wyldfyre believed Kai would have wanted her to be. After his return, Wyldfyre did not hesitate to hug him.

Friends

Friends[]

Lloyd (brief mentor)[]

“Wyldfyre's been training non-stop since…”
“Since my brave, stupid brother got himself trapped forever in the Nether-Space. Wyldfyre's trying to live up to what she thinks Kai would want.”
“I know a thing or two about thinking that way. But I'm worried she's putting too much pressure on herself.”
“Says the king of putting too much pressure on himself.”
— Lloyd and Nya, "The Shape of Motion"

Lloyd is Kai's friend. She first met him when he freed her from the Imperium Prison, mistaking her for Kai, and attacked because he was wearing an Imperian uniform, until he saved her from some guards and joined the ninja after escaping Imperium with Heatwave and the other dragons. Lloyd and Wyldfyre became close where after Cinder knocked Lloyd down she attempted to attack Cinder only for her leg to get broken.

Wyldfyre even cared about him as after he was injured by Zeatrix, she angrily confronted her which made the latter lose her temper. Lloyd was confused when he saw Wyldfyre and Roby roaring their love, only for Sora to drag him away.

Riyu[]

Riyu and Wyldfyre are close friends. They are often together, Riyu notably helped Wyldfyre when she was injured in the leg, by carrying her on his back. They both seem to feel good and comfortable together, Wyldfyre having been raised by dragons and Riyu being a dragon who live with humans. They notably rest together on the same bed in the Tournament of the Sources while Heatwave, Wyldfyre's family, wasn't present.

Nya[]

“You okay?”
“Of course! Kai and I had this totally under control.”
— Nya and Wyldfyre, "Rising Ninja"

Nya is Kai's sister. Nya appears to care for Wyldfyre as she was disappointed to see the latter stowed away, despite having a broken leg. When Kai was sent to the Nether-Space, they became closer. Nya comforted Wyldfyre, but expressed worry upon seeing the latter trying to honor him by becoming the new Red Ninja from being overconfident. Nya also often help retrain Wyldfyre of her anger and Wyldfyre in return show care for Nya, notably when she was worries about Nya when her boyfriend Jay was corrupted by Shatterspin, or when she assiduously supported Nya and showed strong animosity towards her opponent, in Nya's duel with Nokt in the Tournament of the Sources while the latter tried to destabilize her by evoking Kai and Jay; as well as when Wyldfyre and Sora, hugged Nya in "The Vault of Focus" after she was disappointed in Jay's behavior, while she hoped that there was still goodness in him.

Arin[]

Arin is Wyldfyre's teammate. Arin once gave Wyldfyre a brief reprimand for her actuality of relieving herself wherever she wants, and wondering if she'll ever train with him and the others. During the Tournament of the Sources, Arin and Wyldfyre both searched for clues for Bleckt's involvement in Ras' plan to use the Forbidden Five. When Arin leaves the team, she is both upset and concerned about Arin since she has grown close to him. During the battle against the Chaos Dragon, Wyldfyre shows no contempt or any hard feelings on Arin as she helped him to gain the upper hand against the Thunderfang. Following Arin's victory, she is saddened when Arin leaves the team again that she quickly jumped on board in finding Barry and Jenny after Jay reveals that Arin's parents are alive. Showing that she doesn't want Arin to be led astray. When Arin rejoins the team again, she immediately welcomed him back.

Sora[]

“That wasn't Arin, you used your Tech powers to—”
— Wyldfyre figuring out Sora used her powers to help Arin, "Rising Ninja"

Sora is Wyldfyre's teammate. Despite the rocky start, the two began to work together. In "Rising Ninja", Wyldfyre quickly figured out that Sora help Arin throw the Elemental Fire Mech's hand. Both Wyldfyre and Sora agreed to keep it a secret, not wanting to burden Arin with the knowledge of Sora's intervention. When Lloyd interrupted Wyldfyre and Roby's moment, Sora was the one who dragged him away, which she seemed to appreciate. In "Clues and Suspects", Wyldfyre showed a confused face when Arin rejected Sora's help. But she knows full well how Arin must've figured out the secret. When Arin left, Wyldfyre is left concerned of Sora. Then, when Sora chooses to follow Arin following Thunderfang's defeat, she shows shock and concern that Sora is willing to follow Arin but doesn't question it.

Frak[]

Frak is Wyldfyre's teammate. They met in the Tournament of Sources after they found Arin. Arin introduced Wyldfyre and other ninja to his old friend. They had more interaction at the end of season 3 when the ninja split into teams to awkaken the half of the Arc Dragon in Djinjago. As they were flying on Zanth to Djinjago, Frak commented on her singing that they cannot let her to ring bells, which made Wyldfyre angry. Later during the battle, Frak handed Wyldfyre his Reveal Blade to destroy the Spectral Dragonians. After the ninja were scattered across the Merged Lands, she was with Frak and other young ninja in the Land of Eternal Dawn. Frak as well as Sora became under the spell of the land, however, Wyldfyre kept telling that it's not true.

Cole[]

“Wyldfyre, your anger is not helping.”
“It's helping me stay angry.”
— Cole and Wyldfyre, "Truth and Lies"

Cole is Wyldfyre's teammate. They had little interaction, besides Cole trying to calm her down when Nokt insulted Nya’s brother Kai and her boyfriend Jay only for Wyldfyre to cause him look annoyed. Even during Nya's fight Cole tried to help calm her down.

Zane[]

Zane is Wyldfyre's teammate. They had little interaction, apart from him breaking up a fight between her and Kai, as well as become insulted by her when he wasn't able to hit the Spirit of the Temple.

Zur[]

“I dare, cause I've got your ledger. And I can read it, thanks to my new best bud, Zur.”
— Wyldfyre to Bleckt, "The Final Game"

Zur is a fellow Elemental Master, whom Wyldfyre met in the Tournament of Sources. She was disappointed that Zane lost to him. When they came across each other, Zur was able to help translate the writing in Bleckt's ledger, also telling her about himself and his kind. She called him a friend when they confronted Bleckt at Nokt's victory ceremony.

Jay[]

“I haven't met him, to be honest.”
— Wyldfyre to Lightning-Tides, The Missing

Jay is a ninja, yet the two hardly had any interaction. They first met each other at the Tournament of Sources, yet did not interact with each other.

Following the tournament, Wyldfyre joined Kai and Nya to search for Jay. She also commented that Jay is better at disappearing than Mr. Pale.

It is likely that, after the Loyalist assault, Wyldfyre now knows that Jay has regained his memory and that they may have resolved their differences to become true teammates.

Enemies

Enemies[]

Lava-Tides[]

Wyldfyre's constant pranking led to the Lava-Tides having a tremendous dislike for her.

Beatrix[]

As Beatrix was responsible for capturing Heatwave (and thus for Wyldfyre's imprisonment as well), Wyldfyre likely hated the former Empress for her crimes.

Freebooters[]

Wyldfyre first encountered the Freebooters during the Battle of the Second Monastery, when they were known as the Claws of Imperium at the time. Following the Liberation of Imperium, Wyldfyre discovered that the Rapton, despite helping the ninja defeat Beatrix, had rebranded the Claws of Imperium as the Freebooters, allowing him and the Claw Hunters to persist in their dragon hunting endeavors.

Dorama[]

Wyldfyre meet Dorama when she and Kai embarked on a mission to find the purple Dragon Core. Dorama had cunningly snatching it from Kai and Wyldfyre. Wyldfyre found his speech boring and his staff's ability to drain their attacks to be unfair. Together with Kai, they tried to thwart Dorama's plan of delivering the Dragon Core to the Empress. However, their efforts were in vain as Dorama successfully swapped the real core with a fake one during their clash with the Lava-Tides.

Ras (temporary ally)[]

Wyldfyre first met Ras during the Battle of the Second Monastery. She likely resents him for capturing Heatwave and the other dragons. Later, when Ras reassembled the Wolf Clan, Wyldfyre and the rest of the ninja embarked on a mission to prevent Ras from releasing the Forbidden Five. Although Ras managed to free Nokt (and unknowingly Rox) by using Kai as a sacrifice. Her resentment towards him presumably increased because of this. With the help of Zur, she was able to expose his and Bleckt's conspiracy throughout the Tournament of Sources.

Cinder[]

“Oops, sorry”
“That was on purpose Cinder!”
“So? What are you going to do about it? You and your little ninja friends gonna cry?”
“Keep talking and you’ll be the one crying! Tears of fire!”
— Cinder and Wyldfyre, "Inside the Maze"

Wyldfyre has a massive grudge against Cinder. During their first battle, Cinder easily defeated her by breaking her leg. In the Tournament of Sources, Cinder used the non-violence rule to intimidate Wyldfyre as well as insulted Kai, to the point after her defeat against him, Wyldfyre nearly attacked Cinder, only for him to fall back, feigning at being hit. She was presumably satisfied to see her rival incarcerated along with the Wolf Clan.

Zeatrix[]

“Was that supposed to be an apology? I don't know what your caregiver bot taught you, but mine always said apologies should have the words "I'm sorry." You know what should be done to you, Creep-atrix? You should be… tied up! In front of a really delicious melon that you'll never be able to reach. No, no, you should be poked with a million sticks, while playing songs you hate on a loop, forever!”
“You don’t know who you’re messing with!”
— Wyldfyre and Zeatrix, "Clues and Suspects"

Zeatrix is the twin sister of Beatrix. When Zeatrix ruthlessly struck Lloyd down following her loss in the tournament, Wyldfyre began making insults against the former, only for Zeatrix to knock her back with her power before Roby interfered.

Bleckt[]

“I don't care for being jostled about like a sack of beets! But I really don't care for getting crushed, so… thank you for your timely intervention, young lady.”
— Bleckt to Wyldfyre after she 'saves' him from a brick, "The Final Game"

Bleckt is Roby's uncle. She easily found Bleckt suspicious due his harsh attitude towards Roby before she managed to swap his ledger for a menu and, thanks to Zur, discovered he was behind rigging the tournament.
