These are the relationships of Dorama, the former famous pyro-technician and showman in Ninjago.

Allies

Allies[]

Fedulian[]

During the Liberation of Imperium, Dorama reunited with his old friend Fedulian. Together, they devised a plan to capture Algae farmers and Crossroads citizens by using fake maps.

Ras (formerly)[]

“And so enters, stage left, the authorities the ninja said they'd call. Pitiful. So trite. Predictable.”
“That call was intercepted. No authorities are coming.”
“Who are you, then?”
“Your new boss. I have a job for you.”
— Dorama and Ras, "Crossroads Carnival"

When Dorama was imprisoned by Arin, Sora, and Lloyd due to his abduction of the Whack Rats, Ras arrived at Dorama's stagehouse. He intervened the ninja's call to the authorities, effectively thwarting the latter's arrest. In a turn of events, Ras extended an offer to Dorama, having him develop additional Photacs for Beatrix.

Dr. LaRow (formerly)[]

Dorama had an interest in Dr. LaRow and flirted with her at least a few times based on the latter's claims that he "repeatedly" tried inviting her to his one man musical. Apart from that, Dorama kept his relationship with Dr. LaRow fairly professional in the presence of others.

Beatrix (formerly)[]

Dorama was completely enamored with Empress Beatrix and her power, relishing in any and every opportunity to work closer with her and showering her with compliments. His loyalty to the Empress however seemed at least partially motivated by self-interest, for he hoped that the Empress would be pleased with him after he uncovered Rapton's status as a traitor. Nevertheless, even Dorama was forced to admit that the Empress had gone completely insane by the time of the MergeQuake Storm and he acknowledged that he may not have factored in the potential for his own destruction in assisting the Empress.

His stagehands (formerly)[]

Dorama's stagehands served as Dorma's personal guard and were subordinate to him.

Rapton (formerly)[]

Prior to Rapton's betrayal, Dorama seemed to have a slightly more positive attitude toward the Dragon Hunter compared to his other peers. Dorama notably did not participate in any ribbing towards Rapton, unlike Dr. LaRow and Empress Beatrix, and even tried to make small talk with him following the disastrous test of the MergeQuake weapon, only to discover that Rapton had disappeared.

Upon learning that Rapton had turned traitor however, any goodwill Dorama may have had towards his former ally was thrown out the window and Dorama treated him no differently than any of his other enemies.

Jordana (formerly)[]

Jordana was a colleague of Dorama's when they both served Empress Beatrix.

Enemies

Enemies[]

Whack Rats[]

“Why Whack Rats, weirdo?”
“To put on a better show! They have a unique energy, which I can extract and use to create incredibly vibrant, colorful fireworks! That's why my carnival finale is always so spectacular!”
— Arin and Dorama, "Crossroads Carnival"

After the Merge, Dorama kidnapped Targle alongside two other Whack Rats and imprisoned them in cage. As he discovered that Whack Rats have a unique energy that he can use to create more colorful fireworks and used their unique energy from Whack Rats for creating fireworks. However when Arin, Sora, and Lloyd arrived to stop Dorama's plans, they successfully managed to free Targle alongside two other Whack Rats and Dorama was been imprisoned in cage by them for brief time before was been freed by Ras.

Ninja[]

“Oh, you are not the ninja I was hoping for. No matter, my revenge will still be sweet since it was your kind that ruined me.”
— Dorama to Cole, Zane and Bonzle, "To Mysterium"

Dorama's animosity towards the ninja first began when Lloyd, Arin, and Sora disrupted his plans and released the Whack Rats from their imprisonment, ultimately leading to Dorama's capture for his wrongdoings. However, his time in captivity was short-lived as Ras arrived to set him free, with the intention of mending the original Photac and creating duplicates. Subsequently, Dorama ventured into the Wyldness with the aim of pilfering the coveted purple Dragon Core and delivering it to Beatrix. It was during this mission that he encountered Kai and Wyldfyre, who attempted to thwart his efforts. Despite their best efforts, Dorama cunningly managed to deceive the ninja by substituting the real Dragon Core with a counterfeit one. As the Liberation of Imperium unfolded, Dorama launched an assault on the Resistance, determined to prevent them from disseminating crucial information regarding Beatrix's deceit to the Imperians. Despite encountering numerous setbacks, Riyu ultimately succeeded in halting Dorama's assault, leading to his subsequent apprehension and subsequent restraint with handcuffs.

Following the year of the Liberation of Imperium with the assistance of his puppet and Fedulian, Dorama successfully captured Cole, Zane, and Bonzle. However, their capture was short-lived as the trio managed to escape from the electro cage in which they were held captive. They not only freed the algae farmers and Crossroad citizens but also put an end to Dorama's plans.

Algae farmers & Crossroad citizens[]

“It's not easy finding students. Thankfully my old friend Fedulian and I made a deal. His fake maps lead people to my school. And after I recruit them. We rehearse!”
— Dorama to Cole, Zane, and Bonzle, "To Mysterium"

After the Kingdom of Imperium's Liberation, Fedulian aided Dorama in capturing several Crossroads citizens and algae farmers. These locals were then compelled to join Dorama's Academy for Acting. However, Cole, Zane, and Bonzle intervened and successfully rescued them from Dorama's clutches. Once Dorama was defeated, the algae farmers and Crossroad citizens apprehended him for his crimes, finally bringing him to justice by placing him in jail.

Bonzle[]

Following the Liberation of Imperium, Dorama successfully managed to apprehend Bonzle, Zane, and Cole with the assistance of his puppet and Fedulian. Subsequently, he confined them within the confines of Dorama's Academy for Acting. Surprisingly, Bonzle, being a spell, effortlessly maneuvered her way through the Imperium electro cage, disabling it in the process. This act granted Cole and Zane their freedom, enabling them to liberate the algae farmers and Crossroad citizens. Ultimately, the trio, consisting of Bonzle, Cole, and Zane, emerged victorious in their battle against Dorama.
