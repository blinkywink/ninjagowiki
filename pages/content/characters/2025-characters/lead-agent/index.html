Overview

“Filing a false report is a severe policy violation punishable by being made to fill out many reports about the false report. Do you have anything to say in your defense?”
— The Lead Agent to the Lead Loyalist, "Just Under the Surface"

The Lead Agent[1] is an Administration Agent tasked with leading the Delta League to respond to a supposed report on a monster.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Human Resources[]

The Lead Agent was at the HR Department when the ninja and Jay was imprisoned there.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

Tonight We Hunt Monsters[]

As the Freebooters were making lots of noise outside of the Administration, Agent Ramirez led a group of Administration Agents, including the Lead Agent, outside. Then the Lead Agent tried to stop the Freebooters with her colleagues, only for them to overpower the agents. After the Freebooters "retreated", the Lead Agent and the other agents was congratulated and commanded to confiscate the Transforming Car the Freebooters left behind by Agent Ramirez.

Just Under the Surface[]

The Lead Agent led a convoy to the site of a reported attack, only to find the Lead Loyalist instead. The latter proclaims to be taking over the convoy, and reveals that several other members of Delta League have been undercover Loyalists. A fight ensues, during which the Lead Agent is able to overpower a few of the Loyalists, but is ultimately forced to call a retreat when the remaining agents cannot handle the situation.
