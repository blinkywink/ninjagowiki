Overview

“In Dead's End, no one's our friend.”
— Zane, to Kai and Jay in "Iron & Stone"

Dead's End is the home of the Dragon Hunters and the dragons they formerly imprisoned.

History

History[]

Firstbourne[]

Kai, Jay, and Zane, who were captured by the Dragon Hunters, are brought into the settlement blindfolded and taken into Iron Baron's throne room, where their blindfolds are removed for them to meet the latter.

Iron & Stone[]

Kai, Jay, and Zane are brought through the area by Daddy No Legs to be put in the Pit.

Radio Free Ninjago[]

The Dragon Hunters were preparing the Dieselnaut, jets, and trucks, leaving Dead's End to capture a dragon.

How to Build a Dragon[]

The Dragon Hunters returned to the camp after they caught and chained the Wind Dragon. When night came and they were about to feast, the Firstbourne arrived and started to shoot Fire and Ice at the area, destroying everything in her path and causing the hunters to flee.
