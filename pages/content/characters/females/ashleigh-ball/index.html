Ashleigh Adele Ball (born March 31, 1983) is a Canadian voice actress, singer, and musician. She voices a young Aspheera, a doctor in "Under Siege" and Sally in Ninjago. She also voices Dr. LaRow, Kur and Seafoam Green in Ninjago: Dragons Rising.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • She voiced Applejack and Rainbow Dash in My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic, Mary Test and Sissy Blakely in Johnny Test, and Pinky in Pac-Man and the Ghostly Adventures.
    • She is one of eight voice actors to work on Johnny Test before working on Ninjago: the other seven were Trevor Devall, Richard Newman, Scott McNeil, Marÿke Hendrikse, Kathleen Barr, Ian James Corlett, Andrew Francis, and Lee Tockar.
    • Ball was replaced by Emily Tennant as Mary in the revival seasons of Johnny Test.
  • She, along with Adrian Petriw, Vincent Tong, Mackenzie Gray, Michael Adamthwaite, Dean Redman, Michael Daingerfield, Michael Dobson, Brian Drummond, Andrew Francis, Michael Kopsa, Richard Newman, Mark Oliver, Tabitha St. Germain, and Lee Tockar voiced characters in Iron Man: Armor Adventures.
  • She is a part of the Canadian indie pop band Hey Ocean! and is also a music artist.
  • She, alongside Vincent Tong and Kathleen Barr, have voiced characters in Lego Elves, another Lego theme.
  • Dr. LaRow and Kur are the only characters she voices in the Ninjago franchise that has a speaking role in more than one episode.
    • Aspheera appears in more episodes but she is voiced by different voice actresses due to the character's age.
