Overview

Rhona Rees (born January 1, 1984) is an Australian voice actress who voiced Jet Jack in Season 9 of Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu and SsssSsss Underwood in Season 1 and Season 2 of Ninjago: Dragons Rising.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Rees has over 22 years of performing experience.
  • She has starred in various commercials, as well as many films and shows, like Final Vision, Summer Love, and Signed, Sealed, Delivered.
  • She is the first Australian voice actor to portray a character in Ninjago.
