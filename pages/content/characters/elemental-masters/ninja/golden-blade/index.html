Golden blade

Information

Status

Intact

First appearance

9441 Kai's Blade Cycle

Overview

External links
The golden blade is an elaborate golden knife-like weapon with a jagged edge and a small hilt on its side, making it appear similar to a tonfa when held. In addition to having a silver counterpart, this weapon appears in black, yellowish green, purple, and dark gray.

It is usually on Ninja vehicles as details, small weapons that can be launched at enemies, and occasionally on dragons too. It is also a part of larger weapons sometimes.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

The golden blade comes as standard equipment with some of the Ninja vehicles. May be fired at will. Watch your fingers and toes![1]
