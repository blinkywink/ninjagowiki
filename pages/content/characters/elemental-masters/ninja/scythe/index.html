Overview

“I thought I was good with a scythe.”
— Cole to Lloyd, "The Invitation"

The scythe is a basic ninja weapon used in Ninjago. A scythe is a long stick with a blade sticking out of the side of the stick to use to slice things and is one of the greatest ninja weapons that can also be used in melee battles. The scythe also comes in different versions. Cole initially specialized in the use of Scythes, including the Scythe of Quakes, but he switched to a large hammer.
