Overview

For the martial art, see Airjitzu.

Airjitzu is a game that replaced Ninjago Spinners in the summer of 2015.

How to play

How to play[]

Unlike Ninjago Spinners that is played with cards, the objective of the game is to do tricks with the Flyers. Each player needs a Ninjago character, an Airjitzu Flyer, a LEGO brick handle, and a ripcord.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Wrayth has a flyer despite never doing Airjitzu in the show.
  • Lloyd and Nya are the only two ninja to have their Airjitzu Flyers exclusive to a larger set.
  • The Airjitzu Flyers bear a resemblance to spinners and the Ninjago Spinners game.
