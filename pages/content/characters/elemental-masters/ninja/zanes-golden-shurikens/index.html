Overview

The golden shurikens are shurikens that serve as Zane's main weapons.

History

History[]

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Wasted True Potential[]

Zane's golden shurikens were hung on the wall of Zane's room.

Questing for Quests[]

Zane brought his golden shurikens with him while he and the ninja were travelling around Ninjago City.

A Rocky Start[]

Zane was holding his shurikens when he was running in the Monastery's hallway.

Powerless[]

Zane wore his shurikens when he and the ninja were fighting the Elemental Cobras and when they to the Museum of HIstory.

Ancient History[]

Zane wore his shurikens when he, Lloyd and Nya were looking for Aspheera and when he went to find the ShuriCopter.

The Explorers Club[]

Zane was wearing his shuriken when he, Lloyd and Nya went went in the Explorer's Club, after the failed attempts at trying to get the Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu, Zane left his Shurikens at the roof of the Explorer's Club right before he and the others sneaked inside.

Prime Empire[]

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

Zane wore his shurikens when he and the ninja were fighting the Mechanic and his henchmen, he later wore them while he and the others were investigating the Mechanic's headquarters.

Dyer Island[]

Zane wore his shurikens when he and the ninja were investigating Jay's dissappearance.

Master of the Mountain[]

The Worst Rescue Ever[]

Zane used his shurikens to fight some of the Re-Awakened Warriors, he also used them to free some Geckles.

The Island[]

The Keepers of the Amulet[]

Zane wore his shurikens when he and the ninja were exploring the jungle, he later used them to fight the Stone Guardians.

The Tooth of Wojira[]

Zane used his shurikens to fight some of Ronin's henchmen and some Kryptarium inmates.

Seabound[]

A Big Splash[]

Zane wore his shurikens when he and Nya tried to stop Miss Demeanor, unfortunately Nya lost control of her elemental powers causing a small tidal wave.

The Turn of the Tide[]

Zane used his shurikens to knock out some Merlopians on the rooftop of the Police Station.

Crystalized[]

The Shape of Nya[]

Zane brought his shurikens with him in the N line in hopes to find who is behind the Vengestone smuggling, he later used them to fight Miss Demeanor's henchmen but was almost defeated by them if the New Ninja hadn't stopped the convict's minions.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

When Zane came back to the monastery after the Ninja found him after being lost for years due to The Merge, he started using his shurikens again as his main weapons.
