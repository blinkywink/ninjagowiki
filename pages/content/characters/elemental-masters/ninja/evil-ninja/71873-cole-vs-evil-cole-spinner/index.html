71873 Cole vs. Evil Cole Spinner is a Ningao: Dragons Rising - Dragons' Fury set that will be released on January 1, 2027. It includes Cole and Evil Cole.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Reenact thrilling good vs. evil conflicts from LEGO® NINJAGO® Dragons’ Fury with this Cole vs. Evil Cole Spinner battle toy for kids, or go on unique adventures. Place the Cole minifigure in the spinner and push the button on the launcher to send him whirling. Watch the explosive action as the detachable covers fly off when the spinner hits Evil Cole or any other obstacle that gets in the way. Decide which character goes into the spinner to whirl his way to victory, then clip his hammer to the launcher for safekeeping while going for a wild ride. Use the LEGO Builder app to guide little ones through every step. With digital instructions, they can rotate and zoom in on models in 3D to build with confidence on their own. Make it an exciting and entertaining birthday with this engaging gift idea for boys and girls ages 6 and up who love ninja playsets. Set contains 58 pieces.

  • SPINJITZU ACTION – Face off against Cole’s inner evil and challenge him to fast-paced duels with this ninja toy with launcher and spinner for kids ages 6 and up
  • DO THE TORNADO TWIRL – Push the button on the launcher to send the spinner whirling, and watch the covers fly off as they strike a challenger or other obstacles
  • GRAB A WEAPON FOR EACH CHARACTER – Arm the Cole and Evil Cole minifigures with their hammers for one-on-one confrontations between hero and villain
  • LAUNCH INTO EPIC BATTLES– Recreate scenes from the LEGO® NINJAGO® Dragons’ Fury animated series or imagine unique adventures with a different winner each time
  • NINJA GIFT IDEA – Treat a young fan of battle toys and the LEGO® NINJAGO® animated series with this small birthday, holiday, or anytime gift
  • GO ON LIMITLESS ADVENTURES – Inspire kids to explore the universe of action-packed journeys and battles of good vs. evil with other LEGO® NINJAGO® toys (sold separately)
  • BUILD IT MYSELF – The LEGO® Builder app breaks each set into simple steps and lets kids rotate and zoom in on models in 3D, giving them the confidence to build on their own
  • DIMENSIONS – This 58-piece spinner set in the launcher with the minifigure inside measures over 2 in. (6 cm) high, 3 in. (9 cm) wide, and 4 in. (11 cm) deep[1]
