"I was bitten by a snake."
This article is about a subject that is relevant to both the main Ninjago storyline and The LEGO Ninjago Movie. Information may be more directed toward one continuity than the other.

External links
The Ninja Bow is a weapon used by various peoples. It is a simple longbow with an arrow already in firing position. The middle of the longbow has bindings to enhance the user's grip.

A black bow and quiver was used by Koko as the Lady Iron Dragon. At one point, Zane used a black bow and quiver as his main weapon.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Arrows shot from Ninja Bows fly far and true. Able to hit targets a mile away, it's nearly impossible to outrun these metal tipped projectiles. They're also good for hunting wild apples in high trees.[1]
