Overview

This article is about the set. For the temple itself, see Temple of Airjitzu. For the episode, see The Temple on Haunted Hill.

70751 Temple of Airjitzu is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - Possession set released in Summer 2015.

This set includes Kai, Jay, Nya, Zane, Cole, and Lloyd in their Deepstone suits, Wu in his Temple outfit, Misako, the Postman, Dareth, Claire, Jesper, a staute of Sensei Yang, and the Temple of Airjitzu.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Enjoy the ultimate LEGO® NINJAGO™ building experience as you construct the majestic Temple of Airjitzu and surrounding village! The highly detailed Temple building has many delightful architectural features including Oriental–style decorated windows and sliding doors. The intricate wooden-frame-style walls of the nearby Blacksmiths and beautiful curved roofs of the Smugglers Market will also test your LEGO building skills. And with every Ninja included in one set for the first time, plus loads of other key characters, you can role-play all your favorite NINJAGO adventures. Take tea in the reception area of the Temple, practice your Ninja moves in the training room or join Wu in his study to read or paint. Discover the Ninja glider upstairs at the Blacksmith’s workshop, cross the bridge guarded by the stone dragons and seek out hidden treasure at the Smugglers’ Market. Then turn off the lights in your room to watch the Temple’s illuminated spinning shadow theater! Includes 12 minifigures with assorted weapons: Lloyd, Kai, Jay, Zane, Cole, Nya, Wu, Misako, Dareth, Postman, Jesper, and Claire.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Despite being released over a year before "Day of the Departed" was released, this set pays homage to the ending scene of the special, which depicts the Temple of Airjitzu being cleansed after the Rift of Return is closed. The ninja then decide to use the temple as a base, which is depicted in this set.
  • In several promotional images for the set, Cole is accidentally featured twice on the images.
  • This is the first Shop-at-Home-exclusive Ninjago set.
  • The Postman, Claire, and Jesper are exclusive to this set, as are these variants of Wu, Misako and Dareth.
    • This is the first appearance of Misako and the Postman as minifigures, despite being a part of the series for about three to four years prior to this set's release.
    • This is also Dareth's first appearance in a major set. Previously, he was only released in a polybag containing him, a trophy, and a Nindroid.
      • In this set, Dareth a more generic face, one that was first used on the Mayor from LEGO's Fusion theme, while in the polybag he had an accurate face print unique to him. This is because a polybag is merchandise rather than a regular set, and merchandise has more freedom due to how many prints need to be made.[3]
  • This set includes Claire and Jesper, two characters who are not present in the TV series.
  • This is the second non-promotional Ninjago set to feature no antagonists, the first being the 2516 Ninja Training Outpost.
  • This is the largest Ninjago set based on something from the TV series.
  • In front of the temple are three Chinese flags which translate to:
    • 均衡 - Jūnhéng - balanced
    • 正袤 - Zhèng mào - a positive expanse
    • 名誉 - Míngyù - reputation
  • This is the first Ninjago set to have all of the original six ninja.
  • In one of Brent Miller's videos, Miller interviewed one of the LEGO designers who said that it took 16 months to design the set.[citation needed]
  • Claire and Jesper's bios were written by Tommy Andreasen.[4]
  • The glider that is featured in this set is nearly identical to the glider in the 1999 Ninja set, 1187 Glider. However, according to Michael Knap, it can double as one of Jay's gliders as well.[5]
  • The fireworks launcher in this set is featured in Lego Legacy: Heroes Unboxed as a part of Lloyd's abilities (Prince of Pranks).
