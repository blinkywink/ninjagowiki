Bowie Knives are weapons from Ninjago. They appear as simple knives with a short, slightly curved blade, and are described as "timeless" weapons used throughout history by ninja, being light and sturdy. They come in silver and gold versions, sometimes being part of larger weapons, as well as builds.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Time may change, but Bowie Knives are timeless. From cavemen to clowns, to sailors fighting spiders in dance halls, everyone uses these classic hip-holstered weapons.[3][4]
