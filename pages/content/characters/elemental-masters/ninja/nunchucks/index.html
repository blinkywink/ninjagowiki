Overview

“Anyone can hold nunchucks!”
— Cole to Jay, "Never Trust a Snake"

Nunchucks (or nunchaku) are basic ninja weapons used in Ninjago. The nunchucks are two handles that are connected to a flexible chain connected to the top of the handles to make it a weapon. The length of said chain depends on the pair. Nunchucks are one of the greatest ninja weapons that can be used in melee combat.

Jay initially specialized in the use of nunchucks, including the Nunchucks of Lightning, before switching to a kusarigama.

Arin and Sora used a set of nunchucks to play fetch with Riyu at times.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Floating Ruins resemble giant nunchucks.
  • The Martial Arts Boy from Minifigures Series 20 has a one-piece nunchucks weapon that has not appeared in any Ninjago sets but is more accurate to the show than the ones in Ninjago sets.
  • Throughout the series, there have been more than one nunchuck design used, as seen in the difference between the nunchucks used before Season 8 and those seen afterwards.
    • In addition, the Nunchucks of Lightning adopt their own design.
  • Jay's nunchucks were slightly upgraded in the Oni Trilogy, having some blue detailing on the lower side.
  • Jay used his nunchucks in the Pilot episodes but stopped using them after he got the Nunchucks of Lightning. In Season 2, Jay returned to use his normal nunchucks after Garmadon stole his golden ones. Jay started to use a katana in Seasons 5 and 6, but then changed back to his nunchucks from Day of the Departed until Season 10. Jay's nunchucks were later replaced with his Golden Kusarigama in Season 11.
