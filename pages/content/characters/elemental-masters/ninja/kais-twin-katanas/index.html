Overview

The twin katanas are weapons used by Kai. The swords appear as two silver katanas with Kai's name written in Ninjargon on them.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Kai uses these as his main weapons in Sons of Garmadon and in March of the Oni until he receives the Sword of Fire back at the end of the episode "Endings".
  • They don't have Kai's name written on Ninjargon in the sets, so they just appear as regular silver katanas.
  • Kai appears with them in the Season 9 intro despite never using them in the season.
  • Kai never used his twin katanas again after Season 10. He only uses his golden katana now.
