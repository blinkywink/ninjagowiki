Overview

The Butterfly Sword is an ornate black sword with a long hilt, a round guard with curved tsubas on either side and a large, curved blade that widens at the tip and features a small point opposite the cutting edge.

It is generally associated with Samurai X, General Kozu, and the Giant Stone Warrior.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

A pretty name for a weapon that can cause so much damage. It is a must for any mysterious Samurai who is on a mission to fight off evil and restore world peace.[citation needed]
