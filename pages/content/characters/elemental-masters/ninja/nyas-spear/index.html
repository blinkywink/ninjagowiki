This article is about a subject that is relevant to both the main Ninjago storyline and The LEGO Ninjago Movie. Information may be more directed toward one continuity than the other.

The spear is a trident-like weapon used by Nya. The spear appears as a black stick with a sai spike on top and a black tassel on the end. It was eventually replaced by a golden spear.

History

History[]

Before Sons of Garmadon[]

At some point in the intervening year following Wu's disappearance after their confrontation with the Time Twins, Nya obtained this spear and it became her new weapon of choice.

Sons of Garmadon[]

Nya wielded her spear as her primary weapon in defending Ninjago against the Sons of Garmadon and the resurrected Garmadon himself. She either lost or discarded the weapon however, following the Ninja's defeat in the wake of the Fall of Ninjago City, forcing her and Lloyd to go on the run to resist Garmadon's new rule.

March of the Oni[]

During their final battle with the Oni atop the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Nya briefly wielded the reforged Scythe of Quakes before gladly handing it off to Cole following his return. Nya then produced this spear, or a replacement of it, and used it in combat against the Oni. She would later cease to use the spear following her receival of its golden variant.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Nya goes into battle with this mighty spear, which she can use to attack, parry or even as a pole for vaulting over foes.[citation needed]

Trivia[]

  • In Season 8, Nya used it as her main weapon, except it is made differently than the one in the movie sets.
    • It is more like a sai attached to a stick, resembling a trident or a yari spear.
    • In sets, it has a tassel on the end, like Lloyd's sword.
  • It is referred to as a triton in the Netflix captions for "Big Trouble, Little Ninjago."
