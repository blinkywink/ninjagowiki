Overview

“The new tea shop, "Steeper Wisdom," seems to be doing even better than the first.”
— Wu, "Secret Teas"

Steeper Wisdom is a tea shop owned by Wu and operated by the ninja. It is the successor to Steep Wisdom.

History

History[]

Wu's Teas (non-canon)[]

At some point, Master Wu decided to build a successor to Steep Wisdom, called Steeper Wisdom. While it would contain normal teas, it would also contain special teas that can make one fly, grow larger, give extreme musicality, put one at peace, and more.

Before Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Lloyd had some fever-dreams about working for his uncle in the tea shop.[1]

After Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

The Bone Tournament[]

Lloyd's fever dream would actually come true, as Wu would actually organize a new tea shop sometime before the Merge.

One day, a new supplier sent Wu some new tastes of tea. However, after being tested by the ninja, they all were teleported to the Bone King's lair, finding out that it's all was a part of his plan to make revenge on the ninja.
