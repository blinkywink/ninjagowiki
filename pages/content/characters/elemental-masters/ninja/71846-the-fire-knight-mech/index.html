Overview

This article is about the set. For the actual mech, see Rusty.

71846 The Fire Knight Mech is a Ninjago: Legends - Monstrosity set that was released on June 1, 2025. It includes Kai, the Fisherman, and the Fire Knight Mech.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Older NINJAGO® fans aged 14 and up can add to their collection of LEGO® NINJAGO models with this stunning and highly detailed The Fire Knight Mech (71846) building kit. It features a dramatic scene of a cape-wearing, spear-holding mech locked in battle with an ancient monster emerging from the sea, and it will look great on display in a bedroom.

Ninja fans can also remove the model from its base to recreate stories from the NINJAGO Legends Monstrosity series. The mech has posable features and the sea monster has movable tentacles. The model also comes with 2 NINJAGO minifigures: Kai with a magical sword, who can be placed inside the mech’s chest, and a fisherman with a paddle in a boat.

The rest of the LEGO NINJAGO range of sets (sold separately) includes dragons, vehicles and temples for fans to role-play adventures with their heroes. Each ninja playset can be created with the LEGO Builder app, which guides you on an easy and intuitive building adventure. Set contains 996 pieces.

  • Premium gift for NINJAGO® fans – This model is a high-quality gift idea for fans of NINJAGO Legends Monstrosity who want to showcase their fandom

  • A universe of ninja toys – LEGO® NINJAGO® sets let kids escape into a fantasy world of adventures with their ninja heroes
  • Measurements – The mech in this 996-piece building set stands over 15.5 in. (40 cm) tall when posed on the display stand with its spear[1]
