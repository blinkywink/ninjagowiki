Overview

"I was bitten by a snake."
This article is about a subject that is relevant to both the main Ninjago storyline and The LEGO Ninjago Movie. Information may be more directed toward one continuity than the other.

The hammer is a weapon owned by Cole. It is a long stick with a metal head on top of it, allowing it to be strong and very reliable in the heat of a fight. The hammer can also be used in melee battles.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

One blow from Cole’s hammer can do serious damage to a Shark Army vehicle. Plus, Cole knows he looks cool when he’s carrying it.[citation needed]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Cole wields the hammer in the sets and promotional media for The LEGO Ninjago Movie, but he does not use one in the movie itself.
    • Because the TV series adopted elements from the film, the hammer is Cole's main weapon in Season 8: Sons of Garmadon.
  • It's stated in Chapter 2 of the novel, Way of the Departed, that the hammer was given to Cole by his father, Lou. However, the hammer is destroyed in Chapter 24, leaving the origins of his Season 8 hammer unknown.
  • Cole's hammer in Sons of Garmadon sets are identical to the Knockout Nemesis 3000, featured in 9591 Weapon Pack.
  • In the movie's sets and promotional media, the staff in which the hammer is attached to is brown, but in TV series and its sets, the staff is black.
