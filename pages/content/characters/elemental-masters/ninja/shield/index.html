Overview

A shield is a common item that is used to block attacks from enemies.

History

History[]

Rise of the Snakes[]

Home[]

Slithraa used an ice shield when battling Skales in the Hypnobrai Tomb Slither Pit.

All of Nothing[]

Chokun carried a shield during a Slither Pit battle with Mezmo.

Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

The Day Ninjago Stood Still[]

The Stone Army woke up holding their shields.

Island of Darkness[]

Multiple stone warriors wielded shields within Garmadon's encampment. Several warriors would also carry them during their pursuit of the ninja to the Temple of Light.

The Last Hope[]

Multiple Stone Warriors carried shields in Garmadon's encampment, particularly when pursuing the ninja atop of the scaffolding surrounding Garmatron. More shield bearing warriors would later accompany Kozuto the Celestial Clock.

Return of the Overlord[]

Stone Warriors carried shields with them to the coastline of the Dark Island as Garmadon prepared to upset the balance. Once the balance was destroyed, these Stone Warriors carried their shields with them as they crossed over into Ninjago.

Rise of the Spinjitzu Master[]

Emerging in Ninjago City, the Stone Army carried their shields as their forces marched into the city, ushering in The Final Battle. As the battle raged on, the Stone Army used their shields in combat against the ninja, but after their allegiance was changed to that of Dareth once he placed on the Helmet of Shadows, the Stone Army carried their shields as they charged up towards the Overlord instead.

Following the Overlord's defeat during the final battle, the Stone Army's shields were seemingly disintegrated alongside the near majority of the army.

Tournament of Elements[]

Spellbound (flashback)[]

Maya carried a shield while fighting the Anacondrai warriors.

Hunted[]

Iron & Stone[]

When Kai asks for a shield, a shield was thrown down but Kai never catches it.

Lessons for a Master[]

When Iron Baron and Wu made it to the Firstbourne's nest, Iron Baron stole the armor and tried to control the Firstbourne and use her to kill Wu. However, Wu told him that it was not the armor that controls her. She promptly buries him in molten metal. Wu then took possession of the Armor.

Green Destiny[]

When Garmadon launched a large destructive blast, Wu used the Dragon Shield to block the blast. However, as a result, Wu was flung out of Borg Tower and lost the shield.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Ancient History[]

When Lloyd and Nya confronted Aspheera, the Pyro Vipers were using shields and scimitars as weapons.

Vengeance is Mine![]

The Pyro Vipers used shields and scimitars to attack the ninja in the hangar bay.

A Cold Goodbye (dream)[]

In P.I.X.A.L.'s dream, the Pyro Vipers had shields.

Master of the Mountain[]

Queen of the Munce[]

During a duel between Nya and Queen Murtessa, Nya uses a shield in it. However, loses it early in the duel.

The Real Fall[]

When Princess Vania encounters a spider she grabs a shield and a naginata. She throws the naginata at the spider, however, it was ineffective to the spider. Vania throws her shield at it, but it was ineffective to the spider.

Grief-Bringer[]

When the Geckles and the Munce decided to have a truce, the ninja (except Cole) got their own shield.

The Darkest Hour[]

When multiple Awakened Warriors throw their spears at Kai and Lloyd, Kai uses his shield to block them, while Lloyd throws his shield to take out a few warriors. When Grief-Bringer fires a green blast, Kai, Jay, Zane, and Lloyd use all of their combined shields to block the blast. When they try to reach the Geckle Strong-Cave, they drop their shields down.

The Upply Strike Back![]

When preparing to face off against the Skull Sorcerer after acquiring the Blades of Deliverance, Cole picks up a golden shield similar to the ones used by Kai and Zane. During Cole's battle against both the Skull Sorcerer and Hazza D'ur, the floating skull blasts multiple beams at the ninja. However, the beams were all blocked by Cole's shield.

The Son of Lilly[]

When Grief-Bringer tried to blast Cole, Cole blocks it with his shield.

The Island[]

The Keepers of the Amulet[]

Many of the Keepers held shields and spears when they captured the ninja.

The Gift of Jay[]

The Keeper guards held their shields and spears as weapons to ward off intruders.

The Tooth of Wojira[]

The Keeper guards held their shields and spears as weapons to ward off intruders.

Season 14: Seabound[]

The Storm Amulet[]

Keeper guards carried their shields when the Ninja returned to the Island to secure the Storm Amulet

Season 15: Crystalized[]

Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5![]

A shield from Shintaro Mountain was in a convoy of trucks guarded by the New Ninja.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Sword Shatters[]

During his match against Zeatrix, Lloyd took possession of the shield on display and used it to block Zeatrix's Shockwave blasts. He would continue to use the shield for some time in the match, even using it offensively to knock Zeatrix away after tricking her into revealing her position when she was using the power of Figment. However, Lloyd was soon relieved of the shield by Zeatrix with a spear.

During the intermission of their match, the shield was recovered and placed back on its weapon rack, though neither Lloyd nor Zeatrix selected it again following the intermission.
