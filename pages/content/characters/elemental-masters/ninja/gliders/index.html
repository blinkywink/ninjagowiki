Overview

This article is about one of Jay's inventions. For other uses, see Gliders (disambiguation).
“I once built these wings from scrap metal and other—”
— Jay to Nya, "Once Bitten, Twice Shy"

Gliders,[1] also called mechanical wings,[2] are one of Jay's inventions used for gliding long distances without touching the ground, appearing as a set of sail-like wings made of sturdy cloth stretched between several wooden poles and mounted on a person's back. A set of cords on the supporting poles connect to small wooden handles in the user's hands, allowing them to move the wings in flight.

Ronin had his own glider when he chased Kai and Nya.

While operating as a bounty hunter named Rogue, Jay constructed a new prototype model that had retractable wings, with blasters, and featured anti-gravity tech that allows an user to hover over a certain place for a long time. He also built one in a exo-suit armor to allow him to fly. Arin stole one of Rogue's gliders, which he later showed to Sora, who made copies of it for the other ninja. The new gliders were destroyed when the Ninja attempted to stop the Forbidden Five in the Winds of Chaos.

History

History[]

The Golden Weapon/How to Build a Dragon (flashback)[]

When Master Wu met Jay, the latter was testing a set of gliders by gliding off of a building. Unfortunately, Jay ended up crashing into a billboard. After crashing into the billboard, Wu told him to "build the impossible."

King of Shadows[]

Jay brought some gliders with him after he moved to the Monastery of Spinjitzu. The ninja used them to escape the Skulkin attack at the Floating Ruins, after obtaining the Nunchucks of Lightning. The wings allowed them to glide to the Forest of Tranquility, where they set up camp for the night.

Once Bitten, Twice Shy[]

When Jay tries to show off himself to Nya in the Mega Monster Amusement Park, he tells her that he once built wings from scrap metal and other.

All of Nothing[]

When Lord Garmadon was preparing to leave the Destiny's Bounty, he had a final talk with his son, Lloyd. After failing to convince his father to stay with his friends, Lloyd went to a nearby closet to grab a pair of gliders, saying that Lord Garmadon might need them to get off of the flying ship. However, Lord Garmadon had already left before the Green Ninja could get an answer.

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Public Enemy Number One[]

Ronin used his own glider when he chased Kai and Nya in the Corridor of Elders.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

The Ultimate Object of Admiration[]

Jay, now operating as a bounty hunter, used a new glider to swoop down and grab an Oni chalice from a chest the cyclops Powerlid had.

Their Draconic Majesty's Request[]

Jay used his glider, which was attach to a mech to hunt Ras. He encountered Ras and Arin in the World Forest where they battled them in their Super Storm Jet. After Ras was force to eject, he used his hammer to knock the armor glider out of the air. When Leaf Blade took them to the crash site, Arin found the glider and wore it to escape the Mountain Dragons.

Crashing Together[]

When Jay captured Ras, Arin used his glider to fly after them. When Arin caught up they began to fight in the air where Arin's glider was damaged by a lightning dagger. Arin manage to free Ras but ended up fighting Jay. Sora activated Jay's glider allowing Arin to be spared.

Chaos Unchained[]

Arin gave Sora the prototype he had and Sora used it to create more gliders for the other Ninja. The Ninja used their gliders to fight the Forbidden Five and prevent them from using the final Prismatic Blades to free Thunderfang. Although the Five succeeded in claiming the final two blades and flew into the Winds of Chaos to the entrance of Thunderfang's prison and the Ninja flew after them, thinking they can take on the winds. However, the Ninja ended up loosing their gliders when they flew into the tornado, crashing into each other, which resulted in the destruction of their gliders.

The Shatter Dragon[]

Jay also arrived to kidnapped Ras but Arin intervened and damaged the glider.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The wings seem to be based on the Great Kite, made by Leonardo da Vinci.
  • While the glider in 70751 Temple of Airjitzu was based on the gliders from original LEGO Ninja sets, Michael Knap stated it can double as one of Jay's gliders.[3]
  • Jay built a total of six gliders, none of which originally worked.[4]
