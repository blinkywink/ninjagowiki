Overview

This article is about the set. For the mech, see Lloyd's Dragon Mech.

71862 Lloyd's Dragon Mech Battle Pack is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 4 set that was released in most countries on June 1, 2026 and August 1, 2026 in the US.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Recreate action-packed scenes from season 4 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show with Lloyd’s Dragon Mech Battle Pack (71862), an epic action figure playset for boys and girls ages 6 and up. This building set features a battle dragon mech with posable arms, hands, legs, feet, wings and tail and an opening cockpit with room for a minifigure and a sword accessory. Equip the 3 minifigures with their weapon accessories and role-play epic good vs. evil battle: Lloyd with his golden sword, Jay with a set of claws and a Loyalist with dual black swords. The LEGO® NINJAGO range of pretend playsets includes mechs, dragons and temples that let fans immerse themselves in a fantasy world full of adventure. And kids can build with confidence using the LEGO Builder app – zoom, rotate in 3D and track progress with easy-to-follow digital instructions. Set contains 81 pieces.

  • ACTION FIGURE PLAYSET – Play out battle scenes inspired by season 4 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show with Lloyd’s Dragon Mech Battle Pack (71862), a fun building toy for boys and girls
  • POSABLE MECH TOY – Kids can enjoy dynamic action-packed play with Lloyd’s dragon figure, which has movable arms, hands, legs, feet, wings and tail, plus a cockpit that opens to hold a minifigure
  • 3 NINJAGO® MINIFIGURES – Stage pretend battle scenes between the forces of good and evil with familiar characters Lloyd, Jay and a villainous Loyalist
  • BATTLE ACCESSORIES AND WEAPONS – Each character is armed with its own weapon for epic hero clashes: Lloyd has a golden sword, Jay has a set of claws and the Loyalist wields dual black swords
  • SMALL GIFT IDEA FOR KIDS – This dragon mech building set makes a fun birthday or holiday present for boys and girls ages 6 and up who love ninja and imaginative play
  • MORE LEGO® NINJAGO® TOYS – Discover other sets (sold separately) featuring detailed battle dragons, mechs, temples and vehicles inspired by the popular TV show
  • A UNIVERSE OF NINJA ADVENTURES – This extensive collection lets children escape into an immersive fantasy world for imaginative play alongside their heroes
  • DIMENSIONS – This 81-piece set features a posable dragon mech figure that stands over 3.5 in. (9 cm) high[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the cheapest way to get a Loyalist, Draconus Jay, and Draconus Form Lloyd.
    • However, the wings, tail, and shoulder armor for Draconus Form Lloyd are not present in this set.
  • This is the first of the mech battle pack sets to appear in the series, specifically as Lloyd's Dragon Mech.
