Overview

“We've found it to be a perfect cover. It allows us to go wherever we want in the city while keeping an eye on things.”
— Skylor to Lloyd, "Iron & Stone"

The garbage depot is the former base of the Resistance and formerly where garbage was taken to be processed.

History

History[]

Iron & Stone[]

The Resistance made it to the garbage depot as a hideout. After Skylor introduced the Elemental Masters, Mystake offered to explain to Lloyd where the stranded ninja went.

Radio Free Ninjago[]

In the depot, the Resistance watched as Lloyd led Dareth through a battle with Karlof. When Karlof won, Lloyd then suggests they make a plan.

How to Build a Dragon[]

The Resistance celebrated Lloyd's speech. They started planning on who else they could recruit to help them. When Dareth felt left out, he asked Mystake for tea that can give him power.

When Dareth went outside to relieve himself, he saw the depot ambushed by the Sons of Garmadon.

The Gilded Path[]

The garbage depot was ambushed by Sons of Garmadon. Nya, Lloyd, Skylor, and Dareth got away in the armored vehicle while the rest of the Resistance stayed behind to fight.
