Overview

This article is about the set. For the actual vehicle, see Mech Storm Rider.

71830 Kai's Mech Storm Rider is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 3 set that was released on January 1st, 2025. It includes Kai and Nya in their Storm suits, a Dragonian Warrior, two Prismatic Blades, Wyldfyre's mech, and the Mech Storm Rider.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Kai’s Mech Storm Rider (71830) combo ninja toy lets boys and girls aged 7 and up recreate exhilarating scenes from season 3 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show. Kids can play with the large action toy before they separate it into 2 smaller vehicles: Kai’s mech with posable head, arms and legs, a cockpit and a large storm blade accessory, and Nya’s flying motorbike toy with a cockpit, movable wings and 2 spring-loaded shooters.

This ninja vehicle playset comes with 3 NINJAGO minifigures: Kai armed with 2 crystal katana sword accessories, Nya armed with 2 golden katana sword accessories and a villainous Dragonian Warrior with its own sword accessory so kids can role-play epic battles.

The LEGO® NINJAGO range of sets and buildable ninja toys for kids includes dragons, vehicles and temples so fans can role-play adventures with their heroes. Each ninja playset can be created with the LEGO Builder app, which guides you and your child on an easy and intuitive building adventure.

  • Ninja toy for kids – Boys and girls aged 7 and up can play out amazing battles from season 3 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show with Kai’s Mech Storm Rider
  • Combo ninja vehicle toy – This large NINJAGO® vehicle can be separated into 2 smaller vehicles: Kai’s mech and Nya’s flying motorcycle so kids can enjoy even more ninja playtime
  • Packed with features – Kai’s mech has a posable head, arms and legs, a cockpit and a storm blade accessory, and Nya’s flying motorbike toy has a cockpit, movable wings and 2 spring-loaded shooters
  • 3 NINJAGO® minifigures – Playset comes with Kai, Nya and a Dragonian Warrior, all with weapon accessories so kids can play out epic battles
  • Birthday gift for kids – This action toy provides a fun build-and-play experience and is a birthday gift idea for kids who love teaming up with their ninja heroes
  • Bigger ninja adventures – Look out for more LEGO® NINJAGO® toys (sets sold separately) to play out action with the Kai’s Mech Storm Rider
  • A universe of ninja toys – LEGO® NINJAGO® sets let kids escape into a fantasy world of adventures with their ninja heroes
  • Measurements – This 333-piece LEGO® building set for kids includes Kai’s Mech Storm Rider figure, which stands over 5.5 in. (14 cm) tall
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This set is very similar to 71783 Kai's Mech Rider EVO, both of which contain Kai, his mech, and Nya, and the mech can be ridden on some sort of large vehicle.
  • This is the first set where there are two Prismatic Blades, the second set is 71832 Thunderfang Dragon of Chaos.
