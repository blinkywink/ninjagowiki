The ATV is a ninja vehicle owned by Kai.

History

History[]

Shortly after the end of the Oni's invasion, P.I.X.A.L. had been working on the hangar bay for six months. She also built the Land Bounty, Rock Cycle, and the ATV. During construction, the ninja didn't notice anything due to them being lazy.

A Rocky Start[]

The ninja were planning to go to the Desert of Doom on a quest and before they did, P.I.X.A.L. told them about the vehicles she built. After exploring the hangar bay, they took the Land Bounty as well the Rock Cycle and the ATV into the Desert of Doom. After some traveling, The ninja parked their vehicles in an oasis where they relaxed until they encountered Beohernie. They tried to escape him, but the beetle ingested a critical engine part that the Land Bounty could not run without.

The Belly of the Beast[]

Zane was eaten by Beohernie and was able to get the engine part back. After obtaining the component, the ninja placed the Rock Cycle and the ATV in the Land Bounty while using the Land Bounty to flee from the Beohernie.

Ninja vs Lava[]

After P.I.X.A.L. saved the ninja from the pyramid using the ShuriCopter, they used the Land Bounty, Rock Cycle, and the ATV to ride to Ninjago City and stop Aspheera.

Snaketastrophy[]

The ninja rides their vehicles including the ATV into Ninjago City and saved Fred Finely by defeating an Elemental Cobra which nearly devoured him.

Powerless[]

Along with the Land Bounty and the Rock Cycle, the ninja arrive with their vehicles to save the Police Commissioner and his crew when they were surrounded by Elemental Cobras.

The Meaning of Victory[]

While being interviewed, Anthony Brutinelli stands behind his ATV.

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

In order to chase The Mechanic in his noodle truck, Kai, Zane, Nya, Lloyd, and Jay ride the ATV while Cole used his Rock Cycle. During the chase, The Mechanic runs the ATV off track, but Cole is able to slow down The Mechanic by attaching his truck to a pole.

Racer Seven[]

The ATV was used in the Speedway Five-Billion by the Ballistic Missiles.

The Speedway Five-Billion[]

The ATV was once again used in the Speedway Five-Billion, but all of the Ballistic Missiles were wiped out of the competition.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the eighth vehicle owned by Kai.
    • This is also the second car owned by Kai, the first being the Prototype X-1.
  • Anthony Brutinelli and the other Ballistic Missiles have their own versions of the Katana 4x4 in Prime Empire.
  • In the show, the ATV's spinner blade can move to the right. However, in the sets, it can only go forward.
  • The ATV is called the "Katana 4x4" in the sets.
