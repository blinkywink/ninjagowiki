71867 X-1 Ninja Charger is a Ninjago: Legacy Wave 8 set that will release in most countries on June 1, 2026 and August 1, 2026 in the US.[1] It contains Kai in his Legacy ZX suit, Dusk, Cryptor, and the Prototype X-1.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Celebrate the 15th anniversary of the LEGO® NINJAGO® TV show with this incredible 2-in-1 X-1 Ninja Charger (71867), a remake of the original X-1 Ninja Charger (70727). This toy vehicle offers fans ages 14 and up endless dynamic play and display options. This building set for teen boys and girls comes with 3 NINJAGO minifigures: Kai, the villainous General Cryptor and, for the first time, the Elemental Master of Shadow on a stand with a celebration coin. Each character comes with a sword accessory, while Kai has 2 that can be attached to the car and motorcycle. Teens can recreate action-packed scenes from the TV show before placing the model on display in their room. Discover more fun gift ideas for ninja fans in the LEGO NINJAGO range, which includes mechs, dragons and temples. And kids can build with confidence using the LEGO Builder app – zoom, rotate in 3D and track progress with easy-to-follow digital instructions. Set contains 691 pieces. COLLECTIBLE BUILDING SET – Celebrate 15 years of the LEGO® NINJAGO® TV show with this X-1 Ninja Charger 15th Anniversary (71867) toy car for boys, girls and nostalgic fans

  • 2-IN-1 CAR – Lift the hood on this updated remake of the original X-1 Ninja Charger (70727) to launch a hidden motorcycle toy with moving wheels
  • 3 NINJAGO® MINIFIGURES – Role play adventures with Kai, the villainous General Cryptor and for the first time the Elemental Master of Shadow, who can be placed on a stand with a celebration coin
  • WEAPON ACCESSORIES – Give each minifigure a weapon for battle play: General Cryptor’s silver sword, the Master of Shadow’s short sword and Kai’s 2 golden swords that can be attached to the vehicles
  • GIFTS FOR TEEN BOYS AND GIRLS –This NINJAGO® model car kit provides a fun build and endless hours of imaginative play for fans
  • A FUN WAY TO BUILD – Let the LEGO® Builder app guide teens on an intuitive building adventure, where they can save sets, track progress and zoom in and rotate models in 3D while they build
  • LEGO® NINJAGO® BUILDING SETS – This extensive collection lets ninja fans and children escape into an immersive fantasy world for imaginative play with their heroes
  • DIMENSIONS – This 691-piece set includes a toy car measuring over 3 in. (8 cm) high, 9.5 in. (25 cm) long and 4.5 in. (12 cm) wide[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Dusk and Legacy Cryptor are exclusive to this set.
  • This is the second remake of the Prototype X-1, first being the 71737 X-1 Ninja Charger.
  • The preliminary image of the set featured Storm Kai, a Legacy Wave 3 Nindroid Warrior without armor, and Dusk with the hair from the LEGO Wednesday Addams minifigure.
