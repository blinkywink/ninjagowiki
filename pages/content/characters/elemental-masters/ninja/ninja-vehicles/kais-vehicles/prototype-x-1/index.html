Overview

For the original set, see 70727 X-1 Ninja Charger. For the first Legacy set, see 71737 X-1 Ninja Charger. For the second Legacy set, see 71867 X-1 Ninja Charger.
“Yet this car is worth far more than you. I would not advise returning it with a scratch.”
— P.I.X.A.L. to Kai, "Codename: Arcturus"

The Prototype X-1 is a large red roadster that served as an early model for the X-1 Ninja Charger. It was used by Kai during the Nindroid crisis. Years later, Wyldfyre borrowed it from Kai for use in the Mech-Master 10,000, after which she continued to use during various ninja missions.

History

History[]

Rebooted[]

Codename: Arcturus[]

Kai followed the Nindroids on the Prototype X-1, however, due to his reckless behavior, P.I.X.A.L. took control. Later, when Kai accidentally ignited a gas truck, P.I.X.A.L. tried to save him using the Prototype X-1, but then Min-Droid shot the engine.

Possession[]

Kingdom Come[]

The Prototype X-1 eventually found its way into Dareth's hands, though the Brown Ninja crashed it into a cement truck.

Ninjago: Reimagined[]

Gold Rush[]

Kai and Nya rode the Prototype X-1 to Borg Tower to get the Idol of Unity to a beacon on the tower in order to defeat the Shadow Banshees. After defeating the Banshees, they departed in the X-1.

Vehicle components[]

Interceptor bike[]

The interceptor bike[1] is a small two-wheeled bike that serves as the cockpit for the Prototype X-1.

Functions

Functions[]

The Prototype X-1 has boosters, allowing for faster travel, and contains a motorbike. Additionally, it can be controlled by P.I.X.A.L. and has a radio for communication.

Appearance

Appearance[]

The Prototype X-1 is a red car with fire decals.

LEGO.com descriptions

LEGO.com descriptions[]

2014[]

Kai's X-1 Ninja Charger is an awesome 2-in-1 vehicle! Set off in hot pursuit of the Nindroids who have stolen Kai's Techno-Blade. Aim the flick missiles at the Nindroid disc flyer and steer clear of its saw blades. Rotate the engine bay and fire the dual shooter to target the jet-packing Nindroid hovering above. Open up the hood, activate the built-in interceptor bike and accelerate to retrieve that valuable Techno-Blade! Includes 3 minifigures with weapons: Kai and 2 Nindroids.[2]

Legacy (2021)[]

Drive into battle in this awesome X-1 Charger car to take on the Nindroid Warriors! Fire Kai into the action riding on his super-fast motorbike from inside the car! Watch out as the Nindroid Warriors use a backpack jet to fly into attack mode! You must save NINJAGO® City and then display the limited-edition golden Cole Legacy minifigure to celebrate 10 years of LEGO® NINJAGO sets.[3]

Legacy (2026)[]

Celebrate the 15th anniversary of the LEGO® NINJAGO® TV show with an upgraded X-1 Ninja Charger. This 2 in 1 vehicle is a remake of the original X-1 Ninja Charger (70727) and lets fans lift the car’s hood to fire out a motorcycle with moving wheels! The set also comes with 3 minifigures, Kai, General Cryptor and a collectible Elemental Master of Shadow on a stand with a celebration coin.[4]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The finalized version of the vehicle has never been shown, as the Prototype X-1 is merely an earlier prototype of the X-1 Ninja Charger rather than the finished version, hence its name.
    • It's unknown whether the version featured in Dragons Rising is intended to be the finalized version of the vehicle or the same prototype previously seen in Rebooted.
  • In "Gold Rush," it has two seats and one of the seats can convert into the bike.
  • The Prototype X-1 is the only vehicle to have more appearances in set form than in any canon media.
  • The Prototype X-1 is the first and only Ninjago vehicle to have two full Legacy sets made, not including polybags or 4+ sets.
