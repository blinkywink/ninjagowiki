The Golden Dragon Raider was a ninja vehicle that was used by Kai.

History

History[]

Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5![]

P.I.X.A.L. designed the Golden Dragon Raider and other ninja vehicles to assist the ninja in intercepting a cargo truck transporting Aspheera's staff. The Golden Dragon Raider was initially red but was later changed to black by Kai to improve its night camouflage.

Return to Primeval's Eye[]

Kai stole back the Raider from the Ninjago Police impound lot and drove off to establish a perimeter around Primeval's Eye.

Crystastrophe[]

Kai used his Raider in the rice fields to fight off the Vengestone Guards.

A Lesson in Anger[]

After receiving upgrades from P.I.X.A.L., Kai drove the Raider towards Ninjago City to combat the Crystal army.

Brave But Foolish[]

Kai used the power boost that P.I.X.A.L. upgraded to destroy the Vengestone Guards with the raider. However, a Dragonide picked up the Golden Dragon Raider and dropped it on top of the Dragon Cruiser, making the two vehicles crash.

Safe Haven[]

Kai ejected out of the inoperative Golden Dragon Raider and escaped with Cole in tow.
