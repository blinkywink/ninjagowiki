Overview

“As I approached the structure, my vehicle lost power.”

The Golden Dragon Jet is a ninja vehicle used by Zane. Following the Merge, the jet eventually came under the ownership of Arin and Sora, who chose to display it atop their penthouse.

History

History[]

Crystalized[]

Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5![]

Zane used the Golden Dragon Jet during the heist to retrieve Aspheera's staff.

Return to Primeval's Eye[]

The ninja broke into the police impound lot to reclaim back their vehicles. As Zane was about to go to his jet, he is caught by Joey and an Old Guard, but Wu locks them inside.

Later, the ninja and Wu travelled to Primeval's Eye to save Lloyd. When the Council of the Crystal King attacked Wu, Nya and Lloyd on the Destiny's Bounty, Zane used the Golden Dragon Jet to fight off the villains.

Crystastrophe[]

In a rice village, the ninja used their vehicles to fight the Crystal Warriors. Zane used his jet to attack some Dragonides airborne and proceeds to strike at the Crystal Island, though its influencing power causes the jet to malfunction.

A Lesson in Anger[]

When P.I.X.A.L. studied a sample of an infectious crystal, she was able to provide a shield for the ninja's vehicles.

Brave But Foolish[]

Using the Golden Dragon Jet, Zane initiated an assault on Crystal Island by deploying a series of precision missiles. Unfortunately, during an evasion maneuver to avoid the Dragonides, one of the crystal beasts latched onto the jet, resulting in the severe tearing of one of its wings. The subsequent impact caused significant damage, leading to an uncontrollable descent and the eventual failure of the jet's control systems due to a short-circuit. The jet ultimately crashed onto a nearby highway, resulting in a collision with numerous vehicles and triggering a catastrophic explosion.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

The wingless remains of the Golden Dragon Jet underwent repairs and was subsequently positioned atop a penthouse belonging to Arin and Sora at the Crossroads.
