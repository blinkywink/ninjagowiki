Overview

Not to be confused with the species or the character.

71719 Zane's Mino Creature is a LEGO Ninjago - Master of the Mountain set that was released in most countries on June 1, 2020, in most US stores on August 15, 2020, and in Canada and LEGO stores in the US on August 24, 2020. It includes Hero Zane, Hero Cole, Gleck, Ginkle, the Ivory Blade of Deliverance, Zane's Mino, and a small section of the Dungeons of Shintaro. A dice spinner and a health counter for the board game are also included.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This set, along with the Journey to the Skull Dungeons, Skull Sorcerer's Dragon, and Skull Sorcerer's Dungeons sets can be connected to form a board game.
  • This set is the cheapest way to get Hero Zane, Hero Cole, and Ginkle.
  • When Zane briefly rode on this creature during the show, the beast did not have the shoulder armor as Zane took it off.
  • Though the species first appears in "Into the Dark," this set is first seen in "Trial By Mino."
  • Hero Zane and Cole do not have the golden clip on their backs that allows the shield to be attached to the back.
  • Unlike its counterpart in the show, Vengestone is blue in this set.
