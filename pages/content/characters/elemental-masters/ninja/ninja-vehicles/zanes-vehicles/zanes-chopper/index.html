Overview

“On the downlow, Zane's chopper is not as fast as Lloyd's new wings.”
— Arin to Nya and Sora, "Birthright"

The chopper is a vehicle used by Zane.

History

History[]

A Wonder of the Merged Lands[]

Zane arrives in the Whispering Wood on his chopper, surprising Lloyd, Arin, and Euphrasia, who were fighting Elemental Monsters.

Birthright[]

During the Loyalists assault on Imperium, Zane, Arin and Euphrasia arrive on the chopper and join the battle. Following the battle, Zane and Lloyd used the chopper to return to the Whispering Wood, where Lloyd and the Arc Dragon of Life talked before presumably taking the chopper back to the Monastery of Spinjitzu.
