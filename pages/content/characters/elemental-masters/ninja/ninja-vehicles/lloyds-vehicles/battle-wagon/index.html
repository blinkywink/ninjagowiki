Overview

“There's only one thing that can withstand that. I ditched the Battle Wagon across town. If we can get to it... we can leave the city.”
— Nya to Lloyd and Dareth, "Lessons for a Master"

The Battle Wagon is a tank-like vehicle that was built by Nya and Skylor to be used by the Resistance.

History

History[]

Creation[]

Nya and Skylor began building the battle wagon with extra parts outside of the garbage depot.

The Gilded Path[]

Lloyd, Nya, Skylor, and Dareth use the battle wagon to escape the Sons of Garmadon's attack. They get attacked by the Colossus and attempt to make it fall.

Lessons for a Master[]

Nya had ditched the battle wagon across the city. The Resistance later try to find it. On the way, Nya gets into a fight with Ultra Violet. Soon, Lloyd and Dareth get the Battle Wagon and use it to send Ultra Violet into a wall, destroying the Oni Mask of Hatred.

Green Destiny[]

The Sons of Garmadon arrive and the Resistance is fighting them. The dragons and the ninja return to Ninjago and help the Resistance near the battle wagon. Later, while the ninja and the dragons try to destroy the Colossus, Nya comes in with the battle wagon and helps.
