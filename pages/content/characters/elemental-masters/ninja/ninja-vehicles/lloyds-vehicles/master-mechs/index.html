The Master Mechs[1] are exo-suits that belong to the Administration Agents, one of which was stolen by Lloyd.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Administration[]

After landing in the Department of Demolitions, Lloyd spots a Nindroid Warrior Administration Agent in the mech, about to put the Pink Dragon Core in an incinerator to destroy it. Before it could happen, Lloyd kicked the agent out and reclaimed the core, but was soon ambushed by more agents, until Zane arrived in a car he had stolen before they flee the level.

As they made their way through the Administration, a Munce agent tried to stop Lloyd, only for Arin to throw him aside. When laser grids were set up, Arin discovers the mech has a smaller one attached to it, which he was sitting in, and detaches it to get through and destroy the grids, eventually escaping through a portal to the Mother Garden. There the ninja loaded the mech into the Destiny's Bounty and returned to the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

The Power Within[]

During the Liberation of Imperium, Lloyd remotely summoned his Master Mech to the Kingdom of Imperium alongside Arin's Dragon Mech, via remote autopilot. He used the mech, and its spear, to combat Beatrix and her Empress Mech. The Master Mech would be lost however, when Beatrix used her MergeQuake weapon to open a rift beside Lloyd, forcing him to abandon the mech as it was sucked into the Ethereal Divide.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Fugitives From Madness[]

Three Master Mechs were used by a group of Administration Agents led by Roderick Allen and SsssSsss Underwood as they arrived outside the gates of the Monastery of Mysterium in an effort to recapture Zane. After Zane's attempts at stalling the agents' progress, Roderick and two other agents hopped into the Master Mechs to break into the Monastery of Mysterium.

Secrets of the Wyldness[]

Piloting the Master Mechs, the Administration Agents faced off against Cole, who piloted his Elemental Earth Mech, only to be quickly overpowered by Cole. Cole retaliated by using his Earth Punch to send Roderick's mech flying backwards, while Zane took control of another Master Mech and planned to set all the Administration Agents' mechs to self-destruct. Roderick, however, used the spear from his Master Mech to deactivate Zane's, effectively neutralizing Zane. Before Roderick could deliver the final blow, Cole intervened and knocked him back, leading to Gandalaria reactivating Zane, who then ejected all the Administration Agents out of their mechs.

The Shape of Motion[]

A Master Mech was utilized by a Munce agent inside the Department of Demolitions, being utilized to load various items onto the incinerator's conveyor belt.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

Tonight We Hunt Monsters[]

Two Master Mech got the Transforming Cars left from Rapton and the Freebooters and put it from the garage while Jay, Kai, and Nya were still under the vehicle and success to enter the Administration.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The mech bears a striking resemblance to Keetongu from Bionicle.
    • It is also subtly similar to the Legacy 1 redesign of the Golden Mech.
  • An inscription on one of the panels reads "Ronin" in Ninjargon, which is a Japanese therm (rōnin) refering to a samurai who has no master to serve.
    • The mech have a design reminiscent of rōnin, with their flat, straw-like hats, their geta (Japanese sandals), and their naginata.
  • The mech's design was updated for season 4, with the colorscheme instead representing the Administration rather than Lloyd.
    • The Ninjargon inscription on the panels was also changed to "Admin".
