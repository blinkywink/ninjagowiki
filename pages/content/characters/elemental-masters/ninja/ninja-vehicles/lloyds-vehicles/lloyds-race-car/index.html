Overview

This article is about the actual car. For the set, see 71763 Lloyd's Race Car EVO. For other uses, see Race Car.

The Race Car is a ninja vehicle used by Lloyd.

History

History[]

Wisdom[]

Lloyd used his car to attack the Whack Rats blocking the ninja's path to the finish line. Overwhelmed by the number of Whack Rats, Lloyd purposely got shot by one of the Whack Rats and respawned near the finish line.

Courage[]

Lloyd used his car once again at the Island of the Keepers to fight the dragons that kidnapped Wu.

Tentacles Below![]

Days after the Great Flood ended, Lloyd used his Race Car to drive to the Monastery of Spinjitzu and tell Wu and Misako that everything in Ninjago City is going well.

Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5![]

Lloyd used the Race Car to get to the truck that was holding Aspheera's staff, so they can steal it and get rid of Nya's elemental powers. Once they get to the truck, the ninja abandoned their vehicles and set them to autopilot.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Help Lloyd build a bigger, stronger and faster ninja vehicle to take on the evil snake warriors. Woah – Cobra Mechanic arrives on a quad bike with a flamethrower, and Python Dynamite has a gun and a stick of dynamite! You can build Lloyd’s vehicle in 3 stages, from a buggy to an armored car ready for battle. For completing your mission, you will be rewarded with a collectible Adaptability banner.[1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Lloyd's Race Car bears a striking resemblance to the Ninja Nightcrawler from Sons of Garmadon.
  • In Wisdom, Lloyd is shown inside his racecar evo while wearing the Digi Suit's armor. In real life, minifigures wearing Digi armor cannot fit in the vehicle's set form unless the windshield was removed.
  • Lloyd's Race Car is the first Ninjago: Core set vehicle to be seen in the show, excluding the Fire Dragon and Thunder Dragon.
  • This is the only Ninja vehicle from Crystalized not to get a shielding, most likely due to the fact that it was not retrieved by the Ninja in the Police impound due to Lloyd not being present at the time.
    • However, in set form, the car resembles the golden dragon vehicles in their shielded forms.
