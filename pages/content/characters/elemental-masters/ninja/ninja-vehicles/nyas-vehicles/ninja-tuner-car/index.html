The Ninja Tuner Car (or Tuner Car[1] for short) is a vehicle driven by Scott in Prime Empire. It was used by Nya to compete in the Speedway Five-Billion.

History

History[]

The Stowaway[]

Running from Red Visors, Jay hid in the car's trunk. Scott caught the attention of the Red Visors and fled with the car. The Red Visors got in pursuit of the car, but even though they crashed, Jay and Scott were able to escape. The car was then parked in an alley while Jay and Scott introduced themselves.

Racer Seven[]

Nya worked on repairing Scott's car after the engine of the vehicle she was going to use broke down.

The Speedway Five-Billion[]

Nya and the rest of the ninja head out of Scott's garage after being invaded by the Red Visors. The Fast Chickens enter the race with Nya using Scott's car as her vehicle. With the Ninja Tuner Car, Nya was one of the three ninja left standing and helped Racer Seven win the race.

Wisdom[]

Nya used the Ninja Tuner Car to attack the Whack Rats blocking the ninja's path to the finish line. After Lloyd purposely gets shot by one of the Whack Rats and respawned near the finish line, Nya purposely gave herself up by allowing the car to be shot.

Courage[]

Nya was in her Turner Car on the Island of the Keepers and used it to defeat one of the dragons.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • It is unknown why the car is called "Ninja Tuner Car", as it was originally Scott's.
  • The vehicle resembles the DeLorean from the Back to the Future franchise, in that they are both vehicles that can turn the wheels sideways to become jets.
  • Despite using the car in the show, Nya is not included in the set.
