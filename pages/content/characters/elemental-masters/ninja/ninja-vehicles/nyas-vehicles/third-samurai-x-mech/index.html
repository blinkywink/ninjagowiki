The Samurai X Mech is a large exo-suit made for Nya. It was inspired by the original Samurai Mech.

History

History[]

Kryptarium Prison Blues[]

After being reminded about her time using the Samurai X mantle, Nya soon grabbed the blueprints to the original Samurai Mech and asked P.I.X.A.L. if she could build a new one with various improvements she added.

Hounddog McBrag[]

Nya, P.I.X.A.L. and Dareth planned a prison escape for the ninja by distracting the prison bus drivers and using the mech to open the bus' back door and flipping the bus shortly before she flew-off.

The Fall of the Monastery[]

Nya used her mech to escape from the burning Monastery of Spinjitzu after it was destroyed by the Crystal Spiders but the mech itself was badly damaged by the flames, though operable temporarily.

Darkness Within[]

Nya used her damaged mech to save Kai, Zane, Cole, and Jay from the collapsing rubble. She then uses it to fly off to the old Samurai X Cave. The mech was then powered down as a result of the damage.

Quest for the Lost Powers[]

At some point after the defeat of the Overlord, the Samurai X Mech was rebuilt and began to be used by Nya again.

Other appearances

Other appearances[]

Ninjago - Dream Team (dream; non-canon)[]

A dream version of this mech and Nya were manifested by Jan after he and his friends fell asleep while watching a Ninjago marathon. Dream Nya used her mech to respond to Jan's call for help and fought against the horde of Nightmare creatures, particularly the towering Grimkeepers. Unfortunately, Dream Nya was forced to abandon the mech when it was corrupted by the Grimspawn.
