The Samurai VXL is a vehicle used by the new Samurai X. It was given to her after Nya finally accepted that there was a new samurai.

History

History[]

The Hands of Time[]

Secrets Discovered[]

Nya shows the new Samurai X her Samurai VXL.

Samurai X Rising[]

At some point after receiving it, and following Nya's suggestion, Samurai X used the color changer in the Samurai X Cave to change both the suit and the vehicle from red to blue.

Out of the Fire and Into the Boiling Sea[]

When the ninja were cornered, Samurai X came by with the vehicle. They then put Cyrus in the Samurai VXL and drove him to the Iron Doom's apparatus.

Sons of Garmadon[]

The Jade Princess[]

Samurai X used this to scare some Sons of Garmadon away and drove away after the threat had dissipated.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This vehicle is very similar to the Proto Sam-X, as both are very similarly built vehicles that originally belonged to Nya as Samurai X, but were never used on screen by her, because the Samurai VXL was used only by P.I.X.A.L. and Proto-Sam-X was used only by Lloyd and Garmadon.
  • It is one of the few vehicles that was used in the following season after its debut.
