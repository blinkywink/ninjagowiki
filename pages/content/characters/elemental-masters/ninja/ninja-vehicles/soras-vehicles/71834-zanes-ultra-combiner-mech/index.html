Overview

This article is about the set. For the actual mech, see Ultra Combiner Mech.

71834 Zane's Ultra Combiner Mech is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 3 set that was released on January 1st, 2025. The set includes Storm Cole, P.I.X.A.L., Nokt, a Dragonian Warrior, a Dragonian Scout, Dragon Spinjitzu Sora, Riyu, a dragon critter, a Prismatic Blade, and Ultra Combiner Mech Zane.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Boys and girls aged 9 and up can enjoy endless play possibilities recreating exciting action from season 3 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show with Zane’s Ultra Combiner Mech (71834) toy 4-in-1 building set.

Kids can play with the stunning large combiner mech, which has posable legs, feet, arms, wrists, fingers and head, dragon wings, plus a cockpit and 2 shooters. They can then separate it into 4 smaller models: a dragon toy with posable features and wings; a jet toy with movable engines and a rotatable cockpit; a car toy with 4 large off-road wheels and 2 opening cockpits, including one with hidden blasters; and a Zane action figure.

This ninja playset comes with 6 minifigures so kids can enjoy countless hours of pretend play battles. Ninjas Sora and Cole and their ally Pixal are armed with cool ninja weapon accessories to take on the villainous trio of Nokt, a Dragonian Warrior and a Dragonian Scout, who each have their own weapon accessories.

  • 4-in-1 mech toy for kids – Boys and girls aged 9 years old and up can recreate action from season 3 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show with Zane’s Ultra Combiner Mech
  • Posable NINJAGO® toy – The large combiner mech has posable legs, feet, arms, wrists, fingers and head, plus dragon wings, a cockpit, 2 shooters and a large sword accessory
  • Detachable ninja models – Zane’s Ultra Combiner Mech can be separated into 4 smaller models: a jet toy, a car toy, an action figure and a dragon toy
  • 6 LEGO® NINJAGO® minifigures – This adventure playset includes ninjas Sora and Cole and their ally Pixal, who take on the villainous trio of Nokt, a Dragonian Warrior and a Dragonian Scout
  • Birthday gift idea for kids – This adventure playset provides a fun build-and-play experience and is a great gift idea for kids who love imaginative play with their ninja heroes
  • Bigger ninja adventures – Look out for more NINJAGO® toys (sets sold separately) featuring temples, mechs and vehicles
  • A universe ofninja toys – LEGO® NINJAGO® building kits let kids escape into a fantasy world of adventures with their ninja heroes
  • Measurements – The mech in this 1,187-piece LEGO® building set for kids stands over 14 in. (35 cm) tall with its wings raised[2]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the first Dragons Rising set to include P.I.X.A.L.
  • This is the second set containing brick-built Riyu since 71810 Young Dragon Riyu.
  • The included Zane is a brick-built figure, more precisely his mech form.
  • Lloyd was originally going to be part of the combiner mech, it’s unknown if he would’ve replaced P.I.X.A.L. or Sora’s vehicles or be added in addition to them. [3]
  • This set was first sketched by Niek van Slagmaat, then Chi Wing Lee designed it.[1]
