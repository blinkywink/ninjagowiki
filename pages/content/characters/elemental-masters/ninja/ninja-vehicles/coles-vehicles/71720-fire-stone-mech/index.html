Overview

This article is about the set. For the actual mech, see Stone Mech.

71720 Fire Stone Mech is a LEGO Ninjago - Master of the Mountain set that was released in most countries on June 1, 2020, in most US stores on August 15, 2020, and in Canada and LEGO stores in the US on August 24, 2020. It includes Kai, Cole, and Nya in their Hero suits, Murt, Moe, the Stone Mech, and a small rocky pedestal that holds the Shadow Blade of Deliverance.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Moe is exclusive to this set. However, he is just Murt without the armor.
  • This is the cheapest way to get the Hero Kai minifigure.
  • This physical model of the Mech is made out of combined elements of Earth and Fire since Kai and Cole are both depicted piloting it but in the series, the Mech is fully made of stone and can only be controlled by an Elemental Master of Earth.
    • The color scheme of the Mech resembles the destroyed form of the Mech, which was half covered in magma.
  • This set was first seen in "Masters Never Quit."
  • This is the only 2020 set to include Nya but not include Jay.
