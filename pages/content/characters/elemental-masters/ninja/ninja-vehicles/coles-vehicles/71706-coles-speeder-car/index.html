Overview

71706 Cole's Speeder Car is a 4+ LEGO Ninjago - Prime Empire set that was released on January 2, 2020. It includes Digi Cole, Hausner, the Yellow Key-Tana, a Speeder Car, and a small hoverboard. It is intended for younger fans four years old and up.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the cheapest way to get Cole and Hausner, although they are both missing their armor pads.
  • This is the only set in the Season 12 wave to not include any armor pads.
  • This is the only 4+ set in the Season 12 wave.
  • The car is loosely based on the Speeder Cars used by the Whack Rats, with the large rear tires, bulky back and one-seater cockpit. However, it does not include a roll cage, and the color scheme of this set is bright orange, unlike the show's red and gray.
