Overview

Speeder Cars are vehicles driven by Whack Rats in the Speedway Five-Billion within Prime Empire. One of them was taken and used by Cole.

History

History[]

Prime Empire Original Shorts[]

The Meaning of Victory[]

While Ritchie is being interviewed, he stands behind a Speeder Car.

Prime Empire[]

One Step Forward, Two Steps Back[]

Mrs. Dyer holds up a miniature version of a Speeder Car.

Back in Prime Empire, one of the Whack Rats drives their Speeder Car down a street while another Whack Rat is making repairs on it.

Racer Seven[]

The Whack Rats use their Speeder Cars in the Speedway Five-Billion and knock Racer Seven out of the competition.

The Speedway Five-Billion[]

During the race, Cole steals a Whack Rat's Speeder Car and ends up sacrificing himself to allow Lloyd, Jay, and Nya a chance to win the race.

The Whack Rats once again use their Speeder Cars for another Speedway Five-Billion race. However, it doesn't end well for the Whack Rats as they end up destroying their vehicles by the time they reach the finish line.

Stop, Drop and Side Scroll[]

A Speeder Car can be seen on instant replay.

Game Over[]

During the attack by Unagami, Ritchie uses his Speeder Car until it was stolen by Zane, who uses it to drive him, P.I.X.A.L., Wu, and Milton Dyer to Borg Tower.

The Virtues of Spinjitzu[]

Wisdom[]

The Whack Rats used their Speeder Cars to prevent the ninja from crossing the finish line.
