The Elemental Earth Mech is an elemental mech that was once used by Lilly.[1] It was on display next to the Temple City Museum until Cole used it to battle the Forbidden Five several decades later.

History

History[]

Inside the Maze[]

After coming across each other in the market, Geo brought Cole outside the museum, where the mech was displayed. A sign stated it was once used by a former Master of Earth, and Cole believed it was Lilly, his mother.

The Sword Shatters[]

Arin walked past the mech as he was going into the museum.

Clues and Suspects[]

Cole stole the mech in order to help make an Earth bridge to the Monastery of Gates for him and Arin to cross.

The Final Game[]

Cole piloted the mech as he and Arin explored the monastery and talked to Master Wu.

Elements of Betrayal[]

Cole tried to pull the Crowns of Horns off the ceiling of the monastery with the Mech but failed. The gate in the middle of the Monastery shook and Cole used the Mech to punch it. Doing so broke the wall in front of the gate and Nya and Riyu ran out from behind it. Cole then used the mech to fight Nokt and Rox, who possessed Jordana. Later, Rox used a spell to freeze the mech, along with Nya and Zane, in place. After Rox's soul left Jordana's body, the latter unfroze the three ninja and the mech. Cole tried to make a new bridge with the mech but it did not have any more elemental energy in it. The ninja used part of the Combo Vehicle to fly the mech over to the monastery where Cole used it to fight Nokt, but Nokt used Reflex to dodge it's sword and Earth to knock Cole's mech back. Cole attempted to use the mech's sword to attack Nokt but he used Wind and Heat to push the mech into a pillar where it got destroyed when Nokt unleashed Shatterspin while Cole attempted to save Zane.
