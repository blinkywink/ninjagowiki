The Dragon Cruiser is a ninja vehicle used by Cole.

History

History[]

Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5![]

Cole used his Dragon Cruiser to get to a truck that was holding Aspheera's staff, so they can steal it to get rid of Nya's elemental powers. Once they got to the truck, the ninja abandoned their vehicles and set them to autopilot.

Return to Primeval's Eye[]

The Dragon Cruiser was placed in the police impound lot along with the other ninja vehicles, but the ninja manage to break into the lot and get their vehicles back.

Brave But Foolish[]

By driving the Dragon Cruiser, Cole used the power boost that P.I.X.A.L. upgraded to destroy the Vengestone Guards. However, a Dragonide picked up the Golden Dragon Raider and dropped it on top of the Dragon Cruiser, making the two vehicles crash into a nearby building.

Zane's PIXAL Project[]

Cole drove the Dragon Cruiser to Kreel's Junkyard after the junkyard's owners visited the Monastery of Spinjitzu to ask for help with a problem of theirs'.
