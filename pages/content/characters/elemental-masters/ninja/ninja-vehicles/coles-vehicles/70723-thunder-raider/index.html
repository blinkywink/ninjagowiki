Overview

This article is about the set. For the vehicles, see Lightning off-roader and Earth Mech. For the Legacy remake, see 71699 Thunder Raider.

70723 Thunder Raider is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - Rebooted set released on December 1, 2013. It includes Techno Cole and Techno Jay, a Nindroid Warrior, a double laser cannon, the Earth Mech and Lightning off-roader, which can combine into the Thunder Raider.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Take on the Nindroids with the awesome Thunder Raider!

Team up Cole and Jay against the Nindroids with Thunder Raider, a versatile Ninja vehicle full of hidden surprises. Attach Cole’s Earth Mech to the back of Jay’s lightning off-roader. Engage speed mode and power effortlessly over rough terrain with the high-grip front tank treads and huge rear wheels. When the Nindroid attacks with its double laser cannon, detach Cole’s Earth Mech and fight back using the golden sword blasters. Transform the awesome Thunder Raider into attack mode to shoot hidden missiles and reload until the Ninjas are victorious! Includes 3 minifigures with weapons: Cole, Jay and a Nindroid.

  • Includes 3 minifigures with weapons and: Cole, Jay and a Nindroid
  • Huge rear off-road wheels, attachment hub for Earth Mech, gold Ninja swords and lightning motif
  • Earth Mech features golden sword blasters, cockpit, mech armor and poseable elements
  • Weapons include exclusive translucent yellow Techno-Blade for Jay and double laser cannon for the Nindroid
  • Transform into attack mode and fire the hidden missiles
  • Match up Cole in his Earth Mech against the Nindroid
  • Backflip onto the back of the Thunder Raider with Cole’s Earth Mech
  • Revert to speed mode to power through the city
  • Thunder Raider measures over 7” (18cm) high, 7” (19cm) long) and 5” (13cm) wide
  • Earth Mech measures over 4” (12cm) high, 4” (11cm) long) and 6” (16cm) wide[1]
Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

One of my latest sets and definitely my favorite so far. The size is perfect for play, you get three models that provide a lot of play, you have the modularity, the diversity between models and even a fun function that doesn't need a lot of technic. The function on the thunder raider works with the help of the new "A-plate" which I helped design. I have been unable to leave this element out of any models I've been designing since. It opens up a lot of posibilities for someone like me who loves to challenge the straight lines that LEGO bricks are usually restricted to.[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the first set to include Jay's Techno Blade.
  • This set was remade into 71699 Thunder Raider for Ninjago: Legacy.
  • This was the only set to include Techno Jay without shoulder pads.
  • This set can be combined with a LEGO City set (60056 Tow Truck) to build a larger build, this being the Hydro-Turbo Sky Ray.
