Overview

This article is about the set. For the actual vehicle, see Tread Assault.

9444 Cole's Tread Assault is a LEGO Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - Rise of the Snakes set that was released on November 29, 2011. It includes Cole ZX, Skales, the Tread Assault, the Hypnobrai Staff, a Staff of Light, a Hypno Viper, and two golden katanas.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Capture the Hypnobrai snake staff and zoom away in the Tread Assault vehicle!

The general of the Hypnobrai snake tribe, Skales, is using mind control to stop Cole from taking the golden Hypnobrai staff with its powerful anti-venom. Counterattack his hypnotic powers with Cole’s Tread Assault vehicle! Launch the rock shooter and ninja blades! Flip the Tread Assault vehicle over for a surprise attack! Stop Skales and his mind-controlling minions by grabbing that staff! Includes Cole ZX and Skales minifigures, Tread Assault vehicle, golden Hypnobrai staff, golden scythe and mini-snake.

  • Includes 2 minifigures: Cole ZX and Skales
  • Features rock shooter, ninja blades, 2 ninja spinner crowns in rear wheels, flip function and opening cockpit
  • Accessories include golden Hypnobrai staff, 2 golden ninja swords and mini-snake
  • Flip it over for surprise attacks!
  • Blast out of any battle with the rock shooter!
  • Shoot the ninja blades!
  • Cole’s Tread Assault vehicle measures over 11” (28 cm) long and 6” (16 cm) wide[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the only Elemental vehicle that wasn't remade in the Ninjago: Legacy line.
    • 70502 Cole's Earth Driller was chosen over this vehicle for the Legacy line because the Earth Driller is more recognizable and the Tread Assault would have had to be more modified from its original design due to the standards being different from those in 2012.[2]
  • This is the only Elemental vehicle to have a shooter in the Rise of the Snakes sets.
    • This changed when Legacy remakes of the Storm Glider and Snowmobile got stud shooters.
  • The Tread Assault flips over, and on the underside, there are many green-colored bricks to make it look like a snake vehicle if Cole is on an undercover mission or a Fangpyre bit the vehicle.
  • This set is similar to 9445 Fangpyre Truck Ambush, as they are both 4x4s about the same size, and are both used in Rise of the Snakes.
  • This set was the cheapest way to get Skales.
  • The back of the box depicts artwork of Skales in the same art style as the Spinner cards. This artwork is curiously not found anywhere else.
