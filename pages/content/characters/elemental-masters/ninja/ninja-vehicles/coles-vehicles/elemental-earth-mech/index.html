The Elemental Earth Mech is an elemental mech used by Cole.

History

History[]

The Elemental Mechs[]

What the Mech?[]

Sora used Technology to turn Chronosteel into three discs. When thrown into the air, each of the discs assembles into its respective mech: the Elemental Earth Mech, Elemental Fire Mech, and Elemental Tech Mech, each capable of channeling their respective Elemental Master's power. Together, Cole and Kai used their mechs to restrain an out-of-control Lobbo, while Sora drained the harmful energy from him.

The Mech Master[]

After The Mechanic stole the mechs from the Mech Master 5000, the ninja attempted to stop them with their mechs. However, the Mechanic sent one mech crashing into them, breaking their mechs apart. Cole took an arm from Kai's and Sora's mechs, and realized that these allowed him to channel their respective powers because of the Chronosteel.

A Pain in the Mech![]

Cole used his mech to stop the Lava-Tides from attacking the Ice Dragons. When he saw Kai having trouble against them, he swapped an arm with Kai to allow him to fight back. Cole used Fire to defend himself against the Ice Dragons. Then Sora swapped her legs and arm with Cole's, allowing Cole to fly and use Sora's Technology restraints on an Ice Dragon.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

To Mysterium[]

Cole retrieved the Earth Mech from the Monastery of Spinjitzu's Hangar bay and used it to stop an out of control SA Mark 01. He later used it again to defeat Dorama's puppet.

Fugitives From Madness[]

Cole summoned the Earth Mech to do battle with the Administration Agents and their Master Mechs.

Secrets of the Wyldness[]

Cole used his mech to battle with the Administration Agents. At one point, he even joked that the mech made fighting so easy to the point it seemed unfair to the agents.

The Forest of Spirits[]

Cole continued to carry his mech as a disc.

Rising Ninja[]

Cole used the mech in an effort to steer the main rotor of the commandeered portion of Cloud Kingdom. Unfortunately, Cole's strength accidentally snapped the rotor in half and the mech was not large enough to serve as a replacement rotor. Zane then utilized Gandalaria's size-altering potion to serve as a temporary rotor and Cole used the mech to help keep Zane steady.

Official description

Official description[]

Cole’s Elemental Earth Mech features a cockpit and is armed with a hammer to battle the evil Wolf Mask Warrior in the Shadow Forest. And now, for the first time, you can combine the parts of 3 awesome ninja mechs piloted by Cole, Sora and Kai to create your very own mech. Each customizable mech is sold separately and comes with detachable legs, arms, weapons and torso so you can mix and match.[1]
