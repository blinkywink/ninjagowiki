Overview

This article is about the set. For the vehicle, see Roto Jets. For the Legacy remake, see 71736 Boulder Blaster.

70747 Cole's Boulder Blaster is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - Tournament of Elements set released in March 2015. It includes Jungle Cole, Sleven, Zugu, the Boulder Blaster, and a small shrine.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Break out of the caves with Cole in the Boulder Blaster!

Cole has been imprisoned in the island caves during the Tournament of Elements. Place him in the cockpit of the super Boulder Blaster, grab the retractable handle to take off and bust out of there. Cole will need all his elemental powers to get past Zugu and Sleven who are armed and ready for battle. Turn the wheel to unleash the hyper-powerful automatic 8-missile NINJAGO™ rapid shooter and smash their Anacondrai outpost to smithereens! Includes 3 minifigures with assorted weapons: Cole, Zugu and Sleven.

  • Includes 3 minifigures with assorted weapons: Cole, Zugu and Sleven
  • Boulder Blaster features an 8-missile spring-loaded NINJAGO™ rapid shooter, opening cockpit, rotating gears, adjustable wings, attachment points for Cole’s weapons and Ninja gold blade details
  • Also includes Anacondrai outpost with a flame torch
  • Weapons include Cole’s 2 jagged blades, Zugu’s Anacondrai Blade and Sleven’s dagger
  • Open the cockpit and place Cole at the controls
  • Hold the retractable handle and guide the Boulder Blaster
  • Adjust the wings and rotate the gears to shoot the missiles
  • Destroy the Anacondrai outpost with the 8-missile NINJAGO™ rapid shooter!
  • Recreate your favorite scenes from the NINJAGO Masters of Spinjitzu™ TV show
  • Boulder Blaster measures over 3” (8cm) high, 11” (28cm) long and 7” (20cm) wide
  • Anacondrai outpost measures over 1” (4cm) high, 2” (6cm) wide and 1” (4cm) deep[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Boulder Blaster is Cole's first flying vehicle.
  • In the set it is called Boulder Blaster, but in the show it is called the Roto Jet.
  • In the show, the Roto Jet was never specifically Cole's, it was just Cole driving it (and it matches his color scheme). The rest of the Elemental Masters helped to build it, more notably Karlof and Zane.
    • This is similar to the Earth Driller, which was built by Nya and Dr. Julien and used by all of the ninja but marketed as Cole's vehicle in the original set.
  • A mini version of the jet was used for the LEGO Dimensions set Kai and Cole Fun Pack.
  • This set is similar to 70746 Condrai Copter Attack, as they are both air vehicles about the same size and both are used in Tournament of Elements.
  • This set was remade in Ninjago: Legacy as 71736 Boulder Blaster.
