The Earth Mech was a Security Mech that was hacked into by Cole using his Techno Blade.

History

History[]

Rebooted[]

The Surge[]

When all technology of New Ninjago City was under the Overlord's control, the ninja hacked vehicles with their Techno Blades. Cole hacked a Security Mech, turning it into his own Earth Mech. When they were leaving New Ninjago City, the Earth Mech combined with Jay's Lightning off-roader and together they became the Thunder Raider.

The Art of the Silent Fist[]

After the ninja left the city, they found a forest near Master Garmadon's monastery. The ninja hid their vehicles and Cole used a tree to hide the Earth Mech. It was later seen assembled as the Thunder Raider to escape, but all four of the vehicles were shot down by General Cryptor's Destructoid. Luckily, that was the plan, and the vehicle was on auto-pilot.

Enter the Digiverse[]

When the ninja were in the mission to stop the Digital Overlord, Jay, Cole, and Zane summoned the Lightning off-roader, the Earth Mech, and the NinjaCopter to get to the top of the digital Borg Tower.

Codename: Arcturus[]

Jay, Zane, Cole, and Lloyd used their vehicles on a mission to save Kai, who had been captured by Pythor. They used their ninja vehicles to get to the Lost City of Ouroboros.

The Titanium Ninja[]

When the ninja were coming back from space, P.I.X.A.L. sent the Earth Mech alongside the Lightning Offroader to meet with Cyrus Borg at the Temple of Fortitude. When Nya was shot down, all five ninja went to save her but they were soon ambushed by the Overlord, who used Golden Power Spinjitzu to suck in and damage the Earth Mech and the other ninja vehicles.

Possession[]

Peak-a-Boo[]

The mech was eventually salvaged by the ninja and Cole used once again to ascend the Wailing Alps to get to the Cloud Kingdom. Unfortunately, Bansha caused an avalanche and the mech was destroyed in it, though Cole managed to escape in time.

Functions

Functions[]

The Earth Mech had spinning cannon arms with golden long swords on the sides. It can fuse with the Lightning off-roader in order to transform into the Thunder Raider.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Earth Mech is the fourth mech to appear in Ninjago.
  • This is Cole's first mech.
  • This is one of the few vehicles to be used in seasons after its debut.
  • Despite being called "Earth Mech", this mech has no Earth related powers or gadgets.
