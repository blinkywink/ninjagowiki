Overview

Not to be confused with the set.

The Ninja Ultra Combo Mech is a mech that was used by MiniPix Seven and P.I.X.A.L. in the battle foreseen by the final prophecy of Quanish the Elder.

History

History[]

Darkness Within[]

The Combo Mech was seen in the background of the Samurai X Cave.

The Coming of the King[]

The legs and arms of the mech were being repaired by the ninja.

A Lesson in Anger (pieces)[]

Pieces of the arms and legs were present in the area where Garmadon was training Lloyd.

Compatible[]

P.I.X.A.L. asked for MiniPix Seven to send her the Ninja Ultra Combo Mech in preparation for the final battle.

Dragon Form[]

MiniPix Seven returned to the Newspaper warehouse with the Ninja Ultra Combo Mech as other allies of the Ninja gathered together to combat the Crystal army. P.I.X.A.L. then commandeered the mech as the Ninja achieved Dragon Form, using the mech's guns to attempt to strike a distracted Mister F. After this failed, P.I.X.A.L. flew off in the mech, luring Mister F. and his Dragonide away from the warehouse where he would soon be ambushed by Zane.

After the Ninja succeeded in recapturing and purifying the Golden Weapons, P.I.X.A.L. returned to the warehouse and used the mech's multiple weapons to destroy several members of the Crystal Army, including at least two Dragonides. Following the Resistance's victory on the ground, P.I.X.A.L. left the mech to hug Zane in celebration of their victory.

Official description

Official description[]

Join forces with Jay, Kai, Zane and Cole to create a spectacular 4-in-1 vehicle mech to take on the attack against the snakes! On their own the ninjas are no match for Cobra Mechanic and Boa Destructor, but when they come together with a mech, tank, car and jet they have the ultimate fighting machine! For completing your mission, you will be rewarded with a collectible Teamwork banner.[1]

Vehicle components

Vehicle components[]

Fire Mech[]

The Fire Mech[2] is a mech piloted by Kai. It forms the center of the Ninja Ultra Combo Mech.

Cole's car[]

Along with the jet, the car forms the back of the Combo Mech. When combined with the others, the car is separated into two drillers, allowing the user of the Fire Mech to control it and attack their opponents.

Jay's jet[]

The jet forms the head and shoulders of the mech and is the smallest of the components. It also dons dual Teamwork banners.

Zane's Ultra Sonic Raider[]

The Ultra Sonic Raider[2] is a tank that forms the legs for the Ninja Ultra Combo Mech. It is owned by Zane.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The mech is similar to Megazords and Giant Robos from Power Rangers and Super Sentai, as they are both combined of multiple vehicles.
  • This mech was likely built to replace P.I.X.A.L.'s Samurai X Mech which was presumably destroyed when the Council of the Crystal King blew up the Monastery of Spinjitzu.
