Overview

This article is about the actual mech. For the set, see 71834 Zane's Ultra Combiner Mech.

The Ultra Combiner Mech (also known as the Ninja Combo Mech) is a form of Zane. It was created by Sora using her powers to merge P.I.X.A.L.'s car and Sora's jet with Zane after they were caught in the Winds of Chaos.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Ultra Combiner mech is one of the most unique mechs as it doubles as a transformation of Zane in the show. But it is also Ninjago's first take on the Combining Mecha trope.
  • In the sets, Riyu is part of the combination by acting as a set of wings, which was referenced by him carrying Zane in the show.
