Overview

For the actual race, see Street Race of Snake Jaguar.

70639 Street Race of Snake Jaguar is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - Sons of Garmadon set released in 2018. It includes Resistance Zane, Mr. E with an extra two arms torso piece, Zane's bow, a red katana, and the Oni Mask of Vengeance, a small shrine to hold the Oni Mask, Zane's bike, and Mr. E's Oni Bike.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Match Zane’s bike against Mr. E’s Oni Bike and battle for the Oni Mask of Vengeance, with this exciting LEGO® NINJAGO® Street Race of Snake Jaguar set. Titanium Ninja Zane's bike features 2 foldout gold-colored blades, 2 hidden stud shooters on a foldout detachable drone for ground-level or aerial attacks, while the Oni Bike is armed with chopping blades and 2 stud shooters. This cool toy for kids also includes a podium with Oni Mask of Vengeance and Demon Torso elements for the Mr. E minifigure to wear, plus 2 minifigures with weapons to intensify the role-play conflict.

  • Includes 2 minifigures: Zane and Mr. E.
  • Zane’s bike features a minifigure seat, 2 stud shooters on a foldout, detachable drone, 2 foldout gold-colored blades, a flag element and translucent, ice-blue elements.
  • Oni Bike features a minifigure seat, 2 chopping blades with spikes, 2 stud shooters, front wheel with spikes, 2 detachable swords and a ‘Sons of Garmadon' flag element.
  • Push the Oni Bike along to make the blades chop.
  • Also includes a podium with the Oni Mask of Vengeance and Demon Torso transformation elements for Mr. E, plus 2 katanas.
  • Attach the Demon Torso (with 2 extra arms) and Oni Mask of Vengeance to Mr. E to make him even more evil!
  • Includes Zane’s bow-and-arrow weapon.
  • Recreate and role-play epic scenes from the NINJAGO® Masters of Spinjitzu TV show.
  • This cool toy for kids is suitable for ages 7-14.
  • Zane's bike measures over 3” (9cm) high, 6” (17cm) long and 2” (6cm) wide.
  • Detachable drone measures over 4” (11cm) long and 3” (9cm) wide, and under 1” (2cm) high.
  • Oni Bike measures over 3” (9cm) high, 7” (18cm) long and 2” (7cm) wide.
  • Podium (including mask and torso) measures over 1” (5cm) high, 1” (4cm) wide and 1” (3cm) deep.[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Oni Bike was first seen in "The Mask of Deception" and Snake Bike was first seen in "Snake Jaguar."
  • The set follows the trend of 70600 Ninja Bike Chase and 70622 Desert Lightning, with all of them featuring two bikes, though in the former's case, both bikes are owned by ninja, while in the other two, there is a bike for both a hero and villain.
  • This is the only Sons of Garmadon sets in which none of the Minifigures included were exclusive at the time of their release.
    • However, Mr. E's upper torso piece when he wears the Mask of Vengeance is exclusive.
  • This is the third Ninjago set to include only Nindroids and no organic characters except sets where only Zane appeared. The first two were 70724 NinjaCopter and 70726 Destructoid.
  • This is the second to include the Falcon, although as a drone. The first was 70724 NinjaCopter.
  • Out of all the characters in the set, two of them, Zane and Mr. E, all share same the voice actor in the TV show, Brent Miller.
