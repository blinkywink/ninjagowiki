The Destiny's Bounty was a large red airship that was created by Nya.[1] It was based on the original ship with that name.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 2[]

After Sora fixed the new Bounty, she, Lloyd, and Arin used it to deliver Riyu back to the Mountain Dragons. However, they were shot down by the Claws of Imperium and their hover chariots.

Beyond Madness[]

After the old Bounty crashed near the Monastery. Lloyd, Arin, and Sora took the new Bounty to the place where the original Bounty came from: the Land of Madness.

Writers of Destiny[]

The ninja headed for the Monastery of Spinjitzu. However, they happened to run into the Cloud Kingdom, which had drifted into their path. Unfortunately, a MergeQuake appeared, bringing a tentacled beast. Lloyd dropped off the others to save the people while he parked the Bounty near the MergeQuake to close it.

After closing the MergeQuake for the second time, the ninja, minus Nya, left the Cloud Kingdom.

Gangs of the Sea[]

Lloyd and Arin took the Bounty to look for the first of the Dragon Cores. While they were traveling, Lloyd reminded Arin of the time he had transferred Spinjitzu to an inanimate object, and encouraged him to practice this technique. However, although successful, Arin's plate destroyed the Bounty's engine, causing the airship to be grounded. Lloyd and Arin sailed out of the Endless Sea into a sea mixed with red water from another realm. Coming across a village built upon the sea, they attempted to ask for help, but were attacked by an ocean gang and a crab monster.

After retreating, they came across the algae farmers, who told them how they were forced out of their village. Lloyd and Arin agreed to help the villagers in return for repairs. After defeating the crab monster, the Bounty was repaired, and Lloyd and Arin sailed off to find the Dragon Core.

They Call It Doom[]

Lloyd and Arin used the Bounty to land in the Mother Garden, the location of one of the Dragon Cores.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Shattered Dreams[]

The ninja set off on the Bounty to reach the Cloud Kingdom, intending to stop Ras and the Wolf Clan. Unfortunately, despite their determination to protect the Cloud Kingdom, they were swiftly overpowered by Cinder, who used Shatterspin. Faced with this overwhelming defeat, the ninja had no option but to retreat back to the Bounty.

Secrets of the Wyldness[]

In the Cloud Kingdom, Jordana informed Ras that she had located the ninja, observing the Destiny's Bounty through her crystal ball. Ras ordered her to stop the ninja from reaching them, causing Jordana to use Theroxian magic to make an unnatural, violent storm that separated the ninja and left Sora, Kai, and Wyldfyre stuck on the damaged ship, which tipped back and forth on a rock deep in a canyon. Once the three escape, the Bounty loses balance and falls into the canyon, being destroyed.

Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

71797 Destiny's Bounty - race against time[]

Take to the skies with the ninja in Destiny’s Bounty, their iconic and newly updated ship, which can now fly above the merged world below them. Hurtle through storms and use the elemental dragon’s power to go extra fast! But watch out for the evil Rapton, who is pursuing them to capture their dragon! This is a race you must win to protect the dragons and keep the new NINJAGO® world safe![2]

Secret World of the Ninja New Edition[]

No place has ever felt more like home than the Ninja's trusty ship. Not only does the Destiny's Bounty have a training room, weapons store, cosy kitchen, and TV room - it can also fly!

Tales from the Merged Realms[]

Originally an old pirate ship, Destiny's Bounty is the ninja team's flying headquarters.[3]
