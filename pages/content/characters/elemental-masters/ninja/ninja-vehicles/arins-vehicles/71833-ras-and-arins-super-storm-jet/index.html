Overview

This article is about the set. For the actual jet, see Super Storm Jet.

71833 Ras and Arin's Super Storm Jet is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 3 set that was released on January 1st, 2025.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Boys and girls aged 8 plus can recreate scenes from season 3 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show with Ras and Arin’s Super Storm Jet (71833). Featuring a cool jet toy with a detachable drone and escape pod, plus Arin, Ras and Dragonian Warrior minifigures and a mini dragon critter model, this incredible LEGO® building set comes with all kids need to enjoy countless hours of role play.

The stunning action toy comes with a detachable dragon-headed drone toy with removable blades and engines to be used as weapons, 2 cockpits, including one that can be detached as a flying escape pod with wings, 2 spring-loaded shooters, adjustable wings and swirling storm blades.

The LEGO NINJAGO range of building kits and buildable ninja toys for kids (sets sold separately) includes dragons, vehicles and temples so fans can role-play adventures with their heroes. Each ninja playset can be created with the LEGO Builder app, which guides you and your child on an easy and intuitive building adventure.

  • Jet toy for kids – Boys and girls aged 8 and up can role-play aerial action from season 3 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show with Ras and Arin’s Super Storm Jet
  • Combo ninja vehicle toy – The jet toy includes a detachable dragon-headed dronetoy with 2 cockpits, including one that can be detached to use as a flying escape pod with blade wings
  • Packed with features – Ras and Arin’s Super Storm Jet has 2 spring-loaded shooters, adjustable wings, swirling storm blades and removable blades and engines to use as ninja weapons
  • 3 NINJAGO® minifigures – This playset features Arin with a crystal katana sword accessory, Ras with a golden hammer accessory, a Dragonian Warrior, plus a mini dragon critter model
  • Birthday gift for kids – This action toy provides a fun build-and-play experience and is a great birthday gift idea for kids who love imaginative play and adventure toy playsets
  • Bigger ninja adventures – Look out for more LEGO® NINJAGO® toys (sets sold separately) to play out action with Ras and Arin’s Super Storm Jet
  • A universe of ninja toys – LEGO® NINJAGO® sets let kids escape into a fantasy world of adventures with their ninja heroes
  • Measurements – The 510-piece Ras and Arin’s Super Storm Jet LEGO® building set for kids measures over 4.5 in. (11 cm) high, 13 in. (33 cm) long and 15 in. (38 cm) wide
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength is missing from all the official images of the set.
  • This is currently the only boxed set not to include Ras with his armor.
  • This set was originally planned to be for Lloyd rather than Ras and Arin, but the idea was later adapted into 71845 Lloyd's Jet Mech and the non-transforming jet was given to Ras and Arin as it fit the storyline for Season 3.[1]
