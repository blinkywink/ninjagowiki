The Dragon Mechs[2] are exo-suits that are part of the Master Mechs, one of which was used by Arin.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Administration[]

A Dragon Mech was mounted to a Master Mech being operated by a Nindroid Administration Agent in the Department of Demolitions. The Dragon Mech was taken by Lloyd when he kicked out the Nindroid pilot and claimed the Master Mech for himself.

After regrouping with Zane and Arin, the latter sat in the cockpit of the Dragon Mech. When laser grids were set up, Arin detached the Dragon Mech to hop through the grids and destroy their generator, deactivating them. Arin continued to operate the Dragon Mech separate from Lloyd's Master Mech as they escaped the Administration.

The Power Within[]

During the MergeQuake Storm, Lloyd summoned his Master Mech to the City of Imperium, alongside Arin's Dragon Mech, via remote autopilot. Arin used the Dragon Mech to combat Beatrix and her Empress Mech, notably attempting to secure the MergeQuake weapon but was thrown aside by Beatrix. The Dragon Mech would be lost however, when Beatrix used the MergeQuake weapon to open a portal beside Arin, forcing him to abandon the mech as it was sucked into the portal.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Fugitives From Madness[]

Three Dragon Mechs, attached to their respective Master Mechs, were used by a group of Administration Agents led by Roderick Allen and SsssSsss Underwood as they arrived outside the gates of the Monastery of Mysterium in an effort to recapture Zane. After Zane's attempts at stalling the agents' progress, Underwood commandeered one of the Dragon Mechs while her partner, Roderick, piloted the respective Master Mech and forcefully entered the Monastery of Mysterium by breaking down its doors.

Secrets of the Wyldness[]

As a fight ensued between the Ninja and Gandalaria and the Agents of the Administration, Cole derisively mocked the Agents for being too easy, leading to an annoyed Underwood, and the other two Dragon Mech pilots, to detach from their Master Mechs in an attempt to even the odds. As the battle progressed, a Dragon Mech operated by a Merlopian Agent would have one of its legs frozen by Zane, while Underwood's mech and the third Dragon Mech driven by a Munce Agent, would be struck by lightning from Gandalaria, briefly knocking them out. Some point after recovering, Zane ejected all the Administration Agents out of their mechs, including Underwood, the Merlopian, and the Munce Agents.
