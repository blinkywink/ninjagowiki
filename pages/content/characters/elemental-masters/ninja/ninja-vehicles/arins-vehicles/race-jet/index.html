The Race Jet was a jet created by Sora from the race bikes to take her and Arin to the Land of Lee so Arin could see his parents' graves and say goodbye.

History

History[]

The Screaming Earth[]

Sora created the jet from the race bikes so she and Arin could head to the Land of Lee.

Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon[]

Sora used the autopilot to connect the jet to the Temple Bounty. It was later used by Jenny and Barry to fly to the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In "The Vault of Focus," Sora designed a mech called the Jet Mech, which resembled the Race Jet while in its jet form. However, it's implied that the Jet Mech is distinct from the Race Jet, as Sora built it using scraps found in the City of Temples' junkyard while the Race Jet was at the hangar bay at the time.
