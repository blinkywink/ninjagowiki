The Super Storm Jet is a vehicle owned by Ras. He hid it in the First Land so he could use it in a case of need. It was operated by him and Arin during their quest to find Arin's parents.

The jet has two wings on each side and a Dragon-headed drone on the top.

History

History[]

At some point, Ras had placed many of his resources across the Merged Lands as a contingency. The jet was among them, being found in the First Land.

I Alone Can Save Them[]

After walking for some time in the deserts of the First Land, Ras led Arin to the jet, informing him that they would use it to reach Arin's parents. Arin insisted he would be able to fly the jet, despite in actuality not knowing at all how to fly it, and he would activate multiple of the jet's various functions in an attempt to start its engines. Eventually, with Ras' help, the two were able to start the jet to begin their journey to the Land of Lee.

Fallen Wishes[]

The jet remained parked while Ras and Arin briefly trained along the shores of the Land of Lee. Sometime later, they departed for the World Forest, this time with Ras at the helm. As they took off, however, the pair remained unaware that someone had been watching them for some time.

Their Draconic Majesty's Request[]

Ras and Arin flew in the jet when the two were attacked by Jay. Ras deployed the drone in an attempt to get rid of Jay but Jay came out of his armor and landed on one of the wings while his armor fought with the drone. Arin came out to fight Jay, but Jay manage to get in, and caused Ras to crash the plane. Ras ejected out while Arin was still on the jet.

Chaos Unchained[]

Heading back to the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Arin piloted the jet with Nya sitting in the back. When Zane and P.I.X.A.L. modified the portal gate to get to the First Land, Arin drove the jet, along with the drone on the top.

When they land near Thunderfang's prison where the Winds of Chaos, Arin instead piloted the Dragon-headed drone to battle Drix and the Dragonian Warriors.

Official description

Official description[]

Team up with Arin and Ras as they pilot their Super Storm Jet through the chaotic and bumpy skies above NINJAGO® world! Help Arin fire the jet’s spring-loaded shooters. Help Ras detach his dragon-headed flying drone armed with weapons. Together they must aim to take down the Dragonian Warrior and dragon critter chasing them through the clouds. Who will win this fierce battle in the sky?[1]

Trivia[]

  • While Arin is fiddling with the jet's controls in "I Alone Can Save Them", some mariachi music plays briefly. Given that this was originally Ras' jet, it is possible that he enjoys that kind of music.
    • Alternatively this could have been a specific genre program run by the station at the time.
  • It has a similar color scheme to the Explorer suggesting that it, like the jet, is one of Ras' contingency vehicles.
    • The out-universe reason is that they are both vehicles designed for Arin in the sets.
