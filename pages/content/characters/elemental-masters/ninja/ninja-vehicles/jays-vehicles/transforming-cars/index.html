Overview

The Transforming Cars are vehicles used by the Freebooters. One of the cars was confiscated by the Administration, which was then taken by the ninja shortly afterwards.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Team up with Jay, Nya and dragon form Kai to chase villains through the Whispering Woods. Get behind the wheel of Jay’s Transforming Car before boosting it into dragon mode with rotating shurikens and fire its spring-loaded shooter to take down Earth Monster and Loyalist Leader before they can get away! The ninja must defeat them to protect the Merged Lands.[1]
