Overview

66444 Ninjago 3-in-1 Superpack is a Costco-exclusive collection of sets that was released August 9, 2012. It includes 9441 Kai's Blade Cycle, 9442 Jay's Storm Fighter, and 9591 Weapon Pack, with the minifigures being Jay ZX, Snappa, Kai NRG, Chokun, Kai ZX, and Rattla.
