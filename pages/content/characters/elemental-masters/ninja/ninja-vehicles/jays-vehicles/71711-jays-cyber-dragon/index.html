Overview

Not to be confused with the actual dragon.

71711 Jay's Cyber Dragon is a Ninjago - Prime Empire set that was released on January 2, 2020. It includes Digi Jay, Digi Nya, Unagami, Richie, Hausner, the Yellow Key-Tana within a loot box, two hoverboards for each of the Whack Rats, and the Blue Cyber Dragon.

Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

"In the beginning of 2019 I moved to NINJAGO and with that a dream came true: I always loved the wild creativity it offered amongst the LEGO themes, and boy, was I looking forward to the adventure to come!

Right away I got entrusted with the epitome of a NINJAGO set and was soon working away on the big hero dragon, this time for Jay. A daunting task, a welcome challenge, and a great opportunity to hit the ground running, I decided to pair the new Cyber aesthetic with increased poseability and – after a joking remark by my manager – even managed to add a pair of spring shooters.

Having spent weeks on it I'm delighted how the trigger mechanism worked out: No matter the angle of the body segments it will fire the spring shooters reliably. And since it uses a rubber element it even feels nice ;-)

I even managed to squeeze in a small NPU: Two R2-D2-legs are being used for the cheekbones, and it is quite pleasing how they fit snugly under the 4x3 wedge that creates the dragon's eye brows.

Graphic design by Djordje Djordjevic, Angel Grau and Kjeld Sørensen."[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Digi Nya is exclusive to this set and thus is the rarest and hardest-to-find ninja in this suit.
    • Ironically enough, Nya appears in more episodes in Season 12 than any other ninja.
  • The Temple of Madness is seen in the background of the box art.
  • This set was first seen in the episode "The Maze of the Red Dragon."
    • The blue form of the dragon depicted in this set was first seen in the episode "The Temple of Madness."
  • This set is featured in Lego Legacy Heroes Unboxed as an Event Set.
