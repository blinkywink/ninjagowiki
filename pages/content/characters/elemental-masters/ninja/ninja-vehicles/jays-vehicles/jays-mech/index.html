Overview

For the set, see 71843 Rogue's Mech Dragon Rider.

The mech was a vehicle originally owned by Lee but was later taken by Jay. It's operated alongside the Mech Dragon.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Between You and Lee[]

Lee was seen with the mech when he sang his musical number of the Community of Lee, riding the Mech Dragon.

Casket of Bones[]

The mech was seen on top of the Mech Dragon, when Jay passed by it and placed a control device on the dragon part.

The Screaming Earth[]

When Lee rode the Mech Dragon to fight the Ninja, he was piloting the Mech Dragon with the smaller mech. Jay activated the control device on the Mech Dragon, which caused Lee to get out so Jay can use it, jumping into the smaller mech first and then the Mech Dragon, although he was knocked out of the Mech Dragon when Lee fought back.

Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon[]

After Jay recovered, he used the smaller mech to hop onto the Mech Dragon and followed the Ninja out of the Community of Lee to the Temple Bounty.

He later piloted the Mech Dragon when he went into the Spectral Lands but was driven back to the Monastery of the Spectral Lands where the portal gate took him and the Ninja to the City of Temples.

The Vault of Focus[]

Jay was in the mech, when he and the Ninja were on the Monastery of Gates. When he quit to aid the Ninja, he activated the Mech Dragon when he went in the smaller mech.

Chaos Reigns[]

Jay used the smaller mech to fight Zarkt in the Cave of Bells in Mysterium. While the Mech Dragon flew to fight the Spectral Dragonians, Jay used the smaller mech to fight Zarkt, throwing the mech's sword which went into the cave's walls and jump out of his mech to use his Lightning power to defeat Zarkt.

After feeling a huge headache, due to the Life, Strength, and Focus Source Dragons leaving the Council of Sources, Nya and Kai boarded the Mech Dragon as Jay went into the smaller mech piloted the Mech Dragon to the Monastery of Mysterium portal gate but ended up in the Monastery of Madness.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

The Whispering Wood[]

The mech was standing on the Mech Dragon.

The Lean of a Tree[]

After deciding to find a way to go back Ninjago, Jay decided to use his mech's spear to light up the area. Then he rode the Mech Dragon to the outside with his mech alongside with Kai and Nya, only to attacked by slug monsters. Jay used his mech to fight them. After realizing they are inside a Quealiche, Jay used his mech to ride the Mech Dragon. After getting out of the Quealiche's stomach, Jay used the mech to fight against the Earth Dragons, only for them to damage the mech, which caused Jay to leave the mech before it exploded and knocked the dragons.
