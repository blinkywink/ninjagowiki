The Temple Bounty (also known as SB-1[1]) is an airship that Roby gifted to Wyldfyre that serves as the new mobile headquarters of the ninja.

History

History[]

The Hollow Ones[]

After Thunderfang was sent to the Spectral Lands, Roby gave Wyldfyre the Bounty for saving his life. The ninja then rushed over to the training plateau of the Dragon Masters on the airship after getting news that Egalt is dying.

Official description

Official description[]

Set sail with your ninja heroes on this highly detailed LEGO® NINJAGO® The Temple Bounty model. The ship looks great on display, offers hours of storytelling and is packed with cool features, including a gear to turn the sails, removable roofs andfloors to gain access to the interior, a sushi bar and a secret computer. 6 minifigures are included to help you stage your own stories.[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The ship was left by the ninja in the Spectral Lands in "Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon" and has not been recovered since.
    • Thanks to her remote, Wyldfyre can call the ship remotely.
  • The LEGO and animated versions of the Temple Bounty have relatively different designs, the version of the show having a similar design to the Legacy Destiny's Bounty.
    • However, it shares the same door design as the previous Destiny's Bounty.
