Overview

For other uses, see Destiny's Bounty (disambiguation).

71705 Destiny's Bounty is a Ninjago: Legacy Wave 3 set that was released in most countries on June 1, 2020, in most US stores on August 15, 2020, and in Canada and LEGO stores in the US on August 24, 2020. It includes Kai, Jay, Zane, and Cole in their Legacy (dragon) robes, Legacy Wu, Legacy Samurai X Nya, Legacy Young Lloyd Garmadon, all four Golden Weapons, and the Destiny's Bounty.

Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

"Set i designed for the Ninjago 2020 Legacy line. It is a huge milestone for me tackling the most iconic part of Ninjago, the theme that kicked off my career as a LEGO Designer. I tried to stay as close as possible to the look present in the show while making something in the league of the incredible Ninjago Movie Destiny's Bounty."[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Legacy Child Lloyd and Samurai X 2.0 Nya are exclusive to this set.
    • The Samurai X minifigure is nearly identical to her appearance in 70665 The Samurai Mech, though here she has the same shoulder armor as Unagami and the "Samurai X" logo on her chest plate instead of Nya's phoenix logo.
    • Child Lloyd has green eyes to accommodate his new appearance used starting from Season 8.
  • There are many changes in the preliminary image compared to the released set:
    • Originally, Child Lloyd was to use Chopper Maroon's torso and had an ordinary face.
    • Originally, the set was to contain a cannon of normal size and not a smaller version.
    • The set also originally contained a piece of fish that has not been released since 2011.
    • Originally, Garmadon with a hairpiece based on his design in Rise of the Snakes was to be included along with the Mega Weapon. It is unknown why they decided to remove him.
    • The Scythe of Quakes has a much different build compared to the final version of this set, it reassembles Cole's NRG Golden Blade from 9572 NRG Cole.
  • In the background, Darkley's Boarding School for Bad Boys is visible. That is the school that Lloyd Garmadon is sent.
    • The Fire, Earth, Ice, and Lightning Dragons can also be seen in the background.
  • This set is the fourth physical rendition of the Destiny's Bounty and the second to be based on the first iteration of the ship.
  • This rendition becomes the basis for the Bounty's appearance from Season 13: Master of the Mountain onwards.
  • This is the fifth set to include all original six ninja, after 70751 Temple of Airjitzu, 70617 Temple of the Ultimate Ultimate Weapon, 70618 Destiny's Bounty and 70670 Monastery of Spinjitzu.
    • This set has the same minifigures as 70618 Destiny's Bounty but different versions.
  • The Ninja in this set have the SotFS/2019 armor piece but in gold. This piece in gold was first seen in 71702 Golden Mech.
  • This set is a remake of the original set from Rise of the Snakes.
