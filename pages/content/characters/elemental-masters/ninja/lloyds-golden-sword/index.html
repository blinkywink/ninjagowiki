Overview

“I check my newly acquired golden blade gifted to me by Wu, making sure it's up to the task of dealing with any potential threats atop the mountain.”
— Lloyd, The Splinter in the Blind Man's Eye, Chapter 3

The golden sword[1] is a golden dao blade that serves as Lloyd's main weapon, given to him by Master Wu.[2]

History

History[]

Prior to Season 11[]

Shortly after the Oni's invasion, Master Wu gave Lloyd a new sword made to resemble Lloyd's former sword.[2]

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Wasted True Potential[]

During a training exercise, Lloyd fought against Wu using the sword and was quickly defeated and pushed off the roof.

Questing for Quests[]

The ninja traveled around Ninjago City with their weapons including Lloyd, who brought his sword.

A Rocky Start[]

Lloyd was searching for his sword which was hidden in a vase, he later used the sword to cut a rope from the Land Bounty's cannon that allowed the ninja to escape Beohernie.

Ancient History[]

Lloyd pulled out his sword to fight against the Pyro Vipers before Zane rescued him and Nya.

The Explorers Club[]

After being denied entry into the Explorer's Club and planning to sneak through the roof ventilation, Lloyd used the sword like a chock and frozen in place by Zane's ice. Unfortunately the ice quickly broke and the sword fell in Lloyd's hands.

A Cold Goodbye[]

Nya held Lloyd's sword while he went off to steal the Traveler's Tea from Wu. After he got the tea, Nya gave him back his sword and he puts it behind him. When Wu finds out they stole the tea and confronts them, Lloyd pulls out his sword, which Wu knocks it out quickly. After their quick brawl, Lloyd picks it up again and brings it with him to the Land Bounty.

Prime Empire[]

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

Lloyd brought his sword to the old electronics warehouse to stop The Mechanic and his group from stealing the Prime Empire motherboard. Later, the ninja raided The Mechanic's headquarters, he had the weapon strapped to his back.

Dyer Island[]

As the ninja examine the Prime Empire arcade cabinet, Lloyd had the sword with him while examining it.

Master of the Mountain[]

The Worst Rescue Ever[]

He brought his sword with him as they try to rescue Cole in the Dungeons of Shintaro.

The Island[]

The Keepers of the Amulet[]

Lloyd wore his sword when he and the ninja were exploring the jungle. He later used it to fight against one of the Stone Guardians.

The Tooth of Wojira[]

Lloyd used his sword to fight against Ronin's henchmen and some Kryptarium inmates.

Seabound[]

A Big Splash[]

Lloyd used his sword to fight against some of Miss Demeanor's henchmen.

Master of the Sea[]

Lloyd used his sword to fight against some Maaray Guards on the Hydro Bounty.

The Turn of the Tide[]

Lloyd used his sword to fight against some Maaray Guards on the roof of the Police Station.

Crystalized[]

The Shape of Nya[]

Lloyd brought his sword at an abbandoned subway tunnel, after encountering Miss Demeanor and her henchmen, Lloyd used his sword to fight them but failed miserably with the other ninja shortly before the New Ninja rapidly defeated Miss Demeanor and her minions.

Brave But Foolish[]

Lloyd used his sword to fight some Dragonides that were attacking the Destiny's Bounty. The sword later fell somewhere in the city when the Bounty crashed.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

In the flashback of the beginning of The Merge, Lloyd had his sword with him while rescuing citizens. Later, in present day, Lloyd had his sword with him while rescuing Arin and Sora on the Imperium train.
