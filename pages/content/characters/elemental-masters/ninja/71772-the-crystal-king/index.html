Overview

Not to be confused with the actual character.

71772 The Crystal King is a Ninjago - Crystalized set that was released in most countries on June 1, 2022 and on August 1, 2022 in the United States and Canada. It includes Fugitive Lloyd, Harumi, a Vengestone Guard, a Vengestone Warrior, and the Crystal King.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Crystalized Harumi is exclusive to this set.
    • Fugitive Lloyd and the two-armed version of the Crystal King are also exclusive to this set in regions where the Ninjago Magazine isn't available, such as the US.
      • Additionally this set is the only way to get the proper hood for Fugitive Lloyd, and the armor for the Crystal King.
  • This is the only set from the Crystalized wave to not contain any Golden Weapons.
  • The Crystal King's centaur form differs from his TV show appearance by having two large and two small crystal shards/wings branching off his shoulders, but in the show he has four large wings.
  • Harumi's hairpiece was slightly changed for this set. It had previously been made of flexible plastic, but now it is hard plastic.
  • This is Harumi's fifth overall set appearance, and it continues the tradition of her often appearing alongside Lloyd.
  • This is the fourth set to include both the minifigure of the character, and the form they become.
    • The first set was 71713 Empire Dragon with Unagami, the second was 71742 Overlord Dragon with the Overlord, and 71754 Water Dragon with Nya.
    • Unlike most sets, this set differs from the previous ones by not representing the Overlord and his centaur form as distinct characters, as shown in the box art and set description. Additionally, one of the features of the set is designed to illustrate the Overlord inside the centaur form.
