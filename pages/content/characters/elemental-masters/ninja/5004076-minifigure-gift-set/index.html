Overview

The 5004076 Minifigure Gift Set is a promotional set from Ninjago, Legends of Chima, City, and DC Superheroes. It includes Lloyd DX, Fire Lavertus, a City Swamp Policeman, and Superboy.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Lloyd DX is a suit variant that never appeared in the show.
  • Lloyd's face print in this set is more accurate than his face print used from Rebooted to The Hands of Time. One eyebrow is slightly raised, but the difference is mostly because, unlike the relatively straight mouth of the main face print, Lloyd DX's mouth more clearly resembles his signature curved smile from the show. However, both face prints' eyebrows are thicker than Lloyd's in the show, and they thicken toward the inner end. This is unlike his original face print, whose eyebrows are thin and even throughout, as they are in the show.
    • The design for Morro when possessing Lloyd uses the same face design as Lloyd DX with added shading around the eyes. In "Winds of Change," before Morro's skin and hair change color from Lloyd's yellow and blond to Possessed Lloyd's green and black, he has the appearance of Lloyd with thicker eyebrows than his show counterpart, appearing as he would if he had his DX minifigure's face print.
    • Because this face print is more accurate than the main one, it was often the version used to portray Lloyd in promotional images and other media. For example, this can be seen as the basis for Lloyd's face in his Honor robe CGI render,[1] his LEGOLAND costume,[2] and his The Realm of Shadows puppet.[3] The face also appeared on Kai's CGI render for The Realm of Shadows promotional images.[4]
  • Unlike the other four DX ninja, Lloyd does not have the representative kanji of his elements behind him.
  • Lloyd DX's body print represents his Golden Dragon.
