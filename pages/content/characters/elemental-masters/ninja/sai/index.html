Overview

External links
The sai, also known as the Little Striker,[citation needed] is a common weapon in Ninjago. It is a sai, a knife, or dagger of Japanese design with a three-pronged blade. It is featured in several different colors, including silver, metallic dark gray, and gold. It appears on Nya's spear, and Ultra Violet often uses one as well. The Obsidian Sais are one variant.
