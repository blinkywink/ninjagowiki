Overview

You may be looking for Nya's spear.

The golden spear is a spear ressembling a trident, that serves as Nya's main weapon.

History

History[]

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Questing for Quests[]

Nya brought her spear with her while she and the ninja were looking for crime in Ninjago City.

Powerless[]

Nya wore her spear while she and the others were fighting the Elemental Cobras in the City. She later used it to fight some other ones in the Museum of History.

Ancient History[]

Nya used her spear to fight some Pyro Vipers in the City.

The Explorers Club[]

Nya wore her spear while she, Lloyd and Zane visited the Explorer's Club, after having no permission to get the scroll, she and Zane left their weapons on the building's roof.

A Cold Goodbye[]

Nya wore her spear while Lloyd was trying to steal the Traveler's Tea from Wu. She later kept wearing it when she and the ninja were fighting Wu.

Prime Empire[]

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

Nya wore her spear while she and the others were fighting the Mechanic's henchmen. After the Mechanic was arrested, Nya and the ohers started to investing the his headquarters.

Dyer Island[]

Nya wore her spear while she and the others were investigating Jay's dissappearance.

Master of the Mountain[]

The Worst Rescue Ever[]

Nya brought her spear with her when she and the others were trying to save Cole, who was imprisoned in the Skull Dungeons. Unfortunately the ninja's plan did not work and they were all imprisoned.

The Island[]

The Keepers of the Amulet[]

Nya brought her spear with her in the Uncharted jungle. She later used to fight one of the Stone Guardians, but was quickly defeated.

The Tooth of Wojira[]

Nya use her spear to fight Ronin's henchmen and the Kryptarium inmates.

Seabound[]

A Big Splash[]

Nya wore her spear while she and Zane were trying to stop Miss Demeanor's trucks, unfortunately Nya quickly lost control of her powers.

The Call of the Deep[]

Nya's spear was lying in her room, later Maya hung her golden spear and her Munce spear on her wall.

Crystalized[]

Kryptarium Prison Blues[]

Nya's spear was leaning on her room's wall when Wu tried to give her motivation after she temporarily lost her Elemental powers.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

When Nya came back to the monastery after the Ninja found her after being lost for years due to The Merge, she started using her spear again as her main weapon.

The Temple of the Dragon Cores[]

Nya brought her spear when the ninja went to Temple of the Dragon Cores and use it to fight the divided Spirit of the Temple.

Gangs of the Sea[]

Nya had her spear on her back during the meeting in the Hangar bay and brought it with her when she went on the quest of the Dragon Core with Sora and Riyu.

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

Nya had her spear on her back during the search of the Dragon Core.

They Call It Doom[]

Nya had her spear on her back when they left Arrakore in the Pinnacle of Crystals and when they arrived in the Land of Lost Things. She use it to fight Cole in his Golems form not recognizing him. She threw her spear on him before he threw it on the ground.

Land of Lost Things[]

Nya had her spear with her during her journey in the Land of Lost Things
