Overview

71864 Kai and Cole's Combiner Vehicles is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 4 set that was released in most countries on June 1, 2026 and will release on August 1, 2026 in the US. It includes Kai and Cole in their Draconus suits, an Ice Monster, Race buggy, and the Combiner bike.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Kids have all they need to enjoy hours of action play with the LEGO® NINJAGO® Kai and Cole’s Combiner Vehicles (71864) building set, an exciting gift idea for boys and girls ages 7 and up. This impressive transforming vehicle playset includes an off-road race buggy with a shooter, a decorated motorcycle and 3 minifigures. Kids can rebuild both the buggy and the motorcycle into a 3-wheel bike model by detaching and swapping their back sections. Kids can also recreate scenes from season 4 of theNINJAGO Dragons Rising TV show with minifigures of Kai, Cole and, for the first time, a villainous Ice Monster. The LEGO NINJAGO range of pretend playsets includes mechs, dragons and temples for fans to immerse themselves in a fantasy world full ofadventure. And kids can build with confidence using the LEGO Builder app – zoom, rotate in 3D and track progress with easy-to-follow digital instructions. Set contains 394 pieces.

  • NINJA PLAYSET – Play out exciting action from season 4 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show with Kai and Cole’s Combiner Vehicles (71864), a fun building set for boys and girls ages 7 and up
  • 2 TOY VEHICLES – Spark endless pretend play with anoff-road race buggy that has a cockpit and a shooter, and a motorcycle decorated in detachable golden swords and a banner
  • TRANSFORMING FUNCTIONS – Kids can rebuild both the buggy and the motorcycle into a new 3-wheel bike model by detaching and swapping their back sections
  • 3 NINJAGO® MINIFIGURES – Play out exciting battles between heroes and villains with ninja warriors Kai and Cole, plus, for the first time, an Ice Monster
  • FUN GIFT IDEA FOR KIDS – This playset makes a great birthday or holiday gift for boys and girls who love ninja and fantasy adventures
  • MORE NINJA ADVENTURES – Discover other LEGO® NINJAGO® sets (sold separately) featuring detailed dragons, mechs, temples and vehicles
  • A UNIVERSE OF LEGO® NINJAGO® SETS – The extensive collection of sets lets fans escape into an immersive world for imaginative play alongside their heroes
  • DIMENSIONS – This 394-piece toy vehicle playset features a buggy that measures over 3.5 in. (9 cm) high, 7 in. (18 cm) long and 3 in. (8 cm) wide[1]
