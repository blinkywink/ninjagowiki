Overview

2258 Ninja Ambush is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - pilot episodes set that was released on January 4, 2011. It includes Trainee Kai, Bonezai, the Scythe of Quakes, a Silver Sword, and a Silver Bone Axe.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Ambush the evil skeleton soldier and protect the golden scythe!

As part of his training to become the Spinjitzu Master of Fire, Kai is sent into the bamboo forest by Sensei Wu to protect the Golden Scythe. Hit the button to launch a surprise attack on Bonezai before he steals it! Set includes 2 minifigures and 3 weapons.

  • Includes 2 minifigures: Kai and Bonezai
  • Features golden scythe, black sword and bone weapons
  • Set measures over 2” (5cm) tall[1]
Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

Really wanted this to be called "The Bambush" - bamboo and ambush![2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The article about this set in the Character Encyclopedia was headlined as "Ambush Shrine" despite the fact that it is not a shrine.
  • Bonezai doesn't have his armor in this set.
  • This set may be meant to resemble the Caves of Despair, where they ninja found the Scythe of Quakes.
  • This is the first non-promotional Ninjago set to be released.
