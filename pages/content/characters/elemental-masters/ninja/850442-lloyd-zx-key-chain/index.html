Overview

850442 Lloyd ZX Keychain is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu Keychain that was released on June 4, 2012. It includes a Lloyd ZX minifigure (without the shoulder armor).

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Keep the serpentine army at bay with the green ninja!

Attach this authentic LEGO® Ninjago Lloyd ZX minifigure, in green ninja gear, to your key ring or backpack to harness the ultimate spinjitzu power! Defeat the serpentine army and take on Lord Garmadon himself or just battle your way through traffic– spinjitzu-style! Includes authentic LEGO Ninjago Lloyd ZX minifigure with accessories attached to a sturdy metal ring and chain. Makes a great backpack charm!

  • Features authentic LEGO® Ninjago Lloyd ZX minifigure with accessories attached to a sturdy metal ring and chain
  • Attach to your keys or backpack!
  • Minifigures are not detachable from metal chain
  • Makes a great gift for fans of LEGO Ninjago building sets![1]
