Overview

The golden hammer[1] is a hammer that serves as Cole's main weapon.

History

History[]

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Questing for Quests[]

While trying to find crime in Ninjago City, Cole wore his hammer.

Powerless[]

Cole used his hammer to fight against the Elemental Cobras in the Museum.

Ancient History[]

Cole wore his hammer while he, Kai and Jay went to the sewers.

Vengeance is Mine![]

Cole used his golden hammer to fight against the Pyro Vipers in the Hangar Bay.

A Cold Goodbye[]

Cole used his hammer to swing at the Traveler's Tea and prevent Wu from getting it.

Prime Empire[]

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

Cole wore his golden hammer when he was fighting the Mechanic's henchmen, he later kept wearing when he and the ninja were investigating the Mechanic's Headquarters.

Dyer Island[]

Cole wore his golden hammer while and the ninja were inside figuring out Jay's disappearance.

The Island[]

The Keepers of the Amulet[]

Cole attempted to use his hammer to attack the Stone Guardians, but they deflected the attack back at him.

The Tooth of Wojira[]

Cole wore his hammer when he caught Jay, he later used it to fight Ronin's henchmen and some Kryptarium inmates.

Seabound[]

A Big Splash[]

Cole wore his hammer while he was driving his Rock Cycle to try to stop Miss Demeanor's trucks.

Riddle of the Sphinx[]

Cole wore his hammer when he and the others went to the Explorer's Club, he later used it to fight some Merlopians.

Papergirl[]

Cole used his hammer to attack Kalmaar, but Kalmaar was able to dodge his attack and threw him in the streets. Cole was later able to hit him with his hammer and Spinjitzu.

Master of the Sea[]

Cole used his hammer to hit Gripe, unfortunately he was later knocked out in the sea.

The Turn of the Tide[]

Cole used threw his hammer to a group of merlopians. He is later seen wearing it when Nya leaves the ninja.

Crystalized[]

Crystastrophe[]

When Jay tried to steal a Crystal spear from a Vengestone Guard, Cole threw his hammer to him to break one of the spears and steal the crystal that powers it.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Land of Lost Things[]

Cole was wearing his hammer while escaping from the Hoarder. Later, he was seen wearing again while fighting against the Hoarder

Absolute Power[]

While planning to find a way to enter Imperium with Sora and Nya, Cole was seen wearing his hammer and continued to wear it while following Wu.

Casket of Bones (flashback)[]

Cole was wearing his hammer while trying to use his powers to recover the thing Wu wanted him to.

Other appearances

Other appearances[]

Wasted True Potential (dream)[]

In Zane's dream, Cole was frozen with his hammer.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In the show, the hammer is gold and gray, while in the sets, it is gold and black.
    • It is also the only signature golden weapon to not be fully gold in SOTFS sets.
  • As shown in "Papergirl," the hammer is useful for balancing oneself out after being hit out of one's own Spinjitzu tornado.
  • When Cole throws his hammer at the group of the Merlopians in "The Turn of the Tide," he does so in the same way as Thor with Mjølnir in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
  • Although Cole have the hammer on the poster and intro of Dragons Rising season 3, he did not used it at all in the season.
    • He is also not seen using it in season 2, but the hammer is visible in the ninja's tournament quarter.
