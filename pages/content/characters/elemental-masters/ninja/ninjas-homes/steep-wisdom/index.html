Overview

“Your first problem is the opening of a tea shop in the middle of a dry season. Water's at a premium. Then there's the name. Steep Wisdom? Ugh. More like Steep Debt. I mean, heh, where are the customers?”
— Ronin to Wu, Misako and Nya, "The Temple on Haunted Hill"

Steep Wisdom is a tea shop in Ninjago. It was owned by Wu after he retired from being a master with the aid of Nya, Misako, and Ronin. The tea shop was eventually sold to finance new vehicles for the ninja to use against the Ghost Warriors.

History

History[]

Prior to Possession[]

After Garmadon was banished to the Cursed Realm, his monastery was turned into Steep Wisdom.[1]

Possession[]

Winds of Change[]

After Wu retired from being a master, he decided to open a tea shop with the help of Nya, Misako, and the ninja. In order to attract customers, he sent the ninja out to Ninjago City to advertise.

Unfortunately, when they finally had their first customer, Morro arrived seeking Wu's staff. A fight broke out in the little shop and the ninja were forced to flee.

Ghost Story[]

Misako, Nya, and Wu returned to Steep Wisdom and began cleaning up the shop.

Stiix and Stones[]

Wu began Nya's training to become the Water Ninja at the pond behind the tea shop. Meanwhile, in order to save his life, Kai gave Ronin his and the other ninjas' shares of the store.

The Temple on Haunted Hill[]

Ronin arrived at Steep Wisdom and was dismayed at how it was run, prohibiting Misako to give the customers anything extra.

The Crooked Path[]

The ninja and Ronin returned to Steep Wisdom to decipher the third clue to the tomb. While Wu and Misako attempted to figure it out, the ninja armed the tea shop with fortifications to defend against any ghost attacks.

Curseworld, Part I[]

After Morro claimed the Realm Crystal, Wu decided to sell Steep Wisdom so his could finance new vehicles for the ninja to use. They all returned to the tea shop where they met up with Cyrus Borg who presented them with the Ghost Cycle, Ghost Taker GT, and an upgraded version of the Ice Mech.

Dark Island Trilogy[]

Dark Island Trilogy - Part 1[]

After the destruction of the Preeminent, Wu spent several days inside Steep Wisdom meditating.

Wu-Cru (non-canon)[]

The ninja train inside a dojo in Steep Wisdom.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • According to Tommy Andreasen, the tea shop looking different from Garmadon's monastery is "a production thing."[2]
  • The intro to the Wu-Cru videos has the camera quickly moving through the different rooms and halls of the shop, giving a better look at its interior.
  • Sacred Wu-Cru Dojo, located somewhere beneath the tea shop, is almost identical to the dojo area in Garmadon's monastery.
  • The names Ronin suggested as a replacement for "Steep Wisdom" are "Curiosi-tea" or "Shake Your Boo-Tea".
