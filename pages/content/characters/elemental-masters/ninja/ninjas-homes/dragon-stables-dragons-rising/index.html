Overview

This article is about the post-Merge stables. For the original stables, see Dragon stables.
“Having these stables makes it easier.”
“Stables that weren't here before the Merge, right? It's almost like everything's coming together for a reason.”
— Sora and Arin, "The Temple of the Dragon Cores"

The dragon stables are stables located in a cave in the Mountains of Impossible Height that holds the Monastery of Spinjitzu's portal gate. The cave didn't exist before the Merge.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

I Will Be the Danger[]

Kai was led to the caves that would eventually become the dragon stables by Wu's spirit, discovering the Portal gate housed inside the Monastery of Spinjitzu in the process.

After the Battle of the Second Monastery[]

After the Battle of the Second Monastery, some of the dragons liberated from Imperium chose to stay in the stables at the Monastery.

The Temple of the Dragon Cores[]

The ninja and their new dragons returned to the dragon stables after sealing off a MergeQuake. Arin commented as to whether the stables had existed prior to The Merge and suggested that everything came together for a reason. Sora however was more dismissive of the notion and suggested that dragons simply liked sleeping in caves.

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

Zane attempted to analyze and study the portal gate in the dragon stables and was later joined by Alfonzo Frohicky.

The Last Djinn[]

Zane spent most of the day in the dragon stables to study the portal gate.

Before The Administration[]

Zane attempted to activate the portal gate, which gained the attention of Administration Agents, Roderick Allen and SsssSsss Underwood, who detained him for too many failed activation attempts.

Appearance

Appearance[]

The dragon stables are a large cave inside the mountain beneath the Monastery of Spinjitzu. The monastery's portal gate is prominently placed in the center of the stables with a stone staircase leading up toward it. Along the sides of the gate are many hallowed out, rounded chambers featuring carved designs not present anywhere else in the monastery. These chambers were used by some of the dragons residing in the stables as nests. Over time, the ninja decorated the stables with torches and also erected wooden platforms for access or to store various goods and items.
