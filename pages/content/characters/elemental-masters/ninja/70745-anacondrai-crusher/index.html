70745 Anacondrai Crusher is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - Tournament of Elements set released in 2015. It includes Jungle Kai, Krait, and a Condrai Crusher.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Help Kai take on the awesome Anacondrai Crusher!

Deep in the island’s jungle, Kai is under attack from the fearsome Anacondrai Crusher, piloted by Anacondrai warrior Krait. Summon Kai’s elemental powers and build a fire wall to block the Anacondrai Crusher’s path. Evade this destructive vehicle’s dual spinning blades as it powers forward, leap away from its swiping tail and run for cover if the snake’s mouth opens – there’s a hidden dual poison dart shooter inside. Fire back with Kai’s catapult and capture Krait before he escapes from the jungle! Includes 2 minifigures with assorted weapons: Kai and Krait.

  • Includes 2 minifigures with assorted weapons: Kai and Krait
  • Anacondrai Crusher features a cockpit, huge wheels, cool snake details, whipping tail, opening bone engine hood with engine details underneath, opening mouth with dual poison dart shooter and dual spinning blades
  • Kai’s fire wall features a catapult, fire balls and translucent flame elements
  • Weapons include Krait’s Anacondrai Blade and Kai’s 2 swords
  • Stay clear of the Anacondrai Crusher’s swipng tail
  • Dodge the dangerousdual spinning blades
  • Swerve out of the way of the poison dart shooter
  • Return fire with Kai’s catapult
  • Recreate your favorite scenes from the NINJAGO Masters of Spinjitzu™ TV show
  • Anacondrai Crusher measures over 3” (10 cm) high, 11” (30 cm) long and 6” (16 cm) wide
  • Kai’s fire wall measures over 1” (3 cm) high, 1” (4 cm) wide and 1” (5 cm) deep[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • It vaguely resembles some of the Fangpyre vehicles from previous sets.
  • This set is similar to 70755 Jungle Raider, as they are both buggies about the same size, as well as both being used in The Tournament of Elements.
