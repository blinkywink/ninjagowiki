Overview

70606 Spinjitzu Training is a set based on the 2017 film, The LEGO Ninjago Movie. Released in August 2017, it includes Ninja Force Kai and Ninja Force Zane, a dojo wall segment, a Garmadon dummy, a training station, and a weapons rack.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Join the ninja heroes at the dojo with this action-packed THE LEGO® NINJAGO® MOVIE™ Spinjitzu Training set, featuring a dojo wall with a scroll and 2 detachable staffs, weapon rack with 2 detachable katanas, combat training station, Garmadon combat dummy, plus 2 minifigures.

  • Includes 2 minifigures: Kai and Zane.
  • Features a dojo wall with a scroll and 2 detachable staffs, weapon rack with 2 katanas, Garmadon combat dummy and a combat training station.
  • Weapons include Kai’s 2 katanas and Zane’s bow and arrow.
  • Recreate and role-play exciting THE LEGO® NINJAGO® MOVIE™ scenes.
  • Dojo wall measures over 3” (8cm) high, 1” (4cm) wide and 1” (4cm) deep.
  • Weapon rack measures over 1” (3cm) high, 1” (3cm) wide and under 1” (1.5cm) deep.
  • Garmadon combat dummy measures over 1” (5cm) high and under 1” (2cm) wide and 1” (2cm) deep.
  • Combat training station measures over 1” (4cm) high, 1” (3cm) wide and 1” (3cm) deep[1]
Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

"This set is the movie version of 2516: Ninja Training Outpost. I made some sketch models for this, and the wobble function of the Garma-dummy. Chris Stamp made the final model!"[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The set includes Garmadon's Helmet of Shadows as an easter egg.
  • The scroll says "Dojo Wu" in Ninjargon.
  • This is the only LEGO Ninjago Movie set without an antagonist.
  • According to Nick Vás, the set designer, this set is based on 2516 Ninja Training Outpost.[3]
  • 70680 Monastery Training is based on this set.
