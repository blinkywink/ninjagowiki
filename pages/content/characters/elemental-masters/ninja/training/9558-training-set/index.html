Overview

9558 Training Set is a Ninjago Spinners - Rise of the Snakes set and was released onJanuary 10, 2012. It includes Kendo Kai, a spinner, a crown, eight weapons (a Silver Sword, a Golden Dark Blade, a Katana, a Spear, the Staff of the Dragons, a Dark Axe, a Scythe Blade, and a Nin-Jô), a Character Card, and four Battle Cards.

  • Card 33 - Blinding Flash

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • While Card 3 - Kendo Kai isn't exclusive to the set, the other four cards within it are.
  • This set has two instruction booklets despite its smaller size.

Errors[]

  • The instructions contain an error, as the inventory list for a step contains a gray 1x2 brick with a hole rather than a black one, as pictured and provided within the set itself.
    • The error in question occurs on the second step of the 27th page of the first instruction booklet.
