Overview

This article is about the set. For the actual motorbikes, see Skull Motorbikes.

2259 Skull Motorbike is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - pilot episodes set that was released on January 4, 2011. It includes Trainee Jay, Chopov, the Nunchucks of Lightning, a Bronzed Bone Axe, and a Black Katana.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Battle Chopov to retrieve the nunchucks of lightning!

Chopov is escaping on his Skull Motorbike with the nunchucks of lightning and Sensei Wu has sent Jay to stop him. Can he use his new-found skills to avoid the Skull Motorbike’s Ninja swat defense and retrieve the nunchucks? Set features 2 minifigures, 3 weapons and Skull Motorbike with realistic details.

  • Includes 2 minifigures: Jay and Chopov
  • Features Skull Motorbike, nunchucks of lightning, black sword and bone axe
  • Shoot flames from the Skull Motorbike’s tailpipes!
  • Battle for the nunchucks of lightning!
  • Skull Motorbike features cool details and chain link elements!
  • Skull Motorbike is over 11” (28cm) long[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In some pictures, Chopov can be seen with or without his helmet, his body turned backward, and with Frakjaw's chest plate.
  • This is the only Ninjago set to date to include Chopov's helmet.
  • This is the only set that contains the Bronzed Bone Axe.
