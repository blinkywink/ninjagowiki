Overview

This article is about the actual vehicle. For the set, see 2263 Turbo Shredder.

Turbo Shredders are dragster-like Skulkin Vehicles with treads for front wheels.

History

History[]

King of Shadows[]

A few Turbo Shredders accompanied the other Skulkin vehicles through the Forest of Tranquility.

Weapons of Destiny[]

A Turbo Shredder was sucked into the Tornado of Creation.

Wrong Place, Wrong Time[]

A Turbo Shredder appeared as Kai and Nya were ambushed.

The Kaiju Protocol[]

A Turbo Shredder appears in the Underworld.
