Dinosaurs,[1][2] also referred to as dinos for short, are an extinct group of reptiles that once roamed Ninjago.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Various dinosaurs skeletons and figurines can be found in the Museum of History, Chen's Island and Ronin's pawn shop. These include the Velociraptor, Triceratops, Stegosaurus and Tyrannosaurus rex.
    • Some of these models were from the 2012 LEGO Dino toyline.
  • According to the LEGO.com description of the Chained Fang, it was made out of the tooth of a dinosaur's skeletal remains.[1]
  • Grundles resemble dinosaurs and are exhibited alongside dinosaur skeletons.
  • Chenosaurus looks like a Tyrannosaurus Rex.
  • Both the Trexxs and the Acid Monster, which are from the Land of Monsters, are dinosaur-like monsters.
  • Rapton's name is based on "raptor."
  • Before Legends of Chima was eventually cancelled, a dinosaur tribe called "Prehistorics," containing species such as Triceratops, T-rexes, and Pterosaurs, was considered.[4]
  • Tommy Andreasen's favorite dinosaur was Stegosaurus, when he was a kid, and currently is Triceratops.[5]
