Overview

Not to be confused with Elemental Dragons.
“It's Ice. It's pure ice! It's an elemental creation!”
— Nya to Kai, Jay, Cole and Krag, "Awakenings"

Elemental creations are living beings or objects created by Elemental Masters out of their respective Elements.

History

History[]

The Last of the Formlings (flashback)[]

The Ice Emperor created Boreal to freeze the village of the Formlings.

Shadow of Ronin (non-canon)[]

Ronin used the remainder of the Elemental Forges' power to create a massive elemental Golem to fight the ninja with, harnessing the Elements of Creation.

Green Destiny[]

When Lloyd and Wu confronted Garmadon, he used Destruction to summon four daggers to fight the two. Later, when Wu and Firstbourne were blown off Borg Tower, Garmadon used his daggers to fight Lloyd.

Into the Breach[]

When the Omega first appeared, Garmadon used his Destruction daggers to fight him, but was easily overwhelmed.

Endings[]

Garmadon later used this ability again during his showdown with the Omega, creating a similar staff.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Nya discovered that Boreal was an elemental creation of Ice. When Boreal regained consciousness, he attacked the ninja and Krag once more. However, Kai managed to regain his elemental Fire powers fully, and used them to destroy Boreal. After the Liberation of the Never-Realm, the Blizzard Sword Masters and Blizzard Archers were also destroyed after Zane regained his memories and destroyed his scepter.

Crystalized[]

The Crystal army utilized Infectious crystals made from the Overlord as extensions of his elemental essence of Darkness. As their name suggested, these crystals were able to infect living beings with the Overlord's darkness, transforming their victims into crystal zombies. Weapons imbued with Darkness, such as the Weapons of Destruction or Vengestone Spears used by the Vengestone Guards, were able to spread the infection through energy blasts or direct physical contact with their victims. In addition, the Vengestone Guards and crystal zombies could spread the infection as well through physical contact with other living organisms.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Elemental creations are similar to Elemental Dragons as they're both creatures created by elemental powers. The difference is that Elemental Dragons are metaphysical manifestations rather than creatures created by elemental power and vanish when elemental masters lose control of their fears. Elemental creations exist indefinitely.
    • The dragon Boreal was also an elemental creation, showing that dragons can be manifested through both elemental abilities.
  • It is unknown whether other Elemental Powers can also create elemental creations, or just the elements of Creation, Energy, and the elemental essences.
    • So far, Lightning is the only element of Creation not used in any canon material to create elemental creations.
      • In the story about Jay that Fugi-Dove told in "The Absolute Worst," Jay was shown creating lightning ropes, though this is merely a fabricated story.
