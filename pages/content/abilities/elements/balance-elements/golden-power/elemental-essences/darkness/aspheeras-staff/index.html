Overview

“I had my staff then. Without that, I can do nothing.”
— Aspheera to the ninja, "Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5!"

The staff is a naginata used by Aspheera. The staff has the ability to increase Aspheera's powers so that she can use Magic. After being released from her tomb, she added the Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu to the staff, further increasing her powers.

After Aspheera was locked up in Kryptarium Prison, her staff was in the possession of the ninja for a while, where P.I.X.A.L. used it to send them to the Never-Realm until they gave it to Cyrus Borg, who placed the staff in the Borg Tower vaults.

A year after the Great Flood, the ninja stole the staff so Aspheera could steal Nya's powers to return her to normal. After this incident, she lost the staff again. However, the sorceress acquired a new, purple staff from the Crystal King.

History

History[]

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Never Trust a Human (flashback)[]

Aspheera used her staff to fight Wu and Garmadon thousands of years ago, but lost. She was later imprisoned with the staff in the pyramid.

Boobytraps and How to Survive Them[]

After the ninja released Aspheera from her tomb and she asked them where the “traitorous deceiver” is, Lloyd stated he and the other ninja were unsure who the sorceress was looking for, so she attacked him using her staff. She then uses it to drain Kai's fire powers, then leaving him on the floor, unconscious.

Snaketastrophy[]

Aspheera carried her staff while taking over Ninjago City.

Powerless[]

Aspheera used her staff to trap the ninja under some debris in Ninjago Museum of History.

Ancient History[]

On the streets of Ninjago City, Lloyd was able to kick Aspheera's staff out of her hand, but she was able to retrieve it.

Under Siege[]

Aspheera used her staff to banish the monastery weapons.

The Explorers Club[]

Aspheera held her staff while her army was trying to open the hangar bay's doors.

Vengeance is Mine![]

Aspheera attempted to use her staff on Wu, but missed and hit the mech instead. After being frozen in ice, she tried again, but Zane interfered and got hit instead, being banished to the Never-Realm.

A Cold Goodbye[]

In an attempt to stop the ninja from going to the Never-Realm after they had stolen the Traveler's Tea from him, Wu wielded the staff until they tied him up, and P.I.X.A.L. used it on them so they could retrieve Zane from the Never-Realm.

Prior to Crystalized[]

At some point after the staff was used to send the ninja to the Never-Realm, they gave the weapon to Cyrus Borg, who placed it among other dangerous artifacts in the vaults of Borg Tower.

Crystalized[]

A Mayor Problem[]

When the ninja realized that the only possible way to deprive Nya of her powers was to use Aspheera's magic, they went to the mayor to ask him for her release and also to lend her staff. However, the mayor rejected both requests and had the staff, along with the other artifacts, transferred from Borg Tower to a safer place where the ninja could not get it.

Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5![]

When the ninja learned that the staff was not in Borg Tower, they tracked down the trucks and stole the staff from them, although they had to fight the New Ninja.

A Painful Promise[]

When the ninja returned from the mission to the hangar bay, they gave the staff to Aspheera to turn Nya back. However, the sorceress, blinded by revenge, tried to kill the ninja, although she was eventually forced to use the staff to deprive Nya her powers. After the shockwave of elemental energy occurred, the staff has disappeared and Aspheera left the Hangar without it.

When a Crystal Spider visited Aspheera in her pyramid, it brought her a new staff with a purple handle.

The Council of the Crystal King[]

Aspheera was holding her new staff during the Council of the Crystal King meeting. When Lloyd, disguised as the Mechanic, tried to escape, she used the staff's magic to stop him, but failed, although the ninja was later captured by Mister F.

The Fall of the Monastery[]

Aspheera uses her new staff to fight Wu, Nya, P.I.X.A.L., and Skylor as the Crystal Council invaded the Monastery of Spinjitzu for the Golden Weapons.

Darkness Within[]

With all four Golden Weapons now in the possession of the Crystal Council, Aspheera commences a ritual using the magical abilities from her new staff to transform the Golden Weapons into the Weapons of Destruction, as well as summoning the Overlord's physical form as the Crystal King.

The Coming of the King[]

After the Crystal Council were bestowed with new powers, the Overlord handed the Sword of Fire, now known as the Sword of Destruction, to Aspheera, who started using it instead of her new staff.

Other appearances

Other appearances[]

Wasted True Potential[]

In Zane's dream, Aspheera blasted him with her staff on the courtyard of the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

The Belly of the Beast[]

Zane has another dream in which Aspheera attacked him with her staff on the courtyard.

The Message[]

When Cole was telling the young Ice Fishers about Zane's banishment, he used made up puppets of the ninja and Aspheera. Aspheera's puppet had her staff as well.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Magic Channeling - Due to being imbued with magical abilities, the staff’s main purpose allows Aspheera to channel her magic and increase her powers to perform Magic. Magical artifacts like the Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu allow the staff to channel its dark powers so its user can banish or send someone/something to another realm and time.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • As of "Awakenings," her staff is the only weapon to hold a Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu after Zane destroyed his scepter, which held the other scroll. As of "Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5!", this is no longer true, as the scroll is no longer attached to the staff.
  • In "A Cold Goodbye," it was confirmed that the staff was claimed by the ninja after Aspheera's defeat and imprisonment in "Vengeance is Mine!."
  • It was confirmed by Tommy Andreasen that Zane was sent back in time when he was banished, meaning the staff is a method of time travel.[1] He also stated, "it has to do with who uses the scroll and why."[2]
  • The staff had another version that held the second Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu, which was destroyed in "Awakenings." The second staff was known to lack any mystical abilities in the absence of the scroll, unlike the one belonging to Aspheera.
  • In "The Fall of the Monastery," the staff changes color multiple times, between its correct pinkish-purple and black.
  • The fates of both of Aspheera's staffs are unknown.
    • The first one disappeared after Aspheera used it on Nya in "A Painful Promise", presumably getting destroyed in the process of draining Nya's Elemental Power.
    • The second one disappeared between the ending of "Darkness Within" and the beginning of "The Coming of the King" without explanation, despite the fact that it was meant to be the same scene.
