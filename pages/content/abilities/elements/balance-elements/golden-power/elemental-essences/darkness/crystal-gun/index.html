The crystal gun is a weapon used by Mister F.

History

History[]

The Council of the Crystal King[]

As a way to prevent Lloyd from escaping after he infiltrated the Council of the Crystal King, Mister F shot him with the crystal gun while Lloyd was using Spinjitzu.

A Sinister Shadow[]

Lloyd tries to escape but is kicked by Mister F and shot with his crystal gun.

The Spider's Design[]

Lloyd uses his Oni Power on Harumi but is shot by Mister F's crystal gun.

The Coming of the King[]

During the Council of the Crystal King's pursuit after Lloyd, Mister F chases Lloyd while using his crystal gun.
