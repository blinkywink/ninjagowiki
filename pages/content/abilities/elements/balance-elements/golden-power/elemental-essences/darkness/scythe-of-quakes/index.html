The Scythe of Quakes was one of the four Golden Weapons used by the First Spinjitzu Master to create Ninjago. Years after the Oni's invasion, the Scythe of Quakes and the rest of the Golden Weapons were stolen from the Council of the Crystal King for a ritual, being corrupted into the Scythe of Destruction, and before then being purified by Cole and unified with the other Golden Weapons during the final prophecy of Quanish the Elder.

History

History[]

Creation[]

Along with the other three Golden Weapons, the Scythe of Quakes was created by the First Spinjitzu Master using gold that came from the Golden Peaks and was forged in the Temple of Light and subsequently used to create Ninjago. It is unknown if it had any role in fighting the Overlord.

Garmadon's banishment[]

Soon after the First Spinjitzu Master's death, one of his sons, Garmadon, attempted to steal and use the power of the four weapons for his own selfish ends. Garmadon's younger brother, Wu, defeated him and caused him to be banished to the Underworld. Wu then took the weapons and hid them in different places throughout Ninjago. The Scythe was hidden in the Caves of Despair, where Wu placed the Earth Dragon to guard it.

The Golden Weapon[]

Many years later, Wu's four ninja proteges, Kai, Cole, Zane, and Jay, rediscovered the Scythe. It was their first weapon gathered in their attempt to retrieve the four weapons from their hiding places and to keep out of the hands of Lord Garmadon. Although the ninja were charged not to use the Scythe, Kai used its power to provide them an escape route after the Earth Dragon woke up.

King of Shadows[]

The ninja were later ambushed at the Fire Temple, and the Scythe, along with the Shurikens of Ice and the Nunchucks of Lightning, was stolen by Samukai. The weapons were then taken to the Underworld, where Samukai fought Wu with the three weapons he stole. During their battle, Samukai used the Scythe to shatter the ground they stood on. After Samukai defeated Wu, he took up the fourth weapon, the Sword of Fire, which Wu had been fighting with. The powers of the four weapons served to destroy Samukai and create a vortex through space and time, allowing Lord Garmadon to escape his Underworld prison.

In defense of Ninjago[]

After Lord Garmadon escaped, the four ninja took up the weapons to use them in defense of Ninjago. Each ninja took the weapon that matched their elemental properties, so the Scythe was in the possession of Cole. The Earth Dragon, nicknamed Rocky, guarded it by serving Cole as a mount and pet.

Rise of the Snakes[]

During a three-hour video gaming marathon with the other ninja, Cole carelessly left the Scythe of Quakes on the floor, though used it to plug the TV in after Wu unplugged it.

Cole brought the Scythe of Quakes along with him when the ninja journeyed to Jamanakai Village to stop Lord Garmadon, though didn't use it as the threat turned out to be the dark lord's son, Lloyd.

Cole used the Scythe of Quakes during the tournament to determine who would be the Green Ninja. When the Sword of Fire went out of control, Cole used the Scythe to try and extinguish the flames.

When the Hypnobrai attacked Jamanakai Village, Cole brought the Scythe with him and used it in the battle against the snakes.

Home[]

Cole used the Scythe during training, though Zane briefly stole it to use in his own training.

Cole brought the Scythe with him to destroy Lloyd's treehouse fortress and used it to attack the ninja when he was turned against them by Skales.

Snakebit[]

After Rocky leaves for the Spirit Coves, Cole unlocks his weapon's ability to become a vehicle, the Tread Assault.

Day of the Great Devourer[]

When Pythor unleashed the Great Devourer, the only way to stop it was to give Garmadon all four weapons so that he could use them at once in one final blow to the giant snake. It was a victory for the ninja, but at the cost of losing the weapons.

Darkness Shall Rise[]

After this, Garmadon took the weapon to the Golden Peaks, where it was forged with the other three into the Mega Weapon, which was eventually destroyed by the same weapons used to create it in the past when Garmadon went back in time to stop Lloyd from becoming a ninja.

The Titanium Ninja[]

The Nindroids succeeded in getting the Weapons of Spinjitzu from Delta V and have burnt them into the Overlord's ultimate weapon. Later, the Golden Master started to destroy New Ninjago City with his newly formed mech—made out of the gold from the Weapons of Spinjitzu. However, the Nindroids were not the only ones to escape; the ninja have used their elemental shields, and have traveled out of space. The Golden Master gets frustrated and plans to defeat the ninja. He now performs his own Spinjitzu, trying to stop the ninja from going into the Temple of Fortitude. However, he fails. The Heroes of Ninjago put on armor to resist the Golden Power from him. He, somehow, grabs all the ninja except Zane. The White Ninja eventually destroys him and the Overlord weakening the power of the armor in the process.

Endings[]

Kai reforged the weapon and Nya held it until Cole returned to the Monastery of Spinjitzu. Nya was unable to use it because she was a Master of Water, not Earth, and the Scythe had been weakened by Zane's sacrifice. When Cole arrived, Nya gave it to him. It was rendered powerless following the Tornado of Creation.[3]

The Fall of the Monastery[]

The Scythe was in its hiding place in the secret compartment before Aspheera opened it and Vangelis takes it for himself.

Darkness Within[]

Vaneglis carried the Scythe back to the Crystal Temple. During the ritual, the Scythe and the other Golden Weapons were crystalised and powered-up, becoming the Weapons of Destruction, and with their powers, the Overlord took physical form.

The Coming of the King[]

After being bestowed with new powers by the Overlord, Vangelis is handed over the Scythe of Destruction. However, Lloyd knocked the Scythe of Destruction off of Vangelis' hands, cracking the central crystal until it was repaired by the Overlord. Vangelis recovered the scythe and chased Lloyd down with it.

Dragon Form[]

During the Final prophecy of Quanish the Elder, Vangelis used the scythe to briefly fight against Cole and Korgran, but was soon relieved of the weapon by Cole. Cole then channeled his Elemental Power of Earth into the scythe, purifying it of the Overlord's corruption, and repowering it.

Roots[]

The scythe was destroyed when the ninja combined the four Golden Weapons together, freeing Earth, which combined with the powers of the other Golden Weapons to form the Golden Ultra Dragon.

Abilities

Abilities[]

The Scythe of Quakes is strong enough to cut through stone. When its blade is planted in the ground, it can create earthquakes or fissures. It also formed Cole's Tread Assault.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The weapon was originally going to be a hammer called the Hammer of Quakes.[5]
  • In 2012, it was briefly renamed the Staff of Earth on the LEGO website.[citation needed]
  • The Scythe of Quakes is identical to the Staff of Light. It also has a recolored version called the Bone Scythe.
  • So far, there have been four versions of the Scythe of Quakes, but none truly replicate the one in the TV show.
    • However, the Scythe of Quakes in the weapon pack which was included in the 2022 Crystalized wave is by far the most accurate for the TV show's appearance.
  • In the show, its blade is a dragon face with gray/brown gems for eyes.
  • Up until just before its destruction, the Scythe was the only one of the Golden Weapons to not directly shoot out its respective element (instead created earth fissures from the ground).
    • This makes it the only Golden Weapon to control its respective element (the other weapons only released their respective elements), by creating earthquakes to defeat enemies.
      • There has only been one time when the Scythe of Quakes shot its element in Wrong Place, Wrong Time into the past while fighting Garmadon and his Mega Weapon before throwing it and the Golden Weapons in the space.
  • A common scene transition in the first two seasons (seen in "The Snake King", "Once Bitten, Twice Shy", "Double Trouble", "Ninjaball Run", "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master", and other episodes) involves two Scythes of Quakes moving across the screen, though they use the design from the sets.
  • The Scythe of Quakes' design is very similar to Cole's Jadeblade Weapon, likely intentionally.
  • In Shadow of Ronin, Garmadon in his Overlord form is armed with the Scythe of Quakes.
