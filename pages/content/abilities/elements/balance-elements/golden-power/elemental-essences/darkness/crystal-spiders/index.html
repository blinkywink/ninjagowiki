Overview

“Ah, Crystal Spiders. The only way to travel.”
— Pythor, "Kryptarium Prison Blues"

Crystal Spiders (also referred to as spider bombs) were mechanical spiders used by the Council of the Crystal King.

History

History[]

Bad Water![]

A crystal spider was spying the ninja stopping Miss Demeanor's plans to poison the city's water supply with chemicals.

Farewell the Sea[]

At the Ninjago Harbor, a spider secretly scanned the Police Commissioner on one of the containers shortly after the New Ninja stopped a Vengestone smuggling operation.

A Painful Promise[]

The spiders appear before the newly escaped Aspheera to recruit her for the Council of the Crystal King.

Ninjago City vs. Ninja[]

A Crystal Spider traveled to the Kingdom of Shintaro, where Vangelis was imprisoned until the spider arrived. The spider gave him a similar mask that he previously used as the Skull Sorcerer then showed him a projection of the Kabuki Mask.

Kryptarium Prison Blues[]

The spiders infiltrated Kryptarium Prison to free Pythor. After Pythor agreed to join the council, the spiders uses their beam to free him from prison and escape through a hole. During the escape, the Crystal Spiders carried Pythor to a crystal hovercraft as they attacked the ninja to prevent them from interfering. When Jay zapped one of the Crystal Spiders to stop Pythor, the spider rigged itself to explode, giving Pythor enough time to escape.

The Fifth Villain[]

Disguised as the Mechanic, Lloyd was met with a crystal spider and was invited to join the Council of the Crystal King by the Kabuki Mask. After accepting the offer, Lloyd was then told to follow the spider.

The Council of the Crystal King[]

Lloyd continued to follow the crystal spider at the Ninjago Metro until he arrived at a tunnel that would lead him to the Crystal Temple. However, another crystal spider recorded the ninja's fight with the real Mechanic, which was used to expose Lloyd to the Crystal Council.

The Spider's Design[]

Several Crystal Spiders were stored in one of the Crystal Temple's chambers as Harumi talked to Lloyd. Meanwhile, the Crystal Spider spied on the four ninja when they travel through the N line, in which Harumi triggered the spiders to detonate in an attempt to kill the ninja, though they survived in the BRTM-12.

The Fall of the Monastery[]

Dozens of Crystal Spiders infiltrated the hangar bay in the Monastery of Spinjitzu. They shot down the MiniPix and were set to detonate the entire Monastery after the Crystal Council left with the Golden Weapons, though everyone else were saved just in time.

After the Overlord's defeat[]

After the Overlord was defeated by the Golden Ultra Dragon, the Crystal Island was destroyed, and the Crystal Spiders inside were ultimately destroyed in the process.

Official description

Official description[]

Build a Spider with a Superpower![]

Mysterious spiders are lurking around NINJAGO® City. Have they been sent to spy on us, are they here to help, or are they… Well, just spiders?[1]

Day of Destruction![]

This is a crystal spider that works for the crystal king. There are many of them. They deliver messages to the generals and can explode and fire lasers in an emergency.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Scanner Vision: The spiders can scan people with their optics.
  • Audio/Visual Recording: The spiders have an in-built recorder which allows them to broadcast what they see.
  • Holographic Projection: The spiders are equipped with a holographic projector.
  • Umbrakinetic Vision: The spiders are capable of firing beams of Darkness from their optics to attack foes.
    • Umbrakinetic Cutting: The spiders can use their beams to cut through walls.
  • Superhuman Strength: The spiders are incredibly strong, with one carrying Pythor on its thorax.
  • Self-detonation: The spiders have a self-destruct mechanism, in which it is mostly used for special occasions and if used causes them to explode.
