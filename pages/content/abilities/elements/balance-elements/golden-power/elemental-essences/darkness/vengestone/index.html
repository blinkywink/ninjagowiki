Overview

“Vengestone's only practical purpose is to negate Elemental Power.”
— Wu, "Farewell the Sea"

Vengestone, also referred to as Glow Rocks,[5] is a substance that can disable Elemental Powers, making an effective containment measure against Elemental Masters, ghosts, and dragons.

History

History[]

Prior to the series[]

At some point in time, Vengestone came into existence, having the ability to cancel out the powers of an Elemental Master, as well as ghosts.

Rise of the Snakes[]

All of Nothing[]

When the Serpentine trapped Master Wu, the ninja, and Nya inside a cage made of Vengestone, Kai suggested they escape using their elemental powers, but Wu informed him that it's no use, since the rock wards off their elemental powers.

Tournament of Elements[]

Spellbound[]

After loading up the defeated Elemental Masters, minus Lloyd, Chen threw them in a cage made of Vengestone to prevent their escape.

The Forgotten Element[]

After their capture, each Elemental Master was placed in chains made of Vengestone as Chen used his Staff of Elements to steal their powers. Later on, after easily defeating Lloyd, Chen had the Green Ninja placed in Vengestone chains before stealing his elemental power.

Very soon after, Kai stole Chen's staff and destroyed the chains binding Lloyd with the powers of the staff.

The Day of the Dragon[]

Kapau and Chope used a net made of Vengestone to trap Kai and Skylor. They would then be chained in Vengestone and brought to the Crystal Caves on the island, where the ritual to become Anacondrai would commence. Despite being chained, Kai used Fire to break out while Skylor used her Anacondrai form to break out.

Possession[]

Stiix and Stones[]

Ronin attempted to trap the ninja in a net made of Vengestone but fails.

Skybound[]

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Public Enemy Number One[]

While pursuing Jay on the roller coaster, Ronin attempted to capture him by using a cannon that shoots out Vengestone nets but missed.

Enkrypted[]

After Lloyd tries to break the handcuffs, Noble mentions that they are made of Vengestone and can also stop Cole's ghost abilities.

My Dinner With Nadakhan[]

In order to prevent Jay from escaping, the Sky Pirates use a ball and chain made of Vengestone.

Wishmasters[]

After Lloyd and Cole were both trapped with a Vengestone ball and chain as well, Cole used his first wish from Nadakhan to make Vengestone strengthen their powers instead of canceling them. This worked, but the Vengestone restraints caused their powers to grow too much and become uncontrollable. Lloyd countered this by breaking their chains, freeing them.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

The Hands of Time[]

Pause and Effect[]

When the Vermillion captured Karlof to work for the Time Twins, they put him in handcuffs made of Vengestone to prevent him from using his powers.

Sons of Garmadon[]

Snake Jaguar[]

Cole, captured by the Sons of Garmadon, was placed in a cage made of Vengestone within the Sons of Garmadon's headquarters. He attempted to break through the cage with his Earth Punch, but he was unable to.

Hunted[]

Firstbourne[]

The Dragon Hunters used Vengestone chains to capture Zane, Jay, and Kai. The trio are placed in shackles that appear to be made from Vengestone as they are brought into Iron Baron's throne room blindfolded.

Iron & Stone[]

The ninja continue to remain bound in shackles that seem to be Vengestone as they are interrogated by the Dragon Hunters before being dragged through Dead's End to the Pit. While being dragged to the Pit, they saw a Fire Dragon chained in Vengestone that was being forced to create armor and weapons for the Dragon Hunters.

Radio Free Ninjago[]

The Dragon Hunters attempted to use Vengestone chains to capture Cole, Jay, and Kai but ultimately failed. However, Heavy Metal was able to capture the ninja using the chains.

Green Destiny[]

As the ninja continue to fight the Colossus, they used Vengestone chains to try and incapacitate it. However, the chains are ineffective, and they eventually run out of them. Kai jumps onto the Colossus and begins binding the chains together even tighter.

After Hunted[]

Harumi was tasked by the Overlord to gather Vengestone to create an army that would be immune to Elemental Powers. Although she had difficulty finding it at first, Harumi learned about the mines within Shintaro Mountain. Since then, Harumi paid Vangelis for the Vengestone that were mined by the Geckles and Munce.[6]

Prime Empire[]

The Prodigal Father[]

When Zane was captured by the Mechanic and his henchmen, he was wrapped around by Vengestone chains.

Master of the Mountain[]

Into the Dark[]

Geckles and Munce mined Vengestone deep within the Dungeons of Shintaro.

The Two Blades[]

The ninja were mining Vengestone and recognized it, leading them to understand why their powers aren't working.

Trial By Mino[]

Zane's Mino wore armor made of Vengestone. The Vengestone armor prevented Kai and Zane from using their powers, but Zane managed to remove it.

The Upply Strike Back! through The Son of Lilly[]

Vangelis had the ninja locked in a cage made of Vengestone. However, they were freed by Plundar. The large quantity of Vengestone present in the Skull Keep prevented the ninja from using their powers in the ensuing battle against Vangelis and the Re-Awakened.

The Virtues of Spinjitzu[]

Honesty[]

Cole, Lloyd, Nya, and Zane were trapped in a Vengestone cage.

Generosity[]

The ninja fought in the Mines of Shintaro, which contained Vengestone.

Seabound[]

A Big Splash[]

Miss Demeanor runs a Vengestone smuggling operation with several Henchmen. She escaped the Ninja after losing some Vengestone. Master Wu and the Ninja were confused about why Miss Demeanor would smuggle Vengestone because the rocks don’t have many practical uses.

Crystalized[]

Farewell the Sea[]

The New Ninja attempted to stop a Vengestone smuggling operation carried out by Miss Demeanor, but the latter was able to escape.

The Shape of Nya[]

Jay theorized that the Vengestone might be taken through the N line. When the ninja made their way to the N line, they encountered another vengestone seller as well as Miss Demeanor and her henchmen. In the ensuing fight, Miss Demeanor and her goons momentarily gained the upper hand due to the ninja's powers being disabled by the Vengestone.

The Council of the Crystal King[]

After arriving in the Crystal Island under the guise of the Mechanic, Lloyd walked through a chamber containing countless statues sculpted from Vengestone. As he examined the sculptures, Lloyd accidentally bumped into one, knocking down an entire row of them before hurriedly rushing away to the next chamber.

A Sinister Shadow[]

As Harumi explains to Lloyd how she returned and her plans, the latter is shackled with Vengestone cuffs. Although he manages to get the key from Harumi and get the cuffs off, his powers are still negated by the large quantity of Vengestone in the temple.

The Spider's Design[]

Lloyd was imprisoned inside a Vengestone cage that is suspended over a pit of lava in the Crystal Temple's control center. However, he was able to free himself by tapping into his dormant power of Destruction after Harumi kept taunting him over her action of seemingly killing the ninja with a series of Crystal Spiders.

Darkness Within[]

Lloyd was moved to the Central crystal's chamber and suspended from the ceiling by Vengestone chains.

The Coming of the King[]

After exchanging spats with the Crystal King, Lloyd freed himself from his chains and began fleeing through the halls of the Crystal Temple. Although Vangelis took off in pursuit, he was momentarily struck down by a falling Vengestone statue.

Moments later, the Crystal King entered the hall of Vengestone statues and imbued them with Darkness, bringing them to life as his Vengestone Guards. One of these guards would ambush Lloyd just outside of the temple gates and pursued him out of the temple. However, the Guard failed to anticipate the slope at the entrance and slid all the way down to its doom into the jungle of Primeval's Eye.

Crystastrophe[]

Vengestone Guards and Dragonides were deployed to attack the Farmers' village. Although the ninja fought back against them, they discovered to their annoyance that their powers were nullified by the Guards' vengestone bodies, thus greatly limiting their ability to fight back. Ultimately, the Guards were able to successfully overrun the village after the ninja retreated with a broken shard of crystal.

Christofern[]

Believing that Christofern was destroyed, Garmadon used his Oni Form to attack the Crystal Warriors, destroying them with his Destruction. As Wu saw this, he realized that the power of the Oni can surpass Vengestone, and told Lloyd that he needed to unlock his Oni side to defeat the Vengestone army.

Safe Haven[]

Pythor and several Crystal Warriors found Kai and an unconscious Cole. Skylor arrived and used both of their powers to create an Earth wall. Pythor order his warriors to break the wall but found out they already left.

Dragon Form[]

During the final battle, the Crystal Warriors used their Vengestone capabilities to block the ninja's powers as they got cornered them. But when the ninja's allies arrived, the ninja were able to unlock Dragon Form, using it to destroy the Vengestone Guards, and thus destroying all the Vengestone that Harumi collected since the Liberation of Ninjago.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

The new master of Wind, Euphrasia, was held prisoner by the Wolf Clan due to a medallion made of Vengestone so she couldn't use her powers. Jordana stated that it had been stored in the Cloud Kingdom archives.

The Forest of Spirits[]

Euphrasia continued wearing the Vengestone medallion.

Rising Ninja[]

After being freed by Wyldfyre, Euphrasia took off the medallion.

Elements of Betrayal[]

After Cinder and the Wolf Mask Warriors were defeated, they and Bleckt were put into Vengestone cages.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Missing[]

When Kai breaks out of the Lightning-Tides's cage and starts blasting fire at the Lightning-Tides, the Tides throw Vengestone at Kai and put him back into the cell.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Superpower Negation - Vengestone negates some superpowers of those who are chained to or in the presence of it.
    • Elemental Power Negation - If Elemental Masters are in the presence of vengestone their elemental powers are disabled.
    • Intangibility Cancellation - If ghosts are in the presence of vengestone their ability to become Intangible is disabled.
    • Magic Negation - Magic too can be disabled by vengestone.
  • Elemental Power Reduction - Vengestone can weaken the elemental powers of dragons that have them in the presence of it.
  • Elemental Power Augmentation (in an alternate timeline) - Cole made a wish for it to upgrade the Elemental Masters' powers and it made them stronger or more enhanced. This ability was erased due to Jay's final wish.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Dragons - Unlike with elemental masters, the elemental powers of dragons who possess them are only weakened, not fully negated by vengestone.
  • Darkness - Since Darkness isn't a true element, it's unaffected by vengestone.
  • Destruction - Destruction also isn't a true element so it's unaffected by vengestone and can even destroy it.
  • Dragon Form - The powers of elemental masters who can use it are unaffected by vengestone if they enter Dragon Form.
  • Elemental Monsters - The elemental powers of Elemental Monsters are unaffected by vengestone.[7]
  • Augmentation overload (in an alternate timeline) - As a side effect of Cole's wish, Vengestone increased the potency of Elemental powers to an uncontrollable level.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Vengestone looks like regular stone, except it has marks around it with a yellow or orange glow. From Rise of the Snakes to The Hands of Time, Vengestone did not have yellow glows around it, making it harder to recognize. In Sons of Garmadon and Hunted, the glows were orange and since Prime Empire, the markings are yellow.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Vengestone does not keep dragons from using their powers. Its only effect on the dragons seem to be that it is strong enough to keep them from escaping, or it may weaken their powers.
    • The same can be said for how Vengestone affects Ghosts since it only weakens them instead of destroying them.
  • Shintaro Mountain contains mines full of Vengestone.
  • Vengestone is similar and seems inspired from Seastone in One Piece, since Seastone can nullify Devil fruit powers, just like Elemental Powers with Vengestone.
    • The only difference is that Seastone nullifies Devil fruit users instead of Devil fruit powers, rendering them unable to move or use their powers willingly.
  • It is one of the few materials available in multiple realms.
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, Vengestone is likely "a[n] unhealthy by-product of some cataclysmic event."[8]
    • According to Tommy Andreasen, he theorized that Vengestone was most likely formed when the First Spinjitzu Master tried to get rid of his Oni side and inadvertently created the Overlord, who then saturated half of Ninjago.
  • Despite the fact that Vengestone is called in the Czech dubbing of all other seasons the same way as in the original, only in the Czech language, in Seasons 6 and 7, it is called the "antielemental stone".
  • Although Vengestone seems common enough, seeing that most of the villains the ninja have fought use Vengestone, in "The Skull Sorcerer," King Vangelis claims that he's been selling Vengestone from the mines to Harumi, thus making the availability of the substance quite ambiguous.
    • In "A Sinister Shadow," Harumi also claimed that Vengestone is difficult to obtain, and it took her some time to find it before learning about the Vengestone mines.
      • It is possible that she meant it is difficult to find such large quantities of Vengestone, given that she needed to construct an entire army from it.
  • In "Farewell the Sea," Wu states that Vengestone's only practical purpose is to negate Elemental Power.
  • In 71719 Zane's Mino Creature, the Vengestone armor on Zane's Mino is transparent blue instead of transparent orange.
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, Vengestone would "most likely not" block an Oni's power.[9]
    • This is seen when Lloyd uses his Oni power to break out of the Vengestone cage that Harumi put him in.
  • Despite the fact that Vengestone suppresses elemental powers, Kai was able to use fire to melt together Vengestone chains to immobilize the Colossus in "Green Destiny." The same happened in "The Day of the Dragon" when Kai used Fire to melt the Vengestone chains he and Skylor were chained to.
  • A tiny amount of Vengestone can disable an Elemental Master's powers.[10]
  • In the German dub, Vengestone and Deepstone are no longer distinguished since Season 6: Skybound and both are simply referred to as Tiefenstein ("Deepstone").
    • In "All of Nothing", Vengestone is still translated correctly as Rachestein.
