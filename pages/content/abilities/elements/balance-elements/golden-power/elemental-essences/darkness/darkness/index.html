Overview

This article is about the elemental essence. For other uses, see Shadow (disambiguation).
“So you know about how the First Spinjitzu Master created Ninjago, but what if I were to tell you in order for there to be light, there must be shadow, and within shadow, there is darkness. The blackest of darkness that existed from the very beginning.”
— Misako to Lloyd, "The Stone Army"

Darkness (also referred to as Shadow and Dark) is an elemental essence in the Merged Realms.[3] It is not derived from the Source Dragons,[1] but rather it is indirectly linked to them.[4] It is closely related to Destruction,[5][6] it feeds on Creation to grow,[7] and is the dark counterpart of Golden Power. It is associated with the colors lavender, pink, and black.

Notable uses

Notable uses[]

The Stone Army[]

According to Misako, Darkness came about as a natural counterbalance to Light, with the Overlord as its main manifestation. When the First Spinjitzu Master separated Ninjago into two halves, most of the Darkness (including the Overlord) were trapped on what would come to be known as the Island of Darkness.

Dark Island Trilogy[]

Clouse attempted to use the Dark Matter and Darkness to reunite Ninjago into a single landmass that he would control.

Crystalized[]

When the Overlord resurfaced as the Crystal King, he manifested his Dark powers in purple crystals with the capability of providing the user with enhanced strength,[8] bestowing them the elemental essence of Darkness,[9] or corrupting adversaries.[10]

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Umbrakinesis - It allows the user to manipulate, control, perceive, generate, and shape darkness, including shadows.
    • Umbrakinetic Flight/Levitation - It allows the user to propel themselves up with darkness. As seen in "Return of the Overlord," the Overlord used this ability to combat Lloyd Garmadon by using it as an advantage to quickly head back into the battle. In his spirit and Crystal King form, the Overlord was able to float freely, allowing him to travel on the Island of Darkness and the Crystal Island.
    • Slime/Mud Creation - The Overlord's essence is said to have created a dark slimy mud-like substance known as Dark Matter.
      • Corruption & Insanity/Violence/Anger Inducement - Dark Matter can corrupt those who come in contact with it and drive them insane with violent rage.
    • Conflict Empowerment - As stated by Wu in "Crystastrophe", users like the Overlord can grow stronger through conflict.
      • Self-Resurrection/Retroactive Immortality - Users like the Overlord can possibly revive themselves upon their destruction, making them immortal in a sense that even if they do die, they won't stay dead permanently.
      • Supernatural Regeneration/Body Recreation/Reforming - As stated by Wu in "Crystastrophe", when users like the Overlord grow stronger through conflict, it allows them to regenerate/recreate their physical form drastically.
    • Materialization/Animation - The user can use their dark power to create and give life to inanimate statues. The Overlord used this ability to create his own Stone Army out of materials from the Dark Island and later bring the Crystal Warriors to life using the Infectious crystals.
    • Absolute Darkness - It allows the user to create a field of absolute impenetrable darkness and possibly spread darkness everywhere. As seen in "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master," the Overlord (in Dragon form) trapped Lloyd inside a sphere of darkness.
      • Darkness Empowerment - The user can possibly grow stronger through darkness.
      • Umbrakinetic Breath - Only displayed by the Overlord, the user can breathe clouds of darkness and shadow from their mouth to either attack or corrupt people.
        • Corruption Breath - Gives the user the ability to corrupt people and their environment with their breath (as displayed by the Garmatron and the Overlord's dark breath attack). Symptoms of corruption include the destruction of morality, glowing purple eyes, and sickly pale gray skin.
        • Toxic/Acid/Slime Spit - The Overlord's darkness breath can sometimes appear as a poisonous/acidic slime, as shown in "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master" when it landed on the ground and fizzled if it did not hit its target.
      • Tentacle Creation/Extension - Allows the user to create tentacles of darkness which they can extend to incredible lengths. In "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master", the Overlord creates the tentacles to collect energy so he and the Stone Army can invade Ninjago.
    • Umbra-Ergokinesis - The user can create and manipulate a dark form of energy.
      • Umbra-Pyrokinesis - The user can create and manipulate dark fire.
      • Umbrakinetic Beam Emission - Only displayed by the Overlord, the user can project beams of darkness, usually to attack an adversary.
      • Umbrakinetic Ball Projection - The user can generate balls of darkness, commonly used to trap or attack an adversary.
      • Dark Shield Construction - The user can create shields of varying shapes and sizes out of darkness/shadows. The Overlord used this in "Brave But Foolish" to block Wu's sword attack.
    • Umbra-Electrokinesis - The user can create and manipulate dark electricity.
      • Lightning Bolt Projection - As shown by the the Overlord, the user can shoot destructive lightning bolts to attack an enemy.
      • Crystallokinesis - With their dark lightning, the user can create, generate, become, and manipulate dark crystals. They can even move them in space, make them grow out in clusters, and use them as arrows to attack an adversary. It can also possibly be used to surf on crystals and become immune to time, wind, suction, lava, or parasites.
        • Strength Bestowal - The crystals created are able to bestow superhuman strength to those who use them as shown by Harumi, who was able to move massive chunks of debris out of the way after using the crystal's power.
        • Crystal Telekinesis - The user can use crystals to control, move, or manipulate objects.
        • Crystallization/Zombification - The user is able to crystallize anyone or anything by turning them into crystal zombies.
        • Crystal Shard Projectiles - As shown by the Overlord, the user can create dark crystal shards and launch them at his opponents.
          • Crystal Bomb Generation - The user can create crystal shards that explode into lightning.
        • Crystal Constructs - The user can create objects and other structures out of dark crystals (blades, bludgeons, pillars, walls, etc).
          • Crystal Spike Creation - As seen with the Overlord when he was fighting Wu, the user can create spikes of dark crystal from the ground.
          • Crystal Shield Construction - The user can create shields of varying shapes and sizes out of crystal.
          • Crystal Wall Creation - The user can possibly create walls out of crystal.
          • Crystal Imprisonment - The user can possibly imprison opponents in crystal.
        • Crystal/Metal Armor & Power Bestowal/Augmentation - In "The Coming of the King," the Overlord shoots dark lightning at the central crystal that would spread upon the Crystal Council, bestowing them with crystal-encrusted metal armor and new powers, and even upgrading their already-existing power and already-equipped gear (e.g. the Mechanic's Robo-arm).
          • Biological/Body Alteration & Crystalline Shapeshifting - In Aspheera's case, the Overlord was able to alter her biology/body when enhancing her power, turning her scales dark gray and forming crystalline structures on her hood.
          • Conversion Immunity - Despite their armor and parts of their body being covered in infectious crystals in their empowered forms, the members of the Crystal Council appear to be immune to their abilities to turn them into crystal zombies.
        • Crystal Blast - The user can release crystals over a specific target area, causing great damage and/or delivering great shock waves of pure force.
        • Crystal Beam Emission - As seen during the Overlord's battle with Wu, the user can likely fire beams of crystal.
        • Crystal Wave Emission - The user can release waves, spirals or rings of crystals, causing damage by the hit or the cut, or even imprison the target.
      • Control over the Crystal Island - By using the central crystal, the Overlord also used his dark lightning to control the Crystal Island.
        • Electricity Immunity - As seen in "Brave But Foolish", the Overlord appears to be immune to his own dark electricity while he was charging up the central crystal on the Crystal Island.
      • Electricity Empowerment - As seen with the Overlord, the user can empower themselves with dark electricity.
    • Metamorphosis - With this ability, the user can undergo a metamorphic transformation. Depending on what they change into, they would gain beastly traits, such as sharp/extendable claws and feet, scales, a rotating head, expanded view, enhanced/telescopic vision, strong/sharp teeth, etc. The Overlord was able to slowly change Garmadon's body into a giant dragon (which was his original form) in "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master" and his own body into a huge Centaur warrior in "Dragon Form".
      • Crystal Mimicry - When the Overlord metamorphosed into his centaur form in "Dragon Form", part of his body appears to have transformed into crystal.
    • Portal Creation - As shown in "Return of the Overlord", the user can open portals to travel to different places. The Overlord used this to transport himself and his army to Ninjago City.
    • Possession/Mutation - The user can possess and mutate their targets. As stated by the Overlord, he possessed the Great Devourer, causing a mutation that turned a little green serpent into an all-devouring monstrosity. This ability also extends to objects and the undead.
      • Consciousness Transferal - It is often useful for transferring their consciousness, ventriloquism, and moving around digital realms or controlling its functions. If the user is strong enough, they can possibly suppress or remove the victims' consciousness for a long period of time.
      • Remote Possession - The user can possess another being from a distance, as seen when the Overlord possessed a Nindroid at the Tomb of the Stone Army in "The Curse of the Golden Master".
      • Machine/Vehicle Possession - The user can possess machines or vehicles, as seen when the Overlord possessed the MechDragon.
    • Vengestone Immunity - Darkness can't be affected by Vengestone, as the Overlord was surrounded by it but his powers still worked and the Crystal Council was still able to use their powers.
    • Resurrection - The user can resurrect souls from the Departed Realm though it is unknown if it only works on newly-departed souls or ones that have been departed for a while. The Overlord used this shortly after Harumi's death when she vowed to serve him.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Darkness is one of the two strongest powers across the realms and the malevolent side of the Balance alongside its benevolent counterpart Golden Power.
  • It is nearly identical to Destruction even though they are separate.
    • When asked if Darkness and Destruction are the same, Tommy Andreasen said, "Can’t get into that. Too complicated.[11] Bragi Schut said, "They are different in my opinion. The Oni power of destruction is more of an opposite to the dragon power of creation. The overlord is an embodiment of evil and darkness."[12] During Crystalized, it is heavily implied that Destruction and Darkness are closely tied to each other, as the Overlord stated that Lloyd and Garmadon's powers were a gift from him.[13]
      • In 2024, Chris "Doc" Wyatt confirmed that Darkness is related to Destruction.[6]
      • While Destruction is nearly identical to Darkness, The Book of Elemental Powers states that Darkness is a twisted and warped incarnation of Creation that uses the essence to manifest evil while Destruction is the polar opposite of Creation that obliterates all in its path.
      • Darkness appears to be a raw manifestation of the Balance and isn't directly linked to Destruction or Creation, rather, it is a more abstract and far more malevolent force; the embodiment of evil and shadows.
      • Darkness has a weakness to Destruction even greater than that of its weakness to Golden Power, to the point that Destruction is capable of fully destroying it, unlike Golden Power, which merely temporarily extinguishes its power.
  • When asked what the Overlord's element was, Tommy Andreasen said, "Probably darkness."[14] The words "darkness" and "shadow" are used both in a literal and metaphorical sense, such as the many times when "darkness" is mentioned in the series, and as a proper name for the force of power connected to the Overlord, such as when "Darkness" is written in the Dark Island Trilogy and The Book of Spinjitzu.
  • Darkness appears to have the same ability as Dark Matter, being able to corrupting people. Dark Matter turned Nya evil in "Return of the Overlord" and the Overlord's Darkness attack did the same thing to Cole, Jay, and Zane "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master."
    • Due to the fact that Dark Matter is "pure concentrated evil," it is likely that Darkness is a manifestation of evil itself as well.
  • In Tomb of the Fangpyre, it is implied that the Overlord was responsible for creating the Great Devourer by corrupting a snake with darkness.[15] Years after the novel was released, this theory was officially confirmed in "Dragon Form," in which the Overlord revealed that he corrupted the Devourer so that it would bite Garmadon, making other catastrophic events happen because of it.
    • This was also foreshadowed in The Book of Elemental Powers, which states that "Darkness feeds on Creation", similar to how the Great Devourer was repeatedly said "to consume all" in Rise of the Snakes.
  • Since it doesn’t originate from any Source Dragon or Thunderfang, it is the only element that does not stem from any form of dragon energy.
