Overview

Crystal weapons[1] were weapons used by the crystal army. They were imbued with the Overlord's power of Darkness through the process of crystalization.

History

History[]

The Council of the Crystal King[]

When Lloyd is escaping from the Council of the Crystal King once his ruse as the Mechanic is exposed, Mister F blasts him with his crystal gun while he's using Spinjitzu.

A Sinister Shadow[]

Mister F produced his crystal gun and fired a warning shot at Lloyd in an effort to subdue him when the Green Ninja attempted to escape.

The Spider's Design[]

Mister F would once again use his crystal gun to subdue Lloyd right as the latter prepared to kill Harumi.

Darkness Within[]

The Council of the Crystal King brought the stolen Golden Weapons to release their master. During the ritual, the powerless weapons were infused with the Crystal King's Darkness and transformed into the Weapons of Destruction.

The Coming of the King[]

The Crystal King bestowed the Weapons of Destruction upon his Council, who would then utilize them in battle against Lloyd as he tried to escape from the Crystal Temple. During their pursuit, the Council discovered the hard way that they could not allow the weapons to come into contact with one another, lest resulting in a small but powerful explosion.

Return to Primeval's Eye[]

The Council of the Crystal King carried their respective Weapons of Destruction as they searched the jungle for any signs of Lloyd and Harumi. When Aspheera confronted Wu onboard Destiny's Bounty, the latter recognized her weapon as being the Sword of Fire, albeit crystallized.

Crystastrophe[]

Vengestone Guards carrying crystal spears were deployed to attack the Farmers' village. They used the spears to combat the Ninja and transform several of the local populace into Crystal zombies. In the ensuing battle however, one spear was broken and collected by the Ninja who then retreated with it to study its material to better combat their new foes.

Christofern[]

The Crystal Warriors wielded their crystal spears as they stormed Ninjago City. After being attacked by Garmadon, the warriors fought back using their spears, though the energy blasts they channeled proved to be ineffective against Garmadon's Oni form. During the course of battle, one spear was taken by Garmadon who then hurled it into another Vengestone Guard and shattered it upon impact.

A Lesson in Anger[]

The ninja fought against several Vengestone Guards wielding crystal spears as they retreated from the farmer's village.

Brave But Foolish[]

Vengestone Guards wielded crystal spears as their forces stormed across Ninjago City. Several of these spears would be destroyed, however, thanks to the efforts of the ninja and their vehicles' upgraded weapons and shielding.

Quittin' Time![]

The Mechanic's platoon of Vengestone Guards wielded their spears against Jay and Nya, though several were destroyed by Jay's Golden Dragon Motorbike. The Mechanic also attempted to use his Shuriken of Destruction against the pair, but it was deflected back at him by Jay's Spinjitzu and landed beneath his feet where it exploded into a burst of crystals, knocking him and several of his warriors out cold.

Another group of Vengestone Guards used their crystal spears in an attempt to corrupt two civilians, but they were fought off by Wu and the Paperboys.

Return of the Ice Emperor[]

Vengestone Guards carried crystal spears throughout their patrol of Ninjago City. A pair of guards fought against P.I.X.A.L. with their spears, as did a larger group she encountered later after recovering Zane's damaged body.

Safe Haven[]

Pythor wielded the Nunchucks of Destruction in combat against Kai, Cole, and Skylor, using the weapons to deflect the fireballs hurled by Kai and Skylor. He was also accompanied by his platoon of Vengestone Guards, all of whom carried crystal spears but did not engage in battle with the elemental masters.

An Issue of Trust[]

The Council of the Crystal King carried the Weapons of Destruction when they were summoned before the Crystal King's throne to report on their failures at finding the ninja. They would later wield these weapons in battle against the ninja and the Resistance outside the Newspaper warehouse, alongside the entire garrison of Vengestone Guards and their crystal spears.

Harumi also carried a crystal katana stored alongside her back throughout the aforementioned meeting and during her observation of the battle on the ground.

Dragon Form[]

During his battle with Lloyd and Garmadon, the Overlord transforms into a large centaur and equipped himself with a large crystal sword, in which he used to destroy Lloyd's mech. Meanwhile, the Council of the Crystal King use the Weapons of Destruction to fight against the Resistance, but lost possession of them after the ninja defeated them. As for the remaining Vengestone Guards, they and their spears were destroyed after the ninja achieved Dragon Form and purified the Weapons of Destruction.

Elsewhere, Harumi wielded her crystal katana against the Crystal King, having turned against him after learning that he was responsible for corrupting the Great Devourer, and by extension, killing her parents. With her blade, Harumi managed to slice off one of the Crystal King's hands.

Roots[]

Harumi continued to wield her crystal katana in battle against the Crystal King, but lost it after being struck down by the King. It was presumably destroyed later when the Crystal Island was destroyed.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • With the Golden Weapons bring turned into crystal weapons in "Darkness Within," this is the third time that the Golden Weapons have been transformed into something else by the villains for their plans. The first two instances were the Mega Weapon (used by Lord Garmadon) and the Golden Armor (used by the Overlord as the Golden Master).
    • Unlike their predecessors, the Weapons of Destruction are the first not to possess Creation nor Golden Power.
      • They're also the first not to be an amalgamation.
