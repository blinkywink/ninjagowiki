Overview

“When the name of time was still new, there was a war in the First Realm of Creation, a war between dragons and their mortal enemies.”
— Rox, "The Missing"

The War of Oni and Dragon[1] was a war in the First Realm between the two dominant species, the Oni and the Dragons. The First Spinjitzu Master was born during this war. When he saw that neither side seemed intended on ending it, he decided to escape from his realm.

History

History[]

In the beginning, the First Realm was inhabited by Oni, demons with the power of Destruction, and Dragons, majestic reptilian creatures with the power of Creation. Being sheer opposites, the two sides hated each other, causing chaos and war in the First Land. One day, a child born of both worlds approached them both to end their war because he understood both sides, however, they each wanted the child as their own, so they fought harder.[2] During this time, the child befriended a dragon called Firstbourne.[3] Together they forged the Dragon Armor and sought to unite the realm. However, the child saw there would never be peace and left the First Land in hopes of finding a new home. He found one realm that contained the Golden Peaks and the Temple of Light which resulted in the creation of the four Golden Weapons of Spinjitzu which he used to create Ninjago.[2]
