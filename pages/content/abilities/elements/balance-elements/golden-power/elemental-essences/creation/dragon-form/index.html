Overview

This article is about the ability. For other uses, see Dragon Form (disambiguation).
“Hopefully not entirely, for he prophesied that one day, the defenders of Ninjago would achieve a higher form of Elemental Energy. Something he called "Dragon Form," which they would use in the final conflict against evil.”
— Wu, "An Issue of Trust"

Dragon Form is an ability that can be used by any Elemental Master that possessed one of the four elements of Creation or an individual who has Dragon blood,[1] turning them into a humanoid Dragon.

History

History[]

Dragon Form[]

The ninja and Wu realize that the prophecy of Quanish the Elder has become true and the four ninja preform Dragon Form successfully. They fight and charge against the Crystal army and reverted the Weapons of Destruction back to the Weapons of Spinjitzu and defeated the members of the Council of the Crystal King.

Roots[]

The ninja channel their Elemental Powers and Golden Weapons to create the Golden Ultra Dragon which causes them to lose their Dragon Forms and became crystal zombies briefly.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Humanoid Dragon Physiology - Users of Dragon Form become humanoid dragons.
    • Wing Manifestation - Users of Dragon Form gain wings on their back.
      • Flight - Using said wings, users of Dragon Form are able to fly.
        • High-Speed Flight - As seen with Zane when he attacked Mister F, users of Dragon Form can fly at high speeds.
      • Wing Barriers - As seen with Kai when he was battling Aspheera, users of Dragon Form can use their wings as shields.
    • Vengestone immunity - In Dragon Form, Elemental Masters' powers are unaffected by Vengestone.
    • Infectious crystal immunity - User of Dragon Form are able to channel their elemental energy to the weapon but can still be infected the infectious crystals.
    • Enhanced Elemental Powers - Users of Dragon Form have their Elemental Powers heavily enhanced.
    • Purification - Users of Dragon Form can undo corruption from the infectious crystals.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Dragon Form has a similar yet opposite counterpart called Oni Form.
    • Unlike Oni Form, which only works for beings who have Destruction or are part-Oni due to their Oni blood, Dragon Form works for those who have one of the four main elements or at least have dragon blood.[1]
      • The fact that Dragon Form can also work for those who have dragon blood was first implied in "An Issue of Trust," where Lloyd was trying to unlock this ability alongside the ninja, despite no longer being a user of the four elements of Creation, and then by Chris "Doc" Wyatt on a Bluesky post.[2]
  • The Dragon Form is ultimately achieved by "jumping up, kicking back, whipping around, and spinning". This is a reference to the chorus of The Weekend Whip.
    • The importance of the song was first at hinted by Tommy Andreasen's Two Truths, One Lie post for Crystalized.[3]
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, Lloyd while actively in Dragon Form is susceptible to Wasting Sickness.[2]
    • This ultimately confirms Lloyd can access Dragon Form.
      • Although this doesn't elaborate on whether his Dragon Form can be access naturally, or whether it requires the usage of the "jump up, kick back, whip around, and spin" incantation that was used by the four Ninja.
      • Since Lloyd is confirmed to be able to access this power, this means that Wu and Garmadon can as well since they also have Dragon blood.
        • When asked if Lloyd and Wu's dragon forms would be humanoid like the original 4, or if they would be actual dragons, Chris "Doc" Wyatt replied, "Depends".[4]
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, Dragon Form and Oni Form are equally matched. The strength of each depends on the proficiency of the user.[5]
  • In the unreleased Greatest Battles, Lloyd speaks about achieving a "Draconus Form" and depicts him as well as Kai, Zane and Cole in humanoid dragon forms, tough without the golden coloring of Creation. It is unknown if this Draconus Form is the same as Dragon Form or just a similar but different ability.
