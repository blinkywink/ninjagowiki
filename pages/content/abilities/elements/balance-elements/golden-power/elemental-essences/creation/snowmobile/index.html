“Let us blow this popsicle stand!”
— Zane, "Can of Worms"

The Snowmobile was a ninja vehicle owned by Zane. It resembles a blue and white motorcycle with ice. The Snowmobile can be summoned by focusing one's mind on the Shurikens of Ice.

History

History[]

Snakebit[]

Zane transformed his Shurikens of Ice into the Snowmobile. However, when Jay lost his concentration, he, Kai, and Cole also lost their concentration when they argued who wanted to catch Jay first.

Can of Worms[]

Zane and Cole took their vehicles to the Constrictai Tomb to the highest mountain in Ninjago. Later, in the sewers, when they were escaping the Serpentine, Zane made his Snowmobile and the ninja rode on it.

Tick Tock[]

Zane summons the Snowmobile in his battle against the Juggernaut.

The Rise of the Great Devourer[]

Zane used his Snowmobile to catch up to the Serpentine Express as it traveled to the tomb of the Great Devourer.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In the Character Encyclopedia, it's referred to as the Ice Scooter.
  • It's the only Golden Weapon vehicle to not have a set of its own, in both the original and Legacy waves.
  • It's also the smallest Golden Weapon vehicle presumably because the Shurikens of Ice are the smallest.
