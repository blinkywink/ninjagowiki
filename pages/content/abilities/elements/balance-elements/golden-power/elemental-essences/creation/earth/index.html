Overview

“The solid ground beneath our feet, a mountain rising up to challenge the sky.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Earth is one of the four main elements of Creation, and is derived from the Source Dragon of Strength.[1] It is associated with the colors black, brown, green, or orange and corresponds with the Scythe of Quakes, the Earth Dragon, and the Golden Ultra Dragon.

Notable uses

Notable uses[]

Spellbound[]

During a battle between the Elemental Alliance and the Anacondrai, Benton was manipulated by Chen to turn against his own and create a large rift in the earth that disoriented the members of the alliance.

The Greatest Fear of All[]

When the ninja break out of Kryptarium Prison, Cole uses his quakes (Earthquake Generation) to smash through the walls.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Geokinesis/Terrakinesis - The user is able to manipulate, soften, and generate earth and other earth-related substances (dirt, mud, sand, etc).
    • Geokinetic Attacks - The user can attack using Earth.
      • Geokinetic Beam Emission - The user can fire beams of earth.
      • Geokinetic Breath - Earth Dragons can expel earth and likely other earth-related substances (lava, crystal, etc.) from their mouth.
      • Geokinetic Ball Projection - The user can generate balls of earth.
      • Geokinetic Pillar Projection - The user can create pillars of earth.
      • Geokinetic Wall Creation - The user can make a wall completely made from earth to block or stop incoming attacks. They can also use the wall to attack enemies by cutting through them.
    • Tunneling - The user can use earth to tunnel underground as seen in "Land of Lost Things" when Cole made a tunnel for him and his friends to use to evade the Hoarder.
    • Wall Climbing/Surface Scaling - Some users can move effortlessly on walls and scale surfaces.
    • Geo-Telekinesis - The user can use earth to move and manipulate objects.
    • Geokinetic Imprisonment - The user can bind, imprison, and/or otherwise stop an object/being by surrounding them completely by using earth.
      • Immobilization - The users can immobilize enemies by covering them in a layer of earth, as shown when a Stone Warrior got trapped by Lloyd.
    • Lithokinesis - Users of this element can manipulate rocks and other minerals in many ways, like making pillars of rock erupt from the ground.
    • Tectokinesis - Gives the user (possibly only a very powerful one) the ability to move tectonic plates, allowing them to create fractures and chasm (as displayed by Garmadon's Mega Weapon).
      • Earthquake Generation - It can be used to cause earthquakes, allowing the user to create shaking tremors along surfaces (walls, ceilings, etc.) that creates dust and dust particles to reveal a hidden enemy or rupture the ground by pounding it. Lloyd used this to raise a portion of the ground in "The Day Ninjago Stood Still".
        • Fissure Creation - The user can create fissures to trap opponents or destabilize objects that have been implanted in the ground as seen in "Roots".
      • Seismogenesis - The user has the ability to create giant shockwaves.
    • Superhuman Strength - Some users of Earth have demonstrated the ability to possess superhuman strength. Even with his powers disabled, Cole was still able to lift a large rock to stop a kethanol geyser in the Caves of Despair, although he was unable to break out of Vengestone cuffs.
      • Geokinetic Strength - Users like Cole can likely enhance their strength using Earth.
    • Superhuman Durability - Some Elemental Masters of Earth are able to survive and withstand attacks better than an average person.
    • Touvlokinesis - The user can manipulate and generate bricks, likely to create brick pillars or to trap opponents. Cole used this in "The Greatest Fear of All" to tear off a large chunk of brick and throw it at an Anacondrai Cultist in Kryptarium Prison.
    • Geokinetic Infusion - The user can infuse objects (usually a weapon), beings, or powers with earthen materials, empowering and energizing them and allowing the user to manipulate their qualities and efficiency, increasing their concussive force and mass, etc. Cole was able create a fissure and shockwave from his hammer to fight off the Stone Guardians.
    • Geokinetic Constructs - The user can create different objects out of earth. Cole used this in "Force From the East" to create a rock for Geo to sit on and later in "Clues and Suspects" through an Elemental Earth Mech to build an Earth bridge.
    • Geopathy - The user has a special connection that allows them to read/detect the earth's feelings as Cole stated in "Land of Lost Things" that he felt the land was screaming.
      • Danger Sense - As seen in "Dungeon Crawl!", the user can sense danger in the earth, such as when Cole sensed the presence of an incoming Lava Monster.
    • Elemental shield - The user can create an Earth elemental shield.
      • Radiation Resistance - The user's elemental shield can resist the radiation from entering the planet's atmosphere.
      • Space Survivability - The user's elemental shield likely allows them to survive in the vacuum of space, as seen in "The Titanium Ninja" while the Ninja were about to enter their planet's atmosphere.
    • Elemental Dragon - The user can create an Earth Dragon.

Via the user's True Potential[]

  • Geokinetic Body - The user can temporarily transform their body completely into earth and likely anything else related to earth (i.e. stone, crystal, etc).
  • Strength Enhancement - Enhances the user's strength, making them stronger than original super strength.
    • Supernatural Strength - The user possesses incredible strength that allows them to effortlessly lift vehicles, large planes, and even boulders.
  • Durability Enhancement - Enhances the user's durability (possibly giving them thick skin to resist or suppress projectiles and pain), making them tougher than original super durability.

Corrupted powers[]

  • Asphakinesis - Earth Monsters can manipulate asphalt.
    • Asphagenesis - Earth Monsters are able to generate asphalt.
      • Tar Generation - Earth Monsters are also able to generate tar.
    • Asphalt Mimicry - Earth Monsters can turn their body completely into asphalt to avoid most attacks.
      • Reforming - When Earth Monsters are turned into asphalt, they can reform whenever they are destroyed.
    • Asphakinetic Attacks - Earth Monsters can attack with asphalt.
      • Asphakinetic Imprisonment - Earth Monsters can imprison others in asphalt.
      • Asphakinetic Blast - Earth Monsters can fire blasts of asphalt.

Higher-level powers[]

  • Earth Punch - The user can give their arms a magma/molten rock texture and punch through nearly anything, excluding Vengestone and corrupted Ice. This ability does not have a specific meaning as True Potential does, but rather it is simply a higher level of Earth power. Cole is the first Master of Earth to have this ability and it is exclusive to the Earth element.
    • Levitation - Cole was accidentally able to levitate while he was trying to control his Earth Punch.
  • Elemental Golem - The user is able to encase themselves in stone, simulating the appearance of a large stone golem.
  • Dragon Form - Like the rest of the users of the elements of Creation, Earth Elementals can unlock their Dragon Form, although it can only work through a sense of unity and a sequence of movements.
    • Humanoid Dragon Physiology - Dragon Form will cause the user to become a humanoid dragon.
      • Wing Manifestation - Once in Dragon Form, the user gains wings on their back.
        • Flight - Using said wings, the user can fly.
      • Vengestone Immunity - In dragon form, the user's powers are unaffected by Vengestone.
      • Infectious crystal immunity - A user of Dragon Form is unaffected by infectious crystals.
      • Enhanced Elemental Powers - A user of Dragon Form has their Elemental Powers heavily enhanced.
      • Purification - A user of Dragon Form can undo corruption from the infectious crystals.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Though it was through the Scythe of Quakes, Earth is the first Elemental power seen in the Franchise.
  • Despite the element being named as such, it has been pointed out in the show by the characters (specifically the Cook in "Enkrypted" and Jay in "The Mask of Deception") that they do not know what "Earth" is, which implies that the planet on which Ninjago takes place is not called this.
    • However, the word is still used to refer to the ground or land in general, such as when Vania asks "What is it like to command the very earth?" and Cole says "I'm surrounded by earth" in "Into the Dark" and when Hazza D'ur says Vengestone is being mined "from the earth" in "The Skull Sorcerer."
    • It was later confirmed by Tommy Andreasen that, within the world of Ninjago, "Earth" simply means dirt or soil and does not refer to the planet of Ninjago.[3]
  • Cole's elemental shield is made of pure black and gray energy, with no hint of the brown, green, or orange colors seen in his other elemental abilities.
  • It's possible that the powers of Earth and Fire could be combined to create magma.[4]
  • According to The Book of Elemental Powers, sand is included as part of the element of Earth.
  • Kruncha and Chopov are affiliated with Earth in promotional material and the Ninjago Spinners game, though this is non-canon.
    • Kruncha briefly had access to Earth via the Scythe of Quakes in "The New Masters of Spinjitzu."
  • While Lloyd was shown to move mountains in "Blackout" and had the element of Earth at the time, this ability was due to his Golden Power.[5]
  • Earth is also an Element in LEGO Bionicle and LEGO Elves, but the latter is more similar to Nature.
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, Nokt used Earth to destroy the rock bridge in "Elements of Betrayal."[6]
  • Earth is one of 9 Elemental Powers to have more than two colors associated with them, the other 8 being Heat, Gravity, Lightning, Decay, Time, Fire, Mind, and Fusion.
  • The only confirmed users of the Spinjitzu Burst are Earth elementalists, those being Cole and Lilly.
