Overview

This article is about the spinner set. For the character, see Wu. For the Character card, see Card 16 - Sensei Wu (Spinners). For the special edition character card, see Card ★7 - Sensei Wu (Spinners).

2255 Sensei Wu is a Ninjago Spinners - pilot episodes set that was released on August 5, 2011. It includes Sensei Wu, his spinner, the Staff of the Dragons, a Golden Katana, a Golden Chained Fang, a Character card, four Battle cards, and three white bricks: 2 2x1s and 1 2x2 plate.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Lead the spinjitzu masters to victory against the evil skeleton army!

Sensei Wu, master of the power of creation, is the son of the first spinjitzu master and brother of evil Lord Garmadon. Spin the ultimate Spinjitzu master to victory with 4 battle cards to influence the outcome. Set includes 1 pure optic spinner, 3 weapons, 1 character card, 4 battle cards and LEGO® bricks.

  • Features Sensei Wu minifigure with pure optic spinner
  • Weapons include the golden staff, golden kusari gama and golden sword
  • Includes 1 character card, 4 battle cards and LEGO® bricks[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the only Spinner set with all of the packaged weapons being golden. It is also the only set with the Golden Chained Fang in it.
    • This is one of two individual Spinner sets (i.e. only one character and spinner) that has more than one golden weapon packaged with it (the other being 2172 Nya).
