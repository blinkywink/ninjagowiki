Overview

This article is about the vehicle summoned from the Sword of Fire. For the orginal set, see 9441 Kai's Blade Cycle. For the Legacy set, see 70667 Kai's Blade Cycle & Zane's Snowmobile. For the 4+ Legacy set, see 71734 Kai's Blade Cycle.
“Our Elemental vehicles. Created from our Golden Weapons. Remember those, Zane?”
— Kai to Zane, "Elemental Rider"

The Blade Cycle was a ninja vehicle owned by Kai. It resembles a red motorcycle that is surrounded by flames during use. The Blade Cycle can be summoned by focusing one's mind on the Sword of Fire.

History

History[]

Snakebit[]

Kai first transformed his Sword of Fire into the Blade Cycle. However, once Jay lost his concentration, and he, Cole, and Zane also lost control when arguing about who would catch Jay.

Can of Worms[]

Kai used his Blade Cycle to get to the Venomari's Tomb in the Toxic Bogs. However, a Venomari spat on Kai, so Kai went on the Storm Glider with Jay in order to infiltrate a Serpentine congregation.

The Rise of the Great Devourer[]

Kai used the Blade Cycle to catch up to the snakes' train as it traveled to Ouroboros.

Day of the Great Devourer[]

Kai drove the Blade Cycle away from the Destiny's Bounty as it was being eaten.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In 2010, Tommy Andreasen recorded a video of himself performing movements with a toy lightsaber to demonstrate to Peter Hausner the style of movement with which Kai would summon the bike from his sword.[2]
  • It has a black-stained window in Rise of the Snakes. Despite this, none of its sets have it.
  • This is the only Golden Weapon vehicle to not have a shooting feature in both the Rise of the Snakes and Legacy sets.
  • The Blade Cycle has both, a Ninjago: Legacy remake (70667 Kai's Blade Cycle & Zane's Snowmobile) and a Ninjago: Legacy 4+ remake (71734 Kai's Blade Cycle) exactly like the Fire Dragon, and the Storm Glider.
  • This is Vincent Tong's favourite vehicle from the series.[3]
