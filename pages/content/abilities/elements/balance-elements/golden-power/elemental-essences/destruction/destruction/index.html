Overview

For a similar power, see Darkness.
“Do not confuse Destruction with Darkness. Darkness feeds on Creation. It needs life to make evil. Destruction fears Creation. Destruction only wants to erase all life.”
— The Book of Elemental Powers

Destruction is an elemental essence in the Merged Lands.[4] It is not derived from the Source Dragons, but rather it is indirectly linked to them.[1] It is intertwined with Golden Power,[5][6] closely related to Darkness,[7][8] and is the opposite of Creation. It is associated with the color dark purple, and corresponds with the Oni and their sorcery.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Cataclyikinesis/Hakaikinesis - The user can freely manipulate and control destruction.
    • Disintegration/Destruction Inducement - The user can destroy/disintegrate living and non-living objects, possibly even objects of great size, (like in the episode "Spellbound," when Garmadon uses Spinjitzu to destroy the objects possessed by Clouse's Dark Magic).
    • Hakai-Ergokinesis - This ability gives the user the power to manipulate destructive energy as seen in "An Underworldly Takeover," when Garmadon uses this ability in the form of Spinjitzu against Samukai, throwing him backward with a strong force.
      • Energy Absorption - As seen with a few Oni, like the Omega, the user has the ability to absorb destructive energy and use it in various ways, gaining some form of advantage.
        • Energy Conversion - The Omega was able to convert and store the energy he absorbed in his Oni staff and use it to increase the power of his strikes and send the Earth Driller down.
      • Energy Blast - As shown by Garmadon, the user can fire blasts of destructive energy.
      • Energy Ball Projection - The user can shoot balls of destructive energy (demonstrated in the episode "True Potential").
      • Energy Vortex Generation - As shown by Garmadon in "An Underworldly Takeover," the user can create a vortex of destructive energy.
        • Energy Levitation - When in their destructive energy vortex, the user appears to levitate in mid-air through destructive energy.
      • Energy Beam Emission - As demonstrated by Omega and the Oni staffs, they can fire beams of pure destructive energy at an opponent.
      • Energy Bolt Projection - As demonstrated by Garmadon in "Christofern," the user can fire bolts of destructive energy at their opponents.
      • Destructive Blast/Explosion Inducement - As demonstrated by Lord Garmadon in "Green Destiny", this power can be used to generate a massive explosion of pure destructive energy. This power was even strong enough to blast the Firstbourne, Lloyd, and Wu off of Borg Tower.
      • Flyrogenesis/Shield Projection - As demonstrated by Lord Garmadon in "True Potential," the user can use their destructive powers as a force field/shield against enemy attacks, whereas Garmadon used this move to block Lloyd's Energy blasts. In "Green Destiny," this power was used to withstand a massive amount of molten metal generated by Firstbourne.
      • Energy Aura - As demonstrated by Garmadon in "True Potential," the user can surround themselves in an aura of destructive energy.
      • Energy Strike - As seen in "True Potential," the user can supercharge their strikes with destructive energy to have more power in physical attacks.
      • Hakai-Electrokinesis - The user can create and manipulate a corrupted version of Lightning (only seen once when Garmadon used it to disturb the ninja's sleep in "The Green Ninja"). With this ability, they can presumably shock an adversary, though this is never shown.
      • Mov-Pyrokinesis - The user can create and manipulate purple fire.
    • Elemental Creation - The user can create living things or objects using Destruction.
      • Weapon Creation - The user can use Destruction to create weapons (nets, drills, shurikens, whips, etc.), as demonstrated by Garmadon in "Green Destiny", where he created four glowing purple daggers and a staff similar to the Omega's during his showdown with the Oni leader.
    • Colossus Control - As demonstrated by Lord Garmadon in "The Gilded Path", he was able to channel his destructive powers in the form of a weak destructive blast to control the Colossus. When Skylor replicated some of Garmadon's powers, she was able to use it to take command of the Colossus but the strain on her caused her to faint and her eyes to turn purple.
  • Dark Magic - According to Mystake in "The Weakest Link," the Oni have Dark Magic, which they can enhance at will. However, it is unknown what the Oni can do with their Dark Magic, except the fact that they used it to create black clouds that allow them to summon weapons or petrify whoever they touch, perform sorcery, interact with matter through magic, transmute anything, cast spells, enchantments, magic incantations or curses, manipulate illusions, shapeshift, and conjure up/summon other Oni. Mystake used her staff to form a black cloud to allow Skylor to copy Garmadon's Destruction.
    • Weapon Summoning - The user can summon weapons out of thin air or from a distance. This was first shown in "Once Bitten, Twice Shy," where Garmadon summoned four weapons from a distance. Later, he summoned four katanas by pulling them out from his back in "The Royal Blacksmiths." Mystake used this in "Saving Faith" to summon a staff from thin air through Dark Magic. Though he was the only one seen using them, Garmadon could summon Destruction daggers to battle Lloyd and Wu at the top of Borg Tower. Mystake notably summoned a staff from nothing using her dark powers during her fight with Garmadon.
    • Teleportation - The user can use Dark Magic to teleport from one place to another. The Omega can summon his legions of Oni by banging his staff against the ground. He often relies on his armies of Oni to utterly overwhelm his opponents.
      • Remote Teleportation - The user can teleport other beings from one place to another. The Omega used this to bring some of the Oni to his location to attack Lloyd and Garmadon when they attempted to destroy the Realm Crystal.
      • Realm Traveling/Dimensional Travel - As demonstrated by the Omega in "The Fall", the user can travel between different realms.
    • Umbrakinesis - Allows the user to control darkness by creating cloudy tentacles of darkness that have a strong grip on anyone it grabs on, invoking it only to destabilize their opponents who find themselves lost in the fog, or use a more devastating version capable of petrifying all those who come into contact with it. In Season 10, the Oni used this to petrify the citizens of Ninjago. It is shown to have other uses as if the victim manages to resist petrification, their energy will slowly deplete and they will begin to weaken. They will also have a hard time breathing, even if they are part-Oni. Cole mentioned that when he was touched by the tentacles, it was the "coldest thing he had ever felt," implying the tentacles have some form of discomfort.
      • Petrification/Cloud Generation - As demonstrated by the Oni in "The Darkness Comes," users are able to use their Destructive Magic in the form of black clouds that will petrify whomever they touch. This ability, however, was ineffective against other Oni and those of Oni descent.
      • Energy Drain - The more petrification-resistant victims stay in the darkness, the more energy they lose by becoming weaker.
    • Curse Inducement - Oni sorcery can curse others.
      • Dimensional Imprisonment - One such Oni curse can imprison its victims in the Cursed Realm.[9]
  • Shapeshifting - As mentioned by Iron Baron, the Oni can shapeshift into anyone. They could take the exact form of any person and mimic their voice and behavior, as demonstrated by Mystake when she assumed Harumi's identity, and use their shapeshifting power continuously for a very long time. However, it won't protect them when they're in a dragon's presence because they can sense and smell Oni.
    • Animal Morphing - The user can shapeshift into animals real or mythical, as seen when Mystake appeared in "The Gilded Path", disguised as a rat.
    • Voxikinesis - As demonstrated by Mystake disguised as Harumi, the user can manipulate and mask their voice to sound exactly like the person they've changed into.
    • Oni Form - Through anger or frustration, beings that are part-Oni can transform into a demonic state that greatly enhances their strength, energy, and powers. The effects of this ability would make them turn into an Oni, similar to the users of Dragon Form. However, if weakened, they would revert back to normal, as seen with Garmadon whenever he uses the form during battles.
      • Wing Manifestation - As seen with Garmadon, some users gain wings that allow them to fly when they go into Oni form, although it's possible that this is exclusive only to Garmadon since he is also half dragon like his father and brother, and the Oni do not seem to have wings of their own. Lloyd's Oni form has not been shown to have wings either.
  • Combat Empowerment - As demonstrated by Garmadon in "Into the Breach," he gained power from fighting against the Ninja and said, "The fight is what fuels me." His powers were enhanced as long as there was a fight going on between him and his opponent, but if the opponent resists, his powers start to weaken. Garmadon can also demonstrate this ability through his creations like the Colossus, such as when he enhanced his strength with the power of Destruction while fighting for control over it through Skylor. After he lost his powers, he used this very same technique to restore his powers after sparring with the Ninja on their ship.
    • Conflict/Anger Empowerment - The power of Destruction is also said to be fueled by conflict and anger, as seen with Lloyd in "The Spider's Design".
  • Vengestone immunity - As shown by Lloyd and Garmadon, users of destruction are able to use their destructive power regardless of Vengestone nearby.
  • Petrification resistance - As seen with a few Oni, Lloyd, and Garmadon, the user is resistant to all forms of petrification.

There are other abilities originating from Destruction that are available to the Oni or those who use the Oni Masks. However, it is also possible that the Master of Destruction can employ these abilities, seeing as Garmadon’s resurrected form has four arms, and his power is enhanced with Oni magic.

  • Telekinesis - Allows the user to control, move, and manipulate objects by using telekinesis. Garmadon used this in "Big Trouble, Little Ninjago," when he telekinetically pulled huge chunks of rocks with his Creation and Destruction powers to create the Colossus. This is granted by the Mask of Deception.
    • Telekinetic Choking/Crushing - It allows the user to choke people and tear objects apart on certain distances, as seen when Garmadon choked Harumi and tore apart Mr. E.
    • Psychokinesis - The user can likely manipulate/interact with phenomena mentally, as seen when Garmadon was destroying Mr. E and choking Harumi.
  • Invincibility (Magma/Stone Armor) - Covers the user's entire body in an armor of invulnerable magma stone and destructive lava. This is granted by the Mask of Hatred.
  • Enhanced Swordsmanship/Multiple Arms/Power Reflection - Turns the user into a master swordsman with two additional arms and gives them the ability to catch and deflect other people's powers through their skills. It is also shown when the user has no weapons, the mask grants the user two katanas. The katanas turn into four if the user already has two. This is granted by the Mask of Vengeance.
    • Enhanced Agility/Reflexes & Attack Prediction - It also allows the user to have incredible agility and reflexes to react to attacks and send them at opponents at lightning-fast speeds.
    • Enhanced Athleticism/Dexterity - It also likely grants the user incredible acrobatics and dexterity to move around efficiently.
    • Enhanced Leaping - The user can likely jump longer and higher distances.
    • Enhanced Balance - The user can possibly achieve an incredible level of balance.
  • Resurrection - When all three Oni Masks are united, a user can perform an ancient Oni ritual to bring an Oni (full-blooded or hybrid) from death back to the living world. The ritual requires the three Oni Masks and a hair from each living relative of the Oni. Harumi and the Sons of Garmadon performed this ritual to bring back Lord Garmadon from the Departed Realm to Ninjago.
    • Dimensional Portal Creation - During the resurrection ritual, the user can open a portal from the Departed Realm to Ninjago.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Lack of Conflict/Combat - While Destruction is fueled by conflict/anger, refusal to fight weakens it considerably. As shown by Garmadon in "Green Destiny," his powers were weakened to the point they stopped working and he needed to start a fight to reawaken them in "Into the Breach".

Trivia[]

  • Destruction originated in the First Land, specifically being the main power of the Oni. It and Creation were the only powers to be used by mortals, predating every other Elemental Power.[10]
  • Destruction is one of the few "dark" elements, the others being Shadow and Darkness.
    • When asked if Darkness and Destruction are the same, Tommy Andreasen said, "Can’t get into that. Too complicated."[11] Bragi Schut said, "They are different in my opinion. The Oni power of destruction is more of an opposite to the dragon power of creation. The Overlord is an embodiment of evil and darkness."[12] During Crystalized, it is heavily implied that Destruction and Darkness are closely tied to each other, as the Overlord stated that Lloyd and Garmadon's powers were a gift from him.[7] However, the Oni's Destruction power has also been shown and said to be a means to compete with and defeat the Overlord as it is the only power capable of affecting the Crystal army.
      • Tommy Andreasen's personal theory was that the Stone Wars never happened, and the Overlord is the living embodiment of the First Spinjitzu Master's Oni side that the latter, literally, cast aside. In addition, the Great Devourer's venom gave Garmadon the power to use Destruction, even to Lloyd, as he's the son of Garmadon and inherited the same power.[13] However, as he stated, this shouldn't be considered official canon info unless stated in the show.
      • The Overlord's connection to the Oni is hinted in Crystalized, as his new physical form resembled an Oni and he transformed the Oni Temple in the Primeval's Eye into his base as the Crystal Island.
        • The corrupted/crystalized versions of the Golden Weapons are called the Weapons of Destruction despite having the power of Darkness. According to Tommy Andreasen, the reason is because the crystalized versions were meant to represent the opposite of the Weapons of Spinjitzu's powers to create, whereas the same powers of the Weapons of Destruction were used to corrupt Ninjago.[citation needed]
      • While Destruction is nearly identical to Darkness, The Book of Elemental Powers states that Darkness is a twisted and warped incarnation of Creation that uses the essence to manifest evil while Destruction is the polar opposite of Creation that obliterates all in its path.
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, Destruction is not an inherently evil ability. Rather, it went out of check in Garmadon when he was bitten by the Great Devourer.[14] This is seen when Mystake used her Destruction powers to help the Resistance, and when Garmadon used his to help protect Ninjago against the Oni in March of the Oni and the Overlord in "Crystalized".
  • When asked whether if Thunderfang was powered by Destruction when he consumed Oni souls or if Destruction was more related to blood than to souls, Chris "Doc" Wyatt clarified that Thunderfang was only fueled by soul energy.[15]
  • In 2016, Andreasen stated that Garmadon no longer had Destruction after being purified in the Final Battle.[16] However, later that year, he stated that it went out of check after Garmadon was bitten by the Great Devourer and was in check again after he was purified.[17] The latter was eventually proven true when the First Spinjitzu Master's backstory was explained in "The Oni and the Dragon," as both Destruction and Creation were revealed to be an intrinsic part of the First Spinjitzu Master and his sons.
    • Interestingly, Garmadon's Destruction Spinjitzu is shown to have a Lightning-like appearance only when fully corrupted by the Great Devourer's venom and transformed following the Battle Between Brothers. In the episodes that came afterwards, his Spinjitzu lacked this electrical design (except when enhanced as Forbidden Spinjitzu).
    • Garmadon also uses the Art of the Silent Fist as an alternative combat method instead of Destruction.[18]
  • In "Saving Faith," Destruction proved to be too powerful for Skylor to wield, though this may be due to the fact that she was trying too hard to control the Colossus. Also, she was struggling against Garmadon to control it as Garmadon is its true master.
    • Destruction is one of the most powerful elemental essences due to the fact that Skylor, who was one of the most powerful Elemental Masters, could not stand the power it took to use it.
  • Golden Power is stronger than Destruction, as the Oni, the main users of this Elemental Power, cowered before the power held within the Golden Armor.
    • Despite being stronger overall, Darkness has a weakness to Destruction even greater than its weakness to Golden Power, to the point that Destruction is capable of fully destroying it, unlike Golden Power, which merely temporarily extinguishes its power.
      • According to Garmadon in "Safe Haven," Oni Form is the only thing that can destroy the Overlord due to it being the highest form of Destruction, as stated in "A Lesson in Anger."
        • Garmadon most likely meant the Overlord's physical body, since the Overlord's spirit cannot be destroyed as he's a part of the Balance. It was mentioned earlier that Destruction, unlike elemental powers, is not susceptible to the effects of Vengestone. In addition, Creation, according to Lloyd, had the same effect as expected from Destruction.
  • Garmadon is said by Mystake to hold "a destructive power [she] has never seen," meaning he may be more powerful than any Oni. This may be because the power Garmadon uses is combined with Creation, as he is said to have the power to "create and destroy."
    • However, this is contradicted by how Garmadon himself fears the Oni even with his full potential unlocked, seeing the need to unite Ninjago to be able to potentially go to war with the Oni and win. This could mean that while his strength is at least greater than a single Oni, he would be overpowered if he faced all the Oni alone.
      • It was shown that Garmadon is indeed able to overpower other Oni easily but he was outclassed in terms of power by the leader of the Oni, the Omega, as well as being at a disadvantage against Mystake (because he wasn't in his Oni form at the time).
  • Despite the power to shapeshift being similar to the element of Form, Destruction is not related to Form in any way.[19]
    • Both Destruction and Form are associated with the color purple.
  • The ability Garmadon showed with telekinetically choking other beings is very similar to the ability Darth Vader possesses. Darth Vader has choked several Generals working for him when they fail to carry out his bidding, just like how Garmadon killed Mr. E and tried to kill Harumi when they failed to capture Lloyd and stop the Resistance from broadcasting their message respectively.
  • Destruction feeds off of anger and conflict with others because when Lloyd was fighting Garmadon, it made him stronger, and anger made him tap into the essence and enter a fight with Harumi and Mister F, which made him stronger.
    • Additionally, refusing to engage in conflict weakens it because when Lloyd used the Art of the Silent Fist against Garmadon, it dissipated until it stopped working.
    • It seems when an Elemental Master uses Destruction, the person seems to lose any sense of giving up and loses their rationality and by extension, their sanity. However, these effects are apparently temporary, as Garmadon seemed to calm down after being released from his cell. In addition, while Lloyd briefly lost control while in his Oni Form, Garmadon was shown to have better control and awareness in his own Oni Form, showing respect and concern for Wu when he went to fight the Overlord and surprise at seeing Harumi again. This indicates that experience is needed to properly control Destruction.
  • Tommy Andreasen implied that Chen didn't need Destruction for the transformation spell because it is not an element.[17]
  • During the Oni Trilogy, it was stated Destruction was fueled by fighting and conflict, while in Crystalized, it is stated to be fueled by anger.
    • It is possible that by saying conflict fuels Destruction, it also means internal and emotional conflict, thus meaning that anger also enhances the power.
  • As Garmadon unlocked his dormant element of Creation when he unlocked his true potential, Wu may be able to use his dormant element of Destruction when finding his true potential.
    • Similarly, Lloyd's dominant power is Life, yet he unlocked his dormant Destruction in "The Spider's Design," possibly due to the Darkness-powered crystals around him.
      • When Lloyd used Destruction for the first time in "The Spider's Design," the powers manifested as purple version of Life. However, in "A Lesson in Anger" and "Roots," Lloyd used the same destructive energy as his father.
  • "A Lesson in Anger" is so far, the only time that Lloyd used Destruction without his eyes glowing purple.
  • On several occasions, when Garmadon used Destruction, it looked more like Creation. For example, in "The Green Ninja", he created lightning and in "Christofern", he set fire to bushes instead of just destroying them. Lightning and Fire are two of the elements of Creation, but Garmadon may have combined Creation and Destruction in that case using fire in order to destroy, explaining its purple color.
    • Lego.com has suggested in 2012 that Destruction, like Creation, is made up of the original four elements, albeit unbalanced. Additionally, Garmadon's character biography on the site from 2011, 2012, and 2014 described his element as "all," similarly to Wu.
      • However, when asked if there are the Elements of Destruction, as opposed to the Elements of Creation, Tommy Andreasen replied that "Destruction is so much simpler than Creation. It can take you a month to create a statue and a second to destroy it."[20]
  • Depending on the skill level of the user, Destruction can be used to counter Shatterspin.[21]
  • Users of Destruction have a place where their powers can grow stronger, similar to how Masters of Earth grow stronger underground or how Masters of Water grow stronger underwater.[22]
  • When asked whether the indirect connection Destruction has to the Source Dragons was a result of the Oni coming into existence, Chris "Doc" Wyatt responded with "pretty close"[23]
    • This implies that Destruction's origin comes from the Oni themselves, meaning that Destruction appeared as a result of The Alpha or a potential Source Oni coming into existence.
  • The First Spinjitzu Master is the reason why Destruction became part of and intertwined with the Golden Power.[6]
