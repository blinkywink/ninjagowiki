Overview

“Transformation is a powerful ability.”
— Mystake, "Two Lies, One Truth"

Shapeshifting is the ability to change one's form or appearance. While it is granted by the element of Form, some creatures such as the Oni or Formlings possess this power innately.

Types of shapeshifting

Types of shapeshifting[]

Elemental Power of Form[]

This ability always applies to the individual only, although the Elemental Master of Amber can copy this ability. The power lies in the fact that the user can take any form, and it is not clear whether it can be, among other humanoid beings, an animal, or a thing. The user can also copy the clothes and the voice of his template. During shapeshifting itself, the user is surrounded by a flash of purple energy in a few seconds, which passes from one part of the body to another.

Elemental Power of Size[]

This element allows the user to change their size into any proportions. Furthermore, any clothes or objects held by the Elemental Master have their dimensions adjusted as well.

Djinn shapeshifting[]

This ability is very similar to the Elemental Power of Form and can change the user's appearance to someone else when shrouded in orange smoke, just like when fulfilling a wish. However, it turns out that Djinn can also create a fictional creature to which they will change, which Nadakhan showed after turning into a pirate with only two arms, and they can also turn into artificial intelligence on a computer.

Oni shapeshifting[]

Each Oni can change their form to any being, and even animals of completely different sizes are confirmed. The Oni can also change their clothes into original clothes during the transformation or let them disappear completely, but they do not always have to do so and they can keep their original clothes. After the transformation, the Oni can keep their original voice, but they can also change it to the original voice. The transformation process itself can be done in two ways. The first is that the Oni wave their hands in front of their face and thus turn it into someone else's face, but the body retains their own. The second way is to cover themselves in a small cloud of darkness and change their form completely. However, it won't protect them when they're in a dragon's presence because they can sense and smell Oni.

Oni Form[]

“You have been taught to channel Elemental Power, which comes from the dragon. But the Oni has an equal and opposite. And the highest form of that power is the Oni Form.”
— Wu, "A Lesson in Anger"

Beings who are part-Oni have their own way of shapeshifting. Because of their Oni blood and power of Destruction, users such as Garmadon and Lloyd can unlock their Oni Form by channeling their anger, allowing their strength and powers to be greatly enhanced, thus becoming even more powerful than any regular Oni. The color of the user's body corresponds with their garments and changes to look similar to some of the Oni. However, if the user is weakened, they will revert back to normal. They can also revert back to their original state at will.[3]

Smoke creatures shapeshifting[]

In terms of variations of size, Smoke Creatures possess abilities similar to the Oni ones. They also can turn into a creature of any size they want, even the size of a dragon. They can read the minds and memories of the people surrounding their victim, although they still can make mistakes when copying someone's behavior, which was seen when the creature didn't know about Wu's love of tea. They can also copy some abilities of their victim, since they can do Spinjitzu when shapeshifted into Spinjitzu master. If the creature sees its reflection, it returns in a basic smoke form.

Formling shapeshifting[]

The Formlings were gifted their shapeshifting abilities from the First Elemental Master of Form. Unlike all the others mentioned, the Formlings can only change their form to one specific animal, which chooses them during a process called the Choosing, a ceremony that takes place sometimes during the childhood of each Formling. Although it is said that only Formlings that are pure of heart will find their animal form, this is not true as at least one Formling is known to have not found his animal form. Once the Formling is turned into an animal, they cannot speak directly, but when they look at something in which their reflection is visible, they can. The process itself takes place in such a way that, if necessary, Formling begins to glow pink or blue with a white interior until it changes their appearance completely. However, no matter how injured a user is, they do not seem to be able to fully control their ability and can change into their human form during sleep.

Dark Magic[]

Some users of Dark Magic such as Clouse can use their magic abilies to shapeshift into what they want.

Cloaking technology[]

Although not directly shapeshifting, the Nindroids or some advanced vehicles can change their appearance with the help of holographic projection, although their actual body remains unchanged. For this reason, it is impossible to change into anyone smaller or larger. The process itself takes place in such a way that blue holographic strips appear on the user's body and transform him.

Unagami's transformation[]

As the embodiment of Prime Empire itself, Unagami can change his appearance with principles similar to the Nindroids, but he can also change into much larger creatures, such as the Empire Dragon. The process itself takes place by the appearance of red holographic squares on Unagami's body, which change his shape.

The Overlord's transformation[]

While possessing Garmadon, the Overlord was able to metamorphose into a dragon. As the Crystal King, he can change his shape and size at will, as shown when he shifted his form to be that of a giant centaur-like creature.

Dragon Form[]

Like beings who are part-Oni and have the ability to transform into one through anger, users who contain Dragon blood or possess the elements of Creation can turn into a humanoid dragon. For the transformation to work, the users would have to work in an act of unity and a sequence of movements.

Draconus Form[]

Draconus Form is a powerful ability connected to the Source Dragon of Life through the Elemental Power of Life,[4] and can be shared with and accessed by other Elemental Masters through their connection to the current Master of Life.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Elemental Power of Form is almost the same as the shapeshifting of Oni, but Oni do not have elemental powers. According to Tommy Andreasen, the two are most likely unrelated.[5]
  • The actual process of shapeshifting differs for each type; for example, the Elemental Master of Form changes with purple light relatively slowly, so the true shape of the master can be seen at once, as well as the person to whom it has been transformed. Other examples include Formlings, who change at once in pink, blue, or yellow glow, so the transformation itself is not completely visible, or Oni, whose transformation are affected by hand movements; if they are transformed into a human although when they are transformed into an animal, the transformation is similar to that of Formlings.
  • Regarding Oni Form:
    • The Oni can change their size within reason.[6]
    • According to Tommy Andreasen, the First Spinjitzu Master likely knew of the Oni Form, but was never careless enough to unleash it.[7]
      • He also stated that Lloyd would've destroyed the Overlord[8] and end up being lost in the darkness of his Oni side forever, had he not found the empathy in himself.[9]
    • When asked if Lloyd's Oni Form will return one day, Chris "Doc" Wyatt replied with "I probably think so."[10][11]
    • It is possible for Lloyd to combined Draconus Form with Oni Form with enough training.[12]
    • Garmadon, using his Oni Form in Crystalized, is more stronger than he was as Emperor in Hunted.[13]
  • In concept art, Akita is shown as a human-wolf hybrid form on the left, so it's unknown if Formlings possess the ability to transform into human-animal hybrid forms.
