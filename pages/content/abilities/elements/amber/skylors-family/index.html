Skylor's family is the family containing the Elemental Master of Amber, Skylor. They are also known for Chen's Noodle House.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Multiple members of the family members had, at some point, all elements (excluding the elemental essences aside from Destruction, as well as Wind, Water, and Time): Skylor with her power of Amber and Chen with the Staff of Elements. It's unknown which elements Skylor's mother and the Original Master of Amber absorbed.
  • It appears Skylor's red hair comes from Chen, as his younger self was shown to have red hair in "Spellbound." However, a painting depicting present-day Chen (seen in "Winds of Change" and "Infamous") shows him with black hair.
    • Skylor's mother is depicted with black hair, while the Original Master of Amber is depicted with orange hair.
  • The unpublished story, Amber Legacy, would have explained more of the family's backstory, introducing Skylor's mother and explaining why she wasn't in the Serpentine War, why she married Chen, and what her ultimate fate was.[1] The story's development was halted due to the poor distribution of the Dark Island Trilogy.[2]
    • Amber Spiral, a story loosely based on Amber Legacy, was released on LEGO.com and the LEGO Life app in 2022.[3]
