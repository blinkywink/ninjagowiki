Overview

“Internal cohesion against outside forces that would dare to break its hold.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Surface Tension is an Elemental Power in the Merged Realms that was created as a result of the Merge[2] and is derived from the Source Dragon of Strength.[1] It is associated with the colors turquoise and teal.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Superficsiokinesis - The user can manipulate the surface tension of liquids such as water.
    • Nitikinesis - The user can use Surface Tension to create and manipulate bubbles.
      • Nitikinetic Attacks - The user can use bubbles to attack.
        • Nitikinetic Blast - The user can release bubbles over a specific target area, causing great damage and/or delivering great shock waves of pure force.
        • Nitikinetic Bomb Generation - By joining Surface Tension with Shockwave, the user can create and launch bombs composed of bubbles.
      • Nitikinetic Levitation - The user can float in mid-air by surrounding themselves in a bubble.
    • Molecukinesis - The user can likely manipulate the molecular structure in liquids, allowing them to accelerate, halt/immobilize, and vibrate their movements, cause it to combust, or possibly control their own molecules.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the second element to have more than one word in its name, the first being Golden Power.
    • However, Golden Power is the only Elemental essence with a two-word name, while Surface Tension is the first normal element with that attribute, followed by Brute Force.
  • It appears to be one of the weakest elements, as Zeatrix easily overpowered it by popping the bubbles with Shockwave.
  • Surface Tension is one of seventeen elements that are currently not known to be confined to a human host. The other sixteen are Ice, Time, Fusion, Balance, Propulsion, Figment, Plants, Reflex, Quake, Size, Sound, Brute Force, Fear, Swarm, Misfortune, and Decay.
  • So far, Surface Tension is one of four Elemental Powers that are known to have been created by the Merge, the others being Propulsion, Fusion, and Technology.
