Overview

“Like rocks against crashing waves or a sword splintering a shield, Strength resists or overpowers an opposing force.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Strength is a form of dragon energy and one of the seven Sources, personified by the Source Dragon of Strength. It is often associated with the colors purple, black/gray, or orange.

User's abilities

User's abilities[]

  • Dynamikinesis - The user can manipulate the concept of strength.
    • Dynamikinetic Wave Emission - Strength can be used to release waves.
  • Dragon Icon Creation - As it is a form of dragon energy, the user can create a Source Dragon Icon.

Higher-level powers[]

  • Elemental Power Creation/Bestowal - The user can create and grant others Elemental Powers sourced from Strength.
    • Potentikinesis Bestowal - When used and combined with other forms of Source Dragon energy, Strength can bestow the Elemental Essence of Golden Power to other beings.
    • Geokinesis/Terrakinesis Bestowal - Strength can bestow the Elemental Power of Earth to other beings.
    • Ferrokinesis/Metallokinesis Bestowal - Strength can bestow the Elemental Power of Metal to other beings.
    • Gravikinesis/Gyrokinesis Bestowal - Strength can bestow the Elemental Power of Gravity to other beings.
    • Superficsiokinesis Bestowal - Strength can bestow the Elemental Power of Surface Tension to other beings.
    • Fusionism/Object Fusion Bestowal - Strength can bestow the Elemental Power of Fusion to other beings.
  • Dimensional Rift Creation - As seen with the Dragon Cores, which is made from Strength and the other sources, users can tear rifts in the fabric of reality, exposing the Ethereal Divide.
    • Vacuum Effect - Such rifts are identical to MergeQuakes and have a vacuum effect that sucks nearby objects or people in.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Strength is often associated with the color purple. This was first seen when Ras used the Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength in "The Battle of the Second Monastery," and later seen in Season 4.
  • In a DuckBricks video, it is stated that Brute Force is an Element derived from Strength.[10] However, this statement is incorrect as it has been confirmed that Brute Force comes from Chaos.
  • When revealing the Elemental Powers coming from the source of Strength, Glen Lakin used these poetic descriptions:
    • Earth - The solid ground beneath our feet, a mountain rising up to challenge the sky.[5]
    • Metal - The veins of the planet. Hard to break, even harder when it hits back.[6]
    • Gravity - The pull of a larger body on a smaller one, you can resist but you cannot escape.[7]
    • Surface Tension - Internal cohesion against outside forces that would dare to break its hold.[8]
    • Fusion - A bond between two objects, stronger together than separate.[9]
