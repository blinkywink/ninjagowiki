Overview

“The blast that rockets an object forward - make way!”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Propulsion is an Elemental Power in the Merged Lands that was created as a result of the Merge[2] and is derived from the Source Dragon of Energy.[1] It is associated with the colors light red and yellow.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Impulsukinesis - The user is able to create and manipulate propulsion.
    • Sphere Transformation - The user can curl up into a ball for attack, defense, and mobility.
      • Energy Empowerment/Propulsion - The user can charge up and propel themselves with energy to increase the strength of their blow in ball form.
      • Rolling/Enhanced Speed - The user can roll towards an opponent at high speeds.
        • Rolling Attack - The user can roll while attacking, covering considerable distances in the process.
      • Burst Generation - The user can create a burst of force when curling up into a ball.
      • Enhanced Flexibility - The user becomes incredibly flexible when curling up into ball form.
      • Spin Dash - The user can perform a quick dash while spinning in ball form to crash towards opponents.
      • Enhanced Bouncing/Ricocheting - When the user is turned into a ball, they can bounce and ricochet off surfaces.
      • Mobile Durability/Impact Immunity - It is possible for the user to become durable while moving as a ball, making them immune to the impact of crashing into surfaces.
      • Impact Absorption - Users like Arccrack appear to be able to absorb the impact of crashing into surfaces while in ball form.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Momentum - As seen in "The Sword Shatters," its high speeds and ricocheting abilities can make it hard for the user to come to a stop.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt mentioned that the Elemental Power was referred to as "Propulsion" in the scripts. However, he was uncertain if this could be considered an official canon name since the Elemental Power's name was never mentioned in the show, only described in the action notes.[3] He later confirmed the name is canon.[4]
  • Propulsion is one of the seventeen elements that are currently not known to be confined to a human host. The other sixteen are Ice, Time, Fusion, Balance, Figment, Plants, Quake, Size, Sound, Surface Tension, Reflex, Brute Force, Fear, Swarm, Misfortune and Decay.
  • Before revealing the Source of Propulsion, Glen Lakin implied that Propulsion could come from Strength or Motion, stating: "maybe it does! [referring to Motion] But maybe propulsion is caused by the strength of its thrust..."[5]
  • So far, Propulsion is one of four Elemental Powers that are known to have been created by the Merge, the others being Surface Tension, Fusion, and Technology.
