The Elemental Tech Mech was an elemental mech created and used by Sora.

History

History[]

What the Mech?[]

Sora used Technology to turn Chronosteel into three discs. When thrown into the air, each of the discs assembles into its respective mech: the Elemental Earth Mech, Elemental Fire Mech, and Elemental Tech Mech, each capable of channeling their respective Elemental Master's power. Together, Cole and Kai used their mechs to restrain an out-of-control Lobbo, while Sora drained the harmful energy from him.

The Mech Master[]

After The Mechanic stole the mechs from the Mech Master 5000, the ninja attempted to stop them with their mechs. However, the Mechanic sent one mech crashing into them, breaking their mechs apart. Sora took a broken arm from Cole's mech and the legs from Kai's mech and attached them to her own, and chased after the Mechanic. After being knocked off a bridge, Sora realized the legs allowed her to access Kai's Fire because of the Chronosteel, and used it to fly back up to the bridge.

A Pain in the Mech![]

Sora used a Technology restraint on a group of Lava-Tides after Cole defeated them. Then Sora swapped her legs and arm with Cole's, allowing her to use Earth on another group. Seeing another Ice Dragon attacking, Kai quickly swapped arms with Sora, allowing her to defend herself with Fire while Kai used Technology to restrain it.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

Sora carried her mech as a disc while trying to find the Dragon Masters with the other ninja.

Force From the East[]

Sora continued to carry her mech as a disc.

The Spell at the Waterfall[]

Sora used her mech for training.

Secrets of the Wyldness[]

Kai continued to carry his mech as a disc.

The Forest of Spirits[]

Kai continued to carry his mech as a disc.

Rising Ninja[]

During the Ritual of the Blood Moon, Sora used her mech to stop Cinder from using Shatterspin by creating a tech cage to trap him in. However, Cinder managed to escape, damaging the mech in the process.

Official description

Official description[]

Sora’s Elemental Tech Mech is armed with a large sword and rotating blade to battle an evil Wolf Mask Claw Warrior in the Shadow Forest. And now, for the first time, you can combine the parts of 3 awesome ninja mechs piloted by Cole, Sora and Kai to create your very own mech. Each customizable mech is sold separately and comes with detachable legs, arms, weapons and torso so you can mix and match.[1]
