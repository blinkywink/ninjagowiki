Overview

“Stillness brings clarity, study reveals knowledge. Focus leads to understanding - to act without it invites chaos.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Focus is a form of dragon energy and one of the seven Sources, personified by the Source Dragon of Focus. It is opposed to Chaos and associated with a multitude of colors but mostly white, purple, and tan/yellow.

User's abilities

User's abilities[]

  • Animadverterkinesis - The user can manipulate Focus.
    • Animadverterkinetic Breath - The user can shoot Focus energy from their mouth.
    • Animadverterkinetic Beam Emission - The user can unleash bright powerful beams of concentrated Focus which could be concussive or dispersive. If a person is hit by a beam of Focus, it can cause pain.[9]
    • Chaos Countering - As they are natural opposites, Focus can be used to counter Chaos.
    • Light Presence - The user is able to release a bright light with its presence as shown with the Arc Dragon of Focus.
    • Animadverterportation - The user can teleport via Focus.
    • Focus Empowerment - The user can gain strength from Focus.
  • Dragon Icon Creation - As it is a form of dragon energy, the user can create a Source Dragon Icon.

Higher-level powers[]

  • Elemental Power Creation/Bestowal - The user can create and grant others Elemental Powers sourced from Focus.
    • Elemento-Potentikinesis Bestowal - When used and combined with other forms of Source Dragon energy, Focus can bestow the Elemental Essence of Golden Power to other beings.
    • Cryokinesis/Glaciokinesis Bestowal - Focus can bestow the Elemental Power of Ice to other beings.
    • Telepathy/Mind Reading Bestowal - Focus can bestow the Elemental Power of Mind to other beings.
    • Reflexokinesis Bestowal - Focus can bestow the Elemental Power of Reflex to other beings.
    • Hallucikinesis/Mirakinesis Bestowal - Focus can bestow the Elemental Power of Figment to other beings.
    • Technokinesis Bestowal - Focus can bestow the Elemental Power of Technology to other beings.
  • Dimensional Rift Creation - As seen with the Dragon Cores, which is made from Focus and the other sources, users can tear rifts in the fabric of reality, exposing the Ethereal Divide.
    • Vacuum Effect - Such rifts are identical to MergeQuakes and have a vacuum effect that sucks nearby objects or people in.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • When asked, "Does Firstbourne possesses Lloyd's element and/or Focus?", Chris "Doc" Wyatt replied with "Sort of". However, it is possible that his answer concerned only the Element part.[10]
  • When revealing the Elemental Powers coming from the source of Focus, Glen Lakin used these poetic descriptions:
    • Ice - Still and clear, an agent of stasis. To freeze a moment or shape itself to a piercing point.[4]
    • Mind - The mind is a maze to be navigated. To control thought is to control action.[5]
    • Reflex - The ability to observe the world around you and move about it as if choreographed, action and reaction in a single moment.[6]
    • Figment - What you see is not always what you get. Figment can focus the mind’s eye on an idea rather than reality.[7]
    • Technology - To understand the workings of the universe is to bend it to your will. Invention is the reimagining of the natural order.[8]
