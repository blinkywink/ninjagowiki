Overview

For the episode, see True Potential (episode).
“In each and every one of us, there are obstacles that hold us back. Only when you conquer that fear will your heart be free. Only then will you reach your True Potential.”
— Wu, "Tick Tock"

True Potential, also known as Full Potential, is the highest level of classic use of Elemental Powers accessible to Elemental Masters, tapped into by overcoming the emotional obstacles that are holding them back. When in use, the master's bodies glow, they are surrounded by the colors of their elements, and they can freely control and manipulate their elemental powers.

True Potential users

True Potential users[]

Zane's True Potential[]

“You will not hurt my friends. There is nothing that will hold me back. I know who I am!”
— Zane to the Treehorn queen, "Tick Tock"

Zane unlocks his True Potential in "Tick Tock," giving him the ability to levitate and harness his Ice powers as beams or explosions. Zane was able to unlock his True Potential upon discovering his past, learning that he was a Nindroid who nonetheless had a loving father and even if he was a Nindroid, he was still human, and then confronting the Treehorn queen in defense of his friends.

Jay's True Potential[]

“Whatever happens next, just remember, you are the best you.”
— Nya to Jay, "Once Bitten, Twice Shy"

Jay unlocks his True Potential in "Once Bitten, Twice Shy", enabling him to fly, teleport short distances, travel through electrical currents, and gain exceptional strength. Jay was able to unlock his True Potential by discovering Nya's feelings for him, and realizing that he simply needs to be himself.

Cole's True Potential[]

“Cole's found his True Potential. His relationship with his father must have been holding him back.”
— Zane, "The Royal Blacksmiths"

Cole unlocks his True Potential in "The Royal Blacksmiths", giving him increased strength, near indestructibility, and levitation. Cole was able to unlock his True Potential because his father respected him for who he was, not what he wanted him to be, strengthening their relationship.

Kai's True Potential[]

“I knew when I had to make a choice. I wanted the Fangblade so badly, to prove I was good enough to become the Green Ninja. But then I figured it out. All of my training to become the best ninja wasn't in preparation to become the Green Ninja. It was... to protect him.”
— Kai to Zane, "The Green Ninja"

Kai unlocks his True Potential in "The Green Ninja". In this state, he is enveloped in a sphere of fire, granting him flight, and the ability to endure extreme heat. He is also able to protect others within this sphere. Kai was able to unlock his True Potential by realizing it was not his destiny to become the Green Ninja in the prophecy, but to protect Lloyd, the current Green Ninja.

Nya's True Potential[]

“Until you embrace what holds you back, you'll never discover your True Potential. Your weakness is you give up too easily. You only like what comes easy. Don't be afraid to fail, Nya.”
— Wu, "Curseworld, Part II"

Nya unlocks her True Potential in "Curseworld; Part II". In this state, she was enveloped in a vortex of water and was able to summon an enormous tidal wave to drown the Preeminent. Nya unlocked her True Potential by accepting her past failures and realizing that the possibility of failure should not stop her from continuing to try.

Garmadon's True Potential[]

“As Wu had previously taught the ninja, you too can unlock your True Potential, a dark potential. But to achieve it, you must overcome the one obstacle that has always stood in your way. The one person who has always held you back.”
— Harumi to Garmadon, "True Potential"

Garmadon began to unlock his True Potential in the episode of the same name. After freeing Harumi and the rest of The Sons Of Garmadon, Garmadon was told to unlock his True Potential by defeating Lloyd without holding back. By defeating Lloyd following their battle at Kryptarium Prison, Garmadon gains access to his true destructive powers and his dormant element of Creation, due to him being a descendant of dragons. Later, he was also able to create a large Colossus with both his Destruction and Creation powers.

However, in "Two Lies, One Truth", Harumi confirms to Garmadon that his potential is not yet complete. To truly unlock it, rather than defeat Lloyd, he must end Lloyd's life. Garmadon came even closer to unlocking his full power when he fought Lloyd and Wu in the Season 9 finale, "Green Destiny". Lloyd managed to prevent Garmadon from achieving this goal by refusing to engage in combat with him, causing him to weaken to the point of losing his power altogether. However, after sparring with the ninja in "Into The Breach", Garmadon regained his powers and can use Creation when he activates his True Potential. In Crystalized, Garmadon would further develop his true potential to its fullest extent, the ability to access his Oni Form, which enhances his dominant element of Destruction and enables him to transform into an Oni. In Oni Form, Garmadon can still utilize his recessive element of Creation if he chooses due to his dragon blood, has immunity to Vengestone and its effects, and can alter the appearance of both his human and oni forms by sprouting wings and horns along with generating or removing his additional set of arms at will.

Sora's True Potential[]

“We've missed you, Ana. Don't abandon us again.”
“I didn't abandon you. You abandoned me! I thought I had to prove myself to you, but you should never have to prove your worth to your own parents.”
“You're young and naïve, Ana. Just come back to your family!”
“I have a new family now. One that loves me for who I really am!”
— Sora and her parents, "The Power Within"

Sora unlocks her True Potential in "The Power Within". In this state, her hands and eyes glow and release a giant burst of power. Sora unlocked her True Potential by realizing that she can do things her own way, such as choosing the ninja as her true family.

Lloyd's True Potential[]

“Each tree is connected. Trees are life. I am the Master of Life.”
— Lloyd unlocking his True Potential, "Dying to Live"

Lloyd unlocks his second True Potential in "Dying to Live". In this state, Lloyd's eyes glow green, allowing him to connect with his ninja team, establish a permanent connection to Life, and sense Source energy flowing throughout the Merged Lands. He could also tap into a unique draconic transformation known as Draconus Form, which he also shares with others through their connections to him. Lloyd unlocked his True Potential by fully understanding what his powers are during the Ritual of Unrooting. While he didn't pass the trials traditionally, his unique life experience and connections with others still allowed him to unlock his True Potential, as noted by the Source Dragon of Energy.

Similar phenomena

Similar phenomena[]

Power of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

“The scrolls gave us powers we have never felt before. It felt as if... we had reached our True Potential in an instant. It was a wonderful feeling. And a terrible one.”
— Master Wu, "Never Trust a Human"

The power of Forbidden Spinjitzu (channeled through two scrolls) drastically enhances powers, to a level that Master Wu directly compared to unlocking one's True Potential. Its power was enough for the young sons of the First Spinjitzu Master to defeat Aspheera's regular Spinjitzu and her guards, as seen in "Never Trust a Human", and enabled Zane to defeat the Hypnobrai sorceress's Pyro Vipers in "Vengeance is Mine!" and freeze the entire Never-Realm in "Corruption". While this power doesn't require the user to overcome emotional obstacles and only needs them to hold a Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu, it slowly corrupts the user, making them aggressive and gradually turning them evil. As of "Awakenings", only one scroll remains after Zane destroyed the one in his former scepter.

Dragon Metamorphosis[]

“It appears dragons go through a metamorphosis when they reach adulthood. They too have found their True Potential.”
— Zane, "Day of the Great Devourer"

Wu asserts that every dragon goes through a distinctive metamorphosis upon reaching maturity, albeit the majority of them must visit the Spirit Coves to complete the process. In "Day of the Great Devourer," when the ninja's dragons were revealed to have molted and merged into one entity during their time at the Spirit Coves, Zane likened the process to the ninja unlocking their True Potential. YinYang Dragons possess the ability to undergo a transformation into either a Light or Shadow Dragon as they mature, a phenomenon intricately linked to how they're raised.

Ritual at the Temple of Light[]

“The scrolls say there is a hidden temple on the island. If you find it, it will give the ninja pure elemental powers, powers that can destroy the indestructible army.”
— Misako, "Island of Darkness"

In "Island of Darkness," after losing their Golden Weapons and discovering the Island of Darkness, the ninja learned of a prophecy in Misako's scrolls that told of a ritual that would unlock their "true elemental powers." Upon enacting this ritual at the Temple of Light, the ninja received their Elemental Blades and stronger powers, similar to when they unlocked their True Potentials.

Lloyd as the Golden Ninja[]

“I am the Ultimate Spinjitzu Master!”
— Lloyd to the Overlord, "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master"

Lloyd gained the power of the Ultimate Spinjitzu Master when the ninja sent him their combined elemental powers of Creation and what remained of their True Potentials[4] within the Temple of Light in "Island of Darkness". In "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master," he unleashed his full power, becoming the Golden Ninja. He had all of the abilities of the First Spinjitzu Master, including massive control over the four main elements, levitation, the ability to summon the Golden Dragon, and manifest Golden Power into a protective sphere and powerful blasts of energy. These powers allowed him to defeat the Overlord for the first time and purge Ninjago of his evil. Lloyd stayed in this state constantly until the Overlord (now reduced to digital form) later drained him of his Golden Power in "Enter the Digiverse".

It is similar to the other Ninja's True Potentials, but there is no clear answer if it is Lloyd's first and actual True Potential, as it is separate from his main power, Life, and it was technically given to Lloyd instead of him achieving it himself.[5] In 2017, Tommy Andreasen said it was "kind of" his potential,[6] and in 2020, replied indicating that it may have not been unlocked in the Temple of Light because the ninja gave their potentials to him.[7] Lloyd himself eventually claimed that he had already reached his True Potential once.[8] This suggests that Lloyd has two distinct True Potential as the Green Ninja: one for Golden Power and another one for Life, his main element. Given the unusual nature of his aging as a result of Tomorrow's Tea in "Child's Play", the order of Lloyd’s advancement was instead played out in reverse with him having acquired his True Potential with Golden Power at the Temple of Light first.

Day of the Dragon[]

“I faced my fear. When I realized it wasn't something in front of me that held me back, but something inside me, I found a deeper power. A dragon power.”
— Zane, "The Day of the Dragon"

Through the Tournament of Elements in Season 4, Zane had dreams of an ice dragon that terrified him. Later, in "Spellbound", Lloyd discovered he is still able to summon his dragon from when he was the Ultimate Spinjitzu Master, now manifested through his original power of Life, by conquering his fear of freefalling to his doom.

In "The Day of the Dragon", after P.I.X.A.L. helped Zane overcome his fear of death when he had a vision of the mysterious ice dragon while wide awake. Eventually, he became able to manifest this dragon in the real world. Using it to fly to the other Elemental Masters, he and Kai (who unlocked his own fire dragon) explained to the other Masters that this power exists within each of them and can be unlocked by facing their fears, encouraging the others to unlock their own Elemental Dragons and fly away together to face Chen and his army of Anacondrai Cultists.

According to The Book of Spinjitzu, Elemental Masters can only unlock their dragons after unlocking their True Potentials.

Empowered by Vengestone[]

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.
“Cole's wish has made our powers too strong. I can't control it.”
— Jay, "Wishmasters"

In "Wishmasters", one wish made by Cole was for Vengestone to make the Ninja's elemental powers stronger instead of disabling them; while the wish did force Nadakhan to make their powers and Spinjitzu stronger, he also made them go out of control, forcing the Ninja to cut the Vengestone chains to regain control of their powers.

This effect of Vengestone was undone alongside most of the events that happened after Nadakhan's release from the Teapot of Tyrahn as a result of being erased by Jay's wish in "The Way Back", causing Vengestone to revert to its original nullifying effects over elemental powers.

Merging with the Endless Sea[]

“I am Nya. I am the sea. We are one.”
— Nya, "The Turn of the Tide"

As a last resort, a Master of Water can merge with the Endless Sea, increasing their power over water exponentially. After Nya did this in Seabound, she was able to control the water in the Entire Endless sea, removing it from Ninjago City after defeating Wojira. She was also able to extract the water in Jay's lungs, which she was previously unable to do.

Her elemental powers increased so much that Aspheera needed an extended amount of time to deprive her of her powers in "A Painful Promise", which also resulted in her first staff being destroyed from the overload.

Dragon Sparks[]

“Uh, you hovered and your eyes were all white and — and you had powers like I've never seen!”
— Kai to Lloyd, "The Battle of the Second Monastery"

Powerful dragons can share a portion of their dragon energy, enabling others to connect with them via an ability known as sparks. When a dragon uses sparks on an Elemental Master, it can result in the awakening of dormant Elemental Powers or the enhancement of skilled Elemental Masters. When Lloyd was boosted by the Matriarch's dragon power, he created a giant tornado of Life Spinjitzu, using it to defeat all of the Claw Hunters. Later, after becoming a conduit empowered by Source Dragon energy, Lloyd used his enhanced Life to power a portal gate.

  • True Potential is represented in the sets as the NRG ninja variant.
  • According to Garmadon in "The Invitation," the other Elemental Masters (such as Skylor, Griffin, Neuro, etc.) have also unlocked their True Potential on their own, but the exact Elemental Masters were not specified.
    • The comic Mother Earth states that Lilly unlocked her True Potential.
    • No Elemental Master that isn't a ninja or affiliated with them was ever seen unlocking/using their True Potential.
  • Of the four original ninja, Zane, Jay, and Cole all have blue glowing eyes when they unlock or use their True Potentials. Kai is the only ninja to have red glowing eyes instead.
  • According to The Book of Spinjitzu, Elemental Masters can only unlock their Elemental Dragons after unlocking their True Potentials.
  • Considering Garmadon's True Potential gave him access to Creation, Wu's True Potential may give him access to his recessive power, Destruction.
  • In the Character Encyclopedia and Character Encyclopedia Updated and Expanded, it was called "full potential" instead. Both terms are used interchangeably in the original series.
  • The term "full potential" is occasionally applied to situations unrelated to this exact power, such as when Garmadon refers to the Golden Weapons' "full potential" in "Darkness Shall Rise" or Lloyd's "full potential" in "Blackout".
  • In "Winds of Change," the Night Watchman tells a then-unseen Morro that he is legally required to share that he's unlocked his full potential. However, this is likely untrue, as it is played as a joke and he also says he knows Spinjitzu.
  • When asked why the ninja stopped using their True Potential powers, Tommy Andreasen explained that "True potential must be maintained."[9] He had also said earlier that they may have lost the abilities once they gave their powers to Lloyd in "Island of Darkness,"[10] and while they regained their powers in "Codename: Arcturus," they no longer have them to the extent that would allow them to use their True Potential abilities.[11]
    • Zane was still able to use beams of ice and create a bomb to destroy the Overlord in "The Titanium Ninja," although this was likely due to absorbing Golden energy from the Golden Armor.
  • Forbidden Spinjitzu, accessed by the Scrolls of Forbidden Spinjitzu, is stated to have power comparable to that of accessing a True Potential.[12] Whether the scrolls truly unlock one's True Potential as long as they wield it or it simply grants them powers similar is unknown. Aside from Zane and Lloyd, when Wu, Garmadon, and the ninja held the scroll, they merely used the enhanced Spinjitzu and never used their Elemental Powers. Zane was able to defeat the Pyro Vipers quickly by blanketing them in ice.
    • Regardless of whether it grants the wielder access to their True Potential, the scroll's powers are shown to be so potent that Zane, as the Ice Emperor following his corruption from using it, was able to overpower Lloyd. Even with the loss of the latter's Golden Power, he was still the most powerful ninja. This indicates that the scroll could be even stronger than a True Potential.
  • Nya's True Potential resembles the Spinjitzu Burst.
  • When Nya, Sora, and Lloyd unlock their True Potentials, their bodies don't glow or become transparent, like the other ninja. The reason for this is unknown.
    • This may be because Water, Technology, and Life are not elements of Creation, unlike like the other ninja's elements.
    • This may also be because the original ninja's True Potentials could have been tied to their Golden Weapons, as seen by the glow in each weapon when the corresponding ninja unlocked his True Potential.
  • In "Wishmasters," Jay compared Nadakhan unlocking infinite wishes to unlocking his True Potential.
  • When asked if the Time Twins or Morro had unlocked their True Potentials, Tommy Andreasen stated, "The[y] didn’t seem like they had overcome the obstacles within to me."[13]
  • The Book of Elemental Powers and the extended synopsis of "Awakenings" state that Kai regains his True Potential in the episode, but when asked about this, Tommy Andreasen simply said, "He was pretty fired up."[14]
  • Currently, Garmadon is the only Elemental Master to have used his true potential the most often, while Kai and Sora are currently the only Elemental Masters to use their True Potential only once.
  • True Potential is the highest level of Elemental Powers accessible to all the Elemental Masters but there are others higher (or equal) abilities accessible to certain Masters in a specific case (these abilities are not included in the classic process of use of Elemental Powers):
    • Spinjitzu Burst: A powerful form of Spinjitzu accessible to any Elemental Master who would be surrounded by their Elemental Power and mastered the teachings to perform the technique.
    • Merging with the Endless Sea: An ability accessible to a Master of Water.
    • Dragon Form: An ability accessible to Elemental Masters possessing one of the four elements of creation, or have dragon blood.
    • Boosted Power: Any Elemental Master can receive energy from dragons to enhance their power.
    • Oni Form: Exclusively only to those of Oni heritage. As demonstrated by both Garmadon and Lloyd, the user doesn't need access to the essence of Destruction as their primarily elemental power but can access it as a recessive ability by utilizing their anger and darker emotions under the proper techniques, akin to a True Potential.
    • Draconus Form: A unique ability unlocked by Lloyd when he discovered his True Potential, which he can share with the other ninja.
  • Lloyd is the only known Elemental Master to have two True Potentials: one for Golden Power, as the Golden Ninja, and another for Life, his main element, which he achieved in Dragons Rising, Season 4. Due to the unusual nature of his aging as a result of Tomorrow's Tea in "Child's Play", the order of Lloyd’s advancement was instead played out in reverse, with him having acquired his Golden Power first at the Temple of Light before he could reach his True Potential with the Ritual of Unrooting.
