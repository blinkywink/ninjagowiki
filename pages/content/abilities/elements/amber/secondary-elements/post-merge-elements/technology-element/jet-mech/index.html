Overview

This article is about the actual mech. For the set, see 71845 Lloyd's Jet Mech.

The Jet Mech is a mech transformable into a jet created by Sora to be used by Lloyd in the Spectral Lands. It was coated in Prismatic Ore to protect against Spectral Dragonians.

After defeating Thunderfang, Lloyd left the mech in the Spectral Lands but got stranded in the Whispering Woods.

History

History[]

The Vault of Focus[]

Sora created the mech from items in the City of Temples' junkyard alongside Arin's Spinjitzu Battle Mech.

For Whom the Bell Tolls[]

Lloyd and Arin brought their mechs to the Spectral Lands.

When Doves Cry[]

Lloyd used the Jet Mech to fight the Spectral Dragonians, Nokt, Rox, and Drix who were guarding Thunderfang. Lloyd used the mech to punch into the cavern beneath the Well of the Lost.

Chaos Reigns[]

Lloyd used the Jet Mech to fight Thunderfang when he realized he and Arin were connected to the Arc Dragon of Focus after using the Unbidden Storm.

After defeating Thunderfang, Lloyd left the Jet Mech near the Monastery of the Spectral Lands.

You must combat Kur, the Master of Decay, Tyr and a Spectral Dragonian Warrior. Sora can attack them on her flying motorcycle – but watch out for this villainous trio’s dangerous specter dragon. Visual Dictionary Updated Edition[]

Lloyd's powerful mech was specially designed by Sora for use in the Spectral Lands. Mostly built from scraps found in the City of Temples' Junkyard, it boasts Prismatic Steel armour to fend off Dragonian attacks. Even better, it can transform into a jet!
