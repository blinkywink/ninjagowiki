Overview

This article is about the mech. For the set, see 71792 Sora's Transforming Mech Bike Racer.
“Wait, that's your mech? Oh ho, no offense, Sora, but, uh, not one of your more impressive builds, is it, hmm?”
“It's easily underestimated. Just like us.”
— Mr. Frohicky and Sora, "The Merge: Part 1"

The SA Mark 01 is a mech that was used by Sora and Arin in mech street races and then only by Sora to fight. Because of her power of Technology, its features can be modified or take form of another type of vehicle.

History

History[]

Prior to Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

First Try![]

Before entering the preliminaries for the Mech Master 5000, Arin and Sora visited the Crossroads junkyard, the two purchased the remains of the Fire Mech from Skalidor. The duo used the remains to build a prototype for their mech. When racing in the Mech Master 5000, they got second place behind Kreel. After the race, Sora made modifications to the mech.

The Way In[]

Some time later, Arin and Sora participated in their first Mech Master 5000, they drove their mech in the race, but lost to Kreel again.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

Sora had upgraded the SA Mark 01 for the Mech Master 5000 by scavenging mechanical parts from the Crossroads' junkyard. However, Kreel had one of the Whack Rats sabotage the mech by cutting off one of its cables, causing it to explode when the turboboost was used. Since they were unaware of this, Sora and Arin raced in the mech against other competitors and were on the verge of winning until they had to forfeit to rescue Riyu from the Claws of Imperium. To evade the Claws, Sora activated the SA Mark 01's turboboost, but the mech exploded and fell off a bridge, breaking it apart. Fortunately, Riyu used dragon power to activate Sora's Technology. Sora converted the SA Mark 01 into a flying mech, using it to outrun the Claw Hunters. Ras arrived and destroyed the mech with the Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength after Sora attempted to attack him with the mech's booster.

Crossroads Carnival[]

The owners of the Crossroads' junkyard gained possession of the SA Mark 01, using it as a display for the Crossroads Carnival as their Security Bots danced around it. When the Whack Rats kidnapped Lobbo, Sora used Technology to transform the SA Mark 01 into a motorcycle, heading off to the junkyard.

Beyond Madness[]

Despite her efforts, Sora was unable to tap into her Elemental Powers to reconfigure the SA Mark 01 once again. Suddenly, the Destiny's Bounty came crashing down, but Sora managed to ride the SA Mark 01 and safely land on the Bounty. To find out about Kai's whereabouts, Sora, Lloyd, Arin, and Riyu, brought the SA Mark 01 with them as they traveled to the Land of Madness. Seeing the Earth Dragons and Craglings fighting, Sora reconfigured the SA Mark 01 into its mech form, equipping herself to combat the Craglings.

As the Earth Dragons made their return, Riyu shared some of his dragon power with Sora, activating her the Elemental Powers to transform the SA Mark 01 into its flight form. Working in tandem with Nya, Sora used Technology to versatile the mech. She transformed one of the mech's hands into a sturdy platform, providing Nya with support during their battle. Sora then swiftly transformed the mech's other hand into a shield, successfully blocking one of the Earth Dragons' attacks. However, Sora began to lose power, causing the SA Mark 01 to falter and lose stability. One of the Earth Dragons launched a fireball that struck the SA Mark 01, breaking it into pieces.

After Riyu managed to calm down the Earth Dragons and save the Craglings from their demise, Sora initiated the repairs on the SA Mark 01.

Writers of Destiny[]

While reconstructing the SA Mark 01, Sora became aware of an assault on the Cloud Kingdom by a creature with tentacles. Sora engaged in combat with the monster using the SA Mark 01 until the ninja successfully repelled it back into the MergeQuake it had emerged from.

Return to Imperium[]

During the ninja's mission to rescue Jiro from the Claws of Imperium at the Crossroads' junkyard, Sora used the SA Mark 01.

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

Sora honed her Elemental Powers by repeatedly practicing them on the SA Mark 01.

The Temple of the Dragon Cores[]

While Sora practicing her mech fighting abilities using the SA Mark 01, Lloyd diligently worked on fixing the damaged wall caused by the Bounty's crash. However, as soon as Lloyd completed the repairs, Sora accidentally crashed into the same wall.

The Power Within[]

While Sora was fixing the SA Mark 01, Riyu extended his assistance by offering to share his dragon energy. However, Sora declined the offer, emphasizing her determination to accomplish the task independently.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

To Mysterium[]

While completely bored around the monastery, Fritz and Spitz went down to the hangar bay, where they board the SA Mark 01, but their lack of driving skills leads them to move around as Cole, Zane, and Geo try to stop it, until Cole uses his Elemental Earth Mech to hold it in position while Zane activates the kill switch on the back of its left wheel.
