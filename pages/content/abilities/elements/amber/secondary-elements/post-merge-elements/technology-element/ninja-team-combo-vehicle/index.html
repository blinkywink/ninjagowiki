Overview

This article is about the vehicle. For the set, see 71820 Ninja Team Combo Vehicle.

The Ninja Team Combo Vehicle is a raider used by the ninja. It was created by Sora using items from the City of Temples' junkyard. It can split into four vehicles: a racer for Lloyd, motorcycles for Cole and Nya, and a glider for Sora.

History

History[]

United We Fall[]

Sora built the vehicle so that she, Cole, Nya and Lloyd could participate in the Cavern Run to earn immunity from exile. During the race, the team entered the cavern’s green zone and toxic goo burned through the vehicle. Nya and Cole used their Elemental Powers to prevent the goo from touching the vehicle.

Truth and Lies[]

Sora split the Combo Vehicle into four to avoid the toxic goo. The team encountered a cave-in and Sora combined the four vehicles together to break through it. They overtook the Pace Dragon and won the race.

Elements of Betrayal[]

Lloyd, Sora, and Wyldfyre used the Combo Vehicle to get to the Monastery of Gates, and they used the glider component when they picked up Zane, Nya, and Cole, who was in the Titan Mech. The glider component was damaged by Nokt, who used a Heat blast, during the fight.

Official description

Official description[]

Team up with the ninja as they face off against two of Lord Ras’s evil crew! You can go into battle in a massive Ninja Team Combo Vehicle or separate it into 4 smaller vehicles to confuse the enemy and attack from all angles! Swoop down from the sky in Sora’s glider with 2 stud shooters and zoom into the action on Lloyd’s off-road racer and Cole and Nya’s motorcycles. Teamwork will win the battle!
