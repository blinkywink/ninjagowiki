Overview

“To live is to grow then wither. Living beings come in all sizes.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Size is an Elemental Power in the Merged Lands that is derived from the Source Dragon of Life.[1] It is associated with the color yellow.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Megethoskinesis - The user can manipulate the size of themselves and any objects they are holding.
    • Limited Shapeshifting - Through the manipulation of their size, the user can slightly change their body shape, though unlike Form, they can't change their body shape apart from size.
    • Size Enhancement - The user can increase the size of themselves or anything and everything to a hulking giant.
      • Strength Enhancement - The user gains an increase in strength when they grow giant, seeing as how Nokt easily crushed Kai with his bare hand while using some of the Elemental Powers he stole or how Lloyd easily knocked Zeatrix off the Games Plaza with a giant hammer.
    • Size Reduction - The user can even reduce their own size, allowing them to become smaller.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • It is unknown if Gorge's large size is a result of his element, as he is larger than every other member of his race but when he was drained of his element during the Tournament of the Sources, he retained his large stature and no other users of Size during the tournament appeared to have any residual effects from having the element.
    • However, it is possible that if his large size is a result of his element, the other users of the element during the tournament didn't have the element long enough for it to (possibly gradually) begin affecting their size passively and the passive size change is either permanent or takes some time to go away and not enough time passed for the reversion to happen or be noticeable.
  • Size is one of the seventeen elements that are currently not known to be confined to a human host. The other sixteen are Ice, Time, Fusion, Balance, Propulsion, Figment, Plants, Quake, Sound, Surface Tension, Reflex, Brute Force, Fear, Swarm, Misfortune and Decay.
    • Size is one of seven elements that allow the user to alter their body, the other six being Smoke, Form, Light, Metal, Shadow, and Swarm.
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt previously stated that among the elements Size, Surface Tension, Figment, and Reflex, three were formed as a result of the Merge, while one already existed beforehand.[4]
    • However, he later stated that Size, Figment and Reflex weren't created by the Merge.[3]
  • Glen Lakin originally stated that Size and Form are from the same Source Dragon,[5] but then later corrected that they are from two different Source Dragons.[6]
  • Size is the only known element of the Life Source known so far that is not associated with a shade of green, apart from Golden Power.
