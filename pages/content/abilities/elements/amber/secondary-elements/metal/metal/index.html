Overview

“The veins of the planet. Hard to break, even harder when it hits back.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Metal is an Elemental Power in the Merged Realms that is derived from the Source Dragon of Strength.[1] It is associated with the color silver/gray and corresponds with the Metal Dragon.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Ferrokinesis/Metallokinesis - The user can create, manipulate, and generate metal.
    • Metal Mimicry - The user is able to turn themselves entirely into metal.
      • Enhanced Strength - When the user is turned into metal, they develop enhanced strength.
      • Enhanced Durability/Resistance - This grants the user enhanced durability, allowing them to resist many attacks.
        • Tough/Hard/Invincible Skin - The user's enhanced durability makes their skin tough and hard, possibly making it invincible.
      • Enhanced Endurance/Stamina - The user can endure many attacks without tiring when they are turned into metal.
      • Deflection - When the user is turned into metal, they are capable of deflecting attacks, normally projectiles or blunt force, away from themselves.
        • Elemental Deflection - The user can deflect elemental powers, as seen when Karlof deflected Kai’s fire attack.
        • Energy Deflection/Immunity - The user can easily deflect energy/lasers, possibly making them immune to it, as shown when Chen deflected Lloyd's energy balls in "The Forgotten Element."
      • Size Enhancement - When the user is turned into metal, they slightly grow in size.
    • Metal Constructs - The user appears to be able to create objects out of metal, as seen in "The Forgotten Element", when Karlof manifested his metal fists after regaining his power.
    • Dragon Creation - The user can create a Metal Dragon.
Symbol

Symbol[]

The symbol of Metal appears to be a gear shape. It has been seen on Karlof's back, alongside a less detailed version of it on the lower front of his torso.
