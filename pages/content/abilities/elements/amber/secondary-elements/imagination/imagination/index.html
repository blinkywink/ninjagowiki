Imagination is an Elemental Power of Planet Crux (now destroyed), possibly based on or a smaller version of the Tornado of Creation. The only known users of it are Neido and Master Wu.

It is associated with gray and azure and its Spinjitzu tornado appears as a whirling vortex of blue and white light surrounded by small circles of similar colors. It can be performed by using the Imagination Double-Bladed Dagger or Master Wu's Imagination staff.

User's abilities

User's abilities[]

  • Imagination Manipulation - The user can manipulate all they imagine, allowing them to create a new existence, become and do anything they want (i.e. controlling and recreating the universe or manipulating art to create drawings and conjure/transmute objects with artistic means), manipulate the imagination of themselves and others, etc.
    • Essokinesis - The user can manipulate, generate, and even control/alter reality either normally, verbally (and possibly even use it to release a verbal beatdown), and mentally to manipulate, create, or control anything of their desire, such as cosmic forces and energies to shoot cosmic beams.
    • Imaginary Constructs - The user can use their imagination to create tools, weapons, and other items. The users who have mastered this ability can use it for almost any situation, creating anything they need, such as cosmic constructs.
    • Imagination Creation - Users can bring any materials, beings, or tools originating from their imagination into existence and literally bring their wildest ideas into reality.
    • Imagination Blast - The user can charge their body with the power of Imagination and release it as a powerful energy blast (using the Imagination Double-Bladed Dagger).
    • Unlimited Power - As seen with Neido, the user may possess unlimited imaginative powers.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In LEGO Universe, players can acquire the Imagination Ninja Gear after a series of missions.
  • Imagination Spinjitzu was taught by Wu in the early updates, but Neido took up the role after the Crux Prime update.
  • As described by the Double-Bladed Dagger, Imagination is a fusion of Ice and Lightning.
  • Neido is considered a master of Imagination Spinjitzu and is implied to have created the style.
  • In LEGO Universe, Imagination Spinjitzu was going to have three other variations: Meowjitzu, Funjitzu, and Cutejitzu. However, they, along with the characters who would introduce players to them, were removed.
  • When asked if Imagination would ever be canonized, Chris "Doc" Wyatt replied with, "Possibly".[1]
    • It is Chris "Doc" Wyatt's favourite element.[2]
