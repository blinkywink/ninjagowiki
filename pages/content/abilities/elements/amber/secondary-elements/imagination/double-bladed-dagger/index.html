Overview

The Double-Bladed Dagger is a weapon from Ninjago. It is a silver dagger with large, triangular spike-like blades on both ends, described as being a useful weapon for inexperienced fighters to use. There is a single-sided version of this, known as the Golden Nick Dagger.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Twice the fun of a regular dagger, this handy weapon can attack danger from either side. It’s a great weapon for the new hero.[1][2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In LEGO Universe, a weapon known as the "Imagination Spinjitzu Double-Bladed Dagger" appears. It is identical to the Double-Bladed Dagger, but it allows the user to perform "Imagination Spinjitzu," a unique blue and white Spinjitzu tornado.
  • In "The Golden Weapon" and "Weapons of Destiny", several Skulkin are seen wielding similar weapons to the Double-Bladed Daggers.
  • In "Once Bitten, Twice Shy", Lizaru is seen wielding a weapon similar to the Double-Bladed Dagger when he and the other Serpentine are confronted by the Ninja.
