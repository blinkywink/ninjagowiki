The Ninja Combat Vehicle is a vehicle created by Geo using his powers and used by Cole, Kai, and Frak. It splits into two bikes and a jet.

History

History[]

Casket of Bones[]

After Frak accidentally uses his Quake powers and sensed something screaming in the earth, Geo brought the Ninja Combat Vehicle since Kai wasn't in to going on a walking journey. Cole was surprised that Geo created the vehicle but Geo said he fused a bunch of parts that ended up in the Land of Lost Things. Cole commented Geo could give Sora a run for her money, as Kai jumped onto the bike.

The Screaming Earth[]

Cole, Kai, and Frak drove the Combat Vehicle to the location where Cole followed the ghost of Master Wu's voice. However the terrain was different but Cole located it from spiked rocks and merged the bikes with the jet.

Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon[]

After unearthing the Source Dragon of Balance's remains, Cole, Frak, and Kai drove the Combat vehicle back to the Land of Lost Things. Cole and Frak switch veichles with Frak flying the jet and Cole driving the bike. When the three arrived back, they noticed the Finders were fighting something invisible and got their souls drained. After seeing Nokt and the Spectral Dragonians, the three Ninja drove the vehicle to chase Nokt.

The Vault of Focus[]

Cole, Frak, and Kai tracked Nokt to Imperium, where he was stealing souls. After Percival Tartigrade told them a way to upgrade their Prismatic Blades to defeat the Spectral Dragonians, Cole refused and took the jet to fight Nokt while Kai and Frak drove the bikes to the Advanced Systems Lab to upgrade their Prismatic Steel swords. After the scientist brought out the Reveal Blades, Kai and Frak drove back to the center, where Cole's jet was destroyed by Nokt and his soul was soon taken.
