Overview

“A bond between two objects, stronger together than separate.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Fusion is an Elemental Power in the Merged Lands that was created as a result of the Merge[2] and is derived from the Source Dragon of Strength.[1] It is associated with a multitude of colors but mostly white, lavender/purple, green, yellow, and light blue.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Fusionism/Object Fusion - The user can fuse objects together, creating a strong bond between any item and making them into a single unit.
    • Separation - The user can separate objects previously assembled.
    • Adhesion - The user can use Fusion to make objects stick together.
    • Compound Fusion - The user can also fuse parts of a whole to rebuild or fix an object. Geo used this to fix a broken pencil when Sora snapped it in two and later to fix the Imperium portal gate.
    • Weapon Fusion - As seen in "The Sword Shatters", the user can fuse weapons together.
    • Irisgenesis - When using Fusion, the user releases a spread/flow of energy akin to a rainbow.
      • Rainbow Beam Emission - Users like Geo also appear to release a beam of rainbow energy that runs along objects when fusing them together.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Living Beings - The user is unable to fuse living things.
  • Object Fusion Limit - Although the bond between items is shown to be powerful, it has limits. This was seen when the Wolf warriors dug into the wall that Cole and the Finders were hiding in with enough time after being empowered into Wolf Mask Claw Warriors.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Fusion is one of the seventeen elements that are currently not known to be confined to a human host. The other sixteen are Ice, Time, Balance, Propulsion, Figment, Plants, Quake, Reflex, Size, Sound, Surface Tension, Brute Force, Fear, Misfortune, Swarm and Decay.
    • It is also the first one of these elements that has never been used by a human.
  • Fusion is one of 9 Elemental Powers to have more than two colors associated with them. The other 8 are Heat, Earth, Lightning, Time, Fire, Mind, Decay, and Gravity.
  • So far, Fusion is one of four Elemental Powers that are known to have been created by the Merge, the others being Propulsion, Surface Tension, and Technology.
  • When asked if Geo was incapable of fusing any specific matter or material, Kevin Burke responded, "Not that we have discovered yet."[3]
