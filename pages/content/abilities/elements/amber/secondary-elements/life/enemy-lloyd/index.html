Enemy Lloyd is one of the three metaphysical representations of Lloyd in the latter's Ritual of Unrooting, representing Lloyd's mistakes, doubts, and internal conflict within him since he became a master.

History

History[]

Dying to Live[]

As Lloyd continues his journey of the Ritual of Unrooting, he's warped into the courtyard of the Monastery of Spinjitzu, thinking it's just another vision, until he's confronted by a dark version of himself. As they meet, Enemy Lloyd begins telling Lloyd how he's his own enemy, and how poor of a leader he is, always trying to blame others for his failures and reminding him of when he didn't prioritize rescuing Arin's parents, when he also failed to find Jay and caused him to shatter his goodness, and reminding him about Master Wu being alive, with the reason of him not coming back was presumably because of how he was disappointed of Lloyd's inability to lead the ninja. Refusing to believe his dark self's lies, Lloyd immediately attacks him and the two continue fighting.

After both of them clashed with their Elemental Power and Rising Dragon, Enemy Lloyd further taunts Lloyd that now even the Source Dragons themselves are furious with him while claiming that he's done more damage to the Merged Lands than Ras. As they keep fighting again, Enemy Lloyd corners Lloyd to a cliff, telling him he might have stood a chance if he had taken the right path, and was about to finish him off when the latter stopped him, acknowledging that he's made mistakes and he isn't perfect, but without those mistakes, he would never have learned from them and become who he is today. However, Enemy Lloyd refuses to believe his original self can live up to who he claims to be and kicks him off the cliff as the Green Ninja is then warped again into the Source Dragons' void.

Weapons and abilities

Weapons and abilities[]

Abilities[]

  • Life: Enemy Lloyd possesses his original counterpart's element.
    • Spinjitzu: Enemy Lloyd is able to perform Spinjitzu.
    • Rising Dragon: Enemy Lloyd is able to perform the Rising Dragon technique.
  • Master Combatant: Enemy Lloyd showed no difficulty keeping up with Lloyd in a one-on-one fight with or without weapons, even knocking him down with only his foot.
  • Art of the Silent Fist: Enemy Lloyd is able to perform the Art of the Silent Fist as he uses it when Lloyd is trying to fight him.
  • Mirror Immersion/Self-Duplication: Enemy Lloyd could enter crystal mirrors and duplicate himself within them.
  • Teleportation: Enemy Lloyd was able to teleport himself to avoid attacks from Lloyd.
  • Spatial Manipulation: Enemy Lloyd can manipulate space as he managed to shift the atmosphere (stars, sun and moon) during the Ritual of Unrooting upon facing Lloyd.
    • Spatial Telekinesis: Enemy Lloyd can interact with phenomena through spatial manipulation as he was able to move the sun and moon at will.
  • Reality Warping: Enemy Lloyd can alter reality as he was able to warp the monastery wall into the edge of a cliff.
    • Pocket Reality Manipulation: Enemy Lloyd can also control pocket dimensions as he also warped the space that Lloyd fell into by changing it into a glass-like sky inside his dimension.

Weapons[]

  • Golden sword: Enemy Lloyd uses a sword that is identical to Lloyd's. With it, he was able to clash with him on numerous occasions during their fight.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Enemy Lloyd is similar to Harumi prior to her redemption in terms of personality, as both constantly reminded Lloyd of his past mistakes and attempted to manipulate him.
    • Additionally, Harumi's theme can be heard while he torments Lloyd.
  • He wears a dark green version of his original incarnate’s Ghost Hunter outfit, albeit without the shoulder pads.
    • His element is a more tealish shade of green compared to Lloyd's. Additionally, the sound effect of him activating his Rising Dragon sounds different than the normal activation sound.
  • Enemy Lloyd is similar to Shadow Wu, as both were dark manifestations of Lloyd and Wu who represent doubts within the protagonists and tried to break the hero's spirit by exploiting their weakness.
  • Enemy Lloyd is similar to the Ghost of Christmas Yet to Come from A Christmas Carol, as they both torment the main character, bring misery to them, and try to kill them.
  • Enemy Lloyd's Spinjitzu tornado is similar to the Evil ninja's Spinjitzu tornados where it's a darker, duller version of Lloyd's Spinjitzu tornado.
