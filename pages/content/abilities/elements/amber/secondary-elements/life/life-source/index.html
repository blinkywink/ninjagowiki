Overview

This article is about the Source. For the element, see Life.
“The heartbeat of the universe, Life is not just the means but the will to survive.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Life is a form of dragon energy and one of the seven Sources, personified by the Source Dragon of Life. It is associated with the color green. The Whispering Wood serves as a sacred reservoir for Life energy.[9]

User's abilities

User's abilities[]

  • Vivokinesis - The user is able to control or influence the very essence of life.
    • Life Empowerment - The user can gain strength from Life.
  • Dragon Icon Creation - As it is a form of dragon energy, the user can create a Source Dragon Icon.

Higher-level powers[]

  • Elemental Power Creation/Bestowal - The user can create and grant others Elemental Powers sourced from Life.
    • Potentikinesis Bestowal - When used and combined with other forms of Source Dragon energy, Life can bestow the Elemental Essence of Golden Power to other beings.
    • Ergokinesis/Vitakinesis Bestowal - Life can bestow the Elemental Power of Life to other beings.
    • Ecokinesis Bestowal - Life can bestow the Elemental Power of Nature to other beings.
    • Toxikinesis Bestowal - Life can bestow the Elemental Power of Poison to other beings.
    • Chlorokinesis Bestowal - Life can bestow the Elemental Power of Plants to other beings.
    • Megethoskinesis Bestowal - Life can bestow the Elemental Power of Size to other beings.
  • Dimensional Rift Creation - As seen with the Dragon Cores, which is made from Life and the other sources, users can tear rifts in the fabric of reality, exposing the Ethereal Divide.
    • Vacuum Effect - Such rifts are identical to MergeQuakes and have a vacuum effect that sucks nearby objects or people in.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Before revealing its Source, Glen Lakin suggested that Amber comes from either Life or Energy, with Life being a possibility since the dragon energy that created Amber came from a tree.[10]
  • When revealing the Elemental Powers coming from the source of Life, Glen Lakin used these poetic descriptions:
    • Nature - One with the leaves and the dirt, able to read the writing in bark or whisper through spores on the wind. Imbued with the power to command these forces to defend the natural world.[5]
    • Poison - A corruption that turns a body against itself, deadly yet beautiful in its effectiveness.[6]
    • Plants - The greenest of thumbs, curation and growth of vegetation on a grand scale beyond what is normally possible. Not a commander but a caretaker.[7]
    • Size - To live is to grow then whither. Living beings come in all sizes.[8]
    • Life - Able to tap into an energy that [redacted] all living things, to use or [redacted].[4]
