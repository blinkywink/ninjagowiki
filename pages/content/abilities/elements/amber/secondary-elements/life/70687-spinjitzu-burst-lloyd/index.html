Overview

70687 Spinjitzu Burst Lloyd is a LEGO Ninjago - Master of the Mountain set that was released on June 1, 2020 in most countries, on August 15, 2020 in most US stores, and on August 24, 2020 in Canada and LEGO stores in the US. It includes Spinjitzu Burst Lloyd with an interchangeable hood and conical hat, an Energy Spinjitzu Burst spinner, and a launcher.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Spinjitzu Burst Lloyd is exclusive to this set, despite him not using the Burst in the show.
  • This is one of three Spinners sets to include a rubber band, the other two being the other Spinjitzu Burst sets.
  • This is the first set to include the conical hat piece in silver.
