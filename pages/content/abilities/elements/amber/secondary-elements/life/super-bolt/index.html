This topic is considered non-canon because it never appeared in the TV series.

The Super Bolt is a double-ended golden scythe with green blades that is used by Lloyd.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Considering its name, the Super Bolt may be intended to be an upgrade to the Golden Bolt used by Lloyd in a previous set.
  • Just like the spinner sets of Cole ZX, Zane ZX, and Jay ZX, the Super Bolt does not appear in the TV series.
    • Despite this, Lloyd can be seen holding the weapon in a promotional poster for Season 1: Rise of the Snakes.
  • Another weapon Jay equips in 5000030 Kendo Jay Promotional Set resembles the the Super Bolt.
