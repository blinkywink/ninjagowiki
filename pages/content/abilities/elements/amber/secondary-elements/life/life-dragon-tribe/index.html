The Life Dragon Tribe[3] are a dragon tribe in the Land of Monsters who wield the Elemental Power of Life.

Powers and abilities

Powers and abilities[]

  • Flight - Life Dragons can fly using their wings.
  • Pyrokinetic Breath - Life Dragons can shoot fire from their mouth.
  • Telepathy - Life Dragons can telepathically communicate with other living beings.
  • Life - Life Dragons possess the element of Life.[1]
    • Life Energy Breath - Life Dragons can shoot life energy from their mouth.
