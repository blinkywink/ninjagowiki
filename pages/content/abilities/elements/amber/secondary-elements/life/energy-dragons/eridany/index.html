Eridany[2] is the current Matriarch of the Mountain Tribe, the daughter of the late Matriarch and the next leader of their herd.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Shape of Motion[]

Eridany was carried by the Matriarch of the Mountain Dragons within one of the latter's nursing pouches after she had been attacked by Nokt and Rox for her Crowns of Horns. Desperate to carry her daughter to safety, the Matriarch flew as far as she could before collapsing in the Land of Madness. Following her mother's passing, Eridany cried out, leading to her discovery by the Ninja and Riyu, the latter of whom Eridany immediately recognized and reacted happily to seeing again.

The Ninja then vowed to return Eridany to her tribe, with Eridany riding atop Riyu's back as they flew in search of the Mountain Dragons' new home. After several hours of flying and searching, the group was attacked by Rapton and the Freebooters, who were intent on capturing Eridany to sell to the highest bidder. During the aerial battle, Rapton threatened Eridany with his sword's taser configuration but he and the Freebooters were driven away seconds later by the arrival of a group of Mountain Dragons. This group of Mountain Dragons then escorted the Ninja to their new home in the World Forest, where Eridany was reunited with her tribe.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Their Draconic Majesty's Request[]

Eridany greeted Arin when he was brought to the home of the Mountain Dragons within the World Forest, remembering him as one of her rescuers. She then requested for Leaf Blade to assist Arin in rescuing his master, a request which the the latter dragon obliged.

Behind the scenes[]

  • Although her name was never shown or said in the show, her name was later revealed by Chris "Doc" Wyatt on X.[2]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Unlike typical baby Mountain Dragons, she has a unique stripe pattern instead of spots and has a similar skin color as her mother (lime for Eridany and dark green for the Matriarch).
  • She is referred as the Matriarch of the Mountain Tribe in "Their Draconic Majesty's Request," while her mother was called the Matriarch of the Mountain Dragons.
  • She, Firstbourne, Urd Leatherwing and Thunderfang are the only Arc Dragons to be have their actual names revealed, although it is unclear what Firstbourne and Thunderfang's Arc Dragon titles are or if they have one.
  • According to Kevin Burke, the reason Eridany and her mother have the Element of Life while they are mountain dragons is because Life connects all things, meaning they were able to connect to the power and granted them access to it.[3]
