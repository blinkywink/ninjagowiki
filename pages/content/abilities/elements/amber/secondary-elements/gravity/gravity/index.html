Overview

“The pull of a larger body on a smaller one, you can resist but you cannot escape.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Gravity is an Elemental Power in the Merged Lands that is derived from the Source Dragon of Strength.[1] It is associated with the colors red/maroon, black, or purple/violet. It corresponds with the Gravity Dragon.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Gravikinesis/Gyrokinesis - The user can manipulate, create, control, increase, decrease, generate, and negate gravitational fields.
    • Self-Gravikinesis - Some users can manipulate their own gravity.
    • Levitation/Weightlessness Inducement - By removing a target's gravitational field, the user of Gravity can make them float freely in the air, even in high speed.
      • Gyrokinetic Flight - The user can also create gravity fields to propel themselves through the air.
        • Orbitakinesis - As seen with Gravis in "Versus," the user appears to be able to manipulate and generate their own orbit and orbital force while flying.
    • Wall Crawling - The user can redirect a gravitational field to enable people and objects to stick to and walk on walls or ceilings.
    • Gyro-Telekinesis - The user can move objects and control anything he or she wants using Gravity. They can also influence, manipulate, or move objects and matter from great distances, usually to attack or defend against an adversary.
      • Trajectorikinesis - The user can possibly use gravity to control and curve an object/projectile's movements/path.
    • Gravity Aura - Some users can possibly create an aura of Gravity when using their powers.
  • Mist Generation - As seen with Gravis, the power of Gravity can be emitted as a black mist.[3]
  • Dragon Creation - The user can create a Gravity Dragon.
Symbol

Symbol[]

The symbol of Gravity resembles an eight-pointed throwing star, though it could also indicate how it can move in any direction.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Despite the fact that Gravity appears as a black mist in the show, it appears as purplish force fields around Gravis' hands in the set.
  • Gravity is one of 9 Elemental Powers to have more than two colors associated with them. The other 8 are Heat, Decay, Earth, Lightning, Time, Fire, Mind, and Fusion.
  • Before revealing the Source of Gravity, Glen Lakin gave the hint, "Gravity is the PULL of one object on another."[4] Implying Gravity is from the Source Dragon of Strength.
