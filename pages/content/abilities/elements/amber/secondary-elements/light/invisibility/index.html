Overview

“Did you see me? I disappeared! I mean, did you not see me?”
— Cole to Wu, "Infamous"

Invisibility is a common power in Ninjago, accessed through abilities such as Light and Anacondrai invisibility.

Known types

Known types[]

  • Anacondrai invisibility: All members of the Anacondrai tribe have the natural ability to turn to turn invisible even with the clothes and objects they hold, although some objects the user is holding may not be able to turn invisible.
  • Nindroid invisibility: Nindroids created by P.I.X.A.L. can use technology to disappear even with the objects they hold.
  • Light invisibility: The Elemental Power of Light allows the user to disappear even with clothes, although other objects cannot disappear with them. It can also make the user's body invisible so that only their clothes are still visible.
  • Shadow invisibility: The Elemental Power of Shadow allows the user to become invisible and undetectable in darkness and shadow.
  • Ghost invisibility: Ghosts can also use invisibility, although they can be traced by infrared/thermal vision because they still emit heat. Some Ghosts like the Soul Suckers and Spectral Dragonians have hidden spiritual energy that can only be seen by Prismatic Steel and cannot be seen by the regular eye.
Weakness

Weakness[]

  • Invisibility Limitation - Invisibility is not foolproof, this is shown that the user is invisible, not inaudible, as substances can get onto their skin or the invisible user can be effected by the environment like Pythor being taken out by Jay's Lightning.
    • Corrosive substances - It has been discovered that materials like dust and dirt can expose the invisible person, such as when Cole was able to render Mr. Pale visible by knocking dirt onto him, or when Lloyd used Spinjitzu to create dust on the Master of Light. Others can also use different methods to locate the invisible user, such as echolocation. Cole was seen to create footprints from the oil barrel, as a ghost to infiltrate Nadakhan's room.
    • Paranormal essence detector/Thermal vision - Excluding the Anacondrai, invisible people still emit heat.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • While Anacondrai invisibility allows the user to disappear even with the objects he touches, as Pythor demonstrated when he disappeared with the Map of Dens or with the Fangblades, the elemental invisibility does not allow this, since Mr. Pale couldn't turn the tray invisible during lunch. However, he could still turn his clothes invisible.
  • Depending on the type, invisibility has a few weaknesses. Cole was able to render Mr. Pale visible by knocking dirt onto him,[1] while Ronin was able to spot Cole as a ghost using thermal vision.[2]
    • However, thermal vision would not work on the Anacondrai, as all the Serpentine are cold-blooded.
