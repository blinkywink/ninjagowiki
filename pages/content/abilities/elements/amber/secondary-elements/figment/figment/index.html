Overview

“What you see is not always what you get. Figment can focus the mind’s eye on an idea rather than reality.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Figment is an Elemental Power in the Merged Lands that is derived from the Source Dragon of Focus.[1] It is associated with the color white/ivory.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Hallucikinesis/Mirakinesis - The user can manipulate and cast illusions, allowing them to create entirely fabricated constructs.[4]
    • Mímisikinesis - The user can create and control copies, duplicates, and replications.
      • Self-Duplication - The user can duplicate themselves, allowing them to misdirect their enemies.
        • Decoy Creation: The user can outnumber his or her enemies simply by creating more clones as decoys.
        • Incorporeality/Intangibility - The users' clones lack a physical body, causing solid matter and attacks to slip through them.
        • Duplicate Assimilation - The user can join with their clones back into one.
        • Object/Weapon Duplication - If the user is holding an object or weapon while duplicating, they can also create replicates of the said object or weapon.
        • Elemental power usage - As shown by Nokt in "Elements of Betrayal", the user's clones are capable of using various Elemental Powers. However, this is dependent on the other elements held by the host.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Clone Limitations - The user's clones have several limits:
    • Speech Inability - The user's clones are unable to speak as seen in "The Sword Shatters", when Zeatrix spoke, her clones didn't do the same.
    • Low Durability - The user's clones do not appear to be all that durable, as seen when Lloyd easily destroyed Zeatrix's clones in "The Sword Shatters". This is also shown with Algu's clones were easily destroyed with Teelk's direct sound blast.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • It is unknown what is the maximum number of clones Figment can make.
  • Figment is one of the seventeen elements that are currently not known to be confined to a human host. The other sixteen are Ice, Time, Fusion, Balance, Propulsion, Plants, Quake, Size, Sound, Surface Tension, Reflex, Brute Force, Fear, Swarm, Misfortune and Decay.
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt previously stated that among the elements Size, Surface Tension, Figment, and Reflex, three were formed as a result of the Merge, while one already existed beforehand.[5]
    • However, he later stated that Size, Figment, and Reflex weren't created by the Merge.[3]
  • With enough training, a Master of Figment can do a lot more than just multiply themselves or other objects.[6]
  • When asked about Figment being a Focus element due to the attention toward an idea instead of reality and if it means that Algu can create various illusions beyond just clones and even manipulate reality to some degree, Glen Lakin clarified that Algu cannot alter reality itself; he can only influence how others perceive it. In combat, creating clones is easy for him since his mind is already focused on fighting someone.[4]
  • Figment is one of the only Elements that causes the user's body to glow, alongside Size, Form, Reflex, and Propulsion.
