Overview

“You're all clones?”
“Nope! Lee is just Lee! I'm one person who happens to have more than one body.”
— Nya and Lee's multiple bodies, "Between You and Lee"

Self-Duplication is a common power in the Merged Realms, accessed through abilities such as Figment and Magic.

Known types

Known types[]

  • Magic duplication: Magic allows users to make copies of themselves, as seen with Nadakhan in "The Way Back."
  • Figment duplication: The Elemental Power of Figment allows the user to make copies of themselves.
  • Mitosis: A form of duplication used by Lee to create numerous perfect copies of oneself that can be controlled through a passive hive mind connecting all replicas.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Clone Limitations - The user's clones have several limits.
    • Speech Inability - With an exception of Lee, Figment and Magic clones are unable to speak.
    • Low Durability - The user's clones do not appear to be all that durable, as seen when the Ninja used their elemental powers to destroy several of Nadakhan's clones. Even Dogshank and Flintlocke took out several clones on their own and Lloyd easily destroyed Zeatrix's clones in "The Sword Shatters".
