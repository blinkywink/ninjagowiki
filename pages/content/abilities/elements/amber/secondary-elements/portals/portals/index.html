Overview

This article is about the entities. For the element, see Portals (element).
“And even if they do show, any one of us they strike down can simply return through the portal.”
— Wrayth to Morro, "Curseworld, Part I"

Portals are gateways created when various beings travel between realms or generally through various realities. They can be temporary or permanent.

History

History[]

Prior to the series[]

During the War of Oni and Dragon, the First Spinjitzu Master created an object capable of creating portals to all the Realms of Creation except the Departed Realm known as the Realm Crystal and subsequently used it to leave the Realm of Oni and Dragons and travel to another realm containing a planet with an infinite sea ruled by Wojira after befriending the Firstbourne upon realizing both the Oni and the Dragons were too busy fighting over controlling him to peacefully end the war.

At unknown points in time two hard to access portals leading to different realms would appear in the skies of Ninjago, one taking a form of a hurricane known as the Blind Man's Eye only accessible from the summit of the Wailing Alps which lead to the Cloud Kingdom and the other taking the form of a dimensional tear in the fabric of reality leading to the Departed Realm which allows cursed ghosts and departed souls to regain their mortal bodies, but can only be accessed via the Yin Blade on the night of the Yin-Yang Eclipse that occurs every 3,721 years. At some point a portal formed or was created in a lake located in a building in Ninjago establishing a permanent connection between Ninjago and the Wyldness.[1] At some point in the Realm of Madness, a permanent one way portal was created at the summit of the Mountain of Madness.

Corruption (flashback)[]

While wandering the Never-Realm in search of power and food after abandoning the village of the Formlings and being rejected by Grimfax, the Formling Vex witnessed a portal appear in the sky which released a cannon from 60 years into the future of Ninjago that Aspheera banished there. When he approached to investigate another portal appeared which released the right arm of the Titan Mech, followed by another which released the rest of the Titan Mech, and a final portal which released Zane armed with a naginata which had a Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu attached. While falling, Zane dropped the scroll and landed in front of Vex as the portal closed.

Shatterspin[]

The Wyldness[]

Shortly before the Serpentine War, Chen told Garmadon of the Forbidden Five and how amd where he could find them and learn the secrets of Shatterspin. As a result, the latter traveled to the Wyldness via the portal pool.

Homecoming[]

After helping liberate the Wyldness from Zaiden Kane and break their curse over the realm, Garmadon was asked by a Wolf Warrior to take the Mallet of Shattering back to Ninjago so it would never be used for evil again. In response Garmadon bid the Children of the Wolf and freed Wolf Warriors farewell before swimming back through the portal pool and destroying it once he returned to Ninjago.

Spellbound (flashback)[]

Following the Serpentine War, the Anacondrai generals were sentenced to banishment to the Cursed Realm for their many war crimes. In order to carry out their punishment, Garmadon used the Book of Spells to create a portal to the Cursed Realm and imprison the Anacondrai generals within it.

Pilot Episodes[]

King of Shadows[]

After ambushing and capturing Cole, Jay, and Zane in the Forest of Tranquility, the Skulkin managed to claim the Scythe of Quakes, Nunchucks of Lightning, and Shurikens of Ice and store them aboard the Skull Truck. While the Skull Truck was gaining enough speed to travel back to the Underworld with the Ninja and Golden Weapons the Ninja managed to escape and defeat the Skulkin, though they failed to reclaim the Golden Weapons and the Skulkin generals Nuckal, Kruncha, and Samukai managed to drive the Skull Truck through the portal it created to the Underworld with the Golden Weapons aboard once it gained enough speed.

Weapons of Destiny[]

After learning from Nya that dragons can travel to the Underworld, the Ninja allied themselves with the Fire Dragon Flame, Ice Dragon Shard, Earth Dragon Rocky, and Lightning Dragon Wisp to assist them, then proceeded to ride the dragons through portals they created to the Ethereal Divide and eventually the Underworld once they gained enough speed to reclaim the Golden Weapons and rescue Wu.

After Samukai defeated Wu and claimed the Sword of Fire, he was quickly overloaded and killed by the combined power of all the Golden Weapons. As a result, a portal to the Realm of Madness was created and Lord Garmadon fled through it, leaving the Golden Weapons behind as it closed.

Mini-movies[]

The New Masters of Spinjitzu[]

Kruncha and Nuckal opened a portal to Ninjago using the Skull Truck so that they could steal the Golden Weapons from the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

Season 1: Rise of the Snakes[]

Tick Tock[]

Using the Traveler's Tea he purchased from Mystake, Wu created a portal to the Realm of Madness and stepped through it where he was greeted by Lord Garmadon.

The Royal Blacksmiths[]

After defeating the Craglings at the summit of the Mountain of Madness, Wu and Garmadon arrived at the portal back to Ninjago. At the sight, Garmadon kicked Wu towards the portal but the latter managed to grab a ledge on the way down and inform the former Lloyd was in danger. In response, Garmadon told his brother he'd see him on the other side before kicking Wu through the portal and jumping in himself. As a result, a portal opened in the sky above Jamanakai Village in Ninjago and sent the Sons of the First Spinjitzu Master flying out.

Season 2: Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

Ninjaball Run[]

Just before the annual Ninjaball Run began, Kruncha and Nuckal opened a portal to Ninjago City using the Skull Truck to participate in the race.

Between Seasons[]

Green and Gold (flashback)[]

At some point after the Final Battle, the Ultra Dragon flew to the Spirit Coves followed by Lloyd. Upon arriving, Lloyd noticed the Ultra Dragon was homesick and told it to return home. In response, the Ultra Dragon created a portal to the Realm of Oni and Dragons and flew through where it flew towards Firstbourne's nest with the Firstbourne, the Lightning Dragon Stormbringer, the Earth Dragon Slab, a Fire Dragon, and a Wind Dragon.

Season 4: Tournament of Elements[]

The Day of the Dragon[]

During his fight with Garmadon, Clouse created a portal to the Cursed Realm with his Dark Magic and attempted to banish him into it. Garmadon however managed to jump over it and grab Clouse with a vine, then throw him in as the portal closed.

The Corridor of Elders[]

To defeat the Anacondrai-transformed Anacondrai Cultists, end the Second Serpentine War, and make up for his past mistakes, Garmadon opted to release the Anacondrai generals from the Cursed Realm. In order to do so however he would have to take their place in it. After Wu opened a portal to the Cursed Realm with the Book of Spells, Garmadon ascended through and the ghosts of the Anacondrai generals and Morro were freed. Upon seeing the Anacondrai Cultists, the generals forced them all through the portal with Arcturus personally calling out Chen for his lies and manipulation that started the first Serpentine War. After the Anacondrai Cultists were cursed, the portal closed and the Anacondrai generals passed onto the Departed Realm.

Grave Danger[]

Upon exiting the Tomb of the First Spinjitzu Master after claiming the Realm Crystal, Morro spotted Bansha and Soul Archer clinging to the mast of the sinking Ghost ship. In response, he used the Realm Crystal to create a portal through which the ghosts flead.

Curseworld, Part I[]

With the Realm Crystal now in his possession, Morro used it to create a portal to the Cursed Realm in Stiix and released Wrayth, Soul Archer, Bansha, and Ghoultar before releasing the rest of the Ghost Warriors as the portal grew bigger and stronger. When Nya wearing Lloyd's gi was revealed by Morro she used her power of Water to defeat the Ghost Warriors only for them to immediately return via the portal. As the portal was nearing its full strength, Lloyd attempted to destroy the Realm Crystal with Energy only for Morro to remind him that doing so would prevent him from ever seeing his father again. Morro's comment distracted Lloyd long enough for the portal to reach its full strength and allow the Preeminent to stick her tentacles through, grab the Realm Crystal, and drag Lloyd into the Cursed Realm before emerging from the portal.

Curseworld, Part II[]

The Preeminent proceeded to return the Realm Crystal to Morro once she fully emerged. After escaping the Cursed Realm, Lloyd seized the Sword of Sanctuary and began battling Morro. During the battle, Morro used the Realm Crystal to open a portal to the Underworld in an attempt to trap Lloyd there then opened a portal to the Realm of Madness to escape. Lloyd however managed to follow him using Airjitzu. Upon arriving there Morro dropped the Realm Crystal at the summit of the Mountain of Madness but quickly reclaimed it and opened another portal, this one to the Cloud Kingdom and jumped through while Lloyd followed. While there Morro opened another portal, this one to the Wyldness which lead to him and Lloyd landing on the floating island of Chima. There Laval asked them where they came from and in response he opened another portal and pulled Lloyd through. Eventually succeeding in defeating the Green Ninja, Morro left him stranded in another realm before opening a portal to Ninjago and landing on the Preeminent's head before closing the portal. As he was being dragged into the Endless Sea by the Preeminent as she drowned, Morro accepted his fate after a conversation with Wu and handed the latter the Realm Crystal. Using the Realm Crystal, Wu opened a portal that freed Lloyd from the realm Morro trapped him in.

Season 6: Skybound[]

Enkrypted[]

After claiming the Realm Crystal, Nadakhan traveled to a shore and used it to open portals to the realms the Sky Pirates were imprisoned in. Later that night on Misfortune's Keep, the Djinn used the Realm Crystal to create a portal to his home realm of Djinjago and passed through it with his crew. Upon arrival however, they realized the realm was collapsing as a result of the Cursed Realm's destruction. After being asked by his father Khanjikhan to avenge his people and being given the Sword of Souls, Nadakhan opened a portal back to Ninjago with the Realm Crystal and returned to it with the Sky Pirates.

The Last Resort[]

Jay and Nya accidentally created a portal to Tiger Widow Island when they spilled the Traveler's Tea in their possession during a fight with Dogshank. The subsequently knocked her through the portal and used what tea they had left for Jay to escape Lighthouse Island.

Season 8: Sons of Garmadon[]

Dread on Arrival[]

During the Oni resurrection ritual, Harumi used strands of hair from Lloyd, Wu, and Misako and the Oni Masks to open a portal to the Departed Realm with Destruction in an attempt to resurrect Lord Garmadon's evil side. She would eventually succeed though the ninja and Ninjago Police disrupted the ritual, closing the portal and arresting the Sons of Garmadon in the process. The ritual however was completed enough for the evil side of Lord Garmadon's spirit to reform a mortal body, allowing him to eventually force his way out of the Departed Realm.

Big Trouble, Little Ninjago[]

When the Destiny's Bounty was bisected by the Colossus, Jay used the Traveler's Tea given to the ninja by Mystake to open a portal which teleported the rear section of the Bounty along with the ninja and Wu to the Realm of Oni and Dragons.

Season 9: Hunted[]

Lessons for a Master[]

After Wu successfully tamed the Firstbourne, the Arc Dragon summoned a Fire Dragon, an Ice Dragon, Stormbringer, and Slab and had them allow the Stranded ninja to ride them, with the dragons creating portals to the Ethereal Divide in order to reach Ninjago.

Green Destiny[]

Once they gained enough speed, the dragons combined their power to open a large portal in the skies above Ninjago City and join the Resistance in their fight against the Sons of Garmadon.

Season 10: March of the Oni[]

Endings (flashback)[]

After the Dragon Hunters and dragons failed to repel the Oni when they returned to the Realm of Oni and Dragons and all except Faith and the Firstbourne weere petrified by the darkness, the latter grabbed the former in her claw and created a portal to Ninjago which she then flew through before closing it behind her.

The Darkness Comes[]

The Firstbourne emerged from her portal near the Mountains of Impossible Height and subsequently crash landed at the base of the mountains, injured and weakened from her fight with the Oni.

Season 11: Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Under Siege[]

During the siege at the Monastery the ninja activated the defense systems, leading to cannons rising from theor hatches which fired rubber shurikens and water at the Pyro Vipers. In response, Aspheera used the Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu on her staff to open temporal portals to the Never-Realm which sucked the cannons in and 60 years into the past.

Vengeance is Mine![]

While fighting P.I.X.A.L. in the hangar bay Aspheera opened another temporal portal to the Never-Realm via a magical beam which P.I.X.A.L blocked with the Titan Mech's right arm, causing it to be severed before being sucked in.

A short time later after defeating Lloyd, Jay, Cole, and Kai with Forbidden Spinjitzu, Aspheera shot another temporal portal beam from her staff aimed at Wu. Wu however managed to dodge it, causing it to instead hit the body of the disabled Titan Mech and suck it in.

After Zane defeated the Pyro Vipers with Forbidden Spinjitzu augmented Ice, the frozen Aspheera fired another temporal portal beam at Wu. Zane however pushed him out of the way and was hit instead, banishing him to the Never-Realm.

A Cold Goodbye[]

P.I.X.A.L. used Aspheera's staff to open a portal to the Never-Realm which the ninja drove the Land Bounty through to reach the realm, passing through the Ethereal Divide in the process.

The Never-Realm[]

A portal appeared in the sky of the Never-Realm which released the Land Bounty, causing it to crash upside down on the edge of a cliff.

The Kaiju Protocol[]

In an attempt to reach the Never-Realm and rescue the ninja from it, Wu and P.I.X.A.L used Traveler's Tea brewed with Mystake's recipe to open portals to other realms. In the process they opened a portal to the Wyldness amd were swarmed by beavers as a result which P.I.X.A.L handled by snapping a broom in half and throwing it through the portal, lurinf the beavers back in as Wu closed it. Using more of the tea they opened portals to the Underworld, Cloud Kingdom, Realm of Oni and Dragons, and eventually the Departed Realm. Realizing what realm it was and who was there Wu quickly tried to close the portal but was too slow to prevent the Preeminent from escaping.

After P.I.X.A.L defeated the Preeminent in Ninjago City, she dragged her back to the Monastery of Spinjitzu where Wu returned her to the Departed Realm with a portal created from the remaining Traveler's Tea then closed it. Unfortunately in the process he used up all the Traveler's Tea.

Awakenings[]

After the Liberation of the Never-Realm, Sorla created a portal to Ninjago with a berry from the Traveler's Tree which the ninja passed through to return home. Upon returning home, they were welcomed by Wu and P.I.X.A.L as the portal closed.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Rising Ninja[]

During the Ritual of the Blood Moon, Jordana was able to use Theroxian Magic to transform Bonzle back into a spell and create a portal to the Nether-Space. After the ninja freed the captured Writers of Destiny, Ras intended to sacrifice to free the Forbidden Five he resorted to attempting to sacrifice the ninja but was only able to sacrifice Kai which allowed Nokt to exit the portal. Desperate to not let any more of them escape, Bonzle started fighting back against Jordana. Realizing if she closed it she'd trap Kai in the Nether-Space, Bonzle warned him to return before she closed the portal. Kai responded by vowing to seek an escape but pleaded with Bonzle to close the portal even if it would trap him, leading her to continue. Bonzle was eventually able to overpower Jordana and close the portal before anyone else could be sacrificed, but not before Rox released her soul from her body and through the portal to possess Jordana.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Chaos Reigns[]

After trapping Wu in the protective container following the Battle of the Spectral Lands, Ras traveled to the Monastery of Gates and disrupted the portal gates which disabled them throughout the realms. As a result, the ninja were left scattered throughout the merged realms.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • It depends on which method is used to open a portal to a realm, the way to get there is faster or slower, since if one were to use the Traveler's Tea or the Realm Crystal, a portal will open, thus allowing one to pass without difficulty, whereas if a dragon or with a Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu, it takes time to arrive at the desired realm.
  • Dragons have the ability to create portals that allow them to travel between realms. This was demonstrated in "Weapons of Destiny", "Lessons for a Master", "Green and Gold" (Lloyd's dream) and "Endings" (Faith's flashback). Dragons can create portals to travel between realms and from one place to another or to direct their attacks.
