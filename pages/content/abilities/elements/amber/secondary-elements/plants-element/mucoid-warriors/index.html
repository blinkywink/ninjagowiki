Overview

“They hail our forefathers. We have been taught from one generation to the next that one day our vigil over this core would end when heroes claim from the temple, asking permission to save the world. Now our work, and the work of those who came before us, is fulfilled.”
— Zant-Tanz, "They Call It Doom"

The Mucoid Warriors are militant Mucoids resident in the Mother Garden, protecting the local flora and previously the Pink Dragon Core.

History

History[]

The Mucoid Warriors were guarding the Pink Dragon Core when Rapton arrived to claim it. Given he failed their test by attempting to steal the core, the Mucoid Warriors attacked and subdued Rapton. Later, Lloyd and Arin arrived in search of the Pink Dragon Core as well. When Lloyd suggested asking the protectors first for the core, the Mucoids revealed themselves and their general, Zant-Tanz, granted the core to the ninja, explaining that they had passed the secret test. Zant-Tanz then led the ninja to Rapton and explained that Rapton was scheduled to be executed for his attempted theft, prompting Lloyd to ask if it were possible to pardon him. Zant-Tanz admitted that there was one way, if someone could cross the Impossible Path on Rapton's behalf and survive. Lloyd ultimately completed the challenge and Rapton was released by the warriors.
