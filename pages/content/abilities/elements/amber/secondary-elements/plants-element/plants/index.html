Overview

“The greenest of thumbs, curation and growth of vegetation on a grand scale beyond what is normally possible. Not a commander but a caretaker.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Plants is an Elemental Power in the Merged Lands that is derived from the Source Dragon of Life.[1] It is associated with the colors bright green and pink.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Chlorokinesis - The user can manipulate plants.
    • Florakinesis - The user can create, generate, and manipulate flowers, making them grow and bloom.
    • Vitikinesis - The user can generate and manipulate vines, making them grow from the ground.
    • Chlorokinetic Constructs - The user can create objects out of plants (bombs, barriers, whips, projectiles, escape routes, etc.), such as when Zant-Tanz made an arch out of vines and flowers.
    • Acanthokinesis - As seen in “Tendril Terror”, the user can likely manipulate and generate thorns though it may be because the power of Plants went out of control.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • It is strikingly similar to Nature, but they are different elements.[4]
    • When asked what the difference is between them, Chris "Doc" Wyatt stated that "All plants are nature. Not all nature is plants".[5]
    • Glen Lakin compared a Master of Nature to a general in an army, while a Master of Plants as an ultimate gardener.[6]
      • Moreover, he stated a Master of Nature can sense a section of forest in distress from miles away, while a Master of Plants can grow a flower that might have a unique feature such as the bubble bushes in the Mother Garden.[7]
    • In particular, Nature grants the user control over wildlife,[8] which is not the case of Plants since it is restricted to flora.
  • Due to Zant-Tanz being exiled before the beginning of the Tournament of the Sources, this element is the only one not used in the tournament itself, and not to have been possessed by the Elemental Cup and Nokt.
    • Additionally, Plants is one of the seventeen elements that are currently not known to be confined to a human host. The other sixteen are Ice, Time, Fusion, Balance, Propulsion, Figment, Quake, Size, Sound, Surface Tension, Reflex, Brute Force, Fear, Swarm, Misfortune, and Decay.
  • Along with the elements of Chaos, it is the only known Elemental Power not to ever have a human host, since all other known non-human elements have briefly belonged to Lloyd or Sora during the Tournament of the Sources.
  • So far, Plants is the element with the least amount of appearances in the show, appearing in only a single episode.
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt revealed that Plants had already existed before the Merge.[3]
