Overview

“The shifting of stone, a shaking that rattles us to our core - the guts of the world in Motion.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Quake is an Elemental Power in the Merged Lands that is derived from the Source Dragon of Motion.[1] It is associated with the colors orange and yellow.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Vibrokinesis - The user is able to create, sense, manipulate, and generate vibrations and quakes.
    • Vibration Infusion - The user can infuse themselves or an object with seismic energy to increase the strength of their blows.
      • Vibration Empowerment - Users like Frak appear to empower themselves with vibrations to increase the effects of their power.
        • Superhuman Strength - As seen in "The Ultimate Object of Admiration", the user appears to possess a degree of superhuman strength.
    • Earthquake Generation - The user can cause earthquakes by channeling vibrations into the ground.
    • Shockwave Generation - The user has the ability to create giant shockwaves.
    • Quake Immunity - The user is immune to the effects of their own quakes.
    • Bodily/Self-Vibration Manipulation - The user can likely manipulate and cause their own bodily vibrations, as seen with Frak in "The Grand Inquisition" though it may be because he touched a fragment of the Weapon of Desolation.
    • Vibration Detection - As seen with Frak, the user can sense and feel vibrations.
      • Seismic Sense - The user can likely sense vibrations underground, as Frak stated in "Casket of Bones" that he felt the land was screaming.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Dangerous environments - The user can risk destabilizing environments that are naturally dangerous, such as caves and cavernous locations. This was shown when Frak used his power in a cave, which only caused the cave to start collapsing.
  • Limited Range/Airborne Opponents - The user of Quake cannot affect objects that are not touching the same surface as the user. Additionally, Quake cannot be used on opponents that are in the air, such as Dragonian Warriors.
Symbol

Symbol[]

The symbol for Quake is shown to be a small orange crack.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Quake is similar to another Elemental Power, Earth.
    • Quake is most similar to Earth in The LEGO Ninjago Movie with its shockwave and earthquake generation.
  • Quake is the first known Elemental Power to have a Serpentine host.
    • However, Aspheera has wielded Fire and Darkness before and Pythor has wielded Darkness, though neither of them were masters of these Elements.
    • An Elemental Master from the Hypnobrai tribe is known to have taken part in a previous Source Tournament, although their Elemental Power is unknown, as Chris "Doc" Wyatt confirmed that it is not Quake.[3]
  • Quake is one of the seventeen elements that are not currently known to be confined to a human host. The other sixteen are Time, Fusion, Balance, Propulsion, Figment, Plants, Ice, Reflex, Size, Sound, Surface Tension, Brute Force, Fear, Misfortune, Decay, and Swarm.
  • Tommy Andreasen once stated in 2021 that, regarding Wojira's powers of Wave and Storm, "It takes wind (or quake) and water to produce a wave. It takes wind, water, and lightning to make a perfect storm."[4] Years later, a fan asked Chris "Doc" Wyatt on Bluesky if it could be assumed, according to Tommy's statement, that both Quake and Lightning were directly a part of this ability, to which Doc simply responded, "I buy this."[5] It is unknown if this answer is canon, as Quake was not introduced as an element until after "Seabound".
    • Chris "Doc" Wyatt would later contradict this by stating Wojira's abilities are not connected to Quake.[6]
  • The Finnish version of The Epic Dragon Rescue! incorrectly refers to Zeatrix as the Master of Quake instead of Shockwave.
    • Coincidentally, both elements are born from the same source.
