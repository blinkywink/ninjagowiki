Overview

“A funny one, you are. Let us see how hilarious you find my Elemental Power of... Decay!”
— Kur to Roby, "I Alone Can Save Them"

Decay is an Elemental Power in the Merged Realms that is derived from Thunderfang.[1][2] It is associated with the colors red, orange, and rust.

User's abilities

User's abilities[]

  • Taphokinesis - The user can manipulate the process of decay.
    • Decay Inducement - The user can cause other living beings to decay.
      • Decaying Touch - The user can decay individuals that they touch. Kur used this in "The Missing" on two individuals after lifting them off the ground.
      • Disease Inducement - By decaying their victims, the user can make them sick, giving them a pale complexion with sickly greenish markings.
        • Health/Vitality Drain - As seen with Roby, victims of Decay appear to experience a drain of health and vitality.
        • Death Inducement - When victims are affected by the power of Decay, they could die from worsened health in some cases.[4]
      • Blindness Inducement - The user can likely cause blindness by decaying their victims, as Roby stated his eyes were getting worse.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Heat - Heat can be used to heal someone who has been affected by Decay. It is also assumed that heat-based abilities like Fire or Lightning can negate the effects.
  • Time - The reversal attribute of Time can be used to heal someone who has been affected by Decay.[5]
  • Spirit Dragon's energy - Spirit Dragons can cure others of their species that have been affected by Decay. It is unknown if this applies to other dragons or species too.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Decay was first revealed in "The Epic Dragon Rescue!."
  • Kur's decayed appearance is a result of the power's effects.[6]
    • This makes Decay the second elemental power revealed to passively affect the user's appearance following Light, which makes the user's body invisible.
  • This is the only element of the Forbidden Five to have their name mentioned in the series.
  • Decay is one of the seventeen elements that are not currently known to be confined to a human host. The other sixteen are Ice, Time, Fusion, Balance, Propulsion, Figment, Plants, Quake, Reflex, Size, Sound, Surface Tension, Brute Force, Misfortune, Fear, and Swarm.
    • Decay is also one of 9 Elemental Powers to have more than two colors associated with them, the other 8 being Heat, Earth, Fire, Lightning, Time, Mind, Gravity, and Fusion.
