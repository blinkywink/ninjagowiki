Overview

This article is about the element. For other uses, see Balance (disambiguation).
“Walking tightropes, literal and metaphorical. A body in balance can sense imbalance in the world around it.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Balance is an Elemental Power in the Merged Lands that is derived from the Source Dragon of Balance.[1] It is associated with the color lemon.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Constant Equilibrium - The user is able to achieve a perfect state of physical balance.
    • Superhuman Athleticism - The user has a better athleticism at superhuman degrees.
    • Superhuman Maneuverability - The user has superhuman degrees of maneuverability, allowing them to maneuver around with ease during a parkour.
    • Gravity Immunity - The user is immune to the effects of gravity, being able to float around for possibly a short time. This also means the user might be immune to Gravity powers.
    • Superhuman Stacking - The user has the ability to perfectly balance objects and stack them with excellent precision.
    • Physics Defiance - As seen with Kizzy, the power of Balance likely allows the user to ignore the laws of physics.
    • Mental Stability - The power of Balance can also provide mental stability to its user.[4]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Balance is among the seventeen elements that are currently not known to be confined to a human host. The other sixteen are Ice, Time, Fusion, Propulsion, Figment, Plants, Reflex, Quake, Size, Sound, Surface Tension, Brute Force, Fear, Swarm, Misfortune and Decay.
  • Balance is the second element to share its name with its Source, the first being Life.
