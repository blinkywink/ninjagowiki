Overview

This article is about the Source. For other uses, see Balance (disambiguation).
“Every action has a reaction, every question has an answer. Balance makes the parts greater than the whole.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Balance is a form of dragon energy and one of the seven Sources, personified by the Source Dragon of Balance. It is often associated with the color gold.

User's abilities

User's abilities[]

  • Staterakinesis - The user is able to manipulate the concept of balance.
  • Dragon Icon Creation - As it is a form of dragon energy, the user can create a Source Dragon Icon.

Higher-level powers[]

  • Elemental Power Creation/Bestowal - The user can create and grant others Elemental Powers sourced from Balance.
    • Elemento-Potentikinesis Bestowal - When used and combined with other forms of Source Dragon energy, Balance can bestow the Elemental Essence of Golden Power to other beings.
    • Constant Equilibrium Bestowal - Balance can bestow the Elemental Power of Balance to other beings.
    • Umbrakinesis Bestowal - Balance can bestow the Elemental Power of Shadow to other beings.
    • Effingokinesis/Elemental Replication Bestowal - Balance can bestow the Elemental Power of Amber to other beings.
  • Dimensional Rift Creation - As seen with the Dragon Cores, which is made from Balance and the other sources, users can tear rifts in the fabric of reality, exposing the Ethereal Divide.
    • Vacuum Effect - Such rifts are identical to MergeQuakes and have a vacuum effect that sucks nearby objects or people in.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Of the seven Sources, Balance is the only one that is not the origin of any of the ninja's Elemental Powers.
  • When revealing the Elemental Powers coming from the source of energy, Glen Lakin used these poetic descriptions:
    • Balance - Walking tightropes, literal and metaphorical. A body in balance can sense imbalance in the world around it.[4]
    • Shadow - An inseparable twin, a dark reflection. Sometimes hidden but always present, you cannot walk the world without your partner.[5]
    • Amber - When the Masters rose, the world bled Amber and gave an answer should it all go wrong. Fight fire with fire, and so on.[6]
  • When asked what happened to the mortal avatar of the Source Dragon of Balance, Chris "Doc" Wyatt replied, "GREAT QUESTION(S)-- but I sorta don't want to answer for now. Sorry."[7] This somewhat implies there is an Arc Dragon of Balance.
