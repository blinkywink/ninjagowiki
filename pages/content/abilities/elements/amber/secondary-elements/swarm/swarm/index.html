Swarm[4] is an Elemental Power in the Merged Realms that is derived from Thunderfang.[1][2] It is generally associated with the colors red/vermilion and black.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Entomokinesis/Swarm Manipulation - The user can manipulate, command, and communicate with swarms of bugs/insects, such as flies.
    • Entomogenesis - The user can generate swarms of bugs that disturb their victims by chasing or surrounding them.
      • Entomokinetic Attacks - The user can attack with insects.
        • Entomokinetic Beam Emission - The user can shoot beams of insects.
        • Entomokinetic Breath - The user can shoot insects from their mouth.
          • Insect Spit/Communication - The user can spit out a single bug from their mouth to signal or contact others, as seen in "Chaos Unchained".
        • Small Space Maneuvering - When the user is turned into a swarm of bugs or fires bugs, they can move through small spaces as Drix's insects managed to go into P.I.X.A.L.'s car to cause it to crash into a tree.
          • Technology Stopping - The user can use insects to cause technology and technological aspects of any kind to fail, as Drix managed to cause P.I.X.A.L.'s car to malfunction.
    • Entomokinetic Combat - The user can infuse insects into their fighting styles.
    • Swarm Mimicry/Insect Swarming - The user is able to transform themselves into a swarm of bugs/flies, enabling them to disperse, evade attacks, or overwhelm enemies similar to the Elemental Power of Smoke and Vermillion reconstruction.
      • Camouflage - When in their insect swarm form, the user can possibly blend in with the environment, as Drix managed to briefly hide from Lloyd before taking the Blue Prismatic Blade.
      • Swarm Flight - The user can fly through a swarm of bugs.
      • Reforming/Reconstitution - The user can reform part or all of themselves through the swarm of bugs.
        • Reforming Teleportation - The user can teleport to short distances by swarming and reforming.
        • Entomokinetic Regeneration - If the user's body is damaged, they can use bugs to fill in new parts at an enhanced rate. This was seen in "Crashing Together", after Kai blasted a hole through Drix's body with a fireball.
          • Limb Regeneration/Reattachment - If the user's limbs are destroyed or severed, they can use bugs to regrow and reattach them. Drix used this in "Chaos Unchained" after Lloyd destroyed part of his arm with an energy blast but he lost the Blue Prismatic Blade in the process of regenerating it instantly.
      • Intangibility - Similar to Smoke, the user can make attacks phase through them when they become a swarm.
        • Swarm/Intangibility Infusion - The user can infuse objects with their swarm to possibly make them intangible, as seen when Drix also made his sickles disappear with him before he attacked Nya and avoided her Rising Dragon.
      • Wind Resistance - The user of Swarm has a resistance to high winds. One of Drix’s insects was able to fly through a tornado caused by the Winds of Chaos whereas the Ninja got caught in the tornado while trying to fly straight in it.
        • Suction Resistance - The user can resist being sucked in by high winds as seen when Drix and the Forbidden Five flew straight into the Winds of Chaos with no difficulty.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Exploitable Regeneration - Although Swarm allows the user to regenerate and evade attacks, its regenerative ability can be exploited. For example, when Lloyd destroyed Drix’s arm, the insect warrior lost the Blue Prismatic Blade attached to it, showing that vital tools or weapons can be lost through targeted damage despite regeneration.
  • Poison - Poison is also able to neutralize the swarm of insects and could even be fatal for its current user Drix, who is also an insect.[5]
  • Pesticide - Similar to real life, pesticides can be extremely dangerous for the insect swarm and even fatal.[5]
  • Water - With a considerable amount of water, the swarm of insects can be overpowered and drowned.[5]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Swarm is similar to another Elemental Power, Smoke, and Vermillion reconstruction.
  • Swarm is one of the seventeen elements that are currently not known to be confined to a human host, the other sixteen being Ice, Time, Fusion, Balance, Propulsion, Figment, Plants, Quake, Size, Sound, Surface Tension, Reflex, Brute Force, Fear, Misfortune, and Decay.
    • Swarm is also one of seven elements that allow the user to alter their body, the other six being Smoke, Form, Metal, Shadow, Size, and Light.
