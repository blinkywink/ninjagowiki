Overview

“A corruption that turns a body against itself, deadly yet beautiful in its effectiveness.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Poison is an Elemental Power in the Merged Lands that is derived from the Source Dragon of Life.[1] It is associated with the colors lime or teal and corresponds with the Poison Dragon.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Toxikinesis - The user is able to manipulate and generate poison.
    • Toxic/Poison/Venom Immunity - The user, if concentrating, can be immune to any kind of poison/toxin/venom, including Tiger Widow venom, contaminants, or acid.
      • Contaminant Immunity - The user also appears to be immune to contaminants.
    • Toxikinetic Breath - The user is able to breathe a cloud of poisonous gas.
    • Incapacitation Inducement - The user can likely incapacitate opponents with their poison.
    • Poison/Wither Inducement - The user can induce poisoning, as seen in "Enter the City of Temples" when Tox accidentally caused a flower to wilt and wither.
    • Poison Detection - The user can likely analyze and identify any kinds of poison, as seen when Tox identified an Attacker bot's weapon as an acidic/poisonous Gilba Thorn in "They Gather for the Feast".
    • Benzinikinesis - The user can use Poison to manipulate, absorb, generate, and neutralize normal or any kinds of poisonous gas.
      • Poison Gas Generation - The user can generate toxic gases.
        • Knockout Gas Generation - The user can possibly release a poisonous gas that renders people unconscious.
      • Poison/Gas Blast - As seen with Tox, the user can release a blast of poisonous gas.
  • Dragon Creation - The user can create a Poison Dragon.

The symbol of Poison sported by Tox is an upside down biohazard sign with four daggers protruding from it diagonally to resemble a skull and crossbones symbol.

The symbol of Poison shown in the Tournament of the Sources represents a green skull and crossbones symbol.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Hageman Brothers once stated that Tox could have the same effect on a djinn that Tiger Widow venom does.[3]
    • However, in 2026, Tommy Andreasen refused to confirm or deny whether this was the case.[4]
  • Poison seems to alter its possessor's personality, as seen when Tox was nicer to the other competitors of the Tournament of Elements when she was deprived of her element.
    • During Season 9: Hunted, her poisonous attitude appears to have returned, although during the Tournament of the Sources, this trait seems to have faded away or just been forgotten. However, this could be explained with her small amount of screen time.
      • Alternatively, it's possible that Tox can subvert the poisonous attitude acquired by her element, allowing her to act more positively towards others.
    • This could also just be because at the time of Thunderblade, to the time that everyone knew the true meaning of Chen's tournament, Tox held on to the same belief like Chamille that "only one can remain" until she knew the true meaning and sided with Lloyd.
  • When asked how Drix can be killed, Chris "Doc" Wyatt confirmed that Poison could be fatal for him.[5]
