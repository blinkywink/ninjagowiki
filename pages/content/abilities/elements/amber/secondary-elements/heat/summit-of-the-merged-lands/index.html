Overview

“For countless generations, we all lived seperately, most of us never even knowing each other existed, but a supernatural force brought us together. No one knows the reason for it, but here, starting today, we can make the Merge mean something tremendous...”
— Percival Tartigrade to the attendees, "The Summit"

The Summit of the Merged Lands is a conference hosted by Percival Tartigrade and attended by various delegates of the Merged Lands to come to a peace agreement. This initiated the formation of the Council of the Merged Lands.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Aside from the Land of Eternal Dawn, the Land of Monsters was the only realm to have no known representative present during the Summit.
    • Unlike the former, the latter has no representative due to a lack of a governing body, since the Land of Monsters is filled with mindless and aggressive creatures.
    • Next to Suetonius sat a Crossroader and Imperian citizen in the remaining two chairs. It is unknown if they are representatives for the other remaining realms or other regions, or if the Imperian woman in particular was a stand-in for Percival.
      • Additionally, in "The Summit," sixteen flags are shown attached to the courthouse, likely representing the sixteen realms and its representatives. Since only fourteen known realm representatives were present at the Summit, it leaves into question of what realm or specific region is represented by the remaining two flags.
  • With Lloyd not attending the Summit, the other two representatives of the realm of Ninjago interestingly do not originate from the realm. Wyldfyre comes from an unknown location, growing up in the Wyldness and Alfonzo Frohicky is from the Wyldness.
