Overview

“An intensity of temperature, a shift in molecules that can change their very state.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Heat is an Elemental Power in the Merged Realms that is derived from the Source Dragon of Energy.[1] It is generally associated with the colors orange, fire engine red, and blue.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Calokinesis - The user can create, shape, and manipulate heat.
    • Pyrokinesis - The user can use heat to manipulate and generate fire.
      • Azure-Pyrokinesis - The user can generate and manipulate blue fire, which is hotter than orange/yellow flames.
      • Pyrokinetic Wall Creation - The user can raise walls made of flame.
      • Pyrokinetic Ball Projection - The user can attack with fireballs when hitting a single target.
        • Fire Cutting - The user can use fire to cut through objects, such as when Sora (when she had Heat) used a fireball to cut down a tightrope during her fight against Chamille in "The Sword Shatters".
      • Pyrokinetic Blast - The user can release fire over a specific target area, causing great damage and destruction.
      • Pyrokinetic Punch - As seen with Wyldfyre when she tried to attack Lloyd, the user can throw flame-powered punches.
      • Pyrokinetic Propulsion - The user can propel themselves up or forward using flames.[3]
    • Calokinetic Attacks - The user can attack with heat.
      • Calokinetic Blasts - The user can release heat energy over a specific target area, causing great damage and/or delivering great shockwaves of pure force.
      • Calokinetic Wave Emission - The user can create waves of heat over a specific area as a shield or an attack.
        • Shockwave Generation - The user can release heat as shockwaves, as seen while Wyldfyre was fighting Lloyd in "Mindless Beasts".
          • Shockwave Stomp - As shown by Wyldfyre in "Mindless Beasts", the user can also release shockwaves by stomping the ground.
      • Calokinetic Beam Emission - The user can release beams of heat.
      • Calokinetic Bomb Generation - The user can create and launch bombs of heat to release it over a specific target as seen in "The Battle of the Second Monastery".
      • Calokinetic Vortex Creation - The user can create spirals and/or vortexes of heat over a specific area as seen in "Mindless Beasts" when Wyldfyre spun around with her flames to create a spiral against Lloyd.
    • Fire/Heat Resistance - The user is possibly resistant to fire and heat.
    • Burning/Melting - The user can produce enough heat to burn/melt objects.
    • Calokinetic Constructs - The user can create objects out of heat as seen when Wyldfyre created a golf club with her flames while playing Boulder Golf in "The Shape of Motion."
    • Calokinetic Healing - The power of Heat allows the user to heal injuries such as body decay.
      • Decay antidote - The user can heal someone who has been a victim of Decay, thus cancelling its effect.[4]
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Fire Absorption - Certain creatures like the Lava-Tides can absorb the fire created by Heat.
Symbol

Symbol[]

The symbol for Heat is a wisp of flame that is red on the outside and yellow in the inside.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Heat is similar to another Elemental Power, Fire, leading to frequent confusions with the two despite being distinct elements. The ninja's misidentification of Wyldfyre as a Fire Elemental, solely based on her ability to project flames, marks the second instance of their confusion regarding the identity of a Fire Elemental Master, other than Kai. In Tournament of Elements, Skylor was also initially mistaken for a Fire Elemental by the ninja until Garmadon revealed her element to be Amber.
  • In "The Power Within," it was observed that Heat is not as strong as Fire. This was demonstrated when Wyldfyre shot Heat at some cans, causing it to melt. This indicates that Heat had a minimum temperature of 660 degrees, which is the melting point of a can. On the other hand, when Kai threw a fireball with Fire at his cans, they completely turned to ash. This suggests that his fireball had a minimum temperature of 2000 degrees, which is the temperature required for a can to turn into an ash-like consistency.
    • However, in real life, blue fire is hotter than orange fire.
  • Heat is one of 9 Elemental Powers to have more than two colors associated with them, the other 8 being Gravity, Decay, Earth, Lightning, Time, Fire, Mind, and Fusion.
  • It is mistakenly referred to as Flame in LEGO Ninjago Dragons Rising Adventure Atlas.
  • Heat existing before the Merge was initially hinted by Rontu in "The Spell at the Waterfall," as she knew the differences between the element and Fire; before it was confirmed by Chris "Doc" Wyatt.[2]
