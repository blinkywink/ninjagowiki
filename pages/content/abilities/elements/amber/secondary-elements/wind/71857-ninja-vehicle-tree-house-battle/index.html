Overview

71857 Ninja Vehicle Tree House Battle is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 4 set that was released on January 1, 2026. It includes Kai and Zane in their Draconus suits, Draconus Form Jay, a Loyalist, an Acid Monster, Euphrasia, Tree house hideout, Zane's helicopter, and the Ninja motorcycle.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Boys and girls ages 7 plus have all they need to play out battle action with this Ninja Vehicle Tree House Battle (71857) building set, based on season 4 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show. This fighting toy playset comes with a 3-story tree house, a helicopter toy, a motorcycle toy and 6 minifigures. The tree house toy includes a workshop, a tearoom, a terrace, a swinging vine rope, a trap that releases a small rock, a rotating bridge and a stud shooter. Kids can enjoy unlimited role-play options with a motorcycle toy that has moving wheels, a saddle-cockpit and 2 stud shooters, or take to the sky with a helicopter toy that has an opening cockpit and rotating blades. The NINJAGO set features 6 minifigures: Kai, Zane, dragon form Jay and Euphrasia to play out battles against the Acid Monster and Loyalist. Kids can build the playset with confidence using the LEGO® Builder app – zoom, rotate in 3D and track progress with easy-to-follow digital instructions. Set contains 642 pieces.

  • FIGHTING TOYS FOR KIDS – The Ninja Vehicle Tree House Battle (71857) building toy lets boys and girls ages 7 and up recreate battle scenes from season 4 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show
  • NINJA TREE HOUSE – 3-story tree house features a workshop, tearoom, terrace, trap that releases a rock, rotating bridge, stud shooter and swinging vine rope
  • 2 NINJA TOY VEHICLES – Ninja motorcycle toy has moving wheels, a saddle-cockpit for a minifigure and 2 stud shooters, and the helicopter toy has an opening cockpit and rotating blades
  • 6 NINJAGO® MINIFIGURES – This LEGO® NINJAGO battle toy comes with ninja Kai, Zane, dragon form Jay and Euphrasia for good vs. evil battles against the villainous Acid Monster and Loyalist
  • GIFT IDEA FOR KIDS –NINJAGO® playset comes packed with a 3-story tree house, helicopter toy and motorcycle toy and makes a ninja gift for kids with a passion for fantasy and imagination toys
  • A FUN WAY TO BUILD – Let the LEGO® Builder app guide kids on an intuitive building adventure, where they can save sets, track progress and zoom in and rotate models in 3D while they build
  • A UNIVERSE OF LEGO® NINJAGO® SETS – This extensive collection of kids’ toys lets ninja fans and children escape into an immersive fantasy world for imaginative play with their ninja heroes
  • DIMENSIONS – This 642-piece NINJAGO® toy features a tree house standing over 9 in. (22 cm) high, 10 in. (26 cm) wide and 6 in. (15 cm) deep[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Draconus suit Kai and Euphrasia are exclusive to this set.
    • The completed version of Draconus Form Jay is also exclusive to this set in regions where the Ninjago Magazine isn't available, such as the US.
  • This is the cheapest set to include Draconus suit Zane.
