Overview

“Whether twisting a leaf or migrating a storm, Wind whips about the air, unpredictable and unending.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Wind is an Elemental Power in the Merged Lands that is derived from the Source Dragon of Flow.[1] It is associated with white or jade/green and corresponds with the storm spirit Wojira and her amulets.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Aerokinesis/Galekinesis - The user is able to manipulate, control, and generate air/wind.
    • Lilapsokinesis - The user can manipulate and generate tornadoes, allowing them to create a large vortex of air around themselves to attack, confuse, or defend against an adversary. It could also possibly be used for travel.
      • Tornado Spinning - The user can even spin like a tornado for both offensive and defensive purposes.
    • Aero-Telekinesis - The user can move, manipulate, and otherwise interact with matter using air/wind. In "Winds of Change," Morro used this to move a windmill's propellers and throw it onto the powerless ninja.
    • Aerokinetic Flight - The user can fly/surf and glide using wind. A difference of flight with wind is that the user can levitate in a similar style as Gravity (but not as steadily). Both Morro and Euphrasia demonstrated this ability but for Euphrasia, she needs to manipulate air currents to keep herself and others afloat and unlike Morro, she can fly without having to focus on using one of her hands as a makeshift thruster.
      • Air Aura - The user can sometimes surround themselves in air when flying.
      • Aerokinetic Speed - The user also has superhuman levels of wind-like swiftness whenever they fly. The Wind Dragon was stated to be as fast as the wind.
      • Aerokinetic Foot Blasts - As seen with Euphrasia, the user appears to sometimes release wind from their feet when they fly.
      • Air Walking - Users like Euphrasia sometimes appear to freely stand and move on the air when flying.
    • Atmokinesis - As shown by Morro in “Winds of Change”, the user can change the weather to produce strong air currents.
      • Weatherly Presence - As seen with Morro, the user can likely change the weather through only their presence.
    • Aerokinetic Attacks - The user can use Wind to attack or defend against an adversary.
      • Aerokinetic Breath - The user can expel a strong amount of wind from their mouth.
        • Enhanced Breath - The user can possibly enhance their wind breath for a greater range of attack.
      • Aerokinetic Blast - The user can release air/wind over a specific target area, causing great damage and/or delivering great shock waves of pure force.
        • Aerokinetic Beam Emission - The user can release beams of wind to push opponents away, attract objects, or redirect them.
        • Air Wave Emission - The user can release waves, spirals, rings, pulses, blasts, balls, and bursts of wind to damage, push, bind and/or paralyze people. They are able to send wind through any form of matter creating ruptures, and create a pulse that can go through anything, and depending on what the user wants, it can either be harmful or harmless.
      • Air Bolt Projection - Users like Morro appear to be able to fire bolts of wind.
    • Galegenesis - The user can generate winds or flow of gasses on a large scale of varying intensities.
      • Air Augmentation - The user has the ability to increase the amounts of air or powers related to air.
    • Spinjitzu Disruption - As seen with Morro, the user can counteract Spinjitzu by blasting a wind directly at them or using the air currents to knock them off balance.
    • Wind Strike - As seen with Morro, the user can seemingly use wind to power a strike.
      • Aerokinetic Punch - As seen with Morro, the user can seemingly throw wind-powered punches.
    • Air Suction - As seen with Morro, the user can attract objects and people by pulling the wind towards themselves.
    • Power Deactivation/Disruption - As seen in "Winds of Change", the user can disable and disrupt other people's powers through wind.
    • Nephelokinesis - Wind Dragons can manipulate and move clouds.[2]

Higher-level powers[]

  • Fury of the Storm - When used alongside Water, the user of Wind can use both elements to create Wave and Storm. When at full power, the user can create a giant storm that affects a realm and have power over the Endless Sea. As seen in "The Calm Before the Storm," Wojira created a gust of wind from her storms, allowing her to change the weather to produce strong currents and push large amounts of water from the ocean to help flood all of Ninjago.
    • Storm - Consisting of the Elemental Powers of Wind and Water, the user of Storm can manipulate and summon storms, including thunder, lightning, and strong winds that would cause the Endless Sea to rise above its normal level, as Wojira was able to control actual storms while producing heavy rain and stronger winds by using the full power of the Storm Amulet. The user can also control Water from storms, allowing them to create rainstorms.
      • Fulgurkinesis - The Keepers manage to use the Storm Amulet to manipulate and contact lightning, mostly to summon lightning bolts from the sky and project them from their own bodies, redirect natural lightning in an electrical storm, electrocute opponents, and cause electrical explosions.
    • Wave - When combined with Water, the user can inundate areas and sweep away anything with immense force by moving a large volume of water.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Before the encounter with Morro, this element was briefly used by Jay. Despite his element being Lightning, Jay was able to use Wind in "The Green Ninja" to form a vortex.
    • According to Tommy Andreasen, Jay using the power of Wind was merely an early mistake that should be forgotten.[6][7]
  • Wind, alongside Smoke, are the only Elemental Powers to have two Elemental Masters as minifigures: Morro and Euphrasia (Ray and Maya have already lost their powers during the period represented by their minifigures, and the Time Twins are a specific case).
  • Alongside Water and Time, Wind is one of the three elements from the original series that weren't necessary for Chen's Spell of Transformation.[8]
    • Coincidentally, all three Elements originate from the same Source, Flow.
  • Though Tommy Andreasen[9] and the 2019 Visual Dictionary confirmed Morro's dragon to be a corrupted version of Lloyd's Energy Dragon, LEGO.com stated it was the ghost of Morro's Elemental Dragon of Wind.[10]
  • Wind is the first element in the show to be passed down to a successor in present day.
  • Green was the common color shared between most of the known Wind users until Seabound. Morro's Airjitzu was green and the Wind Dragons have green markings on their body.
    • As of Season 1 of Dragons Rising, white became the main color to represent the element.
  • In Seabound, it was established that the First Spinjitzu Master never mastered Wind and Water, despite the Firstbourne having Wind due to predating Wind Dragons, and the First Spinjitzu Master having other elements used by dragons. Tommy Andreasen stated that the situation would be something he would change if he could.[11]
    • Tommy Andreasen, stated that in his mind, dragons before adolescence may temporarily use the Wind element before they get one of the four elements of Creation excluding the other secondary elements.[12] This rule most likely applies to the First Spinjitzu Master due to being part-dragon since at the time he fought Wojira, he had Wind.
    • In 2025, Chris "Doc" Wyatt stated that Water Dragons exist as well, and that the First Spinjitzu Master only had Creation and Destruction before receiving the other elements from the Source Dragons,[13] meaning that he only inherited the elements of Creation from the dragons.
  • Masters of Wind can become one with their element in a similar manner to Masters of Water.[14]
