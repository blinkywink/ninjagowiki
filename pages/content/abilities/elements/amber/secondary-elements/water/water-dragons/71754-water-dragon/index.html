Overview

For other uses, see Water Dragon (disambiguation).

71754 Water Dragon is a Ninjago - Seabound set that was released in most countries on June 1, 2021 and on August 1, 2021 in the United States and Canada. It comes with NRG Nya, Kalmaar, two Maaray Guards, Scuba Zane, a pedestal with the Wave Amulet, Nya's water dragon form and Zane's mini submarine.

LEGO.com description

Incredible stories from the NINJAGO®: Seabound TV series can be played out by kids with this highly detailed Water Dragon (71754) playset. Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

"A Nya dragon! I was very happy to be given this task of making a powerful Water dragon that would depict the qualities of Nya as a character. The wings are unique, inspired by different fish and mixing that with a watery look. Might be the longest dragon ever done in LEGO form yet!"[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This set is the cheapest way to get NRG Nya, Scuba Zane and Kalmaar.
  • Nya in her dragon form was first seen in "The Turn of the Tide."
    • Zane's mini submarine was first seen in "The Calm Before the Storm."
  • This is the third set to include a character, Nya in this case, in their dragon form and to also include their minifigure form.
    • The first set was 71713 Empire Dragon with Unagami and the second was 71742 Overlord Dragon with the Overlord.
  • The Water Dragon reuses the head mold from 71746 Jungle Dragon. The jaw mold is, however, a completely new piece.
  • Many years before she was revealed, a Nexo Power named "Sea Dragon" was created for another LEGO series, Nexo Knights.
