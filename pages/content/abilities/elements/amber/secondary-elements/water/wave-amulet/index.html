The Wave Amulet was an ancient amulet that was given to the Merlopians by the First Spinjitzu Master to protect it from Wojira. Along with the Storm Amulet, it was one of the two amulets that were used by Wojira to rule the Endless Sea.[2] However, during the Great Flood, Nya destroyed the Wave Amulet to defeat Wojira.

History

History[]

Prior to Seabound[]

Before the creation of Ninjago, Wojira used the Storm and Wave Amulets to control the Endless Sea. Although, Nyad was able to defeat her by prying the two amulets from her head.[3] After the battle, the First Spinjitzu Master gave the Wave Amulet to the Merlopians to protect it.[4]

Sometime before Wojira's return, Kalmaar managed to obtain the Wave Amulet and return it to Wojira so he can awaken her.[5]

A Big Splash through Five Thousand Fathoms Down[]

After being tasked to reawaken Wojira, Glutinous spent most of his time at the Temple of Wojira to create a replica of the Storm Amulet while trying to study more on the Wave Amulet.[6]

Great Flood[]

After awaking Wojira with both of her amulets, Kalmaar used his trident to take control of her by striking the Wave Amulet with it, forcing Wojira to obey his commands and create the Great Flood. However, after Kalmaar lost control and was eaten by Wojira, Nya in her new form destroyed the Wave Amulet to defeat the sea serpent.

Appearance

Appearance[]

The Wave Amulet is a round light blue crystal with a glowing core. On the front, Wojira's head is modeled in gold. On the sides of the Amulet and at the back, there are gold protrusions facing inwards. According to Glutinous, no one knows what the Amulet is made of.[6]

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Thalassokinesis- The Wave Amulet can control and manipulate the water from the Endless Sea, allowing the user to have control of it and its aspects. Tampering with its power would cause the Elemental Master of Water’s powers to go out of control.
  • Control over Wojira - Because of the power of the Wave Amulet, the user can temporarily control Wojira's actions.[7] However, it has to be constantly stabbed by a weapon's energy and cause pain to Wojira in order to do so. Otherwise, the user would no longer have control of Wojira.
