Overview

Not to be confused with Lightning or Wind.
“We harnessed the power of lightning, and became one with the storm.”
— Mammatus to the ninja, "The Keepers of the Amulet"

Storm (or simply lightning[4][5]) is a power in Ninjago that consists of the Elemental Powers of Wind and Water. It is connected to Lightning[6] and is generally associated with the colors pink and purple. It manifests as purple electricity which is generated from violent thunderstorms and was mainly used by Wojira through the Storm Amulet and later the Keepers and the Stone Guardians after harnessing its lightning.

User's abilities

User's abilities[]

  • Fury of the Storm - With Wave, the user of Storm can create a destructive storm and deluge that can affect an entire realm.
    • Tempestakinesis - The user can create, summon, and manipulate storms, including thunder, lightning, and strong winds that rise the Endless Sea above its normal level, as Wojira was able to control actual storms and while producing heavy rain and strong winds by using the full power of the Storm Amulet.
      • Fulgurkinesis - The user can create, generate, increase, absorb, conduct, shape, and manipulate lightning of various intensities. Users of this ability can redirect natural lightning in an electrical storm, electrocute opponents, and cause electrical explosions. Also, the user can use it against an adversary.
        • Lightning Bolt Projection - The user is able to summon lightning bolts from the sky or project them from their own bodies.
        • Thunderstorm Creation - The user can create storms accompanied by lightning and thunder.
      • Aerokinesis - Due to Storm comprising of the Elemental Powers of Water and Wind, the user can manipulate and generate strong winds, as seen in "The Calm Before the Storm," when Wojira creates a gust of wind from her storms and even change the weather to produce strong currents and push large amounts of water from the ocean and help flood all of Ninjago.
      • Hyetokinesis - The user can manipulate and generate rain from storms, allowing them to create rainstorms. They can also control the intensity, and the water in them, even in a drought.
History

History[]

Prior to The Island[]

Before the First Spinjitzu Master came to Ninjago, Wojira used the Storm Amulet to take control of the Endless Sea with violent storms.[7]

However, after Nyad was able to pry the Storm Amulet from Wojira, the First Spinjitzu Master would later task the Keepers and the Stone Guardians to protect it, who would later receive the power of lightning from the storm.[5]

Several years later, Twitchy Tim went on a hot air balloon tour until his balloon was blown off-course to the Island of the Keepers. During his time there, he was struck by the lightning from the storm belt twelve times while he was trying to escape.[4]

The Island[]

Uncharted[]

Misako's expedition was attacked by the Stone Guardians when the storm belt awakens them by using Storm. Later, as the ninja and Twitchy Tim were closing in to the Island of the Keepers, the storm belt was used to knock them off.

Keepers of the Amulet[]

While battling the ninja, the Stone Guardians summon the storm belt to empower them with Storm, using it to attack them.

The following information is considered non-canon.

After the ninja escaped from the Stone Guardians, they ran into Zippy's uncle, who attacked them. When the ninja used their Elemental Powers on the dragon, Zippy's uncle used Storm to create an energy field so he could protect himself from the ninja's elements.[2]

Non-canon information ends here.


Later, the ninja got zapped and captured by the Keepers, who explained how they got the power of Storm while telling the ninja about their oath to protect the Storm Amulet.

The Gift of Jay[]

While the ninja were looking for Jay, the Keepers used their spears to zap Zippy with Storm, but missed. Later, when the ninja caught Clutch Powers attempting to steal the Storm Amulet, the Keepers zapped them with their spears.

The Tooth of Wojira[]

After Ronin attempted to escape by using his boat, Twitchy Tim uses the power of Storm to stop him. Later, Clutch attempted to steal the Storm Amulet again, but Nya took a Keeper spear to zap him with it.

Seabound[]

The Storm Amulet[]

The storm belt used its lightning to throw off the ninja.

The Calm Before the Storm[]

After Kalmaar woke up Wojira using her amulets, he took her to Ninjago City so he can use the Fury of the Storm to destroy Ninjago. While traveling, Wojira used Storm to capsize the Mary Louise and later struck a lid up sign.

Assault on Ninjago City[]

During the Great Flood, Storm was used by Wojira to create heavy rain while using lightning to attack the Destiny's Bounty and later Lloyd.

Nyad[]

As the Fury of the Storm continued to destroy Ninjago City, Kalmaar ordered Wojira to use Storm during his battle with Nya.

The Turn of the Tide[]

While battling Nya, Wojira uses Storm to electrocute Nya until she destroyed her. However, after Nya destroyed the Wave Amulet, Wojira was struck by Storm and vanishes.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The way the Keepers merged with the storm is similar to how Nyad and Nya merged with the sea.
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, Storm isn't an element along with Wave but rather complex abilities used by Wojira. In Storm's case, "it takes wind, water and lightning to make a perfect storm."[8]
    • This implies a potential connection between Lightning and Storm[6] that fits with the appearance of Storm and Fury of the Storm even if only Wind and Water are mentioned in the show.
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, the power of Storm is "whatever you experience when caught in a storm."[9]
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, Thunderfang possesses the ability to create storms; however, this differs from Wojira's power of Storm, as it encompasses wind and rain, whereas Thunderfang's storm is of primal chaos.[10]
