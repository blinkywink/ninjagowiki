Overview

“For thousands of seasons, our people have been entrusted with the protection of the amulet. Entrusted by the First Spinjitzu Master himself to keep it from Wojira, for if the serpent ever obtained it again, the seas would rise up and swallow all the world.”

The Storm Amulet of Wojira was an ancient amulet that was given to the Keepers by the First Spinjitzu Master to protect it from Wojira. Along with the Wave Amulet, it was one of the two amulets that were used by Wojira to rule the seas of Ninjago.[3] However, after Nya destroyed the Wave Amulet, the Storm Amulet vanishes along with Wojira, whose fate remains unclear.[2]

History

History[]

Prior to The Island[]

Before the creation of Ninjago, the Storm Amulet originally belonged to Wojira, who used it to affect the Endless Sea with violent storms and control it. Eventually, Nyad pried the Storm Amulet from Wojira's head and the First Spinjitzu Master gave it to the Keepers, so they can protect it from Wojira.[4]

The Island[]

The Keepers of the Amulet[]

Mammatus showed the Storm Amulet to the ninja while it was on its stand, and explained its history to the ninja. Thinking that the ninja were thieves who seek to steal it, Mammatus declared that the ninja would be forever be the Keepers' prisoners.

The Gift of Jay[]

While the ninja were looking for Jay, Clutch Powers attempted to steal the Storm Amulet. However, he was stopped when the Keepers electrocuted him and the others.

The Tooth of Wojira[]

While the Keepers were making amends with the ninja, Clutch Powers attempted to steal the Storm Amulet again, but was stopped when Nya electrocuted him. Unbeknownst to her, the archaeologist was able to get away with the real one while the Keepers were protecting a replica of it.

Seabound[]

Riddle of the Sphinx[]

The Storm Amulet is in Clutch Powers' possession as he falsely explains how he obtained it to his fellow Explorer's Club members. It was in his hands until Kalmaar broke into the club office, where he gave it to Wu. The master and king then fought over the amulet.

Papergirl[]

Kai, Cole, Ray, Wu, and Misako fight against Kalmaar and the amulet reaches Antonia and Nelson, who try to escape with it but Kalmaar ends up retrieving the artifact.

Master of the Sea[]

The ninja continued to fight Kalmaar for the Storm Amulet until they were defeated by him, letting him take possession of the amulet once more. Kalmaar then realized that the other group managed to return to Ninjago, and decided to make off with the Storm Amulet. During his escape, the Amulet was taken from him by Nya and later given to Vania so the Army of Shintaro can protect it. Unbeknownst to them, they were guarding the fake one from the Island of the Keepers while the real one was with Kalmaar, who swapped the real Storm Amulet with the fake one during his escape.

The Calm Before the Storm[]

After getting away with the Storm Amulet, Kalmaar reunites the two amulets and awakens Wojira with them.

The Turn of the Tide[]

During her battle against Nya, Wojira used the Storm Amulet to electrocute her until she destroyed her Water Dragon form. However, after Nya recovered and destroyed the Wave Amulet, Wojira disappears along with the Storm Amulet, leaving its fate unknown.[2]

Other appearances and mentions

Other appearances and mentions[]

The Wrath of Kalmaar[]

While explaining that Wojira was real to Maya, Nya mentions the Storm Amulet until she was interrupted by Kalmaar, who asked for the location to the Island of the Keepers after Nya mentions it. Nya then refuses to answer to prevent Kalmaar from knowing more information about the Storm Amulet.

The Storm Amulet[]

After planning to stop Kalmaar from getting the Storm Amulet, the ninja, P.I.X.A.L., Maya, and Benthomaar went to the Island of the Keepers so they can convince the Keepers to give them the Storm Amulet. However, once Nya was given the amulet, she and the others soon realized that the amulet was a fake and warned Cole where the actual amulet was.

Appearance

Appearance[]

The Storm Amulet is a round purple crystal with a pink glowing core. On the front, an image of Wojira's head is modeled in gold. On the sides of the Amulet and at the back, there are gold protrusions facing inwards. According to Glutinous, no one knows what the Amulet is made of.[5]

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Tempestakinesis - The Storm Amulet can create and manipulate storms, including heavy rain, thunder, lightning, and strong winds.
Official description

Official description[]

Prince (sorry, King!) Kalmaar needs both the Wave and Storm Amulet to awaken Wojira and attack NINJAGO City. With only one in his possession, he has looked far and wide for the second. To no avail. That is until Merlopian scientist, Glutinous, may have found its location! But will it be there?

Let’s hope for NINJAGO City’s sake that the answer is no…[6]
