Overview

The driftwood bo staff[2] is a specialized bo staff used by Bolobo to amplify his Elemental Power of Nature.

History

History[]

The Invitation[]

Bolobo is seen using the driftwood bo staff on board the ferry in preparation for the tournament.

Only One Can Remain[]

When the tournament takes off, Bolobo goes to Jay's room in an attempt to secure a Jade blade, and a fight breaks out. Bolobo uses his staff during the fight in attempts to reach the Jade Blade and pin Jay down.

Versus[]

During his fight with Neuro, Bolobo uses his driftwood bo staff multiple times to restrict Neuro, as well as in attempts to elevate himself to the Jade blade. However, Neuro manages to break free from the staffs grip and use the plants grown by it to reach the Jade blade, causing Bolobo to be eliminated. It is unknown where the staff was taken after this.

The Forgotten Element[]

Bolobo can be seen with the staff in hand some time after regaining his powers.

The Day of the Dragon[]

When ordered to sink the ferry, Bolobo uses the driftwood bo staff to summon vines, dragging it into the sea.

The Greatest Fear of All[]

When the Elemental Masters are positioned by the road to cut the noodle trucks off, Bolobo was holding his bo staff.

Decoded[]

While the staff itself never appears in the present, it can be seen in some flashbacks.

Appearance

Appearance[]

It is similar in appearance to a regular bo staff, but made of driftwood and has a spiral at the top and vines growing out of the top of it.

Abilities

Abilities[]

The driftwood bo staff amplifies the user's power of Nature. Not much is known of its individual abilities.

Trivia[]

  • In LEGO form, the staff is similar to its counterpart from The LEGO Ninjago Movie Videogame. The part representing the top of the staff was previously seen in gold as part of Wu's staff in 70738 Final Flight of Destiny's Bounty.
  • The piece used in the staff's set form used to connect the stick with the plant on top was originally created for LEGO Monkie Kid.
