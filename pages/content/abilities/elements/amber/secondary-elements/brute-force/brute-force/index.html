Overview

“That won't be necessary thanks to my Elemental Power of Brute Force, ninja!”
— Nokt, "Open Sky Surgery"

Brute Force[4][5] is an Elemental Power in the Merged Lands that is derived from Thunderfang.[2][3] It is associated with the colors light red and magenta.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Raw Power - The user is able to charge themselves with an immense level of power, allowing them to overcome physical obstacles and limitations.
    • Aura Generation - The power of Brute Force allows the user to emanate an aura, surrounding their arms or body parts with immense power.
    • Enhanced Strength - The power of Brute Force grants the user enhanced levels of physical strength.[6]
      • Attack Augmentation - The user can enhance their attacks, ranging from their strikes to elemental powers.
        • Enhanced Strike - The user can use enhanced levels of strikes, defined in different forms of combat.
    • Raw Power Attacks - The user can fire Brute Force in the form of offensive power-based attacks.
      • Energy Punch - The user can infuse their fists with energy and throw energy-powered punches as seen when Nokt destroyed the entrance to Thunderfang's prison in "The Spectral Lands" and later when he caused Sora's jet to crash in "Chaos Unchained".
      • Ball Projection - The user can project Brute Force as balls.
        • Remote Explosions - As seen in "The Hollow Ones", these balls can cause distant objects hit by them to explode.
      • Bomb Generation - As seen in "Chaos Unchained", the user can launch Brute Force as bombs.
      • Wave Emission - As seen in "When Doves Cry", the user can project Brute Force as a massive wave.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Prismatic Ore - If the user is a ghost, Prismatic Ore can counter attacks from Brute Force.[7]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, Nokt used Brute Force during the Tournament of the Sources.[8]
  • It is the third element with a multi-word name, following Golden Power and Surface Tension.
    • Like Surface Tension, Brute Force is also the second normal element with that attribute, as Golden Power is an elemental essence.
  • Brute Force is one of the seventeen elements that are currently not known to be confined to a human host. The other sixteen are Ice, Time, Fusion, Balance, Propulsion, Figment, Plants, Quake, Size, Sound, Surface Tension, Reflex, Fear, Swarm, Misfortune, and Decay.
