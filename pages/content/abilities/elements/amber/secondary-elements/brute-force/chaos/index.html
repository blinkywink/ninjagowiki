Overview

“The Shatter Dragon, whose powers were fueled by the souls of his consumed enemies, blasted weaponized storms of chaotic power.”
— Rox, "The Missing"

Chaos (also referred to as chaotic power or dark powers) is a corrupted form of dragon energy created and personified by Thunderfang, enhanced by any soul he consumed, initially drawing from those of the Oni[2] until he died from the healing elixir. Thunderfang desired to become a Source Dragon with Chaos as his Source.[3] It is opposed to Focus and associated with the colors red/crimson or blue.

User's abilities

User's abilities[]

  • Chaokinesis - The user can manipulate the very essence of chaos.
    • Chaos Beam Emission - The user can fire beams of chaos.
    • Chaos Energy Manipulation - The user can manipulate and create chaotic energy.
      • Lightning Bolt Projection - The user can unleash powerful bolts of lightning.
      • Corruption Inducement - Chaos has the power to corrupt those who are exposed to it for extended periods. This is evident in the case of the Forbidden Five, who fell into corruption due to their contact with it. The nature of the corrupting energy is particularly evident in factions such as the Wolf Clan and Dragonian Trackers, which succumbed to its effects after harnessing or being exposed to it.
      • Construct Creation - Chaos can be used to create constructs that take various forms.
        • Object Creation - Chaos can be used to create objects.
          • Weapon Creation - Weapons are among such objects that can be created.
            • Gauntlet Creation - Those empowered by Wolf Masks can create clawed gauntlets.
        • Chaos Infusion - A user can infuse objects with chaotic energy such as the Wolf Masks and the Gong and Mallet of Shattering.
      • Energy claws - When controlled by Rox, wearers of Wolf Masks could release energy claws from their backs.
    • Power countering - As they are natural opposites, Chaos can counter Focus.

Higher-level powers[]

  • Winds of Chaos - After consuming many Oni souls, Thunderfang gained the ability to conjure the Winds of Chaos.
    • Tempestagenesis - The Winds of Chaos take the form of a tumultuous storm.
      • Galegenesis - True to their name, the Winds of Chaos result in strong winds.
        • Lilapsogenesis - Such winds can take the form of tornadoes.
  • Potentikinesis - Chaos can manipulate superpowers in various ways.
    • Power Creation - Chaos can create various supernatural abilities related to it.
      • Elemental Power Creation - The user can create Elemental Powers sourced from Chaos.
      • Magic Creation - Chaos can create forms of magic such as Theroxian Magic.
    • Power Bestowal - Chaos can grant others various superpowers related to it.
      • Elemental Power Bestowal - The user can grant others Elemental Powers sourced from Chaos.
        • Raw Power Bestowal - Chaos can bestow the Elemental Power of Brute Force to other beings.
        • Phobikinesis/Timorokinesis Bestowal - Chaos can bestow the Elemental Power of Fear to other beings.
        • Infortukinesis Bestowal - Chaos can bestow the Elemental Power of Misfortune to other beings.
        • Entomokinesis Bestowal - Chaos can bestow the Elemental Power of Swarm to other beings.
        • Taphokinesis Bestowal - Chaos can bestow the Elemental Power of Decay to other beings.
      • Magic Bestowal - Chaos can grant others the ability to use Theroxian Magic.
      • Martial Arts Bestowal - The Gong of Shattering, Mallet of Shattering, and Wolf Masks can grant others the ability to use Shatterspin, even at full power in Elemental Masters.
      • Weatherly Presence Bestowal - The user can grant others the power to affect the weather merely through their presence.
        • Tempestakinesis Bestowal - Via such power, others are granted the ability to manipulate storms of chaotic energy.
          • Fulgurkinesis Bestowal - Such storms have lightning.
      • Flight/Levitation Bestowal - The user can bestow the power of flight to others as seen when the Sobrient Stone was able to grant the Forbidden Five this after Nokt shattered it.
    • Power Augmentation - Chaos can augment the powers of others.
      • Martial Arts Creation/Augmentation - Using Chaos, the Forbidden Five were able to create an advanced form of corrupted Spinjitzu called Shatterspin.

Via Shatterspin[]

  • Chaokinetic Vortex Creation - Elemental Masters who use Shatterspin can create a vortex of Chaos.
    • Strength Enhancement - Shatterspin increases the user’s strength to superhuman levels.
      • Combat Infusion - The user can infuse Shatterspin into their fighting skills, making their strikes more powerful.
      • Seismogenesis - The user can produce shockwaves by slamming the ground.
        • Shockwave Stomp - The user can also create shockwaves by stomping the ground.
    • Chaokinetic Wave Emission - The user can shoot waves of Chaos.
    • Chaokinetic Foot Blasts - The user can shoot chaos from their feet with kicks.
    • Elemental Deflection - Shatterspin can deflect elemental attacks.
    • Magic Negation - Shatterspin can negate some forms of magic such as the Runes of Binding that bound the Spectral Crown to the Lycan King's head.
      • Wish Negation - Using Shatterspin, Rox was able to overcome Sora's wish that her feet were stuck to the ground.
  • Levitation - When activating Shatterspin, users can levitate in the air.

Via Theroxian Magic[]

  • Spell Manipulation - Theroxian Magic can manipulate spells.
    • Spell Casting - Theroxian Magic can be used to cast spells, such as non-verbal spells or incantations.
      • Tempestagenesis - Theroxian Magic can summon a powerful storm that knocked the Bounty off course.
      • Mysto-Telekinesis - Theroxian Magic can magically lift someone or something in the air and make them float unassisted.
      • Paralysis Inducement/Body Immobilization - One such spell is a static spell that paralyzes those hit by it.
      • Container Imprisonment - Theroxian Magic can be used to imprison others in containers.
      • Rune Creation - Theroxian Magic can be used to create runes, as the Wolf Clan did to create the Runes of Binding.
        • Binding - Some runes can bind objects to each other, such as the Spectral Crown to the Lycan King's head.
        • Reforming/Reconstitution - Some runes can reform upon destruction as seen with the Runes of Binding, though this may only be since they were not destroyed at the same time.
    • Spell Negation - Theroxian Magic can negate spells both from it and other forms of magic.
  • Corruption Inducement - Theroxian Magic can corrupt living beings.
    • Mind Control - Theroxian Magic can allow users to control the minds of those corrupted by it.
  • Soul Projection - Users of Theroxian Magic can separate their souls from their bodies.
    • Possession - Once the user’s soul has been separated from their body, they can possess others.
      • Psychic link - While possessing others, the user can create a physical link between their host and soulless body.
  • Dowsing - Theroxian Magic can be used to locate things.
  • Curse Inducement - Theroxian Magic can induce curses.
    • Lycanthopic Infection - One such curse is a Lycanthrope transformation.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • It is the only "Source" the First Spinjitzu Master did not use as a Conduit, due to Thunderfang not being a true Source Dragon.
  • As Thunderfang is not a true Source Dragon, the Forbidden Five, whose powers are derived from him, would most likely not be allowed to participate in the Tournament of the Sources, with Nokt being an exception due to Ras and Bleckt's deal.[4]
  • Due to the differences in power level, Chaos energy can appear in various colors. The most common is red/crimson, though it sometimes turns blue when in its rudimentary form, and yellow, as seen in the second part of the third season when Thunderfang utilized the soul energy of hundreds of people across the Realms.
  • Any objects and abilities imbued with Chaos energy can still be used even after Thunderfang's defeat.[5]
  • When asked if Thunderfang drew his power from Destruction while consuming the Oni's souls, Chris "Doc" Wyatt clarified that Thunderfang was only fueled by the energy of the souls themselves.[2]
