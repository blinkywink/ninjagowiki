Overview

Misfortune[4] is an Elemental Power in the Merged Lands that is derived from Thunderfang.[1][2] It is associated with the colors poppy red and crimson.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Infortukinesis - The user can manipulate bad luck and jinx, making it so that bad things happen to other people.
    • Bad Luck/Misfortune Inducement - The user can cause other people to have accidents, misfortune, or other type of bad luck.
    • Infortukinetic Ball Projection - The user can project balls of Misfortune, which can directly cause pain to victims.[5]
      • Technology Stopping - These balls can cause technology to fail, such as when Zarkt managed to disable a truck.
      • Energy Negation/Dispersal - Said balls can negate energy attacks, such as Lloyd's life energy balls, causing them to separate and scatter.
      • Explosion Inducement - These balls can also cause explosions upon anything they come in contact with.
    • Misfortune Infusion - The user can infuse themselves with Misfortune to strengthen their blows.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Life - Life can counter and nullify Misfortune if attacks from both elements hit each other.[6]
  • Prismatic Ore - If the user is a ghost, Prismatic Ore can block attacks from Misfortune.[7]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Misfortune is one of the seventeen elements that are not currently known to be confined to a human host. The other sixteen are Ice, Time, Fusion, Balance, Propulsion, Figment, Plants, Quake, Reflex, Size, Sound, Surface Tension, Brute Force, Decay, Fear, and Swarm.
  • Misfortune is the first Chaos element that is shown to be directly used.
  • For unknown reasons, when Zarkt hits Nya several times with Misfortune in "Chaos Reigns," the attacks appear to do nothing more than harm her without any sort of bad luck or aftereffects.
