Overview

This article is about the Elemental Power. For other forms of shapeshifting, see Shapeshifting.
“A body in flux, even your idea of self refuses to be static. Be anyone from moment to moment.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Form is an Elemental Power in the Merged Realms that is derived from the Source Dragon of Flow.[1] It is associated with a variety of colors, mainly purple/violet and yellow, and corresponds with the Form Dragon.

Users' abilities

Users' abilities[]

  • Shapeshifting/Mimicry - The user can shapeshift to change their appearance and the clothes they are currently wearing and replicate the form of the person they change into.
    • Voice Imitation - While shapeshifting, the user is able to mimic the voice of the person they turn into.
  • Dragon Creation - The user can create a Form Dragon.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Physical Contact - The user has to physically touch someone in order to replicate their appearance.[3]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Tommy Andreasen originally stated that the then-unknown Elemental Master Leo seen riding a dragon in "Spellbound" is most likely the past Master of Form taking on an appearance.[4] Leo having Form was later confirmed to be the case in Strike of the Serpentine.
  • Several beings have powers similar to Form, tough none are connected to the Element:
    • The Oni have similar powers of transformation, such as shapeshifting, yet their abilities are not related to the Element of Form.[5]
    • As demonstrated by Nadakhan, Djinns are able to not only impersonate other people and only partially alter their appearance, but also copy their powers.
    • The Formlings have shapeshifting abilities, but they only transform into their spiritual animal form.
    • Zane can also change his appearance, by using holographic technology, as of Sons of Garmadon.
  • Form is one of seven elements that allow the user to alter their body. The other six are Smoke, Size, Light, Metal, Shadow, and Swarm.
  • Tommy Andreasen's character description for Chamille, though not necessarily canon, states that "All she needs is to have touched that person, and she will be able [to] control all cells of her body to impersonate all traits."[3]
    • He also states that Chamille inherited her powers from her father.
    • It is unknown if users of Form can turn into/mimic other species, sapient or not.
  • Form and Shadow are the only known elements present in the most recent Tournament of the Sources to never be used on screen.
  • Glen Lakin originally stated that Form and Size are from the same Source Dragon,[6] but then later corrected that they are from two different Source Dragons.[7]
