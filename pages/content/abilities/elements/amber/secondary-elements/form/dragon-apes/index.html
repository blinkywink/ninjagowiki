Overview

Dragon-Apes,[1][2] also known as Dragon Beasts, are creatures with the characteristics of dragons and gorillas.[3][4] They were formerly used as mounts in the gladiator pit in the Land of Madness.

History

History[]

Background[]

When a cursed thorn pierced the hand of the First Elemental Master of Form, she turned into a Dragon-Ape and went on a rampage for generations. Thankfully, the First Spinjitzu Master destroyed the thorn, releasing her from the curse.[5]

Later, the Pit-Master captured some of the Dragon-Apes for his gladiator pit, thinking that they are vicious creatures.[2]

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

After Rogue was brought to the gladiator pit, it was revealed that his next match would be mounted, which forced him to get on a Dragon-Ape. Then Zilvar, who was revealed to be the Rogue's opponent, also appeared on a Dragon-Ape, who was named Grimtak. After the match started, Zilvar quickly overpowered the Rogue and his Dragon-Ape with Grimtak thanks to his prior to his experience. Even after the Rogue revealed that he is Jay, Zilvar continued to attack him with Grimtak. However, after Jay convinced him to throw the match so he can save all of the gladiators, Zilvar and Grimtak stopped, allowing Jay to throw Zilvar out of his mount. During the rebellion against the gladiator pit, the Pit-Master arrived with a Dragon-Ape and attacked Jay for revenge, almost crushing him. Thankfully, Zilvar arrived with Grimtak and stopped him, quickly overpowering the Pit-Master. Following their escape, Grimtak joined the ninja's journey to the Never Kingdoms with his owner.[6] After a long journey with the ninja, they reached the border of the Never Kingdoms and crossed it together. Then, they arrived on the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms, where they decided to rest for the night. The next morning, Zilvar feed Grimtak with fish he bought from a Fisher.[5] Later that night, when Ras was about to attack Zilvar for not agreeing with his plan, Zilvar whistled for Grimtak, who arrived and knocked Ras. Then he and Zilvar went to find the ninja.[7]

Upon finding their friends in the Boreal River, Grimtak knocked the corrupted Beniro, who was rushing towards Zane as he was tried to get the corrupted Ice in the river under control to recover the Weapon of Desolation. After Lloyd wanted him to get the weapon, Zilvar rode Grimtak towards the weapon, while dodging the corrupted Ice on the way. After taking the Weapon of Desolation, Zilvar rode Grimtak outside the Boreal River. Then, together with Zilvar, Grimtak headed towards the Abyss of Eternal Night with the ninja.[5] During their journey, the Land Bounty got stuck in the snow, forcing Zilvar and Grimtak to see what's going on, discovering that it was the scorpions. As they fought against the scorpions, one of them knocked both Zilvar and Grimtak, separating them. Then, the scorpions captured Grimtak and tried to take him with them. Scared for his friend's life, Zilvar got manipulated by the Weapon of Desolation, who told him to use the power that can save Grimtak. Because of this, Zilvar used the weapon, summoning Elemental Monsters, who fend off the scorpions, rescuing Grimtak. Zilvar comforted his friend, saying that he is okay. Then, Grimtak put Zilvar on top of him and continued their journey to the abyss.[8] Eventually, Grimtak, Zilvar and the ninja arrived in the Abyss of Eternal Night,[7] waiting Zilvar to throw the weapon into the abyss. Unfortunately, Zilvar got possessed by the Weapon of Desolation, summoning Elemental Monsters, which the ninja fought against as Grimtak watched. During the battle, Zilvar whistled for Grimtak, who charged forward and knocked the ninja because of his loyality. Then, Zilvar mounted Grimtak and attacked the ninja with him, forcing the ninja to transform into their Draconus Form. Then Grimtak got locked in combat with Lloyd, who managed to dislodge Zilvar from Grimtak's back. As two Ice Monsters approached, Lloyd knocked Grimtak against them, causing Grimtak to get overwhelmed and retreat, which pleased Lloyd.[9]

Following the Battle at the Abyss of Eternal Night, Grimtak returned to the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms with Zilvar and the ninja. After Zilvar said his bye to Nya and Jay, he started riding Grimtak and left the monastery with him.[10]
