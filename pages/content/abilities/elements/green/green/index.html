Overview

"You just have to look at it from a different point of view."
This article is about a subject that is canonical to the storyline of Warner Bros' The LEGO Movie franchise.
“Green is the color of life, it flows through all living things, connecting them all.”
— Lloyd, The LEGO Ninjago Movie

Green is an Elemental Power in Ninjago. It is associated with the color green.

User's Abilities

User's Abilities[]

  • Nature Connection - The user is connected with nature and all living beings.
    • Elemental Power Detection - The user can sense the presence of dormant elemental powers within others, as shown by Lloyd sensing the elements inside his friends.
    • Empathy - The user has the ability to strongly empathize with living beings, shown when Lloyd managed to calm down Meowthra.
    • Communication - The user has the ability to communicate well with living beings, like how Lloyd did when he was talking to his father about their father-son relationship.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • As it is Lloyd's element, it may be the movie's equivalent of Life, Lloyd's element in the TV show. The two are similar in their ability to connect and unite others, along with having properties similar to that of Nature.
  • Green is the only element that Master Wu cannot use with his own.
  • Green is one of two elemental powers to have the name of a color, the other being Amber.
    • Brown Power also has a color in its name, though it was a fake elemental power.
  • The element is also used differently in The LEGO Ninjago Movie Videogame, where Lloyd's hands form a green light and he uses the power to build things quicker. This may be a reference to the fact that he is a Master Builder in The LEGO Movie.
