Overview

71854 Cole's Mission Mech is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 4 set that was released on January 1, 2026. It includes Draconus Cole, Draconus Form Zane, an Acid Monster and Cole's Elemental Golem.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Boys and girls ages 7 and up will love building this LEGO® NINJAGO® Cole’s Mission Mech & Dragon Zane (71854) ninja toy and recreating scenes from season 4 of the NINJAGO Dragons Rising TV show. This playset features a mech toy with posable head, arms, shoulder armor, fingers, legs and ankles, plus a cockpit to place a minifigure inside. The mech comes with 3 weapons: a sword, a staff and a spring-loaded blaster with 2 darts, which kids can swap with weapons from the Riyu Ninja Dragon (71855) set (sold separately). This NINJAGO toy also comes with 3 minifigures: Cole with a hammer accessory, Zane Dragon Form with a sword and an Acid Monster with a staff for role-play battles. The LEGO NINJAGO range of building sets for kids includes mechs, dragons and temples for fans to enjoy ninja action. Kids can build each playset with confidence using the LEGO Builder app – zoom, rotate in 3D and track progress with easy-to-follow digital instructions. Set contains 364 pieces.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Despite it being treated like a mech in the set, Cole's golem form is not a mech in the show.
  • This is the cheapest way to get an Acid Monster.
  • The set is called "Cole's Mission Mech" on the box but "Cole's Mission Mech & Dragon Zane" on the LEGO website and in the descriptions of the sets.
