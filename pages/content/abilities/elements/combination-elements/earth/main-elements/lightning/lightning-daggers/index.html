The Lightning Daggers are a pair of weapons used by Jay since he became a bounty hunter.

History

History[]

Their Draconic Majesty's Request[]

Jay uses his daggers when Ras spun the Super Storm Jet around. The daggers help Jay to hold on. When Arin got out and used his Object Spinjitzu on the Returning Blade, Jay used his daggers to deflect the blade away before attacking Ras.

Crashing Together[]

Jay uses his daggers to disable Arin's stolen glider, which Arin uses to cut the cable Jay captured Ras in. Jay manage to cause Arin to lose his and reclaim both daggers before Sora intervenes.

Casket of Bones[]

Jay pulled out his daggers in attempt to kill Lee, thinking he can still live even with a few less of his bodies, but Nya stopped him.

Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon[]

Jay pulled out his daggers when he and the Ninja heard sounds in the City of Temples, which turned out to be Roby, who was turned into a Hollow Ones.

The Vault of Focus[]

Jay deactivated the daggers after seeing Roby. He put the daggers away when he questioned Wyldfyre's love for Roby.
