Overview

Not to be confused with the actual vehicle.

71768 Jay's Golden Dragon Motorbike is a Ninjago - Crystalized set that was released in most countries on June 1, 2022 and on August 1, 2022 in the United States and Canada. It includes Golden Dragon Jay, a Vengestone Warrior, a crystal spider, the Nunchucks of Lightning, and the Golden Dragon Motorbike.

Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

"Everyone has to design a Ninjago buke at some point! Made at the same time as 71775, this model almost designed itself. With the big pieces in place (figure with the wings, dragon head front, big wheels and shell decoration pieces on the side), it was my task to fill out the place in between with the budget that was left.

I managed to find some space in the budget to include the crystal spider as they appeared surprisingly often in the scripts that started coming in while I was finishing the designs for the Crystalized sets."[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Jay minifigure reuses the wings from 71760 Jay's Thunder Dragon EVO, and his headpiece from his Spinjitzu Training variant.
  • This, 71771 The Crystal King Temple, and 71774 Lloyd's Golden Ultra Dragon are the only Crystalized sets to not contain at least one of the Crystal Council members.
  • This is the only Crystalized set not to contain an exclusive minifigure.
