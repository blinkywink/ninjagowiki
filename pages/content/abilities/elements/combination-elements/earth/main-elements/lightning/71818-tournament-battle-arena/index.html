Overview

This article is about the 2024 set. For other uses, see Battle arena (disambiguation).

71818 Tournament Battle Arena is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 2 set that was released in most countries on June 1, 2024 and August 1, 2024 in the US. It includes Kai, Jay, Cinder, and Sora in their Tournament of Sources suits, a Wolf Mask Warrior, Tox, and the Games Plaza.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

This detailed Tournament Battle Arena (71818) set lets boys and girls aged 7+ recreate the dramatic final showdown from season 2 of the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV show. The ninja battle playset includes lots of interactive features to provide kids with hours of playtime. Afterward, it makes an eye-catching display model on a shelf or bedside table.

This LEGO® NINJAGO set includes 2 moving platforms so kids can play out exciting battles with a ninja and a villain, a large drum they can push off its stand to roll into the arena, and a staircase that collapses with the pull of a lever, causing any characters on it to slide to the bottom.

This buildable ninja toy for kids comes with 6 NINJAGO minifigures. Ninja Kai and Sora, in their tournament armor and with never-before-seen short katana sword accessories, battle a collection of villains: Cinder, a Wolf Mask Warrior and, exclusive to this set, Evil Jay and Tox.

  • NINJAGO® battle playset for kids – Boys and girls aged 7+ can recreate amazing action scenes from season 2 of the NINJAGO Dragons Rising TV show with the Tournament Battle Arena set
  • Interactive role-play toy – The arena action toy includes 2 moving platforms on which kids can place a ninja and a villain to play out action-packed battles
  • Rolling drum and collapsing stairs – Kids can pull a lever to collapse the arena’s stairs and cause a minifigure to slide down, or push the drum from its stand to roll into the arena
  • 6 NINJAGO® minifigures – This ninja toy for kids includes ninja Kai and Sora, in their tournament armor, who battle Cinder, a Wolf Mask Warrior and, exclusive to this set, Evil Jay and Tox
  • A helping hand – Discover intuitive instructions in the LEGO® Builder app, where builders can zoom in and rotate models in 3D, track their progress and save sets as they develop new skills
  • Bigger ninja adventures – Look out for more LEGO® NINJAGO® toys (sets sold separately) featuring dragons, mechs and vehicles
  • Measurements – This 659-piece Tournament Battle Arena adventure toy measures over 6.5 in. (17 cm) high, 12 in. (30 cm) wide and 7.5 in. (20 cm) deep[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This marks the third appearance of Tox, following her initial release in two Ultra Agents sets around ten years earlier. Furthermore, it marks her first appearance in a Ninjago set.
    • Tox's appearance is starkly different from the show/her other minifigure, trading her tousled hair and torn shirt for a bushy mohawk and jacket. In comparison, Mr. Pale's return in 71814 Tournament Temple City bears more semblance to his original minifigure.
  • Tournament of Sources Cinder and Tox are exclusive to this set.
    • Tournament of Sources Jay is also exclusive to this set in regions where the Ninjago Magazine isn't available, such as the US.
    • Cinder's only exclusive part is his torso.
  • This set can be combined with 71814 Tournament Temple City.
  • On the left side of the box Sora's right arm has a different coloring.
