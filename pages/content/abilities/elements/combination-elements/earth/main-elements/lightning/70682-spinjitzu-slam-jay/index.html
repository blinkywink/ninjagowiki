Overview

70682 Spinjitzu Slam - Jay is a LEGO Ninjago - Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu set that released in most countries on June 1, 2019, and on August 1st, 2019 in the United States and Canada. It includes Spinjitzu Slam Jay, a Lightning Forbidden Spinjitzu Spinner, a Spinner launcher, and a missile launcher.
