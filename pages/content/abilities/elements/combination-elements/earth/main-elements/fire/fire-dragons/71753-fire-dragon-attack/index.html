Overview

This article is about the 2021 Legacy set. For the actual dragon, see Flame. For the Legacy 4+ set, see 71701 Kai's Fire Dragon.

71753 Fire Dragon Attack is a Ninjago: Legacy Wave 5 set that was released in most countries on June 1, 2021 and on August 1, 2021 in the United States and Canada.[1] It includes Kai and Zane in their Legacy (dragon) outfits, Legacy Wyplash, Golden Legacy Nya, the Sword of Fire, the Shurikens of Ice and Flame.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Golden Legacy Nya is exclusive to this set.
    • However, only her torso is exclusive.
  • This is the second Legacy remake of Flame the Fire Dragon. The first was 4+ set 71701 Kai's Fire Dragon.
    • The Storm Glider and Blade Cycle also got 2 Legacy sets, with one of each being 4+ set.
  • This version of Flame appears identical to the dragon keeper from "Golden Legend".
  • Despite being based on the first season, Zane features his titanium head, not his movie head, which appears more human-like.
  • Flame uses recolored version of the dragon head from 71701 Kai's Fire Dragon. This style of head is similar to the Golden Dragon's from 70666 The Golden Dragon.
