The Climber Mech is a mech formed by Sora for Kai to use. It was formed using old ship parts and Kai's Dragon Hook.

History

History[]

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

In an effort to allow Riyu and Wyldfyre to traverse the Phantasm Cave's large drop, Sora formed the Climber Mech out of old ship parts and Kai's Dragon Hook. The mech's hooks were used to climb up the cavern, as well as fight off the tentacles that came out of the cave.

After getting out of the Phantasm Cave, the mech was stored in the Destiny's Bounty for later use.

Secrets of the Wyldness[]

After crashing from Jordana's magic storm, Kai piloted the Climber Mech in a last-ditch effort to save himself, Sora, and Wyldfyre from certain doom.

The Forest of Spirits[]

Using the Climber Mech, Kai was able get out of the cliff with Sora and Wyldyre, only to get ambushed by some Wolf Mask Warriors. However, Kai was able to beat them with the Climber Mech alongside Sora and Wyldfyre's help. Then, Kai got out of the mech, leaving it near the cliff.
