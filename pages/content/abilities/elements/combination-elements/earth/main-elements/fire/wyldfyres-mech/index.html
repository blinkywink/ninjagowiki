Overview

The mech is a vehicle that is paired with the Mech Storm Rider.

History

History[]

Their Draconic Majesty's Request[]

Wyldfyre drove the mech when she went looking for Janus Melons to create Janus Melons stew.

Chaos Unchained[]

After using her Heat to cure Roby's Decay, Wyldfyre jumped into the mech to pilot the Storm Rider to joined the other Ninja to save the world.

The Shatter Dragon[]

Wyldfyre was in the mech when she drove the Storm Rider to rescue the other Ninja. When Thunderfang knocked her down, she allowed Kai to pilot the mech. During the battle when Thunderfang chomp on one of the Storm Rider's engines, Kai complain of scratching it's paint and this is a loaner, before using the mech to channel his Fire to force Thunderfang to let go, but got knocked down.

Later Kai was using the mech to help Sora, Zane, and P.I.X.A.L. when Arin threw the healing elixir at Thunderfang

Official description

Official description[]

Join forces with Kai and Nya as they hurtle through a ferocious storm. In the skies above the NINJAGO® world, Nya is piloting her flying motorcycle while Kai is at the controls of his mech as together they form an awesome combined ninja vehicle! They also need to use their armory of katana swords and shooters to take down a dangerous Dragonian Warrior beneath them. Ninja teamwork must triumph!
