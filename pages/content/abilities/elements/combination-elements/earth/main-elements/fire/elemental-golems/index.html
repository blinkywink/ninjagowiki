Overview

“Now might be a good time to rock monster up, Cole! Put that new power to use!”
— Nya to Cole, "Land of Lost Things"

Elemental Golems[2] are elemental creations that can be created by Elemental Masters of Earth, allowing them to encase themselves in stone. The Elemental Masters of Ice also can make Elemental Golems, but only through the Scrolls of Forbidden Spinjitzu.[1]

History

History[]

Shadow of Ronin (non-canon)[]

In the course of fighting the ninja with the remaining power of the Elemental Forges, Ronin conjured an elemental Golem from the remaining energy of the Elemental Forges and encased himself within it, utilizing the elements of Fire, Lightning, and Ice to imbue the Golem with these abilities as the Earth Forge was used to create it. The ninja were able to destroy each Forge that powered the elemental Golem as the battle continued until they had destroyed them all.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Secret of the Wolf[]

As Lloyd and Red continued their journey in the Never-Realm, an Ice Behemoth was formed, attacking them. During the battle, it injured Red. Thankfully, Lloyd led the Ice Behemoth to a frozen lake where he cut the ice open with the Titan Mech, causing the golem fall through, destroying it in the process.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

They Call It Doom[]

Cole, who ended up in the Land of Lost Things, gained the ability to transform into a golem, and used it to attack Nya, Sora, and Riyu, believing they were harming the children that live there, until he stood down when he recognized Nya's Spinjitzu attack.

Land of Lost Things[]

Cole disassembled his golem form to speak with Nya, Sora, and Riyu. He later reassembled it when he came in contact with Geo's Fusion power, which he thought had helped him use his golem form, but learned that it doesn't affect living things.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Force From the East[]

Upon returning from his quest, Cole used his Elemental Golem to assist the Finders in their battle against the Wolf Mask Warriors and to rescue Bonzle from them. After successfully defeating several of the Wolf Mask warriors, Cole utilized his golem to construct a protective shelter for his friends. Once the shelter was established, he dismantled the golem. Subsequently, Cole reactivated his golem form to repair the destroyed portal gate.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

Dying to Live[]

After Cole grabbed the fragment of the Weapon of Desolation, his Elemental Golem was unexpectedly enhanced by the fragment's power. As the battle continued, Cole used his enhanced Golem against the Loyalists while still holding onto the fragment. However, Beatrix soon arrived, overpowering Cole and seizing the fragment from him, which disassembled his Elemental Golem.

The Heartbeat of all the Realms[]

After touching the Weapon of Desolation, Cole's Elemental Golem was enhanced again, allowing him to knock Beatrix. However, he was noticed by Elemental Monsters, which Beatrix commanded to attack. As Cole bravely fought against the monsters with his Elemental Golem, more of the ninja arrived at Imperium and joined the battle too. Unfortunately, Loyalists in the inside of City of Imperium was able open a hole in the city's walls, allowing Beatrix, Elemental Monsters and Loyalists to enter the city. Cole tried to stop them, only for three Mutation Monsters to grab him and pin him to the ground.

Birthright[]

Cole stayed pinned to the ground with his Elemental Golem. However, after Lloyd blasted a Life wave, Cole was able make a staff and beat the creatures. Then he saved Riyu and gave him a sword. As he and the other ninja continued to fight against the Elemental Monsters, they were able to get into the city, allowing Cole to close the gate from preventing more Elemental Monsters get into the city. Then he noticed Lloyd in his Draconus Form as Beatrix powered of some monsters with the help of the Weapon of Desolation. Cole continued to fight against the monsters, only for an empowered Earth Monster to beat him. Thankfully, he was able to get up. After Arin got the Weapon of Desolation, Cole returned back to normal to tell him the incantations to suck the monsters back into the weapon, disassembling his Elemental Golem in the process.

Monster in the Monastery[]

After one of Dorama's puppets pinned him and Frak to a wall with its arm, Cole reassembled his enhanced Elemental Golem and held the puppet's arm, allowing Frak to climb onto a puppet's head and destroy it by using his powers.
