The Fire Temple is an ancient temple constructed into an active volcano in the Forest of Tranquility guarded by Flame. The temple hid both the Sword of Fire and the Fangpyre Fangblade, which was buried within the volcano itself.

History

History[]

After Lord Garmadon was banished to the Underworld for his attempted theft of the four Golden Weapons, Master Wu decided to hide the Sword of Fire at the Fire Temple, appointing the Flame as its guardian.

King of Shadows[]

Kai was lured into the Fire Temple by Lord Garmadon's shadow, where he discovered the Sword of Fire and his kidnapped sister, Nya. Kai used the Sword to free her, but was soon defeated by the shadow clones Lord Garmadon created. Master Wu then intervened, using his own shadow to fight Lord Garmadon's, and recovered the Sword.

In response, Lord Garmadon awakened Flame and sealed the entrance, intending to let the dragon deal with his foes and have the Skulkin retrieve the Sword of Fire. Master Wu, however, used the Sword to cut loose a rock platform that he rode into the Underworld, preventing his brother from uniting the four Golden Weapons. Unfortunately, this act left Kai and Nya alone to deal with the enraged Fire Dragon.

Weapons of Destiny[]

After failing to prevent Samukai's forces from escaping with the other three weapons, Zane, Cole, and Jay arrived at the Fire Temple. Before they could enter, the temple split open, revealing Kai and Nya on the back of the Fire Dragon - Kai had befriended the beast after explaining that they were trying to protect the Golden Weapons too. Having realized that the Fire Dragon could travel to the Underworld, Kai told the others to hop on. Cole claimed that they would not fit on the Fire Dragon's back, so the ninja left the temple in search of the other three dragons.

Return to the Fire Temple[]

While flying Kai and Nya, Flame took them back to the Fire Temple, to show them a secret entrance to the Underworld. Kai fought the Skulkin guards and was assisted by Flame, who shot a blast of fire to block the entrance to the Underworld.

The Green Ninja[]

After the Falcon spotted the Serpentine heading to the Fire Temple, the ninja pursued them to the volcano behind the temple.

When they arrived, the Serpentine were already working to uncover the Fangpyre Fangblade. Rattla unearthed the object, with Pythor then grabbing it and announcing the event to his followers. While the Anacondrai General looked at the Fangblade with joy, he noticed the ninja in its reflection. Upon the order of their king, the Serpentine sprung into action at once.

The ninja fended them off relatively easily, with Pythor attempting to retreat with the Fangblade in his possession. He ordered Bytar and Chokun to drill holes in the Temple's walls to make the rising lava quicken. Jay urged his teammates to leave as the volcano had become more unstable, but Lord Garmadon was too determined to leave empty-handed. He raced up the stairs and fought off a small group of upcoming Serpentine, though when Pythor realized the trouble was coming from Lloyd, they dropped off his cage to the landing below. Lord Garmadon became more desperate since Lloyd's cage began melting in the lava, causing him to fight his way to his son's location and pull him up before the lava could touch him.

Right when Pythor was about to leave, Kai rushed in and threw his Sword of Fire at him, which resulted in the Sword pinning the Fangblade to the wall. Pythor attempted to pull it out to no avail, being forced to retreat without it.

All but Kai met up at the Fire Temple's exit, while Kai was too occupied retrieving the Fangblade. As Lloyd called out to the Red Ninja, the floor gave out underneath him, causing Lloyd to fall toward the blistering lava below. He ended up floating atop a single piece of land, completely trapped and defenseless. Kai was oblivious to Lloyd's unfortunate fall, concentrating on releasing the Fangblade - though once it was loose, the Fangblade fell to a patch of land just above the lava. The Fire Ninja immediately dived in after it, though he eventually realized that his comrade was calling for his help. Quickly, Kai decided to leave the Fangblade and jumped in to save Lloyd, a circle of spewing lava surrounding the both of them as they huddled together.

Unbeknownst to Kai and Lloyd, a future version of Wu had just been transported into the temple nearby along with the Time Twins, Krux and Acronix. Krux began to run toward Kai and Lloyd in an attempt to alter the timeline, but Wu stopped him and held back the twins, inadvertently zapping them with temporal energy and transporting them and himself away again.[1]

The rest of the team escaped to the Destiny's Bounty, everyone equally worried about the two in the temple. However, not long after, a fireball appears from the top of the volcano, floating to the Bounty and revealing itself to be Kai (who had reached his True Potential) and Lloyd inside.

Wrong Place, Wrong Time[]

Lord Garmadon used his Mega Weapon to return to the past and prevent Kai from joining the ninja, thereby preventing Lloyd from becoming the Green Ninja. The ninja followed him and eventually managed to fix up everything he had tampered with. At the Fire Temple, instead of Lord Garmadon summoning copies of Kai's shadow to fight him, he decided to face Kai himself. The Future Ninja intervened, but Lord Garmadon gained the upper hand. Jay stole the Nunchucks of Lightning, the Scythe of Quakes, the Shurikens of Ice, and the Sword of Fire from their past selves, which the ninja used to destroy the Mega Weapon. In doing so, they erased all the changes they and Garmadon had made to the past, with one exception: all but Lloyd remember the Mega Weapon's former existence.

Shadow of Ronin (non-canon)[]

The ninja returned to the Fire Temple after their memories were erased by Ronin to seek the Obsidian Sword to restore Kai's memory. Ronin's samurai arrived at the same time, also after the object in question, though the ninja were able to fend them off and obtain the Sword. As they had unintentionally caused a cave-in near the entrance, the ninja fled into the volcano only to be attacked by Ronin in a large mech. After a frenzied battle with the mercenary, Jay was able to destroy the mech and rebuild it into separate jetpacks for everyone, allowing for the ninja to escape.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Fire Temple was planned to have been a world in LEGO Universe.[citation needed]
  • Interestingly, the set version of the fire temple is built over water rather than lava.
  • The Fire Temple is apparently very close to the Underworld, to the point that it is possible for beings to physically travel between the two realms.
  • Although the lava in the temple is extremely hot, it does not burn as hot as Torchfire Mountain, because the lava contained inside of the temple was insufficient to destroy a Fangblade.
