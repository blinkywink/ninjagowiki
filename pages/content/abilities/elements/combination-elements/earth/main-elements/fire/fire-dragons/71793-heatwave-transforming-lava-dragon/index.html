Overview

This article is about the set. For the actual dragon, see Heatwave.

71793 Heatwave Transforming Lava Dragon is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 1 set that was released on June 1, 2023. It includes Merge Kai, Merge Lloyd, Wyldfyre, Lord Ras, a Claw Hunter, the purple Dragon Core, a hover chariot for Ras, and Heatwave.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Ninja fans aged 8+ can recreate their favorite scenes from the NINJAGO® Dragons Rising TV series with this Heatwave Transforming Lava Dragon (71793) playset. It features a never-before-seen dragon that kids can play with in 2 modes. In peaceful mode it stands majestically but when flipped upside down it switches to battle mode, which allows it to fire out a purple dragon power element from its chest.

Playset packed with minifigures and cool accessories
This ninja dragon playset comes with 5 minifigures: Kai and Lloyd, each armed with 2 katanas, Wyldfyre with her fireball fist, Imperium Claw Hunter with an Imperium sword and Lord Ras with a golden hammer and a battle hovercraft. There are 4 more dragon power elements in different colors and 3 dragon energy cores for kids to collect in other NINJAGO sets.
