Overview

9591 Weapon Pack is a Ninjago Spinners - Rise of the Snakes set that was released on June 2, 2012. It includes NRG Kai, Chokun, their spinners, a Translucent Orange Viper, a Golden Battle Mace, a Golden Double-Bladed Spear, a Katana, a Trusty Axe, a Dragon's Fire on a Stick, a Lava Spike, a Battle Spear, a Silver Dark Blade, a Knockout Nemesis 3000 Hammer, two Character cards, six bricks, and eight Battle cards.
