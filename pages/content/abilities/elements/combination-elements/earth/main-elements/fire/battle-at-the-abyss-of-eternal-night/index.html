The Battle at the Abyss of Eternal Night was a conflict that occurred in the Never Kingdoms, where the Weapon of Desolation possessed Zilvar, making him summon Elemental Monsters for protection and to stop the ninja from destroying it.

History

History[]

Prelude[]

Deemed too dangerous to remain intact, the original six ninja took the Weapon of Desolation on a mission to destroy it within the Abyss of Eternal Night, leaving the young ninja behind to defend Ninjago City. Storing the artifact inside a containment unit on the Land Bounty, they traveled through the Land of Madness. There, they liberated a group of enslaved gladiators forced to fight by the Pit-Master.[1] Among them were Zilvar and his companion Grimtak, who decided to join the ninja on their journey to the Never Kingdoms. Upon arriving, they met Akita and Beniro. Needing a guide who could navigate to the abyss by scent, Beniro offered to help the ninja on their journey while Zilvar was offered to remain to settle a new life.[2] However, as the ninja left, Ras confronted Zilvar and demanded the weapon remain intact to protect his master and continue with his plan to undo the Merge, which would allow him to resurrect the fallen Tiger Tribe. Horrified, Zilvar refused to cooperate. When Ras threatened to eliminate them all, Zilvar whistled for Grimtak, who headbutted Ras to create a distraction. Zilvar quickly mounted his companion and rode off to warn the ninja,[3] catching up to them just as the weapon's energy corrupted both the Boreal River and Beniro, triggering an attack by Ice Monsters. Zilvar retrieved the weapon, immediately restoring the river and freeing Beniro.[2] But before he could warn the ninja about Ras, the Weapon of Desolation restrained Zilvar, manipulating the tiger's mind by reminding him of Ras' promise. Blinded by the potential hope of bringing back his people, Zilvar hid the truth and claimed he followed the team out of a gut feeling.[3] Because the artifact's containment unit was shattered and Beniro was unable to continue, the ninja tasked Zilvar with carrying the Weapon of Desolation, which was gradually corrupting him.[2]

Battle[]

The battle begins when Zilvar gives in to the corruption of the Weapon of Desolation and was compelled to betray the ninja, attacking them and summoning Elemental Monsters. With no other choice, Lloyd powers them all up into their Draconus Forms so they can defeat the monsters and make it easier to avoid the corrupted tiger. The situation became more complicated for the ninja when the Weapon of Desolation told Zilvar its secret ability: absorbing Elemental Monsters to use their powers, unleashing brutal attacks in the process. Jay believes he can get through to Zilvar, knowing this isn't who he really is. The Blue Ninja attempts to reason with the tiger and says he was just like him; he lost his people to, he was lied to, captured, lost who he was, and was also corrupted by a magical artifact like the warrior is at the very moment and finishes by saying that if he was able to survive, throw off corruption and anger, and still find himself, then the tiger can too. Zilvar briefly pauses after he hears this, but the weapon resumed its power over him and the tiger warrior launches Jay into the air and slams him into the ground with a simultaneous elemental attack, gravely wounding him and scaring Nya. Kai, enraged, charges at Zilvar but he gets launched back by the weapon's elemental energy.[4] As the battle continues, Kai says if Zilvar won't throw the weapon into the Abyss of Eternal Night, then they may have to throw the warrior in too, but Lloyd refuses, and they think of another way to get the sentient scepter free. Lloyd then flies at Zilvar and is able to knock the weapon out of Zilvar's hand while Jay zaps it down into the abyss, thinking they succeeded. However, the Weapon of Desolation floats back up and deepens its corruption on Zilvar before creating more Elemental Monsters. The ninja then think of a new way to destroy the weapon when Lloyd realizes he can stabilize the Elemental Powers coming from the weapon, but Kai screams at Lloyd that he can't touch it. Lloyd assures that he can handle it but quietly says to himself that he hopes he can, so he flies to the weapon itself rather than Zilvar, and is able to grab the evil artifact and shut it down, freeing the tiger from its control. The battle is over and the ninja won, but a grave consequence presents itself. Nya asks Lloyd how he knew that would work and Lloyd attempts to answer by saying he didn't but before he can finish, he senses something is happening when he is flooded by Life, which causes his physical body to explode into Life Energy particles. The ninja revert from their Draconus Forms and call out Lloyd's name but nothing happens. Zilvar then regains consciousness and Kai goes to attack him for seemingly killing Lloyd but Jay stops him, saying the tiger didn't know what he was doing as he was possessed. Horrified, Zilvar then realizes what had happened and asks if the Green Ninja is really gone and Kai responds with a bitter yes. However, Jay refuses to accept this as he just got back his memories of him, but then sees they still have their powers, thinking it's Lloyd but Nya said it could just be their friend’s final gift to them. Zane then says that after the Temple of the Green Ninja, Lloyd had a new energy, a different energy, but he could not feel that energy anymore.[5]

Aftermath

Aftermath[]

The ninja and Zilvar leave the Abyss of Eternal Night and head back to the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms. Zilvar shows remorse for taking Lloyd away from the ninja and says they are too quick to forgive him, but Jay and Nya assure him that it wasn't his fault and Ras is a master at manipulation. However, the tiger says the shame is his own and hopes to one day make up for his actions if such a thing is possible. He then bids farewell to the ninja as he leaves the Never Kingdoms with Grimtak, while the ninja board the Land Bounty to head back to Ninjago. They arrive back to the Monastery of Spinjitzu, with the young ninja excited to tell them what happened in their adventure but notice something is wrong. Wyldfyre asks what the problem is and Arin notices Lloyd is not with them. When he sees Nya's eyes watering and the others deeply saddened, he and the young ninja realize that Lloyd is gone. Arin falls to his knees devastated and Wyldfyre cries into Frak's shoulder. A memorial is held for Lloyd at the Summit of the Merged Lands and Percival gives a speech about him. He expresses how Lloyd Garmadon was a hero, a leader, and inspiration, inspiring them all and he declares that the first act of the United Lands is not only to honor the legendary Green Ninja but to honor those that carry on his ideals. He awards the ninja with medals and says that their world has never been safer and never has the future been brighter than that day and thanks the group for their service as they bow. Jay is still at the Monastery and tells the picture of Lloyd that he didn't go to the memorial, not because he didn't think Lloyd was worth a statue but because he wasn't going to a memorial for someone that he is certain is still alive somehow and then walks off, hoping his fallen friend would understand. However, Lloyd's spirit appears before Jay and leads him to the library. The spirit flies into a stone pillar and Jay walks up to it and presses one of the tiles on it, revealing a secret compartment and realizes the one of the cutout spots is for the object he saw at Four Weapons Blacksmith when he was a kid.[5]
