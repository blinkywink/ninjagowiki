Overview

This article is about the Legacy set. For the original set, see 70721 Kai Fighter. For the actual vehicle, see Kai Fighter.

71704 Kai Fighter is a Ninjago: Legacy Wave 2 set that was released in most countries on June 1, 2020, in most US stores on August 15, 2020, and in Canada and LEGO stores in the US on August 24, 2020. It includes Legacy (sleeveless) Kai, Legacy (sleeveless) Lloyd, two Legacy Nindroid Warriors, the Kai Fighter, and a small Nindroid turret that is equipped with a single spring-loaded shooter.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This set is a Legacy remake of 2014 70721 Kai Fighter from Rebooted.
    • The piece count for this Legacy remake is almost three times higher than the original.
    • In addition to the piece count, there are also twice as many Minifigures in this version.
  • It shares similarities with a 2020 set released earlier, that being the mech jet.
    • Both are jet builds for Kai.
  • One of the Nindroids has an armor piece, which is the same shoulder armor that Mr. E and many other S.O.G. bikers wear.
    • Interestingly, in the 360 turn-around of this set, both Nindroid minifigures are shown wearing shoulder armor, but in the official images, only one of the two includes the armor.
    • The set contains two armor pieces, despite the instructions dictating that only one Nindroid is built with the armor piece.
    • The Nindroid with armor is likely a reference to Cryptor, who was included in the original version of the set, however it is not named after him.
  • The Legacy (Sleeveless) Lloyd in this set rectifies a mistake on previous releases of the figure, where the letters on the gi spelt "MANTER" as opposed to "MASTER."
  • In the promotional picture displaying all that is included in the set, Lloyd's body is misaligned with his legs.
  • When this set was released, the Legacy (sleeveless) Kai minifigure was exclusive to this set, until 71735 Tournament of Elements and 71737 X-1 Ninja Charger were released.
  • This set is very similar to 70668 Jay's Storm Fighter, as they both contain two ninja and two villains with a small side structure. Both fighters are also very similar in function and design.
