— Kai, "What the Mech?"

The Elemental Fire Mech was an elemental mech used by Kai.

History

History[]

The Elemental Mechs[]

What the Mech?[]

Sora used Technology to turn Chronosteel into three discs. When thrown into the air, each of the discs assembles into its respective mech: the Elemental Earth Mech, Elemental Fire Mech, and Elemental Tech Mech, each capable of channeling their respective Elemental Master's power. Together, Cole and Kai used their mechs to restrain an out-of-control Lobbo, while Sora drained the harmful energy from him.

The Mech Master[]

After The Mechanic stole the mechs from the Mech Master 5000, the ninja attempted to stop them with their mechs. However, the Mechanic sent one mech crashing into them, breaking their mechs apart. Kai repaired his using an arm from Cole's mech and the legs from Sora's, and realized this allowed him to channel Cole's Earth power because of the Chronosteel.

A Pain in the Mech![]

Kai attempted to use his mech to fight some of the Lava-Tides, but his Fire had no effect on them. He swapped arms with Cole to allow him to use Earth against them. Seeing another Ice Dragon attacking, Kai quickly swapped arms with Sora, allowing her to defend herself with Fire while Kai used Technology to restrain it.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

Kai used the Elemental Fire Mech to destroy crates inside the hangar bay under the Monastery of Spinjitzu. He did this to vent his anger at losing the battle to Cinder at Cloud Kingdom. This activity bothered Nya, who told Kai that it wouldn't help them save Cloud Kingdom.

Force From the East[]

The Spell at the Waterfall[]

Kai use his mech for training.

Rising Ninja[]

Kai engaged in battle with the Wolf Mask Claw Warriors using the Elemental Fire Mech, but the warriors proved to be resistant to his fire attacks, frustrating Kai. One of the Wolf Mask Claw Warriors managed to sever the right arm of the mech, prompting Kai to swiftly replace it with the arm from the Elemental Tech Mech. Kai persisted in his fight against the Wolf Mask Claw Warriors, successfully keeping them at bay and preventing them from reaching the Writers of Destiny. However, another Wolf Mask Claw Warrior launched an attack on the mech, causing it to explode.

When Ras attempted to sacrifice Nya to the Forbidden Five, Arin attempted to utilize Object Spinjitzu on the mech's hand that had been previously severed. Unfortunately, Arin's attempt failed, leading Sora to discreetly use her Elemental Powers to activate the mech's hand, ultimately knocking Ras away.

Official description[]

Now, for the first time, you can combine the parts of 3 awesome ninja mechs piloted by Sora, Cole and Kai to create your own unique mech.
