Overview

“From the rotation of the earth to the blood pumping through your body, the universe is always in Motion.”
— Glen Lakin on Bluesky[1]

Motion is a form of Source Energy, personified by the Source Dragon of Motion. It is associated with the colors red and orange.

User's abilities

User's abilities[]

  • Motokinesis - The user is able to manipulate the action or process of motion.
    • Motokinetic Beam Emission - The user can unleash powerful beams of concentrated motion.
  • Precognition - The user is capable of viewing and perceiving potential futures.
  • Megethoskinesis - As seen with the Source Dragon of Motion, the user can change the size of their physical body at will.
  • Imagogenesis - The user can create moving images as shown by the Source Dragon of Motion.
  • Dragon Icon Creation - As it is a form of dragon energy, the user can create a Source Dragon Icon.

Higher-level powers[]

  • Elemental Power Creation/Bestowal - The user can create and grant others Elemental Powers sourced from Motion.
    • Elemento-Potentikinesis Bestowal - When used and combined with other forms of Source Dragon energy, Motion can bestow the Elemental Essence of Golden Power to other beings.
    • Pyrokinesis Bestowal - Motion can bestow the Elemental Power of Fire to other beings.
    • Superhuman Speed Bestowal - Motion can bestow the Elemental Power of Speed to other beings.
    • Sonokinesis/Audiokinesis Bestowal - Motion can bestow the Elemental Power of Sound to other beings.
    • Vibrokinesis Bestowal - Motion can bestow the Elemental Power of Quake to other beings.
    • Seismokinesis Bestowal - Motion can bestow the Elemental Power of Shockwave to other beings.
  • Dimensional Rift Creation - As seen with the Dragon Cores, which is made from Motion and the other sources, users can tear rifts in the fabric of reality, exposing the Ethereal Divide.
    • Vacuum Effect - Such rifts are identical to MergeQuakes and have a vacuum effect that sucks nearby objects or people in.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Glen Lakin stated that the differences between the concepts of Flow and Motion as dynamic vs static movement[10] and can be compared to the differences between doing yoga and sprinting.[11]
  • When revealing the Elemental Powers coming from the source of Motion, Glen Lakin used these poetic descriptions:
    • Fire - The dance of a camp fire, the lick of a flame. The hotter it burns, the faster it spreads.[5]
    • Speed - Closing a distance in the blink of an eye, the body itself in motion - IS Motion.[6]
    • Sound - Whether a piercing scream or a song from the heart, Sound is vibration, Motion tiny and numerous.[7]
    • Quake - The shifting of stone, a shaking that rattles us to our core - the guts of the world in Motion.[8]
    • Shockwave - The aftermath of Motion, the air itself exhaling. A release that can move others in its wake.[9]
