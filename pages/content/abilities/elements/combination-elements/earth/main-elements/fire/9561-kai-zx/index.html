Overview

9561 Kai ZX is a Ninjago Spinners - Rise of the Snakes set that was released on March 3, 2012. It includes Kai ZX, his spinner along with a spinner crown, the Sword of Fire, a Silver Serpent Striker, along with a Nin-Jô, a Character Card, and four Battle Cards.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the only spinner set with a ZX Ninja and its correct Golden Weapon that corresponds with a weapon in the show. As for the other ninja, Cole had a Golden Tri Scythe, Zane had a Golden Wiggly Fang, and Jay had a Golden Double-Bladed Sword).
  • The spinner, spinner crown, and all five cards found within this set are exclusive to it.
  • It and 9567 Fang-Suei are the only two spinner sets depicting Hypnobrai in packaging art.
  • It and 9579 Starter Set are the only two spinner sets that include a ZX suit. Jay ZX and Zane ZX will include in Booster Packs.
