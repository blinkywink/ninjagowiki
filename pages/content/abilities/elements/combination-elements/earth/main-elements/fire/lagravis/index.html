Overview

Lagravis is the king of the Lion Tribe, Lavertus' brother, and the father of Laval.

History

History[]

Long ago, when Laval was merely a cub, they exiled one of their greatest warriors and Lagravis' brother: Lavertus. They didn't see or hear from Lavertus for ages.  

The Legend of Chima/The Great Story[]

Lagravis was king of the Lion Tribe that protected the CHI. When Cragger plugged a CHI orb at an early age, Lagravis and the Lions stopped him. He then got in a fight with Crominus. When Laval was in trouble, Lagravis came to his aid with the Lion army. Lagravis also moderated the contest for the Golden CHI. When Laval was to get his first taste of CHI, Lagravis was present. Unfortunately, the Crocs, allied with the Wolves and Ravens, attacked the Lion Temple. Lagravis then gave a roar which summoned the Eagles and Gorillas to his aid. Lagravis fought well but being outnumbered, the Lions began to lose. Fortunately, Laval came with the Lion Legend Beast, and the Lion Temple was saved.

Crocodile Tears[]

The Crocodile Tribe had been taken prisoner by the Wolf Tribe, prompting Laval to urge immediate action. However, Lagravis reminded his son that the Lion Tribe possessed very little Chi and could not challenge the Wolves on their own. Lagravis explained that he had already arranged a meeting with the Elders of Chima, though he was uncertain whether they would be willing to rescue the Crocodiles after all the trouble the tribe had caused throughout Chima. Laval acknowledged the Crocodiles' troubled past but insisted that their imprisonment was still wrong. He argued that no one in Chima could truly be free while even one of them remained locked inside a cage. Determined to do something, Laval left despite Lagravis' warning not to do anything reckless. Longtooth then jokingly wished Lagravis good luck with that, only to realize he had said it aloud when Lagravis gave him an angry look.

At the council meeting, Lagravis and the Elders of Chima agreed that they would consider launching a rescue mission if there was evidence that the Wolf Tribe was mistreating the Crocodile Tribe. Skinnet was stationed as a neutral observer on a hill overlooking the Wolf Camp to monitor the Wolves' treatment of their prisoners. Later, Skinnet informed the elders about the Wolves' treatment of the Crocodile Tribe. Lagravis was shocked by what he heard, wondering whether the Wolves might be redeeming themselves.

Eventually, the Elders of Chima agreed to debate a possible rescue effort for the Crocodile Tribe later that week. When Laval returned to the Lion Temple, Lagravis informed him of the decision. Laval, however, revealed that he had already freed the entire Crocodile Tribe. Lagravis was stunned and immediately questioned how Laval had managed such a feat against the Wolf Tribe. Laval enthusiastically described his prison break, claiming that he had overwhelmed the Wolves with his strength, assisted by Eris and ShadoWind, who was actually Furtivo in disguise. Despite his surprise, Lagravis praised his son's actions and concluded that Laval had helped restore Chima's unity. He repeated the principle that no one could truly be free while the Crocodiles lived in cages. Laval corrected his father's wording, reminding him that his original statement had been that no one in Chima was free if even a single one of them remained locked inside a cage. Laval was delighted that Lagravis had quoted him, particularly because he often repeated his father's wisdom himself. Lagravis warmly acknowledged that he could learn something from his son from time to time.
