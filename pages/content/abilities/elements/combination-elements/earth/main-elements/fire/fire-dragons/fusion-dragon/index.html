Overview

“Only a Fusion Dragon can plunge the depths of the Boiling Sea, and only the Dragon Blade can create one.”
— Ray to Kai and Nya, "Out of the Fire and Into the Boiling Sea"

The Fusion Dragon is an elemental combination dragon made from the Elemental Masters of Fire and Water using the Dragon Blade.

History

History[]

Background[]

After the Battle for All Time, Ray and Maya summoned and used the Fusion Dragon to reach Hono Mizu to hide the Reversal Blade.

Out of the Fire and Into the Boiling Sea[]

Kai and Nya created the Fusion Dragon to traverse through the Boiling Sea. Later, when Kai and Nya needed to escape the Boiling Sea, they summoned the dragon again.

After the Vermillion let the ninja fall into the Sea, the dragon was summoned to save them.

Lost in Time[]

To help aid in the battle, Kai and Nya used the Fusion Dragon. However, Nya jumped off to retrieve the Reversal Blade. Without her, the dragon disappeared. It reappeared once they reversed time, and Kai, Nya, and Wu follow the Time Twins into the vortex.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Symbolizing the unity of Fire and Water, the two-headed Elemental Dragon of Fusion is the only creature powerful enough to withstand the fierce environment of the Boiling Sea. It can be summoned only by the Dragon Dagger and is so strong-willed and powerful that it must be ridden by the Elemental Masters of Fire and Water – only when both Masters mount it can it be controlled. Ray and Maya were the first to ride the Fusion Dragon, using it to plunge to the depths of the Boiling Sea to hide the Reversal Blade, and later Kai and Nya followed in their parents’ footsteps to retrieve that Time Blade to save Master Wu.[1]

Powers and abilities

Powers and abilities[]

  • Pyrokinesis - The Fusion Dragon is able to manipulate, generate, negate, and control Fire.
  • Hydrokinesis/Aquakinesis - The Fusion Dragon can manipulate and generate Water.
  • Underwater Breathing/Swimming: The Fusion Dragon breathe in and out of Water.
  • Flight - The Fusion Dragon can fly using their wings.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Co-dependency - The Fusion Dragon requires both elemental masters to be present in order to maintain its existence.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the second elemental combination dragon, preceded by the Hydroelectric Dragon and followed by the Golden Ultra Dragon.
  • This is the second multi-headed dragon after the Ultra Dragon.
  • This is the first elemental combination dragon to be released in set form.
    • However the Character Encyclopedia Updated and Expanded stated that 70602 Jay's Elemental Dragon is actually the Hydroelectric Dragon rather than the Lightning Dragon.
  • The Dragon Blade is needed to create the dragon, because unlike water and electricity (the Hydroelectric Dragon), water and fire do not combine naturally.[2]
  • In Choose Your Ninja Mission, the Fusion Dragon was also known as the "Water-Fire Dragon" and the "Elemental Dragon of Fusion."
