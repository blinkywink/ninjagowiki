Overview

“As lions, we must maintain the peace and balance of Chima. It is our sacred duty, given to us by nature itself.”
— Lagravis to Laval, "The Great Story"

The Lion Tribe is a tribe of humanoid lions from Chima. They guard the Sacred Pool of Chi.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Lion Tribe's clothing seems to be inspired by Ancient Greece and Ancient Rome, both of which often used lions in many aspects of their culture.
  • Li'Ella is the only female Lion member who is both named and physically seen in the TV show. As Li'Ella does not originate from this tribe, no lioness from it was ever shown in the show, although one does appear in a set.
  • The Lion Tribe was the last of the tribes introduced at the beginning of the Legend of Chima series to have female members of their tribe introduced. The reason for this was that the female lions would need to have their own head mold due to the fact that female Lions do not have manes as male lions do.
  • The Lion Tribe is one of the oldest tribes in Chima that have not extinct as Sir Fangar confirmed their existent even during the first Hunter Wars.
  • In the concept art, the Lions are on many occasions shown with large hair, instead of their current animal head.
