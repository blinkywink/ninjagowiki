Overview

This article is about the Elemental Dragon. For other uses, see Fire Dragon (disambiguation).

The Fire Dragon[1] is an Elemental Dragon. It is a deep red and fire orange metaphysical creature that can be unlocked by an Elemental Master of Fire after facing their fears.

History

History[]

The Day of the Dragon[]

After Kai unlocked his Fire Dragon by facing his fears, he traveled with Skylor to Chen's Palace and motivated the other Masters to do the same.

The Greatest Fear of All[]

Kai used his dragon to fly back to Ninjago. Later, Kai used his Dragon again to fly to Kryptarium Prison.

Winds of Change[]

Kai used his Elemental Dragon to help the other ninja capture a Fangfish that had been terrorizing a coastal village. He later used it to ride back to Wu's new tea shop, Steep Wisdom. Unfortunately, when Morro took control of Lloyd, the ninja lost their elemental abilities, and thus. their dragons.

Infamous[]

The ninja rode their Elemental Dragons to the Ninjago City Hospital.

Public Enemy Number One[]

The ninja each rode their dragons to the Monastery of Spinjitzu and away again after planning their next move.

The Attack[]

Kai attempted to save a falling Lloyd with his dragon but is impeded by flying Vermillion. After Lloyd was saved by the new Samurai X, Kai presumably flew back up to the Temple of Airjitzu's floating island.
