Elemental combination dragons are Elemental Dragons formed using more than one element.

History

History[]

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Skybound[]

Wishmasters[]

The Hydroelectric Dragon was created when Jay and Nya were the only ninja who were not trapped in the Djinn Blade. After falling off the Misfortune's Keep, they were unable to summon their own dragons for their battle since the Sky Pirates had weakened them. They came closer, and started holding hands, forming a fusion ball of water. The Hydroelectric Dragon had been created; Nya stated that water and electricity do mix, and Jay responded with a snicker.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

The Hands of Time[]

Out of the Fire and Into the Boiling Sea[]

Ray and Maya instructed Kai and Nya to use their Elemental Powers to summon the Fusion Dragon. By shooting blasts of their Elemental Powers towards the Dragon Blade, they were able to do so and traversed through the Boiling Sea. They later summoned the dragon two times more in order to escape the Boiling Sea and not to fall into the same sea when the Vermillion threw them and Master Wu off Machia's ship, whereupon they rode the Fusion Dragon into the temporal vortex, following the Time Twins.

Lost in Time[]

Just as Acronix and Krux were deciding whether to finish off Wu or Garmadon first, Kai and Nya summoned the Fusion Dragon once more and rode into battle with the Iron Doom. They tried to take the Reversal Blade but failed: The Iron Doom knocked the Fusion Dragon and caused it to steer out of control. The Iron Doom then shot eggs at the Fusion Dragon. Realizing that the Past Reversal Blade was still in the woods, Nya jumped off the dragon, but with only one person controlling the Fusion Dragon, it started to fade and Kai eventually lost control. Just as the Iron Doom was about to travel into the future, Kai tried to attack and stop the Iron Doom getting away, but the Iron Doom dodged the attack, causing Kai to fall. Using the Past Reversal Blade, Kai, Nya, and Wu brought back time to when Kai was still on the dragon, with Nya and Wu leaping back onto the dragon and steering it into the time vortex.

Crystalized[]

Roots[]

The four original ninja (in their Dragon Forms) decide to combine their Golden Weapons in an attempt to destroy them in hopes of preventing the Overlord from corrupting their powers. They end up becoming crystalized, though the powers from the Weapons came together to form the Golden Ultra Dragon, and saving Lloyd from his fall. The Green Ninja then rides the dragon in a final battle against the Overlord, shooting constant beams of the elements of Creation against him. Once the Overlord is knocked down, the Golden Ultra Dragon shoots beams of Fire, Lightning, Earth and Ice together at once, immobilizing him, and his form is shattered by the swipe of the tail from the Golden Ultra Dragon, thus ending the Overlord's rule over Ninjago once more. After reuniting with Lloyd and their allies, the four ninja meet with the Golden Ultra Dragon, realizing that it is a manifestation of their combinied powers. The Dragon then fades away from existence, thus leaving the four ninja powerless until each of them regain their powers sometime later.

Abilities

Abilities[]

Elemental combination dragons are able to harness the original elemental powers from which they were made.

Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

Elemental combination dragons may require more energy to sustain as they are made from more than one element.[1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Hydroelectric Dragon is the only combination dragon not to be multi-headed.
    • This may be because it is the only Elemental Combination Dragon to be of naturally combining elements and not needing any outside object, such as the Dragon Blade or Golden Weapons.
  • The Character Encyclopedia Updated and Expanded states 70602 Jay's Elemental Dragon is the Hydroelectric Dragon.
  • Jay, Nya and Kai participated in the creation of two of the three seen combination dragons. Consequently, both are partially, ligthning, water or fire dragons.
    • Ray and Maya are chronologically the first Elemental Masters to create a an elemental combination dragon, as they first used the Dragon Blade to create their own version of the Fusion Dragon to hide the Reversal Time Blade under the Boiling Sea.
  • The Dragon Blade is needed to create Kai and Nya's dragon, because unlike water and electricity, water and fire do not combine naturally.[2]
  • The Ultra Dragon is also a dragon that possessed multiple elements and was formed from multiple individual dragons, but it is not an Elemental Dragon.
    • The Golden Ultra Dragon, design and purpose-wise, is like a mix of the Golden Dragon (as an Elemental Golden Power Dragon that was used to defeat the Ovelord) and the Ultra Dragon (as a four-headed Dragon possessing the Elements of Creation, one for a head).
