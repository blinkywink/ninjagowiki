Overview

This article is about the Legacy set. For the original set, see 2508 Blacksmith Shop. For the location, see Four Weapons Blacksmith.

71858 Four Weapons Blacksmith is a Ninjago: Legacy Wave 6 set that was released on January 1, 2026. It is one of the sets released to honor the 15th anniversary of the franchise. It includes Legacy Blacksmith Kai, Legacy Ray, Legacy Maya, Legacy Nya, Legacy Master Wu and Benton.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The blacksmith's sign is upside down compared to how it appears in the series.
  • Benton is exclusive to this set. Although the figure is largely show-accurate, his face is inaccurate, and his helmet is pearl gold rather than dark gold.
    • The head is used in another set, 31168 Medieval Horse Knight Castle.
  • Ray and Maya are exclusive to this set, having last appeared in 70627 Dragon's Forge nearly nine years earlier.
    • They wear their Serpentine War outfits, although Ray lacks his fiery arm printing.
  • Kai's blacksmith outfit is exclusive to this set. After fans criticized the blacksmith Kai figure from 71799 Ninjago City Markets for being made of existing and inaccurate parts, the designers were intent on creating a show-accurate version of the figure.
  • The set contains many secrets and references to the series:
    • Outside the shop is the "keep off the grass" sign that appears in "Never Trust a Snake."
    • Inside is the samurai helmet and armor that Kai used to fight the Skulkin in "Way of the Ninja."
    • Inside is also a painting of what appears to be the Frozen Wasteland.
    • The set includes weapons that appeared in the sets where Kai and Nya debuted: The Double-Bladed Dagger, a black spear, and a golden katana for Kai (which debuted in the 2111 Kai); and a Bo staff and Golden Nick Daggers for Nya (which debuted in the 2172 Nya).
      • It should be noted, however, that the golden katana in this set is a different mold than the one from 2111 Kai, which used the original katana mold with an octagonal guard.
    • The roof contains Kai's ZX hood and a samurai helmet for Nya, a nod to their identities as a ninja and Samurai X respectively.
      • They also contain 2x1 plate versions of the cards Card 2 - Kai ZX and Card 5 - Samurai X.
  • This set marks the first time Wu has a light tan bo staff, faithfully representing his bamboo staff. However, he had already appeared with a dark tan staff in 71702 Golden Mech.
  • This set is a remake of 2508 Blacksmith Shop, depicting a significantly more show-accurate version of the shop.
  • Nya's casual design was updated by replacing the original fire phoenix symbol with the water phoenix symbol. This was done on her child design in WildBrain animation as well.
  • The Finnish name for the set mistakenly refers to it as "Four weaponsmiths".
