Overview

“Our Elemental vehicles. Created from our Golden Weapons. Remember those, Zane?”
— Kai, "Elemental Rider"

Ninja vehicles are vehicles used by the ninja to battle evil forces and navigate different terrains.

Some vehicles are creations from magical means, like the Golden Weapon vehicles, and are typically themed to match its ninja's element and fighting style. At the same time, the majority of the vehicles in the series are purely mechanical, ranging from motorcycles and gliders to mechs and flying ships.

Destiny's Bounty[]

The Destiny's Bounty is a large ship initially owned by pirates. It served as the Ninja's headquarters for a time and eventually gained flight capabilities thanks to Jay and Nya's ingenuity. It was destroyed by the Great Devourer and recreated as the Black Bounty by Lord Garmadon, but the Ninja reclaimed it and restored its original form. The original Bounty lost its flight capabilities while trying to escape from the Stone Army, and it was eventually destroyed and left on the Dark Island, where the Ninja used it as a hideaway and shelter from the evil forces.

Throughout the course of Season 4, Nya spent time rebuilding and upgrading the Bounty. It had a sleek new look with more technological functions, and it held up for a long while, despite multiple crashes and damages. It was ultimately destroyed by Garmadon with his Colossus at the end of Season 8.

As the people of Ninjago City wanted to give back to the Ninja for saving them from Garmadon, they gifted the Ninja with a prestigious new rebuilt version of the Bounty, which has also been through a lot but has withstood the wear and tear through the Ninjas' adventures.

It was repaired by the Ninja after the events of Season 15 and given a new look, being used in the Merge to save citizens. After surviving the Merge, Kai took the Bounty to find answers about the MergeQuakes until it was damaged by Earth Dragons and sent back to the Monastery, crashing into the mountains below. As of right now, this Bounty is ultimately rendered inoperable and replaced by the new Bounty.

The Ultra Sonic Raider was created by the Ninja using the Tornado of Creation to fight the Great Devourer; it is a massive white, black, and gold tank that can seat all four Ninja, armed with grappling hooks and sonic cannons. It was later equipped with a flight mode.

Dragons[]

Although they're not "vehicles" in the traditional sense, various dragons of Ninjago serve as powerful flying mounts, bonded to the ninja through elemental connection or destiny. They've aided the team in many key quests, offering speed, aerial combat capabilities, and are very handy when traditional vehicles aren't an option.

Dragons ridden by the ninja include:

Original dragons[]

The first four dragons were guardians of the Golden Weapons and embodied the four elements of Creation - Earth, Fire, Ice, and Lightning. They bonded with the Ninja and eventually merged into the Ultra Dragon.

  • Rocky: Cole's first dragon; the Earth Dragon that was the original guardian of the Scythe of Quakes, capable of tunneling and brute strength. It later merged with the other original dragons to form the Ultra Dragon. It was first seen in Wu's story in "Way of the Ninja" and appears in set 2509 Earth Dragon Defense.
  • Wisp: Jay's first dragon; the Lightning Dragon that was the original guardian of the Nunchucks of Lightning, known for its speed and electric breath. He became one of the four heads of the Ultra Dragon. It was first seen in "King of Shadows" and appears in set 2521 Lightning Dragon Battle.
  • Shard: Zane's first dragon; the Ice Dragon that was the original guardian of the Shurikens of Ice, able to freeze enemies mid-flight. It joined the others in forming the Ultra Dragon. It was first seen in "King of Shadows" and appears in set 2260 Ice Dragon Attack.
  • Flame: Kai's first dragon; the Fire Dragon that was the original guardian of the Sword of Fire, fierce and aggressive with powerful flame attacks. It also became part of the Ultra Dragon. It was first seen in "King of Shadows" and appears in the following sets:
    • 2507 Fire Temple (original)
    • 30535 Fire Flight (mini-build)
    • 71701 Kai's Fire Dragon (Legacy)
  • The Ultra Dragon: Lloyd's first dragon; a four-headed dragon combined from the aforementioned four dragons that was destined to commanded by the Green Ninja. It helped the ninja defeat the Great Devourer and the Overlord and later returned to the First Realm, where it unfortunately was taken down by the Dragon Hunters. It was first seen in "Day of the Great Devourer" and appears in the following sets:
    • 9450 Epic Dragon Battle (original)
    • 70679 The Ultra Dragon (Legacy)

Elemental combination dragons[]

Formed from multiple Elemental Masters' powers at once, these hybrids possess the powers of multiple elements. They're rare in the series and typically only appear during high-stakes situations.

  • The Hydroelectric Dragon: A fusion of Nya's water powers and Jay's lightning, created during a moment of emotional connection. It was first seen in "Wishmasters", but is one of the few dragons that doesn't appear in set form.
  • The Fusion Dragon: A fusion of Kai's fire powers and Nya's water, crafted using the Dragon Blade to traverse the Boiling Sea and fight the Time Twins. It was first seen in "Out of the Fire and Into the Boiling Sea" and appears in set 70627 Dragon's Forge.
  • The Golden Ultra Dragon: A fusion of the original four ninja's elemental powers from the Golden Weapons, used by Lloyd to defeat the Overlord. It was first seen in "Roots" and appears in set 71774 Lloyd's Golden Ultra Dragon.

First Realm Dragons[]

Native to the Realm of Oni and Dragons, these ancient and powerful dragons are descendants of the Firstbourne and are deeply tied to the origins of elemental power and the First Spinjitzu Master.

  • The Firstbourne: The original dragon and mother of all dragons, tied to the First Spinjitzu Master. She burns Iron Baron, then aids Wu and the ninja in escaping the First Realm and fighting Lord Garmadon's forces. She was first seen in "How to Build a Dragon" and appears in set 70653 Firstbourne.
  • The Fire Dragon: A native of the First Realm, used by Kai during the Ninjas' escape. It breathes fire and is loyal to its rider. It was first seen in "Iron & Stone", but is one of the few dragons that doesn't appear in set form.
  • The Ice Dragon: Ridden by Zane in the First Realm, it freezes enemies and terrain. It's one of the elemental dragons gifted by Firstbourne to escape the First Realm. It was first seen in "Two Lies, One Truth" and appears in set 30547 Dragon Hunter.
  • Stormbringer: Stormbringer is a lightning dragon from the First Realm that the Dragon Hunters harvested for resources. She's fast and powerful, and she returns the favor to the ninja by letting Jay ride her out of the First Realm and back to Ninjago City. She was first seen in "Firstbourne" and appears in set 70652 Stormbringer.
  • Slab: Slab is a strong and grounded dragon was used by the Dragon Hunters to battle the ninja when they were mistaken for the Oni. He went back and redeemed himself by helping Cole and the Ninja escape from the First Realm. He was first seen in "Iron & Stone" and appears in set 70655 The Dragon Pit.

Dragon allies[]

There are various domesticated or trained dragons that serve as mounts or companions, and the Ninja often use them for travel or support on adventures before the Merge.

After the Merge, the Ninja helped liberate a collective of dragons from the prisons of Imperium. Over time, Riyu matures - learning Spinjitzu basics, eventually gaining the ability to fly, and aiding the ninja in major battles such as the Ritual of the Blood Moon.
