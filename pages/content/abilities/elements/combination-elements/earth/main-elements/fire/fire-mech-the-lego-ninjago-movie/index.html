The Fire Mech was a vehicle with flamethrower hands that was used by Kai. It was destroyed by Meowthra through Lord Garmadon.

History

History[]

Kai used the Fire Mech against Garmadon's Shark Army whenever he attacked the city. One day, when Lloyd used the Ultimate Weapon in an attempt to defeat his father, he accidentally summoned Meowthra to the city. Seeing the chance for victory, Garmadon had his generals subdue Lloyd and used the weapon to have Meowthra attack and destroy the Secret Ninja Force's mechs, with the Fire Mech being his first target.

Functions

Functions[]

The Fire Mech is equipped with dual flamethrowers, which is likely where its namesake comes from.
