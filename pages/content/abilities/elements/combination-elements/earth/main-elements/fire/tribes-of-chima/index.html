Overview

"Chima. A magical world of untamed nature, beauty, and wonder."
This article is about a subject that pertains to the Legends of Chima franchise.

The Tribes of Chima are a collection of tribes of anthropomorphic animals native to Chima in the Wyldness. Originally coexistening with each other in an uneasy alliance, everything changed after an incident at the Fangs, leading to the seeming loss of the Crocodiles' king and queen. Under Crooler's manipulation, Cragger, heir to the Crocodile Tribe, demanded a change upon Chima's status quo, ideally one of Crocodile supremacy. Following Cragger's decree of secession, whether through personal gain or obligation, half of Chima's tribes would break away and join Cragger's confederation, who tried to forcibly gain control over the supply of Chi. Hostilities ceased when outside threats endangered the wellbeing of all the tribes. Following conflicts against invaders from the Outlands and Chima's original inhabitants, the tribes would establish a more unified society, dissolving whatever boundaries that separated each race from each other.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • A decade before their introduction in Ninjago: Dragons Rising, a tribe of frogs appeared in a LEGO magazine, though most comic stories about Chima are considered non-canon.
  • Legends of Chima was supposed to feature tribe older than the Hunters called the "Prehistorics" containing dinosaurs such as Triceratops, T-rexes, and Pterosaurus.[2]
  • According to the book The Battle for Chima, there existed a tribe of Cheetahs among the Fire Tribes.
  • All tribes except the peacocks appeared in sets. Only one fox, one skunk, one beaver and one leopard received minifigures.
