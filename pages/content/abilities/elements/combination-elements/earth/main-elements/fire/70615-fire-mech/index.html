Overview

For the 2013 set, see 70500 Kai's Fire Mech.

70615 Fire Mech is a set released on August 1, 2017, based on the film, The LEGO Ninjago Movie. It includes Ninja Force Kai, Ninja Force Zane, Hammer Head, Jelly, Lauren, Henry, and the Fire Mech.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Get set for sizzling battle action with this huge and highly posable THE LEGO® NINJAGO® MOVIE™ mech. The Fire Mech features an opening minifigure cockpit, 2 shoulder disc shooters and 2 arms with non-shooting fire blasters. Includes 6 minifigures with assorted weapons—including Kai’s katanas and Zane’s bow and arrow—to offer even more thrilling role-play possibilities.

  • Includes 6 minifigures: Kai, Zane, Lauren, Henry, Hammer Head and Jelly.
  • Fire Mech features an opening minifigure cockpit, posable arms and legs, 2 non-shooting fire blasters with translucent fire-style elements, 2 shoulder disc shooters, 2 fire tanks decorated with NINJAGO® symbols and 2 flags with samurai-style decoration.
  • Weapons include Kai's 2 katanas, Zane's bow and arrow, Hammer Head's fish hammer and Jelly's fish-zooka.
  • Accessory elements include an ice cream cone and popsicle.
  • Recreate and role-play exciting THE LEGO® NINJAGO® MOVIE™ scenes.
  • Fire Mech measures over 14” (36cm) high, 4” (11cm) long and 8” (21cm) wide.[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Hammer Head has a new, double-sided version of Karlof's face. His new head mold also sports an angry version of his face.
  • The set has a similar name to 70500 Kai's Fire Mech—a much smaller mech that was released in 2012 and used by Kai in the second and fifth seasons.
  • In The LEGO Ninjago Movie, Kai and Lloyd both have vehicles resembling one they previously had in the series. Kai has his Fire Mech, and Lloyd has his Dragon.
  • 70613 Garma Mecha Man appears in the production video, which makes it the first production video to showcase an entirely different set as well as the set it is advertising.
