Overview

71777 Kai's Dragon Power Flip is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 1 set that was released on June 1, 2023. It includes Dragon Power Kai and a spinner.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Kai’s Dragon Power Spinjitzu Flip (71777) action toy lets ninja fans aged 6+ train their hero and help him improve his Spinjitzu skills fueled by dragon power. Kids can enjoy unlimited fun with this flipping toy playset as they spend endless hours getting Dragon Power Kai to perform cool tricks on their own or with friends.

Help a ninja develop Spinjitzu skills
Kids can place Dragon Power Kai in the spinner, then hit one of its arms and watch as Kai performs an amazing backflip. His task is to avoid the flame obstacle elements, triumph over the Imperium Droid figure and grab the collectible golden dragon energy core element from the top of the brick-built flaming holder. There is also an orange dragon power element, and kids can collect 4 more in different colors in other NINJAGO® sets.
