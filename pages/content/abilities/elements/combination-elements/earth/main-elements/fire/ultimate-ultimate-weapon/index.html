The Ultimate Ultimate Weapon is a set of Elemental Pieces that's meant to represent the Elemental Powers that the ninja used.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Ultimate Ultimate Weapon is fairly similar to the Golden Weapons and the Elemental Blades in that they give the ninja their elemental abilities.
  • The six pieces originate form other LEGO themes:
    • Green is represented by the branch/foliage piece introduced in Castle's Forestmen sets in 1987 and 1988. It does physically exist in green, which is its most common color.
    • Fire and Water are represented by the updated version of the classic flame piece first released in Castle sets in 1993. The piece does physically exist in both orange and light blue.
    • Ice piece is represented by the common crystal piece first released in Aquazone in 1995. It does not physically exist in this exact light blue color.
    • Lightning is represented by the lightning piece introduced in Nexo Knights in 2017. It does physically exist in this darker blue color.
    • Earth is represented by the gold nugget piece introduced in the Mining subtheme of City in 2018. It does not physically exist in this black color.
  • When Garmadon takes the pieces back to Ninjago City to fight Meowthra, the cat swallows the ice piece with Garmadon.
