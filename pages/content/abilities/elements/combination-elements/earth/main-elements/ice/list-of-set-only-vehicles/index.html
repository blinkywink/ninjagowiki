Season 1: Rise of the Snakes[]

Fangpyre Mech[]

A sentient mech used by Fang-Suei against Cole. It is the primary focus of the set 9455 Fangpyre Mech. It appears to have been replaced by the Fangpyre Robot.

Season 2: Legacy of the Green Ninja

Season 2: Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

Ice spider[]

A miniature mech used by Zane to battle the Garmatron. It has smaller spinner saws on its front. It appeared in 70504 Garmatron.

Season 3: Rebooted

Season 3: Rebooted[]

Tank bike[]

A large bike which is the main focus of 70722 OverBorg Attack. Cyrus Borg's artificial limbs are able to connect to the top of the vehicle and it has various spinner blades and large swords as weapons.

Season 4: Tournament of Elements

Season 4: Tournament of Elements[]

Kai's motorbike[]

A small motorbike used by Kai that is positioned in the D.B. Express. It only appears in 70750 Ninja DB X.

Season 5: Possession

Season 5: Possession[]

Nya's speedboat[]

A speedboat that was featured in 70732 City of Stiix, and was used by Nya when she was Samurai X.

Hoverboards[]

Two miniature hoverboards that were used to fight off Morro's dragon. The two of them are identical except in color, where the ninja's main color takes precedence. They both appeared in 70736 Attack of the Morro Dragon.

Zane's flyer[]

Wu's hot air balloon[]

A hot air balloon is used by Wu in 70604 Tiger Widow Island. The balloon also appears in the intro for the 6th season. In the set, the balloon appears a bit smaller, as only Wu fits there, while Misako also fits in the show.
