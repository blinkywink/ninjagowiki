Overview

71738 Zane's Titan Mech Battle is a Ninjago: Legacy Wave 4 set that was released on January 2, 2021. It includes Legacy (Deepstone) Zane, Golden Legacy Jay, Legacy Ghoultar, Legacy Soul Archer, and the Ice Mech.

Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

"I was very excited when I was briefed to make a new big version of Zane’s mech. I am really happy with how this thing turned out. As a big fan of the original set, this was so much fun.

From the beginning, I was briefed to make this a $60 version of Zane's mech and that was a really cool thought experiment. Because the show stuck very close to the design of the set, I was told to use this more as an exploration of the concept.

I did some sketches closer to the original design, followed by some sketches that were very far out there. With both extremes mapped out, I was then tasked to find a nice middle ground. This set the final direction and then I went into refining mode, focusing on the build and small details.

Check out this twitter thread for more pictures and insights: https://twitter.com/Toothdominoes/status/1335952649355747328"[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This set is a Legacy remake of the 2015 set 70737 Titan Mech Battle from Possession.
    • This is the first Legacy remake of a Possession set.
    • The Ice Mech is larger and more detailed than the original.
    • Both Titan Mech Battle sets include Zane, Jay, Ghoultar, and the Titan Mech itself, while this version includes Soul Archer and excludes Nya, Bansha, a Skreemer, the Samurai Mech, and the Harpoon shooter.
  • All minifgures are exclusive to this set.
    • However Legacy Soul Archer appearance is identical to the Ghost Warrior included in 71733 Epic Battle Set - Cole vs. Ghost Warrior.
  • The Ice Mech is one of four Ninjago mech builds to have working knee joints. Most mech builds of its size have static knees.
  • The Legacy Soul Archer and Ghoultar are identical, save for Ghoultar's shoulder pads and different masks.
  • The box art depicts the Wailing Alps and the Blind Man's Eye, referencing the Ice Mech's debut episode.
  • While the Samurai Mech is excluded, the product animation depicts Ghoultar taking the Titan Mech over in a similar manner.
  • On the Target website, Jay's mask is fully gold in one of the pictures)
