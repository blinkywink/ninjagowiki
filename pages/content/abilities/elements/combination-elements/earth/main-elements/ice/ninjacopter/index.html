The NinjaCopter was a Hover-Copter that was hacked into by Zane using his Techno Blade.

History

History[]

The Surge[]

Zane managed to hack a Hover-Copter using his Techno Blade and used it to save the students, Master Wu, and Nya from the factory. After they had been sent back to the academy, Zane used it to defeat the other Hover-Copters until they retreated from the city.

The Art of the Silent Fist[]

The NinjaCopter was used as a diversion for the ninja to escape.

Blackout[]

The ninja search for the compact converted jiggly compressor at Ed & Edna's Scrap N Junk, which would allow the NinjaCopter to run on solar power.

The Curse of the Golden Master[]

Zane dropped the ninja off at the city using the NinjaCopter.

Enter the Digiverse[]

In the Digiverse, Zane used the NinjaCopter to reach the reboot activation port faster.

Codename: Arcturus[]

Zane traveled to the Nindroids' launch site using the NinjaCopter.

The Titanium Ninja[]

Zane lands in the NinjaCopter when he entered the atmosphere. He uses it to infiltrate the city in order to defeat the Golden Master, but it is destroyed when it is sucked into The Overlord's Spinjitzu tornado.

Functions

Functions[]

The NinjaCopter is armed with many weapons, ranging from micro missiles to large-scale rockets mounted on the secondary rear thrusters. It also has lasers.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The NinjaCopter is the first vehicle that is created using the Techno Blades.
  • While it is called the NinjaCopter in the sets, it is only referred to as a Hover-Copter in the TV series (by Ed in "Blackout").
  • The NinjaCopter appeared in every episode of Rebooted, except "Blackout".
