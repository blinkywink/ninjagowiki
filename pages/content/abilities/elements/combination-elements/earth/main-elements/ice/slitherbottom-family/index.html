Overview

The Slitherbottom family is a Serpentine family consisting of Constrictai and Venomari.[1] The family is composed of Frak, his father Reginald, his mother and his uncle.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Frak and his father bear resemblance to the snake warriors.
  • They are the second known Serpentine family, following Skales' family.
  • The Slitherbottom family is the first known Serpentine family to be composed of individuals of different tribes, with one even being a hybrid.
    • Considering Frak's dad, Reginald, is a Constrictai, the family used to be solely Constrictai until he got married to a Venomari.
  • Frak is the first known member of his family to be an Elemental Master.
  • The surname Slitherbottom could be a reference to the Longbottom family from Harry Potter.
  • The Slitherbottoms, Pythor P. Chumsworth and SsssSsss Underwood are the only Serpentine to have surnames, and are the only anthropomorphic animals to have surnames, along with Alfonzo Frohicky.
