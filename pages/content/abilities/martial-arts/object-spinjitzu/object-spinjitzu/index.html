Overview

“You transferred Spinjitzu to an object.”
— Lloyd to Arin, "Crossroads Carnival"

Object Spinjitzu is a Spinjitzu-based martial art that was inadvertently created by Arin. It allows the user to transfer their Spinjitzu energy into an inanimate object. It appears to give the object more mass than it actually has. Arin had trouble controlling it, but it seems that he is now able to use it consistently after training under Ras. He was able to use this martial art to restore the Unbidden Storm.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Crossroads Carnival[]

As Arin watched Dorama's puppet crushing Sora, his immediate reaction was to throw a gramophone horn at the puppet. Arin unintentionally activated Object Spinjitzu, causing his Spinjitzu power to transfer to the gramophone horn upon the throw.

Gangs of the Sea[]

Hoping to pass time to the Mother Garden, Lloyd asked Arin to try Object Spinjitzu, which he hadn't used since the battle with Dorama before eventually practicing it with some plates. When Arin fully achieved it, he accidentally damaged the Destiny's Bounty's engine.

The Power Within[]

Arin quickly picked up a rock and channeled his Spinjitzu energy into it as he threw it at Beatrix, causing her to fall to the ground and creating a hole in the Empress Mech.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Spectral Lands[]

When Arin saw Ras' soul slowly getting absorbed by three Soul Suckers, he channeled his Spinjitzu energy into the Returning Blade and used it to knock them away from Ras, saving his life.

Their Draconic Majesty's Request[]

Arin transferred Spinjitzu momentum to the Returning Blade, which he threw at Jay while the latter was attempting to capture Ras. However, Jay managed to deflect the Spinjitzu-infused Prismatic Blade using his Lightning Daggers.

The Shatter Dragon[]

Arin used Object Spinjitzu on the healing elixir, sending Thunderfang to the Spectral Lands in the process.

Casket of Bones[]

Arin used Object Spinjitzu on a bone to attack Ras, but the latter deflected it with his hammer and the bone caused a rockslide and nearly crushed them.

Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon[]

Arin used Object Spinjitzu on a broken Returning Blade to attack Spectral Dragonians, but The Sprite leads way to the Ninja to find a portal gate.

Chaos Reigns[]

After Lloyd failed to repair the Unbidden Storm with Energy, Arin decided to use Object Spinjitzu on the stone, fixing it.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

The Whispering Wood[]

Arin used Object Spinjitzu on a stick to attack Acid Monsters, yet it didn't damage them.

A Wonder of the Merged Lands[]

Arin collects all the rocks while Zane lifts up with his tentacles on him, making the rain of Object Spinjitzu to attack Mutation Monsters.

Just Under the Surface[]

Euphrasia throws rocks on Arin's Spinjitzu while he transfers the Object Spinjitzu on them, defeating all the Elemental Monsters, which tires him out.

Birthright[]

Arin used Object Spinjitzu on a rock to attack Mutation Monsters, saving Sora and Nya in Battle of Imperium.

Other appearances and mentions

Other appearances and mentions[]

The Blood Moon[]

Arin mentions his Object Spinjitzu to Sora when he says that his skills haven't improved.

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

Arin attempted to perform Object Spinjitzu on a rock, but was unsuccessful in his endeavor.

To Mysterium[]

While in the Training plateau, Arin tries to practice his Object Spinjitzu but was unsuccessful.

Fugitives From Madness[]

Sora attempted to uplift Arin's spirits by acknowledging his unique talent for Object Spinjitzu, emphasizing that no one else possessed that skill. However, Arin responded by admitting that his proficiency in Object Spinjitzu was limited.

Rising Ninja[]

Ras tried to sacrifice Nya to the Forbidden Five, but Arin intervened by trying to use Object Spinjitzu on the Elemental Fire Mech's severed hand. Little did he know that Sora, anticipating his failure, discreetly used her Elemental Power to activate the mech's hand, causing Ras to be knocked away.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Tornado Generation/Energy Aura - When the user charges up for Object Spinjitzu, they emit a tornado-like energy aura.
    • Energy Channeling/Transferal - Object Spinjitzu can channel or transfer a tornado-like energy aura into other objects.
    • Object Restoration - Object Spinjitzu can restore other objects, such as when Arin used it to bring the Unbidden Storm back to full power.
  • Excellent Combat - Object Spinjitzu is very effective bare-handed, as Arin was able to fight off opponents with it several times.
  • Aerial Boost - By propelling themselves upwards in a certain direction (using a jump or a running jump) and then doing Object Spinjitzu, the user can be carried through the air in that direction for a short time.
  • Jump Enhancement - This technique is most commonly used as a boost that can significantly increase the reach of a jump.

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • The creative team behind Ninjago dedicated numerous pages to explore Arin's Spinjitzu. The designers anticipated that Arin's Spinjitzu would deviate from the norm due to Arin teaching himself the technique. The process of the exact manifestation of his unique approach led to the development of Object Spinjitzu.[1]
