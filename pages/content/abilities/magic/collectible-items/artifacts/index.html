Overview

The expansive world of Ninjago and the rest of the Merged Realms is home to many powerful artifacts. While several artifacts are simply powerless and ordinary antiques or objects that merely lead to a hidden location or serve as keys, many artefacts are in fact powerful weapons, equipment or objects that are normally rare, extremely hard to collect and dangerous if used for evil.

Many artifacts can be found in places such as the Ninjago Museum of History or the Explorer's Club. Following their many adventures, the Ninja often entrust Cyrus Borg to keep artifacts with supernatural abilities in hidden and guarded locations for safekeeping to not fall into into the wrong hands; locations such as the Borg Tower vaults and formerly Hiroshi's Labyrinth Stronghold.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Several artifacts held either the power of Creation/Golden Power or the the elements which comprised it:
    • The Golden Weapons, the Elemental Blades, the Sword used by Kai in the Land of Monsters (in the case of Fire) and the non-canon Obsidian Weapons and Elemental Forges were imbued with the four elements of Creation.
    • The Staff of Elements, the Djinn Blade (in an erased timeline) and the Elemental Cup either absorbed the elements or captured them alongside their current Elemental Masters and allowed the wielder to use their power.
    • The Techno Blades, the Dragon Blade (in the case of Fire), the Scrolls of Forbidden Spinjitzu (alongside the scepters connected to them) and the Dragon Hooks (save for Lightning) channeled the elements for use in one way or another.
    • The Golden Weapons' merged forms of the Mega Weapon and the Golden Armor also fused the component weapons' base elements to form Creation and Golden Power, respectively.
  • The Idol of Unity's appearance resembles the First Spinjitzu Master.
  • The First Spinjitzu Master is responsible for the creation of several powerful artefacts seen in the series, such as the Golden Weapons, the Elemental Blades, the Realm Crystal, the Dragonbone Blade (alongside the Firstbourne), the Scrolls of Forbidden Spinjitzu and the Dragon Cores.
  • From Season 4 onwards, Cyrus Borg was entrusted by the Ninja to keep artifacts with special powers safe in guarded locations, though it is unknown if he still has the artifacts after the Merge. One such item, Kalmaar's original trident, somehow ended up in the Land of Lost Things before being found by Fritz.
  • Many artifacts serve as collectable items in various sets.
