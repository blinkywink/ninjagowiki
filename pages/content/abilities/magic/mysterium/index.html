Overview

“It's a magical, wonderful place. It was one of the Sixteen Realms, so it must be in the Merged Lands now. The Sorceress loved it there.”
— Bonzle, "The Spell at the Waterfall"

Mysterium was one of the realms before the Merge and is now part of the Merged Lands. It is known for its magical properties and is home to sorcerers and a variety of mystical creatures.

History

History[]

Background[]

The realm came into existence at an unknown point in time. It was known to be a land of magic and wonder, home to various sorcerers and sorceresses, as well as a variety of mystical creatures. At some point the First Spinjitzu Master would visit the realm.[4] Millennia ago, the purple half of the Arc Dragon of Focus was put into slumber in the Cave of Bells in Mysterium following Thunderfang's defeat.[5] Centuries ago, five Elemental Masters known as the Forbidden Five began to get overconfident and veered off onto a dark path. The Dragon Masters visited the high sorceress of the realm, Gandalaria, and sought her assistance to banish the Five forever.[6] At some point, when cyclops twins Torex and Xerot got angry, the wizards ended up in a hundred-year war. In the year 45 AL, there was a shrinking incident in Mysterium.[2]

Between Seasons[]

During a clashing of realms known as the Merge, Mysterium was merged, with its location currently unknown.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Mysterium's name rhymes with another realm, Imperium.
    • The realm's name rhyming with another realm is similar to Ninjago rhyming with Djinjago.
  • It is unknown if the magic that appears in Ninjago is connected to Mysterium's.
  • Mysterium shares a name with a city in the middle-grade book series, Keeper of the Lost Cities.
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, there were Elemental Masters originating from Mysterium, even prior to the Merge.[11]
  • Mysterium uses its own calendar system.
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, the Pontifex Magicus did not create Mysterium, nor the First Spinjitzu Master, nor the Source Dragons.[12]
