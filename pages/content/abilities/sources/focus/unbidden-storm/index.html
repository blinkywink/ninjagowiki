Overview

“Then, once they're awake, the Unbidden Storm must be brought to the place where the Arc Dragon is to be summoned. In this case, as close to Thunderfang's source of power as possible.”
— Bleckt to the ninja, "The Vault of Focus"

The Unbidden Storm is a stone capable of summoning and controlling the Arc Dragon of Focus because of its high concentration of soul energy.[1]

Lloyd and Arin used the Unbidden Storm when both halves of the Focus Dragon were active. They activated it in the Well of the Lost using the Source-Henge, taking advantage of the immense energy contained within the healing elixir, which reacted directly to the soul energy.

History

History[]

Background[]

The Unbidden Storm was created as a method for mortals to summon and control the Arc Dragon of Focus. The stone was placed in the Vault of Focus, a vault made of Prismatic Steel.

The Vault of Focus[]

Bleckt showed the ninja to the Vault of Focus, where he also gave them the Unbidden Storm and instructed them to take it close to Thunderfang's source of power, which this time is in the Spectral Lands, at the Well of the Lost.

For Whom the Bell Tolls[]

Arin and Lloyd went through the Monastery of Gates where Arin held the Unbidden Storm.

When Doves Cry[]

Lloyd and Arin traveled in their Prismatic Ore-coated mechs to the Well of the Lost. However, the two were attacked by Spectral Dragonians led by Nokt, Rox, and Drix. After Lloyd threw Drix into Rox, Nokt noticed the Unbidden Storm and attempted to grab it, almost succeeding in overpowering Arin's Spinjitzu Battle Mech until Lloyd pinned the wolf ghost with his Jet Mech. After Lloyd remembered Bleckt’s words that there was a cavern in the Spectral Lands, he punched a hole in the ground. As the two continued to go to the center of the well, where they found the healing elixir, the Unbidden Storm lost power.

Chaos Reigns[]

With the Unbidden Storm depleted, Lloyd attempted to reactivate it using Energy but was unsuccessful. Getting a new idea, Arin performed Object Spinjitzu, channeling his Spinjitzu power into the Unbidden Storm, which revitalized it. They then used the restored Unbidden Storm to summon and control the Arc Dragon of Focus.

It is unknown what became of the Unbidden Storm since the two of them didn't have it in their possession after Thunderfang's defeat, though it is presumed that they left the stone in the Spectral Lands along with their mechs.

Powers and abilities

Powers and abilities[]

  • Fusion/Combination - When both halves of the Arc Dragon of Focus are awake, the Unbidden Storm can join them into its true two-headed form.
    • Summoning - Afterwards, the Unbidden Storm can summon the Arc Dragon of Focus where the user wants to summon it.
  • Control Inducement - The Unbidden Storm can allow the user to control the Arc Dragon of Focus.
