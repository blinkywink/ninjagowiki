Overview

“Those Energy Cores are created from Source Dragons. We have a Source Dragon imprisoned under my city! Are you telling me we don't have the power to finish this weapon?”
— Beatrix to Dr. LaRow, "Absolute Power"

The Imperium Dragon Core was a core made by Jordana with the Dragon power of the Source Dragon of Energy to replace the pink Dragon Core, which was needed to finish the MergeQuake weapon.

History

History[]

We Are All Dragons[]

Beatrix stopped the ninja from advancing to the Advanced Systems Lab showing her new MergeQuake weapon with Jordana's substitute core.

The Power Within[]

With Sora achieving her True Potential, she destroyed the MergeQuake weapon, which also destroyed the substitute core.

Other appearances and mentions

Other appearances and mentions[]

Absolute Power[]

After Rapton's Dragon Core turned out to be a fake, Dr. LaRow declared that activating the MergeQuake weapon was impossible without three authentic Dragon Cores. However, Beatrix argued that they could create a genuine Dragon Core by extracting energy from the Source Dragon of Energy, since they had all the necessary requirements. Dr. LaRow refused to comply with the order due to the difficulty and potential danger of extracting energy from the Source Dragon, which could potentially destroy Imperium. Nevertheless, Jordana offered to create the fourth Dragon Core for Beatrix.
