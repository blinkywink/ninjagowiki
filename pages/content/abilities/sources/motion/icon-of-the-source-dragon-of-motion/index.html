Overview

“With this, you have the right to summon me to the mortal world... once. Use it only when you truly need me.”
— The Source Dragon of Motion to Lloyd, "Elements of Betrayal"

The Icon of the Source Dragon of Motion, also known as the Vibro Dagger,[1][2] is a Source Dragon Icon in the form of a dagger. Hence its name, it's the Dragon Icon designed to summon the Source Dragon of Motion. It was given to Lloyd by the aforementioned Source Dragon after the ninja returned from the Tournament of the Sources.

History

History[]

Elements of Betrayal[]

When the Source Dragon of Motion visited Lloyd after the Tournament of the Sources, she gifted him her own scale transformed into a Dragon Icon so he could summon her into the mortal world in a case of need.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Summoning Source Dragons - When used, this Dragon Icon has the ability to summon the Source Dragon of Motion to the mortal world.
  • Weapon - This Dragon Icon is also capable of being used as a regular weapon.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Single use - Since it's a Dragon Icon, it can only summon a Source Dragon once.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Icon of the Source Dragon of Motion is a red dagger or a short sword, which are used similarly to the katanas and swords used by Kai, the ninja of Fire, one of the Elemental Powers of the Motion Source Dragon.
  • The icon's color scheme closely resembles that of the Reveal Blades, featuring a red blade paired with a golden hilt.
