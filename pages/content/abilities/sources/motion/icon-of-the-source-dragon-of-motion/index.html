Overview

“With this, you have the right to summon me to the mortal world... once. Use it only when you truly need me.”
— The Source Dragon of Motion to Lloyd, "Elements of Betrayal"

The Icon of the Source Dragon of Motion is a Source Dragon Icon in the form of a dagger. Hence its name, it's the Dragon Icon designed to summon the Source Dragon of Motion.

History

History[]

Elements of Betrayal[]

When the Source Dragon of Motion visited Lloyd after the Tournament of the Sources, she created the Icon of the Source Dragon of Motion by transforming one of her scales into it and gifted it to him so he could summon her into the mortal world if needed.

At some point, Lloyd hid it in a pillar of Wu's study alongside the Realm medallion.[1]

When Doves Cry[]

When Ras invaded the Monastery of Spinjitzu, he stole the Icon along with the Realm medallion after forcing Alfonzo Frohicky to lead him to Wu's study.[1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In the Ninjago Magazine the icon was referred to as the "Vibro Dagger."[2][3]
  • The Icon of the Source Dragon of Motion is a red dagger, which are used similarly to the katanas and swords used by Kai, the ninja of Fire, one of the Elemental Powers of the Source Dragon of Motion.
  • The icon's color scheme closely resembles that of the Reveal Blades, as they are both red blades paired with a golden hilt. The only difference is that the Reveal Blades are swords while the icon is a dagger.
