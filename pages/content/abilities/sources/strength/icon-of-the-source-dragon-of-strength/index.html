Overview

“But Imperium was not ready to contain two dragons, hence why I've been carrying the Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength with me all this time.”
— Ras to Arin, "Fallen Wishes"

The Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength is a Source Dragon Icon in the form of a golden warhammer that is held by Ras. Hence its name, it's the Dragon Icon designed to summon the Source Dragon of Strength. It was confiscated from Ras when he was incarcerated by Empress Beatrix for failing to stop the ninja. When Ras escaped from Imperium, he got the war hammer back and continued to use it as his weapon of choice.

History

History[]

Background[]

At some point prior to the Merge, Ras obtained the Icons of the Source Dragons of Strength and Energy. He successfully summoned the Source Dragon of Energy using one of these Icons, effectively imprisoning him beneath the Kingdom of Imperium. However, Ras faced a limitation; the Imperians were unable to contain two Source Dragons simultaneously, which prevented him from summoning the Source Dragon of Strength. Consequently, he carried the Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength with him starting using it as his primary weapon.[1]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

Ras arrived at the Crossroads with the Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength, striking the ground with it as he created a shockwave. He then threw the hammer at the SA Mark 01, breaking it apart.

Crossroads Carnival[]

Ras had the hammer on his back when he recruited Dorama.

Mindless Beasts[]

Ras had the hammer on his back when he was at the Advanced Systems Lab.

The Calm Inside[]

Ras used his hammer to battle the escaped Dragons. He manage to use it to restrain several of them and later used it to battle Sora and Arin before accidentally releasing Riyu.

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

Ras used his hammer while facing Arin and Sora to prevent them from freeing Riyu, but was defeated. After the battle, Beatrix has Ras stripped of his position with Rapton confiscating his hammer.

Prior to The Forest of Spirits[]

At some point, Ras took the hammer back from Imperium, presumably after escaping.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Forest of Spirits[]

Ras had his hammer when he went after the spell for the Ritual of the Blood Moon. Catching up to Arin, Ras wielded his hammer to struck the Off-Road Buggy Car, knock Riyu aside, and knock Arin to the ground.

Rising Ninja[]

Ras used his hammer to fight against Lloyd who unexpectedly attacked him but Ras disrupted Lloyd's Spinjitzu, with a strong strike that cause him to stumble and then threw his hammer to knock Kai into the Nether-Space, which led to the liberation of Nokt. Subsequently, Nokt defeated Nya by using Shatterspin. Ras attempted to imprison Nya in the Nether-Space using his hammer. But Arin and Sora intervened, and Ras was struck by a hand from the Elemental Fire Mech.

The Shape of Motion[]

When Ras entered in the Shadow Dojo with Jordana he had his Hammer in his back.

The Final Game[]

When Nokt won the Tournament of Sources, Ras had his hammer in his back. When Nokt revealed his betrayal, Ras armed himself with it to threaten him.

Elements of Betrayal[]

Ras used his hammer to fight off several Wolf warriors corrupted by Rox. When Ras flew with Roby, he sued the hammer to launch the hoverpad at Nokt. When Nokt relented, Ras later used it to restrain Nokt before he used Heat to knock back Ras. When Ras recovered, he used the hammer to fight Nokt alongside the Ninja before he was knocked back. Nokt briefly used it to take out several of the Ninja. Although Ras manage to get it back and knock back Nokt as he was doing Shatterspin. As the Forbidden Five were retreating, Ras attempted to stop Nokt, who knocks out the former and drags him along before Arin picked up the hammer and went after them. Arin had the hammer by his side as he began nursing Ras back to health.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Ultimate Object of Admiration[]

The hammer was with Ras when he was waiting for Arin at their campsite. Later as Arin carried Ras in his makeshift sled, he carried the hammer on his back.

The Spectral Lands[]

While training Arin, Ras had his hammer besides him. When Arin went to the border of the Spectral Lands, he carried the hammer on his back. Arriving at the boarder of the Spectral Lands, Arin left the hammer with Ras and took the Returning Blade. When Ras heard the Soul Suckers and saw Arin drop the healing elixir bottle, Ras left his hammer with his sled.

I Alone Can Save Them[]

After the elixir healed his injuries, Ras once again carried his hammer on his back when they got to the Super Storm Jet, which was hidden in the First Land deserts. Ras had the hammer with him in the backseat and later on his back when the two got to the Land of Lee.

Fallen Wishes[]

Ras carried the hammer as he oversaw Arin's training on the coasts of the Land of Lee. As he spoke about his plans to capture the Source Dragons, Ras revealed that the hammer was the Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength.

Their Draconic Majesty's Request[]

Ras carried the hammer with him into the World Forest. Most notably, after ejecting out of the damaged Super Storm Jet, he used it to defeat a bounty hunter who had been pursuing him, knocking him from his flying armor.

Later at the Temple of the Dragon Cores, he used its powers to inflict pain upon the Spirit of the Temple as he demanded information on a way to forge Dragon Icons without Elemental Powers.

Crashing Together[]

Ras carried the hammer on his back when the Ninja found him and Arin. After Arin freed him, Ras attempt to use the hammer to help Arin fight off the bounty hunter but ended up fighting the Dragonian Trackers. After defeating the Dragonian Warriors, Ras was ambush by Drix who knocked the hammer out of his hand. When Ras charged at Drix, Drix kick the hammer up and used it to slam Ras into several trees. As Drix claimed the Returning Blade, he gave the hammer to one of the Dragonian Trackers.

Chaos Unchained[]

As Drix flew back to the First Land, one of the Dragonian Trackers carried the hammer. As Drix flew the Dragonian carried the hammer along with Ras. When Nokt noticed Ras, he grabs the hammer and then a captive Ras. After throwing Ras to the ground, Nokt gave Ras his hammer, refusing to fight someone who was weakened. As Ras got up, picked up his hammer, and clashed with Nokt's sword. Ras managed to hold agaisnt Nokt so Nokt used Shatterspin to take the tiger down before Arin crashed the Dragon-headed drone at Nokt.

The Shatter Dragon[]

As Arin prepared to throw the healing elixir at Thunderfang, Ras arrived using his hammer to keep him standing. After Thunderfang's defeat Ras manage to recover and put his hammer on his back.

The Hollow Ones[]

Ras carried his hammer on his back after his master contacted him.

After arriving at Mysterium, Ras used his hammer in attempt to get in Abracalabria to find Pontifex Magicus. Ras used his hammer to fight of the crystal ball Wizards and Cyclops but found Magicus was turned into a hollow one.

Human Resources[]

Ras had his hammer when he battled Kur and the Spectral Dragonians in Mysterium.

When Ras, Sora, and Arin arrived at the Monastery of Mysterium, he had his hammer on his back and when his master contacted him and gave him his next instructions.

Casket of Bones[]

When Ras fought Arin and Sora at the Casket of Bones to use the Forge of the Forgotten, Ras used his hammer to repel Arin's Object Spinjitzu bone rock.

Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon[]

After activating creating a new Dragon Icon using the Forge of the Forgotten, Ras had his hammer on his back, as his master contacted him on his progress. After contacting his master, several Re-Awakened and Greenbone Warriors, lead by the Greenbone Leader, who accuse him of committing a serious offense by using the Forge of the Forgotten, an act only reserved for the undead and must face death as punishment for his actions. Undeterred and having the two Source Dragon Icons in his possession, Ras readies himself for battle against the undead skeletons.

The Vault of Focus[]

When Ras snuck in the Monastery of Gates, he had his golden hammer Icon and the bone sword Icon on his back.

For Whom the Bell Tolls[]

When Ras walked through the Monastery of Spinjitzu's portal gate, he still carried both his hammer Icon and bone sword Icons when he confronted Geo and Alfonzo Frohicky.

When Doves Cry[]

While battling Geo and Alfonzo, Ras used his hammer Icon. On the training grounds, Alfonzo ordered Ras to leave, Ras pulled out his hammer Icon, which Alfonzo tried to uses his tongue to take it, but Ras easily overpowers him and throws him to the ground. Thanks to Alfonzo triggering the training grounds, Geo manage to get his hands on the hammer and use it against Ras and fuse his bone breastplate to a wooden pole but Ras manage to break out and defeat Geo, disarming him of the hammer before delivering a brutal beatdown. When Rontu arrived, Ras showed her his hammer Icon and bone sword Icon, which surprised Rontu. In the fight, Ras landed two blows from the hammer which left Rontu incapacitated before forcing Alfonzo to take him to Wu's study.

Chaos Reigns[]

After capturing Wu in the protective container, Ras had his hammer Icon in the ground of the Monastery of the Gates while declaring his victory for his master's goals. He later grabbed it and moved forward with his plans.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

Birthright[]

As Ras traveled back to the Shadow Dojo in the Land of Eternal Dawn, he had his hammer Icon on his back along with the bone sword Icon and a bow Icon.

The Summit (flashback)[]

Ras took his hammer Icon with him to the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms when he traveled to it to speak to Zilvar. He drew it after Zilvar angered him and threatened the tiger.

Dangerous Connections[]

Ras had his hammer Icon on his back when he returned to the Land of Eternal Dawn after reobtaining the Icon of the Source Dragon of Energy.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Summoning Source Dragons - When used, the golden hammer has the ability to summon the Source Dragon of Strength to the mortal world.
  • Weapon - This Dragon Icon is also capable of being used as a regular weapon.
  • Enhanced strength - Being a Dragon Icon, the golden hammer is stronger than other weapons. It is also able to knock back a Spinjitzu or Shatterspin tornado as well as knocking down massive dragons. Additionally, the golden hammer appears to channeling the user's strength into hammer strikes that are far greater than a normal hammer.
    • Dynamikinetic Wave Emission - The hammer possesses the power of Strength and can release it as waves with a slam or an impact.[2]
  • Enhanced durability - The hammer is durable than most hammers, as it is able to withstand Elemental Powers like Lightning and Energy. It is also durable to attack Spinjitzu/Shatterspin users.
  • Harming Spirits - It also has power to shock the Spirit of the Temple.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Single use - Since it's a Dragon Icon, it can only summon a Source Dragon once.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In "Ninja vs. Imperium!," the icon was referred to as the "Duel Hammer."
  • The handle of the Dragon Icon in LEGO form was repurposed for the Mallet of Shattering and the Imperial Hammer, which were other hammers used by Ras. Additionally, the color scheme of the golden hammer was also applied to the Imperial Hammer.
  • According to the box art of 71813 Wolf Mask Shadow Dojo, the hammer was originally supposed to be remade to match the Wolf Clan's color scheme and Ras would have used it as his main weapon in the sets for Season 2. For unknown reasons, this was changed and Ras was given a new weapon that he doesn't use in the season.
    • In the sets for Part 2 of Season 2, Ras uses the redesigned Mallet of Shattering, although he still uses his hammer as a weapon of choice in the show.
  • The Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength is a golden hammer and this type of weapon is used by Cole, the ninja of Earth, one of the Elemental Powers of the Strength Source Dragon.
  • Despite first appearing in "The Merge: Part 1", it was not revealed for 46 episodes until "Fallen Wishes" that Ras' golden hammer was a Source Dragon Icon.
  • It is currently unknown how or when Ras made the prior Icons of the Source Dragon of Energy and Strength. However, it is possible that these were made using Elemental Powers, as Ras was unaware of other methods for creating Dragon Icons until "Their Draconic Majesty's Request."
