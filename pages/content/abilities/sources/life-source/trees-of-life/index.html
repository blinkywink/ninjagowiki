The Trees of Life[1] are ancient trees in the Whispering Wood, acting as reservoirs for Life energy.

History

History[]

The Trees of Life are ancient trees from the Whispering Wood, created at the dawn of the realms to channel vital energy and send it throughout all the Realms of Creation, thus allowing life to continue. To prevent this energy from being disrupted, the Ritual of Unrooting is performed to renew the life force.

The Whispering Wood[]

During the ninja's fight with the Elemental Monsters, the Trees of Life started whispering and sent Lloyd a spark, allowing him and Euphrasia to use their Elemental Powers again temporarily.

A Wonder of the Merged Lands[]

When the Elemental Monsters were about to attack the ninja, they heard the whispers of the Trees of Life. Suddenly, a spark from the trees hit Lloyd again, allowing him, Zane, and Euphrasia to use their Elemental Powers again.
