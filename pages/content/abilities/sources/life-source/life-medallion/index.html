The Life medallion is an ornament worn by the Arc Dragon of Life, acting as a reservoir for Life energy.

History

History[]

Birthright[]

After all the Elemental Monsters were recalled to the Weapon of Desolation following Beatrix's defeat, the Life energy of the Whispering Wood returned to the medallion of the Arc Dragon of Life who regained its life force, and restored the energy to the Wood including the Acona.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Vivokinesis - The medallion contains Life energy, allowing its owner to manipulate its essence.
    • Omnidirectional Vivokinetic Waves - The medallion can release omnidirectional waves of Life Energy.
