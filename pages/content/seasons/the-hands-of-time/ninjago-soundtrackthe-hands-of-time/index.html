The following is a list of all the themes and soundtracks first introduced in the seventh season of Ninjago.

Hands of Time theme

Hands of Time theme[]

The Hands of Time theme serves as the theme for the Time Twins, Krux and Acronix. The theme has an ancient vibe, as Krux and Acronix are both ancient Elemental Masters; it also gives an intimidating and threatening vibe to the characters.

Various instruments are used to play the theme. Often, strings are used to play the theme. In addition, to build a connection to their element of Time, Jay and Michael incorporated the sounds of chimes and ticking clocks into the theme, which can be heard in "Acronix Meets the Ninja." The tracks "Hands of Time Reunited" and "Meet the Parents" both exemplify the Hands of Time theme.

Kai and Nya family theme

Kai and Nya family theme[]

The Kai and Nya family theme serves as a theme for their family, as the season focuses on them trying to find their parents, Ray and Maya. The theme is often played with strings and horns, and can evoke either a solemn or uplifting feeling. The theme is exemplified by the tracks "Meet the Parents" and "The Fusion Dragon."

It is later reprised in the March of the Oni finale, "Endings," with "No Ordinary Blacksmith", during Kai and Nya's conversation, and in "Reforging The Golden Weapons", when Kai forges the Golden Weapons.

The theme is featured prominently in Seabound, specifically in scenes focusing on Nya and Maya's relationship.

Vermillion theme

Vermillion theme[]

The Vermillion theme serves as a theme for the Vermillion army. The theme uses low notes and evokes an ominous feeling. It is exemplified by the tracks "The First Vermillion Warrior" and "The Vermillion Commanders."
