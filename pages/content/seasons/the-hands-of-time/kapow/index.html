Kapow! is the 22nd issue of the Ninjago Magazine. It includes 891722 Cole and Honor Kai appears on the cover. It was released on March 1, 2017 in Germany.

The Clean-up!

The Clean-up![]

The ninja were relaxing in the Temple of Airjitzu when Misako came with Ronin and Dareth, who helped her to carry some old stuff to the temple. After discussing the recent events that happened during the Day of the Departed the ninja decided to go to the Ninjago Museum of History and help Dr. Saunders to clean up a mess that happened there at that night. While the ninja went to the city, Wu stopped Lloyd and told him that he will soon fight with his old enemy, and he should fight alone.

When the ninja were going through the harbor, the civilians were suddenly attacked by a Leviathan, who was partially affected by the Dark Matter and entangled in chains of an old crane. When Lloyd got to the scene, the ninja were already in a fight.

After a discussion about how Clouse's actions on the Dark Island could be the reason why the creature is attacking, Cole made a hammer out of the parts of a crane and freed Leviathan from its chains, after which it willingly returned to the sea.

After the battle with the creature, the ninja realized that now they also need to clear up the harbor, so they need to call Misako and say that they will be late for dinner.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • While the comic does not acknowledge the events of Pythor's Revenge and tells its own story about how the ninja decided to help Dr. Saunders to clean up the museum, it can still be put between Chapter 9 and Chapter 10 of the book, where the transition allows space for this event to happen.
  • Similar to "Sail Ahoy!", the events of the Dark Island Trilogy are mentioned in the comic.

Errors[]

  • Leviathan is drawn like a giant octopus instead of a squid, and doesn't have its eyes on its tentacles.
