The unnamed void is a place beyond the Ethereal Divide occupied by the Source Dragons.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

When Lloyd and Zane went to discover the energy source that was powering up the Kingdom of Imperium, they found the imprisoned Source Dragon of Energy in a Glass core. After a brief conversation between the three, the Source Dragon of Energy gave some of his energy to Lloyd, sending him in a flow of different locations, one of which was the void of the Source Dragons.

The Power Within[]

After Beatrix's defeat, when Lloyd attempted to stabilize the three Dragon Cores, he taps into the energy previously given by the Source Dragon of Energy, Lloyd briefly enters the Source Dragon's void to find calm within himself to be able to stabilize the Dragon cores.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

Back to the Monastery of Spinjitzu, after the Ninja had been defeat by Cinder who used Shatterspin at the Cloud Kingdom, Lloyd stayed in the courtyard of the Monastery for the entire day, trying to communicate with the Source Dragons and seek their help on how to counter Shatterspin. After waiting for several hours outside, Lloyd, tired and exhausted, yelled out for their help, then seven little glowing dragons approached him and flew into his body. Lloyd then woke up in the Source Dragons' void, where all of the Source Dragons were discussing whether to help him or not. After the Source Dragon of Energy implored them to aid Lloyd, like he had helped him a year prior, and after a brief discussion, they agreed and the Source Dragon of Motion gave him a vision on how to counter Shatterspin.

Clues and Suspects[]

After being unfairly attacked from behind by Zeatrix and now unconscious, Lloyd wakes up in a void similar to his Visions. As he is trying to find a way out, he is surrounded by several Wolf Mask Claw Warriors accompanied by Zeatrix and Arin, who all seem ready to attack him. He is then attacked by Zeatrix again, the same way she caused him to be in this coma; by striking him with a sword. As she was about to do it again, the Source Dragon of Motion appears, and takes out most of the wolf warriors before roaring fire in anger at Zeatrix, Arin and other remaining wolf warriors. She then shoots fire at the Blood Moon and the void in which Lloyd was has now turned into the Source Dragons' void.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Casket of Bones[]

While Lloyd was trapped in a cage in the Land of Lee, he experienced a mental attack, similar to the visions he often had. He then woke up in the Source Dragons' void, where he found the source dragons engaged in an argument about what to do about Thunderfang. The Source Dragons' symbols had been illuminated, except for the Source Dragon of Balance's symbol, which was noticeably dull and dark, due to the Source Dragon of Balance being deceased. When they were debating whether to awaken the Arc Dragon of Focus, the Source Dragon of Strength had noticed Lloyd, which resulted in them forcing him out of the void.

Chaos Reigns[]

Following the Battle of the Spectral Lands, the Source Dragons of Energy, Motion, and Flow discussed the aftermath of the battle and the resignations of Focus, Life, and Strength from the Council. As their conversation shifted towards the consequences of their siblings' resignations, the remaining three members of the Council commented how the universe was now in grave danger, potentially facing the end of all cycles.
