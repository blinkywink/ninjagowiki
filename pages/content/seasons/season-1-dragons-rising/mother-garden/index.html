The Mother Garden is a garden in one of the realms. It is inhabited by the Mucoids and their leader Zant-Tanz, who were the guardians and protectors of the Pink Dragon Core.

History

History[]

They Call It Doom[]

Aboard the Destiny's Bounty, Arin discovered him and Lloyd were at the garden. They stopped and started to explore it in order to find the Pink Dragon Core, and discovered trees with flowers that chime like bells and bushes with flowers that blow bubbles. They came across the Core with shells surrounding the alter. Arin intended to take it, but Lloyd stopped him and wondered if it's to be taken upon asking someone. The shells rose up and revealed Mucoids, and their leader, Zant-Tanz. He congratulated the two ninja on passing their test of greed. He then mentioned the last person who took the test had failed and was currently in custody, to which Arin and Lloyd were guided to. They discovered it was Rapton, who demanded for his release.

Lloyd and Arin were told that Rapton was to be executed for failing the test. Lloyd, after some reluctance, asked for any way to release the Rapton. He learned from Zant-Tanz of another test which was to cross the Impossible Path, which he accepted. They headed to the Impossible Path, Arin and Zant-Tanz watched Lloyd enter it, and met up with him at the end. Lloyd was completely unharmed which surprised Zant-Tanz. He told Lloyd the path was laced with salt, which is deadly to Mucoids. Upon learning from Arin that salt is used for cooking, Zant-Tanz fainted before he woke up a few minutes later. Zant-Tanz released Rapton, who double crossed Lloyd and Arin, and took the core, only to end up incapacitated by two agents. The agents took the core and entered the portal they had arrived in before Lloyd and Arin went into it to chase them down.

The Administration[]

Lloyd, Arin, and Zane, in the Master Mech, the Dragon Mech, and the Spinjitzu Race Car respectively, go through the portal from the Administration and land back at the Mother Garden, knocking over Zant-Tanz's tower of rocks he was trying to balance. Zane apologizes for knocking over the rocks, but Zant-Tanz says it just means he will get to enjoy doing it over again. Then the trio leave the garden on the Destiny's Bounty and head back to the Monastery.
