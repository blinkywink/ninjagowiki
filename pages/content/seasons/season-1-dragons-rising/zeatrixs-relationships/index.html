These are the relationships of Zeatrix, the former Princess of Imperium.

Family

Family[]

Levo[]

“You will be a wonderful Empress of Imperium someday.”
— Levo to Zeatrix, "Absolute Power"

Zeatrix had a good relationships with her father. As a first-born, Zeatrix inherited the elemental power of her father and had all of Levo's attention.

Beatrix[]

Zeatrix does not have a very good relationship with her twin sister. Ever since Beatrix exiled her from Imperium, she has hated her ever since. She wanted to take revenge on her for the murder of her father and for having exiled her.

During Operation Mentax, the Loyalists swapped Zeatrix's mind in Beatrix body with the Mind Crystal thereby plunging her into a coma. When the young ninja swapped the minds back, Zeatrix declared that she hated her sister.

Allies

Allies[]

Fedulian[]

Zeatrix and Fedulian are allies. Zeatrix asked Fedulian to pass a message to Rogue. During the preparation for the Summit of the Merged Lands, Zeatrix asked Fedulian the informations about the Loyalists.

Ninja (former enemies)[]

Zeatrix meets the Ninja in the Tournament of the Sources. She was very angry with them for dethroning her sister and stealing her revenge. Zeatrix wanted revenge on the ninjas and injured Lloyd. But after the Tournament, her desire for revenge against them has faded away.

Weeks after the Loyalist assault, she helps the New Ninja to protect Ninjago City during the Summit of the Merged Lands against Beatrix and the Loyalists and makes it clear that she is not like her sister. The New Ninja and she are fighting the Loyalists and she becomes a new ally.

Euphrasia[]

Zeatrix met Euphrasia in the Tournament of the Sources. They found themeselves face to face in a duel. Zeatrix won the duel and obtained Euphrasia's elemental power.

During the Summit of the Merged Lands, Zeatrix help Euphrasia and the rest of the New Ninja to protect Ninjago City.

Enemies

Enemies[]

Ras[]

“See how I handled that monk? That's nothing compared to the destiny I'm gonna write for you, Ras.”
— Zeatrix to Ras, "Truth and Lies"

Zeatrix mets Ras when he has come in Imperium to propose his help for capture Source Dragons. Levo refused and ordered Zeatrix to eject Ras with her elemental power from the throne room. Ras allied himself with Beatrix and killed Levo and exiled Zeatrix from Imperium.

After the Merge, in the Tournament of the Sources Zeatrix who hated Ras because of their shared past, was reunited with him and wants revange.

Several week later, Zeatrix was still angered by Ras and wanted him dead for what he did to her and her father, so she hired a bounty hunter for track Ras but he failed. Zeatrix suspected that Ras was merely a pawn in her sister's plan.
