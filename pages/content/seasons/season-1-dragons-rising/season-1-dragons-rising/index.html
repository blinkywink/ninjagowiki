The New Heroes The Elemental Mechs

The first season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising is preceded by Season 15 of Ninjago, and succeeded by The Elemental Mechs and Season 2. Arin and Jiro serve as the mascot characters, Empress Beatrix serves as the main antagonist, and the Claws of Imperium serve as the villainous faction of the season.

A teaser for this season was released on April 1, 2023. On April 11, it was announced that the new season would release on June 1, 2023 in the United States on Netflix. On April 26, a teaser trailer was released on LEGO's YouTube channel. LEGO released the first two episodes of the season on May 22, 2023 in German.

Okoo, a French children's television network, had released two Dragons Rising commercials during April and May 2023. Okoo also had the first three episodes of the series premiere on May 24 at the Paris image forum theater. Episode 1-10 were released on Netflix on June 1. A trailer for the second half of the season aired on July 20 at San Diego Comic-Con 2023, which released to the public on September 5, and it was announced that the second half would be released on October 12.

Episodes 11-15 were released on Disney+ Hotstar on September 27, but were taken down by the next day. Episode 11-20 were released on Netflix on October 12.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • According to Kevin Burke, this season's theme is "change - fear of change, and the importance of change".[34]
  • With the season having 20 episodes and each being 22 minutes long (440 minutes), this, as well as the other seasons of the series, are the longest seasons in the Ninjago franchise by runtime to date, surpassing Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu and Crystalized.
    • This is also the first season since March of the Oni to have 22-minute episodes and the first season animated by WildBrain to have 22-minute episodes.
  • On April 1, 2023, writers Chris "Doc" Wyatt and Kevin Burke each tweeted out a sneak peak at the season in the form of two truths and a lie.
    • Wyatt's three statements were:[35]
      • The Cloud Kingdom will see the rise of a new Elemental Master.
      • The Realm of Madness will defend itself against dangerous dragons.
      • The Departed Realm will release the spirits of ancient creatures forgotten by history.
        • The last statement is a lie: Euphrasia, a Cloud Kingdom resident and monk is revealed to have become the new Elemental Master of Wind, while the ninja help the Craglings of the Realm of Madness protect their village from dragons while searching for Kai.
    • Burke's three statements were:[36]
      • A half-man, half-frog will accept an alternative form of payment.
      • One ninja's golden shield will unlock an ancient crypt of snakes.
      • To be polite, ninja will eat actual mud.
        • The second statement is the lie, as Arin uses a pie as an alternative payment in "The Merge: Part 1," and Lloyd, Kai, Nya, Arin, and Sora eat Cragling mud in "Beyond Madness."
    • This was done in honor of Tommy Andreasen's tradition of announcing details about upcoming seasons in this manner.
  • The designs of some of the characters are slightly different from the designs in the previous series.
    • Master Wu no longer has his braid on the back of his head and no sideburns either.
      • This was done to more closely resemble his Ninjago: Core minifigure,[37] although in the trailer Wu still lacks the cheekbones he has on the minifigure and has had in all previous seasons.
    • Nya's eyebrows are thicker, her hair is darker and since the fifth episode her two side hair strands are shorter for an animation reason: the new shoulder armor piece of the merge suits do not fit with Nya's hair piece so it had to been shortened to fit.
    • Similarly, Lloyd's hair was also slightly shortened to fit his merge suit as well.
      • Interestingly enough, Cole's hair in his merge suit doesn't appear to have been altered like Lloyd and Nya's hair.
  • Jay's voice is noticeably lower pitched compared to the previous WildBrain seasons since Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu.
    • In addition, Jay is the only member of the Ninja team to not use his powers in this season, flashback or present day.
  • This is the first season since The Hands of Time to introduce a new elemental power, in this case, Technology, Heat, Fusion, and Shockwave are introduced.
  • Unlike the seasons of the original series, this season does not have a title.[38][39]
  • Episodes 11-15 were put up early on Disney+ Hotstar before being taken down.
  • This is the only season to have an audio description option.
  • The events of the first half of the season is similar to Hunted, as it involves the ninja being separated due to a mishap involving realms, and then having to save dragons from a dragon hunting regime.
    • Due to the similarities, this season reuses many of Hunted's soundtracks.
  • The season was written in 2021.[40]
  • So far, this is the only season of Dragons Rising not to include three wave sets, where each wave typically highlights a specific story arc within the season.
  • In 2024, the episode "Land of Lost Things" won the Leo Award for Best Direction in an Animation Series, while the season was nominated for 3 other Leo Awards, including Best Animation Series.[41]
  • Starting from this season, all of Cole's audio, including voice lines and action sounds, was done by Andrew Francis as Season 15: Crystalized still used stock audio from Kirby Morrow for some of Cole's action sounds like grunting.

Focus Characters[]

Protagonists[]

  • Sora has the most important role in this season because of her personal relationship with Imperium.
    • Sora was focused on in Episodes 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 14, 16, 19, and 20.
      • She, along with Arin, participate in the Mech Master 5000 and used the element of Technology for the first time in Episode 1.
      • She trains with Lloyd, while having difficulty, and then later sneaks out to the Crossroads Carnival with Arin in Episode 3.
      • She doubts her position as a ninja after a little mishap during the battle with the Earth Dragons in Episode 4.
      • She tells the other ninja about her creating the Photac back in Imperium and then infiltrates it with Lloyd, Arin and Riyu to shut it off in Episode 6.
      • She encounters Dr. LaRow in the Advanced Systems Lab and then reluctantly activates more Photacs in Episode 7.
      • She and Nya get cursed while searching for the Yellow Dragon Core, then encounter Arrakore and fight off the Howlers in Episode 14.
      • She and Nya run into Cole in the Land of Lost Things and then she tries to find a new power for the machine in Episode 16.
      • She broadcasts a message in Imperium about Beatrix’s mistreatment of the Imperians and Dragons and that they should rebel against her in Episode 19.
      • She runs into her parents who refuse to believe the truth about Empress Beatrix and then she unlocks her True Potential after realizing that the ninja are her real family in Episode 20.
  • Arin is a prominent character in this season.
    • Arin was focused on in Episodes 1, 3, 7, 12, 17, and 20.
      • He participates with Sora in the Mech Master 5000 and fights Ras in Episode 1.
      • He starts his training with Lloyd, and later sneaks out the Crossroads Carnival with Sora in Episode 3.
      • He befriends the Imperium Teen Resistance Force and then learns that they want to join the Claws of Imperium and hunt down Dragons in Episode 7.
      • He and Lloyd help the farmers take back their village that was taken over by the Ocean gang who were being mind controlled by the Crab monster.
      • He and Lloyd end up in The Administration where they find Zane and regain the Dragon Core in Episode 17.
      • He transferred Spinjitzu to an object that destroyed a part of Beatrix’s mech and pushed her into a MergeQuake in Episode 20.
  • Lloyd is the most prominent of the original six ninja this season.
    • Lloyd was focused on in Episodes 2, 9, 10, 12, 15, and 20.
      • In Episode 2, he rescues Arin and Sora and then in the Land of the Three Mountains, he gets powered up by the Matriarch of the Mountain Dragons. Then he decides to train Arin and Sora.
      • In Episode 9, he remembers Wu’s advice of finding the calm inside during his training with him as a child.
      • In Episode 10, he gains power from the Source Dragon to power the Portal gate and then comforts Arin about his fear of being alone again.
      • In Episode 12, he trains Arin for him to do Object Spinjitzu and then they help the farmers get back their village.
      • In Episode 15, he and Arin find the Dragon Core, then find Rapton. He then walks into the Impossible Path, and he and Arin go after two agents of the Administration who took the core.
      • In Episode 20, he activates the three Dragon Cores to stop the MergeQuakes and stabilized the world. Then, he uses them to free the Source Dragon from Imperium.
  • Kai and Nya are this season's rostered secondary ninja.
    • Kai was focused on in Episodes 4, 13, 16, and 17.
      • In Episode 4, during a flashback, he came across the Land of Madness, help the Craglings fight against the Earth Dragons, and he was thought dead. But he’s revealed to be alive and reunited with Lloyd and Nya.
      • In Episode 13, he and Wyldfyre travel to the Wyldness to get the Purple Dragon Core as well as cleaning the village of the Lava-Tides because of Wyldfyre’s pranks.
      • In Episode 16, he and Wyldfyre try to heal Heatwave.
      • In Episode 17, he takes Wyldfyre under his wing and teaches her patience. Then they found out that the Core they took was a fake created by Dorama.
    • Nya was focused on in Episodes 4, 14, and 16.
      • In Episode 4, she encounters Lloyd, Arin and Sora at the Land of Madness, tells them about how she ended up there and about Kai’s whereabouts.
      • In Episode 14, she and Sora get cursed while searching for the Yellow Dragon Core and they encounter Arrakore, the last djinn of Djinnjago.
      • In Episode 16, she and Sora run into Cole in the Land of Lost Things.
  • Zane plays a secondary role in this season.
    • He is focused on in Episodes 13 and 14.
      • In Episode 13, he enters a competition during Zane Day to gain the wyconium coil, but loses. Despite this, he gets a gift card for it from Chancellor Gulch.
      • In Episode 14, he’s studying the Portal Gate, then he encourages Mr. Frohicky to not give up on being the assistant of the Monastery.
  • Riyu and Wyldfyre are major characters this season.
    • Riyu is focused on in Episodes 1-2, 4, 7-10, and 20.
    • Wyldfyre is focused on in Episodes 7-8, 13, 16, and 17.
  • Jay, Cole and Wu have relatively minor roles this season.
    • Outside of the prologue, Jay appears only as a cameo in Episode 17.
    • Cole is focused on in Episode 16.
    • Wu is focused on in a flashback in Episode 9 and makes abstract appearances throughout the season.

Antagonists[]

  • Empress Beatrix serves as the main antagonist of this season.
  • Lord Ras serves as the major antagonist of this season.
  • Dr. Larow, Rapton and Dorama serve as the secondary antagonists of the season.
  • The Claws of Imperium serve as the villainous faction.

Part 1[]

  • Lord Ras serves as the main antagonist of Part 1, leading the Claws of Imperium and playing a major role throughout the story arc. He was focused on in Episodes 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9 and 10.
    • In Episodes 1-2, he orchestrated a plan to capture the Mountain Dragons, easily thwarting the efforts of Arin and Sora when they attempted to intervene. Although he ultimately failed to secure Riyu, he found some success in his scheme when he observed the bond forming between Lloyd and the Matriarch of the Mountain Dragons.
    • In Episodes 3 and 4, under Beatrix's direction, Ras enlisted Dorama to create additional Photacs, which were intended to help Imperium capture all dragons. By Episodes 7 and 8, he resorted to blackmailing Sora, threatening to torture Zanth by draining her energy unless she activated the Photacs, as she was the only one capable of doing so. Once the Photacs were operational, Ras commanded Rapton and the Claw Hunters to pursue the dragons that had escaped.
    • In Episode 9, he learned that Riyu had been recaptured, but he was compelled to obey Beatrix's orders to extract Riyu's energy until the ninja launched an attack on the Imperium Palace. This led to a confrontation with Arin and Sora in a battle for Riyu in Episode 10, but despite his determination, Ras was ultimately defeated and imprisoned due to his failure.
  • Empress Beatrix serves as the overarching antagonist of Part 1, although her role in most is relatively minor. However, she becomes more prominent in Episodes 9 and 10.
    • In Episode 9, Beatrix devises a plan to siphon Riyu's energy with the intention of demoralizing the ninja and ultimately defeating them. Her scheme is interrupted when the ninja attack the Imperium Palace during their battle with the Photacs, disrupting her efforts.
    • In Episode 10, while Ras is preoccupied with confronting Arin and Sora, Beatrix takes charge of the Claws of Imperium. She leads them in a ruthless campaign against the ninja, intending to the Photacs to achieve their destruction. Ultimately, Beatrix shifts her focus to seeking revenge on the ninja, determined to eliminate them for their victory over her, as she decides to take matters into her own hands.
  • Rapton and Dr. LaRow serve as the secondary antagonists.
    • Rapton was focused on in Episodes 1-2 and 6-10.
      • In the initial episodes, Rapton is tasked by Ras with tracking down the Mountain Dragons, particularly Riyu as part of Ras' larger scheme.
      • In Episode 6, Rapton tested with the repaired Photac, which he uses to capture Jiro. In Episode 7, he also captures Sora on behalf of Dr. LaRow, intending to force Sora to activate additional Photacs. During Episodes 8-10, Rapton leads the Claw Hunters in battle against the ninja and the dragons.
      • Episode 10, where he assumes leadership of the Claws of Imperium after Ras is arrested due to his failures.
    • Dr. LaRow was focused on in Episodes 6-10.
  • Other important antagonists include Dorama, Jordana, the tentacled beasts, the Imperium Teen Protection Force, and the Imperium Guards.
    • Dorama was focused on in Episodes 3-4 and 7-8.
    • Jordana was focused on in Episodes 9-10.
      • In Episode 10, it was revealed that Jordana was the envious girl in Sora's class and that she had been determined to outshine Sora in every aspect ever since the science fair.

Part 2[]

  • Empress Beatrix serves as the main antagonist of Part 2. She was focused on in Episodes 11, 12, 18, 19 and 20.
    • In Episodes 11-12, Beatrix discovers about the Dragon Cores, leading her to devise a strategy to annihilate the ninja with them as an act of revenge.
    • In Episode 18, Beatrix becomes determined to use the MergeQuake weapon and decides to seek Ras' help after her first attempt fails. However, Beatrix retaliated to Ras by asserting that, in fact, it was her support that had elevated Ras from insignificance in Imperium.
    • In Episodes 19-20, Beatrix engaged in intense battles against both the ninja and the dragons. As the truth about her mistreatment of the Imperians and dragons was revealed, Beatrix resorted to using the MergeQuake weapon to launch a fierce attack against everyone until she was pushed into a MergeQuake by Arin, resulting in her dethronement.
  • Rapton and Dr. LaRow act as the secondary antagonists.
    • Rapton was focused on in Episodes 11, 15, and 18-20.
    • Dr. LaRow was focused on in Episodes 16 and 18-19.
      • In Episode 16, Dr. LaRow travels to the Land of Lost Things to claim the Yellow Dragon Core. She ultimately snatched the core away successfully from the Ninja and Finders.
      • In Episode 19, Dr. LaRow tried to discourage Jordana from trying to drain the Source Dragon's energy only for it to backfire on her and she was ultimately taken away by Imperium Guards.
  • Lord Ras serves as the overarching antagonist of Part 2. He was focused on in Episodes 18 and 20
    • In Episode 18, it is disclosed that Ras was responsible for Beatrix's rise to power by orchestrating the death of Levo and exiling Zeatrix from the Kingdom of Imperium, all to facilitate Imperium's quest to capture the Source Dragons for their energy and rule the realms. In the present, Ras makes an effort to sway Beatrix into freeing him from his prison, implying that without his guidance, she would merely be a nobody. However, Beatrix counters his taunts by reminding him that his ascent in Imperium was entirely dependent on her, highlighting his reliance on her influence.
    • In Episode 20, Ras manages to escape from prison and successfully convinces Jordana to ally with him, aiming to aid his master in the pursuit of the Source Dragons.
  • Other important antagonists include Dorama, Jordana, the Imperium Guards, and the Administration Agents.
    • Dorama was focused on in Episodes 13, 15, 18 and 19.
      • In Episodes 13 and 15, Dorama travels to Wyldness to reclaim the purple Dragon Core and competed with Kai, Wyldfyre, and Lava-Tides for possession of the artifact. In the end, Dorama manages to outsmart them and pilfer the purple Dragon Core.
    • Jordana was focused on in 18, 19 and 20.
      • In Episodes 18-19, she was given the responsibility of extracting the dragon power from the Source Dragon to produce a new Dragon Core for Beatrix. She successfully accomplished this task, but not without facing opposition from Dr. LaRow.
      • In Episode 20, Jordana encountered Ras and discovered that her ability to drain a Source Dragon would be valuable to Ras' Master's evil scheme of capturing all Source Dragons. Jordana ultimately agreed to join forces with Ras.
