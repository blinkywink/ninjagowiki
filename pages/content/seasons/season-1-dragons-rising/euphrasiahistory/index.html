Overview

Early life

Early life[]

Prior to the Merge, Euphrasia was an ordinary inhabitant of the Cloud Kingdom destined to be a Writer of Destiny. When the Merge occurred, it merged her Kingdom and the other realms into one world. Not long after, she discovered she had gained the Elemental Power of Wind.[1] Fearing exile, she kept her newfound power hidden.[2]

Dragons Rising, Season 1

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Writers of Destiny[]

Euphrasia was with Marcus as a tentacled beast began destroying the tower beneath them. They nearly fell, but the Ninja arrived in time to save them. As Euphrasia was taken back to the Cloud Kingdom, she explained the monster’s origin and the Ice Dragons' presence in the realm.

As the ninja battled the tentacled beast during a MergeQuake, Euphrasia discreetly used her Wind powers to save Sora and Arin from falling, all while pretending to focus on writing a scroll. To her dismay, they noticed and confronted her, but she denied it and walked away. When another MergeQuake struck and released the tentacled beasts again, Euphrasia saw Sora struggling, abandoning her duties to help fight them. Soon after, Lloyd, Kai, Nya, and Arin arrived to assist. Once the beasts were forced back into the rift, Euphrasia helped close it with her powers. Returning to praising writers, Euphrasia confessed to Suetonius that she had hidden her powers and now faced exile. However, Suetonius, who saw that the writers did not foresee the end of the MergeQuake, allowed these events to unfold and asked for her assistance in maintaining the realm. Later, Euphrasia watched as Lloyd, Kai, Arin, Sora, and Riyu left, while Nya remained to look for a solution to prevent future MergeQuakes.

Dragons Rising, Season 2

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Shattered Dreams[]

Euphrasia attempted to stop Ras and the Wolf Clan from taking over the Cloud Kingdom, only to be easily defeated by Cinder, who sent her falling off the floating island. She narrowly managed to slow her descent by using her powers.

Euphrasia crash-landed on the outskirts of Imperium, where Percival Tartigrade found her and took her to the Monastery of Spinjitzu, where she warned the Ninja. The Ninja headed for the Cloud Kingdom to rescue Suetonius, who was encased in a cocoon due to Ras turning the Scroll-Worms against him. He and Euphrasia eventually fled to safety, only to be captured later on.

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

Euphrasia and Suetonius were restrained by handcuffs, unable to move freely. Jordana seized the opportunity to taunt Euphrasia, pointing out the medallion adorning her neck was made of Vengestone, rendering her power useless.

The Forest of Spirits[]

Euphrasia was chosen among other 4 Cloud monks to be sacrificed during the Ritual of the Blood Moon.

Rising Ninja[]

During the Ritual of the Blood Moon, Euphrasia was freed from her chains by Wyldfyre, allowing her to tear off the Vengestone medallion so she can use her powers again, and wanted to join the fight. However, Wyldfyre stopped her as she was to be used by Ras for the ritual.

Later, she noticed that the Cloud Kingdom would crash so she used her wind powers to prevent it from happening.

Enter the City of Temples[]

While flying through the Cloud Kingdom with her Wind power as usual, Euphrasia was overjoyed to receive an invitation to the Tournament of the Sources from two Spirit Dragons.

Later, she arrived at the entrance of the City of Temples, where she inserted her invitation and opened the gate. She is seen riding up the elevator as the Ninja watched from afar.

Truth and Lies[]

In the Tournament, Euphrasia battled Zeatrix on floating platforms. Zeatrix used her elemental power of Shockwave and knocked the young monk off the platform, defeating her. Euphrasia's Wind power was then transferred to Zeatrix by Roby using the Elemental Cup.

The Final Game[]

Euphrasia watched the final round of the Tournament of Sources from the spectator stands. Soon after, she was taken by surprise when Wyldfyre arrived with Zur to expose Bleckt, just after Nokt's victory over Sora.

Elements of Betrayal[]

Euphrasia fought alongside several Elemental Masters, shielding Tox after she was knocked down by an empowered Wolf Mask Warrior. Euphrasia found herself in hand-to-hand combat against Cinder and the rest of the Wolf Mask Warriors. Upon the return of their powers, she knocked Cinder down with a powerful gust of wind before he could use his smoke abilities. Later, she watched as Roby praised the Ninja for their efforts and declared Sora the default winner of the tournament. Afterward, she was thanked by both Sora and Tox.

Just Under the Surface[]

While Lloyd was performing the Ritual of Unrooting, Arin was teaching Euphrasia how to perform Spinjitzu. Just then, the Acona appeared suddenly and urged the trio to help them. Euphrasia wondered if it was okay to leave Lloyd behind, but Zane said Lloyd would want them to help. The Acona lead the group to the Arc Dragon of Life, who was being attacked by Elemental Monsters and starting to decay, along with the Whispering Wood. She used her Wind powers on an Earth Monster, but when confronted by an Ice Monster her powers became unstable for a brief moment. After subduing the Ice Monster with help of the Acona, she was attacked by a Mutation Monster. When Arin used Object Spinjitzu on the Ice Monster, she noticed that it was somewhat effective. It gave Arin an idea on how to make it more effective, so Euphrasia hurled rocks into Arin's Spinjitzu tornado, which allowed Arin to destroy the attacking Elemental Monsters.
