Return of the Photac Beasts! is the 110th issue of the Ninjago Magazine. It includes 892401 Zane and Merge Kai and Cole appear on the cover.

Flying Visit to Imperium!

Flying Visit to Imperium![]

Arin, Sora, and Riyu travel to the City of Imperium in search of materials needed to build the mechs Sora plans to design. There, they meet with Percival, who gives them a tour of the city. He explains that, following the release of the dragons, the city now relies on wind and solar energy. Percival leads them to the former Advanced Systems Lab, where the parts Sora needs are stored.

Meanwhile, Riyu senses something unusual and separates from the group, following a scent despite Arin’s pleas to follow the route. When Arin realizes that Riyu has slipped away, he decides to go after him. Sora and Percival quickly follow. Together, they deduce that Riyu likely picked up the scent of a dragon that was once imprisoned there.

Eventually, they find Riyu captured by Dorama, who reveals his plan to restore the Imperium to its former glory. He activates the Photacs that resemble hamsters. Despite their initially cute appearance, the hamsters turn hostile and attack the group. Arin grabs his Grapple Gun and fires it upward, pulling Percival to safety with him. Sora, however, doesn’t react in time and falls.

Terrified Sora runs from the Photacks. She hides behind a stack of batteries and throws them at the hamster creatures, which eagerly eat them. Meanwhile, Arin and Percival, hiding right above Dorama, plan to jump down onto him and free Riyu. However, the plan doesn’t go as expected - the ropes they use to descend become tangled.

Sora continues to throw batteries, eventually overfeeding the Photacs. At the same time, Arin and Percival engage Dorama in combat on the ground. Once the creatures are distracted, Sora rushes to Riyu, who lends her his power. With his help, Sora frees the dragon from its cage and deactivates the Photacs. Dorama manages to escape.

Ninja and Percival slowly leave the battleground, only Sora lingers behind, trying to grab a cable to use in the construction of the mechs.

Errors[]

  • Sora talks about making the Elemental Mechs, even if she would not think about creating them until The Elemental Mechs, which are set almost a year after the comic.
  • Sora still uses dragon power from Riyu, even if she has already unlocked her True Potential by then.
  • Dorama managed to reactivate the Photacs, even though they had been permanently deactivated by Sora in "The Battle of the Second Monastery." Additionally, Sora emphasized that dragon power is essential for the activation of the Photacs in the comic, despite the assertion in the show that only Sora possessed the capability to activate them should additional Photacs be developed.
