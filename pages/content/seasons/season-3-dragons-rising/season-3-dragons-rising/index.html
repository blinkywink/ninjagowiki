The third season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising is preceded by Season 2 and will be succeeded by Crossroads Crosstalk and Season 4. The Arc Dragon of Focus is the mascot character alongside Zane for the first part of the season and Sora for the second part, Thunderfang serves as the main antagonist, and the Dragonian Trackers and Spectral Dragonians serve as the villainous factions for the first and second parts respectively. The season first premiered on April 17, 2025.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game series refers to this season as "Dragon Storm."[24]
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt stated his favorite episode from the season is the 17th episode.[25]
    • He also stated this season was his favorite to write[26]and he prefers this season's intro over the previous two.[27]
  • The season will introduce two new realms.[28]
    • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, these two realms haven't been seen on-screen since the Merge.[29]
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, this season will be darker than the previous ones.[30]
  • There will be a line referencing Christofern in this season.[31]
  • This is the first season since Crystalized to feature Jay on a poster.
    • It is also the first season of Dragons Rising to feature all the ninja (from both generations) on one poster.
      • Despite this, the last poster to feature all of the original ninja as part of the ninja team was Seabound, as Nya was Samurai X for the Crystalized poster, and Jay is featured as an antagonist on this season's poster.
  • The first teaser has a shot of Thunderfang roaring, but it is flipped horizontally from the original episode.
  • On April 1, 2025, writers Kevin Burke and Chris "Doc" Wyatt each made a two truths and one lie post as a sneak peek at the season on Bluesky.
    • Burke's three statements were:
      • Without Nya, the Gates to Androzani will not open.
      • Mr. Frohicky will be denied personal rewards.
      • Sora barfed on Zane.[32]
        • The first statement is the lie: Mr. Frohicky mentioned he wasn't interested in personal rewards anyway, and it was mentioned that Sora once barfed on Zane.
        • The first statement is a reference to the sixth serial of the 21st season of Doctor Who.
    • Wyatt's three statements were:
      • The Merlopians welcome their new protector.
      • Leaf Blade serves as regent of the Mountain Tribe.
      • Vengestone can be known by another name.[33]
        • The first statement is the lie: Leaf Blade serves as the regent for Eridany until she comes of age, and while the Lightning-Tides are aware of Vengestone, they are unfamiliar with its name and abilities, which leads them to simply refer to them as "Glow Rocks."
  • In the intro when Wyldfyre is fighting Nokt, a green glow can be briefly seen. The model of Nokt used is the one where he has the control device Ras used in Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 2.
  • Part 1 exhibits notable parallels with both Rise of the Snakes and Seabound.
    • In Rise of the Snakes, the plot revolves around the main villain's pursuit of ancient artifacts, specifically the Prismatic Blades in Season 3 and the Fangblades in Season 1, to awaken a massive creature, which ultimately leads to betrayal when it is freed in the penultimate episode (Thunderfang devouring the souls of the Forbidden Five, while Pythor was eaten by the Great Devourer). Both final episodes focus on the ninja battling the colossal threats.
    • In Seabound, the two colossal creatures (Thunderfang and Wojira) are depicted as chaotic beings capable of generating destructive storms, and the main antagonists intend to tap into their powers but get betrayed, with Kalmaar trying to control Wojira to command the Fury of the Storm but gets eaten by Wojira, while the Forbidden Five seek to liberate Thunderfang for greater power but end up losing their souls to him.
  • This is the first season of Dragons Rising where Ras isn't a member of the villainous faction.
  • The intro for this season is the first Dragons Rising season that features a remixed version of "We Rise". It mirrors how Rise of the Snakes and Legacy of the Green Ninja both used the Weekend Whip for their intros, whereas Rebooted used a Weekend Whip remix.
  • In the first half of the season, Zane is the mascot character and has a more prominent role. Coincidentally, he is also the mascot and focus character of Season 3 of the original series.
  • This is the first season to include a minifigure of P.I.X.A.L. in several sets of a season.
  • This is the second season to end with the ninja separated and at disadvantage, the first being Sons of Garmadon, with the original four and Master Wu being sent to the First Realm whilst Lloyd and Nya remained in Ninjago.
  • Kevin Burke stated that the overall theme for this season is consequences.[34]
  • This season shares many similarities with Season 5 of the original show:
    • Both have main antagonistic factions that consists of ghosts
    • Morro is involved in both seasons
    • The ninja team up with a bounty hunter who aids them in their battle against the spectral beings, in Season 5 it's Ronin while in Dragons Rising Season 3 it's Jay
    • Villains have a goal in aiding a massive creature
      • The massive creature in both seasons need souls for their plans
    • Something tragic happens to Cole that involves ghosts
    • Lloyd visits the Cursed Realm
    • The Cursed Realm has a major role in both seasons
      • The spectral factions both come from the Cursed Realm
Focus characters

Focus characters[]

Protagonists[]

  • Arin serves as the main protagonist of this season.
    • Arin was focused on in Episodes 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9 and 10.
      • In Episode 8, a flashback was shown of Arin meeting Sora for the first time. Later when she was kidnapped by two Skulkins, Arin saved her. In the present, the two reunite.
      • In Episode 10, he uses the elixir from the Well of the Lost to free the souls Thunderfang had devoured, killing him and restoring Lloyd's soul. After the battle, Arin leaves again with Ras, now with Sora accompanying them.
  • Sora has a main character role in this season, and is one of the mascot characters of the second half.
    • Sora was focused on in Episodes 1, 2, 5, 6, 8 and 10.
      • In Episode 1, she hands out pies in the Crossroads, but after realizing her pies taste bad Sora reluctantly asks Frak for help. Later, when searching for Arin the ninja encounter Dorama's Puppet, after defeating it Sora finds Arin's grapple stuck in the puppet.
      • In Episode 6, she is teleported to Djinjago by Arrakore and defends him against Rox and the Dragonians using Wish Magic. After defeating them, she is reunited with the rest of the Ninja and tells them about the Forbidden Five's plan.
      • In Episode 8, a flashback was shown of Sora leaving Imperium and seeking refugee in the Crossroads, eventually meeting Arin. Later she was kidnapped by two Skulkins, but was saved by Arin. In the present, the two reunite.
      • In Episode 10, after Thunderfang's defeat, she leaves the Ninja team to go with Arin and Ras.
  • Lloyd has a main character role in this season.
    • Lloyd was focused on in Episodes 1, 2, 5, 8 and 10.
      • In Episode 1, he talks to Cole about Arin and Jay’s disappearances, after Cole leaves Lloyd gives Frak advice on his fighting technique.
      • In Episode 10, Thunderfang senses him as a Conduit and attempts to devour his soul, before being stopped by P.I.X.A.L. Later on during the fight, Thunderfang succeeds in devouring Lloyd's soul, but when Arin defeats him Lloyd's soul is returned to his body.
  • Zane has a supporting character role in the first half of season and is one of the mascot characters of the first half, although he has a minor role in the second half.
    • Zane was focused on in Episodes 4 and 8.
  • Kai, Nya, Wyldfire and Frak are main-supporting characters in this season.
    • Nya was focused on in Episodes 1, 3 and 6.
      • In Episode 1, while searching for Jay, she, Kai and Wyldfyre interrupt the Lightning-Tide Community Ceremony and the three are imprisoned and put on trail. After explaining her mistake Nya is freed by the Lightning-Tides, but is forced to break the other two out.
      • In Episode 6, while researching Prismatic Steel she is teleported to Djinjago by Arrakore and defends him against Rox and the Dragonians using Wish Magic. After defeating them, she is reunited with Kai and tells him about the Forbidden Five's plan.
    • Wyldfyre was focused on in Episodes 5, 7 and 9.
    • Kai was focused in episodes 1, 3, 8, 15, 17, 19 and 20
      • In Episode 1, he receives advice from Lloyd after breaking the training course, then is asked by Sora to help her make pies which he happily agrees to. Later, when searching for Arin the ninja encounter Dorama's Puppet, during the fight Frak jumps inside it's head and discovers a Prismatic Blade, he pulls it out causing the puppet to shut down.
  • Riyu plays a secondary role in this season.
    • Riyu was focused on in Episodes 5 and 6.
      • In Episode 6, he is teleported to Djinjago by Arrakore and defends him against Rox and the Dragonians using Wish Magic. After defeating them, he is reunited with the rest of the Ninja.
  • Jay is a minor antagonist in the first half of the season, but is a supporting character in the second half.
    • Jay was focused on in Episodes 2, 6, 8 and 10.
      • In Episode 6, he meets with Zeatrix and accepts her request to locate Ras and bring him to her.
      • In Episode 10, he fights the Dragonians alongside Nya and unintentionally reveals his identity to her by using his powers. After being injured, he goes back to the Monastery of Spinjitzu with the Ninja and reveals Arin’s parents are alive.
  • Cole has a minor role in the first half of this season, but has a secondary role in the second half.
    • Cole appears in Episodes 1 and 10.
      • In Episode 1, he talks to Lloyd about Arin and Jay’s disappearances, then goes to the Land of Lost Things to look for them.
      • In Episode 10, he is seen reacting to the storm with the Finders, after Thunderfang's defeat Cole attends the party at the Monastery of Spinjitzu and reunites with Jay.
  • P.I.X.A.L. plays a secondary role in the first half of the season, although she is a minor character in the second half.
    • P.I.X.A.L. was focused on in Episodes 4 and 5.
      • In Episode 4, P.I.X.A.L. is revealed to have been in a techno-cocoon, she is freed by Zane and helps him fight Rapton and the Freebooters, then later Nokt and the Dragonian Trackers.
      • In Episode 5, she rebuilds Zane with some help from the Freebooters, later she reunites with Lloyd and defeats Zarkt.
  • Morro plays a small but impactful role in the first half of the season.
    • Morro is a major supporting character in Episode 3
      • In Episode 3, Morro reveals what happened to the Cursed Realm and the Departed Realm as he guides Arin to the Well of the Lost in order to get the healing elixir that he uses to save Ras and defeat Thunderfang.
    • He also makes a small cameo appearance at the end of Episode 10, where his soul gets stolen by Thunderfang.
  • The Arc Dragon of Focus serves as the overarching protagonist of this season.

Antagonists[]

  • Thunderfang serves as the main antagonist of the season.
  • The Forbidden Five serves as the secondary antagonists of the season.
  • Lord Ras serves as a major antagonist of the season.
  • Lord Ras' master serves as the overarching antagonist of the season.
  • Powerlid and Rapton serve as minor antagonists in this season.
  • The Dragonian Trackers and Spectral Dragonians serve as the villainous faction of the season.

Part 1[]

  • The Forbidden Five serve as the main antagonists of Part 1. Their goal was to find the Prismatic Blades in order to release Thunderfang, believing that by doing so, they could once again acquire the dark powers he once granted them.
    • Rox was focused on Episodes 1, 3, 5, 6, and 9.
      • In Episode 1, Rox tells the story of the Thunderfang wreaking havoc in the City of Temples, and an intense battle with the Arc Dragon of Focus. She and the rest of the Forbidden Five members to steal supplies to get to the First Land.
      • In Episode 5, Rox becomes infuriated due to Zarkt failing to get the Blue Prismatic Blade, and planned to fix the broken pieces of the Yellow Prismatic Blade, with the help of Djinn Magic.
      • In Episode 6, Rox invaded Djinjago, forcing Arrakore to fix the Yellow Prismatic Blade and threaten to imprison him in the Timepiece, she fought against Nya, Sora and Riyu in a battle at Djinjago.
    • Nokt was focused on Episodes 4 and 9.
      • In Episode 4, Nokt broke into the Freebooters' prison train and fought against Zane, P.I.X.A.L., and the Freebooters in order to collect the Red Prismatic Blade.
      • In Episode 9, Nokt takes Ras from Drix to get his revenge on Ras for using him, and he has several battles against Ras and Arin.
    • Drix was focused on Episodes 8 and 9.
      • In Episode 8, Drix fought against the ninja to get the Returning Blade and the Blue Prismatic Blade. He successfully stole both blades after he fought with the ninja.
      • In Episode 9, Drix was on his way to break the remaining chains of Thunderfang, until he was interrupted by the ninja, forcing in a battle with them. Hence, he sent a bug to signal the remaining members of the Forbidden Five that he was in a battle with the ninja.
    • Zarkt and Kur were focused on Episode 5.
      • In Episode 5, Kur broke into the Temple City Museum, and stole the broken pieces of the Yellow Prismatic Blade. She used her elemental power to decay Roby. On the other hand, Zarkt fought against Lloyd, Sora, Frak, and Riyu in a battle to steal the Blue Prismatic Blade before being defeated by P.I.X.A.L. and subsequently scolded by Rox.
  • Thunderfang serves as the overarching antagonist of Part 1, becoming more prominent in Episodes 9 and 10.
    • He was focused on Episodes 1, 3, 9, and 10.
      • In Episode 1, Thunderfang was seen in a flashback wreaking havoc in the City of Temples, until he engaged in battle with the Arc Dragon of Focus. He was banished and imprisoned beneath the Storm Village.
      • In Episode 3, Thunderfang was visited by the Forbidden Five, to whome he entrusted the quest of the Prismatic Blades to free him from his prison. In return, he promised them even greater power. He gave them a gem that would allow them to find the Prismatic Blades.
      • In Episode 9, Thunderfang was successfully freed by the Forbidden Five. However, he betrayed them and consumed their souls to reclaim his lent power.
      • In Episode 10, Thunderfang fought in a battle with the ninja and was defeated by Arin using the elixir from the Well of the Lost. His soul was then transported to the Spectral Lands, where he appeared in his departed form to consume Morro and the Soul Suckers.
  • Other antagonists of Part 1 include Ras, Powerlid, the Soul Suckers, and the Freebooters.
    • Ras served as a major antagonist throughout part 1. His goal was to find a way to create new Dragon Icons so he could reverse the Merge. He was focused on in Episodes 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10.
      • In Episode 2, Ras trains Arin, and then they set off for the Spectral Lands.
      • In Episode 3, Ras continues to train Arin and they continue their journey to the Spectral Lands. Ras regains his strength and moves on.
      • In Episode 5, Ras and Arin walk through the desert until they find Ras's jet. Ras takes Arin to his parents' grave.
      • In Episode 6, Ras trains with Arin and tells him of his plan. They begin their journey to the Temple of the Dragon Cores.
      • In Episode 7, Ras and Arin headed to the Temple of the Dragon Cores when they were suddenly attacked by Jay. They are rescued by the Mountain Dragons. They eventually reach the Dragon Core Temple, where Ras extracts information about the creation of Dragon Icons from the Temple Spirit. Near the end of the episode, they encounter the ninja.
      • In Episode 8, Ras and Arin confront the ninja and learned about Thunderfang, during which Ras brushes off their concerns until Jay kidnapped him. He would later fight Drix and the Dragonians, only to be kidnapped by them.
      • In Episode 9, Ras is taken by Nokt for revenge, then together with Arin, he fights Nokt and Rox.
      • In Episode 10, Ras leaves with Arin and is joined by Sora in their mission to reverse the Merge.
    • Powerlid was focused on in Episode 2.
      • In Episode 2, Powerlid tried to stop the ninja from freeing the Dragonians he had imprisoned, but was then outsmarted by Sora.
    • The Soul Suckers were focused on in Episodes 3 and 10.
      • In Episode 3, the Soul Suckers attacked Arin, who was helped by Morro to stop them. He then attacked the race but Arin stopped them using his Object Spinjitzu.
      • In Episode 10, the Soul Suckers tried to attack Thunderfang but it sucked out their souls.
    • The Freebooters were focused on in Episode 4.
      • In Episode 4, the Freebooters robbed First Land Monastery, were caught by the Zane, and then tried to escape. They fought Zane and Pixal when Nokt and Dragonians attacked them. They helped P.I.X.A.L. repair Zane.

Part 2[]

  • Thunderfang serves as the main antagonist of Part 2.
  • The Forbidden Five serve as the secondary antagonists of Part 2.
  • Other important antagonists include Lord Ras, Lee (Episodes 13, 14, and 15), Powerlid (Episodes 18, 19), and the Freebooters (Episodes 18, 19), and the Administration Agents (Episode 12).
