Arc Dragon of Focus' theme

Arc Dragon of Focus' theme[]

The theme was first introduced in "The Missing" during the flashback where the Arc Dragon of Focus defeated Thunderfang. The theme is played on strings and sometimes accompanied by a chorus. The theme is possibly an altered rendition of the Ninjago Overture.

The theme is most notable for its diegetic use. In "The Vault of Focus", Bleckt activated the bells in the Vault, which played the theme's melody, after he informed the ninja of the song's vital role in awakening the Arc Dragon of Focus. The melody was hummed by Sora and Frak in "For Whom the Bell Tolls" and played in both Caves of Bells in "When Doves Cry."
