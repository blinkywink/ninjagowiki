Ninjago City in Danger! is the 126th issue of the Ninjago Magazine. It includes 892504 Morro and Storm Zane appears on the cover.

Face the Music!

Face the Music![]

Some time after the Tournament of the Sources, Alfonzo Frohicky was shopping in Ninjago City when he spotted posters that read, "The Forbidden Five are coming tonight!" In a panic, he ran as fast as he could to the Monastery of Spinjitzu with one such poster to warn the Ninja,

Although Lloyd was confused as to why the Forbidden Five would simply announce their impending arrival through posters, he decided that they could not risk the possibility of the Five's arrival. He then ordered Frak, Sora, and Nya to stay behind to guard the monastery while he, Kai, Wyldfyre, Zane, and Mr. Frohicky would make their way to Ninjago City to confront their powerful foes. Upon arriving in Ninjago City, Mr. Frohicky pointed out one of the posters he had seen, although upon closer inspection, the Ninja realized it was just an advertisement for a concert - specifically for five bands that had been banned by Beatrix and would be performing together for the first time tonight. In spite of the misunderstanding, the group of Ninja decided to attend the concert anyway just to make sure there would be no appearances by the real Forbidden Five.

As the evening wore on, each of the Ninja, except for Lloyd, began to ease up on their fears, enjoying the music and each of the bands as they performed. By the time of the fifth band's performance, Lloyd conceded that the real Forbidden Five were not coming and it would just be a harmless evening. Unfortunately, the crowd of wild fans were becoming overbearingly rowdy as they cheered the arrival of the fifth and final band, Catallica. As the band made their debut, multiple fans began to climb atop a nearby stereo tower, burdening the structure with excessive weight as it buckled and began to fall. Thinking quickly, Lloyd used a burst of Energy to stabilize the tower and disperse the crowds from it. The rest of the Ninja then helped in keeping the rowdy fans contained so that everyone could enjoy the rest of the concert and performance. In gratitude, the members of Catallica began to perform The Weekend Whip at the Ninja's request.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The names of the five bands are references to real-world bands:
    • Fleetwood Mech - Fleetwood Mac
    • The Wu - The Who
    • The Rolling Bones - The Rolling Stones
    • 2Morrow - Tomorrow
    • Catallica - Metallica
  • The comic features Munce, Geckles and Skulkin on the streets of Ninjago City. It is unknown if the city is now indeed populated by these people or if they just came from the Crossroads to listen to the concert.
  • A character with brown jacket and Dareth's hair appears in the crowd, but it is unclear if it is indeed Dareth.
  • This issue was not published in the Netherlands.
