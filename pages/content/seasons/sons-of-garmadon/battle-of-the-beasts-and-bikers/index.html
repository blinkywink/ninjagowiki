Battle of the Beasts and Bikers! is the 37th issue of the Ninjago Magazine. It includes 891837 Nya and Resistance Cole appears on the cover.

Brawl of the Jungle!

Brawl of the Jungle![]

The Ninja were left in the jungle, and the monster crab is still hot on their trail. They have to dodge more giant animals and carnivorous plants, and as they escape, they reflect on how they should have realized Harumi was the Quiet One all along. They almost run into a giant spider's web, which gives them an idea for a plan to catch the crab. After Kai, Jay and Cole chase the spider away, they use the web to trap the monster crab. Now that the beast is finally immobilized, Zane is able to scan it and realizes that it is not in fact the mythical guardian of the Oni Temple, but just an ancient crab that has grown into a monster under the influence of the Black River. Suddenly, they are surprised by Luke Cunningham and a group of other Sons of Garmadon. He wants to tame the giant crab for their gang and earn Harumi's approval.

The Ninja easily defeat the bikers using the vines from the trees, but Luke Cunningham is able to free the monster crab from the spiders' web. He manages to knock out the crab temporarily with his bike katanas. Although before he can escape with the giant crab, the Ninja lure him into a trap with which they destroy his motorcycle and he escapes empty-handed. The ninja then use the chains from his motorcycles to tie up the crab so they can ride on the creature. Now the ninja can finally make their way back to the city on Crabby's back.
