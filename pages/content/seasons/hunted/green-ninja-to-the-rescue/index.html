Green Ninja to the Rescue! is the 46th issue of the Ninjago Magazine. It includes 891946 Jay in the UK release, and 891839 Cole USA release. Legacy Lloyd, Nya and Zane appear on the cover.

Have a Nice Flight!

Have a Nice Flight![]

With an Ice Dragon and their imprisoned leader in tow, Jet Jack and her faction of Dragon Hunters begin preparations to release Iron Baron from his metallic prison. Utilizing the Ice Dragon's cryokinetic breath, Iron Baron was freed, only to learn immediately that Faith and her reformed Hunters were closing in on them, riding on the backs of other dragons. Infuriated by this turn of events, Iron Baron immediately berated his liberators, asking them why they couldn't be riding atop of dragons as well; surely it wasn't that hard! All the rogue Hunters had to do was to find a way to get the Ice Dragon to obey. When efforts at kindness failed, Iron Baron took the reigns and forcibly mounted the Ice Dragon, immediately seizing control and herding it towards Dead's End.

Back in Dead's End, Faith was making final preparations before beginning her search for the Ice Dragon, alongside Lloyd and Wu. Shortly afterwards, the latter two mounted Slab, the Earth Dragon, and flew out towards the Rogue Hunters' camp. Unfortunately, the group soon ran into Iron Baron and his hunters - all of whom now mounted dragons of their own and unleashed a barrage of attacks against Faith and her allies.

As Iron Baron screamed at the Ice Dragon to unleash its cryokinetic breath, berating it all the while, Wu mused to Lloyd that captives will eventually turn on their masters. As if like clockwork, the Ice Dragon flew side by side with Faith's Fire Dragon and both dragons began to communicate with one another and formulated a plan. Without warning, the Ice Dragon made a sharp turn in the air, throwing Iron Baron off its back and plunging him to the ground below. Taking advantage of this distraction, Lloyd leapt onto the back of the green dragon and quickly dispatched the rogue hunters riding on its back. One by one, the captured dragons shook off the chains of their captors and turned on them, forcing the rogue hunters to run. Shortly afterwards however, the rogue hunters were corned and captured. Wu and Lloyd then instructed the defeated hunters that the dragons were a vital part of the balance of this realm and that if the hunters truly wished to live in peace with them, they had to treat them like friends.

With the day saved, Faith, Lloyd, and Wu returned to Dead's End where the former thanked her friends for their help in bringing peace to the Realm of Oni and Dragons. Wu and Lloyd then said their goodbyes to Faith and returned to Ninjago using Traveler's Tea, vanishing into a glowing portal.

By sundown, Faith began to address her people, but her speech was quickly interrupted by the watchtower guard who shouted that Iron Baron was approaching the city's gates. From beyond the walls of Dead's End, a weary and panicked Iron Baron shouted for everyone to take shelter and to look out for themselves; the Oni had returned and their invasion was about to begin.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The comic is a continuation of the previous issue.
  • Despite being referred to as an Ice Dragon in this and the previous issue, it shoots Lightning on a few pages.
  • The comic shows an unknown green dragon living in the Realm of Oni and Dragons. If it is intended to be an Energy dragon, then this is the first dragon in the franchise to wield this element. If it is not, then this is the first dragon in the franchise not to wield any element.
