Secret Mission! is the 43rd issue of the Ninjago Magazine. It includes 891843 Samurai X and Hunted Lloyd appears on the cover.

Ups and Downs

Ups and Downs[]

After the Resistance lost their vehicles in the fight against the Sons of Garmadon, they are now building replacement vehicles. However, this is made more difficult by missing components. Suddenly P.I.X.A.L. notices that her mech goes back online. She manages to trace the Samurai X Mech's signal to a garage. The realization that her mech wasn't destroyed gives Lloyd hope that they'll find the other vehicles there too. He immediately decides to form two teams in order to successfully retrieve their vehicles. Lloyd, P.I.X.A.L. and Gravis will break into the garage from the rooftops, while Nya, Skylor and Bolobo will infiltrate from the water.

The resistance sets out at night and Lloyd's team jumps across the rooftops and floats down an elevator shaft using Gravis' elemental power. Nya's team enters via the sewers, where they first have to distract the hungry sharks with food and then use Bolobo's plant power to take out the guards.

Once both teams have made it into the building, there is only one obstacle between them and their vehicles: a set of dangerous traps. However, together Gravis and Bolobo can stop the swinging morning stars and the team has finally made it to the garage. But Killow, Ultra Violet and a group of bikers are already waiting for them there. P.I.X.A.L. manages to regain control of her mech and together the resistance defeats the bikers in no time and flees back to their hideout.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Bolobo and Gravis are shown as part of the Resistance, even though Bolobo was captured by Ultra Violet and Gravis was not present at all in the season.
  • P.I.X.A.L. uses her original design in the comic but with the Skybound shoulder pad.
  • Snake Jaguar (Zanes SOG disguise) can be seen alongside a group of bikers in one of the panels, even though in Season 9 Zane is trapped in the Realm of Oni and Dragons with the other ninja.
  • Chopper Maroon and Ultra Violet are referred as Alex and Frank. Although they are mostly other characters using their designs since they appear in another location later.
