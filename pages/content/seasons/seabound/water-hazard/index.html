Water Hazard! is the 89th issue of the Ninjago Magazine. It includes 892289 Jay and Scuba Lloyd appears on the cover.

Bad Water!

Bad Water![]

At the monastery, Kai, Cole and Jay are having a shower until they realize that the water stinks and is disgusting. The trio go tell the news to Master Wu who was making tea for Misako, she ended up spitting the tea because of its terrible taste shortly before Wu gets told of the news. Zane later checks the water from the well and realizes that it is contaminated with chemicals.

The ninja then go to the city and discover that the water there is also poisoned, killing all the fish there. Lloyd, Kai, Cole and Jay all go to the source of the pollution while Zane goes back to the monastery to warn Wu to not drink any water.

The ninja go outisde of the city and realize that the water supply is being polluted because Miss Demeanor and her henchmen are mining Vengestone by pumping the water out and mixing it with chemicals. The ninja try to stop her with Spinjitzu but are quickly overpowered by the dirty and sticky water.

The ninja are later able to defeat the henchmen using their vehicles but Miss Demeanor is able to get away from there, plotting her next scheme. The ninja celebrate their victory unknowingly being spied by a Crystal Spider.
