The fourteenth season of Ninjago, subtitled Seabound, is preceded by Season 13 and The Island and succeeded by The Virtues of Spinjitzu and Season 15. It aired in April 2021, the same year as the series' 10th anniversary. Nya serves as the focus ninja and mascot character, Kalmaar serves as the main antagonist, and the Maaray Guards serve as the villainous faction of the season.

A special teaser[1] was released on June 26, 2021, at the LEGO CON 2021 livestream.[2] The season was released in the United States on YouTube between July 14, 2021 and July 17, 2021. The official teaser was released on YouTube on September 29, 2021 while the trailer was released on October 3, 2021. This season, along with The Island was released on Netflix on November 19, 2021 in the United States.

Official description[]

When Nya’s powers are suddenly disrupted, the ninjas travel to the the bottom of the Endless Sea for answers. They find a dangerous underwater world ruled by Prince Kalmaar, who wants to destroy NINJAGO City by waking the all-powerful sea serpent Wojira. The ninjas must find the prized amulets to stop him and keep their home safe. Will they triumph over the evil they face in the depths of the ocean?[3]

Along with Nya’s mom, Maya, P.I.X.A.L., and her fellow ninja, Jay, Zane and Lloyd, you’ll join Nya in a battle against the elements and the evil Prince Kalmaar, the Prince of Merlopia. That’s when things really get tricky. He has an evil plan to awaken an ancient sea-serpent, Wojira. Psst spoiler alert! This does NOT look good for NINJAGO City! Just like her mother before her, Nya is the Master of Water, but without the full force of her powers, how will she overcome Prince Kalmaar and save NINJAGO from his evil plan? Join the crew on their epic Seabound adventure to find out. All it takes is some courage, sacrifice and a little help from your friends and family.[4]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • According to Tommy Andreasen, this season can be referred to as the Water Chapter as the season focuses on Nya's elemental powers.[15]
    • The season was originally going to be called "The Abyss."[16]
  • The season draws on expansive world building throughout the show's run, from the soundtrack and former Elemental Masters, to tiger wasps and Wojira.
  • This is the first season for Lloyd to not use his powers besides Spinjitzu since his debut in Rise of the Snakes, as well as the first season for Cole to not use his Earth Punch since its debut in "Day of the Departed."
  • Elements from all previous WildBrain installments have appeared in this season:
    • The Explorer's Club, Lava Zombies, and the Paperboys from Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu.
    • Unagami, Okino, Racer Seven, Milton Dyer, Dancy Pants, and the return of Prime Empire from Prime Empire.
    • Shintaro Mountain from Master of the Mountain.
    • The Keepers and the Storm Amulet from The Island.
  • Nya is the only ninja to not receive a vehicle in this season, despite being the focus and mascot ninja.
  • This season is the first since March of the Oni to not have any gimmick sets in the set wave.
    • This season also has the smallest set wave since March of the Oni, and the second smallest for any full season with only five sets. However, it could be argued that Seabound has the smallest set wave due to March of the Oni only having an accessory pack rather than a full set wave.
  • Although Kirby Morrow unexpectedly passed away in November 18, 2020, he recorded Cole's lines for this season prior to his death. As a result, this is the last season to have Kirby Morrow as a cast member.
    • The dedication to Kirby Morrow was shown at the end of "The Turn of the Tide".
  • According to Tommy Andreasen:
    • On January 14, 2021, Ninjago's 10th anniversary, he said that they had received the render of the last episode of 2021 from WildBrain, and had done the first voice record for the 2022 episodes the day before.[17]
    • On September 9, 2020, seven months before the season's release, he stated that depending on how it turns out, it may be his favorite.[18]
    • The reason Cole has a smaller role in this season is that "Cole just had a season dedicated to him, so [they] thought it was fair to focus elsewhere. Seabound was written and recorded before Kirby Morrow's untimely passing."[19]
    • "There actually exists a 44 minute seamless edit of the last 4 episodes of 'Ninjago: Seabound' … [they] call it 'The Flood'."[20]
  • Tommy Andreasen refers to this season as a chapter.[21]
  • This is the first appearance of Ray and Maya since "Lost in Time". This is also the second season to focus directly on Kai and Nya's family, following The Hands of Time.
    • In regards to Maya's appearance in Seabound, this is the first season since Hunted and the first WildBrain season where Jillian Michaels is part of the voice cast.
      • She had also voiced the Sphinx statue in "Riddle of the Sphinx".
  • This is the second season which was released earlier than the sets. The first was Legacy of the Green Ninja.
    • The first official pictures of the sets were revealed one day after the season finale premiered in the UK.
  • This is the first WildBrain season to feature the death of a sapient character.
    • While Prime Empire featured several in-game deaths, these were all undone by the season finale.
    • King Trimaar's death is the first in-series death of a sapient character since Harumi in "Saving Faith."
  • Unagami (in his child form), Milton Dyer, Korgran, Fungus, Plundar, Murtessa, Gulch, Skylor, The Postman, Ronin, Ed and Edna Walker, and a Monk all make non-speaking appearances.
  • In 2021, a 2D ad was created for Journey to Mastery by PicnicTV Studio, featuring many elements from this season, such as the Temple of the Endless Sea, Wojira, the Hydro Bounty and Hydro Mech.
  • Most of the season was produced with the crew members working from home due to the COVID-19 pandemic.[22]
    • Because of this some animation and voice lines were re-used, such as:
      • The animation of Zane trying to freeze Grief-Bringer in "Grief-Bringer" was reused in "A Big Splash".
      • Gayle Gossip saying "We'll be back after a short message" in "The Kaiju Protocol" being reused in "The Calm Before the Storm".
      • The lines Jay said to Nya after she was turned into an energy cube in "The Temple of Madness" being reused in "The Turn of the Tide".
  • This is the first season to air (albeit outside the US) before any official teaser.
  • In a promotional GIF (and in-season), WildBrain uses stances and forms for waterbending seen in Avatar: The Last Airbender.[23]
    • Jay had also used a very similar motion to those used in Avatar for lightning-bending a couple times in Sons of Garmadon.
  • This season shares similarities with Rise of the Snakes, Skybound, March of the Oni, and Master of the Mountain.
    • With Rise of the Snakes;
      • Seabound's plot shares a lot of similarities and callbacks, specifically its villain and final battle: both are about a villain with purple as their main color scheme (Pythor and Kalmaar) wishing to awaken a giant female serpent (the Great Devourer and Wojira), but requiring an object to do it (the Fangblades and the Storm Amulet).
        • Coincidentally, both villains are introduced in the fourth episodes of their seasons ("Never Trust a Snake" and "Five Thousand Fathoms Down").
      • They are able to obtain it and awaken the serpent, and it damages/destroys a ninja vehicle while trying to eat it (the Samurai Mech and the sub car), and eventually begins attacking Ninjago City.
      • The serpent eats her summoner, and the serpent herself gets defeated by someone (Garmadon and Nya) striking the weak spot on her head.
    • With Skybound;
      • This season's name, Seabound, sounds very similar to said season. Nya and Jay both have major roles in both seasons, though the focus spots are switched for this season, where Nya is the focus for this season, while Jay has a major role in the season. It also seems that Seabound appears to be the water-counterpart to Skybound.
        • Interestingly, there are exactly 100 episodes between the final episode of Skybound and the first episode of this season.
      • Some of the ninja team venture to deeper locations (Tiger Widow Island and Temple of Wojira). This eventually leads to the ninja team getting attacked by the main antagonist and the villainous factions (Nadakhan and his Sky Pirates, Kalmaar and the Maaray Guards).
      • Speaking of, the main antagonists for both seasons (Nadakhan and Kalmaar) share many similarities to each other in the fact that they are both princes of their respective Royal Families, and both act harsh towards a more meek member of their armies, who eventually side with the ninja in the end (Clancee and Glutinous).
        • Both princes also have their fathers (Khanjikhan and Trimaar) killed and had their roles as king succeeded by their sons, but for completely different reasons (Khanjikhan's fate was inevitable due to the destruction of the Cursed Realm and resulted in Djinjago's collapse, whilst Kalmaar willingly and brutally murdered his own father to become king and seize rule over the Endless Sea).
        • Both antagonists eventually capture the items obtained by the ninja (Nadakhan gets the Tiger Widow Venom, and Kalmaar gets both the Amulets of Wojira). But unlike in Skybound, the ninja were not able to obtain these items back.
        • Both antagonists also have similar rivalries with the focus ninja (Nadakhan with Jay, and Kalmaar and Nya). Also, Nya gets captured and confronted by both antagonists at one point ("Wishmasters" and "The Wrath of Kalmaar").
      • In Jay and Nya's relationship, one of them suffers a fatal consequence (Nya gets hit and dies due to the Tiger Widow Venom in "The Way Back," and Jay swallows too much water in his insides in "Nyad"). This leads to the other making a heartful decision to save the other and to defeat the main antagonists (Jay uses his final wish to reverse the events of Skybound by wishing no one ever found the Teapot of Tyrahn, and Nya merges with the Endless Sea to remove the water from Jay's lungs and give her the powers to defeat Kalmaar and Wojira).
      • Also, towards the season finales ("The Way Back" and "The Turn of the Tide") the ninja team become saddened by Nya's fates (her death towards Tiger Widow Venom, and losing her humanity and leaving the ninja team), but both are later undone (Jay's final wish literally reverses all these causes, while Nya returns to her human form much later on in Crystalized, during "A Painful Promise").
    • With March of the Oni, the last four episodes of Seabound are similarly structured with the entirety of the former season.
      • In the first episodes ("The Darkness Comes" and "The Calm Before the Storm") a former enemy of the ninja (Garmadon and Glutinous) warns them of an incoming threat upon Ninjago. When Wu and the others take the time to read this warning in, he prepares the ninja to begin evacuating the civilians of Ninjago City.
      • In the second episodes ("Into the Breach" and "Assault on Ninjago City") the entirety of Ninjago City has been covered up in some way by the villains utilizing their powers (the Oni covering the city in black clouds, and Wojira flooding the city using the Fury of the Storm). Both episodes end with the main heroes standing against the main antagonists (Lloyd and Garmadon against the Omega and Nya against Kalmaar and Wojira).
      • In the third episodes ("The Fall" and "Nyad") the main heroes pick up their fights against the main antagonists following the end of the previous episode but the heroes are forced to retreat from combat. The Ninjago News Center building plays an important role here (with the ninja evacuating the staff from there in "The Fall"; while the ninja use it as their hideaway point in "Nyad"). The ninja team also ends up being down one member (with Cole falling off the Destiny's Bounty and Jay swallowing a large amount of sea water).
      • In the fourth and final episodes "Endings" and "The Turn of the Tide"), after the ninja defeat the main antagonist, the ninja and most of their allies are all gathered together at the Monastery of Spinjitzu, except the tones are contrasted (with the ninja celebrating the defeat of the Oni in a cheery tone, compared to the ninja and everyone else honoring Nya's depature with a melancholic tone).
    • With Master of the Mountain;
      • The ninja explore a place which is connected with an element (the Endless Sea with Water and the Dungeons of Shintaro with Earth) and learn there is something dark going on under the surface (Kalmaar wants to reawaken Wojira and Vangelis is enslaving the Munce and Geckles in the Vengestone mines).
      • The ninja make friends with the family member of the villain (Benthomaar and Vania), who help the ninja and learn the truth of their evil family member (Kalmaar and Vangelis), who is the main villain.
      • The main villain tricked their own guards by telling tell them that the ninja were the enemies (the Maaray Guards and the Army of Shintaro).
      • Both seasons feature a backstory about a creature that was known for terrorizing people a long time ago (Wojira and Grief-Bringer) and got defeated by a female Elemental Master (Nyad and Lilly) using a higher-level ability or form that they discovered (merging with the sea and the Spinjitzu Burst).
        • After a battle, a legendary character (the First Spinjitzu Master and Lilly) gives the collectible items of the season (the Amulets of Wojira and the Blades of Deliverance) to native tribes (the Keepers and Merlopians, and the Munce and Geckles).
      • During the season finales, both collectible items of the season were destroyed by someone striking at them (Nya destroying the Wave Amulet and Hazza D'ur destroying the Blades of Deliverance).
      • A trial appears in both seasons (Trial By Sphinx and Trial-By-Mino).
      • The final battles of both seasons end with the focus ninja (Nya and Cole) unlocking the same higher-level ability that their predecessor used years ago in order to defeat the major villains (Wojira and Vangelis).
        • Both of the season finales feature a farewell (Nya leaving the team and entering the sea, and the ninja leaving Shintaro) and a ceremony dedicated to a female character (Nya's memorial and Vania's coronation).
      • Both seasons feature someone making a promise to a family member or relative (Benthomaar's promise that he will help the ninja stop Kalmaar before he awakens Wojira and Cole's promise to always stand up to those who are cruel and unjust).
      • Both seasons have the mother (Maya and Lilly) of the focus ninja giving them an important message ("That's the way the cookie crumbles" and "Always stand up to those who are cruel and unjust"), with it being fulfilled or applied in both season finales.
  • The teaser trailer published on LEGO's YouTube channel contains a short shot of Wu at night, although the real scene in the episode is in daylight.
  • In the last scene of the series finale trailer, Nya fights Wojira as the embodiment of the sea, although in the actual scene at the end of "Assault on Ninjago City," she was in her pre-merging form.

Focus characters

Focus characters[]

Protagonists[]

  • Nya served as the focus character of this season. Her relationship with her mother and Jay as well as her understanding of her powers are the main plot elements, leading to her ultimate sacrifice.
    • She was focused on in Episodes 165, 166, 168, 169, 173, 174, 179, and 180.
      • In Episode 165, she is unable to control her powers and interferes with a mission.
      • In Episode 166, she becomes frustrated with Maya's overly protecting nature and refuses to want help with her life.
      • In Episode 168, Nya and Maya take the APS suits and travel deep into the Endless Sea where they arrive at Merlopia. They are later captured by Kalmaar, whom they meet for the first time.
      • In Episode 169, she and Maya work together for the first time and luckily free themselves from Kalmaar's pawns.
      • In Episode 173, she is given the Storm Amulet from Mammatus and vows to protect it, however, she and the others soon realized that it was a fake.
      • In Episode 174, she spends the episode attempting to call whales so the team can escape the Island of the Keepers.
      • In Episode 179, Nya has a short battle with Kalmaar and Wojira. Later, she decides to sacrifice her life in order to save Jay, who inhaled too much seawater. This causes Nya to merge with the Endless Sea but costing her humanity.
      • In Episode 180, she battles Kalmaar and Wojira in her new form where she ultimately destroys the Wave Amulet, defeating Wojira. She then says a heartfelt goodbye to Jay and the team and goes back to the ocean. A memorial is later held for her.
  • Benthomaar has a major role in this season. His relationship with his father and brother are important aspects of the story.
    • He is introduced in Episode 169.
    • He starts his vital role in Episodes 170 and 171 when he starts to try and fulfill his promise to his deceased father, helping the ninja to escape from Merlopia after being framed by Kalmaar.
    • In Episode 172, he tells the ninja his backstory about how Kalmaar never accepted him as his brother.
    • In Episodes 173–175 he continues helping the ninja to get the amulets, while also telling them the backstory of Wojira.
    • In Episode 176, after seemingly defeating Kalmaar, he decides to move with the ninja to the monastery.
    • In Episodes 177–178, when he and the ninja discover that Kalmaar still has the Storm Amulet and managed to awaken Wojira, Benthomaar starts helping Ninjago City during Wojira's flood while also trying to convince the Maaray Guards about Kalmaar's lie.
    • In Episode 179, alongside the rest of the ninja, he waits for days for Jay to wake up after having swallowed too much water.
    • In Episode 180, he does his final battle against Kalmaar up until destroying his trident, making him loose control of Wojira and resulting into Kalmaar being eaten alive. At the end of the episode, Benthomaar is present at Nya's memorial.
  • Maya is also a prominent character, especially in the first half of this season with her trying to bond with Nya.
    • In Episode 165, Maya arrives along with Ray when Nya's powers do not work, causing Nya to get angry at Kai for calling them.
    • In Episode 166, Maya tries to bond with Nya as her mother, but Nya still feels annoyed.
    • In Episode 167, Maya infiltrates the Hydro Bounty to accompany Nya in the journey to Merlopia to discover why Nya's powers do not work.
    • In Episode 168, Maya goes with Nya to Merlopia in APS Suits while giving a talk to her about sacrifices. The two then get captured by Kalmaar in the end of the episode.
    • In Episode 169, Maya helps Nya escape from capture.
    • In Episode 170-172, she continues to accompany Nya and the ninja at their escape from Merlopia after Kalmaar kills his father and frames them all.
    • In Episode 173, when Nya and the Ninja are stuck in the Island of the Keepers after Kalmaar damages the Hydro Bounty, Maya gives a speech to Nya that she realized she does not need her help anymore and that she will find a way to get them out of there.
    • In Episodes 174 and 176, Maya, Nya, and the Ninja return to Ninjago and seemingly stop Kalmaar. Maya and Ray then leave from the Monastery, being proud of Nya.
    • In Episode 180, after Nya sacrificed herself to stop the awakened Wojira, Maya and Ray attended her memorial, being deeply saddened for their daughter's apparent demise.
  • Jay is another prominent character in this season, mainly because of his relationship with Nya.
    • He was focused on in Episodes 167, 169, and 177–180.
      • In Episode 167, he tries to assure Nya that that they will find out what is bothering Nya's powers and will find a solution.
      • In Episode 169, he risks his life in an attempt to recharge the battery of the Hydro Bounty, which he successfully does so.
      • In Episode 177, he meets Nya at Benny's Arcade Emporium and they play Dancy Pants together until the game breaks.
      • In Episode 178, he saves Dareth and others from a flooded bus. Later, he is attacked by Wojira and sinks to the flooded streets on Ninjago City in his damaged vehicle.
      • In Episode 179, Benthomaar and Cole save him in the last minute, but Jay still inhales some seawater. Nya is unable to draw it out, which forces her to merge with the Endless Sea in order to save Jay and defeat Wojira.
      • In Episode 180, he fights Kalmaar after he attacked Nya, fueled by fury over the visual loss of Nya. Later, he begs Nya not to leave him, but she leaves nevertheless, but not before openly confessing his love for her. Deeply saddened by the departure of his girlfriend and in a state of depression, he later attends Nya's memorial.
  • Zane, P.I.X.A.L., and Lloyd play important parts in some episodes, however, their roles are much smaller compared to Nya and Jay.
    • Zane and P.I.X.A.L. are focused on in Episode 166 where they follow a distress signal which ends with their vehicles being nearly destroyed, but discover a clue as to why Nya's powers are acting strange.
    • Lloyd is focused on in Episode 171 where he struggles to escape from Merlopia but gains use of an APS suit and successfully flees with the rest of the ninja.
  • Kai and Cole have minor roles in the first half of the season as they are absent. They do, however, receive focus towards the end.
    • Kai receives focus in Episodes 165, 174, 175 and 180.
      • In Episode 165, he tries to convince Nya to call their mother and ask her to help her, which Nya angrily refuses. He then proceeds to call their parents himself.
      • In Episode 174, he trains with his father and Master Wu at the monastery. At the Explorer's Club, Kai is the one who correctly answers the riddle of the Sphinx, which grants them permission to enter the club and find the Storm Amulet.
      • In Episode 175, he follows Kalmaar throughout the whole city in order to stop him from stealing the Storm Amulet. In the end, he nearly drowns, but is rescued by Antonia and Nelson.
      • In Episode 180, he is shocked by Nya's decision to leave the team and tries to convince her to stay. He later attends Nya's memorial, saddened by the departure of his sister.
    • Cole receives focus in Episodes 176 and 180.
      • In Episode 176, he returns to Shintaro Mountain where he brings the Storm Amulet to be kept hidden, albeit it is a fake.
      • He comforts Jay in Episode 180 after Nya's sacrifice.
  • Master Wu once again has a very small role for the first half of this season. But he does play a significant role towards the end as well.
    • He played an important role in Episode 165, when he tells a story about his father, Wojira and Nyad, and searched for scrolls in the library.
    • He was also important in Episode 174, when he carried out his project in the Monastery of Spinjitzu and later fought Kalmaar for the Storm Amulet in the Explorer's Club.
    • His fight with Kalmaar and the Maaray Guards contiuned in Episodes 175 and 176.
    • He was also important in Episodes 178 and 179, when he helps the citizens of Ninjago City to evacuate.
    • He was also important in Episode 180, seeing Nya's farewell, and also being responsible for saying her deulogy in her memorial.
  • Ray, Misako, and Dareth all receive focus in some episodes.
    • Ray appears on Episodes 165-167, 174-176, and 180.
      • In Episode 165, Ray arrives at the Monastery along with Maya. Ray is responsible for carrying all the suitcases.
      • In Episode 166 and 167, Ray has father moments with Kai, playing videogames with him, alongside staying with him, Cole, and Wu when the rest of the team leave to Merlopia.
      • In Episodes 174 and 175, he, Kai, Cole, and Wu fight against Kalmaar across Ninjago City for the Storm Amulet.
      • In Episode 176, when they seemingly defeated Kalmaar, Ray and Maya left.
      • In Episode 180, after Nya sacrificed herself to stop Wojira, Ray and Maya were present at her memorial, deeply saddened.
    • Misako has focus on Episodes 174 and 176.
      • In Episode 174, she helps Wu, Kai, Ray and Cole to get to the Explorer's Club, proposing the Trial By Sphinx. She also fights the Maaray Guards later in the episode.
      • In Episode 176, she attacks Kalmaar, but is almost killed by him until Wu saves her.
    • Dareth has focus on Episodes 177 and appears with no lines in Episode 180.
      • In Episode 177, when Wojira floods the entire city, Dareth helps saving everyone in a bus.
      • In Episode 180, Dareth is present at Nya's memorial.

Antagonists[]

  • Kalmaar served as the main antagonist of this season. His goal of awakening Wojira to use her powers to destroy Ninjago and the rest of the surface was also one of the main plot elements of this season.
    • Between Episodes 165 and 168, he uses replicas of the Storm Amulet as a replacement for it so he can awaken Wojira. However, it instead causes the marine life and Nya's powers to lose control.
    • In Episode 169, he tries to get Nya and Maya to tell him more about the location of the Island of the Keepers so he can get the Storm Amulet.
    • In Episode 170 and 171, Kalmaar tricks the Maaray Guards into thinking that the ninja killed Trimaar and orders them to capture the ninja and Benthomaar; Kalmaar's brother who sided with the ninja.
    • In Episodes 173–176, he fights the ninja for the Storm Amulet after discovering that it was at the Explorer's Club.
    • After awaking Wojira, he uses his trident to take control of her in Episodes 177–180. However, after Benthomaar destroyed the trident, Kalmaar is ultimately devoured by Wojira once he lost control of her.
  • Gripe served as the secondary antagonist of this season.
    • While working with Kalmaar to awaken Wojira, Gripe supported him by helping him look for the Storm Amulet and prevented the Maaray Guards from knowing the truth about Trimaar's death.
  • Wojira served as the overarching antagonist of this season.
    • After Wojira fell into a deep sleep by Nyad, Kalmaar became desperate to find her so he can use her powers to destroy Ninjago.
    • Once awoken, Wojira falls under Kalmaar's control and is used by him to destroy Ninjago City until Episode 180. After being freed from his control, Wojira eats Kalmaar and attempts to attack the ninja, but is defeated by Nya.
  • Other important antagonists include Glutinous (until Episode 177) and Miss Demeanor (Episode 165).
    • Glutinous used replicas of the Storm Amulet to reawaken Wojira for Kalmaar, but failed. However, after researching on the Island of the Keepers, he is able to reveal the location of the island to Kalmaar so he can get the Storm Amulet.
      • In Episode 177, Glutinous redeems himself by warning the ninja about Kalmaar after witnessing Wojira's awakening.
    • In Episode 165, Miss Demeanor runs a Vengestone smuggling operation with her henchmen to meet up with someone, but is interrupted by the ninja when they confront her. However, Miss Demeanor is able to escape.
  • The Maaray Guards served as the villainous faction of this season. While some of the Maaray Guards were already enemies with the ninja, Kalmaar tricked the others into thinking that the ninja killed Trimaar.
