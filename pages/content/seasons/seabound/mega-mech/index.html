Mega Mech! is the 88th issue of the Ninjago Magazine. It includes 892288 Zane and Scuba Kai appears on the cover.

Great Droids Think Alike

Great Droids Think Alike[]

At the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Cole was recalling the times he and the other ninja had together until he realized that the team is starting to break up due to Nya's sacrifice. With the sacrifice causing Jay to be in a depressive state, Kai acting more impulsive, and Lloyd losing his leadership. Wu tried to help the ninja regain their balance, but at the same time, he's been trying to study on the Elemental Powers of Water.

Meanwhile, at the coast, Zane and P.I.X.A.L. used the ShuriCopter to bring a newly built Power Up Mech to the coast to test it. P.I.X.A.L. told Zane that he was able to control the mech with his brain shortly before she went back to the monastery to get some extra armor. Zane was testing the mech, not knowing that he was being spied by Gripe and some Maaray Soldiers, who were plotting to use the mech to reclaim the Kingdom of Merlopia.

Gripe and his soldiers attacked Zane, who was able to defend himself until he accidentally ejected his unconscious body, that was later stolen by the enemy. P.I.X.A.L. later added some armor to the mech that had Zan'e consciousness inside shortly before they decided to attack Gripe's submarine, unfortunately Gripe and the Maaray Soldiers were able to outrun them which made Zane very emotional. To give Zane some courage, P.I.X.A.L. decided to detach the giant shuriken on the ShuriCopter and use it on the mech.

Zane and P.I.X.A.L. were able to catch up to Gripe and use the shuriken to destroy the submarine. Gripe and his soldiers escaped and Zane was able to reconnect his consciousness to his body.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The events of "The Sea Beast!", "Tentacles Below!", and "Back to Merlopia!" are mentioned in the comic.
  • Zane acting very emotional in the comic foreshadows his role in Crystalized, where he turns his emotion off due to an unwanted emotional reaction.
  • In one panel, Gripe's dialogue is attributed to another Maaray Soldier instead of him.
