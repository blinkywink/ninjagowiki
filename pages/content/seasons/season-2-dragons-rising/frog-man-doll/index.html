Overview

“It's to work out conflict. If you have an issue with me, talk to the doll like it's the real me.”
— Alfonzo Frohicky to Zane, "The Spell at the Waterfall"

The frog man doll is a doll depicting Mr. Frohicky, that he gave Zane to speak to if he has conflict within him.

History

History[]

The Spell at the Waterfall[]

Zane approached Mr. Frohicky, who was on a ladder decorating the monastery portal gate. In his hands, he held the frog man doll, and told Mr. Frohicky that he had questions. Mr. Frohicky told him that the doll was for him to talk to if he had a problem within him. Zane was amused, and told him that he would just talk to him if they had a conflict, stating that he does not experience heightened emotions.

Later, when Cole and the Finders arrived through the portal gate, Cole questioned why Zane was holding a frog man doll. Zane dismissed him as two Wolf Mask Claw Warriors burst through the gate. They began to fight and Zane used the doll to punch the warriors back into the portal gate.

Later, while listening to Bonzle's story, Zane was seen clutching the doll.

The Shape of Motion[]

A miniature version of the doll was seen on a Nindroid Warrior's desk in the Administration.
