Overview

“Behold, Foundation Street. The traditional center of the city.”
— Bleckt, "They Gather for the Feast"

Foundation Street is a street that runs through the heart of the City of Temples. It is the city's traditional hub for Elemental Masters and their guests during the Tournament of the Sources.

History

History[]

Enter the City of Temples[]

While the ninja entered the City of Temples for the Tournament of the Sources, Arin and Riyu got separated and found themselves on Foundation Street, where they faced Security Bots and encountered Frak.

They Gather for the Feast[]

Inside the Maze[]

Many fighters and their guests walked through Foundation Street during the downtime between the day's events. Most notably, Arin and Riyu discovered a strange mark imprinted on a wall in an alley before joining Frak for pie; Wyldfyre wandered up and down Foundation Street in search of Roby, running into the former group before spotting Roby and Bleckt; and finally, Cole ran into Geo at a fruit vendor's stall, surprising Cole who didn't know up until this point that Geo had been invited to the tournament. Later, Sora sought out Jordana to inquire about the latter's actions during the banquet from last night, though Jordana coldly rebuffed her, dismissing Sora's questioning as "mind games."

United We Fall[]

Hearing Kai's voice, Riyu ran down Foundation Street in search of the source but grew frustrated after some time of fruitless searching. Later in the evening, following the day's events, Cinder made a point to instigate a fight with the Ninja and feigned injury in an effort to have them thrown out of the Tournament.

The Sword Shatters[]

The Ninja walked down Foundation Street as they returned to their dorm. On the way, Cole discussed with Zane about the mysterious monastery he had been led to the night before, remarking that he did not understand its significance. In response, Zane suggested that Cole should instead be thinking of a way to reach the monastery rather than its purpose.

The Ninja were later gathered on Foundation Street and obtained some concessions before making their way to the Games Plaza to spectate the evening's games.

Clues and Suspects[]

Arin and Wyldfyre walked down Foundation Street as they searched for clues to aid in their investigation. Eventually, their walk led them to Bleckt and Roby's office.

The Final Game[]

As Bleckt walked along Foundation Street in the early morning hours, he was tackled to the ground by Wyldfyre. At first, Bleckt was irate and berated Wyldfyre for jostling him around "like a sack of beets." He would, however, change his tune after realizing that Wyldfyre saved him from being struck by a falling brick. After a brief conversation, the two parted ways, the former unaware that Wyldfyre had secretly switched his ledger with a menu. Some time later, Wyldfyre ran into Zur on Foundation Street, and the latter expressed his interest in the ledger, having recognized the written language to have been an ancient form of Devonian, the language of Zur's people. Zur then kindly offered to help translate the writings, which Wyldfyre accepted.

Elements of Betrayal[]

Following the closing ceremony of the Tournament of the Sources, the Elemental Masters and their guests walked down Foundation Street, exchanging goodbyes as they left the City to go their separate ways. Sora and Lloyd spoke about Arin, who had disappeared through a portal to go after Ras. Elsewhere, Wyldfyre and Roby, now a couple, discussed their budding relationship as the former sadly admitted that she had to go with the rest of the ninja but promised to stay in touch.
