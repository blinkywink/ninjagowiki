The second season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising is preceded by Season 1 and The Elemental Mechs, and succeeded by Season 3. Kai and Egalt serve as the mascot characters in the first part of the season while Lloyd and the Source Dragon of Motion serve as the mascot characters in the second part, Lord Ras serves as the main antagonist, and the Wolf Clan serves as the villainous faction of the season.

At San Diego Comic-Con 2023, it was announced that it will be released in 2024.[1] The first teaser and poster were released on January 31, 2024, and it was also announced that the season would be released on April 4, 2024. On March 23, the first three episodes were shown at the Paris Image Forum theater.[2] A full trailer was released on YouTube on March 7, 2024. Episodes 1-10 were released on April 3, 2024 on Netflix in Finland and were released on April 4, 2024 in the United States. Episodes 11-20 were released on July 25, 2024 on Peacock in the United States and were taken down the next day. The same episodes were then released officially on US Netflix on October 3, 2024.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This marks the 20th mainline installment in the franchise overall and the 17th numbered season.
  • This season breaks the trend of Kai being either the focus or mascot character and having the Serpentine (Anacondrai, Vermillion, Pyro Vipers) be the main antagonistic faction with the Wolf Clan being the main antagonistic faction this time.
  • The LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game series refers to this season as "Blood Moon."[46]
  • Tied with the previous season, this is the longest season in the franchise in terms of runtime, having 20 episodes that are 22 minutes long. (440 minutes total)
  • This is the first season to have 3 set waves.
    • It is also the first season to have waves entirely based of one part of the season: the March wave being based on the first part of the season and the June wave on the second. The January wave is mostly based on the Mechs shorts.[47]
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt:
    • One of the episodes has a classic Doctor Who 1977 episode reference that is deep and obscure.[48]
    • Episode 7 was his favorite episode of the series to write.[49]
  • The season went into production in 2022.[50]
  • On March 12, 2024, writers Chris "Doc" Wyatt and Kevin Burke each tweeted out a sneak peek at the season in the form of two truths and a lie.
    • Wyatt's three statements were:
      • The Ninja will be sorted according to their Fang status.
      • When the Blood Moon rises, Skulkins gain special powers.
      • The Administration will have problems getting the equipment they want.[51]
        • The second statement is the lie: Egalt and Rontu sort the ninja by their Fang status and the ninja prevent the Administration's attempts to recover Zane, who they consider to be their equipment.
    • Burke's three statements were:
      • Scrolls written in ancient Theroxian reveal how to perform an evil ritual.
      • A Writer of Destiny has a direct connection to the Forbidden Five.
      • Slug Merchants travel the Merged Lands less frequently than some would like.[52]
        • The second statement is the lie: the scrolls of the Forbidden Five, which are written in Theroxian, reveal how to execute the Ritual of the Blood Moon, and Gandalaria mentions that she had been waiting for the Slug Merchants to arrive to Mysterium for weeks.
  • Kevin Burke stated that the overall theme for this season is secrets.[53]
  • This is the third consecutive season to have its second part release on Netflix in the US during October, following Crystalized from the original show and Season 1 of Dragons Rising.
  • The trailer for Season 2 had a shot of Cinder powering up to use Shatterspin but the Shatterspin circle was missing, unlike in the episode.
  • This is the first time that someone has been credited as creating original music alongside Jay Vincent and Michael Kramer, that person being Adam Dib. He's worked on the show since 2021 as creating additional music.
  • This is the second season to feature a tournament featuring Elemental Masters competing against each other, with the first being Season 4: Tournament of Elements.
  • This season shares similarities to Season 11: Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu.
    • Kai is the mascot character.
    • At the beginning and throughout the seasons, one of the ninja (Lloyd and Zane) experience troubling visions that reveal future events within the seasons.
    • A new, darker form of Spinjitzu created by ancient beings is practiced in the seasons (Shatterspin, created by the Forbidden Five; Forbidden Spinjitzu, created by the First Spinjitzu Master).
    • One of the ninja ends up being rendered helpless in combat throughout most or all of the first halves following a battle with a new enemy (Wyldfyre having her leg injured by Cinder; Kai having his Fire powers stolen from Aspheera).
    • Wu is revealed to be the indirect cause of the events build up to in the seasons (Wu being the cause of the Merge; Wu causing Aspheera to desire revenge upon him).
    • Towards the end of the first halves, one of the ninja is sent to a distant location thanks to the main antagonists (Ras sending Kai to the Nether-Space and Aspheera sending Zane to the Never-Realm, although the latter happened in the penultimate episode of its first half). Both ninjas are able to return to Ninjago by the season finales ("Elements of Betrayal" and "Awakenings").
    • Also in the second halves, one of the ninja who is amnesiac, becomes a major antagonist as a result of manipulation from the main antagonist (Jay being manipulated by Ras; Zane being manipulated by Vex). Both ninjas also end up fighting one of their former teammates in one episode (Nya against Jay in "Inside the Maze" and Lloyd against Zane in "Awakenings").
  • This is the first Ninjago season where the intro in Part 1 and Part 2, while they share the same style of intro, the characters and environments were changed to reflect the current events of each Part.
  • The season was nominated in 2025 for 3 Leo Awards, including Best Animation Series.[54]
Focus Characters

Focus Characters[]

Protagonists[]

  • Arin and Sora have main character roles in this season.
    • Arin was focused on in Episodes 1, 6, 9, 15, 16 and 20.
      • In Episode 1, he tried finding more information about his missing parents, and joins Sora and Riyu in investigating the recent break ins.
      • In Episode 6, he accidentally injures Egalt during a training session, and is demoted to First Fang.
      • In Episode 9, he tries rescuing Bonzle from Jordana, but is stopped by Ras.
      • In Episode 15 and Episode 16, his conversation with Lord Ras changes his points of view about the ninja and sets him apart from them.
      • In Episode 20, Arin leaves the ninja team to save Ras so they could find his parents together.
    • Sora was focused on in Episodes 1, 6, 9, 16, 18, 19 and 20.
      • In Episode 1, Sora, Arin, and Riyu investigate the recent break ins.
      • In Episode 6, Sora complains to Rontu about being the weak link on the team due to not knowing Spinjitzu, but is told by Rontu she is being too hard on herself.
      • In Episode 9, Sora creates the plan to infiltrate the Wolf Clan using disguises.
      • In Episode 16, Arin confronts her about her lie. She also defeats Cinder.
      • In Episode 18, she defeats Frak and receives a clue from Jordana.
      • In Episode 19, Sora faces off against Nokt in the final round of the tournament.
      • In Episode 20, Sora was crowned champion of the Tournament of the Sources.
  • Lloyd has a main character role in this season and is one of the mascot characters of the second half.
    • Lloyd was focused on in Episodes 1, 3, 6, 10, 12, 17, 18 and 20.
      • In Episode 1, Lloyd has visions that foreshadow the rest of the season and the visions put a lot of stress on him.
      • In Episode 3, he gets a vision from the Source Dragons on how to fight Shatterspin, and worries about his ability to lead the team as well as Wu.
      • In Episode 6, he has a discussion with Rontu about his visions.
      • In episode 10, he starts accepting his visions into his life, allowing him to perform Rising Dragon.
      • In Episode 12, he was tested by Egalt and Rontu.
      • In Episode 17, he struggles with his vision about losing to Zeatrix. Later he defeats her in the tournament but was heavily injured by her.
      • In Episode 18, he comes out of a coma and faces off against Nokt in the tournament.
      • In Episode 20, Motion Source Dragon thanks him and gifts him a Dragon Icon.
  • Kai has a main character role and is partially the main protagonist in this season and is one of the mascot characters of the first half, although he has a smaller role in the second half.
    • Kai was focused on in Episodes 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 14, 17 and 20.
      • In Episode 7, Kai was the first ninja to learn Rising Dragon Technique.
      • In Episode 9, he comes up with the plan to stop the Blood Moon ritual.
      • In Episode 10, he masters Rising Dragon, defeats Cinder, and gets trapped in Nether-Space.
      • In Episode 14, Kai understands a way to communicate with Riyu from Nether-Space.
      • In Episode 17, Kai effectively communicates with Nya and Riyu.
      • In Episode 20, Kai is finally released from Nether-Space, and returns for the final battle against Nokt, and reunites with the ninja.
  • Nya, Riyu and Wyldfyre are supporting characters this season.
    • Nya was focused on in Episodes 3, 7, 10, 14, 16, 17 and 20.
      • In Episode 3, she comforts Lloyd about his leadership abilities, and is the first to break free from the illusion caused by the Dream-Guyver.
      • In Episode 7, her suggestion to train alongside Kai is what causes him to perform Rising Dragon.
      • In Episode 10, Nya is able to perform Rising Dragon Technique after Kai is sent to Nether-Space.
      • In Episode 14, she unexpectedly fights Jay in the Tournament of the Sources, discovers he lost his memories of being a ninja and wins. They break up due his corruption to Shatterspin, leaving her heartbroken.
      • In Episode 16, she fights Nokt in a match and ultimately loses due to Bleckt's rigging.
      • In Episode 17, she discovers a method to communicate with Kai via dragon ivory and their family link, but gets captured by Nokt and Rox along with Riyu.
      • In Episode 20, she frees Kai and Bonzle from the nether-space with Riyu's sparks and her connection through dragon ivory.
    • Riyu was focused on in Episodes 1, 5, 11, 14, 15, 16, 17 and 20.
      • In Episode 5, Riyu learns from Rontu that dragons can perform Spinjitzu, he then trains to learn it.
      • In Episode 11, he find the matriarch of his tribe dying and try to help her. After her death he discover her child and goes in search of his tribe, to entrust them with the young dragon. Together, Riyu and the Mountain Dragons mourn the lost of their matriarch.
      • In Episode 14, he helps Arin find a clue to solve the bot attacks and, by extension, the rigging of the Tournament of the Sources.
      • In Episode 15, he hears Kai's voice and tries to communicate with him by breaking into the Wolf Clan's quarters, with the help of Arin, and finding the Dragon Ivory stone.
      • In Episode 16, he continues hearing Kai.
      • In Episode 17, he shows Nya the Dragon Ivory stone and gives her some of his Dragon power, allowing her to communicate with Kai.
      • in Episode 20, he once again empowers Nya with his dragon power, enabling her to open a portal to the Nether-Space via the Crowns of Horns, freeing Kai and Bonzle in the process.
    • Wyldfyre was focused on in Episodes 3, 7, 11, 12, 18, 19 and 20.
      • In Episode 3, she sneaks onto the Destiny's Bounty, despite Kai telling her to stay due to her injury.
      • In Episode 7, she has a conversation with Egalt about his legendary feats and how he is likely suffering from the Wasting Sickness.
      • In Episodes 11 and 12, she deals with the lost of Kai and try to follow in these footsteps by taking on the Red Ninja mantle.
      • In Episode 18, she and Arin sneak into Roby and Bleckt's office and find the latter's secret room.
      • In Episode 19, she secretly swaps Bleckt's ledger with the banquet menu, meets Zur, and together, they expose the ways in which Bleckt rigged the Tournament in favor of the Wolf Clan.
      • In Episode 20, she saves Roby from falling off a cliff which ultimately leads to Nokt's defeat and returning the elemental powers to their original bearers. Wyldfyre and Roby become a couple by the end of the episode.
  • Zane and Cole play secondary roles in this season.
    • Zane was focused on in Episodes 5, 6, 7, 8, 12 and 14.
      • In Episode 5, he helps Cole fight the Wolf Warriors, and listens to Bonzle's story with the others.
      • In Episode 6, he, Cole, and Bonzle set off on their quest to Mysterium.
      • In Episode 7, he meets Gandalaria, and is confronted by the Administration Agents, who wish to reclaim him.
      • In Episode 8, he fights off the Administration Agents alongside Cole and Gandalaria, and works with them to keep Bonzle safe, but is tricked and trapped by Jordana, who takes Bonzle from them.
      • In Episode 12, he is taught Rising Dragon technique by Egalt and Rontu, with help from Lloyd.
      • In Episode 14, he versus Zur and loses.
    • Cole is focused on in Episodes 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 16, 17, 18 and 19.
      • In Episode 4, he helps the Finders fend off the Wolf Warriors, and with them escapes through the portal gate to the Monastery of Spinjitzu.
      • In Episode 5, he and Zane fight the Wolf Warriors and listen to Bonzle's story with the others.
      • In Episode 6, he, Zane, and Bonzle set off on their quest to Mysterium.
      • In Episode 7, he meets Gandalaria, discusses how best to protect Bonzle, and is confronted by The Administration.
      • In Episode 8, he fights off the Administration Agents alongside Zane and Gandalaria, and works with them to keep Bonzle safe, but is tricked and trapped by Jordana, who takes Bonzle from them.
      • In Episode 16, he versus Frak and looses, and is lead to the monastery of gates by the yellow orb.
      • In Episode 17, he gives Frak advice on how to use the elemental power of Earth.
      • In Episode 18, he uses the Elemental Earth Mech to make a bridge so that he and Arin can get to the Monastery of Gates.
      • In Episode 19, he asks Wu’s ghost questions and is shocked when he admits to have caused the Merge
  • Wu has a minor role in this season.
    • Wu was focused on in Episodes 5, 7, 8, 16 and 19.
      • In Episode 5, he has a heart-to-heart with Bonzle about feeling lonely, and encourages her to create a physical form for herself.
      • In Episode 7, he, in his sprite form, guides Bonzle underneath the Monastery of Mysterium and encounters Jay.
      • In Episode 8, he, in his sprite form, shows Jay a reflection of the latter as a ninja and activates his lightning power.
      • In Episode 16, Ras tells Arin Wu caused the Merge.
      • In Episode 19, he, in his sprite form, appears to Cole and Arin, shows them the Crowns of Horns, and confirms he caused the Merge.

Antagonists[]

  • Lord Ras serves as the main antagonist of this season.
  • Lord Ras' master serves as the overarching antagonist of this season.
  • Cinder, Jordana, Nokt, Rox, and Bleckt serve as the secondary antagonists of this season.
  • The Wolf Clan serves as the villainous faction of the season.

Part 1[]

  • Lord Ras serves as the main antagonist of Part 1, leading the Wolf Clan and intending to free the Forbidden Five from the Nether-space by performing the Ritual of the Blood Moon. He was focused on in Episodes 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9 and 10.
    • In Episode 1, Ras uses the Dragon power from the Source Dragon to unlock the Shadow Dojo, and announces his plan to Jordana to unleash the Forbidden Five.
    • In Episode 2, Ras takes over the Cloud Kingdom, and fights back against the ninja, demonstrating the power of the Wolf warriors and of Shatterspin.
    • In Episode 3, Ras observes Cinder Shatterspin training. Later, Jordana informs him where the final component is. Ras, along with Jordana and Cinder, heads to the engine room and orders the monks to start the engine so they can travel to the Land of Lost Things.
    • In Episode 4, Jordana informs Ras about what is happening in the Land of Lost Things. Ras then orders the Gong of Shattering to be prepared to make obtaining the Spell easier.
    • In Episode 5, Cinder continues his Shatterspin training under Ras supervision. Ras leads Cinder Shatterspin training and then asks Jordana whether she has found the Spell they need. At the same time, he is summoned by his master, who tells him to prepare five sacrifices for the Blood Moon.
    • In Episode 7, Ras observes the rising Blood Moon, after which his stripes turn red.
    • In Episode 8, Ras orders Jordana to stop the ninja.
    • In Episode 9, a flashback reveals Ras’s history and shows that he was found by his master as a child. Ras informs his master that the Blood Moon ritual has begun and then orders Cinder to place the Gong of Shattering in the Shadow Dojo and destroy the Cloud Kingdom. Jordana informs him that the ninja have taken the Spell. As punishment for her failure, Ras attacks Jordana and sets out to retrieve the Spell himself. Ras defeats Arin and recovers the Spell.
    • In Episode 10, Ras performs the ritual, then fights the ninja and throws Kai into the portal, freeing one of the Forbidden Five. When he sees that they are losing, he calls for his master’s help. His master teleports Wolf Clan, along with the Shadow Dojo, to an unknown location. Ras is then summoned by his lord, whom he informs that they will conquer the Merged Realms by reaching the Source Dragons and winning the Tournament of the Sources.
  • Lord Ras' master and the Forbidden Five serve as the overarching antagonists of Part 1.
    • Ras' master was focused on in Episodes 5, 9 and 10.
      • In Episode 5, the master summons Lord Ras to talk to him about their plan.
      • In Episode 9, it is revealed that Ras was saved by his master as a child, and it was the master who taught him about strength being the most important thing.
      • In Episode 10, the master summons Ras once again and they also help him and the Wolf Clan to escape after failing at the Ritual of the Blood Moon.
    • The Forbidden Five were focused in Episode 10.
      • Nokt was focused on in Episode 10.
        • In Episode 10, after being freed from the Nether-Space, he fought in the battle against the ninja, performing Shatterspin. However, after the portal was destabilized, he argued with Ras, stating he didn't free the rest of the Forbidden Five, which their conflict escalated, resulting in Ras attaching a control implant onto Nokt's head to stop disobedience to Ras.
      • Rox was focused on in Episode 10.
        • In Episode 10, she escapes the Nether-Space by extracting her soul from her body, taking control of Jordana without anyone, aside from Nokt, noticing.
  • Cinder and Jordana both serve as the secondary antagonists.
    • Cinder was focused on in Episodes 1, 2, 9 and 10.
      • In Episode 1, Cinder is introduced stealing the Mallet of Shattering.
      • In Episode 2, Cinder fights Euphrasia during Ras' takeover of the Cloud Kingdom, and uses his power of Shatterspin to fight the ninja.
      • In Episode 9, Cinder receives an order from Ras to place the Gong of Shattering in the Shadow Dojo and destroy the Cloud Kingdom.
      • In Episode 10, Cinder notices that the ninja have infiltrated the ritual. He is trapped in Sore prison. Cinder again uses Shatterspin to fight the ninja, before being bested by Kai using Rising Dragon, as well as by Lloyd.
    • Jordana was focused on in Episodes 1, 8, 9 and 10.
      • In Episode 1, Jordana ventures with Ras through the Forest of Spirits to the Shadow Dojo, and supplies the Dragon power he needs to open it.
      • In Episode 8, Jordana knocks the Destiny's Bounty out of the sky, and kidnaps Bonzle from Cole, Zane, and Gandalaria.
      • In Episode 9, Jordana captures Bonzel, but Arin later attacks her and takes Bonzel from her. She informs Ras that she has lost the Spell, and he throws her away.
      • In Episode 10, Jordana opens a portal to Nether-Space, but ultimately fails to stop Bonzle from closing it and becomes possessed by Rox.
  • Other important antagonists include Dorama, the Administration Agents and Jay.
    • Dorama was focused on in Episode 6.
      • In Episode 6, Dorama captured the ninja and imprisoned them in her Acting Academy. Thanks to Bonzel and the fact that he is a Spell, they were able to easily escape the cage and continue on their way to Mysterium.
    • The Administration Agents were focused on in Episode 7 and 8.
      • In Episode 7, Administration Agents arrived at the Mysterium Monastery to recover the equipment called Zane.
      • In Episode 8, the Administration Agents fought the ninja while still attempting to destroy Zane.
    • Jay was focused on in Episode 7 and 8.
      • In Episode 7, Jay encountered Bonzle and Sprite.
      • In Episode 8, Jay tried to stop Bonzle. He said that he did not remember what he had done before working for the Administration. Duszek showed him his former outfit and that he had elemental powers.

Part 2[]

  • Lord Ras continues to serve as the main antagonist of Part 2. His goal is to win the Source Tournament and gain all the powers from the tournament to create the Source Dragon Icons. He was focused on in Episode 11, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19 and 20.
    • In Episode 15 and Episode 16, his conversation with Arin about him wanting to reverse the Merge changes the young ninja's points of view about the ninja and sets him apart from them.
    • In Episode 19, Ras is betrayed by Nokt and in Episode 20, he is taken by the Forbidden Five only to be saved by Arin.
  • The Forbidden Five serve as the overarching antagonists of Part 2. They were focused on in Episode 12, 13, 16, 17, 19 and 20.
    • Zarkt, Drix, Kur were focused on in Episodes 12, 13, 17, 19 and 20.
    • Nokt was focused on in Episodes 16, 19 and 20.
    • Rox was focused on in Episodes 19 and 20.
  • Cinder, Jordana and Bleckt served as the secondary antagonists in Part 2.
    • Cinder was focused on in Episode 15, 16, 19 and 20.
    • Jordana was focused on in Episodes 13, 18 and 20.
      • In Episode 18, she resists Rox enough to give Sora clues related to the Five's plan to escape.
      • In Episode 20, she is freed from Rox's soul, undoes her static spell and leaves to parts unknown.
    • Bleckt was focused on in Episodes 12, 14, 16, 18 and 19.
      • In Episode 12, he stops the ninja from attending the tournament by withholding their invitation and insisting that they should leave. Despite his efforts, he was forced to accept their presence because of Roby's position as Games Master.
      • In Episodes 14, and 16, Bleckt secretly rigged some of the matches involving the ninja, giving their opponents, including the Wolf Clan, an advantage.
      • In Episode 18, Arin and Wyldfyre uncovered that Bleckt harbors a deep-seated resentment towards Roby, which has escalated to the extreme desire to take his life. They also learned that Bleckt had a clandestine meeting with Ras just one day before the start of the tournament.
      • In Episode 19, it was revealed that he was the secret assassin who tried to kill both the ninja and Zur using the attacker bots. It also revealed that Bleckt teamed up with Ras by rigging the games, allowing Nokt to win. In return for this arrangement, Ras was supposed to take out Roby, while Bleckt would become the new Games Master.
  • Other important antagonists include the Freebooters, the Administration Agents and Jay.
    • Freebooters were focused on in Episode 11.
    • The Administration Agents were focused on in Episode 11.
    • Jay was focused on in Episode 14.
      • In Episode 14, Jay revealed himself to be in Ras' Team, competing against Nya, which he stated that he doesn't remember Nya nor the Ninjas. He was ultimately defeated by Nya, but he was sent away by Lord Ras.
