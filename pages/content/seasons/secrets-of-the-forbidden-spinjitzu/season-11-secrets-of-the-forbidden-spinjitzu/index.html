The eleventh season of Ninjago, subtitled Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu and alternatively known as Fire & Ice,[1] is preceded by Season 10 and succeeded by the Prime Empire Original Shorts and Season 12. Though Kai is the mascot character, there is no focus character.[2] Aspheera is the main antagonist of the Fire Chapter and General Vex is the main antagonist of the Ice Chapter. The Pyro Vipers and Blizzard Samurai are the villainous factions of the Fire and Ice Chapters respectively. The main characters take turns reading the title card. The season is split into two chapters, the Fire Chapter and the Ice Chapter.

A teaser for this season was released on May 26, 2019. The official Fire Chapter trailer was released on June 14, 2019, on the LEGO Life app, and June 16, 2019, on the official LEGO YouTube channel. The official Ice Chapter trailer was released on September 3, 2019, on the LEGO Life app, and September 5, 2019, on the official LEGO YouTube channel.

Episodes 1-8 released On Demand and on the US Cartoon Network app/website on June 21, 2019. Episodes 9-15 released On Demand and on the US Cartoon Network app/website on July 19, 2019. Episodes 16-30 released On Demand on November 1, 2019, and on the CN app/website on November 25, 2019. The season started airing on June 22, 2019, in the US.[3] This season drops the subtitle of Masters of Spinjitzu and reduces the run-time to 11 minutes which continues onto the later seasons.

The Ice Chapter, along with Seasons 10, 12, and 13 became available on Netflix in the US starting November 1, 2020. The Fire Chapter was later added on November 4, 2020.

Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

Basking in the glory of their recent victory, the ninja embark on a new adventure to explore a mysterious pyramid – just for a little light rest and recovery, of course. But has success lulled our heroes into a false sense of security? When the ninja accidentally set an ancient threat loose, nothing could ever have prepared them for the mayhem that follows. [4]

Trailer 1 - The Fire Chapter: The Scrolls of Forbidden Spinjitzu have been kept a secret for a thousand years. With good reason. The evil Serpentine sorceress, Aspheera, was banished along with the Scrolls – but now she is back, hungry for revenge, and ready to get her fangs on the Scrolls once more and reclaim her powers. The ninja’s powers, friendships, beliefs and loyalties will be put to the test like never before. [5]

Trailer 2 - The Ice Chapter: Zane’s been captured by the evil Ice Emperor. The Ninja have mounted a desperate rescue mission – but if they want to see their friend again, they’ll have to battle hordes of Blizzard Samurai as they make their way through the Never-Realm, from where no Ninja has ever returned. Join the adventures of Nya, Kai, Jay, Zane, Cole, Lloyd, and Master Wu in 15 exciting new episodes. Bundle up, and let’s go! [6]

When an imprisoned serpentine sorceress uses the long-lost Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu to unleash an ancient army, the ninja must shape up and protect Ninjago yet again. During the fight one ninja is banished to the mysterious Never-Realm and our heroes must embark on a desperate mission to rescue their friend from the frozen grasp of the tyrannical Ice Emperor.[7]

Sets

Sets[]

The marketing of the sets is based around the two respective chapters, with around half having desert-based box art and the other half being based in the Never-Realm. Kai is the mascot character and the Scrolls of Forbidden Spinjitzu are the collectible items. Most of the sets are based on the ninja's new vehicles from this season.

The ShuriCopter's set is the only Ice Chapter set not to be part of the Lloyd's Journey story line. The ShuriCopter and the ninja's new weapons (Kai's golden katana, Jay's golden kusarigama, Zane's golden shurikens, Cole's golden hammer, Nya's golden spear, Lloyd's golden sword) appear in the Ice Chapter sets despite remaining behind when the ninja traveled to the Never-Realm in the show.

This was the first set wave to feature Spinjitzu-Slam spinners.

Nya and Kai don't appear in the Ice Chapter themed sets. In contrast, Zane and Lloyd don't appear in the Fire Chapter themed sets. Cole and Jay are the only two ninja to appear in both chapter type sets.

Designs

Designs[]

Similar to Sons of Garmadon, this season marks some appearance changes for the characters, due to the animation studio changing from WILFilm ApS to WildBrain:

  • Each ninja's hair has more color lines and variation in it.
  • Jay's hair has returned to a more reddish-brown color akin to earlier seasons, rather than the darker brown color used in Seasons 8 through 10.
  • The relaxed lines above Cole and Dareth's eyes have disappeared.
  • Zane's face design is slightly different.
  • Lloyd's hair is a little darker.
  • The lower half of Kai's scar is missing in Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu and the first half of Prime Empire.
  • Nya's hair is a little more gray, compared to Cole's.
  • Wu has a longer beard and a small ponytail, just like his father.
  • P.I.X.A.L. does not have a face bordered by a black helmet, unlike how it was in Seasons 8 through 10.
  • The Mechanic's cheekbones, wrinkles, and the circles under his eyes were removed from his face design.
  • Serpentine scales are only two-dimensional.
  • Nelson looks slightly older, with a slightly different face print and mid-legs piece.
  • Spinjitzu tornadoes have returned to their original shape, with some minor changes. For example, Zane's tornado has bits of ice floating around and there is a ribbon-like texture present on the tornadoes.
Trivia

Trivia[]

The Trivia on Season 11: Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu needs to be organized.

  • This is the fourth season to have the Serpentine be the main antagonistic faction, following Seasons 1, 4, and 7.
  • This is the first season to have 11-minute-long episodes and be 30 episodes long.
    • Despite its shorter individual run-time, this season is the longest season of Ninjago, tied with Season 15: Crystalized. This season features the largest number of episodes in the series, and the highest amount of content, as in total it is 330 minutes.
    • However, in the entire franchise, Crystalized and this season are the second-longest, since the seasons of Dragons Rising are longer with twenty 22-minute episodes.
    • It is also the first season to contain more than 13 episodes.
    • It is not the first time that an episode lasts 11 minutes, the first time was with the split pilot episodes, although this is the first time full episodes that are not split are with that duration.
  • The season is divided into two fifteen-episode chapters: the Fire Chapter and the Ice Chapter.
  • This is the first season to be animated by WildBrain (formerly called DHX Media) instead of WILFilm ApS.
  • LEGO has changed the name of the teaser trailer for this season three times, first called LEGO Ninjago - Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu Teaser Trailer, then LEGO Ninjago - The All-New Series (2019) teaser and finally LEGO Ninjago - New Season (2019).
  • This season shares some similarities to Legacy of the Green Ninja.
    • Both the seasons are split into two chapters. In the first half, Serpentine serve as the faction (Hypnobrai, Venomari, Constrictai, Fangpyre and the Pyro Vipers), with Samurai serving as the faction in the second half (the Stone Army and the Blizzard Samurai).
    • The Ninjago Museum of History serves a role in at least 1 episode ("The Stone Army" and "Powerless").
    • Within the second chapter, the leader of the villainous faction of the second half of the season is manipulated by the main antagonist of said chapter (The Overlord manipulating Garmadon, and Vex manipulating Zane) and both victims end up fighting Lloyd one-on-one and are eventually redeemed by the end.
  • Members of The Fold revealed in their official podcast that they made a new song for the opening of this season.[8]
  • Starting with the episode "Questing for Quests," there is an intro recapping the events of the previous episode with the new remix of The Weekend Whip, called The Wicked Whip, in the background.
    • This is also the first season not to have the traditional "Previously on Ninjago" section at the beginning of the episodes. This was done to allow more time for the episode itself, due to the shorter runtime.[9]
    • The intro says the episode name but does not say the episode number. This is because hearing a large number such as "Episode 103" could be intimidating for newer viewers.[10]
    • This is the first season since Season 7 to feature songs by The Fold in the opening.
  • A new vehicle called the Land Bounty appears in this season. Destiny's Bounty is also used.
  • When the entire Ice Chapter was made available on TV On Demand on November 1, 2019, it was not made available on Cartoon Network's app and website, for unknown reasons.
    • It was later made available on the CN app and website on November 25, 2019.
  • Each episode has a different character announcing the episode's title at the end of the opening.
    • This season marks the first time that Jay, Nya, Kai, P.I.X.A.L., Aspheera, and Vex have introduced the title cards at the beginning of the episodes.
  • Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu was released in the summer/fall of 2019, making it the third time two seasons aired in the same year.
    • The first time was in 2015 with Tournament of Elements in the spring and Possession in the summer.
    • The second time was in 2018 with Sons of Garmadon in the spring and Hunted in the summer.
  • Tommy Andreasen revealed in a tweet that 2D "anime-style" animation would be incorporated in the second half of Season 11.[11]
    • "The Absolute Worst" has 2D animation scenes with Ultra Violet, The Mechanic, and a new character, Fugi-Dove. Andreasen referred to this as "anime-style."
    • "The Last of the Formlings" has 2D animation scenes depicting Akita's story.
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, the "lighter tone" of the season was specifically requested by some broadcasters as "Ninjago had become too dark during the Oni Trilogy."[12]
  • Kai and Nya are the only Ninja to haven't shown their Forbidden Spinjitzu powers. However, in "Vengeance is Mine!," Kai was unable to use the ability since his powers were stolen by Aspheera during that point in time.
    • Although, an animation glitch in "Vengeance is Mine!" briefly showed Kai with Forbidden Spinjitzu powers.

  • This is the first season since her debut in Season 2 where Misako does not make an appearance.

Focus characters

Focus characters[]

Protagonists[]

  • This season has no definite focus character. Every ninja (except Jay) receives some kind of sub-plot.[16]
  • Being the mascot character of the season, Kai is a prominent character in both chapters since Aspheera stealing his Fire was the primary cause of this season's events to happen, and his powerlessness arc was emphasized throughout both chapters.
    • He was focused on in episodes 103, 107, 110, 112, 115, 123, and 128.
      • He was focused in episode 103 when Aspheera takes an interest in Kai, calling him a fire elemental and steals his fire powers, leaving him unconscious after his powers are taken.
      • His anger and suffering for losing his powers and his relationship with Jake was the focus and main storyline for Episode 107.
      • His anger and relationship towards Master Wu and how he forgives Wu in the end is the main focus in Episode 110.
      • He was focused in episode 112 when he got a chance to possess the scroll, he tried to pick it up and use it, but the scroll had no effect since his elemental powers were stolen.
      • His powers return in Episode 115 and the storyline revolves around his relationship with Sorla, blaming himself for not being able to help the village, his abilities to produce fire, his regret for losing them, and recovers his elemental powers but in a weak state and lit up the Hearth Fire that was put out by Grimfax and is entitled as "Fire Maker" by the Ice Fishers.
      • He was the focus in the first half of Episode 123 during his rivalry and fight with Boreal and his regret for not being strong enough to stop the dragon is part of his motivation, as Kai felt guilty for not being able to save the village being frozen leads to Kai and the other ninja to go after Lloyd on the Land Bounty, which is the main storyline for the first half of the episode.
      • He has also shared focus for Episode 128 alongside Lloyd, Zane, and Nya, when he ends his rivalry with Boreal and fully regains his Fire when destroying the ice dragon with his powers.
  • Zane is another prominent character in both chapters despite being the least recurring member of the team in the season.
    • He was focused on in episodes 99, 102, 111, 112, 125, and 128.
      • In Episode 99 his visions are revealed and his role is introduced.
      • In Episode 102 he is a vital part of retrieving the engine piece for the Land Bounty.
      • In Episode 111 he is focused on during his heist to get the second scroll.
      • In Episode 112 he is focused on due to him defeating the Pyro Vipers and his banishment at the hands of Aspheera.
      • Episode 125 reveals how he was manipulated by Vex into becoming the Ice Emperor and ruling the Never-Realm.
      • He is one of the four focuses for Episode 128 as the Ice Emperor and his path to redemption.
    • He also had some focuses as the Ice Emperor in episodes 114, 121, 126, and 127.
  • Lloyd is mostly focused on in the Ice Chapter.
    • He was focused on in episodes 116, 118, 123, 127, and one of the four focuses for Episode 128.
      • In Episode 116 his journey to find Zane begins and he encounters Akita, who he thought was a regular wolf at first.
      • In Episode 118 the main plot is his attempt to find the mech and stopped the Ice Bird.
      • In Episode 123 he is focused on in the second half of the story with him finding out the identity of the Ice Emperor.
      • In Episode 127 his resistance with Kataru and Grimfax is developed and he confronts Zane.
      • In Episode 128 alongside the other three focus ninja, he is focused on in his battle against Zane and his farewell with Akita.
  • Cole has the most separate episode appearances in Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu however his core relevance is narrowed down to only a few episodes.
    • Cole is focused on in Episode 119 and Episode 120.
      • His relationship with Krag is one of the many storylines that are developed from episodes 119 to 128.
  • Nya is not a prominent character in the Fire Chapter but has a side story in the Ice Chapter.
    • Although she didn't have episodes completely based on her, her ability to control Ice as well as Water and her relationship with Sorla was developed.
      • She had focus moments in episodes 116, 119, and 128. However, she was the focus of none.
      • She also a major role in Episode 108, as she almost distracted the Pyro Vipers long enough for Lloyd to get the Forbidden Scroll.
      • In Episode 128, she remembers what Sorla told her before about being herself, and use her powers to control ice and temporarily immobilize Boreal.
  • Master Wu is a main character in the Fire Chapter but isn't present for the majority of the Ice Chapter.
    • He is focused on in Episode 99, 109 and 113 and he was a shared focus of Episode 110.
      • His effort to prevent the ninja from laziness was focused in Episode 99.
      • His backstory with Aspheera was focused on in Episode 109.
      • His relationship with Kai was focused on in Episode 110.
      • His anger towards losing Zane and his reluctance to let the ninja go to the Never-Realm is focused on in Episode 113.
  • Jay's role was very minor in this season and is focused on the least out of the ninja this season, he is more prominent in the Fire Chapter than the Ice Chapter, providing more comedy in the Ice Chapter rather than action.
    • This is most likely because of the next season, Season 12: Prime Empire focusing on Jay entirely.
    • He had a few focus moments in episodes 103 and 105, especially episode 103, where he accidentally unleashes Aspheera.
  • P.I.X.A.L. is a supporting character in the Fire Chapter and isn't present for the majority of the Ice Chapter.
    • She is focused on in Episode 124, although she had focus moments in episode 112, and 113.
      • In Episode 112 her reaction from the loss of Zane and her minor battle with Aspheera are briefly focused on.
      • In Episode 113 her outburst from the loss of Zane and her aiding the ninja travel to the Never-Realm are focused on briefly.
      • In Episode 124 her battle with the Preeminent and her quest for forgiveness from Wu are the main plot points.

Antagonists[]

Fire Chapter[]

  • Aspheera served as the main antagonist of the Fire Chapter.
  • Char served as the secondary antagonist of the Fire Chapter.
  • Other important antagonists are Fire Fang and the Elemental Cobras.
  • The Pyro Vipers serve as the villainous faction of the Fire Chapter.

Ice Chapter[]

  • Aspheera served as the overarching antagonist of the Ice Chapter, being the one who banished Zane and hid scepter to the Never-Realm.
  • General Vex served as the main antagonist of the Ice Chapter, tricking Zane into being evil.
  • Zane, as the Ice Emperor, served as the secondary antagonist of the Ice Chapter. He reformed in "Awakenings" after recovering his memories and destroying his scepter.
  • Other important antagonists include Grimfax, the Ice Behemoth (episode 121), and Boreal, though Grimfax redeemed himself in "A Fragile Hope."
  • The Blizzard Samurai serve as the villainous faction of the Ice Chapter.
