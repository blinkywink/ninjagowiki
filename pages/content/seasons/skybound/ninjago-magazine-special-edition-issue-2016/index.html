This is a special edition issue of the Ninjago Magazine. It includes 30421 Skybound Plane. Destiny Jay, Nadakhan, Flintlocke and Clancee appear on the cover.

In terms of content, the magazine is similar to the later-launched XXL line: it does not include a comic, and instead of a unique toy, it comes with a previously released polybag. Most of the pages of the magazine are dedicated to various activities and a guide to the episodes of the series up to season five.
