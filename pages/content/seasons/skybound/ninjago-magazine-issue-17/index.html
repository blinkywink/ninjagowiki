Untitled issue is the 17th issue of the Ninjago Magazine. It includes 891617 Titanium Zane and Destiny Kai appears on the cover. It was released on September 24, 2016.

The Echo!

The Echo![]

When Jay and Nya were hiding from Nadakhan on a Lighthouse Island, they had a lot of free time. When Jay mentions Zane, Echo Zane asks ninja to tell more about him. Jay tells Echo about how Wu found Zane and he joined the Ninja, and how he found out about him being a Nindroid and how he was the first to find his True Potential. When Jay mentioned Zane's "funny" switch, Echo asks if maybe he also has one, so Nya starts searching for his blueprints.

Meanwhile at the Misfortune's Keep, Clancee is unhappy about the fact that after Flintlocke's riot, Clancee's mop went to him. During his dialogue with Nadakhan, he almost said "I wish" which irritates the djinn.

Later, Jay tells Echo about how Zane was damaged during his fight with Great Devourer, how he shared his heart with P.I.X.A.L., and how he became the Titanium Ninja. At the end, Nya finally finds the blueprints and activates Echo's "funny" switch, after which the Nindroid sings the same song Zane once sang.
