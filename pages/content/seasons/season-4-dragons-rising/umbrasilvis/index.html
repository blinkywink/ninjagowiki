Overview

For the village, see Umbrasilvis (village).

Umbrasilvis was one of the realms[1] before the Merge and is now part of the Merged Lands. It is a hilly, dark realm whose primary location is a village of the same name.

History

History[]

Background[]

At an unknown point in time, Umbrasilvis came into existence as one of the realms. It would somehow end up connected to the Realm of Eternal Dawn via the pit and fountain in the village. At some point, the First Spinjitzu Master visited the realm[2] and hid the red fragment of the Weapon of Desolation in the Tree of Burdens.[3] Five centuries before the Quest for Desolation, a flag would be hung on a wall in the village.[4] Following the Merge years later, Umbrasilvis would end up across the water from the Land of Monsters.[3][1]

Dying to Live (flashback)[]

After Beatrix escaped from the Land of Monsters, Dr. LaRow found her in the hills of Umbrasilvis.

Before Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

After the Liberation of Imperium, one of the Loyalists went undercover in Umbrasilvis and took the charge of the village as the Grand Inquisitor.[5]

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

A Mutation Monster was summoned in Umbrasilvis by the Weapon of Desolation. This prompted the Explorer's Club to dispatch a specialized team, led by Reginald Slitherbottom, to the village to capture the creature. Just before the Explorer's Club reached their destination, Frak, Sora, Wyldfyre, and Riyu emerged from a fountain after falling through a pit in the Land of Eternal Dawn. Upon their arrival in Umbrasilvis, the ninja stumbled upon the Explorer's Club and soon found themselves face to face with Reginald Slitherbottom, much to Frak's unease.
