Overview

This article is about the fourth season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising. For other uses, see Season 4.

The fourth season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising is preceded by Season 3 and Crossroads' Crosstalk. Lloyd and the Arc Dragon of Life serve as the mascot characters, the Chief Loyalist and Beatrix serve as the main antagonists, and the Elemental Monsters and Loyalists serve as the villainous factions.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • According to Kevin Burke, this season's theme is "connection."[6]
  • In June of 2025, Bethany Brown shared an image on a currently unavailable Instagram story confirming that voice acting for this season has been finished.[7]
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, this season will reveal more about Lloyd's Elemental Power.[8]
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt confirmed that the season will be darker than the previous season.[9]
  • At San Diego Comic Con, it was stated that the season will push the ninja to more emotional places and explore more of the Merged Realms.[10][11]
    • It was also announced there that Arin, Sora, Frak and Wyldfyre will have their chance to shine and interact more with each other during the season.[10][11]
  • This season will indirectly explain how Elemental Masters of Balance elements, such as Kizzy, Obscuria, and Skylor, retained their powers after the Source Dragon of Balance's death.[12]
  • The opening sequence's song, "The Merged Whip," was first hinted at during the 2025 San Diego Comic Con, and first heard during an Okoo Tv spot.
  • The main poster for the season marks the first time that all six original Ninja have appeared in a poster together as Ninja in Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the first time since Seabound.
    • This is also the first time all old and current members of the team are on one poster as Ninja. Except P.I.X.AL. for some reason.
    • Additionally, this is the first poster released for a season to not feature Ras on the first poster for the season as he was on the first posters for Season 1, Season 2 and Season 3.
  • Although they are simply younger variants of Lloyd, this season marks the first time Jillian Michaels has voiced Lloyd in Ninjago: Dragons Rising, and the first time since Season 7: The Hands of Time that she has voiced the character in some capacity.
    • Additionally, this marks the first time that Jillian has voiced Lloyd with his redesign, albeit in child and tween forms.
    • This is the first time since Season 14: Seabound where both Sam Vincent and Jillian Michaels provided voices in the same season.
    • This is also Jillian Michaels' first speaking role in Ninjago: Dragons Rising.
  • Towards the end of the intro, Lloyd's silhouette after unleashing his power depicts him in his Ghost Hunter suit.
  • On April 1, 2026, writers Kevin Burke and Chris "Doc" Wyatt each made a two truths and one lie post as a sneak peek at the season on Bluesky.
    • Burke's three statements were:
      • Cole will be judged by a new friend who won't speak to him.
      • Zane will dance with squirrels.
      • Frak will eat pies from a shrub.[13]
        • The second line is a lie, as Cole starts talking to a smiley face in his prison cell in "Dying to Live", and Frak can be seen eating pies from a shrub in "The Lean of a Tree".
    • Wyatt's three statements were:
      • The Ninja will learn pickle-ball.
      • The Ninja will send a robot on vacation.
      • The Ninja will become barf.[14]
        • The first statement was a lie, as Kai, Jay and Nya along with the Land of Madness Monastery and Jay's mech get barfed out by the Kwilish they were stuck inside of, and the three Ninja later pacified the Administration's defense robots by giving them "fake-cations".
  • So far this is the only season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising to not have Arin use his grapple gun.
