Overview

This article is about the fourth season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising. For other uses, see Season 4.
Season numbering for this installment varies between sources. See here for more information.
“I'm the Green Ninja, but I've never discovered that True Potential. Plus, this is the only chance we have to fix the messed up state of Elemental Energy in our world, so I have to take the risk.”
— Lloyd to Arin, "Seed and Soil"

The fourth season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising is preceded by Season 3 and Crossroads' Crosstalk. Lloyd and the Arc Dragon of Life serve as the mascot characters, Dr. LaRow serves as the main antagonist for the first part of the season, and the Elemental Monsters and Loyalists serve as the villainous factions of the season.

The first teaser was released on January 31, 2026. On March 17, 2026, LEGO announced that the season would be released on April 2, 2026. Episodes 1-10 were released on April 2, 2026 on Netflix in the United States, and episodes 11-20 will be released on September 2026.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • According to Kevin Burke, this season's theme is "connection."[10]
  • In June of 2025, Bethany Brown shared an image on a currently unavailable Instagram story confirming that voice acting for this season has been finished.[11]
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, this season will reveal more about Lloyd's Elemental Power.[12]
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt confirmed that the season will be darker than the previous season.[13]
  • At San Diego Comic Con, it was stated that the season will push the ninja to more emotional places and explore more of the Merged Realms.[14][15]
    • It was also announced there that the young ninja will have their chance to shine and interact more with each other during the season.[14][15]
  • This season will indirectly explain how Elemental Masters of Balance elements, such as Kizzy, Obscuria, and Skylor, retained their powers after the Source Dragon of Balance's death.[16]
  • The opening sequence's song, "The Merged Whip," was first hinted at during the 2025 San Diego Comic Con, and first heard during an Okoo TV spot.
    • LEGO did not request a full version of this song.[17]
  • The main poster for the season marks the first time that all six original Ninja have appeared in a poster together as Ninja in Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the first time since Season 14: Seabound.
    • This is also the first time all old and current members of the team are on one poster as Ninja except P.I.X.A.L.
    • Additionally, this is the first poster released for a season to not feature Ras on the first poster for the season as he was on the first posters for Season 1, Season 2 and Season 3.
  • Although they are simply younger variants of Lloyd, this season marks the first time Jillian Michaels has voiced Lloyd in Ninjago: Dragons Rising, and the first time since Season 7: The Hands of Time that she has voiced the character in some capacity.
    • Additionally, this marks the first time that Jillian has voiced Lloyd with his redesign, albeit in child and tween forms.
    • This is the first time since Season 14: Seabound where both Sam Vincent and Jillian Michaels provided voices in the same season.
    • This is also Jillian Michaels' first speaking role in Ninjago: Dragons Rising.
      • Fittingly enough, the first role she voiced for the original series was also Lloyd in Season 1: Rise of the Snakes.
  • Towards the end of the intro, Lloyd's silhouette after unleashing his power depicts him in his Ghost Hunter suit.
  • On April 1, 2026, writers Kevin Burke and Chris "Doc" Wyatt each made a two truths and one lie post as a sneak peek at the season on Bluesky.
    • Burke's three statements were:
      • Cole will be judged by a new friend who won't speak to him.
      • Zane will dance with squirrels.
      • Frak will eat pies from a shrub.[18]
        • The second line is a lie, as Cole starts talking to a smiley face in his prison cell in "Dying to Live", and Frak can be seen eating pies from a shrub in "The Lean of a Tree".
    • Wyatt's three statements were:
      • The Ninja will learn pickle-ball.
      • The Ninja will send a robot on vacation.
      • The Ninja will become barf.[19]
        • The first statement was a lie, as Kai, Jay and Nya along with the Monastery of Madness and Jay's mech get barfed out by the Quealiches they were stuck inside of, and the three Ninja later pacified the Administration's defense robots by giving them "fake-cations."
        • Agent Donovan does mention pickle-ball in "Tonight We Hunt Monsters," although the ninja never actually learn the sport.
  • So far, this is the only season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising to not have Arin use his Grapple Gun.
  • As of this season, all 17 of the realms were finally seen on screen, 11 years after the concept was first introduced.
  • This is the second season of the franchise where the Ninja used their suits from the previous season more than their current suits, the first being Legacy of the Green Ninja.
  • This is the first season to pick up immediately where the previous season ended.
  • Despite Lloyd being both the main protagonist and mascot character, his normal minifigure is only available in a spinner set. All other appearances in sets are him in his Draconus Form.
Focus Characters

Focus Characters[]

Protagonists[]

  • Lloyd serves as the main protagonist of this season and is one of the mascot characters.
    • Lloyd is focused on in Episodes 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 10.
  • Jay and Frak have main character roles.
    • Jay is focused on in Episodes 2, 3, 4 and 10.
    • Frak is focused on in Episodes 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7.
  • Cole, Nya and Sora have secondary character roles.
    • Cole is focused on in Episodes 6, 8 and 9.
    • Nya is focused on in Episodes 4, 7 and 9.
    • Sora is focused on in Episodes 5, 6 and 7.
  • Kai, Zane, Arin, Wyldfyre, Riyu and Rontu are all main-supporting characters.
    • Kai is focused on in Episodes 2, 6, 7, 9 and 10.
    • Zane is focused on in Episodes 4 and 10.
    • Wyldfyre is focused on in Episode 4.
    • Arin is focused on in Episodes 6 and 10.
    • Riyu is focused on in Episodes 3 and 5.
    • Rontu is focused on in Episode 5.

Antagonists[]

Part 1[]

  • Dr. LaRow serves as the main antagonist of Part 1. She was the leader of the Loyalists alliance and the one who came up with the plan of finding the Weapon of Desolation to make the Elemental Monsters their army, with the ultimate goal of helping Beatrix reclaim the throne of Imperium.
  • Beatrix serves as the overarching antagonist of Part 1. Her goal was to reclaim the throne of Imperium with the help of her Loyalists and her army of Elemental Monsters controlled by the Weapon of Desolation.
  • Sora's parents serve as minor antagonists in Part 1.
    • They appear briefly at the end of Episode 10 where they hide their Loyalist masks and head to the Monastery of Spinjitzu.
  • Other antagonists of Part 1 include Enemy Lloyd, Johnny Courageous, Blake Donovan, the Grand Inquisitor, the Lead Loyalist, Bonnie and the Administration Agents.
  • Lord Ras plays a minor role in Episode 10.
    • In Episode 10, Ras arrives through the Portal Gate to the Land of Eternal Dawn with a new Source Dragon Icon.
