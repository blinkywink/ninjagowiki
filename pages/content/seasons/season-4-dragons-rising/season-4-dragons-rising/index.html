Overview

This article is about the fourth season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising. For other uses, see Season 4.
Season numbering for this installment varies between sources. See here for more information.
“Lloyd Garmadon was a hero, a leader, who inspired us to achieve the peace we have today, and this is why not only the First Order of the United Lands to honor of the Legendary Green Ninja, but to honor those who carry on his ideals, never has our world been safer and never has our future been brighter than today.”
— Percival Tartigrade, "Dangerous Connections"

The fourth season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising is preceded by Season 3 and Crossroads' Crosstalk and succeeded by Season 5. Lloyd and the Arc Dragon of Life serve as the mascot characters, Dr. LaRow, General Cryptor and the Weapon of Desolation serve as the main antagonists, and the Elemental Monsters and the Loyalists serve as the villainous factions of the season.

The first teaser was released on January 31, 2026. On March 17, 2026, LEGO announced that the season would be released on April 2, 2026. Episodes 1-10 were released on April 2, 2026 on Netflix in the United States. Episodes 11-20 were released on Tubi on July 22, 2026. The same episodes were then released officially on Netflix on September 3, 2026.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • According to Kevin Burke, this season's theme is "connection."[17]
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt confirmed that the season will be darker than the previous season.[18]
  • At San Diego Comic-Con 2025, it was stated that the season will push the ninja to more emotional places and explore more of the Merged Lands.[19][20]
    • It was also announced there that the young ninja will have their chance to shine and interact more with each other during the season.[19][20]
  • This season will indirectly explain how Elemental Masters of Balance elements, such as Kizzy, Obscuria, and Skylor, retained their powers after the Source Dragon of Balance's death.[21]
  • The opening sequence's song, "The Merged Whip," was first hinted during at San Diego Comic-Con 2025, and first heard during an Okoo TV spot.
    • LEGO did not request a full version of this song.[22]
  • This season marks the first time the Weekend Whip is used in an intro in Dragons Rising.
    • This season also marks the first time the Weekend Whip is used in an intro since Season 15: Crystalized.
  • The main poster for the season marks the first time that all six original Ninja have appeared in a poster together as Ninja in Ninjago: Dragons Rising and the first time since Season 14: Seabound.
    • This is also the first time all old and current members of the team are on one poster as Ninja except P.I.X.A.L..
    • Additionally, this is the first poster released for a season to not feature Ras on the first poster for the season as he was on the first posters for Season 1, Season 2 and Season 3.
  • Although they are simply younger variants of Lloyd, this season marks the first time Jillian Michaels has voiced Lloyd in Ninjago: Dragons Rising, and the first time since Season 7: The Hands of Time that she has voiced the character in some capacity.
    • Additionally, this marks the first time that Jillian has voiced Lloyd with his redesign, albeit in child and tween forms.
    • This is the first time since Season 14: Seabound where both Sam Vincent and Jillian Michaels provided voices in the same season.
    • This is also Jillian Michaels' first speaking role in Ninjago: Dragons Rising.
      • Fittingly enough, the first role she voiced for the original series was also Lloyd in Season 1: Rise of the Snakes.
  • Towards the end of the intro, Lloyd's silhouette after unleashing his power depicts him in his Ghost Hunter suit.
  • On April 1, 2026, writers Kevin Burke and Chris "Doc" Wyatt each made a two truths and one lie post as a sneak peek at the season on Bluesky.
    • Burke's three statements were:
      • Cole will be judged by a new friend who won't speak to him.
      • Zane will dance with squirrels.
      • Frak will eat pies from a shrub.[23]
        • The second line is a lie, as Cole starts talking to a smiley face in his prison cell in "Dying to Live", and Frak can be seen eating pies from a shrub in "The Lean of a Tree".
    • Wyatt's three statements were:
      • The Ninja will learn pickle-ball.
      • The Ninja will send a robot on vacation.
      • The Ninja will become barf.[24]
        • The first statement was a lie, as Kai, Jay and Nya along with the Monastery of Madness and Jay's mech get barfed out by the Quealiches they were stuck inside of, and the three Ninja later pacified the Administration's defense robots by giving them "fake-cations."
        • Agent Donovan does mention pickle-ball in "Tonight We Hunt Monsters," although the ninja never actually learn the sport.
  • As of this season, all 17 of the realms were finally seen on screen, 11 years after the concept was first introduced.
  • This is the third season of the franchise where the Ninja used their suits from the previous season more than their current suits, the first being Season 2: Legacy of the Green Ninja, and the second being Season 13: Master of the Mountain.
  • This is the first season to pick up immediately where the previous season ended.
  • Despite Lloyd being both the main protagonist and mascot character, his normal minifigure is only available in a spinner set. All other appearances in sets are him in his Draconus Form.
  • So far, Season 4 is the only season of Dragons Rising in which Heatwave and Zanth isn't present.
  • On July 22, 2026, Part 2 was briefly released onto the free streaming service Tubi, but was then removed hours later.
  • In the Part 1 intro, the Weapon of Desolation glows green. In the Part 2 intro, it's orange.
  • Arin and Sora fighting off the Acid and Fire Monsters respectively remains the same.
  • In the intro of Part 2, Zane throws his shurikens, which is physically impossible for him to do since Part 2's intro opted to use the Draconus Forms, which means that Kai, Cole and Zane have their claws, which prevent their minifigures from holding any accessories.
    • Kai is the only one of the three who has the extended claws.
  • Because Wyldfyre and Riyu were swapped for Zilvar and a Dragon Beast, not only is the background more visible, but the Ice Monster is now absent in the intro.
    • Zilvar is riding a random Dragon Beast, not Grimtak.
  • There have been changes between the intros of Part 1 and Part 2:
    • The Part 2 intro ends with Ninjago City as the setting compared to Part 1 in the Whispering Wood.
    • The Part 2 intro ends with Riyu in his most recent form as the featured dragon, unlike Part 1 with the Arc Dragon of Life. Both dragons face opposite ways in their respective intros.
    • Compared with the Part 1 intro, everyone except Lloyd and Riyu is now facing a slightly different angle, with Arin, Sora and Wyldfyre still in the same pose as before.
    • In the ending of the Part 2 intro, Jay is now directly above Arin, compared to being beside him in Part 1.
    • Lloyd's Draconus Form claws glow like before, but not like how his hands do in the Part 1 intro nor in "Birthright" to depict him powering up.
    • The Part 2 intro ends with all 6 Draconus Forms on-screen, and the first time everyone else's fully glows up since Lloyd was the only one who fully glowed up in the Part 1 episodes.
  • P.I.X.A.L. and Euphrasia are the only ninja that do not appear in either of the intros of Season 4.
  • All 2026 Ninjago: Legacy sets make an appearance in the season:
    • 71866 Ninja Character Display has the Monastery of Spinjitzu.
    • 71858 Four Weapons Blacksmith has the Four Weapons Blacksmith, which appears in flashbacks.
    • 71861 The Old Town has the Old Town.
    • 71860 Lloyd's Titan Mech has the Titan Mech, which appears in the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms.
    • 71867 X-1 Ninja Charger has the Prototype X-1.
    • Furthermore, multiple Legacy minifigures have their designs used in the season, which include: Wu in his white robe, Ray, and Cryptor.
  • This is the first season in which the headbands for Kai, Nya and Lloyd appear all season long.
  • This season marks General Cryptor's first physical appearance in the main series since special episode "Day of the Departed".

Part 1[]

  • Dr. LaRow serves as the main antagonist of Part 1. She was the leader of the Loyalists alliance and the one who came up with the plan of finding the Weapon of Desolation to make the Elemental Monsters their army, with the ultimate goal of helping Beatrix reclaim the throne of Imperium. She was focused on in Episodes 1, 5, 8, and 10.
  • Beatrix serves as the overarching antagonist of Part 1. Her goal was to reclaim the throne of Imperium with the help of her Loyalists and her army of Elemental Monsters controlled by the Weapon of Desolation. She was focused on in Episodes 5, 8, 9, and 10.
  • Other antagonists of Part 1 include Blake Donovan, Johnny Courageous, the Lead Loyalist, Bonnie, The Grand Inquisitor, Enemy Lloyd, the Administration Agents and Sora's parents.
    • Blake Donovan was focused on in Episode 3.
    • Johnny Courageous was focused on in Episodes 5 and 7.
    • The Lead Loyalist was focused on in Episode 6.
    • Bonnie was focused on in Episode 6.
    • The Grand Inquisitor was focused on in Episode 7.
    • Enemy Lloyd was focused on in Episode 8.
    • Sora's parents only appear at the end of Episode 10, in which they are revealed to be Loyalists and are shown arriving to the monastery to hide themselves.
  • Lord Ras plays a minor role in Episode 10.
    • In Episode 10, Ras arrives through the portal gate to the Land of Eternal Dawn with a new Source Dragon Icon.
