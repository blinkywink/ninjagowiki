Overview

Thief Trouble! is the 143rd issue of the Ninjago Magazine. It includes L0002205 Dragon Zane and Draconus Jay appears on the cover.

The Shintaro Job

The Shintaro Job[]

Shortly before Thunderfang's release, Fedulian met with the bounty hunter known as Rogue regarding another successful hunt. After paying the bounty hunter, Fedulian mentioned that another client of his was looking for a cursed skull buried deep in the mountains of Shintaro - a job the bounty hunter quickly accepted.

Sometime later, as Rogue scaled the mountain range upon which the Ivory City rested, he encountered the Freebooters. Initially, the Freebooters and their leader, Rapton, attempted to rob Rogue, but were effortlessly defeated when Rogue revealed his powers over Lightning. Impressed by this mysterious bounty hunter's combat prowess, Rapton proposed that they join forces in securing the cursed skull, admitting that while he and his Freebooters retained numerical superiority, Rogue was a far more capable combatant than all of them put together. Rogue accepted this proposal and the group resumed their advance.

Rather than flying up towards the Ivory City itself, Rapton led the group towards a cave in the side of the mountain, explaining that this passageway would lead them straight to the heart of the mountain without the risk of being spotted by Shintaro's Winged Guards. Unfortunately, Rapton did not foresee encountering a flock of Dire Bats, forcing him to quickly improvise his plans. Rapton then ordered the Freebooters to utilize their energy cords to wrangle up the bats like they would with dragons. However, this task proved to be harder than anticipated, with the hunters struggling to wrangle even a single dire bat. Rogue then stepped in, grasping the energy cords and capturing all the bats by himself, greatly impressing Rapton. Rogue then dispatched one of the Freebooters to fly off with the Dire Bats while the rest continued onward.

Moments later, the group disembarked and Rapton led them through the Dungeons of Shintaro to the Skull Keep, explaining the history of this place as they went and how it once housed the legendary Blades of Deliverance. Within the Skull Keep, the group discovered a statue containing the cursed skull that they were looking for. While one Freebooter questioned how they would separate it from the statue, Rogue immediately kicked the skull off, proclaiming that he wasn't afraid of any "superstitious fiddlesticks." Before they could celebrate, however, a group of Munce warriors interrupted, ordering Rogue and the Freebooters to drop the Munce Master's skull. In defiance, Rogue handed the skull off to Rapton and declared that he could handle the Munce warriors - at least, until their reinforcements arrived seconds later. Realizing that they were now completely outclassed, Rogue shouted for his allies to retreat.

Fleeing into the Dungeons of Shintaro, the Freebooters scrambled into a minecart with the Munce in fast pursuit. As the carts turned a corner, Rogue flipped a nearby switch to change tracks and flipped it again to send the Munce off in another direction. Unfortunately, he and the Freebooters soon came to a dead end, leaving them at risk of encountering the Munce again. Fortunately, Rogue felt a draft seeping through a corner of the wall and used his lightning to blow open a hole through the mountain. Although they now had a way out, Rapton noted that they were still far too high up to simply jump out. Luckily, the explosion had caught the eye of the Claw Hunter that Rogue had sent off earlier to dispatch the Dire Bats, and the hunter brought his Hover chariot over to collect his allies. As the group flew off, a proud Rapton expressed his gratitude towards Rogue and promised him that they would split the bounty on the skull and that if Rogue ever needed a hand, Rapton would be there to provide assistance.
