Overview

“I thought we were supposed to pack essential items."
"This is essential! How am I supposed to quest and stay hydrated at the same time, Zane?”
— Zane and Cole about the guzzler, "A Rocky Start"

The soda guzzler is a drinking hat used by Cole, Wu's chicken and an unknown chicken in the Speedway Five-Billion. It is known for being used by Cole during the time he and the other ninja got lazy after the Oni's invasion and Wu's chicken.

History

History[]

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Wasted True Potential[]

Cole is using his guzzler while playing Lava Zombies. He keeps it on until the fight with Wu.

Questing for Quests[]

Cole wears the guzzler while in the hot tub, thinking of a quest along with the other ninja.

A Rocky Start[]

The ninja, while packing, grab their things for their new quest to go to the Desert of Doom. Cole quickly grabs the guzzler. While slurping he and the ninja come across an oasis and have a picnic there. When Beohernie shows up and chases the ninja, Cole trips, causing it to fall off. He has no choice but to leave it behind as Beohernie swallows it.

The Belly of the Beast[]

When Beohernie hurls out items, Cole's guzzler was among them. It landed on his head.

Awakenings (flashback)[]

When Zane remembered who he was, Cole's guzzler appeared in some of his flashbacks.

Prime Empire[]

The Speedway Five-Billion[]

A chicken similar to Wu's is seen wearing it in the crowd of the stands in the Speedway Five-Billion.

Master of the Mountain[]

Shintaro[]

Cole wears his guzzler while playing a fixed version of Prime Empire and a smaller version is seen on Wu's chicken.

Seabound[]

A Big Splash[]

Cole wears the guzzler until Nya walks into the room, shortly after the water in the bottles next to him blowup causing him to fall off the couch.
