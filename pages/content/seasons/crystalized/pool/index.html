Pool is a cue sport in Ninjago. Pool tables are mostly seen in bars.

History

History[]

Possession[]

Ghost Story[]

Morro enters the Biker Tavern and saw some men playing pool. He later used the pool table and the Staff of the First Spinjitzu Master to find more clues for the Scroll of Airjitzu and the Tomb of the First Spinjitzu Master.

Skybound[]

Public Enemy Number One[]

When Jay enters the tavern hoping to find food, a pool table is seen vacant.

Sons of Garmadon[]

The Oni and the Dragon[]

Zane and Cole enter Laughy's, while Cole sang Glow-Worm, the Sons of Garmadon members were playing pool.

True Potential[]

Several bar patrons played pool at Laughy's when the Ninja were there to celebrate their perceived victory over the Sons of Garmadon during the Oni resurrection ritual.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Wasted True Potential[]

The ninja were right next to a pool table while playing Lava Zombies.

Prime Empire[]

Ninjago Confidential[]

When Zane and P.I.X.A.L. went to Laughy's, Benny and a henchman were playing pool.

Seabound[]

Master of the Sea[]

Zane, Lloyd and Jay were teaching Benthomaar how to play pool while they were talking in the relaxation room.

Crystalized[]

A Painful Promise[]

As one of several contests she did with Wu, Aspheera chose a pool. Aspheera got every ball into the nets but one. She then nudged the table to get the last one in.
