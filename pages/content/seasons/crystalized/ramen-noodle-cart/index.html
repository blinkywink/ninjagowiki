Overview

The Ramen noodle cart is a cart in Ninjago that sells ramen noodles.

History

History[]

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Questing for Quests[]

After the ninja had gotten lost while trying to find the illegal bootlegging operation at the wharfs, they asked the Ramen noodle cart owner for directions, and he pointed them in the direction they should go.

The News Never Sleeps![]

Nelson and Antonia biked through the streets of Ninjago City, which were full of Elemental Cobras. They passed the carts, which were on fire.

Powerless[]

When Kai was talking to Jake, a destroyed noodle cart was behind him.

Prime Empire[]

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

A street vendor was handing out ramen to a local.

Ninjago Confidential[]

The Ramen noodle cart was seen in front of Laughy's.

Seabound[]

Papergirl[]

A woman pushed her noodle cart while running away from Merlopians, who were attacking Ninjago City.

Crystalized[]

Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5![]

Okino purchased a bowl of noodles from the noodle cart.

Safe Haven[]

The noodle cart owner was making noodles for the resistance.

Compatible[]

Skylor used the noodle cart to cook noodles.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Blood Moon[]

After smelling noodles, Riyu saw a noodle cart and ran towards it with Arin and Sora following him.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

Monster in the Monastery[]

The noodle cart was in front of the Explorer's Clubhouse building in Old Town.

Appearance

Appearance[]

The Ramen noodle cart is a 3-wheeler cart, with a sign that is upheld by 2 wooden poles. A large noodle runs around one of the poles, and the image of a noodle bowl with eggs and noodle appears on the right of "Ramen" on the sign. It also has a handle to pull the cart from place to place.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Although the correct spelling is "Ramen," a type of Japanese noodle soup, the stand is erroneously named "Ramne" in Ninjargon throughout the series.
  • In Prime Empire, a woman worked at the Ramen noodle cart. It is unknown if she is the new owner or just an employee.
    • Later, in "Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5!", the Sushi Chef who appeared in "Racer Seven" was working there.
  • A similar cart that appears to sell noodles can be seen in the Sons of Garmadon episodes, "The Jade Princess" and "True Potential."
