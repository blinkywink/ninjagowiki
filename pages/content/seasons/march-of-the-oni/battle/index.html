Battle! is the 50th issue of the Ninjago Magazine. It includes 891950 Daddy No Legs and Legacy Zane appears on the cover.

Short Circuit

Short Circuit[]

On the Destiny's Bounty, Jay and Kai discuss the fact they have to work with Garmadon, while Garmadon was complaining to Lloyd and life is the Kryptarium Prison was pretty good. Suddenly, the Samurai Mech, left after a battle with Garmadon and Colossus on the roof of one of the buildings in Ninjago City, suddenly detected Garmadon on the board of the ship. Since the last task given to the machine was to attack Garmadon, it shoots some rockets into the Bounty. Garmadon was glad to have a battle to restore his power, so he jumped from the ship and attacked a mech with a system failure by himself. P.I.X.A.L. and Lloyd also jump to the mech to defunct it. However, when the systems of the mech detects that "command block" and Garmadon are fighting on the same side, the system finally gets a final error and the mech deactivates itself. At the end P.I.X.A.L. brings the mech to the bounty to reboot the system, while Garmadon complains that the fight is already over.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The comic reveals what happened to the Samurai Mech between "The Gilded Path" and "Into the Breach."
  • The comic shows that before fighting the ninja to restore his power, Garmadon already had a battle with Samurai Mech and already restored a fraction of his power, so he was already able to summon the Destruction daggers.

Errors[]

  • The Oni cloud appears to be lower than in the actual episode, but the battle happening in the comic still takes place only on the roof of a Ninjago City building, so the comic can still be considered canon.
