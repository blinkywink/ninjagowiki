LEGO Minecraft Trading Card Collection

LEGO Minecraft Trading Card Collection[]

The LEGO Minecraft Trading Card Collection is based around LEGO's Minecraft theme. However, as Kai appeared as a cameo in LEGO Minecraft set 21160 The Illager Raid, he also appears in some of the cards of this line with the same appearance as seen in the set. The line was created by Blue Ocean, and is available in various European countries. English cards are sold in Hungary.[2]

LEGO Toys "R" Us cards

LEGO Toys "R" Us cards[]

This series was released in Toys "R" Us stores in 2017. It contained cards based around various LEGO themes, including ten cards featuring Ninjago characters. In the UK, cards were available for purchase. In Spain, cards were given away as gifts-with-purchases.[6] The cards could be traded, or played with.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Although unofficial, the Wacky Packages trading card series has produced several parody stickers based around Ninjago:
    • A "Lugee Ninjagoo: Masters of Spitjitzu" sticker parodying Ninjago's first wave of sets
    • A "Ninja-go Deluxe Blending System" sticker parodying Ninjago and the company Ninja (known for producing blenders)
    • A "Loco Nunjago: No Mercy" sticker parodying The LEGO Ninjago Movie
