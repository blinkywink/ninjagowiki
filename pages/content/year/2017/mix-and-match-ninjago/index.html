Mix and Match Ninjago was a The LEGO Ninjago Movie-themed card game released in 2017.[1] It came with 24 cards and a game board/arena (with game rules and The LEGO Ninjago Movie set advertisements on the reverse side).[2] It was produced by the Vietnamese toy store chain MYKINGDOM.[3]

Official description

Official description[]

With the Ninjago card set, players will come to the Ninjago land and participate in the battle to protect peace between young Ninjas and the evil Garmadon.[3]
