Overview

Not to be confused with the similarly titled episode.
“Tomorrow is April 1st. A highly unpredictable day. Except here in Denmark it ALREADY April 1st. In which case when I said that tomorrow is April 1st it may be a lie ... and it may be a highly predicable day. And is it May?!?!

Mwooaaaahahahaha! (I'll go to bed now).”

— Tommy Andreasen, X (2022)

Two truths, one lie is a tradition amongst the Ninjago team held every April 1st (purposefully in line with April Fools), where a member/members of the team post a message on social media with two truthful statements, and one false statement about the currently upcoming Ninjago season/instalment. Formerly, Tommy Andreasen would deliver the message, and currently, Chris "Doc" Wyatt and Kevin Burke each post one set of statements. Statements were formerly posted to X, but are now also posted to Bluesky.

  2. "Recurring bad guy(s) will return!"

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Tommy Andresen first announced the event the day before the first one (on March 31, 2017).[16]
  • Although he contemplated it,[17] Tommy Andreasen decided against doing the event in 2020, since "There [seemed] to be enough (too much) info out already."[18]
  • Instead of continuing the tradition in 2021, Tommy Andreasen decided to prank people by tricking them into posting a tweet with "#IgotFooledByTA," saying he would send those who did a drawing.[19]
  • In 2022, Tommy Andreasen made fun of the fact that in Danish time, it was already April 1st as opposed to other parts of the world still on March 31st.[20]
  • Tommy Andreasen once did two truths, one lie for another LEGO theme he worked on, Nexo Knights.[21]
  • Some of Tommy Andreasen's previous lies have since become true in Ninjago: Dragons Rising:
    • New ninja, Arin and Sora, would join the team.
    • Wyldfyre is able to verbally communicate with dragons, and even mentions in "The Spell at the Waterfall" that she can "speak dragon."
  • In 2025, Kevin Burke's lie mentioned the "Gates to Androzani," which is a reference to the British television series Doctor Who.
