The LEGO Ninjago Movie mini-movies are shorts released by Warner Bros. to promote The LEGO Ninjago Movie.

Mini-movies

Mini-movies[]

SDCC Greeting[]

Lloyd presents Garmadon with ideas to go to San Diego, but Garmadon can only think about conquering the locations.

Back to School[]

Nya tells the viewer her secrets to being a ninja while at Ninjago High School.

American Ninja Warrior[]

Jay participates in the game show, failing to complete the course's new "Ninjago Roll" obstacle.

Garmadon's Countdown[]

After firing Omar, Garmadon has the GPL Tech count down to the digital release of The LEGO Ninjago Movie.

Garmadon's Digital Shark Choir[]

Garmadon has his sharks perform Christmas carols to celebrate the digital release of The LEGO Ninjago Movie.

Garmadon's Video for Santa[]

GPL Tech tells Garmadon about the elusive blue ray, and Garmadon tells them to catch it, as a promotion for the DVD release of The LEGO Ninjago Movie.[1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The animation is slightly different from that of the film.
  • In "Back to School," it's shown that Nya keeps photos inside her locker of heroes that inspire her. The characters shown are Wyldstyle, Unikitty, and Wonder Woman from The LEGO Movie and Batgirl from The LEGO Batman Movie.
  • The "Garmadon's Countdown" video has a glitch where it freezes from the fifteen to eighteen second mark. There's also a problem with the video player in which the video freezes at thirty-three seconds despite the thirty-five second long runtime.
    • This is only the case on the YouTube video, as the Facebook version does not have this issue.[2]
  • In the final two shorts, Garmadon wears the Santa hat first used on Santa in Minifigures Series 8, and in "Garmadon's Digital Shark Choir," he wears the bow tie first used on Alfred Pennyworth in The LEGO Batman Movie.
  • An alternate version of the SDCC Greeting was released in Hungary, the main difference being how Lloyd and Garmadon are instead discussing Budapest (Hungary's capital) instead of San Diego.
Errors

Errors[]

  • In "Back to School," Nya forgets to name Wu when listing the people who know her secret identity.
  • In "Garmadon's Countdown," Omar is depicted with a mustache despite not having one in the film or sets.
