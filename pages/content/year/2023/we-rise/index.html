Song information

Artist

Jody Tompkins & Theofania Pavli

Publication date

May 17, 2023

Overview

We Rise is the theme song for Ninjago: Dragons Rising.

Official description

Official description[]

🎵🤘Be a ninja for what’s riiiiight 🎵🤘 New show...new music video! We Rise is the official soundtrack to NINJAGO Dragons Rising. Be warned! It’s seriously catchy...[1]

Season 3 Remix: Relive some of the Ninjas’ best battles. Watch their thrilling showdowns against the most formidable enemies so far in the series. The new LEGO Ninjago®: Dragons Rising music video not only lets you relive the best moments from this season, it also gives you an opportunity to learn the lyrics to the new Ninja Team song.[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is Ninjago's first vocalized opening theme to not be a variant of The Weekend Whip, or sung by The Fold.
  • It was confirmed by Chris "Doc" Wyatt that We Rise will have remixed versions in future seasons in the same manner to how The Weekend Whip had different versions throughout the different seasons.[3]
    • Season 3 uses a remix of the original intro.
      • Fittingly, the first remix of The Weekend Whip from the original series also debuted in its Season 3.
  • Despite being an official song composed for the series, “We Rise” has not been officially released on major music streaming services.
