Overview

The following is about significant events for Ninjago that occurred in 2023.

January

January[]

  • 1 - The third wave of Ninjago: Core was released.
  • 7 - "The Party" was released on YouTube.
  • 20 - "Operation Heavy Metal: Buffmillion" was released by Tommy Andreasen on his X.
  • 24 - Under Arrest! was released in Germany.
February

February[]

  • 21 - Beast Attack! was released in Germany.
  • 28 - "Operation Heavy Metal: Raggmunk" was released by Tommy Andreasen on his X.
March

March[]

  • 7 - Quest for the Lost Powers was released.[1]
  • 21 - Elemental Powers! was released in Germany.
April

April[]

  • 1 - A teaser trailer of Season 1 for Ninjago: Dragons Rising was released on LEGO's YouTube channel and the second half of Crystalized was released on Canadian Netflix.
  • 5 - A preview of an episode from the first season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising was released on LEGO's YouTube channel.
  • 7 - The #MergeTheFragments online scavenger hunt commenced and the first poster fragment was sent to select Lego Ambassador Network members.[2]
  • 9 - The second #MergeTheFragments poster fragment was released on LEGO Ninjago's Facebook page.[3]
  • 11 - The third #MergeTheFragments poster fragment was released on LEGO.com alongside a countdown to the release of the first season with worldwide dates.[4]
  • 13 - The fourth #MergeTheFragments poster fragment was released on LEGO's Instagram page's stories.
  • 15 - The fifth and final #MergeTheFragments poster fragment was released on LEGO's X page.[5]
  • 17 - The full Ninjago: Dragons Rising poster was released on LEGO.com alongside four Ninjago-themed teasers for the new upcoming LEGO theme titled Dreamzzz.
  • 18 - Massive Critters! was released in Germany.
  • 26 - The first teaser trailer of Season 1 for Ninjago: Dragons Rising was released on LEGO's YouTube Channel.
  • 28 - Another preview of an episode from the first season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising was released on LEGO's YouTube channel.
May

May[]

  • 1 - Another preview of an episode from the first season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising and a teaser for the latter were released on LEGO's YouTube channel.
  • 4 - Netflix released a trailer for the first season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising, also Ninjago Music released on their YouTube channel a preview of The Crystalized Whip.
  • 5 - The Crystalized Whip was released.
  • 9 - A trailer of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 was published on the LEGO YouTube channel
  • 16 - Nya's Powers was released.
  • 17 - The theme song for Ninjago: Dragons Rising was released on LEGO's YouTube channel, titled We Rise.
  • 23 - Five teasers for Season 1 of Ninjago: Dragons Rising focused on Sora, Arin, Riyu, Lloyd, and Ras were uploaded to LEGO's YouTube channel.
June

June[]

  • 1 - The first half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising premiered in the United States, Canada, Hong Kong, India, Japan, Netherlands, Mexico and Taiwan, alongside the release of the first season's sets worldwide.[4]
  • 4 - Ninjago: Dragons Rising premiered in France,[4] though the first 3 episodes premiered on the 23rd of May.
  • 5 - Ninjago: Dragons Rising premiered in the United Kingdom, Germany, Portugal and Finland.[4]
  • 13 - Ninjago: Dragons Rising premiered in Korea.[4]
  • 15 - Ninjago: Dragons Rising premiered in New Zealand.
  • 19 - Ninjago: Dragons Rising premiered in Italy and Spain.
  • 24 - Ninjago: Dragons Rising premiered in Africa.
  • 26 - Ninjago: Dragons Rising premiered in Armenia, Azerbaijan, Benelux (Belgium, Luxembourg), Bulgaria, Czech Republic, Hungary, Moldova, Poland, Romania, Slovakia, Tajikistan, and Ukraine.
July

July[]

  • 20 - A trailer for the second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 aired at San Diego Comic-Con.
August

August[]

  • 28 - "The Temple of the Dragon Cores" premiered in Korea.
  • 30 - A teaser trailer for the second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 was published on the LEGO YouTube channel.
September

September[]

  • 5 - The full trailer for the second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 was published on the LEGO YouTube channel, also Training Time was released.
  • 10 - Ninjago: Dragons Rising premiered in Australia.
  • 11 - "Wyldly Inappropriate" premiered in Korea.
  • 13 - Another trailer for the second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 was published on the LEGO YouTube channel.
  • 15 - Tales from the Merged Realms was released in Germany.

  • 19 - A teaser trailer about the Dragon Energy Cores from the second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 was published on the LEGO YouTube channel.
  • 21 - The LEGO Group started advertising Ninjago: Dragons Rising in India with dedicated activities.
  • 26 - A teaser trailer about the villains from the second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 was published on the LEGO YouTube channel.
  • 27 - Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 Episodes 11-15 premiered on Disney+ Hotstar in India.
  • 29 - LEGO released on their YouTube channel a clip from the first episode of the second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1.
October

October[]

  • 3 - LEGO released on their YouTube channel another clip from the first episode of the second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1.
  • 7 - LEGO released on their YouTube channel a clip from the fifth episode of the second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1.
  • 10 - LEGO released on their YouTube channel another clip from the fifteenth episode of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1.
  • 12 - The second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 was released,[6] alongside The Fold hinting for a new whip releasing the day after.
  • 13 - The Fold released the Hallowhip as a Ninjago special Halloween song.
  • 23 - The second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 premiered in Austria, France, Germany and Switzerland.
  • 27 - Ninjago Music hinted for the release of the full track of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 on the Monday after.
  • 30 - Ninjago Music released a soundtrack from Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1, "The Merge".
November

November[]

  • 1 - The second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 premiered in Australia, New Zealand, Benin, Bulgaria, Central and Eastern Europe, Czech Republic, Hungary, Romania and Slovakia.
  • 5 - Ninjago Music released a soundtrack from Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1, "Source Dragon/Lloyd's Vision".
  • 8 - Ninjago Music hinted for the release of a soundtrack for Euphrasia and the Cloud Kingdom from Ninjago: Dragons Rising, Season 1, Episode 5.
  • 11 - The second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 premiered in Hong Kong and South East Asia.
  • 13 - LEGO released on their YouTube channel a clip from the seventh episode of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1. Ninjago Music released the soundtrack from Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 about Euphrasia, "Euphrasia's Destiny".
  • 14 - LEGO released on their YouTube channel a product placement of some of their new sets of this year and Jiro from 71796 Elemental Dragon vs. The Empress Mech.
  • 17 - The second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 premiered in Singapore.
  • 20 - Ninjago Music released a soundtrack from Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1, "Master of Hope".
  • 25 - The second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 premiered in Taiwan.
  • 27 - LEGO released a crossover episode between Ninjago and DREAMZzz, Ninjago - Dream Team. Ninjago music released the soundtrack from Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1, "Exploring the Crossroads". The second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 premiered in Italy.
  • 28 - LEGO released on their YouTube channel a clip from the last episode of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 about Sora and her elemental power.

  • 12 - Ninjago Music released a soundtrack from Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1 Episode 19, "We are All Dragons".
  • 15 - Ninjago Music released a soundtrack from The Island, "Breaking the Waves".
  • 18 - Ninjago Music released a soundtrack from Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 1, "Sora's True Potential".

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This year marks the five-year anniversary of the final five episodes of Decoded, Sons of Garmadon, Hunted and Tales from the Monastery of Spinjitzu, and the ten-year anniversary of Warriors of Stone, Stone Cold, and Destiny of Doom. For the exact dates, see 2018 and 2013.
    • This is the first year of a 10th anniversary without a season celebrating it, as in 2013 no season was released.
  • This is the first year in which Ninjago does not have a new line of ninja suits in the first half of the year.
    • Instead, only Lloyd got a new ninja suit.
