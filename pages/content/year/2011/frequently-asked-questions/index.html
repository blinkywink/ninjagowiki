Timeline and ages

Timeline and ages[]

How old are the ninja?[]

The ninjas' ages are not specified.[1]

Ninjago: In order to keep them young and relatable to the audience, the ninja do not have specifically defined ages, beyond being teenagers.[2][3][4][5] According to "A parent’s guide to LEGO® NINJAGO®" on LEGO.com, the ninja were still officially teenagers by the end of the original series's run in 2022.[6]

Within the show, the ninja were confirmed by Jay to be "grown-up teenagers" in "The Gilded Path." In "The Call of the Deep," Misako says that Kai is a grown man, implying he is a legal adult as of Seabound, which likely applies to the other ninja as well. Nya and Kai are around 3 and 5 years older than Lloyd before he was hit by the effects of Tomorrow's Tea, due to them being around those ages when their parents were kidnapped by Krux, which occured after Garmadon's banishment and Lloyd's birth.

Ninjago: Dragons Rising: Sometime after Crystalized, the Merge occurs and the story skips ahead years later. Ninja students Arin and Sora are teenagers,[7] as well as Wyldfyre and Frak, as they were also a children during the Merge. While the amount of time is unknown, enough years have passed that the ninja students grew up from children with short legs to teenagers with normal-sized legs, instead of the medium legs which pre-teens/young teenagers usually have in LEGO post-2018. This implies that the older six ninja, excluding Zane, are now young adults in their 20s instead of teens, but as mentioned above, the creators do not specify ages.

While the creators have given some statements which may allow for a clearer picture of the ninja's potential ages, they are not necessarily canon, and more recently, Tommy Andreasen no longer states their possible ages in detail beyond "Whatever you like"[8] and instead leaving it up to fans to decide for themselves.[9] In 2026, he wrote that "a year [in Ninjago] may be 300 days or 600 days...no one knows", and that he originally intended for Ninjago to be "timeless".[10] Keep in mind that it is unknown if he would still like his prior words on the matter to be considered.

At the time of the pilot episodes, Kai, Jay, and Cole were around 16 and Nya was around 14.[11][12] At least 2 years passed between Rise of the Snakes (or later) and Sons of Garmadon.[13] As of Hunted, Kai, Jay, and Cole were at most 19 and Nya was at most 17 as Jay stated that they were still teenagers at that point. That took place some time before March of the Oni, which in-universe was at least 6 months prior to Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu.[14]

How old are other characters?[]

Other characters such as Dareth and Nelson do not have defined ages either.[15][16] However, some characters' ages can be deduced from information from the show or from some creator's statements:

  • Wu and Garmadon are thousands of years old. While Kai stated in "Ancient History" that Aspheera was entombed a thousand years ago, Aspheera herself stated in "Snaketastrophy" that she was entombed for thousands of years and Wu later states in "A Painful Promise" that he is thousands of years old. Wu and Garmadon's age is not clear beyond this. Also, in Tommy Andreasen's opinion, Wu and Garmadon are twins while Garmadon was created first.[17] Later in 2022, he stated that they might be 4,000-5,000 years old, but it's uncertain.[18]
  • The First Spinjitzu Master and Mystake at least predate the land of Ninjago, as they migrated from the Realm of Oni and Dragons.[13][19]
  • Misako is at least in her late 50s, because during the Serpentine War, which took place 40 years ago for The Hands of Time, she was old enough to be have mutual romantic feelings for Wu and Garmadon, who were physically in their late teens at the time.
  • The Overlord is presumably around the same age as Ninjago Island or older (it's unconfirmed whether he existed before Ninjago's creation,[20] though Misako's story in "The Stone Army" claims that he has existed since the very beginning), while the Stone Army was younger, as they were created by the Overlord to combat the First Spinjitzu Master for ownership of Ninjago.
  • Clouse is at least in his late 50s because he was around Wu and Garmadon's physical age during the Serpentine War.
  • Chen is a bit older than Clouse,[21] which can be seen from his age lines in the time of the Serpentine War, and he was a master of Clouse and Garmadon, so he must have had some experience. Assuming he was around 30 at the time of the Serpentine War, he could have been around 70 years old during the Tournament of Elements.
  • Ray and Maya are at least 60 years old because they appeared to be young adults and have age lines on their faces at the time of the Serpentine War.
  • Nadakhan is over 200 years old, as the era of the pirates took place 200 years ago,[22] although it is not clear how long he had lived before the battle with Captain Soto.
  • Acronix and Krux were young adults during the Battle for All Time, and in the present day, Krux was 40 years older. Krux was presumably in his 60s in The Hands of Time, and Acronix, who hadn't aged at all, was presumably 20 to 30 years old.
  • Harumi was a young teenager during Sons of Garmadon and Hunted, because she was a young child of around 9–10 years old during the attack of the Great Devourer,[23] and a few years passed between Rise of the Snakes and Sons of Garmadon.
  • Akita and Kataru are at least 70 years old because they were children when the Ice Emperor took control of Never-Realm.[24]
  • Vex is older, appearing as an adult at that time, though in both cases it is worth noting that Formlings age differently than humans.[25]
  • Lilly was possibly around 30 years old during the flashback in "Dungeon Crawl!," because during the Serpentine War, her father still had his Elemental Powers, meaning she wasn't born at the time. As the flashback of Lilly and young Cole took place several years before the pilot episodes, she may have been around 30 in the flashback.

The following are primarily based on creator statements and thus may not be canon.

  • Cyrus Borg is likely in his 50s.[26]
  • P.I.X.A.L. was possibly 3 or 4 years old as of Skybound and "Day of the Departed,"[27] meaning she was 4 or 5 years old as of Sons of Garmadon, as it was stated in "The Mask of Deception" that a year had passed since the events of The Hands of Time.
  • Ronin is in his late 30s.[28]
  • Thunderfang is older than the First Spinjitzu Master,[29] as he is a direct son of the Firstbourne,[30] though not the oldest one.[31]

How long ago was Garmadon banished to the Underworld?[]

Garmadon was banished to the Underworld around a decade before the beginning of the series.[32]

Why did Wu and Garmadon age so rapidly?[]

There are two or three possible reasons for why Wu and Garmadon aged so quickly between "Battle Between Brothers" and the beginning of the series despite having been young for so long.[33] In a live-chat on the Eljay Johnsen Show, Tommy Andreasen confirmed a fan's theory that one reason may be that Wu and Garmadon share an intrinsic connection of some sort that was severed by Garmadon being banished to the Underworld. He also said another explanation could be tea.[34] He also hinted it has to do with Wu and Garmadon being apart from each other and from the Golden Weapons, as Garmadon was banished to the Underworld and Wu hid the weapons shortly after.[35]

Elemental Powers

Elemental Powers[]

Is Lloyd's dominant element Lightning?[]

No. Though this is the case on his Character Card and is stated in a video on LEGO's YouTube channel, the card statistics are not relevant to the show and the video was made by someone who did not know the series well.[36]

Why could the ninja only use their powers via the Golden Weapons, even though the other Elemental Masters could use their powers without weapons?[]

The ninja could always use their Elemental Powers without weapons, but they relied on their weapons too much, which made them weaker when they lost access to them.[37] Misako mentions that the powers are inside them when Zane says they can not use their powers without Golden Weapons in "The Last Voyage."

Why do the ninja no longer use their True Potential abilities?[]

The ninja no longer use their True Potentials because those abilities must be maintained over time,[38] and when they regained their powers in "Codename: Arcturus," they did not regain them to the extent that allowed them to use their True Potential abilities.[39]

When did Lloyd unlock his True Potential?[]

Before Dragons Rising Season 4, it was unclear if Lloyd's first and actual True Potential was when he became the Golden Ninja in "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master."

It is similar to the other ninja's True Potentials, but there is no clear answer if it is Lloyd's actual True Potential, as it is separate from Lloyd's main power, Life, and it was technically given to Lloyd instead of him achieving it himself.[40] In 2017, Tommy Andreasen said it was "kind of" his potential,[41] and in 2020, replied indicating that it may have not been unlocked in the Temple of Light, because it was unlocked by the ninja gave their potentials to him.[40] He may have likely unlocked his True Potential since he's able to conjure his Energy Dragon, which requires the user to unlock their True Potential.[42] Additionally, in "The Lean of a Tree," Lloyd himself eventually claimed that he had already reached his True Potential once.

Lloyd would later unlocked his second True Potential in "Dying to Live."

What is the name of Lloyd's power?[]

In Dragons Rising Season 4, the name of Lloyd's powers is revealed to be "Life".

Previously, his elemental power[43] has had various different names throughout the series. It has been called Energy in "The Day of the Dragon" and green power in "The Titanium Ninja", while Chen referred to Lloyd as the "Master of Power" in "Ninja Roll." However, Tommy Andreasen has said that in his opinion, referring to Lloyd as the "Master of Energy" would be incorrect.[44][45] As for "Power," "Master Chen was a criminal and a liar. His word is hardly anything to consider canon."[46] However, Lloyd does call himself the "Master of Energy" in his "Meet the Ninja" video, the subtitle for his section in the play guide for Choose the Path is "Lloyd (Green Ninja/Master of Energy)," and a video on LEGO.com calls him "The Elemental Master of Energy."[47] In The Book of Elemental Powers, it is also called Energy. Roby in "The Sword Shatters" and the Arc Dragon of Life in Dragons Rising, Season 4 both refer to Lloyd as the "Elemental Master of Life." Additionally, Lloyd's power comes from the Source Dragon of Life, as revealed in "Beyond the Phantasm Cave."

Glen Lakin addressed the discrepancy by suggesting a hypothetical Master of "Life Energy," and the name being simplified to Energy or Life to clarify which Source it is linked to.[48]

Is Lloyd's power an Element or an Essence?[]

While Tommy Andreasen stated that he doesn't consider Lloyd's power to be an element, it is generally considered to be an Elemental Power in the show alongside the other elements. Chris "Doc" Wyatt and Glen Lakin both later declared that Lloyd has an Elemental Power and is an Elemental Master.[49][50]

Are Wu and Garmadon Elemental Masters?[]

Tommy Andreasen stated that Wu and Garmadon are not Elemental Masters, due their powers as the sons of the First Spinjitzu Master being different.[51] Chris "Doc" Wyatt stated that he does consider them Elemental Masters;[52] when asked if the two are Elemental Masters and what their powers would be, he answered with "Master of Creation" and "Master of Destruction."[53] According to The Book of Elemental Powers, a document from the Library of Domu states that "Creation, like Destruction, is not an element...it is an elemental essence".

Can more than one person have the same Elemental Power? Why did the Time Twins have two of the same power?[]

Not usually, because parents lose their powers when they pass them onto their children, as explained in "Pause and Effect." The Time Twins were an exception to this because their power was easily divisible,[54][55] but it cannot be split between four users.[56] However, mechanisms such as time travel and Amber allow two users of the same power to exist through technicalities.

Do you need an Elemental Power to do Spinjitzu?[]

No. A number of characters without Elemental Powers, such as Misako, Doubloon, Aspheera, and Arin, have been shown to do Spinjitzu. The only requirements for learning Spinjitzu are "a master and lot of training."[57]

Can Lloyd still control the other elements?[]

No. Formerly, Fire, Lightning, Earth, Ice was one of the Green Ninja's powers that Lloyd uses in "Wrong Place, Wrong Time" and "Island of Darkness." However, Lloyd can no longer control the elements of Creation, as he gave those powers back to the ninja at the Temple of Light in "Codename: Arcturus."[58] It is worth noting that this ability to control the other elements was due to Lloyd's potential of becoming the Golden Ninja.

How does Zane have an Elemental Power if he's a robot?[]

In the Sons of Garmadon episode, "Snake Jaguar," it was explained that Zane gained his Ice power when an ill Hibernus came by the hidden workshop and passed his power onto Zane, though the exact mechanism of this is unknown.

Why do Garmadon and Wu rarely use their powers?[]

Wu knows not to overuse his powers,[59] or he may not even have powers at all besides creating his dragon.[60] At least after his initial redemption, Garmadon swore not to use his powers, explaining why he created the Art of the Silent Fist as a substitute for fighting.[61] It's unknown why he was rarely ever shown using Destruction in the first two seasons.

Why does Nya no longer seem to create water in Seasons 9-13?[]

Nya can still generate water, as seen when she faced Aspheera in her tomb, battled Pyro Vipers in the Ninjago Museum of History, and in The Island. But controlling pre-existing sources of water requires less energy, so she will draw from her surroundings if given the choice.[62] Nya can also not control water in extremely hot or cold environments: In "The Belly of the Beast," she couldn't draw water from the air due to the low humidity of the Desert of Doom, and in the second half of Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu, she had trouble controlling water in the Never-Realm due to the low temperature.

Can the ninja be harmed by their own elements?[]

Yes, enough of an element can harm the master of that element.[63]

Are Storm and Wave Elemental Powers?[]

No. Although Storm and Wave were described to be elements in The Book of Elemental Powers, Storm and Wave are actually abilities that consists with Water and Wind.[64][65]

Are the elemental essences of Creation and Golden Power related? Is Destruction and Darkness related as well?[]

Yes. Creation and Golden Power, as well as Destruction and Darkness, are indeed related.[66] For Creation and Golden Power, Tommy Andreasen once said to "Think of golden [power] as creation,"[67] and later stated that First Spinjitzu Master got Golden Power from the dragons, implying that the two essences are related.[68] As for Destruction and Darkness, it is heavily implied during Crystalized that two essences are connected, as the Overlord stated that Lloyd and Garmadon's powers were a gift from him.[69] However, in The Book of Elemental Powers, a document from the Library of Domu states that readers should "not confuse Destruction with Darkness. Darkness feeds on Creation. It needs life to make evil. Destruction fears Creation. Destruction only wants to erase all life". This book sugests that while Darkness and Destruction might be related, they are distinct elemental essences.

Is Golden Power the combination of Creation and Destruction?[]

Yes. Chris "Doc" Wyatt confirmed that Golden Power contains both Creation and Destruction.[66][70] This was hinted in The Book of Elemental Powers, where it stated that, "Golden Power can create and destroy," and that the First Spinjitzu Master, who originally had Golden Power, is described as "...a child born of both worlds: a child of light and darkness." This means that Golden Power has ties to the First Spinjitzu Master's Oni and Dragon lineage. Additionally, Tommy Andreasen implied that Creation and Golden Power are related.[68] The First Spinjitzu Master is also responsible for why Destruction is intertwined with Golden Power.[71]

First Spinjitzu Master and related topics and lore

First Spinjitzu Master and related topics and lore[]

Did the First Spinjitzu Master create the entire realm of Ninjago?[]

No. The common statement of "The First Spinjitzu Master created Ninjago" refers to the landmass, not the realm. The Temple of Light and Golden Peaks existed before the rest of the land was created, and the realm existed before the First Spinjitzu Master created the landmass.[72][73] Wojira also existed and ruled the seas before land creation, suggesting that originally in Ninjago, there was only the ocean where the Merlopians lived.[65]

Do Wu and Garmadon have a mother?[]

Though a silhouette of a woman was included on Lloyd's family tree in The Book of Spinjitzu, it's unclear whether the First Spinjitzu Master had a wife or not.[74] It's possible Wu and Garmadon did have a mother and that the First Spinjitzu Master was not able to create life on his own, meaning the First Spinjitzu Master could have had the idea to have children from observing the populations he brought into Ninjago.[75] He later clarified that both Wu and Garmadon likely didn't have a mother, and that they are simply the First Spinjitzu Master's "ultimate creation".[76] In late 2024, Tommy Andreasen stated that a mother for Wu and Garmadon was never discussed when he worked on the show.[77]

Are the Serpentine from the Wyldness?[]

It is confirmed that the Serpentine originated from the Wyldness[78], but it is not known whether the blue orb on Pythor's chest is related to this.[79] In fact, the reason there was no snake tribe in Legends of Chima was specifically because there were already snake people in Ninjago.[80]

How many realms are there?[]

Though there is conflicting information on whether there are sixteen or seventeen realms, the most recent source has stated that there are seventeen,[81] which would mean that one of the realms does not have a sister realm. However, since Cursed Realm and the Departed Realm have merged into the Spectral Lands, both answers are technically correct.

Are themes such as Nexo Knights and Bionicle other realms?[]

No. Only Chima is confirmed to be part of the Wyldness and Legends of Chima is the only canonical series based on another LEGO theme. Other locations such as Knighton (from Nexo Knights),[82] Hidden Side,[83] LEGO Island,[84] and the 2015 Bionicle reboot[85] are not part of the 16 realms. However, Ninjago and Chima do exist as fictional worlds within the world of Nexo Knights.[86] There have never been plans for a crossover between Nexo Knights and Ninjago.[87] Ninjago exists as a fictional show and a section of the dream world in Dreamzzz.[88]

Names[]

What is Garmadon's first name?[]

Garmadon's first (and only) name is Garmadon. Lloyd inherited his father's first name as a last name, as is a custom in some cultures such as Norse.[89][90][91]

What is Wu and the First Spinjitzu Master's last name?[]

Like Garmadon, Wu and the First Spinjitzu Master do not have a last name.[92]

What are the ninja's last names?[]

Lloyd Garmadon, Arin Nived, Jay Walker, Frak Slitherbottom and Zane Julien are the only ninja to have confirmed surnames. Jay is a specific case because he has two surnames, biological - Gordon and legal - given by his adoptive parents - Walker. Zane's surname is Julien, passed on from his father.[93] Arin's family surname was revealed through his parents' fake gravestone in "I Alone Can Save Them." Kai and Nya's surname was considered "Smith" behind the scenes, though this is not necessarily canon to the show.[94][95] Though Cole does not have an official last name, Kirby Morrow jokingly suggested "Cole Steel."[96]

The names "Cole Bucket" and "Zane Roberts" were also given in the 2012 blooper reel, but these are not canon: the video is meant to be humorous and not in the same universe as the TV show, and Tommy Andreasen and the voice actors have acknowledged these specific last names to be unreliable/irrelevant.[96][97]

Regarding the other new ninja, it is not fully clear if they have surnames as: Sora wouldn't identify under her parents' surnames anymore following her change in names, and most dragons don't have last names (with the exceptions of Ekesian Coldfang and Urd Leatherwing), dragons using their homeland or their tribe as a mark of identity (e.g. "Wyldfyre of the Wyldness", "Wyldfyre of the Fire Tribe")

Why did Wu's title change from "Sensei" to "Master"?[]

Because "Sensei" has different meanings in different countries that could possibly cause issues in the future, the word was replaced with "Master" starting when the movie would have originally been released.[98][99][100] Because of this, Wu is now "Master Wu," and this extends to other characters referred to as "Sensei" such as Garmadon, Yang, and so on.

Canon

Canon[]

Are the website descriptions canon?[]

The exact canon status of LEGO.com varies. Tommy Andreasen has said that, while he may not personally like some of the website's additions to the lore of the show, the information on the website is most likely canon,[101] though it "Depends on how false the info on the website is."[102] However, he has indicated that some facts originating from the Rebooted character descriptions are not true,[103] while at least one of the facts is true.[104] According to Tommy Kalmar, all information from the Museum is canon.[105]

Are the graphic novels such as Who is the Phantom Ninja? canon?[]

Though the Greg Farshtey graphic novels are not necessarily canon,[106][107] they can be considered canon if they don't contradict the series.[108] For example, Kingdom of the Snakes is non-canon,[109] while Who is the Phantom Ninja? can be considered canon.[110]

Is Ultra Agents canon to Ninjago?[]

No. Though the designs of Mr. Pale and Tox are taken from Ultra Agents characters, the two themes are not in the same universe.[111] The character designs were taken from the theme to provide fans with the opportunity to obtain more Elemental Masters in minifigure form.[112]

Though Tommy Andreasen collaborated on a fan-made map by Joshua Deck which features Astor City,[113] the location is not canon.[114]

Are the Tall Tales and Wu's Teas canon?[]

No, neither the Tall Tales or Wu's Teas are canon, as they "contain timeline and character errors."[115] The Tall Tale of Flintlocke may be the one exception where it does not contradict the chronology and can be placed in canon.

Are the Ninjago Magazine comics canon?[]

Like the Greg Farshtey comics, the Ninjago Magazine comics can be considered canon if they don't contradict the TV series.[116] However, the "Mother Earth" comic has been confirmed to be canon[117] even though it contains timeline errors when compared to the TV series.[118]

Is Way of the Departed canon?[]

Way of the Departed is not officially canon. This allows Tommy Andreasen more creative autonomy in writing the story.[119] However, it is written to not contradict canon and supplies answers for the show for fans who do consider it canon,[120] and it may be considered canon once completed.[119]

Is Decoded canon?[]

Tommy Andreasen has stated it could potentially be canon, but he has not seen it, so it's not confirmed.[121] According to Glen Lakin, Decoded was written to be "could-be-canon," and that is can be ignored by fans if they want.[122]

Is the Clutch Powers movie canon to Ninjago?[]

No, or at least not necessarily. The Clutch Powers who appears in Ninjago is simply meant to be the "Ninjago take on the character."[123]

Is Ninjago: Reimagined canon?[]

According to Tommy Andreasen, the canon status of these shorts is up to interpretation.[124] According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, "Golden Legend" is non-canon, and "With one big exception, the Legacy shorts are just "what ifs."[125] When asked about which short is the big exception, Wyatt clarified that it was "Golden Hour."[126]

Why is Lloyd's hair blond when both Garmadon and Misako had brown hair?[]

In real life, genetics work in such a way that even if both of a child's parents have brown hair, the child can have blond hair due to recessive genes, as Wu is shown to have a similar hair color to Lloyd. It also sometimes skips generations and does not pass directly from parent to child.

Either way, Tommy Andreasen has stated that, seeing as the series has several fantasy elements, hair color logic should not be studied too closely either.[127][128][129]

Andreasen's answer mentioned above also applies to Kai and Nya's family,[130][131][132] which can likewise be explained with recessive genes as well.

Neither of these design changes are explained in the show but Tommy Andreasen said the reason for this change is that "the on-screen Bounty looks like the one which is currently available to obtain and play with."[133] This may indicate that, like the ninja's design changes for Season 8, there is no real explanation for this within the world of the show. Ninjago: Reimagined

Ninjago: Reimagined[]

Are all shorts animated by one animation studio?[]

No. According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, they worked with different animation studios from around the world to make the short films.[262] "Golden Legend" was animated by Sun Creature Studio, "Gold Rush" was animated by Taiko Studios, both "A Day in the Life of a Golden Ninja" and "Sweatin' to the Goldies" were animated by WildBrain and "Golden Hour" was animated by CC Pixels.

What is "Golden Legend" trying to reimagine?[]

According to Tommy Andreasen, "It is what Ninjago could have been if we were in a different mood when we conceived it."[263]

Is "Golden Hour" animated by WILFilm ApS?[]

No. Despite the similarities to WILFilm's animation style, the short is animated by CC Pixels.[264]

What is LEGO World Builder?[]

LEGO World Builder is a website powered by Tongal. It brings fan and creator communities together to collaborate and develop original stories, shows and more, alongside the LEGO creative team. It was used by Uday Kataria to create an original idea for the story of "Golden Hour."

What was Uday Kataria's original idea for "Golden Hour?"[]

Kataria's original idea would have involved the ninja splitting up and going to different points in the past to correct alternate realities that resulted from various changes to events from previous seasons.[265]

Why at the end of "Golden Hour" is still not known what happened to the Time Twins?[]

According to Tommy Andreasen, this short is intended to still leave the fate of the Time Twins unknown and it only has to explain what happened to Wu after "Lost in Time."[266]

Why does the crack on the wall of the monastery in "Golden Hour" look like the symbol of the Royal Family?[]

According to Uday Kataria, the crack is a reference to the fact that the Royal Family seemingly appeared out of nowhere in Season 8, and it suggests that their existence was caused by this change to the timeline.[267]
