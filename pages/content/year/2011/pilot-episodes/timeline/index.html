The timeline is a chronological list of the important events that have occurred in the known history of the world of Ninjago and the other realms.

Before time had a name

Before time had a name[]

Note: According to Tommy Andreasen, the following event may have started around 10,000 years in the past.[1][2]
  • The Ethereal Divide had always existed before the occurrence of the cycles.
  • The seven Source Dragons come into existence by unknown means.[3]
    • Through the Source Dragons, the Elemental Powers are formed.[4]
  • The current cycle begins.
    • The First Realm (the Realm of Oni and Dragons) comes into being.
  • Ninjago, the Underworld, the Realm of Madness, the Cursed Realm, the Cloud Kingdom, the Wyldness, Djinjago, the Departed Realm, the Never-Realm, the Realm of Lee, Mysterium, the Realm of Monsters, the Whispering Wood, the Realm of Eternal Dawn[note 1], and Umbrasilvis eventually manifest.[5]
    • The First Spinjitzu Master would create Imperium and three of these realms later on.[6]
  • The Cursed Realm and Djinjago become sister realms.[7]
  • In the First Realm, the Source Dragon of Life in their mortal form gives birth to the Firstbourne.[8] The Firstbourne would later become the mother of all mortal dragons in The First Realm and all of the other Realms of Creation.
  • In the First Realm, The Alpha is born as the first Oni.
  • The Firstbourne gives birth to two children: Thunderfang and Estian the Nomad.
  • Mortal life came into existence from the Whispering Wood.[9]
  • The Writers of Destiny take a role in several of the realms' events, helping guide, though not necessarily controlling, their destiny.[5]
  • A war between Oni and Dragons broke out in the First Realm.
  • Centuries later after the beginning of the war,[10] the First Spinjitzu Master, a hybrid between dragons and Oni, was born.[11]
  • The First Spinjitzu Master, together with the Firstbourne, creates the Dragon Armor and the Dragonbone Blade to end the war between the dragons and the Oni. Legends told that the owner of the Dragon Armor would be able to control Firstbourne, like the First Spinjitzu Master did.[12]
  • The First Spinjitzu Master creates the Realm Crystal.[5]
  • After failing to end the war, the First Spinjitzu Master uses the Realm Crystal to escape from the Realm of Oni and Dragons. Firstbourne was left behind in the Realm of Oni and Dragons, waiting for the First Spinjitzu Master to come back.[12]
  • Around 10,000 years before the ghost invasion, Fenwick and Quanish are born in the Cloud Kingdom.[13]
  • At some point, The First Spinjitzu Master entered the Never-Realm for unknown reasons, and while there bestowed the Elemental Power of Form to a human woman he selected as his guardian, and he barely managed to escape back to Ninjago. He later warned Wu that he is forbidden from entering the realm as no one else, but he had the knowledge of how to escape.[14] The First Master of Form would take on many animal forms and came to be held in high esteem by a nameless tribe.[15]

Pre-Ninjago[]

  • The First Spinjitzu Master arrived in an ocean-filled realm engulfed in chaos, caused by a giant serpent named Wojira, who held control over the elements of Water and Wind. He decided that if the world was to be a peaceful realm, he must defeat her, so he worked alongside the Merlopians and the islanders to fight her.
  • The First Spinjitzu Master, Merlopians, and islanders struggled to beat Wojira and were nearly defeated by her. As a last resort, the first Elemental Master of Water, Nyad, attempted to pry the Storm Amulet and Wave Amulet from Wojira's head, but had to resort to merging with the ocean forever to do it. With the sources of her power torn away from from her, Wojira fell into a deep sleep and wouldn't be awakened again for thousands of years.
  • The First Spinjitzu Master gave the Storm Amulet to the islanders and the Wave Amulet to the Merlopians.
  • The First Spinjitzu Master forges the Golden Weapons at the Temple of Light using gold from the Golden Peaks.[16]
  • With the four weapons, he creates the island of Ninjago.[17]
  • Sometime after the First Spinjitzu Master fled from the Realm of Oni and Dragons and created Ninjago, the Oni pursued him and created an Oni Temple in Primeval's Eye.[18]
  • After falling in love with the lands that the First Spinjitzu Master created, Mystake turned on the other Oni.
  • When the Oni are defeated, the warlords' three Oni Masks - the Masks of Vengeance, Deception, and Hatred - are claimed by the First Spinjitzu Master. Over time, the masks would be separated and hidden across Ninjago.

Stone Wars: The First Battle of Ninjago[]

  • The Overlord manifests in the shadows of the new land and declares war to decide the fate of the realm.[19]
  • The Overlord's corruption quickly spreads across the island until he is challenged by the First Spinjitzu Master.
  • In the Stone Wars, the Overlord creates the Stone Army to combat the First Spinjitzu Master, as well as the Temple of Fortitude to fortify against the Golden Power.[19][20] To combat the Overlord, the First Spinjitzu Master uses the power of the Golden Dragon and Golden Mech to fight him off.[21]
  • Unable to stop the Overlord, the First Spinjitzu Master splits Ninjago into two smaller islands (Ninjago and the Dark Island), with the Overlord banished to the Dark Island and his corporeal form destroyed while the Stone Army is entombed underneath an area that would one day become Ninjago City, leaving the ultimate battle between good and evil undecided.[19] However, his evil would still plague the four realms the First Spinjitzu Master created.[22]

Before the creation of the Dragon Cores[]

  • Various tribes of anthropomorphic animals are founded in the Wyldness.
    • The Mucoids move from the Wyldness to the Mother Garden.[23]
  • Various types of Tide-Folk come into existence across the different realms.
    • The Lava-Tides are established in the Lava Wastes.

The creation of the Dragon Cores[]

The creation of the Dragon Cores happened long before time had a name, although it isn't specifically known how many thousand years ago.
  • The First Spinjitzu Master foresaw a coalescence in a vision.[6]
  • The First Spinjitzu Master seeks out the Source Dragons and asks them to freely distribute him some of their energy.
  • With the power of the Source Dragons, the First Spinjitzu Master creates the three Dragon Cores, which is powerful enough to keep all the realms in balance.
  • The First Spinjitzu Master created the Temple of the Dragon Cores in the World Forest.[24]
  • A coalescence was prevented due to the power of the Dragon Cores.[6]
  • Fearing what evil-hearted people would do with the Cores, the First Spinjitzu Master hides the cores in the Mother Garden, the Pinnacle of Crystals, and in the Lava Wastes located in the Wyldness. The Mucoids and Lava-Tides are entrusted to protect the cores.
  • The First Spinjitzu Master created murals that depict the locations of the Dragon Cores, protected by its spirit.

Splitting the Weapon of Desolation[]

  • Millenia ago, the First Spinjitzu Master discovers the Weapon of Desolation that was created by Lord Ras’ Master, and fears that evil-hearted people would seek to use it.
  • The First Spinjitzu Master splits the weapon into two fragments with a spell on a stone tablet.[25]
  • The First Spinjitzu Master hides the two fragments of the weapon, one in the Tree of Madness in the Realm of Madness, and the other in the Tree of Burdens in Umbrasilvis.
  • The First Spinjitzu Master hides the Tablet of Desolation containing the spell in the Realm of Eternal Dawn.[26]
When the name of time was still new

When the name of time was still new[]

Note: It is possible for the First Spinjitzu Master to have brought humans from another realm.[27]
  • The Serpentine were created by the First Spinjitzu Master in the Wyldness and relocated to Ninjago.[28] Alongside the humans, they colonize the land and built cities, villages, and other places.
  • At an unknown point of time, the First Spinjitzu Master creates some of the other realms.[6]
  • The Mythica traverses the realms, sharing stories that its inhabitants might have not known. One of the stories is Wojira's tale that was recounted in the Never-Realm.[29]
According to Tommy Andreasen, the following events may have taken place 3,000 years after the beginning of the realms.[30]
  • Before the birth of his two sons,[31] the First Spinjitzu Master bestows upon a group of mortals the powers of the elements he received directly from the Source Dragons,[32] forming the first Elemental Masters.[33][34]
  • Millenia before Aspheera's imprisonment, a truce between humans and Serpentine was made that would forbid the two species from into trespassing the other's territories.[35]
  • Quanish, banished from the Cloud Kingdom by his brother and with no memory of his life, becomes a prophet and was known as a fool.[36]
  • The First Spinjitzu Master would return to the Never-Realm, to help one of his guardians the First Master of Form, who wreaked havoc, due to a cursed thorn embedded in her paw, until the First Spinjitzu Master managed to destroy it but in doing created the Abyss of Eternal Night.[37]
  • The Master of Form bestowed upon the Nameless Tribe animal forms, leading them to become the Formlings.
  • The Formling King, accompanied by his army, ventured across the Abyss of Eternal Night, despite warnings from his son. The Formling King and half of his army into the Abyss.[38]

The creation of the Tournament of the Sources[]

  • Spirit Dragons from the Mythica swear their allegiances to the Fengxian family's City of Temples. Through the tribe's blessing, the city receives the ability to move to different realms.[39]
  • The first Tournament of the Sources is organized by the First Games Master, Roake Fengxian.
  • At the end of the war between Oni and Dragons,[40] a general of the Dragon army named Thunderfang was convinced that his successes in battles against the Oni gave him the right to ascend as a Source Dragon, but was rejected.[41]
  • Thunderfang decided to destroy the City of Temples and its people, but the Arc Dragon of Focus intervened.[41]
    • Thunderfang was overpowered by the Arc Dragon, who unleashed a powerful blast that sent him crashing down, resulting in him being imprisoned within a mountain.[41]

After the Battle of the City of Temples[]

  • Some of the Temple City monks chose to stay at the First Realm after the tournament. This action would later lead to the emergence of a new humanoid race.[42]
  • King Urd Leatherwing writes The Sagas of the Eternal Sources, detailing the Battle of Thunderfang and the Arc Dragon of Focus and passed it down to The Fengxian family for generations.
  • The remaining Mythica would eventually be all hunted and killed by the Oni, who wanted to silence their stories.[29]
  • Zarkar, an Elemental Master from the Tiger Tribe in the Wyldness, wins one of the Tournaments of Sources.[43]
Millennia ago

Millennia ago[]

The Rise of the Forbidden Five[note 2][]

  • Millennia before the Ritual of the Blood Moon,[45] the Order of the Deep Shadow was formed by the Raven King in the Wyldness. The Order would eventually be taken over by five individuals from different tribes who will later be known as the Forbidden Five.[46]
    • Through unknown circumstances, Thunderfang gives away most of his power to them, and they become the first masters of Chaos elements.[47][48]
  • The Forbidden Five form the Wolf Clan, an army consisting of members of various Wyldness dwellers.[49]
  • The Forbidden Five discover Theroxian and invent Shatterspin, leading to the corruption of themselves and the wolf warriors.[50][51]
  • Throughout a decade,[52] the Forbidden Five lay waste to most of their realm and decimate anyone who opposes them.[53]
  • The Forbidden Five intend to destroy all the realms as a means of conquest.[54][53]
  • In the east of the Wyldness, the Dragon Masters Egalt and Rontu, who fought in the War of Oni and Dragon during their youth,[55] create the Rising Dragon technique, which happens to be a perfect counter for Shatterspin.[56]
  • To find a prison for the Forbidden Five that is more permanent than the Departed Realm, the two dragons travel to Mysterium and request the high sorceress Gandalaria to create a spell that will banish the Forbidden Five to a place beyond the realms. Gandalaria spends all day and night making it.
  • The Dragon Masters defeat the Forbidden Five and use Gandalaria's spell to banish them to the Nether-Space.
  • Unlike other spells, the spell used to banish the Five continues to exist after its initial use and becomes sentient, travelling the realms in the following centuries.

After the Exile of the Forbidden Five[]

  • Lord Argus takes control of the Wolf Clan and continues to train its warriors.
  • When attempting to perform Shatterspin, he is influenced by the sorcerer Zaiden Kane into striking the Gong of Shattering, turning him into the Lycan King and cursing the Wyldness, trapping it in a desolate state.
  • The Lycan King is defeated by the wolf warriors, who are turned into stone in the process. Unable to control him, Zaiden Kane leaves the Forest of Spirits.
  • At some point after, the Dragon Masters seal the Shadow Dojo, enclosing all the remnants of the Five's legacy inside.

The sons of the First Spinjitzu Master and the First Age[]

  • The Cat-Eye Jewel, which grants nine lives to every cat in Ninjago, is created. The Order of Felis is founded to protect it.
  • Several decades before Wu and Garmadon's journey, the First Spinjitzu Master trains a young orphan girl named Nineko. One day, he accuses her of stealing one of his katanas. Realizing that she can't convince him otherwise, Nineko leaves the Monastery of Spinjitzu and promises to herself that one day she would be stronger than her teacher and prove to him that he should've believed her.
  • The Ancient City of Ouroboros is built, serving as the place where the Serpentine study the stars.[57][note 3]
  • Mambo V becomes the ruler of the ancient Hypnobrai and a Serpentine King. He makes a promise with the First Spinjitzu Master to reassure the truce between humans and Serpentine.[35]

  • Wu is born as the second son of the First Spinjitzu Master. He inherits the Dragons' power of Creation from his father,[11] in order for the First Spinjitzu Master's children to represent balance.[59]
  • The First Spinjitzu Master tells Wu campfire stories of the Overlord.[60]
  • Wu and Garmadon successfully perform Spinjitzu for the first time while fighting off bullies.[61]
  • When the two brothers were children, they encountered Quanish the Elder alongside their father.[36]

Before Spinjitzu Brothers[]

  • A monarchy is established as the form of government in Ninjago, headed by the Royal Family of Ninjago. The Ninjago nobility, the warrior class, and the samurai are also established.[note 4]
  • In seek of immortality, Nineko eventually becomes a powerful sorceress. She tries to drain the life force of the crops and fails, but the crops did die, which causes the village of a young girl named Komala to nearly starve. After this, Komala devotes her life to the study of good magic and eventually becomes the leader of the Order of Felis.
  • Nineko realizes that if a cat owns the Cat-Eye Jewel, it possesses infinite lives. She transforms herself into an anthropomorphic cat and tries to steal it, but is stopped by Komala. The good sorceress then puts a spell on the mountain with the Jewel so no cat can climb it.
  • In the Valley of the Sphinx, a society is built around a creature who lives in her pyramid and can grant wishes to people who will get pass the Maze of the Sphinx.
  • A magician named Mindaro finds a way to bring his paintings to life. After his wife died, his grief creates a monster made of paint called the Chroma. The Chroma devourers Mindaro and his daughter Indi, which leaves his older daughter Peri to do nothing but wait for adventurers who will defeat the Chroma.
  • After accidently gaining possession over a djinn medalion, a puppeteer named Tanabrax uses it to drain souls from two thieves and place them into his puppets. He orders them to kidnap people from the closest village so he can create an army of puppets who will obey to him.

Spinjitzu Brothers[]

The Curse of the Cat-Eye Jewel[]

  • The First Spinjitzu Masters sends Wu and Garmadon on a journey to the Northern Ocean to find a tea plant that could cure Garmadon from the Great Devourer's venom.
  • The next day, Wu and Garmadon leave the Monastery of Spinjitzu and head to a village as the first part of their journey.
  • In a dark forest, they are saved by a mysterious cat lady from a ferocious beast. Then, they are attacked by the warriors with cat masks, and out of nowhere, the cat lady saves them yet again.
  • Nineko, the cat lady, takes Wu and Garmadon to her hut in the forest.

The Lair of Tanabrax[]

  • Wu and Garmadon arrive at a quiet village where the people avoid them and seem afraid.
  • At the marketplace, they meet Hana and her grandmother Obachan, who warn them to leave before nightfall.
  • That night, the brothers rescue a man from strange puppet attackers, and Hana explains that people —including her brother— have gone missing.
  • Hana and Obachan tell them about Tanabrax, a shadowy figure who steals souls and turns people into puppets using a medallion.
  • With help from Moody, a talkative puppet, they gather and train the villagers to fight back.
  • Wu is captured and turned into a puppet, while Garmadon and Hana follow the trail to Tanabrax’s underground lair.
  • Garmadon confronts Tanabrax, but the villain begins stealing his soul—until Hana uses a mirror and sunlight to destroy him.
  • The villagers’ souls are returned using a spell from Tanabrax's book, and Moody and Bunch, whose bodies are long gone sacrifice themselves in the process.
  • The brothers help Obachan hide the medallion, then join the village for a feast in celebration of their victory.

The Maze of the Sphinx[]

  • Continuing their journey, Wu and Garmadon find themselves in the Valley of the Sphinx. They hear from people living there that Sphinx will grant one wish anyone who completes the maze in her pyramid.
  • They enter the pyramid, hoping that a wish can cure Garmadon from the Great Devourer's venom.
  • Inside the maze, they meet Shezada, a skilled survivor who's been trapped inside for weeks and who wants to use her wish to rebuild her home village which was destroyed by a flood.
  • Garmadon sees a vision of his future evil self in a magical mirror, which shakes him but also fascinates him.
  • Wu gets poisoned by an arrow in one of the traps and willingly gets bitten by a green spider to survive.
  • In one of the rooms of the maze, Shezada gets bitten by a Ghoul and also becomes one.
  • Due to arriving to the Sphinx together, Wu and Garmadon are granted only one wish, and the brothers use it to save Shezada.
  • Wu and Garmadon leave the pyramid and continue their jorney to the Northern Ocean.

The Chroma's Clutches[]

  • Wu and Garmadon continue their journey and climb a misty mountain, where they meet a girl named Peri and her magical creature Balee.
  • Peri tricks Wu and Garmadon into entering a painting to defeat the Chroma and save her trapped father and sister Indi.
  • Inside the paintings, they meet Jelena, a magical deer, face off against Gray creatures and finally the Chroma itself, a massive monster that feeds on hopelessness and despair.
  • Garmadon briefly hears the Chroma whisper dark thoughts into his mind, hinting at his inner darkness.
  • The brothers discover that joy and color are the Chroma’s weakness.
  • Peri paints a violet flower (named after her mother), restoring hope to Mindaro, who uses his magic to defeat the Chroma with the help of Spinjitzu and colors.
  • All victims of the Chroma, including Mindaro and Indi, are freed and the family reunites once again.

The Creation of Amber[]

  • A blacksmith from Metalonia, who envied the Elemental Masters' abilities found fascination in an ancient, crooked tree which was charged with energy of the Source Dragon of Balance.[1] It sported a mystical amber substance that ensnared anything that approached it.
  • The blacksmith used the mystical amber and crafted the very first Chronosteel and used it to bind fractions of other masters' powers, through the Urn of Enmity, he created the element of Amber.
  • After passing down his power, he crossed paths with Wu and Garmadon, and ultimately trapped himself in the urn by accident after attempting to steal their powers.[71]

Centuries ago[]

The Splinter in the Blind Man's Eye[]

  • There is a legend in Ninjago about the Wishmaster - a mysterious hermit who lives in an abandoned temple atop the highest mountain in Wailing Alps. Desperate people climb to his top, and those who return are transformed, but never reveal what happened at the summit.
  • Lloyd, struggling with the trauma of the fight with the Oni, Harumi's death, Garmadon's return, and memories of Morro, secretly leaves the team to find Wishmaster and find answers to the questions that haunt him.
  • Before beginning the climb, the innkeeper warns Lloyd that Wishmaster's wishes come with a price and should not be trusted.
  • Clancee, a former member of Nadakhan's crew now working at a mountain inn, recognizes Lloyd and decides to secretly follow him.
  • Clancee recalls the fall of the Nadakhan's crew, the imprisonment of his captain in the Teapot of Tyrahn, and his subsequent service under Clouse on the Dark Island.
  • Lloyd begins his solo ascent of the Wailing Alps. Along the way, he is constantly haunted by nightmares and painful memories: his possession by Morro and the suffering he inflicted on his loved ones, his father's death in the Corridor of Elders, the loss of the Golden Power at the hands of the Overlord, his burning in the Fire Temple, his time at Darkley's Boarding School, his rejection by Garmadon, Harumi's death, and the guilt of unleashing the Serpentine and the destruction wrought by Great Devourer. At the same time, he is haunted by mysterious visions of an unknown, peaceful land filled with green plains, floating islands, and golden petals that he cannot recall or explain.
  • As he climbs, Lloyd recalls his childhood, when, as a student at Darkley's Boarding School for Bad Boys, he first reached the Wailing Alps. He recalls letters from his mother, Misako, who had written to him repeatedly about the mountain but never answered his questions about why she had abandoned him. Driven by a desire to find answers and take control of his own destiny, Lloyd found one of the legendary Serpentine tombs on the mountainside. While attempting to open it, he fell inside, where he first heard the hypnotic hiss of the Serpentine. It was this moment that set in motion the events that led to the release of the Serpentine, the beginning of his journey as the Green Ninja, and forever changed his life.
  • Clancee catches up with Lloyd, caught in the act of following him, and explains that he's also looking for Wishmaster. An approaching storm forces them to call a temporary truce and spend the night together.
  • During the night, the camp is buried by an avalanche, and Clancee climbs out from under the snow and rescues the unconscious Lloyd, pulling him to the surface.
  • Clancee reveals that he is looking for his former crew, who have also gone to Wishmaster, believing that the mysterious hermit may be the missing Nadakhan.
  • As Lloyd continues his ascent, he learns about Clancee's lonely life. Clancee confesses that he has worked alone at the mountain inn for years, with no friends except Moppy—his mop and Peggy—his wooden leg, whom he named to feel less lonely. He also reveals how much he misses the old crew of the Nadakhan. Seeing his honesty and kindness, Lloyd begins to see him not as an enemy but as a loyal traveling companion.
  • Lloyd and Clancee reach the legendary Hanging Temple, but find it abandoned and find no trace of the Wishmaster.

  • Upon arrival, the heroes are greeted by a woman named Yana, and Lloyd announces that their goal is to meet the Wishmaster.
  • The mysterious old man Quanish has been wandering Ninjago for thousands of years, suffering from memory loss and writing incomprehensible scrolls that temporarily free him from pain and the flood of memories.

    • It is currently unknown why this period is called the Era of the Stone Warrior, as the Stone Army was defeated thousands of years ago and was not discovered until Legacy of the Green Ninja.
  • In 2026, Tommy Andreasen wrote that "a day [in Ninjago] may be 15 hours or 28 hours. A year may be 200 days or 600 days...no one knows", and that he originally intended for Ninjago to be "timeless".[274]
    • This means that references by characters to time passing may mean something different than what it would mean on Earth.
