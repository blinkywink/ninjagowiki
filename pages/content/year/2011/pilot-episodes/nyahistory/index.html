Overview

Early life

Early life[]

Nya and her brother Kai were raised by their father, a blacksmith, and their mother in the town of Ignacia. Unbeknownst to Nya, she inherited her mother's control over the element of Water. Nya was named after Nyad, the first Elemental Master of Water, whom Maya believed only to be a myth. When Nya was small, Maya told her a story about Nyad, though Nya forgot about it over time.[1]

When Nya and Kai were young, Ray would tell them stories of the dragons, mystical creatures that belonged to both the world of the living and of the dead.[2] He would often teach them proverbs such as "No matter how much fire you have, experience isn't something you learn overnight"[3] and "The world works in mysterious ways.[4] At one point, young Nya and Kai met Wu, a friend of their parents.[5]

At some point during their childhood, Nya and Kai invented a game in the Four Weapons Blacksmith they called Counter Shot, using a variety of obstacles and tools such as tables and horseshoes.[6]

After their parents went missing around the time when Nya was 3 years old, and Kai around 5,[7] the siblings inherited his shop, Four Weapons, and Kai continued his father's legacy while Nya managed the store itself.[citation needed] At one point, the two had a babysitter named Grumbmiller who they disliked and would trip when she made them go to bed early.[8] Because Grumbmiller's cooking was subpar, Nya would often volunteer to make dinner.[9]

At school, Nya went on a number of field trips to the Ninjago Museum of History.[10][11]

Pilot episodes

Pilot episodes[]

Way of the Ninja[]

One morning the two were working in the store when Kai showed off how to craft a sword, only to make it too quickly and be rendered useless. Nya reminded her brother to take his time and of their late father's advice to be patient. They were soon interrupted as the shop was visited by an old man who complained that none of the merchandise was suited for a ninja. After Kai told the man off for loitering, Nya motioned for him to attempt to make a sale, though by then the man had vanished. Moments later, a dark cloud appeared and the small village fell under attack by the Skulkin army. Kai suited up for battle and order Nya to stay inside, though not wanting to be left out of the action, she grabbed a Nin-Jô and joined her brother in battle. However, Nya was quickly captured by the Skull Truck and taken away.

King of Shadows[]

Nya was eventually taken to the Fire Temple where she was used as bait to lure Kai there. There, Garmadon threatened Kai to remove the Sword of Fire for him, warning him that he needed a weapon to sever the chain carrying his sister and save her, or she'll be dropped into the lava below. Despite her pleads for her brother not to go by his demands, Kai unsheathed the Golden Weapon and severed the chain, freeing her just before she could be plunged into the lava. Motioned to stay behind her brother, Nya watched as Garmadon summoned several shadows to mercilessly defeat Kai, remarking angrily on his underhanded tactics. However, the two were saved by Master Wu, only to be attacked by the Fire Dragon. Even as Master Wu took the Sword of Fire through the Underworld entrance, she warns her grieving brother of the dangerous beast.

Weapons of Destiny[]

The Fire Dragon soon realized that they were just trying to protect the weapon as well and became friendly with Nya and Kai. Cole, Jay, and Zane soon arrived at the temple, and Nya mentioned that their father used to tell them stories about the Dragons and how they ferried between their world and the Underworld. Jay asks Nya if she likes the color blue, to which she replies that it is her favorite. Unbeknownst to Jay, Nya gained feelings for him in this moment and saw them having a future together.[12] The two siblings then agree to part ways, and Nya tells the ninja that she'll light a candle in the shop window until they returned.

Nya did as promised, and before long the ninja returned, she rushed out overjoyed to see them, hugging both her brother and Jay. She stated how happy she was they were back, though Kai was quick to remind her that it wasn't over and that Garmadon would one day return.

Mini-movies

Mini-movies[]

Secrets of the Blacksmith[]

With peace restored to Ninjago, Nya decided to move into the Monastery of Spinjitzu with the ninja. Jay flew her back to Four Weapons on his Lightning Dragon for her to pick up a few things. However, upon arriving at the shop, she was ambushed by Wyplash, Krazi, Bonezai, and Chopov. After briefly distracting the group, she knocked Wyplash and Krazi to the ground before springing a trap on Chopov and kicking Bonezai. Nya managed to take the upper hand by cleverly using a gong, but the noise caught Jay's attention and he came charging in, only to find Nya alright. He questioned what the commotion was about, but she explained she had knocked over a few samurai helmets. The two then flew back to the monastery.

Return to the Fire Temple[]

At one point, Nya and Kai took a ride on the Fire Dragon, whom he named "Flame." Suddenly, Flame veered off course and took the two to the Fire Temple where he showed them a secret entrance to the Underworld, which Wyplash a few other Skulkin were using at the time. Kai, however, fell off the edge and was trapped by the Skulkin, though Nya and Flame sent them in retreat. The two eventually used Flame to succesfully destroy the entrance to the Underworld, to avoid any more attacks from the Skulkin.

Rise of the Snakes

Rise of the Snakes[]

Rise of the Snakes[]

Ninjago's era of peace soon came to an end as Nya heard that Lord Garmadon had been spotted approaching Jamanakai Village. She rushed to warn the ninja, who had become rather lazy with peace, and thus scrambled to get on their dragons. When Kai suited up on Flame, Nya inquired if she could do anything to help, but Kai told her that it was a job for a ninja, only to then ask her to hand him up the reigns. As the ninja flew away, Nya asked Wu if they would ever reach their true potential.

While the ninja were away, Nya used the time to attempt to beat her brother's record on the training course. She only managed to get halfway however before she was knocked off by the spinning axe. The ninja returned, the crisis merely being Lloyd Garmadon after all. While the ninja held a match to see who was the Green Ninja, Nya decided to instead visit Jamanakai Village.

During her visit, Nya witnessed Lloyd raid the village again for candy, only this time with the Hypnobrai, who promptly control all of the village's citizens. Nya however managed to avoid their hypnotizing stare and held out until the ninja arrived. She informed them not to look the Hypnobrai in the eyes when they rattle their tails, and also noted that the General of the tribe was holding the Hypnobrai Staff, which contained the anti-venom to cure the villagers. As the ninja prepare to attack, Jay makes Nya their honorary member of their team, much to her annoyance.

During the ensuing battle, Cole managed to take the Staff from Slithraa, though briefly falls under the hypnotism of Skales before Nya dealt the snake warrior a kick in the face. As the Hypnobrai retreated, Nya orders Cole to place the staff in the fountain, so the anti-venom can be distributed through the water supply and restore the villagers back to their normal selves.

Wu soon arrived and the ninja, realizing the error of their ways, asked their master to help them reach their true potential. As they left the village, Nya asked him when she would get her own dragon, though Wu assured her that her time would come.

Home[]

On Zane's night to cook, Nya joined Wu and the ninja in the monastery's dining hall. When Zane entered in a pink apron, Nya began to laugh with the others, noting that even she wouldn't wear such a thing. When Cole later started a food fight, Nya joined in.

The next day after the ninja had left to destroy Lloyd's treehouse fortress, Nya and Wu rode Flame to the scene, arriving just it time to save them from the collapsing structure. They returned to the monastery to find it engulfed in flames, as the Hypnobrai had burned it when they reclaimed their staff.

Nya and the ninja lived for a short time in a makeshift shelter in the Sea of Sand until Zane discovered Destiny's Bounty and they made it their new home.

Snakebit[]

Nya, impressed that the ninja cleaned up the Bounty, informed they were going to have visitors, Jay's mom and dad. She then offered them a tour of the Bounty.

The next day, she went with Jay to visit their junkyard, only to find it taken over by the Fangpyre tribe and Jay's parents slowly turning into Serpentine. She helped fend them off and caught Jay when he fell out of his Storm Glider. When they got back to the Bounty, she made an antidote for Ed and Edna.

Never Trust a Snake[]

In Zane's dream, Nya informed him that no one was safe with Lloyd and the Serpentine at large.

When the alarm for the Bounty went off, she told the ninja that Lloyd and Pythor had invaded Darkley’s. In order to help the ninja stop them, she agreed to drop the anchor so that they could get in the school.

Can of Worms[]

After Pythor stole the Map of Dens from Lloyd, Nya figured out the locations of the Constrictai and Venomari tombs based on the locations of the first three tombs. Later, when the ninja were trapped in the Toxic Bogs, Samurai X arrived and pulled them to safety before scaring off the snakes. When the ninja approached the Samurai to thank her, she sprayed them with sleeping gas. She then flew off just as the Destiny's Bounty arrived.

The Snake King[]

After the ninja got their new ZX suits, Nya reported that Serpentine were terrorizing the amusement park. The ninja were more than ready to reply to the call. Upon arriving, however, the ninja found that Samurai X had taken care of the snakes, tying them up in rope. The ninja were under the belief that whoever caught the Samurai would be the Green Ninja, and so placed a bet. A montage showed how they tried in different ways to capture the Samurai but to no avail.

Back at the Destiny's Bounty, the ninja complained about their failed attempts to try and capture the Samurai. After realizing Lloyd wasn't with them, the ninja tracked him down to the Lost City of Ouroboros. However, upon arriving they realized it was a trap when they were captured by Pythor. Pythor announced another Slither Pit for the entertainment of the Serpentine, to found out whether the ninja or Samurai were stronger than the other. Although seeming to fight, Samurai X revealed he was just putting on a show until an opportunity presented itself, and helped the ninja escape. After the Samurai's exo-suit malfunctioned, they landed in the desert. Cole, Zane, and Jay landed together in the desert, but Kai landed nearby the Samurai, where he discovered that Nya was Samurai X once she removed her mask.

After a discussion, Kai complimented his sister's actions and she happily accepted them before becoming relieved when her brother promised not to tell the others about her secret. She summoned her armor to pick her up and left the area while giving him the item he was looking for.

That night on the Bounty, she was pleased to see Kai had kept his word on not telling the others before Wu gave her advice and told her that "sibling sharpens sibling."

Tick Tock[]

During the ninja's attempts to reach peak physical condition, Nya and Jay helped as additional weights for Cole.

After Master Wu left to find someone to be of aid, Nya and the ninja went to Jamanakai Village and hung up "missing Fang Blade" posters. Jay mentioned that he thought Wu was getting the mysterious samurai for help, though Nya and Kai were quick to dismiss such an idea. They were quickly distracted by the matter however, as Zane spotted the falcon. Kai ordered Nya to return to the Destiny's Bounty while they followed the bird, and she begrudgingly agreed.

Once Bitten, Twice Shy[]

After some nearby villagers unearthed a Fangpyre skeleton, Nya examined it and determined that the effects of their venom could be undone if one's heart-rate was dramatically increased. She presented her findings to the ninja at a meeting, though it was interrupted as Jay had been tricked into wearing perfume (which Nya was allergic to) by Kai. After the other ninja had left, Jay apologized, saying he only did it to impress her. To make it up to her, he offered to take her out to a fancy restaurant on a date. Nya eagerly accepted, though quickly left before the perfume toxins could enter her bloodstream. While, unbeknownst to her, Jay pricked his hand on the skeleton.

Later in the day, she dawned her Samurai X attire and returned to the Bounty just as Jay asked to move their date to Mega Monster Amusement Park. As her bracelet revealed that Serpentine had been spotted there, she accepted and dressed up for the occasion, though secretly hid her samurai gear inside her purse.

At the amusement park's restaurant, Nya and Jay split a meal, though it was still too large for either of them to finish. Jay claimed that he was the first to get his knot badge in little scouts and that he tried daring foods, though Nya was increasingly distracted by the alert of nearby Serpentine. Jay soon began complaining about how much he hated the Samurai, to which Nya quietly agreed with him. He soon excused himself however and when he didn't show signs of returning, she decided that duty called and raced out of the restaurant.

Finding a deserted alleyway in the park, Nya changed into Samurai X and jumped into her mech. She arrived at the entrance of the Ghost Train ride just as Pythor and the other Serpentine were leaving with the first Fang Blade. Using one of the functions of her suit, she grabbed the blade only to find that her mech's boosters had malfunctioned. She fled on foot with Serpentine pursuing her, though eventually was ambushed by Constrictai who disabled the mech. Pythor ordered her helmet to be removed to discover the identity of the mystery man, though quickly corrected himself upon it being revealed the samurai was female.

Nya was tied to a roller coaster cart, bound for doom as the Serpentine had destroyed a part of the track. Jay soon arrived to help her, though Pythor then ordered for the cart to be started on its wild ride. Nya discovered that Jay had been bitten by the Fangpyre, though he hadn't told her so it wouldn't ruin their date. She then remembered Jay's accomplishment of being the first to receive the knot badge, though when she brought it up he admitted he had made it up to impress her. As the cart drew closer to the flaming hole, Jay began frantically calling for the Samurai to help them. It was then that Nya revealed that hadn't been entirely honest and that she was in fact the Samurai. Nearing the end, Nya told Jay that she liked him best when he was himself and kissed him, restoring him back to normal. Coming to terms with this, the Blue Ninja was able to unlock his True Potential and save them.

Later, the two met up with the other ninja and revealed her secret to Cole and Zane. Both were surprised about it, though Cole expressed that he wished he had a sister like her. Kai noted that he had been the first to discover her secret, which meant they he would become the Green Ninja, though the others expressed no recollection of such a bet. Jay then stated that he had learned that day of the importance of being himself, though Cole reminded them that the Serpentine now had the first Fangblade.

The Royal Blacksmiths[]

On the Destiny's Bounty, Nya reminded the ninja of their current standing with finding the Fangblades and how to find them while Jay was still surprised she was Samurai X, something Kai was already over. Cole soon discovered the next Fangblade was the Blade Cup, a trophy won by his father, and his dance team, The Royal Blacksmiths and the ninja set off to find out from Lou who had the Cup now.

Nya was seen in the audience during the show and cheered when the ninja, as Spin Harmony, won.

The Green Ninja[]

After Wu came back, she and the ninja went to greet him, and to their surprise, Garmadon. She and Kai went to the deck to prepare for Serpentine, but Kai states that he must unlock his True Potential.

When Wu suspects Nya to be the Green Ninja, she refused, and told Kai, who was eavesdropping on them, that she's happy being a Samurai. Jay told them the Falcon spotted the Serpentine in the Fire Temple, and they prepare to fight. However, Kai went off to stop Garmadon from stealing the Golden Weapons, much to their disappointments. Nya stayed behind on the Bounty and instructs the ninja not to use their weapons, as the Temple was unstable.

When everyone but Kai and Lloyd made it out of the Temple on time, Nya was upset but cheered up when she sees them and was told Kai unlocked his True Potential.

Back inside the Bounty, Kai informed them Lloyd was the Green Ninja. The Golden Weapons reacted in front of him, signaling Lloyd was the Green Ninja.

All of Nothing[]

Nya, Wu, and the ninja headed to the Serpentine's underground fortress to retrieve the Fangblades, but were caught in a Vengestone cage. After Lloyd saved them and they retrieved the Fangblades, the team celebrated, unaware that Pythor had got on board.

The Rise of the Great Devourer[]

Nya piloted the Bounty to Torchfire Mountain to destroy the Fangblades, but when Pythor stole them, she arrived on her Samurai Mech. The ninja eventually had to chase the Serpentine, but when Wu detached his and Pythor's car, they waited until Lloyd carried them with the Bounty. They got to the City of Ouroboros but watched as the Devourer swallowed Wu and Pythor.

Day of the Great Devourer[]

After just barely avoiding the Devourer, she and the others were devastated at the loss of Wu and the Bounty. Kai motivated them to try their best and followed them to Ed and Edna's junkyard, where they created the Ultra Sonic Raider. However, the snake slithered away toward the city.

Without a way to defeat the snake, the ninja decided to give Garmadon their weapons. After Garmadon defeated it, the ninja cheered when they saw Wu alive.

Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

Darkness Shall Rise[]

After Jay asked if anyone seen Nya, she arrived on her motorbike, along with Wu, claiming to have found a place to live. She handed him a pamphlet saying Patty Keys was a real estate agent that could help them find a place. She then drove off after Wu explained they have to get parts for the Ultra Sonic Raider and the Ultra Dragon’s dragon ointment.

When she and Wu went to their new suite, they had to save Lloyd from Skales. They move back to their old apartment, claiming they don’t need anything other than each other.

Pirates vs. Ninja[]

After giving the Ultra Dragon its medicine, Nya provided Lloyd with a quick lesson about the dragon, explaining that it would be his one day.

Nyad[]

Once the process was complete, Nya swam up to the side of the News Center's windows and placed her hand on the glass to meet Jay's hand, then propelled herself off to find and defeat Kalmaar and Wojira, once and for all. Emerging out of the waters surrounding the Police Station, Nya revealed herself to Kalmaar and declared that he was going to wish he had never crossed her. With a furious shout, Nya launched herself to attack her hated foe.

The Turn of the Tide[]

Flying through the air, Nya hurled ball after ball of water at Kalmaar until she landed atop Wojira's forehead. Glaring at her opponent, Nya began to slowly advance towards him, undeterred by his energy blasts thanks to her new liquid form. This distraction afforded Kalmaar the ability to counterattack, and he struck Nya with a concentrated burst of energy from his trident, momentarily vaporizing her humanoid form.

Following Kalmaar's defeat by Bethomaar's hands and his subsequent devourment by Wojira, Nya resurfaced in the form of a liquid water dragon. As Wojira prepared to obliterate her foes and allies alike, Nya struck at the Storm Serpent with a blast of water, challenging her to battle. Nya then initiated her attack, flying down and plucking Wojira from the waves and hurling her against the side of a building. In retaliation, Wojira responded by ramming Nya against a different building before going after her allies once again. As Wojira attacked the ninja, Nya swooped in and hoisted Wojira away by the latter's horns, slamming her against a skyscraper. Wojira responded to this attack by diving into the depths of the sea before resurfacing behind Nya and calling upon the Storm Amulet to strike Nya repeatedly with bolts of lightning. Once Nya was stunned, Wojira struck and vaporized her again with the Fury of the Storm.

As Wojira prepared now to finally obliterate the watching ninja, a sudden raincloud formed overhead, catching the Storm Serpent's attention. Accompanied by the crackle of thunder, Nya reformed her dragon form with the rain and moisture in the air. Swooping down towards Wojira and building momentum, Nya reverted to her humanoid shape with her fist outstretched and eyes shut, bracing herself for impact. And with one mighty blow, Nya struck and destroyed the Wave Amulet. Without the this amulet's powers to counteract the Storm Amulet, Wojira lost control over her remaining powers and rose into the air as lightning swirled around, vanishing entirely.

With victory secured, Nya launched herself up towards the roof of the police station, where the other ninja cheered her on. However, something seemed off, and Nya could not remember why she had made her way to the rooftop in the first place. As her friends and loved ones gathered around her, Nya expressed confusion over their comments regarding "normalcy" and instead asked if they too heard the call of the sea. As her humanoid form fluctuated, Nya told her friends that she would be leaving them, but she would always remember them. As she began to walk away, Jay attempted to plead with her, begging for her to stay, but his words fell upon deaf ears; although Nya knew he meant something to her once, things had changed and she was no longer the girl he once knew. In spite of this, Jay's pleas managed to briefly reach Nya's fading humanity and sense of self, halting her advance towards the sea. With a sad smile, Nya briefly took Jay's hands into her own, running her hand against the side of his face for one last time before returning to the sea. In their parting exchanges, Nya affirmed to Wu that her identity was not lost (or at least not yet), and for Jay to tell Maya that she now knew what Maya meant by "the way the cookie crumbles." Finally, Nya dove into the Endless Sea and commanded the floodwaters to recede from Ninjago City, ending the Great Flood. With one final look, Nya affirmed her friendship with the ninja before swimming out into the Endless Sea and disappearing from view.

One sundown, days later, Nya swam through the waves with a pod of whales, laughing and giggling as she sped through her new, watery home.

The Fall of the Monastery[]

When Wu and Skylor disagreed about the ninja's fate, Nya reluctantly sided with Wu, remarking that the hangar's seismic sensors had picked up a powerful blast around the time they lost contact with their allies. At that moment, the group were alerted to an activation of the secret entrance elevator and rushed down to greet their new arrivals.

Nya and her allies then watched to their shock as four of their old enemies: Pythor, Aspheera, the Mechanic, and Vangelis, stepped out from the elevator and proclaimed their intention of stealing the Golden Weapons. Determined to prevent this at all costs, Nya and the others rushed into battle against the Council of the Crystal King, with Nya at first focusing on Vangelis, whom she knocked around in midair with a series of jump-kicks. Unfortunately, Pythor used his invisibility to backup his ally, escaping from Skylor to attack Nya as she rushed again at the fallen king. Vangelis would then take this opportunity to lift Nya through the air, hurling her across the hangar before turning his attention towards Wu.

When Nya recovered from her fall, she found herself face to face now with Pythor. In spite of her efforts against one of the ninja's oldest foes, Nya found herself surprised and out-matched by Pythor's combat skills, which had since greatly developed throughout the years since their last encounter. Injured by his vicious and powerful attacks, Nya crawled away from her old foe in desperation while he laughed at her apparent helplessness. Approaching her with a naginata in hand, Pythor mocked Nya over the loss of her powers and derisively stated that she should have stayed in the Endless Sea, adding with particular venom that that was where she belonged. Before the blow could be struck, however, Skylor intervened and grabbed Pythor by the tail, slamming him behind her to the side. Unfortunately, Pythor was quick to recover and counter-attacked, knocking both Skylor and Nya to the ground when the latter tried to exploit his moment of vulnerability.

While Wu fought against Vangelis, Aspheera, and the Mechanic alone, Pythor used his invisibility to sneak up on Nya from behind and ensnared her with his tail and powerful grip, holding the edge of his naginata to her throat. As Nya struggled to free herself, she managed to take advantage over Pythor's momentary distraction from the sight of the MiniPix to finally free herself and kick him in the chest for her troubles. Nya then regrouped with Wu and P.I.X.A.L. as the latter explained the purpose of the MiniPix, revealing them to have been her unseen helpers in expediting the ninja's various mechanical upgrades. Impressed by this, Nya admitted that up until now, she thought that P.I.X.A.L. had been somehow completing hundreds of hours of work in such short time by herself. Although the MiniPix were able to briefly turn the tide against the Council of the Crystal King, the arrival of scores of Crystal Spiders cemented the battle in favor of the Council. Overwhelmed and backed into a corner, Nya was confronted by Aspheera who revealed that Harumi was responsible for leading the Council, much to Nya's shock. Unfortunately, she would not have much further time to dwell on this realization, for Aspheera knocked her back into the main computer with Spinjitzu then brought the massive screen down upon her with a blast of magic.

Eventually, Nya managed to free herself from the rubble of the computer, only to discover that her allies were gone and she was trapped with the army of Crystal Spiders, all of whom were primmed to detonate with only seconds to go! After a frantic attempt at activating the elevator failed, Nya rushed to her Samurai X Mech and barely managed to hop into the cockpit just as the spiders exploded, heavily damaging the mech in the process. Fortunately, the mech managed to remain operational and Nya was able to escape from the ruined hangar bay and burning Monastery with its rockets. Nya was then dispatched by Wu to rescue the trapped ninja, who were buried beneath a mountain of rubble in the abandoned N line.

She then briefed the boys about recent developments, including the destruction of the Monastery and Harumi's surprising return, before ferrying them all to the old Samurai X Cave that was being set up as their new headquarters for the time being.

As they neared the Samurai X Cave, Nya's damaged mech finally gave out and crashed just outside the cave's entrance. After squeezing herself out of the cockpit, Nya patted the mech and thanked it for its service before descending into the cavern with the others. After entering, Nya and Kai were hugged by Skylor, who was grateful for their survival and she then listened on as the reunited team made preparations to rescue Lloyd.

After arriving at the Newspaper warehouse, Nya set down the Bounty's anchor and stayed onboard while Lloyd and Wu headed down to ask the Paperboys about the clippings.

As she watched, Nya whispered into her brother's ear to criticize Garmadon's "teaching" skills. As her mistrust of Garmadon's methods grew, Nya openly challenged him, stating that she couldn't recall channeling rage through her face when she trained to become the Master of Water. Unsurprisingly, after P.I.X.A.L. informed the team that she had made new upgrades to their vehicles that should allow them to combat the Crystal Warriors, Nya was in full support of fighting them head on than continuing Garmadon's attempts at unlocking Lloyd's Oni form.

Brave But Foolish[]

Nya followed the original four ninja onboard Destiny's Bounty, where she monitored the status of their vehicles as three of the boys began using their new upgrades. Alarmed by their excessive usage, Nya warned them that they would need to shut off their new energy weapons lest they burn out their vehicles' engines - an order that was reluctantly complied with. Once the ninja's vehicle status returned to a normal level, Nya turned her attention over to Garmadon and inquired about the plant he was carrying around. Garmadon then explained to her that the plant was the only bit of green in his life and he hoped that under his care, it would one day thrive and eventually forgive him. Nya surmised that Garmadon was using the plant as a metaphor for his relationship with his son, Lloyd, though he denied such matters when she inquired about them. Nevertheless, Nya emphasized her familiarity with the plant, identifying it as a Baracis ninjagai and that they were known to be quite hardy. However after mentioning that it only required water to be revitalized, Nya suddenly remembered her current loss of powers and walked away sadly to the Bounty's deck.

Steering the Bounty towards the Crystal Island, Nya positioned the ship near its target and fired a pair of grappling cords into the sides of the island with the intention of towing it away from Ninjago City. In response to this attack, the Crystal King unleashed his horde of Dragonides upon the Bounty, two of which attempted to snap up Nya in their jaws. Escaping from the clutches of the Dragonides, Nya made her way to the ship's cannon and began returning fire against the crystalline beasts, lamenting once more her current lack of powers as she attempted to call upon a torrent of water. Moments afterward, Harumi attacked the Bounty with one of the Dragonides, commanding it to attack one of the ship's thrusters and successfully destroying it. Nya braced herself against the mast as the ship rocked from the attack and warned the others onboard to do the same.

Following Wu's defeat to the Crystal King, the latter unleashed a massive bolt of Darkness at the Bounty and heavily damaged the ship, which now began to fall from the skies. Clinging tightly to the mast, Nya called out for Lloyd to pull the air brakes, emphasizing that it was their only chance at survival. Although Lloyd succeeded in doing so, the Bounty had sustained too much damage and cracked in half, with Nya stuck on the bow side as both halves plummeted to the ground. Fortunately, Nya's side of the ship landed slightly slower thanks to the mast remaining mostly intact and catching the winds to slow its descent.

In spite of this, Nya was still unable to free herself and many more of the Crystal Warriors were still closing in on them! In her struggle, Nya inadvertently tapped into her slowly returning powers and called upon the water in a nearby fire hydrant, bursting it apart as the water knocked away the approaching warriors. In awe of what just happened, Nya admitted that she was unsure if the hydrant's burst had been her doing, but there was hardly any time to dwell on such matters. Together with Jay's help, Nya finally freed herself from the weight of the mast and embraced her fiancé. Although Nya tried again to call upon the water from the hydrant, nothing happened, and she soon fled with Jay on his motorcycle.

Zane then began to analyze the levers and runes, running together every potential combination. After several hours, during which at one point Nya fell asleep but was jolted awake by Sora, Zane declared that he could find no connection between them. Fortunately, the new generation of ninja realized that the levers simply had to all be pulled down at the same time, granting access to the temple.

Entering the temple, the ninja were confronted by a hostile Spirit of the Temple. In the fight that ensued, Nya attempted to attack the spirit with Spinjitzu but phased through the incorporeal being and slammed into a wall. Nya's further attempt at stabbing the Spirit with her spear was met with similar futility and she received a punch across the room for her trouble. Landing beside Wyldfyre, Nya overheard the younger girl mention having armpit parasites, horrifying and alarming Nya. Shortly afterwards, Lloyd managed to calm down the Spirit by reassembling the alter and the grateful Spirit then regaled to the ninja a tale about the Dragon Cores and the Coalescence, during which Nya realized that the term referred to the Merge.

Gangs of the Sea[]

Inside the hanger bay, Nya laid out a map of the Merged Realms as the ninja planned their search for the Dragon Cores. Lloyd proposed splitting into two teams, and Nya immediately stated that she would take Sora and Riyu. The pair were then approached by "Sora" the dragon and Nya suggested that she could come too. However, Nya's nonchalant usage of the name "Sora" confused everyone present with regards to who exactly Nya was referring to, greatly irritating her. After some back and forth clarifying, Nya exasperatedly groaned that the similar names were going to be problem.

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

Riding atop of Jiro, Nya accompanied Sora, Riyu, and "Sora" the dragon to the Pinnacle of Crystals, only to discover that the Yellow Dragon Core was missing from its resting place.

The Last Djinn[]

With the yellow dragon core missing, Nya, Sora, and Riyu sought out an Ancient tomb, hoping to use a set of Seeker Stones borrowed from the Writers of Destiny to direct them to the missing core. Almost immediately upon entering, the three survived the first trap where they were shot at with spears. As they continued through the tomb, Sora started to cynically speak about her spate of bad luck and stressed that her existence was a black cloud. In response, Nya urged Sora to avoid focusing on the negatives and encouraged her to never lose hope. Nya's words of encouragement however were cut short when she stepped on a slightly raised tile and dropped the three of them down a chamber holding a pool of acid. Fortunately, Nya spotted a safe ledge and propelled herself forward with jets of water before catching Sora and Riyu.

Sora commented on the acid pit, exclaiming that this was exactly what she had predicted, but Nya corrected her, pointing out that the stones didn't fall into the acid. Nya once again emphasized the importance of never giving up or losing hope, noting that without hope, nothing can go right. Sora asked if it's a saying from Wu, but Nya clarified that it's her own, inspired by Wu's teachings beyond fighting. The trio soon entered the tomb's burial vault where Nya explained the Djinn and Djinjago to Sora. Approaching the alter, Nya attempted to arrange the Seeker Stones correctly, but inadvertently unleashed a curse upon herself and Sora.

Leaving the tomb, Nya and Sora met up with "Sora" and Jiro outside. "Sora" seemed troubled and pointed to symbols on Nya and Sora's hands just as two Howlers emerged from the tomb and attacked the group. Unable to defeat the spirits in combat, Nya and the others were forced to flee on the dragons, eventually discovering a sanctum hidden within a cave. There, the group encountered Arrakore, the last Djinn. Nya seemed a bit more uneasy than her allies about the Djinn's presence, noting that Djinjago was destroyed. Arrakore glumly affirmed Nya's comment but revealed he survived due to being in another realm at the time of its destruction alongside "Sora," whose real name was revealed to be "Zanth." When Arrakore learned of the curse placed upon Nya and Sora and demanded to know if either had attempted to use Seeker Stones without proper guidance, Nya sheepishly answered that it was a possibility, much to Arrakore's chagrin. Nevertheless, Nya believed that they had lost the Howlers and there was no chance of being found again until Arrakore clarified the nature of the Howlers as a curse and that therefore, there was a one hundred percent chance the Howlers would continue finding Nya and Sora until both of them were killed.

Shortly thereafter, the Howlers arrived at the sanctum, forcing everyone inside to fight back. Unfortunately, in spite of everyone's best efforts, Nya was forced to admit that the Howlers were unstoppable while Sora complained about Arrakore's attitude. Midst comment, Sora realized that Arrakore's attitude was almost no different than her own and she turned to Nya, asking if the latter felt the same way; Nya declined to comment however. Sora then encouraged Arrakore into rediscovering his hope and Nya wished for the Howlers to be gone, finally dispelling them and lifting the curse.

When Arrakore awoke, Nya and Sora thanked him for his help and Nya suggested that perhaps in time, as Arrakore's strength and magic returned, he may one day restore the entirety of Djinjago. The group then celebrated their victory over the Howlers with feasting and music.

They Call It Doom[]

The next day, Nya and Sora thanked Arrakore again for the lovely evening. Arrakore then expressed his gratitude by granting Nya's wish to find the missing dragon core, creating a magical compass that would direct them to its location. Following the compass, Nya and her allies made their way to the bizarre Land of Lost Things. After commenting on the strangeness of the place, Nya and Sora spotted two kids and tried to flag them down for information. Unfortunately, the kids ran off, forcing the two to give chase until they were attacked by a large stone golem who declared that no one was going to hurt the kids. The fight that followed however was brief and ended when the golem recognized Nya; for her part however, Nya was unsure if the golem truly was her old friend Cole.

Land of Lost Things[]

Nya continued to doubt the golem's identity as Cole up until the stones fell away, revealing the Earth Ninja within. Overjoyed to see her friend, Nya immediately ran up and embraced him with a hug, exclaiming that she wasn't sure she'd ever see him again. Nya then introduced Cole to Sora and Riyu while Cole introduced her to the Finders. Their conversation however, was cut short by the arrival of the Hoarder, who attacked them. The group fled to a giant noodle cup, where Nya first expressed a desire to fight the Hoarder until Cole clarified that he and the Finders have already tried, and every time the Hoarder simply rebuilds itself stronger than before. Cole then created an escape tunnel for everyone to use and Geo used his own powers to fuse the cup with the Hoarder's hand.

The ninja and the Finders regrouped with Zanth and Jiro and made their way to the Rookery. Along the way, they admired Geo's sculptures and Nya insisted to Sora that her powers were her own while remarking that the Merge must have created a slew of new elemental powers. As they entered the Rookery, Cole told Sora and Nya about the Finders, who ended up in the Land of Lost Things after running away from home. Cole then showed off a machine used by the Finders to keep the Hoarder at bay, with its power source being the yellow Dragon Core! Nya attempted to take the core but was stopped by Cole who protested that if the core was removed, the machine would stop, and the Hoarder would eat him and the Finders. In response, Nya proposed to take Cole and the Finders back the monastery but learned that the Finders were unable to leave the Land of Lost Things. Sora proposed reconfiguring the machine with a different power source so they can take the yellow Dragon Core and gave Nya the schematics she just drew up. Impressed, Nya volunteered for her, Cole, and Jiro to find the remaining parts that were needed for Sora's plan.

As they searched for the missing parts, Nya and Cole noted that they had a lot of stories to share with one another but soon ran into a problem when they encountered several Imperium Drones and the Hoarder. Nya fought off the drones harassing Jiro and the trio flew back to the Rookery where they witnessed Dr. LaRow stealing the yellow Dragon Core from the Rookery. Nya and Jiro gave chase but ultimately failed to stop Dr. LaRow from escaping. When she returned to the Rookery, Nya was surprised to see that the Hoarder had seemingly been destroyed by the others, remarking that she must have missed a lot.

Absolute Power[]

The next day, Nya, Sora, and Cole discussed their next steps, deciding that they would have to infiltrate the City of Imperium to retrieve the dragon core taken by Dr. LaRow. Sora began creating a set of weaponized armor for Jiro and Nya complimented the girl on how far she had improved in controlling her powers. Cole then confided in Nya that Wu's voice came to him last night and was directing him towards an unknown goal. Understanding her friend's situation, Nya hugged Cole and encouraged him to follow Wu's voice. Nya then departed for Imperium alongside Sora and Riyu, leaving Cole to continue his journey alone.

We Are All Dragons[]

Approaching the City of Imperium, Nya admitted to Sora that while it was rotten to its core, it did look absolutely perfect from the outside. When Sora brought up her parents again, Nya suggested that they could try talking to them when things settled down, though Sora refused to even entertain such an idea, dismissing it as an impossibility. Nya and Jiro then utilized the guns on the latter's new armor to punch through the city's electro barrier and joined in on the fight on the ground, slamming Empress Beatrix and her Empress Mech to the ground.

As the fight progressed, Jiro proposed for himself and Zanth to keep the empress busy while the others escaped. The Teen Resistance Force then led the ninja to their secret headquarters beneath the city, where Nya and Zane recognized parts of the Monastery of Imperium. Percival explained how Beatrix had ordered the monastery's destruction following the battle that had taken place outside some time ago, leading Nya to recognize why parts of the monastery's portal gate were found in the Land of Lost Things.

The ninja then split into three teams, with Nya accompanying Zane and Lloyd to the Imperium Palace to retrieve the two Dragon Cores that were in Beatrix's possession. By nightfall, the three still had yet to reach the palace and were ambushed again by the Good Empress - greatly impressing Nya. Although the three tried to fight her off, Beatrix's mech proved to be far too powerful. After the three were slammed against the side of a building, Sora's broadcast came through, exposing Beatrix's lies and turning the citizens of Imperium against her. With the way now clear, Nya, Zane, and Lloyd finally reached the palace gates but still faced a massive amount of guards. Before any of them could do anything further, the guards were notified that the Imperium Armory had been attacked, drawing all of them away much to Nya's surprise. However, Beatrix ambushed the three once more, now brandishing her MergeQuake weapon powered by the Dragon Cores the ninja had been seeking.

The Power Within[]

Facing the crazed Empress, Nya and the other two tried to talk her out of firing the MergeQuake weapon, with Nya warning that its use would create a MergeQuake storm that would destroy all of reality. Beatrix however ignored the warnings, branding them as lies before recklessly unleashing rifts across the entire city. Unable to stop her, Nya, Lloyd, and Zane were forced to retreat and regroup with the other ninja before attempting another attack against the Empress.

Following an unsuccessful attack, Nya watched helplessly as her brother was sucked into a rift. Nya was then pulled back behind a pile of debris by Rapton who urged her to fight together. Surprised by the dragon hunter's change of heart, Nya flatly asked when he was on their side, to which Rapton admitted that the end of the world was enough of a factor to do that. Although the two made an effort to attack Beatrix head on, Rapton was almost immediately pulled into a rift created by the Empress. Fortunately, Sora was able to unlock her true potential and dismantled the MergeQuake weapon with the Empress herself being knocked into another rift by Arin. In spite of stopping the more immediate threat, Zane informed the others that Beatrix's reckless actions have now initiated the MergeQuake Storm that they all had feared.

Needing the last dragon core to stop the MergeQuake storm, Nya and the other ninja hastily departed for the Crossroads just as the storm began in full. Upon arriving, Nya and the others worked to save civilians from being pulled into rifts while they waited for Mr. Frohicky to deliver the final core. Once it arrived, the ninja gathered together and tried to figure out a way to activate the cores, with Nya suggesting that they had to be placed together - a suggestion that did not work. Finally, Lloyd was able to activate the cores through his status as a conduit and ended the MergeQuake storm, bringing back all the people who had recently been sucked into rifts including Kai. Nya moved in to give her brother a hug but was beaten to it by Wyldfyre who ran over and tackled Kai to the ground with a massive hug.

The next day, Nya sat beside Zane on the fountain in the Monastery of Spinjitzu's courtyard and told him about everything she had experienced with Cole. When she spoke of how Cole also heard Wu's voice, Zane noted that this now made three of them who have received such communications. Arin then entered carrying a stack of pies and Nya excitedly rushed over to have one.
