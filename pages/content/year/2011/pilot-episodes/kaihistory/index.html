Overview

Early life

Early life[]

Kai was born in Ignacia, the first child of blackmiths Maya and Ray who owned the Four Weapons shop. In the first two months of his life, his parents called him "Ash".[1][2] He and his younger sister, Nya, were raised in their father's craft. Unknown to either of them, their parents were the Elemental Masters of Fire and Water. Kai inherited his father's element of Fire, while Nya inherited their mother's ability of Water though they were unaware of this fact at first.

When Kai and Nya were young, Ray would tell them stories of the dragons, mystical creatures that belonged to both the world of the living and of the dead.[3] He would often teach them proverbs such as "No matter how much fire you have, experience isn't something you learn overnight"[4] and "The world works in mysterious ways.[5] At one point, young Kai and Nya met Wu, a friend of their parents.[6]

At some point in their childhood, Kai and Nya invented a game in the Four Weapons Blacksmith they called Counter Shot, using a variety of obstacles and tools such as tables and horseshoes.[7]

When his parents disappeared, Kai took it upon himself to keep the family business going, vowing to surpass his father's blacksmith skills.[4] Kai took care of his sister while they wondered all their lives what became of their parents.[8] At one point, the two had a babysitter named Grumbmiller, who they disliked and would trip her when she made them go to bed early.[9] Because Grumbmiller's cooking was subpar, Nya would often volunteer to make dinner.[10]

Pilot episodes

Pilot episodes[]

Way of the Ninja[]

One day while working, Kai explained to Nya how to properly make a sword, but became too impatient and thus produced a disfigured weapon. He was reminded by his sister to be more patient, but were interrupted when an old man entered the shop. The potential customer voiced complaints concerning the lack of tools for ninja and Kai quickly shooed him away.

Shortly thereafter, Ignacia fell under attack by Samukai and his Skeleton army. Kai attempted to defend them with samurai armor, though it proved to be slow and clunky. While he managed to defeat several skeleton foot soldiers, he was quickly subdued by Samukai. Pinned down by his armor, Kai would have fallen victim to the Skulkin General had the old man—Master Wu—not returned and saved him. However, while they were preoccupied, the skeletons kidnapped Nya and escaped to the Underworld. The young blacksmith intended to go after them, but was stopped by Wu. He explained to Kai the story of the First Spinjitzu Master and his two sons who fought over the four Golden Weapons, explaining that an honest man—Kai's father—had been entrusted with the map to their resting places. Kai was advised by Wu to become a ninja to save his sister, and Kai reluctantly agreed.

Kai was taken to the Monastery of Spinjitzu in order to be trained as a ninja and learn Spinjitzu. During his training, Kai had to learn patience and how to harness the "fire" inside of him. This training consisted of running a course before Master Wu finishes his tea. Kai accomplished this after several days and did so by knocking the teacup out of his Master's hands when he was about halfway through the course. He was then told by Wu that he had one final test the next day, and should get some sleep.

The Golden Weapon[]

Later that night, while Kai was pondering Master Wu's training while brushing his teeth, he was set upon by three black-suited ninja. Their fight took them out to the courtyard where Kai activated the training course to even the odds. They were quickly stopped by Wu, who revealed that the three ninja were his students as well. Wu then used Creation to give each of the ninja a suit that matched their elemental properties and pronounced Kai as the red Master of Fire. After Wu explained their purpose, he announced that they would now leave to claim the first Golden Weapon.

On the way there, Kai and the others explained where and how Master Wu found them. Kai revealed that he is journeying to the Underworld to save his sister, to which Jay replied "We're saving a girl? Is she hot?"

Once they arrived at the Caves of Despair, Kai ran ahead of the other ninja while Cole was still formulating a plan. The others caught up to him at Samukai's watchtower, and after Zane stole the map of Golden Weapons, Kai raced ahead again. The other ninja followed him into one of the caves and found him attempting to remove a large boulder from blocking a hidden room. They did so, and Cole reminded him that they were a team as they moved it together. Inside they found the Scythe of Quakes, and removed it from its resting place. On their way out, Jay accidentally revealed their position to the skeleton army, and they were soon in a battle. After Jay discovered the key to unlocking his Spinjitzu, Kai and the others were quick to follow. They used it to drive off the skeleton army, after which they discovered that they had a bigger problem in the form of the weapon's guardian, the Earth Dragon. Kai happened to be holding the Scythe at the time and decided that the only way out was to use it, contrary to Master Wu's instructions. After escaping, Kai was strictly reprimanded by Master Wu, and they went onward toward the next weapon, the Shurikens of Ice.

King of Shadows[]

While on their way to the Frozen Wasteland, the location of the Shurikens of Ice, Wu told the ninja about the most powerful technique in Spinjitzu, the Tornado of Creation. Kai began to attempt it but was stopped by Master Wu, who explained that it could lead to disastrous consequences if done incorrectly.

After they succeeded in finding the Shurikens, the ninja ascended to the Floating Ruins for the Nunchucks of Lightning. After retrieving it, they used Jay's gliders to descend into the Forest of Tranquility, where they made camp. Kai was later awoken in the middle of the night and lured to the Fire Temple by Garmadon, who was pretending to be his sister. Kai was forced to remove the Sword of Fire from its pedestal to cut a chain and save his sister from falling. He was then attacked and defeated by his own shadow under Garmadon's manipulation, but Master Wu arrived before the dark lord could claim the sword. Realizing it had just been a trap to lure them away from the other ninja, the three attempted to escape but were stopped by the Fire Dragon. As a last resort, Wu sacrificed himself and ventured into the Underworld with the Sword of Fire. While his act kept the four Golden Weapons apart, Kai and Nya were still left in the hands of the Fire Dragon.

Weapons of Destiny[]

Luckily the Fire Dragon soon realized that they were simply trying to protect the weapons, and by the time the other ninja arrived, Kai and Nya had befriended the Dragon. They assembled the other guardian dragons and mounted them cross over into the Underworld.

Once there, the ninja attempted to use the stalactites to sneak past the guards of the Skulkin fortress but accidentally grabbed onto the legs of a Spykor. Out of fear, Kai and the others fell to the ground outside the fortress where they were quickly surrounded by Skulkin and Spykors alike. With their options limited Jay suggested they perform the Tornado of Creation and the ninja successfully performed the technique, turning the fortress into a Ferris Wheel prison. The ninja descended into the throne room where Master Wu was dueling Samukai for the Golden Weapons. Kai forbade the other ninja to interfere as it was their master's fight. Wu was quickly defeated, but when Samukai took the Sword of Fire, a portal between dimensions was formed, and Samukai was destroyed. This allowed Garmadon to escape from the Underworld.

Knowing that he would one day return, Kai took the Sword of Fire and vowed to protect it, as did the others with their respective weapons.

Kai and the other ninja returned to Four Weapons where they were reunited with Nya and assured the villagers they would be ready when Garmadon returned.

Mini-movies

Mini-movies[]

Flight of the Dragon Ninja[]

The ninja were told to train by Master Wu but instead opted to spend their time racing their dragons. Jay won the race and began bragging about his skills. Kai and the others challenged him to another race over the forest and back, with the winner becoming "Dragon Master" for a month. Zane objected, feeling that the ninja should be training, to which Kai replied that riding their dragons was a form of training.

The race begins, and Kai and Zane flew their dragons over the clouds, where they briefly dueled each other with their dragons' elemental breaths. On the home stretch, they were all neck-and-neck, but Cole won when he "activated the turbo"—a roasted chicken dangled in front of his dragon's nose. Once the others landed, Kai congratulated Cole on the win but said that they would get him the next day. Cole reminds the others that the winner was supposed to be "Dragon Master" for a month, and Kai and the others denied ever agreeing to the bet.

The New Masters of Spinjitzu[]

One night, Nuckal and Kruncha infiltrated the Monastery of Spinjitzu to steal the Golden Weapons from the ninja while they slept. Although the theft went off without a hitch, Kai was awoken when the Skulkin Generals lost control of the weapons and accidentally activated the training course. Half-asleep, Kai went outside to shut the course off, irritably reminding Jay that he had to repair whatever flaw was causing it to turn itself on. With that, Kai went back to sleep, completely unaware of Kruncha and Nuckal's presence in the monastery. They quickly returned the Weapons to the ninja with haste and returned to the Underworld.

Return to the Fire Temple[]

While taking a ride on the Fire Dragon, Kai and Nya were suddenly taken to the Fire Temple. After dismounting the Dragon, Flame led Kai to a passage connecting Ninjago to the Underworld. Kai started planning what to do when the ground falls out from beneath him. He found out that the Skulkin were assembling to reenter Ninjago, and got caught in a fight. He fought back, but they picked him up, disarming him. Nya and Flame then shot a blast of fire, which scared the Skulkin away and seals the passage to the Underworld.

Rise of the Snakes

Rise of the Snakes[]

Rise of the Snakes[]

With peace throughout Ninjago, Kai and the other ninja fell out of training and instead spent their time playing video games. After a three hour marathon, they were interrupted by Wu who unplugged the TV. He encouraged them not to put off tomorrow what could be done today, and to start training again to

prepare for his brother's return to Ninjago. Kai reminded his Master that if Garmadon is to show up, they have the Tornado of Creation. Wu sighed and told him and the others that they had barely scratched the surface of unlocking their True Potentials. Cole turned the TV back on, and the ninja continued playing.

At that moment, Nya rushed in, bringing word that Garmadon was spotted in Jamanakai Village. The out-of-shape ninja rushed out the door and down to the dragon pen where Kai mounted the Fire Dragon with the Nya's help.

Eager to get back into the fight, the ninja raced each other to Jamanakai Village. Upon arrival, Kai argued with Jay about who had arrived first. They were interrupted by the sound of evil laughter from the village, and they drew their weapons. They approached the village but were surprised to find that it was, in fact, Lloyd Garmadon, the dark lord's son, who was causing trouble. Lloyd threatened to release the serpentine, an ancient race of snakes that once ruled Ninjago unless they give him candy. The ninja tied him to the top of a sign and embarrassed him in front of the villagers as they mocked him by eating candy.

As they prepared to return to the monastery, Kai discovered a scroll in Wu's bag which he had taken by mistake. They read it and learned of the Prophecy of the Green Ninja. It was said that one ninja will rise above the others and become the Green Ninja. The scroll was also inscribed with drawings that are identical to the four ninja, with a fifth ninja in the middle. Kai believed himself to be the Green Ninja, as did the others, so when they returned to the monastery, the four Spinjitzu masters challenged themselves to a tournament for the title. Kai faced off against Jay in the first round and won, thus moving on to the final round against Cole. However, the tournament ended when Kai lost control of the Sword of Fire and the ninja were scolded by Master Wu.

Determined to be the Green Ninja, Kai and the others devoted themselves to training. They were soon ordered back to Jamanakai Village and find that Lloyd had unleashed the Hypnobrai tribe of Serpentine. Kai once again mounted the Fire Dragon and upon arriving at the village, met up with Nya. The ninja found that the Hyprobrai had the ability to hypnotize their victims into doing their bidding, and were doing so to the villagers. After discovering they would need the anti-venom from the Snake General's staff to save the village, the ninja split up and after a fight with the snakes, sent them retreating. After the villagers had been cured of their hypnotism, Kai presented Wu with the staff and apologized for their lazy behavior earlier. With a new threat now upon them, Kai and the others returned to the monastery determined to unlock the secrets of their Golden Weapons and to reach their True Potential.

Home[]

In the days that followed, Kai devoted himself to training at the monastery. While he was practicing Spinjitzu, Zane iced the course over, causing Kai to slip. He complained about Zane's strange behavior to Jay and Cole, only to be approached by Master Wu who noted that brothers are often different. Kai remembered one night when he went into the kitchen for a midnight snack, only to find that Zane had consumed everything but the cheese. The ninja's conversation was interrupted by the arrival of the Mailman who had a fan letter for Kai, and other mail for Cole and Jay. They made note that nothing has come for Zane, and Zane revealed that he has been an orphan all his life.

That night, Kai and the others gathered in the dining room and were surprised to see Zane emerge from the kitchen wearing a pink apron. The ninja laughed, and Zane failed to find it amusing. Cole dumped a plate of food on Kai's head and shortly after a food fight breaks out.

The next day, Zane told the others that last night he followed a mysterious bird to Lloyd's treehouse fortress. Together, they headed into the Forest of Tranquility and found it exactly where Zane said it would be.

Determined to destroy it before it becomes operational, Kai and the other ninja snuck into it and destroyed its supports. They reached the last support, but Cole, who was hypnotized by a serpentine earlier, turns on them. Before long, Master Wu came riding in on Flame playing an ancient flute, which canceled the Serpentine's powers. The group returned to the Monastery on Flame and discovered the Serpentine had set fire to it in order to steal back their staff.

Zane asked Shard to put the fire out but, the Monastery was beyond repair. Kai and the others verbally attacked Zane but Master Wu caught them reminding them that Zane was their teammate and had them apologize. Before they could, Zane flew off on Shard. Kai and the others assume that he was so hurt by their words that he ran away. Later on, the ninja ate around a fire on a cliff, homeless, until Nya noticed Zane entering the camp. He revealed to them that he was not hurt by their words, but saw the falcon again, and followed it to an abandoned ship. He told them that was their new home, and that he made them dinner.

Kai and the others joyfully ran towards their new home, ready to eat Zane's home-cooked food.

Snakebit[]

After arriving on Destiny's Bounty they found that it is in disrepair. Master Wu made the ninja clean up their new home while he ran an errand with Nya, which they were reluctant to do.

They proceeded in cleaning it up, dusting the sail, cleaning the cabins, and reworking the electronics. When finished, they put all the trash and junk in the bin outside.

Later on, while the ninja were relaxing, Jay's parents, Ed and Edna, arrive. They were given the tour of the Bounty by Jay, but he insisted that they leave due to the threat of the Serpentine. While there, they revealed that Jay was born in a junkyard, a fact which Jay is embarrassed about. Eventually, his parents were reluctant to go but leave before dark. Before going they implored that Jay visits them sometime soon. Jay agreed, and they headed out.

The next day as they were doing work on the bridge, the other ninja tried to convince Jay to visit his parents. Although he was reluctant at first, he agrees to go. He went to mount Wisp but found that he had no interest in going anywhere. Master Wu told the ninja that their dragons were molting, and were to return to the Spirit Coves in order to reach maturity. The ninja were disheartened, and Jay asked Nya to accompany him on the long walk. Kai and the others thought he wanted them all to go, and so the whole team accompanied him.

When the ninja arrive, they found Jay's parents nowhere to be found, until they found them locked in an old refrigerator. They learned that another tribe of serpentine, the Fangpyre, had bitten Jay's parents and infected them with venom, slowly turning them into serpentine.

Kai and the other ninja were ambushed by the Fangpyre, who, lead by Lloyd, had turned the whole junkyard into a snake infected area. Kai and the others defeated the snakes and attempted to escape. Master Wu told them that they could get out of there faster by turning their Golden Weapons into vehicles. Kai turned his sword into a bike and drove away as the others did the same. They escaped with Jay's parents after stealing the Snake General's staff, returned to the Bounty, and cured Ed and Edna with the anti-venom. The Bounty was rocked to the side by one of the Fangpyre helicopters, and they dropped the staff, returning it to the serpentine and Lloyd.

Never Trust a Snake[]

Zane was woken up with a start one morning and recalled that he had a dream. Kai and the others asked him what it was about, and he told them that he saw the green ninja. The ninja implored him to elaborate, but Master Wu interrupted and reminded them that it is time for training.

On the deck, Wu teached them different poses, all the while they were ignoring him to learn more about the dream. Wu caught them slacking off, and told them that as punishment they must train all day and come up with the answer to a riddle, "What is the best way to defeat an enemy?" Kai failed to interpret the riddle along with the other ninja, so Master Wu left them to train.

Zane finished telling them about the dream, and Kai assumed himself to be the green ninja, due to his fists being aflame. The ninja argued over who was the real green ninja and got back to training.

Ninjago City vs. Ninja[]

Following their trial in Ninjago City v. Ninja, Kai and the others were convicted and now awaited sentencing. As they waited, Kai blasted Dareth for his poor showing as their defense attorney, though Dareth protested that he only had three weeks of law classes (most of which focused on getting out of traffic tickets). When the conversation shifted over whether Judge William Toughbutt's last name or nickname was "Toughbutt", Kai dismissively stated his position that no one's last name could possibly be "Toughbutt," only to be proven wrong moments later when the Honorable Judge William Toughbutt took the stand. Judge Toughbutt then subsequently sentenced all five ninja to five years in Kryptarium Prison for breaking Aspheera out of prison and stealing her staff from its armored convoy.

When the ninja were escorted to their cell later that night, Kai grumbled sadly about their predicament, mumbling how after all they had done to protect Ninjago, they were now the ones being locked behind bars. After arriving at their cell, Zane pointed out how there were only four beds present despite there being five ninja sharing the same cell. Bewildered by Zane's comment, Kai expressed his feelings about how Zane's worry about their beds probably wasn't the most important thing at the moment. When Zane then suggested reactivating his emotions to provide an appropriate reaction to the ninja's current circumstances, Kai suggested that it could be a good idea - only to be instantly proven wrong again when Zane let out an ear-piercing scream, forcing Kai to cover his mouth in an effort to mute the screaming. Turning his attention now once more to his and his friends' imprisonment, Kai proposed breaking out of Kryptarium and ignited flames around his fists to emphasize his faith in the idea. Lloyd however, turned down Kai's suggestion, warning that escaping from prison would only create more problems for the ninja, and instead suggested that Dareth could find a way to have them released legally. Upon hearing this suggestion, Kai broke down into peals of laughter before recomposing himself when he realized that Lloyd wasn't telling a joke.

The next day, the ninja were let out into the prison's yard, where many of their former enemies waited and glared angrily in their direction. Aiming to break the tension, Kai rhetorically asked his friends if any of them had the feeling that the other inmates were staring at them, though Zane answered straight-forwardly by stating that all of the other inmates were in fact, staring at them. Seconds later, Jay sauntered back to the group and excitedy informed them that he had gotten them entrance into The Needles - supposedly the toughest gang in Kryptarium, prompting Kai to sarcastically compliment Jay for his efforts. When Ronin approached the ninja and expressed his desire for revenge moments later, Kai stood alongside his friends in a face-down against Ronin's group but said nothing. Just before tensions could boil over, the Main Guard broke up both sides' rising hostilities and escorted the ninja, save Lloyd, to their cells. As for Lloyd, he was taken to meet an apparent visitor.

When Lloyd returned to their cell later on and told them about his mysterious run in with a stranger in service to someone called "The Crystal King," Kai wondered aloud as to who or what was a "Crystal King."

Kryptarium Prison Blues[]

Alongside the other ninja, Kai was let out of his cell to attend a visitation session with Skylor and Dareth, the latter of whom claimed to have discovered a way to have the ninja released from prison. Unfortunately, Dareth's grand plan was to request a retrial on the grounds that they didn't like the judge's verdict, much to the frustration of Kai and the others. When the conversation pivoted to Vangelis' escape from prison and the Crystal King, Kai dismissed the threat as being nothing more than "villain jabber" from a "weirdo in a Kabuki mask." When the ninja were led back to their cell once visitation time ended, Kai began following the others but held off when he noticed that Jay (who was behind him), stayed behind to speak with Skylor. Kai overheard their conversation about Nya's current state and Kai offered Jay some words of encouragement, insisting that his sister always got back up whenever she was knocked down.

Around lunchtime, Kai sat with the other ninja in Kryptarium's courtyard, picking at bowls of green gloop. As they complained about the quality of the food, Pythor, whom the ninja had not seen in years, approached their table and made several dismissive comments about them and there situation, infuriating Kai who rose to his feet and prepared to attack but was restrained by Cole and Lloyd. Unfortunately, Kai's temper won out, and he struck Pythor across the face with his serving tray, provoking a food fight with Pythor and several other inmates. Immediately after being hit, Pythor retaliated against Kai by snatching one of the rock hard slices of cake off the tray of a nearby prisoner and hurled it Kai with enough force to knock the Ninja of Fire into the wall behind him. This altercation would soon be halted by the Main Guard who ordered for all the prisoners involved to return to their cells.

Later that night, as Kai and the others tried to sleep, Lloyd shouted for all of them to awake, having witnessed several Crystal Spiders crawling past towards Pythor's cell. Realizing that the crystal spiders meant there was truth behind the warning about the Crystal King, Kai leapt to his feet and shouted for the other ninja to stand back while he melted the bars of their cell. Kai and the others then rushed off to intercept Pythor from escaping but were forced to stand back when the Crystal Spider that Jay took out activated its self destruct mechanism. Unsatisfied and determined to prevent Pythor from escaping, Kai quickly rose to his feet and impulsively hurled two fireballs at Pythor, but missed and instead set one of Kryptarium's external watchtowers ablaze. Realizing the error of his actions, Kai could only stand nervously as he and the other ninja were surrounded by Kryptarium's guards.

Hounddog McBrag[]

Kai and the other ninja were awoken by the Main Guard to report for work. As he exited their cell, Kai remarked that he did not know prisoners were put to work, though Zane was quick to note that this was actually a very common practice. As he stepped onto the prison bus to be transported to his work site, Kai took a seat in the back of the bus and listened as the Main Guard explained how they would all be cleaning up litter from Route 22.

As the bus traveled alongside the Sea of Sand and briefly stopped for the guards to assist an elderly man with a broken engine, Kai and several other prisoners stepped forward to peer out the window in an attempt to see what was going on. Seconds later, Nya landed behind the prison bus in her new Samurai X Mech and tore open the backdoor, allowing for Kai and the other ninja to escape. The elderly man then drove his van over to where the ninja were hiding and gestured for them to board, taking off at high speed and leaving the guards behind!

After driving for some time, the man pulled off his hat and fake beard, revealing his identity as none other than Dareth, who explained that this was the "last resort" plan he had in mind. After shaking off a lone police cruiser, Dareth's engine actually gave out, and after Kai briefly examined the engine, he exclaimed that they had to quickly get off the road before the police arrived. After putting on their new uniforms provided by P.I.X.A.L., the ninja and Dareth began to traverse the desert on foot. After some time of walking through the sweltering sands, Kai mumbled aloud if anyone knew where they were, though none did. As night fell and the police began combing the area in search of the ninja, Lloyd proposed for the ninja to split into two groups: he would go alongside Jay, Cole, and Fugidove in one direction while Kai, Zane, and Dareth would go the other. Their objective was to find Twitchy Tim's gas station to ask their old ally for his assistance. With no complaints, Kai, Zane, and Dareth began walking off in the opposite direction in search of Twitchy Tim's gas station.

The Benefit of Grief[]

After roaming across the Sea of Sand from night till morning, Kai and his group were all but exhausted; or at least, everyone but Zane. As they neared an open highway, Kai spotted a van driving their way, and he began to run towards it, frantically waving his hands in the air while crying for help. Fortunately, the driver spotted Kai in the rearview mirror and pulled over on the side of the road, asking the group what they were doing in the Sea of Sand. Thinking quickly, Kai claimed that they were camping and got lost, though he was left at a loss for words when the driver asked why he and the others were dressed like the ninja. Fortunately, Dareth stepped in to add to Kai's lie, claiming that they had gone "costume camping," and that it was the latest trend! The driver was convinced by these assertations and kindly offered the three a lift in her van.

As they began driving off, Dareth asked the driver if she was sure that she didn't recognize Kai or Zane, causing Kai clear distress as he nervously chuckled and stated simply that Dareth was only cracking a joke. He then elbowed Dareth in the shoulder and silently glared at him with a brief headshake before changing the topic to the driver, having noticed on a guitar case that her name was Sally, asking if she was moving. Sally then affirmed Kai's assumption, explaining that she was heading to Ninjago City to pursue her dreams of becoming a famous singer, even revealing that the song being played on her radio was one that she had written. As Sally turned on the volume of her radio, Kai seemed to enjoy Sally's song and bopped his head as he listened to it. Unfortunately, Zane's seemingly innocent question about a photograph on the van's dashboard that depicted Sally and her parents led to the young woman suddenly breaking down with heavy sobbing, losing control over the van in the process! Alarmed by the situation, Kai frantically gasped for Zane to do something, which Zane obliged by pulling the van's emergency brake.

When the van came to a stop, Sally rushed out the door crying, and Kai and the others stepped out to comfort her, though Kai stopped short of doing so when he noticed how hard she was sobbing. The ninja and Dareth then learned that Sally had run away from home after her parents asked her to wait a few years until she was older before pursuing her dream of becoming a singer, leading for her to take her father's van without asking in an effort to pursue her dreams. But now however, the young woman could only feel a sense of regret and shame for abandoning her parents without another word and damaging the van during her journey to Ninjago City. At that moment, a Ninjago Police helicopter flew by, and after spotting the ninja, turned around to order for them to stand down. Sally misinterpreted this situation as her father calling the police on her and she immediately ran back into her van and sped off, even while Kai tried to explain that the police weren't after her. As she began driving off, Kai frantically shouted for his allies to run after her and climb onboard the fleeing van.

In the chase that followed, Kai gripped his seat tightly as Sally nearly ran into the police helicopter when it landed in the street in an attempt to end the chase. When Sally then drove off into the desert and barely stopped at the edge of a ravine, she realized that the police were not after her, but rather her guests, causing Kai to exasperatedly state that he had been trying to tell her this for the entirety of the chase. When Sally demanded to know why the ninja were being hunted by the police, Kai sheepishly restated Sally's own words, claiming that they had "borrowed something without asking," though Sally admonished him instead for stealing! At that moment, the ground beneath Sally's van gave way, taking it and its occupants into the rapid flowing river below.

As the van's occupants rode the rushing waters, Kai was shocked to discover that Hounddog McBrag drove his own SUV into off the ravine after them, incredulously asking who this insane man was. Eventually, the ninja were able to lose McBrag when Zane froze the entire river behind the van, trapping McBrag's SUV while they escaped. After driving a short while later, Sally stopped her van to begin sobbing again, though after some encouragement and reassurance from the ninja, she drove back to her family farm to return to her parents. Kai and his allies then watched with broad smiles as Sally and her parents were reunited before turning and walking off into the distance to continue their long journey home.

The Fifth Villain[]

After traveling through the desert for some time, Kai and his group found their way to Twitchy Tim's gas station far ahead of the other group. As the sun blazed on overhead, Kai, Zane, and Dareth now sat around Twitchy's cool, shaded porch where they refreshed themselves with bottles of crisp, delightful water. After some time, Kai downed yet another bottle as Zane called out the arrival of the other group of ninja, pointing towards Cole who was frantically rushing up to the porch. Mistakingly believing that Cole was rushing forward to see them, Kai extended one of his hands towards his friend while greeting him warmly, but Cole brushed Kai off to rush into the gas station while begging for water, leaving Kai alone to scratch his head in confusion.

Once all the ninja were settled in, they began to discuss their next moves regarding the Crystal King, learning from Fugi-Dove the existence of a Crystal Council. Deciding it would be most efficient to capture and replace the next villain who was likely to be recruited, the ninja began to brainstorm which of their remaining, non-deceased, foes could be invited next, with Kai suggesting the possibilities of either the Hands of Time or Garmadon being likely candidates. Kai's suggestions, however, would be shot down by Lloyd who pointed out that no one knew of the current whereabouts of Acronix, Krux, or even Garmadon, and thus it seemed unlikely they would be approached to join the Crystal King. Jay then suggested the possibility of the Mechanic being recruited to the council, and all of the ninja agreed that he seemed to be the most likely choice. Before they could make any further moves however, Hounddog McBrag and his squadron of officers arrived outside of the gas station and called for the ninja to surrender.

As the police entered the store to search for the ninja, Kai and the others dove behind the store's counter but were soon given up by Twitchy Tim. Before anyone could do anything else, Fugi-Dove revealed himself and attacked the officers, taking attention away from the ninja just long enough for them to escape outside. There, Kai destroyed one of the two police SUVs with a fireball, flipping the vehicle over while setting its chassis ablaze. Once they ensured that they could not be followed, Kai and the others stole the other SUV to begin driving to Ninjago City.

Night had fallen by the time the ninja returned to Ninjago City and investigated the lair of the Mechanic. Although the derelict arcade seemed devoid of any sign of the once small time crook, Kai pointed out that the presence of many robo arms suggested his lingering presence. At that moment, the Mechanic revealed himself, attacking the ninja with his lair's many mechanical arms. During the fight, Kai attempted to strike at the Mechanic with Spinjitzu, but was ripped out of his tornado by the Mechanic's extendable robo-arm and was hurled into Cole, knocking the Ninja of Earth out of his own Spinjitzu tornado as well. Nevertheless, the ninja managed to defeat the Mechanic by dropping an arcade cabinet for Prime Empire onto him and Kai laughed at Jay's joke that poked fun at the irony of the Mechanic's situation.

Once the Mechanic was tied up, Kai and the other ninja, save Lloyd, brought their prisoner onboard his own Noodle Truck of Crime, intending to use it to locate the Council of the Crystal King.

The Council of the Crystal King[]

With Lloyd's tracker on the move, Kai took the wheel of the Noodle Truck of Crime and began navigating across Ninjago City's streets with Zane's directions. As he drove, Kai briefly expressed disdain over Jay's question as to why Lloyd was going undercover instead of Zane, reminding the Ninja of Lightning that Zane could not go undercover because only he was able to access the tracker's location.

After some time, Lloyd's location suddenly sped off very quickly, forcing Kai to press on the truck's acceleration in an effort to catch up. Unfortunately, the Mechanic managed to untie himself at this moment and began fighting against Cole and Jay while also activating some of the truck's gadgets and causing it to flip over. Kai and the other ninja then pursued the Mechanic down into the subway tunnels where he would be knocked into the path of an oncoming train by the villain. Reacting to the imminent threat, Kai used his Spinjitzu to leap back onto the platform ledge and subsequently hurled a fireball at the Mechanic, though the villain was able to evade his attack.

During the fight that broke out, Kai caught Cole after the latter was thrown through the air by the Mechanic, but both would be knocked into the tracks once more by the Mechanic's extendable robo-arm. Before they could recover, Jay's misdirected bolts of electricity ran through the metal train tracks and shocked Kai and Cole into submission just as another train made its approach through the tunnels. Fortunately, both of them would be carried to safety by Zane, and Kai would once again attempt to retaliate with a fireball, but this attack too would be dodged. As the Mechanic escaped along the passing train, Kai informed the other ninja that they would have to warn Lloyd about their failure at keeping the Mechanic restrained before the real Mechanic could meet up with the rest of the Council.

The Spider's Design[]

Kai and the other three ninja made their way down towards the N line, having tracked Lloyd's last known location as being within its vicinity. As they came across a derelict subway car, Kai dismissed it as a "rusted piece of junk," and expressed confusion over Jay's admiration for it. As Jay began to gush over the car while identifying it as a BRTM-12, Kai sarcastically asked Jay if he collected toy trains as a kid, discovering to his surprise that he was half right about the claim. Nevertheless, Kai remained unimpressed by the subway car and impatiently asked for Jay to continue moving so that they could find Lloyd.

As the ninja neared the end of the N line, Zane informed the others that his sensors were picking up a peculiar tapping sound in the surrounding area. Unsure of the source, Kai ordered the others to spread out and search for the source of the sound. When the source was revealed to be the clattering of hundreds of Crystal Spiders armed to detonate, Kai and the others began running back down the tunnel from whence they came. As they ran, Kai tripped twice and was hastily helped back to his feet by Cole before finally leaping into the BRTM-12 just as the spiders detonated. Once the rubble settled, Kai ignited a flame to illuminate the darkened train car and watched on as his allies celebrated their survival. As he listened on to their conversation, Kai briefly shook his head in disappointment when Jay stated that he had no idea how the ninja were to escape from the now entombed train car.

The Fall of the Monastery[]

With the initial joy of survival having worn off, Kai and the other ninja began examining their circumstances more closely. Acknowledging that they were buried under a massive amount of rubble, Kai asked Cole if the latter could use his powers to safely clear a way out. Although Cole tried to do so, Kai and the others quickly realized that Cole would not be able to move any of the stones without causing the BRTM-12 ceiling to completely cave in. With no other options, Kai attempted to radio the Monastery for backup, only to discover that his signal could not penetrate the huge amount of debris. Kai then gave his communicator over to Zane who offered to attempt to amplify the signal.

Mere seconds after successfully reaching the team stationed at the Monastery, the train car's ceiling began to collapse from the weight of the debris, forcing Kai and the others to hastily evacuate through an emergency exit located on the car's floor. Kai would be the last ninja to exit the train, just narrowly managing to avoid being crushed.

Darkness Within[]

Although Kai and the other ninja managed to barely escape from certain doom, they still remained buried beneath the damaged BRTM-12 in the collapsed subway tunnels. The experience of leaping from the frying pan into the fire had so far done little to improve Kai's spirits, for he was just as standoffish and short with his allies as he had been before, snapping at Cole and Zane for their respective commentaries on their situation and rats respectively. Kai's tone would slightly improve however when Zane revealed that rats were experts at sneaking through narrow crevices, and by following the rat, the ninja were able to begin ascending to the surface.

Unfortunately, their ascension was cut short when Jay slipped and shifted the debris around them, forcing Kai to save the lightning ninja from being struck by a large piece of loose concrete. As they sat along in their 'cave', Kai tried to remain hopeful, noting that the rest of their team were now aware of their situation and would soon be arriving. To help pass the time, Jay offered to sing a folksong about cursed ramen, though neither Kai nor the others were familiar with the song. Unfortunately, Jay's song was accompanied by a small dance and that had the consequence of further disturbing the ninja's already unstable position. As a massive piece of asphalt fell atop the ninja, Kai and the others tried their best to hold up the large chunk of debris, but found their strength quickly slipping away. Just as they were about to accept their fate, the ninja felt the piece of debris grow lighter and turned upwards awe as they glimpsed sunlight for the first time in a while.

With some help from Nya and her new mech, Kai and the others finally stepped into the streets of Ninjago City, out from the subway tunnels. They then learned from her that the Council of the Crystal King had destroyed the Monastery of Spinjitzu and that Harumi had returned from her seeming death all those years ago. Without time to lose, the ninja climbed aboard the mech and flew off to the old Samurai X Cave in the Sea of Sand. As they neared the entrance to the hideout, Nya's mech gave out from its sustained damages, landing just outside of the base as Kai commented on the timing of the mech's failure. After entering the hideout, Kai and his sister received a warm hug from Skylor, much to Kai's obvious relief and delight at seeing her again. He then asked Wu and the others if Harumi had truly returned and what she could possibly want with the Golden Weapons. Although none knew the answer to the second question, Kai knew for sure that whatever her plan was, it could not have been good and she has had many years to plan.

The Coming of the King[]

Kai and Cole were working together on one of the drills for the ninja Ultra Combo Mech. Kai was confident that he had fixed it and encouraged Cole to test it. Unfortunately, as soon as Cole activated the drill, it unexpectedly burst into flames. In a state of panic, Kai urgently told Cole to turn it off, and Master Wu swiftly extinguished the fire. Soon, Nya revealed that Lloyd's tracker was now back online and located in Primeval's Eye. Wu firmly declared that they needed to retrieve the Bounty, even though it was impounded and locked away with the other vehicles. He acknowledged that the only solution was to steal them, but would mean breaking the law once again. Kai respectfully addressed Master Wu, expressing that he and the others had already made their decision.

Return to Primeval's Eye[]

Standing atop an adjacent rooftop, Kai and the other four ninja carefully planned their move on the Ninjago City Impound Lot. After infiltrating the impound lot, Kai retook his Golden Dragon Raider and deactivated its camouflage when they were discovered by the security guards, seeing no point in keeping it active anymore. With their vehicles recovered, the ninja sped off to Primeval's Eye where Kai established a perimeter around the jungle to patrol alongside Jay and Cole. As they patrolled, the three were contacted by Wu and Kai affirmed their current positioning before Wu ordered them all to retreat.

Crystastrophe[]

In their vehicles, Kai, Cole, and Jay were ordered to intercept something in the Farmers' village; Wu had neglected to give them specific details as to what they were to encounter, having only stated that they wouldn't miss it for their target was obvious. Not long after, the three stopped their vehicles in disbelief as they caught a glimpse of the Crystal Island floating in the skies above them.

As an army of Crystal Warriors rained down upon the village, Kai ordered his allies to begin making attack runs. During this process, the ninja learned that their foes were made entirely out of Vengestone and strange crystals, the latter of which Kai questioned regarding their functions. The answer would be revealed seconds later as the three watched to their horror as one of the villagers was grabbed by the crystal warriors and transformed into a Crystal zombie. Startled by what he had witnessed, Kai emphasized the importance of not touching the Warriors to the other ninja, following it up afterwards with a warning not to get blasted by one either after witnessing the results of the latter on an unfortunate villager. He and the other two continued fighting on the ground as Zane arrived to back them up. Kai then ordered Zane to take out the Crystal Island, though this endeavor would end in failure as the ninja continued to lose ground in the battle.

After several rounds of fighting, P.I.X.A.L. requested for the ninja to collect a piece of crystal for her to analyze, suggesting that after some study, she might be able to develop counter-measures for it. In the end, Jay was able to break off a piece from one of the spears wielded by the crystal warriors, knocking it towards Kai who caught it in a compartment of his Golden Dragon Raider. With the fragment secured, Kai and the others retreated to deliver their quarry to P.I.X.A.L.

A Lesson in Anger[]

After returning to the Samurai X Cave, Kai and the other ninja gathered around as Wu told them about his discovery of the Oni form and its potential ability to defeat the Crystal army. When Kai asked Wu how he learned about this Oni form, the answer to Kai's question soon revealed itself in the form of Garmadon. Unsurprisingly, the sight of the ninja's old foe alarmed Kai, who immediately ignited his hands and prepared to attack Garmadon. However, Garmadon insisted that he had changed since their last encounter years prior and now aspired to be a kind and patient teacher towards Lloyd so that the latter could achieve Oni form.

As Lloyd struggled to achieve Oni form, Kai and the others watched on with increasing discomfort as Garmadon began lambasting Lloyd for every perceived failure. This led Kai to suggest that Garmadon had no idea how to even achieve Oni form, let alone teach it, though Garmadon retorted in response that one would have to embrace anger and channel that rage through their face. Eventually, Lloyd snapped under Garmadon's comments and inadvertently accessed his dormant power over Destruction, which Kai hesitantly pointed out by informing Lloyd that the former's hands were purple. Not long after, P.I.X.A.L. informed the team that she had completed upgrades to the ninja's vehicles, enabling them to challenge the Crystal army head on. In response to this news, Kai and the other three original ninja rushed to their vehicles and sped off towards Ninjago City.

Brave But Foolish[]

Arriving in the streets of Ninjago City, Kai was quick to activate his Golden Dragon Raider's new upgrades and immediately testing them against a massive platoon of Crystal Warriors, shattering them instantly. Delighted by these results, Kai continued his onslaught, firing his raider's cannons or simply running over the warriors. Eventually, Kai's frequent usage of the Raider's energy shielding began overheating his engine and forced him to reluctantly deactivate it for the time being.

Kai then drove alongside Cole in the streets of the city in search of more enemies to fight, though both ninja's focus left them unprepared for a sneak attack from a Dragonide. As Kai's raider was lifted into the air, Cole gave his friend a cursory glance and remarked that he was unaware that flight was part of Kai's raider's capabilities. The confusion over Cole's comment led Kai to realize his situation, and after several fruitless attempts to shoot behind him, the Dragonide dropped Kai's raider atop of Cole's dragon cruiser. Surprised by the sudden collision, Cole lost control over his vehicle and ended up crashing both his and Kai's vehicles shortly thereafter.

Safe Haven[]

Awakening with a groan, Ka briefly examined his surroundings before recalling the crash. As he glanced downwards, Kai spotted Cole lying unconscious below and began to call out towards him, hoping to rouse him. When mere whispers failed, Kai exited his vehicle's damaged cockpit to retrieve Cole, hearing an all too familiar voice nearby. Kai then dragged Cole's semi-conscious body behind a dumpster as Pythor and his Crystal Warriors searched the surrounding area for the two ninja. When they were finally discovered by Pythor, Kai responded by knocking away the dumpster and hurling several fireballs at their old foe, though Pythor extinguished the flames with his Nunchucks of Destruction.

Fortunately, Skylor arrived just in time to back them up, copying Kai and Cole's powers and using them in conjunction to hold off Pythor and his warriors. As they searched for shelter, Kai and Cole learned from Skylor that the rest of the ninja were scattered across the city and that the Destiny's Bounty had crashed in separate areas of the sprawling metropolis. Kai then had an idea and led his allies to Benny's Arcade, declaring that if they had to hide somewhere, may as well hide somewhere awesome! Seconds later, Kai was startled by Jake, who had also hid in the arcade because it was awesome. Although Kai recognized Jake's face, he could not recall the young boy's name until he stated it.

After receiving Wu's broadcast, Kai realized that his usage of the paperboys' motto ("The news never sleeps!"), was a clue serving to lead other survivors to shelter. He then began to lead his allies to the Newspaper warehouse, scouting on ahead for any dangers that might come their way. Upon reaching the warehouse entrance, the group were surprised by the presence of a large group of Crystal Zombies, forcing Kai and Skylor to activate their fire powers in an effort to ward off the infected. Fortunately, the paperboys swooped in time to provide backup, hurling stacks of newspapers to knock out the zombies and clear the way forward. Upon entering the warehouse, Kai and Cole were given a warm hug by Wu, who was glad to see that they had survived. While Cole ran on ahead to see what he could do for the Resistance, Kai hung back momentarily with Skylor as they watched Jake reunite with his parents.

Compatible[]

In the newspaper warehouse, Kai stood before the citizens of Ninjago, empathizing with their fear and exhaustion caused by the crystal army's invasion. To lift their spirits, he and Skylor discovered bowls of noodles in the warehouse and distributed them among the crowd. As they gave the noodles out, Kai mustered the courage to ask Skylor to be his girlfriend. However, she was understandably annoyed by his prolonged absence and his desire to start a relationship amidst the chaos of the crystal infection. Kai then shared his journey after the Oni's invasion, and Skylor suggested they prioritize saving Ninjago before delving into their relationship.

Distress Calls[]

Alongside Skylor, Kai oversaw the training of the members of the Resistance.

An Issue of Trust[]

Kai reunited with the rest of the ninja outside the newspaper warehouse, where he shared a hug with P.I.X.A.L. He and the others were then ushered inside where Wu went over his discovery of Dragon Form. As Wu explained the concept of Dragon Form, Kai realized that the procedure required the ninja to "Jump up, kick back, whip around, and spin." He and the other ninja, save Nya, then attempted to perform the art though to no avail.

After several hours of failed attempts, Kai conceded to Wu his feeling that their attempts to achieve the technique were not working. At that moment, Cyrus Borg informed the Resistance that the Crystal King was moving his entire army towards the newspaper warehouse. Alarmed by this news, Kai urgently voiced that they had to move the civilians out from the warehouse, though Cole was quick to bring up counterpoints to Kai's insistence of haste. They decided to pay a visit to Kreel's Junkyard, as Kreel had been advertising these hazardous items for days.

Upon their arrival, Lobbo inadvertently absorbed a surge of energy from one of the items, resulting in a transformation that fueled his anger and led him to attack Kai and the ninja. Despite their efforts to stop Lobbo, Kai and the ninja found themselves overpowered. Sora swiftly constructed three mechs, assigning one of them to Kai. Initially unimpressed by the appearance of his assigned mech, Kai dismissed it as a mere frisbee. However, his skepticism quickly turned to awe as he activated the mech and witnessed its remarkable capabilities. Lobbo was held back by Kai and Cole, while Sora extracted the perilous energy from Lobbo, restoring him to his normal state. With caution, the ninja confiscated all the hazardous objects from Kreel. Kai proposed an idea to use these items to enhance his slide course, which earned disapproving looks from the rest of the ninja. Inside the Maze[]

As they wandered through the Nether-Space, Kai and Bonzle noticed the Forbidden Five speaking with someone. It was at that moment they saw Rox among them. Kai pointed out to Bonzle that Rox was the only member of the Forbidden Five they had yet to encounter. Their curiosity piqued when they overheard the Forbidden Five discussing plans to secure their release, which captivated Kai's interest. Once the Forbidden Five left, Kai and Bonzle approached Rox, who lay in a state resembling a coma. Kai confirmed that Rox was alive, though unresponsive. In Kai's attempt to rouse Rox, Bonzle noticed something glowing on Kai's arm. Upon inspection, Kai discovered it was scales from Riyu, remnants that had fallen onto him during the Ritual of the Blood Moon. This discovery prompted Bonzle to realize that these scales established a psychic link between Kai and Riyu, hinting at the possibility of communication between them. Encouraged by this insight, Kai decided to reach out to Riyu. Riyu heard Kai in his head.
