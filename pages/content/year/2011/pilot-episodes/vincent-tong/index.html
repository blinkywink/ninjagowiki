Overview

External links
Vincent Tong (born May 2, 1980) is a Chinese-Canadian[3] voice actor, well known for providing the voice of Kai in Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu and Ninjago: Dragons Rising. He has also provided the voices for Bizarro Kai, Green Ninja (in Zane's vision), Mailman, Snike, Muffled Judge, Lizaru, Subway Controller #1, Lasha, Mezmo, Spitta, Chokun, Tommy, Nindroid Warriors, Sleven, Thug, Restaurant Owner, Chamille, Guard #1, General Venomari, Simon, Doubloon, the Sentry General, Ray, Luke Cunningham/White Mask (Episode 75), Mohawk, Tommy, Officer, Mambo's advisor, Uthaug, Sushimi, Whiplash, Prison Driver, Joey, and PoulErik's second head. He later voiced Koenig, Melvin, Elderly Man 2, Lava-Tide 1, Zane-Alike 1, Fritz, Admin Worker 1, Citizen 3, Driver, Grouce, Bleckt, Zarkar, Geckle, and Admin Office Worker 1 in Ninjago: Dragons Rising. He also voices Fuzz and Bolsen in Kai's Monstrous Journey.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • His other voice acting credentials include Death Note (Touta Matsuda and a Field Reporter), Iron Man: Armored Adventures (Gene Khan and The Mandarin), Voltron Force (Daniel), Super Monsters (Drac Shadows), Packages from Planet X (Dan), LEGO Jurassic Park: Legend of Isla Nublar (Dr. Henry Wu) and My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic (Prince Blueblood, Garble, Donut Joe, Feather Bangs and Sandbar).
    • He also starred in My Little Pony: Equestria Girls (Flash Sentry) and Kit vs Kat (Henry Chan).
  • As of 2015, he voices Muck in the US version of Bob The Builder.
  • He also starred in The Deep (Antaeus Nekton).
  • He had voiced Brax Lectrus in the 2016 movie release of Ratchet & Clank.
  • Besides Ninjago, he also worked on other LEGO TV series, voicing Jestro, Roger the Scrubber and Roberto Arnoldi in Nexo Knights, Tidus Stormsurfer in LEGO Elves, and Logan Chan and Sneak in DREAMZzz.
  • He owns a YouTube channel with over five thousand subscribers.
  • He also made a live-action appearance in A Fairly Odd Summer as Dr. Flemmish.
  • The Blade Cycle is his favourite vehicle from the series.[4]
  • Ninjago isn't the only series that Vincent Tong has worked with both Kirby Morrow and Kelly Metzger in. The three have also interacted with each other in Death Note. Paul Dobson also voiced a character in the same scene as him.
  • Just like Kirby Morrow, Vincent Tong voices a ninja and his father (the former having voiced Cole and Lou, and the latter Kai and Ray).
    • This means that he also voices two Elemental Masters of Fire in the series.
  • He, along with Adrian Petriw, Dean Redman, Mackenzie Gray, Michael Adamthwaite, Ashleigh Ball, Michael Daingerfield, Michael Dobson, Brian Drummond, Andrew Francis, Michael Kopsa, Richard Newman, Mark Oliver, Tabitha St. Germain, and Lee Tockar voiced characters in Iron Man: Armored Adventures.
  • He has a running joke of asking self-congratulatory questions to other crew members in Brent Miller's interviews.[5][6]
    • In a similar vein, crew members will often jokingly criticize Tong's performance as Kai.[7][8]
  • Tong became part of the voice cast of the Johnny Test revival series, making him the only voice actor to do so after joining Ninjago instead of before, and the ninth one to work on it; the others being Andrew Francis, Trevor Devall, Richard Newman, Scott McNeil, Marÿke Hendrikse, Kathleen Barr, Ian James Corlett, and Lee Tockar.
  • In DREAMZzz, he voices Logan, who is one of the main characters of the show, working alongside Brian Drummond and Giles Panton.
