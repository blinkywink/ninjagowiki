Overview

Relaxation room[]

The relaxation room is a living room in the monastery where the ninja usually relax while playing video games. There is also an entrance to the hangar bay. The relaxation room also acts like a bedroom for Arin, Sora, and Riyu.

History

History[]

Appearance

Appearance[]

The relaxation room was originally a small room with a daybed and tv. After the monastery was renovated, a small table and another daybed were added, and a plant was added in the corner with Jay's underwear hanging on it. A few water bottles were placed nearby, and a picture of the ninja and Wu before the Final Battle is hung on the walls, along with a weapons stand.

After the Oni's invasion, the relaxation room was moved to the middle of the monastery, with two purple couches and a table in front of the bigger TV, and a billiard table behind the couch facing the TV. Multiple boxes were present on the floor, and many paintings and stands for katanas were hung on the wall. A dartboard is hung near the TV, with a button behind it that access the elevator to the Hangar bay that is behind the TV. The relaxation room has doors on three of the four walls, while the wall behind the TV features a long black and white painting of mountains.

Years after the Merge, Arin, Sora, and Riyu moved into the monastery and used the relaxation room for their bedroom. The room was slightly changed, with a few more lamps hanging from the ceiling, with small light bulbs connected with strings hanging on the walls, multiple potted plants were added around, Arin's porch swing was placed where the billiard table once was with a carpet under it used by Riyu, with a dark blue hammock used by Arin placed in a corner, and a fuchsia bed for Sora placed next to it. A bigger stand for weapons was added and a smaller one for Arin's cooking utensils, an oven was placed on the corner of the room, and a poster for the Mech Master 5000 was hung on the walls.

Wu's room[]

This room is a small, almost empty room that was inhabited by Master Wu. It is located in the left corner of the monastery.[1]

History

History[]

An Underworldly Takeover[]

While drinking tea in his room, Wu explained to Cole how Garmadon might have gained control of the Skulkin.

Questing for Quests[]

While Master Wu is meditating in his room, Lloyd comes in and asks for advice on getting back into shape. This angers Wu and he tells Lloyd to find the solution himself. As Lloyd leaves, he slams the door and drops all the staffs that hung on the wall behind Wu.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Wu's room has paper sliding doors and other paper walls on the sides. Originally, there was a closet opposite the door where Wu stored a Sacred Flute. Next to it was a dish with icense sticks and spirit smoke. In the center of the room was a small orange seat cushion and a small table with a teapot. There was a black lamp on the wooden beams holding the roof. In the new monastery, the room is almost the same, but no longer contains a seat cushion or closet, although there is a small cupboard where Wu stores Aspheera's staff. The incense sticks' dish is now smaller. There are also two windows and three bo staffs are hung on the wall opposite the door.

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

After Lloyd turned on the portal gate in the Monastery of Imperium, a few days later, he woke up and wondered why he was in bed. He soon went outside.

The Blood Moon[]

After Lloyd saw his last vision of Egalt, he woke up, did Spinjitzu on his bed and hit his window. He claims that that vision/visions were the worst he experienced. While Arin and Sora are watching the “Night Ghost”, Lloyd presumably has another vision and wakes up. He then leaves the room to ask Mr. Frohicky why he is up so late.

Appearance

Appearance[]

In Season 1 of Dragon’s Rising, Lloyd’s room on the left side of the room there is a rack for Lloyd’s sword. On the right, there is a scroll sitting on a desk with a stool and a painting and a bamboo plant. In the middle of the room there is a nice carpet. On the far middle there is a window and yet, another stool with a nice plant. Also, Lloyd’s bed is there. Lloyd’s pillow and blanket are green. There is a green lantern at the top of the room.

In Season 2, Lloyd’s bed is pink/purple with a stool that has an alarm clock on the side. Again, there is a window near Lloyd’s bed. Next to Lloyd’s bed there is a gold/blue hammock. Near the hammock, there are some books and plates. Scattered around the floor, there is trash everywhere. Lloyd’s room has a small training course. Near another window of the room, there is a plant. There is a bookshelf that has a plant near it and also a ladder. There are 2 red lanterns above the room and light bulbs. Near the window there is a small plant and a Mech Master 5000 poster. On the right of the room, there are various plants and a colorful windmill.

The library, also referred as Wu's study is one of the many rooms in the monastery, which houses a number of scrolls and books on the history of Ninjago, which Wu placed there.

History

History[]

Shintaro[]

When Lloyd learns from Jay and Nya that Wu is behaving strangely, he follows him to the library. Wu claims to read the scroll, although this is not true. Later, Misako comes to the library and talks to them. When the Postman delivers an invitation to Kingdom of Shintaro, Lloyd leaves the library, and Wu and Misako do so in a moment.

A Big Splash[]

When the ninja try to find out more about Nya's problem, they go to the library. After a long search in the scrolls, Wu finds what he was looking for and tells the ninja about his father and that he never had the Elemental Powers of Water and Wind. When the ninja ask him why, he unfolds a scroll depicting Wojira on the table and tells them about her and how a Merlopian warrior defeated her. Wu then takes a magnifying glass and attaches it to his eye. With that, he views the scroll. However, Wu wasn't sure if Nya's problems are with Wojira and her amulets, and had no idea who could help her. Therefore, he convinces Kai to call his mother Maya.

The Call of the Deep[]

After Ray and Maya arrived, Wu went to the library, where he searched books and scrolls with Misako for information about the Elemental Power of Water. When Nya, who is not happy with her mother's care, comes to the library, Wu and Misako search the books. Nya asks them if they have found anything, but Wu tells her that there is very little mention of water in the books. He argues that Maya might be the answer. Nya continues to complain, but he advises her to talk to her brother.

Appearance

Appearance[]

The library is a relatively large room in the middle of the monastery in the shape of an octagon. On a wooden floor, there is a large carpet in the middle, on which there are two wooden benches and two chairs with purple pillows and a small table where the ninja and Wu can read books. Opposite the paper sliding door, there is a paper window, under which there is a small table and above which is a calligraphic painting of the mountains. There are also four red pillars in this part of the room, as well as the door. On the wall are two other large paintings of mountains with inscriptions in Ninjargon, under which various scrolls are inserted. On the side walls of the room, there are many books on wooden shelves, mainly black, red and gold. There are also brown ladders to access the upper books and small wooden chairs without a backrest. On the ceiling there is a wooden roof with several lamps and in the middle there is an octagonal thin roof, through which the sun shines into the room.

After the Merge, the floor of the library was changed with bricks and multiple trees have grown there. One of the pillars is changed with a white and gold stone pillar engraved with symbols that came from a Djinn tomb.

Appearance[]

The kitchen was originally an empty room with a red fridge located in the corner, the room was connected to the dining room.

After the Monastery was restored, the kitchen became slightly smaller with a sink located under a window. Additions include multiple drawers, an oven, and a microwave. The fridge became a little bit smaller, and relocated to the corner near the door. The floor was changed to brown wooden planks with a small carpet placed near the sink. Half of the kitchen's walls have been changed to teal tiles on white walls. A table is now present in-between the cooking area and a small empty area. A wooden pillar on the table is covered with small cards and another pillar on top of the table is used to store cooking utensils.

Practice room

Section

Information

Location

Monastery of Spinjitzu

First appearance

The Book of Spinjitzu
The monastery had a practice room where the ninja were training until it was accidentally destroyed by Jay before Ray and Maya came to the monastery.

History

History[]

The Book of Spinjitzu[]

During Wu's training in the practice room, he discovered what damage effects Spinjitzu could do if it is not used properly. As he tried a movement called "Kick of Mantis", he turned his pelvis more than he had and kicked Garmadon so that it flew over the supporting beam and almost the entire roof fell on him. Garmadon then attacked Wu with such anger as Wu had never seen before. In anger, he did not use Spinjitzu properly and almost destroyed the whole monastery. However, his father managed to stop him.

Prior to Seabound[]

At some point Jay's powers started an electrical fire that destroyed the practice room in the monastery, which he blamed on Kai.

Master of the Sea[]

When Nya showed Benthomaar the courtyard of the monastery, she mentioned the incident as Jay destroyed the practice room.
