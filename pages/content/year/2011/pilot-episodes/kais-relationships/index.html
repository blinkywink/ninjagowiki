Overview

These are the relationships of the Fire Ninja, Kai.

Family

Family[]

Nya[]

“Don't you dare touch her! That's my sister.”
— Kai to Nokt, "Elements of Betrayal"

Nya is Kai's younger sister. She was the only family he had when their parents disappeared when they were young. Because of this, Kai is overprotective of his sister as he wants to keep her safe from danger. It was this side of him that caused Kai to decide to become a ninja so he could protect her better.

Kai was also the first ninja to learn about Nya's identity of Samurai X and expressed pride in her vigilante acts, as she happily accepted them. He also agreed to keep her secret from the other ninja who later found out.

In Season 4, he was curious over what power she might have after it is revealed by Garmadon that they'll inherit their ancestors' abilities. He also pretended to betray her, though let her know it was an act.

During Season 5, when Nya begins training to become the Water Ninja, Kai was excited that his sister was the new addition to the team. He was very impressed to see Nya unlock her true potential and defeat the Preeminent leading to the ninja achieving victory over the ghostly threat.

In "Day of the Departed," he and Nya pay tribute to their parents and wonder if they will ever learn what happened to them before the ghost of Chen attacked them. The two siblings fought against their enemy and showed great teamwork as they manage to defeat Chen once more.

During The Hands of Time, Kai informs Nya that their father was still alive and he was helping their enemies, the Time Twins, though Nya refused to believe such a thing until Kai proved it to her. When they come face to face with their father, Kai attacks him and Nya tries to stop him as he is still their father. After their mother appeared, she explained to Kai on what caused them to serve the Time Twins. Kai and Nya were soon forced to help the Time Twins when they threaten their parents and a weaken Wu. After managing to obtain the last time blade, Kai and Nya pursued the villains as they time-traveled to the past and after a long battle across Time, they manage to emerge victorious at the cost of Wu. They return to their friends and save their father, who was rapidly aging due to a blew by a Time Blade. They happily reunite with their parents after many years of being apart.

In the Season 8 finale, Kai is separated from his sister because of being transported by Traveler's Tea.

In Season 9, they are mostly separated since Kai was in the Realm of Oni and Dragons and he worried for her since Garmadon is taking over Ninjago.

In "Green Destiny," Kai returns to Ninjago and worried for his sister's safety. Nya quickly contacts him to tell him she hears him and the siblings were reunited and they fight off the sons of Garmadon together. Afterward, the siblings celebrate their victory once Garmadon is defeated.

During "The Fall," Kai comforted Nya as she broke down at unintentionally causing Cole to fall to the darkness and encouraged her by speaking highly of her creativity and calling her the brains of the group, something that caught her by surprise. Kai's words also caused Nya to get an idea to defeat the threat, as Kai was amazed at her thinking.

In "The Turn of the Tide," Nya sacrifices herself to become apart of the ocean to defeat Wojira. After she defeats the sea serpent, Kai became confident they could turn her back to normal. However, Nya decided to head into the Endless Sea, and her older brother watched with shock as she went into the ocean and disappeared. Kai was saddened by his sister's fate, even as he attended her memorial.

In "Farewell the Sea", one year later, Kai, stricken and broken with his sister’s departure, opened up his very own dojo, where he trained his students (including Jake), while also dealing with his hatred towards Kalmaar for what happened to Nya. When the Ninja formed back for Nya’s sake, Kai looks pleased all while continuing to carry on.

In "A Painful Promise", when Aspheera absorbed Nya’s powers and Nya became human again, Kai was happy to see his sister after so long and cradled to her side with Jay.

After the Merge, Kai and Nya were separated for years, but managed to reunite and Kai joyfully embraced her. The two worked together to defend the Craglings from wild dragons. Although, Kai was thrown away by a rock and presumed dead by his saddened sister. Luckily, he returned to the cragling village and he, Nya, Lloyd, his students and Riyu all managed to save the cragling village. The siblings reunited with Lloyd and rejoined the ninja team. They alter went their spearate ways again for a short time to learn how to stop the Mergequakes and defeat Empress Beatrix but were reunited and worked together to save the Merged Lands.

In Season 2 (Dragons Rising), it was their deep bond with each other that protected each other throughout the adventure. Before and During the Ritual of the Blood Moon, Nya helped Kai unlock his Rising Dragon by going over Counter Shot together and later shared an embrace of pride with the other Ninja for unlocking his Rising Dragon. He later mastered when he saw Cinder about to kill Nya, he defeated Cinder but was later knocked into the Nether-Space by Ras. This caused Nya to unlock her rising dragon and help to thwart Ras' plan but was still hurt that her brother was trapped. Kai was eventually able establish a connection with Riyu from the Nether-Space via the use of Dragon Ivory, with he passed on to Nya, allowing them to effectively communicate with each other. Their family link was what pulled Kai out of the Nether-Space, Nya happy to have her brother back, and later explained what happened with Jay, promising they were going to track him down to make him come back to his senses.

In Season 3 (Dragons Rising), Nya and Kai (along with Wyldfyre) worked together to find Jay, however, as Nya grows more impatient over the search, the more she gets frustrated with the little annoying things Kai does, like making popping noises, jumping into fights even his antics with Wyldfyre puts some strain on Nya. Kai apologizes to his sister, telling her that the things he does are just his ways of handling stress and incompotence with finding Jay, something that Nya understands. Eventually, Kai has the idea of luring Jay by breaking his record on the old arcade game he used to play when he was growing up in his parents' junkyard. Even though Nya was doubtful she still agreed and it partially worked out, and they shared a small embrace afterwards. They later had to call off their search for Jay in order to stop the Forbidden Five from freeing Thunderfang. After their battle with Drix, Kai and Nya reflect on a memory of Jay and Nya on a picnic with Kai annoyingly tagging along with them and was later dared by Jay with Nya telling him not to but refused only to have a swarm of clamp beetles chasing him, which Kai still believed was cool. Kai and Nya went their separate ways to handle their own problems but later reunited to stop the Spectral Invasion, awaken the Arc Dragon of Focus, and defeat Thuderfang. With the missions successful, the siblings along with Jay attempted to go home but they felt a disturbance in their elemental powers, and were later shut off from their Monastery(unaware it was caused by Ras), leaving them three of them trapped in an unknown realm.

Ray[]

Ray is Kai and Nya's father. He was a blacksmith and the one who Kai inherited his powers. He disappeared sometime after Nya turned three and Kai often wondered what happened to him and their mother. Kai took over his father's blacksmith shop and swore to surpass his father's skills, even though his talents were far behind his, due to his impatient nature. In Season 4, he was interested in hearing the hidden story about his parents' disappearance from Chen, who promised the information if he betrayed his friends.

In "Day of the Departed," he and Nya pay tribute to their parents and wonder if they would ever find out what happened to them.

In The Hands of Time, Kai was shocked to hear from Acronix that he knew Kai's father and caused him to question everything. Later on, he is horrified to hear from Krux about his parents being traitors who aided him and his brother. Kai then begins to dive deep into his parents' past and discover that his father did in fact aid the Time Twins. Kai was angered by this revelation and when reuniting with his father, he labeled him a traitor and attacked him until Nya and his mother Maya stopped him.

Kai learned that his parents were forced to help the Time Twins after Krux threatened to harm him and Nya when they were children, which caused Kai to feel guilty over trying to hurt his parents and apologized for accusing them when they were protecting him and Nya. Kai later saved his father from succumbing to the effects of the time blades and was overjoyed to have him back after so long.

The two would meet again in Seabound when Kai requested his parents to stay at the monastery when Nya lost control of her powers. Unlike his sister, Kai enjoyed having his parents' company and he would spend time playing video games and training with his father, strengthening their bond. When Ray and Maya decided it was time to leave, Kai was sad his parents had to leave and he and his father played video games one last time before his departure.

Maya[]

Maya is Kai and Nya's mother. She disappeared sometime after Nya turned three and Kai often wondered about what happened to her and their father. In Season 4, he was interested in hearing the hidden story about his parents' disappearance from Chen who promised the information if he betrayed his friends.

In "Day of the Departed," he and Nya pay tribute to their parents and wonder if they would ever find out what happened to them.

In "The Hands of Time," Kai was shocked to hear from Krux about his parents being traitors who aided him and his brother. Kai then begins to dive deep into his parents' past and discover that his parents did in fact aid the Time Twins. Kai was angered by this revelation and when reuniting with his father, he labeled him a traitor and attacked him until Nya and Maya arrived and stopped him.

While Kai hugged her, he still believed her to be a traitor until he learned the truth of his parents being forced to help the Time Twins after Krux threatened to harm him and his sister, which caused Kai to feel guilty over trying to hurt his parents and sincerely apologizes for accusing them when they were only protecting their family. After Kai managed to save his father from the effects of the time blades, he was overjoyed to have both his parents back after so long.

Sometime later, Kai would request his mother and father to stay at the monastery for a while when Nya's powers went out of control. Kai enjoyed his mother's company and did not mind her pampering him, particularly because they missed a lot of time together thanks to Krux.

Love Interest

Love Interest[]

Skylor (ambiguous)[]

“Just tell me one thing. Was it his idea to make me fall for you, or yours? Because it worked.”
— Kai to Skylor, "The Forgotten Element"

Skylor is Kai's girlfriend. He first encountered Skylor on the ferry to Chen's Island and while there he defended her from Karlof. From the moment they met, Kai immediately fell in love with her right after he got his first look at her and developed a romantic interest in Skylor, but was shocked to learn she uses his element, thinking they may be related but soon learns her power is Amber, which brings back his feelings for her. During the Tournament, Kai grows to trust Skylor, especially when she shows information regarding Cole and Zane. This changes when he is shocked to learn that she is Chen's daughter and was secretly working with her father to undermine his friends, but he also believes there is a conscience in her. His feelings for her seem reciprocated, as Skylor couldn't look him in the eye when Chen captured him. She also seemed touched when he mentioned that he fell for her. Skylor soon became convinced by Kai and turned against her father and was captured as a result, but Kai went off to rescue her from Chen.

Kai's feelings were so great that he accepted her new Anacondrai form, which allowed him to unlock his Fire Dragon. When she was sad about people running from her new appearance, he cheered her up by mentioning she has changed by joining him and his friends. The two fought together to face Chen and his army, emerging victorious from the conflict. After the battle, Kai offers her a spot on the team but she declines and decides to take over her father's noodle business, promising to fully redeem herself. She tries to give Kai a farewell kiss, but Dareth interrupts them. However, she promises to keep in touch.

Near the end of Season 6, Kai meets Skylor again (she was recruited by Jay to rescue the other ninja from Nadakhan's magical sword) after he is freed from the sword. Kai thanks Skylor, saying that he is unsure how he can repay her. Skylor just replies that Kai "owes her one." However, Jay's last wish undid everything and Skylor forget this, as did Kai, unless he was told by Jay and Nya.

In Season 7, Kai goes to her for advice and she relates to his situation and tells him their parents left behind legacies, but they don’t define who they are; they are who they choose to be.

In "Green Destiny," they are reunited only for Kai to become worried about her upon seeing her poisoned condition. After Garmadon was defeated, he is there when she is cured and wakes up in Kai's arms, much to his joy and relief. In the end, they share a romantic embrace.

In "Kryptarium Prison Blues," Kai holds Skylor's left hand, which shows their love for each other: as well as the compassions that Skylor has for Kai's well-being. Kai also winks at her, causing her to smile.

In "Safe Haven" Skylor saved Kai and Cole from Pythor's capture.

In "Compatible" Kai proposed to Skylor that they be in a relationship, to which she replied that she hadn't seen him in ages and that he was suggesting it like that. Kai apologized, explaining all the things that had happened to him.

In "Roots," after defeating the Overlord for one last time, Kai and Skylor had a talk about their relationship. The two wanted to kiss but were interrupted by Dareth again.

By the time of Quest for the Lost Powers, Kai and Skylor have worked out all the problems in their relationship and finally came around to become a couple. However, their relationship has remained ambiguous since The Merge.[1]

Wyldfyre[]

“Okay, no one has ever called me patient. I'm the hothead, in more ways than one. But, when I lost my sister to the sea. . . Long story. Master Wu had me practice my forms. I was never as good as the others, but, Ninja never quit. So I kept going until it worked enough to calm me down. And if it could work for me, it could work for anyone. Even you. Because, you kinda remind me of someone.”
— Kai to Wyldfyre, "The Administration"

Wyldfyre is Kai's student. They first met during the battle in Imperium and was surprised to see her use fire, although he doesn't know it is Heat. He was confused at how she would offer him some pointers on his Fire. After releasing the enslaved dragons and escape from Imperium, Lloyd paired Kai with Wyldfyre for the former to train the latter, due to the similarity of their respective powers, which was met with a very rocky start. Initially, Kai was often annoyed by her antics for pranks (which was a major obstacle during their search for one of the Dragon Orbs) and bicker about who had the best flame attacks. Despite this, they share their mutual agreement on hiding or certain achievements with a flame induced high five, and eventually, Kai took her under his wing when he taught her how to be patient and calm her temper through stances while waiting for Heatwave to heal. This allowed Wyldfyre to develop a great deal of respect for Kai, as he not only treated Heatwave, but also taught her how to be a better person. When Kai was sent to the Nether-Space, Wyldfyre was one of the few (alongside Nya), who took his departure the hardest. As it turns out, while training, Wyldfyre became more reckless and more frustrated with Kai’s departure. She was jubilant in reuniting with her mentor, when he returned.

During their search for Jay with Nya, the two were showed goofing off together whenever they would make a break from it, like blasting rocks, something that would irritate Nya. It's also showed that Kai greatly supports Wyldfyre's relationship with Roby, as he would smile at her excitement over getting a message from him and tease her about it. It is also showed how protective Kai is of Wyldfyre, as he was ready to "kick (Roby's) butt" after initially interpreting Wyldfyre's reacting to a letter from him as him breaking up with her.

Frak[]

Kai, along with Cole, helped train Frak to perform Spinjitzu. When Frak heard the ground scream, Kai went with Frak and Cole to investigate. When Cole mention how Master Wu had lead him there, Kai thinks Wu guided Cole to dig something evil, which Frak said Master Wu would never do something like that but Kai reminded him Wu caused the Merge for some reason and didn't tell anyone.

After arriving back to the Land of Lost Things, Kai and Frak went after Nokt to reclaim the Finders' souls but both ended up loosing Cole's soul when the two went to upgrade their Prismatic Blades into Reveal Blades. The two took Cole back to the Monastery of Spinjitzu and split up when Sora and Wyldfyre came.

Mentors

Mentors[]

Master Wu[]

“Master Wu, don't. I'm sorry I was angry. We all make mistakes. But giving yourself over to Aspheera would be another one. She can't be trusted. She'll still be dangerous and on the loose and—besides all that, we're family. We stick together. No matter what.”
— Kai to Wu, "Under Siege"

Kai is Master Wu's student, along with the other ninja. At the beginning of the series, Kai was recruited by Master Wu to become a ninja. At first, Kai didn't care about training with Wu even considering him a crazy old man. This changed after Nya is kidnapped by Samukai and the Skulkin, as Wu convinced him that becoming a ninja would allow him to become strong enough to rescue his sister. As the series went on, they grew closer. Kai and the other ninja cared about their master and is glad to have him mentoring them.

In Season 7, he and the other ninja come to his aid when he fights Acronix by himself and when he and Nya save him from succumbing to the effects of the time blades. When Wu is back to normal, he helps them foil the Time Twins’ plan. He later aids Kai and Nya in the battle against the Time Twins, but when the villains prepare to finish the ninja off, Wu sacrifices himself to save them. Though happy at their victory, Kai couldn't help but be worried when his master was feared to be lost in time with their enemies. He and the ninja elect Lloyd as their master and are happy to hear his first order is to discover what happened to Wu.

In Season 8, Kai and the others are still searching for Wu before dealing with the Sons of Garmadon. They find an infant and after said child starts to walk and talk, they realize he is their master de-aged because of the time blades. Kai was relieved to hear he would turn back after the blades' effects wear off.

During Season 9, while they are stranded in the Realm of Oni and Dragons Kai is the ninja who is amazed at Wu aging and wonders if his memories will retell lessons to better help him as he rapidly ages. Kai even looked forward to seeing Wu become himself again and helped him with learning how the team works in missions. During their journey towards Firsbourne's nest, Faith started teaching the Ninja how to use chains to capture a dragon as a way to defend themselves in that realm. When teenage Wu loses his will to train after failing many times, Kai is the one to convince him to keep on trying after teaching him how to be confident. After master rapidly aged into an adult and regain his memories, Kai was excited to see he was back.

In "Green Destiny," Kai and Wu fought against Garmadon's forces and were victorious in the conflict.

During Season 11, Kai became angry with his mentor for being the cause of Aspheera stealing his elemental powers, leaving him powerless, while also criticizing him for not being able to teach them the sense of responsibility if he fled from his own, especially when he was still trying to run away from his mistakes and justify them. However, as Wu was willing to give himself up to Aspheera, Kai was able to notice the regret his master was feeling and intervened and reconciled with him.

In "The Screaming Earth", it is revealed that Kai had developed doubts of Wu after learning he caused The Merge, which lead him to believe Wu probably lead him, Cole, and Frak to something evil but it was actually to unearth the remains of the Source Dragon of Balance.

Friends

Friends[]

Lloyd[]

“With my dad gone, sometimes I question where I'm going. Sometimes I worry about who I might become.”
“I know how that feels. After I lost my dad, I lost my way. But I was lucky to have my sister watch over me. Don't worry, big shot. I'll watch over you from now on.”
— Lloyd and Kai, "Winds of Change"

Initially, Kai and Lloyd had a bitter relationship because he and the ninja prevented him from ruling Ninjago, while also finding him to be a brat. When he redeemed, Kai, very much like the other Ninja, only saw Lloyd as just Master Wu's nephew and a nuisance, especially when he had to babysit him. However, when the time came, Kai was the one to realize his destiny was to protect him, after facing the choice of either save Lloyd or retrieve the Fangblade to stop Pythor from awakening the Great Devourer, leading to the revelation that Lloyd was the Green Ninja. The two grew closer and became best friends, especially because Kai could see how hard the possibility of Lloyd having to fight his own father was for him, so he became dedicated in teaching him so that he would be prepared for that fight, becoming the most dedicated out of the Ninja to teach Lloyd (even being the first to have a turn with him). Though Kai was jealous that Lloyd was the Green Ninja instead of him, that didn't hinder their friendship. Kai considers Lloyd as his younger brother. Out of all the ninja, Lloyd is closer to him.

In Season 4, Kai's friendship towards Lloyd was tested througout the tournament. This was first seen when Skylor, during Lloyd's match against Camille, tried to convince Kai to join the others against Lloyd, as he was one of the strongest contestants. Despite his crush towards her and knowing it would ruin his chances to win her affections, Kai remained loyal to Lloyd. Even with Master Chen's attempt to manipulate Kai, the latter managed to remain loyal and tricked the former into believe he had betrayed Lloyd after he led him to a trap, so he could get close enough to take the Staff of Elements from him. However, that action actually caused him to briefly turned on Lloyd when the staff corrupted him and brought his repressed jealousy and resentment towards Lloyd to the surface. But the deep bond the two shared allowed Kai to resist those intrusive thoughts and overcome their influence. When Lloyd's father was banished to the Cursed Realm to defeat Chen, Kai made a promise to look after Lloyd when he was sad about his father's sacrifice and demise.

In Season 5, after Lloyd was possessed by Morro, Kai was the most determined out of all the ninja to get their friend back. This was seen as he gave his share and the other Ninja's of the teashop to Ronin, not only to save his own life, but also to obtain a new clue to learn Airjitzu, the first piece of the puzzle to reach the Fist Spinjitzu Master's grave. It was also thanks to Kai's determination to save Lloyd that he managed to recover the Sword of Santuary from Morro, while motivating him to keep on fighting the possession, an action that allows Lloyd to actually take control for a few moments. When Morro left Lloyd's body and threatened him, Kai was the one to decide to give up the Realm Crystal and leaped into the water (despite his fear and inability to swim) to save Lloyd from drowning. He was relieved to have his friend back and comforted Lloyd when he was upset about dropping his guard by telling him that they would defeat any threat. Kai's words proved to be true as the ninja were able to save the city of Stiix, defeat the Preeminent, and her army of ghosts. Kai grows a very huge and close relationship with Lloyd in Season 5.

In Season 7, he and the other ninja decide to make Lloyd their master after Wu is lost in time with the Time Twins.

During Season 8: Sons of Garmadon, Kai made fun of Lloyd's crush on Harumi and offered bad advice. Other than that, they get along as Kai respected Lloyd's leadership and followed his orders as they worked together. Kai was also worried for Lloyd after Harumi's true colors were revealed and, despite worried they would be defenseless to fight Garmadon and his golem, he didn't hesitate to try and give up his powers to heal Lloyd after he fought his father.

For most of Season 9: Hunted, Kai was mostly in the Realm of Oni and Dragons and worried for Lloyd and the others. In "Green Destiny", Kai was reunited with his friend and they saved Ninjago from Garmadon, and Kai was happy Lloyd's power returned.

In Season 11, after Kai lost his elemental power to Aspheera and found himself needing the others to protect him against the Pyro Vipers, he wanted to remove himself from the battle to not hold the team down. Lloyd tried to motivate Kai to not give up, but to not avail. When Kai rejoined them later on, Lloyd got very happy for his best friend to decide to keep on fighting, even without powers. He was also overjoyed when Kai recovered his fire in "Fire Maker."

In Season 12, in "Superstar Rockin' Jay," Kai was shot off the roof from one of the drones in the game and was killed. Lloyd witnessed his death and grieved until Kai re-spawned next to him with three lives left. Lloyd was happy to see him okay while at the same time confused.

When the Merge occurred and Kai returned to the monastery weeks later, Lloyd dove head first to attack, only to stand down when he saw it was Kai, relieving him. The two were happy to see each other and hugged. After shutting a MergeQuake rift, Lloyd sent Kai to map out the merged realms and find the others, until they reunite at the Land of Madness, and the latter assumed he was babysitting Arin and Sora, unaware that they were his students.

In "Elements of Betrayal," Lloyd was surprised when Kai returned, but happy as well.

Cole[]

“Yeah. Of all the bad ideas I've ever heard, and I've heard a few. Lookin' at you, Kai, this takes the cake!”
— Cole to Kai, "The Belly of the Beast"

Kai and Cole are loyal buddies on great terms. They rarely bicker and get along very well because they share several common things: their confidence, courage, etc. In the earlier seasons, he and Cole were the leaders of the team and sometimes they alternate over the leadership, with Cole being more grounded, while Kai was more intuitive. They often like to hang out with each other and usually like to team with one another as shown in the later seasons. As shown in Prime Empire specifically, they always have each other's backs and would risk their life to save the other, the two also die together in Prime Empire which shows how close the bond between Kai and Cole is. They are overall very close friends.

In Season 5, Kai's and Cole's friendship grew stronger in this season. Kai is very sad that Cole became a ghost and attempted to move him out of his sadness. Throughout the rest of the season, he motivated Cole to never give up and to stay by his side. Overall, Kai respects Cole and they are close friends. Both would put the other ninja before themselves.

In "Day of the Departed," Kai was very happy to see Cole return to being a human.

In Season 7 Cole and Kai didn't interact much, though working as a team. Although, Cole was very worried about Kai after the latter and Nya followed the Time Twins through time, along with Wu. Cole's worries are relieved when Kai and Nya returned unharmed.

In Season 8: Sons of Garmadon, they continue to get along with Kai laughing at Cole singing to their enemies.

During Season 9: Hunted, Kai depended on Cole to rescue him and the others from the Dragon Hunters. Despite this, when threatened by enemies, Kai told them to eat Cole first but this was out of fear not malice.

In Season 12: Prime Empire, when Cole is under attack by Red Visors and a drone, and his rope is about to snap, Kai risks his last life for him by jumping off of the side of the cliff and land on the drone, shooting away from the Red Visors. In "The Speedway Five-Billion," Kai lets Cole ride on the back of his jet in the race until he steals a Whack Rat's Quad Bike. When they see Jay get surrounded by Red Visors, Cole and Kai both agree to go out with a bang and sacrifice themselves for their teammates.

In Season 13, once the team arrives in the city of Shintaro and try to sleep that night, Kai is annoyed when Cole wakes them up after wanting everyone to help find a locket of his parents that was held by a Geckle in his room and went back to bed. The next day, he looks guilty when being informed by Princess Vania that the Geckle was real and that this lead to Cole being captured inside the mountain. After a failed rescue attempt and a giant ruckus caused by the Skull Sorcerer which led to the team splitting up, Kai looks worried when Cole is being chased by the latter and fears for him when he gets blocked off from him and Zane.

In Elements of Betrayal, Cole hugs Kai, happy that he returned.

While Lloyd was away trying to find Arin's parents, Kai and Cole teamed up to train Frak in Spinjitzu. As they headed towards the location Master Wu had sent Cole previously, Kai cheered for both him and Frak to unearth what turned out to be the deceased Source Dragon of Balance, ending up also helping them as well.

However, when Nokt (as a ghost) attacks Imperium with the spectral Dragonian Warriors, including the Land of Lost Things, and takes Bonzle, Fritz and Spitz's souls, Cole becomes determined in recovering them to the point of becoming reckless and disregard the best option to counter the enemy. Kai, noticing his friend acting like him, tries to reason with him, only for Cole to reply he was not his teacher and to hurry and upgrade the Prismatic Blades, while he handles Nokt. Despite becoming worried for him, Kai follows the plan, something that pays off when he and Frak (with the upgraded Prismatic Blades) fends the ghosts away and recover some lost souls. However, they failed to do it before Cole gets his soul taken, something that leaves Kai profoundly sad, but determined to bring his friend back.

Zane[]

“Everyone wondered what powered Zane. I don't know if we'll ever know, but I'd like to think it was brotherhood. Because he powered me. And he'll still power me as his memory lives on. Ninja never quit, and ninja will never be forgotten. Wherever you are, Zane, you'll always be one of us.”
— Kai, "The Titanium Ninja"

Like all the other ninja, Kai initially thought Zane was weird, but when he found out that Zane was a Nindroid, he and the others supported and accepted Zane as their brother. He and Zane grow closer throughout the series. When Zane sacrificed himself to defeat the Overlord and was thought to be destroyed, Kai was greatly upset over his friend's demise that he couldn't be a ninja anymore.

In Season 4, Kai was the most saddened at Zane’s death, being upset that Zane died instead of him, out of guilt. When Lloyd suggested the idea of replacing Zane, Kai, and the others protested at this with Kai even stating that without Zane maybe there was no team. When Kai and the others learned that Zane was still alive, they were very surprised at this and entered the Tournament of Elements to locate him. Jay[]

“I'll take that as a yes. All right, we get it. That's enough. Come on, dude, that's my sister!”
— Kai to Jay as he watches him and Nya kiss., "A Wonder of the Merged Lands"

Jay and Kai are friends on throughout the series, though they interact only occasionally and they both often fight or pick on one another. Nonetheless, they do seem to care about each other.

At first, their status was generally tense and turbulent since they were fairly distant through Season 1, rarely interacting. Their status became even more strained after Kai's sister became enamored with Jay, much to Kai's disgruntled chagrin. However, he soon respected and approved of their relationship.

As of Season 2, Kai and Jay have interacted more and become more agreeable, in spite of their contrasting personalities. There are moments where Kai saves Jay from peril and comforts Jay when he is feeling down.

As the series went on, Kai and Jay have shown themselves to good friends and work well as teammates. During Season 4, Jay was aware of Kai's crush on Skylor and was amused by it and annoyed as the latter nearly let him fall.

In Season 9: Hunted, Kai was put off by Jay not freaking out when they were stranded in the Realm of Oni and Dragons and accepting of it. Kai remarked that Jay not freaking out was making him worried, though he was relieved to see him back to normal after they were captured.

Throughout Season 10, the two interact a lot more than they normally do when Jay turns to Kai when needing help on asking Nya to be his Yang. In "The Darkness Comes," when Jay is practicing his speech, Kai tries to convince him that he needs to ask in an arrogant and cocky manner. Jay declines this suggestion, which Kai agrees. In "Endings" Kai criticizes Jay's sense of timing when he finally asks the question right before they all fight the Oni one last time, but is happy when his sister says yes. He cockily comments that he taught Jay how to ask the question.

However, by the beginning of Season 11 onwards, Kai seems to have gained some sort of low opinion regarding Jay and his capabilities, an example being when he offers to let Jay be eaten by the Beohernie, saying that it should be "someone expendable" like him, he also felt worthless to the team after Jay saved him from two Elemental Cobras, something he seems ashamed of happening.

In Season 12, Kai believes his having a nightmare when encountering the League of Jay, which consists of many people with avatars of Jay himself. Later on the season, Kai willingly gave up his last life along with Cole so that his sister, Lloyd and Jay can move on to stop Unagami.

In Season 13, when Kai needs to jump from a lava cliff, he thinks he can do it if even Jay could, only to almost fall and need help.

In The Island, when Jay is about to be sacrificed to "Wojira", Kai asks who would want Jay as a sacrifice, which annoyed Nya. Still, he was one of the Ninja along with Lloyd and Nya to berate Mammatus on sacrificing Jay, implying Kai does have some respect for his team mate and friend still.

In Seabound, Kai and Jay barely interact due to Jay joining the sub-aquatic expedition with Nya, Lloyd, Zane, P.I.X.A.L and Maya. Only during The Great Flood they worked together to stop King Kalmaar and the sea-serpent Wojira from destroying Ninjago, where during the battle Jay almost drowned and was left with too much water on his lungs, which visibly seemed to concern Kai as he asked how his team mate was. They later attended Nya's memorial following her merging with the Endless Sea and both were visibly distraught for losing her.

In Crystalized, after Nya's sacrifice to save Ninjago from Kalmaar and Wojira, both Kai and Jay were the most affected by it, which contributed to splitting them apart along with Lloyd, leaving only Cole and Zane as full time Ninja.

In Farewell the Sea, when visiting a grieving Jay in the Abandoned Lighthouse, Kai along with Cole and Zane tried to reason with him to bring him back to the team, but to no avail, with Kai giving a angry stare at Jay likely because he feels like Jay only knowledges his own grief.

In The Shape of Nya, Kai’s relationship with Jay became strained and is visibly showing signs of crack, because Kai grows angry and frustrated at Jay's lack of attention to their mission, since his grief for Nya makes him talk to glasses of water and stay un-focused, this anger leads him to accuse Jay of thinking he's the only one who misses Nya while the others don't, complaining that at least the rest of the Ninja were trying to do something while he kept "moping around", with Jay biting back saying that he didn't ask to be there and that they came for him in the first place, and they almost fight until Lloyd breaks the tension.

Later in the episode, after returning from the N line following a battle with Miss Demeanor, Kai called Jay crazy for still talking with glasses of water, and when he accidentally bumps the cups and the water gets spilled, Jay gets paranoid and calls him clumsy, Kai tries to apologize but gets brutally interrupted when Jay furiously kicks him across the room, which enrages Kai enough to summon his Fire and blast Jay with it continuously, destroying Cole's Rock Cycle in the process along with activating the Hangar Bay's sprinkler.

The sprinkler water helped a merged Nya appear in her water form all of the sudden and surprise the Ninja, making Kai and Jay immediately stop their fight in order to listen to what she had to say. After Nya explains that she needs to drain her powers away to come back, the Ninja begin to decide if they should free Aspheera from Kryptarium Prison, with Kai wondering if they were still even a team, Lloyd asked who was in favor of their plan and they all raised their hands, showing that Kai still sees Jay as a teammate despite the fight and their other past clashes over being affected by Nya's departure.

In "The Council of the Crystal King", after Zane tracked Lloyd stopping near the same location the group had investigated earlier in the abandoned N line. Kai realized Jay was right about there being a secret entrance and found it crazy that Jay being right, much to Jay's offense.

In the episode "Darkness Within" when the ninja found a way out of the rock slide Jay was almost crushed and left behind until Kai pulled him out in time.

Years after the Merge, Kai worried about Jay and went out to search for him and the others.

Talking to Nya in "The Sword Shatters" where he was trapped in the Nether-Space, Kai intently joked about Jay's memory loss being a good thing. After he was freed, Kai agreed to join Nya on her quest to bring Jay back and make him come to his senses.

During their search for Jay, Kai's inside knowledge of him allows him to get a clue to find him. Using Jay's obsession for videogames and his pride regarding his top score on a coin-op game he used to play while growing up, Kai is able to lure Jay. It is implied if Jay did appear, Kai would of stopped Jay from reclaiming his high score. Even if he doesn't appear physically, Kai's plan is able to give hope to Nya after they watch Jay play the game remotely... even if the former gets annoyed that Jay ends up beating the top score Kai took a long time to beat and accuse Jay of cheating.

In "Chaos Unchained", Kai and Nya argue about a time where Kai followed and joined up with Nya and Jay despite not being invited on their picnic as a way to protect Nya. But it only led to Jay goofing around with Kai by making him do a stupid dare for fun. Even though it didn't go well for Kai he still thought that accepting the dare made his look cool.

In "When Doves Cry", Kai heard from Sora and Wyldfyre that Jay bailed and called him a jerk.

In "Chaos Reigns", after Kai saw Jay defeat Zarkt, Kai (forgetting Jay still hasn't recovered his memory) tells Jay he was awesome and takes back everything he said of Jay being the worst Ninja, which confused Jay and Kai blamed it on Cole.

In "A Wonder of the Merged Lands," Kai and Nya help Jay regain his memory before the former watched him and Nya's reconciling kiss was taking too long and tried to get them to stop.

P.I.X.A.L.[]

“You got us to the palace. Thanks, Pix... See you on the other side.”
— Kai to P.I.X.A.L., "Dread on Arrival"

Kai and P.I.X.A.L. are good friends on certain terms, they haven't always got along well but throughout the series they work together to defend Ninjago from the forces of evil, even if they don’t always see eye to eye.

In "The Surge" Kai first meets P.I.X.A.L. at Borg Industries while chaperoning a field trip for Wu’s Academy and later expresses amusement at her budding romance with Zane. However, P.I.X.A.L. and Kai do not appear to interact directly with one another until they become forced to work together, revealing their clashing differences.

Kai first becomes annoyed with P.I.X.A.L. in “Codename: Arcturus” when she interrupts a flirtatious encounter between him and a young woman. She then advises him to follow a Nindroid convoy to learn the nature of their mysterious cargo. Tensions arise when P.I.X.A.L. warns Kai not to be careless and he mutes her in response, condescendingly telling her to “Relax,”. This prompts P.I.X.A.L. to seize control of his vehicle, the Prototype X-1, from him remotely. Soon after, Kai disobeys her instructions to keep a low profile (calling it “boring”) and rams into another car. P.I.X.A.L. then scolds him, remarking that the X1 “is worth far more than (him),”. As the mission continues, P.I.X.A.L. grows increasingly more frustrated with Kai's antics as he becomes irritated by her advice. When Kai accidentally reveals his presence to Pythor and his Nindroids, P.I.X.A.L. helps him board the convoy and attempts to rescue him when a tank of jet fuel gets punctured. However, she is unable to and believes that he perished when the tanker exploded. P.I.X.A.L. is concerned for Kai's safety but is relieved with Zane, Wu, and Garmadon when they learn of his survival.

Following this mission, P.I.X.A.L. continues to help Kai and the Ninja achieve their goals in different ways as seen in "The Void" and "The Titanium Ninja"

When P.I.X.A.L. reveals her identity as Samurai X several seasons later in “Dead Man’s Squall”, Kai expresses his disbelief along with the other Ninja. He soon accepts her as a member of the team and even asks Nya if P.I.X.A.L. will be alright when the samurai confronts several Sons of Garmadon members alone, showing a newfound concern for his friend.

Later, P.I.X.A.L. acts as Kai’s navigator in “Dread on Arrival”, similar to her role in “Codename: Arcturus”. She guides Kai in the Katana V11 through submerged passageways to the Royal Palace. However, this interaction differs from the previous one in that they both refrain from anger and focus on the task at hand, revealing a growing respect for one another.

At the end of their journey, P.I.X.A.L. tells Kai to take precaution before he confronts Harumi and the Sons of Garmadon and Kai thanks her for guiding him and watching baby Master Wu.

Later, P.I.X.A.L. joins Kai in fighting Garmadon’s Colossus, liberating the city from Emperor Garmadon’s rule, and defending the Monastery against the Oni.

Following the Oni’s defeat, Kai is pleasantly surprised when P.I.X.A.L. reveals the new hangar bay and vehicles she built beneath the Monastery.

In “Vengeance is Mine!”, Kai displays his frustration with P.I.X.A.L.’s lack of progress on the Titan Mech as Aspheera and her army grow closer to breaking into the Monastery’s hangar bay. However, he becomes impressed when she defends him, Jay, Cole, and Master Wu from Aspheera and they subsequently help her battle the Pyro Vipers.

In "An Issue of Trust", they are seen hugging after being separated by the Crystal King.

Enemies

Enemies[]

Chen[]

Kai holds a dislike for Chen both for kidnapping Zane and stealing his powers. He worked with his friends to undermine Chen's plan and was shocked to learn of Skylor being his daughter, as well as his spy.

Chen did not seem to mind Kai, especially after learning his daughter liked him and agreed to her request of letting Kai joined them. Chen showed the Red Ninja his collection, while Kai was irritated with him but Chen on the other hand found Kai to be amusing, since he still possessed spirit even after his powers were taken. Chen caught Kai's interest with secrets about his missing parents, something that seemingly caused Kai to help in capturing Lloyd. In truth, Kai was deceiving Chen and made this known to a captive Nya and Garmadon. Kai later revealed his ruse after turning Skylor against Chen and return the elemental powers to everyone.

In "Day of the Departed," they meet again after Chen was summoned by Yang and eagerly went after Kai and Nya as his targets, as they both were at fault for his defeat. Kai was shocked to see him again and fought against the ghost. In spite of Chen's relentless attacks, Kai and his sister defeated him and Chen was sent back to the Departed Realm.

Morro (formerly)[]

“Foolish ninja! Why would you try to take me on all by yourself?”
“Because I promised to look after Lloyd!”
— Morro and Kai, “Winds of Change”

In Season 5, after Lloyd was possessed by Morro, Kai was the most determined out of all the ninja to get his best friend back.

Out of all the ninja, Morro seemed to be most annoyed with Kai, due to him being brave enough to fight him alone and encouraging Lloyd to break free of his possession.

In "Winds of Change," Kai refused to leave without Lloyd and engaged Morro one on one until they were interrupted.

In "Ghost Story," Kai vowed that he would do anything to bring down Morro, even if it was the last thing he did.

In "Kingdom Come," Kai catches up with Morro's skiff and duels the Master of Wind. Morro overpowers him using the Sword of Sanctuary and prepares to deliver the final blow, but is stopped by Lloyd, who has temporarily regained control of his body. Kai takes the Sword and uses Airjitzu to return to the Destiny's Bounty right as the skiff goes over a cliff.

In "Grave Danger," Kai baiting the Master of Wind into trapping himself. Immobilized Morro is forced to leave Lloyd's body in order to get out and force the ninja to go to the exchange. Kai converses with Morro before throwing him the crystal, prompting the Master of Wind to catch it while dropping Lloyd—and then drop it accidentally into a nearby stream due to the bottom being heated by Kai's weakened firepower. Enraged, he blows Lloyd into the stream as well, which carries both him and the crystal toward a nearby waterfall. Despite their fears of the water, Kai and Cole manage to rescue Lloyd from drowning.

In "Day of the Departed," Kai was angry to see him. He quickly armed himself for a fight until Wu told them that Morro came to help, to Kai's surprise.

Time Twins[]

“My parents helped Wu and Garmadon defeat you once. And I will do it again.”
— Kai to Krux, "A Time of Traitors"

Kai holds extreme hatred for them, since they were largely responsible for his and Nya's parents disappearance. Kai hadn't known of this until he and the other ninja fought Acronix and the latter made a vague hint at it and he became suspicious of it. Kai later confronted Krux who was revealed to have been masquerading as Dr. Saunders and told him his parents were traitors.

Kai later learned they forced his parents into servitude under threat of harming him and Nya when they were young. Kai's dislike for them boiled especially since the Time Twins forced him and Nya to serve them by threatening his parents and a rapidly aging Wu. Kai followed the Twins to the past and disrupted their plans, saving Ninjago from their influence.

In the final battle, Kai joined his sister and master in fighting them. Kai and Nya overpowered Krux before Acronix stepped in to defend his brother, nearly defeating the ninja until Wu sacrificed himself and sent the ninja back to the present while he stays behind to defeat the enemy.

Ironically, Kai and Krux are somewhat similar since they both despise technology and they are the oldest child.

Faith (formerly)[]

Upon seeing Faith as Heavy Metal, Kai disliked her as she was Iron Baron's Number 2 and best skilled fighter. He also thought of her as a big threat when knowing that she fought the First Borne and lived, getting her scale in the process and making it into a sword.

However, when Faith tracked the ninja and Wu down, with the latter finding out he was the son of the First Spinjitzu Master, she revealed she was a woman and became more honest with them, which made Kai begin to trust her, especially after realizing that she also disliked Baron. Apart from Wu, Kai and Faith seemed to have the most interactions. Faith was impressed with how Kai tripped two of Iron Barron's lackeys with a Vengstone chain, and Kai was thankful when Faith showed him how to use the Chain Gun. In the process, when Kai grew frustrated on the first few failed attempts, Faith placed her hand on his shoulder, letting him know that he must have patience.

When Faith and the First Borne arrived at the Monastery, both injured, Kai was worried for her. In "Endings" when Faith announced that she would fight with them against the Oni, Kai was skeptical as she should be resting, but went with it and they fought together.

Iron Baron[]

“His throne. I already hate him.”
— Kai to Jay and Zane, "Iron & Stone"

Kai developed an instant hatred for Iron Baron, upon seeing he killed the Ultra Dragon (despite not knowing it) and turned his remains into a throne.

Their antagonism became stronger when Kai and his friends released the dragons he and his Hunters captured. Baron would set his Hunters after the ninja for their actions.

Kai and the other ninja were sought out by Baron but managed to avoid him, while also making sure that he doesn't claim Wu after he discovers the latter's heritage.

Aspheera (temporary ally)[]

“You! You're a fire Elemental?”
“Yeah ! And the name's Kai!”
“Your fire warms me. For too long I have been trapped in this cold, dark place. I will take your fire!”
“Oh, yeah?”
— Aspheera and Kai, "Boobytraps and How to Survive Them"

The fire serpentine was responsible for taking Kai's power and leaving the Fire Ninja powerless. For this, Kai gained a dislike for her and wants to stop her plans from coming true.

Even after defeating her, Kai still holds a strong hatred for her and laments that he is useless because of his inability to contribute in his friends' quest.

During his time in the Never-Realm, Kai moved slowly passed his sadness and was able to regain his powers, much to his joy.

A year after the Great Flood, Kai and his teammates broke Aspheera out of prison so she could help them restore Nya to her original form by draining her elemental powers, despite Wu being against it. Although she initially attacked him and the others when they brought her staff back, she otherwise did what she was asked after Lloyd offered her an escape when the New Ninja invaded the monastery.

Ras[]

Cinder[]

“I heard there's a new Elemental Master of Smoke. You know I trashed the last one, right?”
— Kai to Cinder, "Shattered Dreams"

Their rivalry grew after Cinder injured Wyldfyre. They met again in "Rising Ninja", where Kai showed Cinder the Rising Dragon to save Nya and once again Kai manage to easily defeat Cinder. During Kai's absence, due to his being sent to Nether-Space, Cinder insulted or made fun of him several times, which provoked the animosity of the other Ninja.

Nokt[]

“I’ll think I’ll finish you with your own student’s heat power. A poetic end to the red ninja.”
— Nokt to Kai, "Elements of Betrayal"

Like all his teammates, neither Kai nor Nokt share a friendly relationship due to their different sides.

In "Elements of Betrayal", Kai returns from the Nether-Space and fights Nokt to protect his sister and his friends, being actually able to hold him off for a while, despite the fact Nokt had at his disposal the elemental powers of all the participants of the Tournament of the Sources.

Kai's hatred for Nokt grew after the latter stole Cole's soul in "The Vault of Focus".

Despite being enemies, they seem to hold some level of respect for each other, as Kai considers him to be one of the most dangerous of the Five, and Nokt considered Kai to be more of a challenge than Nya. He was eager to meet Kai in the tournament and wanted to give him "a poetic end" during their first battle.
