Overview

Michael Kopsa (January 22, 1956 - October 23, 2022[1]) was a Canadian voice actor who provided the voice of Samukai and Vex in the Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu television series. He passed away due to complications from a brain tumor.[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He is sometimes credited as Mike Kopsa.
  • His other voice-acting roles include Char Aznable in the English dub of Mobile Suit Gundam, Commander Volcott O'Huey in Galaxy Angel, and Beast in X-Men: Evolution.
  • He appeared on the science fiction television series Stargate SG-1 on several occasions, as well as The X Files, The Net, The Outer Limits, and Fringe.
  • He appeared in the 2005 film Fantastic Four as Ned Cecil.
  • He is one of seven people named "Michael" providing voicework for Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu, with the other six being Michael Adamthwaite, Michael Dobson, Michael Antonakos, Michael Peña, Michael Donovan, and Michael Daingerfield.
  • He, along with Adrian Petriw, Vincent Tong, Mackenzie Gray, Michael Adamthwaite, Ashleigh Ball, Michael Daingerfield, Michael Dobson, Brian Drummond, Andrew Francis, Dean Redman, Richard Newman, Mark Oliver, Tabitha St. Germain, and Lee Tockar voiced characters in Iron Man: Armored Adventures.
