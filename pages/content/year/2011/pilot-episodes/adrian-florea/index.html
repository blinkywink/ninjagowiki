Adrian Florea is a Romanian[1] designer and former set designer at LEGO.[2] He provided model design for Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu and designed sets for its accompanying LEGO theme from 2011 until 2017.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He was a key designer during the production of the LEGO theme Legends of Chima.[4]
  • He vacated his position at LEGO in March of 2018 and currently designs at Bombagomba, a company of his own creation.[5] His Facebook accounts lists work on this very wiki as well.[2]
