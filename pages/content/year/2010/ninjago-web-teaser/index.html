The Ninjago web teaser was a section on the LEGO.com website published on October 2010 to promote the upcoming Ninjago theme. It was the first media about the franchise published online.

Because it could not be properly archived, it is almost impossible to know exactly what the page looked like, since it ran on Adobe Flash. However, archived content still exists that shows how it worked.

Content

Content[]

Throughout 2010, from October to December, the website was updated with 4 distinct sections related to the fictional world of Ninjago. The sections that were added are as follows:

What is a ninja?[]

The first section, published on October 15, was an illustrated slideshow presentation featuring a character similar to Wu, accompanied by text that mentioned fictional traits attributed to real-life ninja, before introducing three legendary Ninjago ninja: Yello Beez, Chip C. Rumble, and Hiz Shadoh.

Where do ninja live?[]

The second section, published on October 25, was a slideshow divided into sections that presented information on various locations in Ninjago featured in the Pilot episodes, including descriptions of the sites and the local "wildlife."

The locations featured on the website were:

  • Caves of Despair
  • Floating Ruins
  • Frozen Wasteland
  • Forest of Tranquility
  • Monastery of Spinjitzu
  • Four Weapons Blacksmith
  • Fire Temple
  • Underworld
  • Garmadon's Dark Fortress
  • Ninjago Skies

Are you a ninja?[]

The third section, published on November 1, The Four Paths game was added to the website. It consisted of four sections. Players were invited to test their skills in the four elements, with one minigame for each element. Fire was unlocked automatically, earth on November 8th, lightning on November 15th, and ice on November 22nd.

Meet the Ninja[]

The fourth and final section, published on December 1st, was a segment that included descriptions of 10 characters from the pilot episodes, as well as a section describing "the coming battle between the ninja and the skeleton army."

The characters featured on the site were:

  • Cole
  • Jay
  • Kai
  • Zane
  • Chopov
  • Krazi
  • Frakjaw
  • Bonezai
  • Wu
  • Garmadon

Quizzes[]

Alongside the previous sections, the website also featured four multiple-choice classification quizzes. Completing each quiz allowed users to earn downloadable, Ninjago-themed virtual posters.

The available quizzes were:

  • "Is your mom a ninja?": A quiz that asked whether the user's mother performed actions similar to those of a ninja. The prize was a poster of Wu in various action poses.
  • "What animal are you?": A quiz that asked the user what kind of animal they were, based on their daily activities. The prize was a Ninjago Wildlife poster.
  • "What kind of ninja are you?": A quiz that asked the user which ninja best represented them (Kai, Jay, Zane, or Cole) based on certain fictional scenarios. The prize is unknown.
  • "What kind of skeleton are you?": A quiz that asked the user which Skulkin best represented them (Chopov, Krazi, Bonezai, or Frakjaw) based on various exaggerated personality traits. The prize is unknown.
