American Ninja Warrior[1] was a series of shorts released across 2015 and 2016. Each short was a Tongal contest winning production, created with the intention of being released as official promotional material for Ninjago.[2] It serves as a crossover between Ninjago and the American reality series American Ninja Warrior.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Ninjago would collaborate with American Ninja Warrior again in 2017 for the release of The LEGO Ninjago Movie by including an obstacle in the show's ninth season called the "Ninjago Roll."[3] Furthermore, a mini-movie for the film, which included the Ninja watching Jay fail the course, was shown during an airing of the competition.[4]
  • Ninjago also collaborated with Ninja Warrior UK (the British counterpart of American Ninja Warrior) to release a promotional video where Garmadon reacts to fails from the show.[5]
