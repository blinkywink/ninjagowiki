This page is regarding events that took place during the San Diego Comic-Con 2018 Ninjago panel. The panel took place on July 22, 2018 at 10:30am PDT.[1]

Panel Events

Panel Events[]

  • A clip of Jay practicing his proposal from "The Darkness Comes" was shown.[2]
Panel Guests

Panel Guests[]

  • Tommy Andreasen
  • Michael Knap
  • Kelly Metzger
  • Brent Miller
  • Vincent Tong
Booth

Booth[]

  • Kelly Metzger, Brent Miller, and Vincent Tong signed autographs and took pictures with fans.
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • According to Tommy Andreasen on X, the theme of this panel was love.[3]
    • This was likely in reference to the video of Jay practicing his proposal to Nya shown during the panel.
