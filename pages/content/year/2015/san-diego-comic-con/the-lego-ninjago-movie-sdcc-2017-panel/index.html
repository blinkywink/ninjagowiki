This page is regarding events that took place during the San Diego Comic-Con 2017 The LEGO Ninjago Movie panel. The panel took place on Thursday July 20, 2017 from 6:45pm - 7:45pm PDT.[1]

Panel Events

Panel Events[]

  • Panel guests answered questions related to the movie.
  • A then new trailer for the movie was shown.
  • A short video message from Jackie Chan was played.
Panel Guests

Panel Guests[]

  • Dave Franco
  • Abbi Jacobson
  • Dan Lin
  • Olivia Munn
  • Kumail Nanjiani
  • Michael Peña
  • Justin Theroux
  • Zach Woods
Booth

Booth[]

  • Fans could try out The LEGO Ninjago Movie Videogame early.[2]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The only leading actors from The LEGO Ninjago Movie to not appear in person for the panel were Fred Armisen and Jackie Chan (although the latter did appear in a pre-recorded video message to make up for his absence).
