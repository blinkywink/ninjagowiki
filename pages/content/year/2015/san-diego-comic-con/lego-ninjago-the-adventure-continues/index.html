This page is regarding events that took place during the San Diego Comic-Con 2025 Ninjago panel. The panel took place on July 24, 2025 from 2:15pm-3:15pm PDT.[1]

Official description

Official description[]

Dragons! Combat! And tiny plastic ninja! Come witness the red ninja action as LEGO Ninjago: Dragons Rising stars Vincent Tong (the voice of Kai, elemental master of fire) and Kazumi Evans (the voice of Wyldfyre, elemental master of heat) perform a new canon scene live, written by Dragons Rising head writers and fellow panelists Kevin Burke and Doc Wyatt exclusively for Comic-Con 2025.[1]

Panel Events

Panel Events[]

  • Trailers for the second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 3 and Kai's Monstrous Journey were shown.
  • It was announced that the second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 3 would release in early October of that same year.
    • Despite this, Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 3 Part 2 actually released in September 4.
  • It was announced that Dragons Rising had been greenlit for a fourth season.[2]
    • The fourth season will explore more realms and the ninja will be pushed to more emotional places.[2]
    • Arin, Sora, Frak and Wyldfyre will have their chance to shine and interact in fourth season.[2]
  • Kazumi Evans and Vincent Tong performed a canon scene written by Chris "Doc" Wyatt and Kevin Burke.[2]
    • Chris "Doc" Wyatt confirmed in the panel that ad libs spoken by the actors during the scene were non-canon.[2]
  • A new official Ninjago Roblox tie in game with second half of Ninjago: Dragons Rising Season 3.[2]
  • It was revealed that 2026 ninja suit designs will merge past, present and future.[2]
  • It was revealed that there will be more Ninjago: Legends content in 2026.[2]
  • Already known info about Strike of the Serpentine was mentioned.[2]
  • The panel guests answered questions from the audience, in which several details about Ninjago were revealed:
    • It was revealed that The Weekend Whip will return in some form.[2]
    • It was revealed that Elemental Powers were supposed to be mixed and unexpected following The Merge, explaining why elements such as Technology and Surface Tension exist.
    • Zarkt was revealed to be a wolf and part of the same tribe as Nokt and Rox.[2]
    • It was revealed that Wyldfyre's origin and the identity of The Administrator aren't currently a priority in Ninjago: Dragons Rising.[2]
    • It was revealed that although Legends of Chima is a canon to Ninjago, its place in the timeline isn't a priority, but may be revealed in some other media.[2]
    • It was revealed that Misako and Garmadon won't be showing up in the near future.[2]
    • It was revealed that the original ninja's parents not being acknowledged implies that they are simply being contacted off-screen, with the exceptions of Lloyd, Jay and Zane (whose creator Dr. Julien is dead).[2]
    • It was also revealed that since the Merge, time might pass differently. However, a calendar system or told time isn't established.[2]
    • In Q&A it was answered that there are possibilities of Jay and Dareth getting their own spin/off series or Ninjago: Legends content.[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • After the panel, the LEGO team's table happened to be near the table for the cast of Resident Alien.[3] Chris "Doc" Wyatt decided to go over to say thank you, as he enjoys watching the series with his wife and son.[4] Sara Tomko asked who Doc and the rest were, and after learning they worked on Ninjago, the cast gave a round of applause and said they love Ninjago.[5]
