Bad Chair Day is the fifth short based around Master Chen and his throne, taking place sometime during or before the fourth season of Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

We think Master Chen needs to start listening to Clouse, don't you? Don't say Clouse didn't warn you Chen…that chair has some powerful tricks!

Plot

Plot[]

Excited about his new throne, Chen pressed a button, much to Clouse's annoyance. He tried to tell him to wait until he had properly read the manual but was constantly interrupted by the chair's functions. After Chen accidentally teleported them to a jungle, Clouse has no choice but to carry them back home.
