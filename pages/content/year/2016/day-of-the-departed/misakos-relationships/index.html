Overview

These are the relationships of Misako, wife of Lord Garmadon and mother of the legendary Green Ninja, Lloyd.

Family

Family[]

Garmadon[]

“I was married to him once. I'm up for anything.”
— Misako to Nya, "Big Trouble, Little Ninjago"

Garmadon is Misako's husband. In his younger years, Garmadon always had eyes for her and she was the one person to cause him control of his evil. While she seemed to have eyes for Wu, she fell in love with Garmadon after receiving a letter that he wrote for her.[1]

Misako married him and gave birth to their son Lloyd. However, when Garmadon's evil fully corrupted him and caused him to distance himself from his family, she went off to find a way to prevent him from facing his son, as the two were destined to battle each other. After Garmadon was purified of his evil, he was reunited with his wife, who embraced him joyfully, and he was able to finally be with her. They were happy together as the two reconnected as a couple, with her supporting him being a master.

Garmadon later confided in Lloyd of how he had deceptively earned Misako's affections and lied to her by signing his brother's letter as his own. However, he did not regret his choice, as he was able to have happiness before his evil fully corrupted him, and Lloyd would not have been born otherwise. Though he was purified of the evil, Garmadon kept this secret from Misako out of fear of losing her again.[1]

When telling Lloyd this tale, the latter persuaded him to be honest with her, and Wu as not telling would hurt him even more, and Garmadon agreed. After returning to her, she was surprised at him being an Anacondrai but accepting of him while noticing he was sad about something and told him to tell her, as he was her husband. He tried to tell her the truth but was interrupted, though she soon found out from Chen and was heartbroken by his deceit. Despite this, she still forgave Garmadon before he was banished to the Cursed Realm by Lloyd.[2] She paid respect to him on the Day of the Departed along with her son and continued to remember him with love.

When Garmadon was being resurrected, Misako told Lloyd that the evil person Harumi wanted to bring back was not Garmadon, showing she still had love for him. When Garmadon came back as his evil self, he told Lloyd that he played many roles one of which was a brother, husband, and father but now he's a destroyer. When confronted by Garmadon, Misako told Nya that she can handle him as she was married to him, Garmadon attacked her with no hesitation showing he had lost his affections for her since he was completely consumed by evil. Despite this, it was later revealed that Garmadon's rebirth still encompassed all that he once was and given the fact he did not kill her but instead imprisoned her may imply, he still cared for her enough to spare her but has no qualms putting her in her place so he can save Ninjago by uniting it and unlocking his True Potential.

In "Endings," Garmadon meets Misako again, and she is surprised to see him bringing over the Armor of the Golden Master to the Ninja so that they can use it to fight against the Oni. Admitting his remorse for his past encounters with Misako, Garmadon bluntly told her that he was no longer the man that would say sweet things to her, but didn't show any animosity towards her apart from that.

In "An Issue of Trust", Garmadon and Misako met up again, though they hardly communicated. In spite of this, they were willing to work together alongside Wu and the ninja to fight against the Overlord and his massive army of Crystal Warriors. Following the Overlord's defeat in "Roots", Garmadon reunited with them before helping them rebuild the monastery, implying that he may get a chance to fix his relationship with Misako and their family for good.

Lloyd[]

“Leaving you was the hardest thing I ever had to do. But faced with saving you and the world, I had no other choice.”
— Misako to Lloyd, "The Stone Army"

Lloyd is Misako's son. They care a lot about each other throughout the series. She was forced to leave him for years and they were reunited, Lloyd was initially hostile towards her for her supposed abandoning of him. However, after finding out her reason was to protect him and save his father, he ultimately forgave her and the two became closer in future seasons.

In Season 5, when Lloyd was possessed by Morro, Misako was desperate to get her son back stating she wasn't going to lose him just as she lost her husband. After Lloyd was freed of his possession, Misako was happy to have her son back and consoled him after the defeat of the Preeminent which led to his father's demise.

While fighting Nadakhan, Lloyd was sad at the thought of his mother being one of the Djinn's victims. On the Day of the Departed, Lloyd and his mother pay their respect to Garmadon and Lloyd defended his mother from Pythor when he attacked them.

During Season 8: Sons of Garmadon, Lloyd mentions his mother going off on her own to find Wu but he doesn't know where she is and seems worried for her.

They are reunited in "Dread on Arrival" and Lloyd discovers Misako found Wu before she was captured by the Sons of Garmadon. He was very angry at seeing her situation and wanted her released before the ritual took place.

Wu[]

“I should have chosen you.”
— Misako to Wu, "The Day Ninjago Stood Still"

In his younger years, Wu had strong affections for Misako and it seemed they were reciprocated but Misako also had feelings for Wu's brother, Garmadon. Wu wrote her a love letter for Misako that was intercepted by Chen who gave it to Garmadon and suggested him to sign it instead of Wu knowing that the former also has feelings for Misako. Garmadon followed his master's advice and offered Wu's letter in his place to Misako which finally made her fall in love with him. Unaware of Garmadon's deception, Wu did not object to their union in spite of his heavy feelings for Misako and accepted Misako's choice. Through her marriage to Garmadon, she became Wu's sister-in-law and they got along very well in the years.

They met again after Garmadon's evil fully corrupted him, the two get along very well and often share jokes as Misako once remarked she should have married Wu while dealing with Garmadon's evil. While it looked like they would be a couple again, Misako's love for Garmadon was maintained and she along with Lloyd reconnected with him as Wu was happy to see them as a family again.

In "The Corridor of Elders", Chen revealed the deception surrounding the letter to Wu and Misako, who were devastated by this news and angry at Garmadon for committing the act. They got closer at that time but both showed that they still loved Garmadon as they were against his choice of banishing himself to the Cursed Realm.

Following the Second Serpentine War, they are often paired up and closer than before with occasional smiles and talks between them.

During Season 7, after his fight with Acronix Misako stayed by his side as he confided in her that his condition.

In Season 8, it is revealed by Lloyd that Misako had gone off to find Wu at some point in the year between seasons. In "Dread on Arrival," it is mentioned by Harumi that Misako was successful in locating him before they were captured by the Sons of Garmadon.

Following their subsequent separation and Garmadon's revival, they remain close companions. Wu notably accompanied Misako and her Explorer's Club team on an dangerous excursion to the Island of the Keepers.

Wu seems to have respect for Misako and is a little afraid of her, and she has the power to influence him, as it turns out when she ordered him to go to Shintaro. However, she was a little ticked off when Wu accidentally called her old when he said that adventuring was for the young. Nevertheless, she always seems to know what's best for him.

It is clear that Misako means a lot to Wu as when Kalmaar threatened her in "Master of the Sea," Wu didn't hesitate to attack him and then surrender when he saw that he couldn't win, to protect Misako.

In Tommy Andreasen's opinion, they have been in a romantic relationship since the Second Serpentine War.[3][4]

Friends

Friends[]

Ninja[]

She gets very well with the ninja and appreciative of them since they looked out for her son while she was gone. She cares for their well-being as seen when she was worried after they lost Zane but was happy after he turned out to be alive. Misako was devastated when all of them were presumed dead in Season 8 but was happy to learn in Season 9 that they were alive somewhere.

In turn, the ninja care for her because of her helping them on missions.
