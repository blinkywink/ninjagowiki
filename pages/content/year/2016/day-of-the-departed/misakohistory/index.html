Overview

Background

Background[]

As a young woman, Misako met the sons of the First Spinjitzu Master: Garmadon and Wu. She befriended the brothers and was even taught Spinjitzu by Wu.[1][2] Though she was initially inclined toward Wu, a letter she received from Garmadon swayed her to choose to marry him instead.[3] The two spent many happy years together, and eventually had a son named Lloyd. Shortly afterwards, she began wearing glasses.[1] Eventually, Garmadon succumbed to the evil growing inside him as a result of his bite by the Great Devourer and was cast into the Underworld after a battle against Wu.[4]

Realizing that Lloyd would become the legendary Green Ninja, Misako left him at Darkley's Boarding School for Bad Boys so that she could research the prophecy in the hopes of preventing it and saving her family.[5]

Misako wrote letters to Lloyd while she was away, one of which mentioned the Glacier Barrens.[6]

Eventually, she got a job at the Ninjago Museum of History.[5] She also joined Explorer's Club and, as a member, became familiar with their policy of laughing in the face of danger — which, as outlined in a test, also permitted the use of "Ho, ho, ho" as an acceptable alternative to the typical laughter — and the details of Trial By Sphinx.[7] At some point, she interacted with Dr. Saunders.[8]

Legacy of the Green Ninja

Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

The Stone Army[]

When the ninja visited the Ninjago Museum of History, the workers informed them that the Great Devourer's venom had worked its way through the sewer system of Ninjago City and ended up seeping through into the Ninjago Museum of History, resulting in the venom infecting the Museum's merchandise of the Stone Warrior figurines and bringing them to life. While the ninja were fighting off the toy Stone Warriors, one slipped out of the room and hops down the hallway, and as Master Wu followed it and stomped on it, he ran into Misako, who had just exited the room behind him. Wu seemed surprised to see her, and stated that it had "been a long time." The rest of the ninja and Lloyd then walked out of the Merchandise room where Wu introduced Misako as Lloyd's mother. She was shocked to see her son had grown a lot and tried to explain that she had her reasons for being away, but watched in disappointment as her son refused to listen to her and took off down the hall into another room, angered at being estranged from her.

Misako found Lloyd sitting alone in one of the rooms and started to explain that she had been left with no choice when she abandoned him so long ago. She stated that she knew he was going to be the Green Ninja from the beginning, and so she left him to research the prophecy in hopes of preventing it. The other ninja arrived and she began telling a story of the First Spinjitzu Master that involved the Island of Darkness and the Stone Warriors.

However, in another part of the Museum, the Great Devourer's venom dripped from the ceiling onto a Giant Stone Warrior, which Misako found underneath the museum began to attack the Ninja. They fled and ran through the Museum as they tried to figure out how to defeat it.

They stopped inside a large room and closed the doors, and while the Stone Warrior broke it down Lloyd came up with a plan and told the ninja to distract it. Lloyd left the room and the Stone Warrior broke in, Misako deciding to assist Lloyd by leading the Stone Warrior to Lloyd's location. She met up with him, and Lloyd tricked the Stone Warrior into stepping over the fabricated flooring to reveal the giant sinkhole where it had been unearthed. After the warrior fell, Misako tells Lloyd that she was proud of him, and they share a hug. Master Wu then asks her to stay with them, to which she accepted.

The Day Ninjago Stood Still[]

While Wu was sentimentally gazing at a picture of himself, Garmadon, and Misako before Garmadon's banishment, Misako walked in the room commenting on how Garmadon looked before his dark transformation. She shows Wu a scroll containing her research, and as he looked over it she regretfully stated that she should have chosen him instead. Wu countered this by stating that the past was the past, but Misako told him that there was still the future, while taking his hand in hers. However, a sudden tremor from outside interrupts their moment and they go to investigate.

At Ninjago City, after the ninja saved a woman from the crumbling earth, Misako realized that the earthquakes were a sign of the Stone Warrior Army's return.

As the citizens of Ninjago rushed into a building to get to the roof to escape on the Destiny's Bounty, Dareth bumped into Misako in the rush, making her drop her research scrolls. When they arrived on the roof, Misako attempted to hold Wu's hand again, only to be separated by the enormous crowd heading toward the Bounty.

Right as the Destiny's Bounty was leaving, Misako remembers her scrolls, saying that they would be too important to lose and heads back into the building. She spots the scrolls tangled among the wires of the long ceiling lights and managed to avoid the approaching Stone Army and climb down to retrieve them. Upon obtaining the scrolls, she slips while jumping to the railing, and before she falls, Wu grabbed her and helps her up. The two ran into a room where they were cornered, trying their best to fight off the warriors by using Spinjitzu. Seeing how their efforts were pointless, they decide to escape through the window, only to be caught by Nya on the Destiny's Bounty. Wu loses the photo in the wind, but Misako assured him that the past was the past.

The Last Voyage[]

Misako informed the ninja that there was indeed a way to defeat the Stone Army, which was to reach the Temple of Light—an ancient building where Lloyd would realize his power. However, Nya pointed out the Bounty's state from the Stone Army's previous ambush, but Misako told her that they would be able to sail since the Destiny's Bounty is a ship.

Onboard the Bounty, Misako warned her teammates of the storm overhead, later identifying the ominous laughter from the sea to be the sound of Starteeth. She cautioned the ninja of the Starteeth's devastating ability: to chew through seemingly anything.

Island of Darkness[]

Misako explained to the ninja about her scrolls and how they can find the Temple of Light. She agreed with Wu to keep Lloyd hidden. Later, they watched as the ninja successfully reached the Temple.

The Last Hope[]

Misako tells the team that if the Helmet of Darkness is returned to the Celestial Clock's pedestal, the countdown to the Final Battle might stop. She then volunteers to retrieve the helmet with the ninja's help.

She, along with the ninja disguised as Stone Warriors, are brought up to the Stone Army camp, where she was taken to Garmadon. After convincing him to take his helmet off, she stole it and ran away. She soon discovered the ninja were spotted and tried to escape Garmadon's mech. When their powers are no match, Nya came in to save them on the Earth Driller.

She and the others arrive at the Celestial Clock and tried to find the pedestal, but was too late. The cliff under them fell, leaving them trapped while Nya is captured by the Stone Army.

Return of the Overlord[]

When Lloyd is hesitant about fighting his father, Misako assured him by telling him he doesn't want to fight either. They prepared to fight, but find the Stone Army camp vacant. They then followed Garmatron's tracks, which leads to the coast. They met up with Nya, who was unfortunate enough to be infected with Dark Matter. Cole asked Misako, Wu, and Lloyd to stop Garmadon while the ninja handle Nya.

Misako failed to convince Garmadon not to fire the Garmatron, much to their fears. Eventually, she watched as the Overlord possesses Garmadon and tells Lloyd he has to face him. When Lloyd was thrown, Misako and Wu went to make sure he's not hurt.

Rise of the Spinjitzu Master[]

After the Overlord's attack, Misako hurries up to find her son, Lloyd. Discovering Lloyd's injury, Misako calls Dr. Julien to help Lloyd.

When the Ultra Dragon appears on the Dark Island, Misako and the others travel back to Ninjago. Wu, Misako, and Dr. Julien stay on the dragon while the ninja face the Stone Army so Lloyd can face the Overlord.

Lloyd succeeds in defeating the Overlord and purges Ninjago of darkness. Misako and the others believe Garmadon is gone, but when he emerges from a rock, having been cleansed all of the evil possessing him, Misako is in disbelief at his restoration. She and her family share a hug and go off to spend time with each other as a family.

Misako, Lloyd, and Garmadon soon return to the others for a group photo.[9]

Dark Island Trilogy

Dark Island Trilogy[]

Dark Island Trilogy, Part 1[]

After hearing of reports of missing fishermen off the coast of the Dark Island, Misako sailed to the island to investigate. As Wu did not want her to be alone but was unable to accompany her himself, he hired Ronin to journey with her. After discovering mutant creatures on the island, the two decided to sail back for help. However, on their way, their boat came under attack by the Sky Pirates. As Ronin attempted to fend them off, Misako hurriedly wrote a note to Wu warning him, before she was captured.

Dark Island Trilogy, Part 2[]

Misako was taken by the pirates to the caves of Digger's Deep intending to use her as slave labor along with the captured fishermen to mine for Dark Matter. Being in the caves caused Misako to quickly fall under the Dark Matter's effects and so when Cole, Lloyd, and Zane arrived to rescue her, she alerted the pirates. Luckily, Lloyd's elemental energy was able to reverse the effects, and he and Misako aided in freeing the fishermen. Though the encountered the Sky Pirates again at the exit to the caves, Jay, Cole, Nya, Zane, & Ronin arrived and sent them fleeing. After the fishermen left for their boats, Misako boarded the Ultra Sonic Raider and she and the ninja pursued the pirates, heading toward the Temple of Light.

Dark Island Trilogy, Part 3[]

Misako and her allies pursued Clouse and his airship, following him to the Billy Badlands were they engaged in a confrontation against him and a convoy of Sky Pirates. During the battle, Misako manned the Ultra Sonic Raider's guns and later survived the sandstorm thanks to the Ninja's use of their elemental powers. As she emerged from the sand, Misako briefly overheard the Ninja suggesting that Clouse would soon achieve victory thanks to the delay they had suffered from the sandstorm. In response, Misako chided them, rhetorically asking if Wu had taught them to simply give up when things headed south. "No!" she said, "Ninja never quit!" Heeding Misako's words, the Ninja and Ronin began working to dig out their vehicles to continue their journey to the Temple of Light, where Clouse would inevitably be heading.

By nightfall, several hours later, Misako and the others finally arrived near the Temple of Light, but were still hours behind Clouse's arrival. As the sorcerer's evil master plan began nearing completion, earthquakes erupted across the Dark Island as it began drifting towards Ninjago. As the group continued climbing up towards the Temple, Misako looked around for Wu but he was still nowhere to be seen. At that moment, another earthquake collapsed the ledge the group were traversing upon, hurling Misako and the others down the sides of the mountain as she rhetorically shouted, "To what?!" in response to Nya's call for them all to hang on to something. Fortunately however, Misako would be saved by her son using Airjitzu and she was settled onto the beaches below where Wu was just arriving. Misako and her allies then helped fight against the Sky Pirates and the Shadow Army while Wu challenged Clouse alone with the golden mech.

Following their victory, Misako turned to Wu with a puzzled look, remarking that she thought the golden mech had been destroyed during the Final Battle a long time ago. Wu confirmed this, but revealed that he had secretly recovered the remnants of the mech after the Final Battle and brought them to Cyrus Borg to be repaired, believing that one day it might be needed again when the balance would be shifted once more. With a smile, Misako complimented Wu on his foresight and stood alongside her allies as they looked on outwards to the horizon, light shining upon them to mark the beginning of a new day.

Later that night, with their families and friends gathered around they listen to Cole telling the story before lighting the lanterns for the holiday.

Two Lies, One Truth[]

While sharing a cell with Ronin, Misako told him that Lloyd will save them, something he remarked as easy for her to say.

Green Destiny[]

Somehow, Misako was now sharing a cell with P.I.X.A.L. when they hear Wu and the ninja have returned and chanted "The Resistance never quits!" before Griffin opens all the cell doors. She was among those to help chain down Colossus until her soulless husband lost his powers.

March of the Oni

March of the Oni[]

The Darkness Comes[]

Misako and Wu are in the monastery's courtyard observing the new murals. They see Firstbourne crash. Misako stayed in the monastery while the others went and found that Faith had also been injured in the crash. They brought her to Misako to help her heal.

Endings[]

Misako listened as Faith told her and Wu what happened in the Realm of Oni and Dragons. Faith laments on how she might have brought the Oni to Ninjago, but Misako reassured her and said she had no other choice than to escape.

Meanwhile, the ninja landed near the monastery and Wu and Misako went to see them. They were shocked to learn that Cole fell into the dark clouds and that destroying the Realm Crystal did nothing. However, Misako is surprised to see that Garmadon (who was released to help the ninja fight the Oni) has got the Golden Armor for the ninja to melt down and reforge the weapons so that they can fight the Oni. Before giving the armor to Wu, Garmadon admits his remorse for his past encounters with Misako, sadly admitting that he is no longer the man who cared for her.

After the Oni arrived, Misako refused to leave Lloyd's side, but he convinced her to defend the people inside the monastery. She watched as the ninja retreated into the monastery and used the Tornado of Creation.

However, they found Lloyd under a pile of debris and mourned. He suddenly woke up and the ninja were relieved. As they celebrate, their allies visited the monastery and watched the ninja make a new mural with their handprints.

Master of the Mountain

Master of the Mountain[]

Shintaro[]

While Wu and Lloyd inside the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Misako asks why Wu is feeling so down. Later outside, Misako forces Wu to attend the Kingdom of Shintaro along with the ninja after Wu accidentally called her old when he said that adventuring was for the young and she would understand how he feels more than anyone else.

Mother Earth[]

While the ninja are gone, Misako and P.I.X.A.L. work on a new ship. Misako scolds P.I.X.A.L. for wasting time playing a video game when they have so much equipment to bring onboard. P.I.X.A.L. accidentally knocks a stack of photos onto the floor and shows one of them to Misako, not recognizing someone in a photo with young Wu. Misako tells her it is Lilly, Cole's mother and the previous Master of Earth. Misako proceeds to tell P.I.X.A.L. the story of Lilly's life.

Prior to The Island

Prior to The Island[]

Shortly after the return of the ninja and Wu from Shintaro, she decided to go and confirm her theory about the uncharted island and navigate the storm belt, so she traveled to the storm belt with her friend Wu and a colleague from the Explorer's Club, Clutch Powers and his interns. However, when they went to the island, they were hit by storms and were wrecked on the island. Later, they set up a tent and made a large HELP sign out of stones in case someone went after them. Shortly afterwards, they went to the jungle to explore the island.

The Island

The Island[]

Uncharted[]

Misako, Clutch Powers with his interns and Wu explore the jungle on the island. They are cutting through the jungle since morning when they encounter a statue. They think it is just an ordinary statue, though their hopes are vanished when the Stone Golems attack them.

Prior to the The Gift of Jay[]

The expedition was defeated by the golems who worked with the Keepers, and since they thought they had come to steal the Storm Amulet like everyone else, they imprisoned them in their village.

The Gift of Jay[]

When the ninja were locked up with them, Misako appeared to the ninja, explaining that the Keepers are protective, not hostile. Then, when the ninja were able to escape, Misako followed. However, Clutch Powers tried to steal the Storm Amulet, causing them to be knocked out and put on poles, tied up. Misako then realizes that Jay is the gift to Wojira, and he gets sent off to sea.

The Tooth of Wojira[]

The ninja are able to escape the poles again, with Misako kicking a nearby Keeper. She is then with the others when Mammatus tells the story of the Amulet. When Lloyd ponders in the docks whether there is a real Wojira, Misako and Wu come to see him.

Prior to Seabound

Prior to Seabound[]

Misako returned with the ninja and Wu from the uncharted island back to the monastery after Ronin's imprisonment.

Seabound

Seabound[]

The Call of the Deep[]

Misako helps Wu in the library when they search for information on the Elemental Power of Water. When Nya comes to them and tells them about her mother and her exaggerated care, Misako explains to her that she has been without her for a very long time and wants to make up for lost moments with her daughter. She finally convinces her that she should talk to her brother. Nya then hands her the book and the scroll she took from Wu, and Misako takes it.

Riddle of the Sphinx[]

When Cole, Kai, Ray and Wu found out that the Storm Amulet was in the Explorer's Club, they decided to go there with Misako. When they arrived, Misako tried to persuade Cecil to let them into the club to visit Clutch Powers, even though her membership was revoked. Because Cecil refused to let them go, Misako asked for the Trial By Sphinx, the only way to get inside for non-members. Cecil finally agreed, although Misako, as a former club member, could not answer the Sphinx' riddle. After Kai answered correctly, she and the others entered the club to obtain the Amulet.

When the Merlopians broke into the club, Misako fought several of them.

Master of the Sea[]

As Kalmaar tried to escape with the Amulet in the harbor, Misako, holding the rope of a ship, jumped on him and kicked him in the face, dropping the Amulet on the pier. Then she ran to it, but Kalmaar grabbed her legs with his tentacles and tried to kill her with his trident, but Wu stopped him. However, Kalmaar put the trident on Misako and threatened her, so Wu eventually gave up.

The Maaray Guards subsequently tied them up and Kalmaar tried to throw them into the sea and drown them, but were saved by the ninja who returned from the Island of the Keepers.

The Turn of the Tide[]

After the Great Flood and Nya's sacrifice, Misako attended her memorial in the monastery.

After Seabound

After Seabound[]

Tentacles Below![]

A couple of days after the Great Flood, Misako talked with Wu at the Monastery shortly before Lloyd arrived and told the two about the reapirs in Ninjago City and the lack of fish supply which was currently being fixed. Misako was then told that there was a sea monster in the city's sewer system that was causing chaos.

Great Droids Think Alike[]

After Nya's memorial in the monastery, Misako and Wu worked together on searching ways to bring Nya back by looking through scrolls regarding the elemental power of Water.

Bad Water![]

Wu made some tea for Misako, but after taking a sip, she spit the tea and told Wu that it tasted bad right before the ninja told them about the bad water in the shower.

At one point, she left the ninja and Wu and traveled to the Library of Domu.[11] Eventually, she learned about the Golden Dragon forms.[12]

Crystalized

Crystalized[]

Return of the Ice Emperor[]

Misako entered the newspaper warehouse, showing Wu a prophecy she found by Quanish the Elder about a possible Dragon Form, explaining how it was the only document she could find that referred to a Dragon Form and that she couldn't read what the words said. Wu then replied, saying that all they could do was hope that interpreting the images would be enough and that Quanish wasn't a fool.

Compatible[]

When Wu grew frustrated that the words in Quanish's scroll were unreadable, Misako recommended he take a break. She then followed Wu after Cole informed them that he connected the warehouse's radio to their ninja comms.

Dragon Form[]

Misako arrived to fight the Crystal Army alongside the rest of the Resistance. Shortly after, she watched as the ninja achieved their Dragon Forms.

Roots[]

Misako watched with Wu and Nya as the ninja flew away, then as the Crystal King corrupted all of Ninjago. Purified from corruption, she celebrated with everyone else after the Crystal King was defeated. She joined Wu and Nya in meeting the ninja, where she talked with Cole.

Misako assisted in rebuilding the Monastery, where she helped move some paintbrushes.

Ninjago: Dragons Rising

Ninjago: Dragons Rising[]

Though not seen, it can be assumed that when the Merge happened she was separated from everyone. Her current whereabouts are unknown.
