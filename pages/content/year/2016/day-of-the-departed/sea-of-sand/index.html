Overview

“The first grueling leg starts on the streets of Ninjago City and empties out into the treacherous Sea of Sand.”
— Announcer, "Ninjaball Run"

The Sea of Sand is an expansive desert located near Ninjago City. Around 200 years before the Final Battle,[2] it was a part of the Endless Sea, although later it became a desert.

History

History[]

The Sea of Sand was once part of the Endless Sea. The Destiny's Bounty ran aground here, remaining almost completely intact even as the water around it disappeared and the land became a desert, pushed upwards due to tectonic shifts.[2][3] This allowed the Falcon to lead Zane to the ship as a new home for the ninja.[4]

The Serpentine used the Sea of Sand as their headquarters, although it is unknown whether the area was a desert by then or if the Serpentine's presence predated even its time as an ocean. After the Serpentine were defeated, the Anacondrai tribe was sealed away in the Sea of Sand, the city of Ouroboros was buried beneath the earth, and the Great Devourer was put into its slumber beneath the city.[5][6][7]

The Sea of Sand is used for two legs of the annual Ninjaball Run competition. Racers enter the desert after leaving Ninjago City and drive along a road through the Sea of Sand on their return from the Glacier Barrens.[8]

On the Day of the Departed, the ninja raced through the Sea of Sand on the Titanium Ninja Tumbler, Rock Roader, and Ultra Sonic Raider to get to the Ninjago Museum of History before the gift shop closed.[9]

Following the Merge, a region of the desert, the wastelands, had been relocated from outside of Ninjago to the borders of the First Land.
