Overview

The following is about significant events for Ninjago that occurred in 2016.

January

January[]

  • 1 - The first wave of 2016 Ninjago sets were released in Europe.
  • 2 - "Infamous" aired in Asia.
  • 3 - "Public Enemy Number One" aired in Asia.
  • 9 - "Enkrypted" aired in Asia.
  • 10 - "Misfortune Rising" aired in Asia.
  • 16 - "On a Wish and a Prayer" aired in Asia.
  • 17 - "My Dinner With Nadakhan" aired in Asia.
  • 23 - "Wishmasters" aired in Asia.
  • 24 - "The Last Resort" aired in Asia.
  • 30 - "Operation Land Ho!" aired in Asia.
February

February[]

  • 1 - "The Way Back" aired in Asia.
  • 17 - "The Tall Tale of Flintlocke" was released.
  • 28 - "The Tall Tale of Clancee" was released.
March

March[]

  • 1 - The second wave of 2016 LEGO Ninjago sets were officially released in the United States.
  • 14 - "The Tall Tale of Doubloon" was released.
  • 24 - "Infamous" aired in the United States.
April

April[]

  • 2 - "The Tall Tale of Dogshank" was released.
  • 20 - "The Tall Tale of Monkey Wretch" was released.
  • 25 - "The Tall Tale of Sqiffy and Bucko" was released.
June

June[]

  • 3 - The third wave of 2016 LEGO Ninjago sets were officially released in Europe.
  • 7 - Character Encyclopedia Updated and Expanded was released.
  • 16 - "Public Enemy One" aired in the United States.
  • 23 - "Enkrypted" aired in the United States.
  • 24 - Team Ninja: Discover the Ninja's Battle Secrets! was released.
  • 30 - "Misfortune Rising" aired in the United States.
July

July[]

  • 11 - "On a Wish and a Prayer" aired in the United States.
  • 12 - "My Dinner With Nadakhan" aired in the United States.
  • 13 - "Wishmasters" aired in the United States.
  • 14 - "The Last Resort" aired in the United States.
  • 15 - "Operation Land Ho!" and "The Way Back" aired in the United States.
August

August[]

  • 1 - The fourth wave of 2016 LEGO Ninjago sets were released in the United States.
  • 8 - Part one of the Dark Island Trilogy was released.
  • 9 - Part two of the Dark Island Trilogy was released.
  • 10 - Part three of the Dark Island Trilogy was was released.
September

September[]

  • 23 - "The Master" was released.
  • 29 - The trailer for "Day of the Departed" was released.[1]
October

October[]

  • 21 - "Day of the Departed" aired in Canada.
  • 29 - "Day of the Departed" aired in the United States.
November

November[]

  • 15 - From Ghosts to Pirates was released.
December

December[]

  • 19 - The first trailer for The Hands of Time was released.[2]
  • 21 - The fifth wave of 2016 LEGO Ninjago sets were released at select Toys R Us and Target stores in the United States.
Unknown

Unknown[]

  • 853544 LEGO NINJAGO Accessory Set was released.
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This year marked the five-year anniversaries of the pilot episodes and first two episodes of Rise of the Snakes, which aired on January 14 and December 2 respectively.
  • This year had Ninjago's first holiday special.
