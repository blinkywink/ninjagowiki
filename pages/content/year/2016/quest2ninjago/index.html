Overview

Quest2NINJAGO was a Ninjago-themed tour held in seven cities across the United States of America.[1] Stops spanned from June 10, 2016 to September 10, 2016.

Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

General description[]

LEGO engaged Sparks for the Quest2NINJAGO Wu-Cru Challenge to activate a physical experience surrounding its wildly successful NINJAGO franchise. The Quest2NINJAGO Wu-Cru Challenge gave kids across the U.S. the chance to compete in a 4,520 sq. ft. “American Ninja Warrior” inspired, interactive challenge course. The program traveled to seven markets to reward fans and help build momentum for “The LEGO NINJAGO Movie” and wrapped with a finale event at LEGOLAND® California Resort.[2]

Finale description[]

Save the date for the Quest 2 NINJAGO® Tour Finale featuring LEGO® Club Weekend on September 10 and 11, 2016!
 The finale will be taking place Saturday morning, where you can watch the Quest2NINJAGO tour finalists from around the country compete for the championship title. The competition kicks off at 10:00 AM at these locations throughout the Park: LEGO NINJAGO World, the Model Shop Rooftop, and in the Build & Test area at Imagination Zone. The finale competition ends at 11:45 AM, and the announcement of the winning team will be at 12:30 PM in Miniland.
Starting that Saturday afternoon - put your ninja skills to the test and conquer the obstacles from the tour to prepare for your ultimate conquest: NINJAGO The Ride. LEGO Club Weekend is back! Get fun LEGO swag and sign up for LEGO Club Magazine at the LEGO Club tent located in The Beginning. Don’t forget to pick up your LEGO Club Weekend button at the tent for weekend-only discounts at the LEGO Club House.
The LEGO giveaways, LEGO Club Weekend buttons and discounted items at the LEGO Club House are available this weekend only while supplies last.[3]
