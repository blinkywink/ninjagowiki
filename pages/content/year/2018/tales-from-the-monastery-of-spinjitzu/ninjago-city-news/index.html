Overview

“Hey! Quit hogging the newspaper, I need my daily funnies.”
— A prison guard, "The Absolute Worst"

Ninjago City News (formerly known as Ninjago Times) are newspapers regularly published in Ninjago City. They are delivered by the paperboys.

History

History[]

Home (flashback)[]

When Zane enters the bathroom, Cole is doing some private business and reading a newspaper.

The Curse of the Golden Master[]

When the ninja meet Skales in the sewers, they search his purchase and find a newspaper, to which he replies that he is a sucker for the crossword.

The Oni and the Dragon[]

When Zane and Cole prepare their costumes to enter Laughy's, Zane holds a newspaper with a photo of Cyrus Borg.

Snake Jaguar[]

People waiting in the Ninjago Metro read the newspaper.

Big Trouble, Little Ninjago[]

When the Colossus destroys a building, there is a man sitting in the toilet reading a newspaper.

Green and Gold[]

As Lloyd sleeps in his bed, a newspaper lies on the floor beside him.

The News Never Sleeps![]

When Nelson arrives in Ninjago City to work as a paperboy, he receives a package of newspapers that he must deliver along with the others. He is assigned to Antonia and they have to deliver newspapers together despite the attacks of Elemental Cobras.

The Absolute Worst[]

The two guards at Kryptarium Prison read the newspaper until Noble interrupts. As the newspaper says that the ninja have disappeared, Noble is afraid that the prisoners will find out and rebel. Unfortunately, the newspaper falls to the ground and the wind blows it toward the Mechanic. He reads an article about the disappearance of ninja and goes to Ultra Violet, with whom he plans an escape. She then wrinkles the newspaper and throws it back to the Mechanic.

Papergirl[]

The paperboys continued delivering newspapers throughout Ninjago City during Kalmaar's attack on the city. Later, after nearly losing the Storm Amulet to Nelson and Antonia, Kalmaar briefly picked up a newspaper in disbelief as he wondered what species the kids were.

The Calm Before the Storm[]

During the evacuation, Okino was reading a newspaper until the wind blew it away.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In Season 1, the newspaper is called Ninja Times in English. In Season 8, there is a nonsensical inscription RTYUO in Ninjargon on the newspaper. In Season 11, the name of the newspaper is shown in Ninjargon as NGC News, or Ninjago City News.
    • In Full Throttle!, it is called Ninjago Times and it is colored.
      • It is referred to as Ninjago Times in "The Curse of the Golden Master," but it is not colored.
