Xentrix Studios is an animation studio in Asia which was WILFilm ApS's animation vendor for Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu from seasons 8 through 10. They replaced GDC after production problems in Season 7 caused the original vendor to be dismissed.[1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Because "most characters had a bit of a make-over" for Season 8,[2] the modified version of Obi-Wan Kenobi's hair was retired for these seasons. This left Cyrus Borg and the recurring background character who uses this hair with the more set-accurate version as opposed to the modified version they previously had, while Noble switched to a similar but different hair piece.
