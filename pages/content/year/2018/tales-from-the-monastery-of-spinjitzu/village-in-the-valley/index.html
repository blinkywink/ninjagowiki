Overview

“First, I was drifting on the winds towards a village: a small ring of houses surrounded by rice fields. It was set deep in a valley with snowy peaks in the distance, but I didn’t recognize where it was.”
— Skylor, Amber Spiral

The village in the valley is a small village located under Haunted Hill.[1]

History

History[]

After the Final Battle[]

At some point, the village was attacked by a group of Venomari soldiers. Lloyd noticed this while riding the Ultra Dragon and the two of them defeated the Venomari, saving the village.[2]

Big Trouble, Little Ninjago[]

The Sons of Garmadon and a revived Lord Garmadon return to the village and Harumi demanded everyone to kneel at the new emperor. The villagers refused, so Garmadon created a giant Colossus and the shocked villagers kneel at Garmadon.

Samurai X-Treme (daydream)[]

Looking at the Ninjago history mural, Nya imagined herself arriving in the village when it was attacked by Skulkin. She defeated the Skulkin and saved the village, and the villagers celebrated until Samurai X left.
