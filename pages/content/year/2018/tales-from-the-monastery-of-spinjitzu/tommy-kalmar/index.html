Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He has confirmed the canonicity of multiple Ninjago books,[2] as well as the "Mother Earth" comic from the Ninjago Magazine.[3]
  • In the early stage of the Dark Island Trilogy, Tommy Kalmar, Tommy Andreasen, and Kenneth Bonde spent a day at Andreasen's house brainstorming the ideas for the trilogy.[4][5]
  • He also gave input on the LEGO NINJAGO Explained video.[6]
  • Kalmaar is named after him.[7]
    • His last name and Kalmaar's name are also homophones.
