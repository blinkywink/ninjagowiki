Overview

The following is about significant events for Ninjago that occurred in 2018.

January

January[]

  • 1 - The Sons of Garmadon sets officially released around the world. "The Elemental Masters" was released.
  • 8 - "Beasts and Dragons" was released.
  • 12 - The 4D short film, Master of the 4th Dimension, was released in LEGOLAND.
  • 15 - "Rise of Garmadon" was released.
  • 20 - "The Mask of Deception" aired in Australia.
  • 22 - "Prophecy of the Green Ninja" was released.
  • 27 - "The Jade Princess" aired in Australia.
  • 29 - "Greatest Battles" was released.
February

February[]

  • 3 - "The Oni and the Dragon" aired in Australia.
  • 10 - "Snake Jaguar" aired in Australia.
  • 17 - "Dead Man's Squall" aired in Australia.
  • 24 - "The Quiet One" aired in Australia.
March

March[]

  • 3 - "Game of Masks" aired in Australia.
  • 10 - "Dread on Arrival" aired in Australia.
  • 17 - "True Potential" aired in Australia.
  • 24 - "Big Trouble, Little Ninjago" aired in Australia.

August

August[]

  • 1 - Hunted sets released in the US.
  • 4 - "Two Lies, One Truth" aired in Australia and New Zealand.

  • 25 - The last three episodes of Hunted aired in the US, Australia, and New Zealand.
October

October[]

  • 1 - Gift Set Box (2018) was released
  • 10 - Scott Godon-Decoteau passed away.
December

December[]

  • 16 - The Season 10 teaser was released.
  • 19 - The Tales from the Monastery of Spinjitzu mini-movies were released.
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This year marked the five-year anniversaries of Warriors of Stone, Stone Cold, and Destiny of Doom. For the exact dates, see 2013.
  • This is the only year since 2010 in which The Fold has not released new songs for Ninjago.
  • This year produced all canon Ninjago TV installments within the WILFilm ApS era in some format. Decoded contained clips of everything from the pilot episodes up to The Hands of Time. Sons of Garmadon, Hunted and Tales from the Monastery of Spinjitzu were released through the year, and the teaser trailer for March of the Oni was released in December of 2018.
