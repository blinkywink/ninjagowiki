10 Years of LEGO NINJAGO!

Information

Start Date

January 14, 2021

End Date

February 16, 2021

Overview

10 Years of LEGO NINJAGO! was a Ninjago-themed contest held on LEGO Ideas meant to celebrate Ninjago's ten-year anniversary. It took place from January 14, 2021 to February 16, 2021.[1]

Official description

Official description[]

If a NINJAGO® Museum were to open, what would you put in it? Using the past 10 years of action-packed NINJAGO history, build a relic, painting or something else that you’d display in this museum.[1]

Trivia[]
