Christopher Aaron Wyatt (born October 5, 1975), better known as Chris "Doc" Wyatt, is an American writer and producer who worked on Ninjago from the eleventh season to the fifteenth season. He and Kevin Burke are the lead writers of Ninjago: Dragons Rising.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • His Bluesky page, and formerly his X page, were popular sources of information for the canon and behind-the-scenes details of the shows he has worked on.
    • On his Bluesky profile, he revealed some names of previously unnamed characters. These mostly include Grouce the Geckle, Flameheart the Lava Dragon, Eridany the Mountain Dragon, Roake Fengxian, and the Elemental Masters from the Tournament of the Sources, namely Gorge, Mord, Algu, Arccrack, Teelk, Ellie, Treg, and Henjamin.
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt is in duo with Kevin Burke in almost all his animated projects.
  • He can speak English, Italian, and some French.[2]
  • The "Doc" part of his name was his USC college nickname. He decided to use it for his credits after producing Napoleon Dynamite when he discovered he was the 7th Chris Wyatt on IMDb.[2]
  • He has commented on the existence of this very page, noting that while the then-profile photo of him[3] was almost 20 years old, his status as "Alive" is accurate.[4]
  • He is also known for producing the films Napoleon Dynamite, Think Tank, Broken Hill and Café and some of his other projects are Marvel's Spider-Man, Stretch Armstrong, Transformers: BotBots, Rescue Bots Academy, Avengers Assemble, Star Wars Resistance and Stranger Things: Tales From ’85.
  • As of November 24, 2023, his favorite Ninjago: Dragons Rising episode to write was "Fugitives From Madness."[5]
  • His favorite season of Ninjago: Dragons Rising is Season 3 and his favorite episode is 'The Vault of Focus'.[6][7]
    • However, he states that each new season becomes his favorite when he and the team are working on it.[8]
  • He shares his birthday with Lloyd’s current voice actor Sam Vincent, although he is 4 years younger than him.
