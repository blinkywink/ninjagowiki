Overview

The following is a list of significant events for the Ninjago franchise that occurred in 2021.

This year marked the ten year anniversary of the franchise.

January

January[]

  • 1 - Ninjago: Legacy Wave 4 sets were released.
  • 14 - Ten year anniversary of the pilot episodes and the series as a whole. 71741 Ninjago City Gardens was released for LEGO VIP members.
  • 22 - Jake Pennar, Jake's namesake, passed away at the age of nine.
  • 29 - The Ninjago: Reimagined trailer was released.
February

February[]

  • 1 - 71741 Ninjago City Gardens was released to the public.
  • 5 - The Island teaser trailer was released.
  • 12 - The Island trailer was released.
March

March[]

  • 1 - The Island wave of sets and 71740 Jay's Electro-Mech were released in the US.
  • 4 - Character Encyclopedia New Edition was released.
  • 7 - The Island began in Canada with episodes "Uncharted" and "The Keepers of the Amulet".
  • 12 - The Island concluded in Taiwan, with all four episodes airing at once as a special.
  • 14 - The Island concluded in Canada with episodes "The Gift of Jay" and "The Tooth of Wojira".
  • 26 - The Uncharted Island was released in Germany.
April

April[]

  • 4 - Season 14: Seabound began in Canada with episodes "A Big Splash" and "The Call of the Deep".
  • 11 - "Unsinkable" and "Five Thousand Fathoms Down" aired in Canada.
  • 18 - "The Wrath of Kalmaar" and "Long Live the King" aired in Canada.
  • 23 - Ninjago: Reimagined airs the first episode, "Golden Legend".
  • 25 - "Escape from Merlopia" and "The Tale of Benthomaar" aired in Canada.
  • 26 - "Escape from Merlopia" and "The Tale of Benthomaar" aired in the UK.
  • 27 - "The Storm Amulet" and "Riddle of the Sphinx" aired in the UK.
  • 28 - "Papergirl" and "Master of the Sea" aired in the UK.
  • 29 - "The Calm Before the Storm" and "Assault on Ninjago City" aired in the UK.
  • 30 - "Gold Rush" was released. Seabound concluded in the UK with episodes "Nyad" and "The Turn of the Tide".
May

May[]

  • 2 - "The Storm Amulet" and "Riddle of the Sphinx" aired in Canada.
  • 7 - "A Day in the Life of a Golden Ninja" was released.
  • 9 - "Papergirl" and "Master of the Sea" aired in Canada.
  • 14 - "Sweatin' to the Goldies" was released.
  • 16 - "The Calm Before the Storm" and "Assault on Ninjago City" aired in Canada.
  • 21 - "Golden Hour" was released on YouTube.
  • 23 - Seabound concluded in Canada with episodes "Nyad" and "The Turn of the Tide".
June

June[]

  • 1 - Ninjago: Legacy Wave 5 and Seabound sets were released in most countries.
  • 26 - The Fold released a new song commercially called "Ten for Ninjago". Ninjago: 10 Year Anniversary Soundtrack is released (though it would not come to YouTube until July 29).
July

July[]

  • 30 - The first chapter of The Splinter in the Blind Man's Eye was released.
August

August[]

  • 1 - Ninjago: Legacy Wave 5 and Seabound sets were released in the United States and Canada.
  • 7 - The second chapter of The Splinter in the Blind Man's Eye was released.
  • 14 - The third chapter of The Splinter in the Blind Man's Eye was released.
  • 21 - The fourth chapter of The Splinter in the Blind Man's Eye was released.
  • 28 - The fifth chapter of The Splinter in the Blind Man's Eye was released.
September

September[]

  • 3 - The sixth chapter of The Splinter in the Bland Man's Eye was released.
  • 7 - The Book of Elemental Powers and Frenemies were released.
  • 10 - The seventh chapter of The Splinter in the Bland Man's Eye was released.
  • 17 - The eighth chapter of The Splinter in the Blind Man's Eye was released.
  • 21 - Spinjitzu Brothers was released.
  • 24 - The ninth chapter of The Splinter in the Blind Man's Eye was released.
  • 28 - An official Ninjago themed Unity microgame add-on pack was released on the Unity Asset Store.
  • 29 - The Seabound teaser was released.
  • 30 - The second Seabound teaser was released.
October

October[]

  • 2 - The tenth chapter and end of Phase 1: The Ascent of The Splinter in the Blind Man's Eye was released and a part of a script for Season 15 was released.
  • 4 - The official Seabound trailer was released.
November

November[]

  • 19 - The Island and Seabound released in the United States. Ninjago surpassed Ed, Edd n Eddy as the longest running show to have ever aired on Cartoon Network.
10th Anniversary

10th Anniversary[]

January 14, 2021 marked 10 years since the release of the pilot episodes. On and around this occasion, a number of promotional materials were released to celebrate the anniversary.

  • Ninjago: Legacy sets included golden minifigures of the ninja and Wu with special pedestals.
  • 71741 Ninjago City Gardens was released.
  • A four-part series featuring the soundtrack of the show, called NINJAGO: A Musical Journey, began to be released on the LEGO YouTube channel.[1]
  • An official interview was made with Tommy Andreasen,[2] a video interview was made with Tommy Andreasen and Tommy Kalmar,[3] and three other unofficial interviews were made via the LEGO Ambassador Network with Tommy Andreasen, Michael Knap, and Simon Lucas in one case.[4][5][6] An official interview with fan artist Joshua Deck was also made.[7]
  • The series of 5 shorts called Ninjago: Reimagined was released.
  • A building contest was held.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This year marks the five-year anniversaries of Skybound, the Dark Island Trilogy, and "Day of the Departed," as well as the ten-year anniversaries of the pilot episodes, the mini-movies and the first two episodes of Rise of the Snakes. For the exact dates, see 2016 and 2011.
  • This is the third year to feature a year-long hiatus after a season aired, following 2013 and 2017.
