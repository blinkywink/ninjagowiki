This topic is considered non-canon because the characters directly address the viewer.

Ninja Vlogs is a mini-series made by LEGO. It follows Jay and Nya aboard the Destiny's Bounty, starring in their Ninja Vlogs. The teaser for the first episode was released on May 28, 2021. This series takes place during their adventure on the Island of the Keepers. Fans will have the opportunity to star in their future episodes by participating in activities on the LEGO Life app.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

We’re Jay and Nya - ninjas from the LEGO NINJAGO TV series! Join us as we record our first ever Vlog together, aboard the Destiny’s Bounty! Learn about our latest (super secret) mission from NINJAGO Island, and find out how you, (yes, YOU) can star in our future Vlog episodes.[1]

Plot

Plot[]

Mainly follows Jay and Nya’s adventures around the Island of the Keepers, however the contents of said story appear to be non-canon.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Jay's Island gi is erroneously colored, as the dark blue parts of his suit are now a light blue similar to Nya's suit color.
  • This is the first time LEGO uses motion capture and real-time rendering for their content.
  • Unlike the show, these vlogs are animated to resemble real LEGO bricks. This is visible on vehicles and characters, although not in the background. There is also a lower level of detail.
