Overview

“They caught him feeding pigeons in a park.”
— Lloyd, "Uncharted"

City Square[1] is a park located in Ninjago City. Zane's funeral was held here, and the titanium statue of Zane was unveiled and is currently displayed there. The Temple of Fortitude is also located here.

History

History[]

The Titanium Ninja[]

After the defeat of the Golden Master, a funeral was held for the loss of Zane, who had sacrificed himself in order to save Ninjago City. The attendants then mourned and reflected on the life of Zane as snow began to fall.

The Corridor of Elders[]

Zane spoke to a group of citizens in the park in order to inspire them to fight as one against the Anacondrai Cultists.

Day of the Departed[]

Dareth and The Royal Blacksmiths hosted a Day of the Departed celebration, where they sang songs such as "Be My Day of the Departed Baby" and "Revenge Will Be Mine."

Uncharted[]

A news article showed Fugi-Dove feeding pigeons in a park, with Fugi-Dove having been caught by Ronin after a seemingly long time.

Christofern (flashback)[]

Following the Oni's invasion, Garmadon briefly wandered around the park while contemplating what it meant to be 'good.' While he was there, Garmadon watched people feed birds, trim hedges, and play a game of tug-of-war. Believing that they were struggling to complete their tasks, Garmadon attempted to help them, producing disastrous results. For example, rather than trim a hedge twig by twig, he instead burned the entire bush to ash with a blast of Destruction.

Later on, with the guidance of Vinny Folson, Garmadon returned to the park, still seeking to do good. This time he 'helped' a child on the swings by pushing the child so hard that he rapidly swung around the swing-set until he was brought to a standstill by his mother.

Other appearances

Other appearances[]

Scavengers[]

Wu had a nightmare about visiting the park with Misako, where Vermillion warriors attacked them.
