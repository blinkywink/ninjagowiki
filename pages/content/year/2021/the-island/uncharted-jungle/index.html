The uncharted jungle[1] is a jungle at the Island of the Keepers.

History

History[]

The Island[]

Uncharted[]

Misako, Wu, and Clutch Powers with his interns explore the uncharted jungle at the Island of the Keepers until they encounter the Stone Guardians, which attacked them.

The Keepers of the Amulet[]

The ninja journey to the jungle, where they meet Zippy and were later attacked by the Stone Guardians. The rest of the ninja split up from Lloyd and Twitchy Tim while they were running away from the Guardians.

Soon, they were caught by the Keepers and were sentenced to be on their island forever.

The Gift of Jay[]

As Jay and the ninja are separated, they find Misako and Wu, along with Clutch Powers and his interns. Meanwhile, Jay was being comforted by PoulErik while Lloyd got prepared to find his friends, and tries to convince Twitchy Tim to come with him, but he turned it down.

With Zippy's help, Lloyd was able to free the other ninja, only to be caught by the Keepers because of Clutch's determination to get the Storm Amulet.

The Tooth of Wojira[]

After defeating Ronin, he and his thugs were imprisoned. Mammatus thanks the ninja for their heroic deeds and was about to finish his sentence until they realize that the Storm Amulet was missing. They later found Clutch trying to steal it.

The Virtues of Spinjitzu[]

Courage[]

The ninja venture through the uncharted jungle in order to find Wu and the Legendary Dragon.

Seabound[]

The Storm Amulet[]

The ninja tell Mammatus of Kalmaar and the Maaray Guards, and they debate on whether if the Storm Amulet should stay on the island or not. The Keepers later agree to hand the Storm Amulet to Nya, only to find out that the amulet they have is fake.

Riddle of the Sphinx[]

Jay, Zane, Lloyd, Maya, and Benthomaar were near the uncharted jungle while they were watching Nya trying to summon whales so they can return to Ninjago.
