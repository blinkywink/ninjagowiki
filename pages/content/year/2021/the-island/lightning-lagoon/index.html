Overview

“So be it. Take the intruders to the Lightning Lagoon!”
— PoulErik, Island Challenge!

The Lightning Lagoon[1] is an inland sea located inside the Storm belt and near the Island of the Keepers. The lagoon is hit by a series of never-ending thunderstorms hence its name.

History

History[]

Uncharted[]

As the Ninja sailed closer to the Island of the Keepers aboard their catamarans, they sailed through Lightning Lagoon to reach the island's shores.

The Keepers of the Amulet[]

When Cole asked if anyone brought food, Jay replied that he did, but lost it in the Lightning Lagoon when he and the others were crossing to the Island of the Keepers.

Later, the Keepers took the ninja across the Lightning Lagoon to take them to Mammatus.

Island Challenge![]

Brought to the Lightning Lagoon, the ninja were ordered to participate in the Keepers' ritualistic sailing contest. As they set sail in a race against several Rumble Keepers, Nya realized that the Keepers were able to boost the speed of their catamarans by harnessing the lagoon's namesake lightning with their spears. When Nya tried to follow suit, she found herself nearly fried instead. Passing along the spear to Cole, the two were able to boost themselves enough to reach the end of the race, but finished in dead last. Fortunately, the ritual was not simply about finishing first, but rather to prove that one could handle the lagoon's lightning.

The Gift of Jay[]

Jay was placed atop a raft by the Keepers and cast out to sea from Lightning Lagoon to serve as an offering to "Wojira."

The Tooth of Wojira[]

As Jay continued to drift out towards sea, Zane used his powers over Ice to create an ice bridge out to Lightning Lagoon. When they ninja failed to rescue Jay, however, they returned to the lagoon's shoreline.

The Storm Amulet[]

The ninja returned to the Island of the Keepers, passing through the Lightning Lagoon onboard the Hydro Bounty as they sought to secure the Storm Amulet.

Riddle of the Sphinx[]

Nya waded out into the waters of Lightning Lagoon in an attempt to summon whales to tow the damaged Hydro Bounty.
