The Tooth of Wojira is the fourth and final episode of The Island and the 164th episode overall of Ninjago. It aired on March 14, 2021, paired up with "The Gift of Jay." It released in the US on November 19, 2021, alongside the rest of the episodes of The Island and Seabound.

Synopsis

Synopsis[]

As Jay is sacrificed to the sea serpent that terrorizes the island, the other ninja begin to suspect events on the island are not all as they seem.[2]

Extended: Breaking free of their bonds, the ninja attempt to rescue Jay but are too late as he's taken by the sea serpent. When Lloyd makes an interesting discovery, they realize that the Keepers may have been manipulated. Jay finds himself in a cavern full of hoarded treasure where the real villain is revealed. Heroically, the ninja, and Twitchy Tim, come to Jay's rescue.[3]

Plot

Plot[]

The ninja are stuck on poles and watch as Jay was being sacrificed to "Wojira." Kai eventually breaks out and frees the others while Dwayne freed Clutch Powers. The ninja fought against the Keepers and try to save Jay. But they were too late as the monster defeat the ninja and took Jay. However, Lloyd picks up a wooden tooth from the serpent and soon realized that the creature is fake.

After the ninja went back to the island, Mammatus told them that the Keepers will sacrifice them to Wojira if she returns, but Lloyd told him that Wojira's fake by revealing the wooden tooth to him. When Lloyd asked Mammatus why the Keepers are offering sacrifices to Wojira, Mammatus explained that the First Spinjitzu Master entrusted his tribe to protect the Storm Amulet from Wojira, as Wojira would destroy Ninjago's world with the Endless Sea if she regains possession of the Storm Amulet. One day, PoulErik spotted "Wojira." When a stranger suggested offering gold to resume Wojira's slumber, Mammatus accepted the suggestion by doing so. However, the creature continued to come back, and the Keepers offered everything they had and planned to sacrifice a human to appease Wojira forever.

Upset by this explanation, the ninja chided Mammatus for sacrificing their friend, protesting that by sacrificing him, it had costed the Keepers nothing but everything for Jay. Understanding their mistake, the Keepers agreed to provide the ninja with a map of the coastline so that they could search for the fake "Wojira." Elsewhere, a pair of henchmen grumbled amongst themselves as they steered "Wojira" into a hidden cove. Aware that they had received a person instead of gold, they noted that their leader was likely to be displeased. When they went back to check on their prisoner, the henchmen discovered that Jay was gone. Not long afterwards, the pair were attacked by Jay but he was soon overwhelmed by the sheer number of criminals present in the cove. Fortunately, the rest of the ninja arrived just in time and descended into the cove to help their friend fight back against the criminals.

As the ninja fought against the henchmen and the inmates, Ronin reveals himself to be the criminal gang's leader, although Lloyd thought it was the Mechanic at first. Ronin jumped onto his boat and began to escape with all the Keepers' gold. However, Twitchy Tim sails in one of the battle catamarans and crashes into Ronin's monster, and Ronin and his criminal gang are imprisoned by the Keepers. Mammatus apologized for what the Keepers did, and the ninja accept, and they all soon realize the Storm Amulet was gone. Nya saw Clutch Powers leaving with it, and stopped him by electrocuting him. The next day, Lloyd questions if Wojira is real. Wu says that if she is, then they should hope that she sleeps for another thousand years. Unbeknownst to them, the real Wojira is in her deep sleep at a temple, having possession with one of her amulets.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Narrator: Jay
  • It's unknown why Nya didn't simply control the Water around Jay to bring him closer to them.
  • The English version of this episode (along with the previous) actually premiered in Taiwan two days before the Canadian premiere. This is because Taiwan aired all four episodes at once, while Canada split the episodes into two parts.

Cultural references[]

  • After the ninja and Twitchy Tim defeat him, Ronin says, "And I would have gotten away with it, if it weren't for you ninja and your pesky friend," which refers to a well-known repeated phrase "And I would have gotten away with it, if it weren't for you meddling kids" from the Scooby-Doo franchise.
    • This was a reference to The Island's overall inspiration from the classic Scooby-Doo story structure.[4]
  • At the end of the finale featuring the shot of Wojira, it is similar to the shot of the Evil Entity in "Wrath of the Krampus" from Scooby-Doo! Mystery Incorporated.

Continuity[]

  • Lloyd briefly mentions the Mechanic. He also revealed that the Mechanic is so far the only inmate from Kryptarium not to get recaptured.
  • This is Ronin's first physical appearance since "Endings" and his first speaking role since "Green Destiny."
    • By not counting the non-canon video game Shadow of Ronin, this also marks the second time he is an enemy of the ninja since the first half of Skybound, but as those events were undone, this would be the first.
  • Wojira makes her first onscreen appearance.
  • Lloyd forgets to acknowledge that the ninja saved Clutch Powers, continuing his trend from "The Keepers of the Amulet" of forgetting to mention Clutch and showing little concern when reminded.
  • During "Wojira's" journey to the cave on the uncharted island, a song previously used as the theme for the Destiny's Bounty in the flashback from "Pirates vs. Ninja," was played in the background. During the battle of Kryptarium inmates with the ninja, the song of the pirates presented in the same episode was also used.
  • Although not explained in the episode, it is possible that Clutch Powers was arrested by the Keepers for his repeated amulet robbery.[5]
    • However, "Riddle of the Sphinx" shows that Clutch fled back to Ninjago with the Storm Amulet.

Errors[]

  • When Lloyd uses one of his Energy blasts, he was seen without his face paint. However, in the next shot, he was seen with it again.
    • Also, when he and the others rescue Jay he doesn't have the paint, but when Ronin appears he does. But for the remainder of the battle, it's not shown. It is possible that the paint was washed off by water or smudged off in the battle.
  • Throughout the episode, Zane's voice lacks the robotic filter, sounding very similar to his voice prior to Tournament of Elements, and in episodes of Sons of Garmadon and March of the Oni where he uses his "human" cloak.
  • When the Kryptarium prisoners appear in front of Jay, the one wielding a bat has its head slightly enlarged than it should, but returns back to its normal height in the next shot.
  • During the fight against Ronin and his goons, there are some scenes where the ninja's masks are on and off.
    • The tassel on Lloyd's sword is also stiff in some scenes as well.
  • When Nya asks Dwayne where Clutch is, her shoulder pad is missing.
  • When Wojira was revealed to be real, the sanctum inside her temple was filled with water. Although in Seabound, it isn't.
    • A possible explanation for this could be that Kalmaar had the temple drained, since their experiments seemed to utilize some form of electricity. These experiments could be hampered by having Wojira submerged in water.
