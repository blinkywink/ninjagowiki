(In the ocean, Jay is struggling to break free of the ropes.)

Jay: Aah! Guys, I see something. I see something!

Nya: Can you describe it?

Zane: Can you define "something"?

Cole: Does it have teeth?

Kai: Ugh, these ropes are tough. I think I can burn through them!

Nya: Shh! They'll hear!

Mammatus: Let's hope this final sacrifice appeases Wojira, once and for all. (He turns around to find that the ninja have freed themselves.) Stop them!

Ninja: Ninja, go!

Clutch: Hurry up, Dwayne!

Dwayne: I'm trying, Mr. Powers! (He catches a Keeper spear flying his way and cuts the ropes.)

Clutch: Faster next time, intern!

(The ninja fight their way through the Keepers and reach the end of the docks.)

Lloyd: Zane, a little ice? (Zane freezes the water and they continue running out to sea.)

Jay: Somebody, help! There's definitely something out here! Something big and snaky and glowing!

Nya: Jay!

Jay: Oh, thank goodness! It's so good to see you guys!

(A fireball lands between Jay and the ninja, separating them.)

Nya: Over there! I see something! (She attacks it with water, but it does nothing.)

Kai: What is it?

Jay: I don't know, but it's big! Get me out of here before it comes back! (Zane creates an iceberg for Lloyd to reach Jay.) Hurry!

Lloyd: Just stay still!

Jay: Look out! (Another fireball strikes and Lloyd falls into the water.)

Lloyd: Jay?

Jay: I'm okay. I think I can get loose, just need a few more… aah! (A giant claw reels him in.)

Lloyd: Jay! (He sends an energy ball at Wojira.)

Nya: No!

Jay: Aah! It's got me! Aah!

Cole: Jay!

Kai: Say something! Where are you? It ate Jay! It really ate him!

Lloyd: (He finds a wooden tooth.) Or… did it?

(The ninja reach the shore and are surrounded by Keepers.)

Mammatus: You fools! You risk angering Wojira? If the beast returns, you will be the next sacrifices!

Lloyd: No, we won't, because Wojira doesn't exist.

Mammatus: Blasphemy! You saw her!

Lloyd: We saw something, but tell me: do sea monsters have wooden teeth? (He slides the wooden tooth over to Mammatus.)

Mammatus: I don't understand.

Lloyd: It's a con. You've been tricked into giving sacrifices to a fake monster.

Mammatus: But that's impossible.

Lloyd: Why don't you tell us how all of this started? From the beginning.

Mammatus: For thousands of seasons, our people have been entrusted with the protection of the amulet. Entrusted by the First Spinjitzu Master himself to keep it from Wojira, for if the serpent ever obtained it again, the seas would rise up and swallow all the world. But Wojira slept, and all was peaceful. Until one day ...

Past PoulErik: Come quickly! (He takes Mammatus to the docks.) It's her. Isn’t it? It's Wojira! The sleeper has awoken! What should we do?

Past Mammatus: This doesn't seem right. Wojira should be asleep at the bottom of the sea.

Past PoulErik: Someone must have awoken her!

Unknown figure: We should send her a sacrifice of gold and gems to appease her!

Mammatus: I did not know where the idea came from, but it was worth a try. (They send out a raft full of treasures.) We thought we had appeased her, but only a week later, she was back. Soon our treasures were gone, so we gave fruits and coconuts, whatever was left. But we needed something to placate Wojira forever. We needed an ultimate sacrifice!

Lloyd: So you thought you'd sacrifice our friend?

Mammatus: We were commanded to defend the amulet at all costs! Not some costs, all!

Kai: Sacrificing Jay cost you nothing, but it could cost Jay everything.

Nya: You lost our friend. You're gonna help get him back.

Mammatus: How?

Lloyd: First, you're gonna draw me a map of the coastline, and show me any place near the water where someone could hide something really big.

(Out at sea, two henchmen drive "Wojira" into a cave.)

Henchman #1: Boss ain't gonna be happy with this. They were supposed to give us gems and rubies and gold, not a person.

Henchman #2: Maybe the islanders are out of jewels. Maybe they got nothing left except people.

Henchman #1: Well, I ain't gonna be the one to tell him the news. You find a way to tell him.

Henchman #2: Me? Why me? (They lower the raft, but Jay is missing.) Hey, where'd he go?

Jay: (He lands from above and takes out the two henchmen.) I happen to think I'm quite valuable, thank you very much. (Three criminals land in front of him.) Those are prison uniforms! Who are you guys? And how are there so many of you? (He fights back but is overwhelmed.)

Criminal #1: Throw him out to sea! He's too much trouble!

(Two henchmen lift Jay and carry him to the water, when the ninja swing into the cave on ropes.)

Lloyd: Yes! But he's our trouble!

Jay: (Cole kicks the two henchmen into the water and catches Jay.) Aww, good to see you, guys! How'd you find me?

Lloyd: Once we figured out this alleged sea monster wasn't real -

Cole: We had a little talk with the leader, who told us about a cave -

Nya: Where some thugs just might be hiding a big monster!

Jay: Smart thinking, guys! But this is a pretty bizarre crime ring going on here. Any idea who's behind it?

Lloyd: We figured that out too. Remember there was a breakout to Kryptarium Prison? All kinds of criminals back on the streets?

Jay: Yeah!

Lloyd: Well, guess who still hasn't been caught, and who likes to use weird vehicles to get his way!

(The unknown figure reveals himself to be Ronin.)

Ninja: Ronin!

Lloyd: I was actually gonna say the Mechanic, but Ronin fits too.

Kai: I thought you always worked alone, Ronin.

Ronin: I usually do, but when the police hired me to track down all those escaped prisoners from Kryptarium, I realized I had an opportunity to put them to work, for me!

Cole: Too bad you went through all that trouble for nothing.

Ronin: Not for nothing! These islanders had been sitting on a small fortune of gems and gold bullion! But if they're starting to sacrifice people, I may have cleaned them out, so I'll cut my losses while I'm ahead. So long, ninja. Keep them busy, boys!

Nya: He's escaping!

Twitchy Tim: (He arrives on a catamaran.) Not on my watch! Come on, storm, give me all you've got!

Lloyd: Twitchy?

Twitchy Tim: It's never too late to conquer your fears, especially to rescue a friend! Hold on guys!

Ronin: Whoa, hey! What are you doing? You're gonna sink us both!

Twitchy Tim: That's the plan! (He leaps off the catamaran and into the water just as it rams into "Wojira", sinking both vehicles.)

Lloyd: Someone help Twitchy!

Nya: That was a very brave thing to do, Twitchy.

Ronin: (He tries to escape, but Kai trips him.) Okay, okay. You got me.

Kai: You're gonna see the inside of a jail cell for this, Ronin.

(Back at the village, Ronin and his henchmen are locked in a cell.)

Ronin: And I would have gotten away with it if it weren't for you ninja and your pesky friend.

Cole: Why did I have a feeling he was gonna say that? (Zippy appears and growls at them, scaring the prisoners.)

Mammatus: Let me offer my sincerest apologies, my ninja allies.

Cole: For what?

Mammatus: Having our Stone Guardian attack you, locking you up, sacrificing your friend to a monster, that sort of stuff.

Kai: Nah, forget it.

Cole: Yeah, happens to us all the time.

Lloyd: I'm just happy they captured Ronin and his thugs, and found my mom and Master Wu alive and well.

Zane: And Clutch Powers.

Lloyd: Oh yeah.

Mammatus: On behalf of the Keepers of the Storm Amulet of Wojira - (He realizes the amulet is missing.) What? Where - ?

Nya: (To Dwayne) Where's Clutch? (Dwayne nervously points towards the jungle.) Ugh.

(At the docks, Nya zaps Clutch with a spear.)

Nya: I don't think that belongs to you.

Clutch: Oh, how the heck did that get in there?

(Later, Lloyd, Misako, and Wu are at the docks.)

Misako: Lloyd? Are you okay?

Lloyd: I was just thinking, I know Ronin's monster was fake, but I guess I was wondering if Wojira is really still out there, somewhere.

Wu: If she is, may she sleep for another thousand years.

(The scene shifts under the docks and into the Endless Sea, where the real Wojira is revealed sleeping in front of a temple with another amulet on her head.)

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
