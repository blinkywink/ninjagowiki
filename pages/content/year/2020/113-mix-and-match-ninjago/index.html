113 Mix and Match Ninjago was a promotion made in collaboration between LEGO and The Laughing Cow. It consisted of various giveaways, and a line of Ninjago-themed cards. The cards released on September 1, 2020 in Vietnam, bundled with various The Laughing Cow products. In total, there were 113 cards to collect.

Giveaways

Giveaways[]

On top of having Ninjago-themed cards included with The Laughing Cow products, there were also a variety of giveaways held as part of the promotion.[7] There were ten cash prizes each of 100 million dong (VND), as well as 3,000 Ninjago sets to be won.[1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Although the name of Card 44 - Army Scout and its description reference the Stone Scouts, the card has a picture of Scott.
  • Card 66 - Ashmonger uses the original name for the Skull Sorcerer, "Ashmonger,"[8] for the name of the card. Despite this, the character is still referred to as "Phù Thủy Đầu Lâu" ("Skull Sorcerer") in the card's description.
