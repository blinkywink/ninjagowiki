Overview

This article is about the location in Prime Empire. For the set, see 71708 Gamer's Market.
“Uh, she eats at a noodle stand in the marketplace after every race.”
— Scott to Lloyd, "Racer Seven"

The marketplace is a virtual market located in the Terra Technica region of Prime Empire, where players are able to buy items such as weapons and clothing.

History

History[]

Prime Empire Original Shorts[]

The Meaning of Victory[]

Some of the citizens of Terra Technica were near the Gamer's Market while they were watching the Speedway Five-Billion racers being interviewed.

The Stowaway[]

Jay passed by the Gamer's Market when running from the Red Visors.

Manhunt[]

Jay and Scott fight the Red Visors in the Gamer's Market.

Prime Empire[]

Level Thirteen[]

Lloyd, Cole, Kai, and Nya were near the Gamer's Market after they arrived at Prime Empire.

Superstar Rockin' Jay[]

The ninja explore the Gamer's Market and see users buying new apparel for their avatar as well as weapons and food. However, the Red Visors begin marching down the main street causing most of the business to close in fear of the Red Visors.

Racer Seven[]

Racer Seven is seen walking in the Gamer's Market after losing another race. Lloyd then comes by and tells her that the world she lives in isn't real and she is programmed to lose. Racer Seven threatens to call the Red Visors if he doesn't leave. Lloyd does leave but he tells Racer Seven to meet him in the empty parking lot if she changes her mind.

The Speedway Five-Billion[]

Okino, the avatars, and the other NPCs can be seen cheering after Racer Seven won the Speedway Five-Billion.

Stop, Drop and Side Scroll[]

Lloyd, Nya, and Jay pass through the Gamer's Market before heading into Terra Domina.

The Temple of Madness[]

A red shock wave can be heard and seen at the Gamer's Market.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Team up with Digi Jay, Avatar Cole, Avatar Nya, Avatar Pink Zane and their allies at the Gamer’s Market inside the video game world of Prime Empire. Here you can choose a new weapon, a new hat for a disguise or purchase a collectible Key-Tana. Watch out for the fearsome Red Visors and evil Richie at the Market who want to capture them. Can you win the battle and escape?[1]
