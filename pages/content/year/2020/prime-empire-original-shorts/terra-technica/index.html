Overview

“Guys, welcome to Terra Technica. The second Key-Tana is here, but the only way to win it is to be crowned champion in the Speedway Five-Billion.”
— Jay to the ninja, "One Step Forward, Two Steps Back"

Terra Technica is the second game zone out of three zones in Prime Empire.

History

History[]

Prime Empire Original Shorts[]

The Meaning of Victory[]

While the racers of the Speedway Five-Billion were being interviewed, Jay tells the Announcer about the truth about the race. However, before Jay could finish, he gets attack by the Red Visors and runs away.

The Stowaway[]

While Jay was hiding inside Scott's car, he hears Scott being confronted by the Red Visors. Scott then takes out the Red Visors before escaping in his car.

Scott is chased by the Red Visors and at some point, Jay pops out of the truck and onto Scott's front window. Scott allows him to ride as they dodge most of the Red Visors except one when they crash into him.

Scott presumedly defeated the last Red Visors before introducing himself to Jay, saying that he had been waiting for him for a long time.

Manhunt[]

Scott tells Jay that they much leave immediately so they disguise themselves. They reach a closed-off area guarded by two Red-Visors. To get past them, Scott and Jay use fake code names but are caught when the real Red 27 came for duty. Jay and Scott are chased by the Red Visors and are forced to fight. Despite being outnumbered, Jay single-handily defeats all of them which impresses Scott. They then hide in Scott's garage to avoid detection.

Gayle Gossip: A Closer Look[]

Terra Technica was featured in an old commercial of Prime Empire.

Prime Empire[]

Level Thirteen[]

Lloyd, Kai, Nya, and Cole make it inside of Prime Empire and find themselves in the center of Terra Technica.

Superstar Rockin' Jay[]

The Red Visors are marching towards the ninja and begin shooting at them. The ninja attempt to use their Elemental Powers, but cannot. Fortunately, the League of Jay rescues the ninja and they begin jumping onto buildings. Dee-Jay 81 then reveals the password to Scott's garage for the ninja to take refuge.

One Step Forward, Two Steps Back[]

Jay introduces the ninja to Terra Technica and they begin to sign up for the Speedway Five-Billion only to find out that the entry fee is too high. Before they decide what to do, the Red Visors surround them and begin attacking. The ninja are able to defeat them and they see a dance competition, so they decide to enter in order to get more credits.

Jay and Nya cash out their earnings but realize they need cars. Jay then tells the group that he knows someone who can lend them some vehicles. The Red Visors then arrive moments later and Unagami commands them to find Scott, who might have vehicles the ninja need.

The Speedway Five-Billion[]

The Red Visors begin the attack on Scott's garage. Scott distracts the Red Visors while the ninja are given a chance to escape. Unfortunately, Scott is cubed.

At the Speedway Five-Billion, Kai and Cole decide to sacrifice themselves by taking out the remaining Red Visors. Eventually, Racer Seven is able to win with the help of Lloyd, Nya, and Jay holding them off.

Stop, Drop and Side Scroll[]

The remaining ninja are sneaking around Terra Technica to avoid detection and find the Arcade Cabinet that will lead them to Terra Domina.

The Prodigal Father[]

While Scott is playing Prime Empire, Terra Technica can be seen.

The Virtues of Spinjitzu[]

Wisdom[]

The ninja were teleported to the Speedway Five-Billion and have to cross the finish line in order to get to the next portal. However, the Whack Rats blocked their way and respawned every time they were defeated. Lloyd realized they needed to be defeated in order to get passed and after losing his life, he respawned near the portal. The rest of the ninja copy Lloyd and were able to enter the next portal.
