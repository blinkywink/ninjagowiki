Daniel "Dan" Ife is a Canadian director working under WildBrain. He began working on Ninjago during its eleventh season and continued through its sequel series Dragons Rising.

For his work on "The Son of Lilly" and "The Sword Shatters" alongside Rich Johnson, Ife was nominated twice for the Leo Award for Best Direction in an Animated Series.[1]
