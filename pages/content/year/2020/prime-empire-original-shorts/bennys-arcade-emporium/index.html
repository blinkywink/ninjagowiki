Overview

“So come on down to Benny's Arcade Emporium, where fun is number one!”
— Benny, "Gayle Gossip: A Closer Look"

Benny's Arcade Emporium is an arcade in Ninjago City. When Prime Empire resurfaced, the arcade became more popular than it originally was. However, during Ninjago City's Videogame Prohibition,[1] all the arcade's video games were confiscated until after the events of the attack by Unagami. Not long after, the video games were returned, before the arcade got submerged during the Great Flood, rendering them useless. Within a year, all games were working again, only to be rendered useless again by the Overlord.

History

History[]

Prime Empire Original Shorts and Prime Empire[]

Let's Dance[]

After a dance lesson, Jay took Nya to the arcade, where he shows her his favorite game, Dancy Pants. When they were interrupted by Lloyd, who reported a disturbance, Nya tells Jay to disobey him so that they could play it.

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

While the kids inside the arcade were playing video games, a huge shockwave took out the power. When it came back on, all of the games were replaced by Prime Empire, which attracted everyone to play it.

Gayle Gossip: A Closer Look[]

While everyone was queuing to play Prime Empire, Gayle Gossip decides to do an exclusive report on the game's reality.

She interviews players outside the arcade with Nelson and Benny complementing the praising the game while Zane interrupted saying that the game is dangerous.

Gayle Gossip enters the arcade and talks about how everyone is having fun and if the game will reach its eventual end. Nelson is on the side playing Prime Empire until he disappears once he reached level thirteen while Gayle Gossip was ending her news report.

Dyer Island[]

Leroy, who was spending a lot of time with Prime Empire, finally reached level thirteen and entered the game.

Level Thirteen[]

When players have been going missing upon entering Prime Empire, the police start confiscating all the arcade cabinets. Benny tries to stop one, disbelieving the truth about them, as he makes a lot of money from them.

Prior to Seabound[]

When the Videogame Prohibition lifted, all the cabinets were returned to Benny's arcade, during which they all return to normal with the additionally fixed Prime Empire.

Seabound[]

The Calm Before the Storm[]

Jay arrives at Benny's, where he sees some kids playing Prime Empire, and boasts about it before Nya calls him over to the Dancy Pants game, where they start playing it, gaining everyone's attention, up until they cause the game to crash.

Assault on Ninjago City[]

Leroy and two other kids stop outside Benny's as the tour bus passes by. Later on, the interior is completely submerged, and the three kids attempt to escape, only to discover the door is jammed before being rescued by Lloyd in his APS suit.

Crystalized[]

Safe Haven[]

Kai, Cole and Skylor hide out at Benny's, where they meet Jake, who came to hide as well from the Crystal Warriors before receiving Wu's message, and head for the newspaper warehouse.
