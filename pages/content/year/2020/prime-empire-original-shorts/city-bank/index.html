The City Bank (also known as the Bank of Ninjago[1]) is a bank located in Ninjago City.

History

History[]

Darkness Shall Rise[]

In order to pay for rent, Cole took up a job at the bank. But one day while guarding the front entrance, Cole fell asleep which resulted in Acidicus, Fangtom and Skalidor breaking in the vault underground, and got away with a few sacks. The news of the robbery also distracted Kai and Zane from their jobs, resulting in both of them being fired. When they arrive at the bank, they see the Bank Boss reprimanding and firing Cole for his actions, and the latter later remarked that he disliked the job.

The Day Ninjago Stood Still[]

When the Stone Army was attacking the city, some broke into the bank, and the manager tried to hide in the vault, only to end up having the entire wall, sans vault door, completely destroyed.

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Infamous[]

When Nadakhan commits crimes to frame the ninja, he impersonates Lloyd and robs the bank.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

Questing for Quests[]

While searching for quests, the ninja hear the second city bank's alarms go off, and stop the robbery, only to later find out that they had inadvertently ruined a movie scene.

Upgrade[]

One evening, The Mechanic performed a test run of his new Noodle Truck of Crime by breaking into and robbing the bank's vault.

Other appearances

Other appearances[]

The Absolute Worst[]

While explaining why he thinks Jay is the worst, Fugi-Dove makes up a story, saying he had just robbed the bank, only to be ambushed by Lloyd, Kai and Cole on hang-gliders, which he easily takes down, until Jay, riding a Lightning bolt in his opinion, defeated him.
