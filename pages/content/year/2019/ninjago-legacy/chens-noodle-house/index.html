Overview

“I know noodles! I grew up around my dad's noodle houses, after all. Sure, they were all just fronts for his criminal enterprise, but you still pick up a thing or two.”
— Skylor, "Compatible"

Mr. Chen's Noodle House (also known as Mr. Chen's Noodle Empire) is a restaurant in Ninjago City. It is the number one restaurant chain in the city and popular among the citizens such as Cole and Dareth. During Chen's ownership of the Noodle House, it was supplied by goods produced in the factory on his personal island which are brought over by the ferry to the mainland.

Following Chen's defeat, Skylor took ownership of the noodle house, personally managing the restaurant in Ninjago City.

History

History[]

A Team Divided[]

The ninja went to eat at the noodle shop after unveiling Zane's statue at a ceremony. However, the meal was interrupted by Jay and Cole, who began to argue over Nya. The fight escalated, as they fought each other with hot noodles until the ninja get asked to leave the shop.

Tournament of Elements[]

The Invitation[]

Eyezor, Chope, and Krait attempted to rob the noodle shop but the ninja quickly thwarted their plans and fended them off. After the villains fled, the ninja found an invitation to the Tournament of Elements outside of the shop.

Ninja Roll[]

On the night of the fourth day of the Tournament, the noodle trucks from Chen's Island arrived with the latest shipment of goods. Dareth was visiting the Noodle Shop at the time, expressing outrage that Chen had discontinued the Puffy Potstickers.

Shadow of Ronin (non-canon)[]

While Nya defended the city from the Nindroids, she passed by Chen's Noodle House.

Possession[]

Curseworld, Part I[]

On the day of the Ninjago New Year, Ronin visited the Noodle Shop.

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Skybound[]

Operation Land Ho![]

While Skylor was throwing out a ruffian from the Noodle House, Jay visited the restaurant to recruit the Master of Amber for his Ninja replacement team.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

The Hands of Time[]

Secrets Discovered[]

Upon discovering that his father was working for the Hands of Time, Kai visited the Noodle Shop to seek advice from Skylor. They were soon joined by Nya, though the siblings soon left after learning the other ninja had discovered the secret base of the Time Twins.

Quest for the Lost Powers[]

After moving out of the monastery, Kai would eat there for dinner after some odd jobs, mainly to be around Skylor before the Rotten Rabbits arrive, intending to rob the place. Kai attempted to fight them, but was easily beaten before Skylor kicked them out herself.

Other appearances and mentions

Other appearances and mentions[]

Blue Lightning[]

Late in the evening, Jay orders food for himself over the phone, and asks if they have a delivery.

A Pain in the Mech![]

At the Crossroads noodle cook-off, Cole stirred up controversy while eating noodles from Chen's Noodle House, asserting their superiority. Sora was upset because Cole had brought in noodles from outside the Crossroads to eat in the midst of the cook-off. Later, Cole claimed that combining the Crossroads' noodles with those from Chen's Noodle House actually enhanced the overall taste.

Fugitives From Madness[]

In the monastery, Jay and Cole got into a fight causing the two to accidentally throw their video game controller to the wall, creating a crack on the wall resembling a bowl of noodles, causing the duo to become hungry and go to eat at Chen's Noodle House.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Master Chen’s is the most popular and successful restaurant chain on NINJAGO island. It’s famous for serving the best noodles anywhere. Just the wonderful smell of the delicacies served within is enough to lure people in off the street. What no one knew was that the smiling owner of the chain, Master Chen, was in reality a master criminal and his noodles were made by slave labor. When Skylor took over the family business, she kept the company name, but changed entirely the unethical and illegal way the business was run.[1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Chen's Hall of Villainy exhibit suggests that the recipe for the noodles "might be Serpentine in origin, or [Chen] was a foodie with a lot of time on his hands."[2] This is also mentioned in The Search for Zane, when Jay theorizes, "Maybe giant snake is the secret ingredient in Chen's noodles."
  • It is unknown where the Noodle House now gets their imports from since nobody goes to Chen's Island anymore.[citation needed]
  • The Puffy Potsticker is a very popular treat. It was canceled before the Tournament of Elements but was added again when Skylor took over. It was later used to interrogate Ghoultar in "Kingdom Come".
  • A delivery man from the Noodle House appears in Jay's "Meet the Ninja" video.
  • In The Nastiest of the Nasty!, it was suggested that after the events of Season 4, Skylor became the symbol of the noodle house. However, in Season 5, there is still Chen's statue on the sign of the establishment.
