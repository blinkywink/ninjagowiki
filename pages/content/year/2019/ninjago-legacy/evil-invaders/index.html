Amusements in the Park

Amusements in the Park[]

After awakening from their tomb, the Stone Army have made their way to the surface of Ninjago City where they now begin rampaging and destroying all in their way. In response, the Ninja have sped to the city onboard the Ultra Sonic Raider to combat this new foe.

As the Ninja fight, Misako alerts them to a news report of another group of Stone Warriors who were attacking civilians at an amusement park downtown. With the threat mostly neutralized in their current location, Lloyd orders the ninja, with Wu's blessing, to turn their sights next to the group at the amusement park.

Arriving at an amusement park in utter disarray, the ninja split up and quickly get to work battling the Stone Army; Lloyd confronts a group at the bumper cars, Kai in the hall of mirrors, Cole and Zane at the helter-skelter, Nya and Jay at the Ferris wheel, and Wu and Misako enjoying some cotton candy. In the end, the Ninja were able to defeat the Stone Warriors, but at the cost of completely destroying the amusement park and driving out all of the park-goers. The park's manager then approached the ninja, who braced themselves for a potential earful. Instead, the manager graciously thanked the ninja for defeating the Stone Warriors and offered to reward them with unlimited, free rides the next time they came for a visit. While grateful, the ninja turn down the manager's offer for now; there were many more attacks across Ninjago by the Stone Army that they had to attend to, after all!
