Ronin's pawn shop is a small shop located in Stiix and operated by Ronin. Later, Ronin opened a new shop in Ninjago City.

History

History[]

Stiix and Stones[]

The ninja search the shop when they arrive at Stiix in search for the Scroll of Airjitzu. Ronin then walks in and tries capturing them in a net made of Vengestone, but fails. He says he doesn't know what the Airjitzu Scroll is, but suggests Aeroblades, forged by Deepstone, to stop a ghost. When they leave, however, Ronin checks on the scroll, which is hidden in a vase. As he leaves the shop later that day, Soul Archer and Morro corner him, demanding for the scroll. At that same moment, the ninja break in to steal it.

Sometime after the Ghost Warriors took over Stiix, they converted the pawn shop into a throne room.

The Crooked Path[]

When Ronin arrives back at his shop after stealing the Sword of Sanctuary, he finds that Morro and his allies have taken residence inside his shop. Morro then used Ronin to trick the ninja, Misako, and Wu, claiming that the First Spinjitzu Master's tomb was located in the Caves of Despair.

Curseworld, Part I[]

Returning to Stiix with the Realm Crystal, Morro returned to the pawn shop and placed the crystal atop the Blade Cup to open a portal to the Cursed Realm. With the portal now open, the Ghost Warriors were able to quickly overrun Stiix, marking the beginning of the Ghost invasion. Later, as the Ninja infiltrated the ghost controlled city, Nya confronted Morro outside the pawn shop, fighting against him to buy time for Lloyd to arrive and destroy the Realm Crystal. After Lloyd's intervention, Morro used his powers over Wind to tear apart and lift the shop from its foundations in an effort to delay the green ninja long enough for The Preeminent to cross over. As Lloyd struggled to enter the levitating shop, Ronin arrived just in time and dumped his entire fortune of coins onto the floating structure. Unable to sustain the extra weight from the coins, Morro lost control over the heavy winds that had been lifting the shop into the air, causing it to crash back onto the platforms it had formerly stood on. With the location now stabilized, Lloyd entered and began to use his powers to destroy the Realm Crystal, but unfortunately it would be too late. The Preeminent has arrived.

Curseworld, Part II[]

As the Preeminent fully emerged from the Cursed Realm into Ninjago, what was left of Ronin's pawn shop would be completely destroyed, with several of its remains plunging into the sea below.

Scavengers[]

After discovering that his fake trophies had been stolen, Dareth rushed to Ronin's new pawn shop to purchase new ones. After checking the backroom to fulfill Dareth's order, Ronin was surprised to discover two Vermillion warriors stealing assorted metal items from his store. Annoyed, Ronin informed the snake warriors that his store had a strict 'no shoplifting policy' and activated one of his security measures to knock them to the ground. Moments later, Ronin would depart the store with his Salvage M.E.C., exiting through the roof and grabbing Dareth as they headed to the Temple of Airjitzu to alert the Ninja about the Vermillion warriors.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Small business located in the City of Stiix and operated by master thief Ronin. Here Ronin kept all of the things he had stolen, including the Jade Blade, the Obsidian Armor, the Blade Cup trophy and Master Yang’s Scroll of Airjitzu. During a battle between the ninja and Morro’s ghosts, the shop was destroyed. It later served as Morro’s base in this realm. Needing new space, Ronin eventually rented a place in NINJAGO City.[1]

Trivia[]

  • Some items in the shop include Jadeblades, Vengestone nets, Obsidian Armor, Jacob's sitar, Clouse's skull ink jar, the Blade Cup, and a miniature dinosaur skeleton, implying Ronin traveled to Chen's Island, as all of these objects were previously seen there.
  • The globe visible in the shop represents the Earth instead of Ninjago's planet.
