This article is about the location. For the set, see 71861 The Old Town. For the region in Ninjago City, see Old Town (Ninjago City).

The Old Town is an ancient town located on the outskirts of Ninjago City.

History

History[]

A Sizeable Difference[]

This section is a stub. You can help expand this section by adding some information.


The Whispering Wood[]

The Chief Loyalist had a meeting with the other Loyalists in their hideout as they discussed about the Elemental Monsters and the Weapon of Desolation.

Tonight We Hunt Monsters[]

The Chief Loyalist had another meeting to get useful information from the other Loyalists. Only to get nothing from it.

Seed and Soil[]

Johnny Courageous gave the Chief Loyalist information about the place of the Weapon of Desolation. The Chief Loyalist would later informed Beatrix about it.

Just Under the Surface[]

Cole followed a Loyalist to the Old Town and saw them entering somewhere with another Loyalist. Then he got ambushed by the Chief Loyalists and some other Loyalists. He tried to fight back, only to get stunned from the back and dragged to their hideout.
