Into the Forest

Into the Forest[]

The ninja discover the location of another Bone Weapon in the forest. The ninja, walking through a bamboo forest, come across a sign saying "Only in silence will you find what you are looking for." Lloyd hears the trees whispering, and Zane assures him it's just the wind. The ninja break through the bamboo and split up. Jay and Kai come across two Greenbone Warriors digging in the ground, and they suddenly attack them. Nya and Zane find four more and freeze them, while Lloyd and Cole see another three. Cole uses his power to throw a large boulder down on them. The ninja meet at a rock with the news and leave the Greenbone warriors tied up there. Nya says there were no whispers in the place where she and Zane froze the warriors, so there must be weapons there. The ninja look for weapons, Lloyd pulls the rope and finds two bone shurikens. At the same moment, one uncaptured Bone Warrior frees the others and they all attack the ninja. The ninja run away, but they encounter the Bone Warrior in a mech, who attacks them and takes the shurikens before running away. The ninja go to Master Wu to find out where the last weapon is, they know that if they don't get it, the Bone Warriors will free the Bone King.
