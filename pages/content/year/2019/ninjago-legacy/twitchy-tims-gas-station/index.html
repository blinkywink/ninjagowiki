Twitchy Tim's gas station is a small building located in the Sea of Sand.

History

History[]

Uncharted[]

The ninja traveled to the gas station, where Twitchy Tim was staying. After a conversation, the ninja, along with Twitchy, quickly left the gas station.

The Fifth Villain[]

The ninja, along with Dareth and Fugi-Dove, regrouped here while on the run from Hounddog McBrag.
