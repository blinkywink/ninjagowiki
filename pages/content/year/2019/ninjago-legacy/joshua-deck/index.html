Joshua Deck is an artist whose work has appeared in various Ninjago media.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • His direct contributions to the show are as follows:
    • His "Fugibuff"[1] drawing of Fugi-Dove appeared in the Seabound episode, "Papergirl".
    • He created the character designs for himself, Bragi Schut, Brayden Nelson, Hayley Wolfe, Tommy Andreasen, and the Courthouse civilian in Crystalized.[2][3]
  • He also inspired a few ideas in the WildBrain era of the original series:
    • His artwork was an inspiration for the 2D-animated episodes ("The Absolute Worst," "The Last of the Formlings," and "Dungeon Party!").[4][5]
    • The Clutch Powers story in "Sweatin' to the Goldies" was based on a conversation that he had with Tommy Andreasen.[6]
  • In Deck's YouTube live-streams, Tommy Andreasen would often provide new information on the Ninjago TV series in the live-chats, such as the fact that Zane was transported back in time in Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[7] and that a Master of the Mountain episode would be titled "The Real Fall."[8]
  • He collaborated with Tommy Andreasen on a partially canon, detailed map of Ninjago.[9][10]
  • His poster for the pilot episodes[11] was included in 71741 Ninjago City Gardens.
    • He was interviewed by LEGO for Ninjago's 10th anniversary.[12]
  • He, along with Hayley Wolfe, was one of the four creators of the fan-made documentary, NINJAGO - Ten Years of Spinjitzu: A Documentary.
