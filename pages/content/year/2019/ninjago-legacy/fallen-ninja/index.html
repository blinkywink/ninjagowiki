Fallen Ninja! is the 24th issue of Ninjago: Legacy Magazine. Dojo Cole and Dojo Zane appear on the cover.

Spiders of the Sea

Spiders of the Sea[]

The ninja return to the cave on the Sea of Sand to take the map to the remaining Bone Weapons. Kai takes the map and Bone King mentions that since the ninja came here, it means that his army already has its first weapons. The ninja leave the cave and go looking for more Bone Weapons. The ninja discover the location of the third weapon in the middle of the ocean. They put on underwater breathing masks and jump into the ocean. After a long search, they find a trail and a cave. Deep inside the cave, the ninja find dry land, and the temple inside of it. They spot the Greenbone Warriors in front of it, and go to confront them. The Bone Warriors get their third weapon, and knowing they have no way to escape the Ninja, they create an explosion in the cave and escape through the resulting hole. The cave begins to flood, but all the ninja are wearing masks to swim except for Kai, whose mask has been destroyed. The ninja swim to the surface, and Kai begins to drown, but Nya gives him her mask. On the surface, Kai blames himself for being the reason the Bone Warriors got the third weapon, Wu comforts him by saying that if a teammate wins, the team wins too. Master Wu informs them that he has discovered the location of another weapon. The ninja rush to get it before the Bone Warriors.
