Li-Yu Lin (林粒魚) is a Taiwanese animator and designer. He[1] provided his model design for Ninjago during "Day of the Departed" and the show's seventh and eleventh season. He also designed accompanying sets for LEGO Ninjago and The LEGO Ninjago Movie.

Trivia[]

  • He is the owner of PAPIR LAB (紙間設計), a project described as creating "mini universes" through papercrafting.[3][1]
