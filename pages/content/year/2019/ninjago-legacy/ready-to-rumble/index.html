Ready to Rumble! is the 30th issue of the Ninjago: Legacy Magazine. It includes 112402 Zane vs. Bone King and Dojo Cole appears on the cover.

The Bone Tournament

The Bone Tournament[]

One day at Steeper Wisdom, the Ninja sample some tea brought to them by a new supplier called "Secret SkeleTea Sips". Disgusted by the brew, the Ninja inform Wu of their opinions before passing out and being teleported by the tea. Waking up within the Bone King's lair, the Ninja find themselves chained and face to face once more with their old foe. The Bone King informs the Ninja that they would be participating in the first Bone Tournament, with the winners being granted their freedom while the losers would remain trapped underground forever.

As the games begin, the Ninja are able to free themselves of their chains and begin to easily decimate the Bone King's forces, angering him. As another force of his warriors flee, the Bone King calls in for the Bone Scorpios to deal with the Ninja. Unfortunately, the Ninja are able to quickly overwhelm the Scorpios with their Spinjitzu, defeating the riders and commandeering their Bone Scorpions as they begin to scale the walls to escape. After emerging from the lair, into the Sea of Sand, the Ninja discard the Scorpions and Cole buries the exit, trapping the Greenbone Warriors once more underground.

Trivia[]

  • This is the last issue of any Ninjago Magazine not to include content from Ninjago: Dragons Rising.
