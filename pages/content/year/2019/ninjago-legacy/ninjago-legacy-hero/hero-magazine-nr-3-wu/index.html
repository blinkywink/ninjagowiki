Hero Magazine Nr. 3 Wu is the third issue of the Ninjago: Legacy HERO Magazine. In the original German release, it includes a blister of either 112007 Wu vs. Skulkin or 112109 Wu vs. Garmadon. It was first released circa November 2023 in Germany.[1]
