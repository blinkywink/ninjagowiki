Overview


Tower of Terror! is the 35th issue of the Ninjago: Legacy Magazine.

Tower of Terror!

Tower of Terror![]

As the sun sets outside his office in Borg Tower, Cyrus Borg comments on how it has been a perfect day in which nothing has gone wrong at all! As he prepares to send a final email before ending his workday, the tower's alarm system begins blaring. Someone is attempting to hack into Borg Tower's systems.

Meanwhile in the tower's vaults, two security officers are playing around with a group of deactivated Nindroid Drones, with one officer tossing his hat on one of the drones as he christens it, "Captain Scrapheap." Without warning the drone suddenly activates, lifting both officers off their feet before hurling them across the room. As the officers get back up on their feet, they glance first at the marching army of Nindroids before glancing at each other and declaring that their jobs were not worth the pay.

Meanwhile, at the Monastery of Spinjitzu, the Ninja are enjoying dinner together, sharing jokes as they eat. They would be interrupted by Wu however, informing them of an emergency at Borg Tower. When the Ninja arrive at the tower, they are surprised by how eerily quiet everything seemed; all of the tower's lights were off and not a single sound could be heard anywhere near the building. As they entered the facility's vacant lobby, Lloyd quickly noticed and pointed out that tripwires and traps had been set up. Undeterred, the Ninja are able to easily navigate their way across the various traps as they make their way to a stairwell, finding the door suspiciously locked. After Cole smashed open the doors, the Ninja discover that the entire stairwell was flooded with Nindroid Drones. Having fought Nindroids on many occasions however, the Ninja are able to easily and almost effortlessly overwhelm their foes thanks to their command over their elements. After fighting their way to Cyrus Borg's office, the Ninja polish off the remaining Drones and secure Borg who remarks that his safe room fortunately worked. At that moment however, the windows to Borg's office are suddenly shattered by...hot dogs?!

Ducking for cover, the Ninja briefly glance through the shattered windows, discovering that their old foe, The Mechanic, was behind the hacking of Borg Tower's systems, and was now attacking them with his Noodle Truck of Crime's flying configuration. As he loops around to fire another barrage of hot dogs, the Mechanic laments that despite his hacking of Borg Tower's servers, he is still unable to access, let alone steal, the artifacts stored within the tower's vault. As the Mechanic continues his assault, Zane runs off to retrieve his mech, successfully launching an attack on the Mechanic's truck and forcing the villain to flee. Unfortunately, Zane goes after the Mechanic with his mech, catching up to the villain and capturing him as he destroys the Noodle Truck. Meanwhile, with the danger passed, the other Ninja assist Borg in cleaning up his office just as Zane arrives, declaring that he "Must destroy Cyrus Borg!" Alarmed, the Ninja prepare themselves to fight their friend to defend Borg, fearing that he had been hacked as well. However, Zane quickly drops his act, declaring, "Just kidding!" as everyone except for Borg laughs at his joke.
