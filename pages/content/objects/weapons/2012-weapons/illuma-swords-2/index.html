Overview

“These are authentic replicas that would come in handy were you to live long enough to actually face a Grundle.”
— Rufus McCallister to the ninja in "Child's Play"

Illuma swords are fictional lightsaber-like weapons in the Starfarer comic book series. Toy replicas of them were put on display in Ninjago Doomsday Comix by Rufus McCallister.

History

History[]

Child's Play[]

Rufus informs Lloyd that illuma swords would work well, as the Grundle is nocturnal and its weakness is light, but the shop only gives them out as a prize for winning a comic book trivia competition. Later, Lloyd loses the competition (due to the final question being from a last-issue comic that had just sold out) and the Grundle shows up. The ninja grab the illuma swords to defend themselves against the Grundle, but they break easily, as they are only props.

The Day Ninjago Stood Still[]

After being chased be a Stone Warrior, Rufus found a illuma sword and used it to fight off the warrior.

The Corridor of Elders[]

Rufus McCallister and a few other civilians are seen using them during the battle at the Corridor of Elders.
