Overview

“I expect loyalty. And my control device will guarantee I get it.”
— Lord Ras to Nokt, "Rising Ninja"

The control implant[1] (also referred to as a control collar[2][3] and a control device) was a gadget used to force the target into being loyal to the planter. After the Ritual of the Blood Moon, a control implant was used by Lord Ras to force Nokt into being loyal to him. A small chip is attached to the back of Nokt's head and every time he disobeys Ras, he can trigger the device's remote to electrocute him by doing so. During the final game of Tournament of the Sources with Nokt, Sora used her Tech powers to transform the control implant into a tech bee to attack him, thus accidentally freeing him from Ras' grip. Later, a control implant was used by Jay in order to take over Lee's Mech Dragon, electrocuting Lee off his smaller mech before Jay hopped in it.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Rising Ninja[]

After Nokt tried to attack Lord Ras, he attached the implant to the back of his head and electrocuted him to show him what would happen if he disobeyed him again.

The Shape of Motion[]

When Nokt attempted to kill Cinder after defeating him, Ras activated the device on Nokt to stop him from attacking Cinder.

The Final Game[]

During Nokt's battle with Sora, the latter uses her tech power to turn the control device into a small bee robot, which began to attack Nokt, freeing him at the same time from Ras' control means. When Nokt betrays Ras, the latter tries to use the device again, only to learn Nokt no longer wore the implant.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Casket of Bones[]

Jay installed the control device on Lee's Mech Dragon.

The Screaming Earth[]

Jay activated the control device on Lee's Mech Dragon.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Sora taking off the control device from Nokt's head and turning it into a bee is a callback to "Mindless Beasts" where Sora threatened Rapton that if he didn't let her and Zanth go free, then she would morph his Imperium Drones into a swarm of techno bees.
  • Ras' plan to control the Forbidden Five would have involved the use of Control implants in the same way he controlled Nokt.[4]
