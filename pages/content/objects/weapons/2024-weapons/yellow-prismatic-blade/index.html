The yellow Prismatic Blade is one of the five Prismatic Blades. It was displayed in the Temple City Museum. It was broken by Zeatrix during her battle with Lloyd at the Tournament of the Sources but was later repaired by Arrakore.

History

History[]

The yellow Prismatic Blade was forged as one of five Prismatic Weapons, specifically designed to liberate Thunderfang's right leg from its restraints. Unlike its counterparts, the yellow Prismatic Blade featured a unique design that set it apart. Eventually, it was housed in the City of Temples.

The Sword Shatters[]

At the Games Plaza, Roby declared that he planned a bonus game and announced Lloyd and Zeatrix as the next two competitors. Lloyd recognized the sword from his vision in the Gauntlet. Lloyd used Size to avoid Zeatrix's attack. Lloyd used Light to turn invisible and tried to look for the sword from his visions. Zeatrix found him using Sound and created a bomb using Surface Tension and Shockwave. Lloyd shot the bomb with Energy, and the collision caused the floating platform to break.

Once the match resume, Zeatrix grabbed the sword from Lloyd's visions. Lloyd grabbed a hammer and used it, along with the power of Size, to knock Zeatrix off the platform. Just as Roby declared Lloyd the winner however, Zeatrix jumped up and ruthlessly struck Lloyd with the sword. The sword shattered and the hit knocked Lloyd off the platform, rendering him unconscious.

Prior to I Alone Can Save Them[]

The pieces of the sword were recovered and put on display at the Temple City Museum.

I Alone Can Save Them[]

Kur and Dragonian Warriors went to the City of Temples to find the prismatic blade. Much to Kur's shock the blade was shattered when she saw it on the pedestal. Kur gathered the pieces and put it in a bag, before she decay Roby. Kur brought it back to Rox who thinks of using wish magic to restore it and went to find the last Djinn.

Fallen Wishes[]

Rox arrived in Djinjago where Arrakore was granting wishes. Arrakore refused to help her due to knowing the dangers of the blade, forcing Rox to capture him in a timepiece, forcing Arrakore to repair the blade.

Their Draconic Majesty's Request[]

Rox used the yellow blade to cut the yellow chains in Thunderfang's prison.

Spectral invasions[]

After the battle with Thunderfang, the remaining Prismatic Blades, including the yellow one, were lost and ultimately forgotten, finding their way to the Land of Lost Things. There, they were uncovered by Fritz and Spitz. As the Dragonian Warriors from the Spectral Lands began to steal souls to aid Thunderfang in his transformation into a Source Dragon, Percival had the Advanced Systems Lab enhance two of the four blades for Kai and Frak. This enhancement allowed them to effectively wield the Prismatic Blades against the spectral Dragonian Warriors, ensuring their destruction. However, it remains uncertain whether the yellow Prismatic Blade was among those upgraded.

Appearance

Appearance[]

The yellow Prismatic Blade originally appeared with an icy, crystalline design with sharp, angular features. It was a broad, jagged blade with a central split, a faceted guard set with a blue gem, and a spiraled grip with a rounded pommel. After it was repaired by Arrakore, the blade sported a similar katana design to the other four.

Abilities[]
