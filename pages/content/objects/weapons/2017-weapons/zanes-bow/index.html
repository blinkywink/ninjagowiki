External links
Zane's bow is a black Ninja Bow used by Zane to shoot arrows.

History

History[]

Sons of Garmadon[]

The Mask of Deception[]

When the Royal Family were giving a speech, Zane had his bow ready for anyone attempting to commit sabotage.

The Jade Princess[]

In the courtyard, Zane used his bow to train with Kai.

Zane used his bow and attempted to shoot Mr. E, but he was able to defend himself.

Snake Jaguar[]

Zane had his bow on his back while he was chasing Killow and Mr. E.

Dead Man's Squall[]

Zane attempted to shoot Mr. E with his bow but Mr. E dodged Zane's attacks. His bow later fell off of the cliff.

Game of Masks[]

Zane has his bow in a stare-off with the Sons of Garmadon. Later, he used his bow to shoot Crabby.

Dread on Arrival[]

Zane attempted to shoot the oni mask while Harumi was performing the ritual; however, Mr. E deflected his shot. He later pointed his bow in front of Mr. E after he was unarmed.

Big Trouble, Little Ninjago[]

Zane has his bow when the ninja confronted Lord Garmadon.

March of the Oni[]

Into the Breach[]

Zane atttempted to shoot Garmadon after he attacked the ninja on the Bounty, but Garmadon was able to defend himself.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Despite appearing as Zane's main weapon in the The LEGO Ninjago Movie sets and promotional media, the bow did not appear in the film itself, as is the case with the rest of the Secret Ninja Force's weapons besides Lloyd's sword.
  • While the sets and most of the promotional media display Zane using a Recurve bow, the show has Zane use a Compound bow instead. This is odd since the LEGO company did have Compound bows in other LEGO sets. Why LEGO did not add the Compound bow to their LEGO Ninjago sets based on the show remains unknown.
