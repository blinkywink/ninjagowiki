The arrows are throwing weapons used by Benthomaar.

History

History[]

The Tale of Benthomaar (flashback)[]

While Benthomaar was throwing his arrows at the coral-fastened shells in Merlopia, Kalmaar swam to him and praised his last throw, telling him that was why he always admired him.

Later, when Benthomaar swam quickly to the eel he noticed before and tried to stop it in the Tartarus Trench, he used his arrow and scared it away.

The Turn of the Tide[]

Benthomaar used his arrows when he was fighting Maaray Guards.

Later, during his fall from Wojira's head, Benthomaar threw an arrow from his quiver at his brother and destroyed his trident, thus abolishing his rule over Wojira, who then devoured him.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Each arrow has the form of a scaled-down Benthomaar's spear. On the short gold stick with a gold tip at one end, there is a small, glowing blue energy attachment, which, after pulling the arrow out of the quiver, enlarges and takes the form of a short lightning blade.

Benthomaar always has a few arrows in his quiver, at least twelve, and they are all turned blade down. The quiver itself is covered with copper scales and in front there is a golden part depicting the symbol of Merlopia.
