The Spectral swords are weapons used primarily by the Spectral Dragonians, Kur, and Drix. After Thunderfang was vanquished, it's unknown what happened to the weapons.

History

History[]

Human Resources[]

When Spectral Dragonians invaded Abracalabria, they used Spectral swords as weapons.

Casket of Bones[]

Spectral Dragonians were holding Spectral swords while going back to the Spectral Lands with Drix.

The Screaming Earth[]

When Spectral Dragonians arrived in the Community of Lee through the portal, they were holding Spectral swords.

Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon[]

As Spectral Dragonians drained the souls of the inhabitants of the Community of Lee, they used Spectral swords as weapons.

When Spectral Dragonians invaded the Land of Lost Things, three of the Spectral Dragonians used Spectral swords as weapons.

When the ninja and Jay were spotted by Spectral Dragonians and were chased, some of the Spectral Dragonians were holding Spectral swords.

The Vault of Focus[]

Wehn Spectral Dragonians invaded the City of Imperium, they used Spectral swords as weapons. When Kai and Frak used Reveal Blades against some of the Spectral Dragonians, they disappear alongside with their swords.

For Whom the Bell Tolls[]

As Thunderfang continued to get stronger, some of the Spectral Dragonians around him were holding Spectral swords.

When Doves Cry[]

When Lloyd and Arin arrived in the Spectral Lands to summon the Arc Dragon of Focus and were spotted by Drix, Rox, Nokt and some of the Spectral Dragonians, Drix and some of the Spectral Dragonians were holding Spectral swords.

When the Freebooters left Djinjago, they noticed Kur, who was going to Djinjago with a Spectral sword in her hand.

As the battle in the Spectral Lands continued, Drix and Spectral Dragonians continued to use Spectral swors as weapons.

Chaos Reigns[]

When Kur and Spectral Dragonians arrived in the Cave of Bells in Djinjago, they were holding Spectral swords.

When Zarkt and Spectral Dragonians arrived in the Cave of Bells in Mysterium, some of the Spectral Dragonians were holding Spectral swords.

As the battle in both sides continued, Kur and Spectral Dragonians continued to use Spectral swords against the ninja, while the ninja destroyed Spectral Dragonians alongside with their weapons thanks to Reveal Blades.

After Thunderfang's defeat, the Forbidden Five and Spectral Dragonians disappeared, alongside with Spectral swords.
