The sickles are a pair of weapons used by Drix.

History

History[]

Drix acquired this pair of sickles before he and the Forbidden Five started their quest for the Prismatic Blades. The dual sickles would serve as Drix's weapon of choice until his death.

Most notably, Drix wielded the sickles during his search of the Returning Blade and the Blue Prismatic Blade in the World Forest, using it in combat against Lloyd, who used the Blue Prismatic Blade to fight off Drix, but Drix used Swarm and grabbed the blade. After claiming the blade, Drix sheath his sickles on his back before attacking Ras and claiming the Returning Blade.

In the moments leading up to Thunderfang's release, Drix wielded both the Prismatic Blades to combat the Ninja before the other Five arrived to secured the remaining two blades. Once Rox grabbed the two blades from Arin, Drix used the Returning Blade to cut Thunderfang's chain on his neck.

Ultimately, the dual sickles was lost when the Forbidden Five were betrayed by Thunderfang and Drix's soul was consumed by the Chaos Dragon, remaining sheathed alongside its lifeless master's back.
