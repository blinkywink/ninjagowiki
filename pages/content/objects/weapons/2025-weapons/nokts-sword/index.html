The sword is a weapon used by Nokt.

History

History[]

Nokt acquired this sword before the Forbidden Five began their quest for the Prismatic Blades. The sword would serve as Nokt's weapon of choice until his death.

Most notably, Nokt wielded the sword during his raid on the Freebooters' train in search of the Red Prismatic Blade, using it in combat against the train's defenders as well as Zane and P.I.X.A.L. who were onboard by chance. In the moments leading up to Thunderfang's release, Nokt wielded the sword to combat the Ninja as the Five secured the remaining two blades. During this time, Nokt notably wielded it against Sora and then Ras to satisfy his lust for vengeance against the latter. In his duel with Ras, Nokt wielded this sword with great efficiency and skill, near effortlessly disarming Ras of his golden hammer, and prepared to kill the tiger with the blade. Unfortunately, Nokt's vengeance would go unsatisfied thanks to the intervention of Arin, forcing Nokt to return his focus to freeing Thunderfang.

Ultimately, the sword was lost when the Forbidden Five were betrayed by Thunderfang and Nokt's soul was consumed by the Chaos Dragon, remaining sheathed alongside its lifeless master's back.
