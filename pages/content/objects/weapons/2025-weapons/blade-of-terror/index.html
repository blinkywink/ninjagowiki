The Blade of Terror[1] is a sword used by Rox.

History

History[]

Rox acquired this sword before she and the Forbidden Five started their quest for the Prismatic Blades. The sword would serve as Rox's weapon of choice until her death.

Most notably, Rox wielded the sword during her attack at the algae farmers' village to search for the Green Prismatic Blade. When waiting for Drix to arrive with the final two blades, Rox armed herself with the sword when one of Drix's insects contacted her and she used the sword to battle the Ninja; she used it to defend herself against Lloyd's back attack when she noticed Nokt taking his vengeance on Ras. When Arin saved Ras, Rox defeated Lloyd and attack Arin, claiming the last two Prismatic Blades.

Ultimately, the sword was lost when the Forbidden Five were betrayed by Thunderfang and Rox's soul was consumed by the Chaos Dragon, remaining sheathed on the corpse of its owner's back.
