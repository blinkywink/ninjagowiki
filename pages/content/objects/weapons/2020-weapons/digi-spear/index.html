Overview

The Digi-spear[1] is a spear used by Unagami.

History

History[]

The Maze of the Red Dragon[]

Unagami holds the spear as he talked to Okino.

The Temple of Madness[]

Unagami holds the spear as he talks to Jay and later he uses the spear in battle when Jay tried to prevent him from crossing over to Ninjago. During this fight with Jay, the spear falls out. When he transformed into the Empire Dragon, the spear disappeared.

Game Over[]

Unagami holds the spear as he talks to Jay and later to Milton Dyer. When he changed into his child form, the spear disappeared.
