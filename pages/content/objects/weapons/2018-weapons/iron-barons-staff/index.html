Overview

Iron Baron's staff[1] was a horned staff wielded by Iron Baron himself.

History

History[]

Firstbourne[]

Iron Baron held his staff, while sitting on his throne, when he mocked the captured ninja after their blindfolds are taken off.

Iron & Stone[]

Iron Baron held the staff while interrogating Jay, Zane and Kai, believing the three to be Oni. He continued to carry it while spectating the Ninja's confrontation with Slab in the Pit.

Radio Free Ninjago[]

Iron Baron is holding his staff throughout the episode, including the start of the Dragon Hunt. After the Wind Dragon is lured by the ninja's Elemental Powers, the Wind Dragon attacks, and the staff was blown away.

Two Lies, One Truth[]

When Iron Baron comes across the chained Daddy No Legs and Muzzle in the desert, he has his staff with him.
