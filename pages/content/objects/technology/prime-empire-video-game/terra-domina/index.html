Terra Domina is the third and final game zone out of three zones in Prime Empire.

Prime Empire[]

Level Thirteen[]

A pixelate version can be seen when Cole is playing Prime Empire.

Stop, Drop and Side Scroll[]

Lloyd, Jay, and Nya enter Terra Domina and originally, Lloyd wanted to survey the place and leave, but the exist closes off and they are forced to participate on the side-scrolling course. All three ninja lose a life for different reasons, but make it to the infinity maze.

The infinity maze going upwards and the floor slowly disappearing beneath them. They travel upwards and after a while, they slowly get tired. Just as they think the side-scroller is endless, Nya then grabs a Loot-Box and unlocks the ability to perform Airjitzu. Jay and Lloyd tell Nya to go on without them but says for them to grab and hold tight and flies up to the top.

The ninja make it to the battle arena where the ninja select Lloyd as their fighter. He is paired up against Avatar Harumi. Jay and Nya try to tell Lloyd that she's not real, but Lloyd cannot hear. Avatar Harumi begins attacking Lloyd and Lloyd is hesitant to attack. After realizing she's not real, Lloyd begins fighting and both strike each other at the same time, causing Harumi to die while Lloyd to be cubed. Jay and Nya obtain the Orange Key-Tana and Jay makes a promise saying they will fight for those who were lost.

The Temple of Madness[]

Jay and Nya are able to get through the glitched conveyer belt. They then defeat all of the staff at Sushimi's sushi restaurant before heading to the Fire Wall. While there, Nya is struck and turns into an Energy Cube.

When Jay meets Unagami, he realizes Unagami is going after Milton Dyer trying to find answers. Jay fights Unagami claiming he's confused, but he transforms into his Empire Dragon and heads into Ninjago. Meanwhile, multiple Red Visors also enter and some attack Jay. He summons his Blue Dragon and defeats the Red Visors before heading into Ninjago himself.
