Overview

Inventories are where players store items in Prime Empire.

History

History[]

I am Okino[]

The Newbie Gamer accessed his inventory to use a katana in the Forest of Discontent.

The Glitch[]

Jay uses his inventory to take out torches in the Forest of Discontent.

The Cliffs of Hysteria[]

The ninja use their inventories to access their credits and pay Shifty for grappling hooks and the Rope of Questionable Integrity.

The Maze of the Red Dragon[]

After Cole is almost smashed by a boulder, he checks his health status to make sure he didn't lose a life. When the ninja enter the heart of the maze, Jay checks his stats and confirms that he has the highest stealth rating of the ninja, making him the best candidate to grab the Purple Key-Tana. After they defeat the dragon, Jay stored the Blue Dragon amulet in his inventory.

One Step Forward, Two Steps Back[]

When the ninja arrive at the Speedway Five-Billion to enter the race, they are told that the entry fee is 1000 credits. The ninja open their inventories to check how many credits they have. Lloyd has 160 credits, Nya has 198 credits, Cole has 153, Jay has 160, and Kai has 12 credits, due to spending credits on his new avatar. When the Red Visors attack, Jay is forced to upgrade his weapon for 100 credits.

After Jay and Nya win the Terra Technica Dance-Off, they open their inventories to add their 500 credit reward.

Racer Seven[]

Lloyd uses his inventory to switch to his new avatar as a disguise, and uses it again to switch back to his original appearance.

The Speedway Five-Billion[]

Scott uses his inventory to take out his laser blaster and cause a distraction while the ninja make their way to the Speedway Racetrack. He also accesses a miniature vehicle with a built-in explosive to take out some of the Red Visors.
