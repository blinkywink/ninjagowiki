The mech is a racing mech owned by Lobbo.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

As Alfonzo Frohicky tried to persuade Arin and Sora to not participate in the Mech Master 5000, he pointed out to Lobbo's mech, which he couldn't find out what it was before Lobbo himself entered it. Throughout the race, Lobbo was in dead last, going slow and steady, until the final stretch of the race when Kreel was knocked down trapped under a slab, and Arin and Sora went to rescue Riyu from the Claws of Imperium that ultimately caused the disruption. Lobbo used the slab as a ramp to go over the hole caused in the attack and pass the finish line, winning the race.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Blood Moon[]

Lobbo's mech moved out of control along Ninjago City before Lloyd and Arin manage to get Lobbo out, and Sora used her Technology to take apart the mech, reassemble it into a DJ set, and initiate a dance party, which some citizens, Arin, Wyldfyre, Riyu, and Lobbo himself attended.

Zane's PIXAL Project[]

Lobbo and Kreel used their mechs to fight and destroy K.R.E.E.L. when the android went haywire and began ransacking Kreel's junkyard.
