Overview

“It has a drone?”
— Arin, "I Alone Can Save Them"

The Dragon-headed drone[1] is a drone that is on top of the Super Storm Jet. The drone had built-in blasters and when deployed it can chase after it's targets. The drone can be operated remotely from the jet or flown manually.

History

History[]

I Alone Can Save Them[]

As Arin and Ras stepped onto the Super Storm Jet, Arin fumbled with the intricate controls, accidentally activating the dragon-headed drone, which startled him. In a rush, he attempted to return the drone to its designated spot, but it nearly crashed to them before Ras quickly intervened and powered it down.

Fallen Wishes[]

The drone remained attached to the Super Storm Jet as Ras and Arin trained along the shores of the Land of Lee.

Their Draconic Majesty's Request[]

While journeying to the World Forest, Ras and Arin were attacked by a masked bounty hunter. After failing to shake off their pursuer, Ras deployed the drone to combat him. Although the mercenary escaped from his suit and onto the wings of the Super Storm Jet, the drone continued to pursue the empty suit of armor, eventually succeeding in downing it.

Chaos Unchained[]

When he briefly rejoined the Ninja to stop the Forbidden Five from freeing Thunderfang, the drone was repaired and reattach to the Super Storm Jet when Arin flew it to the Monastery of Spinjitzu. Sora modified it so Arin can pilot the repaired drone. Catching up to Drix, Arin fired the drone's lasers to battle the Dragonian Warriors. Arin flew with both the Blue Prismatic Blade and Returning Blade to head back to the Monastery but chose instead to save Ras and crashed the drone into Nokt. This only temporarily slowed down the dark warrior, however, as Nokt managed to get the drone off of him moments later by using his powers and Nokt manage to kick Arin into the Winds of Chaos, allowing Rox to take the final two blades.
