Overview

“It'll turn forward the hands of time, turning you old and reducing the Grundle back to dust and bones. But be careful, there's no turning back!”
— Master Wu to the de-aged ninja, "Child's Play"

Tomorrow's Tea is a tea found in Ninjago that has the power to rapidly age things and undo the effects of time-reversing magic.

History

History[]

Child's Play[]

Once Master Wu and Nya heard of the Grundle situation from Lloyd's message, Master Wu decided to visit Mystake for Tomorrow's Tea. With the tea in their possession, the pair hurried to the comic store where the Grundle was spotted, finding it menacing the de-aged ninja. Lloyd used the tea to reverse the de-aging magic that revived the Grundle and reduced his friends to children but was also aged to teenage years due to being caught in the "explosion" of the tea.

Because Lloyd aged from one stage of life to another in a matter of seconds, he never received the call from the Whispering Wood to protect it and perform the Ritual of Unrooting.[1]
