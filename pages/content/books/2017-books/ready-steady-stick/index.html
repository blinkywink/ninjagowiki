Overview

This article is about the 2017 sticker book. For other uses, see Ready, Steady, Stick! (disambiguation).

Ready, Steady, Stick! is a sticker book for The LEGO Ninjago Movie.

Official description

Official description[]

Get ready for an amazing adventure! This new book from THE LEGO® NINJAGO MOVIE series is packed with exciting activities … and over 200 stickers!

There are hours of fun to be had with Kai, Zane, Cole, Jay, Lloyd and Nya as their wise leader, Sensei Wu, trains them in the greatest of all martial arts. Stories, word searches, quizzes and mazes will keep the young Ninja entertained as they join the battle for Ninjago.

This fantastic sticker book is based on the brand-new movie and comes with fantastic all-action stickers. It's perfect for LEGO® fans aged 6 years and over.[1]
