Overview

“In the Cloud Kingdom, they believe everything they write in their scrolls is immediately what happens in real life.”
— Kai to the other ninja, "Writers of Destiny"

The Cloud Kingdom was one of the realms before the Merge and is now part of the Merged Lands. It's a floating kingdom inhabited by the Writers of Destiny. Before the Merge, the Cloud Kingdom could be accessed from Ninjago through the Blind Man's Eye, through use of the Realm Crystal, or if the inhabitants were willing to allow someone to enter.

History

History[]

Background[]

At an unknown point in time the Cloud Kingdom came into existence as one of many realms parallel to Ninjago. The monks residing in the Cloud Kingdom assumed the role of the Writers of Destiny, influencing various events across the realms while guiding rather than dictating their fates. At some point the First Spinjitzu Master would visit the realm.[1] Approximately 10,000 years before the ghost invasion, Fenwick and his brother Quanish governed the Cloud Kingdom. Fenwick held the view that the destinies of the realm's inhabitants should be controlled, whereas Quanish advocated for their freedom to make personal choices, resulting in Quanish's banishment to Ninjago. Prior to the ninja's arrival, Fenwick encountered the Preeminent and consented to changed Morro's destiny in exchange for the preservation of his own life.

Possession[]

Peak-a-Boo[]

After scaling the Wailing Alps, the ninja arrived in the Cloud Kingdom through the Blind Man's Eye, seeking the Sword of Sanctuary.

Kingdom Come[]

The ninja were greeted in the Cloud Kingdom by Fenwick, who explained the nature of the world to them and revealed Morro's intentions to them, before imprisoning them with Nimbus. He then enabled Morro and his allies to enter the Cloud Kingdom after being contacted by Bansha, before leading them to the keeping place of the sword. However, Cole's ghostly nature enabled the ninja to escape and intercept Morro, and they pursued him in an effort to recover the sword. Nimbus briefly interfered with their plans, until Nobu—a Cloud Monk not aligned with Fenwick's treachery—rewrote Fenwick's scroll of destiny to make him Nimbus' target instead. The ghosts and ninja then departed the Cloud Kingdom.

Curseworld, Part II[]

Morro and Lloyd later passed through the Cloud Kingdom during their battle over the Realm Crystal. Nobu and another monk observed this, but the older scribe warned Nobu that they couldn't interfere.

Between Seasons[]

The Cloud Kingdom was attacked by the Oni, with all of its inhabitants turned to stone.[2] However the effects reversed when the Ninja defeated the Oni in Ninjago.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

The Kaiju Protocol[]

While trying to open a portal to the Never-Realm, Wu and P.I.X.A.L. concluded the Traveler's Tea was not strong enough. They add more leaves, and instead, opened several portals to different realm, including the Cloud Kingdom.

Between Series[]

During the Merge, the Cloud Kingdom was among the many realms that merged with Ninjago and this event caught the Writers of Destiny by surprise. Over time, the Cloud Kingdom integrated with a realm that was home to the Ice Dragons, who relocated to the Cloud Kingdom for refuge.[3] Ras, at some point, went to the Cloud Kingdom, where he cursed the Scroll-Worms to attack inquirers in order to ensure certain information, including the Merge and Dragon Cores, remains hidden.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Writers of Destiny[]

Following a MergeQuake, the Cloud Kingdom was amalgamated with another realm inhabited by tentacled beasts, who launched an assault on the former. The Writers of Destiny were rescued from the beasts' attacks by the ninja, who sought access to the Cloud Monks' archives. However, the Scroll-Worms had been cursed, preventing the ninja from obtaining the information they needed to end the MergeQuakes. Despite the setback, the ninja successfully defended the Cloud Kingdom from the tentacled beasts and closed the MergeQuake that summoned them, preventing the merging of the Cloud Kingdom with the monsters' realm.

Mindless Beasts[]

Without relying on the Scroll-Worms, Nya eventually found information on the Temple of the Dragon Cores and was lured to a hidden portal gate by Sprite.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Shattered Dreams[]

On a sunny day, Suetonius and Marcus greeted the Writers of Destiny with a new proclamation, desiring to make the kingdom more fun and lively. Although they encouraged the others to contribute with ideas, the other writers only made a request for bagels. Another writer however noticed that her quill was no longer writing, alarming Suetonius who realized that this was a warning that something bad was coming, something that not even destiny could prevent. At that moment, Ras and his newly assembled Wolf Clan revealed their presence. Suetonius called for Euphrasia and a brief fight broke out as the latter attempted to defend her home from the invaders. Unfortunately, she was effortlessly defeated by Cinder, allowing the Wolf Clan to occupy Cloud Kingdom.

Sometime later, Euphrasia managed to arrive at the Monastery of Spinjitzu and summoned the aid of the ninja. Together, they attempted to liberate Cloud Kingdom and while they were able to initially route the Wolf Mask Warriors, the tide turned when Ras rang the Gong of Shattering and empowered his forces. The ninja were ultimately defeated single-handedly by Cinder and his newfound abilities of Shatterspin, leaving the Kingdom in the control of the Wolf Clan.

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

Cinder practiced honing his skills in Shatterspin along the paths of Cloud Kingdom, smashing multiple pots. Meanwhile, Jordana studied in the Kingdom's archives and later reported her findings to Ras, greatly pleasing him. With their next course set, Ras ordered several monks to activate an engine, breaking away a part of the Kingdom to serve as a mobile base for the Wolf Clan.

Force From the East[]

The severed portion of Cloud Kingdom traversed to the Land of Lost Things, where it served as a staging ground for the Wolf Clan's attempts at capturing Bonzle. Geo briefly mistook the Wolf Clan for Cloud Monks upon their first encounter. Meanwhile, from Cloud Kingdom, Jordana lifted the spell placed upon the Land of Lost Things that prevented the Finders from leaving. Ras also ordered for the Gong of Shattering to be rung in spite of Jordana's protests, proclaiming that no cost was too great to stop them from their goal of capturing Bonzle.

The Spell at the Waterfall[]

Ras continued to oversee Cinder's training in Shatterspin at Cloud Kingdom while Jordana searched frantically for Bonzle's location. After receiving new instructions from his master, Ras instructed Jordana to pick out five Cloud Monks as sacrifices for their coming ritual.

Fugitives From Madness[]

The Wolf Clan trained atop of Cloud Kingdom as the Blood Moon rose.

Secrets of the Wyldness[]

The Wolf Clan brought their portion of Cloud Kingdom to the Forest of Spirits.

The Forest of Spirits[]

Atop the Cloud Kingdom, Ras confirmed with the Writers of Destiny that the last of the Theroxian texts have been translated. Pleased with the answer, Ras then ordered for Cinder to destroy the Cloud Kingdom and everything on it, including the remaining monks. The Cloud Kingdom however remained intact by the time Zane, Cole, and Gandalaria arrived. Believing that Bonzle was held captive onboard, the three ascended to the commandeered portion of Cloud Kingdom.

Rising Ninja[]

Cole, Zane, and Gandalaria arrived at the portion of Cloud Kingdom only to discover that the Wolf Clan had long abandoned it. The three freed the captive Writers of Destiny who then revealed that the floating island had been set to crash below. Zane and Cole were led down to the motor room where they worked to straighten out the island's damaged rotor in hopes of saving it. In the process, Cole accidentally snapped the main rotor but Zane was able to fill in as a substitute thanks to Gandalaria's size-altering potion. With some unknown assistance from Euphrasia, the ninja were able to stop the portion of Cloud Kingdom from crashing to the ground.

At the end of the Ritual of the Blood Moon, the remaining ninja regrouped on Cloud Kingdom where Suetonius informed them that he could not find any information to explain the mysterious disappearance of the Wolf Clan and the Shadow Dojo. Nevertheless, he thanked the ninja for saving the world once more and urged them to celebrate their victory.

Enter the City of Temples[]

While Euphrasia was flying around the Cloud Kingdom, two Spirit Dragons arrived to invite her to participate in the Tournament of the Sources, which she gladly accepted.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Cloud Kingdom had four known methods of entry: the Blind Man's Eye, the Traveler's Tea, the Realm Crystal, and through the use of the portal gates.
  • The kingdom's entrance gate resembles the set version of the Tomb of the First Spinjitzu Master, appearing in 70736.
  • Tommy Andreasen doubts the Writers of Destiny have control of the Overlord.[5]
  • In the trailer for Season 10, the Cloud Kingdom was seen being temporarily taken by the Oni until they were defeated.[2]
  • It is unknown whether the Cloud Kingdom affects the other realms besides Ninjago or whether residents of the Cloud Kingdom go to the Departed Realm upon death.[6][7][8]
  • The torso design on some of the monks resembles the Roman Emperor from Minifigures Series 9.
    • This is fitting as the main architectural style of the realm seen is an amalgamation of Japanese and Roman architecture.
  • The Sword of Sanctuary guards have the same faces as the lumberjacks seen in "The Invitation" and "Day of the Departed", the same helmet design as Levi, and similar torso armor to Cole ZX. In The Hands of Time, pictured in the Ninjago Museum of History's painting depicting the Battle for All Time, there is a samurai warrior with the same torso as these guards.
  • The Cloud Kingdom resembles the Kingdom of Shintaro, as they are both elevated white and gold locations.
  • In Ninjago: Dragons Rising, only one piece of the Cloud Kingdom appeared, with the rest being in a different location. According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, the Writers of Destiny keep assiduous track of where everything is.[9]
