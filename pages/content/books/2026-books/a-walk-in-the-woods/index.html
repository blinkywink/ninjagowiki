A walk in the woods is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising comic that was released in an issue of the LEGO Magazine.

Plot

Plot[]

In the Whispering Wood, Lloyd struggles to find clues to unlock his True Form. Briefly wondering if clues could come from his childhood, Lloyd grumbled in annoyance that he could no longer remember his childhood after the time he used Tomorrow's Tea to stop a Grundle.

As Lloyd continued to wander, his attention turned towards a squirrel and a dragonfly, fantasizing if both had draconic True Forms to unlock. Lloyd then kneeled down beside a pond and saw instead of his reflection, that of the Arc Dragon of Life, much to his surprise. At first, Lloyd wondered if the Arc Dragon was his True Form, but the dragon instead replied that Lloyd was merely thirsty and that he was too tired to think clearly. With this realization in mind, Lloyd filled up his canteen and continued his journey.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The comic has several inconsistencies with the series:
    • Lloyd is walking through the Whispering Wood alone. It is unknown at what point in time this could've happened, as he was accompanied by Arin and Euphrasia when searching for the Temple of the Green Ninja.
    • Lloyd mentions that he needs to unlock his "True Form." In the series, such a term for Draconus Form was never used, and the Arc Dragon of Life didn't know about the existence of Draconus Form before Lloyd unlocked it.
    • Lloyd states that he doesn't remember his childhood after being hit by Tomorrow's Tea, which is proven wrong in "The Calm Inside."
