Greatest Battles is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising book.[2]

Official description

Official description[]

Build your awesome Lloyd Garmadon minifigure and arm him with the golden sabre before leading him through the history of the Ninjago world.

Join Lloyd in reliving some of the greatest battles and iconic moments from 15 years of LEGO® NINJAGO® action, searching for hidden characters and objects along the way. Make your way through incredible scenes including the Ultra Dragon’s battle against the Great Devourer, the face-off against the Dragon Hunters and the fight against Empress Beatrix.

In each packed scene, look out for legendary golden weapons such as the Sword of Fire and Shurikens of Ice, along with dangerous enemies including Princess Harumi, Pythor and the Time Twins.

Also included are two pages of stickers that young LEGO NINJAGO fans can use to complete some of the scenes. There are even activities to complete, including mazes and picture puzzles.

Join the ninja and become part of the greatest battles in LEGO NINJAGO history!
