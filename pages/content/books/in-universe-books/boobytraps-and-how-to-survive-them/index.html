Overview

This article is about the book. For the episode, see Boobytraps and How to Survive Them (episode).
“You are the foremost expert on ancient structures and your book, Boobytraps and How to Survive Them, is considered a seminal work.”
— Zane to Clutch Powers, "Boobytraps and How to Survive Them"

Boobytraps and How to Survive Them is a guidebook written by Clutch Powers. As the name suggests, the book is about how to survive boobytraps.

History

History[]

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Boobytraps and How to Survive Them[]

Upon recovering from a fall into a pit of spikes, the ninja and Clutch come across the skeletal remains of Clutch's former competitor, Hageman. Clutch said that Hageman hadn't read the book, and stated that it cost him his life. Zane picked up Hageman's copy of the book and said that he did read it. Clutch snatched and opened the book, saying that he did not reach chapter nine, the chapter on cobweb traps. He then carelessly threw it at the skeleton, causing it to fall apart. Jay picked up the book as they passed.

Ninja vs Lava[]

While chained to a wall, Jay read Hageman's copy of the book, until the torches went out and lava started erupting. He dropped the book out of shock, causing it to burn instantly.

Ancient History[]

Acidicus was reading a copy of the book when Cole, Jay, and Kai arrived in an underground library.

Under Siege[]

A copy of Clutch's book lies on a table next to Clutch's bed in Ninjago City Hospital.

A Cold Goodbye[]

Multiple copies of Clutch's book are located in the cupboard where Wu grabbed the box of Traveler's Tea.

The Absolute Worst[]

As he prepared to tell Ultra Violet and Fugi-Dove why he thinks Nya is the worst, The Mechanic picks up a copy of the book.

Master of the Mountain[]

Shintaro[]

During their trip to the Kingdom of Shintaro, Zane reads about the Dire Bats which inhabit the area in a copy of the book.

Seabound[]

Riddle of the Sphinx[]

Cecil Putnam was putting the book away when the Ninja burst into the Explorer's Club.

Assault on Ninjago City[]

A copy of the book hit the windshield while Dareth was driving a bus.

Crystalized[]

Ninjago City vs. Ninja[]

Jay was reading a copy of the book when Lloyd received a visitor and was taken to meet them. He continued to read the book up until Lloyd was returned to their cell.

Brave But Foolish[]

Ronin was reading the book while the other inmates were watching the news of the ninja saving the citizens from the Crystal Warriors. He then sets the book down on the table and goes to confront the prisoners.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Vault of Focus (flashback)[]

As the Finders guided Cole through the Land of Lost Things, Fritz stumbled upon a copy of Boobytraps and How to Survive Them, remarking on how many copies of the book seem to go missing over time.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Last Djinn[]

Nya, Sora, and Riyu find an archaeologist's skeleton with a copy of the book, which Riyu takes in his mouth before they move on. However, Nya steps on a trap, opening up an acid pit which they nearly fall into, with Riyu dropping the book into the acid.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Blood Moon[]

Some vendor in the Crossroads stored copies of this book.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

A Wonder of the Merged Lands[]

A copy of Boobytraps and How to Survive Them was among the many books in the Explorer's Club's possession after they transported the captured Mutation Monster to their airship.

Monster in the Monastery[]

A copy of Boobytraps and How to Survive Them was on a bookshelf in the Explorer's Club's artifact storage.

Dangerous Connections[]

Dr. LaRow was reading a copy of Boobytraps and How to Survive Them when Arin and Sora visited her in the Borg Tower prison.

Other appearances and mentions

Other appearances and mentions[]

The Real Fall[]

Princess Vania mentions the book's vocal exercises when she, Wu, and Cole were stuck in a giant spider's web.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The book has an unknown amount of chapters. Chapter 8 covers rickety bridges, while Chapter 9 covers cobweb traps.
    • Two chapters of the book are also entitled, "What To Do When All is Lost" and "Escaping from Close Calls".
      • There is also "a whole chapter on rising lava", possibly the page after "Escaping from Close Calls".[1]
  • The cover of the book says "Survival Guide" in Ninjargon.
