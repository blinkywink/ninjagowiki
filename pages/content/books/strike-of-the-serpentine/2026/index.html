15th Anniversary

15th Anniversary[]

January 14, 2026 marked 15 years since the release of the pilot episodes. For this occasion, a new wave of Ninjago: Legacy sets commemorating the anniversary were released on January 1, 2026.

  • Ninjago: Legacy sets included minifigures of the Elemental Alliance with special pedestals.
  • A special remix of the Weekend Whip was released.
