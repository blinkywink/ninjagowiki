Tales from the Merged Realms is a canon[2] Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 1 reader book that was released in 2024.[1] It has 64 pages. It is set before Season 1.

Official description

Official description[]

New heroes, new villains—and dragons!—collide in this full-color chapter book, featuring three action-packed stories based on the all-new LEGO® NINJAGO®: Dragon Rising animated series!

The world of LEGO NINJAGO is changing as many legendary realms have suddenly combined into one world! The adventures of Sora and Arin take readers on an amazing journey across the lush world of Dragons Rising, the newest LEGO NINJAGO animated series. Kids 6-9 will love this full-color chapter book, featuring three new and exciting adventure stories.[1]

Plot

Plot[]

The Way In[]

Chronological order
Previous Next
"First Try!" The New Heroes

Sora and Arin try to compete in the Mech Master 5000. They are concerned that they do not have good enough parts and after Kreel tells them that the entry fee has gone up, they are concerned that they do not have the money. Arin busks using Spinjitzu and Sora finds parts. They both fail. However, Sora tries to fix the mech with the parts she does have and Arin bakes a pie to bribe Mr. Frohicky. They succeed to bribe him, but lose the race. However, Sora is happy and excited that the next race will have a cash prize.

The Explorer's Wise Words[]

The Explorer's Club have a competition to get three items from different realms. These are "The Underworld Umbrella", "A Spatula from the Cursed Realm" and "A Scented Relaxation Candle from the Realm of Madness". Clutch Powers wants to find them so he will be announced explorer of the year. Clutch Powers is late. Underhill found the Underworld Umbrella and Smythe found the Spatula of the Cursed Realm. However, Shippelton went missing trying to find the Scented Relaxation Candle from the Realm of Madness. Clutch Powers wants to find Shippelton because he knows it will get him even more praise. He finds Shippelton. Shippelton tells Clutch Powers that he is not happy with him because there is a trap not mentioned in Boobytraps and How To Survive Them. Clutch Powers doubts this but falls in to the very trap that Shippelton is talking about.

Sealing the Cracks[]

Chronological order
Previous Next
"False Light" "First Try!"

Lloyd and Kai are sealing cracks from MergeQuakes. They find a weird crack that was sucking in things and spewing out monsters and lava. They use rocks from the monsters to protect people. They harden the lava to seal the crack. Then they split. Kai goes to catch the monsters and Lloyd goes back to the monastery to search for ways to stop the merge quakes.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This book is set between the Merge and Dragons Rising, Season 1.
  • The book claims that Mr. Frohicky is "as old as the mountains". But according to himself in "The Temple of the Dragon Cores," he was a tadpole during the events of the original series.
    • However, it is possible that the statement is correct if Frohicky's frog age was used, as frogs have a shorter life span; or if time in the Wyldness moved differently than in Ninjago.
  • The book says that Shippelton has still not removed his arrows and that they are a "souvenir."
  • Kai and Lloyd are using the new Destiny's Bounty, even though it should not be finished yet. Also, it is like the set, which means it has a dragon in it. However, this should not be the case since the dragon was actually Zanth.
  • The book states that Arin learned how to bake from his mother, but it was revealed in "Enter the City of Temples" that Frak was the one who taught Arin how to bake pies.
    • It's possible both are true, as Arin's mother could have been his inspiration to get into baking and Frak taught him specifically about pie-making.
