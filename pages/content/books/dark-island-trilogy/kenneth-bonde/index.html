Overview

Kenneth Bonde is a Danish director and production manager formerly working at WILFilm ApS.[1] He provided his visual effects supervision for Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu and later directed a short for Wu's Teas. He was also responsible for the title design of "Day of the Departed" and Season 7: The Hands of Time.

He was responsible for the initial conceptualization of the Dark Island Trilogy alongside Tommy Andreasen and Tommy Kalmar.[2][3]
