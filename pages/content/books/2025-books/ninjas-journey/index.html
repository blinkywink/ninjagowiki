Overview

Ninja's Journey is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 3 activity book.[1] It released in Finland on April 4, 2025.

Official description

Official description[]

This fun-filled book includes original content inspired by the adventures from the Dragons Rising TV series.

The story is centered around new ninja Arin. Read about his adventures, aspirations, skills including his hobby - baking), and his friends and foes.

  • A mix of fun-filled activities and puzzles
  • Comics packed with LEGO® humor
  • Buildable Arin minifigure with accessories: hairpiece, golden weapons, grappling hook, cake
