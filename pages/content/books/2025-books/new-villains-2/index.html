Overview

Not to be confused with the magazine issue.

New Villains[note 1] is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 3 book released in Germany on February 12, 2025. It includes minifigures of Cinder and Nokt.

Official description

Official description[]

LEGO® NINJAGO® - The Awakening of the Ninja is an exciting set that stimulates imagination and fosters creativity. It includes the minifigures 'Cinder' and 'Nokt', allowing players to immerse themselves in the thrilling world of NINJAGO. This set is ideal for children aged 6 and up and offers an entertaining way to develop stories and experience adventures. The combination of building and play opportunities makes it a valuable addition to any LEGO collection. The high-quality pieces are durable and allow for the creation of various scenarios, enhancing interactivity and play value. The set is not just a toy, but also a tool for promoting problem-solving skills and teamwork.

  • Includes the minifigures 'Cinder' and 'Nokt' for interactive play
  • Promotes creativity and problem-solving skills
  • Suitable for children aged 6 and up.
