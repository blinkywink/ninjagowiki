Overview

LEGO Ninjago World Builder is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising book. It includes Arin and a four-in-one model.

Official description

Official description[]

Get creative with this LEGO® NINJAGO book, minifigure, 150 bricks and sorting tray – and build and play your way through fun-filled LEGO NINJAGO worlds.

What will you build first? With the 4-in-1 model included, there are four ways to build. Find instructions on how to create a Ninja car and rebuild it into a rideable dragon, a cool mech, or a temple scene complete with the Gong and Mallet of Shattering.

Where will you go? Your Ninjago minifigure Arin and his baby dragon companion will guide you through fun-filled LEGO® NINJAGO locations. Will you choose to journey to the heart of Ninjago City or party in the carnival-like Crossroads? Or will you fly to the scorching lava-tides in the Wyldness?

What will you build next? LEGO NINJAGO World Builder is packed with more than 150 inspirational model ideas – from a bustling city market to a beautiful temple – to build and expand your LEGO NINJAGO locations, using the pieces that come with the book and your own

LEGO collection.

Where will your story take you? Discover questions and fun challenges throughout to inspire play and storytelling.

Includes a sorting tray for storing your bricks and mini builds.

Return to this book again and again to expand and play in your LEGO NINJAGO worlds – or create whole new ones!
