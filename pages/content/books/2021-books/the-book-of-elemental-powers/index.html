Overview

The Book of Elemental Powers is a canon Ninjago book that was released in September 2021. It contains information about the Elemental Powers that have appeared in the TV series.

Official description

Official description[]

  • LEGO® Ninjago® remains the best-selling LEGO brand for books!
  • This book will be promoted in the 10th anniversary LEGO NINJAGO celebrations
  • A must-read for existing and recruiting NINJAGO fans with exclusive backstories, secret content, brand new illustrations, and Easter eggs[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This book was released after Seabound and before Crystalized, so it only has information up to the end of Seabound.
  • Some of the quotes for the Elemental Powers that were used on the book (Fire, Energy, Water, etc.) were used on this wiki at the time of the book's development.
  • When talking about how the ninja tamed their original dragons, the book refers to what Jay: Ninja of Lightning claims happened.
  • Should this book be considered canon, it would confirm Decoded is canon, as some of the quotes used for the elemental powers mention the Ninjigma.
  • In "A Big Splash," there is a similar scroll that shows a complete list of the Elemental Powers.
    • However, the scroll does not contain a single mention of Water or Wind.
  • The original description of the book stated that a 2021 season would cover the last elemental power. However, neither The Island nor Seabound mentioned such a thing. This book itself also contains no mention of a final elemental power.
    • A tweet regarding the 2022 season from Tommy Andreasen confirmed that the last Elemental Power is still unknown.[2]
      • The next series, Ninjago: Dragons Rising, reveals multiple new Elemental Powers. The "last elemental power" is likely a scrapped concept, as elements will likely continue to be revealed as the universe is further expanded.
  • Wojira's powers, Wave and Storm, are listed as elemental powers in this book despite not being elemental powers. This is because, according to Tommy Andreasen, the "book is written by Jake … a young boy in Ninjago. That book is an account of his understanding of how things work."[3]
  • This book can be found on Google Play.

Errors[]

  • It was mentioned that Jay broke his leg during one of the rounds in the Tournament of Elements, even though he actually broke his leg by having a painting fall on him.
  • It was implied that the only Elemental Powers that the First Spinjitzu Master couldn't command were Water, Storm, and Wave. However, in the show, Wind is also an element that the First Spinjitzu Master couldn't use.
  • This book states that Nyad was Nya's ancestor. However, Tommy Andreasen later confirmed that they are not related.[4]
