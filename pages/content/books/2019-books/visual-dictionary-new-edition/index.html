Overview

Visual Dictionary New Edition is an updated version of The Visual Dictionary. It now contains information up to Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu, including The LEGO Ninjago Movie. A version of Teen Wu with a golden staff is included.

Official Description

Official Description[]

Spin through the LEGO® NINJAGO® world with this fascinating visual guide

Spin through the LEGO® NINJAGO® world with this fascinating visual guide. Discover NINJAGO City with Lloyd, Kai, Cole, Jay, and Nya and meet their dragons and foes, including the Vermillion and Sons of Garmadon.

Explore the amazing sets, vehicles, mechs, and weapons. Look around the S.O.G. headquarters, examine the awesome Ninja Nightcrawler and Destiny’s Bounty, and learn all about the art of Spinjitzu.

Discover how the awesome LEGO NINJAGO sets and minifigures are created in the “Beyond the Brick” chapter, which features concept art and an interview with the LEGO NINJAGO design team. With the LEGO NINJAGO: Visual Dictionary, you will find out everything there is to know about the LEGO NINJAGO world.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Before it was formally revealed, the cover image was of the ninja along with Garmadon and Mr. E, as the book was only set to have information from the pilot episodes until Sons of Garmadon.[1]
  • This book first revealed that the Ice Emperor made the winter in the Never-Realm harsher, colder, and eternal.
  • Neither the Oni nor the events of March of the Oni are mentioned or acknowledged in the book despite its covering events up to Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu.
  • This book says Chopper Maroon goes by the name "the Maroon Mask," revealing he was one of the masked Sons of Garmadon members who broke into Borg Tower at the beginning of "The Mask of Deception."
  • The version of Teen Wu included with this book did not appear in Hunted, but his design is similar to the de-aging Wu, who appeared in the later-released "Golden Hour."
