Overview

Secrets of the Ninja is a Ninjago activity journal that was released in 2019. It comes with a Resistance Cole minifigure.

Official description

Official description[]

Want to protect the world of Ninjago against its villains and join the brave ninja team? Then this book is for you! Complete with a Cole minifigure and packed with comics, activities, word puzzles, and more, this Ninjago adventure is not to be missed!

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The included minifigure gift is identical to the one from Into the Fire.[1]
  • As a matter of fact, this book seems to be a journal variant of Into the Fire.
