Play Stories is a Ninjago: Dragons Rising activity book that was released in 2023. It comes with a Lloyd minifigure.[1]

Description

Description[]

Kids can unlock a new world of play possibilities for their Sora’s Transforming Mech Bike Racer (71792) set with LEGO® NINJAGO® Play Stories. This 48-page softcover book is designed to be read with the set to inspire kids’ imaginations with stories, activities, comic strips and play ideas to enhance their experience. The book also features 28 accessories and pieces including weapons, a toolbox and a chicken leg, plus a Lloyd minifigure, so kids can expand the adventures with their existing set.

  • LEGO® NINJAGO® play ideas book – Kids can boost their experience with their Sora’s Transforming Mech Bike Racer (71792) set with a book packed with stories, activities, comic strips and play ideas
  • Lloyd minifigure and ninja accessories – The book comes with 28 ninja accessories and pieces that allow kids to role-play action with their existing set and build a Lloyd minifigure
  • Dimensions – The book measures over 9 in. (22 cm) long and 6 in. (15 cm) wide
Summary

Summary[]

Swap and Drop[]

One day, Arin and Sora were bored. In an effort to relieve their boredom, they decided to try out each other's hobbies! As Sora began baking a pie, she remarked that it was harder than it looked and decided to try using Spinjitzu to bake faster. Unfortunately, she lost control and her pie flew through the air and landed into Lloyd's hands while he was training Riyu.

Meanwhile, Arin noticed that Sora's mech needed fuel and he reasoned that chicken wings could serve as fuel for the mech since it was fuel for him. Unfortunately this caused the mech to explode, hurling its remains to where Lloyd was, only barely missing him. Afterwards, the two friends decided to just stick with their own hobbies from now on.

Just Desserts![]

While training together, Arin and Sora encountered Rapton who immediately began firing from his sword's blaster configeration at the two teenagers. In response, Sora called for her mech, which had been storing Arin's pies. The teenagers then began pelting the dragon hunter with pies until he retreated.

Dragon Double-Cross[]

One day, Arin and Sora tried feeding Riyu his favorite foods, but to their concern and confusion, he refused to eat any of them, instead choosing to chew on their metal serving trays or tools the teenagers were using. When the dragon bit Sora's robot arm however, she shook him off and he landed beside Lloyd and...another Riyu??? Not soon after, the trio realized that the first Riyu had been a robot decoy created by Rapton, who was planning his next attack on Ninjago City. After destroying the robot decoy, the real Riyu sought out Rapton and spit up a pie at him.

Humble Stumble[]

Arin declared himself as the greatest ninja ever. In response, Sora pointed out that Lloyd had been teaching them that ninja were supposed to be humble. Upon hearing this, Arin clarified that he was the "greatest, humble ninja ever," leading Sora to point out that he was clearly not understanding the meaning of "humble." In response to this, Arin declared that his ninja skills would prove his claims and began spinning while balancing a teapot atop of a bo staff. Unfortunately, Arin lost control and the teapot smacked into Sora's mech, triggering its transformation function. Fearing that he had broken it, Arin first apologized for his actions until Sora clarified that this was normal. Upon hearing this, Arin proclaimed that he was the greatest ninja ever because he transformed Sora's mech.
