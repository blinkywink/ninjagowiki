Overview

Secret World of the Ninja New Edition is a Ninjago book that was released on October 3, 2023. It comes with an exclusive Samurai Lloyd minifigure.[1]

Official description

Official description[]

Step into the action-packed world of LEGO? NINJAGO™ and discover everything there is to know about the legendary Ninja!

Are you ready to unlock the secrets of the NINJA? Join Lloyd and the Ninja on an amazing journey of discovery. Learn the truth about the Ninja's incredible powers. Uncover secrets about mysterious locations. Revisit ancient history and fierce battles. Get up close with great vehicles, mighty mechs, fantastic dragons and much more in this must-have guide to the NINJAGO world.

Comes with an exclusive Lloyd LEGO minifigure, making it the perfect gift for LEGO NINJAGO fans.[1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In 2024, a variant of the book was released in select stores in the UK, containing the Kimono Lloyd minifigure first included in the original Character Encyclopedia, instead of the Samurai Lloyd included in prior releases of the book.

Errors[]

  • Nadakhan was said to have been imprisoned in the Teapot of Tyrahn by Soto for 500 years, even though "Pirates vs. Ninja" mentioned it to be 200 years.
  • In the pages pertaining to Crystalized, it stated that the infectious crystals were Vengestone, despite certain moments in the season that contradicted the assertion, such as the substances' availability and powers.
  • Bolobo's name is incorrectly spelled "Bolobu".
