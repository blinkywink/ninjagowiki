Battle for Ninjago City is a comic from the LEGO Club Magazine November-December 2014 Issue.

Plot

Plot[]

Zane soars over New Ninjago City in his glider as the Golden Master and his Nindroid Army march on to the Ninja Temple. Zane warns the other ninja and the battle begins: Lloyd, Jay and Nya fight Nindroids while Zane flies above. Nya battles the Golden Master and Zane declares that they will never give up.
