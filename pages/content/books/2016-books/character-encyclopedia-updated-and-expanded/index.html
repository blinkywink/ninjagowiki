Overview

This article is about the 2016 edition. For the 2012 edition, see Character Encyclopedia. For the 2021 edition, see Character Encyclopedia New Edition.

Character Encyclopedia Updated and Expanded is an updated version of the Character Encyclopedia. This edition contains information on the minifigures and dragons up to Skybound.

A then-exclusive Stone Armor variant of Jay was included, as well as a then-exclusive Starfarer comic on a printed 2x2 tile.

Official description

Official description[]

Explore the world of LEGO® NINJAGO® and the characters and vehicles in this #1 New York Times bestseller that contains an exclusive Jay minifigure and 48 new pages.

Also a Wall Street Journal bestseller and a USA Today Best-Selling book, LEGO NINJAGO: Character Encyclopedia, Updated Edition is now expanded with brand-new LEGO NINJAGO minifigures and sets, making it the ultimate book for your little ninja.

Read each character's ninja file to learn what makes each one special and unique, and find out which weapon each ninja uses. From Ninja to Nindroids to Sky Pirates, each character has a brilliantly-photographed page filled with amazing facts and trivia, such as which minifigure is the rarest and which minifigure can be found in the most LEGO NINJAGO sets.

Join the Masters of Spinjitzu and discover the secrets of the vehicles, weapons, and locations that make up the LEGO NINJAGO world with LEGO NINJAGO: Character Encyclopedia, Updated Edition.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The book states that Chen, back when he was training Garmadon and Clouse, "wanted to defeat Master Wu and for evil to rule all." In "Spellbound," however, his younger self is depicted as someone who causes conflict for fun but does not have a specific vendetta against Wu or a plan for evil to rule all.
  • The books states the dragon from 70602 Jay's Elemental Dragon is the Hydroelectric Dragon rather than the Lightning Dragon.
  • The pages for the Morro's dragon and Master Wu's dragon are subtitled "Possessed Elemental Wind Dragon" and "Elemental Creation Dragon" respectively.
    • The former contradicts Tommy Andreasen's statement that Morro's dragon was a corrupted version of the Energy Dragon,[1] which would later be supported by the Character Encyclopedia New Edition.
  • Though the book covers information up to Skybound, the endpapers picture the ninja in their Honor robes, which would not appear until the following wave for "Day of the Departed."
    • This was also the first product to feature Jay's new face print.
  • The image on the OverBorg page has an image of Cyrus Borg identical to the image used on a former version of his page.
  • The Starfarer comic piece would later appear in 70620 Ninjago City and 71799 Ninjago City Markets.
Errors

Errors[]

  • In the set gallery, 70505 Temple of Light is given 2504 Spinjitzu Dojo's image.
  • On Lasha's page, "headpiece" is misspelled as "headpiee".
  • On the page for Cole ZX, it shows the Tread Assault though the captions refer to the Ultra Sonic Raider.
  • On Airjitzu Zane's page it states he only appears in 70742 Airjitzu Zane Flyer, despite him also appearing in 70730 Chain Cycle Ambush.
  • On Skybound Jay's page it lists his dislike as "Making a wish!", despite Jay making wishes for personal gain and being the ninja who had to use his final wish to defeat Nadakhan. Though this could possibly imply that Jay had regretted making the first selfish wish and only used his last one because it was needed to beat Nadakhan.
