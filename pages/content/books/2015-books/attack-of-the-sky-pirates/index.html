Overview

Attack of the Sky Pirates is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - Skybound activity book released in 2015.

Official description

Official description[]

The action continues with all your favorite ninja! This activity book comes with a LEGO(R) NINJAGO(TM) Minifigure and is packed with stories, games, and activities about the Masters of Spinjitzu.

Plot

Plot[]

Lights... Camera... Action![]

Chronological order
Previous Next
The Realm of Shadows "Back in Shape!"

The director describes a scene to Kai and Cole in which the heroes must defeat two bandits. Kai says he understands everything and, immediately after filming begins, uses Spinjitzu. In doing so, he accidentally breaks nearby equipment and sends Cole and the two actors flying. The director says the red ninja overdid it a bit.

Be Careful What You Wish For[]

Chronological order
Previous Next
"Infamous" "Ninja Hunt"

Calmly confronting the Djinn and casually noting Nadakhan's aroma of tea due to his imprisonment in the Teapot, Wu decided to test out his luck by wishing no one would be harmed in their fight, and in response Nadakhan moved their fight momentarily out of the time stream. Satisfied, Wu attempted to defeat the Djinn, but when Nadakhan used his teleportation and speed to evade him, Wu wished to have the power to defeat his opponents. As a result, he became so powerful that he would be able to destroy the planet in a single blow, making any attempts to destroy Nadakhan a disaster in that it would kill everyone else. After pretending to almost sneeze, scaring the Djinn, Wu humbly conceded defeat and wished it all away, being trapped in the Teapot of Tyrahn alongside Clouse.

The Third Wish[]

Chronological order
Previous Next
"Enkrypted" "The Last Resort"

Dogshank complains about using up all her wishes, and Clancee says she can grant wishes, just like a genie. Dogshank wishes for a cool bath, so the Serpentine pours a bucket of water over her. Dogshank complains about being wet, and Clancee rips off the pirate flag so she can use it as dry clothing. An angry Dogshank makes a third wish—she wants Clancee to run really fast, because she plans to catch him and teach him a lesson.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • One of the stories shows Wu's three wishes after the events between "Infamous" and "Public Enemy Number One."
    • He wishes that no innocents would be harmed in his and Nadakhan's battle, that he would have the power to defeat Nadakhan, and, ultimately, that it all would go away.
      • Nadakhan's twist on Wu's second wish is that the library is put one second out of the timestream for the duration of their fight. This is similar to the status of the Time Ninja from Stone Cold, who is one second ahead of everyone else and is also an enemy of Wu.
    • When asked about Wu's wishes in an unrelated question, Tommy Andreasen responded, "I never pursued that. Nadakhan would have been clever to trap one as wise as Wu."[1] This suggests he is unaware of this story.
    • Due to this scenario and dialogue in the comic having some inconsistencies with the series, canonical status of this story is arguable.
