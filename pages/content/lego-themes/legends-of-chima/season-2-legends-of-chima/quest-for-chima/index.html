Plot

Plot[]

Plant Attack![]

In the Outlands, Cragger, Rogon and Gorzan sees plants and discuss what type of plants are they. However, Laval, who was captured by the said plants, interrupted them and wanted them to save him.

Packing It In[]

While packing for the Outlands, Gorzan points out that his bananas are missing. Seeing this, Eris gets annoyed and notes how this is getting ridiculous. Then, the rest of the Heroes of Chima arrived, unhappy that their stuff has been stolen again. Razar questioned why someone would want to stop them from making Chi flowing from the Mount Cavora again, which Eris responded by saying maybe it's the tribes who wanted all the Chi for themselves not so long ago, looking at Cragger and Worriz. However, Cragger blamed the Lions, saying that maybe they want to be sure that when the Chi starts flowing again, it flows their way. Hearing this, Laval told them that's enough and said they need a plan, asking for ideas. After hearing the ideas of Eris and Cragger, Laval asked if anyone has any good ideas, which in response Worriz said they could set up a trap. Thinking for moment, Laval got a better idea and told his friends that he needs some special help for it.

The Heroes of Chima spent the rest of the day wondering what Laval, who disappeared with a box, was going to do. Later, Laval came back with the box, which was now damaged and had jagged holes in the sides. Laval said how this box is really valuable and he would hate it if this box disappeared, making a big show putting the box with the other stuff packed for the trip.

Just after the sundown. Laval and Cragger took up position behind some bushes and started waiting for the thief. As Cragger was getting tired of waiting, they heard someone coming. Then, they saw a shadowy figure in the dark, who made his way to Laval's box and started to open it. However, the thief started to scream after smelling the smell that was coming from the box. Laval and Cragger started running, with Laval saying this was his plan and catching the shadowy figure, who was revealed to be Razcal. Then, Skinnet slipped out of the box, saying how Laval told him if anyone besides him opens the box, he should do "the thing". Razcal revealed that he was stealing stuff because he thought there were some things nobody would miss, saying that he added to his pile of loot. As Laval warned him about stealing their stuff, Cragger pointed that how stealing from your friends really "stinks", while waving the skunk odour away from his snout.

Help![]

After Worriz's argument with Laval, he and Rogon finds himself lost in the Outlands. They call out for help, only to get ambushed by the Scorpions and Spiders. Thankfully, the other Heroes of Chima shows up and attacks the Crawlers, saving their plants.
