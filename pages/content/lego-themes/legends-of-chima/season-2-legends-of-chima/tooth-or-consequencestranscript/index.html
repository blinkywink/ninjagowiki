(Cragger's dream)

*howl*

Crominus: Cragger...

Cragger: dad? are you there? *follows the voice and sees Crominus walking away.* wait! dad! come back!

*Crominus disappears from red red*

Cragger: how willl I ever find you?...

Crominus: *reappears* I am closer than you think, you just have to open your eyes.

(Outlans - Lavertus's lair - inside - night)

*Cragger wakes up to see Lavertus staring right at him and screams in shock*

Lavertus: AH!

Cragger: what are you doing? and... Why are you so close to me?

Lavertus: I am not close. Or am I?

Cragger: *pushes Laverts away.* cut that out!

Lavertus: sorry, heard you shouting in your sleep, just trying to help. Or was I?

Cragger: hey! you don't talk to anyone else like that here! why me?

Lavertus: I don't know what you're talking about. Or... Do I?

Cragger: that's it, I've had enough of this! my father's nearby and I have to find him.

Lavertus: what? no, he can't be. I mean, you can't, it's just a dream.

Cragger: what's that?

Lavertus: *chuckles awkwardly while hiding something behind his back.* it's nothing.

Cragger: it looks like... *pulls out into the open a pink scarf.* The scarf my mother used to have?

Lavertus: oh, sorry! you can't have seen this on your mother, it's very, very old.

Cragger: there's a painting of her in our castle when she was young, wearing a scarf EXACTLY like that.

Lavertus: oh... Really? Well, uh... Okay, maybe it belonged to your mother, a long, long time ago. But it's not like it's my most prised possession in the entire world or anything, that I keep locked in my secret room and only bring out in the middle of the night when no one is looking, so I can "talk" to it. Not at all! sorry, it's a lie. (whispering) The boy can never know our secret.

Cragger: huh? what are you talking about?

Lavertus: oh, uh... Nothing! hey, didn't you say your father was nearby? maybe you should check on that. *walks away.* Okay, great! bye bye.

Cragger: y'know, maybe it's not such a bad idea to take a look around. *runs past everyone to the outside where there's an ongoing howl*

(Outlands - night)

*howling*

Cragger: a howl. Just like my dream. Dad! are you here?! *races through the Outlands*

(Outlands - Lavertus's lair - inside - night)

*the mother tooth strangely begins to glow and causes Worz to wake up*

Woriz: the legends true! the mother tooth shall be our light in the darkness, it shall show us the way! *picks it up.* the wolf beast is near! *excitedly goes to the outside*

(Outlands - night)

*without knowing, Woriz follows Cragger through the same path but eventually Cragger tackles him from the bushes*

Cragger: give up, outlander! struggling will only make it worse!

Woriz: are you serious?! *kicks Cragger off*

Cragger: *laughing*

Woriz: now quit messing around croc, my wolf beast is near, see!

Cragger: the big tooth told you that?

Woriz: it's the mother tooth! and... Yeah.

Cragger: look. I don't know about your funky fang there, but my father spoke to me in a dream, so I KNOW he's here!

Woriz: uh... Let me get this straight. You think the blah blah in your dream is somehow more incredible then my sacred glowing mother tooth?

Cragger: uh... Yeah! well... Now that you put it that way. Hey, why don't we work on this together?

*Woriz agrees and they both go together, hiding away once they find a whole bunch of Crawlers*

Cragger: (whispering) can you turn that thing down a bit?!

Woriz: (whispering) *puts the mother tooth away so its brightness doesn't get them caught.* sorry.

(Outlands - Lavertus's lair - inside - night)

*everyone who stayed back is asleep snoring*

(Outlands - night)

*while the crawlers travel through the Outlands, a howl is heard which makes Woriz excited*

Woriz: LEGEND BEAST!

*a scorpion hears him and goes to check the bushes, luckily the two heroes duck from being seen*

Cragger: (whispering) what's wrong with you, Woriz?! they almost found us!

Woriz: (whispering) but the wolf beast is here,

wolf legend beast: *growling while it fights against the crawlers, causing its mouth to get forcefully shut by webs*

Woriz: oh no!

wolf legend beast: *growling*

Scorm: can't you keep it quiet?! or do I have to do everything myself? *stings the beast*

Woriz: *screams while he tries to go help the beast but is held back by Cragger*

Cragger: (whispering) not now, Woriz! there's too many of them!

*the wolf beast struggles more and ends up howling once Scorm stings it again*

Woriz: *silent scream*

Cragger: (whispering) calm down, Woriz! we can't help anyone if we get discovered!

*the beast falls into submission once its eyes go green, pulling the cage with Crominus in it, with Cragger immediately taking notice*

Cragger: DAD! I'LL SAVE YOU! *tries to go after them but is pulled back by Woriz*

Woriz: did you not just tell ME to calm down?

Cragger: *tries to go help Crominus but is held back.* but it's my father!

Woriz: I know! but we can' ep anyone if we get discovered!

Cragger: who says?

Woriz: uh... You did, about a moment ago?

Cragger: *throws Woriz away.* doesn't matter! I have to-

*Plovar flies into Cragger's mouth and starts pecking his teeth*

Plovar: oh Craggy-wag! I can smell your delicious teeth from across the jungle! how I've missed you!

Woriz: what's he doing here?

Plovar: oh, it was so awful! I was kidnapped, dragged to this savage land, and then... Forced against my will... To do dental work! *cries*

Woriz: wait. You love pecking teeth.

Plovar: not this time. I had to fix the fang of their DREADFUL queen!

Cragger: what?! they can't do that!

Plovar: and between you and me, that's one creature who needs a lot more than dental work, if you know what I'm saying.

Woriz: I never know what you're saying. But can say it quieter? I hear someone coming.

Plovar: okay, but, the only thing that spider's queen of, is the uglies.

Woriz: shh!

Spinlyn: who exactly are these uglies?! and why am I their queen?!

*some more scorpions approach from behind and they end up capturing them into the cage, where Cragger has a family reunion with his father*

Woriz: nice going Plovar!

Cragger: *hugs Crominus.* dad!

Crominus: Cragger!

Cragger: I missed you so much! I'm just so happy we're together again!

Crominus: me too, son. Oh, how I've longed to see our family and tribe once more. Tell me! is everyone okay?

Cragger: yeah! they're all great! well... Crooler... y'know...

Crominus: you didn't listen to HER did you?

Cragger: maybe a little! but it all worked out! eventually.

Crominus: and, your mother?

Cragger: she's fine, Lavertus rescued her.

Crominus: who?!

Cragger: Lavertus? also known as ShadoWind? there's a gang of us out here looking for you and the legend beasts. He's been, helping us out.

Crominus: you say, he "rescued" my queen?

Cragger: well... Yeah.

Crominus: *turns around angrily.* no. Not Lavertus!

Cragger: I know what you mean. Kind of a strange guy. Wait! you know him?

Crominus: once. Long ago.

Cragger: were you friends? because he's been really strange to me.

Crominus: I'm sorry son. I never meant for you to find out about any of this.

Cragger: find out about what?

(Outlands - Lavertus's lair - inside)

Laval: LAVERTUS! when did they leave?! and why didn't you stop them?!

Lavertus: I thought they'd be fine, but then I looked through the periscope.

*Gorzan looks through the periscope and sees the transportation*

Gorzan: they're almost in cave land, dudes!

Laval: we've gotta move!

Lavertus: woah! hold on. You can't just rush into the cave again.

Razar: why not, my friend? it worked the last time, yes?

Lavertus: but they'll be expecting you with more guards, and speedorz too.

Eris: he has a point, Laval. We had speed and surprise on our side last time.

Laval: so what?! we just "leave" our friends there?!

Lavertus: no! you prepare. I took the liberty in upgrading some of your weapons while you slept.

Gorzan: *pulls out his war hammer.* uh... Looks the same to me, dude. huh? *pulls something and the hammer's fist launches itself and punches the wall.* woah! that fist is far out!

Lavertus: *puts a banana in a circle shaped holder.* I also added a convenient banana holder, in case you're hungry.

Gorzan: *peels the skin and eats the banana from its holder.* sweet!

Lavertus: and, for you birds, an extra blade on each of your fighting staffs.

*Eris and Razar pull out their weapons to see they're extra spiky*

Lavertus: as for you Laval. Check out these chi powered stick grenades. *hands him two chi grenades which Laval packs on his back*

Laval: woah! I didn't know you had chi.

Lavertus: oh, uh... I scavenged some from the scorpions last time we fought them. But here's the most important item. *hands to Laval a black sword and shield*

Laval: a royal valious sword?! there are only a few of these in existence!

Lavertus: four, to be exact. They're among the strongest weapons in all of Chima.

Laval: but... How'd you get this? only the lion royal family have these.

Lavertus: go! your friends need you! now!

*everyone rushes outside except for Laval*

Laval: you're not coming?! it's not just our friends and the legend beasts, king Crominus is there too!

Lavertus: Crominus?! never! *marches away*

Laval: huh?

Eris: weird. But we need to go! now!

*they all leave, Lavertus giving a cold glare before shutting the door*

(scorpion caves - inside)

*all the crawlers get ready for battle while the leaders of each tribe has a tiny meeting*

Scorm: our forces shall squish the invaders here, in honour of the great scorpion in the sky!

Spinlyn: oh please. Everyone knows it was the great SPIDER in the sky who dropped that chi down here for us.

Scorm: non sense! there is only ONE creator, the great SCORPION!

Spinlyn: spider!

Scorm: SCORPION!

Spinlyn: SPIDER!

Braptor: *hisses softly as if he's asking the two to stop*

Scorm: *raises his tail.* SCORPION!

Spnlyn: AH!

*Spinlyn shoots her webs at Scorm who catches it with his tail and stings Braptor, mind controlling him along with have him tangled in Spinlyn's web*

Woriz: these bugs really "bug" each other don't they?

Crominus: sometimes I think they destroy each other, given the chance.

Cragger: well then, maybe there's a way we can give them that chance.

(Outlands - scorpion cave - outside)

Laval: there are a lot more guards then last time, and more traps too.

Gorzan: dude, if only we had some chi.

Laval: *shows an orb of chi.* only one orb left.

Rogon: fear not my bold collegues. (something scientifically worded)

Laval: I think he said he found a boom boom double boomer in his truck!

Gorzan: coolness.

Rogon: it is immensely powerful. I shall use this (something) to procure both chi and transit-

Bladvic: *yawns to sleep*

Rogon: into subterranean chamber yonder.

*Rogon shoots a blast that's so loud that even Bladvic wakes up, then destroying the gate up ahead, all the crawlers nearby charging to battle*

*Rogon shoots again*

Sparacon: chi up! defend the- *is shot with Rogon's blast and the chi falls everywhere*

Gorzan: now! *charges into battle on top his gorrila beast*

Laval: woohoo!

*all the heroes charge into battle and one by one they plug the scattered chi*

*the crawlers charge towards them but are easily dealt with. Sparacon and another then holding a tangled web made bag right in front of Gorzan*

Gorzan: dudes, how about a little punch?

Sparacon: I'd like to drink some punch!

*Gorzan extracts the fist from his weapon and punches some crawlers along with getting them trapped under their own web, quickly eating a banana before leaving*

Sparacon: I'm still thirsty.

*on top of the crocodile legend beast, Laval is left to battle nothing but scorpions*

Scutter: this will only sting, A LOT!

*Laval blocks all the scorpion's tails and goes back to the fight*

bats: *screeching while they come to reinforce their allies*

Rogon: experience the preposterous figure of my interpolarity infactuator. *shoots down all the bats*

Gorzan: go boom boom double boomer!

Laval: Bladvic! nap time!

*Bladvic happily nods, him and the bear beast yawning and squashing a few bats while going to lie down*

Laval: sleep tight! don't let the big bugs bite! *chuckles before he and the rest of the crew go inside the cave*

*some more crawlers go to help their squashed bat friends but the bear beast rolls to the other side and pushes all of them over*

*Laval heads for the cave but is interrupted by bats firing from above*

raven legend beast: *crowing while it pushes a single bat vehicle off the radar*

Razar: change in flight plans, my friend!

*with the help of Eris and her eagle beast, they easily dispose of the bats and allow Laval to enter the cave*

(Outlands - scorpion cave - inside)

Scorm: it appears our guests have arrived. Let's give them a very warm and painful welcome! *rides with those on speedorz to battle.* grab your chi on the way out, time to SQUISH!

*all the crawlers grab a chi strapped belt and while riding through the cave, they end up plugging it, meeting Laval and the heroes further up ahead*

Laval: just get past them, I'll handle the rest! *grabs his chi powered grenades and throws them on the ground.* LOOK OUT! COMING THROUGH!

*the heroes charge right through the crawlers, leaving the grenade on the floor about to explode*

Scorm: we've got them trapped now!

*while Scorm chases after the heroes with his army, the grenade's explosion gets rid of most of them and traps them under rocks. With another grenade thrown behind, the rest of the crawlers are blocked off by falling rocks due to the next explosion and leaves a brave Scorm to carry on*

crawlers: *hissing while they wait for the heroes to come out of the green mist from the cave's corridor*

Laval: FOR CHIMA!

*the heroes charge into a massive battle with the enemy, the eagle beast grabbing a spider and tossing it right next to the cage where Cragger, Crominus and Woriz are trapped in*

Cragger: come on! we've got to get out there and help them!

*the spider charges into battle again*

Woriz: I have an idea. Remember that funky fang? *places the mother tooth back into the wolf beast's mouth of which it came, snapping the beast out of the scorpion's control, letting it bite off the chains around its neck, Spinlyn taking notice*

Spinlyn: huh?

*the beast breaks them all out of the cage by smashing the door, Woriz taking a seat on it and watching as it goes to join in the battle, Laval going to reunite the crocodile beast with Cragger*

Laval: your turn! *hops off the beast and lets Cragger and Crominus get on*

Cragger: thanks, Laval!

Laval: and it's nice to see you're safe, sir.

Crominus: none of us are safe yet, especially the lion beast. We heard it roaring-

*a giant blast is shot and blocks the path to where the lion beast is presumably inside*

Cragger, Crominus and Laval: *gasp*

*Scorm and Spinlyn hiss at the heroes before they all open fire and cause a big threat*

Laval: *roars*

Cragger: they're too strong! get to cover!

*all the heroes retreat and shelter themselves where the blasts can't hit them*

Laval: how do we fight those things?!

Cragger: we get them to fight eachother. *hops off his beast and runs to the distance*

Scorm: cease fire! no one hit the great scorpion!

Spinlyn: I said it was the great spider.

Cragger: actually, it was neither!

*all the heroes get confused*

Cragger: you wanna know who really dropped that chi into your cave?

Scorm: this should be entertaining. Sure, why not? tell us!

Cragger: it was a great lion!

Scorm: what?!

Spinlyn: oh please.

Cragger: no really, I was there. There was a time when we crocs and lions couldn't agree on anything, especially when it came to chi.

Laval (flashback from episode 3): THAT'S IT! YOU'RE NEVER GETTING OUR CHI!

Cragger: this one lion was so intent on keeping those orbs away from us, he threw our chi into the Gorge Of Eternal Depth.

Laval (flashback from episode 3): whoops!

Cragger: but the gorge is not eternal, as you all know. It leads down here, into your cave system, where a few innocent creatures first tasted the power of chi. And now, here we are. No great spider, no great scorpion, just this lion, Laval!

*as expected, all the heroes are surprised*

Laval: *sighs*

Gorzan: dude, you mean all this is your fault?

Laval: uh... Well... Sort of?

Cragger: so instead of some scorpion or spider, you should be building a statue of a great lion! all hail, Laval!

Scorm: that's the most ridiculous thing I've ever heard! SQUISH THEM ALL!

Cragger: huh? *quickly goes back into hiding once the shooting restarts, joining his companions with Laval staring eye to eye with him.* hey, it was worth a try.

Laval: let's just get into that fortress and rescue the lion beast. Come on! *leaves but notices no one's following him.* Hey! what are you waiting for?!

Woriz: uh... Sorry, I'm still trying to process this. YOU threw the chi down here that created these overgrown bugs?!

Laval: uh... Yeah? well... Big mistake! but I'm trying to fix it! come on!

Razar: so, "my friend", you did nothing to tell us about this?

Laval: well, I mean... It never came up!

Rogon: preposterously!

Scorm and Spinlyn: huh?

Razar: I maybe a cheater and a thief, but you, "my friend", cannot be trusted.

Laval: I'm sorry. But I didn't think it would cause all this trouble!

Eris: things were different then, we just didn't know-

Woriz: you and Cragger both knew about this?!

Eris: uh... Yeah? sort of.

Gorzan: so not cool.

Laval: look, I understand you may not wanna trust me, or follow me into that fort, but the fact is, we can't save Chima, unless we get the lion beast too! I messed up, but I'm trying to make it right!

Woriz: we need a moment to discuss this.

*all the heroes huddle together and whisper, until Laval sees an opportunity and takes it by running into the open*

Cragger: hey, where's he going?

Eris: Laval! wait!

*the enemy immediately open fires at Laval as he heads straight for an opening, but right when he gets there, the rocks above are shot down and Laval's fate is left unknown*

Cragger: Laval! noooo!
