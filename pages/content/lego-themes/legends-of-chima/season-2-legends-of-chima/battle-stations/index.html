Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The comic shows a Persuader Plant growing in the Outlands, although they were previously described as growing only at a specific place at the Crocodile Swamp. This can be explained by the fact that the Outlands weren't previously explored by Tribes of Chima, so they didn't know about Persuaded Plants growing there.

Errors[]

  • In the first page of the comic, Razar's appearance is same as Razcal's.
  • The flowers in the Gorilla Village are drawn as Persuader Plants, though are supposed to be regular flowers.
