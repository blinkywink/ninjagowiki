Chima Falls/Transcript

Chima Falls
For CHIMA!

(Lion Temple - outside)

*the episode starts where it was last seen. All the tribes blast at the black cloud causing it to flee*

everyone: *cheering in celebration*

*the waterfalls stop running*

Crug: huh? didn't there used to be more waterfalls? hm.. I guess i must've counted wrong. oh well. *cheers*

everyone: *cheering*

Laval: see, Cragger! working together, we can accomplish anything

Cragger: you maybe right about that, fur boy!

*the two see eye to eye once again until the tribes see the waterfalls dry up and are left shocked and speechless*

Cragger: *gasps*

Eris: w-what happened to the falls?...

Crug: yeah! aww, don't worry. You probably just counted wrong! yeah! *Crawley turrns his head around and his jaw drops seeing his observation was wrong*

everyone: *gasps silently*

*everyone is left totally speechless*

Laval: what?... how can that be?...

Cragger: NICE WORK, LAVAL! working together... *his eyes turn red.* WE DESTROYED MOUNT CAVORA!

Laval: no! just wait! the falls will be back! they must!

*nothing happens*

Lagravis: our precious chi water, gone?...

Cragger: without chi water, there's no chi. no power, no life force, no thing! EVERYTHING DEPENDS ON CHI!

Gorzan: dude. this is a major bummer!

Eglor: *calculates.* by my calculations, all of Chima will dry up and crumble within two full moons if the chi is not restored.

everyone: *left shocked, they all chat amongst each other*

Cragger: how could you do this, Laval?!

Laval: ME?! I was trying to SAVE Mount Cavora!

Cragger: THE LIONS HAVE TRICKED US AGAIN! they've made us destroy our only source of chi! THEY MADE US DESTROY ALL OF CHIMA!

crocodiles: *growling*

*every single tribe glares at, Laval*

Laval: no! it's not like that! and don't worry. If we're really in trouble, the legend beasts will help us.

Cragger: oh. That's his answer to everything. The legend beasts!

Laval: but they always return when Chima needs them most!

Cragger: yeah?! so where are your legend beasts now huh?! I'd say it's a pretty good time for them to show.

everyone: *chatter*

Laval: they're coming! just wait!

Cragger: we don't have time to wait! tribes of Chima! you can put all your faith in some old fairy tale! or, you can follow me! and make sure these "helpful" lions, NEVER HURT US AGAIN!

crocodiles: *only them agree with Cragger's suggestion for battle, charging towards Laval*

Lagravis: defend the city! defend the chi!

*the lions charge to the battlefield with Laval totally speechless*

Cragger: DESTROY THE LIONS!

*Lions and Crocodiles meet each other face to face, getting ready to shoot until*

everyone: *screaming while they lose balance from a sudden earthquake*

*all the tribes retreat to the forests, the lions going ack to their temple, Lagravis dragging Laval from the arm*

Lagravis: nature is unbalanced! the land is reacting! get to cover! now!

Laval: what have I done?...

(The Great Story)

Lagravis: It all started with a spark from above, and an earthquake bellow. Mount Cavora rose up, and changed us all. For a thousand ages, Mount Cavora was the heart of Chima, filling us with energy, and life. Yet some creatures stayed pure to their animal selves, and retreated into the Outlands. They say these legend beasts have incredible powers, and will return one day, when Chima needs them most.

(Lion Temple - roof)

Lagravis: there is no doubt in my mind, that one day, is now. The heart of Chima, beats no more.

(Forest)

*Laval rides at the front of a group of eagles and lions, Eris joining his side by turning her jet into a land vehicle*

Laval: any sign of the legend beasts?

*Eris wags her head*

Laval: but they've got to come. The great story promised. This is when Chima needs them most. Look again.

*the eagles try to fly into the Outlands, but are casted out by the predator plants*

Eris: we're trying, but.. The Outlands mist are too thick and dangerous. Those predator plants attack everything. I don't know how anyone can survive out there.

Laval: *sighs.* did you see anything?! anywhere?!

Eris: not in the Outlands. But we did notice an unusual gathering of wolves in the Crocodile Swamp.

(Crocodile Swamp - outside)

crocodiles: *growling*

Woriz: come on, Cragger! half the tribes in Chima blasted at that black cloud, including the crocodiles.

Cragger: yeah, but it was Laval's idea! he tricked us all into destroying our only source of chi.

crocodiles: *growling*

Cragger: now, who controls the last few orbs in the sacred pool? the lions! and they can use that chi to DESTROY US ALL!

crocodiles: *growling*

Woriz: come on, Cragger! I don't like the lions either, but destroying us is not their thing.

Cragger: better safe than sorry. We defeat the lions, we keep those last few orbs for ourselves.

Woriz: I don't know. We're kind of tired of fighting. Can't we all just share and get along for once?!

Cragger: NEVER! not when I have this. *shows them the pledge*

Woriz: *gasps.* THE PLEDGE OF THE PACK! but, we had that thrown into the Gorge! and nothing escapes the gorge!

Cragger: no! nothing, escapes, MY DESTINY!

Woriz: no, seriously. How'd you get it?

Cragger: I am more powerful than the Gorge. More powerful than you can ever imagine!

wolves: *chatter*

Crug: uh.. Didn't Laval just, give that pledge to, Cragger?..

Crawley: shh! boss is on a role

Cragger: I will use this great power to rule Chima. And now that I have the pledge, you wolves MUST assist me! whether you like it, or not.

*the wolves are left speechless*

*Laval and Eris are seen hiding behind a tree*

Laval: uh oh.

(Lion Temple - throne room)

*A given up Lagravis sits by the edge of an empty sacred pool*

*drip*

Laval: DAD! CROCS! AND WOLVES! PLANNING TO ATTACK! PREP THE TROOPS!

Lagravis: *sighs*

Laval: DAD?! what's wrong?!

Lagravis: everything I've ever believed.. About Mount Cavora, the legend beasts, and Chima itself. Has it all been a lie?...

Laval: no way! COME ON! WE NEED TO FIGHT!

Lagravis: but without Mount Cavora, and the legend beasts, we've already lost...

Laval: UGH! WE CAN'T GIVE UP YET DAD! I'M RALLYING THE TRIBES! NOT FOR ME! NOT FOR YOU! BUT FOR CHIMA!

*Lagravis says nothing, Laval understanding it's no use so he walks away*

Laval: hm.. in the meantime, Eris! you need to figure out a way to fix Mount Cavora. *walks away*

Eris: huh?! how am I supposed to do that?

(Mount Cavora - underneath)

Eris: beavers, we need your special skills now more than ever! you are the greatest engineers in Chima. And Mount Cavora, is broken.

beavers: FIX IT! FIX IT! FIX IT!

*Bezar brings over a table and rolls all over it some blueprints*

Bezar: uh.. simple enough for a parent job. Open up the mountain (something), replace the starter new hickey with a fixified replicator thingy, and up switch the fluid porch to maximum chi flow, and kapow wow hoo ha!

Eris: woah woah woah! This isn't some tank engine that you can just patch up! this, is Mount Cavora.

Bezar: right. *sweeps away the blue prints.* We'll need bigger plans!

*Bezar places a giant blueprint on the ground surrounded by everyone*

beavers: FIX IT! FIX IT! FIX IT!

Eris: did I mention, there's an impassable energy field around the mountain?

beavers: *chatter*

Bezar: it can't be that passable. Waterfalls made it through, right? So we just go through the waterfall holes. Here, here and here.

Eris: uh... theoretically, that could work! But reaching those waterfall holes, will acquire some pretty sophisticated engineering. Right?

Bezar: nah! we'll just use our giant slingshot

Eris: WHAT?!

*the beavers bring out a big slingshot*

beavers: SLINGSHOT! SLINGSHOT! SLINGSHOT!

*Bezar equips a safety hat and stretches the slingshot's rope backwards, getting ready for take off*

Eris: *gasps*

(Gorilla Forest - gorilla houses)

Laval: we need to work together to stop these crocs and wolves.

Gorzan: but dude! why the bummer dude? fighting again? so bogus.

Laval: we don't want this either, Gorzan. But Cragger blames ME for destroying Mount Cavora.

Grumlo: didn't they dude?

Gorzan: yeah, the dude did.

Laval: okay, yeah! I did get everyone to shoot at the black cloud, and the falls dried up. But I was only trying to HELP everyone! we can't just attack each other every time one of us tries to do something good, and it doesn't work out! right? DUDE!

Grumlo: doing good, is good dude. Even when it turns out bad.

Gorzan: yeah. We'll so totally be your battle buds dude.

Laval: yes! *fist pumps Gorzan and Grumlo*

Bezar: FIX IT! *comes crashing into the houses and onto the walls*

(Mount Cavora - underneath)

beaver 1: *stretches himself back and goes flying.* fix it!

beavers: *cheering*

beaver 1: *almost reaches Cavora but goes down.* fix it...

beavers: *chatter*

*another beaver goes to send himself flying*

Eris: are you guys sure about this? it seems dangerous. And I've heard you beavers don't have the best eye sight, which might explain why you keep MISSING, that enormous mountain right in front of you.

beaver 2: whatever are you talking about?

beaver 3: *is sent flying and is screaming while he goes down*

Eris: *sighs*

*another beaver goes to try out the slingshot*

Eris: *runs over.* WOAH! how about we try, another way of reaching Mount Cavor.. *trips over a crawling beaver and into the slingshot.* AHHH! *screaming while she goes flying accidentally*

beaver 2: she tripped over US, and she's the one complaining about OUR eye sight?

(Forest)

*Gorzan and Laval travel through the forest with their beaver friends, not knowing they were about to have some more*

beaver 4: fix it!

beaver 5: fix it!

Gorzan: dude! it's raining beavers.

*multiple beavers come crashing down, Gorzan looking up to see if there's more*

Eris: *screaming while falling*

Gorzan: and eagles too!

Laval: Eris?

*Eris crashes into a tree right by another beaver*

Gorzan: dude, this is the weirdest weather ever.

Eris: *makes a dizzy sound while she's helped up by Laval.* no.. more! someone else needs to deal with these beavers!

Gorzan: oh! I'll do it. Those dudes are awesome. To the mountain! *carries all the beavers back to the mountain*

beavers: fix it! fix it! fix it!

Laval: okay, Eris. But I need your help with the rhinos. They're one of the strongest tribes in Chima!

*they walk to Laval's tank*

Eris: and one of the most... unfocused.

Laval: you mean, dumb

Eris: I was trying to be polite.

*they hop into the tank and head over to the quarry*

Laval: that's why you're here. That, and your relationship with, Rogon.

Eris: *awkwardly laughing.* relationship! *continues awkwardly laughing.* you make it sound like we're, dating or something! *chuckles*

Laval: Eris! I just wanna make sure the rhinos are with us, not the crocs.

Eris: *awkwardly chuckling.* of course! whoever said anything about, dating... *awkwardly chuckling*

Laval: whatever. Let's just get there before Cragger messes with them.

(Rhino Quarry)

*It's already too late when the whole tribe is an audience to Cragger who has behind him something hidden*

Cragger: I'm sure those sneaky lions will try to convince you to join their side! but all they can promise you is... honor! and, glory!

*the rhinos don't like the sound of that*

Cragger: I on the other claw, can promise you...

*Crawley and Crug lower the red covering and reveal the hidden thing to be a giant pile of rocks*

Cragger: FREE ROCKS!

Rogon: free rocks? hoo! we're in!

rhinos: *cheering*

(Mount Cavora - underneath)

*the beavers come up with a new idea that they'll use a really big ladder to climb up to Mount Cavora. But even though it's tall, it's not tall enough. Multiple of them climb the ladder, but when they go up further, it goes unsteady and starts to wobble*

beavers: woah!

beaver 6: hold steady! almost... *falls off.* there!

beaver 7: don't worry! we'll help!

beavers: *multiple of them are flung into the sky.* fix it!

(Forest)

beavers: *screaming while falling towards Gorzan.* FIX IT!

Gorzan: woah! another light sprinkling of beavers. *holds his mech sized hands out to catch the beavers*

(Rhino Quarry)

*Laval and Eris walk towards the Quarry entrance, where Runk and Rukus are guarding*

Rukus: you're not welcome here lion!

Laval: what?

Runk: but you're welcome to leave! *chuckles*

Laval: ugh! we're too late! Cragger must've already gotten to them! it's all up to you now, Eris.

Eris: he's not my boyfriend, y'know?

Laval: just CHARM Rogon into joining us! you can do that, right? let's see your charming face.

Eris: *thinksk and ends up with an awkward looking smile.* how's this?

Laval: um... I... think it's... good?...

Eris: ugh... *walks over to Runk and Rukus and gives them a flirty face*

Rogon: *comes running and barges in between the heads of Runk and Rukus.* Eris! I can't believe you're here! and you look so beautiful too.

Eris: *chuckles.* oh Rogon! you're so sweet! can we come into your quarry?

Rogon: *pushes Runk and Rukus away.* oh no. You're not welcome here. But I'm so glad you're here.

Eris: oh! that's okay. Maybe we can just hang out here for a while.

Rogon: that'll be awesome. Now leave.

Eris: *chuckles*

Rogon: *chuckles*

Laval: ahem! *goes in between Rogon and Eris.* excuse me.

Rogon: not welcome here, Laval.

Laval: yeah. I heard. But by any chance, did Cragger tell you to say that?

Rogon: yes. But he also told me to keep it a secret. And one thing we rhinos do very well, is keep a secret. *chuckles*

Laval: ugh... uh... great job there, Rogon! and I'm sure Cragger gave you something really secret to keep this secret.

Rogon: oh did he ever! just look! *shows them the "free" rocks*

Laval: a pile of rocks?...

Rogon: it was so generous of him.

Laval: uh... You do know you live in a quarry, that is FILLED with rocks?

Rogon: yeah, but these rocks were free.

Laval: ALL ROCKS ARE FREE, ROGON! CRAGGER'S JUST TRYING TO TRICK YOU!

Rogon: he said you'd say that too. And them I'm supposed to do THIS! *grabs Rukus's club and bangs it at Laval, missing*

Eris: Rogon! what are you doing?!

Rogon: oh, I'm sorry, Eris. I didn't mean to hurt, Laval. I just made a secret promise to hurt, Laval. *chases after Laval*

Laval: AH! *runs away from getting hit.* ROGON! WILL YOU CUT THAT OUT!

Rogon: we can all still be friends right?!

Laval: ha! *dodges the club that nearly hits Eris in the face*

Eris: *screams to nearly getting hit.* ROGON! I can't be your girlfriend if you're gonna act like this!

Rogon: what?! you're my girlfriend?! for real?! THIS IS SO AWESOME! *continues trying to hurt Laval*

Laval: ah!

Rogon: SHE LIKES ME! SHE REALLY LIKES ME!

Eris: Rogon, stop! You need to quit swinging that big club around!

Rogon: *continues.* okay!

Laval: woah!

Eris: THAT'S IT! *leaves angrily.* I'M LEAVING!

Rogon: but!... but!... I'm just so excited!... This is very confusing. oh, I'm hurt...

Laval: so does that club! *runs away from getting chased even though Rogon's sad*

Rogon: aw! relationships are so complicated...

Runk: *chuckles.* stinky matters of the heart! *chuckles*

(Mount Cavora - underneath)

beaver 7: *tries to pole vault it up to Mount Cavora, but fails by falling.* FIX IT!

Bezar: next plan!

*the beavers bring a tall statue that resembles Spiral Mountain and is somewhat taller in the foundation. Multiple try to walk round and round until they reach Mount Cavora*

Gorzan: dude that's so... you know... dudes!

Bezar: and I suppose you have a better idea?!

*the beavers fall down from the imbalance, causing the whole thing to fall*

Gorzan: mhm. Totally.

*a few minutes later, Gorzan is seen holding onto two beavers down bottom, the beavers doing the same all the way up while Bezar climbs up all of them*

beaver 8: hm... whatever gave you this fix it idea, Gorzan?

beavers: fix it! fix it! fix it!

Gorzan: gorilla dudes do this all the time to fix our homes in the trees, you just need really strong arms.

beaver 9: really strong arms?! But beavers don't have really strong arms!

beavers: *it's shown they're sturggling heavily trying to hold on, so they fall down*

(Lion Temple - roof)

beavers: *screaming while falling to the lion guards, which cause them to flee*

Longtooth: incoming! BEAVERS!

lions: *screaming while they try not to get hit*

(Bear Village)

*Laval goes to a hug a half awake Bladvic, who accidentally squeezes him too hard*

Bladvic: best lions friends forever!

Laval: glad to hear that, Bladvic!... This fight won't be easy though... *holds in his breath as he's squeezed*

Bladvic: for you, we'd do anything! even our super special battle! *falls asleep snoring and Laval gently puts him on the ground to rest*

Eris: at least they agreed to help. And I didn't have to "charm" anyone.

*the two walk away*

Laval: yeah, about that. Did you really call yourself Rogon's girlfriend?

Eris: ex-girlfriend. And no, I don't wanna talk about it!

Laval: are you sure?

Eris: don't we need to be somewhere?

(Raven Town)

*Laval and Eris drive into an empty town*

Laval: y'know. I'm a very good listener.

Eris: changing subject, again.

Laval: but you! and Rogon!

Eris: hey. How about those ravens?

Laval: yeah. They're the last big tribe we need to speak to. If we can keep them from allying with the crocs, we may just be able to save Chima.

*they stop*

Eris: hello? anyone home?

Laval: strange. Even their planes are gone. *goes to stick a paper with writing on it onto a wall.* I'll just ask them to stop by the lion city to discuss, a "valuable opportunity". heh. That should get their interest.

(Lion Temple - roof)

*all the beavers slowly recover from the fall*

beaver 9: *walks over to Bezar and sighs.* I never thought I'd say this, but... Maybe there are somethings, like Mount Cavora, that can't be fixed.

Bezar: let's just hope one of those legend beasts shows up soon.

Lagravis: *silently sighs from above, hearing what he just said*

(Lion Temple - outside)

*Laval and Eris exit the forest and ride back to the temple*

ravens: *loud crowing while they fly past*

Laval: that's quick. I knew they'd be interested once they saw my note.

Eris: *looks back to see more ravens approaching, in a battle formation.* uh... That looks more like an attack formation.

Laval: WHAT?! *stops to hear the ground shaking.* oh. I hope that's an earthquake.

Eris: wrong again!

*charging to the temple, an army of crocs led by Cragger hits at an unexpected time*

Laval: CROCS!

wolves: *howling*

Laval: WOLVES! RHINOS! AND RAVENS! ATTACKING! ALREADY?! and no legend beasts yet?...

*a raven shoots at them and manages to hit their tank*

Laval and Eris: *screaming while they fly out and hit the ground*

Cragger: CHAAARGE!

*the two instantly run for their lives*

Laval: RUUUN!

*Cragger's giant army continues forward*
