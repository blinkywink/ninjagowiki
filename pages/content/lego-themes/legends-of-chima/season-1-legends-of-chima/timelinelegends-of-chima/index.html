Note: Due to the events of Legends of Chima being set in an unknown time period compared to the Ninjago franchise, the history of Chima receives its own page to avoid speculation.

The timeline is a chronological list of the important events that have occurred in the history of Chima.

Ancient Chima

Ancient Chima[]

  • The Mother Sun creates the Phoenixes.
    • 10.000 year ago, Fluminox is born.[1]
  • During the Hundred Year Moon, the skunks broke their wind to create a lush land.
  • During a previous cycle of evolution,[2] multiple tribes of anthropomorphic animals such as the Saber-tooth Tigers, Vultures, Ice Bears, Mammoths, Lions,[3] Leopards and Tigers, come into existence.

Before Season 1

Before Season 1[]

  • At an unknown point in time after the Great Illumination, a lion decided to keep all the Chi for himself, which resulted in earthquakes and other natural disasters.[4]
  • For a thousand years, the lions protected the Chi, and evenly shared it with the other tribes.[5]
  • At an unknown point in time, a bad feud occurred between the Lion and Croc Tribes.[5]
  • Centuries before Season 1, the Golden Chi race becomes a tradition.[6]
  • Reegull is born from an eagle and a raven, but is not wanted by either tribes.[7]
  • At some point after Skinnet's birth, the Skunk Tribe became gone from Chima.[8]
  • Before Laval was born, Lagravis and Crominus raced in Speedorz together.[9]
  • When Laval was a cub,[10] Lavertus fell in love with Princess Crunket.
  • Prince Crominus uses a Persuader Plant he received from Reegull to make Lavertus steal all the Chi, leading to his exile.[11]
  • In the Outlands, Lavertus becomes friends with the Legend Beasts.
  • After becoming king, Crominus helped the Wolf Tribe in their Alpha Troubles and the Pledge of the Pack is created.[12]
  • King Crominus and Queen Crunket lay two eggs, Crooler is born first, however, the near destruction of Cragger's egg lead to him gaining his parents' attention, leading him to become the presumed firstborn and the heir.[10]
  • During their childhood, Laval and Cragger watched Dom de la Woosh's races.[13]
  • Years before Season 1, Dom de la Woosh stopped racing.[13]
  • Laval and Cragger start racing in the Chi Day races, with one of the two always winning.[6]

Time skips

Time skips[]

  • In "Chima Falls" it is stated by Lagravis that Mount Cavora hovered in the sky for a thousand ages.
  • In Season 3, Flinx is stated to be thousands of years old. Due to him being a newborn at the end of the Hunter Wars, that would mean that it occured multiple millenia ago.
  • Weeks have passed between both times Cragger visited the Sacred Pool in "The Legend of Chima".
  • Due to Chi Day and the distribution of Chi occuring once a month, multiple months have passed in Season 1.
    • One month passed between "The Great Story" and "The Warrior Within".
    • One month passed in "Market Day".
    • One month passed in "The Biggest Race Ever".
    • One month passed in "Foxtrot".
    • One month passed in "The Chi Jackers".
    • One month passed in "Fake CHI, Real Trouble".
    • One month passed in "Reunion Gone Wrong".
