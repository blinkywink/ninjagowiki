(Eagle Spire - Library)

Eris: and finally! while we eagles live and work on all these rock towers, our greatest achievements always start here, in the library

Laval: this is amazing Eris. thanks so much for showing me around

Eris: it may not be your typical tribal village, but we call it home

*they take a walk, but Laval accidentally walks into Eglor who's carrying some stuff*

Laval: woah!

*Eglor drops a golden ring, Laval chasing after it before it goes outside through an open wall and down to solid earth below*

Eris: watch your step!

Laval: *stops just in time.* maybe you guys should invent some hallways for this place.

Eris: why? we don't need 'em. *flies down to get the ring.* woo! no hallways! no side walks! no roads! *grabs the ring and goes back up, not noticing a little bit of her feathers dropped.* and usually no visitors too!

(Eagle Spire - underneath)

*Eris's feathers continue to float downwards*

Cragger: *grabs the feathers.* do you think she saw us?

Crooler: don't worry brother, they suspect nothing. no one has ever attacked the eagles way up there before.

Cragger: *turns around.* attack the eagles? but Crooler won't they get hurt?! *coughs when he's mind controlled again.* excellent. *jumps in the front as a new crocodile helcipter takes fligt.* now they'll be completely unprepared for our new air force!

*Crawley flies the helicopter and attaches it to a crocodile ship*

(Crocodile Swamp - outside)

*Crawley stands in the crocodile swamp with a few crocs including Crug that have pilot caps on their heads, on the side being multiple yellow balloon looking things*

Crawley: crocs! this, is a bellow plant! and it only grows here, in our swamp! *grabs a bellow plant.* since we lack the air craft of other tribes, *sticks 2 bellow plants onto Crug's back.* we will use these bellow plants to fly! observe.

Crug: *floats above ground through the usage of the bellow plants.* wee! wee! wee! *has fun.*

Crawley: CRUG! JUST SHOW THEM HOW TO STEER!

Crug: yes sir commander Crawley!

Crawley: now be aware that the gas inside their bellow plants is extremely unstable. one tiny poke an-

*Crug touches one bellow plant and instantly the other explodes, causing him to fly all over the place and eventually make a splash in the water. that makes the troops of crocodiles really doubtful as they growl in fear*

Crawley: so, who wants to try it next?

Crocodiles: *scream and run away*

(Crocodile Swamp - throne room)

Cragger: ugh! no croc is gonna strap those bellow plants on themself now! *moans*

Crooler: *looks at Eris's feathers.* well, if our crocs wont do it, (Ripnik comes out of the shadows) there maybe a way to convince the wolves to give it a try.

Crooler and Ripnik: *chuckling*

(Wolf Camp - night)

*Ripnik sneakily flies in the wolf camp full of sleeping wolves, his scent is kind of picked up though*

(Wolf Camp - Combat Lair - night)

*the sneaky raven sneaks into the combat lair, home to a really shiny and sharp tooth the wolves like to call "the mother tooth"*

Ripnik: AH! *steals the mother tooth and puts in its place Eris's feather, sneaking off with a bit of a chuckle*

*Worriz and Wilhurt hurry to the Combat Lair and to the perch of the mother tooth*

Worriz: THE MOTHER TOOTH! IT'S MISSING!

Wilhurt: *picks up the feathers*

*every single wolf howls to this bad news*

Ripnik: *flies away while chuckling*

(Lion Temple - outside - night)

*hearing the mournful howls coming from the wolf camp, Eris and Laval come outside to listen and can only wonder*

Laval: what's up with the wolves tonight?

Eris: strange.

(Crocodile Swamp - outside)

*furious, Winzar, Wilhurt and Worriz are parked at the Crocodile Swamp*

Wilhurt: *is held back by Winzar.* WE HAVE TO ATTACK THEM ALL, NOW!

Cragger: because you lost a tooth?!

Worriz: *growls.* IT'S SO MUCH MORE THAN THAT CRAGGER! we believe every member of our tribe descended from the great mother wolf! (howling in a sketched backstory) she blessed us with the gifts of strength, instinct! all we have left of her is a single fang. this is the mother tooth! our most sacred relic.

Wilhurt: IT'S BEEN STOLEN BY THE EAGLES!

Cragger: the eagles? how can you be so sure?

Worriz: *shows them the feathers.* we found THIS in our lair!

*the 2 crocs chuckle to a job well done*

Cragger: those scheming wing bags! we'll help you get them! but it wont be easy to attack their sky fort!

Worriz: *growls.* we've been building a flying thingy.

Cragger and Crooler: WHAT?!

Worriz: it's not finished yet! and we only have one.

Crooler: *sighs.* that's a relief.. I mean, that's a really great idea

Cragger: how'd you build that? you're wolves, and wolves don't fly!

Woriz: we fight as one and we think as one, when we put our minds together we can do anything!

*Cragger and Crooler look at each other*

Worriz: but one flying machine is not enough! (Wilhurt and Winzar howl) and we can't wait another moment! without the mother tooth! *howls*

Cragger: *blocks his ears.* okay okay. enough with the howling! I get it, you need vengeance now. *looks at the bellow plants.* Fortunately I have just the thing you need

(Wolf Camp)

*in a jet, Laval and Eris make their way to the wolf camp to see what all the ruckus was last night*

Laval: *walks to the entrance with Eris.* hello? anybody home? heard some howls last night, (a wolf sees them and growls) and just wanted to make sure everyone's okay..

Eris: *sees an eagle drawing with a cross on it, meaning no eagles allowed.* uhh. I'm not okay with this

Wonald (wolf pup): *screams at Eris and jumps on her.* YOU STOLE OUR MOTHER TOOTH! *hits Eris on the head, luckily him not being that strong.* Ho! ho! ho! ha! ha! *is easily overpowered by Eris and held to the front.* give it back! you're just lucky I'm the only one here eagle!

Laval: uh.. where did everyone else go?

(Eagle Spire - underneath)

*down below, every wolf is equipped with multiple bellow plants hanging fro their backs*

Cragger: my troops are happy to provide all the support you need down here but, as far as floating up there with you...

Woriz: *shows a picture of the plan Crooler showed Cragger last episode.* just stay here! *drops a chain.* and pull the big chains when we drop them down!

Cragger: *takes and looks through the drawing.* oh don't worry. *handshakes Woriz.* we'll put those chains to good use.

Woriz: *growls with a grin*

*the wolves fly up to the Eagle Spire with their weapons ready*

Cragger and Crooler: *chuckling*

Crooler: another brilliant plan brother

Cragger: thank you! (Crooler opens a steel box's lid and inside having the mother tooth) they never even suspected who was really behind their missing tooth! *laughs*

Crooler: and the best is yet to come. not only will the eagles get the surprise of their lives, but the wolves, will, too

(Eagle Spire)

Eglor: *looks through his telescope.* according to my weather teller today should be partially cloudy with a strong chance of-

Winzar: *pops up in Eglor's telescope roaring at them*

Eglor: WOLVES! (the rest of the wolves show up) WE'RE UNDER ATTACK! HOLD THEM BACK WHILE I WARN THE OTHERS! *runs inside*

Winzar: *growling and tries to hit Eglor with his blade, but the eagle escapes. he then dispatches the bellow plants from his back and lands on the building's surface, laughing as 2 eagles stand intimdated*

(Eagle Spire - library)

Eglor: *runs past Ewald and Elkar.* WE'RE UNDER ATTACK!

Winzar: *gets in the library by jumping from the top of a library shelf, chasing after Elkar and Ewald*

Ewald: GREAT GIZZARDS! WE'RE SCHOLARS! NOT FIGHTERS! WORDS ARE ALL WE HAVE!

*the 2 eagles climb a shelf that falls on Winzar, luckily they escape by flying*

Elkar: see Ewald. words are all we need, especially if they're written in these extra heavy books

(Eagle Spire)

*more wolves arrive, chasing away multiple eagles back inside. Eglor is the only eagle for now to stand up for himself as he confronts Worriz and Wilhurt*

Eglor: *aims at the wolves and shoots*

Worriz: *reflects the blast.* GIVE US OUR TOOTH EAGLE! OR ELSE!

Eglor: huh? what am I, the tooth fairy?! *shoots again*

*Woriz ducks the blast that reflects from the wall and hits Wilhurt's bellow plants making them explode, allowing Eglor to flee*

Wilhurt: (high pitched voice) WOAH!

Worriz: (high pitched voice) ah! are you okay?

Wilhurt: (high pitched voice) yeah! what's wrong with your voice?!

Woriz: huh?!

*the 2 wolves growl to check out their voices*

Worriz: STUPID PLANTS! Cragger told us they're explodey, BUT HE SAID NOTHING ABOUT THE FUNNY VOICES!

Worriz and Wilhurt: (high pitched voice) *they growl and charge forward.* ATTACK!

*Eris and Laval return on the jet, blasting away at the incoming wolves*

Laval: woohoo!

Eris: *lands the jet by a bunch of eagle soldiers.* YES!

Laval: you guys hold them back, I'll call for reinforcements! *hops down and roars at the enemy, pointing his blade towards them and every eagle soldier rushing to the wolf army to engage in combat*

*Eris pops the bellow plants of 3 wolves (that including Wilhurt), all of them falling into the water down below*

*screaming from the falling wolves*

*splash*

Wilhurt: *rises from the water and spits it out*

*Eglor shoots down 2 wolves that go down howling and make a big landing on top of each other's heads*

Woriz: *points to the top.* UGH! to the top! that must be where the mother tooth is! ATTACK!

*multiple wolves head for the top howling*

eagle: they keep coming! there are too many of them!

*3 eagles meet the incoming wolves with blasters, shooting some down into the water like the rest*

(Lion Temple - outside)

*Lagravis approaches Longtooth who is looking right at the battle with a telescope*

Longtooth: it appears the wolves are attacking the eagles. with some kind of floating plant? THAT EXPLODES?!

(Eagle Spire)

*Laval roars for reinforcements*

(Lion Temple - outside)

Lagravis: LAVAL!

*even though they'd probably still help, hearing his son's roar made Lagravis know it's compulsory that they do, when he leads the whole tribe to the spire*

(Eagle Spire)

*Worriz and Laval look at the battle going on around them*

Wilhurt: they may have won the air, BUT WE'LL TAKE THESE METAL TO THE STREETS!

Woriz: ugh! *grabs Wilhurt's shoulder.* Wilhurt! they don't have any streets.

*3 wolves arrive, surprised that Worriz's statement is true*

*Worriz chucks up a grappling rock and begins to climb the wall*

Wilhurt: everyone, ropes and hooks! WE'LL CLIMB TO THE MOTHER TOO- *is hit in the head by a book from above.* ow! *growls at Ewald and Elkar for doing it*

Ewald: I just love a good book!

Laval: *points at a place a eagle takes a book to.*put it over here. yes! that's good! we need a couple of books here, to the left! (Eglor and Eris arrive) LIONS ARE ON THEIR WAY! but we'll need jets to fly them up here!

Eris and Eglor: *screech while they fly down to the parking area where they keep their jets*

(Eagle Spire - parking area)

Eris: strange.. I thought wolves headed here before, maybe they never made it

*the two walk over to a jet, only to get surprised that Eris was wrong about no wolves being around when a whole group of them blows up most the jets using bombs*

Eris: *leads Eglor to some jets.* OVER HERE! TWO FREE JETS!

wolf: *chuckles and throws another bomb*

Laval: *sees the cloud of red destruction and smoke.* ERIS!

*to Laval's relief, Eris and Eglor escape through the smoke letting out a screech*

Eris: *parks by Laval.* got any other ideas?

Eris: *laughs*

Laval: *hops on the jet.* and give me a ride! there's one other tribe who might be able to help us

Razar: you want "us" to fly your army on our planes into a battle zone?! now why would we do that my friend?

(Eagle Spire)

*the battle continues*

Worriz: okay, *lights a flare and waves it.* now the real fun begins.

Cragger: SECURE THE CHAINS! PREPARE TO PULL!

*the crocodiles drive their trucks that the chains are connected to, pulling the whole spire slowly downwards. the eagles scream in fright, both tribes not being able to hold as a wolf falls off and sadly lands in a not so good place, rolling down it*

*Eris and Laval arrive*

Laval: *jumps off the jet and cuts away a chain.* ha! *lands and goes to cut another..* ha! *Woriz blocks his sword.* huh?!

Worriz: not this time lion! *pushes Laval away with a leg sweep*

Laval: woah! *tries to keep his balance and gets too close to the edge.* hey! no fair! your anchor! *falls* WOAH!

Eris: is everyone okay here?! *gasps and looks at Laval falling*

Laval: *screaming*

Eris: *runs to the edge.* LAVAL! *flies down to save him.* I'll never catch him! unless. *plugs a chi and gains more speed*

Laval: *screaming, luckily his hands are caught by Eris.* huh? nice catch! wing girl!

Eris: I told you to watch your step fur boy

Laval: *chuckles.*

Worriz: *growls and notices Ripnik.* where did you come from?

Ripnik: ha! it doesn't matter! I have information you might wanna buy about our, *chuckles.* flying lion attack!

Worriz: what?

*3 eagle jets fly toward the spire*

Lagravis: can't you fly any faster?!

Razcal: hold on! *goes more closer.* okay! we're all clear for landing! full speed ahead!

*Ripnik waves a flare around*

Worriz: what are you telling your ravens?!

Ripnik: *shows a coin.* that you're another satisfied customer, *flips the coin multiple times.* and they should deliver the lions right to your ambush

Worriz: good! but how do we know you're not on the lion's pay roll too?

Ripnik: we are! *chuckles.* but only for air transport, not for secret ambush information.

Worriz: *sighs and looks away.* wait a sec. you're double and triple dealing everyone here!

*raven screech*

Worriz: *turns around.* hey! where'd you go? and where's my sword?!

(Eagle Spire - underneath)

*Eris and Laval spy on Cragger hiding in a bush*

Laval: huh?! wolves above, crocs below, *looks behind him.* and chains between them?! what's next?

*they notice Ripnik fly over to Cragger and show him Woriz's sword*

Eris: ravens? and is that Worriz's sword?

*the raven jets land on the spire's surface*

Lagravis: *growls and leads the charge*

*two wolves jump down and confront the lions with howls*

Lagravis: look out! it's an ambush!

(Eagle Spire - underneath)

Laval: OH NO! we've got to get back up there now

Eris: hold on. check this out!

*the 2 watch as Ripnik steals the mother tooth and flies off with it*

Laval: is that what I think it is?

Ripnik: *crows*

Eris: after him!

*they chase Ripnik*

Ripnik: *crows some more, taking the mother tooth to Rawzom and Razar*

Razar: hahaha! excellent work Ripnik! you stole the tooth, for the crocs, then you stole it from the crocs, now we can sell it back to the wolves. another highly profitable day

Razar: how can you say that?! we're about to lose some of our BEST customers!

Razar: I second that! and who will buy the mother tooth we stole, and re-stole, IF THE WOLVES ARE GONE?!

*Rawzom and Razar freak out by speaking gibberish like a turkey again*

Laval: wow, can you believe these guys?

*Cragger's troops and himself continue to pull the Eagle Spire down*

(Eagle Spire)

*battle cries and the sound of clashing*

Worriz: surrender now lions and eagles! THE SPIRE IS OURS!

Woriz: *reaches out to Windra.* WINDRA!

Lagravis: you should've known better then to trust Cragger, he could finish us all off now! no one can stop him.

Razcal: *flies toward the spire.* WOO!

Lagravis: ah, there are still some heroes. for Chima!

Razcal: FOR CUSTOMERS!

Lagravis: huh?

(Eagle Spire - underneath)

*all the crocodile's tanks ram into each other*

Cragger and Crooler: *are sent flying while screaming*

Laval: *growls and jumps at Ripnik with Eris.* it's over Ripnik!

*the 2 armies stand on their own sides, growling at each other*

Lagravis: wait, Woriz. before we fight again, how can we settle this?

Worriz: there will be no peace, until we get our mother tooth back! *nearly goes for a strike, but the mother tooth falls from the sky and into the middle of the 2 parties.* huh? *looks around, though he sees nothing he grabs the mother tooth and holds it high.* THE MOTHER TOOTH!

*every wolf rejoices in an everlasting howl, Worriz leading them away*
