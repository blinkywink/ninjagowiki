Balancing Act

(Lion Temple - parking area)

Laval: Thanks, Eris! We could never make these chi deliveries without the eagle's help.

(A bunch of lions stack the eagle jets up with bags filled of fresh out of the sacred pool chi. after that the jets take flight)

(Raven Town)

(Ravens squack while they use a big rusty sheet to catch the incoming chi.)

Laval: So far so good, Eris! We've flown CHI to almost every tribe in Chima! And the wolves can't stop us!

(Beaver Lodge)

(The fleet drop some bags of chi onto the beavers that send them rolling down the roof)

Bezar: I got it! I got it!

(A beaver screams as he's sent flying from a seesaw. Something wooden falls down broken)

Beavers: FIX IT! FIX IT! FIX IT! (they run outside and wave goodbye to the fleet while cheering)

(sky)

Eris: But those wolves already stole so much chi. they have way more then anyone else!

Laval: I know. My dad says that kind of recreates an imbalance in nature, and the land reacts.

Eris: A windstorm?!

Laval: Yeah! But it mostly causes earthquakes and stuff.

Eris: No! A windstorm! Straight up ahead!

(they're caught in a windstorm)

Eris: Hold ON!

(Laval tries to hold on and grunts when tiny little things fly into his face)

Eris: Can't see a thing!

Laval: I can!

Laval and Eris: WOAH!

(Fangs)

Laval: *screams while he tries to hold onto the jet as they reach the fangs*

*the jet's feet scrapes onto the land and eventually stops up ahead*

Laval: Nice landing there wing girl. (Stands up to a shaky jet losing balance.) Woah! (Goes off the edge and hangs onto the rope, but screams once he sees he's hanging right above the Gorge of Eternal Depth.)

(Crocodile Swamp - outside)

(Crooler makes her way to three little persuader plants with a basket.)

Crooler: Now, which one of you special plants will help me control my brother's, (shows the dead persuader plant.) sad little mind? (sees they're too little.) UGH! Too small. Can't you guys grow any quicker?! (picks them into her basket.) I have a king to control and a kingdom to rule. (goes to the fort)

(Crocodile Swamp - throne room)

Crooler: (approaches Crug and Crawley who are guarding.) Step aside, claw kissers!

Crawley: Sorry Crooler. King Cragger says you're not allowed in.

Crooler: What? But I just wanted to show him, *shows a persuader plant.* some new plants I found.

Crug: Ooh! Cool plants! Can we eat them? *grabs it*

Crooler: No! *reaches for the plant.* they don't work if you eat.. them...

Crug: *swallows the plant and breathes out the gas.* mmm

Crawley: *snatches the basket.* Tanks for the snacks Crooler! But we still can't let you pass.

Crooler: What?! And how do you plan to keep me out of my own castle?

*Crawley and Crug look at each other, then smirk*

(Crocodile Swamp - outside)

Crooler: WOAH! *is thrown into the dirt again*

*Crawley and Crug clean the dirt from their hands and go back inside*

(Fangs)

Eris: *pushes up her windshield and peeks out.* pull yourself up! I think I can keep us balanced.

Laval: *climbs up the rope.* ah! ah! *gets back on the jet and it starts to wobble*

Eris: slowly!..

Laval: we need to grab that chi. *goes to get a bag of chi*

Eris: Careful Laval!

Laval: Sorry Eris, but I can't let more chi fall into this gorge. (Reaches for the bag and gets it.) Nothing's been the same since I dropped the Croc's CHI in there a while back. (drops the bag and chases after it.) Woah! Get back here!

Eris: Ah! *runs to the other edge to keep her jet steady*

Laval: *reaches for the bag*

Eris: get back here now!

Laval: *looks back and nods, going to the other edge but that adds too much weight on that side*

Eris: woah.. Too much! *runs with Laval to the other side.* now it's too much here! *hurries to the other side again while Laval stays still.* okay, stop!

(The weight is equal and the jet stays balanced)

Eris: Perfect.

Laval: (looks back) Where's the chi?!

(the two look down in the gorge and see the bag on a slight piece of rock)

Laval: Okay, it's not too far down. can we fly in there?

Eris: Last time I tried that, I almost didn't make it out! there's some kind of powerful force in the gorge that, just pulls you downward! like the gorge doesn't want anything to ever escape!

Laval: Okay. in that case, I'm gonna need some help when I "climb" into it.

Eris: What?!

Laval: *stands up and roars to the distance*

(Gorilla Forest - gorilla homes)

(Gorzan peeks out his house.)

Gorzan: Uh oh! Sounds like a bummer in the Fangs. I'm on my way, lion dude!

(Gorzan hops out his window and onto his mech, making his way to the Fangs)

(Wolf Camp - entrance)

Wolf Guard: (a butterfly lands on his nose and he growls at it)

(Crooler shows up and luckily has the butterfly as a distraction to get inside)

Wolf Guard: *contnues to growl and grins once the butterfly goes away*

(Wolf Camp)

Worriz: well, what'd you expect him to do Crooler?! you betrayed him!

Crooler: Cragger's old news my dear Woriz. you and I make a much better team. *holds onto Woriz's arm like she's trying to flirt with him.* that was a pretty amazing raid at the lion temple don't you think? so, what do you say? shall we rule Chima together? partner.

Windra: *growls and grabs onto Crooler*

(Wolf Camp - entrance)

*both Windra and Winzar chuck Crooler out of the camp and go back in after cleaning the dirt of their hands*

Crooler: *rolls onto her back.* why does this keep happening to me?... *looks back in.* huh? *sees the chi and stands up.* but what's this? the wolves chi? *sneaks away*

(Fangs)

Gorzan: *shows them a washing line with some laundry on it.* strongest vine in all of Chima dude! *lowers it down.* it's totally righteous

Laval: *touches some underwear.* Uhh.. is that a pair of underwear?..

Gorzan: oh, yeah!.. *picks a pair of socks off the line.* I also used this vine as a laundry line.

Laval: Gorzan, I roared for you because gorillas braid the toughest ropes in all of Chima. Are you sure this will hold me in the Gorge?

Gorzan: sure! haven't lost a sock yet! *drops the sock into the darkness of the gorge.* bummer.

Laval: (sighs.) Just hold it tightly, please.

(Gorzan and Eris hold tightly onto the vine, Laval using it to quickly make his way down into the gorge)

Laval: *stops.* still got a good grip?

Gorzan: Dude! you're solid! (pushes out a big piece of rock from the ground and sends it in Laval's direction*

Eris: AH!

Laval: (dodges the rock.) Hey!

Gorzan: Sorry, dude! But you're still solid! ish...

(Gorzan's underwear drops from the line and right into his face.)

Laval: Ew! (takes it off.) Well, at least they're clean... (puts the underwear into his pocket and continues down, almost reaching the surface.) Almost there! just a little more!

Eris: what'd he say?

Gorzan: all I heard was, *imitates the wind*

Laval: (reaches for the bag.) DID YOU HEAR THAT?!?!

(Eris shakes her head)

Laval: IT SOUNDS LIKE THERE'S SOMETHING DOWN THERE! SHOUTING OR, CRYING! I CAN'T TELL! (forcefully bangs onto the side from a strong force)

Eris: What are you doing?!

Gorzan: I can't help it, dudette! The wind!

(Eris looks up to see an incoming windstorm.)

Eris: Gorzan we need to move fast! There's another storm on the way!

Gorzan: I'm on it, dudette. *presses some buttons in his mech*

Eris: uh oh. *peeks into the gorge.* LAVAL!!! HURRY UP!!!

Laval: hold it steady! I've almost got it! *goes more down and swings to the bag, failing to get it the first time*

*Gorzan holds onto the vine extra tight and braces for impact*

Eris: Laval! Can you hear me?!

*Laval continues to swing for the bag*

Eris: *sees the storm get closer.* WE'RE OUT OF TIME, LAVAL! HOLD ON TIGHT!

Gorzan: Oh! Dude!

Eris: PULL HIM UP! NOW!

Gorzan: I'M TRYING! Uh... Eris. Where'd you leave your jet?

Eris: *looks back to her jet and screams when she sees it rolling towards her, luckily ducking*

Gorzan: *is hit by the jet and pushes so far back he accidentally rips the vine.) Oh no...

Eris: LAVAL!

(Wolf Camp)

*Crooler sneaks in using a secret opening through their wooden gate. she sneaks past some wolves and makes her way to the chi*

wolves: *they surround Crooler while growling*

Crooler: *gasps*

Worriz: *approaches.* Crooler! did you really think you could sneak in here and drive out with all our chi?

Crooler: well, you've made it perfectly clear that we're no longer partners. so I see no reason to share any of the chi we stole together.

Worriz: CROOLER! we were never partners. and now, we're not even friends.

Wilhurt: *backs Crooler to the gate with the oter wolves and a rope at hand.* and you know what we do to those who aren't our friends!

Crooler: (sees something.) huh?

*another windstorm comes and blinds the wolves, allowing Crooler to quickly take off with all the chi*

Worriz: huh?

wolves: *growling*

Worriz: *notices Crooler take off.* DON'T LET HER GET OUR CHI!

*Crooler continues on but is immediately pursued*

(Fangs)

Eris: LAVAL! ARE YOU OKAY? LAVAL?!

Laval: *uses Gorzan's underwear to hang on but it starts to slowly slide off.* HELP! *tries to climb up but the underwear just stretches out more. he reaches for the edge but fails.* aw! so close! I could grab it if I just had the strength. if I just had some chi..

Cragger: (flashback) if I just had the strength. (struggles to save his parents.) If I just had...some chi...

Laval: You've taken a lot from us gorge. and now, I'm taking some back! *roars and uses the rest of his strength to jump towards the bag of chi*

*meanwhile, back up top Gorzan shields Eris from the storm*

Eris: Laval?

Laval: *having the bag on his back, he uses the chi and his blade to climb back up*

(Gorzan goes out of his mech to look back down with Eris.)

Eris: do you see him? is he okay? all this wind, I can't even tell where he climbed down..

Gorzan: Nothing. that's all I see Eris. I think he's gone!... gone! GONE! (his knees drop on the ground and he bangs his fist on it, crying dude continously)

Eris: What? No! He can't be!

Gorzan: *continues crying the word, "dude"*

Laval: *gets back up to the surface and approaches his two friends.* what's up with him?

Eris: *hugs Laval.* LAVAL!

Gorzan: *goes to hug Laval.* dude! dude!

Laval: good to see you too Gorzan! but I have some bad news for you. *shows him the stretched underwear.* these got a little stretched

Gorzan: *holds it.* those were my favorite pair.. *presses his face on it.* dude...

(roads of Chima)

wolves: *howling while they chase after Crooler*

*Crooler looks at the wolves. knowing she has no chance against them, she lets up a croc flare*

(Crocodile Swamp - outside)

Cragger: (approaches Crawley and Crug, just seeing the croc flair.) Who's shooting off croc flares? I thought we were through with all this fighting stuff. (sees Crooler's basket and sadly neither Crawley nor Crug ate the persuader plants, so he goes to analyze them.) And what are you doing with those little Crooler plants? (picks the remaining persuader platns and aims them at himself.) Wait. there's something inside this one. *accidentally mind controls himself.* hey! I! I! (turns around.) well! what are you two waiting for? that's a croc flare. we need to fight someone! anyone! NOW!

*confused at Cragger's sudden change of heart, Crawley and Crug just shurg, learn to accept it and nod. going to assemble the crocodile army*

(roads of Chima)

wolves: *howling*

Worriz: you can't out run us Crooler! you need to stop! right now!

Crooler: okay! *stops so the wolves behind her can crash into the truck*

wolf 1: AH! *crashes*

*two wolves go to check on the truck*

Worriz: *growls*

Crooler: hehe. *backs up and takes a short cut to the swamp*

Worriz: after her.

Wolves: *they continue after Crooler growling*

(Crocodile Swamp - outside)

*Cragger walks side to side as the last of his men arrive with their vehicles. he's then surprised to see Crooler arrive*

Crooler: *stands up inside the truck.* brother! you have to forgive me.. I know I messed up, but I hope, *shows him the bags of chi.* this will make it up to you.

Cragger: is that.. all the wolves chi?!

Crooler: yes. it's more than any other tribe in Chima. now it's ours. all, ours!

*Cragger smirks*

wolves: *after howling, they use grappling hooks to pull Crooler back*

Crooler: *screams*

Cragger: CROOLER!

Crooler: I LOVE YOU BROTHER!

wolves: *growling*

Cragger: Crooler?

(Crooler screams as she's thrown into the dirt again.)

Crooler: UIgh... Please make them stop doing that...

Cragger: *gets infuriated and watches the wolves leave.* oh don't worry. I'll stop them. I'M GOING TO CRUSH THEIR WORLD!

(Scene changes to the roads of Chima.)

*the trio walk somewhere, Gorzan holding onto his underwear*

Gorzan: Not happy!

*Eglor and Ewald arrive*

Eris: Eglor. I was hoping you'd find us! we didn't wanna risk flying again with those windstorms.

Eglor: I know! that's why we brought out the dirt gliders. hop on!

*Laval passes Eris the bag of chi as she hops inside, himself going on top, taking off into the forest*

(Forest)

Laval: if we move quickly, we can get the rest of this chi out to the other tribes before any wolves find us.

Eris: too late!

*the wolves show up right in front of them and they're forced to stop*

Laval: *growls*

*Eris hides the chi*

Worriz: *unfortunately sees the bag.* what an unexpected surprise

Laval: Hey! Is that the Chi you stole from us and everyone else in Chima?!

Worriz: Yes it is, lion. But don't even think about taking it back! With this chi, we're stronger. We're smarter! We're the new rulers of Chima!

Laval: Ugh! You don't get it, do you?! It's not about ruling Chima, it's about keeping it balanced! Bad stuff happens when you misuse chi!

Worriz: *sighs*

Laval: these earthquakes, windstorms, they're your fault!

Worriz: *sighs.* you sound just like your father sometimes!

Laval: I do? Really? Thanks! That's a compliment!

Worriz: Whatever! Just give me your chi and we can keep this meeting relatively civil.

Laval: What?! I just saved that chi from the Gorge of Eternal Depth! I'm not giving anything to you.

Worriz: Perhaps then.. you'll give it to them! (some more wolves show up and he points at them)

(the whole pack closes in on them)

Eris: Uh... now might be a good time to run!...

Laval: Eglor, can you get us out of here?

Eglor: Yes! (presses buttons) We just need to do a little "roadwork" first

*Eglor blasts a tree to create an opening, instantly going through it*

wolves: *howling while they chase after them*

*Ewald rushes straight foro Winzar and Wilhurt who are straight up ahead*

Winzar: he does see us, right?

*the two wolves duck, Ewald turning his bike into a type of flying vehicle and as he's in the sky he shoots both Winzar and Wilhurt off their speedorz*

wolves: *howling*

Laval: *sees a jet fly past.* woah! I want one of those

Eglor: JUST HANG ON! *blasts away another tree*

Laval: *screams as he tries to hold on*

*Eglor continues blasting away obstacles in front of them, Gorzan using his giant mech hands to throw trees into the sky and right towards Ewald*

Ewald: *dodges the incoming trees.* hey! watch it up there! *turns his vehicle back into a bike*

(roads of Chima)

Eglor: (stops.) Which way?

Laval: what?! all these fancy new gadgets, and you don't even have a map?!

*the wolves arrive*

Laval: guess we go that way!

wolves: *growling while they blast at them*

(cliff)

Laval: Woah! cliff, up ahead!

Eglor: We can't stop now!

*Ewald jumps off the cliff and transforms his bike back into a flying vehicle*

Laval: I hope, we can do that too!..

Eglor: just hold onto the cockpit! *ejects from the rest of his vehicle and goes falling down the cliff*

Laval: *screaming as he falls with it on the outside, getting surprised when they end up flying.* no way! that was awesome! but where's Gorzan?!

*Gorzan luckily escapes using his really super sized underwear as a parachute*

Laval: is that his, stretched out underwear?..

Gorzan: duuudes! told you these were my favourite pair

Worriz: GET THEM! WHAT ARE YOU WAITING FOR?!

Wilhurt: uh... giant flying underwear!

Worriz: *points to a truck.* try the alpha cannon instead! *hops on the cannon.* let's see if you can keep this balanced, lion! *grins as he aims*

*surprisingly, a blast from behind shoots down another wolf truck and makes it crash into a tree*

Worriz: huh?! *looks behind to see a whole army of crocs*

Cragger: *plugs a chi and screams.* SHOW THEM NO MERCY!

*the two tribes charge towards each other, howling and screaming away at the enemy while plugging chi*

Worriz: will you just give up already Cragger? with all our chi, we wolves are the new top dogs of trouble town

Cragger: HA! we have chi too! and you top dogs are more like the.. the.. puny puppies of poopy vile!

Worriz: huh? that doesn't even make sense!

Cragger: well, I.. I.. uh... ATTACK!

*a giant battle erupts between the two tribes*

wolves: *howling*

Laval: WOAH! WE'VE GOTTA HELP CRAGGER!

Eris: or! we get this chi to a tribe who really needs it.

Laval: yeah! I know what we're "supposed" to do! but the crocs need us! and..

*Eglor and Eris disagree with Laval's choice and boost away*

Laval: *tries to hold on.* woah!

*Cragger is the last man standing in his tribe, kicking away a single wolf but then having no choice but to surrender once he notices there's still a heap of wolves left and none from his except himself*

(party tent)

*somewhere in Chima, the gorillas hold a huge dance party inside a tent with Gorzan's underwear being the roof*

Gorzan: DUDES! PARTY IN MY DUDE'S TENT!

*every gorilla dances except for Laval and Eris on the side lines*

Eris: *clapping*

Laval: We should've helped!.. The wolves are way too powerful. I don't think the crocs can beat them!

Eris: (touches Laval's arm.) Relax, they'll be fine! besides, both those tribes have been jerks lately. and, didn't all their stolen chi upset the balance of Chima?

Laval: well, yeah! but still..

Eris: now they're using up all that that chi, so.. don't think of it as a battle. think of it as, a restoration of Chima's balance

Laval: wow Eris! you make, "getting your tail kicked by the wolves", sound like a really positive experience

Eris: I try. And you know what else is a really positive experience?

Laval: what?

Eris: this! *pulls Laval to the dance floor and they start dancing together with the gorillas while laughing*
