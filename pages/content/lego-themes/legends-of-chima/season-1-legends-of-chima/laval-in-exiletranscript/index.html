(Lion Temple - throne room)

Lagravis: exile, this is your penalty Laval. for stealing an orb of golden chi, exiled from this tribe and from every tribe in Chima. all shall be forbidden to speak to you. you shall be an outcast. do you have anything to say, Laval?

Laval: I.. guess I shouldn't count on being invited to this year's holiday party huh?

Lagravis: stop joking around, Laval! this is serious! no one in Chima will ever see, or speak to you again! including, me.

Laval: I know! and I really am sorry! but have to do this.

Lagravis: or! you could ask us for a pardon. explain how you only stole the golden chi to save your friends and-

Laval: I broke the rules! I deserve the punishment!

Lagravis: *sighs.* then let the exile, begin. *walks down his throne to Laval with the scroll.* you Laval, are forever banished from the tribes of Chima.

*Laval takes off his harness and sword, handing them all over to Lagravis*

(Lion Temple - outside)

*father and son hug for the final time*

Laval: don't worry dad. forever is not as long as you think.

Lagravis: you were just a cub, the last time we exiled one of our tribe...

Laval: you mean.. Lavertus?! I've heard incredible tales of that lion!

Lagravis: yes. Lavertus was once one of our greatest warriors! and my best friend! until.. well, bad things happened. but we haven't seen or heard from Lavertus in ages! I fear the same for you, Laval!

Laval: you can't exile me from your heart dad! that's where I'll be, if you ever need me.

Lagravis: *touches Laval's shoulder.* how'd you get so wise?

Laval: I had the best teacher ever. *walks down the stairs to all his friends awaiting to say goodbye*

Longtooth: you taught him well, Lagravis.

Lagravis: too well...

Laval: *looks back for a second and keeps on going down the city steps.* gotta keep smiling! then I'll be fine, if they see I'm fine!

Gorzan: *hugs Laval.* DUDE! YOU CAN'T LEAVE US!

Laval: thanks Gorzan!.. I'll miss you too! but right now, the thing I miss most, is.. air in my lungs!...

Gorzan: *lets go.* DUDE!

Laval: (whispering) *walks over to Bladvic who's holding a jar of honey.* Bladvic!

Bladvic: *wakes up with a gasp.* OH! LAVAL! *passes him the honey.* honey for your exile!

Laval: thanks Bladvic! I'll think of you when I-

Bladvic: *snoring*

*Laval shrugs and continues downward*

Rogon: *hurries to Laval with a rock.* Laval! I brought my pet rock! he'll keep you company in exile! his name, is Chip! I know no one can talk to you after today, but there are no rules against you talking to rocks!

Laval: huh?

Rogon: he's a very good listener

Laval: thanks Rogon! I'll take good care of your, *carries the rock and finds it to be heavy.* Chip.. *walks down to the most emotional to see him leave, Eris*

Eris: *sobs.* Laval!... I'll!... I'll!... *sobs.*

Laval: I'll miss you most of all Eris...

Eris: *cannot take it anymore and bursts out crying while going to hug Laval*

Laval: Eris, don't cry! *walks away with the song "horizon" beginning to play*

*Longtooth and Lagravis see the young lion off with sad expressions*

Finley: I wanna tell you the story of two friends in a land one step from paradise.

(multiple places)

*while walking, Laval waves goodbye to the beavers, wolves and ravens when passing through their territory. the beavers being the only ones to show respect and say goodbye except for the other two tribes who just walk past*

Finley: so far away from here. And I'm gonna tell you the story of our lives, lot of years flying high and protecting, the only friend that matters. Now something's broken the time. We'll fix everything, chase the light.

(Forever Rock)

*the most emotional part is when Laval goes to remind himself of the memories he had with Cragger by saying goodbye to the scores scratched into the Forever Rock whenever the two were doing some type of competition, only two put his hand on the crocodile head*

Finley: On the horizon we're flying, while, as we lead their lives. And we feel, and we scream, we believe, that we're not at home.

(Bridge)

*the song ends, Laval going over the bridge*

Cragger: and there he goes! GONE FOREVER! *laughter.* he even left his speedor back at the lion city! few more days of exile and then! we'll make our move!

*Ripnik flies away*

(Spiral Mountain)

Laval: *sits on the top of Spiral Mountain with a cup of tea.* well Chip! it's just you and me now, in the middle of nowhere. here's to exile. *slurps his drink until*

Skinnit: TOO EXILE!

Laval: *sees Skinnit approach with a cup of tea of his own.* Skinnit! what are you doing here?!

Skinnit: oh. I live here! the only place in Chima where no one is bothered by my, "special gift"

Laval: you mean your stinkies?..

Skinnit: that's what you call them down there. up here they're, the fragrant smell of happiness!

Laval: y'know! I'm in exile Skinnit! I don't mean to be rude or anything, but I'm supposed to live completely alone. *puts his cup down and grabs Chip*

Skinnit: hey! I have an idea! why don't we live alone, together?

Laval: but then we wouldn't be, "living, alone"! *walks away*

Skinnit: oh! I can't wait to tell the others about my new roommate!

Laval: *stops.* I'M NOT YOUR NEW ROOMMATE! AND WHO ELSE IS UP HERE?!

Plovar: ooh! Skinnit! did you say you have a new roommate?

Laval: aw! *runs away*

Skinnit: *chases after Laval.* HEY! WHERE ARE YOU GOING?!

(Lion Temple - Laval's room)

Lagravis: *stands next to Laval's bed and mourns over Laval's gear, sighing while pressing his fingers against Laval's harness. he then approaches Sir Punch A Lot.* yes. I miss him too Sir Punch A Lot. more then anything else in the world. *leaves in a sad way*

Ripnik: *hops inside once he sees Lagravis leave, taking Laval's sword and harness with a chuckle. before he flies out the window, he takes a robe just in case from Laval's wardrobe*

(Lion Temple - outside)

Lagravis: *walks over to the stairs so he can have a look around and mourn some more.* huh?! *sees a hooded figure riding Laval's speedor into the forest.* Laval?! *goes after him*

(Forest)

*the chase continues*

(roads of Chima)

Lagravis: LAVAL!

*the strange figure speeds up, eventually stopping and lowering his hood just a little bit*

Lagravis: *exits the tank.* Laval?! is that you?!

Cragger: *turns around.* GUESS AGAIN!

crocodiles: *Crug, Crawley and some others surround Lagravis from hiding with some growls*

Lagravis: what?! what's the meaning of this?! how'd you get Laval's sword and speedor?!

Cragger: *drops the hood.* a little birdy gave them to me!

Lagravis: *turns around to see Ripnik above.* huh? what?..

*Ripnik captures Lagravis with a net gun*

(Fangs)

Laval: *runs out of breath once he stops.* I think we lost him in the Fangs, Chip! now this, should be perfect place for my exile! not a soul around.

Skinnit: SO WHAT YOU WANNA DO NOW ROOMMATE?

Laval: *screams in fright.* HOW'D YOU GET HERE SO FAST?!

Skinnit: oh! I know short-cuts all over Chima! hey! since we're here, right don't we play a game together?

Laval: no! I'm supposed to be alone!

Plovar: ooh! I love games!

Laval: huh? UGH! NOT YOU TOO! STOP FOLLOWING ME!

Skinnit: hey! I don't know that game. how does it work?!

Laval: you two, stop following me!

Plovar: wait, how do you win?

Laval: *walks away.* when you stop following me!

Skinnit: okay! but you go first!

Plovar: yeah! stop following me Laval!

Laval: ugh! done!

Plovar: yay! woo! I win!

Skinnit: *runs to Laval.* YAY!

Laval: what?!

Plovar: I said stop following me, and you kept (something) along. so you weren't following me, which makes me the winner!

Laval: *growls.*

Skinnit: he hates that Plovar! let's leave him in his exile! *walks away.* come on!

Laval: finally!

Plovar and Skinnit: SURPRISE!

Laval: *screams in fright and turns around.* what are you doing?!

Plovar: think we'd let you win the "stop following me" game that easily, did you?

Laval: *growls.* IT'S NOT A GAME! IT'S EXILE! must, be, alone! *runs away.* I think we can lose them, Chip! if we just make it around this corner. *goes around the corner and ends up banging into ShadoWind.* ShadoWind?! *stands up.* what are you doing here?..

Skinnit: *catches up.* OH! ShadoWind! we're playing the most awesome game ever with our new roommate! wanna join?

Laval: NO! ENOUGH ALREADY! I CAME HERE TO BE, ALONE! AND NOW THERE'S EVEN MORE OF YOU HERE! THIS EXILE, IS WAY TOO CROWDED FOR ME!

*ShadoWind passes some type of scrappy scroll with green goo on the end of it*

Laval: what's this?! a-and why's it so, gooey?..

*ShadoWind drives away with no answers given, as usual*

Skinnit: hey! I recognize that sticky stuff! it's from a plant in the Outlands

Laval: how do you know about the Outlands? it's one of the most dangerous places in the world! no one in Chima ever goes in, or out of it.

Skinnit: I do! and it's not that bad! if you have, "the gift"!

Laval: *opens the scrolls and gasps.* the pledge of the pack?!

Plovar: oh my! that little old pledge forces the wolves to serve those scrumptious crocs. but I heard, Crooler tossed it into the gorge of eternal depth. and nothing ever escapes the gorge.

Laval: so.. how did it end up in the Outlands?..

Plovar: who knows! but! I'm late for a very important appointment. I enjoyed that fabulous game, way too much.

Laval: *sighs.* it's not a game...

Plovar: toodle-loo!

(Crocodile Swamp - throne room)

Cragger: so.. do you have anything to say for yourself, lion?!

Lagravis: yes! why do you hate us so much Cragger? I thought you and Laval were friends! we risked everything to save you from that fog! *that kind of effects Cragger*

Cragger: HA! *walks to Lagravis who is in chains.* the only thing he saved me from was enjoying MY TRUE DESTINY! A DESTINY YOU'LL NOW GET TO WITNESS FIRST HAND!

*Lagravis sees multiple crocs sharpening their spears and grabbing some blasters. for battle*

Lagravis: you're planning to attack us, again?! that's why you captured me?!

Cragger: with you in chains, and Laval in exile! there's no one to command your guards!

Lagravis: a leader will rise!

Cragger: but will they do it in time? *goes to his throne.* and once I crush your tribe. I shall control the chi and ALL of Chima! meanwhile, you can enjoy it all from the comfort of my deepest darkest dungeon!

Lagravis: CRAGGER!

Cragger: *laughing while his troops march out*

Plovar: *flies in and suddenly sees Lagravis in chains and the dungeon bellow.* OH MY! *flies faster to Cragger*

Cragger: *chuckles but gasps once Plovar goes in his mouth*

Plovar: oh Craggy Wag! do I have news for you? you'll never what just showed up in the Outlands!

Cragger: GET OUT OF MY MOUTH BIRD! *throws Plovar to the ground.*

Plovar: Craggy! what are you doing?

Cragger: *stands up.* THAT'S KING CRAGGER TO YOU! *grabs his spear and tries to hit Plovar, managing to pluck his tail feather's off*

Plovar: ah! I'm naked!

Cragger: NOW GO AWAY BEFORE I THROW YOU IN THE SAME DUNGEON AS THAT LION!

Plovar: really? a-are you serious?

Cragger: does this look serious to you?

Plovar: *screams while flying away.* why I never!

Cragger: AND YOU NEVER WILL, AGAIN!

(Crocodile Swamp - dungeon)

Crooler: *sobbing*

Lagravis: Crooler? is that you?

Crooler: go. away.

Lagravis: uh.. there really isn't anywhere I can go. dungeon, chains, remember? why is Cragger doing this?... what happened to him?

Crooler: you really want to know?

Lagravis: I have to know Crooler... our fate, and the fate of all of Chima is at stake here.

Crooler: well then, listen up! it's not a pretty story but.. it all begins with an egg. make that two eggs! that's Cragger, and that's me. I was always first everything and Cragger was always in my way! my mother was devastated when she lost her poor Cragger! so, when the little miracle baby survived, that's all anyone cared about. they even thought he was born first! and the first born of the king and queen, always takes over the crocodile tribe! I was like, "hello?! check the nest! I'm the first born here! the real heir to the throne!". but they only had eyes for their little miracle baby! I've been trying to make them see me ever since. in the beginning, I even tried helping my brother. but no matter what I did, my parents acted like they only had one child! miracle baby, Cragger! things only got worse for me and my fool of a brother. so, when my parents banished and Cragger became king. I finally had the chance to rule the tribe! I would make Cragger my little puppet! obeying my ever will! but then.. things went terribly wrong. now Cragger is just vengeful and mean, without anyone guiding him.

Lagravis: when you say, "guiding him". you really mean, controlling. don't you?

Crooler: I mean, reclaiming my birth right! I should've been the leader of this tribe! but that sneaky little fool tricked THEM ALL!

Lagravis: hm.. it's a bit hard to tell who's worse. you, or your brother!

Crooler: oh! what do you know LION?!

Lagravis: I know, I either have to deal with Cragger when he's under your control. or Cragger when he's completely out of control! either way.. I fear Chima, is doomed...

(Fangs)

Skinnit: *stares at the pledge with a giant appetite and his mouth waters*

Laval:*analyzes the pledge.* there's fresh scribbling at the bottom of this pledge! someone must've written on it while it was in the Outlands or in the gorge.

Skinnit: *licks the green goo on the pledge.* mmm!

Laval: hey! what are you doing?! that's Outland sap! plants there are notoriously poisonous!

Skinnit: *licks it three more times but Laval pulls it away.* notoriously delicious! *goes crazy while be bounces around out of control*

Laval: SKINNIT!

Skinnit: *is held still while screaming and it eventually ends.* ah.. that was great! *sticks his tongue out in a hungry way.* WANT SAP! NOW! *jumps for the pledge but misses thanks to Laval pulling it away*

Laval: ARE YOU SERIOUS?! THIS GOO MAKES YOU NUTS!

Skinnit: *licks his lips.* nuts...

Plovar: ah! I have never been so insulted in my life! that croc is such a, meany meany!

Laval: huh?! y'know I'm still in exile, Plovar?! NO ONE is supposed to talk to me!

Plovar: fine! then I guess I won't tell you how Cragger just captured your father. harrumph! toodle-loo! *flies away*

Laval: WHAT?! talk to me! now! *runs after Plovar with Skinnit following while holding Chip*

Plovar: Laval! come back! you'll need a lot of help to rescue Lagravis from the crocs! and no one in Chima's even supposed to talk to you!

Laval: guess I'll have to do this alone.

Plovar: oh. you're not alone!

Laval: *stops.* what? thanks Skinnit. but I can't ask you to risk your safety helping me.

Plovar: actually, I said.. you're not alone because you have your rock, Chip. *gives him back Chip.*

Laval: oh! I, uh.. thought you were volunteering.

Plovar: WHAT?! ARE YOU SERIOUS?! NO WAY! those crocs are going to DESTROY you!

Laval: *sighs and goes for the Crocodile Swamp, Skinnit following even with what he just said*

(Crocodile Swamp - outside)

Laval: *peeks from behind a log in the distance with a gasp, seeing too much guards and eventually, his speedor.* what's my speedor doing here?

Skinnit: *peeks.* I don't know! but since you and Chip are completely out numbered and unarmed. you mind if I finish off that sap, before they completely destroy you?

Laval: no!

Skinnit: aw! come on!

Laval: you've been asking me this since the Fangs. you know it makes you crazy! one taste, and you'll be bouncing all over the place! every guard around will come running! oh.. on second thought! don't just take one taste, take it all! *gives him the sap*

Skinnit: *a few minutes later, he distracts the guards by exactly doing what Laval said he'd do. bounce all over the place with crazy noises*

*every croc is interested and go to be an audience*

Crawley: wow! you don't see that everyday

crocodiles: *laughter*

*Laval swings by using a rope*

Crug: funky skunky! *laughter*

*Laval uses another rope to slide down and sneak to his speedor, grabbing his harness and sword just in case he has to do some fighting*

Crawley: *sees Laval.* huh?..

*Laval takes off inside with Chip and his gear equipped*

Crawley: LION!

crocodiles: *every one of them go after Laval while screaming*

Laval: HA! *swings inside the crocodile mouth with a vine.* WOAH! *grunts as he crashes down*

Crawley: TO THE BOATS!

Laval: *slowly recovers but gasps when he sees the crocs coming for him on their boats. luckily he sees the lever that shuts down the crocodile mouth so he grabs Chip and runs for it.* rock on, Chip! *throws Chip at the lever and it lands perfectly, the crocodile mouth closing*

crocodiles: *screaming*

*Crug and Crawlley are the only crocs to make it inside beause they jumped before the crocodile mouth completely shut*

(Crocodile Swamp - throne room)

*Laval charges inside*

crocodiles: *see Laval and they scream while charging at him*

*a croc pulls out a chi orb*

Laval: *sends the crocodile spinning and his chi flying, luckily he grabs it from falling.* I'll take that! *plugs it and lets out a roar, jumping into action.*

crocodiles: *growling while they surround Laval*

Laval: *growls at the enemies. beginning the real fight when he dodges a charging croc with a spear, easily eliminating him and some others*

crocodile 1: *screams at Laval*

Laval: *roars at the crocodile and charges at the croc.* HA! *tackles the croc and sends him flying into two more*

crocodiles: *screaming at Laval*

Crawley and Crug: *they attack from behind with spears*

*Laval ducks from Crawley's spear, which accidentally bangs right into Crug's face*

Crug: *sobs in pain and tackles Crawley to the ground, rolling away with him and fighting him for a second*

Cragger: ENOUGH!

Laval: keep them sending them out Cragger, and I'll keep knocking them down!

Cragger: ah! I'm sure you will! for a while! BUT YOU'RE STILL ONLY ONE LION!

crocodiles: *reinforcements arrive while growling*

Cragger: AGAINST OUR ENTIRE TRIBE!

Laval: *growls at the large change of numbers.* okay then! you have my father! let's make a deal.

Cragger: a deal?! what could you possibly offer, that would make me let you or your father go?!

Laval: just one thing! *shows him the pledge.* hope.

Cragger: the pledge! *walks to Laval.* where'd you get that?..

Laval: from the gorge!

Cragger: what?! nothing ever escapes the gorge! I should know! that's where you sent my parents!

Laval: accident! remember? but the good news is, the pledge made it out. your parents will too!

Crawley and Crug: *looks at each other.* huh?

Cragger: NONSENSE! there's no proof my parents are even alive!

Laval: except this new scribbling at the bottom of the pledge! "make us proud! miracle baby".

Cragger: *growls, but somehow, his eyes turn back to normal meaning the spell is somewhat broken.* my parents used to call me that when I was little

Laval: well! my guess is, your parents found this pledge in the gorge, and are trying to send you a message

Cragger: I.. I!..

Laval: they're alive Cragger! and they want you to know

Cragger: but.. how?...

Laval: I'm not sure. maybe we can, TOGETHER, to figure this out!

*the two crocs nod back and go to fetch Lagravis*

Laval: and you Cragger! *gives him the pledge.* you can have hope

Cragger: *growls and goes back to his throne*

(Forest - night)

*father and son reunited, return to the Lion Temple*

Lagravis: I can't you're here, Laval! freeing me, with nothing but a few words on an old scroll?

Laval: shh, dad! you're not supposed to talk to me! technically I'm still in exile. remember the rules?

Lagravis: oh come on, Laval! we lions need you a lot more then we need our rules! please, come back to us!

Laval: but dad!-

Lagravis: Cragger's planning a huge attack. and that pledge of the pack gives him control over the wolves again! he'll be even more powerful! do our rules really matter now?!

Laval: hey! you're the one who always say, without rules, we're nothing..

Lagravis: yes! but without you! we are even less.

*they stop for a minute*

Laval: okay! I'll help you fend off the crocs! but I still deserve my exile. it wouldn't be right for me to come home forever

Lagravis: thank you, Laval! and don't worry! forever's not as long as you think! or, so I hear

Laval: yeah, I heard that too somewhere

(Lion Temple - night)

*our two lion father and sons return home, where Eris is waiting*

Eris: *sits on the top of the city steps, still with a mournful expression. but spotting Laval in the distance coming home, puts a smile on her face.* Laval? LAVAL! *flies down the stairs and screams in extreme happiness*

*Laval and Lagravis stop their vehicles, Laval happy to see Eris*

Laval: woah! *is tackled to the ground*

Laval and Eris: *laughter*

*Lagravis can only smile at a warm reunion*
