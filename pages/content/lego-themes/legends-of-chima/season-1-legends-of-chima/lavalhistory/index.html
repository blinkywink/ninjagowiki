Possession

Possession[]

Curseworld, Part II[]

Laval was sitting on Spiral Mountain with Cragger, Bezar, Bladvic, Dom de la Woosh, Gorzan, Plovar, Rogon, and Skinnet when a portal created by the Realm Crystal opened in the sky which released Lloyd and Morro. Laval asked where they came from but before he could get a response Morro opened another portal with the Realm Crystal and passed through it with Lloyd.
