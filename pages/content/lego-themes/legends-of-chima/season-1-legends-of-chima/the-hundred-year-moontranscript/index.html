(Eagle Spire - night)

Eglor: *looks through his telescope and suddenly sees the moon slowly turning purple.* uh oh. *looks through it again*

*howling*

Eglor: *gasps.* oh my!

(Lion Temple - night)

*howl*

Lagravis: tell me again why the moon is turning purple.

Eglor: it's a natural phenomenon that occurs every hundred years or so

Eris: and it drives the wolves insane.

*the wolves howl again, intimidating everyone*

Laval: they're already dangerous.. how bad will it get?

Eglor: when the hundred year moon is full, they'll lose all self control. their primal instincts and rage will be unstoppable

Lagravis: and when does this full moon occur?

Eris: two nights from now.

*howling*

(Wolf Camp - night)

*after another howl, the wolves continue to stuff their stomachs with chicken legs in a savage manner, unlike Wanold who eats an apple very tidy*

Wilhurt: *sees Wanold eating the apple.* EW!

Woriz: yuck!

Wanold: what? it's just an apple

Wilhurt: DON'T YOU EVER EAT MEET?!

Wanold: nope! only fruits and vegetables. it's a very healthy diet y'know?

*Woriz and Wilhurt laugh*

Winzar: *approaches Woriz and clears his throat for attention.* someone here to see you Woriz, it's about the hundred year moon.

Woriz: huh?!

*Woriz and a few wolves meet Laval and Eris by the entrance*

Woriz: we're well aware of the special moon Laval! it's already caused some of us to lose, control..

Wanold: I'll say! I usually eat one basket of fruit, and today, *shows Laval a banana.* I ATE THREE!

Woriz: *face plants.* ugh! THAT'S NOT WHAT I MEANT WANOLD!

Laval: look! I know it's not your fault! but there must be something you could do to keep everyone safe!

Woriz: don't worry! we'll all be fine

Windra: *howls and ravages through the camp using one of the wolf trucks, her eyes purple just like the moon*

Laval: *sees Windra coming straight for them, he screams and gets out of the way like everyone else*

Woriz: WINDRA! *goes after her on his speedor and manages to get hold of the truck, having some trouble trying to hold on.* LOOK AT ME WINDRA! NOT THE MOON. *climbs to the front and looks Windra in the eyes.* WINDRA! FOCUS! *waves his hand to distract Windra from looking at the moon.* here!

Windra: *breathes heavily and slowly calms down*

Woriz: it's okay! you're in control! *reaches for the controls.* just breathe, and slow down.

*Windra snapped out of it, Woriz stops the truck in its tracks*

Woriz: that's good. see, you're fine! NOW GRAB HER!

*multiple wolves grab Windra and throw her on the ground where two more hold her down*

Windra: *growls angrily as she loses control again*

*Windra is taken away, Laval and Eris approaching Woriz*

Laval: is that going to happen to your "entire" tribe when the moon is full..?

Woriz: no. for some of us, it will be much, much worse!

Laval: WHAT?! YOU HAVE TO DO SOMETHING! AT LEAST WARN THE OTHER TRIBES!

Woriz: *thinks and turns his head to some wolves to see what they think*

*the wolves nod*

*in the morning, multiple wolves leave the camp and head different directions to go warn all the tribes*

(Beaver Lodge)

Winzar: just a friendly warning! we wolves will be a little out of control on the night of the full moon, so keep your doors locked

Bezar: a-are you gonna hurt us now...?

Winzar: no! I'm trying to help! this is my, *grins.* nice face. I'm actually smiling.

beavers: *they scream and retreat deeper into the building*

*Winzar leaves*

(Rhino Quarry)

Wilhurt: so just be somewhere safe on the night of the full moon!

Rogon: okay, just one question. what's a moon?

*Wilhurt nearly says something but changes his mind*

(Bear Village)

Wakz (wolf): *drives around with a speaker at hand.* DANGER! DANGER! STAY INSIDE ON FULL MOON NIGHT! I'M TALKING TO YOU BEARS! DO YOU HEAR ME?!

*snoring*

Wakz: *drops his speaker and sighs, going to Bladvic.* (whisper) danger danger, stay inside on the night of the full moon, I'm talking to you bears do you hear me?

Bladvic: *wakes instantly and jumps up.* WHAT?! WOLVES ARE FLIPPING OUT ON THE NIGHT OF THE FULL MOON?! WHY DIDN'T YOU SAY SOMETHING EARLIER! *runs to another bear.* I HAVE TO TELL THE OTHERS! *shakes the bear*

(Raven Town)

Razar: ah! this is very good news about the wolves my friend! *opens a rusty box.* since we just got in a brand new selection of, um.. *searches through the junk and pulls out a rusty lock.* hundred year moon locks! *gives them to Wanold.* it's guaranteed to be 100% wolf proof

Wanold: *breaks the lock easily without even trying.* uh.. you know I'm a wolf, right?

Razar: uh... *takes back the lock.* sorry.. wolf valid guaranteed when not touched by an actual wolf.

Wanold: huh..?

(Crocodile Swamp - throne room)

Woriz: so we're just warning everyone about our situation! *points.* and I'm warning you Cragger. don't try any of your little tricks that night!

Cragger: but Woriz! why would we ever try anything when you're so out of control and vulnerable?

Woriz: out of control, yes! vulnerable? NEVER! *growls*

Cragger: *gulps*

(Crocodile Swamp - outside)

*Woriz leaves*

Crooler: are you thinking what I'm thinking?

Cragger: uh.. stay inside and lock all the doors?

Crooler: no. I have a much more interesting idea, it involves, gorillas.

(The Gorilla Forest)

G'Loona: *hums while she goes to collect flowers*

Crooler: help..! somebody, help me...

G'Loona: hello? is some body hurt out there? *sees Crooler and goes to comfort her*

Crooler: *fake crying*

G'Loona: are you okay?

Crooler: *grabs G'Loona and chuckles*

*Crug and Crawley appear from behind and put G'Loona inside a bag*

G'Loona: HEY! *is taken to a truck with Crooler picking up her basket of flowers.* now I'm mad! and I'm giving you the meanest look! (they drive off with her) yeah! you better be scared now!

(Lion Temple - outside - dawn)

Leonidas: *runs to Skinnit who's jumping all around the place.* SKINNIT! WHAT ARE YOU DOING OUT HERE?! IT'S HUNDRED YEAR MOON NIGHT!

Skinnit: I know! I'm so excited!

Leonidas: BUT IT'S DANGEROUS!

Skinnit: not for me! according to legend the hundred year moon makes us skunks smell like fresh cut flowers

Leonidas: no seriously, come inside. we'll wear nose plugs.

Skinnit: it's true! centuries ago, when nothing at all grew in Chima, there was only sand, skunks, and a purple moon. that's when the skunks of Chima blessed this land with their special smell, turning it into the lush paradise it is today

Leonidas: hm. never heard that legend before. but how about to stay safe inside with us anyway?

Skinnit: WHAT?! NO! THIS IS THE ONE TIME IN MY LIFE WHEN I'LL SMELL REALLY GREAT! *walks away.* I CAN'T STAY INSIDE! *hops.* I must share my sweet scent with all of Chima

Leonidas: *sighs*

Longtooth: moons arising! lock down the fort!

*all the lion guards retreat back inside and lower the gate, the hundred year moon turning completely purlple. later on in the night, Lagravis and Laval stand side by side scoping out the area, until they notice Gorzan*

Gorzan: *approaches on a mech.* DUDES! *shows them G'Loona's flowers.* HELP!

*Gorzan comes eye to eye with Lagravis and Laval on foot*

Lagravis: no one should be out tonight, you know that Gorzan!

Gorzan: totally, but G'Loona never came home. she's out there all alone!

Laval: WHAT?! WHERE'D YOU SEE HER LAST?!

Gorzan: in the forest. but I followed a trail of her flowers here!

Laval: she can't be far! I'll help you look for her-

*they're left with bad news when the wolves howl more chaotically then usual*

Lagravis: *sighs.* just get her! before they do.

*Laval nods*

(Wolf Camp - night)

*the whole pack loses control just like Windra did, howling up a storm, Winzar even having the power to take down a whole tree*

(Lion Temple - night)

*Laval roars to the Eagle Spire*

(Eagle Spire - night)

Eris: *hears Laval's roar.* Laval?!

*Crooler and Cragger watch Eris as she heads for the Lion Temple*

Crooler: so predictable aren't they?

Cragger: the ape must've followed that trail of G'Loona's flowers! *touches Crooler's arm.* great idea leaving that for them!

Crooler: yes. it brought them right to the lions who then called the eagles, and now they'll all go off on some heroic search, to their doom! *chuckles*

(Beaver Lodge - night)

*the wolves charge through the forest, breaking into the beaver's lodge and wreaking havoc*

beavers: *scream while huddled up in the corner of a secure room*

*howling away, the wolves leave*

(Rhino Quarry - night)

*while howling, multiple wolves throw rocks everywhere due to there not being much to wreak havoc on*

Rogon: that looks fun! can I play throw the rocks too?! *throws back an incoming rock but is left underneath a giant pile of them straight after*

*the wolves move on*

(the forest)

*Gorzan and Laval start their search through the forest on their machines*

Gorzan: *picks up another flower and gasps, pointing straight down the forest.* the flower trail leads this way.

*they approach the part of the forest that's turned into chaos with wolf trucks everywhere*

Laval: SWEET CHI! it's like a tornado with claws came through here! I guess we should've brought some chi with us..

Gorzan: poor G'Loona... out there all alone! I hope she's safe!

(The Forever Rock - night)

*sitting next to the Forever Rock her leg tied to something, G'Loona's not seen to be harmed and is accompanied by a note and a heap of bananas*

G'Loona: dear little gorilla girl, you are safe. no one ever comes down to the Forever Rock at night, not even wolves, and we've left you lots of bananas so you have all the energy you need to ask for help. please scream loudly, *picks up the rope.* and try to have fun! thanks for playing our game, love the crocs. bit of a strange game but, *sighs.* why not? HEEELLLP!

(The Forest - night)

Gorzan: G'LOONA?!

Laval: sounds like she's over at the Forever Rock! there's only one problem..

*both of them stare at the wolves up ahead ripping down multiple trees while howling*

*looking straight at the wolves destroying the forest, Laval and Gorzan stay perfectly still and undetected*

Laval: we need a way to sneak around these wolves a- *hears stomping.* Gorzan! where'd you go? *mouth drops when he sees Gorzan charge straight for the wolves, having no choice to follow as he sighs and does the same thing*

*the wolves pick up their scent once they go right past and chase after the 2 while howling*

Laval: HOW CAN THEY EVEN KEEP UP WITH US?!

*Woriz grabs hold of Laval's tank and tries to eat it*

Laval: oh no!

*multiple wolves trip over Gorzan*

Gorzan: (while falling) BUMMER!

Laval: *looks to the front but trips over Gorzan's mech arm and is sent flying into a bush.* WOAH!

the wolves continue to howl away, breaking apart Gorzan's mech piece by piece. Wakz taking apart a metal thing and dropping it right by Gorzan*

Gorzan: DUDES!

*Eris arrives on a jet, multiple wolves jumping on forcing Eris to turn around*

Eris uh oh!

*the wolves start to get closer, making Eris eject herself away from the jet and letting it go down with the wolves going down with it*

*she flies slowly above the rest of the howling wolves*

(The Forever Rock - night)

G'Loona: heeeeeelllp! ohh help me!

*a loud howl*

G'Loona: *gasps.* hello? are you here to rescue me?

*growling*

G'Loona: who's out there? is this part of the game..?

*a wolf with purple eyes slowly approaches her growling*

G'Loona: YIKES! this isn't very fun anymore... who are you..?!

*the wolf reveals himself to be Wanold*

G'Loona: oh.. Wanold! it's only you

Wanold: *jumps towards her with a huge snap*

G'Loona: *screams and looks away. but she looks back and sees something strange*

Wanold: *devours the banana pile with a gigantic appetite*

G'Loona: huh?!

(The Forest - night)

*the wolves continue to destroy and much on Laval and Gorzan's machines. Laval spectating it in a hiding position and Gorzan quickly sneaking away*

Gorzan: dude! they munched my mech!

*they both duck an incoming wheel*

Laval: shh! these full moon wolves, *sneaks away with Gorzan.* have super charged instincts! don't make any loud sounds whatsoever!

Skinnit: Laval! Gorzan! what are you guys doing here?

wolves: *they notice the sound and growl, heading for Skinnit*

Laval: (whispering) Skinnit! get down!

Skinnit: *turns his head and screams when he sees the wolves. out of fear, he turns around and lets out a pink stink instead of green*

*the wolves go through the stink and are temporarily stunned out of their rage from its scent that Skinnit described earlier as the smell of fresh cut flowers*

Gorzan: *approaches with Laval.* dude, your stink smells sweet

Skinnit: you're welcome. it's the hundred year moon, it effects me in a completely different way

*the wolves play around with the flowers that grew thanks to Skinnit's stink*

Gorzan: *joins in the fun chuckling*

Skinnit: but that smell doesn't last long! so if you want to run away from the mad wolves, now's the time.

*Laval grabs Skinnit and Gorzan and drags them away at full speed*

(The Forever Rock - night)

Wanold: *ferociously Wanold gobbles up nearly most of the entire banana pile*

G'Loona: so, you're not here to rescue me or play this so called game. it's all about the bananas.

*Wanold glares at her after wiping his mouth, returning back to the food*

G'Loona: that moon must've made your nose super strong so you could smell those bananas right? *stands up and waves around a banana that Wanold stares at.* well, I know where you can find some more! *points to the forest with the banana*

Wanold: *smells for the bananas and lets out a giant howl, dragging G'Loona away from the Forever Rock and even snapping her rope*

G'Loona: *laughing*

Cragger: *looks at them through a telescope.* ugh! I knew this crazy plan of yours wouldn't work!

Crooler: the night is still young brother! we'll get Laval, eventually.

(Gorilla Forest - night)

*the trio continue to run away but stop and turn around when they hear growling coming their way*

Laval: sounds like your sweet stink wore off Skinnit..!

Gorzan: yeah dude! can't you drop another bottom bomb?

Skinnit: *turns back around.* HEY! I can't stink on command like a piece of rotten meat or something! I need to, build up!

*the wolves charge towards them growling, Laval, Skinnit and Gorzan run away until in front of them came 3 ropes from above tossed down by Grumlo and Eris*

Eris: UP HERE! NOW!

*they climb up the ropes, nearly getting caught when Woriz, Winzar and Wilhurt jump really high, landing back on the ground then howling*

(Gorilla Forest - Gorilla homes - night)

*everyone walks inside a Gorilla house*

Grumlo: welcome friends! you're safe now

Laval: but what about G'Loona?!

Gorzan: don't worry dude. this time we're bringing the whole tribe out to find her!

(The Gorilla Forest - Gorilla Homes - night)

the gorillas hop into a mech of their own, including Laval*

Laval: so, uh.. how exactly do you drive one of these things?

Gorzan: first, you meditate! *meditates.* then, you become one with the machine.

*Gorzan lands on the ground and starts marching onwards with the rest of the gorillas*

Eris: whatcha waiting for?

Laval: nothing! I just gotta meditate! and become one with the machine. *is flipped upside down trying to control the mech.* woah! okay, let's try that again.. *flips into different complicated positions.* I am one with the machine. I SAID! I'M ONE WITH THE MACHINE! *falls off the vines and bounces off two giant mushrooms, landing hard onto the ground*

Gorillas: dude.

Laval: *screams when he tries to get up, landing back down.. he sighs* um. maybe I'll just stay back here with Eris! in case G'Loona returns

Gorzan: awesome dude!

*the Gorillas march away*

*meanwhile, Wanold keeps on dragging G'Loona through the forest*

Wanold: *growling*

G'Loona: *she laughs and sniffs for the bananas.* smell that?! more bananas ahead in the gorilla forest, just keep following his creek so we can avoid the rest of your wolf pack!

Wanold: *does a slight howl then moves even faster*

G'Loona: *laughing*

(Gorilla Forest - night)

*a whole army of wolves charge towards the gorillas howling*

Grumlo: *points to the wolves.* DUDES!

*the gorillas come into blows with the wolves, before shortly getting demolished easily*

wolves: *howling as they stand a top the gorilla mech to show dominance*

(Gorilla Forest - Gorilla homes - night)

*looking out for G'Loona, Eris and Laval stand on the balcony*

G'Loona: *laughing*

Wanold: *growling while having G'Loona ride on his back all the way to the gorilla's homes*

Eris: G'Loona?

*inside*

Wanold: *is fed another batch of bananas, easily devouring them with a gigantic appetite while throwing the banana peels right outside, much to Eris, Skinnit and Laval's amazement*

Laval: G'Loona, It's great to have you back! but, are you sure about this guy?! he looks as crazy as the other wolves!

G'Loona: don't worry! he saved me from this awful game at The Forever Rock. we just have to make sure he doesn't run out of food

Wanold: *it's already that time when he sniffs for more food, jumping at Skinnit who dodges, landing on the wall and beginning to eat it apart*

G'Loona: *chuckles awkwardly.* uh.. better find him some more fruits and veggies. while we still have walls! *runs to find more*

(The Gorilla Forest - night)

wolves: *howling while they completely destroy the gorillas*

Grumlo: DUDES! RETREAT!

*all the gorillas retreat, the wolves notice it after they take a few bites of a mech, hopping onto the back of another one to stop them in their tracks*

Gorzan: AH! *punches the wolves off*

(Gorilla Forest - Gorilla homes - night)

*more banana peels are tossed out the window*

Wanold: *chows down more fruit savagely, Eris coming in with a whole basket of a new supply*

Skinnit: where does he put all that food? *notices Wanold has already eaten it all again and backs away*

Wanold: *growls in hunger and goes to break open a big hole in the wall, munching away at the rest of the wall, this time with more of an appetite then before*

Skinnit: *gasps.* he's eating this out of house and bone!

*the floor starts to rip open a bit underneath Skinnit, the skunk letting out another sweet stink that grows flowers all over the house*

Wanold: *chomps at the wall two more times before he sniffs Skinnit's stink and starts to chuckle, falling onto the floor paralysed*

Eris: *approaches with more fruit alongside G'Loona and Laval.* wow! that smells, a-and looks, amazing!

Skinnit: you're welcome.

Laval: *approaches Skinnit.* are you finally ready to stink out the rest of those bad moon mutts?

Skinnit: uh.. yeah, about that. now I need to recharge again. sorry.

Laval: *sighs*

*they suddenly hear gorillas hooting from the outside*

wolves: *growling while chasing the gorillas who are fleeing using the tree branches above*

*the gorillas land back on land and turn around for battle*

Grumlo: this is it dudes! MAKE IT EPIC!

gorillas: *hooting while stomping their feet*

wolves: *growling when charging to the gorillas. they're caught off guard by slipping over the banana peels Wanold threw outside, sliding past the gorillas howling*

Laval: G'LOONA'S BACK! AND SHE'S SAFE!

*those up top send down ropes*

Grumlo: dudes! fruit stakes, now! *they start climbing.* before the furry freaks fight back.

wolves: *they chase after the gorillas, all of them letting out a big howl that shakes everything*

Skinnit: woah! *loses balance and falls out the eaten part of the wall*

Laval: NO! STOP! THE FULL MOONS GONE! AND SO IS YOUR SWEET SMELL!

Skinnit: *lets it out anyway.* too late.

Skinnit: sorry.
