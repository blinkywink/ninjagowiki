(Forest)

*to start the day, Laval races through the trees on his speedor until he stops to ShadoWind himself approaching*

Laval: ShadoWind?

ShadoWind: *points to the rest of the forest at the side*

Laval: a race?! well, I don't know if I should.. *starts his engine and gets a head start.* MEET YOU TO THE END OF THAT PATH! *laughs*

*it doesn't take long for ShadoWind to overtake Laval and become first*

Laval: huh?

(Canyon)

*ShadoWind continues on in front and Laval at the back. but so suddenly, rocks from above block Laval's path*

Laval: *slams the brakes.* WOAH! *screams as he hits into the rocks and falls off his speedor*

*ShadoWind turns around just before he's trapped with another rock slide*

Cragger: FIRE!

*multiple ravens and crocs fire away at ShadoWind with lasers and nets. luckily ShadoWind makes it out of the dust by driving up the cliff and flying above Cragger and his men's heads*

Laval: SHADOWIND!

Cragger: *goes after ShadoWind but is hit by friendly fire and caught in a net.* hey! *is electrocuted thanks to the net being electric*

Laval: *watches ShadoWind fly away.* woohoo! go ShadoWind! *goes to catch up to ShadoWind*

Cragger: *tries to get out the net.* ugh! how could you miss him?! how come the only captured IS ME?! *screams as he's electrocuted again*

Crooler: *sighs.* don't worry brother, *holds Cragger's arm and brings ou a persuader plant.* we'll destroy them, you just need to take a deep breath. *blows in his face the persuader plant's gas*

Cragger: *sniffs, then sneezes, causing him to back up into the jet and roll down with while getting a major electrocution*

Crooler: *sighs*

Cragger: *screams in anger*

(Wolf Camp - Combat Lair - night)

Crooler: if we don't stop this ShadoWind there is no way ANYONE in Chima will EVER win a speedor race again.

*the wolves growl*

Crooler: my brother tried, but failed, as usual. not surprising since he couldn't even beat a bunch of monkeys in his own swamp!

Woriz: we don't disagree with you Crooler, we're just a little confused. why are you here?

Crooler: isn't it obvious? I can't count on Cragger to get anything done. I need you wolves to disrupt the upcoming speedor and steal all the chi in the Lion's Temple

wolves: huh?

Crooler: oh! and did I ever show you, *shows them the persuader plant.* this amazing plant I found in the swamp?

Woriz: *slaps the plant away.* GET THE THING AWAY! I hate plants!

Crooler: how can you "hate" plants?

Woriz: I JUST DO! now what about Cragger?

Crooler: uhh.. we'll tell him about our plan, eventually. but this is too important to risk him mess it up again.

wolves: *they huddle together and whisper*

Woriz: *turns around.* I'm not sure we can help you bertray your brother like this Crooler.

Crooler: what?! don't tell me you wolves "suddenly" have morals.

Woriz: no! but, it's just.. years ago, during our "alpha troubles". wolves were scavengers back then, always fighting amongst themselves to be the tribe's alpha male. the crocodile king Crominus, your father saved us, he taught us to live and fight as one. for this we gave him, the pledge of the pack, promising to forever assist the king of the crocs. for better or worse, Cragger is king, the pledge still binds us.

Crooler: you mean, *shows the pledge.* this pledge?

wolves: *gasp*

Crooler: you help me, I'll destroy it, freeing you from any future obligations. what do you say?

Woriz: *growls.* agreed. but we'll need some help with this! someone who's "really" good at causing trouble

(Lion Temple - outside)

*with a new day, comes a new character. while the crowd talks amongst each other, a sly fox sneaks to all the racer's speedorz and causes mischief, escaping the crime scene just in time as the racers return*

Bezar: *sees Eris with his gizmo gacker (engine).* HEY! *runs over to her.* THAT'S MY SPECIAL GIZMO GACKER! WHAT ARE YOU DOING WITH IT?!

Eris: huh?! how'd that get here? on what happened to my weapon?

Bladvic: *snoring with Gorzan's groovy gooper in hand*

Gorzan: *jumping.* dude! you bummed my groovy gooper!

Razar: a thief? haha. well at least we are safe my friend. *shows Razcal the thief's code (book).* the thieve's code! *places it on his speedor.* it says we don't steal from each other

*Furtivo steals it without Razar knowing*

Razar: HEY! SOME THIEF STOLE OUR THIEVE'S CODE!

Bladvic: I was just sleeping. I don't know how that got there!

*Gorzan doesn't believe him and walks away, meanwhile Furtivo walks over to Bladvic*

Furtivo: keep an eye on those apes. I hear they're gonna get back at you by putting banana skins all over the track to make it super slippery.

Bladvic: really?

Furtivo: *walks over to Gorzan.* and I heard the bears are gonna coat the track with honey to make it super sticky.

Gorzan: no way dude! that's so bogus. I knew they were up to something!

Crawley: Crooler has it the beavers are "fixing" the ramps, so they'll win.

Cragger: and where'd you hear that?

Crug: a very reliable fox

*Furtivo smiles*

Bezar: THIS RACE IS RIGGED!

Razar: these cheaters are not your friend my friend!

Gorzan: dude, they tried stealing my gear, why not the whole contest?

Skinnit: something STINKS about this race! *sniffs himself.* and it's not just me!

*chaotic chatter*

Furtivo: *leans by Rogon.* everyones cheating for real.

Rogon: cool! should be a fun race then

Furtivo: uh.. you're not worried..?

Rukus: nah, it's all good bro.

Furtivo: what if I were to tell you.. *sees Rogon drawing his and Eris's heads inside a heart on a stone tablet.* uh.. the eagles are uh..

Rogon: you mean Eris?! don't tell me she's cheating too! she's perfect

Furtivo: really? well, I heard she has a crush on you

Rogon: she does? *walks around.* I knew it! didn't I tell you? this is so incredible. I can't wait to tell her I love her- *speeds off*

Furtivo: *stops him.* woah! hold on! this is a "secret" crush. you need to keep it quiet.

Rogon: okay. I get it. shh!

Furtivo: just keep her safe, in the race!

Rogon: oh don't worry. I'll CRUSH anyone who tries to crush my crush! *runs away*

Furtivo: nice! I'm counting on that.

Rogon: *walks over to Eris and stares at her awkwardly with a bit of a chuckle*

Eris: *chuckles awkwardly.* Rogon. is something wrong with your eye?

Rogon: *chuckles as he circles Eris*

Lagravis: welcome citizens of Chima! to the monthly race for the golden chi

*the golden chi does its work and makes the land into the ruthless race of ramps*

Equilla: and the golden chi chooses the ruthless race of ramps!

Cragger: a ramp race? you need wings to win one of those *looks at Eris who is with Laval*

Eris: what's gotten into Rogon? he keeps making these weird faces at me.

Laval: *has a peek at the stone tablet's drawing.* I think he may like you.

Eris: what? *chuckles.* are you serious? oh come on Laval! you don't know what you're talking about!

Gorzan: woah dude! even Laval and Eris are freaking out.

Bladvic: this race is gonna be baaad! I've gotta prepare. *runs over to some more bears of which Gorzan sees.* (whispering) bring over the bee hives. with the extra sticky honey.

Gorzan: (whispering) I totally need banana skins dudes!

(starting line)

Gorzan and Bladvic: *growling at each other*

Razar: *chuckles*

Laval: *sighs*

Eris: *hides herself from Rogon using her wing*

Rogon: *laughs as he stares at Eris with a lovey dovey side*

Equilla: all the riders are ready!

*Longtooth and Leonidas start the race and the riders go forward*

Equilla: and.. they're off! hitting the track with unexpected fury and force.

*crowd cheering*

gorillas: *while the crowd cheers, they hoot away and throw a whole basket full of banana peels onto the track*

Equilla: what's this? banana peels?

*Skinnit and Bezar are the first to fall*

Equilla: it looks like the rumors are true. this race will be a dirty one!

*Skinnit tries to push his speedor back up and it ends with some banana peels flying right for Equilla*

Equilla: *panics as the banana peels land on him*

Eris: *loses her balance and screams when she nearly crashes*

Rogon: Eris? *turns her the right away using his speedor.* I'LL SAVE YOU!

Eris: woah!

Razar: *bangs into Rogon and meets Eris.* SORRY! ONLY ONE BIRDS WINNING THIS RACE MY FRIEND!

Rogon: *growls.* ha! *crashes into Razar and sends him swirling circles*

Razar: *screaming*

Equilla: there goes Razar!

*Eris jumps a ramp*

Rogon: FLY LIKE THE WIND MY LOVE!

Eris: huh?

*crowd cheering*

Furtivo: *approaches Woriz.* so, how'd I do?

Woriz: your work is supreme, Furtivo. I'm glad we hired you.

Furtivo: hey! confusion, mistrust, deception. that's my speciality! and the best is yet to come. *goes away*

Equilla: wow! this race is heating up!

Laval: okay everybody! let's "try" to keep this at least a little fair.

Cragger: *goes up a ramp with Laval and pushes him away from being straight.* fair enough for you?

Laval: *loses balance.* woah! WOAH! WOAH!

Equilla: Laval looks to be in trouble

*ShadoWind enters the race and helps Laval to get back on track, driving with the young lion for a bit*

Laval: ShadoWind?

*ShadoWind drives off and easily becomes first place*

Equilla: holy Cavora! it's the mystery rider ShadoWind! coming out of nowhere to challenge for the lead!

*three bears continue to cover the road with the extra sticky honey, ShadoWind having no problem getting past it*

Equilla: that honey, looks sticky

Cragger: *screams as his wheels get stuck in honey and he falls on the ground*

Crug: no fair! they cheated better then us!

beavers: *multiple beavers come to aid the work on a loopty loop ramp.* we'll fix em! fix em!

Equilla: what are the beaver's up to now?

Eris: *screams as she goes up and down the loopty loop*

Rogon: on my way my love!

Crawley: IT'S NOT OUR FAULT! YOUR TRIBE CHEATED FIRST!

Eglor: no! you cheated first you cheater!

Grumlo: mallow please.. I will crush you!

Bulkar: you're not crushing anyone! *pushes Grumlo*

*a giant fight between tribes go on amongst the tribe*

Woriz: that fox is amazing. look! the fans are furious! the race is in chaos! everyone's distracted!

Crooler: the perfect time to raid the lion's temple and steal all, of the chi. *looks at the temple, where Lagravis comes running out with some lions*

Crawley and Crug: *growling, further up the road they place a big rock in the middle of the track*

*ShadoWind gets past the loopty loop*

Equilla: ShadoWind made it through easy

Laval: woohoo! *drives next to ShadoWind.* thanks for showing me the way through all that junk. now it's my turn to show you! *speeds up.* woohoo! *turns his eyes back to the front and is surprised to see the rock, crashing into it and causing his downfall in the mud*

Equilla: ooh! that's gonna hurt!

*ShadoWind parks by Laval to check on him, silently*

Laval: *stands up.* you saw that coming. why didn't you say anything?

*ShadoWind drives off with no answer given*

Laval: oh yeah. I forgot. you don't speak.

*the race being in total chaos, only two others remain except for Laval and ShadoWind*

Rogon: *sees an obstacle up ahead, so he plugs a chi and demolishes it before slowing down to drive next to Eris.* that's not just chi in my chest. it's my heart, beating for you Eris!

Eris: uhh.. thanks? I think.

*ShadoWind goes past the finish line*

Equilla: and the winner is, ShadoWind! again. not that anyone seems to be paying attention.

*the whole crowd battles each other with wooden boards now*

Crawley: I CAN'T STAND THAT EAGLE ANNOUNCER! and you look just like him!

Eglor: no I don't! crawl off scale face!

Belkar: *pushes against Grumlo.* we would've won if you didn't slip us your slippy skins!

Razcal: *chases the beavers with some other ravens.* FIXING THE RAMPS?! WE'LL FIX YOU!

Lagravis: congratulations ShadoWind! but we must dispatch from the normal ceremonies today. *hands ShadoWind the golden chi.* here! golden chi, use it well, wisely and so on. *turns around.* Longtooth! get every lion out here to stop this crowd before they destroy everything!

*more lions go to reinforce the rest down below to try stop the angry crowd*

Crooler: now's our chance! follow me.

Cragger: *sees Crooler.* Crooler! where are you going?! it's not safe here! *goes after her.* are those wolves chasing you?

*Crooler brings the wolves to the sacred pool*

Lagravis: *tires to push away a bear.* move them outside! away from the fort! we must protect our temple!

*they reach the sacred pool and immediately the wolves start collecting*

Crooler: grab all the chi and let's get out of here!

(Lion Temple - outside)

wolves: *howl as they plug some chi*

Woriz: *chuckles but then sees something.* huh?

*there seems to be another wall after the first wall*

Woriz: AGAIN!

*again the wolves blast at the wall, making an exit for them to the outside*

Woriz: LET'S GO!

(Lion Temple - outside)

Cragger: *sees smoke coming from that direction.* Crooler..?

Lagravis: what was that?!

Laval: *holds back a gorilla.* I heard it too dad! don't worry, I'll check it out. Eris?

Rogon: anything for you my love. *charges for the crowd to leave an opening for Laval and Eris*

Cragger: so long as that pledge exists. wolves still serve crocs!
