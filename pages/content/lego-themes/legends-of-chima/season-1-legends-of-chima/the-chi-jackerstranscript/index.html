Narrator: In the magical world of Chima, the animal tribes have become divided in pursuit of the powerful chi. The wolf tribe has long served the crocodile king. But Crooler went behind king Cragger's back, to free the wolves of their obligation. In return, the wolves helped her disrupt the Speedor races, and steal all the chi there. except for a powerful orb of golden chi, won by the mysterious ShadoWind. these are, The Legends Of Chima
