(Lion Temple - Laval's room)

Laval: *puts his sword on his bed, noticing Sir Punch A Lot.* aw! sorry Sir P! not play fighting today. for today is the day we forge a lasting peace in Chima! ah! who am I kidding? a little play fighting never hurt anyone! *punches the dummy multiple times.* HEY NOW! *continues*

Lagravis: *clears his throat*

Laval: *struggles for words in surprise*

Lagravis: *walks to Laval.* you do know you're supposed to be getting ready for a peace conference? not a battle?

Laval: uh.. yes?

Lagravis: we elders of Chima are counting on you and your friends to the settle the differences between our tribes. the future belongs to the young, and you are the young leaders of Chima. hm.. all so I'm told.

Laval: don't worry dad! we won't goof this peace party up!

Lagravis: seriously Laval! there will be challenges! it takes great sacrifice to be a true leader.

Laval: yeah yeah yeah! sacrifice, true leader, peace at last! I got it! I've even been practising, my serious voice!

Lagravis: *sighs and wags his head*

(Crocodile Swamp - outside)

Cragger: this is a huge day for all of us! so make my teeth shine Plovar!

Plovar: *gasps.* oh Craggy Wag! how I've waited for you to say that! *starts cleaning Cragger's teeth*

Cragger: *gibberish*

Plovar: oh, I know exactly what you mean Craggy Wag. you want everything to be perfect on this historic day!

Cragger: *gibberish*

Plovar: oh. so you asked Crooler to set up all the meeting decorations?

Cragger: *gibberish*

Plovar: oh! uh.. nothing! I'm sure she'll do an, uh.. interesting job.

(Forever Rock)

Crooler: *finishes setting up.* there! I've got a place for them to sit, some refreshments, *shows a root.* and one very special ingredient. last time I threw these roots in the fire.

(flashback)

Crooler: I now know my true destiny! to rule Chima! *laughter*

(Forever Rock)

Crooler: *laughter.* if this fog of destiny doesn't upset this little peace gathering! I don't know what will

(Forest - dawn)

*everyone makes their way to the Forever Rock*

(Wolf Camp - entrance - dawn)

Woriz: *is pulled to the outside by Windra but he shakes her arm off.* ugh! do I have to go?!

Wilhurt: YES! we can't be lone wolves forever! we need peace with these tribes so we can get more chi!

Woriz: ugh! but those lions and crocs are SO annoying.

Windra: *growls at Woriz while she looks at him with a dead stare*

Woriz: *sighs and goes to his speedor.* alright... *hops on his speedor and heads for the Forever Rock*

(Forever Rock - night)

*Laval and Cragger are already there, sitting down by each other with a fire burning. everyone else except Woriz arrives*

Laval: *stands up.* welcome friends!

Cragger: *shows his REALLY bright teeth once standing up.* pull up a rock, and let us begin our new age of peace!

*Gorzan and Eris cover themselves from the light*

Eris: AH! TURN OFF YOUR TEETH!

Gorzan: DUDE! THE BLINDNESS!

Rogon: *sits next to a tree and puts on some sunglasses.* time to tan

*Cragger shuts his mouth and the brightness of his teeth suddenly disappears*

Rogon: *puts away his sunglasses while Woriz arrives* aw!

Woriz: ugh. I'm only here cause my tribe thinks it will help us get chi.

Laval: well! we lions plan to start our monthly chi day sharing again, IF this peace treaty works out.

Rogon: *leans towards Eris and chuckles.* a peace of my heart treaty is more like it! right Eris?

Eris: *chuckles.* oh Rogon! you're a sweety!

Gorzan and Bladvic: *look at the two with curious expressions.* huh?

Eris: *sits up straight.* I mean, let's focus on the treaty..

Bladvic: CAN WE HURRY PLEASE?! I'M FEELING VERY! very.... *snores.* tiny, fairy people...

Razar: I'M WITH THE BEAR! HURRY UP! so we stop fighting, *shows a leaf.* AND START SHOPPING! *gives out a part of the leaf to everyone.* discount coupons for all treaty signers!

*half of Laval's leaf rips apart and falls on the ground. Crooler then giving some fruit shakes to everyone, Woriz declining one by looking away*

Woriz: this doesn't seem fair!

Rogon: *slurps his fruit shakes*

Woriz: you lions and crocs are running everything! if you want REAL peace! we've gotta make this meeting more equal.

Laval: Already thought of that! Everyone! Meet our neutral mediator: Skinnit!

Skinnit: *pops up behind Woriz.* hey everyone! *shows the treaty as a scroll.* do I have a peace treaty for you!

Bladvic, Cragger, Eris, Gorzan, Rogon, Razar and Woriz: *they all freak out by Skinnit's appearance and back up, huddled together*

Woriz: *growling*

Razar: Ha! Does it include a cease fire on your stinkies, my friend?

*Skinnit's happy face goes down and he feels left out*

Laval: now now everyone! you all know Skinnit is one of the few creatures in Chima, who has never taken a side in our conflict!

Woriz: because none of the sides can stand his smell!

[Eris and Razar softly chuckle]

Skinnit: don't worry! soon, I will make everyone love me! but first! who wants peace? raise a paw, or claw!

*everyone raises their paws/claws except Woriz*

Woriz: *raises his hand.* whatever.

Skinnit: okay! now if one tribe breaks the peace, all others shall stand against that tribe, until peace is restored! agree, paw or claw?

*everyone happily raises their paws/claws*

Bladvic: *while snoring, Gorzan lifts his paw up*

*Crooler gives a sneaky look*

Skinnit: the treaty also requires you to respect the chi, respect Chima, and respect each other. sound good?

*everyone happily raises their paws/claws*

Skinnit: Super! Now, put your mark on the document, to make it official! [shows the writing, but some extra writing accidentally scrolls downwards, to which everyone becomes curious.] Oh! Don't mind the rest of that! It's just some fine print!

*everyone looks through it all*

Gorzan: Dude! What's this about having to celebrate Skinnit's birthday with fireworks every year?

Eris: And do we really have to smile and say thank you every time you release your smell?!

Skinnit: Oh! Just ignore all those extra clauses in there that refer to me!

Razar: Mount Cavora shall be known as "Mount Skinnit"?!

Skinnit: that's some more plain language I threw in! pay no attention and sign it

Laval: Skinnit. *grabs the treaty.* I know you want us all to love you. but how about we sign the part we already discussed, *rips the fine print from the rest of the important stuff.* and deal with all the other "fine print" later?

Skinnit: *grabs the paper and rolls it up.* sorry... *throws it away*

Bladvic: *yawns and then falls asleep snoring*

*Crooler appears from the side lines and throws into the fire the same roots she had earlier*

everyone: *gasps*

Cragger: Crooler! what are you doing?!

*the purple mist appears from the flames*

Crooler: saving Chima from your silly truce! *goes away.* NOW ENJOY YOUR FOG OF DESTINY!

Woriz: *chi's up and lets out a howl*

*everyone chi's up and make the sounds of their animal, Skinnit somehow not being effected but left confused*

Laval: what's happening to us?

Cragger: I don't know, but I feel so.. strong! so, powerful! so elegant! I feel like.. *turns red.* like I can rule all of Chima!

Laval: woah there old friend! whatever's happening to us, (sleep talking) we have to remember we came from peace..

Cragger: (sleep talking) peace?.. (Fog) HA! NEVER! *charges towards Laval, who puts his guard up*

Eris: *rushes to Laval and Cragger.* STOP THIS YOU TWO!

Woriz: *gets in Eris's way and turns red.* I DON'T THINK SO BIRD GIRL!

Eris: WHAT'S YOUR PROBLEM WORIZ?!

Woriz: simple! I'm just one big bad wolf! *attacks her but is pushed away*

Eris and Woriz: *grunting*

Skinnit: uh.. hello?... everyone okay?... *is left confused*

Laval: *blocks Cragger's spear and pushes him away.* Cragger! what's gotten into you?!

Cragger: nothing! except, MY DESTINY! *continues with a barrage of attacks*

Woriz: *chases Eris andends up blocking an attack from her.* ah!

Eris: *blocks a strike.* Rogon! a little help please!

Rogon: *hops around.* I can't my love! I've just discovered my true destiny! *huddles into a ball.* (sleep talking) I shall become the greatest rock, the world has ever seen

Skinnit: huh?

Eris: *sighs but screams once she sees Woriz's weapon coming for her head, so she dodges*

Woriz: *growling*

Gorzan: dudes! I just realized! *hops over to someone.* my destiny is to spread the love that is needed! *hugs Woriz and Eris tightly.* SO BIG HUGS!

Eris: *coughs*

Bladvic: woah! look at me! *laughs and begins to flex.* full of spunk! (sleep talking) run around, and around! haha! *spins in a circle while twitching his legs.* can't stop! watch me go! (fog) WEEEE!

Razar: strange! I feel like, the richest bird in the world, yes? I like this destiny! (sleep talking) and I am so rich, I can afford to give everything away!..

Skinnit: seriously?..

Razar: take it all! *Bladvic runs past.* ENJOY! *flies after Bladvic.* you! bear! I give you everything!

Bladvic: sorry! can't stop now!

*Razar tackles him and they both roll into Rogon*

Rogon: ow! what are you doing?! no, rock climbing please!

Razar: aw! don't be so selfish!

Bladvic: *tries to push Rogon.* hey, move! you're in my way!

Woriz: *manages to get out of Gorzan's grip with Eris and strikes Gorzan so hard he gets sent flying.* ENOUGH ALREADY!

Gorzan: aw! *lands onto Bladvic, Razar and Rogon, then giving them a giant hug.* love is in the air. feel the love, brothers!

*they all manage to escape*

Laval: Cragger! WE'RE FRIENDS! don't you remember?! you can't keep trying to destroy everything around you!

Cragger: *spins his spear.* DON'T BLAME ME! IT'S MY DESTINY! *whacks Laval multiple times but is locked, eventually though he manages to send Laval flying*

Laval: WOAH! *flies back into reality and into the water*

Skinnit: WATCH OUT FOR THE CREEK LAVAL! YOU CAN'T SWIM! *splash* EVEN WHEN YOU'RE AWAKE!

Laval: *wakes up and can barely hold his breath, but luckily he's dragged back up to shore.* w-what happened?!

Skinnit: I don't know! ask the fog! it knocked everyone out except me!

Laval: *looks at the fog*

everyone; *sleep talking*

Cragger: *growls*

Laval: I'VE GOTTA GET BACK IN THERE BEFORE CRAGGER DESTROYS THEM ALL! *goes back to the fog*

Skinnit: *gets in Laval's way.* woah! hold on a sec soggy paws! what's going on in that fog?!

Laval: I'm not sure.. it puts you in some kind of a dream, with everyone else! and you feel, like.. I don't know. you discover your purpose.

Skinnit: I heard one of these sleepy heads muttering about destiny.

Laval: really? could this be, a fog of destiny?! I thought that was just an old forgotten legend!..

Skinnit: well, this old legend forgot me! all I found out about my destiny is that I'm exactly who I'm supposed to be! nothing has changed.

Laval: *pushes Skinnit out the way as he goes back to the fog.* consider yourself lucky. *falls unconscious and roars back into action*

Cragger: *kicks away Woriz and puts Eris down, spinning his spear fron the finishing blow.* I AM MIGHTY!

*Eris looks away*

Cragger: I AM INVINCIBLE! I AM-

Laval: GOING DOWN! *tackles Cragger and they oth land on their feet.* Cragger! I know you! and this is not who you really are!

Cragger: *screams.* but it's who I could be! *sends Laval flying.* and I like this version of me! *spins his spear once Laval lands.*

Laval: CRAGGER! IT'S THE FOG! it's messing with all of us!

Cragger: *doesn't listen and sends Laval flying again*

Laval: WOAH! *flies out of the fog*

*Skinnit gets him out of the water again*

Laval: *breathes for air.* I DON'T THINK I CAN GET CRAGGER OUT OF THAT FOG! HE'S TOO STRONG! I need to "wake" him somehow! *goes back in the fog*

Eris and Woriz: *grunt as they're getting attacked by Cragger*

*Cragger notices Laval and charges at him*

Laval: *roars but is hit back out of the fog.* WOAH!

*splash*

Skinnit: *sighs before he drags Laval back onto shore once again.* am I a peace mediator, or a life guard?

Laval: WE'VE GOT TO WAKE THEM SOMEHOW!

Skinnit: let me try. *goes inside the fog, unaffected. so he tries to touch Cragger, but his reflexes kick Skinnit away and he's left screaming once he's kicked into Rogon and is spun in a circle, eventually getting literally kicked out of the fog and into Laval's arms.* any other ideas?!

Laval: yeah. we throw the water AT them! *a few minutes later, he chucks two fruit shakes at the fog but is knocked back and spitting it out when the liquid comes right back at him*

Skinnit: did that water just bounce off the fog?!

Laval: yup. new plan! I can't get close enough to wake them or soak them. so I gotta somehow get them completely away from that fog!

Skinnit: ha! you'd have to move the ground itself to do that!

*somehow, the ground shakes. both Skinnit and Laval holding their balance*

Skinnit: woah! that was quick!

*it eventually stops*

Skinnit: did you shake the ground again Laval?

Laval: wasn't me! but I think I know where it came from

(Lion Temple - outside - night)

*Laval rides back to the temple on his speedor. going through the waterfall and using his legs to go inside*

(Lion Temple - throne room - night)

*every lion holds onto something as the earthquake continues*

Lagravis: get ready for it! that shaking means the golden chi is coming!

*from the sacred pool, the golden chi rises and the earthquake stops. Longtooth and a lion elder using a net to hold it up*

Longtooth: nice one! too bad we don't race anymore for these orbs.

*Laval arrives and hides*

Lagravis: oh we will! once Laval and his friends sign that treaty

Longtooth: *points.* you mean that Laval?

*Laval sneaks up to them*

Lagravis: Laval! what are you doing here? are the peace talks over?

Laval: uh!.. wel.. there were some.. uh.. complications!

Lagravis: what do you mean?

Laval: um.. I'm a little "foggy" on the details. very, foggy! *reaches for the golden chi.* but I think I found a solution..

Lagravis: Laval. what are you doing?

Laval: well, dad! you know how we lions have these rules that we're never ever supposed to break, no matter what happens?

Lagravis: yes, of course! without rules, we are nothing.

Laval: well.. I think I'm gonna have to, *steals the golden chi and runs away with it.* BREAK ONE OF THOSE RULES NOW!

Lagravis: Laval! you can't just take that golden chi! GUARDS! STOP MY SON!

lions: *roaring while they charge towards Laval*

Laval: *dodges the guards.* excuse me! pardon me! COMING THROUGH! and, over! *hops on his speedor and rides away with the golden chi*

lions: *they chase after Laval with war cries*

(Forest - night)

Laval: *roars once while he rides through the forest and back to the Forever Rock until he's met on the road by Lagravis and Longtooth*

Lagravis: what in the name of Cavora do you think you're doing Laval?!

Laval: sorry dad! but this golden chi maybe the only way I could save my friends, and all of Chima too!

Lagravis: what? but golden chi is for transforming the land! how will that help a couple of kids down at the Forever Rock?!

Laval: I don't have time to explain! you just have to trust me on this!

Lagravis: but son! you've just stolen chi! breaking one of our mot sacred rules! do you know how harsh the penalty is for this?!

Laval: *sighs.* yeah! I know! but remember how you told me earlier that it takes great sacrifice to be a true leader?

Lagravis: yes. but I never said anything about stealing golden chi!

Laval: well.. this, is one of those sacrifices.

Lagravis: *stays silent for a second but sighs.* I shall not allow you to pass. but! against all my instincts and better judgement, and years of wisdom..

Laval: *takes off.* THANKS DAD!

Lagravis: there will be consequences for this Laval! and they will be severe!

(Forever Rock - night)

*Laval gets back to Skinnit's side*

Skinnit: hurry Laval! I don't know what's going on here, BUT WORIZ AND ERIS SOUND LIKE THEY'RE IN TROUBLE!

Eris and Woriz: *grunting*

Cragger: *bang.* FEEL. *bang.* MY. DESTINY!

Laval: stand back Skinnit! *puts the golden chi on the ground.* this may get a little.. weird..

*the golden chi does its work*

Cragger: *almost goes for the finishing blow but is stopped by the earthquake*

Eris and Laval: *screaming while they go falling out of the fog*

Bladvic, Gorzan, Rogon, and Razar: *screaming while they too are falling out the fog*

Cragger: NO! NOT NOW! I WAS SO CLOSE TO DESTROYING THEM ALL! *falls.* STOP!

*they're all floating in the water and after the golden chi fades, they climb back to land*

Gorzan: woah! dude. that was intense.

Razar: HEY! WHERE'S ALL MY STUFF?!

Bladvic: I don't know. but all that sleeping made be really sleepy.. *goes to sleep snoring and sleep talking in gibberish*

Eris: *gasps*

Woriz: CRAGGER! WHERE ARE YOU?! you messed with the wrong, destiny pal!

Laval: *holds onto Gorzan.* dream's over! fog's gone! it wasn't real! you're all fine!

Cragger: *still has his eyes red.* speak for yourself fur bag.

Laval: huh?

Cragger: I think we all found our TRUE destiny today, don't you?!

*everyone thinks*

Laval: Cragger! I know this was all kind of freaky. but if you just come back and sign our treaty..

Cragger: NEVER! *exits with his speedor*

*the actual Forever Rock goes down into the earth, Skinnit comforting Laval with a pat on the back*

(Lion Temple - throne room)

lions: *Lagravis chats with the elders as it's a new day, Laval being granted an audience*

Lagravis: the elders know of this so-called fog of destiny. it is a highly dangerous commune dream, its effects that can last for years.

Laval: I know. and that's why I had no choice! I had to break the rules.

Lagravis: yes. but you did so to save your friends. perhaps the rules, can.. bend a little this time

Laval: no dad! I deserve my punishment.

Lagravis: what?

Laval: when I was in the fog, I saw my destiny too! and it wasn't just to be a great fighter or a hero. it was to be a true leader! one who leads by example, and that means following the rules! even the ones I don't agree with.

Lagravis: well, yes, of course. *walks up to Laval.* we all want to follow the rules, but.. these were very unusual circumstances! we can make an exception

Laval: as a great lion once said. without rules, we are nothing!

Lagravis: well, yes. that's true. but..

Laval: a true leader makes no exceptions.

Lagravis: but.. but the punishment for breaking this rule.. is exile! from the tribe! and from all of Chima.

Laval: if this is my destiny, then I accept it. for the lions, and for Chima. *walks away, Lagravis almost says something but it's too late*

Lagravis: what have I done?...

Longtooth: I think you've raised a truly noble lion, Lagravis.

Lagravis: *sighs while wagging his head*

(Crocodile Swamp - throne room)

*Cragger returns with Crooler sitting on his throne*

Crooler: *stands up.* brother! so.. how'd it go?

Cragger: it's all VERY CLEAR TO ME NOW! I SHALL RULE OVER ALL! OF. CHIMA!

Crooler: really? you mean.. we'll finally crush all those other tribes!

Cragger: yes!.. but we there is no "we" in this plan! ONLY ME!

Crooler: *back up.* w-what?..

Cragger: GUARDS! lock my sister in the deepest darkest dungeon we have!

Crooler: brother!.. *is dragged away by Crug and Crawley.* what are you doing?..

Cragger: pursuing my TRUE DESTINY! just like you wanted me to do when you threw those roots into the fire!

Crooler: but not like this! we're supposed to do it, TOGETHER!

Cragger: I should have never listened to my sister. *sits on his throne.* wait.. didn't someone tell me that once?.. *sniffs.* HEY! WHAT'S THAT SMELL?!

everyone (and I mean literally everyone): *screaming because of Skinnit letting out the most biggest stink bomb he's ever done, all around Chima*

(Spiral Mountain)

*Skinnit farts right into Plovar's face, luckily he has nose plugs on*

Plovar: must you?

Skinnit: what? I'm celebrating my true destiny. h-hey! just cause I didn't turn into some giant warrior, doesn't mean I didn't learn something from that fog!

Plovar: uh oh!.. *flies away*

Skinnit: FEEL. MY. GREATNESS EVERYONE!

Plovar: *screams as his nose plugs fall off and he's forced to smell the stink, flying into the screen and sliding down*

Skinnit: sorry! but not really!..
