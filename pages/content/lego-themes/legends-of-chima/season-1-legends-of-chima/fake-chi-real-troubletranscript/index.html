(roads of Chima)

*a bear group rides on their speedorz back to their village, asleep*

Bladvic: *yawns.*

Belkar: *snoring*

Bladvic: hey Belkar! wake up!

Belkar: huh?.. oh yeah!.. still driving!

Bladvic: Alert this please. All our chi, remember? The wolves. Everywhere, stopping tanks, stealing chi. and-

Belkar: *snoring*

Bladvic: *turns around to see two more bears asleep so he sighs.* who am I kidding?.. all this conversation makes me sleepy too... *falls asleep and starts snoring*

*not knowing, the bears drive right past a group of wolves in speedorz and trucks*

Woriz: stop the bears! *goes after the bears in a truck.* seize their chi!

wolves: *howling while they go after the bears. then they begin to shoot*

bears: *snoring.*

*for some reason, a slight bump makes Bladvic press a button that makes two bee hives from the side of his speedor to detach and roll towards the wolves*

Woriz: INCOMING!

*the honey splashes all over the wolves driving speedorz*

Winzar: *licks the honey.* honey?

Winzar: it's not the honey that's the problem! it's the bees!

wolves: *those on speedorz howl as they're crowded over by a swarm of bees, trying to shake the swarm away*

*two wolves crash into each other*

Winzar: *crashes and grunts when the bees sting his butt off ground*

Winzar: UGH! you bears think you're so clever! *speeds up*

bears: *snoring while winzar speeds up in front of them and aims at them with a blaster*

Winzar: *grunts once Belkar reflects off a rock and barges into him*

Woriz: *uses a grappling hook to grab the bear's chi from behind but notices something once he catches the bags.* HURRY! THEY'RE SURROUNDING US! *nods to a wolf*

*Laval and a group of lions approach from behind*

Laval: for the bears! for the chi! FOR CHIMA!

*the lions begin to shoot*

wolf 1: *whimpers as he loses balance*

*a wolf truck barges into the speedorz in front of him*

wolf 1: *howls in defeat and retreats with the group of wolves*

*the lions continue to shoot away until they reach the bears on the ground*

Bladvic: *yawns.* wow! I had the weirdest dream.

Laval: *lifts up a sleeping bear.* I bet! we tried to get here sooner, but we had three other convoys to guard. everyone's moving their chi by ground since the windstorms forced the eagle jets down.

Bladvic: WOAH! WHO GRABBED OUR CHI?!

Laval: huh? *sighs and face palms*

(Crocodile Swamp - outside)

*the crocs return home on their giant helicrocter*

Cragger: ah! it's good to be back! I feel so..

Crooler: in need of vengeance?

Cragger: no! strangely clear headed! it's like I've been in a fog for months. *walks up.* but now I'm so happy! we really need to thank Laval for rescuing us from those wolves!

Crooler: but we hate the lions don't we?!

Cragger: nah! *turns around.* I miss my old pal! alot! why don't we go pay him a little friendly visit soon?

Crooler: *walks up to him.* yes. a friendly visit.. we'll pretend to be nice, AND THEN CRUSH HIM when he least expects it!

Cragger: huh?! that's not very friendly! what are you talking about Crooler?

Crooler: oh!.. nothing?..

Cragger: I really just want to do something nice for all the tribes of Chima. maybe we can get everyone a gift! what do you think Crooler?

Crooler: *sighs angrily while she hides a dead persuader plant behind her back.* let me see what I can find!..

(Raven Town)

Razar: I can't find you one of those.

Crooler: why, not?! it's a persuader plant! they grow in the swamp! well, at least they used too.

Razar: *is given the plant.* exactly my friend! we picked them clean years ago. you're lucky to find this one. *gets surprised once he sees its dead.* we call it, "the salesmen's best friend". puff out a few spores and everyone does, or buys exactly what you say!

Crooler: *face palms.* ugh! yes! I know! is there ANYTHING you can do? I need to find something "special" for my brother.

Razar: hm.. well it would cost you my friend. but perhaps there is someone who can help.

(Raven Town - alley)

*Crooler roams through the dark alleys of the town with Razar nowhere to be seen*

Razar: he's an alchemist! always brewing up these "special" things you seek, yes? but his work, it is, well.. unpredictable. so beware my friend!

*Crooler peeks through the window of a door and sees a white and black feathered bird inside stirring something*

Razar: satisfaction not always guaranteed with this raven.

*she almost knocks, but she is so intimidated that she starts to walk away*

???: *opens the door.* may I help you?

Crooler: *gasps and turns around.* are you.. Reegul?..

Reegul: yes! *walks up to her with his eyes on the plant.* and is that a persuader plant? HA! OVERGROWN FUNGUS! I can make you something, *shows a greenish then normal chi orb.* MUCH more interesting then that!

Crooler: did you say, you made that?

(Lion Temple - throne room)

*the elders continue to harvest orbs from the sacred pool*

Lagravis: we are harvesting more orbs, but nowhere near enough to make up for all the chi stolen by all the wolves.

Laval: let me guess. the tribes will get short change. Chima's unbalance. earthquakes, windstorms, and disaster follow. everyone suffers..

Lagravis: are my words of weary wisdom, that predictable?..

Laval: yeah!.. pretty much. so is there any possible way we can figure out how to make more chi here?

Lagravis: sorry, son! but making more chi then Cavora allows is completely impossible!

(Raven Town - Reegul's workshop)

Reegul: *stirs a cauldron.* it's completely possible, to make more chi! if you know what you're doing! and alas! I'm the only one who knows what I'm doing

Cragger: so, this is why you brought me here Crooler?

Crooler; yes! BROTHER! this home made chi is our answer to EVERYTHING!

Cragger: *looks at Reegul.* is it really as safe and powerful as the orbs in the lion temple?

Reegul: uh.. more or less. *chucks at him an orb.* TRY ONE!

*Cragger nearly plugs it*

Crooler: *stops him.* the king would love to try it!.. but a great leader always shares his spoils with UNDERLINGS first.

Cragger: I do?..

Crooler: CRUG!

*Crug has already plugged an orb and lifts up and down two seats with both Crooler and Cragger sitting on them*

Cragger: wow! it really works!

Crug: I've never felt so strong!

Cragger: *hops off his set.* just think! if we can make our own endless supply of chi..

Crooler: we can rule Chima! just like you've always wanted brother!

Cragger: huh?! no! I was thinking we could give it out to all the other tribes!

Crooler: WHAT?! *falls off her chair screaming*

Cragger: *walks away.* yes. it's the perfect gift to make up for all the crumby stuff I done! let's go!

Crooler: *lets out a big sigh and stays lying down*

(Bear Village)

*music playing, Cragger dances on a crocodile vehicle with bellow plants attached, throwing out chi everywhere

bears: *while snoring, some even dance in their sleep*

Belkar: *has a chi thrown right by him and he accidentally plugs it, but goes back to sleep*

(Beaver Lodge)

*music playing*

beavers: *cheering while they're handed out chi, but too bad they're not big enough to plug it*

*a beaver falls off from the top and crashes into something*

beavers: *rush over.* fix it! fix it! fix it! fix it!

(Rhino Quarry)

*music playing*

*Cragger throws out more chi to the rhinos*

rhinos: *they all plug some chi and use it to break through multiple rocks while screaming*

Windra: *spies on them from the top of a rock.* hm... *goes to report back*

(Raven Town)

*music playing*

Cragger: *throws out chi to the ravens while driving through town.* FREE CHI FOR ALL! FREE CHI FOR ALL! FREE CHI FOR ALL! FREE CHI FOR ALL! FREE CHI FOR ALL!

*Razcal and Razar wonder what's happening, so Razcal quickly analyses the chi with a magnifying glass and sees something familiar about the orbs*

Razar: *flies over to Cragger.* uh.. tell me mr free chi fairy! did these orbs come from a raven called Reegul by any chance?..

Cragger: yes! of course! I only have the BEST chi for my friends in Chima!

Razar: *looks at Razcal with a surprised expression who wags his head, sighing while face palming.* I thought Crooler was just going to Reegul for some mind control potions. I didn't think he'd end up dumping this on her.

Cragger: huh? what's this? and why would my sister need to control anyone's mind? everyone loves her.

Razar: this is what we call, "Reegul's chigull". and trust me! if it was actually any good, we'd be selling it by the truck load.

Cragger: what are you talking about?! this chigull- I mean.. chi! is great!

Razar: have you tried it yourself?

Cragger: well.. no!.. but, one of my best crocs plugged it this morning and he feels incredible! right Crug?

Crug: *the chi inside of him starts to fade.* uh... I don't feel so good... *his surge of chi disappears, and he starts clucking like a chicken. then trying to fly by jumping off the vehicle's end while flapping his arms*

Cragger: uh... is he trying to.. fly?

Crug: *gobbles while he bounces away*

Razar: yup! that's Reegul's chi alright.

Cragger: will that happen to everyone who plugs it?!

Razar: *goes away.* pretty much. see ya

Crug: *gobbles*

Cragger: *looks at the fake chi.* uh oh.

(roads of Chima)

*Laval rides to the next delivery destination, the same group of lions following behind*

wolves: *howling while they attack, beginning to blast at them*

Laval: KEEP THOSE WOLVES AWAY FROM THAT CHI! *turns around and goes for the wolves.* sorry wolves! but lane's lanes for LIONS ONLY! *hits a wolf on a speedor off road with his sword.*

*some wolves including Winzar get onto Longtooth's tank and try to hit him but are blocked*

Longtooth: LOOK OUT! CURVES AHEAD!

*the wolves look to the front and most of them are shaken off when Longtooth drives recklessly, except for Winzar who's holding on*

Winzar: will you stop that?!

Longtooth: okay!

Winzar: *screams as he's sent flying off from Longtooth actually stopping*

Longtooth: *chuckles and starts driving again, but moans once he sees more wolves climb aboard and their numbers cause him to go off balance.* TOO MANY WOLVES! TOO STRONG! WE CAN'T SAVE THE CHI!

Laval: *rides in on his speedor and stands up on it, sweeping a wolf off of the tank.* JUST HOLD ON!.. *sits back down on his speedor.* A BIT LONGER!

*a wolf and Wilhurt go on the side of Laval, trying to hit him. but two wolf trucks charge through blasting and manage to shoot some tanks off road*

Laval: *hits away the wolf soldier.* almost there! get ready to duck when I say!..

*the two lions keep on going forward until they see some eagles up ahead*

Laval: DUCK!

*Longtooth ducks*

Wilhurt: duck? looks like eagles to me. *is blasted off.* ah!

wolves: *whimpering*

*Eris and another eagle hop onto Longtooth's tank and battle the remaining the wolves. however, some bags of chi fall off the tank and into a wolf soldier's arms*

wolves: *howling while they retreat with their spoils*

Longtooth: HA! Look at the fur bags run!

Laval: yeah!.. the fur bags who grabbed half our chi! so who really claims victory here?

(Wolf Camp)

wolves: *a crowd of them howl and cheer*

Woriz: victory is ours. Chima is ours. Chi means powers.

*the group of wolves return*

Woriz: and our raids have brought us more chi, THEN ANY OTHER TRIBE IN CHIMA!

wolves: *howling*

*Windra approaches and whispers in Woriz's ear the bad news*

Woriz: WHAT?! HOW'D CRAGGER GET MORE CHI THEN US?!

(Gorilla Forest - gorilla homes)

*the convoy reaches the gorilla homes*

Gorzan: lion dude! *hops down.* what brings you and your buds to our funky forest?

Laval: uh.. did you forget? your chi delivery! we thought you'd be needing a new supply around now.

Gorzan: nah, we're good

Laval and Eris: WHAT?!

Gorzan: yeah! check it out! Cragger stopped by a little while ago.*takes them up to see the chi given to them by Cragger, Laval and Eris totally shocked.* dude flipped us, like, a crazy load of chi!

Laval: did you say Cragger gave you all this chi? but.. how could he do that?..

(Raven Town - Reegul's workshop)

Cragger: *points at Crug.* HOW COULD YOU DO THAT?!

Crug: *pecks some bread crumbs fed to him by Crawley while clucking*

Reegul: ah! the power of nature is glorious. especially when that power takes the form of a bird

Cragger: what?! *points his spear at Reegul.* YOU KNEW THAT WOULD HAPPEN?!

Reegul: PLEASE! PUT DOWN YOUR WEAPON! I've been dreaming of this for years, ever since I was born! my father was an eagle. my mother was a raven. but neither tribe ever really wanted me. all these years, I tried training my body, to become more like them. but I still can't even fly! then I decided instead.. TO MAKE ALL OF CHIMA LIKE ME!

Cragger: uh.. so let me get this straight. you want to turn everyone in Chima, into birds?

Reegul: not just birds! Reegul eye! they shall be my spawn! just like me! obeying my every command! now! ATTACK THE CROCODILE KING!

Crug: *he continues to peck the bread crumbs while clucking. full, then he acts like he tries to poop*

Crawley: I, think he's laying an egg...

Cragger: what?..

Reegul: *sighs.* hm.. *wobbles to Crug.* my Reegul eye formula may still need a little work. *taps Crug's head*

Cragger: *looks at Crooler.* did you know about this?

Crooler: uh!... I was aware there might be some.. uh.. side effects.. but I thought we could these orbs to dominate Chima before!-

Cragger: they turn into birds?

Reegul: FLY OVER! AND CRUSH THESE FOOLS IN YOUR BEAK! FLAP YOUR ARMS! like this!*flaps his arms.* flap! flap! see! see! flap!...

*Crug flaps his arms but ends up on the floor again when trying to fly*

Reegul: *sighs*

Crooler: the effects only last a day or so! we can still use it to take over Chima!

Cragger: uggh! again with the take over Chima stuff! I told you! I just want to make everyone HAPPY! and then this happens! WE'VE GOT TO WARN EVERYONE BEFORE IT'S TOO LATE!

(Gorilla Forest - gorilla homes)

Grumlo: *crows loudly while he flaps his arms and gets watched by Eris*

Laval: Gorzan! *picks up the fake chi.* as less these orbs came from the sacred pool in the lions temple, they're not chi!

Gorzan: bummer. I better tell Grumlo, he plugged once earlier.

*they hear Grumlo crowing and go outside by Eris*

Eris: I think he already knows..

Grumlo: *crows loudly, this time he jumps from a high branch*

Laval: GRUMLO NO! YOU CAN'T FLY!

Eris: *tries to save him by flying down but his weight drags her down.* TOO HEAVY!

Laval: *shows an orb of real chi to Gorzan.* REAL CHI! USE IT TO SAVE THEM!

Gorzan: *plugs the chi and jumps down quickly. using a vine to swing across and save both Eris and Grumlo, Grumlo lying down.* DUDE!

Grumlo: *crows loudly and flaps his arms*

Laval: do you know of anyone else who got this so called "chi" from Cragger?!

(Beaver Lodge)

beavers: *crowing while they flap their arms and peck the ground*

Cragger: *arrives with Crawley driving.* uh.. hello? beavers! there's been a slight.. "product re-call*. I need to take all that chi back.

(Bear Village)

*the two crocs arrive at the bear village*

bears: *murmuring while they sleep bunched up together in a tree*

*Cragger and Crawley look above them before they collect all the chi with a bag*

(Rhino Quarry)

Rogon: *murmurs while he jumps off a rock and Cragger approaches*

Cragger: uh.. Rogon! I hate to tell you this but.. you're not actually a bird. it's that chi I gave you.

Rogon: huh? I didn't plug any chi!

Cragger: but!.. weren't you just trying to fly?

Rogon: oh! I always do that! I want to impress my sweet heart, Eris! she's an eagle y'know?

Cragger: uh.. yeah! I know. so, where is all that chi I gave you guys?

Rogon: *points up.* ask them!

rhinos: *on a solid piece of rock, they cluck while flapping their arms*

Rogon: they used it all up, now they need an easier perch!

rhinos: *while clucking and flapping their wings, they jump up and down, causing the foundation they're standing on to fall with them falling too*

Rogon: yay! it's raining rhinos!

*Cragger wags his head in displeasure*

(Forest)

*the crocs and lions meet each other while driving*

Laval: CRAGGER! WHAT ARE YOU DOING?! *jumps out of the tank and marches to the vehicle filled with bags of fake chi*

Cragger: *jumps out and hides the chi away from Laval.* UH!.. whatever do you mean?!

Laval: YOU POISONED HALF OF CHIMA WITH THIS FAKE CHI! THIS ISN'T WHY I HELPED YOU ESCAPE THE WOLVES!

Cragger: ugh! I know! I was just trying to do something good for a change! it's not fair! it's not! it's not! it's not! IT'S NOT!

Laval: okay. I get it! not fair! but, what are those orbs anyway?..

Cragger: A MISTAKE! but don't worry! the side effects ware off in about a day! and I've already collected all the orbs I gave out!

wolves: *unknowingly, a lot of wolves howl while they surround them*

Laval: uh oh!

Woriz: *approaches.* don't even think about trying to stop us! you're surrounded by our ENTIRE tribe!

Wilhurt: HOLY MOTHER TOOTH! *opens the crocodile mouth and grabs out two bags, heading straight for the wolf soldiers.* THERE'S ENOUGH CHI HERE FOR EVERY WOLF!

Winzar: with that kind of power! we can easily attack the lions! and take over their sacred pool too!

Laval and Cragger: WHAT?!

Woriz: *holds an orb.* these mighty wolves aren't just here for you kids! we're finally conquering ALL of Chima! lions! crocs! EVERYONE!

wolves: *chatter while they're thrown out chi*

Woriz: and your chi will help us do it.

Cragger: I wouldn't plug that if I were you!

Woriz: QUIET CROC!

Cragger: but it's really bad!-

Laval: BAD! that you're stealing our chi! I mean, it's so not fair! *touches Cragger to let him know to go with it*

Cragger: oh no! not our chi! please! it will make you so strong and powerful! no one will defeat you! please! please! please! don't take all that chi now!

Woriz: oh I'm not! I'm saving my chi for when we defeat the lions! I want to see Lagravis's face! when I chi up and thrown him out of his own city! as for the rest of us

wolf: *every one of them plugs their chi and lets out a giant howl*

(Lion Temple - outside)

Longtooth: *sees the wolves charging towards the temple and alerts the lions by howling*

Lagravis: LOCK DOWN GATE! READY BLASTERS!

*the lion head closes, every lion manning the walls with blasters*

Lagravis: this is a powerful wolf army! we need to be prepared for anything! and I mean anything! how are we doing with chi?

Longtooth: we distributed most of it to the other tribes! there are only a handful of orbs left for us!..

wolves: *howling while they charge it straight for the temple, Laval and Cragger tied up together and kept at the back of a truck, driving right towards the battle field without anything to do*

Laval: when exactly will they bird out? because once that fighting starts! we're all doomed.

Cragger: should happen soon! hopefully.

Lagravis: get within range! *aims.* on my command! ready! *closes one eye.* aim!

wolves: *they all aim their blasters but suddenly, they all start clucking*

Lagravis: huh?

wolves: *clucking while they peck at the ground and try to lay eggs*

Woriz: WHAT ARE YOU DOING?! WE'RE SUPPOSED TO BE BLASTING! NOT PECKING!

Lagravis: are they behaving.. like.. birds?...

Longtooth: well! you did say to expect, "anything"

Woriz: *gets out of his truck and walks around.* STOP IT! STOP IT NOW! WHAT IS HAPPENING TO YOU WOLVES?!

Laval and Cragger: *laughing, and luckily they got themselves out of their captivity*

Laval: haha! yeah! looks like your mighty wolves are just a bunch of bird brains! *flaps his arms.*

(Wolf Camp - night)

*the wolves are seen driving back inside their camp*

Laval: *hops out with Cragger, Eris and Gorzan. all of them showing bags filled with bread crumbs.* bread crumbs?

wolves: *clucking while they're led deeper into the camp by pecking a trail of bread crumbs*

Woriz: *speaks gibberish and marches angrily with his troops*

Cragger: they should be fine by morning. but we HAVE to make sure this NEVER EVER happens again!

(Raven Town)

*Reegul is dragged to the edge of a balcony by Laval and Cragger with some bellow plants strapped to his back*

Reegul: YOU CAN'T DO THIS TO ME!

Cragger: well, you always wanted to FLY! didn't you?

*the two throw Reegul into the sky, and the white feathered eagle floats above ground because of the bellow plants.*

Reegul: but the wind will take me into the outlands! and then I'll!.. and I'll!.. hey! this isn't bad! I actually feel like.. I'm flying! finally! after all these years! I'm like a real bird! oh the glory! oh the beuty! oh the majesty! *one by one, his bellow plants pop.* huh? *he falls while screaming. then sighing in despair*

Laval: come on! we ruffled enough feathers for today! let's get some real chi out to everyone

Cragger: ready to fly this coop whenever you are pal!

Laval and Cragger: *laughing as they ride their speedorz out of raven town, a friendship of these two best friends is mended. for now*
