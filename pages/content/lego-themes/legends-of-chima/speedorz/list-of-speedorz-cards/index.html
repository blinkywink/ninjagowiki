Trivia[]

  • The card for Tratratrax incorrectly show the physical Grapt accessory.
  • The cards for Chi Whippa, Stynkjahak, Nightblade, Swiftsting, and Chi Venomor show the only appearances of the Spider, Bat, Skunk, Fox, and Scorpion inner warriors.
  • The card for the Defendor IIX uses the alternate form of Roman numerals for Eight (VIII)
