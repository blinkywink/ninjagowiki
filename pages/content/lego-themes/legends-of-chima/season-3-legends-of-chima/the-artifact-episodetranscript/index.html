(The Grand Archway Canyon)

*Eris alongside a small fleet looks for the Hunters but sees only their temple without any troops*

Eris: Strange. There's the Hunter fort, but no Hunters anywhere.

(Forest)

*Rogon, Runk and Rukus search as well but find nothing*

(Fangs)

*Razar and Razcal search but find nothing*

(Chima's roads)

Worriz: You know, the battle's over, we don't have to ride together anymore.

Gorzan: Dude! I like riding with you. Now let's find those ice dudes.

Worriz: *growls*

(Lion Temple - Map Room)

Laval: Wolves, Ravens, Rhinos, Gorillas and Eagles, searching all of Chima? And not one of them has seen Li'Ella?

Eglor: We believe the Hunters have hidden here somewhere.

Laval: No, duh! And where are the Hunters?

Eglor: Vanished. Underground. Eris is doing one final sweep to make sure.

(Sky)

Eris: *sighs as she heads over to Mount Cavora, but is given a bit of a fright to feel a slight hit on her jet.* Huh? where did you come from?

Mysterious Panther: *growls*

Eris: *gasps to see the falls freezing up again.* Okay. No need to panic. Just another vision. I know you're not real, just tell me, what do I need to know?

*the Panther disappears after she goes through a cloud*

Eris: Ha. Knew I'd scared him off.

Mysterious Panther: *jump scares Eris with a roar on the other side of the jet*

Eris: AH! *screams when she freaks out and pushes down a lever that makes the jet do a 360. Annoyed she growls at the panther and tries to shake him off, somewhat succeeding.* Huh? Oh no, not again! I've gotta stop this, whatever This, is...

Mysterious Panther: *growl at her right in her face, up the front of the jet*

Eris: *screams in fear as she goes down to the Lion Temple*

(Lion Temple - corridor)

Lennox: All tidied up now?

Leonidas: I think so.

Lennox and Leonidas: Huh?

Eris: *comes screaming inside with her jet that demolishes most of the lion statues, luckily she stops and sighs in the throne room.* Sorry. Where's Laval?

*the lions are speechless and together they get back to fixing up the statues with Eris leaving*

(Lion Temple - Market)

Eris: It was so real, Laval. He was right there on my jet wings, I could feel breathing on me.

Flinx: Ew! I wouldn't let some dumb Panther breath on me.

Laval: It's okay Eris, it's over now.

Flinx: Yeah! And if he comes back, just let me handle him! *plays around with his Flikker*

Eris: Wow. Since when did fire boy get so hot headed?

Flinx: *gasps when he accidentally ignites the top layer of a barrel with his weapon, so he puts it out with a blanket*

Laval: Flinx!

Flinx: Uh... Did I do that?

Laval: Stand by the waterfalls, now! Less things to burn down.

Eris: What are we gonna do about these Hunters? if my visions are true-

Laval: There's a face full of stinky Panther breath in your future.

Flinx: *laughs*

Eris: Yeah. That. And, all of Chima is doomed too, so... Where do we start?

Laval: Well first I need to find Li'Ella, which means going underground after the Hunters. And there's only one cat around here who really understands those tunnels.

(Phoenix Temple - inside)

Tormak: Li'Ella... Where are you?

Fluminox: Tormak, we share your pain. Our odyssey together is a long one. It was you, Tormak, who buoyed my sinking spirits, wrenching me from the precipice.

Tormak: The sentiment is mutual.

Fluminox: In ages past, eight Phoenix leaders, conducting the power of the Fire Wing Harnesses. The Great Illumination, the land was healed, the Hunters vanquished. I was to be the ninth Phoenix that day, but I failed them. Now they are gone. Without the ninth Phoenix, the Illumination was imperfect. The unintended side effects were profound, Mount Cavora, the CHI, the Gorge, the Hunters in ice. But this moment atop the precipice, a moment of weakness and hesitation and duties forsaken, this has haunted me the most. You alone understood that. You have always understood.

Tormak: You had your reason for not joining the others, Fluminox.

Fluminox: Yes, and I still have my reason. But alas, I still have my regret as well.

Tormak: I would have done the same. Trust me your majesty, protecting the ones we love is never cause for regret.

Fluminox: I do trust you, Tormak. My savior, my healer, and my friend.

(Gorge of Eternal Depth)

*disappeared, the Hunters seek refuge in the Gorge while they build something*

Stealthor: We are building a new Ice Mountain, Sir Fangar. It shall be larger and more powerful. Strong enough to attack Mount Cavora itself!

Sir Fangar: Splendid news, Stealthor. We must also stop the Phoenix from hitting us with another Illumination.

Stealthor: Oh. That, could be a problem.

Sir Fangar: It is the main problem! But I was quite the student of the Phoenix once. I know all their secrets.

Stealthor: Really? What's their favourite colour?

Sir Fangar: Red.

Stealthor: Wow. You do know them well.

Sir Fangar: And their greatest secret of all? They need eight Fire Wings Harnesses to launch an Illumination.

Stealthor: Okay. So we'll grab the Harnesses, stop the Illumination. The Harnesses are hidden, the only map to them is on an ancient Artifact.

*Sir Fangar silently tells him to change the idea*

Stealthor: Okay. So, we'll grab the Artifact, then the Harnesses, and then stop the Illumination.

Strainor: *screams in pain because Sykor bites him*

Sir Fangar: The Phoenix never let this Artifact leave their precious flying temple.

Stealthor: Okay. Uh...

Sir Fangar: It's protected by walls, guards, and that oppressive Phoenix heat.

Stealthor: Really?

Sir Fangar: We can't even get close to it.

Stealthor: Okay, so... Uh... Uh... Next plan!

Sir Fangar: Fear not, Stealthor. I'm not expecting us to steal the Artifact. I have someone much better in mind.

Vardy: Sir Fangar, your bride is here.

Li'Ella: Will you let me out of here?!

Sir Fangar: Ah, she is more then my bride.

Li'Ella: *gasps*

Sir Fangar: She is the key to the Fire Wing Harnesses, and all of Chima!

(Frozen hills - night)

*Tormak searches desperately for Li'Ella*

Sir Fangar: *laughs after he throws his blade down to catch Tormak's attention.* Oh, you won't find us down there, Tunnel Cat.

*Tormak plugs a Fire CHI and jumps up to Sir Fangar, but Sir Fangar escapes before Tormak can reach him and allows Tormak to angrily demolish his tank*

Tormak: Where's my daughter?

Sir Fangar: Temper, temper, Tunnel Cat.

Tormak: *roars and jumps towards Fangar but misses*

Sir Fangar: we can do this all day if you like. But it won't bring you any closer to you seeing your daughter. Recognize this?

Tormak: Li'Ella's necklace! *jumps after Sir Fangar, but he manages to get to the other side where Tormak can't reach him.* What do you want?

Sir Fangar: Lots of things actually. The Phoenix child, control of Chima, a loving and supportive queen.

Tormak: *screams*

Sir Fangar: But for now, there's only one thing I need from you. The Artifact.

Tormak: What?

Sir Fangar: Round thing, about this big, Fire CHI in the center, and a map to the Fire Wing Harnesses.

Tormak: How do you even know about that?

Sir Fangar: Ah, so you'll steal it for me?

Tormak: NEVER! *jumps to the other side but misses again*

Sir Fangar: Please Tormak, my offer is simple. Li'Ella for the Artifact.

Tormak: *gasps.* Li'Ella...

Sir Fangar: Yes. Unless you steal the artifact for me, you will NEVER, ever see your daughter again. The choice is yours.

Tormak: *jumps to the distance once more but Fangar escapes entirely, so he roars angrily

Sir Fangar: From the sound of it, we should have that Artifact in no time. *laughs while he and the Hunters move on*

(Spiral Mountain - night)

Maula: You really want the wedding up here? Someone's bound to see us, and our prisoner.

Vardy: The king and queen of the Hunters cannot get married in some dark cave! This is a special moment. the start of a royal dynasty! We need food, music, glamour! We need, a great view...

Mottrot: Is the bride on the cake supposed to look so irritated?

Vardy: That's what she looks like!

Li'Ella: LET ME OUT OF HERE!

Maula: I still think we're an easy target up here.

Vardy: Are you planning the wedding? No, I didn't think so. So we stay here. And now, where are those flowers?

Vornon: All we could find are these exploding ice plants.

Vardy: They will do just fine. Set them down and make them look pretty.

Vornon: *screams when he goes flying in the distance thanks to the explosion from the ice plant.* Now I see why people cry at weddings.

Razar: A Hunter party? Atop the spiral mountain? Now this is valuable information yes?

(Crescent - Phoenix Outpost - night)

Lundor: Thanks for helping out on guard duty, Tormak. With Li'Ella gone, we've been one cat short.

Tormak: *picks up Li'Ella's Roarburn.* Is... is this hers, Lundor? *Lundor nods* Li'Ella.

Lundor: Uh, Tormak? Hello? Everything okay?

(Flashback)

*Tormak remembers when Li'Ella meowed at him when he saved her, the memory of when he gave her the necklace, the memories of when they would laugh together in the middle of training but would have to go back to being serious whenever Fluminox was around, and the memory when she gave him a drawing of them together as a family*

Sir Fangar: *laughs while he stalks her from a distance with a telescope*

(Crescent - Phoenix Outpost - night)

Tormak: *silently cries*

Lundor: Tormak ,are you crying?

Tormak: Never! I was just... Smelling Li'Ella's scent. *sniffs the Roarburn.* It's still present here. As sweet as ever. *pulls out the same drawing Li'Ella gave him in the memory.* Will you excuse me, Lundor?

Lundor: Sure thing boss, anything for you.

Tormak: *walks outside the gate.* This is a mistake. A terrible, awful, unforgivable mistake. But I cannot bare the thought of never seeing Li'Ella again. *signals Stealthor that he'll do the deed by shining the Fire CHI outwards*

Stealthor: Alert Sir Fangar. The Tunnel Cat has caved, as expected.

*Tormak goes back inside*

(Phoenix Temple - inside)

*seeing that Fluminox is somewhere else away from the Artifact, he goes to take it off its perch after thinking about it for a second, then takes it outside*

Trakkar: hey, Tormak. What are you doing with the Artifact? I thought it was never supposed to leave this room.

Tazar and Trakkar: *grunts as they're both knocked to the side*

Tormak: Sorry my loyal comrades.

Trakkar: *grunts*

(Crescent - Phoenix Outpost - night)

Lundor: Tormak, glad you're here. Hunters, on the horizon. Hey, is that the Artifac- *grunts as he and more tigers are knocked out*

Tormak: Sleep peacefully, friends. You are not to blame for this. Only I am. *takes the Artifact to Sir Fangar*

Sir Fangar: So that's it? I expected more.

Tormak: It holds the only map to the Fire Wing Harnesses, the ultimate power of the Phoenix.

Sir Fangar: So, without that map, they'll never find their Fire Wing Harnesses. We shall be free to rule all of Chima!

Tormak: *growls.* Where's my daughter?

Sir Fangar: She's safe, in our cage.

*in the mechanic Mammoth's cage like stomach, there lies a figure with a hood, not identified whether it's Li'Ella or trickery*

Tormak: LI'ELLA!

Sir Fangar: Oh, she can't hear a thing, but you'll be reunited soon enough, once I have the Artifact.

Tormak: Li'Ella first!

Sir Fangar: Sorry. As much as I hate to admit it, you're one powerful cat. I need to make sure you don't attack us all and keep the Artifact.

Tormak: I am true to my word.

Sir Fangar: As am I. But just to make sure, bring the Artifact to the Mammoth Mech where your daughter is. We've prepared a special space to contain its... uh... unique heat.

Tormak: *thinks about it and in frustration, growls at Sir Fangar*

Sir Fangar: It's your choice, Tunnel Cat. You want to see your daughter? Go to her, and bring the Artifact.

Tormak: Forget it! The Artifact stays here!

Sir Fangar: Fine. Say goodbye to your daughter then. Forever!

*the Hunters begin to leave, Tormak almost allowing them too until the drawing of him and Li'Ella makes him reconsider*

Tormak: WAIT! *breathes heavily while he takes the Artifact to the Hunters as they stop*

Sir Fangar: *laughs*

*some type of sound alerts Fluminox, and he comes running outside*

Fluminox: Oh no! *runs down.* Tormak! What are you doing?!

Tormak: I'm sorry, Fluminox. It's for Li'Ella! *carries on*

Fluminox: Get back here! the Artifact can't be removed from Phoenix territory!

Tormak: BUT MY DAUGHTER! I HAVE NO CHOICE!

Fluminox: No! The Artifact will protect itself. Stop, before it's too late!

*Tormak reaches the ice with the Artifact and it causes a giant explosion*

Tormak: *roars, with the Artifact gone, and his appearance turned to that of the mysterious Panther from Eris' visions, then he drops the drawing that sets ablaze, gasping, he runs away.* WHAT HAVE I DONE?!

Fluminox: Tormak! Come back!

*Tormak carries on running away while the drawing burns*

Fluminox: Oh, Tormak. Old friend, how could you?

(Crescent - night)

*in the distance, Eris and Laval go towards the temple with the smoke of the explosion greeting them*

Eris: huh?

Eris and Laval: *screams when the smoke goes past and so does Tormak*

Laval: whoa! did you see that guy?!

Eris: yeah, like half a dozen times already. It's MY vision remember?

Laval: that was no vision!

Eris: what? you mean... You saw the Panther?

Laval: I don't know what he is. But yeah, Panther sounds about right.

Eris: Oh no. If the Panther's here, that means the rest of my vision will come true too.

*they look back to Mount Cavora and see that it's not frozen, yet*

Laval: Come on! Let's follow him.

*they chase after Tormak*

(Lion Temple - outside - night)

*Tormak finds the mech that has the hooded figure and goes inside*

Tormak: Li'Ella! *removes the hood to find a Sabertooth, not Li'Ella.* You're not Li'Ella. She was never even here, was she?

Sabertooth 1: Strange black cat, who are you?

Tormak: I AM TORMAK! THE TUNNEL CAT! WHERE IS LI'ELLA?!

Laval: Did you hear that?! Your vision panther knows Li'Ella!

Tormak: *growls and throws away the Sabertooth*

sabertooth 1: *screams when he's thrown away*

Eris and Laval: *gasps to see Tormak approach them, sadly they don't know it's him*

Tormak: *growls in frustration when the blazing bastion arrives, and follows him as he runs away*

(The Blazing Bastion)

Frax: It's blazing time!

Fluminox: No, not now. Just find the Panther, nothing else matters.

Foltrax: So, we're on a Panther hunt? works for me, but... Shouldn't Tormak be here? We never do missions like this without him.

Fluminox: Tormak is no more, and this why we must seize Tormak.

Frax, Firox and Foltrax: Huh?!

Fluminox: He will be held accountable for his actions tonight.

Foltrax: Woah, um... okay, sure, why not?

*Foltrax turns the jet*

Foltrax: Sorry! Sharp turn there. I know you like a smoother ride.

Fluminox: I matter not. Just do what you must to stop that Panther!

Foltrax: Well, then, I guess it really is...

Firox, Foltrax and Frax: BLAZING TIME!

(Canyon)

Tormak: Li'Ella... What have I done?...

*the blazing bastion continues after Tormak*

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
