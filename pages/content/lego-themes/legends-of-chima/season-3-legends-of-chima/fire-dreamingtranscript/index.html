Laval: it all started with a black cloud.

Laval: Our only hope was to journey into the dangerous Outlands, to find the powerful Legend Beasts.

Cragger: Foes were many, friends were few.

Laval: But a great lion came to our aid. He was my uncle, Lavertus, also known, as, ShadoWind. He made the ultimate sacrifice.

Laval: Where Mount Cavora was reborn anew.

Cragger: no idea.

Laval: still trapped in that cave I guess.

(Outlands - scorpion cave - ??? - inside)

*the orb which Scorm fell down through the crack, released its energy through the ice and towards the strange saber-tooth figure from last season's cliff hanger, reawakening from his captivity in a giant amount of ice. Still inside of it though, he kicks his way out but lies on the floor extremely weakened due to the fact he was trapped in ICE for who knows how long, but he sets his eyes on the CHI that can restore his strength if plugged*

Sir Fangar: power. Must. Get, close. *tries to stand up but falls back on his stomach, crawling his way to the CHI slowly. He tries to stand again but falls again, this time on top of the orb that goes in his chest and empowers him enough to stand up and let out a giant roar, looking back at the giant mountain of ice.* Now I just need some of that for the rest of you.

*surprisingly he wasn't the only one trapped because there was numerous others still in the ice*

Cragger: Yeah! that just made the fun and glory even sweeter!

Laval: Woohoo!

*Cragger and Laval end up lifting both their tank's front wheels up and down repeatedly out of fun*

Cragger: yeah, don't be a party pooper, Eris.

Laval: uh... Are you mad at us, Eris?

Eris: what makes you say that?!

Cragger: because you're shouting?

Eris: I'M NOT SHOUTING!

Laval: is it because we didn't listen to your fire dream before?

Eris: no! I mean... Yes, actually, it is. And you've been ignoring me a lot lately. And yes, kids, those tribes stole a lot of chi. And it's still out there, somewhere, just waiting to... Uh...

Cragger: tear us into flaming birds?

Laval: *chuckles*

Eris: *growls in annoyance and flies away*

Laval: *sighs.* hey, Eris! we're sorry! *chases after her.* we were just having fun! we didn't mean to make you upset! come on back!

(Outlands - scorpion cave - inside)

Scutter: King Scorm! look at what we've saved for you! all the stolen chi!

*from above, a whole jackpot of chi orbs are dropped from above, once more hitting Fangar in the head*

Sir Fangar: I am more powerful then ever!

*Fangar immediately collects all the orbs and frees his comrades trapped in the ice who are not only saber tooth, but other animals such as mammoths and vultures*

Sir Fangar: Rise, my hunters. Our dreamless sleep is over.

Maula: *blows her trumpet*

*while Fangar walks amongst his troops, they are confused on what they look like thanks to them being in the ice for so long, it's somehow infected their appearance to cold and frozen parts all along their body*

Sir Fangar: We shall now finish what we started so long ago.

Hunters: *cheering*

Sir Fangar: Chima shall be ours!

(lion temple - market - outside)

Rogon: Get your rocks here! Now with two flavours! High and soft!

Plovar: Spoil yourself today, in our new bubble spa! Skinnit, can you give us a demonstration?

*Skinnit farts stink into the spa turning the water green, scaring away Cragger and his eagle friend*

Razar: Step right up, my friends! And get your portrait painted on a real life replica legend beast! *Hands to Wanold a sketch of the lion legend beast*

Wanold: Hey! This doesn't look anything like a legend beast. And your painting of me stink- *howling while he's sent flying*

Razar: Next customer please.

*Wanold lands in Plovar's spa and immediately goes out because of Skinnit stinking up the water*

Bladvic: *snoring*

Eris: you'll love this, Bladvic! Eglor built an alarm clock that whispers. So it can be loud.

Eris: (whispering) and really soft like this.

Bladvic: wow! I'll never be able to sleep through that! I'll take it!

Eris: I think you'll be really happy, and- *starts screaming because she hallucinates of catching on fire, so she starts to run around in circles while yelling "Hot!". To cool off she runs all the way to the spa while chanting the word "Hot!" again and jumps right in with Skinnit.* Woah. Close one. Glad this water was here, I'd never be able to put out those flames.

Plovar: uh... What flames?

Eris: I was just on fire, didn't you guys see?

*everyone around her laughs*

Plovar; sorry, bird girl, but all I saw was a whole lot of crazy leaping into my bubble spa. Flames NOT Included.

Eris: But... But... First that dream, and now I'm seeing things while awake? *growls*

Crowd: *cheering for the racers going past*

Cragger: even better to be winning on our home track!

Woriz: I couldn't agree more! *pushes through Cragger and Laval to first place*

Laval: hey!

Gorzan: dudes! that's crazy!

*coming up to another corner, Cragger and Laval look to do the same thing but their ropes rip apart and they're sent off the track while screaming and landing into a bush*

Woriz: wow, that's gotta hurt.

Gorzan: *rushes past Woriz.* dude! I'm in first!

Woriz: What?

Gorzan: celebration banana! *eats his banana and throws the peel into Woriz's face so he can't see*

Woriz: What?! *takes away the peel from his eyes and growls at Gorzan who's about to win*

*Gorzan goes past the finish line and wins*

Laval: use it well, use it wisely. *gives Gorzan the golden chi*

gorillas: *hooting in celebration*

Laval: Oh, come on, Woriz! it's been a long time since the gorillas won a race! can't you be happy for them?

Woriz: ugh! this is me being happy!

(Scorpion cave - ??? - inside)

Mottrot: We do?
