The Heart of Cavora/Transcript

Overview

≪
Wings of Fire
The Heart of Cavora
 
≫

Eris: It was Chima's coldest hour, the hunters had launched their final attack on the Lion City and Mount Cavora. The tribes of Chima, aided by the phoenix, fought bravely, turning the tide of the battle, there was even hope that we might uncover the eight ancient fire wings; the only things powerful enough to permanently save Chima. But then, Sir Fangar released the ferocious ice bears. These are the Legends of Chima. The last Legends of Chima.

(The tribes of Chima overpower the Hunters with their firepower that the Hunters retreat inside of their fortress)

Maula: There is no shame in a graceful surrender, Deary.

Sir Fangar: Never. Help is on the way.

Maula: You mean the ice bears? Oh my. We don't even know if those rabid rascals will show up.

Sir Fangar: Oh, they'll be here all right. They love a good fight! And have we got a good fight for them.

(The tribes keep firing until the ground shakes)

Lagravis: (Roars) Did you feel that?

Lavertus: I did, or, did I? (The ground shakes again) Woah! No, definitely felt that.

(The ground continues shaking and Eglor sees something in the forest)

Eglor: What?

(The ice bears arrive and with their strength, easily turn the tide of the battle)

Sir Fangar: (Laughs while the fortress continues forward and clashes against the Lion City walls)

(All the hunters run into the Lion Temple)

Sir Fangar: Grab all the chi! Our mountain shall rise!

(The ice bears continue to wreak havoc upon the tribes (Even the ravens and the eagles on their jets)

Lagravis: Such a powerful tribe, but we will win this, nothing can beat us with that Phoenix Airship.

Foltrax: It's blazing time! Again!

(The Phoenix fire at the fortress with the airship)

Sir Fangar: Load that chi into the heart of the glacier.

(The hunters drop bags of chi into the glacier)

Sir Fangar: Rise my steep ice! Rise!

(A giant piece of ice rises from the fortress up to Mount Cavora, with the phoenix airship heading straight for it)

Foltrax: Look out!

Firox, Foltrax and Frax: (Screams when the airship hits into the ice and goes down, luckily they escape)

Lagravis: (Gasps) No.

(Rhino Quarry)

Laval: I still can't believe you built an aircraft, Cragger.

Cragger: Hey, I've been building this since that battle at the Eagle Spires, but it didn't work until today; I found the fire wings and suddenly I knew how to make it fly.

(Cragger flies his aircraft into the Quarry with Li'Ella and Tormak bringing up from a steep hole a rock)

Laval: Hey, I recognize that rock.

Cragger: Yeah, it's the only one the rhino's could never break so they threw it to the bottom of their Quarry.

Tormak: The rhino's were correct. Nothing can break this stone, except the power of the phoenix. (Uses the power of the map to break the rock, and inside was another fire wing)

Laval: Woah, more fire wings! We almost have all eight of them now: There was that one by the Eagle Spires.

Cragger: Another at Spiral Mountain.

Laval: And a third at the Grand Archway.

Cragger: I found the fourth one at the bottom of our swamp.

Tormak: The ravens had no idea that underneath their bottomless pile of junk was the fifth fire wing.

Laval: I would never cut down one of those magnificent trees in the Sanctuary Forest unless it was the only way to find the sixth fire wing.

Tormak: And this makes seven.

Cragger: Only one more to go, and with any luck, Eris has already found it. You guys coming?

Tormak: We will find you later, but now, there's something else we must take care of. Just be careful, and guard those fire wings.

(Li'Ella and Tormak leave)

(Forest)

Cragger: Oh, duh! Of course we're going to guard these fire wings, all of Chima depends on it.

Laval: The map says the final one is somewhere near the Forever Rock, that's where Eris will be.

Cragger: Ah, the Forever Rock, one of my favourite spots in all of Chima. Saving the best for last, right, Old Friend?

Laval: (Gasps when he sees the ice at Mount Cavora) It's just like Eris' vision: The ice towering mountain, destroying my home!

Cragger: Really? Did her vision have giant white bears too?

Laval: No. We need to get to my city now!

Cragger: Laval, the fire wings, saving Chima, remember? Besides, more troops are on the way.

Laval: (Gasps when he sees the bears charging to Mount Cavora in mechs)

Bears: (Roaring)

Laval: Bladvic? You look so awake, what's gotten into you guys?

Bladvic: Nap times over! Ice bears messing up everything. (Growls)

Cragger: Okay. But, where'd you get all that gear?

Bladvic: Always had it, too tired to use it, but now, ice bears make us angry! We wake up and put them to bed! (Roars)

Laval: Uh...

Cragger: Why don't we do our thing and let the bears beat bears? (Flies away)

(Lion Temple - outside)

(The bears charge towards the ice bears)

Bladvic: Prepare to be... Bear doomed!

(The bears battle it out and the normal bears seem to be winning)

(The Forever Rock)

Laval: Eris, tell me you found the final fire wing!

Eris: Well, not exactly.

Cragger: What? But that ice tower has almost reached Cavora!

Eris: I know. I've been flying all over this area with my fire wings but I found nothing. The fire wings should be around here. It just doesn't make any sense. (Screams as she sits on a rock and it catches on fire) Hello? You guys see this? Big flame on the Forever Rock!

(Cragger and Laval don't respond)

Eris: Wait, another vision. I don't have time for this! I've been searching forever and I still haven't found the last fire wing.

Fluminox: You and your friends have searched for a long time, Sky Born, but you have not searched "forever".

Laval: Eris! Are you okay?

Eris: (Wakes up) The flames, the rock, a message!

Cragger: Uh... Did you just hit your head when you fell off the rock?

Eris: No, it was a vision. Fluminox spoke to me: He said we had to search forever.

Laval: But we don't have forever! We need those fire wings now!

Eris: No, not that forever. This forever: The Forever Rock.

Cragger: Huh?

(Eris searches for the fire wing somewhere by the Forever Rock)

Cragger: Are you sure you didn't hit your head?

(Eris finds a secret area in the Forever Rock by a golden light and finds the final fire wing)

Laval: The final fire wing! It's been here all this time?

Eris: Yeah, but that's not all I learned in my vision. We need to get our friends from each of the eight main tribes. Laval, a roar please?

Laval: This time, why don't you do the honors?

Eris: Me? Really? Well, okay. (Screeches)

(Lion Temple - outside)

Bladvic: (Picks up Icepaw but hears the screech) Lucky for you, ice bear, I am needed elsewhere. (Goes to the Forever Rock)

Icepaw: Huh?

(Phoenix Temple)

Flinx: Dad, why are you smiling? The hunters are getting really close!

Fluminox: I do not smile for us, Son, I smile for Eris, the Sky Born. She truly has the heart of a phoenix.

Flinx: That's great, but what do we do about that giant ice tower outside?

Fluminox The Sky Born understands. She will help us launch the illumination.

Flinx: Yeah, well, I really hope she does it soon.

(Outside of the Temple, Lagravis and Lavertus watch as the ice tower reaches Mount Cavora, and there the hunters and fire tribes battle each other)

(The Forever Rock - night)

Eris: It's simple really, we just need to fly with our fire wings at super sonic speed into the heart of Mount Cavora.

Gorzan: Groovy.

Razar: No, seriously, My Friend.

Eris: Then we collide into the heart of Cavora and trigger the illumination.

Woriz: Huh?

Razar: Hm...

Rogon: Can we go back to the part where we have fire wings?

Worriz: How about a plan where we don't make a fateful high speed collision into a floating mountain?

Laval: Look, if we don't do this like Eris says, there's no illumination, the hunters win and Chima stays frozen forever! They're already climbing onto Mount Cavora! (Puts on a fire wing) I don't know about you, but I miss the old Chima: My non-frozen home, friends and family, and if I've got to fly full speed into a mountain to bring that back...

Cragger: (Puts on a fire wing) I'm going to take these wings and use them well, use them wisely!

Eris: For Chima!

Laval: So, who's with us?

Rogon: Ooh! I want to fly!

Gorzan: Me too!

Razar: Well, now that you put it this way, these hunters have been very bad for business, yes.

Bladvic: If it fixes those ice bears so we can all sleep well, I'm in.

(The others put on their fire wings and fly to Cavora)

Worriz: Whatever. (Puts on a fire wing and flies to Cavora with everyone else)

(Phoenix Temple - outside - night)

Sir Fangar: It is futile to fight the freeze!

(Phoenix Temple - inside)

(Everything freezes inside and slowly enters the room where Flinx and Fluminox are)

Flinx: Ah! Father, we must do something!

Fluminox: Fear not, My Child, the fire wings are on their way.

(The hunters smash through the door)

Sir Fangar: They were on their way.

(Sky - night)

(The heroes fly to Mount Cavora but are shot at)

Woriz: Hey! No fair!

Vultures: (Screeches as they go to attack)

Eris: Everyone, follow my lead! (Flies ahead)

Cragger: Oh, is that all we're supposed to do?

(The vultures confuse the heroes by knocking them into one another)

(Phoenix Temple - inside)

Flinx: Come on, Dad! We've got to fight!

Fluminox: So, Son, we have a much more important duty.

Sir Fangar: And what would that be, Teacher? Are you planning to mess up another illumination?

Flinx: Father, what's he talking about?

Fluminox: Hmph!

Sir Fangar: Oh, didn't he even tell you? We used to study under your father here. He taught us a perfect illumination fixes everything, but the last one was just... Well, a tiny bit off. I mean, do we look fixed?

(The hunters look at themselves and see themselves broken with ice)

Sir Fangar: And you've had to live hidden on a floating mountain for a thousand ages, how'd that work out for you? A perfect illumination requires nine phoenix, not eight. Your father was supposed to be that ninth phoenix. During the last illumination, he let his eight brother and sister phoenix fly into Mount Cavora, but when it came time for him to join them, to jump into the heart of Cavora and make their sacrifice complete, he failed them. He failed us all!

Flinx: What? Father, is this true?

Fluminox: It is as he says, My Son, but not as he believes. Yes, I was the ninth phoenix, but when destiny called I heard a much different cry. You were just a little baby back then, I could not abandon you. The illumination was imperfect, the hunters were frozen instead of cured and I suffered an eternity of sadness and guilt for my failure, but watching you blossom and grow, I have also enjoyed an eternity of pure love and joy. Yes, I've failed my tribe, my world, my destiny, but it was worth it for you, My Son Flinx, you have illuminated me.

Sir Fangar: Oh, boohoo! Blame the little brat for everything! (Puts out his blade) If that's the case, can we deal with him first?

Fluminox: Never! (Chi's up and begins to fight Sir Fangar)

(Sky - night)

Woriz: (Goes to fight a vulture but is sent flying when he's hit by a jet) Will you stop that!

Cragger: Ugh! There's too many of them!

(Phoenix Temple - inside)

(Sir Fangar and Fluminox continue fighting, eventually Fluminox lands a solid hit on Fangar though)

Sir Fangar: That's the spirit, Teacher! But I'm afraid this will be our final lesson, for your own students... (Chi's up) Graduated.

(All the hunters chi up and swarm Fluminox, pinning him to the ground)

Flinx: Dad!

Sir Fangar: (Laughs)

(Sky - Night)

(The others try to fly away from the vultures)

Razar: The fire wings are strong, yes, but we cannot defeat that!

Laval: Sure, they look unbeatable, but we carry the wings of the phoenix on our backs and the strength of Cavora in our souls! Nothing can stop. We are strong. We are Chima strong! (Flies into battle another time)

Cragger: Yeah! What he said!

(The two groups fly at each other, but then the phoenix airship blasts away some vultures)

Laval: Huh?

Tormak: Told you I had something to take care of. Now, it's blazing time! (Shoots at the vultures)

Laval: (Screams as he gets chased)

Li'Ella: I'll handle this one! (Goes outside and jumps on VoomVoom's jet)

VoomVoom: Hey! Whatchu doing?

Li'Ella: Stay away from my boyfriend! (Picks up VoomVoom and chucks him)

VoomVoom: Okay, I will!

Cragger: Woah, did you see what she just did to that vulture?

Laval: Did you hear how she just called me her boyfriend? Woohoo!

(All the heroes continue on to Mount Cavora)

(Phoenix Temple - inside)

Fluminox: (Breathes softly as he's pinned to the ground)

Sir Fangar: Chima is mine now, Phoenix King! My ice shall reign eternal!

Fluminox: Flinx, when the time comes you must succeed where I failed.

Flinx: Me? But I'm just a kid.

Fluminox: Not today. Today you are the ninth phoenix.

Sir Fangar: I don't think so.

Flinx: (Gasps)

Sir Fangar: (Laughs as he tries to push Flinx into a hole that goes into Cavora's heart while the heroes fly into it as well)

(Mount Cavora - inside)

8 Heroes: For Chima!

(Phoenix Temple - inside)

(A slight spark glows from inside Cavora)

Flinx: I am... (Jumps into Cavora and flies out with his wings glowing) The ninth phoenix!

The Hunters: (Screams as they're all pushed outside and to solid ground)

Flinx: (Screeches and laughs as he goes all around Chima, burning away all the ice)

(Chima)

Lavertus: You don't see that everyday, do you, Brother?

Lagravis: No, you do not.

(Flinx continues to burn away all the ice and along the way, cures Sir Fangar)

Sir Fangar: The fire. What is happening to me? I feel warm, I even feel love! Can it be? I am healing! (Laughs in rejoice)

Ewald: They're all cured! I must document this for our Eagle Library, Eglor.

Eglor: Sure, Ewald, but that books going straight into the completely ridiculous section.

Everyone: (Cheers)

(Tormak lands the airship on Mount Cavora)

Li'Ella: Laval? Laval, where are you? Father, tell me they're okay.

Tormak: (Returns to his original form) Fear not my daughter, I have a very good feeling about this illumination.

(Phoenix Temple - inside)

Gorzan: Dude!

Rogon: Bro!

(They all wake up and make their way to the top, only to see the phoenix heading for the sun)

Eris: The phoenix spoke to me for one last time but they didn't use words, it was more of a feeling, a sense that this time, the illumination was perfect. There was no longer any reason for them to stay, they had completed their task and would now ascend gloriously to their mother sun. As Fluminox promised, the illumination made everything and everyone better! Families reunited, new friends and old friends together again, the land and its creatures completely healed. This magical world, this paradise of wonder, completely restored! And when I say everything is better, I mean everything!

Cragger: What do you see out there, Laval?

Laval: Lots of things. I see hope, I see joy, I see the power of nature bringing us together instead of pulling us all apart. I see a world with balance and a future without limits! But most of all, I see the spirit of our friendship. I see... The Legends of Chima!

Cragger: Uh, quick question: How do we get down from here?

(End of the episode. For more information, click here.)
