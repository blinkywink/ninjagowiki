The same power that pulled down the mighty gorge, pushed up Mount Cavora! the illumination pulled so much heat from the Fangs that nothing has grown there since, and the hunters were left frozen in ice, deep inside the gorge. The great illumination left a powerful energy field, keeping us skyward and safe from intrusion. The mountain breathed in the moist breath of the sky, water now blessed by Cavora's illumination energy brought forth the chi, and the phoenix shared it with all.
