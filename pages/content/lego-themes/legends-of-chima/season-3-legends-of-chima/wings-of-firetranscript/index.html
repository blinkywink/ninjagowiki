Fluminox: we are the phoenix, ancient clan of the fire wings, masters of the fire chi, custodians of Cavora and most importantly, sacred guardians of Chima, but as guardians we have utterly failed you Chima. Oh, how we have failed you. The magical world of Chima suffocated by the spite of savages and the power of their ice, but this is not the first time we felt the fiend's fury. Thousands of ages ago, these same brutes devastated Chima. Back then Mount Cavora was still one with the land bellow and this tribe of hunters terrorized us all. Nothing could stop them, except the eight fire wings, the ultimate power of the phoenix. This is the great illumination, paradise restored with a few notable exceptions. Our sanctuary would be forever safe in the sky, as for the hunters, they would now feel the ice cold embrace of destiny forever. But as a wise young ground born said "forever is not as long as you think". The hunters awoke after thousands of years, they savaged Chima once again with a new understanding of the ice and its punishing powers. Again, an illumination could save us, but the eight fire wings are hidden throughout Chima, their locations marked on a sacred map. Alas, this powerful map destroyed itself in order to save itself. We are the phoenix and we have utterly failed you Chima. What happens next will become the greater story of our time, the most powerful and profound of all. The legends of Chima.
