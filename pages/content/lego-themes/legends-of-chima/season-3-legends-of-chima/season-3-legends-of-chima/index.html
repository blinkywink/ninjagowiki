Overview

"Chima. A magical world of untamed nature, beauty, and wonder."
This article is about a subject that pertains to the Legends of Chima franchise.

Chronological order
Previous Next
Season 2 "Merry Chima Everyone!"

The third and final season of Legends of Chima, occasionally subtitled Legend of the Fire Chi[1] is preceded by Season 2. Eris serves as the focus character, Sir Fangar serves as the main antagonist, and the Hunters serve as the villainous faction of the season.
