Overview

"Chima. A magical world of untamed nature, beauty, and wonder."
This article is about a subject that pertains to the Legends of Chima franchise.

Gadgets are tools that can be used to access new areas. If the player doesn't have a certain gadget to pass the structure, then the player cannot move on. This is to prevent players to reach hard areas for their current level.

Gadgets can be created in a crafting building when the player has acquired the Building Instruction for the gadget. The Building Instruction is given after completing a certain goal.

Gadget List

Gadget List[]

Sonic Boomer[]

Sonic Boomer is the first gadget. It is used to deactivate energy fields, and when deactivated, they can pass on to newer and harder areas. It requires 10 minutes to craft. The instructions are acquired after you defeat Crawley in the first dungeon.

Description: Eliminate energy fields and demoralize your enemies!

Builder[]

Builder is another gadget. The builder can assemble a pile of bricks into a bridge. When the bridge is assembled, the player can pass on to newer and harder areas. It requires 3 hours, 20 minutes to craft. 

Description: Assemble bricks, build bridges or confuse enemies with this handy gadget.

Zappler[]

Zappler is a tool that can generate power to charge a device. When the device is fully charged, the player has a few seconds to go to the new area. It requires 4 hours, 40 minutes to craft. The instructions for the Zappler are given to you by Reegul the first time you free him from Wilhurt.

Description: Use this portable power generator to charge devices and shock enemies!

Feeder[]

Description: Consumes and breaks down matter to its basic elements. The Feeder is given to you by Reegul the second time you defeat Wilhurt. It is used to break down the Tears of Cavora.

Grappling Hook[]

Grappling Hook is another useful gadget. This gadget allows the user to reach new and higher points. The instructions for the Tornado are given to you by Reegul the second time you defeat Wilhurt.

Description: Use this grappler to hook enemies, or for those hard to reach areas!

Tornado[]

Tornado is the last gadget that the player needed to build before the Outlands expansion was added. It's used to remove harmful gases from plants. Again, the player has a few seconds to move on, otherwise, it will be harmed. The instructions for the Tornado are given to you when you defeat Runk and Rukus (outside of a dungeon).

Description: Summon a mighty tornado to blow away enemies - and harmful gases!

Gloves[]

Gloves are the required to assess the Outlands. This gadget allowed the player to transport heavy/large obstacles such as boulders. The player could then choose to put the obstacles down to activate a button, or throw the obstacle at an enemy.
