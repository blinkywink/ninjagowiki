Overview



The Legends of Chima Magazine, also titled Chima Magazine is a Legends of Chima magazine series published in various European countries.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Various magazine comics were republished in Papercutz Chima graphic novel series.
  • The last several issues tell an interconnected story of the Quest for the Fire Wings. This story has direct contradictions with the last two episodes of the series:
    • Tormak briefly works with the Hunters after becoming a panther due to still thinking that Li'Ella is captured.
    • The heroes acquired the first Fire Wings without Tormak's help.
    • The first Fire Wings went to Laval instead of Eris.
