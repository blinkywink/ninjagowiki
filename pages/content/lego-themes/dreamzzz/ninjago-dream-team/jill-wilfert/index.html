Jill Wilfert

Information

Occupation

Head of Global Entertainment Partners & Content at Lego

Gender

Female

Overview

Jill Wilfert is the Head of Global Entertainment Partners & Content at the Lego Group.[1] She has served as an executive producer on Ninjago, The LEGO Ninjago Movie, and Ninjago: Dragons Rising.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • For her work producing Ninjago and Ninjago: Dragons Rising, she was nominated for Leo Awards in 2022, 2023, and 2024.
  • She has also served as an executive producer on The Lego Movie, The Lego Batman Movie, The Lego Movie 2: The Second Part, Piece By Piece, Lego DREAMZzz, and many other Lego film and television projects.
