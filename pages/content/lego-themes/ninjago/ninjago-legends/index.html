Overview

Ninjago: Legends is a subtheme of Ninjago sets introduced in 2025. It's first year's campaign was focused on Kai's journey in the Land of Monsters, with the mini-series Ninjago Legends: Monstrosity being released in 2025. The second year's campaign, Ninjago Legends: Duskfall, will be released in 2026, and will focus on a new generation of ninja.[1]
