Overview

Ninjago: Legends is the third installment of the Ninjago franchise. It is an anthology series comprised of short mini-series. Its first two installments, Monstrosity and Kai's Monstrous Journey, released in 2025, were focused on Kai's journey in the Land of Monsters. The third installment, Duskfall, will be released in 2026, and will focus on a new generation of ninja.[1]
