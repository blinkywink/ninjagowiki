Overview

Ninjago: Dragons Rising is the second series of the Ninjago franchise. It is a CGI comedy-action family television show and the sequel of Ninjago. The show was first announced at San Diego Comic Con 2022 and it began with its first season on June 1, 2023 on Netflix.

Official description

Official description[]

In the series, many legendary realms have suddenly combined into one, but their union is unstable. A Spinjitzu Ninja Master must train a new generation of heroes to help find Elemental Dragons who can save the planet before the forces of evil use the same dragon energy to destroy this new world.[1]

Two teenagers from different worlds use their newly discovered Ninja powers to defend dragons from villains who want to use their life-force for evil.[2]

Premise

Premise[]

The loosely East-Asian inspired, anachronistic, modern-esque setting of Ninjago was once the only populated continent on a mostly empty planet. After the event known as "The Merge" takes place, the fantastical and varied Sixteen Realms have been combined into one world, with new islands and continents surrounding and colliding with Ninjago and the Dark Island. In the Merged Realms, otherworldly beings and locations that were only seen sparingly are commonplace, and no longer separated by alternate dimensions.

In the previous series, the world of Ninjago was protected from the forces of evil by a team of six elemental ninja (Lloyd, Kai, Nya, Jay, Zane, Cole), who were trained in the art of Spinjitzu by the wise and ancient Master Wu, son of the First Spinjitzu Master. After a cataclysmic event combined the Sixteen Realms into a single entity, the Merged Lands, the world was forever changed and the ninja were among the many presumed lost, displaced across the land. Years later, Green Ninja Lloyd Garmadon, and his fellow classic ninja mentor the next generation of teen heroes - Arin, Sora, Wyldfyre, Frak and a young dragon named Riyu - to master their powers and learn the way of the ninja. New friends, legendary ninja, and mystical dragons are united to protect the Merged Realms from villains who want to conquer the new world.

Main plot

Main plot[]

Season 1 (2023)[]

The Merge brought together the Sixteen Realms, resulting in the dispersion of the ninja across the diverse lands. Years later, Lloyd encounters Arin, a self-taught Spinjitzu prodigy who looks up to the ninja, alongside Sora, who's from the oppressive Kingdom of Imperium, and a baby Mountain Dragon named Riyu. Together, they uncover a plot orchestrated by the Claws of Imperium, led by Ras, who are on a relentless quest to hunt dragons throughout the Merged Realms and siphon their life force to empower the Kingdom of Imperium, which is under the tyrannical rule of Beatrix, who ascended to power after Ras eliminated Levo and exiled Zeatrix. Among the dragons targeted are the Source Dragons, beings of immense power that Beatrix and Ras intend to subjugate in their quest for dominion over the realms. As Lloyd, Arin, and Sora reunited with Nya and Kai, they embarked on a journey to Imperium, where Beatrix was utilizing the Photacs—an invention of Sora's childhood—in her campaign to capture dragons. During their mission, the group reunites with Zane and encounters Wyldfyre. Lloyd and Zane would eventually confront the Source Dragon of Energy, who was contained in a prison that destabilized the realms since his capture. The Source Dragon made Lloyd his conduit, granting him his power to facilitate the dragons' escape back to Ninjago, with some choosing to remain with the ninja. Following his failures, Ras was imprisoned, prompting Beatrix to declare her intention to join the battle personally.

To stop the escalating danger posed by MergeQuakes, the ninja learned about the Dragon Cores, ancient relics created by the First Spinjitzu Master to prevent the realms from merging during his era. They divided into teams to locate the cores, racing against Empress Beatrix, who seeks to claim them to destroy the ninja. During their quest, Nya and Sora encounter Cole, who had been living in the Land of Lost Things among the Finders. Despite their efforts, Beatrix ultimately manages to get the Dragon Cores, using them for a weapon against the ninja by deliberately instigating her own MergeQuakes. Lloyd used the cores to stop the MergeQuakes and free the Source Dragon of Energy. With Beatrix's defeat, the Kingdom of Imperium is reformed. However, Lord Ras, who had been using Beatrix for his own gain, escapes and aligns himself with Jordana, who had a vial of Source Energy derived from the Energy Source Dragon.

Season 2 (2024)[]

Over a year after the Liberation of Imperium, Lloyd began having visions of the Wolf Clan and the Blood Moon, unable to properly sleep or rest at all. Meanwhile, Ras and Jordana discovered the Shadow Dojo and reassembled the Wolf Clan, launching an invasion on the Cloud Kingdom for intel on the Ritual of the Blood Moon that could free the Forbidden Five, a group of centuries-old Elemental Masters hailing from the Wyldness. The ninja attempted to stop them, but only struggled due to Cinder, the new Elemental Master of Smoke, preceding Ash, used Shatterspin, a corrupted and advanced form of Spinjitzu created by the Forbidden Five, to defeat them. After another vision from the Source Dragon of Motion, the ninja travel to the Dragon Dojo, where they meet the Dragon Masters, Egalt and Rontu, and learn how to perform Rising Dragon technique, a fighting style created by the Dragon Masters and the only known techinque capable of countering Shatterspin. As the Wolf Clan also targets the Land of Lost Things to capture Bonzle, a living spell taken physical form created by Gandalaria, the high sorceress of Mysterium, that could potentially free the Forbidden Five, Bonzle returns to her home realm with Zane and Cole, seeking her creator's advice for safety. Just before the Blood Moon petrifies Egalt and Rontu, Kai masters the Rising Dragon technique and confronts Ras alongside the other ninja, who planned to sacrifice Euphrasia, a new ally of the ninja team and the new Elemental Master of Wind, preceding Morro, and some of the Writers of Destiny in the Nether-Space, a void outside of time and space holding the Forbidden Five. Although Ras succeeding in kidnapping Bonzle and opened a portal, the ninja held their ground against the Wolf Clan at the Shadow Dojo to stop the ritual, while Cole, Zane, and Gandalaria worked to save the Cloud Kingdom from a narrow demise, Euphrasia joining later on to better its safety. The ritual was ultimately thwarted, but Kai and Bonzle were left trapped in the Nether-Space, while Nokt, the first and strongest member of the Forbidden Five, as well as the Elemental Master of Brute Force, was freed. Afterwards, the Wolf Clan and the Shadow Dojo disappeared, sent to an unknown location, known as the Land of Eternal Dawn, by Ras' master.

Some months later, Arin, Sora, and Riyu go to the Administration to find Arin's parents but discover all records are missing with Agent Walker. They narrowly escape capture and meet the dying Matriarch, who gives Lloyd a map and the Source Dragon of Motions warns that the killer might target her kind by partaking the Tournament of the Sources. Meanwhile, Kai and Bonzle evade the Forbidden Five, who are plotting their escape. In the City of Temples, they encounter Roby, Frak, Bleckt, the Wolf Clan, Zeatrix, and Jay, who has allied with Ras, creating tension. Arin learns from Ras that Sora deceived him about his Object Spinjitzu and that Wu caused the Merge, straining his relationships with them. Cole discovers the Monastery of Gates, and during the tournament, Lloyd was attacked by Zeatrix. Frak rebels against Ras and is expelled from the Wolf Clan after losing to Sora. Injured, Lloyd withdraws, leaving Sora and Nokt to compete in the final. Arin and Cole witness what seems to be Wu's spirit, confirming his role in the Merge. Nokt wins the tournament, which was rigged by Bleckt, and claimed all the Elemental Powers, while Rox takes control of the Wolf Clan and attacks. Nokt frees the Forbidden Five and battles the ninja and Ras, who reluctantly team up while Lloyd uncovers Ras' influence over Arin. Riyu and Nya rescue Kai and Bonzle from the Nether-Space. After a confrontation, Roby retrieves the stolen Elemental Powers from Nokt. The Forbidden Five escape with Ras hostage, while Arin pursues them, leading to the destruction of a portal gate caused by the Five and marooning them all in another realm. Ultimately, Sora was declared Champion of the Sources, Wyldfyre and Roby became a couple, Frak joined the ninja, Nya and Kai embarked on a quest to find Jay, the Source Dragon of Motion honored Lloyd with a Dragon Icon, and Arin helped Ras heal so they could search for his parents.

Season 3 (2025)[]

With Arin and Jay missing, the ninja team split up to search for them: Lloyd, Sora, Frak, Zane, and Riyu focused on finding Arin, while Kai, Nya, and Wyldfyre searched for Jay. Zane eventually reunited with P.I.X.A.L. after parting ways in the search for Arin. Meanwhile, Arin continued his training under Ras, hoping to locate his parents, but first, he needed to heal Ras from injuries sustained during the fight with Nokt. With Morro's help, Arin obtained a healing elixir from the Spectral Lands and learned that Wu was still alive. At the same time, the Forbidden Five were on a mission to free Thunderfang, a vengeful dragon intent on destroying the Merged Realms by using an army of corrupted Dragonians to retrieve the Prismatic Blades necessary for his release from underneath the First Land. During his quest for his parents, Arin discovered their gravestones in the Land of Lee, leading him to believe they died in the Merge, which motivated him to help Ras undo the Merge to bring them back. Meanwhile, Jay, now a bounty hunter known as Rogue, was hired by Zeatrix to capture Ras. The ninja, with a Prismatic Blade in their possession, eventually encountered Arin and Ras, who possessed the final Prismatic Blade needed to free Thunderfang. However, Drix and the Dragonian Warriors arrived, kidnapping Ras and seizing the last two blades. In a bid to rescue Ras, Arin joined with the ninja, who used the portal gates to reach the First Land and battled the Forbidden Five and the Dragonians. Unfortunately, Arin's desperation to save Ras allowed the Forbidden Five to escape with the Prismatic Blades, freeing Thunderfang, who then betrayed them by stealing their souls. Thunderfang battled the ninja with the intention of seizing Lloyd's soul to enhance his own power, discovering that Lloyd was a conduit for a Source Dragon. Although Thunderfang succeeded in capturing Lloyd's soul, Arin intervened by using the healing elixir on Thunderfang, which ultimately led to Thunderfang's demise as he departed with the souls of the Forbidden Five. In the aftermath, Lloyd regained his soul, and all the other souls Thunderfang had taken were freed. Meanwhile, Arin, driven by the quest to find his parents, teamed up with Ras and was joined by Sora. At the same time, Jay, who had sustained a leg injury during the fight, was taken to the Monastery of Spinjitzu, where he revealed to the ninja that Arin's parents were alive in the Land of Lee. As Thunderfang's soul transitioned to the Spectral Lands, he began his campaign to collect enough souls, commanding the Forbidden Five and the Spectral Dragonians as his army, but not before collecting the souls of the Soul Suckers as well as Morro's.

Following the battle with Thunderfang, the ninja team is out of sorts; Arin and Sora are journeying with Ras towards the Underworld, seeking to reverse the Merge and bring back Arin’s parents. During their quest, they defend Abracalabria from Kur and Spectral Dragonians while searching for the Underworld. However, during the fight, Gandalaria had been drained of her soul, compelling them to seek an alternative route to their destination. Concurrently, the remaining ninja make their way to the Dragon Dojo to pay their respects to Egalt, who is succumbing to Wasting Sickness. In his last breaths, Egalt tells Wyldfyre she will be a great leader one day, reflects on his treatment of Arin with regret, and urges Lloyd to locate Arin and Sora to continue their training. He bids farewell to Rontu as he passes away. Lloyd and Nya hired Jay to take them, accompanied by Wyldfyre, to the Land of Lee so they can find Arin’s parents. Upon reaching the Land of Lee, they encounter Lee, who runs the whole land and has a small community, which they investigate. After running into Jenny and Barry, the group hatches a plan to get out of the Land of Lee. Meanwhile, Riyu and Frak were being trained; Riyu trained under Rontu, while Frak, with Lloyd occupied elsewhere, received guidance from Cole and Kai in the Land of Lost Things. During his training, Frak senses the screaming earth, reminiscent of Cole's earlier experiences. Cole mentioned how he was instructed by Wu to uncover the source of the disturbance, but failed. Together, they went out and discovered that it was the remains of the Source Dragon of Balance. Meanwhile, in the Underworld, Arin, Sora, and Ras discover the Forge of the Forgotten, where Ras planned to sacrifice Sora to the Casket of Bones to create a Dragon Icon. However, Arin, who strongly opposes this plan, confronts Ras, leading to a conflict that forces him to escape with Sora back to the ninja group. In the aftermath, Ras ultimately sacrifices another individual to successfully create a Dragon Icon. With Arin now reunited with his parents and the team, the ninja learn that Thunderfang is back and plan a way to stop him once more, so they head to the City of Temples to learn more. Upon returning to the Land of Lost Things, Kai, Cole, and Frak found that Nokt and the Spectral Dragonians had drained the souls of the Finders, excluding Geo. They made their way to the City of Imperium to assist in defending the citizens against the specters while Kai and Frak had their Prismatic Blades upgraded. However, Cole, who attacks Nokt in revenge for draining the Finders, gets his soul taken and is hollow. In the City of Temples, the ninja discover that to defeat Thunderfang, they must awaken the Arc Dragon of Focus through a spell song and the Unbidden Storm. However, they are cautioned about the repercussions of acting without the Source Dragons’ consent. The team splits up: Lloyd and Arin venture to the Spectral Lands to awaken the dragon, while Nya and later Kai, after Jay bails on the team, head to the Cave of Bells in Mysterium. Meanwhile, Sora, Wyldfyre, Frak, and Riyu make their way to the Cave of Bells in Djinjago. During this time, Ras invaded the Monastery of Spinjitzu to steal information from Wu's studies. After each team is successful, Lloyd and Arin guide the Arc Dragon of Focus to defeat Thunderfang once and for all. With him gone for good, everyone’s souls are restored, and the Merged Lands are saved. The Arc Dragon praises their success but warns them about the repercussions. Angered by the unauthorized awakening of the Focus Arc Dragon, the Source Dragons of Life, Focus, and Strength withdraw from the council, leaving only the Source Dragons of Energy, Motion, and Flow. This withdrawal causes several consequences as a result, with one being that the Elemental Powers become unstable and unreliable. Unaware of this, the three teams try heading back to Ninjago but are split up, Lloyd and Arin end up in the Whispering Wood, Kai, Jay and Nya are stranded in the Land of Madness inside a Quealiche and Sora, Wyldfyre, Frak and Riyu are stranded in the Land of Eternal Dawn. As it turns out, Ras was able to find and capture Wu, taking control of the gates, thus stranding the ninja so they don’t interrupt his plans. Arin, who asks Lloyd what they will do now that the portal gates aren’t working properly and Elemental Powers are unstable, Lloyd comforts his student by telling him they will find a way back to Ninjago.

Season 4 (2026)[]

Shortly after the Battle of the Spectral Lands, Dr. LaRow arrived at the Shrine of the Seven Sources, where she witnessed the resurgence of the Elemental Monsters, conjured up by the Weapon of Desolation. Recognizing the potential of these creatures, she commanded the Loyalists to locate the weapon, intending to harness the Elemental Monsters for their own purposes. Meanwhile, Lloyd and Arin were surprised when they encountered Euphrasia, who had also found herself stranded in the Whispering Wood after passing through the portal gate from the Cloud Kingdom. After discussing the reasons behind her inability to use her powers and uncovering the ninja's plans, Euphrasia decided to join Lloyd and Arin on their quest back to Ninjago. Meanwhile, Kai, Jay, and Nya have also started figuring out how to get back to Ninjago, but Jay, in the meantime, catches the two up on why he left the City of Temples and truly wants their help in regaining his forgotten memories. After escaping the Quealiche monster they were stranded in, they head over to The Administration to confront Agent Blake Donovan, who may have had something to do with Jay's intentional memory loss, to which Donovan tells the three ninja that, to regain his memories, they must head to the Department of Vacation Simulation to do it. Elsewhere, Sora, Frak, Wyldfyre, and Riyu traverse through the Land of Eternal Dawn, which suspiciously, causes Sora and Frak to start experiencing hallucinations of their favorite things, like cats and pies, Unaffected by this new realm's naturality and determined to get back home, Wyldfyre and Riyu shove themselves and their friends down a hole, where they find themselves in a hilly, dark village known as Umbrasilvis, where they meet Frak's father, Sir Reginald Slitherbottom, a member of the Explorer's Club. The young ninja try to gain his and his team's help in getting back to Ninjago, but they seem more worried about catching a Mutation Monster to put on display back home, and after Frak and Reginald discuss their differences regarding their relationship, Frak angrily brushes his father off and departs from the village with the others. Back in the Whispering Wood, Lloyd, Arin, and Euphrasia come across the Arc Dragon of Life, the mortal avatar for the Source Dragon of Life, who discloses to Lloyd that they have apparently been waiting for him for quite some time and tells the Green Ninja about the Ritual of Unrooting, in which he can unlock the power he skipped out on during his youth to restore the Whispering Wood of its decaying state and stabilize the unbalanced Elemental Powers. While searching for the Temple of the Green Ninja, the trio catch up with Zane, who appears to have changed quite drastically since they last saw him, and journey to the temple together, where Lloyd enters and begins his trial. During this time, he meets and encounters three metaphysical manifestations of himself, who either guide him through or attack him during the remainder of the trial; those being Young Lloyd, representing Lloyd's prior bratty self before joining the ninja and embracing his role and destiny as the Green Ninja, who also leads him toward the roots of his powers, Tween Lloyd, representing the age Lloyd unintentionally skipped over due to the Tomorrow's Tea, and thus, who also teaches him about his true power, which is actually referred to as Life, and that its ability is for him to connect to everything and everyone close to him, including alternate versions of himself, to unlock his True Potential, and then, he encounters Enemy Lloyd, representing Lloyd's internal fears, doubts, and mistakes since he became a leader, master, and a mentor figure, who is also considered to be his "greatest enemy". After enduring these experiences for some time, he completes the Ritual of Unrooting and unlocks a new power: the Draconus Form. Back with Kai, Jay and Nya, they were successfully in restoring Jay's memories, which are slowly coming back to the Lightning Ninja's mind in slow waves. They then start making their escape out of The Administration, only to make their way inside of a cave where they behold a strange tree containing a piece of the Weapon of Desolation and vow to take it from their newfound enemies when they learn a shocking revelation. Following the Liberation of Imperium, it turns out that, former "Good Empress" -- Beatrix, survived her previous encounter with the ninja, having ended up in the Land of Monsters as a result of Arin sending her through one of her own newly made MergeQuake rifts, where Dr. LaRow found her and -- having been horrendously 'fused' with her Empress Mech and having undergone a grueling transformation into a cybernetic monster due to battling the monsters roaming the land for months -- promised to restore Imperium back to what it once was. After gathering the two fragments, she and the Loyalists perform an enchantment, thus activating the weapon for Beatrix to control. Learning that Beatrix has returned to power, the ninja eventually reunite in Imperium and take on Beatrix, her Loyalists, and a whole army of Elemental Monsters. At the climax of the battle, Lloyd connects with his fellow ninja, restoring all of their powers -- both old and new -- and enables his original team to transform into Draconus Forms of their own, which Kai, Jay, Zane, and Nya put to good use and help take out the remaining monsters while Lloyd defeats Beatrix, saving the Whispering Wood and the Merged Lands. After the Battle of Imperium, Dr. LaRow was imprisoned and interrogated by the ninja about the Weapon of Desolation and her plans to restore Beatrix as Empress, to which afterwards, the ninja unite and return home to Ninjago, at the Monastery of Spinjitzu, celebrating their victory as Sora recounted her experiences in Umbrasilvis to Arin. But to her surprise, she received unexpected visitors -- her parents, who had seemingly come to see her. In the Land of Eternal Dawn, though, the Monastery of Gates was present unexpectedly, as Ras emerged through a portal gate, shouting to his master that he had obtained another Source Dragon Icon, with the Shadow Dojo straight across from him.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This series was originally a Netflix original series in the United States, unlike the previous series. However, at some point in January of 2024, Netflix removed the "Original Series" branding.
  • While the LEGO brand is absent in each episode's on-screen title, the show is usually branded under the title "LEGO Ninjago: Dragons Rising" in the marketing and on streaming platforms.
  • This series also is available in Disney+ Hotstar in India.
  • LEGO Ambassador Network member DuckBricks relayed information he received during a LAN meeting with Asa Tait and Niek van Slagmaat:[4]
    • Overview of premise: "A cataclysmic event combines the sixteen mystic realms of Ninjago, both familiar and new, into one world. Two teenagers from different worlds (literally) set out to become heroic ninja and use their newly discovered powers to defend the secret dragons, who will unite their world forever or destroy it completely."
    • Overview of dragons in the series: "The bond between dragons and ninjas runs deep. Dragons hold the keys to Elemental Powers, and the secret of mysterious new dragons which have the power to save the newly united world or destroy it forever."
    • Overview of the ninja's role in the series: "We have new main characters, but the original heroes of Ninjago are still here fighting the good fight. Lloyd, Arin, and Sora will remain the heart of this team, but the makeup of the team can and will change from year to year. Other slots will be filled with a mix of new and returning ninja each year."
      • In response to follow-up questions he asked:
        • "None of the original six ninja are really going anywhere, but there will be some years where they just simply will not feature that much in the story itself, however that doesn't mean that they will be completely absent from the main story. While they may not feature in the main TV show, we could see some shorts, comics books, all sorts of smaller episodes where you could see each of the ninja on their own adventures, showing that basically since the world has expanded, the different team members are kind of going to be off doing their own thing."
    • From a slide titled The Future of Ninjago: "This is now a show that is serialized from season to season. It's not going to be like something we're used to with Prime Empire and Master of the Mountain, for example, being completely unrelated. You could pick up one season and just pick up another one and they would not really tie into each other. No, this is a serialized show now, which means they are building to a massive conclusion, even more so than Crystalized, and this has been planned out for years to come".
      • "Ninjago is pivoting to 'huge stories' that run across years".
      • There will be new characters to "join competing Spinjitzu dojos".
        • In Season 2, there is the Shadow Dojo led by Lord Ras which acts as a counterpoint to the ninja with their fighting technique Shatterspin.
        • Another art martial dojo is introduced in this season, the Training plateau of the Dragon Masters, led by the Dragon Masters Egalt and Rontu, where they teach Spinjitzu and Rising Dragon technique.
      • "New lands filled with new dragons".
      • "Builds on the characters and lore that we love".
    • The reason for the focus on dragons is that in 2021, a survey was ran and research was done with kids that showed that they wanted dragons to be front and center of the new story, according to Nigel Kong, the LEGO Group's Head of Product.
    • According to Asa Tait, they wanted the bond between the heroes and their dragons to replicate the feel of the earlier seasons, instead of them only being energy beasts the Ninja can summon at will.
    • There is a 3-4, and possibly up to 6-year planned story.[5]
      • Kevin Burke states they'll make more seasons if the public keeps watching.[6]
      • Chris "Doc" Wyatt confirmed that the ending of the show is planned, but its timing remains confidential.[7]
        • He also stated that the end of Dragons Rising will not be the end of Ninjago or its canon installments.[8]
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt confirmed all seasons will involve dragons.[9]
  • Unreal Engine 5 is utilized in the development of the show.[10][11][12]
  • It takes roughly 18-24 months to make an episode of the series.[13]
  • It appears each season will focus on a different source and concept embodied by one of the Source Dragons.
    • Season 1 focuses on Energy.
    • Season 2 focuses on Motion
    • Season 3 focuses on Focus (and its opposite Chaos)
    • Season 4 focuses on Life.
  • Like with the original series, the seasons have special weapons or artifacts that are important parts of the main plot.
    • The Dragon Cores in Season 1.
    • The Mallet and Gong of Shattering in Season 2.
    • The Prismatic Blades & Reveal Blades in Season 3.
    • The Weapon of Desolation in Season 4.
  • The series' working title was Ninjago: United, referencing to the realms merging.[14]
  • Unlike the seasons of the original series, the seasons of Dragons Rising are split into two parts with a timeskip in between. Each part covers its own 10 episode story arc within the larger plot of the season.
    • This is reflected with how the aesthetics of the season's intro changes when the second part begins, and how there are set waves dedicated to each part, both of which began in Season 2.
