Overview

Early life

Early life[]

Sora was born in the Kingdom of Imperium from her parents who named her Ana.

She grew up in the Imperian society ruled by the dictatorial regime of Empress Beatrix. Sora was very smart and inventive and was passionate about science and technology since she was little, but was often neglected by her parents for her prodigal mind. An avid fan of Dr. LaRow, Ana dreamed of working for her in the Advanced Systems Lab one day.

One time, Ana once broke down a vacuum cleaner to make a robot to do her chores. When her mother found out, she was angry that Ana hid in the tunnels and ended up lost for two days.

When her latest invention, the Photac, won at her school's science fair, Dr. LaRow personally brought Ana to her lab to hire her. At the lab, Dr. LaRow showed Ana the source of Imperium's power, a dragon nicknamed Sora before doing upgrades to her device to make ferocious animals, as opposed to harmless ones.

Realizing that they were going to use her invention to hunt dragons and harm them in their quest to remain powerful, she refused to work for her.

This action caused her to be despised, shunned, and disowned upon by everyone in Imperium, including her parents, who eventually confiscated her work bench and inventions, demanding she'd find a new interest if she won't use her talents to help Imperium.

While in her room, one day, The Merge occurred before a shockwave came soon after, and Ana discovered a new city in the distance. Deciding to run away from home, she takes her headphones, a few possessions, and changes her name to Sora, after the dragon. As a result of the Merge, Sora unknowingly ended up with the Elemental Power of Technology when the Source Dragon of Focus created it.

After the Merge

After the Merge[]

Crashing Together (flashback)[]

Arriving in Ninjago, Sora went to the Crossroads, a newly established neighborhood created as the home of those who had nowhere else to go following the Merge. Dying of hunger, Sora stole fruit from a Merlopian shopkeeper and ran away. The latter then sent people after her and in her escape Sora ran into a boy named Arin who was carrying pies and who had lost his parents in the Merge. Arin helped her camouflage herself by making her pass as a pie seller with him. Then he offered her some pie and they became friends. Getting used to the Crossroads customs, Sora then stayed there, moving in a penthouse with Arin. She also adopted her own look and started building things, starting to live the life she always dreamed. She also met Alfonzo Frohicky, with whom her and Arin became friends.

Sora would build inventions and mechs and she began participating in street mech races such as the Mech Master 5000 with Arin.

Tales from the Merged Realms[]

The Way In[]

To prepare for the Mech Master 5000, Sora and Arin worked together, using several rusty and heavily damaged parts they had scavenged in hopes of building a mech to race with. As they worked, Sora expressed her desire not only just to win, but to completely crush the competition, to leave them all in the dust and especially Kreel! No sooner than saying her rival's names, Kreel stepped into the teens' penthouse and commented on their work, boasting that she would win the race as always and that Sora didn't stand a chance. To further rub salt in the wound, Kreel turned to Sora specifically and let her know that the registration fee price had increased, knowing fully well that the teens couldn't afford it. After Kreel left their penthouse, an utterly defeated Sora was ready to give up, but Arin managed to talk her out of it.

The next day, Sora went dumpster diving in search of more parts, though she was only able to find a handful of gears, valves, and a suction tube in shoddy condition. Feeling defeated yet again, she sat about the penthouse she shared with Arin, feeling dejected while the latter baked a pie, hoping the sweet treat would cheer her up. When Arin finished his appleberry pie, Sora realized that they could use it in lieu of money for their registration fee and hastily got to work making some further improvements to her mech.

A few hours later, Sora participated in the Mech Master 5000. Although things seemed to shoot off to a great start for her, even managing to overtake Kreel for a few minutes, Sora's mech unfortunately began to collapse due to the shoddy quality of its construction materials. In the end, Sora finished in last place but didn't let the loss discourage her. Instead, she felt more determined than ever to continue to make improvements to her mech and hopefully start winning some races! In fact, there was another one for prize money only a week away!

Crossroads Carnival[]

Now residing in the monastery, Sora, Arin, and Riyu made the recreation room as their bedroom.

When woken up by Lloyd, Sora was the only one unhappy about being woken up for training at an early hour and wants field training, to which Arin instantly knew she wanted to go to the Crossroads Carnival, to which Lloyd wouldn't allow it before, after having trouble due to faulty gears, revealing the training course. He intended to time them with a cup of tea, but had burnt it, leading to Sora wondering why before Lloyd showed them a shrine to dedicate his uncle, Wu, and tried to show them he grew a beard, but only sees a sprouting tiny hair before they start training. While Arin did exceedingly well, Sora, due to slight sleep deprivation, was rag-dolled, hitting a wall, tossed into the pond, jabbed in her rear end by Lloyd, and bounced about on platforms.

As the day came to an end, an irritated Sora voiced her complaints to Arin as they sat in the Monastery, wondering if every day of training was going to be like today. Believing that they were deserving of a break, Sora suggested that she and Arin ought to sneak out to the Crossroads Carnival. Although her friend was hesitant at first, Sora managed to convince him by insisting that sneaking out would be a test of their stealth skills and that Arin's pie smelled like a first place winning one to her! When Riyu expressed interest in tagging along, Sora agreed to bring him with them, though she noted that he would need a disguise.

Sometime later, Sora, Arin, and Riyu marveled their way across the carnival, pointing out various sights and wonders as Arin submitted his pie into the pie-baking contest. Unfortunately, the teens' sense of wonder and amusement would be broken by the arrival of Kreel's Whack Rats, who wasted no time in kidnapping Lobbo as the robot was sharing the prize money he had won from the Mech Master 5000. Although Arin quickly followed in pursuit with his grapple gun, Sora's attention was dragged towards the remains of her SA Mark 01 by Riyu. Despite initially protesting that repairing the mech would take hours at best, Sora was given a powerup from Riyu and immediately reconfigured it into a motorbike, giving her the speed necessary to chase down Kreel's goons.

After arriving in Kreel's Junkyard, Sora briefly fought against several Whack Rats on her bike, briefly pausing after defeating them to remark in surprise that she actually used some of the skills Lloyd had tried to teach her and Arin earlier that morning. When Kreel lamented that her friend Targle had been kidnapped, Sora and Arin cut her a deal: in exchange for rescuing Targle, Kreel would leave Lobbo and his money alone. Kreel agreed and provided the teenagers with the location of the drop point that she was supposed to meet the kidnapper at.

Following the information given to them by Kreel, Sora and Arin made their way to a back alley where Riyu discovered a strange lever hidden behind a dumpster. With a strong pull, the lever revealed the entrance to a hidden lair which the teens ventured deeper into. There, they discovered Targle and several other Whack Rats imprisoned inside of a cage with their tormentor being none other than the famed pyrotechnician and showrunner, Dorama! After initial attempts of talking to the deranged thespian failed, the latter activated his theater's many traps and weapon systems in an effort to kill Sora and Arin, occasionally siphoning energy from the Whack Rats into his staff to blast them with as well. Now stuck on a sliding conveyor belt while dodging swinging pendulum axes and bursts of fire, Sora took a moment to lament that Lloyd was right earlier and there really are times where one would find themselves needing obstacle course training in real life. Although she made strong efforts to avoid the many deadly weapons of Dorama's obstacle course, Sora eventually fumbled and braced herself as a jet of flame erupted towards her, only to be blocked by one of the swinging axe blades as Arin shut down the obstacle course.

Unfortunately, the battle was far from over and Dorama unveiled his massive puppet to attack the teenagers with. Even with Lloyd's timely arrival, the puppet managed to grab Sora and squeezed her tightly as it lifted her into the air. Struggling for breath, Sora desperately tried to fight her way out from the marionette's grasp, but found herself unable to do so. Luckily, an improvised attack from Arin followed by Lloyd's recollection of Sora's earlier comments, managed to down the puppet and release her from its grasp.

With Dorama defeated and the Whack Rats rescued, Sora returned to Kreel's Junkyard with her allies. After returning Targle to Kreel, Sora apologized to Lloyd for taking his meditation carpet and expressed hope that all three of them would be able to find a way to work together in time. With master and students reacquainted, the group made their way over to see the results of the pie-baking contest and Lloyd agreed to his students' plans for a snack tour throughout the carnival - though not without the reminder that training resumed at dawn tomorrow, eliciting a weary groan from Sora.

Writers of Destiny[]

During the process of rebuilding the SA Mark 01, Sora reflected on her own Elemental Powers and considered the possibility that they could belong to Riyu instead. However, Lloyd offered reassurance by confirming that Sora indeed possessed her powers. Amid their conversation, Arin chimed in and mentioned how he was somewhat of a fan of the ninja. Sora interrupted by sharing an anecdote about Arin's obsession with the ninja, recounting the time he attempted to emulate Zane's hairstyle. Sora's attention was abruptly drawn to the Cloud Kingdom as it appeared to be drifting towards them. As they drew nearer, Sora witnessed a MergeQuake taking place, resulting in the fusion of the Cloud Kingdom with a realm teeming with tentacled beasts. Amidst the ensuing pandemonium, two Ice Dragons arrived and launched a fierce assault on the monstrous beings. Sora was surprised at the sight of dragons within the kingdom, prompting Euphrasia to enlighten her about the Ice Dragons seeking refuge in the Cloud Kingdom for their safety. Under Lloyd's instructions, Sora took control of the Sa Mark 01 to ensure the safety of the people. She activated the mech and prepared to face the monster without relying on any Elemental Powers. As she blocked a tentacle with her mech, she realized that the creature was even slimier than it appeared, making it feel like she was fighting a tongue. However, her attempt to defend herself was futile as the monster threw her away, leaving her vulnerable. Sora noticed a group of monks writing but they didn't respond to her urging to get to safety. Before she could continue, the monster grabbed her again. Nya and Kai come to the rescue, using their Spinjitzu to push the monster back to the MergeQuake. Despite Arin's hopes that their plan would work, Sora said that they wouldn't be able to witness the outcome if it failed. The monster was sent back to its realm, but it dropped Sora and Arin, leaving them powerless and vulnerable. As they plummet toward the ground, they suddenly start hovering, lifted by the Elemental Power of Wind.

As the ninja approached the Cloud Monks, they were met by Suetonius, the Master Writer of the Cloud Kingdom. Suetonius anticipated gratitude from the ninja for their heroic act of saving them. However, Sora informed him that it was the ninja who had rescued them while the Cloud Monks were preoccupied with their writing duties. Suetonius, in defense of the Cloud Monks, explained that they were the Writers of Destiny, and whatever they inscribed in their scrolls had the power to manifest in reality. Sora found that assertion difficult to accept and questioned the authenticity of such a claim. Lloyd interjected and pointed out that the writers only took responsibility for shaping fate when it suited their interests. To find a solution to their current predicament, Nya proposed consulting the Cloud Kingdom archives to seek information on how to stop MergeQuakes. Suetonius encouraged the ninja to explore the vast collection of writings held by the Cloud Monks and assigned Marcus to guide them to the library. Lloyd decided to conduct further investigations with Kai and Nya and requested Sora and Arin to remain behind and monitor the situation.

Sora was frustrated as she surveyed the wreckage of her mech. She felt overwhelmed and incapable of fixing it. Reflecting on the situation, she couldn't help but think that Arin should have been allowed to accompany the others on their mission. Arin asked her what she meant. Sora explained that he had taught himself Spinjitzu, and in her eyes, he had the potential to become a ninja more than anyone else, including herself. She acknowledged that she possessed an Elemental Power, but she often struggled to control it. Sora admitted that she believed it would be better for everyone if she refrained from using her powers altogether, as she couldn't seem to control them effectively. Arin extended a heartwarming gesture towards Sora by offering her a hug, which she gratefully accepted as a sign of his support and friendship. However, their conversation was abruptly halted when Arin's attention was drawn to Euphrasia, the girl who had previously saved them by using Wind.

Upon Euphrasia's exit from a building, Sora and Arin immediately confront her. Sora took the initiative to expose Euphrasia as a Wind Elemental by commenting on the powerful winds. However, Euphrasia denied the claim, stating that she could not possess such powers as she hailed from the Cloud Kingdom. Sora, persisting in her belief, informed Euphrasia that she was indeed an Elemental Master. Euphrasia countered by explaining that the Cloud Monks, like herself, solely utilize words to shape destiny, while Elemental Masters rely on actions to impact the world. Sora empathized with her concerns, acknowledging the intimidating nature of her newfound abilities, and suggestsedthat the ninja can provide her with the necessary training. Euphrasia, however, revealed her apprehension about possessing powers, as it would result in her exile from the Cloud Kingdom, therefore losing the entire way of life she held dear. Later on, the ground trembled once again, signaling the reappearance of the monster from the MergeQuake. Sora recalled Lloyd's earlier advice, emphasizing the need to contact him in case anything goes wrong. Arin, attempting to reach out to the ninja, found that they were unresponsive. So he decided to search for them himself, leaving Sora behind.

Sora tried to repair her mech but failed. Despite Riyu's suggestion, she worried that her actions would only make things worse. Suddenly, the monster attacked the Bounty, but Euphrasia stepped in to push it away with her powers. Euphrasia used her powers to push the monster back. However, a second monster emerged from the MergeQuake and grabbed Euphrasia with a tentacle. Sora received some of Riyu's dragon energy, which allowed her to reconfigure her mech and save Euphrasia from the monster's grasp. With the two of them working together, Sora and Euphrasia attack the monsters and vow to shut down the MergeQuake before more creatures can come through. Just as they were fighting, Arin and the ninja arrived. Arin landed on Sora's mech and asked if she received the dragon energy from Riyu. Sora confirmed that she did, and Arin explained that he had been busy freeing the ninja from the Scroll-Worms. Sora was skeptical of his explanation, but they all continued to fight the monsters together. The ninja worked together to push the monsters back into the MergeQuake and close it, averting the fusion of the Cloud Kingdom with the monsters' realm. Arin questioned Sora about the idea of refraining from using her powers for the betterment of the world. Sora, on the other hand, was still uncertain about her role as a ninja but mentioned being motivated by Euphrasia's example to actively assist others. Nya remained in the Cloud Kingdom while Sora and the other ninja traveled back to Ninjago using the Destiny's Bounty.

Return to Imperium[]

Confronting the Claws of Imperium in the Crossroads junkyard, Sora discovered her old invention, the Photac, had resurfaced. After returning to the monastery, Sora plans to return and disable it, only for Arin to insist on joining her, followed by Lloyd. After their hectic entrance through the sewage system, Sora led Lloyd and Arin to the Security Station, where she hacked the computer to put up fake IDs, Sora going with her name, as well as "Surname," and change into some spare clothes and added markings to their faces. However, their cover was instantly blown when a guard spotted they weren't wearing shoulder pads and were sent to the Public Shaming Platform, where Sora was embarrassed as her parents will know she's back. Before they could be screened, Riyu creates a diversion for Sora to sneak into Dr. LaRow's lab and destroy the Photac, only to be confronted by Dr. LaRow herself, who addresses her by her real name.

Mindless Beasts[]

When confronted by Dr. LaRow, Sora was reminded of her return to Imperium due to the reappearance of the Photac. However, she firmly insisted on being addressed by her new name, highlighting the uncanny resemblance between herself and the dragon. Sora was informed that Beatrix's desire to utilize the Photac had grown stronger after the Merge, as it would aid Imperium in hunting down more dragons. Sora expressed surprise that Dr. LaRow had figured it out, considering her lack of expertise in exotic photovoltaic positrons. Dr. LaRow teased Sora about her spirited attitude, prompting her to explain how she had to grow up on the Crossroads after being betrayed by her former idol. Sora questioned why Dr. LaRow hadn't come for her, to which she responded that she knew Sora would return on her own and that Imperium needed her assistance in initializing the copied Photacs. She requested Sora to activate them, but Sora adamantly refused to let her invention be used to harm dragons. Expecting such a response, Dr. LaRow revealed dragon Sora and Dorama, who had reactivated the original Photac. Dorama threatened to harm the dragon if Sora didn't comply. Dr. LaRow questioned Sora's loyalty, to which she reluctantly agreed to help. Sora activated a Photac, causing a bright light that allowed the two Soras to escape.

In their pursuit of an escape route, the two Soras continued their search. However, Sora, who had been absent from Imperium for a long time, struggled to recall the layout of the building. Determined to find the cafeteria, they unintentionally stumbled upon the storage facility, where they encountered Rapton and four Claws Hunters. Asserting her authority, Sora declared herself as the Elemental Master of Technology and threatened to unleash her powers upon them. Rapton, however, challenged her to prove her abilities by using her powers against a Weapon Drone, promising her and dragon Sora's freedom in return. Sora, lacking the assistance of Riyu's dragon power, attempted to tap into her element. Recalling Lloyd's advice about her True Potential, she managed to briefly harness her powers, but struggled to maintain full control. With no other choice, Sora fought the Claws, but was defeated. Upon Sora's return to the Advanced Systems Lab, Ras blackmailed her, claiming that dragon Sora's energy would be depleted unless she activated the copied Photacs. While working on a Photac, Dr. LaRow taunted Sora, pleased that she was helping Imperium. Dr. LaRow believed that Sora now understood that she couldn't stop them and that Imperium would soon be able to capture all the dragons, including the Source Dragons. Sora was confused by the mention of Source Dragons, but she completed the Photacs. Overwhelmed with regret, Sora questioned what she had done.

I Will Be the Danger[]

After the Photacs were activated, the Claws of Imperium used them to halt the dragon breakout, which greatly frustrated Sora. Taking advantage of Dr. LaRow's distraction, Sora swiftly incapacitated LaRow with a Photac before facing Dorama. Sora managed to manipulate Dorama into striking the control panel, resulting in his own electrocution. Consequently, dragon Sora was liberated from her confinement. With a heartfelt embrace, the two Soras successfully escaped from the Advanced Systems Lab and rejoined Lloyd and Arin. However, Arin felt ashamed of Sora's involvement in activating the replicated Photacs. Sora proceeded to explain her actions and reassured them that she can permanently deactivate the Photacs using Technology. Furthermore, Sora disclosed her need for dragon power from Riyu, only to be surprised by Arin's revelation that Riyu was nowhere to be found. Determined, Sora and Arin left to find Riyu.

The Calm Inside[]

Sora and Arin went back to the Security Station in order to find where Riyu is. Lloyd informed Sora that if they can't find Riyu, she would have to shut down the Photacs without his dragon energy. Sora mention that she never been able to use Technology on her own. Lloyd reminded Sora of her True Potential, but Sora questioned if it's as true as Lloyd thought. Suddenly, Sora later found out that Riyu was taken to the Imperium Palace and they started heading towards there. While heading towards the palace, Sora and Arin discussed their plan to sneak past the Imperium Guards. Sora was confident that their ninja stealth training would help them get past the guards easily, as most of them were busy dealing with the dragon breakout. However, Arin reminded Sora that they haven't received any formal stealth training from Lloyd. Sora argued that their previous experience sneaking out to the Crossroads Carnival counted as informal training. As they attempt to sneak past the guards, Arin tripped and get caught by the guards, prompting Sora to comment that he clearly didn't learn anything from their previous training. They were able to trick some guards by lying that Arin tripped because he was uspet by people who appalled the Imperium Guards' shoulder pads. As it worked, Sora, reflecting on the peculiar nature of her homeworld, asked about the cause of Arin's stumble. However, Arin dismissed the topic, not wanting to delve into his own clumsiness. They were able to get into the palace but couldn't reach Riyu due to the Empress and Ras being in the palace. Giving the information to their master Lloyd, Lloyd stated that he'll create a diversion to cause the Empress and Ras to leave the palace. The Monastery of Imperium shakes the city as Sora and Arin attempted to use the shaking as a distraction to reach Riyu. Only for Jordana, Dr. LaRow assistant, to catch them and pushed them away. Jordana expressed her relief as the long-awaited day had finally arrived, bringing with it the opportunity to put an end to her and Sora's rivalry. However, Sora was perplexed by Jordana's mention of a rivalry, questioning if they had met before. Jordana later took both their communicators and destroyed them and made them unable to contact Lloyd.

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

While restrained, Sora reluctantly confessed to Arin that her birth name was Ana, which surprised and embarrassed Arin. Dr. LaRow then revealed her knowledge of Sora using Riyu's dragon power, implying that Sora was taking advantage of Riyu. Sora vehemently denied these accusations, stating that Riyu willingly shared his power with her. Dr. LaRow tried to manipulate Sora further by drawing parallels between their uses of dragon power, suggesting that Sora had absorbed more knowledge than she realized. Dr. LaRow activated her communicator, showing the intense battle between dragons and Photacs outside, leaving Sora and Arin in shock. Just as Dr. LaRow threatened to make them witness the fate of their friends, a sudden disturbance interrupted the scene, causing confusion and speculation among the group. Sora believed it was a MergeQuake, and as the ground shook, the emergence of the Monastery of Imperium brought a glimmer of hope, symbolized by the determination of the ninja. Sensing a shift in the situation, Sora embraced this newfound hope, inspiring Arin to take action against the scientists.

Jordana issued a warning to Sora, claiming that she would never be able to escape from her. Sora questioned Jordana's identity. This led Jordana to reveal that she used to be one of Sora's classmates, driven to surpass Sora's accomplishments since the science fair. Struggling to recall Jordana's identity, Sora questioned if her hair had been a different color back then, only to be met with a firm denial from Jordana. Sora and Arin sought refuge from Jordana's attacks. Arin's mind drifted back to the training sessions with Lloyd, where they honed their skills in the obstacle course, while Sora remembered the long hours spent practicing tirelessly. As they dodged Jordana's strikes, they managed to use her own weapon to break free from their handcuffs. Despite Jordana's menacing threats, Sora couldn't help but wonder about her identity. Just when they were on the brink of reaching Riyu, Ras suddenly appeared, presenting them with a choice to surrender or face certain doom. Defiantly, they chose to fight back, with Sora hurling the Imperium Sword at Ras, initiating a fierce battle. Arin managed to trip Ras from behind while Sora distracted him. Ras retaliated by knocking Arin unconscious with his hammer and delivering a powerful punch to Sora. However, this action inadvertently led to Riyu breaking free from his cage, unleashing his dragon power and activating Sora's Elemental Powers. Seizing the opportunity, Sora used her powers to trap Ras inside Riyu's cage, rendering him immobile. The Shape of Motion[]

As Sora and Arin waited for the ninja to arrive, they observed the Craglings presenting the Matriarch of the Mountain Dragons with their sacred mud, hoping it would aid in her healing. Despite their efforts, Sora believed the mud alone would not suffice. When the other ninja finally joined them, Sora relayed the troubling news, and the Matriarch warned Lloyd of an imminent danger, entrusting him with a map. The Matriarch succumbed to her injuries, leaving the ninja in grief. In the midst of their sorrow, Sora grappled with the brutality of the attack, questioning how anyone could commit such an atrocity. Lloyd shared the Matriarch's warning about the potential peril facing the Source Dragons. At that moment, Sora heard a faint whimper and discovered the Matriarch's infant daughter concealed within one of her nursing pouches. Concerned for the child's safety, Sora feared that the same assailant might target the baby next. Lloyd proposed that they should return the child to her herd, but Sora reminded him that the Mountain Dragons had fled the Land of the Three Mountains following the assault by the Claws of Imperium. Riyu then drew arrow, which initially confused the ninja until they recalled Riyu's unique ability to track the Mountain Dragons like the Matriarch had previously mentioned. Sora rode alongside Lloyd and Arin on Zanth, closely trailing Riyu during their expedition. Soon, they were ambushed by Rapton and the Claw Hunters, catching Sora off guard as Rapton announced his return. He explained that after the Liberation of Imperium, he and the Claw Hunters had rebranded as the Freebooters, struggling to fit into their new roles within the Kingdom of Imperium and turning to mercenary work. With the assistance of the Mountain Dragons, the ninja defeated the Freebooters, with all the Claw Hunters collapsing on Rapton. Using Technology, Sora made Rapton's hover chariot speed away, taking him and the Claw Hunters off course. The ninja were then guided to a World Tree in the World Forest, where they successfully reunited the Matriarch's daughter with her tribe.

Upon the ninja's return to the monastery, they were confronted by a dragon. Sora inquired about the dragon's identity, and the dragon introduced herself as the Source Dragon of Motion, seeking the ninja's assistance. She warned them that the same people who killed the Matriarch demise were also responsible for the deaths of two other Arc Dragons, and that her kind might be next in line. The Source Dragon explained the threats facing her species, particularly since they were already vulnerable before the Merge, citing the capture of the Source Dragon of Energy and the death of another Source Dragon. She indicated that if all Source Dragons were to perish, the Merged Realms will be destroyed, as they're essential to the very fabric of reality. The Source Dragon of Motion emphasized that it was the ninja's responsibility to bring the killers to justice before they could target the remaining Source Dragons, as she and her kin were unable to identify the culprits. Upon learning about the map given to Lloyd by the Matriarch, the Source Dragon of Motion realized it led to the City of Temples, suspecting that the killers would be participating in the Tournament of the Sources. Consequently, she tasked Sora and the other ninja with attending the Tournament to track down the threat.

Enter the City of Temples[]

Arin showed off his new pie, Merlopian Berry Creme, to Sora and Riyu. Arin proudly announced it as a creation from his best friend Frak. Sora's jealousy flared up at the mention of Frak. Arin clarified that Frak was his bestie besides Sora and Riyu and that they were friends before the Merge, sharing a mutual love for the ninja. Sora was impressed, and Arin reminisced about how excited Frak would be for Arin now, even though they've lost touch since he moved away. Arin decided to whip up more pies for the rest of the ninja before they head off to the Tournament of the Sources. Sora insisted he should come along, but Arin felt out of place since the tournament is meant for Elemental Masters. Sora playfully suggested that Arin was the Elemental Master of Pie, emphasizing the importance of his presence as part of the team. However, Arin lamented that, despite the excitement, he felt he does not have a place in the event.

When Lloyd returned from the training plateau of the Dragon Masters, he had Sora and the rest of the ninja sport bowls of on their heads as training. Sora questions the necessity of this arrangement. Lloyd jokes mentions the advantages of stew-jitzu, prompting a groan from Nya, while Sora and Arin share a quiet laugh. Lloyd, eager to proceed, tosses bowls towards the ninja, who catch them with ease.

Sora and Arin made their way to the Dragon Stone Shrine using the Explorer, accompained with the other ninja. As they arrived, the ninja spotted Euphrasia stepping into an elevator, prompting Arin to excitedly shake Sora and express his delight about the unfolding events. Sora, however, reminded him that he had almost decided not to come at all. As they approached the entrance, Sora was eager to move forward, but was uncertain about how to proceed. Zane then inspected the closed slot Euphrasia used. Sora noticed a speaking tube and called out, asking if anyone could hear them as she stated their intention to attend the Tournament. Suddenly, another door opened, revealing Bleckt, who introduced himself as the Minister of Ledgering for the Tournament of the Sources. Bleckt explained that he oversaw all lists and records for the event, highlighting his family's long history of organizing the tournament. However, Bleckt suggested the ninja should leave if they did not have an invitation from the Spirit Dragons. Wyldfyre insisted that they were esteemed Elemental Masters deserving of an invitation, but Bleckt dismissed her, saying they would not be able to compete. Offended, Sora challenged Bleckt's authority, questioning his identity as the Elemental Master of Shade. Bleckt, however, simply bypassed the ninja and allowed Obscuria, the Elemental Master of Shade, to enter the City of Temples.

As Kizzy took the elevator into the city, Sora and Arin opted for the Explorer, which fired a grappling hook that secured itself to the rising elevator. This allowed the elevator to hoist the Explorer, carrying Sora and Arin, up the cliff while the other ninja soared alongside them on their dragons. Suddenly, Security Bots arrived, attacking the ninja for their intrusion. Two of the bots focused on Sora and Arin in the Explorer, forcing them to jump out. Sora landed on one of the Security Bots. While hanging on, Sora attempted to use her Elemental Powers against the bot, but to her dismay, nothing happened. The bot made a sudden turn, causing Sora to lose her grip and tumble onto Zanth. Realizing that the Security Bots were constructed from outdated technology, which rendered her powers ineffective, Sora felt frustrated. As more Security Bots approached, the dragons urged the ninja to secured themselves to the cliff while they engage in battle. Sora and the ninja used their Dragon Hooks to attach to the cliff face and began their ascent, but Sora looked down, calling out for Arin and feeling anxious about his absence. Cole mentioned spotting Arin riding a flying bot, with Riyu in pursuit. Concerned for her friend's safety, Sora pressed on. Upon reaching the top, the ninja were met with onlookers who were watching a recording of their skirmish with the Security Bots. As they took in the scene, the entrance swung open to reveal Roby and Bleckt. Bleckt commanded the Security Bots to detain the ninja, but Roby intervened, revealing his authority as the Games Master of the tournament. He enthusiastically invited Sora and the others to participate in his grand vision for a multimedia event, promising even more surprises as the gates opened to showcase the City of Temples. However, Sora interrupted, voicing her worries about where Arin and Riyu had gone.

Sora and the ninja crew were offered a tour by Roby and Bleckt, but Bleck was interrupted by Roby calling him boring. As the tour continued, Roby claimed that old tradition was going to be replaced by 'the new era'. They then showed the ninja a seven-headed dragon painting on a building. Zane informed them that he had no data about a seven-headed dragon, only for Roby to quickly say that it wasn't a real dragon and that it was interpretive art representing the Seven Source Dragons of myth.

Roby then explained that how the fairy tale went, all Elemental Powers came from the Source Dragons. Bleck quickly interrupted, claiming it was not a fairy tale. Roby ignored him and continued, saying the games were created forever ago to celebrate the gifts of the Source Dragons so Elemental Masters were called to compete. Roby then finished by telling them that it was pretty much a celebration for their awesomeness.

When Cole asked what the winner got, Roby quickly replied that rules had to be discussed first. He then explained that when someone lost a match, they give their power to the Elemental Cup. With that cup, Roby would be able to transfer the loser's power to the winner, who would then have both powers. This would go on in the game as each competitor that won would gain more and more powers to compete with and that the winner would end up with everyone's Elemental Powers.

Bleckt then added that the champion would return the power to their rightful masters in a victory ceremony that commemorated the Source Dragons allowing mortals to wield Elemental Powers. Roby the declared that victory ceremonies were boring and that theirs would be an extreme victory extravaganza.

Nya then mentioned how that seemed super risky, considering the winner could be evil. Sora then said that an evil winner could go rogue and take over the world, or something similar to that. Roby brushed off the obvious stress by explaining that nothing had ever happened like that and that it couldn't. The Elemental Cup was like a safety switch. If anyone tried something stupid, Roby would be able to pull the powers back into the cup.

Soon, the ninja were greeted by Zant-Tanz, discovering that he had the elemental power of Plants. After Zant-Tanz demonstrated his ability, he told them that he would bring his sweet Mucoid Berries to the banquet. When Sora questioned about it, Roby informed them that they were invited.

The ninja later entered the banquet hall and were greeted by Roby. Soon after Roby gave the ninja a few menus, Sora was startled by Jordana begging for help. But the second it happened, it all passed in a flash and Jordana froze and acted as if nothing happened, confusing Sora.

Then, Nokt and Cinder appear at Jordana's side, Nokt openly wondering where she had ran off to. As Cinder advanced toward Sora, saying, "Look who we have here," Wyldfyre jumped beside Sora growling. When Cinder mocked Wyldfyre and asked about the other red ninja, Wyldfyre summoned her Heat power, ready to blast Cinder with it.

Roby quickly intervened, announcing that fighting was prohibited and that anyone would be eliminated from the games for starting one.

When Arin asked Sora what was wrong, she replied saying she wasn't sure and that something strange had just happened.

Inside the Maze[]

Sora was walking back to the ninja quarters when Nya ran to Lloyd about seeing Jay at the Tournament.

The following day, Sora and Arin engaged in a conversation regarding Zant-Tanz and the attacker bots. Arin proposed that Ras might be the mastermind behind the attack. However, Sora pointed out that Ras was also ambushed. Sora encouraged Arin to reach out to the other ninja for assistance, but Arin wanted to handle the situation independently, feeling the need to redeem himself since he was unable to participate in the tournament. As Arin left, Sora reflected to herself that Arin wasn't the only one grappling with a mystery, especially considering Jordana's unusual behavior.

Sora made her way to Foundation Street, keeping a close watch on Jordana. When she finally approached her, Sora demanded to know why Jordana had sought her assistance at the banquet. Jordana denied ever making such a request, but Sora was adamant that she had. In response, Jordana accused Sora of manipulating her thoughts and issued a warning before walking away. As Sora observed, she noticed Jordana engaging in conversation with Nokt, and soon after, the two of them joined Frak. This revelation shocked Sora, as she discovered that Frak was affiliated with the Wolf Clan. Upon returning to their quarters, Sora cautioned Arin about Frak's alliance with Ras. Arin brushed off her concerns, convinced that Sora was merely misinterpreting what she had witnessed. Sora firmly maintained her stance, asserting that she would never lie to Arin. Just then, Lloyd entered the room, suggesting that they all head to the Games Plaza, as the first game of the tournament was about to begin.

At the Games Plaza, Sora joined the other ninja as Lloyd reminded everyone about the villain orchestrating a plan to seize the Source Dragons and the Elemental Powers. When Roby introduced Zane and Zur as the first two Elemental Masters set to compete, Sora cheered for Zane, encouraging him to give it his all. Unfortunately, Zane was defeated by Zur, leading to the transfer of his powers to his opponent. Sora observed with concern as Zane's Ice abilities dissipated more quickly than anticipated during the match. Moments later, Sora and the other ninja were left in disbelief upon seeing Jay with the Wolf Clan. After Nya triumphed in her match, Sora celebrated the victory with Arin and Wyldfyre. However, as Jay faced rejection from Ras, Sora began to question the somber mood among the ninja, despite the importance of winning the tournament, prompting Lloyd to reveal that they had lost Jay to the side of evil.

However, Arin recalled how Lloyd promise to find his parents before is similar to how Lloyd promising Nya they will find Jay but obstacles always seemed to arise whenever.

Truth and Lies[]

In an effort to evade the toxic goo, Sora separated the Ninja Team Combo Vehicle into four units, assuming control of her glider. However, the ninja were unable to track the Pace Dragon. Sora encouraged Cole to maintain his optimism. Gaining a more advantageous perspective, Sora successfully located the Pace Dragon and urged the ninja to increase their speed. However, their progress was abruptly halted by a cave-in within the tunnel. Inspired by Nya's sarcastic suggestion to charge directly into the obstruction, Sora merged the four vehicles. The Ninja Team Combo Vehicle forcefully breached the wall, allowing Sora and the other ninja to surpass the Pace Dragon, securing victory in the race and thereby earning immunity to remain in the City of Temples.

At the Games Plaza, Sora saw the competition between Euphrasia and Zeatrix, with Zeatrix emerging victorious in the match. In the subsequent round, Tox faced off against Nokt, who launched a vicious assault on Tox using Shatterspin. As Tox was being carried away on a stretcher, Sora and the other ninja rushed to provide assistance. Amidst the chaos, Sora inadvertently caught wind of Nokt referring to Jordana as "sister," a revelation that piqued her curiosity.

As the contest between Cole and Frak was on the verge of commencing, Sora noticed Arin's arrival. She attempted to greet him, but he dismissively ignored her, leaving her perplexed. While the match between Frak and Cole unfolded, Sora reminded Arin of her previous assertions regarding Frak's affiliation with the Wolf Clan, emphasizing her honesty towards him. Arin challenged her claim, questioning her certainty about the truth of her statements. This left Sora bewildered until Arin disclosed that he had been informed by someone about how Sora had deceived him into believing he had successfully performed Object Spinjitzu during the Ritual of the Blood Moon. Sora then admitted her wrongdoing and expressed her regret. She insisted that Arin was the only hope for the ninja during the ritual and that she genuinely wanted him to succeed, particularly after his demotion by Egalt during training. Sensing Sora's potential doubts regarding his capabilities, Arin left in frustration, despite her attempts to provide clarification.

After some time, Sora engaged in training with Lloyd and Nya in preparation for the upcoming game. Preoccupied by the conflict with Arin, Sora inadvertently used Balance. Lloyd advised Sora that her distractions would hinder her ability to master Spinjitzu.

Sora watched the match between Nya and Nokt, where the objective was to collect the majority of three gems. Nokt emerged victorious, leaving Sora and Lloyd as the remaining competitors for the ninja. Lloyd attempted to motivate them, but Sora is disheartened to see Arin leave in frustration. Just then, Roby announced that Sora and Cinder would compete next, explaining that their challenge is a puzzle game. As the game commenced, Sora used Technology to create a weapon, which she used to electrocute Cinder, incapacitating him. She then pulled levers to lower a metal pillar, forming a bridge. When Cinder recovered, Sora transformed her weapon into a cage, recalling Cinder's history of being trapped in technological cages. Sora tackled the second puzzle, revealing a numerical code, and successfully inputed 11111 into a lock, which lowered a rope for her to swing to the final platform. After placing the metal orb in the center, Roby declared her the winner. He then transferred Cinder's Smoke and Heat powers to Sora, who rejoined the other ninja.

Arin then revealed a symbol he had discovered the day before, prompting Cole to recommend that he visit the Temple City Museum, which Arin decided to do right away. Although Sora offered to assist him, Arin rejected the offer. Sora then turned to Lloyd to discuss Arin, but Lloyd suddenly experienced another vision, leaving Sora surprised, especially since Rontu had taught Lloyd how to control such occurrences.

The Final Game[]

While training with Lloyd and Zane in the Games Plaza, Sora tried to use Cinder's Smoke but failed to go twice as far, leading her to wonder how Cinder managed to harness it so easily. She felt frustrated about her inability to wield the other Elemental Powers like her competitors. Lloyd pointed out that Sora's main issue was her attempt to emulate the other Elemental Masters instead of embracing her unique abilities. Sora acknowledged this but was eager to know what she could do differently. Lloyd encouraged her to be true to herself, but Sora felt that her current self was not good enough, especially since Arin disliked her, her Spinjitzu skills were inconsistent, and she was facing the prospect of losing to Nokt in the upcoming final match. Lloyd reassured her, suggesting that she should stop trying to replicate how others used their powers and instead focus on how only she could use them. Sora, feeling uncertain, asked for guidance on how to achieve that. Spotting the Wolf Clan nearby, she excused herself to talk to Jordana. When Sora mentioned the note, Jordana claimed not to know what she was talking about. Sora felt a pang of regret for not remembering Jordana better and suggested that they might have been friends during their challenging school days. However, Jordana dismissed her with a harsh remark, calling her pathetic before walking away, leaving Sora feeling disheartened.

Later at night, the final battle between Sora and Nokt commenced. The battle kicked off with Sora making the first move, attempting to perform Spinjitzu for the first time. She managed the rudimentary version of the technique, much like Arin had before her, but Nokt quickly knocked her to the ground. Nokt boasted that Sora had never encountered an opponent like him before, to which Sora retorted by drawing comparisons to her parents and other oppressive Imperians from the City of Imperium, asserting that she had faced worse. Nokt then performed Shatterspin, but Sora dodged the attack and quickly created an Ice wall for defense. She then used Frak's Quake power on ice spikes she raised from the ground to the air, followed by applying Zur's Reflex power into punching them in a rapid motion. When Nokt uses Shatterspin to attack, the barrier collapse onto him and sends him flying into a screen. The two combatants exchanged fierce blows using their Elemental Powers, with Sora using Smoke, Heat, and Reflex to counteract Nokt's attacks. As the battle went on, Nokt used Shockwave to make Sora go into smoke form and managed to knock her down with Shatterspin. Lloyd, watching from the sidelines, urged Sora to outsmart Nokt if she couldn't overpower him. In a moment of insight, Sora spotted a device on the back of Nokt's head, though she was unsure of its function. Utilizing Technology, she transformed the device into a techno-bee, which stung Nokt and delivered an electric shock. Just as Sora prepared to finish Nokt with her Heat attack, the arena unexpectedly shifted, leaving her disoriented. Sora hopped onto a hover pad, and she attempted another Heat attack, but Nokt countered with Shatterspin, knocking her off her pad and securing his victory in the final match.

Sora and Lloyd proceeded to the victory ceremony, where Sora was to relinquish her powers. Lloyd commended Sora, asserting that she surpassed all other Elemental Masters. Nevertheless, Sora felt troubled by the fact that Ras would acquire all the Elemental Powers for his scheme. Roby absorbed Sora's powers with the intention of transferring them to Nokt, thereby claiming the title of Champion of the Sources. At that moment, Wyldfyre and Zur arrived, revealing Bleckt's collusion with Ras. Wyldfyre detailed Ras' entire plot, including the involvement of Bleckt in orchestrating the attacker bots and rigging the matches, including the one between Sora and Nokt. Disgusted by Bleckt's betrayal, Roby announced the cancellation of the victory celebration. However, Nokt attacked Roby with Shatterspin, claiming all the powers from the Elemental Cup and betrayed Ras. When Jordana arrived, Sora learned that Rox, another member of the Forbidden Five, had possessed Jordana since the Blood Moon Ritual, explaining her odd behavior. Both Rox and Nokt admitted to being responsible for the deaths of the three Arc Dragons. When Sora questioned about their motives for killing innocent dragons, Nokt revealed their intent to steal the Crowns of Horns to aid in freeing the rest of the Forbidden Five. Enraged, Ras summoned Cinder and the wolf warriors to confront Nokt and Cinder, but Rox corrupted the Wolf Clan, unleashing their true power. Sora and Lloyd prepared for battle as the Wolf Clan launched their attack.

Elements of Betrayal[]

Sora fought the Wolf Mask Warriors, using a fragment of a railing as an improvised weapon during the skirmish. Sora expressed to Lloyd her dissatisfaction with the limitations imposed by her partial acquisition of the Elemental Powers from the tournament. Lloyd, in turn, relayed information he had received from Arin regarding the location of the Crowns of Horns, which were said to be situated at the Monastery of Gates. He also conveyed his astonishment at Ras' innocence concerning the assassinations of the Arc Dragons. Sora advised Lloyd not to be overly critical of himself, as it was unlikely anyone would suspect Jordana of being possessed by a member of the Forbidden Five. Despite Lloyd's urgency to confront Nokt at the Monastery, Sora reminded him that both Nokt and Rox had already made their way there. As Ras and Roby set off towards the Monastery with the intention of halting Nokt, Lloyd reiterated the necessity of their own journey to the Monastery; however, Sora insisted that their presence was required at the Games Plaza. Frak encouraged Sora and the other ninja to proceed ahead while the Elemental Masters fight the Wolf Clan. Consequently, Sora, Lloyd, and Wyldfyre used the Ninja Team Combo Vehicle to meet up with the remaining ninja at the rock bridge.

Sora and the other ninja reached the Monastery of Gates, where the remaining members of the Forbidden Five had successfully escaped from the Nether-Space. Engaging in battle alongside the other ninja and Ras, Sora used her glider to confront Nokt. During the skirmish, Nokt launched a fierce attack on the ninja, resulting in Sora being dislodged from her glider. Amidst the chaos, both Sora and Lloyd witnessed Arin valiantly defending Ras against Nokt, a sight that left them both bewildered. Meanwhile, Nya tapped into Riyu's dragon power and used them on the Crowns of Horns, which enabled her to liberate Kai and Bonzle from the Nether-Space. A battle erupted between Kai and Nokt, with the latter initially gaining the upper hand and preparing to eliminate Kai. However, Roby intervened by used the Elemental Cup to reclaim the powers that Nokt had usurped, thereby restoring them to their rightful bearers, including Sora. The ninja managed to corner Nokt, but he ultimately escaped through a portal gate along with the remaining members of the Forbidden Five, taking Ras with him. Despite Sora and Lloyd's objections, Arin chose to pursue the Forbidden Five in an attempt to rescue Ras. As Sora and Lloyd approached the portal gate, the Forbidden Five destroyed it, leaving Sora devastated as she called out for Arin, coming to terms with the fact that Arin had opted to abandon the ninja.

In the aftermath, Sora has been designated as the Champion of the Sources by default, earning recognition as the greatest Elemental Master within the Merged Realms. Subsequently, at Foundation Street, Sora thanked Euphrasia and Tox for their invaluable assistance. Shortly thereafter, she sought out Lloyd to asked about the whereabouts of Jordana, to which Lloyd replied that Jordana had disappeared following her aid to the ninja. Sora emphasized that their success would not have been possible without Jordana's contributions. Lloyd reminisced about a time when Sora had claimed she possessed no Elemental Powers, to which Sora humorously remarked that it felt like an eternity ago. She conveyed her appreciation to Lloyd, acknowledging that her triumph was largely due to his encouragement, alongside the inspiration she drew from both him and Arin. Sora then shared her concerns regarding the rift that had developed between her and Arin, prompting Lloyd to reassure her that they would find Arin. However, Sora expressed her belief that Arin might not wish to be found. As the ninja prepared to return to Ninjago, Sora and Lloyd stumbled upon Wyldfyre and Roby sharing a tender moment, leading Sora to pull Lloyd away when he interrupted. Upon their return to the Monastery of Spinjitzu via a portal gate, Sora and Wyldfyre met up with Riyu, and the trio left together.
