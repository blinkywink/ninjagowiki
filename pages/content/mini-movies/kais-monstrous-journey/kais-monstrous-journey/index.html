Kai's Monstrous Journey is a canon[1] mini-series of Ninjago: Legends shorts.[2]

Official description

Official description[]

An exclusive NINJAGO® Legends story is coming soon! When Wyldfyre discovers Kai’s journals, she uncovers the shocking truth about his past. Follow their journey only on kids.LEGO.com/NINJAGO and on the LEGO Play app.[3]
