Plot

Plot[]

Kai decides to record Wyldfyre's training at the Monastery with his phone. He guides her through the exercises, encouraging her to focus and find her center. After several attempts, Wyldfyre hits the target perfectly and feels proud of herself.

She compliments Kai, saying his posts inspired her to improve, and jokingly suggests they go on a big adventure together, like fighting a giant pig. Kai laughs and teases her, saying, "Sure, when pigs fly." Not letting the moment pass, Wyldfyre challenges him to a friendly target competition, which Kai eagerly accepts.
