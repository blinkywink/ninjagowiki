Early life

Early life[]

When she was a baby, Wyldfyre descended from the sky in a capsule, preserved in a Caregiver bot, until it crash landed in the Wyldness, where they meet Heatwave, who came to investigate the crash, a dragon whom Wyldfyre wasn't afraid of.

Throughout her childhood, despite many dragons in the Wyldness, Heatwave played a crucial role in nurturing Wyldfyre, while the Caregiver bot fulfilled its duty of ensuring her safety and serving as her educator, even teaching her the meaning of family, which Wyldfyre knew that was what Heatwave is to her. Growing up, the dragons told her stories about two dragon warriors, Egalt and Rontu, who traveled through the realms and protect anyone who cannot protect themselves. They said that Egalt was the bravest of all dragons, so they named the strongest and largest mountain, Mount Egalt, after him, but Wyldfyre just thought that they were just fairy tales.

One night while she and Heatwave were asleep, the Merge occurred, resulting in Wyldfyre acquiring the element of Heat. With the assistance of her Caregiver bot, she learned to harness and control her Elemental Powers. As the years went by, Wyldfyre started to play pranks on the Wyldness' inhabitants: the Lava-Tides, such as hiding rotten Wyldmelons around their village, crushing Lava-Tides with bricks, and defacing the statue of Lava-Todd the Wise. Throughout her childhood, she started to lose her dragon brethren to a disease called Wasting Sickness, which she begun to fear, believing Heatwave will get it. Then, one day, Heatwave was captured by the Claws of Imperium. In a desperate attempt to rescue him, Wyldfyre left the realm and traveled to Imperium, only to get captured some point after.

Dragons Rising, Season 1

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Return to Imperium[]

Wyldfyre was shooting Heat out of her cell until Lloyd, who mistook her for Kai, came over. As Lloyd opened the cell, Wyldfyre attacked him.

Mindless Beasts[]

Wyldfyre attacks Lloyd, believing he is an Imperian responsible for capturing Heatwave, until he managed to save her from getting struck by some Imperium Guards. They work together to free Heatwave, Lloyd not understanding that Heatwave was Wyldfyre's family until she told him upon freeing the latter. They soon decide to free the rest of the captured dragons.

I Will Be the Danger[]

Wyldfyre, Lloyd, and the dragons were confronted by the Imperium Guards and instructed to return to their cells. Despite Lloyd's suggestion of a diplomatic approach, Wyldfyre refused to comply and challenged the Imperium Guards, leading to another fight. In an effort to provide an escape route, Lloyd announced the opening of the hatch at the Imperium Prison. Wyldfyre took charge and commanded the dragons to ascend with Heatwave leading the way. During their escape, Wyldfyre questioned their plans to escape from Imperium. Lloyd explained that he had previously entered Imperium through the sewers, but the dragons were too large, necessitating their exit through the main gates.

Lloyd was concern regarding the damage caused by the dragons in the streets and proposed organizing them into a formation to prevent further harm. But Wyldfyre disagreed, asserting that dragons should be free and prioritize escape. Lloyd emphasized the danger to innocent Imperians and deemed the situation too perilous. In response, Wyldfyre demonstrated her dangerous nature by using Heat to neutralize the Weapon Drones. As the dragons approached the main gates, they were met by the Claws of Imperium and Photac Beasts. Rapton presented two options between surrendering or fighting. Wyldfyre agreed with Rapton's preference for fighting and signaled the dragons to charge forward and engage in combat.

The Calm Inside[]

Wyldfyre continued to battle the Photacs alongside Lloyd and the dragons, even getting knocked off at one point before swiftly returning to battle. Later, Lloyd tells Wyldfyre to follow him, which she thought was that he planned to retreat, until he told her that they're going to attack the palace, which she agreed, and they all head for the palace.

Later, a strange monastery soon rose up, confusing her, and the entrance opened up, revealing Kai, Zane, and Nya.

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

When the Monastery of Imperium appeared, and Kai, Zane, and Nya assist in the battle, Wyldfyre was surprised by Kai's Fire abilities. When Lloyd gave the order to retreat into the monastery, Wyldfyre objected before complying to give Arin and Sora time to free Riyu. She then watched as Lloyd, powered by the Source Dragon of Energy, activated a portal for the dragons to the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

Shortly thereafter, Wyldfyre and Heatwave made the decision to relocate to the monastery alongside the ninja, taking up residence within the dragon stables nestled within the Mountains of Impossible Heights, alongside Heatwave. However, within the initial days of her arrival, Wyldfyre burnt Wu's meditation carpet.

The Temple of the Dragon Cores[]

While saving some citizens during a MergeQuake, they mistook her for Kai. Later, in the dragon stables, Wyldfyre plays around with Heatwave while lighting the torches, even refusing Lloyd's offer to have a bedroom and a proper bathroom. When Kai offered to train her on Lloyd's insistence, she retaliated by setting him on fire, later confronting him about it in the recreation room and they start to attack each other before Lloyd, Nya, Arin, Sora, and Riyu try to stop them from setting fire to the room until Zane managed to do so before accompanying him to the hangar bay, where she sat on a boulder while the others discuss heading to the Temple of the Dragon Cores.

Coming across the entrance with three levers and Zane started to study it, Wyldfyre spent the time sleeping on Heatwave, until she, Arin, and Sora soon realize all three levers must be pulled simultaneously. Entering the temple, Kai and Wyldfyre used their powers to blast open the doors, which brought out two ghosts, who start to attack them with Wyldfyre, despite Zane's warning, tried to use her power on it before getting what he meant, until Lloyd rearranged the altar, and had Kai and Wyldfyre light the candles, Wyldfyre melting the candle she lit in half.

Gangs of the Sea[]

Wyldfyre joined the ninja in the hangar bay as they planned their mission to find the Dragon Cores. Lloyd suggested splitting into three teams. Nya chose to team up with Sora and Riyu while Lloyd and Arin formed the second team, leaving Kai paired with Wyldfyre, who warned him to stay out of trouble, to search for the purple Dragon Core in the Wyldness. Wyldfyre suggested riding on Heatwave to search for the core. However, Kai raised a concern about where he would sit. Wyldfyre suggested that Kai would sit behind her, believing it to be the obvious choice. Kai protested, claiming that he's older. Wyldfyre countered by saying that it was precisely why sitting behind her was suitable, calling him an old person.

Following their departure, Wyldfyre and Kai sought respite within the Greenbone Warriors' forest. However, Wyldfyre surreptitiously pilfered the gems that rightfully belonged to the warriors, planning to disperse them throughout the forest as a prank. The Greenbone Warriors apprehended Wyldfyre and Kai, and promptly sentenced them to the Walls of Punishment, where their lives would be forfeit. Both Kai and Wyldfyre resorted to shifting blame onto one another.

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

As the Walls of Punishment loomed over them, Wyldfyre disappointed in the wall's lack of excitement. The Bone King demanded the return of their sacred gems, offering them a chance to escape, but Kai denied having them. Wyldfyre added that the gems were unattractive, making it unlikely for them to have stolen them. Growing impatient, the Bone King threatened to let the walls crush them if they didn't comply. Unafraid, Wyldfyre taunted the Bone King with a skeleton joke. Fed up with their defiance, the Bone King reactivated the lever, causing the walls to close in on them. Kai noticed a pattern in the flying arrows and asked Wyldfyre if she had noticed it too. Confidently, she confirmed that she had spotted it long before him and used her skills to escape the pit. With Kai's assistance, they successfully escaped. As the Greenbone Warriors prepared to end them, Wyldfyre chose to cooperate and give up the gems, much to Kai and the Bone King's annoyance. The Bone King agreed to spare them on the condition that they never return. Wyldfyre pretended to agree and offered a handshake, but instead tricked the Bone King by burning his hand. They swiftly escaped on Heatwave, leaving the furious Greenbone Warriors behind. Wyldfyre couldn't contain her laughter after pulling the prank on the Bone King, while Kai advised her to take her Elemental Powers more seriously. In response, Wyldfyre mocked Kai for being boring, but Kai remained determined to retrieve the purple Dragon Core to save the world. Wyldfyre agreed that saving the world was of utmost importance, but she humorously pointed out that if the world was destroyed, there would be no one left for her to play pranks on. When Kai mentioned the Lava-Tides, Wyldfyre proudly declared that they were her friends and that they adored her.

Wyldfyre and Kai arrived at a village to retrieve the Dragon Core. However, the Lava-Tides resisted their demand because of Wyldfyre. Wyldfyre believed that the Lava-Tides were obsessed with her, but the Lava-Tides revealed that her pranks had actually caused them harm. The Lava-Tide Leader rejected Wyldfyre's claim of being a hero due to her past crimes. Kai proposed a solution that if Wyldfyre could prove that she had changed, the Lava-Tides might consider giving them the Dragon Core. Kai and Wyldfyre set out to fix the mess caused by Wyldfyre. They started by collecting all the scattered Wyldmelons and then proceeded to clean the statue of Lava-Todd the Wise. While Wyldfyre reminisced about her past enjoyment of dressing up statues, Kai reminded her that it was important to consider the feelings of others. He explained that if the pranks were not enjoyed by everyone, it could be seen as bullying. Wyldfyre brushed off the lack of appreciation from the Lava-Tides, but Kai stressed that their main objective was to obtain the core, and continuing with pranks would hinder their progress. Kai urged Wyldfyre to refrain from further pranks, as people were relying on them. Reluctantly, Wyldfyre agreed to stop pranking for the time being. The Lava-Tide Leader was surprised as she witnessed Wyldfyre's genuine efforts to make amends. Sensing an opportunity to boast, Wyldfyre confidently stated that Kai had performed adequately, although not exceptionally, under her leadership. The Lava-Tide Leader humbly extended her hand in friendship, suggesting a handshake as a symbol of reconciliation. Initially, Wyldfyre had intentions to prank the leader with the fire handshake, but ultimately decided against it. As the two shook hands, the Lava-Tide Leader suddenly revealed that the entire interaction had been a prank and swiftly threw the Dragon Core back into the Pit of No Return. The Lava-Tide Leader labeled Wyldfyre as a bully who would never change, but still was grateful for the cleanup efforts in their village as she and the other Lava-Tides departed. Wyldfyre, feeling a sense of disappointment reluctantly admitted that it had indeed been a well-executed prank.

With the Dragon Core thrown into the Pit of No Return, Wyldfyre admits that they gave it a try, but it seems like they've hit another dead end. She starts to walk away, prompting Kai to inquire about her destination, reminding her of the importance of finding the Dragon Core. Wyldfyre explains to Kai that the pit is known as the "Pit of No Return" for a reason. She emphasizes her preference for returning from her experiences. Kai voiced his concern and insists that they cannot simply walk away from this mission. In response, Wyldfyre confidently asserts that she can indeed walk away, demonstrating her determination to prioritize her own safety. She assures Kai that she will inform everyone about his death if it were to happen. Kai acknowledges that he may not survive the ordeal unless he has someone to guide him. Seizing the opportunity, Wyldfyre agreeed to lead Kai into the pit, acknowledging that her leadership is his only hope for success. Despite Kai's emphasis on the importance of caution as they descended into the pit together, Wyldfyre disregard his advice. However, Wyldfyre's attention was immediately captivated by a pile of Wyldmelons, with the Dragon Core resting on top of it. As they retrieved the Dragon Core, Wyldfyre couldn't help but question the reasoning behind the pit being named the Pit of No Return. Suddenly, a swarm of death wasps emerged from the Wyldmelons. Wyldfyre and Kai swiftly escaped from the death wasps, managing to close the Pit of No Return behind them. They celebrated their escape. However, their joy was short-lived as Dorama and his stagehands arrived, taking possession of the Dragon Core.

They Call It Doom[]

While in an attempt to get the Dragon Core, the Lava-Tide Leader had launched flaming boulders, to her excitement. Later, Wyldfyre inadvertently makes the leader grow big. Upon grabbing the core and making their escape, Wyldfyre discovers Heatwave has been injured.

Land of Lost Things[]

While trying to tend to Heatwave, Kai suddenly takes off and Wyldfyre follows him before watching him smash some rocks, which she believed was a waste of time, until she discovered he was looking for geodes. After getting enough, Kai and Wyldfyre melt the geode crystals to apply to Heatwave's wound, which Wyldfyre believed won't work, until the wound cooled down, to her surprise.

The Administration[]

While waiting for the melted crystal to heal Heatwave, an impatient Wyldfyre, on Kai's insistence, learnt to be patient by practicing forms alongside him, until Heatwave was better and was surprised when he licked her from behind. As they were about to return, they discover the core they obtained was a fake, as Dorama had the real one. Upon relaying the news to Lloyd, Zane, and Arin, they make their way to Imperium, where they are soon knocked down by an explosion's shockwave.

Absolute Power[]

While surveying how to enter Imperium, Wyldfyre is told by Kai to attract some guards, which worked as some arrive on hoverboards before the other ninja knock them out while she balanced a staff in her hand before being told to stop by Kai, later accompanying him on a hoverboard as they soon enter the city. When their attempt to infiltrate Imperium, Wyldfyre and the other ninja get ambushed, only to be taken down by the Imperium Teen Resistance Force, disappointing her as she wanted to fight them.

We Are All Dragons[]

Wyldfyre follows Percival's resistance, loudly humming a spy tune, until Kai taught her properly before encountering Beatrix in her mech. After making a plan to expose Beatrix's lies, Kai and Wyldfyre decide to destroy the armory as a diversion. However, the place they destroyed was a shoulder pad storage facility, because Kai gave her the map, which she had upside-down. Briefly educated on the difference between rivers and power lines, Wyldfyre discovers Arin, Sora, and Percival successfully uplinked the footage that exposed Beatrix's lies. Eventually finding the armory, they set fire to it and casually walk away from it.

The Power Within[]

Wyldfyre joined the others in facing Beatrix, who uses her MergeQuake weapon to send the former into a rift. After Lloyd stabilized the world, Kai, Wyldfyre, and Riyu were returned before Wyldfyre immediately embraced her mentor. Sometime later, Wyldfyre and Kai engaged in a friendly competition to determine who could successfully hit the maximum number of soda cans. Employing her Heat power, Wyldfyre skillfully melted her cans, while Kai relied on his precision. Curious about the concept of precision, Wyldfyre inquired, prompting Kai to explain that it involved honing one's skills through practice, leading to further improvement. As they observed the aftermath of their efforts, they noticed a stark contrast between their respective cans. The observation led them to realize that Wyldfyre's Elemental Power isn't Fire as her cans were melted. Shortly after, Wyldfyre had one of Arin's Snowberry Pies for their victory.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

NinjaFanInfinity and SpinjitzuForDays99 film Wyldfyre, who stands on top of Riyu as she attempts to get a cat out of a tree, only for it to jump and latch onto her, making Wyldfyre fall off of Riyu while trying to get the cat off.

Later, Wyldfyre joined in on dancing among many citizens, Arin, Sora, and Riyu after Sora turned Lobbo's mech into a DJ set.

Shattered Dreams[]

Wyldfyre was present when Lloyd explained his visions to the other ninja. During this time, she only commented on Mr. Frohicky's Fetid Swamp Tea, excitedly declaring that it looked "so good." Later, she joined the other ninja in an effort to liberate Cloud Kingdom from Ras and the Wolf Clan, believing that they would have no issues in defeating their foes. Unfortunately Wyldfyre's expectations were far from reality, as she would find herself constantly being ragged by both the Wolf Mask Warriors and Cinder, the latter of whom broke her left leg after a reckless attack.

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

Wyldfyre, sporting a bandage for her injured leg, is woken up from her slumber by Kai, who tells her he is going on a quest and wants her to stay behind and heal, but she opposes it. She managed to stowaway on the Destiny's Bounty, where she blew her cover by sneezing before Sora helped her out. Kai intended to have her returned to the monastery, but by then, they were at their location: Phantasm Cave. When questioned how she even escaped from Mr. Frohicky's watchful eye, Wyldfyre boasted in reply that she had devised the most cunning plan imaginable; Sora though cut her off, asking if her plan was stuffing a bunch of rocks beneath her bed sheets to make it look like she was sleeping, completely shocking Wyldfyre who questioned how Sora could possibly know about her plan.

As the ninja realized they would have to climb their way out of the cave, Kai turned to Wyldfyre with a disapproving look, commenting again that she should have stayed home to recuperate from her injury as ordered. Wyldfyre denied having knowledge of who Kai was referring to. In response to Kai's points, Sora constructed for him a new climber mech, which he began to use to haul up Riyu and Wyldfyre, much to the latter's displeasure.

After escaping the first portion of the cave, the ninja were ambushed by a Dream-Guyver, which put them all to sleep while experiencing their worst fears. For Wyldfyre, she saw Heatwave dying form the Wasting Sickness. After being freed by the monster's grasp by Nya, Wyldfyre dwelled for a moment on her experience, even beginning to mutter the "wasting sickness" with fear apparent in her tone before regaining her composure. The group then approached a cliff edge shrouded by mist as far as the eye could see. Believing that Lloyd's visions had led them here, Kai voluntarily leapt out into the mist, leading to a brief moment of silence that was broken by an unnerved Wyldfyre who quietly began asking if Kai had just leapt to his death. Fortunately, Kai was perfectly fine and was actually waiting for the rest to follow suit. Relieved, Wyldfyre boasted that she knew he was fine the entire time before descending downward to the Training plateau of the Dragon Masters.

Force From the East[]

Arin's attempts to reason with the dragon proved futile, leading Wyldfyre to believe that her ability to speak dragon would be more effective. However, even with her communication skills, the dragon continued its aggressive behavior. While the rest of the ninja engaged in distracting the dragon, Lloyd discovered a solution to unlock the gate. By strategically placing boulders in a nearby bucket, they were able to activate a chain mechanism that gradually lowered the gate. With Riyu's assistance, Wyldfyre successfully placed the first boulder in the bucket, marking the beginning of their progress. As the other ninja joined in and placed the remaining boulders, the gate swung open fully. Wyldfyre and the group advanced, only to encounter another dragon emerging from behind the gate. Without hesitation, the ninja prepared for another battle. However, to their surprise, the dragons unexpectedly bowed down. Curious about this change in behavior, Kai turned to Wyldfyre, the resident dragon expert, and inquired about dragons attacking people and then suddenly being friendly. Wyldfyre confirmed that dragons would only be friendly if the people they attacked fell off a cliff. The second dragon, impressed by the ninja's success in passing all three tests, congratulated them by giving a high five with her tail, which both Wyldfyre and Riyu gladly accepted. As the conversation shifted towards the teacher the ninja were searching for, the two dragons revealed themselves as Egalt and Rontu, the very teachers they sought.

Wyldfyre attentively listened as Lloyd explained to the Dragon Masters about Ras rediscovering the Wolf Clan and Shatterspin, and his plan to free the Forbidden Five. The Dragon Masters then revealed themselves as the ones who had defeated and banished the Forbidden Five in the past, erasing them from memory. Egalt expressed confidence that the Forbidden Five could never return, until Lloyd brought up the Ritual of the Blood Moon. The Dragon Masters began deliberating with each other in their dragon language, causing Wyldfyre to speculate that they were discussing the end of the world. Sora, however, assumed that Wyldfyre was joking, to which Wyldfyre firmly stated that she was not. Lloyd emphasized that with the Dragon Masters fighting alongside the ninja, the Wolf Clan stood no chance. Egalt and Rontu, on the other hand, claimed that they were too old to fight with the ninja. Despite this, Rontu suggested that they could train the ninja to learn the Rising Dragon. Egalt questioned Rontu's decision, arguing that the ninja were weak. Wyldfyre challenged Egalt's assertion of the ninja's weakness, before she slipped off but was promptly caught by Kai and Nya. Following a brief argument with Rontu, Egalt reluctantly agreed to train the ninja, setting the start of their training for the next day. He reminded them that while they had received good training, they had only just begun to scratch the surface of what they needed to learn.

The Spell at the Waterfall[]

Wyldfyre engaged in a sparring session between herself, Arin, Riyu, Nya, and Lloyd to serve as a demonstration of the ninja's fighting capabilities. During the session, Wyldfyre initiated her attack by hurling a fireball at Arin, who leapt out of the way. Feeling a sharp pain in her leg, Wyldfyre halted her attack to check on her leg. Nya then leapt out from behind Riyu and blasted Wyldfyre with a jet of water, overpowering the latter's stream of fire and knocking her to the ground. The ninja were then divided into "Fang" categories. Noticing she was not placed into a group, Wyldfyre excitedly asked Rontu if this was because she had no need for training because she was already in a "hundredth Fang level." When Rontu explained gently it was because Wyldfyre needed time to heal from her injury, the heartbroken girl asked bitterly in reply if the truth was really that there was already a Fire ninja on the team. Surprised by her statement, Rontu stated that Wyldfyre was not the Master of Fire but rather the Master of Heat.

While the others began to train, Wyldfyre could do nothing but watch in jealousy underneath a tree. After witnessing Rontu perform Spinjitzu, Wyldfyre grumbled to herself about her predicament but came up with a brilliant idea. Using mud, leaves, and a pair of sunglasses, she crafted for herself a disguise of a Mucoid Warrior and tried to request training from Rontu once more. Unfortunately, Wyldfyre's disguise fooled no one and Rontu once again gently reminded the girl that she could not train until her leg was healed. Wyldfyre though did not take it well and walked off in disappointment and frustration, grumbling just loud enough for everyone else to hear her.

To Mysterium[]

After watching Sora and Riyu's training for some time, Wyldfyre grew bored and frustrated, so she painted a face on a rock to rant at. When she spotted Egalt flying by overhead, Wyldfyre approached him and attempted to make small talk with the elderly dragon. Egalt at first dismissed Wyldfyre and soon flew off in a huff after Wyldfyre pointed out that in spite of the former's negative attitude toward humans, he was still training the ninja because he secretly held hope that they would be capable of learning Rising Dragon technique. Wyldfyre then spotted a scale left behind by Egalt and recognized its distinctive blackened ends as a sign of late stage Wasting Sickness.

Fugitives From Madness[]

Wyldfyre prepared a large bowl of Janus Melon Stew and brought it to Egalt while making small talk with him. Although Egalt was reluctant to speak at first, he slowly began to warm up to the girl as she spoke about the stories her dragon family used to tell her about the Dragon Masters. Wyldfyre then directed Egalt to the stew and noted that while it was not a cure, it could at the very least ease some of the pain he was suffering from late stage Wasting Sickness. Though the prideful dragon rebuffed Wyldfyre's claims at first, he finally relented when Wyldfyre made it clear she was well aware of the symptoms of the disease and that there was no point in protecting his pride. Egalt then devoured the stew, much to Wyldfyre's delight. She later regrouped with the other ninja and watched with excitement as Kai was the first to successfully perform Rising Dragon technique, only to soon watch in horror as the Blood Moon rose and the Dragon Masters were petrified into stone statues.

Secrets of the Wyldness[]

All the ninja reacted to the Dragon Masters' petrified states with shock, but Wyldfyre took it the hardest. Refusing to accept it as reality, Wyldfyre rapidly insisted that their petrified states were merely a prank. To prove her theory, Wyldfyre walked up to Egalt and began tickling him though her efforts were for naught and Wyldfyre's hopefulness began to fade. In a last ditch effort to prove she was right, Wyldfyre stomped on Egalt with her injured leg but only hurt herself in the process. She now accepted the Dragon Masters' petrification but still held hope that it was not permanent. Since nothing else could be done for them now, Wyldfyre and the others were forced to depart in search of Ras and the Wolf Clan.

As the ninja sailed through the skies atop the Bounty, they suddenly encountered a wild storm that Lloyd quickly realized was unnatural. While most of the ninja were thrown overboard in the ensuing turbulence, Wyldfyre, Sora, and Kai managed to stay onboard but found themselves facing a much worse fate: being precariously perched atop a deep chasm, one that Wyldfyre described as "nothingness and instant doom." The three's only hope for survival lay in the climber mech, but it was on the other side of the Bounty and even the slightest bit of movement would tip the ship over to its doom. Following Sora's plan, Sora and Wyldfyre jumped at the same time to tip the Bounty towards its stern to buy Kai enough time to reach the mech. Unfortunately, the mech tipped over in the process and its weight caused the Bounty to fall off its perch facing the bow. Wyldfyre held onto Sora as both screamed, bracing themselves for their impending demise. Fortunately, Kai was able to enter the cockpit of the mech in time and Sora was barely able to catch Wyldfyre. The three then began to make their ascent out from the ravine.

The Forest of Spirits[]

Wyldfyre and Sora clung onto the sides of the Climber Mech as the three continued to ascend up the cliff. When Kai asked if they were doing well, Wyldfyre denied being afraid of heights and that she merely had her eyes closed to rest. At that moment, the Climber Mech lost involuntarily shifted after a part of the rockface collapsed, sending Wyldfyre plunging back down the chasm. Fortunately, her screaming would be halted when Kai caught her with one of the mech's hooks.
