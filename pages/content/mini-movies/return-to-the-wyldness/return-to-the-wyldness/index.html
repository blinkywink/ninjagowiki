Return to the Wyldness is a series of canon Ninjago: Dragons Rising - Season 2 shorts and a part of the Wyldfyre campaign. The series was announced on July 1, 2024 and the episodes were released on July 8, 2024 on LEGO.com.

Official description

Official description[]

“Ninja never quit” – those were Kai’s famous last words. But what ARE you supposed to do when you lose your best friend? Wyldfyre knows: be angry alone! Unless that is the sound of... dragon eggs hatching? Continue the story of Dragons Rising Season 2, only on LEGO® Insiders Club![1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This marks the first series of canon Ninjago shorts to not have a returning character be voiced by the main actor/actress.
    • As Kazumi Evans provides the voice for Wyldfyre in the main Dragons Rising series but did not return for this series of shorts.
      • Wyldfyre is now also the third Ninja to have been recast, the first being Lloyd Garmadon when he got recast for Season 8: Sons of Garmadon replacing Jillian Michaels with Sam Vincent and the second being Cole in Season 15: Crystalized replacing Kirby Morrow with Andrew Francis.
  • These series of shorts were animated by Liquid Animation. Who would also animate Kai's Monstrous Journey the following year.[2]
