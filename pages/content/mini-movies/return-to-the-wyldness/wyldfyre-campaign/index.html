Wyldfyre is a multimedia campaign that was announced on July 1, 2024 that contains three canon Ninjago: Dragons Rising mini-series all released on LEGO.com on July 8, 2024. It depicts Wyldfyre after the events of "Rising Ninja" as she tries to deal with the loss of Kai.

Official description

Official description[]

Find out what happens after Kai’s disappearance, discover the Wyldness and meet new baby dragons – watch the Wyldfyre series![1]
