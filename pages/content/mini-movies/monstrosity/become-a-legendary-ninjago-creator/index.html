Become a legendary NINJAGO creator

Information

Start Date

June 20, 2025

End Date

August 8, 2025

Overview

Become a legendary NINJAGO creator was a Ninjago: Legends-themed contest held on LEGO Ideas and the LEGO Play app. It took place from June 20, 2025[1] to 6:00am EST on August 8, 2025.[2] Winning submissions went on to be featured in The Land of Monsters.

Official description

Official description[]

LEGO Ideas[]

Inspired by the launch of Monstrosity, the new NINJAGO LEGENDS animated series, we’re asking you to show us your brick-built NINJAGO creations – and tell us how they might inspire the next NINJAGO LEGENDS story![2]

LEGO.com[]

Kai is still lost in the Land of Monsters! What kind of new creature will he encounter? Can you imagine and draw a new kind of monster? Share your idea with us – it might inspire the next NINJAGO® Legends story![3]

Prizes

Prizes[]

  • Having one's original monster from their entry be used to inspire a future Ninjago: Legends project.[2]
Winners

Winners[]

LEGO Play submissions[]

  • Clomgax by Tetahimeraki
  • OctoBeast by Sharky Walker
  • PoisonMane by Chinnygarmadon123
  • TwinHeadedDragon by Pizzaflavrouredsanitizer
  • HornedCatMermaid by PrincessMightyFork (adapted into the Horned Cat Tribe)
  • CyberTREX by LieutenantVibrantbean (adapted into the Trexxs)
  • GiantCentipede by 1BR16 (adapted into Kebaber)[4]

LEGO Ideas submissions[]

  • Shadow Cave Monster by The_cool_brick
  • The Dragon Monster: Kuroda by 敏捷迅速的克拉格王爵
  • Chironograx by Trainerkn (adapted into the Chironograx)
  • Kai’s Titan Fire Dragon Mech by McIDontKnowWhatIJustBuilt (adapted into Dragonhead)
  • The Lion of Dragon Temple by Ddiinnoossaauurr rrooaarr (adapted into the Monster lion)
  • Ninja Racer by DDbrick_works (adapted into the Ninja Racer)
  • Nocturox, the Brood Weaver by BuildingRe-Bjorn (adapted into the Queen of the monster insects)
  • Fugidove’s Plan by LastAngryToad (adapted into the Queen of the monster insects)
  • The Beast by NM Brix (adapted into The Beast)[5]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Trainerkn (one of the winners) is a rollback and former administrator on the Ninjago Wiki.[6]
  • Nocturox, the Brood Weaver by BuildingRe-Bjorn and Fugidove’s Plan by LastAngryToad ended up being combined into the Queen of the monster insects in The Land of Monsters, instead of being integrated into the story separately like the other entries.
