This page will outline what is expected for each LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game series page.

Introduction

Introduction[]

Tabbers[]

Tabbers of the following format should be added to the top of the page:

{{Tabs| {{Tabs/1|LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game/Series [series number in German release]|Overview}} {{Tabs/2|LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game/Series [series number in German release]/List of cards|List of cards}} }}

Infobox[]

Please use the {{TCGSeries}} infobox to outline the following information as described:

  • color - The colour of the infobox should roughly correspond with the colour of the top/bottom of the image (for example, the infobox for the Series 9 Next Level page is red to match the image).
  • header - The header should be of the following format: "Series [Series number in German release]" (for Next Level Series, "Next Level" should be added at the end)
  • image - For the image, prioritize using an image of the series' Starter Pack's binder cover, specifically from the Hungarian release (since it's the only modern version where the cover is in English), or the UK release (if applicable). If an image of the UK or Hungarian release's binder cover is unavailable, the German cover may be used in its place, but the {{HelpWanted}} template must also be added to the top of the page as shown (note that the text may be modified if there are other things needed for the page that are required to be indicated on the same template, or if the series was released in the UK):
"Could I be the Green Ninja?"
Calling all Hungarian editors!
This page's infobox requires an English binder cover image, only known to have been available in Hungary. If you have access to such an image and could add it to this page, it would be a great help!
  • release date - The dates when the first card from the series was released in each applicable country should be listed here. That's to say, oftentimes, a card from a series will release prior to the release of the mainline products of a series (e.g., Starter Packs, Blisters, etc.), usually via the Ninjago Magazine or another Blue Ocean product. The release date of said product in a given country may be used as the debut of a series in said country. However, if this date is unknown or this situation has not happened, the release date of the mainline series products may be used instead. All countries to receive the series may have their debut date listed in this section (so long as there's an accompanying citation), but countries must be listed in the following order: the UK (if applicable), Hungary (since their TCG products are in English, and the most consistent among countries to receive English cards), other countries to receive English cards for the series in question (in alphabetical order), Germany (since Blue Ocean is German), then all other countries to receive the series in question (in alphabetical order).
  • card count - Number of mainline cards across all versions.
  • le card count - Number of Limited Edition cards across all versions.
  • xxl card count - Number of XXL cards across all versions.
  • xxxl card count - Number of of XXXL cards across all versions (currently only applicable for Series 4).
  • previous series - The previous series.
  • next series - The proceeding series.

Opening paragraph[]

The opening paragraph should be of the following format:

Series [series number in German release] of the LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game, subtitled [series subtitle], debuted in [earliest year of release across all versions]. It contains [# of mainline cards across all versions] mainline cards, [# of Limited Edition cards across all versions] Limited Edition cards, and [# of XXL cards across all versions] XXL cards.


Note that XXXL cards may be listed here as well, however, this is currently only applicable for Series 4.

Cards[]

This section should list every single card from the series in order. They should be organized in subsections as following:

  • In the order given (excluding the Limited Edition cards section), all subsections as seen on the inner binder covers included with the Starter Pack from the Hungrian release, or UK release if applicable.
  • Limited Edition cards
  • XXL cards
  • XXXL cards

LE and XXL cards should generally follow the German numbering. All cards in the list should be hyperlinked to their respective page, with the text on the list omitting the "(TCG S[series number])" part from the page title. For example, Card 1 - Kai (TCG S1) should be listed on the Series 1 page as "Card 1 - Kai."

If a card is not available in the German release, it should take the card number from the country release with the highest total number of its card type. For example, if an LE card doesn't exist in the German version, but exists in the Polish and Hungarian versions, if the Polish version has a higher number of LE cards than the Hungarian version, the card's listing should take the number from the Polish release of the card as opposed to the Hungarian release.

If a card lacks a number, its page should be named as following: "Card - [card name] (TCG S[series number])." For an example, see Card - Cole (TCG S11).

If the official English translations are inaccessable to editors, the {{HelpWanted}} template must be added to the top of the page as following (note that the text may be modified if there are other things needed for the page that are required to be indicated on the same template):

"Could I be the Green Ninja?"
Calling all Hungarian editors!
The page requires the official English card list. If you have access to the English release of this series, only known to have been available in Hungary, adding the official English card translations to this page would be a great help!

Until the official English translations are added to the page, the {{Section stub}} template should be added under the "Cards" section in their place. Rough translations of the cards from the German release may be added, but the {{HelpWanted}} template must stay until the official translations are added. If pages for each card are created using the roughly-translated names, each page must have the {{HelpWanted}} template on it to make it clear that the English translation is unofficial, but an official English translation does exist. For such pages, the {{NoEnglish}} template should not be used, since such cards do exist in English, but their names just don't happen to be known by wiki editors. Once the official English translations are added, each page must be updated with the official translations, and only then may the {{HelpWanted}} template be removed.

If a card doesn't exist in English, its name should be determined by comparing the official translations for similarly titled cards. For example, Card LE10 - Böser Wu was not released in the English version of Series 1. However, Card LE4 - Böser Ras Limited Edition was later released in English in Series 9 Next Level as Card LE12 - Evil Ras Limited Edition, so we can determine that Card LE10 - Böser Wu would likely have been translated as "Card LE10 - Evil Wu." If a card has a unique naming scheme, it's German name (or other language name, only if not released in German) may be used so long as it overlaps with English. For an example, see Card LE23 - Lloyd, Cole & Zane X-Mas Edition. If it has a unique naming scheme that doesn't overlap with English, a rough translation may be used for the name. Note that for the pages of cards without an official English release, the {{NoEnglish}} template must be added to each of their pages to explain how the English name was determined. However, this template should not be added to the corresponding series' page.

Card rarities[]

Card rarities should be written as seen on the inner binder covers included with the Starter Pack from the Hungrian release, or UK release if applicable. They should be listed in ascending order from least rare to most rare. Their star rarity level (if applicable) may be represented with the "★" character. Trivia[]

The trivia section may include trivia facts related to the series. However, trivia points in relation to the TV season a series corresponds with should be omitted, as series often pull from too many installments to reasonably and clearly list. However, trivia points in relation to similar branding between seasons and TCG series are permitted. For example, Series 2 has season 7 branding, so that may be listed in the trivia of the Series 2 page. Another example is Series 11, which is branded with particular concern to Dragons Rising Season 3 and 4, so that may be listed in the trivia as well.

Gallery[]

In each of the following subsections, images must be organized by country/language using tabbers. The name of the language should be used for the tabber name if there is one version of the given series in that language. For situations where there are multiple versions of the game featuring the same language, the country should be used for the tabber name. For example, Austria, Germany, and Switzerland all recieved the exact same version of Series 8, so the tabber for their version is labelled "German." However, the Czech/Slovak release of that series and the Hungarian release of that series, despite both including English cards, had different packaging and card selections. Thus, they each get their own tabber labelled by country name.

The tabbers must be of the following order:

  • UK
  • Hungary
  • Other countries with English cards for the series in question (in alphabetical order)
  • German
  • Other countries/languages to recieve the series in question (in alphabetical order)

Products[]

Images of individual products from the series may be added here. Images of things not sold on their own should not be added here. For example, an image of the series' Starter Pack may be added, but not an image of the rules book included with the Starter Pack. Images of each type of Multipack and Blister for the series may be added, but images of the Limited Edition cards included with each Blister/Multipack should not be added. However, images of each type of Booster may be added, since they are sold on their own (despite also being included with Starter Packs, Multipacks, Blisters, etc.).

Hungarian product images may be found on sajtoforras.hu.

German product images may be found in high resolution on mueller.de, or if not available there, blue-ocean.de/lego/lego-ninjago/. However, if a product image is not available on either site, or is available in higher resolution elsewhere, other sources may be used.

Cards[]

This section should not include a gallery, but use the {{Main}} template to redirect users to the main gallery page for this series. The page should be named in the following format: "LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game/Series [series number in German release]/List of cards."

Videos[]

Videos posted online by Blue Ocean or other official distributors of this series may be added here. This includes videos focused on advertising a magazine, so long as the magazine includes trading cards from the series in question. If there are no videos available, feel free to omit this section.

Feel free to check the following official YouTube channels for videos featuring the LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game:

  • BlueOceanClips (German)
  • Blue Ocean Entertainment Polska (Poland)

Note that there are more channels out there that post the desired videos. These are just reliable examples.

Translations[]

For this section, please use the {{Foreign names}} template. The official translation of a series' name for a given language should be added here. The series number for a given language/country version should also be specified in the same template. Each foreign name should have an accompanying citation. Note that countries that receive English cards often still have an official translation for the title of the series in the local language (often written on the series' products' packaging, or on advertisements usually found in the Ninjago Magazine).

Redirects

Redirects[]

The following pages should be created, which redirect to the corresponding Series' page:

  • A page named as "Series [number]" (for example, the page "Series 9 Next Level" redirects to the main LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game/Series 9 Next Level page)
  • A page named after the Series' subtitle (for example, the page "Dragon Storm" redirects to the main LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game/Series 10 page)
    • In the case that a Series' subtitle is the same as another wiki page, it should be specified in the page's name by adding "(TCG)" to the end (for example, the page redirecting to LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game/Series 9 is named "Dragons Rising (TCG)")
    • For Next Level Series, the subtitle must be followed by "Next Level" in the page name (for example, the page redirecting to LEGO Ninjago Trading Card Game/Series 9 Next Level is named "Dragons Rising Next Level")
