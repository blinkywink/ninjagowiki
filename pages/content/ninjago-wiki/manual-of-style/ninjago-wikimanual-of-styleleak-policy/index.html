This page will outline our leak policy as well as our spoiler policy.

Leaks

Leaks[]

Images of unreleased figures leaked from factories or watermarked set images are strictly forbidden on the wiki. Any image portraying leaked material will be deleted and the uploader may be blocked.

Leaked information, speculation, and rumors are not allowed on the Ninjago Wiki. Leaked information includes (but is not limited to) images with a "confidential" watermark and minifigures which have been taken from factories that sometimes find their way onto the web.

Speculation and rumors are any assumptions about the story or set that does not have confirmation in official media. Any and all information about leaked sets is not allowed. Please wait until the sets appear on LEGO.com or the site of an official vendor before adding them to the Wiki. Plot points from episodes may only be added to a page once the episode has officially aired in English somewhere in the world.

Information based on future content in all situations must have a reliable source. If you would like to dispute a source, please contact a staff member.

Spoilers

Spoilers[]

Upcoming content[]

When non-English episodes are aired before the episode is released in English, or if episodes are released "prematurely" (when official providers unexpectedly air episodes and soon delist them), special actions are taken to avoid giving spoilers and false information to readers as well as fellow editors.

Users are still welcome to edit the history section of the subject, plot of an episode, appearances section, and gallery; however, usage of various templates and markups are required. These measures will allow users to continue editing the article freely, but it will hide the content from public view.

When brand new information and content is made known in tandem with an upcoming season, book, etc., the {{No Leaks}} template will be used on newly created articles.

If existing pages include upcoming and new information, separate measures will be put into place. For articles that contain major spoilers, the {{Spoiler}} notice will be applied. For articles with minor spoilers and plot details, such as character cameos or minor info from social media, the {{SpoilerBlur}} markup will be utilized sparingly.

Finally, translations of dialogue is often uncertain and inaccurate from its English counterpart, so information about the origin of objects, characters, etc. is not allowed on pages. Under no circumstances may users rename and/or move pages based on information from non-English episodes. If something new is introduced that doesn't have an article, users are welcome to create the article, but this template must be added.

Released content[]

When an episode airs in English, all information related to it will be barred on the discussions forum for a duration of 72 hours. Discussion for new content will be relegated to the Spoilers Discussion post.

On articles, once newly released content passes its worldwide release date, any applicable instances of {{No Leaks}} will be replaced by the {{Spoiler}} notice. The Spoilers template will remain on these pages for a duration of 2 weeks from the worldwide release date, which can then be removed after the timeframe passes. Any usage of {{SpoilerBlur}} will instead remain for only 72 hours before removal is allowed.

Citations

Citations[]

The following are acceptable sources when citing.

  • Information from people who work on Ninjago such as writers and directors (e.g. Tommy Andreasen, Bragi Schut, etc.), cast members (e.g. Vincent Tong, Brent Miller, etc.), set designers and other LEGO employees (e.g. Tommy Kalmar, Simon Lucas, etc), and artists (The Fold, etc)
  • Content from Cartoon Network, LEGO.com, and other official distributors
  • Certified vendors such as LAN members, Amazon, and Target as well as certain foreign sellers

Please avoid citing these sources as they don't have the most accurate information and are not part of the Ninjago franchise.

  • Unofficial YouTube channels dedicated to Ninjago
  • Unofficial websites
    • Wikipedia, or other wikis on FANDOM
    • IMDb and other user edited sites
  • Posts made from accounts with no official connection to Ninjago
