This page will outline what is expected for each Episode page.

Title

Title[]

Episode pages should always have only the episode name as its title.

If a new episode's name has not been revealed but plans to be released, please do not create a page as a sub-holder like "Episode 147," "Season 13, Episode 2," "Unknown Episode," etc.

Introduction

Introduction[]

Tabs[]

After creating the page, please first use the {{Tabs}} template to make a menu of tabs for subpage linking. The tabs should include an overview (the episode page itself), the episode's transcript, and lastly the episode's gallery.

Quote[]

Another part of an episode page is the quote. The {{Quote}} template should go at the very top of the page. For the quote itself, it should be the main idea of the episode and/or a catchphrase said by a character.

Infobox[]

The next step is to use the {{Infobox episode}} template and fill out the necessary information about the episode in the spaces provided.

  • name - This field is for the name of the episode.
  • season - The season number should go here.
  • number - The episode number should go here.
  • image - This field should show a picture of the episode title. If possible, please try to get a high quality, non-watermarked image. Be sure to put [[File: at the start and |250px]] at the end.
  • airdate - This field should list all of the English airdates.
  • writer - The writer of the episode should go here.
  • director - The director of the episode should go here.
  • previous - The episode before the one you're editing goes here.
  • next - The episode after the one you're editing goes here.

Opening paragraph[]

The opening paragraph should have the title of the episode in bold, the episode and season number, and the release date of the episode. Here are a couple of good examples.

  • The Son of Lilly is the sixteenth and final episode of the thirteenth season, and the 160th episode overall of Ninjago. It aired on July 24, 2020, in Australia.
  • The Darkness Comes is the first episode of the tenth season, and the 95th overall of Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu. It aired in New Zealand on January 19, 2019, April 6, 2019 in Canada, and on April 19, 2019, in the United States with the rest of Season 10 as a film.

Another important note is episodes before Season 11 of Ninjago should have Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu while episodes from Season 11 through 15 should only have Ninjago since it was dropped during the soft reboot. Episodes of Dragons Rising should have Ninjago: Dragons Rising.

Required headings

Required headings[]

  • Synopsis

This is to be taken from an official TV website such as Cartoon Network, Foxtel, etc. When possible please make sure to link up any articles that currently exist. Furthermore, if the episode is part of an upcoming season, the {{No Leaks}} is required. Finally, please do not edit the Synopsis unless it was pasted incorrectly or was vandalized.

  • Plot

This section should explain in detail what happened in the episode.

  • Appearances

This section should list all of the characters, creatures, events, items, locations, species, and vehicles that appeared in the episode. Each of the sections listed earlier should each get its own sub-heading followed by bullet points below. For the characters section, another sub-heading should be used to list the cast, non-speaking, and miscellaneous characters.

  • Trivia

Any miscellaneous information such as cultural references, continuity, and errors is placed here. Each of the sections listed earlier should each get its own sub-headings followed by bullet points below.

  • Soundtracks

Any soundtracks which were present in the episode should be placed here.

  • Videos

Any videos of the episodes may go here.

  • Translations

A table which shows the different translations.

  • References (if applicable)

Any references made on the page should be placed here using the <references/> tag.
