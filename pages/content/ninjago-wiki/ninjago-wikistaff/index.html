Administrators[]

Administrators, AKA Spinjitzu Masters, are responsible for working closely with the bureaucrat(s) to help lead the wiki. On the Ninjago Wiki, administrators are typically the ones to usher in reform after coming to a consensus or acquiring approval from the bureaucrat(s). They're similar to content moderators in the sense that they're responsible for protecting, patrolling, and deleting pages, but they have the added permission of being able to block users.

Rollbacks[]

On the Ninjago Wiki, rollbacks are treated as the Trainees of the staff team, as users hoping to become full staff members (content moderators and above) must first hold the position. They're able to utilize rollback permissions to revert all of a user's consecutive edits on a page and are responsible for using said permission to assist with enforcing the policy of the Manual of Style.

Discussions admins[]

Discussions administrators, AKA Dragon Masters, possess the same permissions as administrators but are limited in jurisdiction to Discussions. Their responsibilities and functional permissions more closely resemble that of Discussions moderators in the sense that they're in charge of upholding Discussions guidelines/policy. Discussions moderators report to them or an administrator if a Discussions user needs to be blocked or formally warned.

Community manager[]

The community manager, AKA a Source Dragon, is a FANDOM-appointed representative assigned to oversee large wikis such as the Ninjago Wiki. On the Ninjago Wiki, they're responsible for keeping the bureaucrat, administrators, and general public up to date with FANDOM and MediaWiki updates and new functions, and help the wiki continue to grow and prosper healthily. The Ninjago Wiki hasn't had an official Community Manager since early 2024, however, our previous Community Manager continues to assist us.

Staff history

Staff history[]

Since the beginning of the Ninjago Wiki on December 28, 2010, we've had many staffers of all types come and go. Below, you'll find complete lists of all staffers in the chronological order in which they started serving (as opposed to when they first joined). The current staffers are also included on these lists for easier staff ascension and promotion tracking, marked with "present" as their end date.

These table exempt the time periods where the same user holds both a high and low (usually redundant) role at the same time (examples include bureaucrat/administrator, or administrator/rollback) on the low position's table. For example, this means that if an existing administrator was promoted to bureaucrat, the end date for their administratorship would be on the start date of their bureaucratship. This is done to help track people who are former members of a position before ascending ranks, without causing confusion between said ranks.
