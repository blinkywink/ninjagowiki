Introduction

Introduction[]

As you may know, Ninjago and Legends of Chima are intertwined greatly. Ever since Dragons Rising, Ninjago has been pulling from and even referencing various aspects of Chima and the greater Wyldness. The two wikis often pull information from one another, crosslink to each other, and even share a similar user base.

With this in mind, we believe that it would be mutually beneficial for these wikis to merge. In short, this means that the content from the Chima Wiki will be imported to the Ninjago Wiki. There is no question that this content will need an overhaul. The current Chima Wiki is the result of a merger of 8 wikis, and it shows. The wiki lacks consistent style, quality, and organization. These are areas where the Ninjago Wiki thrives and can truly elevate the foundation.

Benefits

Benefits[]

We believe that merging the two wikis would take some time to complete but would benefit both wiki communities. We have outlined these areas below:

Chima[]

Over the years, the Chima Wiki has had many passionate editors. With this partnership, our goal is to take this existing content to the next level. Currently, the content on the Chima Wiki is fairly unmoderated without a clear or comprehensive manual of style. Additionally, the wiki lacks an active staff team. Over the years, this has led to inconsistent article quality and unreverted vandalism.

Merging with the Ninjago Wiki will bring heightened attention to the quality of content on the Chima side of the wiki and greater visibility to Chima Wiki content. The Ninjago Wiki consistently receives millions of monthly views compared to Chima Wiki's less than 50,000. Fandom's help page for merging communities claims the merged wiki will receive "higher SEO value for the joined community — meaning that more people will read your work, and more editors will join your cause."[1]

Ninjago[]

Ninjago Wiki and Chima Wiki users alike will gain greater interconnectability. As Ninjago: Dragons Rising unfolds and increases the connection between Ninjago and Chima, fans will become more curious about what Chima was about. Thus, having information in one succinct place will create added user retention for the wiki.

Wiki impact

Wiki impact[]

The outcome of this vote determines if the two wikis will move forward with merging. If this vote is successful, the next step is to contact Fandom. If this vote is unsuccessful, this effort to merge the two wikis will be shelved and potentially revisited in the future as more information becomes available.

Chima[]

In the event of a merger, content from the Chima Wiki will be ported to the Ninjago Wiki in phases using the import tool. This will help not overload the wiki with content that needs attention as well as to help preserve SEO. There should be enough time here to archive any blogs/talk pages that may not be able to be brought over.

Ninjago[]

To the general user, the immediate impact on the Ninjago Wiki will remain fairly minimal. The wiki will gain roughly 800 articles and will undergo an editing drive to get these articles up to wiki standards. These new articles will lead to increased traffic and new editors.

The plan

The plan[]

If Fandom grants approval for the merge, an operation page will be created to document the various steps needed to take action. The operation will entail the processes of importing page data, page cleanup, Manual of Style enforcement, categorization, etc. A list of pages to edit will be provided on the operation page to better keep track of what needs accomplishing.

All Chima pages will be marked as such and have a unique page background as well as use a separate font for article titles. There will be an appropriately themed main page for the Chima section that includes a link to the Chima Discord server.

FAQ

FAQ[]

  • Will the Ninjago Wiki's name or URL be changing?
    • Our URL will remain. Any potential name changes should be discussed in further votes.
  • Why don't we just keep both wikis up and simply fix up the Chima wiki?
    • There are multiple factors at play here:
      • This will greatly help moderation and ensure that the Chima content stays quality.
      • There is a high overlap of users between the Chima and Ninjago users.
      • Internal links are better for page views, meaning that in the long run Chima content will get more interactions.
  • How will those who are looking for Chima/Ninjago information know where the content they are looking at is from?
    • Chima articles will have their own backgrounds and header templates. This should help make this information clear to any viewers.
  • Will Chima-related posts be allowed on the Discussions Board?
    • Yes, Chima posts and discussions will be permitted and will abide by the normal discussions guidelines. Posts on the Chima wiki are already far and few between, so Chima discussions likely will remain relatively infrequent.
