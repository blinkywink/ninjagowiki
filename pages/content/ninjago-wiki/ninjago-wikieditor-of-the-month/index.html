How does it work?

How does it work?[]

Every user with at least 50 mainspace edits can nominate any editor who in their opinion deserves the award. They should be friendly, skilled, helpful, and genuinely interested in contributing to and improving the wiki.

Our nominations are open since the 20th day of the month and all of the eligible users can vote until the 10th day of the next month. Then, the award will be given to one of the nominees and after another 10 days nominations for that month will start. The voting itself is the same as regular votes.

Note: If no users are nominated for 1 month, nothing happens, but if this situation happens again for the next month, one of the administrators must choose one suitable candidate.

Rules to follow

Rules to follow[]

  1. You cannot nominate or vote for yourself.
  2. You can only nominate/vote for one user.
  3. You have to be on the wiki for at least a month to be nominated.
  4. You have to have at least 50 mainspace edits to vote, as to stop brand new people from voting.
  5. No canvassing/advertising, obviously. If you are caught canvassing/advertising, you will be removed from nomination.
  6. Once you have won an award, you cannot be nominated again until one month after you received the award.
  7. You cannot use another account to vote or nominate.
  8. If you don't want to be nominated, please put your name at the top of the voting page.
  9. When someone decides to nominate, they have to provide valid points for their decision.
  10. In the event of a tie, the voting is extended for another 3 days after the 10th day of the month.
