General rules

General rules[]

  • Each user is responsible for maintaining their own vote. Once the vote has concluded, it must be moved to the appropriate archive as soon as possible.
  • If you want to propose and/or vote, you must have at least 100 mainspace edits (or 100 posts for Discussions based votes) and have been a member of the community for a month.
  • You may only have one proposal at a time.
  • Before making a proposal, please make sure to check the archives just in case a similar proposal wasn't made earlier. Any duplicate proposals will be removed.
  • If you wish to overturn a previous proposal, you must consult with an admin first and they will decide if the proposal will be allowed back.
  • Proposals must align with Fandom's Terms of Use.
  • If any user is caught doing the following, their request/vote will automatically be disqualified and they may face further consequences such as a block or being banned.
    • You cannot encourage or pressure others to support or oppose a vote.
    • You may not tell people how to vote or make attempts to flood votes.
    • You cannot use sockpuppets to vote.
    • You cannot edit others' votes.

General votes[]

  • General votes are open for 3 days in UTC time zone. In the event of a tie, voting will be extended for another 2 days, also in UTC time zone. If still tied, the vote will be placed into deadlock and an admin will file a casting vote to determine the outcome.
  • On proposals where you can choose between two mutually exclusive options, you can't oppose one of the options if you support the other option.

Image and tabber votes[]

  • Image and tabber votes are open for 24 hours in UTC time zone. In the event of a tie, voting will be extended for another 24 hours, also in UTC time zone. If still tied, the vote will be placed into deadlock and an admin will file a casting vote to determine the outcome.
  • Only character infobox images are required to have a proposal before being changed. If a character is new, any image can be used in their infobox until a vote takes place to change it.
  • Only one infobox image proposal may be active for an individual character at any given time. Multiple simultaneous proposals for the same character are not allowed.
  • If you choose to make a proposal for a non-character infobox images and it's successful, a proposal would be required every time you want to change the infobox image for that particular page.
  • Similarly, if users want to add, replace, or remove any tabber images, you must make a vote regardless if the infobox is a character or not.
  • You do not need to make a proposal if you simply want to upload a higher quality version of the current infobox image, or cropping it.
  • Please consider these factors before proposing an infobox image change:
    • For character infobox images, please take note of the lighting, the angles of their shot, if there's any distracting factors in the background (such as other characters).
    • For more recent images, make sure you take the best possible quality image.
    • Although not essential, it would be helpful to provide the current infobox image with the one you are proposing in your vote so users can compare the images without having to look back at the article before casting their vote.
    • Please propose infobox images that are of high quality. If the shot of a character or non-character image has not yet been uploaded to the wiki, make sure you get the image you want to propose from HD previews of the episode.
    • Make sure there are no watermarks, whether official or not, for your proposed image.
  • Above all, please remember to align with the rest of the wiki's image policy at all times.

Countdown templates[]

  • For the first countdown (3 days or 24 hours for image/tabber votes) please use this template.
  • If a vote is a tie, please add a second countdown (2 days or 24 hours for image/tabber votes) and use this template.
  • If a vote is still a tie, use this template and wait until a casting vote from any admin.

(Define how the wiki treats speculatory information) by Ecto lad778[]

Currently the general baseline of things is said to be concrete answers or nothing, however when you actually look at it the wikis standards are inconsistent when it comes to this thing and appears to be based of personal prefference.

In terms of assumed information, the wiki still considers the Land of Eternal Dawn as a realm despite its status as a realm being called into question under the basis that the presents of a portal gate was enough to decide this despite the location of a gate not being enough to deduce this yet in when it came to the names of the unknown elemental alliance member is was decided to hold of on it despite the name Benton clearly being form bentonite clay meaning it was referring to the Master of Earth and held of for Leo despite there being no one else in the panel that could have taken that name at the time.

We consider Oni from Duskfall to be the same as the original series despite them sharing next to no similarities with our current one under the vague noting they could be a subspecies despite there being no official source suggesting this (while there is a mural in S8 with Oni that look similar, mural depictions aren't nessecarily accurate as seen with the Battle for All Time and the Source Dragon depictions in DRS1 and S2) yet we held off on listing Thunderfang being the source of the F5's elements despite it being all but said in the clip from S3 episode 3 we got.

There is no real consistency, so i have two propositions for this going forward. A) Operate on what can be reasonably deduced, ie: the same way we consider the Duskfall Oni the same. B) Concrete answers or nothing, ie: we refrained form Listing the elements from balance during source week until Glen confirmed it even though there was no other source left or not considering the Arc Dragon of Life a mortal avatar based off just the set name.

Operate on what can be reasonably deduced.
