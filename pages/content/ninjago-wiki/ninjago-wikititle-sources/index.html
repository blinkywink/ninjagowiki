The following is a list of sources for the exact spelling/capitalization/punctuation of names that are spoken in the show (or written in all-caps text). This is because it would be excessive to list these on the pages themselves, but it is useful to record them somewhere to signify that they are not conjectural.

Pages whose names come from the credits, set descriptions, or descriptions that are already on the pages themselves are not listed here because they are already easily accessible sources and in the former two cases are often cited on pages. If a page is not listed here and does not have a source on its own page, its lowercase is most likely conjectural.
