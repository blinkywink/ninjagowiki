The Ninjago Wiki Discussions are part of the Ninjago Wiki. It's the ultimate social center where users can have a blast with other Ninjago fans. Whether you've been a lifetime fan since it first started, you're somewhere in between, or have just started with the franchise, this is the place for you!

Before you begin contributing to our discussions, please make sure to familiarize yourself with our guidelines to ensure a smooth and healthy community.

Guidelines

Guidelines[]

1. Posts that are rude, abusive, make other users uncomfortable, or made in bad faith in any way are not allowed.

1a. Posts that are spam, contain swearing (including censors, shortenings, and abbreviations), or made with generative AI (including discussion about AI) are not allowed.

2. Posts that are off-topic are forbidden. The only off-topic threads, as well as information about what is and isn’t considered “off-topic” can be found below.

A post is considered off-topic when it contains very little to no Ninjago content. Generally, consider if what you are about to post is substantial to or will bring about meaningful conversation about Ninjago. Crossover content is allowed, with the following restrictions:
  • For crossover posts:
    • Content must primarily focus on Ninjago.
  • For crossover polls:
    • If each option is an individual character/item/location (e.g. “who would win”), the poll can have as little as two options, as long as the poll is at least 50% Ninjago.
    • If each option is a group of characters/items/locations (three or more is considered a group), the poll can have as little as four options, as long as the poll is at least 50% Ninjago.
    • The polls cannot be made with the clear winner being the non-Ninjago option(s) or made to spark conversation solely about the non-Ninjago option(s).
In the off-topic threads, make sure comments are kept appropriate for the Ninjago Wiki. In general, think "would this be in Ninjago". There is some leeway in this so ask a Discussions Staff member or Admin if you have any questions. The official off-topic posts are:
  • NinjaFanChat/General Off-topic (venting– sharing personal gripes– and discussion regarding AI is only permitted in this thread, in addition to censored swears).
  • Creative Media
  • Meme Spam
  • Roleplay

3. Limit yourself to making a total of three posts/polls a day. However, you are free to comment on other posts that have already been made.

3a. While you can make as many comments as you wish, avoid making more than three comments in a row.

4. Before making a post, please check the stream to make sure the post you're about to write is not extremely similar to/a duplicate of another previous post made very recently.

Please use these to avoid duplicate posts:
  • Spoilers Discussion
  • Non-Spoiler Episode Discussion
  • Ninjago Collecting
  • Ninjago Wiki Discord Server

5. Do not comment on a post that is older than one month. Instead, start a new one.

6. If your post gets deleted, do not remake it without permission from a moderator or administrator. If you want to contest a post deletion, talk to a Discussions Staffer on their talk page or the Ask Discussions Staff thread.

7. Avoid political discussions unless they are directly related to the structure of Ninjago and make no references to real-life politics.

8. Don't give out your personal information. This includes real names, addresses, ages, email addresses, passwords, and personal images of yourself. This is especially important if venting (sharing personal gripes), which is only permitted in the general off-topic thread.

8a. Additionally, do not ask other people for their personal information. This includes questions such as “are you older or younger than Ninjago?” or “what location in Ninjago is most similar to your area?”

9. Use one account only. Sockpuppets are not allowed.

10. When pulling content from other people, please make sure to properly credit the creator. Don't plagiarize and claim something is yours when it's someone else's work.

11. Do not create posts or comments in languages other than English, as this is an English wiki. If you wish to discuss things in other languages, you may do so on a respective non-English wiki.


Users who break the rules may have their posts locked or deleted and may be subject to the blocking policy. If you see a user break one of the rules, you are welcome to report the comment(s)/post(s). A Discussions Staffer or Admin will then look at the situation and determine if any action is needed. Alternatively, you can contact a Discussions Staffer or Admin via their talk pages or the Ask D-Staff thread.

Prohibited posts[]

  • Asking "Where I can watch Ninjago?" You can watch Ninjago On-Demand (depending on your service provider), the Cartoon Network website and app, Amazon Prime, Teletoon, Nickelodeon, Tubi, VUDU, LEGO, Hulu, Apple TV, Netflix, HBO Max, Warner Bros, and other approved sources.
    • Posts with links to websites, folders or files, or any other means of pirating is forbidden.
  • Role-playing.
  • Self-promotions/advertising including but not limited to posts that ask for subscribers or link to personal servers, websites, and videos. Links are allowed as long as they are used in the context of the discussions or to any of our affiliated partners.
  • Leaked photos of unreleased Ninjago sets, minifigures, or any other potential leaks. If you are unsure if something is a leak, ping a moderator without posting the leak in question.
  • Posts or comments that reference drugs or alcohol are prohibited.
  • Posts made for the purpose of venting (sharing personal gripes) are prohibited. However, venting comments are allowed within the general off-topic post.
  • Posts using or discussion about generative AI; however, discussion about AI is permitted in the off-topic megathread.

Spoilers[]

A spoiler is defined by the wiki as the 72-hour period after new content, such as new episodes, is released. Because of the way spoilers can be seen in discussions, we will try to minimize the chances of users who don't want to be spoiled. During this time, no posts may be created regarding the newly released content.

  • All spoilers must be posted in the Spoilers Discussion thread.
  • Even after the 72-hour spoiler ban is lifted, it is encouraged to add a buffer (filler images or blank space before the main content) and mark the title with a spoiler notice to posts regarding newly-released content for a day or two.
  • Please make sure to pay attention to our leak policy when dealing with leaked information.

Categories

Categories[]

Discussion posts are divided and organized into seven categories. Please make sure to properly categorize your post so it will be easier to access your post.

Category name Description
General Any posts which pertain to multiple themes or don't fit with a theme.
Ninjago Wiki News Any posts with news pertaining to the Ninjago Wiki or any Ninjago news.
Fun and Games Any posts containing games such as "Corrupt a Wish," "Last Letter, First Letter (An Entertaining Game)" and polls.
Theories and Rumors Any posts with theories someone might have or a potential gossip found in the Ninjago community.
Question and Answers Any posts with questions users have about the wiki or the Ninjago theme.
Memes Any memes about Ninjago.
Creative Corner Any fanart or fan-fiction of your own or you stumbled upon.
Chima Discussions Any posts primarily about Legends of Chima.
