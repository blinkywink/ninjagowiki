The Ninjago Wiki is the largest source for all things about LEGO's Ninjago line.

History

History[]

2010[]

On December 28, the Ninjago Wiki was founded by Paifu fan 23.

2011[]

On March 19, Makuta Tarkairadan (now ElishuaJansen) joined the wiki. On June 12, King Joe joined. On September 23, King Joe adopted the wiki and gave Makuta bureaucrat rights. The activity of Ninjago Wiki then started growing. On December 13, Bionclejaller joined and was given admin rights six days later.

2012[]

The activity grew more than ever. Users like Legodude101 and Vector E. Cramp joined over from the LEGO Universe Wiki. Eventually, Legodude was given bureaucrat rights by Makuta. However, on December 4, an incident in chat occurred which caused tensions between Legodude, MisterGryphon, and Meiko. Several people left. On December 19, Legodude demoted himself. SaltNPepper and Vector stayed to help out the wiki.

2016[]

For the past three years, SaltNPepper and Vector ran the wiki. On January 9, Buddermeow was given rollback rights. On March 31, SaltNPepper resigned and gave her bureaucrat rights to Vector. On April 23, Buddermeow was given admin rights.

2017[]

Vector's activity on FANDOM started decreasing and he eventually disappeared after September 7, leaving the wiki with no active bureaucrat.

2018[]

On March 9, Buddermeow requested adoption for the wiki. Her request was accepted on the next day.

2019[]

On January 6, the wiki became part of a Discord server for LEGO wikis.

2020[]

On June 11, Buddermeow announced her resignation, so a vote that lasted a week was made on the next day, with Toa Kopaka Nuva winning. Buddermeow still occasionally edits as a regular user.

2021[]

During this year, many connections were made with the Ninjago community. On February 14, the Ninjago Wiki partnered with the Keepers of Spinjitzu. On July 30, a partnership was reached with V10 Music. On August 19, a partnership was reached with The Fold. On November 22, a partnership was reached with Lachlan Jansen.

2022[]

On November 23, the partnership with Lachlan Jansen was dissolved due to controversies related to his person.

2026[]

The wiki celebrated 15 years of Ninjago. Drawing, writing, and MOC contests were held, and game nights were held on Discord.

List of bureaucrats

List of bureaucrats[]

The bureaucrat (aka Sensei) is seen as the de facto leader of the Ninjago Wiki. They are involved in the decision-making for the community and play an important role in the future of the wiki. Here are the list of bureaucrats the wiki has had in chronological order.

  • Paifu fan23 (founder; December 28, 2010 - Present)
  • King Joe (September 23, 2011 - Present)
  • Makuta Tarkairadan (September 23, 2011 – September 4, 2012)
  • SecretJinx (April 18, 2012 – August 27, 2012)
  • Legodude101 (April 19, 2012 – December 19, 2012)
  • TheGunther (May 11, 2012)
  • SaltNPepper (November 14, 2012 – March 31, 2016)
  • MisterGryphon (November 18, 2012)
  • Vector E. Cramp (March 31, 2016 - Present)
  • Buddermeow (March 10, 2018 – June 19, 2020)
  • Toa Kopaka Nuva (June 19, 2020 – Present)

Paifu fan23 and King Joe are still bureaucrats, but haven't been active since 2012. Vector E. Cramp is also a bureaucrat, but hasn't been active since 2017.

In other languages

In other languages[]

Besides the English version, there are several other wikis which cover LEGO Ninjago, but are in different languages. However, not all of the wikis have the same amount of content as other wikis.

  • Lego Ninjago Wiki (Deutsch)
  • Wiki Ninjago (Español)
  • Encyklopedia LEGO Ninjago (Polski)
  • NinjaGo Wiki (português do Brasil)
  • Ninjago вики (Русский)
  • Ніндзяго вікі (українська)
  • Ninjago-wiki (Magyarul)

In addition, LEGO Ninjago can be found in a plethora of languages if you visit the LEGO.com website or LEGO's YouTube channel.
