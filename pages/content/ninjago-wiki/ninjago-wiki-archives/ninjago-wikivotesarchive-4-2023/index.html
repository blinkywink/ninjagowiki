(Decide if Darkness and Ice actually count for the crystal zombies and Blizzard Warriors) by Samurai viper[]

By looking at the previous edits of the four ninja, you can see that users tried to add Darkness to their element field because they became crystal zombies, but it keeps getting removed because they didn't actually use it. However, I believe there's one fact: None of the crystal zombies use Darkness except for the Fuchsia and Yellow Ninja. Basically, two out of the thirteen notable crystal zombies actually used Darkness to spread out the crystal zombie infection. Therefore, we can't add Darkness for the rice farmers, Mezmo, Killow, Ultra Violet, Ronin, and Wu's chicken since they've never used it, like we can't add Darkness for the ninja just because of that same reason.

I'm also including the Blizzard Warriors in this, because they were corrupted by Ice and one was seen freezing Lloyd's right arm in "An Unlikely Ally."

For the crystal zombies, the status of the vote will decide if Darkness will actually be included in their Element/Essence field for all those who were infected (it will still be included in the users section of Darkness either way). For the Blizzard Warriors, the status of the vote will decide if they actually count as users of Ice.

Remember, the Element/Essence field only includes the ones that the character actually obtains by harnessing it or stealing it. Not elements they used in any way, such as using a separate object or a possession, which I consider to be like using a separate object. The reason for saying that was because another reason Darkness wasn't included for the ninja was because of Garmadon's possession of the Overlord, which are different scenarios.

(Split certain navigation boxes by show) by Samurai viper[]

Recently, users have been putting the content of both Ninjago shows in the navigation boxes. Even though, we never agreed on how we do the navigation boxes when it comes to the shows. It's not because we denied it or anything, it's because we haven't done it yet. And please note that the first user that tried putting the content of the two Ninjago shows together in the navigation box was known for ignoring the season numbering of The Island, Seabound, and Crystalized, despite how many times they were told not to or had their edits reverted. And another user even considered the first season of the new Ninjago series to be "Season 16", which can't be right since the first time we learned that there was going to be a new Ninjago show was when it was confirmed that there wasn't going to be a Season 16.

Normally, wikis don't put all the shows of a franchise in one navigation, such as Tales of Arcadia, The Loud House/The Casagrandes, DC Comics, TMNT, and etc. So why can't we do the same thing with both Ninjago series?

And as far as I can see, this is going to happen either way if the show progresses. Think about it, what it would be like if the entire Ninjago franchise ends up being like Tom and Jerry or Looney Tunes, shows that are known for being from the 1900s and still going on, close to reaching 100 years? Or TMNT, not close it 100, but it does have multiple shows and versions of the character. Additionally, it was once complained that the Characters Navbox was too long just for including the creatures, what do you think that could mean if we add the characters from two shows, and possibly more, into one?

So when it comes to the navigations, I think we should split them up by show, with one example being the episodes. Small ones, such as Elements, will not count, as Elements contain 23 articles while People have hundreds, and still counting. In summary, the navigation boxes that contain nearly 100 articles or more will be divided by show. The Voice Actors template, that will be decided in the comments.

(Allow non-English episodes to be listed on an episode page) by Brick Slash[]

Although this is an English wiki, I think that some exceptions should be made regarding content in other languages. I'm proposing that the policy against non-English content on the wiki be adjusted to exempt videos of Ninjago episodes. It doesn't really hurt to have them listed on the page.

Let's use the "Dream Team" episode as an example. LEGO posted multiple different versions of Dream Team on their YouTube channel. There is a separate video for each language dub of the episode. In the Dream Team article here on the Ninjago Wiki, there is a gallery section labelled "Episode" which is where the English episode is listed. Let's say the Finnish dub of the episode is added to the gallery (and is properly labelled as such to avoid any confusion). It isn't taking away from the experience or doing anything negative. Sure, nobody really speaks Slovak in India, but it isn't doing any harm to the experience of Netflix India. It is only adding to it by providing an alternate version of the episode just in case someone needs to access it, while also not hurting the Netflix experience.

An exemption to the English content only rule allowing non-English episodes to be listed in the "Episode" section on pages alongside their English counterpart could only do good for the wiki by simply providing a point of access to a dubbed episode, while also not disrupting the English nature of this wiki.
