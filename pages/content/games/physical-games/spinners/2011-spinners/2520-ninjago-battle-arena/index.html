Overview

This article is about one of the 2011 sets. For other uses, see Battle arena (disambiguation).

2520 Ninjago Battle Arena is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - pilot episodes set that was released on January 4, 2011. It includes Cole DX, Bonezai, two silver points, a Golden Bone Axe, a Golden Chain, a Silver Spear, two Scythes, a Golden Dark Blade, a Fang Dagger, a chainsaw weapon, a Silver Sword, and a Golden sword, two Character cards, and eight Battle cards.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Battle in the area to win weapons!

Let the battle begin in the LEGO® Ninjago Battle Arena! Place your characters on the spinner, arm them with 14 different weapons and use the battle cards to spin your way to victory! Watch out for the deadly flick and ball shooters! Set includes 2 minifigures with special accessories, 2 spinners, 14 weapons, 2 character cards and 8 battle cards.

  • Includes 2 minifigures: Cole DX and Bonezai
  • Features 14 weapons: 2 scythes, 3 daggers, big golden blade, spiked blade, 3 swords, spear, pair of golden nunchucks, golden bone axe and bone weapon
  • Flick-fire the ball shooters!
  • Use the 2 character cards and 8 battle cards to battle with your friends!
  • Battle Arena measures over 16” (40cm) wide and 5” (12cm) tall[1]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Bonezai's armor isn't included in this set.
  • This is one of the first few battle arenas created for the spinner game.
