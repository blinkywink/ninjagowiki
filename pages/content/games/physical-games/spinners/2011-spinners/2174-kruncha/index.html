Overview

This article is about the spinner set. For the character, see Kruncha. For the character card, see Card 15 - Kruncha (Spinners).

2174 Kruncha is a Ninjago Spinners - pilot episodes set that was released on August 5, 2011. It includes Kruncha, his spinner, the Golden Hatchet Twins, a Silver Bone, a Chained Fang, a Character card, four Battle cards, and three reddish brown bricks: 2 2x1s and 1 2x2 plate.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Battle the spinjitzu masters!

Hard as rock and colder than stone, General Kruncha flattens anything that gets in his way. Spin your way to victory against the Spinjitzu masters and use the 4 battle cards included to influence the outcome. Set includes 1 earth optic spinner, 3 weapons, 1 character card, 4 battle cards and LEGO® bricks.
