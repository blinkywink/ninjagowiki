Overview

This article is about the spinner set. For the character, see Wyplash. For the Character card, see Card 11 - Wyplash (Spinners).

2175 Wyplash is a Ninjago Spinners - pilot episodes set that was released on August 11, 2011. It includes Wyplash, his spinner, the Golden Heli-Chains, a Silver Bone, a Groovy Whip, a Character card, four Battle cards, and three blue bricks: 2 2x1s and 1 2x2 plate.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Battle the spinjitzu masters!

Wyplash wears his head backwards so he’ll always see the enemy approach. Spin your way to victory against the spinjitzu masters with 4 battle cards to influence the outcome. Set includes ice optic spinner, 3 weapons, 1 character card, 4 battle cards and LEGO® bricks.

  • Features Wyplash figure and ice optic spinner
  • Weapons include twin chain whip, whip and bone weapon
  • Includes 1 character card, 4 battle cards and LEGO® bricks
