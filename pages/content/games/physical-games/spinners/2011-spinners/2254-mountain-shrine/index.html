Overview

2254 Mountain Shrine is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu - pilot episodes set that was released on August 5, 2011. It includes Kai DX, his spinner, a training dummy, a Dark Chained Whip, a Double-Bladed Scythe, two Daggers of the Dragon's Breath, the Mountain Shrine, the Sword of Fire, a Character card, and four Battle cards.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Prepare for battle on the secret training grounds of the Mountain Shrine.

At the secret Mountain Shrine, Dragon Ninja Kai is in training to increase his dragon power. Help him get even tougher to protect the dragon sword of fire and treasure from the skeleton army! Spin to victory with the character card, 4 battle cards, and golden dragon spinner.

  • Features 1 skeleton training figure, 2 training axes and a treasure chest
  • Minifigures include Kai DX!
  • Also includes golden dragon spinner, 1 character card, and 4 battle cards
  • Weapons include dragon sword of fire, double scythe, and chain weapon
  • Measures over 5” (12cm) tall and 6” (15cm) wide[1]
Designer's notes

Designer's notes[]

I wanted to give the shrine a more classic, almost castle feel, while keeping the play features for the spinner.[2]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Card 3 - Kai DX is exclusive to this set.
  • The box art incorrectly displays Card 1 - Kai instead of Card 3 - Kai DX.
  • In 2011, this was the cheapest way to get the Sword of Fire.
  • The Kai DX spinner is not exclusive to this set, as it also appears in 2518 Nuckal's ATV.
  • The Mountain Shrine doesn't appear in the show.
