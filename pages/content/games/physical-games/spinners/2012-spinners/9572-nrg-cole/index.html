Overview

9572 NRG Cole is a Ninjago Spinners - Rise of the Snakes set that was released on June 2, 2012. It includes NRG Cole. It comes with a golden Spinner Crown. A dark metal Spear, the NRG Golden Blade, a Nin-Jô, a Character card, four Battle cards, and three bricks.
