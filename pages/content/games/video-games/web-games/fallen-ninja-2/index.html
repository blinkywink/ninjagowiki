Overview

This article is about the web game. For the magazine issue, see Fallen Ninja!.

Fallen Ninja is a network game that aired on the official website for Cartoon Network, in May of 2015. It takes place during the fifth season of the TV series after Morro has claimed the scroll of Airjitzu.

Gameplay

Gameplay[]

At first, you can choose to play as either Kai or Nya. The goal of the game is to get higher and higher while collecting blue, white, and black scrolls. Collecting enough of these scrolls will unlock other ninja such as Jay, Zane, and Cole.

Errors

Errors[]

  • In the short comic strip at the beginning of the game, Morro's name is misspelled as "Moro."
  • The Ninja are wearing their Jungle gi despite having their Deepstone armor at this point in time.
