The Keytana Quest is an online game at LEGO.com.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Help Jay and Kai defeat Unagami by collecting Key-Tanas! Use the Cyber Dragon or Mech Jet and beat the bad guys along the way.[1]

Gameplay

Gameplay[]

The player can choose to play as Kai or Jay. If the player doesn't pick for a few seconds, the game randomly picks for them.

The first phase is a runner's game. The player must collect 15 Key-Tanas in one minute in order to get to phase 2. The player must also watch out for Whack Rats along the way. If a player runs into one, they are temporarily slown down.

In the second phase, the player attacks Unagami using the Blue Dragon or the Mech Jet (depending on whether the player picked Kai or Jay at the beginning) and they must send Unagami's health to zero before time is up to catch him or he will manage to evade capture. Similar to Galaga, phase 2 also has some bad guys, which spawn from the Temple of Madness.

The player has the option to play again as the same character they picked at the beginning once you win or lose the game. To change to Kai or Jay again, the player must refresh the game. Data is also not saved once the player exits, so it will play the tutorial again once they enter the game after exiting.
