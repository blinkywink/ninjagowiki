Spinjitzu Spinball Snake Invasion is an internet game on the LEGO website that involves the ninja taking on the Serpentine.

Gameplay

Gameplay[]

To play, players would click and drag the mouse to adjust different levels of power for launching their spinner across each stage's arena with the objective being to land your spinner in the goalpost to complete the stage. Each stage gives the player a limited number of spins that may be launched, but additional spins can be collected either as powerups on the ground or by defeating Serpentine.

Combat is performed automatically when the player's character gets within a certain range of a Serpentine spinner, deducting health from self and foe alike. Players can increase their strength and defense in combat either by equipping stronger weapons and shields or by boosting themselves by striking specifically colored cushions found throughout each stage (Red for attack, green for defense, purple for speed). However, the boosts are temporary and only last for as long as the stage.

In total, there are eight words with eight stages each for a total of sixty-four stages, excluding secret stages that require the insertion of a code to unlock.
