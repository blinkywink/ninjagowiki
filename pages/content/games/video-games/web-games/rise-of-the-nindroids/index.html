Rise of the Nindroids is an online game on the Cartoon Network website.

Levels

Levels[]

Level 1[]

Sensei Wu is captured by Nindroids and the Ninja chase after him. The Player spawns as a ninja in a forest and is taught gameplay instructions by P.I.X.A.L. as an NPC. The player travels through multiple stages until the player reaches the end of the forest.

Level 2[]

The Player spawns on a street near Ninjago City. The player must play through multiple stages until they reach the city.

Level 3[]

The Player Spawns in Ninjago City and completes multiple stages until they reach Borg Tower.

Level 4[]

The Player spawns in Borg Tower and has to travel through difficult stages with Boobytraps and spikes, Each Stage tests each character's abilities to the limit. In the end, Kai is separated from the Ninja who gets captured in Energy Cages. The Player plays as Kai in a boss battle against a Nindroid Warrior until the player wins and destroys the Nindroid. After this, the Ninja free Wu from his energy cage.
