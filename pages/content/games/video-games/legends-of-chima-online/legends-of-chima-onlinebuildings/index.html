Overview

"You four have merely scratched the surface of your full potential."
This article is a stub. You can help Ninjago Wiki by expanding it.

Buildings are models that can be placed on an Outpost. Construction takes an amount of time that depends on the benefits of the building to complete. Some buildings allow the gaining of currency. Crafting Buildings allow players to make weapons and armor. There are more than a dozen upgradable building types, and they offer minifigures special abilities and powers.

Houses

Houses[]

These produce either studs or bricks after a certain amount of time (shown when the house is highlighted) passes. When it's ready, the player can click the Collect button to collect the items and reset the house's production.

Lion Market[]

Lion Martet is a building that can be built by users level 7 or above. With it, you can sell bricks, clothing, and weapons for studs to other players, which can be viewed in the catalog. If the item you are selling costed gold bricks, you can sell it automatically to the market.

The higher level your market is, the more slots available you have to sell items. Also, if you have a membership, you have more selling slots.
