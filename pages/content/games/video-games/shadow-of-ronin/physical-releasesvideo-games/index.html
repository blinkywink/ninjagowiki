Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In general, Canadian and US releases contain the same disc/cartridge, but have different box art. This is because Canadian releases require both English and French text, whereas US releases lack this restriction. This difference can be spotted on the ESRB rating on the front or back of the box, or on the back of the box where the game description is written.
  • Despite Canada and Latin America having separate releases for other games, they share a release of LEGO Legends of Chima: Laval's Journey. The box art of this release features English, French, and Spanish text on the back of the box, but only accounts for English and French on the ESRB rating.
