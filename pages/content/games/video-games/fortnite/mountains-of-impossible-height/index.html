Overview

“Since ancient times, the hills around this monastery have been populated by a species of highly aggressive predator.”
— Wu to the ninja, "Wasted True Potential"

The Mountains of Impossible Height[1] are an incredibly tall mountain range in Ninjago. The Monastery of Spinjitzu can be found atop one of the mountains' peaks.

History

History[]

Background[]

In ancient times, the mountains were inhabitated by monks, who built high walls to protect themselves from dangerous chickens, following the loss of many monks, the remaining ones adapted, becoming ninja in the progress.[2]

Way of the Ninja[]

Master Wu and Kai scaled the backside of the mountain in order to reach the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

Locations

Locations[]

Monastery of Spinjitzu[]

The Monastery of Spinjitzu is a structure built atop the tallest of the mountains. It originally served as the home of the First Spinjitzu Master, his sons and later as the home of the ninja and P.I.X.A.L. The Monastery is hexagonal in shape, with the main building built out of the three back walls. A tall white wall with red highlights and black shingle roofing protected the other three sides with a heavy wooded door carved out of the center one. Between the wall and the building was a large courtyard that held a retractable training course, which could be activated using a secret button in a dragon statue by the main gate.

Hot tub[]

The Bell of Divinity is one of the Monastery of Spinjitzu's enormous bells[6] located in the mountains. The ninja used it as a hot tub when they got lazy after the Oni's invasion. The bell is made of heavy metal, probably copper and gold. There are many ornaments on it, which also resemble snake scales. At the top of the bell is a large chain. At some point, the bell ceased to be used and was placed in the lower part of the same mountain where the monastery stands. After the Oni's invasion, the ninja supported the bell with wooden supports and attached a wooden platform to give them easier access to the inside of the bell, where they filled the water.

Hangar bay[]

The hangar bay is an underground base P.I.X.A.L. built to serve as a storage for the ninja's mechs and vehicles, located inside the mountain, beneath the monastery. Its main purpose is to act as a garage for the Samurai X Mechs, the Destiny's Bounty, the Land Bounty, the Rock Cycle, and the ATV. It was also built to act as a stationary base of operations for the ninja due to all the previous bases being portable.

Dragon stables[]

The dragon stables are where the ninja's dragons live. It was located on the same mountain as the monastery, which then burned down. Originally, the were four large caves with wooden gates with a horseshoe symbol and had a lantern for each stable. After the Battle of the Second Monastery, the dragon stables were rebuilt in a cave next to Ninjago's portal gate after some of the dragons that were captured by the Claws of Imperium chose to stay at the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

Garden/sakura trees[]

At the same mountain as the monastery is a sakura tree on rocky surface, next to is occasionally placed a wooden table on which the ninja and Wu ate fruit while training. The sakura tree was also originally located close to the monastery wall, but after the reconstruction there was planted a new one a little lower on the mountain. Another sakura tree is also located on a smaller mountain with stairs, a short walk from the stone bridge.

Some years after the reconstruction of the monastery,[7] this place which was formerly only a rocky surface, became a little grassland similar to a garden which stretched along the stairs to the front of the monastery, with grass, colorful flowers and vegetation, and the sakura tree which became much more leafy with purple leaves and flowers instead of pink. There is also a drawn dirt path crossing the grass going from the stairs to the tree.

Stairs[]

On two tall mountains, stairs were once carved into the rock, leading from the rice fields below the mountains to the top, to the monastery. At the top of the first mountain there is a rocky bridge that can be used to get to the next stairs on the tallest mountain. Originally, there were one, smaller staircase that led to the Dragon stables, but they are not used in present.

Trash bins[]

  • Original trash bins
  • Modern trash bins
1/2

At the outer wall of the monastery there were several trash bins, where homeless children were often rummaged through. They were relatively modern metal lockable trash bins for their time. Wu there met his first student, Morro. Later the trash bins were replaced by more modern ones.

Small grassland[]

At the outer wall of the monastery, on the other side as the trash bins, was a small grassland with several bushes. At this point, Wu's katana was lost and Garmadon was bitten by the Great Devourer. During Morro's training in the monastery, this grassland was still in front of the monastery, but it was later removed or wiped out because only the rocks are currently in the same place. Wu also fought the Time Twins here.

After the Merge this small grassland reappeared now part of the grassland surrounding the monastery.

Small forest[]

During Lilly's training, there was a small forest near the monastery with many shrubs and bamboos. Wu meditated with Lilly in this forest. Although it seems that it is possible to see a monastery behind the forest, it is unknown how this is possible, although the forest could be located on a neighboring mountain.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Mountains of Impossible Height are very high peaks in Ninjago. They cover a relatively large area and most of them are covered with snow and are located above the clouds. On one of the mountains is the Monastery of Spinjitzu, which leads to the stairs that connect the two mountains with a rocky bridge. The mountains are made of gray rock, although sometimes it seems brown.

Geography

Geography[]

The Mountains of Impossible Height are located south of Ninjago City.[14] Below the stairs on the mountains are rice fields,[15] while on the other side, where the gate to the hangar bay is, there is desert land.[16] Before the rebuilt of the monastery there was rocky soil under the stairs with many coniferous trees, where the Reversal Time Blade was found.[17] The Wildwood Forest and the Forest of Tranquility are also nearby.[18]
