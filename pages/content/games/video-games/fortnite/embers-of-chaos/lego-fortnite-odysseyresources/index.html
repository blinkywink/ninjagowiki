Bamboo

Bamboo[]

Bamboo is a 4 Resource in LEGO Fortnite Odyssey.

Official description[]

A ninja's favorite Wood.[1]
A Wooden crafting material found on Ninjago Island. Can be Honed at a Ritual Altar.[2]
The only panda food that can be crafted into ninja weapons.[2]

Generation[]

Bamboo naturally generates in bamboo forests on Ninjago Island and can also be found in certain chests.
