Overview

This article is about the video game. For other uses, see Battle arena (disambiguation).

LEGO Battle Arena is an official Ninjago-themed minigame created within Fortnite in collaboration between Epic Games and LEGO.

Official description

Official description[]

Will you be the next Spinjitzu master? Enroll in special training at the Dojo with Lloyd and the Masters of Spinjitzu. Compete against other trainees, using your new abilities to knock them off the arena edge without falling yourself. Be careful as the ground gives way around you! Players knocked off three times are eliminated and the last player standing/spinning wins![1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The Fortnite island code for this game is "0312-7365-5770."
  • This game was published by LEGO under the Fortnite username "THELEGOGROUP."
  • It was given an ESRB rating of E10+ for "Fantasy Violence."
  • The game reuses various musical tracks originally from LEGO Brawls.
  • Lloyd spitting out the tea in the final cutscene is a reference to the tea burning joke in Episode 3.
  • It seems that Lloyd's texts along with the animation in the first cutscene are random.
    • Some of the possible texts variants are:
      • Adaptability is the essence of true skill.
      • Eyes open, yet mind focused-awareness is key to victory!
      • Grab Powerups to unleash devastating attacks!
      • In the dojo and beyond, humility is strenght.
      • Learn to fall gracefully; rise stronger.
      • Precision is the key to effective Spinjitzu technique.
      • Spinjitzu mastery begins with respect for the basics.
      • Strive for self-improvement, not just victory.
      • The power of Spinjitzu is inside all of us.
      • The true warrior knows when not to fight.
      • Train every day to become a true Spinjitzu Master!
      • Use Air Dash to save yourself from an untimely defeat!
  • You have two skills in the game:
    • Spinjitzu (E)
    • Dash (Space)
  • Lloyd is wearing a Merge suit, but instead of a mask, he has a headband.
  • The faces on the leaderboards are the same decals used on the ninja's faces for the Lego version of the Djinn Blades.
  • The symbols on the ground of the monastery and the monastery map arena represent the Source Dragons.
