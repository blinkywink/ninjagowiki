This topic is considered non-canon because it is set in a world that extends beyond the Ninjago franchise.

Fortnite is a video game released developed and published by Epic Games. Since 2023, LEGO has begun collaborating with Epic Games, which resulted in Ninjago being featured in different game modes, including LEGO Battle Arena, LEGO Fortnite Odyssey and Ninjago Battle Games.

Ninjago: Rise of the Ninja[]

The LEGO Ninjago media franchise crossed into LEGO Fortnite on December 11th 2025, with the release of a Ninjago-themed LEGO Pass and a story campaign for LEGO Fortnite Odyssey. Various other Ninjago-themed items were released in the Item Shop.
