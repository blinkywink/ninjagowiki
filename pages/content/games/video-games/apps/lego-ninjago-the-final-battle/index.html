LEGO Ninjago: The Final Battle is an online game and app that was released in March 2013. The app cannot be launched on devices running iOS 11+.

App description

App description[]

The destiny of LEGO Ninjago rests in your hands! Unleash the incredible power of elemental weapons and blast enemies as Lloyd, Jay, Cole, Zane or Kai restore peace to save the day! - Spinjitzu your way through 6 iconic levels from the LEGO Ninjago world - Spin, slash, stomp and smash through wave upon wave of enemies - Mix up and max out your slick ninja skills and weapons - Activate all 4 elemental weapons to play as the Golden Ninja - Play well and become the all-powerful Spinjitzu Master Sensei Wu.

Characters

Characters[]

  • Lloyd (starter character)
  • Kai (unlocked after getting the Fire element)
  • Jay (unlocked after getting the Lightning element)
  • Zane (unlocked after getting the Ice element)
  • Cole (unlocked after getting the Earth element)
  • Golden Lloyd (getting the Fire, Lightning, Ice and Earth elements will summon a bell and touching the bell will make you play as Golden Lloyd temporarily until you turn back into Lloyd)
  • Wu (after getting enough elements, the scroll bar will fill and you will play as Sensei Wu temporarily until you turn back into Lloyd)
