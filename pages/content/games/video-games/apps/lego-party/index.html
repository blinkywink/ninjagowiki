Overview

LEGO Party! is a LEGO party game developed by Australian studio SMG Studio. It was released on September 30, 2025 by Fictions. Many characters from Ninjago appear in this game.

Console description[]

LEGO® Party! is a 4-player party game that’s built different!

Compete against your friends in wacky Challenge Zones and 60 hilarious minigames from across your favorite LEGO sets like LEGO Pirates, LEGO Space, LEGO NINJAGO® and more.

Join your friends online no matter their platform, or get together for a LEGO Party game night. With multiple game modes and tons of minifigures to unlock, you’ll have all the bricks to build the ultimate party!

Challenge players near or far, customize your character and compete in a variety of awesome minigames to get as many Golden Bricks as you can, by any means necessary! But beware, watch out for monsters, traps and flying roast turkeys on your way to become the next star of LEGO Party!

Play your way: Bring your friends and enjoy the mayhem in couch multiplayer or with cross-platform online multiplayer for up to 4 players. Whether it's competing for the perfect score in Rocketball, practicing against that pesky kraken in Kraken Up or boogieing down in Dance Off, you can enjoy LEGO Party! together or solo!

Go for the gold: In LEGO Party! you have one goal: to become the player with the most Golden Bricks! Use power-ups to gain the advantage and set up traps to sabotage your opponents across a range of customizable LEGO themed Challenge Zones.

Everything is awesome: Put your speed, wits, and LEGO expertise to the test across a variety of crazy minigames. Race to make dinner for aliens, run through collapsing tombs and skid round the track in rally races and so much more across 60 different wildly fun minigames.

You do you: With over 1 billion minifigure combinations from iconic LEGO sets, you can go with a classic look or design your own unique and outlandish minifigure. Show off your creation at the next party!

Minigames

Minigames[]

While LEGO Party! has 60 minigames, only 2 of them are based on the world of Ninjago.

NINJAGO® Training[]

In NINJAGO® Training, all 4 contestants throw katanas at a spinning circle to gain the most points. Sensei Wu (Core) also makes a cameo appearance in this minigame.

Stop, Drop and Side Scroll[]

Sporting the same name as Season 12 episode 12, Stop, Drop, and Side Scroll takes place within the confines of Prime Empires digital world, or so the hosts would like you to believe, where contestants go through numerous obstacles to be the first to reach the finish.

Playable Ninjago characters

Playable Ninjago characters[]

There are 43 playable Ninjago characters in LEGO Party! 6 are available from the start, 9 are available for "Ninjago zone" xp unlocks, 11 are available for player xp unlocks, and 16 are available in the shop. One is also unlocked by inputting the secret code, left left left up up up left right down down, on the main menu screen.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • When the Ice Behemoth appears on the board, there is a chance for one of the narrators, Ted, to spoil that "Zane turns evil" in Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu.
  • Wu is accidentally labeled as "Wu Sensei" in the Ninjago zone xp unlocks.
  • The only complete collection of playable Ninja Gi is the Fugitive line.
  • All Ninjago characters are strictly based on their real life minifigure counterpart and not the show, leading to Kai in his deepstone suit with scars, which was never shown in the show.
  • Clutch Powers is a playable character and also appears in the background of the Ninjago zone board, being chased by a scorpion.
  • The game seems to heavily favor references to the 11-minute era of Ninjago, particularly Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu and Crystalized.
  • The minigame, "Rock Paper Claw", is not a reference to Ninjago, despite the ninja saying this exact phrase in Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu
  • The minigame, "Quick Bricks", features Lloyd (Fugitive) in the background.
