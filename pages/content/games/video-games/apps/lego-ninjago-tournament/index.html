LEGO Ninjago: Tournament (also known as LEGO Ninjago: Tournament of Elements[1]) is a fighting video game that was released on iOS and Android. It is based on the fourth season of Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu. The game is no longer available for download due to no longer being updated and therefore not compatible with recent versions of Android and iOS.

Gameplay

Gameplay[]

The game is made up of three levels:

Dojo[]

This is the tutorial area of the game. Sensei Wu teaches the player how to move, attack, jump, jump slam, block, dodge, grab and punch, and use the special move (in this case, Spinjitzu). Upon succeeding, a practice test takes place.

Chen's Island Arena[]

This is where the main game takes place. Groups of enemies attack the players with meelee weapons, bows, and arrows, or bombs. The more waves completed, the more opponents the player has to defeat before the next wave starts.

After every several waves comes a boss. Bosses have their own special abilities, such as Karlof causing tremors and Ash disappearing and reappearing behind the player's back.

Events include the Titanium Dragon (freeze anyone who gets in contact with its Ice ball), Roto Jet (drops missiles and boxes, which drop studs and sometimes hearts when broken), Condrai Crushers (drop off Anacondrai Cultists), and training equipment (with punching bags providing studs and sometimes hearts when broken and spikes dealing damage).

Some enemy factions have distinctive abilities: the Anacondrai Cultists have more health while their Anacondrai form, like the bosses, cannot be carried. The Nindroids, when spawning, start out invisible before revealing themselves.

Additionally, either side contains a gong, which can immediately defeat the opponent—even bosses—upon contact. With this, the player can throw the enemies onto the gong to speed up gameplay.

Draw

Draw[]

The player is allowed one free draw daily, however, they can earn up to three draws by completing daily tasks. The rewards include powerups and studs.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Upon upgrading any Original, DX, ZX, or Zukin Ninja to level 5, they wield their respective Obsidian Weapons.
    • The only exception is that Lloyd Garmadon gains Kai's Obsidian Sword.
    • Despite Zane having Obsidian Sais, he still is able to shoot silver shurikens.
  • Despite never being transformed, Clouse has an Anacondrai variant.
  • Ronin is one of the Bosses.
    • He is the only boss that is unplayable.
  • Techno Wu's Spinjitzu is the same color as Destruction Spinjitzu.
  • In Bulgaria, Tox takes place of Chamille.
  • This is the only Ninjago game by TT Games to feature Samukai (since he only appeared in 16-bit in LEGO Battles: Ninjago).
  • Because this is a 32-bit app, it is no longer playable on iOS 11+.
  • This game was discontinued when Season 5 was in production
  • On LEGO Ninjago: Rebooted, a promo for this game was available prior to this game's release
  • If you look closely at the Pink Zane image, you can see it has his DX dragon printed on.
  • It reuses models, animations, game mechanics and sounds from the LEGO Ninjago: Nindroids game that was released on the 3DS and PS Vita.
