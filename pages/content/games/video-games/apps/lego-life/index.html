Overview

LEGO Life was an app and magazine series for young LEGO fans, featuring Ninjago as one of its themes. An archive of the magazine issues can be found here.

History

History[]

The "Meet the Ninja" series, consisting of six short videos in which the ninja introduced themselves to the audience, was released on the app in 2017.

The official trailer for the Fire Chapter of Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu was released on June 14, 2019, and the official Ice Chapter trailer was released on September 3, 2019.

The Season 13 blooper reel was released on October 14, 2020.

In May 2022, the story Amber Spiral was released on the LEGO Life app and LEGO.com.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In May 2021, Tommy Andreasen released a character interview with Nya that he stated had been released on LEGO Life a few weeks earlier.[1] However, it never appeared on the app.
