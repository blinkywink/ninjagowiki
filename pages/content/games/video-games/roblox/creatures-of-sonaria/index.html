Overview

Take my form, walk the path of light or darkness, the choice is yours. Let the balance of fate decide what you'll become. Avenge my children, or spare the crystal eggs. The choice is yours.”

External links
Creatures of Sonaria is an online multiplayer game developed by Sonar Studios on the Roblox platform. In the game, players can control and interact with a variety of unique creatures in an open-world environment. The gameplay focuses on exploration, survival, and evolution, allowing creatures to grow, adapt, and interact with both the environment and other players.

On June 6, 2025, the official Creatures of Sonaria Discord server announced that the studio has collaborated with The LEGO Group to introduce a limited-time update to the game. This update will be playable for several weeks (June 6th 2025 1:40 PDT - July 18 8:30 PDT) and serves as a promotional event for Monstrosity.

Official description

Official description[]

Hey Sonarians! 🔔

We are thrilled to announce our collaborative partnership with The LEGO® Group to bring you all a one of a kind in-game experience! In our update releasing today you will be able to explore the Land of monsters, a strange realm filled with shadows, stories and secrets from the new series LEGO® Ninjago Legends: Monstrosity!

Test your fate with the new Guardian Dragon questline!

Having witnessed the monster shatter her eggs, the Guardian Dragon stands poised between vengeance and mercy, the Guardian Dragon will either destroy those who took her children from her, or she will choose the path of a Guardian. Will you falter and fall to corruption, or reach peace?

  • Find the Guardian Dragon NPC, speak to her as she recounts her story and accept her quest!
  • Destroy or Heal the crystals found within the Land of Monsters, doing so will shift your alignment between Good or Evil!
  • Transform into a Good or Evil Dragon by completing the quest, earning a unique skin for your Guardian Dragon Creature Slot!
  • Permanently Unlock the Guardian Dragon Species!

This is a Limited Time Collaboration, it's only here for a few weeks, don't miss it!
