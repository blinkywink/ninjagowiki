Blade Ball is an online multiplayer game developed by Wiggity and Gamefam Studios on the Roblox platform.

On June 7, 2025, it was announced that the game had collaborated with The LEGO Group to introduce a limited-time update to the game to promote Ninjago Legends: Monstrosity.
