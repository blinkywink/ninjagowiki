Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The event shows an adult-stage Riyu and the Source Dragon of Energy. It's still unclear if these are the actual designs for the 2 dragons, as Riyu has yet to grow to adulthood and the Source Dragon of Energy was never directly shown without energy shields or lights that misled the sight.
    • The event also shows the Icon of the Source Dragon of Energy, which is made to resemble a double-bladed spear.
