LEGO Dimensions is a video game released on September 27, 2015. It is a toys-to-life game in the style of Skylanders and Disney Infinity, thus, it is a crossover game, with Ninjago being one of the franchises represented in it. Ninjago is one of the worlds in the LEGO Multiverse. Multiple Ninjago-themed expansion packs were released when the game was launch. Batman, Gandalf, and Wyldstyle are the focus characters, Lord Vortech served as the main antagonist, and the Vortech Minions and villains from various worlds were the villainous factions of the video game.

Storyline

Storyline[]

There is an ancient planet at the center of the LEGO Multiverse inhabited by an evil mastermind, Lord Vortech. It is said that he who controls the Foundational Elements that this planet is built upon, controls all of the Multiverse. Lord Vortech has vowed to be that ruler, summoning characters from a variety of LEGO worlds to help him find these building bricks of LEGO civilization. Some have agreed. Others have rebelled. And only the combined powers of the greatest LEGO heroes can stop him.

When a mysterious and powerful vortex suddenly appears in various LEGO worlds, different characters from DC Comics, The Lord of the Rings, and The LEGO Movie are swept away. To save their friends, Batman, Gandalf, and Wyldstyle bravely jump into the vortex together. As they journey to locations beyond their wildest imaginations in search of their friends, they soon realize that Lord Vortech is summoning villains from across different LEGO worlds to help him gain control. As his power grows, worlds mix, unexpected characters meet and all boundaries are broken. Our heroes must travel through space and time to rescue their friends before the vortexes destroy all of LEGO humanity.

Gameplay

Gameplay[]

Idle[]

All characters have one or more animations when they are not walking, swimming, flying, attacking, or interacting with objects or vehicles. The animations consist of random actions that give the character more of a personality than just a playable figure, meant exclusively for entertainment.

  • Jay
    • Puts nunchucks down and lifts hood to eat an apple.
    • Swings nunchucks above head in a helicopter-like motion that lifts him off the ground for a few seconds.
  • Zane
    • Punches shurikens forward in rapid motions for a few moments.
    • Does back flip.
    • Makes random, robot-like movements with body in one motion.
  • Nya
    • Turns to the left and right while pointing katanas outwards.
    • Rests katanas on the ground and lowers head.
    • Uses right katana to scratch back.
  • Kai
    • Slices sword through air in several movements, then positions it, ready to strike.
    • Does a back flip.
  • Cole
    • Looks around cautiously.
    • Spins scythe behind back, while tapping foot, then continues to spin while reading a book.
  • Wu
    • Takes out cup of tea, sips it, then puts it away
    • Jumps, spins, and twirls staff in a motion of fighting, then runs hand through beard.

Quick Speed[]

All Ninjago characters run slightly faster than other characters.

Melee[]

All characters have the ability to hit enemies, breakable objects, and other players at short distances with their corresponding weapon. Graphic effects will appear (if the option is enabled) when a melee attack is made.

  • Kai (Sword of Fire; Red/Orange/Purple streaks and small orange fireballs appear)
  • Jay (Nunchucks of Lightning; Electricity graphics and shows skeletons of enemies)
  • Zane (Shurikens of Ice; Snow flakes and white streaks)
  • Cole (Scythe of Quakes; Red/Orange streaks)
  • Lloyd (Golden Swords; Gold streaks and small gold stars)
  • Nya (Black Katanas; Red streaks)
  • Sensei Wu (Staff; Gold streaks)
  • Lloyd (Gold Katanas; Gold streaks)

Special Melee Attack[]

All characters have a melee attack that kills an enemy instantly. It has much more animation than the set of standard attacks, used for entertainment and efficiency (though, the animation often lasts just as long as it would take to kill the enemy normally). A player can use the special attack by pressing a single button or a combination of buttons simultaneously (most likely the "interact" button).

  • Zane
    • Throws shurikens at the same time in a jumping movement.
    • Jumps, doing a flip, and throws shurikens at the same time.
  • Jay
    • Kicks enemy into the air, jumps up, and strikes the enemy down with nunchucks.
    • Hits enemy slightly off the ground, jumping up to meet them, and bats the enemy around.
  • Kai
    • Bats enemy into the air, jumps up, and strikes the enemy down with sword.
    • Hits enemy's legs, flipping them into the air, then strikes them down mid-fall.
  • Cole
    • Jumps up and spins scythe around in one motion, and strikes the enemy while falling.
    • Picks enemy up with scythe, spins them around, and throws them down.
  • Nya
    • Balances enemy on helmet, and hits the enemy off with swords.
    • Steps back, runs up to enemy, hits them into the air, and lets them land on poised katanas.
  • Lloyd
    • Hits enemy across chest twice, then throws enemy over head, backwards with swords.
    • Does backflip, and then trikes enemy with both swords across chest.
  • Wu
    • Thrusts staff into enemy, jumps onto one end, and leverages the enemy into the air with a back flip, then proceeds to pick up staff.
    • Stabs and smacks enemy several times with free hand, paralyzing them, then hits them with staff.

Ranged[]

Only certain characters and vehicles have the ability to hit multiple enemies, breakable objects, and other players/vehicles at great lengths by throwing or shooting a weapon. The player is also able to select multiple targets (nearby or far off) to hit in one action, if they are using a character's ranged abilities (vehicles don't have the ability). If a character's ranged attack is being used on an enemy, other character, or object that cannot be destroyed, the thrown or shot weapon bounces in a random direction or just does not do any damage.

Ground Slam[]

All characters have the ability to jump and hit the ground to create surrounding damage to enemies, breakable objects, and other players in a small area. When each of the Ninjago characters perform this ability, a circle appears where the ground was hit, creating visual effects of each respective element around it and dragon symbol (or the symbol that represents that character, colored respectively) in the center (both fade after a few seconds).

Activate Batarang Switches[]

Only certain characters have the ability to use their "ranged selection" ability (mentioned above) to target and activate Batarang Switches to solve puzzles. Once the player has targeted the switch in the same way as targeting objects, the throw-able weapon will hit the switch and spin it for a few seconds. The activated switch will then reveal/start another mechanism or just reveal an object or platform.

  • Zane

Stealth[]

All Ninjago characters have the ability to turn partially invisible to sneak past security cameras to solve puzzles (often by opening gates). The cameras give off red beams when a visible player is nearby, and green when no player is nearby or a player is using stealth. Usually when the beams are green, a gate will open below the cameras, allowing access to whatever is behind it.

Acrobatics[]

All Ninjago characters have the ability to double jump, swing on acrobatic poles, and scale acrobatic walls (colored pink, purple, and blue). The double jump ability allows the player to jump twice (the second time while in the air) to reach platforms, climbing grips, and acrobatic poles twice as high as regular jumps. Acrobats are also able to swing continuously on acrobatic poles (either clockwise or counterclockwise), which they can then jump/swing from to reach other poles or platforms. Acrobatic walls are found in pairs that allow the player to jump back and forth from their inner sides to reach higher platforms.

Laser Deflection[]

All Ninjago characters (other than Master Wu) have the ability to deflect steady red lasers to burn objects (that cannot otherwise be destroyed) and hurt enemies. The player must stand on a small, circular platform with the laser shooting over it, which will allow them to turn that laser (by turning themselves) in any desired direction.

Spinjitzu[]

All Ninjago characters have the ability to perform Spinjitzu to destroy objects, enemies, and other players that they approach, by lifting them up, spinning them above the player, and then tossing. The amount of time a player is able to use Spinjitzu is determined by the Spinjitzu bar that circles a character's icon (it will fill up and blink when completely full). The color and other graphical details of the Spinjitzu tornado that appears depends on the character used. The Spinjitzu ability also allows players to use Spinjitzu Switches that activate other devices, usually to help solve problems, which players can use even if their Spinjitzu Bar is empty. Some Spinjitzu tornadoes do other effects to nearby enemies unique to each character.

  • Kai (Fire; Red and Orange)
  • Cole (Earth; Brown)
  • Zane (Ice; Light Blue)
    • Freezes nearby enemies
  • Jay (Lightning; Blue and White)
  • Lloyd (Golden Power; Gold and Yellow)
  • Nya (Maroon)
  • Sensei Wu (Creation; Bright Gold)

Super Strength[]

Only certain characters and vehicles have the ability to break walls and barriers that cannot be broken otherwise. Some of these characters can unearth large chunks of rock that they are then able to throw at enemies, objects, other players, and super strength barriers.

  • Cole
  • Samurai Mech

Underwater Swimming/Diving[]

Only certain characters and vehicles have the ability to dive under the surface of water. Once underwater, the player can swim upwards and downwards in any direction, as well as being able to walk on surfaces and interact with objects (and players, if there are other characters present with the ability) underwater. The ability also allows minor underwater illumination, creating a small circle of light around the character in dark areas or on surfaces.

  • Zane

X-Ray Vision[]

Only certain characters have the ability to scan X-Ray Panels to solve puzzles, usually by completing electrical circuits or plumbing systems. After approaching an X-Ray Panel, the player can see some components inside the panel through a small circle of vision that can be moved around. If there is a movable component (plumbing or electrical wires), the player can then interact with that component (once in the viewing circle) to fix the panel's current.

  • Zane

Laser[]

Only certain characters have the ability to fire a continuous laser projectile to damage enemies, carve and melt gold LEGO and melt ice.

  • Kai

Electricity[]

Only certain characters have the ability to harness electricity to power electrical panels. Once activated, the panels can unlock doors or activate weapons and machinery. Some characters with this ability are resistant to electrical hazards.

  • Jay

Fix-It[]

Only certain characters have the ability to use tools such as wrenches or welding torches to repair damaged machinery. Once repaired, the machinery is available for use.

  • Jay
  • Nya

Intelligence[]

Only certain characters have the ability to use their intellect to solve memory-based puzzles.

  • Jay

Illumination[]

Only certain characters have the ability to brighten dark areas.

  • Lloyd

Pole Vault[]

Only certain characters have the ability to throw staves or arrows into slots and provide poles for acrobatic characters to swing on.

  • Wu

Glide[]

Only certain characters have the ability to glide through the air.

  • Wu
Elements of Surprise

Elements of Surprise[]

Elements of Surprise is the fourth level in the game and is based loosely off the Tournament of Elements from the television show, Ninjago.

In it, Gandalf, Wyldstyle, and Batman arrive at the arena on Chen's Island during one of the tournaments as Eyezor is battling Jacob Pevsner. They fall from a portal onto Eyezor, stopping the battle from finishing. Chen believes them to be surprise competitors and orders Griffin Turner to face them. After the three defeat him by constructing a treadmill, they then face Gravis who is defeated easily by the trio manipulating portals. After constructing a magnet to the Fangpyre Wrecking Ball present in the arena, they defeat their third and final opponent, Karlof, and Chen declares that they have cheated and must be punished. Using the buttons on his throne, he attempts to cast Batman, Wyldstyle, and Gandalf down one of his trapdoors, but is unable to do so. They are then interrupted however by the arrival of Lex Luthor in his power suit from another portal, who orders Chen to hand over his Staff of Elements. Chen refuses to do so and ordering no more surprise guests, uses the staff to shoot fire at the mech as he escapes in his throne down a trapdoor.

Wyldstyle, Batman, and Gandalf pursue him and end up in the maze of the Underground Caverns along with Lex. Chen assures them that they will never escape and send more warriors to attack them, however they manage to make it through and enter the lair of Clouse's Anacondrai serpent. There the trio discover the Elemental Keystone. Before they can reach it however, the serpent appears and Saruman enters, taking control of it using the keystone. After a fierce battle with the serpent, the three manage to defeat the serpent. Saruman is sucked away by a portal and the serpent collapses, letting go of the keystone.

Batman grabs the keystone and the trio enter the Anacondrai Temple where Lex has cornered Chen. Lex grabs the Staff of Elements and faces the others, but is soon defeated. A portal opens and disappointed at his failure, Vortech pulls Lex through. Batman grabs the Staff of Elements, but it quickly merges with the keystone, which Chen claims. Determined to punish them for cheating, Chen uses very powerful elements against the trio (Ice, Lightning, Earth, and Fire) to create difficult environments, but is soon defeated as well. Gandalf grabs the staff as Chen is sucked away by a new portal, which remains open. The three victors decide to enter it, to continue their quest, with Gandalf bringing the staff along.

Errors[]

  • Despite being based on the Tournament of Elements, there are several inconsistencies with the TV show.
    • Eyezor battles Jacob despite none of the Anacondrai Cultists ever fighting the Masters during the tournament.
    • Wu is present at the tournament instead of Garmadon.
    • Lloyd is wearing his golden gi despite no longer being the Golden Ninja at the time of this.
    • Cole is seen wearing his Tournament gi, while Kai and Jay wear their Jungle gis.
  • Chen was able to use Fire with the Staff of Elements before it was connected to the Elemental Keystone, despite Kai not being out of the Tournament yet.
    • Kai was also one of the last to lose his element, so the other Elemental Masters wouldn't have had their powers either.
  • In the subtitles, Clouse's name is misspelled as "Klaus."
  • Chen referred Turner as the Master of Speed and Time, however, his only element is Speed.
  • Karlof was able to create fissures on the ground, despite not having the Earth element.
    • He was also shown to still be in the Tournament, despite being the first to be eliminated.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • LEGO Dimensions is the first large-scale console video game to feature the Ninjago theme.
  • Ninjago is one of the franchises in LEGO Dimensions not to have a level pack.
  • The voice actors of the Ninjago characters (playable and unplayable) are the same ones from Nindroids and Shadow of Ronin.
    • According to both Vincent Tong (Kai's voice actor in the show) and Brent Miller (Zane's voice actor in the show), both (and presumably the rest of the cast) were never asked to reprise their roles in this game.[1][2][3][4][5][6]
  • Some of the Ninjago vehicles are named differently than their show equivalents.
    • The Boulder Blaster is called the Boulder Bomber (but there is a variant called the Boulder Blaster).
    • The Blade Cycle is called the Blade Bike.
    • Master Wu's dragon is called the Flying White Dragon.
  • Griffin Turner, Gravis, Nindroids, Anacondrai Cultists, and Clouse are seen in the Ninjago level.
  • Nindroids are the only Ninjago-related enemies that appear in multiple levels, though they do not appear in the actual Ninjago level, "Elements of Surprise."
  • Nya/Samurai X's Spinjitzu is red, similar to her Spinjitzu in the show.
    • This contradicts "Stiix and Stones," in which Master Wu stated that Samurai X could not do Spinjitzu.
  • The game uses background music featured in both the show and Shadow of Ronin.
  • The achievement "Of Mice and Chen" is a reference to the classic novel "Of Mice and Men."
  • In the game, the ninja are depicted using the Golden Weapons, while they are wearing their Season 4 Jungle gi even though the Golden Weapons were destroyed in the Season 2, "Wrong Place, Wrong Time."
    • This may be due to the Golden Weapons being very iconic as the ninja's first, longest-used, and most powerful weapons, while the gis keep to the modern theme (similarly to the level being based on Season 4).
    • It is also possible that other weapons made less sense to correspond with the gis.
      • The Obsidian Weapons were only featured in another non-canon game.
      • The Jadeblade Weapons were only featured in sets.
      • The Techno Blades were used for only one season and were effective against only one type of enemy.
      • The Elemental Blades were used for a very short period of time.
    • The likely reason for the ninja wearing their Season 4 suits rather than their Season 5 suits is because the latter would offer an alternative way to purchase all the ninja in their current uniforms, which could dissuade consumers from buying the more expensive Season 5 sets.
      • Oddly enough, Lloyd wears his Golden Ninja robes, rather than his Season 4 uniform.
  • A few of the non-Ninjago sets for this game introduced hair pieces that would go on to appear on characters in the TV series years later.
    • The B.A. Baracus The A-Team fun pack introduced the hair that would later appear on Chopper Maroon, Iron Baron, Talon, and Fugi-Dove's brother.
    • The Doc Brown Back to the Future fun pack introduced the hair that would later appear on Bob the Intern, Vangelis, Engelbert, and Twitchy Tim (in the Ninjago Magazine).
  • The only character necessary to 100% complete the game is Zane, as he is the only one who can perform Spinjitzu whilst underwater.
