The LEGO Ninjago Movie Videogame is a Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu video game that was released on September 22, 2017. It takes place within the 2017 film.

Description

Description[]

In the game, players battle hordes of enemies with honor and skill as their favorite Ninja, Lloyd, Nya, Jay, Kai, Cole, Zane, and Master Wu, to defend their home island of Ninjago from the evil Lord Garmadon and his Shark Army.

To do this, they must master the art of Ninjagility, which allows them to traverse Ninjago by wall-running, jumping high, and fighting Ninjago's enemies to improve combat skills. Players can take advantage of the power of the Spinjitzu combat to defend Ninjago together.

The game features eight large, action packed venues based on the story of The LEGO Ninjago Movie. Each place includes its own Dojo Challenge, unique where players can put their fighting skills to the test and fight harder and harder enemies. And with Battle Maps, players can battle their friends and family in four different game modes with competitive split-screen gameplay for up to four players.

Summary[]

The story is nearly identical to that of the movie, but with a few differences.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the first Ninjago Home console game.
    • This is also the first Ninjago videogame that is available on PC and Mac.
  • The video game was released at the same time as the movie.
  • In May 2020, the game was available for free on Playstation, Xbox, and PC for a week.[2]
  • There are eight new Battle Dojos featured in the game which are similar to the Battle Arenas from LEGO Dimensions.
    • Also like LEGO Dimensions, this game features hub world renovations, although this game has the pieces summoned by the Tornado of Creation instead of a vortex.
  • It is the first videogame that you can play with the versions of the TV Series, from Season 1 until Season 7 including the enemies like Serpentine, Stone Army, Nindroids, Anacondrai Cultists, Ghost Warriors, Sky Pirates, Time Twins, and the Vermillion.
  • This game replaces the collectible character tokens from previous LEGO games with blind bags à la LEGO's Collectible Minifigures series which serve the same purpose, a concept that would be later used in the LEGO Incredibles Video Game.
  • The Red Power bricks from previous LEGO games are retextured into Ancient Scrolls.
  • The Overlord and the Skulkin are the only villains who are not in the game.
    • However, in the game files there are unused textures of the Skulkin and code for the Overlord's Staff indicating they were possibly cut during the development of the game.
  • Although most of the background music's original, some tracks from the game are borrowed from Shadow of Ronin.
  • Jacob Pevsner is the only Elemental Master from the Tournament of Elements to not appear in the game.
  • While "videogame" is spelled as one word in the title logo, it is spelled as two words on LEGO.com.[3]
  • The game features minifigure versions of the Green Ninja Mech Dragon, Garma Mecha Man and Mr. Liu as playable characters and a minifigure version of Meowthra as a character collectible through blind bags.
  • In Promotional Images and Trailers the Unclimbable Mountain is shown to take place in daylight, whilst in the final game, and movie, it's set during the night time.
    • Additionally, The Unclimbable Mountain area is set as a snowing location.
