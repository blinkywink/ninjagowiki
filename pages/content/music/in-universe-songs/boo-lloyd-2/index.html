Overview

"You just have to look at it from a different point of view."
This article is about a subject that is canonical to the storyline of Warner Bros' The LEGO Movie franchise.
“And straight in at number one with a bullet, it's "Boo Lloyd!"”
— Radio DJ, The LEGO Ninjago Movie

Boo Lloyd is a song that was made by the cheerleaders of Ninjago High School to harass Lloyd. It soon became popular when it was heard on the radio.

History

History[]

The LEGO Ninjago Movie[]

At some point in time, Chad and the cheerleader squad created negative chant about Lloyd, which would later become a #1 hit, and be played over the radio. It later received a remix, which became just as popular as the first.
