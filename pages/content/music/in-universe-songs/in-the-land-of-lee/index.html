Overview

“Friends, an announcement! The Community of Lee has some new neighbors! And you know what that means! New member orientation!”
— Lee to his community, "Between You and Lee"

In the Land of Lee,[1] also known as the New Neighbor Orientation,[2] is a musical number performed by Lee as part of an orientation for new neighbors of his community.
