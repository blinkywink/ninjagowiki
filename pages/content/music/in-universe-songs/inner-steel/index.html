Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

"Inner Steel (Sally's Song)" is the first single to be released from the brand new season 'NINJAGO Crystalized'. The series features a new character called Sally - an aspiring singer-songwriter who runs away from home to follow her dreams in Ninjago City. "Inner Steel" is her own personal theme song and plays a major role in the new season! It's also a brilliantly catchy, uplifting, pop song in its own right, with themes of perseverance and finding hope that resonate strongly in the current world climate. The song was written by award winning composer Jeppe Riddervold who also wrote the Ninjago TV series' main theme tune - "LEGO Ninjago -The WEEKEND WHIP" - a firm favorite amongst fans.[1]

"Shoutout to Sally, NINJAGO City’s new rockstar! Who doesn’t LOVE when she sings: “Dreams do come true, always believe in yourself no matter the time, no matter the place!” This video is a recap of funny, sad, scary and action-packed moments from NINJAGO® Crystalized with Sally singing away! Let’s unite one last time and sing along with Sally for the ninjas!"[2]

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • The official music video released on the LEGO YouTube channel features footages of Sally playing the guitar and singing her song, which does not appear in the TV series.
  • The full song has a different ending from the one used in the show. However, the show version is used in the official music video.
