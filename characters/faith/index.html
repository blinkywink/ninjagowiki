Overview

“I did. But you taught me that with courage, things can change. And I would rather stay and fight for change in my home, than run.”
— Faith to the stranded ninja, "Lessons for a Master"

Faith is the current leader and former general of the Dragon Hunters. A revered warrior who wielded the Dragonbone Blade, Faith served Iron Baron as second-in-command for some time and went by the name of Heavy Metal — yet over time, her loyalty to his cause faded. When the ninja and Wu wound up in the Realm of Oni and Dragons, Faith struck an alliance with them after learning Wu is the son of the First Spinjitzu Master. Together, the group sought the Dragon Armor, as Faith recounted the legend of the First Spinjitzu Master.

Eventually, the Dragon Hunters realized Faith's betrayal and captured her and the ninja. Iron Baron forced Wu to take him to the Dragon Armor, and after Iron Baron was incapacitated by the Firstbourne, Wu reunited with the ninja. Wu offered for Faith to return with them to Ninjago, but she opted to stay with the Hunters and help them carry on.

Sometime later, the first realm came under attack by the Oni and defeated her tribe. Faith was able to escape with help from Firstbourne and they were tended to by the ninja who she warns of the threat. The ninja successfully defeat the Oni, and she returns home safely.

History

History[]

Prior to Hunted[]

At some point, Iron Baron found Faith as an excellent hunter. He told his story like he did with the other Dragon Hunters to her, and took her in as "Heavy Metal," disguising her so the others would not know she was female.[4]

Hunted[]

Firstbourne[]

Heavy Metal and a team of Hunters were sent by Iron Baron to investigate a crash site on the edge of the realm. They head there to find the stranded ninja, a young Wu, and the wreckage of the Destiny's Bounty on the south side of the realm (the former three having been teleported via Traveler's Tea from Ninjago).

She leads an ambush on the ninja (except for Cole and Wu, who left to find food) at night, and after successfully capturing them, takes them, alongside the ruins of the Bounty, to Dead's End. She brings them into Iron Baron's throne room and is at her master's side laughing with the other Hunters at the ninja after their blindfolds are removed.

Iron & Stone[]

Heavy Metal informs Iron Baron that she found the ninja at the south, making a base. The Baron orders the ninja to The Pit where Heavy Metal watches the battle and soon witnesses the ninja defeat the dragon. Heavy Metal then sees the ninja use their Elemental Powers, as Baron orders his forces to hunt them.

Radio Free Ninjago[]

Heavy Metal uses her Dragonbone Blade to attract Kai, Jay, and Zane's power before capturing them.

How to Build a Dragon[]

When the Hunters prepare for their feast, they were encountered by "Firstbourne." When they fought back, the real Firstbourne came and attacked them. They found Cole and Wu teaming up with the ninja and Daddy No Legs ordered the others to attack them. Iron Baron later told them to hunt for the ninja.

The Gilded Path[]

Heavy Metal finds the ninja's campsite and overhears their conversation. She makes her presence known and threatens the ninja with her weapon, telling them they are her prisoners now. She then interrogates them on Wu truly being the son of the First Spinjitzu Master, and fires an arrow at Cole after stating she heard Kai's mumbling. Wu then stepped forward and revealed it was true, introducing himself to her. Rather than attacking, Heavy Metal reveals herself to be Faith, and that she is now on their side. She tells him about his father's armor. After she asks if he knows its location, Wu tells her that he knows where it is located and she has the ninja escort her vehicle as they journey.

During the journey, Faith is asked by Wu about his father's time in the realm and she tells him with Wu learning about his relationship with the Firstbourne and the unbalance in the realm, which caused him to leave. Wu then tells Faith about Ninjago and she is interested in seeing it before learning what Wu's brother is doing to it.

The ninja soon across two more Hunters, Daddy No Legs and Muzzle, who believe Faith has captured them before they recognize that Wu had a growth spurt and attempt to interrogate him. However, Faith betrays her former comrades and the ninja help her in tying them up. They then steal the two Hunters' vehicles for their journey and leave their enemies behind. Faith was now intrigued about the other realm and desired to see ninjago for herself, as they rode off.

Two Lies, One Truth[]

She continues her journey with the ninja where she gets annoyed by Kai and Jay's squabbling that she cuts a cactus for its water. She questions how they survived in the realm before she flees the area when Baron and his Hunters show up.

Arriving at the Oni Fortress, Faith sees the area is abandoned and gets frustrated that Baron lied to her and the Hunters when discovering the Oni already vacated the realm centuries ago. She asks Wu about the armor and he tells her he admits that he doesn't know it's real as she further descents into a rage. As she does so, Wu notices something on the fortress' door and has her move. Faith then sees that door holds a map to the armor and sees it's real, to her delight.

The Weakest Link[]

Faith continues to journey with the ninja and decides to teach them how to use chain guns and dragon riding as they are necessary for survival in the realm.

When Iron Baron leads the hunt for Faith and the ninja in his HunterCopter, Faith flees with her allies. When Jet Jack hops on her vehicle, she is shocked to discover Faith was a female Hunter like her but Faith replies that she's better and sends her flying, throwing her back at Baron. Faith was made a prize as Baron told his subordinates whoever captured her would be his new second-in-command.

After Cole blasted his copter down, Baron fell onto Faith and Wu's vehicle, he attacks them and this nearly causes Wu to fall off the rider but Faith saves him and states she won't lose the son of the First Spinjitzu Master, which Baron overhears. Shocked by this revelation, he has the Hunters forget Faith and focus the entire hunt to capture Wu instead.

Unfortunately, the ninja steals the Dieselnaut and Faith sacrifices herself by letting the Hunters capture her and half the vehicle while Wu and the ninja escape.

Saving Faith[]

Faith is buried in a hole with only her head up and a gag over her mouth. She is approached by Wu and the ninja who came to rescue her. She berates them and warns them it was a trap after Wu removes the gag, but the Hunters appear and capture them.

Faith is later tied to a stand with Wu and Jay, Wu apologizes for not heeding her warnings but she tells him that he is not at fault since he possessed compassion which was rare in her world. Baron taunts Faith, as she tells the other Hunters that Baron lied to them about the Oni who have been gone from the realm for centuries and tells them that he possibly killed the Hunters to get Dragonbone Blade. This causes Baron to get worried that his secret was exposed and look upon the Hunters as a stir forms amongst them, not wanting any further defiance Baron silences any growing rebellion by asking threatening them. Faith is disgusted by Baron's gutless move, as the annoyed Baron hushes his prisoners by turning on the elemental device.

However, the torture causes Wu to regain all of his memories and slightly aged further into an older teenager. Faith watches as Wu breaks free of his bonds and begins to fight the Dragon Hunters who are knocked aside until Baron breaks it up. She watches as Baron negotiates with Wu who accepts the offer of leading him to the armor to return to Ninjago. Faith and the ninja tell him not to help Baron but he tells them to believe in him.

Faith is left to watch as Wu and Baron leave in search of the armor.

Lessons for a Master[]

As they struggle to get free, Faith is amused the ninja's plan is working before Jet Jack approaches them and frees them. Faith, along with the ninja, are skeptical of the Hunters' intentions. The Hunters reveal they want the ninja to defeat Baron as they see Baron was only using them and will become even more dangerous with the armor.

Wu later returns and reveals that he has obtained the armor. He tells Faith, the ninja, and the Dragon Hunters of Iron Baron's fate, and the Dragon Hunters rejoice that his tyrannical rule was over and Wu has dragons appear to leave the realm.

As the ninja prepare to leave, Wu offers Faith the opportunity to come to Ninjago but she decides to stay in the realm with the hunters since they now have the chance to start a new, better way of life. She happily watches as the ninja depart the realm.

March of the Oni[]

The Darkness Comes[]

Faith and Firstbourne arrive through a portal where they fall unconscious near the monastery. Briefly regaining consciousness, Faith whispers to Wu about the First Realm being attacked and all the Hunters and Dragons turning to stone before she is taken into the monastery to heal. Once there, Faith tells Wu about the darkness that spread through her realm before Wu gives her a powerful healing potion to recover from the shock.

Endings[]

Faith later awakes as Wu and Misako inquire about what happened in the Realm of Oni and Dragons. She reveals that The Oni attacked the realm and the Hunters tried to defend themselves with the help of the Dragons, but they were all frozen with only Faith and Firstbourne managing to escape.

After she heals up, she helped the ninja and Garmadon fight off the invading Oni. After the battle, she stayed in the Monastery to celebrate their victory. She presumably returned to her realm afterwards.

Character Encyclopedia New Edition[]

The Iron Baron's right-hand man isn't the person he believes them to be. Heavy Metal is a name and disguise adopted by an idealistic woman named Faith. She is as full of faith as her name suggests and hopes for a better future for her Realm. If she were in charge, she would run things differently…

Relationships

Relationships[]

Dragon Hunters[]

In "Saving Faith," Faith revealed the truth of the Oni to the other hunters and told them Baron lied to them. This shocks them and caused them to question their loyalties.

During "Lessons for a Master," the hunters ultimately chose to side with her to stop Baron. After Wu returned, he told them of Baron's fate with Faith and the hunters rejoiced at being free. They accepted Faith as their new leader since the Baron has been defeated by the dragons.

Iron Baron[]

Faith was Iron Baron's second-in-command.

At some time, Faith became Iron Baron's best Dragon Hunter and been given the name "Heavy Metal." However, it was revealed in "The Gilded Path" that Iron Baron forced her to disguise herself as a male, as he thought it was foolish that his best Dragon Hunter was a woman.

After hearing Wu betrayed him, Iron Baron gained a dislike for her and told the other Dragon Hunters to bring her to him in order for one of them to become his new second-in-command.

During "Saving Faith," Baron captured Faith and the ninja and taunted her on her defeat. However, he was worried when she revealed the truth of the Oni to Jet Jack and the other hunters.

During "Lessons for a Master," Faith wanted to stop Baron and joined the ninja in trying to escape. After Wu returned, he told them of Baron's fate with Faith and the hunters rejoicing at being free.

Jet Jack[]

“Heavy Metal...you're like...me.”

Jet Jack is Faith's former rival and now one of her good friends.

In "The Weakest Link," Faith and the ninja were chased by the hunters. When Jet Jack attacked Faith and Wu, she discovered Faith was a female Hunter like her but Faith replies she's better and sends her flying, throwing her back at Baron. Jet Jack ended up capturing Faith after Baron promised the reward of becoming his new second-in-command for her capture.

During "Saving Faith," Faith revealed the truth of the Oni to Jet Jack and the other hunters. They start to consider her words after Baron begins to show his real nature.

In "Lessons for a Master," Jet Jack and the other hunters side with Faith and the ninja in order to stop Baron. After Baron's defeat and Wu's return, the hunters accepted Faith as their new leader.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Faith shares a few similarities with Furiosa from Mad Max: Fury Road.
    • Both are high-ranking female followers of an evil wasteland warlord (The Iron Baron and Immortan Joe respectively), but later ended up betraying them to escape from their tyranny.
    • Both are escorting a group of escapees, for Furiosa it was Immortan Joe's Wives, for Faith it was the Ninja.
    • Both ended up having an emotional breakdown after reaching important destinations in their journey. For Furiosa it was despair at seeing her home The Green Place becoming a fetid bog, while Faith was furious at finding out that the Oni Fortress was abandoned, meaning that the Iron Baron had been lying to her and the other Dragon Hunters about the Oni. Alongside learning that Wu had been lying to her about knowing where the Dragon Armor was.
    • Both take over their former leader's factions after their deaths at the end of their respective journeys. With Furiosa taking over the War Boys and the Citadel, while Faith becomes the new leader of the Dragon Hunters.
  • Faith's role after becoming an ally to the Ninja, mirrors Nya's role in the early seasons of the show.
    • She is the sole female member of the group comprised of the first four ninja and Wu.
    • Faith hides her identity and gender behind an armored and concealing outfit, keeping her face hidden, and using a voice changer. Mirroring Nya keeping her identity hidden as Samurai X.
    • They are both ostracized for being women, but ultimately prove themselves to be valuable allies and amazing fighters.
  • Heavy Metal is tied with Muzzle for the most common Dragon Hunter with a total of three boxed set appearances, these being 70653 Firstbourne, 70654 Dieselnaut, and 70655 The Dragon Pit. However, she appears as her alias Heavy Metal in all three, and not with her hair or uncovered face.
  • She seems to have a robotic voice when in her Heavy Metal disguise.
    • In "The Gilded Path," it is revealed that she wears a mask to cover up her face and voice because Iron Baron doesn't want other Hunters to find out his best hunter is a woman.
    • The Hunters discover her secret in "The Weakest Link."
  • She could not pronounce Ninjago right, even after hearing the correction. She pronounced it as "Ninyeego."
    • In "Lessons for a Master," she finally pronounces it correctly.
  • Up until "The Gilded Path," her visible eye was missing the eyelash design almost always present on female characters, making her appear to be male. This allowed the twist to be kept a secret up until the episode.
  • Since Nobu's name in Japanese means "faith," that technically means there are two characters in the show named Faith.
  • Her Swedish voice actress, Matilda Smedius, also voiced Nya in The LEGO Ninjago Movie.
  • Faith refused to go to Ninjago so she and the Dragon Hunters can fight for their home.
    • Two episodes later, she arrives in Ninjago for the first time to escape the Oni with Firstbourne.
  • She resembles Ronin in her Heavy Metal attire, as they both wear rice hats and wear a mask that only shows only one of their eyes.
  • She is the only one of her kind to have a classic black eye like those of humans instead of the bigger red eyes of her kind.
  • Faith has the same hairstyle as Jordana (Season 2).
