Overview

“No, the shame is mine alone. I will make up for my actions, if such a thing is possible. Farewell, ninja.”
— Zilvar to Jay and Nya, "Dangerous Connections"

Zilvar Slashclaw is an anthropomorphic tiger warrior and one of the two survivors of the southern Wyldness Tiger Tribe along with Ras. He was held hostage in the Land of Madness for years before he was freed by the ninja, who he followed to the Never Kingdoms. There, he was manipulated by Ras and possessed by the Weapon of Desolation. Zilvar experienced extreme guilt when his actions while possessed by the weapon resulted in the destruction of Lloyd's mortal body, opting to find redemption in his own way.

History

History[]

Background[]

Hailing from the Tiger Tribe, Zilvar's village faced danger from unsettling tremors. Despite the assurances from the Ancients of the Wyldness that the disturbances would eventually cease, Zilvar remained skeptical, viewing their words as mere superstitions. Concerned for the safety of the tribe, especially with the prospect of starting a family with Mei, he proposed relocating to a more secure area within the Wyldness. When another tremor struck, Zilvar vowed not to let his tribe down. He bid farewell to his fellow tribe members and embarked on a journey, crossing the Lava Wastes and other regions until he reached the Forest of Spirits, which he deemed an ideal new home for his tribe. With a leaf resting on his chest, symbolizing hope, he set out to return to the village, only to discover that it had been ravaged by the tremors. For days, Zilvar searched for survivors, only to find none. Overwhelmed by guilt for the loss of his tribe, he inscribed their names on the memorial stone before leaving the Wyldness. Unbeknownst to him, one other member of his tribe managed to survive. With no destination in mind, he wandered through various realms, including the Never-Realm, where he encountered the Fishers and began the process of rebuilding his life.[1]

However, when Zilvar traveled to the Realm of Madness, he was seized by the Pit-Master's Cragling Recruiters and compelled to battle in the Gladiator Pit. The only way to earn his freedom was to achieve 200 consecutive victories, as promised by the Pit-Master.

In the Pits (flashback)[]

After the Merge, Zilvar encountered Jay, who had also been captured. Despite Jay's determination to free the gladiators, Zilvar regarded this as false hope, convinced that Jay's promise would ultimately be broken.[2] After Jay used his Elemental Power of Lightning in a match, Zilvar never saw Jay again for many years, believing he had betrayed them, not knowing the Pit-Master had Administration Agent Blake Donovan suppress Jay's memories.

In the Pits[]

Many years later, Zilvar would come face-to-face with Jay again after he had regained his memories and finally came back to free the gladiators. Zilvar is determined to defeat Jay so he can get his 200th win and finally be free, but Jay attempts to get through to Zilvar saying he came back to free him and the others but he needs his help to do it, Zilvar rejects at first still believing hope is false, but Zilvar ultimately accepts and lets Jay beat him so he can free him and the other gladiators. With the help of the ninja who were disguised as well, they managed to free the gladiators and Zilvar. Zilvar then looks off into the distance thinking of his home when Jay asks if he is going to head home, but Zilvar tells him he once had a home but it is gone now, realizing he had no where else to go, Jay offers Zilvar to come with them to the Never Kingdoms which he accepts saying he had people in that land and supposes he could find them, so he boarded the Land Bounty and heads to the Never Kingdoms with the ninja, finally free.

A Frozen Scar[]

Zilvar recounted to the ninja the tale of how he lost his tribe to the violent tremors that ravaged his village. Lloyd then mentioned that they had encountered another tiger named Ras, which puzzled Zilvar as he couldn't recall that name. He speculated that Ras might belong to a different branch of the Tiger Tribe, considering the vastness of the Wyldness. As they continued their journey, Zilvar and the ninja witnessed the Weapon of Desolation spiraling out of control, threatening to destabilize its containment unit. Later, while riding Grimtak, Zilvar reached the boundary separating the Land of Madness from the Never Kingdoms alongside the ninja. A debate ensued among the ninja about the safety of crossing the boundary, but Zilvar, growing impatient, decided to forge ahead with Grimtak, crossing the barrier without harm. The ninja followed suit, and they soon arrived at the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms, where they encountered the Fishers. Young Fishers greeted Zilvar with friendly waves, which he reciprocated.

That night, as Zilvar rested alongside the ninja and Grimtak, he was abruptly awakened by Kai, who was possessed by Wu. Wu's voice warned the ninja to be cautious of the Beast Warrior, leaving Zilvar bewildered. Wu then emerged from Kai, heading towards the portal gate of the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms. The following morning, Zilvar wandered through the market, purchasing fish from a Fisher to feed Grimtak. During this, Lloyd approached Zilvar, suggesting that he no longer needed to accompany the ninja on their mission, given how long he had already assisted them. Lloyd pointed out that Beniro could take over from there, emphasizing that Zilvar deserved the opportunity to pursue a new life. Zilvar wished the ninja safe travels, shaking hands with Lloyd in a gesture of gratitude and respect. As night fell again, Zilvar took a moment to admire the view of the Never Kingdoms, picking up a leaf and reflecting on how much Mei would have loved the place. Suddenly, he was startled when the portal gate from the Monastery activated. Zilvar armed himself as the doors opened to reveal Ras.

The Summit (flashback)[]

Zilvar recognized Ras as a scion of the Tiger Tribe, but he was puzzled, failing to identify him by name. As they walked, Ras disclosed his original identity as Zeras, shocking Zilvar. Ras explained the tribe believed Zilvar to be dead or had abandoned them. Zilvar denied leaving, asserting he would have sought out Ras if he had known he was alive. Feeling guilty for not supporting Ras, Zilvar listened as Ras urged him to prevent the destruction of the Weapon of Desolation, which threatened Ras' master, who created the weapon. Horrified, Zilvar questioned Ras about his master, but Ras insisted the focus should be on securing the weapon, as his master's plan was nearly in motion. Although Zilvar was willing to help a fellow Tiger Tribe member, he opposed letting the Weapon of Desolation remain, fearing it could destroy the Merged Realms. Ras dismissed Zilvar's concerns, revealing the ultimate goal to capture the Source Dragons to harness their Source Energy, aiming to reverse the Merge and restore the Tiger Tribe. Zilvar, recalling a leaf from earlier, refused, stating that capturing Source Dragons was blasphemous. Ras implored Zilvar to think of their tribe and Mei, but Zilvar reminded him of Mei's pure heart, asserting that aiding Ras would insult the memory of her and the rest of the tribe. In anger, Zilvar called Ras by his birth name, provoking Ras to claim he worked hard for the tribe that Zilvar failed to save. As Ras brandished the Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength, Zilvar warned him not to challenge him, but Ras threatened to take matters into his own hands, vowing to eliminate the ninja and even Zilvar himself for his perceived betrayal. Zilvar retorted that it was Ras who had betrayed the tribe, not him. Ras prepared to strike when Zilvar let out a whistle, summoning Grimtak, who head butted Ras, creating a moment of distraction. Zilvar quickly climbed onto Grimtak's back and declared his intention to protect the ninja from Ras.

A Frozen Scar[]

Zilvar raced to the Boreal River, where he discovered that the Weapon of Desolation had corrupted the river and Beniro. To save Zane from Beniro, Zilvar had Grimtak deliver a head butt to Beniro. When Lloyd spotted Zilvar, he was taken aback. Before Zilvar could warn the ninja about Ras, Beniro lunged at Lloyd, prompting Zilvar to demand that Beniro leave Lloyd alone. The Green Ninja insisted he could handle Beniro and urged Zilvar to focus on the Weapon of Desolation instead. Zilvar and Grimtak avoided Ice spikes that jutted out from the frozen river as they reached the weapon. Zilvar seized it, freeing Beniro from the weapon's influence. Soon after, the ninja regrouped with Zilvar, and Jay was grateful, recalling how he had always believed Zilvar was a good egg since their first encounter. Zilvar, taking the compliment literally, pointed out that he was not hatched from an egg. Cole asked why Zilvar had returned, but just as the tiger was about to explain, the Weapon of Desolation began to seize control of him, leading to an internal struggle where his tempted side insisted that he must not destroy the weapon. This side reminded him how Ras could restore the tribe if the weapon remained intact before telling himself that he can't tell the ninja. In a moment of deception, Zilvar told the ninja he had a feeling they needed him. With the containment unit shattered and Beniro now unable to join the team for their ongoing adventure, the ninja reluctantly tasked Zilvar with holding the Weapon of Desolation as they pressed forward on their journey.

All Wizards Are Liars[]

As their journey to the Abyss of Eternal Night continued, Zilvar continued to ride Grimtak. However, when the Land Bounty got stuck, they stopped and went to see what's going on, discovering that it was some scorpions that stopped the Bounty. As the scorpions, Zilvar tried to fend them off with Grimtak. However, during the fight, one of the scorpions knocked both Zilvar and Grimtak, separating them. Then the scorpions captured Grimtak and tried to take him with them. Seeing this, Zilvar got scared for his friend's life, wanting scorpions to release him. Taking this opportunity, the Weapon of Desolation manipulated Zilvar into using the weapon's power, leading him to use the weapon to summon an Earth Monster and an Acid Monster. The Elemental Monsters quickly fended off the scorpions, saving Grimtak. Then, Zilvar comforted his friend, saying that he is okay while looking at the Weapon of Desolation. However, after hearing Lloyd's voice, Zilvar sucked the monsters back into the weapon before the ninja could spot them. Lloyd asked if Zilvar was safe and what happened. In response, Zilvar said that he managed to drive the scorpions away. Lloyd said they also drove them away, suggesting that they should get out of here while they still can. Hearing this, Zilvar got on top of Grimtak and started riding him again.

The Summit[]

As Zilvar and the ninja continued their journey to the Abyss of Eternal Night, Jay expressed his concerns about Zilvar, saying that he can sense something is bothering him. In response, Lloyd said how this journey is tough for all of them and the sooner they destroy the Weapon of Desolation, the better, saying that it breeds danger whenever it goes. Meanwhile, Zilvar continued to be tempted by the Weapon of Desolation, who tried to make him believe that it could help him bring back his tribe. Then, Zilvar remembered his meeting with Ras and him helping the ninja in the Boreal River. As the weapon continued to tempt him, he and the ninja arrived in the Abyss of Eternal Night.

Desperately Seeking Sora[]

As Zilvar approached the abyss, he grappled with the decision to drop the Weapon of Desolation into the chasm. Despite the ninja's reassurances, he found himself torn, tempted by the weapon's immense power. He wrestled with his thoughts, questioning whether he should discard the one chance he had to protect his tribe. On one hand, he recognized the weapon's destructive potential, but on the other, he recalled Ras' words, suggesting that keeping the weapon could be the key to their salvation. Zilvar's inner conflict deepened as he reflected on how Ras had changed, doubting his intentions. His tempted side argued that the tribe relied on him, and letting go of the weapon would mean abandoning them. Memories of the Tiger Tribe's fate haunted him, intensifying his struggle. Just as he hesitated, a vision of Mei appeared, pleading for his help as she fell. Overwhelmed by the weapon's influence, Zilvar turned against the ninja and summoned Elemental Monsters to fight on his behalf, resolving to save his tribe.

The ninja battled the Elemental Monsters as Lloyd urged Zilvar to destroy the weapon. Despite Lloyd's plea, Zilvar summoned even more Elemental Monsters to the fray. Zilvar called forth Grimtak, compelling the Dragon-Ape to turn against the ninja as the tiger warrior mounted him. From Grimtak's back, Zilvar continued his attack on the ninja. The ninja transformed into their Draconus Form. Meanwhile, Zilvar and Grimtak confronted Lloyd as he attempted to reason with Zilvar, ultimately managing to dislodge the tiger from the Dragon Beast's back. After Grimtak retreated, the Weapon of Desolation then unveiled its hidden abilities to Zilvar. Learning them, Zilvar absorbed an Ice Monster and unleashed a beam of corrupted Ice at the ninja, only to be thwarted by Zane's creation of an ice wall. Subsequently, Zilvar absorbed a Fire Monster, obliterating the ice wall with a powerful ball of corrupted Fire that knocked the ninja back. Continuing to absorb more Elemental Monsters, Zilvar unleashed a torrent of acid and Fire upon Lloyd and attempted to blast Jay with acid. The Blue Ninja tried reaching out to Zilvar. Despite Jay's efforts, Zilvar absorbed an Earth Monster and attempted to fire asphalt at Jay, inadvertently hitting Cole instead. Persisting in his assault, Zilvar summoned more Elemental Monsters and combined his powers into a beam that sent the ninja reeling. Jay continued to empathize with Zilvar, sharing his own experiences of being torn from his former self and manipulated by others. Jay implored Zilvar to recognize that he too could overcome the corruption and find a new path, just as Jay had done with the support of the ninja. For a moment, Zilvar seemed to break free from the weapon's control in response to Jay's words, but ultimately, the Weapon of Desolation reasserted its control over the tiger. Zilvar struck Jay with an uppercut and unleashed a torrent of Elemental Powers, rendering the latter unconscious. Enraged, Kai charged at Zilvar, only to be met with another beam of Elemental Powers that sent him reeling.

Dangerous Connections[]

As the battle at the abyss continued, Lloyd tried to convince Zilvar to destroy the weapon, saying that it's corrupting him. However, Zilvar told him that this weapon can help bring back his tribe, everything he's lost, and they will not stop him, even if he has to obliterate every last one of them, as he conjured more Elemental Monsters.

As Zilvar continued his attack, Kai said that if he won't throw the Weapon into the Abyss, they might have to knock him in with it. However, Lloyd told him that they only have to get it out of his hands. Then, Zane and Cole tried to approach Zilvar with their Draconus Form, only for him to notice the duo and knock them into a wall by shooting a Mutation beam at them from his eyes. Later, Zilvar tried to attack Lloyd with Elemental Powers as he approached at him, and knocked him down by hitting him with the weapon. Then, Zilvar summoned more Elemental Monsters, as Lloyd told Jay to wait for his signal, which confused him. Lloyd went for Zilvar as he defeated the Elemental Monsters on the way, slashing him with his claws. The corrupted tiger warrior tried to knock him out by shooting Mutation beams as he dodged them. Eventually, Lloyd threw dust at Zilvar, distracting him and allowing Lloyd to knock the Weapon of Desolation out of his hands, which released Zilvar from the weapon's corruption while exhausting him. Then, Jay zapped the ground the weapon was on, causing it to fall into the abyss. However, the Weapon of Desolation started to levitate and possessed Zilvar with its power again. The sentient artifact taunted the ninja through the tiger for their attempts to get rid of it and summoned more Elemental Monster to surround them. The Weapon told them that they can't destroy it, for it draws power from everything, even from the very Sources themselves, before continuing to summon more Elemental Monsters as the ninja fought against them. Later, Lloyd remembers the weapon's words of drawing power from everything and recalled the Ritual of Unrooting and how he can stabilize Elemental Powers. Despite Kai's warning, Lloyd went for the weapon, touching it and stabilizing it while causing Zilvar to scream in pain. This freed the tiger warrior from the weapon's possession, causing him to fall to the ground as Lloyd stabilized the Elemental Powers, sucking the monsters back into the scepter. Suddenly, the weapon filled Lloyd with an uncontrollable surge of Life, disintegrating his mortal body into Life particles, leaving the ninja shocked and devastated as the deactivated scepter fell into the Abyss of Eternal Night. Zilvar, who just came to his senses, asked where his people are. Angry at the tiger for Lloyd's supposed death, Kai approached him angrily. However, Jay stopped him and defended Zilvar, saying that he was possessed. Hearing this, the warrior remembered the Weapon of Desolation and realized what he had done. Cole told him that they should have never made him carry it, but it's destroyed now. As he held one of the Life particles, Zilvar asked if the Green Ninja is gone, which Kai confirmed. However, Jay refused to believe that Lloyd is gone and showed the now returned Elemental Powers as proof, with Nya suggested that this was his final gift to them.

Later, Zilvar returned to the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms with Grimtak and the ninja, revealing that Ras came to him for help. Then, he pointed out how they are too quick to forgive, despite what he had taken from them. However, Nya and Jay said how it wasn't his fault and Ras is a master at manipulation. However, Zilvar said that the shame is only on him and promises to make up for his actions, if such a thing is possible. As he mounted Grimtak, he bid his farewells to Jay and Nya and left the monastery with his beast companion.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Zilvar is an anthropomorphic tiger with white fur and dark red stripes. On the face, these stripes are also on his forehead and under his orange eyes, which are surrounded by thick gray fur that resemble eyebrows. He also has four large white fangs protruding from his mouth. His chin hair is braided into a small white braid akin to a beard and he has three gold claws on his feet.

As an adult, Zilvar wears a white armor of the Tiger Tribe, which had a silver tiger symbol in a gold and black circle on its chest, light gray trims and accents, gold straps holding it in place, white and gold knee pads, and a dark red belt. Underneath his armor, he wears a black sweater, pants, and boots with his claws protruding from them.

As he got older, Zilvar continues to wear the armor, which has taken on a slightly different form, while also adding on two different silver pauldrons. It is now held by two silver paw ornaments with gold claws. The silver tiger symbol was no longer in a circle as it is instead located at the bottom of the chest, while also featuring gold tusks and teeth around the belt, making it resemble Grimtak.

Personality

Personality[]

Zilvar is a noble warrior of the Tiger Tribe, caring deeply for his tribe and his mate, Mei. When the tremors started to threaten their village, Zilvar got concerned about the future of their tribe. Even after the Ancients of the Wyldness reassured them by saying the tremors will pass soon, Zilvar viewed their words as superstition and proposed to relocate the village into a more secure area. After another tremor struck, Zilvar vowed to not let his tribe down and embarked on a journey to find a new place to live, eventually reaching the Forest of Spirits, which he deemed as an ideal place to live. With hope, Zilvar returned back to his village, only to find it in ruins, leaving him devastated. Overwhelmed by guilt, he wrote the names of the lost members of the tribes on the memorial stone. With no destination in mind, he travelled around the realms, including the Never-Realm, where he befriended the Fishers and started to rebuild his life piece by piece.

However, after his arrival in the Land of Madness, the tiger was captured by the Cragling Recruiters and forced to fight in the gladiator pit until he had 200 wins. Because of this, Zilvar became very competitive and saw trying to get out of the pit as false hope. When Jay came to the pit and promised to get him and the other gladiators out of here, Zilvar deemed his hope as cruel and said his promise is destined to break. After Jay was taken by the Administration, Zilvar thought that Jay had betrayed them, not knowing that the blue ninja had his memories erased by Blake.

Years later, when Jay, who disguised himself as Rogue, came back to free the match, he was forced to fight Zilvar, who was about to have his 200th battle. Determined to win, Zilvar attacked the blue ninja tirelessly and violently. Jay tried to convince him to throw the fight, so he can reach the security system and disable it. However, Zilvar did not want to follow his plan, saying that what if he loses and he fails. However, Jay reminded him how he viewed that hope can be dangerous, saying that they could make it dangerous for the ones who put them here. This made Zilvar realize the pain the Pit-Master put him and the others through, accepting Jay's plan and allowing him to win the match. Later, when the Pit-Master tried to kill Jay with a Dragon-Ape, Zilvar fought him back with Grimtak. The Pit-Master questioned his motive, saying that he would have been free if he defeated Jay. However, Zilvar said that no one is free while their fellows are caged by a tyrant, slamming the Pit-Master away. This indicates his renewed faith in hope. When Jay asked him if he's alright, Zilvar told him how he can't remember the last time he felt this good. Following the rebellion, Zilvar joined the ninja in their journey to the Never Kingdoms, hoping to see his friends again.

However, after Zilvar offered to carry the Weapon of Desolation to the Abyss of Eternal Night, the Weapon started tempting him, making him believe that he can bring back his tribe with it, even forcing the tiger to use it to save Grimtak from the scorpions. Eventually, the Weapon fully corrupted Zilvar, causing him to attack the ninja by summoning Elemental Monsters. After Lloyd saved Zilvar from the Weapon's corruption and exploded because of the Weapon, Zilvar came back to his senses, asking where his tribe is. After finding out about what he did and the Green Ninja's death, Zilvar felt grief, guilt, and sadness, promising to the ninja that he will make up for his actions before leaving with Grimtak.

Official description

Official description[]

A warrior tiger from the Wyldness, Zilvar became a good friend to the ninja before the Weapon of Desolation turned his thoughts to evil. He rides a mighty Dragon-Ape called Grimtak, which he rescued from the Land of Madness. Together, Zilvar and Grimtak make quite an imposing pair.[3]

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • His minifigure uses a white version of Ras' head mold. Due to this, he is the only other tiger character with a braided beard.
  • Zilvar was the subject of a hoax wherein an Ecuadorian man claimed to news outlets that he had designed the character as part of a global internet contest conducted by LEGO and WildBrain.[4][5] His claims were disproved when Chi Wing Lee stated that Zilvar was actually designed by LEGO graphic designer Renato Rangel Esper.[6]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He bears a resemblance to Battle Beast from Invincible, as both are white felines with red, yellow, and black color schemes who wield maces.
  • He has many similarities to Tormak, another character from the Wyldness.
    • Both are tigers who have a strong bond with a loved one (Mei for Zilvar and Li'Ella for Tormak) before losing them (Mei's death for Zilvar and Li'Ella's kidnapping by Fangar for Tormak).
    • Both spiral and succumb to the temptations of their enemies (corruption by the Weapon of Desolation for Zilvar and blackmail by Fangar for Tormak).
    • Both end up briefly turning against their allies to briefly further the interest of the very enemy manipulating them (Zilvar fighting the Ninja and Tormak stealing the Artifact to bring it to Fangar).
    • Both of these decisions backfire and leave them worse off than they were before (Zilvar feeling shameful and guilt-ridden for indirectly causing Lloyd's death, and Tormak getting incinerated).
    • Both frequently contemplate inanimate objects reminding them of their loved ones during times their morality is tested (Li'Ella's drawing for Tormak (seen in "The Artifact") and pink leaves for Zilvar/Mei).
  • He did not meet Libber while he was trapped in the gladiator pit.[7]
  • In the Polish dub, Lavertus and Zilvar share the same voice actor.
