Overview

“Fred Finely here, five-time award winning reporter in downtown Ninjago City to report an inclement weather pattern: Fireballs falling from the sky.”
— Fred Finely, "Snaketastrophy"

Fred Finely is a famous reporter for NGTV News.

History

History[]

Background[]

Following the creation of the reality show, Most Ultimate Extreme Ninja Challenge Ever, Finely would become the host, often overseen by the producer Rachel Sparrow. Later, he would start a seventeen-day streak of holding a smile on the show.

A Team Divided[]

Finely would be seen on TV as a depressed Jay recuperated from his loss at the hands of Suzie Wheeler. Inspired, Jay would enter the Gauntlet of Humility, overseen by Finely. Declaring his seventeen-day smile proudly, Finely would introduce Jay to the Gauntlet, warning him of its increase in difficulty to accommodate Jay's skills. Cheerfully commentating on Jay's performance, he would promptly be covered in the chocolate pudding after Jay fell into a vat of it, causing him to lose his smile and in anger quit his job, stomping off.

Afterward, Finely managed to get a job as a news reporter, reporting on attacks by various threats to Ninjago.

The New Ninja[]

During the rebuilding of Stiix following the Preeminent's death, Ghost Warriors attacked, and Finely oversaw the ensuing chaos on a chopper, reporting the news on the television. This alerted Nya and the ninja, who arrived at the city to destroy the spectral entities.

Legend of the Brown Ninja[]

Dareth somehow acquired a prime time spot on Ninjago's hottest talk show, Fred Finely Live at Five, though, the ninja declined. Dareth, not giving up, got not just a guest appearance, but for the entire hour. Dareth then convinced the ninja to do it. A few days later, they air the live show. Fred announced the start of the show, smiled at the camera and did a little dance. The show apparently runs a "Real Reel," which gathers footage from a secret camera, then it takes pieces and puts them together to add drama, much to the detriment of the ninja.

Prior to Pythor's Revenge[]

Sometime before Pythor's theft of the Thermoynamic Temperature Gauge, Fred Finely became a reporter and began to compete with Gayle Gossip for who has better news.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Snaketastrophy[]

Fred Finely is seen talking to the Pyro Vipers and when the ninja save him from being eaten alive by an Elemental Cobra, He tells them that Aspheera was headed for the Ninjago Museum of History to find a Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu.

Prime Empire[]

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

Fred Finely was sitting right next to Captain Soto on a bench.

Level Thirteen[]

Fred presents a report about the disappearances of Prime Empire players.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Until Seliel's reappearance in Way of the Departed, Finely was the only named book-exclusive character to make an appearance in another book, instead of staying exclusive to his debut.
    • He is also the first book exclusive character to have a minifigure and second to stop being a book-exclusive character by appearing in the show.
      • He is the third element from a book to be introduced in the series, the first being Shadow Army appearing on the mural in Season 10, and the second being the Scrolls of Forbidden Spinjitzu, also introduced in Season 11.
    • However, his minifigure was released in a LEGO Ninjago Movie set and was released before his appearance in the show, even though he didn't appear in the movie.
      • He has a thicker mustache, a red tie, and a different hairstyle than he eventually does in the show.
    • Tommy Kalmar was a proponent of including Fred Finely in the show.[1]
  • As mentioned in Pythor's Revenge and "Snaketastrophy," Gayle Gossip is Fred Finely's rival.
  • Alongside Noble, Patty Keys, Dareth and Benny, Finely is one of the few citizens in the series to be seen changing occupations.
  • In The New Ninja and TV show, Fred's last name is spelled Finely.
  • According to Finely, he has won eight awards, and it led to this number because of a running gag in "Snaketastrophy" involving Finely winning awards in between his reports.
  • In the TV series, he uses the hair of Mr. Incredible from Disney's The Incredibles.
  • He has the same mustache as Lou.
