Overview

“That your ride out there? Pretty hot.”
— The Checkout Girl to Kai, "Codename: Arcturus"

The Checkout Girl[1] is a cashier that works at a gas station.

History

History[]

Codename: Arcturus[]

Kai approached the Checkout Girl at a gas station and asked her if the snacks he had chosen were the spiciest ones available. The girl didn't seem to know, so Kai decided to impress her by using his fire to heat up the snacks. The Checkout Girl complimented Kai's car, the Prototype X-1, and Kai joked that he was a secret agent and offered to take her for a ride. However, their conversation was abruptly interrupted by P.I.X.A.L., who called out to Kai through the car's speakers. The Checkout Girl accused Kai of being a playboy when he mentioned P.I.X.A.L's name, and she threw her drink at him in frustration.

Personality

Personality[]

The Checkout Girl exhibits a friendly demeanor, as evidenced by her initial kindness towards Kai. Additionally, she maintains a relatively reserved disposition, as she does not engage in extensive conversation. However, it appears that she may lack consideration, as she did not afford Kai an opportunity to explain himself and instead resorted to throwing her drink at him, assuming he was a player.

Appearance

Appearance[]

The Checkout Girl has blonde hair styled in a ponytail. She is attired in a blue jacket with a white stripe, complemented by a pink sweater and a white skirt.
