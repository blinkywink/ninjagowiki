Overview

“Um... so you're the Big Man?”
— Zane to Nails, mistaking him for Killow, "Snake Jaguar"

Nails is a member of the Sons of Garmadon.

History

History[]

Snake Jaguar[]

Zane was pushed in front of Nails and proceeded to ask him if he was the big man. Nails growled in response and was silenced as soon as Killow started to speak. Later, Nails tries to stop Zane from completing the race after the Sons of Garmadon discovered he was a spy.

Dread on Arrival[]

He and the Sons of Garmadon fought off the Policemen during the ritual to resurrect Garmadon.

Later, he was arrested after the ninja stopped Harumi.

The Gilded Path[]

Nails helped the Sons of Garmadon raid the Resistance base and capture most of the Resistance.

Green Destiny[]

After Lloyd defeated Garmadon, Nails and the other Sons of Garmadon were placed in Kryptarium Prison.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Nails has a striking resemblance to Chopper Maroon, except for the fact he has shorter legs.
  • Nails only speaks in grunts, as seen in "Snake Jaguar".
  • In the show, he wears a helmet like Killow most of the time, although in some scenes he also has the red hair he has in sets.
  • He, Jacob Pevsner, Mr. Righty Tighty, and Michael Kramer are the only known adult human characters to have short legs.
  • He is similar to Min-Droid, as they are both short-legged members of a villain faction, whose size was questioned by another character.
    • Coincidentally, the characters questioning them are Nindroids (Cryptor and Zane).
