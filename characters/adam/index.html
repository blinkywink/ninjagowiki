Overview

“Jeez, buddy, relax! That's Adam. He wouldn't hurt a fly.”
— Plundar to Cole, "Dungeon Party!"

Adam is a giant spider who accompanies Plundar, along with the other members of the Upply.

History

History[]

Background[]

Adam met the Lowly as a young spiderling when they were searching for the Skull of Hazza D'ur, along with many other spiders. Fungus was able to freeze all of the spiders but him. Taking an interest in the trio, Adam accompanied them on their quest without them realizing they were being followed, and he almost crushed them by accidentally setting off a trap. Once they obtained the skull, they began to head back to Vangelis, with Adam in tow.

Once they arrived back in the Kingdom of Shintaro with the skull presented to Vangelis, the King changed his mind and dropped Adam and the rest of the Lowly down the mountain when they disapproved.[1]

The Real Fall[]

Adam greeted Cole, but he freaked out after having bad experiences with spiders so far. He then fainted after going through so much while falling.

Dungeon Party![]

When Cole wakes up, Adam loomed over him, attempting to again say hello. Cole gets scared and causes Adam to hide behind Plundar's back.

Adam then heard the story of the Lowly with narration by Korgran, which also detailed how the group got Adam.

Dungeon Crawl![]

Adam followed the Lowly, Cole, Vania, and Wu as they swim out of Rock-Bottom and find a lava stream. Knowing they have to cross the lava, Adam decided to walk on the ceiling and made it to the other side before everyone else. Suddenly, they encountered a Magma Monster, after wearing it down and Fungus freezing it, Plundar told Adam to push a boulder, which would cause the Magma Monster to sink back into the lava before it could break free. Adam continued to follow Cole's lead and heads deeper into the mountain.

Masters Never Quit[]

Adam followed Cole, Wu, Vania, and the Upply as they stumbled across the Heart of the Mountain.

The Ascent[]

Adam rides in the mine carts along with the Upply and Wu. When they reach the surface, Adam scares Engelbert and an old woman. They then reach the Throne Room where Cole frees Chompy and the dragon begins to play with Adam.

When the Army of Shintaro arrive to arrest Cole, Adam shoots a spiderweb at the guards and then begin fighting. During the fight, Chompy turns into a huge dragon allowing Cole and the Upply to flee.

The Upply Strike Back![]

Adam assists Cole and the Upply to free everyone by helping Plundar free the ninja.

The Son of Lilly[]

Adam helps everyone fight the Awakened Warriors. While he is spewing spiderwebs, an Awakened Warrior throws his spear at the cobweb, causing Adam to fall. However, he regains his balance and continues to fight. After Cole was able to unlock the Spinjitzu Burst, Vangelis, the Re-Awakened, and Grief-Bringer were defeated.

At the ceremony, Adam along with Chompy watch Vania become Queen of Shintaro. He then watches Cole leave along with the ninja.

Master of the Sea[]

Adam was with the Upply in the Kingdom of Shintaro's palace.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Unlike other giant spiders, Adam does not have a skull symbol on him.
  • His name may be based on the voice actor of Plundar, Adam Trask.
    • It also might be a reference to the alter ego of the title character of He-Man.
      • This would make sense due to the overwhelming number of references to that show in his debut, "Dungeon Party!."
  • He is the only known one of his species to be on the heroes' side.
  • In the Fighting Techniques, he is named Charlie.[2]
    • This is because he was previously going to be named Charlie.[3]
