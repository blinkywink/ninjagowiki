The construction workers are people that construct, clean, and work in Ninjago.

History

History[]

Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

Ninjaball Run[]

A team of construction workers tear down Grand Sensei Dareth's Mojo Dojo, only to realize that they did it on the wrong day.

The Stone Army[]

Several workers were cleaning up the Great Devourer's venom at the museum.

Rebooted[]

Blackout[]

Three workers found Cyrus Borg unconscious at Borg Tower. They later learned from Borg that the drive containing the Digital Overlord was missing.

Codename: Arcturus[]

A worker was painting lines on the road when Kai's Prototype X-1 and a Nindroid trick came zooming through the road, forcing the worker to jump out of the way.

The Titanium Ninja[]

A worker attended Zane's funeral at Ninjago Park.

Tournament of Elements[]

The Corridor of Elders[]

After Garmadon had himself banished to the Cursed Realm to defeat the Anacondrai Cultists, construction workers built a monument in the Corridor of Elders to commemorate him.

Skybound[]

Misfortune Rising[]

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

A construction worker appeared in a news report, looking down at another one of the chunks of Ninjago that had gone missing.

The Way Back[]

A construction worker watched alongside other citizens as Djinjago began to fall.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.


Hands of Time[]

A Time of Traitors[]

A few construction workers were among the citizens captured by the Vermillion to create weapons and armor for the Time Twins' plans.

Wu's Teas[]

Spinny Sign (non-canon)[]

Two workers watch Kai and the Robot Manager competing in a "Spinny-Sign Off".

Names (non-canon)[]

A worker picks up his drink at Steeper Wisdom.

Zaney Chess Game (non-canon)[]

Zane plays a game of chess against a construction worker, beating him easily.

The Taste Test (non-canon)[]

As Ronin thanks the citizens of Ninjago for winning the Golden Mug Award, a worker stands in the crowd.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Construction workers wear bright orange hats, pants, and vests with a long blue t-shirt underneath.
