Bone Guards are members of the Greenbone Warriors.

History

History[]

The Bone Guards, along with the Greenbone Warriors, used to reside in the Underworld, under the rulership of the Bone King. Following the Merge, some Bone Guards relocated to a forest with their leader, while some others relocated to the Crossroads.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

A Bone Guard was trying to register for the Mech-Master 5000. Another one (or possibly the same one) was preparing his Underworld Mech.

Crossroads Carnival[]

A Bone Guard was talking to two citizens as Lloyd, Arin, Sora, and Riyu decided to get some candy mushrooms.

Gangs of the Sea[]

Three Bone Guards and two Bone Warriors were with the Bone King when he activates the Walls of Punishment on Kai and Wyldfyre.

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

The three Bone Guards watched on in amusement as Kai and Wyldfyre struggled inside the Walls of Punishment, with two of them jeering at their foes. When the latter two escaped from the pit, the three Guards raised their swords and partook in surrounding the duo.

As Kai and Wyldfyre made their escape atop of Heatwave, the Guards fruitlessly gave chase and flung their swords at their escaping foes.

The Power Within[]

During the MergeQuake Storm, a Bone Guard and a Bone Warrior in the Crossroads were saved from an out of control truck by Zane. The skeletons recognized their savior and wondered aloud if the Ninja had returned.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon[]

After finding out that Ras had used the Forge of the Forgotten, the Greenbone Leader, accompanied by some Bone Guards and Re-Awakened, came to the Forge to stop him. However, the tiger wasn't impressed by this resistance and ultimately defeated them.
