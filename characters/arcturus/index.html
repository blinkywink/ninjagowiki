Overview

This article is about the Serpentine general. For the rocket, see Arcturus (rocket).
“Because of your friends, you have done what we never could: united the Serpentine and your kind as one. Because of you, the Balance is restored. And because of your father, our spirits can be free. You will have our eternal respect as the greatest warriors to ever battle for Ninjago.”
— Arcturus to Lloyd, "The Corridor of Elders"

General Arcturus was one of the Anacondrai generals and the leader of the Serpentine tribes alliance during the Serpentine War. Arcturus and the other generals led their kind into battle during this conflict, winning various battles, such as the battles in Jamanakai Village and Birchwood Forest. He proved relentless in his efforts to conquer Ninjago and defeat the Elemental Alliance until his adversaries found that the Sacred Flutes could hypnotize the Serpentine. As such, Arcturus' army was defeated and sealed away in various tombs, with his own kind winding up in a fang-shaped tomb in the Sea of Sand. Along with his fellow generals, Arcturus was found guilty of war crimes and banished to the Cursed Realm. Despite this, Arcturus' reputation was great enough that a celestial zodiac was named after him.

During the Second Serpentine War, Master Chen transformed his followers into fake Anacondrai, even going so far as to use Pythor to make the transformation permanent. Knowing that Arcturus and the generals wouldn't stand for Chen's actions, Pythor proposed releasing the spirits of the Anacondrai generals to curse Chen's army. However, this could only be done if the person who cursed them, Garmadon, sacrificed himself to take their place. Garmadon chose to do so with help from his son, Lloyd. Freeing Arcturus and the generals; they proceeded to curse Chen's army, with Arcturus personally denouncing Chen before banishing him himself. Arcturus then commended Pythor for his bravery by restoring him to his normal size and thanking Lloyd for restoring peace between humans and Serpentine. He proceeded to the Departed Realm with his fellow generals.

History

History[]

Serpentine War[]

Arcturus was a supreme general of the Anacondrai during a time in history when tensions were high between man and the Serpentine. He attempted to keep peace between both species and collaborated on a truce. However, Chen lied to Arcturus and the Anacondrai generals, claiming that the humans would use the truce to strike against the Serpentine, who - if they'd made concessions as the truce plans suggested - would face greater threat from a human attack. This news inspired Arcturus and the Anacondrai generals to reorganize the unified Serpentine forces - who'd previously engaged in mere skirmishes with human troops - in an effort to stop what he believed was an imminent threat to the Serpentine, while simultaneously securing the position of the Anacondrai and their fellow allied Serpentine tribes as rulers over Ninjago, a role in which they'd previously been dominant. Therefore, he ordered an attack on Jamanakai Village, where the Serpentine oppressed the villagers, and finally, when the Anacondrai army arrived, they defeated the Elemental Masters. Acidicus then approached Arcturus, who was standing on a destroyed chariot in front of his Anacondrai army, and told him that the Serpentines had taken control of the village. Arcturus told him that "only one can remain."[1]

Some time later, Arcturus led the Anacondrai army into a battle in Birchwood Forest against the Elemental Alliance led by Garmadon and Wu. The Alliance prevailed and overwhelmed the Anacondrai army for some time, and Arcturus ordered a retreat, but when the Elemental Master of Earth opposed the Alliance and betrayed them, Arcturus was the first to retreat, but later ordered his army to retaliate and eventually won the battle.[1]

Arcturus, confident after his victory, gradually began to conquer most of Ninjago until the Elemental Alliance created the Sacred Flutes. With the help of them, they then imprisoned all the Serpentine in five separate tombs and brought Arcturus and the other Anacondrai generals to court for their atrocities and war crimes. There, the supreme generalissimo of the Serpentine army argued that Master Chen shared the blame for his own manipulation of the conflict and Garmadon concurred with this statement, then informed the accused that the Court of Ninjago had passed a sentence on Chen. However, though the generals had not triggered the war, Arcturus and his comrades had been willing accomplices to its escalation. Thus, when the lead defendant pleaded for mercy, Mystake refused his request, and Garmadon then relentlessly banished Arcturus and the generals into the Cursed Realm.[1][2]

During the 40 years imprisonment in the Cursed Realm, Arcturus pondered and reflected on his actions and mistakes and eventually felt guilty of the atrocities and crimes, in the process changing his views and mind about the war.[3]

The Corridor of Elders[]

During the Second Serpentine War, Garmadon soon learned that Chen deliberately manipulated both the Serpentine and humans into attacking each other, and he was informed by Pythor that the only way to stop Chen and his army is to release the spirits of Arcturus and the other Anacondrai generals so that they can curse Chen and his army. Garmadon made the selfless decision to offer himself as a sacrifice to the Cursed Realm in order to free Arcturus and the generals. He understood that they could only be released if he took their place in the Cursed Realm, as he was the one responsible for banishing them there.

As Garmadon gets transported to the Cursed Realm, he informs Arcturus and the generals about what Chen did. With this information, Arcturus and the generals left the Cursed Realm and confronted Chen, who tried to plead mercy by claiming that they were on the same side. However, finally aware of Chen's dishonest and treacherous nature, Arcturus angrily refused his request by denouncing Chen as a lying and cheating fraud. Arcturus and the generals sent Chen and his followers to the Cursed Realm as punishment for their actions. Arcturus showed his gratitude towards Pythor, acknowledging his courage and bravery. He believed that Pythor had finally achieved something that no longer made him an outcast to his tribe and made his ancestors proud. As a token of appreciation, Arcturus restored Pythor to his normal size. Arcturus then extended his thanks to Lloyd, recognizing that it was because of him and the other ninja that the Serpentine and humans have been united as one, restoring balance. Arcturus also showed respect for Garmadon's sacrifice. He concluded by stating that Lloyd and his fellow ninja would always be respected as the greatest warriors to have fought for Ninjago by them. Arcturus and the other generals soon headed to a portal to the Departed Realm, finally at peace.

Legacy

Legacy[]

Decades after Arcturus's banishment, during the Nindroid crisis, Garmadon remembered facing Arcturus in battle, and Master Wu noted that a celestial zodiac had been named after him, but the two were unable to guess at the meaning behind the association of his name with the Overlord's project.

"Project: Arcturus" was eventually discovered to be a rocket named after the Serpentine general and a constellation with the same name, and the Nindroids were located at Ouroboros, where the Serpentine used to study the stars. The operation to manufacture the ship is lead and overseen by Pythor — the last survivor of the Anacondrai imprisoned after the war — so it is likely named after the late and deceased General Arcturus out of respect, tribute and admiration for him. The rocket carried the Nindroids and the ninja into space, where the Overlord hoped to obtain the Mega Weapon.[4] Arcturus, among the Serpentine, was highly respected not only by fellow Anacondrai, but by all Serpentine tribes who rallied behind him and the other Anacondrai in the Serpentine War.

Personality

Personality[]

Arcturus was the leader of the Anacondrai tribe which made clear his abilities to govern and his skills as a warrior.

During the Serpentine War, Arcturus was a very cruel and ruthless general who led the Serpentine legions into the war against humans, ordering his army to occupy human villages and cities and oppress their inhabitants. He followed the Anacondrai creed: "Only one can remain."

When the Serpentine were defeated and the war ended, Arcturus initially shifted the blame onto Chen, claiming that he and the other Anacondrai were deceived. After it was pointed out that in spite of Chen's deception, Arcturus and the others had chosen to instigate a war (at the time of the Anacondrai general's trial, it was not known how decietful Chen had been). Arcturus asked for the mercy of three judges, a request Mystake rejected, leading to the Anacondrai generals being sentenced with banishment to the Cursed Realm.

After 40 years inside the Cursed Realm, Arcturus realized his mistakes and reformed, and after his release by Garmadon, he banished Chen and his cult to the Cursed Realm for disgracing the memory of the Anacondrai. He then turned to the ninja and thanked them generously, calling them the greatest warriors of all time and saying that they would have the respect of the Anacondrai generals for eternity. He also thanked Pythor for helping to release them and told him that he had shown great courage, restoring him to his original size. Arcturus then finally rested and left for the Departed Realm satisfied with his fate.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Arcturus looked similar to other Anacondrai, which included purple scales all over his body, a long tail, and a snake-like head with four white fangs, but with green eyes instead of the usual red and marking patterns on the back of his head and neck. He also wore an Anacondrai vest surrounding a green gem on his chest, with a similar gem atop his forehead. Arcturus also wore gold shoulder armor, contrasting with the dark red armor worn by the other Anacondrai Generals.

As a ghost, Arcturus was almost completely transparent and surrounded by green mist, and the skin of the visible part of his tail appeared to be white with purple marking.

Weapons and abilities

Weapons and abilities[]

Abilities[]

  • Anacondrai Physiology: Being a Anacondrai Serpentine, Arcturus has the following abilities like his snake species.
    • Anacondrai invisibility: Arcturus' primary ability allowed him to turn invisible, although he was never seen using it.
    • Prolonged lifespan: Like the rest of the Serpentine, Arcturus had prolonged lifespans.
    • Fire resistance: Like all Anacondrai, Arcturus' hides were immune to fire.
  • Strategy: Arcturus was an excellent strategist at the time and was able to win many victories with his strategy and plans.
  • Combat capability: Arcturus was a very good combatant.
  • Ghost Physiology: As a ghost, Arcturus has the following abilities:
    • Flight/Levitation: As a ghost, Arcturus was able to fly and float.
    • Spectral Magic: When he became a ghost in the Cursed Realm, he was able to use magic.
      • Cursing the living beings: He and the other Anacondrai generals were able to curse living beings and banish them into the Curse Realm.
      • Enlarging living beings: He was also able to enlarge scaled-down beings to their original size.
      • Portal Creation: Arcturus was able to create a portal to head to the Departed Realm

Weapons[]

  • Anacondrai Blade: Arcturus proved to be a capable fighter with the Anacondrai Blade.

Etymology

Etymology[]

"Arcturus" is a Latin word, originally from Greek Arktouros, which means "bear watcher." It is also a name of the brightest star in the northern constellation of Boötes. However, his match with the star was most likely used when choosing a name for the character, as the celestial zodiac in Ninjago is named after him and he has nothing to do with bears.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Arcturus is the only seen Anacondrai to have green eyes rather than red. The reason for this is unknown.
    • However, Pythor had pink eyes in Season 1: Rise of the Snakes and Eyezor and Zugu had blue and yellow eyes in "Day of the Departed."
  • In some shot of Arcturus as a ghost, he appears to have no markings and gem on his head while the other generals do.
  • He and the other cursed Anacondrai generals are the only known ghosts to display the abilities to curse mortals and making something (Pythor in this case) bigger/returning it to its original size.
  • Despite being one of the generals of the tribe, he was never seen carrying the Anacondrai Staff during the Serpentine War, instead wielding an Anacondrai Blade in battle.
    • It is possible that this was because they were at war at the time, as no other general was seen with a staff in the flashbacks.
