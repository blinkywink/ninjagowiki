Overview

This article is about the dragon. For the set, see 71766 Lloyd's Legendary Dragon.

The Legendary Dragon is a green dragon in Ninjago who kidnapped Master Wu in his illusion.

History

History[]

Curiosity[]

After Nya opened the door to the Monastery of Spinjitzu, she was shocked to see Wu in the grasp of the Legendary Dragon. The ninja attempted to fight the dragon, but the dragon was able to deflect their attacks and escaped through a portal.

Balance[]

When the ninja arrived at the pyramid, the Legendary Dragon stood on the opposite side of the lava along with a lightning dragon and a fire dragon. The dragon, still holding Wu, then left in another portal.

Wisdom[]

As the ninja found themselves on the Speedway Five-Billion in Prime Empire, the dragon flew into another portal with Wu in its paw.

Generosity[]

Cole and Zane caught sight of the Legendary Dragon flying into the next portal with Wu.

Courage[]

The Legendary Dragon arrived at the Island of the Keepers, still holding Wu, in a force field. As the ninja fight the dragons in their way, the Legendary Dragon prepared to attack Wu, but was defeated by Kai and Lloyd. However, the ninja learned that Wu being kidnapped by the dragon was merely a test to teach them the Six Fundamental Virtues of Spinjitzu. Later at the Monastery, the Legendary Dragon bowed its head to Wu before leaving along with the Fire and Thunder Dragons, unbeknownst to the ninja.

Other appearances

Other appearances[]

I Just Had the Weirdest Dream (dream; non-canon)[]

In Kai's dream, Logan was riding the Legendary Dragon.

Ninjago - Dream Team (dream; non-canon)[]

A dream version of Lloyd rode atop the Legendary Dragon in Jan's dream. After arriving in the Farmers' village, Lloyd encouraged the dragon to leave while he and the others fought against the Nightmare creatures.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Dragon physiology- As a dragon, the Legendary Dragon has the following abilities:
    • Flight - The Legendary Dragon can fly using their wings.
    • Wing Barriers - The Legendary Dragon can use their wings as shields, as seen when they blocked Kai’s fire attack.
    • Life - The Legendary Dragon possesses the element of Life.
      • Ergokinetic Breath - The Legendary Dragon can shoot Energy blasts from their mouth.
