K.R.E.E.L. (Key Replica Ectomorph Embodying a Lady) was a female Nindroid created by Kreel and Lobbo using parts of a P.I.X.A.L. replica.

History

History[]

Zane's PIXAL Project[]

K.R.E.E.L. was crafted by Kreel using old Nindroid parts dumped in the junkyard by Alfonzo Frohicky. After hearing about the parts being dumped, Zane went to Kreel's Junkyard to retrieve them, where he saw K.R.E.E.L. for the first time, being shocked at the similarities between her and P.I.X.A.L.. Out of desperation, he begins attempting to upload his memories of her into K.R.E.E.L. hoping it would recreate P.I.X.A.L.'s consciousness. The first time he does this, it fails and only affects the basic programming, causing K.R.E.E.L. to begin throwing things at Lobbo and Kreel. As Kreel attempts to switch her off, Zane tells her to wait, instead trying to recreate P.I.X.A.L.'s consciousness a second time by inputting more data. As Zane keeps attempting to feed in more data, K.R.E.E.L. begins grabbing stray parts and connecting them together, slowly expanding her body, causing Kreel and Lobbo to leave the junkyard to go get someone that can help convince Zane to stop.

As they return with Cole, K.R.E.E.L. is now the size of a small mech, still causing havoc. As Cole takes Zane to the side to return him to his sense, Kreel and Lobbo enter their own mechs and begin circling K.R.E.E.L.. Kreel then tells K.R.E.E.L. that she no longer wants K.R.E.E.L. because she's ruining her reputation, and her and Lobbo both punch K.R.E.E.L. from different sides, causing her face plate to come flying off and for her to be ultimately destroyed.
