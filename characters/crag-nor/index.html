Overview

— King Crag-Nor, "Beyond Madness"

King Crag-Nor is the ruler of the Land of Madness and the King of the Craglings.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Beyond Madness[]

Crag-Nor addressed Nya and inquired about the other ninja. Nya introduced Lloyd, Arin, and Sora. Crag-Nor acknowledged that they were also ninja, assuming they have joined the Craglings' side in battle against the Earth Dragons. However, Nya revealed that they had actually fought against them and caused chaos, but she had managed to clarify everything. Crag-Nor understood that mistakes happened and emphasized the importance of accepting others despite their flaws. Suddenly, his attention was drawn to Riyu, a dragon, who was present with the group. Crag-Nor expresses his distrust towards dragons, deeming them treacherous and unwelcome in the village. Nya vouched for Riyu, stating that since the creature travels with her friends, he should be permitted. Crag-Nor, acknowledging Nya's loyalty and defense, allowed Riyu to enter but warned that they will keep a close watch on it. As a peculiar display, Crag-Nor removes one of his eyes, only to consume it and have it reappear in its socket.

Crag-Nor graciously invited the other ninja to partake in a traditional Cragling feast, known for being the best in the Land of Madness. Crag-Nor and Nya elaborate on how the Earth Dragons, deriving their abilities from an external force, consumed Cragling mud from the burial site of the ancient Craglings. Consequently, the Earth Dragons grew stronger while the Craglings weakened and perished. Kai's return inadvertently brought the Earth Dragons back to the village, resulting in a fierce battle between the ninja and the Earth Dragons. The Earth Dragons gain the upper hand and later started consuming the Cragling mud from the field, causing Crag-Nor to crumble and perish. However, Riyu's activation of the burial ground's energy causes a powerful burst that reverts the Earth Dragons to their original state, reviving Crag-Nor and the other Craglings. Crag-Nor was amazed to discover that Riyu was the one who saved them. Ultimately, Crag-Nor rewarded Riyu with a meal of Cragling mud as a token of appreciation.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Shape of Motion[]

After the Ninja discovered the mortally wounded Matriarch of the Mountain Dragons in the Kingdom of Madness, Sora and Arin called their allies for help. Crag-Nor was amongst those who responded to the call, leading a group of Craglings as they brought bowls of their sacred Cragling Mud to feed to the Matriarch in an effort to heal her wounds. Unfortunately, despite their best efforts, the Matriarch dragon still passed, leaving all those who had gathered, including Crag-Nor to mourn her passing.

When the Ninja vowed to return Eridany, the Matriarch's daughter, back to her tribe, Arin thanked the Craglings as they departed, with Crag-Nor waving goodbye.
