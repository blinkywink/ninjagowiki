Overview

This article is about the dragon. For the set, see 71809 Egalt the Master Dragon.
“It is good to be a warrior, but one day, you will make a great leader. Rontu, my companion of many centuries. The dangers we have faced. The life we have shared. As the seven Source Dragons are my witness, I would not change a moment of my time with you.”
— Egalt to Wyldfyre and Rontu, "The Hollow Ones"

Master Egalt was a dragon warrior from the tribe of the Northern Spiral and one of the two Dragon Masters. He was the second fang teacher for the Rising Dragon Technique that he invented alongside his partner Rontu, and taught to the ninja Lloyd, Kai, Nya, and Zane. At some point after Thunderfang's defeat, Egalt died from the Wasting Sickness and became a Departed Soul residing in the Spectral Lands.

History

History[]

Background[]

Originally part of the Dragons of the Northern Spiral, Egalt was mentored in the art of Spinjitzu by Ekesian Coldfang the Wise in his youth.[3] Egalt had a part in the War of Oni and Dragon as a low ranked soldier in the dragon army[1] during which he met a Forest Dragon named Rontu who became his companion for the rest of his life.[4] Together Egalt and Rontu became great recognized ninja warriors and as time passed, in turn rose to become martial art teachers known as the Dragon Masters, focusing on training dragons and occasionally humans in ninja skills. Unfortunately, Egalt's interactions with humans during training were consistently negative, leading him to develop a strong aversion towards them and decide to exclusively train dragons moving forward. The Dragon Masters invented their own technique derived from Spinijtzu and based on the concept of motion: Rising Dragon, which turned out to be a perfect counter to the Forbidden Five's Shatterspin, based on strength. As the Forbidden Five threatened to conquer the realms, Rontu and Egalt called for help from Gandalaria, the High Sorceress of Mysterium. The sorceress dedicated days to crafting a spell capable of banishing the Forbidden Five. With the spell completed, Egalt and Rontu unleashed the might of the Rising Dragon, successfully defeating the Forbidden Five and banishing them to the Nether-Space. In a final act, Egalt and Rontu erased all records of the Forbidden Five, sealing their dark legacy within the confines of the Shadow Dojo. His and Rontu's actions became tales among the dragons in the Wyldness that they even named a mountain after the former, Mount Egalt.

At some point, he ended up contracting Wasting Sickness.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

Egalt flew over the metal dragon-like door that Lloyd saw in his visions. He lands between the ninja and the door, preparing a blast of ice.

Force From the East[]

During the Test of Unity, Egalt engaged in a fierce battle with the ninja. The ninja realized that by placing heavy boulders in the bucket, the weight will pull down the chain attached to it, ultimately opening the gate. The ninja's Elemental Powers and Spinjitzu prove to be a major distraction for Egalt. Meanwhile, Wyldfyre, Sora, Lloyd, and Kai work together to place the boulders into the bucket, gradually opening the gate. As the team nears success, Arin attempts to add the fifth boulder but is unexpectedly grabbed by Egalt's claws and thrown aside. Quick on her feet, Nya stepped in and threw the boulder into the bucket, successfully fully opening the door. With the Test of Unity successfully completed, Egalt and Rontu congratulate the ninja for passing all three tests. Egalt, putting on his straw hat, took a moment to explain to Lloyd the nature of the three tests. He revealed that most people fail to overcome the Test of Fears, which involved navigating through the Phantasm Cave. With everything clarified, Lloyd inquired about the ancient teacher he had heard about, asking for their whereabouts. Egalt commented that Lloyd lacked intelligence, but Rontu kindly clarified that they were the exact teachers the ninja were searching for.

Arin was skeptical about the idea of dragons being ninja, but Egalt strongly believed that dragons are the ultimate ninja. Lloyd then shared a vision he had from the Source Dragons. Egalt immediately accused Lloyd of lying, stating that Source Dragons only intervene in serious mortal matters. Lloyd argued that this situation may be one of those serious matters, explaining that the ninja need to learn how to counter Shatterspin. Egalt questions why, as it had been forgotten. Lloyd revealed that Ras trained Cinder and planned to free the Forbidden Five. This shocked Egalt and Rontu, who later inform the ninja that they were the ones who defeated the Forbidden Five. Egalt continued, mentioning a powerful spell they used to exile the Forbidden Five from the realms. Recalling Lloyd's mention of the Wolf Masks, Egalt and Rontu note that those masks can only be from the Shadow Dojo, which they ensured could only be accessed with Source Dragon energy. Sora added that Ras may have obtained energy from the Source Dragon of Energy after persuading Imperium to hunt Source Dragons for him. Egalt pointed out that if a Source Dragon was captured, darkness would poison the world.

Lloyd believed that the Dragon Masters fighting alongside the ninja will ensure victory. However, Egalt and Rontu express doubts about teaming up with the ninja because of how old they are. Egalt questions Rontu's decision to train the ninja, calling the ninja weak. Despite Egalt reminding Rontu of his oath to not train humans due to a past incident, Rontu emphasized the urgency of the situation with the potential return of the Forbidden Five. She argued that everything they have worked for would be in vain if they do not act. Reluctantly, Egalt agreed to train the ninja, hoping he won't regret his decision. Arin's excitement was tempered by Egalt's warning about the dangers of overconfidence, which the Forbidden Five could exploit. Egalt acknowledged that the ninja had received good training but emphasized that they still have much to learn. He warned that failure on the part of the ninja could spell doom for the Merged Realms.

The Spell at the Waterfall[]

Following the ninja's combat displays, Egalt directed Lloyd, Kai, Nya, and Arin to accompany him for a lesson in Rising Dragon, as they are Second Fang Ninja. Nevertheless, Egalt believed it might be a waste of his time.

Egalt emphasized to the ninja that while strength held significance, intelligence, love, kindness, and motion were even more crucial. To illustrate the importance of motion, Egalt provided an example using the Rising Dragon technique, highlighting that when utilized correctly, motion could surpass strength in terms of power. Egalt advised the ninja to eliminate all unnecessary motion and strive to be as still as the mountains. Despite the ninja's belief that they have achieved stillness, Egalt countered by stating that the world and the universe were constantly in motion. Egalt further explained that even within the body, various organs are in constant motion. Motion, according to Egalt, was vital as life itself cannot exist without it. The ninja were encouraged to embrace and feel the motion within themselves. Finally, Egalt revealed that by aligning one's own motion with that of the universe, they would successfully execute Rising Dragon.

Egalt later instructed the ninja to execute Spinjitzu. He went on to elaborate on the concept of Rising Dragon, emphasizing the importance of being attuned to the surrounding motion and seamlessly adapting to the flow of others. Egalt emphasized that the ninja needed to push themselves beyond mere awareness and incorporate the understanding of motion into their Spinjitzu. However, Arin found the explanation confusing, causing Egalt to reaffirm his earlier statement about the training being a waste of time. Egalt urged the ninja to try again. Unfortunately, Arin's attempt went awry, causing him to collide with the other ninja. In an attempt to console Arin, Lloyd stepped in, assuring him that mistakes were acceptable. However, Egalt accused Lloyd of dishonesty, asserting that Arin's error was far from acceptable. Egalt went as far as suggesting that Arin be demoted to the rank of a First Fang Ninja due to his unstable and unrefined Spinjitzu skills. Arin pleaded for another chance, realizing the gravity of the situation as the fate of the Merged Realms hung in the balance. Egalt reminded Arin of the stakes involved and warned him not to repeat his mistake, demanding better performance from the entire team.

To Mysterium[]

While overlooking their training, Egalt notes Arin's negligence. During their break, Egalt was approached by Wyldfyre, who explains about her past before Egalt leaves, unknowingly leaving behind a scale he had shedded. On the lesson of throwing stones through hoops, Egalt was disappointed at Kai and Nya's turn turning into a sibling squabble. When it came to Lloyd and Arin's turn, the latter ends up throwing stones all around, one hitting Egalt in his right eye. Frustrated at the terms of no longer being able to see out of it anymore and refusing to accept his apology, Egalt harshly demotes Arin to First Fang.

Fugitives From Madness[]

While meditating, he is approached by Wyldfyre, who explained how the dragons who raised her told stories about him and Rontu. She then goes on about knowing the symptoms about his sickness, which he tried to deny, but was seen through, and provides him a stew to help ease his pain. Upon learning the Blood Moon is near, they immediately resume training with Nya requesting to join Kai in his training, which Egalt reluctantly accepted, until it proved a perfect feat as Kai achieves Rising Dragon. After promoting Kai to Third Fang and the Blood Moon approaching, Egalt expresses his mistrust was wrong before he and Rontu reluctantly tell the ninja they cannot help anymore with Egalt wishing the Source Dragons to be with them before they're both turned to stone.

Rising Ninja[]

After the Blood Moon passes, Egalt and Rontu revert to normal. Egalt expressed surprise that the world still remained and remarked that perhaps the Ninja didn't mess up.

Enter the City of Temples[]

Lloyd comes with the special soup from Wyldfyre for Egalt. While he and Rontu throw rocks at Zane to make him harmonize with the universe and master Rising Dragon technique. Their efforts pay off as Zane successfully executes the technique, prompting Egalt to reconsider his previous doubts about the ninja not being able to master it. The group then learned about the demise of three Arc Dragons and the potential threat posed by the killers, who are also participants in the Tournament of the Sources, suggesting they may target the Source Dragons next. Lloyd proposed that the Dragon Masters take on the role of mentors for the ninja during the tournament, but they declined. Egalt insisted that there was nothing more to teach the ninja and advised Lloyd to take on the responsibility of being their mentor himself.

When Egalt asked Lloyd if he believed he was a master, Lloyd insisted he was not. To test his skills, Egalt and Rontu decided to attack him, forcing Lloyd to evade their strikes. He met the challenge head-on, defeating both Egalt and Rontu. However, Egalt noted that they hadn't fought at their best due to their age and requested his stew back. Lloyd returned the stew, and Egalt noticed it was completely intact. After this, both Egalt and Rontu recognized Lloyd as a true master since he had passed their lesson.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Hollow Ones[]

At some point after Thunderfang's defeat, Egalt's illness progressed to its final stages, leaving him at death's doors. Hearing about this, the Ninja hurried to his side. Despite Egalt's lament that the dragons of his tribe won't be able to get there in time to honor him, he is relieved that he will manage to say goodbye to his former students. He is left saddened and heartbroken at Sora and Arin's absence, especially the latter, as, despite his harshness on him, he said he saw great potential on him while the saddest part of his last moments is that he never got a chance to apologize to the latter and urges Lloyd to not give up on his lost students. As Wyldfyre appeared very emotional about having to say goodbye to Egalt because she saw him as a hero, the latter reassured her, stating that all stories have an end and that he was going to move on to another stage of his existence and that. Egalt also confided to the young girl that she represented a real pride for her tribe and that one day she would not be just a warrior but also a leader. After dedicating his last words to Rontu, his lover and companion of many centuries, and she said goodbye to him in return, Egalt breathed his last. His spirit was then sent to the Spectral Lands, where his soul was absorbed by Thunderfang upon its arrival.[5]

Chaos Reigns[]

After Arin and Lloyd defeated Thunderfang using the Arc Dragon of Focus, the Shatter Dragon released a surge of energy that made him disappear with the Spectral Dragonians and the Forbidden Five, while the souls he had the Five collected were restored to the Merged Lands. Egalt's soul was released[5] and he chose to stay in the Spectral Lands as a ghost.[6]

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

Seed and Soil[]

When a departed soul was approaching them, Egalt informs Morro that he will handle it. The soul turns out to be Rontu, leading to their reunion before learning Riyu is dying and that she has failed. Egalt tells her it is not her time and that Riyu's life is important and only she can show him the way. He then tells Rontu to never lose hope before the latter's soul disappears and resurrects her.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Cryokinetic Attacks - Egalt was capable of attacking with ice.
    • Cryokinetic Breath - Egalt could shoot ice from his mouth at will.[8]
    • Cryokinetic Ball Projection - Egalt could shoot balls of ice.[9]
  • Ninja skills - Egalt had combat and martial art skills, being a martial art teacher and a former ninja dragon.
    • Spinjitzu - Egalt was able to perform Spinjitzu after learning it.[10][11]
    • Rising Dragon (formaly) - Egalt was able to perform Rising Dragon technique which he co-created with Rontu. As a master of this technique, he could use Rising Dragon without needing to perform Spinjitzu.[12]
  • Dragon physiology - As a dragon, Egalt had the following abilities.
    • Flight - Egalt was capable of flight using his wings.
    • Wing Barriers - Egalt could use his wings as shields, as seen when he blocked Kai’s fire attack.
    • Longevity - Egalt had been alive for millennia at the time of his death.
    • Bipedalism - Much like Jiro, Egalt could stand on two legs like a human.
  • Speech - Egalt was one of the few dragons capable of natural speech, unlike the Arc and Source Dragons, who communicate telepathically.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Egalt's design bears a striking resemblance to Wu's appearance in the WildBrain seasons of Ninjago. The color of Egalt's wings matches the shade of Wu's cape, both characters share prominent features such as large beards and bushy eyebrows, they both sport conical hats, Egalt's head and tail are coated in black similar to Wu's pants and undershirt, and the red garment adorning Egalt's waist mirrors the design of Wu's belt.
    • His wings were inspired by Wu's cape.[16]
  • He shares similarities to Wan Shi Tong from Avatar: The Last Airbender, as both are ancient mystical creatures that have a black and white body and share a distaste towards humans after past bad experiences.
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt first claimed that Rontu and Egalt both originate from the First Land[17] and later claimed that they were both born in the regions of their tribes, most likely meaning that their ancestors came from the First Land, which is the native realm of all dragons.[18]
  • When asked if Egalt and Rontu are officially married or are in some sort of dragon equivalent of a marriage, Chris "Doc" Wyatt replied, "Some equivalent."[19]
  • Egalt was top of his class in Math.[20]
  • Even though Egalt seems to breathe ice,[8] Chris "Doc" Wyatt suggested that it isn't the actual element.[21]
    • When directly asked in 2025, he simply answered "not revealed."[22]
  • According to Wyldfyre, Egalt is older than Rontu.[23]
  • Egalt is the strongest of the two Dragon Masters while Rontu is the fastest.[24]
  • If the Dragon Masters had a 10 round match in their prime, Egalt would have won 6 out of 10.[25]
  • When asked who would win a 10 round match between the Arc Dragon of Focus and Egalt with both in their prime, Chris "Doc" Wyatt stated the Arc Dragon of Focus would have a win ratio of 6:4.[26]
  • Egalt being a part of the Dragons of the Northern Spiral was first confirmed by Doc Wyatt on BlueSky in late 2024 before it was revealed in "The Hollow Ones."[27]

Relationships

Love Interests

Love Interests[]

Rontu[]

“Rontu, my companion of many centuries, the dangers we have faced, the life we have share, as the Seven Source Dragons are my witnesses, I would not change a moment of my time with you.”
— Egalt to Rontu, "The Hollow Ones"

Rontu was Egalt's lover and life companion. They lived together for centuries, have had adventures and fought together. They formed together the great Dragon Masters. As Egalt was dying of his Wasting Sickness, Rontu felt very sad.

Students

Students[]

Ninja[]

Egalt was at first hesitant about teaching Rising Dragon technique to the ninja because of their many failures in the past, but changed his mind after Rontu was able to convince them. At first, it started off being bumpy. After Arin blinded his right eye, Egalt demoted him to First Fang. However, his teaching paid off as Kai, Nya, and Lloyd were able to do Rising Dragon in their fight against the Wolf Clan. After the Blood Moon passed, Egalt, along with Rontu, successfully trained Zane. When Egalt was about to pass away due to his Wasting Sickness, he was glad to see the Ninja again one last time (even though he didn't train Cole). But when Lloyd told Egalt that Arin and Sora were with Ras, he was heartbroken because he regretted being too harsh on Arin not because of his failures, but because he had potential and tells Lloyd not to give up on him.

Allies

Allies[]

Wyldfyre[]

While Egalt (and Rontu) didn't train Wyldfyre due to her broken leg, she was the only one who understood him and she also gave him the Janus Melon Stew to slow down the Wasting Sickness when she found out about it. She also told Egalt stories about legendary dragon warriors and how a mountain was named after one of them.

Morro[]

Egalt appeared to be aquatinted with Morro. Upon noticing Rontu's soul, Morro attempted to guide her but Egalt told Morro he can handle her and told Morro to rest.
