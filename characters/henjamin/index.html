Overview

Henjamin[1] is a competitor of the Tournament of the Sources, alongside the other Elemental Masters of his generation.

History

History[]

They Gather for the Feast[]

He arrived in a hurry after Lloyd defeated the attacker bot.

Inside the Maze[]

Henjamin was training with the other Elemental Masters before the start of the first game.

United We Fall[]

He was cheering from the stands when Sora defeated Kizzy in a battle.

Truth and Lies[]

He watched the match between Euphrasia and Zeatrix.

The Sword Shatters[]

Following the match between Frak and Zur, Henjamin was seen sitting beside Wyldfyre and enjoying a snowberry pie.

Later, during the intermission during Lloyd and Zeatrix's match, Henjamin and Ellie were seen walking past the ninja and carrying snowberry pies.

The Final Game[]

Henjamin is strutted by Wyldfyre after she stole Bleckt's ledger and gets confused by its writings. Eventually, he met up with Teelk and became absorbed in conversation.

He later cheered in the stands during the final match of the Tournament of the Sources. Soon thereafter, he watched in surprise when Wyldfyre arrived with Zur to expose Bleckt after Nokt defeated Sora.

Elements of Betrayal[]

He and the other Elemental Masters fought against Cinder and the Wolf Mask Warriors while the ninja, Ras, and Roby go after Nokt and Rox. He later got his powers back after Roby used the Elemental Cup to drain Nokt of the stolen powers and return them to their rightful owners.
