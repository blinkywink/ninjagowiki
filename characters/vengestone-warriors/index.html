Overview

The Vengestone Warriors are soldiers that serve The Overlord. Like the Vengestone Brutes, they also lack shoulder armor, but have a unique torso compared to the other Crystal Warriors.

Trivia[]

  • They do not appear in the show, as in the show the army is entirely made of Vengestone Guards. However, they appear in Ninjago Magazine comics, likely meaning they could be considered canon to the show.
    • This makes them similar to Rivett, Vermin, Thunder Keepers and Claw Generals.
