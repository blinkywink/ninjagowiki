Overview

"I was bitten by a snake."
This article is about a subject that is relevant to both the main Ninjago storyline and The LEGO Ninjago Movie. Information may be more directed toward one continuity than the other.
“Chickens are fast and dangerous!”
— Jay to the ninja, "One Step Forward, Two Steps Back"

Chickens are animals that live in Ninjago, the Never Kingdoms, the Wyldness, and Prime Empire. Wu somehow obtained a chicken at some point after the Oni's invasion. Chicken meat is also a common food.

History

History[]

Since ancient times, chickens have inhabited the Mountains of Impossible Height. The first monks built the walls of the Monastery of Spinjitzu extra tall to keep them out. However, they failed and many monks were lost. In order to survive, the monks became faster, stronger, and more cunning, evolving into ninja.

Shatterspin[]

Sometime before the Serpentine War, Zaiden Kane obtained a chicken and named it Buck Buck.

After the Oni's invasion[]

Wu found a chicken in the area around the monastery upon seeing his ninja become lazy.[1]

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Wasted True Potential[]

Master Wu educates the ninja about of monks who became ninja and their encounters with peculiar animals like his chicken. He challenges them to endure five minutes with his chicken. As the box opens, Wu's chicken emits an electric glow. Despite the challenge, the ninja successfully capture the chicken and return it to the box within the given time. During a late-night snack raid, Cole discovers that Wu's chicken has infiltrated the fridge and attacks him. The next morning, after effortlessly defeating the ninja, Wu employs his chicken as a form of punishment.

Questing for Quests[]

Wu's chicken was pecking at the ground as Wu swept the area nearby.

A Rocky Start[]

Jay pursues Wu's chicken as it takes his travel pillow, which it had previously worn. Despite attempting to snatch it back, Jay manages to keep it out of reach.

Ancient History[]

A chicken was on one of the paperboys' bikes during the Pyro Vipers' attack.

Under Siege[]

Wu's chicken attacks a Pyro Viper in the kitchen.

Corruption (flashback)[]

Vex tried grabbing a chicken, but it managed to escape his clutches.

A Cold Goodbye[]

Lloyd attempts to retrieve the bag with Traveler's Tea, but encounters Wu's chicken instead. Despite trying to shoo it away, the chicken ends up knocking him out of the room, and was petted by Wu upon landing next to him.

The Kaiju Protocol[]

While working on a portal to the Never-Realm, Wu's chicken passes by its owner and P.I.X.A.L.

Prime Empire[]

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

A chicken wore a police officer's hat, with the owner chasing it.

Level Thirteen[]

A chicken was behind the bars at Kryptarium Prison.

Superstar Rockin' Jay[]

A chicken appears on top of a building where the League of Jay were fighting aerial drones.

I am Okino[]

A chicken was with Okino as he trained.

The Speedway Five-Billion[]

Among the spectators at the Speedway Five-Billion, a chicken stood in the crowd. The chicken was taken aback when the announcer declared that the final team to arrive was the Fast Chickens, who were tardy for the race.

Ninjago Confidential[]

Milton Dyer fed a chicken while talking to P.I.X.A.L..

The Prodigal Father[]

Fugi-Dove was feeding a group of birds including a chicken.

Game Over[]

Alongside the ninja, avatars, and NPCs, the chicken departed from Prime Empire.

Master of the Mountain[]

Shintaro[]

Wu's chicken is with Cole as he plays Prime Empire, but struggles to play itself before Lloyd comes in and decides to play it. It later chases the Postman away.

The Island[]

The Tooth of Wojira (flashback)[]

A chicken was used as one of the sacrifices that the Keepers gave to "Wojira."

Uncharted[]

A chicken is seen as one of the Kryptarium inmates that escaped.

Seabound[]

A Big Splash[]

When the ninja fight Miss Demeanor's henchmen, there is a chicken on the rice fields. Later, when the villagers run away from the village, the chicken also runs away and falls into Jay's hands.

The Call of the Deep[]

Wu's chicken perches on Nya's bed as Maya enters to inspect before tidying up the room, prompting her to shoo the bird away despite its indignant clucking.

Papergirl[]

After the paperboys left to deliver newspapers, a chicken closed one of the doors of the warehouse.

The Calm Before the Storm[]

A chicken was at Ninjago Harbor until it was blown away by the wind.

The Turn of the Tide[]

A chicken attacks a Maaray Guard and was later present at Nya's memorial.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • When Cole was hypnotized by Skales in a commercial, he was imitating a chicken.
  • The Sky Pirates couldn't deny chicken legs as being a delicious snack.[citation needed]
  • The Dragon Hunters use chicken legs as bait in the sets to capture dragons.[citation needed]
  • The Never Kingdoms have a special kind of chicken with white feathers and blue elements.
  • One of the Formlings can turn into a chicken.
  • "The Master" features a chicken that served as a model for Wu's chicken.[2]
  • Chickens have appeared in every Ninjago season animated by WildBrain.
  • As shown in concept art, the chicken was originally supposed to be white and more reminiscent of real LEGO chicken.
  • An unknown chicken was imprisoned in Kryptarium Prison and escaped during the escape of the prisoners. It is unknown what crime it committed.
  • All known chickens have been hens so far, except for the metal symbol of a rooster on Twitchy Tim's gas station and Buck Buck.
