Overview

The Dragonian Warriors were a battalion of Dragonian soldiers. They were previously made up of those who have been corrupted to serve the Forbidden Five as their army during their quest for the Prismatic Blades alongside Tyr. Following Thunderfang's release, some of the warriors were purified, but those who died while still corrupted became Spectral Dragonians,[2] assigned with stealing souls for Thunderfang.

History

History[]

The Spectral Lands[]

After emerging from Thunderfang's prison back into the Storm Village, the Forbidden Five shattered a gem given to them by Thunderfang in order to release its dark energies. These energies then corrupted Tyr and many other Dragonians, transforming them into an army to serve the Five.

The Great Zane Robbery[]

Dragonian Warriors accompanied Nokt in his search for the Red Prismatic Blade. Raiding the Freebooters' train, the Dragonians fought against Rapton and his Claw Hunters while Nokt searched for the blade. Upon the arrival of Zane and P.I.X.A.L., the Dragonians fought against them as well and later pursued the pair outside and atop of the train. As the battle continued outside, one Dragonian was instructed to damage the train's operating controls while the rest fought against their enemies. After Nokt's retrieval of the prismatic blade, all Dragonians retreated alongside him.

Meanwhile, several Dragonian Warriors accompanied Rox to the Algae farmers' village, where they attacked the farmers while she located the Green Prismatic Blade underwater in the crab tooth.

I Alone Can Save Them[]

Kur led several Dragonian Warriors to the City of Temples, where they fought off the Security Bots, while she gathered the remains of the Yellow Prismatic Blade. Two of the warriors restrain Roby so Kur can use her Decay power on him.

Zarkt led several Dragonian Warriors to search for the Blue Prismatic Blade in the First Land. The warriors fought Riyu, Sora, and Frak while Zarkt fought Lloyd, although they failed to get the blade when Zane, Zanth, and P.I.X.A.L. arrived to help.

Fallen Wishes[]

Rox led several Dragonian Warriors to Djinjago to force Arrakore to fix the broken Yellow Prismatic Blade. After Arrakore tricked Rox into wishing for the Ninja's presence, some of these Dragonians became victims of Sora, Nya, and Riyu's wishes. In particular, Sora focused on making weird and funny wishes, wishing for one Dragonian to be "light as a feather," resulting in him floating off from the battlefield, and for another's helmet to be on backwards. Nya's wishes on the other hand were significantly more harmful, wishing for one Dragonian to be struck by lightning and for another's bones to be made of jelly. As for Riyu, he only wished for one Dragonian to become trapped inside a rubber ball.

The Dragonians would be shortly and easily routed by the three ninja, no thanks to their wishes, but were able to recover following Rox's entrapment of Arrakore. After Sora freed the Djinn however, the Dragonians found themselves once again outmatched and soon retreated under Rox's orders, though the warrior encased in a rubber ball and the one whose bones were turned into jelly did not have these effects undone as they retreated and had to be carried off by their comrades.

Crashing Together[]

Drix led several Dragonian Warriors to the World Forest where they fought the Ninja and helped to reclaim the Blue Prismatic Blade and the Returning Blade. Under Drix's orders, they captured Ras and his hammer as well.

Chaos Unchained[]

Drix and his platoon of Dragonians arrived at the site of Thunderfang's prison, only to be surprised by the presence of the Ninja, who had somehow beaten them there. These Dragonians then fought against the Ninja and were soon backed up by the rest of their brethren, including Tyr, as the entirety of the Forbidden Five joined the battle. During the battle, Nokt relieved Ras and his hammer from the custody of Drix's platoon, who then rejoined the fight against the Ninja.

The Shatter Dragon[]

The Dragonian Warriors continued to fight the Ninja, battling the bounty hunter Rogue as well until Arin threw the healing elixir sourced from the Well of the Lost at Thunderfang. As a result of Thunderfang's death, the warriors were returned to their peaceful selves and apologized to the Ninja for their actions.

Prior to The Hollow Ones[]

After Thunderfang's demise, he seized control of the Spectral Lands and gathered an army of ghosts. This force included the Forbidden Five and the Dragonian Warriors who had perished in their corrupted forms due to the Sobrient Stone. The fallen warriors transformed into Spectral Dragonians, acquiring traits and powers similar to those of the Soul Suckers.

Human Resources[]

When the Spectral Dragonians led by Kur launched their second invasion of Mysterium, the Dragonian Warriors terrorized the inhabitants while siphoning their souls. One of these warriors managed to steal a soul from a Devonian. As another Dragonian Warrior attempted to attack Gandalaria and Ras, Gandalaria defended herself by firing banana peels from her crystal ball. Despite her efforts, the warrior pressed on with its assault. However, as dawn broke, the Spectral Dragonians were forced to retreat, taking the stolen souls with them. The following night, they returned to invade the Administration.

Casket of Bones[]

The Spectral Dragonians accompanied Drix to the Administration, with two of them being Dragonian Warriors. During this encounter, they captured the souls of several agents, including Underwood and a Munce agent. Subsequently, they returned to the Spectral Lands with Drix, where they were met by other Spectral Dragonians and the Forbidden Five.

The Screaming Earth[]

The Spectral Dragonians made their way back to the Administration, continuing their mission of stealing the souls of the Administration Agents. They pursued Agent Roderick Allen through a portal that led them to the Land of Lee. When Sora activated her Prismatic Lamp, the light revealed the Dragonian Warriors and Scouts emerging from the portal.

Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon[]

The Spectral Dragonians turned out to be led by Zarkt in stealing the souls of everyone in the Land of Lee. Using her Prismatic Lamp and Technology power, Sora managed to spread the prismatic energy over the Community of Lee, allowing everyone to see the Spectral Dragonians. As the ninja retreated, many Spectral Dragonians attacked them but the group managed to get away on the Temple Bounty.

When Zarkt was finished, the Spectral Dragonians returned to the Well of the Lost, where he adds in the stolen souls. Three of them noticed Lloyd, Nya, Sora, Wyldfyre, Arin, and Jay in his Mech Dragon. More Spectral Dragonians arrived to fight them off but thanks to Wu, the group were able to escape to the Monastery of the Spectral Lands.

The Vault of Focus[]

Several Spectral Dragonians were led by Nokt to Imperium, where he steals the souls of its citizens. When Cole attacked him, two Dragonian Warriors held the ninja as they drained him of his soul. Kai and Frak arrived with their new Prismatic Blades, the Reveal Blades, to destroy the Spectral Dragonians, causing most of them to retreat. Nokt noticed this and attempts to fight both ninja but Kai threw his Reveal Blade at his magical flute, which releases most of the stolen souls.

For Whom the Bell Tolls[]

When Thunderfang was still powering himself with the soul energy, some Spectral Dragonians were flying around him.

When Doves Cry[]

Several platoons of Spectral Dragonian were led by Nokt, Rox, and Drix to guard the Spectral Lands and fought Arin and Lloyd. But due to their mechs being coated in Prismatic Ore, they manage to hold them off before escaping into a cavern that leads to the Well of the Lost.

Many Spectral Dragonians followed Kur to the Cave of Bells in Mysterium, although because of their spectral nature, Rapton and the Freebooters only saw Kur.

Chaos Reigns[]

Zarkt and Kur lead their respective Spectral Dragonian platoons to each Cave of Bells to destroy the focus crystals but Frak and Kai used their Reveal Blades to destroy the Spectral Dragonian.
