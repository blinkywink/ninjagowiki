Overview

This article is about the security bots in Kreel's Junkyard. For other uses, see Security Bots.
“Access denied. Junk master Kreel is busy. No customer service again until this afternoon.”
— A Security Bot to Zane, "Zane's PIXAL Project"

Security Bots are surveillance robots that patrol Kreel's Junkyard.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

The Security Bots caught Arin and Sora trying to steal a piece from the Crossroads' junkyard. As the trespassers tried to escape, the Security Bots blocked the exit and tried shooting them. To distract the bots, Arin used his Grapple Gun to swing over the robots while Sora hacked into one of them, making them dance.

Crossroads Carnival[]

A few days later, the owners of the Crossroads' junkyard gained possession of the SA Mark 01, having their Security Bots dance around the display as they celebrated the Crossroads Carnival. However, when Riyu used his dragon power on Sora to activate her Tech Power, the Security Bots ran away.

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

The Security Bots all stood with Kreel when a disguised Zane and Alfonzo Frohicky arrived to get a component, only for the Security Bots to throw them out when they didn't have money. One Security Bot judges the Zane-alike contest during Zane Day.
