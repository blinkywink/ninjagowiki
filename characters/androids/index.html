This article is about a subject that is relevant to both the main Ninjago storyline and The LEGO Ninjago Movie. Information may be more directed toward one continuity than the other.

Androids are highly advanced built robots, some of which include Nindroids and former android-like NPCs from Prime Empire.

History

History[]

Wu's Teas (non-canon)[]

Spinny Sign[]

Robot Manager stole Steeper Wisdom's customers with her ability to spin a sign. Kai competed with her in a spinny sign off but burned his shop in the process. Robot Manager saved it by fanning the tea shop with her sign and put out the fire on Wu's clothes with tea.

Trojan Tea Kettle[]

Robot Manager got a giant teapot from "Floyd." Knowing that it was a trap, she threw it into the dumpster, unknowingly giving away the secret ingredient to make her coffee taste good: bananas.

Undercover Zane[]

After serving a customer, Zane, disguised as a maintenance bot, visited the coffee shop to freeze her espresso machine. She ignored her lie detector and believed it when Zane said he grew a mustache due to an upgrade, allowing Zane to complete his mission. Robot Manager served another customer cold coffee; when she said she liked it, she told everyone about it, earning her shop recognition.

Zaney Chess Game[]

Cole told everyone that whoever can beat Zane in a game of chess will win free tea. After she witnessed Zane beating several players at a game of chess, the Robot Manager challenged him. The audience was shocked at seeing she won, but when she outstretched her arm to get her tea, Zane's queen fell out of her sleeve. She was eventually thrown out for cheating.

The Taste Test[]

The Robot Manager entered a tasting competition against Steeper Wisdom. Her coffee won, but Ronin, on the other team, cheered. After Wu asked him why so, given that they lost, he claimed he owned both shops and that the prize, a golden mug with a base, was actually a regular mug spray-painted with wood glued on.

A Beautiful Friendship[]

Wu invited the Robot Manager inside Steeper Wisdom. After giving her coffee beans and asking for friendship, to which she accepts, they found a stand selling free juice. They went out together with the Manager asking him if he thinks they have fresh-pressed oil.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Unlike the Nindroids, who are more advanced, regular androids can't eat or drink.[1]
    • Androids also lack souls, as seen with the Security Bots not being targeted by the Spectral Dragonians, unlike nindroids like Cryptor being sent to the Departed Realm.
  • The android-like NPCs from Prime Empire became real androids when they came into the real world.[2]
