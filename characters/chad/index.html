Chad is a student and cheerleader at Ninjago High School. He is known to be Maggie's friend and one of the more vocal bullies of Lloyd Garmadon.

History

History[]

The LEGO Ninjago Movie[]

During the Good Morning NINJAGO news intro, Chad can be seen helping rebuild the city. When Lloyd gets on the School Bus, Chad looks at him in disgust as he walks down the rows of seats. After Lloyd sits down and looks the other way, Chad along with everyone else has moved to the opposite side of the bus to keep their distance from him.

Later, Chad and a couple of cheerleaders see Lloyd and his friends and show their new cheer called "Boo, Lloyd!" Lloyd sarcastically replied that they have a #1 hit on their hands, but they take it seriously and soon sang it on the radio right before Garmadon attacks the city with his Shark Mech. "Boo, Lloyd!" would later have a remix that plays on the radio before Garmadon attacks the city again with his new Garma Mecha Man.

He is later seen near the end of the movie among happy citizens exclaiming his excitement after Meowthra eats Garmadon, but then shocked after Lloyd revealed himself as the Green Ninja. Chad then watches Lloyd Garmadon confront Meowthra and apologize to Garmadon, which leaves him sympathetic for him.

Official description

Official description[]

Chad[]

A confident cheerleader at Ninjago High School, Chad is no fan of Lloyd. He even goes so far as to start a mean-spirited school cheer that he calls "Boo Lloyd"![1]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He calls Lloyd and his friends "Garmadork and the Dork Squad."
  • He is listed as "Chen the Cheerleader" in the credits due to this being his name in the script.
    • Chen is also the name of the main antagonist of Season 4.
  • Tommy Andreasen sees Brad Tudabone as his TV series counterpart.[2]
  • He has the same hair as Johnny and Olivia, originally used on the Zombie Businessman from Minifigures Series 14.
  • He uses the same lowered-hood piece as Wyldstyle from The LEGO Movie, also appearing in the Ninjago TV series with Harumi's casual design.
  • His minifigure has a few differences from his final movie design. He has no lowered-hood piece, his pants are a different shade of blue without pocket printing or shoes, his eyebrows are also black instead of brown along with his hair being dark brown instead of light brown.
  • There's a female version of Chad that uses the same torso, hips and legs design along with his hairpiece in the background of the school yard.[3]
