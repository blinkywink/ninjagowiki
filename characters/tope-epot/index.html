Tope-Epot is a Mucoid and a citizen of the Crossroads.

History

History[]

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

Tope-Epot participated in Zane Day's Zane-alike contest, wearing a Zane mask and wrapped in foil. While he did well in imitating Zane and throwing ice cubes at targets, when it came to the last round, he didn't perfect his toaster and ended up in second place, losing to Lobbo.
