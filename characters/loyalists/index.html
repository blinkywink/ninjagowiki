Overview

“I have been to the abyss and back, and have the scars to prove it, but you, and only you, my Loyalists, never wavered in your faith in my almighty power. You will stand by my side when I retake the kingdom that is rightfully mine!”
— Beatrix to the Loyalists, "Dying to Live"

The Loyalists were a clandestine terrorist sect that comprised of Imperian supporters and sympathizers of Beatrix, the former and disgraced Empress of Imperium. Led by Dr. LaRow, these individuals were organised into a secret society that aided the former empress' campaign to reclaim the Imperian throne using Elemental Monsters as their pawns.

After their plan failed with Beatrix reduced to a comatose state and several Loyalists arrested, the surviving members secretly went back into hiding where they devised a new plan: rebuild General Cryptor so he can help them find a way to transfer Beatrix's mind into Zeatrix's body and sabotage the Summit of the Merged Lands.

History

History[]

Background[]

After the Liberation of Imperium, the transition from a kingdom to a democratic state led to a divide among Imperians.[1] Though a majority of them argued that the move from dictatorship to civilian leadership was naturally a difficult one, but felt it was worth it, many others were skeptical about the ninja's role in freeing the Kingdom of Imperium from Beatrix's reign, believing their actions ultimately contributed to the kingdom's decline and that the Empress had actually brought prosperity to the civilization, unlike the struggles faced under Senator Percival Tartigrade.[2][1] Whereas some elements of the population that felt deprived of their purpose under the new administration, such as Rapton and the Claws of Imperium, decided to leave New Imperium and pursue alternate prospects abroad,[3] other Imperians were convinced that the rule of Beatrix[4] — though brought about through patricide and conspiracy[5] — had been essential to the continuing glory of the kingdom that they coveted. Sentiments like these were also shown by Dr. LaRow after the disgraced former head scientist of the Advanced Systems Lab fled the Kingdom, believing the loss of Beatrix had deprived her of everything,[4] despite defying the Good Empress' wishes in the final hours of her regime when instructed to drain the captured Source Dragon of Energy, and being aware of the potential threat that such a process could pose to Imperium, which the former tyrant had disregarded.[5] After the nation began to reorganize itself beneath a new senate, LaRow became convinced that she would be able to shackle Beatrix's worst impulses once the former monarch was reinstated. She tracked Beatrix down to a location at the outskirts of Umbrasilvis, where she was able to save the former empress — now a permanent cyborg — from life-threatening injuries through experimental medical treatments and rededicated herself to the monarch, though some doubt about her allegiance remained.[4]

Having found other Imperians who were recipient to a return to life beneath Beatrix through violent tactics, LaRow established a secret society called the Loyalists in order to restore the overthrown monarch to power. Among the Loyalists were Sora's parents,[6] while LaRow herself became the Chief Loyalist. Moreover, the Chief Loyalist travelled to an unknown realm she could never retrace her steps back to and discovered a tablet that detailed a primordial artifact called the Weapon of Desolation, which the First Spinjitzu Master had split apart centuries ago. At LaRow's orders, some of the Loyalists spread themselves out across the Merged Lands in order to infiltrate key organizations over several months, such as the Administration, Borg Industries, and the Explorer's Club, with one of them even taking charge of Umbrasilvis.[7][2] Meanwhile, some Loyalists remained within Imperium, acting as moles to support their cause from the inside,[8] while others inhabited facilities belonging to them, which required a specific handshake between the guard and arrival before the latter would be permitted access. To coordinate their efforts, the secret terrorists established a hideout inside Old Town of Ninjago City, where their leader utilized holographic technology to communicate with her underlings alongside Beatrix, who also relocated there.[9]

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

After the Source Dragons of Focus, Life, and Strength withdrew themselves from the council as retaliation for the ninja awakening the Arc Dragon of Focus without their consent, the imbalance in Source Energy led the Weapon of Desolation to create Elemental Monsters. Seizing this opportunity to empower Beatrix with the means to reclaim Imperium, the Chief Loyalist directed her peers to seek out the weapon to take control of the Elemental Monsters.[9]

The infiltrating Loyalists used their positions to find both halves of the Weapon of Desolation. The ones assigned at the Administration successfully discovered the yellow half hidden at the Tree of Madness underneath a lead member of the organisation that lured out a team of Administration Agents with sleeper operatives mixed into their numbers in order to seize their mechs. Having already determined the whereabouts of their target inside the Tree of Madness, to reach its location, the Loyalists required the mechs to excavate an entrance into the cavern, so members of the organisation inside the field team, including Bonnie, dropped their covers and helped seize the convoy, driving off the Administration Agents.[7] Meanwhile, Johnny Courageous, leveraging his cover within the Explorer's Club, joined Reginald Slitherbottom's expedition to Umbrasilvis to analyze a Mutation Monster, which would lead him to the second half in the Tree of Burdens. Unbeknownst to Johnny, he received covert help from Sora, who manipulated the situation to identify who was responsible for unleashing the monster and recognised him as another native of Imperium, exposing his allegiance to the Loyalists and bringing him before the Grand Inquisitor in accordance to the Grand Inquisition, unaware that its judge was also an undercover sleeper until he announced his intent to deliver the fragment to Beatrix. After the Inquisitor donned a mask and delivered a brief declaration of his relief at the end of his assignment inside the miserable backwater, both he and Johnny managed to escape with the red fragment, hijacking an airship that the Explorer's Club used, though this escape also made the ninja present aware that Beatrix had returned to again threaten the Merged Lands.[2]

Meanwhile, inside Ninjago City, other members of the Loyalists based at their hideout collected analyses from where another Elemental Monster had manifested within the Crossroads. However, these suspicious activities drew attention from residents of the Monastery of Spinjitzu and Cole followed one back to the hideout, but LaRow discovered the Elemental Master of Earth and took him captive with assistance from several henchmen before he could access the base.[7] Upon receiving word that both halves of the Weapon were en route, Beatrix recounted her experiences after the MergeQuake Storm in front of LaRow and six other members, speculating the individuals who would become Loyalists had shared the belief that their empress had perished. As Beatrix detailed her time within the Land of Monsters and subsequent reunion with LaRow, the listeners were recipients of praise from their empress, who acknowledged their unwavering devotion to her would be rewarded, promising the Loyalists a place at her side when she retook Imperium. Afterwards, LaRow briefed her comrades about progress made in the Quest for Desolation and the successful retrieval of both halves, explaining that the completion of the Weapon would afford the Loyalists control over the Elemental Monsters, which Beatrix followed with a declaration where she described the revenge she would then enact against those who once betrayed or questioned her with no quarter. In response, the Loyalists affirmed their allegiance to her, hailing Beatrix as the Good Empress in a collective chant. Though the Loyalists that garrisoned this instillation proved unable to defeat Cole when he tried to escape, Beatrix was able to overpower him and had her people restrain him when the agents from Umbrasilvis arrived to deliver the second half of the Weapon.[4]

With the Weapon of Desolation completed, the Loyalists joined Beatrix as they advanced toward the City of Imperium, using the Elemental Monsters as the front guard for their assault after the citizens declined the chance to surrender. Regardless of the nostalgia some inside Imperians had expressed from the old rule, when confronted with the actual prospect of renewed life underneath Beatrix, all that had not pledged their allegiance to the Loyalists collectively decided to stand against them, but their defences had been weathered from a previous monster attack. Members of the ninja that had clashed with followers of the Empress in the aforementioned search for the Weapon tracked their enemies back to the siege and protected the citizens of Imperium from the impending threat, while Cole — who had been brought to watch Beatrix reclaim her crown — was able to break free of his restraints and divert attention from the attack for a short time, though most of the Loyalists held back as the Elemental Monsters engaged the ninja. Although Cole escaped his confinements and bought Percival and his scientists enough time to reactive their defenses, the covert Loyalists within the city sabotaged the energy barrier and created a breach that allowed Beatrix and the majority of her forces to invade, despite hinderance from Nya and Sora.[8] Dr. LaRow led the Loyalists in the ensuing battle against the ninja and Imperium citizens, but as the conflict escalated and the rest of the ninja arrived, she soon suspected that the Weapon of Desolation was exerting control over Beatrix, revealing powers that the Loyalists had not anticipated, though some remained confident that Beatrix had planned this all. Lloyd managed to wrest the weapon from Beatrix's grasp, while Arin deactivated it with a reverse incantation of the spell used to recreate it, leading to the erasure of the Elemental Monsters, though some of the stragglers opened fire on the ninja as the team reunited, prompting them to fan out to shut the Loyalists down.

After Beatrix was incapacitated by the enhanced Earth Monsters, she was left in a coma and placed in Borg Tower's Medical Suite for treatment. The ninja apprehended Dr. LaRow and several other Loyalists, transporting them to Imperium Prison as Senator Tartigrade ordered the city to be scoured for the other revanchists, expressing the need to find all individuals who might still be loyal to the Empress. However, thanks to the concealment that their standard uniform afforded them, countless Loyalists were able to evade capture, including Johnny Courageous and Wally. Under interrogation, LaRow admitted to orchestrating the entire insurrection, but Sora voiced astonishment that Beatrix had found support from such a large number of their countrymen, despite her crimes.[6][10] Meanwhile, Sora's parents also slipped through the cracks, changing out of their uniforms and blending into the nearby crowd that celebrated witnessing Wyldfyre and Percival capture another follower of the Empress, before moving to the Monastery of Spinjitzu with the ninja to avoid the authorities and begin reconciling with their daughter, though the couple retained their masks, which they kept hidden.[6]

Despite this, not all the Loyalists that had been able to avoid detention were content to retreat belowground. As the City of Imperium recovered from their attack, several individuals who remained devoted to Empress Beatrix reintegrated themselves back into the civilian population, some undercover inside construction crews assigned to repair a section of the wall that their insider operatives breached.[11] Aware that remnants of the Loyalists continued to persist, Senator Tartigrade remained determined to hunt down all the surviving members and have them face justice, but their elusive nature made this task difficult.[12]

Learning about the Summit of the Merged Lands, Dr. LaRow initiated Operation Mentax, intending to sabotage the event while kidnapping Zeatrix and switching her mind with Beatrix to revive the latter from her coma. Her main motivation for doing so stemmed from the belief that a system like the Council of the Merged Lands that the Summit proposed threatened to dilate power, weakening its influence until the realms collapsed; though LaRow could mastermind events and manipulate them to suit her own ends, by her own admission, the former Chief Loyalist lacked the power to inspire others like she believed Beatrix could. Because she was incarcerated, she devised a plan to reconstruct the Nindroid General Cryptor, intending for him to lead the Loyalists in her place during the operation. Infiltrating the Imperium Guards at Imperium Prison, Johnny Courageous secretly passed on Dr. LaRow's orders to the remaining Loyalists while Wally stole the schematics for rebuilding Cryptor from Borg Industries and handed them over to his comrades, who then completed the reconstruction inside their hideout, learning from a preview of the Intelligent George broadcast that the older ninja had departed from a mission elsewhere.[10][13] However, as an organizer of the Summit, Senator Tartigrade suspected Imperium loyalists would seek to disrupt the conference, which unexpected input from Zeatrix later confirmed. While this evidence cast doubt onto the allegiance of the Guards, the insertion of sleeper agents also extended to the civilian staff, which a number of Loyalists infiltrated.[10]

The Loyalists seized a section of the Cloud Kingdom, which had separated from Ras' earlier attack, and transformed it into a factory to repair the Nindroid MechDragon, which would be the end game for Operation Mentax. P.I.X.A.L. — the sole member of the older ninja to have remained behind — learned of this and attempted to confront Cryptor in secret, but was severely damaged by the Nindroid MechDragon and left broken.[13] In the following days, Cryptor stole a map of the underground passages from the Explorer's Club to locate the Mind Crystal. Armed with the map, Cryptor and the Loyalists began their search in the Ninjago Sewers, where they encountered and captured Frak and his father Reginald. Although the two managed to escape, the revived Nindroid and the Loyalists successfully acquired the Mind Crystal before escaping in their hover chariots.[14] Meanwhile, after learning from her sources that the Imperians still dedicated to her much-despised twin sister wanted to sabotage the event and had been able to compromise the security, Zeatrix resurfaced to align with the ninja and protect the population, unaware that she was playing right into the enemies' hands.

To get Beatrix out of Borg Tower, the Loyalists sent a giant Cocoon with little Cocoons in it to ambush Percival and disrupt the media-covered arrival of delegates from the other realms, with each of the smaller robots having a homing device added inside by Cryptor. As the ninja, Zeatrix, and the Imperium Guards got distracted by the Cocoons, the Loyalists seized this opportunity to get the comatose Beatrix out of Borg Tower with inside assistance from Wally, whom Cyrus Borg caught on the job. Donning a mask as he revealed his real allegiance, Wally declared his informal resignation and fled into the night alongside the other intruders and their comatose monarch.[10] Afterwards, other Loyalists, including Squad 5512-A, went to Kreel's Junkyard to steal various devices and other components to make a mind transferring machine. Despite the ninja's attempt to stop them, the Loyalists got away with some of the stolen goods and went back to their hideout in the Ninjago Sewers, unaware that they dropped one of their communicators.[15] With the parts they stole, they made the machine. Meanwhile, at the Monastery of Spinjitzu, some of the residents began to suspect that Sora's parents were members of the Loyalists — albeit inactive ones — after Jenny Nived glimpsed one of their masks before the couple could hide it, which she and her husband Barry told their son Arin about, though none of them were confident enough to confront the two outright.[16]

Trivia[]

  • In "Can You Dig It?," there was another group of Imperians led by Dr. LaRow that have allegiance to Imperium when it was a kingdom, even though their goal was to establish Dr. LaRow as the ruler instead of Beatrix.
  • It is unknown if the different types of Loyalist masks denote anything.
  • In the Arabic, Turkish and Latin American Spanish dubs, some Loyalists have a different voice actor while their identities remain secret. This is the case with Dr. LaRow and Johnny Courageous, unlike in the original version, where they retain the same voice even before their identities are revealed. A similar approach was taken in the original version when Harumi was using the Kabuki Mask persona.
  • The Loyalists have a different hood in the sets compared to the show.
