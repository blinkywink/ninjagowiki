Overview

“I have been to the abyss and back, and have the scars to prove it, but you, and only you, my Loyalists, never wavered in your faith in my almighty power. You will stand by my side when I retake the kingdom that is rightfully mine!”
— Beatrix to the Loyalists, "Dying to Live"

The Loyalists are a faction of Imperian supporters and sympathizers of Beatrix, the former Empress of Imperium. Led by the Chief Loyalist, they aid the former empress's campaign to reclaim the Imperium throne using Elemental Monsters as their pawns.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

After the Liberation of Imperium, Dr. LaRow escaped the kingdom and allied herself with Beatrix, assisting her in her quest to reclaim her throne. This alliance gave rise to the Loyalists, Imperians determined to support Beatrix in her efforts. Dr. LaRow became the Chief Loyalist, taking on the leadership role within the faction. At her orders, some of the Loyalists infiltrated key organizations that would allow them to achieve their goals, such as The Administration and the Explorer's Club, with one of them even infiltrating and taking charge of Umbrasilvis. After the Source Dragons of Focus, Life, and Strength withdrew from the council, the disrupted Source Energy created Elemental Monsters. Recognizing their connection to the Weapon of Desolation, the Chief Loyalist directed her peers to seek out the weapon to take control of the Elemental Monsters.[1]

Following her orders, the infiltrated Loyalists used their positions to find both halves of the Weapon of Desolation, with the ones infiltrated on the Administration finding one of the halves at the Tree of Madness and the one infiltrating the Explorer's Club and joining Reginald Slitherbottom's expedition to Umbrasilvis using a Mutation Monster (and some unknowing help from Sora who used it to bait him to reveal himself) to find the location of the other half at the Tree of Burdens. However, while the Loyalists at the Kingdom of Madness managed to successfully retrieve their half, even with the interference of Kai, Nya and Jay, the one infiltrating Reginald's expedition, falls to Sora and Frak's trap, with them being able to retrieve it instead. Nevertheless, both the half of the weapon and the captured Loyalist are recovered by the one infiltrating Umbrasilvis.
