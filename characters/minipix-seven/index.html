MiniPix Seven is the last intact MiniPix created by P.I.X.A.L..

History

History[]

The Fall of the Monastery[]

MiniPix Seven was used to attack the Council of the Crystal King along with the other MiniPix to prevent them from accessing the Golden Weapons. Only MiniPix Seven survived and was able to save Wu, Skylor and P.I.X.A.L., but was unable to locate Nya and was presumed to have been killed by the destruction of the Monastery of Spinjitzu, until she had survived with her Samurai X Mech.

Darkness Within[]

MiniPix Seven fixed the equipment in the Samurai X Cave, and managed to restore the power. It was then introduced to the ninja by P.I.X.A.L., who revealed that she named it due to it saving them from the explosion, and shook Zane's hand when he greeted it.

Crystastrophe[]

MiniPix Seven monitored one of the computers in the Samurai X Cave.

A Lesson in Anger[]

MiniPix Seven assisted P.I.X.A.L. in studying the fragment of infectious crystal and then helped develop and install upgrades to the ninja's vehicles. When the work was completed, MiniPix Seven accompanied P.I.X.A.L. up to the main platform of the Samurai X Cave.

Compatible[]

MiniPix Seven completed assembly and programming for the Ninja Ultra Combo Mech inside the Samurai X Cave. After being contacted by P.I.X.A.L., MiniPix Seven activated the mech and flew it towards the Newspaper warehouse for the final battle.

Dragon Form[]

MiniPix Seven delivered the completed Ninja Ultra Combo Mech to the Newspaper warehouse so that P.I.X.A.L. could use it in combat against the Crystal army.

Roots[]

MiniPix Seven assisted the ninja in rebuilding their Monastery, and was seen bopping back and forth as it listened to Sally's singing.
