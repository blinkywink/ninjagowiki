This article is about a subject that is canonical to the storyline of Warner Bros' The LEGO Movie franchise.

Blacktron Fan is a student at Ninjago High School who formerly lived in Bricksburg.

History

History[]

The LEGO Movie[]

The LEGO Ninjago Movie[]

When Lloyd arrives at the bus stop, Blacktron Fan starts texting with everyone else at the stop about how they dislike Lloyd before getting on the bus. He can see getting off the bus at Ninjago High School and then a moment later looking at Lloyd in disgust with the other students until Zane arrives. He is later among the crowd of shocked people when Lloyd reveals himself as the Green Ninja.
