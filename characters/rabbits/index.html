Overview

"Hey, where'd you guys come from?"
This article is about a subject that is relevant to both the Ninjago and Legends of Chima franchises. Information may be more directed toward one theme than the other.

Rabbits are animals which live in Ninjago, the Never Kingdoms, and the Wyldness.

History

History[]

Never Trust a Snake[]

Wu read a book to Lloyd about a fictional rabbit named Jack which was betrayed by a snake.

The Hands of Time[]

A rabbit ate a flower while Wu visited the ruins of the Monastery of Spinjitzu.
