Dream-Guyvers[2][3] are creatures from the Land of Monsters[1] that are capable of making anyone experience their deepest fears by putting them in a trance, as well as brainwashing individuals.

History

History[]

Background[]

A long time ago, the Dream-Guyvers came from the Land of Monsters. At one point, one was captured by the Dragon Masters and imprisoned in the Phantasm Cave located in the Wyldness. The Dream-Guyver became the first test for anyone wanting to train with the Dragon Masters, forcing them to face their deepest fears. Unfortunately, many who faced the Dream-Guyver were unable to overcome it.[4] Another would be put in the Land of Lee, where it would brainwash people into thinking Lee is perfect.

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

The ninja fell victim to the Dream-Guyver as they entered the Phantasm Cave en route to the Training plateau of the Dragon Masters. While under the effects of the creatures' powers, each ninja witnessed their worst fears materialize before their eyes. Nya managed to awaken and escape from the creature's powers however by rejecting her vision as being untrue, allowing her to momentarily release the other captive ninja by striking the Dream-Guyver with a bolt of water and causing it to flee.
