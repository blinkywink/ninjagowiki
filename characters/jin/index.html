History[]

The End Begins[]

Sometime in the distant future. Jin, while riding his dragon, lands on top of the transformed Dragon's Blade and Jin removes his mask to get a better look at something he seems to be staring at.

Epilogue[]

Sometime after that, Jin would climb onto Kai's old Titan Mech and he looks out from his home at Ninjago City, which he is just outside of.
