Overview

The Source Dragon of Balance was one of the seven Source Dragons. Hence their name, they represented the source of Balance, having served as the foundation for all the Elemental Powers related to their source.

History

History[]

Background[]

The Source Dragon of Balance was among the seven Source Dragons that helped maintain balance across the realms. Each Source Dragon represented a unique source,[5] with the Source Dragon of Balance specifically embodying balance.

The Source Dragons faced a significant dilemma concerning a promise to the First Spinjitzu Master, which, if broken, could jeopardize the realms by threatening the cycles.[6] Ultimately, the Source Dragon of Balance, favoring mortals, cast the deciding vote. This decision, however, led to Thunderfang, feeling spurned by the council's rejection of his ambition to become a Source Dragon, planning to overthrow the Source Dragons.[7] When Thunderfang attacked the City of Temples, the council decided to send the mortal avatar of the Source Dragon of Focus, the Arc Dragon of Focus to stop the Chaos Dragon. After completing its task, the avatar of the Focus Source Dragon was split into two halves, which were put into a deep slumber.

The First Spinjitzu Master would later implored them to contribute their dragon energy. This request was made in order to create the Dragon Cores, which were meant to prevent the Coalescence,[8] an imminent event where all the realms would merge into a single world, a consequence brought about by the Source Dragon energy.[9]

At some point, a tree in Ninjago became charged with the Source Dragon of Balance's dragon energy and released it in the form of resin that hardened and fossilized into amber, which was eventually extracted by a Metalonian blacksmith and used to create Chronosteel and with it, the elemental power of Amber.

At some point before the Merge occurred,[10] the Source Dragon of Balance was killed while in their mortal form.[11][12] Their corpse then eventually ended up buried in a desert in Imperium. Wishing to be unearthed, their still sentient corpse called out to Cole, resulting in the earth screaming. As the MergeQuakes intensified, they warned Cole that time was running out.

Legacy[]

Years after the Source Dragon of Balance's death, the Source Dragon of Motion alerted the ninja that whoever was responsible for killing three Arc Dragons might soon target the Source Dragons. She recounted that the Source Dragons, despite their revered status, are not as powerful as many might assume when confronted with danger, including the murder of the Source Dragon of Balance. This left the ninja in disbelief, as it was the first time they had heard of such an act against the Source Dragons, despite being aware of the capture of the Source Dragon of Energy.

Cole followed Master Wu to where the corpse was. After finding the point where it was the loudest, he tried using his Earth power on it, but to no avail. It was later unearthed by the combined efforts of Cole and Frak, who used their Elemental Powers of Earth and Quake to unearth the corpse whilst Kai used his Fire to destroy rocks that were preventing the bones from rising out of the ground. It disappeared soon after. Wu's specter form appeared to his students, telling them that the skeleton was lost but now it can be found.

After Thunderfang's return and his ambition to become a Source Dragon by collecting souls in the Spectral Lands, the Source Dragons found themselves in a stalemate over whether to engage with him. The Source Dragons of Life and Strength argued that their promise to the First Spinjitzu Master had led to the conflict with Thunderfang. Strength pointed out that he had refused to grant gifts to the First Spinjitzu Master, while the Source Dragon of Motion reminded everyone that their agreement to assist the First Spinjitzu Master stemmed from the favor the Source Dragon of Balance had towards mortals, which had been the deciding factor. With Balance now deceased, the Source Dragons faced a critical choice about whether to confront Thunderfang or not.

After the ninja called forth the Arc Dragon of Focus, the Council had a fallout. Angered, Focus, Life, and Strength chose to leave the council, withdrawing their energy from the Merged Realms.[13][14] This broke their promise to the First Spinjitzu Master and triggered a chain reaction due to the withdrawn Source Energy, including the destabilization of the realms[15] and imbalance of Elemental Powers. The remaining council members recognized that the mortals would ultimately suffer the consequences of their brethren's actions.[6]

At some point after, the acolytes at the Shrine of the Seven Sources explained to the Chief Loyalist that each column represented the presence of a Source Dragon's power in the Merged Lands, but the column for Balance was inactive because the Source Dragon of Balance had died. They reassured her that this issue could be resolved when the cycle resets. However, before they could elaborate on what would happen after, the Chief Loyalist interrupted them.[13]

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Transcendent Dragon Physiology - As a Source Dragon, the Source Dragon of Balance was a dragon of godlike power that existed beyond concepts like Time, Space, Reality, and Dimensionality unless they took their mortal form,[16] and was comparable to Greek gods in terms of power and abilities.[16]
    • Immortality - As a Source Dragon, Balance couldn't be killed unless they take on their mortal form, which unfortunately led to their downfall.[12]
    • Telepathy - Similar to few other dragons, the Source Dragon of Balance possessed the ability to communicate with other beings by using telepathic powers. They retained such a power after death.
    • Retrocognition - As a Source Dragon, they were capable of viewing and perceiving the past.
    • Precognition - As a Source Dragon, they were capable of viewing and perceiving the future.
    • Balance Embodiment - As the Source Dragon of Balance, they represented and embodied the balance in the Merged Realms.
    • Draco-Ergokinesis - As a Source Dragon, the Source Dragon of Balance was capable of manipulating their dragon energy.
      • Elemental Power Creation/Linkage - By embodying Balance, the Source Dragon of Balance serves as the foundation of various Elemental Powers, with their energy being integral to the source of Balance. They are connected to several Elemental Powers of which they are the source, including Golden Power (with their siblings),[1] Balance,[2] Shadow,[3] and Amber (which was created by the Original Master of Amber using Source Dragon energy from a tree than the Source Dragon of Balance themself).[4]
      • Sparks - Like all dragons with a high level of dragon energy, the Source Dragon of Balance had the ability to share their energy to an Elemental Master. By being a Source Dragon, they could also designate the Elemental Master they prefer to act as a conduit for their dragon energy.
        • Elemental Power Augmentation - By sharing their dragon energy with an Elemental Master, Balance could increase their powers.
        • Power Bestowal - By sharing their dragon energy with mortals, Balance could bestow some of their abilities to them.
          • Staterakinesis Bestowal - By allowing mortals to use their dragon energy, the Source Dragon of Balance could grant them the power to use Balance.
          • Precognition Bestowal - Conduits of the Source Dragon of Balance gain their power of precognition in the form of visions of their future.
    • Dimensiokinesis/Realm Manipulation - The Source Dragon of Balance was endowed with all the characteristic abilities and attributes of a typical member of their species, thereby conferring upon them a position of immense power and influence over the realms.
      • Dimensional Travel - The Source Dragon of Balance had the ability to travel dimensions to hide and transport themself and other living beings they take with them.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Their death was hinted at in "The Battle of the Second Monastery" when the Source Dragons' symbols appeared before Lloyd, in which, according to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, was intentional that the Source Dragon of Balance's symbol was absent.[18]
    • Their death was also hinted at in "Land of Lost Things," when Cole was able to feel the earth "screaming", and then in "Absolute Power," when he left to follow a clue from Master Wu. It turned out that the earth’s scream was due to the presence of the remains of the deceased Source Dragon of Balance wanting to be unearthed.
  • When a fan inquired about the fate of the Source Dragon of Balance, asking if it traveled through the Spectral Lands after its death, Doc Wyatt responded, "How do we know that?",[19] suggesting that the Source Dragon of Balance may not truly be dead. However, he later acknowledged the death of the Source Dragon of Balance when someone mistakenly interpreted the open space in the Source-Henge as a sign of an eighth Source Dragon, in which he noted that there are now six Source Dragons due to Balance's departure.[20][21]
  • Currently, the Source Dragon of Balance is the one with the least confirmed elemental powers connected to their source, with a total of three confirmed.
    • Since two of their elements have been confirmed as still being in use (and one had even been transferred to a new master), it's confirmed that their death didn't cause their elemental powers to vanish.
  • The Source Dragon of Balance is currently the only known Source Dragon with no known mortal dragons that possess an Element connected to their source.
  • When asked the other Source Dragons didn't use their power to revive Balance, Chris "Doc" Wyatt stated, "Revive? Not how it works."[22]
  • Despite having died before the Merge,[10] the Source Dragon of Balance seems to be connected to the key to understanding it.[23]
  • They do not have control over the concept of the Balance, despite their name.[24]
  • When asked what happened to the mortal avatar of the Source Dragon of Balance, Chris "Doc" Wyatt replied, "GREAT QUESTION(S)-- but I sorta don't want to answer for now. Sorry."[25] This somewhat implies there is an Arc Dragon of Balance.
    • The answer was also given where a Source Dragon goes when they die?[25]
  • Many people appear to be unaware of the Source Dragon of Balance's death, as some still refer to their group as if there were still seven Source Dragons, including Egalt and Thunderfang, who are usually very well-informed about the Source Dragons.
    • It's unknown if Ras is even aware of Balance's death or not, as he noted to Arin that the power of all seven Source Dragons are needed to reverse the Merge.
