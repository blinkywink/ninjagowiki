The Ice Dragons are dragons native to Imperium. Two of them moved to the Cloud Kingdom after the Merge. After the Battle of the Second Monastery, one of them decided to move to the Monastery of Spinjitzu with the ninja.

History

History[]

Background[]

Two Ice Dragons originated from Imperium.[1] However, after the Merge united their realm with the Cloud Kingdom, they felt that their previous home was no longer safe, owing to the fact that Imperium had embraced dragon hunting as a means to acquire their energy as power for their civilisation. Therefore, they chose to relocate to the Cloud Kingdom, which they perceived as a more secure place to live.[2]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Writers of Destiny[]

The Ice Dragons fought against the tentacled beast when it arrived from a MergeQuake. After the battle, they were captured by Rapton when the MergeQuake reopened and the ninja were distracted.

Mindless Beasts[]

The Ice Dragons were freed from their cell by Lloyd and Wyldfyre.

I Will Be the Danger[]

Released from its cell, the Ice Dragon joined the other dragons in attempting to escape from the City of Imperium. It later fought against the Claws of Imperium and their new Photacs outside of the gates to the city.

The Calm Inside[]

The Ice Dragon continued to battle the Claws Hunters. When Wyldfyre communicated with the dragons, they all followed Jiro and Heatwave to the palace where they attempted to blast the palace down. The Ice Dragon and the Earth Dragon fired after Jiro and continued to battle the Claw hunters but was pin down when the Monastery of Imperium rose from the ground with the other Ninja Kai, Nya, and Zane joining the fight.

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

As the Ice Dragon continued to battle the Claws of Imperium, the Ice Dragon was attacked by Ras before the Fire Dragon pinned his Photac down. After they returned to the Monastery of Spinjitzu, an Ice Dragon decided to stay with the ninja in the stables.

The Temple of the Dragon Cores[]

Zane rode on the Ice Dragon as the ninja stopped a MergeQuake and saved some citizens in the Crossroads. Later, he rode the dragon to the Temple of the Dragon Cores.

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

The Ice Dragon was sleeping in the Dragon stables while Zane was inspecting the Portal gate.

The Elemental Mechs[]

A Pain in the Mech! (flashback)[]

Two Ice Dragons attacked attack the Lava-Tides in the Crossroads because they were carrying a crate that had their eggs inside. After the eggs were returned, the dragons ceased their attack.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Ice Dragon living in the monastery was sleeping in the dragon stables while Wyldfyre, Kai, Nya and Frohicky were attempting to travel to the Monastery of Gates[3], as well as when Zane and P.I.X.A.L. were charging the portal gate to go to the First Land.[4] The Ice Dragon was also resting when Rontu moved to the dragon stables, and watched the Dragon Master explain her decision to Riyu.[5]

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Ice - The Ice Dragons have control over the Ice element.
    • Cryokinesis Breath - They can breathe and shoot ice blasts from their mouths
  • Flight - Like most dragons, the Ice Dragons can flight using their wings.

ICE Dragons are Dragons in LEGO Fortnite Odyssey.

Official description[]

Dangerous, vicious foes descended from an ancient Dragon clan in the frozen wastes of Imperium. Normally benevolent, something has made these dragons aggressive.[7]

Spawning[]

ICE Dragons can be found on Ninjago island after opening 3 to 5 Chaos Portals.
