Overview

“The Administration isn't a police force. They're a bureaucracy. Lying doesn't require a license or a permit so they don't care. In fact, they lie too... tons.”
— Jay to Lloyd, Nya, and Wyldfyre, "Between You and Lee"

The Administration Agents are a covert group of bureaucratic operatives responsible for the management, operation and control of the Administration.

History

History[]

Background[]

At an unknown point in time, The Administrator founded the Administration and recruited the Administration Agents.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

They Call It Doom[]

During an examination of the Mother Garden's legality, Denholt and Karit unexpectedly encountered Rapton, leading to the discovery of the pink Dragon Core. They swiftly incapacitated Rapton using a taser and promptly retrieved the Dragon Core. With the core in their possession, they returned to the Administration.

Prior to The Administration[]

After detecting several portal activation attempts, Roderick Allen and SsssSsss Underwood investigate and discover it was Zane, who was trying to activate the portal gate in the Monastery of Spinjitzu and detain him.

The Administration[]

Allen and Underwood come across Lloyd and Arin, demanding their forms before the latter tried to stall, until a tube containing papers arrive and they see they are forms with drawings of the two. Clearing them, Allen and Underwood head to their cubicle to file paperwork about the misunderstanding, until Underwood discovered the deception upon seeing a subject had a photo before filling out a later date form in order to search for Lloyd and Arin, who eventually went to the mail room, where Zane was placed in. Allen and Underwood attempt to detain them, but Arin threw unsharpened pencils he was collecting at them to distract them before using the tube to reach the demolition level, where the Dragon Core was deemed a dangerous artifact to be destroyed, until Underwood managed to separate them.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Fugitives From Madness[]

Administration Agents, led by Agent Rodrick Allen and Agent SsssSsss Underwood, came to the Monastery of Mysterium for an equipment reclamation operation. Their main target was Zane, classified as stolen equipment, scheduled for retrieval and incineration. The agents formally demanded the group surrender, but Zane stalled them by exploiting gaps in their paperwork, repeatedly rejecting their breach authorization forms for lacking proper appendices and triplicates. As the agents were forced into bureaucratic back-and-forth, the fugitives prepared defenses. Once the forms were finally in order, the Agents launched a full assault, using Master Mechs to break through the monastery gates. A fight broke out between Gandalaria, Zane and Cole against the Administration agents. Bonzle tried to escape through underground passages, but was intercepted by Agent Jay Walker.

Secrets of the Wyldness[]

They fight against Zane, Cole, and Gandalaria, but the agents struggle to maintain control. Agent Allen focuses on deactivating Zane, while his partner, Agent SsssSsss Underwood, tries to enforce their regulations. The agents are continuously outmaneuvered by the ninjas' clever tactics and Gandalaria’s crystal ball. Agent Walker, who tries to capture Bonzle, becomes distracted by Sprite, leading to a interaction where he reveals his lightning powers. Despite his efforts, Bonzle escapes, leaving Walker to question his own job. Back outside, the agents' Mechs are easily knocked over by Cole and Zane, but Agent Allen continues to fight, even as Zane is temporarily powered down. Eventually, Zane reactivates and successfully hacks into the Mechs, causing chaos for the Administration. Despite the agents' persistence, the Ninja, including Bonzle and Gandalaria, manage to escape on Jiro.

The Shape of Motion[]

Agents Allen and Underwood are stuck on security duty at the Administration. Underwood eagerly looks for dissent, while Allen complains about the lack of action. Arin and Sora sneak into the Department of Reassignment, hoping to find info on Arin's missing parents, but the records are offline. Just as they realize they've missed their chance, Allen and Underwood show up, ready to capture them. The agents threaten Arin and Sora with blasters and paperwork, but the duo escapes using Arin's Grapple Gun and smoke bomb. Allen and Underwood are left frustrated as Arin and Sora make a narrow escape.
