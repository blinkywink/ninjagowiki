Overview

“But a child arrived, born of both worlds. The child understood the power of both. Without one, you could not have the other. But when the Oni and the Dragons fought over which side the child should choose, the child abandoned their world to start a new one called Ninjago.”
— Mystake about the First Spinjitzu Master, "The Oni and the Dragon"

The First Spinjitzu Master was an powerful being who was the creator of Spinjitzu, the island of Ninjago, the realm of Imperium,[6] and three other realms.[7][8][9] He served as an intermediary between the Source Dragons and the mortals,[10] was the father of Garmadon and Wu, Misako's ex-father-in-law, and Lloyd's paternal grandfather.

He was born from the worlds of both the Oni and the dragons. Unwilling to choose a side within the war, abandoning his original realm and creating some of his own.

After defeating a dark entity of pure evil, the Overlord, and splitting the land into two halves (Ninjago and the Dark Island), he fathered two sons, Wu and Garmadon, and entrusted them with defending the Golden Weapons. Shortly before his demise, he sealed himself into a tomb deep in the Endless Sea, where the Realm Crystal was also stored away.

After the Oni were vanquished by the efforts of the ninja and his sons, the First Spinjitzu Master summoned Lloyd to the Grasslands in order to thank him for saving Ninjago.

History

History[]

Early life[]

Long before time had a name, the First Spinjitzu Master was born in the Realm of Oni and Dragons. There, he was a hybrid of both the Oni and the dragons.[11] At some point, he bonded with the mother of all dragons, the Firstbourne. Together, they created the Dragon Armor, and sought to unite the two races of the realm, and end their long war.[12]

However, after the two sides refused to end the war and began to fight over which side he should choose, the First Spinjitzu Master realized that even he could not end the conflict between the two races.[11] Devastated, he took off the Dragon Armor and created the Realm Crystal to leave the Realm of Oni and Dragons and went on to find a new home, leaving Firstbourne behind to guard the Dragon Armor and hoping for his return.[11][12][13]

The First Spinjitzu Master used the Realm Crystal to traverse all the Realms of Creation,[14] except the Departed Realm.[15] While traveling throughout the realms, he eventually went to the Never-Realm and nearly found himself trapped there until he somehow found a way back.[14]

At an unknown point, the First Spinjitzu Master would meet the Seven Source Dragons and made a promise to them to receive Elemental Powers from them to share with mortals. After receiving most of the Elemental Powers, he mastered Golden Power at some point.[16][17]

Eventually, the young master finally found himself in a realm of a vast ocean engulfed in chaos, caused by the sea serpent Wojira who ruled the sea and winds. With the help of the islanders and Merlopians, the First Spinjitzu Master engaged in a battle with Wojira, but she was too powerful even for their combined efforts. Nyad, the first Elemental Master of Water merged with the ocean by sacrificing her physical body to defeat the serpent. The First Spinjitzu Master then gave the Storm Amulet to the leader of the islanders and the Wave Amulet to the king of the Merlopians.[18][19][20][21] The Spinjitzu Master later created a scroll that is a complete list of the elemental powers, but with no mention of Water and Wind. He would later tell his sons an old fairy tale about how he mastered all of the elemental powers, but never Wind or Water.[2]

Forming Ninjago[]

With the seas now calm and the realm at peace, the First Spinjitzu Master forged the four Golden Weapons of Spinjitzu in the Temple of Light using gold from the Golden Peaks. With the weapons, he created the island of Ninjago, and transplanted creatures across the other realms, such as the Serpentine from the Wyldness, to populate his new land. In addition to these, he also created Imperium[6] and three other realms.[7][8][9] Ninjago was in harmony for a while, but darkness eventually manifested to counterbalance the light of the land. From the darkness came an evil spirit called "the Overlord," who challenged the First Spinjitzu Master to a battle for the fate of the world.

The First Spinjitzu Master fought the Overlord for a long time, with neither gaining the upper hand. Eventually, the Overlord created an indestructible army to aid him, nearly overwhelming the First Spinjitzu Master. To save the world from the grip of darkness, the First Spinjitzu Master used his powers to split Ninjago in two, destroying the Overlord's physical form and trapping the spirit on an island what would come to be known as the Dark Island. This act balanced the forces of light and shadow, ensuring that the Overlord could never return to Ninjago.[22]

With the Overlord defeated, the First Spinjitzu Master was able to seal away the dormant Stone Army in an indestructible tomb, which he buried deep beneath what would eventually become Ninjago City.[23] At some point he traveled to the Dark Island to hide his Golden Mech in the Temple of Light in preparation for the day that that his successor would come to claim it.[24]

After the Stone Wars[]

Note: The placements of the First Spinjitzu Master telling Wu about the Never-Realm and his fishing trip with his sons are unconfirmed, but the former is placed after the Devourer's bite due to it being shown before the Devourer when Wu gets his memories back.

One day, while the First Spinjitzu Master was deep in meditation, he had a vision of a cataclysmic event that he referred to as the Coalescence, where all realms would collide. Realizing its imminent arrival, he sought the aid of the Source Dragons; the progenitors of all Elemental Powers. Pleading with them, he requested a portion of their combined dragon energy to create the Dragon Cores, which would hold back the Coalescence. When he realized his time in the realms was coming to an end, he became aware that evil forces could misuse the cores. So, he hid them in the Mother Garden, the Pinnacle of Crystals, and the Wyldness. In order to ensure that the discovery of the cores would be limited to his descendants, the First Spinjitzu Master created a enchantment, which was designed to ensure that the Spirit of the Temple would only reveal the locations of the cores upon the request of the rightful heirs.[9]

At some point, the Spinjitzu Master gifted Elemental Powers to select mortals, making them the first generation of Elemental Masters and his guardians.[25][26]

At some point, the First Spinjitzu Master taught Spinjitzu to King Urd Leatherwing.

At one point sometime before his sons were born, the First Spinjitzu Master caught a young girl named Nineko stealing from him. Although she stole, the First Spinjitzu Master decided to train Nineko instead. Sometime after Nineko turned 18, the First Spinjitzu Master noticed that one of his katanas were missing and accused her of stealing it. Nineko tried to tell him that she didn't steal it, but he wouldn't listen and never trained her again.[27]

Eventually, over the course of millennia, the First Spinjitzu Master grew old. Feeling his time would very soon come to an end, he fathered two sons who inherited his tremendous powers. The eldest, Garmadon, with power over the forces of Destruction, the power of Oni, and the youngest, Wu, a future teacher to some ninja, with power over the forces of Creation, the power of dragons.[28] He taught them the art of Spinjitzu and how to harness their abilities, with the hope that the two would represent the balance.

The First Spinjitzu Master would create two scrolls that contained secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu. He deemed the Scroll too dangerous and when Wu and Garmadon returned home late, after initially questioning them, he soon sternly told them off when they wanted to read the Scrolls. He became furious with his sons when they disobeyed him and used the Scrolls to defeat Aspheera and hid them. Because of this, the First Spinjitzu Master never trusted the two again.[29]

Note: The Book of Spinjitzu told a different story about the Scroll, where the First Spinjitzu Master caught Garmadon reading the Scroll and took it from him and ordered they never touch the Scroll. He apparently knew of its lure as he hid it from Garmadon as if he knew his elder son would want to read it again.

One day, the First Spinjitzu Master went fishing with his sons and told the impatient boys to have faith after they didn't catch a fish. The First Spinjitzu Master was then pleased when Wu caught a big fish and told his eldest son to maintain faith.[12]

At some point, he and his sons moved to the Monastery of Spinjitzu.[30] While he was looking over the Golden Weapons outdoors, Wu lost his katana in a sparring match with his brother. Knowing their father would get angry, Garmadon ventured over the wall to retrieve it. When Garmadon did not return, the First Spinjitzu Master got worried and went out to look for him, eventually finding his son near some bushes, wounded and extremely ill.

The First Spinjitzu Master attempted to heal Garmadon's wound, learning that he had been bitten by a strange viper. Garmadon lashed out at his father by stating he hated him and blamed Wu for it as he was shocked by this behavior, and the First Spinjitzu Master began to no longer recognize his oldest son as the same boy he was before. Unknown to anyone at the time, the viper who bit Garmadon was the Great Devourer, and its venom would steadily corrupt Garmadon into a ruthless, power-hungry villain who wanted to make the world into his image as part of the Overlord's plan to return to Ninjago.[31][32]

The First Spinjitzu Master told Wu of the Never-Realm once, where he described it as the most distant and coldest of all the realms in creation and told him to never step foot in it, saying that no method from Ninjago for interdimensional travel could work there and noted even he was barely able to escape from the Realm.[14]

Sometime during Garmadon and Wu's childhood, the First Spinjitzu Master was encountered by a man who would come to be known as Quanish the Elder.[33]

Later on, the Master constructed a tomb underneath the Endless Sea to serve as both his final resting place and a sanctuary to prevent the Realm Crystal from falling into the wrong hands.[34] To ensure that only a true Spinjitzu Master would be able to find it, the tomb was guarded by three deadly traps which required the Sword of Sanctuary from the Cloud Kingdom to safely traverse. Before he departed, the First Spinjitzu Master promised to leave a clue leading to the tomb to his sons and inscribed three clues on his staff.[35]

Presumed death[]

When Wu and Garmadon were young men,[36] the First Spinjitzu Master charged his sons with the duty to guard the Four Golden Weapons against those who would misuse their power,[37] warning them that the weapons were too powerful for any one man to handle.[38] In his final moments, he sealed himself in his own tomb and died peacefully beside a waterfall, with the Realm Crystal in hand.[34]

In the Grasslands[]

At some point, the First Spinjitzu Master summoned Quanish the Elder to the Grasslands and offered him to stay, but he declined, saying that he had something yet to do in Ninjago.[33]

After the ninja defeated the Oni, the First Spinjitzu Master summoned Lloyd to the Grasslands and proceeded to thank and praise him for having done so much for Ninjago, giving his grandson kind and complicated words regarding life and the future, bowing before Lloyd. He offered his grandson a choice: he could come with him to the Grasslands and find peace and happiness or he could return to Ninjago and be the Green Ninja once again.

Lloyd takes the latter option and the First Spinjitzu Master sends his grandson back to Ninjago by teleporting one of the petals from the Grasslands and having it float over Lloyd.[39]

Not long after this, the First Spinjitzu Master summoned Quanish to the Grasslands once more, and the two spoke about their previous encounters and Quanish's search for answers in Ninjago. The First Spinjitzu Master told him that he would have all the answers he could ever want if he stayed, and Quanish accepted the offer, leaving behind his mortal body in Ninjago.[33]

Legacy

Legacy[]

A statue honoring the First Spinjitzu Master was built in front of the Games Plaza in the City of Temples, as he was the one who brought Elemental Powers to mortals.[10]

The First Spinjitzu Master's bones would eventually be found centuries later by Wu's pupils, the ninja, who sought to prevent the Realm Crystal from falling into the hands of Wu's first student, Morro. However, Morro would ultimately recover the crystal from the ninja to unleash his master, the Preeminent, onto Ninjago.[34][40]

During the Day of the Departed, Wu returned to the ruins of the Monastery of Spinjitzu and drank a cup of Remembrance Tea in his honor.[41]

In the Realm of Oni and Dragons, the First Spinjitzu Master's legacy is known by the Dragon Hunters. Upon learning that Kai, Jay, and Zane were Elemental Masters, Iron Baron deduced that they had been taught by the Master himself.[42] Wu eventually begins regaining his memories, including one of his father teaching him and Garmadon how to fish, giving him the lesson to "have faith," which gives Wu the motivation to trust Heavy Metal.[43] Furthermore, after fully regaining his memories, Wu reveals to the Dragon Hunters that he is the son of the First Spinjitzu Master, and Iron Baron takes advantage of the revelation, knowing that a blood relative will surely allow him to gain the Dragon Armor.[44] After reaching Firstbourne's nest, Wu explains to Iron Baron that Firstbourne had seen the goodness in his father's heart, and therefore allowed him to ride her, unlike Iron Baron, in whom the ancient dragon sensed darkness. Firstbourne eventually senses the goodness in Wu and allows him to wear the Dragon Armor, the same armor that his father wore during the war between the Dragons and Oni.[45] Wu became the Golden Dragon Master and wore the armor in a final fight against his now-reborn brother Garmadon.[46] After the final battle, Wu kept the armor, continuing his father's legacy.

The First Spinjitzu Master's legacy was not entirely good, however. After Garmadon was resurrected with the knowledge of darkness threatening Ninjago, having been reverted to his evil ways, he was so desperate to protect Ninjago that he hunted down his own family to make sure they would not intervene in his quest to unite Ninjago and gain his full power. Garmadon even subconsciously modeled the Colossus after his father.[47] Although Garmadon was, in a twisted way, honoring his father's legacy by protecting all he had created, his actions caused more harm than good. Lloyd, the First Spinjitzu Master's grandson, would ultimately defeat Garmadon, who passed down the responsibility of defeating the upcoming evil to his son, therefore indirectly entrusting Lloyd with the task of protecting his grandfather's legacy.

Years later, when Lloyd was brought before the Source Dragons, the one that represented the Source of Life claimed him as one of theirs when the Source Dragon of Strength inquired, noting his lineage as the descendent of "the first".

Personality

Personality[]

The First Spinjitzu Master was a benevolent individual. His goodness was greatly respected by all who knew him. It is also very likely that he was wise for Wu seems to have learned life lessons from him.[12] The First Spinjitzu Master also did not practice favoritism, as he loved both his sons equally and Wu continued to defend him from Garmadon's bitter belief that Wu was always his favorite. He also seemed patient and kind, gently lecturing Garmadon and Wu when they failed to catch even a single fish. However, he was also very strict with his sons, such as when he became distant and "more silent than ever" for years after they stole the Scrolls of Forbidden Spinjitzu.[27]

His impartiality is also demonstrated by choosing to side with neither the Dragons nor the Oni in their unending war with one another. This shows his compassion, as his hybrid nature would have tipped the balance of power, and possibly resulted in the outright genocide of either species. However, he may have presumably chosen the side of the dragons, but didn't want to involve himself in the war, which could explain why he left the realm.[48]

Despite his generosity, he was actually known for having a harsher side that would leave a great impact on other people such as Nineko, who considered the First Spinjitzu Master to be like a father to her until she felt rejected by him due to her being accused of stealing one of his katanas.[27] Despite this, that doesn't mean he hated her for doing so, nor was he harsh on her in a more lethal or fatal way.

Weapons and abilities

Weapons and abilities[]

Abilities[]

  • Creation & Destruction - Born of the Oni and the dragons, the First Spinjitzu Master inherited the powers of Creation and Destruction, which were his first elemental powers before he received the rest from the Source Dragons. With his Creation powers, he could create the landmass of the realm of Ninjago, as well as create some of the other realms and create his own sons.
  • Golden Power - The First Spinjitzu Master was the first humanoid user of Golden Power, which was the source of his phenomenal power. His power was sufficiently great to wield all four Golden Weapons of Spinjitzu at once. Garmadon once stated that the power of the First Spinjitzu Master could even move mountains. Even the Oni, especially the Omega, who is shown to be more powerful than the Master's older son (when the latter was weakened) with his full potential, still would not dare attack Ninjago until his power had disappeared. The Spinjitzu Master also managed to single-handedly defeat the Overlord and his Stone Army eventually in a great war by splitting Ninjago in half, destroying the entity's physical body and leaving him trapped on the Island of Darkness while sealing away the Stone Army in an ancient tomb. He had fully mastered the use of the four main elements: Fire, Lightning, Earth, and Ice and could bestow them to others, as he was the one who gifted the Elemental Masters with their use.
  • Elemental Powers - As confirmed in "A Big Splash," the First Spinjitzu Master could use all elemental powers predating the Merge that he received from the Source Dragons[17] besides other elemental powers like Water, Wind, Amber, Darkness, and the Chaos elements.
  • Soul Control - Through currently unknown means, the First Spinjitzu Master has control over souls as he was able to summon Lloyd's spirit to him after he had helped stop the Oni and was also able to return Lloyd back to the living world after their brief encounter.
  • Teleportation - While in his afterlife, the First Spinjitzu Master could teleport around while coaching Lloyd on the meaning of life and the future.
  • Longevity - Due to his Oni and Dragon heritage, the First Spinjitzu Master was able to live for several thousands of years. This trait was later passed down to his two sons, as they have lived for thousands of years. It is unknown if his grandson inherited this trait, as he is still very young.
  • Skilled warrior - The First Spinjitzu Master was clearly an exceptional fighter in both armed and hand-to-hand combat, having been the one to develop the martial art of Spinjitzu and wield the Golden Weapons masterfully. He had a Spinjitzu fighting style unique known to him alone (later learned also by Lloyd), which was called the "Golden Dragon". The First Spinjitzu Master was also a highly capable teacher, as, under his tutelage, both of his sons would become excellent fighters in their own right.
  • Shapeshifting - Although never shown, the First Spinjitzu Master is half Oni and therefore able to shapeshift at will into any creature he desires.
  • Dimensiokinesis/Realm Manipulation - The First Spinjitzu Master is capable of manipulating and creating entire dimensions and realms, such as Ninjago.[49]
  • Expert Craftsman/Armorer: The First Spinjitzu Master is an expert in flawlessly crafting items, as he was the one who made the Dragon Armor alongside Firstbourne.

Weapons[]

  • Golden Weapons: The First Spinjitzu Master was one of the only two beings, the other being the Overlord, who could wield the power of the four Golden Weapons at once (until Dark Magic gave Lord Garmadon the same ability), and he likely wielded them all in combat. In "The Stone Army," he is pictured with a glowing sword of pure energy, similar to the Elemental Blades, though in reality, he had been in the Golden Mech at the time.[50]
  • Bo staves: Passively, the First Spinjitzu Master kept with him at least two staves, one containing the clues to his tomb that was passed to Wu, and another made of gold that was placed in his tomb's second test.
  • Dragon Armor: The First Spinjitzu Master was the co-creator and wearer of the fabled Dragon Armor.
  • Scrolls of Forbidden Spinjitzu: The Scrolls of Forbidden Spinjitzu were crafted by the First Spinjitzu Master, with the intention of augmenting any kind of power of those who possess them. Nevertheless, the scrolls come at a price, gradually corrupting those of pure intent over time. However, the user can resist the adverse effects through sheer willpower.
  • Golden Mech: The Golden Mech is physically very strong and has a light that could ward off The Overlord and dispersing weaker dark beings. The power of the mech is tied to that of the user, and if the user is weakened, the mech will be as well.

Official description

Official description[]

The father of Wu and Garmadon, and the powerful creator of Spinjitzu and the entire world of Ninjago. The First Spinjitzu Master trained his sons in the art of Spinjitzu, hoping that one day they would become his successors, protecting the world he had created against the forces of darkness.[51]

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • There was a concept of a statue of the First Spinjitzu Master coming to life and helping save Ninjago.[52] This was inspired by the Danish legend, "Holger Danske."[53]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • A connection can be drawn between the First Spinjitzu Master and the real-world concept of God, as both are said to be creators of their respective worlds.
    • In "Codename: Arcturus," Wu says, "Oh, by the grace of the First Spinjitzu Master," which is a based on the phrase, "by the grace of God."
    • In "The Storm Amulet," P.I.X.A.L. says, "Thank the Master," which is based on the phrase, "Thank God."
    • However, Tommy Andreasen stated that "FSM is not a religion."[54]
  • Though he is almost always painted in a positive light, there have been some lines of dialogue and other implicit evidence that suggested that the First Spinjitzu Master was not a perfect being and that he may have preferred Wu over Garmadon or known Garmadon could become evil.
    • According to Tommy Andreasen, he theorized that the First Spinjitzu Master chose the side of the dragons rather than the Oni, but didn't involve himself too much in the war.[48]
      • Garmadon claimed that Wu had always been their father's favorite in "Weapons of Destiny."
      • Harumi said Wu and the First Spinjitzu Master had kept the truth from Garmadon and feared what he could become with his power in "True Potential."
      • Young Garmadon said "Wu always gets everything" in "The Gilded Path."
      • Garmadon said that Ninjago was built on power rather than balance in "Green Destiny."
  • His true name is unknown, as he is only ever referred to as "the First Spinjitzu Master." This may be because while many know his legend, there are very few alive old enough to have known him in life.
    • For unknown reasons, despite probably knowing their father's name, neither Wu or Garmadon has stated the true name of the First Spinjitzu Master, likely out of respect, but even when Garmadon was bitter at his father due to the Devourer's venom, he never revealed his father's true name. It may be because he simply had gotten used to calling his father "the First Spinjitzu Master" or may not have known his father's actual name at all.
    • It is also unknown whether Mystake is aware of his true name but like with his sons, she never mentions it, although given that she had known him longer than his sons, it can be assumed she was aware of it and just never got the chance to say it before her death.
    • The First Spinjitzu Master didn't have a last name.[55]
    • According to Tommy Andreasen, his proper name has never been specified in working scripts or other behind-the-scenes material.[56]
  • His origins were revealed in Season 8: he originated from the Realm of Oni and Dragons.
    • Being "the child of light and darkness," the First Spinjitzu Master inherited his powers from the Oni and the Dragons (inheriting Creation and possibly Golden Power from the dragons, while inheriting Destruction from the Oni).[57]
      • When asked about how the First Spinjitzu Master got Golden Power, Tommy Andreasen said, "Dragons possess the the four powers of sooooo....",[58] as Golden Power is made of the four elements of Creation.
      • Chris "Doc" Wyatt would later confirm that the First Spinjitzu Master somewhat inherited most of his elemental powers and maybe even his Golden Power from the Source Dragons.[17]
    • While it is known he was a hybrid of the Oni and dragons, what exactly the circumstances that caused his birth to be possible, and why he looked like a human remains a mystery.[59]
    • His "human" form may not be his original form.[60]
  • It is unknown if the First Spinjitzu Master ever needed the Realm Crystal, due to being half-dragon, he may have had the power to travel between realms like full-blooded dragons.
  • It's possible that the First Spinjitzu Master based Spinjitzu on the spinning movement used by dragons to travel between realms.[61]
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, he had several hands.[62] It is unknown how the First Spinjitzu Master wielded all four Golden Weapons in combat all at once, because in all the flashbacks and the appearance of his remains in his tomb, he is shown to have only two arms. In the tale of the Oni and the Dragon from "The Oni and the Dragon," he is seen pulling the four weapons into his Spinjitzu tornado.
  • He referred to the Oni as the Bringers of Doom, though he has Oni blood himself.[63]
  • Tommy Andreasen once theorized that the Stone Wars never happened, but rather, the Overlord is the living embodiment of the First Spinjitzu Master's Oni side that the latter, literally, cast aside.[64] Should this theory be canonized, then this may imply that the First Spinjitzu Master inherited Darkness from the Oni.
    • Chris "Doc" Wyatt stated that he will not canonize this theory.[65]
  • He mastered all the elemental powers he received from the Source Dragons.; explicitly excluded from this are the elements of Water and Wind that belonged to Wojira, Darkness that belongs to the Overlord, Amber that was created by the Original Master of Amber after the First Spinjitzu Master received the elements, and finally all the elements created as a result of the Merge (of which the currently known are Technology, Fusion, Propulsion and Surface Tension).
    • When asked why the First Spinjitzu Master didn't have Wind, despite having all of the others elements, Tommy Andreasen replied that the situation would be something he would change if he could.[66] Later, it's been revealed that dragons also have Water and Energy.[67][68]
    • Tommy Andreasen, stated that in his mind, dragons before adolescence may temporarily use the Wind element before they get one of the four elements of Creation excluding the other secondary elements.[69]
    • In 2025, Chris "Doc" Wyatt stated that the First Spinjitzu Master only had Creation and Destruction before receiving the other elements from the Source Dragons,[17] meaning that he only inherited the elements of Creation from the dragons.
  • In Lloyd's family tree in The Book of Spinjitzu, there is a silhouetted image of a woman next to the image of the First Spinjitzu Master. The image is silhouetted because Wu does not actually know if he and Garmadon have a mother or not.[70]
    • However, Tommy Andreasen once stated that he imagines Wu and Garmadon did have a mother, saying that he liked the idea of the First Spinjitzu Master not being able to create life on his own and implying that the First Spinjitzu Master could have had the idea to have children from observing the populations he brought into Ninjago.[71]
    • Additionally, he would later state that there was probably no mother involved, but rather, Wu and Garmadon are simple the First Spinjitzu Master's "ultimate creation".[72]
  • Tommy Andreasen revealed that the First Spinjitzu Master was nearly immortal[citation needed] and lived for thousands of years.[73] Wu and Garmadon inherited similar longevity, as they have also lived thousands of years.[74]
    • It is unknown if Lloyd also inherited this. If so, he would be the longest-lived of the human ninja. Zane is a robot, so he is potentially immortal, but can be broken or damaged.
    • As explained by Mystake, the reason for this is their Oni blood.
  • It's possible that the First Spinjitzu Master did not die, but instead, he simply left his vessel in Ninjago.[5][75] According to Tommy Andreasen, he is most likely not in the Departed Realm.[76]
    • It is currently unclear whether the First Spinjitzu Master is immortal and apart of The Balance, in a similar fashion to the Overlord.
  • Despite the major importance of the First Spinjitzu Master, Andreasen doubts "the common Ninjagonian knows about FSM"[77] saying that it's not common knowledge.[78]
  • Although he has been mentioned and shown in flashbacks in several episodes, his face is mainly shown only in drawn pictures. However, in the episode "Tick Tock," his face is visible for a short moment, and it is shown to look a little like Garmadon's face when he was recently cured from evil.
  • The First Spinjitzu Master's appearance changes in his various depictions throughout the series. For example, in his pictured appearance in "Way of the Ninja," he has a standard hair piece; in the flashback in "Tick Tock," he has a braided ponytail; and in "Grave Danger," his statue has a full beard.
    • It is unknown in which moment of his life he had such a beard, because he has no beard in any of the flashbacks or visions.
  • His first physical debut in the television series was in "Grave Danger," when his skeletal remains were shown holding the Realm Crystal.
  • In Rise of the Snakes, the First Spinjitzu Master is shown wearing an outfit identical to Wu's black kimono first seen in "Battle Between Brothers." Starting in Hunted, he is shown wearing a different suit, this time resembling to the Black Training gi which previously only worn by the ninja in sets.
  • His voice is heard for the first time in "The Gilded Path."
  • It is possible that the First Spinjitzu Master may be ambidextrous, as he is shown writing with his left hand in the flashback shown in "Never Trust a Human" when Wu and Garmadon came back home after they escaped from Serpentine Valley, although he is also depicted wielding weapons mainly in his right hand. Or it could be that he simply may prefer writing with his left.
  • When asked if the First Spinjitzu Master could return in the future of Dragon Rising, Chris "Doc" Wyatt replied with "Never say never"[79]
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, the First Spinjitzu Master likely knew of the Oni Form but was never careless enough to unleash it.[80]
  • The First Spinjitzu Master may have the power win in a battle against a single Source Dragon however, this would depend on which specific Source Dragon, as he is not powerful enough to defeat all of the Source Dragons. [81]
  • When asked if the First Spinjitzu Master was a Conduit, Chris "Doc" Wyatt, stated "In a way yes; but also so much more than that".[82]
    • He had a more personal relationship with the Source Dragons.[83]
    • The First Spinjitzu Master was a Conduit for all seven Source Dragons, and was able to access more power from the Dragon Cores compared to Lloyd, who was only a Conduit for one Source Dragon.[84]
  • When asked if The First Spinjitzu Master inherited his elemental powers from the Source Dragons, he responded with "Tricky, because sorta yes-- but Creation and Destruction (as wielded by the Dragon and Oni in the First War) pre-existed FSM's meet the SDs."[17]
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, The First Spinjitzu Master never told Wu and Garmadon about the Merge when they were children which could mean, he most likely never told them about the Source Dragons involvement in preventing the merge.[citation needed]
  • The First Spinjitzu Master retained his Elemental Powers after giving them to the first generation of Elemental Masters[85], but how he did it is unknown.
