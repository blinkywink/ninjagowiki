The centipede monster was a monster native to the Land of Monsters that lived in the Cave of Change. It encased its victims into cocoons before eating and integrating them into its body. With this ability, it claimed the lives of several adventurers, including the Fire Master.

History

History[]

Background[]

The centipede monster resided in the Cave of Change within the Land of Monsters, earning a fearsome reputation. Legends warned that anyone who ventured into the cave would vanish, as the creature would trap their victims in cocoons that will transform them into centipede monsters. One day, the Fire Master noticed the disappearances and decided to investigate with Rusty, determined to rescue those that were lost. However, they were ambushed by a centipede monster, and the Fire Master was ensnared in a cocoon. Over time, he was eaten as well and underwent a horrifying transformation, becoming part of the centipede monster.

Fallen Master[]

The centipede monster fought Kai in the Cave of Change, managing to defeat him and trap him in a cocoon. Being overconfident, the centipede monster wandered off and did not realize that Kai had escaped. When Kai returned, the centipede monster rushed to attack him, but was intercepted by Rusty. The centipede monster tried to attack it with its appendages, but Rusty used Fire to burn it completely, fire that destroyed its head and eventually spread to the rest of its body. With its death, its soul was sent to the Spectral Lands.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Cocoon Creation: The centipede monster can create cocoons.
    • Binding: Using said cocoons, it can imprison others to be eaten later.
  • Assimilative Evolution: Those eaten by the centipede monster would evolve into a part of its body.
  • Superhuman Speed: The centipede monster is very fast at attacking and moving around the Cave of Change.
