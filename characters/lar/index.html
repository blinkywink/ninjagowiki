Overview

“Apparently some charlatan called Lar had passed by the village and convinced everyone that HE was the Master of Water. He had even duped a local publisher to release fake stories about his "legendary" battles.”
— A section about Lar in Way of the Departed

Lar is a self-proclaimed Elemental Master of Water.

History

History[]

The Tournament of Elements[]

Lar fought Kai in one of the training fights before the Tournament of Elements. Kai won the round by converting Lar's water into steam.

Way of the Departed[]

When Cole told Seliel that Nya was the Master of Water, Seliel mentioned that Lar had traveled through Nom and proclaimed to be the Master of Water, even getting stories of his "legendary battles" to be published, but he was really a charlatan.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He appears in the 2015 activity book but not in the TV series, and he was most likely designed before the idea of Nya having the Element of Water was thought of, or the authors of the book did not know what the show was planning.[1]
    • Tommy Andreasen described him as "a product of too little control and scrutiny in the early days of managing Ninjago."[2]
  • He is said to be able to use his powers to unscrew water bottle caps.
  • Lar's outfit resembles the ninja's Tournament robes, colored blue to symbolize his element. His wavy hairstyle is similar to Cole's before Season 8 but is the same shade as Lloyd's.
  • In Chapter 4 of Way of the Departed, Seliel mentions that Lar had created fake stories and legends about him being the Elemental Master of Water and not Nya. He had even duped a local publisher to release fake stories about his "legendary battles," meaning his appearance in The Tournament of Elements was just a fake story created by him.
  • He is the first self-proclaimed Elemental Master, followed by the Master of Gluing.
