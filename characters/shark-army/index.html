This article is about a subject that is canonical to the storyline of Warner Bros' The LEGO Movie franchise.

The Shark Army was an organization that serves Lord Garmadon and acts as his basic offense.

Hierarchy

Hierarchy[]

The "General" system[]

Whenever a General #1 fails Garmadon or angers him, he fires them out of his volcano lair, resulting in the fired general being covered in ash or burn marks from the heat of the lair, with one even losing his hearing in the process. The next general then takes his or her place, climbing up their ranks.
