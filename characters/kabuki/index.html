Overview

“Ah, just what this place needs. Creepy clowns.”
“Not clowns. Kabuki, Chen's jesters.”
— Jay and Garmadon, "Only One Can Remain"

Kabuki were Chen's actors who served him daily. They lived in Chen's Palace and are predominantly formed by women, though some men are also present. It is unknown if Chen was paying the Kabuki, had enslaved them, or simply persuaded them into working for him for free.

History

History[]

Tournament of Elements[]

Only One Can Remain[]

After Chen introduced himself to the Elemental Masters, he ushered his Kabuki and they lead the Elemental Masters to their personal suites.

Versus[]

Neuro entered the Kabuki's room in order to read Chen's mind; however, Clouse gets in the way and threatens to tell the other fighters that he's helping the ninja. Neuro reads his mind for a moment and promptly leaves.

The Day of the Dragon[]

When the Elemental Masters took off to find Skylor, Dareth stayed behind and was telling stories about his past adventures.

When Chen and his followers left the island after transforming into Anacondrai, the Kabuki took control of it for a short time, before Skylor taking over her father's family business.

Sons of Garmadon[]

The Jade Princess[]

A Kabuki is seen in a commercial showing the screen of a tower when the Destiny's Bounty passes it.

Hunted[]

Green Destiny[]

A few Kabuki are seen at the celebration after Lloyd defeated Emperor Garmadon.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The kabuki all knew each other, so Nya had to come up with a believable cover story for why she suddenly joined the group.[1]
    • The clothing that the female Kabuki wear resembles Yukata.
  • Like the lumberjacks, the Kabuki consist of a few different design variants, with all of them utilizing design elements from other LEGO themes.
    • Some are dressed in red, with the hair of the Egyptian Queen from Minifigures Series 5 and a dress based on that of the Kimono Girl from Minifigures Series 4.
    • Some are dressed in purple, with the same hair as Ms. Laudita and a dress also based on the Kimono Girl's.
    • Some are dressed in gray, the only male Kabuki, with the same hair as Edna, a torso based on Coleman Trebor's from Star Wars, and legs based on Boromir's from The Lord of the Rings.
    • One appeared briefly in "Spy for a Spy," dressed in red, with the same hairstyle as the purple Kabuki. One appeared again later on in "The Jade Princess," and two appeared in the background during the celebration in "Green Destiny." The design on the back of their kimono also resembled the one on the purple Kabuki.
  • Coincidentally, the red Kabuki's hairstyle is later used in blonde for Nya's disguise in "Public Enemy Number One."
