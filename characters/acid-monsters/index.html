Overview

This article is about a type of Elemental Monster. For the creature from the Land of Monsters, see Acid monster (Land of Monsters).

Acid Monsters were a type of snake-like Elemental Monsters that embody a corrupted version of Water.

History

History[]

Background[]

Like other Elemental Monsters, Acid Monsters first came into existence before the existence of Elemental Masters[1] as a result of the disruption of Source Energy by the Weapon of Desolation. After the First Spinjitzu Master split the Weapon of Desolation into two pieces with an incantation on the Tablet of Desolation, the Elemental Monsters were eventually defeated.

The Whispering Wood[]

After the Source Dragons of Focus, Life, and Strength left the Council of Sources, two Elemental Monsters were conjured by the Weapon of Desolation and emerged from the Shrine of the Seven Sources, one of them being an Acid Monster, which was created when the disrupted Source Energy mingled with the water pooled at the altar, transforming it into a corrosive acid. As the Acid Monster took form, it was soon joined by an Earth Monster, and together, they attacked two Source Dragon acolytes as the Chief Loyalist watched.

Eventually, the Acid and Earth Monsters attacked a few Acona. However, Lloyd, Arin, and Euphrasia arrived just in time. As the Acid Monster unleashed a torrent of acid towards Arin, he swiftly countered with his Spinjitzu, successfully deflecting the attack. However, the Acid Monster quickly turned its attention back to the Acona, prompting Euphrasia to leap into action and protect them. Meanwhile, as the Acid and Earth Monsters closed in on Lloyd, Arin attempted to use Object Spinjitzu by hurling a stick infused with his Spinjitzu energy at the Acid Monster, but the creature's acidic belly dissolved the stick upon contact. Euphrasia also tried to shoot arrows at the Acid Monster, only to see them disintegrate as they struck. After the Earth Monster immobilized Lloyd, the Acid Monster joined it in preparing to attack Arin and Euphrasia. However, after being fueled by Life from a Tree of Life, Lloyd broke free. This allowed both him and Euphrasia to regain control of their Elemental Powers. Euphrasia then unleashed her Wind abilities on the Acid Monster, ultimately defeating it and reducing it to its energy state.

Tonight We Hunt Monsters[]

While Lloyd is meditating to find the Temple of the Green Ninja, a couple Source energy beams start appearing. The beams materialize into an Acid Monster along with two Earth Monsters and a Mutation Monster. The Acid Monster started shooting acid at the ninja. Then, it cornered them alongside the other Elemental Monsters.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Water/Acid Embodiment - Acid Monsters embody a corrupted form of the Elemental Power of Water that manifests as acid.
  • Acidokinesis - Acid Monsters can manipulate a corrupted version of Water that manifests as acid.
    • Acidogenesis - Acid Monsters can generate acid.
    • Acidokinetic Attacks - Acid Monsters can attack with acid.
      • Acidokinetic Breath - Acid Monsters can shoot acid from their mouths.
        • Acidokinetic Saliva - Acid Monsters can also release acid from their mouths as saliva.
      • Acidokinetic Ball Projection - Acid Monsters can shoot balls of acid.
      • Acidokinetic Beam Emission - Acid Monsters can shoot concentrated beams of acid.
    • Acidokinetic Underbelly - Acid Monsters' underbellies are seemingly made of acid, causing anything that hits them to dissolve.
    • Melting - Acid Monsters' acid can liquify solid objects and matter like wood on contact within a split second.
    • Acidokinetic Immunity - Acid Monsters appear to be immune to acid as one emerged from the acid pool that it was created from without any damage.
  • Prehensile Tail - The tails of Acid Monsters function as an additional limb which they can use like an arm.
  • Superhuman Durability - The bodies of Acid Monsters are incredibly durable, being able to survive both blunt force and sharp attacks with little to no damage.
  • Absolute Immortality - Like other Elemental Monsters, Acid Monsters can't be truly killed. When their physical forms are destroyed, they revert to the Source Energy that the Weapon of Desolation used to bring them into existence and reform as long as the weapon remains active.
  • Concept Empowerment - Like other Elemental Monsters, Acid Monsters enter an empowered state if exposed to the unstable Source Energy in the Weapon of Desolation.
    • Combat Augmentation - Like other Elemental Monsters, the combat capabilities of Acid Monsters are increased in their empowered state.
      • Strength Augmentation - Like other Elemental Monsters, Acid Monsters are significantly stronger in their empowered state.
      • Defense Augmentation - Like other Elemental Monsters, Acid Monsters are significantly more durable in their empowered state.
  • Regeneration - Being made of Elemental Energy, Acid Monsters can instantly regenerate their bodies if destroyed by scattering into bolts of unbalanced Source Energy.
  • Conceptuportation - Like other Elemental Monsters, Acid Monsters can teleport by scattering into beams or bolts of Source Energy and reforming in different locations.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Elemental Powers - Elemental Powers can destroy the Acid Monsters' physical forms.
  • Spinjitzu - Spinjitzu can block and deflect the acid attack of Acid Monsters.
    • Object Spinjitzu - Object Spinjitzu can destroy the Acid Monsters' physical forms.
  • Weapon of Desolation - Being creations of the Weapon of Desolation, Acid Monsters are to obey the will of whoever wields it. Their existence also depends on the weapon, as it can summon and seal them at will.
