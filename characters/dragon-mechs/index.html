This article is about a subject that is relevant to both the main Ninjago storyline and The LEGO Ninjago Movie. Information may be more directed toward one continuity than the other.

Dragon Mechs[1] are robotic dragons known to exist in Ninjago, Prime Empire, and the Land of Lee.

History

History[]

In Rebooted, the Digital Overlord created the Nindroid MechDragon to achieve his goal of capturing Lloyd and taking his Golden Power.[2]

In Hunted, the stranded ninja created a mechanical replica of Firstbourne to distract the Dragon Hunters long enough for Jay, Zane, and Kai to escape.[3]

In Prime Empire, Unagami had the ability to transform into the Empire Dragon. In the Maze of the Red Dragon, the ninja also encountered and defeated the Red Dragon,[4] who Jay eventually utilized as the Blue Dragon to combat Unagami.[5]

In The LEGO Ninjago Movie, Lloyd utilized the Green Ninja Mech Dragon as his ninja vehicle before it was destroyed by Lord Garmadon.

In Dragons Rising: Season 3, Lee piloted a Mech Dragon. This was taken control by Jay using a control implant, which he used to fight the Spectral Dragonians and Zarkt in Mysterium's Cave of Bells.
