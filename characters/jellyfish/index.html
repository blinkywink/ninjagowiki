Overview

Not to be confused with the Shark Army henchman.

Jellyfish are animals that live in the Endless Sea. To reawake Wojira, Glutinous used their electricity to charge the Storm Amulet's replica.

History

History[]

The Tale of Benthomaar (flashback)[]

When Trimaar saved little Benthomaar from the eels, several small jellyfish swam near them.

The Tooth of Wojira[]

While Wojira was deep asleep in her temple, several jellyfish swam around her.

Prior to Seabound[]

When Kalmaar commissioned Glutinous to create replicas of the Storm Amulet and reawake Wojira, Glutinous placed a number of jellyfish in the Temple of Wojira, whose electricity charged the cables connected to Wojira.[1]

Unsinkable[]

While gazing out of the Hydro Bounty's porthole, Nya briefly glimpsed at a pair of jellyfish as the ship made its way to Tartarus Trench.

Five Thousand Fathoms Down[]

When Glutinous and the Maaray Guards tested replicas of the Storm Amulet on the sleeping Wojira, a number of jellyfish swam in the water beneath the temple and charge the cables connected to Wojira.

The Calm Before the Storm (flashback)[]

When Kalmaar came to the Temple of Wojira with the Storm Amulet to reawake Wojira, a number of jellyfish swam in the water beneath the temple. When Wojira woke up, she swam into the water below the temple and scattered several jellyfish that stood in her way out of the temple.

The Turn of the Tide[]

While Nya swam with whales in the Endless Sea, several jellyfish swam on the seabed.

Other appearances and mentions

Other appearances and mentions[]

Clues and Suspects[]

During Lloyd and Nokt's match during the Tournament of the Sources, Roby told his uncle, Bleckt, that this particular match was cooler than anything he could have imagined! In fact, it was admittedly cooler than his original plan of using killer jellyfish in some capacity in the tournament - an idea that was vetoed by Bleckt.

Abilities

Abilities[]
