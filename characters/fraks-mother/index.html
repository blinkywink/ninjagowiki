An unnamed Venomari is Frak's mother.

History

History[]

At some point after the attack of the Great Devourer, two Serpentine from the Constrictai and Venomari tribes[1] had a child named Frak and relocated to the neighborhood close to Ninjago City, joining in the human society. However, before the Merge, the family moved away from the district.

Other appearances and mentions

Other appearances and mentions[]

They Gather for the Feast[]

After Arin discovered that Frak is the Master of Quake, he asked to verify that neither one of his parents were an Elemental Master.

United We Fall[]

When Arin and Riyu snuck in Ras' quarters, they found a picture of Frak along with his parents, confirming Arin's suspicions of Frak being a member of the Wolf Clan.
