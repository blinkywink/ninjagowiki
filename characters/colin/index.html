Colin is a Munce that lives in the Community of Lee.

History

History[]

Background[]

At some point after the Merge, Colin got sent to the Land of Lee by the Administration. In there, he meet with Lee and become a member of the Community of Lee.

Between You and Lee[]

He appears dancing with Andrew in a Tango class. While they are turning, he bonks his head with a human who was also learning Tango with a partner.
