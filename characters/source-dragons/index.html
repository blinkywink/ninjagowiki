Overview

“We have existed since before time had a name. We are the source of all Elemental Power in this universe. We hold together the very fabric of reality.”
— The Source Dragon of Motion, "The Shape of Motion"

The Source Dragons (also simply referred as the Sources) are a mysterious group of immensely powerful cosmic dragons that exist beyond the mortal plane, functioning as a council. They all possess godlike abilities to interact with the Realms of Creation, maintaining equilibrium by using their life force. They embody the seven abstract concepts of Balance, Energy, Flow, Focus, Life, Motion, and Strength that are fundamental to the universe, and are the source of all Elemental Powers in existence.

There used to be seven Source Dragons until one of them was killed before the Merge by unknown circumstances.

Following the Battle of the Spectral Lands, the Source Dragons of Strength, Life and Focus withdrew themselves from the council, destabilizing the flow of Source energy throughout the Merged Lands and consequently causing complications for mortals regarding their Elemental Powers and the manifestation of Elemental Monsters.

History

History[]

Background[]

Being the source of all elemental powers, they predate Firstbourne[1] and the First Realm itself,[2] having existed for an extremely long time. At some point in the past, the First Spinjitzu Master sought the assistance of the Source Dragons, requesting a portion of their potent energy. Their energy was harnessed to forge three Dragon Cores, instrumental in maintaining the separation between realms. Working as a council, the Source Dragons debated whether to assist the First Spinjitzu Master, and the decision was contentious until the Source Dragon of Balance, who favored mortals, cast the deciding vote in favor of providing help. This first interaction with mortals led to conflict with Thunderfang,[3] who, after the War of Oni and Dragon, sought to become the Source Dragon of Chaos. However, the Source Dragons consistently denied his ambitions for divinity, deeming him unworthy. Frustrated by their refusals, Thunderfang turned his ambitions toward vengeance, plotting to usurp the Source Dragons by collecting enough souls to gain power,[4] including theirs.[5] Ultimately, the vengeful dragon's quest for retribution was thwarted when the Source Dragons summoned their champion, the Arc Dragon of Focus, who imprisoned him in the depths of the First Realm after he attacked the City of Temples.[4][6]

In the passage of centuries, the Source Dragon of Balance was killed while some point after, the Imperians, under the influence of Ras, managed to apprehend the Source Dragon of Energy thanks to their technology and the Icon of the Source Dragon of Energy. However, the scientists at the Advanced Systems Lab faced challenges in extracting energy from the Source Dragon of Energy, leading them to believe that such a feat was impossible. Driven by their desire to seize dominion over the realms, Imperium aimed to capture the remaining Source Dragons.[7][8]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

During the dragons' escape from Imperium, Lloyd and Zane discovered the Source Dragon of Energy being held captive to power the entire Kingdom of Imperium. They tried to free him, but the Source Dragon forbid them, as doing so now would put the Merged Realms at risk. To let the other dragons escape, the captive Source Dragon offered Lloyd to let him transfer some of his dragon power to the Green Ninja, allowing him to activate the portal gate. After stopping the merge storm with the Dragon Cores, Lloyd goes back to Imperium and uses all three cores to free the captive Source Dragon. After being freed, the dragon seemingly disappears into thin air.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Following his release, the Source Dragon of Energy informed the other Source Dragons of his newfound freedom at the hands of Lloyd. Seeking guidance in the ninja's quest to counter Shatterspin, Lloyd requested a vision from the Source Dragons. In response, the Source Dragons summoned him to their void. However, their opinions of Lloyd varied. The Source Dragon of Life acknowledged Lloyd as an Elemental Master of Life. On the other hand, the Source Dragons of Motion and Strength held a more skeptical view of Lloyd. The former sensed a potential threat emanating from him, while the latter dismissed him as insignificant. Despite the mixed opinions, the Source Dragon of Energy recounted Lloyd's crucial role as a conduit for his life force, underscoring his significance. He emphasized Lloyd's uniqueness as a mortal and his plea for assistance. Realizing the debt owed to Lloyd for his role in freeing the Source Dragon of Energy, the Source Dragon of Motion ultimately relented. In a gesture of support, she granted Lloyd a vision of Egalt and his domain, hinting that the Dragon Masters could aid the ninja in his mission.

After the murder of three Arc Dragons, the Source Dragon of Motion became aware of the tragedy through her connection with the fallen dragons, shared with the Life Source Dragon. Understanding the strong bond between Lloyd and the Source Dragons, she felt compelled to warn him about the threat posed by the killers and seek justice for the slain dragons. Worried about the increasing danger to the Source Dragons, she listened as Lloyd mentioned the map that the late Matriarch of the Mountain Dragons had carried. The Motion Source Dragon immediately recognized it as a map to the City of Temples. She explained that the Tournament of the Sources was crucial, and Lloyd’s participation was key to uncovering the identity of the killers and seeking justice.

After the Tournament ended, the Source Dragon of Motion appeared before Lloyd. He felt he had failed, expressing regret for not meeting her expectations. But she reassured him that the real purpose of the Tournament was to reveal the identity of the killers—the Forbidden Five. Though the Forbidden Five had escaped, she praised Lloyd for uncovering their names and preventing Ras from reaching the Source Dragons. The Motion Source Dragon reminded the Green Ninja that though the killers were still at large, their plans had been set back, and he had protected the Source Dragons. She encouraged Lloyd not to doubt himself, pointing out that while Sora won the Tournament, he had proven himself worthy of the Source Dragons' gifts. Because of this, the Source Dragons now owed him a debt—a debt they would honor. To mark their bond, she gave him a Dragon Icon made from her scale and told him to call upon her through it in one singular time of true need. With that, she left, leaving Lloyd with her promise of help whenever he required it.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

While imprisoned by Lee, the Source Dragon of Energy summoned Lloyd to the council's assembly in secret, where they were discussing whether or not they should stop Thunderfang by reawakening the Arc Dragon of Focus. Because they had lost one of their own, the Source Dragon of Balance, the one that made the final vote during an impasse to help the First Spinjitzu Master, they simply couldn't come to an agreement. After the Source Dragon of Strength noticed Lloyd's presence, the connection was cut. However, through that, Lloyd came to learn that even in death, Thunderfang was still a threat.

After the Ninja realized what Thunderfang is doing, they went to the City of Temples to find a way to defeat the Shatter Dragon once and for all. Former Minister of Ledgering Bleckt took the ninja to the Vault of Focus, where Lloyd told him that the Source Dragons were in a deadlock on summoning the Arc Dragon of Focus. Bleckt showed them a way to summon the Arc Dragon, though it would make them enemies of the Source Dragons.

After reaching the Caves of Bells in Djinjago and Mysterium, the ninja successfully awakened and summoned the Arc Dragon of Focus and, with the combined efforts of Arin and Lloyd controlling the dragons, defeated Thunderfang, which greatly angered the Source Dragons of Strength, Life, and Focus. Consequently, the Source Dragons who preferred to stay out of mortal affairs decided to withdraw from the Council of Sources, leaving only the Source Dragons of Motion, Flow, and Energy remaining. This shift greatly disrupted the balance of the Elemental Powers and Source Energy. The remaining council members recognized that the mortals would ultimately suffer the consequences of their brethren's actions.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

After withdrawing from the Council, the spheres representing Strength, Life, and Focus deactivated in the Shrine of the Seven Sources. The disrupted Source Energy caused the two fragments of the Weapon of Desolation to create Elemental Monsters throughout the Merged Lands.

The Source Dragons of Energy, Flow, and Motion reappeared after Lloyd failed the Ritual of Unrooting. He ends up in the Source Dragons' void when the three bring him into a vision after he is knocked unconscious by his enemy manifestation. The Source Dragon of Energy heals Lloyd and sends the Green Ninja back to his trial, where he connects with the Trees of Life in the Whispering Wood, and further connects his powers to the other ninja across the Merged Lands.

Abilities

Abilities[]

“Source Dragons experience time differently than the rest of us. They can see what came before, and the things that might come to be.”
— Rontu, "Fugitives From Madness"
  • Transcendent Dragon Physiology - The Source Dragons are dragons of godlike power that exist beyond concepts like Time, Space, Reality, and Dimensionality unless they take their mortal forms,[9] and are comparable to Greek gods in terms of power and abilities.[9]
    • Draco-Ergokinesis - Like other powerful dragons such as Arc Dragons, Thunderfang, and Riyu, Source Dragons are capable of manipulating their dragon energy.
      • Draco-Ergokinetic Breath - Source Dragons can throw their respective source energy from their mouths.[10]
      • Sparks - Like other powerful dragons, such as the Arc Dragons[11] and Riyu, Source Dragons can share their dragon energy with Elemental Masters. This allows recipients to tap into powers usually exclusive to dragons using dragon energy. However, in contrast to regular dragons, when a Source Dragon shares its energy with an Elemental Master, the Master serves as a conduit for that Source Dragon and their energy.
        • Elemental Power Augmentation - By sharing their dragon energy with Elemental Masters, they can increase the strength of Elemental Powers.
        • Draco-Ergokinesis Bestowal - Conduits gain the power to manipulate the dragon energy of their Source Dragon and others.
          • Precognition Bestowal - Conduits can receive visions of the future.
    • Nigh-Omnipotence - The Source Dragons are the most powerful creatures in the realms. They exert near-total control over reality.
      • Dimensiokinesis/Realmkinesis - Source Dragons hold a position of significant power and influence over dimensions and realms. They are intricately linked to the Merge, an event that unified all realms, and their power within the Dragon Cores was what prevented this merging during the First Spinjitzu Master's time. In "The Battle of the Second Monastery," it was suggested that capturing a Source Dragon could have resulted in the realms' destruction if it hadn't been confined in a sphere limiting its powers.
        • Dimension/Realm splitting - According to Ras, the combined power of all seven Source Dragons could reverse the Merge.[12][13]
        • Dimensional Travel - The Source Dragons, like other dragons, can travel between dimensions and can bring living beings with them.
        • Dimension/Realm Creation - They are capable of creating dimensions and realms.
        • Dimension/Realm Destruction - They are also capable of destroying entire dimensions and realms.
          • Omnicide - Destruction of a realm would result in the death of all life in it.
        • Dimensional Rift Creation - As demonstrated by the Dragon Cores, objects wrought from pure condensed Source Dragon energy, the Source Dragons are capable of tearing rifts in the fabric of reality, exposing the Ethereal Divide.
          • Vacuum Effect - Such rifts are identical to MergeQuakes and have a vacuum effect that sucks nearby objects or people in.
      • Omnificence - According to Ras, the combined power of all seven Source Dragons can create anything.[12][13]
        • Elemental Power Creation/Linkage - By embodying their sources, the Source Dragons serve as the foundation for various Elemental Powers, with their energy being integral to their respective source. They are connected to the elements they had created, as well as to those developed by others with the use of a specific Source Dragon's energy.[14] They also depend on each other to maintain the balance of Elemental Powers. Otherwise, it would weaken the Elemental Masters' connection to their powers and make them unstable unless a Ritual of Unrooting is performed.[15]
          • Elementokinesis - Source Dragons can use the Elemental Powers that are derived from their Source.[16][17]
            • Elemental Powers Bestowal - Source Dragons can bestow their Elemental Powers to others.
              • Elemento-Potentikinesis Bestowal - As Golden Power is derived from all their sources, all the Source Dragons can bestow it to others.
        • Draco-Telumgenesis - Source Dragons can create Source Dragon Icons, weapons that contain their dragon energy and can be used to summon them.
      • Nigh-Invincibility - The Source Dragons are described to be almost impervious in terms of power.
      • Telepathy - Similar to few other dragons, Source Dragons possess the ability to communicate with other beings by using telepathic powers.
      • Nigh-Omniscience - Source Dragons know and are aware of almost everything that happen in the realms. They are also able to perceive time.
        • Retrocognition - Source Dragons are capable of viewing and perceiving the past.
        • Precognition - Source Dragons are capable of viewing and perceiving the future.
      • Shapeshifting - Source Dragons can manifest themselves in different mortal forms.[18]
        • Megethoskinesis - Source Dragons can change the size of their mortal form at their own will.
      • Concept Embodiment - Each Source Dragon represents and embodies a concept, such as balance, energy, flow, focus, life, motion, and strength.
        • Dragon Embodiment - The Source Dragons all embody dragons and are linked to mortal dragons somehow.
        • Avatar Manipulation - Each Source Dragon has control over their mortal avatar.
      • Nigh-Absolute Immortality - The Source Dragons cannot be killed in their symbolic forms, only in their mortal forms.
      • Dream/Vision Walking - The Source Dragons can likely enter other people’s dreams or visions, as shown by the Source Dragon of Motion.
      • Healing - The Source Dragons can heal other beings, as shown by the Source Dragon of Energy.
    • Pyrokinetic Breath - Source Dragons can shoot streams of fire from their mouths in their mortal forms.
    • Flight - Source Dragons can fly in their mortal forms by flapping their wings.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

“But you're a Source Dragon. You're more powerful than anything!”
“I wish that were always true, but as you know, one of our kind was recently captured and contained by mortals with evil intent. And before that, the unthinkable happened. A Source Dragon's very life was extinguished.”
— Wyldfyre and the Source Dragon of Motion, "The Shape of Motion"
  • Mortal Form - While immortal and near invincible in their symbolic forms, Source Dragons can still be harmed or even killed in their mortal forms.[19]
  • Glass core - The glass core could keep a Source Dragon trapped inside it, neutralizing most of said Source Dragon’s powers as well as drain its dragon energy.
  • Source Dragon Icons - Source Dragon Icons have the ability to forcefully summon Source Dragons into the Realms of Creation.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In "TEAM MECH OR TEAM DRAGON?!," it was suggested that the Source Dragons are made out of source energy.
  • When a Source Dragon assumes a mortal form, it means they are fully present within a mortal body by concentrating their cosmic forms to manifest that form. This is not to be confused with their mortal avatar, which is a distinct form. An example includes Source Dragons when they descend to the mortal plane, as they're in the mortal plane with mortal bodies.[56]
    • Source Dragons have to take a mortal form when communicating with mortals.[57]
  • The Crowns of Horns/symbols of six of the seven Source Dragons are renowned for being based on the Elemental Powers used by the six original ninja, who each has the symbol of the Source Dragon associated with their respective element on most of their Dragons Rising suits. This is because each of their elemental powers take their sources from one of the six Source Dragons.[58]
    • However, these symbols do not strictly represent the Elemental Powers.[59] They actually correspond to a concept specific to each Source Dragon.
    • The Source Dragon of Balance stands out from their peers by being the only one not associated with any of the original 6 ninja's Elemental Powers though he may be seen as being associated with Wu.
  • According to Kevin Burke, the reason the Matriarch of the Mountain Dragons was pictured in a scroll that mentioned Source Dragons is because the drawing is just a representation of one since Source Dragons were believed to be myths and few have barely seen a Source Dragon. This is similar to depictions of gods from mythology.[60]
    • One of the interpreted depictions of the Source Dragons is them as a seven-headed dragon.[61]
  • Before the reveal of the sources, writer Glen Lakin gave possible derivations for several elements through his Bluesky:

  • According to Chris “Doc” Wyatt, the concepts of birth and death are meaningless to beings like the Source Dragons, except in very unusual circumstances.[116]
