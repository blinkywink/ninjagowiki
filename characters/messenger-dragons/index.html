Overview

“For each tournament, our city would rotate to another realm and invitations would go out by Spirit Dragon courier to all Elemental Masters there.”
— Bleckt to the ninja, "Enter the City of Temples"

Messenger Dragons are Spirit Dragon couriers in the Merged Lands. They used to be part of the Mythica and act as messengers, with one of their primary roles being to deliver invitations to the Tournament of the Sources. They also work for the Source Dragons and the Dragons of the Northern Spiral.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

When Lloyd called out to the Source Dragons, they sent seven Messenger Dragons which surrounded Lloyd to allow him to communicate with the Source Dragons.

Enter the City of Temples[]

The Messenger Dragons handed out invitations to Elemental Masters across the Merged Lands.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Ultimate Object of Admiration[]

A Messenger Dragon gave Wyldfyre a drawing of Roby that he sent to her. Wyldfyre then drew herself, with Kai "photo bombing", and gave the scroll to the Spirit Dragon to send back to Roby.

I Alone Can Save Them[]

Hermes is instructed to send a scroll to Geo, requesting a sculpture for the City of Temples museum. Unfortunately, Kur attacks the museum in search of a Prismatic Blade before Hermes can do so. Kur uses her Elemental Power of Decay on the little dragon. Later, a different Messenger Dragon gave Wyldfyre a scroll informing her, Kai, and Nya that the city was attacked. In the hospital, Roby noted that Hermes was decayed as well.

Their Draconic Majesty's Request[]

Decayed Hermes continued to be resting on a pillow.

Chaos Unchained[]

The decayed Hermes was still trying to recover. However, another Spirit Dragon blasted rainbow colored fire at Hermes, curing him of Kur's effects. He and the other Spirit Dragon then flew away together.

The Hollow Ones[]

The Dragons of the Northern Spiral sent a Spirit Dragon to one of their dying members, Egalt, to inform him and Rontu that they were on their way to honor his passing.

Later, in the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Zane heeded that he and P.I.X.A.L. had access to the City of Temples's messengers thanks to Roby, and that they would use them to keep in touch with the ninja on their explorations.
