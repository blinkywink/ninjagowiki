Overview

“The Anacondrai generals were given the strictest sentence: banishment to the Cursed Realm, so that they will never set foot in Ninjago again.”
— Garmadon, "Spellbound"

The Anacondrai generals were the leaders of the Anacondrai and the Serpentine as a whole during the Serpentine War.

History

History[]

Spellbound (flashback)[]

They led the Anacondrai and the Serpentine into battle, thus starting the First Serpentine War when they heard from Chen and Clouse the humans are planning to attack them. When the Elemental Alliance defeated the Serpentine and banished them into tombs using flutes, the Anacondrai generals including Arcturus were put on trial for their atrocities and war crimes. Afterwards were banished to the Cursed Realm by Garmadon.

The Corridor of Elders[]

After Chen started the Second Serpentine War, Garmadon knew the only way to stop them was to unleash the spirits of the Anacondrai generals by sacrificing himself into the Cursed Realm. Once completed, the generals were freed and began helping the people by cursing Chen's imposter Anacondrai Army.

After this was done the generals thanked both Pythor for finally making them proud by restoring him to his size and give their gratitude to Garmadon's son Lloyd for helping his father free them and for doing what they never could do: uniting the Serpentine and humans as one before departing to the Departed Realm to be at peace.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In "Spellbound," six Anacondrai generals, including Arcturus, were banished to the Cursed Realm. However, when Garmadon was banished in order to free the generals, there are seven of them that emerge from the portal. It is possible that one general either surrendered or captured and was banished earlier or later than the others or it could be just an error.
  • The Anacondrai generals (excluding Arcturus) shared the same torso as Clouse.
