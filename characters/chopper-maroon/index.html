Overview

“Hey, haven't you heard? The color green has been outlawed. Emperor Garmadon's orders.”
— Chopper Maroon to Dareth, "Firstbourne"

Chopper Maroon (also known as the Maroon Mask[1]) is a former member of the Sons of Garmadon. He has short red hair and often wields a red katana.

After Garmadon's disappearance, Chopper was broken out of prison by Lord Mogra and joined the Red Crows.

History

History[]

Sons of Garmadon[]

The Mask of Deception[]

Chopper Maroon was one of the Sons of Garmadon that infiltrated Borg Tower.

The Jade Princess[]

Chopper Maroon was one of the bikers who was thwarted by Samurai X when bullying kids in the street.

The Oni and the Dragon[]

When Cole sings at Laughy's, Chopper Maroon can be seen cheering in the audience.

Snake Jaguar[]

He was one of the two Sons of Garmadon members who guarded the captured Cole, only for him to be tricked and knocked out by Cole during his escape.

The Quiet One[]

Chopper Maroon is one of the Sons of Garmadon that assisted Mr. E, Ultra Violet, and Killow in their successful search for the ninja in Primeval's Eye.

Game of Masks[]

Chopper Maroon was watching the captive ninja and later laughed at baby Wu when he tripped before he ends up hit in the groin by a wrench that Wu was playing with before chasing after him, only for the toddler to jump on his back for enjoyment. Later, Ultra Violet left him in charge to watch after the ninja, only for him to be tricked by Cole again, while baby Wu frees the ninja and they knock him out. Later, he escaped with all of the Sons of Garmadon and captive Lloyd in tow on the Destiny's Bounty.

Dread on Arrival[]

Chopper Maroon, along with Mohawk, was tasked with patrolling the grounds of the Royal Palace. After their patrol, they guarded the entrance to the Temple of Resurrection until they were knocked out by the ninja while they were distracted by Harumi performing the ritual to resurrect Lord Garmadon. They were both arrested by the Ninjago police force and sent to Kryptarium Prison for their crimes.

True Potential[]

Chopper Maroon, along with the rest of the Sons of Garmadon, were freed from their cells in Kryptarium Prison by Harumi and the resurrected Lord Garmadon.

Hunted[]

Firstbourne[]

Now an Sons of Garmadon Police Officer, he, along with Mohawk, let the captured Dareth go because he isn't on the list of people to catch.

Radio Free Ninjago[]

He is at the radio station with Ultra Violet when the Resistance arrive, tying him up.

The Gilded Path[]

Now freed, he joins the ambush on the base of the Resistance alongside Mohawk, fighting the members present.

Two Lies, One Truth[]

Chopper Maroon is present with Ultra Violet and Killow at Lord Garmadon's throne.

Green Destiny[]

Chopper, Mohawk, and another biker harass Gayle Gossip and Vinny until Dareth arrives, warning them that he is the Brown Ninja. As one of the bikers makes a joke, Dareth says they twisted his words, before attempting to attack, only to slip up, go flying and take out all three bikers, landing on top of Chopper Maroon and knocking him unconscious. Later, He runs to Kryptarium and warns them that the ninja are back. The Elemental Masters then break out. Chopper Maroon was later arrested and sent back to Kryptarium as a prisoner after Garmadon's defeat.

After the Oni's invasion[]

After the Oni's invasion, Chopper was one of the Sons of Garmadon that wrecked chaos in the city in Garmadon's honor until they were inprisoned again by the ninja. There they waited for his return until they discovered that he helped the ninja, causing them to start a riot in prison until Lord Mogra saved freed them. Chopper and the rest of the Sons of Garmadon joined the Red Crows.

Garmadon[]

Sins of the Past[]

Chopper, Francis and Killow broke into Saeko's house, threatning to her if she didn't give her her possessions, until Saeko knocked Francis out with her pan, shocking Chopper. When Garmadon entered the house, he asked Killow what was going on, Chopper reminded Killow that they no longer served Garmadon but was hushed up by Killow. Later, to taunt Mogra, Garmadon aksed his former soldiers to find his drum sticks, causing mutliple Red Crows members, including Chopper, to look for it, Chopper asked Garmadon if he wanted a burger, but had to be reminded once again by Mogra to not follow Garmadon.

The Snake in the Garden[]

Chopper, and two other members found Min in the forest, and told her about the new curfews, after they saw the unconscious Garmadon, they realised they could get rewarded by Mogra but were defeated by te Two Moon Village Commandos and flead back to the village, threatning the commandos by telling them that they were going to inform Mogra.

Beginnings[]

Chopper, along with most of the Red Crow members attacked Glimwillow Woods, and forced the bears to leave. After Mogra's defeat, he was forced to rebuild Two Moon Village and was presumably sent back to Kryptarium Prison.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He ranges back and forth from a police officer gear to biker gear in Hunted.
  • Throughout Hunted, he can be seen in multiple places at once, despite there being many unnamed Sons of Garmadon members who look different from each other appearing in multiple episodes.
  • There is another character with the same torso and head but with short legs. This character is named Nails. Nails appears in the show in group shots, and sometimes at the same time as Chopper.
  • Chopper Maroon and Mohawk have a similar role in the show as Kapau and Chope, as they are often together, commenting on the situation, complaining, and entertaining.
  • While in the sets he has black legs, in the show, he has gray legs with printing.
    • These legs are supplied on his LEGO Juniors version and on Luke Cunningham and Skip Vicious.
  • His hairpiece is the same as Iron Baron's, originally used on B.A. Baracus from The A-Team in LEGO Dimensions. Chopper Maroon and Nails are the only minifgures to use this hair piece in a color other than black.
