Overview

— Prentis to Arin, "The Administration"

Sub-Agent Prentis is an Administration Agent who works at the Department of Reassignment.

History

History[]

The Administration[]

Prentis and another agent were sending beings to their possible home-realms when Arin was sent there. He asked what they were doing, so Prentis explained the Department of Reassignment. However, after Arin asked about his parents' whereabouts, Prentis realized that Arin wasn't authorized to be there, and asked him for his authorization form. Arin quickly made an excuse and departed, causing Prentis to call for his manager, Agent Walker. Jay then told him this was not part of their duties and was therefore a waste of his time.

Human Resources[]

While going to the Department of Reassignment, Ninja encounter a crowd full of Administration Agents, which Prentis is also in it. But Jay tricks them by saying there's a birthday and they all have to sign a card.
