The attacker bots (also referred to as attack bots[1] and assassin bots[2]) were three androids sent by Bleckt to stop the ninja and Zur from winning the Tournament of the Sources, in Ras' favor, even sending a fake one to attack the latter to throw everyone off track.

History

History[]

The Final Game (flashback)[]

Bleckt sends the attacker bots to attack the Ninja, the Wolf Clan and Zur. The two that were sent after the ninja and Zur were meant to attack them, while the bot that was sent after Ras was to throw the ninja and Zur off track.

They Gather for the Feast[]

In the middle of the night, a hooded attacker bot snuck into the Ninja’s quarters. Riyu woke up and roared to alert the others of the bot’s presence before it attempted to stab Lloyd. The group attacked the bot and Lloyd tried to get information out of it.

Security Bots swarmed Lloyd and the bot whom he had just caught. Lloyd pulled back the hood of the bot to reveal its identity. It proceeded to glitch and break down. Ras threw a similar bot on top of it and Zur did the same.

Zane scan the bots but due to the damage each bot had sustained, accessing their mechanical memory would be difficult. Tox identified their weapons as acidic Gilba Thorns from the Sevateam plant found in the Mother Garden. This caused the blame to fall on Zant-Tanz, the Elemental Master of Plants and leader of the Mother Garden. While he denied the accusation, he agreed to leave, but not before he stated that the real culprit was still within the City of Temples.

Abilities and weapons

Abilities and weapons[]

Abilities[]

  • Super Strength: The attacker bot that attack the Ninja, proved to be very strong, as he mange to smash through crates.
  • Stealth: The attacker bot that attack the Ninja proved to be extremely stealthy with only Riyu being able to sense it when it snuck in.
  • Combat: The attacker bot proved to be formidable in combat, being able to outmatch most of the ninja and Lloyd resorted to throwing a crate at one to catch him off guard.

Weapons[]

  • Gilba Thorns: One of the attacker bots had a Gilba thorn that had acid properties.
  • Throwing stars: One attacker bot had throwing stars.
  • Smoke bombs: One of the attacker bots had a smoke bomb.
