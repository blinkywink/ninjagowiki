Overview

This article is about the species. For other uses, see Lightning Dragon (disambiguation).

The Lightning Dragons are dragons from the Realm of Oni and Dragons. One of them was placed by Master Wu to protect the Nunchucks of Lightning in the Floating Ruins.

History

History[]

Pilot episodes[]

When Wu hid the Nunchucks of Lightning in the Floating Ruins, he placed Wisp to guard the weapon until the nunchucks were claimed by Jay. When Jay grabbed the nunchucks, Wispy appeared but Jay manage to escape using his gliders.

Later Jay rode Wisp to get to the Underworld.

Rise of the Snakes[]

Wisp continued to be owned by Jay until one day, Wisp was being lethargic and uncooperative, which Master Wu identifies as the beginning of the dragons molt, and forced Jay to release Wisp so they can complete his molts at the Spirit Coves. Once their molting transformation was complete, Wisp joined the other three in being the Ultra Dragon.
