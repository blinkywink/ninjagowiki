Overview

This article is primarily about the appearances of tigers in the Ninjago franchise. For information about the tigers in the realm of the Wyldness, specifically Chima, visit the article on Legends of Chima Wiki.

Tigers are large felines native to the realms of Ninjago and the Wyldness.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Night vision - Like many felines, tigers possess exceptional night vision, enabling sight in dimly lit environments or complete darkness.[1]
  • Superhuman speed/stamina - As demonstrated by Ras, tigers can move at incredibly fast speeds for a long time without tiring out, being able to catch up to Arin when the ninja was driving the Explorer.
  • Superhuman durability/endurance - As demonstrated by Ras, tigers have superhuman levels of durability and endurance, managing to endure Rapton's taser and hits from dragons. They also can tank Shockwave attacks and leave without showing any signs of exhaustion. Ras also fought several corrupted Wolf Mask Warriors without showing any signs of fatigue.
  • Superhuman leaping - Tigers can jump at incredibly high distance.
  • Superhuman strength - As demonstrated by Ras, tigers are shown to be strong enough to lift and throw humans and Serpentine to the ground with ease.
  • Quadrupedalism - Tigers can move on all four limbs like an animal.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Shatterspin - Despite their superhuman levels of strength and durability, Shatterspin is still capable of overpowering tigers as demonstrated by Nokt managing to defeat Ras twice. Even Ras' empowered state didn't prevent him from being seriously injured and knocked unconscious by Nokt's Shatterspin in a fight at the Monastery of Gates.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • It is currently unknown if non-anthropomorphic tigers even exist in the Wyldness, since they did not appear even in the Legends of Chima series.
  • Tigers never appeared in the original show, but their existence was heavily implied by the names for species and moves, such as Triple Tiger Sashay, Templegate Tigers, Tiger Widows and Tiger wasps.
    • Their first direct appearance was as a plush toy in "Don't Rain on my Parade".
