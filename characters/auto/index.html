Auto,[2] also known as Mini Robot,[3] is the autopilot of the D.B. Express, activated to aid the driver in precarious situations. Auto briefly aided Dareth while he was trying to escape the Anacondrai Cultists, but upon realizing that they were about to go over a cliff, Auto panicked and lost control of the vehicle. It is unknown what became of Auto after Chen briefly took hold of the D.B. Express.

History

History[]

Auto was built by Nya, programmed to act as the autopilot of the D.B. Express.

Spy for a Spy[]

While Dareth fled from Eyezor and the other Warriors on Chen's Island in the D.B. Express, he activated the autopilot to navigate the perilous cliffside roads while he served as a gunner. However, Auto was only able to navigate the vehicle a short way before they reached a dead end. Auto shielded his eyes as they crashed, though they were able to remain on the road.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Auto has a small blue head with golden binoculars for eyes. His body is black with blue plating on the chest and shoulders. His upper arms, hips, and feet are light metallic gray, while his forearms and legs are black.
