Overview

Treg[1] is a competitor of the Tournament of the Sources, alongside the other Elemental Masters of his generation.

History

History[]

Enter the City of Temples[]

Treg was at the Games Plaza along with his fellow competitors.

They Gather for the Feast[]

Treg was surprised to see Zeatrix enter the buffet and later witnessed her confronting Ras and the ninja.

Inside the Maze[]

He was behind Cole before he met up with Geo.

The Sword Shatters[]

He was talking with a few spectators while Cole was discussing with Zane about the strange Monastery.

The Final Game[]

Treg was behind Wyldfyre after she stole Bleckt's ledger and got confused by its writings.

Elements of Betrayal[]

Despite not appearing during Nokt's and later Sora's winning ceremony, Treg appeared at Foundation Street afterwards.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Treg is the first Elemental Master in the franchise to be in a wheelchair.
  • He's the only known participant of the Tournament of the Sources who is never seen wearing another suit than the tournament tracksuit.
  • He has the same goggles as Ellie, but in another color, although their relation to each other is unknown.
  • He has the same beard as Twitchy Tim.
  • It is unknown what his power is, as he was never seen fighting in the Tournament.
    • His Elemental Power is not Speed.[2]
