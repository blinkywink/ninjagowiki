Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Cryokinesis - Ice Monsters can manipulate and generate ice in many ways.
    • Cryokinetic Flight - Ice Monsters can fly and propel themselves up or forward using ice.
    • Cryokinetic Breath - Ice Monsters can shoot ice from their mouths.
    • Shard Generation - Ice Monsters can launch ice shards from their claws.
    • Cryokinetic Regeneration - Ice Monsters can use ice to regenerate severed limbs.
    • Cryokinetic Infusion - Ice Monsters can infuse themselves with ice to increase the strength of their blows, especially when in ball form.
  • Spherical Form - Ice Monsters can convert themselves into a sphere for attack.
