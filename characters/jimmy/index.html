Overview

“Sorry, sir, but we got a serious problem! The phones are ringing off the hook!”
— Jimmy to the Police Commissioner, "The Darkness Comes"

Jimmy is a policeman in Ninjago City.

History

History[]

The Darkness Comes[]

Jimmy bolted into the Police Commissioner's office telling him about the phones ringing off the hook. Later, they try to stop the darkness ravaging the city only to find it is an Oni in dark mist. Jimmy, the Police Commissioner, and the officers find themselves surrounded by it, but luckily, the ninja arrive and get them into the Bounty.

Questing for Quests[]

Jimmy reports many false or non-important crimes or other things for the ninja to stop. He eventually gets the ninja's help in paper filing for the Police Commissioner.

Papergirl[]

Jimmy was with another policeman hiding behind a police car when the Merlopians attacked.

The Calm Before the Storm[]

When the Commissioner ordered Jimmy to evacuate everyone in Ninjago City in anticipation of Wojira's attack, he complied.

Nyad[]

He was hiding behind the Police Commissioner when Kalmaar confronted and threatened him.

The Turn of the Tide[]

Jimmy went to Nya's funeral.
