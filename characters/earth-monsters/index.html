Overview

Earth Monsters are a type of Elemental Monster that embody a corrupted version of Earth.

History

History[]

The Whispering Wood[]

After the Source Dragons of Focus, Life, and Strength left the Council of Sources, the disruption caused at the Sanctuary of the Seven Sources led to a mix of Source Energy with the earth, resulting in the creation of asphalt. The asphalt gave rise to an Earth Monster, which was soon accompanied by an Acid Monster. Together, they attacked the Source Dragon acolytes as the Chief Loyalist watched.

After Lloyd, Arin, and Euphrasia followed the whispers, they saw the Earth and Acid Monsters attacking some Treewoks. Lloyd tried to attack the Earth Monster, only for it to dodge and trap him in asphalt, causing the Green Ninja to realize that these monsters are made out of Elemental Powers. After Lloyd got rid of the asphalt, he tried to attack again, only for the Earth Monster to shoot asphalt at him once more and stick him to a tree. However, thanks to a power boost from the Life Source energy in the trees, Lloyd got his powers back alongside Euphrasia, defeating both the Earth Monster and Acid Monster and reverting them to their energy forms.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Asphakinesis - Earth Monsters can manipulate asphalt.
    • Asphagenesis - Earth Monsters are able to generate asphalt.
      • Tar Generation - Earth Monsters are also able to generate tar.
    • Asphalt Mimicry - Earth Monsters can turn their body completely into asphalt to avoid most attacks.
      • Reforming - When Earth Monsters are turned into asphalt, they can reform whenever they are destroyed.
    • Asphakinetic Attacks - Earth Monsters can attack with asphalt.
      • Asphakinetic Imprisonment - Earth Monsters can imprison others in asphalt.
      • Asphakinetic Blast - Earth Monsters can fire blasts of asphalt.
