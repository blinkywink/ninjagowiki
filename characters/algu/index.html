Overview

Algu[1] is a Greenbone Warrior and the current Elemental Master of Figment who was a competitor in the Tournament of the Sources, alongside the other Elemental Masters of his generation.

History

History[]

At some point in time, Algu was granted the power of Figment.

Enter the City of Temples[]

Algu was at the Games Plaza when Roby presents the City of Temples to the ninja.

They Gather for the Feast[]

Algu was at Foundation Street engaging in an arm-wrestling competition against Gorge, a battle the former soon wins.

He attends the feast and turns around to focus on Roby after he begins his speech, which is soon interrupted by Zeatrix.

Algu arrives in a hurry after Lloyd defeats the attacker bot.

Inside the Maze[]

Algu walked the streets nearby where Nya thought she saw Jay.

Truth and Lies[]

He watched the match between Euphrasia and Zeatrix.

The Sword Shatters[]

Algu faced off against Teelk and won, thus gaining the latter's powers via the Elemental Cup. This was short-lived, as he lost to Zeatrix soon after.

The Final Game[]

Algu passed Bleckt shortly before Wyldfyre saved him from a falling brick. Shortly thereafter, he walked through the City of Temples far behind Wyldfyre, who is confused by the writings in Bleckt's ledger. He walked by her after she ran into Zur. He later appeared watching the final match between Sora and Nokt until the latter won and was shocked when Wyldfyre exposed Bleckt as the one sabotaging the games in Ras' favor.

Elements of Betrayal[]

Algu fought alongside several other Elemental Masters against Cinder and the Wolf Mask Warriors in hand-to-hand combat. He eventually got his powers back after Roby drained Nokt of the stolen elements and defeated the Wolf Clan. He later watched as Roby declares Sora the default winner of the tournament. After the victory ceremony, Algu and Teelk hugged each other goodbye as they departed.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Skeleton Physiology: As a Greenbone Warrior from the Underworld, Algu has the following abilities:
    • Superhuman strength: Algu demonstrated superhuman strength, being able to beat Gorge, the Master of Size and a large Geckle, in an arm wrestling match. He also managed to battle several Wolf warriors in a fight.
    • Superhuman stamina/endurance: Algu has higher stamina and endurance than a regular human, even without his powers, as he managed to endure powerful blows from the Wolf warriors. Futhermore, in his match with Zeatrix, he managed to hold on longer against her wind blast.
  • Figment: As an Elemental Master of Figment, Algu can replicate himself to overwhelm his opponents.
  • Elemental Powers (briefly): With enough skill, Algu was able to use the Elemental Powers he won during the Tournament of the Sources, like Sound.
  • Combat: Thanks to his Figment power, Algu is a formidable combatant. He managed to defeat Teelk, thanks to his Figment, and fight off the Chaos-empowered wolf warriors even without his element.
