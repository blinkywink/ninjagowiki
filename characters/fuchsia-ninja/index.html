The Fuchsia Ninja is a member of the New Ninja.

History

History[]

Farewell the Sea[]

The Fuchsia Ninja helped take out Miss Demeanor's henchmen and introduce the team to her.

The Shape of Nya[]

Alongside his team, the Fuchsia Ninja fought against Miss Demeanor and her goons in the N line. After introducing themselves to the Ninja, the Fuchsia Ninja revealed they were all big fans of the original ninja who served as their inspiration for becoming ninja themselves. However, his compliment came off more as an insult to the original Ninja namely due to his calling of them as "old timers."

Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5![]

The Fuchsia Ninja and his team were stationed inside an armored truck carrying Aspheera's staff, specifically to protect the weapon following its former owner's escape from prison. When the ninja, disguised in all black gi, infiltrated the truck to steal the staff, the New Ninja sprung into action to defend it.

At the start of the skirmish, the Fuchsia Ninja fought against Lloyd in a grapple for the former's sword. When Lloyd was briefly distracted, the Fuchsia Ninja managed to throw him off before turning his focus onto Kai, who was attempting to break into the locker holding Aspheera's staff. As the Fuchsia Ninja swung his blade, Kai ducked and he instead shattered the cage's lock, releasing the staff! Following this slipup, the Fuchsia Ninja continued fighting the ninja until their identities were unmasked, during which he and his teammates hesitated just long enough for their former heroes to steal the staff and escape.

A Painful Promise[]

The Fuchsia Ninja and the other New Ninja arrive at the Monastery of Spinjitzu by a police helicopter before going through the elevator into the hangar bay that Kai forgot to close up. After Aspheera removed Nya's Power and escaped with Lloyd's help, the New Ninja arrest them all.

A Lesson in Anger[]

The Fuchsia Ninja and the rest of his team tried to defeat the Crystal Warriors, but were overpowered and turned into Crystal zombies.

Roots[]

After the Crystal King was defeated, the Fuschia Ninja was cured. He later helped rebuild the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

Relationships

Relationships[]

Ninja[]

Like his teammates, he idolized the original ninja. Although he says he and his teammates have the utmost respect for the original ninja, he comes off as condescending and considers them old. But when they stole Aspheera's staff, he along with his teammates became enemies with the old ninja and they arrested the entire ninja team, except Nya. In the series finale, he and the other new New Ninja are seen helping rebuild the Monastery, which could imply that they no longer have a grudge against the original ninja.
