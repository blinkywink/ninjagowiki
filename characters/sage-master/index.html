Overview

“We hope you will forgive our suspicion, good samurai, for we have faced many deadly traps and ambushes on our journey.”
— The Sage Master to Okino, "I am Okino"

The Sage Master[1] is a gamer who was trapped in Prime Empire. During his time there, he had Successful Samurai as his guide and met Okino.

History

History[]

NOTE: Most of the Sage Masters were used multiple times in group shots. As such, any notable appearances of a Sage Master will be categorized here for convenience.

The Meaning of Victory[]

A Sage Master was seen laughing at a flustered Hyper-Sonic.

The Stowaway[]

A Sage Master is one of the players walking through the crowd at Terra Technica while Jay tries to avoid Red Visors.

Manhunt[]

When Jay and Scott were walking down the downtown area of Prime Empire, he is seen gasping in surprise. A Sage Master was in the background cheering when Jay defeats a group of Red Visors.

Superstar Rockin' Jay[]

When Lloyd, Cole, Nya, and Kai entered Prime Empire, a Sage Master was talking to another gamer.

I am Okino[]

He and the Successful Samurai come out of the arcade cabinet. They then briefly talk to Okino about their journey so far, telling him that his guide has spared him from fate.

One Step Forward, Two Steps Back[]

The Sage Master was seen walking down Terra Technica. He was later seen at the dance competition.

Racer Seven[]

He was seen in the stands during the Speedway Five-Billion.

The Speedway Five-Billion[]

He was seen in the stands during the Speedway Five-Billion.

Stop, Drop and Side Scroll[]

He was seen at Terra Technica.

The Temple of Madness[]

He was seen hearing the shockwave caused back at the Temple of Madness.

Game Over[]

Multiple Sage Masters were released from Prime Empire by Unagami.
