Overview

“Hello, fabled ninja. I am Suetonius, the current Master Writer of the Cloud Kingdom, and you're welcome.”
— Suetonius to the ninja, "Writers of Destiny"

Master Suetonius is the leader of the Writers of Destiny and the current Master Writer of the Cloud Kingdom.

History

History[]

Prior to Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Sometime after Fenwick fell from the Cloud Kingdom, Suetonius became the realm's Master Writer.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Writers of Destiny[]

Suetonius observed a group of writers during a MergeQuake beside Cloud Kingdom. When the Ninja sealed off the MergeQuake and defeated an attacking Tentacled beast, Suetonius greeted their visitors while pompously adding "You're welcome," believing that and and the other writers had willed the ninja into victory.

In the ensuing conversation, Suetonius tolerated but expressed annoyance with the ninja's comments relating to the culture of Cloud Kingdom and appointed Marcus to guide them to the archives. Not long after, a second MergeQuake hit the area and Suetonius responded to Euphrasia's doubts by insisting that destiny was testing their resolve. Moments later, when Euphrasia abandoned her scrolls to take a more active approach to resolving the MergeQuake, Suetonius expressed his outrage in a controlled manner, watching as the other monks pointed out that Euphrasia was saving them from the tentacled beasts. Suetonius' beliefs would be tested again shortly after when the ninja saved the monks, yet they all admitted that none of them had been writing anything when the ninja intervened.

Following the closing of the MergeQuake, Suetonius approached Euphrasia who immediately explained that following the Merge, she had discovered she had become an Elemental Master and thus would be exiled from the Cloud Kingdom. Shaking off Euphrasia's doubts, Suetonius kindly admitted that the Merge had changed things about their world and instead of exile, he acknowledged that they would all require help in understanding the rules of this new world. In fact, Suetonius expressed hope that Euphrasia would assist him in this matter.

Later, Suetonius bid farewell to the ninja as they departed from the Cloud Kingdom.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Shattered Dreams[]

Suetonius captured the attention of the Writers of Destiny, but instead of urging them to continue writing, he emphasized that they had discovered other ways to influence the Merged Realms. This realization occurred when Euphrasia unveiled herself as the Master of Wind, a development he initially opposed. Suetonius motivated the writers to generate innovative ideas, however, they were unfortunately lacking in inspiration. To ignite their creativity, Suetonius proposed the concept of Mucoid Hat Mondays. Surprisingly, the monks abruptly ceased their writing. Observing this, Suetonius highlighted that the inability of the writers to write indicated the impending arrival of an unstoppable and dreadful event. Just as this realization sank in, a group of figures appeared, prompting Suetonius to urgently send one of the monks to fetch Euphrasia, hoping that there was still time to avert disaster. As the group drew nearer, Suetonius immediately recognized them as the Wolf Clan, filling him with horror. While Ras and Jordana infiltrated the Cloud Kingdom archives, Suetonius and Marcus hurriedly made their way there, with Suetonius vowing to defend the library with their lives. Amused by his determination, Ras declared that he needed the Writers of Destiny alive, for now. Ras revealed was the one responsible for cursing the Scroll-Worms, who had been instructed to attack. Suetonius was taken aback to discover that Ras had been behind the curse all along. As a result, the Scroll-Worms sealed Suetonius and Marcus in cocoons, leaving them trapped.

Upon receiving the news, the ninja swiftly journeyed to the Cloud Kingdom, where they successfully liberated Suetonius from his confinement. Suetonius was overjoyed to discover that Euphrasia survived her encounter with Cinder. Suetonius proceeded to recount the events that unfolded during her absence. Lloyd inquired about the scrolls Ras desired, suspecting a connection to the Blood Moon. Suetonius sadly confirmed this suspicion, elaborating on the Blood Moon and the Forbidden Five. He revealed Ras' intentions to free the Forbidden Five by unlocking their secrets and conducting the Ritual of the Blood Moon. The ninja engaged in combat with the Wolf Mask Warriors, while Suetonius ensured Euphrasia's safety.

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

Suetonius and Euphrasia were captured and witnessed the main structure of the Cloud Kingdom being separated from the rest of the realm.
