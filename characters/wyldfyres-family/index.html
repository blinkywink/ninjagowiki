Overview

“Heatwave is my family. He raised me in the Wyldness.”
— Wyldfyre to Lloyd, "Mindless Beasts"

Wyldfyre's family is the family line of the Master of Heat, Wyldfyre. It consists of her guardians: the Caregiver bot and Heatwave, with the latter's extended family.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Similar to Jay's family, the family features adoptive family members. However, Wyldyfyre's family has no known biological members.
  • It is the only family whose biological parents are never acknowledged.
  • Technically, Firstbourne could be considered part of the family due to her being Heatwave and Flamehart's ancestor, similar to Lloyd's family, however, it is unknown if Wyldfyre is aware of her existence, and she lacks any other collections with her, unlike the other members.
