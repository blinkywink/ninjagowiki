Agent Etha is a Merlopian agent who works at the HR Department in the Administration.

History

History[]

Background[]

At some point, Etha wound up in the Administration and took a position in the HR Department. At some point after Jay joined the Administration, Etha developed feelings for him, described as being "head over gills" for him by Blake Donovan.[1]

Human Resources[]

In the HR Department, Etha was working when Jay asked her to tell Lloyd, Nya, Wyldfyre, and him about Merlopian dance rituals and celebrations. After exclaiming that she had been waiting for someone to ask her that, she explained the dance rituals in great detail, putting Jay to sleep. Jay asked if the Merlopian winter dance involved a blindfold, and Etha was amazed at Jay's knowledge. She pulled out a blindfold and demonstrated the dance while Jay secretly stole her key card. He scanned it, and the four ran out of the cell while Etha continued to dance obliviously.
