Overview

“Arin, you're the only one without Elemental Powers.”
“Thanks, Euphrasia. Rub it in.”
“I mean, it's an advantage. You're the most dependable at the moment. You've never had powers, and you don't need them. Lloyd will rely on you now more than ever.”
— Euphrasia to Arin, "The Lean of a Tree"

Arin Nived[1] is the son of Barry and Jenny Nived, a self-taught Spinjitzu prodigy, creator of Object Spinjitzu, a ninja student of Lloyd's, and a former student of Ras. He is the first known person to teach himself Spinjitzu without a master. He used to live in a neighborhood outside Ninjago City, until the Merge caused his parents and region to disappear. Years after the Merge, Arin and his best friend Sora became students of Lloyd Garmadon.

A year passed and he began to express frustration at not improving his Spinjitzu. He would later discovered that Sora had lied to him about his "triumph" during the Ritual of the Blood Moon, and that Master Wu was responsible for the Merge, which led him to embrace Ras and his teachings.

During the weeks of healing and being trained with Ras, Arin eventually went to the Spectral Lands, where he met Morro, who helped him obtain an healing elixir to heal Ras. Ras would eventually take him to the graves of his parents before Ras persuaded Arin by offering to reverse the Merge to revive them. After Thunderfang absorbed Lloyd's soul, Arin, using his Object Spinjitzu, defeated the dragon singlehandedly, reviving Lloyd in the process. Although on friendly terms with the ninja now, Arin continued his journey to bring back his parents with Ras, this time, accompanied by Sora, leading them to the Forge of the Forgotten, where Arin turned against Ras after the latter attempted to sacrifice Sora in order to obtain a Dragon Icon. After he and Sora managed to escape Ras, he went back to his parents' tombstones where he discovered that they were fake and was reunited with his parents in the Community of Lee.

He would go on to restore the Unbidden Storm and assist Lloyd in guiding the Arc Dragon of Focus with Lloyd, defeating the spectral Thunderfang, being warned that they had angered the Source Dragons. Upon seeing Lloyd's Elemental Power act up, they headed to the Portal Gate in Spectral Land's Monastery, only to get sent to the Whispering Wood and reunite with Euphrasia, where they learnt of Lloyd's unnatural aging and, with the arrival of Zane, helped fend off Elemental Monsters and protect the Arc Dragon of Life and the Acona long enough for Lloyd to return from his Ritual of Unrooting in his Draconus Form, and together, they helped defeat the Loyalist assault.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Arin has coily black hair in "The Duke" hairstyle, featuring stubbled sideburns and two scars on his right cheek. From "The Merge: Part 1," to "Return to Imperium," "The Battle of the Second Monastery," and "What the Mech?," he wears a light orange hoodie with a deep orange dragon design and right sleeve and his initial 'A' colored white in Ninjargon, located above the left pocket, navy blue pants sporting his initial in a lighter color on the right pocket, a very bright celadon belt, and black gloves. Underneath his hoodie is a white t-shirt. In "The Merge: Part 1," he wore Lloyd's mask with his clothes, but neglected to wash it as it had a dirty appearance, as noted when he gave it back to Lloyd in "The Merge: Part 2." After escaping Imperium, Lloyd gave it back to him in "The Battle of the Second Monastery."

On the day of the Merge, as a child, Arin was smaller and wore a different light orange hoodie, but with the initial in the very center, no dragon design, different navy blue pants, and black gloves.

From "Return to Imperium" to "The Battle of the Second Monastery", Arin dons the disguise of an Imperium citizen under the alias of Dr. Lampshade Floortile, with three markings on his left cheek.

Sometime prior to "The Temple of the Dragon Cores," Arin changed into his own ninja suit, which consisted of the hoodie he usually wore and a different celadon belt, paired with matching light orange pants and boots with deep orange streaks, as well as a mask of the same light orange color that included a golden plate on the front with two horn-like studs of the same gold color. On his right shoulder is a deep orange shoulder pad split in two sections.

From "Enter the City of Temples" to "Elements of Betrayal," Arin donned his Tournament of Sources suit. It consisted of a sleeveless orange robe that is held by dark blue fasteners and has two front pockets and a saffron colored dragon-like pattern on the left side, as well as stitching details and his initial 'A' of the same color in Ninjargon on his bottom left. He wore matching bright orange pants with a dark blue belt and mask as well as black gloves. Underneath his robe, he wears what appears to be a gray undershirt. This attire strongly resembles his regular ninja suit.

From "The Missing" to "The Grand Inquisition," he wore a suit similar to his Merge suit, except it included a black hood and mask with ripped black fabric covering his left shoulder and sleeve. It also incorporated a black belt with a gold buckle dangling from the left side, black gloves, and various orange straps and pouches on his legs. The dragon design was swapped for a dark magenta with splattered, spray-paint like details.

From "Dying to Live" and onward, he wore another suit similar to his Merge suit. It included a yellow hood, a deep orange left sleeve and stitchings, and a dark blue mask and right sleeve with a right shoulder pad of the same color split in two sections. It also incorporated a dark blue belt, black gloves and knee straps, and a robe that strongly resembles his Tournament of Sources suit, is held by orange and gold fasteners, and has two dragon designs on both sides (dark blue on his right and deep orange on his left).

Personality

Personality[]

“The Arin I know, my best friend, his strength comes from his kindness. His generosity. His hope.”
“But all that didn't help me.”
“It helped me. And it helped everyone who ever met you.”
— Sora and Arin, "Chaos Unchained"

Arin was once an ordinary child who aspired to be a hero. However, after the Merge, he underwent a significant change. When he believed that the ninja had vanished, he strove to take on the role of protecting the world, gaining his parents' pride, and continuing the ninja's legacy. Despite his noble intentions, Arin faced several challenges in his ninja journey due to his lack of coordination and finesse compared to his peers. This often resulted in clumsy mishaps, such as self-inflicted punches.

Arin is caring, kind-hearted, compassionate, helpful, trusting, affectionate, honourable, optimistic, thoughtful and friendly. However, he can sometimes act naive and is willing to trust others easily. He is incredibly fond of his friends such as Sora, Riyu, and Lloyd.

Season 2 explores Arin's struggles as he grapples with his skills. He tries to control them while feeling the weight of expectations to become a skilled ninja. Arin's accident, which injured Egalt, his lack of Elemental Powers, and the looming threat of Ras, compound his insecurities and self-doubt from Season 1, intensifying his internal struggles. The fear of inadequacy and the need to prove himself in the face of adversity deepen Arin's inner conflicts. These conflicts are especially evident during a battle orchestrated by Ras, where his vulnerabilities and doubts were exposed.[2]

After being told by Ras that Wu was responsible for the Merge and that Sora lied to him, Arin started to become cold and willing to side with Ras to find his parents, as his teaching helped improve his Spinjitzu while Lloyd’s teachings barely helped him at all. He stopped seeing Lloyd as a role model and started to hold a grudge against Sora and pull away from her, sometimes being harsh to her when she tried to approach him. All this caused him to reach the point to where he abandoned the ninja to save Ras from the Forbidden Five.

Despite the above, Arin was quite insecure and uncertain about his decision to trust Ras, especially when the latter taught him harshly and told him to dismiss the ninja's teachings. After learning of his parents' apparent deaths, Arin broke down and decided to help Ras in his quest to reverse the merge after the latter claimed that could bring back his parents, showing the extreme yearning he had developed for his parents and a growing hatred that fuels his Spinjitzu even more. However, his insecurity deepened when he watched Ras hurt the Spirit of the Temple and was considered a villain by the Forest Dragons since he sided with Ras. Despite this, Arin had decided to accept this and became more hardened in the process with his indifference. However, though he decided to follow Ras, Arin does not reflect the same antagonism towards the ninja that his new teacher expressed, crediting them for saving the world. Furthermore, the teachings that Arin learnt as a student of Lloyd continued to influence his actions, which made acclimating to the ruthless nature of Ras a difficult challenge. While told that it was time for him to prioritize his own needs above others, Arin still demonstrated shreds of his original personality, rarely accepting violence as a first opinion and being friendly towards others, which marked a clear difference between him and Ras. He also exhibited visible hesitation when his friends in the ninja were referred to as enemies and clearly misses them deep inside, then demonstrates visible discomfort at their reunion in the World Forest, but little to no antagonism, with Ras noting that there was internal turmoil inside his student. His mellowed anger is best demonstrated through his renewed interaction with Sora after the two best friends are brought back together. Despite the rift that had flourished between them at the Tournament of Sources, Arin proves that he still cared about Sora and when the battle with the Forbidden Five broke out, the two were able to fall back into their previous teamwork without issue, which enabled them to save one another from death. Swinging from opposite sides of the same rope, when Sora asked him to return to the Monastery, Arin revealed the death of his parents to her and made no move to pull away when she pulled him into a hug that he returned after several seconds. From then on, though there was still a certain fissure between them that stemmed from their different teachers in the past few months, the two appear to have reconciled without further issue. Arin even acknowledged that he was the one at fault for their falling out and opened up to Sora about the uncertainty that he felt about his powers and how he had only grown stronger under the tutorage of Ras. When he briefly rejoined the ninja to fight against the Forbidden Five and Thunderfang, Arin had a moment of realization about himself and finally realized that his true strength lies in his positive traits, such as his kindness, and absolutely denounced Ras' teachings and advice. This allowed him to ultimately kill Thunderfang, ending the tumultuous battle and saving Lloyd, though he did return to Ras in the end out of desire to be reunited with his parents, with Sora following him, to his gratitude.

During his journey with Sora accompanying him, he is still assertive with himself and demanding his friend to take the quest seriously, showing that he has no time to mess around. However, he was still amicable and retained the heroic streak that Ras had encouraged him to suppress. The presence of Sora caused his more light-hearted nature to resurface, and Arin agreed when his best friend affirmed that the two of them were a team, then laughed when she discounted Ras in a joking manner. Like Sora, he prioritized the welfare of innocent lives and defended this effort to help the people who had been hollowed to Ras. Upon discovering Ras' intention to sacrifice Sora, Arin displayed his immense devotion to his friend, even calling her family, and completely regretted his decision to join Ras, abandoning him, despite being reminded that the reversal of the Merge was the sole means to restore his parents to life. Upon escaping from the Underworld, Arin broke down into tears, believing that his parents were truly gone and admitting to Sora that he was scared of this change. Arin would unexpectedly reunite with his parents and the ninja later, much to his joy. He seems to have learned from his disastrous time with Ras and become much calmer after finding his parents, but still showed insecurities about his role as a ninja, only helping his friends face Thunderfang out of kindness and generosity to release the captured souls and have people reunite with their loved ones and stating that he intends to quit the ninja because he is at his limits and showing his tiredness with his situation. The effort to stop Thunderfang caused Arin to realise that the path of a Ninja was his calling however, though he intended to take some time off with his parents before the resumption of his training.

Even in the Whispering Wood, Arin continued to show great remorse for leaving the Ninja for Ras, calling his decisions stupid and that his obsession with finding his parents made him vulnerable to the tiger’s manipulation. When he initially thought they were to deceased, he eventually realized they were alive, but in the Land of Lee, and knew Lloyd was just under a lot of pressure and dealing with lots of things that he doesn't blame him for. This shows Arin has matured quite a bit, and he was always capable of admitting that he and his decisions were wrong.

Weapons and abilities

Weapons and abilities[]

Abilities[]

“Your self-taught Spinjitzu is a bit wonky, but it's incredible you learned it on your own. It makes sense to me you have other skills you haven't tapped into yet. We just have to learn what's holding you back.”
— Lloyd to Arin, "Gangs of the Sea"
  • Spinjitzu: Arin's relentless efforts to decipher the maneuvers performed by the ninja in order to master Spinjitzu have led him to develop a rudimentary version of the art, distinct from the more sophisticated forms. Lloyd's observations acknowledged that Arin possesses untapped Spinjitzu abilities and talents beyond his foundational skills, including Object Spinjitzu, which he has yet to explore.[3][4] After using the advice that Frak and Lord Ras had given him, Arin was able to achieve a full version of Spinjitzu, however due to using a forceful manner to perfect it, the technique carries some wisps of Chaos energy found in Shatterspin. As a result, he can also perform abilities associated with Shatterspin using his Spinjitzu.[5]
    • Object Spinjitzu: Arin possesses the ability to transfer his Spinjitzu energy into any inanimate object he throws. These objects possess explosive power, and severely damage anything they hit. It can only be used when Arin feels a strong need to protect others.
  • Skilled combatant: Arin is skilled in combat. Due to being mostly self taught, he managed to evade multiple attacks. Arin initially to Ras, but after a year of training under Lloyd, he managed to defeat several Wolf warriors by himself. By the time of the Tournament of Sources, Arin's skills improved where he managed to hold against Nokt for a brief time and pin him down with Ras’ help. Training under Ras also helped Arin improve his skills, fighting off Soul Suckers and briefly against Jay (in his armor). Later on, with the help of Sora, he was able to fight on par with Ras but didn't best him in combat as the tiger was only left stuck under a rockslide that was caused by both of their strikes.
  • Baking: Arin is good at baking, his speciality being, pies.
  • Deductive Intelligence: Even though Arin is simple, straight-forward, and optimistic, he has shown to be very skilled at solving mysteries which he learned from his parents, being able to solve what happened to the Matriarchs and Patriarchs of the 3 dragon tribes and what find happened to their horns.

Weapons[]

  • Grapple Gun: At some point after the Merge, Arin obtained a grappling hook that Sora created for him, which he uses to get around places or as a weapon.
  • Throwing stars: Arin regularly uses throwing stars.
  • Imperium Sword (briefly): Arin briefly used an Imperium Sword but accidentally almost shot himself when he had it in blaster mode.
  • Returning Blade: Formerly owned by Ras, Arin wielded the Returning Blade until it was taken by Drix. Although he managed to get it back after Thunderfang was freed, it was broken after fighting Spectral Dragonians in Mysterium.
  • Lightning Dagger (briefly): Arin briefly used one of Rogue's Lightning Daggers when fighting him in the World Forest.
  • Weapon of Desolation (briefly): Arin briefly used the Weapon of Desolation to deactivate it, sealing the Elemental Monsters away and saving Imperium and the Whispering Wood in the process.

Etymology

Etymology[]

Arin is a gender-neutral name of Irish origins. It means "high mountain; enlightened; warrior lion." The name is also translated as "first rays of the sun; joy; peace" but the origins of these meanings are unknown.

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • In early concept, Arin was originally named Max, but was possibly changed to avoid confusion with the LEGO Life character of the same name.[14]
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt has stated that Arin has a last name,[15] which was later confirmed to be "Nived" in "I Alone Can Save Them".
  • While Arin wasn't written to be autistic, Chris "Doc" Wyatt sees no issue with fans head-canon him to be.[16]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Arin is the first character to speak and appear in Ninjago: Dragons Rising.
  • He has an unfortunate habit of punching himself in the face.
    • He likely stopped doing this once he got better during his training with Lloyd and later Ras.
  • He once tried to have Zane's hairstyle, which, according to Sora, did not go so well. Both Kai and Cole share the belief that having Zane's hairstyle isn't the most ideal decision.[17][18][19]
    • Chamille was the one who gave Arin the haircut.[20]
  • In "Beyond Madness", he said that Kai was his favorite ninja, then after Sora smiled at him, he changed to "second favorite". It can be inferred that Sora is his "first favorite ninja", but other critics might said Lloyd is his favorite ninja given that Sora at that time is a student instead of a "fully well-trained ninja"
  • Arin's sweatshirt's hood is a modified version of Scott's hood, with the hat replaced with his hair.
  • His sweatshirt bears similar designs to the DX Suits.
  • Arin is one of eight known people who know Spinjitzu but lack Elemental Powers. The other seven are Doubloon, Aspheera, Misako, Master Yang, Nineko, Rontu, and Egalt. Arin is unique out of them due to being self-taught.
  • He is the first, and so far the only, ninja who is not an Elemental Master.
    • This is further confirmed by Chris "Doc" Wyatt who has stated that Arin does not have an elemental power,[21]As such, he has no connection to the Source Dragons.[22]
  • His mother taught him how to bake pies.[13]
    • However, his former best friend Frak taught him how to bake Merlopian berry cream pie before the Merge.
  • He is the third ninja to perform the Electric Jaya.[23]
  • Despite being the Ninja's biggest fan, Arin doesn't seem to know all the enemies they've faced.
    • He didn't know who The Mechanic was before "The Mech Master", even though he knew about the Ninja's adventures in Prime Empire during Season 12 of the original series.
    • He didn't know who Morro was before "The Spectral Lands", even though he had heard about the existence of the other realms before the Merge, which were revealed during Season 5 of the original series, the season in which Morro was the main antagonist.
    • He didn't seem to recognize Kruncha and Nuckal in "Casket of Bones", even though they were one of the first enemies the Ninja faced during the pilot episodes.
    • He also didn't seem to know of Lloyd using the Tomorrow's Tea to accelerate his age.
      • As of the Season 4 episode "The Lean of a Tree" Arin is now aware that Lloyd skipped over his childhood using the Tomorrow's Tea.
  • Arin's arc in Season 2 of Dragons Rising about wanting to get better at being a ninja is similar to what Kai went through during Season 1 of the original series.
  • Arin can perfectly understand Riyu's words.[24]
  • Arin is the first member of the new generation of ninja to utter the phrase "NINJA GO!!" for the first time.
  • Arin has horns on his hood because he thinks they look cool.[25]
    • This is similar to Kai wearing a bandage near his left eyebrow, he also thought it looked cool.
  • In "Chaos Unchained," it is implied that he either has not washed Lloyd's old hood, or it is permanently stained due to years of use without washing.
    • Though it's likely the former since Arin didn't know he could wash Lloyd's hood until the latter asked him about it in "The Merge: Part 2".
  • Arin has heard of the Firstbourne but only to a minor extent.[26]
  • Arin's relationship with Lloyd shares many parallels with Wu's relationship with Morro.
    • Both Arin and Morro were their masters' first students who went to great lengths to prove themselves worthy in the eyes of their master.
    • Both left their masters as they believed they failed them since they couldn't provide what they needed.
    • Both Wu and Lloyd express great sadness about their pupil's leaving.
  • Arin also shares some similarities with Harumi.
    • Both at a young age admired and idolized the Ninja.
    • Both lost their parents due to actions of their heroes (Harumi lost her parents when the Great Devourer was released by Pythor who was freed by Lloyd, and Arin lost his parents in the Merge caused by Wu).
  • Arin is the third ninja to have his surname revealed, following Lloyd and Jay, however, similar to the latter, his last name was revealed through his parents.
  • Arin's last name is a nod to his voice actor Deven Mack's first name, albeit spelled backwards and with an "i" instead.[27]
    • Deven Mack would later poke fun at this idea of Arin's last name being his first name but backwards and with an "i" during San Diego Comic-Con 2026.
  • When asked if Arin will retire from his role as a ninja when Dragons Rising ends, Chris "Doc" Wyatt replied that spoiler but noted that the question assumes Arin will survive the events of Dragons Rising, which is a hopeful outlook based on what they are facing.[16]

History

Early life

Early life[]

Arin grew up in a small neighborhood with his parents, Barry and Jenny, outside of Ninjago City, where he became a fan of the ninja. He always loved detective work, and his parents would always play Track with him or read him detective books. Whenever he was sad, his mother would make him a pie before she eventually began teaching him how to bake.[1] At some point, he was teased by some bullies due to being a Ninja fan, only to be scared off by a young Serpentine named Frak, who was also a fan of the ninja, and with whom he befriended. Frak taught Arin how to make a pie with Merlopian ingredients, and the two kids were playing ninja together all the time. One day, Frak had to move away, leaving Arin disappointed.

While practicing ninja moves with a broom one day, a strange earthquake happened as several cracks showing the realms appeared in the sky. Arin's parents told him to get inside the house. Although he tried, Arin was blown away by the wind until he was rescued by Lloyd. Along with a few other citizens, Arin was taken back to his neighborhood. As the ninja left, Arin noticed the cracks in the sky disappearing before creating a shockwave. Shortly after, Arin saw his entire neighborhood gone while the Kingdom of Imperium was located right across. That was when Arin learned that all sixteen realms had merged with Ninjago. Arin found himself to be the only one left in the area as his parents' bench fell behind him. He tried calling for them, only for a seagull to fly by as Lloyd's mask fell onto his hands.[2]

After the Merge

After the Merge[]

Crashing Together (flashback)[]

With his parents gone, Arin began a new life in the Merged Realms, moving in the Crossroads newly established where his neighborhood once stood, coexisting with various denizens and starting baking Snowberry Pies. In the Crossroad Arin meet Sora, a girl who came from Imperium, with whom he became best friend and move in together. Together they also got to know Alfonzo Frohicky and Arin entered into multiple pie contests, but always lost to Grab-Barg. Because the ninja were missing, Arin started to carry on their legacy by training to become a ninja. Sora built him a Grapple Gun as his ninja weapon, and over time, he was able to learn a rudimentary version of Spinjitzu all by himself.[2][3]

First Try![]

One day in the Crossroads, Arin and Sora spotted a poster advertising for the preliminary rounds of the Mech Master 5000. Desiring to enter, Sora brought Arin to Skalidor's Junkyard in search of parts. Amongst the offerings were half of Kai's Fire Mech, the sight alone being enough to amaze Arin as he recounted how the mech was one used to combat the Stone Army many years ago. Unfortunately, Arin's joy was cut short when Skalidor revealed the price for the Fire Mech's remains, leading him to protest that it was nothing more than scrap these days. Some time later, Arin helped Sora haul away the scrap they bought, though Arin grumbled that Sora had been talked into buying a cart for all the money they managed to save by haggling with Skalidor.

Sometime later, Arin helped Sora in constructing a new mech for the upcoming race, all the while talking about the ninja and how he dreamed of one day being one himself. When the mech was finished, Arin exclaimed that they might be able to win the preliminary rounds with it! As they later participated in the preliminary rounds, Sora instructed Arin to take control of the mech's arms and urged him to stay calm throughout the race. Taking off, Arin helped defend against an Underworld Mech but soon groaned that they were unlikely to bypass Kreel, who had outfitted her mech with all sorts of nasty surprises. Lobbo lost control over his mech shortly afterwards and smashed into the cockpit of Sora and Arin's mech, though the new hole allowed Arin to use his grappling gun to connect with Kreel's mech, towing them into second place!

Now that they qualified in the preliminary rounds, Arin and Sora walked off to make preparations for the Mech Master 5000, during which Arin noted that Kreel seemed awfully confident that she'd win the coming race.

Tales from the Merged Realms[]

The Way In[]

At the penthouse he shared alongside his best friend, Sora, Arin assisted the latter in preparing her mech for the upcoming Mech Master 5000. After a less than pleasant surprise visit by Kreel left Sora feeling dejected and hopeless, Arin encouraged her not to give up, insisting that she was one of the best mech pilots in the Crossroads.

The next day, Arin stood on a street corner and tried performing Spinjitzu moves to entertain passerby for, holding out a hat afterwards for donations, hoping to raise enough money to pay for Sora's registration fee for the race. Unfortunately, Arin was only able to obtain two coins after several hours, filling Sora with despair once more. Arin then thought back to his own childhood and how his mother would always bake him a pie whenever he felt down. Believing that this would help cheer up his friend, Arin got to work baking an appleberry pie and presented it to Sora when it was finished.

A few hours later, Arin offered the pie to Alfonzo Frohicky, the manager of the Mech Master 5000, in lieu of money for Sora's registration fee. Although Mr. Frohicky declined at first, one whiff of the pie's delicious smell was more than enough to get him to change his mind. After Sora came in last place in the race, Arin rushed over to her side, fearing that she would be upset and disappointed about her loss. To his surprise, Sora actually declared this to have been the best race ever and that she looked forward to pushing herself harder going forward and to make even greater improvements to her mech so that she could start winning.

Dragons Rising, Season 1

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

Arin and Sora went to the Crossroads' junkyard to obtain mechanical parts for an engine. To distract the guard dogs, Arin told them the story of the Merge and how he plans to become a ninja. Despite Sora's protests, Arin believed he needed a ninja master to train him. Just as Sora found the piece, she and Arin were caught by the Security Bots. Arin used his Spinjitzu to knock away one of the Security Bots, only to be bounced back. Sora asked Arin if he can distract the robots. Although Arin thought his intent to tell the robots about the Merge wouldn't work, Sora tells him to think of something else. So Arin used his grappling hook to swing over the robots, distracting them as Sora hacked into one of the robots. As the robots began dancing, Arin expressed concern about how Sora can hack things so fast. To make up for the stolen mechanical piece, Arin placed a Snowberry Pie for the owners of the junkyard to get. He then left with Sora to return to their penthouse.

While going back, Arin expressed joy about how amazing the Kingdom of Imperium is, despite Sora's hatred towards the city. Arin said he wanted to explore other places since he rarely ventured outside his neighborhood. Although Sora explained that the Crossroads already has the best places from the realms, Arin said that it's different since the people of the Crossroads only live there because they had nowhere else to go because of the Merge. As they reached their penthouse, Arin was challenged to see who would get there first. By using his grappling hook, Arin beat Sora, but ended up hurting himself as a result. After making another Snowberry Pie, Arin and Sora used the parts they took from the junkyard to build necessary components for their mech to participate in the Mech Master 5000, Arin hoping to use the prize money to build a monastery.

On the day of the race, Arin bribed Alfonzo with the pie to let him and Sora race, but they were later forced to abandon it, when a baby dragon was being hunted down by the Claws of Imperium before rescuing him, and attempt to get away, but their racer had previously been sabotaged by a rival, knocking them off the bridge. When the dragon transferred his power to Sora, allowing her to power to transform the racer into a flying mech, it surprised Arin before they attempt to fight the hunters, only to be incapacitated by Lord Ras.

En route to Imperium on a prison train, Arin couldn't help but wonder what fate awaited them. Sora, however, started discussing how the Claws of Imperium were tasked to hunt dragons, as their life force was crucial for powering the technology in the Kingdom of Imperium. Arin questioned the safety of dragon power, as it was considered safe. But Sora challenged that notion. Changing the subject, Arin couldn't help but mention Sora's feat of transforming the SA Mark 01 into a flying mech. Arin drew a parallel between Sora's powers and the Elemental Powers possessed by the ninja. He confessed that he had always dreamt of having such powers himself. Sora informed Arin that not everything was connected to the ninja. Arin voiced his belief that if he lost hope in the return of the ninja, he also lost hope in his parents' return, as hope was the only thing that he hadn't lost since the Merge . Sora acknowledged that hope alone wouldn't be sufficient. Arin couldn't deny the truth in Sora's words. Suddenly, they heard a noise from their compartment and looked up to see their savior, to Arin's surprise: Lloyd.

The Merge: Part 2[]

Lloyd frees Arin and Sora before Arin returned Lloyd's mask to him, where he was appalled by Arin not washing it before they exit through the roof. Arin then surprises Lloyd by doing Spinjitzu, which was the main reason he thought there was a ninja on the train before moving to rescue the dragon, who compliantly licks him, and watched him empower Sora again to turn the Imperians' swords into bombs and they quickly get off the train. While getting acquainted and hoping to warn the dragon's herd, Arin notices him make a sculpture of his home: The Land of the Three Mountains.

As Sora got to work on Lloyd's new Destiny's Bounty, Arin took to time to get reacquainted with Lloyd since when the latter rescued him during the Merge and what happened afterward. Arin then told Lloyd his reappearance is fate and that he was meant to train him and Sora, something the latter believed he was unreliable and that Wu should be the one, but he isn't around.

Once Sora got the Bounty working, they make their way to the Three Mountains, where they discover the Claws of Imperium had followed them and shot them down. Only for the Matriarch of the Mountain Dragons to save them. Using telepathy, the Matriarch accuses them of harming the baby dragon, identified as Riyu, but is reassured by him before they move to deal with the hunters to prove their trust. During the fight, Arin, in his Spinjitzu tornado, crashed into a tree. After Lloyd, empowered by the Matriarch, defeats them and the herd prepare to relocate, Arin and Sora said their goodbyes to Riyu, only for him to want to stay with them, with Lloyd complying that he will be their master.

Crossroads Carnival[]

Arin was more eager to be trained by Lloyd than Sora. After training for the whole day, he, Sora, and Riyu, both disguising him like a dog, attend the Crossroads Carnival with Arin determined to beat Grab-Barg. They then notice pieces of their mech were salvaged and used as a DJ set, but Arin was more focused on the content before they head over to Mr. Frohicky to enter it. As he does so, Grab-Barg deliberately bumps him with his shell before he mocks him about being put in second again, even calling Riyu a "weird little dog". During their argument, however, they spot Whack Rats kidnapping Lobbo for his prize money, and he, Sora, and Riyu follow them to the junkyard, where another rival racer Kreel needed the money to pay for her friend.

From Kreel's information, they search the drop point and discover a secret hatch which leads to a stage with many Whack Rats in a cage, drained by magician Dorama, who didn't want to be upstaged by rivals. Narrowly escaping his traps, Arin used a broom to shut it down before Dorama unleashed a puppet on them, only for Lloyd, who had been following a trail of his meditation carpet's fabric, to come to their rescue. The puppet, despite being off its strings, then went to attack again, but Arin grabs a gramophone horn and, using Spinjitzu, threw it at the puppet to expose gears on its chest. The move of transferring Spinjitzu into items was a surprise to him and Lloyd before he used the fabric pieces through a fan to jam the puppet's gears, destroying it, and Riyu prevents Dorama from escaping.

Returning Targle to Kreel, they went back to the pie baking stall, where Arin was disappointingly placed in second again, but was relieved when Lloyd told him that they will participate the following year.

Beyond Madness[]

In the morning, Arin eagerly partook in the Monastery's obstacle course but had to be rescued by Lloyd when he fell. After thanking Lloyd, Arin sheepishly remarked that he was probably the "worst student in the history of students," though Lloyd denied such notions. Riyu[]

“Riyu? Is your name Riyu, buddy?”
— Arin to Riyu, "The Merge: Part 2"

Riyu is a dragon that Arin and Sora first saved from the Claws of Imperium before they became ninja, and remained with them after saving his herd from them. He became very close, friendly, loyal and affectionate towards him and even Sora. He became distraught when Beatrix sent Riyu into a MergeQuake rift that he sent her into one, proclaiming it was for the baby dragon. When Riyu was among those that were brought back when the storm was over, Arin, along with Sora, was happy to see him again. He and Sora love Riyu very much, as their pet, friend and even as a member of their family, going as far to consider him their best friend.[2]

Arin and Riyu continued training together even after the latter grew taller but was unable to fly. He and Riyu accompany the ninja to the Dragon Masters' Dojo, where Arin had been assigned Second Fang with Riyu in First Fang, until the latter solemnly looked on when Egalt demoted Arin for half-blinding him. When the Blood Moon interrupted their training, Arin and Riyu end up separated by the ninja in the storm, where they try to rescue Bonzle, but fail when Ras took them down.

Riyu accompanied Arin and Sora on their mission to The Administration and was with them when they witnessed the death of The Matriarch of the Mountain Dragons. He and Arin flew into the City of Temples and were about to be arrested by the guards before Frak helped them. Riyu and Arin took a tour around the Temple City with Frak. While Lloyd, Cole, Nya, and Sora competed in the Cavern Run, Arin and Riyu snuck into Ras' hut.

After Arin left with Ras at the end of the tournament, Riyu was sad and participated in the search to find him.

In "Chaos Unchained", Arin fights alongside Riyu and the rest of the Ninja to stop the Forbidden Five from collecting the other two Prismatic Blades. When Thunderfang was summoned in "The Shatter Dragon", they worked together to defeat him. When Arin and Sora left with Ras, Riyu showed signs of sadness to see his two best friends leave and especially with someone like Ras.

Euphrasia[]

“Sorry hanging out with us got you into this.”
“I'm ready to write this destiny with you, however it turns out.”
— Arin and Euphrasia, "The Heartbeat of all the Realms"

Euphrasia, the Elemental Master of Wind, shares a deeply supportive and rapidly evolving relationship with Arin.

In "Writers of Destiny", Arin and Euphrasia met for the first time when the Ninja traveled to the Cloud Kingdom. During a chaotic MergeQuake, Euphrasia discreetly used her newly discovered wind powers to save Arin and Sora from falling off a collapsing tower. After Arin and Sora noticed her abilities and confronted her, Euphrasia initially denied it out of fear of being exiled by the Cloud Monks. Upon learning about her secret, Arin openly shared his own insecurities with her, admitting that he deeply wished he possessed an Elemental Power of his own. Despite her initial hesitation, Euphrasia ultimately abandoned her strict duties to help Arin and the Ninja fight off the attacking tentacled beasts, establishing a baseline of trust and mutual respect between them.

In "The Whispering Wood", when Lloyd, Arin and Euphrasia reunited in the Whispering Wood, Arin was happy to see her again and was sorry to see her stranded there too to which she responded with smile.

In "The Lean of a Tree" Arin openly expressed heavy remorse and self-doubt regarding his lack of elemental powers and past mistakes. Euphrasia gently held his hand and reassured him that being a non-elemental ninja is actually an advantage, explaining that his dependable nature makes him the exact ally Lloyd needs to rely on.

In "Tonight We Hunt Monsters", Euphrasia temporarily lost control of her malfunctioning wind powers and plummeted from the sky, but Arin successfully caught her, leading to a lingering moment between the two.

In "Just Under the Surface", Arin physically guided Euphrasia to teach her the core movements of Spinjitzu. They later put this into practice to defend the Arc Dragon of Life, combining Euphrasia's wind-hurled rocks with Arin's Object Spinjitzu tornado to defeat the attacking elemental monsters. Following the fierce confrontation, Arin was knocked unconscious, prompting a deeply worried Euphrasia to immediately rush to his side alongside Zane to ensure he was safe.

Wyldfyre[]

“For the record, I'm the one that found it.”
“It was smart of Bleckt to hide the room's access in this old thing. No way Roby was gonna touch a rotary phone.”
“I still don't know what this is.”
— Wyldfyre and Arin, "Clues and Suspects"

Wyldfyre is Kai's student. They didn't have much interaction, although Arin gave Wyldfyre a brief reprimand for her actuality of relieving herself wherever she wants, and wondering if she'll ever train with him and the others. During the Tournament of the Sources, Arin and Wyldfyre both searched for clues for Bleckt's involvement in Ras' plan to use the Forbidden Five.

When Thunderfang was summoned in "The Shatter Dragon", they worked together alongside the Ninja to defeat him.

Kai[]

“Not bad, kid! You really... should probably work on that.”
— Kai to Arin, "Return to Imperium"

Kai is Arin's second favorite ninja. While their introduction was brief, they were able to speak properly, where Kai was surprised to hear Arin learned Spinjitzu without proper training.

Cole[]

“So, how do I shut this terrifying thing down?”
“Dr. LaRow, she activated it by saying some enchantment backwards.”
“Okay, how does that help us now?”
— Cole and Arin, "Birthright"

Cole is Lloyd's fellow ninja. They first met when Cole returned to the monastery upon discovering many dangerous artifacts including Chronosteel, were brought to Kreel's junkyard.

They both went to the Monastery of Gates together, where they learn from the spirit of Wu that he was responsible for the Merge, which Cole refused to believe while Arin, due to Ras' deception, did.

After a brief separation due to Arin following Ras, he and Cole manage to reunite when Arin got ahold of the Weapon of Desolation, which the latter tells Arin the reversal spell to send the Elemental Monsters back into the Weapon.

Zane[]

“Zane, did you... reprogram yourself or something? "Bummer" didn't use to be part of your vocab.”
“My time with Pixal did wonders for my disposition. Some might say I'm more "chill," and when did you rejoin the team, Arin?”
“Oh, man, you've missed a lot!”
— Arin and Zane, "A Wonder of the Merged Lands"

Zane is one of Lloyd's fellow ninja. He once tried to have Zane's hairstyle, but did not go so well. When Arin told Zane that Wu caused the Merge, Zane found it hard to process.

After Arin betrayed the ninja to save Ras from the Forbidden Five, by entering the portal gate, Zane showed concern for Arin, as the portal gate explosion sent Arin somewhere in the Merged Lands.

Upon reuniting in the Whispering Wood, Zane inquires as to his presence in rejoining the ninja.

Nya[]

“That's Nya. She's one of the original ninja. Didn't you read the scrolls Lloyd gave us?”
— Arin to Sora about Nya, "Beyond Madness"

Nya is one of Lloyd's fellow ninja. Their introduction was hostile when Nya attacked Arin for aiding the Earth Dragons, until they were able to settle their differences. They were able to speak properly not long after, where Nya was surprised to hear Arin learnt Spinjitzu without proper training.

Alfonzo Frohicky[]

“So you don't want a fresh pie, filled with roasted snowberries from the Never-Realm? Fine, I guess we'll just have to toss it. Maybe the dogs are hungry. Or maybe the raccoons?”
“Well, it'd be a shame to just throw it out.”
— Arin and Mr. Frohicky, "The Merge: Part 1"

Mr. Frohicky is Arin's friend and fellow resident in the Crossroads before moving to the monastery. Arin often bribed him with his Snowberry Pies, which he accepts. When Lloyd was struggling to maintain the monastery, Arin brought Mr. Frohicky in to assist maintaining it.

Percival Tartigrade (former enemy)[]

“It's kinda weirding me out how we're on the same side now, Percival.”
— Arin to Percival, "We Are All Dragons"

Percival is leader of the Imperium Teen Resistance Force, whom Arin saved from a damaged scaffolding. Before discovering he was harbouring Riyu, Percival was impressed by Arin's skills, known as "Dr. Lampshade Floortile" to him, until the former discovered Riyu. After the ninja broke the dragons out, Beatrix spreading lies, running an investigation in her involvement in her father's death, and researching dragons, Percival and the teens save him and the ninja from the Claws of Imperium before working out their differences in order to expose Beatrix. Arin and Percival meet each other again when Percival brought Euphrasia to the Monastery of Spinjitzu in order to tell him and the ninja what happened in the Cloud Kingdom.

Morro[]

Arin meet Morro in the Spectral Lands. Despite knowing about the Ninja's adventures Arin was unaware that the departed Ninja was Morro, the former Master of Wind.

Jay (former enemy)[]

Like the other Ninja, Arin idolized Jay, even having a poster of Jay in his room.

When Arin infiltrated the Administration with Sora to find records of his parents, the records were left unfinished when Jay escaped from there. Later during the Tournament of the Sources, when he was shocked that Jay is with the Wolf Clan and told Nya about what happened to him during the Merge, he found out that Jay is Agent Walker and was worried about him shattering his goodness with the Wolf Mask when he was about to perform Shatterspin. After Jay left the City of Temples, he thought that he might have the records to help him find his parents. It’s unknown how he feels about him after he left the Ninja.

After Jay became a bounty hunter, he was after Ras who Zeatrix placed a bounty on and Arin fought him without even knowing who he really was. And after Thunderfang’s defeat, Jay revealed to the ninja that Arin's parents were still alive and that he knew their whereabouts. He agreed to help locate them, on the condition that he be paid.

In "Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon", Arin was unconcern of Jay, when Wyldfyre turned the ship, and caused Jay to almost fall off but ended up hitting his head. Although Arin did found out that Jay didn't unshatter his goodness or recover his memories.

In "The Vault of Focus", Arin showed annoyance to Jay's rude behavior commenting of romantic relationships and attacking the Security Bots.

It is likely that, after the Loyalist assault, Arin now knows that Jay has regained his memory and so, their relationship has improved.

Enemies

Enemies[]

Beatrix[]

“That's for Riyu.”
— Arin to Beatrix, "The Power Within"

Beatrix is Arin's enemy. Arin is aware that Beatrix was responsible for enslaving dragons and torturing them for their energy and worked hard to stop her. After joining The Resistance, Arin combats her tyranny and was pleased when Sora revealed to the entire Imperium about her true colors. In “The Power Within”, Arin’s dislike for Beatrix turned into complete hatred after the Empress sent Riyu to the rift, during the MergeQuake Storm. Full of rage and hatred, Arin performed Object Spinjitzu with a rock at Beatrix’s mech, greatly damaging it. After Sora achieved her True Potential and disassembled her MergeQuake weapon, Arin took the chance to perform Spinjitzu and kick Beatrix into the rift, where she disappears.

They reunite years later when a group of Imperians still loyal to her called the Loyalists invade Imperium. He ultimately used the Weapon of Desolation she used to control the Elemental Monsters to send them back.

Rapton (temporary ally)[]

“I thought you and the Claws of Imperium were on our side now. You helped us take down the Empress.”
“I was never on your side. I was just on the Empress’ side even less.”
— Arin and Rapton, “The Shape of Motion”

Arin had a massive dislike for Rapton due to his goal of capturing and draining dragons. When Beatrix became too obsessed with defeating the ninja, Rapton defected to the Resistance, to Arin's surprise before reluctantly putting their differences aside to expose Beatrix.

After Beatrix's defeat and thwarting the MergeQuake Storm, their feelings for each other were mutual, until Rapton began hunting dragons for money, reigniting his hatred for him.
