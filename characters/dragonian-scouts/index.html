Overview

The Dragonian Scouts were soldiers of the Spectral Dragonians.

History

History[]

Prior to The Hollow Ones[]

Following Thunderfang's death, he took over the Spectral Lands and assembled an army of ghosts, consisting of the Forbidden Five alongside the Dragonian Trackers who had died while still corrupted by the Sobrient Stone. Among the fallen Dragonian Warriors, some became Dragonian Scouts while also gaining abilities reminiscent of the Soul Suckers.

Human Resources[]

During the second invasion of Mysterium by the Spectral Dragonians, led by Kur, the Dragonian Scouts launched an assault on the populace, capturing several souls in the process. Arin attempted to intervene with the Returning Blade but was thwarted when a Dragonian Scout destroyed the weapon. One Scout targeted Gandalaria, forcefully knocking her into a barrel within the Crystal Ball Repair Store, while another Scout aimed to attack both her and Ras. Gandalaria, resolute in her defense of the village, was ambushed from behind, resulting in her soul being taken for Kur. Just as another Scout prepared to strike, the sun began to rise, forcing the Spectral Dragonians and Kur to retreat. However, they returned the next night to invade the Administration.

Casket of Bones[]

The Spectral Dragonians joined Drix to the Administration, with four of them being Dragonian Scouts. They captured the souls of several agents, including Underwood and a Munce. Afterward, they returned to the Spectral Lands with Drix, where Thunderfang and the ghostly Hollow Ones awaited their arrival.

The Screaming Earth[]

The Spectral Dragonians returned to the Administration, resolutely continuing their mission to capture the souls of the Administration Agents. They tracked Agent Roderick Allen through a portal that transported them to the Land of Lee. Upon Sora activating her Prismatic Lamp, the light unveiled the Spectral Dragonians emerging from the portal. A Dragonian Scout subdued Lee and seized his soul, resulting in all the Lees becoming Hollow Ones.
