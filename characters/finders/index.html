Overview

“You lose it, we find it. Hence, the Finders.”
— Spitz, "Land of Lost Things"

The Finders are a family of runaways who found themselves lost in the Land of Lost Things.

History

History[]

Background[]

The Finders were those who experienced a sense of rejection and being unwanted within their respective communities. Consequently, they made the difficult decision to escape from their homes. Unfortunately, their journey led them to become lost and forgotten, ultimately leading them to the Land of Lost Things in Imperium. Geo ended up in the Land of Lost Things when he was a child after being rejected by the Munce and Geckles,[1] Fritz ended up there after his village was attacked by a monster,[2] and Spitz and Bonzle arrived at an unknown point after Geo and together they formed their new family. Trapped within the valley, they found themselves unable to escape due to the presence of an enigmatic fog. Following the Merge, the Finders crossed paths with Cole. Despite having the opportunity to depart from the valley, Cole made the conscious decision to remain there, safeguarding the Finders from the Hoarder.[3]

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Fritz and Spitz encountered Sora and Nya when the latter two arrived at the Land of Lost Things in search of the Yellow Dragon Core. Fleeing from the strangers, they led the pair to Cole who initially attacked the strangers, believing that they were after the children. After misgivings were cleared up and Cole recognized Nya, he properly introduced her and Sora to the finders. They later worked together to defeat the Hoarder after the yellow Dragon Core was stolen by Dr. LaRow.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Wolf Clan arrived in the Land of Lost Things to search for Bonzle, causing a fight between them and the Finders. Even though they were able capture her for a while, they were distracted by Cole's arrival, allowing him and the Finders to escape to the Monastery of Spinjitzu through the portal gate thanks to Jordana lifting the boundary spell that prevented them from leaving the land. In the monastery Bonzle explained the situation to Zane, Cole and Geo. Then they noticed that Spitz and Fritz were playing with SA Mark 01, only to lose control. Thankfully, Cole was able to stop them. Then Zane, Cole and Bonzle decided to fly to Mysterium with Jiro after Fedulian gave them a map, only to end up in the foggy desert where Dorama's puppet captured them and took them to Dorama's Academy for Acting. Thankfully, the ninja was able to get out of there with the help of Bonzle and arrived in the Monastery of Mysterium, where they meet Gandalaria. After the Sorceress reunited with Bonzle, the ninja explained her the situation they were in. Luckily, Gandalaria accepted to help them, only for the Administration Agents to arrive and Blood Moon to rose as the other Finders witnessed it from the monastery. While Gandalaria and the ninja were trying to push them back, Bonzle hid in the passages with Sprite, only for Agent Walker to confront them. Thankfully, Bonzle was able to get out there with the help of Sprite and left the monastery with the ninja and Gandalaria. Gandalaria took them to the Magical storagehouse, where she sealed Bonzle in a protective container, just in time for Jordana to intervene and take Bonzle with her. As she was heading to the Shadow Dojo with the Explorer, she was stopped by Arin and Riyu, who took control of the Explorer. Unfortunately, Ras defeated both of them and took Bonzle with him, planning to use her for the Ritual of the Blood Moon. During the ritual, Jordana returned Bonzle back to her spell form and used her to open a portal to the Nether-Space. However, Bonzle tried to close the portal gateway, telling Kai to get out of the Nether-Space. At the end, Bonzle closed the gate, trapping her and Kai in the Nether-Space.

After the Ritual of the Blood Moon, Geo was invited to the Tournament of the Sources, causing him to leave Spitz and Fritz with Gandalaria.[4] Meanwhile, Kai and Bonzle found a way to communicate with Nya and Riyu. In the tournament, Geo won against Gorge, only to lose against Lloyd. After Rox used Theroxian Magic on the Wolf Mask Warriors and Cinder, Geo fought against them alongside the other Elemental Masters. During this, Nya was able release Kai and Bonzle after using the Crowns of Horns stolen by her adversaries. While Roby declared Sora as the default winner of the tournament, Geo watched them. Later, he reunited with Bonzle back at the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

During Thunderfang's release, the Finders and Cole sheltered themselves in the Rookery from the Winds of Chaos. Following Thunderfang's release, Geo went to the party in the Monastery of Spinjitzu with Cole.

Days later, the Finders were helping Kai and Cole with Frak's training. After Frak suggested that they should find the source of Earth's screaming, Geo gave them the Ninja Combat Vehicle. However, Bonzle, Spitz and Fritz were hollowed by Nokt and the Spectral Dragonians, causing Geo to take them to the Monastery of Spinjitzu. While he was looking after the Finders with Alfonzo Frohicky, Ras arrived. Geo tried to protect his family from him, only for Ras to throw him under the training area, injuring him. Following the Battle of the Spectral Lands, the Finder's and Cole's souls returned to them.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

The Finders continued to stay at the Monastery of Spinjitzu with Geo even helping Frohicky and aiding Rontu.

Later, the Finders attended Lloyd's memorial.
