Collins is crew member of the Mary Louise.

History

History[]

The Call of the Deep[]

The Mary Louise was attacked by an octopus, but Zane and P.I.X.A.L. saved the crew by fending off the creature.

The Calm Before the Storm[]

When the Mary Louise got caught in a squall, the crew briefly saw Wojira passing by before the storm calmed and disappeared.

The Call of Home[]

When Kwon was overboard, the captain instructed Collins to throw out the life buoys for him. Moments later, Nya appeared and saved Kwon.
