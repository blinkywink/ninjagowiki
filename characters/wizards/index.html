Culture

Culture[]

The wizards treat magic as a craft that can be studied and applied through spells, potions, and crystal balls. Their society is ancient and deeply structured, led by the Pontifex Magicus.

Some wizards are organized into specialized groups and conclaves, such as the Carnus and Argor Conclaves. They also maintaining its own dating system where major events are recorded as "A.L.". Despite their power, wizards often depicted as somewhat eccentric or prone to magical mishaps; for example, the Wizard Guard lost public trust in 45 A.L. after a head-shrinking incident and the wizards once endured a hundred-year war triggered by the anger of Torex and Xerot.[3]
