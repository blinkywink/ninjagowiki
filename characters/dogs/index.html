Overview

“What's more fun than puppies? Petting puppies.”

Dogs are canines which live in Ninjago and Imperium.

History

History[]

Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

The Day Ninjago Stood Still[]

During the strong earthquakes affecting Ninjago City, Snickers' owner tried to run to her pet, who was running around in the destruction and jumped on a nearby red truck. The ground then fell down before the woman, but luckily the ninja came just in time to help. Snickers watched as the ninja formed a chain to its owner, whimpering to Cole. The ground fell beneath them again, but Snickers, even before the ninja, jumped out of the way in time. Its owner was rescued by the team and Snickers jumped into her arms. While Wu, Misako, and the ninja discussed the earthquakes, Snickers' owner stood near the hole she was rescued from, holding the poodle.

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Skybound[]

Misfortune Rising[]

As Kai and Zane searched for the Destiny's Bounty, they were spotted by the vehicle compound's owner and his dog, Sir Chomps-A-Lot, who chased them out.

Operation Land Ho![]

As Jay is gathering his friends to help him overtake Nadakhan, his first recruit is Dareth, who is showing off Sir Chomps-A-Lot, claiming that this was one talented dog. Unfortunately, Dareth and Sir Chomps-A-Lot were later thrown out after the latter urinated on stage.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

Wu's Teas[]

Music Night Part 1 (non-canon)[]

As Dareth plays a clarinet in the background, Sir Chomps-A-Lot can be seen cowering over the sounds, howling and barking.

Music Night Part 2 (non-canon)[]

After the ninja drink Musicalitea, they play a song about not wanting to do the dishes. A man rushes into Steeper Wisdom, interested in the concert, and the dog is seen in front of the shop, watching him enter.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

In the Crossroads junkyard, Arin distracts some dogs until Sora found a spark plug before the Security Bots arrive. The dogs watch Arin leave a pie behind before they try to get at it.

The Power Within[]

A dog is caught up in the MergeQuake Storm before being saved by Arin and started to lick him in appreciation.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Blood Moon[]

The Forest of Spirits[]

At the Magical storagehouse, Gandalaria handed a potion to Cole for him to smell, only to sneeze upon inhaling the contents, briefly turning him in a dog. After Zane tells him that he is adorable, Cole remarks how he always is adorable before sneezing again, reverting back to human.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Ultimate Object of Admiration[]

A young boy named Aru plays with his dog before throwing a toy for him to catch, but the dog preferred to lick Aru before Arin arrives, looking for food for Ras and himself.
