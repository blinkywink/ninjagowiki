Overview

“Change is inevitable. It is the nature of all things. Life itself, but still it remains water. You must feel the truth of this. Do not fight it. Embrace the change.”
— Sorla to Nya, "The Traveler's Tree"

Sorla is an elderly Ice Fisher and the leader of the Great Lake. She helped the ninja when they traveled there to find Zane, saving them from Frost Wolves and showing them the way to the Castle of Ice.

History

History[]

The Never-Realm[]

Sorla greeted the ninja when they arrived at her cabin. After exchanging introductions, she used a mystical fire to show the ninja what happened to Zane. She told them that Zane was taken captive by the Ice Emperor and he is "lost." However, Lloyd believed Zane's strong and they will still come for him despite all the dangers she said. Sorla then warned them of General Vex, the Ice Emperor's second-in-command.

Fire Maker[]

Lloyd and Sorla discussed the path to get to the Castle of Ice. She told him that the safest path to get there is called Mala-Wojira. When Lloyd questioned about the the path, Sorla answered it by describing the path where the people who cross through it has to be quiet, or else Wojira will "strike you down."

When she headed outside to hear the news of Blizzard Samurai heading to the Great Lake, she told everyone to head inside, so they can regroup.

Inside her cabin, the Ice Fishers threw ideas around on what they should do, but disagreements arise. Sorla stated that can't leave the Fire and they must protect it. When Kai asked why they "don't just light another fire," he was met by criticism by the Ice Fishers, but Sorla explained that they can't light another Hearth Fire since the weather is too cold. After realizing they can't transport the Hearth Fire, Sorla made it clear they will protect it, and the ninja agreed that they will help.

When the Ice Fishers and the ninja head out to prepare, Kai stayed behind with Sorla. He explained that he could solve the problem, but without his powers, he can't help. Sorla gave him advice, saying Elemental Powers can't be stolen, and it will come back.

The Ice Fishers won the battle, but it's at the cost of losing their sacred Fire. The Ice Fishers sit around the empty Fire, freezing in the winter night. Kai continued to blame himself for not having his Elemental Powers, and Sorla asked if Kai has done everything he can do. This gave Kai the spark to try to light another Fire, which turns out to be a success, with Kai slightly starting to regain his powers.

An Unlikely Ally[]

When Nya walked away from Kai, Cole, and Jay, she is soon confronted by Sorla who says that her powers are useful even if the water is frozen.

The Message[]

Sorla and the other Ice Fishers went out to see what Uthaug wanted to show them. They were shocked to see it's a piece of Lloyd's gi.

The Traveler's Tree[]

Sorla is shown with Nya, beginning her training on mastering ice.

My Enemy, My Friend[]

Sorla and the rest of the Ice Fishers try to escape Boreal but they were frozen in ice.

Awakenings[]

Upon Zane destroying his scepter, the Ice Fishers, including Sorla, were all unfrozen as a result. Several days later, Sorla tells the reunited ninja that she cannot brew Traveler's Tea due to Ninjago being a distant realm until Cole gives her the berry he had been saving. Once the portal was created, the ninja go through as Sorla watches Krag say goodbye to Cole before comforting the lone yeti, and watches Lloyd head in as the portal disappears.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Zane briefly disguised himself as Sorla in "The Explorers Club", although he wasn't aware of her existence.
  • Her role in the Never-Realm is similar to Vakama from Bionicle.
    • Sorla is the leader of the Ice Fishers, whereas Vakama is Chieftain of the Ta-Matoran who were former residents of Ta-Metru.
    • Sorla consults with the spirits of the Hearth Fire in "Awakenings" just like how Vakama 'consults' with the "Sacred Fire" from the Bohrok-Kal Online Animation "Out of Their Elements".
  • Sorla's role is similar to Mystake as both are wise and elderly women.
