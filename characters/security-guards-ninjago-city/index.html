The security guards are a group of guards at the Ninjago Museum of History, the City Bank, and Borg Tower.

History

History[]

The Darkness Comes[]

Two security guards were checking the Borg Tower vaults, with the elder guard telling his partner about the artifacts that were moved from Hiroshi's Labyrinth Stronghold, the elder guard remarked on the vault's safety, when the Darkness came through the Realm Crystal, the two guards were barely able to escape from the vaults and evacuated Borg Tower, helping Cyrus Borg walk down the stairs.

Powerless[]

During the attack of the Pyro Vipers in Ninjago City, the Old Guard and Joey were in the Ninjago Museum of History. They thought they were safe until Aspheera invaded the museum. In fear, they told her where to get to the Serpentine exhibit.

Upgrade[]

Joey and the Old Guard had a night watch at City Bank when the Mechanic broke into the bank using his Noodle Truck of Crime. He easily defeated them and stole money from the bank.

Gayle Gossip: A Closer Look[]

Security guards appeared in archival footage relating to Vast Industries and their mysterious game title, Prime Empire.

Level Thirteen[]

When the Mechanic, Ultra Violet, Soto, and other prisoners from Kryptarium Prison broke into Borg Tower, Joey and the Old Guard were on patrol. The prisoners ran through them and tied them up.

A Mayor Problem[]

The Old Guard and Joey were on duty at Borg Tower when Cole and Jay infiltrated the premises, disguised as employees, to steal Aspheera's staff from the Borg Tower vaults. The two were able to sneak past unnoticed because the Old Guard was sleeping on duty while Joey was distracted by a handheld video game.

Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5![]

Security guards drove a series of armored trucks transporting dangerous artifacts from Borg Tower to an unknown, secure location. Of these guards, two of them had the misfortune of being lifted into the air by the Ninja, who were aiming to steal Aspheera's staff from their truck's contents. These two security guards would spend the entire duration of the theft helplessly screaming and wishing things were over before eventually being set down safely once the ninja retrieved the staff.
