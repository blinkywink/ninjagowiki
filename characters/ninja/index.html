Overview

“The ninja have protected Ninjago for years. We've been a symbol of hope for so many. But with the others missing, that hope had been lost. Now I see there is a reason to hope once again, because I can't do this alone.”
— Lloyd to the Matriarch, "The Merge: Part 2"

The ninja[note 1] (also known as the Masters of Spinjitzu[2]) are a group of young warriors, mostly Elemental Masters, founded by Master Wu, that protect the land of Ninjago, and later the Merged Lands.

The original iteration initially composed of Zane, Jay, Cole, and Kai, led by Wu, but were later joined by the fabled Green Ninja of prophecy, Lloyd Garmadon, and Kai's sister Nya, who originally aided the ninja as Samurai X and later became Elemental Master of Water; the mantle of Samurai X was later adopted by Nindroid ally P.I.X.A.L.. After a cataclysmic event known as the Merge scattered the ninja and their closest allies across the Merged Realms, Lloyd took on a new generation of ninja students in Arin, Sora, Riyu, Wyldfyre, and Frak.

History

History[]

Pilot episodes[]

Dreading the return of his evil brother, Lord Garmadon, who is desperate to claim the Golden Weapons of Spinjitzu, Master Wu, the younger son of the First Spinjitzu Master who created Ninjago with the weapons, searched the land for four teenagers to find the weapons first. He came across the current Elemental Masters of the Elements of Creation: Cole—the Master of Earth, Zane—the Master of Ice, Jay—the Master of Lightning, and Kai—the Master of Fire, whose sister Nya had been kidnapped by Garmadon's army of Skulkin, though none of the four had unlocked their respective Elemental Powers yet. With Wu's teaching, the four became ninja, learned the art of Spinjitzu, and retrieved all but the last weapon, the Sword of Fire. Unfortunately, Garmadon used Nya to lure Kai to the Fire Temple and stole the sword while the Skeleton Army snuck up on the sleeping ninja and stole the other three weapons.

Wu followed the Skulkin general Samukai to the Underworld while the ninja followed him by taming the four dragons who guarded the Golden Weapons.

The four watched as Wu and Samukai fought, the former only possessing the Sword of Fire against Samukai with the other three weapons, giving him the advantage. Samukai eventually gained a hold of all four and intended to use them against Garmadon, however, their immense power overwhelmed him leading to his death, the ensuing tear through space and time allowing Garmadon to escape to the Realm of Madness, which would enable him to wield all four weapons without death. Once the ninja, along with Nya, had moved to Wu's Monastery, they continuing their training for Garmadon's return.

Rise of the Snakes[]

Unfortunately, lack of action led them to laziness, preferring to play video games instead of staying fit and powerful. Unbeknownst to them, a new threat was brewing. Wishing to live up to his father's feared name, while harboring feelings of sadness for his dad's departure, Garmadon's son Lloyd sought to cause chaos in Ninjago, and achieve his selfish desires in the process. After the ninja made a fool of him, he inadvertently led to the successful release of all five tribes of the mythical Serpentine (one tribe, the Hypnobrai, burning down Wu's Monastery, forcing the team to relocate to the Destiny's Bounty, a damaged pirate ship Zane found by following a mysterious Falcon), now led by the last of the Anacondrai, Pythor P. Chumsworth, who seeks the Fangblades to awaken the Great Devourer, the monster responsible for turning Garmadon's heart evil.

The ninja battle the united Serpentine, while a mysterious samurai continuously steals their thunder. Eventually, Lloyd, who was taken in by the ninja after being betrayed by the snakes, is captured and Wu leaves to find someone who can help free him. On their own, the ninja engage in battle with the Serpentine to stop the awakening of the Devourer, each one unlocking their true potential in the process (with the team learning Zane is a Nindroid). They also learn Nya is the Samurai due to being left out.

Wu returns with Garmadon to help save Lloyd, who to everyone's surprise (and Garmadon's dismay) is the Green Ninja, the warrior prophesied to defeat the "dark lord." Though the ninja, with Garmadon's help, recoveri all four Fang Blades, Pythor steals them and manages to awaken the Great Devourer, though it devours him and Wu. The Devourer was only able to be defeated by Garmadon with the help of the ninja, wielding all four Golden Weapons, the "dark lord" escaping afterward.

Finding Wu in a puddle of the Devourer's venom, the ninja promise to train Lloyd so he'll be ready for the decisive battle against his father.

Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

After the defeat of the Great Devourer, the ninja, along with the Green Ninja, fought against Garmadon and the remaining Serpentine army. Garmadon tries to get rid of the ninja using his newly made Mega Weapon many times in order for Lloyd to not become the Green Ninja but they eventually destroyed it with help from the past versions of the Golden Weapons. Lloyd also used Tomorrow's Tea to age up in the process.

After Garmadon was betrayed by the Serpentine, he came across an evil spirit called the Overlord and formed an alliance with him to take over Ninjago. But the Overlord ended up possessing him and turn him into his true Dragon form to purge Ninjago into pure Darkness. Luckily Lloyd, having become the Golden Ninja thanks to the other ninja, was finally able to defeat him and Garmadon returns to normal along with anyone else being corrupted by the Dark Matter.

Rebooted[]

Ninjago was at peace, thanks to Lloyd, who defeated the Overlord, but he returns, having integrated himself into the Borg Industries' computer systems. The ninja had to fight again with the Overlord, only now with an old enemy, Pythor, and an army of Nindroids created based on Zane's blueprints to serve the Overlord.

However, the ninja emerged victoriously, but at the cost of Zane, who sacrificed himself. Unbeknownst to the others, however, Zane's mind and spirit had transferred into the Digiverse and managed to rebuild himself.

Soon after, however, he was captured by a thief named Ronin along with his fellow Nindroid/girlfriend, P.I.X.A.L.. Zane was taken to Garmadon's old mentor, Chen, where he was stripped of his power and held hostage.

Tournament of Elements[]

After the apparent sacrifice of Zane, the ninja are divided, where they part ways. However, everything changed when Lloyd attempts to bring them back together and they discovered that Zane was still alive. To rescue him they had to go to Chen's Island and take part in the Tournament of Elements.

During this time, they discovered the truth of their powers after they met and befriended the Elemental Masters. The ninja was also able to unlock the ability to use Elemental Dragons.

The ninja allied with their new friends and old ones to make one final effort at the Corridor of Elders in order to defend their home against Chen and his cult of Anacondrai worshippers.

With the sacrifice of Garmadon, Chen and his army were banished to the Cursed Realm, officially bringing peace between the Serpentine and the humans.

Shadow of Ronin (non-canon)[]

Despite defeating Chen, the Ninja continue their training with Wu in the city of Spinjago. One day, the team were suddenly attacked by the mercenary group known as the Dark Samurai. As they fought the forces of the Dark Samurai across the city, they encountered Ronin, the group's leader. After their initial confrontation with the mercenary, he used an ancient artifact, the Obsidian Glaive, to drain the Ninja of their memories. Thanks to Wu's timely intervention however, Ronin failed to completely drain the Ninja of their memories and was forced to escape.

In order to restore their memories, the Ninja embarked on a quest to discover the four Obsidian Weapons. Throughout this quest, the Ninja fought against Ronin and his Dark Samurai throughout multiple encounters, eventually coming to a head at the Floating Ruins where Ronin revealed his possession of one of the Elemental Forges. Realizing the evil that Ronin could unleash with such artifacts, Wu split the ninja into two teams to recover the forges of fire and ice before Ronin could get to them. Kai, Zane, and Wu embarked to Torchfire mountain to retrieve the forge of fire, while Jay, Cole, and Dareth searched for the forge of ice. Although both teams were to rendezvous outside the vault of ice, Ronin intercepted them and used his newly possessed forge of earth to force the Ninja into giving him the two that they had collected.

Having lost the forges, Wu leads the Ninja to Hiroshi's Labyrinth in search of the Primal Fulcrum, now with the assistance of Lloyd and Misako. Upon uncovering the fulcrum however, Misako betrayed the team, revealing that she had her memories altered by Ronin and now believed that the mercenary was her beloved. Enraged by this, Lloyd destroyed the Obsidian Glaive, restoring Misako's memories as Ronin and his soldiers escaped to Chen's island to complete their plan. The Ninja then pursued Ronin to Chen's Island, fighting their way through the Dark Samurai ranks, until they reached the palace's courtyard where Ronin was activating the fulcrum. After an attempt to reason with the mercenary failed, the Ninja pursued him into the vortex he had opened.

Atop a series of stones floating within the Ethereal Divide, the Ninja face off against Ronin and the last of his guards as they attempt to return Chen from the Cursed Realm. In the ensuing battle, the Ninja exhaust the powers of the elemental forges, successfully destroying them and foiling Ronin's plan. Infuriated by his defeat, the mercenary covers his escape with gun fire as a giant, ghostly hand briefly emerges from the fading portal. In order to prevent further horrors from escaping, the Ninja channel their elemental powers through the Obsidian Weapons to destroy the primal fulcrum, resulting in an explosion that scatters the Obsidian Weapons while returning the Ninja back to Ninjago.

Before Possession[]

At some point in time, the ninja had another encounter with Ronin, who ultimately abandoned them in a precarious situation.[3]

Possession[]

When opening the portal to the Cursed Realm, a ghost named Morro, who was the first student of Master Wu, escaped and possessed Lloyd and shortly after takes the Realm Crystal and liberates the Preeminent, the physical embodiment of the Cursed Realm. The ninja were faced with problems, as their power depended on Lloyd, which left them powerless, and Cole was turned into a ghost, and Ronin betrayed them to the Ghost Warriors. However, things shifted for the better as they learned a new technique Airjitzu and Cole learned how to accept his form and learned some new ghost moves. Nya also learned she was the master of Water and trained to use her powers.

Eventually, Lloyd was freed of his possession in exchange for the Realm Crystal and the ninja regain their powers and put up a better fight with the ghosts.

In the end, thanks to Nya, who unlocked her True Potential, they defeated the Preeminent and Morro, thus sending them to the Departed Realm along with the rest of the Ghost Warriors as well as the people who were still trapped in the Cursed Realm, including Chen and Garmadon. With this, the ninja achieved victory and gained a new member for their team.

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Skybound[]

When Chen's right-hand man Clouse, who escaped the Cursed Realm, released a Djinn, Nadakhan, the ninja will have to stop him before he marries Nya and gains infinite wishes. Unfortunately, he stole the Realm Crystal and released the rest of his pirate crew, who helped him build Djinjago from parts of Ninjago. All the Ninja other than Jay and Nya were trapped inside of the Djinn Blade and will count on Jay to save them. Fortunately. Jay's last wish caused everyone, except him and Nya, to forget the majority of the season as well as preventing Clouse from releasing Nadakhan.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

Dark Island Trilogy[]

Unwilling to give up his vengeance against the Ninja, Clouse stumbled on the Dark Island and regains his magic. He then released Nadakhan's crew and used them to help him corrupt the Temple of Light which in turn corrupt Ninjago. To do so, they kidnap numerous fishermen, and Lloyd's mother, Misako to retrieve the Dark Matter to corrupt the temple. The Ninja and Master Wu went to rescue them but were briefly separated. Fortunately, Wu uses the Golden Mech to cleanse the Temple from the darkness, causing Ninjago to turn back to normal and Clouse ends up getting banished to the Underworld.

Day of the Departed[]

Following the Sky Pirate conflict, Ninjago celebrated Day of the Departed, a holiday, and the ninja spread out across the land of Ninjago to remember their ancestors. Zane remembers his father, Dr. Julien, at Birchwood Forest, Kai and Nya remember their parents, Ray and Maya, at their blacksmith shop, Lloyd and Misako, remember Garmadon at the Corridor of Elders, and Wu remembers his father at the Monastery of Spinjitzu, while Jay spends time with his parents at their junkyard.

In the meantime, Cole was suddenly forgotten by his friends and goes to Master Yang's Haunted Temple to settle his debt with the ghostly master. However, a sinister plot by Yang leads Cole to free the spirits of the ninja old enemies from the Departed Realm, including Samukai, Kozu, Cryptor, Chen, and Morro, all of whom possess statues at the Ninjago Museum of History. Seeking revenge, the revived villains spread throughout Ninjago to confront their adversaries.

The ninja defeated their ghostly foes and gathered at the museum, where they were told by Wu and a redeemed Morro of Yang's intentions and how they forgot Cole. The ninja then realized of the danger Cole is in and rushed to aid him, while Morro returns to the Departed Realm at peace.

With the Yin Blade in hand, Yang proceeded to go to the roof of his temple and activates the artifact, using its reality-warping powers to open the Rift of Return. However, Cole confronted Yang, they fought with the master being forced the latter to defend himself with the Yin Blade with success, slicing apart Cole's blade in the process.

However, when Cole's support by his friends powered him up, Cole used his charged fists to attack Yang, who attempted to shield himself with the Yin Blade. Unable to withstand the assault, the Yin Blade was shattered by Cole's attack, and with its destruction and the ending Yin-Yang Eclipse, the Rift of Return started to close as a result. However, a reformed Yang threw Cole through the rift changing him back to a human and the ninja gained a new base in the Temple of Airjitzu.

The Hands of Time[]

After the Day of the Departed, the ninja returned to Ninjago Museum of History and discovered that Master Wu and Garmadon confronted two Elemental Masters, after which the ninja decided to stop the Time Twins, but it was too late as they had recollected the Time Blades. Finally they together with Kai, Nya, and Wu traveling to the past, 40 years before, after the defeat of Acronix and Krux. Master Wu sacrificed himself to stop them and Kai and Nya returned to the present to cure their rapidly aging father. Wu then resolved to defeat the Time Twins once and for all.

Realizing that they lost their master, the ninja name Lloyd their new Master. As the new Master, Lloyd had the team to bury the Time Blade and to find their teacher who was lost in time.

Way of the Departed[]

After Wu was lost in time, the ninja went to search in Wu and split in the beginning to widen their search: Cole and Jay, Kai and Zane, Nya and Lloyd. The ninja decided to look for Wu in different places such as the Library of Domu, the Monastery of Spinjitzu and the Caves of Despair. After looking with no avail, Jay and Cole continued for looking for Wu while the others continued to be the protectors of the realm.

Sons of Garmadon[]

One year later, the ninja discovered that a group called Sons of Garmadon are trying to revive Garmadon but in his evil form. They are also reunited with their master, who is revealed to have been de-aged into an infant after his journey through time but is slowly aging back to normal. The ninja also had to protect the Oni Masks, but what they did not have in mind was that Princess Harumi was really the leader of the Sons of Garmadon.

The Sons of Garmadon finally revived Garmadon, who was able to defeat Lloyd with ease and leave the latter powerless. This allowed Garmadon to take control of Ninjago after Kai, Jay, Zane, and Cole, along with Baby Wu, ended up in the Realm of Oni and Dragons when they escaped Garmadon's creation, the Colossus, leading the others to think they were killed.

Hunted[]

After being defeated, the ninja have been separated as Ninjago falls under the control of Lord Garmadon. The remaining ninja, Lloyd, and Nya form a rag-tag team with brown ninja Dareth, P.I.X.A.L., and Misako. They were attacked by Garmadon's forces but soon learn that their friends were not killed but were instead teleported elsewhere. They are nearly defeated by the enemy but are saved and gather more allies in old friends to form the Resistance in order to defeat Garmadon and his followers as well as save the city.

The four original ninja (Kai, Cole, Jay, and Zane) along with a rapidly aging Wu must face the dangers of the Realm of Oni and Dragons by dealing with the threats of Dragon Hunters led by Iron Baron, as well seeking to free the dragons and find a way back home to Ninjago.

In Ninjago, Lloyd was able to bring hope back to the citizens of Ninjago and personally opposed his father in the process. This resulted in Garmadon leading an attack on the Resistance's base and having most of his allies imprisoned.

Very soon, the marooned ninja were able to ally with the hunters and the dragons, defeat Iron Baron, and locate the Dragon Armor, and gained new dragons to return back home.

The ninja returned to Ninjago and saved Lloyd's group by using the dragons to rescue them. While Wu and Lloyd fought Garmadon, the ninja evacuated the citizens who realized their saviors returned. The Resistance also broke out to help the ninja in taking back the city. The ninja dealt with the Colossus and with help from their allies defeated the behemoth while Lloyd regained his powers after overcoming his father. In the end, the Sons of Garmadon (except for Harumi and Mr. E due to their deaths) and Garmadon himself were sent to Kryptarium Prison. The ninja were then hailed as heroes by the people for saving them once again.

March of the Oni[]

The ninja were honored by the city for their feat of defeating Garmadon, as they were rewarded with a restored Destiny's Bounty. They also have rebuilt the Monastery of Spinjitzu and moved back into their old home.

They faced a new problem when one of the Dragon Hunters Faith and Firstbourne, the mother of all dragons, arrive in their realm, revealing a threat is coming. Very soon, darkness emerges from the Realm Crystal, and the city is engulfed. To combat the threat, they reluctantly free Garmadon from prison to help them have a chance against them. While evacuating people from the city, Cole is lost when dark tentacles start to pull him down and Nya accidentally destroys his ropes, causing him to plummet. They regained hope when Nya tells Kai that he can reforge the Golden Weapons out of the armor and fight the Oni since they fear it.

The ninja proceed to forge their weapons, while Cole, who survived the drop, narrowly manages to avoid being petrified in the city and used his drill vehicle to escape the Oni and head for the monastery to help his friends.

As the ninja prepare for their confrontation, the Oni make their way to the Monastery where they fight their enemy. Despite using the Golden Weapons and having a fully powered Garmadon and Wu on their side, the ninja are unable to do much until Cole arrives and helps them with the tides evening for a while, but the Oni were still having the upper-hand. However, Lloyd got the idea to use the Tornado of Creation to fight them off. In the process, Lloyd meets his grandfather in the Grasslands, who praises him on his efforts and gives him the chance to come with him, but Lloyd decided to be reunited with his friends, and only remember it as a dream.

With the battle over and the Oni seemingly vanquished, the ninja commemorate their victory on the Ninjago history mural while Garmadon leaves the monastery to head out on his own path.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Six months after the fight with the Oni, the ninja were able to enjoy a time of peace, but became so lazy that Wu easily tossed them aside in training.

The ninja resorted to traveling around the city to find a sense of adventure but nothing came up because most of their enemies are either arrested, in the Departed Realm, or in hiding. As a result, they travel to the Desert of Doom with explorer Clutch Powers. Unknowingly, the ninja accidentally release the Serpentine sorceress Aspheera, who steals Kai's power and leaves them trapped.

However, the ninja were able to escape the pyramid and learned that Aspheera was after someone known as the "Treacherous Deceiver," who was later revealed to be Wu.

The ninja then later confronted Aspheera at the hangar bay and fought against her forces. After a long battle, Zane was able to defeat her, but at the cost of him being banished to the Never-Realm to save Wu from the blast.

After the ninja went to the Never-Realm, Lloyd and a Formling named Akita went to the Castle of Ice to rescue Zane from the Ice Emperor while the others stayed behind to defend the Ice Fishers, but only for Lloyd to realized that the Ice Emperor was actually Zane, who became the Ice Emperor by being corrupted by the Forbidden Scroll and had his memories lost by Vex.

In order to free Zane from Vex's manipulation, Lloyd forms another Resistance with Kataru and Grimfax, but only for the Resistance to be quickly defeated by the Blizzard Samurai. However, when Lloyd was mocked by Vex, Zane regained his memories and freed the realm from its eternal winter, allowing the ninja to be reunited and return to Ninjago and have Vex exiled far away for his tyranny.

Prime Empire[]

After helping the police arrest the Mechanic for stealing a motherboard, the ninja investigate his base where Jay finds an old arcade game called Prime Empire, which the motherboard was needed. After turning it on, he had inadvertently sent a shockwave that turned all the video games into Prime Empire and plays it before entering it.

After investigating the game and learning more about its history and creator, the rest of the ninja, minus Zane, enter Prime Empire and reunite with Jay. They receive new suits and weapons before heading out to achieve the Purple Key-Tana, one of three keys they need to escape unlock the final arcade cabinet and enter the Temple of Madness.

Allying with Okino for a brief amount of time, they get the Purple Key-Tana. After entering a dance competition and winning 500 credits to enter the Speedway Five-Billion, they win, but at the cost of their new friend Scott, as well as Cole and Kai.

Eventually, after many trials, Jay emerged as the last ninja standing. However, Jay, with the help of Zane, P.I.X.A.L, and Wu, was able to get Unagami and Milton Dyer to make amends and rescue the trapped players along with the NPCs. As a result, the remaining ninja were released from the game as well.

Master of the Mountain[]

After the ninja and Wu were invited to by King Vangelis, they traveled to Shintaro to celebrate Princess Vania's birthday. However, when Cole met a purple creature that had his mother's necklace, he and Vania discover an army of skeletons forcing two warring tribes to mine Vengestone in the Dungeons of Shintaro for the evil Skull Sorcerer.

The ninja were separated through the perilous mountain where they each work to end the conflict of the tribes. Meanwhile, Cole discovers that King Vangelis is really the Skull Sorcerer and that he was responsible for everything happening in the Dungeons. Not wanting his plans to be exposed, he banished Cole and Wu, who were aided by Vania.

Crystalized[]

After being defeated by Miss Demeanor and saved by the New Ninja, who call them old timers, this leads to Kai and Jay arguing, until Nya appeared and told the team she must have her powers removed to return to normal.

But, the latest recruit, The Mechanic, blows Lloyd's cover, which leads to him discovering the Crystal King's messenger is Harumi, who was resurrected by the Crystal King, who is actually the Overlord, by swearing her allegiance to him. Harumi soon manipulates Lloyd into unleashing his dormant Destruction powers by killing the Ninja, unaware that they seeked refuge in a BRTM-12 train. The council then steals the Golden Weapons and destroy the monastery.

After being rescued by Nya and stealing back their impounded vehicles, the Ninja manage to retrieve Lloyd from Primeval's Eye, where they try to fight back against the Overlord and his Crystal army while Lloyd and Wu reunite with Garmadon, who had been hiding out in Vinny Folson's apartment before witnessing him using Oni form against some crystal warriors, inspiring Wu to have Garmadon teach Lloyd to unlock his own Oni form.

Briefly separated after a failed strike, the Ninja reunite at the newspaper warehouse, where they learn of Dragon Form, which can turn the tide on the battle, but are unable to achieve it, until many allies, past and present, came to their aid, allowing them to unlock Dragon Form, reclaim their Golden Weapons and defeat the Crystal Council. Lloyd and Garmadon attempted to fight against the Overlord, alongside a vengeful Harumi, who discovered the truth about her biological parents' death. With Lloyd resisting his unlocked Oni form, the original Ninja use their Golden Weapons to combine them into a dragon and defeat the Overlord, at the cost of their powers.

A week later, the Ninja, along with Garmadon, the redeemed Harumi, their allies, the New Ninja and former enemies, work on rebuilding the monastery.

Ages of the ninja

Ages of the ninja[]

“He passed his Elemental Powers to us. Of all people, a bunch of kids.”
— Lloyd, "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master"
“Jay, we're still teenagers.”
“Yeah, but grown-up teenagers.”
— Kai and Jay, "The Gilded Path"


The ninja's ages are not specified.[7]

Ninjago: The classic ninja were in their teenage years during the original series, with the exception of Zane, who is decades older but was built to have the appearance of a teenager. Lloyd became physically around the age of his older friends due to the effects of the Tomorrow's Tea, but he is still chronologically the youngest of the team due to having originally been a child. Zane is over 100 years old, due to spending around 60 years as the Ice Emperor in the Never-Realm.

While the ninja were officially teenagers during the original series, with Jay referring to them as "grown-up teenagers" in "The Gilded Path," their specific ages and the amount of time that passes between seasons was kept vague for the purpose of keeping them in an age-range that is young and relatable to the show's target audience.[8][9][10] Furthermore, Tommy Andreasen noted that the age of the ninja is not specific and is up to the viewer's interpretation.[11] According to the Hageman Brothers, the ninja's aging is comparable to Bart Simpson's, whom is often used as an example of the phenomenon of cartoon characters remaining the same age despite the passage of time.[12] Similarly, Tommy Andreasen compared the ninja's aging to that of Batman, stating that "time is less of a factor" in fiction.[13] Tommy Kalmar refers to the group as "teen ninjas" in his 2022 "What is Ninjago?" video on LEGO.com, meaning they were still officially teenagers during the events of Crystalized.[14]

Ninjago: Dragons Rising: Sometime after Crystalized, the Merge occurs and the story skips ahead years later. Ninja students Arin and Sora are teenagers,[15] Wyldfyre and Frak are teenagers as well because they have long legs in the present, and were children before the merge, and until the Merge for Wyldfyre. In the case of Frak, he could age differently due to being a Serpentine. While the amount of time is unknown, enough years have passed that the ninja students grew up from children with short legs to teenagers with normal-sized legs, instead of the medium legs which pre-teens/young teenagers usually have in LEGO post-2018. This may imply that the older six ninja, excluding Zane, are now young adults in their 20s instead of teens, but as mentioned above, the creators do not specify ages.

See more specific information and possible estimations of the ninja's ages, as well as the sources for the information, on the individual pages' Age sections linked here: Arin, Cole, Jay, Kai, Lloyd, Nya, Sora, Zane.

    • Lloyd, Zane and Riyu met the Source Dragon of Energy during his imprisonment in Imperium in "The Battle of the Second Monastery", resulting in Lloyd becoming a conduit. Lloyd and Riyu would once again interact with him in "The Power Within", with Lloyd setting the source dragon free.
    • As a conduit, Lloyd was transported to the Source Dragon's void in "Beyond the Phantasm Cave" after asking for aid. He received a vision of guidance as result.
    • In "The Shape of Motion", Lloyd, Nya, Arin, Sora, Wyldfyre and Riyu all met the Source Dragon of Motion, who tasked the ninja with discovering the identity of the Mountain Matriarch's killers by competing in the Tournament of the Sources.
    • In "Clues and Suspects", the Source Dragon of Motion encouraged Lloyd to keep fighting. She would later appear to him in "Elements of Betrayal", congratulating him and the ninja for their success and even giving him a dragon icon as a gift.
    • In "Casket of Bones", Lloyd inadvertently had a vision which teleported him to the Source Dragon's void, allowing him to overhear their discourse.
    • Kai, Cole and Frak managed to unearth the sentient corpse of the deceased Source Dragon of Balance and witnessed it in wonder before it disappeared in "The Screaming Earth".
