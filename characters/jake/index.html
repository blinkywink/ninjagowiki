Overview

This article is about the TV series character. For the movie character, see Jake (The LEGO Ninjago Movie).
“I haven't given up on the ninja, and the darkest times is when we need to believe we can win the most, and the ninja wouldn't let us—”
— Jake to Fred Finely, "Snaketastrophy"

Jake is a young boy and a fan of the ninja, particularly Kai.

History

History[]

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Snaketastrophy[]

When Fred Finely was asking questions about the ninja, he interviewed Jake and the boy showed optimism that the ninja will return and save Ninjago City. Unfortunately, Finely moved on from Jake because the reporter wanted to interview an Elemental Cobra.

Powerless[]

When the ninja left Kai, Jake came running into him and asked him for his autograph; however, Kai tells Jake that was the other ninja who did all the work. When the young boy asked why Kai wasn't helping the ninja, he told Jake that he was without powers. This makes Jake upset and he calls Kai a quitter. In addition, he chased after the ninja and decided he will help the ninja.

Just before the ninja were going to be defeated by the Elemental Cobras, Jake came running in to shield the ninja from harm, despite having no weapons to defend himself. A moment later, Kai saved Jake while defeating the Elemental Cobras.

After Kai saved the ninja, they celebrated and Jake was glad that Kai helped the ninja, even though he was without his Elemental Powers. Moreover, Kai calls Jake a hero for being so brave.

Prime Empire[]

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

Jake was inside a car playing on his mobile device.

Jake, who was inside Benny's Arcade Emporium, was chatting with another child when the shockwave takes the power out. When it was restored, all of the games were replaced with Prime Empire.

Prior to Crystalized[]

When Kai opened his dojo, where he decided to teach the children martial arts, Jake joined him and began taking lessons.

Crystalized[]

Farewell the Sea[]

Jake was practicing different forms in the dojo, including a move to obliterate the enemy before they saw the person coming, before class ended.

Safe Haven[]

Kai, Cole and Skylor stumble across Jake, who hid in Benny's Arcade Emporium, where he thought he would be safe, until they receive a transmission from Wu with the motto; 'The news never sleeps', discreetly revealing his location: the Newspaper warehouse. The four of them manage to reach it, and after being saved by Antonia and the paperboys from some crystal zombies, Jake was able to reunite with his parents.

Compatible[]

Jake sat glumly in the warehouse alongside his parents, but was cheered up when Kai presented him with a bowl of noodles.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Being a fan of Kai, Jake mainly dresses similar to Kai with a red shirt as well as a couple of packs across his shirt. However, he does not have the same hairstyle as Kai, with Kai's color being maroon and Jake's being light-dark.

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • This character was inspired by a real Jake[1] in the U.S.[2] His namesake passed away early in 2021 from cancer.[3][4]
    • He is the second character in which he is inspired by a real person, the first being Antonia.
  • He has the same bowl-cut hairstyle as young Lloyd, Wu, and Garmadon, originally used on Ron Weasley from LEGO Harry Potter.
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • His shirt is similar to Kai's Armor gi but with shorter sleeves and no gloves.
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, The Book of Elemental Powers was written by Jake, and that the book "...is an account of his understanding of how things work."[5]
