Mrs. R[1] is a citizen of the Crossroads and a mech racer.

History

History[]

Note: Mrs. R has the appearance of a common background character design used in different contexts, so this page only lists her appearances as the Crossroads citizen, not the visitor of the Administration or Kur's victim from "The Missing."

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

Mrs. R participated in Mech Master 5000, but her mech was crushed by Sora. However, she was already cheering from the stands minutes later.

Crossroads Carnival[]

Mrs. R was watching the Pie-baking contest.

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

Mrs. R was watching the Zane Day competition.

The Power Within[]

Mrs. R was among the scared crowd during the MergeQuake Storm.

The Elemental Mechs[]

The Mech Master[]

Mrs. R was going to participate in Mech Master 5000, but her mech was stolen by The Mechanic.

Prior to Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

At some point, Mrs. R asked Fedulian for the maps of the Merged Realms, not knowing that the maps were fake. Dorama managed to capture Mrs. R and few other citizens of the Crossroads, making them captives at Dorama's Academy for Acting, along with the algae farmers.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

To Mysterium[]

Mrs. R was held captive by Dorama at his Acting Academy. Later on she was freed by the joint efforts of Cole, Zane and Bonzle. After Dorama was defeated, Mrs. R helped the Crossroad citizens and algae farmers transport Dorama to the authorities.

Enter the City of Temples[]

Mrs. R was sitting in a wheelchair and talking with an anthropomorphic frog in the Crossroads.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Spectral Lands[]

After Fugi-Dove leaves the arcade, Mrs. R can be seen playing Lobbo-Roll-O at the background.
