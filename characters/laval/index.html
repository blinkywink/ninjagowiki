Laval is the prince of the Lion Tribe, the son of Lagravis, an accomplished Speedor racer, and the leader of the Heroes of Chima. He is best friends with Cragger.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Laval has beige fur on his arms, torso, and legs, which are also detailed with black claws. His hands are also white. His face is beige, and he has a white muzzle. His nose is black, as well as his mouth, and his eyes are yellow.

In Season 1, Laval wears a sleeveless, blue tunic. He wears a golden harness over his chest with a blue CHI orb in the center. Over his waist is a blue belt fastened by a buckle with a lion head. He wears a kilt on his legs which extends about halfway downwards.

A piece of golden armour which covers much of his chest and points over the shoulders is placed below his neck. A shallow part with a stud holds a transparent light blue disk-plate to represent a CHI orb. Over the armour, a blue cape is placed and, because of the armour's shoulder blades, rises and expands at a higher area than usual, but also does not end up reaching as low.

Laval's head uses an extension piece with a cat-like head fitted over a standard mini-figure one. The extension features an extended snout with the muzzle coloured white and the nose black. An open-mouthed smirk is printed under the nose. Laval has golden, cat-eyes which are surrounded by white show through holes in the head. Around Laval's face is a crown which spikes up in the top. It separates the rest of his head from a spiky, dark red mane pointed backwards.

Personality

Personality[]

Laval is the young prince of the Lion Tribe in Legends of Chima, a character who combines the nobility of his lineage with the boundless energy of an adventurer. He has a curious spirit and a brave heart, always ready to leap into action without a second thought. His nature is playful and joking; he enjoys speedor races, impromptu adventures, and pranks with his friends, which often gets him into trouble with his father, King Lagravis, who would like to see him more focused on his royal duties. Laval wants to be respected as a leader and prove himself a worthy heir to the throne, but his impulsiveness and love of fun can make him seem immature. Even so, he possesses a natural charisma that inspires confidence and unites tribes that would normally be at odds. Deeply loyal, he passionately defends his friends, even when it means risking his life or defying orders. His relationship with Cragger, marked by friendship, betrayal, and reconciliation, forces him to mature and understand the value of forgiveness. With Eris, he finds support and advice; with Li'Ella, he shows his more tender and protective side. Throughout the story, Laval transforms from an enthusiastic and carefree prince into a wiser leader, who understands that true power lies not in strength or speed alone, but in the ability to unite, protect, and guide all the peoples of Chima.

Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

Laval's Beginnings[]

Laval is the headstrong prince of the Lion Tribe who still has a lot to learn about what it means to be a leader. He may want others to see him as a powerful and responsible adult, but he's still a big kid at heart. Duty doesn't always take priority over having a good time. But what's the harm in pulling a little prank every so often? Like a lot of kids, he has an insatiable curiosity and a relentless flair for challenging the rules. This can be a problem since the Lions are all about following and challenging the rules. Can Laval figure this out and become the great warrior and leader his tribe expects of him?

After The Battles for CHI[]

The prince of the Lion Tribe, Laval was once very headstrong and more interested in having a good time than anything else. But the battles over CHI have helped him to grow up a little bit. While he still loves nothing more than a new adventure or a chance to have fun, he also understands more about the duties and responsibilities of a prince. He showed that by his willingness to put the past aside and ally with Cragger against a common threat. As the leader of a new, united team of heroes, the fate of Chima rests on his shoulders.

Legends of Chima: Character Encyclopedia[]

Brave, loyal, fun-loving and heroic, Laval is the young prince of the proud Lion Tribe. He is eager to prove his worth as a leader, but anxious about the responsibility. Mischievous Laval would rather be playing jokes on his friends and seeking adventure at every turn. Even though he has caused much trouble in the land of Chima, he always seeks to make things right.

Laval loves riding his Speedor more than any other vehicle. He is king of the Market Day Speedor track!

As prince of the Lions, Laval must keep peace among the tribes and help protect Chima's sacred CHI. Thankfully he has a great role model in his father Lagravis.

  • Laval makes a brief cameo in "Curseworld, Part II" when Lloyd and Morro emerge from portal and into Chima. Laval asks them where they came from before Morro leaves with Lloyd with the Realm Crystal.

  • He, Bladvic, Gorzan, Worriz, and Rogon are the only mammals of the Heroes of Chima.

Relationships

These are the Relationships of the Lion Prince Laval.

Family

Family[]

Lagravis[]

Laval is Lagravis' son. They have a great family bond. Lagravis is always teaching and guiding his son to become a great leader like him. He cares very much for his son, and Laval, although he may not like his father's rules, cares for him just as much. Lagravis can be very protective of his son at some points. It is possible that since Laval's mother is not seen in the show, they have a stronger bond than most fathers and sons.

Laval's relationship with his father at the start of Season One is much different than it is at the end. Laval thinks his father is a good one, but he hates doing chores and listening to his rules and stories. Throughout the season, he learns lessons from his father and they respect each other as well as show how much they mean to the other much more.

In episode 2, Laval listens to the Great Story and mocks his father as he tells it. He says it is boring and that he has heard it hundreds of times. By the end of the episode, after his Age of Becoming ceremony and battle with Cragger, he starts realizing the meaning of the story and he says that now it has a better ending.

In many other episodes, Laval learns lessons from Lagravis, but usually does something rash without thinking of the consequences of his actions.

In episode 17, Laval is exiled, and his father begs him to ask the elders for a pardon. Laval tells him that doing so would be unfair and that he'll take the punishment. Lagravis wonders how his son became so responsible and wise, and Laval says because he had the best teacher in the world. Later, when they are reunited after Laval saves Lagravis from Cragger's dungeon, Lagravis convinces Laval to return since he needed him much more than he needed the rules.

When Laval fakes his death, Lagravis is devastated. He decides to fight back against the Crocs, Wolves, and Ravens even though their vehicles have been destroyed, since he is so furious for his son's apparent death. Later, he gives a small 'speech' about his son's brilliant plan without even knowing for sure if that's the plan.

This shows his love and confidence in Laval's abilities along with the hope that his son is alive.

When Lagravis is watching the heroes of Chima leave for the Outlands, he gives Laval a special shield and wishes him a safe trip. They don't see each other until the end of the season. Lagravis notices the hero coming and immediately whispers his son name in relief. After the other tribes arrive, he declares it "one of the happiest days in all of the history of Chima." He states that it's because the Legend Beasts have returned "and so has my son." before pulling Laval into a close hug. Their experiences in the war for CHI brought them much closer together as family and tribe-mates.

In the beginning of season 3, Laval makes a comment about how his father gave him "a sweet new ride". This implies that Lagravis is very proud of his son. During the episode 'The Crescent', Lagravis is horrified when Laval admits that he can't remember when Lagravis had last laughed, he makes a goal to show his son that he is capable of laughter. At the end, he laughs at his son when he tells him about Li'Ella. He clearly believes his son is to young for love, which shows that he is still a bit over protective of his son.

In 'The King Thing', Laval is forced to take on the responsibility of being king, as his father was captured in the previous episode. He struggles with it greatly. All he wants is his father back. When the Ice Hunters attack, he fights Sir Fangar, telling him to "Give me my father" in "A Very Slippery Slope" when they free the captives. Laval hugs his father, telling him how glad he was to have him back and his dislike of being king.

Lavertus[]

Lavertus and Laval are closely related, but they never met until Laval was a teenager and had to journey into the Outlands to find the Legend Beasts. They have a lot in common and have a close bond.

They are apart in season one, and Laval doesn't even know Lavertus is still alive or that Lavertus is his uncle. Laval only met Lavertus when Lavertus was disguised as ShadoWind.

They meet in the first episode of season two, and although Lavertus acts odd, Laval thinks he is cool and is amazed that he has a workout dummy too. They become good friends, and Laval is amazed that he is actually ShadoWind. In the last episode of season 2, Laval learns that they are uncle and nephew right before Lavertus apparently dies in a rockfall. Laval later learns more about him by consulting Skinnet, exploring his home, and reading his book about his adventures, known as the "ShadoWind Chronicles".

Laval is amazed at the fact that his uncle is alive. They meet again in the gorge where Lavertus rescues him and his friends.

Love Interest

Love Interest[]

Li'Ella[]

When Laval and Li'Ella meet in "The Crescent", Laval starts to have a crush on her. Li'Ella thinks that some male lions are cute, mostly Laval. When she was captured by Sir Fangar, Laval showed great care for Li'Ella as he went to rescue her right away in "A Very Slippery Slope" and continued to seek her with all of Chima's tribes in "The Artifact". In "The Heart of Cavora", Li'Ella calls Laval her boyfriend.

They've since started dating.

Friends

Friends[]

Cragger[]

Laval and Cragger are best friends. They have known each other since they were little kids. Until the battles for CHI began, they never had any fights (except play-fights). Their friendship all changed when Cragger started only caring about getting more and more CHI. Eventually, they resolved their conflicts and became friends again. They have one of the strongest bonds of friendship in all of Chima.

In season one, they are best friends at the beginning, but when Cragger plugs CHI before the Age of Becoming, everything changes for many months.

In season two, their friendship is intact again, and it has become even stronger since they have to venture to the Outlands together and save Chima.

Eris[]

Laval and Eris have been friends for a very long time and they became best friends after Cragger became temporarily evil. It is hinted that they may have stronger feelings for each other, but they do not actually say it aloud to anyone. Both of them share common ideas on peace in Chima and they enjoy going on adventures together, but Eris often has to cheer Laval up when he whines about chores and fights. Eris is always there for Laval when he needs help and Laval is eager to do the same for her. Laval likes to tease Eris that he calls her "Wing-Girl" while Eris often calls him "Fur-Boy" in return.

Many residents of Chima see them as a couple, but they both immediately deny that they like each other in that way. In some ways, this makes it seem like they do in fact have crushes on each other since they act very awkward afterwards. In one of Eris' biographies, it states that she has a secret crush on Laval (at least since the start of Season 1).

In the first season, Eris and Laval are almost always seen together, whether in battles, quests, gatherings or just hanging out or partying.

  • In The Warrior Within, Cragger (when he was under Crooler's manipulation by using a Persuader Plant) calls Eris "Laval's girlfriend", which embarrasses them both. Later, Eris saves Laval and risks her own life for her friend in the Gorge of Eternal Depth.
  • The Joy Ride is one of the episodes in which they spend the most leisure time together. They drive around Chima having a mostly fun time (except when they get stuck or crash). They often tease and flirt with each other about their bad driving and navigational skills.

It is shown that Laval is good friends with Eris when he asks for a tour of her home since no one really ever asks to go to the Eagle Spires. He later helps her defend her home by calling his Lion Tribe friends and fighting off Wolves while Eris saves Laval from falling to his death as well.

  • In Balancing Act, Eris showed great concern for Laval when he was trying to retrieve a bag of CHI orbs that was in the Gorge of Eternal Depth. When the rope snapped, Eris was saddened when she feared that Laval was gone. However, Laval managed to escape the gorge with the CHI orbs and after Eris saw Laval that he was alright, she immediately hugged him passionately. Later, they battle the Wolves together, and then they go to a party with the Gorilla Tribe. Eris flirts with Laval and they start dancing and having a great time with the Gorillas.

In many of the later episodes, their friendship stays very strong where Eris comes to Laval's aid first whenever he needs help, like in Crocodile Tears and Chima Falls. Their friendship was possibly the strongest it ever was then since Eris begins to cry and hugs Laval when he is briefly exiled, when he comes back at the end, Eris is seen excitedly flying towards him tackling him in a hug only for both to be knocked to the ground laughing.

Laval and Eris' friendship is much the same as it was in the first season where they have a very strong bond and they are supportive of each other, although they interact a little less now that they are traveling with Gorzan, Cragger, Razar, Bladvic, Worriz, Rogon, Rinona and Lavertus.

  • In "Fire Dreaming", Eris tells Laval how much she liked being with him before Cragger returned to being good again, the Legend Beasts being saved, and Mount Cavora being restored possibly meaning that she has a crush on Laval.
  • In "The Crescent", while riding on their Speedorz, they share a loving glance after he roared for the other tribe members so that they can defend the crescent.
  • In "Cool and Collected", Eris becomes annoyed on seeing Laval more interested in speaking with Li'Ella than helping her watch the Phoenix prince Flinx, which might also mean that she became jealous that Laval was spending time with Li’Ella than with her.

Worriz[]

Laval and Worriz were friends as children, but not best friends. They soon became enemies during the conflict over Chi. They never really became very good friends after that, but they have learned to work together for the sake of Chima.

They are seen playing hide-and-seek together when they were a little younger. However, Worriz shows that he has outgrown his friendship with Laval when he complains to Cragger about how boring Laval's "kiddie games" are. They are enemies throughout the rest of the season, and whereas Cragger occasionally becomes Laval's friend again, Worriz always stays vicious and mean. He even is reluctant to sign the treaty of peace and has to be forced to by this tribe mates. Eventually, he does become good again, but he and Laval have many differences and are not very good friends.

The two heroes go into the Outlands, but they rarely talk with each other. Worriz is seen sad, because his Legend Beast hasn't been found yet and Laval cheers him up a little, showing that he still wants to be friends with Worriz. Worriz and Laval work together for one of the first times in the Webisode "Game of Legends". By the end of this season they are completely back to being good friends like old times.
