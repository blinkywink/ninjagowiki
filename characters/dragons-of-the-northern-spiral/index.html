Overview

“Members of my tribe, the Dragons of the Northern Spiral, are on their way to honor my passing. But they will not arrive in time.”
— Egalt to the ninja, "The Hollow Ones"

The Dragons of the Northern Spiral are a tribe of dragons who live in the Northern Spiral.

History

History[]

Background[]

Millenia ago, Egalt, Kex'k Metalwing, and King Urd Leatherwing of the Northern Spiral, fought in the War of Oni and Dragon as part of the Dragon army. At some point, a former leader of the tribe, King Urd Leatherwing was trained in the art of of Spinjitzu by the First Spinjitzu Master himself. He later trained Ekesian Coldfang the Wise, who consequently taught the skill to the future Dragon Masters, Egalt and Rontu, the former also belonging to the tribe. Another accomplishment of Leatherwing was writing The Sagas of the Eternal Sources, a book that would later find its way to the Fengxian family. The Dragon Masters would band together and create the Rising Dragon technique to counter the Forbidden Five's Shatterspin, once the conquerors of the Wyldness were defeated, the two dragons banished the Five to the Nether-Space.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Thousands of years later, after Lord Ras reunited the Wolf Clan, Lloyd was guided by his visions to seek out the Training plateau of the Dragon Masters. Once the ninja travelled to the dojo, they succesfully passed the three tests and were trained by the Dragon Masters, who were initially hesitant to help humans again before discovering Ras' plan to free the Forbidden Five. During the ninja's training, Arin had accidentally blinded one of Egalt's eyes, and was demoted. After Kai had unlocked Rising Dragon, the Blood Moon arose, and the Dragon Masters were turned to stone due to their curse.

After two members of the Forbidden Five escaped from Nether-Space, they targeted and murdered the current Patriarch of Northern the Spiral to steal his Crowns of Horns. After his demise, his child became the next Arc Dragon of Northern Spiral.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

In the following weeks after the Tournament of the Sources, Egalt's Wasting Sickness had progressed into the dragon's final stages. The ninja and the Dragons of the Northern Spiral had been invited to join him in his final moments. But only the ninja arrived in time to say goodbye.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • It was initially revealed through a post by Chris "Doc" Wyatt that Egalt is a member of the Tribe of the Northern Spiral.[4]
    • He later confirmed King Urd Leatherwing also originated from the tribe.[1]
  • Currently, all the members from the tribe who are shown or mentioned in the show are deceased, with the only current member who is still alive having their existence online being revealed on X.[5]
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, the Dragons of the Northern Spiral are more than just Ice Dragons.[6]
  • Unlike the Dragons of Fallen Leaves, which contains Forest Dragons, there isn't a name for the subspecies for the members of the tribe and they're only referred by their region.[2]
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, it can be assumed that the Dragons of the Northern Spiral possess a Motion element and can breathe it.[7] However, the tribe is also divided into many sub-tribes with different Elemental Powers amongst the Dragons.[8]
