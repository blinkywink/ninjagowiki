Overview

This article is about the character. For the campaign, see Wyldfyre (campaign).
“I realize now, if the world can't have you, it can still have awesomeness you put out into the world. It can have what Kai the Red Ninja means, even if it can't have the real you. And what I mean by that is – I'm gonna become the new Red Ninja and I'm gonna carry on your teachings and your bravery and your awesomeness.”
— Wyldfyre recording voice notes to Kai, "Ready to return!"

Wyldfyre (nicknamed Wyfy by Roby[1]) is the current Elemental Master of Heat, a ninja student, and Roby's girlfriend. She is a feral child raised by a tribe of Lava Dragons in the Lava Wastes. She came to the Wyldness via a mysterious crash landing, where she was raised by her Caregiver bot and Heatwave. Following the Merge, Wyldfyre assumed the role of the Elemental Master of Heat. When the Claws of Imperium managed to capture Heatwave, Wyldfyre ventured into Imperium, though she herself was later captured. With the help of Lloyd, she freed Heatwave and the rest of the dragons and later joined the ninja.

Wyldfyre joined the Council of the Merged Lands to represent Ninjago in Lloyd's absence, but her ninja business forced her to stand down and pass her position on to Alfonzo Frohicky.

Appearance

Appearance[]

From "Mindless Beasts" to "Rising Ninja", Wyldfyre wore a celadon robe with maroon highlights over two shirts, one red and one orange, maroon pants, tan knee pads, and an orange cloth around her waist with both sides of her hips covered by a gray samurai armor's haidate, held together with a red rope tied around a bone. She had slightly unkempt shoulder-length red hair with parts of it tied into a bun by a crescent-shaped bone on her head and straight bangs with longer pieces framing her face. She has bushy eyebrows, a burn scar on her left cheek, and what appears to be a scar on her lower lip.

She gave the bone in her hair to the baby Lava Dragons.

From "The Shape of Motion" and onward, she wore her Tournament of Sources suit because she decided to take the mantle of the "red ninja." Her suit consisted of a sleeveless red kimono-inspired robe with celadon green accents, an orange undershirt and matching belt, red knee straps, and bronze colored armor on her chest and shoulders. She also added on black gloves and switched to a neatly combed half up half down hairstyle.

As a child, Wyldfyre's unkempt hair was similar, but only went down to jaw length, and her face had no scar. Her clothes were also similar, except her shirt had a patched up gray cloth around the lower right part, a cloth of the same color tied around the waist, a rope secured by itself, and sleeves that were short to expose her orange shirt that had patches on both sleeves and her pants.

As a baby, Wyldfyre was simply wrapped in a small celadon blanket.

Personality

Personality[]

Wyldfyre is hotheaded with a short temper and very limited patience bred from a life raised in the wild. She would rather fight than use her head, even when she has no chance of winning and the odds are severely against her. She's also unintentionally disrespectful as she insulted Kai upon meeting him and seeing him use his powers for the first time. She has no filter and usually says whatever comes to mind, no matter how it may come across.

Despite her flaws, Wyldfyre is always willing to lay down her life and listen to reason if it means protecting Heatwave or her new found family. Before, the dragon was the only being she cared about until she met the ninja. She learned to appreciate and care for them and show particular affection for her mentor Kai, due not only for their common interests, but also for the way he saved Heatwave after he got hurt and helped her control her emotions. When he disappeared into Nether Space, she cried alongside Nya, indicating that she does have concern for him. Despite the loss of her mentor, she used this as an opportunity to get closer to other members of the team such as Nya.

She shows empathy for others throughout the show, displaying that she can be caring despite being seemingly self-centered, especially with dragons. This was seen in her interactions with Egalt, whom she was able to bond with after she recognized the signs of his Wasting Sickness. She also enjoys playing pranks on others, often not realizing how hurtful or dangerous they can be, something that caused her to have a very antagonizing relationship with the Lava-Tides, as she had terrorized them for years with her pranks. Due to her self-centered attitude, she came to think they actually loved her pranks and even adored her after failing to realize the posters with her picture were actually wanted posters.

She believes herself to be a great leader, despite the lack of qualities, having manipulated Frak and Euphrasia to vote for her by using their affections for Sora and Arin respectively against them.

Weapons and abilities

Weapons and abilities[]

Abilities[]

  • Heat: As an Elemental Master of Heat, Wyldfyre can create and manipulate heat, which she can also manifest as blue flames. She mostly uses this ability in the form of fireballs, heat attacks, or fire walls. Initially, her power was thought to be Fire, since they share many similarities. While she initially had terrible precision, Wyldfyre managed to blast Sora and Kai's Wolf Masks off after training under Kai for a year.
  • Skilled combatant: Although Wyldfyre had no formal training, she was taught to fight and defend herself by her dragon family, making her a formidable opponent. She gradually improved while being trained under Kai, managing to take on several Wolf warriors in "Shattered Dreams", and later in "Rising Ninja", where she managed to take out a large number of Wolf warriors, though she didn't have a mech, unlike Kai and Sora. Even when powerless, Wyldfyre managed to fight Chaos-empowered Wolf warriors. In "The Lean of a Tree", she managed to fight both Frak and Sora in the Land of Eternal Dawn to get into a pit with the two only defeating her using an illusion of Arin’s grapple gun.
  • Multilingualism: Having been raised by dragons, Wyldfyre can understand and communicate with them. She can also speak basic English, but has a limited vocabulary, and she learned how to understand the Devonian language from Zur.
  • Pilot/Driver skills: Although Wyldfyre had lived her younger years in the Wyldness raised by dragons, she learned piloting and driving skills, driving the Mech Storm Rider and her own mech very well. She also drove the Temple Bounty, albeit recklessly, which led to her fellow Ninja teammates often criticizing her driving. However, her driving skills improved over time while piloting the bounty.
  • Illusion/Temptation Resistance: Due to her upbringing with dragons, Wyldfyre was able to resist the tempting illusory effects of the Land of Eternal Dawn, after seeing how her favorite food (which only exists on the Wyldness) just mysteriously appeared.

Weapons[]

  • Dragon Hook: Wyldfyre used a Dragon Hook, channeling her elemental power to climb mountains.
  • Reveal Blade: Wyldfyre used Frak's Reveal Blade to battle Kur and the Specral Dragonians, before being defeated.

Etymology

Etymology[]

  • Her name is a pun on the word "wildfire." the spelling could be referencing her being from the Wyldness and was based on Wyldstyle form the The LEGO Movie.[5]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Having been raised by the Lava Dragon tribe, Wyldfyre can make dragon noises to communicate with dragons and even understand them.
    • She also can't read, due to not getting education.[7]
      • During the Return to the Wyldness mini-series, she handles a BorgPad with no problems, as she was raised by her Caregiver Bot.
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, Wyldfyre feels the power of Heat in her heart.[8]
  • Wyldfyre knows about the Firstbourne due to the stories of the Mythica.[5]
  • She previously shared the same hairpiece as Chang'e from the Monkie Kid sets.
  • She is one of Kevin Burke's favorite characters[9] and Chris "Doc" Wyatt finds her fun to write.[10]
  • Her name, namely the "Wyld" part, is spelled similarly to Wyldstyle from The LEGO Movie.
    • Half of her name, 'Wyld', comes from part of the name of the titular realm in which she grew up in, and the second half, 'fyre', shares the same spelling as Tygafyre, Tormak's bow.
  • Alongside Riyu, she is one of the only ninja that has never worn a hood or bandanna.
    • She is the second ninja to never wear the hood of one of their Ninja suits, the first being Nya in "Hunted" and the third being Frak in the first half of Season 3 in Ninjago: Dragons Rising.
  • She's the only ninja whose biological parents are unknown and were never shown.
  • She's the only ninja whose birth name is unknown, as her name was given to her by her dragon family.
  • She is the only ninja whose realm she was born from is unknown. In The New Heroes, it was said that she came from another planet. However, when Kevin Burke was questioned about this, he clarified that no one, not even Wyldfyre, knows her backstory. He emphasized that any information shared about her past is merely speculation.[11]
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, Wyldfyre was in the Wyldness during The Elemental Mechs.[12]
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt has confirmed that in the Phantasm Cave, during the Test of Fear, Wyldfyre saw Heatwave getting sick with Wasting Sickness.[13]
  • She has acrophobia, a fear of heights.[14]
    • She is fine as long as she is on Heatwave.[15]
  • At some point, Wyldfyre played a "prank" on the Lava-Tides which may have killed some of them.[16]
  • Despite her questionable bathroom habits, Wyldfyre is toilet-trained.[17]
  • In "For Whom the Bell Tolls", it's shown that Wyldfyre has a terrible singing voice.
    • This is ironic, considering her voice actress, Kazumi Evans, is a singer.
  • Wyldfyre, Morro, and Lloyd are the only Elemental Masters to have been seen using their elements when they were still children.
  • In early development, Wyldfyre was planned to have more of a guest role.[18]

Early life

Early life[]

When she was a baby, Wyldfyre descended from the sky in a capsule, preserved in a Caregiver bot, until it crash landed in the Wyldness, where they meet Heatwave, who came to investigate the crash, a dragon whom Wyldfyre wasn't afraid of.

Throughout her childhood, despite many dragons in the Wyldness, Heatwave played a crucial role in nurturing Wyldfyre, while the Caregiver bot fulfilled its duty of ensuring her safety and serving as her educator, even teaching her the meaning of family, which Wyldfyre knew that was what Heatwave is to her. Growing up, the dragons told her stories about two dragon warriors, Egalt and Rontu, who traveled through the realms and protect anyone who cannot protect themselves. They said that Egalt was the bravest of all dragons, so they named the strongest and largest mountain, Mount Egalt, after him, but Wyldfyre just thought that they were just fairy tales.

One night while she and Heatwave were asleep, the Merge occurred, resulting in Wyldfyre acquiring the element of Heat. With the assistance of her Caregiver bot, she learned to harness and control her Elemental Powers. As the years went by, Wyldfyre started to play pranks on the Wyldness' inhabitants: the Lava-Tides, such as hiding rotten Wyldmelons around their village, crushing Lava-Tides with bricks, and defacing the statue of Lava-Todd the Wise. Throughout her childhood, she started to lose her dragon brethren to a disease called Wasting Sickness, which she begun to fear, believing Heatwave will get it. Then, one day, Heatwave was captured by the Claws of Imperium. In a desperate attempt to rescue him, Wyldfyre left the realm and traveled to Imperium, only to get captured some point after.

Kai[]

“No one ends the Red Ninja.”
— Wyldfyre to Nokt, "Elements of Betrayal"

Kai is Wyldfyre's master. They meet in "The Battle of the Second Monastery" she unintentionally insulted him about his Fire powers being the same as hers, Kai was confused and frustrated between them but they still shared a high five over their agreement of battle. Over time Kai and Wyldfyre kept being at odds with each other but with Kai's lessons on patience, Wyldfyre slowly learned to accept and care for Kai.

In "The Temple of the Dragon Cores", the grudge goes to a new height as Wyldfyre's cocky attitude angers Kai to the point they fight each other only to be broken up by Zane. Later Kai and Wyldfyre work together to fix the sanctuary but Kai says that her percision need work

In "Gangs of the Sea", Wyldfyre makes a joke about Kai's age.

In "Wyldly Inappropriate", Kai and Wyldfyre are trapped by the Greenbone Warriors; sentenced to death for stealing their gems, while they both deny they stole them, they both figured out a way to get out of the pit and Wyldfyre admits to stealing them as a prank. Kai made an agreement to the Greenbone Warriors that they won't come back but Wyldfyre makes a joke with her Heat power allowing them to escape. Kai lectures to Wyldfyre that she needs to take her power seriously but she ignores Kai and claims he's boring. He later reminds Wyldfyre of the plan to get the Dragon Core but she still takes it lightly. When they reach the Lava-Tides: Kai is surprised and irritated with Wyldfyre's antics and ignorance towards the Lava-Tides, but tries to win them over by explaining the dire situation, convinces them that Wyldfyre has changed, and begs for the Dragon Core. With Wyldfyre slowly understanding Kai's lesson on bullying, Wyldfyre accepts her deeds and made "peace" with the Lava-Tides only to be betrayed as a prank but they work together to get the Dragon Core and escape the Pit of No Return.

In "They Call It Doom", Wyldyre's ignorance and stubborness get in the way of getting the Dragon Core and lead to Heatwave getting injured.

In "Land of Lost Things", Kai's lesson on patience and his cure for Heatwave lead to Wyldfyre being more calm and a little more patient to the current situation.

In "The Administration", Wyldfyre's impatience makes her upset and stressed with Heatwave's injury and getting the Dragon Core back to the other ninja. But Kai makes Wyldfyre follow his forms to calm her down and make her more patient. Despite Wyldfyre's doubts, Kai encourages this because she reminds Kai of himself. The forms helped Wyldfyre calm down and be more patient, letting Heatwave heal.

In "Absolute Power", Kai tells Wyldfyre to let loose and ignore his lessons about patience long enough for the ninja to get into Imperium.

After some complications, such as her grudge with the Lava-Tides, Heatwave being injured, and being taught patience, she eventually warmed up to him. When she got injured by Cinder, Wyldfyre, despite Kai's demands, came with him after smuggling aboard the Bounty. When Kai was sent to the Nether-Space, she was completely sad and dedicated herself to ensuring that she lived up to what Wyldfyre believed Kai would have wanted her to be. After his return, Wyldfyre did not hesitate to hug him.

Lloyd[]

Lloyd is Kai's friend. She first met him when he freed her from the Imperium Prison, mistaking her for Kai, and attacked because he was wearing an Imperian uniform, until he saved her from some guards and joined the ninja after escaping Imperium with Heatwave and the other dragons. Lloyd and Wyldfyre became close where after Cinder knocked Lloyd down she attempted to attack Cinder only for her leg to get broken.

Wyldfyre even cared about him as after he was injured by Zeatrix, she angrily confronted her which made the latter lose her temper. Lloyd was confused when he saw Wyldfyre and Roby roaring their love, only for Sora to drag him away.

Riyu[]

Riyu and Wyldfyre are close friends. They are often together, Riyu notably helped Wyldfyre when she was injured in the leg, by carrying her on his back. They both seem to feel good and comfortable together and remind each other of their origins and family, Wyldfyre having been raised by dragons and Riyu being a dragon who live with humans. They notably rest together on the same bed in the Tournament of the Sources while Heatwave, Wyldfyre's family, wasn't present. Of all the young ninja Riyu seems to be the one Wyldfyre gets along with best.

When Riyu was dying in after being poisoned by a Mutation Monster, Wyldfyre was very worry and let tears flow. Later, when he was healed and had evolved into his new form, Wyldfyre was very proud of him and said that him beating the Mutation Monster was the coolest thing she have ever seen.

Nya[]

“You okay?”
“Of course! Kai and I had this totally under control.”
— Nya and Wyldfyre, "Rising Ninja"

Nya is Kai's sister. Nya appears to care for Wyldfyre as she was disappointed to see the latter stowed away, despite having a broken leg. When Kai was sent to the Nether-Space, they became closer. Nya comforted Wyldfyre, but expressed worry upon seeing the latter trying to honor him by becoming the new Red Ninja from being overconfident. Nya also often help retrain Wyldfyre of her anger and Wyldfyre in return show care for Nya, notably when she was worries about Nya when her boyfriend Jay was corrupted by Shatterspin, or when she assiduously supported Nya and showed strong animosity towards her opponent, in Nya's duel with Nokt in the Tournament of the Sources while the latter tried to destabilize her by evoking Kai and Jay; as well as when Wyldfyre and Sora, hugged Nya in "The Vault of Focus" after she was disappointed in Jay's behavior, while she hoped that there was still goodness in him.

Arin[]

Arin is Wyldfyre's teammate. Arin once gave Wyldfyre a brief reprimand for her actuality of relieving herself wherever she wants, and wondering if she'll ever train with him and the others. During the Tournament of the Sources, Arin and Wyldfyre both searched for clues for Bleckt's involvement in Ras' plan to use the Forbidden Five. When Arin leaves the team, she is both upset and concerned about Arin since she has grown close to him. During the battle against the Chaos Dragon, Wyldfyre shows no contempt or any hard feelings on Arin as she helped him to gain the upper hand against the Thunderfang. Following Arin's victory, she is saddened when Arin leaves the team again that she quickly jumped on board in finding Barry and Jenny after Jay reveals that Arin's parents are alive. Showing that she doesn't want Arin to be led astray. When Arin rejoins the team again, she immediately welcomed him back.

Enemies

Enemies[]

Lava-Tides[]

Wyldfyre's constant pranking led to the Lava-Tides having a tremendous dislike for her.

Beatrix[]

As Beatrix was responsible for capturing Heatwave (and thus for Wyldfyre's imprisonment as well), Wyldfyre likely hated the former Empress for her crimes.

Freebooters[]

Wyldfyre first encountered the Freebooters during the Battle of the Second Monastery, when they were known as the Claws of Imperium at the time. Following the Liberation of Imperium, Wyldfyre discovered that the Rapton, despite helping the ninja defeat Beatrix, had rebranded the Claws of Imperium as the Freebooters, allowing him and the Claw Hunters to persist in their dragon hunting endeavors.

Dorama[]

Wyldfyre meet Dorama when she and Kai embarked on a mission to find the purple Dragon Core. Dorama had cunningly snatching it from Kai and Wyldfyre. Wyldfyre found his speech boring and his staff's ability to drain their attacks to be unfair. Together with Kai, they tried to thwart Dorama's plan of delivering the Dragon Core to the Empress. However, their efforts were in vain as Dorama successfully swapped the real core with a fake one during their clash with the Lava-Tides.

Ras[]

Wyldfyre first met Ras during the Battle of the Second Monastery. She likely resents him for capturing Heatwave and the other dragons. Later, when Ras reassembled the Wolf Clan, Wyldfyre and the rest of the ninja embarked on a mission to prevent Ras from releasing the Forbidden Five. Although Ras managed to free Nokt (and unknowingly Rox) by using Kai as a sacrifice. Her resentment towards him presumably increased because of this. With the help of Zur, she was able to expose his and Bleckt's conspiracy throughout the Tournament of Sources.

Cinder[]

“Oops, sorry”
“That was on purpose Cinder!”
“So? What are you going to do about it? You and your little ninja friends gonna cry?”
“Keep talking and you’ll be the one crying! Tears of fire!”

Wyldfyre has a massive grudge against Cinder. During their first battle, Cinder easily defeated her by breaking her leg. She was presumably satisfied to see her rival incarcerated along with the Wolf Clan.
