Overview

Wyldfyre (nicknamed Wyfy by Roby[1]) is the current Elemental Master of Heat, a ninja student, and Roby's girlfriend. She is a feral child raised by a tribe of Lava Dragons in the Lava Wastes. She came to the Wyldness via a mysterious crash landing, where she was raised by her Caregiver bot and Heatwave. Following the Merge, Wyldfyre assumed the role of the Elemental Master of Heat. When the Claws of Imperium managed to capture Heatwave, Wyldfyre ventured into Imperium, though she herself was later captured. With the help of Lloyd, she freed Heatwave and the rest of the dragons and later joined the ninja.

Appearance

Appearance[]

From "Mindless Beasts" to "Rising Ninja", Wyldfyre wore a celadon robe with maroon highlights over two shirts, one red and one orange, maroon pants, tan knee pads, and an orange cloth around her waist with both sides of her hips covered by a gray samurai armor's haidate, held together with a red rope tied around a bone. She had slightly unkempt shoulder-length red hair with parts of it tied into a bun by a crescent-shaped bone on her head and straight bangs with longer pieces framing her face. She has bushy eyebrows, a burn scar on her left cheek, and what appears to be a scar on her lower lip.

She gave the bone in her hair to the baby Lava Dragons.

From "The Shape of Motion" and onward, she wore her Tournament of Sources suit because she decided to take the mantle of the "red ninja." Her suit consisted of a sleeveless red kimono-inspired robe with celadon green accents, an orange undershirt and matching belt, red knee straps, and bronze colored armor on her chest and shoulders. She also added on black gloves and switched to a neatly combed half up half down hairstyle.

As a child, Wyldfyre's unkempt hair was similar, but only went down to jaw length, and her face had no scar. Her clothes were also similar, except her shirt had a patched up gray cloth around the lower right part, a cloth of the same color tied around the waist, a rope secured by itself, and sleeves that were short to expose her orange shirt that had patches on both sleeves and her pants.

As a baby, Wyldfyre was simply wrapped in a small celadon blanket.

Personality

Personality[]

Wyldfyre is hotheaded with a short temper and very limited patience bred from a life raised in the wild. She would rather fight than use her head, even when she has no chance of winning and the odds are severely against her. She's also unintentionally disrespectful as she insulted Kai upon meeting him and seeing him use his powers for the first time. She has no filter and usually says whatever comes to mind, no matter how it may come across. Despite her flaws, Wyldfyre is always willing to lay down her life and listen to reason if it means protecting Heatwave or her new found family. Before, the dragon was the only being she cared about until she met the ninja. She learned to appreciate and care for them and show particular affection for her mentor Kai. When he disappeared into Nether Space, she cried alongside Nya, indicating that she does have concern for him. Despite the loss of her mentor, she used this as an opportunity to get closer to other members of the team such as Nya. She shows empathy for others throughout the show, displaying that she can be caring despite being seemingly self-centered. She also enjoys playing pranks on others, often not realizing how hurtful or dangerous they can be.

Weapons and abilities

Weapons and abilities[]

Abilities[]

  • Heat: As an Elemental Master of Heat, Wyldfyre can create and manipulate heat, which she can also manifest as blue flames. She mostly uses this ability in the form of fireballs, heat attacks, or fire walls. Initially, her power was thought to be Fire, since they share many similarities. While she initially had terrible precision, Wyldfyre managed to blast Sora and Kai's Wolf Masks off after training under Kai for a year.
  • Combat: Although Wyldfyre had no formal training, she was taught to fight and defend herself by her dragon family, making her a formidable opponent. She gradually improved while being trained under Kai, managing to take on several Wolf warriors in "Shattered Dreams", and later in "Rising Ninja", where she managed to take out a large number of Wolf warriors, though she didn't have a mech, unlike Kai and Sora. Even when powerless, Wyldfyre managed to fight Chaos-empowered Wolf warriors.
  • Multilingualism: Having been raised by dragons, Wyldfyre can understand and communicate with them. She can also speak basic English, but has a limited vocabulary, and she learned how to understand the Devonian language from Zur.
  • Pilot/Driver skills: Although Wyldfyre had lived her younger years in the Wyldness raised by dragons, she learned piloting and driving skills, driving the Mech Storm Rider and her own mech very well. She also drove the Temple Bounty, albeit recklessly, which led to her fellow Ninja teammates often criticizing her driving. However, her driving skills improved over time while piloting the bounty.

Weapons[]

  • Dragon Hook: Wyldfyre used a Dragon Hook, channeling her elemental power to climb mountains.
  • Reveal Blade: Wyldfyre used Frak's Reveal Blade to battle Kur and the Specral Dragonians, before being defeated.

History

Early life

Early life[]

When she was a baby, Wyldfyre descended from the sky in a capsule, preserved in a Caregiver bot, until it crash landed in the Wyldness, where they meet Heatwave, who came to investigate the crash, a dragon whom Wyldfyre wasn't afraid of.

Throughout her childhood, despite many dragons in the Wyldness, Heatwave played a crucial role in nurturing Wyldfyre, while the Caregiver bot fulfilled its duty of ensuring her safety and serving as her educator, even teaching her the meaning of family, which Wyldfyre knew that was what Heatwave is to her. Growing up, the dragons told her stories about two dragon warriors, Egalt and Rontu, who traveled through the realms and protect anyone who cannot protect themselves. They said that Egalt was the bravest of all dragons, so they named the strongest and largest mountain, Mount Egalt, after him, but Wyldfyre just thought that they were just fairy tales.

One night while she and Heatwave were asleep, the Merge occurred, resulting in Wyldfyre acquiring the element of Heat. With the assistance of her Caregiver bot, she learned to harness and control her Elemental Powers. As the years went by, Wyldfyre started to play pranks on the Wyldness' inhabitants: the Lava-Tides, such as hiding rotten Wyldmelons around their village, crushing Lava-Tides with bricks, and defacing the statue of Lava-Todd the Wise. Throughout her childhood, she started to lose her dragon brethren to a disease called Wasting Sickness, which she begun to fear, believing Heatwave will get it. Then, one day, Heatwave was captured by the Claws of Imperium. In a desperate attempt to rescue him, Wyldfyre left the realm and traveled to Imperium, only to get captured some point after.

Dragons Rising, Season 1

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Return to Imperium[]

Wyldfyre was shooting Heat out of her cell until Lloyd, who mistook her for Kai, came over. As Lloyd opened the cell, Wyldfyre attacked him.

Mindless Beasts[]

Wyldfyre attacks Lloyd, believing he is an Imperian responsible for capturing Heatwave, until he managed to save her from getting struck by some Imperium Guards. They work together to free Heatwave, Lloyd not understanding that Heatwave was Wyldfyre's family until she told him upon freeing the latter. They soon decide to free the rest of the captured dragons.

I Will Be the Danger[]

Wyldfyre, Lloyd, and the dragons were confronted by the Imperium Guards and instructed to return to their cells. Despite Lloyd's suggestion of a diplomatic approach, Wyldfyre refused to comply and challenged the Imperium Guards, leading to another fight. In an effort to provide an escape route, Lloyd announced the opening of the hatch at the Imperium Prison. Wyldfyre took charge and commanded the dragons to ascend with Heatwave leading the way. During their escape, Wyldfyre questioned their plans to escape from Imperium. Lloyd explained that he had previously entered Imperium through the sewers, but the dragons were too large, necessitating their exit through the main gates.

Lloyd was concern regarding the damage caused by the dragons in the streets and proposed organizing them into a formation to prevent further harm. But Wyldfyre disagreed, asserting that dragons should be free and prioritize escape. Lloyd emphasized the danger to innocent Imperians and deemed the situation too perilous. In response, Wyldfyre demonstrated her dangerous nature by using Heat to neutralize the Weapon Drones. As the dragons approached the main gates, they were met by the Claws of Imperium and Photac Beasts. Rapton presented two options between surrendering or fighting. Wyldfyre agreed with Rapton's preference for fighting and signaled the dragons to charge forward and engage in combat.

The Calm Inside[]

Wyldfyre continued to battle the Photacs alongside Lloyd and the dragons, even getting knocked off at one point before swiftly returning to battle. Later, Lloyd tells Wyldfyre to follow him, which she thought was that he planned to retreat, until he told her that they're going to attack the palace, which she agreed, and they all head for the palace.

Later, a strange monastery soon rose up, confusing her, and the entrance opened up, revealing Kai, Zane, and Nya.
