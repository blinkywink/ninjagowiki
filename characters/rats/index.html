Overview

“Rats are exceedingly clever animals with an impressive survival instinct.”
— Zane to the ninja, "Darkness Within"

Rats are rodents in Ninjago and Prime Empire.

History

History[]

Tournament of Elements[]

The Corridor of Elders[]

After escaping on Rodrigo and finding the Book of Spells, Pythor traveled on Rodrigo to the Corridor of Elders to tell Lloyd about a spell that can stop the Serpentine War. Afterwards, Pythor set him free.

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Skybound[]

Public Enemy Number One[]

When the Ninjago Police was chasing the ninja, they were tricked into following a rat with a location device.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

Hunted[]

The Gilded Path[]

While turning from a rat to human, Mystake revealed herself to be the Oni.

Prime Empire[]

Game Over[]

The Whack Rats arrive in Ninjago with the former citizens of Prime Empire and the NPCs, becoming actual anthropomorphic rats. However, many of the Whack Rats willingly return to Prime Empire.

Crystalized[]

Darkness Within[]

Kai, Jay, Cole, and Zane were trapped within the rubble. They manage to find an exit by following a rat, but they fail to find a way to the surface.
