Overview

“Acronix was a skilled warrior, one of the best I've ever seen, but his real power was his ability to control the forward movement of time. By sending himself forward, he was always ahead of the action, as if he had super speed. However, a slow progression can be just as dangerous. Different power, but equally effective.”
— Wu to the ninja, "The Hatching"

Acronix is one of the two current (albeit powerless) Elemental Masters of Time. He is the younger twin brother of Krux. Once a proud Elemental Master who helped win the Serpentine War, Acronix and his brother betrayed the Elemental Alliance due to them believing that their Elemental Power was the strongest and entitling them to rule Ninjago. In response, their former allies Ray and Maya, under Wu's orders, forged a set of weapons that absorbed the twins' element. Acronix and Krux were lost in the temporal vortex, but Krux managed to escape and planned on how they would rule Ninjago by using the Vermillion as foot soldiers and the Time Blades to go back in time to defeat their former allies.

For four decades, Acronix remained in the temporal vortex and didn't age during that time. Upon his return at the Monastery of Spinjitzu, he was confronted by Wu, who had been waiting for him. Acronix was swiftly beaten by Wu, but by using the Forward Time Blade, Wu began to rapidly age. Acronix reunited with his brother, who had bred an army of Vermillion snakes since his escape. After capturing Cyrus Borg, they began building a time-traveling weapon, the Iron Doom, so they can travel into the past and give themselves a better future. Despite his brother's animosity towards technology, Acronix greatly enjoyed it, even going as far as to acquire a BorgPad and BorgWatch. The twins disagree constantly about technology, but nevertheless love each other and strive towards their goal together.

Without a master to guide them, the ninja fought against Acronix and Krux numerous times but were unable to gain the upper hand. Ultimately, the Iron Doom was completed, and Acronix traveled back in time to defeat the Elemental Masters. Kai and Nya made a final effort to thwart their plans by aiding the Elemental Masters. When the Iron Doom tried traveling into the future, Wu removed the Reversal Time Blade from the weapon, causing it to spiral out of control. Though Kai and Nya escaped, Acronix was lost in the Temporal Vortex along with his brother and their greatest enemy.

While Wu managed to escape, he and his brother's fates are currently unknown.

History

History[]

Background[]

Acronix was born 3 minutes after Krux.[1] They were both descendants of the original Elemental Master of Time and had been raised by their mother. At some point in his life, he learned about the Destiny's Bounty. Eventually he and Krux discovered they were the current Elemental Masters of Time.

While Krux could go back in Time and pause Time, Acronix could go forward in Time to avoid opponents and attack them from behind and slow down Time to slow down attacks and move away from the line of fire. Although Acronix used these powers to great advantage, he was still a very skilled warrior without them, being one of the best Wu has ever seen.

As Wu traveled the lands to form the Elemental Alliance, he was able convince Krux and Acronix to join it. During the Battle of Tanaka City, they saved Libber's life. However, after the battle, they disappeared for unknown reasons.[2] After the war, they came back and were congratulated along with the other Elemental Masters at the Monastery of Spinjitzu.[3]

To achieve some form of victory in his downfall, Master Chen had his right-hand man, Clouse, use his Dark Magic to manipulate Krux and Acronix into betraying the Elemental Alliance, and Chen informed the twins of the location of the Great Devourer's eggs.[4]

Knowing that they were more powerful than the other Elemental Masters, Krux and Acronix decided to betray the alliance and use their powers to control all of Ninjago. The other Elemental Masters fought against the Time Twins, but the two proved too powerful. During this, he fought Ray and Maya. When they try to blast him with Fire and Water, he went forward in time and attack Ray from behind and slowed down Ray's fireball to move away from it. After he and Krux finished the battle, they recite their signature motto to the Elemental Alliance; "Control Time, control everything."[3]

With the Time Blades made from Chronosteel forged by Ray and Maya,[5] Wu and Garmadon fought a lengthy battle against the Time Twins at the Monastery of Spinjitzu. Eventually, Krux and Acronix tried to blast Wu and Garmadon with their powers, but their Elemental Powers were absorbed into the Time Blades, rendering them normal men.

Aware that the power to control time was too dangerous, Wu and Garmadon used the Time Blades to open a temporal vortex that would leave the Time Blades lost forever. However, Acronix and Krux decided to go through the temporal vortex themselves, which left the two "lost to time." Acronix would be lost in the temporal vortex for the next forty years.[3] However, unbeknownst to the Elemental Alliance, Krux was somehow immediately teleported back to Ninjago and experienced a vision of the future, foretelling of Acronix's return.[5]

Wu tells them that he can hear every one of their attempts at humor, but Acronix and Krux laughed it off as they continue onward. Krux then calls for Blunck and Raggmunk, told them that he and his brother have been concerned for the leadership of the Vermillion warriors, saying that their mission didn't go quite as planned. The two general thought Machia would be in trouble, but Acronix told Machia that he and Krux were impressed with her actions, ultimately promoting her to the Supreme Commander of the Vermillion while demoting Blunck and Raggmunk to order under her, angering Blunck and Raggmunk, but Machia put them in their place. Wu then asks them if all of this promotion was premature, saying his ninja was still out there, but as they arrive at the Vermillion Swamps, Acronix told him that they had no master, no Time Blades and certainly no chance. Acronix taunted Wu by saying he almost felt sorry for them, the group laughs excluding Wu.

Acronix begins to use his BorgWatch, irking Krux. Acronix tells him he was sharing this victory with his Instabook followers, texting in that he has "achieved total victory". Krux angerly asked Acronix why does he have to ruin every good moment with his "modern techno-garbage", Acronix got annoyed and asked Krux why he was so stubborn about living in the ancient past before they head into the swamp. No one noticed a pair of red eyes watches them head-on.

Before the ninja moved out to go search for answers, Misako revealed that during Wu and Acronix's battle at the monastery, Acronix struck him with a punch powered by the Forward Time Blade, explaining that the Blade unleashed the forward energy of time onto Wu.

At the swamp, Acronix presents the Forward Time Blade to Cyrus Borg for the apparatus, but he claims he is not ready before Krux remarks that the disabled inventor released three upgrades to the BorgPad in one year as Machia threatened him with the Slow-Mo Time Blade.

Pause and Effect[]

During one of Wu's hallucinations, Acronix and Krux arrive as one of Wu's many mistakes, with Acronix telling Wu should have never came all by himself.

While Krux talked with Borg once the time apparatus is completed, Acronix arrived with Machia, saying the third Time Blade is about to arrive. Krux then took the Slow-Mo Time Blade from the apparatus and left with Acronix, Machia and Cyrus Borg to retrieve the third Time Blade. Traveling to the Glacier Barrens, Krux questioned Acronix on the Blade, Acronix said it was about to arrive in just a moment. On cue, the temporal vortex opens in the sky above, releasing the Pause Time Blade. After it landed, Time was paused. Once its effects wore off, Krux claimed the Blade and Acronix told him that the BorgWatch worked perfectly. He then tried to get Krux to admit that not all modern technology was bad, but Krux told him that he won't until the find the final Blade, a task to which Acronix's BorgWatch was utterly useless. As Krux returns to Machia's ship, he quietly told himself that their mother always said Krux was stubborn.

While this happened, Kai and Nya reunited with Ray and Maya, their father and mother respectively. Kai told Nya that they were the enemy, saying they've been helping Krux and Acronix for years. Maya told them they forced them to work for them, explaining what truly happened. When Krux and Acronix were still the Elemental Masters of Time, Wu devised a plan that involved Ray and Maya's skill. Wu tasked them with creating the Time Blades in order to strip Krux and Acronix of their powers. However, that left them weakened once the deed was done, explaining why they weren't present during the battle with Krux and Acronix and the monastery. They further explained that after Krux and Acronix went after the Blades by going into the temporal vortex, Wu saw a vision that described the exact date of when Acronix would return to the monastery. However, Krux escaped the vortex and saw this vision as well. In the time between the battle at the monastery and Acronix's return, Krux created his master plan, starting off by breeding some Vermillion warriors. He then went on to capture Ray and Maya, threatening that if they didn't work for him, he would kill their children. Ray then tells them that his markings on the Vermillion helmets were a message for someone to find out the truth, saying that together, they could stop Krux and Acronix's ultimate plan to control all of Time.

Machia's ship had returned to the Vermillion Swamps. Kai forgave them for his accusations, Nya asked how Krux and Acronix planned on controlling all of Time. Ray revealed the blueprints for the Iron Doom, a mech that served as a transport for the Vermillion across Time. Maya then reveals that the reason why they captured Cyrus Borg was so he could build a power source for the Iron Doom, which involved harnessing the powers of all four Time Blades. Simultaneously, Krux and Acronix, along with a battalion of Vermillion warriors lead by Machia, snuck up onto the blacksmith shop, where they high-fived and overhear that the Reversal Time Blade, the fourth and final Time Blade, was ultimately placed in the Boiling Sea by them under the orders of Wu after they discovered it in the woods below the monastery, saying they only the Masters of Fire and Water could get it. Maya realize that Kai and Nya could be used, the group were about to leave but were confronted by Krux and Acronix, who then wants them to obtain it for them. After pausing Ray with the Pause Time Blade and revealing Wu, Acronix told them the Reversal Time Blade was the only way to undo the effects of the Time Punch on Wu, with Krux saying that if they want to save Wu, they will retrieve the fourth Blade for them. With that, Acronix, Krux, Machia, Wu, Kai, Nya, Ray, and Maya board Machia's ship offscreen and proceed to the Boiling Sea, passing over the other ninja when they were confronted by the Vermillion lead by Blunk and Raggmunk.

Out of the Fire and Into the Boiling Sea[]

After Machia's ship approachs the Boiling Sea, Krux told his brother that the final Time Blade would soon be theirs. Machia wondered if it was a ninja trick, Acronix responded that it best not be for the sake of the dying Wu. Upon arriving, Ray explained they need the Dragon Blade, he took it from Krux. Acronix asked him what he was doing, Machia immediately attacked Ray, but he told them that the Dragon Blade was the key in getting them the last Time Blade. Krux told him that it's only one Blade and that they still have three Time Blades. Acronix said that escape was futile, though he would like to see them try. Krux ordered Machia to give the Blade to Kai and Nya. Acronix then witnessed Kai and Nya create the Fusion Dragon and used it to plunge into the Boiling Sea in hopes of obtaining the final Time Blade.

After a while, Acronix wonders where Kai and Nya are, asking Krux what to do if they failed. At that moment, the two return on the Fusion Dragon with the Reversal Time Blade in hand. After landing on the ship, Kai rushes to use it on Wu but is stopped by Acronix and Krux, who revealed they lied about allowing Kai to use the Reversal Time Blade to save Wu, asking him what did they expect to allow Wu to remain after all the trouble he has caused them in the past. A fight begins, with Acronix fighting Nya with the Pause and Forward Time Blades. However, he is kicked away by Nya after Machia is knocked into Krux.

As Kai rushes to Wu's aid, Acronix appears from behind and is about to strike Kai with the Forward Time Blade, but Kai is pushed out of the way by Ray, who takes the hit instead, causing him to begin to rapidly age. Rejoining with his brother, Acronix then pauses the group, with Krux commending his brother for his work. Now with all four Time Blades, Acronix says it is time to return to the swamps, with Krux asking what to do with the others, with Acronix ordering Machia to drop them off at their current location. After disposing of them, they return to the Vermillion Dome of Doom at the Vermillion Swamps.

Upon arriving at the Dome, they see that the other ninja are attacking and that Zane, Samurai X, and Cyrus Borg are attempting to disable the Iron Doom. Ordering Machia to proceed as planned, Krux and Acronix jump off the ship and land behind the trio. Krux then proceeds to pause the trio with the Pause Time Blade just before Zane could put in the final number to disable the Iron Doom, with Acronix saying they have zero chance. After plugging in the four Time Blades into the Time Apparatus and the Iron Doom begins to power on, Acronix punches the trio off the side of the scaffolding, but they are caught by Jay, Cole, and Lloyd. Acronix, Krux, and Machia then board the Iron Doom after the other Vermillion warriors. As the Iron Doom destroys the surrounding scaffolding, it charges up and creates a temporal vortex in the skies above the Iron Doom. Krux tells Acronix that it will be ready in just a few more moments and Acronix says that Ninjago will be theirs and that nothing can stop them. At that moment, the Fusion Dragon arrives, to which Krux angrily asks his brother why he jinxed it. As the Iron Doom became fully ready, The Hands of Time then say their signature quote as the Iron Doom launches into the temporal vortex, unaware that the Fusion Dragon carrying Kai, Nya, and Wu followed them into the vortex.

Lost in Time[]

The Iron Doom proceeds to travel back in time through the temporal vortex. Acronix admires the majesty of time travel, he takes a picture of the vortex with his BorgPad, saying it wasn't quite capturing the majesty and needed a different filter. However, Krux knocks the BorgPad aside and tells him that they are approaching their temporal destination before ordering Machia to ready her troops. As Krux explains to Acronix that decades of planning was finally about to pay off and that the battle will be re-fought, Acronix tells him that no one can stop them. The Iron Doom then begins to shake violently as it arrives in the past.

Landing at the Monastery of Spinjitzu forty years in the past directly after the past versions of Krux and Acronix were lost in time, the current versions of Krux and Acronix confront the past Elemental Alliance, where Acronix gloats over their confidence. Krux explains to them that they have stopped them once today, but it was before they had a legion spawned from the Great Devourer. After young Wu tells the group to focus, Acronix tells Machia it was time, who then orders her Vermillion army into battle.

Kai and Nya began to secretly help the past Elemental Alliance by posing as Ray and Maya, they begin to defeat the Vermillion. Krux says they are losing, Raggmunk and Blunk arrive, saying it was only a temporary setback. Acronix tells them that they did not travel across decades to suffer temporary setbacks, Krux nods to Acronix as they smash the two commanders into the component snakes before taking their helmets. As Machia arrives, Krux then reveals she too has outlived her usefulness and destroyed her, with Acronix saying she was relieved of her duties before boarding the Iron Doom.

Using their helmets, Acronix and Krux telepathically smash the rest of the Vermillion warriors and order them to return to the Iron Doom. As the snakes merge with the Iron Doom, Acronix tells the Iron Doom to arise as it comes to life and towers over the monastery. Using the Iron Doom, the Hands of Time are able to overpower the Elemental Alliance and capture young Wu, where they tell him their terms of surrender. Krux tells him that if he yields, they will destroy the Elemental Alliance, but will spare the citizens of Ninjago while Acronix tells him that if he refuses, they will still destroy them, but the citizens will not be spared. Krux asks him to yield, young Wu yields, ultimately changing the future into their image, but saving the citizens.

After throwing young Wu back to the other masters, the Iron Doom confronts them, with Acronix asking who they should destroy first between Garmadon and Wu. At that moment, the Fusion Dragon arrives and attacks the Iron Doom. Acronix said "Ray and Maya" are just as bad as their kids. Krux then tells him that they are their kids, ordering for a maximum barrage. As the Iron Doom fires Vermillion eggs at the Fusion Dragon, they all miss, and Kai and Nya blast the cockpit with their powers, knocking Krux and Acronix away from the controls. As Acronix says to fight Fire with Time, Krux tells him he hates his sayings before Acronix orders for full temporal energy. After the Iron Doom sends a Slow-Mo shot towards Nya, who bailed from the Fusion Dragon to retrieve something, the past Elemental Alliance distracts the Iron Doom. With this distraction, Kai slams the Fusion Dragon into the Iron Doom, causing it to fall onto the structure and look down the cliffside in a similar fashion as to Acronix and Wu's fight at the ruins of the monastery in the future.

After getting the Iron Doom back up, Krux said that he would destroy Kai and Nya even if he must do it with his bare hands. Acronix then told him that they still have the Iron Doom and that they could go anywhere in Time they please, saying that if the battle was too difficult in the past, they should go to a time where they are sure a victory and a time where Kai and Nya do not exist. As Krux suggests going further back in the past, Acronix suggests the opposite; far into the future. Using his helmet, Acronix uses the Iron Doom to open another temporal vortex. Just before they could leave, the Fusion Dragon attempted to strike it one last time, but Acronix orders it to duck. As the Fusion Dragon de-materialized, the Iron Doom entered the vortex as it closed behind them. Unfortunately for the Hands of Time, Nya managed to recover the past Reversal Time Blade from the woods below the monastery and used it to restore Wu. With the past Reversal Time Blade, Kai, Nya, and Wu reverse time so that they could follow the Hands of Time into the temporal vortex.

Aboard the Iron Doom, Acronix and Krux discussed their next course of action when they are suddenly confronted by Kai, Nya, and Wu. Furious over their constant interference with their plans, the twins engaged the trio in a final battle. When Kai and Nya began to overpower Krux, Acronix kicked down Wu in order to assist his brother. However, this allowed Wu to sabotage the Iron Doom while the twins were distracted with finishing off Kai and Nya, causing the Iron Doom to spiral out of control within the vortex. Once Kai and Nya were pushed out of the Iron Doom by Wu, Acronix and Krux regained their bearings before engaging Master Wu, ultimately becoming lost in time in the process.

Personality

Personality[]

Unlike his calculated brother, Acronix is a young, arrogant warrior and very excitable with a fascination for modern technology after having been sent forty years into the present. While he may give off a clueless air, he is a very skilled warrior who is very confident in his abilities and lives to fight all the time. He is quite ruthless as he didn't hesitate to batter Wu and insult him for being an old man since he last saw him. Acronix is also very impatient and short-tempered in nature, and would rather prefer the use of his modern technology rather than Krux's old-fashioned ways.

It appears Krux is the only person he cares about as he went to visit him and reconnected with him by hugging him in their reunion. He also ceased his battle with Wu and went to his brother's aid when Nya and Kai overwhelmed him. He greatly cares for and respects Krux, calling him "Brother" instead of his first name, and he never disobeys him despite their differences.

According to Krux, Acronix has a habit of making up sayings as wisdom, which the former hates. After returning to Ninjago and discovering the technology of the present era, he began adopting modern language to his otherwise formal speech.

Abilities and weapons

Abilities and weapons[]

Abilities[]

  • Time (formerly): Acronix was gifted with half of the ability of Time, specifically Forward and Slow-Mo. He used this to great lengths, often using them to get the advantage over his opponents.
    • Time Acceleration - Acronix was able to speed up time forward. It can be used to accelerate the aging of an opponent or to make him "teleport" by accelerating his own time for short periods. He could "teleport" others with him by touching them, such as his twin, or by choice. This appears to involve green temporal energy. This power is currently stored in the Forward Time Blade.
      • Paleokinesis - Nicknamed the Time Punch by Acronix, this allows him to accelerate someone's age, eventually leading to death. It can only be undone by using the opposite power, Reverse, on the one affected.
    • Time Reduction - Acronix was able to slow down time, reducing the movement speed of the affected object, person, or area. He mainly used this power to slow down an opponent's attack and moved away from them. This appears to involve blue temporal energy. This power is currently stored in the Slow-Mo Time Blade.
      • Time Reduction Ball Projection - When Acronix fired a Slow-Mo bubble at his opponent, this created a powerful energy field, slowing down the target and nearby objects caught within the bubble. It is possible to knock objects or people out of the energy field.
  • Hive Mind: With Blunck's helmet, he can control the Vermillion.
  • Skilled Combat: Acronix is a highly capable fighter, one of the best Wu has ever seen.
    • Perfect timing: Acronix knows when to dodge opponents with ease. This is not part of his Elemental Powers, but a skill of his own.
    • Swordsmanship: Acronix is a skilled swordsman. He can wield two blades without setbacks.
  • Fast learning: Acronix had quickly able to learn how to use basic technology within a day despite being lost in time for four decades.
    • Technology Proficiency: Even though he was outside of time for four decades, Acronix is fairly capable of using technology.
      • BorgWatch: Acronix's second and favorite piece of technology. He uses this to find the remaining Time Blades with the temporal scanner. He also uses it to post on his Instabook account.
      • BorgPad: Acronix's first piece of technology to own. He got it from the museum gift shop. He mainly uses it to take pictures or play games on it.

Weapons[]

  • Time Blades: Acronix wielded the Time Blades before plugging them in the Iron Doom.
    • Forward Time Blade: Acronix main weapon. He wielded the Forward Time Blade that gave him his former ability to go forward Time. He used the Time Blade to inflict the Time Punch onto Wu.
    • Pause Time Blade (briefly): Acronix briefly wielded the Pause Time Blade, which gave him Krux's power to pause Time. He used this to pause Ray, Maya, Nya, and Kai to leave them at the Boiling Sea.
  • Bo staff (formerly): When he and Krux fought against the Elemental Alliance, he wielded a bo staff.
  • Katanas: Acronix wields several katanas.
  • Dagger: Acronix wielded a dagger when he was fighting with Wu at the Monastery. He dropped it when he nearly fell to his death. He later obtain another one when he fought Wu, Kai, and Nya on the Iron Doom.

Weaknesses[]

  • Short attention span: When he's using his devices, he doesn't seem to pay attention to what's around him, often ignoring his brother or surroundings until Krux takes it away from him.

Etymology

Etymology[]

  • Acronix and his brother are named after a digital file-sharing software used for processing the show. Acronix is named after Acronis.[7]
    • Acronix's name also sounds similar to the prefix "chrono", which refers to time, though this may be a coincidence.
    • Acronix's name is similar to the word "chronic" which means long-lasting.
    • His name is also similar to "anachronic" which means "a person or a thing that is chronologically out of place," fitting as he's originally someone from 4 decades ago.
Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • Acronix and Krux were originally meant to be one character, named "Kronos."[8]
  • Like Lloyd, Acronix's hairpiece was originally used for a Star Wars character, as a brown version first released with Qui-Gon Jinn.
  • Chen and Clouse being responsible for the betrayals of Krux and Acronix was originally a scrapped idea for The Hands of Time.[9]
  • He and his brother are two of the eight characters to have a cape in the sets, the others being Chen, Morro, Harumi, Wu, Akita, and Mammatus.
  • In the sets, his minifigure's head has a wireless earbud, though it wasn't in the television show at any point.
    • He has a MP3 player printed onto his torso, as a sign of his love for technology.
    • His outfit and armor are much brighter and vivid than his brother's due to him being in a temporal vortex for forty years.
    • Until "Scavengers," his torso lacked the MP3 player, making that torso non-physical.
  • Though the colored detailing around Acronix and Krux's eyes was simply a designer's choice with no specific explanation,[10] one possibility is tattoos.[11]
  • In regards to where Krux and Acronix went in time, Ian Hanlin, Acronix's voice actor, stated, "There's a whole lot of timelines and a whole lot of time streams, and they could be anywhere."[12]
    • When asked if he hoped Acronix would return, Hanlin said he hoped that, "when least expected, suddenly everybody starts slowing down and reversing, and then oh-ho-ho-ho-ho-ho..."[13]

Relationships

These are the relationships of one of the Time Twin, Acronix.

Family

Family[]

Krux[]

Acronix's brother Krux is his older twin by 3 minutes and co-villain.

The twins rebelled against the Elemental Alliance after the Serpentine War and sent themselves into a Time Vortex to escape their punishment.

Acronix went 40 years into the future while Krux escaped and lived out that time in Ninjago City using the name Dr. Saunders, awaiting his brother's arrival. In their reunion, Acronix hugged him for the first time in decades and started making plans with to get their revenge and conquer Ninjago. The Time Twins were a good team, in spite of their opposing views such as technology and other matters. Nonetheless they still cared for each other, as Acronix was quick to disregard his fight with Wu to save his brother when he was overwhelmed by Kai and Nya and they were nearly successful in killing the ninja but Wu intervened.

They are currently still trapped together in the vortex because of Wu.

Allies

Allies[]

Machia (formerly)[]

“I mean, she's cute, sure. On the surface, but deep down, she's a pile of snakes.”
— Acronix, "A Line in the Sand"

Supreme Commander Machia was one of the three Vermillion Commanders created by Krux and entrusted to command the Vermillion Army for the twins. Acronix liked and favored Commander Machia for her cunning intelligence over the other two incompetent Commanders Blunck and Raggmunk. Krux even (teasingly) suggested that Acronix had a crush on her, which he angrily and quickly rebuffed. He explained to Krux that he considers Machia cute on the surface but deep down she's a pile of snakes. Her worth was so great to the twins that they promoted her to the newly created rank of Supreme Commander of the Vermillion Army, and made Blunck and Raggmunk her subordinates. Despite Machia winning the Twins favor, neither Acronix or Krux hesitated to fire her from her ranks and privileges before proceeding to smash and store her component snakes when they took over control of the Vermillion warriors.

Blunck and Raggmunk (formerly)[]

“We did not travel across decades to suffer temporary setbacks.”
— Acronix, "Lost in Time"

Commanders Blunck and Raggmunk were the two other Vermillion Commanders created by Krux and entrusted to command the Vermillion Army for the twins. Acronix despised the two for their stupidity, general annoyance, and incompetence. In turn, the two were jealous at Commander Machia for winning the twins favor and always sought to win Acronix and Krux's full respect to be their only commanders. The twins trust in the two was severely tarnished when Blunck and Raggmunk failed to retrieve the Slow-Mo Time Blade and lied about using and losing the Time Blade. Eventually, they demoted Blunck and Raggmunk to serve as Machia's subordinates for their failures. After traveling back in time to exact their ultimate revenge on Wu and Garmadon and the Elemental Alliance and the twins were losing, this was the last straw for the twins, especially Acronix against the two incompetent commanders for their failures. The twins then fired the two commanders and smashed them to their component snakes, and used their telepathic helmets to take over control of the Vermillion warriors.

Enemies

Enemies[]

Wu and Garmadon[]

Once good allies in the Serpentine War however, they became enemies after the twins sought to take over Ninjago. Acronix and his brother fought Wu and Garmadon but the two stole their enemies' powers, which they trapped in the blades and threw them in a temporal vortex. Wu and Garmadon watched as their foes went after them but he saw they'd return.

Forty years later, Acronix later returned from the vortex where he fought Wu and mocked him for his now old age. He was able to leave Wu in a incapacitated state with a strike from the time blade, further aging the latter. Acronix left the man to be saved by his pupils.

Acronix truly despises both Wu and his brother for their actions though never saw Garmadon again because of his death (coincidentally Garmadon was resurrected after the Twins' defeat). His grudge to the brothers extends to anyone related to them, as he detested Lloyd for being Garmadon's son and Wu's nephew. The Time Twins later abducted Wu and used him as leverage against Kai and Nya, while Acronix mocked his situation even after the ninja complied they had refused to cure him after what he and his brother put them through as a final revenge. The twins did not stop there, as when traveling back in time, they humiliated past Wu and forced him to surrender. However, Kai and Nya adverted this and got the younger master to fight back.

After being cured by his students, Wu got his revenge on the twins by confronting them with his students. The twins were greatly shocked by his return and fought him again. Acronix fought Wu once more and this time, he put up a better fight. When seeing his brother overwhelmed, he disregarded Wu and went to aid his sibling, promising to finish Wu later. However, Wu saw the aftermath of the villains' actions on his friend Ray and nearly watched them kill Kai and Nya, he decided to finally put an end to them by sabotaging the Iron Doom and choosing to stay behind to finally end their conflict. Acronix and his brother fought Wu for the last time, as they traveled through time. Acronix and his brother were Wu hindered by Wu, as he prevented them from altering time and attempted to capture him to control time after he suffered from de-aging. Acronix and his brother were finally defeated by him, when the old master ended their long feud by stranding them in the vortex. After being turned into a toddler, Wu was able to escape the vortex while the Time Twins behind and they are currently still lost in time.

Elemental Masters[]

The Elemental Alliance was a group of Elemental Masters during the serpentine wars. The Time Twins fought alongside the alliance during the war, but rebelled believing they could conquer Ninjago since they had the most powerful element.

When sent forward into the future, Acronix fought some of their descendants in the form of the ninja at the monastery. He was amused to see his old enemies lived on in a way.

Ninja[]

He attacked Wu at the ruins of the monastery, as the ninja arrived to aid Wu. Acronix fought them and saw they were the children of his enemies the Elemental masters. Acronix was the one who hinted at what caused the disappearance of Kai and Nya's parents. He holds a strong dislike for Lloyd who is related to his mortal enemies Wu and Garmadon. Acronix met Kai and Nya again, where he and his brother threatened the parents of the two ninja along with Wu unless they got the last time blade. The siblings struck back with help from the rejuvenated master and foiled the time twin's plans. Acronix and his brother fought the two and overpowered them, they would have finished them had Wu not intervened.
