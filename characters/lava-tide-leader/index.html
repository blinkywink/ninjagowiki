Overview

“We've heard great tales of the ninja, even before the Merge. For generations, our people had been entrusted with protecting the Dragon Core, until such a day that a great hero came to claim it. Is today, that day? Are you, that hero?”
— The Lava-Tide Leader to Kai, "Wyldly Inappropriate"

An unnamed Lava-Tide is the leader of the Lava-Tides.[1]

History

History[]

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

With Kai and Wyldfyre arriving at the Wyldness they asked for the Purple Dragon core, with all Lava tides including the leader to object and refuse to give the core. Kai quickly tried to turn things around introducing himself as the fire ninja, with the leader responding stating that them and their people had heard about the ninja and told them they were entrusted on protecting the core until a hero would come for it. They asked Kai if he was that hero with Wyldfyre thinking she was pointing at her saying she was. The leader quickly refused to give the core to them saying there was no way that Wyldfyre was the foretold hero before throwing the core back into the Pit of No Return. The leader tells Wyldfyre that anyone that is associated with her has a bad influence, much to Wyldfyre's disbelief. After that conversation, Kai tried convincing the leader that if Wyldfyre can prove that she has gotten better then if they get have the Purple Dragon Core, which the leader agrees too.

With Wyldfyre and Kai cleaning up the entire Lava-Tide Village, the leader was surprised that Wyldfyre managed to make amends and asked Wyldfyre to shake their hand, which she agrees to. Surprising both to Kai and Wyldfyre, the leader blasts the core up into the air landing into the Pit of No Return, saying it was a prank and telling Wyldfyre that she would never change. Kai stated without the core the world could be destroyed. The leader stated that maybe be true but told Kai to look at his face.

They Call It Doom[]

The Lava-Tide Leader and their tribe retrieved the Dragon Core from Dorama. However, Kai confronts the Lava-Tide Leader, cornering them. The Lava-Tide Leader challenged Kai by threatening to attack if he said the word "what." Falling into the trap, Kai's actions prompt the leader to order a Lava-Tide to launch flaming boulders, effectively preventing other parties from accessing the Dragon Core. Recognizing the crucial role of the Dragon Cores in saving the Merged Realms, Kai attempted to persuade the skeptical Lava-Tide Leader. Wyldfyre seized the Dragon Core from the Lava-Tide Leader, much to their dismay. The Lava-Tide Leader managed to retrieve the Dragon Core and escape with it, but was soon confronted by Dorama. The Lava-Tide Leader tossed the Core to another Lava-Tide, only for Kai to intercept and take possession of it. As the Lava-Tide Leader recovered the Core once again, they were met with a forceful assault from Wyldfyre. Unintentionally, the Lava-Tide Leader absorbed the powerful attacks unleashed by Wyldfyre's Heat blasts, resulting in their experiencing a tremendous growth. Despite the surprise, the Lava-Tide Leader falsely claimed that the transformation was intentional.

Kai and Wyldfyre were being chased by the Lava-Tide Leader, who found the situation ironic. However, Dorama and his Imperium soldiers interrupted her pursuit, but they quickly defeated them. The Lava-Tide Leader managed to trap Kai and Wyldfyre, with the intention of eliminating them permanently. Unfortunately for them, Heatwave intervened and caused them to lose the Dragon Core. During the ensuing battle, the Lava-Tide Leader struck Heatwave with a fiery blast, but he retaliated and pushed the leader back, allowing him, Wyldfyre, and Kai to make their escape with the Dragon Core. The Lava-Tides were left defeated and sorrowful over the loss of the Dragon Core, while Wyldfyre taunted them.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Fire Absorption - Like the other lava-Tides they can absorb fire, while removing it from the source, into their body and use it in various ways, gaining some form of advantage, either by enhancing themselves, gaining the drained power, using it as power source etc., either temporarily or permanently.
    • Size Enhancement - Through the absorption of Heat, They possess the power to expand their size significantly, becoming colossal entities. Additionally, they bestow upon their target the ability to enhance their physical capabilities, granting them superhuman strength of extraordinary proportions.
    • Object Propulsion - To propel objects at high speed in a fashion similar to a rocket launch off. They can produce an aura around the object, which causes ions to form around the object and propel it.
    • Lava Ball Projection - They can create spheres of compressed molten rock, and increase the ball of magma in size, intensity of heat, and power capable of incinerating their opponents and decimating large structures.

“You expect me to believe that annoying girl you're with is gonna save the world? Ha! Give me a break.”

Like all Lava-Tides, they have a deep hatred for Wyldfyre messing up their village and the statue of the great Lava-Todd the Wise. Consequently, when Kai and Wyldfyre asked for the core they and their people immediately refused to give the core.
