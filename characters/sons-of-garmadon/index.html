Overview

This article is about the biker gang. For the season, see Season 8: Sons of Garmadon. For the actual son of Garmadon, see Lloyd.
“Sons of Garmadon, no longer shall I be the Quiet One! No longer shall I be silent and no longer shall you, for our father has returned!”
— Harumi's speech to the Sons of Garmadon, "True Potential"

The Sons of Garmadon (abbreviated as S.O.G.) were a militant biker gang pledged with resurrecting the Oni side of Garmadon. Consisting of over 300 members at its peak, the group was originally led by Princess Harumi, a.k.a. "The Quiet One," and sought to conquer Ninjago and resurrect Lord Garmadon, who Harumi viewed as Ninjago's savior due to his killing the Great Devourer. To achieve his rebirth, the group searched for the three Oni Masks and ultimately revived Garmadon at the Temple of Resurrection, though they were arrested when the ceremony seemingly failed.

The reborn Garmadon freed Harumi along with the rest of the group, and seized control of Kryptarium Prison. There, Garmadon fought and nearly killed his son, while his new army planned to return to Ninjago City and showcase their new emperor. As Garmadon's Colossus terrorized Ninjago, it seemingly killed Wu and the original ninja. As a result, Lloyd created a Resistance to oppose Garmadon's rule, as his forces occupied the city.

The Resistance fought the Sons of Garmadon, holding up against them for some time. During the conflict, Garmadon destroyed Mr. E after he failed to capture Lloyd, and Harumi seemingly perished in a building collapse. During a climactic battle against Garmadon and the Colossus, Garmadon lost his powers and the Colossus crumbled apart. As a result, most, if not all of the Sons of Garmadon were arrested and sent back to Kryptarium Prison.

The group was largely disbanded after their defeat, though members such as Killow and Ultra Violet had further run-ins with the Ninja, and fragments of the Sons of Garmadon later formed Lord Mogra's Red Crows.

History

History[]

Prior to Sons of Garmadon[]

Harumi started the organization after the ghost invasion. She would later gain the help of Killow, Mr. E, and Ultra Violet and grow their gang.

Sons of Garmadon[]

The Sons started their hunt for the masks with Mr. E and his crew going to Borg Tower for the Oni Mask of Vengeance, only Mr. E escaped as Lloyd appeared and stopped them. Later, the Sons of Garmadon would launch an attack on the Royal Palace on the Quiet One’s orders to get the Oni Mask of Deception and would succeed. The Sons would kidnap Misako and a baby Wu and the Quiet One, who was Harumi would hide with the ninja to obtain the third Oni mask, the Mask of Hatred and succeeded. The crime group would use the masks to resurrect Garmadon from this moment, they would launch an attack on Ninjago City where they conquered the city and Garmadon became its emperor.

Hunted[]

A week after ruling Ninjago City, the hunt for Lloyd and his allies begin. After Mr. E fails to capture Lloyd, he is given a swift penalty by Garmadon, being killed. Eventually a |resistance was made against the Sons and they made huge broadcast across the City. But the Sons of Garmadon had found the Resistance and arrested majority of their force. Harumi, now as Garmadon’s new daughter hunts for Lloyd but is captured. A plan by Skylor and Mystake forms in order for Skylor to control the Colossus to defeat Garmadon. But the plan is disrupted by Harumi and Mystake dies during the mission but Skylor gets the essence of Destruction and attempts to control it but ends up poisoning herself. While Garmadon is fighting for control, Harumi retreats but is killed in a collapsing building. This angers Garmadon and he goes on a rampage, destroying Ninjago City. As the Stranded ninja return to Ninjago, the ninja reunite and make their finale stand against the Sons of Garmadon, destroying 2/3 of their masks. While Kai, Cole, Zane and Jay fight the Colossus, Wu and Lloyd face off Garmadon, soon only Lloyd as Wu falls of Borg Tower. Lloyd uses the Art of the Silent Fist to defeat Garmadon and Ninjago is freed from the Sons of Garmadon. All of the group including Garmadon, was sent to Kryptarium Prison.

Garmadon[]

After the Oni's invasion during which Garmadon was freed by the ninja to help them fight the Oni, the Sons of Garmadon felt betrayed by their former leader for helping the ninjas and not trying to free them, his followers. They were eventually freed from prison by Lord Mogra, an old enemy of Garmadon, to whom they pledged allegiance, becoming the Red Crows. The biker gang eventually crossed paths again with their former leader in Two Moon Village, who in turn reproached them for their lack of allegiance. Garmadon once again clashed with Mogra, whom he ultimately defeated.

Crystalized[]

During the crystal invasion by the Crystal King, all the key former members of the Sons of Garmadon were involved in the conflict. Harumi was the right arm of the Crystal King, who resurrected her, while Mr. E was replaced by a new model known as Mister F, who took on the role of a general for the crystal army. Regarding Garmadon, he sided with his family to oppose and fight the Overlord, just like Killow and Ultra Violet who joined the the Resistance, opposing Harumi and Mister F. Once the conflict was over, those who had joined the Resistance, including a reformed Harumi, contributed to the rebuilding of the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

Culture

Culture[]

The Sons of Garmadon are a militant biker street gang who are based in Ninjago City. They worship Garmadon in his Oni form as their savior, looking to him like a father. They were led by 'The Quiet One' who was the founder of the gang and based all of the gang's beliefs off of her childhood experience if her parents death, believing that Garmadon is her savior and the Ninja are her enemy. The gang follows her beliefs and work in a militant hierarchy. A high ranking member of the gang named Killow is the one who decides who is in the gang and who is out. New recruits partake in a street race to determine if they are fit. Ultra Violet was a general who managed many of the team's decisions. Mr. E was a nindroid general who led many attacks for the gang.

The gang used Laughy's Karaoke Bar as a hangout before gaining control of Kryptarium Prison and Ninjago City. The gang frequently enjoy drinking boba, biking and karaoke.

Known members

Known members[]

  • Lord Garmadon - The most powerful member and leader of the organization, outranking Harumi. He was resurrected by the Sons of Garmadon at the Temple of Resurrection to defeat the ninja and take over Ninjago. He once reigned as Emperor but was overthrown by his son, Lloyd Garmadon.
  • Harumi - The Jade Princess and the original leader of the Sons of Garmadon before becoming second-in-command to Lord Garmadon after his resurrection. She is commonly referred to as the "Quiet One." For years she kept her true identity as the leader of the Sons a secret. She vowed revenge on the ninja for failing to stop the Great Devourer, which killed her parents. She was killed when the building she was standing on was toppled by the Colossus, only to have been resurrected by the Overlord. She possessed the Oni Mask of Hatred.
  • Killow - The first general, a behemoth with pale skin who wields a giant spiky club. He was in charge of recruitment into the Sons. He also possessed the Oni Mask of Deception.
  • Mr. E - The mysterious second general who spoke only once, and when he did, he did it in reverse. He was a Nindroid. He was ripped apart by Garmadon after failing to capture Lloyd as a warning to the others. He possessed the Oni Mask of Vengeance. (Destroyed)
  • Ultra Violet - The third general. She has pale skin and a long ponytail. She is known to be crazy and act out serious stunts. She hosted Ultra Hunt. She also possessed the Oni Mask of Hatred.
  • Luke Cunningham - An avid biker, also known as "The White Mask." Cole calls him "The Man Behind The White Mask." (incarcerated)
  • Chopper Maroon - A low-ranking biker, also known as "The Maroon Mask." (incarcerated)
  • Mohawk - A low-ranking biker. He is friends with Chopper Maroon. (incarcerated; formerly)
  • Skip Vicious - The member that got picked up by Killow in "The Quiet One." (incarcerated)
  • Nails - The member that looks like Chopper Maroon. He has fewer stars on his head and has shorter legs. (incarcerated)
  • Sawyer (non-canon) - The only member who is never seen riding a bike. He calls his chainsaw "Betsy."
  • Scooter - A low-ranking biker. He participated in Luke Cunningham's attack in the ninja in the Primeval's Eye in Brawl of the Jungle!.
  • Buffer - A low-ranking biker.
  • Snake Jaguar - An alias of the Ice Ninja when he attempted to sneak into the Sons. (undercover)
  • Colossus - A massive being created by Garmadon with the use of his True Potential. (destroyed)
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The suits that the Sons of Garmadon team wore during their heist in "The Mask of Deception" have the same matching colors of the six ninja. These outfits also use torso design elements from Zane's ZX design and leg design elements from Skylor's 2016 design.
    • Half of these masked members' identities have been revealed. The one in the green mask was revealed to be Mr. E shortly after the group was confronted by Lloyd, Luke Cunningham was revealed to be the Man in the White Mask in "The Oni and the Dragon," and Chopper Maroon was revealed to be "the Maroon Mask" in the Visual Dictionary New Edition. The thieves in the black, blue, and light blue masks have not been revealed.
  • They refer to Lord Garmadon as their "father."
    • Additionally, Ultra Violet said, "Hello, mama." to Misako in "The Gilded Path".
  • After Garmadon successfully took over Ninjago City, the Sons acted as his police force. This force was abolished after Garmadon was overthrown.
  • Mr. E is the first general to be killed, as well as the first Sons of Garmadon member to die onscreen. Harumi is the second member to die, however she was later resurrected by the Overlord.
  • They are the second villainous faction to remain the antagonists of more than one season, the first being the Serpentine.
  • They are the second cult to appear in the series, the first being the Anacondrai Cultists.
  • They are the fourth villainous faction to consist of mainly humans in the franchise, the first three being the Anacondrai Cultists, the Sky Pirates, and the Shark Army.
  • Most members, except from Lord Garmadon and Mr.E, have served 2 villainous factions: the Sons of Garmadon and the Red Crows, with the exception of the Quiet One serving the Overlord.
  • They are the second villainous faction to be entirely or almost entirely incarcerated in Kryptarium Prison, the first being the Sky Pirates after the Dark Island Trilogy.
  • Despite using red katanas in the sets, they only used normal silver katanas and knives in the show, except Mr. E.
  • According to Garmadon Rulez!, Killow was the first recruit.
  • The generic biker (Scooter) from 30531 Sons of Garmadon has the same head piece as Okino, which also resembles Karlof's.
  • Luke Cunningham and Skip Vicious have nearly identical minifigures, with the only difference being the visors on their helmets.
  • The most common hair pieces include the Mayor McCaskill's hair from The LEGO Batman Movie, the Genie Girl's from LEGO Minifigures, and the Hunter Orc's from LEGO The Hobbit.
  • According to Tommy Andreasen one early concept for the group would've been for them to be undercover dragons whose true form could only be seen through a visor, while the gang's iconography would've been dragon themed. This was inspired by the John Carpenter film They Live.[1]
