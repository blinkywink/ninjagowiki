Overview

“Release the Dragonides!”
— The Overlord, "Brave But Foolish"

Dragonides (also known as Crystal beasts[1][2][3]) were stone dragonic beast sculptures animated by the infectious crystals[4], that were part of the crystal army.

History

History[]

Return to Primeval's Eye[]

The Council of the Crystal King rode the Dragonides to search for Harumi and later attack the ninja.

Crystastrophe[]

When Zane tried to launch an assault on the Crystal Island using his Golden Dragon Jet, he was attacked by three Dragonides.

Christofern[]

On the NGTV news report, Dragonides could be seen entering Ninjago City.

Later, after Christofern was seemingly destroyed, Garmadon attacked a Dragonide, sending it crashing into the street.

Brave But Foolish[]

As a way to stop the ninja from stopping his plans, the Overlord summoned the Dragonides. The Dragonides succeeded by attacking the Bounty and destroying the ninja's vehicles.

Quittin' Time![]

A Dragonide chased after Wu, but the latter escaped by ripping off the wings of the Dragon Chestplate.

Return of the Ice Emperor[]

A pair of Dragonides flew past Cyrus Borg's office in Borg Tower after P.I.X.A.L. opened his window shutters.

Safe Haven[]

A Dragonide ridden by Harumi flew through the skies of Ninjago City, searching for the ninja.

Distress Calls[]

Cole, Vinny Folson, and Blazey H. Speed tried to send a message to Vania to request the aid of the Army of Shintaro, but Dragonides attacked, destroying the broadcast tower.

An Issue of Trust[]

The Crystal Council rode the Dragonides to launch their assault on the Resistance.

Dragon Form[]

As the final battle began, the Dragonides partook in the Crystal army's attack on the Resistance. In the early moments of the battle, Pythor's Dragonide managed to crystallize Ultra Violet while the Mechanic's Dragonide crystallized Ronin. When victory seemed assured for the Crystal army, the ninja managed to turn the tide by achieving Dragon Form. As the ninja now began their attack, the Council of the Crystal King fled atop their Dragonides.

Mister F chased after P.I.X.A.L. on his Dragonide, but was intercepted by Zane. Elsewhere, Vangelis's Dragonide pinned Cole to the ground, allowing Vangelis to nearly defeat him before Adam and Plundar intervened. After the ninja purified the Weapons of Destruction, the remaining Dragonides were weakened and were subsequently destroyed. Of these, at least two were destroyed by P.I.X.A.L. and at least one was run over by Blazey H. Speed.
