Fedulian is a Merlopian that owns an antiques shop in the Crossroads.

History

History[]

Background[]

Fedulian relocated to the Crossroads and established a store named All-Realm Antiques Emporium at some point after the Merge. He was known to being kind of sketchy. He then meets Hantro, who supplies his antiques shop with random items, one of them being the Mallet of Shattering.[1]

Following the Liberation of Imperium, Fedulian became acquainted with his old friend Dorama. Fedulian engaged in selling counterfeit maps to algae farmers and the residents of the Crossroads, who were later captured by Dorama and forced to participate in Dorama's Academy for Acting.[2]

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Shattered Dreams[]

When the Mallet of Shattering was stolen by Cinder the night before, Lloyd, Zane, Arin, and Sora question Fedulian, who claims to know nothing before they leave. Once they do so, Fedulian contacts Hantro to warn her about the ninja, unaware that Lloyd, Zane, Arin, and Sora were eavesdropping on the conversation.

To Mysterium[]

Upon arriving at All-Realm Antiques Emporium, Cole and Bonzle were met with Zane's distrust towards Fedulian. However, despite Zane's reservations, Cole and Bonzle decided to investigate Fedulian's trustworthiness. Just as they were about to do so, Fedulian approached them and inquired about Zane's identity, considering he met him when the ninja questioned him about the Mallet of Shattering. After a brief exchange, Fedulian suggested they check out an authentic toothbrush of the ancients or a party toaster. Nevertheless, Cole, Zane, and Bonzle declined the offer and wanted a map of the Merged Lands to reach Mysterium. Fedulian regurgitated a fake map and hands it to them before they leave. As they did, Fedulian labeled them as fools, saying that they should've bought the toaster when they had the chance. Fedulian proceeded to notify Dorama that they would be arriving at Dorama's Academy for Acting. Additionally, Fedulian mentioned that Dorama would have the opportunity to seek vengeance on the ninja as an added benefit.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Prior The Great Zane Robbery[]

Fedulian came in contact with Jay, who became a bounty hunter under the name Rogue. They had several dealings where Jay often showed up at his shop during bad hours and made a lot of holes in his door with a golden shuriken.

Fedulian later came in contact with Zeatrix who needed help for her revenge plan against Ras.

The Great Zane Robbery[]

While heading back to his shop he’s sees a golden shuriken which leads to meet back with the mysterious mercenary known as Rogue who he pays him for the rare Oni chalice from the First Land and later Fedulian receives his reward in return but as Rogue is about to leave he gives the bounty hunter a letter from someone.

The Shatter Dragon[]

Fedulian attended a party at the Monastery of Spinjitzu to celebrate the Ninja saving the Merged Realms.
