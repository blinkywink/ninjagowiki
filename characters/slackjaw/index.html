Overview

Slackjaw was a Vermillion warrior in Krux and Acronix's army.

History

History[]

A Time of Traitors[]

Tannin and Slackjaw bring in a captured Cyrus Borg and locks him in a sarcophagus.

Later, they attacked the city, only to be encountered by Jay and Cole.

Scavengers[]

Slackjaw stole Dareth's trophies, and later went to the Samurai X Cave to take apart Zane. Afterwards, the Vermillion Army traveled in underground tunnels to their new headquarters.

He later helped attack the ninja. They also attacked the power source to cause a blackout.

A Line in the Sand[]

The Vermillion steal metal from the amusement park and junkyard. They left for the Sea of Sand to retrieve the Slow-Mo Time Blade, but kidnapped Edna, thinking she may be a builder.

They succeeded in retrieving the Slow-Mo Time Blade, but Jay came to rescue the ninja and his mom and the commanders slowed them down.

Once freed, the ninja used the Time Blade to slow the Vermillion down, thus getting away.

The Attack[]

The Vermillion arrived at the temple and attacked the ninja. They were able to steal the Slow-Mo Time Blade.

Secrets Discovered[]

Slackjaw brought Wu to their dome.

Pause and Effect[]

Slackjaw was on the lookout for the ninja but didn't realize Jay and Cole passed them. Later, they heard a loud sound and went in to check. They were able to outnumber Jay and Cole but when Lloyd and Zane helped, the Vermillion were defeated.

Out of the Fire and Into the Boiling Sea[]

The Vermillion chased the ninja and regrouped at the dome when they left. Later, when the ninja were granted access to the Iron Doom, Jay talked and blew their cover. The Vermillion were instructed to attack them, but they were ran over by the Samurai VXL. They continued to chase the ninja to the Iron Doom. When Machia told the Vermillion to go to the Iron Doom, they stopped fighting.

Lost in Time[]

Machia prepared the Vermillion to fight the past Elemental Masters. When they are battling, the snakes are destroyed and fused into the Iron Doom.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Slackjaw's name bears a very close resemblance to that of Frakjaw. Both are common, low-rank minions in a villainous faction with red as their signature color.
  • He is the most common Vermillion figure in the sets.
  • Orange variants of Slackjaw appear in the show despite not appearing in the sets.
