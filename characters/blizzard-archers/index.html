Overview

“Some of them are frozen warriors, others are just ice!”
— Cole, "Fire Maker"

The Blizzard Archers were members of the Blizzard Samurai. They were foot-soldiers made of snow and ice.

History

History[]

Background[]

Several decades ago, Zane started his reign by creating Blizzard Archers and Sword Masters. He also corrupted Grimfax's army, turning them into Blizzard Warriors and took over the Never-Realm.

The Never-Realm[]

The Blizzard Archers were among the Blizzard Samurai sent by Vex to destroy the ninja and Ice Fishers.

Fire Maker[]

The Blizzard Archers attacked Great Lake with legions of Blizzard Warriors and Blizzard Sword Masters.

An Unlikely Ally[]

Several of them attacked Lloyd and almost froze him. However, he screamed, causing an avalanche to stop them.

Secret of the Wolf[]

Two Blizzard Archers watch Grimfax nearly being punished by the Ice Emperor.

Awakenings[]

The Blizzard Archers, along with the Blizzard Sword Masters, were destroyed when Zane destroyed his scepter.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Like the Blizzard Warriors, the head of their minifigure has a two-sided face with one side having both eyes and only having one eye in the other.
    • However, in the series, the Blizzard Archers' faces are blue and have the one-eyed sided face while the Blizzard Warriors have the two-eyed sided face.
  • This is the second minifigure to have a Samurai X-style helmet without anything on the clip, the first being Karlof. However, the clip is shown in the series, unlike Karlof's.
