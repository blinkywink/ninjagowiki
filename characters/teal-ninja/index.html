Overview

“We don't have any reason to arrest you three yet. But I warn you, we're watching you. And if you give us any reason to even think you're following the ninja's example, we'll be back. And you will face, the New Ninja!”
— The Teal Ninja, to Wu, P.I.X.A.L. and Skylor, "A Painful Promise"

The Teal Ninja is the former leader of the New Ninja.

History

History[]

Farewell the Sea[]

The Teal Ninja helped take out Miss Demeanor's henchmen and introduce the team to her.

The Shape of Nya[]

He and his team saved the Ninja from Miss Deamenor and her henchmen

Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5![]

The Teal Ninja was among the other New Ninja to ambush the disguised Kai, Jay, Cole and Lloyd, when they attempt to steal Aspheera's staff. He then ends up disabling Zane's jet, resulting in him crashing into many buildings.

Managing to grab Lloyd, the Teal Ninja hears Kai call out to Zane by his real name before unmasking Lloyd, surprising the New Ninja before they manage to escape with the staff. While the New Ninja tended to the drivers, the Teal Ninja informed the mayor about the old ninja having been the ones responsible before the mayor demands their capture, proclaiming them public enemies.

A Painful Promise[]

The Teal Ninja and the other New Ninja arrived at the Monastery of Spinjitzu via a police helicopter, then took an elevator down to the hangar bay, which Kai had forgotten to secure. Upon their arrival, the Teal Ninja announced themselves with the team's elaborate catchphrase, only to be criticized by Lloyd. Despite being confused about the situation, the Teal Ninja accused the original ninja of breaking the law, breaking his own sword to emphasize his point and claiming the New Ninja would "unbreak" it. However, the sword remained broken, prompting the Orange Ninja to lend him one of her swords. He then commanded the New Ninja to attack, leading to a battle between the two teams while Aspheera drained Nya's elemental powers.

Aspheera ultimately succeeded, causing a massive surge of energy that overwhelmed the New Ninja. Seizing the moment, Lloyd showed Aspheera a secret exit from the monastery, allowing her to escape. Afterward, the New Ninja arrested the original ninja and contacted the Ninjago Police, promising to find Aspheera afterward. As the original ninja were taken away, the Teal Ninja stated he had no immediate reason to arrest Wu, Skylor, or P.I.X.A.L., but warned them that any suspicious actions would lead to their arrest. He then attempted a dramatic exit using a smoke bomb.

A Lesson in Anger[]

The Teal Ninja and other New Ninja come across some Crystal Warriors not far from City Hall and attempt to fight them, only to be easily defeated. After being disarmed, the Teal Ninja noticed his fellow teammates being turned into crystal zombies before ending up like one himself.

Compatible[]

The Teal Ninja was among the many infected citizens attracted to the sound of Blazey H. Speed's blaster shot hitting a car.

Roots[]

Not long after being purified by the Overlord's defeat, the Teal Ninja was among the many citizens that decided to help the ninja rebuild the monastery. During which, he is held up in the air by the other New Ninja While placing a lantern on the edge of the roof.

Relationships

Relationships[]

Ulysses Trustable[]

Unlike the rest of his team, the Teal Ninja seemed to have access to direct communications with Mayor Ulysses Trustable. Aside from this privilege, the Teal Ninja did not have any more of a notable relationship with the mayor than his comrades.

Ninja[]

Like all the other members of the New Ninja, the Teal Ninja first admired the ninja and was inspired by them to become a defender of Ninjago City. Despite this, he still considered the ninja to be low-leveled compared to himself and the others, calling them weak and out of shape. When the ninja freed Aspheera and caused damage to Ninjago City, the Teal Ninja began to see them as criminals and reported them to the Ninjago Police. In "Roots," he and the other New Ninja are seen helping rebuild the Monastery, which could imply that they no longer have a grudge against the original ninja.
