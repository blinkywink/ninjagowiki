Overview

“Uh, you don't actually pay me, Professor Powers. I'm an intern.”
— Dwayne to Clutch Powers, "Uncharted"

Dwayne is Ulysses Trustable's former assistant who formerly worked as one of Clutch Powers' interns.

History

History[]

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Boobytraps and How to Survive Them[]

Dwayne hands over the phone to Clutch, claiming that it was a call from the Explorer's Club.

The Island[]

Uncharted[]

Dwayne was among Misako and Clutch's group when they explore the Island of the Keepers. When Clutch complains about his arm being sore from chopping vines, he gives his machete to Dwayne, claiming it is knowledge. They then get attacked by the Stone Guardians and was captured by them.

The Gift of Jay[]

Dwayne was in the Keepers' prison along with Misako's Explorer group and Kai, Zane, Cole and Nya. Later, he tried to help Clutch steal the Storm Amulet before being electrocuted and getting placed on poles.

The Tooth of Wojira[]

After escaping some poles, he cut through the ropes that held Clutch. Later on, when Nya asked where Clutch was, Dwayne pointed at the jungle.

Seabound[]

The Turn of the Tide[]

Dwayne was at Nya's memorial.

Prior to Crystalized[]

At some point in the year after Nya's departure, Dwayne left the Explorer's Club and became assistant to recently elected mayor Ulysses Trustable.

Crystalized[]

A Mayor Problem[]

Dwayne, who unintentionally interrupts Ulysses' golfing time, informs him of Kai, Jay, Cole, and Lloyd's arrival.

Public Enemies 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5![]

Dwayne adjusts a portrait of Ulysses and Mr. Fussly Fussly as they watch him. He was soon attacked by Mr. Fussly Fussly when he was about to answer the phone. Later on, Dwayne told Ulysses about one of the trucks from Borg Tower is 'airborne,' which Ulysses disbelieves, until said truck crashes into the building, and ends up attacked by Mr. Fussly Fussly again. He then went to rearrange the portrait with Mr. Fussly Fussly supervising him.

Hounddog McBrag[]

Dwayne, who unintentionally interrupts Ulysses' golfing time again, informs him of the Ninja's escape from Kryptarium Prison. Later, after the Commissioner refused to comply with Ulysses' order to arrest the Ninja due to jurisdiction, Dwayne, who had Mr. Fussly Fussly on his head, who cover his eyes with his tail, was told to bring in Hounddog McBrag.

A Lesson in Anger[]

During the press conference, Ulysses tells Dwayne to get his car ready before learning they're live. After the New Ninja failed to defeat the Crystal Warriors and became zombies, Ulysses demands the public run for their lives, with Dwayne passing by, Mr. Fussly Fussly on his head again.

Appearance

Appearance[]

  • Intern
  • Mayor's assistant
1/2

Dwayne has brown hair swept to the left and wears glasses. As an intern in Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu, The Island, and Seabound, Dwayne wore a light brown shirt, brown pants, and a blue ascot. As Mayor Trustable's assistant in Crystalized, Dwayne retains the glasses and wears a charcoal blazer, a light gray shirt, black pants, and a white, light purple and dark green striped tie.

Personality

Personality[]

Dwayne can be extremely nervous when it comes to adventures, phone calls, informing important figures and tempers being raised. He is also completely honest as he revealed Clutch's current whereabouts to Nya without question and told Ulysses about the Ninja's escape from Kryptarium Prison. He also has great reflexes.

Abilities[]
