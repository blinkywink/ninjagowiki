Overview

Ellie[1] is a competitor of the Tournament of the Sources, alongside the other Elemental Masters of her generation.

History

History[]

At some point in time, Ellie was granted an Elemental Power.

They Gather for the Feast[]

Ellie is chatting with other Elemental Masters in Foundation Street the day of the welcome feast. She later attends the feast and turns around to focus on Roby when he was about to begin his speech.

Later, Ellie arrives in a hurry after Lloyd defeats the attacker bot.

Inside the Maze[]

Ellie made her way through Foundation Street, accompanied by a handful of Elemental Masters spectators. Nya, who was in search of Jay, hurriedly pushed past them. Subsequently, Ellie passed by Lloyd and Nya, who were engaged in a conversation about Jay.

Later, Ellie was passing down the street when Geo and Cole were going to Temple City Museum.

Later, Ellie was seen walking past Sora, who was confronting Jordana about what she said at the banquet.

United We Fall[]

After Roby's phrase about "raining elemental powers", an audience member sitting in the stands put on an umbrella hat, to which Ellie made a facepalm.

Truth and Lies[]

Ellie was sitting on the stands during the battles between Euphrasia and Zeatrix and later between Mr. Pale and Lloyd.

The Sword Shatters[]

During the intermission during Lloyd and Zeatrix's match, Ellie and Henjamin were seen walking past the ninja and carrying snowberry pies.

The Final Game[]

Ellie walks by Wyldfyre after she steals Bleckt's ledger and gets confused by its writings.

Later, she watched in surprise when Wyldfyre arrived with Zur to expose Bleckt after Nokt defeated Sora.

Elements of Betrayal[]

Ellie fought alongside several other Elemental Masters against Cinder and the Wolf Mask Warriors in hand-to-hand combat. She later got her powers back after Roby drained Nokt of the stolen Elemental Powers and returned them to their rightful masters. Afterwards, she is later cheering in the crowd for the ninja during the victory ceremony.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • While Ellie is never seen fighting within the Tournament of the Sources, her element can be seen returning to her in "Elements of Betrayal."
    • Her Elemental Power is not Metal.[2]
  • She has a Ninjargon letter G on her helmet. It is unknown what it stands for.
  • Between herself, Treg, and Henjamin, the three Elemental Masters whose elements are currently unknown, she is the only one seen fighting against Cinder and the Wolf Mask Warriors.
  • In "In the Pits," the bandits don outfits that closely resemble Ellie's, along with having identical goggles. Their attire also shares similarities with that of the Blizzard Warriors, albeit in a different color scheme, and they sport helmets that align with the Blizzard Warriors'. It's unknown whether the similarities between the warriors and raiders are coincidental or if it suggests that Ellie might be from the Never Kingdoms.
