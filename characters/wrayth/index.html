Overview

“You think a train can stop a ghost? Your world will pay dearly when Morro finds the tomb.”
— Wrayth to the ninja, "Ghost Story"

Chain Master Wrayth was a chain master fond of turning others into ghosts. He was a spirit that was at one point banished to the Cursed Realm, and was imprisoned there for a time. Upon Morro's return to Ninjago, he was freed from the Cursed Realm using the Allied Armor of Azure to battle the ninja.

Riding a massive Chain Cycle, Wrayth confronted Cole, Kai, Jay, and Zane at a train station, where he proved himself to be a formidable adversary. However, when a water tower collapsed and its contents spilled onto him, Wrayth was dissolved and banished to the Cursed Realm, where he stayed for a while. After staying imprisoned for several days, Wrayth was freed once more by Morro, this time through the use of the Realm Crystal. Upon his arrival in Stiix, he began wreaking havoc on the town and its citizens, and ensured Morro that he had become invincible.

Soon enough, the ninja snuck into the town and began battling the ghosts. When The Preeminent was freed, Wrayth helped to guide the creature as it strove to destroy the steamboat evacuating the citizens of Stiix. Yet when Nya managed to unlock her True Potential, she created a tidal wave that drowned The Preeminent and destroyed the Cursed Realm and all its inhabitants, including Wrayth.

History

History[]

Wrayth was, at one point in time, banished to the Cursed Realm.

Ghost Story[]

Wrayth was freed from the Cursed Realm by Morro using the Allied Armor of Azure with orders to stop the ninja while he sought the Airjitzu scrolls. Wrayth turned a regular cycle into the Chain Cycle and rode off into the desert.

Wrayth eventually came across the ninja at a train station and attacked them relentlessly. While fighting the ninja, Wrayth turns one of the Ninja's walloper into a ghost, forcing the Ninja to se them free. Cole catches Wrayth's flail to a nearby water tower, which forces Wrayth to crash his bike. As he gets up a train to Stiix hits Wrayth, with the Ninja thinks he is dead but since he is a ghost, it passes right through and surprises them. He laughs at their attempts and says when Morro gets to the First Spinjitzu Master's tomb he will get the Realm Crystal. Unfortunately, his chain caught on a nearby water tower and toppled it on himself. The large amount of water from it caused Wrayth to dissolve and be sent back to the Cursed Realm, before he can confirm what was in the tomb.

Curseworld, Part I[]

As Morro began using the Realm Crystal to unleash The Preeminent, Wrayth was able to return to Ninjago, remarking that "it's good to be back." Wrayth then proceed to help the other ghosts take over Stiix. Later as Soul Archer reported to Morro that the gateway became more stable for the Preeminent to crossover, Wrayth and Ghoultar were with Morro above Ronin's pawn shop where Wrayth reassured Morro that they have two spooks for every nook and cranny in Stiix. And if the ninja do show and strike any one of them down, the other ghosts can simply return through the portal.

Curseworld, Part II[]

When Morro orders the other ghosts to protect the realm, Wrayth helped directed several buildings onto the tentacles. He later help the Preeminent by lifting her tentacles to move around in her new armor. He later caught Zane with his chain, but Jay manage to destroy it before he gets to turn him into a ghost. As a result of Jay's Lightning blast, Wrayth was zapped by Jay before he fell into the sea. Although he was briefly revived by the Preeminent, he was presumably killed following the Preeminent's death.

Personality

Personality[]

Wrayth is shown to be loyal and obedient to Morro. He has an admiration for Morro's dragon and vehicles; which lead him to take a motorbike. In his fight with the Ninja, Wrayth showed being thrill-streak, as he constantly attack the Ninja. When the Ninja though he died from the train, Wrayth laughs at their attempts. However Wrayth failed to notice his flail on the water tower which lead him to being sent back to the Cursed Realm.

After coming back from the Cursed Realm, Wrayth showed no resentment that Morro didn't bring him back to reclaim the Realm Crystal.

Appearance

Appearance[]

He had a green ghost face with a blue mask and a purple band, a shirt all destroyed with chains with black arms, and transparent green legs with gray belt.

Abilities and Weapons

Abilities and Weapons[]

Abilities[]

  • Spectral Magic: Being a ghost, Wrayth could turn others into ghost, through the use of his chained flail. He was also able to transform a motorbike into a Chain Cycle, and also gave it more abilities like it could fly and travel through the ground.
  • Possession: Wrayth was capable of possessing vehicles, like a regular motorbike and turned it into a Chain Cycle.
  • Intangibility: As a ghost, Wrayth could pass through solid objects.
  • Invisibility: As a ghost, Wrayth could also turn invisible.
  • Flight/Levitation: Since he was a ghost, Wrayth could float and fly, as he also lacked legs.

Weapons[]

  • Chained Whip/Flail: Wrayth wielded a flail, a chained whip with a long handle, in combat. He used this to catch an opponent, and if the victim did not escape the chain in time, they will turn into a ghost.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Water: Like all other ghosts, Wrayth was weak against water.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In the sets he's shown to know Airjitzu, but in the series he doesn't.
  • He is the only Ghost Master to not appear in the Final Flight of the Destiny's Bounty set.
  • He is the only Ghost Warrior other than Morro to have an Airjitzu flyer.
  • He is the only Ghost Master to not be seen with legs once during the season.
    • However he does have legs in the sets.
  • Wrayth is seemingly "destroyed" in "Ghost Story" by a collapsing water tower, which is interesting considering he appears in three sets and also has his own Airjitzu flyer, making him the most recurring ghost minifigure, even though he is absent for the majority of the season.
    • Due to the ghosts' ability to regenerate through the Cursed Realm, Wrayth returns near the end of the season in "Curseworld, Part I."
  • Wrayth was the first Ghost Master and Ghost Warrior to be summoned by Morro.
    • He is also the first Ghost Master to be destroyed, by being doused in water.
  • He is able to turn other creatures into ghost, an ability he shares with Soul Archer. He also has the same mask piece as Soul Archer, causing many fans to confuse them.
  • Wrayth seems to be the ghost equivalent of Jay, as both wield weapons that include chains in their designs as their prominent weapon and have an affinity for vehicles.
    • He also has the same voice actor as Jay, Michael Adamthwaite.
  • His name is misspelled as "Raythe" in the credits of the two Curseworld episodes.
  • His name is a reference to "Wraith," a synonym for ghost, and possibly the word "wrath."
  • His head resembles a mummy.
    • Thus, he is the only Ghost Master to not have a transparent head.
    • Wrayth is the first Ninjago villain to resemble a mummy, the second is Kur.
  • He could be a reference to Ghost Rider, as he uses a chain and fights briefly on a motorcycle.
  • He is similar to Kai from the Dreamworks movie Kung Fu Panda 3 (not to be confused with the Fire Ninja with the same name), as both are from another realm (Kai from the Spirit Realm, Wrayth from the Cursed Realm), both are ghosts while Kai is a Spirit Warrior, wield green-bladed chains, and can transform others.
    • Kung Fu Panda 3 came out in 2016 a year after the Possession season came out in 2015.
