Overview

“I put my heart, my soul into every spell I weave. And even though spells usually fade away after use, I always hoped you would be different— the most complex spell I've ever woven, and the first of my creations to ever come back to me!”
— Gandalaria to Bonzle, "Fugitives From Madness"

Gandalaria (nicknamed Gandy by Powerlid) is the high sorceress of Mysterium. She created Bonzle to enlist the Dragon Masters in their quest to defeat the Forbidden Five.

History

History[]

Background[]

Gandalaria rose to become the high sorceress of her homeland. She often engaged in minor disputes with her neighbor Janet, another sorceress,[1] and occasionally journeyed across different realms. One day, the Dragon Masters sought Gandalaria's assistance, seeking a spell to exile the Forbidden Five to the Nether-Space. Devoting day and night to its creation, Gandalaria finally completed the spell. At some point long ago, Gandalaria would begin using a crystal ball that she would hold sentimentally after it saved her life when the Carnus Conclave fell. At some point, Gandalaria would design a backup copy in the form of a coconut in case Janet ever managed to wipe her mind. She also became best friends with the cyclops Powerlid until an incident in which the latter organized a gem hunt without the former's consent, who was preoccupied with vocal practice, leading to a divide in their goals and the two of them drifting apart. She was familiar with The Administration, but was also the owner of numerous items which did not recieve its approval. Centuries later, following the Merge, Gandalaria relocated to the Monastery of Mysterium.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Fugitives From Madness[]

Sprite alerted Gandalaria to the arrival of the ninja and Bonzle. Upon seeing them, Gandalaria mistakenly identified the three as the Slug Merchants. Feeling relieved, she declared that their arrival would bring about a significant change. Gandalaria then proceeded to explain that she required 20 striped slugs, five red betties, two extra slimly slugs, and some slugs with wings. However, Cole and Zane clarified that they were not the Slug Merchants Gandalaria had been eagerly anticipating for weeks, much to her disappointment. Suddenly, an idea struck her, and she asked the three if she could transform them into slugs. However, as Gandalaria noticed Bonzle, she immediately recognized her as the same spell that banished the Forbidden Five and warmly embraced her.

Cole conveyed to Gandalaria the importance of protecting Bonzle from Ras. Initially, Gandalaria was perplexed by the unfamiliar names, Bonzle and Ras. However, Bonzle clarified that she had chosen the name for herself. Gandalaria found the name "Bonzle" to be quite delightful. Zane proceeded to explain that Ras had intentions of using Bonzle as a means to unleash the Forbidden Five. Concerned, Gandalaria reassured them that there was no need to worry, as the Ritual of the Blood Moon was necessary to accomplish the feat, and no one possessed the knowledge of how to perform it. Unfortunately, Cole and Zane revealed that Ras had managed to uncover the intricate details of the ritual during his time at the Cloud Kingdom archives. They emphasized that it was only a matter of time before the Blood Moon would reappear, signaling the potential release of the Forbidden Five. Understanding the gravity of the situation, Gandalaria elucidated that if the Forbidden Five were to be set free, their reign would result in the annihilation of all realms. She emphasized the urgency of preventing their liberation.

Gandalaria clarified that she currently lacks the necessary equipment to protect Bonzle, but she still possessed a few items stored in a magical storagehouse in the Wyldness. Cole and Zane proposed traveling to the storehouse on Jiro, a dragon, which delighted Gandalaria. Suddenly, there was a knock on the door, and Gandalaria hoped it was the Slug Merchants, but it turned out to be the Administration Agents who had come for Zane. Observing this, Gandalaria remarked that the agents wouldn't make good slugs. After the Blood Moon appeared, Zane managed to delay the Administration Agents by exploiting their own rules. Gandalaria scolded Zane for attracting the agents to her, as they wouldn't approve of her magical possessions, which she had to discard from Hatty. Gandalaria insisted on using an escape spell, but it would require two to three days. She instructed Bonzle to seek refuge in the passages beneath the Monastery while Sprite guided her. In the meantime, Gandalaria would deal with the agents using her crystal ball. Suddenly, the Administration Agents stormed into the monastery. Gandalaria, wielding her crystal ball, electrocuted the Agents during the intense battle.

The Forest of Spirits[]

Gandalaria, Cole, and Zane remain trapped in the magical storagehouse inside a force field set by Jordana. After being freed due to Zane disabling the force field, they search for a way to exit the storagehouse. However, one was revealed to be an aggressive Fang Mouth, another one had the darkness of the tunnel made solid, and Gandalaria opened a door that led to a series of progressively smaller doors, until it reached a small one that opened to reveal Lava-Tides dancing around a party toaster, which she found to be amusing. They eventually resolved to find a potion to open the door. She tries to find the correct potion in a box full of items but finds useless ones that have adverse effects on Cole and Zane. Eventually, she finds the right potion that can instantly open any lock, but slips, with the potion shattering on a sarcophagus opening to reveal a Geckle mummy. Luckily, Zane and Cole find a way to open the door by utilizing Zane's changing size, an effect lasting from one of the potions Gandalaria threw at them. After escaping with them and spotting the off-balanced Cloud Kingdom, Gandalaria used Hatty to fly upwards to it.

For Whom the Bell Tolls[]

Gandalaria's body was seen at the Monastery of Mysterium, where she was walking into walls when Nya appeared from the portal gate. Nya encountered her backup body a Coconut copy. But it possessed an armor to fight Nya. After losing the battle, Nya was shocked to see her opponent was a coconut, much to the coconut's confusion and anger. The coconut then told Nya its origins and helped her to get to the Cave of Bells in Mysterium.

Chaos Reigns[]

After Thunderfang was defeated by the Arc Dragon of Focus, Gandalaria's soul returned to her body. At first she was puzzles how she got back to the Monastery and assumed Janet. Later Jay, Kai, and Nya returned her Coconut copy to her.

Weapons and abilities

Weapons and abilities[]

Abilities[]

  • Magic: Gandalaria has perfect mastery in the art of magic, being considered a legendary sorceress.
    • Telekinesis: Gandalaria is able to lift someone or something in the air.
    • Spellcasting: Gandalaria can cast powerful spells with varying results.
    • Spell Creation: Gandalaria's greatest power is the ability to create and invent spells, being her most famous and distinctive ability.
    • Object Creation: Gandalaria is also able to create objects like the Protective container
    • Energy Projection: With her magical sphere, Gandalaria can fire powerful beams of destructive magical light.
  • Longevity: Gandalaria is older than she looks, being thousands of years old. She maintains her youthful appearance through magic.

Weapons[]

  • Staff: In the past, Gandalaria had a magic staff.
  • Hatty: Gandalaria's hat is able to store various items.
  • Crystal ball: Gandalaria has a crystal ball that allows her to fire different kinds of projectiles.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Due to the complexity of her real name, Gandalaria is often addressed as "Sorceress" by the other characters in the show.[3]
  • Her name is similar to that of Gandalf, a wizard character from The Lord of the Rings.
  • Although several characters wear glasses, she, Dr. LaRow, Dr. Julien and the Administration Agents (Humans and Munce) are the only ones whose glasses are three-dimensional.
  • One of the medallions on her necklace is reminiscent of the Matoran Alphabet from Bionicle. However, it does not resemble any particular letter.
  • Prior to and during the events of "Fugitives From Madness," she hadn't ridden on a dragon in over a century.
  • Gandalaria has red stripes on her face, similar to the Formlings, Zaiden Kane and the Children of the Wolf.
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, the reason that Gandalaria has a long lifespan is because of her magic.[4]
