Overview

“I'm sure Nimbus will love his new playthings.”
— Fenwick, "Kingdom Come"

Nimbus is a strange hound-like creature that resides in the Cloud Kingdom.

History

History[]

At some point, Nimbus was placed in a house with various toys and games, which he became incredibly fond, as well as territorial of.

Kingdom Come[]

The head writer Fenwick attempted to trap the ninja with Nimbus as part of a bargain he made with Morro and the other Ghost Warriors. However, Cole's recent transformation into a ghost enabled him to free himself and his friends, but Nimbus escaped.

He later found the ninja in a flying skiff and attempted to reclaim them as his playthings but was set upon Fenwick thanks to the efforts of Nobu.

The Splinter in the Blind Man's Eye[]

Nimbus chased Fenwick through Cloud Kingdom. He caught up to Fenwick, ripping half of his face off, although both of them fell to Ninjago. Fenwick later used Nimbus' scroll to use him as the executioner and put him in a pit.

Fenwick later had Yana thrown in, who Nimbus killed, and Clancee, who was almost killed. Fenwick stopped Nimbus, however, after Lloyd agreed to his demands.

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • Nimbus' name came from a type of cloud.
    • This makes him the first character in Ninjago to be named after a type of cloud. The second is Mammatus.
  • His name could be a reference to Nimbus Station in LEGO Universe.
Trivia

Trivia[]
