Overview

“Chen brought us here so that only one would remain. Well, we are one!”
— Kai, "The Day of the Dragon"

The Elemental Fighters[1][2][3] were a group of Elemental Masters invited to compete in Master Chen's Tournament of Elements and later opposed him during the Second Serpentine War.

History

History[]

Background[]

To collect the elemental powers for a tranformation spell, Master Chen organized an underground tournament between elemental masters. The winner was falsly promised the Staff of Elements, that would contain the powers of all those who lost.

The Ninja Roll[]

During the match between Lloyd and Chamille the Ninja revealed Chen's intention to steal their elemental powers for an unknown spell, thus recruiting most of the competitors to their side.

Spy for a Spy[]

Nya reveals to the Ninja that there's a spy in their alliance. Each master was interrogated until Shade escaped the room. The distrust and infighting broke their alliance. Skylor's identity of Chen's spy remained undiscovered.

Spellbound[]

During the hunt after Nya all competitors except Lloyd were captured by Chen, making them realize the ninja were right about his plans.

The Forgotten Element[]

The depowered elemental masters under Cole and Zane's leadership built a Roto Jet and escaped Chen's noodle factory. They then broke in and fought back against the Cultists, as Kai destroyed Chen's staff allowing everyone to reclaim their elements. With their powers restored, they defeat the Cultists, forcing Chen and a select few of his followers to escape while taking Skylor as their hostage.

The Day of the Dragon[]

The Elemental Masters had taken over Chen's Island, trapping the Anacondrai Cultists while in the meantime, unbeknownst to them, Chen, Kapau and Chope were fulfilling the transformation spell using Skylor's element. Eventually, the spell was completed, turning Chen and his entire army into Anacondrai. The Elemental Masters tried fighting back, but the fake Anacondrai proved too strong. Eventually, the fake Anacondrai retreated, abandoning the stranded Elemental Masters on the island and heading off to terrorize Ninjago. However, with the guidance from Zane each master unlocked their Elemental Dragons allowing them to leave the island.

The Greatest Fear of All[]

The Elemental Masters reached the city a day after Chen only to find he hasn't yet invaded. Having their allies watch over the city, the ninja reconvenes at the Samurai X Cave which became their base of operations during the conflict. There, they devised their next plan of actions.

An army of what seemed like at least two-hundred fake Anacondrai spread out across Ninjago in noodle trucks, with the Elemental Masters setting off to stop them. However, upon stopping each of the trucks, they found they were all empty. With the Elemental Masters divided, Chen's army began attacking Ninjago, first with Jamanakai Village—a pivotal location in the first war.

The Corridor of Elders[]

The Elemental Masters and their allies block off the Corridor of Elders to prevent Chen's army from passing through, with Lloyd using his element to create a line in the ground, which they cannot allow the Anacondrai to pass. Seeing the alliance far ahead, Chen orders his army to charge. The fake Anacondrai slithers towards their adversaries, but their attack is cut short as the ground caves in before them. Zane seals the crevice with a blast of Ice and the battle begins. At first, the Elemental Masters overwhelm the Anacondrai and they begin to retreat, but Chen forces them to continue the battle. The fake Anacondrai charge at the ninja once more, this time attempting to cross the line that Lloyd drew. Chen takes off in the Roto Jet along with several Blade-Copters. One Blade-Copter is quickly hijacked by Shade, who uses it to fire down upon Chen's army. Despite this, the fake Anacondrai continue their attempts to cross the line, shoving back the Elemental Masters who give their all to hold their ground.

Garmadon, realizing they don't stand a chance, sacrifices himself to bring back the spirits of the Anacondrai Generals. Chen is confronted personally by Arcturus, who he pleas to but is ignored as the latter denounces Chen as a liar, a cheat and imposter before condemning him to the Cursed Realm. With Chen's army banished, the portal closed, and the war came to an end.

Later on, the Elemental Masters make amends with the ninja as they part ways but not before promising to offer future aid in other conflicts.

Official description

Official description[]

On the boat, the ninja soon realize that the powers that have always made them special… well, they are not so special after all. There are many Elemental Fighters on the boat. They are all descendants of the Elemental Masters, the guardians of the First Spinjitzu Master. They were granted power over the elements, so they could better protect him. These powers have been passed down through the generations. Many possess them within, but only few manage to unlock them. The fighters on the boat, on their way to the tournament of elements, have all unlocked their true potential. Wonder who is the strongest...[4]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Zane is the only member of the group who wasn't an official participant of the Tournament of Elements.
  • They act as the Second Serpentine War equivalent of the Elemental Masters that were part of the Elemental Alliance during the Serpentine War.
    • Most of the Elemental Fighters are descendants of the Elemental Alliance members.
  • Ash, Bolobo, Chamille, Gravis and Jacob are the only fighters who didn't join the Resistance.
