Overview

“My family is in this monastery, and I'll defend them to my last breath.”
— Geo to Ras, "When Doves Cry"

Geoder,[2] nicknamed Geo, is the Elemental Master of Fusion and a member of the Finders. He is a Munce and Geckle hybrid,[1] which led to him being rejected by both tribes due to their historical rivalry. Geo eventually ended up in the Land of Lost Things where he encountered and integrated the Finders. After the Merge, Geo discovered his Elemental Power and met Cole, who ended up in the Land of Lost Things. He later participated as a competitor in the Tournament of the Sources alongside the other Elemental Masters of his generation.

History

History[]

Background[]

Geo was born in Shintaro Mountain from a Munce and a Geckle as a hybrid, making him look unique.

However, this circumstance led him to being despised by both tribes due to their historical rivalry. Neither the Munce nor the Geckles wanted Geo, leaving him in a state of rejection and isolation. Because of this, he ran away from home. Due to getting lost and being forgotten by the tribes, Geo ended up in the Land of Lost Things when he was still a child.[3] At some point after in his older years, he encountered the Finders, fellow runaways who had also left their homes and were brought to the valley as a consequence of being forgotten.

However, following the Merge, Geo Became the Elemental Master of a new Elemental Power created by the Source Dragon of Strength, Fusion. Not long after, Geo met Cole, who ended up at the Land of Lost Things, where the latter achieved a golem form, but he believed Geo is giving the former a boost. Fearing he will leave if told the truth, Geo kept it secret from Cole.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Land of Lost Things[]

Geo waited in a giant noodle cup for Fritz and Spitz to arrive. As they did, Geo scolded the children for running off before Nya, Sora, and Cole enter and Geo checked on Cole's well-being. As the Hoarder roared outside, Geo offered to buy them some time while the ninja and the Finders made a run for it. Using Fusion, Geo attached the noodle cup on to the Hoarder's hand, distracting the creature as Geo meet up with the others. As they made their way towards the Rookery, Sora couldn't help but be amazed by the sculptures crafted by Geo. The hybrid revealed how his Elemental Powers work. While discussing the emergence of the new Elemental Powers resulting from the Merge, their discussion was abruptly halted by the sudden reappearance of the Hoarder. However, Geo informed them that they were secure within the protective confines of the Rookery, where they discovered the yellow Dragon Core was what was powering the machine.

Sora worked on the machine while Geo's arm remained trapped inside. Geo questioned whether he would be stuck there indefinitely. Sora reassured him that this was not the case. Geo admitted his lack of understanding regarding the machine's workings. Sora explained that the machine operated on dragon energy, with a Dragon Core being responsible for its functionality. She suggested that Geo's power could potentially charge a new battery to function like a Dragon Core. Geo expressed amazement at Sora's knowledge, wondering if she learned all of this while growing up in Imperium. Sora explained that while the Kingdom of Imperium is technologically advanced, it lacked respect for its citizens and dragons. She revealed that her parents were not supportive or loving, prompting her to leave Imperium. Geo shared his own experience, explaining that he left Shintaro Mountain due to the animosity between Munce and Geckles back then, as he looked like a Geckle despite being a Munce, even admitting he had forgotten about his parents. Geo remarked that living in Imperium seemed to have had a detrimental effect on Sora, who agreed by expressing her doubts about ever being able to fix the damage. In frustration, she slammed her pencil on the table, breaking it. Geo, however, took the broken pencil and fused the pieces back together, impressing Sora.

Geo asked if the process would be painful. Sora hesitated, admitting that she was unsure and had never attempted it before. Geo expressed his disappointment, finding her answer less than comforting. Sora then suggested that Riyu can give her Elemental Powers, allowing her to rebuild the machine. Geo's expression turns into a frown as Sora mentioned Cole's Elemental Golem form, as Sora believed it to be something that wasn't inherited. Soon, after a warning from Bonzle of Dr. LaRow and some Imperium Drones’ arrival, Geo was instructed to keep the other Finders safe, later pulling Bonzle away when she threw a bone at Dr. LaRow. As Geo attempted to retrieve the Dragon Core from a drone, he attached a broom to the drone. However, his plan backfired as he found himself being whisked away alongside the core before LaRow used the drone to electrocute Geo, causing him to plummet. Luckily, Cole came to the rescue, catching Geo.

As the Hoarder returned, Cole was in dire need of Geo's help and pleaded with him to energize him. Geo was hesitant to reveal the truth about how Cole's Elemental Golem form worked, but Cole emphasized the urgency of the situation. Eventually, Geo relented and appeared to used his Elemental Power on Cole, who used his Elemental Golem form. As Sora unleashed her Technology upon the Hoarder, Geo and the rest of the group realized that the Hoarder was actually a magnetic organism known as Sweeper. Geo thanked Sora. Cole then credited Geo for allowing him to hold off the Hoarder. However, Geo confessed that he didn't actually enhance Cole's power, explaining that his powers don't affect living beings. Geo admitted that he was afraid to tell Cole the truth as he thought he would be abandoned. However, Cole told Geo that he still needed him, believing that Geo helped him realize his true potential.

For Whom the Bell Tolls[]

As Sora and Wyldfyre made their way back to the dragon stables, they were met by Geo, who asked about the fate of the Finders. Sora took a moment to explain the dire situation unfolding in the Merged Realms, revealing that the Forbidden Five and the Spectral Dragonians were capturing the souls of everyone, with the ultimate goal of enabling Thunderfang to transform into a Source Dragon. Intrigued, Geo pressed for details about their plan, knowing that the ninja were always prepared with a plan. Sora nodded, confirming that there was indeed a plan in place, though she cautioned that it was quite unconventional.

After some time, Alfonzo Frohicky decided to offer his aunt's broth to the Finders, hoping it might help cure their hollowed state. However, Geo was concerned about the safety of the broth, noting that its odor was particularly unpleasant, even by the standards of someone living in the Land of Lost Things. Frohicky reassured him that as long as those who consumed it could keep it down, it would be fine. Geo then suggested that perhaps the smell itself could have curative properties. Frustration bubbled up when Frohicky began to question how Bonzle could eat at all, to which Geo pointed out that this was far from the most pressing issue they faced. He attempted to connect with Cole, hoping that a blink would indicate understanding, but Cole remained unresponsive. Suddenly, they noticed the portal gate activating, and Geo speculated that if the ninja had returned, it meant something had gone awry. Instead of the ninja, however, Ras appeared. Geo warned him to stay back. Ras declared that the ninja were scattered throughout the Merged Realms and demanded that Geo and Frohicky submit or perish. Geo stood firm, asserting that they could not be intimidated, as ninja never give up. Ras countered this claim by pointing out that neither Geo nor Frohicky were actual ninja before making his move. Suddenly, Frohicky hurled the broth at Ras' face, leaving Geo bewildered. Enraged, Ras pursued them as they quickly gathered the Finders and Cole, making their way to the elevator.

When Doves Cry[]

During the invasion of the Monastery of Spinjitzu, Geo and Mr. Frohicky worked together to protect the monastery. Geo tells Ras his family is in the Monastery and he'll defend them to his last breath. Ras, however, taunted him, suggesting that his resolve wouldn't last long. Mr. Frohicky's activation of the training grounds caused Ras to lose his weapons, giving Geo the chance to snatch Ras' hammer and send him flying across the training area. Before Ras could recover, Geo managed to fuse Ras' bone breastplate to a wooden pole, but Ras quickly kicked him away and broke free from the breastplate. Declaring that he didn't need a weapon to defeat Geo, Ras evaded Geo's hammer strike and disarmed him. As Geo attempted to land punches, Ras blocked and countered, kicking Geo to the ground. Ras then seized Geo, mocking the hybrid for his failures to protect the Finders, before brutally throwing him up and kicking him to the ground. After reclaiming his Source Dragon Icons, Ras left Geo trapped beneath the training course.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Being a Munce and Geckle hybrid, Geo takes features from both of those species. He is of a standard height as a Munce, also taking their yellow eyes, black bushy eyebrows, and two big white fangs protruding from the bottom of their mouth. His Geckle features are his big goblin-like ears and purple skin color. He has long olive green hair that has violet and turquoise streaks and is tied into a braid at the top of his head. Over all of his body, he has indigo markings that glow light blue when using his powers.

His attire consists of an old white/gray tank top under a sleeveless dark red coat accented with yellow stripes and orange, yellow, and gold mandala-like symbols. On his left wrist is a dark gray cuff and on his right wrist are a red wristband and a bracelet made of yellow and blue beads. He also wears a rusty hoop earring on his left ear, torn black shorts held by a dark brown belt with a skull on it, brown sandals, and a multicolored necklace with a brown diamond shape at the end.

Personality

Personality[]

Geo is a Munce and Geckle hybrid who was rejected by both the tribes due to his origins. Because of this, he has grown up with a strong sense of being an outsider. This experience made him empathetic toward others who are lost or forgotten.

He has a strong desire to belong to and protect those he cares about, such as his found family, the Finders, and especially Cole. He was scared he would lose Cole if the ninja realized he did not need Geo's powers to help him but found out those fears were unnecessary. Geo is thoughtful and inventive and his Elemental Power of Fusion allows him to express himself in art as he is very good at creating statues from various objects.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Fusion - As a Master of Fusion, Geo possesses the ability to fuse objects together, creating a strong bond between any item and making them into a single unit.
  • Combat skills - Although rather peaceful by nature, Geo knows how to defend himself even though he's not a ninja specifically to protect those he loves. He was able to hold his own against Wolf Mask Warriors in the Land of Lost Things and fight against the Chaos-empowered warriors in the Tournament of the Sources, even defeating the corrupted warriors once his elemental power was restored. Later, he was able to resist for a short while and land some good blows on Ras, a formidable opponent armed with two Source Dragon Icons when the tiger attacked the Monastery of Spinjitzu, but was easily defeated by him.
  • Art - Geo is an artist, using his elemental power to create sculptures and other works of art.

Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

The unsung of heroes and villains[]

Geo is a Munce with the colour of a Geckle. But Munces and Geckles hate each other, so he became the odd one out, the one nobody wanted and everyone forgot about. So after the Merge, he ended up in the Land of Lost Things. There he found a new family: Cole and the Finders, and he acquired the unique Elemental Power of Fusion.[5]

Mysteries of the Merged World[]

Geo himself is something of a fusion. He comes from a land of two warring tribes: the Muntzes and the Geckles. He's a Muntz with the pattern of a Geckle, which made him an outcast.

Etymology

Etymology[]

Geo- is a prefix derived from the Greek word γῆ or γαῖα, meaning "Earth," usually in the sense of "Ground" or "Land."

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Prior to his introduction, Geo was initially hinted at by Asa Tait through a drawing of Lobbo dressing up as Geo.[6]
  • Geo's Munce features are his height, hair, eyes, and teeth, and his Geckle features are his purple skin color, long ears, and the shape of his eyebrows.
    • He is the only Munce whose name doesn't start with an M because it starts with a G, the letter every Geckle name starts with.
  • It is evident that Geo's exile by both Munce and Geckles occurred prior to the events of Master of the Mountain, as it transpired during the Munce and Geckles' conflict that concluded in Master of the Mountain. Additionally, Geo and Cole are confirmed to be of the same age.[7]
  • Geo is the last new Elemental Master to appear in Season 1 of Dragons Rising following the Merge. Unlike the previous three, his Elemental Powers do not align with his place of origin, instead they align with his appearance. Additionally, he is the only one out of the four to come from Ninjago, while the others are from parallel realms.
  • Cole and Geo act as guardians and parental figures to Fritz, Spitz and Bonzle. Chris "Doc" Wyatt has alluded to a relationship between Cole and Geo.[8] He has also liked tweets in support of it.[9]
    • Writer Glen Lakin also supports the relationship, though saying he "cannot comment on what is/isn't canon."[10]
    • When asked if they will kiss at some point, the writers said to "keep watching the show."[11]
    • If the ship becomes canon, Geo and Cole will be the first Elemental Masters to be confirmed as a part of the LGBTQ+ community.
  • He is the first Munce to be an Elemental Master, the second Munce being Mord.
  • He is the second Geckle to be an Elemental Master, the first being an Unnamed Previous Champion, and the third being Gorge.
  • Geo was told by Cole about his adventures, including the Battle for Shintaro Mountain. However, he has no need or desire to go back to Shintaro after the Munce and Geckles made peace because he wasn't treated well there.[12]
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt think the idea of Geo being born to Gulch and Murtessa after they were drunk or high and had a "night of pleasure" is a "very funny idea".[13]
  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, Geo avoided other Munce and Geckles during the Tournament of the Sources.[14]
  • Glen Lakin believes Geo's favorite food would be a granola bar.[15]
  • Geo shares a similar origin story with Reegull from Legends of Chima, as both are hybrids of two different species—Geo being a hybrid of Munce and Geckle, and Reegull a hybrid of Raven and Eagle. Because of their hybrid nature, both characters faced rejection from their respective species, resulting in a sense of isolation. However, they did not make the same life choices afterwards; Reegull wanted revenge on everyone for rejecting him, while Geo remained peaceful.
    • They are also both part of the same universe, with Chima being located in the Merged Realms.
