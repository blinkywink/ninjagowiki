Overview

“You have to trust the evidence, Arin. Even if it goes against what you believe.”
— Jenny to Arin, "Clues and Suspects"

Jenny Nived[1] is the mother of Arin and the wife of Barry. Along with her husband, she disappeared during the Merge and was reassigned to the Land of Lee by the Administration.

History

History[]

Background[]

Some years before the Merge, she got married to Barry and they had Arin, and lived in a small neighborhood with them. Arin was a fan of the ninja and dreamed of becoming one, which both she and Barry seemingly supported. Jenny taught Arin how to bake, and whenever he was sad, she would make him a pie.[2] They would also get together and play detective games.

Inside the Maze (flashback)[]

One day, Jenny and Arin were sitting on a porch swing at their house. As she reads him a book about detectives, she asks him a question, before praising him after he gets it right.

Clues and Suspects (flashback)[]

Jenny was playing a detective game with Arin and Barry. During the game, Jenny tells Arin about believing the evidence, before Barry throws his cards.

The Merge: Part 1 (flashback)[]

Jenny and Barry sit on the porch swing as they watch their son practice his skills on the gate before the Merge began to occur. As they head inside, Jenny tells him to get inside as well, but it was too late. When the Merge concluded, Arin's neighborhood had seemingly vanished, along with his parents.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Power Within[]

While rescuing citizens of the Crossroads, Arin sees his parents through a rift. They see him as the rift closes, but for a moment, they saw their son as a ninja, becoming aware of his dream becoming a reality.

Prior to The Shape of Motion[]

At some point, Jenny and her husband were reassigned by the Department of Reassignment, where they ended up in the Land of Lee.[3]

Other appearances and mentions

Other appearances and mentions[]

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

Arin ventured into the Phantasm Cave, where a creature's electric tendrils extended towards him, signaling the beginning of the Test of Fears. Inside, he encountered a haunting illusion of his parents. Despite his desperate efforts to reach them, he found himself unable to do so. Jenny questioned why he had abandoned them, pondering if the ninja had taken their place as his new family. Arin's heart filled with anguish as he cried out, only to witness his parents vanish into thin air.

The Forest of Spirits[]

After Arin gets knocked out by Ras in the Forest of Spirits, he hallucinates his parents approaching him.

Rising Ninja[]

Still hallucinating from his defeat, Arin tries to apologize to his parents, only to discover it was just Lloyd and Nya upon regaining his senses.

Chaos Reigns[]

After defeating Thunderfang with the help of Lloyd, Arin noted to Lloyd on their way to the Monastery of the Spectral Lands' portal gate that he would like to take a vacation with his parents before properly rejoining the ninja, to which Lloyd agreed.
