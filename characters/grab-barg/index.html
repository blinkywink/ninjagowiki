Overview

— Grab-Barg to Arin, "Crossroads Carnival"

Chef Grab-Barg is a Mucoid chef and has a rivalry against Arin.

History

History[]

Background[]

Grab-Barg is a chef, who has a rivalry with Arin over who makes the best pies, with Grab-Barg winning every contest and Arin being in second.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Crossroads Carnival[]

During the Crossroads Carnival, Grab-Barg deliberately bumps into Arin as he enters his Snowberry Pie to Alfonzo Frohicky before taunting him about him potentially coming in second again, while also claiming his pie looks mangy, and even calling his 'weird little dog' like it, unaware it was the baby dragon Riyu in disguise. He watches the Whack Rats kidnap Lobbo for his prize money, but preferred to mind his own business afterwards by entering his pie. In the end, Grab-Barg, once again, won the contest.

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

Grab-Barg is one of the judges of the Zane-a-like contest during Zane Day, unaware that Zane himself was one of the participants.

The Power Within[]

Grab-Barb almost gets pulled into the MergeQuake storm, but is saved by Zane.

The Elemental Mechs[]

The Mech Master[]

In another pie-baking competition, Grab-Barg and Arin went head-to-head. This time, Grab-Barg's Snowberry Pies were adorned with a delightful layer of Crème de Imperium. Grab-Barg emerged victorious once again, and his pies received high praise from Kai and Cole.

After Arin was tossed off The Mechanic's stolen mech and thrown into his pie stall, Grab-Barb glares at Arin as he was about to eat one of his Snowberry Pies without paying.

A Pain in the Mech! (flashback)[]

Grab-Barg participated in the noodle cook-off. As the ninja arrived to spectate, Kai had a conversation with Grab-Barg and was served a bowl of his noodles. As the Ice Dragons were about to attack Lava-Tony and his sous-chefs for stealing their eggs, Grab-Barg fled further into his stall for safety.

Before Crossroads Crosstalk[]

Shortly after the spectral invasions, Grab-Brag opened a new restaurant in the Crossroads near Kreel's Junkyard.[1]
