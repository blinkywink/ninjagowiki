Overview

“They don't call her "The Quiet One" for nothing.”
— Simon to the Police Commissioner, "True Potential"

Detective Simon (also known as the Good Detective) is a detective in the Ninjago Police department. Unlike his counterpart Tommy, Simon takes a much more peaceful approach to his job.

History

History[]

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Skybound[]

Public Enemy Number One[]

Simon and Tommy interrogate Misako, but she refused to tell them where the ninja were hiding. She tried telling them they were framed, but they didn't believe her. They left the room, but when they came back, they noticed she is gone.

The police eventually chased what they thought was Kai, but it turned out to just be a rat.

Misfortune Rising[]

When the Commissioner told the police force the ninja escaped from prison, Simon helped track them down.

When news broke out that the Sky Pirates are attacking the city, Simon told the Commissioner they didn't arrest the ninja since they were trying to help. The Commissioner went after them anyway.

My Dinner With Nadakhan[]

The detectives, Commissioner, and Ronin found Nya, Lloyd, and Cole near Tiger Widow Island. After telling them they realized the ninja are not criminals, they showed them a bottle with Jay's message inside.

Wishmasters[]

Simon helped build the Raid Zeppelin so the ninja can get close to Djinjago.

The Last Resort[]

Simon and Tommy were defending the police station from the Sky Pirates, after a police car almost falls on them, Simon is captured by the Sky Pirates.

When the trio were captured by Nadakhan, they were threatened to walk the plank. Tommy accidentally told them the ninja still have the venom and jumped off. The other two followed, and they were surrounded by sharks.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

Sons of Garmadon[]

Snake Jaguar[]

Simon and Tommy are in a police car drinking coffee when the Sons of Garmadon drive by having a race. Tommy spills his coffee on him while Simon gloats he didn't. Tommy tells Simon to chase after Zane and the Sons of Garmadon and he does it. Zane jumps over the cops and hits their hood. Simon says he still didn't spill his coffee. After that, the airbag activates, squishing him and spilling his coffee.

Dread on Arrival[]

After being defeated by the ninja, Simon and Tommy approach Harumi and then arrest her, later placing her into a police truck and transport her away to the Police Precinct.

True Potential[]

Simon and the Commissioner watch Harumi as she remained silent before a crash was heard and to their shock, it was Lord Garmadon.

Later on he's seen in the background helping in cleaning up the mess caused by the breakout.

Big Trouble, Little Ninjago[]

Simon is seen in the police blockade along with Tommy.

Hunted[]

Green Destiny[]

The Resistance help chain down the Colossus. When the Colossus starts to fall apart, the Resistance runs away to safety.

The police force begin to arrest the Sons of Garmadon once more.

Other appearances

Other appearances[]

Radio Free Ninjago[]

Simon is seen in one of the crossed out photos on the floor in Borg Tower when the Resistance broadcasts Lloyd's message to Ninjago, indicating Simon has been captured as part of Ultra Violet's Ultra Hunt.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Simon is named after Simon Lucas, Ninjago's Senior Creative Director.[1]
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, Simon, Tommy, and the Police Commissioner were able to get back to Ninjago and escape the sharks from "The Last Resort" with the help of giant sea turtles.[2]
  • His CGI image uses a generic LEGO City face.
  • Despite the fact that he and Tommy wore beige clothes similar to detectives' coats in Skybound, since Sons of Garmadon they already have a regular police uniform. The reason for this change is unknown, but it may indicate that they are no longer detectives.
    • In "Green Destiny," Simon and Tommy are both wearing their original outfit. But when they were arresting the Sons of Garmadon, their outfits changed back into police uniforms.
