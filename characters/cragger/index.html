Overview

“These phoenix call themselves the guardians of Chima, but me, you, Laval, we're the true guardians of Chima!”
— Cragger to Laval, "Wings of Fire"

Cragger is the Crown Prince of the Crocodile Tribe, the son of Crominus and Crunket, and the younger twin brother of Crooler. He is also one of Laval's best friends.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Cragger is a muscular, light green-colored crocodile with lighter colored chest and abdomen scales, two pairs of white claws on his feet, red spikes running down his head, and a long tail (in some media). A distinguishing feature about Cragger is his lack of a left eye, with a notable scar running across that area of his face.

Cragger gained more attire throughout the course of the sets and TV show. In the first wave, he was nearly completely bare, clad in a pair of crimson chest straps in the shape of an X with a CHI-holder in the center and several teeth and a fish-bone adorning them. A large belt hung at his waist with more fangs and a couple of yellow ropes attached. From this belt extended a badly damaged loincloth, being the only garment below his waist.

Later, during the Outlands mission, Cragger gained what is seemingly chainmail over his chest. He wears a metal breastplate with a CHI-holder once again in its center. His arms are bare. Cragger's new leggings consist of a deep grey belt, holding on another crimson loincloth much like his original, only slightly larger and less notable, being concealed by a series of dark grey or silver straps. His loincloth is decorated with a matching red rope and several teeth.

During the Fire Wars, Cragger was seen fully clothed for the first time. He wore a red and gold fire suit much like the others. Being a major character alongside Laval, Cragger had a pair of unique, Crocodile-head-shaped kneepads. His suit also contained several golden pieces of armor for each of his claws. With Fire Wings, Cragger dons a Fire Wing Harness, with flaming wings and a Crocodile symbol.

Personality

Personality[]

At first, Cragger was an extremely-competitive, but sometimes friendly with Laval, yet that his friendship would have later been broken when he had his first taste of CHI. After his parents’ "death", he felt guilty for his mistakes, believing that it was his fault if jis parents died, but his sister Crooler’s brainwashing reignited his bloodlust and drove him to attack the Lions.

After being saved by Laval from his fall, he remembered their friendship and felt remorseful and guilty for Laval’s apparent death, but upon realizing he had survived, and seeing his mother, Crunket, alive and well, Cragger redeems himself and joins to the good side.

During the second season, Cragger appears more heroic and rescuing, as he fights back along with the heroic tribes against the Crawlers from the Outlands, but upon being brainwashed again by Crooler, Cragger angrily locked her up, only to later forgive his sister and redeem her once the brainwashing had faded, proving he had truly changed for the better.

In season 3, he and his friends join to the fire tribes to fight back The Ice Hunters, and at the end of the conflict he felt grateful toward them for having saved Chima. He retained his sense of humor as well, as his last line is him comically questioning how they would be able to get off Mount Cavora, having being driven high in the sky by the battle with the Ice Hunters.

Weapons and abilities

Weapons and abilities[]

  • Chi empowerment: Under the influence of chi, Cragger is granted vast superhuman powers.
    • Superhuman strength: Whilst using chi Cragger is much stronger than an ordinary crocodile. However, he wasn’t able to overpower the entire gorilla tribe at once.
    • Superhuman durability: Cragger can take much more damage when using chi allowing him to remain unharmed.
    • Superhuman speed: When Cragger plugged chi for the first time, he went out of control and started running around at great speeds.
    • Superhuman agility: Cragger is extremely agile whilst under the influence of chi allowing him to perform difficult aerial tricks.
    • Shockwave generation: When Cragger tired to remove the orb of chi from his chest, he unintentionally created a powerful shockwave of energy that destroyed his own dam.

Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

From LEGO.com[]

Cragger was always an extremely-competitive kid. He lived for the rush of victory. Laval actually liked that about him when they first met, and the two became best friends at a very young age. At the time, both cared only about adventure, fun, and the occasional prank. Cragger challenged Laval’s Lion sense of propriety, and Laval had a way of getting Cragger to laugh at himself – no small feat given Cragger’s oversized ego. But everything changed when Cragger had his first taste of CHI. There are strict rules about how old one must be to use CHI, and Cragger was far too young to handle its awesome power. For Cragger, CHI gave him that sense of ultimate power he had always been seeking. He never completely recovered from the experience. This was the beginning of the end for Laval and Cragger, as well as Chima’s thousand-year Age of Peace.

Legends of Chima: Character Encyclopedia[]

The quest for the Fire Wings has actually been a good thing in one way for Cragger. Having a common enemy in the Icehunters has helped him to strengthen his bonds of friendship with Eris and Laval and regain their trust. The reappearance of his parents after their long absence has also made Cragger feel like the future will be bright … if they can just get rid of all this ice. He has privately vowed to do whatever he must to save Chima and his friends from Sir Fangar and his armies.

Other[]

Cragger has had an eventful life for one so young. Believing his parents to be dead, he has assumed the Crocodile throne and fought against the other Tribes. Slowly, Cragger is learning to put aside his greed and ambition for the good of Chima. Cragger rides a specially designed Croc Speedor with a long, low body. An expert rider, Cragger's natural competitive streak emerges during tournaments. Cragger has tried to befriend the dangerous Wolves, foolish Rhinos and untrustworthy Ravens in order to fight the Lions. Now he is learning that the best of friends, like Laval, are the ones that forgive you when you make mistakes. Cragger's rivalry with his twin sister Crooler started early on in their lives - when she pushed his egg out of the family nest.

History

Background

Background[]

Cragger was very competitive from the start. He was best friends with the prince of the Lion tribe, Laval, and the two played together for much of their younger years.

Legends of Chima, Season 1

Legends of Chima, Season 1[]

The Legend of Chima/The Great Story[]

In a game of hide-and-seek, Cragger hid from Laval but suddenly jumped out and playfully tackled him. Laval protested, insisting the game was about hiding, not attacking, while Cragger suggested that sometimes it's fun to break the rules and improvise. Laval countered that his tribe had specific rules for improvisation, introducing the "banana" rule, which he explained involves placing a banana on a crocodile's tail. Cragger soon realized that Laval had indeed put a banana on his tail. Just then, Gorzan swung in on a rope, grabbed Cragger's tail, and flipped him upside down, prompting Cragger to demand to be set free. Meanwhile, Laval marked another point for himself on the Forever Rock. After finding Cragger, Gorzan, and Rogon, Laval noticed that Worriz was still hiding. Skinnet then released a strong odor, forcing Worriz to emerge from the bushes, furious about the unfair tactic due to his sensitive nose. Later, Cragger and Laval were playing with wooden swords when Laval bragged about predicting Cragger's move. In response, Cragger tripped Laval with his tail, leaving him on the ground and teasing him about it. Laval quickly retaliated by throwing his sword at a beehive, which landed on Cragger's head, causing a swarm of bees to surround him. Cragger panicked and shouted for help. Luckily, Bladvic showed up and removed the hive, prompting Cragger to thank him. As Bladvic drifted back to sleep, Cragger pondered if he ever truly woke up, while Laval explained that only whispers could rouse him. Later, at the Forever Rock, Cragger asked Laval who his best friend was, and Laval joyfully declared it was Cragger. Cragger reciprocated the sentiment, and Laval was confident that their strong friendship could withstand anything. Cragger then suggested they sneak inside to see the sacred Pool of Chi.

Laval felt uncertain due to the Lion Tribe's strict rules, which prohibited young animals from using Chi until they reached the Age of Becoming. Cragger, however, was restless and argued that they could just take a quick look at the Pool of Chi without waiting. He teased Laval for being overly cautious and adhering to the rules. Eventually, their curiosity got the better of Laval, and they climbed the vines to the temple. Once inside the throne room, Laval was eager to leave, but Cragger was captivated by the Pool of Chi and wondered what it would be like to use its power. Ignoring Laval's warnings, Cragger took an orb of Chi, triggering a surge of energy that overwhelmed him. In a wild frenzy, he charged out of the temple, disregarding everything around him. Laval tried to intervene, but Cragger's reckless behavior escalated, leading him to toss Bladvic toward Laval and Eris. As Cragger continued his rampage, he began smashing his head against the Forever Rock. Despite Laval's pleas for him to calm down, Cragger attacked Laval, prompting Lagravis to step in and blast him away. Following this, Lagravis ordered Cragger to be chained up and for his parents to be informed. When Crominus and Crunket learned of their son's actions, they were instead furious about how the Lions treated him. Lagravis justified his actions by stating that restraining Cragger was essential due to the threat he represented. Crominus countered, claiming that the punishment was excessive for a young crocodile like Cragger. Although Cragger attempted to assert his maturity, Crunket silenced him. Both Crominus and Crunket demanded an apology from Lagravis and Laval, accusing Laval of exploiting Cragger. The discussion grew more intense, with Lagravis defending the Lions' long-standing role in safeguarding the sacred Pool of Chi, deeming Cragger's actions unacceptable. Crominus, however, remained unconvinced by the Lions' defense and, before leaving, asserted that the issue was far from resolved.

After Crominus prohibited Cragger from playing with Laval, Cragger began to spend time with Worriz, who was eager to experience Chi. Cragger described it as an incredible feeling and decided to take Worriz to the Lion Temple, which they found to be inadequately guarded. However, they were soon caught by Lagravis and his guards. In a panic, Cragger tried to shift the blame to Laval, claiming it was his idea, but Laval quickly refuted this, stating he had been confined to his room for weeks. Lagravis ordered the guards to keep an eye on Cragger while he contacted Crominus. In fear, Cragger lashed out, attacked the guards with his tail, and managed to escape. Laval, enraged by the attack on his father, chased after Cragger on his Speedor. During the pursuit, Cragger fired the Croc Flare, alerting the Crocodile Tribe and prompting the Lion Tribe to prepare for conflict. Cragger thought he had evaded Laval when he reached the Fangs, but Laval, familiar with the terrain, appeared right in front of him, having been taken there by Lagravis. Cragger complained about not being taken to the Fangs by Laval, who explained the dangers of the area, which his father had forbidden him from exploring alone. Ignoring the warnings, Cragger fell into the Gorge of Eternal Depth, and Laval saved him, cautioning that rules often exist for a reason. However, Cragger, fixated on Chi, dismissed the lesson and reminded Laval of their shared desire to see the sacred pool. Laval refused to believe he would ever make the same choices as Cragger and accused him of abandoning their friendship, leaving Cragger feeling ashamed. Cragger apologized, but it wasn't for stealing the Chi or betraying Laval; instead, he regretted calling the Crocodile Tribe. Laval was shocked to discover that Cragger had used a Croc Flare. Soon, Crominus and Crunket arrived in their tank, with Crominus ordering Cragger to stay away from Laval, while Crunket worried that Laval had negatively influenced their son. Cragger defended Laval, claiming the lions were innocent. When the Lion Tribe showed up, an accidental shot from the crocodiles sparked a conflict. Cragger climbed onto his parents' tank, trying to clarify the misunderstanding, but Crominus ignored him and commanded a claw-poon to be fired at Lagravi s' Lion Tank. However, Lagravis' tank was heavier than anticipated, causing it to be dragged instead of pulled. This led to the Crocodile Tank being pulled toward a field of spikes, ultimately overturning. Crominus ordered an evacuation, and while Cragger escaped, his parents remained trapped inside. As a sinkhole opened beneath the tank, Cragger pleaded for peace, admitting his role in the disaster. Laval reassured him that his parents would be okay, as Lagravis attempted to stabilize the Crocodile Tank. Yet, Cragger feared losing his parents and, in desperation, cut the claw-poon's rope to separate the tanks. He then tried to lift the overturned tank but quickly realized he lacked the strength. He thought of Chi, believing it could give him the power he needed. Crominus and Crunket, thinking it was the end, declared Cragger the new Crocodile King and urged him to make them proud. Crominus attempted to caution Cragger about something, but before he could complete his warning, the tank fell into the sinkhole. Cragger hurried to the edge and almost fell once more, but Laval was there to save him, pulling him back to safety. Unbeknownst to Cragger, Crominus and Crunket had survived the fall and were trying to warn him not to listen to Crooler.

After the battle, Cragger felt deeply downcast. Crawley and Crug tried to lift his spirits, but their attempts were ineffective. Crooler then approached her brother, suggesting she had something that could help him feel better. Cragger quickly dismissed the notion of attacking the Lions. Crooler feigned innocence, claiming she wasn't trying to incite conflict, and instead offered him a seemingly harmless plant. However, this was actually the Persuader Plant. She released its pollen into Cragger's face and suggested that his parents would be proud if he took down the Lions. The plant's influence soon warped Cragger's thoughts, leading him to declare that the Crocodiles must annihilate the Lions.

In Chi Day, the Lions unveil the Golden Chi, which was awarded to the victor of the Joust of Jungle Judgment. After Laval's victory, Cragger appeared and disputed the outcome, claiming that the true winner should not leave an opponent standing. Lagravis clarified that Cragger cannot challenge the result since he was absent during the competition. Cragger saw this as another instance of the Lions manipulating the rules and proposed a joust with Laval. Although Laval was inclined to accept, Lagravis forbade it. Cragger then targeted Eris, charging at her while she struggled to start her Speedor, leaving her vulnerable. Laval rushed in to protect her, colliding with Cragger to thwart the attack. As Cragger tried to flee, Laval chased him into the Falling Jungle. There, Cragger used the terrain to his advantage, taunting Laval and blaming him for the tragedy of his parents' apparent deaths, accusing him of not saving them by withholding Chi. Laval attempted to reason with Cragger, explaining that the incident was an accident and reminding him of the time when he broke the rules for him, but he won't do so again. Cragger retaliated with his spear, and despite Laval's desire to avoid serious harm, he urged Cragger to surrender. Cragger refused and ultimately cut down a tree that fell on Laval, treating it as yet another "accident" as he prepared to strike. Just as he raises his spear, Eris intervened, disarming him, while Gorzan lifted the fallen tree off Laval, allowing Cragger to escape. Cragger returned to the Crocodile Swamp, plotting a major assault during Laval's Age of Becoming ceremony. He enlisted the Raven Tribe's help for the attack by paying them, while the Wolf Tribe, angered by Worriz's defeat in the Golden Chi race, agreed to support the Crocs. As Cragger rallied the Rebel Tribes, he boldly declared that today they would seize control of Chima and eliminate the Lions. However, his speech was interrupted by Plovar, who started pecking at Cragger's teeth. After Cragger asked to postpone the dental discussion, Plovar agreed, allowing Cragger to resume his speech and reiterate his plan to defeat the Lions.

As the Rebel Tribes approached the Lion City, they ensnared Eris, who stopped Cragger. Despite Eri s' attempts to reason with him, Cragger remained obstinate, placing his father's crown as the Rebel Tribes advanced. The Rebel Tribes launched an assault on the Lion City, resulting in chaos that knocked the Chi orb intended for Laval away. Seizing the opportunity, Cragger snatched Laval's Chi and others before fleeing into the forest on his Speedor. Laval, not recognizing him, gave chase. Cragger taunted Laval and refused to hand over the Chi, leading to a collision between their Speedorz that sent them both flying. As Cragger tried to finish Laval off, Laval evaded his attacks, while Cragger recklessly swung his weapon, damaging the surrounding trees. Their battle eventually brought them to the Forever Rock, where Laval grabbed Cragger's tail and threw him onto the rock. He readied his sword, demanding Cragger's surrender, but was taken aback when Cragger's crown fell off, revealing his identity. Cragger urged Laval to end him, but Laval hesitated, recalling their past friendship. Cragger bitterly claimed their bond ended since the battle at the Fangs, to which Laval explained it was an accident and emphasized that Chi belonged to all of Chima. Cragger dismissed this notion, ridiculing the teachings of Mount Cavora, the Legend Beasts, and sharing Chi for peace, asserting that the conflict at the Lion City proved such beliefs were futile. Laval countered that it was Cragger who initiated the attack. Cragger ordered Laval to surrender or else. Laval, however, marked the Forever Rock with his sword, leaving a tally mark for Cragger before walking away. This infuriated Cragger, who couldn't comprehend why Laval would still see him as a friend. Laval prepared to recover his Chi, but Cragger attacked him again. In their struggle, Cragger overpowered Laval, shouting that he was no longer his friend before throwing him into the water. As Laval fought for air, he still called out to Cragger as an old friend, but Cragger only mocked him and left him to sink.

Upon returning to the battlefield, Cragger met Crooler, who inquired about Laval. By then, the effects of the Persuader Plant had faded, leaving Cragger remorseful for his actions. Before he could act on his feelings, Crooler reactivated the Persuader, regaining control over him. Cragger confidently proclaimed that Laval would never return, but to everyone's surprise, Laval appeared with the Lion Legend Beast. The tribes showed their respect to the legendary creature, but under the plant's influence, Cragger dismissed it as just another animal. He charged at the beast with a Chi orb, vowing to prove what a true legend was. The Lion Legend Beast easily countered, sending Cragger crashing into the Lion Temple walls and knocking the Chi orb from his harness. Laval then reclaimed his Chi, and when Cragger attempted to attack again, Laval effortlessly stopped him. Finally, Cragger surrendered, grumbling that the fight was unfair since Laval had Chi and the support of a Legend Beast, while he had nothing to match it. Laval commanded Cragger to withdraw his troops from the Lion Temple, but Cragger warned that the war was not finished. The Rebel Tribes then retreated, forcing Cragger to follow suit.

The Warrior Within[]

Crooler has seemingly took complete control over Cragger's mind, with him only questioning her statements when the effect wears off. Laval comes after them while they're leaving in his Command Ship, and warns them about their Chi usage. After Crawley is swatted away, and Crug is losing against Laval, Cragger gives Laval all of his Chi, with Crug bearing witness. He then broadcasts that the Lions stole their Chi, and will never give them any more, in front of his entire tribe. Laval and Eris come back to give the Chi back, but Cragger mumbles threats to them, (that his tribe can't hear) which forces Laval to scream "YOU'RE NEVER GETTING OUR CHI", in front of all the crocodiles, making them hate him even more. Laval throws their Chi down the Gorge of Eternal Depth, and keeps one to fight off their army. However, Cragger pushes him over, only for Laval to be saved by Eris' Eagle Intercepter. Cragger then sent a message to Razar and Worriz to come and attack Laval with their armies, but because Gorzan was protecting a flower on the road, they couldn't come. Because their allies were held back, Cragger had to retreat.When Laval goes to find the Legend Beast, Cragger cuts him off, and reveals that he still had an orb of Chi, which he uses. When everything heats up, Plomar comes in, annoying Cragger, Laval, and all of the Crocs. When they throw weapons at him and force him to leave, Cragger's forces and his Chi overwhelm Laval, until an army of Eagles and Lions come in to save him. However, Crooler comes up with another brilliant plan, thinking that the lions only have allies because they control the Chi.

The Hundred Year Moon[]

As the Hundred Year Moon approached, Worriz went to the Crocodile Swamp to alert the Crocodiles about the Wolves' potential loss of control during this time. Cragger, however, dismissed the warning, sarcastically questioning why the Crocodiles would exploit the Wolves' vulnerability. Worriz emphasized that while the Wolves might be chaotic, they would not be defenseless. After Worriz left, Cragger suggested hiding indoors, but Crooler proposed a plan to lure Laval and the heroes away from safety. Later, Crooler kidnapped G'Loona, leaving a trail of her flowers that led Gorzan to seek help from the Lions, who contacted Eris to encourage the heroes to venture into the wolf-infested woods.

Cragger admired Crooler's plan, believing it would lead the heroes to their doom. However, while G'Loona was tied near the Forever Rock, her isolation was unexpectedly interrupted by Wonald, who, driven by hunger from the Hundred Year Moon, freed her in search of bananas. Watching through a telescope, Cragger expressed his frustration over Crooler's failed plan, but Crooler remained optimistic that they would still capture Laval. Despite their initial success in drawing the heroes into the forest, Cragger and Crooler ultimately failed, as G'Loona was rescued, the heroes survived, and the Wolves returned to normal after the moon passed.

Foxtrot[]

Cragger and his forces tried to ambush ShadoWind in a canyon, but the plan failed when ShadoWind escaped by climbing the cliff with his Speedor. In the chaos, Cragger got caught in Razar’s electric nets while chasing after him. Frustrated, he struggled to free himself and blamed his troops for letting ShadoWind slip away. Crooler attempted to regain control using her Persuader Plant, but her efforts backfired, resulting in Cragger getting shocked again by the next.

On Chi Day, rumors of cheating among the tribes surfaced, with Crawley telling Cragger that the beavers were secretly tampering with the ramps. When Cragger asked for the source, Crug confidently claimed it was reliable. Soon, Lagravis announced the start of the Chi Day race, transforming the landscape into the Ruthless Race of Ramps. Cragger observed the course and noted that winning a ramp race required wings, glancing at Eris and Laval. As the race commenced, Cragger pushed Laval off the straight path when they reached a ramp, but ShadoWind intervened, helping Laval recover as he tool the lead. Later, Cragger got stuck in the bears' honey, causing him to fall from his Speedor and lose the race. By the time ShadoWind crossed the finish line as the winner, the tribes were already fighting. Amid the chaos, Cragger noticed Crooler moving away with the wolves, worrying about his sister's safety.

Cragger soon realized that Crooler had been collaborating with the wolves, who were invading the Sacred Pool and stealing vast amounts of Chi. He confronted her, demanding to understand her actions and the implications of destroying the Pledge of the Pack. Crooler tried to use her plant, but Cragger, fed up, slapped it away and questioned her continued use of it, but didn't get a clear answer. When Crooler finally confessed that she and the wolves plan to steal all the Chi in Chima, Cragger asserted that no one would take Chi without his involvement and refused to let the pledge be destroyed. Worriz tried to defend his actions by claiming the pledge was insignificant and that he was willing to bend the rules, to which Cragger angrily retorted that Worriz always bended the rules. Worriz pointed out the odds, stating there were three wolves against one crocodile, but Cragger countered by indicating Crooler, insisting there were two crocodiles. To his shock, Crooler stepped back, choosing to side with the wolves.

Just then, Laval arrived with Eris and Rogon, and Cragger found himself unexpectedly fighting alongside Laval against Worriz. Laval remarked he never expected Cragger to try to stop a Chi thief. Pretending to be noble, Cragger claimed he was helping Chima, and Laval accepted this. Initially, they struggle against the wolves, who were empowered by the stolen Chi, but they eventually recall an old childhood move, working together to defeat Winzar and Wilhurt. As Cragger and Laval focused on Worriz, he summoned Windra with a howl. Windra arrived in a helicopter and launched an aerial attack on them. Forced to retreat, Cragger soon noticed the wolves making off with the stolen Chi. He clung to the helicopter's rail, causing several Chi orbs to drop. While hanging on, Cragger fought Worriz, but Windra stompsedon his hand, making it harder for him to hold on. When he finally lost his grip and fell, Laval used Chi to save him. After the fight, Cragger was heartbroken that Crooler has betrayed him as Crawley and Crug took him home in a Claw Ripper.

The Chi Jackers[]

Cragger was enraged with Crooler for betraying the Crocodile Tribe. While Crooler defended her alliance with the wolves as a means to help the crocodiles raid the lions and acquire more Chi, Cragger argued that she had no right to make such a significant decision behind his back. He recalled how Laval saved him after he fell from the wolf copter, leading him to question the ongoing conflict between the tribes and wonder if friendship was possible. However, Crooler rejected this notion and tried to use the Persuader Plant to sway him, but it was dying, and she needed a new one. In response, Cragger sarcastically instructed Crug and Crawley to handle her, joking that his "backstabbing sister" needed help with gardening, and had her thrown into the swamp's dirt.

Balancing Act[]

Upon witnessing a Croc Flare being launched, Cragger approached Crawley and Crug, perplexed as to why the crocodile tribe was gearing up for yet another confrontation, especially after he had previously commanded an end to hostilities. Unbeknownst to him, it was Crooler who had initiated the flare. Cragger's attention was drawn to Crooler's Persuader Plants. He was inadvertently exposed to one of the plants, leading to his mind being controlled. This compelled him to instruct Crawley and Crug to gather the Crocodile Tribe in response to the flare's signal.

Later, Crooler arrived at the swamp, having pilfered Chi from the Wolves, and sought to mend her relationship with Cragger by offering him the stolen energy source. Initially, Cragger was taken aback by the sheer amount of Chi. However, the Wolves show up, using grappling hooks to seize Crooler and drag her away. Witnessing his sister being treated so harshly angered Cragger, who vowed to take action against the Wolves, declaring his intent to "croc their world."

Later, as the Crocodiles launch a surprise attack at a canyon, disrupting the Wolves' pursuit of Laval, the Eagles, and Gorzan, Cragger rallied his warriors, charging headfirst into the fray. He boldly taunted Worriz, claiming that the Crocodiles also possess Chi and ridiculing the Wolves' assertion of dominance. Although his attempt at an insult was nonsensical, Cragger immediately ordered his forces to attack, beginning a battle between the two tribes. As the battle progressed, his warriors were defeated one by one until he was the last Crocodile standing, leading to his capture and imprisonment.

Crocodile Tears[]

Cragger dreamt of wandering through the Gorge of Eternal Depth, where he found his injured parents lying together. Crunket encouraged him to save Chima, while Crominus urged him to make them proud, but they vanished just as Crominus was about to give a warning. Taking their words to heart, Cragger vowed that the crocodiles would rule Chima forever, only to wake up as a captive in the Wolves' camp. Confused, he learned from Crawley and Crug about the recent events. Cragger had hoped they would devise a plan to escape and overthrow the Wolves, but instead, Crawley and Crug informed him that they secured him a spot in a kiddie pool given to them by the Wolves.

When Crooler was thrown into the cage, she informed Cragger that she had cleverly convinced the Wolves to provide the Crocodiles with luxuries by revealing that other tribes in Chima would launch a rescue and retaliate if the Crocodiles were harmed. Cragger admired her cunning and quickly adopted her approach, using a bell to demand warmer spa water. This led the Crocodiles to request increasingly lavish amenities, which annoyed the Wolves but also created the illusion that the prisoners were being treated exceptionally well while the Wolves focused on fixing their damaged equipment. That night, Cragger and his tribe watched a puppet show featuring a Crocodile and Winzar, where the Crocodiles wanted the Winzar puppet to be beaten by the Crocodile puppet. Winzar hesitated but was persuaded by Cragger's reminder of Skinnit, prompting him to comply. Later, while relaxing with Crug and ringing the camp bell, Cragger nonchalantly asked for the torches to be dimmed and warm milk for everyone in the tribe.

When Laval and Eris break into the Wolf Camp to save the Crocodile Tribe, Cragger showed little desire to leave, especially with the offer of warm milk. As Laval unlocked the cages and urged the Crocodile Tribe to flee, Cragger and the others reluctantly followed Laval and Eris in their escape.
