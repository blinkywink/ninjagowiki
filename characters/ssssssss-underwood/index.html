Agent SsssSsss Underwood[1] is a Hypnobrai agent of the Administration.

History

History[]

Background[]

Underwood was once part of the Serpentine, but she became an agent of the Administration. At one point, Underwood told her colleague Jay Walker about how their fellow agent Etha was fascinated with Merlopian dance rituals.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Prior to The Administration[]

At some point after the Ninja's quest to find the Dragon Cores and before Lloyd and Arin's arrival in the Administration, Underwood and her partner, Roderick Allen, investigated the Monastery of Spinjitzu and detained Zane for "too many failed portal activation attempts."

The Administration[]

Underwood and Allen came across Lloyd and Arin, demanding their forms before the latter tried to stall, until a tube containing papers arrived and they saw that they were forms with drawings of the two. Clearing them, both agents headed to their cubicle to file paperwork about the misunderstanding, where Underwood shortly discovered the deception upon discovering that identifications forms required subject photos. The two then filed out a "later date" form in order to search for Lloyd and Arin. When the two confronted the Ninja in the room where Zane was being held, Arin hurled the pencils that he had been collecting from other agents at the two, landing in front of the agents' feet just as they stepped forward and causing them to slip. When the three ninja escaped through one of the tubes, Underwood used one of the room's computers to reroute Arin and Zane to other destinations while Lloyd made his way to the Department of Demolitions.

Later, she and Roderick attempted to stop the Ninja as they attempted to flee through the halls of the Administration. After their attempts failed however, Roderick muttered that they would have to explain their failure, to which Underwood bemoaned would be, "So much paperwork."

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Fugitives From Madness[]

As Zane, Cole, Bonzle, and Jiro went to find Gandalaria in Mysterium, they passed through the Kingdom of Madness. Unbeknownst to them, their presence caught the attention of the Administration Agents, who held a grudge against Zane for his daring escape from the Administration alongside Arin and Lloyd.

Underwood would later accompany Roderick, and a force of other agents, to Mysterium to recapture Zane. After Zane offered to surrender, Underwood revealed that he would be condemned to the Department of Demolitions as being a Nindroid, Zane was considered mere equipment rather than a living being. When Cole dismissed the Agents and told his allies to just hop on Jiro and leave, Underwood snidely revealed that Jiro had been detained by the Agents, prompting Cole to demand his release. Roderick then ordered the agents to breach the Monastery of Mysterium. However, Zane informed the agents that in order to gain access to the monastery, they must complete the 35-C91 form. In response to this, Underwood flipped through her forms index to verify the truth of Zane's statement, admitting that they needed forms before breaching the monastery's walls.

Secrets of the Wyldness[]

Underwood and Roderick faced off against Cole in his Elemental Earth Mech, only to be quickly overpowered by Cole. After Cole mocked the Agents for being too easy, an annoyed Underwood detached her Dragon Mech from the Master Mech operated by Roderick in an attempt to even the odds. Unfortunately, she and her mech would be struck by lightning from Gandalaria, briefly knocking out the Serpentine and her mech. Some point after recovering, Zane ejected all the Administration Agents out of their mechs, including Underwood.

Agents All Around![]

During the ninja's raid on the Administration to return their technology, Underwood was also attacked.

The Shape of Motion[]

She and Roderick were seen on security duty. While Roderick admitted he despised it, Underwood replied that she enjoys security duty because she gets to crush dissent. Seconds later, she accused a yawning applicant of causing trouble before he explained himself, leaving Underwood disappointed. Some time later, Underwood and Roderick encountered Arin and Sora in the Department of Reassignment, leading Roderick to call for reinforcements while Underwood warned the teenagers they were armed with blasters and paperwork. Unfortunately, the intruders managed to escape through the use of a smoke bomb, leaving Underwood futilely fanning away the smoke with her paperwork while remarking that the teens' disappearance must have been due to "some kind of magic."

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • She and Prentis are named after the two youngest main characters from LEGO Nexo Knights, Robin Underwood and Ava Prentis.
  • She is the sixth known female Serpentine, the first five being Selma, Rivett, Machia, Aspheera, and Mikloshe.
  • She is also the second known female Hypnobrai with different color of their scales. The first is Selma.
  • Her first name, SsssSsss, seems to just be a snake's hiss.
  • She and Roderick Allen are the most frequently appointed Administration Agents, appearing in almost every episode featuring the Administration.
  • Her color scheme is similar to Pythor before being bleached.
