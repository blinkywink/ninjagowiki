Overview

“Everything we desire belongs to us by right of conquest!”
— Zarkt to Lloyd, "I Alone Can Save Them"

Zarkt was a corrupted anthropomorphic melanistic wolf,[1] a member of the Forbidden Five, Nokt and Rox's cousin,[5] and the Elemental Master of Misfortune. For millennia, he and his fellow members were imprisoned in the Nether-Space. During the Ritual of the Blood Moon, Ras planned to liberate all five members as part of his grand scheme by having them compete in the Tournament of the Sources; however, his efforts were only partially successful, resulting in the release of Nokt and inadvertently, Rox's soul, while Zarkt, Drix, and Kur remained imprisoned.

Throughout the Tournament of the Sources, Zarkt and his companions managed to establish a form of communication with Nokt and Rox while they were in pursuit of Kai and Bonzle. Ultimately, at the tournament's conclusion, Nokt succeeded in liberating Zarkt and the rest, albeit in a weakened state due to the method of their release. Together, Zarkt and the other four used a portal gate to escape to the Cloud Kingdom, subsequently destroying the portal gate to ensure their transition to a different realm.

Recovering from their weakened state, Zarkt and the others went to the First Land where Thunderfang is imprisoned and made a deal with the Shatter Dragon. Zarkt went after the blue Prismatic Blade, but he was unable to capture it from Lloyd, when Zane, P.I.X.A.L., and Zanth arrived. Thanks to Drix, he managed to use one of the Prismatic Blades to cut Thunderfang's final chains. However, Thunderfang betrayed the Five and took their souls after being freed.

After Thunderfang's defeat, Zarkt became a ghost under the Shatter Dragon’s control and was compelled to collect souls for Thunderfang's ritual to become a Source Dragon. While soul collecting, Zarkt went to the Administration and the Land of Lee, where he encountered the Ninja. Once Thunderfang collected enough souls, he sensed the Ninja nearing his mortal enemy, the Arc Dragon of Focus, and dispatched Zarkt to the Cave of Bells in Mysterium, where he fought Kai, Nya, and Jay. He eventually disappeared along with the rest of the Five after Thunderfang was defeated.

History

History[]

Background[]

Born into a Wolf Tribe in the Wyldness, Zarkt would abandon the tribe at some point with his cousins Nokt and Rox and go on to meet Kur and Drix within the Order of the Deep Shadow,[6] whom they joined forces with to form the Forbidden Five. Selected by Thunderfang, the Five were granted the elusive power of Chaos, allowing them to possess Elemental Powers and cultivate remarkable abilities, including a unique and corrupted form of Spinjitzu called Shatterspin, along with the use of Theroxian Magic. This enabled the group to exceed the capabilities of traditional Elemental Masters. The Five founded the Wolf Clan in order to transmit their teachings, combat techniques, and ideology to their supporters. However, the very powers that Zarkt and the Forbidden Five had cultivated began to corrupt them, eroding the inherent goodness within them and transforming the clan into agents of chaos.[7][8] Using Shatterspin, they wreaked havoc across the Wyldness, asserting their dominance through destruction and compelling the inhabitants to either submit to their rule or be killed.[9]

One day at the Shadow Dojo, Zarkt and rest of the Forbidden Five learned a grim aftermath of Nokt's recent campaign against a Tiger Tribe, where a survivor named Zarkar lamented the cruelty of their actions, only to be met with derisive laughter from Zarkt and his allies. As Nokt and Rox urged the group to extend their reign of terror into other realms,[9] a path that would ultimately lead to destruction for all,[10] for they were unexpectedly challenged by the Dragon Masters. Using Rising Dragon, the Dragon Masters effectively countered the Forbidden Five's Shatterspin, leading to their defeat and subsequent banishment to the Nether-Space through a powerful spell for millennia.[11]

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Rising Ninja[]

During the Ritual of the Blood Moon, when Jordana opened the gateway to the Nether-Space, Zarkt and the rest of the Forbidden Five gathered before the portal and watched as Nokt stepped forward to await a sacrifice.

Inside the Maze[]

Zarkt contacted Nokt and Rox, voicing his frustration over the delay as he remained stuck in Nether-Space. He pressed them to act quickly, highlighting that liberating the remaining members of the Forbidden Five would enable their merciless rule to resume without delay.

The Sword Shatters[]

Zarkt got into an argument with Kur, up to the point where he started shoving the latter before Drix stepped in and broke up the fight. The three then left the area, with Zarkt and Kur carrying Rox's soulless body (since she had escaped by transferring her soul into Jordana during the Ritual of the Blood Moon).

The Final Game[]

While Zarkt, Drix, and Kur were communicating with Nokt and Rox, Kai and Bonzle tried to sneak around the three. Kai accidentally kicked a rock, alerting them. Zarkt looked around the area but the two managed to stay out of sight. After this, Zarkt and the other two continued to communicate with Nokt and Rox.

Elements of Betrayal[]

Despite some interference from the ninja, Roby and Ras, Nokt is able to free Zarkt and the other members of the Forbidden Five, as well as Rox's physical body, from the Nether-Space. However, he and the others were weakened from the corrupted method used to bring them out. Rox carried Zarkt's body to the portal gate, where Nokt faced the Ninja and Ras until the three regained consciousness. Zarkt soon recovered and the Forbidden Five fled through a portal gate leading to the Cloud Kingdom, but the gate collapsed and sent them somewhere else.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Missing[]

Zarkt and the Five attacked two civilians to steal supplies for their journey to the First Land to unchain Thunderfang. During their assault, Zarkt used Misfortune to disable the truck while Kur used Decay on the truck's occupants, and Drix used Swarm to further drive them off so the civilians could spread the word that the Forbidden Five have returned.

The Spectral Lands[]

Zarkt and the others arrived in the Storm Village, where the Dragonians lived. The village leader Tyr greeted them but they tore their way through the village to find Thunderfang's prison. The group eventually discovered the prison and ventured inside. Much to their surprise, Thunderfang had been expecting their arrival. The Chaos Dragon then agreed to further empower the Five in exchange for his freedom, instructing them to recover five Prismatic Blades to shatter his chains. Thunderfang gave them the Sobrient Stone, a large gem from his ring, to aid in their search, which Zarkt carried with Kur outside of the Storm Mountain. There, Nokt used Shatterspin to break the gem, releasing the Shatter Dragon's energies to corrupt the Dragonians into warriors due to their species’ ability to sense Prismatic Steel, while granting Zarkt and the others the ability to fly.

I Alone Can Save Them[]

Zarkt and the Dragonian Warriors searched for a Prismatic Blade. Arriving somewhere in the First Land, he ordered his minions to fan out and find the blade. Unknown to Zarkt however, Lloyd and his students were hiding nearby. Shortly after the Master of Misfortune grew impatient with the warriors, he described the blade as being made of a shimmering metal, which alerted Frak of the Blue Prismatic Blade he recently retrieved from Dorama's puppet. Zarkt grabs a corrupted Tyr and complained that Thunderfang said he and the other soldiers could sense Prismatic Steel, and threw him. Tyr sensed the Blue Prismatic Blade nearby in the bushes, which surprised Zarkt when he saw that the Ninja were holding the blade. Zarkt then fought Lloyd, while the Trackers fought Sora, Frak, and Riyu.

After blocking a blade from Lloyd, Zarkt was briefly thrown back but got up to use Shatterspin. Hearing the stomping sound, Lloyd uses Spinjitzu to perform Rising Dragon and blows Zarkt back into a rock. Zarkt quickly recovers and charges at Lloyd. In a weapon lock, Zarkt claims the Prismatic Blade belongs to him, but Lloyd said it does not belong to the Forbidden Five. Zarkt counters by saying everything the Five desired belong to them through conquest. Lloyd drops the blade, which Zarkt attempts to reach for, but it turned out to be a trick for Lloyd to kick him. Quickly recovering, Zarkt uses Misfortune but Lloyd dodged his blasts and fires an Energy blast, which continues their fight. Zarkt managed to hold against Lloyd and the other Ninja but was overpowered when Zanth, P.I.X.A.L., and Zane arrived to further reinforce their allies. When Lloyd notices P.I.X.A.L., he fires an Energy blast, which briefly knocked Zarkt back but he recovers and battles P.I.X.A.L., who quickly defeats him and forces him and the Dragonian Warriors to retreat. Despite Zarkt's warning that he and the Five will find them and the Prismatic Blade, the Ninja didn't take his warning seriously.

Returning to the Storm Village, Zarkt reported his failure to Rox, who angrily reprimanded and threw him to the ground. She warned him that the "Forbidden Five" would become the "Forbidden Four" if he failed again. Kur returned shortly thereafter with the broken Yellow Prismatic Blade, and Zarkt listened on to Rox's plans of finding the last Djinn to repair the blade.

Chaos Unchained[]

Zarkt waited with the others until Drix sent one of his insects for help since the Ninja unexpectedly beat him to the First Land. Zarkt flew with the Five and they engaged the Ninja, attacking Kai head-on but was kicked from behind by Nya. Zarkt continued to battle Kai until Rox alerted them to free Thunderfang after she regained both the Blue and Returning Blades from Arin. Zarkt threw Kai away and flew into the tornado to use it as a distraction for the Ninja, who became caught in the Winds of Chaos. Heading into Thunderfang's prison, Rox gave Drix the Returning Blade and Zarkt the Blue Prismatic Blade. Once the Five got to Thunderfang, Zarkt used the Blue Prismatic Blade to free the Shatter Dragon. After Thunderfang was released, he unleashed a wind blast that blew Zarkt and the other members of the Five back. Rox then demanded the powers Thunderfang had promised, but the Chaos Dragon revealed that he had no intention of keeping his promise, much to Zarkt's shock. The dragon then absorbed his soul alongside the the rest of the Five's, killing them.

Casket of Bones[]

Zarkt was waiting at the Well of the Lost when Drix returned from the Administration and ejected the souls he absorbed.

Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon[]

Zarkt arrives at the Land of Lee to harvest souls with his magical flute. He later returned to the Well, where he adds in the stolen souls he gathered from the Land, which the Ninja saw him do.

For Whom the Bell Tolls[]

When Nokt and Rox transfer their stolen souls into the well, it erupted with power, and Zarkt watched as his unwanted master slowly became a Source Dragon.

Zarkt continued to watch as the Well of the Lost empowered Thunderfang further, growing a large claw. He floated with Rox in front of Drix, Nokt, and Kur when Thunderfang sensed someone is nearing the Arc Dragon of Focus. The Shatter Dragon realized the Ninja were at the resting places of the two halves and dared to wake his eternal enemy. He then ordered Zarkt and the rest of the Five to stop them, unaware that both Lloyd and Arin were in the Spectral Lands, waiting for the two halves to be awakened so they can use the Unbidden Storm.

Chaos Reigns[]

Zarkt arrived in the Cave of Bells in Mysterium, where he spotted Kai, Nya, and a coconut copy of Gandalaria. Kai brought out his Reveal Blade, which revealed the Spectral Dragonians. Zarkt sent the Spectral Dragonians to fight the three.

As the fight continues, Zarkt lands in the center, where he attempted to use Shatterspin to break the cave's center crystals, which Kai and Nya think are connected to the Arc Dragon of Focus. As Zarkt charges his Shatterspin, he flies up but was stopped by Nya, who used Rising Dragon, which pushed him to the ground.

He attempted again but was stopped by Nya. As he gets up to fight Nya, he got distracted when he heard the Arc Dragon of Focus' roar and saw a screen through the eyes of the Arc Dragon's purple half, which Nya used to her advantage by blasting Water in his face. As Kai and Nya noticed this, Zarkt gets up and quickly uses Shatterspin to fly up in another attempt to destroy the crystals but he ends up fighting Nya, who started to get exhausted from using Rising Dragon.

He managed to defeat Nya using Misfortune and knocked down Kai with a Shatterspin-powered kick when the Fire ninja attempted to attack him from behind, sending Spectral Dragonians after him when his enemy lost his Reveal Blade. Before Zarkt could finish Nya off, Jay arrives in his Mech Dragon, headbutting the wolf with the Mech Dragon. In response, the wolf ghost fought his opponent with Misfortune. Fighting Jay, Zarkt fires Misfortune, but he misses when Jay dodges it and Zarkt managed to dodge his smaller mech's sword. However, he was struck down by his opponent's elemental power of Lightning, being taken out for the rest of the fight.

Zarkt's soul disappeared with the rest of the Forbidden Five when the Arc Dragon of Focus defeated Thunderfang again.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Zarkt was an anthropomorphic wolf who has black skin with dark purple facial markings, red eyes, and yellow fangs protruding from the bottom of his mouth. His head was covered by a dark roningasa hat and he wears a black and dark purple flowing gown with a large green robe over it that has bright green trims, is covered in gold chains and lock, and bears a dark purple and black version of the Forbidden Five's symbol on the back. Presumably, he also wore black pants underneath his gown. However, after being hollowed and falling under Thunderfang’s command in the Spectral Lands, he took the form of a ghost, where his skin color changed to light blue and his eyes permanently glowed red.

Personality

Personality[]

“Why the delay? What's taking so long?”
— Zarkt to Nokt and Rox, "Inside the Maze"

Zarkt is an evil being who has no empathy towards his enemies like the rest of the Forbidden Five, such as when he and the others mockingly laughed at Zarkar, who was emotionally shocked after losing the majority of his tribe to them and their destructive power.

While being in the Nether-Space, he was shown to get easily frustrated, being the only trapped member who complained to Nokt and Rox's soul and then ordering the two to hurry and find a way to free him and the others during their exile. This is further proven when he shoved Kur during an argument over being patient for their release. However, when he heard rocks moving, he went to check the location but in his arrogance, he didn't bother to see Bonzle and Kai, who were hiding under an arch he and the other trapped members were standing on. Also, when he realized Sora was pretending to be unable to hear him after he failed to get the Prismatic Blade, he left with a growl. His frustration also made him impatient when looking for the blue Prismatic Blade with the Dragonians, grabbing Tyr's collar and ordering him to hurry. He also underestimated his opponents, including P.I.X.A.L. as he failed to claim the Blue Prismatic Blade. When telling Rox of his failure, Zarkt remained calm when he tried to explain that the ninja had the blade before he did but was ignored.

Like Thunderfang and the rest of the Five, Zarkt also has a sense of entitlement, such as when he backed his claim that everything they desire belongs to them by right of conquest. He is also very power-hungry, desiring the extra power Thunderfang had promised them once the Five free him. After Thunderfang revealed he was never going to fulfill his part of the deal, Zarkt showed visible shock, fear, and anger at this act of betrayal, but the Shatter Dragon reaped his soul before he could do anything.

After Thunderfang absorbed his soul, Zarkt became a Hollow One with complete obedience to his master. Like Nokt, he appeared to retain much of his personality as he was easily distracted by the focus crystals when they were first activated, which allowed Nya to get the drop on him and his arrogance to cloud his judgment to constantly attack the crystals head-on despite the Water Ninja's interference. He managed to hold against Jay's mech but failed to notice him at the last minute.

Weapons and abilities

Weapons and abilities[]

Abilities[]

  • Misfortune: Zarkt had the Elemental Power of Misfortune, allowing him to manipulate jinx and harm others with it.
  • Shatterspin: Since Zarkt was one of its creators, he possessed an advanced Shatterspin skillset. Zarkt is able to harness the powerful waves of the technique in a controlled manner of attack. He was also one of the few that don’t require the Gong of Shattering and a Wolf Mask. When activating the technique, he often stomps his feet about two or five times before his eyes glow red.
  • Theroxian Magic: Being a member of the Forbidden Five, Zarkt was highly skilled in Theroxian magic, but his abilities are currently unknown.
  • Wolf Physiology: As an anthropomorphic wolf from the Wyldness, Zarkt has the following abilities:
    • Night vision: Like a regular wolf, Zarkt possesses exceptional night vision, enabling him to see in dark environments like a cave or at night.
    • Superhuman strength: Zarkt demonstrated superhuman strength, being able to send Kai flying while in the air.
    • Superhuman durability/endurance: Zarkt has superhuman durability and endurance, as he managed to continue fighting Lloyd and the Ninja despite taking multiple hits from them. As a ghost, he managed to repeatedly survive hits from Nya, with Jay's Lightning being the only thing that took him out for good.
    • Superhuman speed/stamina: Zarkt can move at incredibly fast speed for a long time without tiring out.
    • Superhuman agility: Zarkt has superhuman agility, able to make quick turns and maneuvers even while airborne.
    • Superhuman reflexes: Zarkt has superhuman reflexes, as he managed to keep up with his enemies in combat, although he is much slower and dull than his comrades.
    • Superhuman leaping: Zarkt can jump incredibly high distances.
  • Skilled combatant: As a member of the Forbidden Five, Zarkt was a skilled combatant, as he managed to easily knock Kai away, match against Lloyd in a fight, and defeat Nya. However, in his first fight with the Ninja, he was unable to keep up with P.I.X.A.L., who had the upper hand thanks to Zane’s knowledge of his style from Nokt, forcing him to retreat. This may have been a side effect of his escape from the Nether-Space and the portal gate explosion, which hindered his abilities. When fighting the ninja outside Thunderfang's prison in the First Land, he easily managed to defeat Kai head on and briefly fight him and Nya with the help of Kur. In the Cave of Bells, he managed to easily defeat Kai, Nya, and a Coconut copy of Gandalaria and even match Jay's mech.
  • Leadership: As one of the leaders of the Wolf Clan, Zarkt led the wolf warriors to launch attacks wherever the Forbidden Five wanted in the Wyldness. In the present, he led a platoon of Dragonian Trackers to hunt the Blue Prismatic Blade, but was unsuccessful due to his impatience. He later led Spectral Dragonians to capture souls in the Land of Lee.
  • Chaos empowerment: Because Zarkt gained powers from Thunderfang's dark dragon energy, he can gain more from Thunderfang.
    • Flight/Levitation: After receiving energy from the Sobrient Stone, Zarkt gained the ability to fly and levitate in the air while standing still.
    • Weatherly Presence: Zarkt could generate a field that triggers chaotic storms, creating a dark weather phenomenon merely through his presence.
  • Ghost physiology: As a ghost, Zarkt has the following abilities:
    • Immortality: Zarkt is unable to die naturally due to lacking a mortal body.
    • Intangibility: Zarkt could phase through solid matter.
    • Flight/Levitation: Zarkt is permanently suspended in the air and can freely move through it and control his altitude.
    • Spectral Perception: Zarkt can perceive hidden spiritual energy.
    • Spectrokinesis: Zarkt can manipulate and control other ghosts, such as the Spectral Dragonians.
    • Soul absorption: Zarkt can absorb and store the souls of another individual via his Magical flute.
    • Cold Presence: Zarkt can make the temperature drop through his presence.
      • Caligogenesis: Zarkt's presence can also cause a cold fog to appear.
    • Electronic Disruption: Zarkt can cause electronics shut down.

Weapons[]

  • Flail: Zarkt wielded a flail as his primary weapon.
  • Blue Prismatic Blade: After recovering it in battle, Zarkt used the blade to cut the chain on Thunderfang's right leg.
  • Magical flute: After Zarkt fell under Thunderfang's control, he used a magical flute to absorb souls for Thunderfang.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Arrogance: Zarkt has a tendency to underestimate his opponents similar to Nokt and Rox, as he underestimated P.I.X.A.L and ended up being defeated by her, resulting in his failure to obtain the Blue Prismatic Blade and making him prone to Sora's trick of not hearing him, and in "Chaos Reigns", his arrogance often clouded his judgment as he constantly attacked the Focus crystals head-on despite Nya's interference with him.
  • Impatience/Temper: Zarkt is known for his impatience and explosive temper, as he often gets frustrated when things don't go his way and makes poor decisions. Another example of this is when he shoved Kur during an argument which could have gotten violent had Drix not stepped in and broke up the fight between the jackal and the wolf, and when searching for the Prismatic Blades, he furiously grabbed Tyr's collar and shoved the Dragonian aside to make him hurry and find the Blue Prismatic Blade. When he failed to obtain the blade, he flew off with an angry growl.

Etymology

Etymology[]

In real life, Zarkt is the name of a Moroccan commune.

According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, the name Zarkt was chosen as it sounded evil, due all the plosive sounds.[13]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Zarkt wore a roningasa hat, which never appeared in the show before his appearance. His hat also stands out the most among the rest of the Forbidden Five due to its basket-like shape.
  • Zarkt was the only member of the Five to retain the weapon he used in Bonzle's depiction, since the other four members switched to new weapons starting in Season 3.
  • He was the only member of the Forbidden Five to not successfully obtain a Prismatic Blade.
  • He was shown to be at least somewhat dim-witted, as he fell for Sora's trick of pretending to be unable to hear him.
  • Zarkt shares some similarities with Jay:
    • Both utilize chained weapons.
    • Both are voiced by Michael Adamthwaite.
    • Zarkt's Misfortune abilities appeared similar to that of Jay's Lightning.
    • Zarkt and Kur tended to argue, which was first seen in "The Sword Shatters."[14] His habit of arguing with Kur could be seen as a foil to Jay's and Cole's close friendship.
  • When asked what Zarkt and Kur brought to the Forbidden Five, Chris "Doc" Wyatt responded with "style".[15]
  • He is one of Jay’s least favorite people,[16]
  • When asked about Zarkt's species, the writers said he was "cut from the same cloth as Nokt and Rox," which implies he is also a wolf like them.[1]
  • Zarkt's fur is similar to that of the Alexander Archipelago wolf.
  • Zarkt doesn't wear his gown when cloaked.
    • He's the only member of the Forbidden Five to wear different clothes under his cloak.
  • Zarkt is the only member of the Forbidden Five who has never been shown smiling.
    • However, he did laugh in the flashback of “The Final Game”, which means he also likely smiled there.
  • According to Doc Wyatt, Zarkt and Rox have the strongest bond of the Forbidden Five, besides the latter and her brother.[17]
  • He is the only Forbidden Five member who have a dual faceprint in minifigure form because the back of his head is hidden.
  • When asked on how Zarkt is related to Nokt and Rox, due to being from the same tribe, Chris "Doc" Wyatt replied that they are 'cousins'.[18]
  • Zarkt can howl like a real wolf if he wanted to. [19]
  • He is the only member of the Forbidden Five to have not personally met Lord Ras.

Relationships

These are the relationships of Zarkt, the master of Misfortune, a member of the Forbidden Five and a former leader of the Wolf Clan.

Allies

Allies[]

Forbidden Five[]

The other members of the Forbidden Five, aside from Nokt and Rox, are equals to Zarkt. Though they did not show any care for each other, Kur, Zarkt and Drix all worked together in the Nether-Space to escape and continue their brutal dominance over the realms. Zarkt was the most verbal of Nokt and Rox's slow progress and urged them to be quick so they could continue to dominate the realms. During their imprisonment, Zarkt got into a fight with Kur but was separated by Drix. Following their escape from Nether-Space and the City of Temples, Zarkt and the Forbidden Five set in motion their plan to obtain the five Prismatic Blades to free Thunderfang. Unlike the other Five, Zarkt failed to secure his Blade, angering Rox, who knocked him to the ground and threatened to kill him if he failed again. Zarkt also has a strain relationship with Kur, as they have a habit of arguing with each other, which was first seen in "The Sword Shatters". Despite Rox threatning to kill him, Co-creator Doc Wyatt did reveal that Rox and Zarkt have the strongest bond out of the Forbidden Five on BlueSky.

Wolf warriors (formerly)[]

Before his imprisonment, Zarkt was one of the leaders of the Wolf Clan and led the wolf warriors to launch attacks wherever the Forbidden Five wanted in the Wyldness.

Thunderfang (unwillingly)[]

As with the rest of the Forbidden Five, Zarkt believed that Thunderfang would grant him greater powers in exchange for freedom. Believing the Shatter Dragon, Zarkt dutifully carried out his task in retrieving the Primsatic Blades. Unfortunately, he and the rest of the Five would be betrayed by Thunderfang, who then absorbed his and the others' souls to strengthen himself after a millennia of captivity.

Following his transformation into a ghost, Zarkt was bound to Thunderfang's will, serving his master's orders and whims without question or hesitation.

Dragonian Trackers (formerly)[]

Zarkt, like the rest of the Five, did not care about the Dragonian Trackers and only used them to track down the Prismatic Blades to free Thunderfang. While in the First Land searching for the Blue Prismatic Blade, Zarkt showed to be more demanding, as shown when he physical grabbed Tyr's neck before shoving him.

Spectral Dragonians[]

After Zarkt fell under Thunderfang's control, he led the Spectral Dragonians to capture souls of the people in the Land of Lee, Roderick Allen and Lee. He later led the Spectral Dragonians towards the Cave of bells where he fought Kai, Nya and Jay.

Enemies

Enemies[]

Dragon Masters[]

Because Egalt and Rontu were the ones who trapped Zarkt and his allies in the Nether-Space, Zarkt likely has a huge grudge against them.

Ninja[]

Zarkt first met the Ninja in "I Alone Can Save Them". Even though he does not know them as well as Nokt and Rox does, Zarkt considers them as his enemies, and fought them in a desert somewhere in the First Land and fought Lloyd, Sora, Frak, and Riyu over the Blue Prismatic Blade, although he ultimately failed when Zane, P.I.X.A.L., and Zanth arrived. He later encountered them again in "Chaos Unchained" where he fought them again to obtain all the Prismatic Blades to free Thunderfang. He manage to fight both Kai and Nya. He later fought Kai and Nya again in "Chaos Reigns" at the Cave of Bells in an attempt to destroy the crystal that are connected to the Arc Dragon of Focus. During the fight he brutally used Shatterspin in the battle and almost killed Nya using Misfortune before Jay interfered with his Mech Dragon.

Dragonians[]

Zarkt and his colleagues were merciless with the the peaceful Dragonian people, by ransacking their village to find Thunderfang's prison, not hesitating to be violent with them, and then by corrupting them through Thunderfang's dark energy to make them their personal army to find the Prismatic Blades.
