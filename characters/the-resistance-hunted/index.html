Overview

This article is about the resistance formed against Emperor Garmadon. For other uses, see The Resistance.
“I may not have Elemental Power, but I call upon the power of the people. When we get knocked down, we rise up. Stronger than before. This is our city. He thinks he can bully us with stone giants. Well, this isn't the first time our city has faced ruin. Buildings fall, but we rise! And to you, Father, I say this: fighting me may make you feel powerful, but you're about to see what real power is. The Resistance never quits!”
— Lloyd to the citizens of Ninjago City, "Radio Free Ninjago"

The Resistance was a team that Lloyd and Nya formed after the assumed deaths of the rest of the original ninja (Kai, Jay, Cole, and Zane) and Wu. The team was formed to oppose the newly-resurrected Lord Garmadon, Harumi, and the Sons of Garmadon in a bid to end their tyrannical rule over Ninjago City.

Lloyd and Nya were almost kidnapped by Garmadon's forces, but then the other Elemental Masters who partook in the Tournament of Elements saved them, making good on their promise of helping the ninja if the need arose.

In retaliation for breaking into Borg Industries, the Sons of Garmadon raid the base and arrest everyone except Lloyd, Nya, Skylor, Dareth, and Mystake, sending them to Kryptarium Prison. Mystake was killed by Garmadon while attempting to have Skylor absorb his power.

As a result of Garmadon losing his power and reign of Ninjago, the Resistance dissolved with their goal successful.

History

History[]

Background[]

After Destiny's Bounty was destroyed by the Colossus, Wu, the original ninja—Cole, Kai, Jay, and Zane—were all presumed deceased. Unbeknownst to their allies, however, they survived and were all transported to the first realm in existence.[1]

Though struck with despair at his loss, Lloyd regrouped with Nya, Misako, and P.I.X.A.L. with the intention of fighting another day. In the meantime, Lord Garmadon's tyrannical rule over Ninjago City strengthened, as Harumi and the Sons of Garmadon maintained their hold on the city. In response to this and the loss of the ninja, Lloyd went into hiding to train.[2]

Firstbourne[]

While hiding, they are hunted by Mr. E and Killow as well as their grunts. The group discovers the ninja was not killed but teleported to somewhere before Garmadon's forces close in. They are then saved by the Elemental Masters.

Iron & Stone[]

Returning to their hideout, Skylor introduced Lloyd to the rest of the Resistance, which consisted of most of the other Elemental Masters and Mystake. After some brief introductions, Lloyd spoke with Mystake about the whereabouts of the Stranded Ninja and their current circumstances now that Garmadon ruled over Ninjago City. Meanwhile, Nya and the rest of the Elemental Masters began constructing an armored vehicle to use in combat against the SOG.

Radio Free Ninjago[]

With the help of the Resistance, Lloyd advertised an announcement of hope to all the people with the help of his allies. However, this led to the group's location to be discovered by Harumi.

The Gilded Path[]

Garmadon led an ambush of his followers on the garbage depot. This resulted in most of the members being captured and sent to Kryptarium Prison, though Lloyd and a few others manage to invade this.

Green Destiny[]

These members were rallied from prison when the stranded ninja returned, and they assisted in restraining the Colossus until Garmadon lost his power.

Members

Members[]

  • Lloyd Garmadon, the team's leader; also the Green Ninja, the Elemental Master of Energy, and the son of Lord Garmadon. He regains his power by refusing to fight his father, resulting in his father losing his powers.
  • Nya, the team's second-in-command and Lloyd's unofficial lieutenant; also the Elemental Master of Water and the sister of Kai. She is also the girlfriend of Jay and close friend to Cole and Zane.
  • P.I.X.A.L., also known as Samurai X; aNindroid created by Cyrus Borg, who helps the ninja on their missions. She's also the love interest of Zane and the team's third-in-command and Lloyd's backup protector and friend.
  • Misako, the wife of the newly-resurrected Lord Garmadon, mother of Lloyd, and history informant.
  • Dareth, a great ally of the ninja who recently provided a safe haven for Misako and Wu, and self-proclaimed "Brown Ninja."
  • Skylor, the Elemental Master of Amber and the daughter of the late Master Chen. She eventually turned on her father and helped the ninja. She is Kai's girlfriend and a great ally to the ninja.
  • Mr. Pale, the Elemental Master of Light, who competed in the Tournament of Elements.
  • Karlof, the Elemental Master of Metal, who competed in the Tournament of Elements.
  • Griffin Turner, the Elemental Master of Speed, who competed in the Tournament of Elements.
  • Shade, the Elemental Master of Shadow, who competed in the Tournament of Elements.
  • Tox, the Elemental Master of Poison, who competed in the Tournament of Elements.
  • Neuro, the Elemental Master of Mind, who competed in the Tournament of Elements.
  • Mystake, an Oni, an ally of the ninja who owns a tea shop and knew the location of the other ninja and Wu, who were thought to have perished. She was killed off-screen by Garmadon.
  • Ronin, an on/off ally of the ninja who was captured by the Sons of Garmadon. While he does not join The Resistance before the ninja return, he joins after the mass breakout and helps take down the Colossus.
  • Police Commissioner, the chief of the Ninjago City Police Department and ally of the Ninjago. Though, like Ronin, he does not join The Resistance during their founding, having been captured beforehand, he escaped with them in a mass breakout and assisted in destroying the Colossus.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • This is the second team formed by ninja to replace team members, the first being the Ninja Replacements in the erased timeline of Skybound, formed by Jay. Skylor, Ronin, and Dareth were members of both teams.
  • Most of the Elemental Masters also return and join the team for the first time since their last appearance in the Tournament of Elements (aside from Skylor, Griffin Turner, Tox, and Karlof who have appeared after Season 4).
  • All the captured members escape Kryptarium in "Green Destiny."
  • The Elemental Masters absent are Ash, Bolobo, Gravis, Jacob Pevsner, and Chamille.
    • Although Chamille became a "bad girl" according to Karlof.[3]
    • Ninjago Magazine issues 40 and 43 show Bolobo, Gravis and Ash as a part of the resistance, but this is presumed non-canon.
    • Ash's and Jacob's fates are unknown since Ninjago: Dragons Rising introduced new masters for their elements.
