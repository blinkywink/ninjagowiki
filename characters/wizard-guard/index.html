History

History[]

Background[]

In the year 45 A.L., the Wizard Guard thought he had a spell for normal-sized heads, but it failed. As a result, people began refusing to listen to him. The wizard was present when his people were thrown into the hundred-year war after circumstances caused the twin cyclopes Torex and Xerot to become angry, which he found most inconvenient.[1]

The Hollow Ones[]

When Ras, Sora and Arin arrived at Abracalabria, the Wizard Guard blocked their way alongside with the other wizards, Torex and Xerot. The Wizard Guard welcomed, only to say strangers are no longer welcomed. When Ras said he seek an audience with the Pontifex Magicus, the Wizard Guard replied by saying maybe Ras' homeland is too wild for his sensibilities, saying that they are not welcomed no longer. When Ras attacked them, the Wizard Guard and the other wizards pulled their crystal balls, ready to fight back. As the fight continued, the Wizard Guard tried to zap Ras, only for Ras to dodge it and eventually enter the village, only to encounter the hollowed inhabitants of Abracalabria. When Ras questioned the current state of the village, the Wizard Guard this could have been avoided if he had listened to him, but no one listens to him after the head shrinking incident. When Ras asked what he is talking about, the Wizard Guard told him about the incident, only for Ras to say he was asking about what happened to the villagers. The Wizard Guard said they were attacked. Then, the remaining villagers questioned about how they know they aren't the ones that attacked them, the Wizard Guard got suspicious of Ras, Arin and Sora, only for Gandalaria to appear and clear the situation. Then, Kur come back to the village to finish the job, as the Wizard Guard and the others watched.

Human Resources[]

When Kur attacked Abracalabria again, the Wizard Guard sees cold air coming out of Sora's mouth and he says they came back. After that, he got hollowed and started walking mindlessly.

Chaos Reigns[]

The Wizard Guard is seen unhollowed along with the rest of Abracalabria after Thunderfang is defeated.
