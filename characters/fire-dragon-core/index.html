The Fire Dragon is a dragon owned by Kai.

History

History[]

Balance[]

When the ninja arrived at the pyramid, the Fire Dragon stood on the opposite side of the lava along with the green dragon the ninja fought earlier and a blue dragon. After the green dragon left through a portal, the Fire Dragon roared so loud it caused a stalactite to fall from the ceiling and into the lava. Cole was able to make it to the other side, but realized the dragon were not enemies when the Fire Dragon licked him playfully. The dragons then assisted the ninja who were struggling to get across with the Fire Dragon helping Jay and Zane.

Wisdom[]

Kai rode the dragon and used to attack the Whack Rats blocking the ninja's path to the finish line. After Lloyd purposely got shot by one of the Whack Rats and respawned near the finish line, Kai purposely gives himself up by allowing his dragon to be shot. The dragon respawned near the finish line and Cole, Kai, and Lloyd rode it through the next portal.

Honesty[]

Kai rode his dragon to joust a Blizzard Samurai, a match which he won.

Courage[]

Kai rode his dragon to where Wu was being held hostage. When Lloyd asked him if the lessons were real, Wu told him that reality was all in one's perception, looking at the roof of the monastery where all the three dragons were.

LEGO.com description

LEGO.com description[]

Soar into the sky with Kai and his Fire Dragon to combat two evil snake warriors – but be careful! One is controlling a shooting drone, while the other has a jetpack and a chain gun. Upgrade the dragon twice to make it bigger, stronger and quicker, for this must-win battle to protect NINJAGO® City. For completing your mission, you will be rewarded with a collectible Courage banner.[1]
