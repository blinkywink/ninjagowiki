Overview

“Yes, I know who you are. You are Vex, the Formless. I know of your lies. Formlings have never troubled us and we will not trouble them. Give him food, water, and a decent cloak and send him on his way, before his jealousy sours us all.”
— Grimfax after Vex attempted to persuade him, "Corruption"

Grimfax is the king of the Never Kingdoms. Previously, he was overthrown by the Ice Emperor and Vex. After he surrendered to join their army so he wouldn't get killed, he subsequently became a high-ranking Blizzard Warrior that led battles. He later rebelled and sided with Lloyd and Kataru to overthrow Zane. After Zane regained his memories and destroyed the scepter that resulted in him returning to being human, Grimfax reclaimed his throne after a long time.

History

History[]

Corruption[]

When Grimfax was once the king of the Never Kingdoms, his Warriors caught a trespassing Vex, who is brought before Grimfax. Vex tries to persuade Grimfax to attack the Formlings, but Grimfax refuses and has him thrown out.

Later, Vex returns with an amnesiac Zane who Vex proclaims as the rightful ruler. Disgusted by this audacity, Grimfax orders for them to be thrown out. However, Zane easily defeats Grimfax's Warriors with his powers, putting them under his control. Vex then gives a choice to Grimfax: either he submits to Zane's power or perish to death. With the odds against him, Grimfax is forced to surrender to Zane, who becomes the new ruler of the Never Realm (known as the Ice Emperor) while Vex becomes the new General.

Ever since then, Grimfax (presumably turned into a Blizzard Samurai) was forced to acknowledge the Ice Emperor's power and enforced his reign across the realm for decades, something that he himself truly regretted.

The Never-Realm[]

Upon Vex informing the Ice Emperor about the ninja's presence at the Ice Fishers' village, the Ice Emperor orders Vex to send Grimfax and the Blizzard Samurai after the ninja with the order of destroying them and the Ice Fishers. Grimfax is seen leading the Blizzard Samurai out from the Castle of Ice.

Fire Maker[]

As they made their way to the Great Lake, Grimfax directed the Blizzard Samurai, briefly pausing to observe the birds taking flight. Undeterred, they pressed on until reaching their destination, where Grimfax commanded the Blizzard Samurai to engage in battle against the Ice Fishers and the ninja.

Grimfax lunged at Kai, knocking him to the ground, and questions his defiance against the Ice Emperor. He attempted to strike Kai with his axe, but Kai evaded and grabbed a bamboo stick to defend himself. However, Grimfax's axe cuts it to pieces. Lloyd blocked Grimfax's strike with his sword, while Cole uses his Earth Punch to send Grimfax flying. Lloyd confidently declared the battle over, but Grimfax, noticing the Hearth Fire, accused Sorla of sheltering the ninja and warned them of the consequences of defying the Ice Emperor. Grimfax smashed through the roof and extinguished the fire with his axe. He proclaimed their victory in the battle but asserted that without the Hearth Fire, they will soon perish. Grimfax then ordered a retreat, and the Blizzard Samurai fall back.

Secret of the Wolf[]

Grimfax and his men return to the castle, with him stepping forward to report their victory. However, Vex is quick to tell him that they actually failed after displaying that the fire still remains, and that their numbers are lacking. Grimfax is stunned to learn this and tries to explain. The Ice Emperor is displeased at this and starts to freeze Grimfax, while Vex persuades him to carry out severe punishment. However, the Ice Emperor stops midway and tells Grimfax that he will atone for his mistake, much to Grimfax's relief. Following this, the Ice Emperor ordered Grimfax and his men out.

My Enemy, My Friend[]

Grimfax watches Vex and two Blizzard Warriors bringing Lloyd into the Emperor's throne room, where Lloyd discovers the Ice Emperor's real identity.

A Fragile Hope[]

Grimfax listens to Lloyd's pleas to the Ice Emperor and learns he was once a good man.

When Vex leaves the dungeon after taunting Lloyd, Vex nearly spots Grimfax, who was hiding but doesn’t see him. Making his way to the dungeon, Grimfax confers with Lloyd about his friendship with Zane and questions if he could get through to him. After Lloyd confirms this, he opens the prison doors for Lloyd and Kataru while releasing their cuffs. They form an alliance to overthrow Zane and Vex while calling themselves The Resistance.

Once and for All[]

The rebels escaped into the halls of the castle, and Grimfax told them to truly reach Zane that they needed to destroy Vex's crystal since it would give them away.

As they traveled, Grimfax believed Vex doesn't see them coming, stating that if Vex did see them, they wouldn't have gotten this far. However, Vex had anticipated this and had the Blizzard Samurai attack Kataru and Grimfax, while the latter told Lloyd to confront Zane. Grimfax and Kataru then valiantly fight off the soldiers.

Awakenings[]

Grimfax and Kataru fought the soldiers, eventually being defeated by the overwhelming numbers. Vex mocked them both, before ordering the two to be placed in the dungeon.

Zane eventually regains his memories due to Vex taunting Lloyd by saying he couldn't protect his friends. He then destroys his scepter.

With the Ice Emperor gone, Grimfax took back to power as King and was celebrated by his followers. He later consulted with the Formlings on what to do about Vex, before it was decided that he be banished to the outskirts and to never be seen again.

Personality

Personality[]

Originally, Grimfax was a benevolent ruler and did not oppose anyone. This was seen when he rejected Vex's proposal to unnecessarily attack the Village of the Formlings because they did nothing to deserve it. He also told his men to give Vex food and supplies when kicking him out, despite the latter's anger.

After being overthrown, Grimfax submitted to Vex and Zane's demands when his life was threatened by them. He followed their orders without question, as he attacked the Great Lake and ninja on their command. However, upon the revelation that the Ice Emperor was once a good man, he aligned with Lloyd and Kataru to overthrow Vex and his master.

In battle, he appears to have a tactical mindset, as he snuffed out the Hearth Fire of the Great Lake after seeing he couldn't beat the ninja and Ice Fishers in battle and knew the cold would affect them later, although Kai reignited it later on. He also chose to destroy Vex's crystal, so his group could have a chance to surprise Vex.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The moment when Grimfax was nearly punished is similar to Harumi being choked by Garmadon in "How to Build a Dragon."
    • Grimfax freeing Lloyd and Kataru from their cells after he found out that Zane was once a good person was also similar to Faith helping the ninja after she heard that Wu is the son of the First Spinjitzu Master.
  • He is the only named Blizzard Samurai aside from Vex. Additionally, they both wear a helmet in the same color.
  • He is the only Blizzard Warrior that is not corrupted and instead served Vex and Zane out of fear of being destroyed.
  • He is the second king to appear in season 11, the first being King Mambo V.
  • As a Blizzard Warrior, his signature weapon is an axe. Ironically, his voice actor, Brian Drummond, voiced a character named Axl from the Nexo Knights line whose signature weapon is also an axe and is named after said weapon. Their voices are also similar.
  • He is also known as "King Long Beard" in Samurai Storm!.
    • He is referred to as "Frost Beard" in the captions on the Cartoon Network website.[citation needed]
      • These are despite the fact that Grimfax does not have a beard in human or Blizzard Warrior form.
