Overview

Mord[1] is a Munce, the Elemental Master of Surface Tension and a competitor of the Tournament of the Sources, alongside the other Elemental Masters of her generation.

History

History[]

At some point in time, Mord was granted the Elemental Power of Surface Tension as a result of the Merge when the Source Dragon of Strength created it.[2]

Enter the City of Temples[]

Mord was at the Games Plaza when Roby presents the City of Temples to the ninja.

They Gather for the Feast[]

Mord arrives in a hurry after Lloyd defeats the attacker bot.

Inside the Maze[]

Mord fought walking the streets nearby where Nya thought she saw Jay.

Later, she trained against Teelk with Gorge training nearby. The Elemental Master of Sound pops the bubbles she's created with a soundwave. Soon, she watches alongside the other two Elemental Masters in surprise as Wyldfyre becomes outraged by Cinder.

During the first match of the tournament, she cheered from the stands as Zane and Zur fight.

United We Fall[]

She was was cheering from the stands when Sora defeated Kizzy in a battle.

The Sword Shatters[]

Mord faced off against Zeatrix and lost, thus transferring her powers via the Elemental Cup.

The Final Game[]

Mord passed Bleckt shortly before Wyldfyre saved him from a falling brick. Shortly thereafter, she walked by Wyldfyre after she stole Bleckt's ledger and got confused by its writings. Eventually, she met up with Henjamin and becomes absorbed in conversation.

She later cheered in the stands during the final match of the Tournament of the Sources.

Soon thereafter, Mord watched in surprise when Wyldfyre arrived with Zur to expose Bleckt after Nokt defeated Sora.

Elements of Betrayal[]

Mord fought alongside several other Elemental Masters against Cinder and the Wolf Mask Warriors in hand-to-hand combat. She later got her powers back when Roby drained Nokt of the stolen Elemental Powers and returned them to their respective masters and defeated the Wolf Warriors. She later watched Roby declare Sora the default winner of the tournament.
