Overview

“So you can't lie, huh? Oh! You pee in a swimming pool?”
— Luke to Cole, "The Oni and the Dragon"

Luke Cunningham, also known as the Man in the White Mask,[1] is a member of the Sons of Garmadon. He is an avid bike rider, often wearing a tight sleeveless vest. On one occasion, he was seen with a white ninja suit with a biker helmet.

History

History[]

Sons of Garmadon[]

The Mask of Deception[]

Luke was one of the Sons of Garmadon that infiltrated Borg Tower. He was the one who accidentally triggered the alarm, and was ultimately left behind by Mr. E in order to escape from Lloyd. He was then arrested by the police.

The Oni and the Dragon[]

After being arrested, Luke was brought into the interrogation room in the police precinct, where Cole and Zane attempted to use the Tea of Truth to interrogate and make Luke utter the truth.

When Cole accidentally mixes up the regular tea and the Tea of Truth, it causes him to utter the truth instead, causing Luke to ask him ridiculous questions as well as to get more information about the ninja. Later, they then set off for a Sons of Garmadon hangout Luke accidentally mentioned.

Brawl of the Jungle![]

Luke was released from custody and went with the other bikers to the Primeval's Eye. When the gang left the jungle on the Destiny's Bounty, Luke decided to stay to tame the giant crab for their gang and earn Harumi's approval. The Ninja easily defeat the bikers using the vines from the trees, but Luke is able to free the monster crab from the spiders' web. He manages to knock out the crab temporarily with his bike katanas. Although before he can escape with the giant crab, the Ninja lure him into a trap with which they destroy his motorcycle and he escapes empty-handed.

Dread on Arrival[]

Luke returned to the Ninjago City. As he was patrolling the grounds of the Temple of Resurrection at the Royal Palace, he discovered some scuba gear. As he was about to inform one of the generals that there are intruders on the premises, he was knocked out by Nya and dragged behind a bush. He was presumably found and arrested by the police.

True Potential[]

Luke was freed from Kryptarium Prison by Harumi and the newly-resurrected Lord Garmadon. After turning the prison into a bunker, Luke served as a lookout near the prison entrance, observing Lloyd and notifying the rest of the Sons of Garmadon of the Green Ninja's approach.

Hunted[]

Radio Free Ninjago[]

Luke and another guard listen to Killow talking before Harumi tells them to keep an eye out. Killow suddenly feels like doing karaoke due to a Mind trick from Neuro. Later at Laughy's, they're interrupted by Lloyd's speech and immediately head back to Borg Tower.

The Gilded Path[]

He is ordered by Killow to catch Mystake. When he asks why, as she is just an old lady, Killow replies she is so much more. Luke goes to get her but finds nothing but a rat running from the room. He returns empty-handed.

Green Destiny[]

Despite not appearing in the episode, Luke was presumably arrested following Emperor Garmadon's defeat and sent back to Kryptarium.

After March of the Oni[]

At some point after the Liberation of Ninjago, the remnants of the Sons of Garmadon, including Luke, became a group of street thugs, aimlessly robbing banks, terrorizing local businesses, and bullying young children in Garmadon's name. Without a strong leader to rally behind however, they were easily defeated by the Ninja and sent to Kryptarium Prison. For a long time, the remnants of the SoG, including Luke, trained and prepared themselves, believing that one day their lord would come for them. He never did. Following the Oni Invasion, the remnants of the SoG learned through the NGTV channel that Garmadon had allied himself with the Ninja to defeat the Oni, angering the remnants who felt betrayed, abandoned, and forgotten by their former leader. Suddenly, Lord Mogra broke into Kryptarium and freed the remnants of the SoG, reforming them under his leadership as the Red Crows.

Garmadon[]

Sins of the Past[]

Under the leadership of Mogra, Luke participated in the razing of Two Moon Village. After Garmadon blasted Killow through the roof of a villager's home, Luke and a few other bikers gathered around to discover the source of the commotion. To their shock and fear, they watched as Garmadon emerged from the burning building, vowing to punish the former Sons of Garmadon for betraying him. In response, Luke begged for Garmadon to forgive them. He would later watch the duel between Mogra and Garmadon.

Secret of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

The Absolute Worst[]

In Ultra Violet's story, the Sons of Garmadon, including Luke, were fighting Lloyd and Nya.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • His LEGO.com page and description were deleted a few days after they got published for unknown reasons.[citation needed]
  • His hairpiece is from another LEGO theme called Exo Force. It was only released in green, purple and red, but Luke's is white with a black stripe.
  • In sets, Luke wears black shoulder pads and black biker helmet, but in the series he lacks both.
    • Because of that, Luke looks like Skip Vicious in the sets, but in the series, it is revealed that they have different hairpieces.
  • His face looks somewhat like Jay's before Season 8 and Unagami, in part to a notch in his right eyebrow too.
  • Despite the fact that he's called "the Man in the White Mask," his mask's main color is gray and only the detailing is white. This is unlike most of the other masked Sons of Garmadon members from "The Mask of Deception," whose masks were their respective colors.
  • He is the character to have been arrested the most known times by the police, as he got arrested in "The Mask of Deception" for participating in the heist of the Oni Mask of Vengeance, and later in "Dread on Arrival," he was arrested for participating in the ritual to resurrect Garmadon, and finally he got arrested in "Green Destiny" for his many crimes during the Sons of Garmadon's occupation of Ninjago City.
  • Luke likes to eat pepperoni pizza and chocolate ice cream but does not like to drink tea.
