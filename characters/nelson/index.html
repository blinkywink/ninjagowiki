Overview

“Master Wu, you once told me that the important thing isn't winning or losing. It's never quitting.”
— Nelson to Wu, "Quittin' Time!"

Nelson, also referred to as Lil' Nelson, is a paperboy that lives in a village outside of Ninjago City. He broke both of his legs while rigorously training to be a ninja,[1] but eventually they healed. After getting a job as a paperboy, he became friends with Antonia in the process. He fought alongside the ninja in a battle against the crystal army. He calls himself the Purple Ninja.

History

History[]

Skybound[]

Infamous[]

Dareth arranged for the ninja to visit Lil' Nelson and make him an honorary ninja for a day. The ninja stopped by on their way to stop Clouse, remembering their lesson to "not put off what can be done today." However, due to Kai's addiction to his fans, he alerted them about their location and ended up being surrounded.

A fellow fan himself, Lil' Nelson dons a hand-made gi, becomes the Purple Ninja, and helps the ninja by blocking the fans so they can escape to the roof. Lloyd expresses his gratefulness in his assistance, but Lil' Nelson says that he is grateful his wish was granted, thanks to Lloyd and the ninja.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

The News Never Sleeps![]

On his first day of delivering newspapers, Nelson wakes up early and gets ready quickly, even before his mother woke up herself. Just as he was about to leave on his bike, his mother gives him his helmet. Putting it on, he leaves for Ninjago City.

When he arrives at the warehouse, Antonia is instructed to lead him. While they deliver newspapers, the Elemental Cobras invade the city. Antonia decides to continue delivering the news.

When they finished delivering the newspapers, Antonia and Nelson went to the Monastery of Spinjitzu and told Wu about what happened to the ninja.

Nelson delivers a newspaper with Antonia.

Prime Empire & Prime Empire Original Shorts[]

Nelson is Antonia's dance partner in dance class. He and the rest of the class watched in amazement as Jay showed off his moves.

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

Gayle Gossip: A Closer Look[]

Gayle Gossip interviews Nelson on his opinions of Prime Empire. He called it "the greatest game ever" and talked about his mom playing it and not returning since.

When Nelson gets a chance to play, he reaches level 13 and enters Prime Empire.

Game Over[]

Seabound[]

Papergirl[]

Nelson and the other paperboys were getting ready for their rounds, and he was shocked when he heard of Antonia's decision to leave. He then attempted to persuade her to stay while the two delivered their newspapers, to no avail. He then became upset, but the two quickly reconciled.

Nelson later assisted Antonia in keeping the Storm Amulet out of the hands of Kalmaar, who chased them. The two avoided Kalmaar using their bikes, but eventually he managed to catch up and take the amulet.

Nelson watched Antonia as she dived into the river and saved Kai, and he helped push the water out of his lungs. He and Antonia are later thanked by Wu, Ray and Cole for their actions.

Antonia then informed Nelson that she changed her mind and decided to stay, much to his surprise.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Nelson is a boy with light brown hair combed to the side. He has a generic child's face from LEGO City, although he doesn't have the freckles he has in sets.

In Season 6, Nelson wears a self-made purple gi and has leg casts, due to his injuries.

Since Season 11, Nelson is shown to be a little taller, wearing a purple sweatshirt with white Ninjago letters "PN" for "Purple Ninja," and light blue pants. While working, Nelson wears a green backpack.

From "Quittin' Time!" to "Roots," Nelson wore a purple cap, black gloves, purple puffer vest, dark purple bandanna, light blue sweatshirt and dark blue pants. He later added armour made of newspaper and duct tape as well as some face paint from "Compatible" to "Roots."

Personality

Personality[]

Nelson is a brave boy, demonstrating this trait ever since he was young. As a teenager, Nelson demonstrated relatively few qualms about delivering newspapers during the invasion of the Pyro Vipers, and was willing to brave the dangerous conditions of Route 23 just to make it to the Monastery to warn Wu about the disappearance of the ninja. Additionally, Nelson was also fine with delivering papers during the Crystal army's invasion, only agreeing to call it off at Wu's behest. In spite of such dangers, he expressed a desire to stay behind to defend Ninjago City as part of the Resistance.

In addition to his bravery, Nelson displays a significant amount of loyalty; this loyalty is not just limited to his friends and idols, but to organizations he is apart of as well, most notably displayed when he expressed shock and discomfort over Antonia's intention of leaving the Paperboys for a higher paying job.

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • Nelson is named after Nelson LaMonica, the Ninjago Brand Director.[2]
  • His original torso design resembles Kai's Techno suit.
    • His minifigure has a somewhat different torso design than his show appearance.
  • He uses the hairpiece of Larry the Barista from The LEGO Movie, which is later used on Tony.
  • Though he appeared in the Rock Roader set for "Day of the Departed," Nelson did not appear in the special. According to Tommy Andreasen, his part was taken out because "The idea was to isolate Cole".[3]
  • This is the third character to use the hat/hoodie combo of Jack Davids, the others being Scott and a Henchman.
  • Despite the fact that Nelson does not have freckles in the show, his minifigure has them. This is because his minifigure reuses a generic LEGO City child face print.
    • He later gains a unique face print in 71775 Nya's Samurai X MECH, though his minifigure uses short legs even though in the show he has grown up and has medium-size legs.

  • Despite being restrained to a wheelchair due to his injury, he developed impressive ninja-like techniques in moving around that impressed even the ninja.
    • It is also shown that aside from his legs, he also once broke his entire body, arm, skull, and once lost a tooth. This strongly indicates he is incredibly accident-prone, although this may be due to his training to develop his ninja-like skills that were shown in "Infamous".

    • When asked about Nelson's age once more, Tommy Andreasen more said "Whatever age you personally think. We don't define ages. He does have a job though."[6]
  • In the Rock Roader set, he had a Ninjago Spinners game card, Card 15 - Kendo Cole.
  • He has grown and is the first character to have mid-legs (posable short legs introduced by LEGO in 2018) in the show.

  • According to Bragi Schut, Nelson learned CPR at swim class.[7]
