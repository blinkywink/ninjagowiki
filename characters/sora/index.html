Overview

This article is about the Elemental Master. For the dragon labelled the same name by the Advanced Systems Lab, see Zanth. For the Ice Fisher with a similar name, see Sorla.
“I was alone once, too. A single lie pushed away the first real friend I ever made. Friends shouldn't lie to friends, ever, but especially on, like, a city-wide level.”
— Sora to Lee, "The Screaming Earth"

Sora (born Ana) is the Elemental Master of Technology, an inventor, and one of Lloyd's ninja students.

Born as an Imperian citizen in the dictatorial regime of Empress Beatrix, Sora was one of the smartest inventors of her kingdom. After seeing how it treated dragons and being mistreated by her parents, she left her home after the Merge. She chose her current name and befriended Arin in the Crossroads.

Years later, Sora made a connection to a dragon called Riyu, who helped her discover her Elemental Power. She was trained by Lloyd and eventually unlocked her True Potential.

When the ninja participated in the Tournament of the Sources, Sora began to feel suspicious of Jordana due to her strange behavior, while also developing distrust for Frak. Throughout the tournament matches, Sora advanced to the finals, where she faced Nokt but was ultimately defeated. However, due to the tournament's sabotage, she was declared the winner anyway.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Sora has long pink hair done up in a ponytail with pink eyeshadow and whisker-like markings on both cheeks to mark her status as an Imperian. From "The Merge: Part 1" to "The Shatter Dragon", she wore a navy blue sweatshirt with cat ears on the hood underneath a white racing suit which had navy blue markings. On it were the first two letters of her name in dark blue and the last two in pink, both on the right side. She wore pink belts with golden accents and had a pink cat design on the back of her suit. She wore a dark blue glove on one arm and gold Imperian armor on the other, along with navy blue cat-eared headphones. The armor is speculated to be a prosthetic arm by some fans.

During her time in Imperium as Ana, she had space buns and wore a standard black Imperian children’s uniform.

From "Return to Imperium" to "The Battle of the Second Monastery", Sora donned the disguise of an Imperian citizen under the alias of Sora Surname, with an added marking on both cheeks.

From "Enter the City of Temples" to "Elements of Betrayal", Sora wore her Tournament of Sources suit. It included a white undershirt with three bows, pink accents and pink sash, as well as white pants. On top of that she wore a white and blue sleeveless robe with golden cat details along with a dark blue belt. She also had navy blue gloves, golden armor on one arm and a navy blue mask she wore occasionally.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Technology: As an Elemental Master of Technology, Sora can take control of any technological constructs, allowing her to modify their mechanisms or change it into something else, as she was able to morph the SA Mark 01 into a bike.
  • Elemental Powers (briefly): With enough skill, Sora was able to use almost every Elemental Power she won during the Tournament of Sources, such as Balance, Heat, Ice, Reflex, Quake, and Smoke, although she had more difficulty with the latter.
  • Spinjitzu: Although only momentary and very short lived, Sora did briefly display the ability to generate pink swirling wisps around her body while she was rapidly rotating, which indicated she was in the first stage of Spinjitzu, even before she was able to manifest an actual tornado. During the final half of Season 3, Sora began growing more in tune with using Spinjitzu, to the extent of actually manifesting proto-spinjitzu on more than on occasion and for extended periods of time.
  • Skilled combatant: Initially, Sora had struggled through the training course but she became a skilled fighter. Like the rest of the Ninja, she often combined her elemental power with her combat skills. Even powerless, she manage to briefly fight off Chaos-empowered Wolf warriors, but still struggled to defeat them, resorting to using a piece of metal to help knock them back. Later on, she managed to improve her skills and hold her own against Dragonian warriors several times. Later on, with the help of Arin, she was able to hold against Ras, but was unable to defeat him.
  • Mechanical skills: Sora is a gifted inventor whose mechanical genius was recognized by Dr. LaRow and the Advanced Systems Lab, even as a child. She pioneered the creation of the indestructible hard light Photacs. Her engineering skills would even receive praise from Nya, a prodigal inventor herself, for being innovative and unique.
  • Hacking: Because of her interest in technology since childhood, Sora also became a professional at hacking and splicing, being able to hack through the mainframes of any machinery in seconds and bypass online security.
  • Stealth: Being a ninja, Sora is able to sneak into places without being noticed.
Personality

Personality[]

Sora is a kind and caring young girl towards the people she loves, but at the same time she is cynical and has trouble trusting people. This comes from having her childhood idol, Dr. LaRow, betray her and use her invention to hunt down and enslave dragons and later on her own parents turn against her. Due to having been raised in a different realm, Sora never came to know the Ninja or their feats, so she would show some cynicism towards her best friend Arin's admiration towards them. But after Lloyd saved them from the Claws of Imperium and he took them in to teach them how to be a Ninja, she started to develop a great deal of trust towards them. When it comes to trust on people that once were on the opposite side as her, she takes longer to warm up with them, as seen with Frak, who used to be Ras' student.

She often lacks confidence, as she is afraid of letting down the people she cares about, as she used her power and made Arin think that he did Spinjitzu, and even feels insecure about her elemental power and worries she cannot control it. This had led her to feel she didn't have a place on the Ninja, after seen how her power would fail on many occasions and was dependent on Riyu to work. After she managed to free herself from what was holding her back, Sora managed to unlock her full potential, with that increasing her confidence.

Despite her insecurity, Sora is brave, loyal, and willing to do almost anything for the people she cares about, such as the Ninja. She is kind and compassionate towards dragons and her friends, but she can also show it towards strangers. Sora is also skilled, independent and creative, qualities she developed through her passion for technology and having to fend for herself after the Merge. She is always ready to help those she cares about in any way she can.

Etymology

Etymology[]

The name Sora is primarily a gender-neutral name of Japanese origin that means "sky". However, in-universe, her name comes from the ancient Imperium word for orphan. She named herself after Zanth, who was nicknamed Sora at the time.[6] After she cuts her ties with her parents, she fully embraced the Imperium's meaning of the word.

Her birth name, Ana, means "favored grace."

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, the plating on her left arm is just armor and not a prosthetic robot arm.[7]
    • Despite this, Play Stories claims that her left arm is a prosthetic.
  • Both minifigures of Sora in casual attire use a hair piece of the pigtails she had in her youth.
  • In early concept, Sora was originally depicted as a non-human kitsune as a callback to Akita.[8]
  • Chris "Doc" Wyatt has stated that Sora has a last name, though it's currently unknown when it will be revealed.[9]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, Sora feels the power of Technology in her brain.[10]
  • In particular designs, Sora's appearance and hair color are based off the character Aelita Schaffer from Code Lyoko and her powers are technology and creation as well.
  • In "Return to Imperium," the ID card Sora creates for herself says "Sora Surname" in Ninjargon.
  • She appears to be fond of cats, based on her headphones, hood, and the cat symbol on the back of her racing suit. Even her facial markings resemble cat whiskers.
    • In "Fallen Wishes", she is shown to love cats and begs Nya to tell Arrakore to wish for kittens.
  • It is unknown when or why she got her Imperium face markings, as she is without them when living in the Crossroads with Arin, and had seemingly renounced her Imperian cultural heritage.
  • She is the first ninja who was not born in Ninjago, the second being Wyldfyre.
  • Before the Tournament of the Sources, she and Wyldfyre were the only ninja who didn't have ninja suits, as her outfit was a racing suit and Wyldfyre's was a normal outfit.
    • Sora did not have a ninja suit before the Tournament of Sources because "even though it's been a while since she left Imperium, Sora's still got a thing against wearing what everyone else is wearing."[11]
    • However, it is possible that she is starting to slowly warm up the idea of wearing similar outfits to the other Ninja, because starting with "The Hollow Ones" she can be seen wearing a Ghost Hunter suit, matching the other ninja in terms of shoulder armor and mask style but still making the torso unique to her.
  • Her minifigure face print has two additional markings on her forehead that are normally obscured by her hair.
  • She shares some similarities with her master, Lloyd:
    • Both of them were outcasts in their schools, due to not fitting in with the norm (Sora for being too unique and Lloyd for not being bad enough).
    • Both were opposed and disowned by at least one of their parents.
    • Both had to deal with the dangerous consequences of the choices they made as children (Sora for creating the Photacs and Lloyd for releasing the Serpentine).
    • They both doubt themselves at times, like Sora believing she is a "black cloud" in "The Last Djinn" and Lloyd questioning whether or not he will live up to the legacy of his father and uncle as heard in "The Hands of Time".
    • Both took over a tower in their home cities and delivered a speech against an emperor/empress (Garmadon and Beatrix respectively).
    • Both feel a tremendous guilt when they are unable to help people and are forced to leave them behind.
  • She is claustrophobic.[12]
  • Based on what is shown in "Land of Lost Things" it's possible she could be ambidextrous since she uses both hands to write in the episode.
  • She shares some similarities to Marina Ida from Splatoon.
    • Both wear headphones.
    • Both come from societies of their respective villainous faction. (Octarians and Imperians)
    • Both are geniuses at technology and build powerful weapons later used. (Flooders and Photacs)
    • Both ran away from their societies during an important event that changed their lives. (The Calamari Inkantation and the Merge)

Early life

Early life[]

Sora was born in the Kingdom of Imperium from her parents who named her Ana.

She grew up in the Imperian society ruled by the dictatorial regime of Empress Beatrix. Sora was very smart and inventive and was passionate about science and technology since she was little, but was often neglected by her parents for her prodigal mind. An avid fan of Dr. LaRow, Ana dreamed of working for her in the Advanced Systems Lab one day.

One time, Ana once broke down a vacuum cleaner to make a robot to do her chores. When her mother found out, she was angry that Ana hid in the tunnels and ended up lost for two days.

When her latest invention, the Photac, won at her school's science fair, Dr. LaRow personally brought Ana to her lab to hire her. At the lab, Dr. LaRow showed Ana the source of Imperium's power, a dragon nicknamed Sora before doing upgrades to her device to make ferocious animals, as opposed to harmless ones.

Realizing that they were going to use her invention to hunt dragons and harm them in their quest to remain powerful, she refused to work for her.

This action caused her to be despised, shunned, and disowned upon by everyone in Imperium, including her parents, who eventually confiscated her work bench and inventions, demanding she'd find a new interest if she won't use her talents to help Imperium.

While in her room, one day, The Merge occurred before a shockwave came soon after, and Ana discovered a new city in the distance. Deciding to run away from home, she takes her headphones, a few possessions, and changes her name to Sora, after the dragon. As a result of the Merge, Sora unknowingly ended up with the Elemental Power of Technology when the Source Dragon of Focus created it.

The Final Game[]

While training with Lloyd and Zane in the Games Plaza, Sora tried to use Cinder's Smoke but failed to go twice as far, leading her to wonder how Cinder managed to harness it so easily. She felt frustrated about her inability to wield the other Elemental Powers like her competitors. Lloyd pointed out that Sora's main issue was her attempt to emulate the other Elemental Masters instead of embracing her unique abilities. Sora acknowledged this but was eager to know what she could do differently. Lloyd encouraged her to be true to herself, but Sora felt that her current self was not good enough, especially since Arin disliked her, her Spinjitzu skills were inconsistent, and she was facing the prospect of losing to Nokt in the upcoming final match. Lloyd reassured her, suggesting that she should stop trying to replicate how others used their powers and instead focus on how only she could use them. Sora, feeling uncertain, asked for guidance on how to achieve that. Spotting the Wolf Clan nearby, she excused herself to talk to Jordana. When Sora mentioned the note, Jordana claimed not to know what she was talking about. Sora felt a pang of regret for not remembering Jordana better and suggested that they might have been friends during their challenging school days. However, Jordana dismissed her with a harsh remark, calling her pathetic before walking away, leaving Sora feeling disheartened.

Later at night, the final battle between Sora and Nokt commenced. The battle kicked off with Sora making the first move, attempting to perform Spinjitzu for the first time. She managed the rudimentary version of the technique, much like Arin had before her, but Nokt quickly knocked her to the ground. Nokt boasted that Sora had never encountered an opponent like him before, to which Sora retorted by drawing comparisons to her parents and other oppressive Imperians from the City of Imperium, asserting that she had faced worse. Nokt then performed Shatterspin, but Sora dodged the attack and quickly created an Ice wall for defense. She then used Frak's Quake power on ice spikes she raised from the ground to the air, followed by applying Zur's Reflex power into punching them in a rapid motion. When Nokt uses Shatterspin to attack, the barrier collapse onto him and sends him flying into a screen. The two combatants exchanged fierce blows using their Elemental Powers, with Sora using Smoke, Heat, and Reflex to counteract Nokt's attacks. As the battle went on, Nokt used Shockwave to make Sora go into smoke form and managed to knock her down with Shatterspin. Lloyd, watching from the sidelines, urged Sora to outsmart Nokt if she couldn't overpower him. In a moment of insight, Sora spotted a device on the back of Nokt's head, though she was unsure of its function. Utilizing Technology, she transformed the device into a techno-bee, which stung Nokt and delivered an electric shock. Just as Sora prepared to finish Nokt with her Heat attack, the arena unexpectedly shifted, leaving her disoriented. Sora hopped onto a hover pad, and she attempted another Heat attack, but Nokt countered with Shatterspin, knocking her off her pad and securing his victory in the final match.

Sora and Lloyd proceeded to the victory ceremony, where Sora was to relinquish her powers. Lloyd commended Sora, asserting that she surpassed all other Elemental Masters. Nevertheless, Sora felt troubled by the fact that Ras would acquire all the Elemental Powers for his scheme. Roby absorbed Sora's powers with the intention of transferring them to Nokt, thereby claiming the title of Champion of the Sources. At that moment, Wyldfyre and Zur arrived, revealing Bleckt's collusion with Ras. Wyldfyre detailed Ras' entire plot, including the involvement of Bleckt in orchestrating the attacker bots and rigging the matches, including the one between Sora and Nokt. Disgusted by Bleckt's betrayal, Roby announced the cancellation of the victory celebration. However, Nokt attacked Roby with Shatterspin, claiming all the powers from the Elemental Cup and betrayed Ras. When Jordana arrived, Sora learned that Rox, another member of the Forbidden Five, had possessed Jordana since the Blood Moon Ritual, explaining her odd behavior. Both Rox and Nokt admitted to being responsible for the deaths of the three Arc Dragons. When Sora questioned about their motives for killing innocent dragons, Nokt revealed their intent to steal the Crowns of Horns to aid in freeing the rest of the Forbidden Five. Enraged, Ras summoned Cinder and the wolf warriors to confront Nokt and Cinder, but Rox corrupted the Wolf Clan, unleashing their true power. Sora and Lloyd prepared for battle as the Wolf Clan launched their attack.

Elements of Betrayal[]

Sora fought the Wolf Mask Warriors, using a fragment of a railing as an improvised weapon during the skirmish. Sora expressed to Lloyd her dissatisfaction with the limitations imposed by her partial acquisition of the Elemental Powers from the tournament. Lloyd, in turn, relayed information he had received from Arin regarding the location of the Crowns of Horns, which were said to be situated at the Monastery of Gates. He also conveyed his astonishment at Ras' innocence concerning the assassinations of the Arc Dragons. Sora advised Lloyd not to be overly critical of himself, as it was unlikely anyone would suspect Jordana of being possessed by a member of the Forbidden Five. Despite Lloyd's urgency to confront Nokt at the Monastery, Sora reminded him that both Nokt and Rox had already made their way there. Sora and the other ninja reached the Monastery of Gates, where the remaining members of the Forbidden Five had successfully escaped from the Nether-Space. Engaging in battle alongside the other ninja and Ras, Sora used her glider to confront Nokt. During the skirmish, Nokt launched a fierce attack on the ninja, resulting in Sora being dislodged from her glider. Amidst the chaos, both Sora and Lloyd witnessed Arin valiantly defending Ras against Nokt, a sight that left them both bewildered. Meanwhile, Nya tapped into Riyu's dragon power and used them on the Crowns of Horns, which enabled her to liberate Kai and Bonzle from the Nether-Space. A battle erupted between Kai and Nokt, with the latter initially gaining the upper hand and preparing to eliminate Kai. However, Roby intervened by used the Elemental Cup to reclaim the powers that Nokt had usurped, thereby restoring them to their rightful bearers, including Sora. The ninja managed to corner Nokt, but he ultimately escaped through a portal gate along with the remaining members of the Forbidden Five, taking Ras with him. Despite Sora and Lloyd's objections, Arin chose to pursue the Forbidden Five in an attempt to rescue Ras. As Sora and Lloyd approached the portal gate, the Forbidden Five destroyed it, leaving Sora devastated as she called out for Arin, coming to terms with the fact that Arin had opted to abandon the ninja.

In the aftermath, Sora has been designated as the Champion of the Sources by default, earning recognition as the greatest Elemental Master within the Merged Realms. Subsequently, at Foundation Street, Sora thanked Euphrasia and Tox for their invaluable assistance. Shortly thereafter, she sought out Lloyd to asked about the whereabouts of Jordana, to which Lloyd replied that Jordana had disappeared following her aid to the ninja. Sora emphasized that their success would not have been possible without Jordana's contributions. Lloyd reminisced about a time when Sora had claimed she possessed no Elemental Powers, to which Sora humorously remarked that it felt like an eternity ago. She conveyed her appreciation to Lloyd, acknowledging that her triumph was largely due to his encouragement, alongside the inspiration she drew from both him and Arin. Sora then shared her concerns regarding the rift that had developed between her and Arin, prompting Lloyd to reassure her that they would find Arin. However, Sora expressed her belief that Arin might not wish to be found. As the ninja prepared to return to Ninjago, Sora and Lloyd stumbled upon Wyldfyre and Roby sharing a tender moment, leading Sora to pull Lloyd away when he interrupted. Upon their return to the Monastery of Spinjitzu via a portal gate, Sora and Wyldfyre met up with Riyu, and the trio left together.

Arin[]

“Stronger together, right?”
“Together forever.”
— Sora and Arin, "Crashing Together"

Arin had been Sora's best friend since after running away from Imperium when the Merge occurred, when they became mech racers together. Their dynamic together was very close and supportive, as they seemed to be affectionate, supporting and cared for and loved each other very much and considered each other as family, they were seen to be very, very close. They were fond of each other. After rescuing Riyu from the Claws of Imperium and meeting up with Lloyd, they both became his students, though Sora felt she was incapable of being a ninja as she lacked focus and believed her newly discovered powers are Riyu's, although Arin tried to reassure her through out these moments. She deeply cares for him, and loves him.

They were seen to become closer in Season 2 but, after the latter find out Sora lied to him about his Object Spinjitzu ending the Ritual of the Blood Moon, he became cold and distant to her, constantly avoiding and ignoring her in situations where he clearly needed some help solving a mystery, making Sora feel upset, frustrated and hurt. When they met again in "Crashing Together", both apologized to each other, and Arin informed Sora of the supposed death of his parents which led the latter to comfort and hug him.

When Arin return with Ras after Thunderfang' s defeat, Sora decide to go with them, no wanting to abandon her friend again and wanting to watch over him.

Riyu[]

Enemies[]

Beatrix[]

Sora had a mass hatred for Beatrix, due to her dictatorial regime and her cruelty against dragons in order to empower Imperium.

Dr. LaRow (former idol)[]

“Hmm. You've grown feisty. Where's that sweet, wide-eyed little girl who was excited just to be in a real science lab?”
“She grew up. On the streets of a strange city. When she had to escape a family poisoned against her by her former hero.”
— Dr. LaRow and Sora, "Mindless Beasts"

Dr. LaRow used to be Sora's idol while she lived in Imperium. When she was offered a job after winning her school's science fair, she refused upon discovering she was using Zanth to keep the city powered, which led to Sora being shunned.

Over the years. Sora held massive grudge against Dr. LaRow, viewing her responsible for ruining her life. When meeting her for the first time in years, Sora is very hostile towards her former idol and even openly insults her, which surprises Dr. LaRow that Sora grew very feisty and when the scientist wonders what happened to the sweet, naive girl that used to be Ana, Sora answers that she grew up in the streets after being betrayed by her former idol. Dr. LaRow tells her that some heroes may disappoint. When Dr. LaRow asks Sora to create more of the Photac beasts, Sora refused, however, she relented after Dr. LaRow threatened to drain Zanth of her energy. But after activating multiple Photacs, Sora betrayed Dr. LaRow and helped the other ninja to defeat the Claws of Imperium and save dragons.

After the Battle of the Second Monastery, upon hearing from Rapton about the Dragon Cores, Beatrix ordered Dr. LaRow alongside Rapton and Dorama to find three Dragon Cores for MergeQuake weapon. Which led Dr. LaRow to the Land of Lost Things. After Zanth was captured, upon hearing from Bonzle about Dr. LaRow, Sora tried to face her before Dr. LaRow calls her broken experiment. She then demonstrates how the drones when combined can make a barrier of energy before eventually holding Sora down while a single drone heads for the core. Sora alongside Nya, Riyu, Cole and Finders tried to stop her from getting the core but failed. Despite Sora's failure, the ninja successfully managed to defeat Beatrix and liberate the Kingdom of Imperium from her tyrannical rule.

Jordana (rival; formerly)[]

“Look, I don't understand what's going on with you, Jordana, but I wanna tell you, I'm sorry. I didn't remember you from when we were in school together. I know that upset you and made you feel unimportant. Me and my friend Arin have been… I just… I wished I hadn't been so wrapped up in own stuff. I wished I'd remembered you. School was really a lonely time in my life, and I could've used a friend. Maybe we both could have.”
— Sora to "Jordana", "The Final Game"

Jordana was Sora's classmate, whose grudge against the latter started when she became popular upon showing her Photac device. After Sora refused to work for Dr. LaRow, Jordana took it. However, when they saw each other, Sora doesn't remember her, enraging Jordana even more. However, during their next encounter at the Cloud Kingdom, she then recognizes her as the girl from her class in Imperium. In the episode The Final Game, Sora apologizes to Jordana for ignoring her all these years, and admitting could have become good friends. It is unknown if Jordana heard these words while still being possessed by Rox.

The Mechanic[]

“They call you The Mechanic huh? I am not impressed.”
— Sora to the Mechanic, "The Mech Master"

Sora had her first encounter with the Mechanic when she witnessed him sabotaging the Mech Master 5000 at the Crossroads. At first, Sora mistakenly assumed that he was a genuine mechanic. However, her perception quickly changed when she caught sight of him stealing the mechs. Determined to put a stop to his nefarious activities, Sora swiftly activated her own mech. Unfortunately, The Mechanic took her down by using his commandeered mechs to ram into the Elemental Mechs, causing them to be dismantled. As chaos ensued and the remaining mechs engaged in a fierce battle with the ninja, the Mechanic managed to escape. Being the fastest racer out of the ninja, Sora relentlessly pursued the Mechanic, fueled by her determination to bring him to justice. Mocking her technological prowess, the Mechanic arrogantly taunted Sora before forcefully knocking her off the bridge. However, Sora's resourcefulness came to her aid as she used the legs from the Elemental Fire Mech as boosters, enabling her to regain her footing on the bridge. She struck the Mechanic's mech with such force that it split in half. Emerging victorious, Sora confidently declared that she was unimpressed with the Mechanic's skills.
