Overview

Gweeg is a member of the Explorer's Club as well as a friend of Alfonzo Frohicky.

History

History[]

The Lean of a Tree[]

When Sora, Frak, Wyldfyre, and Riyu arrived in Umbrasilvis, Gweeg was putting a small crate on top of a larger crate.

Tonight We Hunt Monsters[]

In Umbrasilvis, Gweeg, along with his other colleagues, got introduced by Reginald, with him belittling Gweeg while doing so. Reginald said he was simply teasing him, much to Gweeg's irritation. Gweeg was repeatedly teased by Reginald, much to the enjoyment of the other Explorer's Club members and the dismay of Gweeg. When Wyldfyre asked why Reginald kept picking on Gweeg, Reginald replied that it was in good fun, and Gweeg enjoyed it. Gweeg shook his head and walked away.

At night, Gweeg and the rest of the Explorers got ready to monster hunt with their new capture devices, which Reginald tested on Gweeg. Gweeg successfully escaped the ropes and ran off to find the monster, looking around the village for it. Gweeg later encountered a Mutation Monster. Gweeg fell to the ground when Frak used Quake to scare the monster away.

A Wonder of the Merged Lands[]

When Frak and Sora went to the Expanding Frontier, they saw the Explorer's Club celebrating the Mutation Monster's capture, including Gweeg. During the celebration, Johnny Courageous's "accidentally" hit Gweeg with one of his drumsticks. When all of the Explorer's Club members said cheered to Reginald, Johnny hit him again.

Seed and Soil[]

When the mutated Mutation Monster captured Wyldfyre and Frak, Gweeg appeared and drawn attention to himself, attacking the creature with the lariat launcher. Which knocked him while also successfully hitting the creature, releasing the ninja during the process. While escaping, Frak thanked and said that his dad is wrong about him. Then, Gweeg ran away to not get attacked by the Mutation Monster. After the Mutation Monster's disappearance, Gweeg thanked Riyu, alongside with the Umbrasilvans.

The Grand Inquisition[]

During the Inquisition, Gweeg was in the crowd with the rest of the Explorer's Club, where his innocent was ironically proved by Reginald. After the Inquisition, the Explorer's Club members were gathered around Reginald, including Gweeg.

Other appearances and mentions

Other appearances and mentions[]

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

Concerned that Zane's fame would attract too much attention in the Crossroads, Frohicky suggested he wear a fake mustache. Zane activated his cloaking technology to create a holographic mustache. Believing the mustache to be genuine, Frohicky remarked that it was the second fastest he had ever seen someone grow a mustache, just behind Gweeg.[1]
