The Dragons of Fallen Leaves are a tribe of Forest Dragons who live in the Fallen Leaves.

History

History[]

A member of the tribe, Rontu, allied with another dragon, Egalt, with whom she created the Rising Dragon technique to overthrow the Forbidden Five together with the sorceress Gandalaria. Many years later, Egalt and Rontu trained the ninja.

After two members of the Forbidden Five returned, they murdered the Matriarch of Fallen Leaves and took her Crowns of Horns. Then her child became the next Arc Dragon of Fallen Leaves.
