Overview

The Acona are humanoid acorn-like creatures residing in the Whispering Wood.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • They may resemble the Tide-Folk, but the existence of a link between the two groups is currently unknown.
  • When an Acona loses all of their life force, they don't die, but instead revert to a seed that needs to be planted in order for them to be reborn, as they are infused with the Life energy of the Whispering Woods. However, if the wood dies, they also die as well, as they cannot survive without its Life Energy.
  • Their name in concept art was known as "Treewoks."[1]
    • Additionally, their name and lifestyle in the trees share similarities to the Ewoks from Star Wars.
  • They appear to be based on acorns as their names sound similar, and they have acorn-shaped backpacks.
