Overview

“To be a master sushi chef, you must apprentice for years, become an expert at every aspect of the sushi dinner, from ingredients to preparations... to table settings. And that includes... chopsticks. It also includes... defeat!”
— Sushimi to Jay and Nya, "The Temple of Madness"

Sushimi (also known as the mad sushi chef) was a member of Unagami's army and a sushi chef in Terra Domina.

History

History[]

Prior to Prime Empire[]

At some point in time, Sushimi indirectly told Shifty about the Manifestation Gate when the latter sold him his knives.[2]

The Temple of Madness[]

After two of the sushi chefs were defeated, the third one summons Sushimi to engage in a fight with Jay and Nya, but only for him to be defeated by them.

However, after Jay installed the Key-Tanas to their slots, Sushimi attempts to throw a mace at him, only for Nya to save him and letting it hit her instead, turning her into an energy cube. Jay then grabs the mace and throws it at Sushimi, destroying him.

Other appearances

Other appearances[]

Gayle Gossip: A Closer Look[]

Sushimi made a cameo during an old commercial of Prime Empire being shown.

Level Thirteen[]

Sushimi appeared in one of the arcade cabinets when a police officer was taking a Fist to Fist game cabinet away from Benny.
