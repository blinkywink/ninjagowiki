Overview

“Zane, I was once just a Nindroid. A machine. And then, long ago, in a different battle, you gave up half your power source to save me. In that moment, you changed me. I felt things… I never felt before. I felt... love. For you.”
— P.I.X.A.L. to Zane, "Compatible"

P.I.X.A.L. (Primary Interactive X-ternal Assistant Life-form), known as Pix by her friends, is a female Nindroid created by Cyrus Borg to serve as his assistant, the current Samurai X, a member of the ninja, and Zane's girlfriend.

When Cyrus Borg invited the ninja to discuss an important matter, P.I.X.A.L. gave a tour of the building to Nya, Wu, and their students. However, this tour was cut short when P.I.X.A.L. was put under the control of the Overlord. While under his control, she designed and produced the Nindroid Army using blueprints of Zane. She would remain under his control until Zane freed her using a Techno Blade. During the Nindroid crisis, P.I.X.A.L. assisted in turning the power to Ninjago City off. They are successful, but this deactivated P.I.X.A.L. in the process. Zane gave her half of his power source, reactivating her and the knowledge of how to fight properly. P.I.X.A.L. would continue to help the ninja in battling against Nindroids, the Overlord, and even Pythor, until Zane sacrificed himself to defeat the Overlord and save Ninjago.

During Zane's memorial, P.I.X.A.L. remained in Borg Tower mourning his loss, until Zane communicated to her from within the Digiverse. Together, they built Zane a new titanium body, but were shortly captured by Ronin and sold to Chen soon after. P.I.X.A.L.'s body would be scrapped, while her consciousness would be dumped on a neural drive. She would remain there until Zane put her neural drive in his processor, essentially putting P.I.X.A.L. in his head. From there, P.I.X.A.L. would assist Zane and the ninja to the best of her ability in stopping the Anacondrai Cultists from taking over Ninjago.

During the attack of the Vermillion, P.I.X.A.L. left Zane's head and built a new physical body. She took on the alias of Samurai X so she could adopt a more physical role in the ninja's missions. P.I.X.A.L. then assisted the ninja in dealing with the Time Twins.

Eventually, her identity as the new Samurai X became known to the ninja, which was met with Zane's approval. Due to this, she helped battle the Sons of Garmadon with the ninja in a more prominent role. Later, she joined the Resistance against Garmadon's oppressive hold of Ninjago City.

While the other ninja were in Prime Empire, she and Zane embarked on a search for Milton Dyer. They succeed, but not before Zane was used as a power source to fuel the gateway between Prime Empire and Ninjago. P.I.X.A.L. would then assist Jay in stopping Unagami, leading to the eventual release and revival of those trapped in the game.

A year after the Great Flood, she and the rest of the resistance would band together to fulfill the final prophecy of Quanish the Elder and stop the Overlord once more.

At some point, following the Merge, P.I.X.A.L. was placed on standby in a stasis pod. After Thunderfang was defeated, P.I.X.A.L. and Zane rode off together to explore the Merged Lands.

In LEGO Ninjago: Nindroids (non-canon)

In LEGO Ninjago: Nindroids (non-canon)[]

P.I.X.A.L. is a playable character in LEGO Ninjago: Nindroids. She also serves as a boss in the level "Mad Machines".

In LEGO Ninjago: Shadow of Ronin (non-canon)

In LEGO Ninjago: Shadow of Ronin (non-canon)[]

P.I.X.A.L. is a playable character in LEGO Ninjago: Shadow of Ronin. She can be purchased for 250,000 studs after collecting her character token in the Ninjago Hub. She wields two spike blades and possesses no special abilities.

Appearance

Appearance[]

“Hmm. New color?”
— P.I.X.A.L., "Secrets Discovered"

P.I.X.A.L. has a white face covered with purple conduction tracks (similar to those found on printed circuit boards), highlighting the fact that she is an android. She also has wide, white lips and glowing green eyes, which also underscore the fact that she is an android. Shortly after Garmadon took control of Ninjago City, she began wearing a black mask with a blue border bordering her face, but after the Oni's invasion she stopped. Some parts of her head don't appear to be waterproof, as she needed to cover her entire head except for her face with a mask to dive.

Her original hair was silver, has a fringe to the left side, and behind a tall disheveled ponytail. When she rebuilt her body, she also recreated her hair. Now, the fringe is to the right side, the ponytail is less disheveled and is lower. It also seems that her hair is bigger and thicker. Originally, her hair was very shiny, but after the Oni's invasion they became more dull.

As an assistant at Borg Industries, she wore a purple uniform with a gray and red trim and an equally colored belt. She has an opening in her left arm that showed a part of her breastplate, also with purple conduction tracks, and the rest of her body was white. When she was controlled by the Overlord, her eyes turned red.

When Chen captured her, he ordered her whole body to be dismantled, but when Zane acquired her neural drive and put it inside of his head, she kept the same design as before as the hologram in his mind.

When she rebuilt her body, she put on Nya's Jungle Samurai X Suit. The suit consisted of black-red armor with a dark green chestplate depicting Nya's phoenix symbol. She also had dark green leg protectors, red arms, and black gloves. She wore a black Samurai helmet with a red mask over her mouth and golden horns. She also had silver shoulder pads. Her whole face was covered with a black mask with silver glasses and red eyes.

When she recolored the original Samurai X suit for her new color scheme, the suit consisted of silver armor and belly and leg protectors and consisted mostly of dark blue clothing, black shoes with gold details, black hands, and dark blue arms. She also had a gold belt and a gold Samurai X symbol in the middle of her chest in a gray circle. She wore the same helmet as before but with a mask on her mouth dyed gold. She also had black, slightly larger shoulder pads. She had a gold mask on her face, the torah obscuring her identity, and she had a radiant blue cover on her eyes that connected the two eyes.

Over the next year, she changed her suit again, adding white and red to the color scheme. Her suit is therefore now dark blue, with red and white details on the chest and skirt on the legs. Her shoes are now black with a white sole. In the middle of her chest, she has a golden Samurai X symbol in a red circle. She has the same shoulder pads as before, but golden. Her helmet remained unchanged, but shortly before the Oni's invasion changed the horns of the helmet so that they were now slightly larger and twisted. She kept the same mask on her face but began to wear another, slightly different mask under it. This mask is mostly white, with a black eye cover, red pupils, and red stripes pointing upwards from the eyes.

When she used the Scroll of Forbidden Spinjitzu, her eyes turned white.

She also wore Zane's gray fedora for a while after the Mechanic kidnapped him.

At some point before or after The Merge happened, P.I.X.A.L changed her look again, now with purple eyeliner and lips, and she now has a new Samurai X suit mostly white with lavender details with grey arm sleeves, red gloves and two smaller golden shoulder pieces. Her Samurai X logo is now a neon red. She also has new face markings.

Personality

Personality[]

“You can be honest with me, because I want to be a useful member of the team, but I would prefer not to return to the computer. I like having a body, but if you require my assistance in your headset...”
— P.I.X.A.L. to Zane, "The Quiet One"

P.I.X.A.L. was originally shown to be quite emotionless and impassive and had only her duties to Cyrus Borg as her primary concern. Things like duty, honor, and compassion at first meant nothing to her, but throughout her adventures with the Ninja, and her interactions with Zane, she developed immensely as a person. P.I.X.A.L. originally had no interest in others besides a basic curiosity and desire to learn, but after meeting with Zane, her behavior changed. When she was pondering on helping the ninja to shut off power, she quickly ignored any doubts or reasons in it to save. When she was shut down and given part of Zane's power, she slowly became more human and her emotions soon surfaced, showing that she was capable of exemplary bravery, compassion, humor, and friendship.

Her most human traits were shown primarily around Zane, an android like her, who taught her to embrace her hidden humanity and subsequently know things like deep love, civic duty, friendship, but also loss and heartache. This was shown as she was deeply shocked after Zane's apparent death and sacrificing of his original body to defeat the Overlord; she had been affected more than anyone by his death to the point where she avoided his memorial in her inability to accept his passing but was overjoyed by discovering his resurrection. She also developed a deep faith in Zane and his strength of character, showing a profound level of emotional intimacy and devotion to her love. Although she is a good fighter, she prefers peaceful ways to end the fighting, as she asked the Preeminent and the octopus to stop their activities, rather than being forced to defeat them by force.

After adopting the Samurai X persona, P.I.X.A.L. hid the fact that she had a new body and was protecting people to have her own identity. As she told Zane, she would have preferred to have her own body and not be part of the computer, but she asked him if he agreed, as she really wanted to be a useful member of the team. Following her reunion with Zane, she was very happy and they embraced in their reunion.

As of late, P.I.X.A.L. shows a snarky side thanks to her spending too much time with Zane and how he is starting to get on her nerves with his habit of emulating characters from data that he found online.

Abilities[]

  • Skilled combatant - Although originally P.I.X.A.L. could not fight because it wasn't in her programming, when she gained Zane's power source, her fighting skills increased. She was notably able to easily disarm Pythor and Techno Wu. When she built a new body for herself and became Samurai X, she became a much better fighter as she mastered several different fighting styles and was even able to defeat all the Ninja when she was hacked by the Quiet One. P.I.X.A.L. also easily stands up to factions of villains such as Sons of Garmadon, Freebooters or Dragonian Warriors and has no difficulty beating bandits and criminals as well as Crystal Warriors. Years later after The Merge, her combat skills became better that she was able to effortlessly overpower and defeat Zarkt, a member of the Forbidden Five, who did not use any of his powers. Although she struggled to fight Nokt who managed to defeat her and take the Prismatic Blade from her which is likely partly due to the fact P.I.X.A.L. was unaware of her opponent's abilities
  • Spinjitzu (formerly) - After Zane gave her half of his power source, she was able to use Spinjitzu with Zane. However, she does not know how to use it after rebuilding her body, because no one has taught her how to accomplish it.[1]
  • Great agility: P.I.X.A.L. has very good agility and is capable of performing acrobatics such as somersaults.
  • Nindroid Physiology/abilities: Being a Nindroid, P.I.X.A.L. has the following abilities.
    • Intelligence - As a Nindroid, P.I.X.A.L. is very intelligent and has access to a wealth of information.
    • Voice replication - As a Nindroid, P.I.X.A.L. can replicate anyone's voice whether they are alive or dead.
    • Holographic projection - P.I.X.A.L. can project holograms and images from her eyes like a video projector.[2]
    • Probability Prediction: P.I.X.A.L. can survey her environments and predict the likelihood of a possible outcome or tell whether or not there are any better options.
    • Scanner Vision: Through her eyes, P.I.X.A.L. can scan a person, an area, a vehicle for various reasons.
    • Hidden grappling hook (probably formerly) - P.I.X.A.L. had a grappling hook that could come out of her arm of her first body, that she could use to climb.[3]
  • Mechanical skills - P.I.X.A.L. is a skilled mechanic and inventor. She built the majority of the vehicles of the Ninja and of the team since the Oni's invasion.

Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

Rebooted[]

P.I.X.A.L.(Primary Interactive X-ternal Assistant Life-form) is Cyrus Borg’s android assistant.

When she first meets the ninja, she is under the influence of the Digital Overlord and uses her skills to copy the plans for Zane so the Nindroids can be built.

She is cold and mechanical and incapable of any feeling for anyone.

P.I.X.A.L. is actually the 16th model in her line, the first 15 having malfunctioned due to a faulty thermo-coil and a malfunctioning AIE-ES (artificial intelligence enhancer-emotion suppressor) chip.

Etymology

Etymology[]

P.I.X.A.L.'s name is based on the word, "pixel". It was originally going to be spelled this way, but it was changed for legal reasons.[11]

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • P.I.X.A.L. is based on Rachael from the film Blade Runner.[12]
  • P.I.X.A.L.'s name is written differently depending on the source. In the set descriptions mentioning her name[13][14] and various episode descriptions, she is referred to as "Pixal," while in other sources such as Garmadon Rulez!, she is referred to as "P.I.X.A.L.".
  • The clip on P.I.X.A.L.'s original hair piece on her minifigure from 2014 is absent in the show.
  • According to Michael Knap, P.I.X.A.L. having a revealed breastplate does not mean she is nude because "She is a nindroid minifigure, I'm not sure if they have human features."[15]
  • P.I.X.A.L. is the character with the most voice actors, as she uses the voices of several different characters in "Secrets Discovered."
    • Like the original Samurai X, her identity had already been revealed through her minifigure released in the set wave for Season 8.
  • From Season 8 until Dragons Rising, she has the same eyebrows used on Season 7 Nya and flashback Maya.
  • Her hair piece was changed to the same as Nya's current in Season 8, albeit silver.
    • Nya's and therefore also P.I.X.A.L.'s post-movie hair piece was modeled after P.I.X.A.L.'s original hair piece,[16] which itself originates from the Cave Woman from Minifigures Series 5.
    • Her hair piece was not physically released in silver until Ninjago: Core in 2022, four years after Season 8 aired in 2018.
  • The design of her second dark blue Samurai X suit seems to be based on the Iron Patriot armor suit from Iron Man 3, as they have a very similar color scheme and design.
  • After becoming Samurai X, P.I.X.A.L. has been shown to have what seems to be a black helmet printed on her head in seasons 9 and 10. This has been reflected in LEGO sets.
    • Since Season 11, she no longer has this helmet. The reason for this change is unknown, though it may be connected to the animation studio change.
      • However, she had this helmet in "Long Live the King," when she was underwater, indicating it is waterproof.
  • There are a few differences between her previous faces in the sets and the show and her faces in Ninjago: Core.
    • The most striking difference is the subtle change of purple symbols, which are a little more detailed and elaborate.
    • Another change is slightly larger eyes and instead of a few lashes, only one large eyelash is above each eye.
    • The last changes are notches on both eyebrows and full silver lips, as in previous performances only the contours of the lips were silver and the inside was white.
  • Tommy Andreasen provided two reasons why P.I.X.A.L. doesn't know how to use Spinjitzu. The in-universe reason is that "no one taught her" and the out-of-universe reason is that "you don't give all characters the same abilities. If everyone is special, no one is special. PIXAL is amazing as she is."[1]

History

Background

Background[]

P.I.X.A.L. was the last in a line of android assistants to Cyrus Borg. Her fifteen predecessors had malfunctioned due to a faulty thermo-coil and a malfunctioning AIE-ES (artificial intelligence enhancer-emotion suppressor) chip. Cyrus managed to fix the thermo-coil with P.I.X.A.L., but the AIE-ES chip remained problematic.[1][2]

Rebooted

Rebooted[]

The Surge[]

When the ninja and their students arrived at Borg Tower for a tour of the facility, they were greeted by P.I.X.A.L., who was under the influence of the Overlord. She assured them that their crashed bus would be repaired before she introduced herself to Zane and asked him what his name stood for. Because she was under the influence of the Overlord,[2] P.I.X.A.L. scanned Zane and found the Nindroid to be made of outdated parts. She was perplexed, however, that she could not locate Zane's power source. When he admitted he was unsure himself, she informed the ninja that Borg would like to see them in his office.

P.I.X.A.L. guided Master Wu, Nya, and their students on a tour through Borg Industries, stating the inventor's beliefs that technology was the key to making dreams come true. She showed them several of his latest inventions, including virtual reality gaming where the player was scanned into the game itself.

However while P.I.X.A.L. was showing off the factory line, the Digital Overlord took control of the system, including the android. She attacked the group with the rest of the machines, though they managed to escape due to the ninja arriving in a hover-copter.

As the sun rose over New Ninjago City the next morning, P.I.X.A.L. used her scans of Zane to create more upgraded Nindroids to serve the Overlord and began manufacturing them in the factory.

The Art of the Silent Fist[]

While the Nindroids searched for the ninja, P.I.X.A.L. used the technology in Borg Tower to extract the Golden Power Lloyd had used on a Security Mech in an attempt to create a body for the Overlord. Yet the transference required more power than they had, and the Overlord was forced back into his digital prison where he took control of the assimilated Cyrus Borg. He ordered P.I.X.A.L. to find the Golden Ninja, but she pointed out that the Techno Blades were a higher priority as they could erase him.

Learning of Garmadon's monastery from Wu's memory, the Overlord ordered P.I.X.A.L. and a squad of Nindroids to retrieve the Techno Blades. Fortunately, her attempted theft was quickly spotted by Zane, who easily caught up to her. The Spider's Design[]

P.I.X.A.L. tracks the Ninja’s movements along the abandoned N-line and informs Master Wu and Skylor of them. Later, she tells them (along with Nya who just arrived) that sensors suggest that an explosion of some kind occurred in the tunnel and further details regarding the incident are unknown.

The Fall of the Monastery[]

P.I.X.A.L. fights alongside Master Wu, Nya, and Skylor to defend the Monastery against several Crystal Council members (Aspheera, Pythor, Vangelis, and the Mechanic) who came for the Golden Weapons of Spinjitzu. She counters and dodges the Mechanic’s attacks by herself until he propels her against the stairs and injures her, to Master Wu’s concern. P.I.X.A.L. then warns Nya seconds before an invisible Pythor captures her.

P.I.X.A.L. rushes to Master Wu’s side when he collapses from the Council’s attacks. Sensing the unfairness of the fight, P.I.X.A.L. summons the MiniPix (revealing their existence to the others) and orders them to attack the present Council members. The MiniPix fare well against the villains until an army of Crystal Spiders arrive from the main elevator and nearly destroy the MiniPix.

P.I.X.A.L. and the others soon collapse from their injuries and exhaustion while the Council Members steal the Golden Weapons, activate the Crystal Spiders' self-destruction sequences, and exit through the escape elevator.

After the last functioning MiniPix saves P.I.X.A.L., Master Wu, and Skylor, they notice Nya’s absence. P.I.X.A.L. and Skylor prevent Master Wu from charging into the Monastery and tell him that he cannot help Nya. She and Skylor comfort him as they believe Nya perished in the explosion. However, Nya soon bursts out of the broken Monastery in her Samurai X Mech, and the team expresses their joy at her survival.

Moments later, Zane contacts P.I.X.A.L. from the abandoned N-line and asks for assistance. P.I.X.A.L. expresses her relief at his and the Ninja’s survival before Nya departs to help them. P.I.X.A.L. remains behind at the burning Monastery with Skylor and Master Wu.

Darkness Within[]

At some point, P.I.X.A.L., Master Wu, and Skylor travel to Nya's old Samurai X Cave. P.I.X.A.L. also names the remaining MiniPix, calling them MiniPix Seven in honor of their effort in saving her, Master Wu, and Skylor from the Monastery. They then work with Master Wu to restart the old Samurai X Cave's computers.

She embraces Zane upon his arrival and introduces the ninja to MiniPix Seven.

Crystastrophe[]

P.I.X.A.L. and Skylor remain in the Samurai X Cave and monitor the Ninja’s progress against the Crystal Army. Seeing the ninja struggling to defeat their opponents, P.I.X.A.L. asks them to gather a sample of the crystals from the Vengestone Warriors so she can devise a means of shielding and defensive capabilities.

A Lesson in Anger[]

After watching Garmadon train Lloyd for some time, Master Wu asks P.I.X.A.L. for assistance in reaching Misako at the Library of Domu. She agrees and they leave, prompting an angry Garmadon to call them "backseat drivers".

At some point during Lloyd’s training, P.I.X.A.L. upgrades the Ninja’s vehicles, “coating them in a nano-tech-polymer” which allows them to draw power from Crystal Temple (instead of being weakened by it) and activate a special power boost. She warns them not to use the power boost too often or else it may damage their vehicles. She then presents them to the Ninja to their delight and Garmadon’s disgust.

Brave But Foolish[]

Sometime after upgrading the Ninja’s vehicles, P.I.X.A.L. modifies Wu’s Dragon Armor, inserting makeshift wings inside so Wu can utilize the armor to fly.

Return of the Ice Emperor[]

P.I.X.A.L. defeats two Crystal Warriors before finding Zane’s broken jet. At first, she is confused when she spots Clutch Powers in the cockpit, but realizes that the crash must have damaged Zane’s cloaking technology when Clutch transforms into Akita, Racer Seven, and Benthomar.

P.I.X.A.L. opens the cockpit and multiple parts of Zane’s body fall out. Zane’s head then morphs to the Ice Emperor’s and declares that “There is no Zane,”.

P.I.X.A.L. then gathers Zane’s dismantled body and carries him on her back, deciding to take Zane to Borg Tower to repair him there. She hides behind a broken vehicle when she spots a gathering of Crystal Warriors and asks Zane to be quiet. In response, the damaged Zane orders P.I.X.A.L. not to “tell (him) what to do” and calls her a “non-frozen fool”. Due to Zane’s noisy outburst, the pair becomes surrounded by Crystal Warriors. P.I.X.A.L. then tells Zane that she loves him and for a brief moment, Zane’s cloaking technology glitches and reveals Zane’s real face, indicating that a part of him received her message. However, Zane (as the Ice Emperor) dismisses her comment, scoffing that “Love is for the weak,”. P.I.X.A.L. rolls her eyes in annoyance.

Suddenly, a Kryptarium Prison bus rams into the Crystal Warriors. P.I.X.A.L. and Zane board the bus and she expresses her surprise when she spots Ronin, Killow, and Ultra Violet there. P.I.X.A.L. tells Ronin to drive to Borg Tower and asks why the criminals helped her and Zane. Fugi-Dove and Ultra Violet recount how the criminals were released from prison and became inspired to fight the Crystal Army. P.I.X.A.L. then thanks Ronin and advises him to ignore Zane when he expresses gratitude through his Ice Emperor persona and “plays footsie” with Ronin’s hair. In response, Zane orders a non-existent Vex to “…cast (P.I.X.A.L. and Ronin) in the dungeon”. P.I.X.A.L. scowls at him while Ronin shakes his head.

At Borg Tower, P.I.X.A.L. informs her distracted creator of the Crystal King's invasion, asks him to repair Zane, and comments on how little time they have.

Compatible[]

When Master Wu contacts the ninja using the Paperkids' radio, P.I.X.A.L. informs him that she is with Zane. She also mentions that she can provide the team with some assistance (i.e. Ronin and his gang in their new mechs provided by Cyrus Borg). However, when Fugi-Dove plays with his mech and hits Killow and Ronin, P.I.X.A.L. remarks that she “do(es) not know how reliable they will be,”.

Later, P.I.X.A.L. aids Ultra Violet with her mech. When Ultra Violet declares that she could “take down those stupid ninja” with it, P.I.X.A.L. frowns at her. Seeing P.I.X.A.L.’s expression, Ultra Violet hastily backtracks, assuring her that she will “only save lives and be good.” Then, Fugi-Dove asks P.I.X.A.L. if his new name (Mecha-Dove), catchphrase (“My whirring servos fill the night!”) , and "new moves" are awesome after nearly crushing P.I.X.A.L. and crashing his mech into one of Dr. Borg’s computer screens. She hesitates before nodding. P.I.X.A.L. also attempts to help Killow control his mech before Cyrus Borg asks for her.

P.I.X.A.L. inquires about Zane’s condition and Cyrus Borg compares it to a coma. When P.I.X.A.L. remarks how some “...coma patients are known to recover,”, Dr. Borg tells her that since Zane is a nindroid, that may not be the case. P.I.X.A.L. informs him that she understands and asks him to try the best he can to bring Zane online.

Before departing with the others to fight the Crystal Army, P.I.X.A.L. checks once more to see if Zane regained consciousness. After no response, she tells the team that she will say her goodbyes to Zane before leaving Borg Tower with them.

P.I.X.A.L. holds Zane’s hand and voices a heartfelt farewell to him regarding how much he has done for her and means to her. She recalls how Zane gave her half of his heart during the Overlord’s previous invasion and taught her to feel human emotions, including love. She promises to return to him after the battle if she survives. If not, she tells him she will meet him in the afterlife, if such a place exists for nindroids. P.I.X.A.L. gives him a final hug and kiss before turning to leave with the others.

Before P.I.X.A.L. and Ronin’s gang depart, Zane’s eyes glow, signifying his emergence from the coma, and he asks for P.I.X.A.L. She turns in shock and leaps into his arms, exclaiming her joy at his awakening. When Dr. Borg asks Zane how he arose from his coma, Zane replies that P.I.X.A.L.’s voice helped him remember and tells her that she “...brought him back,”.

Their reunion brings a smile to Dr. Borg's and Ronin’s faces and moves Killow and Fugi/Mecha-Dove to tears.

Annoyed by the length of this interaction and Fugi/Mecha-Dove’s sobbing, Ultra Violet groans and asks P.I.X.A.L. if she’s ready to leave.

P.I.X.A.L. then contacts Minipix Seven, asking them to bring the Ninja Ultra Combo Mech to the battlefield. Afterwards, she declares that she is ready to fight.

Following the defeat of the Crystal King, P.I.X.A.L. traveled with Zane to the Samurai X Cave in the Sea of Sand (as the one in the Monastery was destroyed) to devise a way to return Zane’s powers to him. Family[]

Cyrus Borg[]

“P.I.X.A.L., remember this: you belong to no one. If you want to be with Zane, then you should be with him.”
— Cyrus Borg to P.I.X.A.L., "The Curse of the Golden Master"

Cyrus Borg is P.I.X.A.L.'s creator/father.

P.I.X.A.L. was created by Cyrus Borg to be his assistant and to help him run his company, Borg Industries. Unlike Zane and his creator, Dr. Julien, P.I.X.A.L. and Dr. Borg appear to have a mainly professional relationship, absent of aspects typical of a familial bond. However, as time passes, their undeniable concern for one another is revealed.

In "The Curse of the Golden Master", P.I.X.A.L. confesses her desire to be with Zane to Dr. Borg. In addition, she expresses her reluctance to abandon her creator despite her new feelings by stating her confusion as to who she belongs to (Cyrus Borg for creating her or Zane for saving her). Borg assures P.I.X.A.L. that that he would not interfere with her decision and that she does not belong to anyone, revealing his perception of her as an individual, not simply a creation constructed to serve him.

Later in "Enter the Digiverse", P.I.X.A.L. expresses sorrow when her creator is abducted by Nindroids and undoubtedly considers helping him before he orders her to remain with and protect the Ninja. After the Ninja complete their mission, P.I.X.A.L. gazes at Cyrus’s abandoned wheelchair solemnly, indicating her care for the wellbeing of her creator.

In "The Invitation", Cyrus Borg states that he hasn’t seen or spoken to “...P.I.X.A.L. since Zane’s memorial… (and that) It’s heartbreaking,” to Lloyd and Wu. This brief comment reveals his concern for P.I.X.A.L. and her feelings in the wake of Zane’s sacrifice in Rebooted.

After being installed into Zane's systems during the Tournament of Elements, P.I.X.A.L. most likely remained in contact with Cyrus Borg.

As Samurai X, P.I.X.A.L. was determined to save her creator from the Time Twins and their Vermillion horde. She was successful in her mission and stood by his side, as the ninja defeated their foes. Borg most likely did not know P.I.X.A.L. was his savior at the time.

P.I.X.A.L. was distraught when Borg was found frozen by the Oni, and a statement by Nya referring to Borg as P.I.X.A.L.'s father indicates that the Ninja and (possibly) P.I.X.A.L. see Borg as such.

In Prime Empire, P.I.X.A.L. visited her creator in Borg Industries with the Ninja. Unlike her friends, she clasped her hands behind her back, indicating her respect for her creator and that a part of her may still see him as an authority (instead of a parental) figure.

While it is unclear whether or not P.I.X.A.L. and Dr. Borg see each other as family, they undoubtedly care for one another.

Love Interest[]

Zane[]

“Zane. I was once just a Nindroid. A machine. And then, long ago, in a different battle, you gave up half your power source to save me. In that moment, you changed me. I felt things I never felt before. I felt love. For you. Our friends need us now, and I must go to battle. If I am able, I will return. I do not know if there is anything after this life for beings such as you or I, but if there is, I will find you there. Goodbye, my Zane.”
— P.I.X.A.L. to Zane, "Compatible"

Zane first encountered P.I.X.A.L. when he and the other ninja arrived at Cyrus Borg's building and he felt an attraction towards her and she was confused by his functioning. During the conflict with The Overlord, P.I.X.A.L. accompanied and soon broke free from her basic programming to help Zane. Her actions brought time for the ninja, though her power source caused her to shut down, to Zane's sadness. However, she would be given half of Zane's power source to save her and would begin to feel human emotions with both saying the other was vital to them. Indeed, they would often come to the other's aid on many occasions after the Cryptor and the Nindroids attack. Their attraction was noted by a few characters such as Edna, Nya, and Kai, who all expressed happiness at Zane having found someone so special to him. When Zane sacrificed himself, P.I.X.A.L. was the one who took his death notably hard, to the point where she didn't attend his funeral. Because of this, she would be the first person to see him reconstruct himself in a new updated body, and smiled greatly when he revealed his presence to her in the Borg Industries computers by asking if they were now compatible.

In between Seasons 3 and 4, the two were captured by Ronin and brought to Chen, who held them prisoner on his secluded island to draw out the other ninja. In the early stages of the fourth season, Zane in his imprison state was amnesiac, and it was P.I.X.A.L. who guided him back to his memories while telling him she was a friend. The return of his memory is signaled by Zane asking P.I.X.A.L. if they are compatible, which she affirms. When Zane broke out, he was disheartened to learn that P.I.X.A.L. had been dismantled. Despite P.I.X.A.L.'s urging to leave without her, he refused to abandon her, placing her memory card into his system and allowing her to continue the journey with him—audible only as a voice inside his head, and visible in his field of vision as a small image on a screen. It was P.I.X.A.L. who helped Zane in conquering his fears and unlocking his dragon.

P.I.X.A.L. would continue offering aid to Zane in his later fights such as with Morro. During Skybound, when Zane came face to face with Nadakhan, P.I.X.A.L. tried to warn him of the consequence, but he ignored her warning. He came to regret his choice when Nadakhan removed her from his processor, which left him distraught at the thought of living without her and forced him to make his last wish for it to go away. Due to Jay's last wish, Zane was reunited with P.I.X.A.L. and would continue to ask for her input in later adventures.

In Season 7: The Hands of Time, when an attack by the Time Twins put Zane offline, P.I.X.A.L. expresses worry and tries to wake him. When Zane was reactivated by Nya, he was confused by P.I.X.A.L. not responding to him and became sad as he realized he was alone.

He overcame this grief in Season 8: Sons of Garmadon, as she soon returned to him. He was later shocked to see her in her new samurai gear and learn she was the new Samurai X, the vigilante hero. P.I.X.A.L. spoke to Zane where she asked him if he was upset that she was no longer in his head. To her surprise, Zane stated said he likes seeing her with a body again and told P.I.X.A.L. that he was fine with her being Samurai X as it made her happy, which left her touched as they promised to be honest and not to keep secrets from each other.

In "Big Trouble, Little Ninjago," P.I.X.A.L. was under the impression that Zane was dead and was sad at his loss. She was unaware he was transported to another realm.

In "Firstbourne," P.I.X.A.L. is sad about losing her companion and wants to honor his memory by continuing to fight. However, she learns that he was instead teleported to somewhere after she and the others find a piece of a leaf for traveler's tea and were happy Zane was still alive.

In "Green Destiny," P.I.X.A.L. hears that Zane and the ninja have returned and breaks out of custody to see him again. She is reunited with him and they hug after peace is restored to Ninjago. After the conflict, they hold hands at the celebration.

After Zane's sacrifice to defeat Aspheera in "Vengeance is Mine!," she was very devastated to lose him again and broke down at his fate.

During "A Cold Goodbye," P.I.X.A.L. has a nightmare of Zane's fate before waking up screaming and looking at a portrait of them together. She spoke with the ninja on the loss, before a frantic Wu came in and told her Zane was still alive. P.I.X.A.L. was surprised but overjoyed to learn that her companion was okay but worried about the dangers of the Never-Realm.

In "The Kaiju Protocol," she tells Wu to inform Zane that she loves him if she fails to defeat The Preeminent, admitting how much she valued Zane.

During "Awakenings," the ninja found Zane and returned home, with her giving him a big hug in their reunion.

In the following season, P.I.X.A.L. and Zane search for Milton Dyer together in Ninjago City. In "Ninjago Confidential", Zane dresses as and pretends to be a detective, claiming that “this method has to work,”. P.I.X.A.L. expresses her confusion at Zane’s roleplaying, but hesitantly “plays along”.

P.I.X.A.L. protects Zane when a number of the patrons at Laughy’s are insulted by his “detective narration” and he declares happily that P.I.X.A.L. “…had saved (his) nindroid bacon,”.

Later, P.I.X.A.L. voices her irritation with Zane’s role-playing. He then speaks to his belief that she secretly loves it. P.I.X.A.L. then asserts that “(Her) heart did not reveal that” and “Every part of (her) is annoyed,” while glaring at him. When they investigate Buddy’s Pizza for Dyer, the Mechanic kidnaps Zane. P.I.X.A.L. becomes worried and saddened by Zane's disappearance. Upon spotting Zane’s abandoned detective hat, P.I.X.A.L. groans before donning it, adopting Zane’s “detective theory” and showing that she appreciates her companion even when his “acting” peeves her.

When P.I.X.A.L. later finds the Mechanic forcing Zane to power his Manifestation Gate, she quickly moves to help him. She battles the Mechanic’s thugs alone before attempting to free Zane from the portal machine. She eventually does so with Jay’s help and expresses her relief when Zane survives the Gate's effects.

P.I.X.A.L. and Zane work together to topple a lamppost onto a Whack Rat’s car and seize it to travel to Borg Industries. Later, when the Ninja are released from Prime Empire, Zane and P.I.X.A.L. link arms and greet their friends.

In “The Call of The Deep”, P.I.X.A.L. and Zane investigate a distress call together. After P.I.X.A.L.’s mech crashes into the ocean, Zane attempts to help her with a grappling hook but fails. They both try to contact each other to no success. When P.I.X.A.L. regains power and safely breaches the surface, Zane expresses his happiness.

In “Unsinkable”, the pair joins Jay, Nya, and Lloyd to investigate a mysterious signal in the Tartarus Trench. Zane henceforth pretends to be a pirate (similar to his detective roleplaying in “Ninjago Confidential'') and P.I.X.A.L. sighs in response.

In “The Fall of the Monastery”, P.I.X.A.L. is reassured when Zane contacts her following the explosion in the abandoned N-Line. Similarly, in “Darkness Within”, Zane anxiously asks Nya if P.I.X.A.L. is alright after learning of the Crystal Council’s attack on the Monastery. When he arrives at Nya’s old Samurai X cave in the same episode, P.I.X.A.L. hugs him and asks if he was injured in the recent events. He replies that he “...is fine,” and holds her hand.

In “Return of the Ice Emperor”, P.I.X.A.L. searches for Zane in the streets of Ninjago City after learning that his jet crashed while battling the Crystal King’s dragonides. She finds him grievously injured and carries him on her back. When they become surrounded by Crystal Warriors, P.I.X.A.L. senses that this may be their last moment together and then tells Zane that she loves him for the first time. For a brief moment, Zane’s cloaking technology glitches and reveals Zane’s real, beaming face, indicating that a part of him understood her message. However, the dismantled Zane dismisses her comment due to his damaged memory banks. While P.I.X.A.L. feels annoyance at his behavior, she recognizes his need for assistance. When they are subsequently rescued by Ronin and his gang, they travel to Borg Tower. When they arrive, P.I.X.A.L. asks her creator to help her repair Zane.

In “Compatible”, P.I.X.A.L. inquires about Zane’s condition and Cyrus Borg compares it to a coma. When P.I.X.A.L. remarks how some “...coma patients are known to recover,” Dr. Borg tells her that since Zane is a nindroid, that may not be the case. P.I.X.A.L. informs him that she understands and asks him to try the best he can to bring Zane online.

Before departing with the others to fight the Crystal Army, P.I.X.A.L. checks once more to see if Zane regained consciousness. After no response, she tells the team that she will say her goodbyes to Zane before leaving Borg Tower with them.

P.I.X.A.L. holds Zane’s hand and voices a heartfelt farewell to him regarding how much he has done for her and means to her. She recalls how Zane gave her half of his heart during the Overlord’s previous invasion and taught her to feel human emotions, including love. She promises to return to him after the battle if she survives. If not, she tells him she will meet him in the afterlife, if such a place exists for nindroids. P.I.X.A.L. gives him a final hug and kiss before turning to leave with the others.

Before P.I.X.A.L. and Ronin’s gang depart, Zane emerges from his coma and asks for P.I.X.A.L. She turns in shock and leaps into his arms, exclaiming her joy at his awakening. When Dr. Borg asks Zane how he arose from his coma, Zane replies that P.I.X.A.L.’s voice helped him remember and tells her that she “...brought him back,”.

Their reunion brings a smile to Dr. Borg's and Ronin’s faces and moves Killow and Fugi-Dove to tears. P.I.X.A.L. and Zane subsequently travel to the Paperkids’ Warehouse with Ronin’s gang to defend it from the Crystal Army.

In “Dragon Form”, When Mister F chases P.I.X.A.L. on his dragonide, Zane intervenes and battles him, resulting in Mister F’s defeat. Later, Zane and P.I.X.A.L. embrace after defeating more members of the Crystal Army.

In “Roots”, P.I.X.A.L. and Zane rebuild the Monastery with the others and are last seen carrying a bathtub together.

After The Merge, P.I.X.A.L. was among the ones that were missing, leaving Zane saddened when he came back in the team. He tried to find her by trying to operate the Portal gate and taped a photo of her to a broom and would talk to it as if she were around.

In "The Great Zane Robbery," Zane found Pixal in a Techno-cocoon and woke her up. He explained to her how much he struggled in his time without her, and that her voice was the thing that kept him going when things seemed hopeless.

The couple spends as much time together as possible before Thunderfang's release and planned to go exploring the Merged Lands together to make up for lost time. They began their adventure in the techno balloon in "The Hollow Ones".

Friends

Friends[]

Ninja[]

“And you should step away from my friends.”
— P.I.X.A.L. to Aspheera, "Vengeance is Mine!"

The ninja were accepting of her and supported Zane's affections to her in Season 3.

During Season 4, they were amazed that P.I.X.A.L. is now integrated into Zane's system and helped them with strategies. Since she was installed in Zane's system, P.I.X.A.L. started working with the ninja, even though she could not fight with them.

In "Dead Man's Squall," P.I.X.A.L. revealed her status as Samurai X as they were surprised at this. While being controlled, she fought the ninja, winning the battle and destroying one of the Bounty's engines. After regaining control, P.I.X.A.L. officially joined the team as Samurai X and promised to devote herself to the work of protecting Ninjago alongside them.

In "Big Trouble, Little Ninjago," P.I.X.A.L. was under the impression that her friends were dead and were sad at their loss. She was unaware they were transported to another realm.

In "Firstbourne," P.I.X.A.L. continues to help Lloyd and the others but is sad about losing her other friends. However, she learns that they were instead teleported to somewhere after she and the other find a piece of a leaf for traveler's tea.

During "The Gilded Path," P.I.X.A.L. continued fighting by the remaining ninja's side when they were attacked by Garmadon. When Garmadon was attempting to crush Lloyd's group, she intervened on her friends' behalf. Her interference had allowed Lloyd's group to escape the enemy, even though she was captured by their enemies.

In "Green Destiny," while imprisoned P.I.X.A.L. hears that the rest of the ninja returned and was happy to hear this before she breaks out of custody to see them again. She offers them aid in taking down the Colossus and celebrates peace being restored to the city with her friends.

In "A Cold Goodbye," after she wakes up from a nightmare about Zane’s supposed death they comfort her about their loss until Wu comes in and reveals that Zane is alive. She later helps them in detaining their master and traveling to the Never-Realm but concerned after she hears the danger of the area.

During "Awakenings," she was elated with them returning home with Zane.

After Nya was merged with the Endless Sea, she was a guest at Nya's memorial, mourning at the loss of her friend.

When The Merge happened the ninja and P.I.X.A.L. were lost in the Merged Lands. At the time of "The Temple of the Dragon Cores" the team was reconstituted but Jay, Cole, Master Wu and P.I.X.A.L. were still missing so the present ninja wanted to find them.

When P.I.X.A.L. finally reunited with the ninja after Zane find her, her friends were very happy to see her, especialy Lloyd who hugged her twice and express his joy at meet her again, as well as Nya who also threw herself into P.I.X.A.L.'s arms when she saw her enter in the Dragon stables behind the others in "Their Draconic Majesty's Request". P.I.X.A.L. also met the new ninja for the first time.

Wu[]

“P.I.X.A.L., you are amazing.”
— Wu to P.I.X.A.L., "The Kaiju Protocol"

She and the old master get along and show themselves to be friends.

They first meet in Season 3 and faintly acquainted after that.

In Season 8, she is informed of the ninja that Wu has become an infant and agreed with Zane's theory of him returning to his proper age.

During "A Cold Goodbye," P.I.X.A.L. learns from frantic Wu that Zane was still alive. P.I.X.A.L. was surprised but overjoyed to learn that her companion was okay but worried about the dangers of the Never-Realm. When Wu forbade the ninja from trying to go there, she helped the ninja in stealing the Scrolls of Forbidden Spinjitzu from him after tying him up.

During "The Kaiju Protocol," she and Wu try to open a portal to the Never-Realm to retrieve the ninja. However, their actions end up releasing the Preeminent, which proceeds to attack the city. While P.I.X.A.L. goes to stop the creature, Wu stays behind to open a portal to the Departed Realm. When she is overwhelmed by the beast she was also using drastic measures, as Wu tries to warn her of the danger of what could happen but after she defeats the creature he is relieved. The pair then proceed to regain hope of rescuing the ninja.

During "Awakenings," she alerts him of a portal opening and they watch as the ninja return home.

MiniPix[]

“I call them 'MiniPix'.”
— P.I.X.A.L. to Wu, "The Fall of the Monastery"

The MiniPix are P.I.X.A.L.'s creations/assistants.

P.I.X.A.L. created the MiniPix (along with MiniPix Seven) to assist her in building vehicles and the Hangar Bay for the Ninja (between March of the Oni and Secrets of Forbidden Spinjitzu). They appear to get along very well and spend a significant amount of time working together when the Ninja leave on their missions (to the Never-Realm, Prime Empire [though perhaps not as much in this instance since she worked with Zane and Master Wu to track down Milton Dyer], Shintaro Mountain, and the Island of the Keepers).

When the Crystal Council attacked in "The Fall of the Monastery", P.I.X.A.L. called on the MiniPix to help and they readily began to fire upon the Council using the lasers P.I.X.A.L. equipped them with. The MiniPix were willing to risk themselves for their creator and her friends, showcasing their loyalty.

MiniPix Seven[]

“This one saved our lives. Giving it a name seemed the least I could do.”
— P.I.X.A.L. (about naming MiniPix Seven), "Darkness Within"

MiniPix Seven is one of P.I.X.A.L.'s creations/assistants.

P.I.X.A.L. designed and built the MiniPix (including MiniPix Seven) to assist her in building vehicles and weapons for the Ninja. After the Crystal Council attacked the Monastery and knocked P.I.X.A.L., Wu, Nya, and Skylor unconscious, MiniPix Seven briefly paused to repair herself before dragging Wu, Skylor, and their creator to the elevator and outside the Monastery, delivering them safely out of the Crystal Spiders' bombs blast radius. However, due to the amount of debris covering Nya, MiniPix Seven failed to locate her.

At some point between “The Fall of the Monastery” and “Darkness Within”, P.I.X.A.L., MiniPix Seven, Master Wu, and Skylor travel to Nya’s old Samurai X Cave. P.I.X.A.L. decides to give MiniPix Seven their name in honor of their effort in saving her, Master Wu, and Skylor from the Monastery. They then work with Master Wu to restart the old Samurai X Cave’s computers. When the Ninja return from their incident in the abandoned N-line, P.I.X.A.L. proudly introduces them to MiniPix Seven.

In “A Lesson in Anger”, P.I.X.A.L. and MiniPix Seven upgrade the Ninja’s vehicles, “coating them in a nano-tech-polymer” which allows them to draw power from Crystal Temple (instead of being weakened by it) and activate a special power boost.

In “Compatible”, P.I.X.A.L. contacts MiniPix Seven from her communicator and asks them to bring the Ninja Ultra Combo Mech to the battlefield and they readily agree.

In “Dragon Form”, MiniPix Seven successfully delivers the mech to P.I.X.A.L. and she both exclaim their happiness in reuniting with one another.

Milton Dyer[]

“Incredible.”
— Milton Dyer (reacting to P.I.X.A.L.'s bravery), "The Prodigal Father"

Milton Dyer is P.I.X.A.L.'s ally during the events of Prime Empire (more specifically from "Ninjago Confidential" onward).

P.I.X.A.L. first encounters Dyer on the roof of Buddy’s Pizza while she was masquerading as a detective in order to find him. Soon after, they both realize that Dyer’s creation (the Artificial Intelligence running Prime Empire) was in fact the “villain” Unagami who sought to trap people in the game and escape into the real world of Ninjago.

Soon after, P.I.X.A.L. shows Dyer the Prime Empire console in Buddy’s Pizza to the latter’s shock and he recounts the story of how Scott got trapped in Prime Empire. Dyer soon remarks that he thought Unagami was only a harmless program and that he couldn’t have known Unagami was alive because machines don’t experience real emotions, only simulations of them. To this, P.I.X.A.L. angrily replies that emotions of “sufficiently advanced programs” are no less real than those of humans.

Soon, Master Wu sends P.I.X.A.L. a panic signal from his communicator and she rushes to help him. Before she departs, Milton Dyer asks if she should call the police, as the situation sounds dangerous. However, P.I.X.A.L. replies, “My friends are in danger. There’s no time!” Before following her, Dyer appears perplexed that P.I.X.A.L., being an android, can conceive of and form friendships with others.

At the Mechanic's warehouse, Dyer is soon astounded by P.I.X.A.L.’s expressions of concern for the captured Zane and Wu and remarks how she must be programmed incredibly well to simulate feelings so accurately. To this, P.I.X.A.L. replies: “Why can you not understand? Any sufficiently advanced program like Unagami, like myself, is no less real than you.”

When the Mechanic begins to torture Zane, P.I.X.A.L. leaps down from the rafters to rescue him, despite being outnumbered by the Mechanic’s thugs and Dyer’s awe at this prompts him to call her “Incredible.”

Later, Dyer assists P.I.X.A.L. by uniting Master Wu during her battle with the Mechanic’s thugs.

Because of P.I.X.A.L., Dyer was able to accept that advanced technological beings are in fact sentient and have the realizations that motivated him to apologize to and connect with Unagami.

Ronin (formerly)[]

P.I.X.A.L. and Ronin first interacted between the events of “The Titanium Ninja” and “The Invitation”. Ronin was ordered to kidnap Zane for Chen and discovered P.I.X.A.L. with the newly-built nindroid in Borg Industries. Ronin abducted them but felt slightly guilty for doing so. However, the threat of Chen and Clouse hurting his family motivated him enough to complete the task, despite his unease (Source: Way of the Departed, Chapter 22).

Ronin’s actions led to P.I.X.A.L. being scrapped by Chen, Zane’s imprisonment and loss of his elemental powers, and the Ninja’s engagement in the Tournament of Elements. While it is unclear how P.I.X.A.L. felt about Ronin specifically, she was adamant about how she wanted Zane to stop Chen (Ronin’s “boss”). In addition, she felt deeply saddened about being dismantled and unable to join Zane and the other Ninja in leaving Chen’s Island (She later overcame those feelings almost entirely when Zane’s actions allowed her to travel with him and leave the island as a part of his circuitry).

In "Public Enemy Number One", while P.I.X.A.L. was guiding Zane as an AI program in his processor, Ronin hacked into Zane remotely to capture him for the Police Commissioner. In doing so, the bounty hunter disabled both him and P.I.X.A.L., preventing Zane from fighting back and P.I.X.A.L. from protecting her companion electronically, marking the second instance Ronin deliberately attacked P.I.X.A.L.

P.I.X.A.L. and Ronin appear not to interact face-to-face again until many years later when the Crystal King, his Council, and his Army overran Ninjago. When P.I.X.A.L. and a damaged Zane were surrounded by Crystal Warriors, Ronin rammed a hijacked prison bus into them, saving her and Zane. Ronin then told her how he inspired the inmates to rise up against the Crystal Army. P.I.X.A.L. was shocked at Ronin’s change of heart but soon grew to see him as an ally and even created a battle mech for him with her creator’s help.

Ronin beamed when P.I.X.A.L. was reunited with a (now functioning) Zane in “Compatible” and clasped Cyrus Borg’s shoulder in happiness.

Chen and his Anacondrai Cultists[]

“Find your friends (and) stop Chen. I know you have it in you.”
— P.I.X.A.L. to Zane, "Versus"

Chen and Clouse employed Ronin to capture Zane and he ended up abducting P.I.X.A.L. as well. When the two nindroids arrived on Chen's Island, Chen had P.I.X.A.L. dismantled and locked her and Zane in adjacent cells.

As revealed in “Versus”, P.I.X.A.L. felt deeply saddened about being scrapped (though she later overcame those feelings) and adamantly declared (to Zane) that the actions of Chen and his Anacondrai Cultists must be prevented by him and the Ninja. One could infer that P.I.X.A.L. held anger for Chen and the Anacondrai cultists due to their malicious treatment of her, holding Zane hostage, using Zane to bait her Ninja friends to participate in the Tournament of Elements, plotting and almost succeeding to initiate a massive war, and harm her friends by stealing their elemental powers.

Sons of Garmadon[]

“Step away from the pedestrians.”
— P.I.X.A.L. to three Sons of Garmadon members, "The Jade Princess"

As Samurai X, P.I.X.A.L. would often face them and stop them from picking on others. Unbeknownst to her, Harumi was their leader the Quiet One, and took control of P.I.X.A.L. to make her attack the ninja. She was freed from the control and help the ninja face them.

When Garmadon was resurrected, P.I.X.A.L. joined the resistance to battle them for control of the city. However, she was captured and placed in Kryptarium Prison but broke out after hearing the ninja returned.
