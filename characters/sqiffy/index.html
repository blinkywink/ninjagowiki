Overview

“Nadakhan's right, they are planning to attack. Don't worry, I can read lips.”
— Sqiffy to Bucko, "Operation Land Ho!"

Landon is a citizen of Ninjago. During the construction of Djinjago, Landon and his colleague, Colin, sought to become Sky Pirates, and was given the name Sqiffy by Nadakhan in the progress. This wouldn't last long, as due to Jay's final wish reversing the recent events, Landon would never become a Sky Pirate.

History

History[]

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Wishmasters[]

Alongside his colleague, Colin, he sought to become a Sky Pirate and was likewise introduced by Flintlocke to Nadakhan. Introducing himself, Sqiffy and his partner Bucko, were promptly dismissed.

Operation Land Ho![]

Alongside Bucko, he would attempt to spy on Jay and his team of Ninja Replacements, relying on his lip-reading skills. However, Jay would fool him by uttering random gibberish, and Sqiffy would be forced to escape upon realizing he and Bucko had been spotted. Flying away on their vehicles, Sqiffy would ultimately be captured by Captain Soto and arrested by the Police Commissioner.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

Like Son, Like Father[]

During the semi-finals of Dareth's Grand Karate Tournament, Sqiffy battled against Kai to win and earn the title of Grand Karate champion, but lost.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • His name is rare in that it includes the letter Q with no U after it. However, in the series it is still pronounced as if it has a U.
  • In the Character Bios,[citation needed] Bucko is said to have originally been named Landon, when in reality this was Sqiffy's true name. Additionally, Nadakhan originally named him Landon, but after hearing that Sqiffy's original name was Landon, he came up with the names Bucko and Sqiffy for them.
  • Alongside Bucko, it's unknown if he used all, if any, of his wishes with Nadakhan, as his captain never sought them out due to Clancee being closer.
  • Sqiffy has a generic black LEGO hairstyle, first appearing on Town characters.
