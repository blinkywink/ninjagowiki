Agent Denholt[1] is an Administration Agent.

History

History[]

They Call It Doom[]

During their routine examination of the Mother Garden's legality, Denholt and Karit were taken by surprise when they came across Rapton, which led to the unexpected discovery of the pink Dragon Core. Denholt incapacitated Rapton using her blaster, tasing him. Frustrated, Denholt remarked that their simple task had turned into uncovering an cosmic power source. Karit suggested taking it back to the Administration. Denholt activated a portal, and the two exited through it.
