Nobu[1] is one of the Writers of Destiny.

History

History[]

Background[]

Nobu was taken as Fenwick's apprentice because he reminded him of his brother Quanish.[2] However, his similarities to Quanish began to challenge Fenwick's rule of the Cloud Kingdom.

Kingdom Come[]

Nobu was writing in the main hall as the ninja arrived and looked in astonishment at them when Fenwick lead them by.

Later, while he was writing about the ninja, his ink turned red, signalling they were in danger. Nobu rushed to report this to the Master Writer, only to find that he had made a deal with Morro and locked the ninja away for Nimbus to play with.

Nobu followed Fenwick and the ghosts to the tallest tower where the Sword of Sanctuary was kept, only to encounter the ninja there as well. He informed them of Fenwick's betrayal and showed them where the sword was kept in the hopes that they would be able to get it first.

Shortly thereafter, Nobu saw Nimbus grab the ninja's boat while they were trying to follow Morro. Knowing he had to do something, Nobu ran into the library where he altered Fenwick's destiny to be chased by Nimbus, allowing for the ninja to escape, which in turn punished Fenwick for his betrayal.

Curseworld, Part II[]

Nobu observed Morro and Lloyd fighting over the Realm Crystal as they fell from the sky, but was ordered not to intervene by another monk, who stated that their fate was for them to decide.

Personality

Personality[]

He is shown to be a kind young man, helping the ninja by telling them of Fenwick's betrayal and telling them where to retrieve the Sword of Sanctuary. He also helped them escaped Nimbus by altering Fenwick's destiny as punishment for his betrayal.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Destiny Writing: Like all writers in the Cloud Kingdom, Nobu had the power to write and/or alter one's destiny, as he did with Fenwick's to save the ninja.

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • He has the same hairpiece as child Lloyd, Wu, Garmadon and several other characters.
  • He has Merry's face design from LEGO The Lord of the Rings.
  • His torso design is based on Ki-Adi-Mundi's original torso from LEGO Star Wars.
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • While his name is never stated in the episode itself, it was revealed in the end credits.
  • Nobu is a Japanese name (like Misako).
    • Nobu means "faith."
    • That means technically there are two characters in the show named Faith: Nobu and Faith herself.
      • Including Nobu from the Spinjitzu Smash! web game, there are three characters in the franchise named Faith.
  • He appears to have a British accent.
