Overview

This article is about the monster. For the short, see Queen of Monsters!.
“Finish him, Bolsen! He will make a nice dessert after this dragon!”
— The queen to a controlled Bolsen, "Queen of Monsters!"

The Queen of Monsters was the queen of the jungle insects, residing in a mountain in the Land of Monsters. She attempted to devour Daidan before being killed by Kai and Bolsen.

History

History[]

Background[]

At some point, the Queen of Monsters gave birth to Fuzz.

Queen of Monsters! (flashback)[]

When Daidan was brought to her as her next meal, the Queen of Monsters began to hungrily feed off the dragon's life force. Her feast would be interrupted by the arrival of Kai and Bolsen, both of whom had come to save the dragon. Not wishing to be distracted from her meal, the Queen exerted her thrall over Bolsen and commanded him to fight Kai while she continued to feed. When Kai turned the tables on Bolsen, the Queen decided that Bolsen had outlived his usefulness and Kai would become her new champion. This, however, allowed for Bolsen to break free from her control, and together alongside Kai, the two warriors attacked and slew the Queen. With her death, her soul was sent to the Spectral Lands.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Biology Enhancement - The monster could enhance the physical ability of her subjects, as shown by Bolsen.
    • Transformation Inducement - The monster could alter her victims' appearances, giving them a monstrous biology.
    • Mind Control - By enhancing her victims' biology, the Queen can place them under her thrall, as seen with Bolsen.
  • Life Force Consumption - The Queen can likely consume her victims' life force, which she attempted to do on Daidan.
  • Speech - The Queen of Monsters is capable of natural speech.
