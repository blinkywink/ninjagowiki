Vipers (also known as mini-snakes[1][2][3][4]) are recently-hatched Serpentine[5] each appearing in different colors that correspond with their tribes. When they get old enough, their arms grow and the tail turns into legs, turning them into the Serpentine children.[6]

History

History[]

The Tall Tale of Flintlocke[]

When Nadakhan transported Flintlocke into a desert, Flintlocke saw two Red Vipers before wishing to go some place cooler.

Rise of the Snakes[]

Hypno Vipers accompanied the Hypnobrai tribe when Lloyd unlocked the Hypnobrai Tomb and when the tribe attacked Jamanakai Village.

Home[]

Hypno Vipers accompanied the Hypnobrai in the tomb during the Slither Pit duel between Skales and Slithraa.

Snakebit[]

Four Red Vipers were in the crowd of Fangpyre attacking Ed & Edna's Scrap N Junk.

Never Trust a Snake[]

Two Hypno Vipers and two Red Vipers were in the crowd of Hypnobrai and Fangpyre when they were chasing Lloyd.

Known vipers

Known vipers[]

Golden Vipers[]

Golden Fangpyre vipers wielded by Fang-Suei, Bytar, and Lloyd Garmadon.

Sly Vipers[]

White Fangpyre vipers associated with Fang-Suei and Fangdam.

Red Vipers[]

Red vipers wielded by Fangdam.

Hypno Vipers[]

Dark blue Hypnobrai vipers with a weaker version of Hypnobrai hypnosis.

Toxic Vipers[]

Lime green Venomari vipers with the ability to produce Venomari venom.

Translucent Purple Vipers[]

Clear purple vipers wielded by Spitta.

Translucent Blue Viper[]

A clear blue viper wielded by Slithraa.

Translucent Orange Viper[]

A clear orange viper used by Chokun and the first Constrictai viper.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • The tribe with the most vipers in the sets is the Fangpyre tribe, with a total of three vipers.
  • Vipers only have a few appearances in the TV series despite being a prominent element in the Rise of the Snakes sets.
    • Hypno Vipers and Red Vipers are the only vipers to have appeared in the TV series.
  • In the sets they're used as weapons.
  • The lever seen to open the door to the Hypnobrai Tomb in "Rise of the Snakes" has a resemblance to a viper.
  • In "Tick Tock," the young Great Devourer is shown to have a similar appearance to a Toxic Viper.
  • Although they look like ordinary snakes, there are also normal snakes in Ninjago[7] as can be seen in the "Spellbound" flashback.
  • The Anacondrai serpent could be a viper with a mutation.[8]
