The Fire Dragon is a dragon from the Wyldness that was previously imprisoned in Imperium.

History

History[]

Background[]

The Fire Dragon used to live in the Wyldness. At some point, it got into a fight with a Lava Dragon in the Lava Wastes.[1]

At some point before the Battle of the Second Monastery, the Fire Dragon was captured by the Claws of Imperium and locked in the Imperium Prison.

Mindless Beasts[]

The Fire Dragon was freed from his cell by Lloyd and Wyldfyre.

I Will Be the Danger[]

With the assistance of Lloyd and Wyldfyre, the Earth Dragon orchestrated its escape alongside the other dragons. Upon reaching the main gates, the ninja and the dragons were met with opposition from the Claws of Imperium and the Photacs. In an effort to secure their freedom, Wyldfyre took charge, leading the Fire Dragon and its fellow dragons into battle against the Claws and the Photacs.

The Calm Inside[]

The Fire dragon continued to fight the Claw hunters, until Lloyd came up with a plan and Wyldfyre communicated with the dragons so they followed Jiro and Heatwave to the palace where they attempted to blast the palace down. During the fight Ras crashed his hover chariot into Zanth and took down the Fire Dragon with his Source Dragon Icon.

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

The Fire Dragon continued to battle the Claws of Imperium, knocking Ras' Photec. Lord Ras jumped at the dragon and knock the dragon down. When Wyldfyre communicated with the other dragons he went with the other dragons into the Monastery of Imperium where they went through the portal gate to the Monastery of Spinjitzu.

The Last Djinn[]

The Fire Dragon was flying with a Mountain Dragon on top of the monastery's portal gate's cave.
