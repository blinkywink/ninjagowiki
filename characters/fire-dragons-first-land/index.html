Overview

This article is about the species. For other uses, see Fire Dragon (disambiguation).

The Fire Dragons are dragons from the Realm of Oni and Dragons.

History

History[]

Background[]

Years ago, a Fire Dragon who will later be named Flame was placed in the Fire Temple by Master Wu to guard the Sword of Fire.

Big Trouble, Little Ninjago[]

A Fire Dragon flew past the Stranded ninja after they awoke and found themselves in the Realm of Oni and Dragons.

Iron & Stone[]

A Fire Dragon was being forced to create armor and weapons for the Dragon Hunters.

How to Build a Dragon[]

The Fire Dragon was continued to be forced to create armor and weapons for the Dragon Hunters again. When Firstbourne comes and Kai frees the dragons, the Fire Dragon leaves Dead's End and goes to Firstbourne's nest along with the Wind Dragon and Slab.

Lessons for a Master[]

After Wu tames Firstbourne, the dragon Matriarch summons a Fire Dragon as well as an Ice Dragon, a Lighnting Dragon, and a Earth Dragon in order for Kai and the other ninja to get back to Ninjago.

Green Destiny[]

The Fire Dragon follows its fellow dragons through a portal that forms in the air. It helps Kai save his sister and friends from the Sons of Garmadon. Kai later flies on the dragon to evacuate the citizens and destroy the Colossus.

In the end, the Fire Dragon and the other dragons return to their realm.

Endings[]

Faith told Wu and Misako about what happened in the Realm of Oni and Dragons. The Oni attacked the realm and the Hunters try to defend themselves with the help of the Dragons, but only Faith and Firstbourne managed to escape. A Fire Dragon was left in a petrified state until after the ninja defeated the Oni.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Flight - Like most dragons the Fire Dragons can fly using their wings.
  • Fire - The Fire Dragons have control over the Fire element.
    • Pyrokinetic Breath - They can breathe and shoot fire blasts from their mouths.
  • Oni Blood Detection - Like the other dragons, the Fire Dragons can smell the blood of Oni hybrids.
  • Realm travel - The Fire Dragons possesse the ability to traverse between realms.
