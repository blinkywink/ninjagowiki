Fire Monsters are a type of Elemental Monster that embody a corrupted version of Fire.

History

History[]

Background[]

Like other Elemental Monsters, Fire Monsters first came into existence before the existence of Elemental Masters[1] as a result of the disruption of Source Energy by the Weapon of Desolation. After the First Spinjitzu Master split the Weapon of Desolation into two pieces with an incantation on the Tablet of Desolation, the Elemental Monsters were eventually defeated.

Seed and Soil[]

While Jenny and Barry were in the Crossroads, a Source Energy beam appeared, which is revealed to be a Fire Monster. The Fire Monster shot a fireball at a couple from its mouth, only for it to miss and destroy an Urchin Fruit stand, which caused chaos and fear. Then, the creature started approaching and continued shooting fireballs until it noticed two kids who were trying to escape. The Fire Monster launched itself into the air and targeted the kids, only to miss because of Jenny and Barry saving them.

After a while, the Fire Monster teleported by scattering into a beam and left the Crossroads, leaving a big mess behind.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Fire Embodiment - Fire Monsters embody a corrupted form of the Elemental Power of Fire.
  • Pyrokinesis - Fire Monsters can manipulate and generate flames in many ways.
    • Pyrokinetic Flight - Fire Monsters can fly and propel themselves up or forward using fire.
    • Pyrokinetic Attacks - Fire Monsters can attack with fire.
      • Pyrokinetic Breath - Fire Monsters can shoot flames from their mouths.
        • Fire Spit - Fire Monsters can release fire from their mouths as spits.
      • Pyrokinetic Infusion - Fire Monsters can infuse themselves with fire to increase the strength of their blows, especially when in ball form.
      • Pyrokinetic Ball Projection - Fire Monsters can launch fireballs from their claws.
    • Fire/Heat Immunity - The Fire Monsters appear to be immune to fire and heat.
  • Superhuman Strength - Fire Monsters are abnormally strong, able to pick up and throw around humans and heavy objects.
  • Superhuman Durability - Fire Monsters are abnormally durable, able to survive physical attacks without any damage.
  • Spherical Form - Fire Monsters can convert themselves into a sphere for attack.
  • Conceptuportation - Fire Monsters can teleport by scattering into beams or bolts of Source Energy and reforming in different locations.
  • Absolute Immortality - Like other Elemental Monsters, Fire Monsters can't be truly killed. When their physical forms are destroyed, they revert to the Source Energy that the Weapon of Desolation used to bring them into existence and reform as long as the weapon remains active.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Elemental Powers - Elemental Powers can destroy the Fire Monsters' physical forms.
  • Weapon of Desolation - Being creations of the Weapon of Desolation, Fire Monsters are to obey the will of whoever wields it. Their existence also depends on the weapon, as it can and summon and seal them away at will.
