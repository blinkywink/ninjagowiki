The security guards are employees at Borg Industries.

History

History[]

The Surge[]

The guards stood by on the ground floor of Borg Tower as the ninja and Wu's Academy students entered. Later, two guards admitted that they were bored before an elevator smashed into the floor behind them in a fiery explosion.

The Invitation[]

A Borg guard watched the Kai and Kruncha's Slither Pit match at Yang Tavern.

The Corridor of Elders[]

A Borg security guard was among the group of citizens in the Ninjago Park who Zane encouraged to fight against the Anacondrai Cultists, a guard was at Chan's Theater when it was attacked by Anacondrai warriors, and another guard was in the group outside the Ninjago City Bank spoken to by Lloyd. Soon after this, the guard from Lloyd's crowd fought at the battle of the Corridor of Elders.

Infamous[]

A (possibly former) Borg guard operated one of the cameras for the Announcer's interview with a few of the ninja in the Concert Hall.

Misfortune Rising[]

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

A (possibly former) Borg guard was in an office building when the Sky Pirates shot a cannon ball through the window.
