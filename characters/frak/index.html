Overview

“I fought bullies long before I became the Master of Quake. There's more to us than Elemental Powers.”

Fracture "Frak" Slitherbottom[2] is a Serpentine hybrid of both the Constrictai and Venomari tribes,[1] Arin's childhood best friend, the current Elemental Master of Quake, Ras' former student, and a current ninja student. He competed in the Tournament of the Sources alongside the other Elemental Masters of his generation under the Wolf Clan banner with Ras as his master. Following his defeat in the Tournament and his refusal to use a Wolf Mask, Ras kicked him out of the Clan. Frak eventually joined the ninja at the end of the Tournament after he helped them fight the Wolf Mask Warriors, becoming Lloyd's third student.

After becoming part of the ninja, Frak faced the likes of the Forbidden Five, Thunderfang, and the Dragonians. He was later stranded in a mysterious realm with Sora, Riyu and Wyldfyre after Ras tampered with the portal gates. After a skirmish in Umbrasilvis with the Loyalists, he later partook in the Loyalist assault to stop Beatrix from reclaiming her throne.

Personality

Personality[]

Frak is a brave, friendly, compassionate, caring, good-natured and honest person who strongly believes in doing what is right. Even as a kid, he helped Arin when he was bullied, which shows his kind nature. He is also a bit naive, since at some point after the Merge, he was manipulated by Lord Ras and recruited to the Wolf Clan so he could attend the Tournament of the Sources. However, he refused to wear a Wolf Mask during a match because it went against his beliefs and because Arin revealed that the Wolf Mask eventually shatters all goodness in the user's soul if the Gong is used enough times. This shows that he has a strong sense of honor and that he values his morals more than following orders. Even when facing challenges, Frak stays strong and determined. He is also very loyal to his friends, always standing by them no matter what.

Very much like Arin, Frak is a great admirer of the Ninja, especially Cole (his favorite). Despite initially siding with Ras' team, Frak never once showed hostility against his heroes, considering it a great honor to fight against Cole and even seeking his advice about using the latter's element, even if that caused problems with Ras. After joining the Ninja and becoming Lloyd's student, Frak had some difficulty in separating himself from Ras' teachings, but his dedication allowed him to adopt the Ninja's fighting style of relying on other sources of strength, not just physical. Additionally, due to previously being one of Ras' students, Frak would try to prove himself worthy, with that sometimes leading to some unfortunate results.

However, Frak ultimately proves himself as a decent person where he shows a great deal of remorse for causing Arin to stray, and he took it upon himself to blame himself for the mess. Additionally, when Frak reunites with Arin, he shows no hostility and accepts his rejection since Frak acknowledges his mistakes.

Despite being friendly and easygoing, Frak is often bad at social cues and often fumbles at small talk as Sora would point out. However, he is extremely passionate for the art of pie-baking, having accumulated binders of entire recipes and he gets excited about passing along his knowledge to Sora, the same way he did with Arin. He is also a fan of Intelligent George much like Arin, believing in conspiracies (like gravity not existing) and being afraid of supernatural things such as living puppets (resulting in Frak developing a phobia of them), the Oni, and even dark locations like the Spectral Lands.

Frak also seems to have developed a crush on Sora, as when Zane said that he was having trouble finding the most attractive and intelligent member of the ninja, Frak mistook him for Sora and stated that she was already here. More of that is hinted when he is reunited with Sora, he expressed how he was so worried about her that he couldn't even sleep, with him then awkwardly adding that the others may not have been able to sleep as well. During their journey to Djinjago, when Sora tried to rehearse the notes they would need to play to awaken one of the halves of the Arc Dragon of Focus, Frak tried to harmonize with her, with the first attempt failing before he managed to do it properly. And when fightning Kur and the Spectral Dragonians, he feared he would not have another chance, he attempted to tell her his feelings towards her, but, at the last moment, got nervous, chickened out and came out with a feeble excuse by telling her that he ate all the yogurt in the Monastery fridge. Later, when Wyldfyre commented on his poor attempts to conceal this attraction, Frak tried to denying having feelings for Sora, though the Elemental Master of Heat told him that his crush was obvious.

Even if Frak tries to hide it, he possesses a great deal of insecurity and vulnerability, which he masks with bits of knowledge and a little of fake confidence. It's later revealed that the root of his insecurity is his own father, Reginald, with whom he has a very much complicated relationship. Despite not spending much time with his dad growing up due to him working hard to earn recognition as an archeologist and adventurer, in the time they were together, Frak had to endure his father's perfectionism and condescendance, which hindered his own self-confidence. These also caused him to develop a lot of resentment towards Reginald, which only got worse when the latter didn't make an effort to help his own son and his friends when they were put on trial for being suspected of being connected with the monster that attacked Umbrasilvis. It's highly possible this resentment is what fueled his moves back when he was Ras' student.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Frak is a young hybrid Serpentine, being half-Venomari, half-Constrictai, with legs and skin composed of oranges scales and a lime crest at the back of his head. He has the same head shape as the snake warriors and the eel Merlopians but with a shorter neck. He has lime eyes, a reddish forked tongue, and two fangs sticking out of his mouth like all Serpentine. Unlike many of his peers, he and his parents wears classic clothes.

As a child, Frak had a shorter neck and smaller legs.

During the Tournament of Sources, Frak wears both the tournament tracksuit and a gray Wolf Clan robe with purple details similar to Cinder’s outfit, crimson gloves, and a dark gray Wolf Clan armor that has a collar surrounding his neck, a right shoulder pad of the same color, and three blue claws in place of the left. His robe also has a purple belt with a red and gray Wolf Clan emblem on the right side. The same emblem is also likely located on the back of his robe.

As a ninja, Frak wore at first a black and lime dojo gi with a snake-themed emblem of the same lime color on the back and then after Thunderfang's banishment to the Spectral Lands, a grey and lime suit with a lime hood/mask combo.

Abilities and weapons

Abilities and weapons[]

Abilities[]

  • Quake: As the Elemental Master of Quake, Frak can create, manipulate, and generate vibrations. This allows him to create earthquake by channeling vibrations into the ground but he can also use his power on any materials, as long he can vibrate them. He can only vibrate solid matter.
  • Elemental Powers (briefly): With enough skill, Frak was able to use the Elemental Powers he won during the Tournament of the Sources, such as Earth, Ice, Reflex and at least one unknown power.[3]
  • Serpentine Physiology: As a hybrid of a Serpentine, Frak has the following abilities:
    • Superhuman Strength: Being a Serpentine, Frak possessed super physical strength beyond that of an average human being, as he managed to destroy some of the Monastery's training equipment. It's possible that he inherited the strength from his Constrictai side of the family. Frak can also use Quake to increase his strikes and weaken structures to pull them out.
    • Superhuman reflexes: Even without the elemental power of Reflex, Frak has faster reflexes than a human, being able to keep up with Zur for a brief time.
    • Superhuman Smell: Being a Serpentine, Frak can smell using the tip of his tongue, allowing him to sense which direction a smell is coming.
  • Skilled combatant: Thanks to Ras' training, Frak became a very good combatant, infusing his Quake power into his strikes along with the other temporary elemental powers. Frak's skills also allowed him to fight off Chaos-empowered wolf warriors, despite being powerless and unarmed. When joining the ninja, Frak learned to balance the aggressive style he learned from Ras, while also learning Spinjitzu. Later on, he was shown to skillfully fight off Dragonian warriors. In "The Lean of a Tree", he managed to fight Wyldfyre with Sora's help in the Land of Eternal Dawn but both only defeated her using an illusion of Arin’s grapple gun.
    • Swordsmanship: Although he has yet to possess a signature weapon, Frak is a competent swordsman, managing to take out many Spectral Dragonians with his newly-acquired Reveal Blade.
    • Shatterspin: Under Ras, Frak was likely taught rudimentary Shatterspin and could even use it to its full power through a Wolf Mask charged by the Gong of Shattering, but chose not to when he remembered Arin’s warning about the mask.
  • Baking: Frak was the one to teach Arin how to bake pies and the latter said the former is even better than him.
  • Singing: Frak is shown to possess a great singing voice, which was shown when he harmonized with Sora when the two were practicing the notes they would need to play to awaken the half of the Arc Dragon of Focus located in Djinjago.

History

Background

Background[]

Frak was born soon after the Serpentine were trapped inside the Stone Warriors chamber to a Constrictai called Reginald Slitherbottom and a female Venomari. After the crisis with the Golden Master, Frak's family decided to return to the surface and live with humans, taking residence in the same neighborhood where Arin also lived. Frak developed a strong admiration for the Ninja, particularly Cole. One day, Frak met Arin after scaring off two bullies, who were teasing the latter about the ninja before they eventually became friends.[1]

Prior to Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

At some point, Frak taught Arin how to bake pies. Unfortunately, Frak had to move away, causing him to lose contact with Arin.[2] At some point, Frak was given a family pass into the Explorer's Club when his father, Reginald joined the club.[3] After the Merge, he gained the Elemental Power of Quake, which he accidentally triggered and led to bursting a sewer pipe and creating chaos.[1] Frightened and unsure of how to control his powers, Frak met Ras, who guided him in mastering his powers. With Ras' help, Frak gradually overcame his fears regarding his powers. During this time, Ras kept Jay and Frak separate, thinking that Frak would recognize Jay.[4] Eventually, Ras invited Frak to join the Wolf Clan and participate in the Tournament of the Sources.[5]

Dragons Rising, Season 2

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Enter the City of Temples[]

Hearing of a disturbance, Frak went to investigate and discovered the city's Security Bots were surrounding Arin and Riyu, surprising the former.

They Gather for the Feast[]

As Arin and Riyu faced the threat of arrest, Frak intervened by informing the Security Bots that they were his guests, which led to their release. Once the bots departed, Frak took the opportunity to share with Arin the events that unfolded after the Merge. Curiously, he asked about Arin's presence in Temple City. At that moment, the ninja arrived, accompanied by Roby and Bleckt, leaving Frak amazed to discover that Arin was friends with the ninja. His surprise grew when Arin mentioned that he had recently talked about Frak to Sora, which prompted Cole to praise Frak's pie recipes. Arin then invited Frak to join the ninja for some time together. Just as Frak was about to agree, his phone buzzed with a training alert from Ras, urging him to leave quickly, though he made sure to express his enthusiasm about meeting everyone.

Frak attended the welcome banquet for the Elemental Masters participating in the tournament. However, things took a turn when an argument erupted among the ninja, Ras, Roby, and Zeatrix upon Zeatrix's arrival.

Inside the Maze[]

Frak decided to take a break from his training with Ras to enjoy some pie. On his way, he ran into Arin and Riyu and invited them to join him for a treat. While they were getting their pie, Frak brought up Zant-Tanz, accusing him of being behind the assassination attempt. Arin, however, was skeptical and thought it was unlikely that Zant-Tanz was involved, but Frak was convinced that evidence suggest otherwise. Curious about Arin's experiences, Frak asked him what it was like to work with the ninja. Arin recounted how he first became a ninja while rescuing Riyu from the Claws of Imperium, emphasizing that it was tough work. Frak agreed, recalling how Ras had helped him master his Elemental Powers. Just then, they spotted Wyldfyre looking for Roby. As Wyldfyre left with Roby and Bleckt, Frak and Arin realized that Wyldfyre had feelings for Roby, prompting Frak to make a lighthearted joke about it. Frak then mentioned that he needed to head off to meet with the Wolf Clan but promised to check in on Arin later. He then met up with Nokt and Jordana, unaware that Sora was secretly watching them.

Family

Family[]

Reginald Slitherbottom[]

“Frak, I just wanted you to have a father you could be proud of. You don't know what is used to be like for Serpentine in Ninjago. 'Never trust a snake,' they used to say. I had to work twice as hard for twice as long to get any recognition.”
“Yeah, well, you didn't even work half as hard at being a good dad.”
— Reginald and Frak, "The Grand Inquisition"

Reginald is Frak's father, with whom he had a strained relationship with. At one point, Frak made a chest plate for him, which he instead used as a plate for his food. Reginald neglected Frak growing up to instead further his career in exploration, hoping that Frak could be proud of his father. However, despite his desire for having his son to admire and be proud of him, Reginald let his own vanity guide the way he rose Frak, causing him to be perfectly obedient and never to speak up, which allowed Reginald, even if without intended malice, to verbally abuse Frak and dismiss his thoughts and ideas on a regular basis. All the neglect, combined with the condescendance he endured from his father, caused Frak to develop a great deal of resentment towards him, which came to its highest point after Reginald made no effort to help him and Sora when they were made suspects of being connected with the monster that attacked Umbrasilvis to the point of suspecting he was the one that released the monster and was using it to search for something.

Later, it is revealed Frak was wrong about suspecting his father, but even with Reginald saving him, both from the true culprit and from half of the Weapon of Desolation that was overcharging Frak's Quake, and confessing the motivation behind his actions, Frak simply couldn't overcome the resentment he felt for him to forgive him and left him in order to return to Ninjago with the others as soon as possible.

When the Loyalists rebuilt Cryptor, who sabotaged Frak's bike and stole a map from the Explorer's Club, Frak and his father teamed up to investigate his return. During the time they spent together, Reginald demonstrated that he had changed since his last encounter with his son—particularly in how he treated others, and his son specifically. He had stopped being condescending and belittling, and was showing that he understood the mistakes he had made in the past with his son. Reginald decided to use the chest plate his son had made for him as a child to protect himself when they faced Cryptor and the Loyalists, which ultimately saved him from a shot fired by the Nindroid General. Believing his father was dead from the shot, Frak was distraught, and he let tears flow until Reginald revealed that he had survived thanks to the chest plate. The two of them then resumed a good relationship.

Instructors

Instructors[]

Ras (formerly)[]

“You have failed me!”
“It was a good fight. A fair fight.”
“I will not suffer disobedience! I offered you certain victory, and you refused. I never want you in my sight again!”
— Ras and Frak, "Clues and Suspects"

Before the Tournament of the Sources, Ras recruited Frak and taught him how to fight properly. Despite being strict with Frak, the latter considered him to be a good teacher. However, their relationship was filled with bumps, especially due to Frak's admiration towards the Ninja (Ras' enemies). When Frak approached Cole to seek for advice over how to use his element, Ras got upset. During his fight against Sora, Ras ordered Frak to wear his Wolf Mask, but the latter refused to wear it, after hearing from Arin that the masks shatter the goodness in the wearer, and lost his match. This made Ras very angry, and he banished Frak from the Wolf Clan and told him never to appear in his sight again.

After leaving Ras' team and joining the Ninja, Frak came to realize how toxic Ras really was. On one hand, he came to realize Ras was wrong about physical strength being the only source of power, as Lloyd came to teach him there were other ways to channel strength. Not just that, but he came to see Ras as nothing more but a manipulator that tricks others to follow him and, when he sees how his influence is affecting Arin, Frak's resentment towards him increased even more.

Lloyd[]

After Ras expelled him from his team and the end of the Tournament of the Sources, Frak joined the Ninja as a new ninja student. When Frak found it difficult to let go of the residues left by Ras' teachings, Lloyd gave him the advice of existing many sources of stength besides the physical one, an advice that Frak took to the heart and even came to apply later on.

Cole[]

Ever since Frak was little and would heard about the Ninja, he always admired Cole, finding him to be his favorite ninja. He would always try to mimic his moves. When years later after the merge he came to face him in the Tournament of the Sources, Frak couldn't help but feel excited in facing his idol. Despite defeating him, Frak's admiration towards Cole never faulted and even sought his advice over how to properly use the Earth element, even if that caused some trouble with Ras.

With Lloyd gone to search for Arin's parents, Frak was trained by Cole and Kai to perform Spinjitzu. When a crash with Riyu causes damages to the training course, Frak went with Cole and Kai to the Land of Lost Things so they could have a place that wouldn't mind some destructive training.

When Cole was training him to apply his elemental power, Frak managed to sense the earth screaming, the same way Cole had been feeling. The two and Kai go to the place Cole went through after parting ways with Nya and Sora. Upon reaching it, Frak and Cole combined their powers to unearth what was lying beneath. Together, with Kai's help, they were able to unearth the remains of the Source Dragon of Balance.

During their fight in Imperium against the Dragonian Warriors, Cole's soul is taken, much to Frak's worry. Despite fending off the spectral Dragonians and Nokt's invasion. Frak does not celebrate due to Cole's fate, and he and Kai brought him back to the Monastery.

Frak and Cole would later be separated from each other after Ras captured Wu and shut down the portal gates, Frak was sent to the Land of Eternal Dawn while Cole was still in Ninjago. However, the two would eventually reunite in Imperium once again and after seeing Frak there, Cole then apologized for how he acted before when Nokt and the Spectral Dragonians attacked to which Frak accepts Cole's apology for being bitter and hot-headed.

Kai[]

Kai, along with Cole, helped train Frak to perform Spinjitzu.

Friends

Friends[]

Arin[]

Arin is Frak's childhood best friend. They first met when Frak saved him from two bullies, who were insulting the ninja, and scared them off before they bonded over their fandom for the ninja, as well as Frak showing Arin how to cook pies, until the former and his family moved away. They reunited years later at the City of Temples, where Frak stopped the guards arresting him on behalf of his master and showed off that he discovered he had the Elemental Power of Quake. However, their newly reformed friendship was put to question when Sora discovered Ras is Frak's master before Arin eventually confronted him about, which the former confirmed. Arin then warned Frak not to wear his Wolf Clan mask, which the latter took during his battle with Sora, and in exchange he helped Arin improve his Spinjitzu.

When Arin defected the team, Frak shows regret for causing Arin to channel his anger and stray from the team so the former can find his missing parents. He even shows no hostility when he reunited with Arin only to be cruelly rejected by Arin with his newfound anger. During the battle, Frak take it upon himself to tell Arin to blame him (Frak) for causing him to believe that the ninjas' teachings are not as good as he felt. He even tells Arin that if he is stronger than before without them, maybe he doesn't know what being a ninja means. Afterwards, when Thunderfang was defeated, Frak is saddened when Arin chose to leave again after learning how he wants to bring his parents back from the dead. Frak's spirits are lifted when he learns that Arin's parents are alive through Jay that he wishes Lloyd, Jay, Nya, and Wyldfyre luck when they went to find them. Hoping that his best friend will return once his parents are back with him.

Sora (crush)[]

Frak's initial interactions with Sora were very distant, as they were from different teams and she distrusted him due to being one of Ras' students. When they fought during their match, Frak thought in using the wolf mask, just like Ras told him to do, but, remembering Arin's warning and wanting to have a fair fight against Sora, he refused to wear it. Despite he lost to Sora and she took his element and the ones he had gained, Frak never hold it against her, finding their battle as being a good one.

After he was expelled from Ras' team and Arin abandoned the Ninja to save the former, Frak joined the Ninja, becoming Sora's fellow student. Their initial interactions were distant, but eventually Sora approached him to ask him to help her bake pies the same way he did with Arin. This caused Frak to get very excited, as he would be able to keep on passing the knowledge of baking pies. While teaching Sora how to bake, Frak is showed as being a patient teacher, although he is not above in teasing her about her inaptitude.

It is showed during their search for Arin that, despite everything, Frak and Sora still had some issues, with the former being too eager to prove himself that he ended up putting the team in trouble, while the latter still didn't fully trust him because of his past as Ras' student. Eventually, the two of them were able to work out those issues.

It seems that Frak has a crush on Sora, seen when Zane expressed how he missed P.I.X.A.L., describing her as the smartest and most attractive member of the team, which caused Frak to think he was talking about Sora. More of that crush is showed when Frak is reunited with Sora, as he expressed how he was so worried about her that he couldn't even sleep, with him then awkwardly adding that the others also may have not been able to sleep as well. During their journey to Djinjago, when Sora tried to rehearse the notes they would need to play to awaken one of the halves of the Arc Dragon of Focus, Frak tried to harmonize with her, with the first attempt failing before he managed to do it properly. During their fight against Kur and the spectral Dragonian Warriors at the Cave of Bells, fearing he would not have another chance, Frak tried to express his feelings towards Sora, however, at the last moment, he chickened out and came out with an feeble excuse, saying that he ate all the yogurts in the monastary.

During their adventures in Umbrasilvis, the two of them started to bond over the fact both have terrible relationships with their respective parents. After witnessing how Frak's dad verbally abuses him and is very condescending, Sora tried to convince Frak to stand up against him. Later, after seeing how Frak suspected of Reginald of freeing the monster they had captured, Sora did her best to tell Frak to not assume the worst. When Frak and Reginald go their separate ways on bad terms, Sora is showed feeling bad for him, as he witnesses Arin's good relationship with his parents, and Sora comes to comfort him.


Riyu[]

During the ninja's research for Arin in the First Land, Frak showed a lack of trust in Riyu regarding the path he proposed to follow, that turned out to lead them to the Dragonian Trackers, which reinforced Frak's belief that they should not have followed his trail.
