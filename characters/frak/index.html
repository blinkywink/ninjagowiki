Overview

“You were never there for me when I needed you. Gone for every important moment in my life, chasing some discovery that would be your legacy? Guess you never thought I could be your legacy.”
— Frak standing up to Reginald, "The Grand Inquisition"

Frak Slitherbottom is a Serpentine hybrid of both the Constrictai and Venomari tribes,[1] Arin's childhood best friend, the current Elemental Master of Quake, Lord Ras' former student, and a current ninja student. He competed in the Tournament of the Sources alongside the other Elemental Masters of his generation under the Wolf Clan banner with Ras as his master. Following his defeat in the Tournament and his refusal to use a Wolf Mask, Ras kicked him out of the Clan. Frak eventually joined the ninja at the end of the Tournament after he helped them fight the Wolf Mask Warriors, becoming Lloyd's third student.

After becoming part of the ninja, Frak faced the likes of the Forbidden Five, Thunderfang, and the Dragonians. He was later stranded in a mysterious realm with Sora, Riyu and Wyldfyre after Ras tampered with the portal gates. After a skirmish in Umbrasilvis with the Loyalists, he later partook in the Battle of Imperium to stop Beatrix from reclaiming her throne.

Personality

Personality[]

Frak is a brave, friendly, compassionate, caring, good-natured and honest person who strongly believes in doing what is right. Even as a kid, he helped Arin when he was bullied, which shows his kind nature. He is also a bit naive, since at some point after the Merge, he was manipulated by Lord Ras and recruited to the Wolf Clan so he could attend the Tournament of the Sources. However, he refused to wear a Wolf Mask during a match because it went against his beliefs and because Arin revealed that the Wolf Mask eventually shatters all goodness in the user's soul if the Gong is used enough times. This shows that he has a strong sense of honor and that he values his morals more than following orders. Even when facing challenges, Frak stays strong and determined. He is also very loyal to his friends, always standing by them no matter what.

Very much like Arin, Frak is a great admirer of the Ninja, especially Cole (his favorite). Despite initially siding with Ras' team, Frak never once showed hostility against his heroes, considering it a great honor to fight against Cole and even seeking his advice about using the latter's element, even if that caused problems with Ras. After joining the Ninja and becoming Lloyd's student, Frak had some difficulty in separating himself from Ras' teachings, but his dedication allowed him to adopt the Ninja's fighting style of relying on other sources of strength, not just physical. Additionally, due to previously being one of Ras' students, Frak would try to prove himself worthy, with that sometimes leading to some unfortunate results.

However, Frak ultimately proves himself as a decent person where he shows a great deal of remorse for causing Arin to stray, and he took it upon himself to blame himself for the mess. Additionally, when Frak reunites with Arin, he shows no hostility and accepts his rejection since Frak acknowledges his mistakes.

Despite being friendly and easygoing, Frak is often bad at social cues and often fumbles at small talk as Sora would point out. However, he is extremely passionate for the art of pie-baking, having accumulated binders of entire recipes and he gets excited about passing along his knowledge to Sora, the same way he did with Arin. He is also a fan of Intelligent George much like Arin, believing in conspiracies (like gravity not existing) and being afraid of supernatural things such as living puppets (resulting in Frak developing a phobia of them), the Oni, and even dark locations like the Spectral Lands.

Frak also seems to have developed a crush on Sora, as when Zane said that he was having trouble finding the most attractive and intelligent member of the ninja, Frak mistook him for Sora and stated that she was already here. More of that is hinted when he is reunited with Sora, he expressed how he was so worried about her that he couldn't even sleep, with him then awkwardly adding that the others may not have been able to sleep as well. During their journey to Djinjago, when Sora tried to rehearse the notes they would need to play to awaken one of the halves of the Arc Dragon of Focus, Frak tried to harmonize with her, with the first attempt failing before he managed to do it properly. And when fightning Kur and the Spectral Dragonians, he feared he would not have another chance, he attempted to tell her his feelings towards her, but, at the last moment, got nervous, chickened out and came out with a feeble excuse by telling her that he ate all the yogurt in the Monastery fridge.

Even if Frak tries to hide it, he possesses a great deal of insecurity and vulnerability, which he masks with bits of knowledge and a little of fake confidence. It's later revealed that the root of his insecurity is his own father, Reginald, with whom he has a very much complicated relationship. Despite not spending much time with his dad growing up due to him working hard to earn recognition as an archeologist and adventurer, in the time they were together, Frak had to endure his father's perfectionism and condescendance, which hindered his own self-confidence. These also caused him to develop a lot of resentment towards Reginald, which only got worse when the latter didn't make an effort to help his own son and his friends when they were put on trial for being suspected of being connected with the monster that attacked Umbrasilvis. It's highly possible this resentment is what fueled his moves back when he was Ras' student.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Frak is a young hybrid Serpentine, being half-Venomari, half-Constrictai, with legs and skin composed of oranges scales and a lime crest at the back of his head. He has the same head shape as the snake warriors and the eel Merlopians but with a shorter neck. He has lime eyes, a reddish forked tongue, and two fangs sticking out of his mouth like all Serpentine. Unlike many of his peers, he and his parents wears classic clothes.

As a child, Frak had a shorter neck and smaller legs.

During the Tournament of Sources, Frak wears both the tournament tracksuit and a gray Wolf Clan robe with purple details similar to Cinder’s outfit, crimson gloves, and a dark gray Wolf Clan armor that has a collar surrounding his neck, a right shoulder pad of the same color, and three blue claws in place of the left. His robe also has a purple belt with a red and gray Wolf Clan emblem on the right side. The same emblem is also likely located on the back of his robe.

As a ninja, Frak wore at first a black and lime dojo gi with a snake-themed emblem of the same lime color on the back and then after Thunderfang's banishment to the Spectral Lands, a grey and lime suit with a lime hood/mask combo.

Abilities and weapons

Abilities and weapons[]

Abilities[]

  • Quake: As the Elemental Master of Quake, Frak can create, manipulate, and generate vibrations. This allows him to create earthquake by channeling vibrations into the ground but he can also use his power on any materials, as long he can vibrate them. He can only vibrate solid matter.
  • Elemental Powers (briefly): With enough skill, Frak was able to use the Elemental Powers he won during the Tournament of the Sources, such as Earth, Ice, Reflex and at least one unknown power.[2]
  • Serpentine Physiology: As a hybrid of a Serpentine, Frak has the following abilities:
    • Superhuman Strength: Being a Serpentine, Frak possessed super physical strength beyond that of an average human being, as he managed to destroy some of the Monastery's training equipment. It's possible that he inherited the strength from his Constrictai side of the family. Frak can also use Quake to increase his strikes and weaken structures to pull them out.
    • Superhuman reflexes: Even without the elemental power of Reflex, Frak has faster reflexes than a human, being able to keep up with Zur for a brief time.
    • Superhuman Smell: Being a Serpentine, Frak can smell using the tip of his tongue, allowing him to sense which direction a smell is coming.
  • Skilled combatant: Thanks to Ras' training, Frak became a very good combatant, infusing his Quake power into his strikes along with the other temporary elemental powers. Frak's skills also allowed him to fight off Chaos-empowered wolf warriors, despite being powerless and unarmed. When joining the ninja, Frak learned to balance the aggressive style he learned from Ras, while also learning Spinjitzu. Later on, he was shown to skillfully fight off Dragonian warriors. In "The Lean of a Tree", he managed to fight Wyldfyre with Sora's help in the Land of Eternal Dawn but both only defeated her using an illusion of Arin’s grapple gun.
    • Swordsmanship: Although he has yet to possess a signature weapon, Frak is a competent swordsman, managing to take out many Spectral Dragonians with his newly-acquired Reveal Blade.
    • Shatterspin: Under Ras, Frak was likely taught rudimentary Shatterspin and could even use it to its full power through a Wolf Mask charged by the Gong of Shattering, but chose not to when he remembered Arin’s warning about the mask.
  • Baking: Frak was the one to teach Arin how to bake pies and the latter said the former is even better than him.
  • Singing: Frak is shown to possess a great singing voice, which was shown when he harmonized with Sora when the two were practicing the notes they would need to play to awaken the half of the Arc Dragon of Focus located in Djinjago.

Weapons[]

  • Silver Dark Blade (briefly): Frak used a Silver Dark Blade while fighting the Dragonian warriors.
  • Prismatic Blade (formerly): Frak used a Prismatic Blade and later had Imperium upgrade it into a Reveal Blade.

Etymology

Etymology[]

His name is a reference to fracking, a method used to extract natural gas and oil from deep rock formations.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • His existence and status of joining the Ninja was first revealed by Sam Vincent on Cameo in 2023. Frak wouldn’t be seen or mentioned until "Enter the City of Temples", and he wouldn’t be seen as a member of the Ninja until "The Missing".[citation needed]
  • He has a modified version of the orange head mold of the snake warriors.
    • Unlike the snake warriors who have dark grey as their secondary color, Frak has a light green crest and light green eyes, which he gets from his mother, who has completely green skin and green eyes, and he gets his orange skin from his father.
  • He is chronologically the second known Serpentine to be an Elemental Master. The first one is an unknown Hypnobrai.
  • He's the first main ninja to be introduced as a member of the season's villainous faction, having previously part to the Wolf Clan. Lloyd briefly controlled the Hypnobrai, Fangpyre, and Pythor before defecting. Skylor was a member of the Anacondrai Cultists before defecting, but she didn't join the ninja's team, as she only became an ally.
  • He is the first Serpentine to be a ninja.
    • Additionally, he is the third nonhuman member of the ninja, the first and second being Zane and Riyu respectively.
  • He is the second Serpentine hybrid, the first being Clancee.[1]
    • Coincidentally, both are half Venomari.
  • He is currently the youngest ninja, as he was born at least after the Attack of the Great Devourer, due to being a hybrid and the Serpentine being separated beforehand (for almost 40 years), along with the Serpentine having quick early development.
  • He has a crush on Sora.[3][4]
  • Frak's ninja suit in Season 3 contains a recolored version of the torso of the apprentices from 71787 Creative Ninja Brick Box while his legs are a recolor of the ninja's dojo suits.
    • This is not the first time Frak wears a suit inspired by an existing Ninjago figure, as his Wolf Clan suit was based on the wolf warriors' suit and Cinder's tournament suit.
    • It is also similar to Lloyd's movie outfit based on the green and black color scheme on his suit.
    • According to Chris "Doc" Wyatt, the green in Frak's ninja suit isn't meant to represent that Frak is Lloyd's student.[5]
    • The pattern found on the left side of his suit is also found on the leg printing of the snake warriors, and the emblem on the back of his suit is the same as that of the snake warriors.
  • He has an interest in Speleology (the study of caves) as a hobby. This fits his elemental power of Quake, one of the elements related to the ground and rocks, and matches his admiration towards Cole, the Earth Ninja.
  • Frak has perfect pitch.[6]
  • He is currently the only member of the ninja to not receive a minifigure until the following season after his debut, and even then, he did not receive a minifigure until the Summer wave of Dragons Rising Season 3 sets.
  • He is one of seven current Elemental Masters to have a known last name, the others being Lloyd, Jay, Zane, Skylor, Griffin, and Zeatrix.
  • When asked what Frak's favorite season of Ninjago would be, Chris "Doc" Wyatt stated, "NOT FOUR. He goes through way to much stuff to enjoy that."[7]
  • He is allergic to cats.[8]
  • Based on the photo Frak has of his parents, it seems to suggest that he, along with his father and mother, once lived in the Tomb of the Stone Army, since the background and ground match what was seen in "The Curse of the Golden Master".
  • Frak shares similarities with Lloyd, because when both of them were children, their parents, Reginald and Misako respectfully, left them, but for different reasons:
    • For Reginald, he was absent during Frak's childhood because he was focused on being recognized as a great explorer, and Misako was absent in Lloyd's childhood because she went to learn everything she could about the Green Ninja prophecy in hopes that she could prevent her husband and her son from fighting each other.
    • Both Frak and Lloyd felt resentment towards their respective absentee parent, but while the latter forgave his mother after hearing the reason why she left, the former has not forgiven his father yet.

History

Background

Background[]

Frak was born soon after the Serpentine were trapped inside the Stone Warriors chamber to a Constrictai called Reginald Slitherbottom and a female Venomari. After the crisis with the Golden Master, Frak's family decided to return to the surface and live with humans, taking residence in the same neighborhood where Arin also lived. Frak developed a strong admiration for the Ninja, particularly Cole. One day, Frak met Arin after scaring off two bullies, who were teasing the latter about the ninja before they eventually became friends.[1]

New Blood Ninja (flashback)[]

At some point, Frak taught Arin how to bake pies. Unfortunately, Frak had to move away, causing him to lose contact with Arin.[2] Following The Merge, Frak gained the Elemental Power of Quake, which he accidentally triggered and led to bursting a sewer pipe and creating chaos.[1] Frightened and unsure of how to control his powers, Frak met Ras, who guided him in mastering his powers. With Ras' help, Frak gradually overcame his fears regarding his powers. Eventually, Ras invited Frak to join the Wolf Clan and participate in the Tournament of the Sources.[3]

Dragons Rising, Season 2

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Enter the City of Temples[]

Hearing of a disturbance, Frak went to investigate and discovered the city's Security Bots were surrounding Arin and Riyu, surprising the former.

They Gather for the Feast[]

As Arin and Riyu faced the threat of arrest, Frak intervened by informing the Security Bots that they were his guests, which led to their release. Once the bots departed, Frak took the opportunity to share with Arin the events that unfolded after the Merge. Curiously, he asked about Arin's presence in Temple City. At that moment, the ninja arrived, accompanied by Roby and Bleckt, leaving Frak amazed to discover that Arin was friends with the ninja. His surprise grew when Arin mentioned that he had recently talked about Frak to Sora, which prompted Cole to praise Frak's pie recipes. Arin then invited Frak to join the ninja for some time together. Just as Frak was about to agree, his phone buzzed with a training alert from Ras, urging him to leave quickly, though he made sure to express his enthusiasm about meeting everyone.

Frak attended the welcome banquet for the Elemental Masters participating in the tournament. However, things took a turn when an argument erupted among the ninja, Ras, Roby, and Zeatrix upon Zeatrix's arrival.

Inside the Maze[]

Frak decided to take a break from his training with Ras to enjoy some pie. On his way, he ran into Arin and Riyu and invited them to join him for a treat. While they were getting their pie, Frak brought up Zant-Tanz, accusing him of being behind the assassination attempt. Arin, however, was skeptical and thought it was unlikely that Zant-Tanz was involved, but Frak was convinced that evidence suggest otherwise. Curious about Arin's experiences, Frak asked him what it was like to work with the ninja. Arin recounted how he first became a ninja while rescuing Riyu from the Claws of Imperium, emphasizing that it was tough work. Frak agreed, recalling how Ras had helped him master his Elemental Powers. Just then, they spotted Wyldfyre looking for Roby. As Wyldfyre left with Roby and Bleckt, Frak and Arin realized that Wyldfyre had feelings for Roby, prompting Frak to make a lighthearted joke about it. Frak then mentioned that he needed to head off to meet with the Wolf Clan but promised to check in on Arin later. He then met up with Nokt and Jordana, unaware that Sora was secretly watching them.

Truth and Lies[]

Frak was practicing with a wooden dummy when he met Arin, who cautioned him about Ras' ambitions, which included hunting dragons and taking over the Cloud Kingdom. Frak remained skeptical, as he had only experienced kindness from Ras. He disclosed that his rigorous training was aimed at mastering Shatterspin. However, Arin advised against this pursuit, cautioning that donning a Wolf Mask could lead to the user's moral corruption, asserting that Ras was solely driven by a thirst for power. Frak was conflicted, as he attributed his progress to Ras' guidance. He demonstrated Ras' teachings, prompting Arin to showcase his own unconventional Spinjitzu. As Frak shared Ras's insights, Arin successfully performed a genuine Spinjitzu tornado, leaving both Frak and Arin in awe. Just then, Frak received a message from Ras, indicating that it was now his turn to participate in the next match.

Upon arriving at the Games Plaza alongside Arin, Frak found himself matched against Cole, viewing this challenge as a prestigious opportunity. In order to secure victory in the match, Frak was required to ring the bell positioned at the top of the arena, a task that involved navigating and climbing the swinging metal walls. Fortunately for Frak, his Quake powers provided him with a significant advantage, as they caused the arena to shift, aiding his ascent. Ultimately, Frak triumphed over Cole and successfully rang the bell, thereby winning the contest. Following his win, Frak was awarded the Elemental Power of Earth by Roby through the Elemental Cup, a moment that filled him with delight as he eagerly began to try with his abilities.

The Sword Shatters[]

Frak faced Zur in the second round of a game called Grid Drop. Just before the match started, Frak approached Cole to ask for tips on how to control Earth, as he found it more challenging than using Quake. Cole advised him to reach out and push up when using Earth. During the match, Frak found himself in a tough spot against Zur, who was using Reflex and Ice powers. Zur managed to freeze Frak to the blocks, and the tiles he was standing on suddenly collapsed, putting him at risk of losing. Just in time, Frak was reminded of Cole's advice, allowing him to harness Earth effectively. Frak charged at Zur, using Earth to lift the ground and send Zur crashing into the stands, ultimately eliminating him from the tournament. Frak emerged victorious in the round, and Roby transferred Zur's powers to him. However, after gaining these powers, Frak faced criticism from Ras, who insisted that he should only recognize him as his master, a claim Frak accepted with some reluctance.

Between The Sword Shatters and Clues and Suspects[]

Frak won in his third round.[4]

Clues and Suspects[]

Frak faced off against Sora in the semi-finals. The battle took place in a multi-platform fight, with Frak employing Ice as his primary element while Sora countered with Heat. Frak skillfully dodged Sora's fiery attacks and managed to damage Sora's hoverpad using Ice, ultimately knocking Sora off the platform with an Earth attack. During the fight, Sora questioned Frak's allegiance to Ras, prompting Frak to defend his mentor by stating that the ninja were misjudging Ras, who had taught him so much. As both combatants unleashed blasts of Ice and Heat, a thick smoke enveloped the arena, obscuring Frak's vision. Sora seized the opportunity to teleport in front of Frak and knocked him toward the edge of the platform. In a moment of desperation, Ras urged Frak to don the Wolf Mask to execute Shatterspin. However, recalling Arin's warning, Frak hesitated and ultimately chose to reject Ras' command, discarding the mask and opting for a fair fight.

The battle continued with hand-to-hand combat until Frak unleashed Ice and Quake, but Sora retaliated with a powerful Heat blast that sent Frak tumbling off the platform, resulting in his defeat. Following the match, Roby transferred Frak's powers to Sora using the Elemental Cup, and Frak congratulated Sora on her victory. Upon returning to the Wolf Clan, Frak was met with disappointment for his loss. Ras confronted Frak, scolding him for disobeying orders, and in a fit of rage, he lifted Frak by the throat and slammed him to the ground. As Frak lay there, he watched as Ras declared he never wanted to see him again, leaving Frak feeling disheartened as the Wolf Clan left.

The Final Game[]

Frak watched in surprise when Wyldfyre arrived with Zur to expose Bleckt after Nokt defeated Sora.

Elements of Betrayal[]

As the corrupted Wolf Clan launched their attack, Frak stood among the Elemental Masters, battling against Cinder and the Wolf Mask Warriors. While Nokt and Rox planned to free their comrades, Frak urged the ninja to pursue Nokt, assuring them that he and the others would hold back the Wolf Clan. Reflecting on his past of confronting bullies long before he became an Elemental Master, Frak emphasized that a person's worth goes beyond merely Elemental Powers. When Lloyd questioned Frak's confidence, he firmly replied, "ninja, go."

The fight intensified, and the wolf warriors initially gained the advantage, pushing Frak and the Elemental Masters to the brink of defeat. The Missing[]

Frak was deeply immersed in his training in the courtyard, channeling all his anger into each movement. Suddenly, Alfonzo Frohicky rushed over, distraught as he surveyed the destruction Frak had caused. Caught off guard, Frak apologized just as Lloyd approached him. Frak, feeling a bit uneasy, asked if he had done something wrong, to which Lloyd responded that the intensity of Frak's emotions was striking. He reminded Frak that such feelings were not the way of the ninja trained at the Monastery of Spinjitzu and questioned whether Ras had taught him to harness his anger in that manner. Frak insisted that he believed those emotions could be a source of strength, but Lloyd countered that there are many forms of strength, and relying solely on physical power is not the wisest choice. Determined yet still puzzled, Frak declared he would practice all night until he grasped the concept. Lloyd admired his enthusiasm, comparing it to Arin's spirited nature, but suggested that Frak take a break instead. As Frak contemplated the idea of resting, he noticed Sora and Riyu approaching. Sora awkwardly greeted him, acknowledging the tension that had built up between them. She explained her attempts to take over Arin's pie run, which had not gone well due to her lack of baking skills. Frak eagerly offered his assistance, boasting about his extensive collection of recipes. Without a moment's hesitation, he dashed off to gather all the necessary ingredients for their baking adventure.

After awhile, Frak and Sora were making pies. With a sense of pride, Frak pulled his pie from the oven, exclaiming that it was flawless, and eagerly turned to Sora to admire her creation. However, Sora's offering was less than perfect; she unveiled a charred pie. Sora lamented her lack of baking prowess. Frak attempted to comfort her, but when Sora's pie unexpectedly deflated, he remarked that such a phenomenon was against the laws of science. Realizing they needed a fresh start, Frak decided to bake another pie. Frak awkwardly shifted the topic to puppets. He recounted a video he had seen about a puppet wreaking havoc in the wastelands, and to his surprise, Sora was quick to learn that this was another rumor from Intelligent George. Sora cautioned Frak against trusting George's wild claims, especially since he had once asserted that gravity was merely a construct. This prompted Frak to challenge Sora, asking if she could prove gravity's existence. In response, Sora recalled Dorama's puppet, which led Frak to declare that he would steer clear of the Crossroads in the future. When Zane walked in, Sora casually remarked that he had a fondness for her pies. Intrigued, Frak turned to Zane and asked if that was true. Zane confirmed his liking but added that, as a Nindroid, he lacked taste buds to truly enjoy them. Annoyed, Sora directed their focus to the urgent matter of locating Arin. Zane expressed his frustration over the inability to track the gate system that could lead them to Arin, Ras, and the Forbidden Five. He also voiced his disappointment in not being able to find the ninja team's most brilliant and attractive member. Frak, assuming Zane was talking about Sora, was taken aback when Zane clarified he meant P.I.X.A.L., leaving Frak feeling a bit embarrassed. At that moment, the oven timer went off. However, the pie erupted, sending filling splattering everywhere. Zane's eyes widened in sudden realization, and he quickly dashed toward the elevator. Frak turned to Sora, suggesting that perhaps the pie mess had somehow disrupted Zane's circuits.

Frak and Sora approached Zane at the hangar bay, where the latter was focused on the screen displaying Sora's pie explosion. Zane insists that Sora's culinary disaster is brilliant, a sentiment echoed by Frak, who finds it comically genius. Zane clarified that the insight it provided into the mystery surrounding Arin's disappearance. He explained that while he had been analyzing the potential paths Arin might have taken through the portal gate, his initial calculations yielded no results. However, the unpredictable nature of Sora's pie explosion sparked a realization about the chaotic variables involved in space-folding gateways. As he input this new information into the system, the screen revealed a possible trajectory for Arin's location. With a renewed sense of purpose, Sora turned to Frak, stressing the urgency of finding Arin and suggesting that their pie-baking activities could wait. Frak quipped about whether Sora's pie-making could even be classified as baking.

Crashing Together[]

As Frak and the other ninja confronted Arin and Ras, Frak attempted to greet Arin, who was taken aback by Frak's presence among the ninja, while Ras glared at Frak with evident anger. To their astonishment, Frak and the ninja discovered that Arin regarded Ras as his new mentor. Frak retorted that was until he learned Ras' true nature. The group soon realized that both Ras and Arin possessed the final Prismatic Weapon. Sora tried to warn them that the Forbidden Five were after the Returning Blade to release Thunderfang, but Ras and a hesitant Arin chose to leave. Not wanting Arin to fall victim to the same fate as him, Frak charged at Ras, only for Arin to use Spinjitzu to push Frak aside. At that moment, a bounty hunter named Rogue arrived and abducted Ras. While Zane, Sora, and Riyu pursued Arin, who was intent on rescuing Ras, Frak and the others attempted to follow. However, a storm erupted, conjured by Drix and several Dragonian Warriors searching for the last two Prismatic Blades. Frak, along with Lloyd, Kai, Nya, and P.I.X.A.L., confronted Drix. After Drix knocked Kai out of the way, Frak stepped up to fight him, but Drix countered with Shatterspin, nearly sending Frak tumbling off the World Tree until P.I.X.A.L. intervened to save him. Drix then unleashed his Elemental Power of Swarm, revealing that he was composed of insects, a sight that Frak found far more terrifying than living puppets. Drix unleashed Swarm on Frak and P.I.X.A.L., causing them to crash. Drix snatched the blue Prismatic Blade from Lloyd and used Shatterspin against the ninja, forcing Frak and the others to cling to the branches for safety. Ultimately, Drix succeeded in claiming the final two Prismatic Blades necessary to free Thunderfang.

Chaos Unchained[]

Arin shared the heartbreaking news with Frak and the other ninja about the tragic death of his parents, a direct result of the chaos that erupted during the Merge. Frak felt deep sorrow for Arin, who cautioned that the awakening of Thunderfang would pose an even greater threat to many lives. Arin asked to work with the ninja, and they quickly rallied around him, eager to offer their support. Frak, in particular, yearned for the opportunity to fight alongside Arin again. However, Zane interrupted their moment, highlighting the formidable challenge they faced in trying to catch up to Drix, whose incredible speed made the task seem nearly impossible. When Frak questioned whether "impossible" truly meant it could not be achieved, Zane confirmed that it was indeed a significant obstacle. An argument between Kai and Nya gave Lloyd the idea that they could use the portal gate from their monastery to access the First Land, which can give them a head start.

Frak, Lloyd, and Kai mounted Riyu as they journeyed toward the hangar bay. Upon their arrival, the ninja lost their footing and tumbled to the ground, having slipped on the freshly waxed floors that Frohicky had just polished. Lloyd quickly gathered everyone, instructing them to reconvene at the dragon stables in a few minutes. Once the portal gate was activated, Frak and the ninja stepped through, heading to the First Land, while Frak, Lloyd, Kai, and Nya took to the skies on new gliders designed by Sora. They soon reached the location of Thunderfang's prison, where they could see the Winds of Chaos swirling around. As they waited for Drix to catch up, Frak turned to Arin, expressing his excitement about embarking on an adventure with the ninja, a dream they had cherished since childhood. However, Arin had a different perspective, insisting that his experiences were not as glamorous as Frak imagined. Frak suggested that Arin should blame him for instilling in him the belief that Ras' teachings were valuable. Arin argued that he had become a stronger ninja because of Ras' guidance. Frak countered, asserting that if Arin believed Ras could make him a better ninja than Lloyd, then he misunderstood what it meant to be a ninja.

As Drix arrived, he was confronted by the ninja. Amidst the fray, several Dragonian Warriors targeted Arin, but Frak rushed in and fended them off. In a brief moment of respite, he reminded Arin of the time he saved him against bullies. The battle intensified as the ninja and the Forbidden Five clashed over the blue Prismatic Blade. In a stroke of luck, Frak managed to seize the blade after Zane intervened to prevent a Dragonian Warrior from reclaiming it. However, three additional warriors charged in, overwhelming Frak and causing him to lose the Prismatic Weapon. Meanwhile, the Forbidden Five successfully acquired the last two blades and set off to liberate Thunderfang. Frak and the ninja quickly took up the chase, but their pursuit was abruptly halted as they were ensnared in the Winds of Chaos.

The Shatter Dragon[]

After their narrow escape from the Winds of Chaos, Frak and the others learned how the Forbidden Five had successfully absconded with the blades. It was Frak who first realized the implications of their actions: the Forbidden Five now had the means to release Thunderfang. In that moment, Thunderfang erupted from the earth, leading to a battle between the ninja and the Chaos Dragon. As Frak attempted to evade Thunderfang's stomps, he was struck by several boulders that fell in the chaos. Frak used his Quake powers, but they backfired, causing rocks to teeter dangerously close to him. In his frantic escape, he stumbled upon a group of Dragonian Warriors who launched an attack, leaving him battered. Just as Thunderfang's foot loomed over him, ready to crush him, Arin swooped in to save Frak at the last moment, in which Frak thanked Arin. Frak and Arin worked together to battle the Dragonian Warriors. During the skirmish, Arin was overwhelmed by them, but Frak used Quake to cause a cascade of rocks to crash down upon the Dragonian Warriors, effectively rescuing Arin and echoing his earlier sentiments about the desire to avoid harm during their first ninja adventure. However, the duo, along with their fellow ninja, witnessed Thunderfang draining Lloyd's soul to seize his Conduit powers.

Despite Frak's relentless efforts against the Dragonian Warriors, he soon found himself outmatched. Luckily, Arin hurled an elixir from the Well of the Lost at Thunderfang, resulting in the Chaos Dragon's demise. As Thunderfang fell, the souls he had consumed were released, allowing Lloyd's soul to return to his body. In a heartfelt reunion, Frak and the other ninja enveloped Lloyd in a warm embrace, with Frak joining in the hug, causing them all to tumble over. Ras arrived to remind Arin of the urgent mission to rescue his parents, so Arin left him. Despite Frak's attempts to dissuade him by insisting that Arin should reconsider, his words fell on deaf ears. To everyone's astonishment, Sora decided to accompany Arin and Ras on their quest. When Lloyd explained his support for Arin and Sora's choice to join Ras, Frak asked if he wasn't concerned for their safety, to which Lloyd admitted he was worry. Frak and the others soon discovered that Rogue was actually Jay, who had been taken to the monastery for treatment of his injuries. Upon their return, they learned that a celebration party had been organized in honor of the ninja. In the midst of these events, Frak and the group were shocked to find out that Arin's parents were alive.

Family

Family[]

Reginald Slitherbottom[]

“Frak, I just wanted you to have a father you could be proud of. You don't know what is used to be like for Serpentine in Ninjago. 'Never trust a snake,' they used to say. I had to work twice as hard for twice as long to get any recognition.”
“Yeah, well, you didn't even work half as hard at being a good dad.”
— Reginald and Frak, "The Grand Inquisition"

Reginald is Frak's father, with whom he has a strained relationship with. Reginald neglected Frak growing up to instead further his career in exploration, hoping that Frak could be proud of his father. However, despite his desire for having his son to admire and be proud of him, Reginald let his own vanity guide the way he rose Frak, causing him to be perfectly obedient and never to speak up, which allowed Reginald, even if without intended mallice, to verbally abuse Frak and dismiss his thoughts and ideas on a regular basis. All the neglect, combined with the condescendance he endured from his father, caused Frak to develop a great deal of resentment towards him, which came to its highest point after Reginald made no effort to help him and Sora when they were made suspects of being connected with the monster that attacked Umbrasilvis to the point of suspecting he was the one that released the monster and was using it to search for something.

Later, it is revealed Frak was wrong about suspecting his father, but even with Reginald saving him, both from the true culprit and from half of the Weapon of Desolation that was overcharing Frak's Quake, and confessing the motivation behind his actions, Frak simply couldn't overcome the resentment he felt for him to forgive him and left him in order to return to Ninjago with the others as soon as possible.

Ras (formerly)[]

“You have failed me!”
“It was a good fight. A fair fight.”
“I will not suffer disobedience! I offered you certain victory, and you refused. I never want you in my sight again!”
— Ras and Frak, "Clues and Suspects"

Before the Tournament of the Sources, Ras recruited Frak and taught him how to fight properly. Despite being strict with Frak, the latter considered him to be a good teacher. However, their relationship was filled with bumps, especially due to Frak's admiration towards the Ninja (Ras' enemies). When Frak approached Cole to seek for advice over how to use his element, Ras got upset. During his fight against Sora, Ras ordered Frak to wear his Wolf Mask, but the latter refused to wear it, after hearing from Arin that the masks shatter the goodness in the wearer, and lost his match. This made Ras very angry, and he banished Frak from the Wolf Clan and told him never to appear in his sight again.

After leaving Ras' team and joining the Ninja, Frak came to realize how toxic Ras really was. On one hand, he came to realize Ras was wrong about physical strength being the only source of power, as Lloyd came to teach him there were other ways to channel strength. Not just that, but he came to see Ras as nothing more but a manipulator that tricks others to follow him and, when he sees how his influence is affecting Arin, Frak's resentment towards him increased even more.
