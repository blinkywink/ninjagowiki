Overview

This article is about the band. For the episode, see The Royal Blacksmiths (episode).
“Dad? But you guys are just called The Royal Blacksmiths. You're not, you know, actual blacksmiths.”
— Cole to Lou, "Pause and Effect"

The Royal Blacksmiths is a barbershop quartet. They have won the Blade Cup several times in the Ninjago Talent.

History

History[]

The Royal Blacksmiths[]

The Blade Cup had been won by the Royal Blacksmiths multiple times, and Cole's father wanted to train the ninja to take the band's place.

Day of the Great Devourer[]

The Royal Blacksmiths were in Ninjago City for an anti-Serpentine rally when the Great Devourer attacked.

Darkness Shall Rise[]

Two members are being served food by Zane, who was working at the restaurant.

Ninjaball Run[]

The Royal Blacksmiths were contestants in the Ninjaball Run against the ninja but crashed in Birchwood Forest.

The Last Voyage[]

When Cole met up with Lou and his quartet to say goodbye, they sang him a song about how he's going to save Ninjago and make his father proud.

Rise of the Spinjitzu Master[]

The Overlord turned everyone, including the Royal Blacksmiths members, evil.

The Corridor of Elders[]

The Royal Blacksmiths listened to Jay as he gave a speech about saving Ninjago.

Day of the Departed[]

The Royal Blacksmiths performed at the city's stage to celebrate Day of the Departed.

Pause and Effect[]

After having been mistaken for real blacksmiths, the band performed for the other captives.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Jeppe Riddervold provides The Royal Blacksmiths' singing voice, though he is only credited for this in the 2012 blooper reel (as "The Mega Ultra Dragon Quartet") and "Day of the Departed".
  • The band uniforms are an altered version of Indiana Jones' white tuxedo torso from LEGO Indiana Jones.
  • In "The Royal Blacksmiths," the ninja take The Royal Blacksmiths' place and perform as a band called "Spin Harmony."
  • At certain points in "Day of the Great Devourer" and "Rise of the Spinjitzu Master," Lou is replaced with a second version of one of the other members.
  • One of the member's hair piece was similar to Lou's, up until "Day of the Departed", when it was replaced by a black version of Lloyd's original teenager hair piece (later used on Clutch Powers). The reason for this is unknown, though possibly to avoid confusion.
  • The character designs of two of the members (the one using Lloyd's Pre Season 8 hairpiece and the one using Ed/Dr. Julien/Griffin Turner's hairpiece) appear multiple times in the crowd of the concert in "Day of the Departed".
  • It is unknown when the group was formed, but it is known that it existed during Cole's childhood, as a painting of The Royal Blacksmiths appeared in "Dungeon Crawl!."
