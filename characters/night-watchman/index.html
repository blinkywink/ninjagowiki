Overview

“That's it. I'm calling it in. Let's see who's laughing.”
— Night Watchman to Morro, "Winds of Change"

The Night Watchman is the nocturnal patrol officer for the Ninjago Museum of History.

History

History[]

The Tall Tale of Doubloon (non-canon)[]

At one point, the watchman was stationed at Kryptarium Prison and noticed when Doubloon escaped.

Later, he patrolled Stiix, but he failed to notice the escaped thief slip by.

Winds of Change[]

One night while guarding the Museum's ninja exhibit, the Watchman spent his time playing with the large animatronics. However, he was interrupted when a shadowy figure began lurking around and haunting the exhibits. This figure turned out to be Morro, who later possessed the Watchman.

When the Watchman finally gained control of his body once more, Lloyd had arrived and the Watchman witnessed him being possessed by Morro, though its unknown if he tried to prevent the Ghost Warrior from doing so.

Kingdom Come[]

The Night Watchman walked with a police officer in Ninjago City.

Personality

Personality[]

The Watchman is known to be childish, playing with the mannequins to pass the time and telling an intruder that he unlocked his Full Potential and knows Spinjitzu. Moreover, he was somewhat callous, as he mocked the late Garmadon, even though the former dark lord had sacrificed himself to protect Ninjago.

Despite this, he is shown to worry about the fate of Ninjago when he read Morro's mind while being possessed.
