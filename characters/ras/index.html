Overview

“My master and I are in the process of undoing the Merge, and in that process, we could bring back our tribe!”
“Bring them back? How?”
“By capturing all of the Source Dragons, and through controlling their power, we will undo the Merge and build a new reality, one that will revive our people!”
— Ras and Zilvar, "The Summit"

Lord Ras (born Zeras) is an anthropomorphic tiger from the Wyldness, one of the two survivors of the Tiger Tribe along with Zilvar and the last living descendant of Zarkar. He was trained by an unknown being and chosen for their plan to conquer all the Source Dragons to reverse the Merge to create a new reality.

Before the Merge, Ras came to Imperium and assisted Empress Beatrix to gain control of the kingdom by assassinating her father, becoming the leader of the Claws of Imperium afterwards. Despite being a capable commander and a highly professional warrior, he has failed to stop Arin, Sora, and Lloyd from freeing and escaping with the dragons Imperium had captured. Consequently, Ras was stripped of his title as leader of the Claws and imprisoned for his failures by Empress Beatrix, who now decided to do the job herself. During the MergeQuake Storm, Ras managed to escape his cell and, in the aftermath, recruited Jordana for her achievement in extracting energy from a Source Dragon on behalf of his master.

A year after the Liberation of Imperium, Ras traversed the Forest of Spirits with Jordana and used the Source Dragon energy to open the Shadow Dojo. He reassembled the Wolf Clan and attacked the Cloud Kingdom and the Land of Lost Things, seeking to free the Forbidden Five during the Ritual of the Blood Moon, but only succeeded in releasing Nokt after banishing Kai to the Nether-Space and unknowingly Rox's soul who took possession of Jordana's body. After he and his allies escaped, he promises his master that he would not fail him again.

Attending the Tournament of the Sources, Master Ras recruited the amnesiac Jay, deceiving the latter in the process, and Arin's old friend, Frak beforehand to teach them how to win the tournament. After both combatants lost, he casted them aside while also telling Arin about how Master Wu was responsible for the Merge before watching Nokt defeat Sora and prepared to take over the Merged Realms. However, Nokt betrayed Ras and released the rest of the Forbidden Five, until his seized powers were taken from him, forcing them to retreat to the Cloud Kingdom portal gate with the tiger dragged along when he attempted to intervene. Ras ended up in the First Land alongside Arin after the gate was destroyed, and the latter helped nurse Ras back to health with intentions of finding his parents.

For several weeks, Ras trained Arin, who took care of him while he was in a bad state. After Ras' condition got worse, Arin carried him to the Spectral Lands, where he found the Well of the Lost and used its healing elixir to save Ras from dying. Ras then took Arin to a mysterious graveyard and showed him the tombstones of his parents, explaining that he can bring them back by reversing the Merge as they could alter reality during that process. Both of them travelled to the World Forest and found out the information they needed from the Spirit of the Temple, before encountering the ninja. Ras was soon kidnapped by the Forbidden Five, who took him to the Storm Village, where Ras had his final showdown with Nokt before being saved by Arin and the Forbidden Five released Thunderfang. After the Shatter Dragon got defeated, Ras left the ninja with Arin and also Sora, who joined them, to continue their quest to reverse the Merge.

Ras took Arin and Sora to the Forge of the Forgotten, where he revealed his true intentions of sacrificing Sora to forge a Source Dragon Icon. Arin realized that Ras was manipulating him by giving little information and left with Sora to reunite with the ninja. Ras then killed an unknown mortal to forge a Source Dragon Icon and fought his way out of the Underworld. With the ninja spread throughout the Merged Lands to awaken the Arc Dragon of Focus, Ras resumed his master's plans, eventually using the portal gates to arrive at the Monastery of Spinjitzu. There, he sought out Wu's research after defeating Alfonzo Frohicky, Geo, and Rontu, stealing the Realm medallion and the Icon of the Source Dragon of Motion in the process. After trapping Wu in a protective container, Ras tampered with the portal gates, dispersing the ninja to the most remote corners of the Merged Lands, all the while turning the portals off so the ninja couldn't use them to return. In the following days, he obtained a new Source Dragon Icon and recreated the Icon of the Source Dragon of Energy. He also manipulated Zilvar to betray the ninja to protect his master's Weapon of Desolation which resulted in the destruction of Lloyd's mortal body although the weapon was lost in the Abyss of Eternal Night.

Lord Ras is a humanoid tiger with black fur and pink stripes all over his body. On the face, these stripes are only in the forehead area and under his red eyes, which are surrounded by gray fur that resemble eyebrows. He also has four large white fangs protruding from his mouth. His chin hair is braided into a small black braid akin to a beard and he has three white claws on his feet. On certain occasions such as during the Blood Moon, or when he is empowered by his master, all of his pink stripes change color to red along with his hands and he gains extra strength. As a result of his battle with Nokt following the return of the Forbidden Five, Ras attained a scar over the left side of his face.

As a child, Ras wore a simple purple kimono with pink trims, his name written in pink in Ninjargon, and a red suit underneath in addition to purple pants. He also didn't have his chin hair braided as it was too short.

As an adult, Ras wore the traditional attire of his tribe until he joined Imperium. It is composed of a gray kimono that left his entire right side exposed, had gold ornamental trims, and was tied with a simple tan cloth.

When Ras became part of Imperium, he began to wear sleeveless red robes with gold detailing that is tied by a red belt with golden symbols and a gold and red buckle. He wore a sleeveless gray suit under his robes and gold hexagonal plating that covered his black pants, in addition to black boots with gold detailing. He also wore golden gauntlets and a large golden armor that covers his neck and shoulders.

When Ras became leader of the Wolf Clan, he switched to a black kimono with gray trims and a dark purple clergy stole with silver symbols and light blue edges under a new dark purple armor and gloves. He also wears a gray and light blue belt, his boots have detailing of the same colors, and his new kimono has a light blue plating that bears the Wolf Clan symbol. The back of his kimono has a large light blue crescent symbol with his name written in silver in Ninjargon.

During the Tournament of the Sources, Ras is shirtless, revealing his bare torso, which has pink stripes and six pack. He also removed his dark purple gloves and has changed to a black belt with light blue crystals and a big brown rope on top with the same blue crystals on the ends. His boots no longer have the gray detailing and he wears light blue crescent-like knee pads and two-pronged arm armor, in addition to shoulder pads made of black spikes and joined to two light blue claw-like plating, which have chains connecting a small light gray armor plating and a dark gray circle bearing a silver version of the Wolf Clan symbol on his back.

After defeating some of the Greenbone Warriors and Re-Awakened, Ras discarded his spike-like shoulder pads and briefly had armor apparently made from their bones. It resembled a ribcage, covering his upper torso, and has bone-like shoulder pads and two large bone-like plates covering his upper chest. He also started wearing the Greenbone Leader's helmet and a skull-like mask that covers his face. However, he was forced to destroy the armor when Geo restrained him using Fusion and he later removed the mask and helmet, returning to his previous attire.

Personality

Personality[]

“Are you sure you should've told the ninja brat all of that, Lord Ras? Information can be power.”
“And information can be poison. I ommitted some of my intentions, but everything I told him was true. And that truth will poison his mind.”
“And that poison will spread to the other ninja. And drive them all apart.”
— Jordana and Ras, "Truth and Lies"

Ras is a very manipulative, ruthless, intelligent, power-hungry and intimidating tiger humanoid. When he was a child in the Wyldness, Ras was abandoned until he met his master. During the long years they spent together, Ras learned from his master that strength was all that mattered and decided to fulfill his master's plan, even if it took years. His master's influence deeply shaped Ras and solitified a doctrine of strength in his mind, making him view helping and acts of kindess as a sign of weakness. He was indocrinated at a very young age and driven by the motivation to bring back the Tiger Tribe, which would only be possible by reversing the Merge, the ultimate goal of his master. As pointed out by Nokt and Rox, Ras is sometimes too arrogant and blind to see the truth right in front of him, being blinded by the thought of his own cleverness and the perfection of his master's plan. Ras can also be quite prideful and sometimes pick the wrong battles, as he was prepared to fight Nokt (who had just accumulated all of the Elemental Powers in the Tournament of the Sources), Rox (possessing Jordana), and the entire Wolf Clan corrupted by the latter.

Over the course of his life, Ras was chased and hated by multiple people or creatures and that led him to believe that his strength attracts opponents who want to eliminate him.

When he first came to Imperium, he managed to win Princess Beatrix over to his side with lies, making use of Beatrix' extreme desire for appreciation and her short temper, and then helped her rise to power by ruthlessly murdering her father. He later imprisoned the Source Dragon of Energy, exploiting the resources Imperium had to offer, and started draining its energy.

Being granted the position of the leader to the Claws of Imperium, Ras became a respected and feared authority among the dragon hunters. He didn't tolerate failure and expected absolute discipline and nothing but success from his subordinates. When it came to setbacks, Ras would punish the accountable Imperians by grabbing them at their collar and slamming them to the ground. However, despite having a powerful and influential occupation, Ras remained a low profile, simply following his master's will and not seeking attention or glory from the Imperium population. Even the people that worked close to him, never really got to know Ras as he didn't disclose much about himself and hid his true motivations from outsiders.[1] Some Imperians, like Sora, didn't even seem to know of Ras' existence, emphasizing how he mostly stayed under the radar, acting as a secretive yet powerful and manipulative force in the background. Just like most of the people he met, Ras deemed the Imperians as mere tools for him to exploit in his master's plan.

Once he was imprisoned by the Empress, he tried to manipulate her again, telling her that she had no chance of winning without him. When he then managed to escape, he met Jordana, whom he chose as the next person he could manipulate. He revealed to her his true intentions, which is that he serves his true master, to whom he is truly devoted. His devotion is even so great that he considers his mission the only significant thing and sees no possibility of failure.

A year after the downfall of the Kingdom of Imperium, Ras revealed to Jordana how he perceived Imperians to be self-obsessed when it came to how little Imperians knew about the world beyond their city's borders. This shows that he hated the culture of the Imperians and their ways of completely ignoring the other realms and thinking they were superior to everyone else. He then opened the Shadow Dojo and welcomed another member of his new army, Cinder. Together with Jordana, he then taught them, Cinder how to properly use Shatterspin and he encouraged Jordana to study the Theroxian scrolls and learn their magic. A rivalry, fed by the two's desire to become Ras' right hand, in their ambition to please their master, led to Jordana and Cinder blindly following his every order. When they succeeded, Ras was satisfied with them, but when they showed signs of displeasure or disappointment, he would become very angry and even physically hurt them.

Before the Tournament of the Sources, Ras manipulated Jay into thinking he was his student that fought alongside him before the Merge and that he hated the ninja and also recruited Arin's childhood friend, Frak, to become his champion in the tournament. Although being strict, Frak was amazed by his master's skills and lessons, not knowing about all the evil he had done.

He also seems to display some sadistic tendencies as well, as shown when brutalizing Arin and Riyu in the Forest of Spirits, he taunts the young Ninja about how the Ninja make him "weak" and "fail" him. And when Jay expresses his hatred for Nya, he cruelly smiles at Nya's misery and walks away, also when Zeatrix wrathfully strikes Lloyd with the Yellow Prismatic Blade, knocking him off the platform, he gleefully laughs at his suffering.

During the Tournament, Ras started to manipulate Arin into thinking the ninja, especially Lloyd, weren't good masters for him. He revealed to Arin his true intentions about wanting to reverse the Merge and also that it had been caused by Master Wu. When asked about this by Rox possessing Jordana, he told her that information, albeit true, could work as poison if put into a right mind, Arin's mind. This manipulation later paid off as Arin abandoned the ninja and saved Ras.

There seems to be a pattern to Ras' manipulation victims as he only chooses vulnerable, insecure, and heavily naïve people to manipulate. Beatrix, Jordana, Bleckt, Arin, Frak, and Jay all share some of these traits. He always poisons their minds with something he has to offer to fulfill their dreams but in reality, he only sees them as devices to help him achieve his goals. If these victims fail him in any way, he is not hesitant to discard them from his alliance and abandon them.

During Arin's training, Ras was very weak from his battle with Nokt but he was still determined to teach Arin his ways of fighting. He constantly repeated to the young ninja how strength is the most important thing and he has to focus on his anger and rage so he can truly access his real powers. Despite Arin saving Ras' life multiple times, he never thanked the ninja because of his nature.

However, Ras still showed some genuine feelings, mostly when his mysterious past is mentioned. His motivation to reverse the Merge seems to come from the loss of his people. He claims that by undoing the Merge, he can alter reality in the process and thus bring his people back, believing that his goals are noble and despite his actions being evil, he thinks it is necessary because every great warrior has to make difficult choices to save those who are dear to them. At some point, he realized that reaching his end goal would be a long, thankless journey, and he developed the belief that needing to be liked is equivalent to weakness; he would do what he felt necessary to be done regardless if he would be hated for it. Despite this, he also seemingly cared for Arin at least a little bit, as he saw himself in the young ninja, a weakling with no master, and tried to help him by teaching him how to be a ferocious warrior. While Arin was mourning the supposed deaths of his parents, Ras tried to comfort him by placing a hand on his shoulder, and later sympathized with Arin's goal of saving his parents, as he also always wanted to save his people. Ras also seemed to be genuinely smiling after Arin defeated Thunderfang, though he groaned once his pupil briefly ran off to celebrate with the ninja.

Ras' main philosophy comes from what his master had taught him, believing that strength is the most important thing in the world and it is the only way to solve any issues and how to become the greatest. As Arin points out, due to this, Ras has anger management issues, as he always tries to solve everything by fighting instead of talking. Ras doesn't care about anyone or anything except for himself, as he sees everyone only as a device for gaining his goals and after having no use for them, he abandons them.

After Sora joined their team, Ras tolerated her despite being annoyed by her but later revealed that he had only allowed her presence so he could use her a sacrifice in the Forge of the Forgotten. He tried to manipulate Arin into thinking it is what's best but the ninja opposed him. Ras then snapped, used his powered-up form, and tried to kill both of them, finally breaking his bond with Arin.

Ras focused on his goals and obtained a Source Dragon Icon by sacrificing a person. After defeating a group of Greenbone Warriors, he took advantage of the ninja's absence from the Monastery of Spinjitzu to steal a scroll about containing energy and later captured Wu, controlling every portal gate for his own agenda, leaving the ninja stranded in different realms while defeating Geo, Frohicky, and Rontu with both Source Dragon Icons in his possession.

Abilities[]

“Ras is a master manipulator. He could be brainwashing Arin and Arin would have no clue.”
— Frak about Ras' skills, "The Great Zane Robbery"
  • Tiger Physiology: As an anthropomorphic tiger from the Wyldness, Ras has the following abilities:
    • Night vision: Like many felines, Ras possesses exceptional night vision, enabling sight in dimly lit environments or complete darkness.[2]
    • Superhuman speed/stamina: In addition to his feline nature, Ras can move at incredibly fast speed for a long time without tiring out, being able to catch up to Arin when the ninja was driving the Explorer away from the Shadow Dojo.
    • Superhuman durability/endurance: Ras has superhuman levels of durability and endurance, managing to endure Rapton's taser and hits from dragons. In the past, he managed to take on Zeatrix's shockwave attack and leave without showing any signs of exhaustion. He was also able to take several elemental blows from Nokt after the latter had absorbed nearly every Elemental Powers. He was also undamaged after getting repeatedly attacked by several Chaos-empowered Wolf Mask Warriors. Even when critically injured from Nokt's Shatterspin, Ras managed to gather enough strength to walk to the elixir. He took a direct Shatterspin punch to the face by Drix and even survived being crushed under large rocks from the Underworld caused by Arin’s Object Spinjitzu. While fighting Rontu, he managed to take a surprised tail whip from her.
      • Heat Resistance: Ras has a resistance to high temperatures, allowing him to shrug off the heat from the recently created Dragon Icon bone sword. Although he is capable of feeling some pain from heat, as when Nokt broke his grip using Heat, he is still tough enough to survive the wolf's Heat blast. He also managed to survive in the high temperatures of the Wyldness.
    • Superhuman leaping: Ras can jump at incredibly high distance. He was able to leap far enough to catch up to the Explorer during the Ritual of the Blood Moon.
    • Superhuman strength: Ras has superhuman strength to overpower his opponents. Thanks to the Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength, he managed to bring down several dragons; species larger than him, knock Lloyd out of his Spinjitzu Tornado during the Blood Moon ritual, smash Nokt out of his Shatterspin, and even overpower Rontu with two strikes. Even without the Strength Icon, he is still able to demonstrate several feats such as lifting people with one arm and throwing Arin down to the ground during the Ritual of the Blood Moon. He did the same to Geo and proved to be stronger than Alfonzo, leaving both injured. During the Underworld fight, Ras managed to repel Arin's Object Spinjitzu-powered rock. Later, at the Monastery of Spinjitzu, he managed to take apart his fused bone armor, punch Geo to the ground after throwing him upward, and knock him out.
    • Superhuman agility: Ras has superhuman agility, able to do flips when fighting enemies. He also managed to dodge Rontu's tail whip, despite having both icons in his hands.
    • Superhuman reflexes: Ras has superhuman reflexes, as he managed to keep up with humans and several Elemental Masters. However, he is slower than members of the Forbidden Five like Nokt and Drix, who managed to get the drop on him.
    • Regenerative healing factor: Ras has regenerative healing factor, able to heal wounds from battles but gain scars. The limits of his healing factor are unknown but he required the healing elixir to heal from his injuries from Nokt's Shatterspin. But, he managed to recover and walk on both legs during a fight with Thunderfang immediately after Arin defeated him.
    • Quadrupedalism: Owing to his tiger-like nature, Ras can move on all four limbs like an actual animal, an ability that he uses to run faster, which makes it hard for humans to keep up.[3]
  • High intelligence: Ras has shown to have a high intelligence, being highly knowledgeable in many things the Ninja are unaware of. He even claims to know a great many things, including the truth about this universe.
    • Master Manipulator: Ras' intellect also makes him highly skilled and dangerous at manipulating people. He showed an ease in recruiting and rallying people to his cause like Beatrix, Jordana, Jay, Frak, Arin, and probably Cinder. He also knows how to use people's weaknesses, information he possesses, and lies to manipulate others as Frak, Jay, Arin, and Zilvar paid the price. This is especially seen in the way he manipulates Arin to help him in his cause, taking advantage of his insecurity on his skills and using the fact that Master Wu caused the Merge and the information he got from Jay about Arin's parents being in the Land of Lee to create a divide between the young Ninja and his friends (especially Sora and Lloyd) and drive him to side with him.
    • Skilled tactician: One of Ras’ greatest uses of his intelligence is that he is also a skilled tactician, often having a contingency plan in case the original one failed.
  • Skilled combatant: Ras is a fearsome combatant in martial arts who can efficiently hold against most of the ninja, easily defeat Arin on his own, and even stand up to powerful Elemental Masters like Lloyd and Nokt. He can also take on multiple opponents like Chaos-empowered Wolf warriors and corrupted Dragonian warriors, whereas powerless Elemental Masters and the Ninja had struggled to defeat them. While slightly weakened, he was able to hold against Nokt one-on-one until the wolf managed to knock him down with a Shatterspin blow. After getting his injuries healed, Ras managed to defeat two Dragonian Warriors at the World Forest and later several Re-Awakened and Greenbone Warriors in the Underworld. He also had the upper hand against Arin and Sora in the Underworld, the two ninja only defeating him thanks to luck. At the Monastery, he was able to easily defeat Geo without any of his dragon icons and even defeat Rontu.
    • Martial arts: Being a martial arts teacher, Ras is great at passing his martial arts to his students. He managed to teach Cinder to be a formidable opponent, improve Nokt's skills, teach Frak how to use his Quake powers and Shatterspin, and even taught Arin to do a full Spinjitzu tornado, where the ninja also managed to briefly fight Nokt using Ras' teachings.
    • Hammer Mastery: Ras primarily fights with the Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength, a Source Dragon Icon in the form of a war hammer which he's skilled in using to attack others and defend himself.
    • Swordsmanship: Having carried several blades over his life, Ras is a skilled swordsman. He used the Forge Icon together with his hammer to fight Rontu.
  • Magic: Despite being a warrior, Ras has an unspecified degree of magic, allowing him to use magic spells or curses. Using one of the Theroxian scrolls, he was able to recite the incantation to use the Forbidden Five's Theroxian magic that aided Jordana in taking control of Bonzle. He also used magic to imprison Wu in the protective container.
    • Curse inducement/Mind control: Ras can place curses on his chosen targets. He used this ability to hide information by cursing the Scroll-Worms and turning them hostile. Moreover, he can also control the Scroll-Worms and make them obey his commands.
  • Leadership: As leader of the Claws of Imperium, Ras led the Claw Hunters on dragon hunts. He even led the Wolf Clan during the invasion of the Cloud Kingdom and the Tournament of the Sources.
  • Pylikinesis: Ras has somehow gained control over the portal gates in each of the Merged Realms, which he used to transport the ninja into the farthest reaches of the Merged Lands and shut down the gates, rendering them inaccessible to everyone except himself.
    • Portal Negation: Ras was able to disrupt and disable the portal gates.
  • Communication with his master: Ras is able to communicate with his master in their void when they call him.
    • Levitation: When communicating with his master, Ras levitates in the air.
  • Anger/Empowered Form: When angered, Ras can access an empowered form where he gains red hands and stripes and greater power. This enhancement is possible due to his master's powers.
    • Attribute Enhancement: In his anger form, Ras develops increased physical attributes. His strength is enhanced to the point where he can easily take down multiple Wolf warriors. His durability and endurance are also enhanced, as he managed to take hits from the corrupted Wolf warriors, including Nokt's stolen Elemental Powers, and it took a Shatterspin blow to knock him out.   • Expert Pilot/Driver: Ras is an expert pilot and driver.
  • Expert Biker: Ras is also an expert biker.
  • Vast resources: Ras has many contingencies and resources placed across the Merged Lands in case of need, such as the Super Storm Jet in the First Land and possibly the Explorer in the Wyldness.
  • Expert Craftsman/Armorer: Ras is an expert in flawlessly crafting items, seeing that he was able to construct armor from the bones of Greenbone Warriors and Re-Awakened.

Etymology

Etymology[]

Ras means "head" in Amharic and it is a royal title in Ethiopian Semitic languages being similar to "duke" or "prince."

His birth name, "Zeras", is similar to the Hebrew name "Zerah", which literally means "Dawn".

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • According to concept art, Ras was originally supposed to be a big fig like Dogshank and Killow but this was changed in the process.
    • Additionally, his original head design was based on 70200 CHI Laval[10] and his whole body was similar to Hulk but in gold.
    • On the other concept art, he is more similar to real life tigers based on his orange color, black stripes, and his head design. His attire was based on ancient Greece, Persia, and the Mongol Khanate as was the rest of Imperium in early stages of development.[11]
  • Similarly to the Crystal King's minifigure, Ras' teeth aren't painted in his minifigure form.
  • Based on the box art of 71813 Wolf Mask Shadow Dojo, Ras' Wolf Clan outfit was supposed to be different in some stages of development. The biggest changes are that his shoulders armor was the Imperium armor recolored in purple, his belt was originally made of rope, there was a word "blood" written on his suit in Ninjargon, and he had arm printing similar to his other minifigures.[12]
  • By the second half of Season 1, Ras' voice has drastically changed, being higher and softer.
    • Although there is no in-universe reason for the change, it was likely to provide some form of accommodation for his voice actor Brian Drummond, as doing a voice so deep and low can be damaging to one's throat.

History

Background

Background[]

Many years before the Merge, Ras was born in the Realm of the Wyldness as a member of the Tiger Tribe under the name Zeras and learned of the Forbidden Five and Shadow Dojo from the Ancients of the Wyldness. When he was a cub, his village faced a series of tremors. During one such tremor, Zeras was knocked down by the shaking and helped up by another cub. As Zilvar left the village in search of a new home for the tribe, Mei, Zeras, and the other cubs waved goodbye to him. Over time, Zeras and the others came to believe that Zilvar had either perished or abandoned them. The tremors persisted, ultimately leading to the destruction of their village and leaving Zeras and Zilvar as the only survivors.[1]

Shortly after escaping the doom of his tribe, Zeras got lost in the Forest of Spirits where he fell into a chasm while trying to retrieve his ball and, feeling hopeless, broke down until a mysterious being rescued him. They took him under their wing and taught him the importance of strength, which Zeras came to value above all else.[2] In service of his master, he later changed his name to Ras.[1]

Sometime later, Ras would duel an opponent who owned the Returning Blade, and after winning the bout, kept the Prismatic Blade for himself.[3]

At some point before he traveled to Imperium, Ras encountered at least one of the Source Dragons.[4]

Several years later, he had a mission to assist his Master in gaining complete domination over the Source Dragons, which would allow them to use their life force to have power of the realms.[2][4][5] Ras succeeded in acquiring two Source Dragon Icons, specifically those representing the Source Dragons of Energy and Strength. He later departed from the Wyldness and embarked on a journey to Imperium. His strategy involved utilizing the kingdom's advanced technology to capture the Source Dragons. Ras — who by then went by the title of lord — approached the Royal Family of Imperium with a bold claim, asserting that he possessed something truly extraordinary that could greatly benefit the kingdom. He explained that he and Levo were well aware of the eventual decline of the dragon power that had been crucial in powering Imperium. Ras proposed with capturing a Source Dragon, which would provide an unlimited supply of dragon energy to sustain the kingdom. However, Levo disagreed, dismissing Source Dragons as mere myths and emphasizing the peaceful coexistence between dragons and Imperians. Despite Levo's skepticism, Ras persisted in his attempts to convince him, arguing that a single Source Dragon could enable them to conquer all realms. This led to disagreements, with Ras criticizing Levo for his lack of foresight. Levo made it clear that Imperium had no need for Ras' offers. As Ras left the throne room, he cast a final glance towards Beatrix, respectfully bowing to her before exiting the Imperium Palace.[4]

As Ras prepared to depart, he unexpectedly encountered Beatrix, intentionally mistaking her for Zeatrix. Beatrix agreed to Ras' proposal of assisting Imperium in capturing Source Dragons. Curious, Ras inquired if Beatrix would defy her own father. Beatrix revealed that she had fallen out of her father's favor due to Zeatrix inheriting Elemental Powers. Ras expressed his belief that Elemental Powers were frequently bestowed upon those who were unworthy of them. He further acknowledged Zeatrix's future role as the ruler of the Imperium due to her being the firstborn. In response, Beatrix informed Ras that if he assisted her in ascending to the throne, she would provide him with the necessary resources to capture the Source Dragons. Accepting her proposition, Ras acquiesced, addressing her as Empress. Ras plotted to sabotage Emperor Levo's hover chariot, which resulted in a catastrophic crash that claimed the Emperor's life. He also had the Imperium Guards arrest Zeatrix.[4]

After Beatrix ascended to the throne as Empress of Imperium, she established the Claws of Imperium, an organization led by Ras. Ras managed to capture the Source Dragon of Energy by employing the Icon of the Source Dragon of Energy to summon him, successfully imprisoning the dragon within a glass core.[6] However, Imperium could not contain two Source Dragons simultaneously, which hindered Ras from using the Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength to call forth the Source Dragon of Strength so he instead kept the icon as a weapon and for later use.[7] As time passed and the realms merged due to the Merge, Ras set his sights on a young Mountain Dragon named Riyu, recognizing his crucial role in his grand scheme.[8] At some point, Ras broke into the Cloud Kingdom archives to collect information and later placed a curse upon the Scroll-Worms, rendering them unable to reveal any information that he wished to keep hidden. As a result, the worms would become hostile towards anyone who inquired about such forbidden knowledge.[9][10] One of the secrets that Ras sought to conceal was the existence of the Dragon Cores, which served an immense purpose in bringing an end to the MergeQuakes.[11]

After the Claws of Imperium had succeeded in capturing Riyu for Ras' plan, Ras contacted Rapton, who assured Ras that everything had been taken care of as they start the transfer in the next day. Ras tells Rapton not to let his confidence get the best of him, as rare dragons such as Riyu are considered to be dangerous. As Rapton continued to be arrogant, Ras warned him that he didn't want any interruptions in the plan, ending the call.

The next day, Ras received news of Riyu's escape and made his way to the Crossroads. He censured Rapton for his failure and went to confront Arin and Sora. Arin tried to fight Ras, but the tiger grabbed him by the foot and threw him to the ground. He then defeated Sora by dodging her attack and threw the Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength at the SA Mark 01, dismantling it. Before he could finish her off, however, Riyu came up behind him. Ras used an Imperium Sword to shoot the baby dragon, taunting him that he plans to capture his entire herd. Suddenly, a MergeQuake appeared in the sky, much to Ras' satisfaction. He then instructed Rapton and the Claw Hunters to take the two teenagers and Riyu to a prison train, not wanting any further hindrances in the plan.

The Merge: Part 2[]

Rapton reached out to Ras to inform him that they had successfully captured a Matriarch Dragon. Ras remained stationed in his hover chariot to see what would happen next. Soon, Lloyd was able to defeat Rapton and the Claw Hunters with his Spinjitzu, which was augmented by dragon energy. Following this, Ras returned to Imperium. On his way, Ras informed Beatrix that Lloyd had managed to establish a connection with the Matriarch Dragon, meaning that they could now pursue more Source Dragons like the one that Imperium had previously captured.

Crossroads Carnival[]

Because Beatrix wanted to use the Photac to hunt down dragons, Ras intercepted the ninja's call to the police and freed Dorama from his cage.

Beyond Madness[]

Ras entered the Advanced Systems Lab with two Imperium Guards, demanding answers about the Photac, to which Dr. LaRow hesitated to give a definitive response. Ras insisted on receiving a clear answer, prompting Dr. LaRow to admit that while Dorama could make the original Photac work, activating the copies was beyond his capabilities. Dr. LaRow explained that only Sora possessed the necessary skills for that task. Frustrated, Ras stormed out of the lab.

Writers of Destiny[]

Ras and Beatrix had Rapton, and the Claw Hunters go over to the Cloud Kingdom and capture the Ice Dragons while the ninja were distracted from fighting the tentacled beasts. When Rapton called Ras to him that the plan went well, Ras gloated about Imperium's victory.

Mindless Beasts[]

As Sora was forced to activate the Photac in order to save her dragon companion, Ras explained that the dragon's energy would still be drained until the device was operational. Ras ordered Dorama to begin the extraction process as Sora was fixing the device. In the midst of Sora's repairs, Ras listened to Dr. LaRow's taunts to Sora, saying that with the Photacs, Imperium can capture all dragon-kind, even the Source Dragons. However, Ras silenced her.

After Sora completed the Photac, Ras marveled at the Photac Beast that had been summoned and ordered Dorama to stop the extraction process. With the Photac now complete, Ras declared that nothing could stand in the way of Imperium.

I Will Be the Danger[]

Ras ordered Rapton to gather the Claw Hunters and bring them to the Advanced Systems Lab. Yet, Rapton was hesitant, as their orders were to prevent the escape of the dragons. Despite his reservations, Ras remained adamant about taking immediate action and abruptly terminated the communication. Before long, Rapton and the Claw Hunters reached the lab, where Ras commanded them to mount the Photacs.

The Calm Inside[]

Lord Ras was ordered to return to the Imperium Palace. As he went back Ras discovered that Riyu had been apprehended by Percival Tartigrade and was now back in custody. Beatrix reminded Ras of the crucial role Riyu played in the ongoing plan, despite the ninja's constant interference. Beatrix then ordered Ras to bring in Dr. LaRow so that she could extract all of Riyu's dragon energy. However, Ras was hesitant to comply, explaining that the energy was required for the plan. Beatrix was unhappy with Ras's reluctance and scolded him, stating that she would not take orders from anyone, including outlanders like Ras. The outburst enraged Ras, but he had no choice but to comply and call in Dr. LaRow.

As Dr. LaRow and Jordana began the extraction process, Ras watched, only to be interrupted by a sudden attack on the Imperium Palace by the dragons. Ras and Beatrix quickly evacuated the palace and confronted Lloyd and the dragons. When the battle continued, Ras drove his hover chariot into Sora and attacked the dragons with his hammer until the arrival of the other ninja coincided with the Monastery of Imperium emerging from the ground.

The Battle of the Second Monastery[]

Despite more of the ninja arriving, Ras led the Claws of Imperium into battle. Ras commandeered one of the Photac Beasts to engage in a fight with the dragons, but was eventually knocked off from the Photac by the Fire Dragon. When the ninja and the dragons retreated to the Monastery of Imperium, Ras attempted to break into the monastery but was informed by Dr. LaRow that Arin and Sora had escaped. Ras expressed his disappointment to Dr. LaRow, stating that she only had to keep an eye on them. Ras returned to the Imperium Palace.

Confronting Arin and Sora, Ras offered them the choice between surrendering or perishing, with the result being that Riyu will be his in either case. However, Arin and Sora refused to surrender, engaging in a fierce battle with Ras. Despite his attempts to attack them, Ras continued to miss his targets, causing frustration and anger to build up inside him. He belittled the duo, claiming that they were too insignificant to even begin to understand his plan. Ras threw his hammer at Arin, knocking him unconscious. He then punched Sora, causing her to collide with the control panel of Riyu's cage, and rebuked them for not giving up when they had the chance. But doing so allowed Riyu to break free, unleashing his dragon power and activating Sora's Technology. Utilizing her powers, Sora trapped Ras in Riyu's cage, immobilizing him and ending the battle.

Days later, Ras received a summons to the throne room accompanied by the Claws of Imperium. He intented to launch more dragon hunts, but Beatrix had different ideas by wanting the ninja gone. Ras tried to convince her of the potential benefits Imperium could gain from just one Source Dragon, but before he could finish, Beatrix commanded the Claws to arrest him for his failure. One of the Claw Hunters restrained Ras with handcuffs, leaving him confused. In an attempt to defend himself, Ras retaliated with a kick, only to be instantly incapacitated by an electric shock from Rapton. Ras pleaded with Beatrix, emphasizing that his plan would not succeed without his involvement. Beatrix, on the other hand, warned Ras that with the dragons gone, he would be placed in the Imperium Prison.

The Temple of the Dragon Cores[]

Ras was in his cell at the Imperium Prison, where he caught sight of an Imperium Guard using their tablet. He intently observed Beatrix's speech about any Imperian daring to broach the subject of the dragons' escape or the energy crisis would face immediate arrest.

Gangs of the Sea[]

Ras paced restlessly within his cell, emitting low growls under his breath, until he eventually settled on his bench. He became aware of the surveillance camera fixed upon him. He glared toward the security camera, while Rapton taunted him for his misfortunes.

Absolute Power[]

Ras was visited by Beatrix in his cell, where he tries to ask for his release in order to continue their efforts, but she refuses before dealing with the ninja when they infiltrated the front gate.

We Are All Dragons[]

Ras overhears the people chanting 'We are all dragons' in retaliation for Beatrix's deception and looked out to see two guards applaud as well, amusing him.

The Power Within[]

When the MergeQuake Storm occurred, it took out the power, therefore, Ras used it as an opportunity to escape. It was then that he learned about Jordana successfully extracting Source Dragon energy, a remarkable feat that was deemed impossible. Blaming the ninja for the chaos that ensued, Ras made his way back to his master to inform them about Jordana's accomplishment. In the aftermath, he approached Jordana, who was feeling ashamed for her role in the MergeQuake Storm and believed she could never match up to Sora. Ras assured Jordana that she had the potential to surpass Sora in abilities. Ras explained that with the raw energy Jordana had drained from the Source Dragon, they could unlock forbidden secrets and tap into dark powers that had been dormant for centuries. He pictured a future where they would be unstoppable, with no one, not even the ninja, able to stand in their way. As he prepared to leave, he invited Jordana to join him.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Blood Moon[]

En route to the Shadow Dojo, Ras and Jordana navigated through the Forest of Spirits. Jordana was surprised at the vastness of Wyldness, a stark contrast to what the Imperians had been taught, where the focus was solely on the Lava Wastes. Ras took the opportunity to educate Jordana about the various landmarks of the Wyldness, highlighting the ignorance and self-centeredness of the Imperians who believed they were shielded from problems beyond Imperium. The unity of the realms had shattered this belief, forcing everyone to confront the realities beyond their own realm.

Upon arriving at the Shadow Dojo, Ras used the Source Dragon energy extracted by Jordana to unlock the entrance, revealing his determination despite the fall of the Kingdom of Imperium. He spoke of his grand plan, emphasizing the significance of unlocking the long-hidden secrets within the Shadow Dojo to achieve what had been deemed impossible for centuries. Once inside, Ras introduced Jordana to the Gong of Shattering and hinted at the ominous Ritual of the Blood Moon, suggesting that with the return of the Blood Moon, they would have the power to bring back the Forbidden Five, a prospect that would render any opposition futile.

After a while, Ras and Jordana delved into Theroxian scrolls, with Jordana struggling to decipher the intricate language. Curious about how they could possibly uncover the hidden secrets within the scrolls without understanding the language, Ras reassured Jordana that patience was key, a lesson instilled in him by his master who believed that true power required time. Just then, Cinder made an unexpected arrival. Ras wasted no time in introducing Cinder as one of the new members of the Wolf Clan, entrusted with the important task of retrieving the Mallet of Shattering for him.

Upon receiving the Mallet of Shattering, Cinder was awaited by Ras and the rest of the reassembled Wolf Clan. Ras proclaimed that their ominous journey could commence with the acquisition of the artifact. With the mallet at hand, Ras then addressed the Wolf Mask Warriors, urging them to brace themselves for an unprecedented surge of power. Ras struck the Gong of Shattering with the mallet, infusing the Wolf Mask Warriors with newfound Chaos.

Shattered Dreams[]

Ras had a plan to infiltrate the Cloud Kingdom in order to obtain the necessary information for the Ritual of the Blood Moon and to translate the Theroxian texts. Upon their arrival, the Wolf Mask Warriors managed to overpower the Writers of Destiny, while Ras chose not to use the Gong of Shattering due to the disastrous consequences. Euphrasia arrived and engaged in a fight with Cinder, eventually knocking Euphrasia out of the Cloud Kingdom.

Accompanied by Jordana, Ras proceeded to the Cloud Kingdom archives. Suetonius vowed to protect the library with his life. Ras found this amusing and declared that he needed the Writers of Destiny to remain alive, at least for now. Ras then revealed that he was the one responsible for cursing the Scroll-Worms, who had been instructed to attack. The Scroll-Worms captured Suetonius and Marcus, sealing them in cocoons and leaving them trapped.

Upon learning of the Wolf Clan's attack on the Cloud Kingdom and their intention to summon the Forbidden Five, the ninja swiftly traveled to the Cloud Kingdom. Engaging in combat, they successfully defeated a number of the Wolf Mask Warriors, while Ras and Jordana watched the battle unfold from the rooftop of the main building. Ras used the Mallet of Shattering to sound the Gong of Shattering, granting additional power to a select few wolf warriors who became Wolf Mask Claw Warriors. The fight between the ninja and the Wolf Mask Claw Warriors ended in a stalemate.

When Lloyd made the announcement about putting an end to Ras' control over the Cloud Kingdom, Ras strongly disagreed with him. In an attempt to prove his power, Ras called upon Cinder for assistance, but unfortunately, Kai easily defeated him. Despite this setback, Ras taunted Lloyd by claiming that he had already started unraveling the mysteries of the Forbidden Five. As an illustration of his progress, Ras struck the Gong of Shattering while Cinder adorned a Wolf Mask, enabling him to perform Shatterspin. Ras continued to strike the Gong of Shattering while Cinder successfully took down the ninja. Encouraged by their success, Ras commanded Cinder to deliver the final blow to the ninja. Faced with no other choice, the ninja were compelled to retreat. With their retreat, Ras triumphantly declared that the Cloud Kingdom and all its hidden secrets now belonged to him. He proclaimed that the Forbidden Five would once again rise to power.

Beyond the Phantasm Cave[]

Ras decided to demonstrate Cinder's skill in Shatterspin by having him destroy vases while Ras sounded the Gong of Shattering. Impressed by Cinder's accuracy, Ras praised him for his performance. Cinder, feeling empowered by the experience, commented that he could feel the power of the Gong of Shattering resonating within him. However, Ras didn't seem amused by Cinder's remark and left the scene.

Later on, Ras was engrossed in examining a scroll when Jordana approached him. She hinted that she had found the final component for the Blood Moon Ritual, a spell located in the Land of Lost Things. Ras was impressed by Jordana's growing skills in Theroxian magic and commended her for her discovery. Excited by the find, Ras immediately announced that they would depart for the valley without delay.

Accompanied by Jordana and Cinder, Ras led the way to the room where the Cloud Kingdom's motor was located. Upon reaching the motor room, Ras noticed that the Writers of Destiny had never used it for hundreds of years. Displeased by this oversight, Ras criticized the monks, considering them fools for neglecting such a valuable resource. Taking charge, Ras ordered the captive Writers of Destiny to activate the engine, causing the main building of the Cloud Kingdom to drift away, heading towards the Land of Lost Things.

Force From the East[]

Jordana received a calming suggestion from Ras when she was in the midst of explaining the events unfolding in the Land of Lost Things. In response to Jordana's mention of Bonzle being the spell to liberate the Forbidden Five, Ras acknowledged his awareness of this fact. Despite the potential disastrous consequences of excessive usage, Ras instructed the Wolf Mask Warriors to prepare the Gong of Shattering by having two Writers of Destiny strike it. He emphasized to Jordana that the desired outcome justified any possible sacrifice. Ras attentively observed as the Wolf Mask Warriors compelled two Cloud Monks to repeatedly strike the gong with the mallet, thereby empowering the wolf warriors in the Land of Lost Things to transform into Wolf Mask Claw Warriors. When Cole and the Finders managed to escape from the valley, Jordana felt frustrated, but Ras assured her that they would persistently pursue them wherever they may go.

Ras conveyed to his master about the commencement of the Ritual of the Blood Moon. Ras then instructed Cinder to swiftly gather the Wolf Clan at the Shadow Dojo, ensuring that the Gong of Shattering was properly positioned. Subsequently, a Writer of Destiny sorrowfully revealed that the final remnants of the Theroxian text had been deciphered. Curiously, Cinder inquired about the fate of the Writers of Destiny, prompting Ras to emphasize that they too were included in his directive, as they were no longer required. Ras asserted that they had become the masters of their own destinies.

Upon reaching the Shadow Dojo, Ras instructed the Wolf Clan to make preparations for the Ritual of the Blood Moon. However, his patience wore thin when Jordana arrived late, expecting her to have already been present. To his dismay, Jordana arrived without Bonzle, which only served to further frustrate Ras. He demanded to know the whereabouts of Bonzle, and Jordana revealed that Ari had actually saved her, a revelation that only fueled Ras' anger. He articulated that the master's patience and precise planning were soon going to yield results, exposing the immense power of the Merged Realms for Ras and his master to wield.

Rising Ninja[]

Ras gave instructions to a Wolf Mask Warrior to strike the Gong of Shattering, which would initiate the ritual. With a nod from Ras, the warrior struck the gong, causing the other warriors to transform into Wolf Mask Claw Warriors. Jordana reminded Ras about the importance of pronouncing the words perfectly, but Ras confidently assured her that he had it under control. He then asked Jordana if she was ready to reverse the spell and summon the Forbidden Five. Despite Bonzle's objections that she was more than just a spell, Ras silenced her. Ras began reading the scroll in Theroxian. However, Ras noticed that the ninja had infiltrated the Shadow Dojo. Ras quickly commanded the Wolf Mask Claw Warriors and Cinder to stop the ninja's interference, allowing him to continue reading undisturbed. As the portal opened, Ras demanded that the sacrifices be brought in. However, Ras soon realized that the Writers of Destiny had managed to escape during the battle, causing him to order his warriors to retrieve them immediately.

As a Wolf Mask Claw Warrior descended towards Ras, he evaded the fall by stepping aside, swiftly redirecting his attention to Cinder. Ras commanded Cinder to use Shatterspin to defeat the ninja, but to his surprise, Kai unleashed the Rising Dragon technique, surpassing Cinder's abilities. Ras couldn't believe it was possible. Suddenly, Lloyd launched an attack on Ras, but Ras evaded the energy bolts and engaged in a fierce battle with Lloyd, despite his use of Energy and Spinjitzu. With a strong strike, Ras disrupted Lloyd's Spinjitzu, causing him to stumble. Taking advantage of the situation, Ras taunted Lloyd, claiming that the ritual was almost complete. However, Lloyd revealed that Cinder had found his match and the Blood Moon was about to pass.

The Writers of Destiny had also managed to escape, preventing themselves from being sacrificed. Lloyd confidently declared that Ras had lost. Amused by Lloyd's words, Ras explained that he only needed an unshattered soul to be a sacrifice and he knew exactly who to target. With a swift motion, Ras threw his hammer at Kai, sending him through the gateway and freeing Nokt from his exile. Ras then revealed to the wolf that he was the one responsible for his release and shared his plan to release the other four beings by sacrificing the ninja.

As Bonzle retaliated, Jordana struggled to maintain the open portal. Ras angrily commanded Jordana to keep the gateway accessible. Ras declared that he would personally sacrifice Nya first by placing her into the gate. However, before he could do so, Ras was struck by a hand from the Elemental Fire Mech, courtesy of Arin and Sora. Once Ras regained his composure, he discovered that the portal had closed and the ninja were gaining the advantage. Frustrated, Ras called upon his master for assistance. Suddenly, Ras, Nokt, Jordana, Cinder, the wolf warriors, and the Shadow Dojo were transported to an unfamiliar field.

Nokt seethed with anger towards Ras for abandoning the mission before he could release the remaining members of the Forbidden Five. Ras boldly proclaimed that Nokt's newfound freedom was solely thanks to him, and he adamantly refused to tolerate being addressed in such a disrespectful manner. Ras affixed a gadget to Nokt's head and triggered it using a remote, shocking him. Ras stressed the importance of loyalty from Nokt and mentioned that the gadget would guarantee it. Turning his attention to Jordana, Ras berated her for failing to maintain the open portal. However, to his surprise, Jordana seemed to be completely disregarding him.

Ras received an urgent summons from his master, interrupting the escalating confrontation. Ras proceeded to explain to his master that the Ritual of the Blood Moon was merely a single step in their grand plan, far from being the sole objective. Determined not to disappoint his master, Ras vowed to triumph in the Tournament of the Sources and assert dominance over the Source Dragons. He assured his master that once he achieved this feat, all the power they desired would be within their grasp.

Truth and Lies[]

Ras explained to Arin that Wu, being the architect of the Merge, bore the responsibility not only for the disappearance of Arin's parents but also for the myriad consequences that the Merge had wrought. Arin was skeptical regarding this assertion, given Wu's wisdom. Ras contended that Arin's youth limited his understanding, highlighting the irony in Arin's perception of him as a villain while viewing the ninja as virtuous. Ras elaborated on the complexity of morality, suggesting that good could exist within evil and vice versa. When Arin asked if Ras' plan was to reverse the Merge, Ras refrained from disclosing his true intentions, but emphasized that reversing the Merge was the sole means to save the Merged Realms from their self-destructive path. He asserted that this action could potentially restore all those who have been lost, including Arin's parents. Despite Ras' assurances, Arin remained unconvinced. Ras insisted that the timing of Arin's belief was inconsequential, confident that a day would arrive when Arin recognized the validity of his claims. He hoped that this realization occurred before it was too late. As Ras permitted Arin and Riyu to depart, he chuckled, fully aware that his shared revelations would sow discord among the ninja.

Ras observed the match between Zeatrix and Euphrasia. Following Zeatrix's victory, she provocatively mocked Ras, suggesting that defeating Euphrasia was merely a prelude to her plans for him, which incited anger within Ras. Subsequently, after Nokt's bout against Tox, Ras summoned Frak to the Games Plaza, where Frak was set to engage in a match against Cole that had been secretly manipulated to ensure Frak's triumph.

As time passed, Jordana who was being possessed by Rox of the Forbidden Five, approached Ras with a question regarding the wisdom of his decision to disclose the truth to Arin, emphasizing the notion that information can serve as a powerful tool, to which Ras countered that information could also act as a poison. After he elaborated on his rationale for revealing the truth to Arin, Ras articulated the overarching objective of winning the tournament, thereby enabling the summoning of the Source Dragons once they create the Dragon Icons. He underscored the imperative nature of securing victory in the tournament by any means necessary.

In the course of Cinder's confrontation with Sora, Arin approached Ras to disclose that Ras' earlier assertions regarding Sora using of her Elemental Powers during the Blood Moon ritual were right, prompting Ras to assert that he possessed insight on numerous matters. Arin further revealed that Jay held information concerning Arin's parents from his tenure within the Administration, to which Ras responded by indicating that he was privy to all the same details that Jay possessed. Subsequently, Ras used the Gong and Mallet of Shattering to enhance Cinder with Shatterspin in an effort to secure victory over Sora. Despite these efforts, Sora emerged victorious in the round.

At night, Ras saw Arin training to perform a proper Spinjitzu tornado, smirking as it was only because of his advice through Frak, knowing he was slowly getting Arin to his side.

The Sword Shatters[]

At the Monastery of the Gates, Ras told the captured Wu how the ninja are lost in the furthest parts of the Merged Lands, and declared that nothing would stand in his and his master's way.

Dragons Rising, Season 4

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

Prior to Birthright[]

Ras obtained another Source Dragon Icon in the form of a bow through unknown means.

Birthright[]

Ras used a portal gate to travel to the Land of Eternal Dawn. There he returned to the Shadow Dojo and informed his master of the Source Dragon Icon he had created.

Prior to A Frozen Scar[]

Ras learned about the ninja's plan to destroy the Weapon of Desolation. Because it was created from the essence of his master, its destruction would severely harm them, jeopardizing the plan to reverse the Merge.

A Frozen Scar[]

Ras used a portal gate to reach the Monastery of the Never Kingdoms, where he encountered and revealed himself to Zilvar. The tiger recognized Ras as a member of the Tiger Tribe but was confused about his identity. As they walked together, Ras revealed he was a cub named Zeras, which shocked Zilvar. Ras expressed his sorrow over the tribe's belief that Zilvar had died in his journey, but his feelings turned bitter when he suggested that Zilvar might have abandoned them instead. Zilvar denied this, claiming he would have searched for Ras if he had known he was alive. Ras urged Zilvar to redeem himself by preventing the Destruction of the Weapon of Desolation.[note 1]

When Zilvar learned that Ras' master created the weapon, he questioned their identity, but Ras dismissed his concerns, emphasizing that his master's plan was almost ready and that the ninja must not be allowed to interfere. Although Zilvar would agree to help a fellow Tiger Tribe member, he opposed the idea of keeping the weapon, fearing it could destroy the Merged Lands. Ras brushed off Zilvar's worries as shortsighted and shared his ultimate goal of capturing the Source Dragons to harness their energy, intending to reverse the Merge and restore their tribe. Zilvar firmly rejected this plan. Ras urged Zilvar to think of their tribe, especially Mei. Zilvar claimed that Mei had died with a pure heart and that helping Ras and his master would be a betrayal to their people. He addressed Ras by his birth name, disappointed and suggesting that Ras should feel ashamed of his actions. Angered, Ras shot back at Zilvar, asserting that he had no right to insult him. He emphasized his dedication to the tribe, accusing Zilvar of failing to protect them. Wielding the Icon of the Source Dragon of Strength, Ras threatened to destroy Zilvar for what he saw as betrayal, planning to deal with the ninja afterward. Just as Ras was about to attack, Zilvar whistled for Grimtak, who charged in and head-butted Ras, momentarily distracting him as the two set off to find the ninja.[note 1]

Prior to Dangerous Connections[]

After leaving the Never Kingdoms with the realization that the Weapon would corrupt Zilvar and not let him destroy it, Ras reobtained the Icon of the Source Dragon of Energy through unknown means.

Dangerous Connections[]

Ras later learned that the ninja managed to dispose of the Weapon of Desolation, which resulted in Lloyd's apparent demise. Upon returning to the Land of Eternal Dawn, Ras mocked Wu, claiming that while Wu might believe the ninja had won, it was merely a minor skirmish. With the icons of five of the seven Source Dragons now in his possession, Ras asserted that true victory was within reach. He proclaimed that his master would prevail, then forcefully brought his Source Dragon Icons together, unleashing a surge of red Source Energy as he growled.
