Overview

This article is about the Security Bots in the City of Temples. For other uses, see Security Bots.
“You are disturbing the peace. Conflict is only allowed during the games.”
— A Security Bot to Lloyd, "They Gather for the Feast"

The Security Bots are androids created by Gerard Gearhead. They served as the security personnel for the City of Temples. Following the recent Tournament of the Sources, they were reprogrammed to function as tour guides for the City of Temples.

History

History[]

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

Enter the City of Temples[]

After being rejected by Bleckt, the ninja decided to sneak into the City of Temples, only to be caught by the Security Bots. However, not backing down, the ninja continued on before they eventually reached the top. As more bots confronted them, Roby ordered them to stand down. Meanwhile, several more arrested Arin and Riyu.

They Gather for the Feast[]

Before the Security Bots could detain Arin and Riyu, the former’s old friend and Master of Quake, Frak, eased the situation by inviting them on behalf of his master.

The Security Bots held Wyldfyre back before she was able to attack Cinder at the welcome feast.

Later that night, they responded to a disturbance and discovered Lloyd had taken down an assassin that was a bot before Zur and Ras showed they were attacked themselves. With the bots' weapons identified as a Gilba Thorn from the Mother Garden, the Security Bots escorted Zant-Tanz away from the city because of his disqualification.

Inside the Maze[]

Multiple bots were on Foundation Street when Nya saw Jay in the crowd. The following day, multiple security bots patrolled Foundation Street before the games began.

United We Fall[]

Cinder shot a blast of Heat in the air and alerted the bots. Cinder fell to the floor and acted distressed, knowing the bots were watching him. Believing the ninja had attacked Cinder, Bleckt ordered the security bots to escort the ninja out of the city.

Truth and Lies[]

The security bots held Nokt at gunpoint as he attacked Tox away in one of the Tournament’s rounds.

Later, two bots flew over the Games Plaza. The bots watched Roby transfer Nya's elemental powers to Nokt.

The Sword Shatters[]

Some bots fixed the floating platform that broke mid-way through Lloyd and Zeatrix's battle. After Zeatrix struck down Lloyd after her loss, some Security Bots detained her.

Clues and Suspects[]

Several Security Bots brought Zeatrix into the ninja's room to prepare the extraction of elemental powers. Zeatrix blasted the bots away with a shockwave, but Roby managed to extract her powers. The bots grabbed hold of Zeatrix and escorted her out.

A few moments afterwards, Arin and Wyldfyre hid from two security bots that were flying around Roby and Bleckt's office.

Later, Cole snuck by a bot watering a plant in order to steal the Elemental Earth Mech.

The Final Game[]

Roby called the Security Bots to arrest Bleckt for rigging the tournament and conspiring with Ras. The bots followed Roby's orders. The bots carried Bleckt away from Nokt. More came to help Roby when Nokt attacked the former.

Elements of Betrayal[]

The Security Bots, along with Ras and the Elemental Masters, fought off the wolf warriors and Cinder at the Games Plaza.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

I Alone Can Save Them[]

The Security Bots help Roby remodel the inside of the Museum. Although they didn't comments on his "genius word play." Kur of the Forbidden Five and several Dragonian Warriors broke into the museum to get the Yellow Prismatic Blade. The Security Bots fought the Dragonians but were outmatch.

The Dragonian Dilemma (non-canon)[]

At some point, Fedulian purchased a Security Bot to use to guard the All-Realm Antiques Emporium. While on the ninja's search for the Returning Blade, they mistakenly followed the Blue Prismatic Blade's signal to the antique shop, after the bot denied the ninja's request to enter, due to it not being opening hours, it was blown up by Zane's missiles. After Fedulian lamented the android's destruction, Sora offered to build him a new one.

The Shatter Dragon[]

A Security Bot approach Roby, he he saw an chaotic storm from the City of Temples. He warned Roby to get indoor to shelter from the natural disaster, but Roby refused and said the weather is anything but natural.

The Vault of Focus[]

After the Spectral Dragonians attack the City of Temples, the Security Bots were the only ones left due to not having a soul. Lloyd attempt to get them to explain the Battle of the City of Temples, but the Security Bots followed their protocol and only answer to Games Master Roby. Later, several Security Bots encountered the ninja when Wyldfyre led them to Bleckt's prison cell. The halt the ninja because Bleckt can't have unauthorized visitors, and continue to deny them entry despite the emergency. Jay attempts to zap the bots with Lightning, but Sora didn't tell him that the Security Bots were immune to Elemental Powers and Jay attempt to attack them thus the bots began to attack the ninja. When Wyldfyre told them to stand down, the bots obeyed revealing Roby had them answer to her in case he was incapacitated.

After Sora and Arin arrived from the junkyard with the Jet Mech and Spinjitzu Battle Mech coated in Prismatic Ore, Wyldfyre ordered the Security Bots to take Bleckt back to his cell.

Abilities and weapons

Abilities and weapons[]

Abilities[]

  • Flight: The Security Bots are capable of flight.
  • Elemental Power Immunity: The Security Bots are built to be immune to Elemental Powers. They were unaffected by Sora's Technology and Jay's Lightning blast.
  • Combat: The Security Bots are skilled in combat being able to take on wolf warriors and intruders.

Weapons[]

  • Blasters: The Security Bots primary use blasters that fire lasers.
