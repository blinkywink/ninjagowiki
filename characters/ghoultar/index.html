Overview

“Ghoultar never talk. Oops, Ghoultar talked. Uh, Ghoultar no talk from now on.”
— Ghoultar to Nya, Wu, Ronin and Misako, "Kingdom Come"

Scythe Master Ghoultar was one of the Ghost Warriors. He was at one point banished to the Cursed Realm. After Morro escaped from the Cursed Realm, he conjured Ghoultar, Soul Archer, and Bansha using the Allied Armor, as Morro sought to locate the Tomb of the First Spinjitzu Master. The ghosts went to Nya's Samurai X Cave, where Ghoultar corrupted the Samurai Mech for Morro.

Ghoultar helped Morro in his quest, traveling up the Wailing Alps and into the Caves of Despair in a series of attempts to stop the ninja. Eventually, Morro claimed the Realm Crystal and traveled to Stiix, where he opened the Cursed Realm and released its prisoners and the Preeminent. Ghoultar fought against the ninja in the ghost invasion, eventually joining the Preeminent as she walked out into the Endless Sea. When Nya unlocked her True Potential, she created a massive tidal wave that drowned The Preeminent and destroyed the Cursed Realm, ultimately killing Ghoultar and the other ghosts.

History

History[]

At an unknown point in time, Ghoultar was banished to the Cursed Realm.

The Temple on Haunted Hill[]

Morro freed Ghoultar from his banishment to the Cursed Realm along with Bansha to aid him in deciphering the second clue to the location of the First Spinjitzu Master's tomb. Ghoultar admired his Scythe while the others talked, though when they brought up potentially taking things, he joined in the conversation.

The three ghosts accompanied Morro to the Samurai X Cave in order to claim the mechs stored there. Before they could do so, however, Nya ambushed them in the Samurai Mech, though the ghosts gained the upper hand as she couldn't physically hit them. She eventually ejected from the mech, leaving Ghoultar to possess it and used the mech against Nya and Ronin. Chasing her and Ronin outside, they cornered them as Morro stood in the R.E.X. Just as they were about to finish them off, it miraculously started to rain, and the ghosts flew away, taking the Samurai Mech, now possessed, with them.

Peak-a-Boo[]

Sometime later, Morro and the Ghost Masters made their way up the Wailing Alps in order to reach the Blind Man's Eye and get the Sword of Sanctuary. As they were moving close to the hanging temple, Morro sensed the ninja coming, and, despite Bansha's efforts, they managed to get back up and cover more ground.

It didn't take long for Ghoultar to notice the ninja and pull the Ice Mech up in order to begin the battle. As the ninja and the other ghosts fought, everyone was interrupted by the incoming wind. Shortly after, the ninja escaped, and Zane left the Ice Mech and Ghoultar to go plummeting and rolling down the mountain. Despite escaping the mess, Ronin trapped Ghoultar in a cage, much to the Scythe Master's surprise.

Kingdom Come[]

Ghoultar was captured and imprisoned behind deepstone bars in Destiny's Bounty where he was questioned by Wu, Nya, and Ronin, though he refused to say anything. After they left the ghost alone with Ronin, Ghoultar suggested he make good of the bounty hunter's deal he made of Soul Archer, though was quickly silenced.

The other three eventually returned and tried to make Ghoultar talk with puffy potstickers, though were interrupted when the ninja returned from the Cloud Kingdom in pursuit of Morro. In the rush, they left the prison keys close to the prison, and Ghoultar used them to escape. After devouring the puffy potstickers left behind, Ghoultar made his way to the deck and attacked Nya. He was quickly knocked off the Bounty and landed on Morro's skiff but due to all the food he ate, Ghoultar unintentionally slowed the ship down because he weighed so much. When Kai attacked the skiff, Ghoultar fell off and landed into the snow.

The Crooked Path[]

Ghoultar relocated to Stiix with Morro, Bansha, and Soul Archer where they ordered Ronin to steal the sword in exchange for lifting his curse. When the mercenary arrived with the sword, Ghoultar checked to make sure it was real before presenting it to Morro. As his leader went around their deal in order to lure the ninja into a deadly trap, he was instrumental as Morro possessed their aid while he filmed the transmission. Then was sent to the Caves of Despair where he created a trap for the ninja. Creating a host of false leads to the remains of Morro's mortal body before trapping them within the caves. Unfortunately, they were able to escape and Jay sent the ghost back to the Cursed Realm using his Aeroblade.

Curseworld, Part I[]

When Morro used the Realm Crystal to open the gateway, Ghoultar was able to return to Ninjago to help the ghosts prepare for the Preeminent's arrival. Ghoultar was with Morro and Wrayth above Ronin's pawn shop when Soul Archer and Wrayth assured him of their victory.

Later on, he attacked Cole on Wrayth's Chain Cycle and proceeded to trick the Ghost Ninja by leaving Wrayth's flail as a trap, causing Cole's bike to crash. This prevented Cole from helping Zane who was being attacked by Bansha.

Shortly afterward, he was sucked into the Ghost Taker GT but used his scythe to keep himself from being fully sucked in and freed himself and the other ghosts.

Curseworld, Part II[]

After the Preeminent entered Ninjago, Ghoultar flew around Stiix, laughing triumphantly, and later helped attach parts of Stiix to his master. He later help the Preeminent by lifting her tentacles to throw a house at the paddle steamer in her new armor to destroy the engine. He was presumably killed following the Preeminent's death.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Because of his status as a ghost, Ghoultar has translucent green skin and a skull head. He wears a dark purple rice hat, a bandana covering his mouth, and shoulder pads. He usually has a ghost tail, but sometimes has legs too.

Personality

Personality[]

Ghoultar speaks in the third person. He also has basic vocabulary. He is shown to have a liking for Puffy Pot Stickers scarfing down offering to give away Morro's plans when bribed with them and scarfing down several before making his escape slowing down his colleges in the process. He has no qualms about killing having trapped the Ninja in The Caves of Despair with Morro's deceased remains, on Morro's orders apparently counting on this to kill them.

Abilities and Weapons

Abilities and Weapons[]

Abilities[]

  • Spectral Magic: Because he was a ghost, Ghoultar could transform Nya's Samurai Mech into Mech-enstein.
  • Intangibility: As a ghost, he could pass through solid objects.
  • Possession: Ghoultar was capable of possessing vehicles, like the Samurai Mech.
  • Combat: Ghoultar could go toe-to-toe with the Ninja, battling them in close-range at the Wailing Alps, either in the Mech-enstein or by himself as he did with Nya. He also managed to trick Cole using Wrayth's flail and Chain Cycle.

Weapons[]

  • Scythe: Ghoultar wielded a scythe and is proficient in using during combat.
Weaknesses

Weaknesses[]

  • Water: Like all other ghosts, Ghoultar is weak against water. If he comes into contact with large amounts of it, he'll be disintegrated.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He appears to have low intelligence, as shown in his dialect.
    • He also talks in third person, making him the second character to speak in third person, the first being Karlof.
      • It is possible Ghoultar is from Metalonia like Karlof and Korgran.[1]
  • He is the second Ghost Master to be destroyed on-screen in the series, the first being Wrayth.
  • He is the only main Ghost Warrior with a skull head (his head's front and back printing indicate that his head is a ghostly skull).
  • According to the Hageman's outline of "The Temple on Haunted Hill," Ghoultar was originally named "Brute."[citation needed]
  • His face is similar to the Cemetery Warrior from the LEGO Indiana Jones line, but in trans-light green.
  • He is the only Ghost Master that doesn't appear with legs in any set.
    • However, he does have legs in the show.
    • Ghoultar's leg design in the show is identical to that of Soul Archer's.
  • He seems to be the ghost equivalent of Cole, as they both wield scythes, are the strongest members of their group, and love food. This may have been intentional as a result of Morro's attempts to emulate Lloyd, the true Green Ninja.
  • His name is a pun on "ghoul."
  • In "Kingdom Come," he is seen dancing to a remixed version of the Ghostbusters theme.
