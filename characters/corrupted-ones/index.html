Corrupted Ones are people and animals turned evil under an effect of Dark Matter.

History

History[]

Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

Return of the Overlord[]

At the beginning of The Final Battle, Garmadon and the Stone Army managed to capture Nya. Desiring to test out the effects of the Dark Matter firsthand, Garmadon used it to corrupt Nya before sending her off to delay the Ninja from following his forces to the beach.

Corrupted by the effects of Dark Matter, Nya faithfully carried out her duties in service to the Dark Lord, ambushing the ninja and fighting against them until she was encased in ice by Zane.

Elsewhere, Garmadon unleashed Garmatron's ultimate weapon to rain dark matter upon several cities in Ninjago, corrupting their populace and shifting the balance to evil.

Rise of the Spinjitzu Master[]

Entering Ninjago City virtually unopposed, the Overlord quickly overran and corrupted the city's entire populace, save for Dareth. Anticipating the arrival of the ninja, Nya manned one Garmatron's turrets as part of the Overlord's defenses.

As the ninja assisted Lloyd in ascending Garmatron, which had since transformed into a massive tower, Jay and Cole were corrupted by the Overlord's breath, leaving Zane to hold them off. Kai too would be forced to stay behind to hold back Nya while Lloyd made the rest of the way to the top of the tower on his own.

Following the Overlord's defeat, all that had been corrupted by the Overlord were restored to normal.

Dark Matter Comics (non-canon)[]

Following his second defeat, the Overlord manifested once more in the sewers of Ninjago City. After possessing Rufus McCallister, the Overlord devised a new way to corrupt all of Ninjago. Summoning his Stone Army, the Overlord infected copies of the latest issue of Starfarer with dark matter before distributing it to unassuming customers. When the customers reached the last pages of the tainted comics, they were corrupted by the dark matter soaked pages.

The presence of corrupted citizens alerted Dareth, who immediately contacted the ninja for help. After defeating the Overlord once more, the Dark Lord fled McCallister's body and the corrupted citizens were restored to normal.
