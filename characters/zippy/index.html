Zippy is a Jungle Dragon that lives on the Island of the Keepers.

History

History[]

The Island[]

The Keepers of the Amulet[]

Lloyd first senses Zippy's presence before he jumps down with a coconut. Before Kai, Jay and Cole are able to attack him, Nya realizes the dragon does not appear to be harmful and plays with him. Jay names him Zippy and Zane states he is docile. When Lloyd expresses his concerns, Nya reluctantly throws a coconut across a distance to keep the dragon preoccupied as they leave. Later on, when Cole is about to drink a coconut, Zippy takes it and keeps it in his mouth. He then cowers in fear at the Stone Guardians.

The Gift of Jay[]

Zippy appeared behind Lloyd, wanting to play. After a few attempts to get the dragon to leave, Lloyd realized he could use Zippy's playfulness to his advantage, throwing coconuts at the Keepers so that Zippy would scare them off. Later, Zippy was being chased by or was chasing some Keepers.

The Tooth of Wojira[]

Zippy appeared and scared the Kryptarium inmates and Ronin.

Seabound[]

The Storm Amulet[]

Zippy was present at the Keepers' Village when Nya and the others were convincing Mammatus to give them the Storm Amulet.

Crystalized[]

Dragon Form[]

During the final battle, Ziggy aided the Resistance with the Merlopians and Keepers, letting Benthomaar ride on his back while fighting the Vengestone Guards.

Roots[]

After the defeat of the Crystal King and his forces, Zippy celebrated in the streets of Ninjago City alongside all others who had fought against the Crystal army.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Zippy is a dragon with teal scales, gray curving horns, a white underbelly, two tusk-like teeth protruding from both side of his mouth, gray and spotted feet and gold spikes running down from his head to tail. His tongue generally is out of his mouth and he looks happy or playful most of the time. He has bulging eyes, gold markings on his forehead, thin, plant-like wings, and a few yellow-orange leaves running down his neck in between his horns and ending at his wings.

Powers and abilities

Powers and abilities[]

He is shown to be capable of jumping far distances despite his size, and seems to possess remarkable speed.

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • He was originally called the "Tiki Dragon" and served as the dragon of the Keeper shaman. The concept art can be found below within the Gallery section.
  • Zippy's movement seems to be inspired by that of Scooby-Doo from the franchise of the same name. This is likely because the overall story structure of The Island is inspired by the Scooby-Doo franchise.[5]
    • Zippy's set designer, Jme Wheeler, had in mind a "big hyper dog" for the personality of Zippy.[6]
  • He is called the Jungle Dragon in the sets because the sets and the show are developed at different times and it tends to cause some inconsistency at times.[7]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He appears to be attracted to coconuts.
  • In the sets, Zippy has golden blades but he does not have them in the show. The golden blades appear to have been replaced by thin, plant-like wings.
  • His face resembles a chameleon's as he has bulging eyes.
  • Jay previously referred to Griffin Turner as "Zippy" in "Spellbound," making it appear that "Zippy" is a go-to nickname of his, coincidentally, he also nicknamed both Griffin Turner and Zippy for their speed.
  • Despite the Character Encyclopedia New Edition referring to Zippy as Nya's dragon, the descriptions of 71746 Jungle Dragon and "Lloyd & the Jungle Dragon" considered Zippy as Lloyd's dragon instead. However, Lloyd was the only ninja who wasn't interested in Zippy until "The Gift of Jay."
  • His tongue is five times longer as Master Wu's beard.[1]
  • Several years before he was introduced, a Nexo Power named "Jungle Dragon" was created for another LEGO series, Nexo Knights.
