Overview

“Samurai stuff, huh? I knew I should've got some armor. And this Red Dragon fella, he's got the key thing, right?”
— The Newbie Gamer to Okino, "I am Okino"

The Newbie Gamer[1] is one of the former players that was once trapped in Prime Empire.

History

History[]

NOTE: Most of the Newbie Gamers were used multiple times in group shots. As such, any notable appearances of a Newbie Gamer will be categorized here for convenience.

Prime Empire Original Shorts[]

The Meaning of Victory[]

He looks up as the announcer claims that the Speedway Five-Billion will start in three days.

The Stowaway[]

He is one of the players walking through the crowd at Terra Technica while Jay tries to avoid Red Visors.

Manhunt[]

Newbie Gamer watched Jay fight the Red Visors.

Prime Empire[]

Superstar Rockin' Jay[]

He walked by as Nya reminds Cole of their mission to find Jay.

I am Okino[]

Okino tries explaining the three deadly challenges to the Newbie Gamer. However, the Newbie Gamer ignores him and ends up losing all four of his lives.

One Step Forward, Two Steps Back[]

Newbie Gamer was seen cheering at the dance competition.

Racer Seven[]

Newbie Gamer was leaving the Speedway Five-Billion by the time Lloyd arrived.

The Speedway Five-Billion[]

Newbie Gamer was seen in the stands during the Speedway Five-Billion.

The Temple of Madness[]

Newbie Gamer heard the shockwave from the Temple of Madness.

Game Over[]

He is in the group of players and NPCs trapped in Prime Empire to come out of the Mechanic's warehouse.
