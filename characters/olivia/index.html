Olivia,[1] also known as General #1, is the former commander of Lord Garmadon's Shark Army and leader of the Burnt Generals.

History

History[]

The LEGO Ninjago Movie[]

After Garmadon's latest defeat to the Secret Ninja Force, Olivia and the rest of the generals were summoned to a meeting to discuss potential ideas for finally conquering Ninjago. During the meeting, Olivia kept quiet and notably kept her head down after witnessing many of her colleagues get fired for suggesting bad ideas.

The next day, Olivia served as part of Garmadon's guard of generals and was summoned alongside them to the top of Ninjago Tower to restrain Lloyd. Sometime after this victory, she was promoted to General #1 after her predecessor, Omar, was fired (likely for failing to capture Wu). As the newest General #1, Olivia was assigned a new mission: to locate the Secret Ninja Force, Wu, and Koko. However, she instead spent the whole day celebrating the victory with the other generals, leading to her firing out of Garmadon's Volcano Lair.

After this, Olivia found the Burnt Generals and became their leader. When the ninja and Garmadon arrived, she imprisoned Lloyd and Garmadon and planned to throw them into a volcano for revenge. However, the ninja saved them and they built a makeshift helicopter to escape, but General #1 attacked Lloyd, causing him to fall and lose his arm. She then attempted to finish him off, but before she could, Garmadon drives the ninja's vehicle right into her, dragging her offscreen. As she's never seen again after this, her ultimate fate is unknown. But it can be inferred she either died from getting hit by the vehicle, or was incapacitated when it hit her and dragged her through the dirt.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Olivia has black hair brushed to the right side, and red lipstick. She wears the standard Shark Army uniform, but unique to her version of it is a scale-like pattern on the pants, and a cape, collar, and skirt that look like fish fins. On her chest is a big gold star with the number 1 on it and a red ribbon sticking out the bottom. She's usually seen drinking from a slushy cup that's colored different shades of pink and has a pink straw sticking out the top.

After she's fired and becomes the leader of the Fired Generals, parts of her hair have burned off, her uniform is scorched in various places, her right glove is completely black, the medals on her chest have melted and are dripping, and her cape, collar, and skirt are tattered. The top of the gold star has blackened, and its red ribbon is now a darker shade of red. She still has the slushy cup from before, but the straw is now black, and the colorful pattern on the cup itself has been rendered monochromatic.

Personality

Personality[]

Olivia had a lax attitude to her job as General #1, choosing to party with her fellow soldiers after Garmadon takes over Ninjago City instead of locating the Secret Ninja Force and Koko. She apparently likes slushies, as she's always seen with one in her hand and drinks from it after each sentence she utters.

After getting fired, like all of the other General #1's who were fired before her, her psyche's damaged to the point where she devolves to a cackling, twitchy monster that wants nothing but to kill Garmadon and Lloyd. She still drinks from her slushy cup when talking, though since it's the same cup she got fired out of Garmadon's volcano with, it's unknown if she refilled it with some other drink, or the cup is empty and she "drinks" out of it as a force of habit.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • She has a unique outfit in the Shark Army in that she doesn't wear any distinctive fish-themed headgear.
  • In the video game, her unfired variant has Zane's fade despite this not being a part of her design.
  • Her burnt variant also appears in the game, as well as in the movie, but not in physical form.
  • She has the same hair as Johnny and Chad, originally used on the Zombie Businessman from Minifigures Series 14.
  • Her original design had an eyepatch.
  • Judging by the number of empty seats during the meeting after Garmadon fired a series of generals, it can be inferred that she was originally General #9 at the start of the film.
