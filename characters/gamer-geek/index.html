Gamer Geek[1] is a gamer who was formerly trapped in Prime Empire.

History

History[]

NOTE: Most of the Gamer Geeks were used multiple times in group shots. As such, any notable appearances of a Gamer Geek will be categorized here for convenience.

Prime Empire Original Shorts[]

The Meaning of Victory[]

She was among the many players in Prime Empire. She watched the Speedway Five-Billion on a TV at a nearby restaurant. Later, she walked around when Racer Seven glanced at Jay.

The Stowaway[]

She was walking around when Jay skidded to a stop in the large plaza. Later, she talked to a Whack Rat when Scott raced away from the Red Visors.

Manhunt[]

When Jay and Scott were walking down the downtown area of Prime Empire, she gasped in surprise. she was walking around when Jay and Scott ran away from the Red Visors. She cheered after Jay defeated a group of Red Visors.

Prime Empire[]

Superstar Rockin' Jay[]

As Lloyd, Nya, Cole, and Kai entered Prime Empire and looked around in awe, she waved at them.

I am Okino[]

She appeared out of the arcade cabinet in Terra Karana and was instantly excited. When Okino tried to tell her what she will face, she interrupted him, saying she had read about the three challenges in a blog and attempted to name the Forest of Discontent and the Cliffs of Hysteria. She was inattentive in the Forest of Discontent and was quickly cubed after being subdued by four traps.

One Step Forward, Two Steps Back[]

As Jay welcomed the ninja to Terra Technica, she walked past them with another player. When Nya and Jay competed together in a dance competition for a chance to win credits, the Red Visors appeared and pushed several players aside, including Gamer Geek.

Racer Seven[]

She was talking to another player while Lloyd was sneaking around the Speedway Five-Billion track to avoid the Red Visors. Later, she was standing and cheering behind Lloyd when he was in the stands watching the race. As Lloyd talked to Racer Seven at the marketplace, Gamer Geek stopped by the noodle shop and sat by them.

The Speedway Five-Billion[]

She was walking in Terra Technica as the announcer reported the start of the Speedway Five-Billion. Before the teams were announced, she was cheering in the crowd. Later, when the race ended and Racer Seven and the Fast Chickens were declared the winners, Gamer Geek lifted Racer Seven in celebration along with the crowd. The Red Visors suddenly arrived on the scene, and as Lloyd, Nya, and Jay ran off, she stood among the crowd.

Stop, Drop and Side Scroll[]

In Terra Technica, Red Visors patrolled up and down the lanes. Gamer Geek talked to a Red Visor and another player for a moment.

The Temple of Madness[]

After Nya's Energy cube was added to Unagami's gateway, a shock wave travelled through Prime Empire. Gamer Geek looked up, startled.

Game Over[]

She, along with the other NPCs and players, were released through the Manifestation Gate.

Seabound[]

The Calm Before the Storm[]

Gamer Geek appeared in Benny's Arcade Emporium.

The Turn of the Tide[]

She stood outside the Monastery of Spinjitzu during Nya's memorial.
