Overview

“Would you look at that? If a foot soldier like Eyezor can move up the chain of command, why can't we? I mean, the guy's only got one good eye, and we each got two. We could spot a ninja twice as fast as he could.”
— Kapau to Chope about Eyezor, "The Forgotten Element"

Kapau was one of Master Chen's Anacondrai Cultists. Unlike most members of Chen's army, Kapau and his best friend Chope tended to get in a little mayhem with their tasks, though they dreamt of rising up the ranks of Chen's cult. In the midst of the Tournament of Elements, their loyalty to Chen grew, as they eventually captured Kai and Skylor and allowed for the transformation spell to be completed.

With Chen's army transformed into Anacondrai, Kapau and Chope set off to Kryptarium Prison to capture Pythor, the last of the Anacondrai, whose sweat could be used to make the transformation permanent. Kapau and Chope fought the ninja at the prison, eventually being aided by their fellow cultists, who captured Pythor.

Kapau joined the cultists as they set off to the Corridor of Elders to battle the Elemental Masters, as he helped carry the Roto Jet with Chen inside. Kapau fought against the Elemental Masters and their allies, with Chen's army slowly overwhelming them. Garmadon made the fateful decision to be banished to the Cursed Realm, which in turn released the spirits of the Anacondrai generals. The generals cursed Chen's army, including Kapau, and sent them to the Cursed Realm.

Some time later, Nya unlocked her True Potential to destroy The Preeminent, the physical manifestation of the Cursed Realm. Consequently, the Cursed Realm was destroyed along with all its inhabitants, including Kapau.

History

History[]

Only One Can Remain[]

On the first night of the Tournament of Elements, Kapau, Chope, and several other Anacondrai Cultists made their way to the Anacondrai Temple to partake in a secret ceremony, but were captured by the ninja who went in their place.

Spy for a Spy[]

On the day of a feast in honor of the final eight competitors, Chope and Kapau stood guard at the Kabuki's entrance to the palace.

Later, while guarding the bridge to the waterfall, Chope and Kapau heard a guitar solo emanating from the waterfall and began dancing to it until they were ordered to investigate by Eyezor. They found the D.B. Express hidden inside with Dareth jamming out. When Dareth attempted to escape in the DB X, Chope and the other guards pursued him, and managed to capture the Brown Ninja.

The Forgotten Element[]

While on duty, he and Chope expressed their jealousy of Eyezor's position as general, and discussed what their names should be, only to be yelled at by Eyezor to be silent. Later, they brought Lloyd to the ceremony, and joined their fellow cultists in chanting on Eyezor's orders. When the Elemental Masters escaped and Kai destroyed the Staff of Elements, the pair escaped alongside Chen, Clouse, Zugu and Eyezor, with an abducted Skylor being brought with them following her being taken captive by them off-screen in the midst of the chaos.

The Day of the Dragon[]

While hiding behind the waterfall, Kapau and Chope were ecstatic at being in Chen's inner circle and guarding Skylor, though she messed with them to get Eyezor to yell at them. They released her under Chen's orders, and were later sent to recapture her after she escaped, which they successfully did, catching her and Kai in a vengestone net.

The two then performed the spell in Clouse's place due to his being sent to the Cursed Realm, and they both were amazed with their new forms and left the island with Chen and the other Cultists.

The Greatest Fear of All[]

Chen became frustrated when Kapau and Chope inform him that the spell was wearing off and a true Anacondrai essence was required for permanence. This was disheartening for Chen as the Anacondrai were almost extinct. Nevertheless, Kapau tried to uplift Chen by suggesting that if any Anacondrai still exist, they can be located.

Later, Kapau helped infiltrate Kryptarium Prison by sneaking in through a laundry van.[1] After fighting the ninja and getting Pythor, they went back to their hideout.

Kapau would later participate in Chen's attack on Jamanakai Village.

The Corridor of Elders[]

Kapau would help Chope, Zugu and Eyezor carry Chen in the Roto jet through the Corridor of Elders. He would join the rest of the army as they then battle the ninja, the Elemental Masters, and the citizens of Ninjago. They nearly got past their enemies after Chen destroyed part of the path, before they were shocked to see the spirits of the Anacondrai generals who were freed after Garmadon sacrificed himself. Kapau, along with Chope, their master and his entire army, was then banished to the Cursed Realm by the spirits, ending their threat.

Curseworld, Part II[]

Sometime after his imprisonment in the Cursed Realm, Kapau eventually transformed from his Anacondrai form back to his normal self, due to his banishment.

As the Ghost Warriors released the Preeminent, chaos ensued in Stiix. Eventually, the Preeminent ventured into the Endless Sea, only to meet her demise when Nya unleashed a colossal tidal wave. This act not only ended the life of the Preeminent but also brought about the destruction of the Cursed Realm, leading to the demise of all its inhabitants, including Kapau, whose soul was sent to the Departed Realm.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Kapau wears a large Anacondrai skull atop his head. He has red markings on his eyes and purple Anacondrai markings on the left side of his face and his forehead. He sports a large, leather, dark red pad on his right chest, which itself is connected to a dark red strap holding two gray knives. Below this, a printing of an Anacondrai Snake is seen on his chest. He wears a dark red and black belt with golden studs and Anacondrai skull-prints on it. His upper legs are dark red with golden chains, while his lower legs are purple with silver chains and claw-prints on his feet. Like most of his companions and best friend Chope, Kapau is shown to be very muscular.

Personality

Personality[]

Kapau and his friend Chope don't have a lot of skill or talent, but they have plenty of ambition despite their comical shortcomings. As the secret plan to transform the Cultists into Anacondrai progresses, they find themselves gaining the higher rank they have always wanted, but they also find themselves in way over their heads. With help from Chope, they complete the spell to turn the Cultists into Anacondrai, and the new Serpentine War begins.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In LEGO Dimensions, several of Chen's guards use Chope's Minifigure head, though they have either Zugu, Sleven, or Kapau's (with a yellow left arm instead of dark red) torso.
  • Kapau is voiced by Alessandro Juliani, who later voices Okino and Shifty.
  • He, along with Chope, did not believe in ghosts, until seeing the spirits of the Anacondrai generals.
  • Kapau and Chope have a similar role in the show as Chopper Maroon and Mohawk, as they are often together, commenting on the situation, complaining and entertaining.
    • They are also similar to Kruncha and Nuckal as well as Raggmunk and Blunck, with all of them being comedic villain duos subsidiary to more serious, higher-ranking villains (Samukai, Eyezor, and Machia respectively).
