Overview

“I've longed for the chance to repay you for what you did!”
“What did I do again?”
“You stole my throne! Turned my people against me, turned my own daughter against me!”
— Vangelis and Cole, "Dragon Form"

Vangelis is the former king of Shintaro and the father of Vania. Years before the ninja arrived at Shintaro, Vangelis sent the Lowly to find one of the skeletal remains of Hazza D'ur before tricking and banishing them to Rock-Bottom. He then got the power to resurrect the Re-Awakened while taking control of the Dungeons of Shintaro and forcing the Geckles and Munce to mine Vengestone for him, which he traded on the black market with Harumi for. Following his rise to power in the Dungeons, he became known as the Skull Sorcerer. Eventually, Cole learned the power of the Spinjitzu Burst and destroyed the Skull of Hazza D'ur. Having been defeated, Vangelis tried to ask for forgiveness, but Cole refused, and Vania had Hailmar arrest him. He was ultimately succeeded by Vania.

More than a year later, Vangelis was freed and recruited by the Kabuki Mask to join the Council of the Crystal King along with more of the ninja's enemies to have his revenge. After he was given new powers by the Overlord, he joined in the latter's invasion of Ninjago, in which he also battled the ninja and the Resistance. However, he was eventually defeated by Cole and left stuck in a crevice created by the former's use of the Scythe of Quakes. Following the battle, he was apprehended and taken to Kryptarium Prison along with most of the Crystal King's Council.

History

History[]

Early life[]

Years ago, Vangelis extended an invitation to the Lowly to embark on a quest to the Dungeons of Shintaro. He entrusted them with the mission of retrieving the Skull of Hazza D'ur, the most malevolent artifact in Shintaro Mountain, with the intention of seemingly destroying it and protecting the kingdom. The Lowly agreed to undertake the task, and Vangelis wished them luck. Soon, Vangelis congratulated them on their triumphant mission of recovering the Skull of Hazza D'ur. Korgran requested to destroy the skull as a mark of honor, but Vangelis had a divergent opinion. He believed that the skull's power could be advantageous for the Kingdom of Shintaro. However, the Upply opposed his idea, as they were aware of the skull's malevolent nature and insisted on its destruction. Vangelis asked if they all shared the same sentiment, and they all nodded in agreement. He then shared his apprehension about their resistance and proceeded to banish the Upply down to Rock-Bottom by sending them into the Pit.[1]

With the skull in his possession, Vangelis was accompanied by Hazza D'ur, whose spirit was inside the skull. Later, Vangelis used the skull to resurrect the Re-Awakened and take over the Dungeons of Shintaro. After some time, Vangelis stole the Ivory and Shadow Blades of Deliverance from the Geckles and Munce, leading to a rift between the two tribes. Their fragile relationship made it easier for him and his Re-Awakened to capture them.[2] He then forced them to mine Vengestone for him, which he later sold to Princess Harumi through the black market. In exchange for the Vengestone, Harumi paid Vangelis to maintain the Kingdom of Shintaro's luxurious lifestyle.[3]

A Sinister Shadow (flashback)[]

Before the ninja and Wu visited Shintaro after Vangelis invited them at Vania's request, Vangelis gets visited by Harumi for her to negotiate the price of Vengestone with, but the king, after greeting her warmly, tells her that they have to change their plans since the ninja are arriving, much to Harumi’s dismay. The former Jade Princess furiously tells Vangelis that the ninja will ruin his plan and that the wealth of his city depended on the payment received for Vengestone, but Vangelis says he’s already prepared for the ninja and produces the Skull of Hazza D'ur, which he used to transform into the Skull Sorcerer. The Skull Sorcerer reassured his ally she had nothing to fear with his enslaved miners hard at work harvesting the Vengestone desired by the Crystal King and promised to annihilate the ninja, were Harumi's fear to come into fruition. When the ninja and Wu arrive, he transforms back seconds before they enter and gestures for Harumi to hide behind his throne, which she complied to, then welcomes the newcomers.

Master of the Mountain[]

Into the Dark[]

With great reverence, Hailmar approached King Vangelis and introduced the ninja. King Vangelis extended a warm welcome and was delighted at the ninja's acceptance of their invitation. He bowed in respect, acknowledging the honor of their presence. Wu showed his gratitude, acknowledging the magnificence of the Kingdom of Shintaro. Vangelis humbly acknowledged his role as a figurehead, recognizing that the true essence of the city lies within the Sky Folk. He graciously introduced Vania to the ninja and directed them to follow him as he prepared them for the upcoming day.

Disguised as the Skull Sorcerer, Vangelis issued a stern warning to the Geckles and Munce, cautioning them against any form of disruption. Hazza D'ur, in a plea to Vangelis, urged him to take decisive action against the tribes. In response, Vangelis unleashed a barrage of Dark Magic from the Skull of Hazza D'ur, targeting both the Geckles and Munce. Vangelis threatened a group of Geckles, cautioning that any future defiance would result in severe repercussions. Cole subsequently confronted Vangelis, prompting him to command his Awakened Warriors to launch an attack. Despite the odds, Cole emerged victorious in the ensuing battle. Vangelis cautioned Cole that he had interfered in a place beyond his comprehension and would now bare witness to his might. He then used the Skull of Hazza D'ur to reconstruct the Dark Skeletons and capture Cole.

The Worst Rescue Ever[]

Upon being informed of Cole's situation by Vania, Vangelis expressed disappointment and reiterated the prohibition of entering the mountain for safety reasons. He emphasized the potential danger that Vania had put herself and the ninja in. Vangelis apologized to Master Wu and assured him that his best guards would be dispatched to locate Cole. However, when Kai offered his assistance, Vangelis cautioned against any further attempts to enter the dungeons, as there were certain things that should not be disturbed. He then explained that he lied about Mr. Sparkles to Vania to protect her, and that she would understand when she was older. Vangelis then instructed his men to take action while urging everyone else to remain calm. As Kai and Jay insisted on helping Cole, Vangelis refused, citing the risk of endangering more lives. Wu pledged to comply with Vangelis' request, and the King thanked him before excusing himself and Vania.

Following a feud between the Geckles and Munce, Vangelis discovers the ninja's attempt to escape after Cole's release. Using the Skull of Hazza D'ur, Vangelis successfully reconstructed the Re-Awakened and commanded them to apprehend the ninja.

The Two Blades[]

Vangelis admonished Lloyd and Cole for their entry into the Skull Keep in pursuit of the Blades of Deliverance. He then launched an attack on them, using the Skull of Hazza D'ur agaisnt them. However, the ninja managed to disarm him, prompting Vaneglis to retrieve the artifact. Vangelis subsequently pursued the ninja during their escape, relentlessly chasing Cole until he lost him in the labyrinthine tunnels, shouting in anger.

The Skull Sorcerer[]

Vangelis requested the Army of Shintaro a report on the latest shipment of Vengestone from the Accounting Department. Suddenly, Vania, Wu, and Cole entered, urgently requesting to speak with him. Vangelis inquired about the emergency, and Vania informed him about the Vengestone mines. Vangelis expressed his disappointment to Vania for disobeying his orders, clarifing that he was not angry but saddened by her actions. Vangelis explained that he loved her dearly and was only trying to protect her. Vania apologized for her actions but warned Vaneglis of the Skull Sorcerer. During the conversation, Cole made a reference to the skull's color being red. However, Vangelis interjected and corrected Cole by presenting the Skull of Hazza D'ur to them. In doing so, Vangelis also disclosed his identity as the Skull Sorcerer.

Vangelis admitted that his reason for becoming the Skull Sorcerer was because of Vengestone, which was essential to the Kingdom of Shintaro's functioning. Cole and Wu vowed to stop Vangelis' plan, prompting him to attack them with the Skull of Hazza D'ur. Cole, Wu, and Vania attempt to retrieve the artifact from Vangelis. However, their efforts were thwarted when Vangelis struck Chompy with his wing and activated a mechanism that plunged Wu and Cole into the Pit. Vangelis attempted to dissuade Vania from pursuing Cole and Wu, warning her that it was futile. However, Vania was determined to make the attempt. Vangelis then threatened to disown her if she proceeded with her plan. Despite this, Vania jumped into the Pit to pursue her friends.

The Real Fall[]

As Vangelis looked into the Pit, Hazza D'ur spoke of sacrifices. He then proceeded to store the Skull of Hazza D'ur in a box, reverting back to normal. Vangelis urgently called for Hailmar and two guards, reporting a the incident of Vania falling into the Pit. When questioned by Hailmar, Vangelis falsely accused Chompy of attacking her, demanding that the dragon to be locked up immediately.

Grief-Bringer[]

In the Kingdom of Shintaro, Hazza D'ur told Vangelis about a meeting between the Munce and Geckles, where they plan to make peace. Vangelis was surprised and asked who was responsible. Hazza D'ur revealed that it was the ninja and also informed the King that they survived. He also encouraged Vangelis to revive Grief-Bringer, emphasizing the importance of taking action to stop the truce. Vangelis became the Skull Sorcerer by utilizing the Skull of Hazza D'ur and accessed the Dungeons of Shintaro through the Pit. Vangelis resurrected Grief-Bringer by using the Skull of Hazza D'ur upon entering the tomb.

Afterwards, the ninja fled from Grief-Bringer and the Re-Awakened. Vangelis watched them and compared their escape to that of rats. Nevertheless, he was certain that they would not be able to evade capture.

The Darkest Hour[]

Grief-Bringer cleared a path through the lava, allowing Vangelis, Hazza D'ur, and the Re-Awakened to cross over. They found that the ninja had barricaded themselves inside the Geckle Strong Cave with the Geckles and Munce. Vangelis addressed the Geckles and Munce, offering them a chance to save their lives. He suggested that if they handed over the ninja, they could resume their work in the mines. However, Gulch and Murtessa began to argue about who should speak first. Vangelis tried to intervene but was interrupted every time he spoke. As Murtess and Gulch refused to serve him, Vangelis accepted their defiance and ordered the Re-Awakened to tear down the wall between them.

When Grief-Bringer broke through the cave, Lloyd agreed to Vangelis' deal, offering to turn the ninja in if he would spare the Geckles and Munce. However, Lloyd made it clear that the prisoners should not be sent back to the mines. Vangelis agreed to the terms, and the ninja were escorted out by Grief-Bringer. Murtessa reminded Vangelis to keep his promise by leave the tribes in peace. However, Vangelis revealed that he had no intention of keeping his word, had the Re-Awakened capture the Geckles and Munce.

The Upply Strike Back![]

Vangelis taunts the now caged ninja and voices his intentions to have Grief-Bringer destroy them in front of the re-imprisoned Geckles and Munce. He also reveals his identity to them and confirms that he did in fact steal the Blades of Deliverance from the tribes.

Later, when Cole retrieves the Blades and the Upply free the imprisoned workers, Vangelis confronts Cole, mocking him for putting faith in the swords. The two engage in fierce combat as the battle for Shintaro Mountain begins.

The Son of Lilly[]

While fighting him, Vangelis tells Cole the Blades of Deliverance are just regular swords and that his mother gave them to the Geckles and Munce so they could have hope. Vangelis soon used the Skull of Hazza D'ur to destroy them, and he starts taunting Cole by insulting his mother. Cole soon realized the power his mother used to destroy Grief-Bringer was actually the Spinjitzu Burst and unlocks it, destroying the Skull in the process and turning Vangelis back to normal as well as destroying the Re-Awakened and Grief-Bringer once more. He tries to beg for forgiveness, but Cole tells him to beg for it from everyone he enslaved and abused. He tries to explain his actions to Vania but she simply had the Winged Guards arrest him, and he is dragged away, protesting that he is their king. He was eventually dethroned, and Vania becomes queen, succeeding her father.

Vangelis was then put in a big cell reminiscent of a classic Shintaro room in his palace.

Crystalized[]

Ninjago City vs. Ninja[]

While Vangelis was spending the night sitting on the bed and playing a lyre, a hole appeared in a ceiling of his cell. When part of the ceiling fell in front of him, the former king was startled. Then he watched the crystal spiders crawl into the room through the hole and then he grabbed the Skull Sorcerer's mask the spiders had brought. He then listened to the message from the Kabuki Mask that he was invited to join the Council of the Crystal King. Vangelis accepted the offer eagerly and put on the mask, which immediately began to glow a pinkish hue.

The Council of the Crystal King[]

Vangelis has subsequently left the cell, got new Shintaro wings, and went to the Crystal Temple. When Lloyd, disguised as the Mechanic, entered the meeting room, Vangelis was sitting at the table with the other villains, Aspheera, Pythor, and Mister F. Vangelis asked Lloyd who he was, and when Lloyd introduced himself to him as the Mechanic, the former king asked if he should know the name. As the Kabuki Mask entered the room and explained to the villains that he was not the Crystal King but only his herald, Vangelis muttered mockingly that he was his servant. However, the Kabuki Mask warned him not to talk to him like that, because he would send him back to his cell.

When the Kabuki Mask then revealed that there was an impostor among them, Vangelis was shocked like the others, and when Aspheera accused him of being the impostor because he was the only one wearing a mask, he objected that Mister F did the same. When Lloyd tried to escape after the reveal of the impostor, Vangelis flew to him to stop him, but Lloyd threw him on the table using Spinjitzu. However, Lloyd was eventually stopped by Mister F and the Kabuki Mask revealed his secret identity: Harumi. Lloyd then blacked out.

The Fall of the Monastery[]

Vangelis and the rest of the Crystal Council arrived at the Monastery of Spinjitzu from the secret elevator. Curious about their unexpected presence, Wu questioned the purpose behind their visit. Vangelis responded to Wu's inquiry, implying that the reason should be apparent. However, before Vangelis could elaborate, Aspheera interrupted and declared that they want revenge. Vangelis, determined to correct Aspheera's assumption, clarified that their intention now was not driven by revenge. He emphasized that their purpose was solely to acquire the Weapons of Spinjitzu, highlighting that not everything they do was fueled by a desire for vengeance.

Vangelis, along with the others, proceeds to launch an assault on the defenders, wielding his naganta. In the midst of the chaos, Vangelis confronted Nya, but she swiftly retaliated and kicked him away. However, Pythor intervened and utilizes his Anacondrai invisibility to catch Nya off guard, enabling Vangelis to seize the opportunity and forcefully throw her onto the ground. Vangelis then directed his aggression toward Master Wu, who pushed him back, causing him to collide with Aspheera. Vangelis, Aspheera, and the Mechanic launched a relentless attack on Wu. Vangelis managed to strike Wu while he was airborne, but his momentum was abruptly halted as Wu's hat propelled him backwards.

P.I.X.A.L. called upon the MiniPix, who swiftly opened fire on Vangelis and the Council. Despite their best efforts to defend themselves against the droids, the villains found themselves overwhelmed. However, the Crystal Spiders arrived. The spiders eliminated every MiniPix, leaving only one standing. With the heroes immobilized, the Crystal Council seized the Golden Weapons, while Vangelis claimed the Scythe of Quakes as his own. With a sense of urgency, Vangelis commanded the Mechanic to detonate the Crystal Spiders as they made their escape from the Monastery.

Darkness Within[]

After raiding and destroying the Monastery, Vangelis with the Crystal Council return to the Crystal Temple with the four Golden Weapons. Around the big crystal, he placed the Scythe of Quakes around it as Aspheera uses her new staff and its powers to transform the Golden Weapons into the Weapons of Destruction through a ritual and through that, they bring the Overlord back into the world of Ninjago, now known as the Crystal King.

The Coming of the King[]

Shortly after gaining his new form, the Overlord used his powers to empower the Crystal Council, giving them new armor as well as their signature Weapon of Destruction. Happy with what he got, Vangelis thanked the Overlord in return as well as bowing towards him. When Lloyd managed to free himself and tilted the Crystal Temple to one side, the Crystal Council and the Overlord held on to prevent himself from sliding down. After Lloyd fled the area, The Overlord ordered his council to pursue and kill the Green Ninja. During the chase, he managed to corner Lloyd, who tries to warn them that their master will destroy everything. When they all threw their weapons at Lloyd, the Green Ninja easily avoided them and the Weapons of Destruction collided, which caused a powerful explosion that knocked the Crystal Council to the ground. As Lloyd was willing to jump off the temple while Harumi tries to get him on her side, the Vangelis with the Council arrived to watch. He was left shocked and confused as Lloyd jump down from the Crystal Island with Harumi. Knowing that jumping after the two is too risky, Pythor and Vangelis went back into the temple while Aspheera and the Mechanic argue on who should jump after Lloyd and Harumi.

Return to Primeval's Eye[]

Vangelis chases Lloyd and Harumi on Dragonides.

Quittin' Time![]

After Ninjago City was crystalized, the Council was ordered by the Overlord to bring the ninja to him.

An Issue of Trust[]

Vangelis meets the Overlord with the rest but is reprimanded by the Overlord due to his failure to secure the Ninja before Harumi and Mister F reveal that they know where the ninja are. He and the Council later surround the Resistance with their army to overwhelm them.

Dragon Form[]

Together with the crystal army, Vangelis engaged in combat against the Resistance, riding on a Dragonide. Nevertheless, the allies of the Resistance appeared, leading Vangelis and the army to face the challenge of the ninja's Dragon Form. Vangelis relentlessly chased after Cole on his Dragonide. He reminded him of the consequences of his actions. Confused, Cole questioned Vangelis about what he had done to deserve such animosity. Vangelis wasted no time in listing his grievances, accusing Cole of overthrowing him and turning his own people, including Vania, against him. Cole nonchalantly brushed off Vangelis' accusations, noting that Vangelis brought that upon himself. Unwilling to accept Cole's response, Vangelis vehemently denied the accusations. Vangelis used his Dragonide to pin Cole to the ground. Vangelis acknowledged that the Overlord, who wanted Cole alive, was absent, allowing him to take matters into his own hands. However, just as Vangelis was about to deliver the final blow, Adam intervened, webbing Vangelis. Seizing the opportunity, Vania took advantage of Vangelis' distraction and knocked him off his Dragonide. With Vangelis momentarily incapacitated, Cole swiftly grabbed the scythe from Vangelis and tapped into his Elemental Powers, channeling them into the weapon. As a result, the Scythe of Destruction purifies itself back into the Scythe of Quakes. Cole used the scythe to create a pothole beneath Vangelis, trapping him within its depths.

After the battle, Vangelis was arrested and sent to Kryptarium Prison.[4]

Appearance

Appearance[]

Vangelis, like all of the other Sky Folk, has white skin. He has balding black hair and a purple symbol on his forehead and dons white robes with golden accents on the torso sides and brown and light blue kimono-like clothing underneath with a gold V-neck and a black sash around his waist. He usually has some Shintaro wings on his back. In the anime-styled scenes of "Dungeon Party!," he is shown to have long hair tied into a Japanese chonmage and his robe is shown to have a large white collar that surrounds the back of his neck and has gold patterns underneath, along with gold shoulder pads split in two sections.

As the Skull Sorcerer, he wears a black rice hat and a white mask with visible gray detailing and glowing yellow-green eyes. The mask has a mouth, which it lacks in the sets. His face has now changed to black, although his actual face is not seen besides the sets due to the mask covering it. However, his hands are still the same white coloring. He dons a black garment with dull gold Ninjargon texts and a dark green cloth held by a magic symbol of the same gold color. His Shintaro wings also change to resemble large bat-like wings that are black in color with green spikes on them.

After being empowered by the Overlord, Vangelis keeps his mask and hat from before, but the mouth is no longer visible and the eyes glow magenta and no longer have the gray detailing. Like Aspheera, Vangelis' face is black again and he gains black gloves. He is dressed in the same armor worn by the other Crystal Council members, consisting of a purple suit with metal on the torso, glowing magenta detailing, and the Overlord's symbol on it. He also has black pants with metallic gray detailing on it while infectious crystals cover his shoulders like shoulder pads. His wings also revert to the way they were as the Skull Sorcerer, only the spikes are now pink.

Personality

Personality[]

“My father is a good man but very strict. If he knew the ninja had defied him and gone into the mountain, he would be quite upset.”
— Vania, "The Two Blades"

He had initially shown himself to be a very benevolent individual as he welcomed the ninja into his palace.

He was a loving father as he lied to his daughter about the truth of her imaginary friend to protect her from the real horrors that were supposed to be kept secret from her and the other young people of the Kingdom of Shintaro. He also forbade her from accompanying the ninja into the Dungeons of Shintaro.

This ultimately proved to be a façade, as he has proven to be a very deceptive individual as he lied to his followers and daughter about how he is the Skull Sorcerer. In contrast to his persona as a loving father, he showed no remorse as he condemned his daughter to fall into the Pit along with his enemies after revealing his true purposes and he blamed it on her pet. However, he is shown to have mercy, when he gives Vania the choice of jumping into the Pit or not and was implied to generally love her, calling her "my heart". Eventually after she played her role in his defeat, he pins all the blame on Cole, roaring to the Master of Earth during their last confrontation that he blamed Cole for Vania's desertion.

He is a very greedy person, as he forced the Munce and Geckles tribes into slavery and profited from the selling of the Vengestone they mined to keep his kingdom. Not surprisingly for his two-faced actions, he is deceptive, as he had no qualms in taking back his part on the deal to the ninja about sparing the Munce and Geckles tribes by quickly putting them back to slavery.

After he was stripped of his power, Vangelis resorted to begging for mercy. He was also desperate to beg his daughter for help, to no avail. However, it is later proven that this was not truly genuine, as when he was offered another opportunity for power by receiving a position in the Council of the Crystal King, he was more than eager to accept it.

Despite his cruel personality, Vangelis was highly diplomatic and treated his ally Harumi respectfully and politely, not reacting with anger when she furiously reminded him his city was dependent on her gold to continue to maintain its luxury. This respectful treatment, however, does not extend to his fellow Council of the Crystal King members, and he constantly butted heads with Aspheera especially.

Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

Shintaro Secrets Revealed![]

Wearing the skull of the dark magician Hazza D’ur gave him incredible powers - he is now able to bring skeletons to life! He uses them to control the cave dwellers, forcing them to mine Vengestone. The Skull Sorcerer stole the Blades of Deliverance from the Geckles and Munces, using them divide the two tribes.

Monstrous Mission![]

The Skull Sorcerer is the master of the dungeons! He wants to mine as much Vengestone as possible. His powers come from the Skull of Hazza D’Ur, and with it he can turn skeletons into invincible warriors. With his boney army behind him, this villain can control the monsters and tribes of the underworld. can our ninja heroes stop this dangerous enemy?[6]

LEGO Life[]

King Vangelis is the ruler of the Kingdom of Shintaro, famous for being the most perfect kingdom on NINJAGO Island. He's very powerful and popular among his loyal subjects. Under his command is the royal guard known as the Winged Army of Shintaro. His daughter, however, is not always quite as easy to command. (Maybe that's not such a bad thing, actually.)

Character Encyclopedia New Edition[]

Shintaro seems like a perfect city, but its king, Vangelis, has a guilty secret. He lives a double life as the wicked Skull Sorcerer, who has enslaved the kingdom's underground tribes and made them dig for Vengestone. In this disguise, Vangelis wears a ghostly mask and flies on batlike wings.

The Big Baddie Check![]

When it comes to magic, this dude relied on a skull instead of lightning. His plans were spoiled when the ninja united the Munce and Geckles against him. We only rely on ourselves and our cool purple flashes.[7]

Crystalized[]

Once the King of Shintaro, the NINJAGO® world’s most perfect kingdom, Vangelis’s reign ended in dishonor when the ninja unmasked him as the evil Skull Sorcerer. What path lies ahead for this dangerous, Vengestone-obsessed foe?[8]

Very Bad Bunch[]

Under his kingdom of Shintaro, the former Skull Sorcerer mined the Vengestone. It goes without saying that Vangelis - as he is now called - is an important part of the Crystal Council.[9]

LEGO.com[]

The former King of Shintaro went by the alias Skull Sorcerer when he secretly ruled the dungeons beneath his city. It was there that the ninja unmasked and defeated him, but he has risen again with an invitation to join the Council of the Crystal King. His new ruler gives him a pair of Shintaro wings and the Scythe of Destruction to exact his revenge against the ninja. King Vangelis was succeeded by his daughter Vania as the ruler of Shintaro.[10]

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • He was originally named the "Ashmonger."[11]
  • He is the first Ninjago Minifigure to have wings.
    • He reuses the wing pieces of the Bats from Legends of Chima.
  • He is the second main villain to have the 2018 slope piece, the first being Unagami. Unlike Unagami, he has no back printing on the slope piece.
    • Both Vangelis and Unagami were the main villains of 2020.
  • As the Skull Sorcerer, the symbol on his torso is called the triquetra, which is also typically used with a symbol known as the Triple Moon. In his design, it is depicted as inverted.
    • A triquetra is an ancient symbol commonly used by many pagans to represent the threefold nature of the Triple Goddess: maiden, mother, and crone.
    • It also symbolizes the goddess' three faces: mind, body, and spirit.
    • It is also commonly used to reference the Holy Trinity in Christianity, being the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.
  • As the Skull Sorcerer, the words "Dead Rise" in Ninjargon text are written on his garment's torso sides and extending to the slope piece in the sets.
    • However, in the show, the words are incorrectly spelled "Dead Irse."
  • As the Skull Sorcerer, he has only black eyes on his mask in the sets while he has a mouth, green eyes, and highlighted features on his mask in the show.
    • In his crystalized form, his mask loses the mouth, making it more accurate to the minifigure.
  • The concept of the Skull Sorcerer is reminiscent of Skeletor from the show He-Man.
    • Korgran also bears similarity to the title character from that show.
  • He is similar to Hela from the Thor: Ragnarok, as both of them use green and black as their main colors, use the triquetra symbol, resurrected dead beings for their army, and are both royalty that were later imprisoned by their own family.
    • However, in Hela's case, the father imprisoned the daughter, while in Vangelis' case, the daughter imprisoned the father.
  • He uses the hairpiece of Doc Brown from LEGO Dimensions, also used on Bob the Intern and Engelbert.
  • In the Polish dub of the season, Vangelis was voiced by Mikołaj Klimek, an actor who passed away July 12, 2020. The dub was released posthumously on October 10, 2020.
    • Mikołaj Klimek also voiced Soul Archer.
    • Mikołaj Klimek was replaced by Jacek Kopczyński in Crystalized, the same situation happened with Netflix's Carmen Sandiego character Gunnar Maelstrom, whose also Polish VA was Mikołaj Klimek and was replaced by Jacek Kopczyński.
    • This was also the case in the French dub where his voice actor: Erwin Grünspan also died on December 29, 2021, he was replaced by Jean-François Rossion for Crystalized who had already voiced Daddy No Legs, Okino, and Hailmar.
Etymology

Etymology[]

Derived from "Vangel," Vangelis is a Greek name meaning "bringer of good news" or "good news."

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He is the eighth known king in Ninjago, the others being Pythor, Skales, Khanjikhan, Nadakhan (in an alternate timeline), Mambo V, Grimfax, and Atta the Ratta.
  • He is the third main villain to have a child who is a protagonist. The first was Garmadon with Lloyd and the second was Chen with Skylor.
    • However, Vangelis is the first main villain to have a child who was a protagonist from the beginning, as Lloyd and Skylor were originally antagonists.
  • He is the sixth major villain to have an identity hidden from the ninja. The first was Pythor, who was the stranger who helped the Overlord; the second was Krux, who was Dr. Saunders; the third was Harumi, who was the Quiet One; the fourth was Zane, who was the Ice Emperor; and the fifth was Unagami, who was Prime Empire itself, rather than Milton Dyer as the ninja initially thought.
  • He is the only royal parent:
    • to have a minifigure.
    • to appear beyond its season other than in flashback or mention.
    • to be alive right now.
    • to serve as a major antagonist in one season.
  • He is the fifth villain to have a dragon. The other four were the Overlord with the Nindroid MechDragon, Morro with Morro's dragon, the Ice Emperor with Boreal, and Unagami with the Empire Dragon.
  • He is one of the five main antagonists that doesn't appear in the first episode of their season, the others being Pythor, the Preeminent, Kalmaar, and the Crystal King.
  • He is the fourth villain, after Yang, Iron Baron, and Vex, not to pose a direct threat to Ninjago City, as this he was the main antagonist of a season that didn't feature Ninjago City.
  • He is one of the six known villains that had no intentions to take over Ninjago, as his main goal was to keep the Kingdom of Shintaro.
    • The other five are Yang, Iron Baron, Vex, Unagami, and Ronin.
  • For some reason, in the Castillian dub, Vangelis is referred to as Hazza D'Ur when he wears the mantle of the Skull Sorcerer.
