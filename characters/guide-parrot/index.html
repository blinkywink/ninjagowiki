Overview

“I gave Pete a talking parrot that tells him how to sail, so as not to crash into the rocks.”
— Soto about the parrot, Destiny's Bounty Adventure Box

The Guide Parrot[1] is a pirate parrot owned by No-Eyed Pete. It helps Pete orient himself even though it always gives the wrong command, saying "left" or "the other left."

History

History[]

Prior to the series[]

On the tenth day of the voyage on the Destiny's Bounty, Soto donated a parrot to No-Eyed Pete, who would tell him how to sail.[2]

Some time later, the parrot began to claim to have seen fish-like people, but Soto wasn't sure if he should trust it, as its only witness was No-Eyed Pete.[2] Eventually, when the pirates set sail in search of the Dark Island, No-Eyed Pete was steering the Destiny's Bounty under the direction of the Guide Parrot, which caused the ship to crash, where the parrot and the rest of the pirate perished.[3]

Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

Pirates vs. Ninja[]

Many years later, the parrot was resurrected by the power of Lord Garmadon's Mega Weapon, and he joined the other revived pirates in trying to reclaim their ship (now a flight-capable vessel called the Black Bounty). When the ninja arrived, he fought with No-Eyed Pete, but the Earth Ninja defeated him after stealing his parrot to give misguided directions. After the ninja reclaimed control of the ship, the pirates were taken away by the police.

Ninjaball Run[]

The pirates competed in Ninjaball Run, with an ordinary car that No-Eyed Pete drives, guided by the parrot. They easily fail, barely into the race, and crash into a canyon. Soto then angrily questions why he lets Pete drive.

Tournament of Elements[]

The Greatest Fear of All[]

In the heat of the ninja battling the Anacondrai in Kryptarium Prison, a spear flew and landed in front of No-Eyed Pete's cell. Under the direction of the parrot, he used it to flip the switch that opened all the cell doors in the prison.
