Overview

Gorge[1][2] is a Geckle and the current Elemental Master of Size who was a competitor in the Tournament of the Sources, alongside the other Elemental Masters of his generation.

History

History[]

At some point in time, Gorge was was granted the Elemental Power of Size.

They Gather for the Feast[]

Gorge was at Foundation Street, participating in an arm-wrestling match with Algu, ultimately losing to the latter.

Later at the welcome feast, Gorge sat beside Arccrack, enjoying a sandwich. However, after seeing Arccrack regurgitate his Snowberry Pie, the Geckle promptly discarded his meal. As Gorge and everyone else gathered to listen to Roby's upcoming speech, the atmosphere was disrupted by the sudden arrival of Zeatrix, creating a scene.

Inside the Maze[]

Gorge was training nearby Mord and Teelk. Soon, he watches alongside the other two Elemental Masters in surprise as Wyldfyre becomes outraged by Cinder.

Truth and Lies[]

He was present when Roby announced the Ninja won the Cavern Run.

The Sword Shatters[]

During a tournament match, Gorge went head-to-head with Geo. To prevent himself from tumbling into the gaps between the blocks, he decided to increase his size. Despite his efforts, Geo used Fusion to merge the grids, resulting in all the blocks beneath Gorge to collapse. As a consequence, Gorge was defeated, leading to the transfer of his powers to Geo through the Elemental Cup.

The Final Game[]

Gorge watched the final match between Sora and Nokt, and was surprised when Wyldfyre arrived with Zur to expose Bleckt after Nokt defeated Sora.

Elements of Betrayal[]

Gorge fought alongside several other Elemental Masters against Cinder and the Wolf Mask Warriors in hand-to-hand combat. He eventually got his powers back after Roby drained Nokt of the stolen Elemental Powers and defeated the Wolf Clan. He later watched as Roby declared Sora the default winner of the tournament.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He is the seventh big figure to appear in the franchise, the first six being Dogshank, the samurai Vermillion warrior from "A Time of Traitors," Cruel Lord's guard from Forced Entry, Killow, Krag, and Big Han.
    • He is also the fifth big figure to have fingers.
    • He is also the first one to appear in Dragons Rising while being the first Elemental Master to be a big figure.
  • He is the third Geckle to be an Elemental Master, the first being an Unknown Elemental Master and the second being Geo, although Geo is only half-Geckle.
    • He faced Geo in the Tournament of the Sources and lost.
  • It is unknown if Gorge's large size is a result of his element, as he is much larger than every other member of his race, yet after being drained of his element during the Tournament of the Sources, he retains his large stature and no other user of Size during the tournament appeared to have any residual effects from having the element.
