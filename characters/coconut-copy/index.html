Overview

“I'm a magical back-up copy of the Sorceress's personality. The non-coconut Sorceress made me a bunch of years ago, in case Janet's whole mind-wipe scheme ever worked.”
— The coconut copy to Nya, "For Whom the Bell Tolls"

The coconut copy[1] is a magical backup copy of Gandalaria. She was designed to preserve the essence of who Gandalaria is, ensuring that her traits and characteristics remain intact, even in the event of a mindwipe.

History

History[]

Prior to For Whom the Bell Tolls[]

Years prior to the Merge, Gandalaria ran out of melons and created the magical coconut in case Janet's mind-wipe scheme would ever worked.

For Whom the Bell Tolls[]

The coconut possessed an armor to fight Nya. After losing the battle, the latter was shocked to see her opponent was a coconut, much to the coconut's confusion and anger. The coconut then told Nya its origins and helped her to get to the Cave of Bells in Mysterium.
