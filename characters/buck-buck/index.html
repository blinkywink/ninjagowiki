Overview

Buck Buck is a chicken owned by Mama Mawa.

History

History[]

The Lycan King[]

Buck Buck was trying to peck out a small carrot when Mama Mawa was complaining about the current harvest. Later, he was sitting in her hands when she told Garmadon the story of the Wolf Clan.

Masters and Students[]

Buck Buck was taken on a walk by Mama Mawa.

Destinies[]

Buck Buck accompanied Asa as the latter foraged for food. After failing to catch a rabbit, the two followed it into the Shadow Dojo, where Buck Buck noticed a discarded Wolf Mask. Shortly afterward, The Lycan King emerged from the shadows of the Dojo and captured Asa; the lycanthrope's presence was enough to cause Buck Buck to turn tail and flee in terror, eventually making his way back to the Children of the Wolf where he collapsed in terror. As the children cradled the exhausted chicken and offered him some water, Buck Buck revealed to them that Asa had been taken by the Lycan King.

Homecoming[]

Buck Buck accompanied the Children of the Wolf and the revitalized Wolf Warriors as they gathered before the Stone Wolves to bid farewell to Garmadon. As they said their goodbyes, Zuri spoke on Buck Buck's behalf and told Garmadon that the chicken felt he should stay with them.
