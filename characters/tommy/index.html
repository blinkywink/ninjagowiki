Overview

“We're detectives. We detect things.”
— Tommy to Nya, "My Dinner With Nadakhan"

Detective Tommy (also known as the Bad Detective) is a detective for the Ninjago Police. Unlike his counterpart Simon, Tommy takes a much more aggressive approach to his job.

History

History[]

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Skybound[]

Public Enemy Number One[]

He and Simon questioned Misako after she was arrested, though she refused to reveal where the ninja were hiding, instead insisting they were framed. Tommy decided to let her sweat it out, though when they returned, she had vanished.

Misfortune Rising[]

Tommy was present at police headquarters when the news was broken that the ninja had escaped Kryptarium Prison. While he consumed a handful of doughnuts, Tommy revealed the computer password to another detective (who was none other than Jay in disguise).

Later when the ninja appeared during the Sky Pirates' attack on the city, the Police Commissioner sprinted after them, surprising Tommy with how nimble he was.

My Dinner With Nadakhan[]

The detectives, Commissioner, and Ronin found Nya, Lloyd, and Cole near Tiger Widow Island. After telling them they realized the ninja are not criminals, they showed them a bottle with Jay's message inside.

Wishmasters[]

Tommy helped build the Raid Zeppelin so the ninja can get close to Djinjago.

The Last Resort[]

Simon and Tommy were defending the police station from the Sky Pirates, after a police car almost falls on them, Simon is captured by the Sky Pirates and Tommy scares off the pirate that was about to capture him, Tommy and a group of police officers retreats into the police station and warned the Police Commissioner that Nadakhan found them.

When the trio were captured by Nadakhan, they were threatened to walk the plank. Tommy accidentally told them the ninja still have the venom and jumped off. The other two followed, and they were surrounded by sharks.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

Sons of Garmadon[]

Snake Jaguar[]

While in a cop car with Simon, the Sons of Garmadon race by on their bikes. The two cops soon give chase, but they are stopped after Zane jumps over their car with his bike.

Dread on Arrival[]

Tommy would participate in distracting the Sons of Garmadon long enough for them to enter the Royal Palace, which was converted by the Sons of Garmadon and Harumi into the Temple of Resurrection. After the ninja prevent the ritual from being completed, Tommy and Simon both personally arrest Harumi.

True Potential[]

Tommy is seen carrying some debris after Lord Garmadon's attack.

Big Trouble, Little Ninjago[]

Tommy is seen along with Simon in the police blockade, before the Colossus stomps onto the police cars, a female officer saves him from being crushed and helps him up afterwards.

Hunted[]

Green Destiny[]

The Resistance help chain down the Colossus. When the Colossus start to fall apart, the Resistance runs away to safety.

The police force began to arrest the Sons of Garmadon once more.

Other appearances

Other appearances[]

Radio Free Ninjago[]

Tommy is seen in one of the crossed out photos on the floor in Borg Tower when the Resistance broadcasts Lloyd's message to Ninjago, indicating Tommy has been captured as part of Ultra Violet's Ultra Hunt.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Tommy is named after Tommy Andreasen, one of Ninjago's co-creators.[1]
  • He uses the face of the Barbarian from Collectible Minifigures Series 11 but without the scars. A scarred version is seen on two background character designs in various episodes, one being a Stiix citizen and another being a Kryptarium inmate.
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, Tommy, Simon, and the Police Commissioner were able to get back to Ninjago and escape the sharks from "The Last Resort" with the help of giant sea turtles.[2]
  • His CGI image has the same hair as Clouse, but in the TV series he has the same hair as Rufus McCallister, originally used on Will Turner from LEGO's Pirates of the Caribbean theme.
  • Despite the fact that he and Simon wore beige clothes similar to detectives' coats in Season 6, since Season 8 they already have a regular police uniform. The reason for this change is unknown, but it may indicate that they are no longer detectives.
    • In "Green Destiny," Tommy and Simon are both wearing their original outfit. But when they were arresting the Sons of Garmadon, their outfits changed back into police uniforms.
  • He shares his name with a Wu's Academy student, though the latter was only named in the credits.
