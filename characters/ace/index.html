Ace is a paperboy in Ninjago City.

History

History[]

The News Never Sleeps![]

Ace was one of the three paperboys that notice Nelson when he first arrived at the newspaper warehouse.

Dyer Island[]

Ace, Sammy, and a girl was watching Leroy play Prime Empire. They soon decided to leave because Leroy was playing for a long time.

Papergirl[]

Ace was getting ready to deliver newspapers, but was surprised to hear Antonia's last day as a papergirl. He thought Antonia was joking, to which she denies. When Antonia told the other paperboys that she was going to work at the Dairy Dragon, Ace stated that they had great ice cream.

The Calm Before the Storm[]

Ace was playing Prime Empire in Benny's Arcade Emporium when Jay entered. He was one of the many people that were cheering for Jay and Nya when they started dancing.

Crystastrophe[]

Ace and Nelson were talking to each other while Antonia was teaching Skids how to throw.

Quittin' Time![]

Ace and the other paperboys were seen receiving their orders in the Newspaper warehouse, with Ace being assigned Route 4. Those orders however would be cancelled after Wu was brought to the warehouse and urged the kids to escape the city before it was too late. Before departures could begin however, the growls of nearby Crystal Warriors caught Wu's attention and he rushed outside to save a pair of civilians from their pursuers. Ace and the other kids assisted in Wu's efforts, dropping a large pallet of newspapers atop a pair of Crystal Warriors so that the civilians could get to safety. Invigorated by this encounter, Ace and the other kids decided to forego escaping in favor of starting the Resistance.

Return of the Ice Emperor[]

Ace was present with Nelson, Sammy, and Skids while Wu was examining a scroll, searching for answers to stop the Overlord's attack.

Distress Calls[]

Ace and the other paperboys underwent training, overseen by Kai and Skylor.

An Issue of Trust[]

Ace was present in the Newspaper warehouse and was seen talking to Skids while Wu examined the Dragon Form scroll. He would later be amongst those who gathered to watch the Ninja attempt to achieve Dragon Form.

Roots[]

Ace and Skids were amongst those in the Resistance cheering after the Overlord was defeated.
