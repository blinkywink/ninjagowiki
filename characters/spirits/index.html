Overview

Spirits are metaphysical entities that reside in the Merged Lands. Some spirits take on physical shape while most spirits remain in their non-physical form.

History

History[]

Background[]

The two powerful spirits: the Overlord and Wojira existed before the creation of Ninjago island. They were both defeated in the battle with the First Spinjitzu Master and lost their power for thousands of years. While Wojira was thrown into a deep sleep, The Overlord was still awake and was plotting for revenge.

After The First Spinjitzu Master created the Dragon Cores, the duty of protecting the temple was bestowed upon a spirit. It was imbued with a powerful enchantment, compelling him to reveal the hidden locations of the Dragon Cores exclusively upon the earnest plea of a direct descendant of the First Spinjitzu Master.

At some point, the City of Temples starts using Spirit Dragons to deliver invitations to the Tournament of the Sources.

When Wu and Garmadon were still very young, the First Spinjitzu Master would tell the boys campfire stories of the Overlord.[1]

At some point after his defeat, the Overlord corrupted a snake that would later become known as the Great Devourer and set his plan in motion, pinning the serpent on a young Garmadon, in order to corrupt his power and use Garmadon to create strife, as well as create a variety of other problems that would stem from the Great Devourer's influence, which would ultimately lead the Overlord to obtaining a worthy vessel to inhabit.

Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

Thousands of years later, a corrupted Garmadon was confronted by the Overlord's material being, who was reduced to an orb of dark energy. Influenced to join forces, Garmadon assumed command of the indestructible Stone Army and began construction of an ultimate weapon. Eventually, the Overlord revealed he was using Garmadon and possessed his body - commencing a metamorphosis that turned him into his monstrous original form. The ninja and Lloyd returned to Ninjago, where Lloyd fought and defeated the Overlord in the ultimate battle.

Rebooted[]

Sometime later, the Overlord once again returned a virus known as the Digital Overlord. With the help of Pythor and the Nindroids, the Overlord became the all-powerful Golden Master using the Golden Weapons and Lloyd's Golden Power. In a final effort to defeat the Overlord, Zane sacrificed himself, destroying both their physical forms. As the balance must be maintained, the Overlord survived, and his material being went dormant.

The Island[]

Ronin discovered the Island of the Keepers and the tribe that lives there. With his new crew, he constructed a fake Wojira and exploited them of their treasures. He was exposed and defeated by the ninja.
