Overview

“I made it further than any of you! Forget team games, I'll do my own thing!”
— Leroy to his friends, "Dyer Island"

Leroy is a paperboy that lives in Ninjago City. He was one of the gamers trapped in Prime Empire. However, after Jay helped Unagami and Milton Dyer make amends, Leroy and the other Ninjago City gamers were freed along with a few NPCs.

History

History[]

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

The News Never Sleeps![]

Leroy was one of the three paperboys that notice Nelson when he first arrived at the newspaper warehouse.

Snaketastrophy[]

At the Fur Mart, Leroy was at the annual puppy petting competition until an Elemental Cobra appeared and scared him off.

Powerless[]

When the Elemental Cobras were attacking Ninjago City, one of them popped Leroy's balloon.

Ancient History[]

Leroy is one of the many civilians that ran away as the Pyro Vipers and Elemental Cobras attacked Ninjago City.

The Message[]

Leroy was delivering newspapers with Nelson and Antonia.

Prime Empire & Prime Empire Original Shorts[]

Let's Dance[]

Leroy was playing Fist to Face at Benny's Arcade Emporium.

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

Inside Benny's Arcade Emporium, Leroy is waiting for his turn to play a video game, but another person wouldn't let him. Suddenly, the power goes out and when it comes back on, all of the arcade cabinets are replaced with Prime Empire.

Gayle Gossip: A Closer Look[]

Leroy was holding a woman's hand while waiting in line to play Prime Empire at Benny's Arcade Emporium.

Dyer Island[]

Leroy is playing Prime Empire for a long time which causes his friends to leave. Just as they left, he reaches level 13 and the arcade cabinet opens. He then decides to enter Prime Empire.

Seabound[]

Papergirl[]

Leroy was getting ready to deliver newspapers, but was surprised to hear Antonia's last day as a papergirl. He also mentioned the Dairy Dragon after Antonia did.

The Calm Before the Storm[]

Leroy was one of the many people that were cheering for Jay and Nya.

Assault on Ninjago City[]

Leroy rode his bike to Benny's Arcade Emporium. He was also one of the children that were stuck inside the arcade until Lloyd saved them.

The Turn of the Tide[]

After Nya defeated Wojira, Leroy attended in her memorial.

Crystalized[]

Christofern (flashback)[]

Leroy cowered behind a lamp post when Garmadon approached Vinny's apartment.

Crystastrophe[]

Leroy sat outside the newspaper warehouse with the rest of the paperboys when Wu and Lloyd approached them to ask about several newspaper clippings.

Brave But Foolish[]

Leroy was amongst a group of citizens rescued by the ninja from the Crystal Warriors.

Quittin' Time![]

Leroy was present at the newspaper warehouse and waited to receive his orders for delivery for the day. When Wu was brought to the warehouse by Nelson and Antonia, Leroy handed him a bottle of water and helped him into a chair that Spokes brought over. Although Wu ordered for him and the other children to flee the city, Leroy and the others chose to stay behind to help establish the Resistance.

Safe Haven[]

Leroy hurled stacks of newspapers from the roof of the newspaper warehouse at some of the surrounding Crystal zombies so that Kai, Cole. Skylor, and Jake could safely enter.

Distress Calls[]

Leroy was among the citizens receiving combat training from Kai and Skylor.

An Issue of Trust[]

Leroy and the other Paperboys watched the ninja's attempts at unlocking Dragon Form.

Dragon Form[]

Leroy participated in the final battle against the Crystal Army in front of the newspaper warehouse.

Roots[]

Leroy celebrated in the streets of Ninjago City following the defeat of the Crystal King and his army.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • He is one of the few male characters to have a female voice actor, the others being Lloyd, Brad, Gene, Skales Jr., Wu (as a baby), Sammy, and Ginkle.
  • He is the second character to have mid-legs in the show, the first being Nelson.
    • However, in Season 12, his legs were downgraded to have short legs instead. He also had normal minifigure legs in "Powerless."
  • He uses the same hairpiece as Dwayne, originally used on Commissioner Gordon from The LEGO Batman Movie.
  • In Season 11 and "Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?" he wears a blue beanie, which he no longer has later.
  • Leroy, along with Garmadon and Wu, is the only character who has been shown during the show with all types of LEGO legs - normal, middle and short.
    • However, like the sons of the First Spinjitzu Master, Leroy's legs length is inconsistent. While in Season 11 he had mid-legs during all episodes except "Powerless," when he had normal legs, since Prime Empire Original Shorts his legs have been changed to short.
