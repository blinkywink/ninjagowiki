This article is about a subject that is canonical to the storyline of Warner Bros' The LEGO Movie franchise.

Nomis, also known as Hot Dog Man,[1] is a citizen of Ninjago City that runs Hot Noodle Dog, a hotdog stand.

History

History[]

The LEGO Ninjago Movie[]

Nomis was revealed to share a birthday with Lloyd Garmadon and the Panda Suit Guy, and he was also revealed to be a citizen of Ninjago City. He later gasped when Garmadon and his Shark Army emerged from the ocean to attack the city.
