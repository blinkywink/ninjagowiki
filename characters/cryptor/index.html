Overview

“My fight is with everyone in the Merged Lands! There will never be peace here!”
— Cryptor to Frak, "Ancient History"

General Cryptor is one of the many Nindroids designed by P.I.X.A.L. who served as the general of the Nindroid Army. Cryptor found himself at odds with the original Nindroid, Zane, whose design had inspired his. Cryptor and his allies fought to stop the ninja from erasing the Digital Overlord from the Digiverse. Despite failing to do so, Cryptor and the Nindroids claimed the Golden Weapons, which allowed for the birth of the Golden Master. During his assault on New Ninjago City, Cryptor fought against Nya and Min-Droid and was frozen when Zane sacrificed himself to defeat the Golden Master. Subsequently, Cryptor was destroyed after shattering apart.

A statue of Cryptor was later placed in the Ninjago Museum of History. When Cole accidentally opened the Departed Realm, Cryptor's ghost escaped and possessed the statue. Cryptor set off to exact vengeance, in his case going after Zane with help from three Nindroid Warriors. Cryptor fought Zane in Birchwood Forest before accidentally hitting himself with his weapon, sending him back to the Departed Realm.

Following the Merge, Cryptor's soul did not move on from the Spectral Lands.[1] His body was rebuilt by the Loyalists on Dr. LaRow's orders with blueprints that an inside agent had stolen from Borg Industries. Cryptor then led them in orchestrating Operation Mentax to swap Beatrix's mind into Zeatrix's body and stop the Summit of the Merged Lands. During the Summit, he transferred his consciousness into the Nindroid MechDragon but was defeated by Wyldfyre. His current status following his defeat is unknown.

History

History[]

Rebooted[]

The Surge[]

When the Digital Overlord took over New Ninjago City, P.I.X.A.L. built an army of Nindroids in the factory in Borg Tower according to the original plans of Dr. Julien to build Zane, but with many improvements. One of these Nindroids was Cryptor, who was specially modified and programmed to serve as a general.

The Art of the Silent Fist[]

Shortly after his creation, General Cryptor was sent by the Overlord to attack Garmadon's monastery with his army and P.I.X.A.L. where the ninja were taking refuge. However, he failed and the ninja escaped, so he called the Overlord who was angry on him.

Later Cryptor was sent by the Overlord to guard the Power Station. There, General Cryptor made fun of Min-Droid's small stature, and gave him his nickname. When the ninja came, he tried to stop them an almost destroyed P.I.X.A.L. Eventually, he encountered Zane and told him to get a move on with the future. When the Power Station was destroyed, he was powered down with the rest of the Nindroids and taken to Ed & Edna's Scrap N Junk to be scrapped.

Blackout[]

Cryptor, along with Min-Droid and the other Nindroids, were reawakened by Techno Wu using Electro-Cobrai in Ed and Edna's junkyard. Min-Droid attacked their mobile home with a crane, and General Cryptor rose on one of the junk piles. More Nindroid reinforcements were brought by the stranger, and Ed and Edna escaped by using bubble-powered jetpacks. Cryptor fought Zane and P.I.X.A.L. and ridiculed them until he was defeated and the ninja managed to escape. During their escape, Cryptor got a bubble in his eye and escaped with the stranger, Techno Wu, and the other Nindroids.

The Curse of the Golden Master[]

When the ninja were in the Serpentine village in the tomb of the Stone Army, Cryptor led the attack on them. When Skales Jr. was running to his mother, Cryptor tripped him and mocked him that he was the future of the fearless Serpentine, but he was kicked in the face by Kai. As he was about to battle the four ninja by himself, he was taken down by Kai, Cole, Jay, and Zane, but soon overpowered them easily and then left his Nindroids to finish them. However, when Cole discovered that they were powered by Electro-Cobrai on their back, Cryptor vanished and the ninja didn't notice his disappearance.

Enter the Digiverse[]

General Cryptor, Pythor, and Techno Wu broke into Borg Tower to stop the ninja from erasing the Digital Overlord, but before they could break in, they were attacked by an army of civilians, led by the Postman. They overpowered the civilians and started to go through the tower to the top floor. Then when they were nearly close enough to reach for the Techno Blades, Nya gave them a chance to surrender. Cryptor laughed and simply said that she was cute. However, when he, Pythor, and Techno Wu went to battle her with their Nindroids, her Samurai Mech arrived and she jumped inside of it. Cryptor and his Nindroids fought her while Pythor and Techno Wu decided to go for the Techno Blades to gain an advantage.

When the Digital Overlord was destroyed, Pythor escaped, Techno Wu was turned back to normal, and Cryptor and his Nindroids stopped battling Nya as they realized the ninja rebooted the system, meaning their master has been defeated. Cryptor told them to switch their power offline. He also said he looked forward to his second date with Nya as he and his Nindroids were forced to retreat.

Codename: Arcturus[]

Inside the Lost City of Ouroboros, General Cryptor told Pythor that his drones were questioning the continued existence of the Overlord ever since the ninja purged him from the Digiverse. Pythor said that the Overlord’s a bit consumed at the moment, but Cryptor warned with him that if he and his Nindroids found out that he was pretending that the Overlord was alive only to use the Nindroids, he would pay dearly. But then Pythor claimed that the Dark Lord would appear only when he wanted to, and threatened to re-program Cryptor if he commanded his Nindroids to attack.

Later, he was driving the convoy with Pythor and a few Nindroids to Ouroboros. Kai tried to put a stop to them but got occupied with battling Nindroids and escaping from bombs. Pythor had spotted Kai, and commanded for chains to be put on him. The ninja tried to save their chained friend, but the Nindroids were fighting them, including General Cryptor. He later got on Arcturus and traveled into outer space. However, he didn’t know that the ninja were with them.

The Void[]

General Cryptor commanded the rocket Arcturus, ordering his Nindroid Warriors to the controls of the rocket. Upon discovering that the ninja were aboard, he ordered his minions out to get rid of them. After they failed, he demanded that they take the rocket into Delta V's tail to lose the ninja. Eventually, they made it onto the comet, and Cryptor and the other Nindroids ventured to the Golden Weapons in their lunar rovers.

Soon, the ninja once again encountered them, and Cryptor and his minions escaped on the lunar rovers, with the heroes and a swarm of extraterrestrial beetles on their tail. Three rovers were lost as they jumped over a pit, with Cryptor commanding his Nindroids to try and hold the ninja off. Eventually, they reach the rocket, but the ninja thwart their escape and are soon stuck with the beetles, who are devouring the rocket. Cryptor and his Nindroids used their jets to fly off of the comet along with the melted Golden Weapons back to Ninjago.

The Titanium Ninja[]

Cryptor was present in Borg Tower during the transformation of the Overlord into the Golden Master. While they were in the factory, Cryptor released Cyrus Borg so he could watch his city fall and bow to his new master. When Borg wondered aloud what he could do, Cryptor mockingly said that he should already start bowing.

Later, he was attacking Nya as they both began to battle with their bare hands, Cryptor claiming that she kicked like a little girl. In return, Nya threw continuous hits at the Nindroid, but he simply pushed her off with one hand in mere seconds, aware that she couldn't use her Samurai Mech.

Cryptor soon gained the upper hand, but Nya saw Min-Droid behind him. Thus, she said that Min-Droid thought Cryptor was short of a hard drive. Irritated and unaware of the Nindroid behind him, Cryptor began to insult Min-Droid, claiming that he had met toasters more intelligent than him. Moments later, an enraged Min-Droid got his general's attention and punched him in the chest. Abandoning Nya for the moment, Cryptor began fighting his diminutive subordinate, distracting them both from the main battle until Zane defeated the Golden Master. Cryptor and Min-Droid were frozen mid-battle, shattering into pieces and sending their souls to the Departed Realm.

Legacy[]

Some years after he was destroyed, a statue of Cryptor was placed in the Ninjago Museum of History's Hall of Villainy exhibit alongside many of the other villains the ninja have fought over the years.

Day of the Departed[]

Due to the machinations of Master Yang, Cryptor and several other villains—Samukai, Kozu, Chen, and Morro—were freed from the Departed Realm, with their ghosts inhabiting the mannequins created in their image. After some initial bickering, such as Cryptor mocking Kozu over his defeat, the group was informed by Yang that they would return to the Departed Realm after the Yin-Yang Eclipse unless they used their new Departed Blades to dispatch an enemy. At that moment, Pythor appeared and greeted Cryptor as his old friend while he appeared neutral to the greeting. Armed with a weapon in the image of a Techno Blade, Cryptor refuted Samukai's claim to destroy Zane by stating that the Nindroid was his to destroy, being his clone; Pythor, who had joined the group, remarked that Cryptor was technically Zane's clone.

After reviving a small group of Nindroids from the Hall of Sidekicks to serve as backup, Cryptor found Zane in the Birchwood Forest and smashed the statue of Dr. Julien, Zane's creator. As he did so, he mused that the old scientist was his creator, in a way. The Nindroids then attacked Zane in force, with Cryptor confident in his chances of victory given that he knew all of Zane's moves, even those that Zane had learned since Cryptor's demise. However, Zane soon bested the lesser Nindroids, and when Cryptor faced him alone and declared his knowledge that the Master of Ice would "never give up," Zane did just that. His backing away from the struggle left Cryptor off-balance, and the Nindroid General inadvertently struck himself with his Departed Blade, resulting in his return to the Departed Realm.

Between series[]

When the Merge occurred, the Departed Realm and Cursed Realm were merged into one combined land known as the Spectral Lands. While most departed souls moved onto the next stage of existence, Cryptor stayed in the Spectral Lands for unknown reasons.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

Prior to The Mech-Master 10,000[]

After stealing Cryptor's schematics from Borg Industries under Dr. LaRow's orders and thanks to Wally, the Loyalists rebuilt the general's body in their hideout.

The Mech-Master 10,000[]

With Cryptor's body fully rebuilt, the Loyalists powered it up, which allowed his soul to leave the Spectral Lands and inhabit it. In the absence of LaRow due to her incarceration in Imperium, Cryptor took charge of the Loyalists and Operation Mentax.

Prior to Ancient History[]

Cryptor and the Loyalists found and occupied the cloud factory to repair and hide the Nindroid MechDragon for the endgame of Operation Mentax. As P.I.X.A.L. was hunting down Cryptor, she found the cloud factory, where the MechDragon attacked her and damaged her.[2] Later, because the Loyalists need the Mind Crystal for Operation Mentax, Cryptor stole a map of the passages under Old Town from the Explorer's Club by using his laser to precisely cut through its case.[3]

Ancient History[]

While the ninja were trying to stop Lobbo's rampage through Old Town after he swallowed rotten casseroles, Cryptor hid in the crowd and shot the rear wheel of Frak's Battle Bike with his laser vision, which caused it to stall.

Now with the map in their possession, Cryptor and the Loyalists began digging under Old Town with pickaxes in search for the Mind Crystal. After the Loyalists captured Frak and Reginald Slitherbottom in the Ninjago Sewers, they were brought to the mine, where Cryptor interrogated them. After distracting him with false information, Reginald was able to free himself and Frak from their restraints. Cryptor ordered the Loyalists to continue searching for the Mind Crystal while he fought Reginald and Frak. After they escaped the sewer, he followed them out and continued fighting them while attacking civilians and damaging the surrounding buildings with his lasers. As the fight continued, he shot a laser at a pair of fleeing civilians, which Reginald intercepted and body blocked but was knocked unconscious by the blast. Enraged, Frak violently attacked Cryptor and knocked him into a wall. Immediately afterward, he was informed by a Loyalist that the Mind Crystal had been obtained. Having what they came for, Cryptor and the Loyalists left via their hover chariots.

Prior to 100 Eyes to See Your Soul[]

Cryptor added a homing device into the each of the Cocoons so the ninja can get easily distracted with destroying them while he and the Loyalists retrieve the comatose Beatrix from Borg Tower with the help from Wally.

100 Eyes to See Your Soul[]

As Wally was about to get Beatrix out of Borg Tower, Cryptor arrived with the other Loyalists and cut a hole through the building's wall with his laser, saying that they don't have time for obsequiousness, which Wally agreed. Then, Cryptor left the building with the Loyalists after retrieving Beatrix.

All Wizards Are Liars[]

In the Ninjago Sewers, as Cryptor was looking at the unconscious Beatrix, the Loyalists arrived with reports that they couldn't get everything they need from the junkyard, but think they can still perform the mind transfer. In response, Cryptor hopes they are able to, and says their punishment will be severe if they can't.

The Summit[]

While Zeatrix was talking to the young ninja, Cryptor watched them from their hideout in the sewers. He said that the Merged Lands will finally crumble under their boots today as he focused the screen on Zeatrix, hailing the Good Empress as he watched her sister.

Later, while the young ninja were fighting off against the Loyalists to save Zeatrix, Cryptor arrived in his hover chariot with the other Loyalist, commanding them to attack the ninja. After telling his army to leave no one alive, Cryptor fights off against Riyu, causing him to crash into a wall after he shot him with his laser. As Cryptor was about to shoot Arin, Zeatrix returned and hit Cryptor, knocking him. After the Loyalists stunned Zeatrix and captured her again, Cryptor said they have what they need and commands them to retreat, escaping with their hover chariots while taking Zeatrix with them, unaware that Sora climbed one of the vehicles.

Desperately Seeking Sora[]

After arriving in their hideout in the sewers and putting Zeatrix in a mind transfer machine that Beatrix is connected to, Cryptor orders one of the Loyalists to begin the exchange, activating the Mind Crystal, which started to swap the sisters' minds. After one of the Loyalists caught Sora, Cryptor welcomes her to his lab and bids her goodbye, as the Loyalists get ready to shoot Sora with their Imperium Swords. However, Cryptor stopped them, saying they will need Sora's body for Beatrix's mind if the transfer fails, which annoyed the ninja. Suddenly, he realized there are intruders in the cloud factory thanks to the security camera and orders the Loyalists to continue the procedure without him as he transferred his consciousness to the Nindroid MechDragon, leaving his body inactive, much to Sora's confusion. As the MechDragon got activated, the other two ninja started to get worried. Then, the machine’s eyes lit up, making them to realize that it's Cryptor.

Dangerous Connections[]

After fully transferring his consciousness to the MechDragon, Cryptor stood up. Euphrasia said this is bad, which Cryptor confirmed while including that it's for them. Then, he started attacking them with the MechDragon's turrets, causing them to hide. After Cryptor says they will not stop them, Riyu attacked him, forcing the general to pull out the MechDragon's laser sword fight him. As Riyu fought against him, P.I.X.A.L. told the ninja what happened, saying the draconic mech attacked her when she tracked it down. Then, Wyldfyre charged towards Cryptor with her Transforming Dragon Mech, knocking the Nindroid and angering him. Cryptor continued to fight Riyu and Wyldfyre, eventually grabbing Wyldfyre's mech and pinning it to the ground. As the MechDragon was about to shoot her down, Euphrasia stops him by throwing boxes at him, which caused both her and P.I.X.A.L. to get swallowed by the mechanical menace. Later, Cryptor realized that it's time to go to the Summit of the Merged Lands and departed from the cloud factory, as Wyldfyre and Riyu watched him.

After Zeatrix told the audience that she is actually Beatrix, who swapped minds with his sister, Cryptor arrived at the Old Town Courthouse and cut a hole through the wall, allowing him and the Cocoons to enter the building. The Acona tried to fight off the MechDragon by shooting arrows at it, only for the robotic dragon to swallow and trap them as well. Then, Cryptor shifted his focus to Sora and Arin, saying that he should have done away with them when he had the chance before roaring at them. Thankfully, Wyldfyre arrived in her mech and launched him to the sky, announcing that it's round two.

While flying, Cryptor continued to fight off against Wyldfyre. As she was holding one of the MechDragon's wings, Wyldfyre realized her powers are back, blasting the wing off with them. Then, Euphrasia used Wind to rip open the giant machine from the inside, allowing herself, P.I.X.A.L., and the Acona to escape. Continuing the fight against the ninja, Cryptor got ready to strike Wyldfyre with his laser sword. Thankfully, Riyu arrived and cut the MechDragon’s other wing, causing it to fall into the courthouse while the Nindroid's consciousness leaves the mech, deactivating all of the Cocoons.

It is unknown what happened to Cryptor after the Summit of the Merged Lands, although his body being missing from his lab indicates his escape.

Personality

Personality[]

Cryptor is a very cruel, supremely arrogant, and ruthless Nindroid due to his built-in ego program. He treats his enemies very cruelly and derisively, as he often ridicules his opponents and always mocks them. This characteristic of him had gone so far that he even mocked a member of his army only because he was smaller and even slandered him, even though he thought he did not know about it. Cryptor was basically cruel to anyone he thought was less than him. He ridiculed Zane because he was much older and didn't seem to like women because he believed they weren't meant to fight. Cryptor also seems to treat everything as war, pridefully declaring his intent to mercilessly fight everyone in the Merged Lands. Nevertheless, after his reactivation, he exhibited a strong allegiance towards Beatrix and he labored to restore her mind to a new body, hailing her as the Good Empress.

Appearance

Appearance[]

General Cryptor is similar to a Nindroid Warrior in terms of appearance and clothing but with the addition of curved black pauldrons joined by a cybernetic breastplate of the same color with red circuitries and red and white buttons. His eyes glow red, his torso has slightly different mechanical detailing from a normal Nindroid's, and his silver robotic arm is on the right side instead of the left. He also has purple circuitry-like marks near his left eye.

After being accidentally revived by Cole as a ghost in Day of the Departed, he had a green glow like the other villains in the special. However, in the alternate version of the special, he has dark green glowing skin.

After being rebuilt by the Loyalists, his left silver robotic arm is visible instead of his right, and he lacked his breastplate and right pauldron. He additionally wore a shorter robe, with the sleeve now moved to his right arm, and a silver left pauldron.

Weapons and abilities

Weapons and abilities[]

Abilities[]

  • Leadership: As he was built to act as a general, Cryptor naturally is programmed with leadership skills.
  • Airjitzu: Due to sharing some of his programming with Zane, Cryptor knows some of his moves including Airjitzu.
  • Twin Katana Proficiency: Cryptor is skilled at using twin katanas.
  • Vehicular Mastery: Cryptor is skilled at operating vehicles.
  • Nindroid Physiology: As the leader of the Nindroids, Cryptor is built with the most advanced technology available to the Digital Overlord, giving him the following abilities:
    • Superhuman Strength: Cryptor has superhuman strength, strong enough to pick up and throw humans and after being rebuilt, he was strong enough to fight the dragon Riyu.
    • Superhuman Speed: Cryptor can move at super high speeds.
    • Superhuman Durability: Cryptor has superhuman durability.
    • Invisibility: Cryptor can cloak himself from view.
    • Laserkinetic Attacks: Cryptor can attack with lasers and is said to have a faster firing rate than other Nindroid units.
      • Laserkinetic Vision: Cryptor can shoot lasers from his eye scope.
      • Laserkinetic Chest Blasts: Cryptor can shoot lasers from his breastplate powerful enough to blast doors away and push someone away if they attempt to block with a weapon. After being rebuilt by the Loyalists, he could directly shoot his lasers from his chest.
      • Laserkinetic Cutting: After being rebuilt, Cryptor could precisely cut through objects with his lasers, as he did to steal Reginald Slitherbottom's map of the tunnels under Old Town from its case and the wall of Borg Tower to free Beatrix.
    • Elemental Absorption: Cryptor can absorb elemental powers, such as Golden Power.
      • Strength Augmentation: By absorbing Golden Power, Cryptor can enhance his strength, though he never had the chance to demonstrate this.
    • Vacuum Adaptation: Cryptor's robotic nature allows him to survive in outer space with no protection.
    • Engine Protrusion/Jet Feet: The soles of Cryptor's feet are equipped with jet engines.
      • Jet Propulsion: Cryptor can use the jet engines in his feet to propel himself.
        • Flight: Cryptor can fly with his jet engines.
          • Outer Space Maneuverability: Cryptor’s jet engines are fully functional even in outer space, allowing him to freely move and fly through it.
    • Individual Detection: After being rebuilt by the Loyalists, Cryptor gained the ability to detect an enemy walking through his security systems from his Headquarters.
    • Hover chariot Calling: After being rebuilt by the Loyalists, Cryptor could call Hover Chariots to him via the button on his chest.
    • Consciousness Transferral: After being rebuilt by the Loyalists, Cryptor could transfer his consciousness into the Nindroid MechDragon, allowing him to control it.
      • Technokinesis: Cryptor appears to manipulate technology such as when he activated the control station for the Nindroid MechDragon.
      • Cheriakinesis: Cryptor could manipulate the Nindroid MechDragon's arms while controlling it.
        • Arm Swapping: Cryptor could switch the Nindroid MechDragon's arms for more humanoid robotic arms.
      • High-Speed Flight: Cryptor could fly at high speeds via the Nindroid MechDragon's wings while controlling it.
      • Ergo-Spathígenesis: Cryptor could create energy swords while controlling the Nindroid MechDragon.
      • Gut Imprisonment: While controlling the Nindroid MechDragon, Cryptor could imprison others in its stomach by swallowing them.
    • Electro-Aspídagenesis: In the video games, Cryptor can create an electric shield around himself.
  • Ghost Physiology (formerly): After dying and becoming a ghost, Cryptor gained the following abilities:
    • Absolute Possession: Cryptor could possess anything, such as objects or beings.
    • Levitation: Cryptor could levitate in the air as a ghost.

Weapons[]

  • Twin Nindroid Katanas: When he served as the general of the Nindroid Army, Cryptor dual wielded a pair of Nindroid Katanas that he carried on his back when not in use.
  • Techno Blade Departed Blade: After returning as a ghost on the Day of the Departed, Cryptor used a Departed Blade in the form of a Techno Blade given to him by Yang.
  • Twin Katanas: After being rebuilt by the Loyalists, Cryptor dual wielded a pair of katanas in an identical manner to his Nindroid Katanas.

General Cryptor is the robotic leader of the Nindroid army, and a servant of the Digital Overlord.

Unlike the Nindroids, who rarely speak, Cryptor talks all the time.

He loves to taunt and insult his foes in battle, and has even been known to criticize his own troops (saying they are as stealthy as a herd of hungry yaks).

Rebooted (Museum)[]

General Cryptor is the robotic leader of the Nindroid army, and a servant of the Digital Overlord. Unlike the Nindroids, who rarely speak, Cryptor talks all the time. He loves to taunt and insult his foes in battle, and has even been known to criticize his own troops (saying they are as stealthy as a herd of hungry yaks). Cryptor’s emotional programming is also more advanced than that of the Nindroids, and his quick temper can be used against him. Cryptor dismisses the Ninja as a serious threat to his army. That may be a mistake that comes back to haunt both him and the Digital Overlord.

General Cryptor was in command of the Nindroid army. He was the first Nindroid created, an upgraded version of Zane with enhanced programming and heavy armor. Unlike the other Nindroids, Cryptor had a strong personality. He was very conceited, loudmouthed and severely underestimated the power of the Ninja. This powerful Nindroid leader was destroyed after the defeat of the Golden Master.[5]

Day of the Departed[]

General Cryptor is the most advanced of all the Nindroids built by Cyrus Borg. He’s more capable, smarter, more powerful and, thanks to his built-in ego program, supremely arrogant. He loves to talk and his favorite subject is … General Cryptor. He holds the NINJAGO record for most consecutive hours spent talking about oneself. Most of the time, he’s talking to himself, since even the emotionless Nindroids start to cry from boredom after an hour or two.[6]

Etymology

Etymology[]

Cryptor's name is likely based on the word "cryptic" or "encrypt," although the name is also possibly derived from the computer virus "Gen-Cryptor."

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • Cryptor's Rebooted minifigure has a unique torso print. However, his render in the show has an identical torso design to the other Nindroids.
    • This is one of the few times in which a character's render in the show is less detailed and unique than a physical minifigure.
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, Cryptor was sent to the Departed Realm because he "must have a programmed soul like Zane and Pixal."[7]
  • When asked why Cryptor wasn't rebuilt like Zane was, Tommy Andreasen responded, "Who would want to rebuild that bastard?"[8]
  • The writers of Dragons Rising wanted to build up the young team to the point where they could split up the team with the young ninja to fighting a old villain, while old ninja fought a new threat. Cryptor was ultimately chosen for this, with the Time Twins and Clouse also being discussed to be brought back.[9]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • According to Tommy Andreasen, if Cryptor got in a fight with Lloyd, Cryptor would easily be defeated.[10]
  • In Shadow of Ronin, Cryptor is the most expensive of the Nindroids, costing 200,000 studs. However, if P.I.X.A.L. were to be considered a member of the Nindroid army, then she is the most expensive, costing 250,000 studs.
  • Cryptor appears as a boss in The LEGO Ninjago Movie Videogame in the training dojo "The Secret Factory" and can be unlocked as a playable character by achieving silver medal status or higher in the said dojo, which requires the player to accumulate at least 100,000 studs.
  • It is implied by Cryptor himself that he knows Airjitzu. In "Day of the Departed," Cryptor told Zane that he knew his every move before he even made it. Zane then responded with "Even this one?" as he did Airjitzu and landed on a tree branch. When Zane turned to look at Cryptor, he was already on the branch and responded with "If you know it, I know it."
    • This makes him the third villain to know Airjitzu, the first two being Yang and Morro.
    • Considering Zane and Cryptor share the same programming, this may also imply that he knows how to do Spinjitzu as well.
      • This could potentially make Cryptor one of the strongest villains as each time Zane gets strong, Cryptor will become even stronger.
  • Cryptor's return in Dragons Rising was subtly hinted by Chris "Doc" Wyatt in Bluesky, in which he mentioned that among Chen, Samukai, Cryptor, and Kozu, one of them remained in the Spectral Lands while some of the others moved on, refering to Cryptor.[1]
  • His Dragons Rising character model uses a similar face rig to Lloyd.

Relationships

These are the relationships of the former leader of the Nindroid Army and Loyalists, Cryptor.

Allies

Allies[]

The Overlord (formerly)[]

The Overlord was Cryptor's original master.

Cryptor was the general of the Overlord's Nindroids, being loyal to the former. However, this was not always the case because Cryptor did have doubts and threatened Pythor if he lied about their master only to be intimidated with the threat of reprogramming should he step out of line. When the Overlord revealed himself, Cryptor was excited to see him in person and followed his commands to destroy Ninjago.

Pythor (formerly)[]

“Ah, Cryptor, my loyal friend.”
— Pythor to Cryptor, "Day of the Departed"

As Pythor was an advisor to the Overlord and Cryptor was the general of the Overlord's Nindroids, they were often seen together and worked well. However, this was not always the case because Cryptor did threaten Pythor if he lied about their master, and Pythor intimidated him with the threat of reprogramming should he step out of line.

In "Day of the Departed," Pythor happily greets his old friend after Cryptor's revival, showing no ill will towards him. In return, Cryptor didn't show much emotion to Pythor but wasn't hostile to him.

Nindroid Army (formerly)[]

Cryptor was the general of the Nindroids.

They followed his command and attacked on his orders. Cryptor appeared to care for them, as he didn't want them to be lost on false orders and threatened Pythor if he lied to them about the Overlord's survival.

Min-Droid[]

“Min-Droid? That little piece of scrap isn't even fit to tighten my bolts. I've met toasters more intelligent than him.”
— Cryptor, "The Titanium Ninja"

Cryptor mocked Min-Droid and gave him his nickname. Min-droid was bossed around and thrown around by Crpytor despite Min-droid's combative abilities. In the final battle, Cryptor mocked Min-droid once more, which led to the two of them fighting. Eventually, they were both frozen and destroyed.

Loyalists[]

After the Loyalists rebuilt Cryptor's new body under Dr. LaRow's orders, Cryptor became the general of the Loyalists and used them as his henchmen to find the Mind Crystal and use it to transfer Beatrix's mind into Zeatrix's body and thwart the Summit of the Merged Lands.

Zane[]

“I know your every move. I know how you hit. I know how you will kick. And I know you will never give up.”
— Cryptor, "Day of the Departed"

Cryptor was Zane's clone and main enemy.

Cryptor constantly mocked Zane for being outdated, and Zane often lost in their matches. When Zane sacrificed himself, he destroyed Cryptor in the process as well, ending his terror.

During the Day of the Departed, Cryptor sought to kill Zane and destroyed a statue Zane made of his father. Zane was surprised to see him again and now having a more updated body, he showed himself to be more than a match for the undead Nindroid. Cryptor was impressed with Zane's new skill, though confident in copying his moves to fight him. However, Zane soon defeated him with tactical thinking and watched as he returned to the Departed Realm.

Nya[]

“You kick like a little girl.”
— Cryptor, "The Titanium Ninja"

Cryptor enjoys flirting with Nya to get her riled up, calling their fights "dates" while she is disgusted by him.

Nya fought against Cryptor in "The Titanium Ninja." They fought until Nya tricked him into angering Min-Droid and soon watched as he was destroyed.
