Overview

“Oh, I forgot Zane's father liked his toys.”
— Nya, "The Last Resort"

Tai-D[1][2] (also known as Gizmo[3][4]) is a robot created by Dr. Julien used to assist the inventor. Tai-D resided at Lighthouse Island for many years, keeping Julien company during his time there. Later, in the alternate timeline, it aided Jay and Nya when they were hiding from the Sky Pirates.

After the Great Flood, Tai-D and Echo Zane left the lighthouse and went on their own quest after Jay moved there.[5]

History

History[]

Prior to the series[]

At some point after his imprisonment, Dr. Julien created the robot for serving tea and cleaning.

The Last Voyage[]

After Wu, Misako and the ninja arrived at the lighthouse, Julien had Gizmo serve them tea. As Tai-D rolled around, the ninja were amazed by his abilities, with Jay trying to mess with Tai-D. The robot didn't take kindly to this and burnt Jay, warding him off.

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

The Last Resort[]

During Jay and Nya's visit, Tai-D was helping to clean up, even putting items back into Jay's bag when they were trying to unpack. Tai-D was the one who leads Nya and Jay to Echo Zane and played chess with him afterward, in which he sneaked pieces under the table, much to Echo Zane's confusion.

Later, upon Nadakhan's arrival, he tried to fight back the Sky Pirates alongside Echo Zane, but to no avail. He was eventually shut down by the Djinn when he struck the robot with the Djinn Blade.

Operation Land Ho![]

Echo Zane was in the process of repairing Tai-D when Jay arrived to recruit him.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

Separate Ways[]

Tai-D was seen through some stuff, Echo Zane was going through when a visitor came. Jay sees Echo Zane, who does not remember him. Jay tells him about the time that they will spend together.Later, Jay inspired Echo Zane to go out and become a hero Jay no longer was. Echo Zane then brought Tai-D with him and later was joined by the Falcon on his journey.[5]

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • His name in the set is a respelling of the word, "tidy."
  • Despite being in the show since 2012, he didn't appear in LEGO form until 2016.
  • In his concept art file name he is called "Robot butler".[6]
  • He is similar to the Robo Usher 3000, as both are small, wheeled robots built for assistance and companionship.
  • In the Lighthouse Siege product animation, he seems to be able to fly or levitate, something he has not done in the show.
  • While his head in the show looks like a 1x1 cone without a top groove, it's a round 1x1 open stud brick in the set. This is likely because printing is not possible on the former due to its more complex shape.
