The Lead Loyalist[1] is a Loyalist of high rank who led the search for the Weapon of Desolation in the Land of Madness.

History

History[]

Background[]

The Lead Loyalist lived in the Kingdom of Imperium during the reign of Empress Beatrix and was among the many Imperians who remained loyal to her after the Liberation of Imperium, believing that her reign brought prosperity to the former kingdom despite her murder of her father, cruelty towards dragons, outsiders, and her own people, and almost destroying the Merged Lands due to believing her defeat led to the downfall of the kingdom. She would later joined a society known as the Loyalists, quickly rose to a high-ranking position within it, and dedicated herself to aiding Beatrix in her quest to reclaim Imperium and restore its former glory.

The Grand Inquisition[]

The Lead Loyalist and the other Loyalists escaped from the Land of Madness using Hover chariots while also throwing down an electric bomb that malfunctions the vehicles.

Dying to Live[]

While Dr. LaRow and Beatrix were talking about the Weapon of Desolation in the Loyalist hideout, the Lead Loyalist arrived with two other Loyalists and gave the yellow fragment to LaRow, saying that they extracted it from the Tree of Madness. When LaRow asked if they were detected while getting the fragment, the Lead Loyalist said they had a run-in with the ninja, and also mentioned that they trapped them in a cave and probably won't be seeing them for a while. Dr. LaRow said that it had better be true and dismissed her.
