Overview

“Hey, you can tell all the stories you want when we return to the museum tomorrow to explain why you stole this.”
— The Police Officer to the ninja, "Child's Play"

The Police Officer,[1] also known as the Aquarium Guard,[2] is a policeman that patrols Ninjago City. He briefly was a guard at the Ninjago City Aquarium.

History

History[]

Legacy of the Green Ninja[]

Pirates vs. Ninja[]

After arresting the Destiny's Bounty pirates, he thanks the ninja for saving Ninjago City and questioned Jay if the Destiny's Bounty was their ship.

Ninjaball Run[]

He and another policeman (in two police cars) chase Skales, who is driving the Charter Bus. They bounce off the Skull Truck's Wheels. After a while, they give up and just try to win the race. This doesn't work well, and they run into one of the natives' igloos. The two policemen return at the end in an attempt to arrest Garmadon, but Skales and his Bus blocked the way, allowing Garmadon to escape.

Child's Play[]

He puts the ninja (as kids) under arrest for stealing.

Rebooted[]

The Curse of the Golden Master[]

He was a guard at the Ninjago City Aquarium. When he saw Pythor entering inside he tried to stop him but failed after he turned invisible and disappeared.
