“I am Ritchie. I serve my King, Atta the Ratta, for the glory of all rats. I will win the Key-Tana.”
— Ritchie in "The Meaning of Victory", "The Meaning of Victory"

Ritchie is a Whack Rat that competed in the Speedway Five-Billion. He's one of Unagami's former henchmen.

History

History[]

Prime Empire Original Shorts[]

The Meaning of Victory[]

Ritchie is seen in an interview talking about what it would be like if he could win the Yellow Key-Tana. He soon has to compete with other racers who want the spotlight as well.

Prime Empire[]

The Glitch[]

When the ninja and Okino were at the Forest of Discontent, Ritchie attacks Lloyd, and Richie and another Whack Rat attack the others. However, when Ritchie was tied up, Richie and the other Whack Rat escaped.

When Ritchie was questioned by Lloyd, he replies that he was ordered by Unagami to attack the ninja. Moments later, Ritchie tells Lloyd that he can help the ninja by showing them a shortcut, a glitch.

After Ritchie was freed and lured the ninja to a glitch, the Red Visors ambushed the ninja and attacked them, while Ritchie escaped.

The Speedway Five-Billion[]

At the Speedway Five-Billion, he encountered Jay and attempted to throw a torpedo attack, but failed, hitting a Chrome Dome instead.

Later, Ritchie, Richie, and another Whack Rat attack Nya by setting the Ninja Tuner Car's engine on fire.

After Racer Seven reached first place, Ritchie commands Richie and another Whack Rat to go after her. However, he and the other Whack Rats failed when Lloyd, Jay, and Nya collided with them.

Game Over[]

Richie was among the A.I. that were freed from Prime Empire through Unagami's Manifestation Gate. He took to terrorizing the streets of Ninjago City along with his fellow Whack Rats. He was then kicked out of his Quad Bike by Zane and had it stolen.

After Unagami aired out his grievances with Milton Dyer, Ritchie, along with the other Whack Rats, decided Ninjago wasn’t for them and went back through the Gate into Prime Empire.

The Virtues of Spinjitzu[]

Wisdom[]

Ritchie and the Whack Rats attempted to stop the ninja from finishing the Speedway Five-Billion, but the ninja were able to escape when they purposely lost and were teleported near the portal.

Crystalized[]

At some point, Ritchie exited Prime Empire and became a citizen of Ninjago City.

Dragon Form[]

Ritchie was one of the many former inhabitants of Prime Empire who helped the ninja defeat the Crystal Army.

Roots[]

Ritchie was among the many citizens cheering the ninja for their victory against the Overlord.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • In the sets, he is named after Peter Hausner, one of Ninjago's directors.[1][2]
  • Like all the other racers in "The Meaning of Victory", he had his name wrong in Ninjargon. In this case, Ritchie's name was Ratsq instead.
  • He is called Hausner in the sets, but is referred to as Ritchie in the credits and Richie in the synopses. Richie is what another member of the Whack Rats is called in the sets, likely meaning that either the show or the sets mixed up their names.
  • Along with Richie, the form of Ritchie is reused many times for generic members of the Whack Rats in Prime Empire and The Keytana Quest.
  • He looks similar to some of the generic Whack Rats in the show, with Ritchie having a different ponytail, purple hands, and orange pants while the generic Whack Rats having a mohawk, orange hands, and purple pants.
  • He appears in every set that includes the Yellow Key-Tana except 71712 Empire Temple of Madness.
