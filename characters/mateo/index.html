Mateo Christopher Garcia is a member of the Dream Chasers and a student in Francis B. Wooley Middle School.

History

History[]

Ninjago - Dream Team[]

Mateo, Izzie, Cooper, and Logan arrive in Janakai Village upon receiving Zoey's distress call, but tumbled the moment he got out of Cooper's car. As Zane wasn't convinced they were the cavalry, Mateo and Izzie showed off their Dream Crafting skills by turning Z-Blob into a robot and bringing Bunchu to life respectively before they start taking on Grimspawn, Grimwolves and Night Terrors. Noting the Night Terrors were circling Jan, Max, and their friends, Mateo and Zoey wonder if they're to prevent them crossing over to the Real World before the Night Hunter shows up and takes Jan hostage, with Max being dragged along when she attempted to rescue the latter, and another student grabbed by a Grimkeeper. Meeting the Ninja, who were confused by many questions before they discover they're just dream creatures, created by Jan in his version of Ninjago when he, Max and their friends were watching a Ninjago TV marathon, where Kai is the Master of Wind instead of Fire. Changing Z-Blob into his drone form, Mateo takes Kai and Logan while Jay went with Izzie, Lloyd, Zane, and Nya went with Cooper, and Cole went with Zoey to rescue Jan, Max, and their friend.

During the flight, Logan makes fun of Kai's Element switch before the latter blew his hat off, causing him transform into his rage form, resulting in them trading insults before Mateo breaks it up. He then brings the drone above the Night Hunter's ship to drop Logan on it before heading back to the others for Kai to keep the Destiny's Bounty from falling and Z-Blob to keep it afloat as Mateo dreamcrafts it to make it bigger while getting off, until the Bounty's thrusters were active again and rammed the ship back to the Grim Realm just as Logan had rescued Jan and Max.

The following morning at Francis B. Wooley Middle School, Mateo, Izzie, Cooper, Logan, and Zoey come across Jan, who wants to do more marathons, to their annoyance, as it would lead to an increase in missions within the Dream World.
