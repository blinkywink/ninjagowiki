Overview

“I, Princess Vania, of the Kingdom of Shintaro, do hereby solemnly swear to stand up to those who are cruel and unjust. To uphold the laws of the Kingdom and to protect and faithfully serve all who dwell above and below the mountain.”
— Vania's coronation speech, "The Son of Lilly"

Queen Vania is the current ruler of Shintaro and the daughter of Vangelis. She allied with the ninja against the Skull Sorcerer, who was revealed to be her father.

When the Crystal King invaded Ninjago City, Queen Vania and her army joined the The Resistance to help the ninja to defeat the Crystal army.

History

History[]

Early life[]

Years before meeting the ninja, Vania befriended a Geckle who she called "Mr. Sparkles". However, all the adults, including Vangelis, insisted that the Geckle was just part of her imagination.[1]

Master of the Mountain[]

Into the Dark[]

Vania arrives at the Throne Room to greet the ninja and she takes a special interest in Cole. She started getting close to Cole as they leave the Throne Room, causing Lloyd to get very suspicious of Vania.

During the night, Cole walks back to his room and hears and sound. He's able to hide and catches what he thinks is a purple monster, but it turns out to be Vania. She then tells Cole about Mr. Sparkles as she shows him to the entrance of a mine.

Along the way, they talk about the creator of the Dungeons of Shintaro, an evil wizard named Hazza D'ur, before stumbling upon an underground mining operation. Vania catches the Geckles, Munce, and Minos mining Vengestone and is heartbroken to see them enslaved. After seeing them be punished by the Skull Sorcerer, Cole tells Vania to head back to the palace to warn her father while he would take care of the wizard.

The Worst Rescue Ever[]

Vania arrives back and warns the ninja and Wu about what she just witnessed.

Inside the Throne Room, her father is very disappointed in her decision to venture into the mine. Despite King Vangelis telling the ninja to not go into the Mines of Shintaro, Vania gives them torches and shows them.

The Two Blades[]

Vania and Wu use cardboard cuts of the ninja to show the Winged Guards of Shintaro that the ninja are inside the kingdom although that's not the case. Vania hopes the ninja are fine, and Wu believes they will save Cole.

The Skull Sorcerer[]

Vania and Chompy save Cole when he was attacked by a gigantic spider. What Vania doesn't realize is that Chompy grew in size to scare off the spider.

They make it back to the surface to warn Wu who criticizes her for not following her word. She apologizes for her actions, and they head to the Throne Room to warn Vangelis.

They tell Vangelis about everything they saw, and he is disappointed in Vania for going into the tunnels despite telling her earlier to not do so. Cole talks about the Skull Sorcerer and the Skull of Hazza D'ur, so Vangelis reveals the skull and transforms into the Skull Sorcerer. Vania is shocked that her father would do such terrible things such as enslaving the Munce and Geckles. When Cole and Wu fall due to a trap door, Vania attempts to go after them, but her father threatens her saying if she were to rescue the ninja, she would no longer be Vangelis' daughter: despite this, Vania still jumps.

Dungeon Party![]

Vania meets up with Cole after the Earth Ninja finally wakes up. She then listens to the Lowly's story narrated by Korgran before watching Cole encourage them and proclaim them the 'Upply'.

Dungeon Crawl![]

Cole leads the Upply as well as Vania and Wu to try and find a way out, but he is unable to. Plundar begins to complain about Cole's leadership; however, Vania points out that Cole is a good leader and is trying to help.

After Cole discovers a way out, everyone follows Cole's lead as swim their way out of Rock-Bottom. While swimming, Vania gets scared of a skeleton, but Cole helps her stay calm.

The group reaches a lava field and they begin to cross it using the provided rocks. Nevertheless, they encounter the Lava Monster who stands in their way. Vania points out all of the fire balls the Lava Monster is spewing which allows Wu to defend the group. Eventually, they are able to defeat the Lava Monster and safely reach the other side.

Masters Never Quit[]

Vania and the rest of the group are chased by the Lava Monster. They find some mine carts and decide to ride it. After it's all over, they find themselves at the Heart of the Mountain. Vania explores the ancient temple and discover that Cole's mother performed the Spinjitzu Burst. She watches Cole try to perform it, but he fails at it multiple times. Cole tries to get aid from Wu, but his master is blunt and doesn't offer good advice. Vania criticizes Wu for not trying hard enough to help his student which makes Wu reflect on himself.

The Ascent[]

Vania was in the Fire Stone Mech along with Cole while everyone else was in the mine carts. During their journey, Vania feared the Lava Monster would return and Cole thought it wouldn't until they actually saw it. That's when Cole sped up the Fire Stone Mech and the group fled.

Vania watched as Cole battled the Dire Bats as well as the Lava Monster. She then instructed Cole to stop the mine carts as they were veering into the lava.

After making it to the surface, Vania was horrified to see Chompy locked up in a cage. With the help of Cole, Chompy is freed. The group is then stopped by the Winged Guards of Shintaro who call Cole a traitor. The two groups then proceed to fight.

While the Winged Guards of Shintaro were attacking Vania and the others, she was pleasantly surprised to see Chompy grow into a larger size. She ushered Cole and the others to run while she held off the guards. Vania tried to convince Hailmar that Vangelis was lying to him, but Hailmar charged at her.

The Son of Lilly[]

Vania was able to get Hailmar and the Winged Guards to ally with her and Wu before they storm the caves and help drive back the Awakened Warriors with Vania helping protect Gleck. Once Cole had destroyed the skull, Vania and Hailmar quickly caught him before he could collapse. When Vangelis tries to explain his actions, Vania simply tells Hailmar to get him out of her sight. Sometime later, a ceremony was brought on to coronate Vania as queen. Afterward, when the ninja are about to leave for another adventure, she bid Cole farewell with them promising to help each other in the future. Vania also accepted Lloyd's apology for distrusting her when they first met.

Seabound[]

Master of the Sea[]

Cole briefly returns to Shintaro and asks Vania to secure the Storm Amulet. She complies and secures it in a vault and tells Hailmar to boost the security, unaware that the one they have is fake.

The Turn of the Tide[]

Vania attends Nya's memorial after she merges with the ocean.

Crystalized[]

Dragon Form[]

Having received Cole's distress broadcast, Vania, along with Chompy, the army of Shintaro, the Munce and Geckles arrive to help fight against the Crystal army.

Roots[]

Vania, along with Chompy and the army, assist in rebuilding the monastery.

Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

LEGO Life description[]

As the daughter of King Vangelis, Princess Vania is the rightful heir to Shintaro. She's a huge fan of the ninja and is truly honored when they accept her invitation to her birthday party. She loves books, is strong-willed, stubborn, and has a tendency not to do what her father says. Little does she know that she will soon become part of the ninja's action-packed new adventures in the Dungeons of Shintaro.

Character Encyclopedia New Edition[]

Princess Vania often disagrees with her father King Vangelis, but she is still surprised to find out he is the Skull Sorcerer! She is even more shocked when Vangelis banishes her to the Vengestone mine. Vania teams up with the ninja and helps them bring about a brighter, fairer future for Shintaro.

Appearance

Appearance[]

Vania has white skin and mainly wears a white dress with lines of light blue across the apparel. In addition, she has long amber-colored hair which she usually wears tied up in a braided bun with another braid laid down on the top of her head. On her coronation, she gains a white robe and a large gold, white, and turquoise crown with a blue gem in the middle and turquoise tassels, along with gold shoulder pads that form a large white and gold collar around her neck and a pair of Shintaro Wings on her back.

Abilities and weapons

Abilities and weapons[]

Vania displays solid fighting skills with her main weapon being a Naginata. Although she can fight, she mainly goes into battle alongside her allies or Chompy.

Relationships

Relationships[]

Vangelis[]

“Vania? My daughter? All I did, I did for the glory of-”
“Hailmar, get him out of my sight.”
— Vangelis and Vania, "The Son of Lilly"

Vangelis is Vania's father. She was horrified when she found out that he was the Skull Sorceror and was devastated to learn that her father had always had a dark soul.

After his defeat, he tried to persuade her to let him go, but she coldly told Hailmar to take him away and imprison him. Vania subsequently took her father's place as ruler of their people.

Chompy[]

When Vania was a child, she acquired Chompy and the two played with each other. She would continue her relationship with her pet and even took Chompy into battle to save Cole. While Vania was underground, Chompy was locked up by Vangelis. After returning to the surface, Vania was shocked to see her pet in a cage but was also happy to reunite with her pet at the same time.

Vania was shocked to discover Chompy could turn into a gigantic dragon, though quickly used it to her advantage to force Hailmar into aligning with her and the ninja.

Once being crowned Queen of Shintaro, Chompy watched Vania in excitement.

Hailmar[]

“Then it is my great honor and privilege to pronounce you, Queen Vania, the rightful ruler of Shintaro.”
— Hailmar to Vania, "The Son of Lilly"

Being part of the royal family, Hailmar treated Vania with respect. However, when Vania returned along with Cole, Wu, and the Upply, Hailmar followed Vangelis' orders to arrest the ninja should they return to the surface. Vania tried to convince Hailmar that Vangelis was lying, but Hailmar wouldn't listen and charged at Vania. During their quarrel, Vania somehow convinced Hailmar and the two aligned with each other to stop Vangelis.

After Vangelis was defeated, Hailmar presented Vania with the crown and pronounced her Queen of Shintaro.

Ninja[]

“I am such a fan! I've read all about your adventures. It was my idea to invite you to my birthday.”
— Vania to the ninja, "Into the Dark"

Hearing about the ninja through stories, Vania decided to invite them to her birthday and was thrilled to see them in person. She asked questions about their adventures as well as the powers they possessed.

During the Battle for Shintaro Mountain, Vania assisted the ninja when she brought Hailmar and his army as well as Chompy. After they defeated Vangelis, the ninja watched Vania becoming the Queen of Shintaro.

When the Overlord became Crystal King and was attacking Ninjago, Cole, on behalf of the Ninja, broadcasted a message, which she answered and came to their assistance. After the Overlord's defeat, she helps them rebuild the monastery that her father had a part in its destruction.

Cole[]

“You don't need me. You're one of the strongest and bravest people I've ever met.”
— Cole, "The Son of Lilly"

Out of all the ninja, Vania took the most interest in Cole. Although she doesn't have a love interest with the Earth Ninja, she spent the most time with Cole and he earned Vania's respect.

Cole and Vania both discovered the Dungeons of Shintaro and when Cole fell through the Pit, she went after Cole despite Vangelis threatening her. Despite her attempts not working, she continued to stay alongside Cole and saved Cole from being eaten by a giant spider.

The two continued to build their relationship while at Rock-Bottom and during their ascent to the surface. When Cole was being criticized by others, Vania backed up Cole and often cited his excellent leadership and his ability to succeed if everyone else could be positive.

After defeating Vangelis, Cole watched Vania become Queen of Shintaro. She then waved goodbye and promised to help the ninja if needed.

When the Overlord attacked Ninjago, Cole broadcasted a message to her for assistance, which she immediately complied with, where she watched Cole and the other original Ninja achieve Dragon Form. After he took down her father once again, Cole embraced Vania.

Wu[]

“Master Wu? I hope you will forgive me saying this, but instead of standing around doing nothing, maybe you should be helping Cole.”
— Vania, "Masters Never Quit"

Vania built a solid relationship with Wu and spent time with Wu while they were at Rock-Bottom. However, she was upset when Wu didn't offer any advice to a struggling Cole and made Wu rethink the way he acted towards his students.

Vania assisted in their venture back to the surface as well as the Battle for Shintaro. After defeating Vangelis, Wu was proud to see Vania becoming queen as she promised equality for all.

Upply[]

Vania aligned with the Upply and together they were able to escape Rock-Bottom. Vania and the Upply continued to fight alongside each other during the Battle for Shintaro Mountain.

After the defeat of Vangelis, the Upply watched Vania being announced queen of Shintaro.

Geckles and Munce[]

When Vania was a little girl, she didn't really know that much of the Geckles and Munce except for the fact that Mr. Sparkles would play with Vania. Sometime later, she would see the Geckles and Munce enslaved by the Skull Sorcerer. She would fight alongside the two tribes during the Battle for Shintaro Mountain.

After the defeat of Vangelis, the Geckles and Munce watched Vania becoming Queen of Shintaro and promising equality for everyone.

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • She was originally named "Ayella."[2]
    • According to Tommy Andreasen, "The name Vania wasn't legally approved until the very last minute".[3]
  • She is the second Ninjago minfigure to have a gold-colored hairpiece. The first is Avatar Lloyd.
  • Both of her hairpieces were previously used by LEGO Disney characters who are also princesses. Her first hairpiece was used by Mulan, and her second hairpiece was used by Anna.
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, "‘Bubbly’ was the key word we used to describe her."[4]
  • When asked if Vania has cheekbones, Michael Knap replied, "That was the intention. But it could also have been facepaint markings. In the final product, they came across as check bones."[5]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • She is the second princess to appear in Ninjago, the first being Harumi.
    • She is also the second queen to appear on the show, the first is Murtessa.
  • She is the second daughter to have a father who was evil, the first was Skylor.
  • She is similar to the character with the same name in Granblue Fantasy, as both of them live in secluded kingdoms with small pet dragons who can also transform into a full-grown sized ones.
    • She is also similar to Rapunzel from Disney's Tangled, as they both have small reptilian pets, spunky, adventurous personalities, and parental figures who acted protective of them in order to hide a dark secret.
  • She appears to be a fan of Clutch Powers, as she mentioned his books Boobytraps and How to Survive Them and Famous Places That I Will Find Before Anyone Else in "The Real Fall" and "Masters Never Quit" respectively.
    • She often quotes some of the lines from his books.[6]
  • It is not confirmed whether she and Cole have feelings for each other and is up for audience interpretation.[7]
  • She shares some similarities with Benthomaar from Seabound:
    • They are both the youngest members of their families and serve the purpose of their respective seasons ally to the ninja.
      • Both also learn that another member of their family (Vangelis and Kalmaar) hid their darker intentions from them. (enslaving the Munce and Geckles and using Wojira to destroy the surface world)
        • During the end of their respective seasons, both allies disown their former family member and watch them be taken away or killed by their own allies. (Army of Shintaro and Wojira)
    • Both are members within a secluded kingdom's royal family.
    • Both allies try to convince the guards of their kingdom of the malicious intentions that their corrupted family member had at some point in the season, however Vania succeeds at this while Benthomaar does not until after Seabound.
  • According to Tommy Andreasen, she "has the weird power to conceal an already lit torch on her body", which is a joking reference to the time she produced two lit torches seemingly out of nowhere in "Into the Dark."[8]
  • Her story bears some resemblance to Zuko's story from Avatar: The Last Airbender. Both of them decide to help the heroes against the main antagonists, their fathers, who are the rulers of their home. In the end, their fathers get defeated, and they both replace them as the ruler.
  • Despite Vania becoming the new ruler of Shintaro and thus a queen, Cole still calls her a princess, and even "corrects" Blazey when she correctly refers to Vania as a queen.[9]
  • She has a very strong resemblance to Lady Bone Demon's true form, this can be seen as both have visible cheekbones, and white skin albeit the hair and the eyes color.
