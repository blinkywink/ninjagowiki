Overview

“Dusk is right. And if we're lucky, we can put a quick end to all of this!”
— Levi, Ninjago: Strike of the Serpentine

Levi was the Elemental Master of Gravity during the Serpentine War.

History

History[]

Spellbound (flashback)[]

Levi was a member of the Elemental Alliance, a partnership of Masters that fought against the brutal Anacondrai during the Serpentine War. He was present at the start of the war for the battle of Jamanakai Village, where he was eaten by the Anacondrai serpent.

After the Serpentine War[]

Levi used a Sacred Flute and escorted the Constrictai tribe into their tomb inside the Mountain of a Million Steps where they were sealed.[2]

The Hatching (flashback)[]

In a battle, Levi fought Krux. Krux used his Elemental Power against him and reversed time to know his next move.

Pause and Effect (flashback)[]

He watched as Wu told Maya and Ray he has a plan to defeat the Hands of Time. When Wu and Garmadon defeated the Time Twins, Levi restrained Acronix.

Lost in Time[]

The Time Twins traveled back in time when the Elemental Masters restrained the twins, but they went into the portal. Eventually, the Iron Doom landed nearby and the Masters were confused to see Krux and Acronix. They fought them and their army of Vermillion to keep them out of the monastery. When they were outnumbered, Ray"and Maya came to help. The Vermillion suddenly turned into snake form and fused inside the Iron Doom.

The Masters watched as "Ray" and "Maya" summoned the Fusion Dragon to defeat the Iron Doom. The Masters fought the Iron Doom as a distraction. When Ray, Maya, and Wu followed the twins into the vortex, Wu told the Alliance to drink Obscuritea to restore the future.

Etymology

Etymology[]

Levi is derived from the verb "levitate". However, it coincidentally is also a common Jewish surname.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Like several other members of the Elemental Alliance, Levi's name is finally revealed in the Ninjago: Strike of the Serpentine book - a decade after his debut in the show.
  • Levi is the third character in Ninjago to have been swallowed by a snake and survived, the first two being Wu and Pythor.
    • Chronologically, Levi was the first, having been consumed and liberated during the Serpentine War, decades before the release of the Great Devourer.
  • His trading card picture mistakenly uses Master Garmadon's face instead of his own.
  • He uses:
    • The same face as Gandalf from LEGO The Lord of the Rings but in yellow.
    • A multi-colored version of the Roman Soldier and Roman Commander's helmet from the LEGO Collectible Minifigures theme.
    • Some of Kai's ZX torso design.
