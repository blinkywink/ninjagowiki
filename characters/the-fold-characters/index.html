Overview

This article is about the band in Ninjago City. For the real-life band of the same name, see The Fold.

The Fold is a pop-rock band in Ninjago City.

History

History[]

Green Destiny[]

The Fold were singing The Weekend Whip during the ninja's celebration after they defeated Garmadon.

The Weekend Drill (flashback)[]

While Cole was fighting the Giant Stone Warrior and Killow, The Fold were at Kryptarium Prison.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • Based on their appearances, the band members are as follows:
    • Dan Castady is the main singer in red.
    • George Castady is the guitarist with black hair.
    • Matt Pittman is the bassist with brown hair.
    • Mark Rhoades is the percussionist.
  • They share several similarities with the Hageman Brothers. Both groups are based on real people, and both groups were briefly incarcerated in Kryptarium Prison.
  • Their designs are based on the clothing they wore in the original music video for The Weekend Whip.
  • Before their physical appearance in Season 9, The Weekend Whip was played in-universe in "The Royal Blacksmiths", "The Art of the Silent Fist", "The Invitation", "The Hatching," and "True Potential." This means that retroactively, The Fold (as a band within the world of Ninjago) have appeared since Season 1 via audio recordings.
    • Since The Weekend Whip also appeared in The LEGO Ninjago Movie, it may mean that they also exist in the movie universe.
  • They are not the first band who worked for a LEGO theme to appear as LEGO minifigures. In 2016, the theme song for Nexo Knights, "Unbreakable", saw the band, Carpark North, becoming minifigures.
  • Samukai is pictured on Matt Pittman's shirt.
  • In the TV show animation, Matt Pittman has Lloyd's post-movie face with black eyes.
  • The "Ten for Ninjago" album cover character renders use designs from other LEGO themes and Ninjago characters, such as Cole and Kai's faces from The LEGO Ninjago Movie, the Driver's mouth from the Dino theme, and the Mayor's face from the Fusion theme.
