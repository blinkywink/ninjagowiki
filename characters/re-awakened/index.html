The Re-Awakened were undead human skeletons resurrected to fight as the Skull Sorcerer's army. After the Battle for Shintaro Mountain and the Skull Sorcerer's defeat, some of the Re-Awakened were resurrected by various types of magic unleashed by the Merge[2] and now live as sentient beings alongside the other denizens of the Crossroads, the Underworld, and other locations after the Merge.

History

History[]

Prior to the series[]

At some point before the Battle for Shintaro Mountain, an unknown army of warriors perished in the Dungeons of Shintaro, where their bones remained. Many years later, King Vangelis resurrected them as undead skeletons through the Dark Magic of the Skull of Hazza D'ur.

Master of the Mountain[]

Into the Dark[]

The Re-Awakened were patrolling the Mines of Shintaro. When Cole decided to intervene, the Re-Awakened attack, but Cole defeated them. However, the Skull Sorcerer used the Skull of Hazza D'ur to resurrect the Re-Awakened and had them capture Cole.

The Worst Rescue Ever[]

The Re-Awakened monitor the work of the Geckles and Munce while forcing Cole into labor. When Cole is caught talking to a Geckle, a Re-Awakened uses its whip to force him to work.

Later, the Re-Awakened serve porridge to the inmates and watch over the tribes.

The ninja arrive at the Dungeons of Shintaro and defeat a couple of Re-Awakened to rescue Cole. When the reinforcements arrive, the ninja easily defeat the skeletons; however, the Skull Sorcerer uses the Skull of Hazza D'ur to resurrect his army and take down the ninja.

The Two Blades[]

While the ninja are working, Cole catches sight of a Re-Awakened whipping a Mino. Angered, he throws his pickaxe at the skeleton. The Re-Awakened loses its whip and attempts to punish Cole, but Nya grabs it and Cole destroys it. Nevertheless, more reinforcements arrive and the ninja are imprisoned.

The ninja begin their escape plan by freeing themselves and destroying the skeletons surrounding them. They then free everyone, but more skeletons arrive. The ninja buy time by pouring Glop on the warriors and releasing the Minos.

Grief-Bringer[]

The Skull Sorcerer flies to the Dungeons of Shintaro and demands the Re-Awakened to open the door to Grief-Bringer's tomb. He then uses the Skull of Hazza D'ur to resurrect the former dragon.

During the truce meeting, the ninja spot Grief-Bringer as well as the Re-Awakened marching towards them. Lloyd and Zane try to hold back Grief-Bringer, but they are unable to and as they flee, the Re-Awakened throw their spears at them.

The Darkest Hour[]

The Re-Awakened follow everyone to the Geckle Strong-Cave. In order to allow the Munce to close the gate, the ninja buy time by fighting the Re-Awakened. When Murtessa finally breaks the chains, the ninja flee the fight.

Barricaded inside, the Re-Awakened use a ram to destroy the wooden door and with the help of Grief-Bringer, they destroy the door. The ninja strike a deal with the Skull Sorcerer to surrender themselves, but in exchange, the Munce and Geckles could be free. Some of the Re-Awakened head with the ninja, but the others stay behind. The Skull Sorcerer ends up double-crossing them and captures everyone.

The Upply Strike Back![]

Enslaved once again, the Re-Awakened observe all of the Munce and Geckles and whip anyone for stopping. A Re-Awakened is about to whip Murtessa, but Korgran punches him and hands the keys to free themselves.

During the execution, the Re-Awakened who was supposed to sound the gong to start the execution was sleeping, so the Skull of Hazza D'ur shoots a beam at it. Moments later, the Re-Awakened that was supposed to release Grief-Bringer was destroyed by Cole when he threw a rock at the skeleton.

As Cole is fighting the Skull Sorcerer, the Munce and Geckles free themselves and start an uprising. In addition, the Upply and ninja soon join and are forced to keep up with the Re-Awakened being resurrected.

The Son of Lilly[]

The ninja, Geckles, Munce, and the Upply continue to fight the Re-Awakened, but are overwhelmed by the number of skeleton warriors and the fact they keep resurrecting themselves at a quick pace. Luckily, Vania and the Army of Shintaro arrive and help fight the warriors.

While Cole is fighting the Skull Sorcerer, he unlocks the Spinjitzu Burst and destroys the Skull of Hazza D'ur. This causes almost every Re-Awakened to crumble to bones once more.

Magma Mayhem[]

Somehow, some of the Re-Awakened survived after Vangelis' defeat and were still loyal to him. They took Munce and Geckles, such as Menkes and Gnippo as prisoners, and unleashed three Magma Monsters to attack Shintaro. However, they were later defeated by Cole and Fungus.

Generosity[]

In the illusion, the ninja fought the Re-Awakened, hoping to get to the next portal. While fighting, the Geckles assisted the ninja by firing boulders at the warriors. Defeating most of the warriors, the ninja were about to leave, but the Re-Awakened surrounded the Geckles. The ninja defeated them as well before using the catapult to enter the next portal.

After the Merge[]

The Re-Awakened were reanimated by the various types of magic unleashed by the Merge[2] and became sentient beings. They relocated to the Crossroads and the Underworld. Two skeletons started kidnapping children from the Crossroads, sending then to work in the mines. Many of the Re-Awakened were tasked to move the Skeleton Village across Mysterium to secure the Passage to the Underworld from the living.

Crashing Together (flashback)[]

Two Re-Awakened child traffickers attempted to kidnap Sora, but she was rescued by her future best friend Arin.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Crossroads Carnival[]

One Re-Awakened was holding Moss-milk ice cream, implying that their war with the Munce and Geckles is over.

The Administration[]

In the Department of Reassignment, Prentis sends two Re-Awakened to the Cloud Kingdom, believing that's their homeland.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Blood Moon[]

Arin and Sora asked two Re-Awakened about the Night Ghost, but to no avail.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

Under the Light of a Mechanical Moon[]

After finding out that Ras had used the Forge of the Forgotten, the Greenbone Leader, accompanied by some Bone Guards and Re-Awakened, came to the Forge to stop him. However, the tiger wasn't impressed by this resistance and ultimately defeated them.

Official description

Official description[]

The ninja have almost as much history with Skulkins as they do with snakes, but they've have never seen anything like this before! Brought back to life by the Skull Sorcerer, the Awakened Warriors can rebuild their bony bodies whenever a part breaks off them, making them almost invincible![3]

Appearance

Appearance[]

The Re-Awakened are comprised of black bones and have no flesh covering their skeletons. They have glowing, green light that emits from their eyes and nasal cavities, which disappears whenever they lose the magical presence that animates them. Moreover, they all wear helmets with a bone placed on top, though following the Merge, they have been seen wearing conical hats or no hats at all, as they are no longer a strictly warrior race.

Powers and abilities

Powers and abilities[]

For most characters, defeating the Re-Awakened is very effortless due to being made out of bones. They would shatter on contact with any weapon or fighting ability.

Due to the power of the Skull of Hazza D'ur, they are one of the tougher villainous factions due to the ability to resurrect themselves after being destroyed. Unlike the Vermillion, opponents are unable to separate any pieces of the Re-Awakened since they will automatically rebuild themselves. This makes it very difficult for opponents since they'll have to fight a virtually endless wave of Re-Awakened. It gets even tougher when there are a horde of Re-Awakened as they can overwhelm a group of opponents as seen with the ninja in their first attempt to rescue Cole.

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • In the series, they use Vengestone spears and whips, while in the sets they exclusively use silver katanas.
  • They used the same pieces as Legacy Kruncha but have black bones added to their helmets.
    • The helmets they wore bear a striking resemblance to Garmadon's original helmet.
  • The Re-Awakened are similar to Dry Bones from the Mario franchise. Both are skeletons and able to rebuild themselves after being defeated.
    • They are also similar to the undead Asgardians from Thor: Ragnarok, as they are both armies of black skeletons reanimated by green magic.
  • When asked if there is an origin story for the Re-Awakened and Hazza D'ur, Tommy Andreasen responded, "Yes. Only I know."[4]
  • Despite being the villainous faction of Master of the Mountain, only three of them appear in sets. This is because the Geckles and the Munce were also marketed as villains.
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • They are the second skeletal antagonists in the series after the Skulkin.
    • Despite having no connections to them, they share some similarities, with both groups being undead skeletal warriors that are known for existing for several years before the events of the series.
    • They dislike being mistaken for the Skulkin.[3]
  • They are referred to as "Awakened Warriors" in the sets.
    • They are also referred to as "Dark Skeltons" on the synopses of "Generosity" and on the LEGO website.[5]
    • In Ninjago: Dragons Rising, several Re-Awakened are shown in the Crossroads and the Underworld. However, the credits of "Crashing Together," they are mistakenly referred to as Skulkin.
  • They are also similar to the Vermillion as they are able to be rebuilt after being destroyed as well. However, the Re-Awakened can only be rebuilt by the power of the Skull of Hazza D'ur while the Vermillion can rebuild themselves.
  • "Magma Mayhem" suggested that some Warriors just survived Vangelis' defeat, which is similar to how the Giant Stone Warrior survived the Overlord's first defeat.
