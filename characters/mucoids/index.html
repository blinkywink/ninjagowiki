The Mucoids are a race of anthropomorphic snails originating from the Wyldness which now live in the Mother Garden.[1]

History

History[]

Background[]

The Mucoids originated from the Wyldness,[1] but moved or were moved to the Mother Garden. Millennia ago, the First Spinjitzu Master entrusted the Mucoids to protect the Pink Dragon Core until someone would request it from them to save the world, a promise the Mucoids strictly followed.

They Gather for the Feast[]

Zant-Tanz learns he is the Elemental Master of Plants and is invited to compete in the Tournament of the Sources. Later that night, Bleckt sends Attacker bots after the Ninja and Zur to get rid of Ras' top competition, and sending a third one after Ras himself to throw off suspicion. Although the robots fail, Tox identifies the weapon of one of the robots as a Gilba Thorn, something only found from the Sevateam plant in the Mother Garden. This causes everyone to believe Zant-Tanz was responsible. While he denies having to do with it, he agrees to leave, but not before telling everyone the true culprit was still out there.

Trivia[]

  • All Mucoid names are either palindromes or imperfect palindromes.
  • Certain types of salt from the other realms aren't deadly to the Mucoids.[2]
  • When he asked if the Mucoids' and anthropomorphic frogs' ancestors came from Chima or became humanoid through other ways, Chris "Doc" Wyatt replied with "sort of."[3]
    • He later confirmed that the Mucoids originated from the Wyldness.[1]
