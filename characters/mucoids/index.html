Overview

“They hail our forefathers. We have been taught from one generation to the next that one day our vigil over this core would end when heroes claim from the temple, asking permission to save the world. Now our work, and the work of those who came before us, is fulfilled.”
— Zant-Tanz, "They Call It Doom"

The Mucoids are a race of anthropomorphic snails originating from the Wyldness which now live in the Mother Garden.[1]

History

History[]

Background[]

The Mucoids originated from the Wyldness,[1] but moved or were moved to the Mother Garden in Ninjago. Millennia ago, the First Spinjitzu Master entrusted the Mucoids to protect the Pink Dragon Core until someone would request it from them to save the world, a promise the Mucoids strictly followed.

Crashing Together (flashback)[]

A Mucoid citizen was walking in the Crossroads when Sora arrived there for the first time.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Crossroads Carnival[]

Grab-Barg participatеd in a pie-baking contest with Arin during the Crossroads Carnival.

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

Grab-Barg was judging the Zane-alike contest for Zane Day. Also, Tope-Epot was a participant in the contest.

They Call It Doom[]

Lloyd and Arin encountered Mucoid Warriors after arriving in the Mother Garden in search of the Pink Dragon Core. When Lloyd suggested asking its protectors first for the core, the Mucoids revealed themselves and their general, Zant-Tanz, granted them the core.

The Administration[]

Zant-Tanz was building a tower out of stones when a Portal opened beside him and Lloyd, Arin, and Zane rushed out.

The Power Within[]

Grab-Barg was saved from a rift by Zane during the MergeQuake Storm.

The Elemental Mechs[]

The Mech Master[]

Chef Grab-Barg participated in, and won as usual, the annual Pie-Making Contest. During The Mechanic's heist at the Mech Master 5000, Arin was thrown into Grab-Barg's pie stall, prompting the chef to walk over with an annoyed look until Arin admitted that his pies really were the best. Arin then bought several pies from Grab-Barg to share with the other ninja.

A Pain in the Mech! (flashback)[]

Kai purchased noodles from Grab-Barg during the Crossroads noodle cook-off.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

To Mysterium[]

A Mucoid citizen was walking in the Crossroads.

They Gather for the Feast[]

Zant-Tanz learns he is the Elemental Master of Plants and is invited to compete in the Tournament of the Sources. Later that night, Bleckt sends Attacker bots after the Ninja and Zur to get rid of Ras' top competition, and sending a third one after Ras himself to throw off suspicion. Although the robots fail, Tox identifies the weapon of one of the robots as a Gilba Thorn, something only found from the Sevateam plant in the Mother Garden. This causes everyone to believe Zant-Tanz was responsible. While he denies having to do with it, he agrees to leave, but not before telling everyone the true culprit was still out there.

Dragons Rising, Season 3[]

The Hollow Ones[]

When Gandalaria arrived in Abracalabria, some Mucoids were present.

Next day, a Mucoid was in the crowd, thinking that Arin, Sora and Ras were the ones who attacked them last night, only for Gandalaria to come and defend the ninja. As Gandalaria was explaining the trio what happened, another spectral invasion started, causing inhabitants to get worried, including the said Mucoid.

Human Resources[]

During the spectral invasion in Abracalabria, a Mucoid hid behind the counter.

Between You and Lee[]

Some Mucoids were present in the Community of Lee, some of them even joining the New Neighbor Orientation.

Dragons Rising, Season 4[]

The Heartbeat of all the Realms[]

During the Loyalist assault some Mucoids Imperians were present in Imperium, witnessing Beatrix's speech.

Birthright[]

As Beatrix and her forces entered the City of Imperium, Mucoids and the other Imperians started to run to as the Elemental Monsters and Loyalists attacked them. After the Loyalist assault, a Mucoid citizen witnessed Percival and the ninja arresting the remaining Loyalists.

Trivia[]

  • All Mucoid names are either palindromes or imperfect palindromes.
  • Certain types of salt from the other realms aren't deadly to the Mucoids.[2]
  • When asked if the Mucoids' and anthropomorphic frogs' ancestors came from Chima or became humanoid through other ways, Chris "Doc" Wyatt replied with "sort of."[3]
    • He later confirmed that the Mucoids originated from the Wyldness.[1]
