Overview

“I don't need Elemental Power to be a Master of Technology!”
— The Mechanic to Sora, "The Mech Master"

The Mechanic is a well-known professional criminal and a former employee of Master Chen who was at one point imprisoned in Kryptarium Prison. During the Second Serpentine War, the Mechanic was affiliated with the Anacondrai Cultists, repairing noodle trucks for the underground crime organization. After Chen's army was banished, the Mechanic went out of business since his employer has been cursed. Due to his involvement with Chen, he was locked up in Kryptarium, where he earned the name "The Mechanic."

History

History[]

During and after Tournament of Elements[]

Before Master Chen's banishment to the Cursed Realm, the Mechanic made money repairing the noodles trucks of the Anacondrai Cultists. After their defeat at the Corridor of Elders, the Mechanic was arrested and sent to Kryptarium Prison where he swore revenge on the ninja with the intent of tearing Zane apart for parts.[1]

The following events were undone due to Jay's final wish, and thus, did not happen in the current timeline.

Skybound[]

Enkrypted[]

When the ninja arrived at Kryptarium for being framed for a crime spree, the Mechanic opened his and several other inmates’ cell doors, leading them to confront the ninja. Explaining his situation to Lloyd, he demanded they hand over Zane to use as spare parts in exchange for the grievances he believed the ninja had caused. However, before he could take any action, Warden Noble arrived, ordering him and Lloyd to shake hands.

During the following meal in the cafeteria, the Mechanic confronted the ninja again but was dismissed by them as at most a fourth-tier bad guy. Infuriated, he started a fight during which the Mechanic dragged Zane away, determined to take him apart. However, an electric shock orchestrated by P.I.X.A.L. sent the Mechanic flying back and he was returned to his cell by the guards before he was able to inflict any further damage to the Nindroid.

The following events occurred after Jay made his final wish, and thus, happened.

Legend of the Brown Ninja[]

Following the Ghost invasion, the Mechanic developed a remote control that granted him access to all of Kryptarium's prison cells. Rather than escape, the Mechanic instead built himself a new business within the prison, offering his fellow inmates their chance for freedom in exchange for payment. After releasing Wyplash, the mysteries surrounding these escapes from Kryptarium reached the public eye, drawing in the attention of the Ninja. To prepare for their arrival, the Mechanic obtained and coated several cells with Vengestone, developing a complex plan in the process to defeat them.

Soon enough, the Ninja were called to Kryptarium to assist in identifying the culprit behind the recent prison breakouts, and after they entered the "super mean and dangerous section," they were greeted by the Mechanic. Introducing himself, the Mechanic revealed that when the Ninja defeated Chen, they put him out of a job. Before he could finish his sentence, however, Nya and Jay finished it for him, somehow already knowing what his next words were to be. When the Ninja demanded to know where Noble was, the Mechanic activated his remote control and freed all of the other inmates to fight against the ninja.

While the ninja fought back against the inmates, the Mechanic cornered Zane into the vengestone coated cell, expressing his intention to harvest Zane's parts. However, an electric shock orchestrated by P.I.X.A.L. sent the Mechanic flying back. This time however, without Noble and his guards intervening, the other inmates were able to overwhelm the ninja, forcing them into the vengestone cell as the Mechanic recovered and slammed the doors shut with his remote control. The inmates' victory would be short lived however, as Noble and Dareth then entered the section of prison, interrupting the fight. Noble thanked the ninja for their help in uncovering the culprit behind the recent escapes and moved to confiscate the Mechanic's remote. Unfortunately, Dareth had sent out all of the prison's guards to lunch, having mistaken them for extra camera crew. Realizing that the warden was effectively defenseless, the Mechanic laughed and grabbed both men by their collars.

The Mechanic then handed off Dareth to the other inmates, intending to begin torturing the Brown Ninja first because of his seemingly close relationship with the Ninja, ordering the others to throw Noble into a cell. As the inmates jeered, the Mechanic overheard the imprisoned Ninja refer to Dareth as the "Brown Ninja" and offered first pickings to the Giant Stone Warrior. After Dareth managed to trick the Giant Stone Warrior into knocking itself out, the Mechanic turned to Soto and told him it was his turn to exact his revenge. And after Soto made a fool of himself, the Mechanic had enough and decided to torture Dareth himself.

Although Dareth tried to bluff his way out of danger again like he had with the Giant Stone Warrior and Soto before him, the Mechanic was not as weak minded and ignored the Brown Ninja's words. Instead, he grabbed Dareth by the throat with his robo-arm and began choking him as he lifted Dareth into the air. While threatening Dareth with grevious bodily harm, Dareth took the opportunity to quickly hit the release button on the Mechanic's remote control, freeing the Ninja from their vengestone cell. With their powers restored, the Mechanic could do nothing but watch as the Ninja decimated the rest of the prison inmates, and he himself would be upper-cutted by Jay.

Sons of Garmadon[]

The Mask of Deception[]

The Mechanic manages to somehow break out of Kryptarium Prison and fights Kai and Zane with a new flamethrower robo-arm at Ninjago Harbor. However, Zane manages to freeze the villain in place.

Tales from the Monastery of Spinjitzu[]

The Weekend Drill (flashback)[]

When Cole is riding his Earth Driller, Warden Noble calls him and informs him that there are problems in the Kryptarium prison. Cole guesses what happened and mentions the Mechanic and Sons of Garmadon.

Later, the Mechanic was sharing a cell with a Sons of Garmadon member at Kryptarium Prison, watching the fight between Cole and the Giant Stone Warrior. He later threw random objects at Cole, for defeating and placing the Giant Stone Warrior and Killow back into their cells.

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

The Absolute Worst[]

While in the exercise yard, a newspaper comes at the Mechanic, who reads it, and discovers that the ninja are gone before relaying the news to Ultra Violet, who tells him they breakout after lights out. Later that night after the guards turned off the lights, the Mechanic used his robo-arm to get the keys to his and Ultra Violet's cells before they escape through a tunnel she had dug in the prison.

Once they reached the sewers, the Mechanic and Ultra Violet argue about who they thought was the worst (Zane or Nya) before Fugi-Dove shows up, telling them it is Jay. Once they heard Ultra Violet's story about her capture, the Mechanic tells her and Fugi-Dove about the time Nya stopped him and later stole his Noodle Truck of Crime before eventually listening to Fugi-Dove's story about his hatred for Jay, only to easily find it false, until they reach a grate, and start to make plans for crime breaking, only to end up captured by Samurai X when they had inadvertently triggered her hidden sensors, resulting in the three recaptured prisoners to proclaim her as the worst.

After Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Sometime at this point, the Mechanic managed to break out from Kryptarium Prison again. Then he was confronted by Unagami and he decided to help him with his plan, thus beginning preparations for the release of Prime Empire. Unagami then created or improved the Mechanic's Noodle Truck of Crime. The Mechanic was also associated with some henchmen by this point.

Prime Empire Original Shorts[]

Let's Dance[]

When Nya and Jay arrive at Benny's Arcade Emporium, Lloyd calls them and notifies them that they've received a report of the Mechanic breaking into a warehouse.

Upgrade[]

After stealing money from the Ninjago City Bank, the Mechanic went to his headquarters and told a computer about the Noodle Truck's upgrades. When the Mechanic asked if the computer can reveal his identity to him, the computer replied that he'll reveal his identity when the time is right.

After failing to use a noodle machine in his Noodle Truck, the Mechanic asks the computer if he was able to get a robo-arm that is able to feel things. Luckily for the Mechanic, the computer was able to print blueprints of the arm, which the Mechanic quickly built. After testing out the robo-arm, the Mechanic once again questions the computer's identity again, only for the computer to reply that his name is Unagami.

Prime Empire[]

Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?[]

The Mechanic and his henchmen enter an old electronics warehouse and steal the Prime Empire motherboard. However, they are spotted by the ninja so he escapes in his noodle truck. After a short chase through the streets, he is captured and arrested when Cole ties him to a pole.

While the ninja were investigating his hideout, Jay finds an old arcade cabinet which is titled Prime Empire. He inserts the recovered motherboard into the cabinet and a wave of red energy bursts from the machine. Before being escorted to the Ninjago City Police Station, he laughs wickedly as he hears the shockwave of red energy pass through.

Dyer Island[]

The Mechanic was interrogated by the ninja about the mysterious V-shaped symbol as well as Unagami, the latter surprising him. He then told them it was too late and it had already started. Lloyd then has the Commissioner take the Mechanic away while he gloats. As he was still gloating with officers dragging him, he peeks his head at the doorway to resume gloating before the Commissioner closes the door on his face.

Level Thirteen[]

The Mechanic was able to escape the prison and convinced the prisoners (except Fugi-Dove) to work for Unagami.

Later, the Mechanic and the prisoners invaded Borg Tower to retrieve the original Arcade Pod. When they finally make it upstairs, the prisoners fight off the ninja and the Mechanic tells Lloyd that Unagami plans to start a new revolution and that he'll be serving at his side. Moments later, Lloyd attempts to enter Prime Empire but was stopped by the Mechanic. However, the Mechanic was pushed by Zane, allowing the ninja to enter Prime Empire while Zane escapes with P.I.X.A.L. and Cyrus Borg. When Soto tried to go after him, the Mechanic stops him, saying that they're only at Borg Tower to retrieve the original Arcade Pod. Although, when the Mechanic pressed the start button, the Arcade Pod wouldn't work, as Zane took the Prime Empire motherboard when he escaped with P.I.X.A.L. and Cyrus.

Ninjago Confidential[]

The Mechanic and his henchmen ambush Zane and P.I.X.A.L. while they are investigating Buddy's Pizza. Zane is electrocuted by one of the Mechanic's gadgets. When P.I.X.A.L. checks to see if he's alright, but she too is electrocuted by The Mechanic.

The Prodigal Father[]

The Mechanic explains to Zane that they are building the Manifestation Gate which requires Energy cubes from the players who lost all of their lives. The Mechanic then demands the Prime Empire motherboard, but Zane claims he destroyed it. Knowing that he's lying, The Mechanic unveils Wu who was captured earlier by henchmen. He goes as far as to threaten to harm Wu, so Zane finally confesses and gives him the motherboard. The Mechanic inserts the motherboard in the Manifestation Gate and also uses Zane for a power source despite having a high risk of being destroyed. A portal is created and The Mechanic waits for Unagami to enter.

Game Over[]

Once the Manifestation Gate was complete, an anger-riddled Unagami escaped Prime Empire, along with his armies and the AI within the game. The Mechanic and his henchmen were overwhelmed by the spectacle and, ironically, ran away from the master they were serving.

The Mechanic then returned to his headquarters and hid there.

After Master of the Mountain[]

City Tour[]

The Mechanic was wandering in the city, and saw Murtessa, who was secretly able to go inside the Destiny's Bounty after the ninja's trip to Shintaro. The Mechanic was able to convince Murtessa to give her a tour of the city.

He first brought her to a food stand, the vendor there ran away after seeing Murtessa's unnatural appearance, he later told her that everything in the city is free and was able to trick her to bring a large device called the Nexus 6 Compensator from a Borg Store. He also lied to her telling her that he had a surprise for the ninja and told he to drive a driller with a secret bomb attached on it's back to the monastery.

Unfortunately the Mechanic's plan backfired and the ninja stopped the driller, after discovering the what really happened Wu offered her a proper tour of the city. The Mechanic later went back to his headquarters.

Crystalized[]

The Mechanic was hiding in his noodle truck, watching the city's new mayor, Ulysses Trustable's annoucement about the city's parade. The planning didn't trust Trustable as mayor and planned to have revenge against him. Unfortunately, Cole recognized his laugh and removed the curtain covering his truck, after being seeing the ninja, the Mechanic started the truck's engine and the flipped the mayor's stage with chains that were attached at the truck's back, causing the mayor to also fall.

The ninja tried to follow the Mechanic, Jay and Cole used a parade vehicle to follow him but accidentally crashed, the five ninja used a big balloon to try to stop him, but caused a lot of damage instead. The Mechanic was able to outrun the ninja, and the damage they caused would worsen their reputation in the eyes of the mayor.

The Fifth Villain[]

As the ninja entered the old arcade that was the Mechanic's base, the criminal attacked them using the robot arms of his truck. When Lloyd shouted at him that he would not escape, the Mechanic agreed with him, as it was his home, and he continued to fight. When Zane attacked him, the Mechanic fired his robo-arm and tried to hit him. However, Lloyd then managed to drop the Prime Empire arcade cabinet on the Mechanic and trapped him. Subsequently, the ninja gagged him and tied him up, and placed him in his truck.

The Council of the Crystal King[]

While Lloyd pretended to be the Mechanic and went to the meeting of the Crystal Council instead, the real Mechanic was still tied up and gagged in the truck. However, when no one was watching, he managed to disconnect his robo-arm and free himself. Subsequently, he started fighting with Jay and Cole, and he even managed to flip the truck over. After the accident, he quickly opened the door and fled across the manhole to the subway tunnel. As the ninja climbed into the tunnel, the Mechanic hid and then knocked out the distracted ninja onto the train tracks. Although they managed to save, the Mechanic insisted that he just wanted to make sure they were awake. Then he started fighting them and even told them he could do it all day as the ninja got out of shape. As another train passed by, the Mechanic grabbed it with his robo-arm and escaped so he could meet the Crystal King.

After The Council of the Crystal King[]

The Mechanic was eventually brought to the Crystal Temple and met the other members of the council.

The Fall of the Monastery[]

The Mechanic and most of the other members of the Crystal Council were sent to steal the Golden Weapons from the Monastery of Spinjitzu by Harumi. He, Pythor, Aspheera and Vangelis were able to enter in the Hangar bay by using the secret tunnel Aspheera used previously. Despite interference from them and the MiniPix, the council were able to steal them with the Mechanic taking the Shurikens of Ice.

Darkness Within[]

After raiding and destroying the Monastery, the Mechanic returns to the Crystal Temple with the four Golden Weapons, along with his fellow members. Around the big crystal, they place the four weapons around it as Aspheera uses her new staff and its magic to transform the Golden Weapons into the Weapons of Destruction through a ritual and through that, they bring the Overlord back into the world of Ninjago, known as the Crystal King.

The Coming of the King[]

Upon the Overlord's return, he gives the council powers before handing them back the crystalized Golden Weapons. When the Mechanic saw Mister F handle one of the shurikens, he protests before he is given the other. He soon mocks the other members' failed leadership before being silenced by the Overlord. After Lloyd jumps, dragging Harumi along, Aspheera demands for the council to jump after them, but the Mechanic refuses and retorts back, telling her to jump.

Return to Primeval's Eye[]

The Council chases Lloyd and Harumi on Dragonides.

Quittin' Time![]

In midst of the crystal infection, the Crystal Council received a directive from the Overlord to deliver the ninja to him. The Mechanic was worried about the slow progress of the Vengestone Guards and urged them to speed up their search. Meanwhile, Nya attempted to break free from the mast on top of her, but the Mechanic intercepted her. He ridiculed her for her lack of abilities and cautioned her that her tenure as a ninja had come to an end. Subsequently, he instructed the guards to crystallize her. Upon Jay's arrival, the Mechanic praised the Lightning Ninja's skills and proceeded to inquire about his relationship with Nya, pointing out the clash between water and electricity. Jay, however, responded with a retort and fought with the Mechanic. The Mechanic attempt the throw his Shuriken of Destruction at Jay, but the latter deflects it, sending the Mechanic and the Vengestone Guards backward. As he recovered, the Mechanic ordered the Vengestone Guards to go after Jay and Nya.

An Issue of Trust[]

The Mechanic meets with the Overlord but he is reprimanded by the spirit before Harumi and Mister F reveal that they know where the ninja are. He later joins the Council to surround the Resistance with their army to overwhelm them.

Dragon Form[]

Along with the rest of the crystal army, the Mechanic engaged in battle against the Resistance while mounted on a Dragonide. Using his Dragonide, the Mechanic attacked Ronin, transforming him into a crystal zombie. However, the Mechanic and the army were confronted by the allies of the Resistance, allowing the ninja to achieve Dragon Form. The Mechanic taunted Nya, highlighting her perceived inability to attain Dragon Form, which he deemed as a crucial aspect of being a ninja. The Mechanic made an attempt to use his Shuriken of Destruction, however, it was ineffective as the opposing shuriken had been restored. Nya retaliated by trapping the Mechanic under a water pipe, and Okino intervened by severing his robo-arm.

Following the battle, the Mechanic was apprehended and incarcerated in Kryptarium Prison with the remainder of the Crystal Council.[2]

After Crystalized[]

At some point, the Mechanic was able to escape from Kryptarium Prison once again and retrieve or replace his robo-arm.

Learning By Teaching![]

However, upon hearing about the tea shortages, the ninja discovered what was going on and trained the tea farmers and suppliers on how to fight and defend themselves. Eventually, the Mechanic led an attack towards the plantation, leading to his defeat and hostage.

During a party organized by the ninja, to thank all their friends and allies that helped them in their battle. The Mechanic and Miss Demeanor tried to enter the party, with the Mechanic wearing a pink dress. Unfortunately, the villains disguises were not successful, causing the Mechanic and Miss Demeanor to be humiliated by the ninja and the crowd shortly before leaving the party.

The Mechanic's henchmen stole a bag of Vengestone, after which the group was celebrating it with hot dogs. Suddenly, Elemental Master of Portals opened a portal to their hideout, and without any introduction, asked for their help against the ninja. The Mechanic and his henchmen didn't even realise what happened, but were attacked by the ninja coming from that portal.

Personality

Personality[]

The Mechanic can be best described as sadistic and vengeful, such as when he attempted to remove parts from Zane and used him to power up the Manifestation Gate. He also has a bit of pride, as when he made up the story about how Nya stole his noodle truck. At times, he can be cowardly, such as when he ran away after the Manifestation Gate started to go unstable.

Appearance

Appearance[]

The Mechanic is a middle-aged man with brown teeth, a single golden tooth, and a small mustache. As shown in his anime-style version, his eyes are gray. He originally had circles under his eyes and cheekbones and wrinkles on his face, but those have disappeared since the animation studio changed from WILFilm ApS to WildBrain. He always wears a black bowler hat that has a piece of a Nindroid mask with three red lights placed over it to cover his eye. These lights turned red in Sons of Garmadon and in Dragons Rising and were light blue in Skybound and from Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu till Crystalized, he has a red sash on his hat. He also has a robo-arm in place of his left arm, which has two versions, either regular or as a flamethrower.

When enclosed in Kryptarium Prison, he has a plain white prison suit with gray stripes and pants. Unlike the other inmates, his sleeves are torn. He has metal parts and cables on the suit that hold his robo-arm.

In his civilian clothes, he has a beige sweater covered in metal parts and cables that hold his robo-arm. He also has a long torn red coat, pale gray pants, and since Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu, he began wearing gray shoes.

From the Crystalized episodes, "The Coming of the King," up until "Dragon Form," the Mechanic's appearance changed after being empowered by the Overlord. The stripe around his hat became purple, as well as his eye color. The lights on the Nindroid mask became purple, and his torso was changed to have him wear the Council of the Crystal King purple and metal armor, though on the left side, he still kept the metal parts and cables that were present before, but the cables are now glowing purple-pink. He also has purple sleeves and infectious crystals on his shoulders, and his legs are black with metallic gray printing. His robo-arm was also upgraded, to appear colored in black, and with glowing purple parts. The shape of it changed to a more rectangular style, and he can shoot blasts of Darkness. He reverted back to his original form following the Overlord's defeat.

Abilities[]

  • Cyborg Enhancements: The Mechanic had enhance himself with robot parts making him part robot. In "Upgrade," due to gaining designs from Unagami, The Mechanic became much more formidable than before as he manage to become stronger and had better reflexes than before. After his arm was sliced by Okino, the Mechanic's enhancements was reduced and he ended up created robotic arms attached to his back.

    • Nindroid visor: The Mechanic has a Nindroid visor over his left eye. He mange to use this to track the droid's movements to get through the invisible lasers in "Level Thirteen."
  • Combat: The Mechanic is extremely skilled in combat, he managed to give the original four ninja a struggle, granted they were out of practice and very rusty at the time.

  • Shuriken of Destruction: The Mechanic shared one of the Shurikens of Destruction with Mister F.

Official description[]

LEGO.com[]

The Mechanic is a renowned villain who used to work for Master Chen and has clashed with the ninja several times over the years. He has an arsenal of powerful weapons, and got his nickname after repairing vehicles for Chen’s army. After several spells in prison, The Crystal King invites him to join his council and steal the Weapons of Spinjitzu. The Mechanic has a robotic arm and eye.[9]

"The Dojo" on LEGO.com wanted poster[]

The Mechanic is NOT a popular guy around NINJAGO® City, having unleashed the deadly ‘Prime Empire’ game upon them. Part man, part cyborg and the owner of some serious firepower, The Mechanic should be watched very carefully...[10]

Behind the scenes

Behind the scenes[]

  • He uses part of a Nindroid's head as part of his head.
    • The eye part of the Nindroid head is now red, but it appears blue in Season 6, Season 11, Prime Empire Original Shorts, Season 12 and Season 15 before he was empowered by the Overlord and appears purple when he is empowered by the Overlord in Crystalized.
    • This makes him the third character to have cyborg parts, the first being Cyrus Borg and the second being Wu in Rebooted, and followed by Iron Baron, Daddy No Legs, Arkade and Glutinous.
  • In issue 59 of the Ninjago Magazine, he is shown having two robo-arms instead of one.
  • Prime Empire marks his first major antagonistic role. In all his other appearances, he only appeared in one episode and was a minor antagonist. In Season 12, he is the secondary antagonist as he was the one who commandeered the bringing of Unagami into the living world.
  • Since Season 11, his cheekbones, wrinkles, and the circles under his eyes were removed from his face design.
    • According to Tommy Andreasen, "When [the] Mechanic was first created it was done by WF and he was not a toy. LEGO supplied a new design to WB since [the] Mechanic is now a toy (Ninjago City set) and it was requested that WBs portayal looked like that."[11]
  • There are several differences between his regular and anime-style versions:
    • Despite he has brown teeth with one gold tooth, his anime-style version has a complete set of white teeth.
    • In his anime-style version he doesn't have a red ribbon on his hat.
    • He doesn't have a glove on his hand in his anime-style version.
    • His regular version has a yellow-green sweater, but his anime-style version has a gray prison jumpsuit.
    • In his anime-style version, he has no metal parts and cables on his chest.
    • His robo-arm is bigger and has a different look.
    • His anime-style version has a belt, but the regular version does not.
    • His regular version has a torn coat, but the anime-style version has it intact.
  • The only season in which he does not appear in his civilian clothes is Season 6. In Season 11, he appeared in these clothes only in his story.
  • One of the pirates[12] in Captain Soto's crew has the same face as the Mechanic before Season 11.
    • When asked about it, Tommy Andreasen implied that they are the same character, stating, "What's a pirate to do when his Captain in jail? Work for another crime boss of course!"[13]
      • However, he later stated that the two aren't the same person.[14]
    • In issue 94 of the Ninjago Magazine, he is directly stated to be Captain Soto's pirate.[15]
  • He uses a recolored hat piece of Dr. Rodney Rathbone from LEGO Monster Fighters.
  • His minifigure has the Nindroid eye piece printed on his head rather than being molded to his bowler hat as a physical piece. He also lacks leg and arm printing.
    • According to Michael Knap, the production of the Mechanic's minifigure would require too many new elements, which is why his minifigure looks different.[16]
Trivia

Trivia[]

  • His real name is unknown.
  • It's unknown how he lost his left arm and replaced it with a robot arm.
  • He speaks with a Southern American accent.
  • He is the prisoner who escaped from Kryptarium the most times. He ran away before Season 8, during Season 11, after Season 11, during Season 12 and at some point before The Elemental Mechs.
    • He is also shown getting arrested in "City Tour" (after Season 13) and in "Learning By Teaching!" (between Season 15 and the Merge), but it is unknown if he escaped before or after being put in the prison.
  • According to him, the absolute worst ninja is Nya, although at the end of the episode "The Absolute Worst", together with Ultra Violet and Fugi-Dove, he agrees that Samurai X is the worst.
  • Although he wants to get Zane's spare parts in Season 6, he doesn't show interest in them in his appearances during other seasons.
    • It is possible that he simply finds no reason to continue seeking out Zane's parts, after finding other means of upgrading himself.
  • He is suspected in 37 unsolved criminal cases, at the time of "Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?."
  • Lloyd implied that the Mechanic is so far the only known Kryptarium inmate not to get captured by Ronin after the breakout from "Level Thirteen" or "Sail Ahoy."[17]
  • When asked if there are things we don't know about him, Tommy Andreasen replied, "His name? Shoe size? Obviously lots."[18]

Relationships

Allies

Allies[]

Anacondrai Cultists (formerly)[]

“I used to repair noodle trucks for Master Chen and his underground organization. When you put him out of business, you put me out of business.”
— The Mechanic to Lloyd, "Enkrypted"

Little is known about the Mechanic's relationship with the Anacondrai cultists, only that he worked and repaired noodle trucks for them. When the ninja defeated Chen, the Mechanic was locked up in Kryptarium Prison.

Fugi-Dove (formerly)[]

“Sorry, was this a private escape or can anyone join?”
“You can come, bird guy.”
— Fugi-Dove and the Mechanic, "The Absolute Worst"

The Mechanic and Ultra Violet met Fugi-Dove in their attempt to escape from the Kryptarium Prison. They are both taken by surprise at the beginning, but they very quickly accept him among themselves and start telling stories about who is the worst ninja. Later, when Unagami frees the Mechanic and the other prisoners from Kryptarium Prison, the Mechanic wants the prisoners to work with him for Unagami, but he doesn't want Fugi-Dove to go with him.

The Mechanic and Fugi-Dove later became enemies after they joined opposite sides in the final prophecy with the Mechanic becoming a general for the Overlord and Fugi-Dove helping the Resistance.

Unagami (formerly)[]

“We've been working together for weeks. You gotta tell me who you are.”
“You will know all... when the time is right.”
— The Mechanic and Unagami, "Upgrade"

The Mechanic met Unagami under unknown circumstances. First, Unagami helped the Mechanic repair his Noodle Truck of Crime and his robo-arm, and later explained his plan to him. The Mechanic quickly believed that he would rule Unagami's side after the revolution and began searching for the Prime Empire motherboard. Unagami was very ruthless to the Mechanic when he told him that if he let him down again, he would be game over. The Mechanic later managed to build the Manifestation Gate and open the gate from Prime Empire to Ninjago. However, when Unagami came out of the gate, the Mechanic fled cowardly.

Unagami later helped the Resistance fight the Overlord and his Crystal Army, in which the Mechanic was a general of.

Henchmen (formerly)[]

“Who's trying to get away? There are more of us than you! And we're not afraid to use our weapons!”
— The Mechanic about him and the henchmen to the ninja, "Would You Like to Enter Prime Empire?"

Sometime before "Upgrade" the Mechanic recruited a couple of thugs to be his henchmen. Despite this, the Mechanic was sometimes rude and domineering to them.

Ultra Violet (formerly)[]

“If the ninja are out of the picture, we might actually have a chance!”
— The Mechanic to Ultra Violet about their escape, "The Absolute Worst"

During his stay at Kryptarium Prison, he became close with Ultra Violet, so when he learned that the ninja had disappeared, he followed her to plan an escape together. At night, they managed to get out of Kryptarium Prison, and during their escape they met Fugi-Dove. With him they told stories about who the worst ninja was. After their escape, however, they were closed again by Samurai X.

Later, when Unagami released the prisoners from Kryptarium Prison, the Mechanic was among the first to ask her to go work for Unagami.

During the Final prophecy of Quanish the Elder, both the Mechanic and Ultra Violet fought in opposite sides as the Mechanic joined the Crystal Council and fought for the Overlord while Ultra Violet allied with the ninja and joined the Resistance.

Enemies

Enemies[]

Ninja[]

“Then by my observations, that was your own error. Working for a crime boss isn't a very reliable career choice.”
“By my observation, actions have consequences. And you have to pay. This Nindroid here, it's got spare parts. I could use a few in a place like this.”
— Zane and the Mechanic, "Enkrypted"

After the defeat of Chen, the Mechanic swore revenge on the ninja for getting him into prison. He originally wanted revenge by disassembling Zane and later trying various other methods.

Nya[]

“What happened? No golden wings for you? Guess that means you're not a real ninja! Just a girl in a costume. Huh?”
— The Mechanic to Nya, "Dragon Form"

Out of all the ninja, the Mechanic despises Nya the most. In Season 15, he makes fun of her lack of power, as well as her not able to achieve Dragon Form, and calls her a 'girl in a costume.'

Sora[]

“The new ninja with Tech Powers, huh? Ha! I don't need Elemental Power to be a master of technology!”
— The Mechanic to Sora, "The Mech Master"

The Mechanic encountered Sora when he stole the mechs in the Mech Master 5000 at the Crossroads. Amongst the chaos, it became evident that the Mechanic possessed superior skills compared to the other ninja present. However, Sora was to be the only one capable of keeping up with the Mechanic's relentless pace and putting a stop to his misdeeds. Aware of Sora's reputation as the Elemental Master of Technology, the Mechanic couldn't resist taunting her. He arrogantly claimed that his expertise in technology surpassed even Elemental Powers. The Mechanic resorted to pushing Sora off a nearby bridge. Unbeknownst to him, Sora propelled herself back up and catching the Mechanic off guard. She dismantled the Mechanic's mech, rendering it useless. As the Mechanic lay defeated, Sora and the other ninja couldn't help but openly mention their lack of admiration for his abilities.

P.I.X.A.L.[]

“The Mechanic could always use more spare parts.”
— The Mechanic to P.I.X.A.L., "Dragon Form"

Although the Mechanic was not aware of the presence of P.I.X.A.L. in Season 6, when he threatened Zane she gathered Zane's energy and defeated the Mechanic. Later, after his, Ultra Violet, and Fugi-Dove's escape, P.I.X.A.L. as Samurai X stops and places them back to Kryptarium Prison, resulting in his hatred for her.

Later, during Zane and P.I.X.A.L.'s investigations, the Mechanic attacked them and ruthlessly injured P.I.X.A.L. by electricity in the cable.
