Overview

“...versus, the liquid warrior who will never bore ya! From the Azure Reef, it's Kizzy, Elemental Master of Balance!”
— Roby, "United We Fall"

Kizzy (nicknamed Kiz by Sora) is a Water-Tide from the Azure Reef and the current Elemental Master of Balance who was a competitor of the Tournament of the Sources, alongside the other Elemental Masters of her generation.

History

History[]

At some point, Kizzy was granted the power of Balance.

Enter the City of Temples[]

Kizzy arrived at the waterfall towards the City of Temples, where she submitted her invite and rode the elevator. At the same time, the ninja, who weren't invited, infiltrated with Arin and Sora in the Explorer grappling to the front of the lift, causing the lift to shake and confusing Kizzy.

Kizzy was later outside the gates of the City of Temples cheering at the big screen displaying the disruptive entrance of the ninja.

They Gather for the Feast[]

Kizzy attended the welcome feast, where Roby began a speech before being interrupted by Zeatrix, who started an argument with the ninja and Roby as a result of Bleckt sidelining her.

United We Fall[]

Kizzy was matched against Sora in the Tournament of the Sources where the two were competing in a parkour challenge, where Kizzy had the initial advantage, thanks to her Balance power. However, Sora used her Technology power and won against Kizzy, which led to the latter's exit from the tournament and she gave up her powers to Sora via the Elemental Cup.

The Final Game[]

Kizzy walked along Foundation Street, moving past other Elemental Masters while Wyldfyre cleverly swiped Bleckt's ledger and replaced it with the menu from the welcome banquet. Later that evening, she and the others prepared to participate in the victory ceremony, where Roby was set to crown Nokt as the Champion of the Sources. However, they soon discovered from Wyldfyre and Zur that Bleckt had manipulated the games to ensure that the Wolf Clan emerged victorious.

Elements of Betrayal[]

Kizzy fought alongside several other Elemental Masters against Cinder and the Wolf Mask Warriors in hand-to-hand combat. Upon the return of their elements, Kizzy and the rest of the allied Elemental Masters successfully defeated them. She later watched Roby declare Sora the default winner of the tournament.
