History[]

Legends of Chima, Season 1[]

The Legend of Chima (flashback)[]

To find Worriz, Eris brought Skinnet and nose plugs with her. After Eris and her friends plugged their noses, Skinnet released his odour, causing Worriz to reveal himself, much to his annoyance. Then Skinnet apologized to Worriz as he left.

The Biggest Race Ever[]

During the Chi Day, Skinnet arrives in the Lion City for the monthly Speedor race with the other racers. Then, he starts tending his Speedor.

Later, Skinnet goes to the start line to wait for the race to start. After the race started, Skinnet and the other racers started racing. However, while passing the waterfalls, the running water makes Skinnet release his odour, which annoys Rogon and Bladvic, forcing Skinnet to apologize for it.

Foxtrot[]

After Furtivo spreads lies about the race, Skinnet gets worried like everyone and tells Bladvic that something really stinks about this race, and it's not just him. Then, he goes to the finish line in the Grand Arena, where the race starts. However, at the start of the race, the Gorilla Tribe threw banana peels into the Grand Arena, which Skinnet's Speedor slips on, causing him to get eliminated from the race.

Crocodile Tears[]

While the Wolf Tribe were treating the captured Crocodile Tribe nicely, Skinnet watched them from far away, finding the fruit shakes the Wolves made delicious.

Later, Skinnet went to the Lion Temple, telling the Elders of Chima that the Wolves made fruit shakes for everyone, asking if there's any way he can be taken prisoner by the Wolves too, which Balkar found as a wrong act. Then, he told Ewald that they don't need to rush there if they do not want to taste Winzar's pastries, saying that they are delicious, which pleased Grumlo.

Ravens vs Eagles[]

Seeing his friends racing with their Speedorz, Skinnet joins them. While passing by the Gorilla Village , they see Crooler lying in the floor. However, Gorzan tells them to not wake her up since she is having so much fun. Then, he also joins the race. As the race continues, Dom de la Woosh andShadoWind also joins the race, with the ShadoWind leading them to the Spiral Mountain, where they decide to relax for the rest of the day after seeing the view of Mount Cavora from there. While relaxing, Laval and Cragger decides to make a peace treaty, which makes everyone cheer, including Skinnet. However, when they were continuing to relax, Razar and Eris fell from the sky, much to their surprise. Then they also joined their friends to relax with them.

Into the Outlands[]

As the Heroes of Chima were about to leave and go to the Outlands, Skinnet arrived and gave Laval stick grenades that he spent all night making it. Laval explained that Skinnet is the only person he knows of aside from the ShadoWind that made regular trips to the Outlands, much to everyone's surprise. Then, he waved at the Heroes of Chima as they left for the Outlands.

Unfinished Business[]

While going to the Outlands, Laval took Skinnet with him, traveling through the Outlands as Skinnet released his odour to stop the Predator Plants from eating them. After they arrived the Outland Base, Skinnet what are they doing here, which in response Laval explained that Lavertus' journal said he has unfinished business left here. After entering the base, Skinnet started to look everywhere, making a huge mess, much to Laval's annoyance, who wanted Skinnet to search somewhere else.

After Laval defeated the Bat Tribe, Skinnet came and said that he found the "unfinished business", showing Laval the working oven.

Legends of Chima, Season 3[]

Fire Dreaming[]

In the Chi Day Market, Plovar advertised the bubble spa alongside with Skinnet. Plovar wanted him to give a little demonstration to show it to Elon and Cragger, which in response Skinnet released his odour to the spa, causing water to turn green and making Elon and Cragger run away. Later, when Razar launched Wonald with their Legend Beast replica, he ended up in the bubble spa, much to Skinnet and Plovar's shock. Then, Eris entered the bubble spa, thinking that her wings are on fire because of her vision, shocking Skinnet. After Eris realized that she now sees things while she is awake, Skinnet started farting in the spa, much to Eris' annoyance.

Later, at night, Skinnet was hanging out with the other tribes in the Forever Rock. Laval says that Eris should consider herself lucky to get dunked with the skunk, since Skinnet believes that the Skunks made Chima, and they all laugh. However, this eventually turns into an argument about what really made Chima, only for Laval to stop them. Then, all of them laughed when Cragger called Eris "Hot Wings", including Skinnet, who leaved the Forever Rock shortly after.

While passing from the Fangs, Skinnet starts to feel himself cold. Then, he looks at the Gorge of Eternal Depth and sees something is rising, which worries him.

The Crescent[]

After hearing Laval's call, Skinnet used his stink signal to let Laval know he is coming, accidentally knocking out a Raven in the process.

Later, when the Heroes of Chima were protecting the Crescent from the Hunters, Skinnet arrives with his custom armor and his Battle Stick, much to the heroes' surprise. Cragger explaines him that they got things in under control as long as they stay inside the Crescent. Skinnet gets disappointed and says that he doesn't have to hold "this" anymore, releasing his odour despite Eris' warning, annoying the heroes, which makes him apologize. However, the odour scares the Hunters, as the heroes come out of it running.

While Fluminox was thanking the heroes for protecting the Crescent, Skinnet said how it sure holds his stink nicely. Fluminox says that they can use the Fire Chi whenever it is needed, which excites Skinnet. Then, Skinnet watches the Blazing Bastion leave the Phoenix Outpost.

Cool and Collected[]

While searching for Flinx with his telescope, Fangar spots Skinnet, who is releasing his odour, and looks at him for a while, before panning his telescope somewhere else.

Later, when the Hunters try to capture Flinx, they accidentally ambush Skinnet and freeze him, bringing him to their cavern. However, Skinnet releases his odour and frees himself from ice, much to the Hunters' annoyance, who decided to retreat. Skinnet exists the cavern and tells the Hunters that it's a great idea to have him make his stink underground. Then, he leaves the area. Later, he passes in front of the Hunters as Fangar's looks at them with his telescope.

Prepare for Battle[]

Shortly before the Hunters arrived at the Lion City with their Hunter Glacier, Skinnet prepared himself for the fight by wearing his custom armor.

The Heart of Cavora[]

Following the Second Great Illumination, Skinnet was celebrating the victory by chasing a butterfly. However, while chasing it, he accidentally released his odour, which was now smelling good and helping flowers to grow thanks to the Illumination, much to his happiness.

Merry Chima Everyone![]

During Chima, Skinnet celebrated the holiday with Furtivo, making flowers grow thanks to his sweet odour.

Possession[]

Curseworld, Part II[]

Skinnet and his friends were enjoying a relaxing day before being interrupted by Morro and Lloyd, who fell into the area from a portal. He then glanced the others before Morro opened another portal, with both him and Lloyd falling in.

Abilities

Abilities[]

  • Skunk Physiology: Being a skunk, Skinnet has the following abilities.
    • Foul Stench (formerly): Skinnet could emit a massive, overwhelming cloud of defensive fumes. His heavy odor provided a natural immunity against the Predator Plants.
    • Flora Growth & Calming Scent: Under the influence of the Hundred Year Moon, Skinnet's natural abilities reverse. His stink transforms into a sweet perfume that makes flowers instantly sprout and can hypnotically calm enraged animals. Following the Great Illumination, his default foul odor permanently shifted into the pleasant, perfume-like scent.

Official descriptions

Official descriptions[]

Skinnet is one of the most reliable elements of comic relief in CHIMA. Nothing ever goes right for this bumbling skunk. His tribe has vanished. His mightiest weapon is a foul odor... that accidentally slips out at all the wrong times. And even his name, Skinnet, implies a less-than-prosperous purpose. Yet Skinnet never lets the world bring him down. He always tries to tag along, or be part of the latest happening. Inevitably his presence backfires – or, more appropriately, “stink-fires.” But he is always quick to apologize when this happens. “Sorrrrry,” he says, with an awkward drawl. It’s not his fault that he can’t control his natural skunk powers, but he still feels compelled to atone for its punishing effect on others. Skinnet is not aligned with any tribe, and is possibly the most neutral creature in all of CHIMA. This is not by choice. He wishes he could be accepted by one side or the other, and prove himself a hero. However, while he has possibly the most potent weapon in CHIMA, both sides in the CHIMA Conflict are reluctant to use this “nuclear option” for fear it will harm them as well. Everyone actually likes Skinnet – they just don’t want to be too near him. He sometimes acts as a negotiator between the two sides, but he usually ends up clearing the room with his stink before a truce is agreed upon[1]
