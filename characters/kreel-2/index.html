Kreel is the daughter of the Pontifex Magicus from Mysterium. After the Merge, she became a citizen of the Crossroads, the owner of Kreel's Junkyard, and the co-host of Crossroads' Crosstalk. She is a mech racer with a crew of Whack Rats.

History

History[]

Early life[]

Before the Merge, Kreel was born as the daughter of the Pontifex Magicus in Mysterium, which put a lot of pressure on her. She would be kicked out of magic school, prompting her to want to leave Mysterium.[1] During the time after the Merge, Kreel moved to the Crossroads and befriended the Whack Rats, becoming close to one, in particular named Targle. At some point in time, Kreel told Arin she was a robot, who believed her after being impressed by her dance skills.[2]

Tales from the Merged Realms[]

The Way In[]

Kreel made her way to Arin and Sora's penthouse to inspect the mech the teenagers were constructing for the upcoming Mech Master 5000. After a quick glance at the rusted parts Sora was using, Kreel laughed and declared that she was going to win the race, as usual. To add further insult to injury, she revealed to the teens that the registration fee had increased, knowing fully well they couldn't pay for it. With a laugh, Kreel advised Sora to simply give up as she left their penthouse.

The next day, Kreel participated in the Mech Master 5000 and won first place. Following her win, she happily celebrated with her Whack Rats.

Prior to Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

Unfortunately, Targle ended up kidnapped by Dorama, who intended to use his life energy for his fireworks display for the upcoming Crossroads Carnival, and demanded a ransom from Kreel for his release. Because of this, she entered the Mech Master 5000, intending to use the prize money to pay for the ransom.

Dragons Rising, Season 1[]

The Merge: Part 1[]

On the day of the race, she mocks Arin and Sora on their repeated racing failures before they leave to grab more components, and Kreel has a Whack Rat sabotage their racer. During the race, Kreel takes out a few racers, until she, and Arin and Sora's racers reach for the finish line, until a blast, caused by Riyu's escape attempt, knocks some debris into her, knocking her down, leaving her pinned down as another rival racer, Lobbo, passes by and wins the race.

Crossroads Carnival[]

Still determined to get the money which she believed is hers all along, Kreel has the Whack Rats kidnap Lobbo and demands the money, but he immediately swallows it to prevent her before Arin and Sora, who witnessed the kidnapping, arrive. She soon admits how she did it for Targle before Arin and Sora vow to rescue him. While waiting, Kreel and Lobbo make conversations while eating moss-milk ice cones, until Lloyd, Arin, and Sora return with Targle.

Prior to Wyldly Inappropriate[]

At one point, Kreel engaged in a lice game with the original proprietor of the Crossroads junkyard. Kreel emerged victorious, thereby assuming ownership of the dilapidated yard and subsequently rechristening it as "Kreel's Junkyard."

Wyldly Inappropriate[]

Alfonzo Frohicky and a disguised Zane came across Kreel, who questioned the identity of Frohicky's companion but soon disregarded it. Zane then explained their requirement for a wyconium coil. Kreel, being well-versed with wyconium, offered to procure a coil but requested payment in exchange. Regrettably, Zane confessed to not possessing the requisite funds, leading to the both of them thrown out.

The Power Within[]

Kreel was buying Targle a moss-milk ice cone before the MergeQuake Storm began. When the storm began, she and Targle immediately hide behind the ice cone truck.

The Elemental Mechs[]

What the Mech?[]

Kreel advertises many dangerous items she got her hands on, including Chronosteel, which she tried to pass off as harmless before Kai, Cole, Arin, and Sora show up to confiscate them, she tried to deny their dangers, until Lobbo mutated upon touching a dangerous artefact. After Sora used some Chronosteel to make three mech for herself, Kai, and Cole, and turning Lobbo back to normal, Kreel attempted to take the artefacts away, but was prevented by Cole, who confiscates it.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Blood Moon[]

Kreel ends up having her junkyard easily broken into by Cinder, who was looking for the Mallet of Shattering, due to the security bots being overridden and her dogs practically not ferocious enough, and called Arin, Sora, and Riyu about the break in. Since nothing was taken, they find it hard to believe her, until she tells them that many stalls around the Crossroads had the same problem before she has them take a selfie with her, claiming she is friends with them.

Kreel went to the Monastery of Spinjitzu to celebrate the Ninja saving the Merged Realms and watched as Lobbo crushes his pie eating record.

Kreel went into her junkyard where she spotted Riyu and Rontu. Recognizing Riyu, Kreel sighed and asked about Rontu, who apologized for the mess, but Kreel reassured them that she was okay with them breaking the junk.

Sometime later on, she begun going on dates with Fugi-Dove, and let him stay at her apartment.

Relationships[]

Arin and Sora[]

Arin and Sora were Kreel's rivals, Kreel's rivalry began during the Mech Master 5000 which Kreel entered in order to pay the ransom that Dorama had ordered in order to free Targle. Eventually, they came to a compromise and after Arin and Sora rescued Targle, Kreel was extremely grateful and embraced Targle with a hug. When Cinder broke into her junkyard, they were the first people she could think of to call and tells them how others suffered the same problem as her junkyard before having them take a selfie with her.

Trivia

Trivia[]

  • She shares the same character design as Antonia and Blazey H. Speed, the only difference being her hair color.
  • She seems to be fond of leopards, shown with her clothing and headwear.
  • With her and Fugi-Dove being confirmed to have dated, this marks the first couple in the Ninjago franchise to be made of one character from each series, with Fugi-Dove from Ninjago and Kreel from Ninjago: Dragons Rising.
  • Throughout the entirety of Crossroads' Crosstalk, Kreel couldn’t remember any of the ninja's names, including Arin and Sora's, despite her remembering Riyu's name in "Between You and Lee".
    • It's also interesting that she keeps forgetting Arin and Sora's names, despite the fact she has known them for years, since the time she came to the Crossroads, as they have been competing against each other in mech races.
  • While Chris "Doc" Wyatt previously stated in 2024 that Kreel originated from Ninjago,[4] it was later revealed in "Celebration!" that she originated from Mysterium.
  • She states to be younger than the original Ninja.
