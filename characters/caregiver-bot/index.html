Overview

“Request assistance. Child protected from impact, but requires care.”
— Caregiver bot to Heatwave, "I Will Be the Danger"

The Caregiver bot is one of Wyldfyre's fellow caregivers and family, along with Heatwave.

After Wyldfyre went to rescue Heatwave, the bot switched to self care mode[1] and left to wander the Wyldness, having developed a desire to explore the world.[2]

History

History[]

I Will Be the Danger (flashback)[]

The Caregiver bot's ship ends up crashing near a dragon named Heatwave. Regardless of the species, the caregiver bot asks Heatwave for assistance in raising the baby girl it had inside. Naming her Wyldfyre, Heatwave and the Caregiver bot raised her with the latter programmed to teach her essential things, such as fire being too dangerous and family. When Wyldfyre unlocked her Heat powers, Caregiver bot helps her control them. One day, the Claws of Imperium arrive and kidnap Heatwave. Wyldfyre leaves to find him, much to Caregiver bot's protests that it will be dangerous, Wyldfyre proclaims she will be the danger.

Other appearances and mentions[]

Clues and Suspects[]

Wyldfyre mentions her caregiver bot when confronting Zeatrix over her dirty tactic that got Lloyd injured.

The Spectral Lands[]

Wyldfyre mentions her caregiver bot while she was in Lobbo's Totally Rad Arcade, Lobbo-Lobbo.
