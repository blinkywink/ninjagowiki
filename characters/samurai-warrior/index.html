The samurai warrior[1] was a warrior that lived in the post-apocalyptic future of Ninjago.

History

History[]

Golden Legend[]

The samurai warrior walks through Ninjago to look for the golden statues of the ninja. When he found them, he was ambushed by a dragon that was guarding the statues and was stabbed by its tail, turning him into a statue.

Trivia[]

  • He wears a belt very similar to the one that Epic Battle Jay wears.
  • His appearance is somewhat similar to Jay's Rogue costume.
  • Although he may initially act as the central protagonist, towards the end of the episode, he is found to be the antagonist trying to reach the ninja statues.
