Overview

For Harumi's adoptive parents, see Emperor of Ninjago and Empress of Ninjago.

Two unnamed individuals were residents of Ninjago City and the biological parents of Harumi. They were killed by the collapse of their apartment complex, which was caused by the Great Devourer during her attack on the city. Their deaths would have a profound effect on Harumi, who managed to escape before the collapse due to her parents sacrificing themselves for her. Realizing that Lord Garmadon was the one who had killed the Devourer, Harumi went on to worship and resurrect him, all of which was heavily caused by her parents' fates. She would later betray The Overlord for his indirect involvement in that event.

History

History[]

Game of Masks (flashback)[]

During the Great Devourer's attack on Ninjago City, Harumi and her parents were forced to flee from their collapsing apartment complex. However, there wasn't enough room left in the elevator for the three of them, so Harumi's father managed to get Harumi into the elevator, much to their daughter's distress. Harumi's mother grabbed her daughter's hand and promised they would meet outside once they got out in an attempt to soothe her. As the elevator doors closed, the apartment collapsed, killing both of Harumi's parents.

Other appearances and mentions

Other appearances and mentions[]

Garmadon Rulez![]

Harumi was adopted by the Royal Family, she despised them and often looked back at her original life with sorrow.

Sons of Garmadon[]

The Mask of Deception[]

When meeting the ninja, Harumi related to Lloyd by saying that she lost her parents, and the Empress and Emperor explained that they adopted her this.

The Oni and the Dragon[]

In the Destiny's Bounty's sleeping quarters, Harumi looked at her photos of both sets of parents before pinning them to a bulletin board alongside the ninja's photos.

Game of Masks[]

Harumi explained to Lloyd that after she realized Lord Garmadon was responsible for destroying the Devourer, she grew to idolize him while hating Lloyd and the ninja for unknowingly allowing her parents to be killed.

Hunted[]

Saving Faith[]

When Garmadon and Skylor fought for control of the Colossus, Harumi witnessed a boy in the same situation she was in when she was younger. Remembering her parents, she decided to help the family escape. This ultimately lead to Harumi's death when the building collapsed (although she was later resurrected by The Overlord[1]).

Tales from the Monastery of Spinjitzu[]

Samurai X-Treme[]

Nya imagined Harumi and her parents living in small village that was attacked by the Skulkin and saved by Nya in an updated Samurai X Mech. Being a daydream, this depiction of Harumi's family may have been inaccurate.[2]

Crystalized[]

A Sinister Shadow[]

Harumi's parents were briefly seen in a flashback when Harumi explained her life story, and she a moment later she said that she prevented a family a from being torn apart like hers was.

Darkness Within[]

Harumi told Lloyd that she felt so alone after her parents died, and she wanted Lloyd to feel the same way after losing his loved ones, to which Lloyd responded that she was no better than the Devourer.

Dragon Form[]

Harumi was horrified to hear the Overlord revealed that he corrupted the Great Devourer ages ago to corrupt Garmadon, making him a vessel for his return to Ninjago one day. She betrayed the Overlord and called out for his indirect involvement to her parents' deaths.
