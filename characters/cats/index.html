Overview

“Because the life of a cat is precious, and often dangerous, the stone protects them by giving each one eight extra lives.”
— Dillon, The Curse of the Cat-Eye Jewel

Cats are felines which live in various realms such as Ninjago and Imperium. Cats from Ninjago have nine lives thanks to the Cat-Eye Jewel.

History

History[]

Secrets of the Forbidden Spinjitzu[]

Snaketastrophy[]

A photo of Nya chasing a kitten is shown on NGTV News. Later, a woman carries a bunch of cats as she is interviewed by Fred Finely.

Dragons Rising, Season 2[]

The Blood Moon[]

Wyldfyre tries to get a cat down from a tree, only for it to latch onto her and attack her as she frantically moved about.

Fugitives From Madness[]

While Gandalaria dumps contents out of Hatty when Administration Agents arrived to retrieve Zane, a cat was among them.
